# Software Licences — Commercial, Free, Open-Source and Shareware — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is a software licence?" is: The maker's written permission saying what a user may do with a program**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What four questions does every licence answer?" is: Piracy — whatever the copy cost**
**Answer: False** — Cost, copying/sharing, seeing and changing the code, and how long it may be used (any three)

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Using software outside its licence is called what?" is: Piracy — whatever the copy cost**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is commercial software?" is: Software given at no cost, usable without payment on its maker's terms**
**Answer: False** — Software that is sold; its licence is bought and copying/sharing are forbidden

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is free software?" is: Software given at no cost, usable without payment on its maker's terms**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What extra freedom does open-source add?" is: Software given to try for a limited time or with limits, after which the full licence must be bought**
**Answer: False** — Its source code is open to study and change, and changed versions may be shared under the licence

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is trial (shareware) software?" is: Software given to try for a limited time or with limits, after which the full licence must be bought**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Order the four types from tightest to most open." is: No — the licence, not the copy, fixes what you may do**
**Answer: False** — Commercial, trial, free, open-source (tightest to most open as taught)

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Does owning a copy mean owning the program?" is: No — the licence, not the copy, fixes what you may do**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Where does Libya teach the licence types?" is: The maker's written permission saying what a user may do with a program**
**Answer: False** — Grade 12 IT, Chapter 3 — legal legislation and computer systems, in both sections' books
