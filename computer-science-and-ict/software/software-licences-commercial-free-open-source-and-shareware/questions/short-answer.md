# Software Licences — Commercial, Free, Open-Source and Shareware — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a software licence?**
**Answer: The maker's written permission saying what a user may do with a program**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What four questions does every licence answer?**
**Answer: Cost, copying/sharing, seeing and changing the code, and how long it may be used (any three)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Using software outside its licence is called what?**
**Answer: Piracy — whatever the copy cost**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is commercial software?**
**Answer: Software that is sold; its licence is bought and copying/sharing are forbidden**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is free software?**
**Answer: Software given at no cost, usable without payment on its maker's terms**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What extra freedom does open-source add?**
**Answer: Its source code is open to study and change, and changed versions may be shared under the licence**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is trial (shareware) software?**
**Answer: Software given to try for a limited time or with limits, after which the full licence must be bought**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Order the four types from tightest to most open.**
**Answer: Commercial, trial, free, open-source (tightest to most open as taught)**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Does owning a copy mean owning the program?**
**Answer: No — the licence, not the copy, fixes what you may do**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Where does Libya teach the licence types?**
**Answer: Grade 12 IT, Chapter 3 — legal legislation and computer systems, in both sections' books**
