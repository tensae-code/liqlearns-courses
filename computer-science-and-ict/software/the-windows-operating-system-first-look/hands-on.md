# Hands-on — The Windows Operating System — First Look

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Watch it arrive
Switch a computer on and write down, in order, everything that appears before the desktop. Say which of those things is Windows itself and which are programs.

## Name the landmarks
On a real desktop, point to and name: an icon, the taskbar, the Start button and the clock. Then close every open window and say what you are looking at.
