# Hands-on — Integrating Information — Import and OLE Linking and Embedding

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Three ways, one page', 'Place the same small table into a document three ways: imported, linked, embedded. Change the source table and record which copies followed and which did not.')

## Task two
('Plan the tour', 'Plan your own 'homeland tour' presentation of eight slides. For each slide's parts, mark I, L or E (import, link, embed) and defend the marks by the choosing rule.')
