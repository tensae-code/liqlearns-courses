# Hands-on — Open-Source and Web-Based Software

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Sort the software** — List six programs you use. Mark each: installed, open-source, web-based (some will carry two marks). Say what each mark tells you.

## Task two
**The trade** — For one web-based program you use, write what you gain against an installed program, and what you give up. Would you switch? Why?
