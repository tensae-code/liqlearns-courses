# Video script — Open-Source and Web-Based Software

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Open-source software** (phần mềm nguồn mở) is software whose **source code is published**: anyone may **use it, study how it works, change it, and share it** under its licence — Linux, LibreOffice and Inkscape (met in the design lessons) are the book's kind of examples. The idea is the opposite of the secret recipe: because many eyes can read the code, faults are found by the crowd, and because anyone may build on it, good open programs gather improvers worldwide. Vietnam's Grade 11 core teaches it beside its twin below, as the two ways software now reaches its users.
**Web-based software** (phần mềm chạy trên Internet) is used **through the browser** and runs on a **server**: nothing is installed, the same program and your files meet you from **any machine you sign in on**, and the maker updates it once, for everyone at once. Its costs are the mirror: it needs a **connection**, and your data lives on **someone else's computer**, under their care and their terms. The Grade 11 judgement: installed, open, web-based — each way of reaching software trades control, convenience and trust differently, and the user should know which trade they are making.
