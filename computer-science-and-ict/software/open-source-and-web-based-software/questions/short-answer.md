# Open-Source and Web-Based Software — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is open-source software?**
**Answer: Software whose source code is published for anyone to use, study, change and share under its licence**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What four freedoms does the licence give?**
**Answer: Use, study, change, and share**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name two open-source examples.**
**Answer: Linux and LibreOffice (also Inkscape)**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why do many eyes help?**
**Answer: Faults in published code are found by the crowd**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is the opposite image?**
**Answer: The secret recipe — closed source code**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How is web-based software used?**
**Answer: Through the browser — it runs on a server, nothing is installed**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What follows you from machine to machine?**
**Answer: The same program and your files, wherever you sign in**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How do updates reach users?**
**Answer: The maker updates it once, for everyone at once**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What are web-based software's two costs?**
**Answer: It needs a connection, and your data lives on someone else's computer**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What should the user know?**
**Answer: Which trade they are making — control, convenience and trust differ by kind**
