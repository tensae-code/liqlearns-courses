# Changing Windows Appearance and Screen Saver — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is the general appearance of Windows?**
**Answer: How Windows looks — the background picture, the colours of windows and bars, and the size of what is shown**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Where is the appearance changed?**
**Answer: In the appearance settings, reached through the Control Panel**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Does changing the appearance change how programs work?**
**Answer: No — it dresses the same Windows in a chosen look**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is the background?**
**Answer: The picture behind the icons on the desktop**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why does Sudan's book teach appearance early?**
**Answer: So the learner makes the computer their own before using its tools for real work**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is a screen saver?**
**Answer: A moving picture or pattern that covers the screen when the computer is left alone for a set time**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Why were screen savers first made?**
**Answer: To protect screens from a still picture burning into them**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What two choices do you make when setting a screen saver?**
**Answer: Which saver, and how many idle minutes start it**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What else does a screen saver do today?**
**Answer: It hides your work from passing eyes**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How does the screen wake from the saver?**
**Answer: Touch the mouse or press a key — your work returns as you left it**
