# Hands-on — History of Operating Systems

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Timeline wall** — Draw the history timeline: no system, batch monitors, time-sharing, Unix 1969, MS-DOS 1981, Macintosh 1984, Linux 1991, iOS 2007, Android 2008 — one line of 'the pain it solved' under each.

## Task two
**Interview the elders** — Ask an older person about the first computer system they used. Note its name, how they gave it orders, and one thing today's systems do that it could not.
