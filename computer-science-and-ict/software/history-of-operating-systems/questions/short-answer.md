# History of Operating Systems — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What did the first computers run without?**
**Answer: An operating system — operators loaded programs by hand, one at a time**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What pain did batch monitors solve?**
**Answer: The machine idling while humans loaded work — jobs were queued automatically**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What did time-sharing add?**
**Answer: Many users interacting with one machine at once**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What lasting pattern did Unix set in 1969?**
**Answer: Small tools, files for everything, and portability across machines**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which modern systems descend from Unix?**
**Answer: Linux directly; today's mobile systems indirectly (Android from Linux, iOS from Unix kin)**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What did the graphical systems replace?**
**Answer: Typed command lines — windows, icons and a mouse took their place**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) When did MS-DOS arrive, and the Macintosh?**
**Answer: MS-DOS in 1981; the graphical Macintosh in 1984**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What did Linux prove in 1991?**
**Answer: A system could be built openly, by a world of volunteers**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What did iOS and Android rebuild the system around?**
**Answer: Touch, apps and sensors — the phone in everyone's pocket**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What question does each generation's system answer?**
**Answer: Its hardware's question — scarce machine time, then one person's desk, then everyone's pocket**
