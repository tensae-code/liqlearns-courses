# Hands-on — Computer software — instructions that make hardware work

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Instructions for a person
Write instructions for making tea or tying a shoe, step by step, for a partner who must follow them exactly and do nothing else. Where your instructions have a gap, your partner stops — just like a computer with missing software. Fix the gap and try again.

## Two piles
On cards, write names of programs you know or have seen. Sort the cards into two piles: runs the computer itself, and does a job for me. Keep any card you cannot sort yet — the next lessons will settle it.
