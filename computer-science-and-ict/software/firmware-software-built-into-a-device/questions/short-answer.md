# Firmware — Software Built into a Device — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is firmware?**
**Answer: Software stored permanently inside a device, telling its hardware how to start and do its basic work**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Where is firmware stored?**
**Answer: In the device's ROM, inside the device itself**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why is it called firm?**
**Answer: It sits between soft software, which changes easily, and hard hardware, which does not change**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Which programs are firmware besides the computer's start-up?**
**Answer: The controlling programs inside devices such as a washing machine, a router or a printer**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does a computer's firmware do at start-up?**
**Answer: It checks the hardware and hands over to the operating system**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) When does firmware work?**
**Answer: At moments other software cannot — starting and controlling the device**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How often is firmware changed?**
**Answer: Rarely — it is rarely seen and rarely changed**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How must a firmware update be done?**
**Answer: Carefully**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What can a damaged firmware cause?**
**Answer: A device that may not start at all**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How long does a device's firmware serve?**
**Answer: For the device's whole life**
