# Firmware — Software Built into a Device — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is firmware?**
a) Software stored permanently inside a device, telling its hardware how to start and do its basic work  b) In the device's ROM, inside the device itself  c) It sits between soft software, which changes easily, and hard hardware, which does not change  d) The controlling programs inside devices such as a washing machine, a router or a printer
**Answer: a) Software stored permanently inside a device, telling its hardware how to start and do its basic work**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Where is firmware stored?**
a) It sits between soft software, which changes easily, and hard hardware, which does not change  b) In the device's ROM, inside the device itself  c) The controlling programs inside devices such as a washing machine, a router or a printer  d) It checks the hardware and hands over to the operating system
**Answer: b) In the device's ROM, inside the device itself**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why is it called firm?**
a) The controlling programs inside devices such as a washing machine, a router or a printer  b) It checks the hardware and hands over to the operating system  c) It sits between soft software, which changes easily, and hard hardware, which does not change  d) At moments other software cannot — starting and controlling the device
**Answer: c) It sits between soft software, which changes easily, and hard hardware, which does not change**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Which programs are firmware besides the computer's start-up?**
a) It checks the hardware and hands over to the operating system  b) At moments other software cannot — starting and controlling the device  c) Rarely — it is rarely seen and rarely changed  d) The controlling programs inside devices such as a washing machine, a router or a printer
**Answer: d) The controlling programs inside devices such as a washing machine, a router or a printer**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does a computer's firmware do at start-up?**
a) It checks the hardware and hands over to the operating system  b) At moments other software cannot — starting and controlling the device  c) Rarely — it is rarely seen and rarely changed  d) Carefully
**Answer: a) It checks the hardware and hands over to the operating system**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) When does firmware work?**
a) Rarely — it is rarely seen and rarely changed  b) At moments other software cannot — starting and controlling the device  c) Carefully  d) A device that may not start at all
**Answer: b) At moments other software cannot — starting and controlling the device**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How often is firmware changed?**
a) Carefully  b) A device that may not start at all  c) Rarely — it is rarely seen and rarely changed  d) For the device's whole life
**Answer: c) Rarely — it is rarely seen and rarely changed**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How must a firmware update be done?**
a) A device that may not start at all  b) For the device's whole life  c) Software stored permanently inside a device, telling its hardware how to start and do its basic work  d) Carefully
**Answer: d) Carefully**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What can a damaged firmware cause?**
a) A device that may not start at all  b) For the device's whole life  c) Software stored permanently inside a device, telling its hardware how to start and do its basic work  d) In the device's ROM, inside the device itself
**Answer: a) A device that may not start at all**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How long does a device's firmware serve?**
a) Software stored permanently inside a device, telling its hardware how to start and do its basic work  b) For the device's whole life  c) In the device's ROM, inside the device itself  d) It sits between soft software, which changes easily, and hard hardware, which does not change
**Answer: b) For the device's whole life**
