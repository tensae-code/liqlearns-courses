# Changing the Desktop Background, Sounds and Volume — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is the desktop background?**
a) The picture behind the icons  b) The little sounds Windows makes at events  c) They can be silenced  d) A bundle — background, sounds and colours chosen together
**Answer: a) The picture behind the icons**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How is the background changed?**
a) Starting the computer; an error (or a notification)  b) By choosing a picture in the settings  c) How loud all sound is  d) As one
**Answer: b) By choosing a picture in the settings**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What picture of your own can become the background?**
a) They can be silenced  b) A bundle — background, sounds and colours chosen together  c) A drawing you made in Paint, saved as a file  d) Your own — your Paint drawing
**Answer: c) A drawing you made in Paint, saved as a file**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What are system sounds?**
a) How loud all sound is  b) As one  c) The picture behind the icons  d) The little sounds Windows makes at events
**Answer: d) The little sounds Windows makes at events**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Name two events with system sounds.**
a) Starting the computer; an error (or a notification)  b) A bundle — background, sounds and colours chosen together  c) Your own — your Paint drawing  d) By choosing a picture in the settings
**Answer: a) Starting the computer; an error (or a notification)**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What else can be done to system sounds besides changing them?**
a) As one  b) They can be silenced  c) The picture behind the icons  d) A drawing you made in Paint, saved as a file
**Answer: b) They can be silenced**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does the volume control set?**
a) Your own — your Paint drawing  b) By choosing a picture in the settings  c) How loud all sound is  d) The little sounds Windows makes at events
**Answer: c) How loud all sound is**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is a theme?**
a) The picture behind the icons  b) A drawing you made in Paint, saved as a file  c) Starting the computer; an error (or a notification)  d) A bundle — background, sounds and colours chosen together
**Answer: d) A bundle — background, sounds and colours chosen together**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How is a theme applied?**
a) As one  b) By choosing a picture in the settings  c) The little sounds Windows makes at events  d) They can be silenced
**Answer: a) As one**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Whose work can stand behind every program you open?**
a) A drawing you made in Paint, saved as a file  b) Your own — your Paint drawing  c) Starting the computer; an error (or a notification)  d) How loud all sound is
**Answer: b) Your own — your Paint drawing**
