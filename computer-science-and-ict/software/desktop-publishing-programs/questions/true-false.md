# Desktop Publishing Programs — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is desktop publishing?" is: Producing designed pages on a computer — text, pictures, headlines and columns placed exactly**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does a DTP program work with?" is: By the designer, to the millimetre**
**Answer: False** — Frames and pages — each block of text or picture in its own positioned frame

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "How exactly are blocks placed?" is: By the designer, to the millimetre**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Name Fiji's DTP programs." is: Publishing houses — the makers of newspapers, magazines and books**
**Answer: False** — FrameMaker, PageMaker, Ventura Publisher and QuarkXPress

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Whose tools are they?" is: Publishing houses — the makers of newspapers, magazines and books**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "When is the word processor the right tool?" is: When the page is looked at as a whole and its look is part of its message**
**Answer: False** — When the document is read in order — a letter, an essay, a report

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "When is DTP the right tool?" is: When the page is looked at as a whole and its look is part of its message**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Give two DTP jobs in a school." is: Yes — letters in the word processor, the magazine in DTP**
**Answer: False** — A poster and the school magazine (or newsletter)

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Can one office need both programs?" is: Yes — letters in the word processor, the magazine in DTP**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is Fiji's deciding question?" is: Producing designed pages on a computer — text, pictures, headlines and columns placed exactly**
**Answer: False** — Is the page read in order, or looked at as a whole?
