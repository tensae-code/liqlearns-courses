# Desktop Publishing Programs — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is desktop publishing?**
**Answer: Producing designed pages on a computer — text, pictures, headlines and columns placed exactly**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does a DTP program work with?**
**Answer: Frames and pages — each block of text or picture in its own positioned frame**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How exactly are blocks placed?**
**Answer: By the designer, to the millimetre**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Name Fiji's DTP programs.**
**Answer: FrameMaker, PageMaker, Ventura Publisher and QuarkXPress**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Whose tools are they?**
**Answer: Publishing houses — the makers of newspapers, magazines and books**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) When is the word processor the right tool?**
**Answer: When the document is read in order — a letter, an essay, a report**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) When is DTP the right tool?**
**Answer: When the page is looked at as a whole and its look is part of its message**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Give two DTP jobs in a school.**
**Answer: A poster and the school magazine (or newsletter)**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Can one office need both programs?**
**Answer: Yes — letters in the word processor, the magazine in DTP**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is Fiji's deciding question?**
**Answer: Is the page read in order, or looked at as a whole?**
