# Desktop Publishing Programs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is desktop publishing?**
a) Producing designed pages on a computer — text, pictures, headlines and columns placed exactly  b) Frames and pages — each block of text or picture in its own positioned frame  c) By the designer, to the millimetre  d) FrameMaker, PageMaker, Ventura Publisher and QuarkXPress
**Answer: a) Producing designed pages on a computer — text, pictures, headlines and columns placed exactly**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does a DTP program work with?**
a) By the designer, to the millimetre  b) Frames and pages — each block of text or picture in its own positioned frame  c) FrameMaker, PageMaker, Ventura Publisher and QuarkXPress  d) Publishing houses — the makers of newspapers, magazines and books
**Answer: b) Frames and pages — each block of text or picture in its own positioned frame**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How exactly are blocks placed?**
a) FrameMaker, PageMaker, Ventura Publisher and QuarkXPress  b) Publishing houses — the makers of newspapers, magazines and books  c) By the designer, to the millimetre  d) When the document is read in order — a letter, an essay, a report
**Answer: c) By the designer, to the millimetre**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Name Fiji's DTP programs.**
a) Publishing houses — the makers of newspapers, magazines and books  b) When the document is read in order — a letter, an essay, a report  c) When the page is looked at as a whole and its look is part of its message  d) FrameMaker, PageMaker, Ventura Publisher and QuarkXPress
