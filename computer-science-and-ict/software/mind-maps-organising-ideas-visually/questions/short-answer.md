# Mind Maps — Organising Ideas Visually — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Where does the main topic sit?**
**Answer: In the centre of the map**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What grows from the centre?**
**Answer: Branches — the main ideas**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What hangs from the branches?**
**Answer: Twigs — the details**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What rides each branch?**
**Answer: One or two keywords — not sentences**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which software does Grade 6 name?**
**Answer: MindMaple Lite**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What are the map's three uses?**
**Answer: Planning, learning, and presenting together**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Why is a map easier to remember than a page of lines?**
**Answer: The whole structure is held in one picture**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does Grade 9 use mind maps for?**
**Answer: Presenting information in exchange and collaboration**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does the shared map give a group?**
**Answer: One picture everyone can point at, question and improve**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is cheaper than rewriting notes?**
**Answer: Reordering a mind map's branches**
