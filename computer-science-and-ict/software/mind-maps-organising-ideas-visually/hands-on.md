# Hands-on — Mind Maps — Organising Ideas Visually

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Map a lesson** — Take the last lesson you learned in any subject. Draw its mind map: centre, at least four branches, keywords only.

## Task two
**Plan with a map** — Plan a real project — a class event — as a mind map in software. Then list what the map shows that a written list would hide.
