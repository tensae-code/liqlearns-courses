**Acceptance count: taught in 5 countries (Ethiopia, Tanzania, Eritrea, Cambodia, Afghanistan).**

Afghanistan's own way (Grade 11): grade 11 is the health-and-PowerPoint year — a full chapter on computer health and strain-relief exercises, then Word finished off (Format, Insert, Edit) before a five-lesson PowerPoint block and a first step onto the Web.

Afghanistan's own way (Grade 12): the final year turns outward — computers in society (e-commerce, communications, even military systems), system software and virus protection, then Excel for the first time and a closing block on the Web, e-mail and browsers (taught through Internet Explorer).

The teacher guide's Unit 3 review key contains two items flagged unreliable in the placements and NOT used here: a discussion key that answers a screen-elements question with a list of Word capabilities, and a create-a-document key naming Microsoft Word 2010 where the student book teaches Word 2016.

The Grade 8 teacher guide's Unit 3 answer key (printed pp. 32–33) was used as a verified-answer check for the Grade 8 questions added in Sub lesson 3 (teacher guide — evidence).
