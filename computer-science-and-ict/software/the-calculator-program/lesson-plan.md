# Lesson plan — The Calculator Program

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Software · **Lesson:** The Calculator Program
**Place in the curriculum:** ICT expansion — Azerbaijan (2026-11-14) — in the title Software on the ICT course ladder (TITLES.md). Built new from Azerbaijan's *İnformatika 2, Grade 2*, because no existing lesson teaches this thing as its own lesson at this level.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The Calculator is a program for calculating: type the numbers and the sign, press equals, and the program works out the answer — adding, subtracting, multiplying and dividing.
**This lesson's own level:** Grade 2 simple — it stands on the child's own everyday world and on the lessons named below; every new word is built inside the lesson from things the child can do at once.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: The Desktop and a Program Window in Software — Calculator opens in a window on the desktop. Recommended, never forced — a learner may land on this lesson directly, and it stands alone.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what the Calculator program is for, and name the signs +, −, ×, ÷ and =.
2. Work out a short calculation in Calculator: number, sign, number, =.
3. Clear a finished calculation before starting a new one, and check whether an answer makes sense.

## What you need
A computer with the Calculator program.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — What is 7 + 5? Work it out in your head — then let the computer do it: open Calculator, press 7, +, 5, =. The answer, 12, appears. This lesson teaches calculating in the Calculator program. (lesson/lesson.md).

## If the learner is stuck
Answer mixes old numbers → clear first, every time; make clearing the opening habit. Presses signs in the wrong order → say the calculation aloud in full before touching the buttons.
