# Hands-on — The Calculator Program

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Calculate and check
Work out 8 + 4, 9 − 3 and 3 × 2 in Calculator. First guess each answer in your head, then check it against the screen.

## Shop sums
Your teacher gives three small shopping sums with prices. Calculate each total in Calculator and say whether each answer makes sense.
