# Video script — Utilities and language translators

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Some programs keep the computer healthy.
Backup keeps a safe copy.
Recovery brings back what was lost.
Virus protection stands guard.

Other programs translate.
They change a program from one language to another.
So the machine can follow it.
