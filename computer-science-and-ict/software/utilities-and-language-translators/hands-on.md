# Hands-on — Utilities and language translators

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Backup drill
Copy an important piece of your own school work onto a second paper, and keep the copy in a different place from the original. You have just done by hand what backup software does.

## Translate a sentence
Take one instruction — ' bring me water' — and say it in two languages you know. The meaning stayed the same; the language changed. That is what a language translator does for programs.
