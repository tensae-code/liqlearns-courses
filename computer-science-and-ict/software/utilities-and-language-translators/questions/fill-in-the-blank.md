# Utilities and language translators — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** ______ software keeps a safe copy of your work.
**Answer: Backup**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A ______ converts programs from one computer language to another.
**Answer: language translator**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** ______ protection guards the computer against viruses.
**Answer: Virus**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Data recovery, data compression and data conversion are ______ management utilities.
**Answer: file**

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** A disk ______ editor divides a disk into separately managed regions called partitions.
**Answer: partition**
