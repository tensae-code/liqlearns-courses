# Hands-on — Types of Operating Systems

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Sort the systems** — List six operating systems you know or can find. Sort each by job-handling, by users and by home — some will sit in two sorts at once; say why.

## Task two
**Deadline hunt** — Name three machines near you that need real-time answers and three that do not. For each real-time one, say what 'late' would mean.
