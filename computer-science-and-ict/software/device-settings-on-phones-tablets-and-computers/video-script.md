# Video script — Device Settings on Phones Tablets and Computers

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This part of South Sudan's book teaches **Device settings** — covering: Device settings; Device setting in phones and tablets. South Sudan's Secondary ICT book teaches this as its own named step, in the book's own order, as a practical skill to be shown — not only named. The learner follows the book's own steps for this part, in order, and practises until the steps can be done without the book open. Here the steps are the book's own: Device settings; Device setting in phones and tablets. Because the Secondary ICT series builds skill on skill: this part is one of the steps its year is made of, and later work in the book stands on it — device settings is part of that chain..
