# Video script — Application software — programs for particular jobs

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Application software does your job.
You open it. You work in it. You close it.

Writing? Word processing.
Numbers in tables? Spreadsheets.
Records? Database. Drawing? Graphics. Slides? Presentation.

Five kinds. Five kinds of job.

More programs, more activities.
Edit a photo. Keep the accounts. Browse the web.
Play the music. Fly the simulator. Play the game.

And look closer at the street.
The traffic light runs on software.
The cash machine runs on software.
Software is inside the machines too.

One program alone: Notepad.
Programs together on one task: a package.
A whole family under one roof, one face: a suite.
Microsoft Office is a suite.
LibreOffice does the same work — free.
