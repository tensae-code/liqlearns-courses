# Hands-on — Adjusting Sound and Multimedia Settings

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Silence detective
On a real computer (with a teacher's leave), make it silent without unplugging anything, in one of the lesson's ways — volume, mute or device. A partner must restore the sound using the lesson's order: plug, power, mute, volume, device — and name the fault they found.

## Balance the sources
Open the Volume Control and read every slider aloud with its job. Play a sound and adjust the levels so the main sound is clear and nothing drowns it. Write down the settings you chose and why.
