# Adjusting Sound and Multimedia Settings — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What are sound's two directions on a computer?**
**Answer: Output — what you hear; input — what a microphone records**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Where do most sound troubles live?**
**Answer: In the settings — volume down, muted, wrong device, wrong socket — not in broken hardware**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why does the book teach this in the operating-system chapter?**
**Answer: Because adjusting media settings is a system skill — running the machine, not only using its programs**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What sits between output and input?**
**Answer: The volume control**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Where is the Volume Control opened from?**
**Answer: The taskbar's speaker icon or the Control Panel**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What do its sliders set?**
**Answer: The master volume and the level of each source**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is the first slider to raise?**
**Answer: The master volume — after checking Mute is off**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What device choices do the fuller settings hold?**
**Answer: The playback and recording devices**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the troubleshooting order?**
**Answer: Plug, power, mute, volume, device**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Most silent computers — what happens by the end of that order?**
**Answer: Most speak before you reach the end**
