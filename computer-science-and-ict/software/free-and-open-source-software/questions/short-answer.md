# Free and Open-Source Software — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What freedoms does free software grant?**
**Answer: To run, study, share and improve the software**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Is 'free' about the price?**
**Answer: No — about the rights**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is the GPL?**
**Answer: The General Public License — granting the freedoms and requiring shared versions to keep them**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does open-source name the world by?**
**Answer: Its visible side — the source code standing open**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Name four great free programs from the lesson.**
**Answer: Linux, OpenOffice, GIMP, Inkscape (any four of: Blender)**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is Soyombo Linux?**
**Answer: Mongolia's own Linux distribution, named for the Soyombo symbol**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Why was Soyombo made?**
**Answer: So the free system speaks Mongolia's language and serves Mongolia's users**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Which office suite has a Mongolian edition in the book?**
**Answer: OpenOffice — Mongolian OpenOffice**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What cannot be done downstream under the GPL?**
**Answer: The freedoms cannot be quietly taken away**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the lesson's closing claim?**
**Answer: Open source is not only received by Mongolia; Mongolia builds in it**
