# System Administration Tasks — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is system administration?**
**Answer: Looking after a computer system so it works for its users**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does diagnosing mean?**
**Answer: Finding what is wrong**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is the first administration step with a fault?**
**Answer: Diagnose before changing anything**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Who performs system administration tasks?**
**Answer: The system administrator**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why keep notes of changes to a system?**
**Answer: So the system can be understood and restored**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name one basic administration task.**
**Answer: Managing user accounts**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How do administrators protect a system?**
**Answer: By keeping settings and access under control**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What should an administrator do after a fix?**
**Answer: Test that the system works**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does regular maintenance prevent?**
**Answer: Faults and breakdowns**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How does documentation help the next administrator?**
**Answer: It shows what was done and why**
