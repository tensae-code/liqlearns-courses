# Menus — Main and Sub-Menus — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a menu?**
**Answer: A list of commands the program offers you to pick from**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Where does a main menu open from?**
**Answer: The menu bar at the top of a window, or the Start button**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What are File, Edit and View examples of?**
**Answer: Names on the menu bar that open main menus**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What happens when you click a command in a menu?**
**Answer: The command is carried out and the menu closes**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How do you close a menu without choosing?**
**Answer: Press Escape, or click somewhere else**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What marks an item that holds a sub-menu?**
**Answer: A small arrow at its right edge**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How do you open a sub-menu?**
**Answer: Point at the item with the arrow, without clicking**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Where does a sub-menu open?**
**Answer: Out sideways, beside the main-menu item**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How deep can a command be?**
**Answer: Two steps — main menu, then sub-menu, then click**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What happens if you slide off the sub-menu path?**
**Answer: The sub-menu closes and you point at the item again**
