# Menus — Main and Sub-Menus — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a menu?**
a) A list of commands the program offers you to pick from  b) The menu bar at the top of a window, or the Start button  c) Names on the menu bar that open main menus  d) The command is carried out and the menu closes
**Answer: a) A list of commands the program offers you to pick from**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Where does a main menu open from?**
a) Names on the menu bar that open main menus  b) The menu bar at the top of a window, or the Start button  c) The command is carried out and the menu closes  d) Press Escape, or click somewhere else
**Answer: b) The menu bar at the top of a window, or the Start button**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What are File, Edit and View examples of?**
a) The command is carried out and the menu closes  b) Press Escape, or click somewhere else  c) Names on the menu bar that open main menus  d) A small arrow at its right edge
**Answer: c) Names on the menu bar that open main menus**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What happens when you click a command in a menu?**
a) Press Escape, or click somewhere else  b) A small arrow at its right edge  c) Point at the item with the arrow, without clicking  d) The command is carried out and the menu closes
**Answer: d) The command is carried out and the menu closes**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How do you close a menu without choosing?**
a) Press Escape, or click somewhere else  b) A small arrow at its right edge  c) Point at the item with the arrow, without clicking  d) Out sideways, beside the main-menu item
**Answer: a) Press Escape, or click somewhere else**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What marks an item that holds a sub-menu?**
a) Point at the item with the arrow, without clicking  b) A small arrow at its right edge  c) Out sideways, beside the main-menu item  d) Two steps — main menu, then sub-menu, then click
**Answer: b) A small arrow at its right edge**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How do you open a sub-menu?**
a) Out sideways, beside the main-menu item  b) Two steps — main menu, then sub-menu, then click  c) Point at the item with the arrow, without clicking  d) The sub-menu closes and you point at the item again
**Answer: c) Point at the item with the arrow, without clicking**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Where does a sub-menu open?**
a) Two steps — main menu, then sub-menu, then click  b) The sub-menu closes and you point at the item again  c) A list of commands the program offers you to pick from  d) Out sideways, beside the main-menu item
**Answer: d) Out sideways, beside the main-menu item**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How deep can a command be?**
a) Two steps — main menu, then sub-menu, then click  b) The sub-menu closes and you point at the item again  c) A list of commands the program offers you to pick from  d) The menu bar at the top of a window, or the Start button
**Answer: a) Two steps — main menu, then sub-menu, then click**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What happens if you slide off the sub-menu path?**
a) A list of commands the program offers you to pick from  b) The sub-menu closes and you point at the item again  c) The menu bar at the top of a window, or the Start button  d) Names on the menu bar that open main menus
**Answer: b) The sub-menu closes and you point at the item again**
