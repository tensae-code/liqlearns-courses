# Hypotheses in Data Analysis — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a hypothesis?**
**Answer: A clear, testable statement made before the test**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What two qualities must it have?**
**Answer: Falsifiable and specific**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What three things does specific name?**
**Answer: Population, measure and direction**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is the null hypothesis?**
**Answer: 'No difference' — the sceptical default**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is a claim no result could refute?**
**Answer: Not a hypothesis — a decoration**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What question does testing ask?**
**Answer: Could this difference be chance?**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What strengthens evidence against the null?**
**Answer: The smaller the chance-explanation**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What are the honest verdicts?**
**Answer: The data supports, or fails to support**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why never 'proves'?**
**Answer: Samples vary; one test is one witness**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What shape does the unit close with?**
**Answer: Question, hypothesis, test, honest verdict**
