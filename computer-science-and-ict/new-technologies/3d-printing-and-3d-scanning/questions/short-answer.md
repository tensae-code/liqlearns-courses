# 3D Printing and 3D Scanning — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) How does 3D printing build?**
**Answer: Layer by layer, from a digital 3D model**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does slicing do?**
**Answer: Cuts the model into thin layers for the printer**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name a virtue.**
**Answer: Shapes no mould could make (or one-off parts on demand, or prototypes in hours)**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is it paid for in?**
**Answer: Printing time — and the model's quality deciding the object's**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which chapter teaches it?**
**Answer: Greece Grade 11 EPAL Basika Themata, Chapter 9**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does 3D scanning do?**
**Answer: Measures a real object's surface and builds its digital model**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Name its two measuring ways.**
**Answer: Sweeping light/lasers; photographing from many angles**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) State the closed loop.**
**Answer: Scan the broken part, repair the model, print the replacement**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which surfaces read badly?**
**Answer: Shiny, dark and hollow places**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What must be checked before trusting the loop?**
**Answer: The model's honesty against the thing itself**
