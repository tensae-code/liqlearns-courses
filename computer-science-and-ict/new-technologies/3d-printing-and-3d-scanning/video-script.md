# Video script — 3D Printing and 3D Scanning

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**3D printing** builds a physical object **layer by layer** from a **digital 3D model**: the model is **sliced** into thin layers by the preparing program, and the printer lays material — melted filament, in the common school printer — **one layer on the last**, until the object stands whole. Its virtues are the chapter's list: shapes no mould could make, one-off and repaired parts made on demand, prototypes in hours — paid for in printing time and in the model's quality deciding the object's.
**3D scanning** runs the road backwards: the scanner **measures a real object's surface** — sweeping light or lasers over it, or photographing it from many angles — and builds the **digital model** of its shape, which can then be **studied, corrected, shared — and printed**, closing the chapter's loop (scan the broken part, repair the model, print the replacement). Scanning's care is its surfaces: shiny, dark and hollow places read badly, and the model's honesty must be checked against the thing itself before the loop is trusted.
