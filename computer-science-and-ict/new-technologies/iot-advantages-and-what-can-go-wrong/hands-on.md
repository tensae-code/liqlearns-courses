# Hands-on — IoT advantages — and what can go wrong

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Weigh your own house
Draw two columns for your home: gains IoT would bring, warnings it would bring. Fill each side honestly — at least three entries a side — then circle the single entry that decides it for your family, and write why.

## Failure rehearsal
Imagine your home fully connected, then the power cuts for one evening. List everything that stops, in the order it would trouble you. Beside each, write the old non-connected way of doing it. That list is the dependence limitation, measured.
