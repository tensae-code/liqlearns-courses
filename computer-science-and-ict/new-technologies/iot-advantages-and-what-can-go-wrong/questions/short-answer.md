# IoT advantages — and what can go wrong — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the five advantages of IoT.
**Answer:** Efficiency; technical optimisation; convenience and improved customer experience; conservation; personalisation.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the four limitations of IoT.
**Answer:** Security and privacy; connectivity and power dependence; complexity and integration; higher cost.
