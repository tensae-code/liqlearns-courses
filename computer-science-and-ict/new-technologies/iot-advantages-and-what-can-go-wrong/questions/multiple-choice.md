# IoT advantages — and what can go wrong — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Efficiency in IoT means —
a) trustworthy real-time data turned into useful information, saving resources  b) buying more devices  c) using more power  d) working slower
**Answer: a) trustworthy real-time data turned into useful information, saving resources**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Controlling TV volume, thermostats and lights from one smartphone is —
a) conservation  b) technical optimisation  c) personalisation  d) a limitation
**Answer: b) technical optimisation**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Monitoring traffic, water and electricity use and air quality serves —
a) personalisation  b) conservation and smart cities  c) advertising  d) gaming
**Answer: b) conservation and smart cities**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Devices learning your likes and dislikes is —
a) efficiency  b) convenience  c) personalisation  d) security
**Answer: c) personalisation**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** Siri, Alexa, Google Home and Cortana are examples of —
a) voice assistants  b) sensors  c) protocols  d) limitations
**Answer: a) voice assistants**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The security and privacy limitation is sharpest in —
a) healthcare and banking  b) games only  c) weather watching  d) music playing
**Answer: a) healthcare and banking**

**7. (Sub lesson 2)** When connectivity or power fails —
a) nothing changes  b) the device and everything connected to it fails  c) devices get faster  d) only the bill fails
**Answer: b) the device and everything connected to it fails**

**8. (Sub lesson 2)** Complexity and integration include —
a) unsettled protocols and incompatible makers' hardware  b) too many colours  c) heavy boxes  d) long manuals only
**Answer: a) unsettled protocols and incompatible makers' hardware**
