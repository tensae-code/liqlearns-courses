# Web 2.0 and Web 3.0 — Evolution of the Web — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What could the first web's readers not do?**
a) Publish — it was read-only  b) An author  c) Social networks and blogs (or video platforms, wikis)  d) In the crowd of users creating inside it
**Answer: a) Publish — it was read-only**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What did Web 2.0 turn the reader into?**
a) Social networks and blogs (or video platforms, wikis)  b) An author  c) In the crowd of users creating inside it  d) Participation
**Answer: b) An author**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name two kinds of Web 2.0 places.**
a) In the crowd of users creating inside it  b) Participation  c) Social networks and blogs (or video platforms, wikis)  d) Meaning — it is the semantic web
**Answer: c) Social networks and blogs (or video platforms, wikis)**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Where does a Web 2.0 place's value live?**
a) Participation  b) Meaning — it is the semantic web  c) Understand what data says and work with it  d) In the crowd of users creating inside it
**Answer: d) In the crowd of users creating inside it**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is Web 2.0's one-word mark?**
a) Participation  b) Meaning — it is the semantic web  c) Understand what data says and work with it  d) A list of links, with one joined answer
**Answer: a) Participation**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does Web 3.0 link by?**
a) Understand what data says and work with it  b) Meaning — it is the semantic web  c) A list of links, with one joined answer  d) People — Web 3.0 connects meanings
**Answer: b) Meaning — it is the semantic web**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What can programs do in Web 3.0?**
a) A list of links, with one joined answer  b) People — Web 3.0 connects meanings  c) Understand what data says and work with it  d) In the Grade 12 Internet chapter, beside the social networks
**Answer: c) Understand what data says and work with it**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does a Web 3.0 answer replace?**
a) People — Web 3.0 connects meanings  b) In the Grade 12 Internet chapter, beside the social networks  c) Publish — it was read-only  d) A list of links, with one joined answer
**Answer: d) A list of links, with one joined answer**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What did Web 2.0 connect, that Web 3.0 goes beyond?**
a) People — Web 3.0 connects meanings  b) In the Grade 12 Internet chapter, beside the social networks  c) Publish — it was read-only  d) An author
**Answer: a) People — Web 3.0 connects meanings**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Where does Cuba teach the web's generations?**
a) Publish — it was read-only  b) In the Grade 12 Internet chapter, beside the social networks  c) An author  d) Social networks and blogs (or video platforms, wikis)
