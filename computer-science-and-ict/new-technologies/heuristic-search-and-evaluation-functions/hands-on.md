# Hands-on — Heuristic Search and Evaluation Functions

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Build a heuristic** — For a maze problem, invent an h(n), test its admissibility on three nodes, and repair it if it lies.

## Task two
**Walk by f** — On a small weighted tree, expand three nodes by f = g + h, showing every score.
