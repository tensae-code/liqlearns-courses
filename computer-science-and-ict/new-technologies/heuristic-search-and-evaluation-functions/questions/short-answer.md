# Heuristic Search and Evaluation Functions — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does h(n) estimate?**
**Answer: The cost from node n to the goal**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Give the route heuristic.**
**Answer: Straight-line distance, whatever the roads do**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Give the puzzle heuristic.**
**Answer: Tiles out of place**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does admissible mean?**
**Answer: Never overestimating the true cost**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How does heuristic search order its frontier?**
**Answer: By promise — most hopeful first**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does an evaluation function do?**
**Answer: Scores a node for expanding**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What are g(n) and h(n)?**
**Answer: The cost already spent, and the heuristic estimate ahead**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is f(n) in A*?**
**Answer: g(n) + h(n)**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does A* balance?**
**Answer: How far I've come against how far I seem to go**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What can a lying heuristic do?**
**Answer: Walk the search confidently away from the goal**
