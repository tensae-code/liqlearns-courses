# Hands-on — Biotechnology and Information Technology

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Old and new', 'Take one product (airag, bread, a vaccine). Write its ancient-biotech story and what the modern, gene-level version of the same craft changes.')

## Task two
('Find the data', 'Choose one bioinformatics job (comparing two gene sequences). Write what data it holds, what question it answers, and why no unaided human could do it at scale.')
