# Biotechnology and Information Technology — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is biotechnology?" is: Using living things and their processes to make products**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Name its ancient form and a Mongolian product." is: Reads and edits the instructions of life itself — genes**
**Answer: False** — Fermentation — airag (any one of: bread, yoghurt)

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does genetic engineering do?" is: Reads and edits the instructions of life itself — genes**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "How does modern biotech differ from the ancient?" is: Medicine, food, agriculture and environment**
**Answer: False** — It works by design at the gene level, not by patience

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Name the four fields it serves." is: Medicine, food, agriculture and environment**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is bioinformatics?" is: Its data grew past any human reading**
**Answer: False** — Biology's information — gene sequences — stored, searched and compared by computers

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Why did biology become an information science?" is: Its data grew past any human reading**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Since when has Mongolia followed a biotech policy?" is: Its wealth is substantially alive — a livestock-and-steppe country**
**Answer: False** — 1986

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Why is biotech a national-household sector for Mongolia?" is: Its wealth is substantially alive — a livestock-and-steppe country**
**Answer: True**
