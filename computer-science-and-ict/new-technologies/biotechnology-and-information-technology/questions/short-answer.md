# Biotechnology and Information Technology — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is biotechnology?**
**Answer: Using living things and their processes to make products**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name its ancient form and a Mongolian product.**
**Answer: Fermentation — airag (any one of: bread, yoghurt)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does genetic engineering do?**
**Answer: Reads and edits the instructions of life itself — genes**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How does modern biotech differ from the ancient?**
**Answer: It works by design at the gene level, not by patience**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Name the four fields it serves.**
**Answer: Medicine, food, agriculture and environment**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is bioinformatics?**
**Answer: Biology's information — gene sequences — stored, searched and compared by computers**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Why did biology become an information science?**
**Answer: Its data grew past any human reading**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Since when has Mongolia followed a biotech policy?**
**Answer: 1986**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why is biotech a national-household sector for Mongolia?**
**Answer: Its wealth is substantially alive — a livestock-and-steppe country**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Where is biotech's information half taught?**
**Answer: In Mongolia's Grade 12 IT book, in the new-technologies strand**
