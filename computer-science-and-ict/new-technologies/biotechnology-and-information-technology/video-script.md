# Video script — Biotechnology and Information Technology

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Biotechnology** uses living things and their processes to make products, and it is as old as its first product: **ancient fermentation** — bread, yoghurt, and Mongolia's own airag — was biotechnology before the word. The modern turn works at the level of **genes**: **genetic engineering** reads and edits the instructions of life itself, breeding and curing by design rather than by patience. Between the churn and the gene editor stands one continuous craft: persuading life to work for people, with rising precision.
The craft serves named **fields**: **medicine** (vaccines, insulin, diagnostics), **food** (fermentation and improved strains), **agriculture** (hardier crops and livestock) and the **environment** (living clean-up of wastes). Its meeting with this course is **bioinformatics**: life's information — gene sequences grown past any human reading — is stored, searched and compared by computers; biology's data became an information science. Mongolia's stake is dated: a national **biotech policy followed since 1986**, in a livestock-and-steppe country whose wealth is substantially alive — the lesson's close is that for Mongolia, biotechnology is not an imported fashion but a sector of the national household, and its information half is taught in this book because its other half grazes outside.
