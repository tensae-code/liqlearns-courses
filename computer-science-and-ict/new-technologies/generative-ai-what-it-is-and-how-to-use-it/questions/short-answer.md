# Generative AI — What It Is and How to Use It — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does generative AI produce?**
**Answer: New content — text, images, code**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How is it trained?**
**Answer: On very large collections of human-made material, learning their patterns**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does the user give it?**
**Answer: A prompt — and it continues in the learned pattern**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Is generative output guaranteed true?**
**Answer: No — the system aims at what fits the pattern, not at what it has checked**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How is its output described in one phrase?**
**Answer: New in arrangement, learned in pattern**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is the first working rule for using it?**
**Answer: Prompt clearly — say what you want, for whom, in what form**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What must be done with its facts and code?**
**Answer: Checked — facts verified, code run**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does 'own the result' mean?**
**Answer: What you submit with AI help is your work, to explain and defend**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why does Egypt introduce generative AI before its project lessons?**
**Answer: So the web, quiz-app and game projects that follow are built with open eyes**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What happens to work its maker never examined?**
**Answer: It is work its maker cannot stand behind**
