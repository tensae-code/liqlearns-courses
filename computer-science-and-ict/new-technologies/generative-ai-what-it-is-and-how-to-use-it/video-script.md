# Video script — Generative AI — What It Is and How to Use It

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Generative AI** is artificial intelligence that **produces new content** — text, images, code — rather than only classifying or predicting. It is trained on very large collections of human-made material and learns the patterns of how that material is built, so that, given a **prompt**, it can continue in the pattern: the next sentence, the plausible picture, the working function. What it produces is **new in arrangement, learned in pattern** — fluent, fast, and not guaranteed true, because the system aims at what fits the pattern, not at what it has checked. Egypt introduces it at the head of its project chapter precisely so the projects that follow are built with open eyes.
The book's working rules are a craftsman's, not a fan's. **Prompt clearly**: say what you want, for whom, in what form — vague prompts earn vague answers. **Check everything**: code must run, facts must verify, because the system's confidence is part of its pattern, not evidence. **Own the result**: what you submit with AI help is your work, to explain and defend — the Egypt course makes pupils present and review their AI-assisted projects for exactly this reason. Used this way, generative AI is a power tool in the workshop; used the other way — unexamined, unowned — it produces work its maker cannot stand behind.
