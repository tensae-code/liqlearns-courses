# Bioinformatics — Computers and Biological Data — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is bioinformatics?**
**Answer: Computing applied to biological data**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What are its chief data?**
**Answer: DNA and protein sequences**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What four letters write a gene?**
**Answer: A, C, G and T**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Name two things sequences can be, once stored.**
**Answer: Searched and aligned (also counted)**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does aligning measure?**
**Answer: The likeness of two sequences**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How are disease genes located?**
**Answer: Comparing the sequences of the sick and the well**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What can be designed against a protein's shape?**
**Answer: Medicines**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Name the two Bangladeshi examples.**
**Answer: Improved rice varieties and the jute genome**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How is species kinship measured now?**
**Answer: Exactly — letter by letter**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why could this field not exist before computers?**
**Answer: Its material could not be handled without them**
