# Hands-on — Bioinformatics — Computers and Biological Data

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Align by hand** — Take two short letter-strings (12 letters, two differences). Align them, mark the differences, and say what a bioinformatician would conclude.

## Task two
**Search the pattern** — Given a page of random ACGT letters, find every occurrence of one 4-letter pattern and count them — then write how a program would do it faster.
