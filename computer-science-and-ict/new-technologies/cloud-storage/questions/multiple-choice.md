# Cloud Storage — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is cloud storage?**
a) Files kept on a provider's servers, reached over the internet  b) Redundancy — copies spread across machines and buildings  c) As a settings change — rented, stretching space  d) Phone, school computer and home alike
**Answer: a**
