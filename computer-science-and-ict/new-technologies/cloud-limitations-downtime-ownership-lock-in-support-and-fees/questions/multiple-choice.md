# Cloud limitations — downtime, ownership, lock-in, support and fees — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** If the internet connection goes down, the cloud service —
a) keeps working fully  b) goes down with it  c) gets faster  d) moves to paper
**Answer: b) goes down with it** — Downtime rides on the connection.

**2.** Data ownership asks whether uploaded data —
a) becomes the provider's property  b) is printed daily  c) is free  d) is deleted hourly
**Answer: a) becomes the provider's property**

**3.** Inflexibility means the provider may —
a) force its own applications and formats  b) give you every tool free  c) let you run the data centre  d) never change anything
**Answer: a) force its own applications and formats** — and ultimately controls the infrastructure.

**4.** With lack of support, users turn to —
a) internet forums  b) the police  c) printed manuals only  d) their neighbours
**Answer: a) internet forums**

**5.** The cost limitation is about —
a) servers being heavy  b) unknown fees making the service costly despite saving on-site infrastructure  c) electricity only  d) free services
**Answer: b) unknown fees making the service costly despite saving on-site infrastructure**

**6.** On security, the book says the cloud is —
a) completely safe  b) not completely safe — intruders keep trying new ways in  c) safer than any locked room in all cases  d) never attacked
**Answer: b) not completely safe — intruders keep trying new ways in**
