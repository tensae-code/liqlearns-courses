# Cloud limitations — downtime, ownership, lock-in, support and fees — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1.** Server ______: no provider guarantees no interruption.
**Answer: downtime**

**2.** Minimal to no customer ______ is the support limitation.
**Answer: assistance (support)**

**3.** The provider ultimately controls the ______ — that is the root of inflexibility.
**Answer: infrastructure**
