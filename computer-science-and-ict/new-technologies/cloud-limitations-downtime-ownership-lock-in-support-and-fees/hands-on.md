# Hands-on — Cloud limitations — downtime, ownership, lock-in, support and fees

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The midnight test
For one cloud service you use, answer honestly: when it last failed, how long was it down, who could you contact, and what did you do instead? Write the answers — that is downtime and support, measured in your own life.

## Lock-in detective
Pick one service you use. List its file formats. Could you take your files to a different provider tomorrow and still open them? Write what would move easily and what would be trapped.
