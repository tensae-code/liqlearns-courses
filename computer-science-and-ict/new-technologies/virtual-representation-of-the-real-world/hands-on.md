# Hands-on — Virtual Representation of the Real World

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Represent a classroom** — Represent your classroom as data for two different tasks: (a) finding a free seat, (b) a fire-evacuation count. List what each representation keeps and drops, and why they differ.

## Task two
**Age check** — Find one representation you use (a map app, a timetable, a game avatar). Say what it stands for, what it leaves out, and how it stays up to date — or doesn't.
