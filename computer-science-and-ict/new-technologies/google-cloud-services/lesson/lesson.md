# Google Cloud Services

## Do it first

The second cloud Jordan's pupils open is Google's — the cloud that grew out of running the world's busiest search engine. This lesson is its services and its character.

## Sub lesson 1 — What Google Cloud is

**Google Cloud** (Google Cloud Platform, GCP) rents out the infrastructure Google itself runs on: data centres, a private global network, and services for computing, storage, data and AI. Its characteristics as the book presents them: **data and AI strength** (born from search and YouTube's scale), **a fast private network** between its regions, and **openness** (built around open tools like Kubernetes, which Google created and gave away).

## Sub lesson 2 — Its services — the working set

The working set mirrors Azure's with Google's names: **Compute Engine** (rented virtual machines — IaaS), **Cloud Storage** (files in the cloud), **Cloud SQL** (managed databases), **App Engine** (hosting apps without servers — PaaS), and the data/AI shelf — **BigQuery** (analysing huge tables with questions) and Google's machine-learning services. Jordan teaches the two clouds side by side so the pupil learns the pattern, not the brand: every cloud rents the same few ideas under its own names.
