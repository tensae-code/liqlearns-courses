# Google Cloud Services — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does Google Cloud rent out?**
**Answer: The infrastructure Google itself runs on**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name its three characteristics as the book presents them.**
**Answer: Data and AI strength, a fast private network, openness**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What famous open tool did Google create and give away?**
**Answer: Kubernetes**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) From what work was Google's data strength born?**
**Answer: Search and YouTube's scale**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What joins Google's regions?**
**Answer: Its private global network**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is Compute Engine?**
**Answer: Rented virtual machines — IaaS**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is App Engine?**
**Answer: Hosting apps without managing servers — PaaS**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is BigQuery for?**
**Answer: Analysing huge tables with questions**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is Cloud SQL?**
**Answer: A managed database service**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why does Jordan teach two clouds side by side?**
**Answer: So the pupil learns the pattern, not the brand**
