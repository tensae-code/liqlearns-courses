# Video script — How an IoT system works — sense, connect, process, respond

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Four steps. Always in order.

One: **sense**. The sensor collects.

Two: **connect**. Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, cellular — the data travels.

Three: **process**. Software checks: normal — or danger?

Four: **respond**. An alert. Your action. Or the system acts itself.
