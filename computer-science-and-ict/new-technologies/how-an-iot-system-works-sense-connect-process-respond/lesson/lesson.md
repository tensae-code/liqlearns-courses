# How an IoT system works — sense, connect, process, respond

## Do it first

Follow one temperature reading on its whole journey. It is born at a sensor on a wall. It travels across a network. Software judges it: normal, or too hot? Then something happens — an alert, an adjustment, an automatic response. Four stops, always in the same order. Walk them now, then read their names.

## The four components, in order

**1. Sensors / devices collect data from the environment.** A phone is a device with many sensors — **camera, accelerometer** among them — and sensor types include **temperature, proximity, humidity, smoke, water quality, infrared and motion**.

**2. Connectivity sends the data to the cloud** — by **cellular, satellite, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth or Ethernet**, **chosen for the application**.

**3. Data processing** — software checks the data, **simply** (a temperature within limits) or **complexly** (computer vision spotting intruders on video).

**4. The user interface makes the information useful** — through **an alert by email, text or notification**. Then **the user may act** (adjusting temperature by app), or **the system may act automatically by predefined rules** — even **notifying authorities about an intruder instead of the user**.
