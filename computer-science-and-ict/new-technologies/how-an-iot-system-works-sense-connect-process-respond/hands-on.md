# Hands-on — How an IoT system works — sense, connect, process, respond

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Walk a reading
Take one reading — room temperature, a door opening, a water level. On paper, draw its full journey in four boxes: the sensor that collects it, the connection that carries it, the check the software runs, and the response at the end. Label every box with its component name.

## Design the response
For the same reading, design TWO endings: one where the user acts after an alert, and one where the system acts automatically by a rule you write down exactly ('IF … THEN …'). Say which ending you would trust for a smoke alarm, and why.
