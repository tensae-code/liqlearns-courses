# IaaS — computing resources rented in the cloud

## Do it first

Follow the renting down. SaaS rents you finished software. PaaS rents you the workshop to build in. What is left underneath both? The machines themselves — servers, storage, networks. This lesson rents those out too, without you ever touching a single physical box.

## IaaS — the computing itself, rented

**Infrastructure as a Service (IaaS)** **provides access to computing resources in a virtualised environment — 'the cloud' — over the internet**.

It is **a complete computing package**, and **one solution for small businesses cutting IT infrastructure costs**: instead of buying servers and networks, the business rents the resources and builds its systems on them.

[Picture 30116 — IaaS, rent the computing itself]

The book places it plainly: IaaS **stands alongside PaaS as a fundamental service model**.

Examples: **Amazon Web Services, Cisco Metapod, Microsoft Azure and Google Compute Engine**.
