# Lesson plan — IaaS — computing resources rented in the cloud

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** New Technologies · **Lesson:** IaaS — computing resources rented in the cloud
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 2 — Emerging Technologies (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** IaaS rents out the computing itself — virtual servers, storage and networks over the internet — a complete computing package that lets small businesses cut IT infrastructure costs.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the SaaS and PaaS lessons before it. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or in steps — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short, single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09).

## Before this lesson

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Define IaaS: resources in a virtualised environment, over the internet.
2. Say why it is a complete computing package for small businesses.
3. Name the four IaaS examples.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else.

## If the learner is stuck
- Mixes IaaS with PaaS → PaaS rents the building PLATFORM (tools and environment ready); IaaS rents the raw RESOURCES (servers, storage, networks) the platform itself stands on. Thinks 'virtualised' means 'imaginary' → the resources are real computing power, delivered without the renter owning or touching the physical machines.
