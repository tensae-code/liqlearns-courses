# Hands-on — IaaS — computing resources rented in the cloud

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Three floors of renting
Draw a three-floor building. Ground floor: IaaS — the machines. Middle floor: PaaS — the workshop standing on them. Top floor: SaaS — finished software used at the top. Label what a renter receives on each floor, using this title's three lessons.

## A small business plan
A five-person business needs servers, storage and a network but has little money. Write its IaaS plan in four lines: what it rents, what it never buys, how it pays, and one risk from the limitations lesson it should still weigh.
