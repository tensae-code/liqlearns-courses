# Building a Machine Learning Model — Steps and Tools — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the four requirements.**
**Answer: A problem stated as a prediction, honest data enough, computing, a way to judge success**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What two parts is data split into?**
**Answer: Training and test parts**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What happens at training?**
**Answer: The algorithm finds its pattern in the training data**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Which step eats most of the time?**
**Answer: Preparation of the data**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is chosen before training?**
**Answer: The algorithm family that fits**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Which score is the honest one?**
**Answer: The score on unseen test data**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is overfitting?**
**Answer: Memorising the training data and failing strangers**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Name the tuning loop's three acts.**
**Answer: Adjust, retrain, retest**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Name the tools' two kinds.**
**Answer: Python with its libraries; visual drag-and-build tools**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why teach steps before tools?**
**Answer: The steps do not change with the tool**
