# Physical Computing — Coding for the International Space Station — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is the station's computer here?**
**Answer: The Astro Pi, with its Sense HAT of sensors**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name three of its senses.**
**Answer: Temperature, humidity, pressure, movement/orientation, light/colour (any three)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is handling a sensor, as a pattern?**
**Answer: Read the value, decide by it, act on it**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Where do the readings arrive from?**
**Answer: A real, moving, orbiting room**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which unit teaches it?**
**Answer: Greece Grade 9 Pliroforiki C, Unit 3**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is the matrix?**
**Answer: 8×8 — sixty-four lights in an 8-by-8 grid**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How is a pixel set?**
**Answer: By its coordinates and its colour**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What can be built on it?**
**Answer: Pictures, letters, icons and small animations**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the grid's discipline?**
**Answer: Every pixel is a decision — only 64 lights**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Who is the audience?**
**Answer: Astronauts — the station's crew**
