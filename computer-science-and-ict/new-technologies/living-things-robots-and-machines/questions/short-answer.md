# Living Things, Robots and Machines — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name life's five signs.**
**Answer: Born/germinates, feeds, grows, reproduces, dies**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is le cycle de vie?**
**Answer: The life cycle — running from itself, powered from within**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Is a resting seed alive?**
**Answer: Yes — it runs the cycle slowly**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Name two border cases the activity argues.**
**Answer: A flame and a river (also: the seed itself)**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Where is the definition earned?**
**Answer: At the border cases**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What can a robot imitate?**
**Answer: Life's outside — move, sense, respond, even “learn”**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What life-cycle acts can a robot not do?**
**Answer: Feed itself, grow, reproduce**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What dies when a robot is switched off?**
**Answer: Nothing — that is the point**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What mistake does the activity train against?**
**Answer: Calling a robot alive because it moves — mistaking imitation for the thing**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is an automaton in this comparison?**
**Answer: Plainer still than a robot — a machine following its pattern**
