# Hands-on — Living Things, Robots and Machines

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Sort the world** — Cards/pictures of twenty things (seed, flame, robot dog, tree, stone, robot, baby, river…). Sort: living / not living / argue-it. The argue pile gets the class debate.

## Task two
**Test the robot** — Write the five life signs as a checklist. Test a classroom robot (or a video of one) against each sign, with evidence. Verdict in one sentence.
