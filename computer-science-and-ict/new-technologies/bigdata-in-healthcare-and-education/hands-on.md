# Hands-on — Big data in healthcare and education

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Your school's data
List the data your school already holds: marks, attendance, applications. For each, write one honest decision it could improve if it were analysed together — and one thing that must be kept private while doing it.

## A clinic's week
Imagine a small clinic. Write three data sources it generates in a week, then one way analysing them could improve prevention, and one way it could cut cost.
