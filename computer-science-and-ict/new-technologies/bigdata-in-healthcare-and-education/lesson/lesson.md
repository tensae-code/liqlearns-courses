# Big data in healthcare and education

## Do it first

A hospital holds records for a whole city. A school holds results for thousands of learners, year after year. Separately, each file says little. Together, analysed, they can change how care and teaching are done. This lesson shows how, in both places.

## Healthcare

In healthcare, analysts use big data from **patient and hospital records, exam findings and testing machines** — plus **AI-powered fitness devices, telemedicine and remote monitoring**.

Used well, it improves **prevention, intervention, management, patient experience and cost** — stopping illness earlier, treating it better, running care better, treating patients better, and spending less.

## Education

In education, big data **tailors balanced learning plans and blended online/offline learning to grades and attention spans**, helps institutions **assess applications and shorten selection**, and supports **evidence-based decisions**.

The guide adds the school-level picture (teacher guide — evidence): educators use big data to **craft personalised lesson plans, predict learning outcomes, and help pupils find colleges and majors that fit their interests and skills**.
