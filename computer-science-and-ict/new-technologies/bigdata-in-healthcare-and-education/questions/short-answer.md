# Big data in healthcare and education — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1.** Name the five things big data improves in healthcare.
**Answer:** Prevention, intervention, management, patient experience and cost.

**2.** Give two uses of big data in education.
**Answer:** Any two of: tailored balanced learning plans, blended online/offline learning fitted to grades and attention spans, assessing applications and shortening selection, evidence-based decisions.
