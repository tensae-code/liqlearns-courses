# Big data in healthcare and education — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Healthcare big data comes from —
a) patient and hospital records, exam findings, testing machines  b) only school marks  c) only adverts  d) weather reports only
**Answer: a) patient and hospital records, exam findings, testing machines**

**2.** Telemedicine and remote monitoring feed healthcare data through —
a) AI-powered fitness devices and remote care  b) paper letters  c) radio only  d) shop tills
**Answer: a) AI-powered fitness devices and remote care**

**3.** Which is NOT one of healthcare's five improvements?
a) prevention  b) patient experience  c) cost  d) advertising reach
**Answer: d) advertising reach** — The five: prevention, intervention, management, patient experience, cost.

**4.** In education, learning plans are tailored to —
a) grades and attention spans  b) shop prices  c) bus routes  d) weather
**Answer: a) grades and attention spans**

**5.** Blended learning means —
a) only online learning  b) online and offline learning blended  c) no learning  d) only examinations
**Answer: b) online and offline learning blended**

**6.** The guide says educators use big data to —
a) craft personalised lesson plans and predict learning outcomes  b) close schools  c) replace all teachers  d) print textbooks
**Answer: a) craft personalised lesson plans and predict learning outcomes** — Teacher guide — evidence.
