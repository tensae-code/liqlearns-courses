# Types of Machine Learning Algorithms — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the three families by learning.**
**Answer: Supervised, unsupervised, reinforcement**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What data does supervised learning need?**
**Answer: Labelled examples**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What are classification and regression?**
**Answer: Sorting into known kinds; predicting a number**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does unsupervised learning find?**
**Answer: Structure in unlabelled data — clusters and associations**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How does reinforcement learning learn?**
**Answer: From reward and punishment, by acting**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name four algorithms on the working shelf.**
**Answer: Decision trees, nearest neighbours, linear regression, support vector machines (or neural networks, k-means)**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does k-means do?**
**Answer: Clustering — grouping by likeness**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Which algorithm can show its reasons?**
**Answer: A decision tree**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What three things does choosing weigh?**
**Answer: The data's size and shape, the need to explain, the cost of errors**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the chooser's real question?**
**Answer: Not 'the best algorithm' but 'the best for this data, this decision, this price of being wrong'**
