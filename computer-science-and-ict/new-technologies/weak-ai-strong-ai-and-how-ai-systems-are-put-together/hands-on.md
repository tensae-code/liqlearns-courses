# Hands-on — Weak AI, Strong AI and How AI Systems Are Put Together

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sort the systems
List five AI systems you have heard of. For each, name its one task — and confirm it is narrow AI.

## Parts hunt
Take one AI system and find its three parts: where is its data from, what is its model doing, how was it trained? Present the three in one sentence each.
