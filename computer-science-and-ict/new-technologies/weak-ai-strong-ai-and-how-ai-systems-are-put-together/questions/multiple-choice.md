# Weak AI, Strong AI and How AI Systems Are Put Together — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Weak AI is also called what?**
a) Narrow AI  b) One kind of task  c) Handle many kinds of tasks, like a human  d) No — today's systems are weak (narrow) AI
**Answer: a) Narrow AI**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How many kinds of task does weak AI do?**
a) Handle many kinds of tasks, like a human  b) One kind of task  c) No — today's systems are weak (narrow) AI  d) Artificial general intelligence
**Answer: b) One kind of task**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What would strong AI be able to do?**
a) No — today's systems are weak (narrow) AI  b) Artificial general intelligence  c) Handle many kinds of tasks, like a human  d) Data, a model (program), and training
**Answer: c) Handle many kinds of tasks, like a human**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Does strong AI exist today?**
a) Artificial general intelligence  b) Data, a model (program), and training  c) Improving the system using examples and data  d) No — today's systems are weak (narrow) AI
**Answer: d) No — today's systems are weak (narrow) AI**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does AGI stand for?**
a) Artificial general intelligence  b) Data, a model (program), and training  c) Improving the system using examples and data  d) Systems acting beyond human control and intentions
**Answer: a) Artificial general intelligence**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) An AI system is built from what three things?**
a) Improving the system using examples and data  b) Data, a model (program), and training  c) Systems acting beyond human control and intentions  d) So rules can be set while it is being built
**Answer: b) Data, a model (program), and training**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is training an AI system?**
a) Systems acting beyond human control and intentions  b) So rules can be set while it is being built  c) Improving the system using examples and data  d) The people who build and use it
**Answer: c) Improving the system using examples and data**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Name a risk of AGI discussed in the guide.**
a) So rules can be set while it is being built  b) The people who build and use it  c) Narrow AI  d) Systems acting beyond human control and intentions
**Answer: d) Systems acting beyond human control and intentions**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why are AGI risks studied before AGI exists?**
a) So rules can be set while it is being built  b) The people who build and use it  c) Narrow AI  d) One kind of task
**Answer: a) So rules can be set while it is being built**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Who stays responsible for an AI system?**
a) Narrow AI  b) The people who build and use it  c) One kind of task  d) Handle many kinds of tasks, like a human
**Answer: b) The people who build and use it**
