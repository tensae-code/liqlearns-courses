# Weak AI, Strong AI and How AI Systems Are Put Together — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Weak AI is also called what?" is: Narrow AI**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "How many kinds of task does weak AI do?" is: No — today's systems are weak (narrow) AI**
**Answer: False** — One kind of task

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What would strong AI be able to do?" is: Handle many kinds of tasks, like a human**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Does strong AI exist today?" is: Data, a model (program), and training**
**Answer: False** — No — today's systems are weak (narrow) AI

**Answer: True**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is training an AI system?" is: Improving the system using examples and data**

**Answer: False** — Systems acting beyond human control and intentions
