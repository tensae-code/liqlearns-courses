# Weak AI, Strong AI and How AI Systems Are Put Together — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Weak AI is also called what?**
**Answer: Narrow AI**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How many kinds of task does weak AI do?**
**Answer: One kind of task**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What would strong AI be able to do?**
**Answer: Handle many kinds of tasks, like a human**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Does strong AI exist today?**
**Answer: No — today's systems are weak (narrow) AI**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does AGI stand for?**
**Answer: Artificial general intelligence**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) An AI system is built from what three things?**
**Answer: Data, a model (program), and training**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is training an AI system?**
**Answer: Improving the system using examples and data**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Name a risk of AGI discussed in the guide.**
**Answer: Systems acting beyond human control and intentions**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why are AGI risks studied before AGI exists?**
**Answer: So rules can be set while it is being built**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Who stays responsible for an AI system?**
**Answer: The people who build and use it**
