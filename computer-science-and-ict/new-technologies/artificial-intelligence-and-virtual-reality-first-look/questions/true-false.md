# Artificial Intelligence and Virtual Reality — First Look — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is artificial intelligence?" is: Computer systems performing jobs that, done by a person, would be called intelligent**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Name three such jobs." is: No — it imitates the result of understanding by working on patterns in data**
**Answer: False** — Understanding language, recognising pictures, learning from examples (or making decisions)

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Does the machine understand as a person does?" is: No — it imitates the result of understanding by working on patterns in data**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is AI, in one plain phrase?" is: Strong at pattern jobs; blind outside them**
**Answer: False** — Software, built by people

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Where is AI strong, and where blind?" is: Strong at pattern jobs; blind outside them**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is virtual reality?" is: Through a headset that fills the eyes and ears**
**Answer: False** — A computer-made world a person steps into and can look around and act in

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "How is VR usually entered?" is: Through a headset that fills the eyes and ears**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Give a school use of VR." is: Both stretch what application software means — what a program can do, and where the user can be**
**Answer: False** — Practising flying a plane that cannot crash (or walking an unbuilt building, or visiting a reef from class)

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Why does Fiji pair AI and VR?" is: Both stretch what application software means — what a program can do, and where the user can be**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "At what depth does Fiji teach them in Year 11?" is: Computer systems performing jobs that, done by a person, would be called intelligent**
**Answer: False** — A first look — the shape of each field, not its building
