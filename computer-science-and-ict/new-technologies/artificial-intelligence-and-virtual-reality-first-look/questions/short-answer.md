# Artificial Intelligence and Virtual Reality — First Look — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is artificial intelligence?**
**Answer: Computer systems performing jobs that, done by a person, would be called intelligent**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name three such jobs.**
**Answer: Understanding language, recognising pictures, learning from examples (or making decisions)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Does the machine understand as a person does?**
**Answer: No — it imitates the result of understanding by working on patterns in data**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is AI, in one plain phrase?**
**Answer: Software, built by people**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Where is AI strong, and where blind?**
**Answer: Strong at pattern jobs; blind outside them**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is virtual reality?**
**Answer: A computer-made world a person steps into and can look around and act in**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How is VR usually entered?**
**Answer: Through a headset that fills the eyes and ears**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Give a school use of VR.**
**Answer: Practising flying a plane that cannot crash (or walking an unbuilt building, or visiting a reef from class)**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why does Fiji pair AI and VR?**
**Answer: Both stretch what application software means — what a program can do, and where the user can be**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) At what depth does Fiji teach them in Year 11?**
**Answer: A first look — the shape of each field, not its building**
