# Video script — The Data Life Cycle in the Cloud

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The cycle begins with **creation** (data made or collected, and classified — public, internal, secret), then **storage** (placed in the cloud, encrypted and copied), then **use** (processed by services and people, under permissions), then **sharing** (sent to partners and users — the stage where control is hardest, because shared data travels beyond the owner's roof). At every stage the questions repeat: who may touch it, is it encrypted, is its touching logged?
When data's working life ends it is **archived** — kept cold for the years law or history demands — and finally **destroyed**: truly erased, from live copies and backups alike, with the erasure provable. In the cloud, destruction is a duty the customer must demand and the provider must perform, because copies live in places the customer never sees. Jordan's lesson closes the security unit's frame: protecting data means guarding a whole life, not a moment.
