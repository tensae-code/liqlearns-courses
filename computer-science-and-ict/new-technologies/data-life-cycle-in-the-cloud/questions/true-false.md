Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Create, store, use, share, archive, destroy** — as the answer to: What happens at creation besides making data?

**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Encrypted and copied** — as the answer to: Why is sharing the hardest stage for control?

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Who may touch it, is it encrypted, is its touching logged**
