# The Data Life Cycle in the Cloud — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the life cycle's stages in order.**
**Answer: Create, store, use, share, archive, destroy**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What happens at creation besides making data?**
**Answer: It is classified — public, internal, secret**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How is data stored in the cycle?**
**Answer: Encrypted and copied**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why is sharing the hardest stage for control?**
**Answer: Shared data travels beyond the owner's roof**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What three questions repeat at every stage?**
**Answer: Who may touch it, is it encrypted, is its touching logged**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is archiving?**
**Answer: Keeping data cold for the years law or history demands**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What must destruction erase?**
**Answer: Live copies and backups alike**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What must be provable about destruction?**
**Answer: The erasure itself**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Whose duty is cloud destruction?**
**Answer: The customer must demand it and the provider perform it**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does protecting data mean, in the closing phrase?**
**Answer: Guarding a whole life, not a moment**
