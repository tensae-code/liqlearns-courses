# Data Security in Cloud Computing — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the four protections.**
**Answer: Encryption, access control, backup and replication, monitoring**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does encryption at rest protect against?**
**Answer: Stolen disks yielding only noise**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What three things does access control hold?**
**Answer: Accounts, permissions, multi-factor sign-in**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Against what do copies in other buildings guard?**
**Answer: Fire and failure**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does monitoring watch for?**
**Answer: The intruder's footsteps in the logs**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does the provider secure?**
**Answer: The cloud itself — buildings, machines, platform soundness**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does the customer secure?**
**Answer: What they put in the cloud — accounts, permissions, classification, habits**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Name three customer-half breaches.**
**Answer: A public bucket, a phished password, a permission given to everyone**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the field's most expensive misunderstanding?**
**Answer: Blaming the provider for the customer's half**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Who switches much of the machinery on?**
**Answer: The customer**
