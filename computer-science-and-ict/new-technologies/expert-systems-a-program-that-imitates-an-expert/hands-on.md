# Hands-on — Expert systems — a program that imitates an expert

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Be the engine
Write five if–then–else rules for one skill you know (cooking, football, phone repair). A partner gives you a case; you apply the rules out loud, step by step. You were the inference engine; your rules were the knowledge base.

## Interview an expert
Ask someone skilled three 'what do you check first?' questions. Write their answers as if–then–else rules.
