No book error touches this lesson. The guide's keyed application examples are labelled teacher evidence.

Laos's own way (Grade 10 ICT student book): the upper-secondary course turns conceptual — a full information-systems syllabus (organisations, TPS/MIS/DSS/EIS/expert/office systems, the development life cycle), then the series' deepest security chapter (firewalls, cookies, spyware, spam, employee monitoring, privacy law), closing with artificial intelligence, with DOS and Unix taught alongside Windows in the senior years' background.

Jordan's own way (Jordan Grade 11 G11T2 Student): Cloud services are taught through the two commercial providers students can actually open — Microsoft Azure and Google Cloud — before data security in the cloud; the AI unit goes from expert systems through machine learning and NLP to modelling and simulation.


Kazakhstan's own way (Heathcote ICT Grade 10): an imported British ICT course (Express Publishing's Grammar Schools series) adapted for Kazakhstan — ten modules that include two topics the national books do not teach at all: expert and control systems, and developing a mobile app.

Kazakhstan's own way (Express Publishing G10 «ICT»): alongside the national publishers, Kazakhstan's English-medium Grade 10 classes use this imported Express Publishing ICT course — ten self-contained modules that include two topics the national books do not teach at this level: expert and control systems, and developing a mobile app.
