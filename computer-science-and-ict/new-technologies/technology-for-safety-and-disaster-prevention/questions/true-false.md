# Technology for Safety and Disaster Prevention — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is disaster technology's first job?" is: To work before the event — sensing, forecasting and alerting**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What do sensors and monitoring watch?" is: A prediction with a place and a time**
**Answer: False** — The things that become disasters — river levels, ground movement, weather, fire

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does forecasting add to sensor readings?" is: A prediction with a place and a time**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What makes an alert actually save people?" is: Every link is an information system — data collected, transmitted, processed and delivered as a decision**
**Answer: False** — It must reach the people in the path, in a form they understand, with time to act

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Why does Egypt's book teach this as information technology?" is: Every link is an information system — data collected, transmitted, processed and delivered as a decision**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is technology's second job when prevention fails?" is: Survive the disaster itself**
**Answer: False** — Limiting harm — emergency communication, maps and records, coordination of help

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What must emergency communication do?" is: Survive the disaster itself**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is graceful failure in a safety system?" is: The same idea at two scales — information placed where the decision is made, before it is urgent**
**Answer: False** — Being built for the worst day — backup power, backup links, and copies the event cannot reach

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What do a wall safety sign and a national warning system share?" is: The same idea at two scales — information placed where the decision is made, before it is urgent**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Name the four alert decisions the lesson names?" is: To work before the event — sensing, forecasting and alerting**
**Answer: False** — Move, shelter, close, evacuate
