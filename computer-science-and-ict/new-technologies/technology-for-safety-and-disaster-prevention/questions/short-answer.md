# Technology for Safety and Disaster Prevention — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is disaster technology's first job?**
**Answer: To work before the event — sensing, forecasting and alerting**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What do sensors and monitoring watch?**
**Answer: The things that become disasters — river levels, ground movement, weather, fire**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does forecasting add to sensor readings?**
**Answer: A prediction with a place and a time**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What makes an alert actually save people?**
**Answer: It must reach the people in the path, in a form they understand, with time to act**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why does Egypt's book teach this as information technology?**
**Answer: Every link is an information system — data collected, transmitted, processed and delivered as a decision**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is technology's second job when prevention fails?**
**Answer: Limiting harm — emergency communication, maps and records, coordination of help**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What must emergency communication do?**
**Answer: Survive the disaster itself**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is graceful failure in a safety system?**
**Answer: Being built for the worst day — backup power, backup links, and copies the event cannot reach**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What do a wall safety sign and a national warning system share?**
**Answer: The same idea at two scales — information placed where the decision is made, before it is urgent**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Name the four alert decisions the lesson names**
**Answer: Move, shelter, close, evacuate**
