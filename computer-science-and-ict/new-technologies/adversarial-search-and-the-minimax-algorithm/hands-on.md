# Hands-on — Adversarial Search and the Minimax Algorithm

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Value a tree** — Value a small two-ply game tree by minimax, marking MAX and MIN levels and the chosen move.

## Task two
**Be MIN** — Play three tic-tac-toe games against a partner where you announce your minimax reasoning aloud for each move.
