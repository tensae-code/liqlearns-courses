# Adversarial Search and the Minimax Algorithm — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What makes adversarial search different?**
**Answer: An opponent searches against you — the world moves back**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Who are MAX and MIN?**
**Answer: The player wanting the highest score, and the one wanting it lowest**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does MIN choose?**
**Answer: The move leading to the worst value for MAX**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What scores the leaves?**
**Answer: A utility function — win, loss, draw, or a number**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Is a position's value found or fought over?**
**Answer: Fought over**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does minimax suppose?**
**Answer: That both players play perfectly**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What happens at MAX's and MIN's nodes?**
**Answer: Take the maximum of children; take the minimum**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) From where does minimax work up?**
**Answer: From the leaves**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which move does MAX choose?**
**Answer: The one whose minimax value is highest**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) In which game does Jordan program it?**
**Answer: Tic-tac-toe, in Python**
