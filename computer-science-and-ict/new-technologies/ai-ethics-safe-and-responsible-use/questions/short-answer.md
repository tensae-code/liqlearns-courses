# AI Ethics — Safe and Responsible Use — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What closes Kuwait's Grade 10 AI unit?**
**Answer: The ethical principles for safe and responsible use of AI**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does safe use mean?**
**Answer: AI's powers spent without harming people**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name two safe-use habits.**
**Answer: Checking answers before acting on them; knowing AI learns patterns, not truth (also: keeping inside law and rules)**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Is safety the tool's property or the user's?**
**Answer: The user's habits — the same system is safe in careful hands, harmful in careless ones**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does honesty require?**
**Answer: AI-helped work is declared as helped, not presented as unaided**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Whose material may AI outputs carry?**
**Answer: Other people's — not made yours by being generated**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is the privacy duty?**
**Answer: Real people's data is not fed to a system without right**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Who answers for what the tool does?**
**Answer: The human who uses it**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Is 'the AI did it' accepted as an excuse?**
**Answer: No — under none of the principles**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which book teaches this?**
**Answer: Kuwait Grade 10 Information Technology Term 2 (Book L1), Unit 2**
