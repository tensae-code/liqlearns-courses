# Video script — AI Ethics — Safe and Responsible Use

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Safe** use of AI means its powers are spent without **harming people**: its answers checked before they are acted on (a confident error repeated is the user's error too), its limits known (it learns patterns, not truth), and its use kept inside the law and the setting's rules — a school, a workplace, a country's. Kuwait teaches safety as a property of the **user's habits**, not the tool's promises: the same system is safe in careful hands and harmful in careless ones.
**Responsible** use adds the duties: **honesty** — work helped by AI is declared as helped, not presented as unaided; **credit and rights** — AI's outputs may carry other people's material and ideas, which are not made yours by being generated; **privacy** — real people's data is not fed to a system without right. And beneath them all, the unit's framing principle: the human who uses the tool **answers for what it does** — 'the AI did it' is not an excuse any of Kuwait's principles accepts.
