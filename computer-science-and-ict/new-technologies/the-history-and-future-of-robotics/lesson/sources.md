Provenance scope note: the placements stocked here are from Kazakhstan's okulyk.kz (OKULYK) textbooks, recommended by the Ministry of Education and Science of the Republic of Kazakhstan — the Digital Literacy and Informatika lines of the Almatykitap, Arman-PV and Atamura teams and the Nazarbayev Intellectual Schools (NIS) books, all student books except the two Kadirkulov teacher guides named in the entries below, whose placements are stocked exactly as filed. Many books exist in parallel language editions (Kazakh / Russian / Uzbek / Uyghur / English) of the same textbook: each distinct book is taught once, and every distinct edition is named and cited in the book entry. Kazakhstan's own ways are kept: one textbook line in up to five parallel language editions, the okulyk.kz digital library as the books' home, Scratch from Grade 1 and robotics strands (LEGO WeDo 2.0 and Mindstorms EV3) from Grade 1–2, and every keyword given in Kazakh–Russian–English in the early grades. Media slots in lesson/lesson.md are described slots only ([Picture — …]); no picture numbers are invented.


Kazakhstan's own way (Kadirkulov G5 «Информатика»): Grade 5 in the Almatykitap line is a robotics year: after information representation (encryption, binary, binary-coded graphics) and raster/vector graphics, two full sections run LEGO robots — gyro sensor, turns, line following and robo-sumo competitions — before a closing safety section that ends with shared folders and mini-projects. Every term is glossed in three languages (Kazakh/Russian/English) in the margin.

Kazakhstan's own way (Mukametzhanova G5 «Информатика» 2020 edition): the 2020 Atamura revision of the Grade 5 book is rebuilt on the renewed programme — the same five sections as the Almatykitap book (Book 18): presenting information with encryption and binary, raster and vector graphics with curves, robotics with the gyroscope sensor and turns, robot competitions (line following and robo-sumo), and computer safety moved to the end.

Kazakhstan's own way (Kobdikova G5 «Информатика»): the Arman-PV Grade 5 book is the practicum twin of the same programme as Books 18 and 35 — nearly every unit comes in a theory + practice pair, and it adds a block the others lack: sharing files (placing, changing and downloading shared files), setting a password on documents, and organising a document effectively for print, taught as "mini-project works".
