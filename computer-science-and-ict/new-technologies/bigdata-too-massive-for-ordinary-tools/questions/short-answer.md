# Big data — too massive for ordinary tools — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why did big data appear in the digital age?
**Answer:** Computers, the internet and sensors convert the real world into digital data in exploding amounts.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the two steps of the collection process, in order.
**Answer:** First identify useful sources; then build a pipeline that moves the data from generation to where the organisation stores it for use.
