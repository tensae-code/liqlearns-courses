# Hands-on — Big data — too massive for ordinary tools

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Count your own data day
For one day, list every piece of data you create: messages, photos, searches, movements with a phone. Multiply your list by the number of pupils in your school. Write the total and say honestly: could one spreadsheet hold your school's day?

## Find the pipeline
Pick one source from the lesson — a shop till, a phone's location, a survey. On paper, draw its pipeline: where the data is born, the pipe it travels, and the store it lands in. Label the two steps.
