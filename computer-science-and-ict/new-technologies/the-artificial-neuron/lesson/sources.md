Placement choice: this lesson was built new for the ICT expansion (Germany, 2026-11-14) because no existing lesson in the course covers this thing as its own lesson — the live tree was checked for the slug and for near matches before building. 

Germany's own way: a Bavarian NTG Gymnasium book built on four deep strands — graph theory (adjacency matrices, breadth-first search, Dijkstra), coding and cryptography (symmetric/asymmetric encryption, signatures), network communication, and AI up to neural networks — closing with student projects including programming a social bot. This lesson keeps that depth: the idea is taught whole, with the book's own worked way, not thinned down.

Egypt's own way (Grade 8, Term 2 student book (Arabic)): Term 2 teaches AI from the neuron up — artificial neuron, its role in AI — then Python for data analysis and even encryption with XOR, a depth most countries save for secondary school.
