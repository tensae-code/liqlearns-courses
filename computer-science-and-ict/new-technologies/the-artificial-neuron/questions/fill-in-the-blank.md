# The Artificial Neuron — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Each input is multiplied by its ____ before the sum is taken.**
**Answer: weight**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) The number added to the weighted sum as the neuron's tendency is the ____.**
**Answer: bias**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) The function that turns the net input into the output is the ____ function.**
**Answer: activation**
