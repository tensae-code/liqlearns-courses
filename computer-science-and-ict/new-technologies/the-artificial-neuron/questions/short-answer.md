# The Artificial Neuron — short answer

Answer in your own words, in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 2) Compute and judge: inputs x₁ = 0.5 (rain), x₂ = 1 (friend going), weights w₁ = −2, w₂ = 3, bias b = −0.5, threshold 0. Does the neuron fire?**
**Answer: z = (−2)(0.5) + 3·1 + (−0.5) = −1 + 3 − 0.5 = 1.5. Since 1.5 ≥ 0, the neuron fires: output 1.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Why is 'the weights are the learning' a fair summary of this lesson?**
**Answer: Because everything else in the neuron is fixed by design or given by the world — the activation rule, the inputs — while training's whole action is nudging the weights and bias until outputs fit the examples. What the neuron 'knows' is stored in those numbers.**
