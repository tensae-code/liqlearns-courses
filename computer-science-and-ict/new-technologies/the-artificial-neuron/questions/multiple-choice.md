# The Artificial Neuron — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A weight tells the neuron —**
a) The input's name  b) How much that input counts  c) The time  d) The threshold
**Answer: b) How much that input counts** — Positive weighs for, negative against, near zero means ignored.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) The net input z is —**
a) The largest input  b) The sum of inputs times their weights, plus the bias  c) The output  d) The number of inputs
**Answer: b) The sum of inputs times their weights, plus the bias** — z = w₁x₁ + w₂x₂ + … + b — one line of arithmetic.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) With w₁ = 2, x₁ = 1, w₂ = −3, x₂ = 0.6, b = 0.5, the net input is —**
a) 0.7  b) 2.3  c) −0.5  d) 1.5
**Answer: a) 0.7** — 2 − 1.8 + 0.5 = 0.7.

**4. (Sub lesson 1) The bias is best described as —**
a) An error  b) The neuron's built-in tendency, added before any evidence  c) A fourth input from the world  d) The threshold
**Answer: b) The neuron's built-in tendency, added before any evidence** — A thumb on the scale, part of what training tunes.

**5. (Sub lesson 2) With a step activation at threshold 0, a neuron with z = −0.5 outputs —**
a) 1  b) −0.5  c) 0  d) 0.5
**Answer: c) 0** — Below threshold: no fire, output 0.

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Training a neuron adjusts —**
a) Its inputs  b) Its weights and bias  c) Its activation's definition  d) The examples
**Answer: b) Its weights and bias** — Learning is weight-adjustment — nothing else inside the neuron changes.
