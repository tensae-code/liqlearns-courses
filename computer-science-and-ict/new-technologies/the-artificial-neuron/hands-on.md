# Hands-on — The Artificial Neuron

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Be the neuron
Fix the jacket neuron's weights from the lesson. A partner calls out weather pairs (sunshine, wind) as numbers and you compute z and the decision aloud, showing each multiplication. After six calls, change one weight as 'training' — make wind count less — and redo the two calls whose answers flip. Those flips are learning, made visible.

## Design a snack neuron
Invent a neuron that decides 'buy the snack?' from three inputs you choose (price, hunger, health), with weights and a bias you defend in one sentence each. Compute its answer for two shoppers you invent. If an answer embarrasses you, adjust a weight — and say which example taught you.
