# Video script — The Artificial Neuron

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

One artificial neuron is a tiny calculator. Numbers come in.

Each input is multiplied by its weight — how much that input counts.

Add them all. Add the bias, the neuron's own tendency. That sum is the net input.

The activation function decides: at or over the threshold, the neuron fires 1. Below it, 0.

Learning does not rebuild the neuron. It adjusts the weights, little by little.

One neuron, one tiny judgement. The next lesson wires thousands together.
