# Hands-on — Building an EV3 Robot Model

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Work the book's own example** — §10: assembling the educational robot model (EV3 module, large motors, wheels, cables) Work it with the book open first, step by step, then close the book and do it again.

## Task two
**Show it to someone else** — Teach building an ev3 robot model to a companion in your own words, with your worked example in front of you, and answer their one question from the lesson.
