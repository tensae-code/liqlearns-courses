# NLP — Tasks, Techniques and Applications — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the six basic tasks.**
**Answer: Tokenisation, part-of-speech tagging, parsing, named-entity recognition, sentiment, summarisation**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is tokenisation?**
**Answer: Cutting text into words and pieces**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does named-entity recognition spot?**
**Answer: People, places, dates**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What are the meaning tasks?**
**Answer: Sentiment and summarisation**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Of what is every application a composition?**
**Answer: These basic tasks**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name the techniques' three generations.**
**Answer: Hand-written rules, statistics from corpora, neural models**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does each generation trade?**
**Answer: Less brittle, more hungry for data**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Name four applications.**
**Answer: Translation, chatbots/assistants, question-understanding search, spam and sentiment filters (or summarisation, speech)**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is NLP's honest footnote for Arabic speakers?**
**Answer: Its riches are unevenly spread across languages; Arabic's share is a frontier**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Where can an application's failure be hunted?**
**Answer: To the basic task that failed**
