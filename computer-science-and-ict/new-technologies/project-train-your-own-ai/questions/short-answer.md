# Project — Train Your Own AI — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does the pupil do in this project?**
**Answer: Trains an AI of their own choosing — teaches it with examples and tests what it decides**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is chosen first?**
**Answer: What the AI will learn to tell apart or answer**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How is it taught?**
**Answer: With examples — self-directed training**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How is it tested?**
**Answer: On cases it has never seen**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What do the project worksheets hold?**
**Answer: The aim, the recorded examples, and the tests with their results**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Why keep the training as a record?**
**Answer: So it can be judged, not exaggerated from memory**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Which four arc lessons are spent together?**
**Answer: What AI is, data, training (machine learning), and decisions**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What surprise do the worksheets usually catch?**
**Answer: The AI learned exactly what it was taught — its failures are the training's failures**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is this project in the unit?**
**Answer: Project (1) of the Digital World unit — the AI arc's closing project**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which book sets this project?**
**Answer: Kuwait Grade 4 Digital Explorer Term 2, Unit 1 Lesson 7**
