# Video script — Data Analysis — from Collecting to Conclusions

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Analysis begins before any data exists: **define the problem** — the exact question the data must answer, in words a collector could obey. Then **collect** — gather the data the question needs, from counting, measuring or asking, honestly and completely. Then **organise** — put the collected data in order: tables, groups and tallies, so it can be read at all. Kuwait's order is the discipline: a vague question collects useless data, and unorganised data answers nothing, however much of it there is.
**Analyse** works the organised data: count, compare, total and average until the question's shape shows. **Interpret the results** says what the analysis **means** for the problem defined at the start — the conclusion, stated in the question's own terms, with its honesty kept: what the data did **not** cover is part of the interpretation. Five steps, and the last reaches back to the first — which is why the problem's definition, made before any counting, is the step masters check twice.
