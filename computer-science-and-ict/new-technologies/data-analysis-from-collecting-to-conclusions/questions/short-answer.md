# Data Analysis — from Collecting to Conclusions — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the five steps of data analysis, in order.**
**Answer: Define the problem, collect, organise, analyse, interpret the results**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is defining the problem?**
**Answer: Stating the exact question the data must answer, in words a collector could obey**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How is data collected, in the lesson's terms?**
**Answer: By counting, measuring or asking — honestly and completely**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does organising do?**
**Answer: Puts the data in order — tables, groups, tallies — so it can be read**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What happens to a vague question's data?**
**Answer: It collects useless data**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does analysis do?**
**Answer: Counts, compares, totals and averages until the question's shape shows**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is interpretation?**
**Answer: Saying what the analysis means for the problem — the conclusion in the question's own terms**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What honesty belongs in an interpretation?**
**Answer: What the data did not cover**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which step do masters check twice, and why?**
**Answer: Defining the problem — the last step reaches back to it**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which book teaches this?**
**Answer: Kuwait Grade 5 Digital Explorer Term 2, Unit 1 Lesson 2 — The World of Data**
