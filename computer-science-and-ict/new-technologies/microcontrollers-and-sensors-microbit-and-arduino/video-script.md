# Video script — Microcontrollers and Sensors — micro:bit and Arduino

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A **microcontroller** is a **small computer on a single chip**: it joins a **processor, memory, and inputs and outputs** on one piece, and it is built **into a device** to control it. It runs **one program** — the device's own work — again and again: in a **solar lamp**, a **washing machine** or a **microwave**, all Sri Lanka's own examples. It is not a small desktop computer: it has no screen of its own and does no other job — the chip, the program and the device are one thing.
A microcontroller learns about the world through **sensors**: parts that **detect a change in the environment** — light, heat, movement — and turn it into a **signal** the chip can read. The chip reads the signal and **acts** as its program says: switching, timing, warning. Pupils meet this control with real kits — the **BBC micro:bit** and **Arduino** — building circuits that sense and respond, so the lamp that lights itself stops being magic and becomes a program they wrote.
