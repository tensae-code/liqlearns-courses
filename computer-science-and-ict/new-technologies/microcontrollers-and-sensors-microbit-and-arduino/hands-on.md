# Hands-on — Microcontrollers and Sensors — micro:bit and Arduino

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Sense and respond on paper', 'Design (on paper, or with a kit if you have one) a device that senses one change — darkness, heat or movement — and responds. Name its sensor, its microcontroller and the action its program orders.')

## Task two
('Find the chip', 'List three devices at home or school that hold a microcontroller — Sri Lanka's solar lamp, washing machine and microwave are the starting set — and write what each one's chip controls.')
