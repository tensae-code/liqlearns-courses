# Microcontrollers and Sensors — micro:bit and Arduino — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is a microcontroller?" is: A small computer on a single chip, built into a device to control it**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What parts does a microcontroller join on its chip?" is: Inside a device — in a solar lamp, a washing machine or a microwave**
**Answer: False** — A processor, memory, and inputs and outputs

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Where does a microcontroller work?" is: Inside a device — in a solar lamp, a washing machine or a microwave**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does the microcontroller run?" is: It is one small chip fitted to one device's job, with no screen of its own**
**Answer: False** — One program — the device's own work, again and again

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "How is a microcontroller different from a desktop computer?" is: It is one small chip fitted to one device's job, with no screen of its own**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does a sensor do?" is: It reads the signal and acts — switching, timing or warning as its program says**
**Answer: False** — It detects a change in the environment — light, heat or movement — and turns it into a signal

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does the microcontroller do with a sensor's signal?" is: It reads the signal and acts — switching, timing or warning as its program says**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Name a learning kit for microcontrollers." is: Real circuits that sense and respond**
**Answer: False** — The BBC micro:bit (or an Arduino kit)

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What do learners build with a micro:bit or Arduino kit?" is: Real circuits that sense and respond**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Give one of Sri Lanka's local examples of a microcontroller at work." is: A small computer on a single chip, built into a device to control it**
**Answer: False** — A solar lamp that lights itself when darkness falls (any one of: a washing machine, a microwave)
