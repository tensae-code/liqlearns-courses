**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a microcontroller?**
a) A small computer on a single chip, built into a device to control it  b) A processor, memory, and inputs and outputs  c) Inside a device — in a solar lamp, a washing machine or a microwave  d) One program — the device's own work, again and again
