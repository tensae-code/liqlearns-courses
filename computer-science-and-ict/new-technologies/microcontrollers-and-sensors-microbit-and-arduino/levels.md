# Microcontrollers and Sensors — micro:bit and Arduino — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, expressed as its SUB LESSONS. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — A microcontroller — a small computer on one chip
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: A small computer on a single chip, built into a device to control it; A processor, memory, and inputs and outputs; Inside a device — in a solar lamp, a washing machine or a microwave; One program — the device's own work, again and again; It is one small chip fitted to one device's job, with no screen of its own.

## Sub lesson 2 — Sensors — and learning with micro:bit and Arduino
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: It detects a change in the environment — light, heat or movement — and turns it into a signal; It reads the signal and acts — switching, timing or warning as its program says; The BBC micro:bit (or an Arduino kit); Real circuits that sense and respond; A solar lamp that lights itself when darkness falls (any one of: a washing machine, a microwave).
