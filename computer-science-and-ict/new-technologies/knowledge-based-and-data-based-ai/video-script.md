# Video script — Knowledge-Based and Data-Based AI

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

There are two ways to build intelligence into a machine.

Way one: write down what experts know. Facts, and rules: if this, then that.

A knowledge-based system can show its reasons. But it knows only what was written.

Way two: write nothing. Show the machine thousands of examples, and let it find the pattern.

A data-based system can learn what nobody can write as rules. But it cannot explain itself.

Two ways. Opposite strengths. Every AI you meet is one, the other, or a mix.
