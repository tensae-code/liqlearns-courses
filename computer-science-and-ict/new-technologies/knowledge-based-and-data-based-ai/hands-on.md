# Hands-on — Knowledge-Based and Data-Based AI

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Be the expert system
Choose a small domain you know well — which drink suits which weather, which fault fits which bicycle symptom. Write six honest IF–THEN rules, then let a partner present cases and fire your rules by hand. Note every case your rules cannot settle: you have felt knowledge collection, the bottleneck, in your own hands.

## Sort the systems
List five AI systems you have used or heard of. For each, decide: knowledge-based, data-based, or a mixture — and write the evidence for your verdict (does it cite rules? was it 'trained'? does it explain itself?). One line each; defend the hardest one aloud.
