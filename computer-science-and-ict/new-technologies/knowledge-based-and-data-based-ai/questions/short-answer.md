# Knowledge-Based and Data-Based AI — short answer

Answer in your own words, in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A hospital wants an AI whose every decision can be audited by doctors. Which approach fits, and why?**
**Answer: Knowledge-based. Its answers come from inspectable IF–THEN rules, so a doctor can see exactly which rules fired for a case, challenge them, and correct the knowledge base — an audit a data-based system's tuned numbers cannot offer.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Why did face recognition resist the knowledge-based approach for decades?**
**Answer: Because nobody could write the rules: the variation of faces — light, angle, age, expression — cannot be listed as IF–THEN conditions. The data-based way never needed the list; shown enough examples, it found the pattern itself.**
