# Knowledge-Based and Data-Based AI — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A knowledge-based system stores its expertise as —**
a) Photographs  b) Facts and IF–THEN rules in a knowledge base  c) Dial settings  d) Recordings
**Answer: b) Facts and IF–THEN rules in a knowledge base** — Written knowledge is its defining shape.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) The part of an expert system that chains rules over new facts is the —**
a) Knowledge base  b) Printer  c) Inference engine  d) Dataset
**Answer: c) Inference engine** — The engine fires matching rules and adds their conclusions as new facts.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) A knowledge-based system's proud strength is that it —**
a) Learns by itself  b) Can explain its answer as a chain of rules  c) Needs no experts  d) Works without rules
**Answer: b) Can explain its answer as a chain of rules** — Inspectable reasoning is what rules buy.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) A data-based system acquires its behaviour by —**
a) Interviewing experts  b) Finding patterns in many examples through a learning procedure  c) Reading a rule book  d) Copying another system
**Answer: b) Finding patterns in many examples through a learning procedure** — No rules are given; the data shapes the model.

**5. (Sub lesson 2) Which job suits the data-based way best?**
a) Applying a fixed tax table  b) Recognising faces in photographs  c) Checking a form's required fields  d) Following a recipe exactly
**Answer: b) Recognising faces in photographs** — Nobody can write rules for 'a face'; data found the pattern instead.

**6. (Sub lesson 2) A data-based system's weakness, mirroring the rule-book's strength, is that it —**
a) Is slow to answer  b) Cannot explain its answers as rules a human can audit  c) Needs electricity  d) Forgets overnight
**Answer: b) Cannot explain its answers as rules a human can audit** — Its knowledge is tuned numbers, not readable reasoning.
