# Knowledge-Based and Data-Based AI — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) An expert system can settle a case that no rule in its knowledge base covers, by generalising.**
**Answer: False** — It is blind outside its rules — that is its named weakness.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Collecting an expert's knowledge into rules is quick because experts can say all they know.**
**Answer: False** — Knowledge collection is slow and expensive — experts know more than they can say.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) A data-based system trained on one-sided examples can still be one-sided itself.**
**Answer: True** — It inherits its data: few, wrong or one-sided examples give a system to match.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Data-based AI contains a readable list of the rules it discovered.**
**Answer: False** — What it holds is a pattern of tuned numbers, not readable rules.
