# Building AI Apps with App Inventor — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is an AI app?**
**Answer: An application whose behaviour includes a model's judgement — it recognises, not only responds**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What three things do AI apps recognise in this lesson?**
**Answer: Images, sounds and text**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How does AI power arrive in App Inventor?**
**Answer: As AI components and extensions added like any other part**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does the app hand the component, and receive?**
**Answer: It hands input (camera, microphone, text) and receives an answer with a confidence**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is the one new thing, against ordinary App Inventor apps?**
**Answer: One component thinks**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Who trained the model, and where?**
**Answer: Others, elsewhere — on examples the builder never saw**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What three questions must the builder ask?**
**Answer: What was it trained on, where does it fail, and what should the app do when unsure**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) An AI component's answer is what?**
**Answer: Advice with a confidence**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What remains the builder's own work?**
**Answer: The app's design — what it shows, asks, and does with doubt**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Where does Indonesia teach this?**
**Answer: Grade 11, Bab IV C — developing AI applications with App Inventor**
