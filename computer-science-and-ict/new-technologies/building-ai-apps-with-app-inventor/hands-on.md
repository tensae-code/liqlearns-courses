# Hands-on — Building AI Apps with App Inventor

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Design an AI app** — Sketch an App Inventor AI app for a school job (sorting recycling by photo, reading a sign aloud). Name its AI component, its input, its answer — and what it does when the answer is unsure.

## Task two
**Bound the model** — For the same app, write the three builder's questions with your best answers, and one test you would run to find where the model fails.
