# Building AI Apps with App Inventor

## Do it first

Building AI apps with App Inventor — mobile applications that use artificial intelligence (recognising images, sounds and text) built in App Inventor's blocks

## Sub lesson 1 — AI as components — the app asks, the model answers

An **AI app** is an application whose behaviour includes a model's judgement — it does not only respond to touches, it **recognises**: a face in a photo, a word in a sound, the sense of a sentence. In **App Inventor**, that power arrives as **AI components and extensions** the builder adds to the app like any other part: the app collects the input (camera, microphone, text), hands it to the component, and receives its answer with a confidence. Indonesia's Grade 11 chapter builds AI apps right after ordinary App Inventor apps, so the new thing stands out cleanly: everything is the same, except one component thinks.

In your mind's eye: aI as components — the app asks, the model answers.

## Sub lesson 2 — Building honestly — training, limits and the builder's judgement

The builder does not train the model inside the app — it was trained elsewhere, on examples the builder never saw — so the app's quality is bounded by questions the builder must ask: what was it trained on, where does it fail, and what should the app do when the answer is unsure? The chapter's apps are school-sized (classifiers and recognisers a pupil can test in a day), and their lesson is the standing one of applied AI: the component's answer is advice with a confidence, and the app's design — what it shows, asks and does with doubt — is the builder's own work.

In your mind's eye: building honestly — training, limits and the builder's judgement.
