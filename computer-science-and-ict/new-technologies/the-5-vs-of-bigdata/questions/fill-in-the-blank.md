# The 5 Vs of big data — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1.** Data created in real time shows ______, the speed V.
**Answer: Velocity**

**2.** ______ forms were once only spreadsheets and databases; now email, photos and video count too — that is Variety.
**Answer: Structured (and unstructured / semi-structured)**

**3.** Value is not how much is stored, but the ______ that analysis can draw out.
**Answer: insight**
