# The 5 Vs of big data — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** The five Vs are Volume, Variety, Velocity, Veracity and Value.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The list of Vs is final and can never grow.
**Answer: False** — The book says the list may grow as research continues.

**3.** Veracity includes filtering huge unstructured data into usable data.
**Answer: True**
