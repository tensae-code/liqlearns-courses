# The 5 Vs of big data — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1.** Tell Veracity from Value in one line each.
**Answer:** Veracity: how reliable and trustworthy the data is. Value: how much valuable, reliable data can be analysed for insight.

**2.** Name the five Vs.
**Answer:** Volume, Variety, Velocity, Veracity, Value.
