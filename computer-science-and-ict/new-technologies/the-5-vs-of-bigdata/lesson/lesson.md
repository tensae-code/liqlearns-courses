# The 5 Vs of big data

## Do it first

Imagine six things arriving at once: a neat table of sales; a pile of videos and photos; live posts flooding in every second; a rumour nobody has checked; a pattern hidden in last year's sales that could earn money. Each one shows a different face of big data. This lesson names all five faces.

## The five Vs

Big data is characterised by **five Vs**:

**Volume** — **huge amounts of data generated daily**, from social media, business processes, machines, networks and human interaction.

**Variety** — data in **structured, unstructured and semi-structured forms**. Once it was only spreadsheets and databases; now it is also **email, PDFs, photos, video, audio and social posts**.

**Velocity** — **the speed at which data is created, in real time**.

**Veracity** — **how reliable and trustworthy the data is** — including filtering huge unstructured data into usable data.

**Value** — **not how much is stored, but how much valuable, reliable data can be analysed for insight**.
