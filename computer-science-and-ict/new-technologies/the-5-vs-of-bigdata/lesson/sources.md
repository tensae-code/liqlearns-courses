**Acceptance count: taught in 4 countries (Ethiopia, Egypt, Armenia, Kazakhstan).**

Egypt's own way (Grade 8, Term 1 student book): Two units — "Artificial Intelligence and Protecting Our Digital Data" and "Website Design and Creation" — ending with pupils creating their own school website with AI help.

Armenia's own way (Armenia Grade 10 G10 student): The senior-school series (Stepanyan, Torosyan, Martirosyan, Amirkhanyan, Matinyan) is built on four ideas around digital tools and collaboration; Grades 10–12 switch the programming language to C++ and go deep: Boolean logic, translators, types, code quality, debugging, procedures, recursion, divide-and-conquer, testing, development stages, AI and data.


Kazakhstan's own way (Zhunusov G10 Computer Science, English-medium): the English-medium Grade 10 book is the most technical of the wave: the CIA triad and three separate authentication lessons (passwords twice, biometrics), encoding standards, Python dictionaries, DFS/BFS graph traversal, and a full SQL chapter in MySQL (CREATE, INSERT, SELECT/WHERE, MIN/MAX, JOIN, GROUP BY, SELECT INTO) before a JavaScript chapter. (Its preface is the publisher's generic science template, left over from another book.)
