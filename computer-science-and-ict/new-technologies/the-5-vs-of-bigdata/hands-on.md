# Hands-on — The 5 Vs of big data

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Five-V hunt at home
Find one real example of each V in your home or school day: something huge (Volume), something in a strange form (Variety), something arriving fast (Velocity), something you had to check before trusting (Veracity), something that taught you a useful lesson (Value). Write all five down.

## Structured or not?
Lay out six cards: a mark table, a voice note, a photo, an email, a database row, a video. Sort them into structured, semi-structured and unstructured piles, and defend each pile out loud.
