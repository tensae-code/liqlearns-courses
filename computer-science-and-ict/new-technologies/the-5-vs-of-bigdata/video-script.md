# Video script — The 5 Vs of big data

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Big data has five faces. Five Vs.

**Volume** — a mountain of data, every day.

**Variety** — tables, photos, video, sound.

**Velocity** — arriving fast, in real time.

**Veracity** — can you trust it?

**Value** — the insight hidden inside.

Five Vs. And the list may still grow.
