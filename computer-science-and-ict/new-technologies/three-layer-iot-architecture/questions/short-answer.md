# The Three-Layer IoT Architecture — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the three layers in order.**
**Answer: Perception, network, application**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What lives in the perception layer?**
**Answer: Sensors, actuators and the tagged things**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What two questions do the bottom layers answer?**
**Answer: What is happening out there, and how the news travels**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Over what links does the network layer carry data?**
**Answer: Short-range and long-range, wired and wireless**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which layer touches the physical world?**
**Answer: The perception layer**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is the application layer?**
**Answer: The services built on the carried data**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Give three application examples.**
**Answer: Smart-home control, health monitoring, traffic guidance**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Where does processing live in this map?**
**Answer: In the journey between network and application**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why is the model taught first?**
**Answer: It is the skeleton every richer architecture refines**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How do five- and seven-layer models relate to it?**
**Answer: The same building with floors split and named more finely**
