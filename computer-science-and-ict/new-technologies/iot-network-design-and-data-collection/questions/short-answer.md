# IoT Network Design and Data Collection — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a communication scenario?**
**Answer: The pattern of an IoT system's conversations**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name the four standing patterns.**
**Answer: Device to device, device to gateway, device to cloud, cloud to application**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Which job must not wait for a distant cloud?**
**Answer: The fire alarm**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What can wait for the cloud?**
**Answer: The monthly meter reading**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How are scenarios used in a design?**
**Answer: Chosen and mixed per job**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name four things network design fixes.**
**Answer: Links, gateway places, addresses and security, failure behaviour**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What four collection questions are designed?**
**Answer: What is collected, at what rhythm, kept where, for how long**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does undesigned data become?**
**Answer: A swamp — expensive, untrustworthy, dangerous**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) When is the data's journey planned?**
**Answer: With the network design, before devices are placed**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What two link ranges does design choose between?**
**Answer: Short range and long range**
