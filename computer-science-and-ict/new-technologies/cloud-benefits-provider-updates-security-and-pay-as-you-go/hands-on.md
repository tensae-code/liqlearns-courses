# Hands-on — Cloud benefits — provider updates, security and pay-as-you-go

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two bills
On paper, build two one-year bills for a small school: (a) buy a server, power it, update it, repair it once; (b) pay-as-you-go cloud for the storage and services it needs, quiet months and busy months drawn separately. Invent honest prices. Which bill is easier to control, and why?

## Update diary
For one week, note every update a device or app around you asks for. Count them. Then write one line: who would do all of this work if a provider did not?
