# Lesson plan — Cloud benefits — provider updates, security and pay-as-you-go

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** New Technologies · **Lesson:** Cloud benefits — provider updates, security and pay-as-you-go
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 2 — Emerging Technologies (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the provider maintains the service and supplies every update; cloud security outruns most organisations' own efforts; and cost is controlled by paying only for what is used.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the cloud doorway and the anywhere-access benefits. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or in steps — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons

## Before this lesson

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say who maintains the service and supplies updates.
2. Say why cloud security is presented as stronger than most organisations' own.
3. Name the three cost controls, including pay as you go.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks pay-as-you-go means 'cheap in every case' → it means controlled and matched to use; the limitations lesson next door shows how fees can still surprise. Thinks 'provider updates' means the user does nothing at all → the user still does their own work; the provider maintains the service itself.
