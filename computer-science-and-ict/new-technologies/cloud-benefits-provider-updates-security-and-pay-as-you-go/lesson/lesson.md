# Cloud benefits — provider updates, security and pay-as-you-go

## Do it first

Price two worlds in your head. World one: you buy a server, house it, update it, guard it and repair it, forever. World two: a provider does all of that, and you pay for exactly the computing you use. Which bill can a small school actually control? This lesson is world two's case.

## Updates and security — the provider's job

**The provider maintains the service and supplies all updates**, so users can **focus on their work** instead of on maintaining systems.

And **cloud security is presented as stronger than most organisations' own efforts** — most organisations are limited by **time, expertise and budget**, where a provider specialises.

## Controlled cost — pay as you go

Cost is controlled three ways: **paying only for what is used (pay as you go)**; **scaling allocated resources up or down** as needs change; and **avoiding buying and maintaining equipment such as servers**.

[Picture 30109 — pay only for what you use]

So the cloud bill follows the work: busy month, bigger bill; quiet month, smaller bill — and no server gathering dust in the corner either way.
