# Cloud benefits — provider updates, security and pay-as-you-go — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Who maintains the cloud service and supplies all updates?
a) each user  b) the provider  c) the school janitor  d) nobody
**Answer: b) the provider**

**2.** Cloud security is presented as stronger than most organisations' own efforts because organisations are limited by —
a) time, expertise and budget  b) imagination  c) office size  d) holidays
**Answer: a) time, expertise and budget**

**3.** Pay as you go means —
a) paying a fixed fortune yearly  b) paying only for what is used  c) never paying  d) paying in advance for everything
**Answer: b) paying only for what is used**

**4.** Scaling allocated resources means —
a) weighing servers  b) raising or lowering resources as needs change  c) painting the data centre  d) hiring more guards
**Answer: b) raising or lowering resources as needs change**

**5.** A cost the cloud lets you avoid is —
a) buying and maintaining equipment such as servers  b) paying staff  c) renting rooms  d) buying paper
**Answer: a) buying and maintaining equipment such as servers**
