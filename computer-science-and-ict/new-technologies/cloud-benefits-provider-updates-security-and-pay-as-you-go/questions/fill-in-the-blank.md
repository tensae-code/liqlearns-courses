# Cloud benefits — provider updates, security and pay-as-you-go — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1.** The provider supplies all ______ so users can focus on their work.
**Answer: updates**

**2.** Pay ______ you go: pay only for what is used.
**Answer: as**

**3.** Most organisations' own security is limited by time, ______ and budget.
**Answer: expertise**
