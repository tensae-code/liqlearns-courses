# Lesson plan — Emerging technologies — new, evolving, soon

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** New Technologies · **Lesson:** Emerging technologies — new, evolving, soon
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 2 — Emerging Technologies (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** an emerging technology is a new technology, or an existing one still evolving, that is in development or expected within five to ten years and expected to bring significant social or economic effects.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Grade 11, which already covered data science, AI, and virtual and augmented reality. This unit adds four more: big data, cloud computing, fog computing and the Internet of Things. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or in steps — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what an emerging technology is, with its time window.
2. Name what Grade 11 already covered.
3. Name the four technologies this unit adds.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else.

1. **Do it first** — Name one technology that did not exist, or barely existed, ten years ago. Then judge it against the definition you are about to read. (lesson/lesson.md).

## If the learner is stuck
- Counts any new gadget as emerging → run the three checks: new or evolving? Expected within five to ten years? A significant social or economic effect expected? All three must hold.
