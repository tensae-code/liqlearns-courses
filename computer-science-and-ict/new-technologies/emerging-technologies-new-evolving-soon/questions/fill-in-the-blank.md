# Emerging technologies — new, evolving, soon — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1.** An emerging technology is expected within ______ to ______ years.
**Answer: five, ten**

**2.** The expected effects are significant ______ or ______ effects.
**Answer: social, economic**

**3.** The four technologies this unit adds are big data, cloud computing, fog computing and the ______.
**Answer: Internet of Things (IoT)**
