# Emerging technologies — new, evolving, soon — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** An emerging technology is expected within —
a) five to ten years  b) one hundred years  c) one week  d) the last century
**Answer: a) five to ten years** — the definition's own window.

**2.** An emerging technology must be —
a) completely new, with nothing before it  b) new, or the evolution of an existing one  c) already in every home  d) free of charge
**Answer: b) new, or the evolution of an existing one**

**3.** Its expected effect must be —
a) tiny  b) only on games  c) significant — social or economic  d) secret
**Answer: c) significant — social or economic**

**4.** Which of these did Grade 11 already cover?
a) big data  b) fog computing  c) artificial intelligence  d) the Internet of Things
**Answer: c) artificial intelligence** — AI was Grade 11; the other three are this unit's additions.

**5.** This unit adds —
a) big data, cloud, fog, IoT  b) word processing and spreadsheets  c) printing and scanning  d) keyboards and mice
**Answer: a) big data, cloud, fog, IoT**
