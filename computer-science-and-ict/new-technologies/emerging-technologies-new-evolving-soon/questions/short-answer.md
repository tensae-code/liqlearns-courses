# Emerging technologies — new, evolving, soon — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1.** Give the definition of an emerging technology.
**Answer:** A new technology, or the evolution of an existing one, in development or expected within five to ten years, expected to create significant social or economic effects.

**2.** What did Grade 11 cover that this unit builds on?
**Answer:** Data science, artificial intelligence, and virtual and augmented reality.
