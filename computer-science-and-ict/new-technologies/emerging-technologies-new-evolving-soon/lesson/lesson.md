# Emerging technologies — new, evolving, soon

## Do it first

Name one technology that did not exist, or barely existed, ten years ago — in your home, your school or your town. Hold it in mind. Now test it against the definition in this lesson: was it new, or an old technology evolving? Did it change society or money matters in a big way?

## What “emerging” means

An **emerging technology** is a **new technology, or the evolution of an existing one**, that is **in development or expected within the next five to ten years**, and that is **expected to create significant social or economic effects**.

So three checks make a technology “emerging”: it is new or still evolving; its time is now to about ten years ahead; and its effect is expected to be big — on how people live together, or on work and money.
