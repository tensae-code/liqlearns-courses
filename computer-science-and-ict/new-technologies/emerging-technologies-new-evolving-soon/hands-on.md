# Hands-on — Emerging technologies — new, evolving, soon

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Ten years back, ten years forward
Write two lists. First: three technologies your family uses now that were new or rare ten years ago. Second: three changes you honestly expect in the next ten years. Beside each one, write which of the three checks it passes.

## Ask at home
Ask an older person at home: what technology changed your work most? Write their answer in two lines and say whether it was new, or an old technology evolving.
