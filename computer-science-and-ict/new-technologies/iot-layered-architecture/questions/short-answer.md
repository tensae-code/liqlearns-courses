# IoT Layered Architecture — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a layered architecture?**
**Answer: A system divided into stacked layers, each with its own job**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does each layer do for its neighbours?**
**Answer: Serves the layer above and uses the layer below**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name the four layers from the world up.**
**Answer: Perception, network, processing, application**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What can be swapped without rewriting the application?**
**Answer: A better sensor**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How is a fault hunted in a layered system?**
**Answer: Layer by layer**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does the processing layer do?**
**Answer: Stores, analyses and decides**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does the application layer present?**
**Answer: Services — the dashboard, the alert, the automatic action**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What sits between layers?**
**Answer: Interfaces — agreed ways of handing work across**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Name two concerns no single layer owns.**
**Answer: Security and management**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does the network layer guarantee?**
**Answer: Reliable transmission and addressing of the data's journey**
