# AI at work — eleven application areas — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Online shopping AI gives —
a) personalised recommendations  b) only paper catalogues  c) no suggestions  d) slower pages
**Answer: a) personalised recommendations**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Google's named search AI is —
a) RankBrain  b) MYCIN  c) Robonaut  d) Alexa
**Answer: a) RankBrain**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Autonomous cars use —
a) GPS, radar and cameras  b) only maps on paper  c) horses  d) steam
**Answer: a) GPS, radar and cameras**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) In agriculture, AI helps with —
a) precision farming and pest detection  b) only harvesting by hand  c) rain making  d) fence painting
**Answer: a) precision farming and pest detection**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) NASA's named robot is —
a) Robonaut  b) RankBrain  c) MYCIN  d) Siri
**Answer: a) Robonaut**

**6.** (Sub lesson 3) Smart homes use AI for —
a) security, chores and smoke alerts  b) only lighting  c) farming  d) banking only
**Answer: a) security, chores and smoke alerts**
