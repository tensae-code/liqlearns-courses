# Hands-on — AI at work — eleven application areas

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Field hunt
For two days, log every AI encounter and label it with one of the eleven fields. Bring the log: which field met you most?

## Debate in pairs
The guide stages this as a debate: one argues autonomous cars will be safer, one argues against. Use the lesson's own points — shared hazard information and the 360-degree view against your own risks.
