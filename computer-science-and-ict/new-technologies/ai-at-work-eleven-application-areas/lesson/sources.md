Egypt's own way (Grade 7, Term 2 student book): Term 2 pivots from applications to computing itself — AI, sensors and robots, then a Scratch-to-Python bridge in the same term.

Egypt's own way (Grade 8, Term 1 student book): Two units — "Artificial Intelligence and Protecting Our Digital Data" and "Website Design and Creation" — ending with pupils creating their own school website with AI help.

Egypt's own way (Grade 8, Term 2 student book (Arabic)): Term 2 teaches AI from the neuron up — artificial neuron, its role in AI — then Python for data analysis and even encryption with XOR, a depth most countries save for secondary school.
