# AI at work — eleven application areas

## Do it first

Count today: how many times did a machine recommend, recognise, translate or decide something for you? List them. Then read the eleven fields where AI works — and check your list against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Shopping, social media, search and customer service

**Online shopping:** personalised recommendations built from shopper data. **Social media:** advertising, personalised content and facial recognition. **Search engines:** understanding queries and ranking results — Google's **RankBrain** is named. **Customer service:** **chatbots** giving fast, cheap support by text or voice.

## Sub lesson 2 — Cars, surveillance, farming and banks

**Autonomous cars:** GPS, radar and cameras — **Tesla autopilot** is named — with expected safety gains from shared hazard information and a 360-degree view. **Surveillance:** automatic, real-time detection of suspicious behaviour. **Agriculture:** precision farming, pest detection, soil and crop health, climate-ready advice. **Banks:** spotting fraud patterns in card transactions.

## Sub lesson 3 — Healthcare, space and smart homes

**Healthcare:** diagnosis, drug development, hospital care and surgery. **Space exploration:** huge data processing, mission planning and navigation — NASA's **Robonaut** on the International Space Station is named. **Smart homes:** security, chores and smoke alerts.

## Inside social media — how it works (teacher guide — evidence)

The teacher guide keys the mechanisms: **advertising is targeted from buying behaviour**, **content is personalised to each user**, and platforms use AI in named ways — one network **recommending jobs**, another **recognising faces in photos**. The guide also keys the medicine-and-home lists used in this lesson's questions: in medicine, diagnosis help, earlier cancer detection, surgical robots and virtual nursing assistants; at home, a cooker that senses when food is ready, voice-controlled appliances and face-recognition home monitoring (teacher guide — evidence).
