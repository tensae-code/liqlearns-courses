# Hands-on — Hybrid cloud and community cloud

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sort the school's data
List ten kinds of school data — notices, marks, exam papers, library records, fees, photos. Sort each into public-cloud keeping or private-cloud keeping and defend the sort. You have just designed a hybrid cloud.

## Find your community
Name one field where organisations near you share the same security or compliance concerns — clinics, banks, schools, government offices. Write what a shared platform would have to get right for all of them.
