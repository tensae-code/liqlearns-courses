# Hybrid cloud and community cloud

## Do it first

Your school keeps two kinds of thing: notices anyone may read, and exam papers nobody outside may touch. One school, two kinds of keeping, side by side. Now imagine five schools sharing one system together because their needs are the same. Those two pictures are this lesson's two clouds.

## Hybrid cloud — both, joined

A **hybrid cloud combines public and private**: **public services run in the public cloud; confidential and data services run in the private cloud** — gaining **both models' flexibility**.

## Community cloud — shared by a field

A **community cloud** is a **collaborative multi-tenant platform** where **several distinct organisations share the same applications** because they **work in the same industry and share security, compliance and performance concerns**.

Its users: **government institutions, healthcare, financial services and other professional communities**. The book describes it as **essentially a privately managed platform that functions much like a public cloud**.
