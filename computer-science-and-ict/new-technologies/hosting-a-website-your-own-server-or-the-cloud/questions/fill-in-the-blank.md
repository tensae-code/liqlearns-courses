# Hosting a website — your own server or the cloud? — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1.** The old way needs a public ______ address before the world can find the site.
**Answer: IP**

**2.** In cloud hosting you press a ______ to upload your content.
**Answer: button**

**3.** The cloud way makes the site public in ______, for much less.
**Answer: minutes**
