# Descriptive Statistics in Python — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the three centres.**
**Answer: Mean, median, mode**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is the mean's weakness?**
**Answer: It is dragged by extremes**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) When is the median chosen?**
**Answer: For dragged data — incomes**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is the mode?**
**Answer: The most frequent value — the only centre a kind can have**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Whose judgement is the choice of centre?**
**Answer: The analyst's, not the library's**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Why does centre without spread lie?**
**Answer: Two sets can share a mean and differ in everything else**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is standard deviation?**
**Answer: The typical distance from the mean**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does describe() gather?**
**Answer: Count, mean, deviation, min, quartiles, max**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is describe() called in the lesson?**
**Answer: The analyst's first page on any new column**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What must be done before quoting one number?**
**Answer: Read the summary whole**
