# Hands-on — Descriptive Statistics in Python

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Five numbers** — For a real column (class marks), compute the five-number summary in Python or by hand and write the column's story in three sentences.

## Task two
**Centre fight** — Invent two classes with the same mean and different medians and spreads; say which class you'd rather join and why.
