# Intelligent Agents — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is an intelligent agent?**
**Answer: A program that senses its environment, decides, and acts on its own toward a goal**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What loop defines an agent?**
**Answer: Sense → decide → act → sense again**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Through what does an agent perceive and act?**
**Answer: Sensors for perceiving; effectors for acting**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Name the simplest kind of agent.**
**Answer: A simple reflex agent — one condition, one action**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What do learning agents do beyond acting?**
**Answer: Build a picture of their world and improve with experience**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name three agents at work.**
**Answer: An assistant, a spam filter, and a recommender (also robots, game players)**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How do agents gather the chapter's other branches?**
**Answer: An agent may carry a neural network as eyes and fuzzy logic as judgement**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What goal does an agent pursue?**
**Answer: Only the goal it was given**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Who answers for an agent's acts?**
**Answer: Its makers — the agent is a tool with initiative, not a mind with intentions**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which chapter teaches this?**
**Answer: Laos Grade 10 ICT, Chapter 4 §6.6**
