# Video script — Intelligent Agents

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

An **intelligent agent** is a program that **perceives its environment through sensors, decides what to do, and acts through effectors, on its own, toward a goal it was given**. The loop is its definition: **sense → decide → act → sense again** — a thermostat's heir grown capable: where the thermostat's decision is one rule, an agent's may search, plan and learn. Agents differ by how much deciding they carry: from **simple reflex agents** (one condition, one action) to **goal-based and learning agents** that build a picture of their world and improve with experience.
Intelligent agents already run errands everywhere: the **assistant** that answers and schedules, the **filter** that sorts mail from spam, the **recommender** that chooses what to offer next, the **robot** that navigates a floor, the **player** that opposes you in a game. Laos closes its AI branches with agents because they gather the others: an agent may carry a neural network as its eyes and fuzzy logic as its judgement, wrapped in the loop that makes it behave as a someone rather than a something. Its limits are the chapter's standing limits of AI: an agent does only the goal it was given, understands nothing beyond its sensors and model, and its acts are its makers' responsibility — the agent is a tool with initiative, not a mind with intentions.
