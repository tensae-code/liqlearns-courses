# My Robot Draws Shapes — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. Explain, in your own words: program the robot to drive a square: equal steps, equal quarter-turns.**
**Answer:** The learner's own explanation, true to the lesson: A floor robot draws shapes by driving them: equal steps and equal quarter-turns make a square; change the steps and turns deliberately, and new shapes come — planned in robot language first.

**2. A classmate gets this lesson's work wrong. What is the most likely fault, and how is it fixed?**
**Answer:** Shape will not close → one side or turn disagrees with its opposite: read the program back and compare side with side, turn with turn, until the odd one shows itself.
