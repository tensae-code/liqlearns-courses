# My Robot Draws Shapes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. A robot-drawn square needs —**
a) four equal step-counts and four quarter-turns  b) any steps at all  c) no turns
**Answer: a) four equal step-counts and four quarter-turns**

**2. A rectangle from the square's program changes —**
a) all the turns  b) the robot  c) two step-counts, keeping the turns
**Answer: c) two step-counts, keeping the turns**

**3. A shape that will not close has —**
a) a shy robot  b) a step or turn that disagrees with its opposite  c) too much floor
**Answer: b) a step or turn that disagrees with its opposite**

**4. Planning a robot shape happens —**
a) in robot language, before the robot moves  b) after the robot finishes  c) never
**Answer: a) in robot language, before the robot moves**
