# Hands-on — My Robot Draws Shapes

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Drive a square
Plan and enter a square in robot steps. Run it, check it closes, and write the program under a drawing of the shape it drove.

## Same turns, new shape
Keep the square's turns and change two step-counts to drive a rectangle. Say beforehand how the shape will differ — then prove it on the mat.
