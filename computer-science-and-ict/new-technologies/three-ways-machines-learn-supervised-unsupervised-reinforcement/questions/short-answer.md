# Three Ways Machines Learn — Supervised, Unsupervised, Reinforcement — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does supervised learning train on?**
**Answer: Examples with their answers**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does a supervised system learn to do?**
**Answer: Produce the answer for new cases**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does unsupervised learning have none of?**
**Answer: Answers — labels**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does unsupervised learning find?**
**Answer: Structure — groups of similar things, unnamed in advance**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Name an unsupervised example.**
**Answer: Grouping similar customers into families**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does the world answer with, in reinforcement learning?**
**Answer: Reward or penalty**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How does a reinforcement learner improve?**
**Answer: By trial — keeping what scores, abandoning what loses, over enormous numbers of attempts**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How do the three forms differ?**
**Answer: In their feedback: an answer key, no key but structure, or a score**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What names the learning?**
**Answer: Naming the feedback**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Give a reinforcement example.**
**Answer: A game program learning by playing and scoring**
