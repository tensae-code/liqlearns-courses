# Hands-on — Three Ways Machines Learn — Supervised, Unsupervised, Reinforcement

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Sort the learners** — Cards describe eight learning situations (spam filter trained on labelled mail, photo grouping, a robot learning to walk by falling…). Sort them into the three forms and defend each sort by its feedback.

## Task two
**Be the learner** — Play a guessing game three ways: with answers shown (supervised), by grouping word cards freely (unsupervised), and by points for good guesses (reinforcement). Say how each felt different.
