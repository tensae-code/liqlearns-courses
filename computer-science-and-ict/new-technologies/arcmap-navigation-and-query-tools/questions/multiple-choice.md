# ArcMap Navigation and Query Tools — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What do navigation tools move?**
a) The view, without moving the data  b) Full extent  c) Go to XY  d) Click a feature and read its attributes
**Answer: a) The view, without moving the data**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Which tool shows the whole area at once?**
a) Go to XY  b) Full extent  c) Click a feature and read its attributes  d) Rules a distance or an area on the map
**Answer: b) Full extent**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How do you jump to typed coordinates?**
a) Click a feature and read its attributes  b) Rules a distance or an area on the map  c) Go to XY  d) The view, without moving the data
**Answer: c) Go to XY**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does the identify tool do?**
a) Rules a distance or an area on the map  b) The view, without moving the data  c) Full extent  d) Click a feature and read its attributes
**Answer: d) Click a feature and read its attributes**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does the measure tool do?**
a) Rules a distance or an area on the map  b) The view, without moving the data  c) Full extent  d) Go to XY
**Answer: a) Rules a distance or an area on the map**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does select by attribute use?**
a) Place — inside, touching or within a distance  b) The attribute table  c) A zone drawn at a set distance around a feature  d) Union keeps everything; intersect keeps only what overlaps
**Answer: b) The attribute table**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does select by location use?**
a) A zone drawn at a set distance around a feature  b) Union keeps everything; intersect keeps only what overlaps  c) Place — inside, touching or within a distance  d) Legend, north arrow (orientation) and scale — plus title and text
**Answer: c) Place — inside, touching or within a distance**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is a buffer?**
a) Union keeps everything; intersect keeps only what overlaps  b) Legend, north arrow (orientation) and scale — plus title and text  c) The attribute table  d) A zone drawn at a set distance around a feature
**Answer: d) A zone drawn at a set distance around a feature**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the difference between union and intersect?**
a) Union keeps everything; intersect keeps only what overlaps  b) Legend, north arrow (orientation) and scale — plus title and text  c) The attribute table  d) Place — inside, touching or within a distance
**Answer: a) Union keeps everything; intersect keeps only what overlaps**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Name three things a finished map layout carries.**
a) The attribute table  b) Legend, north arrow (orientation) and scale — plus title and text  c) Place — inside, touching or within a distance  d) A zone drawn at a set distance around a feature
**Answer: b) Legend, north arrow (orientation) and scale — plus title and text**
