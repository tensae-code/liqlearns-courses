# ArcMap Navigation and Query Tools — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What do navigation tools move?**
**Answer: The view, without moving the data**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Which tool shows the whole area at once?**
**Answer: Full extent**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How do you jump to typed coordinates?**
**Answer: Go to XY**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does the identify tool do?**
**Answer: Click a feature and read its attributes**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does the measure tool do?**
**Answer: Rules a distance or an area on the map**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does select by attribute use?**
**Answer: The attribute table**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does select by location use?**
**Answer: Place — inside, touching or within a distance**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is a buffer?**
**Answer: A zone drawn at a set distance around a feature**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the difference between union and intersect?**
**Answer: Union keeps everything; intersect keeps only what overlaps**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Name three things a finished map layout carries.**
**Answer: Legend, north arrow (orientation) and scale — plus title and text**
