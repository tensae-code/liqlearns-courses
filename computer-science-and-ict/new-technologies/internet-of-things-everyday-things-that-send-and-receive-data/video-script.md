# Video script — Internet of Things — everyday things that send and receive data

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Things are joining the internet.

A toaster. A watch. A cow's collar. A room's sensor.

Sensors collect. Networks carry. Data is exchanged.

Can it send — or receive — over the internet?

Then it is smart. That is the **Internet of Things**.
