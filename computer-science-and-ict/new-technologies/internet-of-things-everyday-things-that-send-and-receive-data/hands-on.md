# Hands-on — Internet of Things — everyday things that send and receive data

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Smart or not?
List ten things in your home. Test each against the book's rule — can it send and/or receive information over the internet? Mark each smart or not-smart, and for every not-smart one, write the smallest change that would make it smart.

## Equip a thing
Choose one ordinary thing — a water tank, a door, a bicycle. On paper, equip it as an IoT thing: name its sensor, its connection, the data it collects, and who or what receives it. Label all four equipment kinds from the lesson.
