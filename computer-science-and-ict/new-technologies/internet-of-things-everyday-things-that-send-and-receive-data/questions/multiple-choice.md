# Internet of Things — everyday things that send and receive data — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** IoT is a network of —
a) physical objects or people equipped to collect and exchange data  b) paper files  c) television stations  d) roads and bridges
**Answer: a) physical objects or people equipped to collect and exchange data**

**2.** Things in IoT are equipped with —
a) software, electronics, networks and sensors  b) wheels and ropes  c) paper and ink  d) glass and wood
**Answer: a) software, electronics, networks and sensors**

**3.** IoT extends internet connectivity —
a) to fewer devices  b) beyond computers and smartphones to home and business devices  c) only to offices  d) only to phones
**Answer: b) beyond computers and smartphones to home and business devices**

**4.** Which can be an IoT 'thing', per the book?
a) a toaster  b) a stone  c) a printed photograph  d) a wooden spoon with nothing added
**Answer: a) a toaster** — Also: a diabetes-monitor implant, an animal tracker, an AC sensor, a wearable.

**5.** A thing becomes smart when it can —
a) glow in the dark  b) send and/or receive information over the internet  c) be sold  d) be painted
**Answer: b) send and/or receive information over the internet** — The book's rule.

**6.** IoT makes things virtually smart through —
a) data collection, AI algorithms and networks  b) bigger batteries only  c) brighter screens  d) louder speakers
**Answer: a) data collection, AI algorithms and networks**
