# Internet of Things — everyday things that send and receive data — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1.** Define the Internet of Things.
**Answer:** A network of physical objects or people — things — equipped with software, electronics, networks and sensors to collect and exchange data.

**2.** State the book's rule for when something becomes smart.
**Answer:** When something can send and/or receive information over the internet, it becomes smart.
