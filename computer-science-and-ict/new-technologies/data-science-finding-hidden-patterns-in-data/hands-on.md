# Hands-on — Data Science — finding hidden patterns in data

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Find the hidden pattern
Record a week of one small thing (buses past the gate each hour, goals in practice games). Lay the numbers out and hunt: what repeats, what spikes? That hunt is Data Science by hand.

## Insight sentence
From your pattern, write one insight sentence a decision could use: 'Because …, we should …'. Data only becomes an insight at that sentence.
