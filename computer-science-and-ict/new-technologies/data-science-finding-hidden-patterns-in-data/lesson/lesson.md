## What Data Science is

**Data Science** is a **blend of tools, algorithms and machine-learning principles** used to **discover and extract hidden patterns in data**. It is **interdisciplinary** — it draws on **statistics, mathematics and computer science** together.

## The data scientist and the uses

The professional is the **data scientist**, using algorithms to **turn data into useful insights**. The named uses: **forecasting product demand** to set raw-material orders, **personalising social-media content**, **weather prediction for farming**, **preventive medicine**, and **risk management in business**.
