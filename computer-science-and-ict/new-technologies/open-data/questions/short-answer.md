# Open Data — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is open data?**
**Answer: Data published so anyone may access, use and share it**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Who typically publishes it?**
**Answer: Governments and public bodies, about the public world**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name its three marks.**
**Answer: Free to reach, free to use (reuse licence), machine-readable**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Give an example.**
**Answer: A bus timetable (or budget, weather archive, statistics)**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Openness is what kind of thing?**
**Answer: A publisher's decision — not a property of the data**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name a civic use.**
**Answer: Citizens checking the budget (or services built on it, or research/journalism on inspectable evidence)**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) A journey planner stands on what?**
**Answer: The published timetable**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What must never be opened?**
**Answer: Personal data**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How may people-contributed data be opened?**
**Answer: Aggregated and anonymised**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Openness stands beside which teaching of the same unit?**
**Answer: Privacy — one lesson's two hands**
