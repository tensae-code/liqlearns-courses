# Open Data — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is open data?** → **Data published so anyone may access, use and share it**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Who typically publishes it?** → **Governments and public bodies, about the public world**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name its three marks.** → **Free to reach, free to use (reuse licence), machine-readable**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Give an example.** → **A bus timetable (or budget, weather archive, statistics)**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Openness is what kind of thing?** → **A publisher's decision — not a property of the data**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) A journey planner stands on what?** → **The published timetable**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What must never be opened?** → **Personal data**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How may people-contributed data be opened?** → **Aggregated and anonymised**
