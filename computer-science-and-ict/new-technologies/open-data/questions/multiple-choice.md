# Open Data — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is open data?**
a) Data published so anyone may access, use and share it  b) Governments and public bodies, about the public world  c) Free to reach, free to use (reuse licence), machine-readable  d) A bus timetable (or budget, weather archive, statistics)
**Answer: a**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Who typically publishes it?**
a) Free to reach, free to use (reuse licence), machine-readable  b) Governments and public bodies, about the public world  c) A bus timetable (or budget, weather archive, statistics)  d) A publisher's decision — not a property of the data
**Answer: b**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name its three marks.**
a) A bus timetable (or budget, weather archive, statistics)  b) A publisher's decision — not a property of the data  c) Free to reach, free to use (reuse licence), machine-readable  d) Citizens checking the budget (or services built on it, or research/journalism on inspectable evidence)
**Answer: c**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Give an example.**
a) A publisher's decision — not a property of the data  b) Citizens checking the budget (or services built on it, or research/journalism on inspectable evidence)  c) The published timetable  d) A bus timetable (or budget, weather archive, statistics)
**Answer: d**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Openness is what kind of thing?**
a) A publisher's decision — not a property of the data  b) Citizens checking the budget (or services built on it, or research/journalism on inspectable evidence)  c) The published timetable  d) Personal data
**Answer: a**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name a civic use.**
a) The published timetable  b) Citizens checking the budget (or services built on it, or research/journalism on inspectable evidence)  c) Personal data  d) Aggregated and anonymised
**Answer: b**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) A journey planner stands on what?**
a) Personal data  b) Aggregated and anonymised  c) The published timetable  d) Privacy — one lesson's two hands
**Answer: c**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What must never be opened?**
a) Aggregated and anonymised  b) Privacy — one lesson's two hands  c) Data published so anyone may access, use and share it  d) Personal data
**Answer: d**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How may people-contributed data be opened?**
a) Aggregated and anonymised  b) Privacy — one lesson's two hands  c) Data published so anyone may access, use and share it  d) Governments and public bodies, about the public world
**Answer: a**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Openness stands beside which teaching of the same unit?**
a) Data published so anyone may access, use and share it  b) Privacy — one lesson's two hands  c) Governments and public bodies, about the public world  d) Free to reach, free to use (reuse licence), machine-readable
