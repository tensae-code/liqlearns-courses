# Types of Knowledge — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is declarative knowledge?**
a) Knowing that — facts and relations  b) Knowing how — steps and skills  c) Facts as checkable statements; procedures as runnable methods  d) A system stuffed with facts about swimming cannot swim
**Answer: a**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is procedural knowledge?**
a) Knowing how — steps and skills  b) Facts as checkable statements; procedures as runnable methods  c) A system stuffed with facts about swimming cannot swim  d) Paris is in France (or: fever accompanies flu)
**Answer: a**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How is each held in a knowledge base?**
a) Facts as checkable statements; procedures as runnable methods  b) A system stuffed with facts about swimming cannot swim  c) Paris is in France (or: fever accompanies flu)  d) Rules of thumb from experience
**Answer: a**
