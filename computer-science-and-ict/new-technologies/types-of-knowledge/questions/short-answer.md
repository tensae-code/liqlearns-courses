# Types of Knowledge — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is declarative knowledge?**
**Answer: Knowing that — facts and relations**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is procedural knowledge?**
**Answer: Knowing how — steps and skills**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How is each held in a knowledge base?**
**Answer: Facts as checkable statements; procedures as runnable methods**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is the swimming fault?**
**Answer: A system stuffed with facts about swimming cannot swim**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Give a declarative example.**
**Answer: Paris is in France (or: fever accompanies flu)**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is heuristic knowledge?**
**Answer: Rules of thumb from experience**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is meta-knowledge?**
**Answer: Knowledge about knowledge — what the system knows and doesn't**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is tacit knowledge?**
**Answer: What the expert does but cannot say**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which kind is hardest to capture?**
**Answer: Tacit knowledge**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why sort knowledge first?**
**Answer: Each kind has its own way of being written down**
