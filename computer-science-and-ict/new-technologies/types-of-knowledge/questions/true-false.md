# Types of Knowledge — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Knowing that — facts and relations**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Knowing that — facts and relations** — as the answer to: What is procedural knowledge?
**Answer: False — Knowing how — steps and skills**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Facts as checkable statements; procedures as runnable methods**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Facts as checkable statements; procedures as runnable methods** — as the answer to: What is the swimming fault?
**Answer: False — A system stuffed with facts about swimming cannot swim**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Paris is in France (or: fever accompanies flu)**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Paris is in France (or: fever accompanies flu)** — as the answer to: What is heuristic knowledge?
**Answer: False — Rules of thumb from experience**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Knowledge about knowledge — what the system knows and doesn't**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Knowledge about knowledge — what the system knows and doesn't** — as the answer to: What is tacit knowledge?
**Answer: False — What the expert does but cannot say**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Tacit knowledge**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Tacit knowledge** — as the answer to: Why sort knowledge first?
**Answer: False — Each kind has its own way of being written down**
