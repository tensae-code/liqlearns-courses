# Propositional Logic and Predicate Logic — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a proposition?**
**Answer: A whole statement that is true or false**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name the four connectives.**
**Answer: AND, OR, NOT, IF–THEN**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How is a joined statement's truth found?**
**Answer: Computed from its parts' truths**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is propositional logic blind to?**
**Answer: What is inside statements**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Give its limit example.**
**Answer: 'All birds fly' and 'some birds fly' are unrelated atoms to it**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What do predicates say?**
**Answer: Things about objects — Bird(x), Flies(x)**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What do quantifiers speak of?**
**Answer: All (∀) or some (∃)**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Write 'all birds fly' in predicate form.**
**Answer: ∀x (Bird(x) → Flies(x))**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which form do AI knowledge bases use?**
**Answer: Predicate logic**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the closing trade?**
**Answer: Propositional is simpler and decidable; predicate is expressive but costs more to reason with**
