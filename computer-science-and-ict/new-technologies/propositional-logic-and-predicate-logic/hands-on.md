# Hands-on — Propositional Logic and Predicate Logic

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Translate both ways** — Write three statements in words, then in propositional form, then in predicate form; say what the last form can say that the middle cannot.

## Task two
**Quantifier hunt** — Find 'all' and 'some' claims in one page of a textbook and write each with ∀ or ∃.
