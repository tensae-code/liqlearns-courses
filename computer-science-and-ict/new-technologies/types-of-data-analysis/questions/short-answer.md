# Types of Data Analysis — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the four types with their questions.**
**Answer: Descriptive — what happened; diagnostic — why; predictive — what will happen; prescriptive — what should we do**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does descriptive analysis produce?**
**Answer: Summaries of the past — totals, averages, charts**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How does diagnostic analysis hunt causes?**
**Answer: Drilling in, comparing groups and periods**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why must description precede diagnosis?**
**Answer: No prediction stands on an unexplained past**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What pair is the foundation?**
**Answer: Descriptive and diagnostic**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What must predictive analysis state with its outcomes?**
**Answer: Their uncertainty**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does prescriptive analysis weigh?**
**Answer: Possible actions against predicted outcomes**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Which type spends all the others?**
**Answer: Prescriptive**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What grows up the ladder?**
**Answer: Ambition and risk — worth more, wrong more expensively**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why climb in order?**
**Answer: Each rung stands on the ones below**
