# Types of Data Analysis — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the four types with their questions.**
a) Descriptive — what happened; diagnostic — why; predictive — what will happen; prescriptive — what should we do  b) Summaries of the past — totals, averages, charts  c) Drilling in, comparing groups and periods  d) No prediction stands on an unexplained past
**Answer: a**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does descriptive analysis produce?**
a) Summaries of the past — totals, averages, charts  b) Drilling in, comparing groups and periods  c) No prediction stands on an unexplained past  d) Descriptive and diagnostic
**Answer: a**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How does diagnostic analysis hunt causes?**

**Answer: a**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why must description precede diagnosis?**
a) No prediction stands on an unexplained past  b) Descriptive and diagnostic  c) Their uncertainty  d) Possible actions against predicted outcomes

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What pair is the foundation?**
