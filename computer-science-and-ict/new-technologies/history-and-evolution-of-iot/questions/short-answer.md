# History and Evolution of the Internet of Things — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What did barcodes and RFID first give objects?**
**Answer: An identity a machine could read**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is machine-to-machine communication?**
**Answer: Devices talking to devices without a person in between**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why did IPv6 matter for IoT?**
**Answer: Its vast address space gives every thing its own address**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What three limits did the early stages remove?**
**Answer: Naming things, letting them talk, addressing them all**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What carried things beyond wired links?**
**Answer: Wireless communication**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What three arrivals let things sense and report alone?**
**Answer: Cheap sensors, small processors and cloud computing**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Give three examples of the sensing stage.**
**Answer: The smart meter, the tracked lorry, the wearable**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does AI add to IoT in the newest stage?**
**Answer: Analysis for prediction and automatic response**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) In what three things does the evolution grow?**
**Answer: Number of things, what each can do, how independently it acts**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the fridge example's point?**
**Answer: A plain appliance became a communicating thing**
