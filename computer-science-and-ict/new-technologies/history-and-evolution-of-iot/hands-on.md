# Hands-on — History and Evolution of the Internet of Things

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Stage line** — Draw a timeline of IoT's stages and place five real devices on it, saying which stage each belongs to and why.

## Task two
**Next stage guess** — Pick one ordinary object at home and write what its next IoT stage would add — sensing, reporting, or acting alone.
