# Hands-on — SaaS — software you subscribe to, not install

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Your SaaS census
List the software you use this week. Mark each: installed-and-owned, or hosted-and-subscribed? For every hosted one, write who updates it and what you would lose if your subscription stopped tomorrow.

## Benefit matchmaker
Three customers arrive: an office that wants zero technical work, a developer team that wants faster releases, a growing firm that wants flexible pay-as-you-go computing. Write each one a one-line recommendation — model plus its chief benefit.
