# SaaS — software you subscribe to, not install — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** SaaS means a vendor —
a) sells you a box to install  b) hosts applications and makes them available over the internet  c) repairs your computer  d) prints your documents
**Answer: b) hosts applications and makes them available over the internet**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** SaaS is paid for by —
a) one upfront purchase only  b) monthly or yearly subscription  c) barter  d) never paying
**Answer: b) monthly or yearly subscription**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** SaaS works on —
a) one named computer only  b) any internet-enabled device  c) paper  d) devices without internet
**Answer: b) any internet-enabled device**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Which list is all SaaS examples from the book?
a) Google Workspace, Dropbox, Slack, Concur  b) a hammer, a saw, a nail, a plank  c) soil, seed, rain, sun  d) a bus, a road, a bridge, a river
**Answer: a) Google Workspace, Dropbox, Slack, Concur**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Per the guide, SaaS is the most popular business option because all you need is —
a) a browser and an internet connection  b) a server room  c) a programming team  d) a printing press
**Answer: a) a browser and an internet connection** — Teacher guide — evidence.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Per the guide, PaaS's chief benefit is —
a) developer productivity — faster development, more frequent releases  b) free storage  c) no internet needed  d) automatic accounting
**Answer: a) developer productivity — faster development, more frequent releases**

**7. (Sub lesson 2)** Per the guide, IaaS is cost-effective because it is —
a) owned forever  b) pay-as-you-go, with automated hardware deployment  c) printed  d) tax-free
**Answer: b) pay-as-you-go, with automated hardware deployment**
