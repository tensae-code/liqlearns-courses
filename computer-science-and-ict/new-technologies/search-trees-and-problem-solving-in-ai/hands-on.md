# Hands-on — Search Trees and Problem Solving in AI

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**State a problem** — State a small problem (a river crossing, a blocked sliding puzzle) in the four parts, in writing.

## Task two
**Grow a tree** — Grow the first two levels of a search tree for a 3-move puzzle and mark any goal you can already see.
