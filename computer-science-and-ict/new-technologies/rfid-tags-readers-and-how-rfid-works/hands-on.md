# Hands-on — RFID — Tags, Readers and How RFID Works

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Trace a tag** — Follow one tagged thing — a library book or toll card — through a read and a write, naming each part's act.

## Task two
**Barcode against RFID** — Make a two-column comparison for a school library: which would you choose, and what settles it?
