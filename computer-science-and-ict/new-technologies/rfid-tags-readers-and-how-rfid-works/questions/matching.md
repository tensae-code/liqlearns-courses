# RFID — Tags, Readers and How RFID Works — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What are RFID's three parts?** → **The tag, the reader and the application system**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does the tag hold?** → **The object's identity data**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does the reader do?** → **Sends radio waves and receives the tag's answer**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does the application system do?** → **Records and uses the identity — stock, entry, payment**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How do passive and active tags differ?** → **Passive tags are powered by the reader's wave; active tags carry a battery**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What happens in the write process?** → **Data is stored onto the tag**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What wakes a passive tag in reading?** → **The reader's radio wave**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does the system do with the tag's reply?** → **Matches it against its records and acts**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Name RFID's two advantages over barcodes.** → **No line of sight needed, and many tags read at once**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Name three places RFID has spread.** → **Shops, libraries and roads (or warehouses)**
