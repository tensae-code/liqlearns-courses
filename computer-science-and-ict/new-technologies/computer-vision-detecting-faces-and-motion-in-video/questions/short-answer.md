# Computer Vision — Detecting Faces and Motion in Video — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is practised in the first experiment?**
**Answer: Face detection alone, over images — as a recorded experiment**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name three honest result kinds to record.**
**Answer: Faces found, faces missed, and false faces**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is a detector's worth?**
**Answer: Its rate across many images — not its best picture**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does motion detection compare?**
**Answer: Each video frame with the one before**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does it report?**
**Answer: Movement — where the picture changed enough**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Does motion detection know what moved?**
**Answer: No — it notices the event, not the thing**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Give a use of pure motion detection.**
**Answer: A corridor camera that only needs to know the corridor changed**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) State the spectrum the pair teaches.**
**Answer: Detection names a thing; motion notices an event**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which library powers both experiments?**
**Answer: OpenCV**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which book teaches this?**
**Answer: Kuwait Grade 10 Information Technology Term 2 (Book L1), Unit 2 — AI Projects**
