# Prompt Design — Writing Instructions for Generative AI — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a prompt?**
**Answer: The instruction given to a generative AI**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What four things does a designed prompt say?**
**Answer: What is wanted, for whom, in what form, and with what limits**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why is the prompt the output's ceiling?**
**Answer: The AI continues the pattern the prompt establishes — literally**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Name three working moves of prompt design.**
**Answer: Be specific where it matters; give an example; ask in steps (also: revise and re-ask)**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is the output, read as?**
**Answer: The prompt's report card**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What do you do after reading it?**
**Answer: Change the instruction and ask again**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Why is prompt design taught in the AI unit?**
**Answer: Operating a generative system well is part of knowing what it is**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Give an output shape a prompt may demand.**
**Answer: A list, a paragraph, a table, code (any one)**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What should be left loose in a prompt?**
**Answer: Where variation is welcome**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which book teaches this?**
**Answer: Kuwait Grade 10 Information Technology Term 2 (Book L1), Unit 2**
