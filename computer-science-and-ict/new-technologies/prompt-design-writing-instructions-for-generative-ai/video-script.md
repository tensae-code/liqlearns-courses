# Video script — Prompt Design — Writing Instructions for Generative AI

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A **prompt** is the instruction given to a generative AI — and like every instruction, it can be vague or **designed**. A designed prompt says **what** is wanted, **for whom**, **in what form**, and with **what limits**: the topic and its treatment, the audience and their level, the output's shape (a list, a paragraph, a table, code), and the boundaries (length, style, what to avoid). The unit teaches design because the AI's pattern-following is literal: it continues the pattern the prompt establishes, so the prompt's quality is the output's ceiling.
The skills are a craftsman's set: be **specific** where the result matters and loose where variation is welcome; give an **example** of the wanted thing when words about it fail; ask in **steps** when the task is a journey; and **revise** — read the output as the prompt's report card, change the instruction, and ask again. Kuwait places prompt design inside the AI unit, not the writing unit, because it is a technology skill: operating a generative system well is part of knowing what it is.
