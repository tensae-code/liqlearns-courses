# Blockchain — Trusted Digital Records — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does a block hold?" is: Records, its own hash and the previous block's hash**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is a hash?" is: Each block storing the previous block's hash**
**Answer: False** — A short fingerprint computed from the contents

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What happens if an old record is altered?" is: Its hash changes and the chain link breaks — the tampering shows**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "How are new records added?" is: No one keeper can quietly rewrite the book**
**Answer: False** — As new blocks, by agreed rules — never by editing old ones

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What links the blocks into a chain?" is: Each block storing the previous block's hash**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does distributed mean here?" is: Cryptocurrency**
**Answer: False** — Many computers each hold a full copy of the chain

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Why does distribution remove the single-keeper problem?" is: No one keeper can quietly rewrite the book**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Where does trust come from in a blockchain?" is: Records, its own hash and the previous block's hash**
**Answer: False** — From the structure, not from a trusted person

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is the famous use of blockchain?" is: Cryptocurrency**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Name another use from the lesson." is: Its hash changes and the chain link breaks — the tampering shows**
**Answer: False** — Land records (certificates or supply chains also count)
