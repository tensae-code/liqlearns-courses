# Blockchain — Trusted Digital Records — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does a block hold?**
**Answer: Records, its own hash and the previous block's hash**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is a hash?**
**Answer: A short fingerprint computed from the contents**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What happens if an old record is altered?**
**Answer: Its hash changes and the chain link breaks — the tampering shows**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How are new records added?**
**Answer: As new blocks, by agreed rules — never by editing old ones**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What links the blocks into a chain?**
**Answer: Each block storing the previous block's hash**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does distributed mean here?**
**Answer: Many computers each hold a full copy of the chain**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Why does distribution remove the single-keeper problem?**
**Answer: No one keeper can quietly rewrite the book**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Where does trust come from in a blockchain?**
**Answer: From the structure, not from a trusted person**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the famous use of blockchain?**
**Answer: Cryptocurrency**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Name another use from the lesson.**
**Answer: Land records (certificates or supply chains also count)**
