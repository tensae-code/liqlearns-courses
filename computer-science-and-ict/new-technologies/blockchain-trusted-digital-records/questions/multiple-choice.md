# Blockchain — Trusted Digital Records — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does a block hold?**
a) Records, its own hash and the previous block's hash  b) A short fingerprint computed from the contents  c) Its hash changes and the chain link breaks — the tampering shows  d) As new blocks, by agreed rules — never by editing old ones
**Answer: a) Records, its own hash and the previous block's hash**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is a hash?**
a) Its hash changes and the chain link breaks — the tampering shows  b) A short fingerprint computed from the contents  c) As new blocks, by agreed rules — never by editing old ones  d) Each block storing the previous block's hash
**Answer: b) A short fingerprint computed from the contents**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What happens if an old record is altered?**
a) As new blocks, by agreed rules — never by editing old ones  b) Each block storing the previous block's hash  c) Its hash changes and the chain link breaks — the tampering shows  d) Many computers each hold a full copy of the chain
**Answer: c) Its hash changes and the chain link breaks — the tampering shows**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How are new records added?**
a) Each block storing the previous block's hash  b) Many computers each hold a full copy of the chain  c) No one keeper can quietly rewrite the book  d) As new blocks, by agreed rules — never by editing old ones
**Answer: d) As new blocks, by agreed rules — never by editing old ones**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What links the blocks into a chain?**
a) Each block storing the previous block's hash  b) Many computers each hold a full copy of the chain  c) No one keeper can quietly rewrite the book  d) From the structure, not from a trusted person
**Answer: a) Each block storing the previous block's hash**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does distributed mean here?**
a) No one keeper can quietly rewrite the book  b) Many computers each hold a full copy of the chain  c) From the structure, not from a trusted person  d) Cryptocurrency
**Answer: b) Many computers each hold a full copy of the chain**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Why does distribution remove the single-keeper problem?**
a) From the structure, not from a trusted person  b) Cryptocurrency  c) No one keeper can quietly rewrite the book  d) Land records (certificates or supply chains also count)
**Answer: c) No one keeper can quietly rewrite the book**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Where does trust come from in a blockchain?**
a) Cryptocurrency  b) Land records (certificates or supply chains also count)  c) Records, its own hash and the previous block's hash  d) From the structure, not from a trusted person
**Answer: d) From the structure, not from a trusted person**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the famous use of blockchain?**
a) Cryptocurrency  b) Land records (certificates or supply chains also count)  c) Records, its own hash and the previous block's hash  d) A short fingerprint computed from the contents
**Answer: a) Cryptocurrency**
