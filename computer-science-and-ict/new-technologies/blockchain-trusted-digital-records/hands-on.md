# Hands-on — Blockchain — Trusted Digital Records

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Chain on paper
Make three paper blocks, each with two records and a made-up fingerprint that includes the previous block's fingerprint. Change a record in block 1 and show exactly which link breaks.

## Who keeps the book?
Pick one record type from the lesson (a certificate, say). Write two columns: who keeps it today and how it could be changed; how a blockchain copy changes both answers.
