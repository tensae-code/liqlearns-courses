# Requirements for Using Cloud Computing — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is the cloud's only road?**
**Answer: A reliable internet connection of enough speed**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What must devices be able to run?**
**Answer: A browser or the service's apps**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does identity management fix?**
**Answer: Who each user is and what each may reach**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What compatibility must be checked?**
**Answer: Formats and services partners can also open**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) When are the requirements knowable?**
**Answer: Before the move**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name the four human/organisational requirements.**
**Answer: Skills, support, policies, and a plan for failure**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What must policies decide?**
**Answer: What may go to the cloud, what must not, who decides**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Give the failure plan's example.**
**Answer: The connection dying in the middle of exam week**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How is cloud cost counted honestly?**
**Answer: Subscriptions are a rent forever, weighed against the purchase replaced**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is a cloud adopted without its requirements?**
**Answer: Merely moved fragility**
