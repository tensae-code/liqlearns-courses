# AI Search Algorithms and Problem Representation — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is problem representation?**
**Answer: Choosing how states, actions and goals are written for a computer**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does a good representation keep?**
**Answer: Everything the solution depends on, and nothing else**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does the city map drop?**
**Answer: The colour of the houses**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Where is an algorithm's speed half-decided?**
**Answer: In the representation, before searching begins**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does a state record?**
**Answer: What the solution depends on**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is a search algorithm?**
**Answer: A strategy for exploring the tree — which node next, what to remember, when to stop**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Name the four judging measures.**
**Answer: Completeness, optimality, time, space**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is completeness?**
**Answer: Finding a solution if one exists**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is optimality?**
**Answer: The found solution being the best**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Name the two families ahead.**
**Answer: Blind search and heuristic search**
