# Robots and Humans — What Is the Difference? — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name three things robots do better.**
**Answer: Repeat exactly without tiring; calculate faster; work in dangerous or dull places (also: sense some things finer; never sleep)**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does a robot never need?**
**Answer: Sleep**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How does the honest comparison start?**
**Answer: By honouring the machine — naming its real superiorities**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) In what are robots superior?**
**Answer: In named tasks**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does a flattering difference-list teach?**
**Answer: Nothing**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name three things that stay human.**
**Answer: Feeling with others; judging what matters; inventing unprogrammed questions (also: caring; answering for one's acts)**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does comforting a friend show?**
**Answer: Feeling with others — without instructions**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Who is the human beside a robot?**
**Answer: Its author and its judge — not its rival**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How should pupils meet robots?**
**Answer: Without fear or worship**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is a robot, in the parcours' phrase?**
**Answer: A powerful tool with a shape**
