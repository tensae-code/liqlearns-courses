# Robots and Humans — What Is the Difference? — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Repeat exactly without tiring; calculate faster; work in dangerous or dull places (also: sense some things finer; never sleep)**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Repeat exactly without tiring; calculate faster; work in dangerous or dull places (also: sense some things finer; never sleep)** — as the answer to: What does a robot never need?
**Answer: False — Sleep**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: By honouring the machine — naming its real superiorities**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: By honouring the machine — naming its real superiorities** — as the answer to: In what are robots superior?
**Answer: False — In named tasks**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Nothing**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Nothing** — as the answer to: Name three things that stay human.
**Answer: False — Feeling with others; judging what matters; inventing unprogrammed questions (also: caring; answering for one's acts)**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Feeling with others — without instructions**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Feeling with others — without instructions** — as the answer to: Who is the human beside a robot?
**Answer: False — Its author and its judge — not its rival**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Without fear or worship**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Without fear or worship** — as the answer to: What is a robot, in the parcours' phrase?
**Answer: False — A powerful tool with a shape**
