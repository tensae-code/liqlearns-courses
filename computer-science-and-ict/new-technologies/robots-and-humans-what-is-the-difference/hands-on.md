# Hands-on — Robots and Humans — What Is the Difference?

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Two columns, honestly** — Build the class two-column chart for one real robot (a vacuum robot, Thymio, a factory arm): three honest wins per column, each tied to a named task.

## Task two
**Teach the robot to comfort** — Act it out: a “robot” pupil may only follow written rules while a “friend” is sad. The class writes rules, watches them fail kindly, and names what no rule captured.
