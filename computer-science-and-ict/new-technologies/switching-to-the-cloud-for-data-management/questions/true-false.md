# Switching to the Cloud for Data Management — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does switching to the cloud mean for data management?" is: The organisation's stored files and records move from its own disks to a cloud storage service**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "How is cloud data reached?" is: Google Drive, Dropbox and SpiderOak**
**Answer: False** — Over the internet

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Which services has Fiji named before the switch?" is: Google Drive, Dropbox and SpiderOak**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What happens on the day of the switch?" is: The service provider's — not the school's**
**Answer: False** — Accounts are made and folders uploaded — the service's computers hold the working copy

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Whose computers hold the data after switching?" is: The service provider's — not the school's**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Name the switch's three gains." is: The internet connection, and the provider**
**Answer: False** — Data reached from anywhere; sharing by link; storage that grows without buying disks

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What two things does cloud data now depend on?" is: The internet connection, and the provider**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What must be managed as carefully as filing-room keys once were?" is: Balanced — switch for the reach, with eyes open about whose hands the data rests in**
**Answer: False** — Accounts and permissions

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is Fiji's verdict shape?" is: Balanced — switch for the reach, with eyes open about whose hands the data rests in**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What chapter does the switch close?" is: The organisation's stored files and records move from its own disks to a cloud storage service**
**Answer: False** — Fiji's Year 12 cloud chapter
