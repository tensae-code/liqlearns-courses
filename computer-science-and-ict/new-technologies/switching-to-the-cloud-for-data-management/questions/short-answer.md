# Switching to the Cloud for Data Management — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does switching to the cloud mean for data management?**
**Answer: The organisation's stored files and records move from its own disks to a cloud storage service**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How is cloud data reached?**
**Answer: Over the internet**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Which services has Fiji named before the switch?**
**Answer: Google Drive, Dropbox and SpiderOak**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What happens on the day of the switch?**
**Answer: Accounts are made and folders uploaded — the service's computers hold the working copy**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Whose computers hold the data after switching?**
**Answer: The service provider's — not the school's**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name the switch's three gains.**
**Answer: Data reached from anywhere; sharing by link; storage that grows without buying disks**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What two things does cloud data now depend on?**
**Answer: The internet connection, and the provider**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What must be managed as carefully as filing-room keys once were?**
**Answer: Accounts and permissions**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is Fiji's verdict shape?**
**Answer: Balanced — switch for the reach, with eyes open about whose hands the data rests in**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What chapter does the switch close?**
**Answer: Fiji's Year 12 cloud chapter**
