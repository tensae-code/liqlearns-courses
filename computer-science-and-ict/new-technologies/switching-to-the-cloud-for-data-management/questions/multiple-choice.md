# Switching to the Cloud for Data Management — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does switching to the cloud mean for data management?**
a) The organisation's stored files and records move from its own disks to a cloud storage service  b) Over the internet  c) Google Drive, Dropbox and SpiderOak  d) Accounts are made and folders uploaded — the service's computers hold the working copy
**Answer: a) The organisation's stored files and records move from its own disks to a cloud storage service**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How is cloud data reached?**
a) Google Drive, Dropbox and SpiderOak  b) Over the internet  c) Accounts are made and folders uploaded — the service's computers hold the working copy  d) The service provider's — not the school's
**Answer: b) Over the internet**
