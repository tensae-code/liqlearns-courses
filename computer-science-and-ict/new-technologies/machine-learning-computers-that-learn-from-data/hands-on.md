# Hands-on — Machine Learning — computers that learn from data

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Teach by examples
Show a partner ten small stones, sorted into 'smooth' and 'rough' piles, without saying the rule. Then hand them a new stone to sort. They learned from examples — like a model training.

## Spot the application
For one day, note every time a phone suggests, filters or recognises something. Label each: recommendation, spam, speech or fraud.
