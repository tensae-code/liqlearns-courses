# Hands-on — Evaluating a Model and Model Ethics

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Work the book's own example** — Unit 5 §5.3.2 Evaluating and Interpreting Models (§5.3.2.1 performance metrics — error and accuracy; §5.3.2.2 interpreting outputs; §5.3.2.3 ethical considerations — fairness and bias, data privacy) Work it with the book open first, step by step, then close the book and do it again.

## Task two
**Show it to someone else** — Teach evaluating a model and model ethics to a companion in your own words, with your worked example in front of you, and answer their one question from the lesson.
