# Hands-on — Training and Optimising an AI System

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Train a fruit sorter honestly
Collect 20 real examples (fruit, leaves, coins — anything two-kinded) with labels. Split them 14 training / 6 test, test set face down. Invent one measurable rule from the training set only ('heavier than X and round → apple'). Score it on training, then turn over the test set and score honestly. If the scores differ, name what your rule memorised instead of learned.

## Tune one knob
Take the rule from the first activity and change exactly one setting (the threshold). Retrain your judgement on the training set, re-measure on a fresh set of 6 examples a partner kept hidden from you. Write which setting generalised better — you have done one honest optimisation step.
