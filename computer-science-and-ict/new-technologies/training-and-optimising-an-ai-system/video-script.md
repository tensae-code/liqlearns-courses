# Video script — Training and Optimising an AI System

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

An AI system is built like a student is taught. First: examples with answers.

Most examples are for learning. Some are locked away, unseen. Those are the test.

Training means: try, measure the error, adjust. Again and again.

Then the honest exam: the unseen test. That score is the true one.

A system can cheat without meaning to: it memorises the practice, and fails the fresh questions. That is overfitting.

Optimising means tuning the settings — and judging only by the honest score.
