# Training and Optimising an AI System — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 2) Judging a system on the data it trained on gives an honest measure of what it learned.**
**Answer: False** — It measures memorisation of practice; only unseen data measures learning.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Mislabelled training examples are learned just as faithfully as correct ones.**
**Answer: True** — Data quality is destiny: the procedure cannot tell a wrong label from a right one.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Underfitting means the model memorised too much detail.**
**Answer: False** — Underfitting is the opposite failure — too simple to catch the pattern at all.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Each optimisation trial should be judged by test performance, not training performance.**
**Answer: True** — The honest score is the judge at every tuning step.
