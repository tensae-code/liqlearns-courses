# Training and Optimising an AI System — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Examples with their correct answers attached are called ____ data.**
**Answer: labelled**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) The held-back, never-trained-on part of the data is the ____ data.**
**Answer: test**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) A system that memorises its practice examples instead of learning the pattern is ____.**
**Answer: overfitting**
