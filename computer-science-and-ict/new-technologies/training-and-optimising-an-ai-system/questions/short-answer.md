# Training and Optimising an AI System — short answer

Answer in your own words, in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 2) Explain overfitting using a human examination candidate.**
**Answer: The candidate memorises last year's paper word for word and scores perfectly on it, then fails a fresh paper on the same topic. Just so, an overfitted system perfects the training examples — quirks included — and sags on unseen examples, because it stored answers instead of learning the pattern.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Why must the test data stay locked away not only during training but also between optimisation trials?**
**Answer: Every judgement made against the test data steers the builder's choices, so repeated tuning against it quietly trains the design on it. The set then stops being unseen in effect, and its score stops being honest.**
