# First Arduino Applications — and the Tinkercad Simulator — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is the first application?**
**Answer: A lamp switched by its pin — on, wait, off**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is the second?**
**Answer: Chase lights with two lamps, alternating in a running pattern**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is the program, in timing work?**
**Answer: The sequence of pin states and the waits between them**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What do the waits change?**
**Answer: The dance's speed — slower or faster**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which unit teaches it?**
**Answer: Greece Grade 9 Pliroforiki C, Unit 3**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is the Tinkercad simulator?**
**Answer: The same bench on screen — virtual Arduino and components, wired and run in simulation**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What can be done there at no cost?**
**Answer: Test, break and repair the circuit**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is Greece's order?**
**Answer: Simulate first, then wire the real board with a proved design**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does the simulator not do?**
**Answer: Replace the board — it makes the board's minutes count**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is a burnt lamp in simulation?**
**Answer: Nothing — mistakes cost nothing there**
