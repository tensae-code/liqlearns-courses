# Biotechnology — Technology with Living Things — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Biotechnology uses what?**
a) Living organisms and cells  b) Life — living things  c) Using yeast in fermentation  d) Genes and DNA
**Answer: a) Living organisms and cells**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) 'Bio' relates to what?**
a) Using yeast in fermentation  b) Life — living things  c) Genes and DNA  d) Medicines and improved crops
**Answer: b) Life — living things**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Give an old example of biotechnology.**
a) Genes and DNA  b) Medicines and improved crops  c) Using yeast in fermentation  d) Better and healthier crops and animals
**Answer: c) Using yeast in fermentation**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Modern biotechnology can work with what?**
a) Medicines and improved crops  b) Better and healthier crops and animals  c) Drugs and vaccines  d) Genes and DNA
**Answer: d) Genes and DNA**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Name a kind of biotechnology product.**
a) Medicines and improved crops  b) Better and healthier crops and animals  c) Drugs and vaccines  d) Fermentation products such as yoghurt
**Answer: a) Medicines and improved crops**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Farming uses biotechnology for what?**
a) Drugs and vaccines  b) Better and healthier crops and animals  c) Fermentation products such as yoghurt  d) Safely and responsibly
**Answer: b) Better and healthier crops and animals**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Medicine uses biotechnology for what?**
a) Fermentation products such as yoghurt  b) Safely and responsibly  c) Drugs and vaccines  d) Technology — the design-and-build strand
**Answer: c) Drugs and vaccines**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) The food industry uses biotechnology for what?**
a) Safely and responsibly  b) Technology — the design-and-build strand  c) Living organisms and cells  d) Fermentation products such as yoghurt
**Answer: d) Fermentation products such as yoghurt**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Biotechnology must be used how?**
a) Safely and responsibly  b) Technology — the design-and-build strand  c) Living organisms and cells  d) Life — living things
**Answer: a) Safely and responsibly**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Biotechnology sits inside which Papua New Guinea study area?**
a) Living organisms and cells  b) Technology — the design-and-build strand  c) Life — living things  d) Using yeast in fermentation
**Answer: b) Technology — the design-and-build strand**
