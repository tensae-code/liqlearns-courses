# Hands-on — GIS Concepts and Vector Data

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Three shapes walk', 'Walk a short route near school. List six things you pass and sort each: point, line or polygon — with the map scale you are imagining written beside your list.')

## Task two
('Attribute it', 'Choose one feature from your walk. Write its attribute table row: name, kind, and two facts a GIS would keep about it.')
