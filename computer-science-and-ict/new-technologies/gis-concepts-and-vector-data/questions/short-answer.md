# GIS Concepts and Vector Data — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a feature in GIS?**
**Answer: One real thing on the map — a school, a road, a lake**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What are features grouped into?**
**Answer: Layers, by kind**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What are attributes?**
**Answer: Facts about a feature, kept in the attribute table**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does the map's scale tie together?**
**Answer: Map distance and ground distance**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Name the five key words of the lesson.**
**Answer: Feature, layer, attribute, scale, location**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What are the three kinds of vector data?**
**Answer: Points, lines and polygons**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does a point mark?**
**Answer: A place with no size at map scale — a school, a borehole**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does a line follow?**
**Answer: A path — a road, a river**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does a polygon close?**
**Answer: A boundary around an area — a district, a lake, a parcel**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How can the same village be two shapes?**
**Answer: A point on a national map, a polygon on a district map**
