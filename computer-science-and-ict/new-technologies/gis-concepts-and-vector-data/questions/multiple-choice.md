# GIS Concepts and Vector Data — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a feature in GIS?**
a) One real thing on the map — a school, a road, a lake  b) Layers, by kind  c) Facts about a feature, kept in the attribute table  d) Map distance and ground distance
**Answer: a) One real thing on the map — a school, a road, a lake**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What are features grouped into?**
a) Facts about a feature, kept in the attribute table  b) Layers, by kind  c) Map distance and ground distance  d) Feature, layer, attribute, scale, location
**Answer: b) Layers, by kind**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What are attributes?**
a) Map distance and ground distance  b) Feature, layer, attribute, scale, location  c) Facts about a feature, kept in the attribute table  d) One real thing on the map — a school, a road, a lake
**Answer: c) Facts about a feature, kept in the attribute table**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does the map's scale tie together?**
a) Feature, layer, attribute, scale, location  b) One real thing on the map — a school, a road, a lake  c) Layers, by kind  d) Map distance and ground distance
**Answer: d) Map distance and ground distance**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Name the five key words of the lesson.**
a) Feature, layer, attribute, scale, location  b) One real thing on the map — a school, a road, a lake  c) Layers, by kind  d) Facts about a feature, kept in the attribute table
**Answer: a) Feature, layer, attribute, scale, location**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What are the three kinds of vector data?**
a) A place with no size at map scale — a school, a borehole  b) Points, lines and polygons  c) A path — a road, a river  d) A boundary around an area — a district, a lake, a parcel
**Answer: b) Points, lines and polygons**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does a point mark?**
a) A path — a road, a river  b) A boundary around an area — a district, a lake, a parcel  c) A place with no size at map scale — a school, a borehole  d) A point on a national map, a polygon on a district map
**Answer: c) A place with no size at map scale — a school, a borehole**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does a line follow?**
a) A boundary around an area — a district, a lake, a parcel  b) A point on a national map, a polygon on a district map  c) Points, lines and polygons  d) A path — a road, a river
**Answer: d) A path — a road, a river**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does a polygon close?**
a) A boundary around an area — a district, a lake, a parcel  b) A point on a national map, a polygon on a district map  c) Points, lines and polygons  d) A place with no size at map scale — a school, a borehole
**Answer: a) A boundary around an area — a district, a lake, a parcel**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How can the same village be two shapes?**
a) Points, lines and polygons  b) A point on a national map, a polygon on a district map  c) A place with no size at map scale — a school, a borehole  d) A path — a road, a river
**Answer: b) A point on a national map, a polygon on a district map**
