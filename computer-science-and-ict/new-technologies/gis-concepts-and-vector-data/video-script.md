# Video script — GIS Concepts and Vector Data

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The S1 unit's key concepts: a **feature** is one real thing on the map — a school, a road, a lake. Features are grouped into **layers** by kind. Each feature carries **attributes** — facts about it, kept in the layer's **attribute table**. The map's **scale** ties map distance to ground distance, and every feature sits at a **location** fixed by coordinates. Feature, layer, attribute, scale, location: the five words the whole GIS strand uses.
**Vector data** draws the world with three shapes. A **point** marks a place with no size at map scale — a school, a borehole. A **line** follows a path — a road, a river. A **polygon** closes a boundary around an area — a district, a lake, a land parcel. Choosing the shape is choosing what the thing *is* on the map: the same village is a point on a national map and a polygon on a district map.
