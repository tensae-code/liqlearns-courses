# Programming a Small Robot — Ozobot — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is Ozobot?** → **A small robot — sensors and wheels in a palm-sized dome — that follows lines and obeys colour codes**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What comes before programming, in the book's order?** → **Care (perawatan), then calibration (kalibrasi)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does calibration teach the robot?** → **This paper's white and this line's black — so its light sensor reads truly here**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) An uncalibrated robot does not disobey; it does what?** → **It misreads**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is Ozobot's first language?** → **Drawn colour codes on a line it follows**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is a colour code?** → **A short sequence of coloured segments laid across the line — a command**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What two families of commands do the codes give?** → **Direction at crossings, and speed**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is programming, in this medium?** → **Drawing the right code at the right place on the track**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How is an Ozobot program debugged?** → **By watching where the robot's reading and the drawer's intention part**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Where does Indonesia teach this?** → **Grade 8, Bab 3 F — introducing the Ozobot robot**
