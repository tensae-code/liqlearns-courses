# Programming a Small Robot — Ozobot — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is Ozobot?**
**Answer: A small robot — sensors and wheels in a palm-sized dome — that follows lines and obeys colour codes**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What comes before programming, in the book's order?**
**Answer: Care (perawatan), then calibration (kalibrasi)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does calibration teach the robot?**
**Answer: This paper's white and this line's black — so its light sensor reads truly here**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) An uncalibrated robot does not disobey; it does what?**
**Answer: It misreads**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is Ozobot's first language?**
**Answer: Drawn colour codes on a line it follows**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is a colour code?**
**Answer: A short sequence of coloured segments laid across the line — a command**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What two families of commands do the codes give?**
**Answer: Direction at crossings, and speed**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is programming, in this medium?**
**Answer: Drawing the right code at the right place on the track**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How is an Ozobot program debugged?**
**Answer: By watching where the robot's reading and the drawer's intention part**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Where does Indonesia teach this?**
**Answer: Grade 8, Bab 3 F — introducing the Ozobot robot**
