# Hands-on — Programming a Small Robot — Ozobot

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Draw a program** — On paper, draw a line track with two crossings. Place colour codes so a robot would turn left at the first and slow down after the second; write beside each code what it commands.

## Task two
**Calibrate and predict** — List the calibration steps in order for a new sheet of paper and a brighter room. Then predict: what will an uncalibrated robot do at a grey, washed-out code?
