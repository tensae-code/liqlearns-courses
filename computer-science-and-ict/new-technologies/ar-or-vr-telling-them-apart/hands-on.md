# Hands-on — AR or VR? Telling them apart

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sort the cases
Write eight short cases (phone labels, headset worlds, smart glasses, simulator…) on cards. Sort into AR / VR piles, naming the deciding row for each.

## Teach the table
Cover one column of the comparison. Rebuild it aloud from the other column's contrasts.
