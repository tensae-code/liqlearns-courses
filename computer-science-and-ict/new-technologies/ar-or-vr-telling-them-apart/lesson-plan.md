# Lesson plan — AR or VR? Telling them apart

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** New Technologies · **Lesson:** AR or VR? Telling them apart
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 2 — Emerging Technologies (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** AR works in the real world, partly immersive, on familiar devices, blending real and virtual; VR works in a fully virtual world, fully immersive, under the system's control, through a headset.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the Grade 11 Emerging Technologies unit around it. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or in steps — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Give all five contrasts: setting, immersion, control, devices, content.
2. Sort any new example into AR or VR with reasons.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — Two learners put on two devices. One still sees her classroom, with labels floating over the desks. The other sees only a jungle that is not there. Which is which? Decide first. Then read the comparison. (lesson/lesson.md).
2. **The explanation** — the parts below, in order (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 16513]: a picture of the comparison redrawn as a clean two-column chart — the student reads one row across and says both sides of the contrast.
   - ADD AUDIO: a voice reading this lesson's key words aloud — the student listens, then says each word and what it means.
3. **Say it to your companion** — explain the lesson back in your own words (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD VIDEO: the script in video-script.md, filmed — the lesson retold in the super-simple voice (made later, when videos are made).
4. **Questions** — from questions/: multiple-choice, true-or-false, fill-in-the-blank, short-answer — served at the learner's level (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** — the doing block at the end of lesson/lesson.md.

## If the learner is stuck
- Sorts by 'has a screen' → both have screens; sort by setting and immersion. Thinks AR needs special kit → AR runs on a smartphone; the headset marks VR.
