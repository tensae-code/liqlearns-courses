# AR or VR? Telling them apart

## Do it first

Two learners put on two devices. One still sees her classroom, with labels floating over the desks. The other sees only a jungle that is not there. Which is which? Decide first. Then read the comparison.

## The comparison — five contrasts

**Setting:** AR works in a **real-world setting**; VR in a **completely virtual world**. **Immersion:** AR is **partially immersive**; VR is **fully immersive**. **Control:** AR users **control their presence in the real world**; VR users are **under the system's control**. **Devices:** AR runs on **familiar devices such as smartphones**; VR **requires a headset**. **Content:** AR **blends real and virtual**; VR is **only fictional / simulated reality**.

## A quick test for any case

Ask three questions. Can the user still see the real room? (Yes → AR.) Is a headset required? (Yes → VR.) Who controls the presence — the user in the real world, or the system? The three answers together never disagree.

## Say it to your companion

Quiz your companion: you describe a case, they answer AR or VR and give two reasons.
