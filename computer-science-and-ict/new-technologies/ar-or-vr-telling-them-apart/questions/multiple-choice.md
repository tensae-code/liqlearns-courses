# AR or VR? Telling them apart — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** AR works in —
a) a real-world setting  b) a fully virtual world  c) dreams  d) printed books
**Answer: a) a real-world setting**

**2.** VR immersion is —
a) full  b) partial  c) none  d) audio only
**Answer: a) full**

**3.** Who controls presence in VR?
a) the system  b) the user in the real world  c) a teacher  d) nobody
**Answer: a) the system**

**4.** AR runs on —
a) familiar devices such as smartphones  b) only headsets  c) only televisions  d) paper
**Answer: a) familiar devices such as smartphones**

**5.** VR content is —
a) only fictional / simulated reality  b) real and virtual blended  c) only text  d) only photos
**Answer: a) only fictional / simulated reality**
