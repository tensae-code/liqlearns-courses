# AR or VR? Telling them apart — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** Give three contrasts in one breath.
**Answer:** Any three of: real vs fully virtual setting; partial vs full immersion; user control vs system control; smartphone vs headset; blended vs only simulated content.

**2.** A phone game lays creatures over your street. AR or VR, and why?
**Answer:** AR — real-world setting, blended content, runs on a familiar device.
