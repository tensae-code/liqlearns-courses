# IoT applications — home, city, health, cars and farms — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A pet monitoring camera belongs to —
a) smart city  b) smart home  c) supply chain  d) connected cars
**Answer: b) smart home**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Traffic management, waste management and water distribution belong to —
a) smart home  b) smart city  c) connected health  d) smart outlets
**Answer: b) smart city**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Real-time goods tracking serves the —
a) supply chain  b) smart home  c) hospital  d) farm only
**Answer: a) supply chain**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Heart rate, temperature and activity levels are tracked in —
a) connected cars  b) connected health  c) smart outlets  d) smart city
**Answer: b) connected health**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** Automatic billing, insurance and parking belong to —
a) smart outlets  b) connected cars  c) supply chain  d) smart home
**Answer: b) connected cars**

**6. (Sub lesson 1)** A smart outlet can —
a) switch a device on/off remotely and track its energy  b) cook food  c) print documents  d) drive a car
**Answer: a) switch a device on/off remotely and track its energy**

**7. (Sub lesson 2)** Per the guide, the connected home includes —
a) lighting control, locks, and energy and water meters  b) only a television  c) only printed books  d) a bus stop
**Answer: a) lighting control, locks, and energy and water meters** — Teacher guide — evidence.

**8. (Sub lesson 2)** Per the guide, for each home use pupils must identify —
a) its price only  b) the devices and connectivity it needs  c) its colour  d) its inventor
**Answer: b) the devices and connectivity it needs**
