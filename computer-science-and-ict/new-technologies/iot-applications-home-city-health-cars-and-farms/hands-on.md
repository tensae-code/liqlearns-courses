# Hands-on — IoT applications — home, city, health, cars and farms

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Application census
Walk your home or school block and census every IoT application you can honestly find or imagine in place — meters, locks, cameras, trackers. File each under the lesson's six areas, and note the ones your area does not have yet.

## Design one room
Design one fully connected room on paper — a sick person's bedroom, a baby's room, a guard's post. Place at least five items from the guide's home inventory, and label each with its device and its connectivity.
