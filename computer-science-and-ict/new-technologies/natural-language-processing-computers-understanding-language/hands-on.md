# Hands-on — Natural Language Processing — computers understanding language

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sentiment sort
Write six short opinion sentences — two positive, two negative, two neutral. Swap with a partner and sort theirs. That sorting is sentiment analysis, done by hand.

## Translate and check
Translate one sentence with a phone translator, then translate it back. What changed? That gap is the NLP problem.
