# Natural Language Processing — computers understanding language

## What NLP is

**Natural Language Processing (NLP)** is the branch of AI that lets computers **understand written and spoken human language as people do**. 'Natural' means the language people naturally use — your sentences, not programming code.

[Picture 16505 — NLP — language in, meaning out]

## Its six named jobs

NLP works in six named places: **spam detection in email** (is this message junk?), **machine translation such as Google Translate**, **voice virtual assistants such as Alexa and Siri**, **sentiment analysis** of social-media opinion — is it **positive, negative or neutral**? — **fake-news identification**, and **automatic summarisation** of long documents.
