# Charts in Python — Line, Bar and Pie — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is the line chart's job?**
**Answer: A measure over time — its slope is the story**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is the bar chart's job?**
**Answer: Comparing kinds, lengths honestly from zero**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is the pie chart's only job?**
**Answer: Parts of one whole**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is a pie of non-summing things?**
**Answer: A decorated lie**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Whose work is choosing the chart?**
**Answer: The analyst's — matching chart to question**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name five patterns to read.**
**Answer: Trend, season, peak or dip, gap between groups, outlier**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is a season in a chart?**
**Answer: A rhythm that repeats**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What three steps discipline a reading?**
**Answer: State the pattern, check it against numbers, ask what else could draw it**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is a chart's pattern, in the closing phrase?**
**Answer: A claim — and claims are checked**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What must a peak or dip send you to find?**
**Answer: The event that explains it**
