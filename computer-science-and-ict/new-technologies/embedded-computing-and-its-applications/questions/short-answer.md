# Embedded Computing and Its Applications — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is an embedded system?**
**Answer: A computer built inside a device to control that device's one job**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How does it differ from a general computer?**
**Answer: It runs its fixed program, not whatever the user invents**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does real time mean here?**
**Answer: A late answer is a wrong answer**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) By what three things are embedded systems judged?**
**Answer: Reliability, cost and power**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Give the lesson's real-time example.**
**Answer: The airbag, which cannot compute after the crash**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Why is embedded computing the majority of computing?**
**Answer: Most computers live inside devices, not on desks**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How many embedded controllers can a car hold?**
**Answer: Dozens**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Name four application areas.**
**Answer: Home appliances, cars, medical devices, industrial robots (or smart cards, lifts, printers)**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is an IoT thing, in this lesson's phrase?**
**Answer: An embedded system that gained sensors and a network**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is embedded computing to IoT?**
**Answer: Its body — connectivity is its voice**
