# Data Analysis and Its Stages — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is data analysis?**
**Answer: Turning data into findings to discover patterns, answer questions and support decisions**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) By what is analysis defined?**
**Answer: Its destination — a decision or an answer — not its tools**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name three tools the same stages run in.**
**Answer: A notebook, a spreadsheet, Python**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is each later lesson in the unit?**
**Answer: One stage, done properly**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What four verbs define the work?**
**Answer: Inspecting, cleaning, transforming, modelling**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name the stages in order.**
**Answer: Define the question, collect, clean and prepare, explore and analyse, present and interpret, decide and act**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Why are the stages a cycle?**
**Answer: Findings send you back to collect better data**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What starts the cycle again?**
**Answer: Deciding and acting, with a sharper question**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What do honest analysts say?**
**Answer: When they have gone back a stage**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which stage carries the faults craft?**
**Answer: Clean and prepare**
