## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **What data analysis is** — **Data analysis** is the work of turning data into findings: inspecting, cleaning, transforming and modelling data to **discover patterns, answer questions and support decisions**. It is defined by its destination — a decision or an answer — not by its tools; the same stages run in a notebook, a spreadsheet or Python. Jordan opens the unit with the whole process because each later lesson is one stage, done properly.
2. **The stages of the process** — The stages in order: **define the question** (what decision, what answer is needed), **collect** the data the question needs, **clean and prepare** it (the faults lesson's whole craft), **explore and analyse** (summaries, comparisons, models — patterns found and tested), **present and interpret** (charts and words a decision-maker can use), and **decide and act**, whose results start the cycle again with a sharper question. The stages are a cycle, not a staircase: findings send you back to collect better data, and honest analysts say when they have gone back.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
