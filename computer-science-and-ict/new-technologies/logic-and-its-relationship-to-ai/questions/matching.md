# Logic and Its Relationship to AI — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does logic study?** → **Arguments — when conclusions truly follow from premises**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name a premise indicator and a conclusion indicator.** → **'Because' (or 'since'); 'therefore' (or 'so')**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What makes an argument valid?** → **Its conclusion must follow from its premises**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What question does logic NOT ask?** → **Whether the premises are true**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is logic's colder question?** → **If the premises were true, would the conclusion have to be?**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Why does AI need logic?** → **A machine that knows must also conclude, by rule**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What three gifts does logic give AI?** → **Knowledge representation, inference, and an honesty check**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What can a validly drawn conclusion from true premises never be?** → **False**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) In the bird example, what is produced by rule?** → **The new fact — this bird flies**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What two working forms come next?** → **Propositional logic and predicate logic**
