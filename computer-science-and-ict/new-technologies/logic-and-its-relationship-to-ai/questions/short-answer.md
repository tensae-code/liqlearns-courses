# Logic and Its Relationship to AI — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does logic study?**
**Answer: Arguments — when conclusions truly follow from premises**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name a premise indicator and a conclusion indicator.**
**Answer: 'Because' (or 'since'); 'therefore' (or 'so')**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What makes an argument valid?**
**Answer: Its conclusion must follow from its premises**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What question does logic NOT ask?**
**Answer: Whether the premises are true**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is logic's colder question?**
**Answer: If the premises were true, would the conclusion have to be?**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Why does AI need logic?**
**Answer: A machine that knows must also conclude, by rule**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What three gifts does logic give AI?**
**Answer: Knowledge representation, inference, and an honesty check**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What can a validly drawn conclusion from true premises never be?**
**Answer: False**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) In the bird example, what is produced by rule?**
**Answer: The new fact — this bird flies**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What two working forms come next?**
**Answer: Propositional logic and predicate logic**
