# Data Centres — Where the Cloud Lives — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Power in a data centre arrives by ____ separate routes so one failure cannot stop it.**
**Answer: two — Doubled supply, UPS batteries and generators form the chain that never lets the servers feel a cut.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Energy used is power multiplied by ____.**
**Answer: time — Measured in kilowatt-hours: a kilowatt running for one hour.**
