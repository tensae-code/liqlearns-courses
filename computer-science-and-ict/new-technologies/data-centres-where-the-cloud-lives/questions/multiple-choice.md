# Data Centres — Where the Cloud Lives — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A UPS in a data centre is —**
a) A cooling fan  b) Batteries that deliver power instantly when the supply fails  c) A security guard  d) A type of server
**Answer: b) Batteries that deliver power instantly when the supply fails — They bridge the gap until the generators take over.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Why does a data centre need powerful cooling?**
a) Servers work better frozen  b) Thousands of machines turn electricity into heat that would destroy them  c) To keep the guards comfortable  d) To make rain
**Answer: b) Thousands of machines turn electricity into heat that would destroy them — Cooling is life-support, not comfort.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) A hall of servers draws 2 megawatts. Its consumption in one day (24 h), before cooling, is —**
a) 48 MWh  b) 2 MWh  c) 24 MWh  d) 200 MWh
**Answer: a) 48 MWh — Energy = power × time: 2 MW × 24 h = 48 megawatt-hours.**
