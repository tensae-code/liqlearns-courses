# Video script — Data Centres — Where the Cloud Lives

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The cloud is not a cloud. It is a building. A data centre.

Inside: rows and rows of servers. They hold your files and run your services, day and night.

They must never stop. So power comes by two routes, with batteries — the UPS — that bridge any cut in an instant, and generators behind them.

They must never cook. So cooling carries away the heat of thousands of machines.

They must never be robbed. So the building is guarded like a bank.

All of it burns electricity — to run, and again to cool. Remember that meter when you use the cloud. It is spinning in a real place.
