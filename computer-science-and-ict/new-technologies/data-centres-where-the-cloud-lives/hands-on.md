# Hands-on — Data Centres — Where the Cloud Lives

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The twenty-second cut
A centre loses city power for twenty seconds. Write the story of those seconds inside the building: what the UPS does at second zero, what starts up behind it, what the servers notice (nothing), and what would have happened at second twenty-one without the generators. 

## Do the meter sum
One server draws 300 watts. A rack holds 40 servers; a hall holds 100 racks. Work out the hall’s power draw, then its consumption for one day in kilowatt-hours — before cooling. If cooling adds half as much again, what is the true daily figure? Compare it with your home’s daily use from an electricity bill, if you have one.
