# Big data — and the privacy problem — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) Big data is data —
a) too large for traditional methods  b) on paper only  c) with no variety  d) that arrives slowly
**Answer: a) too large for traditional methods**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) The three Vs are —
a) volume, variety, velocity  b) video, voice, volume  c) value, variety, vision  d) volume, vacuum, velocity
**Answer: a) volume, variety, velocity**

**3.** (Sub lesson 1) Facebook's 2020 example was —
a) 4 petabytes a day  b) 4 gigabytes a day  c) 4 terabytes a year  d) 40 files a day
**Answer: a) 4 petabytes a day**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Anonymisation means —
a) removing personally identifiable information  b) adding names  c) selling data  d) deleting all data
**Answer: a) removing personally identifiable information**

**5.** (Sub lesson 2) Generalisation groups data into —
a) broad categories such as age groups and areas  b) exact addresses  c) full names  d) passwords
**Answer: a) broad categories such as age groups and areas**
