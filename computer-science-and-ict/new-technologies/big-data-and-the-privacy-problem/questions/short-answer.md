# Big data — and the privacy problem — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** (Sub lesson 2) Why does the tension 'remain' even with protections?
**Answer:** Because both protections limit insight: safer data says less, richer data risks privacy.

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Define big data without using the word 'big' twice.
**Answer:** Data too large to process by traditional methods, marked by volume, variety and velocity.
