# Hands-on — Big data — and the privacy problem

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Generalise your class
Collect one harmless fact (birth month) for the class two ways: exact list, then grouped into quarters of the year. Compare: what can each version tell? What can each version expose?

## Anonymise a record
Write a fake user record with name, age, area and hobby. Now anonymise it, then generalise it. Show all three versions and say what insight survived each step.
