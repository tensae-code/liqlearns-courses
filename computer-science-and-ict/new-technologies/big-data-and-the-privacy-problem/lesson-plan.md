# Lesson plan — Big data — and the privacy problem

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** New Technologies · **Lesson:** Big data — and the privacy problem
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 2 — Emerging Technologies (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** big data is data too large for traditional methods — volume, variety, velocity — and because using it means collecting masses of user data, privacy must be protected by anonymisation and generalisation, which themselves limit insight.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the Grade 11 Emerging Technologies unit around it. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. ## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Define big data and name the three Vs.
2. Give the scale example with its year.
3. Explain why privacy becomes contentious.
4. Define anonymisation and generalisation, and their shared cost.
