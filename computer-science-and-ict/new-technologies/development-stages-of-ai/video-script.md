# Video script — Development Stages of AI

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

AI begins as a named field in the **1950s** (the Dartmouth workshop, 1956), with early triumphs — programs proving theorems and playing checkers — and large promises. The promises outran the machines: by the **1970s** came the first **AI winter**, funding and faith withdrawn. A second flowering in the **1980s** — expert systems in real companies — met a second winter when their brittleness and upkeep showed. The pattern of the stages is itself the lesson: capability and claim must be told apart, in every decade.
The return came on three legs: **data** (the internet's oceans of it), **computing power** (chips that could train large models), and **machine learning** methods that improved with both. Landmarks followed — a chess champion beaten (1997), speech and images recognised reliably (2010s), language models conversing (2020s). Jordan's Grade 9 history ends with the pupil inside the story: the current stage is theirs to judge, with the winters as the standing warning against both despair and hype.
