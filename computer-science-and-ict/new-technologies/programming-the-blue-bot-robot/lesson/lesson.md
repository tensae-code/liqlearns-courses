# Programming the Blue-Bot Robot

## Do it first

Be the bee: take two steps forward, turn right like a clock hand, two more steps. A classmate reads the route back from the buttons they would press. That reading-back is Blue-Bot programming.

## Programming the Blue-Bot Robot

The book's programmable device is **Blue-Bot** — a small **bee robot** that drives on the floor and on mats. **"Роботът се движи" (The robot moves)** teaches its movement commands: **forward, back, left turn, right turn**, entered with the **buttons on the robot's back**, with **clear** wiping the old program and **go** running the entered route. The double lesson also meets the **Blue-Bot control environment (Среда за управление Blue Bot)** — the app/screen side where a route can be built and sent to the robot. The craft is exact counting: the robot moves in its own step-lengths and turns in quarter-turns, so a route must be planned in robot steps, not human strides — and a forgotten clear means yesterday's program drives today's route into the wall.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion, in your own words: The Blue-Bot is a bee-shaped floor robot: you program it with its buttons — forward, back, turns, go — or in its control environment, and it drives the route your commands describe. Then let your companion ask you one question about it — and answer from the lesson, not from memory of the book's exact words.

## Do It Yourself

1. Show it, don't just say it: name the Blue-Bot movement commands and what each does. Do this with a real example from your own work or your own school day, and write or say how you know it is right.
2. Show it, don't just say it: plan a route in robot steps and quarter-turns, and enter it. Do this with a real example from your own work or your own school day, and write or say how you know it is right.
3. Show it, don't just say it: clear the old program before entering a new route, and say why. Do this with a real example from your own work or your own school day, and write or say how you know it is right.
