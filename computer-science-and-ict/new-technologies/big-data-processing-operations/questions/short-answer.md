# Big Data Processing Operations — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the four operations.**
**Answer: Transformation, generalisation, simplification, visual representation**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does transformation change, and not change?**
**Answer: The data's shape for use — not its values**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does generalisation climb from and to?**
**Answer: From details to kinds**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Name two gains of generalisation.**
**Answer: Smaller, safer (less personal) — and often clearer**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is generalisation bought with?**
**Answer: Detail that can never be recovered**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name simplification's three ways.**
**Answer: Sampling, aggregating, selecting fields**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does visual representation turn data into?**
**Answer: Charts and maps the eye can judge**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What must each operation be?**
**Answer: Declared**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is a chart hiding its generalisations?**
**Answer: A claim hiding its evidence**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What road must stay walkable?**
**Answer: From raw to shown, by a checker**
