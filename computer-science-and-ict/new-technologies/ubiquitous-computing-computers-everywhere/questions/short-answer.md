# Ubiquitous Computing — Computers Everywhere — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does 'ubiquitous' mean?**
**Answer: Everywhere**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is ubiquitous computing?**
**Answer: Computation woven into everyday objects and places, serving without being sat at**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is the temporary form of the computer?**
**Answer: A box on a desk**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Give an example of dissolved computation.**
**Answer: A door that knows its holder (any one of: a road reporting traffic, a herd collar reporting the animal)**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How is its success measured?**
**Answer: By its invisibility — nobody says they are using a computer**
