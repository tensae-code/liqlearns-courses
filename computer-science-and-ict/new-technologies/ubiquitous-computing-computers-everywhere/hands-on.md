# Hands-on — Ubiquitous Computing — Computers Everywhere

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Find the invisible', 'Walk one room or street and list every computing presence you can find, including the ones not shaped like computers. Mark which are already 'ubiquitous' in spirit.')

## Task two
('Vision with guard', 'Design one ubiquitous service for your school. Then write the one protection it must carry, using the citizen qualities from the earlier lesson.')
