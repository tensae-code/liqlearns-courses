# Hands-on — AI Search Strategies — Blind Search

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Walk both ways** — On a small drawn tree with a goal, list the visiting order of breadth-first and of depth-first until the goal.

## Task two
**Cost the walks** — For your tree, count nodes remembered at the worst moment by each strategy; say which you'd choose and why.
