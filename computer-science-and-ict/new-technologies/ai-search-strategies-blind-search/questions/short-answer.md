# AI Search Strategies — Blind Search — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What information does blind search use?**
**Answer: Only the problem's own — no hint of the goal's nearness**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How does breadth-first visit?**
**Answer: Level by level — every node at one depth before deeper**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How does depth-first visit?**
**Answer: Dives down one branch to its end, then backtracks**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is blind search's other name?**
**Answer: Uninformed search**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What would a glance at the goal prune?**
**Answer: Whole wasted branches**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does breadth-first guarantee?**
**Answer: The shallowest goal**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is breadth-first's great cost?**
**Answer: Remembering the whole frontier — space**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does depth-first remember?**
**Answer: Only its path**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is depth-first's danger?**
**Answer: Diving forever down an infinite branch past the goal**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why teach blind search first?**
**Answer: It is the baseline every smarter strategy must beat**
