# Data Visualisation — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does visualisation represent data as?**
**Answer: Graphically — charts, maps, diagrams**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name three patterns pictures reveal.**
**Answer: The trend, the outlier, the season**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is a good visualisation, in the lesson's claim?**
**Answer: Not decoration — it is analysis**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is its first rule?**
**Answer: The match — form fits data shape and question**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) When does finding often happen?**
**Answer: At the moment of seeing**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name the four honesty rules.**
**Answer: Honest scales, labels and units, one picture one message, nothing drawn the data does not hold**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) From where must a bar's axis start?**
**Answer: Zero**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does a chart arguing three things argue?**
**Answer: None of them**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Name two drawing cheats.**
**Answer: 3D tilts fattening a slice; smoothed lines through wild data**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why are rules taught with tools here?**
**Answer: Data too big to read is too easy to draw falsely**
