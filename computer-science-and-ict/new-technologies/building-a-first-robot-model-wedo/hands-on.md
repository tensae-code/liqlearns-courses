# Hands-on — Building a First Robot Model WeDo

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Work the book's own example** — Lesson 11: The windmill model — building a Lego Education WeDo 2.0 model (Smart Hub, motor, sensors) Work it with the book open first, step by step, then close the book and do it again.

## Task two
**Show it to someone else** — Teach building a first robot model wedo to a companion in your own words, with your worked example in front of you, and answer their one question from the lesson.
