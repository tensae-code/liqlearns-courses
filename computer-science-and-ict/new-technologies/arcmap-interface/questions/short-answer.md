# The ArcMap Interface — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What draws the layers in ArcMap?**
**Answer: The map display in the centre**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does the table of contents list?**
**Answer: The layers in drawing order**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does a layer's tick box do?**
**Answer: Shows or hides the layer**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Where are the menu bar and toolbars?**
**Answer: Above the map display**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why does the book name the parts first?**
**Answer: So every later instruction can point to a part**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Which layer sits on top of the drawing?**
**Answer: The top layer in the table of contents**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How do you change the drawing order?**
**Answer: Drag a layer in the table of contents**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How do you hide a layer without removing it?**
**Answer: Untick its tick box**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How do you make tools act on one layer?**
**Answer: Select it by clicking its name**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why does Rwanda's walkthrough start with the table of contents?**
**Answer: Because controlling it means controlling the map**
