Placement choice: this lesson was built new for the ICT expansion (Djibouti, 2026-11-14) because no existing lesson in the course covers this thing as its own lesson — the live tree was checked first: the one Robotics lesson in this title teaches what robots are and the jobs they do for people, from a Grade 11 student book; it is not a first hands-on initiation into programming a robot, and is named above as a recommended companion, not a duplicate. The placements file named this slug directly (NEW LESSON NEEDED — new-technologies/first-steps-in-educational-robotics). It sits in the New Technologies title (new-technologies/).

Djibouti's own way: the *Guide manuel* frames Informatique as both a subject and a teaching tool, and sequences the year from switching on a computer through Windows, the web and Scratch to a first robotics initiation; typing is taught on the AZERTY keyboard, the keyboard of French-language schooling. This lesson keeps that place in the sequence.

Papua New Guinea's own way (Grade 11): the senior guide turns learners into junior system administrators and builders — computer administration, datasheets ("data books") and timing technology, documenting server and printer configurations, data science, pseudocode, Green IT, and building static web pages with tools like Canva and Figma; typing rises to 40 wpm at 85–100% accuracy. (teacher guide — evidence)

Vietnam's own way (Grade 10 Chuyên đề Tin học 10 Khoa học máy tính): the CS study-topics book for Grade 10 is not more Python — it is a full educational-robotics course (a STEM orientation, per its preface): build the robot, connect it to the computer, then program it to move, sense obstacles and follow a line on a test course (sa bàn).
