# First Steps in Educational Robotics — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Why must a robot's instructions be exact and in order?**
**Answer:** The robot does exactly what it is told and cannot guess what was meant, so a missing, wrong or wrongly ordered step makes it do the wrong thing in the real world.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) A robot stops before reaching its target. Walk through what you do next.**
**Answer:** Watch what it really did, find the step that made it stop short (a distance too small), change that one thing, and run the program again.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name a robot's sensing, deciding and acting parts for a classroom robot that follows a line.**
**Answer:** It senses the line with a light or colour sensor, decides from its program which way to steer, and acts through its wheels and motors.
