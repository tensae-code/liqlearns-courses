# Hands-on — First Steps in Educational Robotics

The lesson with your feet and hands, not only your eyes.

## Be the robot
Mark a start and a finish on the floor. One learner writes the steps on paper. The other learner is the robot: they follow the written steps exactly — no guessing, no extra steps. When the "robot" fails to reach the finish, the writer changes one step and the robot runs again. Swap roles.

## A job in four steps
Write a program of no more than four steps for a small job: go forward, turn, go forward, stop. If a school robot or kit is available, enter the steps and run them; if not, walk them yourself as the robot. Record what happened on the first run and what you changed.

## Sense, decide, act
For one run you watched, write three lines: what the robot sensed, what its program decided, and what it did.
