# Components and Characteristics of AI Systems — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the four standing components.**
**Answer: Data, algorithms/model, computing machinery, interface**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name the preparation stages in order.**
**Answer: Define the problem, gather and prepare data, build the model, train and test, deploy and watch**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What can be assigned thanks to components and stages?**
**Answer: A failure — to a component and a stage**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does the interface do?**
**Answer: Carries what people give and receive**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What makes it a system, not a mystery?**
**Answer: Its parts can be listed and its failures assigned**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name the five characteristics.**
**Answer: Learning, reasoning, perception, language, adaptation**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is adaptation?**
**Answer: Holding performance as the world shifts**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does perception take in?**
**Answer: Speech, images, signals**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How many marks does a thermostat hold?**
**Answer: None of the five — only reaction**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the list for?**
**Answer: A measuring stick for any claimed 'AI'**
