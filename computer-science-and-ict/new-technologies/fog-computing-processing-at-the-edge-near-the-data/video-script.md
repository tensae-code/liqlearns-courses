# Video script — Fog computing — processing at the edge, near the data

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Everything travelling to one far cloud jams the road.

So the cloud comes closer. That is **fog**.

Fog nodes sit near the data. They decide: handle here — or send up?

Quicker answers. Lower latency. Less travel.

A gateway that thinks — that is fog.
