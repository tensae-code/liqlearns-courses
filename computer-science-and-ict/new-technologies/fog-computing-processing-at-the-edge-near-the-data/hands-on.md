# Hands-on — Fog computing — processing at the edge, near the data

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Edge or centre?
Five jobs arrive: a doorbell camera spotting a visitor, a monthly sales report, a self-driving car's braking decision, a photo backup, a smart meter's yearly summary. For each, decide: judge it at the edge, or send it to the centre? Defend each call with speed or sensitivity.

## Bandwidth diary
List the streams your home or school sends to distant services in a day — video, photos, messages, calls. Estimate which are 'large and cumbersome to transfer' like surveillance video. Write which of them a nearby fog node could judge locally, and what would still need to travel.
