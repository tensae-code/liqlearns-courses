# Fog computing — processing at the edge, near the data — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Fog nodes analyse locally, at the ______.
**Answer: edge**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Fog lowers ______ — the delay before an answer returns.
**Answer: latency**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The fog sits between hardware and remote servers as an intelligent ______.
**Answer: gateway**
