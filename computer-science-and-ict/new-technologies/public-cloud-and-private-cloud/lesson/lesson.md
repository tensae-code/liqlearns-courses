# Public cloud and private cloud

## Do it first

Picture a big shared water tank. Many houses draw from it, each through its own pipe and its own tap. Now picture a second tank behind one house's fence, serving that house alone, locked by its owner. Hold both pictures — they are the two clouds in this lesson.

## Public cloud — shared, but divided

A **public cloud** is the **standard model**: a provider's resources are **shared across different clients**. Renting 10 GB means holding a **logically divided, isolated part of a bigger storage device that is shared with others** — shared tank, your own locked pipe.

## Private cloud — dedicated, and controlled

A **private cloud** **dedicates all resources to your application** — run by a provider, or from **your own company data centre**. It gives **complete control over data security and privacy**, and an **extra protection layer for sensitive, confidential information**.
