# Lesson plan — Public cloud and private cloud

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** New Technologies · **Lesson:** Public cloud and private cloud
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 2 — Emerging Technologies (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a public cloud shares a provider's resources across many clients, each holding an isolated part; a private cloud dedicates everything to one owner, with complete control of security and privacy.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the cloud doorway lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or in steps — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons

## Before this lesson

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Define the public cloud and the isolated-part idea.
2. Define the private cloud and its two homes.
3. Say what private control buys: security, privacy, an extra protection layer.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks 'public' means 'everyone can read your files' → public means the RESOURCES are shared; your part is logically divided and isolated. Thinks private must mean on your own premises → a provider can run a private cloud too; the mark is dedication, not address.
