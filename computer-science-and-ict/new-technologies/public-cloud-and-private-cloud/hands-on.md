# Hands-on — Public cloud and private cloud

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Tank survey
Find three shared-versus-dedicated pairs in daily life — a shared water point vs a home tank, a shared minibus vs a private car, a school library vs your own shelf. For each, write what is shared, what stays isolated, and who controls it. Then map the pairs onto public and private cloud.

## Choose for three owners
Three owners need cloud: a photo club, a bank, a student with homework files. Choose public or private for each and defend each choice in one line, using control and sensitivity as your measures.
