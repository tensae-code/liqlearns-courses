# The Fourth Industrial Revolution — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the four revolutions by their engines.**
**Answer: Steam mechanisation; electricity and mass production; computers and automation; the fusion of physical, digital and biological**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does Industry 4.0 fuse?**
**Answer: The physical, digital and biological worlds**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What do its systems do together?**
**Answer: Sense, decide and act**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How has the worker's value moved across the waves?**
**Answer: From muscle, to attendance, to supervision — toward judgement, design and care**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is the famous forecast?**
**Answer: 47% of jobs at risk of automation**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Why is the forecast not a sentence?**
**Answer: Every past wave destroyed trades and raised new ones no forecaster had named**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is Mongolia's policy answer?**
**Answer: The Three Pillars policy, to start Industry 4.0 in Mongolia**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What choice does the lesson put to the pupil?**
**Answer: To arrive in the revolution as its operators or its audience**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which wave automated production?**
**Answer: The third — computers and electronics**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which book teaches the revolution?**
**Answer: Mongolia's Grade 12 IT book**
