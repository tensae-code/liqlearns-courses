# Sensors in an IoT System — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does a sensor do?**
a) Measures a property of the world and turns it into data  b) Temperature, light, movement and pressure (or moisture)  c) The perception layer  d) Bad sensing cannot be repaired by good transmitting
**Answer: a**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name four things sensors measure.**
a) Temperature, light, movement and pressure (or moisture)  b) The perception layer  c) Bad sensing cannot be repaired by good transmitting  d) Its first fact
**Answer: a**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Which layer do sensors form?**
a) The perception layer  b) Bad sensing cannot be repaired by good transmitting  c) Its first fact  d) Readings from many nearby sensors
**Answer: a**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why does the book place sensors before networks?**
a) Bad sensing cannot be repaired by good transmitting  b) Its first fact  c) Readings from many nearby sensors  d) First processing and translation between protocols
**Answer: a**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is a sensor's reading to the system?**

**Answer: a**
