# Sensors in an IoT System — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does a sensor do?** → **Measures a property of the world and turns it into data**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name four things sensors measure.** → **Temperature, light, movement and pressure (or moisture)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Which layer do sensors form?** → **The perception layer**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why does the book place sensors before networks?** → **Bad sensing cannot be repaired by good transmitting**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is a sensor's reading to the system?** → **Its first fact**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does a gateway collect?** → **Readings from many nearby sensors**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What may a gateway do before passing data on?** → **First processing and translation between protocols**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Between what and what does a gateway translate?** → **The sensors' short-range protocols and the internet's**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What else is a gateway?** → **A guard post where local traffic is checked**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What can one gateway serve?** → **A room, a greenhouse or a street of lamps**
