# Sensors in an IoT System — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does a sensor do?**
**Answer: Measures a property of the world and turns it into data**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name four things sensors measure.**
**Answer: Temperature, light, movement and pressure (or moisture)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Which layer do sensors form?**
**Answer: The perception layer**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why does the book place sensors before networks?**
**Answer: Bad sensing cannot be repaired by good transmitting**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is a sensor's reading to the system?**
**Answer: Its first fact**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does a gateway collect?**
**Answer: Readings from many nearby sensors**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What may a gateway do before passing data on?**
**Answer: First processing and translation between protocols**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Between what and what does a gateway translate?**
**Answer: The sensors' short-range protocols and the internet's**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What else is a gateway?**
**Answer: A guard post where local traffic is checked**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What can one gateway serve?**
**Answer: A room, a greenhouse or a street of lamps**
