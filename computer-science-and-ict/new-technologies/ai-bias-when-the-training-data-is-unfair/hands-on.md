# Hands-on — AI Bias — When the Training Data Is Unfair

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Skew the training** — Train a “human classifier”: show the class 20 animal pictures, 18 of them dogs. Then test with cats. Record the bias you built, and name the data choice that built it.

## Task two
**Correlation hunt** — Given three rising-together pairs (provided by the teacher), decide for each: cause, effect, or third cause? Defend each verdict in two sentences.
