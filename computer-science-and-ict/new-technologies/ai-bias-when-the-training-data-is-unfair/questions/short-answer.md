# AI Bias — When the Training Data Is Unfair — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) From what is a learning system built?**
**Answer: Its training data**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is a bias?**
**Answer: A systematic tilt in the system's answers, learned from the data**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name two ways data can be unfair.**
**Answer: Under-representing a group; encoding yesterday's prejudices (also: skewing to one place or period)**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What warning does every Décodage introduction carry?**
**Answer: Attention aux biais — attention to bias**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Where do the machine's mistakes often begin?**
**Answer: In our data's mistakes**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is not causation?**
**Answer: Correlation**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Give the classic example.**
**Answer: Ice-cream sales and drowning both rise in summer — caused by the heat**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is a correlation, properly?**
**Answer: A hint to investigate, never a proof**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does acting on correlation as cause build?**
**Answer: Unfair and foolish systems**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What third thing causes the classic example?**
**Answer: The heat — summer itself**
