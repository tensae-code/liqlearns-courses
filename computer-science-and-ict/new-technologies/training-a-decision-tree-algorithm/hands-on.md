# Hands-on — Training a Decision Tree Algorithm

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Grow by hand** — From a 10-row table of pupils (mark, attendance, pass/fail), grow a two-question tree by hand and show its purity at each split.

## Task two
**Walk and judge** — Run three new cases down your tree; then prune one question and re-run — what changed?
