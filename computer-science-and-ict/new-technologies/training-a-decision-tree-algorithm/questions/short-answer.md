# Training a Decision Tree Algorithm — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does training a tree choose?**
**Answer: Its questions — the splits**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What split is kept?**
**Answer: The one that separates the kinds best — purest groups**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) When does growing stop?**
**Answer: When leaves are pure enough or the data runs out**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is the resulting tree?**
**Answer: The training data reorganised into an asking order**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why can a tree show its reasons?**
**Answer: Every decision is a path of named questions**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How does a new case use the tree?**
**Answer: It walks from the root answering questions to a leaf**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is the prediction?**
**Answer: The leaf's majority kind**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is an overgrown tree's fault?**
**Answer: Memorising noise — overfitting**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How is overgrowth cured?**
**Answer: Pruning or limiting depth**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is a stump's fault?**
**Answer: Too simple to catch the pattern**
