# Components of Cloud Computing — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a data centre?**
**Answer: A building of servers, storage and networking, with power and cooling**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does virtualisation do?**
**Answer: Slices each physical machine into many virtual ones**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why does virtualisation matter?**
**Answer: One server serves many customers in isolation**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does the platform software do?**
**Answer: Allocates, monitors and bills**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What turns a warehouse of computers into a rentable service?**
**Answer: The platform software over virtualisation**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name the user-side components.**
**Answer: Client devices, the internet connection, the services**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is the cloud's road?**
**Answer: The internet connection**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Name the three service models.**
**Answer: IaaS, PaaS and SaaS**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why insist on the whole chain?**
**Answer: Every link can fail or be attacked**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) As what is a cloud exactly as strong?**
**Answer: As its weakest component**
