## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The physical and virtual parts** — A cloud system stands on **data centres** — buildings of servers, storage and networking, with power and cooling — and on the **virtualisation** layer that slices each physical machine into many virtual ones, so one server serves many customers in isolation. Over that sits the **platform software** that allocates, monitors and bills: the machinery that turns a warehouse of computers into a service that can be rented by the hour.
2. **The reaching parts — clients, networks, services** — On the user's side: the **client devices** (anything with a browser or app), the **internet connection** that is the cloud's road, and the **services** themselves, offered in the models the next lesson names (IaaS, PaaS, SaaS). Jordan's components lesson insists on the whole chain because every link can fail or be attacked: a cloud is exactly as strong, and exactly as private, as its weakest component.
