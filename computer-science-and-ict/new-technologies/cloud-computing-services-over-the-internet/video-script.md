# Video script — Cloud computing — services over the internet, not on your PC

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Your files do not live on your computer any more.

They live in **the cloud** — servers, storage and databases, over the internet.

On demand. From one data centre. For users everywhere.

Gmail. Maps. Netflix. Spotify. You use the cloud every day.

Why 'cloud'? Engineers drew the network as one. The name stuck.
