# Hands-on — Cloud computing — services over the internet, not on your PC

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Your cloud census
List every cloud service you used this week — mail, messages, music, maps, classroom, meetings. Beside each, write what it stores or does for you, and where you could reach it besides your own device.

## Save it up
On any computer available to you, save one harmless file (a drawing, a paragraph) to a cloud folder or drive. Then open it from a different device or browser and tick off each step you took. No second device? Write the steps you would take, in order.
