# Cloud computing — services over the internet, not on your PC — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Define cloud computing in one sentence.
**Answer:** Storing and accessing data and computing services over the internet instead of on your personal computer.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Where does the word 'cloud' come from? (Teacher guide — evidence.)
**Answer:** Network engineers drew networks as a cloud shape to stand for where network devices are and how they interconnect; the name stuck to the computing model.
