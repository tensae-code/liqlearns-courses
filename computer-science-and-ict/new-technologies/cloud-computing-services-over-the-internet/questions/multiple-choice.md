# Cloud computing — services over the internet, not on your PC — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Cloud computing means storing and accessing data and services —
a) over the internet instead of on your personal computer  b) only on a flash disk  c) on paper  d) inside the monitor
**Answer: a) over the internet instead of on your personal computer**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The cloud offers, on demand —
a) servers, storage, networking and databases  b) only games  c) only printers  d) desks and chairs
**Answer: a) servers, storage, networking and databases**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Many users are served from —
a) one laptop each  b) a single data centre  c) their own homes only  d) a satellite each
**Answer: b) a single data centre**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Which list is all everyday cloud examples from the book?
a) Gmail, Google Maps, Netflix, Spotify  b) a notebook, a pen, a ruler, a bag  c) a bicycle, a bus, a road, a bridge  d) soil, seed, rain, sun
**Answer: a) Gmail, Google Maps, Netflix, Spotify**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The word 'cloud' comes from —
a) the weather over data centres  b) engineers drawing networks as a cloud shape  c) a company named Cloud  d) the shape of a server
**Answer: b) engineers drawing networks as a cloud shape** — Teacher guide — evidence.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** In the engineers' drawing, the cloud stood for —
a) rain  b) the location of network devices and how they interconnect  c) a password  d) a screen saver
**Answer: b) the location of network devices and how they interconnect**
