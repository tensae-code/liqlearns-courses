# Hands-on — Cloud benefits — anywhere access and flexible resources

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Three screens, one file
Take one piece of your own work — a paragraph, a photo, a song file. Open or imagine it on three different devices in three different places. Write where the real copy lives in each case.

## Support tools audit
Find the support tools on a phone or computer near you: voice typing, word prediction, text-to-speech. Try one of them for five minutes and write what it did well and what it did badly for a learner who needs it.
