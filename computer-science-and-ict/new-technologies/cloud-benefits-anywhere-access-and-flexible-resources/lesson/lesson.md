# Cloud benefits — anywhere access and flexible resources

## Do it first

Your phone breaks tonight. Tomorrow morning, on a borrowed phone, what of your work, your photos and your messages survives — and why? If your answer is 'almost everything', you have already felt this lesson's benefits. Now read how they work.

## Anywhere access — and working apart

Users can **reach their tools anywhere, at any time, from any device with an internet connection**, and **store and retrieve from distant servers**. People can **work from any location and communicate without being physically together**, and **shared resources let users collaborate in real time on the same content**.

And if your own device or service fails, **provider backup means the data is not lost** — the copy in the cloud survives the machine on your desk.

## Inclusive education — and flexibility

The book ties these benefits directly to **inclusive education — schools for everybody**: cloud tools carry **voice recognition, mind mapping, word prediction and text-to-speech**, supporting **learners with special needs**.

Services are also **fully flexible: resources can be restricted or enhanced at any time to meet needs** — grown when the work grows, shrunk when it shrinks.
