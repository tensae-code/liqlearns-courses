# Lesson plan — Cloud benefits — anywhere access and flexible resources

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** New Technologies · **Lesson:** Cloud benefits — anywhere access and flexible resources
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 2 — Emerging Technologies (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the cloud can be reached anywhere, at any time, from any device; it backs work up, frees people to work apart, supports inclusive education, and stretches or shrinks to fit the need.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the cloud doorway lesson before it. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or in steps — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short, single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09).

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the other lessons of this unit. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say how the cloud is reached: anywhere, any time, any connected device.
2. Explain provider backup against a failed personal service.
3. Name the inclusive-education tools: voice recognition, mind mapping, word prediction, text-to-speech.
4. Explain flexibility: resources restricted or enhanced at any time.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks 'anywhere' means 'without internet' → every benefit here rides on an internet connection; no connection, no cloud. Thinks flexibility means 'free' → it means adjustable, up or down, at any time.
