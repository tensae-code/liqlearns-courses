# Cloud benefits — anywhere access and flexible resources — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1.** Give two anywhere-access benefits of the cloud.
**Answer:** Any two of: reach tools anywhere, any time, from any connected device; store and retrieve from distant servers; work from any location; communicate without being physically together; collaborate in real time on the same content.

**2.** How does the cloud support learners with special needs?
**Answer:** Through tools such as voice recognition, mind mapping, word prediction and text-to-speech, inside inclusive education — schools for everybody.
