# Hands-on — IoT Network Design — Practical Applications

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Full design** — Design the complete IoT network for a school library (books, air, entry) through all five steps, in writing.

## Task two
**Compare two** — Take a classmate's design for a different ground and list three answers that differ from yours — and the ground-reason for each.
