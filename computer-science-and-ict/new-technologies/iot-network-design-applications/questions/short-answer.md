# IoT Network Design — Practical Applications — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the design act's steps in order.**
**Answer: Problem and measures, sensing, architecture and scenarios, security and failure, the data plan**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is the classic fault?**
**Answer: Skipping the first step — building beautifully for the wrong problem**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does the first step name?**
**Answer: The problem and what will count as success**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What two scenario families are chosen between?**
**Answer: Device-to-gateway and device-to-cloud (and device-to-device)**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What two plans close the design?**
**Answer: Security-and-failure, and the data plan**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name the three worked grounds.**
**Answer: A smart home, a greenhouse, a city service**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is special about the greenhouse design?**
**Answer: Moisture rhythms, a gateway in a shed, alerts that must arrive**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is special about the city service?**
**Answer: Hundreds of points, public data, stubborn reliability**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the unit's deepest point?**
**Answer: There is no standard IoT network, only standard questions**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is a design, in the closing phrase?**
**Answer: The set of answers one ground demands**
