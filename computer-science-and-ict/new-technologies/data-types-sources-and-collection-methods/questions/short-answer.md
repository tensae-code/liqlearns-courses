# Data Types, Sources and Collection Methods — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the two types at collection time.**
**Answer: Quantitative and qualitative**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is a primary source?**
**Answer: Data you collect yourself, for your question**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What three words describe primary data?**
**Answer: Fresh, fitted, expensive**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is a secondary source?**
**Answer: Data collected by others — records, statistics, tables**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is secondary data fitted to?**
**Answer: Someone else's question and someone else's care**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name the four primary methods.**
**Answer: Observation, questionnaires/surveys, interviews, experiments/measurements**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is observation silent about?**
**Answer: Reasons**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What do surveys trade on?**
**Answer: Breadth, at the mercy of honest answers**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which method is strongest for causes?**
**Answer: Experiments / measurements**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) When is the collection plan written?**
**Answer: Before the collecting**
