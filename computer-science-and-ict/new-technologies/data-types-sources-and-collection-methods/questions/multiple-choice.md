# Data Types, Sources and Collection Methods — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the two types at collection time.**
a) Quantitative and qualitative  b) Data you collect yourself, for your question  c) Fresh, fitted, expensive  d) Data collected by others — records, statistics, tables
**Answer: a**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is a primary source?**
a) Data you collect yourself, for your question  b) Fresh, fitted, expensive  c) Data collected by others — records, statistics, tables  d) Someone else's question and someone else's care
**Answer: a**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What three words describe primary data?**
a) Fresh, fitted, expensive  b) Data collected by others — records, statistics, tables  c) Someone else's question and someone else's care  d) Observation, questionnaires/surveys, interviews, experiments/measurements
**Answer: a**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is a secondary source?**
a) Data collected by others — records, statistics, tables  b) Someone else's question and someone else's care  c) Observation, questionnaires/surveys, interviews, experiments/measurements  d) Reasons
**Answer: a**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is secondary data fitted to?**
a) Someone else's question and someone else's care  b) Observation, questionnaires/surveys, interviews, experiments/measurements  c) Reasons  d) Breadth, at the mercy of honest answers
**Answer: a**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name the four primary methods.**
a) Observation, questionnaires/surveys, interviews, experiments/measurements  b) Reasons  c) Breadth, at the mercy of honest answers  d) Experiments / measurements
**Answer: a**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is observation silent about?**
a) Reasons  b) Breadth, at the mercy of honest answers  c) Experiments / measurements  d) Before the collecting
**Answer: a**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What do surveys trade on?**
a) Breadth, at the mercy of honest answers  b) Experiments / measurements  c) Before the collecting  d) Quantitative and qualitative
**Answer: a**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which method is strongest for causes?**
a) Experiments / measurements  b) Before the collecting  c) Quantitative and qualitative  d) Data you collect yourself, for your question
**Answer: a**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) When is the collection plan written?**
a) Before the collecting  b) Quantitative and qualitative  c) Data you collect yourself, for your question  d) Fresh, fitted, expensive
**Answer: a**
