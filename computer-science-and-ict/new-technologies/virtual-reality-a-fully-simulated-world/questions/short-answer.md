# Virtual Reality — a fully simulated world — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** Name the guide's three VR training examples.
**Answer:** Pilots in flight simulators, learner drivers in immersive environments, law-enforcement officers rehearsing scenarios safely.

**2.** How does VR convince the mind?
**Answer:** It presents artificial information to the senses so convincingly — tracked, real-time, touchable — that the mind treats it as almost real.
