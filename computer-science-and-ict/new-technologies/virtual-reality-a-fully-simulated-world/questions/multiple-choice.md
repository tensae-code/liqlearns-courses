# Virtual Reality — a fully simulated world — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** VR creates —
a) a 3-D computer-generated simulated environment  b) paper maps  c) phone calls  d) printed photos
**Answer: a) a 3-D computer-generated simulated environment**

**2.** Which is VR entry kit?
a) goggles, headsets, gloves, bodysuits  b) a keyboard only  c) a printer  d) a radio
**Answer: a) goggles, headsets, gloves, bodysuits**

**3.** Sensors in VR —
a) track movements and adjust the view in real time  b) take photos  c) play music  d) charge the headset
**Answer: a) track movements and adjust the view in real time**

**4.** Force-feedback gloves let users —
a) touch and handle virtual objects  b) type faster  c) see colours  d) hear better
**Answer: a) touch and handle virtual objects**

**5.** VR is described as —
a) fully immersive  b) partly immersive  c) not immersive  d) audio only
**Answer: a) fully immersive**
