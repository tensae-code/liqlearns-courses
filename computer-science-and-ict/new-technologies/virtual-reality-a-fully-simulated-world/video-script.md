# Video script — Virtual Reality — a fully simulated world

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

VR does not add to the world. It builds a new one.

Goggles on — you are inside a simulated place.

Turn your head: the sensors turn the view with you.

Gloves let your hands touch what is not there.
