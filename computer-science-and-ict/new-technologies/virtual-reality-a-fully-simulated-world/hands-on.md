# Hands-on — Virtual Reality — a fully simulated world

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Turn test
Stand, eyes on one wall. Turn your head slowly and name what enters your view. VR sensors must rebuild that changing view in real time — your turning is their input.

## Design a safe rehearsal
Pick one dangerous or costly job. Write the VR rehearsal for it: what the trainee sees, does, and may safely get wrong.
