# Virtual Reality — a fully simulated world

## Do it first

Close your eyes and picture standing inside a place that does not exist — you turn your head, the place turns with you. Open your eyes. Then read how VR builds that place.

## What VR is

**Virtual Reality (VR)** creates a **three-dimensional computer-generated simulated environment**. A person **enters** it using **goggles, headsets, gloves or bodysuits**. VR aims to present **artificial information to the senses so convincingly that the mind treats it as almost real**.

[Picture 16511 — VR — a fully simulated world]

## Tracked, responsive, touchable

**Sensors track the user's movements and adjust the view in real time** — turn your head, and the world turns. **Data gloves with force feedback** let users **touch and handle virtual objects**. The result is a **fully immersive** experience — unlike AR, nothing of the real room needs to stay in view.
