# PaaS — a ready platform for building applications — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** PaaS provides —
a) a platform and environment for developers to build applications and services  b) finished software for office tasks  c) physical servers to buy  d) internet connections only
**Answer: a) a platform and environment for developers to build applications and services**

**2.** In the book's comparison, the developer is given —
a) a finished picture  b) paint colours, brushes and paper — and draws the picture  c) a gallery to visit  d) nothing
**Answer: b) paint colours, brushes and paper — and draws the picture**

**3.** PaaS is hosted —
a) in the cloud, reached over the internet  b) on one school computer  c) on paper  d) in a warehouse
**Answer: a) in the cloud, reached over the internet**

**4.** Which list is all PaaS includes?
a) storage, networking, deploying, testing, hosting  b) cooking, cleaning, driving, farming  c) printing, scanning, copying, faxing  d) singing, dancing, acting, painting
**Answer: a) storage, networking, deploying, testing, hosting**

**5.** Which is a PaaS example from the book?
a) Heroku  b) Slack  c) Dropbox  d) Concur
**Answer: a) Heroku** — Slack, Dropbox and Concur are the SaaS lesson's examples.

**6.** PaaS stands for —
a) Platform as a Service  b) Program as a Shop  c) Paper as a Service  d) Platform as a Sale
**Answer: a) Platform as a Service**
