# Hands-on — PaaS — a ready platform for building applications

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Ready-made audit
Pick one thing you make — a meal, a poster, a song. List what a 'platform' for it would hand you ready-made, and what would stay yours to create. Then map your list onto PaaS: platform given, application yours.

## Includes hunt
The lesson lists eight things PaaS includes. Write each on its own card. Shuffle, then lay them in the order a real project would meet them: build, test, deploy, host, maintain. Defend your order out loud.
