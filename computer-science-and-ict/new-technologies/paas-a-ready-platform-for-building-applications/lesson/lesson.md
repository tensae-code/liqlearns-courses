# PaaS — a ready platform for building applications

## Do it first

A painter does not grind her own pigments, weave her own canvas and build her own easel before painting — she is handed paints, brushes and paper, and she draws the picture. The book uses exactly that comparison for this lesson's model. What is handed over, and what is left for you to make?

## PaaS — the picture, then the definition

**Platform as a Service (PaaS)** **provides a platform and environment for developers to build applications and services**, **hosted in the cloud and reached over the internet**.

The book's comparison: it is like **being given paint colours, brushes and paper, so you only have to draw the picture** — the platform is ready; the application is yours to create.

[Picture 30115 — PaaS, paints brushes and paper]

It **includes software support and management, storage, networking, deploying, testing, collaborating, hosting and maintaining applications**.

Examples: **Windows Azure, Heroku, Force.com, Google App Engine, Apache Stratos and OpenShift**.
