# Hands-on — Sensors in Daily Life

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Sensor hunt', 'Walk through your home or school and list every sensor you can find — tap, light, alarm, phone, door — writing for each what it notices and what acts on its signal.')

## Task two
('Design one', "Choose one waste at home — water, light or heat — and design a sensor system to cut it: name the sensor, what it notices, the control decision and the actuator.")
