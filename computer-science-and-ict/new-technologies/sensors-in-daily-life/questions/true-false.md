# Sensors in Daily Life — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does a sensor do?" is: It detects a change in the world and turns it into a signal**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What sensor works an automatic tap?" is: It watches the air and sounds the alarm at the first trace of smoke**
**Answer: False** — A presence sensor that notices your hands

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does a smoke sensor do?" is: It watches the air and sounds the alarm at the first trace of smoke**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "When do lighting sensors switch lights on?" is: How hot or cold a place is**
**Answer: False** — When darkness falls — and off again at dawn

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does a temperature sensor report?" is: How hot or cold a place is**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "In a sensor system, what is the sensor?" is: It decides what should happen**
**Answer: False** — The input — it notices and reports

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does the control unit do with the signal?" is: It decides what should happen**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What part acts — opens the valve, lights the lamp?" is: Sensors, a control unit and actuators**
**Answer: False** — The actuator

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What three parts does Egypt's robot lesson name?" is: Sensors, a control unit and actuators**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Why must sensors be placed well and kept clean?" is: It detects a change in the world and turns it into a signal**
**Answer: False** — A badly placed or dirty sensor reports a wrong world, so the system acts wrongly
