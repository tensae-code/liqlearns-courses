# Sensors in Daily Life — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does a sensor do?**
**Answer: It detects a change in the world and turns it into a signal**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What sensor works an automatic tap?**
**Answer: A presence sensor that notices your hands**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does a smoke sensor do?**
**Answer: It watches the air and sounds the alarm at the first trace of smoke**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) When do lighting sensors switch lights on?**
**Answer: When darkness falls — and off again at dawn**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does a temperature sensor report?**
**Answer: How hot or cold a place is**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) In a sensor system, what is the sensor?**
**Answer: The input — it notices and reports**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does the control unit do with the signal?**
**Answer: It decides what should happen**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What part acts — opens the valve, lights the lamp?**
**Answer: The actuator**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What three parts does Egypt's robot lesson name?**
**Answer: Sensors, a control unit and actuators**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why must sensors be placed well and kept clean?**
**Answer: A badly placed or dirty sensor reports a wrong world, so the system acts wrongly**
