# Sensors in Daily Life — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does a sensor do?**
a) It detects a change in the world and turns it into a signal  b) A presence sensor that notices your hands  c) It watches the air and sounds the alarm at the first trace of smoke  d) When darkness falls — and off again at dawn
**Answer: a) It detects a change in the world and turns it into a signal**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What sensor works an automatic tap?**
a) It watches the air and sounds the alarm at the first trace of smoke  b) A presence sensor that notices your hands  c) When darkness falls — and off again at dawn  d) How hot or cold a place is
**Answer: b) A presence sensor that notices your hands**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does a smoke sensor do?**
a) When darkness falls — and off again at dawn  b) How hot or cold a place is  c) It watches the air and sounds the alarm at the first trace of smoke  d) The input — it notices and reports
**Answer: c) It watches the air and sounds the alarm at the first trace of smoke**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) When do lighting sensors switch lights on?**
a) How hot or cold a place is  b) The input — it notices and reports  c) It decides what should happen  d) When darkness falls — and off again at dawn
**Answer: d) When darkness falls — and off again at dawn**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does a temperature sensor report?**
a) How hot or cold a place is  b) The input — it notices and reports  c) It decides what should happen  d) The actuator
**Answer: a) How hot or cold a place is**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) In a sensor system, what is the sensor?**
a) It decides what should happen  b) The input — it notices and reports  c) The actuator  d) Sensors, a control unit and actuators
**Answer: b) The input — it notices and reports**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does the control unit do with the signal?**
a) The actuator  b) Sensors, a control unit and actuators  c) It decides what should happen  d) A badly placed or dirty sensor reports a wrong world, so the system acts wrongly
**Answer: c) It decides what should happen**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What part acts — opens the valve, lights the lamp?**
a) Sensors, a control unit and actuators  b) A badly placed or dirty sensor reports a wrong world, so the system acts wrongly  c) It detects a change in the world and turns it into a signal  d) The actuator
**Answer: d) The actuator**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What three parts does Egypt's robot lesson name?**
a) Sensors, a control unit and actuators  b) A badly placed or dirty sensor reports a wrong world, so the system acts wrongly  c) It detects a change in the world and turns it into a signal  d) A presence sensor that notices your hands
**Answer: a) Sensors, a control unit and actuators**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why must sensors be placed well and kept clean?**
a) It detects a change in the world and turns it into a signal  b) A badly placed or dirty sensor reports a wrong world, so the system acts wrongly  c) A presence sensor that notices your hands  d) It watches the air and sounds the alarm at the first trace of smoke
**Answer: b) A badly placed or dirty sensor reports a wrong world, so the system acts wrongly**
