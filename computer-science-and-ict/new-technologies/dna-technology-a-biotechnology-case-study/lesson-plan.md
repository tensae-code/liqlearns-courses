## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **DNA — instructions in living things** — **DNA** carries **inherited instructions**, found in **the cells of living things**. Sections of DNA with specific instructions are **genes**. **DNA technology** means working with DNA in laboratories for a purpose.
2. **DNA technology as a case study** — A case study studies **one real use in detail**. DNA testing can **identify individuals and relations**; medicine uses DNA technology for **finding and treating inherited diseases**; farming uses it for **selecting and improving plants and animals**. The case sits in the guide's Bio-Technology topic — and new DNA work raises questions of ethics and safety alongside its powers.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
