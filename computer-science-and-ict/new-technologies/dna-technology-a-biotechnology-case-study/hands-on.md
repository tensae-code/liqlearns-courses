# Hands-on — DNA Technology — a Biotechnology Case Study

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Instruction hunt
DNA is instructions: write the 'instructions' a seed carries as a five-line list a gardener could follow. Compare with a companion's list.

## Case file
Build a one-page case file on one DNA use (identity, disease, farming): the problem, the DNA step, the result, one ethics question.
