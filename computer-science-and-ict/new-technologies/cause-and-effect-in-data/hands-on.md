# Hands-on — Cause and Effect in Data

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Headline court** — Take three 'X causes Y' headlines; for each, name the likeliest third factor and the road that would settle it.

## Task two
**Design the gold road** — For one school claim (homework causes marks), design the fairest experiment ethics allows, and say what it cannot prove.
