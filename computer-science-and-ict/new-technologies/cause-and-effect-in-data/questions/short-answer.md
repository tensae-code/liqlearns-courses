# Cause and Effect in Data — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is correlation?**
**Answer: Two measures moving together**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) When does X cause Y?**
**Answer: If changing X changes Y**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name the three traps.**
**Answer: A third factor, reverse cause, coincidence**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) In the ice-cream example, what drives both?**
**Answer: The summer sun**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is the standing question?**
**Answer: What else could move both?**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name the four honest roads.**
**Answer: Experiment, mechanism, order and dose, ruling out third factors**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is the gold road?**
**Answer: Experiment — change only X, hold the rest**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is mechanism?**
**Answer: A believable chain from X to Y, link by link**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What do order and dose ask?**
**Answer: Cause first; more cause, more effect**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What words are used until cause is earned?**
**Answer: 'Is linked to'**
