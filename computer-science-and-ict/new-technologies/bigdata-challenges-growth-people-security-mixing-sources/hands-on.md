# Hands-on — Big data challenges — growth, people, security, mixing sources

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Challenge audit
Take one real store of data you know — a school office, a shop's records, your family's photos. Score it honestly against the four challenges: growth? skilled people? security done or postponed? sources mixed or separate? Write the worst one and one fix.

## The postponed list
Write a list of security jobs people commonly postpone — passwords, backups, updates, access lists. Beside each, write what an intruder gains while it waits.
