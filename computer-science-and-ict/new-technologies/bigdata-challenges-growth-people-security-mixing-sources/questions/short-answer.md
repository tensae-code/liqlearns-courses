# Big data challenges — growth, people, security, mixing sources — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the four challenges of big data.
**Answer:** Managing growth; lack of data professionals; securing data; integrating data from many sources.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give two of the guide's limits of big data.
**Answer:** Any two of: costly traditional storage; privacy-standard risk; long study before benefits; sometimes-false results; bad choices when experience and judgement are set aside.
