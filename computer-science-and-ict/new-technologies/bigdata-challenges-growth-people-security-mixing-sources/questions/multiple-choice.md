# Big data challenges — growth, people, security, mixing sources — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Managing growth is hard because data sets —
a) shrink quickly  b) grow quickly and are hard to store and manage  c) stay the same size  d) are always small
**Answer: b) grow quickly and are hard to store and manage**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The professionals in short supply are —
a) data scientists, analysts and engineers  b) drivers and cooks  c) teachers only  d) farmers only
**Answer: a) data scientists, analysts and engineers** — The book's own career hint.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Security is often —
a) done first  b) postponed while storing and analysing come first  c) unnecessary  d) free
**Answer: b) postponed while storing and analysing come first** — leaving repositories open to intruders.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Integrating many sources means —
a) choosing what is most relevant, to save time and money  b) keeping every source separate forever  c) deleting all sources  d) printing everything
**Answer: a) choosing what is most relevant, to save time and money**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The guide warns that big-data results are —
a) always true  b) sometimes false  c) never useful  d) always instant
**Answer: b) sometimes false**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Decision-makers who lean on data too heavily and set aside experience and judgement —
a) always win  b) make bad choices  c) save money  d) need no data
**Answer: b) make bad choices** — Teacher guide — evidence.
