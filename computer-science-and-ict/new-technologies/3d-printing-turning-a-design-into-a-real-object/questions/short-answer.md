# 3D Printing — Turning a Design into a Real Object — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) How does a 3D printer build?**
**Answer: Layer by layer — thin slices of material laid until the stack is the object**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does it read the design as?**
**Answer: A stack of thin slices**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Are the model's measures kept?**
**Answer: Yes — the object is printed at the size the design declared**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Whose are the printed failures?**
**Answer: The design's — the printer builds the file faithfully**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What stands between Blender and the printer?**
**Answer: The export — saving in a printable format**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is a printable format for?**
**Answer: Read by printers and their slicing programs**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Name the exporter's final checks.**
**Answer: Model whole and packed, size confirmed, shape buildable (overhangs judged)**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What are the file formats' three destinations, across the course?**
**Answer: Keeping, sharing, and printing**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which unit's last lesson is this?**
**Answer: Grade 8's 3D design unit (Unit 1)**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which book teaches this?**
**Answer: Kuwait Grade 8 Digital Intelligence Term 1, Unit 1 — 3D Printing**
