## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **How 3D printing works — building in layers** — A **3D printer** builds an object **layer by layer**: it reads the design as a stack of thin slices and lays material down slice by slice — the bottom first — until the stack **is** the object. What modelling made as surfaces, printing makes as substance, with the model's measures kept true: the tower on screen becomes a tower on the desk at the size the design declared. The failures are the design's, printed faithfully — a model that cannot stand in the file will not stand in plastic.
2. **Exporting in a printable format** — Between Blender and the printer stands the **export**: the design saved in a **printable format** — the exchange format printers and their slicing programs read (the file-formats lesson's third destination, after keeping and sharing). Exporting is the designer's final check: the model whole and packed, its size confirmed, its shape one the printer can build — overhangs and floating parts judged before material is spent, not after.
