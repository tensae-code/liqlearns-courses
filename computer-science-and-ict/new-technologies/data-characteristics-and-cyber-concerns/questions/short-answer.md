# Data Characteristics and Cyber Concerns — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name data's four cyberspace characteristics.**
**Answer: Copyable without loss, movable at light speed, persistent, searchable**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is theft without an empty shelf?**
**Answer: A stolen copy that leaves the original sitting still**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does persistent mean for data?**
**Answer: Copies outlive intentions**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) For whom is data searchable?**
**Answer: For the owner and for the thief alike**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How fast can data move?**
**Answer: At light speed — abroad before an alarm rings**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name the four main concerns.**
**Answer: Confidentiality, integrity, availability, privacy**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is the privacy concern, exactly?**
**Answer: Whether data's use respects the people it describes**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Name an ethical issue of purpose.**
**Answer: Data collected for one purpose used for another**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Name an ethical issue of knowledge.**
**Answer: People profiled without knowing**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Where does Jordan place ethics?**
**Answer: Inside the security lesson, not after it**
