# Lesson plan — What big data is good for

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** New Technologies · **Lesson:** What big data is good for
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 2 — Emerging Technologies (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** analysed well, big data keeps customers, aims adverts, builds products, sets prices, manages risk and speeds decisions — and the teacher guide adds how customer data is gathered and what its analysis decides.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the big-data doorway and the 5 Vs. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or in steps — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons (ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **What analysis buys a business** — personalisation engines, targeted advertising, product development, price optimisation, risk management, quicker and better decisions — and the wider promise.
2. **Customer data — gathered three ways, used for decisions (teacher guide — evidence)** — ask directly, follow indirectly, add outside data; analysis finds and keeps clients, raises satisfaction, manages marketing, monitors social media, forecasts sales.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the other lessons of this unit. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name six business benefits of big data.
2. Give the personalisation examples: Amazon, Netflix, Spotify.
3. Name Ethiopian institutions starting to use big data.
4. Name the three ways customer data is gathered and what analysis decides (teacher guide — evidence).

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks the benefits are automatic → every benefit starts 'analysed well': stored data alone buys nothing. Mixes the gathering ways → ask = they tell you; follow = you watch their trail; add = outside data joins yours.
