# Hands-on — What big data is good for

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Follow your own trail
List five traces you left this week that a company could analyse: a purchase, a search, a viewing, a journey, a form. Beside each, write which benefit a business could build from it — and whether you are comfortable with that.

## Gather three ways
You run a small school shop and want to know your customers. Write one real action for each gathering way: one question you would ask directly, one thing you could follow indirectly, one piece of outside data you could add.
