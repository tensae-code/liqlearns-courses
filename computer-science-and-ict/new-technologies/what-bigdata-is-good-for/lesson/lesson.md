# What big data is good for

## Do it first

Think of a music or video service that recommends exactly what you like. Or an advert that shows you the very thing you searched for yesterday. Where did that knowledge come from? From data about you, analysed. This lesson lists what that analysis buys.

## Sub lesson 1 — What analysis buys a business

Analysed well, big data helps with **customer acquisition and retention** — keeping customers — through **personalisation engines**, the book's examples being **Amazon, Netflix and Spotify**.

It gives **targeted advertising**, built from **past purchases, interaction patterns and viewing history**; **product development and innovation**; **price optimisation with less manual error**; **risk management from data patterns**; and **quicker, better decision-making**.

[Picture 30103 — benefits of big data]

The wider promise reaches further: **curing disease, maximising crop yields, exploring planets, predicting disasters and preventing crime**. In Ethiopia, **Ethiopian Airlines, Ethio Telecom, the Artificial Intelligence Center and some banks** are named as institutions starting to use big data.

## Sub lesson 2 — Customer data — gathered three ways, used for decisions (teacher guide — evidence)

The guide works the business frame in detail. **Customer data** is the **behavioural, demographic and private data** companies gather, and it is gathered **three ways: asking customers directly, following customers indirectly, and adding outside data to your own** — through surveys, newsletter and blog subscriptions, promotions, competitions and offers, customer orders, transaction history, web-tracking, marketing analytics and social media.

[Picture 30122 — customer data gathered three ways]

Analysing it serves named decisions: **finding new clients, keeping clients, raising satisfaction, managing marketing more effectively, monitoring social media, and forecasting sales trends**.
