# What big data is good for — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Personalisation engines are exemplified by —
a) Amazon, Netflix and Spotify  b) a till and a ledger  c) paper surveys only  d) radio adverts
**Answer: a) Amazon, Netflix and Spotify**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Targeted advertising is built from —
a) guesses  b) past purchases, interaction patterns and viewing history  c) the weather  d) shop rent
**Answer: b) past purchases, interaction patterns and viewing history**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Price optimisation with big data brings —
a) more manual error  b) less manual error  c) fixed prices forever  d) no prices
**Answer: b) less manual error**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Which is an Ethiopian institution named as starting to use big data?
a) Ethiopian Airlines  b) a village market  c) a primary school only  d) none — no Ethiopian example is given
**Answer: a) Ethiopian Airlines** — Ethio Telecom, the Artificial Intelligence Center and some banks are also named.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Customer data is gathered in —
a) one way only  b) two ways  c) three ways: ask, follow, add outside data  d) five ways
**Answer: c) three ways: ask, follow, add outside data**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Web-tracking and transaction history belong to —
a) asking directly  b) following indirectly  c) adding outside data  d) deleting data
**Answer: b) following indirectly**

**7. (Sub lesson 2)** Analysing customer data helps a business to —
a) forecast sales trends  b) close its shops  c) hide from clients  d) stop marketing
**Answer: a) forecast sales trends**
