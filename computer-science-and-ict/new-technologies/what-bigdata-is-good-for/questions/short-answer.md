# What big data is good for — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name three business benefits of big data.
**Answer:** Any three of: customer acquisition and retention, targeted advertising, product development and innovation, price optimisation, risk management, quicker and better decision-making.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What decisions does analysing customer data serve? Name three.
**Answer:** Any three of: finding new clients, keeping clients, raising satisfaction, managing marketing more effectively, monitoring social media, forecasting sales trends.
