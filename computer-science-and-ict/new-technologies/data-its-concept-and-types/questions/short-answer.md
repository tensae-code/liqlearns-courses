# Data — Its Concept and Types — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is data?**
**Answer: Raw recorded facts before they are organised into meaning**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is information?**
**Answer: Data processed, organised and given context**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What confusion does the lesson warn against?**
**Answer: Mistaking heaps of numbers for knowledge**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Where is data held?**
**Answer: In files and tables**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is data to the analysis unit?**
**Answer: Its raw material**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name the sorts by nature.**
**Answer: Quantitative and qualitative**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How does quantitative split?**
**Answer: Discrete counts and continuous measures**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Name the sorts by structure.**
**Answer: Structured, semi-structured, unstructured**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Give an unstructured example.**
**Answer: Free text, images or sound**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why does the sort come before the method?**
**Answer: The type decides the tool — you cannot average a colour**
