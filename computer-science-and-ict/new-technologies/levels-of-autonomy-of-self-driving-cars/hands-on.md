# Hands-on — Levels of Autonomy of Self-Driving Cars

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Place the cars** — Cards describe six vehicles (lane beep; cruise control; motorway pilot with hands-on checks; robotaxi in one city; a concept car with no wheel…). Place each on the levels ladder and defend the placing.

## Task two
**Thymio's rung** — Run or imagine a Thymio mission: obstacle avoidance. State which ladder rung it honestly sits on, and what would have to change to climb one rung.
