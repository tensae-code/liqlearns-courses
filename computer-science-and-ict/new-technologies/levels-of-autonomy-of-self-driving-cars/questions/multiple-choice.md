# Levels of Autonomy of Self-Driving Cars — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does the car do at the ladder's bottom?**
a) Assists — warns, or steadies one thing while the human drives  b) Watch, and take over at once when asked  c) Stop watching — the car drives itself in its domain  d) No human driver at all, anywhere
**Answer: a**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What must the human do at the supervised middle levels?**
a) Watch, and take over at once when asked  b) Stop watching — the car drives itself in its domain  c) No human driver at all, anywhere  d) Thymio Mission 3
**Answer: a**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What may the human do at the top levels?**
a) Stop watching — the car drives itself in its domain  b) No human driver at all, anywhere  c) Thymio Mission 3  d) The human
**Answer: a**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is needed at the final level?**
a) No human driver at all, anywhere  b) Thymio Mission 3  c) The human  d) The car's makers and operators
**Answer: a**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which mission asks “and in real life?”**
a) Thymio Mission 3  b) The human  c) The car's makers and operators  d) Delegation and responsibility
**Answer: a**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Who answers while the human must supervise?**
a) The human  b) The car's makers and operators  c) Delegation and responsibility  d) The buyer, the law, and the insurer
**Answer: a**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Toward whom does responsibility shift at true self-driving?**
a) The car's makers and operators  b) Delegation and responsibility  c) The buyer, the law, and the insurer  d) What the advertisement calls it
**Answer: a**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Which reading keys does this apply?**
a) Delegation and responsibility  b) The buyer, the law, and the insurer  c) What the advertisement calls it  d) Assists — warns, or steadies one thing while the human drives
**Answer: a**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Who needs to know a car's real level?**
a) The buyer, the law, and the insurer  b) What the advertisement calls it  c) Assists — warns, or steadies one thing while the human drives  d) Watch, and take over at once when asked
**Answer: a**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What should not decide the level?**
a) What the advertisement calls it  b) Assists — warns, or steadies one thing while the human drives  c) Watch, and take over at once when asked  d) Stop watching — the car drives itself in its domain
