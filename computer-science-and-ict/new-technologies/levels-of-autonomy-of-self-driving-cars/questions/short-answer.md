# Levels of Autonomy of Self-Driving Cars — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does the car do at the ladder's bottom?**
**Answer: Assists — warns, or steadies one thing while the human drives**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What must the human do at the supervised middle levels?**
**Answer: Watch, and take over at once when asked**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What may the human do at the top levels?**
**Answer: Stop watching — the car drives itself in its domain**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is needed at the final level?**
**Answer: No human driver at all, anywhere**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which mission asks “and in real life?”**
**Answer: Thymio Mission 3**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Who answers while the human must supervise?**
**Answer: The human**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Toward whom does responsibility shift at true self-driving?**
**Answer: The car's makers and operators**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Which reading keys does this apply?**
**Answer: Delegation and responsibility**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Who needs to know a car's real level?**
**Answer: The buyer, the law, and the insurer**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What should not decide the level?**
**Answer: What the advertisement calls it**
