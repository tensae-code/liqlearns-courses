Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Assists — warns, or steadies one thing while the human drives** — as the answer to: What must the human do at the supervised middle levels?

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Stop watching — the car drives itself in its domain** — as the answer to: What is needed at the final level?
