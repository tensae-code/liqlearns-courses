# Multi-User Virtual Environments

## Do it first

The virtual-reality lesson put one person inside a simulated world. The EPAL chapter's next step removes the loneliness: the same world, entered by many people at once, each present to the others.

## Sub lesson 1 — Connecting — entering a shared world

A **multi-user virtual environment** is a simulated world **hosted once and entered by many users together**: each user **connects** (account, connection, their **avatar** — the figure that stands for them inside), and the environment keeps **one shared world-state**, showing every user the same world with all the others in it, updated as each moves and acts. Connection is therefore not downloading a place; it is **arriving** in one.

In your mind's eye: connecting — entering a shared world.

## Sub lesson 2 — Navigating together — movement, presence, meeting

**Navigating** the shared world means **moving the avatar** through its spaces, **seeing and being seen** — presence is the environment's social fact: others appear as figures whose movements and words arrive as they happen, so the world supports **meeting, talking and working together** inside it, not merely sightseeing side by side. The chapter places these environments beside VR/AR systems as their social form: reality simulated once, society included.

In your mind's eye: navigating together — movement, presence, meeting.
