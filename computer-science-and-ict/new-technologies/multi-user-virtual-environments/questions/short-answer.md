# Multi-User Virtual Environments — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a multi-user virtual environment?**
**Answer: A simulated world hosted once and entered by many users together**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What stands for the user inside?**
**Answer: Their avatar**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does the environment keep?**
**Answer: One shared world-state, shown to every user, updated as each acts**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Connecting is not downloading a place; it is what?**
**Answer: Arriving in one**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which chapter teaches it?**
**Answer: Greece Grade 11 EPAL Basika Themata, Chapter 8**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is navigating here?**
**Answer: Moving the avatar through the world's spaces, seeing and being seen**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is presence?**
**Answer: Others appear as figures whose movements and words arrive as they happen**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does the world support beyond sightseeing?**
**Answer: Meeting, talking and working together**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) These environments are VR's what form?**
**Answer: Its social form — reality simulated once, society included**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Each user enters by what three things?**
**Answer: Account, connection, avatar**
