# IoT Network Architecture — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the architecture's parts from the edge in.**
**Answer: Things, access network, gateways, core/cloud, applications**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What do gateways do in the architecture?**
**Answer: Gather and translate**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Where is data stored and processed?**
**Answer: The core / cloud**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What road does every reading travel?**
**Answer: The same road from edge to application**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What lives at each stop on the road?**
**Answer: Design choices — and failures**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is a star arrangement?**
**Answer: Every thing talks to one hub**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is a star's weakness?**
**Answer: The hub is a single point of failure**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is a mesh's strength?**
**Answer: Things relay for each other — far-reaching and stubborn**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What four things does the architecture decision weigh?**
**Answer: Range, power, cost and reliability**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Name three grounds that answer differently.**
**Answer: A flat, a farm and a factory floor**
