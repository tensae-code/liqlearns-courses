# GIS Software Architecture — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What are the two GIS architectures the guide names?**
**Answer: Project-oriented and database-oriented**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Where does a project-oriented system keep its data?**
**Answer: In its own files, made for that project**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is the weakness of project-oriented storage?**
**Answer: It is hard to share**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Where does a database-oriented system keep data?**
**Answer: In one shared database**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is the trend the guide names?**
**Answer: From projects toward the shared database**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does RDBMS stand for?**
**Answer: Relational database management system**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does the RDBMS hold beside attributes?**
**Answer: Geometry**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What do features become in the RDBMS?**
**Answer: Rows, with their facts as columns**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What happens when you query the table?**
**Answer: The map answers**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does the architecture decide, in the lesson's words?**
**Answer: Who can use the data, and whether everyone sees the same truth**
