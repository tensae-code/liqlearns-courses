# GIS Software Architecture — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What are the two GIS architectures the guide names?**
a) Project-oriented and database-oriented  b) In its own files, made for that project  c) It is hard to share  d) In one shared database
**Answer: a) Project-oriented and database-oriented**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Where does a project-oriented system keep its data?**
a) It is hard to share  b) In its own files, made for that project  c) In one shared database  d) From projects toward the shared database
**Answer: b) In its own files, made for that project**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is the weakness of project-oriented storage?**
a) In one shared database  b) From projects toward the shared database  c) It is hard to share  d) Project-oriented and database-oriented
**Answer: c) It is hard to share**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Where does a database-oriented system keep data?**
a) From projects toward the shared database  b) Project-oriented and database-oriented  c) In its own files, made for that project  d) In one shared database
**Answer: d) In one shared database**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is the trend the guide names?**
a) From projects toward the shared database  b) Project-oriented and database-oriented  c) In its own files, made for that project  d) It is hard to share
**Answer: a) From projects toward the shared database**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does RDBMS stand for?**
a) Geometry  b) Relational database management system  c) Rows, with their facts as columns  d) The map answers
**Answer: b) Relational database management system**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does the RDBMS hold beside attributes?**
a) Rows, with their facts as columns  b) The map answers  c) Geometry  d) Who can use the data, and whether everyone sees the same truth
**Answer: c) Geometry**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What do features become in the RDBMS?**
a) The map answers  b) Who can use the data, and whether everyone sees the same truth  c) Relational database management system  d) Rows, with their facts as columns
**Answer: d) Rows, with their facts as columns**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What happens when you query the table?**
a) The map answers  b) Who can use the data, and whether everyone sees the same truth  c) Relational database management system  d) Geometry
**Answer: a) The map answers**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does the architecture decide, in the lesson's words?**
a) Relational database management system  b) Who can use the data, and whether everyone sees the same truth  c) Geometry  d) Rows, with their facts as columns
**Answer: b) Who can use the data, and whether everyone sees the same truth**
