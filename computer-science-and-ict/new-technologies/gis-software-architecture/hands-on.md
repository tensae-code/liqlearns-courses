# Hands-on — GIS Software Architecture

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Files or database?', 'A district office keeps its land maps. Argue both sides in two short paragraphs: project files per survey, or one shared database — then give your verdict in one sentence.')

## Task two
('Row the feature', 'Take one feature — your school. Write it as a database row: five columns (name, kind, and three facts), one of which must be its geometry described in words.')
