# Fuzzy Logic — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What two answers does crisp logic allow?**
**Answer: True or false — in the set or out**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What replaces the hard edge in fuzzy logic?**
**Answer: A degree of membership between 0 and 1**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Read 'tall to degree 0.9'.**
**Answer: The thing belongs to 'tall' almost fully, but not completely**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What speech can fuzzy logic write that crisp logic cannot?**
**Answer: Rather hot, quite far, almost full — judgements in degrees**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Where is fuzzy logic's home?**
**Answer: In control systems**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What shape do fuzzy rules take?**
**Answer: If-then rules stated as people state them**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What action does a fuzzy controller produce?**
**Answer: A graded action — easing instead of snapping on and off**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Name two fuzzy-controlled examples.**
**Answer: An air conditioner and a washing machine (also camera focus)**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What kind of intelligence is fuzzy logic?**
**Answer: Judgement in degrees — handling vagueness without pretending precision**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which chapter teaches this?**
**Answer: Laos Grade 10 ICT, Chapter 4 §6.5**
