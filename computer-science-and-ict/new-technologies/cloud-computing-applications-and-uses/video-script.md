# Video script — Cloud Computing — Applications and Uses

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The cloud's common applications: **storage and backup** (files safe beyond one device), **email and office work** (documents shared and edited together), **hosting** (websites and apps without owning servers), **streaming** (music and film delivered, not possessed), and **software by subscription** (the SaaS of daily life). Each use spends the cloud's same gifts — anywhere access, stretching capacity, and someone else's machinery maintained for you.
Field by field: **education** (classes, files and learning platforms reaching any home), **health** (records shared between clinics, with the strictest privacy duties), **business** (shops and banks scaling at their busy seasons), **government** (services online for every citizen), and **science** (renting a thousand computers for a week's calculation). Jordan's closing point: the fields differ in their duties — health's privacy is not a shop's — but the cloud's shape serves them all, which is why it became the default ground of new systems.
