# Augmented Reality — digital information laid over the real world — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** AR blends real and virtual into one experience.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In AR, virtual information is laid over the object before the camera captures it.
**Answer: False** — Camera first, measuring second, overlay last.

**3.** AR's hardware includes sensors and input devices.
**Answer: True**
