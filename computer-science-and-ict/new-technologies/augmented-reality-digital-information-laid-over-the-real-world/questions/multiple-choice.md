# Augmented Reality — digital information laid over the real world — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** AR —
a) overlays digital data on the real world  b) replaces the world fully  c) works only on paper  d) is a kind of printer
**Answer: a) overlays digital data on the real world**

**2.** Which is NOT AR hardware?
a) a kitchen stove  b) a processor  c) sensors  d) a display
**Answer: a) a kitchen stove**

**3.** Phones can run AR because they —
a) carry all four hardware parts  b) are waterproof  c) have no camera  d) are new
**Answer: a) carry all four hardware parts**

**4.** AR's first working step is —
a) the camera captures the real object  b) the overlay appears  c) measuring distances  d) printing
**Answer: a) the camera captures the real object**

**5.** Smart glasses and head-mounted displays —
a) can also serve for AR  b) cannot show AR  c) are phones  d) are cameras only
**Answer: a) can also serve for AR**
