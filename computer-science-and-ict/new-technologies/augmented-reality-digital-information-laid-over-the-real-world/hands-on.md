# Hands-on — Augmented Reality — digital information laid over the real world

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Paper AR
Draw an object on paper. On a clear plastic sheet (or a second paper laid over), draw the labels and measurements AR would add. Lift and replace the sheet: real, then augmented.

## Hunt the hardware
On a real phone or tablet, point to the processor's home (inside), the sensors, an input device and the display — the four AR parts.
