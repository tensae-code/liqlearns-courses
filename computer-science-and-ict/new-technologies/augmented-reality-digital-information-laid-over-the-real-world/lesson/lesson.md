# Augmented Reality — digital information laid over the real world

## Do it first

Hold a phone up to an object in the room. Now imagine labels, sizes and arrows appearing over that object, stuck to it as you move. Hold that picture. Then read how AR does exactly that.

## What AR is

**Augmented Reality (AR)** **enhances the real world** by **overlaying computer-generated digital data on real objects**, **blending** the two into one experience. The real world stays — AR adds to it.

[Picture 16509 — AR — labels over the real world]

## The hardware — four parts

AR needs a **processor, sensors, input devices and a display**. **Phones and tablets already carry all four**, which is why AR runs on familiar devices; **head-mounted displays and smart glasses** can also serve.

[GIF 16510 — the overlay appearing over the real object]

## How AR works — in order

First, **the camera captures the real object**. Then **software works out measurements, nearby objects and distances**. Then **virtual information is generated and laid over the object** — in that order, every time.
