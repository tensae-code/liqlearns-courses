# Hands-on — Genetic Engineering — Changing an Organism's DNA

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Cut and join on paper** — Write a 20-letter 'gene text'. Choose a 5-letter 'gene', cut it, and join it into a second text. State the trait your paper organism gains.

## Task two
**Both sides, sourced** — Prepare the citizen's brief on one GMO question — safety testing — with the two strongest points on each side and the source of each.
