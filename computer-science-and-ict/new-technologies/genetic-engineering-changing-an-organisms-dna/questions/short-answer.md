# Genetic Engineering — Changing an Organism's DNA — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does genetic engineering change?**
**Answer: An organism's DNA, deliberately**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is recombinant DNA?**
**Answer: A gene cut from one organism and joined into another's DNA**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does the receiver gain?**
**Answer: A trait its lineage never had**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Name the classic recombinant example.**
**Answer: A bacterium making human insulin**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What finds and checks the gene before the lab work?**
**Answer: Bioinformatics**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does GMO stand for?**
**Answer: Genetically modified organism**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is BRRI?**
**Answer: The Bangladesh Rice Research Institute**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Name two trials BRRI breeds rice against.**
**Answer: Flood and salt water (also drought)**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What genome did Bangladeshi scientists decode?**
**Answer: The jute genome**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Name one standing question of the field.**
**Answer: Safety testing (also labelling, gene ownership)**
