## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Writing a good prompt — the picture in words first** — An image **prompt** is a description from which an AI **generates an image**: the better the description, the closer the picture. A good prompt names the **subject** exactly (a port, its cranes and ships), its **setting and light** (at dawn, from the sea), its **style** (a poster, a photograph, a drawing) and its **composition** (what stands where, what the eye meets first). The engineering is in the precision: the generator cannot be shown the picture in your head — it can only be told, and every untold thing will be invented for you.
2. **Designing Mubarak Al-Kabeer Port — iterate the description** — The lesson's work is a design brief: **Mubarak Al-Kabeer Port** — Kuwait's great port project — imagined and pictured by prompt. The method is **iteration**: generate, **look** at what came back as a designer (not a fan), name what is wrong or missing in **words**, and put those words into the next prompt. The pupil is the art director and the AI is a tireless, literal draughtsman — the design skill being taught is seeing clearly and saying exactly, which the GIMP lessons demanded of the hand and this one demands of the sentence.
