# Prompt Engineering — Writing Prompts for AI Images

## Do it first

Inside Grade 7's design year sits a newer craft: making a picture by describing it. Kuwait names it plainly — Prompt Engineering — and spends it on a Kuwaiti subject: designing a great port. This lesson is the writing that makes the picture.

## Sub lesson 1 — Writing a good prompt — the picture in words first

An image **prompt** is a description from which an AI **generates an image**: the better the description, the closer the picture. A good prompt names the **subject** exactly (a port, its cranes and ships), its **setting and light** (at dawn, from the sea), its **style** (a poster, a photograph, a drawing) and its **composition** (what stands where, what the eye meets first). The engineering is in the precision: the generator cannot be shown the picture in your head — it can only be told, and every untold thing will be invented for you.

## Sub lesson 2 — Designing Mubarak Al-Kabeer Port — iterate the description

The lesson's work is a design brief: **Mubarak Al-Kabeer Port** — Kuwait's great port project — imagined and pictured by prompt. The method is **iteration**: generate, **look** at what came back as a designer (not a fan), name what is wrong or missing in **words**, and put those words into the next prompt. The pupil is the art director and the AI is a tireless, literal draughtsman — the design skill being taught is seeing clearly and saying exactly, which the GIMP lessons demanded of the hand and this one demands of the sentence.
