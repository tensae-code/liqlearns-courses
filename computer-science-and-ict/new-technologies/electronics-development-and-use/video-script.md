The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Electronics** began as light and heat in wires: **Edison's lamp** stands at the head of the book's road — the first mass electric device, and the valve technology that followed it learned to control currents, the act every electronic device still performs. The road's great fork is the **signal divide**: **analogue** signals vary continuously, a wave copying the world; **digital** signals step between states, a code standing for the world. The whole course lives on the digital side of that fork — this lesson is where the fork itself is finally named, in the book's last technology chapter.
The field's human face is the **electronics engineer**: designing circuits, choosing components, testing signals — the profession that stands behind every device the course has touched. Its modern face is stranger: **robots assembling motherboards**, machines placing the components of new machines at speeds and accuracies no hand holds — electronics manufacturing electronics. Mongolia closes the survey with the loop made visible: the course began with information in the abstract and ends with the physical line where information's machines are born, built by their own kind.
