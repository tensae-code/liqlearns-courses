# Hands-on — Electronics — Development and Use

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Fork the signals', 'List five signals in your home. Mark each analogue or digital, and for two, say what would change if it crossed to the other side of the fork.')

## Task two
('Meet the engineer', 'Take one broken or open device (power safe, teacher-led). Name the components you can, and write which of the engineer's three jobs — design, choose, test — each visible part passed through.')
