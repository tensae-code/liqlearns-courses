# Electronics — Development and Use — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What device stands at the head of the book's electronics road?**
**Answer: Edison's lamp**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What act does every electronic device still perform?**
**Answer: Controlling currents**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is an analogue signal?**
**Answer: A continuously varying wave copying the world**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is a digital signal?**
**Answer: A signal stepping between states — a code standing for the world**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which side of the fork does the whole course live on?**
**Answer: The digital side**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does an electronics engineer do?**
**Answer: Designs circuits, chooses components, tests signals**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What assembles motherboards in the modern factory?**
**Answer: Robots — machines placing components of new machines**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why can robots do the assembly better than hands?**
**Answer: Speeds and accuracies no hand holds**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What loop does the survey close with?**
**Answer: Electronics manufacturing electronics — machines built by their own kind**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Where does this lesson stand in the book?**
**Answer: The last of Grade 12's technology-development chapters**
