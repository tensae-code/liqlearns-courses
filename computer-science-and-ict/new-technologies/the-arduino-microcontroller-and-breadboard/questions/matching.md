# The Arduino Microcontroller and the Breadboard — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is Arduino?** → **A microcontroller board exposing the chip's pins for wiring**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is wired to the pins?** → **The outside world — lights, buttons, sensors, motors**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How does the program reach the board?** → **Written on the computer and uploaded to it**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How does the board then run?** → **Alone — reading its pins and driving its pins**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which unit teaches it?** → **Greece Grade 9 Pliroforiki C, Unit 3**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is a breadboard?** → **A solderless board of connected holes where circuits are tried**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How are components placed?** → **Pushed in and pulled out — nothing melted, nothing wasted**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What do its inside rows do?** → **Carry the connections — power rails at the edges, joined strips in the middle**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What completes the bench?** → **Connecting the board to the computer — board and port chosen in the environment**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why try on a breadboard first?** → **Correction and rebuilding take minutes, with no solder and no waste**
