# The Arduino Microcontroller and the Breadboard — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is Arduino?**
**Answer: A microcontroller board exposing the chip's pins for wiring**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is wired to the pins?**
**Answer: The outside world — lights, buttons, sensors, motors**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How does the program reach the board?**
**Answer: Written on the computer and uploaded to it**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How does the board then run?**
**Answer: Alone — reading its pins and driving its pins**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which unit teaches it?**
**Answer: Greece Grade 9 Pliroforiki C, Unit 3**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is a breadboard?**
**Answer: A solderless board of connected holes where circuits are tried**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How are components placed?**
**Answer: Pushed in and pulled out — nothing melted, nothing wasted**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What do its inside rows do?**
**Answer: Carry the connections — power rails at the edges, joined strips in the middle**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What completes the bench?**
**Answer: Connecting the board to the computer — board and port chosen in the environment**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why try on a breadboard first?**
**Answer: Correction and rebuilding take minutes, with no solder and no waste**
