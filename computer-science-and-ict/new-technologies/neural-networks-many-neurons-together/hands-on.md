# Hands-on — Neural Networks — Many Neurons Together

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Trace a digit
Draw a tiny 3×3 pixel grid of the digit 1 (middle column dark). Invent a first-layer neuron whose weights are +1 on the middle column and −1 elsewhere, bias 0, and compute whether it fires for your 1 — then for a 3×3 drawing of 7 (top row and a diagonal). You have built, by hand, one feature detector of a real network's first layer.

## Count the knowledge
Draw a network: 9 inputs, one hidden layer of 4 neurons, 2 outputs. Count every weight (each line) plus every bias. Then say how many numbers 'the system knows'. Compare with a classmate's bigger drawing — feel why training data must grow with the dials.
