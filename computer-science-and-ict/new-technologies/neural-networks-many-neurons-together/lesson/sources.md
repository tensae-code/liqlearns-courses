**Acceptance count: taught in 4 countries (Germany, Egypt, Laos, Kazakhstan).**

Germany is counted from a partially-held copy: the held PDF is a PARTIAL, disordered screenshot compilation (see the book entry above), and this placement rests on the book's verified complete contents and sampled pages.

Placement choice: this lesson was built new for the ICT expansion (Germany, 2026-11-14) because no existing lesson in the course covers this thing as its own lesson — the live tree was checked for the slug and for near matches before building. 

Germany's own way: a Bavarian NTG Gymnasium book built on four deep strands — graph theory (adjacency matrices, breadth-first search, Dijkstra), coding and cryptography (symmetric/asymmetric encryption, signatures), network communication, and AI up to neural networks — closing with student projects including programming a social bot. This lesson keeps that depth: the idea is taught whole, with the book's own worked way, not thinned down.

Egypt's own way (Grade 8, Term 2 student book (Arabic)): Term 2 teaches AI from the neuron up — artificial neuron, its role in AI — then Python for data analysis and even encryption with XOR, a depth most countries save for secondary school.

Laos's own way (Grade 10 ICT student book): the upper-secondary course turns conceptual — a full information-systems syllabus (organisations, TPS/MIS/DSS/EIS/expert/office systems, the development life cycle), then the series' deepest security chapter (firewalls, cookies, spyware, spam, employee monitoring, privacy law), closing with artificial intelligence, with DOS and Unix taught alongside Windows in the senior years' background.


Kazakhstan's own way (Kolyeva G10 Informatika, humanities track): Mektep's humanities-track Grade 10 book mirrors the Arman-PV one (information security, video content, design theory, web design) and adds a fifth part no other humanities book has: artificial intelligence and blockchain — machine-learning principles, how neural networks are organised and work, the fields where AI is used, and blockchain technology.
