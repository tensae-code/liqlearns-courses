# Neural Networks — Many Neurons Together — short answer

Answer in your own words, in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 2) Explain 'features upon features' with the handwritten 9.**
**Answer: First-layer neurons fire for edges and strokes in the pixels; later neurons combine those into parts like the 9's loop and its descending stroke; the output neuron for 9 fires when that arrangement of parts is present. Each level judges combinations of the level below — wholes are built from parts, parts from edges, edges from pixels.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) A classmate says: 'The network understands the photo like I do.' Correct the sentence in two lines.**
**Answer: The network never sees a photo — it receives pixel numbers and produces output numbers, with weights between. Its 'recognition' is a pattern of arithmetic tuned by examples, powerful and useful, but not a person's understanding of a picture.**
