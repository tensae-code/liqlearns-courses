# Video script — Neural Networks — Many Neurons Together

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

One neuron judges one tiny thing. A network wires thousands of them in layers.

First, the input layer: the world as numbers. For a photo, one number per pixel.

Then hidden layers. Each neuron weighs the layer before it, and fires or not.

Last, the output layer. The strongest output is the answer.

Training shows the network millions of examples, and adjusts every weight.

Early layers learn edges. Later layers learn parts. The last layer learns wholes. Together, neurons are strong.
