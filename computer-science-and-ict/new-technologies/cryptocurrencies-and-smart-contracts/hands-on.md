# Hands-on — Cryptocurrencies and Smart Contracts

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Work the book's own example** — Unit 6 §6.4.2 Use Cases of Blockchain Technology (cryptocurrencies, supply chain, healthcare, voting) and §6.4.3 Cryptocurrencies and Smart Contracts (§6.4.3.1 role of cryptocurrencies; §6.4.3.2 smart contracts — Ethereum) Work it with the book open first, step by step, then close the book and do it again.

## Task two
