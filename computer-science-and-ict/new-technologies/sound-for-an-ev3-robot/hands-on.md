# Hands-on — Sound for an EV3 Robot

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Work the book's own example** — Lesson 25: Sound for the robot (EV3 sound editor, uploading a sound file to the robot) Work it with the book open first, step by step, then close the book and do it again.

## Task two
**Show it to someone else** — Teach sound for an ev3 robot to a companion in your own words, with your worked example in front of you, and answer their one question from the lesson.
