North Macedonia's own way (Year 2): a strikingly current first theme (cybersecurity, cryptography, blockchain, robotics, AI/ML and large language models) precedes deep C++ (data representation, functions, one-dimensional arrays), a full databases theme ending in SQL, multimedia with generative AI for graphics, and HTML web authoring.

Papua New Guinea's own way (Grade 10): Grade 10 jumps from using computers to building with them — simple electronic circuits, programming that controls an external device, robotics and artificial intelligence sit inside "Computer Architecture", while the software unit pairs operating-system installation with machine learning; typing rises to 30 wpm. (teacher guide — evidence, PARTIAL)

Egypt's own way (Grade 7, Term 2 student book): Term 2 pivots from applications to computing itself — AI, sensors and robots, then a Scratch-to-Python bridge in the same term.

Mongolia's own way (Grade 12): the final book measures everything against Mongolia itself — the ITU index and Mongolia's slipping rank in it, 36,760 km of fibre to 299 soums, the Mongolsat-1 satellite, IoT chips on livestock, telemedicine at the State Central Hospital, the ХУР state data-exchange and ТҮЦ service machines, nano-products from horse bone and ger covers — and its chapter projects are real Mongolian systems: a school ICT-development survey, a study of the ebarimt VAT-receipt system, and a Small Basic "Bouncing Ball" game; programming examples draw the alkhan khee meander with a Turtle subprogram.
