# Robotics — machines that do people's tasks

## Do it first

List the jobs in your home and town that are tiring, boring or dangerous. Which of them could a machine take over? Write your list first. Then read where robots already work.

## What robotics studies

**Robotics** studies machines — **robots** — that **take over tasks traditionally done by people**. A robot does the task again and again, or goes where a person cannot safely go.

[Picture 16503 — robots — factory, space, home]

## Routine and dangerous work

In factories, robots do **routine work such as car assembly lines** — the same movement, all day, without tiring. They also do **dangerous work such as handling radioactive waste**, and work in hard places: **underwater work** and **space exploration**.

[GIF 16504 — a robot vacuum steering itself]

## Robots at home

Robots are increasingly at home too: doing **chores**, **assisting older people**, **watching young children** — and the **robot vacuum cleaner**, which **sweeps and cleans floors on its own**, steering itself around the furniture.
