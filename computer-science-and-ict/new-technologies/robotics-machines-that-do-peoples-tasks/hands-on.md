# Hands-on — Robotics — machines that do people's tasks

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task audit
Walk your home and list five repeated tasks. Mark each: could a robot do it today, partly, or not at all? Defend each mark in one line.

## Vacuum path
Draw your room from above. Draw the path a robot vacuum must take to cover the floor without crossing the furniture. That path-planning is the robot's daily problem.
