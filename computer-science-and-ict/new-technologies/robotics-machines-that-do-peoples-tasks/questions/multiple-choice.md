# Robotics — machines that do people's tasks — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Robotics studies machines that —
a) take over tasks traditionally done by people  b) only play music  c) replace books  d) make paper
**Answer: a) take over tasks traditionally done by people**

**2.** Car assembly lines are an example of —
a) routine factory work  b) home work  c) space work  d) underwater work
**Answer: a) routine factory work**

**3.** Handling radioactive waste goes to robots because it is —
a) dangerous  b) easy  c) cheap paper work  d) a game
**Answer: a) dangerous**

**4.** The robot vacuum —
a) sweeps and cleans floors on its own  b) cooks food  c) drives cars  d) teaches classes
**Answer: a) sweeps and cleans floors on its own**

**5.** Home robots can also —
a) assist older people and watch young children  b) only vacuum  c) fly planes  d) print books
**Answer: a) assist older people and watch young children**
