# GIS and ArcGIS — Components — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does GIS stand for?**
**Answer: Geographic information system**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is a GIS?**
**Answer: A computer system for capturing, storing, checking and displaying data about places**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is every fact in a GIS tied to?**
**Answer: A location on the earth**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is a GIS map built of?**
**Answer: Layers that can be shown, hidden and queried**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) In which Rwanda years is GIS taught?**
**Answer: S1, S2 and S3**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is ArcGIS?**
**Answer: The GIS software family Rwanda's books use**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is ArcMap for?**
**Answer: Making and querying maps**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is ArcCatalog?**
**Answer: The filing cabinet that organises the geographic data**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does each data layer carry with it?**
**Answer: An attribute table of facts**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which part draws and asks, in the lesson's words?**
**Answer: ArcMap**
