# Hands-on — GIS and ArcGIS — Components

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Layer the town', 'On paper, draw your school area as three separate layers on three sheets: buildings, roads, and land use. Stack them, then lift one sheet at a time and say what the map loses.')

## Task two
('Ask the map', 'Write three questions only a GIS could answer about your district (not a paper map) — each must need a location and a fact together.')
