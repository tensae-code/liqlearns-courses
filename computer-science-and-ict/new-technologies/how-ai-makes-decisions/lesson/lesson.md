# How AI Makes Decisions

## Do it first

The youngest Kuwaiti pupils meet AI deciding. After what AI is and how a machine is trained, the third AI lesson follows a decision as it is made — and then looks around the room at the deciders already there. This lesson is the deciding.

## Sub lesson 1 — The steps AI follows to decide

An AI decides in **steps**: it **takes in** information (what its senses or its user give it), **compares** what it takes in with what it was **trained** on — the patterns its learning stored — and **chooses** the answer that fits those patterns best, then **acts** on the choice. No step is magic and none is understanding: the machine decides the way it was taught to decide, which is why the training lessons come first — a decision is only as good as the examples behind it.

## Sub lesson 2 — AI around us — smart surveillance cameras

Deciders are already at work around the pupils: Kuwait's named example is the **smart surveillance camera**, which does not merely record but **decides** — is that movement a person? a car? nothing? — and alerts or ignores accordingly. The lesson's closing habit is spotting the steps in the wild: for any AI around us, ask what it takes in, what it was trained on, and what it chooses — three questions that turn a marvel into a mechanism a Grade 4 pupil can reason about.
