# How AI Makes Decisions — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What are the steps of an AI decision?**
**Answer: Take in information, compare with what it was trained on, choose the best-fitting answer, act**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does the AI compare its input with?**
**Answer: The patterns stored from its training**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Is any step magic or understanding?**
**Answer: No — the machine decides the way it was taught to decide**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why do the training lessons come first?**
**Answer: A decision is only as good as the examples behind it**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is Kuwait's named example of AI around us?**
**Answer: The smart surveillance camera**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does the smart camera decide?**
**Answer: Whether movement is a person, a car, or nothing — and alerts or ignores**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Name the three questions to ask of any AI around us.**
**Answer: What does it take in? What was it trained on? What does it choose?**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What do those questions turn a marvel into?**
**Answer: A mechanism that can be reasoned about**
