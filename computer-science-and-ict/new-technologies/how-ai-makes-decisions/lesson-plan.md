## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The steps AI follows to decide** — An AI decides in **steps**: it **takes in** information (what its senses or its user give it), **compares** what it takes in with what it was **trained** on — the patterns its learning stored — and **chooses** the answer that fits those patterns best, then **acts** on the choice. No step is magic and none is understanding: the machine decides the way it was taught to decide, which is why the training lessons come first — a decision is only as good as the examples behind it.
2. **AI around us — smart surveillance cameras** — Deciders are already at work around the pupils: Kuwait's named example is the **smart surveillance camera**, which does not merely record but **decides** — is that movement a person? a car? nothing? — and alerts or ignores accordingly. The lesson's closing habit is spotting the steps in the wild: for any AI around us, ask what it takes in, what it was trained on, and what it chooses — three questions that turn a marvel into a mechanism a Grade 4 pupil can reason about.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
