# Smart Homes and How They Work

## Do it first

The lights come on as you reach the door; the house cooled itself before you arrived. This lesson takes the smart home apart to see the working, not the magic.

## Sub lesson 1 — What a smart home is

A **smart home** is a home whose devices — lighting, heating, locks, cameras, appliances — are connected and can be **monitored and controlled**, by the people in it, by an app from anywhere, or **automatically** by rules and schedules. Its parts are the IoT parts at home scale: sensors that notice (movement, temperature, smoke), a hub or network that carries, and actuators that act (switches, motors, valves).

## Sub lesson 2 — How it works — notice, decide, act

The working is a loop. A **sensor notices** — movement at the door. The system **decides** by its rules — after dark, a movement means turn the path light on and send the owner a picture. An **actuator acts**. Jordan's Grade 10 book teaches the smart home as IoT's home ground: the same loop, with the family's comfort, safety and energy bills as its measures — and its failures (a flat network, a wrong rule) felt immediately.
