# Smart Homes and How They Work — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a smart home?**
**Answer: A home whose connected devices can be monitored and controlled**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) In what three ways can devices be controlled?**
**Answer: By the people in it, by an app from anywhere, or automatically by rules**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name three sensor jobs in a smart home.**
**Answer: Noticing movement, temperature and smoke**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What do actuators do?**
**Answer: Act — switches, motors, valves**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What carries the home's messages?**
**Answer: A hub or network**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name the loop's three steps.**
**Answer: Notice, decide, act**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) In the door example, what is the rule?**
**Answer: After dark, movement turns the path light on and sends a picture**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) By what three measures is a smart home judged?**
**Answer: Comfort, safety and energy bills**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Name two failures felt immediately.**
**Answer: A flat network and a wrong rule**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Whose home ground is the smart home?**
**Answer: IoT's**
