# Data Analysis Techniques — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the describing family.**
**Answer: Statistical summaries, grouping and cross-tabulation, correlation, visualisation**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does correlation ask?**
**Answer: Do two measures move together, and how strongly**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why does every analysis open with describing?**
**Answer: It is how the analyst first meets the data honestly**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does grouping do?**
**Answer: Splits by kind and reads the cells**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is visualisation called here?**
**Answer: The technique of letting the eye analyse**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name the modelling family.**
**Answer: Regression, classification, clustering, time-series methods**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does regression relate?**
**Answer: Measures — predicting one from others**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does clustering find?**
**Answer: Groups no one named in advance**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) By what is a technique chosen?**
**Answer: The question's type and the data's shape**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the beginner's trap?**
**Answer: Knowing one technique and seeing every problem as its shape**
