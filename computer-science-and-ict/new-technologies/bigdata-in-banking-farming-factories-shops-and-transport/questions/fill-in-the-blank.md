# Big data in banking, farming, factories, shops and transport — fill in the blank

Fill in the missing word or words.

**1.** Detecting card misuse the moment it happens is ______ detection in real time.
**Answer: fraud**

**2.** Smart farming is also called ______ farming.
**Answer: precision**

**3.** Transport operators ______ routes and control traffic and congestion.
**Answer: optimise**
