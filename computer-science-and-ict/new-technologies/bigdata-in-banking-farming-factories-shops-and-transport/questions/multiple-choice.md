# Big data in banking, farming, factories, shops and transport — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Banks use big data to detect —
a) rainfall  b) fraud such as card misuse, in real time  c) crop diseases  d) traffic jams
**Answer: b) fraud such as card misuse, in real time**

**2.** Granular rainfall and fertiliser information serves —
a) retail  b) transport  c) agriculture — smart and precision farming  d) banking
**Answer: c) agriculture — smart and precision farming**

**3.** A transparent infrastructure that predicts uncertainties and weaknesses belongs to —
a) manufacturing  b) healthcare  c) education  d) media
**Answer: a) manufacturing**

**4.** Point-of-sale, scanner, card and inventory data belong to —
a) agriculture  b) retail  c) transport  d) government
**Answer: b) retail**

**5.** Optimising routes and controlling congestion is —
a) banking  b) retail  c) manufacturing  d) transport
**Answer: d) transport**

**6.** Which list is also named, without detail?
a) media, entertainment, energy, utilities, government  b) sports, music, cooking, fashion, games  c) mining only  d) fishing only
**Answer: a) media, entertainment, energy, utilities, government**
