# Big data in banking, farming, factories, shops and transport

## Do it first

Five workplaces: a bank, a farm, a factory, a shop, a bus company. Each one pours out data every day. Before you read on, guess one decision each could make better with that data. Then check your guesses — the lesson takes the five in turn.

## Five sectors, five uses

**Banking** — banks **detect fraud such as card misuse in real time**, and big data **eases compliance, auditing and reporting**, while collecting **customer feedback**.

**Agriculture** — farmers get **granular rainfall, water-cycle and fertiliser information** for harvest and crop choices: **smart farming and precision farming**.

**Manufacturing** — factories build a **transparent infrastructure that predicts uncertainties and weaknesses** before they stop production.

**Retail** — shops turn **point-of-sale, scanner, card and inventory data** into **personalised experiences, sales and service**.

**Transport** — operators **optimise routes, control traffic and congestion, and improve services**.
