# Hands-on — Big data in banking, farming, factories, shops and transport

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sector walk
Walk one street near home or school. List every business you pass that generates data — a shop, a bank agent, a bus stop, a workshop. Match each to its sector in this lesson and write the decision its data could improve.

## Precision farmer for a day
You farm one field. Write the three data kinds the lesson names — rainfall, water-cycle, fertiliser — and one decision each one changes: when to plant, when to water, how much to feed the crop.
