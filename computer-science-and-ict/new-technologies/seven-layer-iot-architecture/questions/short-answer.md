# The Seven-Layer IoT Architecture — and the Five-Layer Model — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the seven-layer model's floors in order.**
**Answer: Perception, network, middleware/processing, application, business — with security and management across them**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does the business layer hold?**
**Answer: The organisation's goals, plans and profits built on the services**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What work do added layers name?**
**Answer: Work the simple map left hidden**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Which concerns stand vertically across all layers?**
**Answer: Security and management**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is layer 3 called?**
**Answer: Middleware / processing — storage and analysis**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name the five layers of the five-layer model.**
**Answer: Perception, transport, processing, application, business**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How is the five-layer model made?**
**Answer: By splitting the three-layer skeleton once**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why do many vendors use the five-layer map?**
**Answer: Fine enough for responsibilities, coarse enough to hold in the head**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What decides which model to use?**
**Answer: The audience and the argument**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is fluency with the models?**
**Answer: Moving between the three maps without losing the system**
