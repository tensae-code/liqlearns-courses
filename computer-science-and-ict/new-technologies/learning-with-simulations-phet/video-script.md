# Video script — Learning With Simulations — PhET

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A **simulation** is a program that imitates a real system and **obeys its rules**: change a value, and the world answers as the real one would (within the model's honesty). **PhET** is a library of such worlds for science and maths — free, visual, built for exploring: the pupil does not read about the circuit; the pupil **builds it**, closes the switch, and watches the bulb. Jordan's Grade 4 guide sets PhET inside a science environment (electricity): the simulation is the laboratory the school can always afford.
Learning with a simulation follows the experimenter's loop: **predict** (what will happen if…), **change one thing**, **observe**, **explain** — and the guide's project spends it fully: pupils **design a circuit** in the PhET electricity world, test it, and carry the design's understanding to their presentation work. The lesson's discipline is the scientist's: a simulation is believed **as far as its rules are the real rules** — the pupil who can say where the model simplifies is using the simulation; the pupil who cannot is being used by it.
