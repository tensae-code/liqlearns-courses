# Learning With Simulations — PhET — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a simulation?**
**Answer: A program imitating a real system and obeying its rules**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is PhET?**
**Answer: A free library of science and maths simulation worlds**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How does the pupil learn in PhET?**
**Answer: By building and experimenting, not only reading**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) In which environment does the guide set PhET?**
**Answer: Science — electricity**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is the simulation called?**
**Answer: The laboratory the school can always afford**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name the experimenter's loop.**
**Answer: Predict, change one thing, observe, explain**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What do pupils design in the project?**
**Answer: A circuit in the PhET electricity world**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is changed at a time?**
**Answer: One thing**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How far is a simulation believed?**
**Answer: As far as its rules are the real rules**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Who is using the simulation, in the closing line?**
**Answer: The pupil who can say where the model simplifies**
