# Hands-on — Learning With Simulations — PhET

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Run the loop** — In PhET (or a described simulation), run the experimenter's loop three times on one world, writing prediction and outcome each time.

## Task two
**Design a circuit** — Design a working circuit in the PhET electricity world (or on paper in its terms): components, prediction, test, one repair.
