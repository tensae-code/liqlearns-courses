# Nanotechnology — What It Is — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What scale does nanotechnology work at?**
**Answer: 1 to 100 nanometres**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How fine is that?**
**Answer: About a thousand times finer than a hair's width**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How does matter change at the nano scale?**
**Answer: It behaves by its size, not only its kind — new strengths, colours, reactivities**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is a carbon nanotube?**
**Answer: Carbon rolled into tubes of atomic walls — stronger and lighter than bulk carbon**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is Moore's law doing in this lesson?**
**Answer: The chip road itself arrives at nano dimensions as transistors shrink**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name Mongolia's three nano products from the book.**
**Answer: Waterproof ger covers; horse-bone calcium; fireproof insulation from wool waste**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is the horse-bone product?**
**Answer: Bone calcium milled to nano fineness as a food**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is the wool product, and what is it for?**
**Answer: Fireproof insulation — against fire and winter**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) When is a technology truly developed for a country, in the lesson's claim?**
**Answer: When its products are made from the country's own ground**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Which book teaches nanotechnology through Mongolian products?**
**Answer: Mongolia's Grade 12 IT book**
