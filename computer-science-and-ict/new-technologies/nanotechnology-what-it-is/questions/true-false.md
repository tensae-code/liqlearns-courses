# Nanotechnology — What It Is — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What scale does nanotechnology work at?" is: 1 to 100 nanometres**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "How fine is that?" is: It behaves by its size, not only its kind — new strengths, colours, reactivities**
**Answer: False** — About a thousand times finer than a hair's width

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "How does matter change at the nano scale?" is: It behaves by its size, not only its kind — new strengths, colours, reactivities**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is a carbon nanotube?" is: The chip road itself arrives at nano dimensions as transistors shrink**
**Answer: False** — Carbon rolled into tubes of atomic walls — stronger and lighter than bulk carbon

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is Moore's law doing in this lesson?" is: The chip road itself arrives at nano dimensions as transistors shrink**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Name Mongolia's three nano products from the book." is: Bone calcium milled to nano fineness as a food**
**Answer: False** — Waterproof ger covers; horse-bone calcium; fireproof insulation from wool waste

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is the horse-bone product?" is: Bone calcium milled to nano fineness as a food**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is the wool product, and what is it for?" is: When its products are made from the country's own ground**
**Answer: False** — Fireproof insulation — against fire and winter

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "When is a technology truly developed for a country, in the lesson's claim?" is: When its products are made from the country's own ground**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Which book teaches nanotechnology through Mongolian products?" is: 1 to 100 nanometres**
