## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **How emerging technologies evolve** — An **emerging technology** is a **new technology still spreading into use**. It does not stay new: it becomes everyday, or it disappears. As it spreads it can **replace older ways of doing the same thing**, and its impacts show first in **how people work and communicate**. Studying a technology's evolution shows how it grew and what it changed.
2. **Impact on society and environment — a music case study** — The same technology can bring **changes in jobs and the skills needed** and **new waste and energy use from devices** — good and bad effects together. The guide's **case study is music**: recording and digital sharing changed how music is made and how it reaches listeners. A case study uses one real example, studied in detail, to show the whole pattern.
