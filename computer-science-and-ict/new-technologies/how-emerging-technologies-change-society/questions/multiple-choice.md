# How Emerging Technologies Change Society — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is an emerging technology?**
a) A new technology still spreading into use  b) No — they become everyday or disappear  c) Older ways of doing the same thing  d) In how people work and communicate

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Do emerging technologies stay new?**
a) Older ways of doing the same thing  b) No — they become everyday or disappear  c) In how people work and communicate  d) To see how it grew and what it changed

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What can a spreading technology replace?**
