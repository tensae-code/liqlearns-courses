# How Emerging Technologies Change Society — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is an emerging technology?**
**Answer: A new technology still spreading into use**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Do emerging technologies stay new?**
**Answer: No — they become everyday or disappear**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What can a spreading technology replace?**
**Answer: Older ways of doing the same thing**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Where do a new technology's impacts show first?**
**Answer: In how people work and communicate**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why study the evolution of a technology?**
**Answer: To see how it grew and what it changed**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name one impact of new technology on society.**
**Answer: Changes in jobs and the skills needed**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Name one impact on the environment.**
**Answer: New waste and energy use from devices**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) In the music case study, what changed how music reaches listeners?**
**Answer: Recording and digital sharing**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Can a technology's impacts be both good and bad?**
**Answer: Yes — the same technology can help and harm**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does a case study use?**
**Answer: One real example studied in detail**
