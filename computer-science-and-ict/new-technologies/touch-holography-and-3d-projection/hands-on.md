# Hands-on — Touch, Holography and 3D Projection

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Touch audit
List every touch screen you used today. For each, write what command your touch gave — tap, swipe, pinch — and what the machine did back.

## Depth on paper
Choose one use of holography or 3D projection from the lesson. Write two sentences: what the flat version would show, and what the depth version adds.
