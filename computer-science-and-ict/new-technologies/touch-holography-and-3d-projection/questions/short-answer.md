# Touch, Holography and 3D Projection — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What two jobs does a touch screen do?**
**Answer: It shows the picture and senses touches as input**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does a touch become on a touch screen?**
**Answer: A command, from the position touched**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is multi-touch?**
**Answer: Reading several fingers at the same time**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Which gesture needs multi-touch?**
**Answer: Pinch-to-zoom**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Name two places touch screens are used.**
**Answer: Phones and ticket machines (any two from the lesson)**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does a hologram rebuild?**
**Answer: The light pattern of a real scene**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How does a hologram differ from a flat photo?**
**Answer: It looks solid from different angles**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is projection mapping?**
**Answer: Wrapping a surface or object in projected moving pictures**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Do holograms need a headset?**
**Answer: No — the depth is in the image itself**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Name two fields that use holography or 3D projection.**
**Answer: Medicine and museums (any two from the lesson)**
