# Touch, Holography and 3D Projection — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What two jobs does a touch screen do?**
a) It shows the picture and senses touches as input  b) A command, from the position touched  c) Reading several fingers at the same time  d) Pinch-to-zoom
**Answer: a) It shows the picture and senses touches as input**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does a touch become on a touch screen?**
a) Reading several fingers at the same time  b) A command, from the position touched  c) Pinch-to-zoom  d) Phones and ticket machines (any two from the lesson)
**Answer: b) A command, from the position touched**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is multi-touch?**
a) Pinch-to-zoom  b) Phones and ticket machines (any two from the lesson)  c) Reading several fingers at the same time  d) The light pattern of a real scene
**Answer: c) Reading several fingers at the same time**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Which gesture needs multi-touch?**
