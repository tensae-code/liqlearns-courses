# Programming a Robot in a Virtual Environment — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does a simulator imitate?**
**Answer: A robot and its world, by the same rules as the real machine's**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is the playground?**
**Answer: The practice ground — floors, lines, walls and objects**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is the teaching, in the lesson's phrase?**
**Answer: The failure — free, instant and repeatable**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How do the virtual robot's sensors behave?**
**Answer: As the real machine's do**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does the pupil watch?**
**Answer: The virtual robot obey or fail**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name the three programming basics here.**
**Answer: Movement commands, sensing, control**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What quantities do movement commands carry?**
**Answer: Distances and angles**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Name three sensor questions.**
**Answer: Is there a wall? a line? how far?**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why virtual before physical?**
**Answer: Every pupil has a robot, and no robot can be broken**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the playground's promise?**
**Answer: The program that works here is the one a real robot runs**
