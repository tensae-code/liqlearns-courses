# Hands-on — Smart Devices — What Makes a Device Smart

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Smart or not
List six devices at home. For each, write whether it senses, processes, responds and connects — and give your verdict: smart or not, with the reason.

## Follow the response
Pick one smart device. Write the chain: what it senses, what it processes, and how it responds.
