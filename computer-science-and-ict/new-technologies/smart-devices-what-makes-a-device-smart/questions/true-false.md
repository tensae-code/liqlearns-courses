# Smart Devices — What Makes a Device Smart — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is a smart device?" is: A device that does more than its plain job**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What three things does a smart device do?" is: Their own operating systems and applications**
**Answer: False** — It senses, processes and responds

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "From which devices does the book build the concept?" is: The devices pupils already carry — the smartphone above all**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What do smart devices run on?" is: The Internet of Things**
**Answer: False** — Their own operating systems and applications

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What else do smart devices do?" is: They connect — to the internet and to each other**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does the book lead straight into from smart devices?" is: Processing, responding and connecting**
**Answer: False** — The Internet of Things

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Does a screen make a device smart?" is: No**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What earns a device the word smart?" is: Sensing, processing and responding**
**Answer: False** — Processing, responding and connecting

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Which grade's book opens with smart devices?" is: Grade 11**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Taking in information and acting on it is called what, in this lesson?" is: It senses, processes and responds**
**Answer: False** — Sensing, processing and responding
