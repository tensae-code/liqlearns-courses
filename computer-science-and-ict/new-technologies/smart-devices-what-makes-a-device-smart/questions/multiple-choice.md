# Smart Devices — What Makes a Device Smart — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a smart device?**
a) A device that does more than its plain job  b) It senses, processes and responds  c) Their own operating systems and applications  d) The Internet of Things
**Answer: a) A device that does more than its plain job**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What three things does a smart device do?**
a) The devices pupils already carry — the smartphone above all  b) It senses, processes and responds  c) They connect — to the internet and to each other  d) No
**Answer: b) It senses, processes and responds**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) From which devices does the book build the concept?**
a) Their own operating systems and applications  b) The Internet of Things  c) The devices pupils already carry — the smartphone above all  d) Processing, responding and connecting
**Answer: c) The devices pupils already carry — the smartphone above all**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What do smart devices run on?**
a) They connect — to the internet and to each other  b) No  c) Grade 11  d) Their own operating systems and applications
**Answer: d) Their own operating systems and applications**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What else do smart devices do?**
a) They connect — to the internet and to each other  b) The Internet of Things  c) Processing, responding and connecting  d) Sensing, processing and responding
**Answer: a) They connect — to the internet and to each other**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does the book lead straight into from smart devices?**
a) No  b) The Internet of Things  c) Grade 11  d) A device that does more than its plain job
**Answer: b) The Internet of Things**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Does a screen make a device smart?**
a) Processing, responding and connecting  b) Sensing, processing and responding  c) No  d) It senses, processes and responds
**Answer: c) No**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What earns a device the word smart?**
a) Grade 11  b) A device that does more than its plain job  c) The devices pupils already carry — the smartphone above all  d) Processing, responding and connecting
**Answer: d) Processing, responding and connecting**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which grade's book opens with smart devices?**
a) Grade 11  b) Sensing, processing and responding  c) It senses, processes and responds  d) Their own operating systems and applications
**Answer: a) Grade 11**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Taking in information and acting on it is called what, in this lesson?**
a) A device that does more than its plain job  b) Sensing, processing and responding  c) The devices pupils already carry — the smartphone above all  d) They connect — to the internet and to each other
**Answer: b) Sensing, processing and responding**
