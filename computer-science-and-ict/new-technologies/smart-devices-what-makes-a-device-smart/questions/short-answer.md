# Smart Devices — What Makes a Device Smart — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a smart device?**
**Answer: A device that does more than its plain job**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What three things does a smart device do?**
**Answer: It senses, processes and responds**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) From which devices does the book build the concept?**
**Answer: The devices pupils already carry — the smartphone above all**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What do smart devices run on?**
**Answer: Their own operating systems and applications**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What else do smart devices do?**
**Answer: They connect — to the internet and to each other**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does the book lead straight into from smart devices?**
**Answer: The Internet of Things**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Does a screen make a device smart?**
**Answer: No**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What earns a device the word smart?**
**Answer: Processing, responding and connecting**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which grade's book opens with smart devices?**
**Answer: Grade 11**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Taking in information and acting on it is called what, in this lesson?**
**Answer: Sensing, processing and responding**
