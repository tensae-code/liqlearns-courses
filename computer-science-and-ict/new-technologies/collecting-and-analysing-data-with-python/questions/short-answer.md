# Collecting and Analysing Data With Python — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does loading turn a file into?**
**Answer: A table — rows as rows, first line as column names**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is the table structure called?**
**Answer: A DataFrame (with the data library)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What three things are inspected after loading?**
**Answer: The shape, the column names and types, the first and last rows**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does a silently misread file do?**
**Answer: Poisons every later number while looking like success**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Give a misreading example.**
**Answer: A shifted column or a swallowed header**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Why display in full, not a sample?**
**Answer: So the eye meets missing values and strange entries before statistics hide them**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Name three things the full look catches.**
**Answer: Missing values, a strange entry, a column all one value**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What earns its turn only after the full look?**
**Answer: Statistics and charts**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the lesson's habit, in order?**
**Answer: Load, look at everything, then analyse**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why must the discipline be deliberate in Python?**
**Answer: The commands are short — skipping is easy**
