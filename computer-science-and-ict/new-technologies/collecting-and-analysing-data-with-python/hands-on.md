# Hands-on — Collecting and Analysing Data With Python

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Load and look** — Load any CSV (real or made) in Python or a spreadsheet; record its shape, columns, and three things the full look revealed.

## Task two
**Poison drill** — Deliberately misload a file (skip its header) and write what each later statistic would wrongly say.
