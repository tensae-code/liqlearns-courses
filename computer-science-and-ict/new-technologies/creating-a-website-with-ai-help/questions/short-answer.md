# Creating a Website with AI Help — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What three jobs does AI help do in website building?**
**Answer: It drafts, suggests and generates**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does each AI job hand the pupil?**
**Answer: A starting point, not a finish**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Against what is an AI layout suggestion judged?**
**Answer: The design principles — audience, navigation, consistency, simplicity, accessibility**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How must generated code be treated?**
**Answer: Checked in the browser like any other code**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What steers the AI's draft?**
**Answer: The pupil's prompt — the clearer the description, the closer the draft**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Why does Egypt teach this after the unaided school project?**
**Answer: Only a pupil who has built by hand can tell a good AI suggestion from a glossy bad one**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does AI help never take?**
**Answer: Responsibility for the finished site**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Name two checks only the maker can run**
**Answer: That the site's facts are true, and that its pages actually work (also: that the design serves its real audience)**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How do AI drafts fail on facts?**
**Answer: They invent confidently**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) How does AI code fail?**
**Answer: Silently — it must be run to be believed**
