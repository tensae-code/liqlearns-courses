# Hands-on — Collecting Data With IoT

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Design a collection** — Design a month-long IoT collection for your school's water use: sensors, rhythm, storage, and the story recorded beside it.

## Task two
**Poison hunt** — A temperature graph shows a flat line for three days, then a jump. List three collection faults that could explain it and how you'd check each.
