# Video script — Characteristics of an IoT System

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

An IoT system is first defined by **connectivity**: its things are linked — to each other, to gateways and to the internet — by wired or wireless means, and the link is the system's lifeline. Second, **device heterogeneity**: the things differ — different makers, sensors, processors, protocols and power — yet must work as one system. Designing for difference is not an extra in IoT; it is the normal condition, and standards and gateways exist to translate between the unlike parts.
**Scalability** means the system can grow — from ten sensors to ten thousand — without being rebuilt: addresses, networks and data handling must stretch. **Security** is a characteristic, not an afterthought: every connected thing is a door, so identity, encryption and updates are designed in from the start. Jordan's list closes the definition of IoT itself: a system that cannot connect, mix, grow and protect is not yet an IoT system, however smart its parts.
