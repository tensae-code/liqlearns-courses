# Characteristics of an IoT System — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is an IoT system's lifeline?** → **Connectivity between its things, gateways and the internet**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does device heterogeneity mean?** → **The things differ in maker, sensors, protocols and power yet work as one**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What translates between unlike parts?** → **Standards and gateways**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Is heterogeneity an exception in IoT?** → **No — it is the normal condition**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) By what means are things linked?** → **Wired or wireless means**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does scalability mean?** → **The system grows from ten sensors to ten thousand without being rebuilt**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What must stretch as the system grows?** → **Addresses, networks and data handling**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why is every connected thing called a door?** → **Each is a way in that security must guard**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What three protections are designed in from the start?** → **Identity, encryption and updates**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) When is a system not yet an IoT system?** → **If it cannot connect, mix, grow and protect**
