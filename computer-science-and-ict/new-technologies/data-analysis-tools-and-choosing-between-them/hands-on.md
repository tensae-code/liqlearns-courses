# Hands-on — Data Analysis Tools — and Choosing Between Them

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Choose for three jobs** — Choose a tool for three jobs (class marks, a city's traffic year, a monthly shop report) and name the loss in each choice.

## Task two
**Pass the work** — Plan one analysis across two tools: what is done in each, and what passes between them, in what shape?
