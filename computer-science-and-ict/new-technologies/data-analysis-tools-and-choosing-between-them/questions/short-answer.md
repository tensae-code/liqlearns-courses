# Data Analysis Tools — and Choosing Between Them — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the four tools on the shelf.**
**Answer: Spreadsheet, Python with libraries, statistical packages, visual/BI tools**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is the spreadsheet's weakness?**
**Answer: It weakens as data grows and steps multiply**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What are Python's strengths?**
**Answer: Scale, repeatability, automation**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What do BI tools offer non-programmers?**
**Answer: Dashboards and drag-and-drop analysis**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why keep several tools?**
**Answer: Each is a trade; professionals pass work between them**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name the six grounds of choice.**
**Answer: Data size, repeatability, depth, audience, cost and availability, the analyst's skill**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What expels the spreadsheet?**
**Answer: A million rows**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What wants code, not clicks?**
**Answer: A monthly (repeatable) report**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is trading off, in Jordan's phrase?**
**Answer: Every gain bought with a named loss**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Who has not yet chosen?**
**Answer: The chooser who cannot name the loss**
