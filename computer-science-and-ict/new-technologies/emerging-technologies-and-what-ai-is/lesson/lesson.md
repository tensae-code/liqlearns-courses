## What an emerging technology is

An **emerging technology** is a technology that is **developing and evolving fast**, with a **prominent socio-economic impact** — it changes how people live, work and trade. This unit tours the clearest examples: Artificial Intelligence and its branches, robotics, Augmented Reality, Virtual Reality and Data Science.

## What Artificial Intelligence is

**Artificial Intelligence** is the branch of computer science that designs systems **mimicking human intelligence**. Two human abilities sit at its heart: **reasoning** — working a conclusion out step by step — and **generalisation** — applying past experience to a similar new problem.

The term **dates to the 1950s**. AI is powerful now mainly for two reasons: **computer capacity has grown**, and **very large amounts of data are available** for systems to learn from. Its algorithms are already used across **business, medicine and entertainment**.

## How technology is changing work (teacher guide — evidence)

The teacher guide adds how computer technology is changing work. **Repetitive manual jobs are automated first**, freeing workers for higher work. **Robots and AI raise productivity** by replacing human labour on some tasks. And **internet working removes the workplace itself**: a person can live in Ethiopia and work for an organisation on another continent — which the guide names as a **counterweight to the brain drain** that developing countries suffer, because skilled people no longer have to leave home to use their skills abroad.
