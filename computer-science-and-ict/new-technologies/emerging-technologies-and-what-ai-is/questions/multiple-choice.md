# Emerging technologies — and what AI is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** An emerging technology is one that —
a) is developing fast with a prominent socio-economic impact  b) is very old  c) is used by one person only  d) never changes
**Answer: a) is developing fast with a prominent socio-economic impact** — Fast growth plus a big effect on society and the economy.

**2.** AI mimics —
a) human intelligence  b) only computer memory  c) paper filing  d) telephone lines
**Answer: a) human intelligence** — Reasoning and generalisation are its core.

**3.** Reasoning means —
a) working a conclusion out step by step  b) memorising a list  c) printing a page  d) charging a battery
**Answer: a) working a conclusion out step by step**

**4.** Generalisation means —
a) applying past experience to a similar new problem  b) forgetting everything  c) copying exactly  d) working one problem only
**Answer: a) applying past experience to a similar new problem**

**5.** AI is powerful now mainly because —
a) computer capacity grew and huge data is available  b) computers became cheaper to paint  c) the term is new  d) schools teach it
**Answer: a) computer capacity grew and huge data is available**
