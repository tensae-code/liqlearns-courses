# Hands-on — Emerging technologies — and what AI is

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Fast or not?
List six technologies. Beside each, write how many years it took to reach most homes. The fastest-spreading ones are your emerging technologies.

## Reasoning on paper
Take a small problem — 'the bus is late, the market closes at six' — and write the reasoning steps out loud, one by one. That step-by-step working-out is what AI's reasoning mimics.
