# Lesson plan — Emerging technologies — and what AI is

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** New Technologies · **Lesson:** Emerging technologies — and what AI is
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 2 — Emerging Technologies (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** an emerging technology develops fast with a big effect on society and the economy, and Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the branch of computer science that makes systems mimic human intelligence — reasoning and generalisation.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the Grade 11 Emerging Technologies unit around it. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or in steps — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Define an emerging technology: fast-developing, with prominent socio-economic impact.
2. Define AI and name its two core human abilities: reasoning and generalisation.
3. Say when the term AI dates from, and the two reasons AI is powerful now.
4. Name three fields already using AI algorithms.
5. Explain the guide's three changes to work, including the brain-drain reversal (teacher guide — evidence).

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks AI means any computer program → an ordinary program follows fixed instructions; AI mimics human intelligence — it reasons and generalises. Thinks AI is new → the term is from the 1950s; what is new is the computing power and the data.
