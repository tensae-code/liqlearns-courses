# Hands-on — Hologram Technology and AI

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Spot the hologram** — Find three holograms in daily life (cards, packaging, notes) and write what each is protecting or showing.

## Task two
**Explain the walk-around** — Explain to a younger pupil, without the word 'phase', why you can see around a hologram but not around a photo.
