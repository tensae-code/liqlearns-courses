Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does a photograph record?**
**Answer: Light intensity from one viewpoint**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does a hologram record?**
**Answer: The light's interference pattern — intensity and phase together**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What two beams make a hologram?**
**Answer: A reference beam and an object beam**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What two sights does replaying give?**
**Answer: Depth and parallax — seeing around the image**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does a hologram store, in the closing phrase?**
**Answer: Not a picture of the object but the object's light, encoded**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name four hologram applications.**
**Answer: Security, storage, medicine/engineering measurement, displays and education**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Why is the card's dove a security device?**
**Answer: It is hard to forge**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What invisible things do engineering holograms measure?**
**Answer: Strain and vibration**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Name two of AI's parts with holograms.**
**Answer: Computing holograms for 3D displays; cleaning or reading them**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is Jordan's pattern in pairing them?**
