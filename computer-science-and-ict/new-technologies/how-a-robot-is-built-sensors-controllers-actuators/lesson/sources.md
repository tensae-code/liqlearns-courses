Papua New Guinea's own way (Grade 10): Grade 10 jumps from using computers to building with them — simple electronic circuits, programming that controls an external device, robotics and artificial intelligence sit inside "Computer Architecture", while the software unit pairs operating-system installation with machine learning; typing rises to 30 wpm. (teacher guide — evidence, PARTIAL)

Switzerland's own way (Grades 3–4, Décodage 3e–4e): the 3e–4e volume repeats the 1re–2e unplugged activities at a higher level (loops and debugging join the robot and crane games and the sorting machine) and adds three new strands: Thymio robotics, a full ScratchJr scenario, and media “parcours” — Parcours des bestioles (how images influence emotions), Parcours du partage (image rights, digital traces, thinking before sharing) and Parcours de la pub (recognising and creating advertising). Pixel paravent introduces binary coding as transmitting a black-and-white image across a screen with an agreed code; Deviner la carte teaches the conditional through a magic trick and its logigramme.

Vietnam's own way (Grade 10 Chuyên đề Tin học 10 Khoa học máy tính): the CS study-topics book for Grade 10 is not more Python — it is a full educational-robotics course (a STEM orientation, per its preface): build the robot, connect it to the computer, then program it to move, sense obstacles and follow a line on a test course (sa bàn).
