# Video script — How a Robot Is Built — Sensors, Controllers, Actuators

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A robot takes in information through its **sensors** — input parts that detect things like **light and darkness**. The signals travel through the robot's circuits to the **controller**, the part that **decides what the robot does** with what the sensors report.
The controller's decisions are carried out by **actuators** — output parts like **motors** that turn decisions into movement and action. The parts are held together by the robot's **structure or frame** and powered by its **power source**, such as a battery. The robot works in a loop: **sense, decide, act**.
