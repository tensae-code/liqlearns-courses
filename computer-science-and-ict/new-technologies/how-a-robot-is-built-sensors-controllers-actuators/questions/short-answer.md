# How a Robot Is Built — Sensors, Controllers, Actuators — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What do a robot's sensors do?**
**Answer: Take in information about its surroundings**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does the controller do?**
**Answer: Decides what the robot does with sensor information**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What can a light sensor detect?**
**Answer: Light and darkness**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How do instructions travel between a robot's parts?**
**Answer: As signals through the robot's circuits**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Is a sensor an input part or an output part?**
**Answer: An input part**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What do actuators do?**
**Answer: Turn controller decisions into movement and action**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) A motor is which kind of robot part?**
**Answer: An actuator**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What holds a robot's parts together?**
**Answer: Its structure or frame**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What supplies a robot's energy?**
**Answer: Its power source, such as a battery**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) In the robot's loop, what comes after sensing?**
**Answer: Deciding (control), then acting**
