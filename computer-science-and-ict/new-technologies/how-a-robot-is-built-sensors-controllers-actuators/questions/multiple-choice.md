# How a Robot Is Built — Sensors, Controllers, Actuators — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What do a robot's sensors do?**
a) Take in information about its surroundings  b) Decides what the robot does with sensor information  c) Light and darkness  d) As signals through the robot's circuits
**Answer: a) Take in information about its surroundings**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does the controller do?**
a) Light and darkness  b) Decides what the robot does with sensor information  c) As signals through the robot's circuits  d) An input part
**Answer: b) Decides what the robot does with sensor information**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What can a light sensor detect?**
a) As signals through the robot's circuits  b) An input part  c) Light and darkness  d) Turn controller decisions into movement and action
**Answer: c) Light and darkness**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How do instructions travel between a robot's parts?**
a) An input part  b) Turn controller decisions into movement and action  c) An actuator  d) As signals through the robot's circuits
**Answer: d) As signals through the robot's circuits**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Is a sensor an input part or an output part?**
a) An input part  b) Turn controller decisions into movement and action  c) An actuator  d) Its structure or frame
**Answer: a) An input part**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What do actuators do?**
a) An actuator  b) Turn controller decisions into movement and action  c) Its structure or frame  d) Its power source, such as a battery
**Answer: b) Turn controller decisions into movement and action**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) A motor is which kind of robot part?**
a) Its structure or frame  b) Its power source, such as a battery  c) An actuator  d) Deciding (control), then acting
**Answer: c) An actuator**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What holds a robot's parts together?**
a) Its power source, such as a battery  b) Deciding (control), then acting  c) Take in information about its surroundings  d) Its structure or frame
**Answer: d) Its structure or frame**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What supplies a robot's energy?**
a) Its power source, such as a battery  b) Deciding (control), then acting  c) Take in information about its surroundings  d) Decides what the robot does with sensor information
**Answer: a) Its power source, such as a battery**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) In the robot's loop, what comes after sensing?**
a) Take in information about its surroundings  b) Deciding (control), then acting  c) Decides what the robot does with sensor information  d) Light and darkness
**Answer: b) Deciding (control), then acting**
