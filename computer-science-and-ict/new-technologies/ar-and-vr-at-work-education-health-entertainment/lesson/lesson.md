# AR and VR at work — education, health, entertainment

## Do it first

Imagine learning the human heart by walking around a beating 3-D heart, instead of reading one flat picture. Hold that. Then read what AR and VR do in classrooms, hospitals and places of fun.

## Sub lesson 1 — In education

They let learners **travel in time and space**, see **body systems concretely and interactively**, **simplify complex concepts in 3-D**, **stand in for scarce laboratory equipment**, **work from anywhere on accessible devices**, and **connect with learners worldwide**.

[Picture 16514 — AR and VR at work — three fields]

## Sub lesson 2 — In healthcare

They **train physicians and students in risk-free simulated surgery**, make **diagnosis less invasive**, help surgeons **see muscles, bones and organs during operations**, support **tele-surgery across distance**, and **distract long-stay patients from stress**.

[Picture 16519 — VR training — practice without danger (teacher guide — evidence)]

## Sub lesson 3 — In entertainment

They **enrich museum and gallery visits**, create **immersive VR cinema**, add **new layers to amusement parks**, and power a **growing wave of AR games**.
