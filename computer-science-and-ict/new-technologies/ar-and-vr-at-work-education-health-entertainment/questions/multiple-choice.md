# AR and VR at work — education, health, entertainment — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** (Sub lesson 1) In education, AR/VR can stand in for —
a) scarce laboratory equipment  b) teachers' salaries  c) school buildings  d) exam papers
**Answer: a) scarce laboratory equipment**

**2.** (Sub lesson 1) Learners can travel in —
a) time and space  b) only their town  c) print only  d) the past week's news
**Answer: a) time and space**

**3.** (Sub lesson 2) Simulated surgery training is —
a) risk-free  b) done on patients  c) forbidden  d) paper-based
**Answer: a) risk-free**

**4.** (Sub lesson 2) Surgery across distance is called —
a) tele-surgery  b) tele-learning  c) e-banking  d) spam
**Answer: a) tele-surgery**

**5.** (Sub lesson 3) Entertainment uses include —
a) immersive VR cinema and AR games  b) only radio  c) printed posters  d) chalkboards
**Answer: a) immersive VR cinema and AR games**
