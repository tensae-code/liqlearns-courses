# Hands-on — AR and VR at work — education, health, entertainment

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Match the use
Write the lesson's uses on cards and the three fields on the table. Sort every card, then defend the two hardest sorts.

## Design one use
Choose a subject you study. Design the AR/VR lesson for its hardest idea: what is seen in 3-D, what the learner does, why flat pages fail it.
