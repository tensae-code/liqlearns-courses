# Hands-on — The Expert System Development Life Cycle

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Interview a rule** — Interview a partner about one skill they have (a game, cooking) and write three of their tacit rules as IF–THEN rules.

## Task two
**Awkward case** — Design three test cases for a plant-disease expert system: one easy, one tricky, one where 'I don't know' is the right answer.
