# Hands-on — Types of Robots and How Robots Move

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Sort the family** — Find six real robots in the news or at home; sort each by movement and by field, with one line of evidence.

## Task two
**Choose the mover** — A robot must inspect a flooded, rubble-filled basement. Choose its movement and defend it against two rivals.
