# Types of Robots and How Robots Move — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Wheeled, legged, tracked, flying, swimming (and fixed)**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Wheeled, legged, tracked, flying, swimming (and fixed)** — as the answer to: What stops a wheeled robot?
**Answer: False — Stairs — it needs floors**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Rubble and mud**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Rubble and mud** — as the answer to: What does a fixed robot move?
**Answer: False — Only its working end**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Its sensors and program, driving its motors**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Its sensors and program, driving its motors** — as the answer to: Name the fields of robot use.
**Answer: False — Industrial, medical, exploration, service and home, agricultural, military**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Where people cannot or must not go — space, deep sea, ruins**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Where people cannot or must not go — space, deep sea, ruins** — as the answer to: What work do robots take, in the three words?
**Answer: False — Dull, dirty or dangerous work**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Precise work beyond a hand's steadiness**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Precise work beyond a hand's steadiness** — as the answer to: Where does each field adopt robots first?
**Answer: False — At its own painful places**
