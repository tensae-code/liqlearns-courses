# Types of Robots and How Robots Move — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name five ways robots move.**
**Answer: Wheeled, legged, tracked, flying, swimming (and fixed)**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What stops a wheeled robot?**
**Answer: Stairs — it needs floors**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Where do tracked robots excel?**
**Answer: Rubble and mud**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does a fixed robot move?**
**Answer: Only its working end**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What guides a robot's movement?**
**Answer: Its sensors and program, driving its motors**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name the fields of robot use.**
**Answer: Industrial, medical, exploration, service and home, agricultural, military**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Where do exploration robots go?**
**Answer: Where people cannot or must not go — space, deep sea, ruins**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What work do robots take, in the three words?**
**Answer: Dull, dirty or dangerous work**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What work beyond a hand do they do?**
**Answer: Precise work beyond a hand's steadiness**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Where does each field adopt robots first?**
**Answer: At its own painful places**
