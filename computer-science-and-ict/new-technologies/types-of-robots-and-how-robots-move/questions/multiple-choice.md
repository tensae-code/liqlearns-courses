# Types of Robots and How Robots Move — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name five ways robots move.**
a) Wheeled, legged, tracked, flying, swimming (and fixed)  b) Stairs — it needs floors  c) Rubble and mud  d) Only its working end
**Answer: a**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What stops a wheeled robot?**
a) Stairs — it needs floors  b) Rubble and mud  c) Only its working end  d) Its sensors and program, driving its motors
**Answer: a**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Where do tracked robots excel?**
a) Rubble and mud  b) Only its working end  c) Its sensors and program, driving its motors  d) Industrial, medical, exploration, service and home, agricultural, military
**Answer: a**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does a fixed robot move?**
a) Only its working end  b) Its sensors and program, driving its motors  c) Industrial, medical, exploration, service and home, agricultural, military  d) Where people cannot or must not go — space, deep sea, ruins

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What guides a robot's movement?**
