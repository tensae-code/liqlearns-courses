# Image Recognition with AI in App Inventor

## Do it first

The App Inventor unit's summit: the app stops only answering touches and starts answering the world — point the camera, and the app says what it sees. Grade 9 builds it from an AI image-recognition extension.

## Sub lesson 1 — Recognition as a component — the app asks an AI

Image recognition enters the app as an **AI extension/component**: the app hands it a **picture** (taken with the camera component or chosen), and the component hands back its **judgement — the labels it recognises, with confidences**. The builder's work is the honest framing the machine-learning lessons taught: the component was **trained on examples** elsewhere; the app merely asks it — so the app's quality is bounded by the asker's judgement about when to trust the answer.

## Sub lesson 2 — Building the seeing app — and judging its answers

The built app follows the unit's pattern: **camera → recognition → response** (speak the label, show it, act on it), tested against the class's own objects in the room's own light — where its **mistakes become the lesson**: the recogniser confuses the similar, fails in the strange light, and answers confidently where it should doubt, exactly the machine-learning limits the course has taught since Grade 8's neural network. The builder's closing craft is designing the app to **show its confidence and survive its errors** instead of hiding them.
