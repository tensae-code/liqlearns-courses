# Image Recognition with AI in App Inventor — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) How does recognition enter the app?**
**Answer: As an AI extension/component the app hands a picture to**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does it hand back?**
**Answer: Its judgement — recognised labels, with confidences**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Where does the picture come from?**
**Answer: The camera component, or chosen from the device**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Who trained the component?**
**Answer: It was trained on examples elsewhere — the app merely asks it**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which unit teaches it?**
**Answer: Greece Grade 9 Pliroforiki C, Unit 4**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) State the app's pattern.**
**Answer: Camera → recognition → response**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Where is it tested?**
**Answer: Against the class's own objects, in the room's own light**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Name a mistake the lesson expects.**
**Answer: Confusing the similar (or failing in strange light, or confident where it should doubt)**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What must the app show?**
**Answer: Its confidence**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What must it survive?**
**Answer: Its errors — by design, not by hiding them**
