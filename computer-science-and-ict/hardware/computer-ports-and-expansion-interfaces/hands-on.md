# Hands-on — Computer Ports and Expansion Interfaces

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Port census', 'Examine a real computer (power off). List every port and slot you can find, name each, and say what joins there. Mark the ones this lesson names.')

## Task two
('Choose the connection', 'For a printer, an external disk, a monitor and a video camera, choose the best connection this lesson offers and defend each choice in one sentence.')
