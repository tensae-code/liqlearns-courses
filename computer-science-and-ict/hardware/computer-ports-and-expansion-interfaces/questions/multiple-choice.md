# Computer Ports and Expansion Interfaces — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is an interface?**
a) A bargain between two sides — a physical connector plus the rules of exchange  b) The general expansion bus for add-in cards such as network and sound cards  c) A dedicated slot for the graphics card  d) An interface for chains of devices, above all disks
**Answer: a) A bargain between two sides — a physical connector plus the rules of exchange**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is PCI?**
a) A dedicated slot for the graphics card  b) The general expansion bus for add-in cards such as network and sound cards  c) An interface for chains of devices, above all disks  d) USB
**Answer: b) The general expansion bus for add-in cards such as network and sound cards**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What was AGP for?**
a) An interface for chains of devices, above all disks  b) USB  c) A dedicated slot for the graphics card  d) The serial and parallel ports
**Answer: c) A dedicated slot for the graphics card**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is SCSI?**
a) USB  b) The serial and parallel ports  c) Picture and sound to a screen in one cable  d) An interface for chains of devices, above all disks
**Answer: d) An interface for chains of devices, above all disks**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Name the universal modern port.**
a) USB  b) The serial and parallel ports  c) Picture and sound to a screen in one cable  d) PATA (older, parallel) replaced by SATA (serial)
**Answer: a) USB**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Which ports did USB largely replace?**
a) Picture and sound to a screen in one cable  b) The serial and parallel ports  c) PATA (older, parallel) replaced by SATA (serial)  d) Speed, width and simplicity
**Answer: b) The serial and parallel ports**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does HDMI carry?**
a) PATA (older, parallel) replaced by SATA (serial)  b) Speed, width and simplicity  c) Picture and sound to a screen in one cable  d) Its age and its possibilities
**Answer: c) Picture and sound to a screen in one cable**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Name the two disk interfaces in the lesson's order.**
a) Speed, width and simplicity  b) Its age and its possibilities  c) A bargain between two sides — a physical connector plus the rules of exchange  d) PATA (older, parallel) replaced by SATA (serial)
**Answer: d) PATA (older, parallel) replaced by SATA (serial)**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does every connection generation trade?**
a) Speed, width and simplicity  b) Its age and its possibilities  c) A bargain between two sides — a physical connector plus the rules of exchange  d) The general expansion bus for add-in cards such as network and sound cards
**Answer: a) Speed, width and simplicity**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What do a machine's ports tell you?**
a) A bargain between two sides — a physical connector plus the rules of exchange  b) Its age and its possibilities  c) The general expansion bus for add-in cards such as network and sound cards  d) A dedicated slot for the graphics card
**Answer: b) Its age and its possibilities**
