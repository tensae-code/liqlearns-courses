# Computer Ports and Expansion Interfaces — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is an interface?**
**Answer: A bargain between two sides — a physical connector plus the rules of exchange**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is PCI?**
**Answer: The general expansion bus for add-in cards such as network and sound cards**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What was AGP for?**
**Answer: A dedicated slot for the graphics card**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is SCSI?**
**Answer: An interface for chains of devices, above all disks**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Name the universal modern port.**
**Answer: USB**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Which ports did USB largely replace?**
**Answer: The serial and parallel ports**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does HDMI carry?**
**Answer: Picture and sound to a screen in one cable**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Name the two disk interfaces in the lesson's order.**
**Answer: PATA (older, parallel) replaced by SATA (serial)**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does every connection generation trade?**
**Answer: Speed, width and simplicity**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What do a machine's ports tell you?**
**Answer: Its age and its possibilities**
