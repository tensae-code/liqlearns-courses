# Computer Ports and Expansion Interfaces — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is an interface?" is: A bargain between two sides — a physical connector plus the rules of exchange**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is PCI?" is: A dedicated slot for the graphics card**
**Answer: False** — The general expansion bus for add-in cards such as network and sound cards

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What was AGP for?" is: A dedicated slot for the graphics card**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is SCSI?" is: USB**
**Answer: False** — An interface for chains of devices, above all disks

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Name the universal modern port." is: USB**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Which ports did USB largely replace?" is: Picture and sound to a screen in one cable**
**Answer: False** — The serial and parallel ports

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does HDMI carry?" is: Picture and sound to a screen in one cable**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Name the two disk interfaces in the lesson's order." is: Speed, width and simplicity**
**Answer: False** — PATA (older, parallel) replaced by SATA (serial)

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does every connection generation trade?" is: Speed, width and simplicity**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What do a machine's ports tell you?" is: A bargain between two sides — a physical connector plus the rules of exchange**
**Answer: False** — Its age and its possibilities
