# Speech recognition — entering data by talking

## Do it first

Say a sentence out loud, slowly. Now imagine the computer writing it down as you speak, word by word, without any keyboard. Before reading on, say where the words enter, and what the computer must do with your voice before it can obey.

## Recognising human speech

A speech recognition system is the computer's ability to recognise human speech. Speaking becomes a way of entering data — the voice replaces the keyboard.

[Picture 10936 — speech recognition]

## Microphone in, action out

The microphone takes speech in; the computer processes it and performs the action ordered by speech. So data is entered by speaking instead of typing. Watch the GIF: as the words are spoken, they appear as text on the screen. The book describes speech recognition as the latest technology, still under research — it is good, and still being improved.
