# Speech recognition — entering data by talking — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** A speech recognition system is the computer's ability to —
a) Speak like a person  b) Recognise human speech  c) Print voices  d) Record music only
**Answer: b) Recognise human speech** — It is the computer's ability to recognise human speech.

**2.** In speech recognition, speech is taken in by —
a) The printer  b) The microphone  c) The monitor  d) The scanner
**Answer: b) The microphone** — The microphone takes speech in.

**3.** After taking speech in, the computer —
a) Stores it forever unheard  b) Processes it and performs the action ordered by speech  c) Prints it at once  d) Deletes it
**Answer: b) Processes it and performs the action ordered by speech** — The computer processes the speech and performs the action it ordered.

**4.** Speech recognition lets data be entered by —
a) Typing only  b) Speaking instead of typing  c) Drawing  d) Scanning
**Answer: b) Speaking instead of typing** — Data is entered by speaking instead of typing.
