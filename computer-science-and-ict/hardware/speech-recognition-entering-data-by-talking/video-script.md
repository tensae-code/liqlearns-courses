# Video script — Speech recognition — entering data by talking

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Speech recognition.**

First, brainstorm: what do you already know about this? Say it out loud.

Now the one thing: a speech recognition system lets data be entered by speaking instead of typing: the microphone takes speech in, the computer processes it and performs the action the speech ordered.

**Recognising human speech.** A speech recognition system is the computer's ability to recognise human speech.
**Microphone in, action out.** The microphone takes speech in; the computer processes it and performs the action ordered by speech.

Now you: Draw a voice wave entering a microphone, with words appearing on a screen.
