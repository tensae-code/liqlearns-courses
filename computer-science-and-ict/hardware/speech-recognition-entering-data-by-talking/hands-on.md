# Hands-on — Speech recognition — entering data by talking

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Dictation test
If a phone or computer offers voice typing, dictate one sentence slowly and one quickly. Read what appeared. Where did it succeed, and where did the still-under-research part show?

## Command chain
With a partner playing the computer: speak a command ('open the book to page five'). The partner must repeat what they 'recognised' before acting. Mishearings show why recognition is a real processing task.
