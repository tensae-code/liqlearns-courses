# Installing Main Memory — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What must be matched before installing memory?**
a) The module's type to the board's memory slots  b) The off-centre notch in its contact edge, over the slot's key  c) Its end clips are opened outward  d) By its edges, contacts down, standing straight above the slot
**Answer: a**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What makes a module fit only one way round?**
a) Its end clips are opened outward  b) The off-centre notch in its contact edge, over the slot's key  c) By its edges, contacts down, standing straight above the slot  d) Both thumbs press straight down, evenly at both ends
**Answer: b**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is done to the slot first?**
a) By its edges, contacts down, standing straight above the slot  b) Both thumbs press straight down, evenly at both ends  c) Its end clips are opened outward  d) The end clips snapping up into the module's side notches by themselves
**Answer: c**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How is the module held?**
a) Both thumbs press straight down, evenly at both ends  b) The end clips snapping up into the module's side notches by themselves  c) A dead machine or beep codes  d) By its edges, contacts down, standing straight above the slot
**Answer: d**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How is it seated?**
a) Both thumbs press straight down, evenly at both ends  b) The end clips snapping up into the module's side notches by themselves  c) A dead machine or beep codes  d) Laos Grade 8 ICT, Chapter 2 §2.3
**Answer: a**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is the receipt of a seated module?**
a) A dead machine or beep codes  b) The end clips snapping up into the module's side notches by themselves  c) Laos Grade 8 ICT, Chapter 2 §2.3  d) Even pressure seats the whole contact edge at once
**Answer: b**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What announces an unseated module at switch-on?**
a) Laos Grade 8 ICT, Chapter 2 §2.3  b) Even pressure seats the whole contact edge at once  c) A dead machine or beep codes  d) No — the notch and key prevent it; force only damages
**Answer: c**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Which chapter teaches this?**
a) Even pressure seats the whole contact edge at once  b) No — the notch and key prevent it; force only damages  c) The module's type to the board's memory slots  d) Laos Grade 8 ICT, Chapter 2 §2.3
**Answer: d**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why press at both ends, not one?**
a) Even pressure seats the whole contact edge at once  b) No — the notch and key prevent it; force only damages  c) The module's type to the board's memory slots  d) The off-centre notch in its contact edge, over the slot's key
**Answer: a**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Can a module be forced the wrong way round?**
a) The module's type to the board's memory slots  b) No — the notch and key prevent it; force only damages  c) The off-centre notch in its contact edge, over the slot's key  d) Its end clips are opened outward
**Answer: b**
