# Installing Main Memory — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What must be matched before installing memory?**
**Answer: The module's type to the board's memory slots**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What makes a module fit only one way round?**
**Answer: The off-centre notch in its contact edge, over the slot's key**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is done to the slot first?**
**Answer: Its end clips are opened outward**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How is the module held?**
**Answer: By its edges, contacts down, standing straight above the slot**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How is it seated?**
**Answer: Both thumbs press straight down, evenly at both ends**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is the receipt of a seated module?**
**Answer: The end clips snapping up into the module's side notches by themselves**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What announces an unseated module at switch-on?**
**Answer: A dead machine or beep codes**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Which chapter teaches this?**
**Answer: Laos Grade 8 ICT, Chapter 2 §2.3**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why press at both ends, not one?**
**Answer: Even pressure seats the whole contact edge at once**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Can a module be forced the wrong way round?**
**Answer: No — the notch and key prevent it; force only damages**
