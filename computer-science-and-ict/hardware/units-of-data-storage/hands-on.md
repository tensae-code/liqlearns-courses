# Hands-on — Units of data storage — bit, byte, KB, MB, GB, TB

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Staircase from memory
Draw the staircase on paper with six steps. Label each step and write what it equals in the unit below. Cover it and redraw it until you can do it without looking.

## Size detective
Find three sizes written anywhere at home — on a phone's storage screen, a disc, a flash stick package. Write each size and place it on your staircase: which step, and roughly how many of the step below it holds?
