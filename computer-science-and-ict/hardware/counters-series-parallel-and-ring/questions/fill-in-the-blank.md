# Counters — Series, Parallel and Ring — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) The series counter is also called the ____ counter, because the change travels stage by stage.**
**Answer: ripple** — Also 'asynchronous', since the stages do not share one clock instant.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) An n-bit binary counter counts modulo ____.**
**Answer: 2ⁿ (two to the n)** — n flip-flops give 2ⁿ states before the count wraps to zero.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) In a parallel counter, the stages change together because they all share one ____.**
**Answer: clock** — Synchronous means: same pulse line, same instant, for every stage.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) A ring counter starts with exactly ____ bit(s) set to 1 in its loop.**
**Answer: one** — The single 1 circulates; its position is the state.
