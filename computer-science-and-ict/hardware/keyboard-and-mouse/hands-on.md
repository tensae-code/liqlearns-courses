# Hands-on — Keyboard and mouse

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Air desk
Practise in the air: mime typing a sentence, then mime pointing, clicking and dragging an icon across a table. A partner names each motion as you do it.

## Drag on paper
Draw a screen on paper with three icons. Draw the path a pointer would take to drag the middle icon to a corner, marking where the click-hold starts and where the release happens.
