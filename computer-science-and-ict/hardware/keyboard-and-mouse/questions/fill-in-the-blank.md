# Keyboard and mouse — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) The ____ is the most common input device for entering data.**
**Answer: keyboard**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) The mouse is a ____ device.**
**Answer: pointing**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) To move an icon: press, hold, ____ and release.**
**Answer: drag / move**
