# Keyboard and mouse — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) The keyboard is —**
a) a rare device  b) the most common input device for entering data  c) an output device
**Answer: b) the most common input device for entering data**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Data entered by keyboard is usually —**
a) text — names, essays, passwords  b) photographs  c) sound
**Answer: a) text — names, essays, passwords**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) The mouse is a —**
a) printing device  b) pointing device  c) storage device
**Answer: b) pointing device**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) The mouse's three jobs are —**
a) type, print, scan  b) point, click, move things around  c) copy, save, delete
**Answer: b) point, click, move things around**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) Dragging means —**
a) clicking twice fast  b) pressing, holding, moving and releasing  c) shaking the mouse
**Answer: b) pressing, holding, moving and releasing**
