# Integrated Circuits and the CPU — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is an integrated circuit?**
**Answer: A whole circuit built as one piece on a single chip of semiconductor**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name its characteristics.**
**Answer: How many components it gathers, its speed, its power hunger, its pins**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is the integration scale?**
**Answer: How many components one chip gathers — from handfuls to billions**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What did integration do to the computer?**
**Answer: Shrank it — the room of panels became a thumbnail, cheaper and faster**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which chapter teaches it?**
**Answer: Greece Grade 11 EPAL Basika Themata, Chapter 13**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name the CPU's three inside parts here.**
**Answer: Control unit, arithmetic-logic unit, registers**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Name a modern trend the chapter traces.**
**Answer: More cores in parallel (or smaller transistors, speed by organisation)**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) The ladder's climb, in one line.**
**Answer: Gates became circuits, circuits became chips, chips became the CPU**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does the control unit do?**
**Answer: Directs the work**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What do registers hold?**
**Answer: The moment's work**
