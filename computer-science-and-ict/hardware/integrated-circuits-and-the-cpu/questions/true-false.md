# Integrated Circuits and the CPU — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: A whole circuit built as one piece on a single chip of semiconductor**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: How many components one chip gathers — from handfuls to billions** — as the answer to: Name its characteristics.
**Answer: False — How many components it gathers, its speed, its power hunger, its pins**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: How many components one chip gathers — from handfuls to billions**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Greece Grade 11 EPAL Basika Themata, Chapter 13** — as the answer to: What did integration do to the computer?
**Answer: False — Shrank it — the room of panels became a thumbnail, cheaper and faster**
