# Hands-on — The Computer Platform — Choosing Specifications

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Judge three machines
Find three real computer offers (a shop leaflet or a website). List each one's processor, memory, storage and ports, and pick the one you would buy for school work — saying why.

## Upgrade plan
Take the oldest computer you know. Write which two parts you would upgrade first and what each upgrade would improve.
