# The Computer Platform — Choosing Specifications — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does Grade 8's hardware unit call the whole machine?**
**Answer: The computer platform**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What sits at the centre of the platform?**
**Answer: The motherboard**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What works on the motherboard?**
**Answer: The processor**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What connects everything else to the platform?**
**Answer: The ports**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does the book set out before any single part is studied?**
**Answer: The platform's standard specifications**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does choosing a computer mean?**
**Answer: Comparing specifications**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Against what are specifications compared?**
**Answer: The work the machine must do and the money there is**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Name two specifications to compare.**
**Answer: The processor and the amount of memory or storage**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What can an upgradeable platform take later?**
**Answer: More memory or a bigger disk**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why is upgradeability itself a specification to check?**
**Answer: A platform that can be upgraded serves longer**
