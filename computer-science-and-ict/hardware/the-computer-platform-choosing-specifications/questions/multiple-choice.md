# The Computer Platform — Choosing Specifications — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does Grade 8's hardware unit call the whole machine?**
a) The computer platform  b) The motherboard  c) The ports  d) Comparing specifications
**Answer: a) The computer platform**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What sits at the centre of the platform?**
a) The processor  b) The motherboard  c) The platform's standard specifications  d) The work the machine must do and the money there is
**Answer: b) The motherboard**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What works on the motherboard?**
a) The ports  b) Comparing specifications  c) The processor  d) The processor and the amount of memory or storage
**Answer: c) The processor**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What connects everything else to the platform?**
a) The platform's standard specifications  b) The work the machine must do and the money there is  c) More memory or a bigger disk  d) The ports
**Answer: d) The ports**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does the book set out before any single part is studied?**
a) The platform's standard specifications  b) Comparing specifications  c) The processor and the amount of memory or storage  d) A platform that can be upgraded serves longer
**Answer: a) The platform's standard specifications**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does choosing a computer mean?**
a) The work the machine must do and the money there is  b) Comparing specifications  c) More memory or a bigger disk  d) The computer platform
**Answer: b) Comparing specifications**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Against what are specifications compared?**
a) The processor and the amount of memory or storage  b) A platform that can be upgraded serves longer  c) The work the machine must do and the money there is  d) The motherboard
**Answer: c) The work the machine must do and the money there is**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Name two specifications to compare.**
a) More memory or a bigger disk  b) The computer platform  c) The processor  d) The processor and the amount of memory or storage
**Answer: d) The processor and the amount of memory or storage**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What can an upgradeable platform take later?**
a) More memory or a bigger disk  b) A platform that can be upgraded serves longer  c) The motherboard  d) The ports
**Answer: a) More memory or a bigger disk**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why is upgradeability itself a specification to check?**
a) The computer platform  b) A platform that can be upgraded serves longer  c) The processor  d) The platform's standard specifications
**Answer: b) A platform that can be upgraded serves longer**
