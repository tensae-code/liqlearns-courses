# Keyboard Trainer Practice — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a keyboard trainer?**
**Answer: A program that teaches typing**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does the trainer show?**
**Answer: Letters, words and lines to type**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What two things does it count?**
**Answer: Speed and mistakes**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Where do the eyes stay while training?**
**Answer: On the screen, not down at the hands**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) In the book's order, what comes just before the trainer?**
**Answer: Meeting the keyboard itself**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does the second trainer lesson add?**
**Answer: Longer lines, steadier rhythm, fewer looks down**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How is the second lesson marked in the book?**
**Answer: Continuation (продолжение)**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What kind of thing is typing, in this lesson's view?**
**Answer: A habit to build, not a fact to finish**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Whose score do you race?**
**Answer: Only your own yesterday's**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What two numbers does the trainer report?**
**Answer: Speed and mistakes**
