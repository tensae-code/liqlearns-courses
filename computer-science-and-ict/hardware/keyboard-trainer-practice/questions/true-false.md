# Keyboard Trainer Practice — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is a keyboard trainer?" is: A program that teaches typing**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does the trainer show?" is: Speed and mistakes**
**Answer: False** — Letters, words and lines to type

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What two things does it count?" is: Speed and mistakes**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Where do the eyes stay while training?" is: Meeting the keyboard itself**
**Answer: False** — On the screen, not down at the hands

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "In the book's order, what comes just before the trainer?" is: Meeting the keyboard itself**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does the second trainer lesson add?" is: Continuation (продолжение)**
**Answer: False** — Longer lines, steadier rhythm, fewer looks down

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "How is the second lesson marked in the book?" is: Continuation (продолжение)**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What kind of thing is typing, in this lesson's view?" is: Only your own yesterday's**
