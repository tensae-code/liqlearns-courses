# Keyboard Trainer Practice — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a keyboard trainer?**
a) A program that teaches typing  b) Letters, words and lines to type  c) Speed and mistakes  d) On the screen, not down at the hands
**Answer: a) A program that teaches typing**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does the trainer show?**
a) Speed and mistakes  b) Letters, words and lines to type  c) On the screen, not down at the hands  d) Meeting the keyboard itself
**Answer: b) Letters, words and lines to type**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What two things does it count?**
a) On the screen, not down at the hands  b) Meeting the keyboard itself  c) Speed and mistakes  d) Longer lines, steadier rhythm, fewer looks down
**Answer: c) Speed and mistakes**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Where do the eyes stay while training?**
a) Meeting the keyboard itself  b) Longer lines, steadier rhythm, fewer looks down  c) Continuation (продолжение)  d) On the screen, not down at the hands
**Answer: d) On the screen, not down at the hands**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) In the book's order, what comes just before the trainer?**
a) Meeting the keyboard itself  b) Longer lines, steadier rhythm, fewer looks down  c) Continuation (продолжение)  d) A habit to build, not a fact to finish
**Answer: a) Meeting the keyboard itself**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does the second trainer lesson add?**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How is the second lesson marked in the book?**
a) A habit to build, not a fact to finish  b) Only your own yesterday's  c) Continuation (продолжение)  d) Speed and mistakes
**Answer: c) Continuation (продолжение)**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What kind of thing is typing, in this lesson's view?**
a) Only your own yesterday's  b) Speed and mistakes  c) A program that teaches typing  d) A habit to build, not a fact to finish
**Answer: d) A habit to build, not a fact to finish**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Whose score do you race?**
a) Only your own yesterday's  b) Speed and mistakes  c) A program that teaches typing  d) Letters, words and lines to type

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What two numbers does the trainer report?**
