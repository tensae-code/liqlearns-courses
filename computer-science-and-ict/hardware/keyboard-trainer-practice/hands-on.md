# Hands-on — Keyboard Trainer Practice

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Ten minutes at the trainer', 'Do ten minutes of trainer exercises, note your speed and mistakes, then repeat one exercise and note both numbers again.')

## Task two
('Eyes-up drill', 'Type one trainer line without looking down at all; count how many times you wanted to look, and try the line again.')
