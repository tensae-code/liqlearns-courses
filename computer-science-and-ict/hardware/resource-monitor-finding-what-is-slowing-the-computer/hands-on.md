# Hands-on — Resource Monitor — finding what is slowing the computer

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Read the CPU tab
On a school computer: open Resource Monitor by search. Find the CPU tab. Write the name of the process using the most CPU, its colour, and what the colour means.

## End or learn?
Your teacher names three processes (one heavy and known, one heavy and unknown, one not responding). For each, write your move — End process, Search online first, or the not-responding reading — and defend it from the lesson.
