# Video script — Resource Monitor — finding what is slowing the computer

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. Short script; the placement names no full video need for this lesson.

---

Slow machine. The quick view blames the CPU —
but not the culprit. Open Resource Monitor.
Search its name. Open it.

The CPU tab names names.
Black: running. Blue: suspended.
Red: not responding.

One app eating the CPU?
Right-click. End process.
Never heard of it?
Right-click. Search online. Learn first.

Find the greedy one. Stop it, or restart it.
