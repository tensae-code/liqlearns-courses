# Boolean Laws and De Morgan's Theorems — short answer

Answer in your own words, in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Why must the second distributive law be proved by truth table instead of just believed from school algebra?**
**Answer: Because school algebra does not have it — in ordinary numbers a + bc is not (a+b)(a+c) — so the habit 'it looks like algebra' is not evidence. An eight-row truth table shows both sides agree on every row, which is the proof.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Push the bar through: write the bar over (A + B̄) using De Morgan, step by step.**
**Answer: Break the bar over the bracket: each letter gets its own bar and + becomes ·, giving Ā · (bar over B̄); the double bar on B cancels, so the result is Ā · B.**
