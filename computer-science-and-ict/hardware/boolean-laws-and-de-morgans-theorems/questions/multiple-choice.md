# Boolean Laws and De Morgan's Theorems — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) The absorption law says A + A·B equals —**
a) A·B  b) B  c) A  d) 1
**Answer: c) A** — The term A·B is swallowed by A: whenever A is 1 the answer is already 1, and when A is 0 both terms are 0.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) A · Ā always equals —**
a) A  b) 1  c) 0  d) Ā
**Answer: c) 0** — The complement law for AND: a value and its opposite together give 0 on every row.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Which of these is the null (dominance) law for OR?**
a) A + 0 = A  b) A + 1 = 1  c) A + A = A  d) A + Ā = 1
**Answer: b) A + 1 = 1** — A single 1 settles an OR: A + 1 = 1 whatever A is.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) By De Morgan, the bar over (A + B) equals —**
a) Ā + B̄  b) Ā · B̄  c) A · B with a bar left on top  d) Ā · B
**Answer: b) Ā · B̄** — Break the bar, change the operation: NOT (A OR B) = (NOT A) AND (NOT B).

**5. (Sub lesson 2) By De Morgan, the bar over (A · B) equals —**
a) Ā · B̄  b) Ā + B̄  c) A + B  d) Ā · B̄ with a bar on top
**Answer: b) Ā + B̄** — Break the bar, change the operation: NOT (A AND B) = (NOT A) OR (NOT B).

**6. (Sub lesson 2) The expression A·B + A·B̄ simplifies to —**
a) B  b) A  c) A·B  d) 1
**Answer: b) A** — Factor out A: A·(B + B̄) = A·1 = A, by distribution, complement and identity.
