# Boolean Laws and De Morgan's Theorems — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) The law A · 1 = A is called the ____ law.**
**Answer: identity** — Multiplying by 1 (or adding 0) leaves the value unchanged.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) A + Ā = 1 is the ____ law.**
**Answer: complement** — A value or its opposite covers everything.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) In De Morgan's theorems, when the bar breaks, the operation ____.**
**Answer: changes** — + becomes · and · becomes + — break the bar, change the operation.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) NOT (A · B) can be said in words as: not A ____ not B.**
**Answer: or** — The negation of an AND is the OR of the negations.
