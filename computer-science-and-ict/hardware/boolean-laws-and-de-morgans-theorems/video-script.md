---

Two expressions can look different and do exactly the same job.

The laws of Boolean algebra are the safe ways to rewrite them.

A + A·B is just A. The second term is swallowed. That is absorption.

A thing or its opposite is everything: A + not-A is 1.

De Morgan has two theorems. Learn the picture: break the bar, change the operation.

Not (A or B) is not-A AND not-B.

Not (A and B) is not-A OR not-B.

Always finish the same way: build both truth tables and see them match.
