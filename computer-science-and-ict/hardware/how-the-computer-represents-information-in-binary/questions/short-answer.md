# How the Computer Represents Information in Binary — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) In what form does the computer hold information?**
**Answer: Binary form — patterns of 0s and 1s in memory**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How is text stored?**
**Answer: By character codes — each character matched to a number, stored in binary**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Do pictures and sounds escape the binary rule?**
**Answer: No — they are held in their own binary codings**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What two kinds of binary does memory hold?**
**Answer: Data and the program**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How are data and program stored?**
**Answer: In the same memory, in the same form**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name the scheme's four parts.**
**Answer: Input, processor, memory and output**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does the processor work in step with?**
**Answer: Memory — fetching data and instructions piece by piece**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is a character code?**
**Answer: The number matched to a character for storage**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why is the scheme taught before the parts multiply?**
**Answer: Every later hardware lesson hangs its parts on this scheme**
