# Troubleshooting — find the fault, find its cause — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Troubleshooting is —
a) random guessing until something works  b) a systematic approach to locating a fault and identifying its cause  c) replacing the whole computer  d) a kind of antivirus
**Answer: b) a systematic approach to locating a fault and identifying its cause**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A malfunctioning printer is a —
a) software problem  b) hardware problem  c) network store  d) driver licence
**Answer: b) hardware problem**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Protecting a system from viruses and worms belongs to troubleshooting's —
a) hardware side  b) software side  c) furniture side  d) paper side
**Answer: b) software side**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Troubleshooting skill grows from —
a) age alone  b) knowledge and experience  c) buying tools  d) watching others only
**Answer: b) knowledge and experience**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** When stuck, the first move is to —
a) give up  b) search the internet — others have shared solutions  c) format the disk  d) buy a new machine
**Answer: b) search the internet — others have shared solutions**
