# Troubleshooting — find the fault, find its cause — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Why can two troubleshooters work the same fault differently?
**Answer: No two processes are identical; each user refines the systematic skill from their own knowledge and experience.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the two kinds of problems troubleshooting covers, with one example each.
**Answer: Hardware problems, e.g. a malfunctioning printer or modem; software problems, e.g. operating-system or application faults, including damage from viruses and worms.**
