# Hands-on — Troubleshooting — find the fault, find its cause

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Fault sort
Write eight faults on cards — four hardware, four software. Shuffle and sort them against the clock; a partner checks each sort against the lesson's examples.

## The black-screen investigation
Take the black screen from Do it first. Write a systematic investigation: what you check first, second, third — each check ruling something in or out, no random fixes.
