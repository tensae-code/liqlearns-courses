# Lesson plan — Troubleshooting — find the fault, find its cause

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Hardware · **Lesson:** Troubleshooting — find the fault, find its cause
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 5 — Maintenance and Troubleshooting (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Troubleshooting is a systematic hunt: locate the fault, identify its cause — a learned skill that grows with knowledge and experience, not guesswork.
**This lesson's own level:** It stands on the install and uninstall lessons: the learner can now work a machine's software before learning to fix it.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or in steps — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
1. **A systematic approach — hardware and software** — the definition; hardware faults and software faults, including malicious damage.
2. **A skill that grows — and what to do when stuck** — no two processes identical; skill from knowledge and experience; search first, then ask — without fear.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Installing software on a PC and Uninstalling software on a PC, in this same title. Recommended, never forced — a learner may land on this lesson directly, and it stands alone.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Define troubleshooting as a systematic approach.
2. Tell hardware problems from software problems, with examples.
3. Say how troubleshooting skill grows, and what to do when stuck.
