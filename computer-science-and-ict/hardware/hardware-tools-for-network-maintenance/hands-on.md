# Hands-on — Hardware tools for network maintenance — crimper to multimeter

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Tool bench sketch
Draw the five tools in a row and label each with its job and the thing it works on — connector, cable, panel, wire pair, circuit.

## Job cards
Write five job cards (make a cable, prove a cable, seat wires in a panel, trace one pair, measure resistance). A partner assigns the correct tool to each and defends the choice.
