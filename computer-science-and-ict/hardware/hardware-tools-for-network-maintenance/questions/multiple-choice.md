# Hardware tools for network maintenance — crimper to multimeter — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A wire/cable crimper —
a) attaches media connectors to cable ends to make or modify network cables  b) measures voltage  c) seats wires in a panel  d) sends a tone
**Answer: a) attaches media connectors to cable ends to make or modify network cables**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A cable tester (line tester) —
a) makes cables  b) verifies whether a signal is transmitted by a given cable  c) measures resistance  d) connects wires to a panel
**Answer: b) verifies whether a signal is transmitted by a given cable**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The punch-down tool connects wires to —
a) a printer  b) a patch panel or punch-down block  c) a battery  d) a monitor
**Answer: b) a patch panel or punch-down block**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A tone generator sends its signal through —
a) the whole network  b) one pair of UTP wires  c) the power cable  d) a wireless link
**Answer: b) one pair of UTP wires**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A multimeter measures —
a) voltage, current and resistance  b) cable length only  c) signal names  d) packet routes
**Answer: a) voltage, current and resistance**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The multimeter comes in —
a) pocket and wall forms  b) hand-held field and bench-top forms  c) only bench-top form  d) only wearable form
**Answer: b) hand-held field and bench-top forms**
