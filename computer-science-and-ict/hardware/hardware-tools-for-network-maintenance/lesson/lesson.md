# Hardware tools for network maintenance — crimper to multimeter

## Do it first

A new classroom needs its network cables made, checked and wired into a panel — and one cable's signal must be proved. Write any tool names you know for making and testing cables. Then meet the five.

## Sub lesson 1 — Making and proving cables — crimper and cable tester

A **wire/cable crimper attaches media connectors to cable ends**, to **make or modify network cables**.

A **cable tester (line tester) verifies whether a signal is transmitted by a given cable**, when **diagnosing connectivity**.

> **Teacher evidence (teacher guide — evidence, printed pp. 111–112):** the guide's review answers define the same two tools in the same words — the crimper attaching media connectors so cables can be made or modified, and the cable tester verifying whether a signal is transmitted by a cable when diagnosing connectivity problems. The guide gives **no teaching section** for this part of the unit; these review definitions are filed as labelled evidence only.

## Sub lesson 2 — Seating, tracing, measuring — punch-down tool, tone generator, multimeter

A **punch-down tool connects cable wires directly to a patch panel or punch-down block** in a wiring closet, **more easily than by hand**.

A **tone generator sends an electrical signal through one pair of UTP wires** to **verify signals are passing** — often to **confirm phone connectivity**.

A **multimeter** is the **electronic measuring instrument for voltage, current and resistance**, in **hand-held field** and **bench-top** forms.
