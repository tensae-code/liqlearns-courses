# Video script — Shut down properly — or hibernate

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. Short script; the placement names no full video need for this lesson.

---

End of the day. End it properly.

Save your work.
Close the programs.
Shut the PC down.

A machine left on cannot cool —
and a hot machine's performance suffers.

Shutting down would break the routine?
Then low-power. Or hibernate.
Rest — but do not just leave it running.
