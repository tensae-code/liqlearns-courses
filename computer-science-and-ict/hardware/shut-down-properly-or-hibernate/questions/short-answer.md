# Shut down properly — or hibernate — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** State the book's full closing rule in one sentence.
**Answer: End every session by saving work, closing programs and shutting the PC down — or, where shutdown would break the daily routine, use low-power or hibernation mode.**

**2.** Why does the order save → close → shut down matter?
**Answer: Saving after closing could lose the work; the order protects the work first, then closes its programs, then powers down.**
