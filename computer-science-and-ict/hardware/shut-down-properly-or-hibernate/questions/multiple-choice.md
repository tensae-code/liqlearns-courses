# Shut down properly — or hibernate — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The closing steps, in order, are —
a) close, save, shut down  b) save, close programs, shut the PC down  c) shut down, then save  d) switch the screen off
**Answer: b) save, close programs, shut the PC down** — save → close → shut down.

**2.** A machine left on —
a) rests better  b) cannot cool, and its performance suffers  c) backs itself up  d) uses no power
**Answer: b) cannot cool, and its performance suffers** — the book's reason.

**3.** The accepted alternative to shutdown is —
a) leaving it on  b) low-power or hibernation mode  c) unplugging it  d) closing the screen only
**Answer: b) low-power or hibernation mode** — low-power or hibernation.

**4.** Hibernation is accepted where —
a) you feel like it  b) shutting down would break the daily routine  c) the machine is new  d) never
**Answer: b) shutting down would break the daily routine** — only under that condition.

**5.** The first closing step is —
a) close the programs  b) save your work  c) shut down  d) log out of the internet
**Answer: b) save your work** — save first — always.
