# Shut down properly — or hibernate

## Do it first

It is the end of the school day. The last pupil leaves the computer as it is — programs open, machine humming. Write the three things that should have happened before they stood up. Then read the book's closing rule for the whole unit.

## Shut down properly — or hibernate

**End every session by saving work, closing programs and shutting the PC down.** Three steps, in order:

1. **Save your work.**
2. **Close the programs.**
3. **Shut the PC down.**

[Picture 17718 — save, close, shut down as a three-step closing strip]

Why it matters: a machine **left on cannot cool**, and **its performance suffers**.

The book's one exception: where shutting down would **break the daily routine**, **low-power or hibernation mode** is the accepted alternative — rest, without the full close.
