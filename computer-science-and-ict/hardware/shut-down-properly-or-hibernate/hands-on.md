# Hands-on — Shut down properly — or hibernate

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## The closing drill
At the end of a real session on a school computer: save, close, shut down — narrating each step aloud as you do it. Your partner ticks the three steps in order.

## On, off, or hibernate?
Cards for five situations (end of term; lunch break in a busy office; a home machine at midnight; a library desk machine between borrowers; a machine mid-backup). Rule on each: shut down, or hibernate — with the book's reason.
