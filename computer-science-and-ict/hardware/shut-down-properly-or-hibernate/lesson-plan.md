# Lesson plan — Shut down properly — or hibernate

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Hardware · **Lesson:** Shut down properly — or hibernate
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 5 — Hardware Troubleshooting and Preventive Maintenance (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** End every session the book's way — save your work, close the programs, shut the PC down — because a machine left on cannot cool and its performance suffers; where shutdown breaks the routine, hibernate.
**This lesson's own level:** It stands on Protecting a computer from power problems: the unit closes with the habit that closes every day.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. ## If the learner is stuck
- Just switches the screen off → the machine is still on, still hot, still suffering; the close is save, close programs, shut the PC down.
- Hibernates everywhere 'because it is easier' → hibernation is the alternative only where shutdown would break the daily routine.
