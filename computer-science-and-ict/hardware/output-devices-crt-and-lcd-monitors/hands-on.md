# Hands-on — Output devices — soft copy and hard copy; CRT and LCD monitors

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Soft or hard?
Collect five outputs around you — phone screen, printed notice, projected slide, calculator display, receipt. Label each soft copy or hard copy, and name the output device.

## CRT hunt
Find an old CRT television or monitor if one exists nearby. Compare its depth and weight with a flat LCD. The tube is the reason.
