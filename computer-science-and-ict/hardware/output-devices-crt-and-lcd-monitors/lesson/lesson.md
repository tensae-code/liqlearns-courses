## Output devices — back into human form

Output devices get data out of the computer, converting it from computer-understandable form into human-understandable form. The output comes in two kinds: soft copy, which is displayed — a monitor or a projector — and hard copy, which is printed. The next lessons take the remaining monitor types and then printers and plotters.

## The monitor — CRT and LCD

The monitor is the most frequently used output device; its display surface is called a screen. Monitors are of four types: CRT, LCD, LED and gas plasma. The CRT (Cathode Ray Tube) is the technology of traditional computer monitors and televisions — the deep, heavy screens. The LCD (Liquid Crystal Display) is the most commonly used display, found in digital watches, clocks, calculators, smartphones, desktops, laptops and televisions.
