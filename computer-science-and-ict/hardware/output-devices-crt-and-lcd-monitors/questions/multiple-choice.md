# Output devices — soft copy and hard copy; CRT and LCD monitors — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Output devices convert data —
a) Into 0-and-1 form  b) From computer-understandable form into human-understandable form  c) Into electricity  d) Into storage
**Answer: b) From computer-understandable form into human-understandable form** — Output devices convert the computer's form into human-understandable form.

**2.** Soft copy is output that is —
a) Printed  b) Displayed — e.g. on a monitor or projector  c) Stored  d) Spoken only
**Answer: b) Displayed — e.g. on a monitor or projector** — Soft copy is displayed; hard copy is printed.

**3.** The most frequently used output device is —
a) The plotter  b) The monitor  c) The scanner  d) The joystick
**Answer: b) The monitor** — The monitor is the most frequently used output device.

**4.** A monitor's display surface is called —
a) The panel  b) The screen  c) The plate  d) The page
**Answer: b) The screen** — The display surface is called a screen.

**5.** CRT stands for —
a) Central Read Terminal  b) Cathode Ray Tube  c) Computer Reading Tool  d) Colour Raster Tube
**Answer: b) Cathode Ray Tube** — CRT = Cathode Ray Tube, the traditional monitor and television technology.

**6.** LCD is found in —
a) Only desktop computers  b) Digital watches, clocks, calculators, smartphones, desktops, laptops and televisions  c) Only cinemas  d) Only printers
**Answer: b) Digital watches, clocks, calculators, smartphones, desktops, laptops and televisions** — The LCD is the most commonly used display, found in all of these.
