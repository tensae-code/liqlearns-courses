**Output devices.**

First, brainstorm: what do you already know about this? Say it out loud.

Now the one thing: output devices convert the computer's form back into human-understandable form — soft copy on a display, hard copy on paper — and the monitor, the most used of them, comes as CRT, LCD, LED and gas plasma.

**Output devices — back into human form.** Output devices get data out of the computer, converting it from computer-understandable form into human-understandable form.
**The monitor — CRT and LCD.** The monitor is the most frequently used output device; its display surface is called a screen.

Now you: Draw one document as soft copy on a screen and as hard copy on paper, with a bulky CRT beside a flat LCD.
