# Device Manager — finding problem hardware

## Do it first

Somewhere inside Windows there is one organised list of every piece of hardware the machine knows — and the broken ones are flagged. Write the route you think reaches it. Then follow the book's five steps exactly and score your guess.

## Sub lesson 1 — Reaching Device Manager — five steps

**Device Manager** is the **central, organised view of all Windows-recognised hardware**. It is used to **configure devices, manage drivers, enable or disable hardware and spot conflicts**.

Reach it in the book's five printed steps:

1. Open the **search box**.
2. Open **Control Panel**.
3. Choose **Hardware and Sound**.
4. Look under **Devices and Printers**.
5. Click **Device Manager**.

## Sub lesson 2 — The four problem signs

Problem devices carry icons. Learn the four signs:

- **Yellow triangle with an exclamation mark** — the device **has a problem**.
- **Red X** — the device is **disabled, removed, or not found**.
- **Downward arrow** — the device is **disabled**.
- **Yellow question mark** — **Windows does not know which driver to install**; the fix is to **install the right driver**.

## Sub lesson 3 — Fixing a flagged device (teacher guide — evidence)

The teacher guide keys the working fix for a flagged device, step by step:

1. **Right-click** the device with the yellow flag.
2. Click **Properties**.
3. Open the **Driver** tab.
4. Click **Update Driver**.

If that does not cure it, the fallback is an **internet search** for the device and its fault.
