# Hands-on — Device Manager — finding problem hardware

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Five steps, live
On a school computer, reach Device Manager by the book's five steps — no shortcuts. Count the device groups you can see. Look for any flagged device and write its sign.

## Click-path rehearsal
On paper (or a spare machine), rehearse the fix: point at a flagged device, say 'right-click', 'Properties', 'Driver', 'Update Driver' at each step of GIF 17708 as it plays. Your partner checks the order.
