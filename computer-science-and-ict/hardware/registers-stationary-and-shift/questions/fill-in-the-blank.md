# Registers — Stationary and Shift — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A register stores a whole binary ____ at once.**
**Answer: word** — Words of 8, 16, 32 or 64 bits are stored one per register.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) The book's name for the parallel register, whose bits stay in place, is the ____ register.**
**Answer: stationary** — Stationary = the bits do not move once stored.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) In a shift register, on every clock tick each bit moves ____ place along the row.**
**Answer: one** — One place per tick — no more, no less.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) The combination serial-in, parallel-out is shortened to ____.**
**Answer: SIPO** — Serial in gathers the word; parallel out reads it whole.
