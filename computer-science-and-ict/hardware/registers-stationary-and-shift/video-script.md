# Video script — Registers — Stationary and Shift

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

One flip-flop holds one bit. A computer's words are many bits long.

Stand flip-flops in a row, share one clock — that is a register.

A stationary register swallows the whole word in a single tick. All in at once, all out at once.

Eight flip-flops, eight bits: one byte stored.

Rewire the row so each output feeds the next input — now every tick shifts every bit one place.

That is a shift register. Bits arriving one by one on a wire gather into a standing word.

Serial in, parallel out gathers. Parallel in, serial out sends. The shuffle converts between wire and word.
