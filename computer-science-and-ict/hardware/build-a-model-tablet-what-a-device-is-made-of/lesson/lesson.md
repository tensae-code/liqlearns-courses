# Build a Model Tablet — What a Device Is Made Of

## Do it first

A tablet looks like one smooth slab — nothing to see, nothing to open. Before reading on, guess what must be hiding inside the slab for it to show pictures, hear touches, and work all day without a plug.

## Sub lesson 1 — What a tablet is made of

Making a model tablet teaches the real one's anatomy: a **screen** that shows and feels touches, a **body** that holds everything, **buttons** for power and sound, a **camera** eye, a **speaker** mouth, a socket for charging — and inside, a **battery** for energy and the electronic parts that think. The scenario's build (Fabrique ta tablette) gives every part a place and a name, so the smooth slab becomes a made thing with insides a pupil can point to.

## Sub lesson 2 — A model teaches what a photo can't

A model is handled, turned over, pointed at and argued over: where does the camera go — and why there? What happens if we forget the battery? Building forces the questions a picture slides past, and the finished model stays on the shelf as the class's shared answer: this is what our tablet is, part by part, when anyone needs to say how it works or what to take care of.
