# Build a Model Tablet — What a Device Is Made Of — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name four outside parts of a tablet.**
**Answer: Screen, body, buttons, camera (also: speaker, charging socket)**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What two things hide inside?**
**Answer: A battery and the electronic parts that think**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does the screen do besides show?**
**Answer: It feels touches**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is the build called in French?**
**Answer: Fabrique ta tablette — make your tablet**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does the build give every part?**
**Answer: A place and a name**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name two questions building forces.**
**Answer: Where does the camera go — and why there? What if we forget the battery?**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does the finished model become?**
**Answer: The class's shared answer to what the tablet is, part by part**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What can a model do that a photo can't?**
**Answer: Be handled, turned over, pointed at and argued over**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What two later talks does the model serve?**
**Answer: How it works, and what to take care of**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is forgotten if the battery is left out?**
**Answer: The tablet's energy — it cannot work**
