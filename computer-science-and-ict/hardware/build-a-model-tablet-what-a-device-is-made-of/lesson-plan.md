## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **What a tablet is made of** — Making a model tablet teaches the real one's anatomy: a **screen** that shows and feels touches, a **body** that holds everything, **buttons** for power and sound, a **camera** eye, a **speaker** mouth, a socket for charging — and inside, a **battery** for energy and the electronic parts that think. The scenario's build (Fabrique ta tablette) gives every part a place and a name, so the smooth slab becomes a made thing with insides a pupil can point to.
2. **A model teaches what a photo can't** — A model is handled, turned over, pointed at and argued over: where does the camera go — and why there? What happens if we forget the battery? Building forces the questions a picture slides past, and the finished model stays on the shelf as the class's shared answer: this is what our tablet is, part by part, when anyone needs to say how it works or what to take care of.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — A tablet looks like one smooth slab — nothing to see, nothing to open. Before reading on, guess what must be hiding inside the slab for it to show pictures, hear touches, and work all day without a plug. (lesson/lesson.md).
2. **The explanation** — the lesson text in lesson/lesson.md, in its parts in order.
   - ADD IMAGE: a picture of what a tablet is made of as the lesson describes it — the student studies the picture and names what each part shows.
   - ADD GIF: a GIF of a model teaches what a photo can't in action — the student watches once, then says what changed and why.
   - ADD AUDIO: a voice reading the lesson's key names and definitions aloud, in the order the lesson gives them — the student listens once through, then reads the same lines aloud along with the voice. (Audio is a described slot in this build; no audio file is made.)
3. **Say it to your companion** — the companion block in lesson/lesson.md.
   - ADD VIDEO: the script in video-script.md, filmed — the lesson in the super-simple voice (made later, when videos are made).
4. **Questions** — from questions/: multiple-choice, true-false, matching, short-answer — served at the learner's level and teaching-mix (levels.md).
