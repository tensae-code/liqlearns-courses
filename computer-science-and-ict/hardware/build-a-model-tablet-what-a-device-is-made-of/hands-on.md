# Hands-on — Build a Model Tablet — What a Device Is Made Of

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Build the tablet** — From card, clay or recycled materials, build a model tablet with every part labelled: screen, buttons, camera, speaker, socket — and a cutaway or flap showing battery and thinking parts inside.

## Task two
**Part pointing test** — Swap models with another pair. They must find and name six parts on your model. Any part they cannot find is a part to rebuild clearer.
