# Lesson plan — Preventive maintenance — keeping dust out

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Hardware · **Lesson:** Preventive maintenance — keeping dust out
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 5 — Hardware Troubleshooting and Preventive Maintenance (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Preventive maintenance is the routine care that stops failures before they happen — and its most regular job is cleaning: dust blocks airflow, traps heat and invites breakdown.
**This lesson's own level:** It stands on the troubleshooting lessons: first find faults, now prevent them.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or in steps — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **What preventive maintenance is** — Routinely taking measures that reduce the risk of hardware failure and improve the chance of quick recovery: diagnosing boards and memory, replacing parts showing excess wear, and keeping components clean.
2. **Cleaning — the three dust methods** — A soft cotton or microfiber cloth for case and screens; chemical cleaners made for screens; and a vacuum cleaner — with compressed air and a small lint-free cloth — for dust inside the machine, as a monthly habit.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

3. Write, in one sentence, the chain from dust to breakdown.

- Reaches for a wet wipe first → cloth first, liquid second: the dry microfiber lift comes before any liquid wipe.
