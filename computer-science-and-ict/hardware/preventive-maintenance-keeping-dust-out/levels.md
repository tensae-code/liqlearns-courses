# Preventive maintenance — keeping dust out — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons"). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number, as before. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — What preventive maintenance is
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: the learner can state and use what this part teaches — Routinely taking measures that reduce the risk of hardware failure and improve the chance of quick recovery: diagnosing boards and memory, replacing parts showing excess wear, and keeping components clean.

## Sub lesson 2 — Cleaning — the three dust methods
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: the learner can state and use what this part teaches — A soft cotton or microfiber cloth for case and screens; chemical cleaners made for screens; and a vacuum cleaner — with compressed air and a small lint-free cloth — for dust inside the machine, as a monthly habit.
