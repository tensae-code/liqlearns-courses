# Preventive maintenance — keeping dust out — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Replacing parts showing excess wear is part of preventive maintenance.
**Answer: True** — one of its named kinds of work.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Any household spray is fine on a screen.
**Answer: False** — the book names chemical cleaners made for screens; alcohol-free sprays are its safe example.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Compressed air is used for dust inside the machine.
**Answer: True** — with the vacuum and a lint-free cloth.
