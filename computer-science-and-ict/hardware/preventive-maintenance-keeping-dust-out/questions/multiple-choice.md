# Preventive maintenance — keeping dust out — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Preventive maintenance is —
a) fixing machines after they break  b) routinely taking measures that reduce the risk of hardware failure  c) buying new machines yearly  d) a kind of antivirus
**Answer: b) routinely taking measures that reduce the risk of hardware failure** — routine measures, before failure.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Preventive maintenance also improves —
a) the screen size  b) the chance of quick recovery if a failure happens  c) the internet speed  d) the beep codes
**Answer: b) the chance of quick recovery if a failure happens** — quicker recovery is half its point.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Its named work includes —
a) diagnosing boards and memory, replacing worn parts, keeping components clean  b) only cleaning  c) only backups  d) writing programs
**Answer: a) diagnosing boards and memory, replacing worn parts, keeping components clean** — the three named kinds of work.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Dust harms a machine because it —
a) scratches the screen  b) blocks airflow, traps heat and invites breakdown  c) deletes files  d) slows the internet
**Answer: b) blocks airflow, traps heat and invites breakdown** — the dust chain: airflow, heat, breakdown.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The first cleaning method is —
a) a chemical spray  b) a vacuum  c) a soft cotton or microfiber cloth  d) compressed air
**Answer: c) a soft cotton or microfiber cloth** — cloth first — the book's order.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Microfiber is praised because it —
a) is cheapest  b) lifts fingerprints, smudges, oils and dust without damage  c) smells nice  d) works wet only
**Answer: b) lifts fingerprints, smudges, oils and dust without damage** — the gentle lift, without damage.

**7. (Sub lesson 2)** The cloth goes —
a) after the liquid wipe  b) first, before any liquid wipe  c) only on keyboards  d) never on screens
**Answer: b) first, before any liquid wipe** — dry first, liquid second.

**8. (Sub lesson 2)** Dust INSIDE the machine is cleaned with —
a) a wet cloth  b) a vacuum cleaner, with compressed air and a small lint-free cloth  c) screen spray  d) a brush of steel
**Answer: b) a vacuum cleaner, with compressed air and a small lint-free cloth** — vacuum + compressed air + lint-free cloth.

**9. (Sub lesson 2)** The cleaning habit is —
a) yearly  b) monthly — vents, ports and keyboards  c) only when it breaks  d) daily, all day
**Answer: b) monthly — vents, ports and keyboards** — monthly, at the three named places.
