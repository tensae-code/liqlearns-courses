# Hands-on — Preventive maintenance — keeping dust out

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## The monthly clean
With your teacher's leave and the machine OFF: wipe the case and screen with a microfiber cloth first. Then clean the keyboard and vents as the book directs. Tick each place — vents, ports, keyboard — as you finish it.

## Sort the tools
Cards for six jobs: smeared screen, dusty vent, fingerprints on a tablet, dust inside the case, oily case, clogged port. Match each job to its method from the lesson and say the order when two methods meet on one job.
