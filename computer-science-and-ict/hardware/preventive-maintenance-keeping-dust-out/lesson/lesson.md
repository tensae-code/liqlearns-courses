# Preventive maintenance — keeping dust out

## Do it first

Troubleshooting fixes what broke. This lesson is the opposite habit: stopping the break before it comes. Write two things you could do EVERY month to a school computer so it never needs the troubleshooter. Then read the book's methods.

## Sub lesson 1 — What preventive maintenance is

**Preventive maintenance** is **routinely taking measures that reduce the risk of hardware failure** — and **improve the chance of quick recovery** if a failure still happens.

Its named work: **diagnosing boards and memory**, **replacing parts showing excess wear**, and **keeping components clean of dust**. Dust is the everyday enemy: it **blocks airflow, traps heat and invites breakdown**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Cleaning — the three dust methods

The book's three methods, in its order:

1. **A soft cotton or microfiber cloth** — for the case and screens. Microfiber **lifts fingerprints, smudges, oils and dust without damage**, and it **goes first, before any liquid wipe**.
2. **Chemical cleaners made for screens** — including **alcohol-free sprays** safe from tablets to laptops.
3. **A vacuum cleaner — with compressed air and a small lint-free cloth** — for **dust inside the machine**.

Make it a **monthly habit**: clean the **vents, ports and keyboards** every month, before the dust builds its blanket.

> **Teacher evidence:** the guide keys dust in the same consequence terms — dust leads to **overheating and component failure** (teacher guide, printed pp. 95–98).

> **Teacher evidence — the Grade 12 definition (GROWN 2026-11-12, Wave F band F5):** the Grade 12 guide's review answers define preventive maintenance as **regular and systematic inspection, cleaning and replacement of worn parts, materials and systems**, which **prevents failure by keeping them in good working order** (Grade 12 Teacher Guide — evidence, printed pp. 111–112). The dust work in this lesson is that definition with its sleeves rolled up.

## Say it to your companion

Define preventive maintenance in your own words, name its three kinds of work, then give the three dust methods in order and the monthly habit.
