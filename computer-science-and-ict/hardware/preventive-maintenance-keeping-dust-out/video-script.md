# Video script — Preventive maintenance — keeping dust out

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. Short script; the placement names no full video need for this lesson.

---

Do not wait for the breakdown.
Prevent it.

Dust is the slow enemy.
It blocks the air. It traps the heat.
Heat invites failure.

Cloth first — soft cotton, microfiber.
It lifts prints and oils, gently.
Then the screen cleaner, made for screens.
Inside? Vacuum, compressed air, a lint-free cloth.

Every month: vents, ports, keyboards.
Small habit. Fewer funerals for machines.
