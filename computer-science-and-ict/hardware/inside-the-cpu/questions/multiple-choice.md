# Inside the CPU — ALU, control unit, registers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) The ALU performs —**
a) only arithmetic  b) arithmetic and logical operations  c) only storage
**Answer: b) arithmetic and logical operations**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Comparing letters and special characters is —**
a) a logical operation of the ALU  b) done by the monitor  c) a storage job
**Answer: a) a logical operation of the ALU**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) The control unit —**
a) does all the sums  b) controls and directs the whole system's operation  c) prints results
**Answer: b) controls and directs the whole system's operation**

**4. (Sub lesson 3) Registers are —**
a) the CPU's small, high-speed memory  b) a kind of keyboard  c) permanent storage
**Answer: a) the CPU's small, high-speed memory**

**5. (Sub lesson 3) Registers hold —**
a) everything forever  b) temporary results and control information  c) only pictures
**Answer: b) temporary results and control information**

**6. (Sub lesson 3) A pupil says the ALU stores temporary results. The correction is —**
a) true, it does  b) registers hold temporary results, not the ALU  c) the monitor stores them
**Answer: b) registers hold temporary results, not the ALU** — Guide review key, printed pp. 31–34.
