# Inside the CPU — ALU, control unit, registers — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Add, subtract, multiply and ____ are the ALU's arithmetic operations.**
**Answer: divide**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) The ____ unit controls and directs the whole system.**
**Answer: control**

**3. (Sub lesson 3) Temporary results are held in the ____.**
**Answer: registers**
