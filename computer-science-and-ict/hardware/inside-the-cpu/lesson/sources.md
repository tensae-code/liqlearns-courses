**Acceptance count: taught in 7 countries (Ethiopia, Fiji, Bhutan, Mongolia, Sri Lanka, Laos, Indonesia).**

No book error touches this lesson's content.

Fiji's own way (Year 11 Computer Education student book): The first locally written text of its kind (the book's Preface): written by a panel of Computer Studies officers and University of the South Pacific lecturers for the new Year 11 Computer Education syllabus trialled in 2014; each chapter uses "Discovery Learning" prompts, and the database chapter is a full Access 2007 practical building a Student database in a "Lab Chapter 3" folder.

Bhutan's own way (Class VI, Literacy with ICT student book): School computing runs on Google's G Suite for Education — email is Gmail on the school account, collaboration is Google Classroom, and the end-of-year food-chain project is agreed in Classroom, built in Word, password-protected, cited, filed in folders and reported to the teacher by email.

Mongolia's own way (Grade 12): the final book measures everything against Mongolia itself — the ITU index and Mongolia's slipping rank in it, 36,760 km of fibre to 299 soums, the Mongolsat-1 satellite, IoT chips on livestock, telemedicine at the State Central Hospital, the ХУР state data-exchange and ТҮЦ service machines, nano-products from horse bone and ger covers — and its chapter projects are real Mongolian systems: a school ICT-development survey, a study of the ebarimt VAT-receipt system, and a Small Basic "Bouncing Ball" game; programming examples draw the alkhan khee meander with a Turtle subprogram.

Sri Lanka's own way (Grade 7 Reading Book): the CPU is taught through a hotel-kitchen/chef analogy; programme development (decomposition, flowcharts, variables, bugs) precedes presentation software; HTML page-building is already in Grade 7.

Laos's own way (Grade 8 ICT student book): a full PC-technician year in full colour — learners take a computer apart and rebuild it (CPU, RAM, board, cards, drives), install Windows XP and drivers, fix install faults by BIOS beep codes, then finish with networks and the internet.

Indonesia's own way (Indonesia Grade 10 G10 Informatika SMK Sem1): The SMK Semester 1 book teaches the same Informatika elements with a logic-first opening (propositions and inference), applied office integration, hands-on network-device configuration, and an OS-installation practical.
