# Hands-on — Inside the CPU — ALU, control unit, registers

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Three workers
On three cards, write a job card for each component: its name, its job in your own words, and one example moment from a real sum (7 + 5, then comparing the result with 10). Act the sum through the three cards in order.

## Spot the mistake
A pupil says: "The ALU stored my half-finished answer." Write the corrected sentence, naming the component that really holds temporary results.
