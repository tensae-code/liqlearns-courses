# Hands-on — Using Digital Devices with a Computer

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Capture, transfer, keep
Capture one photograph with a digital camera, phone or webcam. Connect the device to the computer, transfer the photograph, open it to check it arrived whole, and save it in a folder with a clear file name.

## Webcam check
With a webcam, start the camera program on the computer and record a few seconds. Say what the webcam sent to the computer while it worked, and how that differs from the camera's capture-first way.
