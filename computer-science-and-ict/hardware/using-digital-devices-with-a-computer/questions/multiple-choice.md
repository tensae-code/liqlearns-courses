# Using Digital Devices with a Computer — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does a digital camera capture?**
a) Photographs in digital form  b) By a cable to the computer, or without wires  c) Capture on the device, transfer to the computer, check, process, then save as a named file  d) Open the transferred material and check it arrived whole
**Answer: a) Photographs in digital form**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does a webcam capture?**

**Answer: b) Moving pictures of what is in front of the computer, sent while it is used**
