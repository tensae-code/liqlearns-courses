# Using Digital Devices with a Computer — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does a digital camera capture?**
**Answer: Photographs in digital form**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does a webcam capture?**
**Answer: Moving pictures of what is in front of the computer, sent while it is used**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why is capturing only the first step?**
**Answer: Because the captured material must still be transferred, checked, processed and saved on the computer**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How are a device and a computer usually connected?**
**Answer: By a cable to the computer, or without wires**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What lets the computer speak to a connected device?**
**Answer: The driver and program that came for the device**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is the joint-work order in this lesson?**
**Answer: Capture on the device, transfer to the computer, check, process, then save as a named file**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How does a webcam's timing differ from a camera's?**
**Answer: A webcam sends pictures while you use it; a camera stores them first**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What should you do right after a transfer?**
**Answer: Open the transferred material and check it arrived whole**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does processing a photograph mean here?**
**Answer: Working on it on the computer, for example cropping it**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why save with a clear file name?**
**Answer: So the file can be found again later**
