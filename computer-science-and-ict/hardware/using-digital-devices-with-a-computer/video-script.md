# Video script — Using Digital Devices with a Computer

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A **digital device** captures information in digital form: a **digital camera** captures photographs, a **mobile phone** captures photographs, sound and video and carries its own small computer, and a **webcam** captures moving pictures of whatever is in front of the computer.
None of these works alone for long — the picture or recording is made on the device, but it is kept, sorted, edited and shared from the computer.
To use a device with the computer, the two are **connected** — most often by a cable to the computer, or without wires — and the computer must be able to speak to the device, through the driver and program that came for it.
Then the joint work follows one order: capture on the device, transfer to the computer, check what arrived, process it (crop the photograph, trim the recording), and save the result as a file with a name you will find again.
