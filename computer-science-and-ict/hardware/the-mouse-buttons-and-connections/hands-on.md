# Hands-on — The mouse — buttons and connections

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Button drill
On a real computer, practise: left-click to select a word of text; scroll a long page with the middle button or wheel; right-click a file and read its properties menu, then close it without choosing anything.

## Pad and glide
Move a mouse with and without a mouse pad. Notice the glide. The pad is the mouse's road.
