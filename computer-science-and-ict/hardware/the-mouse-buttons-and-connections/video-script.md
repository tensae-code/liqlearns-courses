**The mouse.**

First, brainstorm: what do you already know about this? Say it out loud.

Now the one thing: the mouse is a hand-operated device that controls the cursor: left button to click and select, right button for menus such as a file's properties, a middle button for scrolling — connected by cable or wirelessly.

**Three buttons.** The mouse is a hand-operated device controlling the cursor or pointer, named from its shape.
**Wired and wireless.** Mice connect in two ways: by cable, or wirelessly — a cordless mouse with no cable at all.
**Right-click in motion.** Watch the GIF: the pointer moves onto a file, the right button clicks, and the file's properties menu opens.

Now you: Draw a mouse from above and label its three buttons and what each does.
