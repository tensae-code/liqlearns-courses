# The mouse — buttons and connections

## Do it first

Move an imaginary mouse on the desk. Your index finger presses — click. Your middle finger presses — something else happens. Before reading on, guess what the right-hand press is for, and what the little wheel or middle part does.

## Three buttons

The mouse is a hand-operated device controlling the cursor or pointer, named from its shape. It commonly has three buttons. The left button is for clicking — for example, to select text. The right button is for right-clicking — for example, to see a file's properties. The middle button is used for scrolling, and it is sometimes missing.

## Wired and wireless

Mice connect in two ways: by cable, or wirelessly — a cordless mouse with no cable at all. Either way, the mouse moves on a mouse pad, and the pointer on screen follows the hand.
