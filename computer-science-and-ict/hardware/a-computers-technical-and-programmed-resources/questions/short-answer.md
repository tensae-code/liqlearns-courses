# A Computer's Technical and Programmed Resources — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What are technical resources?**
**Answer: The hardware estate — processor, memory and devices, with their measures**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How is the processor measured?**
**Answer: By clock frequency, word length and instruction set**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does a machine usually lack when it cannot do a job?**
**Answer: A resource — memory too small, a processor too slow**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Are technical resources infinite?**
**Answer: No — they are finite and priced**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does naming a machine honestly mean?**
**Answer: Listing its resources with their numbers, not its advertisements**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What are programmed resources?**
**Answer: The software estate — system software and application software**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is system software's job?**
**Answer: Making the hardware usable — the operating system and utilities**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How do the estates relate?**
**Answer: They multiply each other — hardware without software is inert; software without hardware is a text**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How is a computer's capability for a job judged?**
**Answer: By both estates together**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What two questions judge any machine?**
**Answer: What does it have (technical), and what does it run (programmed)?**
