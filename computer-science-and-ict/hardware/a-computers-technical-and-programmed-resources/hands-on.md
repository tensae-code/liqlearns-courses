# Hands-on — A Computer's Technical and Programmed Resources

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Estate inventory** — Write the full two-estate inventory of your school or home computer: technical resources with numbers, programmed resources by family.

## Task two
**Buyer verdict** — Two machines are offered for a video-editing job — one fast with little memory and no editor, one slower with much memory and the editor installed. Judge by both estates and defend the verdict.
