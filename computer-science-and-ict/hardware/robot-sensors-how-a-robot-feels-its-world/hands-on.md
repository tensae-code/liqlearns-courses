# Hands-on — Robot Sensors — How a Robot Feels Its World

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Sensor census** — Meet a Thymio (or its picture/diagram). Find and name every sensor, say what each one feels, and point to where it sits on the body.

## Task two
**Materials test** — Test one robot sensor (or a phone's proximity sensor) against six materials. Table the results: detected / not detected / unreliable. Conclude in one sentence.
