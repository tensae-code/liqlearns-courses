# Robot Sensors — How a Robot Feels Its World — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a capteur?**
**Answer: A sensor — a part that turns a piece of the world into a signal**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How do proximity sensors work?**
**Answer: They bounce light off what is near and read the return**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What do ground sensors read?**
**Answer: The surface below — dark line from light floor**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does each sensor hand the program?**
**Answer: A number or a yes/no it can use**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is a robot without sensors?**
**Answer: Blind to everything it cannot sense**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What question does the Grade 6 inquiry ask?**
**Answer: Whether Thymio's sensors are effective with all materials**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Name two surfaces that can fool a proximity sensor.**
**Answer: A surface that swallows light, or one that reflects it away (answers vary: dark, transparent)**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What must pupils record?**
**Answer: Where detection succeeds and where it fails**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why know a sensor's failures?**
**Answer: The program must be written for the sensor the robot actually has**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What kind of question is the inquiry's?**
**Answer: A scientist's question — tested, not assumed**
