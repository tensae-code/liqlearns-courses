# Arduino Supporting Components — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What are supporting components?**
**Answer: The kit that surrounds the board — the parts its projects are built from**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is the breadboard?**
**Answer: The solderless field where circuits are assembled and changed**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What do jumper cables do?**
**Answer: Join board, breadboard and components as removable roads**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why inventory the kit before projects?**
**Answer: A builder who cannot name the parts cannot read a circuit diagram — diagrams are written in these parts**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which part limits current to protect the LED?**
**Answer: The resistor**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name three output devices in the kit.**
**Answer: LED, piezo buzzer, servo/stepper motors, LCD (any three)**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Name the input devices in the kit.**
**Answer: The push button and the sensor modules**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Which sensor modules does the book name?**
**Answer: Light, sound, temperature and soil-moisture sensors**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What pattern is every project built from?**
**Answer: Sense something, decide, act**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Where does Indonesia teach this?**
**Answer: Grade 12, Bab 2 B.3 — supporting components of the single-board controller**
