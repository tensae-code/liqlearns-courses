# Hands-on — Arduino Supporting Components

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Name the kit** — Lay out (or draw) the supporting kit. Label each part, its direction (input or output), and one job it could do in a plant-watering or door-alarm project.

## Task two
**Read a diagram** — Take a simple Arduino circuit diagram. List every component it uses from this lesson's kit, and write the sense–decide–act story the circuit tells.
