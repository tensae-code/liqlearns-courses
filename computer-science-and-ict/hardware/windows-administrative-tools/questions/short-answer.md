# Windows Administrative Tools — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the administrative tools gathered.**
**Answer: Computer Management, Disk Management, Services, Task Scheduler, Firewall with Advanced Security, Performance Monitor**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is Computer Management?**
**Answer: The umbrella console — users, disks, services, events in one frame**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does Task Scheduler automate?**
**Answer: Jobs at times and triggers — the maintenance calendar**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does Disk Management shape?**
**Answer: Partitions and drives**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What are Services?**
**Answer: The background workers — started, stopped and set**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) By what do administrative tools differ?**
**Answer: Reach — they change the system for every user, persistently**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Name the procedure's four parts.**
**Answer: Administer only when administering, record changes, test on one machine first, keep a way back**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is the change log called?**
**Answer: The next troubleshooter's diary**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What two ways back are named?**
**Answer: Restore points and backups**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the lesson's last word on the levers?**
**Answer: They are lent, not owned**
