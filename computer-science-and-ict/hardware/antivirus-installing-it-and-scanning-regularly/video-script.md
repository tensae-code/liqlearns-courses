# Video script — Antivirus — installing it and scanning regularly

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. Short script; the placement names no full video need for this lesson.

---

A virus is a program that copies itself —
into other programs, hiding its code inside.

It rides in two ways:
the internet, and a friend's flash disk.
And it stays quiet — until the damage shows.

The guard is antivirus.
Search. Open the maker's page.
Install — or save the setup and run it.
AVAST. AVG. AVIRA. Bitdefender. Free guards exist.

Then: scan regularly.
Some malware sleeps, waiting for its trigger.
