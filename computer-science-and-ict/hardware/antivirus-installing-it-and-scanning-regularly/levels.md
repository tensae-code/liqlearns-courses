## Sub lesson 1 — What a virus is — and how infection arrives
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: the learner can state and use what this part teaches — A virus is a program that, when run, copies itself by modifying other programs and inserting its own code. Infection arrives over the internet or on someone else's flash disk, and usually shows itself only once it causes a problem.

## Sub lesson 2 — Antivirus — installing it and scanning regularly (doing)
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: the learner can state and use what this part teaches — Antivirus (anti-malware) software prevents, detects and removes malware, free and commercial; the free examples are AVAST, AVG, AVIRA and Bitdefender. Install by the ordinary route, then scan regularly.
