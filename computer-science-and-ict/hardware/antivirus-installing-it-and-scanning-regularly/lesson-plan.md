# Lesson plan — Antivirus — installing it and scanning regularly

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Hardware · **Lesson:** Antivirus — installing it and scanning regularly
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 5 — Hardware Troubleshooting and Preventive Maintenance (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** A virus copies itself into other programs; antivirus software prevents, detects and removes such malware — install it by the ordinary route and scan regularly, because some malware hides and waits.
**This lesson's own level:** It stands on Preventive maintenance — keeping dust out: cleaning is the outside care, antivirus is the inside care.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **What a virus is — and how infection arrives** — A virus is a program that, when run, copies itself by modifying other programs and inserting its own code. Infection arrives over the internet or on someone else's flash disk, and usually shows itself only once it causes a problem.
2. **Antivirus — installing it and scanning regularly (doing)** — Antivirus (anti-malware) software prevents, detects and removes malware, free and commercial; the free examples are AVAST, AVG, AVIRA and Bitdefender. Install by the ordinary route, then scan regularly.
