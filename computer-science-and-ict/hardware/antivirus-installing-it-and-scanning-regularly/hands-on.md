# Hands-on — Antivirus — installing it and scanning regularly

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## The two roads in
List everything that entered a school computer this week that you know of: downloads, flash disks, files from friends. Mark each with its road — internet or flash disk — and say whether an antivirus stood guard.

## Install rehearsal (paper)
Lay out the install route as cards: search, maker's result, install directly OR save setup and run. A partner scrambles them; you restore the book's order and say the rule that follows installation.
