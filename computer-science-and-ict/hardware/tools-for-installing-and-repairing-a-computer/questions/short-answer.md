# Tools for Installing and Repairing a Computer — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What opens the practical chapter?**
**Answer: The tools for installing and repairing**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name the basic hand tools.**
**Answer: Screwdrivers (flat and cross-head), pliers and tweezers**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why keep a container for screws?**
**Answer: So none are lost or left rolling inside the case**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does forcing the wrong screwdriver head do?**
**Answer: It chews the screw and ruins screw and tool**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is the first safety rule?**
**Answer: Power off and unplugged — no work on a live machine**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Why discharge static before handling parts?**
**Answer: A body's invisible spark can kill a chip**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How is static discharged?**
**Answer: Touch the case's metal, or wear an anti-static strap**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How are boards and cards held?**
**Answer: By their edges, never by contacts or chips**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which chapter teaches this?**
**Answer: Laos Grade 8 ICT, Chapter 2 §2.1**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why are tools and safety taught together?**
**Answer: On the bench they are one habit**
