# Tools for Installing and Repairing a Computer — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: The tools for installing and repairing**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: So none are lost or left rolling inside the case** — as the answer to: Name the basic hand tools.
**Answer: False — Screwdrivers (flat and cross-head), pliers and tweezers**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: So none are lost or left rolling inside the case**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Power off and unplugged — no work on a live machine** — as the answer to: What does forcing the wrong screwdriver head do?
**Answer: False — It chews the screw and ruins screw and tool**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Power off and unplugged — no work on a live machine**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Touch the case's metal, or wear an anti-static strap** — as the answer to: Why discharge static before handling parts?
**Answer: False — A body's invisible spark can kill a chip**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Touch the case's metal, or wear an anti-static strap**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Laos Grade 8 ICT, Chapter 2 §2.1** — as the answer to: How are boards and cards held?
**Answer: False — By their edges, never by contacts or chips**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Laos Grade 8 ICT, Chapter 2 §2.1**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: The tools for installing and repairing** — as the answer to: Why are tools and safety taught together?
**Answer: False — On the bench they are one habit**
