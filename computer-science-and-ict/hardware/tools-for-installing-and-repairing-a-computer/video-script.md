# Video script — Tools for Installing and Repairing a Computer

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The installer's basic tools are **screwdrivers** in the sizes and heads the case and cards use (flat and cross-head), **pliers and tweezers** for the small and the dropped, a **container for screws** so none are lost or left rolling inside the case, and cleaning tools for dust. Each tool has its one job: a screwdriver forced into the wrong head chews the screw and ruins both. Laos lists the toolkit as its own section because choosing the tool is the first act of every later install in the chapter.
Two rules stand before the toolkit is opened. **Power off and unplugged**: no installing or removing is done on a live machine. And **static electricity must be discharged** — the invisible spark from a worker's body can kill a chip it never visibly touches, so the installer touches the case's metal (or wears an anti-static strap) before handling boards and cards, and holds parts **by their edges**, never by their contacts or chips. The book teaches the rules with the tools because on the bench they are one habit.
