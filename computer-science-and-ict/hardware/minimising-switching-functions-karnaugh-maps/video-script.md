# Video script — Minimising Switching Functions — Karnaugh Maps

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The first way you write a function is almost never the smallest way.

Smallest matters: every gate costs money, space and power.

Algebra's golden pattern: two terms that differ in one variable merge into one.

A·B plus A·not-B is just A. The B disappears.

The Karnaugh map makes the pattern visible. It is the truth table as a grid.

Labels go 00, 01, 11, 10 — so neighbours differ in exactly one variable.

Group the 1s in rectangles of 1, 2, 4 or 8. As large as possible. The edges wrap around.

One rectangle, one term. Variables that change inside it are dropped.
