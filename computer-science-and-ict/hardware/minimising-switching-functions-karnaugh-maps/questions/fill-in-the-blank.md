# Minimising Switching Functions — Karnaugh Maps — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A Boolean expression that tells a circuit when to switch its output to 1 is called a ____ function.**
**Answer: switching** — Switching functions are what the gates of the next lesson are wired from.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) A·X + A·X̄ simplifies to ____.**
**Answer: A** — Factor out A, then X + X̄ = 1, then A·1 = A.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) The map's labelling order 00, 01, 11, 10 is called ____-code order.**
**Answer: Gray** — In Gray code each step changes exactly one bit.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) A group of 8 ones on a four-variable map reads off as a term with ____ variable(s).**
**Answer: one** — A group of 8 drops three variables, leaving one.
