# Minimising Switching Functions — Karnaugh Maps — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A minimised expression must have exactly the same truth table as the original.**
**Answer: True** — Minimising changes the form, never the behaviour — the truth-table check is the proof.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Any two terms can be merged algebraically if they share at least one letter.**
**Answer: False** — They must be identical except for exactly one variable appearing plain in one and barred in the other.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) On a Karnaugh map, the leftmost and rightmost columns are neighbours.**
**Answer: True** — The map wraps around: edge cells differ in exactly one variable, just like cells side by side.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) A group of 6 ones is a good group because it covers more ones at once.**
**Answer: False** — Six is not a power of two, so no single term describes it; groups must be 1, 2, 4 or 8.
