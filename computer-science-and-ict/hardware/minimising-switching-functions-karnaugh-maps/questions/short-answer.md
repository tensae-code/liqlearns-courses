# Minimising Switching Functions — Karnaugh Maps — short answer

Answer in your own words, in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Why does minimising matter outside the classroom — name two concrete savings.**
**Answer: Every gate removed is a component that is never bought, never wired, takes no space and draws no power — in every copy of the circuit produced. Fewer gates also mean fewer connections that can fail.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Why must the Karnaugh map use Gray-code order instead of ordinary counting order?**
**Answer: Because merging works only between cells that differ in exactly one variable. In ordinary order 01 and 10 sit side by side but differ in two variables, so 'neighbours' would lie; Gray order guarantees every physical neighbour differs in exactly one variable.**
