# Hands-on — Frequently Used Combinational Circuits

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Choose the circuit** — For five jobs (light one of 8 lamps by code; name a pressed key; send one of 4 sensors to one wire; route one signal to 4 rooms; test two marks for equality) name the circuit and its inputs/outputs.

## Task two
**Decoder table** — Write the full truth table of a 2-to-4 decoder and mark the one-hot pattern of its outputs.
