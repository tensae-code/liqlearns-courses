# Logic Gates — NOT, AND, OR, NAND, NOR, XOR — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) The NOT gate is also called the ____.**
**Answer: inverter** — It inverts its single input.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) In the expression Y = A · B, the gate is the ____ gate.**
**Answer: AND** — The · sign is logical multiplication — AND.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) NAND is AND followed by ____.**
**Answer: NOT (an inverter)** — The bubble on the AND symbol is the inverter on its output.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) XOR's output is 1 when its inputs are ____.**
**Answer: different** — 0⊕1 = 1 and 1⊕0 = 1; same inputs give 0.
