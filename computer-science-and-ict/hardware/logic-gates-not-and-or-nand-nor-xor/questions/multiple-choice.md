# Logic Gates — NOT, AND, OR, NAND, NOR, XOR — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) The small circle (bubble) on a gate symbol means —**
a) The gate is broken  b) Negation at that point  c) The gate has two outputs  d) The signal is analogue
**Answer: b) Negation at that point** — The bubble is the mark of NOT, on the inverter and on the outputs of NAND, NOR and XNOR.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) An AND gate's output is 1 —**
a) When at least one input is 1  b) Only when all inputs are 1  c) When the inputs differ  d) Always
**Answer: b) Only when all inputs are 1** — AND is 'all or nothing': one 0 input forces the output to 0.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) A NAND gate's output is 0 —**
a) When all inputs are 0  b) When the inputs differ  c) Only when all inputs are 1  d) Never
**Answer: c) Only when all inputs are 1** — NAND is AND flipped: AND's only 1-row (all inputs 1) becomes NAND's only 0-row.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) A NOR gate's output is 1 —**
a) Only when all inputs are 0  b) When at least one input is 1  c) When all inputs are 1  d) When inputs differ
**Answer: a) Only when all inputs are 0** — NOR is OR flipped: OR's only 0-row (all inputs 0) becomes NOR's only 1-row.

**5. (Sub lesson 2) Why are NAND and NOR called universal gates?**
a) They are the fastest gates  b) Each one alone can build NOT, AND and OR — and so any switching function  c) They use no power  d) They have the most inputs
**Answer: b) Each one alone can build NOT, AND and OR — and so any switching function** — From NOT, AND and OR every function can be built; NAND alone (and NOR alone) can make all three, so it can make everything.

**6. (Sub lesson 2) The complex gate AND-OR-INVERT with inputs A, B, C, D computes —**
a) A·B + C·D  b) The bar over (A·B + C·D)  c) A + B · C + D  d) (A+B)·(C+D)
**Answer: b) The bar over (A·B + C·D)** — AND the pairs, OR the results, then invert — the final bubble puts a bar over the whole expression.
