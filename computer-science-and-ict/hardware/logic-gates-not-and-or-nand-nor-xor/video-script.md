# Video script — Logic Gates — NOT, AND, OR, NAND, NOR, XOR

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A gate is a Boolean operation built as a real circuit. Signals in, answer out.

The inverter flips: 0 in, 1 out. Its mark is the little circle — the bubble.

AND answers 1 only when all inputs are 1. OR answers 1 when at least one is.

Put a bubble on AND and you get NAND: 0 only when all inputs are 1.

Put a bubble on OR and you get NOR: 1 only when all inputs are 0.

XOR asks 'different?' XNOR asks 'same?'

NAND can build everything. So can NOR. That is why real chips are made of them.

A complex gate packs a whole expression — ANDs into an OR into an inverter — in one piece.
