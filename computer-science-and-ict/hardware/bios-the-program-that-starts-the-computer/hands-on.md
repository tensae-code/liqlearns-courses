# Hands-on — BIOS — the program that starts the computer

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Bridge on paper
Draw the bridge: operating system at the top, BIOS in the middle, the five devices at the bottom. Draw the data-flow arrows both ways through BIOS — nothing reaches the system except through the bridge.

## Error, not settings
Your teacher shows (or describes) a BIOS error message. Write what the message says and what you would do — and write, in red, what you would NOT do: change the settings yourself.
