# Video script — BIOS — the program that starts the computer

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. Short script; the placement names no full video need for this lesson.

---

Before Windows. Before everything.
One small program wakes the machine.

BIOS. Basic input/output system.
It starts the system when the power comes on.
And it carries the data —
between the system and the disk,
the screen card, the keyboard,
the mouse, the printer.

The machine boots, then stops after its self-check?
Likely a BIOS setting.
Do not change settings yourself.
That job belongs to a technician.
