# Video script — Network troubleshooting — detect, then fix

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

One computer is a island. Join them — a network.

They share printers. They swap files. They talk.
By cable, by waves, even by satellite.

When the joining breaks, we troubleshoot:
find the cause first — then fix.
Keep the network running, day to day.
