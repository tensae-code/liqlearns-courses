# Network troubleshooting — detect, then fix — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A network is —
a) one fast computer  b) two or more computers connected  c) a kind of cable  d) a printer with wifi
**Answer: b) two or more computers connected**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Networks let computers —
a) share resources, exchange files, communicate electronically  b) run without electricity  c) fix themselves  d) print without printers
**Answer: a) share resources, exchange files, communicate electronically**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Which is a network connection medium?
a) radio waves  b) chalk  c) paper  d) glass
**Answer: a) radio waves**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Network troubleshooting begins by —
a) replacing cables  b) detecting the cause  c) restarting everything  d) buying tools
**Answer: b) detecting the cause**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A network problem is —
a) a slow typist  b) anything that disrupts the connection between your computer and the content you are trying to access  c) a full disk  d) a frozen application
**Answer: b) anything that disrupts the connection between your computer and the content you are trying to access**
