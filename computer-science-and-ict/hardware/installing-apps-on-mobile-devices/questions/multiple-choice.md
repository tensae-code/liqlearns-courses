# Installing apps on mobile devices — use the proper store — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Apps for a phone should come from —
a) any link a friend sends  b) the operating system's own store  c) unknown internet sources  d) email attachments
**Answer: b) the operating system's own store**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these is a named app store?
a) Google Play  b) Ethiopia Play  c) Phone Market  d) App River
**Answer: a) Google Play**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The stores hold —
a) paid apps only  b) free apps only  c) both free and paid apps  d) no apps, only games
**Answer: c) both free and paid apps**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Downloading from unknown sources can —
a) speed the phone up  b) damage the phone and data and expose personal information  c) double the storage  d) update the system
**Answer: b) damage the phone and data and expose personal information**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The phone's security settings can —
a) scan for harmful apps, alert you, and delete the app if necessary  b) install any app silently  c) remove the store  d) charge the battery
**Answer: a) scan for harmful apps, alert you, and delete the app if necessary**
