# Installing apps on mobile devices — use the proper store — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the five stores the lesson lists.
**Answer: Apple App Store, Google Play, Amazon Appstore, Samsung Galaxy Apps, Huawei App Store.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why is the store safer than an unknown site?
**Answer: The store is the device's own checked source; unknown sources can carry apps that damage the phone and data and expose personal information, while the phone's security settings scan the store route for harmful apps.**
