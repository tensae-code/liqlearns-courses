# Installing apps on mobile devices — use the proper store

## Do it first

A friend sends you a link: 'Download this app from this site — it is free!' Your phone has a store of its own. Write which door you would use, and why — then work the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — The proper store for each device

Phones, watches, personal digital assistants and smart TVs get apps from **their operating system's store**. Five stores are named: **Apple App Store, Google Play, Amazon Appstore, Samsung Galaxy Apps and Huawei App Store**. The stores hold **both free and paid apps**. The important skill is **finding the right source first** — the store that belongs to your device.

## Sub lesson 2 — Why unknown sources damage phones

Downloading from **unknown internet sources** can **damage the phone and its data** and **expose personal information to damage or hacking**. The phone itself helps: its **security settings scan for potentially harmful apps**, **alert you when one is found**, and **delete it if necessary**. Keep that protection on.
