# Hands-on — Installing apps on mobile devices — use the proper store

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Store map
Draw five store signs and label them. Under each, write one device that uses it. Hang the map where the class can check it.

Write the message from Do it first as a note. Under it write your reply: the store you will use instead, and the two harms you are avoiding.
