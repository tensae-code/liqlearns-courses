# Video script — Installing apps on mobile devices — use the proper store

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Your phone has its own shop for apps.

Apple has the App Store. Android has Google Play.
There are more — Amazon, Samsung, Huawei.

Use your phone's own shop. Free apps live there too.
A strange site can damage your phone,
and strangers can reach your private things.

Your phone can scan for harmful apps.
Keep that guard on.
