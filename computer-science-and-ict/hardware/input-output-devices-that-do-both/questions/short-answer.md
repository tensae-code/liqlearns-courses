# Input-Output Devices — Devices That Do Both — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What makes a device input-output?**
**Answer: It carries data both ways — in and out**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) The touchscreen's two directions are what?**
**Answer: Shows the picture (output) and feels the finger (input)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name the headset's two devices in one.**
**Answer: Speaker out, microphone in**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Name a storage device that does both.**
**Answer: The hard disk (or USB stick — reads and writes)**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which unit teaches it?**
**Answer: Greece Grade 7 Pliroforiki A, Unit 2**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What do input and output name?**
**Answer: Directions of data travel — not species of device**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How is a device sorted?**
**Answer: By which way data moves for the job at hand**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Which devices answer 'both'?**
**Answer: Touchscreen, headset, modem, read-write storage (any from the lesson)**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the grown-up rule?**
**Answer: A sort that cannot admit its exceptions starts lying about everything else**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Both at once or how else?**
**Answer: At once, or in turns**
