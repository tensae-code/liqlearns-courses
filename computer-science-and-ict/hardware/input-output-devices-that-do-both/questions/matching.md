# Input-Output Devices — Devices That Do Both — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What makes a device input-output?** → **It carries data both ways — in and out**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) The touchscreen's two directions are what?** → **Shows the picture (output) and feels the finger (input)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name the headset's two devices in one.** → **Speaker out, microphone in**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Name a storage device that does both.** → **The hard disk (or USB stick — reads and writes)**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Which unit teaches it?** → **Greece Grade 7 Pliroforiki A, Unit 2**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What do input and output name?** → **Directions of data travel — not species of device**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How is a device sorted?** → **By which way data moves for the job at hand**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Which devices answer 'both'?** → **Touchscreen, headset, modem, read-write storage (any from the lesson)**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the grown-up rule?** → **A sort that cannot admit its exceptions starts lying about everything else**
