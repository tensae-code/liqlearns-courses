# Video script — Flip-Flops — RS, JK, D and T

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A gate forgets. Change the input, and the past is gone.

Wire two gates in a loop, each feeding the other — and the loop remembers. One bit.

That circuit is a flip-flop. Its output is Q, and Q-bar is always the opposite.

RS: set makes Q 1. Reset makes Q 0. Neither: it holds. Both at once: forbidden.

JK is RS repaired. Both inputs active now means: toggle — flip to the opposite.

D copies. On each clock tick, Q becomes whatever D is.

T toggles. While T is 1, every tick flips Q — the output runs at half the clock's speed.
