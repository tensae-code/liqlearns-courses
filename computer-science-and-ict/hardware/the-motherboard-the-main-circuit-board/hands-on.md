# Hands-on — The Motherboard — the Main Circuit Board

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Board map', 'On a labelled diagram or an opened (powered-off) system unit, point out the socket, two slots and three connectors.')

## Task two
('Will it fit?', 'Given a part and a board description, decide fit or no-fit from socket, slot and connector, and say why.')
