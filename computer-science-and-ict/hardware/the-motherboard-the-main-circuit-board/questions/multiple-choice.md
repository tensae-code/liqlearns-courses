# The Motherboard — the Main Circuit Board — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is the motherboard?**
a) The computer's main circuit board — the large board every other component connects to  b) The system board, or main board  c) Inside the system unit, filling the floor of the box  d) One computer — it joins them into a single working system
**Answer: a) The computer's main circuit board — the large board every other component connects to**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What are the motherboard's other names?**
a) Inside the system unit, filling the floor of the box  b) The system board, or main board  c) One computer — it joins them into a single working system  d) The parts are a box of unrelated electronics
**Answer: b) The system board, or main board**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Where is the motherboard found?**
a) One computer — it joins them into a single working system  b) The parts are a box of unrelated electronics  c) Inside the system unit, filling the floor of the box  d) The CPU socket
**Answer: c) Inside the system unit, filling the floor of the box**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does the motherboard make of the separate parts?**
a) The parts are a box of unrelated electronics  b) The CPU socket  c) Memory modules and expansion cards  d) One computer — it joins them into a single working system
**Answer: d) One computer — it joins them into a single working system**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What happens without it?**
a) The parts are a box of unrelated electronics  b) The CPU socket  c) Memory modules and expansion cards  d) Data, instructions and power between everything plugged in
**Answer: a) The parts are a box of unrelated electronics**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What holds the CPU on the board?**
a) Memory modules and expansion cards  b) The CPU socket  c) Data, instructions and power between everything plugged in  d) Its socket, slots and connectors decide what fits this machine
**Answer: b) The CPU socket**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What do slots hold?**
a) Data, instructions and power between everything plugged in  b) Its socket, slots and connectors decide what fits this machine  c) Memory modules and expansion cards  d) The motherboard — its socket, slots and connectors
**Answer: c) Memory modules and expansion cards**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What do the board's buses carry?**
a) Its socket, slots and connectors decide what fits this machine  b) The motherboard — its socket, slots and connectors  c) The computer's main circuit board — the large board every other component connects to  d) Data, instructions and power between everything plugged in
**Answer: d) Data, instructions and power between everything plugged in**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why does a technician ask about the board before fitting a part?**
a) Its socket, slots and connectors decide what fits this machine  b) The motherboard — its socket, slots and connectors  c) The computer's main circuit board — the large board every other component connects to  d) The system board, or main board
**Answer: a) Its socket, slots and connectors decide what fits this machine**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What decides what a computer can become?**
a) The computer's main circuit board — the large board every other component connects to  b) The motherboard — its socket, slots and connectors  c) The system board, or main board  d) Inside the system unit, filling the floor of the box
