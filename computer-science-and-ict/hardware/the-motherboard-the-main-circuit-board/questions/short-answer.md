# The Motherboard — the Main Circuit Board — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is the motherboard?**
**Answer: The computer's main circuit board — the large board every other component connects to**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What are the motherboard's other names?**
**Answer: The system board, or main board**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Where is the motherboard found?**
**Answer: Inside the system unit, filling the floor of the box**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does the motherboard make of the separate parts?**
**Answer: One computer — it joins them into a single working system**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What happens without it?**
**Answer: The parts are a box of unrelated electronics**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What holds the CPU on the board?**
**Answer: The CPU socket**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What do slots hold?**
**Answer: Memory modules and expansion cards**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What do the board's buses carry?**
**Answer: Data, instructions and power between everything plugged in**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why does a technician ask about the board before fitting a part?**
**Answer: Its socket, slots and connectors decide what fits this machine**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What decides what a computer can become?**
**Answer: The motherboard — its socket, slots and connectors**
