# Troubleshooting — start with the simple checks — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Troubleshooting means —
a) guessing until something works  b) a systematic approach to finding the cause of a fault, from general to specific  c) replacing every part  d) reinstalling Windows
**Answer: b) a systematic approach to finding the cause of a fault, from general to specific** — systematic, general to specific — that is the book's definition.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Software troubleshooting works on —
a) broken cables  b) errors and bugs in software  c) dust in the case  d) dead batteries
**Answer: b) errors and bugs in software** — the same method, aimed at software.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The FIRST simple check is —
a) open the case  b) is the computer plugged into a working outlet?  c) replace the monitor  d) search online
**Answer: b) is the computer plugged into a working outlet?** — power first — the widest, easiest check.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** For a blank screen, you check —
a) the printer  b) the monitor's power and its connection to the system unit  c) the keyboard battery  d) the speakers
**Answer: b) the monitor's power and its connection to the system unit** — the monitor's own power and cable.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Which list is all in the simple checks?
a) keyboard, mouse, monitor, speakers plugged in  b) RAM, CPU, fan, disk  c) antivirus, backup, defrag  d) BIOS, CMOS, POST
**Answer: a) keyboard, mouse, monitor, speakers plugged in** — the four plugged-in devices named in the book.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** A known-good mouse also fails in one port. The fault is —
a) the mouse  b) the port  c) the monitor  d) the power supply
**Answer: b) the port** — a good device failing in one port points at the port.

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** Before removing or replacing any part, you must —
a) search online  b) switch the computer off  c) open Event Viewer  d) back up the screen
**Answer: b) switch the computer off** — the safety rule carried with the checks.
