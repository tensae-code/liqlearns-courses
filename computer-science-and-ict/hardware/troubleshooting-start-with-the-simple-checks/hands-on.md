# Hands-on — Troubleshooting — start with the simple checks

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## The dead-computer drill
With the computer off and unplugged by your teacher, a partner secretly makes ONE simple fault: a plug out, a switch off, a cable loose. Run the four checks in order and find it. Swap roles and repeat.

## Port or device?
Take a keyboard or mouse that 'does not work' (your teacher prepares it). Test it in a second port, then test a known-good device in the first port. Write your verdict: port fault or device fault, and your evidence.
