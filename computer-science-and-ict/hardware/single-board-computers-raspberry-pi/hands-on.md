# Hands-on — Single-Board Computers — Raspberry Pi

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Sort the family** — Take five devices (PC, smartphone, Raspberry Pi, Pi Pico, Arduino). Place each on the line from 'sealed computer' to 'bare controller', and defend each place with its pins and its operating system.

## Task two
**GPIO plan** — Design a Pi project that needs two inputs and two outputs (a plant guard, a door sentinel). List the GPIO jobs and what each pin will sense or drive.
