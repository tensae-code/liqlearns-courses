# Single-Board Computers — Raspberry Pi — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a single-board computer?** → **A complete computer — processor, memory, storage, connections — on one printed circuit board**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does an SBC run?** → **A real operating system and ordinary programs, like a PC**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Which SBC does the book work with?** → **The Raspberry Pi — the book uses the Pi 3**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why does the chapter open with the SBC?** → **It is where the course's two worlds meet — a computer you use, and a board you wire**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is GPIO?** → **General-purpose input/output pins through which the computer senses and drives the world**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What can GPIO drive or sense?** → **Lights, buttons, sensors and motors**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How does a PC differ from an SBC here?** → **A PC is a computer of many boards, with no GPIO**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How does a smartphone differ?** → **A whole computer, but sealed — no open pins**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the Raspberry Pi Pico, despite its name?** → **A controller board (a microcontroller), not a full computer**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Where does Indonesia teach this?** → **Grade 12, Bab 2 A — single-board computers**
