# Single-Board Computers — Raspberry Pi — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a single-board computer?**
**Answer: A complete computer — processor, memory, storage, connections — on one printed circuit board**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does an SBC run?**
**Answer: A real operating system and ordinary programs, like a PC**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Which SBC does the book work with?**
**Answer: The Raspberry Pi — the book uses the Pi 3**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why does the chapter open with the SBC?**
**Answer: It is where the course's two worlds meet — a computer you use, and a board you wire**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is GPIO?**
**Answer: General-purpose input/output pins through which the computer senses and drives the world**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What can GPIO drive or sense?**
**Answer: Lights, buttons, sensors and motors**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How does a PC differ from an SBC here?**
**Answer: A PC is a computer of many boards, with no GPIO**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How does a smartphone differ?**
**Answer: A whole computer, but sealed — no open pins**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the Raspberry Pi Pico, despite its name?**
**Answer: A controller board (a microcontroller), not a full computer**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Where does Indonesia teach this?**
**Answer: Grade 12, Bab 2 A — single-board computers**
