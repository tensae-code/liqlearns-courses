# Choosing a Computer or Smartphone — Specifications — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does choosing well mean, in this lesson?**
a) Comparing criteria — specifications — against the work the device must do  b) The processor's speed and the amount of memory  c) The battery  d) Processor, memory, storage and operating system
**Answer: a) Comparing criteria — specifications — against the work the device must do**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name two criteria for choosing.**
a) Storage and the screen  b) The processor's speed and the amount of memory  c) The computer and the smartphone  d) Size, battery, and how they are carried and used
**Answer: b) The processor's speed and the amount of memory**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name two more criteria.**
a) The battery  b) Processor, memory, storage and operating system  c) Storage and the screen  d) Choose either device well — and say why
**Answer: c) Storage and the screen**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What extra criterion matters for a carried device?**
a) The computer and the smartphone  b) Size, battery, and how they are carried and used  c) Grade 11  d) The battery
**Answer: d) The battery**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) For which two devices does the lesson set the criteria?**
a) The computer and the smartphone  b) Processor, memory, storage and operating system  c) Choose either device well — and say why  d) One of the criteria a device is chosen by
**Answer: a) The computer and the smartphone**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What do the computer and smart device share?**
a) Size, battery, and how they are carried and used  b) Processor, memory, storage and operating system  c) Grade 11  d) Comparing criteria — specifications — against the work the device must do
**Answer: b) Processor, memory, storage and operating system**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Where do they differ?**
a) Choose either device well — and say why  b) One of the criteria a device is chosen by  c) Size, battery, and how they are carried and used  d) The processor's speed and the amount of memory
**Answer: c) Size, battery, and how they are carried and used**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What can a buyer who reads both lists with the same criteria do?**
a) Grade 11  b) Comparing criteria — specifications — against the work the device must do  c) Storage and the screen  d) Choose either device well — and say why
**Answer: d) Choose either device well — and say why**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which grade's book teaches this choosing lesson?**
a) Grade 11  b) One of the criteria a device is chosen by  c) The processor's speed and the amount of memory  d) The battery
**Answer: a) Grade 11**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is a specification, here?**
a) Comparing criteria — specifications — against the work the device must do  b) One of the criteria a device is chosen by  c) Storage and the screen  d) The computer and the smartphone
