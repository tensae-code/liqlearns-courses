# Hands-on — Choosing a Computer or Smartphone — Specifications

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Judge two phones
Find two real smartphones at the same price. List processor, memory, storage, screen and battery for each, and choose one for a named job — saying which criterion decided it.

## One list, two devices
Write one criteria list and use it twice: once for a computer, once for a smartphone. Mark the criteria that change meaning between the two.
