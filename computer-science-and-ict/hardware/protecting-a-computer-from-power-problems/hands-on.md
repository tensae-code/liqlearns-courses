# Hands-on — Protecting a computer from power problems

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Match harm to guard
Eight cards: four faults, four devices. Match them, then justify each match in the lesson's words. Scramble and repeat against the clock.

## Guard the lab (on paper)
Draw your school computer corner. Add the guards you would fit for: storm surges, an unsteady supply, evening brownouts, and sudden cuts. Label each with its harm.
