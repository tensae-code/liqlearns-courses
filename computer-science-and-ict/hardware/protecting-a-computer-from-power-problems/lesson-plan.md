# Lesson plan — Protecting a computer from power problems

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Hardware · **Lesson:** Protecting a computer from power problems
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 5 — Hardware Troubleshooting and Preventive Maintenance (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Electric power harms hardware four ways — too much, fluctuating, too little, and gone — and each harm has its own guard: surge protector, stabilizer, voltage regulator, and UPS.
**This lesson's own level:** It stands on Defragmenting — putting a file's pieces back together: the last preventive guard stands at the wall socket.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Write the four devices in a row, with the fault each guards against.
2. The voltage in a workshop rises and falls all day. Write which device belongs there, and what it does.
3. The power fails at 7 pm with unsaved work open. Write which device saves the work, and how many minutes it buys.

## If the learner is stuck
- Buys a surge protector for brownouts → too LITTLE electricity is the voltage regulator's harm; the surge protector guards against too MUCH.
- Treats the UPS as a long-term supply → it is a short bridge: 5–30 minutes, for saving and shutting down.
