# Protecting a computer from power problems

## Do it first

The lights flicker in a storm; a moment later they die completely. Between those two events, a computer can be harmed four different ways. Write the four harms as you imagine them — then match each to its guard.

## Power protection — four devices for four faults

Electric power damages hardware in **four distinct ways**, and each has its device:

- **Surge protector (suppressor)** — against **too much electricity**: it **absorbs a power surge**.
- **Stabilizer** — against **fluctuating voltage**: it **holds the supplied voltage at the desired level**. Fluctuations can severely damage motors and components and shorten their life.
- **Voltage regulator (line conditioner)** — against **too little electricity**: **sags and brownouts**.
- **UPS (uninterruptible power supply)** — against **power failure**: a short-term battery that keeps the computer running **5–30 minutes** after the power fails — long enough to **save data and shut down**. A UPS **also acts as a surge protector**.
