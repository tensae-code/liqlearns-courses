# Protecting a computer from power problems — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** A surge protector guards against —
a) too little electricity  b) too much electricity — it absorbs a surge  c) power failure  d) fluctuating voltage only
**Answer: b) too much electricity — it absorbs a surge** — too much: the surge.

**2.** A stabilizer —
a) absorbs surges  b) holds the supplied voltage at the desired level against fluctuations  c) is a battery  d) conditions sags only
**Answer: b) holds the supplied voltage at the desired level against fluctuations** — steady against fluctuation.

**3.** Fluctuations can —
a) do no harm  b) severely damage motors and components and shorten their life  c) only dim the screen  d) delete backups
**Answer: b) severely damage motors and components and shorten their life** — the book's warning on fluctuation.

**4.** Sags and brownouts — too little electricity — are guarded by —
a) the surge protector  b) the UPS only  c) the voltage regulator (line conditioner)  d) the stabilizer only
**Answer: c) the voltage regulator (line conditioner)** — the line conditioner's harm.

**5.** A UPS keeps the computer running for —
a) all night  b) 5–30 minutes after a power failure  c) one minute exactly  d) a week
**Answer: b) 5–30 minutes after a power failure** — a short-term battery bridge.

**6.** The UPS minutes are for —
a) finishing a game  b) saving data and shutting down  c) charging phones  d) running the printer
**Answer: b) saving data and shutting down** — save and shut down.

**7.** Besides its battery job, a UPS also acts as —
a) a stabilizer  b) a surge protector  c) a backup drive  d) an antivirus
**Answer: b) a surge protector** — its second job.
