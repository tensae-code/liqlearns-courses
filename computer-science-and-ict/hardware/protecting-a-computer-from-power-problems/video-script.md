# Video script — Protecting a computer from power problems

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. Short script; the placement names no full video need for this lesson.

---

Power can hurt a computer four ways.

Too much? A surge.
The surge protector absorbs it.

Rising and falling?
The stabilizer holds the voltage steady.

Too little — sags and brownouts?
The voltage regulator guards.

Power gone completely?
The UPS — a battery bridge,
five to thirty minutes:
save your work, shut down safely.
And it guards against surges too.
