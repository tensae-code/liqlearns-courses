# Video script — Scanner — paper into the computer

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Scanner.**

First, brainstorm: what do you already know about this? Say it out loud.

Now the one thing: a scanner accepts printed text, handwriting or an object and converts it into a digital document — data enters directly from the source document, with no copying and typing.

**What a scanner does.** A scanner accepts printed text, handwriting or an object and converts it into a digital document.
**What can be scanned.** What is scanned can be a picture, text, or a mark on paper.

Now you: Draw a page on a scanner glass becoming the same page on a screen.
