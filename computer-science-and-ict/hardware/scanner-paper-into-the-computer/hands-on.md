# Hands-on — Scanner — paper into the computer

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Scan if you can
If a scanner is available, scan a drawing you made. Compare the screen copy with your paper: what travelled, and what stayed the same?

## Typing race
Time a partner typing one paragraph. Then discuss: how long would the scanner's light bar take on the same page, and which copy can be edited afterwards?
