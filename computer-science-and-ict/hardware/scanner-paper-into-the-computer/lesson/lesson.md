# Scanner — paper into the computer

## Do it first

Hold a handwritten page. To get it into a computer by keyboard would take minutes of typing, with mistakes. Before reading on, guess the machine that takes the page as it is — and what travels across the page to read it.

## What a scanner does

A scanner accepts printed text, handwriting or an object and converts it into a digital document. Data goes into the computer directly from the source document, without copying and typing — the page itself is the input.

[Picture 10931 — scanner]

## What can be scanned

What is scanned can be a picture, text, or a mark on paper. Watch the GIF: the light bar travels under the glass from one end of the page to the other — that travelling light is how the scanner reads the page, line by line, into the computer.
