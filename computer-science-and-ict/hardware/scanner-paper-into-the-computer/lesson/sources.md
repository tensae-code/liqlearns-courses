**Acceptance count: taught in 4 countries (Ethiopia, Belarus, Libya, Pakistan).**

Belarus's own way (Grade 10): the Zabaroŭski–Pupcaŭ course treats informatics as applied mathematics with office tools — a full-year arc from data archiving through Pascal strings and information modelling to Excel and 2010-era Internet communication (NetMeeting, Skype, blogs).

Nothing in this lesson claims a page the placements do not give; the lesson's content is the placements' content, re-expressed for the learner, with the book's own examples kept.

Libya's own way (Grade 8, Computer student book): a three-unit arc — computer history/generations and number systems first, then a whole practical unit built around the scanner (scan, save, copy, delete, scan via Paint), then Microsoft Excel 2007 taught step by step with screenshots. The basic-education line is الحاسوب (Computer) in Grades 8–9.

Jordan's own way (Jordan Grade 4 G4 Teacher): Guide plans mirror the workbook blocks (PowerPoint, Excel, Word) with the same per-block learning products and assessment appendices as the other early-grades guides.

Jordan's own way (Grade 4 Digital Skills Teacher Guide — links-pass L2 sections): Digital Skills is not a standalone subject book at this level — the guide embeds digital skills inside the child's other subjects as six "learning environments" (Science and Maths units) plus staged learning projects, each with named digital tools (PowerPoint, Excel, Word, Padlet, PhET), learning outcomes, and a performance rubric.


Pakistan's own way (Grade 6 Computer Science 6): every unit opens with Students Learning Outcomes, is dotted with "Do you Know?", "Extra Bit!" and "Tips" boxes, and closes with Summary, MCQ/short/detailed exercises, project-based and activity-based questions, with an answers key at the back. Digital skills (Windows desktop, file management, Paint 3D, a typing tutor) are taught hands-on BEFORE programming; digital citizenship is framed through Pakistani citizenship first; the entrepreneurship unit teaches from Pakistani founders (Foodpanda/EatOye, Shopsy.pk, Zero Makeup, Investors Lounge).

Pakistan's own way (Grade 7 Computer Science 7): Grade 7 opens with emerging technologies and a deep ports-and-peripherals hardware treatment, then teaches email all the way to DKIM/SPF/DMARC authentication and POP3/IMAP/SMTP protocols; binary data representation (ASCII/Unicode, RGB colour, sound sampling) is taught inside the Programming unit, and entrepreneurship becomes business-plan craft — promotion, value proposition, quality assurance, SEO and platform business models, with Twitter and Snapchat origin stories.
