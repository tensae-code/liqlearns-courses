# Video script — OMR marks exams — and barcode readers

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

**OMR marks exams.**

First, brainstorm: what do you already know about this? Say it out loud.

Now the one thing: OMR senses a pencil mark's presence or absence and matches answer sheets against an answer key — as Ethiopia's school leaving examination does — while a barcode reader reads the printed codes that identify products.

**OMR — marks, not letters.** Optical Mark Recognition senses the presence or absence of a mark, such as a pencil mark.
**The barcode reader.** A barcode reader reads barcodes printed on products.

Now you: Draw a pencil-marked answer sheet being checked against an answer key, beside a barcode on a product.
