# OMR marks exams — and barcode readers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** OMR senses —
a) The meaning of essays  b) The presence or absence of a mark, such as a pencil mark  c) Only colours  d) Sound
**Answer: b) The presence or absence of a mark, such as a pencil mark** — OMR senses the presence or absence of a mark.

**2.** Does OMR identify letters?
a) Yes, always  b) No — it does not identify letters  c) Only capital letters  d) Only numbers
**Answer: b) No — it does not identify letters** — OMR does not identify letters; it senses marks.

**3.** OMR matches the marks against —
a) A dictionary  b) A previously entered answer key  c) A photograph  d) A price list
**Answer: b) A previously entered answer key** — Marks and symbols are matched against a previously entered answer key.

**4.** The book's OMR example is —
a) A supermarket till  b) Ethiopia's School Leaving Certificate Examination (ESLCE)  c) A library  d) A petrol pump
**Answer: b) Ethiopia's School Leaving Certificate Examination (ESLCE)** — The ESLCE is the book's own example of OMR exam marking.

**5.** UPC identifies —
a) The price and the date  b) The manufacturer and the product number  c) The shop owner  d) The customer's name
**Answer: b) The manufacturer and the product number** — Universal Product Codes identify manufacturer and product number.

**6.** Barcode readers are used in —
a) Library management and inventory control  b) Cooking  c) Painting  d) Farming only
**Answer: a) Library management and inventory control** — Barcode readers are used in library management and inventory control.
