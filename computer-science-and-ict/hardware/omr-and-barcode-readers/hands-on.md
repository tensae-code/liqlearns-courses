# Hands-on — OMR marks exams — and barcode readers

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Bubble sheet
Make a ten-question answer sheet with bubbles, fill it in pencil, and swap with a partner. Mark each other's sheets against a key by sensing only mark presence — you have done OMR by eye.

## Barcode hunt
Find five barcodes on products at home. Under each is the UPC idea: manufacturer + product. Photograph or copy the bars and say what each product is.
