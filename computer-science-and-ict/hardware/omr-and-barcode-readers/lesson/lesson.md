# OMR marks exams — and barcode readers

## Do it first

Thousands of examination answer sheets must be marked. No teacher could read every pencilled bubble by eye in reasonable time. Before reading on, guess: does the marking machine need to read letters — or only to sense where the dark marks are?

## OMR — marks, not letters

Optical Mark Recognition senses the presence or absence of a mark, such as a pencil mark. It does not identify letters. It converts marks and symbols into electronic signals, which are matched against a previously entered answer key. That is how it is used to correct exams — and Ethiopia's School Leaving Certificate Examination (ESLCE) is the book's own example.

## The barcode reader

A barcode reader reads barcodes printed on products. Most commonly these are Universal Product Codes (UPC), which identify the manufacturer and the product number. Watch the GIF: the reader's beam sweeps across the black-and-white bars, and the code is read. Barcode readers are used in library management and inventory control — knowing what book or item is where.
