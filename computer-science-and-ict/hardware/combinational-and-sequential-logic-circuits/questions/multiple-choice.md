# Combinational and Sequential Logic Circuits — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does a combinational output depend on?**
a) Only on the inputs it holds now — no memory of before  b) Two bits, into a sum and a carry  c) XOR for the sum, AND for the carry  d) Three bits — two bits and the carry-in
**Answer: a**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does a half-adder add?**
a) XOR for the sum, AND for the carry  b) Two bits, into a sum and a carry  c) Three bits — two bits and the carry-in  d) Add whole numbers, place by place
**Answer: b**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Which gates make the half-adder?**
a) Three bits — two bits and the carry-in  b) Add whole numbers, place by place  c) XOR for the sum, AND for the carry  d) Its inputs and what it remembers — its stored state
**Answer: c**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does a full-adder add?**
a) Add whole numbers, place by place  b) Its inputs and what it remembers — its stored state  c) A circuit holding one bit, set and reset by its inputs, keeping it until told to change  d) Three bits — two bits and the carry-in
**Answer: d**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What do chained full-adders do?**
a) Add whole numbers, place by place  b) Its inputs and what it remembers — its stored state  c) A circuit holding one bit, set and reset by its inputs, keeping it until told to change  d) Counters (and registers)
**Answer: a**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does a sequential output depend on?**
a) A circuit holding one bit, set and reset by its inputs, keeping it until told to change  b) Its inputs and what it remembers — its stored state  c) Counters (and registers)  d) Pulses, in binary — each flip-flop a place
**Answer: b**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is a flip-flop?**
a) Counters (and registers)  b) Pulses, in binary — each flip-flop a place  c) A circuit holding one bit, set and reset by its inputs, keeping it until told to change  d) Does it remember?
**Answer: c**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What do chained flip-flops become?**
a) Pulses, in binary — each flip-flop a place  b) Does it remember?  c) Only on the inputs it holds now — no memory of before  d) Counters (and registers)
**Answer: d**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does a counter count?**
a) Pulses, in binary — each flip-flop a place  b) Does it remember?  c) Only on the inputs it holds now — no memory of before  d) Two bits, into a sum and a carry
**Answer: a**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) The one question that sorts any circuit is what?**
a) Only on the inputs it holds now — no memory of before  b) Does it remember?  c) Two bits, into a sum and a carry  d) XOR for the sum, AND for the carry
**Answer: b**
