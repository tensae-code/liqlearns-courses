# Combinational and Sequential Logic Circuits — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does a combinational output depend on?**
**Answer: Only on the inputs it holds now — no memory of before**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does a half-adder add?**
**Answer: Two bits, into a sum and a carry**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Which gates make the half-adder?**
**Answer: XOR for the sum, AND for the carry**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does a full-adder add?**
**Answer: Three bits — two bits and the carry-in**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What do chained full-adders do?**
**Answer: Add whole numbers, place by place**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does a sequential output depend on?**
**Answer: Its inputs and what it remembers — its stored state**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is a flip-flop?**
**Answer: A circuit holding one bit, set and reset by its inputs, keeping it until told to change**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What do chained flip-flops become?**
**Answer: Counters (and registers)**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does a counter count?**
**Answer: Pulses, in binary — each flip-flop a place**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) The one question that sorts any circuit is what?**
**Answer: Does it remember?**
