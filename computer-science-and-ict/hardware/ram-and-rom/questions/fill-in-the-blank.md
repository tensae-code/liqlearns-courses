# RAM and ROM — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) RAM is ____: its contents are lost when the computer is switched off.**
**Answer: volatile**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ____ holds permanent data and can only be read.**
**Answer: ROM**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) In keeping, RAM is temporary and ROM is ____.**
**Answer: permanent**
