# Hands-on — RAM and ROM

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Pencil and print
Write a sentence in pencil on scrap paper, then copy one line from a printed book. Rub out the pencil sentence. That erased page is RAM at switch-off; the printed line, still readable, is ROM.

## Chart from memory
Fold a paper in half: RAM left, ROM right. Fill the three rows — use, keeping, speed — from memory, then check against the lesson's chart.
