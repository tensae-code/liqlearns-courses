# Magnetic tape and magnetic disk — the hard disk

## Do it first

A cassette tape must wind forward to find a song near the end. A hard disk jumps straight to any file. Before reading on, say what makes the jump possible for one and impossible for the other.

## Magnetic tape

Magnetic tape is a thin plastic ribbon coated on one side with iron oxide. It stores data by magnetising and demagnetising the tape. Tape was the main way of storing large amounts of data in the 1950s and 1960s; now it is used mainly for backup — remember the backup rule: a copy in a different place.

## Magnetic disk and the hard disk

A magnetic disk is a round plastic or metal disk covered with magnetic material. It is the most widely used storage medium, because it allows random access — jumping straight to the data — instead of tape's slow access, which must wind through what comes before. The hard disk is the fixed magnetic disk inside the system unit: metal, very fast to access, its surface divided into tracks and sectors, where the information is stored.
