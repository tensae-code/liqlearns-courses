**Magnetic tape and magnetic disk.**

First, brainstorm: what do you already know about this? Say it out loud.

Now the one thing: magnetic tape stores data by magnetising a coated ribbon — once the main store, now mainly for backup — while the magnetic disk, above all the hard disk with its tracks and sectors, allows fast random access and is the most widely used storage medium.

**Magnetic tape.** Magnetic tape is a thin plastic ribbon coated on one side with iron oxide.
**Magnetic disk and the hard disk.** A magnetic disk is a round plastic or metal disk covered with magnetic material.

Now you: Draw a tape reel beside a hard disk opened to show tracks and sectors.
