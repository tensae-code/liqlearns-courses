# Hands-on — Magnetic tape and magnetic disk — the hard disk

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Wind or jump
With a roll of paper tape (or a long ribbon), find a mark near the end by winding through — then point straight at a mark on a round paper disk. The difference you felt is sequential against random access.

## Tracks and sectors
Draw concentric circles on a paper plate and divide them with lines, like cutting a cake across the rings. Label: tracks (the rings), sectors (the slices). Information lives in their crossings.
