# Magnetic tape and magnetic disk — the hard disk — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Magnetic tape stores data by —
a) Burning pits  b) Magnetising and demagnetising the tape  c) Printing ink  d) Charging batteries
**Answer: b) Magnetising and demagnetising the tape** — Tape stores data by magnetising and demagnetising its iron-oxide coating.

**2.** Magnetic tape is coated on one side with —
a) Plastic paint  b) Iron oxide  c) Copper  d) Glass
**Answer: b) Iron oxide** — A thin plastic ribbon coated on one side with iron oxide.

**3.** Today, magnetic tape is used mainly for —
a) Games  b) Backup  c) Printing  d) Displays
**Answer: b) Backup** — Now tape is used mainly for backup.

**4.** A magnetic disk allows — instead of tape's slow access.
a) Random access  b) No access  c) Slower access  d) Sound access
**Answer: a) Random access** — The disk allows random access instead of tape's slow access.

**5.** The hard disk's surface is divided into —
a) Pages and chapters  b) Tracks and sectors  c) Rows and columns only  d) Keys and buttons
**Answer: b) Tracks and sectors** — Its surface is divided into tracks and sectors where information is stored.

**6.** The hard disk is —
a) A removable paper disk  b) The fixed magnetic disk inside the system unit, metal and very fast to access  c) A type of tape  d) An output device
**Answer: b) The fixed magnetic disk inside the system unit, metal and very fast to access** — The hard disk is fixed inside the system unit, metal, very fast to access.
