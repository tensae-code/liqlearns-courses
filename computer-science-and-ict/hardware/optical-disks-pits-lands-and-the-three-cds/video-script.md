# Video script — Optical disks — pits, lands and the three CDs

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

**Optical disks.**

First, brainstorm: what do you already know about this? Say it out loud.

Now the one thing: an optical disk is written and read by laser light: the burned part is a pit (1), the unburned part is land (0) — and a CD holds 700 MB and comes as CD-ROM, CD-R or CD-RW.

**Pits and lands.** An optical disk is read and written by laser light.
**The CD and its three types.** A CD stores 700 MB on one side.

Now you: Draw a magnified disk surface with pits and lands labelled 1 and 0, and the three CD types under it.
