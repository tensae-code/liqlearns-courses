# Lesson plan — Optical disks — pits, lands and the three CDs

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Hardware · **Lesson:** Optical disks — pits, lands and the three CDs
**Place in the curriculum:** Title 2 — Hardware of the Computer Science and ICT ladder (TITLES.md); Ethiopia Grade 8, Unit 2 — Computer Hardware.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** an optical disk is written and read by laser light: the burned part is a pit (1), the unburned part is land (0) — and a CD holds 700 MB and comes as CD-ROM, CD-R or CD-RW.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the magnetic-storage lesson: magnetic storage writes with magnetism; optical storage writes with light.

## If the learner is stuck
- Swaps pit and land → LAND (unburned) = 0; PIT (burned) = 1.
- Mixes CD-R and CD-RW → R = write once, read many (WORM); RW = write and erase many times.
