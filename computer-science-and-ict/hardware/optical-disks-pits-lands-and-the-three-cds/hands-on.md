# Hands-on — Optical disks — pits, lands and the three CDs

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Burn the bits
On dark paper, represent a row of bits: a white chalk dash for land (0), a pressed black dot for pit (1). Write your age in binary as pits and lands for a partner to read back.

## Write-once test
Sort task cards: a school's final exam archive, a music album, a draft document you revise weekly. Match each to CD-ROM, CD-R or CD-RW and defend the match.
