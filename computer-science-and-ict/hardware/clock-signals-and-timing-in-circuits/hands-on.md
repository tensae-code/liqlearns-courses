# Hands-on — Clock Signals and Timing in Circuits

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Clap the clock
Clap a steady beat — you are the oscillator. A companion moves a counter one step on each clap. Speed the beat: what happens to the 'circuit'?

## Hertz hunt
Find three clock speeds (a processor, a watch crystal, a blinking light). Convert each to pulses per second and order them.
