# Clock Signals and Timing in Circuits — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What makes clock pulses?**
**Answer: An oscillator**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is a clock signal?**
**Answer: A steady stream of pulses**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does the clock do for a circuit?**
**Answer: Times when parts of the circuit act**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Clock speed is measured in what unit?**
**Answer: Hertz (pulses per second)**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How do the parts of an integrated circuit act?**
**Answer: In step with the clock signal**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) More pulses per second means what?**
**Answer: A faster clock**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is one full pulse cycle called?**
**Answer: A clock period (one clock cycle)**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why must a circuit's parts share timing?**
**Answer: So data moves between them correctly**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does the clock coordinate in a computer?**
**Answer: The processor and the other circuits**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why is a crystal oscillator valued?**
**Answer: It gives steady, accurate pulses**
