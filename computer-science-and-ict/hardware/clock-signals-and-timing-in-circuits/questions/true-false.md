# Clock Signals and Timing in Circuits — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What makes clock pulses?" is: An oscillator**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is a clock signal?" is: Hertz (pulses per second)**
**Answer: False** — A steady stream of pulses

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does the clock do for a circuit?" is: Times when parts of the circuit act**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Clock speed is measured in what unit?" is: A faster clock**
**Answer: False** — Hertz (pulses per second)

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "How do the parts of an integrated circuit act?" is: In step with the clock signal**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "More pulses per second means what?" is: So data moves between them correctly**
**Answer: False** — A faster clock

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is one full pulse cycle called?" is: A clock period (one clock cycle)**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Why must a circuit's parts share timing?" is: It gives steady, accurate pulses**
**Answer: False** — So data moves between them correctly

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does the clock coordinate in a computer?" is: The processor and the other circuits**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Why is a crystal oscillator valued?" is: A steady stream of pulses**
**Answer: False** — It gives steady, accurate pulses
