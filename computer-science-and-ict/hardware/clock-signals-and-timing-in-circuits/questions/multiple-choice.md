# Clock Signals and Timing in Circuits — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What makes clock pulses?**
a) An oscillator  b) A steady stream of pulses  c) Times when parts of the circuit act  d) Hertz (pulses per second)
**Answer: a) An oscillator**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is a clock signal?**
a) Times when parts of the circuit act  b) A steady stream of pulses  c) Hertz (pulses per second)  d) In step with the clock signal
**Answer: b) A steady stream of pulses**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does the clock do for a circuit?**
a) Hertz (pulses per second)  b) In step with the clock signal  c) Times when parts of the circuit act  d) A faster clock
**Answer: c) Times when parts of the circuit act**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Clock speed is measured in what unit?**
a) In step with the clock signal  b) A faster clock  c) A clock period (one clock cycle)  d) Hertz (pulses per second)
**Answer: d) Hertz (pulses per second)**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How do the parts of an integrated circuit act?**
a) In step with the clock signal  b) A faster clock  c) A clock period (one clock cycle)  d) So data moves between them correctly
**Answer: a) In step with the clock signal**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) More pulses per second means what?**
a) A clock period (one clock cycle)  b) A faster clock  c) So data moves between them correctly  d) The processor and the other circuits
**Answer: b) A faster clock**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is one full pulse cycle called?**
a) So data moves between them correctly  b) The processor and the other circuits  c) A clock period (one clock cycle)  d) It gives steady, accurate pulses
**Answer: c) A clock period (one clock cycle)**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why must a circuit's parts share timing?**
a) The processor and the other circuits  b) It gives steady, accurate pulses  c) An oscillator  d) So data moves between them correctly
**Answer: d) So data moves between them correctly**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does the clock coordinate in a computer?**
a) The processor and the other circuits  b) It gives steady, accurate pulses  c) An oscillator  d) A steady stream of pulses
**Answer: a) The processor and the other circuits**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why is a crystal oscillator valued?**
a) An oscillator  b) It gives steady, accurate pulses  c) A steady stream of pulses  d) Times when parts of the circuit act
**Answer: b) It gives steady, accurate pulses**
