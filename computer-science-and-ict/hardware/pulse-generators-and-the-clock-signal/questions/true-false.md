# Pulse Generators — and the Clock Signal — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: A signal jumping between two levels — drawn as a rectangle**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: A signal jumping between two levels — drawn as a rectangle** — as the answer to: What does a clock generator produce?
**Answer: False — An endless, even train of pulses — the clock signal**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: By frequency — pulses per second, in hertz; and by its period, the seconds per cycle**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: By frequency — pulses per second, in hertz; and by its period, the seconds per cycle** — as the answer to: What does 1 MHz mean?
**Answer: False — A million pulses per second**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Low and high levels**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Low and high levels** — as the answer to: When do sequential circuits change state?
**Answer: False — At the clock's edge — all together**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: One synchronous machine**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: One synchronous machine** — as the answer to: What goes wrong without a clock?
**Answer: False — Signals arrive at their own speeds and memory smears**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: The whole computer — operations per second track it**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: The whole computer — operations per second track it** — as the answer to: Where does the computer's advertised speed come from?
**Answer: False — Its clock frequency — gigahertz, billions of beats per second**
