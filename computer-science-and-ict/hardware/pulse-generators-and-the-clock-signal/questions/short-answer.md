# Pulse Generators — and the Clock Signal — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a pulse?**
**Answer: A signal jumping between two levels — drawn as a rectangle**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does a clock generator produce?**
**Answer: An endless, even train of pulses — the clock signal**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How is the clock measured?**
**Answer: By frequency — pulses per second, in hertz; and by its period, the seconds per cycle**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does 1 MHz mean?**
**Answer: A million pulses per second**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What two values does a pulse jump between?**
**Answer: Low and high levels**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) When do sequential circuits change state?**
**Answer: At the clock's edge — all together**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does the clock make of a million separate circuits?**
**Answer: One synchronous machine**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What goes wrong without a clock?**
**Answer: Signals arrive at their own speeds and memory smears**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What does the clock's frequency rate?**
**Answer: The whole computer — operations per second track it**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Where does the computer's advertised speed come from?**
**Answer: Its clock frequency — gigahertz, billions of beats per second**
