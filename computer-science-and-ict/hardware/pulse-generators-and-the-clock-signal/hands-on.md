# Hands-on — Pulse Generators — and the Clock Signal

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Period arithmetic** — Compute the periods of a 1 kHz, a 1 MHz and a 3 GHz clock, and how many beats each gives in one second and in one millisecond.

## Task two
**Conductor drawing** — Draw two clock cycles and mark the edges at which a flip-flop may change; annotate what a register does between edges.
