# Hands-on — Step 1 — identify the network problem

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Boundary map
Draw your school network and mark the boundary: inside it, what your school manages; outside it, the provider's network. Label each side with the guide's definitions.

## Identification rehearsal
Invent a morning fault: half a lab offline after a new program arrived. Write the six moves applied to it, one line each.
