# Video script — Step 1 — identify the network problem

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The network is down. Stop. Think first.

Is the fault ours — our own lab?
Or theirs — the provider outside?

Gather the facts. Copy the fault, if you can.
Ask the users. Name the symptoms.
What changed yesterday?
Define the problem. Clearly.

That is step one.
