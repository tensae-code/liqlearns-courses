# Step 1 — identify the network problem — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The very first decision is whether the fault is —
a) old or new  b) inside your control (local network) or outside it  c) a cable or a setting  d) big or small
**Answer: b) inside your control (local network) or outside it**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The guide's example of a local network is — (teacher guide — evidence)
a) a telecom network  b) the school computer-laboratory network  c) the national grid  d) a satellite link
**Answer: b) the school computer-laboratory network**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** An outside network is managed by — (teacher guide — evidence)
a) the pupils  b) an outsider, third party or service provider  c) the lab teacher  d) nobody
**Answer: b) an outsider, third party or service provider**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Identifying the problem includes —
a) gathering information on the network's current state with the available tools  b) replacing the router at once  c) documenting the solution  d) ordering parts first
**Answer: a) gathering information on the network's current state with the available tools**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** "Complete loss? slowness? network-wide or one user?" — these questions identify —
a) the budget  b) the symptoms  c) the provider  d) the tools
**Answer: b) the symptoms**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Before the problems appeared, you check whether —
a) the weather changed  b) anything changed — new hardware, new users, a software update  c) the term started  d) new paint arrived
**Answer: b) anything changed — new hardware, new users, a software update**
