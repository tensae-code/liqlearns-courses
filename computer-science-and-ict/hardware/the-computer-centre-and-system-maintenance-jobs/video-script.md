# Video script — The Computer Centre and System Maintenance Jobs

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

In the world of Sudan's Grade 11 book, an organisation's computing lives in one place: the **computer centre** — the unit that houses the machines, the data and the people who run them. Work flows into the centre (data to be processed) and out of it (results, reports, printouts), and the centre guards both: the machines against damage and power failure, the data against loss. The book places the centre inside its systems unit because a system is not only programs — it is a place, machines and staff, run as one.
A system does not stay working by itself, and the book names the human side of keeping it so: the **maintenance jobs** — the people whose work is the system's health. They keep the machines clean and working, find and fix faults, keep backup copies safe, and keep the system's programs and files in order as the organisation's needs change. A breakdown in this world is not an inconvenience; payrolls and records stop with the machines — which is why maintenance is a named job in the book, not an afterthought.
