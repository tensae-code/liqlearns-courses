# Hands-on — The Computer Centre and System Maintenance Jobs

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Map the centre
Draw a computer centre: machines, data store, the door where work enters and results leave, and mark what each part must be guarded against.

## A week of maintenance
Write a one-week maintenance list for a school computer room — cleaning, fault checks, backups, program upkeep — one job per day.
