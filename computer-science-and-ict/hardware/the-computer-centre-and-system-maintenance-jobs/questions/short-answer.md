# The Computer Centre and System Maintenance Jobs — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a computer centre?**
**Answer: The unit that houses an organisation's machines, data and the people who run them**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What flows into the centre?**
**Answer: Data to be processed**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What flows out of it?**
**Answer: Results, reports and printouts**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does the centre guard?**
**Answer: The machines against damage and power failure, and the data against loss**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why does the book put the centre in its systems unit?**
**Answer: Because a system is also a place, machines and staff, run as one**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What are the maintenance jobs?**
**Answer: The jobs whose work is the system's health — keeping machines and programs working**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Name three maintenance duties.**
**Answer: Keeping machines clean and working, finding and fixing faults, and keeping backup copies safe**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What else do maintainers keep in order?**
**Answer: The system's programs and files, as the organisation's needs change**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What stops when the machines break down in this book's world?**
**Answer: Payrolls and records**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Is maintenance an afterthought in the book?**
**Answer: No — it is a named job**
