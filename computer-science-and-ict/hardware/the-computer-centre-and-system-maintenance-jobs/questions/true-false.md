# The Computer Centre and System Maintenance Jobs — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is a computer centre?" is: The unit that houses an organisation's machines, data and the people who run them**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What flows into the centre?" is: The machines against damage and power failure, and the data against loss**
**Answer: False** — Data to be processed

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What flows out of it?" is: Results, reports and printouts**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does the centre guard?" is: The jobs whose work is the system's health — keeping machines and programs working**
**Answer: False** — The machines against damage and power failure, and the data against loss

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Why does the book put the centre in its systems unit?" is: Because a system is also a place, machines and staff, run as one**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What are the maintenance jobs?" is: The system's programs and files, as the organisation's needs change**
**Answer: False** — The jobs whose work is the system's health — keeping machines and programs working

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Name three maintenance duties." is: Keeping machines clean and working, finding and fixing faults, and keeping backup copies safe**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What else do maintainers keep in order?" is: No — it is a named job**
**Answer: False** — The system's programs and files, as the organisation's needs change

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What stops when the machines break down in this book's world?" is: Payrolls and records**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Is maintenance an afterthought in the book?" is: Data to be processed**
**Answer: False** — No — it is a named job
