# The Computer Centre and System Maintenance Jobs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a computer centre?**
a) The unit that houses an organisation's machines, data and the people who run them  b) Data to be processed  c) The machines against damage and power failure, and the data against loss  d) The jobs whose work is the system's health — keeping machines and programs working
**Answer: a) The unit that houses an organisation's machines, data and the people who run them**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What flows into the centre?**
a) Results, reports and printouts  b) Data to be processed  c) Because a system is also a place, machines and staff, run as one  d) Keeping machines clean and working, finding and fixing faults, and keeping backup copies safe
**Answer: b) Data to be processed**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What flows out of it?**
a) The machines against damage and power failure, and the data against loss  b) The jobs whose work is the system's health — keeping machines and programs working  c) Results, reports and printouts  d) The system's programs and files, as the organisation's needs change
**Answer: c) Results, reports and printouts**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does the centre guard?**
a) Because a system is also a place, machines and staff, run as one  b) Keeping machines clean and working, finding and fixing faults, and keeping backup copies safe  c) Payrolls and records  d) The machines against damage and power failure, and the data against loss
**Answer: d) The machines against damage and power failure, and the data against loss**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why does the book put the centre in its systems unit?**
a) Because a system is also a place, machines and staff, run as one  b) The jobs whose work is the system's health — keeping machines and programs working  c) The system's programs and files, as the organisation's needs change  d) No — it is a named job
**Answer: a) Because a system is also a place, machines and staff, run as one**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What are the maintenance jobs?**
a) Keeping machines clean and working, finding and fixing faults, and keeping backup copies safe  b) The jobs whose work is the system's health — keeping machines and programs working  c) Payrolls and records  d) The unit that houses an organisation's machines, data and the people who run them
**Answer: b) The jobs whose work is the system's health — keeping machines and programs working**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Name three maintenance duties.**
a) The system's programs and files, as the organisation's needs change  b) No — it is a named job  c) Keeping machines clean and working, finding and fixing faults, and keeping backup copies safe  d) Data to be processed
**Answer: c) Keeping machines clean and working, finding and fixing faults, and keeping backup copies safe**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What else do maintainers keep in order?**
a) Payrolls and records  b) The unit that houses an organisation's machines, data and the people who run them  c) Results, reports and printouts  d) The system's programs and files, as the organisation's needs change

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What stops when the machines break down in this book's world?**
a) Payrolls and records  b) No — it is a named job  c) Data to be processed  d) The machines against damage and power failure, and the data against loss
