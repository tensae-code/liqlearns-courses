# Inside the System Unit — Motherboard and Power Supply — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is the motherboard?" is: The large main board inside the system unit that every other part connects to**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What sits on the motherboard as the machine's processor?" is: It sits on the processor and draws its heat away**
**Answer: False** — The CPU

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What do expansion slots take?" is: Extra cards that grow what the machine can do**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What carries data, instructions and power between the parts?" is: The machine stays silent and dark, however healthy the rest is**
**Answer: False** — The motherboard's printed pathways

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does a heat sink do?" is: It sits on the processor and draws its heat away**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does the power supply do?" is: It plugs into slots on the motherboard**
**Answer: False** — It takes electricity from the wall and feeds every part at the level it needs

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What happens if the power supply is dead?" is: The machine stays silent and dark, however healthy the rest is**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Why are board and power supply taught together?" is: The large main board inside the system unit that every other part connects to**
**Answer: False** — One joins everything and the other feeds everything — the system unit's foundation

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Where does memory go inside the unit?" is: It plugs into slots on the motherboard**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What grows a machine's abilities through the board?" is: Extra cards that grow what the machine can do**
**Answer: False** — Expansion cards in the expansion slots
