# Inside the System Unit — Motherboard and Power Supply — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is the motherboard?**
a) The large main board inside the system unit that every other part connects to  b) The CPU  c) Extra cards that grow what the machine can do  d) The motherboard's printed pathways
**Answer: a) The large main board inside the system unit that every other part connects to**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What sits on the motherboard as the machine's processor?**
a) Extra cards that grow what the machine can do  b) The CPU  c) The motherboard's printed pathways  d) It sits on the processor and draws its heat away
**Answer: b) The CPU**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What do expansion slots take?**
a) The motherboard's printed pathways  b) It sits on the processor and draws its heat away  c) Extra cards that grow what the machine can do  d) It takes electricity from the wall and feeds every part at the level it needs
**Answer: c) Extra cards that grow what the machine can do**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What carries data, instructions and power between the parts?**
a) It sits on the processor and draws its heat away  b) It takes electricity from the wall and feeds every part at the level it needs  c) The machine stays silent and dark, however healthy the rest is  d) The motherboard's printed pathways
**Answer: d) The motherboard's printed pathways**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does a heat sink do?**
a) It sits on the processor and draws its heat away  b) It takes electricity from the wall and feeds every part at the level it needs  c) The machine stays silent and dark, however healthy the rest is  d) One joins everything and the other feeds everything — the system unit's foundation
**Answer: a) It sits on the processor and draws its heat away**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does the power supply do?**
a) The machine stays silent and dark, however healthy the rest is  b) It takes electricity from the wall and feeds every part at the level it needs  c) One joins everything and the other feeds everything — the system unit's foundation  d) It plugs into slots on the motherboard
**Answer: b) It takes electricity from the wall and feeds every part at the level it needs**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What happens if the power supply is dead?**
a) One joins everything and the other feeds everything — the system unit's foundation  b) It plugs into slots on the motherboard  c) The machine stays silent and dark, however healthy the rest is  d) Expansion cards in the expansion slots
**Answer: c) The machine stays silent and dark, however healthy the rest is**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why are board and power supply taught together?**
a) It plugs into slots on the motherboard  b) Expansion cards in the expansion slots  c) The large main board inside the system unit that every other part connects to  d) One joins everything and the other feeds everything — the system unit's foundation
**Answer: d) One joins everything and the other feeds everything — the system unit's foundation**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Where does memory go inside the unit?**
a) It plugs into slots on the motherboard  b) Expansion cards in the expansion slots  c) The large main board inside the system unit that every other part connects to  d) The CPU
**Answer: a) It plugs into slots on the motherboard**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What grows a machine's abilities through the board?**
a) The large main board inside the system unit that every other part connects to  b) Expansion cards in the expansion slots  c) The CPU  d) Extra cards that grow what the machine can do
**Answer: b) Expansion cards in the expansion slots**
