# Inside the System Unit — Motherboard and Power Supply — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is the motherboard?**
**Answer: The large main board inside the system unit that every other part connects to**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What sits on the motherboard as the machine's processor?**
**Answer: The CPU**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What do expansion slots take?**
**Answer: Extra cards that grow what the machine can do**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What carries data, instructions and power between the parts?**
**Answer: The motherboard's printed pathways**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does a heat sink do?**
**Answer: It sits on the processor and draws its heat away**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does the power supply do?**
**Answer: It takes electricity from the wall and feeds every part at the level it needs**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What happens if the power supply is dead?**
**Answer: The machine stays silent and dark, however healthy the rest is**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why are board and power supply taught together?**
**Answer: One joins everything and the other feeds everything — the system unit's foundation**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Where does memory go inside the unit?**
**Answer: It plugs into slots on the motherboard**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What grows a machine's abilities through the board?**
**Answer: Expansion cards in the expansion slots**
