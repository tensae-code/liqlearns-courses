# Video script — Uninstalling software on a PC

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today we remove a program — properly. The full walkthrough.

Deleting the picture on the desktop does nothing. The program is still there.
We use the computer's own door.

One. Open the Control Panel.
Two. Under Programs, click: Uninstall a Program.
Three. The list opens — every program on the computer. Choose the one to remove.
Four. Click Uninstall/Change, at the top.
Five. A warning asks — give the administrator password, or click Continue.
Six. Follow the screen until it says: removed.

Now the space is free, and the computer breathes again.
And remember — on a school computer, ask the owner first.
