# Hands-on — Uninstalling software on a PC

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Paper uninstall
Draw the Programs and Features window with four programs listed. Cross out the route: select one program, mark Uninstall/Change, mark the UAC answer. A partner checks your order.

## Permission first
Write the short note you would give a teacher asking permission to uninstall a named program from a school computer, giving your reason — freed space or speed.
