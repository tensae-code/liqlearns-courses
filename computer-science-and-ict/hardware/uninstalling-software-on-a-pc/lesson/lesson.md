# Uninstalling software on a PC

## Do it first

A computer is full and slow, and three programs on it are never used. Deleting their icons feels quick — but it is not uninstalling. Write what you would do instead — then follow the procedure.

## Sub lesson 1 — Why uninstall — and whose machine it is

**Uninstalling unused or unwanted programs frees hard-drive space and increases speed.** But software must be **properly uninstalled**, and the **reasons known first**. On an **organisation, school, library or laboratory device — or someone else's personal device** — **consult a technician or the owner before acting**.

> **Teacher evidence (teacher guide — evidence, printed pp. 100–101):** pupils should only propose removing applications from a shared or school computer **with prior approval from the teacher or the computer's owner**; and operating-system **version changes move icons and layouts around**, so your screens may look different from the book's.

## Sub lesson 2 — The procedure — Control Panel to removed

The procedure, in order:

1. Open the **Control Panel**.
2. Click **"Uninstall a Program"** under the **Programs** heading.
3. In the **Programs and Features** window, which lists **all installed software**, **select the program**.
4. Click **Uninstall/Change** on the toolbar.
5. Answer any **User Account Control** prompt with the **administrator password or Continue**.
6. **Follow the on-screen instructions** until the program is removed — directions **vary by program**.
