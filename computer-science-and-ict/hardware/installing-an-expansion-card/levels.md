# Installing an Expansion Card — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, expressed as its SUB LESSONS. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Choosing the slot and opening the case back
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: A free expansion slot of its kind; The blanking plate over that slot's opening; By its edges and its metal bracket; Pressed straight down with even pressure until its contacts are fully home; A card not connected.

## Sub lesson 2 — Seating and securing the card
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Its bracket screwed to the case; The card's power lead or internal cable; The seat, not the software; Laos Grade 8 ICT, Chapter 2 §2.5; At switch-on after a correct install.
