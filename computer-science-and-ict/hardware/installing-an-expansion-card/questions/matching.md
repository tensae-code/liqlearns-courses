# Installing an Expansion Card — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What must the card match?** → **A free expansion slot of its kind**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is removed from the case back?** → **The blanking plate over that slot's opening**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How is the card held?** → **By its edges and its metal bracket**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How is the card seated?** → **Pressed straight down with even pressure until its contacts are fully home**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does a tilted card mean?** → **A card not connected**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What fixes the card against its cables' pull?** → **Its bracket screwed to the case**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What else may need connecting?** → **The card's power lead or internal cable**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is checked first if the machine does not find the card?** → **The seat, not the software**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which chapter teaches this?** → **Laos Grade 8 ICT, Chapter 2 §2.5**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) When should the machine find the new part?** → **At switch-on after a correct install**
