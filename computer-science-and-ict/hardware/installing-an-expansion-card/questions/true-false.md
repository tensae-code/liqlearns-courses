# Installing an Expansion Card — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: A free expansion slot of its kind**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: By its edges and its metal bracket** — as the answer to: What is removed from the case back?
**Answer: False — The blanking plate over that slot's opening**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: By its edges and its metal bracket**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: A card not connected** — as the answer to: How is the card seated?
**Answer: False — Pressed straight down with even pressure until its contacts are fully home**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: A card not connected**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: The card's power lead or internal cable** — as the answer to: What fixes the card against its cables' pull?
**Answer: False — Its bracket screwed to the case**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: The card's power lead or internal cable**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Laos Grade 8 ICT, Chapter 2 §2.5** — as the answer to: What is checked first if the machine does not find the card?
**Answer: False — The seat, not the software**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Laos Grade 8 ICT, Chapter 2 §2.5**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: A free expansion slot of its kind** — as the answer to: When should the machine find the new part?
**Answer: False — At switch-on after a correct install**
