# Installing an Expansion Card — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What must the card match?**
a) A free expansion slot of its kind  b) The blanking plate over that slot's opening  c) By its edges and its metal bracket  d) Pressed straight down with even pressure until its contacts are fully home
**Answer: a**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is removed from the case back?**
a) By its edges and its metal bracket  b) The blanking plate over that slot's opening  c) Pressed straight down with even pressure until its contacts are fully home  d) A card not connected
**Answer: b**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How is the card held?**
a) Pressed straight down with even pressure until its contacts are fully home  b) A card not connected  c) By its edges and its metal bracket  d) Its bracket screwed to the case
**Answer: c**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How is the card seated?**
a) A card not connected  b) Its bracket screwed to the case  c) The card's power lead or internal cable  d) Pressed straight down with even pressure until its contacts are fully home
**Answer: d**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does a tilted card mean?**
a) A card not connected  b) Its bracket screwed to the case  c) The card's power lead or internal cable  d) The seat, not the software
**Answer: a**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What fixes the card against its cables' pull?**
a) The card's power lead or internal cable  b) Its bracket screwed to the case  c) The seat, not the software  d) Laos Grade 8 ICT, Chapter 2 §2.5
**Answer: b**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What else may need connecting?**
a) The seat, not the software  b) Laos Grade 8 ICT, Chapter 2 §2.5  c) The card's power lead or internal cable  d) At switch-on after a correct install
**Answer: c**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is checked first if the machine does not find the card?**
a) Laos Grade 8 ICT, Chapter 2 §2.5  b) At switch-on after a correct install  c) A free expansion slot of its kind  d) The seat, not the software
**Answer: d**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which chapter teaches this?**
a) Laos Grade 8 ICT, Chapter 2 §2.5  b) At switch-on after a correct install  c) A free expansion slot of its kind  d) The blanking plate over that slot's opening
**Answer: a**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) When should the machine find the new part?**
a) A free expansion slot of its kind  b) At switch-on after a correct install  c) The blanking plate over that slot's opening  d) By its edges and its metal bracket
**Answer: b**
