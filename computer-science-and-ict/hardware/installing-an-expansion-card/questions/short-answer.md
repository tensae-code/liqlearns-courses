# Installing an Expansion Card — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What must the card match?**
**Answer: A free expansion slot of its kind**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is removed from the case back?**
**Answer: The blanking plate over that slot's opening**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How is the card held?**
**Answer: By its edges and its metal bracket**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How is the card seated?**
**Answer: Pressed straight down with even pressure until its contacts are fully home**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does a tilted card mean?**
**Answer: A card not connected**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What fixes the card against its cables' pull?**
**Answer: Its bracket screwed to the case**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What else may need connecting?**
**Answer: The card's power lead or internal cable**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is checked first if the machine does not find the card?**
**Answer: The seat, not the software**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which chapter teaches this?**
**Answer: Laos Grade 8 ICT, Chapter 2 §2.5**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) When should the machine find the new part?**
**Answer: At switch-on after a correct install**
