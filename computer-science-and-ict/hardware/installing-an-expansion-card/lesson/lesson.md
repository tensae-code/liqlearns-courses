# Installing an Expansion Card

## Do it first

A computer grows by cards: a better display, sound, a network connection. Laos's Grade 8 chapter installs an expansion card as its own bench act, after the board itself. This lesson adds one.

## Sub lesson 1 — Choosing the slot and opening the case back

The card must match a **free expansion slot of its kind** on the board. With the machine off and opened, the **blanking plate** covering that slot's opening in the case back is removed, and the card is held **by its edges and its metal bracket**, contacts over the slot, kept straight. The card's bracket, not its chips, is what the installer's hand manages — the same edges-only rule as every board in this chapter.

## Sub lesson 2 — Seating and securing the card

The card is pressed **straight down into the slot with even pressure** until its contacts are **fully home** — a card sitting at a tilt is a card not connected. Its **bracket is then screwed to the case**, fixing it against the pull of its own cables. Where the card needs power or an internal cable, that lead is connected now. Laos's closing check is the chapter's habit: card level, bracket screwed, then at switch-on the machine should **find the new part** — and if it does not, the seat, not the software, is checked first.
