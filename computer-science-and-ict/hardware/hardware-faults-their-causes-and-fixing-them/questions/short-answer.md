# Hardware Faults — Their Causes and Fixing Them — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the six fault families by component.**
**Answer: Power, display, storage, input, overheating, connection**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is the largest and cheapest family?**
**Answer: Connection faults — loose and oxidised contacts**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name overheating's three causes.**
**Answer: Dust, fans, dried thermal paste**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What are storage faults' warning signs?**
**Answer: Disk unseen, clicking, vanishing files**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why list causes beside faults?**
**Answer: The same symptom has many parents**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name the fixing order.**
**Answer: Observe/record, test cheap and external first, open the case only then, verify under load**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What do swaps do?**
**Answer: Separate the symptom's parents — the fault moves with the guilty part**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Name two safety rules inside the case.**
**Answer: Power off; static care (and one change at a time)**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) When is a part condemned?**
**Answer: Only on evidence**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is done with a fault beyond the school's tools?**
**Answer: Reported with its evidence, not hidden by guessing**
