**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the six fault families by component.** → **Power, display, storage, input, overheating, connection**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is the largest and cheapest family?** → **Connection faults — loose and oxidised contacts**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name overheating's three causes.** → **Dust, fans, dried thermal paste**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What are storage faults' warning signs?** → **Disk unseen, clicking, vanishing files**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why list causes beside faults?** → **The same symptom has many parents**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name the fixing order.** → **Observe/record, test cheap and external first, open the case only then, verify under load**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What do swaps do?** → **Separate the symptom's parents — the fault moves with the guilty part**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Name two safety rules inside the case.** → **Power off; static care (and one change at a time)**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) When is a part condemned?** → **Only on evidence**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is done with a fault beyond the school's tools?** → **Reported with its evidence, not hidden by guessing**
