# Hands-on — Barcode reader and microphone

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Read a real barcode
Find a barcode on a package at home. Look closely: count thick lines and thin lines in one small stretch. Draw what you see, enlarged, and write why a pattern like this can stand for one exact item.

## Voice in
List three things you could say to a computer as commands, and three spoken things worth recording as data. Say which list a microphone treats as commands and which as data.
