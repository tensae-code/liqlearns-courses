# Barcode reader and microphone — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) All the lines in a barcode are the same thickness.**
**Answer: False** — They are of different thickness — that difference carries the data.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) A barcode reader removes the need to type each item.**
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Spoken commands can enter through a microphone.**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) A microphone is an output device.**
**Answer: False** — It inputs the spoken word.
