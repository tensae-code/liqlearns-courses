# Barcode reader and microphone — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A barcode is —**
a) a pattern of lines of different thickness  b) a printed price in numbers  c) a kind of screen
**Answer: a) a pattern of lines of different thickness**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Barcode data enters —**
a) slowly, with many errors  b) quickly and without error  c) only by typing
**Answer: b) quickly and without error** — That is the barcode's purpose.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) The barcode reader works by —**
a) photographing the shop  b) reading the pattern of lines  c) listening to the assistant
**Answer: b) reading the pattern of lines**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) A microphone inputs —**
a) printed text  b) the spoken word, as data or commands  c) barcodes
**Answer: b) the spoken word, as data or commands**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) Which completes the eight input devices?**
a) Printer  b) Microphone and camera  c) Monitor
**Answer: b) Microphone and camera** — Keyboard, mouse, scanner, touch screen, light pen, barcode reader, microphone, camera.
