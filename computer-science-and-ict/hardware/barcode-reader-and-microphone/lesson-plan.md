# Lesson plan — Barcode reader and microphone

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Hardware · **Lesson:** Barcode reader and microphone
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 2 — Computer Hardware — in the title Hardware on the ICT course ladder (TITLES.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** A barcode is a pattern of lines of different thickness that lets data enter quickly and without error, read by a barcode reader; a microphone inputs the spoken word as data or commands.
**This lesson's own level:** It stands on More input devices: the last two of the book's eight input devices.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Barcode reader** — The barcode — lines of different thickness — and its reader: data entering quickly and without error, the sweep shown.
2. **Microphone** — The spoken word entering as data or commands.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the lessons before this one in Hardware on the ladder. Recommended, never forced — a learner may land on this lesson directly, and it stands alone.
- The book's own rhythm is kept: this lesson opens with a brainstorm — the learner first says what they already know — before any definition is given.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what a barcode is: a pattern of lines of different thickness.
2. Say why barcodes are used: data enters quickly and without error.
3. Say what a microphone inputs: the spoken word, as data or commands; and recite the eight input devices complete.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil; a real barcode from any package at home.
