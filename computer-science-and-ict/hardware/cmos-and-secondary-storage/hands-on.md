# Hands-on — CMOS — and secondary storage keeps data for good

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Backup check
Take one important file or photo of yours. Where is the original? Is there a copy in a DIFFERENT place? If not, make one — that act is this lesson done by hand.

## Settings survivor
On a phone or camera, change a setting, switch fully off, and on again. The setting survived — talk about the small memory that kept it, as CMOS does in a computer.
