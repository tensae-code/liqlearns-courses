# Hands-on — Disk Cleanup — deleting the files a disk no longer needs

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Accumulation log
For one week, note (without deleting anything) the kinds of temporary and internet files a school computer collects. Present the list: this is what Disk Cleanup removes.

## Schedule it
Write a weekly cleanup plan for the lab: day, time, manual or scheduled — and who checks it was done.
