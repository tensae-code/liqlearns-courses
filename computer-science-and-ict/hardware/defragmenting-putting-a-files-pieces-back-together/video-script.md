# Video script — Defragmenting — putting a file's pieces back together

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. Short script; the placement names no full video need for this lesson.

---

Delete a file. Change a file.
Little by little, one file's pieces
end up scattered all over the disk.
That is fragmentation.
Reading scattered pieces takes longer.
The machine slows.

The defragger gathers the pieces —
into adjoining regions, side by side.
Reading is fast again.

Steps: Administrative Tools.
Defragment and Optimize Drives.
Pick the drive. Optimize.
Zero percent fragmented? Nothing to do.
