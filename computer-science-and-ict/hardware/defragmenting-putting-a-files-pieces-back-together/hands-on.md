# Hands-on — Defragmenting — putting a file's pieces back together

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Pages in rooms
Write a 12-page mini-book, one sentence per card. A partner scatters the cards across the room; time your reading. Then gather them in order — adjoining — and time it again. Write both times: that difference is fragmentation.

## Run the route (or rehearse it)
On a school computer, follow the steps to the drive list and READ the fragmentation percentage aloud. Run Optimize only if your teacher says so — and only if the number is not 0%.
