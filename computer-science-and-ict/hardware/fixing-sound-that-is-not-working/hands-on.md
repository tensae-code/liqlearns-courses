# Hands-on — Fixing sound that is not working

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Chain card
Make a four-link chain card for the lab: volume → player → speakers/pairing → driver. Under each link write the exact thing checked.

## Silent-video drill
On a school computer, deliberately set one link wrong (volume down, or player muted). A partner walks the chain aloud and finds it without touching anything out of order.
