# Video script — Fixing sound that is not working

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

No sound? Walk the chain.

One — volume up, sound on.
Two — the player's own controls, up high.
Three — speakers in, speakers on, right port.
Headphones? Paired and connected.

Four — the driver. Device Manager.
Right-click the sound driver. Update Driver.

In order. The chain finds it.
