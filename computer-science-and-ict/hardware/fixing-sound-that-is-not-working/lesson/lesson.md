# Fixing sound that is not working

## Do it first

A video plays on the school computer and there is no sound at all. Write your checks in the order you would try them — cheapest and simplest first. Then compare with the lesson's chain.

## Sub lesson 1 — Checks 1–2 — volume and the player's own controls

In order:

1. **Check the volume level** — that **sound is on and the volume is up** — and the **audio button**.
2. Check the **player's own separate audio/video controls** — they are **on and at the highest setting**. A player can be silent while the computer is loud.

[Picture 31308 — a checklist chain — volume, player, speakers, driver — with a speaker at the end]

## Sub lesson 2 — Checks 3–4 — speakers, pairing and the driver

3. Check **external speakers**: **plugged in, switched on**, and connected to the **right audio or USB port**; and that **wireless headphones are successfully paired and connected**.
4. Check the **sound driver** — its **version shows in Device Manager** — and **update it manually**: **right-click the audio driver and choose Update Driver**, or obtain the **latest driver for the current operating system from a reputable source**.

## Say it to your companion

Walk your companion down the four-check chain in order. They stop you if the driver comes before the volume.

## Do It Yourself

1. Write the four sound checks in order, from memory.
2. The system volume is up but one video is silent. Write which check you have missed.
3. Write the two ways to update the sound driver.
