# Fixing sound that is not working — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first sound check is —
a) update the driver  b) the volume level — sound on, volume up, the audio button  c) replace the speakers  d) restart the computer
**Answer: b) the volume level — sound on, volume up, the audio button**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The player's own controls must be —
a) ignored  b) on and at the highest setting  c) deleted  d) muted
**Answer: b) on and at the highest setting**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** External speakers must be —
a) plugged in, switched on, in the right audio or USB port  b) unplugged for testing  c) placed far away  d) turned off
**Answer: a) plugged in, switched on, in the right audio or USB port**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Wireless headphones must be —
a) charged only  b) successfully paired and connected  c) wired instead  d) shared
**Answer: b) successfully paired and connected**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The sound driver's version shows in —
a) Disk Cleanup  b) Device Manager  c) the Recycle Bin  d) the Start menu picture
**Answer: b) Device Manager**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** To update the driver manually you —
a) right-click the audio driver and choose Update Driver  b) delete the driver  c) rename the speakers  d) close Device Manager
**Answer: a) right-click the audio driver and choose Update Driver**
