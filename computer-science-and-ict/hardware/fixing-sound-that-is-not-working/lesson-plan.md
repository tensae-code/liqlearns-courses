# Lesson plan — Fixing sound that is not working

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Hardware · **Lesson:** Fixing sound that is not working
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 5 — Maintenance and Troubleshooting (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Silent sound is fixed by walking one chain in order: volume, the player's own controls, speakers and connections, and only then the sound driver.
**This lesson's own level:** It stands on Troubleshooting: the systematic chain applied to one sense — sound.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or in steps — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
1. **Checks 1–2 — volume and the player's own controls** — the system volume and audio button; then the player's own separate controls at their highest.
2. **Checks 3–4 — speakers, pairing and the driver** — speakers plugged in, on, in the right port; wireless pairing; the driver in Device Manager and its update.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Troubleshooting — find the fault, find its cause, in this same title. Recommended, never forced — a learner may land on this lesson directly, and it stands alone.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Check volume and the audio button first.
2. Check the player's own controls second.
3. Check speakers, ports and pairing third.
4. Check and update the sound driver last, through Device Manager.

## What you need
A computer with speakers or headphones if available — paper works too. Paper and a pencil.

## If the learner is stuck
- Goes straight to the driver → the chain starts with volume: most silence is a turned-down control, not a broken driver.
- Speakers 'connected' but silent → check they are switched ON and in the RIGHT port, not just plugged somewhere.
