# Logic Elements — the Building Blocks — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a logic element?**
**Answer: A small circuit with inputs and one output**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) When does an AND element give 1?**
**Answer: Only when all inputs are 1**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) When does an OR element give 1?**
**Answer: When at least one input is 1**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does a NOT element do?**
**Answer: Flips its single input**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) In the book's order, what comes just before elements?**
**Answer: Logical operations**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) How do elements join?**
**Answer: The output of one feeds the input of another**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Name two jobs a chain of elements can do.**
**Answer: Add numbers and compare them (or remember a bit)**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is the whole processor, in this picture?**
**Answer: Chains of millions of logic elements**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How big is one element's job?**
**Answer: One tiny logical job**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Why are they called building blocks?**
**Answer: Because everything bigger is built from them**
