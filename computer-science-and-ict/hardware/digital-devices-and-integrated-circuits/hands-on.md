# Hands-on — Digital Devices and Integrated Circuits

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Device census** — List ten digital devices in your home. For each, name the gates-family job it must contain — deciding, remembering or counting.

## Task two
**Board against chip** — Find a picture of an old circuit board and a modern chip doing similar work. Write three differences integration made.
