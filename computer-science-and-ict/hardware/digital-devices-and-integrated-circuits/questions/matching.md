# Digital Devices and Integrated Circuits — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What makes a device digital?** → **It works with discrete values — 0s and 1s**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name three digital devices.** → **Computers, phones and digital watches (also cameras, calculators, controllers)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What logic family lives inside them?** → **Gates deciding, flip-flops remembering, counters counting**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does naming a device digital name?** → **Its arithmetic — binary**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Name one place controllers hide.** → **Inside washing machines and cars**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is an integrated circuit?** → **A whole circuit of many gates built into a single chip**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How are ICs made?** → **Printed photographically, layer by layer, in semiconductor**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Name two gifts of integration.** → **Smaller and cheaper devices (also less power, fewer failures, identical millions)**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What are a chip's pins?** → **Its doors — power and signals in and out**
