# Digital Devices and Integrated Circuits — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What makes a device digital?**
**Answer: It works with discrete values — 0s and 1s**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name three digital devices.**
**Answer: Computers, phones and digital watches (also cameras, calculators, controllers)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What logic family lives inside them?**
**Answer: Gates deciding, flip-flops remembering, counters counting**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does naming a device digital name?**
**Answer: Its arithmetic — binary**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Name one place controllers hide.**
**Answer: Inside washing machines and cars**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is an integrated circuit?**
**Answer: A whole circuit of many gates built into a single chip**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How are ICs made?**
**Answer: Printed photographically, layer by layer, in semiconductor**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Name two gifts of integration.**
**Answer: Smaller and cheaper devices (also less power, fewer failures, identical millions)**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What are a chip's pins?**
**Answer: Its doors — power and signals in and out**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Where has the chapter's road been leading?**
**Answer: To gates standing in their millions inside chips**
