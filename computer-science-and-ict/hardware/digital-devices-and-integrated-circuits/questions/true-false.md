# Digital Devices and Integrated Circuits — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: It works with discrete values — 0s and 1s**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: It works with discrete values — 0s and 1s** — as the answer to: Name three digital devices.
**Answer: False — Computers, phones and digital watches (also cameras, calculators, controllers)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Gates deciding, flip-flops remembering, counters counting**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Gates deciding, flip-flops remembering, counters counting** — as the answer to: What does naming a device digital name?
**Answer: False — Its arithmetic — binary**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Inside washing machines and cars**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Inside washing machines and cars** — as the answer to: What is an integrated circuit?
**Answer: False — A whole circuit of many gates built into a single chip**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Printed photographically, layer by layer, in semiconductor**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Printed photographically, layer by layer, in semiconductor** — as the answer to: Name two gifts of integration.
**Answer: False — Smaller and cheaper devices (also less power, fewer failures, identical millions)**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Its doors — power and signals in and out**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Its doors — power and signals in and out** — as the answer to: Where has the chapter's road been leading?
**Answer: False — To gates standing in their millions inside chips**
