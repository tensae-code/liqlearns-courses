# Audio Devices — Sound Card, Speaker, Headphone — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: As digital audio — numbers describing the wave**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: As an expansion card or built into the motherboard** — as the answer to: What does the sound card convert?
**Answer: False — Digital numbers into the electrical signal speakers use — and back again for a microphone**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: As an expansion card or built into the motherboard**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Carry the sound to one listener's ears alone** — as the answer to: What do speakers do?
**Answer: False — Turn the signal into sound in the air, for everyone in the room**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Carry the sound to one listener's ears alone**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: A sound fault can sit at any link of the chain** — as the answer to: Name a place for speakers and a place for headphones.
**Answer: False — Speakers for the classroom; headphones for the language lab**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: A sound fault can sit at any link of the chain**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Laos Grade 8 ICT, Chapter 1 §1.8** — as the answer to: What are the chain's three links?
**Answer: False — The numbers, the conversion (sound card), and the deliverer**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Laos Grade 8 ICT, Chapter 1 §1.8**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: As digital audio — numbers describing the wave** — as the answer to: Can a machine without conversion play its stored song aloud?
**Answer: False — No — stored numbers must be converted to a signal first**
