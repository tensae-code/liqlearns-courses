# Audio Devices — Sound Card, Speaker, Headphone — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) How does sound exist inside the computer?**
**Answer: As digital audio — numbers describing the wave**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does the sound card convert?**
**Answer: Digital numbers into the electrical signal speakers use — and back again for a microphone**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Where can a sound card live?**
**Answer: As an expansion card or built into the motherboard**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What do speakers do?**
**Answer: Turn the signal into sound in the air, for everyone in the room**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) What do headphones do differently?**
**Answer: Carry the sound to one listener's ears alone**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name a place for speakers and a place for headphones.**
**Answer: Speakers for the classroom; headphones for the language lab**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Why are the three taught as one family?**
**Answer: A sound fault can sit at any link of the chain**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What are the chain's three links?**
**Answer: The numbers, the conversion (sound card), and the deliverer**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which chapter teaches this?**
**Answer: Laos Grade 8 ICT, Chapter 1 §1.8**

**10. (Sub lesson 1) Can a machine without conversion play its stored song aloud?**
**Answer: No — stored numbers must be converted to a signal first**
