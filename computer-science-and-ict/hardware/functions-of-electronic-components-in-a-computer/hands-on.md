# Hands-on — Functions of Electronic Components in a Computer

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Name the job, name the part
Your companion names a job — limit, store, one-way, switch, light. You name the component that does it, and say why.

## Board hunt
Look at a picture of a computer board (or a real old board, power off). Find and point to a resistor, a capacitor and a diode, and say each one's job.
