# Functions of Electronic Components in a Computer — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does a resistor do?**
**Answer: Limits the flow of current**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does a capacitor do?**
**Answer: Stores electrical charge and releases it**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does a diode do?**
**Answer: Lets current flow in one direction only**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does a transistor do?**
**Answer: Works as a switch or an amplifier**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does an LED do?**
**Answer: Gives out light when current passes through it**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Which component resists current to protect a circuit?**
**Answer: The resistor**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Which component stores charge for a steady supply?**
**Answer: The capacitor**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Which component makes sure current goes one way?**
**Answer: The diode**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which component switches signals on and off in processors?**
**Answer: The transistor**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the first step in choosing a component?**
**Answer: Name the job it must do**
