# Functions of Electronic Components in a Computer — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does a resistor do?" is: Limits the flow of current**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does a capacitor do?" is: Works as a switch or an amplifier**
**Answer: False** — Stores electrical charge and releases it

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does a diode do?" is: Lets current flow in one direction only**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does a transistor do?" is: The resistor**
**Answer: False** — Works as a switch or an amplifier

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does an LED do?" is: Gives out light when current passes through it**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Which component resists current to protect a circuit?" is: The diode**
**Answer: False** — The resistor

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Which component stores charge for a steady supply?" is: The capacitor**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Which component makes sure current goes one way?" is: Name the job it must do**
**Answer: False** — The diode

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Which component switches signals on and off in processors?" is: The transistor**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is the first step in choosing a component?" is: Stores electrical charge and releases it**
**Answer: False** — Name the job it must do
