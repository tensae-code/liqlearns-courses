# Functions of Electronic Components in a Computer — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does a resistor do?**
a) Limits the flow of current  b) Stores electrical charge and releases it  c) Lets current flow in one direction only  d) Works as a switch or an amplifier
**Answer: a) Limits the flow of current**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does a capacitor do?**
a) Lets current flow in one direction only  b) Stores electrical charge and releases it  c) Works as a switch or an amplifier  d) Gives out light when current passes through it
**Answer: b) Stores electrical charge and releases it**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does a diode do?**
a) Works as a switch or an amplifier  b) Gives out light when current passes through it  c) Lets current flow in one direction only  d) The resistor
**Answer: c) Lets current flow in one direction only**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does a transistor do?**
a) Gives out light when current passes through it  b) The resistor  c) The capacitor  d) Works as a switch or an amplifier
**Answer: d) Works as a switch or an amplifier**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does an LED do?**
a) Gives out light when current passes through it  b) The resistor  c) The capacitor  d) The diode
**Answer: a) Gives out light when current passes through it**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Which component resists current to protect a circuit?**
a) The capacitor  b) The resistor  c) The diode  d) The transistor
**Answer: b) The resistor**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Which component stores charge for a steady supply?**
a) The diode  b) The transistor  c) The capacitor  d) Name the job it must do
**Answer: c) The capacitor**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Which component makes sure current goes one way?**
a) The transistor  b) Name the job it must do  c) Limits the flow of current  d) The diode
**Answer: d) The diode**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which component switches signals on and off in processors?**
a) The transistor  b) Name the job it must do  c) Limits the flow of current  d) Stores electrical charge and releases it
**Answer: a) The transistor**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the first step in choosing a component?**
a) Limits the flow of current  b) Name the job it must do  c) Stores electrical charge and releases it  d) Lets current flow in one direction only
**Answer: b) Name the job it must do**
