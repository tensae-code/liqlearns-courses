**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Each electronic component has one main job. A **resistor** limits the flow of current. A **capacitor** stores electrical charge and releases it. A **diode** lets current flow in one direction only. A **transistor** works as a switch or an amplifier. A **light-emitting diode (LED)** gives out light when current passes through it.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **What the main components do** — Each electronic component has one main job. A **resistor** limits the flow of current. A **capacitor** stores electrical charge and releases it. A **diode** lets current flow in one direction only. A **transistor** works as a switch or an amplifier. A **light-emitting diode (LED)** gives out light when current passes through it.
2. **Matching a component to its job in a circuit** — To choose a component, first **name the job**: protect a part from too much current — the resistor; hold charge for a steady supply — the capacitor; keep current moving one way — the diode; switch a signal on and off — the transistor, the part processors are built from. The component is chosen by its function, never by its look.
