# Uninstalling and disabling apps on a phone — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The removal routes are common across Android, Samsung, Apple and other phones.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Every app on a phone can be uninstalled.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Tapping the app's name in manage apps and devices leads to Uninstall.
**Answer: True**
