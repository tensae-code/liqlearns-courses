# Uninstalling and disabling apps on a phone — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give both routes to uninstall an app.
**Answer: Through Settings → manage apps and devices: tap the app's name, then Uninstall. Or swipe up to the apps, long-press the app icon for more options, and select Uninstall.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does one app on a new phone refuse to uninstall? (teacher guide — evidence)
**Answer: It is a system application critical to the operation of the phone, or it was preinstalled on the device; such apps can be disabled instead.**
