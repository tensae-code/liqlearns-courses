# Uninstalling and disabling apps on a phone — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Settings route runs through —
a) the camera settings  b) manage apps and devices  c) the wallpaper menu  d) airplane mode
**Answer: b) manage apps and devices**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** On the home screen route, you —
a) shake the phone  b) swipe up to the apps and long-press the app icon  c) hold the power button  d) remove the battery
**Answer: b) swipe up to the apps and long-press the app icon**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** After long-pressing, you select —
a) Rename  b) Share  c) Uninstall  d) Update
**Answer: c) Uninstall**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** System apps that came with the phone can be —
a) uninstalled like any app  b) disabled instead of uninstalled  c) sold to another phone  d) hidden in the camera
**Answer: b) disabled instead of uninstalled**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** An app may refuse to uninstall because it is — (teacher guide — evidence)
a) too popular  b) a system application critical to the phone's operation, or preinstalled  c) too new  d) in another language
**Answer: b) a system application critical to the phone's operation, or preinstalled**
