# Hands-on — Uninstalling and disabling apps on a phone

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Route rehearsal
On a paper phone screen, draw an app grid. Mark one icon with a held finger and draw the options that open, with Uninstall on top. Label the second route underneath: Settings → manage apps and devices.

## Sort the apps
List six apps on a real or imagined phone. Mark each: uninstall, disable, or keep — and write the reason for one disable.
