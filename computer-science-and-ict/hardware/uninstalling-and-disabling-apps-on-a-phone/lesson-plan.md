# Lesson plan — Uninstalling and disabling apps on a phone

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Hardware · **Lesson:** Uninstalling and disabling apps on a phone
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 5 — Maintenance and Troubleshooting (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Apps you added can be uninstalled through Settings or by long-pressing the icon; apps the phone itself needs can only be disabled — and the guide names why.
**This lesson's own level:** It stands on Uninstalling software on a PC: the same proper-removal idea, on a phone.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or in steps — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
1. **Two routes to remove an app** — the settings route (manage apps and devices, tap the name, Uninstall) and the long-press route, across phone families.
2. **System apps — disable instead (teacher guide — evidence)** — disabling system apps; the guide's two reasons an app refuses to uninstall (teacher evidence).

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Uninstalling software on a PC, in this same title. Recommended, never forced — a learner may land on this lesson directly, and it stands alone.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Uninstall an app through Settings — manage apps and devices.
2. Uninstall an app by long-pressing its icon and choosing Uninstall.
3. Disable a system app instead, and say why it cannot be uninstalled.

## What you need
A phone if one is available to look at — paper works too. Paper and a pencil.

## If the learner is stuck
- No Uninstall option appears → it is likely a system app: disable it instead of forcing removal.
- The screens look different from the lesson → version changes move icons and layouts; follow the same two routes by their names.
