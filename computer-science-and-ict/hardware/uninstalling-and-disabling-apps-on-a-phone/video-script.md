# Video script — Uninstalling and disabling apps on a phone

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Full phone? Remove what you do not use.

Open Settings. Manage apps and devices.
Tap the app's name. Tap Uninstall.

Or hold the icon down — the options open.
Tap Uninstall.

One app will not go? It came with the phone.
That one you disable. The phone needs it to work.
