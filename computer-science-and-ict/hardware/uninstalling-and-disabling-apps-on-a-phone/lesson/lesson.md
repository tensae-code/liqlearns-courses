# Uninstalling and disabling apps on a phone

## Do it first

Your phone is full. One app you never open takes a large space — and one app that came with the phone refuses to leave. Write what you would try for each — then work the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Two routes to remove an app

Apps can be **uninstalled or deleted**. The common route across **Android, Windows, Samsung, Apple, Blackberry and other phones** runs two ways:

- Through **Settings → manage apps and devices**: tap the **app's name**, then **Uninstall**.
- Or **swipe up to the apps**, **long-press the app icon** for more options, and **select Uninstall**.

## Sub lesson 2 — System apps — disable instead (teacher guide — evidence)

**System apps that came with the phone can be disabled instead** of uninstalled.

> **Teacher evidence (teacher guide — evidence, printed pp. 100–101):** some mobile applications **cannot be uninstalled** for two named reasons — they are **system applications critical to the operation of the phone**, or they were **preinstalled on the device**. Operating-system version changes can also move icons and layouts, so your screens may differ from the book's.
