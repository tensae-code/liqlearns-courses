# Single-Board Controllers — Microcontrollers — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a single-board controller?**
**Answer: A microcontroller mounted on a printed circuit board, exposing its pins**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does the microcontroller chip hold?**
**Answer: Processor, memory and input/output on one chip**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How many programs does a controller hold?**
**Answer: One — written for the device it lives in**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) When does its program run?**
**Answer: From the moment power arrives**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How does it differ from a single-board computer?**
**Answer: The computer runs many programs under an operating system; the controller is one program in a board**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is the controller's life-loop?**
**Answer: Read inputs, decide by its program, drive outputs — forever**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does the board around the chip add?**
**Answer: Power parts, a clock, connection pins and often a USB door for loading the program**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Name two devices a controller lives in.**
**Answer: A washing machine and a smart trash bin (also: toys)**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which platform is the chapter's working example?**
**Answer: Arduino**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Where does Indonesia teach this?**
**Answer: Grade 12, Bab 2 B — single-board controllers**
