# Hands-on — Single-Board Controllers — Microcontrollers

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Computer or controller?** — List six devices around your home or school. Mark each C (computer) or K (controller) and write the one program each controller is probably running.

## Task two
**Loop it** — Take the smart trash bin. Write its loop in three lines — what it reads, what it decides, what it drives — and name the pins' jobs.
