# Lesson plan — Joystick — direction and amount of push

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Hardware · **Lesson:** Joystick — direction and amount of push
**Place in the curriculum:** Title 2 — Hardware of the Computer Science and ICT ladder (TITLES.md); Ethiopia Grade 8, Unit 2 — Computer Hardware.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a joystick controls games and cursor speed by push: the direction it is pushed and how far it deflects decide the change in position and the change in speed.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the other input devices: this one reports not a press but a lean — direction and amount together.

- Thinks the joystick only reports direction → it reports direction AND how far it deflects; amount decides the speed change.
