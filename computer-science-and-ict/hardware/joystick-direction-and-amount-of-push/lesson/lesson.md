# Joystick — direction and amount of push

## Do it first

## What a joystick is for

A joystick is mainly used for playing video games and controlling the speed of the cursor. It is popular in speed games like racing and flying, where steering must be smooth and continuous, not click by click.

[Picture 10940 — joystick]

## Direction and deflection

Two things about the push matter: the direction the stick is pushed, and how far it deflects — how far from upright it leans. Together they decide the change in position and the change in speed. Watch the GIF: the further the stick tilts, the faster the on-screen racer goes; ease it back, and the racer eases off.
