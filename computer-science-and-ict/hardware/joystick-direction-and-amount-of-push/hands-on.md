# Hands-on — Joystick — direction and amount of push

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Lean control
If a joystick or game controller is available, hold a steady small lean, then a big lean, in a racing or flying game. Feel the speed answer the amount, not only the direction.

## Human joystick
One partner is the stick: they lean a broom handle in the direction you call, a little or a lot. You call the racer's change — position and speed — from the lean alone.
