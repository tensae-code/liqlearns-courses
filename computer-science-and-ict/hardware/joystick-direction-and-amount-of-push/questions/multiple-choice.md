# Joystick — direction and amount of push — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** A joystick is mainly used for —
a) Typing documents  b) Playing video games and controlling the speed of the cursor  c) Printing  d) Scanning
**Answer: b) Playing video games and controlling the speed of the cursor** — It is mainly used for playing video games and controlling cursor speed.

**2.** The change in position and speed is decided by —
a) The colour of the stick  b) The direction of push and how far it deflects  c) The size of the screen  d) The game's name
**Answer: b) The direction of push and how far it deflects** — Direction pushed and amount of deflection decide position and speed changes.

**3.** Pushing the stick further in the same direction makes the racer —
a) Stop  b) Change speed more — go faster  c) Turn off  d) Reverse always
**Answer: b) Change speed more — go faster** — Greater deflection means a greater change in speed.

**4.** Joysticks are popular in —
a) Word processing  b) Speed games like racing and flying  c) Accounting  d) Email
**Answer: b) Speed games like racing and flying** — Popular in speed games like racing and flying.
