**Joystick.**

First, brainstorm: what do you already know about this? Say it out loud.

Now the one thing: a joystick controls games and cursor speed by push: the direction it is pushed and how far it deflects decide the change in position and the change in speed.

**What a joystick is for.** A joystick is mainly used for playing video games and controlling the speed of the cursor.
**Direction and deflection.** Two things about the push matter: the direction the stick is pushed, and how far it deflects — how far from upright it leans.

Now you: Draw a joystick tilted forward with an on-screen racer speeding up.
