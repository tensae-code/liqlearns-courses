# Hands-on — How Real Numbers Are Represented Inside the Computer

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Field work** — In an 8-bit toy format (1 sign, 3 exponent, 4 mantissa bits), store 5.5 and −0.75 step by step, naming each field.

## Task two
**Edge cases** — Explain, in the model's terms, why adding a tiny number to a huge one can change nothing, and why 0.1 + 0.2 ≠ 0.3 on a machine.
