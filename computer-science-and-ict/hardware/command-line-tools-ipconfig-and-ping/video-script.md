# Video script — Command-line tools — ipconfig and ping

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Words can test wires. Open the prompt.

Search the Start menu — or type: cmd.
Type ipconfig. Your address appears:
IP address. Subnet mask. Gateway.

Now ping the gateway.
The packet travels out — and back.
It returned? The road is open.
