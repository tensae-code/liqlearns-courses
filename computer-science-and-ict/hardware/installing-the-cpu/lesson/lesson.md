# Installing the CPU

## Do it first

The CPU is the most delicate and the most valuable part on the bench. Laos's Grade 8 chapter gives its installation the first full procedure of the practical year. This lesson follows it step by step.

## Sub lesson 1 — Alignment first — the marked corner

Installing begins before touching: the machine is **off, unplugged and opened**, the installer **static-safe**. The socket's lever is raised, and the processor is held **by its edges** and turned until its **marked corner (the alignment mark / missing-pin corner) matches the socket's marked corner** — the CPU fits only one way round, and the mark, not force, is how that way is found. Lowered squarely into the socket, a rightly aligned CPU **drops in under its own weight**; any need to push is the signal to stop and check the alignment again.

## Sub lesson 2 — Seating, locking and cooling

With the processor seated, the **socket lever is closed** to lock it in place. Then its cooling: **heat-sink compound (thermal paste)** is spread thin where sink meets chip if the book's build requires it, the **heat sink and fan are fitted and clipped down evenly**, and the **fan's power lead is connected** to the board. An uncooled or badly cooled CPU overheats within moments of work, so the install is not finished when the chip sits — it is finished when the chip sits, locked and cooled, with its fan powered.
