# Installing the CPU — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What must the machine be before work starts?** → **Off, unplugged and opened, with the installer static-safe**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How is the CPU held?** → **By its edges**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How is the right way round found?** → **The marked corner of the CPU matches the socket's marked corner**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How should a rightly aligned CPU enter the socket?** → **It drops in under its own weight — pushing means stop and re-check**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What locks the CPU in place?** → **Closing the socket lever**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What goes between chip and heat sink?** → **A thin spread of thermal paste, where the build requires it**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How is the heat sink fitted?** → **Clipped down evenly, with the fan's power lead connected**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why is cooling part of the install, not an extra?** → **An uncooled CPU overheats within moments**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which chapter teaches this?** → **Laos Grade 8 ICT, Chapter 2 §2.2**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) When is the install finished?** → **When the chip sits locked and cooled, its fan powered**
