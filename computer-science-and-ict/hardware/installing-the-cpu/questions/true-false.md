# Installing the CPU — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Off, unplugged and opened, with the installer static-safe**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: The marked corner of the CPU matches the socket's marked corner** — as the answer to: How is the CPU held?
**Answer: False — By its edges**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: The marked corner of the CPU matches the socket's marked corner**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Closing the socket lever** — as the answer to: How should a rightly aligned CPU enter the socket?
**Answer: False — It drops in under its own weight — pushing means stop and re-check**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Closing the socket lever**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Clipped down evenly, with the fan's power lead connected** — as the answer to: What goes between chip and heat sink?
**Answer: False — A thin spread of thermal paste, where the build requires it**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Clipped down evenly, with the fan's power lead connected**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Laos Grade 8 ICT, Chapter 2 §2.2** — as the answer to: Why is cooling part of the install, not an extra?
**Answer: False — An uncooled CPU overheats within moments**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Laos Grade 8 ICT, Chapter 2 §2.2**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Off, unplugged and opened, with the installer static-safe** — as the answer to: When is the install finished?
**Answer: False — When the chip sits locked and cooled, its fan powered**
