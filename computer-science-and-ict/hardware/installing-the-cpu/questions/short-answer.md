# Installing the CPU — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What must the machine be before work starts?**
**Answer: Off, unplugged and opened, with the installer static-safe**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How is the CPU held?**
**Answer: By its edges**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How is the right way round found?**
**Answer: The marked corner of the CPU matches the socket's marked corner**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How should a rightly aligned CPU enter the socket?**
**Answer: It drops in under its own weight — pushing means stop and re-check**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What locks the CPU in place?**
**Answer: Closing the socket lever**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What goes between chip and heat sink?**
**Answer: A thin spread of thermal paste, where the build requires it**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How is the heat sink fitted?**
**Answer: Clipped down evenly, with the fan's power lead connected**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why is cooling part of the install, not an extra?**
**Answer: An uncooled CPU overheats within moments**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which chapter teaches this?**
**Answer: Laos Grade 8 ICT, Chapter 2 §2.2**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) When is the install finished?**
**Answer: When the chip sits locked and cooled, its fan powered**
