# Installing the CPU — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What must the machine be before work starts?**
a) Off, unplugged and opened, with the installer static-safe  b) By its edges  c) The marked corner of the CPU matches the socket's marked corner  d) It drops in under its own weight — pushing means stop and re-check
**Answer: a**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How is the CPU held?**
a) The marked corner of the CPU matches the socket's marked corner  b) By its edges  c) It drops in under its own weight — pushing means stop and re-check  d) Closing the socket lever
**Answer: b**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How is the right way round found?**
a) It drops in under its own weight — pushing means stop and re-check  b) Closing the socket lever  c) The marked corner of the CPU matches the socket's marked corner  d) A thin spread of thermal paste, where the build requires it
**Answer: c**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How should a rightly aligned CPU enter the socket?**
a) Closing the socket lever  b) A thin spread of thermal paste, where the build requires it  c) Clipped down evenly, with the fan's power lead connected  d) It drops in under its own weight — pushing means stop and re-check
**Answer: d**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What locks the CPU in place?**
a) Closing the socket lever  b) A thin spread of thermal paste, where the build requires it  c) Clipped down evenly, with the fan's power lead connected  d) An uncooled CPU overheats within moments
**Answer: a**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What goes between chip and heat sink?**
a) Clipped down evenly, with the fan's power lead connected  b) A thin spread of thermal paste, where the build requires it  c) An uncooled CPU overheats within moments  d) Laos Grade 8 ICT, Chapter 2 §2.2
**Answer: b**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How is the heat sink fitted?**
a) An uncooled CPU overheats within moments  b) Laos Grade 8 ICT, Chapter 2 §2.2  c) Clipped down evenly, with the fan's power lead connected  d) When the chip sits locked and cooled, its fan powered
**Answer: c**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why is cooling part of the install, not an extra?**
a) Laos Grade 8 ICT, Chapter 2 §2.2  b) When the chip sits locked and cooled, its fan powered  c) Off, unplugged and opened, with the installer static-safe  d) An uncooled CPU overheats within moments
**Answer: d**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which chapter teaches this?**
a) Laos Grade 8 ICT, Chapter 2 §2.2  b) When the chip sits locked and cooled, its fan powered  c) Off, unplugged and opened, with the installer static-safe  d) By its edges
**Answer: a**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) When is the install finished?**
a) Off, unplugged and opened, with the installer static-safe  b) When the chip sits locked and cooled, its fan powered  c) By its edges  d) The marked corner of the CPU matches the socket's marked corner
