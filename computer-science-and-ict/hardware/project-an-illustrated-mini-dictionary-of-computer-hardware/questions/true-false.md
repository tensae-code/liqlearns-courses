# Project — An Illustrated Mini-Dictionary of Computer Hardware — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What is this project's dictionary about?" is: The computer's hardware — the parts you can touch**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Name three peripherals that belong in it.?" is: A picture of the part, its name, and one line on its job**
**Answer: False** — The mouse, the keyboard and the printer

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What rule decides whether a word goes in?" is: It must be a thing you can touch and point to**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "How does the class work on the project?" is: What the part does, in the learner's own words**
**Answer: False** — In pairs or groups, agreeing the word list first

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Why agree the word list before starting?" is: So nothing important is missing and nothing is doubled**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does each dictionary entry join together?" is: Whenever a hardware word is forgotten**
**Answer: False** — A picture of the part, its name, and one line on its job

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is the picture's job in the entry?" is: It teaches the word to a reader who does not know it**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What does the line under the picture say?" is: The mouse, the keyboard and the printer**
**Answer: False** — What the part does, in the learner's own words

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What is done with the finished pages?" is: They are gathered in order into the mini-dictionary**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "When is the finished dictionary used?" is: In pairs or groups, agreeing the word list first**
**Answer: False** — Whenever a hardware word is forgotten
