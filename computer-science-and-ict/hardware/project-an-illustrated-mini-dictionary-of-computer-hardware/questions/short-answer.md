# Project — An Illustrated Mini-Dictionary of Computer Hardware — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is this project's dictionary about?**
**Answer: The computer's hardware — the parts you can touch**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name three peripherals that belong in it.**
**Answer: The mouse, the keyboard and the printer**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What rule decides whether a word goes in?**
**Answer: It must be a thing you can touch and point to**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How does the class work on the project?**
**Answer: In pairs or groups, agreeing the word list first**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why agree the word list before starting?**
**Answer: So nothing important is missing and nothing is doubled**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does each dictionary entry join together?**
**Answer: A picture of the part, its name, and one line on its job**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is the picture's job in the entry?**
**Answer: It teaches the word to a reader who does not know it**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does the line under the picture say?**
**Answer: What the part does, in the learner's own words**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is done with the finished pages?**
**Answer: They are gathered in order into the mini-dictionary**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) When is the finished dictionary used?**
**Answer: Whenever a hardware word is forgotten**
