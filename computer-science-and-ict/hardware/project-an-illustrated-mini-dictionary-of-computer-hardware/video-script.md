# Video script — Project — An Illustrated Mini-Dictionary of Computer Hardware

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The project's words are the hardware of the year: the computer and its peripherals — the mouse, the keyboard, the screen, the speakers and headphones, the camera, the microphone and the printer. Each word chosen must be a thing you can touch and point to on a real machine: the dictionary is of matériel — hardware — not of programs. Working in pairs or groups, as the guide's units do, the class agrees the word list first, so no important piece is missing and no word appears twice.
Each entry joins a drawing or picture of the part to its name and one line saying what it does: the mouse — it moves the pointer and clicks; the printer — it puts documents and images on paper. The illustration is not decoration: a reader who does not know the word learns it from the picture, and the line under it says the part's job in the learner's own words. Finished pages are gathered in order into the class's mini-dictionary, ready to be used whenever a hardware word is forgotten.
