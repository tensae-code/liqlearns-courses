# Hands-on — Project — An Illustrated Mini-Dictionary of Computer Hardware

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Draw your entries
Choose two hardware words. For each, draw the part as carefully as you can and write its name and one line saying what it does. Swap with your partner: can they name your parts from the pictures alone?

## Use the dictionary
Your teacher names a job — “putting a document on paper”. Find the page in the class dictionary that answers it, and read the entry aloud.
