# Hands-on — Keyboard Shortcut Keys

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Chord hunt** — Open a program's menus and list ten commands with their printed shortcuts. Mark the five that belong in your working set.

## Task two
**No-mouse page** — Produce one short formatted page using only the keyboard — shortcuts and navigation keys — and note every place you were forced back to the mouse.
