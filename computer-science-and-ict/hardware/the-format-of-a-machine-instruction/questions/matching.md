Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What do address fields name?** → **Where the operands are — registers or memory addresses**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) In a 16-bit instruction with a 4-bit opcode, how many operations and addresses fit?** → **16 operations; a 12-bit address reaches 4096 cells**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What is the format, in the lesson's phrase?** → **A budget of bits — spent between variety of operations and reach of addresses**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What classes has the format by address count?** → **Three-address, two-address, one-address and zero-address**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does a one-address instruction work with?** → **An implied accumulator**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does zero-address work on?** → **A stack — the operands are understood**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What do fewer addresses buy, and cost?** → **Shorter instructions and a simpler processor; more instructions per job**
