# Hands-on — The Format of a Machine Instruction

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Budget design** — Spend a 12-bit instruction two ways (5-bit opcode + 7-bit address; 3-bit opcode + 9-bit address) and argue which machine each suits.

## Task two
**Format reading** — Given the format opcode(4) addr1(6) addr2(6), decode the bit row 0011 000101 000110 into its operation and addresses, naming every field.
