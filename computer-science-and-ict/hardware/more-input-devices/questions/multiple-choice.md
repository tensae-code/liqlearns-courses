# More input devices — scanner, touch screen, light pen, camera — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A scanner converts —**
a) softcopy into hardcopy  b) a hardcopy document or photograph into softcopy  c) sound into text
**Answer: b) a hardcopy document or photograph into softcopy**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Hardcopy means —**
a) the version inside the computer  b) the paper version you can hold  c) a photograph only
**Answer: b) the paper version you can hold**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) A touch screen is worked by —**
a) typing  b) touching — it is a touch-sensitive pointing device  c) speaking
**Answer: b) touching — it is a touch-sensitive pointing device**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) A light pen is used for —**
a) choosing menu items or drawing on the screen  b) printing posters  c) storing data
**Answer: a) choosing menu items or drawing on the screen**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) A camera, as an input device, —**
a) captures photographs and saves them in its memory  b) prints photographs  c) shows films
**Answer: a) captures photographs and saves them in its memory**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) The joystick is added to the input list by —**
a) the student book's eight  b) the teacher guide's key — for moving within a screen environment, widely used in games  c) nobody
**Answer: b) the teacher guide's key — for moving within a screen environment, widely used in games** — Guide printed pp. 31–32.
