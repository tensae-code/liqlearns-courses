# More input devices — scanner, touch screen, light pen, camera — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A scanner converts hardcopy into ____.**
**Answer: softcopy**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Choosing menu items with a pen-shaped device is done with a ____ pen.**
**Answer: light**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) The ____ is widely used in games for moving within a screen environment.**
**Answer: joystick**
