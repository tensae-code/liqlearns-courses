# More input devices — scanner, touch screen, light pen, camera — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Softcopy is the version inside the computer.**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) A scanner prints paper documents.**
**Answer: False** — It converts paper into a computer version — the opposite direction.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) A light pen is shaped like a pen.**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) A camera saves its photographs in its memory.**
**Answer: True**
