# Hands-on — More input devices — scanner, touch screen, light pen, camera

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Hard to soft
Take one paper item at home — a photo, a letter, a drawing. Write the steps its journey through a scanner would take, and what you could do with its softcopy that you cannot do with the paper.

## Pointing three ways
List the three pointing devices from Units 2's lessons so far (mouse, touch screen, light pen — joystick makes four with the guide's addition). For each, write one job it is best suited to.
