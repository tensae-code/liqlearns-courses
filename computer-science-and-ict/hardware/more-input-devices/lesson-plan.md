# Lesson plan — More input devices — scanner, touch screen, light pen, camera

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Hardware · **Lesson:** More input devices — scanner, touch screen, light pen, camera
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 2 — Computer Hardware — in the title Hardware on the ICT course ladder (TITLES.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** A scanner converts a hardcopy document or photograph into softcopy; a touch screen is a touch-sensitive pointing device worked by touching; a light pen is a pen-shaped pointing device for choosing menu items or drawing on the screen; and a camera captures photographs and saves them in its memory.
**This lesson's own level:** It stands on Keyboard and mouse: four more of the book's eight input devices, taken in one lesson.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Scanner and touch screen** — The scanner turning hardcopy into softcopy, and the touch screen — a touch-sensitive pointing device worked by touching, tap shown responding.
2. **Light pen and camera — and the joystick (teacher guide — evidence)** — The pen-shaped light pen for choosing menu items or drawing on screen; the camera capturing photographs into its memory; and the joystick, which the teacher guide's key adds to the input list.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

- Recommended first: the lessons before this one in Hardware on the ladder. Recommended, never forced — a learner may land on this lesson directly, and it stands alone.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what a scanner does: converts hardcopy into softcopy; and say what hardcopy and softcopy mean.
2. Describe a touch screen and a light pen as pointing devices, and how each is worked.
3. Say what a camera does as an input device; and name the joystick as the guide's added input device, used in games.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil.

## If the learner is stuck
Mixes hardcopy and softcopy → hard = paper in the hand; soft = the version inside the computer. Thinks the touch screen is output only → in the book's list it is an input pointing device: your touch is data going in.
