# The Connection Ports on a Computer — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What joins the parts of a computer to the system unit?**
a) Its connection ports — each part has its own port and plug  b) Sound out to speakers and in from a microphone  c) The telephone line and the network  d) With the power off — the machine unplugged while hands learn
**Answer: a) Its connection ports — each part has its own port and plug**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does the power port take?**
a) Flash memory and many other devices  b) The power cable  c) So a part cannot easily be forced into the wrong socket  d) Gently and straight, one part at a time — never forced
**Answer: b) The power cable**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Which three parts each have their own port named first in this lesson?**
a) The telephone line and the network  b) With the power off — the machine unplugged while hands learn  c) The monitor, the keyboard and the mouse  d) The teacher
**Answer: c) The monitor, the keyboard and the mouse**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What do the audio ports carry?**
a) So a part cannot easily be forced into the wrong socket  b) Gently and straight, one part at a time — never forced  c) Its connection ports — each part has its own port and plug  d) Sound out to speakers and in from a microphone
**Answer: d) Sound out to speakers and in from a microphone**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does the USB port take?**
a) Flash memory and many other devices  b) With the power off — the machine unplugged while hands learn  c) The teacher  d) The power cable
**Answer: a) Flash memory and many other devices**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What do the modem and internet ports join the computer to?**
a) Gently and straight, one part at a time — never forced  b) The telephone line and the network  c) Its connection ports — each part has its own port and plug  d) The monitor, the keyboard and the mouse
**Answer: b) The telephone line and the network**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Why do plugs and ports have different shapes?**
a) The teacher  b) The power cable  c) So a part cannot easily be forced into the wrong socket  d) Sound out to speakers and in from a microphone
**Answer: c) So a part cannot easily be forced into the wrong socket**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) How is connecting practised in the school lab?**
a) Its connection ports — each part has its own port and plug  b) The monitor, the keyboard and the mouse  c) Flash memory and many other devices  d) With the power off — the machine unplugged while hands learn

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How do you put a plug in?**
a) Gently and straight, one part at a time — never forced  b) The power cable  c) Sound out to speakers and in from a microphone  d) The telephone line and the network
**Answer: a) Gently and straight, one part at a time — never forced**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Who checks the connections before power is switched on?**
a) The monitor, the keyboard and the mouse  b) The teacher  c) Flash memory and many other devices  d) So a part cannot easily be forced into the wrong socket
