# The Connection Ports on a Computer — true-false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What joins the parts of a computer to the system unit?" is: Its connection ports — each part has its own port and plug**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does the power port take?" is: The monitor, the keyboard and the mouse**
**Answer: False** — The power cable

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "Which three parts each have their own port named first in this lesson?" is: The monitor, the keyboard and the mouse**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What do the audio ports carry?" is: Flash memory and many other devices**
**Answer: False** — Sound out to speakers and in from a microphone

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: the answer to "What does the USB port take?" is: Flash memory and many other devices**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "What do the modem and internet ports join the computer to?" is: So a part cannot easily be forced into the wrong socket**
**Answer: False** — The telephone line and the network

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Why do plugs and ports have different shapes?" is: So a part cannot easily be forced into the wrong socket**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "How is connecting practised in the school lab?" is: Gently and straight, one part at a time — never forced**
**Answer: False** — With the power off — the machine unplugged while hands learn

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "How do you put a plug in?" is: Gently and straight, one part at a time — never forced**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: the answer to "Who checks the connections before power is switched on?" is: Its connection ports — each part has its own port and plug**
**Answer: False** — The teacher
