# Hands-on — The Connection Ports on a Computer

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Name the ports
On a switched-off computer (or a drawing of its back panel), point to each port and name the part that joins there: power, monitor, keyboard, mouse, audio, USB, modem, internet.

## Connect without power
With the machine unplugged, connect the monitor, keyboard, mouse and power cable, one at a time, gently and straight. Ask for a check before anyone switches on.
