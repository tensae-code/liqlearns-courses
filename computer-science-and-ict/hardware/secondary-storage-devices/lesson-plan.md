# Lesson plan — Hard disk, optical disk, flash disk and SD card

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Hardware · **Lesson:** Hard disk, optical disk, flash disk and SD card
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 2 — Computer Hardware — in the title Hardware on the ICT course ladder (TITLES.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The secondary storage devices: the hard disk (most common and usually largest), the optical disk (CD up to 700 MB, DVD up to 8.4 GB, read and written by laser), the USB flash disk (storage, backup and transferring files) and the SD card (removable card for phones, cameras and mobile electronics).
**This lesson's own level:** It stands on Storage devices and RAM and ROM: the permanent side of the divide now gets its four named devices.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the four secondary devices and the hard disk's standing: most common, usually largest.
2. State the optical sizes: CD up to 700 MB, DVD up to 8.4 GB, both read and written by laser.
3. Give the flash disk's three jobs and the SD card's home: phones, cameras and mobile electronics.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil; any of the four devices to hold, if your home has them.
