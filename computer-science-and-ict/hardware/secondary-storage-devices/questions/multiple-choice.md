# Hard disk, optical disk, flash disk and SD card — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. The most common and usually largest secondary store is —**
a) the SD card  b) the hard disk  c) the CD
**Answer: b) the hard disk**

**2. A CD holds up to —**
a) 8.4 GB  b) 700 MB  c) 1,024 GB
**Answer: b) 700 MB**

**3. A DVD holds up to —**
a) 700 MB  b) 8.4 GB  c) 70 MB
**Answer: b) 8.4 GB**

**4. Optical disks are read and written by —**
a) a magnet  b) a laser  c) a pen
**Answer: b) a laser**

**5. The USB flash disk is used for —**
a) storage, backup and transferring files  b) only games  c) printing
**Answer: a) storage, backup and transferring files**

**6. The SD card is a removable card for —**
a) printers  b) phones, cameras and mobile electronics  c) monitors
**Answer: b) phones, cameras and mobile electronics**
