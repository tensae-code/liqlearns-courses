# Hard disk, optical disk, flash disk and SD card

## Do it first

Look at the things your family keeps files on for good: the computer's inside store, a shiny disc, a small stick, a tiny card from a phone. Four keepers. This lesson gives each its name, its size and its job.

## Hard disk, optical disk, flash disk and SD card

Four secondary storage devices keep data permanently. 

[Picture 10136 — the four devices side by side with their jobs captioned]



**Hard disk** — the most common secondary store, and usually the largest. Most of a computer's kept files live here.

**Optical disk** — read and written by **laser**. A CD holds up to **700 MB**; a DVD holds up to **8.4 GB**.

**USB flash disk** — a small stick for storage, for **backup** (a spare copy kept safe) and for **transferring files** from machine to machine.

**SD card** — a removable card for phones, cameras and mobile electronics.

The teacher guide's key adds two older keepers to this list, as evidence: **magnetic tape** and the **floppy disk** (teacher guide — evidence) — named here so the family picture is complete, while the book's four remain the taught set.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion, in your own words: The secondary storage devices: the hard disk (most common and usually largest), the optical disk (CD up to 700 MB, DVD up to 8.4 GB, read and written by laser), the USB flash disk (storage, backup and transferring files) and the SD card (removable card for phones, cameras and mobile electronics). Then let your companion ask you one question about it — and answer from the lesson, not from memory of the book's exact words.
