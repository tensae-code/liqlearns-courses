# Hands-on — Hard disk, optical disk, flash disk and SD card

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Keeper census
At home, find every secondary storage keeper you can: a hard disk inside a computer, a CD or DVD, a flash stick, an SD card in a phone or camera. Write each one's owner, job and — for optical disks — its size from the lesson.

## Backup plan
Choose one file that would hurt to lose. Write a backup plan using a flash disk: what you copy, when, and where the stick is kept. A backup only counts if you could actually follow the plan.
