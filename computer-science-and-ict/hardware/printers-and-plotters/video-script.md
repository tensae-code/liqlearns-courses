# Video script — Printers — impact and non-impact — and plotters

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Printers.**

First, brainstorm: what do you already know about this? Say it out loud.

Now the one thing: a printer produces hard copy, in two classes — impact printers that strike, and non-impact printers that do not — while a plotter draws high-quality charts, maps and banners in many colours on paper larger than printers can handle.

**Printers — two classes.** A printer produces a hard copy of data or information.
**The plotter — drawing big.** A plotter produces high-quality drawings — bar charts, maps, banners — in many colours, on larger paper sizes that most printers cannot handle.

Now you: Draw a printer feeding out a page and a plotter drawing a large map.
