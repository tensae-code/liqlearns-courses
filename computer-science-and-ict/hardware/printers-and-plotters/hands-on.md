# Hands-on — Printers — impact and non-impact — and plotters

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Feed a page
If a printer is available, print one page and watch the journey: blank in, printed out. Time it. A plotter's journey is the same, at banner size — discuss what drawing line by line means for a map.

## Strike or not?
Sort printer cards — dot-matrix, daisy-wheel, ink jet, laser, thermal — into impact and non-impact. The test: does it strike the paper through a ribbon?
