# Printers — impact and non-impact — and plotters

## Do it first

An office needs three things on paper: a letter, a receipt printed with a striking ribbon, and a wall-sized map in many colours. Before reading on, guess which jobs belong to a printer and which need something bigger.

## Printers — two classes

A printer produces a hard copy of data or information. Printers differ in speed, capability and method, and they fall into two classes. Impact printers strike the paper through a ribbon — the dot-matrix and daisy-wheel printers. Non-impact printers form the page without striking — ink jet, laser and thermal printers.

## The plotter — drawing big

A plotter produces high-quality drawings — bar charts, maps, banners — in many colours, on larger paper sizes that most printers cannot handle. Watch the GIF first for the printer: a blank page feeds in and comes out printed. The plotter does the same journey at the size of a wall chart, drawing line by line.
