# Printers — impact and non-impact — and plotters — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** A printer produces —
a) Soft copy  b) Hard copy of data or information  c) Input  d) Storage
**Answer: b) Hard copy of data or information** — A printer produces a hard copy.

**2.** Impact printers are —
a) Ink jet and laser  b) Dot-matrix and daisy-wheel  c) Thermal and laser  d) Only plotters
**Answer: b) Dot-matrix and daisy-wheel** — Impact printers: dot-matrix and daisy-wheel.

**3.** Which of these is a non-impact printer?
a) Dot-matrix  b) Daisy-wheel  c) Laser  d) None of them
**Answer: c) Laser** — Non-impact printers: ink jet, laser and thermal.

**4.** Printers differ in —
a) Speed, capability and method  b) Colour of case only  c) Country only  d) Nothing
**Answer: a) Speed, capability and method** — Printers differ in speed, capability and method.

**5.** A plotter produces —
a) Only text letters  b) High-quality drawings — bar charts, maps, banners — in many colours on larger paper  c) Sound  d) 3D objects
**Answer: b) High-quality drawings — bar charts, maps, banners — in many colours on larger paper** — A plotter draws charts, maps and banners in many colours on larger paper sizes.
