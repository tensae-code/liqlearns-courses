# The keyboard — ten groups of keys — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The ______ key sits at the top-left and cancels an operation.
**Answer: Escape**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Windows + ______ shows the desktop.
**Answer: D**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Numbers grouped like a calculator sit on the numeric ______.
**Answer: keypad**
