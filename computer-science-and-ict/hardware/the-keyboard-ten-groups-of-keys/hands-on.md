# Hands-on — The keyboard — ten groups of keys

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Key-group hunt
On a real keyboard, a partner calls a group name; you point to it within three seconds. Ten groups, two rounds. The groups you miss are the ones to redraw.

## Windows-key drill
On a real computer (with permission), tap the Windows key to open Start, tap again to close it. Then try Windows+D, Windows+E, Windows+L — exactly as the GIF shows — and say what each did.
