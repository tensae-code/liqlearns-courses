# Lesson plan — The keyboard — ten groups of keys

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Hardware · **Lesson:** The keyboard — ten groups of keys
**Place in the curriculum:** Title 2 — Hardware of the Computer Science and ICT ladder (TITLES.md); Ethiopia Grade 8, Unit 2 — Computer Hardware.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the keyboard enters data through keys grouped by function into ten groups — from Escape and Backspace to the Windows key and the numeric keypad.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the hardware lesson: the keyboard is the first input device, taken in depth.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. ## If the learner is stuck
- Mixes Backspace and Delete → Backspace deletes the character BEFORE the cursor; Delete (in the navigation group) works forward.
- Thinks CTRL works alone → CTRL is used WITH other keys; alone it does nothing.
