# Lesson plan — Installation, updates and device drivers

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Hardware · **Lesson:** Installation, updates and device drivers
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 5 — Maintenance and Troubleshooting (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Keeping software updated and installed properly is preventive maintenance — and a device only works when its driver, the program that controls it, is in place.
**This lesson's own level:** It stands on the earlier Hardware lessons: the learner knows the parts of a computer and what programs are. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or in steps — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
1. **Preventive maintenance — updates that prevent failure** — software updates for security and reliability save time, money and failures; the guide's full definition of preventive maintenance (teacher evidence).
2. **Installing — making a program ready to run** — installing defined; configuring, drivers and plugins; the Windows basis of the unit's hands-on work and the version warning.
3. **Device drivers — the program that controls a device** — the driver as the controlling program for one specific device; why the right driver is essential.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the Grade 11 Hardware lessons on preventive maintenance and troubleshooting. Recommended, never forced — a learner may land on this lesson directly, and it stands alone.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Define software preventive maintenance and say what it saves.
2. Define installing, including configuring and setting up drivers and plugins.
3. Define a device driver and explain why the right driver is essential.
4. Say why the same activity can have different steps on another system or version.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. A school computer and one device, such as a printer, if available.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks installing means only copying files → installing also configures hardware or software and sets up drivers and plugins.
- Thinks a silent device must be broken → check the driver first: the device may be fine and its controller missing.
