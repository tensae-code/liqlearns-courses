# Hands-on — Installation, updates and device drivers

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## The driver hunt
List three devices attached to a school or home computer. For each one, write the device, and the job its driver does — the controlling program between the computer and that device.

## Maintenance timetable
Draw a simple monthly timetable for one computer: when updates are checked, when they are installed. Write one line under it: what failure this timetable is preventing.
