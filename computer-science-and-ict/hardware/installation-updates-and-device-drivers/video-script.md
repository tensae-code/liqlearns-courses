# Video script — Installation, updates and device drivers

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

A computer needs care before it breaks.

Installing updates keeps it safe and reliable.
That is preventive maintenance.

Installing makes a program ready to run.
And every device needs its driver —
the small program that controls it.

No driver, no working device.
