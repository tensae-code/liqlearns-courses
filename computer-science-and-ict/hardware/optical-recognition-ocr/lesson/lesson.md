## Reading with light

Optical recognition systems use light to read characters, codes and marks, and convert them into the computer-understandable 0-and-1 form. There are three kinds: OCR, OMR and the barcode reader. OMR and the barcode reader get the next lesson; this lesson is OCR.

## OCR — matching shapes

Optical Character Recognition scans the shape of typewritten, computer-printed and sometimes handwritten characters, and compares them with font shapes held in memory. When a scanned shape matches a stored shape, the character is known — and so paper documents are turned into computer code without typing.
