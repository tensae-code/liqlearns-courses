**Optical recognition.**

First, brainstorm: what do you already know about this? Say it out loud.

Now the one thing: optical recognition systems use light to read characters, codes and marks into 0-and-1 form — and OCR scans the shape of characters and matches them against font shapes held in memory.

**Reading with light.** Optical recognition systems use light to read characters, codes and marks, and convert them into the computer-understandable 0-and-1 form.
**OCR — matching shapes.** Optical Character Recognition scans the shape of typewritten, computer-printed and sometimes handwritten characters, and compares them with font shapes held in memory.

Now you: Draw a printed page being read by light, with one letter travelling to a row of stored font shapes and matching one.
