# Hands-on — Optical recognition — OCR reads characters

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Shape matching
Write the letter A in five different handwritings. A partner matches each to the same stored 'A'. That matching — shape to stored shape — is what OCR does at machine speed.

## Picture or text?
If you can scan a page, try searching the result for a word. If the scan is only a picture, the search fails — discuss why OCR is the missing step.
