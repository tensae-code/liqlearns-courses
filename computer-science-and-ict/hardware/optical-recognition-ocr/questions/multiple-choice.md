# Optical recognition — OCR reads characters — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Optical recognition systems use — to read characters, codes and marks.
a) Sound  b) Light  c) Heat  d) Magnets only
**Answer: b) Light** — Optical recognition systems use light to read.

**2.** The three kinds of optical recognition are —
a) OCR, OMR and the barcode reader  b) RAM, ROM and CMOS  c) CRT, LCD and LED  d) CTRL, ALT and DELETE
**Answer: a) OCR, OMR and the barcode reader** — OCR, OMR and the barcode reader are the three kinds.

**3.** OCR works by —
a) Typing the page again  b) Scanning character shapes and comparing them with font shapes held in memory  c) Printing the page  d) Recording the reader's voice
**Answer: b) Scanning character shapes and comparing them with font shapes held in memory** — OCR compares scanned shapes with font shapes held in memory.

**4.** OCR turns paper documents into —
a) Ash  b) Computer code, without typing  c) Sound  d) Plastic
**Answer: b) Computer code, without typing** — OCR turns paper documents into computer code without typing.

**5.** OCR can read —
a) Only barcodes  b) Typewritten, computer-printed and sometimes handwritten characters  c) Only handwriting  d) Only pictures
**Answer: b) Typewritten, computer-printed and sometimes handwritten characters** — Typewritten, computer-printed and sometimes handwritten characters.
