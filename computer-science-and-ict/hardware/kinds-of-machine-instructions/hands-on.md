# Hands-on — Kinds of Machine Instructions

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Family census** — Sort twelve given machine instructions (ADD, LOAD, JMP, IN, STORE, AND, CALL, SUB, OUT, RET, PUSH, SHL) into the four families with one line of justification each.

## Task two
**Program reading** — Take an assembly fragment from the book and annotate each line with its family and its effect on registers, memory or the program counter.
