# Video script — Assembling a Desktop Computer

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Parts on a table do nothing.

Name each part. The box. The screen. The keyboard. The mouse.

Power off. Join one part at a time.

Match the cable to its port. Never force it.

Power last. Check every join. Now switch on.
