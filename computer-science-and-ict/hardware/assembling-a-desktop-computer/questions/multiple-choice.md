# Assembling a Desktop Computer — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Which part holds the processing and storage of a desktop computer?**
a) The monitor  b) The system unit  c) The mouse
**Answer: b) The system unit** — The other parts connect to it; the computer itself lives inside it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) The keyboard and mouse are used to —**
a) Show the work  b) Store files  c) Give the computer its instructions
**Answer: c) Give the computer its instructions** — They are the user's input parts; the monitor shows the result.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) While parts are being connected, the power must be —**
a) Off and unplugged  b) On, so you can test each cable  c) Half on
**Answer: a) Off and unplugged** — Connections are made while nothing is live.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) A cable will not go in easily. The right move is to —**
a) Push harder  b) Stop, turn it and match its shape to the port — never force it  c) Cut it to fit
**Answer: b) Stop, turn it and match its shape to the port — never force it** — Force bends pins and breaks ports.

**5. (Sub lesson 2) Which connection is made last?**
a) The keyboard  b) The monitor  c) The power cable
**Answer: c) The power cable** — Parts are joined to each other first; power comes last.

**6. (Sub lesson 3) The screen stays dark at the first switch-on. First, you —**
a) Open the system unit  b) Check the connections in order — monitor cable, monitor power, wall power  c) Declare the monitor broken
**Answer: b) Check the connections in order — monitor cable, monitor power, wall power** — Loose joins are the first suspects, not broken parts.
