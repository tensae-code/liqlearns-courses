# Assembling a Desktop Computer — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 2) Put the assembly in order: power cable, monitor, keyboard, mouse — and say what state the power is in while you work.**
**Answer:** A sound order: with the power off and unplugged, join the monitor to the system unit, then the keyboard, then the mouse — matching each cable to its port without forcing — and connect the power cable last.

**2. (Sub lesson 3) Why does assembling join by join make the start-up check easier?**
**Answer:** Because the learner knows exactly what was connected where and in what order, so the check can work through the same joins in the same order instead of hunting at random.
