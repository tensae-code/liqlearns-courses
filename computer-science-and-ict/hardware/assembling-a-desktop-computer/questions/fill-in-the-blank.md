# Assembling a Desktop Computer — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) The box that holds the computer itself is the ____ unit.**
**Answer: system**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) A cable end fits the ____ — the socket — made for it.**
**Answer: port**

**3. (Sub lesson 3) At a dark screen, the first suspects are ____, not broken parts.**
**Answer: connections**
