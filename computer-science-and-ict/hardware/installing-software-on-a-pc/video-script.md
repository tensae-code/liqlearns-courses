# Video script — Installing software on a PC

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today we install a program. The full walkthrough.

First, the source. Buy it, download it from a trusted place, or use the disc or USB it came on. Never take software from a source you do not trust.

Step one. Put the disc in the drive — or the USB in its port.
Step two. Run the installer. It usually starts by itself. The AutoPlay box appears — choose Install, or Setup.
If nothing appears, do this instead: open the Computer window, right-click the drive, and choose Install — Run Program from your media. That is the fallback route.
Step three. Follow the screen. A warning asks — give the administrator password, or choose Yes. Agree to the licence. Type the serial number if it asks — it is on the box, in the manual, or in the email. Change the disc if it asks. Choose where the files go.
Step four. Wait. The program tells you: success, or failure.
Step five. Close the installer window.
Step six. If it asks, restart Windows.
Step seven. Open your new program. It is installed.
