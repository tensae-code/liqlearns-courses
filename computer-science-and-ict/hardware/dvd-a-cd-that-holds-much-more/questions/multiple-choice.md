# DVD — a CD that holds much more — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** DVD stands for —
a) Digital Video Disc  b) Digital Versatile Disc  c) Data Visual Disk  d) Digital Volume Drive
**Answer: b) Digital Versatile Disc** — DVD = Digital Versatile Disc; 'Digital Video Disc' is the common mistake.

**2.** A DVD holds more than a CD because —
a) It is a larger disk  b) Its tracks are narrower, so more fit  c) It spins slower  d) It uses magnets
**Answer: b) Its tracks are narrower, so more fit** — A DVD is like a CD but with narrower tracks.

**3.** A DVD stores — of information.
a) 700 MB  b) 4.7–17 GB  c) 1 TB exactly  d) 100 MB
**Answer: b) 4.7–17 GB** — A DVD stores 4.7–17 GB.

**4.** A CD stores —
a) 700 MB  b) 4.7 GB  c) 17 GB  d) 700 GB
**Answer: a) 700 MB** — A CD stores 700 MB on one side.
