# DVD — a CD that holds much more

## Do it first

A CD holds 700 MB — about one album of music or a short film in low quality. A whole film with room to spare needs more. Before reading on, guess how the DVD fits more on the same-sized disk.

## The DVD

The DVD is like a CD but with narrower tracks — the data rings are packed closer together, so far more of them fit the same disk. Its full name is Digital Versatile Disc; some people mistakenly say Digital Video Disc, but 'Versatile' is the true word — the disk serves many kinds of content, not only video.

## How much more

A DVD stores 4.7–17 GB of information, against the CD's 700 MB. That jump — from hundreds of megabytes to many gigabytes — is why films and large programs moved from CD to DVD.
