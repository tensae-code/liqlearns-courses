# Video script — DVD — a CD that holds much more

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**DVD.**

First, brainstorm: what do you already know about this? Say it out loud.

Now the one thing: the DVD — Digital Versatile Disc — looks like a CD but has narrower tracks, and stores much more: 4.7 to 17 GB.

**The DVD.** The DVD is like a CD but with narrower tracks — the data rings are packed closer together, so far more of them fit the same disk.
**How much more.** A DVD stores 4.7–17 GB of information, against the CD's 700 MB.

Now you: Draw a CD and a DVD side by side with their capacities: 700 MB against 4.7–17 GB.
