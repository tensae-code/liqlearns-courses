# Hands-on — DVD — a CD that holds much more

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Track spacing
Draw two circles: rings spaced wide (CD) and rings spaced narrow (DVD). Count the rings on each. The narrow-track drawing holds more rings — that is the DVD's trick, seen by eye.

## Shelf maths
A school has 14 CDs of lessons. How many 4.7 GB DVDs could hold the same, at most? Do the division and discuss what 'versatile' means for what else those DVDs could carry.
