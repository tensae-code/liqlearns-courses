# Hands-on — Primary storage — RAM and ROM

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Power-off test
On a real computer, open a document, type a line, and (with permission and nothing important open) watch what an unsaved program loses when it closes or power goes. That vanishing is volatility — felt, not just read.

## Desk and plate
Write three notes on loose scraps (RAM) and one instruction on a card you tape down permanently (ROM). 'Switch off' — sweep the scraps away. The card remains: startup instructions survive.
