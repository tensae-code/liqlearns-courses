# Naming the Digital Devices Around Us — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name four digital devices in French.**
**Answer: Tablette, ordinateur, écran, souris (tablet, computer, screen, mouse)**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What three things does the fiche match together?**
**Answer: Picture, name, and the thing itself**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why name devices first?**
**Answer: A class cannot talk about, share or care for tools it cannot name together**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What is the fiche called?**
**Answer: Les différents appareils — the different devices**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does souris mean as a device?**
**Answer: The mouse**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What is a screen not?**
**Answer: The whole computer — it is one part**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does each name pin?**
**Answer: One thing**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What fails when names slide between things?**
**Answer: Instructions, programs and care routines**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is naming the beginning of?**
**Answer: The course's later precision**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Is the mouse a toy animal in this lesson?**
**Answer: No — it is a device of its own**
