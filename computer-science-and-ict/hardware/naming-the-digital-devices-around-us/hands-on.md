# Hands-on — Naming the Digital Devices Around Us

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Name hunt** — Walk the classroom (or use device cards). Name every digital device aloud, in both languages if you can. The class repeats each name together.

## Task two
**Pin the name** — Shuffle picture cards and name labels for ten devices. Match them against the clock — then match the labels to the real devices in the room.
