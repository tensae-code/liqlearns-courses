**The one thing this lesson teaches:** naming the digital devices around us (les différents appareils): learning the names of the devices in the classroom and home — tablet, computer, screen, mouse and their kin (Switzerland Grades 1–2 Décodage, Scénario 3 Fiche 2).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The names of the devices** — Before using devices well, the youngest pupils learn to **name** them: the **tablet (tablette)**, the **computer (ordinateur)**, the **screen (écran)**, the **mouse (souris)**, the keyboard, the phone, the camera. The scenario's fiche (Les différents appareils) is a naming hunt: picture, name, and the thing itself matched together — because a class cannot talk about its tools, share them, or take care of them until everyone calls them by the same names.
2. **One name, one thing** — Naming is also sorting: a tablet is not a computer-without-keyboard in the child's first map, a screen is not the whole computer, and the mouse is a device of its own, not a toy animal. Each name pins one thing, and the pinning is the beginning of all the course's later precision — instructions, programs and care routines all fail when names slide between things.

Paper and a pencil, and a computer with the program this lesson uses, if available.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — Point at the things in this room you could name in one word each. The digital ones hide among the furniture. Before reading on, list every digital device you can see or remember in this room — names only. (lesson/lesson.md).
2. **The explanation** — the lesson text in lesson/lesson.md, in its parts in order.
   - ADD IMAGE: a picture of the names of the devices as the lesson describes it — the student studies the picture and names what each part shows.
   - ADD GIF: a GIF of one name, one thing in action — the student watches once, then says what changed and why.
   - ADD AUDIO: a voice reading the lesson's key names and definitions aloud, in the order the lesson gives them — the student listens once through, then reads the same lines aloud along with the voice. (Audio is a described slot in this build; no audio file is made.)
3. **Say it to your companion** — the companion block in lesson/lesson.md.
   - ADD VIDEO: the script in video-script.md, filmed — the lesson in the super-simple voice (made later, when videos are made).
4. **Questions** — from questions/: multiple-choice, true-false, matching, short-answer — served at the learner's level and teaching-mix (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** — the doing block at the end of lesson/lesson.md.
