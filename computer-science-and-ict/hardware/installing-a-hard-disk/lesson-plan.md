## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Mounting in the bay** — The disk is fitted into a **drive bay** of its size inside the case and **screwed firm on its sides** — a hard disk spins at thousands of turns a minute, and a loose disk vibrates, suffers and fails early. It is handled **by its body, never dropped or knocked**: of all the parts in the chapter, the disk is the one a single fall can kill outright, taking every file it held with it.
2. **Power and data — the two leads** — A mounted disk is still nothing until it is joined twice. The **power lead** comes from the power supply; the **data lead** joins the disk to the board, carrying everything read and written. Each lead fits its own socket, one way, without force. Laos's check: both leads seated, disk firm, and at the install of the system later in the chapter the disk must **appear to the machine** — an unseen disk is a lead or a seat to check, not a program to reinstall.
