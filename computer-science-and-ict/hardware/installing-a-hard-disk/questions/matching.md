# Installing a Hard Disk — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Where is the hard disk fitted?** → **In a drive bay of its size inside the case**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How is it fixed?** → **Screwed firm on its sides**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why must it be firm?** → **It spins thousands of turns a minute; a loose disk vibrates and fails early**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why is the disk handled most carefully of all parts?** → **A single fall can kill it and every file it holds**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What are the disk's two leads?** → **The power lead (from the power supply) and the data lead (to the board)**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does the data lead carry?** → **Everything read from and written to the disk**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How do the leads fit?** → **Each in its own socket, one way, without force**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What announces a bad install later?** → **The disk does not appear to the machine at system install**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which chapter teaches this?** → **Laos Grade 8 ICT, Chapter 2 §2.6**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is checked for an unseen disk?** → **A lead or the seat — not the program**
