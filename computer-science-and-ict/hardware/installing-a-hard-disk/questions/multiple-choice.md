# Installing a Hard Disk — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Where is the hard disk fitted?**
a) In a drive bay of its size inside the case  b) Screwed firm on its sides  c) It spins thousands of turns a minute; a loose disk vibrates and fails early  d) A single fall can kill it and every file it holds
**Answer: a**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How is it fixed?**
a) It spins thousands of turns a minute; a loose disk vibrates and fails early  b) Screwed firm on its sides  c) A single fall can kill it and every file it holds  d) The power lead (from the power supply) and the data lead (to the board)
**Answer: b**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why must it be firm?**
a) A single fall can kill it and every file it holds  b) The power lead (from the power supply) and the data lead (to the board)  c) It spins thousands of turns a minute; a loose disk vibrates and fails early  d) Everything read from and written to the disk
**Answer: c**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why is the disk handled most carefully of all parts?**
a) The power lead (from the power supply) and the data lead (to the board)  b) Everything read from and written to the disk  c) Each in its own socket, one way, without force  d) A single fall can kill it and every file it holds
**Answer: d**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What are the disk's two leads?**
a) The power lead (from the power supply) and the data lead (to the board)  b) Everything read from and written to the disk  c) Each in its own socket, one way, without force  d) The disk does not appear to the machine at system install
**Answer: a**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does the data lead carry?**
a) Each in its own socket, one way, without force  b) Everything read from and written to the disk  c) The disk does not appear to the machine at system install  d) Laos Grade 8 ICT, Chapter 2 §2.6
**Answer: b**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) How do the leads fit?**
a) The disk does not appear to the machine at system install  b) Laos Grade 8 ICT, Chapter 2 §2.6  c) Each in its own socket, one way, without force  d) A lead or the seat — not the program
**Answer: c**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What announces a bad install later?**
a) Laos Grade 8 ICT, Chapter 2 §2.6  b) A lead or the seat — not the program  c) In a drive bay of its size inside the case  d) The disk does not appear to the machine at system install
**Answer: d**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which chapter teaches this?**
a) Laos Grade 8 ICT, Chapter 2 §2.6  b) A lead or the seat — not the program  c) In a drive bay of its size inside the case  d) Screwed firm on its sides
**Answer: a**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is checked for an unseen disk?**
a) In a drive bay of its size inside the case  b) A lead or the seat — not the program  c) Screwed firm on its sides  d) It spins thousands of turns a minute; a loose disk vibrates and fails early
**Answer: b**
