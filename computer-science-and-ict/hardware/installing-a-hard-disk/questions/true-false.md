# Installing a Hard Disk — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: In a drive bay of its size inside the case**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: It spins thousands of turns a minute; a loose disk vibrates and fails early** — as the answer to: How is it fixed?
**Answer: False — Screwed firm on its sides**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: It spins thousands of turns a minute; a loose disk vibrates and fails early**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: The power lead (from the power supply) and the data lead (to the board)** — as the answer to: Why is the disk handled most carefully of all parts?
**Answer: False — A single fall can kill it and every file it holds**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: The power lead (from the power supply) and the data lead (to the board)**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Each in its own socket, one way, without force** — as the answer to: What does the data lead carry?
**Answer: False — Everything read from and written to the disk**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Each in its own socket, one way, without force**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Laos Grade 8 ICT, Chapter 2 §2.6** — as the answer to: What announces a bad install later?
**Answer: False — The disk does not appear to the machine at system install**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Laos Grade 8 ICT, Chapter 2 §2.6**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: In a drive bay of its size inside the case** — as the answer to: What is checked for an unseen disk?
**Answer: False — A lead or the seat — not the program**
