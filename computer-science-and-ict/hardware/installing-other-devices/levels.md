# Installing Other Devices — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, expressed as its SUB LESSONS. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — The remaining devices and their connections
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Mount or place the device, join it by its lead, without force; The floppy drive; The case's power and reset buttons and lights, joined to the board by thin leads; The machine will not start from its own button; Monitor, keyboard and mouse.
