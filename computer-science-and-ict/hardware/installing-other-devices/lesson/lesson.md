# Installing Other Devices

## Do it first

After the named installs, Laos's Grade 8 chapter closes the bench work with the rest: the other devices a complete machine needs. This lesson gathers them — and the check that proves the whole build.

## Sub lesson 1 — The remaining devices and their connections

The remaining installs follow the chapter's one pattern — **mount or place the device, join it by the lead made for it, without force**. Into this family fall the **floppy drive** of the era, fitted and led like the other drives; the **front-panel connections** — the case's power and reset buttons and its small lights, joined to the board by their thin leads, on the right pins or the machine will not start from its own button; and the **outside devices** — monitor, keyboard and mouse — joined at the back ports they fit. Each is small; together they finish the machine.

## Sub lesson 2 — The whole-machine check

The section closes the bench the way the guide times it — as supervised bench work ending in a **whole check**: every device seated and screwed, every lead home, no screw left loose inside, no tool left inside, the case closed only after a last look. Then the first switch-on, watched and listened to: fans turning, lights on the case, the start-up screen, and the **beep signals** the next sections teach — one short beep of health, or a pattern that names the install to revisit. Laos builds the habit this lesson ends on: a build is not finished when the last screw turns, but when the whole machine has answered for itself.
