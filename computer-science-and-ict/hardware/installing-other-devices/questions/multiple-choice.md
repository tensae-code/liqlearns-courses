# Installing Other Devices — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What pattern do all the remaining installs follow?**
a) Mount or place the device, join it by its lead, without force  b) The floppy drive  c) The case's power and reset buttons and lights, joined to the board by thin leads  d) The machine will not start from its own button
**Answer: a**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Which drive of the era is fitted like the other drives?**
a) The case's power and reset buttons and lights, joined to the board by thin leads  b) The floppy drive  c) The machine will not start from its own button  d) Monitor, keyboard and mouse
**Answer: b**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What are the front-panel connections?**
a) The machine will not start from its own button  b) Monitor, keyboard and mouse  c) The case's power and reset buttons and lights, joined to the board by thin leads  d) A loose screw and a tool
**Answer: c**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What happens if front-panel leads sit on the wrong pins?**
a) Monitor, keyboard and mouse  b) A loose screw and a tool  c) Only after a last look inside  d) The machine will not start from its own button
**Answer: d**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Name the outside devices joined at the back.**
a) Monitor, keyboard and mouse  b) A loose screw and a tool  c) Only after a last look inside  d) Fans, lights, the start-up screen and the beep signals
**Answer: a**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What two things must not be left inside the case?**
a) Only after a last look inside  b) A loose screw and a tool  c) Fans, lights, the start-up screen and the beep signals  d) Laos Grade 8 ICT, Chapter 2 §2.8
**Answer: b**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) When is the case closed?**
a) Fans, lights, the start-up screen and the beep signals  b) Laos Grade 8 ICT, Chapter 2 §2.8  c) Only after a last look inside  d) When the whole machine has answered for itself at switch-on
**Answer: c**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is watched and heard at first switch-on?**
a) Laos Grade 8 ICT, Chapter 2 §2.8  b) When the whole machine has answered for itself at switch-on  c) Mount or place the device, join it by its lead, without force  d) Fans, lights, the start-up screen and the beep signals
**Answer: d**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which chapter teaches this?**
a) Laos Grade 8 ICT, Chapter 2 §2.8  b) When the whole machine has answered for itself at switch-on  c) Mount or place the device, join it by its lead, without force  d) The floppy drive
**Answer: a**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) When is a build finished?**
a) Mount or place the device, join it by its lead, without force  b) When the whole machine has answered for itself at switch-on  c) The floppy drive  d) The case's power and reset buttons and lights, joined to the board by thin leads
