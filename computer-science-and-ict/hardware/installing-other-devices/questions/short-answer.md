# Installing Other Devices — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What pattern do all the remaining installs follow?**
**Answer: Mount or place the device, join it by its lead, without force**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Which drive of the era is fitted like the other drives?**
**Answer: The floppy drive**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What are the front-panel connections?**
**Answer: The case's power and reset buttons and lights, joined to the board by thin leads**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What happens if front-panel leads sit on the wrong pins?**
**Answer: The machine will not start from its own button**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Name the outside devices joined at the back.**
**Answer: Monitor, keyboard and mouse**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What two things must not be left inside the case?**
**Answer: A loose screw and a tool**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) When is the case closed?**
**Answer: Only after a last look inside**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is watched and heard at first switch-on?**
**Answer: Fans, lights, the start-up screen and the beep signals**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which chapter teaches this?**
**Answer: Laos Grade 8 ICT, Chapter 2 §2.8**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) When is a build finished?**
**Answer: When the whole machine has answered for itself at switch-on**
