# Installing Other Devices — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Mount or place the device, join it by its lead, without force**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: The case's power and reset buttons and lights, joined to the board by thin leads** — as the answer to: Which drive of the era is fitted like the other drives?
**Answer: False — The floppy drive**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: The case's power and reset buttons and lights, joined to the board by thin leads**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Monitor, keyboard and mouse** — as the answer to: What happens if front-panel leads sit on the wrong pins?
**Answer: False — The machine will not start from its own button**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Monitor, keyboard and mouse**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Only after a last look inside** — as the answer to: What two things must not be left inside the case?
**Answer: False — A loose screw and a tool**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Only after a last look inside**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Laos Grade 8 ICT, Chapter 2 §2.8** — as the answer to: What is watched and heard at first switch-on?
**Answer: False — Fans, lights, the start-up screen and the beep signals**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Laos Grade 8 ICT, Chapter 2 §2.8**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Mount or place the device, join it by its lead, without force** — as the answer to: When is a build finished?
**Answer: False — When the whole machine has answered for itself at switch-on**
