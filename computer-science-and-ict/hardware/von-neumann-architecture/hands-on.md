# Hands-on — Von Neumann Architecture

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Name the parts
Draw the design from memory: memory in the middle, processor beside it. Label where the program lives, where the data lives, and the path between them — then check your drawing against the lesson.

## Follow one instruction
Pick a simple instruction, such as ADD. Write its journey in three lines — fetch, decode, execute — saying what happens at each step.
