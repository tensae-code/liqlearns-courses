# The Motherboard — Chipset, Sockets and Slots — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What directs the motherboard's work?**
**Answer: Its chipset — the set of controller chips**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What does the chipset manage?**
**Answer: The flow of data between processor, memory and the other parts**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What are the board's pathways called?**
**Answer: Buses**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why does the chipset decide compatibility?**
**Answer: It decides which processors and memory the board can work with**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) What holds the processor?**
**Answer: The CPU socket, shaped for one processor family**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Where do RAM modules go?**
**Answer: In the memory slots, clicked in by their edges**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What do expansion slots take?**
**Answer: Extra cards — display, sound, network**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is the installer's first question about a part?**
**Answer: Does it fit this socket, this slot?**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which chapter teaches this?**
**Answer: Laos Grade 8 ICT, Chapter 1 §1.3**

**10. (Sub lesson 1) Why must a CPU and a board be chosen for each other?**
**Answer: The socket is shaped for one family of processors**
