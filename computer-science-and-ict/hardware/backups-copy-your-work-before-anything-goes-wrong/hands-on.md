# Hands-on — Backups — copy your work before anything goes wrong

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## The pre-repair interview
Role-play: one is the troubleshooter, one the machine's owner. The troubleshooter must ask the four check questions and write the answers before 'repair' may begin. Swap roles with a different failure story.

## Run the route (or rehearse it)
On a school computer, follow the route to the destination-drive choice — and stop there unless your teacher says run it. On paper, draw each window's name in order.
