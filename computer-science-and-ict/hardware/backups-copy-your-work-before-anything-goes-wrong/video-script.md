# Video script — Backups — copy your work before anything goes wrong

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. FULL script, as the placement names a video need for this lesson.

---

Before you fix anything — copy everything.

A backup is your work, copied to a safe place:
an external drive, or a cloud server.
If the machine fails, the work survives.

THE RULE of this unit:
Backup is the FIRST step before troubleshooting.
No backup? Do not start.

Ask the owner four things:
When was the last backup?
What is in it?
Is it sound?
Is everything needed for a restore at hand?

Imagine a bank, hit by a virus.
Not one withdrawal can be served —
until the backup brings the data back.
That is what a backup is FOR.

Now the Windows route, step by step:
Open Control Panel.
Choose Backup and Restore.
Choose Set up backup.
Choose Create a system image.
Pick the destination drive —
hard disk, DVD, or network.
Click Next. Click Start backup.

Or search the word backup,
and choose Backup settings —
the same room, a second door.

Copy first. Then troubleshoot.
