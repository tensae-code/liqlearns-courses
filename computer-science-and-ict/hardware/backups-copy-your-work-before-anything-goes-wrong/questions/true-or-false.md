# Backups — copy your work before anything goes wrong — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** One backup, made once, is enough forever.
**Answer: False** — update it as often as needed.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A backup lets work be recovered after a failure.
**Answer: True** — that is its whole purpose.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Searching 'backup' and choosing Backup settings reaches the same backup window.
**Answer: True** — the book's second door.
