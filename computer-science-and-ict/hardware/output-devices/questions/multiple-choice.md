# Output devices — getting results out — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. An output device —**
a) inserts data  b) lets the computer communicate processing results to the outside world in a form humans understand  c) stores data permanently
**Answer: b) lets the computer communicate processing results to the outside world in a form humans understand**

**2. The monitor displays information as —**
a) sound  b) pictures or text  c) paper
**Answer: b) pictures or text**

**3. The three monitor types the book pictures are —**
a) CRT, LCD and LED  b) big, medium and small  c) old, new and future
**Answer: a) CRT, LCD and LED**

**4. A hard copy is made by —**
a) the scanner  b) the printer — data printed on paper  c) the speaker
**Answer: b) the printer — data printed on paper**

**5. The plotter is for —**
a) playing sound  b) highly graphical pictures and posters  c) typing text
**Answer: b) highly graphical pictures and posters**

**6. The guide's key adds which output examples?**
a) Robot and headphone  b) Keyboard and mouse  c) Disk and CD
**Answer: a) Robot and headphone** — Guide printed pp. 31–34.
