# Hands-on — Output devices — getting results out

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Out, in four forms
Collect four real outputs if you can: something on a screen, a printed page, a poster or large drawing, a sound from a speaker. Write which device made each, and what form the result took.

## Hard copy hunt
Find one hard copy at home that began inside a computer — a printed letter, a report, a photo print. Write its journey: processed where, printed by what, read by whom.
