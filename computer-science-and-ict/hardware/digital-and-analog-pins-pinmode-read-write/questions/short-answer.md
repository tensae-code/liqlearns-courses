# Digital and Analog Pins — pinMode, Read and Write — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What must every pin be told first?**
**Answer: Its direction — with pinMode(pin, INPUT or OUTPUT)**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is the beginner's most common silent fault?**
**Answer: A pin set to the wrong mode**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does digitalWrite do?**
**Answer: Drives an output pin fully on (HIGH) or off (LOW)**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does digitalRead report?**
**Answer: An input pin's state — the button pressed or not**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does analogRead do?**
**Answer: Converts a varying voltage from a sensor into a number — 0 to 1023 on the Uno**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does analogWrite set?**
**Answer: An output's average level — 0 to 255 — dimming an LED or pacing a motor**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Digital is the language of what, analog of what?**
**Answer: Digital for decisions, analog for degrees**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Name a sensor each analog input might read.**
**Answer: A light sensor or a temperature sensor**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) The course's one commandment for pins is what?**
**Answer: Set the mode, then read or write**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Where does Indonesia teach this?**
**Answer: Grade 12, Bab 3 J–K — digital and analog pin commands**
