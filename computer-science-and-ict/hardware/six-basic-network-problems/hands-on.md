# Hands-on — Six basic network problems — cable to overload

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Six scenes
Draw the six scenes from the picture yourself, one per problem, and label each. Cover the labels and have a partner name the problem from the drawing.

## Fault clinic
Write six one-line fault reports, one per problem, shuffled. A partner diagnoses each by number and names the check that would prove it.
