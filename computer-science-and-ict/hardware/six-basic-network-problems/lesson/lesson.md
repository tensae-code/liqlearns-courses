# Six basic network problems — cable to overload

## Do it first

The network is slow — or gone. Six usual suspects stand behind almost every case. Write any causes you can guess before the lesson names all six.

## Sub lesson 1 — Problems 1–3 — cable, connectivity, out of reach

**Slow transmission or no connection at all** can come from:

1. A **cable problem** — the connecting cable **faulty, shortened or physically damaged**.
2. A **connectivity problem** — the **port/interface** a device is connected or configured on is **physically down or faulty**, so the **source cannot reach the destination**.
3. An **out-of-reach issue** — a wireless host **too far from the access point**, or an **obstructing object** between them.

## Sub lesson 2 — Problems 4–6 — configuration, software, overload

4. A **configuration issue** — a **wrong configuration**, such as a **routing problem**.
5. A **software issue** — **compatibility problems and version mismatch** interrupting **IP packet transmission**.
6. **Traffic overload** — a **link or device carrying more than its capacity** and behaving abnormally.
