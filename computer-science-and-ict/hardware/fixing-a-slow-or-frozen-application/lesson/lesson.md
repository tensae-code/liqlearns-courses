# Fixing a slow or frozen application

## Do it first

You are typing an assignment and the program stops answering. The mouse moves; the program does not. Write your moves in order — gentle first, forceful last — then compare them with the lesson's order.

## Sub lesson 1 — First moves — reopen, then update

In order:

1. **Close and reopen the application.**
2. If it persists, **update the application** — **Help menu → check for updates**, or **search online for application updates**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Force quit — Task Manager and End Task

If the application is **stuck or frozen**, so **no button or close works**, force-quit it:

1. Hold **Ctrl+Alt+Delete** to open **Task Manager**.
2. Under the **Processes tab**, click the **frozen application**.
3. Click **End Task**.

If force quit is **impossible**, **restarting the computer** closes all open apps — the last resort of this lesson.

## Sub lesson 3 — The wider keys — frozen computer and blank screen (teacher guide — evidence)

> **Teacher evidence — the ordered answer keys (teacher guide — evidence, printed pp. 104–105).** For a **completely frozen computer**: first open Task Manager with **Ctrl+Alt+Delete**, find **Windows Explorer** in the Processes tab and **restart it**; second, **press and hold the power button for 5 to 10 seconds** to force shutdown; third and **last resort only**, **unplug the power cable** — or remove a laptop's battery. For a **blank screen**, four checks in order: the computer may be in **Sleep mode** (click the mouse or press a key to wake it); the **monitor is plugged in and turned on**; the **computer is plugged in and turned on**; and, on a desktop, the **monitor cable is properly connected at both the tower and the monitor**.
