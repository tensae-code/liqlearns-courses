# Video script — Fixing a slow or frozen application

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The program froze. Do not panic.

Close it. Open it again.
Still slow? Update it — Help, check for updates.

Frozen hard? Hold the three keys:
Control, Alt, Delete.
Task Manager opens. Processes tab.
Choose the frozen program. End Task.

Nothing works at all? Restart the computer.
That closes everything.
