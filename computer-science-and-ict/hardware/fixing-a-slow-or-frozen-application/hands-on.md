# Hands-on — Fixing a slow or frozen application

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Escalation ladder
Draw a four-rung ladder: reopen, update, End Task, restart. Beside it draw the guide's three-rung ladder for a frozen computer, with the last rung marked LAST RESORT.

## Blank-screen drill
With a partner, act the blank-screen checks in order on a real (sleeping) school computer: wake, monitor power, computer power, cable at both ends.
