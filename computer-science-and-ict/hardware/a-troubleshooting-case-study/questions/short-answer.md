# A Troubleshooting Case Study — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does a troubleshooting case study start with?**
**Answer: The fault as it was found**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is recorded for each check in the case?**
**Answer: What was checked and what happened**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why are the checks kept in order?**
**Answer: So the cause can be followed step by step**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How many things are changed at a time?**
**Answer: One thing only**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What ends a troubleshooting case study?**
**Answer: The fix that removed the fault**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Why change one thing at a time?**
**Answer: So you know which change mattered**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What do you do if a check changes nothing?**
**Answer: Record it and move to the next check**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What proves the cause of the fault?**
**Answer: The change after which the fault disappears**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Why write the steps of a fix down?**
**Answer: So the same fault can be fixed faster next time**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) Is guessing the method of a case study?**
**Answer: No — the case works by checked steps**
