# Hands-on — A Troubleshooting Case Study

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Rebuild a case on paper
Your teacher or companion describes a fixed fault (a loose cable, a muted speaker). Write it as a case: the fault, your checks in order, and the fix.

## One change at a time
With a partner's computer problem on paper, list three possible checks. Put them in an order where each step changes only one thing.
