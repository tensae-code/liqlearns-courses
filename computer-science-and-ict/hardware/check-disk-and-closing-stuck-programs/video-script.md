# Video script — Check Disk and closing stuck programs

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Two tools for two troubles.

The disk hides errors. Check Disk finds them.
Right-click the C drive. Properties. Check.
Every week — even when all looks well.

A program will not close?
File menu first. Close. Exit.
Still stuck? Three keys — End Task.
Gone at once.
