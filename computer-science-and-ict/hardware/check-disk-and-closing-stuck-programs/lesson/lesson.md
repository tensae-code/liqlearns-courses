# Check Disk and closing stuck programs

## Do it first

Two jobs: a disk that may carry hidden errors, and a program stuck open that will not close from its own menu. Write a tool or route for each — then work the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Check Disk — fix errors before they show

**Check Disk fixes errors on the computer** and **should be run weekly, even with no visible problems**. The route: **right-click the C drive**, choose **Properties**, then the **Check button** to start the **scan wizard**.

[Picture 31311 — a drive being scanned with a tick, beside a stuck program being ended]

## Sub lesson 2 — Closing a halted program — the second way

A halted or unwanted program is **best closed by its File menu — Close/Exit**. But there is a **second way**: press **Ctrl+Alt+Delete together** to bring up **Task Manager / the Close Program dialog**, **choose the program** and press **End Task** — and it **closes at once**. (The student book's Task Manager figures are laid out in swapped order; this lesson teaches the route in its correct order.)

## Say it to your companion

Give your companion the Check Disk route, then both ways to close a stuck program. They check the order of each.
