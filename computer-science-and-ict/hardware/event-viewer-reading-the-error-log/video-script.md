The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. Short script; the placement names no full video need for this lesson.

---

Windows keeps a diary of its troubles.
Its name: Event Viewer.

Search box. Type Event Viewer. Open it.

Three logs do most of the work:
Application. System. Security.

Every event tells six things:
what happened, when, how bad,
where from, its ID number,
and who was logged in.

Double-click. Read it all.
Still stuck? Search the ID online.
