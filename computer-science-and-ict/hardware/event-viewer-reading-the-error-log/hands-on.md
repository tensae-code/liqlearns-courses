# Hands-on — Event Viewer — reading the error log

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Open the log
On a school computer: open Event Viewer by the book's three steps. Find the Windows Logs. Count the events in one log and read the date and time of the newest one aloud.

## Read one event in full
Double-click one event. On paper, write its six facts in the lesson's order. If your log is empty, write what each fact WOULD say for a made-up crash your teacher describes.
