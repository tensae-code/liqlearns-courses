# Installing an Optical Drive — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Where must the optical drive's bay open?**
a) To the case front  b) It slides in from the front until its face is flush with the case  c) So the tray opens freely and its buttons can be reached  d) Screwed firm on its sides
**Answer: a**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How does the drive enter its bay?**
a) So the tray opens freely and its buttons can be reached  b) It slides in from the front until its face is flush with the case  c) Screwed firm on its sides  d) Power from the power supply and data to the board
**Answer: b**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why flush?**
a) Screwed firm on its sides  b) Power from the power supply and data to the board  c) So the tray opens freely and its buttons can be reached  d) A thin audio lead to the sound card
**Answer: c**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How is it fixed?**
a) Power from the power supply and data to the board  b) A thin audio lead to the sound card  c) The tray opens and closes at the button  d) Screwed firm on its sides
**Answer: d**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What two leads does it share with the hard disk?**
a) Power from the power supply and data to the board  b) A thin audio lead to the sound card  c) The tray opens and closes at the button  d) The machine cannot see the drive
**Answer: a**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What third lead did drives of this era commonly take?**
a) The tray opens and closes at the button  b) A thin audio lead to the sound card  c) The machine cannot see the drive  d) Laos Grade 8 ICT, Chapter 2 §2.7
**Answer: b**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is the tray check?**
a) The machine cannot see the drive  b) Laos Grade 8 ICT, Chapter 2 §2.7  c) The tray opens and closes at the button  d) The mounting — its position in the bay
**Answer: c**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What announces a lead fault?**
a) Laos Grade 8 ICT, Chapter 2 §2.7  b) The mounting — its position in the bay  c) To the case front  d) The machine cannot see the drive
**Answer: d**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which chapter teaches this?**
a) Laos Grade 8 ICT, Chapter 2 §2.7  b) The mounting — its position in the bay  c) To the case front  d) It slides in from the front until its face is flush with the case
**Answer: a**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does a jammed tray point back to?**
a) To the case front  b) The mounting — its position in the bay  c) It slides in from the front until its face is flush with the case  d) So the tray opens freely and its buttons can be reached
**Answer: b**
