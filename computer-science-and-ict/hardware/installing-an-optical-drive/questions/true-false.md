# Installing an Optical Drive — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: To the case front**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: So the tray opens freely and its buttons can be reached** — as the answer to: How does the drive enter its bay?
**Answer: False — It slides in from the front until its face is flush with the case**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: So the tray opens freely and its buttons can be reached**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Power from the power supply and data to the board** — as the answer to: How is it fixed?
**Answer: False — Screwed firm on its sides**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Power from the power supply and data to the board**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: The tray opens and closes at the button** — as the answer to: What third lead did drives of this era commonly take?
**Answer: False — A thin audio lead to the sound card**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: The tray opens and closes at the button**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Laos Grade 8 ICT, Chapter 2 §2.7** — as the answer to: What announces a lead fault?
**Answer: False — The machine cannot see the drive**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Laos Grade 8 ICT, Chapter 2 §2.7**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: To the case front** — as the answer to: What does a jammed tray point back to?
**Answer: False — The mounting — its position in the bay**
