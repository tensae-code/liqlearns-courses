# Installing an Optical Drive — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Where must the optical drive's bay open?** → **To the case front**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) How does the drive enter its bay?** → **It slides in from the front until its face is flush with the case**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Why flush?** → **So the tray opens freely and its buttons can be reached**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) How is it fixed?** → **Screwed firm on its sides**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What two leads does it share with the hard disk?** → **Power from the power supply and data to the board**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What third lead did drives of this era commonly take?** → **A thin audio lead to the sound card**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is the tray check?** → **The tray opens and closes at the button**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What announces a lead fault?** → **The machine cannot see the drive**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which chapter teaches this?** → **Laos Grade 8 ICT, Chapter 2 §2.7**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What does a jammed tray point back to?** → **The mounting — its position in the bay**
