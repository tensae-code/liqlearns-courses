# Video script — Installing an Optical Drive

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The optical drive mounts in a **bay that opens to the case front**: the drive slides in **from the front** until its face is **flush with the case**, so its **tray can open and close freely** and its buttons can be reached. It is **screwed firm on its sides** like the hard disk. A drive mounted one bay too deep or too proud is a tray that jams or a face that spoils the case — position is part of the install.
Behind, the drive takes the same **two leads** as the hard disk — **power** from the power supply and **data** to the board, each one way, without force — and in this era commonly a thin **audio lead** to the sound card, so a music disc's sound reaches the speakers. Laos's check: tray opens and closes at the button, and at first use the machine **sees the drive and reads a disc**. A drive the machine cannot see sends the installer back to the leads; a tray that cannot open sends them back to the mounting.
