# Software Faults — Their Causes and Fixing Them — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the five fault families by layer.** → **Application, system, malware, capacity, settings faults**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is the largest innocent family?** → **Settings faults — the wrong option faithfully obeyed**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name two application-fault causes.** → **Corrupt files; bugs (or a version at war with the system)**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Name two system-fault causes.** → **Damaged system files; driver troubles (or too much starting at once)**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why name causes beside faults?** → **The fix for a bug is not the fix for a full disk**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name the fixing order.** → **Restart, update, check capacity/settings, scan, repair/reinstall, restore**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Why is restart the honest first test?** → **Half of software's ghosts are states, not faults**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) When is restore used?** → **When the fault survives repair and reinstall**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Name four maintenance habits.** → **Regular updates, disk cleaning, backup habits, removing the unused**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the best fix, in the closing phrase?** → **The fault that never happens**
