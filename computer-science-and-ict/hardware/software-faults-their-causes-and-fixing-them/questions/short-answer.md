# Software Faults — Their Causes and Fixing Them — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the five fault families by layer.**
**Answer: Application, system, malware, capacity, settings faults**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is the largest innocent family?**
**Answer: Settings faults — the wrong option faithfully obeyed**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Name two application-fault causes.**
**Answer: Corrupt files; bugs (or a version at war with the system)**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Name two system-fault causes.**
**Answer: Damaged system files; driver troubles (or too much starting at once)**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Why name causes beside faults?**
**Answer: The fix for a bug is not the fix for a full disk**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name the fixing order.**
**Answer: Restart, update, check capacity/settings, scan, repair/reinstall, restore**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Why is restart the honest first test?**
**Answer: Half of software's ghosts are states, not faults**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) When is restore used?**
**Answer: When the fault survives repair and reinstall**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Name four maintenance habits.**
**Answer: Regular updates, disk cleaning, backup habits, removing the unused**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the best fix, in the closing phrase?**
**Answer: The fault that never happens**
