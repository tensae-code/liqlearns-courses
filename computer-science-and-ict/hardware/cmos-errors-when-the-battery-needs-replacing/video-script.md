# Video script — CMOS errors — when the battery needs replacing

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. Short script; the placement names no full video need for this lesson.

---

The clock is wrong. Again.
An alert at start-up says: CMOS.

Inside, a small chip remembers —
the time, the date, the settings.
Its battery has died.

The fix: switch off.
Lift the old battery out, carefully.
Fit a new one — exactly the same.

A laptop? Harder.
No experience? Stop.
A technician does this job.
