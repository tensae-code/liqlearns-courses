# CMOS errors — when the battery needs replacing — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A dying CMOS battery can make the machine forget the time and date.
**Answer: True** — CMOS stores them, kept alive by the battery.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Replacing a CMOS battery in a laptop is easier than in a desktop.
**Answer: False** — laptops are harder than desktops.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A person without enough troubleshooting experience should leave this job to a professional technician.
**Answer: True** — the book's second caution.
