# Hands-on — CMOS errors — when the battery needs replacing

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Battery out, battery in — on paper
Draw a motherboard corner with a round battery holder. Number the doing: OFF, old battery out carefully, new battery — exactly the same — in. Under it, write both cautions.

## Desktop or laptop?
Sort six job cards your teacher gives (or write your own): CMOS battery in a desktop, CMOS battery in a laptop, by a first-timer, by an experienced pupil. Mark each: do it, or hand it to a technician — and say why.
