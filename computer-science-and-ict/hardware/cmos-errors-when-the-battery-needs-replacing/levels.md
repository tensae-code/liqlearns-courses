## Sub lesson 2 — Replacing the battery — the doing
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: the learner can state and use what this part teaches — A CMOS alert on screen means the battery needs replacing: remove the old battery carefully and fit a new one exactly the same. Cautions: laptops are harder than desktops; without enough experience, leave the job to a professional technician.
