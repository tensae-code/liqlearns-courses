# Hands-on — Reading Component Data Books and Datasheets

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Read a sheet
Take any datasheet (printed or on screen). Find and read out: the maximum voltage, one current rating, and the job of pin 1.

## Match the replacement
You are given an old component and two possible replacements with different ratings. Use the data-book values to pick the match and defend the pick.
