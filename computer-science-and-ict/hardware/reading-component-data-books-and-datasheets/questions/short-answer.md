# Reading Component Data Books and Datasheets — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A data book gives information about what?**
**Answer: Electronic components**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What is another name for a component's data sheet?**
**Answer: A datasheet**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What ratings does a datasheet list?**
**Answer: Voltage, current and other limits**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does a datasheet show about a component's pins?**
**Answer: What each pin is for and how to connect it**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) Who writes data books?**
**Answer: The component's manufacturer**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Why check a component's maximum voltage?**
**Answer: So the component is not damaged**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) Before replacing a component, what do you check?**
**Answer: That its data-book values match**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What can happen beyond a component's rated limits?**
**Answer: The component can fail or be damaged**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Data book or guess — which does a builder use?**
**Answer: Always the data book**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) When is the data book used in Papua New Guinea's lessons?**
**Answer: When setting up computer electronic systems**
