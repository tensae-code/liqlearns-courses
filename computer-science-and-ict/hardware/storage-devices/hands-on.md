# Hands-on — Storage devices — keeping data

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Desk and cupboard
List what is on your study desk right now and what is stored away at home. Write the computer parallel beside each list: primary storage beside the desk, secondary beside the cupboard.

## Now or for good?
A friend saves: (a) the sum the CPU is working on this second; (b) a photo to keep for years; (c) the instructions of the program running now. Label each primary or secondary, and say why.
