# Installing the Motherboard — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, expressed as its SUB LESSONS. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Stand-offs and alignment — the board must not touch the case
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Stand-offs — small metal spacers; Its underside circuits must touch nothing conductive; A short circuit waiting; The board's rear ports through the case's opening; A few turns each, across the board, before any is tightened.

## Sub lesson 2 — Screwing down — firm, even, not forced
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: It would pull the board crooked; Cracks the board or strips the stand-off; The power supply's leads to the board's sockets; Laos Grade 8 ICT, Chapter 2 §2.4; Board flat, every screw firm, ports showing, nothing trapped underneath.
