# Installing the Motherboard — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What holds the board off the case wall?** → **Stand-offs — small metal spacers**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Why must the board not touch the case?** → **Its underside circuits must touch nothing conductive**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is a spare stand-off under a hole-less part?** → **A short circuit waiting**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What must align at the case back?** → **The board's rear ports through the case's opening**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How are the screws driven?** → **A few turns each, across the board, before any is tightened**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Why not tighten the first screw fully at once?** → **It would pull the board crooked**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What does over-driving a screw do?** → **Cracks the board or strips the stand-off**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What is connected after mounting?** → **The power supply's leads to the board's sockets**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Which chapter teaches this?** → **Laos Grade 8 ICT, Chapter 2 §2.4**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What is the closing check?** → **Board flat, every screw firm, ports showing, nothing trapped underneath**
