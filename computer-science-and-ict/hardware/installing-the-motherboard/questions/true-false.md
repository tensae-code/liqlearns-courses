# Installing the Motherboard — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Stand-offs — small metal spacers**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: A short circuit waiting** — as the answer to: Why must the board not touch the case?
**Answer: False — Its underside circuits must touch nothing conductive**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: A short circuit waiting**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: A few turns each, across the board, before any is tightened** — as the answer to: What must align at the case back?
**Answer: False — The board's rear ports through the case's opening**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: A few turns each, across the board, before any is tightened**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Cracks the board or strips the stand-off** — as the answer to: Why not tighten the first screw fully at once?
**Answer: False — It would pull the board crooked**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Cracks the board or strips the stand-off**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Laos Grade 8 ICT, Chapter 2 §2.4** — as the answer to: What is connected after mounting?
**Answer: False — The power supply's leads to the board's sockets**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Laos Grade 8 ICT, Chapter 2 §2.4**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Stand-offs — small metal spacers** — as the answer to: What is the closing check?
**Answer: False — Board flat, every screw firm, ports showing, nothing trapped underneath**
