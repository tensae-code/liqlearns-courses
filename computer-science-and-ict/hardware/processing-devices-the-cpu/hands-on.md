# Hands-on — Processing devices — the CPU, the brain of the computer

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Follow the flow
On a real or drawn system, trace one keystroke's journey with a finger: keyboard, into the box (CPU), out to the screen. Say 'processed here' at the middle stop.

## Cycles count
Clap once per second — that is 1 Hz of clapping. A CPU's cycles run in the millions and billions per second. Discuss what 'speed in hertz' lets you compare between two machines.
