# Hands-on — Typing With Correct Fingers on the Home Row

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Find home blind** — Rest your fingers on the home row, close your eyes, and say each finger's starting keys aloud. Then type your name ten times without looking down.

## Task two
**Trainer session** — Spend ten minutes in a typing trainer on home-row lessons. Record your speed and accuracy, and which finger made the most mistakes.
