# Typing With Correct Fingers on the Home Row — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Where do the fingers rest when typing correctly?**
**Answer: On the home row — the middle row of letter keys**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Which keys carry the small bumps?**
**Answer: F and J — the index fingers find them by touch**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Where do the thumbs rest?**
**Answer: Over the space bar**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What does each finger own?**
**Answer: Its starting keys, and the keys above and below them that it reaches for**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What does a finger do after reaching for a key?**
**Answer: It returns home to its starting key**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does typing-trainer software do?**
**Answer: It drills finger placement, shows which finger strikes each letter, and measures speed and accuracy**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is the trainer's order?**
**Answer: Placement first, accuracy second, speed last**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) Why must speed come last?**
**Answer: Speed built on wrong fingers must be unlearned; speed on correct placement only grows**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) How should practice be scheduled?**
**Answer: Short daily practice, not one long session**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What two numbers does the trainer report?**
**Answer: Typing speed and accuracy**
