# Frequently Used Sequential Circuits — matching

Match each item on the left with the one it belongs to on the right.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a register?** → **A row of flip-flops holding one word — loaded and read together**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What sizes do registers come in?** → **8, 16, 32 or 64 bits**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does a shift register do at each clock pulse?** → **It moves its bits one place along the row**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What arithmetic is shifting, in the book's link?** → **Multiplication by 2 (left) — the shift of the arithmetic lessons**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What bridge does a shift register build?** → **Between parallel words and serial streams — for transmission**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does a counter do?** → **It counts pulses in binary — its state advances by one at each pulse**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is each flip-flop in a counter?** → **One binary place of the count**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does a counter do to frequency?** → **It divides it — each stage pulses at half the previous rate**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Name two jobs of counters.** → **Timing events and sequencing operations (also: frequency division)**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What ladder does the chapter close with?** → **Gates → functions → combinational circuits → memory → registers and counters → the computer's architecture**
