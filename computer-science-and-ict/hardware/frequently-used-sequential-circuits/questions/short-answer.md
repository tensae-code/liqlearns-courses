# Frequently Used Sequential Circuits — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is a register?**
**Answer: A row of flip-flops holding one word — loaded and read together**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What sizes do registers come in?**
**Answer: 8, 16, 32 or 64 bits**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What does a shift register do at each clock pulse?**
**Answer: It moves its bits one place along the row**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) What arithmetic is shifting, in the book's link?**
**Answer: Multiplication by 2 (left) — the shift of the arithmetic lessons**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) What bridge does a shift register build?**
**Answer: Between parallel words and serial streams — for transmission**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) What does a counter do?**
**Answer: It counts pulses in binary — its state advances by one at each pulse**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What is each flip-flop in a counter?**
**Answer: One binary place of the count**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does a counter do to frequency?**
**Answer: It divides it — each stage pulses at half the previous rate**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) Name two jobs of counters.**
**Answer: Timing events and sequencing operations (also: frequency division)**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What ladder does the chapter close with?**
**Answer: Gates → functions → combinational circuits → memory → registers and counters → the computer's architecture**
