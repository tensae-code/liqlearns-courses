# Frequently Used Sequential Circuits — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: A row of flip-flops holding one word — loaded and read together**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: A row of flip-flops holding one word — loaded and read together** — as the answer to: What sizes do registers come in?
**Answer: False — 8, 16, 32 or 64 bits**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: It moves its bits one place along the row**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: It moves its bits one place along the row** — as the answer to: What arithmetic is shifting, in the book's link?
**Answer: False — Multiplication by 2 (left) — the shift of the arithmetic lessons**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Between parallel words and serial streams — for transmission**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Between parallel words and serial streams — for transmission** — as the answer to: What does a counter do?
**Answer: False — It counts pulses in binary — its state advances by one at each pulse**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: One binary place of the count**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: One binary place of the count** — as the answer to: What does a counter do to frequency?
**Answer: False — It divides it — each stage pulses at half the previous rate**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Timing events and sequencing operations (also: frequency division)**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Timing events and sequencing operations (also: frequency division)** — as the answer to: What ladder does the chapter close with?
**Answer: False — Gates → functions → combinational circuits → memory → registers and counters → the computer's architecture**
