# Hands-on — Frequently Used Sequential Circuits

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Count in bits** — Write the first ten states of a 4-bit binary counter and mark which flip-flop changes at every pulse, and which at every eighth.

## Task two
**Register roles** — For a processor adding two numbers, list the registers it needs (two operands, one result) and trace a shift register turning 1011 into a serial stream.
