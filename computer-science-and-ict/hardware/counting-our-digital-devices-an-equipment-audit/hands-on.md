# Hands-on — Counting Our Digital Devices — An Equipment Audit

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
**Home audit** — Audit your home (or one room, honestly bounded). List every device with working / kept / duplicate / age. Bring the count — compare it with your guess.

## Task two
**Class fleet** — Audit the classroom's digital equipment as a class. Present the four views and one decision the audit suggests.
