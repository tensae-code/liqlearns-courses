# Counting Our Digital Devices — An Equipment Audit — short answer

Answer in a short sentence of your own.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does an equipment audit list?**
**Answer: Every digital device in a place**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Name four things the audit includes beyond phones and computers.**
**Answer: Tablets, televisions, consoles, printers (also: routers, connected objects)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What else must be counted?**
**Answer: The broken, the drawer-bound and the forgotten**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) Why count before weighing the footprint?**
**Answer: You cannot weigh the footprint of devices you have not counted**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) How do households usually count?**
**Answer: They undercount their own shelves**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Name the four views of a finished audit.**
**Answer: How many work; how many are kept just in case; how many duplicate a job; how old the fleet is**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) What four decisions can the audit feed?**
**Answer: Repair, share, pass on, recycle**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) What does the audit give the environmental discussion?**
**Answer: Its facts**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) What is the cost of digital life, in this lesson?**
**Answer: This list, in this room — not an abstraction**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) What duplicates should be spotted?**
**Answer: Devices doing the same job**
