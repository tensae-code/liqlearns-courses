# Counting Our Digital Devices — An Equipment Audit — true or false

Say true or false, and fix the false ones in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Every digital device in a place**
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: Every digital device in a place** — as the answer to: Name four things the audit includes beyond phones and computers.
**Answer: False — Tablets, televisions, consoles, printers (also: routers, connected objects)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: The broken, the drawer-bound and the forgotten**
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: The broken, the drawer-bound and the forgotten** — as the answer to: Why count before weighing the footprint?
**Answer: False — You cannot weigh the footprint of devices you have not counted**

**5. (Sub lesson 1) True or false: They undercount their own shelves**
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: They undercount their own shelves** — as the answer to: Name the four views of a finished audit.
**Answer: False — How many work; how many are kept just in case; how many duplicate a job; how old the fleet is**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Repair, share, pass on, recycle**
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: Repair, share, pass on, recycle** — as the answer to: What does the audit give the environmental discussion?
**Answer: False — Its facts**

**9. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: This list, in this room — not an abstraction**
**Answer: True**

**10. (Sub lesson 2) True or false: This list, in this room — not an abstraction** — as the answer to: What duplicates should be spotted?
**Answer: False — Devices doing the same job**
