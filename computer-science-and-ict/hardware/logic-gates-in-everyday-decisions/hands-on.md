# Hands-on — Logic Gates in Everyday Decisions

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task one
('Gate one real rule', 'Take one real rule from your school or home, write it as gates with named inputs, and build its full truth table.')

## Task two
('Find the missing row', "Exchange tables with a partner and hunt for the input combination their rule never considered. Say what their rule outputs there — and whether it should.")
