# Hands-on — Computer hardware — four categories

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Real-device sort
In a lab or at home, handle each device you can safely touch and place it in its category by what it does to data. Say the category aloud as you set it down.

## Four-category walk
Walk round a computer room and count one device per category, then find a second device for each. No device may be counted twice.
