# Video script — Computer hardware — four categories

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Computer hardware.**

First, brainstorm: what do you already know about this? Say it out loud.

Now the one thing: hardware is the physical part of a computer you can see and touch, divided by the flow of information into input, processing, storage and output devices.

**Hardware — what you can touch.** Hardware is the physical part of a computer that can be seen and touched.
**Input devices — data enters, changed into 0 and 1.** Input devices enter data into the computer, converting it into the 0-and-1 form the computer understands.
**How this unit is taught (teacher guide — evidence).** The teacher guide fixes how this unit is evidenced: every family — input, processing, storage, output — is taught in the laboratory or with the real devices brought into class, in groups, with a representative reporting back; and a pupil must be able to explain, for input, the mouse, the keyboard, optical mark recognition and optical character recognition, and for output, the monitor, printer, headphones, speakers, projector, braille reader and plotter (teacher guide — evidence).

Now you: Draw a desktop computer with its parts colour-coded into the four categories.
