# Computer hardware — four categories — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Hardware is —
a) The programs in a computer  b) The physical part of a computer that can be seen and touched  c) The electricity supply  d) The internet
**Answer: b) The physical part of a computer that can be seen and touched** — Hardware is the physical part that can be seen and touched.

**2.** Hardware is divided into four categories by —
a) Price  b) The information-processing flow  c) Colour  d) Size of shop
**Answer: b) The information-processing flow** — The categories follow the flow: input, processing, storage, output.

**3.** Input devices convert entered data into —
a) Pictures  b) The 0-and-1 form the computer understands  c) Sound  d) Paper
**Answer: b) The 0-and-1 form the computer understands** — Input devices convert data into the 0-and-1 electronic form.

**4.** Which of these is in the unit's input list?
a) Printer  b) Joystick  c) Monitor  d) Hard disk
**Answer: b) Joystick** — The list: keyboard, mouse, scanner, optical recognition, speech recognition, joystick, light pen, touch screen.

**5.** Output devices turn the computer's form back into — (teacher guide — evidence)
a) More 0s and 1s  b) A human-understandable soft or hard copy  c) Electricity  d) Heat
**Answer: b) A human-understandable soft or hard copy** — Output converts the computer's form into a human-understandable soft or hard copy (teacher guide — evidence).
