# Video script — DA and AD Converters

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Numbers live inside the machine. Voltages live outside. Two circuits guard the border.

The DAC walks numbers out: give it a binary value, it returns the matching voltage.

Half the counting range, half the voltage. The output climbs in equal steps — a staircase.

Inside, an R–2R ladder lets each bit add its own weight: a half, a quarter, an eighth.

The ADC walks signals in. It samples, it quantises, it codes — the digitising lesson as a circuit.

Its bits are its fineness: 8 bits, 256 levels. 16 bits, 65 536.

Fast signals need speed. Fine differences need bits. The signal chooses the converter.
