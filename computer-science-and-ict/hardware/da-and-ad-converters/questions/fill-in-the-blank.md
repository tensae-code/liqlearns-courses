# DA and AD Converters — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) The circuit that turns a binary number into a proportional voltage is a ____ converter.**
**Answer: DA (digital-to-analogue, DAC)** — DA walks numbers out to the analogue world; AD walks signals in.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) In an R–2R ladder, if the top bit contributes ½ of full scale, the next bit contributes ____.**
**Answer: ¼ (a quarter)** — Each bit contributes half the weight of the bit above it — the place values.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) The fineness of an ADC, set by its bit count, is called its ____.**
**Answer: resolution** — Resolution in bits fixes the 2ⁿ levels of quantisation.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) In a flash ADC, the components that each test the input against one level boundary are called ____.**
**Answer: comparators** — One comparator per boundary, all judging at the same instant.
