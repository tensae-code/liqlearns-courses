# Lesson plan — Steps 2–6 — test a scenario, fix, verify, document

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Hardware · **Lesson:** Steps 2–6 — test a scenario, fix, verify, document
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 5 — Maintenance and Troubleshooting (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** After identification, network troubleshooting repeats one loop: build a scenario, test it, plan, implement, verify — and document everything, so the next fault is shorter.
**This lesson's own level:** It stands on Step 1 — identify the network problem: this lesson is the rest of the same method.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or in steps — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
1. **Steps 2–3 — scenario, test, plan** — the working explanation; testing it with tools and swaps; the plan, short or long.
2. **Steps 4–6 — implement, verify, document** — implement alone or with others; verify, watching for new issues; document symptoms, scenario, test and solution.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Step 1 — identify the network problem, in this same title. Recommended, never forced — a learner may land on this lesson directly, and it stands alone.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Develop and test a scenario for a network fault.
2. Make a plan of action, short or long.
3. Implement, verify — watching for new issues — and document the issue fully.

## If the learner is stuck
- Treats a disproved scenario as wasted work → the lesson's rule: proving your theory wrong sends you to a better one; it is the method working.
- Skips documenting → the record is what shortens the NEXT fault's downtime; an undocumented fix helps once only.
