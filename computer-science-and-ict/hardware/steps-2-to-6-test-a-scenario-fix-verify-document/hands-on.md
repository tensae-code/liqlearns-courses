# Hands-on — Steps 2–6 — test a scenario, fix, verify, document

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Full-loop case
Take a real or invented lab fault. Write it through all the steps: identification summary, scenario, test, plan, implement, verify, document — one honest line each.

## Router swap rehearsal
On paper, plan the router-swap test: what you record before, what result proves the scenario, what result kills it, and the new scenario each result sends you to.
