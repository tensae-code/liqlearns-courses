# Steps 2–6 — test a scenario, fix, verify, document — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A scenario is —
a) the final report  b) a working explanation of the fault  c) a new cable  d) the provider's bill
**Answer: b) a working explanation of the fault**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Testing a scenario for a suspected router means —
a) buying three routers  b) swapping in another router and seeing  c) restarting every computer  d) waiting a day
**Answer: b) swapping in another router and seeing**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Proving your own theory wrong is —
a) failure — stop  b) not failure — it sends you back to develop a new scenario  c) cheating  d) documentation
**Answer: b) not failure — it sends you back to develop a new scenario**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** A plan of action can be —
a) only long  b) one step, like restarting the router, or a longer plan, like ordering a part  c) written after the fix only  d) kept secret
**Answer: b) one step, like restarting the router, or a longer plan, like ordering a part**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Verification watches for —
a) the issue resolved — and new issues your changes caused  b) the time  c) other pupils  d) nothing
**Answer: a) the issue resolved — and new issues your changes caused**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Documentation records —
a) symptoms, scenario, test strategy and solution  b) names of pupils  c) the weather  d) only the solution
**Answer: a) symptoms, scenario, test strategy and solution**
