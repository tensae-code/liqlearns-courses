# Steps 2–6 — test a scenario, fix, verify, document

## Do it first

Step 1 named the problem: the lab loses its network whenever the old router warms up. Write what a troubleshooter does next — five more moves — then compare with steps 2 to 6.

## Sub lesson 1 — Steps 2–3 — scenario, test, plan

2. **Develop a scenario** — a **working explanation**; sometimes as simple as **a tripped wire**.
3. **Test the scenario with your tools** — for a suspected defective router, **swap in another router and see**. **Proving your own theory wrong is not failure** — it sends you back to develop a new one.
4. **Make a plan of action** — one step, such as **restarting the router**, or a longer plan, such as **ordering a part** or **rolling software back on many machines**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Steps 4–6 — implement, verify, document

5. **Implement the solution**, alone or with other experts and users.
6. **Verify system functionality** — the issue resolved, and **watch for new issues your changes caused**.
7. **Document the issue** — **symptoms, scenario, test strategy and solution** — so it can **shorten future downtime** for other technicians, students or users.

## Say it to your companion

Walk your companion from scenario to document. They check: did verification watch for new issues? Did the document carry all four parts?
