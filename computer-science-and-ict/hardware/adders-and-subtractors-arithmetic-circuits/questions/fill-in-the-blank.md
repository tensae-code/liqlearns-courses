# Adders and Subtractors — Arithmetic Circuits — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) The half adder's Carry is the ____ of its two inputs.**
**Answer: AND** — Carry = A · B: 1 only when both bits are 1.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) A full adder has three inputs: A, B and ____.**
**Answer: Cin (the carry-in)** — The carry arriving from the column on the right.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) In the adder/subtractor, one control line named ____ chooses between adding and subtracting.**
**Answer: SUB** — SUB = 0 add, SUB = 1 subtract.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) An X ⊕ 0 gives X, and X ⊕ 1 gives ____ — the two faces the SUB steering relies on.**
**Answer: X̄ (inverted X)** — XOR with 0 passes, XOR with 1 inverts — a controllable inverter.
