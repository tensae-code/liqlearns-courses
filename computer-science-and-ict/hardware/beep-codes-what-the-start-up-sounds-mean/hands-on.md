# Hands-on — Beep codes — what the start-up sounds mean

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Tap the code
With a partner: one taps beep patterns on the desk — short tap, long press of a finger. The other writes the pattern as bars and names the fault. Five patterns each, then swap.

## One stick at a time
On paper, draw a machine with two RAM sticks, A and B. Write the test order that finds the bad stick with the fewest removals, following the book's method.
