# Video script — Beep codes — what the start-up sounds mean

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. Short script; the placement names no full video need for this lesson.

---

One short beep. All is well. A normal boot.

No beep, but the machine runs?
The beeper itself may be dead.
No beep at all? Power supply —
unplugged, off, or dead.

Short beeps, again and again: power supply bad.
Long beeps, again and again: power supply gone bad.

One long beep that never stops: RAM has failed.
Two sticks? Take one out at a time. Find the bad one.

One long, two short: the video card.
Push it firmly back in its slot first.
Still faulty? Replace it.

Note the exact pattern. Search it. Your BIOS speaks its own code.
