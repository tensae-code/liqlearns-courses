# Hands-on — Boolean Algebra and Logic Operations

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Tables from memory
Draw the truth tables of NOT, OR, AND and XOR on paper without looking. Then write under each its saying: 'flips it', 'at least one', 'all', 'different'. Check against the lesson and fix any row you missed — the row everyone misses is OR's 1 + 1 = 1.

## Build a combined table
Build the full truth table of Y = Ā · B + A · B̄, column by column. When it is done, compare its final column with the XOR table. They should match exactly — you have just discovered that XOR can be built from NOT, AND and OR, a trick the gates lesson uses again.

## In the simulator
In TINA or Circuit Wizard, place switches and a lamp and wire (or select) the AND and OR behaviours. Run through all four switch combinations for each and write the lamp's state as a truth table. The simulator's table and your paper table must agree row for row.
