# Video script — Boolean Algebra and Logic Operations

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Some things have only two states. On or off. 1 or 0.

Boolean algebra is the mathematics of those two states. Nothing else exists in it.

NOT flips: 0 becomes 1, 1 becomes 0.

OR asks: is at least one input 1? Then the answer is 1.

AND asks: are all inputs 1? Only then is the answer 1.

XOR asks: are the inputs different? Different gives 1. The same gives 0 — like a staircase light.

A truth table lists every possible input, and the answer for each. It is the whole operation, complete.
