# Boolean Algebra and Logic Operations — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Logical addition is the operation called ____.**
**Answer: OR** — Written A + B and read 'A or B' — at least one input 1 gives 1.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) The negation of 1 is ____.**
**Answer: 0** — NOT flips the value, so NOT 1 = 0.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) 1 ⊕ 1 (XOR) equals ____.**
**Answer: 0** — The inputs are the same, and XOR answers 1 only for different inputs.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) An expression with 3 inputs needs a truth table of ____ rows.**
**Answer: 8** — 2³ = 8 rows, from 000 to 111.
