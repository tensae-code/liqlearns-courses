# Lesson plan — LED and gas plasma monitors

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Hardware · **Lesson:** LED and gas plasma monitors
**Place in the curriculum:** Title 2 — Hardware of the Computer Science and ICT ladder (TITLES.md); Ethiopia Grade 8, Unit 2 — Computer Hardware.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the LED monitor gives great colour and image quality in a lightweight flat screen, while the older gas plasma monitor makes its pixels by glowing neon gas.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the CRT and LCD lesson: the last two of the four monitor types.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks LED means slow and poor → LED gives GREAT colour and image quality; its weakness named in the book is response time.
- Thinks plasma uses liquid crystal → plasma uses neon GAS instead of liquid crystal; the gas glows to make the pixels.
