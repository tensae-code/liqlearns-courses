# Hands-on — LED and gas plasma monitors

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Pixel close-up
Look very closely (or with a magnifier, if available) at a phone or TV screen showing white. The picture is dots — pixels. On a plasma screen each dot was a tiny cell of glowing gas; discuss the idea with a partner.

## Screen survey
List the screens in your home: for each, guess its type from its age and build — bulky (CRT), flat and common (LCD), slim with rich colour (LED), or an old flat portable screen (plasma). Defend each guess.
