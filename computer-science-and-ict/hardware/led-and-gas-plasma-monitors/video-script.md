**LED and gas plasma monitors.**

First, brainstorm: what do you already know about this? Say it out loud.

Now the one thing: the LED monitor gives great colour and image quality in a lightweight flat screen, while the older gas plasma monitor makes its pixels by glowing neon gas.

**The LED monitor.** An LED monitor — Light Emitting Diode, sometimes called LED-backlit — gives great colour and image quality, but not a good response time.
**The gas plasma monitor.** A gas plasma monitor uses neon gas instead of liquid crystal.

Now you: Draw a slim LED screen beside glowing gas-filled pixel cells.
