# LED and gas plasma monitors

## Do it first

Two flat screens glow in a shop. One's light comes from tiny solid lamps; the other's picture is made of gas that glows, cell by cell. Before reading on, say which idea sounds newer — and what each screen might be best at.

## The LED monitor

An LED monitor — Light Emitting Diode, sometimes called LED-backlit — gives great colour and image quality, but not a good response time. It is a lightweight flat screen, now used across laptops, mobile phones, TVs, computer monitors and tablets.

## The gas plasma monitor

A gas plasma monitor uses neon gas instead of liquid crystal. The gas glows to produce the pixels or dots that form the picture. Gas plasma was used in older portable computers and televisions.
