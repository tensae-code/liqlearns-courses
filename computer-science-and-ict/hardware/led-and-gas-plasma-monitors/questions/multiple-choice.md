# LED and gas plasma monitors — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** LED stands for —
a) Light Emitting Diode  b) Large Electronic Display  c) Liquid Emitting Device  d) Low Energy Dial
**Answer: a) Light Emitting Diode** — LED = Light Emitting Diode, sometimes called LED-backlit.

**2.** An LED monitor gives —
a) Poor colour but fast response  b) Great colour and image quality, but not a good response time  c) No image at all  d) Only black and white
**Answer: b) Great colour and image quality, but not a good response time** — Great colour and image quality, but not a good response time.

**3.** A gas plasma monitor uses — instead of liquid crystal.
a) Mercury  b) Neon gas  c) Water  d) Oil
**Answer: b) Neon gas** — A gas plasma monitor uses neon gas instead of liquid crystal.

**4.** In a plasma monitor, the picture's pixels are produced by —
a) Ink  b) The gas glowing  c) A spinning disk  d) Printed dots
**Answer: b) The gas glowing** — The gas glows to produce the pixels or dots that form the picture.

**5.** Gas plasma monitors were used in —
a) Modern smartphones only  b) Older portable computers and televisions  c) Printers  d) Keyboards
**Answer: b) Older portable computers and televisions** — Gas plasma was used in older portable computers and televisions.
