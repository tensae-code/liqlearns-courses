# Lesson plan — The CPU — the brain of the computer

**Course:** Computer Science and ICT · **Title:** Hardware · **Lesson:** The CPU — the brain of the computer
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 2 — Computer Hardware — in the title Hardware on the ICT course ladder (TITLES.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The CPU is the brain of the computer, doing all arithmetic, logical and control operations, and it has three components: the arithmetic-logic unit, the control unit and registers.
**This lesson's own level:** It stands on the input lessons: data now gets in; this lesson opens the family that does the processing.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what CPU stands for, and why it is called the brain of the computer.
2. Name the operations the CPU does: arithmetic, logical and control.
3. Name its three components: ALU, control unit, registers.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil.
