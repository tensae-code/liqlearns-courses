**Acceptance count: taught in 10 countries (Ethiopia, Afghanistan, Yemen, Sudan, Sri Lanka, Laos, Greece, Moldova, Indonesia, Pakistan).**

Yemen's own way (Grade 10): a skills ladder — every unit alternates short teaching topics with a named تدريب (guided training) and نشاط (activity), and Windows itself is taught as a school subject for a full unit before any application is opened.

Afghanistan's own way (Grade 10): a Windows XP-era office apprenticeship — one full chapter per application, taught menu by menu (File, Edit, Insert, Format), with the Dari keyboard language added as its own lesson; networks arrive only in the final chapter.

No book error touches this lesson's content.

Sudan's own way (Grade 5): a short, highly practical Arabic primary series that revises the previous grade first, then works entirely inside Windows and its built-in apps (Calculator, Paint) before reaching Word — every lesson ends with hands-on activities and each unit with an assessment.

Sri Lanka's own way (Grade 6 Reading Book): a story/dialogue-led "Reading Book" with a talking computer character; every chapter cross-refers to numbered Workbook activities; local sectors (school office, ATM, ICU, factories, agriculture) and the 3R e-waste method are taught from Grade 6.

Sri Lanka's own way (Grade 7 Reading Book): the CPU is taught through a hotel-kitchen/chef analogy; programme development (decomposition, flowcharts, variables, bugs) precedes presentation software; HTML page-building is already in Grade 7.

Sri Lanka's own way (Grade 7 Workbook): a companion workbook mirroring the Reading Book chapter-for-chapter (CPU, OS, security, word processing, program development, presentations, internet).

Laos's own way (Grade 7 ICT student book): the hardware year is taught through the von Neumann model (the book pictures John von Neumann beside an early machine) and the software chapter ends with a full comparison table of real compilers (Visual Basic, Turbo Pascal, C++, Fortran…) by platform and licence.

Greece's own way (Grade 10, Efarmoges Pliroforikis A' Lykeiou): the older Lykeio applications book in four thematic units — hardware/software and applications, programming environments and creating applications (life cycle, modern IDEs, mobile-app and 3D object-oriented programming), communication and the internet (HTML5/CSS, learning online), and collaboration and safety online (cloud applications, telework, social networks, privacy).

Moldova's own way (Grade 11, Informatică): Grade 11 is the theory year — how numbers, logic and circuits actually work inside the machine (Boolean algebra, adders, flip-flops, instruction formats, assembly language) — with structured programming (arrays, records, sets, files) carried over from Grade 10's languages; some sections are profile-specific (Real/Umanist).

Indonesia's own way (Indonesia Grade 10 G10 Informatika SMK Sem1): The SMK Semester 1 book teaches the same Informatika elements with a logic-first opening (propositions and inference), applied office integration, hands-on network-device configuration, and an OS-installation practical.


Pakistan's own way (Grade 6 Computer Science 6): every unit opens with Students Learning Outcomes, is dotted with "Do you Know?", "Extra Bit!" and "Tips" boxes, and closes with Summary, MCQ/short/detailed exercises, project-based and activity-based questions, with an answers key at the back. Digital skills (Windows desktop, file management, Paint 3D, a typing tutor) are taught hands-on BEFORE programming; digital citizenship is framed through Pakistani citizenship first; the entrepreneurship unit teaches from Pakistani founders (Foodpanda/EatOye, Shopsy.pk, Zero Makeup, Investors Lounge).
