# The CPU — the brain of the computer

## Do it first

Every sum, every decision, every instruction followed inside a computer happens in one place. Before you read on, guess: is that place spread through the whole machine, or gathered in one small part? This lesson names the part — and the three parts inside it.

## The CPU — the brain of the computer

The CPU — the central processing unit — is the brain of the computer. All arithmetic operations (adding, subtracting, and the rest), all logical operations (comparing and deciding), and all control operations happen in the CPU. Data enters through input devices, but it is here that the data is actually worked on.

The brain itself has three components. 

[Picture 10129 — a chip opened into its three labelled parts]



1. **Arithmetic-logic unit (ALU)** — works out and compares.
2. **Control unit** — directs everything.
3. **Registers** — hold things for a moment.

This lesson names them. The next lesson takes each one in turn and gives it its full job.
