# Hands-on — The CPU — the brain of the computer

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Brain jobs
Write three tasks — a sum, a comparison (which mark is higher?), and directing a set of steps. For each, write which CPU component's family of work it belongs to: arithmetic, logical, or control.

## Three labels
Draw a chip as a square. Divide it into three labelled regions — ALU, control unit, registers — from memory. Then check against the picture.
