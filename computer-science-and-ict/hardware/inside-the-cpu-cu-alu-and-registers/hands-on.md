# Hands-on — Inside the CPU — CU, ALU and registers

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Three-job office
In a group of three: one directs (calls the task), one calculates (does the sum or comparison on paper), one holds (keeps the task cards in order). Run five tasks through the office. You have modelled the CPU.

## Comparison drill
Write ten pairs of numbers. For each, an 'ALU' partner must answer only with <, > or =. Then five pairs with <= and >= allowed.
