# Inside the CPU — CU, ALU and registers — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The cable carrying data between the CPU's parts is the ______.
**Answer: bus**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** ALU stands for the unit that does arithmetic and ______ operations.
**Answer: logical**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Registers are ______ storage — they hold instructions for a moment.
**Answer: temporary**
