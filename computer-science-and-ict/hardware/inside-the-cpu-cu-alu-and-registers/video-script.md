**Inside the CPU.**

First, brainstorm: what do you already know about this? Say it out loud.

Now the one thing: inside the CPU, three sub-components are joined by a bus: the control unit directs, the ALU calculates and compares, and registers hold instructions for the moment they are needed.

**The control unit — it directs.** The CPU has three sub-components, joined by a bus — the cable that carries data between them.
**The ALU — it calculates and compares.** The ALU executes instructions.
**Registers — they hold for a moment.** Registers — the CPU's memory unit — are temporary storage locations where instructions loaded from main memory wait before the ALU works on them.

Now you: Draw three boxes joined by a bus line, labelled 'directs', 'calculates and compares', 'holds for a moment'.
