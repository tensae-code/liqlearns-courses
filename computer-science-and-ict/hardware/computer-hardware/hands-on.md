# Hands-on — Computer hardware — the part you can touch

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Family sort
Write ten computer parts on slips. Sort them into the four families. Any slip you cannot place, set aside and say what extra information you would need.

## Touch walk
Walk around a real computer (school, home, or a shop's display). Touch — gently, or point at — one part from each family and say its job aloud.
