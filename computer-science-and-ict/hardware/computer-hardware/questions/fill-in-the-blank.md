# Computer hardware — the part you can touch — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the missing word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Hardware is the ____ part of the computer you can see and touch.**
**Answer: physical**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) The four families: input devices, the central processing unit, ____ devices and storage devices.**
**Answer: output**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Families are sorted by ____ — the job each part does.**
**Answer: function**
