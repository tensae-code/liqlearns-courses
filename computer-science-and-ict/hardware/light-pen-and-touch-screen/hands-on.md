# Hands-on — Light pen and touch screen

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Tap test
On any touch device, tap ten targets of shrinking size. Notice accuracy with finger against a stylus or pen if one exists — the light pen lesson explains the older tool for fine work.

## Touch in the town
List every touch screen you meet in a week: ATMs, information points, phones. Mark which replaced a mouse or keyboard completely.
