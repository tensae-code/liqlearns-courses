# Light pen and touch screen

## Do it first

You already enter data by touching a phone screen. Older machines had a pen that touched the screen instead. Before reading on, guess how the machine knows where the pen — or your finger — is.

## The light pen

A light pen is a light-sensitive device used with the monitor for creating or modifying graphics. A light cell at its tip senses light from the screen, and that sensing fixes the pen's position — the computer knows where the pen points because the pen reads the screen's light at that spot.

## The touch screen

A touch screen lets data be entered with a finger — an alternative to mouse or keyboard. Watch the GIF: the finger taps, and the screen responds at exactly that spot. Touch screens are used on laptops, smartphones and tablets, and in applications such as ATMs, public information computers, hospitals, and airline and railway reservations.
