**Light pen and touch screen.**

First, brainstorm: what do you already know about this? Say it out loud.

Now the one thing: the light pen draws and fixes its position by sensing the screen's light, and the touch screen lets data be entered with a finger — the input of ATMs and public information computers.

**The light pen.** A light pen is a light-sensitive device used with the monitor for creating or modifying graphics.
**The touch screen.** A touch screen lets data be entered with a finger — an alternative to mouse or keyboard.

Now you: Draw a pen drawing on a screen, and a finger tapping an ATM screen.
