# Light pen and touch screen — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** A light pen fixes its position by —
a) GPS  b) A light cell at its tip sensing light from the screen  c) A cable of fixed length  d) Sound
**Answer: b) A light cell at its tip sensing light from the screen** — A light cell at the tip senses light from the screen to fix the pen's position.

**2.** The light pen is used with the monitor for —
a) Printing  b) Creating or modifying graphics  c) Storing files  d) Cooling
**Answer: b) Creating or modifying graphics** — It is used for creating or modifying graphics.

**3.** A touch screen lets data be entered with —
a) A finger  b) A foot pedal  c) A joystick only  d) Printed cards
**Answer: a) A finger** — Data is entered with a finger on a touch screen.

**4.** A touch screen is an alternative to —
a) The monitor  b) Mouse or keyboard  c) The printer  d) Storage
**Answer: b) Mouse or keyboard** — It is an alternative to mouse or keyboard.

**5.** Which of these uses touch screens?
a) ATMs and public information computers  b) Only wristwatches  c) Only calculators  d) No public machines
**Answer: a) ATMs and public information computers** — ATMs, public information computers, hospitals, airline and railway reservations use touch screens.
