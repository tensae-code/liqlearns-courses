# Growing ginger and the spice crops — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does organic manuring carry the ginger crop?
**Answer: Because manuring is essential in Ethiopian conditions and no fertiliser recommendation exists for ginger.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give mulch's three jobs in ginger growing.
**Answer: It is a source of organic manure, it prevents soil washing, and it conserves moisture.**
