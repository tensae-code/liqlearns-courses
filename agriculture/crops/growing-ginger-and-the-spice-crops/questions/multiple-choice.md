# Growing ginger and the spice crops — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** For ginger in Ethiopian conditions, manuring is —
a) essential  b) forbidden  c) useless
**Answer: a) essential**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Ginger has no fertiliser recommendation, so —
a) no feeding is done  b) only imported fertiliser works  c) organic manuring carries the crop
**Answer: c) organic manuring carries the crop**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Mulch is itself —
a) a harvesting tool  b) a source of organic manure  c) a pest
**Answer: b) a source of organic manure**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Mulching also —
a) prevents soil washing and conserves moisture  b) dries the soil out  c) stops roots growing
**Answer: a) prevents soil washing and conserves moisture**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Ginger irrigation intervals run —
a) every day twice  b) once a season  c) every 4–10 days
**Answer: c) every 4–10 days**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The schedule given runs from mid-September to —
a) mid-March  b) the third week of December  c) the end of June
**Answer: b) the third week of December**
