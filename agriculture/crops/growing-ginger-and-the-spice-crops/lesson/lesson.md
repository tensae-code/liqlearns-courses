# Growing ginger and the spice crops

## Do it first
Ginger grows hidden, swelling under the soil. Above it, the grower's three friends are manure, mulch and timely water. Guess what each one does before reading.

## Sub lesson 1 — Manuring and mulching ginger
For ginger in Ethiopian conditions, **manuring is essential** — no fertiliser recommendation exists, so **organic manuring carries the crop**. **Mulching** matters doubly: it is a source of organic manure, and it **prevents soil washing and conserves moisture**. The spice crops are taken crop by crop in this section; ginger's practice is the representative worked example.

## Sub lesson 2 — Watering ginger on a schedule
Ginger's irrigation runs at **4–10-day intervals** through the season — in the schedule given, from **mid-September to the third week of December**. Spice growing is calendar work: manure in, mulch down, water on its rhythm, rhizomes swelling beneath it all.
