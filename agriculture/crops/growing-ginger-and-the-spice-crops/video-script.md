Growing ginger and the spice crops.
For ginger in Ethiopian conditions, manuring is essential — no fertiliser recommendation exists, so organic manuring carries the crop.
Ginger's irrigation runs at 4–10-day intervals through the season — in the schedule given, from mid-September to the third week of December.
