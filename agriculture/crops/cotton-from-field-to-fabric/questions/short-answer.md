# Cotton — from field to fabric — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Run cotton's chain from field to fabric in order.
**Answer: Field production, then ginning (lint separated from seed), then spinning (lint to yarn), then fabric for local and export markets.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name cotton's three products.
**Answer: Lint for textiles, cooking oil from the seed, and feed cake from the seed residue.**
