# Cotton — from field to fabric — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Cotton lint is the key raw material of Ethiopia's textile industry.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Cotton is grown only for export and never used locally.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Spinning turns lint into yarn.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Cottonseed has no use after ginning.
**Answer: False**
