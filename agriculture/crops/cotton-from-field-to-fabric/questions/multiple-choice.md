# Cotton — from field to fabric — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Fibre crops produce —
a) sugar  b) fibres for textiles and other industries  c) milk
**Answer: b) fibres for textiles and other industries**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Cottonseed gives —
a) only waste  b) cooking oil and feed cake  c) silk
**Answer: b) cooking oil and feed cake**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Ginning does what to cotton?
a) dyes it  b) separates lint from seed  c) plants it
**Answer: b) separates lint from seed**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In 2021 Ethiopia had —
a) 2 textile factories  b) more than 50 garment/cloth manufacturers and 14 textile factories  c) no textile industry
**Answer: b) more than 50 garment/cloth manufacturers and 14 textile factories**
