# Lesson plan — Cotton — from field to fabric

**Course:** Agriculture · **Title:** Crops · **Lesson:** Cotton — from field to fabric
**Place in the curriculum:** Agriculture — the Crops title (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** cotton — the fibre crop feeding Ethiopia's textile industry — from field production through ginning and spinning into fabric, with cottonseed giving cooking oil and feed cake
**This lesson's own level:** Grade 11 depth — it stands on the learner's own farm and fields, and on the course's earlier titles where named below.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
- Sub lesson 1 — Fibre crops and cotton's gifts: **Fibre crops produce fibres for textiles and other industries.
- Sub lesson 2 — Field to fabric — gin, spin, weave: **Cotton lint is the key raw material of Ethiopia's textile industry** — more than **50 garment/cloth manufacturers and 14 textile factories in 2021**, with demand and foreign-currency earnings growing.
Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Industrial crops — and growing sesame. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what fibre crops produce and cottonseed's side gifts: cooking oil and feed cake.
2. State cotton lint's place: key raw material of Ethiopia's textile industry, with the 2021 factory counts.
3. Run cotton's chain: field production, ginning, spinning, fabric, for local and export markets.

## What you need
A cotton boll or a piece of cotton cloth; the lesson picture chain.

## If the learner is stuck
- Stops the chain at harvest → the crop's story runs ginning and spinning into fabric; lint quality carries through all of it.
- Forgets the seed → cottonseed gives cooking oil and feed cake; only the lint goes to cloth.
