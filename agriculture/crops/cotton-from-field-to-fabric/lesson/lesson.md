# Cotton — from field to fabric

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — Fibre crops and cotton's gifts
**Fibre crops produce fibres for textiles and other industries.** Cotton leads them — and cotton gives more than fibre: **cottonseed also gives cooking oil and feed cake**, so the plant serves the cloth, the kitchen and the herd at once.

**Cotton lint** is the soft fibre in the opened boll — the part the textile industry buys. Everything in this lesson follows that lint from the field to the shirt.

## Sub lesson 2 — Field to fabric — gin, spin, weave
**Cotton lint is the key raw material of Ethiopia's textile industry** — more than **50 garment/cloth manufacturers and 14 textile factories in 2021**, with demand and foreign-currency earnings growing.

The chain runs: **field production → ginning** (the lint is separated from the seed) **→ spinning** (lint becomes yarn) **→ fabric**, for **local and export markets**. A break anywhere in the chain — poor lint, idle gins — reaches all the way back to the farmer's price.
