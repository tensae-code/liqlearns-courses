# Hands-on — Cotton — from field to fabric

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Boll to thread
If a cotton boll is available, pull the lint, twist a few fibres between your fingers into a thread. Feel spinning at its smallest scale; write what the gin must do before this is possible.

## Chain poster
Draw the five-stop chain — field, gin, spinning, weaving/fabric, market — with one label per stop and the seed's side-road to oil and cake.
