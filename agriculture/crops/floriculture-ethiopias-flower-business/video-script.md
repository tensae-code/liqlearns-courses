Floriculture — Ethiopia's flower business.
Ethiopia's flower industry rests on real advantages: a suitable climate for various flower crops, ample land, cheap labour and abundant water, proximity to Middle East and European markets, a growing domestic market, and government policy and investment incentives.
Beside flower production stands landscaping: the design of planted places — arranging plants, paths and spaces so grounds are beautiful and useful.
