# Floriculture — Ethiopia's flower business

## Do it first
A rose cut this morning near Addis Ababa can be in a European shop within days. What does Ethiopia have that makes that possible?

## Sub lesson 1 — Why flowers fit Ethiopia
Ethiopia's flower industry rests on real advantages: a **suitable climate** for various flower crops, **ample land**, **cheap labour** and **abundant water**, **proximity to Middle East and European markets**, a **growing domestic market**, and **government policy and investment incentives**. Its first importance is **foreign currency earnings** — flowers are an export crop that earns what the country needs from abroad.

## Sub lesson 2 — Landscaping — the design half
Beside flower production stands **landscaping**: the design of planted places — arranging plants, paths and spaces so grounds are beautiful and useful. Floriculture grows flowers as a crop; landscaping arranges plants as a design. The section teaches both as one business family.
