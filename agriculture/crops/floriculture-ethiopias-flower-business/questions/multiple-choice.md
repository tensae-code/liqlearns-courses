# Floriculture — Ethiopia's flower business — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ethiopia's flower advantages include —
a) suitable climate, ample land, cheap labour and abundant water  b) no water at all  c) a cold sea coast
**Answer: a) suitable climate, ample land, cheap labour and abundant water**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Ethiopia is close to which flower markets?
a) only its own villages  b) the Pacific islands  c) the Middle East and Europe
**Answer: c) the Middle East and Europe**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The first importance of floriculture is —
a) animal feed  b) foreign currency earnings  c) home decoration
**Answer: b) foreign currency earnings**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Landscaping is —
a) the design of planted places  b) a way of drying flowers  c) an export tax
**Answer: a) the design of planted places**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Floriculture and landscaping differ as —
a) two names for the same job  b) indoor vs outdoor work only  c) crop growing vs designing with plants
**Answer: c) crop growing vs designing with plants**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Government support for flowers comes as —
a) price fixing  b) policy and investment incentives  c) a ban on exports
**Answer: b) policy and investment incentives**
