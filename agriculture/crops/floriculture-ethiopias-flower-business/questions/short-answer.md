# Floriculture — Ethiopia's flower business — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** List four advantages Ethiopia holds for floriculture.
**Answer: Any four of: suitable climate, ample land, cheap labour, abundant water, proximity to Middle East and European markets, growing domestic market, government policy and investment incentives.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Distinguish floriculture from landscaping.
**Answer: Floriculture grows flowers as a crop (for sale and export); landscaping designs and arranges planted places.**
