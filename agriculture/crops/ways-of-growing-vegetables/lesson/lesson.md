# Ways of growing vegetables — from gathering to commercial farms

## Do it first
Think of four gardens: a few plants by a house, a family garden, a garden that sells its surplus, and a farm growing only for buyers. What changes as you climb from one to the next?

## Sub lesson 1 — From gathering to growing
Vegetable production began with **gathering wild vegetables** — taking what grew on its own. From there it evolved step by step into specialised systems: people began planting, tending and selecting, until production became a planned activity with chosen seed, prepared land and a purpose for the harvest.

## Sub lesson 2 — The ladder of systems today
Today's systems run from the **home garden** through to **intensive commercial production**. Each step up the ladder uses land, labour and inputs more intensively and aims more of the harvest at the market. Placing your own plot on the ladder tells you what the next step up would demand — and what it could return.
