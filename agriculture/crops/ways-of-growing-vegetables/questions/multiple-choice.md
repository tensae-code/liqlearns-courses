# Ways of growing vegetables — from gathering to commercial farms — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Vegetable production began with —
a) gathering wild vegetables  b) greenhouses  c) export farms
**Answer: a) gathering wild vegetables**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Production evolved from gathering toward —
a) no planting at all  b) importing only  c) specialised systems
**Answer: c) specialised systems**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A specialised system means production is —
a) done only in forests  b) planned, with chosen seed and prepared land  c) left entirely to chance
**Answer: b) planned, with chosen seed and prepared land**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The ladder of systems runs from home gardens to —
a) intensive commercial production  b) wild gathering  c) fallow land
**Answer: a) intensive commercial production**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Each step up the ladder aims —
a) less at any use  b) only at home use  c) more of the harvest at the market
**Answer: c) more of the harvest at the market**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Placing your plot on the ladder helps you see —
a) the weather next year  b) what the next step up would demand and return  c) the price of fuel abroad
**Answer: b) what the next step up would demand and return**
