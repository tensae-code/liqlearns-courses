# Ways of growing vegetables — from gathering to commercial farms — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How did vegetable production change from its beginning?
**Answer: It evolved from gathering wild vegetables into specialised, planned systems of planting, tending and selecting.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the two ends of the system ladder.
**Answer: The home garden at the low end and intensive commercial production at the top end.**
