# Ways of growing vegetables — from gathering to commercial farms — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Gathering wild vegetables is the oldest form of vegetable production.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Vegetable production has not changed since gathering began.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Intensive commercial production sits at the top of the ladder.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A home garden aims all of its harvest at export buyers.
**Answer: False**
