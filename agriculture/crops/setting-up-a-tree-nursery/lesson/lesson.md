# Setting up a tree nursery

## Do it first
A tree begins as a seedling in a nursery row, shoulder to shoulder with a thousand brothers. The quality of the forest is decided here, in miniature.

## Sub lesson 1 — Best practice from the start
Tree nursery establishment and management follows **best practice: high-quality seedlings of the desired species**, produced using the most **cost-effective, environment-friendly and socially acceptable technologies**. Quality is not inspected into seedlings at the end; it is built in from the first decision.

[Picture 26665 — a tree nursery with seedling beds in rows]

## Sub lesson 2 — Reconnaissance and choosing site and species
The work begins with **site reconnaissance and selection of the planting site and species** — looking before digging. The nursery question and the plantation question are asked together: what species, for what site, raised where?
