# Setting up a tree nursery — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Best practice produces —
a) high-quality seedlings of the desired species  b) any seedlings cheaply  c) only fast seedlings
**Answer: a) high-quality seedlings of the desired species**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Technologies should be —
a) the most expensive  b) imported only  c) cost-effective, environment-friendly and socially acceptable
**Answer: c) cost-effective, environment-friendly and socially acceptable**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Seedling quality is —
a) added at planting  b) built in from the first decision  c) inspected in at the end
**Answer: b) built in from the first decision**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The work begins with —
a) site reconnaissance  b) sowing  c) selling
**Answer: a) site reconnaissance**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Reconnaissance selects —
a) the buyer  b) the price  c) the planting site and the species
**Answer: c) the planting site and the species**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Nursery and plantation questions are asked —
a) never  b) together  c) years apart
**Answer: b) together**
