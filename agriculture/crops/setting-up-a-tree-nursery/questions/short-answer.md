# Setting up a tree nursery — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** State best practice for a tree nursery in one sentence.
**Answer: Produce high-quality seedlings of the desired species using the most cost-effective, environment-friendly and socially acceptable technologies.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the work begin with?
**Answer: Site reconnaissance, and selection of the planting site and the species.**
