Setting up a tree nursery.
Tree nursery establishment and management follows best practice: high-quality seedlings of the desired species, produced using the most cost-effective, environment-friendly and socially acceptable technologies.
The work begins with site reconnaissance and selection of the planting site and species — looking before digging.
Do it yourself.
Then say it to your companion.
