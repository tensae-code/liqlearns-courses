# Hands-on — Growing barley

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two-buyer debate
In pairs: one argues 'feed it hard for yield', one argues 'protect the malt'. Settle with the lesson's balance rule and write the agreed feeding plan.

## Pest cards
Make three cards — armyworm, grasshopper, barley shoot fly — with the crop stage each attacks, from observation or farmer talk.
