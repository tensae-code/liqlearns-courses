# Growing barley — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name barley's three major insect pests.
**Answer: Armyworm, grasshopper and barley shoot fly.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why must barley fertilising be balanced?
**Answer: Because heavy nitrogen application lowers malt quality, so feeding for yield must be balanced against the crop's malting use.**
