# Growing barley — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Barley shares most of its weeds, diseases and controls with —
a) teff  b) wheat  c) sorghum
**Answer: b) wheat**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which is a major barley insect pest?
a) barley shoot fly  b) aphid only  c) weevil in store
**Answer: a) barley shoot fly**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Barley matures in —
a) three to four months  b) nine months  c) two weeks
**Answer: a) three to four months**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Heavy nitrogen application on barley —
a) improves malt  b) lowers malt quality  c) has no effect
**Answer: b) lowers malt quality**
