# Growing barley — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Armyworm and grasshopper attack barley.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Barley is judged mature by its green colour.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Barley is used for both food and malt.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** More nitrogen always means better barley for every use.
**Answer: False**
