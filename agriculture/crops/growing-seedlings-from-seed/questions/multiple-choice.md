# Growing seedlings from seed — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Raising from seed starts with —
a) sowing  b) planting out  c) selling
**Answer: a) sowing**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Germination care protects —
a) only the leaves  b) the fence  c) the seed's most defenceless days
**Answer: c) the seed's most defenceless days**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The pass mark of germination is —
a) colour of trays  b) even, quick emergence  c) any emergence over months
**Answer: b) even, quick emergence**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Seedlings leave the nursery when —
a) their size and strength say they are plantable  b) the calendar alone says so  c) the beds are needed
**Answer: a) their size and strength say they are plantable**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Growing-on includes —
a) neglect  b) deep shade always  c) tending, watering, weeding and hardening
**Answer: c) tending, watering, weeding and hardening**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Plantable size means —
a) flowering  b) able to survive the field  c) one day old
**Answer: b) able to survive the field**
