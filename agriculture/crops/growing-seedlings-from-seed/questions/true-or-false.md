# Growing seedlings from seed — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Germination care covers moisture, cover and protection.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Uneven, slow emergence is the ideal result.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Hardening prepares seedlings for field life.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Seedlings should be planted out as early as possible regardless of size.
**Answer: False**
