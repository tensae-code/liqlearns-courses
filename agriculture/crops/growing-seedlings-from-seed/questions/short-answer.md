# Growing seedlings from seed — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What two phases make raising plants from seed?
**Answer: Sowing with germination care, then growing the seedlings on to plantable size.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** When is a seedling ready to leave the nursery?
**Answer: When its size and strength say it can survive the field — plantable size, not a calendar date alone.**
