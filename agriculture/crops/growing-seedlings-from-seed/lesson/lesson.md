# Growing seedlings from seed

## Do it first
A seed tray is a promise with a date. Sow, and the tray answers in days: first a crack, then a hook of green, then leaves. Your job is everything around that miracle.

## Sub lesson 1 — Sowing and germination care
Raising plants from seed in the nursery begins with **sowing** and **germination care**: the seedbed's moisture, cover and protection through the seed's most defenceless days. Germination is the nursery's first examination — even, quick emergence is the pass mark.

## Sub lesson 2 — Growing to plantable size
After emergence, seedlings are **grown to plantable size**: tended, watered, weeded and hardened until they can survive the field. A seedling leaves the nursery not when the calendar says so, but when its size and strength say it is ready to be planted out.

## Do It Yourself
1. Sow a small tray and log emergence daily: first, half, full.
2. Write the germination-care list for your tray: moisture, cover, protection.
3. Grade your seedlings weekly against 'plantable' and record the verdict.
