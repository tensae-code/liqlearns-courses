# Video script — Growing seedlings from seed

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Growing seedlings from seed.
Raising plants from seed in the nursery begins with sowing and germination care: the seedbed's moisture, cover and protection through the seed's most defenceless days.
After emergence, seedlings are grown to plantable size: tended, watered, weeded and hardened until they can survive the field.
Do it yourself.
Then say it to your companion.
