# Establishing coffee, tea and spice fields

## Do it first
A nursery seedling is ready. Between it and a paying field lie transplanting, spacing, shade and years of care. Walk that road in your mind.

## Sub lesson 1 — Establishing the field planting
Field establishment moves the nursery plant into its permanent place: **transplanting** at the right stage, **spacing** that gives each perennial plant its lifetime room, and **shade** where the crop needs it. Establishment is done once and lived with for decades — the field's skeleton is set here.

[Picture 26619 — young coffee rows under shade trees]

## Sub lesson 2 — Managing the standing planting
Management follows: **weeding, feeding and the standing care** of perennial plantings that must pay for decades. A coffee, tea or spice field is not replanted each year; its yield is the reward of patient, repeated care of the same plants.

## Say it to your companion
Explain this lesson to your companion in your own words, using one real example from your own area for each sub lesson. Your companion asks one question about each part — answer from the lesson, not from memory of other lessons.

## Do It Yourself
1. Lay out a spacing grid with string and pegs for a small coffee plot.
2. Write a shade plan: which trees, where, and why.
3. Make a five-year care calendar for a young coffee field.
