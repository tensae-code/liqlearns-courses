# Establishing coffee, tea and spice fields — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** List the establishment jobs of this lesson.
**Answer: Transplanting, spacing, and shade provision for the young perennial planting.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why is perennial field care different from annual crop care?
**Answer: The same plants must be kept paying for decades, so care is repeated standing care — weeding, feeding, tending — not a fresh planting each year.**
