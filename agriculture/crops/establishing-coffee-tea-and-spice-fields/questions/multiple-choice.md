# Establishing coffee, tea and spice fields — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Field establishment includes —
a) transplanting, spacing and shade  b) harvesting  c) exporting
**Answer: a) transplanting, spacing and shade**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Spacing gives each perennial plant —
a) a temporary corner  b) a market price  c) its lifetime room
**Answer: c) its lifetime room**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Establishment is done —
a) after each rain  b) once, and lived with for decades  c) every week
**Answer: b) once, and lived with for decades**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Standing care includes —
a) weeding and feeding  b) replanting yearly  c) burning the field
**Answer: a) weeding and feeding**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** These fields pay over —
a) one week  b) a single season only  c) decades
**Answer: c) decades**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Yield in a perennial field rewards —
a) constant moving  b) patient, repeated care of the same plants  c) neglect
**Answer: b) patient, repeated care of the same plants**
