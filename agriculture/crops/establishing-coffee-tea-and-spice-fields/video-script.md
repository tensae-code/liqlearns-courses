# Video script — Establishing coffee, tea and spice fields

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Establishing coffee, tea and spice fields.
Field establishment moves the nursery plant into its permanent place: transplanting at the right stage, spacing that gives each perennial plant its lifetime room, and shade where the crop needs it.
Management follows: weeding, feeding and the standing care of perennial plantings that must pay for decades.
Do it yourself.
Then say it to your companion.
