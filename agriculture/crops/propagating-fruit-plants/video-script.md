The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Propagating fruit plants.
A mature seed germinates when it meets a favourable environment.
Fruit plants are also propagated vegetatively: cuttings — pieces of stem that root into new plants — and grafting — joining a piece of one plant onto another so they grow as one.
Do it yourself.
Then say it to your companion.
