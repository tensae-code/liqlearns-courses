# Propagating fruit plants — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A mature seed germinates when it meets —
a) a favourable environment  b) any dry shelf  c) deep darkness forever
**Answer: a) a favourable environment**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Pre-sowing treatments are used to —
a) colour the seed coat  b) make seeds bigger  c) overcome seed dormancy
**Answer: c) overcome seed dormancy**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Pre-sowing treatments are applied —
a) after harvest only  b) immediately before sowing  c) a year before sowing
**Answer: b) immediately before sowing**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A cutting is —
a) a piece of stem that roots into a new plant  b) a kind of fruit  c) a storage box
**Answer: a) a piece of stem that roots into a new plant**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Grafting means —
a) planting two seeds together  b) cutting a tree down  c) joining a piece of one plant onto another so they grow as one
**Answer: c) joining a piece of one plant onto another so they grow as one**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Vegetative propagation is valued because the new plant —
a) never fruits  b) carries the mother plant's exact character  c) is always different
**Answer: b) carries the mother plant's exact character**
