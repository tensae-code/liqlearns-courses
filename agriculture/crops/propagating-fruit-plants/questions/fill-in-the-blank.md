# Propagating fruit plants — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Seeds that will not wake carry ___.
**Answer: dormancy**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Treatments are applied immediately before ___.
**Answer: sowing**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Joining plant pieces so they grow as one is ___.
**Answer: grafting**
