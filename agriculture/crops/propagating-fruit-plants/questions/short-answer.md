# Propagating fruit plants — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What three qualities should treated germination have?
**Answer: Rapid, uniform and timely germination.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the two vegetative techniques of this lesson and define one.
**Answer: Cuttings and grafting; a cutting is a stem piece that roots into a new plant, grafting joins a piece of one plant onto another.**
