# Propagating fruit plants — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Treated seed should germinate rapidly, uniformly and on time.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Dormant seeds germinate at once in any conditions.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A grafted plant grows as one plant from two pieces.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Cuttings produce plants unlike the mother plant.
**Answer: False**
