## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Explain seed dormancy and how pre-sowing treatment beats it.
2. Say when treatments are applied and what germination they aim for.
3. Describe cuttings and grafting and why vegetative plants match the mother.

## What you need
Seeds, wet cloth, a notebook; a grafted tree to inspect.
