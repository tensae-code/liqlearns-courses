# Lifting and storing roots and tubers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Lifting must avoid —
a) mechanical damage  b) all soil contact  c) sunlight entirely
**Answer: a) mechanical damage**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Mechanical damage at lifting becomes —
a) extra yield  b) better seed  c) rot in the store
**Answer: c) rot in the store**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Harvest timing is part of —
a) next year's planting only  b) quality, not just the calendar  c) the buyer's work
**Answer: b) quality, not just the calendar**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Storage guards against —
a) sprouting, water loss and rot  b) buyers  c) planting dates
**Answer: a) sprouting, water loss and rot**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A good store for roots and tubers is —
a) hot and sealed wet  b) in full sun  c) shaded and carefully kept
**Answer: c) shaded and carefully kept**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The handling chain decides —
a) the field's size  b) how much of the crop is actually eaten  c) the crop's name
**Answer: b) how much of the crop is actually eaten**
