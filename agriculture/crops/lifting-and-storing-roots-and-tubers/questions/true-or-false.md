# Lifting and storing roots and tubers — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A fork through a potato is mechanical damage.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Bruises at lifting do not affect storage.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Sprouting is one of the losses storage guards against.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Once lifted, roots and tubers need no further care.
**Answer: False**
