# Roots and tubers — the gap-filling foods — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain the gap-filling role in one sentence.
**Answer: Roots and tubers stay ready in the ground and can be dug to feed the household during the gap before other harvests come in.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What two sides does the unit weigh about expanding these crops?
**Answer: Ethiopia's prospects for expanding them, and the problems of their production.**
