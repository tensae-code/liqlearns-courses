# Roots and tubers — the gap-filling foods — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Roots and tubers provide —
a) vitamins, carbohydrates and other nutrients  b) only water  c) only protein
**Answer: a) vitamins, carbohydrates and other nutrients**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Their special timing role is to —
a) be eaten only at feasts  b) replace all grain forever  c) fill food gaps until other harvests come in
**Answer: c) fill food gaps until other harvests come in**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A gap-filling crop is dug when —
a) never  b) the household store runs low before the main harvest  c) only for export
**Answer: b) the household store runs low before the main harvest**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Ethiopia's prospects for these crops are weighed against —
a) the problems of their production  b) nothing  c) import prices only
**Answer: a) the problems of their production**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Planning expansion with open eyes means —
a) ignoring problems  b) expanding everywhere at once  c) naming both prospects and problems
**Answer: c) naming both prospects and problems**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The food-security value of these crops is —
a) their export name  b) food ready in the ground during the hungry gap  c) their flower colour
**Answer: b) food ready in the ground during the hungry gap**
