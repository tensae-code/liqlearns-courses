Roots and tubers — the gap-filling foods.
Roots and tubers provide vitamins, carbohydrates and other nutrients — and their special role is timing: they fill food gaps that exist until other harvests come in.
Ethiopia's prospects for expanding these crops are real — but they stand against the problems of their production.
