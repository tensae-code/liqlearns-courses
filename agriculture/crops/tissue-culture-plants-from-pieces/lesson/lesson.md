# Tissue culture — plants from pieces

## Do it first
A piece of leaf no bigger than a fingernail can become a whole plant — without soil, without a field. Hold that idea and read how.

## Sub lesson 1 — Growing cells on a medium, aseptically
**Plant tissue culture** grows plant **cells, tissues and organs** on an **artificial nutrient medium** under **aseptic (sterile) conditions**. As **micro-propagation** it produces **clones** from a minute part — the **explant** — of the mother plant: pieces of leaf, stem or root.

[Picture 26622 — tiny plantlets growing in laboratory jars]

## Sub lesson 2 — What it is used for
Its crop applications include **medicinal plants**, whose active compounds serve herbal and pharmaceutical industries, and the **conservation of genetic material of threatened medicinal plants** — a piece in a jar can keep a rare plant's line alive when the wild plant is disappearing.
