# Hands-on — Tissue culture — plants from pieces

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Clean-hands drill
Practise aseptic handling: wash, flame-free clean workspace, handle 'explants' (leaf pieces) with cleaned tools only.

## Jar garden drawing
Design a labelled tissue-culture jar: medium, explant, seal, light — and present it to the class.
