# Tissue culture — plants from pieces — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tissue culture grows plant material on —
a) an artificial nutrient medium  b) open field soil  c) river water
**Answer: a) an artificial nutrient medium**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Tissue culture is done under —
a) dusty conditions  b) field conditions  c) aseptic (sterile) conditions
**Answer: c) aseptic (sterile) conditions**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The minute piece taken from the mother plant is the —
a) pollen  b) explant  c) seed coat
**Answer: b) explant**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Micro-propagation produces —
a) clones of the mother plant  b) random hybrids  c) only roots
**Answer: a) clones of the mother plant**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** An explant can be a piece of —
a) fruit only  b) bark only  c) leaf, stem or root
**Answer: c) leaf, stem or root**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Tissue culture helps conserve —
a) soil samples  b) genetic material of threatened medicinal plants  c) only common weeds
**Answer: b) genetic material of threatened medicinal plants**
