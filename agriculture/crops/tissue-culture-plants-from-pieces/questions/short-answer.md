# Tissue culture — plants from pieces — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Define plant tissue culture.
**Answer: Growing plant cells, tissues and organs on an artificial nutrient medium under aseptic conditions.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two applications of tissue culture from the lesson.
**Answer: Producing planting material of medicinal plants (for herbal and pharmaceutical industries) and conserving genetic material of threatened medicinal plants.**
