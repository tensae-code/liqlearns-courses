# Growing field pea — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Field pea is grown for its —
a) fibre  b) edible seed  c) oil
**Answer: b) edible seed**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Field pea stands among Ethiopia's —
a) lowland oil crops  b) highland pulses  c) sugar crops
**Answer: b) highland pulses**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Field pea's plant habit is —
a) upright and sturdy like faba  b) climbing and sprawling  c) a tree
**Answer: b) climbing and sprawling**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Faba bean seed, beside field pea seed, is —
a) smaller and rounder  b) larger and flatter  c) identical
**Answer: b) larger and flatter**
