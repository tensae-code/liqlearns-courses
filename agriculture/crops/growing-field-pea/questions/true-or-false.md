# Growing field pea — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Field pea is a legume and fixes nitrogen like other pulses.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Field pea and faba bean are the same plant with two names.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The book pictures the two pulses side by side to tell them apart.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Field pea seed is larger and flatter than faba bean seed.
**Answer: False**
