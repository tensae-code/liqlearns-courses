# Growing field pea — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the four headings of the standard crop pattern field pea is taught in.
**Answer: Its place among Ethiopia's highland pulses, its growth requirements, its field management, and its harvest.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give two ways to tell field pea from faba bean.
**Answer: By plant: field pea climbs and sprawls while faba bean stands upright. By seed: field pea seed is smaller and rounder; faba seed is larger and flatter.**
