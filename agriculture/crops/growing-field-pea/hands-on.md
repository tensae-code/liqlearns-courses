# Hands-on — Growing field pea

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Seed sorting race
Mix faba beans and field peas (or dried equivalents) in a bowl. Sort them against the clock, then check every seed by shape: flat and large, or round and small.

## Pair portrait
Grow or observe one plant of each where possible; sketch both habits — upright against climbing — and label the support a pea plant wants.
