# Hands-on — Sorting vegetables — leaf, flower, root; cool season, warm season

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Stall sorting game
At a market stall or with kitchen vegetables, race a partner: sort the pile by part eaten, then re-sort it by season.

## Nitrogen watch
If a cool-season crop is growing nearby, look at its shallow roots gently at the bed edge and talk about why it answers quickly to nitrogen.
