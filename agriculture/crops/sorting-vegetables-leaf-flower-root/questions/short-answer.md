# Sorting vegetables — leaf, flower, root; cool season, warm season — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name three leafy vegetables from the lesson.
**Answer: Any three of: cabbage, Swiss chard, celery, lettuce, asparagus.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How do cool-season vegetables differ from warm-season crops in roots and feeding?
**Answer: Cool-season vegetables are often small and shallow-rooted and respond more readily to nitrogen fertiliser; warm-season crops need the warm season.**
