# Sorting vegetables — leaf, flower, root; cool season, warm season — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Cabbage belongs to which part-group?
a) leafy vegetables  b) flower vegetables  c) root vegetables
**Answer: a) leafy vegetables**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Swiss chard, celery and lettuce are examples of —
a) warm-season fruits  b) bulb crops only  c) leafy vegetables
**Answer: c) leafy vegetables**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Vegetables are classified first by —
a) the price  b) the part used (eaten)  c) the colour of the shop
**Answer: b) the part used (eaten)**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Cool-season vegetables are often —
a) small and shallow-rooted  b) deep-rooted trees  c) grown only in rivers
**Answer: a) small and shallow-rooted**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Cool-season crops respond readily to —
a) no water at all  b) shade only  c) nitrogen fertiliser
**Answer: c) nitrogen fertiliser**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The two sortings (by part, by season) —
a) cannot both be used  b) cross: a vegetable sits in one group of each  c) are the same sorting
**Answer: b) cross: a vegetable sits in one group of each**
