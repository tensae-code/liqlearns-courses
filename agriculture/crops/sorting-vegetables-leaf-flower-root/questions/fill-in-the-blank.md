# Sorting vegetables — leaf, flower, root; cool season, warm season — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Cabbage and lettuce are ___ vegetables.
**Answer: leafy**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Cool-season vegetables respond readily to ___ fertiliser.
**Answer: nitrogen**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Cool-season vegetables are often small and ___-rooted.
**Answer: shallow**
