# Setting up a fruit nursery

## Do it first
Choose a nursery site in your mind: a windy hilltop, a swampy hollow, or a sheltered flat near the field. Defend your choice before you read on.

## Sub lesson 1 — The site checklist — shelter, drainage, soil
A fruit nursery site should be **sheltered from strong wind** — or protected by an established **windbreak** — **well drained** with **fertile soil**, and **free from flooding**. Young seedlings are the most delicate plants on the farm; the site must not fight them with wind, standing water or poor soil.

## Sub lesson 2 — Near the field, with labour to hand
The site should be **separated from the production field but near the main field** — close enough to move seedlings easily, apart enough to keep the nursery's care and hygiene its own. It also needs an **adequate and dependable labour supply**: a nursery fails in a week of neglect, so people must reliably be there.
