# Setting up a fruit nursery — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A windy site is protected by an established ___.
**Answer: windbreak**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The nursery stands separated from but ___ the main field.
**Answer: near**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Labour for a nursery must be adequate and ___.
**Answer: dependable**
