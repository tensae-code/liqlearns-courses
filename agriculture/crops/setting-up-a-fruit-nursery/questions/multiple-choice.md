# Setting up a fruit nursery — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A nursery site should be sheltered from —
a) strong wind  b) sunlight entirely  c) all rain
**Answer: a) strong wind**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** If the site is windy, protect it with —
a) a plastic sheet  b) taller seedlings  c) an established windbreak
**Answer: c) an established windbreak**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The nursery's soil should be —
a) pure sand  b) well drained and fertile  c) waterlogged
**Answer: b) well drained and fertile**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The nursery should stand —
a) separated from, but near, the main field  b) inside the busiest field path  c) as far away as possible
**Answer: a) separated from, but near, the main field**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A dependable labour supply matters because —
a) seedlings need no care  b) labour is cheap everywhere  c) a nursery fails in a week of neglect
**Answer: c) a nursery fails in a week of neglect**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The site must be free from —
a) all insects  b) flooding  c) birds entirely
**Answer: b) flooding**
