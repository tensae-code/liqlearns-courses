# Setting up a fruit nursery — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** List the site conditions a fruit nursery needs.
**Answer: Shelter from strong wind (or a windbreak), well-drained fertile soil, freedom from flooding, separation from but nearness to the main field, and dependable labour.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why is the nursery separated from the production field yet near it?
**Answer: Separation keeps its care and hygiene its own; nearness makes moving seedlings to the field easy.**
