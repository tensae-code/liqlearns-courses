Setting up a fruit nursery.
A fruit nursery site should be sheltered from strong wind — or protected by an established windbreak — well drained with fertile soil, and free from flooding.
The site should be separated from the production field but near the main field — close enough to move seedlings easily, apart enough to keep the nursery's care and hygiene its own.
Do it yourself.
Then say it to your companion.
