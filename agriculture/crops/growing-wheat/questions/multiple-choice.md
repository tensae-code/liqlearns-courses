# Growing wheat — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Wheat is made into —
a) only animal feed  b) bread, macaroni and spaghetti  c) sugar
**Answer: b) bread, macaroni and spaghetti**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Ethiopia wheat suits —
a) the hot lowlands  b) the cool highlands  c) the desert edge
**Answer: b) the cool highlands**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Rust and smut are fought by —
a) flooding the field  b) rotation, resistant varieties and shifting planting time  c) planting denser
**Answer: b) rotation, resistant varieties and shifting planting time**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Wheat weeding starts —
a) at harvest  b) from about a month after planting  c) before sowing only
**Answer: b) from about a month after planting**
