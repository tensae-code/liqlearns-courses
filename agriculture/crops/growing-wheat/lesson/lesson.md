# Growing wheat

## Do it first
Wheat travels the world in bread. But plant it in hot lowland and it sulks. Where in Ethiopia will it thrive? And its enemies are fungi with names like rust — how do you fight an enemy you cannot see until it strikes?

## Sub lesson 1 — The cool-highland bread cereal
Wheat, believed to have **spread from Asia**, is **globally the most important cereal**, made into **bread, macaroni and spaghetti**. It **needs cool weather**, and in Ethiopia it **suits the highlands**.

Wheat is also the cereal that **contains gluten** — the protein that lets bread dough stretch and rise. Cool air, high land, and a grain the whole world bakes with: that is wheat's identity.

## Sub lesson 2 — Managing wheat — weed, and outwit the fungi
Management: **land preparation and sowing**, then **weeding from about a month after planting** — **2,4-D against broad-leaf weeds**, as with teff.

The fungal enemies are **rust and smut**. The defence is threefold and clever rather than chemical-first: **rotation**, **resistant varieties**, and **shifting planting time so the susceptible stage misses peak disease**. Harvest follows **drying and yellowing** of the crop.
