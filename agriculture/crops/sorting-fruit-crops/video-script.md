Sorting fruit crops.
Fruit crops are classified by climate adaptation — the climate belt a fruit is built for — and by growth habit, how the plant itself grows: tree, shrub, vine or herb-like.
Fruits are also classified by fruit type — the structure of the fruit itself.
Do it yourself.
Then say it to your companion.
