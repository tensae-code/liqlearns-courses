# Sorting fruit crops — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Fruit crops are classified by —
a) climate adaptation  b) the grower's age  c) the market day
**Answer: a) climate adaptation**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Growth habit describes —
a) the price of the fruit  b) the colour of the skin  c) how the plant grows: tree, shrub, vine or herb-like
**Answer: c) how the plant grows: tree, shrub, vine or herb-like**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Climate adaptation tells you —
a) how to pack fruit  b) which climate belt a fruit is built for  c) when to harvest
**Answer: b) which climate belt a fruit is built for**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Fruit type classification is based on —
a) the structure of the fruit itself  b) the size of the farm  c) the buyer
**Answer: a) the structure of the fruit itself**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The classification is used by later sections to —
a) raise prices  b) name new fruits  c) choose techniques for this plant in this climate
**Answer: c) choose techniques for this plant in this climate**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** How many sortings does this lesson use?
a) seven  b) three — climate, habit, fruit type  c) one
**Answer: b) three — climate, habit, fruit type**
