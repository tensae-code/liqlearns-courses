# Sorting fruit crops — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Fruits are classified by climate ___.
**Answer: adaptation**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** How a plant grows — tree, shrub, vine — is its growth ___.
**Answer: habit**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Fruit type is based on the ___ of the fruit.
**Answer: structure**
