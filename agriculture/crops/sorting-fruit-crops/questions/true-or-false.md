# Sorting fruit crops — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Growth habit is one basis for classifying fruit crops.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** All fruits are classified by climate only.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A technique should be chosen for the classified plant, not for 'fruit' in general.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Fruit type has nothing to do with the fruit's structure.
**Answer: False**
