# Sorting fruit crops

## Do it first
Take six fruits you know. Sort them twice: first by the climate they like, then by how the plant grows. Notice the same fruit lands in different groups each time.

## Sub lesson 1 — Sorting by climate and habit
Fruit crops are classified by **climate adaptation** — the climate belt a fruit is built for — and by **growth habit**, how the plant itself grows: tree, shrub, vine or herb-like. The classification gives every fruit a described place before any technique is chosen for it.

## Sub lesson 2 — Sorting by fruit type
Fruits are also classified by **fruit type** — the structure of the fruit itself. Together the three sortings (climate, habit, fruit type) form the vocabulary the propagation and orchard sections use: a technique is never chosen for 'a fruit' in general, but for this classified plant in this climate.

## Do It Yourself
1. Classify five fruits you know by climate adaptation and growth habit.
