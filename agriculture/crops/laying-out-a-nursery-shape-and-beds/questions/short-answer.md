# Laying out a nursery — shape and beds — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why is the square ideal for a nursery?
**Answer: Because for a given area it minimises boundary lines — and therefore the fencing that must be bought and maintained.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What three flows does bed layout serve?
**Answer: Water reaching the beds, access for workers, and the work flow of the nursery.**
