# Laying out a nursery — shape and beds — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A square nursery uses less fencing than an irregular one of equal area.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Shape does not affect fencing cost.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Bed orientation is an important consideration.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Work flow is irrelevant inside a nursery.
**Answer: False**
