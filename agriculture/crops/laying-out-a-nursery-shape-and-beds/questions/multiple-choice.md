# Laying out a nursery — shape and beds — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A nursery's shape should be —
a) square or rectangular  b) irregular  c) circular
**Answer: a) square or rectangular**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Irregular shapes increase —
a) seedling quality  b) water  c) boundary lines and fencing
**Answer: c) boundary lines and fencing**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The ideal shape is the square because it —
a) faces north  b) minimises boundary lines  c) looks formal
**Answer: b) minimises boundary lines**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Bed layout considers —
a) orientation and location  b) only bed colour  c) the gate only
**Answer: a) orientation and location**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Beds are laid out for —
a) shade only  b) visitors  c) water, access and work flow
**Answer: c) water, access and work flow**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Seedlings should travel —
a) through the office  b) the shortest road to the gate  c) the longest path
**Answer: b) the shortest road to the gate**
