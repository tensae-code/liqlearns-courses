Laying out a nursery — shape and beds.
Design and layout begins with shape: the nursery should be square or rectangular — an irregular shape increases boundary lines and fencing, and the square is ideal because it minimises them.
The orientation and location of the nursery beds are important considerations, laid out for water, access and work flow.
Do it yourself.
Then say it to your companion.
