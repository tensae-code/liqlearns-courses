# C3 and C4 plants — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A C4 plant's first stable product of CO2 fixing has —
a) two carbons  b) three carbons  c) four carbons
**Answer: c) four carbons**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In C3 plants, photosynthesis —
a) falls as CO2 rises  b) rises with more carbon dioxide  c) does not use CO2
**Answer: b) rises with more carbon dioxide**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which crop is C3?
a) sorghum  b) maize  c) barley
**Answer: c) barley**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The two groups differ in response to —
a) only soil colour  b) light, temperature and water  c) day of the week
**Answer: b) light, temperature and water**
