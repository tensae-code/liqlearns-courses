# C3 and C4 plants — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the number in C3 and C4 count?
**Answer: The number of carbon atoms in the first stable product formed when the plant fixes carbon dioxide.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A farmer on hot, dry lowland wants a cereal. Which pathway should he choose, and which crops show it?
**Answer: A C4 crop — sorghum, maize and sugarcane are the lesson's C4 examples, suited to strong light, heat and limited water.**
