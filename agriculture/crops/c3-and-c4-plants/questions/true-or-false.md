# C3 and C4 plants — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** C3 and C4 are named by counting carbons in the first fixed product.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Sugarcane is a C3 plant.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Different crops suit different climates partly because of their pathway.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Maize is a cool-climate C3 cereal.
**Answer: False**
