# Hands-on — C3 and C4 plants

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Climate match cards
Make four crop cards — sorghum, maize, sugarcane, barley. On the back write C3 or C4 and the climate it suits. Quiz a partner until all four are right twice.

## Leaf counting sketch
Draw two leaves. In one write 'first product: 3 carbons'; in the other 'first product: 4 carbons'. Under each, sketch its weather: hot sun and little rain, or cool highland air.
