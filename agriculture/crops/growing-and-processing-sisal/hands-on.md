# Hands-on — Growing and processing sisal

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Fibre feel test
Compare a sisal-type rope or sack fibre with cotton cloth fibre between your fingers: hard against soft. Write which jobs each fibre suits and why.

## Grading table
Lay out lengths of string or fibre in mixed lengths and colours. Grade them into piles by length, then by colour, and 'pack' the best pile — the market order, practised.
