# Growing and processing sisal — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give sisal's planting spacing and the reason for its width.
**Answer: 2.5 m between rows and 80 cm between plants — wide because the plant is large and its long leaves need room to spread and be harvested.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Run sisal processing in order.
**Answer: Fibres are extracted from the leaf, untangled from the wavy mass, graded by length and colour, and packed for market.**
