# Growing and processing sisal — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Sisal fibres are used for ropes, sacks and carpets.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Sisal is a soft-fibre crop like cotton.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Sisal fibre is also used in paper-making and cloth polishing.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Fibres are packed before they are extracted.
**Answer: False**
