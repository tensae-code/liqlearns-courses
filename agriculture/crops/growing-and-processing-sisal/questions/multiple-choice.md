# Growing and processing sisal — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Sisal originally comes from —
a) Ethiopia  b) Central America  c) Asia
**Answer: b) Central America**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Sisal is planted at —
a) 25 × 75 cm  b) 2.5 m between rows and 80 cm between plants  c) 10 × 10 cm
**Answer: b) 2.5 m between rows and 80 cm between plants**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Sisal fibres are graded by —
a) weight and smell  b) length and colour  c) price and size of farm
**Answer: b) length and colour**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The last processing step is —
a) extraction  b) packing for market  c) planting
**Answer: b) packing for market**
