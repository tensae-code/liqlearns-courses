# Growing noug — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give noug's four management steps in order.
**Answer: Land preparation, sowing, weeding through the first weeks, and harvest at maturity.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What two products come from noug seed?
**Answer: Oil (mainly for local consumption) and residue cake used as animal feed.**
