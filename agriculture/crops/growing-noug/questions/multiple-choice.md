# Growing noug — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Noug originated in —
a) Mexico  b) Ethiopia  c) Central America
**Answer: b) Ethiopia**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Noug prefers —
a) sea-level swamps  b) mid-altitude areas  c) the highest peaks
**Answer: b) mid-altitude areas**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Noug oil is mainly for —
a) export only  b) local consumption  c) paint only
**Answer: b) local consumption**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** After pressing, noug's residue cake is used as —
a) fuel only  b) animal feed  c) building material
**Answer: b) animal feed**
