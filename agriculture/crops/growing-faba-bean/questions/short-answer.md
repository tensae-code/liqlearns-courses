# Growing faba bean — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** List the four parts of faba bean's integrated control.
**Answer: Hand weeding twice within six weeks of planting, crop rotation, a clean seedbed, and chemicals where needed.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How do you know faba bean is ready for dry-seed harvest?
**Answer: The leaves have fallen and the pods have dried and turned from green to black.**
