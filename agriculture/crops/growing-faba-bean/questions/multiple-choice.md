# Growing faba bean — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Faba bean control is integrated: it combines —
a) only spraying  b) hand weeding, rotation, clean seedbed and chemicals where needed  c) only hand weeding
**Answer: b) hand weeding, rotation, clean seedbed and chemicals where needed**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Hand weeding is done —
a) twice within six weeks of planting  b) once at harvest  c) never
**Answer: a) twice within six weeks of planting**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Dry faba bean is harvested when —
a) pods are green  b) leaves fall and pods dry and turn black  c) flowers open
**Answer: b) leaves fall and pods dry and turn black**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Faba bean's average national yield is about —
a) 0.5 t/ha  b) 1.9 t/ha  c) 6 t/ha
**Answer: b) 1.9 t/ha**
