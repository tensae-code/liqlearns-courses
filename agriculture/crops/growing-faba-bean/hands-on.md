# Hands-on — Growing faba bean

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Colour ladder
Draw a faba pod in three colours — green, darkening, black — and label under each: vegetable picking, wait, dry-seed harvest.

## Weeding calendar
Mark a six-week strip from planting day. Place the two hand weedings inside it and write what happens if both slip past week six.
