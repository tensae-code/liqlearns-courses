# Hands-on — What a vegetable crop feels — temperature, light, humidity, soil

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Four-dial check
For one crop in a real garden, rate its four dials — temperature, day length, humidity, soil moisture — as good or poor, and say why.

## Compacted or loose?
Compare seedlings in a trampled path edge and in a loose bed. Which emerged faster and stronger? Talk about why.
