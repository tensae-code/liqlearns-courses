# What a vegetable crop feels — temperature, light, humidity, soil — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A cold spell needed before flowering is called ___.
**Answer: vernalisation**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Flowering answering to day length is called ___.
**Answer: photoperiod**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Cabbage and lettuce need ___ humidity.
**Answer: high**
