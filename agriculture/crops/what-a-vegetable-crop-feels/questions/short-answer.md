# What a vegetable crop feels — temperature, light, humidity, soil — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain vernalisation in one sentence.
**Answer: It is the cold spell some plants (biennials and some cool-season crops) need before they switch from vegetative growth to flowering and seed production.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why is slow emergence in compacted soil dangerous for seedlings?
**Answer: Because the seedlings stay longer at the stage where disease, insects and weeds can attack them.**
