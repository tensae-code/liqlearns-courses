# What a vegetable crop feels — temperature, light, humidity, soil — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Vernalisation is —
a) a cold spell a plant needs before it switches to flowering  b) a way of drying seeds  c) a kind of fertiliser
**Answer: a) a cold spell a plant needs before it switches to flowering**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Photoperiod means —
a) the colour of a leaf  b) the weight of a fruit  c) the length of the day (and night) a plant answers to
**Answer: c) the length of the day (and night) a plant answers to**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Vernalisation matters most for —
a) fish and bees  b) biennials and some cool-season crops  c) all tropical crops
**Answer: b) biennials and some cool-season crops**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which crop needs high humidity?
a) cabbage  b) pepper  c) watermelon
**Answer: a) cabbage**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Which crop needs low humidity?
a) lettuce  b) cabbage  c) onion
**Answer: c) onion**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Slow emergence in compacted soil leaves seedlings longer vulnerable to —
a) buyers  b) disease, insects and weeds  c) frost only
**Answer: b) disease, insects and weeds**
