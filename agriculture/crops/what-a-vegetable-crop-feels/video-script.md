What a vegetable crop feels — temperature, light, humidity, soil.
Some crops need a cold spell — vernalisation — before they switch from vegetative growth to reproductive growth and flower; this is true of biennials and some cool-season crops.
Crops differ in the humidity they need: cabbage and lettuce need high humidity, while peppers, carrots, onion, watermelon and sweet potato need low humidity.
Do it yourself.
Then say it to your companion.
