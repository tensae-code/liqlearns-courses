# Running an orchard

## Do it first
Walk in your mind down an orchard row: trees on both sides, year after year. List every job that row will ask of you in one year.

## Sub lesson 1 — The standing planting's year
Orchard management is the care of a **standing fruit planting** through its year: the operations that keep trees growing, cropping and healthy in the ground they will occupy for many years. Spacing was fixed at planting — orchard spacings are tabled, with figures such as **6.2 × 7.6 m** among them — so management works with the grid it inherits.

## Sub lesson 2 — Irrigation — controlled water, an ancient art
**Irrigation** is the **controlled application of water to land** — a practice over **5,000 years old** across many cultures. It grows crops, maintains landscapes, and revegetates disturbed soils in dry areas and dry periods. In the orchard it is water delivered by plan — the right amount, at the right place, at the right time — not water left to chance.

## Do It Yourself
1. Measure or pace out a real orchard spacing and compare it with the tabled 6.2 × 7.6 m.
2. Keep a one-week water diary for one tree: what it got, when, and how.
3. Write the orchard year as a calendar of jobs, month by month.
