# Running an orchard — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does orchard management work with an inherited grid?
**Answer: Because spacing was fixed at planting; the standing trees cannot be re-spaced, so care fits the grid.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Define irrigation and give one use besides growing crops.
**Answer: The controlled application of water to land; it also maintains landscapes and revegetates disturbed soils in dry areas and periods.**
