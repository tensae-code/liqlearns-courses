# Running an orchard — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Orchard management cares for —
a) a standing fruit planting through its year  b) one harvest only  c) the nursery only
**Answer: a) a standing fruit planting through its year**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** An orchard spacing figure tabled in the book is —
a) 0.2 × 0.3 m  b) 100 × 100 m  c) 6.2 × 7.6 m
**Answer: c) 6.2 × 7.6 m**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Spacing is fixed —
a) every season  b) at planting  c) after the first harvest
**Answer: b) at planting**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Irrigation is —
a) the controlled application of water to land  b) any rain that falls  c) flooding a field
**Answer: a) the controlled application of water to land**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Irrigation has been practised for over —
a) 50 years  b) 500 years  c) 5,000 years
**Answer: c) 5,000 years**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Irrigation is also used to —
a) replace soil  b) revegetate disturbed soils in dry areas  c) dry out wetlands
**Answer: b) revegetate disturbed soils in dry areas**
