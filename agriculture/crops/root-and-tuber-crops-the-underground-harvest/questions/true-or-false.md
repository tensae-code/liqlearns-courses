# Root and tuber crops — the underground harvest — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Potato and sweet potato are root and tuber crops.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Root and tuber crops are eaten for their leaves only.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Classification by use is the frame for the unit's management sections.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** All root and tuber crops are managed identically whatever their use.
**Answer: False**
