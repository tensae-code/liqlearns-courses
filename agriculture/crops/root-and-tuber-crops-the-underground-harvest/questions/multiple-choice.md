# Root and tuber crops — the underground harvest — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Root and tuber crops carry their eaten part —
a) below the soil surface  b) in their flowers only  c) on the stem tips
**Answer: a) below the soil surface**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which is a root or tuber crop?
a) coffee  b) teff  c) cassava
**Answer: c) cassava**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Beet root and carrot belong to —
a) the spice basket  b) the root-and-tuber basket  c) the fruit basket
**Answer: b) the root-and-tuber basket**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** These crops are classified by —
a) their use  b) their price only  c) the seller's town
**Answer: a) their use**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Classification by use helps the grower —
a) avoid planting  b) rename crops  c) match each crop to the need it fills
**Answer: c) match each crop to the need it fills**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Each use carries —
a) the same practice for all  b) its own planting, feeding and harvest practice  c) no practice at all
**Answer: b) its own planting, feeding and harvest practice**
