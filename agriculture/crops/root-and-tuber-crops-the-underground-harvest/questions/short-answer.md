# Root and tuber crops — the underground harvest — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name four crops of the Ethiopian root-and-tuber basket.
**Answer: Any four of: potato, sweet potato, beet root, carrot, cassava.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why classify these crops by use?
**Answer: Because each use carries its own planting, feeding and harvest practice, and the grower can match crop to need.**
