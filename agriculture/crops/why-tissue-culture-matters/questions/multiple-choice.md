# Why tissue culture matters — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tissue culture multiplies planting material —
a) rapidly, identically and healthily  b) slowly and randomly  c) only in the rains
**Answer: a) rapidly, identically and healthily**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Laboratory plantlets are valued because they are —
a) always larger  b) already fruiting  c) disease-free
**Answer: c) disease-free**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Clean plantlets result from —
a) luck  b) clean tissue in sterile conditions  c) field soil
**Answer: b) clean tissue in sterile conditions**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Production in the laboratory is —
a) year-round, independent of season  b) only in the wet season  c) only at harvest
**Answer: a) year-round, independent of season**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Tissue culture conserves —
a) only maize  b) pests  c) threatened species
**Answer: c) threatened species**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The four benefits are the case for the technique in —
a) road building  b) Ethiopian crop production  c) ocean fishing
**Answer: b) Ethiopian crop production**
