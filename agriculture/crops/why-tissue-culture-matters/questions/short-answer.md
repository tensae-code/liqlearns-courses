# Why tissue culture matters — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** List the four benefits of tissue culture.
**Answer: Rapid multiplication of identical healthy planting material; production of disease-free plants; conservation of threatened species; year-round production independent of season.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why are laboratory plants disease-free?
**Answer: Because they grow from clean tissue under sterile conditions, away from the field's disease pressure.**
