# Why tissue culture matters — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tissue culture can multiply plants at any time of year.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Plantlets from the laboratory usually carry field diseases.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Rapid multiplication is one of the benefits.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Tissue culture cannot help threatened species.
**Answer: False**
