Why tissue culture matters.
Tissue culture's benefits begin with rapid multiplication of identical, healthy planting material and the production of disease-free plants: because the work starts from clean tissue in sterile conditions, the plantlets leave the laboratory without the diseases the field plants carry..
It also conserves threatened species, and gives year-round production independent of season — the laboratory does not wait for rains.
