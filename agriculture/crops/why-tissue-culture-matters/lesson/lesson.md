# Why tissue culture matters

## Do it first
One excellent mother plant, and a farmer who needs ten thousand copies of her — this season. Ordinary propagation cannot do it. What can?

## Sub lesson 1 — Multiplication and health
Tissue culture's benefits begin with **rapid multiplication of identical, healthy planting material** and the production of **disease-free plants**: because the work starts from clean tissue in sterile conditions, the plantlets leave the laboratory without the diseases the field plants carry.

[Picture 26623 — one mother plant becoming a hundred identical plantlets]

## Sub lesson 2 — Freedom from seasons; conservation
It also conserves **threatened species**, and gives **year-round production independent of season** — the laboratory does not wait for rains. Together these four benefits are the case for the technique in Ethiopian crop production: more plants, clean plants, rare plants kept, plants at any time.
