# Video script — Growing teff

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Teff: first by area in Ethiopia.
It endures poor soil, wet soil, dry spells — sea level to 2,800 metres.
Weed early: the seedling is a weak fighter.
Harvest when stem and panicle turn yellow.
