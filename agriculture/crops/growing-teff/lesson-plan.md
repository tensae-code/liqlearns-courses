# Lesson plan — Growing teff

**Course:** Agriculture · **Title:** Crops · **Lesson:** Growing teff
**Place in the curriculum:** Agriculture — the Crops title (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** growing teff — Ethiopia's first crop by area — its wide tolerance, careful land preparation, early weeding, disease care and harvest at yellowing
**This lesson's own level:** Grade 11 depth — it stands on the learner's own farm and fields, and on the course's earlier titles where named below.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
- Sub lesson 1 — Where teff will grow: Teff ranks **first in Ethiopia by area planted** — and it earns the place by tolerance.
- Sub lesson 2 — Managing teff — weed early, harvest yellow: Management runs in order: **careful land preparation**, **sowing**, then **early weeding about a month after planting** — teff **competes poorly as a seedling**, so the weeds must not get ahead; **2,4-D is used against broad-leaf weeds**.
Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Field crops — cereals and pulses; choosing the site. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State teff's tolerances: low-fertility and waterlogged soils, moisture shortage, sea level to 2,800 m.
2. Run teff management in order: land preparation, sowing, early weeding about a month after planting, disease care, harvest at yellowing.
3. State teff's average national yield, about 1.46 t/ha.

## What you need
A pinch of teff seed to see its size; the lesson picture.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks teff needs rich soil → it performs well on rich loam and clay but tolerates low-fertility and waterlogged soils too.
- Plans late weeding → teff competes poorly as a seedling; weed about a month after planting.
