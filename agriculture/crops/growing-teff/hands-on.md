# Hands-on — Growing teff

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Seed-size study
Count roughly how many teff grains fit on a fingernail beside ten maize grains. Write what the size means for seedbed care and weeding.

## Yellow-signal walk
In teff season, walk a teff field weekly and note the panicle colour. Mark the week it turns yellow — the harvest signal — in your notebook.
