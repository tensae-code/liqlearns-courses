# Growing teff — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name three conditions teff tolerates that defeat other crops.
**Answer: Low-fertility soils, waterlogged soils and moisture shortage.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why must teff be weeded early?
**Answer: Because teff competes poorly as a seedling — weeds left standing will outgrow and smother it.**
