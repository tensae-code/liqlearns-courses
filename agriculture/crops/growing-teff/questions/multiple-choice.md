# Growing teff — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Teff ranks in Ethiopia —
a) third by area  b) first by area planted  c) last by area
**Answer: b) first by area planted**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Teff grows from —
a) only 2,000 m up  b) sea level to 2,800 m  c) only sea level
**Answer: b) sea level to 2,800 m**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Weeding teff happens —
a) about a month after planting, because seedlings compete poorly  b) only at harvest  c) never
**Answer: a) about a month after planting, because seedlings compete poorly**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Teff is harvested when —
a) stems and panicle turn yellow  b) the field floods  c) flowers first open
**Answer: a) stems and panicle turn yellow**
