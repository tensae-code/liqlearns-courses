# Growing teff — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Teff tolerates waterlogged soils.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Teff's average national yield is about 5 t/ha.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** 2,4-D is used against broad-leaf weeds in teff.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Teff seedlings compete strongly against weeds from the start.
**Answer: False**
