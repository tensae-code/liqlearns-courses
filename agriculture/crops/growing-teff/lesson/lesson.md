# Growing teff

## Do it first
Teff seed is almost dust — thousands of grains in one pinch. A seedling that small cannot fight weeds. Guess which job in teff farming cannot wait, and which soil that defeats other crops teff can still use.

## Sub lesson 1 — Where teff will grow
Teff ranks **first in Ethiopia by area planted** — and it earns the place by tolerance. It grows on **low-fertility soils**, **waterlogged soils** and through **moisture shortage**, from **sea level to 2,800 m** (main areas **1,500–2,800 m**), performing well on **nutrient-rich loam and clay**.

The tiny seed carries a mighty reputation: teff is nutritionally prized — called a **'super food'** — and its average national yield is about **1.46 t/ha**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Managing teff — weed early, harvest yellow
Management runs in order: **careful land preparation**, **sowing**, then **early weeding about a month after planting** — teff **competes poorly as a seedling**, so the weeds must not get ahead; **2,4-D is used against broad-leaf weeds**. Care follows against **fungal disease where water accumulates**.

**Harvest when stems and panicle turn yellow.** The panicle's colour is the signal: green means wait, yellow means cut.
