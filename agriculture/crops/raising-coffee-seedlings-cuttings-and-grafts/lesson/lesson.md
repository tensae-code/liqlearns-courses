# Raising coffee seedlings — cuttings and grafts

## Do it first
A coffee plant can begin three ways: from seed, from a cut piece of a good plant, or from two pieces joined. Which beginning copies a proven mother plant exactly?

## Sub lesson 1 — Nurseries for the seedling crops
Coffee, tea and spices begin in **nurseries**: establishment and management of the nursery gives seedlings a sheltered, tended start before the field. In the nursery the propagator's arts are practised at small scale, where every young plant can be watched.

## Sub lesson 2 — Coffee's arts — cuttings, grafts, micro-propagation
Coffee propagation is worked in detail: **single-node cuttings with half-trimmed leaf pairs**; **grafting** — joining two pieces of living plant tissue — done at the seedling's **soldier or butterfly stage**; and **micro-propagation**, the biotechnology route to coffee planting material. Each art multiplies a chosen plant while keeping its character.
