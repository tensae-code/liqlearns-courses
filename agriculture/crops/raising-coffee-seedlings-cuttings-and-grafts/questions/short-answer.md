# Raising coffee seedlings — cuttings and grafts — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Describe a coffee cutting as the book teaches it.
**Answer: A single-node cutting whose leaf pair is half-trimmed, set in the nursery to root.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name coffee's three propagation routes in this lesson.
**Answer: Seed (through the nursery), cuttings and grafting (vegetative), and micro-propagation (biotechnology).**
