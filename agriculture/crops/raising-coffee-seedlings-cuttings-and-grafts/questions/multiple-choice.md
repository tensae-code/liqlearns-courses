# Raising coffee seedlings — cuttings and grafts — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A nursery gives seedlings —
a) a sheltered, tended start before the field  b) no water  c) a market stall
**Answer: a) a sheltered, tended start before the field**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Coffee cuttings are made as —
a) whole branches  b) roots only  c) single-node cuttings with half-trimmed leaf pairs
**Answer: c) single-node cuttings with half-trimmed leaf pairs**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The leaf pairs on a coffee cutting are —
a) doubled  b) half-trimmed  c) removed entirely
**Answer: b) half-trimmed**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Grafting joins —
a) two pieces of living plant tissue  b) two seeds  c) a leaf to soil
**Answer: a) two pieces of living plant tissue**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Coffee grafting is done at the seedling's —
a) flowering stage  b) harvest stage  c) soldier or butterfly stage
**Answer: c) soldier or butterfly stage**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Micro-propagation is —
a) a drying method  b) the biotechnology route to planting material  c) a pruning method
**Answer: b) the biotechnology route to planting material**
