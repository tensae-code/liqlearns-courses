# Hands-on — Raising coffee seedlings — cuttings and grafts

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Cutting bench
With a grower, prepare real cuttings: one node, half-trimmed leaves, set in the bed, labelled and dated.

## Graft-stage spotting
In a nursery, find seedlings at soldier and butterfly stage and mark which are ready for grafting.
