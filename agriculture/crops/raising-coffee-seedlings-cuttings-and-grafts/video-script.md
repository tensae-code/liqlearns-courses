Raising coffee seedlings — cuttings and grafts.
Coffee, tea and spices begin in nurseries: establishment and management of the nursery gives seedlings a sheltered, tended start before the field.
Coffee propagation is worked in detail: single-node cuttings with half-trimmed leaf pairs; grafting — joining two pieces of living plant tissue — done at the seedling's soldier or butterfly stage; and micro-propagation, the biotechnology route to coffee planting material.
Do it yourself.
Then say it to your companion.
