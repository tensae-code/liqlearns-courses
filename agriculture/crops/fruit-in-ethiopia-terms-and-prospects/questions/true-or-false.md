# Fruit in Ethiopia — terms and prospects — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A mistake in a young fruit planting can be kept for a decade.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Fruit terms are not needed to follow propagation and orchard work.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Ethiopia could grow far more fruit than it grows today.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Fruit crops matter only as decoration.
**Answer: False**
