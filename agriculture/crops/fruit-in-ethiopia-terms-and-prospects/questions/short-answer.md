# Fruit in Ethiopia — terms and prospects — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does a fruit grower need exact terms more than a one-season crop grower?
**Answer: Because a fruit planting lives for years, so a mistake in understanding becomes a mistake kept in a tree for a long time.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** State Ethiopia's fruit position in two sentences.
**Answer: Fruit is important for food, income and nutrition, and the country holds large potential, but problems hold production back; technique closes the gap.**
