# Fruit in Ethiopia — terms and prospects — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why are fruit terms learned first?
a) so later fruit work can be followed precisely  b) to fill the first pages  c) because trees have no other needs
**Answer: a) so later fruit work can be followed precisely**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A fruit planting is special because it —
a) is harvested once and forgotten  b) needs no care  c) lives for years, so early mistakes last
**Answer: c) lives for years, so early mistakes last**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The words of fruit production cover —
a) only export rules  b) trees, their parts, planting material and care  c) only shop prices
**Answer: b) trees, their parts, planting material and care**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Fruit crops matter to Ethiopia for —
a) food, income and nutrition  b) decoration only  c) shade only
**Answer: a) food, income and nutrition**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The unit's technique sections exist to —
a) replace farmers  b) end fruit growing  c) close the gap between problems and potential
**Answer: c) close the gap between problems and potential**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Ethiopia's fruit potential rests on —
a) one single region  b) land, climates and markets  c) imports only
**Answer: b) land, climates and markets**
