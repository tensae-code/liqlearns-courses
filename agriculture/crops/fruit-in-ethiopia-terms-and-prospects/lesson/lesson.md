# Fruit in Ethiopia — terms and prospects

## Do it first
Name every fruit you have eaten this month. Which grew near your home, and which travelled far to reach you?

## Sub lesson 1 — The words of fruit growing
The common terms of fruit production are learned first — the words for the trees, their parts, their planting material and their care — so that every later section of fruit work can be followed precisely. Fruit work uses its own vocabulary because a fruit planting lives for years: a mistake in words becomes a mistake in a tree you keep for a decade.

## Sub lesson 2 — Ethiopia's fruit position
Fruit crops matter to Ethiopia for food, income and nutrition. Against that importance stand the **problems holding production back**, and against the problems stands the country's **potential** — land, climates and markets that could carry far more fruit than is grown today. The unit's technique sections exist to close that gap.
