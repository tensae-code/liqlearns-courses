# Genetic engineering — moving single genes — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Breeding can drag undesirable genes along with desirable ones.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Genetic engineering shuffles millions of crosses.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Engineering can transfer a gene between distantly related organisms.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Conventional breeding guarantees the exact gene combination wanted.
**Answer: False**
