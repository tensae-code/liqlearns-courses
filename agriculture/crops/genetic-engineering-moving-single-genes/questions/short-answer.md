# Genetic engineering — moving single genes — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** List conventional breeding's three limits.
**Answer: No guarantee of a particular gene combination from millions of crosses; undesirable genes may move with desirable ones; it takes a long time.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How does genetic engineering differ from breeding?
**Answer: It transfers one or just a few chosen genes directly, even between distantly related organisms, instead of shuffling whole genomes by crossing.**
