# Genetic engineering — moving single genes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Conventional breeding offers —
a) little or no guarantee of a particular gene combination  b) exact control of each gene  c) instant results
**Answer: a) little or no guarantee of a particular gene combination**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Crossing may transfer —
a) no genes at all  b) only the wanted gene  c) undesirable genes along with desirable ones
**Answer: c) undesirable genes along with desirable ones**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Conventional breeding also —
a) uses laboratories only  b) takes a long time  c) is finished in a day
**Answer: b) takes a long time**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Genetic engineering transfers —
a) one or just a few genes directly  b) the whole genome by crossing  c) no genetic material
**Answer: a) one or just a few genes directly**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Engineered genes can move between —
a) identical twins only  b) plants of one field only  c) closely or distantly related organisms
**Answer: c) closely or distantly related organisms**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The key difference is —
a) plant height  b) precision: engineering moves only the chosen gene  c) speed of harvest
**Answer: b) precision: engineering moves only the chosen gene**
