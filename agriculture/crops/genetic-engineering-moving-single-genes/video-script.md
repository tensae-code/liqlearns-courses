Genetic engineering — moving single genes.
Conventional breeding has hard limits: it has little or no guarantee of any particular gene combination from millions of crosses, it may transfer undesirable genes along with desirable ones, and it takes a long time.
Genetic engineering (modification) instead transfers one or just a few genes directly, between closely or distantly related organisms.
Do it yourself.
Then say it to your companion.
