# Vegetables in Ethiopia — small plots, big potential — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Over 6.7 million households were estimated to make a living involving vegetables.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Commercial farms grow vegetables only for their own households.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Leafy and leguminous vegetables are rich in protein.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Drought is not a problem for vegetable production.
**Answer: False**
