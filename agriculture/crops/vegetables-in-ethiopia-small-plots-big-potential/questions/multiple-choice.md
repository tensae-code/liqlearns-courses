# Vegetables in Ethiopia — small plots, big potential — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A vegetable is a plant whose — are eaten
a) fruit, seeds, roots, tubers, bulbs, stems, leaves or flower parts  b) flowers only, never roots  c) wood and bark only
**Answer: a) fruit, seeds, roots, tubers, bulbs, stems, leaves or flower parts**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Smallholders grow vegetables mainly for —
a) export only  b) animal feed only  c) home consumption
**Answer: c) home consumption**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Commercial private farms grow vegetables —
a) never to sell  b) solely for the market  c) only for their own pots
**Answer: b) solely for the market**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Beta-carotene in vegetables protects —
a) the visual process (eyesight)  b) the soil from rain  c) the plant from cold
**Answer: a) the visual process (eyesight)**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Prolonged beta-carotene deficiency leads to —
a) taller plants  b) sweeter fruit  c) blindness
**Answer: c) blindness**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is one of the problems holding vegetable production back?
a) soil that is too fertile  b) the high price of fuel for pumping irrigation water  c) too many buyers
**Answer: b) the high price of fuel for pumping irrigation water**
