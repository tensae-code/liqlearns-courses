# Vegetables in Ethiopia — small plots, big potential — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two parts of a plant that can be eaten as vegetables.
**Answer: Any two of: fruit, seeds, roots, tubers, bulbs, stems, leaves or flower parts.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** List three problems that hold vegetable production back.
**Answer: Any three of: poor soil fertility, pests, drought, postharvest losses, the high price of fuel for pumping irrigation water.**
