## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Define a vegetable by the plant parts eaten.
2. Say who grows vegetables for home use and who for the market, with the 6.7 million households figure.
3. Name the main problems holding vegetable production back.

## What you need
A notebook; a walk to a vegetable plot or market if one is near.
