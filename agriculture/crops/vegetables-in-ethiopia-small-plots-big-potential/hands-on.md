# Hands-on — Vegetables in Ethiopia — small plots, big potential

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Market or garden walk
Visit a vegetable plot or market stall. Count how many different vegetables you can name and note which plant part each one is.

## Problem hunt
With a grower, walk a plot and look for one sign of each problem you can find: poor soil, pests, dryness. Talk about one fix for each.
