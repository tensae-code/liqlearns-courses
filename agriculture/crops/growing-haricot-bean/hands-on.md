# Hands-on — Growing haricot bean — and safe storage

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Dryness line
Lay beans in the sun in two trays — one for a short drying, one long. With an adult, compare sound and bite. Label which tray approaches the below-12% storage rule and why the number matters.

## Store inspection
Inspect a home grain store with permission: look for weevil holes in beans. Write two store improvements following the lesson: drier grain, treated and sealed storage.
