# Growing haricot bean — and safe storage — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Haricot bean originally came from —
a) Ethiopia  b) Mexico  c) Asia
**Answer: b) Mexico**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Haricot bean grows at —
a) sea level only  b) 1,400–2,000 m  c) above 3,500 m
**Answer: b) 1,400–2,000 m**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In store, haricot grain is attacked by —
a) rust  b) weevils  c) armyworm
**Answer: b) weevils**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Grain is protected in store by drying it —
a) to 30% moisture  b) below 12% moisture  c) not at all
**Answer: b) below 12% moisture**
