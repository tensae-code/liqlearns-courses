# Growing haricot bean — and safe storage — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give haricot bean's weed control and harvest signals.
**Answer: A clean seedbed and hand weeding within four weeks; harvest when leaves fall and pods yellow and dry (or green for vegetable use).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How is stored haricot grain protected from weevils?
**Answer: By drying the grain below 12% moisture, with chemical treatment where used.**
