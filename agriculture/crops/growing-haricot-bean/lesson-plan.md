# Lesson plan — Growing haricot bean — and safe storage

**Course:** Agriculture · **Title:** Crops · **Lesson:** Growing haricot bean — and safe storage
**Place in the curriculum:** Agriculture — the Crops title (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** growing haricot (common) bean — from Mexico, at 1,400–2,000 m, alone or mixed with maize and sorghum — and keeping the harvest safe in store by drying grain below 12% moisture against weevils
**This lesson's own level:** Grade 11 depth — it stands on the learner's own farm and fields, and on the course's earlier titles where named below.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Pulses — protein and nitrogen from one crop. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Place haricot bean: origin Mexico, 1,400–2,000 m, most soil types, alone or mixed with maize and sorghum, in rotation.
2. Run its weed control: clean seedbed and hand weeding within four weeks; maturity in three to four months.
3. State the storage rule exactly: dry grain below 12% moisture (with chemical treatment) against weevils.

## What you need
A handful of dried beans to test dryness (the bite/sound test as described); the lesson picture.
