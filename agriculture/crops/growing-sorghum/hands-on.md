# Hands-on — Growing sorghum

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Earth-up trial
In pots or a garden row, earth up half the young sorghum (or maize) plants and leave half flat. After wind or a fan test, compare which stand. Record the result.

## Dry-land match
List crops for a dry lowland field and defend sorghum's place at the top using its moisture-deficit fit.
