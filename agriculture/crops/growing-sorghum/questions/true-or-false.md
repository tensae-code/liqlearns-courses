# Growing sorghum — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Sorghum is sown from March to May.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Sorghum seed is planted deeper than maize seed.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Earthing up is done about a month after planting.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Lodging means the plants are eaten by birds.
**Answer: False**
