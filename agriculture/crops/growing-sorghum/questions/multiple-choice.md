# Growing sorghum — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Sorghum suits —
a) wet highlands  b) lowland and moisture-deficit areas  c) waterlogged clay
**Answer: b) lowland and moisture-deficit areas**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Sorghum is sown —
a) 0.5–1 m between rows, about 40 cm between plants, about 2.5 cm deep  b) 10 cm apart and 20 cm deep  c) only broadcast on the surface
**Answer: a) 0.5–1 m between rows, about 40 cm between plants, about 2.5 cm deep**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Earthing up helps sorghum —
a) flower earlier  b) resist lodging in wind  c) make more seed heads
**Answer: b) resist lodging in wind**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The common sorghum fertiliser practice is about —
a) 32 kg nitrogen and 10 kg phosphorus per hectare  b) 100 kg of each  c) no fertiliser
**Answer: a) 32 kg nitrogen and 10 kg phosphorus per hectare**
