# Growing sorghum — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give sorghum's full sowing specification.
**Answer: Cultivate two or three times; broadcast or row-sow at 0.5–1 m between rows and about 40 cm between plants, about 2.5 cm deep, from March to May.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is lodging, and how does earthing up fight it?
**Answer: Lodging is the crop being laid flat by wind; earthing up heaps soil around the stem, giving it a firmer, anchored base.**
