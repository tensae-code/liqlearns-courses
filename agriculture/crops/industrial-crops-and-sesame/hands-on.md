# Hands-on — Industrial crops — and growing sesame

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Third-yellow judge
Draw a sesame plant three times: green, one-third yellow, fully yellow. Label the harvest moment and the loss that follows it.

## Shatter test
If sesame or a similar pod is available, let one pod fully dry on the plant and observe it shatter. Record how many seeds escaped.
