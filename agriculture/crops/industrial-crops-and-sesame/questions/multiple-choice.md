# Industrial crops — and growing sesame — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Industrial crops split into —
a) cereals and pulses  b) oil, fibre and sugar crops  c) roots and leaves
**Answer: b) oil, fibre and sugar crops**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Industrial crops are grown mainly —
a) for home decoration  b) as raw material for industry  c) as animal feed only
**Answer: b) as raw material for industry**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Sesame is harvested when —
a) fully green  b) about a third of leaves, stems and pods have yellowed  c) all pods have shattered
**Answer: b) about a third of leaves, stems and pods have yellowed**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Delaying sesame harvest —
a) improves oil  b) lets pods shatter and seed is lost  c) stops shattering
**Answer: b) lets pods shatter and seed is lost**
