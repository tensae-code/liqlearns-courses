# Industrial crops — and growing sesame — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Sesame is believed to be African in origin.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Sesame is a fibre crop.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Sesame matures in up to five months.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Sesame's average national yield is about 3 t/ha.
**Answer: False**
