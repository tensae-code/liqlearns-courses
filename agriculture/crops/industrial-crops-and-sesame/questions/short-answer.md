# Industrial crops — and growing sesame — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three groups of industrial crops.
**Answer: Oil crops, fibre crops and sugar crops.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why must sesame harvest not be delayed?
**Answer: Because the ripe pods shatter and scatter the seed, so delayed harvest loses the crop on the ground.**
