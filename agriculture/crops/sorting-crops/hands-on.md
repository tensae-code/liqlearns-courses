# Hands-on — Sorting crops — grains, oils, fibres, forage

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Split and sort
Split a bean and try to split a maize grain. Draw both seeds and label endosperm and cotyledons where each keeps its food.

## Four baskets at home
Find or name one item at home for each basket: grain, oil, fibre (a cloth), forage (animal feed). Photograph or list them in order.
