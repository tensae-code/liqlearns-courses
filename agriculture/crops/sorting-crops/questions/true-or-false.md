# Sorting crops — grains, oils, fibres, forage — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Legume protein lacks some essential amino acids.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A bean seed stores its food in the endosperm like maize.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Oil crops are sources of essential fatty acids and fat-soluble vitamins.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Flax, jute and hemp are grain crops.
**Answer: False**
