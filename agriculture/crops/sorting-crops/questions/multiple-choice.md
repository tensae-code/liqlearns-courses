# Sorting crops — grains, oils, fibres, forage — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Monocots store their seed food in the —
a) cotyledons  b) endosperm, mostly starch  c) roots
**Answer: b) endosperm, mostly starch**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Dicot seeds are rich in —
a) only water  b) protein and oil  c) sand
**Answer: b) protein and oil**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The most important fibre crop named is —
a) teff  b) cotton, for its lint  c) maize
**Answer: b) cotton, for its lint**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Forage crops are grown —
a) for clothing  b) to feed animals  c) for oil
**Answer: b) to feed animals**
