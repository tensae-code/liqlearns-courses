# Sorting crops — grains, oils, fibres, forage — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How do you tell a monocot seed from a dicot seed in your hand?
**Answer: A dicot seed splits into two cotyledons that store protein- and oil-rich food; a monocot stores mostly starchy food in the endosperm and does not split into two halves.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the four use-groups of crops with one example each.
**Answer: Grain crops (e.g. maize), oil crops (e.g. noug), fibre crops (e.g. cotton), forage crops (grown to feed animals).**
