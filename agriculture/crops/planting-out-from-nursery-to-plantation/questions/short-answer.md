# Planting out — from nursery to plantation — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** List establishment's steps.
**Answer: Site preparation, then planting out of plantable nursery seedlings at spacing in lines.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does young-plantation management include?
**Answer: Protection, replacement of failures, and tending through the first seasons until the planting becomes a stand.**
