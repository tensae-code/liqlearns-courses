# Planting out — from nursery to plantation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Plantation establishment starts from —
a) nursery seedlings  b) wild seeds only  c) imported logs
**Answer: a) nursery seedlings**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Site preparation comes —
a) after the stand matures  b) never  c) before planting out
**Answer: c) before planting out**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Seedlings are set —
a) in the nursery shade  b) at their spacing, in lines  c) at random
**Answer: b) at their spacing, in lines**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Management carries the plantation —
a) into a stand  b) back to the nursery  c) to immediate harvest
**Answer: a) into a stand**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Early management includes —
a) no visits  b) thinning mature trees  c) protection and replacing failures
**Answer: c) protection and replacing failures**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Establishment truly ends when —
a) planting day ends  b) the planting stands as a young stand  c) the truck leaves
**Answer: b) the planting stands as a young stand**
