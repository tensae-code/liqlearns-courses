# Planting out — from nursery to plantation — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Planting out uses plantable seedlings from the nursery.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A plantation needs no management after planting day.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Replacing failed seedlings is part of young-plantation management.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Site preparation happens after planting.
**Answer: False**
