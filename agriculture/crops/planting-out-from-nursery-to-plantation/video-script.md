# Video script — Planting out — from nursery to plantation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Planting out — from nursery to plantation.
Plantation establishment takes nursery seedlings to the field: site preparation, planting out — the prepared site receives the plantable seedlings, set at their spacing in their lines, with the care their nursery quality deserves..
Then management of the young plantation into a stand: protection, replacement of failures, tending through the first seasons until the planting stands as a young forest or plantation in its own right.
Do it yourself.
Then say it to your companion.
