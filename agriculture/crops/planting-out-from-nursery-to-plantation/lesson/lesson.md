# Planting out — from nursery to plantation

## Do it first
Graduation day: the seedlings leave in bundles for a hillside they have never seen. Everything the nursery built can be lost in one careless planting day — or kept.

## Sub lesson 1 — Establishing the plantation
**Plantation establishment** takes nursery seedlings to the field: **site preparation, planting out** — the prepared site receives the plantable seedlings, set at their spacing in their lines, with the care their nursery quality deserves.

[Picture 26668 — seedlings being planted out in lines on a prepared site]

## Sub lesson 2 — Managing the young plantation
Then **management of the young plantation into a stand**: protection, replacement of failures, tending through the first seasons until the planting stands as a young forest or plantation in its own right. Establishment ends not at planting, but at a standing crop of trees.

## Say it to your companion
Explain this lesson to your companion in your own words, using one real example from your own area for each sub lesson. Your companion asks one question about each part — answer from the lesson, not from memory of other lessons.

## Do It Yourself
1. Write a planting-day plan: transport, spacing, tools, water, order of work.
2. Mark a planting line with string and pegs at a chosen spacing.
3. Keep a survival count: revisit planted seedlings after a month and log losses.
