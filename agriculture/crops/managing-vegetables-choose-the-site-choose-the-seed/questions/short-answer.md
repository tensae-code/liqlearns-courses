# Managing vegetables — choose the site, choose the seed — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** List the four things weighed in site selection.
**Answer: The site's history, soil analysis results, the techniques to be used, and market information (sales, demand and prices).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** List the three tests for choosing planting material.
**Answer: Adaptation to local conditions, resistance to pests and diseases, and fit with consumer demand.**
