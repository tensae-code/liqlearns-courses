# Managing vegetables — choose the site, choose the seed — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A site's history — what grew and sickened there — matters in site selection.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Market prices should be checked only after harvest.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Seed adapted to local conditions is a management principle.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Resistance to pests and diseases does not matter when choosing planting material.
**Answer: False**
