# Managing vegetables — choose the site, choose the seed — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Site selection weighs the site's —
a) history, soil analysis, techniques and market information  b) colour only  c) distance from the road only
**Answer: a) history, soil analysis, techniques and market information**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Market information means —
a) the weather in other countries  b) seed colours  c) sales, demand and prices
**Answer: c) sales, demand and prices**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Soil analysis results are used to —
a) choose the buyer  b) know the soil before planting  c) price the harvest
**Answer: b) know the soil before planting**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Planting material should be chosen for —
a) adaptation to local conditions  b) the prettiest packet  c) the longest name
**Answer: a) adaptation to local conditions**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Good planting material also needs —
a) no water needs  b) a foreign origin  c) resistance to pests and diseases
**Answer: c) resistance to pests and diseases**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Planting material must fit —
a) last year's prices only  b) consumer demand  c) the seller's shelf only
**Answer: b) consumer demand**
