- The Grade 11 Agriculture course's opening of this enterprise or system, where there is one — a recommended first read, never a gate; this lesson stands alone for a learner who lands on it directly.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Run a site selection using history, soil analysis, techniques and market information.
2. Choose planting material by adaptation, resistance and consumer demand.
3. Outline the standing-crop management that follows planting.

## What you need
A notebook; one real plot and two seed lots or packets to compare.
