# Video script — Managing vegetables — choose the site, choose the seed

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Managing vegetables — choose the site, choose the seed.
Management starts with site selection: weigh the site's history (what grew and what sickened there before), the soil analysis results, the techniques you will be able to use, and market information — sales, demand and prices.
Planting material is chosen for adaptation to local conditions, resistance to pests and diseases, and fit with consumer demand — seed that suits the place, survives its enemies, and gives what buyers want.
Do it yourself.
Then say it to your companion.
