# Coffee and tea — where they come from, what they earn — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the legend, tea began when leaves blew into —
a) Shen Nung's servant's boiling water  b) a river  c) a coffee pot
**Answer: a) Shen Nung's servant's boiling water**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Tea originated in —
a) Ethiopia  b) India  c) southwest China
**Answer: c) southwest China**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Tea first served as —
a) a dye  b) a medicinal drink  c) animal feed
**Answer: b) a medicinal drink**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Coffee stands as Ethiopia's —
a) flagship export crop  b) least important crop  c) imported luxury
**Answer: a) flagship export crop**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The unit treats coffee's —
a) price only  b) legends only  c) history, origin, production status and economic importance
**Answer: c) history, origin, production status and economic importance**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Origins matter in this unit because they explain —
a) the colour of cups  b) where crops grow, who wants them and what they earn  c) nothing practical
**Answer: b) where crops grow, who wants them and what they earn**
