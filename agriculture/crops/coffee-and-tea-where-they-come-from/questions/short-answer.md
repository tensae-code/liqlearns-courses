# Coffee and tea — where they come from, what they earn — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tell the tea-origin legend in two sentences.
**Answer: The Chinese herbalist Shen Nung sat under a tree while his servant boiled water; leaves blew into the water, and the drink that resulted began tea's story.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does coffee anchor this unit?
**Answer: Because coffee is Ethiopia's flagship export crop, with major production status and economic importance.**
