# Coffee and tea — where they come from, what they earn

## Do it first
Two drinks begin this unit: one from a cherry, one from a leaf. Both travelled the world. Trace each one's journey in your mind before reading.

## Sub lesson 1 — Tea — leaves in boiling water
Tea's history opens with the legend of the Chinese herbalist **Shen Nung**, under whose tree leaves blew into his servant's boiling water. Tea originated in **southwest China** as a **medicinal drink**, then spread across cultures over thousands of years until it became a world crop and a world trade.

## Sub lesson 2 — Coffee — Ethiopia's flagship
Coffee's history, origin, production status and economic importance stand beside tea's. Coffee is **Ethiopia's flagship export crop** — the crop whose earnings anchor the unit, and the reason this unit carries the heaviest weight of the Grade 12 year. Origins are not decoration here: they explain where each crop grows, who wants it, and what it earns.

## Say it to your companion
Explain this lesson to your companion in your own words, using one real example from your own area for each sub lesson. Your companion asks one question about each part — answer from the lesson, not from memory of other lessons.

## Do It Yourself
1. Write the two origin stories side by side: tea and coffee.
2. Find coffee and tea in a local shop and note their forms and prices.
3. Ask a coffee grower or seller what coffee earnings pay for in their household.
