# Lesson plan — Cropping systems — mono, mixed, intercrop, relay, rotation

**Course:** Agriculture · **Title:** Crops · **Lesson:** Cropping systems — mono, mixed, intercrop, relay, rotation
**Place in the curriculum:** Agriculture — the Crops title (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the five cropping systems — mono-cropping, mixed cropping, intercropping, relay planting and crop rotation — and what each does for soil, erosion and disease
**This lesson's own level:** Grade 11 depth — it stands on the learner's own farm and fields, and on the course's earlier titles where named below.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
- Sub lesson 1 — Sharing land — mono, mixed, intercrop: A **cropping system** is the types, sequences and practices used to grow crops.
- Sub lesson 2 — Sharing time — relay and rotation: **Relay planting** starts time-sharing: a **second crop is planted after the first has grown alone for a while**, so the two overlap for part of the season.
Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Sorting crops — grains, oils, fibres, forage. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Define a cropping system.
2. Tell mono-cropping, mixed cropping and intercropping apart by how crops share the plot.
3. Explain relay planting and crop rotation, and name intercropping's gifts: soil cover, less erosion, slower disease spread.

## If the learner is stuck
- Mixes mixed cropping with intercropping → intercropping is the tidy one: crops in regular rows at the same time.
- Mixes relay with rotation → relay overlaps within a season; rotation changes across seasons.
