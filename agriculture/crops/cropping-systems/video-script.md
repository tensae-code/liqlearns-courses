# Video script — Cropping systems — mono, mixed, intercrop, relay, rotation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

One crop alone: mono.
Crops together: mixed. In rows: intercrop.
Second crop joins late: relay.
Crop changes each season: rotation.
