# Hands-on — Cropping systems — mono, mixed, intercrop, relay, rotation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Five-way field
Draw one field five times, once per system. Label rows, seasons and the second crop's entry point for relay.

## Rotation wheel
Cut a paper circle into three season slices. Write a crop on each, pin the centre, and turn the wheel: cereal → legume → cereal. Say aloud what each turn gives the soil.
