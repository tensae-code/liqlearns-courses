# Cropping systems — mono, mixed, intercrop, relay, rotation — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two advantages of intercropping maize with a legume.
**Answer: Better soil cover, less erosion, and slower disease spread (any two).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How does relay planting differ from crop rotation?
**Answer: Relay planting overlaps two crops within one season — the second joins after the first has grown alone a while; rotation changes the crop across whole seasons in sequence.**
