# Cropping systems — mono, mixed, intercrop, relay, rotation — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Intercropping gives better soil cover and less erosion.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Mono-cropping is typical of small household gardens only.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A disease spreads more slowly through an intercropped stand than a pure stand.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Rotation and relay planting are the same system.
**Answer: False**
