# Cropping systems — mono, mixed, intercrop, relay, rotation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Mono-cropping means —
a) many crops mixed  b) one crop on a plot  c) no crop at all
**Answer: b) one crop on a plot**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Intercropping is —
a) crops in regular rows at the same time, e.g. maize with a legume  b) one crop per year only  c) crops grown in pots
**Answer: a) crops in regular rows at the same time, e.g. maize with a legume**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Relay planting is when —
a) the second crop is planted after the first has grown alone for a while  b) all crops are harvested together  c) the field is burned
**Answer: a) the second crop is planted after the first has grown alone for a while**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Crop rotation —
a) changes the crop on a plot in sequence across seasons  b) spins the field  c) means planting in circles
**Answer: a) changes the crop on a plot in sequence across seasons**
