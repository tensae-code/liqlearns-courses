# Cropping systems — mono, mixed, intercrop, relay, rotation — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The types, sequences and practices used to grow crops make up a cropping ___.
**Answer: system**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Changing the crop on a plot season by season is called crop ___.
**Answer: rotation**
