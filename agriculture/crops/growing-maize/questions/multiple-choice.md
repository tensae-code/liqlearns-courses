# Growing maize — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Maize rows are spaced —
a) 25 cm apart  b) 75 cm apart, with seeds 25 cm apart in the row  c) 2 m apart
**Answer: b) 75 cm apart, with seeds 25 cm apart in the row**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Maize seed is planted —
a) 5–7 cm deep  b) 30 cm deep  c) on the surface only
**Answer: a) 5–7 cm deep**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Farmers commonly apply about —
a) 41 kg nitrogen and 20 kg phosphorus per hectare  b) 200 kg nitrogen per hectare  c) no fertiliser ever
**Answer: a) 41 kg nitrogen and 20 kg phosphorus per hectare**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Maize is Ethiopia's —
a) least productive cereal  b) most productive cereal per hectare  c) only imported cereal
**Answer: b) most productive cereal per hectare**
