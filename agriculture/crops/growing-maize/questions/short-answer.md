# Growing maize — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give maize's full planting specification.
**Answer: Plough two or three times; row-plant 25 cm between seeds and 75 cm between rows, 5–7 cm deep, at the start of the rains (March–April).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why should fertiliser rates follow the soil's level?
**Answer: Because soils differ in what they already hold; the right rate feeds this soil's gap, and a fixed recipe can under-feed one field and waste money on another.**
