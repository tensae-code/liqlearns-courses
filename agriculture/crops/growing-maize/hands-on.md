# Hands-on — Growing maize

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Spacing in the soil
With string and sticks, lay out two maize rows 75 cm apart and mark seed spots every 25 cm. Bury three test seeds at 3, 6 and 12 cm in pots and watch which rises best.

## Fertiliser arithmetic
A farmer has 2 hectares. At 41 kg N and 20 kg P per hectare, how much of each does the common practice call for? (82 kg N, 40 kg P.) Then write why a soil test could change the answer.
