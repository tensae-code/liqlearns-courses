# Pulses — protein and nitrogen from one crop — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What are pulses, and what else are they used for besides dry seed?
**Answer: Pulses are the edible dry seeds of legumes; they are also eaten as immature pods and used as animal feed.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain how a pulse helps the cereal planted after it.
**Answer: The legume fixes atmospheric nitrogen through its root nodules and leaves nitrogen in the soil, which the following cereal crop uses.**
