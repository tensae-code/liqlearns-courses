# Pulses — protein and nitrogen from one crop — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Pulses are —
a) the leaves of legumes  b) the edible dry seeds of legumes  c) a kind of grass
**Answer: b) the edible dry seeds of legumes**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Pulse seeds carry —
a) one cotyledon  b) two cotyledons  c) no cotyledons
**Answer: b) two cotyledons**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Legumes fix nitrogen —
a) in their flowers  b) through root nodules  c) in their pods
**Answer: b) through root nodules**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Pulses belong in rotations with cereals because they —
a) shade the cereals  b) leave nitrogen for following crops  c) ripen faster
**Answer: b) leave nitrogen for following crops**
