# Pulses — protein and nitrogen from one crop — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Pulses can also be eaten as immature pods.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Pulses are monocots.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Fixed nitrogen benefits the crops planted after the pulse.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Root nodules are a disease that must be cut off.
**Answer: False**
