# Hands-on — Pulses — protein and nitrogen from one crop

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Nodule hunt
With permission, lift one legume plant, wash the roots gently, and count the nodules. Draw the root system and label a nodule 'nitrogen factory'. Return the plant's soil as you found it.

## Rotation ribbon
Lay a two-colour ribbon for two seasons: cereal colour, pulse colour. Write 'takes N' on the cereal half and 'leaves N' on the pulse half.
