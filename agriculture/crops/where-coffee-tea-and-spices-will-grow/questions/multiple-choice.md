# Where coffee, tea and spices will grow — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ecological requirements include —
a) altitude, temperature and rainfall  b) shop prices  c) road names
**Answer: a) altitude, temperature and rainfall**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Ecology is the —
a) last thing to check  b) buyer's concern  c) first test a planting site must pass
**Answer: c) first test a planting site must pass**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A crop outside its belt —
a) needs no care  b) spends its life struggling  c) always yields double
**Answer: b) spends its life struggling**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The soil test matters because these plantings —
a) stand for decades in the chosen place  b) are replanted weekly  c) never root deeply
**Answer: a) stand for decades in the chosen place**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Site choice is a double test of —
a) price and packaging  b) labour and lorries  c) climate belt and soil
**Answer: c) climate belt and soil**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The test must be passed —
a) only at export  b) before planting  c) after the first harvest
**Answer: b) before planting**
