# Where coffee, tea and spices will grow — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the ecological requirements named in this lesson.
**Answer: Altitude, temperature and rainfall (with soil conditions as the second test).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why must the test come before planting for coffee and tea?
**Answer: Because these perennial plantings stand for decades in the chosen place; a wrong site cannot be corrected later.**
