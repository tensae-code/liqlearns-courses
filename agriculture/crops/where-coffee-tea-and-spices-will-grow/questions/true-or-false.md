# Where coffee, tea and spices will grow — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Altitude is an ecological requirement for these crops.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Soil conditions do not matter for coffee, tea and spices.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Perennial plantings stand for decades in one place.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A site can be judged after planting just as well as before.
**Answer: False**
