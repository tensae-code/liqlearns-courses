# Where coffee, tea and spices will grow

## Do it first
Stand at the foot of a highland and look up: different crops live at different heights. What decides which crop gets which belt?

## Sub lesson 1 — The ecological test
Each crop group — coffee, tea and the spices — has **ecological requirements**: the **altitude, temperature and rainfall** conditions under which it thrives or fails. Ecology is the first test any planting site must pass: a crop planted outside its belt spends its life struggling.

[Picture 26617 — an altitude ladder with coffee, tea and spice belts marked]

## Sub lesson 2 — The soil test
Beside ecology stands **soil**: the soil conditions each group needs. Site choice for these perennial crops is a double test — climate belt above ground, soil below it — and it must be passed before planting, because a coffee or tea planting stands for decades in the place chosen.

## Say it to your companion

1. Draw an altitude ladder for your area and place coffee, tea and two spices where they belong.
2. Take soil from two sites and compare colour, texture and stones in your hands.
3. Ask a coffee grower why their field sits where it sits.
