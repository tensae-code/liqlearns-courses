# Keeping fruit quality — harvest and after

## Do it first
Two mangoes from the same tree reach the market: one gleaming, one bruised black. Same tree, same day — why will one earn more?

## Sub lesson 1 — Maturity and the moment of harvest
Fruit quality is decided first by **maturity at harvest**: picked at the right stage, a fruit can ripen into full flavour; picked wrong, no later care can repair it. The harvest moment is a quality decision, not just a date on the calendar.

[Picture 26610 — two mangoes — one bruised by rough handling, one perfect — with their prices]

## Sub lesson 2 — Handling and the post-harvest chain
After harvest, **handling** and the **post-harvest chain** decide what the buyer actually tastes — and what the grower is paid for. Bruising, heat, delay and rough packing destroy quality that the tree built over a whole season; careful handling carries the tree's quality all the way to the eater.
