Keeping fruit quality — harvest and after.
Fruit quality is decided first by maturity at harvest: picked at the right stage, a fruit can ripen into full flavour; picked wrong, no later care can repair it.
After harvest, handling and the post-harvest chain decide what the buyer actually tastes — and what the grower is paid for.
