# Keeping fruit quality — harvest and after — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why can later care not repair a badly picked fruit?
**Answer: Because maturity at harvest sets what ripening can achieve; picked wrong, the fruit's potential is already lost.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name three things in the post-harvest chain that destroy quality.
**Answer: Bruising (rough handling/packing), heat and delay — the chain's failures between tree and buyer.**
