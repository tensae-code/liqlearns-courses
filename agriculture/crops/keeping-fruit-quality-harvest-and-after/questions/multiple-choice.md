# Keeping fruit quality — harvest and after — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Fruit quality is decided first by —
a) maturity at harvest  b) the colour of the box  c) the buyer's mood
**Answer: a) maturity at harvest**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Picked at the wrong maturity, a fruit —
a) ripens better  b) earns more  c) cannot be repaired by later care
**Answer: c) cannot be repaired by later care**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The harvest moment is —
a) the buyer's job  b) a quality decision  c) only a date
**Answer: b) a quality decision**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The post-harvest chain decides —
a) what the buyer tastes and what the grower is paid  b) nothing after picking  c) only the tree's growth
**Answer: a) what the buyer tastes and what the grower is paid**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Bruising and rough packing —
a) improve flavour  b) add weight  c) destroy quality the tree built all season
**Answer: c) destroy quality the tree built all season**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Careful handling carries quality —
a) only on paper  b) from the tree to the eater  c) only to the farm gate
**Answer: b) from the tree to the eater**
