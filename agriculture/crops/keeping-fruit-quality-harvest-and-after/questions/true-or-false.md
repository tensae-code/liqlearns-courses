# Keeping fruit quality — harvest and after — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Harvest maturity affects the flavour a fruit can reach.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Rough handling improves fruit quality.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What the grower is paid depends partly on post-harvest handling.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Once picked, nothing can change a fruit's quality.
**Answer: False**
