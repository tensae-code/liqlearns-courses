# Keeping fruit quality — harvest and after — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Quality is decided first by ___ at harvest.
**Answer: maturity**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The chain after harvest is the ___ chain.
**Answer: post-harvest**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Rough ___ destroys quality built over a season.
**Answer: handling**
