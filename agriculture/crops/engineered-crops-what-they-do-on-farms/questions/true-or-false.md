# Engineered crops — what they do on farms — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Engineered traits work inside the living crop.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Biotechnology's results stay in the laboratory and never reach farms.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Protection is one thing engineered traits can change on farms.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Engineered traits change only a crop's name.
**Answer: False**
