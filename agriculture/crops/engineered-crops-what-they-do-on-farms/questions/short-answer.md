# Engineered crops — what they do on farms — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does an engineered trait do once inside a crop?
**Answer: It becomes a plant character: the crop does something it could not do, or withstands something that used to destroy it.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the three farm-level changes the lesson names.
**Answer: Yield, protection and quality.**
