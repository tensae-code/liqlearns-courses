# Engineered crops — what they do on farms — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An engineered trait becomes —
a) a character of the plant itself  b) a label on a sack  c) a law
**Answer: a) a character of the plant itself**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Applications put engineered traits into —
a) jars only  b) books only  c) real crops on real farms
**Answer: c) real crops on real farms**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The crop with an engineered trait can —
a) never need water  b) withstand something that used to destroy it  c) grow without soil
**Answer: b) withstand something that used to destroy it**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** On farms, engineered traits change —
a) yield, protection and quality  b) only the crop's colour  c) the farm's name
**Answer: a) yield, protection and quality**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The gene's journey finishes —
a) in the laboratory  b) at the border  c) in a farmer's field
**Answer: c) in a farmer's field**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson surveys applications in —
a) fish ponds  b) crop production  c) animal housing
**Answer: b) crop production**
