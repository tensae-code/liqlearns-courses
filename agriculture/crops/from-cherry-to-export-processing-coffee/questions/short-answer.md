# From cherry to export — processing coffee — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Contrast hulling for wet- and dry-processed coffee.
**Answer: Wet-processed: hulling removes the parchment layer (endocarp). Dry-processed: hulling removes the whole dried husk — the mesocarp and endocarp coverings of the dried cherry.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Where does polishing sit in the chain?
**Answer: It is an optional finishing step after hulling, before export.**
