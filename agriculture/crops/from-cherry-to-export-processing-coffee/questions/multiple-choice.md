# From cherry to export — processing coffee — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Before export, coffee beans are —
a) milled  b) replanted  c) fermented in the field
**Answer: a) milled**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Hulling wet-processed coffee removes —
a) the whole dried husk  b) the bean itself  c) the parchment layer (endocarp)
**Answer: c) the parchment layer (endocarp)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The parchment layer is the —
a) skin of the cherry only  b) endocarp  c) mesocarp
**Answer: b) endocarp**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Hulling dry-processed coffee removes —
a) the whole dried husk (mesocarp and endocarp coverings)  b) only the parchment  c) only the pulp stain
**Answer: a) the whole dried husk (mesocarp and endocarp coverings)**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Polishing is —
a) a required first step  b) a washing stage  c) an optional finishing step
**Answer: c) an optional finishing step**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Wet and dry hulling differ because —
a) of the export country  b) the two processes leave different coverings on the bean  c) of the mill's colour
**Answer: b) the two processes leave different coverings on the bean**
