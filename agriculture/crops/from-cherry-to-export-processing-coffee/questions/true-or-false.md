# From cherry to export — processing coffee — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Hulling is part of milling coffee before export.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Wet-processed hulling removes the whole dried husk.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Polishing is optional.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Dry-processed coffee has no coverings to remove.
**Answer: False**
