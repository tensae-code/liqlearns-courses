# Managing roots and tubers — feed and protect — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** State the two steps of feeding by the book.
**Answer: Determine the fertiliser type and quantity from the crop's recommendation; apply the selected rate by the appropriate method.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** List the three families of post-harvest loss.
**Answer: Mechanical damage; physiological condition (maturity, respiration, water loss, sprouting); diseases and pests.**
