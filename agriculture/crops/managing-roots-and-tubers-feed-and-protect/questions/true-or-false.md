# Managing roots and tubers — feed and protect — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The crop's recommendation sets the fertiliser type and quantity.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Any fertiliser method is as good as any other.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Protection of the crop ends at harvest.
**Answer: False**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Mechanical damage is a cause of post-harvest loss.
**Answer: True**
