# Managing roots and tubers — feed and protect — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Fertiliser type and quantity come from —
a) the crop's recommendation  b) the bag's colour  c) last year's habit only
**Answer: a) the crop's recommendation**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The selected rate is applied —
a) by scattering anywhere  b) only at harvest  c) by the appropriate method
**Answer: c) by the appropriate method**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Guessing the fertiliser rate risks —
a) faster harvest  b) starving the crop or wasting money  c) nothing
**Answer: b) starving the crop or wasting money**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Post-harvest losses come from —
a) mechanical damage, physiological condition, diseases and pests  b) buyers only  c) rain only
**Answer: a) mechanical damage, physiological condition, diseases and pests**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Physiological condition includes —
a) the price  b) the sack size  c) maturity, respiration, water loss and sprouting
**Answer: c) maturity, respiration, water loss and sprouting**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Sprouting potatoes in store are —
a) ready for eating seed only  b) a physiological loss to guard against  c) a sign of quality
**Answer: b) a physiological loss to guard against**
