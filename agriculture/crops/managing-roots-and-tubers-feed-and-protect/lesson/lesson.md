# Managing roots and tubers — feed and protect

## Do it first
A grower holds a bag of fertiliser over a potato field. Too little starves the crop; too much wastes money. How does the grower know the amount?

## Sub lesson 1 — Feeding by the recommendation
Management begins with feeding: **determine the type and quantity of fertiliser from the crop's recommendation**, then **apply the selected rate by the appropriate method**. The recommendation — not habit, not guesswork — sets both what is applied and how much.

[Picture 26614 — a fertiliser rate chart beside a store of sprouting potatoes]

## Sub lesson 2 — Protection past harvest
Protection extends past harvest. Post-harvest losses come from **mechanical damage**, **physiological condition** (maturity, respiration, water loss, sprouting), **diseases and pests** — each needing its guard. A crop protected in the field can still be lost in the store if these are ignored.
