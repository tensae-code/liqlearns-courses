# Hands-on — Growing sugarcane for the factories

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sett cutting practice
On a cane-like stem (sugarcane if available, otherwise a substitute stem), measure and mark 30 cm sett lengths from the top. Count how many setts one stem gives and inspect each cut end for damage.

## Factory map
Sketch Ethiopia and place the eight factory names from the lesson in their regions as best you can from the names; mark the lesson's production figure beside the map.
