# Video script — Growing sugarcane for the factories

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Sugarcane is planted from pieces of itself.
Setts: 30 centimetres, from young, healthy tops.
Rows 1.6 metres apart. The crop is hungry — feed it.
Eight factories. 400,000 tonnes. Still not enough.
