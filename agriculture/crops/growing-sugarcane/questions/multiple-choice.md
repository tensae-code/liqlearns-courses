# Growing sugarcane for the factories — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Sugarcane is planted from —
a) true seed  b) stem cuttings called setts, about 30 cm long  c) leaves
**Answer: b) stem cuttings called setts, about 30 cm long**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Setts are taken —
a) from old diseased plants  b) from the top of young, disease- and pest-free plants  c) from the roots
**Answer: b) from the top of young, disease- and pest-free plants**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In 2022 Ethiopia had —
a) eight state-owned sugar factories  b) one sugar factory  c) thirty sugar factories
**Answer: a) eight state-owned sugar factories**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The factories produced about —
a) 400,000 tonnes a year, below national demand  b) 4 million tonnes, above demand  c) 40,000 tonnes
**Answer: a) 400,000 tonnes a year, below national demand**
