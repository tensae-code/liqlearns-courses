# Growing sugarcane for the factories — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give the full sett specification.
**Answer: Setts about 30 cm long, cut from the top of young, disease- and pest-free plants, planted at about 1.6 m between rows.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is Ethiopia's sugar position, in the lesson's figures?
**Answer: Eight state-owned factories (2022) produce about 400,000 tonnes a year — still below national demand in 2023, so production must rise toward self-sufficiency and export.**
