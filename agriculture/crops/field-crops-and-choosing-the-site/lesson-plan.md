# Lesson plan — Field crops — cereals and pulses; choosing the site

**Course:** Agriculture · **Title:** Crops · **Lesson:** Field crops — cereals and pulses; choosing the site
**Place in the curriculum:** Agriculture — the Crops title (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** what field crops are — wide-scale annual food crops splitting into cereals and pulses — and how site and crop choice set production before planting
**This lesson's own level:** Grade 11 depth — it stands on the learner's own farm and fields, and on the course's earlier titles where named below.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
- Sub lesson 1 — Field crops — cereals and pulses: **Field crops** are grown **on a wide scale mainly for human food**.
- Sub lesson 2 — Choosing the site — control what you can: Production is set **first by site and crop choice**.
Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Sorting crops — grains, oils, fibres, forage. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Define field crops and name their two families: cereals and pulses.
2. Say what makes cereals cereals: grass family, edible seed, wide tolerance.
3. Sort production factors into what the farmer controls and what the farmer cannot, and state the matching rule before planting.

## If the learner is stuck
- Calls a perennial tree a field crop → field crops are annuals, done in one season, grown wide for food.
- Blames weather alone for a bad match → weather is uncontrollable; the match of soil, water and crop was the controllable choice.
