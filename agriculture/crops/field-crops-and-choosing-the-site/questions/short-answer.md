# Field crops — cereals and pulses; choosing the site — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give two controlled and two uncontrolled production factors.
**Answer: Controlled: crop and variety, soil preparation, timing. Uncontrolled: locust invasion, weather.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** State the matching rule before planting.
**Answer: Soil type, water supply and the crop's growth requirements must be matched to the chosen crop before planting.**
