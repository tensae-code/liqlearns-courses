# Field crops — cereals and pulses; choosing the site — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A field crop completes its life cycle in one season.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Pulses are a kind of cereal.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Soil preparation is a factor the farmer controls.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Careful site choice can control the weather.
**Answer: False**
