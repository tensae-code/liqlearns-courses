# Field crops — cereals and pulses; choosing the site — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Field crops are —
a) perennial trees  b) annuals grown on a wide scale mainly for human food  c) only grown in pots
**Answer: b) annuals grown on a wide scale mainly for human food**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Cereals belong to the —
a) legume family  b) grass family, grown for edible seed  c) rose family
**Answer: b) grass family, grown for edible seed**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which factor can the farmer NOT control?
a) crop variety  b) timing of planting  c) locust invasion
**Answer: c) locust invasion**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Before planting, the farmer must match —
a) the crop to the market day  b) soil type, water and growth requirements to the crop  c) the crop to the neighbour's crop
**Answer: b) soil type, water and growth requirements to the crop**
