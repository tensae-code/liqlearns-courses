# Field crops — cereals and pulses; choosing the site

## Sub lesson 1 — Field crops — cereals and pulses
**Field crops** are grown **on a wide scale mainly for human food**. They are **annuals** — each completes its life cycle **in one season**. They split into two families: **cereals** — the **grass family**, grown for **edible seed** and tolerant of a **wide range of conditions** — and **pulses**, the protein seeds the next lessons take up one by one.

Teff, maize, wheat, barley and sorghum are cereals; faba bean, field pea and haricot bean are pulses. The whole of Unit 2 walks these crops in this order.

## Sub lesson 2 — Choosing the site — control what you can
Production is set **first by site and crop choice**. The farmer **controls** some factors — **crop and variety, soil preparation, timing** — but **not others** — **locust invasion, weather**. Because the uncontrollable factors cannot be argued with, the controllable ones must be got right.

The rule: **soil type, water and the crop's growth requirements must be matched to the crop before planting**. A water-loving crop on dry land, or a cool-land crop in the heat, fails at the choosing, not at the harvesting.
