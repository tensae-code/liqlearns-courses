# Hands-on — Field crops — cereals and pulses; choosing the site

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Three-field match
Draw wet clay, dry lowland and cool highland fields. Place a water-loving, a drought-enduring and a cool-air crop, and label the clue that placed each.

## Control chart
Make a two-column chart for your family's field: WE CONTROL / WE CANNOT CONTROL. Fill four rows each side from real life.
