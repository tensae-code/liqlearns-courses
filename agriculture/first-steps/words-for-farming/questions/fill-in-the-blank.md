# Words for farming — shifting, settled, subsistence, commercial — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In ___ cultivation, land is farmed for a few seasons and then left as the farmer moves on.
**Answer: shifting**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Farming that produces only for the household is called ___ farming.
**Answer: subsistence**
