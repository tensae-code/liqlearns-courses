# Words for farming — shifting, settled, subsistence, commercial — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the farming type: a family clears land, farms it three seasons, then leaves it to rest.
**Answer: Shifting cultivation.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A farmer stays on one plot and adds manure and bunds each year. Which type is this, and why?
**Answer: Settled agriculture — the same plot is used continuously and its yield is raised with care practices.**
