# Words for farming — shifting, settled, subsistence, commercial — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Shifting cultivation means the farmer —
a) farms the same plot forever  b) clears land, farms a few seasons, then moves on  c) grows only for the market
**Answer: b) clears land, farms a few seasons, then moves on**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Agriculture is —
a) taking what wild land gives  b) the deliberate growing of crops and rearing of animals  c) hunting with better tools
**Answer: b) the deliberate growing of crops and rearing of animals**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Settled agriculture keeps yields up by —
a) abandoning the plot  b) manure, fertiliser, bunds and careful water use  c) moving to new land each year
**Answer: b) manure, fertiliser, bunds and careful water use**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Subsistence farming produces —
a) mainly for export  b) only for the household, with any surplus stored or sold  c) only for factories
**Answer: b) only for the household, with any surplus stored or sold**
