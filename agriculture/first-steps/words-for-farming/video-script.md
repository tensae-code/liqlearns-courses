# Video script — Words for farming — shifting, settled, subsistence, commercial

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Farming has four words.
Shifting: clear, farm, move on.
Settled: same plot, fed and kept.
Subsistence: the harvest feeds the house.
Commercial: the harvest feeds the market.
