# Hands-on — Words for farming — shifting, settled, subsistence, commercial

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Field-words walk
Walk past four fields. For each, say the farming word aloud and the clue: moved-on land resting, same plot fed with manure, a pot-sized harvest, a truck-sized harvest.

## Then and now
Sketch one plot twice: farmed-then-left under shifting cultivation, and the same plot kept and fed under settled agriculture. Label what the farmer does differently.
