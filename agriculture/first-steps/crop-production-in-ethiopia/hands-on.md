# Hands-on — Crop production in Ethiopia — where it stands

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Era spotting
On a walk or from family talk, find one example of each era still at work near home: a hoe, oxen, a tractor, a sprayer, an irrigation channel. The eras live side by side.

## Yield-gap talk
Ask a farmer: what most limits your yield — soil, rain, seed, fertiliser, water? Compare the answer with the lesson's five reasons.
