# Lesson plan — Crop production in Ethiopia — where it stands

**Course:** Agriculture · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** Crop production in Ethiopia — where it stands
**Place in the curriculum:** Agriculture — the First Steps title (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the eras of crop production, Ethiopia's farming picture in numbers, and the reasons yields stay low
**This lesson's own level:** Grade 11 depth — it stands on the learner's own farm and fields, and on the course's earlier titles where named below.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
- Sub lesson 1 — The eras, and Ethiopia's picture: Production moved through **eras**: hand work was given first to **draft animals**, then came **machinery (the Mechanical Era)**, then **agrochemicals (the Chemical Era)**, and today **irrigation, biotechnology and plant breeding**.
- Sub lesson 2 — Why yields lag: Yields have **risen over the decades** — the long record from 1961 to 2019 climbs — but they **stay low**.
Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Words for farming. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the eras of production from hand work to biotechnology.
2. State Ethiopia's picture: about 51 million hectares arable, about a fifth cultivated, five crops on about 75% of the cultivated area.
3. Give the main reasons yields lag: soil degradation, low technology, variable rain, little irrigation, low input use.

## If the learner is stuck
- Mixes the Mechanical and Chemical eras → machines first (they move), chemicals after (they feed and protect).
- Loses the numbers → anchor them: 51 (arable), a fifth (cultivated), 75% (five crops), 64% (calories).
