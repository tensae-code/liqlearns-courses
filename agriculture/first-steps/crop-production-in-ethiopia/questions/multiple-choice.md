# Crop production in Ethiopia — where it stands — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The correct era order is —
a) chemical, mechanical, hand  b) hand, draft animals, machinery, agrochemicals, then irrigation/biotechnology/breeding  c) tractor, hand, ox
**Answer: b) hand, draft animals, machinery, agrochemicals, then irrigation/biotechnology/breeding**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** About how much of Ethiopia's arable land is cultivated?
a) almost all of it  b) about a fifth  c) about half
**Answer: b) about a fifth**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The five major crops supply about what share of calories?
a) about 64%  b) about 10%  c) about 95%
**Answer: a) about 64%**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is NOT a reason yields lag?
a) soil degradation  b) variable rainfall  c) too much irrigation everywhere  d) low use of improved seed
**Answer: c) too much irrigation everywhere**
