# Crop production in Ethiopia — where it stands — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** About 51 million hectares of Ethiopia are arable.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Most cultivated land is farmed by large estates, not smallholders.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Yields have fallen steadily since 1961.
**Answer: False**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Very little irrigation is one reason yields stay low.
**Answer: True**
