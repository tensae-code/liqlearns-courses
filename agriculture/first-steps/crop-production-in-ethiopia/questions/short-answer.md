# Crop production in Ethiopia — where it stands — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the five major crops.
**Answer: Teff, wheat, maize, sorghum and barley.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give four reasons Ethiopian yields stay low.
**Answer: Any four of: soil degradation, traditional low-technology farming, variable rainfall, very little irrigation, low use of improved seed, fertiliser and pesticides.**
