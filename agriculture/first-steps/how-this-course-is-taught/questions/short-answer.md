# How this course is taught — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** State the guide's doctrine of the teacher's role.
**Answer: Student-centered learning does not diminish the teacher; it redeploys the teacher's expertise into designing activities, organising groups and judging learning, with whole-class presentation as one deliberate tool among several.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** List the lesson skeleton in order.
**Answer: Competencies first; content overview; teaching-learning process (brainstorming, group work, field/farm visits, presentations); 'Answer to the Activities' keys; closing 'Can you: Yes/No' self-check.**
