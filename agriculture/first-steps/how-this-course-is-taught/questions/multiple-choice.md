# How this course is taught — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Student-centered learning — the teacher
a) does not sideline the teacher; it redeploys their expertise  b) removes the teacher  c) makes the teacher only lecture
**Answer: a) does not sideline the teacher; it redeploys their expertise**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The teacher's expertise goes into —
a) only marking  b) only discipline  c) planning, organising and delivering activities
**Answer: c) planning, organising and delivering activities**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Whole-class presentation is —
a) banned  b) one tool among several, used deliberately  c) the only tool
**Answer: b) one tool among several, used deliberately**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The skeleton begins with —
a) competencies  b) answers  c) self-checks
**Answer: a) competencies**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Teaching processes include —
a) only copying notes  b) only exams  c) brainstorming, buzz groups, pair work, field visits and presentations
**Answer: c) brainstorming, buzz groups, pair work, field visits and presentations**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Each lesson or unit closes with —
a) a new textbook  b) a 'Can you: Yes/No' self-check list  c) a fee
**Answer: b) a 'Can you: Yes/No' self-check list**

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** In the Grade 11 guide, Pre-Lesson Preparation gathers materials from —
a) the school pedagogical centre  b) a city shop  c) the learners' homes only
**Answer: a) the school pedagogical centre**

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** The Grade 11 guide treats the school backyard as —
a) a playground  b) a practice farm  c) a store
**Answer: b) a practice farm**

**9. (Sub lesson 4)** Grade 11 group work is capped at —
a) five students  b) ten students  c) seven students
**Answer: c) seven students**
