How this course is taught.
The guide's doctrine: student-centered learning does not diminish the teacher — it redeploys the teacher's expertise into designing activities, organising groups and judging learning, with whole-class lesson presentation used deliberately rather than by default.
Each unit guide follows one skeleton: Competencies first; the content overview; a teaching-learning process built on brainstorming openings, group work (buzz groups, pair work), field and farm visits and presentations; 'Answer to the Activities' keys; and a closing 'Can you: Yes/No' self-check list per lesson or unit, judged continuously..
The Grade 11 guide scripts every lesson the same way: competencies, an overview, Pre-Lesson Preparation gathering materials from the school pedagogical centre, a brainstorming opening, the body with assessment and answer keys, and a unit assessment — and the class keeps leaving the classroom, to farms, markets and clinics, with the school backyard as a practice farm.
Its methods library: guest speakers; group work capped at seven students, mixed fast, medium and slow; notes taken from the textbook; feedback on field and group work given as presentations; assessment applied continuously.
