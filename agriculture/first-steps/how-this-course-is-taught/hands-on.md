# Hands-on — How this course is taught

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Skeleton audit
Take any lesson plan and label its parts against the skeleton; note what is missing.

## Buzz-group run
Run a five-minute buzz-group opening on an agriculture question and debrief how it worked.

## Backyard practice farm
Walk your school backyard (or any small plot) and list what one Grade 11 unit could practise there, lesson by lesson.

## Seven-cap group run
Form a group of at most seven, mixed fast/medium/slow, and present field findings as the group's feedback, as the Grade 11 library directs.
