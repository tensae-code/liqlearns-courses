# Hands-on — How wild plants became crops

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Wild grass judge
Collect three wild grass heads. Score each against the six selection qualities. Crown the one an early farmer would have kept, and say why.

## Poison made safe
With an adult, list foods in your home that are only safe after cooking or soaking. Match each to the trial-and-error idea: somebody first learned the safe way.
