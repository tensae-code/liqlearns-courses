# How wild plants became crops — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Agriculture's exact beginning —
a) is recorded in 8000 BC  b) cannot be known  c) began with the tractor
**Answer: b) cannot be known**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Early farmers selected wild plants for —
a) colour of the farmer's shirt  b) ease of harvest, availability, transport, climate tolerance, flavour and grain size  c) how tall the plant grew
**Answer: b) ease of harvest, availability, transport, climate tolerance, flavour and grain size**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which list is all wild foods early people used?
a) wild rice, wild oats, legumes, sweet potato  b) pizza, bread, cheese  c) teff injera, coffee, sugar
**Answer: a) wild rice, wild oats, legumes, sweet potato**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Toxic legumes were made safe —
a) by painting them  b) by trial and error  c) by freezing them
**Answer: b) by trial and error**
