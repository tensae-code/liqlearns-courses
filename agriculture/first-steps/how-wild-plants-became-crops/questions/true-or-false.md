# How wild plants became crops — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Domestication happened in a single season.
**Answer: False**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A plant that held its grain until harvest was more likely to be kept and sown.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Coconut, oil palm and olive were early oil sources.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Wild legumes were all safe to eat raw from the start.
**Answer: False**
