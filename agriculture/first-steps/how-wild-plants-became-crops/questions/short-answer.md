# How wild plants became crops — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give three qualities a wild grass needed to become a crop.
**Answer: Any three of: ease of harvest, availability, easy transport, tolerance of the climate, good flavour, large grain.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why did trial and error matter for legumes?
**Answer: Because some legumes were toxic; people had to discover, by testing, how to make them safe to eat.**
