# Lesson plan — Building an animal house that works

**Course:** Agriculture · **Title:** Livestock · **Lesson:** Building an animal house that works
**Place in the curriculum:** Agriculture — the Livestock title (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** construction: a drained concrete floor on a stone foundation, walls/roof with about 3 m minimum height and ventilation, and an east–west long axis against direct sun
**This lesson's own level:** Grade 11 depth — it stands on the learner's own farm and fields, and on the course's earlier titles where named below.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
- Sub lesson 1 — Floor, walls, roof, air: **The floor is critical — laid on a solid compact foundation of stones with a smooth inner side, ideally well-drained cement concrete that allows hygienic feeding and effective manure removal.
- Sub lesson 2 — Orientation — the sun rule: **An east–west long axis avoids direct sunlight inside, keeps the floor dry and puts more manure in the shade.
Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Choosing where to build — site selection. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Describe the floor: stone foundation, smooth inner side, well-drained cement concrete for hygiene and manure removal.
2. State minimum height about 3 m for air movement, with adequate ventilation.
3. Explain east–west orientation: avoids direct sunlight inside, keeps floor dry, more manure in shade.

## What you need
A tape or pacing for 3 m; a notebook.
