# Building an animal house that works

## Do it first
Sun pours through a barn's long side all afternoon; the floor steams, animals crowd one corner. Turn the whole building a quarter-turn in your mind. What changes? Orientation is a free climate machine — learn to set it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Floor, walls, roof, air
**The floor is critical — laid on a solid compact foundation of stones with a smooth inner side, ideally well-drained cement concrete that allows hygienic feeding and effective manure removal.** **Walls and roof give shelter with a minimum height of about 3 m for air movement**; **ventilation must be adequate**.

[Picture 26020 — a cutaway barn showing drained concrete floor, 3 m height and east–west orientation against the sun path]

## Sub lesson 2 — Orientation — the sun rule
**An east–west long axis avoids direct sunlight inside, keeps the floor dry and puts more manure in the shade.** A farm visit compares real walls, roofs, floors and feeding equipment against these rules — the building is judged by what the sun and water do inside it across a day.
