# Building an animal house that works — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The ideal floor is —
a) mud  b) well-drained cement concrete on a stone foundation  c) loose sand
**Answer: b) well-drained cement concrete on a stone foundation**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Minimum height for air movement is about —
a) 1 m  b) 3 m  c) 10 m
**Answer: b) 3 m**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** An east–west long axis —
a) invites sun in  b) avoids direct sunlight inside and keeps the floor dry  c) faces the road
**Answer: b) avoids direct sunlight inside and keeps the floor dry**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Ventilation must be —
a) absent  b) adequate  c) blocked
**Answer: b) adequate**
