# Making hay — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Hay is forage preserved by —
a) fermenting  b) drying  c) freezing
**Answer: b) drying**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Conservation evens out —
a) animal colours  b) the seasons' feed supply  c) prices only
**Answer: b) the seasons' feed supply**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Curing takes moisture down to —
a) 70–90%  b) 15–20%  c) 0%
**Answer: b) 15–20%**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A tripod stores hay —
a) underground  b) lifted off damp ground  c) in water
**Answer: b) lifted off damp ground**
