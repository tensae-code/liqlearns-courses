# From udder to market — milk hygiene and processing — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Milk must be kept at —
a) room temperature  b) 4 °C or below  c) 40 °C
**Answer: b) 4 °C or below**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Milk moves to market in —
a) open baskets  b) cans or bulk tanks  c) paper sacks
**Answer: b) cans or bulk tanks**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is NOT a dairy product in the book's list?
a) yoghurt  b) ghee  c) injera
**Answer: c) injera**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Processing converts raw milk into —
a) only waste  b) higher-value products  c) water
**Answer: b) higher-value products**
