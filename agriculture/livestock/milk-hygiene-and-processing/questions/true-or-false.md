# From udder to market — milk hygiene and processing — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Cooling tanks are one way to hold milk at 4 °C or below.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Hygiene matters only at the factory, not at collection.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Butter and ghee are both made from milk's fat.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Pasteurised milk is raw milk with a new label.
**Answer: False**
