# From udder to market — milk hygiene and processing — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the two defences of milk between udder and market.
**Answer: Cold (4 °C or below, by refrigeration or cooling tanks) and hygiene guarded at every stage.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** List the six products processing makes from raw milk.
**Answer: Pasteurised liquid milk, cream, yoghurt, cheese, butter and ghee.**
