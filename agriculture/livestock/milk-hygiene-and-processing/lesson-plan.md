# Lesson plan — From udder to market — milk hygiene and processing

**Course:** Agriculture · **Title:** Livestock · **Lesson:** From udder to market — milk hygiene and processing
**Place in the curriculum:** Agriculture — the Livestock title (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** keeping milk safe — cold at 4 degrees C or below, hygiene at every stage, moving in cans or bulk tanks — and processing it into pasteurised milk, cream, yoghurt, cheese, butter and ghee
**This lesson's own level:** Grade 11 depth — it stands on the learner's own farm and fields, and on the course's earlier titles where named below.

Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
- Sub lesson 1 — Cold and clean — the two defences: Milk must be kept **cold — 4 °C or below** — by **mechanical refrigeration or cooling tanks**, from collection onward.
- Sub lesson 2 — Processing — turning milk into more: **Processing converts raw milk into higher-value products**: **pasteurised liquid milk, cream, yoghurt, cheese, butter and ghee**.
Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: The lactation cycle. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the cold rule: milk kept at 4 °C or below, by mechanical refrigeration or cooling tanks.
2. Say milk moves in cans or bulk tanks, with hygiene guarded at every stage of collection and processing.
3. List the products processing makes: pasteurised liquid milk, cream, yoghurt, cheese, butter and ghee.
