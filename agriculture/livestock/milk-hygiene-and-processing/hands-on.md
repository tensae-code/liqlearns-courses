# Hands-on — From udder to market — milk hygiene and processing

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Chain audit
Follow (or imagine) one can's journey: list every surface and hand it meets. Mark each as a hygiene win or risk.

## Product match
Match each product to its trick: pasteurised milk — safer liquid; ghee — fat that keeps without cold; yoghurt — fermented keeping.
