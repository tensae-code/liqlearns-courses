# Hands-on — What farm animals give us

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Whole-animal audit
Choose one animal your family keeps. List everything it gave in the last year — food, work, money, materials, manure — and value the list roughly. Compare with its feed cost.

## Residue trail
Follow one slaughter residue (bones, blood, hooves) from a butcher or home slaughter: where does it go? Write its second life as feed, adhesive or fertiliser.
