# What farm animals give us — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give the three livelihood/economy figures for Ethiopian livestock.
**Answer: Supports about 65% of the population; 45–48% of agricultural GDP since 2018; 16–19% of export earnings.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name four raw materials animals give, with a use for each.
**Answer: E.g. skins and wool for cottage industry; bones and hooves for adhesives; fats for candles and soap; organ extracts for pharmaceuticals.**
