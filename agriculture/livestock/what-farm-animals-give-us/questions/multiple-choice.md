# What farm animals give us — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Livestock supports about what share of Ethiopia's population?
a) about 10%  b) about 65%  c) about 95%
**Answer: b) about 65%**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Livestock's share of agricultural GDP since 2018 is —
a) 45–48%  b) 5%  c) 90%
**Answer: a) 45–48%**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Bones and hooves are used for —
a) adhesives  b) textiles  c) sugar
**Answer: a) adhesives**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Slaughter residues are —
a) always buried as waste  b) recycled as livestock feed  c) burned at once
**Answer: b) recycled as livestock feed**
