# What farm animals give us — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Oxen, horses, donkeys and camels give draught power.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Livestock contributes 16–19% of export earnings.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Fats from animals are used for candles and soap.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Manure harms the farm ecosystem and should be removed.
**Answer: False**
