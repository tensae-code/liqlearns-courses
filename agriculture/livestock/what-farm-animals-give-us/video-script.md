# Video script — What farm animals give us

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Animals give food: meat, milk, eggs, honey.
They give work: oxen, horses, donkeys, camels pull.
They carry 65 percent of Ethiopia's people.
Skins, bones, fats, manure — nothing wasted.
