# Ethnoveterinary knowledge — learning from local farmers — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why do plant remedies matter in Ethiopia?
**Answer: They are the most important — sometimes only — inexpensive treatment many farmers have.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** List the four interview questions.
**Answer: Which plants/parts; how prepared; how dose decided; how given.**
