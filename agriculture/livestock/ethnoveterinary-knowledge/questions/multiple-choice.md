# Ethnoveterinary knowledge — learning from local farmers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ethnoveterinary remedies in Ethiopia are mostly —
a) imported chemicals  b) plant-based  c) surgical
**Answer: b) plant-based**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The knowledge is passed down from —
a) textbooks only  b) elders  c) tourists
**Answer: b) elders**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The interview asks how the dose is —
a) ignored  b) decided  c) doubled
**Answer: b) decided**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Remedies in this lesson are —
a) to self-prescribe freely  b) knowledge to document, with proven care for sick animals  c) banned from mention
**Answer: b) knowledge to document, with proven care for sick animals**
