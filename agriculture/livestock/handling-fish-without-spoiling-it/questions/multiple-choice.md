# Handling fish without spoiling it — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Fish, compared with other foods, —
a) spoils fast  b) keeps for weeks unchilled  c) improves with delay
**Answer: a) spoils fast**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The handling chain runs —
a) from shop to lake  b) only in the pond  c) from capture to consumer
**Answer: c) from capture to consumer**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The chain's links are —
a) netting only  b) cleaning, chilling, processing and transport  c) feeding and breeding
**Answer: b) cleaning, chilling, processing and transport**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Chilling does what to spoilage?
a) slows it  b) speeds it  c) ends the need for cleaning
**Answer: a) slows it**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Cleaning matters because it removes —
a) the flavour  b) the bones only  c) what rots first
**Answer: c) what rots first**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** A badly handled fish arrives as —
a) a dried fish  b) a risk, not food  c) a better fish
**Answer: b) a risk, not food**
