# Handling fish without spoiling it — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** List the handling chain's links in order.
**Answer: Cleaning, chilling, processing and transport — from capture to consumer.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does fish handling decide safety, not only taste?
**Answer: Because fish spoils fast; each link either slows spoilage or feeds it, so the handler decides whether the fish arrives as food or as a risk.**
