The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Handling fish without spoiling it.
Handling from capture to consumer decides quality because fish spoils fast.
Cleaning removes what rots first; chilling slows the spoilage clock; processing and careful transport carry the fish to the consumer still safe.
Do it yourself.
Then say it to your companion.
