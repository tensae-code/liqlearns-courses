# Handling fish without spoiling it

## Do it first
Fish spoils faster than almost any food you handle. The clock starts at the net. Every hand the fish passes through can beat the clock — or lose to it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Fish spoils fast
**Handling from capture to consumer** decides quality because **fish spoils fast**. The chain — **cleaning, chilling, processing and transport** — is a race: each link either slows spoilage or feeds it.

[Picture 26654 — fresh fish on ice at a lakeside market]

## Sub lesson 2 — The chain decides quality and safety
Cleaning removes what rots first; **chilling** slows the spoilage clock; processing and careful transport carry the fish to the consumer still safe. A fish handled well arrives as food; handled badly, the same fish arrives as a risk. The handler, not the fish, decides which.
