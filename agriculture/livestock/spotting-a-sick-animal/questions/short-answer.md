# Spotting a sick animal; how disease spreads — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name four spread routes.
**Answer: Any four of: direct contact, ingestion, inhalation, urogenital tract, placenta, vectors.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** List five sick signs.
**Answer: Any five of: appetite loss, changes at openings, sunken eyes, raised coat, rough skin, dry muzzle, swollen joints, abnormal temperature/pulse/respiration.**
