# Spotting a sick animal; how disease spreads — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Disease is —
a) perfect wellbeing  b) any departure from health  c) a feed type
**Answer: b) any departure from health**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which is a spread route?
a) vectors  b) sunshine only  c) silence
**Answer: a) vectors**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A sick sign is —
a) bright eyes and appetite  b) sunken eyes and dry muzzle  c) fast grazing
**Answer: b) sunken eyes and dry muzzle**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Abnormal measures in sickness include —
a) temperature, pulse, respiration  b) height only  c) horn length
**Answer: a) temperature, pulse, respiration**
