# Housing beef cattle — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Beef housing is fitted to —
a) a meat herd's simpler needs compared with dairy  b) a milking parlour routine  c) show animals only
**Answer: a) a meat herd's simpler needs compared with dairy**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Beef animals differ from dairy in housing because they are not —
a) fed at all  b) kept in herds  c) milked daily
**Answer: c) milked daily**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The enterprise pattern places housing —
a) only at slaughter  b) after breeds and feeding  c) before any animals exist
**Answer: b) after breeds and feeding**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A beef yard must let the herd —
a) rest, feed, drink and be handled safely  b) never move  c) avoid all people forever
**Answer: a) rest, feed, drink and be handled safely**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Housing is judged by —
a) its first-day appearance  b) its paint colour  c) the herd's life in it across seasons
**Answer: c) the herd's life in it across seasons**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Crowding, mud and sun are —
a) the animals' fault  b) housing problems to design against  c) irrelevant to yards
**Answer: b) housing problems to design against**
