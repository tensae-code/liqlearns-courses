# Housing beef cattle — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Beef shelter is fitted to a ___ herd's needs.
**Answer: meat**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The herd must rest, feed, drink and be ___ safely.
**Answer: handled**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Housing is judged across ___, not on building day.
**Answer: seasons**
