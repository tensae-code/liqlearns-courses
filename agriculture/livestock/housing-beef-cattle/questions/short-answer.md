# Housing beef cattle — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why is beef housing simpler than dairy housing?
**Answer: Because beef animals are not milked daily or handled as closely; shelter answers protection, rest, feeding and management needs instead.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** List what a beef yard must provide.
**Answer: Rest, feed, drink and safe handling for the herd, across seasons.**
