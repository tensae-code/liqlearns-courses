# Housing beef cattle

## Do it first
A beef herd does not ask for a palace. Walk a yard in your mind at noon and at midnight rain: what must that yard give the animals?

## Sub lesson 1 — Shelter fitted to a meat herd
Beef cattle housing follows the enterprise pattern — **shelter fitted to a meat herd's simpler needs compared with dairy**. Beef animals are not milked daily or handled as closely, so their housing answers different questions: protection, rest, feeding access and herd management rather than a milking routine.

[Picture 26628 — a beef herd resting in a fenced, shaded yard]

## Sub lesson 2 — The yard as a working place
A beef yard must let the herd rest, feed, drink and be handled safely. Housing is judged by the herd's life in it across seasons — sun, rain, mud and crowding — not by how it looks on building day.
