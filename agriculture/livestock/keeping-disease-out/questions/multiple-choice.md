# Keeping disease out — hygiene and biosecurity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Hygiene includes —
a) washing feed and water troughs  b) never cleaning  c) mixing sick with healthy
**Answer: a) washing feed and water troughs**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Dead animals should be —
a) left in the pen  b) burned or buried  c) sold
**Answer: b) burned or buried**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Biosecurity includes —
a) open gates for all  b) controlling entry and protective clothing  c) no fences
**Answer: b) controlling entry and protective clothing**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Vaccination belongs to —
a) biosecurity only  b) good management  c) neither
**Answer: b) good management**
