# Choosing beef cattle

## Do it first
Stand beside a bull in your mind and look along its body. You are not admiring it — you are reading it. What does its build promise?

## Sub lesson 1 — Breeds for meat
Beef **breeds** are the starting set: the breeds whose build and growth are made for meat. The enterprise pattern of this year begins here for every animal: know the breeds before choosing the animal.

[Picture 26626 — a beef bull judged from the side — depth, muscle, legs]

## Sub lesson 2 — Selection — choosing the promising animal
**Selection** methods choose, within and across breeds, the animals whose **build and growth promise meat**. A selected herd improves year by year; an unselected herd repeats its faults. The judge reads the animal's body the way a reader reads a page: depth, muscle and sound legs tell the future carcass.

## Say it to your companion
Explain this lesson to your companion in your own words, using one real example from your own area for each sub lesson. Your companion asks one question about each part — answer from the lesson, not from memory of other lessons.
