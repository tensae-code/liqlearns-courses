# Choosing beef cattle — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Every animal unit this year begins with —
a) breeds and selection  b) diseases  c) marketing
**Answer: a) breeds and selection**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Beef breeds are breeds whose build suits —
a) milk only  b) draught only  c) meat production
**Answer: c) meat production**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Knowing breeds comes —
a) never  b) before choosing the animal  c) after slaughter
**Answer: b) before choosing the animal**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Selection chooses animals whose —
a) build and growth promise meat  b) colour is rarest  c)  horns are longest
**Answer: a) build and growth promise meat**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** An unselected herd —
a) improves fastest  b) needs no feeding  c) repeats its faults year by year
**Answer: c) repeats its faults year by year**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Judging reads —
a) the weather  b) depth, muscle and legs  c) the owner's name
**Answer: b) depth, muscle and legs**
