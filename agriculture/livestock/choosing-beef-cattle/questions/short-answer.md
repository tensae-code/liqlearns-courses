# Choosing beef cattle — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does selection look for in a beef animal?
**Answer: Build and growth that promise meat — depth, muscle and sound legs.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does selection matter across years?
**Answer: Because a selected herd improves year by year, while an unselected herd repeats its faults.**
