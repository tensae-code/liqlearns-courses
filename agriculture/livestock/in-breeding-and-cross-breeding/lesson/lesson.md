# In-breeding and cross-breeding compared

## Do it first
Mate close kin and a trait doubles down — the good and the bad. Mate strangers and the calf may outgrow both parents. Two opposite bets. Which risk belongs to which system? Decide, then check.

## Sub lesson 1 — Related breeding — in-breeding
**Related breeding mates animals closer than the population average.** **In-breeding (e.g., a ram to his daughter) concentrates and preserves specific qualities and produces pure lines** — **at the risk of concentrating faults too**. Sameness is both its power and its danger.

## Sub lesson 2 — Unrelated breeding — cross-breeding
**Unrelated breeding mates animals from different populations.** **Cross-breeding (e.g., an Arsi cow to a Holstein Friesian bull) exploits hybrid vigour** — the cross often grows, yields and resists beyond either parent line. The book's table sets the two systems' examples, advantages and disadvantages side by side: concentrate within, or combine across.
