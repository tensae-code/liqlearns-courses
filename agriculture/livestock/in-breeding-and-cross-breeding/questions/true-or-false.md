# In-breeding and cross-breeding compared — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In-breeding can concentrate faults.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Related breeding means unrelated parents.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** An Arsi cow to a Holstein bull is cross-breeding.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Cross-breeding removes all risk.
**Answer: False**
