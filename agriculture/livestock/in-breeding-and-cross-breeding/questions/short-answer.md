# In-breeding and cross-breeding compared — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give in-breeding's gain and risk.
**Answer: Gain: concentrates/preserves qualities, pure lines. Risk: concentrates faults too.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Define cross-breeding with the book example.
**Answer: Mating different populations — e.g. Arsi cow × Holstein Friesian bull — for hybrid vigour.**
