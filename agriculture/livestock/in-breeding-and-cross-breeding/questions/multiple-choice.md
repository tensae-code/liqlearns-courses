# In-breeding and cross-breeding compared — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In-breeding mates —
a) strangers  b) animals closer than average, e.g. a ram to his daughter  c) different species
**Answer: b) animals closer than average, e.g. a ram to his daughter**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In-breeding produces —
a) hybrid vigour only  b) pure lines  c) no offspring
**Answer: b) pure lines**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Cross-breeding mates —
a) full siblings  b) animals from different populations  c) the same animal
**Answer: b) animals from different populations**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Hybrid vigour is exploited by —
a) in-breeding  b) cross-breeding  c) starvation
**Answer: b) cross-breeding**
