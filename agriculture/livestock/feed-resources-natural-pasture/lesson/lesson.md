# Feed resources — natural pasture

## Sub lesson 1 — Feed — the biggest cost
Feed is the **biggest cost in livestock production — 50–80% of operating cost** — so the **right amount of the right feed** decides performance and profit. A cheap animal fed badly gives little; good feed wasted also loses money. Every feed lesson in this unit starts from that money fact.

[Picture 26000 — a managed pasture, half lush and half weedy, showing what management changes]

## Sub lesson 2 — Natural pasture, kept productive
Ethiopia's **first feed resource is natural pasture**. It stays productive only with care: **grazing-land management**, **fertiliser — urea and DAP — for rapid, succulent growth**, **oversowing legumes** into the grass, and **weed, pest and disease control**. Unmanaged pasture thins, weeds take it, and the dry-season gap widens.
