# Feed resources — natural pasture — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Feed takes what share of livestock operating cost?
a) under 10%  b) 50–80%  c) nothing
**Answer: b) 50–80%**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The right feed decides —
a) only coat colour  b) performance and profit  c) nothing
**Answer: b) performance and profit**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Natural pasture is Ethiopia's —
a) last resort  b) first feed resource  c) not used
**Answer: b) first feed resource**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Oversowing pasture adds —
a) stones  b) legumes  c) sand
**Answer: b) legumes**
