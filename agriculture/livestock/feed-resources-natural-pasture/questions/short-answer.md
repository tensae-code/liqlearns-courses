# Feed resources — natural pasture — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does feed decide profit?
**Answer: It is 50–80% of operating cost, so its amount and quality set both cost and output.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name three pasture care jobs.
**Answer: Any three of: grazing-land management, fertiliser (urea/DAP), oversowing legumes, weed/pest/disease control.**
