# Feed resources — natural pasture — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Feed is the biggest cost in livestock production.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Any amount of any feed gives the same result.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Urea and DAP can give rapid, succulent pasture growth.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Weeds improve natural pasture.
**Answer: False**
