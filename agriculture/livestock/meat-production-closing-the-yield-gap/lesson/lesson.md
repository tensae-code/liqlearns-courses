# Meat production — closing the yield gap

## Do it first
Four numbers tell this lesson: 110, 143, 156, 212 kilograms of beef per animal. Ethiopia owns the smallest. What would closing that gap feed?

## Sub lesson 1 — Ethiopia's yield gap, measured
Beef productivity is quantified: Ethiopia produces about **110 kg per head** — some **25–30% below East Africa's 143 kg/head**, against a continental average of **156** and a world average of **212 kg/head**. In the same accounting, national mutton production is **77 thousand metric tonnes** and goat meat **62 thousand metric tonnes**.

## Sub lesson 2 — From slaughter onward — processing
The **processing chain from slaughter onward** turns the animal into meat the market can use. The gap is not fate: it is the sum of breed, feeding, health and processing choices this unit has taught — each enterprise section is one lever for closing it.
