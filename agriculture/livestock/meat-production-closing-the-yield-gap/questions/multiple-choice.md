# Meat production — closing the yield gap — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ethiopia's beef productivity is about —
a) 110 kg per head  b) 212 kg per head  c) 156 kg per head
**Answer: a) 110 kg per head**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** East Africa averages —
a) 110 kg/head  b) 62 kg/head  c) 143 kg/head
**Answer: c) 143 kg/head**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The world average is —
a) 77 kg per head  b) 212 kg per head  c) 143 kg per head
**Answer: b) 212 kg per head**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Ethiopia sits below East Africa by about —
a) 25–30%  b) 1–2%  c) 90%
**Answer: a) 25–30%**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** National mutton production is given as —
a) 62 thousand metric tonnes  b) 212 thousand metric tonnes  c) 77 thousand metric tonnes
**Answer: c) 77 thousand metric tonnes**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The yield gap is closed by —
a) importing all meat  b) breed, feeding, health and processing choices  c) waiting
**Answer: b) breed, feeding, health and processing choices**
