Meat production — closing the yield gap.
Beef productivity is quantified: Ethiopia produces about 110 kg per head — some 25–30% below East Africa's 143 kg/head, against a continental average of 156 and a world average of 212 kg/head.
The processing chain from slaughter onward turns the animal into meat the market can use.
