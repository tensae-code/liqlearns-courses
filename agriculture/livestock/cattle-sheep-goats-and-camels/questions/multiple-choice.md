# Cattle, sheep, goats and camels — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Cattle belong to genus —
a) Ovis  b) Bos  c) Capra
**Answer: b) Bos**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Cattle are —
a) carnivores  b) ruminant herbivores feeding mainly on pasture  c) browsers only
**Answer: b) ruminant herbivores feeding mainly on pasture**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Goats characteristically —
a) graze only grass  b) browse on shoots and shrubs  c) eat thorns with tough lips
**Answer: b) browse on shoots and shrubs**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The Angora goat gives —
a) silk  b) mohair  c) cotton
**Answer: b) mohair**
