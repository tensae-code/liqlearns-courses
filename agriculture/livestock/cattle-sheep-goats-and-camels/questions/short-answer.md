# Cattle, sheep, goats and camels — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** List cattle's five gifts.
**Answer: Meat, milk, draft power, hides for leather, and manure.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How do goats and camels each feed beyond the pasture?
**Answer: Goats browse shoots and shrubs; camels use thick, tough lips to feed on thorny bushes in dry country.**
