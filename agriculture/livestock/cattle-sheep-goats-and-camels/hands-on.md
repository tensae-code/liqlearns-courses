# Hands-on — Cattle, sheep, goats and camels

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Mouth matching
Draw the four heads in a row. Under each, sketch its food: pasture grass, pasture grass, shrub shoots, thorn bush. Label grazer or browser.

## Products hunt
At home or market, find one product of each species: milk or leather (cattle), wool or meat (sheep), skins (goat), transport or milk (camel). List them with their species.
