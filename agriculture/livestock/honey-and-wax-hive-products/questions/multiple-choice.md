# Honey and wax — hive products — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first hive product is —
a) honey  b) propolis only  c) pollen only
**Answer: a) honey**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Beside honey stands —
a) tallow  b) cotton  c) beeswax
**Answer: c) beeswax**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The harvest is —
a) only wax  b) a basket of products, not one jar  c) only honey
**Answer: b) a basket of products, not one jar**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Processing takes products —
a) from harvest to marketable form  b) from market to hive  c) out of use
**Answer: a) from harvest to marketable form**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Honey processing includes —
a) boiling it dry  b) mixing it with water  c) extracting it from frames
**Answer: c) extracting it from frames**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Wax is rendered into —
a) smoke  b) blocks  c) frames
**Answer: b) blocks**
