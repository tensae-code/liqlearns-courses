# Honey and wax — hive products — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the hive products this lesson carries.
**Answer: Honey first, beeswax, and the other products of the hive.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does processing do for hive products?
**Answer: It takes them from harvest to marketable form — honey extracted and settled, wax rendered — turning hive work into income.**
