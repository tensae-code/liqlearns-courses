# Honey and wax — hive products

## Do it first
The hive pays in more than honey. Wax built every comb you harvested. Before reading, list everything a hive gives that can be sold or used.

## Sub lesson 1 — Hive products
**Hive products** are headed by **honey**, with **beeswax** and the other products of the hive beside it. The beekeeper's harvest is a small basket of goods, not a single jar — each product with its own handling.

## Sub lesson 2 — Processing to marketable form
**Processing** takes the products **from harvest to marketable form**: honey extracted and settled clean, wax rendered into blocks. Processing is where hive work becomes income — careless processing wastes what careful beekeeping built.
