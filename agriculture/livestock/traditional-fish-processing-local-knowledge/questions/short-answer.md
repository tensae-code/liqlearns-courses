# Traditional fish processing — local knowledge — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is recorded in this lesson?
**Answer: The traditional fish-processing methods of Ethiopia's fishing communities, as working knowledge.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give two reasons for recording them.
**Answer: They preserve methods proven to keep fish safe and saleable without cold chains, and they teach the principles inside the practices (honouring the communities that developed them).**
