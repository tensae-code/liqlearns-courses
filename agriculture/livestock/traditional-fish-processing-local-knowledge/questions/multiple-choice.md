# Traditional fish processing — local knowledge — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The unit closes with —
a) local knowledge in fish processing  b) a new pond design  c) export law
**Answer: a) local knowledge in fish processing**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Traditional methods are recorded as —
a) superstition  b) secrets  c) working knowledge
**Answer: c) working knowledge**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The methods belong to —
a) laboratories  b) Ethiopia's fishing communities  c) importers
**Answer: b) Ethiopia's fishing communities**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Traditional processing kept fish —
a) safe and saleable without modern cold chains  b) frozen  c) only for home feasts
**Answer: a) safe and saleable without modern cold chains**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Recording local knowledge —
a) replaces all science  b) is done for decoration  c) preserves what works and teaches its principles
**Answer: c) preserves what works and teaches its principles**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The unit ends by —
a) banning old methods  b) listening to the communities  c) building a factory
**Answer: b) listening to the communities**
