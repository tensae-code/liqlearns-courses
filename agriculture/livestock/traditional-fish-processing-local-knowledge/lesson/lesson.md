# Traditional fish processing — local knowledge

## Do it first
Long before ice reached the lakes, fishing communities kept fish from spoiling. Their methods still work. What do the old ways know that a new farmer should record?

## Sub lesson 1 — Local knowledge closes the unit
**Local knowledge in fish processing** closes the unit as the course's pattern promises: the **traditional processing methods of Ethiopia's fishing communities**, recorded as **working knowledge** — not folklore decoration, but technique proven over generations beside the lakes.

[Picture 26656 — fish drying on racks in the traditional way]

## Sub lesson 2 — Why it is recorded
Traditional methods kept fish safe and saleable without modern cold chains. Recording them preserves what works, teaches handlers the principles inside the practices, and honours the communities who developed them. The unit ends, deliberately, by listening.
