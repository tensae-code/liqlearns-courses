# Feeding sheep and goats from grazing land

## Do it first
Sheep crop the grass at your feet; goats stretch into the shrubs above it. Same hillside, two different dinners. Why does that matter for feeding them?

## Sub lesson 1 — Grazing land is the feed base
**Natural grazing land is the predominant feed source** — very little land is planted specifically for pasture or forage. Improvement decisions must **weigh production alongside feeding**: what the land gives decides what the flock can give, so feeding management is mostly grazing management.

## Sub lesson 2 — Milk-production requirements, tabled
Nutrient requirements of sheep and goats are **similar to each other's**. Tables give daily requirements **per kg of milk production**, rising with fat content: at **3.5% fat — 4.5 MJ metabolisable energy and 47 g digestible crude protein**; at **5.5% fat — 5.7 MJ and 73 g** (NRC 1981 figures). Richer milk costs more feed — the table prices the litre before it is produced.
