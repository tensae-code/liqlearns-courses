# Feeding sheep and goats from grazing land — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Grazing land feeds most sheep and goats.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Most land is planted pasture for sheep and goats.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Richer milk (higher fat) needs more energy and protein per kg.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Sheep and goats have completely different nutrient needs.
**Answer: False**
