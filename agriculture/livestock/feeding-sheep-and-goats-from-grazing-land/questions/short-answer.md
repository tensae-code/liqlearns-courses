# Feeding sheep and goats from grazing land — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why is feeding management mostly grazing management here?
**Answer: Because natural grazing land is the predominant feed source and very little land is planted for pasture or forage.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Compare the per-kg milk requirements at the two fat levels.
**Answer: 3.5% fat: 4.5 MJ metabolisable energy and 47 g digestible crude protein. 5.5% fat: 5.7 MJ and 73 g — richer milk costs more of both.**
