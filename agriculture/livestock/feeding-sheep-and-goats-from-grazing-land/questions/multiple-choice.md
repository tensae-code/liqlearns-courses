# Feeding sheep and goats from grazing land — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The predominant feed source for sheep and goats is —
a) natural grazing land  b) planted pasture  c) concentrate feed
**Answer: a) natural grazing land**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Very little land is —
a) used for grazing  b) available at all  c) planted specifically for pasture or forage
**Answer: c) planted specifically for pasture or forage**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Improvement decisions must weigh —
a) market days  b) production alongside feeding  c) colour of animals
**Answer: b) production alongside feeding**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Sheep and goat nutrient requirements are —
a) similar to each other's  b) opposites  c) unknown
**Answer: a) similar to each other's**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** At 3.5% fat, milk needs per kg —
a) 5.7 MJ and 73 g  b) no protein  c) 4.5 MJ energy and 47 g digestible crude protein
**Answer: c) 4.5 MJ energy and 47 g digestible crude protein**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** At 5.5% fat, requirements rise to —
a) 3.5 MJ and 35 g  b) 5.7 MJ and 73 g  c) 4.5 MJ and 47 g
**Answer: b) 5.7 MJ and 73 g**
