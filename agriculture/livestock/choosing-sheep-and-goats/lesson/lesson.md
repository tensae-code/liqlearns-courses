# Choosing sheep and goats

## Do it first
Two young animals stand at market: one heavy for its age, one light. Their birth and weaning weights were written long before today. What story do weights tell?

## Sub lesson 1 — Breeds, and the figures that anchor selection
Breeds of sheep and goats and their selection open the enterprise, anchored by performance figures: birth, weaning (at **90 days**) and one-year weights of about **2.3 kg, 11 kg and 26–30 kg** in the data quoted, and adult goat body measurements around **66 cm**. Numbers like these turn 'a good animal' from an opinion into a measurement.

## Sub lesson 2 — Gain through selection — outside breeds with care
**Genetic gain is to come through selection and better management.** Introducing outside breeds is considered **only where the introduced animals — or their crosses with local breeds — prove adaptable to the area**: a breed that cannot live in the place cannot improve the flock there.
