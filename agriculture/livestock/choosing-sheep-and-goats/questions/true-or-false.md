# Choosing sheep and goats — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Weaning weight is quoted at 90 days.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Any outside breed will improve any flock anywhere.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Selection plus management is the route to genetic gain.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Adaptability to the area does not matter for introduced breeds.
**Answer: False**
