# Choosing sheep and goats — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give the three performance weights and their ages.
**Answer: Birth about 2.3 kg; weaning at 90 days about 11 kg; one year about 26–30 kg.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** State the rule for introducing outside breeds.
**Answer: Only where the introduced animals, or their crosses with local breeds, prove adaptable to the area.**
