# Choosing sheep and goats — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The quoted birth weight is about —
a) 2.3 kg  b) 11 kg  c) 26 kg
**Answer: a) 2.3 kg**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Weaning at 90 days weighs about —
a) 2.3 kg  b) 30 kg  c) 11 kg
**Answer: c) 11 kg**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** One-year weight in the data is about —
a) 66 kg  b) 26–30 kg  c) 11 kg
**Answer: b) 26–30 kg**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Genetic gain should come through —
a) selection and better management  b) buying any outside breed  c) chance mating
**Answer: a) selection and better management**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Outside breeds are introduced only where —
a) they are most expensive  b) they are largest  c) they or their crosses prove adaptable to the area
**Answer: c) they or their crosses prove adaptable to the area**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Adult goat body measurements quoted are around —
a) 90 cm  b) 66 cm  c) 2.3 cm
**Answer: b) 66 cm**
