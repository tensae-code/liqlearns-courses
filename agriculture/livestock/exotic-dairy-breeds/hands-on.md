# Hands-on — Exotic dairy breeds — the Holstein and others

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Breed cards
Make a card per exotic breed: name, one look, one number. Shuffle and quiz a partner.

## Milk maths
At 6,600 litres over a 305-day lactation, about how many litres a day is the Holstein giving? (About 21-22 litres.) Write the working.
