# Exotic dairy breeds — the Holstein and others — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give the Holstein's bargain in one sentence.
**Answer: It needs more feed than other breeds but repays it as the outstanding producer, over 6,600 litres per lactation.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name four other exotic breeds the book profiles.
**Answer: Any four of: Jersey, Brown Swiss, Guernsey, Ayrshire, Milking Shorthorn, Red Danish.**
