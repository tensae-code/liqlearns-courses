# Exotic dairy breeds — the Holstein and others — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Holstein is small and needs very little feed.
**Answer: False**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Holsteins can be red-and-white as well as black-and-white.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Milk fat and protein percentages differ between breeds.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Volume of milk is the only thing that matters in breed choice.
**Answer: False**
