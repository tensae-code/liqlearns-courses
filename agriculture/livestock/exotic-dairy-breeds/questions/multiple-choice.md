# Exotic dairy breeds — the Holstein and others — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Holstein Friesian comes from —
a) Ethiopia  b) the Netherlands  c) India
**Answer: b) the Netherlands**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A Holstein gives over — per lactation.
a) 600 litres  b) 6,600 litres  c) 66 litres
**Answer: b) 6,600 litres**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is NOT one of the exotic breeds profiled?
a) Jersey  b) Brown Swiss  c) Boran
**Answer: c) Boran**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Jersey-type milk is prized because it is —
a) blue  b) richer, around 3.6% fat and 3.2% protein  c) mostly water
**Answer: b) richer, around 3.6% fat and 3.2% protein**
