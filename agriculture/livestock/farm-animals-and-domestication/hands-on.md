# Hands-on — Farm animals — what domestication made

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Number bars
Draw six bars for the CSA counts to scale (70, 43, 53, 8, 57, 7 million). Label each animal. The picture makes the ranking unforgettable.

## Home herd census
Count your family's or neighbourhood's farm animals by species. Compare your small census with the national pattern: which species leads at home?
