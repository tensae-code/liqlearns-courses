# Farm animals — what domestication made — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Domestication means —
a) hunting wild animals  b) selectively breeding wild animals to live alongside humans  c) selling animals at market
**Answer: b) selectively breeding wild animals to live alongside humans**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Domestication followed —
a) the tractor  b) settled farming  c) the railway
**Answer: b) settled farming**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Ethiopia has about how many cattle (CSA 2021)?
a) 7 million  b) 70.3 million  c) 700 million
**Answer: b) 70.3 million**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Local breeds make up what share of Ethiopia's cattle?
a) about half  b) 97.4%  c) 10%
**Answer: b) 97.4%**
