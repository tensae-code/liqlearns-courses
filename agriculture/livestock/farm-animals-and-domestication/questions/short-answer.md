# Farm animals — what domestication made — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give any four CSA 2021 populations.
**Answer: Cattle 70.3M, sheep 42.9M, goats 52.5M, camels 8.1M, chickens 57.0M, beehives ~7.0M (any four).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does 'overwhelmingly indigenous herd' mean?
**Answer: That nearly all Ethiopia's farm animals are local breeds — e.g. 97.4% of cattle — fitted over centuries to local conditions, not exotic imports.**
