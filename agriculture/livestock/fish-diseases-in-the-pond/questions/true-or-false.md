# Fish diseases in the pond — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Farmed fish get diseases that farmers must know.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A sick fish in a cage cannot affect others.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Water is part of disease management.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Disease management waits until most fish die.
**Answer: False**
