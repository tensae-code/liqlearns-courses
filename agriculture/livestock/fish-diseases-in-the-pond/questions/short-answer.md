# Fish diseases in the pond — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why is pond disease a shared problem?
**Answer: Because all the fish share one water body; disease travels through that water to the whole stock.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does disease management act on?
**Answer: The water and the stock together — watching, spotting the sick, and managing both in ponds and cages.**
