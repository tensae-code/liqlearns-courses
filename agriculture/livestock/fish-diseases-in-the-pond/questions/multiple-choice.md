# Fish diseases in the pond — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In shared pond water, disease is —
a) never a private event  b) limited to one fish always  c) impossible
**Answer: a) never a private event**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The section names diseases —
a) of wild fish only  b) of poultry  c) of farmed fish
**Answer: c) of farmed fish**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A fish farmer must —
a) never look at fish  b) recognise disease in the stock  c) wait for deaths
**Answer: b) recognise disease in the stock**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Disease management covers —
a) ponds and cages  b) dry ponds only  c) markets only
**Answer: a) ponds and cages**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Management acts on —
a) one fish at a time only  b) the feed only  c) the water and the stock together
**Answer: c) the water and the stock together**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** In aquaculture, the patient's home (water) is also —
a) the cure itself  b) the medicine's road  c) irrelevant
**Answer: b) the medicine's road**
