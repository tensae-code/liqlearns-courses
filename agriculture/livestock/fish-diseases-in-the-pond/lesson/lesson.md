# Fish diseases in the pond

## Do it first
In a pond, you share water with every fish you own. When disease enters water, it does not visit one fish — it visits the address. How is pond disease managed?

## Sub lesson 1 — Diseases of farmed fish
The **diseases of farmed fish** are the sicknesses of ponds and cages: in shared water, a disease is never a private event. The section names the diseases a fish farmer must recognise in the stock.

[Picture 26655 — a sick fish with visible lesions beside a healthy one]

## Sub lesson 2 — Management in ponds and cages
Disease **management in ponds and cages** is the response system: watch the stock, spot the sick, and act on the water and the stock together. In aquaculture the patient's home is also the medicine's road — management thinks about the whole water body, not one fish at a time.
