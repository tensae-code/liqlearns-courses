Fish diseases in the pond.
The diseases of farmed fish are the sicknesses of ponds and cages: in shared water, a disease is never a private event.
Disease management in ponds and cages is the response system: watch the stock, spot the sick, and act on the water and the stock together.
Do it yourself.
Then say it to your companion.
