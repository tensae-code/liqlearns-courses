# Hands-on — The dairy business — where the profit is

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Forage year plan
Sketch a year wheel for a small farm: which months bought feed is dearest, and what on-farm grasses and legumes would cover them.

## Margin interview
Ask a milk seller what most eats their margin. Compare the answer with the lesson's 60-70% feed figure.
