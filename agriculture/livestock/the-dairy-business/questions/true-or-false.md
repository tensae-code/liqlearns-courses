# The dairy business — where the profit is — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Rising milk demand creates investment and employment opportunities.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Growing your own forage raises the feed bill.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Hygienic production helps protect profit.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Once the market is good, management no longer matters.
**Answer: False**
