# The dairy business — where the profit is — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why is the dairy outlook in Ethiopia described as good?
**Answer: Production, processing and marketing have profitable margins, and demand will rise with the economy and the urban middle class, creating investment and employment.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the three profit protectors.
**Answer: Year-round on-farm forage production (good grasses and legumes), hygienic production, and marketing.**
