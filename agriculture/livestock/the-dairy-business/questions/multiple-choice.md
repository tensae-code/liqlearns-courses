# The dairy business — where the profit is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In Ethiopia, milk production, processing and marketing have —
a) no margins  b) profitable margins  c) falling demand forever
**Answer: b) profitable margins**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Demand for milk will rise with —
a) the economy and the urban middle class  b) nothing  c) imports only
**Answer: a) the economy and the urban middle class**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The biggest lever protecting dairy profit is —
a) buying dearer feed  b) year-round on-farm forage production  c) milking less
**Answer: b) year-round on-farm forage production**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Feed's share of milk's cost, driving the forage lever, is —
a) 5%  b) 60-70%  c) 100%
**Answer: b) 60-70%**
