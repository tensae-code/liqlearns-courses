# Internal parasites — worms and flukes

## Do it first
An animal eats well and still thins. Something inside eats first. Four lodgers live in gut, liver and blood. Name the rooms they live in before the lesson names the lodgers.

## Sub lesson 1 — Lodgers inside
**Endoparasites live inside the host — tapeworm, roundworm, liver fluke and the trypanosome are pictured and described — draining the animal from within.** The feed the farmer paid for feeds the parasites first; growth, milk and strength leak away invisibly.

[Picture 26023 — the four internal parasites drawn at the organs they inhabit]

## Sub lesson 2 — Deworming
**Expelling them by drugs is deworming.** Deworming is done with the right drug at the right dose for the animal's weight, following veterinary or extension guidance — guessing doses wastes money and breeds drug resistance. The drained animal, correctly dewormed, turns its feed back into itself.
