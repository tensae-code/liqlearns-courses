# Internal parasites — worms and flukes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Endoparasites live —
a) on the skin  b) inside the host  c) in the soil only
**Answer: b) inside the host**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which is an internal parasite?
a) tick  b) liver fluke  c) louse
**Answer: b) liver fluke**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Deworming means —
a) washing the coat  b) expelling internal parasites by drugs  c) feeding more
**Answer: b) expelling internal parasites by drugs**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Doses should follow —
a) guesses  b) veterinary or extension guidance  c) neighbours' habits
**Answer: b) veterinary or extension guidance**
