# Choosing poultry — layer and broiler breeds — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which is a named layer breed?
a) Bovans Brown  b) Ambaza  c) Camelus ferus
**Answer: a) Bovans Brown**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** White Leghorn and Babcock are —
a) fish species  b) bee types  c) layer breeds
**Answer: c) layer breeds**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Layers are used primarily for —
a) draught  b) egg production  c) meat only
**Answer: b) egg production**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Parent stock serves —
a) hatchery purposes  b) only meat  c) guarding
**Answer: a) hatchery purposes**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Most parent-stock eggs are —
a) laid by wild birds  b) made locally from feed  c) imported
**Answer: c) imported**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Fayoumi is —
a) a spice  b) one of the named layer breeds  c) a fish
**Answer: b) one of the named layer breeds**
