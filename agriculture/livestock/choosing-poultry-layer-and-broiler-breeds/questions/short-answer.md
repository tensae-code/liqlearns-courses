# Choosing poultry — layer and broiler breeds — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the four layer breeds of the lesson.
**Answer: Bovans Brown, White Leghorn, Babcock and Fayoumi.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is parent stock for?
**Answer: It serves hatchery purposes: its eggs produce the generations that go on to lay (most parent-stock eggs are imported).**
