# Choosing poultry — layer and broiler breeds

## Do it first
Four brown-and-white hens stand in a row, all famous for eggs. A fifth bird grows like a balloon for meat. Choosing poultry starts with one question: eggs or meat?

## Sub lesson 1 — Layer breeds, named
Poultry breeds, classification and selection open the unit. Layer breeds are pictured and named: **Bovans Brown, White Leghorn, Babcock and Fayoumi**. Layers are used **primarily for egg production** — the breed is chosen for the laying life ahead of it.

## Sub lesson 2 — Parent stock and imports
Most **parent-stock eggs are imported**: the parent stock serves **hatchery purposes**, while their generations lay in Ethiopia. Several layer breeds **used to be imported** to Ethiopia — the history the section recounts, and the reason breed choice connects a village flock to a world trade in breeding stock.
