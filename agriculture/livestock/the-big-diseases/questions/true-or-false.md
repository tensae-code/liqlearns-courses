# The big diseases — FMD, anthrax and the rest — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Newcastle disease attacks poultry.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Non-infectious diseases spread between animals.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Anthrax can affect humans.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Opening a suspect anthrax carcass is safe.
**Answer: False**
