# The big diseases — FMD, anthrax and the rest — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name five major diseases.
**Answer: Any five of: FMD, anthrax, TB, brucellosis, CBPP, mastitis, Newcastle, coccidiosis, trypanosomiasis.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give anthrax's signs.
**Answer: High fever, convulsions, rapid breathing and sudden death.**
