# The big diseases — FMD, anthrax and the rest — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Infectious diseases are caused by —
a) cold floors only  b) viruses, bacteria, fungi or protozoa  c) hunger only
**Answer: b) viruses, bacteria, fungi or protozoa**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** FMD is —
a) mild and rare  b) highly contagious  c) a plant disease
**Answer: b) highly contagious**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Anthrax is caused by —
a) a virus  b) Bacillus anthracis  c) a worm
**Answer: b) Bacillus anthracis**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A suddenly dead suspect animal should be —
a) butchered fast  b) reported; carcass disposed of under veterinary direction  c) sold
**Answer: b) reported; carcass disposed of under veterinary direction**
