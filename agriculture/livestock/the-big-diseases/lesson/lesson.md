# The big diseases — FMD, anthrax and the rest

## Do it first
Nine names can empty a district's herds. One of them can also kill the farmer. Which names must a keeper know before the vet even arrives — and which one demands hands OFF the carcass? Learn the list and the warning.

## Sub lesson 1 — Two kinds of disease
**Diseases divide into infectious — caused by viruses, bacteria, fungi or protozoa, and transmissible — and non-infectious.** Ethiopia's major list runs: **foot-and-mouth disease (highly contagious), anthrax, tuberculosis, brucellosis, contagious bovine pleuropneumonia, mastitis, Newcastle disease of poultry, coccidiosis and trypanosomiasis** — each described by **cause, host, transmission, symptoms and prevention/control**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Anthrax — the human warning
**Anthrax is caused by *Bacillus anthracis*, infecting through contact or inhaled spores, with high fever, convulsions, rapid breathing and sudden death — and it can affect humans.** Prevention: **isolating infected animals and disposing of carcasses** under veterinary direction. A suddenly dead animal is reported, not opened or butchered — that rule protects the family as much as the herd.
