# Balancing a ration — the Pearson Square — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A ration is —
a) a week's feed  b) the feed given in a day  c) a medicine
**Answer: b) the feed given in a day**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A production ration adds feed for —
a) sleep only  b) growth, milk or eggs  c) nothing
**Answer: b) growth, milk or eggs**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The book's square for 16% protein gives —
a) 50 kg maize, 50 kg soybean  b) 80 kg maize, 20 kg soybean meal  c) 20 kg maize, 80 kg soybean
**Answer: b) 80 kg maize, 20 kg soybean meal**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A good ration is —
a) balanced, palatable, cheap and available  b) any one of those  c) expensive only
**Answer: a) balanced, palatable, cheap and available**
