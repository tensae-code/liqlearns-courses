# Genes, heredity and why breeding matters

## Do it first
A calf looks like its mother in the face, its father in the legs. Nobody taught it those shapes. Where were they written before the calf was born? Follow the writing — from trait to gene to code.

## Sub lesson 1 — Heredity and the code
**Genetics is the study of heredity — traits passing from parents to offspring through genes, small sections of DNA**, the molecule carrying the organism's **genetic code**. Every trait a breeder selects begins as information in that code.

[Picture 26010 — a calf standing between its parents, inheriting a marked trait from each]

## Sub lesson 2 — The four pillars
**Genetic improvement is one pillar of livestock development alongside health, nutrition and housing**: **feeding, health care and management raise what an animal does now, but only breeding raises what its offspring can do.** Neglect breeding and each generation starts where the last one started.
