# Lesson plan — Genes, heredity and why breeding matters

**Course:** Agriculture · **Title:** Livestock · **Lesson:** Genes, heredity and why breeding matters
**Place in the curriculum:** Agriculture — the Livestock title (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** genetics as the study of heredity — traits passing through genes, small sections of DNA — and why only breeding raises what offspring can do
**This lesson's own level:** Grade 11 depth — it stands on the learner's own farm and fields, and on the course's earlier titles where named below.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
- Sub lesson 1 — Heredity and the code: **Genetics is the study of heredity — traits passing from parents to offspring through genes, small sections of DNA**, the molecule carrying the organism's **genetic code**.
- Sub lesson 2 — The four pillars: **Genetic improvement is one pillar of livestock development alongside health, nutrition and housing**: **feeding, health care and management raise what an animal does now, but only breeding raises what its offspring can do.
Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Compound feed — factory-mixed rations. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Define genetics and genes: small sections of DNA carrying the code.
2. Say DNA carries the organism's genetic code.
3. Contrast feeding/health/housing (raise what an animal does now) with breeding (raises what offspring can do).
