# Four kinds of animal housing — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Deep litter suits poultry and pigs.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Loose housing cages each animal alone.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Sheep and goats may range by day and barn at night.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Free-range gives the highest productivity.
**Answer: False**
