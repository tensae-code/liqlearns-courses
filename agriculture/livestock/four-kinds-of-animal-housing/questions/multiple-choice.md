# Four kinds of animal housing — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Deep-litter bedding is —
a) removed daily to bare stone  b) spread and topped with fresh layers as it soils  c) burned
**Answer: b) spread and topped with fresh layers as it soils**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Loose housing gives animals —
a) cages  b) a free open yard with shelter  c) no shelter
**Answer: b) a free open yard with shelter**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Free-range is —
a) costly and controlled  b) cheap but exposed to predators, low productivity  c) intensive
**Answer: b) cheap but exposed to predators, low productivity**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Modern farms use —
a) open hills only  b) separate pens with cut-and-carry or prepared feed  c) no feed
**Answer: b) separate pens with cut-and-carry or prepared feed**
