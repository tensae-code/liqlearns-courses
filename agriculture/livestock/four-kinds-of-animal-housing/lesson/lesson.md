# Four kinds of animal housing

## Do it first
Four keepers: one locks poultry on deep litter; one lets cattle roam a yard; one ranges sheep over hills; one runs walled pens with prepared feed. Four houses, four bargains. Guess each bargain's price and prize.

## Sub lesson 1 — Conventional and loose housing
**Conventional barns/houses include deep-litter poultry and pig housing, where straw or sawdust bedding is spread and fresh layers added as it soils.** **Loose housing: animals move freely in an open yard with shelter** — shown for chickens, beef and dairy cattle.

[Picture 26018 — the four housing types in four panels]

## Sub lesson 2 — Free-range and modern farms
The **free-range system: stock on large natural or cultivated pasture with watering points and shelters, headquarters at the centre, an attendant moving with the animals — cheap but exposed to predators and low in productivity**, with **sheep and goats often ranged by day and barned at night**. **Modern farms: full- or half-walled separate pens with cut-and-carry or prepared feed** — the controlled end of the ladder.
