# Concentrates — energy and protein in small amounts

## Do it first
A small scoop of feed lifts a cow's milk more than another armload of straw. How can less feed do more? Hold the question — density is the answer.

## Sub lesson 1 — The dense opposite
**Concentrates are the opposite class to roughages: low fibre, high TDN**, fed in **smaller amounts** to **lift energy or protein**. They do not replace the bulky base; they correct and enrich it.

[Picture 26004 — a small scoop of concentrate beside the big pile of roughage it supplements]

## Sub lesson 2 — Energy and protein concentrates
**Energy concentrates come from grains**. **Protein concentrates** come from **plants (oilseed cakes)** and from **animal sources — meat meal, dried blood meal and fish meal**. Choose by the gap: short of energy, add grain; short of protein, add cake or meal.
