# Concentrates — energy and protein in small amounts — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Contrast concentrate with roughage.
**Answer: Concentrate: low fibre, high TDN, small amounts. Roughage: bulky, high fibre, low TDN, the diet's base.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A ration lacks protein. What do you add?
**Answer: A protein concentrate — an oilseed cake or an animal meal such as fish meal.**
