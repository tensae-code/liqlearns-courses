# Concentrates — energy and protein in small amounts — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Concentrates are —
a) high fibre, low TDN  b) low fibre, high TDN  c) pure water
**Answer: b) low fibre, high TDN**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Concentrates are fed —
a) by the truckload only  b) in smaller amounts to lift energy or protein  c) never
**Answer: b) in smaller amounts to lift energy or protein**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Energy concentrates come from —
a) grains  b) stones  c) hay only
**Answer: a) grains**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is an animal protein concentrate?
a) fish meal  b) grass  c) silage
**Answer: a) fish meal**
