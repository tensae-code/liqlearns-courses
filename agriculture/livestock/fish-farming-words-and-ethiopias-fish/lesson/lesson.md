# Fish farming words — and Ethiopia's fish

## Do it first
Four fish on a market slab: a tilapia, a great silver perch, a whiskered catfish, a barbus. If you cannot name them, you cannot farm them. Names first.

## Sub lesson 1 — Aquaculture terms
Basic terms in aquaculture open the unit — the words for farming fish: the water, the enclosures, the young fish, the feeding and harvesting vocabulary every later section uses. Fish farming has its own language because its farm is water.

## Sub lesson 2 — Ethiopia's farmed and fished species
The species are pictured and named. The **cichlid family includes three tilapias in Ethiopia — Nile tilapia (*Oreochromis niloticus*), *Tilapia zillii* and *Tilapia galilaea*** — alongside **Nile perch (*Lates niloticus*)**, the **African catfish (*Clarias gariepinus*, commonly called 'Ambaza')** and ***Barbus* species (commonly named 'Nech-asa')**.
