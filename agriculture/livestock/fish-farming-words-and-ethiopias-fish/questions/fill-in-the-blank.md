# Fish farming words — and Ethiopia's fish — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Nile tilapia is Oreochromis ___.
**Answer: niloticus**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The African catfish is commonly called ___.
**Answer: Ambaza**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Barbus is commonly named ___.
**Answer: Nech-asa**
