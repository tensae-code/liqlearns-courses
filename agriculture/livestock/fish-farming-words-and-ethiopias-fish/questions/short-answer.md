# Fish farming words — and Ethiopia's fish — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name Ethiopia's three tilapias.
**Answer: Nile tilapia (Oreochromis niloticus), Tilapia zillii and Tilapia galilaea — of the cichlid family.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give the scientific names of Nile perch and the African catfish.
**Answer: Nile perch: Lates niloticus. African catfish: Clarias gariepinus.**
