# Fish farming words — and Ethiopia's fish — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Three tilapias of the cichlid family are named for Ethiopia.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Nile perch and Nile tilapia are the same fish.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Clarias gariepinus is commonly called Ambaza.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Tilapia zillii is a catfish.
**Answer: False**
