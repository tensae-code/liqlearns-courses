# Fish farming words — and Ethiopia's fish — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Aquaculture is —
a) farming fish (and other water animals)  b) catching only wild fish  c) a net type
**Answer: a) farming fish (and other water animals)**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The unit opens with —
a) disease control  b) marketing  c) basic aquaculture terms
**Answer: c) basic aquaculture terms**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A fish farm's 'field' is —
a) a forest  b) water  c) dry soil
**Answer: b) water**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Nile tilapia's scientific name is —
a) Oreochromis niloticus  b) Lates niloticus  c) Clarias gariepinus
**Answer: a) Oreochromis niloticus**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** 'Ambaza' is the common name of —
a) Nile perch  b) Barbus  c) the African catfish
**Answer: c) the African catfish**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** 'Nech-asa' names —
a) Nile perch  b) Barbus species  c) the tilapias
**Answer: b) Barbus species**
