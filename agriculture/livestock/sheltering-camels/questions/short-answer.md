# Sheltering camels — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why is camel housing treated briefly in the enterprise?
**Answer: Because camels live most of their lives on the move with pastoral herds; their shelter is a night resting arrangement, not permanent yard housing.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Describe a camel herd's night.
**Answer: The animals settle together in a protected resting place, guarded, until the next day's browsing round.**
