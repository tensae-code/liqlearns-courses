# Sheltering camels — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Camels spend most of their lives moving with pastoral herds.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Camels need dairy-style stall housing.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Night shelter protects the resting herd.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Pastoral camels rest alone, scattered.
**Answer: False**
