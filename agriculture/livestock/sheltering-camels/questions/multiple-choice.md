# Sheltering camels — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Camel housing is fitted to animals that —
a) live on the move with pastoral herds  b) stay in stalls all year  c) are milked by machine
**Answer: a) live on the move with pastoral herds**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The housing section is —
a) the unit's longest  b) about palaces  c) brief, in proportion to a moving life
**Answer: c) brief, in proportion to a moving life**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Shelter must serve —
a) storage  b) rest and safety  c) showing animals
**Answer: b) rest and safety**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** At night the herd —
a) settles together in a protected resting place  b) scatters alone  c) returns to a barn with stalls
**Answer: a) settles together in a protected resting place**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A camel's 'housing' is best described as —
a) a milking shed  b) a feed store  c) the herd's night arrangement
**Answer: c) the herd's night arrangement**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The next day after rest begins —
a) a race  b) the browsing round  c) a market trip
**Answer: b) the browsing round**
