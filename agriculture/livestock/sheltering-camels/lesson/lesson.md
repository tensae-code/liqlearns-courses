# Sheltering camels

## Do it first
A camel herd walks all day and rests at night wherever the herd stops. What shelter does a moving animal need?

## Sub lesson 1 — Shelter in the enterprise pattern
Camel housing follows the enterprise pattern — **shelter** — but fitted to camels: animals whose housing must serve rest and safety without pretending they are yard animals. The section treats shelter briefly, in proportion to a life spent on the move.

[Picture 26638 — a night enclosure (boma) with camels resting inside]

## Sub lesson 2 — The pastoral herd's night
Camels live most of their lives **on the move with pastoral herds**. Their shelter is the herd's night arrangement: a protected resting place where the animals settle together, guarded, until the next day's browsing round begins.
