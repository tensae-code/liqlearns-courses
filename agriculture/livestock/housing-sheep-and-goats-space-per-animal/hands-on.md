# Hands-on — Housing sheep and goats — space per animal

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Chalk the squares
Chalk 1.2, 2.0 and 0.8 m² squares; have learners stand in them and judge the fit honestly.

## Pen census
Count a real flock by class and measure its pen; deliver the verdict: enough space or not, by the table.
