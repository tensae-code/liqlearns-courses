# Housing sheep and goats — space per animal — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ground-level housing suits drier tropical regions.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Space requirements are the same for every class of stock.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A breeding male gets more space than a breeding female.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Crowding helps flock health.
**Answer: False**
