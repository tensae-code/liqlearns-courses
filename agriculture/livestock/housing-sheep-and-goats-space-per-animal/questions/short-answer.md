# Housing sheep and goats — space per animal — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give the three zero-grazing space figures.
**Answer: 1.2 m² per breeding female; 2.0 m² per breeding male; 0.8 m² per young animal.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why measure the pen before building?
**Answer: Because space is a health input: crowding spreads disease and stress, and the tabled figures differ by class of stock.**
