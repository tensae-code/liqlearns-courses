# Housing sheep and goats — space per animal — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ground-level housing is common where —
a) rainfall is not excessive, in semi-arid and sub-tropical regions  b) rain is heaviest  c) snow falls
**Answer: a) rainfall is not excessive, in semi-arid and sub-tropical regions**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A lean-to is built —
a) in a river  b) underground  c) against an existing wall
**Answer: c) against an existing wall**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Housing type should fit —
a) the market  b) the climate  c) the owner's taste only
**Answer: b) the climate**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Under zero grazing, a breeding female needs —
a) 1.2 m²  b) 2.0 m²  c) 0.8 m²
**Answer: a) 1.2 m²**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A breeding male needs —
a) 1.2 m²  b) 0.8 m²  c) 2.0 m²
**Answer: c) 2.0 m²**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** A young animal needs —
a) 5 m²  b) 0.8 m²  c) 2.0 m²
**Answer: b) 0.8 m²**
