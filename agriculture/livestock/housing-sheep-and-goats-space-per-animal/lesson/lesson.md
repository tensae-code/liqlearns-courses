# Housing sheep and goats — space per animal

## Do it first
Chalk a square on the ground, 1.2 metres by 1 metre. That is one breeding female's whole home under permanent confinement. Is it enough? Step inside the numbers.

## Sub lesson 1 — Housing types — ground-level and lean-to
Housing types begin with **ground-level housing**, common across the tropics especially in **semi-arid and sub-tropical regions where rainfall is not excessive**, and **lean-to** forms built against an existing wall. The type fits the climate: heavy-rain country demands more roof and drainage than dry country.

## Sub lesson 2 — Space per animal, tabled
Space requirements are tabled by class of stock. Under **permanent confinement (zero grazing)**: **1.2 m² per breeding female, 2.0 m² per breeding male, and 0.8 m² per young animal**. Space is a health input: crowding spreads disease and stress, so the pen is measured before it is built.
