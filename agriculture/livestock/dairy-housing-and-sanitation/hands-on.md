# Hands-on — Dairy housing and farm sanitation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Byre wash-down study
Watch or help with a real wash-down. Note the order surfaces are cleaned and where the dirty water goes.

## Handler hygiene drill
Practise the milking-time routine: clean clothes, washed hands, clean pails — in order, timed, until it is habit.
