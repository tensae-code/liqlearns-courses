# Dairy housing and farm sanitation — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does regular sanitation clean in the byre?
**Answer: Floors, bedding, walls and feeding surfaces.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why is sanitation called disease control done daily?
**Answer: Because guarding every contamination route prevents the infections that debilitate calves and cows, before treatment is needed.**
