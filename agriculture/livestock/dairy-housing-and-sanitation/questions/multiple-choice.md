# Dairy housing and farm sanitation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The dairy herd's two common houses are —
a) loose housing and the conventional barn  b) cages and pits  c) trees and caves
**Answer: a) loose housing and the conventional barn**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Dairy cattle house successfully —
a) only in total confinement  b) from close confinement to near-freedom except at milking  c) only outdoors
**Answer: b) from close confinement to near-freedom except at milking**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Sanitation requires regular cleaning of —
a) floors, bedding, walls and feeding surfaces  b) only the milk can  c) nothing in particular
**Answer: a) floors, bedding, walls and feeding surfaces**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Guarding contamination routes prevents —
a) rain  b) infections that debilitate calves and cows  c) milking
**Answer: b) infections that debilitate calves and cows**
