Layers vs broilers — two managements.
Layer management aims at a long, steady lay: birds kept healthy, fed for eggs, and housed with nests and collection flow, producing over a long laying life.
Broiler management aims at fast, efficient meat growth to slaughter weight: feed converted to body, quickly, in a short life.
