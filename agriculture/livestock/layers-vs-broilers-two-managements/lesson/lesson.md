# Layers vs broilers — two managements

## Do it first
Two houses, two clocks: one bird is managed for a long, steady year of eggs; the other for a few fast weeks of meat. Manage them the same way and both fail.

## Sub lesson 1 — Layers — managed for a long, steady lay
Layer management aims at a **long, steady lay**: birds kept healthy, fed for eggs, and housed with nests and collection flow, producing over a long laying life. The layer keeper's calendar is long and even — no rushing a hen.

[Picture 26646 — one house of layers at nest boxes, one of broilers at feeders]

## Sub lesson 2 — Broilers — managed for fast, efficient meat
Broiler management aims at **fast, efficient meat growth to slaughter weight**: feed converted to body, quickly, in a short life. The broiler keeper's calendar is short and intense. The two managements are separated and taught in turn because almost every decision — feed, space, timing — differs between them.
