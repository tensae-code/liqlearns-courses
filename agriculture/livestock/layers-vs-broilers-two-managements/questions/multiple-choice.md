# Layers vs broilers — two managements — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Layers are managed for —
a) a long, steady lay  b) fastest slaughter weight  c) brooding only
**Answer: a) a long, steady lay**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A layer's productive life is —
a) a few weeks  b) one month  c) long
**Answer: c) long**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Layer housing centres on —
a) no fittings  b) nests and egg collection flow  c) only feeders
**Answer: b) nests and egg collection flow**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Broilers are managed for —
a) fast, efficient meat growth to slaughter weight  b) a long lay  c) egg production
**Answer: a) fast, efficient meat growth to slaughter weight**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Broiler life to slaughter is —
a) long and even  b) two years  c) short and intense
**Answer: c) short and intense**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The managements are taught separately because —
a) of tradition only  b) almost every decision differs between them  c) they are the same bird
**Answer: b) almost every decision differs between them**
