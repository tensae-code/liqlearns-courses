# Layers vs broilers — two managements — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** State each type's management aim.
**Answer: Layers: a long, steady lay over a long laying life. Broilers: fast, efficient meat growth to slaughter weight in a short life.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why are the two managements taught in turn?
**Answer: Because almost every management decision — feed, space, timing — differs between layers and broilers.**
