# Hatching eggs — the incubator and its hygiene — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The incubator must supply —
a) heat, humidity and ventilation  b) only heat  c) only light
**Answer: a) heat, humidity and ventilation**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Humidity in the incubator comes from —
a) the eggs themselves  b) the fan  c) water to moisten the air
**Answer: c) water to moisten the air**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Ventilation means —
a) heating the air  b) fresh air in, stale air out  c) sealing the box
**Answer: b) fresh air in, stale air out**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Hatchery sanitation —
a) breaks the disease cycle  b) colours the chicks  c) speeds the hatch by a day
**Answer: a) breaks the disease cycle**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Cleaning and disinfection happen —
a) once a year  b) never  c) after each hatch and before the next cycle
**Answer: c) after each hatch and before the next cycle**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Hatching eggs are collected and stored —
a) in the laying nests for weeks  b) under rules that keep them fertile and clean  c) anywhere warm
**Answer: b) under rules that keep them fertile and clean**
