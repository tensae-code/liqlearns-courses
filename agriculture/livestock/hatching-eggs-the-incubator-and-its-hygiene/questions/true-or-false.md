# Hatching eggs — the incubator and its hygiene — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Eggs in an incubator are turned like a hen turns them.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** An incubator can succeed with heat alone.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Disinfection before the next cycle is part of sanitation.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Dirty hatching eggs hatch as well as clean ones.
**Answer: False**
