# Hatching eggs — the incubator and its hygiene — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** List the incubator's three supplies and what each does.
**Answer: Heat (warmth for development), humidity (water moistening the air), ventilation (fresh air in, stale air out) — plus turning of the eggs.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** When is the hatchery disinfected, and why?
**Answer: After each hatch and before the next cycle, to break the disease cycle between batches.**
