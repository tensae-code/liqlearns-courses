Hatching eggs — the incubator and its hygiene.
The incubator must supply heat, humidity (water to moisten the air) and ventilation (fresh air in, stale air out) — the hen's body, rebuilt as a machine.
Hatchery sanitation breaks the disease cycle: cleaning and disinfection after each hatch and before the next cycle.
Do it yourself.
Then say it to your companion.
