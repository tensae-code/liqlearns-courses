Ways of farming fish — ponds, cages, pens.
Culture is classified by the sex/species mix — including poly-sex culture, where either the male or the female of two or more species is reared in the same pond.
Culture is also classified by enclosure: pond fish culture (dug water), cage fish culture (a floating net enclosure in open water) and pen/enclosure fish culture (a fenced part of a natural water body) — pictured side by side so the three can be told apart at a glance..
