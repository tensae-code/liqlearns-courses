# Ways of farming fish — ponds, cages, pens — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Culture classification has two bases in the lesson —
a) the sex/species mix, and the enclosure  b) price and size  c) owner and market
**Answer: a) the sex/species mix, and the enclosure**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Poly-sex culture rears —
a) only one species ever  b) fish without water  c) one sex of two or more species in the same pond
**Answer: c) one sex of two or more species in the same pond**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The mix in a pond decides —
a) the price of nets  b) competition, breeding and harvest  c) the pond's colour
**Answer: b) competition, breeding and harvest**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Pond culture uses —
a) dug water (a pond)  b) a floating cage  c) a fenced lake corner
**Answer: a) dug water (a pond)**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Cage culture is —
a) a dug pond  b) a dry pen  c) a floating net enclosure in open water
**Answer: c) a floating net enclosure in open water**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Pen culture is —
a) a river net  b) a fenced part of a natural water body  c) a glass tank
**Answer: b) a fenced part of a natural water body**
