# Ways of farming fish — ponds, cages, pens — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** One sex of several species in one pond: ___-sex culture.
**Answer: poly**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A floating net enclosure is a ___.
**Answer: cage**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A fenced corner of a lake is a ___.
**Answer: pen**
