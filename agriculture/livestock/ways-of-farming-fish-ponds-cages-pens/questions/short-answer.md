# Ways of farming fish — ponds, cages, pens — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Define poly-sex culture.
**Answer: Rearing either the male or the female of two or more species in the same pond.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Tell pond, cage and pen culture apart.
**Answer: Pond: culture in dug water. Cage: a floating net enclosure in open water. Pen: a fenced part of a natural water body.**
