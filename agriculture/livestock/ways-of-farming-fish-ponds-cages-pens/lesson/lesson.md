# Ways of farming fish — ponds, cages, pens

## Do it first
Three farmers farm the same lake: one digs beside it, one floats a cage on it, one fences a corner of it. All are fish farmers. What differs?

## Sub lesson 1 — Sorting by who shares the water
Culture is classified by the **sex/species mix** — including **poly-sex culture, where either the male or the female of two or more species is reared in the same pond**. The mix decides competition, breeding and harvest in the shared water: who lives with whom is a farming decision.

## Sub lesson 2 — Sorting by enclosure — pond, cage, pen
Culture is also classified by **enclosure**: **pond fish culture** (dug water), **cage fish culture** (a floating net enclosure in open water) and **pen/enclosure fish culture** (a fenced part of a natural water body) — pictured side by side so the three can be told apart at a glance.
