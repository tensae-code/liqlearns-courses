# Hands-on — The dairy enterprise — an overview

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Milk-chain walk
Walk from a milk point (shop, can, collection centre) as far back toward the farm as you safely can. Note each hand the milk passes through.

## Chain on paper
On one page, draw your home area's milk chain: cow, can, carrier, seller, drinker. Mark where hygiene matters most.
