# The dairy enterprise — an overview — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A dairy cow is judged mainly by the milk she gives.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Dual-purpose cattle milk more than true dairy types.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Feeding is part of the dairy enterprise, not separate from it.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Disease has no place in planning a dairy enterprise.
**Answer: False**
