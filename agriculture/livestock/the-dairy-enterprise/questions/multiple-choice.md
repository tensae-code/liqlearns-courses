# The dairy enterprise — an overview — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Dairy-type cattle are raised to —
a) convert feed into high-quality milk  b) pull ploughs only  c) give wool
**Answer: a) convert feed into high-quality milk**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Dual-purpose cattle give —
a) milk only  b) both meat and milk  c) eggs and milk
**Answer: b) both meat and milk**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The dairy unit's chain ends with —
a) breeds  b) profitability  c) housing
**Answer: b) profitability**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which comes first in the enterprise chain?
a) processing  b) breeds and selection  c) profit
**Answer: b) breeds and selection**
