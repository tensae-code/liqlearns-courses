# The dairy enterprise — an overview

## Do it first
Follow one litre of milk backwards in your mind: shop, can, cow, feed, field. How many different jobs had to go right before that litre existed? Name them before the lesson names the enterprise's parts.

## Sub lesson 1 — Dairy cattle and dual-purpose cattle
**Dairy-type cattle** are raised for one main job: to **convert feed into high-quality milk**. Everything about them — their shape, their feeding, their housing — is judged by the milk they give.

**Dual-purpose cattle** give **both meat and milk**. They milk less than a true dairy type but return beef as well, which suits farms that need both from one animal.

## Sub lesson 2 — The whole enterprise, in order
The dairy unit runs the **whole enterprise** as one chain: **breeds** first (which cattle milk best, here and from abroad), then **selection** (choosing the breed, then the individual cow), **feeding** (the biggest cost), **milk production and processing** (the lactation cycle, hygiene, cheese and ghee), **housing**, **disease**, and finally **profitability** — whether the business pays.

Hold the chain in order: a break anywhere — a poor breed, thin feed, dirty milk, a sick cow — breaks the whole enterprise.

## Say it to your companion
