# Lesson plan — The dairy enterprise — an overview

**Course:** Agriculture · **Title:** Livestock · **Lesson:** The dairy enterprise — an overview
**Place in the curriculum:** Agriculture — the Livestock title (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** what a dairy enterprise is — cattle kept to turn feed into milk — and the whole chain the unit walks: breeds, selection, feeding, milk, housing, disease and profit
**This lesson's own level:** Grade 11 depth — it stands on the learner's own farm and fields, and on the course's earlier titles where named below.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
- Sub lesson 1 — Dairy cattle and dual-purpose cattle: **Dairy-type cattle** are raised for one main job: to **convert feed into high-quality milk**.
- Sub lesson 2 — The whole enterprise, in order: The dairy unit runs the **whole enterprise** as one chain: **breeds** first (which cattle milk best, here and from abroad), then **selection** (choosing the breed, then the individual cow), **feeding** (the biggest cost), **milk production and processing** (the lactation cycle, hygiene, cheese and ghee), **housing**, **disease**, and finally **profitability** — whether the business pays.
Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the Livestock title's earlier lessons on cattle and production systems. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what dairy-type cattle are raised for, and what dual-purpose cattle give.
2. List the parts of the dairy enterprise in order: breeds, selection, feeding, milk production and processing, housing, disease, profitability.
3. Explain why the unit ends with profitability — the enterprise must pay.

## What you need
A notebook; a walk past a local dairy farm or milk collection point if one is near.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks dairy means only milking → the enterprise starts long before milking: breed, selection and feed decide the milk.
- Mixes dairy-type with dual-purpose → dairy-type = milk first; dual-purpose = milk AND meat.
