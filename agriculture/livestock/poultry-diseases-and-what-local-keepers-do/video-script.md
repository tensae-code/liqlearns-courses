# Video script — Poultry diseases — and what local keepers do

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Poultry diseases — and what local keepers do.
The unit names the major poultry diseases and their controlling methods — the sicknesses that can empty a house, and the keeper's defences against them, in the enterprise pattern every animal unit this year follows..
The unit's activity sends pupils in small groups to list the major poultry diseases they have observed or experienced in their area and explain the possible indigenous controlling mechanisms.
Do it yourself.
Then say it to your companion.
