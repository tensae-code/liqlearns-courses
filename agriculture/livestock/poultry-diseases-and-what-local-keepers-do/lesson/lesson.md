# Poultry diseases — and what local keepers do

## Do it first
Before any book knowledge: what do the poultry keepers around you already do when birds sicken? This lesson sends you to collect their knowledge first.

## Sub lesson 1 — The major diseases and their control
The unit names the **major poultry diseases and their controlling methods** — the sicknesses that can empty a house, and the keeper's defences against them, in the enterprise pattern every animal unit this year follows.

[Picture 26647 — a sick hen isolated in a small pen]

## Sub lesson 2 — Field work — collecting indigenous control knowledge
The unit's activity sends pupils **in small groups** to **list the major poultry diseases they have observed or experienced in their area and explain the possible indigenous controlling mechanisms**. Local knowledge is treated as evidence to collect: what keepers do, what they have seen work, recorded carefully beside the book's control methods.

## Say it to your companion
Explain this lesson to your companion in your own words, using one real example from your own area for each sub lesson. Your companion asks one question about each part — answer from the lesson, not from memory of other lessons.

## Do It Yourself
1. Run the group activity: your area's disease list with local controls.
2. Isolate-and-observe: write what you would watch on a sick hen's first day.
3. Compare one local control with the book's method for the same disease.
