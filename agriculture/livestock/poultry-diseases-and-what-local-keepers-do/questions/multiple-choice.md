# Poultry diseases — and what local keepers do — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The unit names —
a) the major poultry diseases and their control methods  b) only layer diseases  c) no control methods
**Answer: a) the major poultry diseases and their control methods**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Disease control in poultry follows —
a) no pattern  b) market rules  c) the enterprise pattern of the animal units
**Answer: c) the enterprise pattern of the animal units**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A sick hen should first be —
a) mixed with chicks  b) isolated  c) sold
**Answer: b) isolated**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The field activity is done —
a) in small groups  b) alone at home  c) by the teacher only
**Answer: a) in small groups**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Pupils list diseases they have —
a) read about abroad  b) never seen  c) observed or experienced in their area
**Answer: c) observed or experienced in their area**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Indigenous controlling mechanisms are treated as —
a) secrets to hide  b) evidence to collect  c) rumours to ignore
**Answer: b) evidence to collect**
