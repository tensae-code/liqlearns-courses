# Poultry diseases — and what local keepers do — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** State the field activity exactly.
**Answer: In small groups, list the major poultry diseases observed or experienced in your area and explain the possible indigenous controlling mechanisms for them.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does the unit collect local knowledge?
**Answer: Because keepers' observed experience is evidence: their controlling mechanisms, recorded carefully, stand beside the book's methods.**
