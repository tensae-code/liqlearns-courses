# Inside a fish — bony fish anatomy

## Do it first
A fish breathes water, swims without legs, and floats without sinking. Point to the parts that do each job before you read their names.

## Sub lesson 1 — The body plan
Basic anatomy of **bony fishes** gives the body plan a fish farmer or handler must know: the labelled structure of a bony fish — the parts on the surface and inside that feeding, health and handling all refer back to.

## Sub lesson 2 — Fins, gills and swim bladder
The named parts include the **fins** (movement and steering), the **gills** (breathing — taking oxygen from water) and the **swim bladder** (buoyancy — floating at depth without effort). Every later section — feeding, disease, handling — speaks this anatomy, so it is learned as a working map, not a picture.
