# Inside a fish — bony fish anatomy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's anatomy is of —
a) bony fishes  b) cartilage sharks only  c) frogs
**Answer: a) bony fishes**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The farmer learns anatomy because later sections —
a) never use it  b) only draw it  c) refer back to these parts
**Answer: c) refer back to these parts**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The labelled structure covers —
a) only the scales  b) surface and internal parts  c) only the tail
**Answer: b) surface and internal parts**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Gills do the fish's —
a) breathing: taking oxygen from water  b) digesting  c) seeing
**Answer: a) breathing: taking oxygen from water**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The swim bladder gives the fish —
a) speed  b) colour  c) buoyancy
**Answer: c) buoyancy**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Fins give the fish —
a) buoyancy only  b) movement and steering  c) breathing
**Answer: b) movement and steering**
