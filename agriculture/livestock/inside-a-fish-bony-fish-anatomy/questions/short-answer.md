# Inside a fish — bony fish anatomy — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three parts the lesson highlights and each one's job.
**Answer: Fins — movement and steering; gills — breathing (oxygen from water); swim bladder — buoyancy.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why is anatomy a 'working map' for a fish farmer?
**Answer: Because feeding, disease and handling sections all refer to these parts; handling and health work need the body plan.**
