# Roughages — bulky, fibrous feeds

## Do it first
Fill a manger. One feed takes a whole armload to fill it and the cow chews all afternoon. Another fills a small scoop. Which pile is the roughage? Judge by bulk and fibre before the numbers are given.

## Sub lesson 1 — The two great feed classes
Feeds classify as **roughages or concentrates**. **Roughages are bulky, high in fibrous carbohydrate — over 18% crude fibre — and low in digestible energy — under 60% total digestible nutrients (TDN)**, the sum of digestible fibre, protein, fat and carbohydrate.

[Picture 26003 — a manger piled high with hay and straw, volume emphasised]

## Sub lesson 2 — The herbivore's natural feed
Roughages are **the natural feed of herbivores** and make **over half of a ruminant's diet**. They divide into **dry roughages** (hay, straw) and **succulents** such as **pasture and silage**. Bulk is not a weakness here — the rumen is built to work on fibre.
