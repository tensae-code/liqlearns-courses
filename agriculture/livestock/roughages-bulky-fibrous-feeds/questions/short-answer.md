# Roughages — bulky, fibrous feeds — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Define roughage with its two numbers.
**Answer: Bulky feed with over 18% crude fibre and under 60% TDN.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does bulk suit a ruminant?
**Answer: The rumen is built to ferment fibre; roughage is its natural feed and over half its diet.**
