# Roughages — bulky, fibrous feeds — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Roughages have crude fibre —
a) under 5%  b) over 18%  c) exactly 0%
**Answer: b) over 18%**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** TDN of roughages is —
a) over 90%  b) under 60%  c) 100%
**Answer: b) under 60%**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Roughages make what share of a ruminant's diet?
a) a pinch  b) over half  c) none
**Answer: b) over half**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Succulent roughages include —
a) pasture and silage  b) stones  c) bran only
**Answer: a) pasture and silage**
