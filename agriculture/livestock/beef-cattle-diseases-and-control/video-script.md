Beef cattle diseases and control.
The health section of the enterprise pattern names the major diseases of beef cattle — the sicknesses a beef keeper must recognise early, because a meat herd's value walks on four legs and a disease strikes the whole investment at once..
For each disease the section gives its methods of control: the keeper's defences — hygiene, isolation of the sick, vaccination and veterinary help where the book directs.
Do it yourself.
Then say it to your companion.
