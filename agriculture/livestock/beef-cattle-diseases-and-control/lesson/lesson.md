# Beef cattle diseases and control

## Do it first
One sick animal in a herd is never only one animal's problem. Trace what a single disease can cost a beef herd before reading the control side.

## Sub lesson 1 — The major diseases
The health section of the enterprise pattern names the **major diseases of beef cattle** — the sicknesses a beef keeper must recognise early, because a meat herd's value walks on four legs and a disease strikes the whole investment at once.

[Picture 26629 — a vaccination crush in use at a beef farm]

## Sub lesson 2 — Control methods
For each disease the section gives its **methods of control**: the keeper's defences — hygiene, isolation of the sick, vaccination and veterinary help where the book directs. Control is a system practised before animals fall sick, not a panic after; the healthy herd is protected by routine, not luck.
