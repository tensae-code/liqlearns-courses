# Beef cattle diseases and control — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why must beef diseases be recognised early?
**Answer: Because the herd's value is in living animals; one disease can strike the whole investment at once.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name three control defences from the lesson.
**Answer: Hygiene, isolation of the sick, and vaccination (with veterinary help where directed).**
