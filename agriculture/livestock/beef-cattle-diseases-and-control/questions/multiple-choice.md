# Beef cattle diseases and control — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The health section names —
a) the major diseases of beef cattle  b) only rare diseases  c) no diseases
**Answer: a) the major diseases of beef cattle**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Disease must be recognised —
a) only at slaughter  b) never mind  c) early
**Answer: c) early**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A sick beef animal threatens —
a) only its own feed bill  b) the whole herd investment  c) nothing else
**Answer: b) the whole herd investment**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Control methods include —
a) hygiene, isolation and vaccination as the book directs  b) waiting and hoping  c) selling sick animals quickly
**Answer: a) hygiene, isolation and vaccination as the book directs**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Control is practised —
a) only during outbreaks  b) after the herd is lost  c) before animals fall sick, as a routine
**Answer: c) before animals fall sick, as a routine**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** A sick animal should be —
a) hidden from the vet  b) isolated from the herd  c) mixed with calves
**Answer: b) isolated from the herd**
