# Beef cattle diseases and control — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Early recognition matters in beef disease control.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** One sick animal is only its own problem.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Vaccination can be part of disease control.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Control begins only after many animals are sick.
**Answer: False**
