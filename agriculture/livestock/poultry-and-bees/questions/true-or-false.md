# Poultry — layers and broilers; the bee colony — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Layers are kept for eggs.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Broilers are usually kept for a year of laying.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Workers and the queen are both female.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Bees give honey but do not pollinate crops.
**Answer: False**
