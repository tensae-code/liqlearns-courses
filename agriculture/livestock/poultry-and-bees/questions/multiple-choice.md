# Poultry — layers and broilers; the bee colony — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Broilers are chickens kept —
a) for eggs  b) for meat, usually slaughtered at 6–8 weeks  c) for feathers only
**Answer: b) for meat, usually slaughtered at 6–8 weeks**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which is NOT poultry?
a) guinea fowl  b) turkeys  c) camels
**Answer: c) camels**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In a bee colony, drones are —
a) female and reproductive  b) male, stingless, with much larger compound eyes  c) the largest caste
**Answer: b) male, stingless, with much larger compound eyes**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The queen bee is —
a) male  b) female, reproductive and larger  c) stingless
**Answer: b) female, reproductive and larger**
