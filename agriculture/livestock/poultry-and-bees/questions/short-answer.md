# Poultry — layers and broilers; the bee colony — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How do layers and broilers differ in job and time?
**Answer: Layers are kept for eggs over a long laying life; broilers are kept for meat and usually slaughtered at 6–8 weeks.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Describe the three bee castes.
**Answer: The queen: female, reproductive, larger. Workers: female, doing the colony's work. Drones: male, stingless, with much larger compound eyes.**
