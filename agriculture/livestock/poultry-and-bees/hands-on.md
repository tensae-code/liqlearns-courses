# Hands-on — Poultry — layers and broilers; the bee colony

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Egg-or-meat audit
Visit a poultry keeper if you can: ask which birds are layers, which broilers, and the broilers' slaughter age. Compare with the lesson's 6–8 weeks.

## Caste cards
Draw queen, worker and drone in a row at true relative sizes. Label sex and one duty each. The queen must be visibly largest.
