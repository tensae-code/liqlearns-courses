# Lesson plan — Poultry — layers and broilers; the bee colony

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Cattle, sheep, goats and camels. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. List the poultry kinds and split chickens into layers (eggs) and broilers (meat, slaughtered at 6–8 weeks).
2. Run the bee colony's three castes with sexes and duties: queen (female, reproductive, larger), workers (female), drones (male, stingless, larger compound eyes).
3. Say bees live on pollen and nectar and give pollination as well as honey.

## If the learner is stuck
- Keeps broilers for eggs → broilers are meat birds, slaughtered at 6–8 weeks; layers are the egg birds.
- Stings the drone → drones are stingless males; workers and queen are the females.
