# Breed introduction — gains and risks

## Do it first
A famous foreign bull arrives, champion at home. Will he transform the herd — or sicken, suffer in the heat and disappoint? List one hope and one fear before the lesson weighs both.

## Sub lesson 1 — The gain — quick lift
**Introducing a breed from elsewhere can raise productivity quickly**, and **crossbreds may outperform local breeds if they adapt**. New blood brings traits the local herd lacks — milk, growth, size — in one generation rather than many.

[Picture 26011 — an exotic bull arriving at an Ethiopian farm, with a question mark over the climate]

## Sub lesson 2 — The risks — disease and adaptation
The disadvantages are plain: **introduced animals can bring new diseases and pests**, **exotic breeds may struggle to adapt**, and **they may never reach full productivity in the new environment**. The condition in the gain — *if they adapt* — is the whole lesson: introduction is a bet on adaptation.
