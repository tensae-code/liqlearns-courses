# Breed introduction — gains and risks — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Breed introduction can raise productivity —
a) slowly only  b) quickly  c) never
**Answer: b) quickly**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Crossbreds outperform locals —
a) always  b) if they adapt  c) never
**Answer: b) if they adapt**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Introduced animals can bring —
a) rain  b) new diseases and pests  c) free feed
**Answer: b) new diseases and pests**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A third risk is exotics may —
a) fly away  b) never reach full productivity in the new environment  c) turn local
**Answer: b) never reach full productivity in the new environment**
