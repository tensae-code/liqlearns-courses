# Breed introduction — gains and risks — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Introduction guarantees success.
**Answer: False**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Adaptation decides whether gains appear.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Exotic breeds always adapt easily.
**Answer: False**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** New pests can arrive with introduced animals.
**Answer: True**
