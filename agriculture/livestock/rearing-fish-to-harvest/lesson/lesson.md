# Rearing fish to harvest

## Do it first
A fingerling is a fish the length of your finger. A table fish fills a plate. Between them lies every day of rearing. What does the farmer actually do in between?

## Sub lesson 1 — The rearing job
**Fish rearing management** is the **day-to-day and season-to-season care of growing fish from fingerling to table size**. Rearing is the long middle of fish farming: the stock is alive, growing, and depending on the keeper every single day.

[Picture 26652 — fingerlings being stocked into a pond]

## Sub lesson 2 — Care that follows the growth
The rearing keeper watches growth, adjusts feeding and water care, and guards the stock through the seasons. No single act makes a table fish — the accumulated daily care does, which is why rearing is taught as management, not as an event.
