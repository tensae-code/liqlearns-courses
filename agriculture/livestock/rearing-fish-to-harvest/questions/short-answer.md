# Rearing fish to harvest — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Define fish rearing management.
**Answer: The day-to-day and season-to-season care of growing fish from fingerling to table size.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why is rearing 'management, not an event'?
**Answer: Because no single act produces the table fish; accumulated daily care — watching, feeding, water care, guarding — does.**
