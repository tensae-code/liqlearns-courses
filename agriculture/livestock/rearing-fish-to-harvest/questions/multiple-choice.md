# Rearing fish to harvest — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Rearing manages fish from —
a) fingerling to table size  b) egg to fingerling only  c) table to export only
**Answer: a) fingerling to table size**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Rearing care is —
a) done once at stocking  b) only at harvest  c) day-to-day and season-to-season
**Answer: c) day-to-day and season-to-season**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A fingerling is —
a) a net  b) a young fish, about a finger long  c) an old breeder
**Answer: b) a young fish, about a finger long**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The rearing keeper adjusts —
a) feeding and water care as fish grow  b) nothing after stocking  c) only prices
**Answer: a) feeding and water care as fish grow**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A table fish is made by —
a) one big feeding  b) the harvest net  c) accumulated daily care
**Answer: c) accumulated daily care**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Rearing is taught as —
a) a market skill  b) management, not an event  c) a single act
**Answer: b) management, not an event**
