# Rearing fish to harvest — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Rearing covers the growing period of the fish.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Once stocked, fish need no further care.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Season-to-season care is part of rearing.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Harvest size is reached without feeding adjustments.
**Answer: False**
