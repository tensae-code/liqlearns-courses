# Hands-on — How farm animals digest — ruminant vs monogastric

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Chamber chain
Draw the cow's tract as a chain of labelled boxes: mouth → oesophagus → four chambers. Size the rumen box largest — the picture must show 'first and largest'.

## Crop timing
Feed observation: watch chickens feed, then rest. Note the bulging crop after feeding — the 12-hour store at work — and write what you saw with times.
