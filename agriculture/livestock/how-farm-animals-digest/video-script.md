# Video script — How farm animals digest — ruminant vs monogastric

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Digestion starts at the mouth.
Ruminants: four chambers — rumen first and largest.
Camels: three chambers. Monogastrics: one.
Chickens store in the crop, grind in the gizzard.
