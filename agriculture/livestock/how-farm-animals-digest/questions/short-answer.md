# How farm animals digest — ruminant vs monogastric — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the ruminant's four stomach chambers in order.
**Answer: Rumen, reticulum, omasum, abomasum.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How does the chicken handle grain without teeth?
**Answer: Feed is stored in the crop for up to 12 hours, then trickles into the stomach and the gizzard, where it is ground.**
