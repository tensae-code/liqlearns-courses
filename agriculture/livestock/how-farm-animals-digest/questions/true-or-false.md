# How farm animals digest — ruminant vs monogastric — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Digestion starts at the mouth, where tongue and teeth gather and break feed.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Camels, as pseudo-ruminants, have four chambers like cattle.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The chicken grinds feed in its gizzard.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The oesophagus carries feed from stomach to mouth.
**Answer: False**
