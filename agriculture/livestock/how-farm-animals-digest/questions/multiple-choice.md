# How farm animals digest — ruminant vs monogastric — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The ruminant stomach's first and largest chamber is the —
a) omasum  b) rumen  c) abomasum
**Answer: b) rumen**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The four chambers in order are —
a) rumen, reticulum, omasum, abomasum  b) abomasum, omasum, reticulum, rumen  c) reticulum, rumen, abomasum, omasum
**Answer: a) rumen, reticulum, omasum, abomasum**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Monogastrics have —
a) four stomachs  b) a single stomach and need easily digested feed  c) no stomach
**Answer: b) a single stomach and need easily digested feed**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A chicken stores feed in its crop for —
a) up to 12 hours  b) 5 minutes  c) a week
**Answer: a) up to 12 hours**
