# How farm animals digest — ruminant vs monogastric — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The tube carrying feed from mouth to stomach is the ___ .

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Cattle, sheep and goats, with four stomach chambers, are called ___ .
