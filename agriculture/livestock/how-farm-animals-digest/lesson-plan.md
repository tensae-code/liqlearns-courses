# Lesson plan — How farm animals digest — ruminant vs monogastric

**Course:** Agriculture · **Title:** Livestock · **Lesson:** How farm animals digest — ruminant vs monogastric
**Place in the curriculum:** Agriculture — the Livestock title (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** digestion from mouth to stomach in the three plans — the ruminant's four chambers (rumen, reticulum, omasum, abomasum), the pseudo-ruminant camel's three chambers, and the monogastric's single stomach, with the chicken's crop and gizzard
**This lesson's own level:** Grade 11 depth — it stands on the learner's own farm and fields, and on the course's earlier titles where named below.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
- Sub lesson 1 — The ruminant plan — four chambers: Digestion starts at the **mouth — tongue and teeth gather and break feed** — and the **oesophagus** carries feed to the stomach.
- Sub lesson 2 — Monogastrics — and the chicken's crop and gizzard: **Monogastrics** have a **single stomach** and rely on **easily digested feed** — one chamber cannot do the rumen's slow work, so the feed must arrive ready.
Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Cattle, sheep, goats and camels. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Trace digestion's start: mouth (tongue and teeth gather and break feed) and oesophagus to the stomach.
2. Name the ruminant's four chambers in order and the pseudo-ruminant's three-chamber plan.
3. Describe the monogastric plan and the chicken's special path: crop storing feed up to 12 hours, then stomach and gizzard grinding.
