# Brooding chicks — the first weeks — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Brooding is —
a) care of young birds through the chick stage  b) incubation of eggs  c) culling old hens
**Answer: a) care of young birds through the chick stage**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Above all, brooding provides —
a) flight training  b) deep water  c) warmth
**Answer: c) warmth**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Cold chicks —
a) grow fastest  b) pile up, smother and die  c) spread out and eat
**Answer: b) pile up, smother and die**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Warm chicks —
a) spread, eat and grow  b) pile silently  c) stop drinking
**Answer: a) spread, eat and grow**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Rearing carries birds from chicks into —
a) layers in one day  b) parent stock  c) growers
**Answer: c) growers**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Brooding includes —
a) only guarding  b) feed, water and guarding  c) only heat
**Answer: b) feed, water and guarding**
