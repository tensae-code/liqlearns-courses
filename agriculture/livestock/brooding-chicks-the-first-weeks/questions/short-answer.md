# Brooding chicks — the first weeks — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How do you read brooder temperature without a thermometer?
**Answer: By the chicks' behaviour: piling means cold; spreading evenly, eating and growing means the warmth is right.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name brooding's provisions besides warmth.
**Answer: Feed, water and guarding, continuing through rearing into the grower stage.**
