# Brooding chicks — the first weeks — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Care of young chicks is called ___.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Cold chicks pile; warm chicks ___.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Chicks grow into ___ before adult stages.
