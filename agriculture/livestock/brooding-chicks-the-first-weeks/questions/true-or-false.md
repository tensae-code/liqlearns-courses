# Brooding chicks — the first weeks — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Chick behaviour shows whether brooder warmth is right.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Chicks can keep themselves warm from day one.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The first weeks shape the future flock.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Growers come before chicks in the rearing order.
**Answer: False**
