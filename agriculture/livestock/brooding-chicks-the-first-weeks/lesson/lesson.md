# Brooding chicks — the first weeks

## Do it first
A day-old chick cannot keep itself warm, feed itself wisely, or escape danger. For its first weeks, you are its mother. What does a mother hen actually provide?

## Sub lesson 1 — Warmth first
**Brooding** is the care of young birds through the chick stage: above all **warmth** — chicks gather under the heat the way they gather under a hen. Cold chicks pile, smother and die; warm chicks spread, eat and grow. The brooder's temperature is read in the chicks' behaviour.

## Sub lesson 2 — Feed, water and guarding into growers
Brooding and **rearing** continue with **feed, water and guarding** through the chick stage into **growers**. The first weeks decide the flock: birds brooded well become the growers — and then the layers and broilers — of the later sections.
