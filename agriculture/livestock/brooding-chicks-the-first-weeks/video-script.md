Brooding chicks — the first weeks.
Brooding is the care of young birds through the chick stage: above all warmth — chicks gather under the heat the way they gather under a hen.
Brooding and rearing continue with feed, water and guarding through the chick stage into growers.
Do it yourself.
Then say it to your companion.
