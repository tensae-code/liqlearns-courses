# Catching fish — the gears — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What decides the choice of a fishing gear?
**Answer: The water, the fish being caught, and the keeper using it.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does a farming unit teach capture gears?
**Answer: Because the same waters and communities hold farmed and wild fish; the gears complete the picture of how fish reach the table.**
