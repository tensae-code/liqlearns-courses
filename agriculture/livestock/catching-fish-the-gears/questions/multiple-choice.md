# Catching fish — the gears — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Fishing methods use —
a) nets and other gears  b) only hands  c) only hooks ever
**Answer: a) nets and other gears**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Capture fisheries take —
a) only farmed fish  b) only fingerlings  c) wild fish
**Answer: c) wild fish**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A gear is chosen for —
a) its price alone  b) its water, fish and keeper  c) its colour
**Answer: b) its water, fish and keeper**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The cast net lands —
a) as a circle and sinks  b) in a line  c) on the shore
**Answer: a) as a circle and sinks**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Capture and culture —
a) never meet  b) are enemies  c) work the same waters and communities
**Answer: c) work the same waters and communities**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson completes the keeper's picture of —
a) feed making  b) how fish reach the table  c) pond digging
**Answer: b) how fish reach the table**
