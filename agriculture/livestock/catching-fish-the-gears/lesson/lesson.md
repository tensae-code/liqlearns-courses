# Catching fish — the gears

## Do it first
A net blooms open in the air like a parachute, lands as a circle, and sinks with its edges weighted. Inside it: breakfast. Catching fish is a craft of gears.

## Sub lesson 1 — Fishing methods and gears
The **methods of catching fish** — **nets and the other gears of the section** — serve **capture fisheries** alongside the farmed stock. Each gear fits its water, its fish and its keeper: the gear is chosen, not accidental.

## Sub lesson 2 — Capture beside culture
This lesson stands in a farming unit on purpose: the same waters hold farmed fish and wild fish, and the same communities work both. Knowing the gears of capture completes the fish keeper's picture of how fish reach the table.
