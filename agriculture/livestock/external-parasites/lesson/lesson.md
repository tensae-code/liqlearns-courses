# External parasites — ticks, lice and mites

## Do it first
A cow rubs, stamps, never rests; its hide — future leather — is scarred, its blood drunk by lodgers ON the skin. Inside parasites hide; these show themselves. Where do they sit, and what do they cost?

## Sub lesson 1 — Lodgers on the skin
**Ectoparasites — ticks, flies, lice and mites — bite, embed in or irritate the skin, attacking blood, skin and hair.** The tick swells with the animal's blood while the animal pays in rest and condition.

[Picture 26024 — a tick engorged on a cow's hide, with lice and mites magnified beside it]

## Sub lesson 2 — The damage and the control
They cause **anaemia, weight and condition loss, restlessness, skin irritation and destruction** — and damaged hides lose their leather value. **Control routes include spraying and dipping**, done with the product's directions followed exactly and protective measures taken — these are poisons aimed at parasites, handled as such.
