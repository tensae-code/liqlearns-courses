# External parasites — ticks, lice and mites — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ectoparasites attack —
a) the blood, skin and hair from outside  b) only the stomach  c) seeds
**Answer: a) the blood, skin and hair from outside**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which is an ectoparasite?
a) tapeworm  b) tick  c) fluke
**Answer: b) tick**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** External parasites cause —
a) anaemia and condition loss  b) extra milk  c) stronger hides
**Answer: a) anaemia and condition loss**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Control routes include —
a) spraying and dipping  b) singing  c) starving
**Answer: a) spraying and dipping**
