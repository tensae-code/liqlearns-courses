# External parasites — ticks, lice and mites — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** List four harms of ectoparasites.
**Answer: Any four of: anaemia, weight loss, condition loss, restlessness, skin irritation/destruction.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two control routes.
**Answer: Spraying and dipping.**
