Feeding poultry.
Poultry feeds and feeding methods match feed to the bird's stage: the ration behind a growing chick is not the ration behind a laying hen.
Behind ration choices stand feed composition figures — protein and energy values such as the 0.28/0.6 figures of the section's tables.
Do it yourself.
Then say it to your companion.
