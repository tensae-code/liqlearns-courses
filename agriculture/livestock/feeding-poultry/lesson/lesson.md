# Feeding poultry

## Do it first
A chick, a grower and a laying hen walk to the same feeder. Should they eat the same feed? Their bodies are doing three different jobs.

## Sub lesson 1 — Feeds and feeding methods
Poultry **feeds and feeding methods** match feed to the bird's stage: the ration behind a growing chick is not the ration behind a laying hen. Feeding is the enterprise's biggest daily decision, repeated at every feeder, every day.

[Picture 26642 — chicks, growers and layers at three feeders with three rations]

## Sub lesson 2 — Feed composition figures
Behind ration choices stand **feed composition figures — protein and energy values** such as the 0.28/0.6 figures of the section's tables. The keeper reads composition the way a cook reads a recipe: the numbers decide what a ration can and cannot do for birds at each stage.
