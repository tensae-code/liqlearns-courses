# Feeding poultry — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why do chicks and layers eat differently?
**Answer: Because their bodies do different jobs — growing versus laying — so their rations are matched to their stage.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What figures stand behind ration choices?
**Answer: Feed composition figures — protein and energy values (such as the section's 0.28/0.6 table figures).**
