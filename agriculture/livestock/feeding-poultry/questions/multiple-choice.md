# Feeding poultry — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Poultry feeding matches feed to —
a) the bird's stage  b) the weather only  c) the colour of the feeder
**Answer: a) the bird's stage**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A laying hen's ration differs from a chick's because —
a) hens eat nothing  b) chicks eat meat  c) their bodies do different jobs
**Answer: c) their bodies do different jobs**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Feeding is the enterprise's —
a) last step  b) biggest daily decision  c) rarest job
**Answer: b) biggest daily decision**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Ration choices rest on —
a) feed composition figures: protein and energy values  b) guesswork  c) feeder size
**Answer: a) feed composition figures: protein and energy values**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Composition figures act like —
a) a market price  b) a breed name  c) a recipe the keeper reads
**Answer: c) a recipe the keeper reads**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The section's tables include figures such as —
a) 3–5%  b) 0.28/0.6 protein and energy values  c) 110/212 kg
**Answer: b) 0.28/0.6 protein and energy values**
