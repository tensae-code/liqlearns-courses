# What holds livestock back — and the answers

## Do it first
The same herd, two pastures: one dry-season-bare with thin cattle, one managed and green. The animals did not change — the constraints did. Which constraint starves first, which kills fastest, and which answers answer them?

## Sub lesson 1 — The major constraint — feed shortage
**Feed shortage is the major constraint.** **Natural pasture and crop residues supply over 90% of feed** — but they run only **3–6% crude protein**, with **severe dry-season gaps** when the pasture fails entirely.

Bulk is not nutrition: a full belly of 3% protein straw cannot build milk or meat. The feed problem is quality and season at once — too little protein all year, too little of anything in the dry months.

## Sub lesson 2 — Disease, tradition and weak services — and their answers
**Disease and parasites** are answered by **feeding, vaccination, isolating and treating sick animals, and clean housing management** — the constraint list carries its mitigations with it.

Beyond them stand **the traditional production system itself** — low-input, low-output by design — and **socio-economic constraints such as poor infrastructure and weak extension services**: roads, markets and advice that never reach the herd. Naming all four constraints is the start of answering them.
