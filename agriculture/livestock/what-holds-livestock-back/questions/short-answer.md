# What holds livestock back — and the answers — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why is feed shortage more than a quantity problem?
**Answer: Because the main feeds — natural pasture and crop residues, over 90% of feed — carry only 3–6% crude protein, and the dry season brings severe gaps; animals lack quality all year and quantity in dry months.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the four constraints on animal production.
**Answer: Feed shortage; disease and parasites; the traditional production system itself; socio-economic constraints such as poor infrastructure and weak extension services.**
