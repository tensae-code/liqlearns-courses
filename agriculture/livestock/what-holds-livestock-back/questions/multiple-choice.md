# What holds livestock back — and the answers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The major constraint on animal production is —
a) too many animals  b) feed shortage  c) too much rain
**Answer: b) feed shortage**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Natural pasture and crop residues supply —
a) over 90% of feed  b) about 20% of feed  c) no feed
**Answer: a) over 90% of feed**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Disease and parasites are answered by —
a) doing nothing  b) feeding, vaccination, isolating and treating sick animals, clean housing  c) selling all animals
**Answer: b) feeding, vaccination, isolating and treating sick animals, clean housing**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Pasture and residue feed runs about —
a) 30% crude protein  b) 3–6% crude protein  c) 60% crude protein
**Answer: b) 3–6% crude protein**
