# Hands-on — What holds livestock back — and the answers

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Protein reality check
Compare a handful of dry straw with a handful of legume forage or oil cake. Write which carries protein and which mostly fills — the 3–6% problem in your hands.

## Constraint walk
Walk a local herd's ground: feed state, housing cleanliness, sick animals separated?, road/market access. Score each constraint present or absent.
