# Selection — mass and progeny

## Do it first
Two cows: one milks heavily herself; the other milks modestly but every daughter is a champion. Which do you keep for breeding? Your answer picks between two kinds of selection — learn their names.

## Sub lesson 1 — Mass selection — judge the animal
**Selection improves a herd from within.** **Mass selection: animals are judged on their own performance; the desired ones are kept from a large group.** It is fast and direct — the record in front of you is the animal itself.

[Picture 26012 — a row of cows, the best marked to keep, judged once by themselves and once by their calves]

## Sub lesson 2 — Progeny selection — judge the offspring
**Progeny selection: animals are judged on how their offspring perform — mothers of the best offspring are retained, mothers of poor performers removed.** It is slower, but it reads heredity itself. Over both stands the contrast: **artificial selection by the farmer** against **natural selection**, where the environment alone decides.
