# Selection — mass and progeny — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Mass selection judges —
a) the offspring only  b) the animal's own performance  c) the weather
**Answer: b) the animal's own performance**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In mass selection the desired ones are kept from —
a) a large group  b) one animal  c) imports only
**Answer: a) a large group**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Progeny selection judges —
a) coat colour  b) how offspring perform  c) the sire's price
**Answer: b) how offspring perform**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Mothers of poor performers are —
a) retained  b) removed  c) prized
**Answer: b) removed**
