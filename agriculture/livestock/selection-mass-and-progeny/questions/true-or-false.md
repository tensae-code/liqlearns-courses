# Selection — mass and progeny — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Selection improves a herd from within.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Mass selection waits for grandchildren.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Progeny selection keeps mothers of the best offspring.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Artificial selection is done by the environment.
**Answer: False**
