# Choosing where to build — site selection

## Do it first
Choose the plot: a damp hollow by the road, or a drained rise set back, with empty land beside it. The house lasts decades — a wrong site taxes every year. Judge the two plots before the checklist is given.

## Sub lesson 1 — Ground, roads and distance
**Site selection comes first in a new farm** and weighs: **soil structure and drainage**; **road access**; **distance from other farm houses, national highways and main market roads — to cut disease transmission**; and **enough space between houses to slow disease spread**. Disease travels on roads and through neighbours — distance is a fence.

## Sub lesson 2 — Size — plan to grow
The site needs **adequate total size with room to expand — plan for doubling in future years**. Pupils practise by **choosing and justifying a site on school land**: a good choice defends every factor aloud, not just the view.
