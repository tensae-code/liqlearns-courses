# Choosing where to build — site selection — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name four site factors.
**Answer: Any four of: soil/drainage, road access, distance from houses/highways/markets, spacing between houses, size/expansion.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why keep distance from main roads?
**Answer: To cut disease transmission from passing stock and traffic.**
