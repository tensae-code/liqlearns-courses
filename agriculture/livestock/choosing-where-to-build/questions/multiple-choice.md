# Choosing where to build — site selection — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Site selection weighs —
a) soil structure and drainage  b) paint colour  c) animal names
**Answer: a) soil structure and drainage**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Distance from highways and markets cuts —
a) rent  b) disease transmission  c) sunlight
**Answer: b) disease transmission**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Space between houses slows —
a) growth  b) disease spread  c) rain
**Answer: b) disease spread**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Plan the site for —
a) shrinking  b) doubling in future years  c) one season
**Answer: b) doubling in future years**
