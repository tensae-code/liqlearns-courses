# Beekeeping in Ethiopia — log, top-bar and frame hives

## Do it first
Three hives stand in a row: a hollow log, a long sloping box, a stacked box of frames. Each is a generation of beekeeping. What did each generation fix — and what did it cost?

## Sub lesson 1 — Traditional beekeeping
Ethiopia's systems run in three generations. **Traditional**: forest beekeeping and the **traditional log hive** — the oldest system, bees kept in logs hung or placed where the bees work, with harvest simple and control limited.

## Sub lesson 2 — Transitional and modern
**Transitional**: the **top-bar hive** — a **single-storey long box with sidewalls sloping inward toward the bottom, covered with bars of fixed width** — intermediate between traditional and modern. **Modern**: **movable-frame beekeeping**, where frames lift out for inspection and harvest. Each step trades simplicity for control: the beekeeper gains inspection and honey quality, and pays in cost and skill.
