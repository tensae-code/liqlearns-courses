# Beekeeping in Ethiopia — log, top-bar and frame hives — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ethiopia's beekeeping systems number —
a) three generations  b) two  c) five
**Answer: a) three generations**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The traditional hive is the —
a) frame hive  b) glass hive  c) log hive
**Answer: c) log hive**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Traditional beekeeping includes —
a) no hives  b) forest beekeeping  c) only factory apiaries
**Answer: b) forest beekeeping**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The top-bar hive is —
a) transitional, between traditional and modern  b) the oldest  c) the most modern
**Answer: a) transitional, between traditional and modern**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A top-bar hive's sidewalls —
a) are vertical glass  b) form a pyramid  c) slope inward toward the bottom
**Answer: c) slope inward toward the bottom**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Modern beekeeping uses —
a) no boxes  b) movable frames  c) solid logs
**Answer: b) movable frames**
