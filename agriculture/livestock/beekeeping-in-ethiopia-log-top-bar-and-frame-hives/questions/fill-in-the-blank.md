# Beekeeping in Ethiopia — log, top-bar and frame hives — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The three systems: traditional, ___, modern.
**Answer: transitional**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Top-bar walls slope ___ toward the bottom.
**Answer: inward**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Modern hives use movable ___.
**Answer: frames**
