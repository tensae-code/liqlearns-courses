# Beekeeping in Ethiopia — log, top-bar and frame hives — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Describe the top-bar hive exactly.
**Answer: A single-storey long box with sidewalls sloping inward toward the bottom, covered with bars of fixed width.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does each generation trade?
**Answer: Simplicity for control: later systems gain inspection and honey quality, and pay in cost and skill.**
