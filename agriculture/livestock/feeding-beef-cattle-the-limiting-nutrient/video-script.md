The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Feeding beef cattle — the limiting nutrient.
Nutrient requirements are stated in layers: maintenance, maintenance + production, and maintenance + production + reproduction.
Most nutrients can be fed in excess without harm — but if any one nutrient is short, performance is limited by it, like the barrel's shortest stave.
Do it yourself.
Then say it to your companion.
