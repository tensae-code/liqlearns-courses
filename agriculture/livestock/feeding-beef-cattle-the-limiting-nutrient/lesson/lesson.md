# Feeding beef cattle — the limiting nutrient

## Do it first
A barrel holds only as much water as its shortest stave allows. Feed an animal ten nutrients and starve it of one — which one sets its growth?

## Sub lesson 1 — Requirements stack: maintenance, production, reproduction
Nutrient requirements are stated in layers: **maintenance**, **maintenance + production**, and **maintenance + production + reproduction**. Each layer adds its demand: keeping the body alive, then growing or fattening, then breeding on top.

[Picture 26627 — a barrel with staves of different heights, the shortest labelled with the limiting nutrient]

## Sub lesson 2 — The limiting nutrient; requirements vary
Most nutrients can be fed in excess without harm — but **if any one nutrient is short, performance is limited by it**, like the barrel's shortest stave. Required levels vary with **breed, age, weight, level of production and activity**: there is no single ration for all beef cattle, only the right ration for this animal now.
