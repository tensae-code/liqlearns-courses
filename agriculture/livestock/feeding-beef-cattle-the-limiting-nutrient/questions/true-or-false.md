# Feeding beef cattle — the limiting nutrient — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Reproduction adds its own nutrient demand on top of maintenance and production.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A ration short in one nutrient still gives full performance.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The limiting nutrient works like a barrel's shortest stave.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** One fixed ration suits all beef cattle.
**Answer: False**
