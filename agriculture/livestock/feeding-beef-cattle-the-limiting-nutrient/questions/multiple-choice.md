# Feeding beef cattle — the limiting nutrient — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Requirements are stated as maintenance, maintenance + production, and —
a) maintenance + production + reproduction  b) maintenance only  c) production without maintenance
**Answer: a) maintenance + production + reproduction**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Maintenance means —
a) fattening for market  b) breeding  c) keeping the body alive
**Answer: c) keeping the body alive**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The layers of requirement —
a) are the same for all animals  b) stack: each adds its demand  c) cancel out
**Answer: b) stack: each adds its demand**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** If one nutrient is short, performance is —
a) limited by that nutrient  b) unaffected  c) doubled
**Answer: a) limited by that nutrient**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Feeding most nutrients in excess is —
a) always fatal  b) the main problem  c) usually harmless
**Answer: c) usually harmless**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Required levels vary with —
a) the colour of the trough  b) breed, age, weight, production and activity  c) the day of the week
**Answer: b) breed, age, weight, production and activity**
