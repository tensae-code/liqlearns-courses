# Feeding beef cattle — the limiting nutrient — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three requirement layers.
**Answer: Maintenance; maintenance + production; maintenance + production + reproduction.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain the limiting nutrient with the barrel image.
**Answer: A barrel holds water only to its shortest stave; likewise an animal performs only to the level of its shortest (most lacking) nutrient.**
