# How bees live — biology and behaviour

## Do it first
Every management act in beekeeping is a bet on how bees behave. Before managing them, learn the animal: how a bee lives, grows and organises its work.

## Sub lesson 1 — Bee biology
**Bee biology** — the honey bee's life: its development and body, the living machine the beekeeper works with (building on Grade 11's colony castes). Management that fights the bee's biology fails; management that follows it succeeds quietly.

[Picture 26658 — a bee's life stages from egg to forager]

## Sub lesson 2 — Bee behaviour
**Bee behaviour** — the colony's behaviour: how bees communicate, forage, defend and organise their work. All management rests on these two: the beekeeper reads behaviour the way a herder reads a herd, and times every intervention to the colony's own rhythms.

## Say it to your companion
