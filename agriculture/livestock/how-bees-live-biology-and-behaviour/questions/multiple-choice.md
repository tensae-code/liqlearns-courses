# How bees live — biology and behaviour — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson rests management on —
a) bee biology and behaviour  b) hive colour  c) market prices
**Answer: a) bee biology and behaviour**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Grade 11 taught the colony's —
a) diseases only  b) hive types  c) castes
**Answer: c) castes**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Management that fights bee biology —
a) is cheapest  b) fails  c) works best
**Answer: b) fails**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Colony behaviour includes —
a) communication, foraging and defence  b) only stinging  c) sleeping only
**Answer: a) communication, foraging and defence**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The beekeeper reads behaviour like —
a) a book price  b) a weather chart only  c) a herder reads a herd
**Answer: c) a herder reads a herd**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Interventions are timed to —
a) the buyer's visits  b) the colony's own rhythms  c) the calendar alone
**Answer: b) the colony's own rhythms**
