# How bees live — biology and behaviour — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does bee biology give the beekeeper?
**Answer: Knowledge of the bee's life and development — the living machine every management act works with.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why time interventions to the colony's rhythms?
**Answer: Because management that follows bee behaviour succeeds, while management that fights the colony's own organisation fails.**
