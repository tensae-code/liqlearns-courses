# Managing bees through the year

## Do it first
A hive is not managed once. It is visited through the year — opened, read, fed when hungry, left alone when working. Walk the beekeeper's year.

## Sub lesson 1 — The standing practice — inspection
**Management of honey bees** is a standing practice: **inspection** of colonies — opening, reading and acting on what the frames say — repeated through the seasons. The smoker, the veil and the calm hand are the inspector's tools.

## Sub lesson 2 — Feeding and the seasonal round
The unit pictures **common feeding practices**: bees are fed when their own stores and the flowers fail them. Feeding, inspection and timing make the **seasonal round of the apiary** — management as a calendar that follows flowers and weather, not a single event.
