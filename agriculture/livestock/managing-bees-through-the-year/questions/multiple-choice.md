# Managing bees through the year — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Bee management is —
a) a standing, seasonal practice  b) a one-time setup  c) only harvest day
**Answer: a) a standing, seasonal practice**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Inspection means —
a) looking at the hive from the road  b) counting hives  c) opening, reading and acting on the frames
**Answer: c) opening, reading and acting on the frames**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The inspector's manner is —
a) loud  b) calm, with smoker and veil  c) fast and bare-handed
**Answer: b) calm, with smoker and veil**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Bees are fed when —
a) their stores and the flowers fail them  b) they are full  c) never
**Answer: a) their stores and the flowers fail them**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The apiary's round follows —
a) the market only  b) the moon only  c) flowers and weather
**Answer: c) flowers and weather**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Feeding practices are —
a) the same for all seasons  b) pictured in the unit  c) left to guesswork
**Answer: b) pictured in the unit**
