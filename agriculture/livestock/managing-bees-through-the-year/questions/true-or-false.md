# Managing bees through the year — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Inspection repeats through the seasons.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A hive needs no visits between setting up and harvest.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Feeding answers hunger when stores and flowers fail.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The apiary calendar ignores flowering seasons.
**Answer: False**
