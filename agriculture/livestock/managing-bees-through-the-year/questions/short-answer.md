# Managing bees through the year — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does hive inspection consist of?
**Answer: Opening the colony, reading what the frames say (colony state, brood, stores), and acting on it.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** When are bees fed?
**Answer: When their own stores and the available flowers fail them — the dearth moments of the seasonal round.**
