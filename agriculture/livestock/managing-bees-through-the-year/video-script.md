The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Managing bees through the year.
Management of honey bees is a standing practice: inspection of colonies — opening, reading and acting on what the frames say — repeated through the seasons.
The unit pictures common feeding practices: bees are fed when their own stores and the flowers fail them.
Do it yourself.
Then say it to your companion.
