# Crop residues and forage crops — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Crop residues are —
a) roots only  b) straws and stovers left after harvest  c) stones
**Answer: b) straws and stovers left after harvest**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Residues matter most —
a) in the rains  b) in the dry season  c) never
**Answer: b) in the dry season**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Forage crops are grown for —
a) timber  b) their edible vegetative parts  c) flowers only
**Answer: b) their edible vegetative parts**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Legume forages give —
a) more bulk, less protein  b) less bulk, more protein and less fibre  c) no feed value
**Answer: b) less bulk, more protein and less fibre**
