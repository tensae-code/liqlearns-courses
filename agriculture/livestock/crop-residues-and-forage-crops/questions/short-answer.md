# Crop residues and forage crops — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** When do residues matter most and why?
**Answer: In the dry season — pasture stops growing and stored residues fill the gap.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Contrast grass and legume forage.
**Answer: Grasses: cheaper bulk, more yield per area. Legumes: less bulk, more protein, less fibre.**
