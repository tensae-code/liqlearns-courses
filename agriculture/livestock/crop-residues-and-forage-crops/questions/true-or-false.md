# Crop residues and forage crops — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Crop residues supplement pasture.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Stovers are the grain itself.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Rhodes grass and Sudan grass are forage grasses.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Forage can only be fed fresh, never preserved.
**Answer: False**
