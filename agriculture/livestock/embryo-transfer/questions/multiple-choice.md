# Embryo transfer — many calves from one best cow — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** ET multiplies the genetics of a superior —
a) bull only  b) cow  c) chicken
**Answer: b) cow**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Embryos are flushed about —
a) one day later  b) seven days later  c) a year later
**Answer: b) seven days later**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Good embryos per cow average about —
a) sixty  b) six  c) none
**Answer: b) six**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Embryos are evaluated under a —
a) telescope  b) microscope  c) stethoscope
**Answer: b) microscope**
