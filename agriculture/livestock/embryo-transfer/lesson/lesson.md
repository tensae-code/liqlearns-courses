# Embryo transfer — many calves from one best cow

## Do it first
AI spreads a best bull. Can a best COW be spread the same way — ten calves a year instead of one? She cannot carry them all. But others could carry for her. How? Walk the idea before the steps.

## Sub lesson 1 — Multiplying the best cow
**ET multiplies a superior cow's genetics**: a **donor cow is treated to produce many eggs**, then **inseminated**, and **about seven days later a veterinarian flushes the embryos out with a catheter and recovery fluid through a filter**.

[Picture 26015 — the ET sequence — donor cow, flushing, microscope check, recipient cow]

## Sub lesson 2 — Checking, transferring, storing
The **embryos — about six good ones per cow on average — are evaluated under a microscope** and can be **transferred to recipient cows or stored**. Pupils compare AI and ET: AI spreads the male side widely; ET multiplies the female side — one best cow mothers a herd through other cows' wombs.
