# Lesson plan — Embryo transfer — many calves from one best cow

**Course:** Agriculture · **Title:** Livestock · **Lesson:** Embryo transfer — many calves from one best cow
**Place in the curriculum:** Agriculture — the Livestock title (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** embryo transfer: superovulation, insemination, flushing at about seven days, about six good embryos per cow on average, microscope evaluation, transfer to recipients or storage
**This lesson's own level:** Grade 11 depth — it stands on the learner's own farm and fields, and on the course's earlier titles where named below.

## Sub lessons
- Sub lesson 1 — Multiplying the best cow: **ET multiplies a superior cow's genetics**: a **donor cow is treated to produce many eggs**, then **inseminated**, and **about seven days later a veterinarian flushes the embryos out with a catheter and recovery fluid through a filter**.
- Sub lesson 2 — Checking, transferring, storing: The **embryos — about six good ones per cow on average — are evaluated under a microscope** and can be **transferred to recipient cows or stored**.
Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Artificial insemination — how and why. Recommended, never forced.
