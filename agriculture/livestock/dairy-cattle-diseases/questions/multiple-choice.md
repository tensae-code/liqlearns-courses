# Dairy cattle diseases — the control table — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The dairy disease table gives, for each disease —
a) symptoms, transmission and prevention/control  b) only its name  c) its price
**Answer: a) symptoms, transmission and prevention/control**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Chronic respiratory disease is caused by a —
a) virus  b) mineral lack  c) worm
**Answer: a) virus**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Chronic respiratory disease spreads through —
a) clean air only  b) contaminated feed, water and bedding  c) milking alone
**Answer: b) contaminated feed, water and bedding**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Mastitis is an infection of the —
a) hoof  b) udder  c) eye
**Answer: b) udder**
