# Dairy cattle diseases — the control table — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Work chronic respiratory disease through the table's three columns.
**Answer: Symptoms: swollen face, nasal discharge, difficulty breathing. Transmission: contaminated feed, water and bedding. Prevention/control: vaccination, sanitation and ventilation.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why is mastitis the dairy-specific threat?
**Answer: It infects the udder — the organ the whole dairy enterprise sells from — so it strikes milk yield and quality directly.**
