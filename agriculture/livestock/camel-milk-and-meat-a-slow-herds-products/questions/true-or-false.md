# Camel milk and meat — a slow herd's products — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Camel reproduction is slow compared with other stock.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A 30% meat offtake is safe for camel populations.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Camel milk production and processing are part of this section.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Camel meat markets are strong everywhere in Eastern Africa.
**Answer: False**
