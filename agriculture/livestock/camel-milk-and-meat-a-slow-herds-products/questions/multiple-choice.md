# Camel milk and meat — a slow herd's products — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Camel meat markets are well developed in Eastern Africa mainly in —
a) Sudan  b) every country equally  c) no country
**Answer: a) Sudan**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Export opportunities for camel meat are encouraging in —
a) Japan  b) Brazil  c) Egypt and Libya
**Answer: c) Egypt and Libya**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Camels are inefficient meat producers because of their —
a) colour  b) low reproductive rate  c) size
**Answer: b) low reproductive rate**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** An offtake of just — might already stress the population
a) 3–5%  b) 30–50%  c) 90%
**Answer: a) 3–5%**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The product that pays year-round is —
a) camel meat  b) camel hides  c) camel milk
**Answer: c) camel milk**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The wise camel economy slaughters —
a) never under any custom  b) rarely, inside what the population can bear  c) as many as possible
**Answer: b) rarely, inside what the population can bear**
