# Camel milk and meat — a slow herd's products — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** State the hard limit on camel meat production.
**Answer: Camels reproduce slowly, so they are inefficient meat producers; an offtake of just 3–5% might already stress the population.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does milk lead the camel economy?
**Answer: Because milk production and processing pay year-round from the living herd, while meat offtake is capped by the slow reproduction rate.**
