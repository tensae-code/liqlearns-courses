# Feeding farmed fish — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Fish feed is matched to —
a) the stage of growth  b) the colour of the pond  c) the day of the week
**Answer: a) the stage of growth**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Young fish and table-size fish —
a) must eat identical feed  b) eat only plants  c) eat stage-matched feeds
**Answer: c) eat stage-matched feeds**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Feeding fish well means —
a) feeding once a month  b) watching the stock's response, not just scattering  c) always doubling the ration
**Answer: b) watching the stock's response, not just scattering**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Feed is given in —
a) ponds and cages  b) dry troughs  c) nests
**Answer: a) ponds and cages**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Uneaten feed becomes —
a) extra fish  b) cleaner water  c) wasted money and pond pollution
**Answer: c) wasted money and pond pollution**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Amounts and timing are fitted to —
a) the weather alone  b) the stock  c) the seller's habit
**Answer: b) the stock**
