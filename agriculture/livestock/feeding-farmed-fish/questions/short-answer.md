# Feeding farmed fish — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How does fish feeding resemble poultry feeding in principle?
**Answer: Both match feed to the animal's stage, because the body's work changes as it grows.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why is uneaten feed a double loss?
**Answer: It is money spent that made no growth, and it pollutes the pond water the fish live in.**
