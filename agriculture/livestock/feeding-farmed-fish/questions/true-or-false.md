# Feeding farmed fish — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Farmed fish eat different feeds at different stages.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Overfeeding a pond is harmless.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Feeding practice includes how feed is given, not only what.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Fish in cages cannot be fed by hand practices.
**Answer: False**
