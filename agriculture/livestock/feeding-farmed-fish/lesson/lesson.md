# Feeding farmed fish

## Do it first
You cannot see a pond fish's stomach. Scatter feed and watch: the surface tells you what the fish below are doing. Feeding fish is reading water.

## Sub lesson 1 — What farmed fish eat, stage by stage
Fish **feeds and feeding practices**: what farmed fish eat **at each stage** of growth — the young fish's feed is not the table-size fish's feed. Stage-matched feeding, as with poultry, follows the body's changing work.

[Picture 26650 — feed pellets scattering over a pond surface as fish rise]

## Sub lesson 2 — How feed is given — ponds and cages
Feed is given **in ponds and cages** by the practices of the section: amounts and timing fitted to the stock, so feed becomes fish growth instead of pond pollution. Uneaten feed is money dissolved — feeding well is watching, not just scattering.
