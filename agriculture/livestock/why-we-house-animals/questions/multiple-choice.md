# Why we house animals — comfort and welfare — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In a bad house, good feeding and breeding give —
a) maximum production  b) less than maximum production  c) double
**Answer: b) less than maximum production**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A good house makes which work easier?
a) milking and cleaning  b) mining  c) weaving
**Answer: a) milking and cleaning**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Welfare requires the animal to be —
a) fearful  b) free of pain, fear and distress  c) always working
**Answer: b) free of pain, fear and distress**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Welfare requires —
a) no veterinary care  b) disease prevention and veterinary treatment  c) crowding
**Answer: b) disease prevention and veterinary treatment**
