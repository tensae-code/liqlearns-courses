# Why we house animals — comfort and welfare — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does a bad house waste good breeding?
**Answer: Feeding and breeding cannot give maximum production in a bad house — the environment caps the payoff.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give four parts of the welfare definition.
**Answer: Any four of: healthy, comfortable, well nourished, safe, able to express innate behaviour, free of pain/fear/distress.**
