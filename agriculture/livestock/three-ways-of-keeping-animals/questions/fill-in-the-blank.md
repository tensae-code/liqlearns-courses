# Three ways of keeping animals — extensive, semi-intensive, intensive — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Keeping animals on large low-productivity grazing areas is called ___ production.
**Answer: extensive**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Forage cut and carried to housed animals is called ___ -and-carry feeding.
**Answer: cut**
