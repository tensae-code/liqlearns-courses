# Three ways of keeping animals — extensive, semi-intensive, intensive — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Production systems are classified by —
a) animal colour  b) how much land, labour and bought feed they use  c) country
**Answer: b) how much land, labour and bought feed they use**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Ranching belongs to which family?
a) intensive  b) extensive — its modern, fenced, profit-oriented form  c) semi-intensive
**Answer: b) extensive — its modern, fenced, profit-oriented form**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Intensive keeping feeds animals —
a) only open pasture  b) cut-and-carry forage, by-products and balanced rations with supplements  c) nothing in the dry season
**Answer: b) cut-and-carry forage, by-products and balanced rations with supplements**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is an intensive form?
a) pastoralism  b) commercial dairy production  c) open-range herding
**Answer: b) commercial dairy production**
