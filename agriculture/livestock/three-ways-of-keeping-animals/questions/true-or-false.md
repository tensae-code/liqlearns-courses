# Three ways of keeping animals — extensive, semi-intensive, intensive — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Extensive keeping is low input, slow growth and late maturity, but cheap.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Semi-intensive means fully housed with no grazing.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Intensive systems give meat, milk and eggs at the highest rate.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Feedlot production is an extensive system.
**Answer: False**
