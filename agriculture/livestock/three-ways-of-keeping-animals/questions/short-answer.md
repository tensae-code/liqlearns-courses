# Three ways of keeping animals — extensive, semi-intensive, intensive — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How does semi-intensive differ from extensive and intensive?
**Answer: Semi-intensive is the middle way — part grazing, part housed feeding — between extensive open grazing and intensive fully housed, hand-fed keeping.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does an intensive ration add beyond forage?
**Answer: Industrial by-products and balanced rations supplemented with protein, minerals and vitamins.**
