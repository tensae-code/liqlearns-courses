# Hands-on — Three ways of keeping animals — extensive, semi-intensive, intensive

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Ladder survey
Interview or observe three keepers using different systems. Record land, labour and bought feed for each and place them on the ladder.

## Cut-and-carry trial
Cut and carry forage to a penned animal for a day, weighing what you bring. Write what intensive feeding demands in labour — the ladder's cost in your own arms.
