# Video script — Three ways of keeping animals — extensive, semi-intensive, intensive

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Three ways to keep animals.
Extensive: wide land, low input, slow — but cheap.
Semi-intensive: half grazing, half housed.
Intensive: housed and hand-fed — the highest rate of all.
