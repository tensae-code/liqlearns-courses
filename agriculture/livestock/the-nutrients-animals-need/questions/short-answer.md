# The nutrients animals need — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give three vitamin deficiency signs.
**Answer: Any three of: rough coat, coughing, watery eyes, diarrhoea, staggering, scaly skin.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why must minerals be measured carefully?
**Answer: Deficiency causes major metabolic disorders; excess causes toxicity.**
