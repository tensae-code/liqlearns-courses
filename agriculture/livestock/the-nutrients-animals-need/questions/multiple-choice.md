# The nutrients animals need — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The main energy source is —
a) vitamins  b) carbohydrates  c) water
**Answer: b) carbohydrates**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Carbohydrates come from —
a) grains, residues, molasses, forages  b) limestone only  c) salt
**Answer: a) grains, residues, molasses, forages**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Too much mineral causes —
a) nothing  b) toxicity  c) extra legs
**Answer: b) toxicity**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is a vitamin deficiency sign?
a) staggering and scaly skin  b) faster growth  c) glossier coat
**Answer: a) staggering and scaly skin**
