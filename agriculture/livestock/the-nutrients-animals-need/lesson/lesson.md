# The nutrients animals need

## Do it first
An animal eats plenty yet its coat roughens, eyes water, it staggers. Full belly, failing body — something tiny is missing. Which small things can bring a big animal down? Guess, then learn the families.

## Sub lesson 1 — Energy, body and storage nutrients
**Carbohydrates are the main energy source** — from **grains, residues, molasses and forages**. **Fats** give **stored energy and insulation**, needed in **small amounts**. **Proteins** build and repair the body. Together they are the ration's big three, weighed in kilograms.

## Sub lesson 2 — Minerals, vitamins and water — small but decisive
**Minerals** are a **small share of the diet, but deficiency causes major metabolic disorders and excess causes toxicity**; sources include **bone meal, limestone, common and bole salt and mineral licks**. **Vitamins**, needed in **traces**, serve metabolism, growth, reproduction, blood, bone and stress resistance — deficiency shows as **rough coat, coughing, watery eyes, diarrhoea, staggering and scaly skin**. **Water** completes the families: no nutrient works without it.
