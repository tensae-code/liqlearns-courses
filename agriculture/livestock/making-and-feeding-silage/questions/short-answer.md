# Making and feeding silage — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give the making steps in order.
**Answer: Harvest at flowering (35–40% DM), wilt to ~30% DM, chop to pack well, pack and seal airtight.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name three animals/states excluded from silage.
**Answer: Late-pregnant cows; calves under six months; sick, hungry and weak animals.**
