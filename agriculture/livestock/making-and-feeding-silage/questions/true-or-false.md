# Making and feeding silage — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Over-wilting beyond the target is good practice.
**Answer: False**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Grass–legume mixtures make very good silage.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Silage is safe on an empty rumen.
**Answer: False**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Late-pregnant cows are excluded from silage in the book's rules.
**Answer: True**
