# Making and feeding silage — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Silage is preserved by —
a) drying in sun  b) controlled fermentation without air  c) salting
**Answer: b) controlled fermentation without air**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Harvest for silage at —
a) seed stage  b) flowering stage, 35–40% dry matter  c) any time
**Answer: b) flowering stage, 35–40% dry matter**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Silage may be fed to —
a) all animals freely  b) ruminants only, carefully  c) calves under six months first
**Answer: b) ruminants only, carefully**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** First silage meals should —
a) be huge  b) start a bit at a time and increase gradually  c) replace water
**Answer: b) start a bit at a time and increase gradually**
