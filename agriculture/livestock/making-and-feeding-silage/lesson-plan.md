# Lesson plan — Making and feeding silage

**Course:** Agriculture · **Title:** Livestock · **Lesson:** Making and feeding silage
**Place in the curriculum:** Agriculture — the Livestock title (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** silage making step by step — harvest at flowering (35–40% DM), wilt to about 30% DM, chop, pack and seal airtight — and the feeding rules: ruminants only, never on an empty rumen, not for late-pregnant cows, calves under six months or sick/weak animals, start small
**This lesson's own level:** Grade 11 depth — it stands on the learner's own farm and fields, and on the course's earlier titles where named below.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
- Sub lesson 1 — Making silage — the airtight method: **Silage is green crop preserved by controlled fermentation without air (ensilage)**, keeping succulent feed for scarce times; **grasses and grass–legume mixtures make very good silage**.
- Sub lesson 2 — Feeding silage — the rules: Feed carefully: **to ruminants only**, **never on an empty rumen**, **not to late-pregnant cows, calves under six months, or sick, hungry and weak animals**.
Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Making hay. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Define silage: green crop preserved by controlled fermentation without air.
2. Give the making steps with numbers: flowering harvest at 35–40% DM, wilt to about 30% DM, chop, pack, seal airtight.
3. State the feeding rules exactly: ruminants only, never empty rumen, exclusions, start small and increase gradually.

## What you need
A notebook; a visit view of a silo if available. Observe only — packing work is adult work with protective clothing.
