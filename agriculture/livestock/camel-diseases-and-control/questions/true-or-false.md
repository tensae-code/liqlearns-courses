# Camel diseases and control — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Knowing disease signs helps a keeper act early.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A sick camel can always be left at a clinic.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Separation can be part of camel disease control.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Keepers need no disease knowledge if a vet exists somewhere.
**Answer: False**
