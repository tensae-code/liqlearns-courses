# Camel diseases and control — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The section names —
a) the major diseases of camels  b) only skin problems  c) human diseases
**Answer: a) the major diseases of camels**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Recognition of signs is —
a) useless  b) the vet's job only  c) the first control
**Answer: c) the first control**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Pastoral herds may be —
a) stationary  b) days from a town and its clinic  c) always beside a hospital
**Answer: b) days from a town and its clinic**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Control methods include —
a) early treatment and separation where possible  b) ignoring sickness  c) driving sick animals hardest
**Answer: a) early treatment and separation where possible**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** In camel country, the first veterinary worker is —
a) the buyer  b) the tourist  c) the keeper
**Answer: c) the keeper**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Hygiene at camp is —
a) only for cattle  b) part of control  c) irrelevant on the move
**Answer: b) part of control**
