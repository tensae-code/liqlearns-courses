# Camel diseases and control — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why is the keeper the key figure in camel disease control?
**Answer: Because pastoral herds travel far from towns and clinics; the keeper must recognise signs and begin control — treatment, separation, hygiene — on the spot.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name three control actions from the lesson.
**Answer: Early treatment; separation of the sick where possible; hygiene at camp (and veterinary help when reachable).**
