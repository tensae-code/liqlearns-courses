# Camel diseases and control

## Do it first
A sick camel in a moving herd cannot be left at a clinic. The herd walks on. How does disease control work where the patients travel?

## Sub lesson 1 — The major camel diseases
The section names the **major diseases of camels** — the sicknesses pastoral keepers must know by their signs, in animals that may be days from a town. Recognition is the first control: a keeper who knows the signs acts while action still helps.

[Picture 26639 — a camel being examined by a veterinary worker in a pastoral camp]

## Sub lesson 2 — Methods of control
Against each disease stand its **methods of control** — the pastoral defences: early treatment, separation where possible, hygiene at camp and veterinary help when reachable. In camel country, the keeper is the first veterinary worker; the section arms that keeper with knowledge.
