Camel diseases and control.
The section names the major diseases of camels — the sicknesses pastoral keepers must know by their signs, in animals that may be days from a town.
Against each disease stand its methods of control — the pastoral defences: early treatment, separation where possible, hygiene at camp and veterinary help when reachable.
