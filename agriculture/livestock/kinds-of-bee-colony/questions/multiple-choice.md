# Kinds of bee colony — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Colony types are —
a) forms and conditions of colonies  b) hive colours  c) honey flavours
**Answer: a) forms and conditions of colonies**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A colony's state is read before —
a) selling it  b) moving it  c) managing it
**Answer: c) managing it**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Colony kinds are recognised from —
a) the owner's word  b) bee numbers, brood, stores and behaviour  c) the hive's age only
**Answer: b) bee numbers, brood, stores and behaviour**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A boiling-full hive suggests —
a) a strong colony  b) a failing colony  c) an empty hive
**Answer: a) a strong colony**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Naming the colony's kind is —
a) a naming game  b) the harvest  c) the diagnosis management follows
**Answer: c) the diagnosis management follows**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The lesson's skill is —
a) strength  b) the beekeeper's eye  c) speed
**Answer: b) the beekeeper's eye**
