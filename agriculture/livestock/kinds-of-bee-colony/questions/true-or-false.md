# Kinds of bee colony — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Colonies differ in form and condition.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** All colonies should be managed identically.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Brood and stores help judge a colony.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Recognition can come after management.
**Answer: False**
