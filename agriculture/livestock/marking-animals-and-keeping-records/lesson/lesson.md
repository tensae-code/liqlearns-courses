# Marking animals and keeping herd records

## Do it first
Thirty brown cows, one farmer's memory. Which cow's mother milked best? Which should be culled? Memory guesses; records answer. But a record needs a name that stays on the animal. How many ways can an animal carry its name?

## Sub lesson 1 — Records — the manager's eyes
**Performance records are one of the best management tools for genetic improvement**: they **expose weak areas of herd performance** and **guide flock management, selection of breeding stock and culling**. Without records, selection is guessing — with them, every earlier lesson in this unit can be done on evidence.

## Sub lesson 2 — Marking — permanent and temporary
**Identification methods divide into permanent — branding, tattooing, ear notching — and temporary — tail marking and similar.** Permanent marks carry the animal's identity for life, so its record follows it; temporary marks serve a season or a task.
