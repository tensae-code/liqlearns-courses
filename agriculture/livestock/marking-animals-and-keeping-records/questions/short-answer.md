# Marking animals and keeping herd records — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two jobs records do.
**Answer: Expose weak performance areas; guide selection/culling (also flock management).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Sort: branding, tail marking, tattooing.
**Answer: Permanent: branding, tattooing. Temporary: tail marking.**
