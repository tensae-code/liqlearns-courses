# Marking animals and keeping herd records — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Performance records are —
a) useless paper  b) one of the best tools for genetic improvement  c) feed
**Answer: b) one of the best tools for genetic improvement**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Records guide —
a) selection of breeding stock and culling  b) the weather  c) market prices only
**Answer: a) selection of breeding stock and culling**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is a permanent identification?
a) tail marking  b) ear notching  c) chalk dust
**Answer: b) ear notching**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Tail marking is —
a) permanent  b) temporary  c) surgical
**Answer: b) temporary**
