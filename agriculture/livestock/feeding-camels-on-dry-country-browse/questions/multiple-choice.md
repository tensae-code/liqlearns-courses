# Feeding camels on dry-country browse — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Camels feed mainly by —
a) browsing thorny, salty dry-country forage  b) grazing lawns  c) eating grain only
**Answer: a) browsing thorny, salty dry-country forage**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The camel can use plants that —
a) grow in rivers  b) are poisonous to all  c) other livestock cannot use
**Answer: c) other livestock cannot use**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A camel's feed base is —
a) milk  b) the land itself, not a trough  c) imported feed
**Answer: b) the land itself, not a trough**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The quoted daily gain is —
a) 1.4 kg  b) 14 kg  c) 0.14 kg
**Answer: a) 1.4 kg**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Camels are managed as animals built to —
a) drink hourly  b) never drink  c) go long between drinks
**Answer: c) go long between drinks**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Feeding rhythms follow —
a) market days only  b) the pastoral round  c) the school timetable
**Answer: b) the pastoral round**
