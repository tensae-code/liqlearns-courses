# Feeding camels on dry-country browse — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does a camel browse, and why can it use it?
**Answer: Thorny, salty dry-country forage; its mouth, neck and digestion are built for plants other livestock cannot use.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How do water rhythms differ for camels?
**Answer: They are built to go long between drinks, so watering follows the pastoral round rather than daily farmyard routines.**
