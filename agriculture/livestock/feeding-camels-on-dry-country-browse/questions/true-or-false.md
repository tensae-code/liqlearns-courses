# Feeding camels on dry-country browse — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Thorny and salty plants are part of the camel's menu.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Camels need the same trough feeding as stall cattle.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A daily gain of 1.4 kg is quoted for camels.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Camels must drink as often as dairy cows.
**Answer: False**
