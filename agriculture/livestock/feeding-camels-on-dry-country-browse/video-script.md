Feeding camels on dry-country browse.
Camel feeding is browsing: the thorny, salty forage of dry country, taken high and low as the herd moves.
The unit's feeding figures quote a daily gain of 1.4 kg in the growth discussion — growth is possible on browse when it is managed.
Do it yourself.
Then say it to your companion.
