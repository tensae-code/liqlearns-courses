Camels — dromedary and Bactrian.
The dromedary (Arabian, one-humped) camel is larger than the Bactrian, with a short, fine, sparsely distributed coat, lean tissue, longer limbs and broad feet, important to the hotter areas of Africa.
Against it stands the wild Bactrian camel (*Camelus ferus*) — the two-humped camel of colder Asia.
