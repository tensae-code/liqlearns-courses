# Camels — dromedary and Bactrian

## Do it first
One hump or two? That single question splits the camel world. Look closer: feet, coat, nostrils — every part answers the desert.

## Sub lesson 1 — The dromedary — built for hot Africa
The **dromedary (Arabian, one-humped) camel** is larger than the Bactrian, with a **short, fine, sparsely distributed coat, lean tissue, longer limbs and broad feet**, important to the hotter areas of Africa. Its **trapdoor nostrils keep sandstorms out of the nasal cavity** — desert engineering written into the animal.

## Sub lesson 2 — The Bactrian, and selection
Against it stands the **wild Bactrian camel (*Camelus ferus*)** — the two-humped camel of colder Asia. **Selection** follows the breed description: knowing which camel, and which animal within it, fits the keeper's country and work.
