# Camels — dromedary and Bactrian — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** List four dromedary features from the description.
**Answer: Any four of: larger than the Bactrian; short, fine, sparse coat; lean tissue; longer limbs; broad feet; trapdoor nostrils.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Contrast the two camels in one sentence.
**Answer: The dromedary is the larger, one-humped camel of hot Africa; the Bactrian is the two-humped camel (the wild form Camelus ferus) of colder Asia.**
