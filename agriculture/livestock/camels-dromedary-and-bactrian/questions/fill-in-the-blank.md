# Camels — dromedary and Bactrian — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The one-humped camel is the ___.
**Answer: dromedary**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Its nostrils are ___ nostrils that shut out sand.
**Answer: trapdoor**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The wild Bactrian is Camelus ___.
**Answer: ferus**
