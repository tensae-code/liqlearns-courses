# Camels — dromedary and Bactrian — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The dromedary has —
a) one hump  b) two humps  c) no hump
**Answer: a) one hump**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The dromedary is —
a) smaller than the Bactrian  b) the same animal  c) larger than the Bactrian
**Answer: c) larger than the Bactrian**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Trapdoor nostrils —
a) store water  b) keep sandstorms out of the nasal cavity  c) cool the blood
**Answer: b) keep sandstorms out of the nasal cavity**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The wild Bactrian's scientific name is —
a) Camelus ferus  b) Oreochromis niloticus  c) Clarias gariepinus
**Answer: a) Camelus ferus**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The dromedary's coat is —
a) long and thick  b) absent  c) short, fine and sparsely distributed
**Answer: c) short, fine and sparsely distributed**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The dromedary matters most to —
a) wetlands  b) the hotter areas of Africa  c) cold Asia
**Answer: b) the hotter areas of Africa**
