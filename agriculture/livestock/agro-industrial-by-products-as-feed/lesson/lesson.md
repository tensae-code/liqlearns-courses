# Agro-industrial by-products as feed

## Do it first
A flour mill sweeps up bran. An oil press squeezes out cake. A sugar factory drains molasses. Rubbish — or the richest feed on the farm? Sort the three by what you think each gives an animal.

## Sub lesson 1 — What industry leaves behind
**Agro-industrial by-products supplement residues** and are **rich in energy or protein**. **Flour-milling by-products are the brans** — the coats of grain, useful feed. Nothing a factory discards should be judged before its feed value is asked.

## Sub lesson 2 — Cakes, molasses and brewery grains
**Oilseed cakes**, left after oil extraction — **soybean, noug, cottonseed, peanut/groundnut cakes** — are **high in protein, carbohydrate, minerals and nitrogen**. **Molasses** from sugar-making carries **about 54% energy value**. **Brewery by-products** complete the set: industry by-products turn waste cost into animal gain.
