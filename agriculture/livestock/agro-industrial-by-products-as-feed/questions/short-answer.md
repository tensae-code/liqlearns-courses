# Agro-industrial by-products as feed — short answer

Answer in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name four oilseed cakes.
**Answer: Soybean, noug, cottonseed and peanut/groundnut cakes.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why are by-products valuable?
**Answer: They are rich in energy or protein and supplement plain residues cheaply.**
