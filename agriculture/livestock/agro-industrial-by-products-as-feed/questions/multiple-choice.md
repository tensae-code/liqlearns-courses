# Agro-industrial by-products as feed — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** By-products are fed to —
a) replace all water  b) supplement residues, rich in energy or protein  c) poison pests
**Answer: b) supplement residues, rich in energy or protein**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Brans come from —
a) breweries  b) flour milling  c) sugar factories
**Answer: b) flour milling**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Oilseed cakes are high in —
a) protein, carbohydrate, minerals and nitrogen  b) sand  c) water only
**Answer: a) protein, carbohydrate, minerals and nitrogen**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Molasses has about what energy value?
a) about 5%  b) about 54%  c) about 100%
**Answer: b) about 54%**
