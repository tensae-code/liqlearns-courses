# Indigenous beekeeping knowledge

## Do it first
In the forest, a log hive hangs high in a tree where your grandfather's way put it. Before the unit closes, it stops to listen to that way — as it has at the end of every enterprise.

## Sub lesson 1 — Traditional knowledge closes the unit
**Traditional beekeeping knowledge** closes the unit — the **forest and log-hive practices of Ethiopian beekeepers** as **working knowledge worth recording**, matching the traditional system of the unit's first lesson.

## Sub lesson 2 — What the old way knows
The old way knows its forests, its bee seasons and its harvest craft — knowledge carried in practice, not in books. Recording it preserves technique, teaches the principles inside the customs, and keeps the modern beekeeper humble: the frame hive was built on centuries of log-hive wisdom.
