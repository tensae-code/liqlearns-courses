# Indigenous beekeeping knowledge — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The unit closes with —
a) traditional beekeeping knowledge  b) a frame catalogue  c) export rules
**Answer: a) traditional beekeeping knowledge**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The knowledge lives in —
a) laboratories  b) imported books  c) forest and log-hive practices
**Answer: c) forest and log-hive practices**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** It is recorded as —
a) tourist stories  b) working knowledge  c) obsolete custom
**Answer: b) working knowledge**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Traditional practice matches —
a) the traditional system of the unit's first lesson  b) the modern frame system  c) the transitional system only
**Answer: a) the traditional system of the unit's first lesson**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The old way knows —
a) nothing of bees  b) only prices  c) its forests, bee seasons and harvest craft
**Answer: c) its forests, bee seasons and harvest craft**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Recording it teaches —
a) how to ban log hives  b) the principles inside the customs  c) disrespect for elders
**Answer: b) the principles inside the customs**
