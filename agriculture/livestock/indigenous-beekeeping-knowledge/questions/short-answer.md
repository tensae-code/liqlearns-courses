# Indigenous beekeeping knowledge — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What knowledge does this lesson record?
**Answer: The forest and log-hive practices of Ethiopian beekeepers — their traditional beekeeping knowledge.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why record it, in two reasons?
**Answer: It preserves proven technique, and it teaches the principles inside the customs for new beekeepers.**
