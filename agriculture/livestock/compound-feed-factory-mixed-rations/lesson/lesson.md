# Compound feed — factory-mixed rations

## Do it first
One sack, every bite the same — energy, protein, minerals all inside. A factory made that sameness on purpose. Why would a farmer buy sameness instead of mixing at home? Decide before the lesson answers.

## Sub lesson 1 — One mixture, one target species
**Commercial compound feed blends several raw ingredients of different composition into one homogeneous mixture meeting the target species' requirements.** Homogeneous matters: every mouthful carries the full balance, not a lucky scoop.

[Picture 26009 — ingredient piles entering a mixer and one uniform feed coming out]

## Sub lesson 2 — Ethiopia's compound ingredients
In Ethiopia the **main ingredients are cereals and agro-industrial by-products**, with **salt and limestone among the additions** — the mineral family entering the mix by measure, not by chance.
