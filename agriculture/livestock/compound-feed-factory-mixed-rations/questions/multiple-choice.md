# Compound feed — factory-mixed rations — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Compound feed is —
a) one raw grain  b) a homogeneous blend of several ingredients  c) hay only
**Answer: b) a homogeneous blend of several ingredients**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Homogeneous means —
a) different every scoop  b) the same throughout the mixture  c) wet
**Answer: b) the same throughout the mixture**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Ethiopia's main compound ingredients are —
a) cereals and agro-industrial by-products  b) fish only  c) imported vitamins only
**Answer: a) cereals and agro-industrial by-products**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Additions include —
a) salt and limestone  b) sand and gravel  c) ashes
**Answer: a) salt and limestone**
