# Lesson plan — The lactation cycle — 305 days and a dry period

**Course:** Agriculture · **Title:** Livestock · **Lesson:** The lactation cycle — 305 days and a dry period
**Place in the curriculum:** Agriculture — the Livestock title (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the lactation cycle — about 305 days of milk followed by about two dry months, colostrum first, yield rising then declining along the lactation curve
**This lesson's own level:** Grade 11 depth — it stands on the learner's own farm and fields, and on the course's earlier titles where named below.

## Sub lessons
- Sub lesson 1 — 305 days of milk, two months dry: A **normal lactation lasts about 10 months — 305 days** — followed by about **two dry months**, the **drying off** period when milking stops and the cow rests and prepares for the next calf.
- Sub lesson 2 — The lactation curve: **Yield rises in the first months after calving**, reaches its height, then **declines steadily through late lactation** — the shape called the **lactation curve**.
Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Feeding dairy cattle. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the cycle: a normal lactation lasts about 10 months (305 days), followed by about two dry months.
2. Say what colostrum is and why it matters to the calf.
3. Read the lactation curve: yield rises in the first months after calving, then declines steadily.
