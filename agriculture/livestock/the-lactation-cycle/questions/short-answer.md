# The lactation cycle — 305 days and a dry period — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Describe the lactation curve in one sentence.
**Answer: Yield rises in the first months after calving, then declines steadily through late lactation.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does the cycle include dry months?
**Answer: Drying off rests the cow and lets her prepare for the next calf and lactation.**
