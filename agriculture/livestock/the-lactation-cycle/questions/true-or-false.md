# The lactation cycle — 305 days and a dry period — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Drying off means milking stops for a rest period of about two months.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Colostrum is ordinary milk with a different name.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Milk carries calcium, iodine, potassium and phosphorus.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Feeding and breeding are planned without reference to the curve.
**Answer: False**
