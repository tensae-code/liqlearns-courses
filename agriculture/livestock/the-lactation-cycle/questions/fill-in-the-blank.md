# The lactation cycle — 305 days and a dry period — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A normal lactation is ___ days, followed by about ___ dry months.
**Answer: 305; two**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The first milk after calving, rich for the calf, is called ___.
**Answer: colostrum**
