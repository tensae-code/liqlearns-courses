# The lactation cycle — 305 days and a dry period — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A normal lactation lasts about —
a) 100 days  b) 305 days (about 10 months)  c) 3 years
**Answer: b) 305 days (about 10 months)**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The first milk after calving is —
a) ghee  b) colostrum, specially rich for the calf  c) skim milk
**Answer: b) colostrum, specially rich for the calf**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** On the lactation curve, yield —
a) falls, then rises  b) rises in the first months, then declines steadily  c) never changes
**Answer: b) rises in the first months, then declines steadily**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The dry period lasts about —
a) two months  b) two days  c) a year
**Answer: a) two months**
