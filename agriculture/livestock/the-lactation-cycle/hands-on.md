# Hands-on — The lactation cycle — 305 days and a dry period

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Curve in string
Lay a string on a table in the lactation curve's shape across a 12-month ruler. Pin labels: calving, peak, drying off, next calving.

## Calf-first plan
Write the first three days after a calving as a plan: what the calf gets, what the farmer checks, when saleable milk begins.
