# Lesson plan — Artificial insemination — how and why

**Course:** Agriculture · **Title:** Livestock · **Lesson:** Artificial insemination — how and why
**Place in the curriculum:** Agriculture — the Livestock title (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** AI as a skilled procedure — heat signs (stands to be mounted, mounts others), semen collection/storage, recto-vaginal insemination — and its five advantages
**This lesson's own level:** Grade 11 depth — it stands on the learner's own farm and fields, and on the course's earlier titles where named below.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
- Sub lesson 1 — Heat and the procedure: **AI is a skilled procedure**: **recognising a cow on heat — she stands to be mounted and mounts others** — **collecting and storing semen**, and **inseminating by the recto-vaginal method, the safe and best route in cattle**.
- Sub lesson 2 — The five advantages: **A wider variety of superior males can be used**; **one superior male can breed far more females**; **spread of infectious diseases such as contagious abortion and vibriosis is reduced**; **no male need be kept on the farm (semen comes from AI centres)**; and **breeding and calving records stay accurate**.
Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: In-breeding and cross-breeding compared. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the heat signs: stands to be mounted, mounts others.
2. Name the procedure route: recto-vaginal, the safe and best route in cattle.
3. List AI advantages: superior males widely used, one male breeds far more females, less disease spread, no male kept, accurate records.

## What you need
A notebook. Procedure is for trained technicians — this lesson teaches understanding, not performing AI.
