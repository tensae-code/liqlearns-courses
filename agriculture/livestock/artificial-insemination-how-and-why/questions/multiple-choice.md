# Artificial insemination — how and why — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A cow on heat —
a) hides and fasts  b) stands to be mounted and mounts others  c) moos once
**Answer: b) stands to be mounted and mounts others**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The safe and best insemination route in cattle is —
a) oral  b) recto-vaginal  c) through the ear
**Answer: b) recto-vaginal**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** AI reduces spread of —
a) rain  b) contagious abortion and vibriosis  c) hay fever in plants
**Answer: b) contagious abortion and vibriosis**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Semen for farms comes from —
a) AI centres  b) the market river  c) wild bulls
**Answer: a) AI centres**
