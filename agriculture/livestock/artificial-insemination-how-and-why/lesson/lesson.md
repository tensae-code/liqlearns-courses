# Artificial insemination — how and why

## Do it first
One champion bull, a thousand farms. Nature allows one farm. How can his calves be born everywhere without moving him anywhere? And how does the farmer know the exact day? Two puzzles — heat signs and a flask hold the answers.

## Sub lesson 1 — Heat and the procedure
**AI is a skilled procedure**: **recognising a cow on heat — she stands to be mounted and mounts others** — **collecting and storing semen**, and **inseminating by the recto-vaginal method, the safe and best route in cattle**. Timing and skill decide success; the work belongs to trained hands.

[Picture 26014 — an AI technician at work with a semen flask from an AI centre]

## Sub lesson 2 — The five advantages
**A wider variety of superior males can be used**; **one superior male can breed far more females**; **spread of infectious diseases such as contagious abortion and vibriosis is reduced**; **no male need be kept on the farm (semen comes from AI centres)**; and **breeding and calving records stay accurate**. AI turns one centre's best bulls into a nation's herd improvement.
