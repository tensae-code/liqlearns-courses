# Enemies of the hive — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three bee diseases listed.
**Answer: Chalk brood, nosematosis and amoeba disease.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** List the pests and predators with their scientific names where given.
**Answer: Small hive beetle (Aethina tumida), bee lice, ants (Dorylus fulvus), wax moth (Galleria mellonella), mice, spiders and snakes.**
