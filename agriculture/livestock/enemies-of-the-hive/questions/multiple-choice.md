# Enemies of the hive — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Chalk brood is —
a) a bee disease  b) a pest beetle  c) a hive type
**Answer: a) a bee disease**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Nosematosis and amoeba disease are —
a) ant species  b) honey grades  c) bee diseases
**Answer: c) bee diseases**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Disease strikes —
a) only honey  b) brood and bees  c) only the hive box
**Answer: b) brood and bees**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The small hive beetle's scientific name is —
a) Aethina tumida  b) Galleria mellonella  c) Dorylus fulvus
**Answer: a) Aethina tumida**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Galleria mellonella is —
a) an ant  b) a spider  c) the wax moth
**Answer: c) the wax moth**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Dorylus fulvus names —
a) mice  b) ants among the hive's enemies  c) bee lice
**Answer: b) ants among the hive's enemies**
