# Enemies of the hive — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The wax moth damages comb.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Mice and snakes are listed among hive predators.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Aethina tumida is the small hive beetle.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Diseases and pests cause different kinds of damage.
**Answer: True**
