Enemies of the hive.
The threats list opens with diseases, including chalk brood, nosematosis and amoeba disease.
Then pests and predators: the small hive beetle (*Aethina tumida*), bee lice, ants (*Dorylus fulvus*), wax moth (*Galleria mellonella*), mice, spiders and snakes.
Do it yourself.
Then say it to your companion.
