# Sheep and goat products — meat and milk

## Do it first
The flock's work ends on a table: meat, and milk in a jug. Everything before this lesson — breeds, feed, housing, health — is priced here.

## Sub lesson 1 — Meat production and processing
The products section closes the enterprise: **meat production and its processing** turn the selected, fed, housed and healthy animals of the previous sections into the first product. Processing decides how much of the animal's value survives to the buyer.

[Picture 26635 — goat milk being processed beside a meat carcass]

## Sub lesson 2 — Milk production and processing
**Milk production and processing** stand beside meat: the daily product against the final one. Together they carry the flock's whole economics — the enterprise's earlier sections exist so that these two products arrive in quantity and quality.
