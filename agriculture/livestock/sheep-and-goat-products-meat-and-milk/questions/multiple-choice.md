# Sheep and goat products — meat and milk — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The products section closes —
a) the sheep and goat enterprise  b) the feeding section  c) the course
**Answer: a) the sheep and goat enterprise**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Meat processing decides —
a) the animal's breed  b) the grazing area  c) how much of the animal's value survives to the buyer
**Answer: c) how much of the animal's value survives to the buyer**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Meat comes from animals that were —
a) imported only  b) selected, fed, housed and kept healthy  c) never managed
**Answer: b) selected, fed, housed and kept healthy**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The two products of the enterprise are —
a) meat and milk  b) wool and horns  c) milk and eggs
**Answer: a) meat and milk**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Milk is the —
a) only product  b) imported product  c) daily product, against meat the final one
**Answer: c) daily product, against meat the final one**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Earlier sections exist so products arrive —
a) unprocessed  b) in quantity and quality  c) late
**Answer: b) in quantity and quality**
