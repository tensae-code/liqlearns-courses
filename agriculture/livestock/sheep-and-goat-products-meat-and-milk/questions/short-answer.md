# Sheep and goat products — meat and milk — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How do the earlier enterprise sections show up in the products?
**Answer: Selection, feeding, housing and health decide the quantity and quality of the meat and milk that the products section handles.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Contrast meat and milk as products.
**Answer: Meat is the final, one-time product of the animal; milk is the daily, repeating product.**
