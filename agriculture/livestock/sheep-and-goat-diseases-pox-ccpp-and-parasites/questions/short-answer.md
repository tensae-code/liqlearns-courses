# Sheep and goat diseases — pox, CCPP and parasites — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the two outbreak diseases heading the section.
**Answer: Sheep and goat pox, and contagious caprine pleuropneumonia (CCPP).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why is control flock-level for sheep and goats?
**Answer: Because the animals live closely together and share feed and infection, so inspecting, separating, treating and preventing must cover the whole flock.**
