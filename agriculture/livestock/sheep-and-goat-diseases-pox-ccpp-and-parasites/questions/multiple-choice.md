# Sheep and goat diseases — pox, CCPP and parasites — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The section heads its list with —
a) serious outbreak diseases  b) rare show diseases  c) human diseases
**Answer: a) serious outbreak diseases**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** CCPP stands for —
a) a feed type  b) a breed name  c) contagious caprine pleuropneumonia
**Answer: c) contagious caprine pleuropneumonia**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Sheep and goat pox is —
a) a grazing plant  b) an outbreak disease of the flock  c) a housing type
**Answer: b) an outbreak disease of the flock**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Control in small stock is —
a) flock-level: inspect, separate, treat, prevent  b) animal-by-animal luck  c) only slaughter
**Answer: a) flock-level: inspect, separate, treat, prevent**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Parasites are covered —
a) nowhere  b) only in cattle  c) alongside the major diseases
**Answer: c) alongside the major diseases**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** A sick goat in the flock should be —
a) ignored  b) separated  c) mixed with lambs
**Answer: b) separated**
