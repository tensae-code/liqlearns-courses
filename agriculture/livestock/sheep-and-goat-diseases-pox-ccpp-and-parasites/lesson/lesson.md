# Sheep and goat diseases — pox, CCPP and parasites

## Do it first
A goat coughs at the back of the flock and another's skin is spotted. In a flock, disease travels on four legs at walking pace — toward every other animal.

## Sub lesson 1 — Serious outbreaks head the list
The section opens with the outbreak diseases — **sheep and goat pox** and **contagious caprine pleuropneumonia (CCPP)** among them — the sicknesses that can sweep a flock. Naming the worst first is triage: know what can empty a pen before it arrives.

[Picture 26634 — a flock being inspected, one sick goat separated]

## Sub lesson 2 — The major diseases, parasites, and control
The **major diseases and parasites** of sheep and goats are worked through with their **control methods**. In small stock, control is flock-level thinking: inspect, separate the sick, treat and prevent — because animals kept closely together share both feed and infection.
