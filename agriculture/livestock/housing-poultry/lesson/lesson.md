# Housing poultry

## Do it first
Design a chicken house in your head. The birds must sleep, lay, eat, drink and be collected from — daily, by you, in the rain. What goes where?

## Sub lesson 1 — A house that protects
Poultry housing in the enterprise pattern: houses fitted to **protect birds** — from weather, predators and theft — because a small bird in the open is every predator's dinner. Housing is the flock's fortress and its first health defence.

[Picture 26643 — a poultry house interior — perches, nests, feeders, drinkers]

## Sub lesson 2 — A house that works
The house must also make **feeding, watering and egg collection workable**: the daily jobs flow through it. Perches, nests, feeders and drinkers are placed so birds live well and the keeper works easily — protection and workflow in one small building.

## Say it to your companion
Explain this lesson to your companion in your own words, using one real example from your own area for each sub lesson. Your companion asks one question about each part — answer from the lesson, not from memory of other lessons.
