# Housing poultry — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Predator protection is a housing job.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Egg collection does not need designing into the house.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Feeders and drinkers are placed for birds and keeper both.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A poultry house is only a sleeping shed.
**Answer: False**
