# Housing poultry — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name housing's two jobs.
**Answer: Protection of the birds (weather, predators, theft) and workable daily flow for feeding, watering and egg collection.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** List the house fittings named in the lesson.
**Answer: Perches, nests, feeders and drinkers.**
