# Housing poultry — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Poultry housing first must —
a) protect birds from weather, predators and theft  b) look decorative  c) be far from the keeper
**Answer: a) protect birds from weather, predators and theft**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A small bird in the open is —
a) perfectly safe  b) a better layer  c) vulnerable to every predator
**Answer: c) vulnerable to every predator**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Housing is the flock's first —
a) market  b) health defence  c) expense to avoid
**Answer: b) health defence**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The house must make which jobs workable?
a) feeding, watering and egg collection  b) only sleeping  c) only guarding
**Answer: a) feeding, watering and egg collection**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Nests in the house are for —
a) storing feed  b) perching all day  c) laying and egg collection
**Answer: c) laying and egg collection**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Good housing combines —
a) paint and signs  b) protection and workflow  c) size and height only
**Answer: b) protection and workflow**
