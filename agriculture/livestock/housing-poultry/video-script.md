# Video script — Housing poultry

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Housing poultry.
Poultry housing in the enterprise pattern: houses fitted to protect birds — from weather, predators and theft — because a small bird in the open is every predator's dinner.
The house must also make feeding, watering and egg collection workable: the daily jobs flow through it.
Do it yourself.
Then say it to your companion.
