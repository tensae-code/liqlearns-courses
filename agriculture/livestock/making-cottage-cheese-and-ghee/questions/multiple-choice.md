# Making cottage cheese and ghee — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The cottage cheese practical starts with —
a) tasting the milk  b) protective clothing  c) selling the cheese
**Answer: b) protective clothing**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which can stand in for rennet or starter culture?
a) lemon juice or acid whey  b) plain water  c) salt alone
**Answer: a) lemon juice or acid whey**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Ghee is made by heating butter to —
a) 40 °C  b) 110-120 °C, until the water evaporates  c) 300 °C
**Answer: b) 110-120 °C, until the water evaporates**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Ghee keeps well because it is —
a) mostly water  b) almost entirely butterfat  c) frozen
**Answer: b) almost entirely butterfat**
