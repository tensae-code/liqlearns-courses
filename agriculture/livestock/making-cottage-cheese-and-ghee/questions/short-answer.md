# Making cottage cheese and ghee — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** List the cottage cheese apparatus.
**Answer: Whole fresh or sour milk and skim milk, muslin cheese sieve, weighing balance, salt, pails, moulder with presser (protective clothing worn).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does ghee store more easily than butter?
**Answer: Its water has been evaporated off; almost entirely butterfat, it keeps without refrigeration.**
