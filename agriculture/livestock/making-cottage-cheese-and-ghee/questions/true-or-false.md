# Making cottage cheese and ghee — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A muslin cheese sieve and a moulder with presser are part of the cheese apparatus.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Rennet is the only thing that can curdle the milk for cottage cheese.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Ghee needs refrigeration to keep.
**Answer: False**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Heating butter drives its water off as the ghee clarifies.
**Answer: True**
