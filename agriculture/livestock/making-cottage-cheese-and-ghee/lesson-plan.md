# Lesson plan — Making cottage cheese and ghee

**Course:** Agriculture · **Title:** Livestock · **Lesson:** Making cottage cheese and ghee
**Place in the curriculum:** Agriculture — the Livestock title (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the dairy practicals — cottage cheese pressed in muslin (with lemon juice or acid standing in for rennet) and ghee clarified by heating butter to 110-120 degrees C until the water evaporates
**This lesson's own level:** Grade 11 depth — it stands on the learner's own farm and fields, and on the course's earlier titles where named below.

## Sub lessons
- Sub lesson 1 — Making cottage cheese: The workshop starts with **protective clothing first** — the hygiene rule worn on the body.
- Sub lesson 2 — Making ghee — butter clarified: **Ghee** begins as butter and one careful heating: butter is **heated to 110-120 °C until the water evaporates**.
Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: From udder to market — milk hygiene and processing. This is a doing-lesson: the hot-pan steps are adult work. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. List the cottage cheese apparatus and the acid stand-ins (lemon juice, lactic or acetic acid, or acid whey) for rennet or starter culture.
2. State the safe order: protective clothing first, then the making steps, pressing in a muslin cheese sieve with a moulder and presser.
3. Give the ghee method exactly: butter heated to 110-120 °C until the water evaporates; ghee is almost entirely butterfat and keeps without refrigeration.

## What you need
For the practical (with an adult): whole fresh or sour milk and skim milk, muslin cheese sieve, weighing balance, salt, pails, moulder with presser, lemon juice; butter and a pan for ghee. Protective clothing.
