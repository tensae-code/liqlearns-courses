# Hands-on — Making cottage cheese and ghee

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Cheese practical (with an adult)
With protective clothing on, coagulate warmed milk with lemon juice, strain and press the curd in muslin in the moulder. Weigh before and after pressing; note what the whey carried away.

## Ghee practical (adult-led)
An adult heats butter to 110-120 °C until bubbling quiets and the fat clears. Jar the ghee when cool enough to handle safely; compare its keeping with butter over the following weeks.
