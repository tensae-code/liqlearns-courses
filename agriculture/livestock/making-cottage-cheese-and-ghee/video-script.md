# Video script — Making cottage cheese and ghee

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Cottage cheese: clean clothes first.
Milk, muslin, salt — lemon juice can curdle it.
Press the curd. The cheese is made.
Ghee: heat butter to 110-120 degrees. Water leaves. Gold keeps.
