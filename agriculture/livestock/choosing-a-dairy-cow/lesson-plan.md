# Lesson plan — Choosing a dairy cow

**Course:** Agriculture · **Title:** Livestock · **Lesson:** Choosing a dairy cow
**Place in the curriculum:** Agriculture — the Livestock title (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** selection at two levels — choosing the breed (objective, adaptability, feeding system, maturity; price last) and then judging the individual animal
**This lesson's own level:** Grade 11 depth — it stands on the learner's own farm and fields, and on the course's earlier titles where named below.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
- Sub lesson 1 — Choosing the breed: Choice of breed weighs four things first: the **production objective** (what milk, for what market), **adaptability** (will this breed thrive here?), the **feeding system** (can you feed what it demands?) and **maturity** — when production starts.
- Sub lesson 2 — Choosing the individual: Breed chosen, the second level begins: **choice of the individual animal** — judging **the particular cow against the breed's promise**.
Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Ethiopia's own dairy breeds and Exotic dairy breeds. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. List what breed choice weighs: production objective, adaptability, feeding system and maturity.
2. State the maturity figures: small breeds into production soon after 24 months, large breeds at 28-30 months, local breeds later still.
3. Explain why price is the last consideration, and what individual choice adds after breed choice.
