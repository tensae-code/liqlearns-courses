# Choosing a dairy cow — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Breed choice weighs all EXCEPT —
a) production objective  b) the seller's friendship  c) adaptability and feeding system
**Answer: b) the seller's friendship**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Small breeds like Jersey come into production —
a) soon after 24 months  b) at 6 months  c) after 5 years
**Answer: a) soon after 24 months**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Price in breed choice is —
a) the first consideration  b) the last consideration, within reasonable limits  c) never considered at all
**Answer: b) the last consideration, within reasonable limits**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Individual choice means judging —
a) the breed average  b) the particular cow against the breed's promise  c) the price tag only
**Answer: b) the particular cow against the breed's promise**
