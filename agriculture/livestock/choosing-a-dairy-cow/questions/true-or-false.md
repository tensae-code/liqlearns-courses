# Choosing a dairy cow — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Large breeds enter production at about 28-30 months.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Local breeds enter production earlier than small exotic breeds.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Every cow of a good breed is automatically a good cow.
**Answer: False**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Adaptability means asking whether the breed will thrive in your place and system.
**Answer: True**
