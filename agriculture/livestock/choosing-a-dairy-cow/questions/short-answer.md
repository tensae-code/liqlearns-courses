# Choosing a dairy cow — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the four things breed choice weighs before price.
**Answer: Production objective, adaptability, the feeding system, and maturity.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does individual choice follow breed choice?
**Answer: Because the individual cow, not the breed average, is what will actually produce; she must be judged against her breed's promise.**
