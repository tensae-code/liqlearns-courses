# Video script — Choosing a dairy cow

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Choose the breed first: objective, place, feed, maturity.
Small breeds milk soon after 24 months.
Large breeds at 28 to 30. Local breeds later.
Price comes last. Then judge the one cow.
