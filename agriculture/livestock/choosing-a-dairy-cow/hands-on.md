# Hands-on — Choosing a dairy cow

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Market judging (or picture judging)
Judge one cow — at market or in the lesson picture: udder, legs, body, health. Score her against her breed's promise and defend the score.

## Maturity timeline
Draw a line 0-36 months. Mark when small breeds (soon after 24), large breeds (28-30) and local breeds (later) enter production.
