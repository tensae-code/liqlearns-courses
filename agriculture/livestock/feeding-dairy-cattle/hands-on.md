# Hands-on — Feeding dairy cattle

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Measure a trough
Measure a real trough (or lay out string): does each cow get her 10 cm? Count the cows it can truly serve at 90 cm height.

## Cost pie
Draw milk's cost as a pie: shade 60-70% for feed. Label what the remaining slice must cover.
