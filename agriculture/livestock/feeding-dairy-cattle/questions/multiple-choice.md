# Feeding dairy cattle — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Feed alone is what share of the cost of milk?
a) about 10%  b) 60-70%  c) over 95%
**Answer: b) 60-70%**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Good nutrition maintains the cow's —
a) colour only  b) health and productivity  c) temper
**Answer: b) health and productivity**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The recommended water trough gives each cow a minimum of —
a) 10 cm of length  b) 10 metres  c) 1 cm
**Answer: a) 10 cm of length**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The trough height recommended is —
a) 30 cm  b) 90 cm  c) 2 m
**Answer: b) 90 cm**
