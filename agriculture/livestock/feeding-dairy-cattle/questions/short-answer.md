# Feeding dairy cattle — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why is feeding called decisive twice over?
**Answer: Because nutrition maintains the cow's health and productivity, and feed alone is 60-70% of the cost of milk.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why do the trough figures matter for the shy cow?
**Answer: With too little length, dominant cows crowd the trough and shy cows drink last and least; the minimum length per cow protects every cow's share.**
