# Feeding dairy cattle — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Feed is the largest single cost in milk production.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A well-bred cow needs no feeding management.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The ration should follow the cow's state, not one unchanging habit.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Trough space per cow does not matter if water is plentiful.
**Answer: False**
