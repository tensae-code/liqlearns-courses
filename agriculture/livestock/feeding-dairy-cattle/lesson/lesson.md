# Feeding dairy cattle

## Do it first
Milk money comes in. Where does most of it go back out? Before the lesson gives the figure, guess the share of milk's cost that is feed alone — a third? a half? more?

## Sub lesson 1 — Feed: health, milk, and 60-70% of the cost
Feeding management is decisive **twice over**. First, **nutrition maintains the cow's health and productivity** — an underfed cow sickens and dries up. Second, money: **feed alone is 60-70% of the cost of milk**. No other single cost comes close.

That is why the dairy farmer manages feed like money — because it is money, eaten daily. Every later lesson in this unit leans on this one: the lactation curve is fed, the profit is what feeding leaves behind.

## Sub lesson 2 — Feeding across states, and the fittings
The unit covers **feeding across the cow's states** — the ration follows the cow: milking, dry, young, each stage fed for its work, not from one unchanging heap.

The feed area has **practical fittings**: **alleys** the cows feed along, and **water troughs** — with a **minimum of 10 cm of trough length per cow, at 90 cm height** recommended. Crowded, low troughs mean the shy cows drink last and least; the fitting figures protect every cow's share.

## Say it to your companion
