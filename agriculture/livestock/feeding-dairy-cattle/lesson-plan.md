# Lesson plan — Feeding dairy cattle

**Course:** Agriculture · **Title:** Livestock · **Lesson:** Feeding dairy cattle
**Place in the curriculum:** Agriculture — the Livestock title (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** feeding management for the dairy herd — nutrition holds health and productivity, feed alone is 60-70% of the cost of milk — plus the practical fittings: alleys and water troughs at a minimum of 10 cm per cow at 90 cm height
**This lesson's own level:** Grade 11 depth — it stands on the learner's own farm and fields, and on the course's earlier titles where named below.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
- Sub lesson 1 — Feed: health, milk, and 60-70% of the cost: Feeding management is decisive **twice over**.
- Sub lesson 2 — Feeding across states, and the fittings: The unit covers **feeding across the cow's states** — the ration follows the cow: milking, dry, young, each stage fed for its work, not from one unchanging heap.
Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Choosing a dairy cow. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the double weight of feeding: it maintains health and productivity, and feed alone is 60-70% of the cost of milk.
2. Describe feeding across the cow's states (the ration follows the cow's stage, not habit).
3. Give the trough fitting figures exactly: a minimum of 10 cm of trough length per cow, at 90 cm height.

## What you need
A tape measure; the lesson picture of cows at a feed alley.

## If the learner is stuck
- Underrates feed cost → feed alone is 60-70% of the cost of milk; it is the biggest lever on the farm.
