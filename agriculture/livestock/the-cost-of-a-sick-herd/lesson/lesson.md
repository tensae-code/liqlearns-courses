# The cost of a sick herd

## Do it first
A herd sickens for one month. Count the loss like a shopkeeper: milk unsold, a calf dead, hides scarred, a buyer turned away. How many different purses does one disease empty? Tally before the lesson tallies.

## Sub lesson 1 — Loss at the farm gate
**A sick animal produces less — lost productivity is lost income — may die (mortality), and leaves the farmer with fewer animals and less to sell**; **external parasites add hide and condition damage**. Prevention is not a cost beside these losses; it is the cheaper side of the ledger.

[Picture 26025 — a farmer's ledger — milk, animals and money shrinking as disease strikes]

## Sub lesson 2 — Loss at the border
**Disease also hampers export market development**: buyers abroad refuse produce from disease-known regions. One farm's outbreak taxes the district's trade — which is why disease control in the next lesson is a duty beyond the fence as well as within it.
