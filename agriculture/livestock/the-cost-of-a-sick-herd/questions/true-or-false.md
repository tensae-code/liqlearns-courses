# The cost of a sick herd — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A sick herd leaves fewer animals to sell.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Disease raises farm income.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Export buyers welcome disease-region produce.
**Answer: False**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Prevention is the cheaper side of the ledger.
**Answer: True**
