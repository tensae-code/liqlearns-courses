# The cost of a sick herd — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Lost productivity means —
a) more income  b) lost income  c) free feed
**Answer: b) lost income**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Mortality means animals —
a) multiply  b) die  c) sleep
**Answer: b) die**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Parasites add damage to —
a) hides and condition  b) roads  c) wells
**Answer: a) hides and condition**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Disease hampers —
a) imports only  b) export market development  c) rainfall
**Answer: b) export market development**
