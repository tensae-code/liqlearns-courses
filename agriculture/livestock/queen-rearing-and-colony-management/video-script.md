Queen rearing and colony management.
Movable-frame hives allow the modern practice set: migratory beekeeping, adding or reducing supers, regular inspection and quality honey harvest, plus swarm control, feeding during dearth periods and stimulating early colony growth — using supers, foundation sheets and queen excluders.
Queen rearing raises new queens to renew and multiply colonies.
