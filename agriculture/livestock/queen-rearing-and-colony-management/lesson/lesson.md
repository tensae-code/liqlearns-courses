# Queen rearing and colony management

## Do it first
A colony's queen ages. Its beekeeper can make new queens, add room before swarming, and move hives to flowers. This is beekeeping at its most skilled — enter carefully.

## Sub lesson 1 — The movable-frame practice set
Movable-frame hives allow the modern practice set: **migratory beekeeping, adding or reducing supers, regular inspection and quality honey harvest**, plus **swarm control, feeding during dearth periods and stimulating early colony growth** — using **supers, foundation sheets and queen excluders**. Each tool answers a colony problem: room, order, travel, hunger, timing.

## Sub lesson 2 — Queen rearing
**Queen rearing** raises **new queens to renew and multiply colonies**. A beekeeper who rears queens is never hostage to chance swarms: queens are produced deliberately, colonies are renewed before they fail, and good colonies become more colonies.
