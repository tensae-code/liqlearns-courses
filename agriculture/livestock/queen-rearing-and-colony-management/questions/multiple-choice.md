# Queen rearing and colony management — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Movable frames allow —
a) the modern practice set  b) no inspection  c) only traditional harvest
**Answer: a) the modern practice set**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Adding or reducing supers —
a) shrinks the colony  b) feeds the bees  c) matches the colony's room to its size
**Answer: c) matches the colony's room to its size**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A queen excluder is used with —
a) smokers  b) supers and foundation sheets in frame management  c) log hives only
**Answer: b) supers and foundation sheets in frame management**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Queen rearing raises new queens to —
a) renew and multiply colonies  b) sell as pets  c) end colonies
**Answer: a) renew and multiply colonies**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Swarm control belongs to —
a) chance  b) the bees alone  c) the frame-hive practice set
**Answer: c) the frame-hive practice set**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Feeding during dearth and stimulating early growth are —
a) harvest steps  b) management practices of this lesson  c) mistakes
**Answer: b) management practices of this lesson**
