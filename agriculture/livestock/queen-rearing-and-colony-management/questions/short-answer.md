# Queen rearing and colony management — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** List the movable-frame practice set.
**Answer: Migratory beekeeping; adding or reducing supers; regular inspection; quality honey harvest; swarm control; feeding during dearth; stimulating early colony growth — using supers, foundation sheets and queen excluders.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why rear queens?
**Answer: To renew colonies before they fail and to multiply good colonies deliberately, instead of depending on chance swarms.**
