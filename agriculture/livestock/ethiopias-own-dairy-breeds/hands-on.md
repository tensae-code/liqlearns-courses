# Hands-on — Ethiopia's own dairy breeds

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Breed spotting
At a market or on a walk, count how many cattle colours and sizes you can see. All of them are local zebu variety — note the hump that marks the type.

## Adaptation list
List five hardships of your area (heat, dry season, rough feed, disease, distance) and write how a local breed meets each one.
