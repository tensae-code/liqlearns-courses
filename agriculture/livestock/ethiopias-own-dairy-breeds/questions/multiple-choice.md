# Ethiopia's own dairy breeds — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ethiopia's local cattle breeds are —
a) indigenous zebu, Bos indicus  b) all Holsteins  c) breedless
**Answer: a) indigenous zebu, Bos indicus**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Local breeds vary in —
a) nothing at all  b) size and colour  c) species
**Answer: b) size and colour**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Compared with exotics, local breeds —
a) mature faster  b) mature slower but survive harsh conditions  c) never produce milk
**Answer: b) mature slower but survive harsh conditions**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Sahiwal, Gir and Kenana in the book's tables are —
a) European breeds  b) regional zebu breeds named alongside Ethiopian ones  c) goat breeds
**Answer: b) regional zebu breeds named alongside Ethiopian ones**
