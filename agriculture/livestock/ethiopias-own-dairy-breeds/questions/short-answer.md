# Ethiopia's own dairy breeds — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What unites Ethiopia's many local breeds?
**Answer: They are all indigenous zebu (Bos indicus) types adapted to local conditions.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** State the local-breed trade-off in one sentence.
**Answer: Local breeds mature more slowly than exotics, but survive and produce in harsh conditions where exotics struggle.**
