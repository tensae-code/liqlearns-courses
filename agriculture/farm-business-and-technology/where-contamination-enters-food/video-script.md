Where contamination enters food.
The sources of food contaminants are traced through production, harvest, postharvest handling and preparation — the points where hazards enter the food chain.
Each entry point is also a blocking point: what can enter at harvest can be stopped at harvest.
Do it yourself.
Then say it to your companion.
