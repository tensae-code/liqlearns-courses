# Where contamination enters food — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Contaminant sources are traced through —
a) production, harvest, postharvest handling and preparation  b) only the kitchen  c) only transport
**Answer: a) production, harvest, postharvest handling and preparation**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Contamination usually enters —
a) in one event only  b) never in the field  c) at several points along the chain
**Answer: c) at several points along the chain**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The food chain runs —
a) in a circle  b) from production toward the plate  c) from plate to field
**Answer: b) from production toward the plate**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** An entry point is also —
a) a blocking point  b) a guarantee of safety  c) the seller's problem
**Answer: a) a blocking point**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The handler's question at each stage is —
a) how fast can we move?  b) who is watching?  c) what could get in here, and how do I shut the door?
**Answer: c) what could get in here, and how do I shut the door?**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Postharvest handling is —
a) the only stage  b) one of the traced stages  c) outside the chain
**Answer: b) one of the traced stages**
