# Where contamination enters food — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Hazards can enter during production itself.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Once food leaves the field, no more contamination can enter.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Tracing entry points produces a practical blocking map.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Preparation in the kitchen is free of contamination risk.
**Answer: False**
