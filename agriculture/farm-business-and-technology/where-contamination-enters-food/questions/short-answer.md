# Where contamination enters food — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** List the chain stages where contaminants enter.
**Answer: Production, harvest, postharvest handling and preparation.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why trace the chain at all?
**Answer: Because each entry point found is a point where the hazard can be blocked; the trace is a practical map for the handler.**
