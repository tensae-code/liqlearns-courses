# Where contamination enters food

## Do it first
Walk a tomato's journey: field, basket, truck, market, kitchen, plate. At every handover, a hazard can climb aboard. Mark the doors.

## Sub lesson 1 — Sources along the chain
The **sources of food contaminants** are traced through **production, harvest, postharvest handling and preparation** — the points where hazards enter the food chain. Contamination is rarely one event; it is an accumulation of small entries along a journey.

[Picture 26683 — a food chain from field to plate with contamination entry points marked]

## Sub lesson 2 — Entry points can be blocked
Each entry point is also a **blocking point**: what can enter at harvest can be stopped at harvest. Tracing the chain is therefore a practical map — the handler walks it asking, at every stage, 'what could get in here, and how do I shut the door?'
