# Energy nutrients — carbs, fats, proteins

## Do it first
Three foods, three jobs: one fills you with energy fast, one packs energy into a spoonful, one builds the body itself. Hold a grain, an oilseed and a bean (or think of them) and guess which does which — and which spoonful carries the most energy.

## Sub lesson 1 — Carbohydrates and lipids — the fuels
**Carbohydrates are the body's main fuel** — the starches and sugars of grains and staples that everyday energy runs on.

**Lipids are the concentrated fuel**: oils are liquid and fats solid at room temperature. **A gram of fat gives 9 kilocalories**, and **fat should supply 20–35% of daily energy**. Lipids also carry **essential fatty acids and fat-soluble vitamins**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Proteins — build and repair
**Proteins build and repair the body** — muscle, skin, blood and the constant rebuilding of worn tissues.

Each nutrient's digestion and functions are worked through in turn in the book; hold the three jobs apart: carbohydrates fuel the day, lipids concentrate the fuel and carry vitamins, proteins build the body that burns the fuel.

## Say it to your companion
Give your companion the three jobs and the two fat figures — 9 kilocalories per gram, 20–35% of daily energy.
