# Energy nutrients — carbs, fats, proteins — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The body's main fuel is —
a) carbohydrates  b) proteins  c) water
**Answer: a) carbohydrates**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** One gram of fat gives —
a) 9 kilocalories  b) 4 kilocalories  c) no energy
**Answer: a) 9 kilocalories**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Proteins are the nutrients that —
a) build and repair the body  b) carry only vitamins  c) are liquid at room temperature
**Answer: a) build and repair the body**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Fat should supply about what share of daily energy?
a) 20–35%  b) 0%  c) 80–90%
**Answer: a) 20–35%**
