# Energy nutrients — carbs, fats, proteins — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Oils are liquid and fats solid at room temperature.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Lipids carry fat-soluble vitamins.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Proteins are the body's main fuel.
**Answer: False**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A gram of fat gives less energy than the book states for it — 5 kilocalories.
**Answer: False**
