# Energy nutrients — carbs, fats, proteins — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give the three energy nutrients and each one's job.
**Answer: Carbohydrates — the main fuel; lipids — the concentrated fuel, carrying essential fatty acids and fat-soluble vitamins; proteins — build and repair the body.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What share of daily energy should fat supply, and why not zero?
**Answer: 20–35% — fat is a needed concentrated fuel and carries essential fatty acids and fat-soluble vitamins.**
