# Hands-on — Energy nutrients — carbs, fats, proteins

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Kitchen sort
Take six real foods and sort them into three piles — main fuel, concentrated fuel, builders. Write the piles and defend each choice in one line.

## Energy maths
A spoon of oil holds about 14 g of fat. Use the lesson's figure to work out its energy (14 × 9), and compare it with the same weight of a starchy food as the lesson describes the difference.
