# Empowering women farmers — resources, income, leadership, time — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A productive-resources domain concerns —
a) access and decision-making power (autonomy in production)  b) only land price  c) weather
**Answer: a) access and decision-making power (autonomy in production)**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Autonomy in production means —
a) working alone always  b) owning a tractor  c) power to decide about productive resources
**Answer: c) power to decide about productive resources**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Income control can be —
a) nobody's  b) sole or joint  c) always the buyer's
**Answer: b) sole or joint**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The leadership domain is —
a) leadership in the community  b) leading only at home  c) no domain
**Answer: a) leadership in the community**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Time allocation is judged from —
a) a guess  b) the harvest size  c) a log of different activities
**Answer: c) a log of different activities**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Empowerment has —
a) nine domains  b) four domains in this lesson  c) one domain
**Answer: b) four domains in this lesson**
