# Empowering women farmers — resources, income, leadership, time — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Control over income use is an empowerment domain.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A farmer deciding in the field but never over money is fully empowered.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Time allocation is judged from an activity log.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Community leadership is not part of empowerment.
**Answer: False**
