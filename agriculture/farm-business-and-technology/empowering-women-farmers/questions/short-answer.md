# Empowering women farmers — resources, income, leadership, time — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** List the four empowerment domains.
**Answer: Access to and decision-making power about productive resources; control over the use of income; leadership in the community; time allocation.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How is time allocation judged?
**Answer: From a log of the person's different activities — how the hours are actually spent.**
