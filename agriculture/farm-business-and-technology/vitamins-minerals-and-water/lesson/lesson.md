# Vitamins, minerals and water

## Do it first
The energy nutrients come by the plateful. These three come by the pinch, the spoonful and the cup — yet a body missing any of them fails. Guess what each guards before the lesson names their work: what do bones need, what does blood need, and what is most of you made of?

## Sub lesson 1 — Small amounts, guard work
**Vitamins and minerals are needed in small amounts** — nothing like the platefuls of the energy nutrients — **but they guard metabolism, growth, blood, bone and teeth**.

They are the protective nutrients: the diet can carry energy in plenty and still fail for want of these small things.

## Sub lesson 2 — Water — a nutrient in its own right
**Water is treated as a nutrient in its own right**, closing the six-part nutrient survey: carbohydrates, lipids, proteins, vitamins, minerals and water.

No nutrient works without it — digestion, absorption and the blood that carries everything all run on water. Small or large, every nutrient on the list is one the body must have.
