# Vitamins, minerals and water — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A diet can have plenty of energy and still fail for want of vitamins and minerals.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Vitamins are needed in platefuls like carbohydrates.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Water closes the six-part nutrient survey.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Minerals play no part in bone and teeth.
**Answer: False**
