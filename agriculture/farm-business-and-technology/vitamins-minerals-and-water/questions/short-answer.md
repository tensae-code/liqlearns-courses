# Vitamins, minerals and water — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** List the six nutrients of the survey.
**Answer: Carbohydrates, lipids, proteins, vitamins, minerals and water.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why are vitamins and minerals called protective?
**Answer: Though needed in small amounts, they guard metabolism, growth, blood, bone and teeth.**
