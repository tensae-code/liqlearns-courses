# Vitamins, minerals and water — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Vitamins and minerals are needed in —
a) small amounts  b) the largest amounts of all nutrients  c) no amounts — they are optional
**Answer: a) small amounts**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The protective nutrients guard —
a) metabolism, growth, blood, bone and teeth  b) only the appetite  c) only body temperature
**Answer: a) metabolism, growth, blood, bone and teeth**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Water is treated in the survey as —
a) a nutrient in its own right  b) not a nutrient  c) a kind of vitamin
**Answer: a) a nutrient in its own right**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The nutrient survey has how many parts?
a) six  b) three  c) nine
**Answer: a) six**
