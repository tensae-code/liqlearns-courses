# Hands-on — Vitamins, minerals and water

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Protective plate
Build one meal on paper that covers all six nutrients. Label each item with the nutrient it stands for — the protective items in a different colour.

## Water diary
For one day, note every drink and watery food you take. Write one line on what the lesson says water does as a nutrient.
