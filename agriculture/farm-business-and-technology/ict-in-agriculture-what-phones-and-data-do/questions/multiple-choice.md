# ICT in agriculture — what phones and data do for farmers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Agriculture is increasingly —
a) knowledge-intensive  b) labour-free  c) indoor
**Answer: a) knowledge-intensive**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** ICT improves —
a) rainfall  b) soil depth  c) access, efficiency and affordability of information and services
**Answer: c) access, efficiency and affordability of information and services**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The roles of ICT include —
a) only banking  b) information, communication, markets and extension  c) only games
**Answer: b) information, communication, markets and extension**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The market role of ICT gives farmers —
a) prices and buyers  b) free produce  c) transport
**Answer: a) prices and buyers**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Extension by ICT delivers —
a) seed  b) tractors  c) advice at distance
**Answer: c) advice at distance**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** In this unit the phone is —
a) a luxury only  b) farm equipment  c) a toy
**Answer: b) farm equipment**
