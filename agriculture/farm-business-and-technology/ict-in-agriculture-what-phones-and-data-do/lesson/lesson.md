# ICT in agriculture — what phones and data do for farmers

## Do it first
A farmer used to learn the market price after selling — from the buyer. Now the price arrives before the harvest leaves the field. What changed?

## Sub lesson 1 — Knowledge-intensive farming
Agriculture is increasingly **knowledge-intensive**, and **ICT provides a pathway to better access, efficiency and affordability of information and services**. The roles of ICT — **information, communication, markets, extension** — are set out as the unit's frame: the phone is farm equipment now.

[Picture 26686 — a farmer with a phone standing in a maize field, price information on the screen]

## Sub lesson 2 — What the roles do
Each role answers an old farm problem: information for decisions, communication for distance, market access for prices and buyers, extension for advice. ICT does not plough — it tells the plough where, when, and for what price. (The unit's pages carry a wrong running head in the printed book — named only in sources.)
