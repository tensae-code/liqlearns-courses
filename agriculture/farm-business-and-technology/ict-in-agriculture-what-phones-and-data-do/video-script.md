ICT in agriculture — what phones and data do for farmers.
Agriculture is increasingly knowledge-intensive, and ICT provides a pathway to better access, efficiency and affordability of information and services.
Each role answers an old farm problem: information for decisions, communication for distance, market access for prices and buyers, extension for advice.
