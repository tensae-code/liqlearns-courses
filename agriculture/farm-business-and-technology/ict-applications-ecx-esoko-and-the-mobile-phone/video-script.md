ICT applications — ECX, Esoko and the mobile phone.
The major applications are tabled: the Ethiopian Commodity Exchange (ECX), formed to overhaul the country's commodity marketing; and Esoko (Africa), where smallholders sign up for weekly advisory packages — current market prices, matching bids and offers, and advisory services.
Beneath them all is the enabling fact: the expansion of mobile networks into rural Ethiopia — mobile subscriptions grew from a mere 1.2 million in 2007 to around 54.3 million.
