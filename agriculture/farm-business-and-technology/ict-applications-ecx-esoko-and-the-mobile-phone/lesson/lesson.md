# ICT applications — ECX, Esoko and the mobile phone

## Do it first
Two names and one astonishing number: an exchange, a phone service for smallholders, and subscriptions that grew forty-five-fold. Meet the applications.

## Sub lesson 1 — ECX and Esoko
The major applications are tabled: the **Ethiopian Commodity Exchange (ECX)**, formed to **overhaul the country's commodity marketing**; and **Esoko (Africa)**, where smallholders sign up for **weekly advisory packages — current market prices, matching bids and offers, and advisory services**. One is a national marketplace rebuilt; the other is market intelligence in a farmer's pocket.

## Sub lesson 2 — The mobile explosion underneath
Beneath them all is the enabling fact: the **expansion of mobile networks into rural Ethiopia — mobile subscriptions grew from a mere 1.2 million in 2007 to around 54.3 million**. In extension communication the **mobile telephone is judged the most affordable and swift way to reach farmers**. The applications exist because the network arrived.
