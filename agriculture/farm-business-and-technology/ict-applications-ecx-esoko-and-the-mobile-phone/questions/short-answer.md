# ICT applications — ECX, Esoko and the mobile phone — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Contrast ECX and Esoko.
**Answer: ECX is the national exchange formed to overhaul commodity marketing; Esoko is a service where smallholders sign up for weekly advisory packages of prices, bids/offers matching and advice.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give the mobile growth figures and their meaning.
**Answer: Subscriptions grew from 1.2 million in 2007 to about 54.3 million; the rural network's arrival is what makes phone-based farm services possible.**
