# ICT applications — ECX, Esoko and the mobile phone — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** ECX is —
a) the Ethiopian Commodity Exchange  b) a phone brand  c) a seed company
**Answer: a) the Ethiopian Commodity Exchange**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** ECX was formed to —
a) replace all farms  b) export phones  c) overhaul the country's commodity marketing
**Answer: c) overhaul the country's commodity marketing**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Esoko offers smallholders —
a) tractors  b) weekly advisory packages  c) free land
**Answer: b) weekly advisory packages**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Esoko's packages carry —
a) current market prices, matching bids and offers, and advisory services  b) only weather jokes  c) music
**Answer: a) current market prices, matching bids and offers, and advisory services**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Mobile subscriptions grew from 1.2 million (2007) to about —
a) 5.4 million  b) 543 million  c) 54.3 million
**Answer: c) 54.3 million**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** For reaching farmers, the mobile phone is judged —
a) useless in extension  b) the most affordable and swift way  c) the slowest way
**Answer: b) the most affordable and swift way**
