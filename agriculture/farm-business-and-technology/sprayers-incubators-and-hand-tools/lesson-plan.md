# Lesson plan — Sprayers, incubators and hand tools

**Course:** Agriculture · **Title:** Farm Business and Technology · **Lesson:** Sprayers, incubators and hand tools
**Place in the curriculum:** Agriculture — the Farm Business and Technology title (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the wider equipment list taught by use — the sprayer for crops and animals, the incubator that hatches eggs in 21 days, pruning shears and the rest — with safe handling throughout
**This lesson's own level:** Grade 11 depth — it stands on the learner's own farm and fields, and on the course's earlier titles where named below.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
- Sub lesson 1 — The sprayer and the shears — tools that apply and cut: The **sprayer applies insecticides, herbicides, pesticides or water to crops** — and is also used to **spray animals against external parasites**.
- Sub lesson 2 — The incubator — a machine hen: The **incubator keeps fertile eggs warm through 21 days of incubation**, with a **fan and heater** holding the warmth even, the way a sitting hen does.
Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Tillage tools and the tractor. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the sprayer's two uses: applying crop chemicals or water, and spraying animals against external parasites — always by the label, in protective clothing.
2. State the incubator's term: 21 days, with fan and heater keeping fertile eggs warm.
3. Say what pruning shears are: heavy-duty scissors for branches and stems.

## What you need
A knapsack sprayer (empty and clean) or its picture; an egg to examine.
