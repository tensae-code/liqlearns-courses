# Hands-on — Sprayers, incubators and hand tools

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Label-following drill
With an empty, clean sprayer (or its picture), walk through the safe routine without any chemical: protective clothing on, check the label, point away from people and water, wash after. A partner checks each step against the lesson.

## Egg candling look
Hold a torch behind an egg in a dark corner and look at the shell and air space. Write what a farmer checks before setting eggs in an incubator.
