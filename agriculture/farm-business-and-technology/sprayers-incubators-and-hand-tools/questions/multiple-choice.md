# Sprayers, incubators and hand tools — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A sprayer is used on crops to apply —
a) insecticides, herbicides, pesticides or water  b) only diesel  c) seed by hand
**Answer: a) insecticides, herbicides, pesticides or water**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** On animals, a sprayer is used against —
a) external parasites  b) internal worms only  c) fever
**Answer: a) external parasites**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** An incubator keeps fertile eggs warm for —
a) 21 days  b) 7 days  c) 40 days
**Answer: a) 21 days**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Pruning shears are —
a) heavy-duty scissors for cutting branches and stems  b) a kind of plough  c) egg trays
**Answer: a) heavy-duty scissors for cutting branches and stems**
