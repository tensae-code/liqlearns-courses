# Sprayers, incubators and hand tools — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Spraying should follow the product label and use protective clothing.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** It is safe to spray toward people if the wind is light.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** An incubator uses a fan and a heater to keep warmth even.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Pruning shears are used for harvesting grain.
**Answer: False**
