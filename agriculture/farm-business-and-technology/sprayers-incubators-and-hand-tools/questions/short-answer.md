# Sprayers, incubators and hand tools — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give the sprayer's safety rules as the lesson states them.
**Answer: Follow the product label, wear protective clothing, never spray toward people, animals or water sources, and wash yourself and the equipment afterwards.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How does an incubator do a hen's job?
**Answer: Its fan and heater keep fertile eggs warm and even through the 21 days of incubation, as a sitting hen does.**
