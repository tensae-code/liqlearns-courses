# Hands-on — Tillage tools and the tractor

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Tool spotting
Visit a farm or cooperative and identify every tillage tool you can. Write each tool's name and the pass it makes — primary or secondary.

## Seedbed feel test
Compare two patches of soil — one freshly ploughed, one harrowed and firmed. Write how each feels and which a seed would sit better in, and why.
