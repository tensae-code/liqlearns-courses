# Tillage tools and the tractor

## Do it first
A seedbed is made in passes: first the ground is opened, then it is broken fine and firmed. Sort these jobs before the tools are named: turning the soil over, pulverising and stirring, crushing clods and pressing stones down. Which pass is each?

## Sub lesson 1 — Primary cultivation — opening the ground
The **tractor is the most popular and essential power source** of mechanised farming — the engine the tools hang on.

**Primary cultivation** opens the ground: **mouldboard ploughs and tillers** turn and loosen the soil first, before any seedbed work begins.

## Sub lesson 2 — Secondary tillage — making the seedbed
**Secondary tillage** prepares the seedbed: **disk harrows** break the opened ground; **cultivators remove weeds and pulverise and stir the soil before planting, often with rotary motion**.

Last comes the **cultipacker: it crushes clods, presses small stones down and removes air pockets, leaving a smooth, firm seedbed** — the finishing pass. (The **baler** belongs to harvesting, not to this family.)

## Say it to your companion
Walk your companion through the passes: plough opens, harrow breaks, cultivator weeds and stirs, cultipacker firms.
