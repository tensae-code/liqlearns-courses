# Tillage tools and the tractor — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The most popular and essential power source in mechanised farming is —
a) the tractor  b) the ox  c) the diesel generator alone
**Answer: a) the tractor**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Primary cultivation is done by —
a) mouldboard ploughs and tillers  b) the cultipacker  c) the baler
**Answer: a) mouldboard ploughs and tillers**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The cultivator's jobs include —
a) removing weeds and pulverising and stirring soil before planting  b) crushing clods as the final pass  c) baling hay
**Answer: a) removing weeds and pulverising and stirring soil before planting**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The cultipacker leaves the seedbed —
a) smooth and firm  b) loose and cloddy  c) deeply ploughed
**Answer: a) smooth and firm**
