# Tillage tools and the tractor — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Put the tillage family in working order and give each its job.
**Answer: Mouldboard plough/tiller opens the ground (primary); disk harrow breaks it; cultivator removes weeds and pulverises and stirs before planting; cultipacker crushes clods, presses stones, removes air pockets and firms the seedbed.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why is the baler not in this lesson's family?
**Answer: It is a harvesting machine, not a soil-cultivation tool.**
