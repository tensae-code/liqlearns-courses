# Tillage tools and the tractor — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Disk harrows belong to secondary tillage.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The baler is a soil-cultivation tool.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The cultipacker removes air pockets from the seedbed.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Cultivators are never used before planting.
