# Diversified food production — why variety matters — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Agricultural food diversification means —
a) producing a variety of foods from farm activities  b) growing one crop very well  c) buying all food in town
**Answer: a) producing a variety of foods from farm activities**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Variety matters because —
a) different foods provide different nutrients  b) variety is fashionable  c) one food covers everything
**Answer: a) different foods provide different nutrients**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** An integrated farming system joins —
a) crops, livestock and other parts in one system  b) only crop fields  c) only fish ponds
**Answer: a) crops, livestock and other parts in one system**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Agriculture is described as ___ to food and nutrition security.
a) indispensable  b) unrelated  c) a minor helper
**Answer: a) indispensable**
