# Diversified food production — why variety matters — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does diversified production matter, in the lesson's one sentence?
**Answer: Different foods provide different nutrients and no single food covers the diet.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does an integrated farming system look like?
**Answer: Crops, livestock, pond and garden working as one system, each part supporting the others and the family.**
