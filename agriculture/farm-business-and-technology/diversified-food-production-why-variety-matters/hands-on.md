# Hands-on — Diversified food production — why variety matters

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Integrated farm drawing
Draw an integrated farm — crops, livestock, pond and garden in one system — with arrows showing how each part feeds another (manure to fields, residues to animals, pond water to garden).

## Variety count
Count the different foods your household ate in the last three days. Write the count and the groups missing.
