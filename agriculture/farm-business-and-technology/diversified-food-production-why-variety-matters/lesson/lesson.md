# Diversified food production — why variety matters

## Do it first
Could one perfect food feed a family alone? Teff is prized, milk is rich, eggs are famous — yet the nutrition units kept needing six groups. Before the reasons are named, argue it: what does each part of an integrated farm give that the others cannot?

## Sub lesson 1 — No single food covers the diet
**Agricultural food diversification means introducing or producing a variety of foods from farm activities.** It matters because **different foods provide different nutrients and no single food covers the diet**.

That one sentence is the whole unit: variety is not a luxury or a taste — it is how nutrients are covered.

## Sub lesson 2 — The integrated farm
The unit pictures an **integrated farming system** — crops, livestock, garden and water working as one system, each part feeding the others and the family.

Because **agriculture is indispensable to food and nutrition security**, diversified production is how a farm produces that variety itself instead of hoping the market supplies it.
