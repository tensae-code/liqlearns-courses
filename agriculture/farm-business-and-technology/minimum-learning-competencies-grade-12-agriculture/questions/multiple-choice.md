# Minimum learning competencies — Grade 12 Agriculture — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The MLC list sets —
a) the floor every Grade 12 learner must reach  b) the ceiling for the best learner  c) next year's syllabus
**Answer: a) the floor every Grade 12 learner must reach**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** It is organised by —
a) alphabet  b) teacher  c) thematic area
**Answer: c) thematic area**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Units 1–5 sit under —
a) Natural Resources  b) Crop Production and Management  c) Agricultural Technology
**Answer: b) Crop Production and Management**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Agricultural Technology carries —
a) Unit 17 alone  b) Units 6–11  c) Units 12–14
**Answer: a) Unit 17 alone**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Livestock Production and Management covers —
a) Units 1–5  b) Unit 17  c) Units 6–11
**Answer: c) Units 6–11**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Competencies are stated as —
a) pages read  b) demonstrable abilities  c) topics mentioned
**Answer: b) demonstrable abilities**
