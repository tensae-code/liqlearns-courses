# Minimum learning competencies — Grade 12 Agriculture — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the four thematic areas with their unit spans.
**Answer: Crop Production and Management (Units 1–5); Livestock Production and Management (Units 6–11); Natural Resources Management (Units 12–14); Agricultural Technology (Unit 17).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give an example competency form from the list.
**Answer: 'Students demonstrate the ability to…' — e.g. to establish a nursery; competencies are demonstrable abilities.**
