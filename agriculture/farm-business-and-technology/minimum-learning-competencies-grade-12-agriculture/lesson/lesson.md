# Minimum learning competencies — Grade 12 Agriculture

## Do it first
This lesson stands on teacher-guide evidence only. It names the floor of Grade 12 Agriculture: the least every learner must be able to do, organised not by unit but by the work itself.

## Sub lesson 1 — The floor, organised by thematic area
The **Minimum Learning Competency list** sets the floor every Grade 12 learner must reach, organised by **thematic area**: **Crop Production and Management (Units 1–5)**, **Livestock Production and Management (Units 6–11** — camel competencies among them: identify camel breeds, feeds and feeding, housing, diseases and products), **Natural Resources Management (Units 12–14)** and **Agricultural Technology (Unit 17, standing alone under its theme)** — with the gender and safe-food units carried in the table's remaining thematic rows.

[Picture 26690 — the four thematic areas of Grade 12 Agriculture as four labelled fields]

## Sub lesson 2 — Competencies as demonstrable abilities
Each unit's competencies are stated as **demonstrable abilities** — 'Students demonstrate the ability to establish nursery…' — things a learner can be seen doing, not topics a teacher can claim to have mentioned. The floor is a doing floor.

## Say it to your companion
Explain this lesson to your companion in your own words, using one real example from your own area for each sub lesson. Your companion asks one question about each part — answer from the lesson, not from memory of other lessons.

## Do It Yourself
1. Sort the 17 units under the four themes from memory.
2. Write three MLC-style 'demonstrate the ability to' lines for your favourite unit.
3. Check one unit's lessons against its competencies: is the floor covered?
