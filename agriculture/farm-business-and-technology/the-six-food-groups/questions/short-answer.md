# The six food groups — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the six food groups.
**Answer: Staples; fats and oils; protein foods; legumes; vegetables and fruit; animal-source foods — the groups of the book's table.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why are staples called the base?
**Answer: They are the cereal foods most people, especially the poor, eat most — the energy foundation the other groups build on.**
