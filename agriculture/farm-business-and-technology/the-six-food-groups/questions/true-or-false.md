# The six food groups — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Sorghum, maize, barley and oats are staple cereals.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** There are twelve food groups in the book's table.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Groundnut is named among the oilseeds.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The staples group is the smallest part of most diets.
**Answer: False**
