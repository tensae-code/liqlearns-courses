# The six food groups — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Foods are grouped by —
a) the nutrient they carry in abundance  b) their colour  c) their price
**Answer: a) the nutrient they carry in abundance**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Staples are —
a) the base most people, especially the poor, eat most  b) a rare luxury group  c) only eaten at feasts
**Answer: a) the base most people, especially the poor, eat most**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is in the fats and oils group?
a) sesame  b) teff grain  c) milk
**Answer: a) sesame**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Fats and oils give energy, essential fatty acids and —
a) fat-soluble vitamins  b) only water  c) fibre only
**Answer: a) fat-soluble vitamins**
