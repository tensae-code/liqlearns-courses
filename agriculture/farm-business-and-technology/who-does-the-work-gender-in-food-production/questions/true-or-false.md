# Who does the work — gender in food production — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Women are about half the agricultural workforce.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Gender roles have no effect on productivity.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Consumption decisions are part of the who-does-what map.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Only production work counts in the division of labour.
**Answer: False**
