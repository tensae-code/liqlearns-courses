# Who does the work — gender in food production — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Women form about — of the agricultural workforce
a) half  b) a tenth  c) nearly all of it officially
**Answer: a) half**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Understanding gender's role could —
a) end farming  b) change nothing  c) increase productivity and reduce poverty
**Answer: c) increase productivity and reduce poverty**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The stakes include —
a) export paperwork  b) food production and food security  c) only village custom
**Answer: b) food production and food security**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The mapping covers —
a) production, processing and consumption decisions  b) only field work  c) only cooking
**Answer: a) production, processing and consumption decisions**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The division of work has —
a) only costs  b) no effects  c) both costs and contributions
**Answer: c) both costs and contributions**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The map prepares —
a) a market day  b) the empowerment lesson's actions  c) a census
**Answer: b) the empowerment lesson's actions**
