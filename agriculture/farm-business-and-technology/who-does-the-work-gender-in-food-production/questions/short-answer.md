# Who does the work — gender in food production — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does understanding gender raise productivity?
**Answer: Because women are about half the agricultural workforce; planning that sees their work, constraints and decisions uses the farm's full strength, reducing poverty and improving food security.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What three fields does the who-does-what map cover?
**Answer: Production, processing, and household consumption decisions.**
