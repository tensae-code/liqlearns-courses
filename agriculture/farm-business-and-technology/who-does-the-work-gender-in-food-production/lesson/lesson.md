# Who does the work — gender in food production

## Do it first
Follow one farming household from dawn to dark, twice: once walking with her, once with him. Same house, same field — two different days.

## Sub lesson 1 — Half the workforce
Understanding gender's role in agricultural food production could greatly increase productivity, reduce poverty and improve food production and food security. **Women form about half of the agricultural workforce** — any plan that ignores half the workers plans with half its strength.

## Sub lesson 2 — Mapping who does what
The section maps **who does what — in production, processing and household consumption decisions** — and what that division **costs and contributes**. The map is the management tool: once work and decisions are visible, the next lesson (empowerment) can act on them.
