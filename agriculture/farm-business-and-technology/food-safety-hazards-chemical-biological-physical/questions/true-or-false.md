# Food safety hazards — chemical, biological, physical — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Chemical hazards can occur naturally, as mycotoxins do.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** All chemical hazards are added on purpose.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Physical hazards are objects that should not be in food.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Pesticides in food are a biological hazard.
**Answer: False**
