# Food safety hazards — chemical, biological, physical — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give the three chemical sub-groups with an example each.
**Answer: Naturally occurring — mycotoxins; intentionally added — the preservative sodium nitrate; unintentionally added — pesticides.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the three hazard families.
**Answer: Chemical, biological and physical hazards.**
