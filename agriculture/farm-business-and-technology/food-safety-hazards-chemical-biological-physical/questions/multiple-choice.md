# Food safety hazards — chemical, biological, physical — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Mycotoxins are —
a) naturally occurring chemical hazards  b) physical hazards  c) preservatives
**Answer: a) naturally occurring chemical hazards**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Sodium nitrate is given as —
a) a pesticide  b) a germ  c) an intentionally added chemical (a preservative)
**Answer: c) an intentionally added chemical (a preservative)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Pesticides reach food as —
a) biological hazards  b) unintentionally added chemicals  c) ingredients
**Answer: b) unintentionally added chemicals**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Biological hazards are —
a) living dangers (germs)  b) stones and glass  c) spray residues
**Answer: a) living dangers (germs)**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A stone in grain is a —
a) chemical hazard  b) biological hazard  c) physical hazard
**Answer: c) physical hazard**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The hazard families number —
a) five  b) three: chemical, biological, physical  c) two
**Answer: b) three: chemical, biological, physical**
