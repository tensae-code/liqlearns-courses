# Video script — Food safety hazards — chemical, biological, physical

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Food safety hazards — chemical, biological, physical.
Hazards are classed, with chemical hazards detailed: naturally occurring chemicals such as mycotoxins; intentionally added chemicals such as the preservative sodium nitrate; and unintentionally added chemicals such as pesticides — pictured by a tractor spraying pesticide that can inadvertently find its way into the food we consume.
Biological hazards (living dangers — the germs) and physical hazards (objects that should not be in food) complete the scheme.
Do it yourself.
Then say it to your companion.
