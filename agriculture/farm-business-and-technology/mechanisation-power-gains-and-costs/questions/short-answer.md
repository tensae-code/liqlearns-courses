# Mechanisation — power, gains and costs — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name three costs of mechanisation the lesson names.
**Answer: It can hamper social capital such as debo and guza; it reduces manual work for labourers; it needs continuous fuel and electricity.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How are the mechanisation levels decided?
**Answer: By the share of animal versus machine power — low, fair and high; at the fair level animal power still supplies about a third to two-thirds.**
