# Mechanisation — power, gains and costs — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Mechanisation covers harvesting, storage and animal rearing as well as field work.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Mechanisation needs no fuel or electricity.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Mechanisation can reduce the manual work available for labourers.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The levels of mechanisation are classed by farm size alone.
**Answer: False**
