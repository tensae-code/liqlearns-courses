# Mechanisation — power, gains and costs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Mechanisation replaces human or animal labour with —
a) machinery  b) more communal parties  c) hand tools only
**Answer: a) machinery**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which is a field of mechanisation?
a) farm-gate processing  b) only ploughing  c) only transport to town
**Answer: a) farm-gate processing**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Debo and guza are —
a) communal work parties that mechanisation can hamper  b) types of tractor  c) fuel brands
**Answer: a) communal work parties that mechanisation can hamper**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** At the fair level of mechanisation, animal power supplies about —
a) a third to two-thirds of the power  b) nothing  c) all of the power
**Answer: a) a third to two-thirds of the power**
