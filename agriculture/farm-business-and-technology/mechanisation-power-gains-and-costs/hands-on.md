# Hands-on — Mechanisation — power, gains and costs

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Power census
List every power source used on farms near you — human, animal, machine — task by task. Class your area low, fair or high and write the evidence.

## Debo and guza account
Ask an elder how debo or guza work parties are organised and what machines have changed. Write half a page.
