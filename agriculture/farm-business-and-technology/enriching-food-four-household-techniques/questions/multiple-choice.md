# Enriching food — four household techniques — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Fortification means —
a) adding vitamins and minerals to foods  b) sprouting grain  c) roasting over dry heat
**Answer: a) adding vitamins and minerals to foods**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Germination is —
a) sprouting grain  b) micro-organisms breaking food down  c) adding minerals at a mill
**Answer: a) sprouting grain**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Fermentation in yoghurt is done by —
a) bacteria  b) yeast only  c) roasting
**Answer: a) bacteria**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which Ethiopian food is NOT fermented?
a) kita  b) injera  c) milk curd
**Answer: a) kita**
