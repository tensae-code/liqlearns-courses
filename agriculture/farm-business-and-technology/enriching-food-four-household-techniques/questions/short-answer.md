# Enriching food — four household techniques — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the four enrichment techniques and define two of them.
**Answer: Fortification (adding vitamins and minerals), germination (sprouting grain), fermentation (micro-organisms breaking food down), roasting.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which fermented foods does the lesson name for Ethiopia, and which food is the trap?
**Answer: Injera and milk curd are fermented; kita is not fermented.**
