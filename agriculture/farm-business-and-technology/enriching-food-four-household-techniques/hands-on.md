# Hands-on — Enriching food — four household techniques

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sprout a handful (the germination practice)
Soak a handful of grain overnight, drain it, and keep it under a damp cloth, rinsing morning and evening. Watch for two days as the rootlets show — germination in your own jar. Do not eat experimental sprouts; this jar is for watching, and food sprouting for the table follows your household's own known practice.

## Fermentation watch
With your household's permission, observe an injera batter or milk curd being made: note the bubbles, smell and time. Write which micro-organism work the lesson names for bread and yoghurt.
