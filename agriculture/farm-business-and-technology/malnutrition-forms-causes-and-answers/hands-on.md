# Hands-on — Malnutrition — forms, causes and answers

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## BMI with supervision
With a teacher or health worker, measure an adult volunteer's height and weight and work out BMI as weight in kilograms divided by height in metres squared; read the result against a BMI chart. No child is measured for display.

## Community lesson draft
Draft a ten-minute community talk that teaches one form of malnutrition, one cause and one answer from this lesson. Deliver it to your study group.
