# Malnutrition — forms, causes and answers — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Overweight is counted as a form of malnutrition.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Micronutrient deficiency means eating too much food.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Children under five and pregnant and lactating women are hit hardest.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Malnutrition has no effect on a nation's productivity.
**Answer: False**
