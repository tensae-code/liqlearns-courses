# Malnutrition — forms, causes and answers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these is a form of malnutrition?
a) stunting  b) tall stature  c) strong appetite
**Answer: a) stunting**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Malnutrition's causes are classed as —
a) immediate, underlying and basic  b) mild, medium and severe  c) urban and rural
**Answer: a) immediate, underlying and basic**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** BMI is a measure of —
a) weight against height  b) income against food prices  c) age against growth only
**Answer: a) weight against height**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The book's solutions to malnutrition begin with —
a) educating communities  b) importing all food  c) closing markets
**Answer: a) educating communities**
