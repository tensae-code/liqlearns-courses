# Malnutrition — forms, causes and answers — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the five forms of malnutrition.
**Answer: Stunting, wasting, underweight, overweight and micronutrient deficiency.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Who does malnutrition hit hardest in Ethiopia, and what impacts does it carry?
**Answer: Children (under five in the book's trend table) and pregnant and lactating women; impacts run from failure to perform daily work to lost productivity at individual, society and national level.**
