# The Grade 11 Agriculture syllabus — themes and periods — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the five themes with their unit spans.
**Answer: Crop Production and Management (Units 1–3); Animal Production and Management (Units 4–9); Natural Resources Management (Units 10–13); Agricultural Technology (Unit 14); Nutrition Sensitive Agriculture (Units 15–16).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which units get the most periods, and which the fewest?
**Answer: Units 1 and 9 get the most, 8 periods each; Units 7 and 14 get the fewest, 3 periods each.**
