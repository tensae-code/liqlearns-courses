# The Grade 11 Agriculture syllabus — themes and periods — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Grade 11 syllabus organises its units under —
a) three themes  b) five themes  c) sixteen themes
**Answer: b) five themes**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Animal Production and Management covers —
a) Units 4–9  b) Units 1–3  c) Units 10–13
**Answer: a) Units 4–9**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Agricultural Technology carries —
a) Units 15–16  b) Unit 14 alone  c) Units 10–13
**Answer: b) Unit 14 alone**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The year totals —
a) 89 periods  b) 99 periods  c) 79 periods
**Answer: a) 89 periods**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The biggest allocations (8 periods) go to —
a) Units 7 and 14  b) Units 2 and 3  c) Units 1 and 9
**Answer: c) Units 1 and 9**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The smallest allocation is —
a) 3 periods  b) 4 periods  c) 5 periods
**Answer: a) 3 periods**
