# Lesson plan — The Grade 11 Agriculture syllabus — themes and periods

**Course:** Agriculture · **Title:** Farm Business and Technology · **Lesson:** The Grade 11 Agriculture syllabus — themes and periods
**Place in the curriculum:** Agriculture — the Farm Business and Technology title (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The Agriculture Syllabus for Grade 11 is the official course framework: five themes over 16 units, with a period allocation per unit totalling 89 periods.
**This lesson's own level:** Grade 11 depth — it stands on the learner's own farm and fields and on the Grade 11 Agriculture course it frames.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
- Sub lesson 1 — Five themes frame the year: The syllabus organises the 16 units under five themes: Crop Production and Management (Units 1–3), Animal Production and Management (Units 4–9), Natural Resources Management (Units 10–13)…
- Sub lesson 2 — The period allocation — 89 periods: Unit 1: 8 periods; Unit 2: 7; Unit 3: 7; Unit 4: 5; Unit 5: 6; Unit 6: 5; Unit 7: 3; Unit 8: 6; Unit 9: 8…
Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
