The Grade 11 Agriculture syllabus — themes and periods.
The Agriculture Syllabus for Grade 11 organises the 16 units under five themes: Crop Production and Management (Units 1–3), Animal Production and Management (Units 4–9), Natural Resources Management (Units 10–13), Agricultural Technology (Unit 14) and Nutrition Sensitive Agriculture (Units 15–16).
The periods: Unit 1: 8; Unit 2: 7; Unit 3: 7; Unit 4: 5; Unit 5: 6; Unit 6: 5; Unit 7: 3; Unit 8: 6; Unit 9: 8; Unit 10: 6; Unit 11: 6; Unit 12: 5; Unit 13: 5; Unit 14: 3; Unit 15: 5; Unit 16: 4 — 89 periods in total.
