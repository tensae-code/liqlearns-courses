# The Grade 11 Agriculture syllabus — themes and periods

## Do it first
This lesson stands on teacher-guide evidence only. A syllabus is a budget of time: 89 periods for a year of agriculture, grouped under five themes. Where the periods go, the priorities are.

## Sub lesson 1 — Five themes frame the year
The **Agriculture Syllabus for Grade 11** is the official course framework, absent from the student book. It organises the 16 units under **five themes**: **Crop Production and Management (Units 1–3)**, **Animal Production and Management (Units 4–9)**, **Natural Resources Management (Units 10–13)**, **Agricultural Technology (Unit 14)** and **Nutrition Sensitive Agriculture (Units 15–16)**. Per unit it gives **learning outcomes** plus a table of **competencies, main contents and suggested activities** — buzz groups, pair work, field assignments to livestock farms to check healthy and sick animals, and presentations with monitored feedback.

## Sub lesson 2 — The period allocation — 89 periods
Unit 1: 8 periods; Unit 2: 7; Unit 3: 7; Unit 4: 5; Unit 5: 6; Unit 6: 5; Unit 7: 3; Unit 8: 6; Unit 9: 8; Unit 10: 6; Unit 11: 6; Unit 12: 5; Unit 13: 5; Unit 14: 3; Unit 15: 5; Unit 16: 4 — **89 periods in total**. The two biggest allocations, 8 periods each, go to the year's opening unit (Introduction to Crop Production) and to Dairy Cattle (Unit 9); the smallest, 3 periods each, go to Farm Animals Housing (Unit 7) and Mechanised Farming (Unit 14).
