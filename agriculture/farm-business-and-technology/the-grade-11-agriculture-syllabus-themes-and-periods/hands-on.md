# Hands-on — The Grade 11 Agriculture syllabus — themes and periods

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Build the chart
Cut 16 paper strips, one per unit, each strip's length matching its period count, and lay the year out on a table under its five themes.

## Term plan
Take one unit and spread its periods across a school term, naming what each period must carry from the unit's competencies.
