Minimum learning competencies — Grade 11 Agriculture.
The Minimum Learning Competency list sets the floor every Grade 11 learner must reach: for each of the 16 units, a short competency list.
The competencies are things a learner can be seen doing. Unit 5 requires pupils to prepare a ration by the Pearson Square method and demonstrate hay and silage conservation; Unit 16 requires appreciating indigenous knowledge in food diversification.
Do it yourself.
Then say it to your companion.
