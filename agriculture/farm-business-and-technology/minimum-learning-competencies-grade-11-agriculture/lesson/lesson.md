# Minimum learning competencies — Grade 11 Agriculture

## Do it first
This lesson stands on teacher-guide evidence only. It names the floor of Grade 11 Agriculture: the least every learner must be able to do, set out unit by unit across all sixteen units.

## Sub lesson 1 — The floor, unit by unit
The **Minimum Learning Competency (MLC) list** sets the floor every Grade 11 learner must reach: for **each of the 16 units a short competency list**. The units run in the course's own order — Unit 1 Introduction to Crop Production; Unit 2 Field Crops; Unit 3 Industrial Crops; Unit 4 Introduction to Farm Animals; Unit 5 Animal Feeds and Feeding; Unit 6 Animal Genetics and Breeding; Unit 7 Farm Animals Housing; Unit 8 Basic Animal Health and Disease Control; Unit 9 Dairy Cattle; Unit 10 Introduction to Natural Resources; Unit 11 Management of Natural Resources; Unit 12 Concepts of Biodiversity; Unit 13 Climate Change Adaptation and Mitigation; Unit 14 Mechanised Farming; Unit 15 Introduction to Human Nutrition; Unit 16 Diversified Food Production and Consumption.

## Sub lesson 2 — A floor of things you can do
The competencies are **things a learner can be seen doing**. Unit 5 requires pupils to **prepare a ration by the Pearson Square method** and to **demonstrate hay and silage conservation**; Unit 16 requires **appreciating indigenous knowledge in food diversification**. The floor is not a list of pages covered — it is a list of abilities every learner carries out of the year.

## Say it to your companion
