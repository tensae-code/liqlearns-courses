# Minimum learning competencies — Grade 11 Agriculture — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the first three and the last three units of the Grade 11 course, in order.
**Answer: Unit 1 Introduction to Crop Production; Unit 2 Field Crops; Unit 3 Industrial Crops — and Unit 14 Mechanised Farming; Unit 15 Introduction to Human Nutrition; Unit 16 Diversified Food Production and Consumption.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give two Unit 5 floor items.
**Answer: Prepare a ration by the Pearson Square method, and demonstrate hay and silage conservation.**
