# Minimum learning competencies — Grade 11 Agriculture — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The MLC list sets —
a) the floor every Grade 11 learner must reach  b) the ceiling for the best learner  c) next year's timetable
**Answer: a) the floor every Grade 11 learner must reach**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** It is organised —
a) alphabetically  b) unit by unit across all 16 units  c) by teacher
**Answer: b) unit by unit across all 16 units**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Unit 9 of the course is —
a) Dairy Cattle  b) Biodiversity  c) Mechanised Farming
**Answer: a) Dairy Cattle**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Unit 5's floor requires preparing a ration by —
a) guessing  b) the Pearson Square method  c) weighing only
**Answer: b) the Pearson Square method**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Unit 5 also requires demonstrating —
a) hay and silage conservation  b) cheese making  c) beekeeping
**Answer: a) hay and silage conservation**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Unit 16's floor requires appreciating —
a) imported foods only  b) examination technique  c) indigenous knowledge in food diversification
**Answer: c) indigenous knowledge in food diversification**
