# Traditional ways of keeping food safe — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this closing lesson record?
**Answer: The traditional practices Ethiopian communities use to keep food safe and handle the harvest — protecting stored and prepared food.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How do traditional practices connect to the unit's hazards?
**Answer: They are working defences against the same hazards — their designs and customs block contamination and spoilage the earlier lessons classified.**
