# Traditional ways of keeping food safe — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The unit closes with —
a) traditional knowledge for keeping food safe  b) a chemical catalogue  c) import rules
**Answer: a) traditional knowledge for keeping food safe**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The practices protect —
a) only fresh fruit  b) only exports  c) stored and prepared food
**Answer: c) stored and prepared food**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The knowledge is recorded as —
a) children's tales  b) working knowledge  c) dead custom
**Answer: b) working knowledge**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A traditional granary is —
a) raised and sealed against pests and damp  b) built in a swamp  c) left open
**Answer: a) raised and sealed against pests and damp**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Traditional practices block —
a) nothing  b) only insects  c) hazards the earlier lessons named
**Answer: c) hazards the earlier lessons named**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Modern safety and traditional practice are —
a) the same thing  b) partners, not rivals  c) enemies
**Answer: b) partners, not rivals**
