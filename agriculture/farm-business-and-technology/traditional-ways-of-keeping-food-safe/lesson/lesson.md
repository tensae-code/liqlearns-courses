# Traditional ways of keeping food safe

## Do it first
A raised granary, sealed and dry, has guarded grain longer than any refrigerator has existed. The unit closes by recording how communities keep food safe — from their own knowledge.

## Sub lesson 1 — Traditional knowledge for safe food
**Traditional knowledge for keeping food safe and handling the harvest** closes the unit: the practices Ethiopian communities use to **protect stored and prepared food**, recorded as **working knowledge**.

## Sub lesson 2 — The pattern of the course, again
As with fish, bees and soil, the book ends the enterprise by listening: traditional safe-food and postharvest practices are proven technology — granaries raised against damp and pests, handling customs that block hazards the earlier lessons named. Modern food safety and traditional practice are partners, not rivals.
