# Hands-on — Nutrition-sensitive agriculture

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Farm plan for nutrition
Sketch a farm plan — garden, poultry, legumes and staples together — so the family's plate covers all six food groups across the year. Label each part with its group.

## Plate-to-field trace
Take one real family meal and trace each food back: grown here, bought, or missing? Write what the farm could grow of the missing parts.
