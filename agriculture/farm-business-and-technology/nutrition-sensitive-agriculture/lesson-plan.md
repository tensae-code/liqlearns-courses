# Lesson plan — Nutrition-sensitive agriculture

**Course:** Agriculture · **Title:** Farm Business and Technology · **Lesson:** Nutrition-sensitive agriculture
**Place in the curriculum:** Agriculture — the Farm Business and Technology title (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** farming deliberately for nutrient-rich, diverse diets — the food-based approach that has emerged in recent decades because incomes and food both come from agriculture
**This lesson's own level:** Grade 11 depth — it stands on the learner's own farm and fields, and on the course's earlier titles where named below.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
- Sub lesson 1 — Why agriculture must carry nutrition: **Because incomes and food both come from agriculture, the sector must now develop in ways that maximise health and nutrition gains** — the harvest is the diet, for farm families first of all.
- Sub lesson 2 — The food-based approach: **Nutrition-sensitive agriculture is the food-based approach that has emerged over recent decades: farming deliberately for nutrient-rich, diverse diets, not only for yield and income.
Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Food security is not nutrition security. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Define nutrition-sensitive agriculture: farming deliberately for nutrient-rich, diverse diets, not only yield and income.
2. Give its root reason: incomes and food both come from agriculture.
3. Say when it emerged: over recent decades, as a food-based approach.
