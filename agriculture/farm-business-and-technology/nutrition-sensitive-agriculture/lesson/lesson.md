# Nutrition-sensitive agriculture

## Do it first
Most farms are planned for yield and income. Plan the same farm a second way: for the nourishment of the family that eats from it. What changes — a garden? hens? a legume field? Draft the changes before the approach is named.

## Sub lesson 1 — Why agriculture must carry nutrition
**Because incomes and food both come from agriculture, the sector must now develop in ways that maximise health and nutrition gains** — the harvest is the diet, for farm families first of all.

A farm that sells everything nourishing and buys back staples has done the nutrition sum backwards.

[Picture 26413 — a farm plan drawn for nutrition — garden, poultry, legumes and staples together]

## Sub lesson 2 — The food-based approach
**Nutrition-sensitive agriculture is the food-based approach that has emerged over recent decades: farming deliberately for nutrient-rich, diverse diets, not only for yield and income.**

Yield and income stay — they are joined by a third target: what the farming puts on the family's plate, in all six groups.
