# Nutrition-sensitive agriculture — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Nutrition-sensitive agriculture is a food-based approach.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** It asks farmers to give up income entirely.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Agriculture should develop in ways that maximise health and nutrition gains.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A farm's own diet is none of agriculture's concern.
**Answer: False**
