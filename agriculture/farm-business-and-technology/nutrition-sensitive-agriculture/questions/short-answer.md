# Nutrition-sensitive agriculture — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Define nutrition-sensitive agriculture.
**Answer: The food-based approach of farming deliberately for nutrient-rich, diverse diets, not only for yield and income.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why must the agriculture sector carry nutrition?
**Answer: Because both incomes and food come from agriculture, so its development decides health and nutrition gains.**
