# Nutrition-sensitive agriculture — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Nutrition-sensitive agriculture farms deliberately for —
a) nutrient-rich, diverse diets  b) yield only  c) export crops only
**Answer: a) nutrient-rich, diverse diets**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** It has emerged over —
a) recent decades  b) a single season  c) ancient times only
**Answer: a) recent decades**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The approach is needed because —
a) incomes and food both come from agriculture  b) clinics are closing  c) farms grow no food
**Answer: a) incomes and food both come from agriculture**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Beside yield and income, the third target is —
a) what farming puts on the family's plate  b) farm size  c) market distance
**Answer: a) what farming puts on the family's plate**
