# Strategies for a diverse diet — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the five production strategies for a diverse diet.
**Answer: Mixed cropping; integrated farming systems; introducing new crops of high nutritional importance; poultry and fish farming; vegetable and fruit production.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** For what four purposes may a diet itself be modified?
**Answer: To correct nutritional status, include all nutrients, raise or lower energy, or change feeding intervals.**
