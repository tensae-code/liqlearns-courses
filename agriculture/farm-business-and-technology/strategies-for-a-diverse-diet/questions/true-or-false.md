# Strategies for a diverse diet — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Dietary diversity is low in Ethiopia.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Introducing new crops of high nutritional importance is a named strategy.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Changing feeding intervals is one reason to modify a diet.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Vegetable and fruit production does not widen dietary diversity.
