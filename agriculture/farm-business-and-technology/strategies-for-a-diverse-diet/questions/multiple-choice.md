# Strategies for a diverse diet — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Dietary diversity is —
a) the number of different foods or food groups eaten over a period  b) the weight of food eaten  c) the price of a diet
**Answer: a) the number of different foods or food groups eaten over a period**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Ethiopian diets rest mainly on —
a) starchy staples such as teff, maize and sorghum  b) meat and milk  c) imported foods
**Answer: a) starchy staples such as teff, maize and sorghum**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is a named diversification strategy?
a) poultry and fish farming  b) eating one staple only  c) skipping vegetables
**Answer: a) poultry and fish farming**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A diet may be modified to —
a) raise or lower energy  b) remove all nutrients  c) end all farming
**Answer: a) raise or lower energy**
