# Hands-on — Strategies for a diverse diet

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Basket building
Draw a week's market basket and fill it day by day with one new food group at a time, using only foods sold near you. Write the diversity count at the week's end.

## Strategy match
Match each of the five strategies to one household near you that already uses it — or could start it this season — and write the match.
