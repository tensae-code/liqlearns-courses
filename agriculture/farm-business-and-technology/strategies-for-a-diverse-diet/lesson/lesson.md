# Strategies for a diverse diet

## Do it first
Count the different foods in your last three days. Ethiopia's diets rest heavily on starchy staples — was yours wider? Before the strategies are named, list the ways a farm family could widen the basket without a bigger income.

## Sub lesson 1 — What dietary diversity is, and its Ethiopian gap
**Dietary diversity is the number of different foods or food groups eaten over a period.** It is **low in Ethiopia, where diets rest on starchy staples such as teff, maize and sorghum**.

The strategies are **food-based activities that widen availability and variety** — production changes first, because the basket fills at the farm and the market.

## Sub lesson 2 — The strategies, and modifying the diet
The named strategies: **mixed cropping, integrated farming systems, introducing new crops of high nutritional importance, poultry and fish farming, and vegetable and fruit production**.

And **the diet itself may be modified — to correct nutritional status, include all nutrients, raise or lower energy, or change feeding intervals**. Production widens the basket; modification aims it.
