# Nutrition — the basic words — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tell food and diet apart.
**Answer: Food is the culturally acceptable nutritious substance eaten or drunk; diet is the pattern — the way people feed themselves and the foodstuffs they use.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give the five body processes in the definition of nutrition.
**Answer: Ingests, digests, absorbs, assimilates and builds (from nutrients).**
