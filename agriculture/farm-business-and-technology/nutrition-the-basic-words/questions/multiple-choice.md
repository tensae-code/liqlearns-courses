# Nutrition — the basic words — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Nutrition is the science of how the body —
a) ingests, digests, absorbs, assimilates and builds from nutrients  b) grows crops  c) cooks food
**Answer: a) ingests, digests, absorbs, assimilates and builds from nutrients**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Food is —
a) any culturally acceptable nutritious substance eaten or drunk to maintain life and growth  b) only meat  c) only food from shops
**Answer: a) any culturally acceptable nutritious substance eaten or drunk to maintain life and growth**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Diet means —
a) the way people feed themselves and the foodstuffs they use  b) one single meal  c) a slimming plan only
**Answer: a) the way people feed themselves and the foodstuffs they use**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A nutrient is —
a) the useful part the body takes from food  b) a cooking pot  c) a food group name
**Answer: a) the useful part the body takes from food**
