# Nutrition — the basic words — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Nutrition covers digestion and absorption, not only eating.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Food and diet mean exactly the same thing.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The unit defines its terms before studying the nutrients themselves.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Malnutrition is a kind of nutrient.
**Answer: False**
