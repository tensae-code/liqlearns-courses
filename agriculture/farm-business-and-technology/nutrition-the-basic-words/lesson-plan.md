# Lesson plan — Nutrition — the basic words

**Course:** Agriculture · **Title:** Farm Business and Technology · **Lesson:** Nutrition — the basic words
**Place in the curriculum:** Agriculture — the Farm Business and Technology title (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the basic words of nutrition — nutrition, food, diet, nutrient and malnutrition — defined before the nutrients themselves are studied
**This lesson's own level:** Grade 11 depth — it stands on the learner's own farm and fields, and on the course's earlier titles where named below.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
- Sub lesson 1 — Nutrition, food, diet: **Nutrition is the science of how the body ingests, digests, absorbs, assimilates and builds from nutrients** — what happens between the plate and the body's growth.
- Sub lesson 2 — Nutrient and malnutrition: A **nutrient** is the useful part the body takes from food — the thing nutrition studies.
Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the Livestock title's feeding lessons. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Define nutrition as the science of how the body ingests, digests, absorbs, assimilates and builds from nutrients.
2. Tell food (a culturally acceptable nutritious substance) from diet (the way people feed themselves and the foodstuffs they use).
3. Place nutrient and malnutrition in the word family.

## What you need
One real meal to talk about — your own last meal, remembered in detail.

## If the learner is stuck
- Uses food and diet as the same word → food is the substance; diet is the pattern of feeding.
- Thinks nutrition is just eating → it is the whole science of what the body does with nutrients.
