# Nutrition — the basic words

## Do it first
Your last meal: the food on the plate, the diet it belongs to, and the nutrition happening inside you afterwards are three different ideas wearing similar clothes. Sort them before the definitions are given: which is the substance, which is the pattern, which is the science?

## Sub lesson 1 — Nutrition, food, diet
**Nutrition is the science of how the body ingests, digests, absorbs, assimilates and builds from nutrients** — what happens between the plate and the body's growth.

**Food is any culturally acceptable nutritious substance eaten or drunk to maintain life and growth.** **Diet is the way people feed themselves and the foodstuffs they use** — the pattern, not the single plate.

## Sub lesson 2 — Nutrient and malnutrition
A **nutrient** is the useful part the body takes from food — the thing nutrition studies. The unit's glossary defines its terms, **malnutrition** among them, before the nutrients are taken one by one.

Learn the words in this order and the rest of the food units open easily: food carries nutrients; diet is how we take food; nutrition is what the body does with it; malnutrition is what happens when that goes wrong.
