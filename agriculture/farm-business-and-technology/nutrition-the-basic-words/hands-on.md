# Hands-on — Nutrition — the basic words

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Meal labelling
Write down everything in one real meal. Label each item 'food', then write one line naming the diet it belongs to and one line on the nutrition it will do.

## Word hunt
Find the five lesson words used in a newspaper, radio talk or health poster's words as you hear them this week, and note each use.
