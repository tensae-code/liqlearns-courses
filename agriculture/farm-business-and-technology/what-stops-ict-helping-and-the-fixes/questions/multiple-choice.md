# What stops ICT helping — and the fixes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The overlooked drawback is —
a) capacity: knowing services exist and being able to use them  b) the colour of phones  c) too much information existing
**Answer: a) capacity: knowing services exist and being able to use them**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A service a farmer cannot operate —
a) helps a little automatically  b) helps the seller  c) helps exactly nobody
**Answer: c) helps exactly nobody**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Challenges are drawn from —
a) ocean trade  b) applying ICT in developing-country agriculture  c) space farming
**Answer: b) applying ICT in developing-country agriculture**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Solutions include —
a) extension demonstration and services fitted to real farmers  b) more expensive phones only  c) banning old phones
**Answer: a) extension demonstration and services fitted to real farmers**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Awareness is part of —
a) hardware  b) pricing  c) capacity
**Answer: c) capacity**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The course's closing point is —
a) ICT has no drawbacks  b) technology helps when people can use it  c) technology replaces farmers
**Answer: b) technology helps when people can use it**
