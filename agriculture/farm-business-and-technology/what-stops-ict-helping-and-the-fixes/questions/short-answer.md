# What stops ICT helping — and the fixes — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain the capacity problem.
**Answer: ICT can make tools and information available, but farmers must know the services exist and be able to use them; without that capacity, availability alone changes nothing.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How does the section treat the challenges?
**Answer: It works through the challenges of applying ICT in developing-country agriculture and pairs them with their solutions.**
