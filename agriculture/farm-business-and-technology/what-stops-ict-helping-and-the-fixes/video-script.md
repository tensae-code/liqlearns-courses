What stops ICT helping — and the fixes.
The drawbacks are faced squarely: capacity is the overlooked one — ICT may make tools and information available, but farmers must know they exist and be able to use them.
The section works through the challenges of applying ICT in developing-country agriculture and their solutions: access, awareness, skills and fit — each challenge answered by a fix, from extension demonstration to services designed for real farmers' phones and languages.
