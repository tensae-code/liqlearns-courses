# What stops ICT helping — and the fixes

## Do it first
A perfect price service exists — and a farmer three valleys away has never heard of it, and could not work it if she had. The last unit of the course ends with the honest problem: capacity.

## Sub lesson 1 — The drawbacks faced squarely
The drawbacks are faced squarely: **capacity is the overlooked one — ICT may make tools and information available, but farmers must know they exist and be able to use them**. A service nobody knows about, or cannot operate, helps exactly nobody.

[Picture 26688 — a farmer being shown how to use a phone service by an extension worker]

## Sub lesson 2 — Challenges and their solutions
The section works through the **challenges of applying ICT in developing-country agriculture and their solutions**: access, awareness, skills and fit — each challenge answered by a fix, from extension demonstration to services designed for real farmers' phones and languages. The course closes here: technology helps when people can use it.
