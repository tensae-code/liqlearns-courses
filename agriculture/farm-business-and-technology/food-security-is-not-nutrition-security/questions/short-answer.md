# Food security is not nutrition security — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** List the parts of nutrition security.
**Answer: Access to sufficient, safe and nutritious food; safe water and adequate sanitation; the ability to reach health care; knowledge of sound household and caring practices.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does the lesson separate the two ideas so carefully?
**Answer: Because food quantity alone does not guarantee nourishment — a household can be food-secure and still malnourished.**
