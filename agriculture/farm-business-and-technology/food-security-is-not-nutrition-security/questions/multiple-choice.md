# Food security is not nutrition security — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A household can be food-secure and still —
a) malnourished  b) free of all illness  c) unable to store grain
**Answer: a) malnourished**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Nutrition security requires food that is —
a) sufficient, safe and nutritious  b) only sufficient  c) imported
**Answer: a) sufficient, safe and nutritious**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is part of nutrition security?
a) safe water and adequate sanitation  b) a bigger grain store only  c) more land
**Answer: a) safe water and adequate sanitation**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Nutrition security also includes —
a) the ability to reach health care and knowledge of sound caring practices  b) only clinic medicine  c) only school feeding
**Answer: a) the ability to reach health care and knowledge of sound caring practices**
