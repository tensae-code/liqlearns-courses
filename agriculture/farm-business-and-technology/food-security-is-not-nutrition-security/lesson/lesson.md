# Food security is not nutrition security

## Do it first
Two households fill the same grain store. One eats that grain three times a day and nothing else; the other eats from all six groups, with clean water and a clinic nearby. Both have 'enough food'. Are both secure? Guess what the second word — nutrition — adds.

## Sub lesson 1 — Enough food is not the whole test
**Food security in quantity alone is not enough.** A full store answers the amount question and no other.

The lesson distinguishes the two ideas carefully **because a household can be food-secure and still malnourished** — fed in bulk, failed in nourishment.

[Picture 26412 — two households with the same full grain store — one eating one food only, one eating varied foods]

## Sub lesson 2 — What nutrition security adds
**Nutrition security means access to sufficient, safe and nutritious food, safe water and adequate sanitation, the ability to reach health care, and knowledge of sound household and caring practices.**

Count its parts: the food itself, the water and sanitation around it, the clinic within reach, and the knowledge in the home. Miss any one and nourishment can fail while the store stands full.

## Say it to your companion
Explain to your companion how a household can be food-secure and still malnourished, naming what nutrition security adds.
