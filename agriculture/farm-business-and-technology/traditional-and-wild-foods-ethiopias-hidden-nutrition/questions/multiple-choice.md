# Traditional and wild foods — Ethiopia's hidden nutrition — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Chuko is —
a) barley flour mixed with concentrated butter  b) a fermented drink  c) roasted teff
**Answer: a) barley flour mixed with concentrated butter**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Chuko is common in —
a) Oromia  b) the northern coast  c) Addis Ababa only
**Answer: a) Oromia**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Kocho is a food made from —
a) enset  b) barley  c) milk
**Answer: a) enset**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The named wild foods are rich in —
a) minerals  b) only water  c) starch only
**Answer: a) minerals**
