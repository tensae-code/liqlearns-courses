# Traditional and wild foods — Ethiopia's hidden nutrition — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What farming pattern does the lesson say Ethiopian farmers already practise?
**Answer: Mixed crop/livestock farming, producing plant and animal foods for home and market, with organic vegetable and fruit production spreading.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the three wild foods and the regions named for them.
**Answer: Bamboo shoots, stinging nettle and wild mushrooms — of the north-west, central highlands and south-west.**
