# Traditional and wild foods — Ethiopia's hidden nutrition — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Enset foods such as kocho are common in the south.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Organic vegetable and fruit production is spreading in Ethiopia.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Bamboo shoots and stinging nettle are named among the wild foods.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Any wild plant is safe to eat if it looks like a known one.
**Answer: False**
