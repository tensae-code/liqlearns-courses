# Hands-on — Traditional and wild foods — Ethiopia's hidden nutrition

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## A week's food diary (the book's own follow-up)
Keep a food diary for one week: every food, every day, marked traditional, wild, grown or bought. At the week's end, count how much of your nourishment the hidden foods carried.

## Market survey for untried foods (the book's own follow-up)
Survey a local market for foods you have never tried. Write each one's name, price and the group it belongs to — and ask the seller how it is prepared. Do not buy or eat anything you cannot identify with certainty.
