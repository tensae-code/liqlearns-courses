# The Grade 12 Agriculture syllabus — units and periods — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the syllabus give per unit?
**Answer: Learning outcomes, plus a table of competencies, main contents and learning strategies.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which unit dominates the year, and by how much?
**Answer: Unit 4 (Coffee, Tea and Spices) with 16 periods — more than double any other unit; the year totals 99 periods.**
