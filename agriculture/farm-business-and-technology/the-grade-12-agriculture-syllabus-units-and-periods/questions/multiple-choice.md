# The Grade 12 Agriculture syllabus — units and periods — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The syllabus is —
a) the official pacing framework  b) a student chapter  c) a price list
**Answer: a) the official pacing framework**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Per unit it gives —
a) only page numbers  b) only exam dates  c) outcomes, competencies, main contents and learning strategies
**Answer: c) outcomes, competencies, main contents and learning strategies**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The syllabus appears in —
a) neither book  b) the teacher guide, not the student book  c) the student book only
**Answer: b) the teacher guide, not the student book**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The year's total allocation is —
a) 99 periods  b) 50 periods  c) 200 periods
**Answer: a) 99 periods**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The flagship unit by periods is —
a) Unit 12 with 3  b) Unit 17 with 3  c) Unit 4, Coffee, Tea and Spices, with 16
**Answer: c) Unit 4, Coffee, Tea and Spices, with 16**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The smallest allocations (3 periods) go to —
a) Unit 11  b) Units 12, 15 and 17  c) Units 1 and 2
**Answer: b) Units 12, 15 and 17**
