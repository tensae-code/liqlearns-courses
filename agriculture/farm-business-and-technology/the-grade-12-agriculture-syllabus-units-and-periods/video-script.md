The Grade 12 Agriculture syllabus — units and periods.
The Agriculture Syllabus for Grade 12 is the official pacing framework, absent from the student book: per unit, learning outcomes plus a table of competencies, main contents and learning strategies (buzz groups, pair work, presentations with monitored feedback).
