# The Grade 12 Agriculture syllabus — units and periods

## Do it first
This lesson stands on teacher-guide evidence only. A syllabus is a budget of time: 99 periods for a year of agriculture. Where the periods go, the priorities are.

## Sub lesson 1 — The pacing framework
The **Agriculture Syllabus for Grade 12** is the official pacing framework, absent from the student book: per unit, **learning outcomes** plus a table of **competencies, main contents and learning strategies** (buzz groups, pair work, presentations with monitored feedback). It is the skeleton the whole Grade 12 build follows.

## Sub lesson 2 — The period allocation — coffee towers
The allocation reveals the year's priorities — Unit 1: 7 periods; Unit 2: 7; Unit 3: 5; **Unit 4 (Coffee, Tea and Spices): 16 — the flagship, more than double any other unit**; Unit 5: 4; Unit 6: 6; Unit 7: 6; Unit 8: 4; Unit 9: 6; Unit 10: 6; Unit 11: 8; Unit 12: 3; Unit 13: 6; Unit 14: 5; Unit 15: 3; Unit 16: 4; Unit 17: 3 — **99 periods in total**.

## Say it to your companion
Explain this lesson to your companion in your own words, using one real example from your own area for each sub lesson. Your companion asks one question about each part — answer from the lesson, not from memory of other lessons.
