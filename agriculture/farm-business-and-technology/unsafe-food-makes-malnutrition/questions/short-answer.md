# Unsafe food makes malnutrition — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Draw the linkage chain in words.
**Answer: Unsafe food → infections and disease → weakness and poor nutrient absorption → malnutrition, even where food is plentiful.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why can malnutrition exist where food is plentiful?
**Answer: Because unsafe food causes illness, and illness both weakens the person and makes the body absorb nutrients poorly.**
