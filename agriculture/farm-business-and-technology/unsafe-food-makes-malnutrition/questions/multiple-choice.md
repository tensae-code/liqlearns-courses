# Unsafe food makes malnutrition — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Unsafe food causes —
a) infections and diseases such as diarrhoea and meningitis  b) only stomach aches for an hour  c) nothing serious
**Answer: a) infections and diseases such as diarrhoea and meningitis**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Diarrhoea and meningitis from unsafe food can be —
a) improving  b) imaginary  c) life-threatening
**Answer: c) life-threatening**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Food safety, nutrition and public health form —
a) a market chain  b) one linked system  c) three unrelated topics
**Answer: b) one linked system**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Illness from unsafe food makes people —
a) weak, unable to work or concentrate  b) stronger  c) richer
**Answer: a) weak, unable to work or concentrate**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Some infections make the body —
a) absorb double  b) need no food  c) absorb nutrients poorly
**Answer: c) absorb nutrients poorly**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Unsafe food can cause malnutrition —
a) never  b) even where food is plentiful  c) only in famines
**Answer: b) even where food is plentiful**
