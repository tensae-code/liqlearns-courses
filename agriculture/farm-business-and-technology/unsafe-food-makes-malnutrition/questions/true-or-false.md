# Unsafe food makes malnutrition — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Food safety is a health intervention, not only a kitchen habit.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Malnutrition only comes from lack of food.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Poor absorption is a link between illness and malnutrition.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A sick person absorbs nutrients exactly as well as a healthy one.
**Answer: False**
