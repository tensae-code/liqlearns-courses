# Video script — Unsafe food makes malnutrition

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Unsafe food makes malnutrition.
Safe food, nutrition and public health are one linked system.
The sick are weak, cannot work or concentrate, and some infections make the body absorb nutrients poorly — so unsafe food manufactures malnutrition even where food is plentiful.
Do it yourself.
Then say it to your companion.
