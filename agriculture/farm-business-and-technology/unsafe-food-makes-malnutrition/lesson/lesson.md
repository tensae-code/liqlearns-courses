# Unsafe food makes malnutrition

## Do it first
A village has plenty of food, yet its children are malnourished. The missing link is not the harvest. Follow the arrow from a dirty plate to an empty body.

## Sub lesson 1 — The linkage drawn
**Safe food, nutrition and public health are one linked system.** Unsafe food causes **infections and diseases such as diarrhoea and meningitis, which can be life-threatening**. Food safety is therefore not a kitchen nicety — it is a health intervention on every plate.

[Picture 26684 — the linkage diagram — unsafe food → illness → poor absorption → malnutrition]

## Sub lesson 2 — How unsafe food manufactures malnutrition
The sick are **weak, cannot work or concentrate**, and some infections make the body **absorb nutrients poorly** — so **unsafe food manufactures malnutrition even where food is plentiful**. The chain runs: unsafe food → illness → poor absorption → malnutrition. Guarding food is feeding people twice: once with nutrients, once by letting the body keep them.
