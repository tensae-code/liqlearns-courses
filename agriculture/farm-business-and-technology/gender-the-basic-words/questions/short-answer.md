# Gender — the basic words — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Distinguish sex from gender.
**Answer: Sex is the biological difference; gender is the socially made roles and expectations built around it.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Define roles and equity in the unit's vocabulary.
**Answer: Roles: the work and duties assigned to women and men. Equity: fairness in access and treatment.**
