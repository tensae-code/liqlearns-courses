# Gender — the basic words — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Sex and gender are the same word in this unit.
**Answer: False**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Gender roles are socially made.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Roles describe work assigned to women and men.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Equity means treating fairness as irrelevant.
