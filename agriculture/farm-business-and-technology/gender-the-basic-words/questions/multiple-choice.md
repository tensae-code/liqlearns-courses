# Gender — the basic words — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Sex names —
a) the biological difference  b) social expectations  c) market roles
**Answer: a) the biological difference**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Gender names —
a) biology only  b) a crop type  c) the socially made roles and expectations
**Answer: c) the socially made roles and expectations**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The unit defines terms first so that —
a) roles never change  b) discussion uses the words precisely  c) exams are easier
**Answer: b) discussion uses the words precisely**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Roles are —
a) the work and duties assigned to women and men  b) biological facts  c) prices
**Answer: a) the work and duties assigned to women and men**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Equity means —
a) identical work for all  b) no differences anywhere  c) fairness in access and treatment
**Answer: c) fairness in access and treatment**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Equity is not merely —
a) access  b) sameness  c) fairness
**Answer: b) sameness**
