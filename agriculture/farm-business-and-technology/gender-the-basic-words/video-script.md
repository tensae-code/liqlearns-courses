# Video script — Gender — the basic words

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Gender — the basic words.
The basic gender-related terminologies are defined: sex names the biological difference; gender names the socially made roles and expectations built around it.
The working vocabulary continues: roles — the work and duties assigned to women and men — and equity — fairness in access and treatment, not merely sameness.
Do it yourself.
Then say it to your companion.
