# Gender — the basic words

## Do it first
Two farmers work the same field all day. At the market, one sells the harvest. At home, the other cooks it. Untangling who does what starts with exact words.

## Sub lesson 1 — Sex and gender — not the same word
The basic gender-related terminologies are defined: **sex** names the biological difference; **gender** names the socially made roles and expectations built around it. Discussion that mixes the two words argues past itself — the unit fixes the vocabulary first.

## Sub lesson 2 — Roles and equity
The working vocabulary continues: **roles** — the work and duties assigned to women and men — and **equity** — fairness in access and treatment, not merely sameness. With the words precise, the next lessons can map who does the work and who holds the power over its fruits.
