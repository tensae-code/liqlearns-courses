# Agriculture course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

Agriculture is one world course: the land, the crops and the
animals work the same everywhere; only the way of teaching
differs, and the way shows inside as examples. The path climbs
from the learner's own ground to the whole farm business.

**First Steps → Soil and Land → Crops → Livestock → Farm
Business and Technology**

The first stocked part is built from Ethiopia's Agriculture
books for Grades 11 and 12 — see CURRICULUM.md and ABOUT.md.
