# Adapting Ethiopian farming to climate change — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Adaptation means —
a) adjusting to the change already happening  b) stopping all emissions at once  c) measuring the climate
**Answer: a) adjusting to the change already happening**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Diversifying crops protects a farm because —
a) total loss becomes unlikely  b) every crop yields double  c) rain always comes
**Answer: a) total loss becomes unlikely**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Animals for a changing climate should be —
a) suited to hotter, drier conditions with lower productivity risk  b) the highest yielders at any risk  c) kept only indoors
**Answer: a) suited to hotter, drier conditions with lower productivity risk**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In mixed cropping, if one crop fails in drought —
a) the other may still yield, because tolerances differ  b) both always fail together  c) the field must be abandoned
**Answer: a) the other may still yield, because tolerances differ**
