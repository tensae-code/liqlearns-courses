# Adapting Ethiopian farming to climate change — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Mixed cropping is one of Ethiopia's adaptation strategies.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Adaptation means preventing any climate change from happening.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Selecting animals for hotter, drier conditions can lower productivity risk.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A single crop is the safest plan when rains become unreliable.
**Answer: False**
