# Adapting Ethiopian farming to climate change — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How does mixed cropping work as insurance?
**Answer: The crops differ in drought tolerance, so if one fails the other still yields — the family does not lose everything.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What animal strategy does the lesson name for Ethiopia?
**Answer: Selecting or breeding animals more suitable to hotter, drier conditions, with lower productivity risk.**
