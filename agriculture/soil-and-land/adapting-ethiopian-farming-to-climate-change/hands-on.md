# Hands-on — Adapting Ethiopian farming to climate change

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Risk-spread plan
For your family's or a neighbour's field, draw the present crop plan, then redraw it diversified for unreliable rain. Write what each added crop protects against.

## Animal suitability check
List the farm animals kept near you and mark which already suit hotter, drier conditions — and which would struggle. Base each mark on what you can observe.
