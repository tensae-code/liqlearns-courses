# Adapting Ethiopian farming to climate change

## Do it first
Mitigation slows the change; this lesson lives with it. If the rains of your area become less sure, what would you plant differently — and what animal would you choose? Draft the farm's answer before Ethiopia's strategies are named.

## Sub lesson 1 — Spread the risk across crops
**Adaptation means adjusting to the change that is already happening.** Ethiopia's strategies include **diversifying crops so that total loss is unlikely**.

In **mixed cropping the crops differ in drought tolerance — if one fails, the other still yields**. The mixed field of the cropping-systems lesson is an insurance policy: different crops, different weaknesses, so one bad season cannot take everything.

## Sub lesson 2 — Animals for a hotter, drier farm
The animal side adapts too: **selecting or breeding animals more suitable to hotter, drier conditions, with lower productivity risk**.

The lesson of the breeding units returns with a climate purpose — the animal that thrives in the new conditions, even at modest yield, beats the high-yielder that the heat defeats.
