# Indigenous soil and water conservation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The land arc closes with —
a) local conservation knowledge  b) a new chemical  c) import law
**Answer: a) local conservation knowledge**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Traditional practices are recorded as —
a) failed experiments  b) tourist scenery  c) proven conservation technology
**Answer: c) proven conservation technology**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The practices belong to —
a) textbook writers  b) Ethiopian communities  c) foreign projects only
**Answer: b) Ethiopian communities**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The Konso terraces return as —
a) a system  b) a single wall  c) a legend
**Answer: a) a system**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Konso terraces follow —
a) straight lines downhill  b) river banks  c) the hillside's contours
**Answer: c) the hillside's contours**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Indigenous knowledge in this course counts as —
a) a warning  b) evidence  c) decoration
**Answer: b) evidence**
