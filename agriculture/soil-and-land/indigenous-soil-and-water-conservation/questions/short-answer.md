# Indigenous soil and water conservation — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson record?
**Answer: The traditional soil and water conservation practices of Ethiopian communities, as proven conservation technology.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why do the Konso terraces return 'as a system'?
**Answer: Because they are not one wall but a whole maintained system — stone terraces on contours, kept by the community's organisation over generations.**
