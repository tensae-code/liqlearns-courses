# Indigenous soil and water conservation

## Do it first
Some Ethiopian terraces were old when the oldest living farmer's grandfather was born. The land arc closes where it must: by listening to the communities who conserved first.

## Sub lesson 1 — Local conservation knowledge
**Local conservation knowledge** closes the land arc: the **traditional practices of Ethiopian communities** recorded as **proven conservation technology**. These are not curiosities — they are working systems that have held soil on steep land for generations.

[Picture 26678 — stone terraces following a steep hillside's contours]

## Sub lesson 2 — The Konso terraces return
The **Konso terraces of Grade 11 return here as a system**: stone terraces following steep contours, maintained by the community's own organisation. The pattern of the whole course repeats — indigenous knowledge is evidence, and in conservation it is the oldest evidence a farmer in Ethiopia can stand on.
