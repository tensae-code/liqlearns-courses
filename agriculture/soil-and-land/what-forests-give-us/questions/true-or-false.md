# What forests give us — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Timber and construction materials are forest gifts.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Forests give nothing until they are cut down.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Aesthetic and spiritual value count among the forest's gifts.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The human relationship with forests is recent.
**Answer: False**
