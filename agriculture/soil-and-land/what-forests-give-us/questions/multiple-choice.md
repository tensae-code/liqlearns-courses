# What forests give us — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which is a product gift of the forest?
a) firewood and timber  b) rainfall tax  c) electricity
**Answer: a) firewood and timber**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Forest medicines come from —
a) bark, leaf and root  b) plastic  c) the market only
**Answer: a) bark, leaf and root**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Shade and wind protection are the forest's —
a) products  b) services  c) wastes
**Answer: b) services**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A standing forest, compared with a felled one —
a) pays once  b) pays every year in gifts and services  c) pays nothing
**Answer: b) pays every year in gifts and services**
