# Video script — Soil erosion — how soil leaves the farm

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Soil erosion — how soil leaves the farm.
Soil erosion is defined: the soil's removal from the land — the farm's capital carried away grain by grain and storm by storm.
Its major causes are worked through: water and wind on unprotected, sloping, overworked land.
Do it yourself.
Then say it to your companion.
