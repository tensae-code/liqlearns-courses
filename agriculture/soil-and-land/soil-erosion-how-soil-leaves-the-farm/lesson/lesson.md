# Soil erosion — how soil leaves the farm

## Do it first
After heavy rain, the stream below the fields runs brown. That colour is somebody's farm leaving. Trace the journey of a grain of topsoil from field to stream.

## Sub lesson 1 — Erosion defined
**Soil erosion** is defined: the soil's removal from the land — the farm's capital carried away grain by grain and storm by storm. Erosion is theft by weather, aided by exposure: soil that is covered and held stays; soil that is bare and loose leaves.

## Sub lesson 2 — The major causes
Its **major causes** are worked through: **water and wind** on **unprotected, sloping, overworked land**. Slope speeds the thief, bareness invites it, and overwork weakens the soil's hold on itself. Every cause named is a place where conservation can intervene.
