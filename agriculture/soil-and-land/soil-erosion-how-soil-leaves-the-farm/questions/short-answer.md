# Soil erosion — how soil leaves the farm — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Define soil erosion.
**Answer: The removal of soil from the land — the farm's topsoil capital carried away.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name erosion's major causes as the lesson gives them.
**Answer: Water and wind acting on unprotected, sloping, overworked land.**
