# Soil erosion — how soil leaves the farm — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Soil erosion is —
a) the removal of soil from the land  b) soil formation  c) soil testing
**Answer: a) the removal of soil from the land**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Erosion carries away —
a) only stones  b) river water  c) the farm's capital (topsoil)
**Answer: c) the farm's capital (topsoil)**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Soil that is covered and held —
a) turns to sand  b) stays  c) always leaves
**Answer: b) stays**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Major causes involve —
a) water and wind  b) only earthquakes  c) only animals
**Answer: a) water and wind**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Erosion strikes hardest on land that is —
a) flat and covered  b) resting  c) unprotected, sloping and overworked
**Answer: c) unprotected, sloping and overworked**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Slope affects erosion by —
a) protecting soil  b) speeding the water's theft  c) slowing all loss
**Answer: b) speeding the water's theft**
