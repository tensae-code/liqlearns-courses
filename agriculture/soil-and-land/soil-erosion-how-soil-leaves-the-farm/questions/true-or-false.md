# Soil erosion — how soil leaves the farm — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Wind can erode soil as well as water.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Bare soil is safer than covered soil.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Overworked land is among erosion's causes.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Erosion builds topsoil on farms.
**Answer: False**
