# Lesson plan — Choosing and managing agro-forestry trees

**Course:** Agriculture · **Title:** Soil and Land · **Lesson:** Choosing and managing agro-forestry trees
**Place in the curriculum:** Agriculture — the Soil and Land title (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Agro-forestry species are chosen for the system's jobs — each species planted where its products and services fit the farm's design: boundary, fodder …
**This lesson's own level:** Grade 12 depth — it stands on the Grade 11 Agriculture course where the same enterprise or system was opened, and on the learner's own farm and fields.
