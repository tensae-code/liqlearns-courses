# Choosing and managing agro-forestry trees

## Do it first
A tree in a system has a job description: hold the boundary, feed the goats in the dry season, shade the coffee. Choose the wrong species and the job stays undone for twenty years.

## Sub lesson 1 — Species for the system's jobs
**Agro-forestry species** are chosen for the **system's jobs** — each species planted where its products and services fit the farm's design: boundary, fodder bank, shade line, soil line. Species choice is the system's personnel decision.

## Sub lesson 2 — Establishing and managing them
Species are then **established and managed within the farm**: planted, protected, pruned and harvested as components of the working system — not as wild trees that happen to stand there. Management keeps each tree doing its job without stealing the crops' light, water or room beyond its wage.

## Say it to your companion

## Do It Yourself
1. Write job descriptions for three trees on one real farm.

3. List the management a young system tree needs in its first two years.
