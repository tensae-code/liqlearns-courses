# Choosing and managing agro-forestry trees — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Agro-forestry species are chosen for —
a) the system's jobs  b) their rarity  c) their height alone
**Answer: a) the system's jobs**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A fodder bank species is chosen to —
a) mark a boundary only  b) shade a house  c) feed animals in its season
**Answer: c) feed animals in its season**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Species choice is like —
a) a decoration  b) a personnel decision for the system  c) a lottery
**Answer: b) a personnel decision for the system**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Trees in a system are —
a) planted, protected, pruned and harvested as components  b) left entirely wild  c) never touched
**Answer: a) planted, protected, pruned and harvested as components**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Management keeps each tree —
a) shading the whole field  b) feeding on the crops  c) doing its job without over-stealing crop light, water or room
**Answer: c) doing its job without over-stealing crop light, water or room**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Establishment and management happen —
a) after harvest  b) within the farm system  c) in a distant forest only
**Answer: b) within the farm system**
