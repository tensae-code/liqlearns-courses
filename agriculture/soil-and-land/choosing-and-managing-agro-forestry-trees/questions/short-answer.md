# Choosing and managing agro-forestry trees — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How are agro-forestry species chosen?
**Answer: For the jobs the system needs done — boundary, fodder, shade, soil care — each species matched to its role.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does managing a system tree involve?
**Answer: Establishing it in the farm and then protecting, pruning and harvesting it as a working component, kept to its job.**
