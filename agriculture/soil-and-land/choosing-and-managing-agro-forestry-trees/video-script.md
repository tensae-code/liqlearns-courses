Choosing and managing agro-forestry trees.
Agro-forestry species are chosen for the system's jobs — each species planted where its products and services fit the farm's design: boundary, fodder bank, shade line, soil line.
Species are then established and managed within the farm: planted, protected, pruned and harvested as components of the working system — not as wild trees that happen to stand there.
Do it yourself.
Then say it to your companion.
