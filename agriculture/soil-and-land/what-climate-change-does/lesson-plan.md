# Lesson plan — What climate change does

**Course:** Agriculture · **Title:** Soil and Land · **Lesson:** What climate change does
**Place in the curriculum:** Agriculture — the Soil and Land title (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the cascading effects of climate change — changed rainfall, melting ice, rising seas, worse drought and acidifying oceans — and what they do to farming
**This lesson's own level:** Grade 11 depth — it stands on the learner's own farm and fields, and on the course's earlier titles where named below.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
- Sub lesson 1 — Rain, heat, ice and sea: The effects cascade.
- Sub lesson 2 — Farms feel it, oceans sour: For farming areas the changed rain, heat and drought strike germination, grazing and harvests — the effects on agriculture run through every earlier lesson of this course.
Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Climate change — what the words mean. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Trace the chain: warming → melting icecaps and glaciers → sea-level rise.
2. Explain why droughts worsen: heat increases evaporation.
3. State the ocean figure: about 30% of human-released carbon dioxide is absorbed by the oceans, causing acidification.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks sea-level rise is only about rain → it is melting land ice plus warming seas.
- Thinks oceans absorbing CO2 is purely good → the uptake acidifies the water.
