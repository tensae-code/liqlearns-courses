# Hands-on — What climate change does

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Cascade chain cards
Write each effect on a card — changed rain, melting ice, rising sea, worse drought, acid oceans — and lay them in cause order, drawing arrows. Photograph or copy the chain.

## Local effects interview
Ask a farmer or elder which of these effects they have seen locally — rain timing, dry spells, water sources — and write the answers beside the lesson's list.
