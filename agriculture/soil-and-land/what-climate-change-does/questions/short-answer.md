# What climate change does — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name three effects of climate change on the natural world.
**Answer: Changed rainfall, melting icecaps/glaciers, sea-level rise, worse droughts, ocean acidification (any three).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why is ocean acidification a problem, in the lesson's terms?
**Answer: The oceans take up more carbon dioxide as emissions rise and become more acidic, changing the water sea life lives in.**
