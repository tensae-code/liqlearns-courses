# What climate change does — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Droughts can worsen because heat increases evaporation from soils.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Rainfall amounts and patterns stay exactly the same under climate change.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Ocean acidification is caused by oceans absorbing carbon dioxide.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Climate change has no effects on agriculture.
**Answer: False**
