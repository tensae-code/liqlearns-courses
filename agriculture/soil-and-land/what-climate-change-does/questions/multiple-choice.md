# What climate change does — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Rising global temperatures melt —
a) icecaps and glaciers  b) only sea ice in winter  c) nothing
**Answer: a) icecaps and glaciers**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Sea-level rise follows from —
a) melting ice and warming seas  b) less rain everywhere  c) more evaporation only
**Answer: a) melting ice and warming seas**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Oceans absorb about how much of human-released carbon dioxide?
a) about 30%  b) about 3%  c) about 90%
**Answer: a) about 30%**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** As oceans take up more carbon dioxide they —
a) acidify  b) turn fresh  c) warm the icecaps
**Answer: a) acidify**
