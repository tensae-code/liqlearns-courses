# What climate change does

## Do it first
Follow one chain before it is taught: the Earth warms → ice melts → what happens to the sea? And the sea takes in more carbon dioxide → what happens to the water itself? Guess both ends of the chain.

## Sub lesson 1 — Rain, heat, ice and sea
The effects cascade. **Rainfall amounts and patterns change.** **Rising global temperatures melt icecaps and glaciers** — the polar ice is shrinking. Meltwater and warming seas bring **sea-level rise**, threatening low coasts.

**Droughts bite harder**: hotter air increases evaporation, drying soils faster even where rain totals barely change.

## Sub lesson 2 — Farms feel it, oceans sour
For farming areas the changed rain, heat and drought strike germination, grazing and harvests — the effects on agriculture run through every earlier lesson of this course.

And the oceans change chemistry: **oceans absorb about 30% of the carbon dioxide humans release**; as emissions rise the oceans take up more and **acidify** — ocean acidification, hard on shellfish and the sea life that depends on shells.
