# Agro-forestry — trees farming with crops — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Define agro-forestry.
**Answer: Trees deliberately grown with crops and/or animals on the same land, as one managed system.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does 'deliberately' matter in the definition?
**Answer: Because an accidental tree is not a system; agro-forestry is planned — components chosen and managed together.**
