# Agro-forestry — trees farming with crops — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Agro-forestry grows trees with —
a) crops and/or animals on the same land  b) nothing else ever  c) only other trees
**Answer: a) crops and/or animals on the same land**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The trees in agro-forestry are grown —
a) by accident  b) wild only  c) deliberately, as one managed system
**Answer: c) deliberately, as one managed system**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** An accidental tree in a field is —
a) a plantation  b) not yet agro-forestry  c) a full system
**Answer: b) not yet agro-forestry**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The unit defines —
a) agro-forestry, its systems, practices and technologies  b) only tree species  c) only pastures
**Answer: a) agro-forestry, its systems, practices and technologies**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Components share —
a) nothing  b) only the fence  c) the land and the management
**Answer: c) the land and the management**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The farmer in agro-forestry —
a) removes all trees  b) composes a system  c) abandons the field
**Answer: b) composes a system**
