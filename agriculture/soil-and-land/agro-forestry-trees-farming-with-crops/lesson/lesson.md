# Agro-forestry — trees farming with crops

## Do it first
One field, three citizens: trees standing, crops growing, animals grazing — all on the same land, all managed as one farm. Is that a forest, a farm, or both?

## Sub lesson 1 — The words defined
**Agro-forestry** is defined with its family of terms — **agro-forestry systems, practices and technologies**: trees **deliberately grown with crops and/or animals on the same land, as one managed system**. The key word is deliberately: an accidental tree in a field is not yet agro-forestry.

## Sub lesson 2 — One land, one system
The components share the land **and the management**: the trees are planned with the crops and animals, each part chosen for the whole. Agro-forestry is a design discipline — the farmer composes a system, not a crowd.
