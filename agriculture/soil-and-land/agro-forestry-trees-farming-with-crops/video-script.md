Agro-forestry — trees farming with crops.
Agro-forestry is defined with its family of terms — agro-forestry systems, practices and technologies: trees deliberately grown with crops and/or animals on the same land, as one managed system.
The components share the land and the management: the trees are planned with the crops and animals, each part chosen for the whole.
