# Managing the whole environment — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is environmental management?
**Answer: The unit's soil, water and forest practices drawn together — managing the environment as one whole system.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why must the aim carry both words, productive and intact?
**Answer: Productive without intact spends the base; intact without productive is unaffordable preservation — farming's base must keep yielding while staying whole.**
