# Managing the whole environment — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Environmental management treats the environment as —
a) separate, unrelated parts  b) one system  c) only air
**Answer: b) one system**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The practices drawn together are the unit's —
a) soil, water and forest management  b) marketing methods  c) breeding systems
**Answer: a) soil, water and forest management**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The aim is keeping the natural base —
a) productive only, whatever breaks  b) productive and intact  c) untouched and unusable
**Answer: b) productive and intact**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A terrace that saves soil but ruins a neighbour's water has —
a) managed the environment  b) moved the problem, not managed it  c) failed at terracing only
**Answer: b) moved the problem, not managed it**
