# Hands-on — Managing the whole environment

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Whole-valley survey
From one viewpoint, survey a valley: list the state of its soil, water and forest, and one care action for the whole, not the parts.

## Moved-problem hunt
Find or imagine one fix that moved a problem elsewhere (a drain flooding a neighbour). Redesign it as whole-system management.
