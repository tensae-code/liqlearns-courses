# Lesson plan — Managing the whole environment

**Course:** Agriculture · **Title:** Soil and Land · **Lesson:** Managing the whole environment
**Place in the curriculum:** Agriculture — the Soil and Land title (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** environmental management — the unit's soil, water and forest practices drawn together into care for the whole environment as one system, keeping the natural base farming depends on productive and intact
**This lesson's own level:** Grade 11 depth — it stands on the learner's own farm and fields, and on the course's earlier titles where named below.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
- Sub lesson 1 — One system, not separate parts: **The environment as a whole is managed as one system**: the practices of the unit are **drawn together into environmental management**.
- Sub lesson 2 — Keeping the base productive and intact: The aim is stated as stewardship: **keeping the whole natural base that farming depends on productive and intact** — productive (it must still yield food, fuel and income) and intact (it must still be whole for the next generation).
Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Managing soil, Managing farm water, Managing forests and wildlife. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say the environment is managed as one system, not as separate parts.
2. Name the practices drawn together: the unit's soil, water and forest management.
3. State the aim: keeping the whole natural base that farming depends on productive and intact.
