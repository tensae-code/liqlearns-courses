# Hands-on — Soil degradation — erosion and its cousins

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Gully walk
Walk to a gully or eroded bank (safely, from the edge). Sketch the profile it exposes and mark what was lost from the top.

## Compaction test
After rain, compare how a stick pushes into a garden bed vs a trampled path. The hard one shows physical degradation.
