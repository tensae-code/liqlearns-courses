# Soil degradation — erosion and its cousins — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three major forms of soil degradation.
**Answer: Erosion (water and wind); chemical degradation (acidification, salinisation, pollution); physical degradation such as compaction.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does erosion hurt so much, in profile terms?
**Answer: It removes the dark, fertile topsoil first — the layer that feeds the crop.**
