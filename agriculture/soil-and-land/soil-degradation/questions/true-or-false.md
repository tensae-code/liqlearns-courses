# Soil degradation — erosion and its cousins — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A gully is a form of water erosion's damage.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Degradation always means soil moving downhill.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Pollution is one member of chemical degradation.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Compacted soil lets roots and water enter easily.
**Answer: False**
