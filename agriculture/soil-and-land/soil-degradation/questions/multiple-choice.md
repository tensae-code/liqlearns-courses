# Soil degradation — erosion and its cousins — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Soil degradation is —
a) a long-term fall in soil productivity  b) one bad harvest  c) soil resting
**Answer: a) a long-term fall in soil productivity**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Erosion carries soil away by —
a) water and wind  b) earthworms  c) planting
**Answer: a) water and wind**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Acidification and salinisation belong to —
a) physical degradation  b) chemical degradation  c) erosion
**Answer: b) chemical degradation**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Compaction is an example of —
a) chemical degradation  b) physical degradation  c) pollution
**Answer: b) physical degradation**
