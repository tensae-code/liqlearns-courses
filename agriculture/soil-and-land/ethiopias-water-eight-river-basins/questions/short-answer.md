# Ethiopia's water — eight river basins — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Trace the simplified water cycle.
**Answer: Water evaporates, gathers as cloud, falls as rain, and runs back through rivers.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What makes Ethiopia a 'water tower'?
**Answer: Its eight river basins (plus one lake basin) rise in its highlands and flow outward — a highland source for a continent's rivers.**
