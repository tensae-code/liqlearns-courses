# Ethiopia's water — eight river basins — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The water cycle runs —
a) evaporation, cloud, rain, river  b) river, desert, sea, stone  c) rain only
**Answer: a) evaporation, cloud, rain, river**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In the water cycle, water is —
a) made fresh each rain  b) never made or lost, only moved around the circle  c) used up by rivers
**Answer: b) never made or lost, only moved around the circle**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Ethiopia has —
a) eight river basins and one lake basin  b) one river basin  c) eighty river basins
**Answer: a) eight river basins and one lake basin**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Ethiopia is called the water tower of Africa because —
a) it has the tallest dam  b) its highland basins' sources flow outward to the continent  c) it rains all year everywhere
**Answer: b) its highland basins' sources flow outward to the continent**
