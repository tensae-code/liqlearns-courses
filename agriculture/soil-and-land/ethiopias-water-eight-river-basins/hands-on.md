# Hands-on — Ethiopia's water — eight river basins

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Cycle in a jar
Put warm water in a jar with a cold lid: watch evaporation rise and 'rain' drip from the lid — the cycle on a table.

## Flow-direction mapping
On an outline map of Ethiopia, draw the highlands at the centre and arrows flowing outward. Label it: the water tower.
