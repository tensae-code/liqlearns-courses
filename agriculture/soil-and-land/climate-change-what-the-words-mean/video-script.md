# Video script — Climate change — what the words mean

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Climate change: the pattern itself moves, over decades.
Variability: the swing — a wet year, a dry year.
Extremes come more often: flood, drought, wildfire.
Mainly human causes: fuels, farming, land-use change.
