# Hands-on — Climate change — what the words mean

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Thirty-year weather line
On paper, draw a line for thirty years of rains as elders remember them — good years, dry years — and mark where the pattern itself seems to move. Label the swings 'variability' and any long drift 'change'.

## Cause hunt at home
List the things your household and farm do that burn fuel or change land. Mark which the lesson names as causes of climate change.
