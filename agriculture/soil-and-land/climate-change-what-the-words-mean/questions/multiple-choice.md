# Climate change — what the words mean — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Climate change means —
a) a change over decades in temperatures and weather patterns  b) one hot week  c) one rainy day
**Answer: b) one hot week**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Climate variability is —
a) the shorter-term swing around the pattern  b) a change over decades  c) the warming of the Earth only
**Answer: a) the shorter-term swing around the pattern**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A main human cause of climate change is —
a) burning fossil fuels  b) planting trees  c) terracing fields
**Answer: a) burning fossil fuels**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Land-use change matters because —
a) it is one of the mainly human causes of climate change  b) it stops all rain  c) it cools the Earth
**Answer: a) it is one of the mainly human causes of climate change**
