# Climate change — what the words mean — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tell climate change and climate variability apart in one sentence each.
**Answer: Climate change is the decades-long shift in temperatures and weather patterns; variability is the shorter-term swing around the pattern.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name four extremes that climate change makes more frequent.
**Answer: Any four of: unusual floods, long droughts, wildfires, unusual snowfalls, intense hurricanes, the warming of the Earth.**
