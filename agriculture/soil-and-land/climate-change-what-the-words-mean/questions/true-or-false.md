# Climate change — what the words mean — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A single cold morning proves the climate is changing.
**Answer: False**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Climate change makes extremes such as floods and long droughts more frequent.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Agriculture is named among the mainly human causes of climate change.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Fossil-fuel burning has nothing to do with climate change.
**Answer: False**
