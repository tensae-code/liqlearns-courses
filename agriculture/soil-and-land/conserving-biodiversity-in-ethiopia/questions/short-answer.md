# Conserving biodiversity in Ethiopia — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What two things does conservation protect?
**Answer: Habitats, and genetic resources.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why keep life in two keeping-places?
**Answer: Each covers the other's failure: a collection cannot adapt where it sits, and a living place can be destroyed — together they insure the variety.**
