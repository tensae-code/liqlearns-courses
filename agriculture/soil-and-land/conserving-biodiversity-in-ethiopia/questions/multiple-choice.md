# Conserving biodiversity in Ethiopia — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Conservation measures are paired to —
a) the threats they answer  b) market prices  c) the seasons only
**Answer: a) the threats they answer**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Protecting habitats answers the threat of —
a) habitat destruction  b) drought only  c) invasive labels
**Answer: a) habitat destruction**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Genetic resources are kept —
a) only in fields  b) in place and in collections  c) only in books
**Answer: b) in place and in collections**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Ethiopia's conservation status is framed against —
a) no pressures  b) the named pressures on its forests and species  c) foreign forests
**Answer: b) the named pressures on its forests and species**
