# Conserving biodiversity in Ethiopia — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A seed collection cannot adapt to a changing climate the way a living population can.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Keeping life in place alone is enough; collections add nothing.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Farm-scale management of soil, water and forest is part of conservation in practice.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Once a conservation measure exists, the pressures disappear.
**Answer: False**
