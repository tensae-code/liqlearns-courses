# Hands-on — Conserving biodiversity in Ethiopia

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Kept-place visit
Visit a locally kept place (church forest or enclosure). List what lives there that has vanished outside it — in-place conservation made visible.

## Seed-keeping plan
Plan a household seed collection for three local varieties: how dried, labelled, stored, and when grown out to stay alive.
