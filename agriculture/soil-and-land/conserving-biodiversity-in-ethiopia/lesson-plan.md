# Lesson plan — Conserving biodiversity in Ethiopia

**Course:** Agriculture · **Title:** Soil and Land · **Lesson:** Conserving biodiversity in Ethiopia
**Place in the curriculum:** Agriculture — the Soil and Land title (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** conservation measures answering the threats — protecting habitats and genetic resources in place and in collections — and the reported status of biodiversity conservation in Ethiopia, whose forests and species stand under the named pressures
**This lesson's own level:** Grade 11 depth — it stands on the learner's own farm and fields, and on the course's earlier titles where named below.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
- Sub lesson 1 — Protecting habitats and genetic resources: **Conservation measures answer the threats** — the unit pairs them deliberately: habitat destruction is answered by **protecting habitats**, and the loss of genetic resources by **protecting genetic resources** themselves.
- Sub lesson 2 — Two keeping-places, and Ethiopia's status: Life is kept in **two keeping-places: in place** — in the habitats and farms where it lives and adapts — **and in collections**, where seeds, genes and specimens are held against disaster.
Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: What threatens biodiversity. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say conservation answers the threats by protecting habitats and genetic resources.
2. Distinguish the two keeping-places: in place (where life lives) and in collections.
3. Report the framing of Ethiopia's status: conservation stands against the named pressures on its forests and species.

## What you need
The lesson picture of a protected forest on Ethiopia's map; knowledge of any local protected place (church forest, park, enclosure).
