# What agro-forestry gives a farm

## Do it first
A crop pays once a year. A tree pays in fruit, fodder, shade, timber and soil care — in different months, for different needs. Count what one good tree gives a farm.

## Sub lesson 1 — The tree component's gifts
The **significance of agro-forestry** is what the tree component adds: **products** of many kinds, from the same land that already grows crops and carries animals. The tree is a second, third and fourth enterprise standing inside the first one.

[Picture 26670 — a farm with tree products, shade and fodder all labelled from the same trees]

## Sub lesson 2 — Protection and spread income
Trees add **soil protection** and **microclimate** care — shade, shelter, held soil — and they **spread income across seasons and years**: when the crop is not paying, the tree may be. Significance is measured in risk spread as much as in products listed.
