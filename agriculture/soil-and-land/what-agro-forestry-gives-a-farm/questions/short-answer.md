# What agro-forestry gives a farm — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** List what the tree component adds to a farm.
**Answer: Products of many kinds, soil protection, microclimate benefits (shade and shelter), and income spread across seasons and years.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain 'risk spread' in agro-forestry.
**Answer: Because trees pay at different times from crops, a bad season for one component need not be a bad year for the whole farm.**
