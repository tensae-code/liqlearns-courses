# What agro-forestry gives a farm — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Trees can give several products from the same land as crops.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Agro-forestry trees reduce a farm's income spread.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Soil protection is part of agro-forestry's significance.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Trees pay only at their final felling.
**Answer: False**
