# What agro-forestry gives a farm — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The tree component adds —
a) products, soil protection and microclimate benefits  b) only shade  c) only timber
**Answer: a) products, soil protection and microclimate benefits**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A tree inside a crop field is —
a) a weed  b) a boundary post  c) another enterprise on the same land
**Answer: c) another enterprise on the same land**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Soil protection from trees means —
a) faster erosion  b) held, covered soil  c) bare soil
**Answer: b) held, covered soil**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Income from trees is spread —
a) across seasons and years  b) into one week  c) to buyers only
**Answer: a) across seasons and years**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Microclimate benefits include —
a) frost pockets  b) wind damage  c) shade and shelter
**Answer: c) shade and shelter**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Significance is measured also in —
a) leaf colour  b) risk spread  c) tree height only
**Answer: b) risk spread**
