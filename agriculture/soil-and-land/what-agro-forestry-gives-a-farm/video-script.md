What agro-forestry gives a farm.
The significance of agro-forestry is what the tree component adds: products of many kinds, from the same land that already grows crops and carries animals.
Trees add soil protection and microclimate care — shade, shelter, held soil — and they spread income across seasons and years: when the crop is not paying, the tree may be.
