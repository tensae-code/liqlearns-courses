# What biodiversity does for us — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How does biodiversity underpin food and farming?
**Answer: It holds the genetic resources — landraces, wild relatives, local breeds — that crops and livestock are bred from.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name biodiversity's wider values beyond farming.
**Answer: Ecosystem services, materials, and the national economy.**
