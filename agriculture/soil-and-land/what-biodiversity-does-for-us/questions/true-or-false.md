# What biodiversity does for us — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Local breeds are part of the genetic library breeders draw on.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Once a variety is improved, its wild relatives are useless.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Ecosystem services are among biodiversity's values.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Conservation benefits only wild animals, never farmers.
**Answer: False**
