# What biodiversity does for us — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Crops and livestock are bred from —
a) genetic resources held in biodiversity  b) factory parts  c) imported genes only
**Answer: a) genetic resources held in biodiversity**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A wild relative of a crop may hold —
a) nothing useful  b) genes for resisting a future disease or drought  c) only weeds
**Answer: b) genes for resisting a future disease or drought**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Biodiversity's values include —
a) ecosystem services, materials and the national economy  b) only beauty  c) only tourism
**Answer: a) ecosystem services, materials and the national economy**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The book frames conservation as —
a) charity  b) self-interest  c) punishment
**Answer: b) self-interest**
