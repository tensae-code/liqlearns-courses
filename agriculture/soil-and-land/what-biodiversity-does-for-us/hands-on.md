# Hands-on — What biodiversity does for us

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Dinner genealogy
Pick tonight's main dish and draw its family tree back to landraces and wild relatives as far as family knowledge goes.

## Library shelf
Draw the 'breeding library' as shelves: crop landraces, wild relatives, local breeds, microbes — one named example per shelf from your area.
