# Hands-on — Indigenous knowledge — caring for land the local way

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Resource survey walk
Walk your surroundings with the survey notebook: every water source and wooded place, its management, its state — the lesson's set field task.

## Bund line check
Find a stone bund line (North Shewa style) and walk it: is it on the contour, is it maintained, where has it broken? Report as an inspector.
