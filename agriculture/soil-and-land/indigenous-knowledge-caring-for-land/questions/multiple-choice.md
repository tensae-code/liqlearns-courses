# Indigenous knowledge — caring for land the local way — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Indigenous knowledge contributes directly to —
a) conserving Ethiopia's diverse natural vegetation  b) replacing all science  c) nothing measurable
**Answer: a) conserving Ethiopia's diverse natural vegetation**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The book's example is farmers in — applying indigenous soil and water conservation.
a) North Shewa  b) the coast  c) another country
**Answer: a) North Shewa**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The field-work survey asks pupils to —
a) list local water and forest resources, the management in use, and judge the practice  b) memorise a poem  c) avoid their surroundings
**Answer: a) list local water and forest resources, the management in use, and judge the practice**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** North Shewa's indigenous practices fight —
a) land degradation  b) rainfall  c) harvesting
**Answer: a) land degradation**
