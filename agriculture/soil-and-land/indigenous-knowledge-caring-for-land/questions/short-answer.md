# Indigenous knowledge — caring for land the local way — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does indigenous knowledge do for natural vegetation?
**Answer: It contributes directly to conserving Ethiopia's diverse natural vegetation across its altitudes and climates.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the three parts of the field-work survey.
**Answer: List local water and forest resources; record the management in use; judge the community's practice.**
