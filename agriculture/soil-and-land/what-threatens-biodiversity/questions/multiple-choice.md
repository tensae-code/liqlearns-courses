# What threatens biodiversity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Land-use change threatens biodiversity because converting land to cultivation —
a) destroys habitats  b) creates habitats  c) has no effect
**Answer: a) destroys habitats**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Fertiliser-rich eroded soil in water harms —
a) only stones  b) aquatic animals such as fish, to extinction  c) nothing
**Answer: b) aquatic animals such as fish, to extinction**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is among Ethiopia's named pressures?
a) poor regeneration and illegal tree harvest  b) over-planting  c) too much rain
**Answer: a) poor regeneration and illegal tree harvest**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Invasive species threaten biodiversity by —
a) spreading into new ground and crowding out natives  b) staying in one garden  c) feeding all natives
**Answer: a) spreading into new ground and crowding out natives**
