# What threatens biodiversity — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Population growth raises demand for food and services, pressing biodiversity harder.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Pollution cannot cause extinction.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Forest fires are among Ethiopia's threats to tree species.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A forest is safe as long as cutting stops, even if nothing regrows.
**Answer: False**
