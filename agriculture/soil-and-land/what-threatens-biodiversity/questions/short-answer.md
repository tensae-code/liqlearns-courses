# What threatens biodiversity — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the four general threats.
**Answer: Land-use change, pollution, population growth raising demand, and invasive species.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** List Ethiopia's specific pressures on biodiversity.
**Answer: Deforestation, habitat destruction, poor regeneration, clearing forest for agriculture, forest fires and illegal tree harvest.**
