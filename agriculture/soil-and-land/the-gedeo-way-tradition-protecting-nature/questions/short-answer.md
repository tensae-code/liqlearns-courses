# The Gedeo way — tradition protecting nature — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does Gedeo tradition protect?
**Answer: Nature — mountains, trees, hillsides and riversides — respected and protected by tradition.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does the climate unit end with the Gedeo example?
**Answer: Because the tradition is a working mitigation system built over hundreds of years — local knowledge as a real climate strategy.**
