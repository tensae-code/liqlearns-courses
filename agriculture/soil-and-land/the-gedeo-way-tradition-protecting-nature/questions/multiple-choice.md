# The Gedeo way — tradition protecting nature — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Gedeo community lives in —
a) southern Ethiopia  b) the northern coast  c) central Addis Ababa
**Answer: a) southern Ethiopia**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Gedeo tradition, nature is —
a) respected and protected  b) sold to the highest bidder  c) cleared for farms
**Answer: a) respected and protected**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Gedeo system counts as mitigation because —
a) protected trees and hillsides hold carbon for centuries  b) it uses imported machinery  c) it bans all farming
**Answer: a) protected trees and hillsides hold carbon for centuries**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The tradition has been built over —
a) hundreds of years  b) one project season  c) a single decade
**Answer: a) hundreds of years**
