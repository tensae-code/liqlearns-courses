# Hands-on — Soil — the resource under our feet

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Profile sketch
Sketch a real soil profile from a pit or cutting, layers shaded, with one job written on each layer.

## Spadeful census
Spread one spadeful of topsoil on a cloth. Count roots, creatures, stones and crumbs of old manure — the resource at work.
