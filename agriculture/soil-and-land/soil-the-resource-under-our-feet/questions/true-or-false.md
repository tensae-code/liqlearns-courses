# Soil — the resource under our feet — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Soil belongs on the natural-resources list beside forests and water.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Crops grow independently of the soil beneath them.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Erosion removes the soil's deepest layers first.
**Answer: False**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Looking at a soil pit teaches how the resource is built.
**Answer: True**
