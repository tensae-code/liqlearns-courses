# Soil — the resource under our feet — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In this unit, soil is introduced as —
a) waste material  b) a natural resource in its own right  c) a building material only
**Answer: b) a natural resource in its own right**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** For farming, soil is —
a) the base on which crops grow  b) an obstacle to crops  c) unimportant beside rain
**Answer: a) the base on which crops grow**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A soil profile shows —
a) one uniform layer  b) layers, dark topsoil over paler subsoil  c) only stones
**Answer: b) layers, dark topsoil over paler subsoil**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The top layer of the profile mainly —
a) feeds the crop  b) holds the road  c) stores tools
**Answer: a) feeds the crop**
