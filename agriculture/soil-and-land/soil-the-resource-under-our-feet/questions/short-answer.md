# Soil — the resource under our feet — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does the unit introduce soil as a resource in its own right?
**Answer: Because it is the base on which crops grow — a natural stock farming draws on, which can be spent or kept like forests and water.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What do you see in a soil profile?
**Answer: Layers — dark, root-filled topsoil over paler subsoil; the top feeds the crop, the lower layers hold water and anchor roots.**
