# Why resources must be managed — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** State the finite fact and its consequence.
**Answer: Many resources, especially coal, natural gas and mineral ores, are finite; failure to use them wisely depletes them in a short time.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does this lesson give the rest of the unit?
**Answer: The concepts and objectives that the following management practices (soil, water, forest, environment) serve.**
