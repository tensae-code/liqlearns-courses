# Why resources must be managed — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Coal, natural gas and mineral ores are examples of resources that —
a) exist in finite quantities  b) regrow yearly  c) are manufactured
**Answer: a) exist in finite quantities**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Failure to use finite resources wisely —
a) has no effect  b) depletes them in a short time  c) multiplies them
**Answer: b) depletes them in a short time**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson sets out —
a) only techniques  b) the concepts and objectives the practices serve  c) prices
**Answer: b) the concepts and objectives the practices serve**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A practice truly manages a resource only if —
a) it is new  b) the stock lasts for this generation and the next  c) it is imported
**Answer: b) the stock lasts for this generation and the next**
