# Forests and wildlife in decline — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** List the drivers of forest degradation the book names.
**Answer: Increasing fires and disease, logging (clearing for firewood and timber), and climate change.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How does wildlife degradation show itself?
**Answer: In declining animal populations — fewer animals and kinds where they were once common.**
