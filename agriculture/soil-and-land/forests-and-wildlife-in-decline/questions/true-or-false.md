# Forests and wildlife in decline — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Climate change is among the pressures degrading forests.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Degradation only counts when a forest is completely cleared.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** An animal's population falls when its habitat shrinks.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Wildlife can thrive indefinitely without habitat.
**Answer: False**
