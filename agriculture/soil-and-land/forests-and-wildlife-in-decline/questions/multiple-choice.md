# Forests and wildlife in decline — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Since the 1960s, the share of the world's tropical forests degraded is —
a) a tenth  b) over half  c) none
**Answer: b) over half**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which is NOT a named driver of forest degradation?
a) increasing fires and disease  b) logging for firewood and timber  c) tree planting
**Answer: c) tree planting**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Wildlife degradation shows itself in —
a) declining animal populations  b) animals growing larger  c) more species everywhere
**Answer: a) declining animal populations**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Forest and wildlife decline are driven together by —
a) habitat loss  b) rainfall  c) market prices
**Answer: a) habitat loss**
