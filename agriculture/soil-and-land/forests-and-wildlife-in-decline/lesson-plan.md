# Lesson plan — Forests and wildlife in decline

**Course:** Agriculture · **Title:** Soil and Land · **Lesson:** Forests and wildlife in decline
**Place in the curriculum:** Agriculture — the Soil and Land title (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** degradation of forests and wildlife — over half the world's tropical forests degraded since the 1960s through fires, disease, logging and climate change — and wildlife decline driven with it by habitat loss
**This lesson's own level:** Grade 11 depth — it stands on the learner's own farm and fields, and on the course's earlier titles where named below.

## Sub lessons
- Sub lesson 1 — Forests degraded — the drivers: The scale first: **over half the world's tropical forests have been degraded since the 1960s**.
- Sub lesson 2 — Wildlife declines with its home: **Wildlife degradation shows itself in declining animal populations** — fewer animals, fewer kinds, in the places they were common.
Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: What forests give us. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the scale: over half the world's tropical forests have been degraded since the 1960s.
2. List the forest degradation drivers named: increasing fires and disease, logging (clearing for firewood and timber), and climate change.
3. Say how wildlife decline shows itself (declining animal populations) and what drives both declines together: habitat loss.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks decline means only clear-felling → degradation includes thinning and sickening under fire, disease, logging and climate pressure.
- Separates wildlife decline from forest decline → habitat loss drives both together.
