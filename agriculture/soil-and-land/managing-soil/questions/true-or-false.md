# Managing soil — structure and fertility — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Physical structures and cover help hold soil against erosion.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Compaction helps rain enter the soil.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Legumes in a cereal planting raise the soil's organic matter and nitrogen.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Inorganic fertiliser alone builds lasting soil wealth.
**Answer: False**
