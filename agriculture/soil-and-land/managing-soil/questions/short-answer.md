# Managing soil — structure and fertility — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why do erosion control and fertility building belong together?
**Answer: Because compacted, bare soil both loses its topsoil to runoff and starves; structures and cover hold the soil while organic matter rebuilds its fertility.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** State inorganic fertiliser's trade in one line.
**Answer: A faster fertility lift, but shorter-lived than organic matter's lasting build.**
