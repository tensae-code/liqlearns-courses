# Managing soil — structure and fertility — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Repeated cultivation compacts soil so that rain —
a) enters deeply  b) runs off instead of entering  c) never falls
**Answer: b) runs off instead of entering**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Soil management answers with —
a) erosion control and fertility building together  b) more traffic  c) leaving soil bare
**Answer: a) erosion control and fertility building together**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which raises organic matter?
a) legumes in cereal plantings, manure and compost  b) burning residues  c) compacting the field
**Answer: a) legumes in cereal plantings, manure and compost**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Inorganic fertiliser's fertility lift is —
a) slower and permanent  b) faster but shorter-lived  c) imaginary
**Answer: b) faster but shorter-lived**
