# Hands-on — Managing soil — structure and fertility

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Runoff race
On a slope, pour equal water above a bare strip and a covered (grass/mulch) strip. Time the runoff and catch what each carries.

## Compost start
Start a small compost heap: layers of dry matter, green matter, manure if available, soil. Note the recipe — this is fertility being manufactured slowly.
