# Hands-on — Managing farm water — harvesting the rain

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Gutter watch
In the next rain, watch your roof's drip line. Mark where a gutter and tank would sit, and what garden they would serve.

## Bottle irrigation trial
Sink a pierced bottle beside one plant in a dry spell, filled from stored water. Compare it with an unwatered twin after a week.
