# Managing farm water — harvesting the rain — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is harvested rainwater, in one sentence?
**Answer: Rain and stored water conserved — in ponds, tanks or from roofs — to be used in times of shortage.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** State the carbon bonus of good water practice.
**Answer: It reduces carbon loss and improves the soil's carbon stock.**
