# Managing farm water — harvesting the rain — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Harvesting moves water across time; it does not create water.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Roofs are useless as water catchments.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Stored water can carry a garden through a short drought.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Water management and soil carbon have nothing to do with each other.
**Answer: False**
