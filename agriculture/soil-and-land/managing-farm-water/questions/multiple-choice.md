# Managing farm water — harvesting the rain — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Water management conserves —
a) rain and stored water for times of shortage  b) only river water  c) water by creating it
**Answer: a) rain and stored water for times of shortage**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Rainwater harvesting in the book is pictured in —
a) eastern Ethiopia  b) Europe  c) the coast only
**Answer: a) eastern Ethiopia**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Excess water collected from house roofs can —
a) irrigate through short droughts  b) never be used  c) only wash roofs
**Answer: a) irrigate through short droughts**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Good water practice also —
a) increases carbon loss  b) reduces carbon loss and improves soil carbon stock  c) has no soil effect
**Answer: b) reduces carbon loss and improves soil carbon stock**
