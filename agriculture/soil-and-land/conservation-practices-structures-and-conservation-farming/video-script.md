Conservation practices — structures and conservation farming.
The practice families include the physical structures — bunds, terraces and their kin — built lines that slow water, hold soil and step a slope into workable ground.
Beside them stands conservation tillage / conservation farming: practices conserving soil, water and soil moisture, enhancing fertiliser and seed use and saving time and money — under three principles: minimum soil disturbance; crop residue management (leaving residues on the surface); and rotation/diversification.
