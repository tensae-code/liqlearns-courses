# Conservation practices — structures and conservation farming — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Conservation farming can save time and money as well as soil.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Conservation tillage means ploughing as deeply as possible.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Leaving residues on the surface is a conservation principle.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A farm must choose either structures or conservation farming, never both.
**Answer: False**
