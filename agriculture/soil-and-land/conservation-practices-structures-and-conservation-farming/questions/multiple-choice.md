# Conservation practices — structures and conservation farming — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Bunds and terraces are —
a) physical conservation structures  b) types of fertiliser  c) crop varieties
**Answer: a) physical conservation structures**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Structures work by —
a) raising rainfall  b) removing slopes  c) slowing water and holding soil
**Answer: c) slowing water and holding soil**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Terraces turn a slope into —
a) a pond  b) stepped, workable ground  c) a road
**Answer: b) stepped, workable ground**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Conservation farming's first principle is —
a) minimum soil disturbance  b) deep ploughing  c) burning residues
**Answer: a) minimum soil disturbance**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Crop residue management means —
a) removing all stalks  b) feeding all residues to stock  c) leaving residues on the surface
**Answer: c) leaving residues on the surface**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The third principle is —
a) fallow only  b) rotation / diversification  c) single cropping forever
**Answer: b) rotation / diversification**
