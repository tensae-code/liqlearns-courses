# Conservation practices — structures and conservation farming — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name conservation farming's three principles.
**Answer: Minimum soil disturbance; crop residue management (leaving residues on the surface); rotation/diversification.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Contrast the two practice families.
**Answer: Structures (bunds, terraces) are built lines that slow water and hold soil; conservation farming disturbs the soil minimally, keeps residues on the surface and rotates/diversifies crops.**
