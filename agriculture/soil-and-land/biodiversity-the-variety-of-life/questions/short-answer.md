# Biodiversity — the variety of life — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Define biodiversity in one sentence.
**Answer: The range of all forms of life on Earth — plants, animals, humans, bacteria and other organisms — counted as species, sub-species and communities in a habitat.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the four assessment scales.
**Answer: Site, region, country and global scale.**
