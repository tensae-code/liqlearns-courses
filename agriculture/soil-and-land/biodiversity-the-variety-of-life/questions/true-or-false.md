# Biodiversity — the variety of life — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Bacteria are part of biodiversity.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A field of many individuals of one kind is richer than a field of many kinds.
**Answer: False**
