# Biodiversity — the variety of life — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Biodiversity is —
a) the number of people on Earth  b) the range of all forms of life on Earth  c) one species' population
**Answer: b) the range of all forms of life on Earth**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Biodiversity is counted as —
a) individuals only  b) species numbers, sub-species and communities in a habitat  c) kilos of biomass
**Answer: b) species numbers, sub-species and communities in a habitat**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is inside biodiversity's scope?
a) microbial genetic resources  b) only wild animals  c) only forests
**Answer: a) microbial genetic resources**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Biodiversity can be assessed at —
a) site, region, country or global scale  b) one scale only  c) household scale only
**Answer: a) site, region, country or global scale**
