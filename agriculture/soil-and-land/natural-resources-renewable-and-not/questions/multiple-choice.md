# Natural resources — renewable and not — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Natural resources are things humans —
a) manufacture in factories  b) use but cannot produce  c) never touch
**Answer: b) use but cannot produce**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which list is all natural resources?
a) soil, rain, forest, minerals  b) phones, cars, books  c) money, prices, markets
**Answer: a) soil, rain, forest, minerals**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Renewable resources —
a) never run out however used  b) are able to regenerate themselves  c) are fixed in supply
**Answer: b) are able to regenerate themselves**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Non-renewable resources must be used wisely because —
a) they are fixed in supply; survival across generations is in question  b) they are worthless  c) they regrow yearly
**Answer: a) they are fixed in supply; survival across generations is in question**
