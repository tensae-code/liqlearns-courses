# Natural resources — renewable and not — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Define natural resources in one sentence.
**Answer: Objects, materials, creatures or energy found in nature that humans use but cannot produce.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give one renewable and one non-renewable resource, with the reason for each.
**Answer: Renewable: forest/rain/grass — it regenerates itself. Non-renewable: minerals/fossil stores — fixed in supply.**
