# How water gets polluted — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Water pollution is —
a) any water outdoors  b) harmful substances at unacceptable levels, spoiling water quality  c) muddy water only
**Answer: b) harmful substances at unacceptable levels, spoiling water quality**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Pollution is judged by —
a) levels against what the water must serve  b) the river's name  c) the season only
**Answer: a) levels against what the water must serve**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Industrial waste water can carry —
a) acids and alkalis  b) only clean water  c) seeds
**Answer: a) acids and alkalis**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Farm sources of water pollution include —
a) pesticides and fertilisers washing from farms  b) harvesting by hand  c) terracing
**Answer: a) pesticides and fertilisers washing from farms**
