# How water gets polluted — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Define water pollution exactly.
**Answer: The presence of harmful substances at unacceptable levels, spoiling water quality.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the two source families the book gives.
**Answer: Industrial waste water (with acids and alkalis), and pesticides and fertilisers washing from farms.**
