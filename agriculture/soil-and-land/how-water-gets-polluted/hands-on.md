# Hands-on — How water gets polluted

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Stream survey (look, don't touch)
Walk a stream reach and list, from the bank, every input you can see or infer. Note the water's colour and life at each bend.

## Rate-and-time pledge
Write a field rule card: right chemical, right rate, right time, no spraying before heavy rain — the habits that keep farm inputs on the farm.
