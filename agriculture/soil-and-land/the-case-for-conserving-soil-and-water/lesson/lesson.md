# The case for conserving soil and water

## Do it first
Conservation asks for labour today against a loss that arrives slowly. Farmers are not fools for hesitating — the case must be made whole. Make it.

## Sub lesson 1 — The need, argued as a whole
The **need for soil and water conservation** is argued as a whole: the case a farmer or community weighs before investing labour in structures and new practices. It gathers the unit's whole chain — properties, erosion, costs, practices — into one decision.

[Picture 26677 — two neighbouring farms after five years — one conserved and green, one eroded]

## Sub lesson 2 — Weighing investment against loss
Conservation is an **investment**: labour now, against topsoil, fertility, yields and water kept for every coming year. Argued honestly — costs of building counted, costs of erosion counted — the case is the unit's closing skill: not just knowing practices, but persuading a community to adopt them.
