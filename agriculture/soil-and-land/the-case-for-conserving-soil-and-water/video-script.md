The case for conserving soil and water.
The need for soil and water conservation is argued as a whole: the case a farmer or community weighs before investing labour in structures and new practices.
Conservation is an investment: labour now, against topsoil, fertility, yields and water kept for every coming year.
Do it yourself.
Then say it to your companion.
