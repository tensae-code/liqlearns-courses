# The case for conserving soil and water — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson argues —
a) the need for conservation as a whole  b) a new terrace design  c) soil chemistry
**Answer: a) the need for conservation as a whole**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The case gathers —
a) only costs  b) only structures  c) properties, erosion, costs and practices into one decision
**Answer: c) properties, erosion, costs and practices into one decision**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The decision-maker is —
a) only buyers  b) a farmer or community  c) only the ministry
**Answer: b) a farmer or community**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Conservation is framed as —
a) an investment: labour now against losses avoided  b) a tax  c) a one-day event
**Answer: a) an investment: labour now against losses avoided**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** An honest case counts —
a) only erosion costs  b) neither  c) both building costs and erosion costs
**Answer: c) both building costs and erosion costs**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The closing skill is —
a) testing soil  b) persuading a community to adopt conservation  c) drawing maps
**Answer: b) persuading a community to adopt conservation**
