# The case for conserving soil and water — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the case for conservation gather together?
**Answer: The whole unit's chain — soil properties, erosion and its causes, erosion's costs, and the practices — into one community decision.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why call conservation an investment?
**Answer: Because labour spent now keeps topsoil, fertility, yields and water for every coming year; the return arrives over time.**
