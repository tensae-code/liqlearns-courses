What erosion costs.
Erosion's costs begin with lost topsoil and fertility: the richest layer, built over centuries, is the layer erosion takes first.
Then falling yields year by year, and sedimented water — soil choking streams and reservoirs downstream — among the other negative impacts the section counts.
