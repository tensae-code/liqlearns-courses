# What erosion costs — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Topsoil is built over centuries.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Erosion removes the poorest soil layer first.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Sedimentation harms downstream water users.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Yields rise as topsoil thins.
**Answer: False**
