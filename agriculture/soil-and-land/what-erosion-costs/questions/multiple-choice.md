# What erosion costs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Erosion takes first —
a) the topsoil, the richest layer  b) the subsoil  c) stones only
**Answer: a) the topsoil, the richest layer**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Topsoil holds —
a) nothing the crop needs  b) only sand  c) the soil's stored fertility
**Answer: c) the soil's stored fertility**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Lost fertility leads to —
a) no change  b) falling yields  c) rising yields
**Answer: b) falling yields**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Sedimented water means —
a) soil choking streams and reservoirs  b) cleaner rivers  c) more fish always
**Answer: a) soil choking streams and reservoirs**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The erosion bill is paid by —
a) nobody  b) only the government  c) the farmer uphill and the water-user downhill
**Answer: c) the farmer uphill and the water-user downhill**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Counting erosion's costs shows why —
a) slopes should be abandoned  b) conservation pays  c) farming should stop
**Answer: b) conservation pays**
