# What erosion costs — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name erosion's costs as the section counts them.
**Answer: Lost topsoil and fertility, falling yields, sedimented water, and the other negative impacts.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why do the costs make conservation pay?
**Answer: Because conservation's labour is weighed against a real, continuing bill — lost fertility, shrinking harvests and damaged water.**
