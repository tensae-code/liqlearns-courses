# Hands-on — Mitigation — cutting emissions, growing sinks

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sink survey
Walk a nearby farm or school garden and list everything that is storing carbon — trees, grass, mulched soil, terraces. Count the sinks and note one place a new sink could grow.

## Practice match
Match each of the six soil practices to the field nearest your home where it is — or could be — used, and write the match down.
