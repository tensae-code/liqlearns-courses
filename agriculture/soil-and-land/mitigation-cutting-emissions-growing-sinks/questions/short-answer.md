# Mitigation — cutting emissions, growing sinks — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three ways mitigation works.
**Answer: Reducing greenhouse-gas emissions from their sources; enhancing carbon sinks; improved soil management.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name four soil practices the lesson lists for keeping carbon in soils.
**Answer: Any four of: crop rotation, mixed cropping, reduced tillage, mulching, terraces and other physical conservation structures.**
