# Mitigation — cutting emissions, growing sinks — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Reducing greenhouse-gas emissions from their sources is one way of mitigation.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Mitigation and adaptation are the same thing.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Crop rotation and reduced tillage can help keep carbon in soils.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Terraces have no connection to climate mitigation.
**Answer: False**
