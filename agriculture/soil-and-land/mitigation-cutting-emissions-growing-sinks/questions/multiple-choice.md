# Mitigation — cutting emissions, growing sinks — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Mitigation means —
a) slowing climate change down  b) moving to a cooler town  c) measuring rainfall
**Answer: a) slowing climate change down**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Enhancing carbon sinks means —
a) growing the forests, grasslands and soils that hold carbon  b) burning residues faster  c) draining wetlands
**Answer: a) growing the forests, grasslands and soils that hold carbon**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these keeps carbon in farm soil?
a) mulching  b) burning stubble  c) bare fallow on a slope
**Answer: a) mulching**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A high proportion of the carbon plants remove from the air stays in —
a) soils under good management  b) the air above the field  c) rivers only
**Answer: a) soils under good management**
