# Mitigation — cutting emissions, growing sinks

## Do it first
A farm can fight climate change two ways: make less of the gases, and lock more carbon away. Before the three ways are named, list one farm job for each side — one that cuts a gas, one that stores carbon.

## Sub lesson 1 — Cut the gases, grow the sinks
**Mitigation means slowing climate change down.** It works first by **reducing greenhouse-gas emissions from their sources** — burning less, wasting less, managing manure and fertiliser with care.

Second, by **enhancing carbon sinks** — the forests, grasslands and soils that pull carbon dioxide out of the air and hold it.

[Picture 26402 — a farm with mulched, terraced fields labelled as a carbon store]

## Sub lesson 2 — Soil — the farm's carbon store
Third, **improved soil management**: **a high proportion of the carbon plants remove from the air stays in soils** when farms use **crop rotation, mixed cropping, reduced tillage, mulching, terraces and other physical conservation structures**.

Every one of those practices is already in this course for soil reasons. Mitigation gives them a second job: the well-kept soil is a carbon store.
