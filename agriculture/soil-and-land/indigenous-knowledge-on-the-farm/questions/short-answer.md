# Indigenous knowledge on the farm — Konso terraces and more — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does indigenous knowledge deserve recording alongside scientific methods?
**Answer: Because it protects soil and crops in the exact places it grew, fitted to local rain, slope and pests, and tested over generations.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Match each practice to its problem: Konso, Bale, Gondar.
**Answer: Konso terracing — erosion; Bale trenching — porcupines around potato plots; Gondar barn-moving — pests/field management.**
