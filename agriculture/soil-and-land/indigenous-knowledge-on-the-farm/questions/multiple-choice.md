# Indigenous knowledge on the farm — Konso terraces and more — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Indigenous knowledge is —
a) imported from textbooks  b) what communities accumulate over generations in one place  c) a brand of fertiliser
**Answer: b) what communities accumulate over generations in one place**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Indigenous knowledge is carried in —
a) only laboratory reports  b) beliefs and traditions and daily practice  c) satellites
**Answer: b) beliefs and traditions and daily practice**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Konso terracing fights —
a) porcupines  b) erosion  c) locusts
**Answer: b) erosion**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Bale farmers trench potato plots against —
a) porcupines  b) drought  c) birds
**Answer: a) porcupines**
