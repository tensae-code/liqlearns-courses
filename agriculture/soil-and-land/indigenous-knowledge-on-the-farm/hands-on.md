# Hands-on — Indigenous knowledge on the farm — Konso terraces and more

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Terrace in miniature
On a small slope or a soil tray, pour water on a bare slope, then on a stepped slope. Watch where the soil goes. Record the difference in two lines.

## Elder interview
Interview a farmer or elder: one old practice, its problem, its method, who taught it. Write it as a record card — this is the recording the lesson asks for.
