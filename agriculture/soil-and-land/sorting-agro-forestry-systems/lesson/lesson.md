# Sorting agro-forestry systems

## Do it first
Three farms: trees with crops; trees with pasture animals; all three together. The classifier's eye must sort systems the way earlier lessons sorted vegetables — by clear criteria.

## Sub lesson 1 — Classification criteria
Systems are classified by several criteria: their **structure**, the **function of the system** (the role and output of its components), the **agro-ecological zones** where the system exists or is adoptable, and **socioeconomic scales and management levels**. One system takes several labels — one per criterion — just as a vegetable took two sortings.

## Sub lesson 2 — The three structural families
On the structural criterion there are **three major classes**, beginning with **agrisilvicultural systems (crops with trees)**, and the **silvopastoral** (trees with animals/pasture) and **agrosilvopastoral** (all three) families completing the scheme. Structure answers one question: which components stand in the system?
