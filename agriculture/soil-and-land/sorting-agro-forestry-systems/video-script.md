Sorting agro-forestry systems.
Systems are classified by several criteria: their structure, the function of the system (the role and output of its components), the agro-ecological zones where the system exists or is adoptable, and socioeconomic scales and management levels.
On the structural criterion there are three major classes, beginning with agrisilvicultural systems (crops with trees), and the silvopastoral (trees with animals/pasture) and agrosilvopastoral (all three) families completing the scheme.
