# Sorting agro-forestry systems — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** List the classification criteria.
**Answer: Structure; function (role and output of components); agro-ecological zones; socioeconomic scales and management levels.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the three structural classes with their components.
**Answer: Agrisilvicultural — crops with trees; silvopastoral — trees with animals/pasture; agrosilvopastoral — trees, crops and animals.**
