# Sorting agro-forestry systems — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Classification criteria include —
a) structure and function  b) only price  c) only farm size
**Answer: a) structure and function**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Function means —
a) the owner's purpose only  b) the fence line  c) the role and output of the components
**Answer: c) the role and output of the components**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Agro-ecological zones classify systems by —
a) their yield  b) where they exist or are adoptable  c) their age
**Answer: b) where they exist or are adoptable**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Agrisilvicultural systems combine —
a) crops with trees  b) trees with animals only  c) animals with crops only
**Answer: a) crops with trees**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Silvopastoral systems combine —
a) crops with trees only  b) nothing  c) trees with pasture/animals
**Answer: c) trees with pasture/animals**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Agrosilvopastoral systems combine —
a) crops and animals without trees  b) trees, crops and animals  c) trees only
**Answer: b) trees, crops and animals**
