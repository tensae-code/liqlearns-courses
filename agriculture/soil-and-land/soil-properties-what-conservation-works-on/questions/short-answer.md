# Soil properties — what conservation works on — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What are soil properties, as the unit opens?
**Answer: The physical and chemical character of a soil — its texture, structure and chemistry.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why do properties come before practices?
**Answer: Because they decide both the soil's erosion risk and which conservation practice fits it.**
