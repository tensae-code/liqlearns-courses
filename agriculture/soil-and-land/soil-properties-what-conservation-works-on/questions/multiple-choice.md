# Soil properties — what conservation works on — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Soil properties are the soil's —
a) physical and chemical character  b) price  c) colour only
**Answer: a) physical and chemical character**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The conservation unit opens with —
a) terraces  b) crop choice  c) soil properties
**Answer: c) soil properties**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Properties are the —
a) buyer's concern  b) ground truth later decisions stand on  c) last thing to learn
**Answer: b) ground truth later decisions stand on**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A soil's character decides —
a) its erosion risk and which practice fits it  b) nothing about conservation  c) the weather
**Answer: a) its erosion risk and which practice fits it**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The same rain can be —
a) identical for all soils  b) always harmful  c) a drink to one soil and a thief to another
**Answer: c) a drink to one soil and a thief to another**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The order of work is —
a) copy the neighbour  b) know the soil, then choose the practice  c) build first, look later
**Answer: b) know the soil, then choose the practice**
