# Soil properties — what conservation works on — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Texture is part of a soil's physical character.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** All soils erode at the same rate under the same rain.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Conservation practices must fit the soil they protect.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Soil chemistry is not a soil property in this lesson.
