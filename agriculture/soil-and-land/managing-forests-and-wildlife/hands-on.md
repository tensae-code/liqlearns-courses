# Hands-on — Managing forests and wildlife

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Woodlot strip plan
Draw a woodlot divided into strips, one cut and replanted each year. How many strips make the cutting sustainable on a 10-year regrowth? (Ten.)

## Firebreak walk
Walk a plantation or grove edge and mark where a firebreak belongs and what it must be kept clear of.
