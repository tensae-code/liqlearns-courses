# Managing forests and wildlife — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Forest management includes —
a) preventing wild forest fires  b) burning freely  c) excluding all people forever
**Answer: a) preventing wild forest fires**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which is NOT among the book's reasons for forest management?
a) sustainable fuelwood supply  b) increasing deforestation  c) rural employment and income
**Answer: b) increasing deforestation**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Forest management reduces —
a) deforestation and desert expansion  b) rainfall only  c) biodiversity
**Answer: a) deforestation and desert expansion**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Wildlife management is —
a) unrelated to forests  b) the animal half of the same duty  c) only about zoos
**Answer: b) the animal half of the same duty**
