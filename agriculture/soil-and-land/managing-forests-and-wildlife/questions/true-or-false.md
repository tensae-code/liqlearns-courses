# Managing forests and wildlife — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Forest management helps conserve biodiversity.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Sustainable fuelwood means cutting until the near trees are gone.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Habitat care is wildlife care.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Forest management increases vulnerability to climate change.
**Answer: False**
