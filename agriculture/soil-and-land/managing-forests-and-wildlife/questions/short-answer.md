# Managing forests and wildlife — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** List the reasons forest management is needed.
**Answer: Sustainable fuelwood, more productive use of forest vegetation, rural employment and income, biodiversity conservation, reduced deforestation and desert expansion, and less vulnerability to climate change (including preventing wild forest fires).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why is wildlife management taught with forest management?
**Answer: Because habitat loss drives both declines together — wildlife management is the animal half of the same duty of care.**
