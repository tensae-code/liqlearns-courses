# Air pollution and the cost of mining — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Air pollution is —
a) any air with scent  b) harmful particles and gases at high concentration  c) wind
**Answer: b) harmful particles and gases at high concentration**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Pollen and spores in air are —
a) human-made pollution only  b) natural air loads  c) mining products
**Answer: b) natural air loads**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Human-caused air loads include —
a) smoke, dust and gases  b) pollen only  c) rain
**Answer: a) smoke, dust and gases**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Mineral exploitation damages —
a) land only  b) land, water and air  c) nothing
**Answer: b) land, water and air**
