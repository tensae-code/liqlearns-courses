# Air pollution and the cost of mining — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give one natural and two human sources of air loading.
**Answer: Natural: pollen (or spores/particles). Human: smoke and dust (or gases).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does the resources unit close with mining?
**Answer: Because exploiting non-renewable minerals damages land, water and air together — the cost side of the fixed stock.**
