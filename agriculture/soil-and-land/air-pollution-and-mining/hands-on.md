# Hands-on — Air pollution and the cost of mining

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Air diary
For one day, note each time the air around you turns smoky or dusty: source, how long, who breathed it. Total the human-caused hours.

## Mine-impact sketch
Sketch a mine beside a village and draw the three damage arrows: to land, to water, to air, each labelled with how it travels.
