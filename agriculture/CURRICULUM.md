# Agriculture course — CURRICULUM (the path)

Agriculture is ONE world course, organised by **levels, not
grades**: a learner climbs when they are ready, not when a
school calendar says so. The school grades the books come from
appear only in each lesson's sources (provenance), never as the
course's organisation. The path climbs from the ground up:
**First Steps → Soil and Land → Crops → Livestock → Farm
Business and Technology**.
