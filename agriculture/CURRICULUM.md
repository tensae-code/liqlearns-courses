# Agriculture course — CURRICULUM (the path)

Agriculture is ONE world course, organised by **levels, not
grades**: a learner climbs when they are ready, not when a
school calendar says so. The school grades the books come from
appear only in each lesson's sources (provenance), never as the
course's organisation. The path climbs from the ground up:
**First Steps → Soil and Land → Crops → Livestock → Farm
Business and Technology**.

## Ethiopia (Wave 4, built 2026-11-12)

The first stocked part is built from Ethiopia's Agriculture
books for Grades 11 and 12 (student books read in full; teacher
guides folded in as labelled evidence). Bands register here as
they land; counts are per band, from the build logs in
ethiopia-2026-11-11/build-logs/.

- **Band B — Grade 11 Units 5–8** (28 lessons, 336 questions,
  all in Livestock): animal feeds and ration balancing (incl.
  the Pearson square, taught exactly as the book states it),
  breeding and genetics, animal housing, animal health —
  pictures 26000–26027, GIFs 26028–26031 (drawn, looked at /
  frame-checked). Safety carried exactly as the books state it
  (silage exclusions, anthrax handling, veterinary guidance).
- **Band D — Grade 11 Units 13–16** (19 lessons, 228
  questions; pictures/GIFs 26400–26422, all present 1:1):
  climate change in Soil and Land (what the words mean, what
  it does, adapting Ethiopian farming, mitigation, the Gedeo
  way) and Units 14–16 in Farm Business and Technology
  (mechanisation and tools, human nutrition, diversified food
  production). Coordinator-verified on disk — the band's
  session ended before it wrote its build log, so this record
  was compiled from the tree (full shape checked, questions
  counted file by file, media verified); the G11 guide's
  folds for these units ride in the lessons' sources as
  labelled evidence. With this band, Grade 11 Agriculture is
  stocked in full (Units 1–16, Bands A–D).
- **Band E — Grade 12** (92 lessons, 1,380 questions; 15 per
  lesson): the whole Grade 12 book — placements 2–90 built as
  89 lessons (no two share a name, so nothing stacked), plus
  3 guide-only standalone lessons carrying the 0-countries
  line, among them the Minimum Learning Competencies and the
  Grade 12 Syllabus lessons in Farm Business and Technology.
  The guide's unit pedagogy summaries fold into the student
  lessons' sources as labelled evidence rows. Pictures/GIFs
  pictures 26600–26691 and GIFs 26692–26702 (drawn PIL, all looked at / frame-checked).
- **Band C — Grade 11 Units 9–12** (29 lessons, 348 questions):
  the dairy unit in Livestock (11 — breeds, feeding, the
  lactation cycle, milk hygiene, cottage cheese and ghee, the
  dairy business) and Units 10–12 in Soil and Land (18 —
  natural resources, forests, soil, Ethiopia's eight river
  basins, degradation and pollution, managing soil/water/
  forests/the whole environment, indigenous knowledge,
  biodiversity). Pictures 26200–26228, GIFs 26229–26233
  (drawn, looked at / frame-checked). Figures checked before
  teaching (305-day lactation, milk ≤4 °C, ghee at 110–120 °C

  four units. Pictures 25800–25828, GIFs 25829–25839 (drawn, looked at /
  frame-checked).
  The band created the agriculture/ tree itself; the course
