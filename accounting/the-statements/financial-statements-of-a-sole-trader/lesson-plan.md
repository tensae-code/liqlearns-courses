# Lesson plan — Financial Statements of a Sole Trader

**Course:** Accounting · **Title:** The Statements · **Lesson:** Financial Statements of a Sole Trader
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit — The Statements (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** at the end of a period a sole trader's records become statements: the trading account finds gross profit, the profit and loss account finds net profit, and the balance sheet shows the financial position at a date.
**This lesson's own level:** Unit — The Statements, lesson 12 of 30 in the course ladder.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: at the end of a period a sole trader's records become statements: the trading account finds gross profit, the profit and loss account finds net profit, and the balance sheet shows the financial position at a date.

Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson's scenes are the learner's own home, market and neighbourhood.
