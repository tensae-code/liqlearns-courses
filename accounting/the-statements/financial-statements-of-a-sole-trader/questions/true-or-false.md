# Financial Statements of a Sole Trader — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The balance sheet as a statement of the financial position (assets, capital and liabilities) at a particular date, the idea of an accounting/trading period, and the format: heading with business …. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** This lesson teaches the opposite of its one thing: it does not teach that at the end of a period a sole trader's records become statements: the trading account finds gross profit, the profit and loss account finds net profit, and the balance sheet shows the financial position at a date. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches exactly that: at the end of a period a sole trader's records become statements: the trading account finds gross profit, the profit and loss account finds net profit, and the balance sheet shows the financial position at a date.
