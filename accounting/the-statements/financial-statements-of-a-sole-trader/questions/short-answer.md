# Financial Statements of a Sole Trader — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is the one thing this lesson teaches? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** At the end of a period a sole trader's records become statements: the trading account finds gross profit, the profit and loss account finds net profit, and the balance sheet shows the financial position at a date.

**2.** What does this lesson teach about balance sheet part of the lesson? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The classified balance sheet (fixed then current assets; capital, long-term then current liabilities), items arranged in order of permanency or in order of liquidity, a business solvent when assets exceed liabilities ….
