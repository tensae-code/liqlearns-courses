# Hands-on — Financial Statements of a Sole Trader

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
A trader asks three questions at year's end: Did I make a gross profit? After all expenses, a net profit? And what do I own and owe today? Three questions, three statements. Naming which statement answers which is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: at the end of a period a sole trader's records become statements: the trading account finds gross profit, the profit and loss account finds net profit, and the balance sheet shows the financial position at a date. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at the market, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
