# Adjustments to Financial Statements — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach about accrual vs cash basis (the matching idea)? (Sub lesson 1)
a) preparing a cash flow statement (Penyata Aliran Tunai) for the planned business
b) Accrual accounting records revenue when earned and expenses when incurred, cash-based accounting only when …
c) Posting a full month from every journal into the subsidiary ledgers and the general-ledger Accounts …
d) Balance-day adjustments exist because business does not stop at midnight on balance day: amounts owed, …
**Answer: b) Accrual accounting records revenue when earned and expenses when incurred, cash-based accounting only when …** — That is what this lesson teaches in its accrual vs cash basis (the matching idea) part.
