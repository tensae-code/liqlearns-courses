# Adjustments to Financial Statements — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Accrual accounting records revenue when earned and expenses when incurred, cash-based accounting only when cash moves; a transaction-by-transaction comparison shows why accrual gives the clearer …. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** This lesson teaches the opposite of its one thing: it does not teach that business does not stop at midnight on balance day, so amounts owed, prepaid, earned or received in advance are adjusted so each period carries exactly its own revenues and expenses. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches exactly that: business does not stop at midnight on balance day, so amounts owed, prepaid, earned or received in advance are adjusted so each period carries exactly its own revenues and expenses.
