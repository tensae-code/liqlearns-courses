# Hands-on — Adjustments to Financial Statements

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
A business pays a full year's insurance on the first day of its year. Should the whole payment count as this month's expense? What about wages earned this week but paid next? Sorting amounts into their right period is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: business does not stop at midnight on balance day, so amounts owed, prepaid, earned or received in advance are adjusted so each period carries exactly its own revenues and expenses. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at the market, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
