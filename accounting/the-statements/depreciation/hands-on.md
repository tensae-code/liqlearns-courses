# Hands-on — Depreciation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
A delivery van is bought new. Five years later it is worth far less, though it still runs every day. Where did the lost value go, year by year? Spreading that loss fairly across the years is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: a long-life asset loses its power to serve year by year, so its cost less its residual value is spread as an expense over its useful life. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at the market, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
