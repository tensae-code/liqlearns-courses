# Lesson plan — Depreciation

**Course:** Accounting · **Title:** The Statements · **Lesson:** Depreciation
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit — The Statements (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a long-life asset loses its power to serve year by year, so its cost less its residual value is spread as an expense over its useful life.
**This lesson's own level:** Unit — The Statements, lesson 14 of 27 in the course ladder.
