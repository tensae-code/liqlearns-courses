# Depreciation

## Do it first

A delivery van is bought new. Five years later it is worth far less, though it still runs every day. Where did the lost value go, year by year? Spreading that loss fairly across the years is this lesson.

## Why assets depreciate

Depreciation spreads an asset's cost less residual value over its useful life because the asset loses future service potential; it is an expense based on historical cost.

## Straight-line method

Straight-line method: the same amount each year from (cost − residual value) ÷ useful life, entered by General Journal, with accumulated depreciation as a contra-asset deducted from cost to give book value; a depreciation schedule tracks all three figures year by year.

## Reducing-balance method

Diminishing (reducing) balance method: a fixed rate on the falling book value, suiting assets like computers that lose value fastest early.

**Contra-account.** Contra accounts (αντίθετοι λογαριασμοί): accumulated depreciation is kept in a contra account standing against its main asset account, and when the asset leaves the business the contra account is transferred against the main account so the asset's remaining story (cost, depreciation, sale proceeds, extraordinary loss) can be recorded (Greece's book.)

**Depreciation part.** Depreciation and accumulated depreciation: susut nilai is the value of a non-current asset that falls in the current financial year — an expense that reduces the asset's value in the current period, categorised as an expense of the period and reducing net profit, calculated at the end of the period; susut nilai terkumpul is the total depreciation accumulated over the asset's life, reducing the related asset's value; non-current assets lose value through wear and tear (physical condition growing faded, worn and poor), technological change (the asset left behind — e.g. computers) and flood and fire; and the three calculation methods — on cost (kaedah atas kos / garis lurus on cost: % × cost × period), straight line (kaedah garis lurus: (Kos − Nilai Skrap) / Usia Guna, scrap value being the asset's final value at the end of its life) and reducing balance (kaedah baki berkurangan: % × book value × period), with Nilai Buku Aset = Kos − Susut Nilai Terkumpul (Malaysia's book.)

**Disposal part.** Disposal of non-current assets for cash (Pelupusan Aset Bukan Semasa secara Tunai): the reasons for disposal (the asset has no value at the end of its useful life, an old asset is exchanged for a new one, the asset is destroyed by natural disaster, the asset is sold) and the methods (sold for cash or on credit, traded in, or donated); the profit or loss on disposal known from the difference between the asset's book value and its selling price; and the recording through the Jurnal Am, the asset account, the accumulated depreciation account and the disposal account, with the profit or loss recorded in the Profit & Loss Account (Malaysia's book.)

**PPE recognition and cost.** Recognition and cost of property, plant and equipment: an item is recognised as an asset only if future economic benefits will probably flow to the entity, and its cost is built up from invoice price (after cash discount), sales tax, transportation, import duties, repair before use and installation (Bhutan's book.)

**Depreciation and carrying amount.** Why depreciation is charged (including retaining funds out of profits for replacement), carrying amount = cost − depreciation expense, and the accumulated depreciation account maintained as a contra-asset (Bhutan's book.)
