# Depreciation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Depreciation spreads an asset's cost less residual value over its useful life because the asset loses future service potential; it is an expense based on historical cost.
**Answer: True** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** This lesson teaches the opposite of its one thing: it does not teach that a long-life asset loses its power to serve year by year, so its cost less its residual value is spread as an expense over its useful life.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches exactly that: a long-life asset loses its power to serve year by year, so its cost less its residual value is spread as an expense over its useful life.
