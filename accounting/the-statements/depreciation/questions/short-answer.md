# Depreciation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** A long-life asset loses its power to serve year by year, so its cost less its residual value is spread as an expense over its useful life.

**2.** What does this lesson teach about straight-line method?
