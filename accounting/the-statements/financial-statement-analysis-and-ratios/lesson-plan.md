# Lesson plan — Financial Statement Analysis and Ratios

**Course:** Accounting · **Title:** The Statements · **Lesson:** Financial Statement Analysis and Ratios
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit — The Statements (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** ratios and percentages read the financial statements: one figure set against another judges profitability, liquidity and efficiency, and lets a business be compared across years and against other businesses.
**This lesson's own level:** Unit — The Statements, lesson 17 of 37 in the course ladder.
