# Financial Statement Analysis and Ratios — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is the one thing this lesson teaches? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Ratios and percentages read the financial statements: one figure set against another judges profitability, liquidity and efficiency, and lets a business be compared across years and against other businesses.

**2.** What does this lesson teach about proposed accounting-ratios lesson? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Converting between mark-up and margin: the same gross profit on different bases, so given the mark-up fraction the margin is found by adding the numerator to the denominator, and the reverse to return to mark-up.
