# The Cash Flow Statement — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach about the whole of the new lesson?
a) clubs and societies as non-profit organisations: their features and sources of funds
b) preparing a cash flow statement (Penyata Aliran Tunai) for the planned business
c) How a pay advice slip works: wages from an hourly rate, salary divided from an annual amount, and deductions …
d) The Statement of Change in Cash Position (cash flow statement): it shows cash receipts and cash payments only …
**Answer: b) preparing a cash flow statement (Penyata Aliran Tunai) for the planned business** — That is what this lesson teaches in its the whole of the new lesson part.

**2.** What does this lesson teach about purpose and preparation of the cash statement?
a) How a pay advice slip works: wages from an hourly rate, salary divided from an annual amount, and deductions …
b) clubs and societies as non-profit organisations: their features and sources of funds
c) The Statement of Change in Cash Position (cash flow statement): it shows cash receipts and cash payments only …
d) preparing a cash flow statement (Penyata Aliran Tunai) for the planned business
**Answer: c) The Statement of Change in Cash Position (cash flow statement): it shows cash receipts and cash payments only …** — That is what this lesson teaches in its purpose and preparation of the cash statement part.
