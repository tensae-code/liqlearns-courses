# The Cash Flow Statement — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** A cash flow statement shows only cash in and cash out for a period — where the cash came from and where it went — reconciling the opening bank balance to the closing one.

**2.** What does this lesson teach about purpose and preparation of the cash statement?
**Answer:** The Statement of Change in Cash Position (cash flow statement): it shows cash receipts and cash payments only — credit transactions are ignored — and can be built from a transaction list, source documents or a bank ….

**3.** Explain the cash flow part of this lesson in your own words.
**Answer:** Cash flow: the transfers of cash in and out of a business over time, and net cash flow as the difference each month; seasonal variations must be forecast; cash flow is the lifeblood of a business — even a profitable business can fail if its profit is tied up in money owed or unsold stock and it cannot pay its bills; improving cash flow by delaying payments and chasing debtors, and keeping liquidity like a bank
