# The Cash Flow Statement

## Do it first

A business shows a good profit, yet its bank account is nearly empty. List where the cash could have gone — stock bought, a machine paid for, drawings taken. A statement that follows only the cash is this lesson.

## The whole of the new lesson

Preparing a cash flow statement (Penyata Aliran Tunai) for the planned business.

## Purpose and preparation of the cash statement

The Statement of Change in Cash Position (cash flow statement): it shows cash receipts and cash payments only — credit transactions are ignored — and can be built from a transaction list, source documents or a bank statement; it answers whether the business has enough cash or too much tied up in fixed assets.

## Cash flow statement

The Statement of Changes in Cash Position built from a transaction summary, source documents or bank statements: sources of cash (sales, fees, interest, asset sales, extra capital, loans) against uses (suppliers, expenses, asset purchases, drawings, loan repayments, VAGST) reconciling opening to closing bank balance.

## Say it to your companion
