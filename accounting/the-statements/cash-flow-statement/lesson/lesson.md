# The Cash Flow Statement

## Do it first

A business shows a good profit, yet its bank account is nearly empty. List where the cash could have gone — stock bought, a machine paid for, drawings taken. A statement that follows only the cash is this lesson.

## The whole of the new lesson

Preparing a cash flow statement (Penyata Aliran Tunai) for the planned business.

## Purpose and preparation of the cash statement

The Statement of Change in Cash Position (cash flow statement): it shows cash receipts and cash payments only — credit transactions are ignored — and can be built from a transaction list, source documents or a bank statement; it answers whether the business has enough cash or too much tied up in fixed assets.

## Cash flow statement

The Statement of Changes in Cash Position built from a transaction summary, source documents or bank statements: sources of cash (sales, fees, interest, asset sales, extra capital, loans) against uses (suppliers, expenses, asset purchases, drawings, loan repayments, VAGST) reconciling opening to closing bank balance.

## Cash flow

Cash flow: the transfers of cash in and out of a business over time, and net cash flow as the difference each month; seasonal variations must be forecast; cash flow is the lifeblood of a business — even a profitable business can fail if its profit is tied up in money owed or unsold stock and it cannot pay its bills; improving cash flow by delaying payments and chasing debtors, and keeping liquidity like a bank

**Reporting past and future cash.** The projected funds flow (projected cash flow): all the venture's money in and out — not only sales income and cost/expense payments but also investment and financing movements — and how it differs from the projected income statement; contribution margin calculated (Ecuador's book.)

**Reporting past and future cash.** The annual projected funds flow for the first five years: year 0 shows contributions and loans received against fixed-asset investment and pre-operational spending; years 1–5 summarise the monthly flows with gradual increases (Ecuador's book.)

**Reporting and forecasting cash.** Estimating the project's cash flows: the financial feasibility study is built on all the previous studies; (Palestine's book.)
**The cash flow statement.** The cash-flow statement named and placed as the fourth statement of the service company's reporting stage, drawn after the other statements (Indonesia's book.)

**The cash flow statement.** Introduction to the statement of cash flows: why the cash-flow statement and the statement of changes in owners' equity complete the set — changes in the balances of assets and liabilities between two periods show how cash was used or released, and under the indirect route net income is adjusted for the non-cash items in the income statement (depreciation, deferred tax, gains and losses on… (Tanzania's book.)

**Cash flow statement.** The statement of cash flows as one of the statement types: it presents the position of the entity's cash and cash equivalents (cash in hand and demand deposits; cash equivalents as short-term, highly liquid investments) (Bhutan's book.)

**Indirect method part.** The company cash flow statement by the indirect method: starting from profit before tax and adjusting for non-cash transactions (depreciation) and changes in working capital (inventory, receivables) to reach cash from operating activities (Bhutan's book.)
