# Video script — The Cash Flow Statement

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A cash flow statement shows only cash in and cash out for a period — where the cash came from and where it went — reconciling the opening bank balance to the closing one.

Preparing a cash flow statement (Penyata Aliran Tunai) for the planned business. The Statement of Change in Cash Position (cash flow statement): it shows cash receipts and cash payments only — credit transactions are ignored — and can be built from a transaction list, source documents or a bank statement; it answers whether the business has enough cash or too much tied up in fixed assets. The Statement of Changes in Cash Position built from a transaction summary, source documents or bank statements: sources of cash (sales, fees, interest, asset sales, extra capital, loans) against uses (suppliers, expenses, asset purchases, drawings, loan repayments, VAGST) reconciling opening to closing bank balance.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at the market, in your street.
