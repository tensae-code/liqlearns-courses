# Hands-on — The Cash Flow Statement

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
A business shows a good profit, yet its bank account is nearly empty. List where the cash could have gone — stock bought, a machine paid for, drawings taken. A statement that follows only the cash is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: a cash flow statement shows only cash in and cash out for a period — where the cash came from and where it went — reconciling the opening bank balance to the closing one. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at the market, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
