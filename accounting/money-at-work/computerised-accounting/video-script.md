# Video script — Computerised Accounting

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Computers process accounting the same way hands do, only faster: source documents are coded by the chart of accounts, and accounting packages produce the ledgers, trial balance, reconciliations and statements.

Manual vs computer processing: hardware (CPU, input, storage, output) and software (systems and application), electronic data processing coding source documents by the chart of accounts, and accounting packages producing ledgers, trial balance, GST returns, reconciliations and statements.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at the market, in your street.
