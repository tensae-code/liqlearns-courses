# Computerised Accounting — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — computers process accounting the same way hands do, only faster: source documents are coded by the chart of accounts, and accounting packages produce the ledgers, trial balance, reconciliations and statements — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, market or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
