# Computerised Accounting — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Manual vs computer processing: hardware (CPU, input, storage, output) and software (systems and application), electronic data processing coding source documents by the chart of accounts, and ….
**Answer: True** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** This lesson teaches the opposite of its one thing: it does not teach that computers process accounting the same way hands do, only faster: source documents are coded by the chart of accounts, and accounting packages produce the ledgers, trial balance, reconciliations and statements.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches exactly that: computers process accounting the same way hands do, only faster: source documents are coded by the chart of accounts, and accounting packages produce the ledgers, trial balance, reconciliations and statements.
