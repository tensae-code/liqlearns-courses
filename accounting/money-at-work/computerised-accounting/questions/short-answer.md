# Computerised Accounting — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Computers process accounting the same way hands do, only faster: source documents are coded by the chart of accounts, and accounting packages produce the ledgers, trial balance, reconciliations and statements.

**2.** What does this lesson teach about computers in accounting (Samoa's cost/resources way of framing it)?
**Answer:** Manual vs computer processing: hardware (CPU, input, storage, output) and software (systems and application), electronic data processing coding source documents by the chart of accounts, and accounting packages ….

**3.** Explain the computerised accounting software part of this lesson in your own words.
**Answer:** QuickBooks as a specialist accounting application: it automates bookkeeping (entering transactions and calculations), sales tax on invoices, bill tracking and online bill payment, financial reports (cash flows, profits, balance sheets, money owed), invoicing and invoice tracking, estimates and quotes, and receipt scanning from a phone; desktop vs cloud access; advantages (single data entry, speed, fewer errors, less labour, integration with online banking) against disadvantages (cost, set-up and training, technical failures, dependence on accurate input)
