# Break-even Analysis

## Do it first

A baker's daily costs stay the same whether he sells ten loaves or a hundred, but each loaf sold adds a little towards those costs. How many loaves must he sell before he stops losing money? Finding that point is this lesson.

## The whole of the new lesson

Break-even analysis: contribution margin per unit, break-even point in units and in RM, and the output needed for a target profit.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: break-even analysis finds the output where contribution exactly covers fixed costs — the break-even point — and the output needed for a target profit. Give one real example from your own home, market or neighbourhood, and name the lesson's key words: the whole of the new lesson. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
