# Hands-on — Break-even Analysis

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
A baker's daily costs stay the same whether he sells ten loaves or a hundred, but each loaf sold adds a little towards those costs. How many loaves must he sell before he stops losing money? Finding that point is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: break-even analysis finds the output where contribution exactly covers fixed costs — the break-even point — and the output needed for a target profit. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at the market, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
