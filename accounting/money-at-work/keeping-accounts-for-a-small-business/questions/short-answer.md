# Keeping Accounts for a Small Business — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** A small business keeps its accounts by knowing what counts as a business transaction, keeping its documents as proof, classifying its spending, and budgeting — simple, accessible and up to date.

**2.** What does this lesson teach about transactions in a small business?
**Answer:** What counts as a transaction for a sole trader (money or goods moving in or out of the business, not the owner's private spending), opening the Sagone Car Repairs case that the rest of the book follows.
