# Keeping Accounts for a Small Business — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Personal financial documents must be kept as proof of payment and for tax, stored accessibly, simply and up to date (binder, box or cabinet).
**Answer: True** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** This lesson teaches the opposite of its one thing: it does not teach that a small business keeps its accounts by knowing what counts as a business transaction, keeping its documents as proof, classifying its spending, and budgeting — simple, accessible and up to date.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches exactly that: a small business keeps its accounts by knowing what counts as a business transaction, keeping its documents as proof, classifying its spending, and budgeting — simple, accessible and up to date.
