# Hands-on — Cash Budgets

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
A shop knows a big stock payment falls due in March, but its best sales come in December. Will it have the cash in March? Writing the coming months' receipts and payments down in advance — a cash budget — is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: a cash budget forecasts receipts and payments month by month, so a business can see surpluses and shortages coming and plan for them. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at the market, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
