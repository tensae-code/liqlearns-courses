# Cash Budgets — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** A cash budget forecasts receipts and payments month by month, so a business can see surpluses and shortages coming and plan for them.

**2.** What does this lesson teach about the whole of the new lesson?
**Answer:** The cash budget (Belanjawan Tunai): forecasting receipts and payments month by month to predict surpluses and shortages.

**3.** In your own words, what does this lesson say about forecasting money?
**Answer:** Financial projections built from budgets: the sales budget, cost budget and expense budget rest on basic assumptions, and together they project the venture's financial figures (spreadsheets used for the projections)
