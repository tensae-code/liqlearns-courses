# Cash Budgets — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The cash budget (Belanjawan Tunai): forecasting receipts and payments month by month to predict surpluses and shortages.
**Answer: True** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** This lesson teaches the opposite of its one thing: it does not teach that a cash budget forecasts receipts and payments month by month, so a business can see surpluses and shortages coming and plan for them.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches exactly that: a cash budget forecasts receipts and payments month by month, so a business can see surpluses and shortages coming and plan for them.
