# Capital Investment Appraisal — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.


**1.** True or false: Capital investment appraisal is used for long-term decisions, not day-to-day short-term ones. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is what this lesson teaches in its decision-process part (Sri Lanka's teacher guide).

**2.** True or false: The payback period discounts future cash flows to their present value. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — Discounting to present value is the net present value method; payback only counts how long recovery takes (Sri Lanka's teacher guide).
