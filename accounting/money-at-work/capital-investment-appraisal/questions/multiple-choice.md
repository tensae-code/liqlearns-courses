# Capital Investment Appraisal — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.


**1.** What makes a capital investment decision a long-term decision? (Sub lesson 1)
a) The money is committed for years and the cash flows arrive over the project's whole life
b) The decision is taken by the most senior manager
c) The project costs more than the petty cash limit
d) The project is paid for in cash
**Answer: a) The money is committed for years and the cash flows arrive over the project's whole life** — That is what this lesson teaches in its decision-process part (Sri Lanka's teacher guide).

**2.** Which three cash flows are estimated for a capital investment project? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Sales, purchases and wages
b) Initial, operating and terminal cash flows
c) Cash, cheque and credit flows
d) Fixed, variable and semi-variable flows
**Answer: b) Initial, operating and terminal cash flows** — That is what this lesson teaches in its decision-process part (Sri Lanka's teacher guide).

**3.** What does the payback period tell the decision maker? (Sub lesson 2)
a) The profit the project earns in its best year
b) The project's rate of return on the initial investment
c) How long the project's net cash flows take to repay the initial investment
d) The present value of the project's cash flows
**Answer: c) How long the project's net cash flows take to repay the initial investment** — That is what this lesson teaches in its appraisal-methods part (Sri Lanka's teacher guide).

**4.** Which appraisal method discounts the project's cash flows to today's value? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Payback period
b) Net present value
c) Accounting rate of return
d) Margin of safety
**Answer: b) Net present value** — That is what this lesson teaches in its appraisal-methods part (Sri Lanka's teacher guide).
