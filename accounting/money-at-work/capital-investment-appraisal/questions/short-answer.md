# Capital Investment Appraisal — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.


**1.** Explain the steps of the capital investment decision process. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Distinguish the long-term decision from short-term ones, work through the steps of the process, and estimate the project's initial, operating and terminal cash flows and its net cash flows before judging it (Sri Lanka's teacher guide.)

**2.** Explain how payback, accounting rate of return and net present value are compared when appraising a project. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Payback says how quickly the investment is recovered, the accounting rate of return says the average accounting return on the investment, and net present value discounts all the cash flows to today's value; the methods are used and compared because each shows a different side of the same project (Sri Lanka's teacher guide.)
