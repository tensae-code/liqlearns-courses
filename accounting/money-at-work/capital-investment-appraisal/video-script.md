The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Capital investment appraisal is how a business judges a long-term project before committing the money: estimate the project's initial, operating and terminal cash flows, then test them with payback, the accounting rate of return and net present value.

The long-term (capital investment) decision process: long-term decisions are distinguished from short-term ones, the steps of the process are worked through, and the project's initial, operating and terminal cash flows and net cash flows are estimated. Appraisal methods for capital investment decisions: the payback period, the accounting rate of return and net present value, used and compared — each method shows a different side of the same project.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at the market, in your street.
