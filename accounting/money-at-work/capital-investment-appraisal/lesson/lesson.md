# Capital Investment Appraisal

## Do it first

Take a real business you know — a shop, a farm, a workshop. Ask the question this lesson answers about it, and write down what you would need to look at first. That looking is where this lesson begins.

## Sub lesson 1 — The capital investment decision process

**Decision-process part.** The long-term (capital investment) decision process: long-term decisions are distinguished from short-term ones, the steps of the process are worked through, and the project's initial, operating and terminal cash flows and net cash flows are estimated. (teacher guide) (Sri Lanka's teacher guide.)

## Sub lesson 2 — Appraisal methods — payback, accounting rate of return and net present value

**Appraisal-methods part.** Appraisal methods for capital investment decisions: the payback period, the accounting rate of return and net present value, used and compared — each method shows a different side of the same project. (teacher guide) (Sri Lanka's teacher guide.)

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: capital investment appraisal is how a business judges a long-term project before committing the money: estimate the project's initial, operating and terminal cash flows, then test them with payback, the accounting rate of return and net present value. Give one real example from your own home, market or neighbourhood, and name the lesson's key words: decision-process part, appraisal-methods part. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
