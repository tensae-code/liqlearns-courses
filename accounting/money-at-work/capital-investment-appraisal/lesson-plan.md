# Lesson plan — Capital Investment Appraisal

**Course:** Accounting · **Title:** Money at Work · **Lesson:** Capital Investment Appraisal
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit — Money at Work (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** capital investment appraisal is how a business judges a long-term project before committing the money: estimate the project's initial, operating and terminal cash flows, then test them with payback, the accounting rate of return and net present value.
**This lesson's own level:** Unit — Money at Work, lesson 37 of 39 in the course ladder; it stands on Break-even Analysis, the lesson before it in path order (recommended, never forced).
