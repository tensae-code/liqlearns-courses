# Investment Accounts — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why does an investor hold an investment, according to this lesson? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** To generate more money than the investment's current amount — the extra income compensating for time and inflation.

**2.** What year-end entry is made when the books close on a date that does not match the dividend or interest payment dates? (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** The accrued interest or dividend income earned to the closing date is established and brought into the accounts.
