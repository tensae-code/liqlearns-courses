# Investment Accounts — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is a financial asset? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Any machine a business owns  b) An asset whose value derives from a contractual claim, evidenced by a certificate, record, receipt or legal document  c) Cash in the till
**Answer: b) An asset whose value derives from a contractual claim, evidenced by a certificate, record, receipt or legal document** — Treasury instruments, corporate bonds and preference shares are the book's examples (Tanzania's book).

**2.** The three columns of an investment account are… (Sub lesson 2)
a) Date, details and folio  b) Nominal value, capital and income  c) Debit, credit and balance
**Answer: b) Nominal value, capital and income** — Each side of the investment account carries the three columns (Tanzania's book).
