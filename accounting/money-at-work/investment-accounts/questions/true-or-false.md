# Investment Accounts — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The full cost of an investment is its price alone; buying expenses are ignored. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The full cost is the price plus the additional expenses of acquiring the investment, recorded in the capital column.

**2.** Under an ex-div quotation, the buyer pays the seller the accrued dividend to the transaction date on top of the quoted price. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer: True** — The price and costs go in the capital column and the accrued dividend in the income column.
