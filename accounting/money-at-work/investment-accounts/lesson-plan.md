# Lesson plan — Investment Accounts

**Course:** Accounting · **Title:** Money at Work · **Lesson:** Investment Accounts
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit — Money at Work (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** an investment account records a holding of shares or fixed-interest securities in three columns — nominal value, capital and income — so the cost of the holding, the income it earns and the profit or loss on selling it are never mixed up.
**This lesson's own level:** Unit — Money at Work, lesson 37 of 37 in the course ladder; it stands on Computerised Accounting, the lesson before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Investments and financial assets** — what an investment is, and financial assets as claims evidenced by a certificate or record.
2. **Buying and selling financial assets; the investment account for shares** — the three-column investment account, and the full cost of an investment.
3. **Quotations, fixed-interest securities and year-end entries** — cum-div and ex-div, cum-interest and ex-interest, and accrued income at the year end.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
