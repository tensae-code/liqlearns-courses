# Investment Accounts — depth

This lesson has 3 sub lessons (Investments and financial assets; Buying and selling financial assets; the investment account for shares; Quotations, fixed-interest securities and year-end entries). Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — an investment account records a holding of shares or fixed-interest securities in three columns — nominal value, capital and income — so the cost of the holding, the income it earns and the profit or loss on selling it are never mixed up. — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, market or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
