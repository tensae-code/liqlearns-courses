# Video script — Budgeting and Variance Analysis

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Budgeting plans the period ahead — the sales budget driving the production and expense budgets inside one master budget — and variance analysis then compares standard against actual results, splitting each difference (material price, material usage, labour efficiency) into favourable and unfavourable so managers can see what to fix.

The budgeting process: a business requires proper budgeting, and managers must predict the factors affecting the business with reasonable precision, with problem-solving, organisational and monitoring skills. The operating budgets worked: the production budget built from the sales budget in units (sales + desired ending inventory = total required), and the selling and administration expense budget. Standard costing and variance analysis: the master budget as a static budget, unethical practices in budgeting named as a danger, and the variances calculated — labour efficiency variance = standard rate × (actual hours − standard hours). The direct material variances: material price variance and material usage variance, favourable against unfavourable, worked from the month's figures.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at the market, in your street.
