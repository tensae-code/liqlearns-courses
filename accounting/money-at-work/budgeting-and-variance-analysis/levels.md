# Budgeting and Variance Analysis — depth

This lesson has sub lessons; each sub lesson is proved before the next opens (the gate pool is the sub lesson's whole question set). Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — budgeting plans the period ahead — the sales budget driving the production and expense budgets inside one master budget — and variance analysis then compares standard against actual results, splitting each difference (material price, material usage, labour efficiency) into favourable and unfavourable so managers can see what to fix — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, market or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
