# Lesson plan — Budgeting and Variance Analysis

**Course:** Accounting · **Title:** Money at Work · **Lesson:** Budgeting and Variance Analysis
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit — Money at Work (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** budgeting plans the period ahead — the sales budget driving the production and expense budgets inside one master budget — and variance analysis then compares standard against actual results, splitting each difference (material price, material usage, labour efficiency) into favourable and unfavourable so managers can see what to fix.
**This lesson's own level:** Unit — Money at Work, lesson 34 of 37 in the course ladder; it stands on Taxation Accounting, the lesson before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## If the learner is stuck
- Confuses a budget with a cash budget → a cash budget forecasts cash in and out; the budgeting of this lesson plans the whole operation (sales, production, expenses) in one master budget, and variance analysis then judges how the period actually went against standard.
