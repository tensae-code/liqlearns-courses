# Video script — Taxation Accounting

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

This lesson is about one thing.
Taxation accounting records what a business owes the government: sales tax collected from customers is a liability held for the state, deferred tax is tax on this year's income whose payment falls in a later period, and Bhutan's Business Income Tax (BIT) is computed on business income after its allowable deductions and declared on the annual return.

Sales tax collected from customers is a liability owed to the government, levied on both cash sales and credit sales and accounted for as such. Deferred tax: tax whose payment is deferred to a future accounting period for income earned in the current year. Bhutan's Business Income Tax (BIT): its meaning and scope, and how Bhutan's taxation system evolved through the first major tax reform. Allowable deductions in computing business income: conveyance/transport facilities for employees valued at actual cost, insurance premiums on the enterprise's own assets, publicity and advertisement (actual, or 2% of assessed gross profit) and the other listed expenses. The annual BIT return (Form BIT 2): its format and how the current and previous accounting periods are shown in it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at the market, in your street.
