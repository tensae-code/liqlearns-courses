# Taxation Accounting — depth

This lesson has sub lessons; each sub lesson is proved before the next opens (the gate pool is the sub lesson's whole question set). Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — taxation accounting records what a business owes the government: sales tax collected from customers is a liability held for the state, deferred tax is tax on this year's income whose payment falls in a later period, and Bhutan's Business Income Tax (BIT) is computed on business income after its allowable deductions and declared on the annual return — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, market or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
