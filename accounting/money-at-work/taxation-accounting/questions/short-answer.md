# Taxation Accounting — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.


**1.** Explain the sales tax part of the proposed lesson of this lesson. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Sales tax collected from customers is a liability owed to the government, levied on both cash sales and credit sales and accounted for as such (Bhutan's book.)

**2.** Explain the deferred tax part of the proposed lesson of this lesson. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Deferred tax: tax whose payment is deferred to a future accounting period for income earned in the current year (Bhutan's book.)
