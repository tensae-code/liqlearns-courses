# Lesson plan — Taxation Accounting

**Course:** Accounting · **Title:** Money at Work · **Lesson:** Taxation Accounting
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit — Money at Work (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** taxation accounting records what a business owes the government: sales tax collected from customers is a liability held for the state, deferred tax is tax on this year's income whose payment falls in a later period, and Bhutan's Business Income Tax (BIT) is computed on business income after its allowable deductions and declared on the annual return.
**This lesson's own level:** Unit — Money at Work, lesson 32 of 37 in the course ladder; it stands on Payroll and Payslips, the lesson before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## If the learner is stuck
- Treats sales tax collected as the business's own income → it is collected for the government and stands as a liability from the moment it is charged, on cash sales and credit sales alike.
- Go back to the one thing: taxation accounting records what a business owes the government: sales tax collected from customers is a liability held for the state, deferred tax is tax on this year's income whose payment falls in a later period, and Bhutan's Business Income Tax (BIT) is computed on business income after its allowable deductions and declared on the annual return.
