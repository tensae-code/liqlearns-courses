# Taxation Accounting

## Do it first

Take a real business you know — a shop, a farm, a workshop. Ask the question this lesson answers about it, and write down what you would need to look at first. That looking is where this lesson begins.

## Sub lesson 1 — Sales tax and deferred tax in the accounts

**Sales tax part of the proposed lesson.** Sales tax collected from customers is a liability owed to the government, levied on both cash sales and credit sales and accounted for as such (Bhutan's book.)

**Deferred tax part of the proposed lesson.** Deferred tax: tax whose payment is deferred to a future accounting period for income earned in the current year (Bhutan's book.)

## Sub lesson 2 — Bhutan's Business Income Tax

**BIT part of the proposed lesson.** Bhutan's Business Income Tax (BIT): its meaning and scope, and how Bhutan's taxation system evolved through the first major tax reform (Bhutan's book.)

**Deductions part of the proposed lesson.** Allowable deductions in computing business income: conveyance/transport facilities for employees valued at actual cost, insurance premiums on the enterprise's own assets, publicity and advertisement (actual, or 2% of assessed gross profit) and the other listed expenses (Bhutan's book.)

**Tax return part of the proposed lesson.** The annual BIT return (Form BIT 2): its format and how the current and previous accounting periods are shown in it (Bhutan's book.)

**How tax works.** Option 2 Taxation: the types of tax paid by income earners and businesses; the reasons for paying tax; taxpayer requirements; the taxation documents and completing a tax return; deductions and rebates (teacher guide) (Papua New Guinea's teacher guide.)

**Taxation.** Strand 2 Unit 4: taxation — direct tax against indirect tax; the purposes of tax (revenue, redistribution, re-pricing); GST at 10% under the Goods and Services Act 2003, with a worked example; customs duties (export and import, Customs Tariff Act 1990); excise duty; the professional licensing bodies (CPA PNG and others) (teacher guide) (Papua New Guinea's teacher guide.)
