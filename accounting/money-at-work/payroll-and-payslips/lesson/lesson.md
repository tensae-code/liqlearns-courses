# Payroll and Payslips

## Do it first

Look at any worker's pay slip — or imagine one. The pay at the top is not the pay at the bottom. List what could be added (overtime) and what could be taken off (tax) between the two. Reading that slip is this lesson.

## Payslips and net pay

How a pay advice slip works: wages from an hourly rate, salary divided from an annual amount, and deductions (PAYE tax, National Provident Fund) taken from gross pay to reach net pay.

## Payslips and overtime

The pay advice slip's required contents (employee and employer names, employee number, pay period, rate, gross pay, deductions, net pay) and how overtime at 1.5× and 2× the hourly rate is worked out from an annual salary.

**Recording-the-payroll.** The payroll worked inside the expense-accounts section: the monthly payroll statement (μισθοδοτική κατάσταση) is recorded with staff pay and expenses debited against the amounts owed — pay payable to personnel through Sundry Creditors, personnel-pay taxes withheld, and IKA insurance contributions — salary advances during the month go through Sundry Debtors (staff advances) and are cleared at month end, and payment of wages, taxes and IKA stamps debits those liability accounts against cash (Greece's book.)

**Payslips and net pay.** How pay is worked out from a pay advice slip: ordinary time and overtime hours at their rates give gross pay, PAYE tax and SNPF (Samoa National Provident Fund) are deducted to reach net pay (Samoa's book.)

**Payroll records and controls.** Accounting for payroll: changes in the remuneration payable to an employee must be properly authorised and validated, and the salary and wage payments to employees must be properly recorded (Bhutan's book.)

**Working out take-home pay.** Option 2 Taxation: wage and tax calculations — gross pay, deductions and net pay (a 22% rate example is worked in the activities) (teacher guide) (Papua New Guinea's teacher guide.)

**Running the payroll.** Strand 2 Unit 3: the payroll management tasks inside compensation administration (teacher guide) (Papua New Guinea's teacher guide.)

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: a payslip shows how pay is built: hours at a rate (with overtime at one-and-a-half or double) make gross pay, deductions such as tax come off, and what is left is net pay. Give one real example from your own home, market or neighbourhood, and name the lesson's key words: payslips and net pay, payslips and overtime. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
