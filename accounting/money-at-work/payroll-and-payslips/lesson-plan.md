# Lesson plan — Payroll and Payslips

**Course:** Accounting · **Title:** Money at Work · **Lesson:** Payroll and Payslips
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit — Money at Work (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a payslip shows how pay is built: hours at a rate (with overtime at one-and-a-half or double) make gross pay, deductions such as tax come off, and what is left is net pay.
**This lesson's own level:** Unit — Money at Work, lesson 32 of 39 in the course ladder.
