# Video script — Payroll and Payslips

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A payslip shows how pay is built: hours at a rate (with overtime at one-and-a-half or double) make gross pay, deductions such as tax come off, and what is left is net pay.

How a pay advice slip works: wages from an hourly rate, salary divided from an annual amount, and deductions (PAYE tax, National Provident Fund) taken from gross pay to reach net pay. The pay advice slip's required contents (employee and employer names, employee number, pay period, rate, gross pay, deductions, net pay) and how overtime at 1.5× and 2× the hourly rate is worked out from an annual salary.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at the market, in your street.
