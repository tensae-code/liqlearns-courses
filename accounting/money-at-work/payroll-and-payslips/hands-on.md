# Hands-on — Payroll and Payslips

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Look at any worker's pay slip — or imagine one. The pay at the top is not the pay at the bottom. List what could be added (overtime) and what could be taken off (tax) between the two. Reading that slip is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: a payslip shows how pay is built: hours at a rate (with overtime at one-and-a-half or double) make gross pay, deductions such as tax come off, and what is left is net pay. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at the market, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
