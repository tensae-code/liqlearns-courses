# Payroll and Payslips — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** A payslip shows how pay is built: hours at a rate (with overtime at one-and-a-half or double) make gross pay, deductions such as tax come off, and what is left is net pay.

**2.** What does this lesson teach about payslips and overtime?
**Answer:** The pay advice slip's required contents (employee and employer names, employee number, pay period, rate, gross pay, deductions, net pay) and how overtime at 1.5× and 2× the hourly rate is worked out from an annual salary.
