# Payroll and Payslips — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** How a pay advice slip works: wages from an hourly rate, salary divided from an annual amount, and deductions (PAYE tax, National Provident Fund) taken from gross pay to reach net pay.
**Answer: True** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** This lesson teaches the opposite of its one thing: it does not teach that a payslip shows how pay is built: hours at a rate (with overtime at one-and-a-half or double) make gross pay, deductions such as tax come off, and what is left is net pay.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches exactly that: a payslip shows how pay is built: hours at a rate (with overtime at one-and-a-half or double) make gross pay, deductions such as tax come off, and what is left is net pay.
