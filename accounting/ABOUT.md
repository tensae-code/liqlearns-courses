# About the Accounting course

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in this
course comes from government textbooks — the accounting and
Business Studies books of Dominica, Ecuador, Fiji, Greece, Kenya, Malaysia, Nicaragua, Nigeria, Palestine
and Samoa, Indonesia, Sudan and Tanzania.
The one exception is the **Tip boxes**: a tip in a box is from
us, the people making LiqLearns. Tips are ours; the lessons are
the books'.

## How this course is organised

Accounting is ONE top-level course, one of the three courses of
the Business / Commerce / Accounting split (the split the books
themselves forced: the same school subject carries business
management, trade and bookkeeping side by side, and each part is
stocked in the course it belongs to). Inside the course the
organisation is by **units and levels, not grades**: a learner's
place is found by what they prove, and each lesson stands on the
ones before it as a recommended-first path — never a forced
gate. The school grade a book comes from appears only in each
lesson's `lesson/sources.md`, as provenance.

The course as built has **five units**: First Steps (what
accounting is, the accounting equation, double entry, the
accounting cycle), The Books (books of original entry, the cash
book, the ledger, the trial balance, correcting errors, bank
reconciliation, internal control over cash), The Statements
(financial statements of a sole trader, adjustments,
depreciation, the cash flow statement, financial statement
analysis and ratios), Kinds of Business (non-profit
organisations, partnerships, companies, manufacturing,
incomplete records, control accounts, zakat, branches) and Money at Work
(keeping accounts for a small business, payroll and payslips,
cash budgets, break-even analysis, computerised accounting,
investment accounts).
**39 lessons, 769 placements stocked.**
