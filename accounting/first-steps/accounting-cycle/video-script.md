# Video script — The Accounting Cycle

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Accounting runs in a cycle: transactions are recorded on source documents (input), classified and summarised in the journals and ledgers (process), and finished as financial statements (output).

The accounting cycle closed out: preparing the financial statements and the reports developed from them is the final stage — input (transactions recorded on source documents), process (the information classified and summarised in the journals and ledgers), output (financial statements prepared).

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at the market, in your street.
