# Hands-on — The Accounting Cycle

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Follow one sale in a shop from start to finish: the receipt written, the day's sales listed, the totals moved into the accounts, and at the year's end the profit statement drawn. Write the four steps in order. You have just walked the accounting cycle.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: accounting runs in a cycle: transactions are recorded on source documents (input), classified and summarised in the journals and ledgers (process), and finished as financial statements (output). Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at the market, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
