# Double Entry — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is the one thing this lesson teaches? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Every transaction has two sides: for every debit entry there is a corresponding credit entry, so the books always balance.

**2.** What does this lesson teach about the dual-effect part of the lesson? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The dual effect: every transaction increases or decreases balance-sheet items in a way that keeps the balance sheet balancing (an asset and a liability can move together; some transactions touch more than two items, and ….
