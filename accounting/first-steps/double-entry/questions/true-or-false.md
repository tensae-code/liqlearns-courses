# Double Entry — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Sorting transactions before recording them: cash transactions, where both sides of the exchange happen at once (payment in cash, in other forms of money such as cheques and money orders, or in kind), …. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** This lesson teaches the opposite of its one thing: it does not teach that every transaction has two sides: for every debit entry there is a corresponding credit entry, so the books always balance. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches exactly that: every transaction has two sides: for every debit entry there is a corresponding credit entry, so the books always balance.
