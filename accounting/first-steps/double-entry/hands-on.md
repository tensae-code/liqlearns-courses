# Hands-on — Double Entry

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
A trader sells goods for cash. Two things changed at once: goods went down, cash went up. Find one more sale or payment in a shop you know and name the TWO things that changed. Every transaction moves two things — that is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: every transaction has two sides: for every debit entry there is a corresponding credit entry, so the books always balance. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at the market, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
