# Lesson plan — The Accounting Equation

**Course:** Accounting · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** The Accounting Equation
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit — First Steps (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** everything a business has (its assets) comes from somewhere: from the owner (capital) or from outsiders (liabilities) — so assets always equal capital plus liabilities, and every transaction keeps that balance.
**This lesson's own level:** Unit — First Steps, lesson 2 of 27 in the course ladder.

1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: everything a business has (its assets) comes from somewhere: from the owner (capital) or from outsiders (liabilities) — so assets always equal capital plus liabilities, and every transaction keeps that balance.
