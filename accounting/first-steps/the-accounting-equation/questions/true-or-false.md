# The Accounting Equation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The basic terms the equation is built from: debtor, creditor and goods, then assets — property with a monetary value, split into fixed assets (kept to run the business, expected to last over a year) …. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** This lesson teaches the opposite of its one thing: it does not teach that everything a business has (its assets) comes from somewhere: from the owner (capital) or from outsiders (liabilities) — so assets always equal capital plus liabilities, and every transaction keeps that balance. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches exactly that: everything a business has (its assets) comes from somewhere: from the owner (capital) or from outsiders (liabilities) — so assets always equal capital plus liabilities, and every transaction keeps that balance.
