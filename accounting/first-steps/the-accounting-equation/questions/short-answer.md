# The Accounting Equation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is the one thing this lesson teaches? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Everything a business has (its assets) comes from somewhere: from the owner (capital) or from outsiders (liabilities) — so assets always equal capital plus liabilities, and every transaction keeps that balance.

**2.** What does this lesson teach about assets, liabilities and capital part of the lesson? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Liabilities as what the business owes outsiders: long-term liabilities (not settled within a year) and short-term/current liabilities (payable within a year, such as bank overdrafts and creditors), with the ….

**3.** Explain the the accounting equation part of this lesson in your own words. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The statement of financial position (balance sheet): adds up total assets (cash, stocks, land, property, equipment), total liabilities (loans and all amounts owed to creditors and employees) and owner's equity (capital invested by owners/shareholders); assets are always equal to liabilities plus owner's equity — Assets = Liabilities + Owner's equity — and the equation shows what the business is worth to its owners once debts are settled (worked values)
