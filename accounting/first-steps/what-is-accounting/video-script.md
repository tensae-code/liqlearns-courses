# Video script — What Is Accounting

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Accounting is the system that records a business's money transactions, sorts and summarises them into statements, and analyses them so the people who use the information can decide — and book-keeping, the systematic recording, is its first part.

What counts as a business transaction (an exchange with a monetary value), why transactions must be recorded, and book-keeping as the systematic recording of transactions inside the wider subject of accounting, which also analyses and interprets the records for decisions. What financial statements are. The stakeholders who are interested in a business's financial statements. Analysing why financial statements matter to a business's financial operations and decisions.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at the market, in your street.
