# What Is Accounting — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** What counts as a business transaction (an exchange with a monetary value), why transactions must be recorded, and book-keeping as the systematic recording of transactions inside the wider subject of …. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** This lesson teaches the opposite of its one thing: it does not teach that accounting is the system that records a business's money transactions, sorts and summarises them into statements, and analyses them so the people who use the information can decide — and book-keeping, the systematic recording, is its first part. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches exactly that: accounting is the system that records a business's money transactions, sorts and summarises them into statements, and analyses them so the people who use the information can decide — and book-keeping, the systematic recording, is its first part.
