# What Is Accounting — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is the one thing this lesson teaches? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Accounting is the system that records a business's money transactions, sorts and summarises them into statements, and analyses them so the people who use the information can decide — and book-keeping, the systematic recording, is its first part.

**2.** What does this lesson teach about opening part? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** What financial statements are.
