# Investment Property — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.


**1.** Explain the opening part of the proposed lesson of this lesson. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Investment property as its own topic: property held to earn rentals or for capital appreciation, distinguished from owner-occupied property, plant and equipment (with the significant-ancillary-services test) (Bhutan's book.)

**2.** Explain the fair value part of the proposed lesson of this lesson. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Measuring investment property at fair value: the gain or loss from changes in fair value is reported in the income statement for the period, and the property is derecognised on disposal like PPE (Bhutan's book.)
