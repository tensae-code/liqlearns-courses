# Investment Property

## Do it first

Take a real business you know — a shop, a farm, a workshop. Ask the question this lesson answers about it, and write down what you would need to look at first. That looking is where this lesson begins.

## Sub lesson 1 — What investment property is

**Opening part of the proposed lesson.** Investment property as its own topic: property held to earn rentals or for capital appreciation, distinguished from owner-occupied property, plant and equipment (with the significant-ancillary-services test) (Bhutan's book.)

## Sub lesson 2 — Fair value and disposal

**Fair value part of the proposed lesson.** Measuring investment property at fair value: the gain or loss from changes in fair value is reported in the income statement for the period, and the property is derecognised on disposal like PPE (Bhutan's book.)

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: investment property is property held to earn rentals or for capital appreciation — not for use in the business and not for sale in the ordinary course — measured at fair value, with the gain or loss from fair-value changes reported in the income statement, and derecognised on disposal like property, plant and equipment. Give one real example from your own home, market or neighbourhood, and name the lesson's key words: Opening part of the proposed lesson, Fair value part of the proposed lesson. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
