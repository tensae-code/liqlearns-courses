# Lesson plan — Investment Property

**Course:** Accounting · **Title:** Kinds of Business · **Lesson:** Investment Property
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit — Kinds of Business (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** investment property is property held to earn rentals or for capital appreciation — not for use in the business and not for sale in the ordinary course — measured at fair value, with the gain or loss from fair-value changes reported in the income statement, and derecognised on disposal like property, plant and equipment.
**This lesson's own level:** Unit — Kinds of Business, lesson 27 of 37 in the course ladder; it stands on Biological Assets, the lesson before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## If the learner is stuck
- Calls any property an investment property → the test is why it is held: rent or capital appreciation makes it investment property; using it in the business, or holding it for sale, makes it something else.
- Go back to the one thing: investment property is property held to earn rentals or for capital appreciation — not for use in the business and not for sale in the ordinary course — measured at fair value, with the gain or loss from fair-value changes reported in the income statement, and derecognised on disposal like property, plant and equipment.
