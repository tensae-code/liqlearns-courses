# Investment Property — depth

This lesson has sub lessons; each sub lesson is proved before the next opens (the gate pool is the sub lesson's whole question set). Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — investment property is property held to earn rentals or for capital appreciation — not for use in the business and not for sale in the ordinary course — measured at fair value, with the gain or loss from fair-value changes reported in the income statement, and derecognised on disposal like property, plant and equipment — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, market or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
