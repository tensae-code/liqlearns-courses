This lesson is about one thing.
Investment property is property held to earn rentals or for capital appreciation — not for use in the business and not for sale in the ordinary course — measured at fair value, with the gain or loss from fair-value changes reported in the income statement, and derecognised on disposal like property, plant and equipment.

Investment property as its own topic: property held to earn rentals or for capital appreciation, distinguished from owner-occupied property, plant and equipment (with the significant-ancillary-services test). Measuring investment property at fair value: the gain or loss from changes in fair value is reported in the income statement for the period, and the property is derecognised on disposal like PPE.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at the market, in your street.
