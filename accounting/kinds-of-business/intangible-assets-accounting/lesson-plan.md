# Lesson plan — Intangible Assets

**Course:** Accounting · **Title:** Kinds of Business · **Lesson:** Intangible Assets
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit — Kinds of Business (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** intangible assets are non-monetary assets without physical substance: research spending is written off as it is incurred, development spending may be capitalised only when it meets the tests, and internally generated brands, mastheads, publishing titles and customer lists are never capitalised.
**This lesson's own level:** Unit — Kinds of Business, lesson 28 of 37 in the course ladder; it stands on Investment Property, the lesson before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video.
