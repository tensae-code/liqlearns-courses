# Intangible Assets — depth

This lesson has sub lessons; each sub lesson is proved before the next opens (the gate pool is the sub lesson's whole question set). Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — intangible assets are non-monetary assets without physical substance: research spending is written off as it is incurred, development spending may be capitalised only when it meets the tests, and internally generated brands, mastheads, publishing titles and customer lists are never capitalised — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, market or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
