# Video script — Intangible Assets

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Intangible assets are non-monetary assets without physical substance: research spending is written off as it is incurred, development spending may be capitalised only when it meets the tests, and internally generated brands, mastheads, publishing titles and customer lists are never capitalised.

Intangible assets: research against development in creating an asset (the software-development illustration — research, design, programming and testing), and what may be capitalised. Internally generated items that are NOT capitalised: brands, mastheads, publishing titles and customer lists, with the football-club player-registration question worked as the test case.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at the market, in your street.
