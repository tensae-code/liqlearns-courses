# Video script — Government Grants

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Government grants are help from the state given in return for meeting set conditions: a grant related to an asset is accounted for against that asset, and a grant related to income reimburses costs, each recognised as the conditions it answers to are met.

Government grants: grants related to assets against grants related to income (reimbursement of cost), and how each is accounted for.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at the market, in your street.
