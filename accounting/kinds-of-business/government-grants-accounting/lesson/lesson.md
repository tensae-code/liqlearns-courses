# Government Grants

## Do it first

Take a real business you know — a shop, a farm, a workshop. Ask the question this lesson answers about it, and write down what you would need to look at first. That looking is where this lesson begins.

**The whole of the proposed lesson.** Government grants: grants related to assets against grants related to income (reimbursement of cost), and how each is accounted for (Bhutan's book.)

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: government grants are help from the state given in return for meeting set conditions: a grant related to an asset is accounted for against that asset, and a grant related to income reimburses costs, each recognised as the conditions it answers to are met. Give one real example from your own home, market or neighbourhood, and name the lesson's key words: The whole of the proposed lesson. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
