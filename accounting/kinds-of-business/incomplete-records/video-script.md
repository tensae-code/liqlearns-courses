This lesson is about one thing.
When a trader's records are incomplete, profit can still be found — by comparing opening and closing statements of affairs, or by rebuilding the figures through control accounts and cash analysis.

What incomplete records are, why traders keep them (single entry, missing books) and the two recovery methods the book teaches. The comparison method: opening and closing statements of affairs and working out profit from the movement in capital, adjusted for drawings and extra capital. The analysis method: rebuilding sales, purchases and cash figures through control accounts and cash analysis so full final accounts can be prepared. A personal Statement of Affairs (statement of net worth) lists an individual's assets and liabilities to find net worth, and sits beside a weekly personal budget of fixed and variable expenses; both are the statements a bank asks for with a loan application.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at the market, in your street.
