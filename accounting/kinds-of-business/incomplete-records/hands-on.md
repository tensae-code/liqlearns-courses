# Hands-on — Incomplete Records

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
A trader kept no proper books — only a notebook of cash and a pile of invoices — yet the tax office still asks: what was your profit? Starting from what he owns and owes at the start and end, could you find it? That rescue work is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: when a trader's records are incomplete, profit can still be found — by comparing opening and closing statements of affairs, or by rebuilding the figures through control accounts and cash analysis. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at the market, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
