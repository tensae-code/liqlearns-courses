# Incomplete Records — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** What incomplete records are, why traders keep them (single entry, missing books) and the two recovery methods the book teaches. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** This lesson teaches the opposite of its one thing: it does not teach that when a trader's records are incomplete, profit can still be found — by comparing opening and closing statements of affairs, or by rebuilding the figures through control accounts and cash analysis. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches exactly that: when a trader's records are incomplete, profit can still be found — by comparing opening and closing statements of affairs, or by rebuilding the figures through control accounts and cash analysis.
