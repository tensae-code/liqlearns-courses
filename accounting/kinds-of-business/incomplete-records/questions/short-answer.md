# Incomplete Records — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is the one thing this lesson teaches? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** When a trader's records are incomplete, profit can still be found — by comparing opening and closing statements of affairs, or by rebuilding the figures through control accounts and cash analysis.

**2.** What does this lesson teach about statement-of-affairs part? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The comparison method: opening and closing statements of affairs and working out profit from the movement in capital, adjusted for drawings and extra capital.
