# Video script — Accounts of Non-Profit Organisations

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A club or society does not trade for an owner's profit: its year's cash is summarised in a receipts and payments account, its result in an income and expenditure account, and what it owns sits in an accumulated fund.

Clubs and societies as non-profit organisations: their features and sources of funds. The receipts and payments account as the summary of the club's cash book. The members' subscription (yuran) account, including subscriptions in arrears and paid in advance. The income and expenditure account for a club, adjusted like a profit and loss account.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at the market, in your street.
