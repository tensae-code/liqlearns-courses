# Hands-on — Accounts of Non-Profit Organisations

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Your sports club collects subscriptions, runs a canteen and ends the year with money in the bank. Nobody owns that money as profit. How should the club report its year? A club's own kind of accounts is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: a club or society does not trade for an owner's profit: its year's cash is summarised in a receipts and payments account, its result in an income and expenditure account, and what it owns sits in an accumulated fund. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at the market, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
