# Accounts of Non-Profit Organisations — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Clubs and societies as non-profit organisations: their features and sources of funds. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** This lesson teaches the opposite of its one thing: it does not teach that a club or society does not trade for an owner's profit: its year's cash is summarised in a receipts and payments account, its result in an income and expenditure account, and what it owns sits in an accumulated fund. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches exactly that: a club or society does not trade for an owner's profit: its year's cash is summarised in a receipts and payments account, its result in an income and expenditure account, and what it owns sits in an accumulated fund.
