# Accounts of Non-Profit Organisations — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is the one thing this lesson teaches? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** A club or society does not trade for an owner's profit: its year's cash is summarised in a receipts and payments account, its result in an income and expenditure account, and what it owns sits in an accumulated fund.

**2.** What does this lesson teach about receipts-and-payments part? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The receipts and payments account as the summary of the club's cash book.
