# Video script — Control Accounts

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Control accounts hold the totals of the many personal accounts: the sales and purchases ledger control balances must agree with the schedules of debtors and creditors, and only the control balances enter the trial balance.

Posting a full month from every journal into the subsidiary ledgers and the general-ledger Accounts Receivable and Accounts Payable Control Accounts; the schedules of receivables and payables must agree with the control balances, and only control balances enter the trial balance.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at the market, in your street.
