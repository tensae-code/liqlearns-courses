# Control Accounts — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Control accounts hold the totals of the many personal accounts: the sales and purchases ledger control balances must agree with the schedules of debtors and creditors, and only the control balances enter the trial balance.
