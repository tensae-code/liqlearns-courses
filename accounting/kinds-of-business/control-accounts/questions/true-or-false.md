# Control Accounts — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Posting a full month from every journal into the subsidiary ledgers and the general-ledger Accounts Receivable and Accounts Payable Control Accounts; the schedules of receivables and payables must ….
**Answer: True** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** This lesson teaches the opposite of its one thing: it does not teach that control accounts hold the totals of the many personal accounts: the sales and purchases ledger control balances must agree with the schedules of debtors and creditors, and only the control balances enter the trial balance.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches exactly that: control accounts hold the totals of the many personal accounts: the sales and purchases ledger control balances must agree with the schedules of debtors and creditors, and only the control balances enter the trial balance.
