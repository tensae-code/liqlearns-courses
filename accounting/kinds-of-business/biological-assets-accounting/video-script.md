This lesson is about one thing.
Biological assets are the living plants and animals a business holds — accounted for as their own topic under BAS 41, with bearer plants set apart from consumable assets, and shown as their own line in the statement of financial position, split current and non-current.

Biological assets as their own accounting topic: what a biological asset is, bearer biological assets distinguished from consumable ones, and accounting for them as per BAS 41. Presenting and disclosing biological assets: shown as a separate line item in the statement of financial position, split current/non-current by the usual rule (e.g. consumable biological assets as a current asset), with the BAS 41 disclosures.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at the market, in your street.
