# Biological Assets — depth

This lesson has sub lessons; each sub lesson is proved before the next opens (the gate pool is the sub lesson's whole question set). Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — biological assets are the living plants and animals a business holds — accounted for as their own topic under BAS 41, with bearer plants set apart from consumable assets, and shown as their own line in the statement of financial position, split current and non-current — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, market or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
