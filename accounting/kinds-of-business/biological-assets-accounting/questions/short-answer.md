# Biological Assets — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.


**1.** Explain the opening part of the proposed lesson of this lesson. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Biological assets as their own accounting topic: what a biological asset is, bearer biological assets distinguished from consumable ones, and accounting for them as per BAS 41 (Bhutan's book.)

**2.** Explain the presentation part of the proposed lesson of this lesson. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Presenting and disclosing biological assets: shown as a separate line item in the statement of financial position, split current/non-current by the usual rule (e.g. consumable biological assets as a current asset), with the BAS 41 disclosures (Bhutan's book.)
