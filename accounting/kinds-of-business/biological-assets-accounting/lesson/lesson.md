# Biological Assets

## Do it first

Take a real business you know — a shop, a farm, a workshop. Ask the question this lesson answers about it, and write down what you would need to look at first. That looking is where this lesson begins.

## Sub lesson 1 — What a biological asset is

**Opening part of the proposed lesson.** Biological assets as their own accounting topic: what a biological asset is, bearer biological assets distinguished from consumable ones, and accounting for them as per BAS 41 (Bhutan's book.)

## Sub lesson 2 — Presenting and disclosing biological assets

**Presentation part of the proposed lesson.** Presenting and disclosing biological assets: shown as a separate line item in the statement of financial position, split current/non-current by the usual rule (e.g. consumable biological assets as a current asset), with the BAS 41 disclosures (Bhutan's book.)
