# Lesson plan — Biological Assets

**Course:** Accounting · **Title:** Kinds of Business · **Lesson:** Biological Assets
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit — Kinds of Business (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** biological assets are the living plants and animals a business holds — accounted for as their own topic under BAS 41, with bearer plants set apart from consumable assets, and shown as their own line in the statement of financial position, split current and non-current.
**This lesson's own level:** Unit — Kinds of Business, lesson 26 of 37 in the course ladder; it stands on Branch Accounts, the lesson before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## If the learner is stuck
- Treats every living thing on the farm as the same kind of asset → bearer plants (held to produce over many seasons) and consumable assets (held to be harvested or sold) are distinguished, and the split decides where each stands in the statement.
- Go back to the one thing: biological assets are the living plants and animals a business holds — accounted for as their own topic under BAS 41, with bearer plants set apart from consumable assets, and shown as their own line in the statement of financial position, split current and non-current.
