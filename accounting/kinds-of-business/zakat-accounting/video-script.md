# Video script — Zakat Accounting

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Zakat accounting determines and values the wealth and income zakat is due on, measures the amount due at the fixed sharia rates — after deducting debts and costs, counting only wealth held a full lunar year and reaching the nisab — and distributes the proceeds to their channels.

For a trader, the base comes straight from the books: current assets less current liabilities, then tested against the nisab. For a salary, take the year's gross pay and deduct pension contributions, debts and basic needs first.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at the market, in your street.
