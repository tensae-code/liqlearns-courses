# Zakat Accounting — depth

This lesson has 3 sub lessons (Zakat accounting as its own branch; Zakat on trade goods; Zakat on salaries, income-producing assets and crops). Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — zakat accounting determines and values the wealth and income zakat is due on, measures the amount due at the fixed sharia rates — after deducting debts and costs, counting only wealth held a full lunar year and reaching the nisab — and distributes the proceeds to their channels — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, market or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
