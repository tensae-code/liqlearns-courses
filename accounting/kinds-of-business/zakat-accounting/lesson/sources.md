Filed name note: this lesson was proposed as a NEW lesson (`zakat-accounting`) by Sudan's reader. It was checked against every existing Accounting lesson first: no lesson teaches the accounting treatment of zakat — how the wealth and income zakat is due on are determined and valued, how the amount due is measured at the fixed sharia rates after deducting debts and costs, or how its proceeds are distributed to their channels. It is genuinely a separate one thing, so the lesson is built here, in Kinds of Business after Control Accounts, and the resolution is recorded in the registry's Phase 3 addendum.
