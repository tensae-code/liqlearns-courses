# Hands-on — Zakat Accounting

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Take a simple set of figures for a small trader — stock, money owed to the trader, money the trader owes, cash — and build the zakat base on paper: add the current assets, take away the current liabilities, and say whether what remains reaches the nisab. That building of the base from the books is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: zakat accounting determines and values the wealth and income zakat is due on, measures the amount due at the fixed sharia rates — after deducting debts and costs, counting only wealth held a full lunar year and reaching the nisab — and distributes the proceeds to their channels. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at the market, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
