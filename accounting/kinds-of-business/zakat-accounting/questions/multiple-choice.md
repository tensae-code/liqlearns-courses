# Zakat Accounting — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does zakat accounting do? (Sub lesson 1)
a) It sets the state's tax rates  b) It determines and values the wealth and income zakat is due on, measures the amount due and distributes the proceeds to their channels  c) It audits a company's books for its shareholders
**Answer: b) It determines and values the wealth and income zakat is due on, measures the amount due and distributes the proceeds to their channels** — Zakat accounting is its own branch of accounting, working under sharia-derived principles (Sudan's book).

**2.** A trader's zakat base for trade goods is built from the books as… (Sub lesson 2)
a) All assets the trader has ever owned  b) Current assets less current liabilities, tested against the nisab  c) The year's sales alone, with nothing deducted
**Answer: b) Current assets less current liabilities, tested against the nisab** — Stock, debtors, bills receivable and investments, less what the business owes (Sudan's book).
