# Zakat Accounting — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name two ways zakat accounting differs from tax accounting. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Any two of: zakat deducts all debts and costs before computing; it falls on Muslims only; it excludes wealth from unlawful sources; it exempts the family's basic needs uniformly; it excludes gains not held a full lunar year; it works at fixed proportional rates.

**2.** How is the zakat base found for a salary earner? (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** The gross salary over the year, less pension and social-insurance deductions, the taxpayer's debts and the cost of basic needs — the remainder is then compared with the year's nisab.
