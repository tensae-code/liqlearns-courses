# Zakat Accounting — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Zakat is computed before any debts and costs are deducted. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: False** — Zakat deducts all debts and costs before computing; that is one of the points on which it differs from tax accounting.

**2.** For income-producing assets such as rented property, the zakat base is the gross yield with nothing taken away. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer: False** — The base is the gross yield less costs and expenses, depreciation of the exploited assets, debts due and the cost of basic needs.
