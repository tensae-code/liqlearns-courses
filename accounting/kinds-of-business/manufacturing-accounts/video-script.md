# Video script — Manufacturing Accounts

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

This lesson is about one thing.
A maker must first find what production cost: direct materials, direct labour, direct expenses and factory overheads are gathered in a manufacturing account, and the cost of production passes to the trading account.

Types of cost and work in progress: direct material, direct labour, direct expenses, factory overheads and prime cost. Preparing the manufacturing account in both T format and statement format and transferring cost of production to the trading account.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at the market, in your street.
