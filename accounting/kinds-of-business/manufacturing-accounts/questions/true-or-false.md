# Manufacturing Accounts — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Types of cost and work in progress: direct material, direct labour, direct expenses, factory overheads and prime cost.
**Answer: True** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** This lesson teaches the opposite of its one thing: it does not teach that a maker must first find what production cost: direct materials, direct labour, direct expenses and factory overheads are gathered in a manufacturing account, and the cost of production passes to the trading account.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches exactly that: a maker must first find what production cost: direct materials, direct labour, direct expenses and factory overheads are gathered in a manufacturing account, and the cost of production passes to the trading account.
