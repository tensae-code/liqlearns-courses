# Manufacturing Accounts — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** A maker must first find what production cost: direct materials, direct labour, direct expenses and factory overheads are gathered in a manufacturing account, and the cost of production passes to the trading account.

**2.** What does this lesson teach about main part?
**Answer:** Preparing the manufacturing account in both T format and statement format and transferring cost of production to the trading account.
