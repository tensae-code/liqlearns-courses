This lesson is about one thing.
A lease under SLFRS 16 puts a right-of-use asset and a lease liability in the lessee's books: the asset is depreciated over the lease term and the liability carries interest, so the cost of using the leased asset is spread over the years of the lease.

Leases under SLFRS 16: when a business leases an asset, the lessee does not simply pay rent off the books — it records a right-of-use asset (its right to use the asset) and a lease liability (what it owes for that right) in its own books. The lease is carried year by year in the lease creditor, asset, interest and accumulated-depreciation accounts: each payment bears interest on the outstanding liability and reduces it, and the leased asset is depreciated over the lease term.
