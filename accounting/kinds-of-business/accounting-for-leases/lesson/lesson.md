# Accounting for Leases

## Do it first

Take a real business you know — a shop, a farm, a workshop. Ask the question this lesson answers about it, and write down what you would need to look at first. That looking is where this lesson begins.

## Sub lesson 1 — The lessee's lease on the books — the right-of-use asset and the lease liability

**Leases part.** Leases under SLFRS 16: when a business leases an asset, the lessee does not simply pay rent off the books — it records a right-of-use asset (its right to use the asset) and a lease liability (what it owes for that right) in its own books. (teacher guide) (Sri Lanka's teacher guide.)

## Sub lesson 2 — The lease accounts year by year — interest, depreciation and the lease creditor

**Lease-accounts part.** The lease is carried year by year in the lease creditor, asset, interest and accumulated-depreciation accounts: each payment bears interest on the outstanding liability and reduces it, and the leased asset is depreciated over the lease term. (teacher guide) (Sri Lanka's teacher guide.)

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: a lease under SLFRS 16 puts a right-of-use asset and a lease liability in the lessee's books: the asset is depreciated over the lease term and the liability carries interest, so the cost of using the leased asset is spread over the years of the lease.
