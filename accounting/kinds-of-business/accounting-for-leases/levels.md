# Accounting for Leases — depth

This lesson has sub lessons; each sub lesson is proved before the next opens (the gate pool is the sub lesson's whole question set). Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — a lease under SLFRS 16 puts a right-of-use asset and a lease liability in the lessee's books: the asset is depreciated over the lease term and the liability carries interest, so the cost of using the leased asset is spread over the years of the lease. — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, market or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
