# Accounting for Leases — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.


**1.** Explain how a lease is recorded in the lessee's books under SLFRS 16. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The lessee recognises a right-of-use asset and a lease liability; the lease is then carried in the lease creditor, asset, interest and accumulated-depreciation accounts, and the leased asset is depreciated (Sri Lanka's teacher guide.)

**2.** Explain why a lease payment is split in the lessee's books. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Each payment carries interest on the outstanding lease liability and reduces the liability itself, while the right-of-use asset is depreciated separately — so the interest account, the lease creditor account and accumulated depreciation each show their own part (Sri Lanka's teacher guide.)
