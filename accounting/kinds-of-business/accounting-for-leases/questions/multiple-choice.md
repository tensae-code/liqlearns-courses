# Accounting for Leases — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.


**1.** Under SLFRS 16, what does a lessee put on its statement of financial position when a lease begins? (Sub lesson 1)
a) Only the first lease payment as an expense
b) A right-of-use asset and a lease liability
c) The leased asset as its own property with no liability
d) Nothing until the lease ends
**Answer: b) A right-of-use asset and a lease liability** — That is what this lesson teaches in its leases part (Sri Lanka's teacher guide).

**2.** Which accounts does the lessee keep for a lease under SLFRS 16? (Sub lesson 2)
a) Only a rent expense account
b) The lease creditor, asset, interest and accumulated-depreciation accounts
c) A sales ledger control account
d) The petty cash book
**Answer: b) The lease creditor, asset, interest and accumulated-depreciation accounts** — That is what this lesson teaches in its leases part (Sri Lanka's teacher guide).

**3.** How is the leased (right-of-use) asset written down over the lease? (Sub lesson 2)
a) It is never written down
b) It is revalued every year to market price
c) It is depreciated over the lease term
d) It is written off in full in the first year
**Answer: c) It is depreciated over the lease term** — That is what this lesson teaches in its leases part (Sri Lanka's teacher guide).

**4.** What is the lease liability? (Sub lesson 1)
a) The amount the lessee owes for the right to use the leased asset over the lease term
b) A fine for ending a lease early
c) The deposit paid to the lessor, never returned
d) The lessor's profit on the lease
**Answer: a) The amount the lessee owes for the right to use the leased asset over the lease term** — That is what this lesson teaches in its leases part (Sri Lanka's teacher guide).
