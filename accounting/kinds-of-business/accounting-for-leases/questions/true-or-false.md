# Accounting for Leases — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.


**1.** True or false: Under SLFRS 16 the lessee records a right-of-use asset and a lease liability. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is what this lesson teaches in its leases part (Sri Lanka's teacher guide).

**2.** True or false: Under SLFRS 16 a lessee records nothing but rent expense for a lease. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: False** — The lessee records a right-of-use asset and a lease liability, with interest and depreciation accounts (Sri Lanka's teacher guide).
