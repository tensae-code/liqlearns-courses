# Lesson plan — Accounting for Leases

**Course:** Accounting · **Title:** Kinds of Business · **Lesson:** Accounting for Leases
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit — Kinds of Business (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a lease under SLFRS 16 puts a right-of-use asset and a lease liability in the lessee's books: the asset is depreciated over the lease term and the liability carries interest, so the cost of using the leased asset is spread over the years of the lease.
**This lesson's own level:** Unit — Kinds of Business, lesson 30 of 39 in the course ladder; it stands on Government Grants, the lesson before it in path order (recommended, never forced).
