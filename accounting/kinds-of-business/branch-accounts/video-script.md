This lesson is about one thing.
Branch accounts keep the record between a head office and its branches — the branch account in the head office's books for branches that keep no full system of their own, and the reciprocal current accounts for branches that do — so each branch's profit can be found.

For a branch that keeps no books of its own, the head office prepares the branch account itself — by the debtors method or the stock-and-debtors method. A big branch keeps its own books, and the two sides mirror each other through current accounts.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at the market, in your street.
