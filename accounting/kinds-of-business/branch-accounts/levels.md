# Branch Accounts — depth

This lesson has 3 sub lessons (Branches and branch accounting; Non-autonomous (integrated) branches; Autonomous branches and items in transit). Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — branch accounts keep the record between a head office and its branches — the branch account in the head office's books for branches that keep no full system of their own, and the reciprocal current accounts for branches that do — so each branch's profit can be found. — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, market or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
