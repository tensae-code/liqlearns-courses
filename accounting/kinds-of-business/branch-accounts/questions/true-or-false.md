# Branch Accounts — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** An autonomous branch keeps no books at all. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer: False** — An autonomous branch keeps its own books and treats the head office through a head-office current account.

**2.** Goods sent to a branch but still on the way at the period end are an item in transit and must be adjusted for. (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer: True** — Items in transit are adjusted at the period end so the branch and head-office current accounts agree.
