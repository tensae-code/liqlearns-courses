# Branch Accounts — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the two methods this lesson gives for preparing a non-autonomous branch's account in the head office's books. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The debtors method and the stock-and-debtors method.

**2.** How do an autonomous branch and its head office record their dealings with each other? (Sub lesson 3)
**Answer:** The branch keeps a head-office current account and the head office keeps a branch current account, and every inter-branch transaction is recorded in both sets of books.
