# Branch Accounts — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Why does a business open a branch? (Sub lesson 1)
a) To avoid keeping any accounts  b) To increase its visibility and accessibility as it grows  c) To stop paying its head-office staff
**Answer: b) To increase its visibility and accessibility as it grows** — A branch is a business operating unit away from the head office (Tanzania's book).

**2.** Under the stock-and-debtors method, where is the branch account prepared? (Sub lesson 2)
a) In the branch's own full ledger  b) In the head office's books  c) At the bank
**Answer: b) In the head office's books** — A non-autonomous branch keeps no full double-entry system of its own, so the head office prepares the branch account to find branch profit (Tanzania's book).
