# Hands-on — Branch Accounts

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Take two sheets of paper — one for a head office, one for its branch. Send imaginary goods from the first sheet to the second, sell them there, and send the cash back on paper, writing each movement on both sheets. That two-sided record is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: branch accounts keep the record between a head office and its branches — the branch account in the head office's books for branches that keep no full system of their own, and the reciprocal current accounts for branches that do — so each branch's profit can be found. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at the market, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
