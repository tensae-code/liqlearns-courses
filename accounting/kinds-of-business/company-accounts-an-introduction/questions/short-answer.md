# Company Accounts — an Introduction — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is the one thing this lesson teaches? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** A company is owned through shares: share capital in its stages, dividends paid only on paid-up capital, profit kept back as retained earnings, and debts raised as debentures — all shown in the shareholders' equity section.

**2.** What does this lesson teach about share-issue part? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Types of capital and share issue: ordinary and preference shares, issue at par and at a premium, and the journal and ledger entries for an issue.
