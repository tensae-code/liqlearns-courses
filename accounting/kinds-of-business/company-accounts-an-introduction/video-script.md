# Video script — Company Accounts — an Introduction

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A company is owned through shares: share capital in its stages, dividends paid only on paid-up capital, profit kept back as retained earnings, and debts raised as debentures — all shown in the shareholders' equity section.

Introduction to the limited company: features and the private (Sendirian) versus public (Awam) company in outline. Types of capital and share issue: ordinary and preference shares, issue at par and at a premium, and the journal and ledger entries for an issue. The owners' equity of a limited company as presented in its statement of financial position. Cash dividends: declaring and paying dividends on ordinary and preference shares and the entries involved.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at the market, in your street.
