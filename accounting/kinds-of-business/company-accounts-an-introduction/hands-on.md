# Hands-on — Company Accounts — an Introduction

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
A partnership grows and becomes a company. Instead of two owners' capital accounts there are now many shareholders, each holding shares. What is a share, and how is the owners' part of a company shown? That is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: a company is owned through shares: share capital in its stages, dividends paid only on paid-up capital, profit kept back as retained earnings, and debts raised as debentures — all shown in the shareholders' equity section. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at the market, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
