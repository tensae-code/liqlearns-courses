# Partnership Accounts — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is the one thing this lesson teaches? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Partners share what the business earns by agreement: the appropriation account divides profit through interest on capital, salaries and the agreed ratio, and each partner's capital and current accounts keep their claims apart.

**2.** What does this lesson teach about appropriation part? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The profit and loss appropriation account: interest on capital, interest on drawings, partners' salaries and sharing the residual profit.
