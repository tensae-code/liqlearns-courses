# Partnership Accounts — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The partnership business itself: features and the contents of a partnership agreement as the basis for the accounts. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer: True** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** This lesson teaches the opposite of its one thing: it does not teach that partners share what the business earns by agreement: the appropriation account divides profit through interest on capital, salaries and the agreed ratio, and each partner's capital and current accounts keep their claims apart.
