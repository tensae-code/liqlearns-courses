# Video script — Partnership Accounts

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Partners share what the business earns by agreement: the appropriation account divides profit through interest on capital, salaries and the agreed ratio, and each partner's capital and current accounts keep their claims apart.

The partnership business itself: features and the contents of a partnership agreement as the basis for the accounts. The profit and loss appropriation account: interest on capital, interest on drawings, partners' salaries and sharing the residual profit. Partners' capital and current accounts and the partnership statement of financial position (owners' equity section). Dissolution of a partnership: the realisation account, settling liabilities and distributing what is left to partners.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at the market, in your street.
