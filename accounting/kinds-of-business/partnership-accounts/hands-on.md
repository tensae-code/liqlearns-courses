# Hands-on — Partnership Accounts

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Two friends run a shop together — one put in more money, one works longer hours. At year's end, how should the profit be shared fairly? Interest on capital, a salary for work, then the rest in ratio — that sharing is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: partners share what the business earns by agreement: the appropriation account divides profit through interest on capital, salaries and the agreed ratio, and each partner's capital and current accounts keep their claims apart. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at the market, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
