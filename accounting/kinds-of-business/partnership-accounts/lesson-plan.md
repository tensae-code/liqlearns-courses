# Lesson plan — Partnership Accounts

**Course:** Accounting · **Title:** Kinds of Business · **Lesson:** Partnership Accounts
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit — Kinds of Business (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** partners share what the business earns by agreement: the appropriation account divides profit through interest on capital, salaries and the agreed ratio, and each partner's capital and current accounts keep their claims apart.
**This lesson's own level:** Unit — Kinds of Business, lesson 18 of 27 in the course ladder.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. 1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: partners share what the business earns by agreement: the appropriation account divides profit through interest on capital, salaries and the agreed ratio, and each partner's capital and current accounts keep their claims apart.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson's scenes are the learner's own home, market and neighbourhood.
