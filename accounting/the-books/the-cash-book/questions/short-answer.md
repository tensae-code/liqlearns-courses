# The Cash Book — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is the one thing this lesson teaches? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The cash book is the business's own record of its cash and bank money — receipts on one side, payments on the other — kept apart because most transactions pass through cash or bank.

**2.** What does this lesson teach about cash book part of the lesson? (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The single-column cash book: cash account and bank account kept as ordinary ledger accounts on separate pages, one money column on each side, for businesses operating on cash in hand only or mainly through the bank.

**3.** Explain the single entry bookkeeping part of this lesson in your own words. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** Single entry bookkeeping: a simple record of income and expenses in a manual cash book or spreadsheet, entering each receipt and payment and carrying a running balance after every entry; the system used by sole traders, charities and clubs (worked example: the cash book of Jameson's Cakes)
