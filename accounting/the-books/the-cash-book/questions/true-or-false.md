# The Cash Book — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The cash book as the separate ledger that holds only the cash and bank accounts, kept apart from the general ledger because most transactions pass through cash or bank, because money needs special …. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: True** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** This lesson teaches the opposite of its one thing: it does not teach that the cash book is the business's own record of its cash and bank money — receipts on one side, payments on the other — kept apart because most transactions pass through cash or bank. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches exactly that: the cash book is the business's own record of its cash and bank money — receipts on one side, payments on the other — kept apart because most transactions pass through cash or bank.
