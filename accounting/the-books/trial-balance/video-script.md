# Video script — The Trial Balance

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A trial balance lists every ledger account's debit balance and credit balance at a date; if double entry was kept, the two columns agree — and agreement checks the arithmetic, not everything.

The trial balance as a statement at a date listing every account's debit balance in one column and credit balance in the other to check the arithmetical accuracy of the ledger, and its purposes (checking double entry was observed, summarising the ledger, facilitating the final accounts). The trial balance lists the balanced ledger accounts to check that total debit balances equal total credit balances; agreement does not prove there are no errors, but disagreement locates one.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at the market, in your street.
