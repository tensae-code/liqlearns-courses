# The Trial Balance — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** A trial balance lists every ledger account's debit balance and credit balance at a date; if double entry was kept, the two columns agree — and agreement checks the arithmetic, not everything.

**2.** What does this lesson teach about preparing a trial balance; what it proves and does not?
**Answer:** The trial balance lists the balanced ledger accounts to check that total debit balances equal total credit balances; agreement does not prove there are no errors, but disagreement locates one.
