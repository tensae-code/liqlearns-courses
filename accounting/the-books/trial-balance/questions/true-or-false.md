# The Trial Balance — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The trial balance as a statement at a date listing every account's debit balance in one column and credit balance in the other to check the arithmetical accuracy of the ledger, and its purposes ….
**Answer: True** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** This lesson teaches the opposite of its one thing: it does not teach that a trial balance lists every ledger account's debit balance and credit balance at a date; if double entry was kept, the two columns agree — and agreement checks the arithmetic, not everything.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches exactly that: a trial balance lists every ledger account's debit balance and credit balance at a date; if double entry was kept, the two columns agree — and agreement checks the arithmetic, not everything.
