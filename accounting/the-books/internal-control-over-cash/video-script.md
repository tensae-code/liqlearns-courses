# Video script — Internal Control over Cash

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Cash is the asset most at risk, so a business guards it with controls: bank takings intact and daily, separate the duties, authorise payments, and check the bank record independently.

Cash control as internal control: purposes (enough cash on hand, preventing misappropriation and theft, productive use of cash) and methods over cash receipts and payments, including banking in takings promptly and paying by cheque. Internal control over cash: cash is the asset most at risk, so receipts controls (bank intact daily, two people open mail, cheques crossed "Not Transferable", pre-numbered receipts, locked-in register tape, separation of duties) and payments controls (cheque-only payments, authorisation, imprest petty cash, independent bank reconciliation) protect it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at the market, in your street.
