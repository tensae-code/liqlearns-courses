# Internal Control over Cash

## Do it first

Imagine one person in a shop takes the cash, writes the receipts, banks the money and checks the bank statement — alone. List three ways money could quietly disappear. The guards against exactly that are this lesson.

## The whole of the new lesson

Cash control as internal control: purposes (enough cash on hand, preventing misappropriation and theft, productive use of cash) and methods over cash receipts and payments, including banking in takings promptly and paying by cheque.

## Internal control systems for cash

Internal control over cash: cash is the asset most at risk, so receipts controls (bank intact daily, two people open mail, cheques crossed "Not Transferable", pre-numbered receipts, locked-in register tape, separation of duties) and payments controls (cheque-only payments, authorisation, imprest petty cash, independent bank reconciliation) protect it.
