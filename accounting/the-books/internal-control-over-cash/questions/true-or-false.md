# Internal Control over Cash — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Cash control as internal control: purposes (enough cash on hand, preventing misappropriation and theft, productive use of cash) and methods over cash receipts and payments, including banking in ….
**Answer: True** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** This lesson teaches the opposite of its one thing: it does not teach that cash is the asset most at risk, so a business guards it with controls: bank takings intact and daily, separate the duties, authorise payments, and check the bank record independently.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches exactly that: cash is the asset most at risk, so a business guards it with controls: bank takings intact and daily, separate the duties, authorise payments, and check the bank record independently.
