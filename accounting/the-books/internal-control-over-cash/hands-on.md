# Hands-on — Internal Control over Cash

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Imagine one person in a shop takes the cash, writes the receipts, banks the money and checks the bank statement — alone. List three ways money could quietly disappear. The guards against exactly that are this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: cash is the asset most at risk, so a business guards it with controls: bank takings intact and daily, separate the duties, authorise payments, and check the bank record independently. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at the market, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
