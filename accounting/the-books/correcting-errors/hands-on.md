# Hands-on — Correcting Errors

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
A bookkeeper enters a payment of 90 as 9 on one side only. The two sides of the books no longer agree. How would you hunt the mistake? And could a mistake hide even when the sides agree? Both questions are this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: some errors make the trial balance disagree and some hide even when it balances; each kind is found and corrected in its own way, often through the journal. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at the market, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
