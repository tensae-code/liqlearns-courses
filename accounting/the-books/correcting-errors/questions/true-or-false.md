# Correcting Errors — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The limits of the trial balance: the errors that make it disagree (partial omission, unequal amounts, wrong balances posted, wrong side) versus the errors it cannot reveal even when it balances — ….
**Answer: True** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** This lesson teaches the opposite of its one thing: it does not teach that some errors make the trial balance disagree and some hide even when it balances; each kind is found and corrected in its own way, often through the journal.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches exactly that: some errors make the trial balance disagree and some hide even when it balances; each kind is found and corrected in its own way, often through the journal.
