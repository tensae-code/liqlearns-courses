# Correcting Errors — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** Some errors make the trial balance disagree and some hide even when it balances; each kind is found and corrected in its own way, often through the journal.

**2.** What does this lesson teach about errors part of the lesson?
**Answer:** The limits of the trial balance: the errors that make it disagree (partial omission, unequal amounts, wrong balances posted, wrong side) versus the errors it cannot reveal even when it balances — total omission, error ….
