# Video script — Bank Reconciliation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The cash book and the bank statement rarely show the same balance on the same day; a bank reconciliation explains the difference, item by item, until the two agree.

The bank reconciliation statement (Penyata Penyesuaian Bank): why the cash book and bank statement balances differ and how the reconciliation is prepared. A personal bank statement is read line by line and reconciled with the holder's own record: unpresented cheques and a late deposit explain why the two balances differ, worked through a four-step reconciliation.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at the market, in your street.
