# Bank Reconciliation

## Do it first

Your own record says you have 500 in the bank; the bank's statement says 430. Before you panic, list reasons both could be right at once — a cheque not yet presented, a deposit not yet cleared. Reconciling the two is this lesson.

## Main part

The bank reconciliation statement (Penyata Penyesuaian Bank): why the cash book and bank statement balances differ and how the reconciliation is prepared.

## Bank reconciliation, personal form

A personal bank statement is read line by line and reconciled with the holder's own record: unpresented cheques and a late deposit explain why the two balances differ, worked through a four-step reconciliation.

**Bank reconciliation.** Bank differences at stocktaking: the balance in the business's books is compared with the bank's current-account statement, the differences between the two are identified, and the balance that appears in the balance sheet is the agreed, real balance (Sudan's book.)

**The cheque-account record.** Keeping the record of a cheque account: tracking cheques written, deposits, EFTPOS transactions, direct credits (wages paid straight in) and automatic payments against the account balance (Samoa's book.)

**Bank reconciliation, personal form.** Comparing the bank statement with the cheque-account record and preparing a bank reconciliation statement for an individual: items on the statement (bank fees and charges) missing from the record explain the difference (Samoa's book.)

**Bank statement concept.** The bank statement: a summary of the banking transactions over a period in an account held with a bank or financial institution, issued by the bank (Bhutan's book.)

**Bank reconciliation preparation.** Preparing the bank reconciliation: unpresented cheques issued to suppliers, bank charges debited by the bank, and a customer depositing directly into the bank account by mobile application are the differences to reconcile (Bhutan's book.)
