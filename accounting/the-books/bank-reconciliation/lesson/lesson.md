# Bank Reconciliation

## Do it first

Your own record says you have 500 in the bank; the bank's statement says 430. Before you panic, list reasons both could be right at once — a cheque not yet presented, a deposit not yet cleared. Reconciling the two is this lesson.

## Main part

The bank reconciliation statement (Penyata Penyesuaian Bank): why the cash book and bank statement balances differ and how the reconciliation is prepared.

## Bank reconciliation, personal form

A personal bank statement is read line by line and reconciled with the holder's own record: unpresented cheques and a late deposit explain why the two balances differ, worked through a four-step reconciliation.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the cash book and the bank statement rarely show the same balance on the same day; a bank reconciliation explains the difference, item by item, until the two agree. Give one real example from your own home, market or neighbourhood, and name the lesson's key words: main part, bank reconciliation, personal form. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
