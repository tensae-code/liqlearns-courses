# Hands-on — Bank Reconciliation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Your own record says you have 500 in the bank; the bank's statement says 430. Before you panic, list reasons both could be right at once — a cheque not yet presented, a deposit not yet cleared. Reconciling the two is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the cash book and the bank statement rarely show the same balance on the same day; a bank reconciliation explains the difference, item by item, until the two agree. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at the market, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
