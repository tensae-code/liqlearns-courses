# Bank Reconciliation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is the one thing this lesson teaches?
**Answer:** The cash book and the bank statement rarely show the same balance on the same day; a bank reconciliation explains the difference, item by item, until the two agree.

**2.** What does this lesson teach about bank reconciliation, personal form?
**Answer:** A personal bank statement is read line by line and reconciled with the holder's own record: unpresented cheques and a late deposit explain why the two balances differ, worked through a four-step reconciliation.
