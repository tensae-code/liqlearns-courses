# Lesson plan — Bank Reconciliation

**Course:** Accounting · **Title:** The Books · **Lesson:** Bank Reconciliation
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit — The Books (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the cash book and the bank statement rarely show the same balance on the same day; a bank reconciliation explains the difference, item by item, until the two agree.
**This lesson's own level:** Unit — The Books, lesson 10 of 30 in the course ladder.
