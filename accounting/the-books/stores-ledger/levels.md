# The Stores Ledger — depth

This lesson has sub lessons; each sub lesson is proved before the next opens (the gate pool is the sub lesson's whole question set). Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — the stores ledger is the cost-accounting record of the factory store: every receipt and issue of material is priced and balanced in it, fed by the stores documents, so the business always knows what materials it holds, what they cost, and what each job used — in their own words, give one real example from their own life, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, market or street situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
