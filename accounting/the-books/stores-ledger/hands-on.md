# Hands-on — The Stores Ledger

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Take a real business you know — a shop, a farm, a workshop. Ask the question this lesson answers about it, and write down what you would need to look at first. That looking is where this lesson begins.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the stores ledger is the cost-accounting record of the factory store: every receipt and issue of material is priced and balanced in it, fed by the stores documents, so the business always knows what materials it holds, what they cost, and what each job used. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at the market, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
