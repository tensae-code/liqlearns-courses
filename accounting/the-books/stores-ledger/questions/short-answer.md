# The Stores Ledger — short answer

Answer in your own words, from the lesson.


**1.** Explain the opening part of the proposed lesson of this lesson. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Cost accounting's stores system opened: the elements of cost (direct labour cost — wages of labour directly engaged in production, altering the raw material — and the other cost elements) that the stores records serve (Bhutan's book.)

**2.** Explain the documents part of the proposed lesson of this lesson. (Sub lesson 2)
**Answer:** The documents used in the stores procedure, starting with the bill of materials prepared for a job and sent to the stores department showing the quantities of material required (Bhutan's book.)
