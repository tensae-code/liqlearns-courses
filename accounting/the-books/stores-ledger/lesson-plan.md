# Lesson plan — The Stores Ledger

**Course:** Accounting · **Title:** The Books · **Lesson:** The Stores Ledger
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit — The Books (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the stores ledger is the cost-accounting record of the factory store: every receipt and issue of material is priced and balanced in it, fed by the stores documents, so the business always knows what materials it holds, what they cost, and what each job used.
**This lesson's own level:** Unit — The Books, lesson 8 of 37 in the course ladder; it stands on The Ledger, the lesson before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## If the learner is stuck
- Mixes the stores ledger up with the general ledger → the general ledger holds the business's accounts in money terms for the financial statements; the stores ledger is the store's own record, in quantities and prices, that serves costing.
- Go back to the one thing: the stores ledger is the cost-accounting record of the factory store: every receipt and issue of material is priced and balanced in it, fed by the stores documents, so the business always knows what materials it holds, what they cost, and what each job used. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
