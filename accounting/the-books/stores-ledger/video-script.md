# Video script — The Stores Ledger

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The stores ledger is the cost-accounting record of the factory store: every receipt and issue of material is priced and balanced in it, fed by the stores documents, so the business always knows what materials it holds, what they cost, and what each job used.

Cost accounting's stores system opened: the elements of cost (direct labour cost — wages of labour directly engaged in production, altering the raw material — and the other cost elements) that the stores records serve. The documents used in the stores procedure, starting with the bill of materials prepared for a job and sent to the stores department showing the quantities of material required. Keeping the stores ledger: receipts and issues priced and balanced (issues under the set valuation method), normal loss entered in the quantity column only with the price inflated over the remaining units, stock verification shortages, and why transfers of excess material between jobs should be avoided.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at the market, in your street.
