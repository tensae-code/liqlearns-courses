# Books of Original Entry — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is the one thing this lesson teaches? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Before anything reaches the ledger, each transaction is first written, in time order, in a book of original entry — a journal fed by a source document that proves the transaction happened.

**2.** What does this lesson teach about source documents part of the lesson? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The two correcting documents: the debit note, sent by a supplier when the buyer has been undercharged (in effect an additional invoice), and the credit note (traditionally printed in red), sent when the customer's ….
