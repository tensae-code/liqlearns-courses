# The Ledger — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is the one thing this lesson teaches? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** The ledger is the main book of accounts: one account for each item, where every transaction is classified and summarised so the balance of any item can be found at any date.

**2.** What does this lesson teach about recording in the ledger part of the lesson? (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Recording stock movements: purchases of goods for resale go to the purchases account (not a stock account), sales and the two returns accounts (returns inwards from customers, returns outwards to suppliers) carry the ….

**3.** Explain the ledger accounts part of this lesson in your own words. (Sub lesson 1)
**Answer:** Ledger accounts: larger businesses keep separate ledger accounts for the bank, for each type of equipment and other assets, for each expense (rent, electricity, insurance), for cash sales, for each credit customer (showing what they owe and repay) and for each creditor the business owes
