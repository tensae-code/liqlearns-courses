# Accounting — CURRICULUM (the path)

One course — Accounting — organised by **units and levels, not
grades**: a learner climbs when they have proved the level, not
when a school calendar says so. The school grade a book comes
from appears only in each lesson's sources (provenance), never
as the course's organisation. Accounting is one of the three
courses of the Business / Commerce / Accounting split: the
running of a business itself lives in Business; trade lives in
Commerce; the books of account live here.

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in this
course comes from government textbooks — the accounting and
Business Studies books of Dominica, Ecuador, Kenya, Malaysia, Nicaragua, Nigeria, Palestine
and Samoa —
except the **Tip boxes**: a tip in a box is from us, the people
making LiqLearns. Tips are ours; the lessons are the books'.
See ABOUT.md for the full book list, the contents maps, and the
acceptance reckoning.
