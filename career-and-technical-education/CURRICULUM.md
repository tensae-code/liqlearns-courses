# Career and Technical Education course — CURRICULUM (the path)

CTE is the vocational course, organised by **levels, not
grades**: a learner climbs when they are ready, not when a
school calendar says so. The school grades the books come from
appear only in each lesson's sources (provenance), never as the
course's organisation. The path follows the books' own arc (see
TITLES.md): **First Steps → Choosing a Career → Farm Trades →
Salon Trades → Garment and Cloth → Reception and Copying →
Building and Retail → Food, Cleaning and Events → Getting Hired
→ Starting a Business** — choose a career, learn the skill
properly and safely, be employable, plan a business on it.

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in this
course comes from government textbooks — this part from
Ethiopia's CTE books for Grades 7 and 8 — except the **Tip
boxes**: a tip in a box is from us, the people making LiqLearns.
Tips are ours; the lessons are the books'. See ABOUT.md for the
full book list, the two truncated image-only teacher guides
(cited at section level only), and the acceptance reckoning.
