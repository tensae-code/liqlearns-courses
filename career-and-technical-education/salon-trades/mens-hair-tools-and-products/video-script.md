# Video script — Men's hair — the barber's tools and products

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on
list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A barber is known by his station.
The chair. The wrap. The mat.
Clippers and scissors, comb and brush.
Razor and strop, sharp and ready.
Powders and creams, gel, wax, tonic, spray.
The shave: brush, oil, soap.
And in the middle of it all — disinfectant.
Clean tools. A complete station. A professional.
