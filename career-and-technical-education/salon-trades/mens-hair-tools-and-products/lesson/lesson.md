# Men's hair — the barber's tools and products

## Do it first

A client sits down for a cut and a shave. Mentally lay out the station: what does the barber
reach for, in order, from the first comb to the last tonic? Compare your layout with the book's
full kit list in this lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — The cutting and station kit

The barber's station itself: barber chair, barber cloth/wrap, station mat, back mirror and neck
paper/tape. The cutting and styling tools: hair clipper, scissors, comb, hairbrush, hair
dryer/blower, straight razor and strop. Each item has its moment in a service — chair and wrap
first, mirror last, so the client judges the work.

- Station: chair, cloth/wrap, station mat, back mirror, neck paper/tape.
- Tools: clipper, scissors, comb, hairbrush, dryer/blower, straight razor, strop.

## Sub lesson 2 — Products and the shave

The products and shaving side: barber powder, brilliantine, disinfectant, hair cream, gel, hair
spray, tonic and wax — and for the shave: shave brush, shaving oil and shaving soap/cream.
Disinfectant sits in this list on purpose: the hygiene line runs through the kit, into the
sterilization lesson ahead.

- Products: powder, brilliantine, hair cream, gel, hair spray, tonic, wax, disinfectant.
- Shaving: shave brush, shaving oil, shaving soap/cream.
- Pupils list what their local men's stylists actually use.

## Say it to your companion

Lay the kit out for your companion in visiting order: seat the client, wrap, cut, shave, finish
with product, mirror. Name every item as its moment comes.

## Do It Yourself

1. Draw a barber station and label every item from the list.
2. Group the list under cutting / shaving / products / station.
3. Visit or ask a local men's stylist: which listed items do they actually use, and what do
they use that the book omits?
4. Circle the hygiene item in the list and say why it is there.
