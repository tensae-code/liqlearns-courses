# Men's hair — the barber's tools and products — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Which item seats the client at the station?**
a) Barber chair  b) Strop  c) Neck tape
**Answer: a) Barber chair** — the barber chair is the station's centre.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) The back mirror is used so —**
a) the barber can see behind  b) the client can judge the back of the work  c) the light
improves
**Answer: b) the client can judge the back of the work** — the client sees the back in the back
mirror.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) A strop belongs with —**
a) the hair dryer  b) the straight razor  c) the clippers
**Answer: b) the straight razor** — razor and strop are a pair.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Which item is the hygiene member of the list?**
a) Wax  b) Disinfectant  c) Tonic
**Answer: b) Disinfectant** — disinfectant carries the hygiene line in the kit.

**5. (Sub lesson 2) The shaving set named is —**
a) shave brush, shaving oil, shaving soap/cream  b) gel and spray  c) powder and cream
**Answer: a) shave brush, shaving oil, shaving soap/cream** — those three are the shave items.

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Brilliantine, gel, wax and tonic are —**
a) cutting tools  b) hair products  c) cleaning agents
**Answer: b) hair products** — they are among the styling products listed.
