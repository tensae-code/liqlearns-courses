# Men's hair — the barber's tools and products — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Neck paper/tape is part of the barber's kit.**
**Answer: True** — it is listed with the station items.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) A hair dryer/blower is listed among the equipment.**
**Answer: True** — dryer/blower is in the full list.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Shaving soap/cream and a shave brush are both listed.**
**Answer: True** — both appear in the shaving group.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Local stylists must use exactly the book's list and nothing else.**
**Answer: False** — pupils list what local stylists actually use — the lists can differ.
