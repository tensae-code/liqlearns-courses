# Men's hair — the barber's tools and products — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name six items from the barber's kit.**
**Answer:** Any six of: barber chair, hair clipper, barber cloth/wrap, barber powder,
hairbrush, comb, neck paper/tape, back mirror, brilliantine, disinfectant, hair cream, hair
dryer/blower, gel, scissors, hair spray, tonic, wax, shave brush, shaving oil, straight razor,
strop, station mat, shaving soap/cream.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Name the three shaving items.**
**Answer:** Shave brush, shaving oil, and shaving soap/cream.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Which listed product serves hygiene, and where does it lead?**
**Answer:** Disinfectant — it leads into the salon's sterilization and disinfection lesson.
