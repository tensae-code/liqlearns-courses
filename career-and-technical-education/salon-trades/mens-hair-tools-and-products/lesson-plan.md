# Lesson plan — Men's hair — the barber's tools and products

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** Salon Trades · **Lesson:** Men's hair —
the barber's tools and products
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 4 — Salon Trades (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the barber's full kit, from chair and clippers to razor
and strop: the station a men's stylist works from.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the men's technique and safety lessons — hands and
hygiene known; now the complete station.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below,
the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written
as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**.
The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books;
on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as
the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its
source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead,
each opening when the one before it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub
Lessons (ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **The cutting and station kit** — The barber's station itself: barber chair, barber
cloth/wrap, station mat, back mirror and neck paper/tape.
2. **Products and the shave** — The products and shaving side: barber powder, brilliantine,
disinfectant, hair cream, gel, hair spray, tonic and wax — and for the shave: shave brush,
shaving oil and shaving soap/cream.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so
the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Men's hair — the four basic cutting techniques. Recommended, never forced.
- The other Salon Trades lessons stand next door in this same title.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the barber's full listed kit.
2. Group the kit: cutting tools, shaving tools, products, and station equipment.
3. Compare the list with what local men's stylists actually use.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil.

## If the learner is stuck
- Learns the list as a chant → lay it in visiting order; each item earns its place at a moment
of the service.
- Forgets disinfectant → it is the bridge to the hygiene lesson; a kit without it is
incomplete.
