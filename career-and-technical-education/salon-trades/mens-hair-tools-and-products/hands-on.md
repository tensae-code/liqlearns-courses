# Hands-on — Men's hair — the barber's tools and products

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes. Real trade practice, done safely and exactly as
the book teaches it.

## Station set-up drill

Lay out a training station in the book's order — chair, wrap, tools right-to-left as they will
be used, products, mirror — and have a partner check it against the full list, item by item.

## Local kit survey

Record a real local barber's kit photographically or by list (with permission) and diff it
against the book's list, both ways: missing and extra.
