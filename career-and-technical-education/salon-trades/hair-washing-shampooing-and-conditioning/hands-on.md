# Hands-on — Hair washing — how shampooing and conditioning work

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes. Real trade practice, done safely and exactly as
the book teaches it.

## Hairdresser observation visit

Visit a hairdresser and list the techniques actually used in shampooing and conditioning — the
book's own activity — then compare the list with the lesson's massage and warm-water points.

## Wash practice

Wash a model's or volunteer's hair (or a practice head) at a basin: wet, shampoo, massage in
small circles with fingertips through the whole scalp, rinse with warm water, condition —
narrating the why of each step.
