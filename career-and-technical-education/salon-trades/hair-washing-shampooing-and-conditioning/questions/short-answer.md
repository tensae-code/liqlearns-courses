# Hair washing — how shampooing and conditioning work — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Why is detergent needed to wash hair?**
**Answer:** Water alone cannot mix with fatty dirt, so a dispersing agent (detergent) is needed
to dissolve the dirt of hair and scalp.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) How is shampoo worked into the hair, and what does the working add?**
**Answer:** It is massaged into wet hair; the massaging mechanically increases the washing's
effectiveness.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) What water is recommended, and what does its heat do?**
**Answer:** Warm/hot water; heat dilates pores and stimulates the scalp.
