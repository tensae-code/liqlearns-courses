# Hair washing — how shampooing and conditioning work — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Shampoo can come as liquid, cream, solid or powder.**
**Answer: True** — all four forms are named.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Surfactants only clean; they can never also treat hair.**
**Answer: False** — the book says shampoo can also treat the hair.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) The massage is decoration with no cleaning effect.**
**Answer: False** — it mechanically increases washing effectiveness.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Cold water is the book's recommendation for shampooing.**
**Answer: False** — warm/hot water is recommended.
