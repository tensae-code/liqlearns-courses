# Hair washing — how shampooing and conditioning work — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Shampoo and conditioner are cosmetic products built on —**
a) wax  b) surfactants  c) sand
**Answer: b) surfactants** — surfactants are their working base.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Water alone fails on hair dirt because —**
a) it is too cold  b) it cannot mix with fatty dirt  c) it evaporates
**Answer: b) it cannot mix with fatty dirt** — water and fatty dirt will not mix.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) The dispersing agent that dissolves the dirt is —**
a) a detergent  b) a comb  c) hot air
**Answer: a) a detergent** — detergent disperses the fatty dirt of hair and scalp.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Shampoo is applied by —**
a) pouring and waiting  b) massaging it into wet hair  c) rubbing with a towel
**Answer: b) massaging it into wet hair** — massaged into wet hair.

**5. (Sub lesson 2) Massaging during washing —**
a) only relaxes the client  b) mechanically increases washing effectiveness  c) damages the
hair
**Answer: b) mechanically increases washing effectiveness** — the massage adds mechanical
cleaning power.

**6. (Sub lesson 2) The water recommended is —**
a) ice cold  b) warm/hot — heat dilates pores and stimulates the scalp  c) boiling
**Answer: b) warm/hot — heat dilates pores and stimulates the scalp** — warm/hot, with the
pore/scalp note.
