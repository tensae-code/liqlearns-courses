# Video script — Hair washing — how shampooing and conditioning work

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on
list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Water alone cannot wash hair. Here is why.
Hair's dirt is fatty. Water and fat refuse to mix.
Shampoo carries a bridge: surfactants.
They grab the dirt and let the water carry it away.
Now the hands: massage it in, small circles.
The massage itself cleans — mechanically.
Warm water, too: it opens the pores, wakes the scalp.
Science, in a basin. That is a proper wash.
