# Lesson plan — Women's hair — the basic styling techniques

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** Salon Trades · **Lesson:** Women's hair
— the basic styling techniques
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 4 — Salon Trades (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** women's styling is washing with shampoo, applying
styling lotion, setting the hair on rollers and drying it gently before shaping a soft curl
style — with six basic techniques: blow drying, straightening, smoothing, curling, finger
drying, and hair up — and every styling begins with three preparations.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the hair-care lesson and beside the men's lessons —
the women's side of the salon, in its own terms.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below,
the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written
as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**.
The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books;
on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as
the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its
source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead,
each opening when the one before it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub
Lessons (ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **The definition and the three preparations** — Women's styling is defined as washing with
shampoo, applying styling lotion, setting the hair on rollers and drying it gently before
shaping a soft curl style.
2. **The six techniques** — The basic techniques: blow drying (flat, root lift, movement),
straightening, smoothing, curling (waves and curls), finger drying (natural look, spiky effect)
and hair up (plait, pleat, rolls, curls).

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so
the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Hair care — clean, healthy, attractive hair as a trade. Recommended, never
forced.
- The other Salon Trades lessons stand next door in this same title.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the book's definition of women's styling: shampoo wash, styling lotion, rollers,
gentle drying, soft curl shaping.
2. Name the six techniques, with blow drying's three looks (flat, root lift, movement), finger
drying's (natural look, spiky effect), and hair up's forms (plait, pleat, rolls, curls).
3. Recite the three preparations that begin every styling: prepare yourself, prepare the
client, prepare the work area.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Rollers and a dryer help for practice, under a practitioner.

## If the learner is stuck
- Treats the definition as one style only → it is the book's styling sequence; the six
techniques range far beyond it. Learn both, in their places.
- Forgets a preparation → the three are a gate rhyme: yourself, client, area — in that order,
every time.
