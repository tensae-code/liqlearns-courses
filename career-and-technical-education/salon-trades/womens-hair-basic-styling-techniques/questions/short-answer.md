# Women's hair — the basic styling techniques — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Give the book's definition of women's styling.**
**Answer:** Washing with shampoo, applying styling lotion, setting the hair on rollers and
drying it gently before shaping a soft curl style.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Name the six basic techniques.**
**Answer:** Blow drying, straightening, smoothing, curling, finger drying, and hair up.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Which technique gives root lift, and which gives a spiky effect?**
**Answer:** Blow drying gives root lift (flat, root lift, movement); finger drying gives the
spiky effect (and the natural look).
