# Women's hair — the basic styling techniques — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Styling lotion is applied after the hair is dried.**
**Answer: False** — lotion comes after the shampoo wash, before the rollers.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) The work area is one of the three preparations.**
**Answer: True** — yourself, the client, the work area.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Curling produces waves and curls.**
**Answer: True** — that is its named output.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Hair up is a single fixed shape.**
**Answer: False** — it takes plait, pleat, rolls and curls forms.
