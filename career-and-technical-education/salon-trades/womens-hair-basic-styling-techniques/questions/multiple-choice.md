# Women's hair — the basic styling techniques — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) The book's styling chain begins —**
a) rollers first  b) washing with shampoo, then styling lotion  c) drying
**Answer: b) washing with shampoo, then styling lotion** — shampoo, then lotion, then rollers.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) After the rollers, the hair is —**
a) cut short  b) dried gently, then shaped into a soft curl style  c) left wet
**Answer: b) dried gently, then shaped into a soft curl style** — gentle drying, then the soft
curl shaping.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Every styling begins with three preparations: —**
a) wash, rinse, dry  b) prepare yourself, prepare the client, prepare the work area  c) comb,
cut, colour
**Answer: b) prepare yourself, prepare the client, prepare the work area** — yourself, client,
work area.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Blow drying can give —**
a) flat, root lift, or movement  b) only curls  c) a shaved look
**Answer: a) flat, root lift, or movement** — flat, root lift and movement are its looks.

**5. (Sub lesson 2) Finger drying gives —**
a) a plait  b) a natural look or a spiky effect  c) waves only
**Answer: b) a natural look or a spiky effect** — natural look and spiky effect are finger
drying's.

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Hair up includes —**
a) plait, pleat, rolls and curls  b) only a ponytail  c) shaving
**Answer: a) plait, pleat, rolls and curls** — plait, pleat, rolls and curls are named.
