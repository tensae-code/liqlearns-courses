# Video script — Nail care — trimming, filing and setting up a treatment

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on
list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Nails are health, not just beauty.
Trim them. File them.
Fungus finds less room. Thick nails press less painfully.
And before any treatment, four preparations:
the worker — ready.
The client — ready.
The area — ready.
The products, tools and equipment — ready.
Then, and only then, begin.
