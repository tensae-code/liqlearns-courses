# Hands-on — Nail care — trimming, filing and setting up a treatment

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes. Real trade practice, done safely and exactly as
the book teaches it.

## Treatment set-up drill

Set up a full nail treatment station by the four preparations, in order, narrating each: worker
ready, client seated and comfortable, area clean and lit, products/tools/equipment laid out —
checked by a partner against the card.

## Filing practice

On a practice nail or model (or your own, as a practitioner directs), file in ONE direction
along the edge, shaping it smooth — the direction is the technique, as the GIF shows; never saw
back and forth.
