# Lesson plan — Nail care — trimming, filing and setting up a treatment

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** Salon Trades · **Lesson:** Nail care —
trimming, filing and setting up a treatment
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 4 — Salon Trades (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** keeping nails trimmed and filed reduces fungus in the
nails and relieves the pressure pain thickened nails cause — and a treatment is set up in four
preparations: beautician, client, work area, and products, tools and equipment.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the salon lessons before it — the hair side is
stocked; now hands and nails.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below,
the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written
as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**.
The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books;
on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as
the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its
source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead,
each opening when the one before it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub
Lessons (ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **Why nails are cared for** — Keeping nails trimmed and filed reduces fungus in the nails
and relieves the pressure pain thickened nails cause.
2. **Setting up — the four preparations** — A treatment is set up in four preparations: (1)
prepare the beautician/worker, (2) prepare the model (client), (3) prepare the work area, and
(4) prepare the products, tools and equipment.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so
the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the hair lessons in this title. Recommended, never forced.
- The other Salon Trades lessons stand next door in this same title.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say the two health reasons for trimmed, filed nails: less fungus in the nails; relief of
pressure pain from thickened nails.
2. Say what nail-care tools and equipment are: the durable, hand-held articles of the service.
3. Recite the four preparations of a treatment, in order.

## What you need
For practice: nail clippers and a file, used only as a supervising practitioner directs; a
model hand or practice nails help. Paper and pencil.

direction is the named technique.

afterthought.
