# Nail care — trimming, filing and setting up a treatment — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Give nail care's two health reasons.**
**Answer:** Trimmed and filed nails reduce fungus in the nails, and trimming relieves the
pressure pain thickened nails cause.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) List the four preparations in order.**
**Answer:** Prepare the beautician/worker; prepare the model (client); prepare the work area;
prepare the products, tools and equipment.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) How does the book define nail-care tools and equipment?**
**Answer:** The durable, hand-held articles of the service.
