# Nail care — trimming, filing and setting up a treatment — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Nail care is appearance only, with no health reasons.**
**Answer: False** — fungus reduction and pressure-pain relief are health reasons.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Hand-held articles that are durable define the nail tools.**
**Answer: True** — that is the book's definition.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) The work area is prepared third in the four preparations.**
**Answer: True** — worker, client, area, then kit.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) A treatment can begin with nothing prepared.**
**Answer: False** — four preparations set it up.
