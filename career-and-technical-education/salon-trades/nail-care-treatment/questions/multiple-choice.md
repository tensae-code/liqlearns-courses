# Nail care — trimming, filing and setting up a treatment — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Trimmed and filed nails reduce —**
a) fungus in the nails  b) hair growth  c) nail colour
**Answer: a) fungus in the nails** — less fungus is the first health reason.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Thickened nails, kept trimmed, give relief from —**
a) pressure pain  b) hunger  c) tiredness
**Answer: a) pressure pain** — pressure pain is relieved by trimming.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Nail-care tools and equipment are —**
a) the durable, hand-held articles of the service  b) single-use papers  c) the salon's
furniture
**Answer: a) the durable, hand-held articles of the service** — durable and hand-held defines
them.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) The first preparation of a treatment is —**
a) the work area  b) the beautician/worker  c) the products
**Answer: b) the beautician/worker** — prepare the worker first.

**5. (Sub lesson 2) The second preparation is —**
a) the model (client)  b) the tools  c) the area
**Answer: a) the model (client)** — the model/client is second.

**6. (Sub lesson 2) The four preparations are completed by —**
a) payment  b) prepare the products, tools and equipment  c) a photograph
**Answer: b) prepare the products, tools and equipment** — products, tools and equipment are
the fourth.
