# Salon hygiene — sterilizing and disinfecting tools — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Sterilization removes —**
a) most bacteria  b) all bacteria, fungi and viruses  c) only dust
**Answer: b) all bacteria, fungi and viruses** — all is the sterilization claim.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Sterilization matters most for —**
a) new tools in packets  b) tools that have been used  c) the salon's chairs
**Answer: b) tools that have been used** — used tools are its concern.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Which belongs to the sterilization kit?**
a) Labelled, rigid, airtight washable containers for used equipment  b) A hair dryer  c) Barber
powder
**Answer: a) Labelled, rigid, airtight washable containers for used equipment** — the
containers are kit, with hand rub, soap, towels and gloves.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Disinfection kills —**
a) everything, always  b) most — not all — bacteria and fungi on tool surfaces  c) nothing
**Answer: b) most — not all — bacteria and fungi on tool surfaces** — most, not all, is the
book's exact claim.

**5. (Sub lesson 2) Disinfection cannot destroy —**
a) soap bubbles  b) resistant bacterial spores or some viruses  c) water
**Answer: b) resistant bacterial spores or some viruses** — spores and some viruses resist it.

**6. (Sub lesson 2) The between-clients routine order is —**
a) store, wash, rinse, dry, disinfect  b) wash in hot soapy water, rinse, dry, disinfect, store
clean  c) disinfect only
**Answer: b) wash in hot soapy water, rinse, dry, disinfect, store clean** — wash, rinse, dry,
disinfect, store clean.
