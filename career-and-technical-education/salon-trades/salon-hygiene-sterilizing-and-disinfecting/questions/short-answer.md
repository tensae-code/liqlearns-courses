# Salon hygiene — sterilizing and disinfecting tools — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Define sterilization and disinfection against each other.**
**Answer:** Sterilization removes all bacteria, fungi and viruses — the strongest form of
cleaning. Disinfection kills most (not all) bacteria and fungi on tool surfaces and cannot
destroy resistant bacterial spores or some viruses.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Run the between-clients routine in order.**
**Answer:** Wash in hot soapy water (dishwashing liquid, liquid soap or shampoo), rinse
thoroughly, dry, disinfect, and store clean.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Name the sterilization kit.**
**Answer:** Alcohol-based hand rub, liquid soap, disposable hand towels, disposable gloves, and
labelled, rigid, airtight washable containers for used equipment.
