# Video script — Salon hygiene — sterilizing and disinfecting tools

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on
list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Clean is not a feeling. It is a routine.
Sterilization: the strongest cleaning there is.
It removes all bacteria, all fungi, all viruses.
Disinfection: strong — but honest.
It kills most. Not all. Spores resist it.
So between every client, every tool:
wash it hot and soapy. Rinse it. Dry it.
Disinfect it. Store it clean.
Every client. Every time. No exceptions.
