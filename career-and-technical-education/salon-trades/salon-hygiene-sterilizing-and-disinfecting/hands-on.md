# Hands-on — Salon hygiene — sterilizing and disinfecting tools

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes. Real trade practice, done safely and exactly as
the book teaches it.

## Between-clients routine drill

Run the full routine on training tools exactly in order — wash in hot soapy water, rinse, dry,
disinfect, store clean — wearing disposable gloves, under a practitioner. The chemicals are
handled only as the supervising adult directs, exactly as the book teaches.

## Salon hygiene audit

Audit a working salon's hygiene against the lesson: containers labelled and airtight? towels
disposable? routine between every client? Report each point plainly.
