# Salon hygiene — sterilizing and disinfecting tools

## Do it first

One comb, six clients. After client one, the comb goes straight to client two's head. What
travels with it? Now design the comb's day properly — what should happen to it between every
two clients? This lesson is the salon's hygiene line, taught exactly as the book teaches it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Sterilization — the strongest cleaning

Sterilization is the strongest form of cleaning: it removes all bacteria, fungi and viruses,
and matters most for tools that have been used. Its kit: alcohol-based hand rub, liquid soap,
disposable hand towels, disposable gloves, and labelled, rigid, airtight washable containers
for used equipment.

- Sterilization removes ALL bacteria, fungi and viruses.
- Matters most for tools that have been used.
- Kit: hand rub, liquid soap, disposable towels, disposable gloves, labelled rigid airtight
washable containers.

## Sub lesson 2 — Disinfection — strong, with limits, and the routine

Disinfection kills most — not all — bacteria and fungi on tool surfaces, and cannot destroy
resistant bacterial spores or some viruses. Tools are sanitised and disinfected between every
client: first washed in hot soapy water (dishwashing liquid, liquid soap or shampoo), rinsed
thoroughly, dried, disinfected — a barbicide-type solution is named as common salon practice —
and stored clean.

- Disinfection: kills most, not all; spores and some viruses resist it.
- Routine between every client: wash (hot soapy water) → rinse → dry → disinfect → store clean.
- Named disinfectant practice: barbicide-type solution.

## Say it to your companion

State the two definitions against each other for your companion: "Sterilization — all.
Disinfection — most, with named limits." Then run the routine in order, five stops.

## Do It Yourself

1. Write the routine as a five-stop journey card for one tool and pin it at the wash point.
2. List the sterilization kit and check a real salon's kit against it (by visit or asking).
3. Write the limits of disinfection in your own words, without softening them.
4. Say why 'between every client' has no exceptions.
