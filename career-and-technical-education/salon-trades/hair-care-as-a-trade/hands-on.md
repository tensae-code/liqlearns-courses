# Hands-on — Hair care — clean, healthy, attractive hair as a trade

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes. Real trade practice, done safely and exactly as
the book teaches it.

## Salon and barbershop observation

Visit a salon and a barbershop (the guide's salon-visit method belongs to this unit's opening —
teacher guide — evidence, placement 13) and record: services offered, products sold, and how
the two places differ.

## Hair-type survey

With permission, survey volunteers' hair types (no touching without consent) and note how care
routines differ by type and by any processes applied.
