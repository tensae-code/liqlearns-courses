# Video script — Hair care — clean, healthy, attractive hair as a trade

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on
list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Your hair is your crown. Care is a trade.
Hair care keeps hair clean, healthy, attractive.
Washing, treating, styling — methods and tools.
But listen: all hair is not the same.
Type differs. Treatments differ. Cultures differ.
Salons. Barbershops. Spas.
People earn their living here. You could too.
