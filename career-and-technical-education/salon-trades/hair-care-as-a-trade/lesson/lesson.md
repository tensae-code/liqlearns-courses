# Hair care — clean, healthy, attractive hair as a trade

## Do it first

Three clients sit down: one with straight fine hair, one with tight curls, one with hair
treated by chemicals. Can one routine serve all three? This lesson opens the salon unit with
what hair care is — and why all hair is not the same.

## Sub lesson 1 — What hair care is

Hair care is the hygiene and cosmetology of head hair: keeping hair clean, healthy-looking and
attractive by different methods and tools. It differs by hair type, by the processes applied,
and by the individual's culture — all hair is not the same, and hair shows human diversity.

- Hygiene + cosmetology of head hair.
- Goal: clean, healthy-looking, attractive.
- Differs by: hair type, processes, culture.

## Sub lesson 2 — Hair care as a trade

Services are offered in salons, barbershops and day spas, and products are sold for home use.
Hair care is named as an income-generating career supporting livelihoods — a trade a person can
build a living on, as this whole unit shows.

- Places: salons, barbershops, day spas.
- Products sold for home use.
- An income-generating career supporting livelihoods.

## Say it to your companion

Define hair care for your companion and finish with the diversity line: "All hair is not the
same." Your companion names a difference — type, process or culture — for two clients you
describe.

## Do It Yourself

1. List the hair-care places in your locality: salon, barbershop, spa.
2. Note three hair types you see daily and one care difference for each.
3. Write the definition from memory and check it.
4. Ask a practitioner: what share of clients take services, and what share buy products for
home?
