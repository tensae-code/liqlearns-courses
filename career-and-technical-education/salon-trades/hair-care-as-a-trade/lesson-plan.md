# Lesson plan — Hair care — clean, healthy, attractive hair as a trade

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** Salon Trades · **Lesson:** Hair care —
clean, healthy, attractive hair as a trade
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 4 — Salon Trades (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** hair care is the hygiene and cosmetology of head hair —
keeping hair clean, healthy-looking and attractive by different methods and tools — differing
by hair type, processes applied and culture, and offered as an income-generating career.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the learner's own hair and the barbershops and salons
they already know.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below,
the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written
as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**.
The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books;
on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as
the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its
source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead,
each opening when the one before it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub
Lessons (ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **What hair care is** — Hair care is the hygiene and cosmetology of head hair: keeping hair
clean, healthy-looking and attractive by different methods and tools.
2. **Hair care as a trade** — Services are offered in salons, barbershops and day spas, and
products are sold for home use.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Define hair care exactly.
2. Say the three ways hair care differs: hair type, processes applied, the individual's
culture.
3. Name where services are offered: salons, barbershops and day spas; and what is sold for home
use.
4. Say hair care's economic place: an income-generating career supporting livelihoods.

## What you need

## If the learner is stuck
- Treats hair care as 'washing' only → it is hygiene AND cosmetology: cleanliness, health,
appearance.
