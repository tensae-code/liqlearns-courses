# Hair care — clean, healthy, attractive hair as a trade — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Hair care is —**
a) the hygiene and cosmetology of head hair  b) only cutting hair short  c) a kind of medicine
**Answer: a) the hygiene and cosmetology of head hair** — hygiene and cosmetology of head hair.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Hair care aims to keep hair —**
a) hidden  b) clean, healthy-looking and attractive  c) always wet
**Answer: b) clean, healthy-looking and attractive** — clean, healthy-looking and attractive is
the aim.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Hair care differs by —**
a) hair type, processes applied, and culture  b) the weather only  c) price only
**Answer: a) hair type, processes applied, and culture** — those three are named.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Hair services are offered in —**
a) salons, barbershops and day spas  b) schools only  c) markets only
**Answer: a) salons, barbershops and day spas** — the three named places.

**5. (Sub lesson 2) Besides services, salons sell —**
a) products for home use  b) food  c) tools only to barbers
**Answer: a) products for home use** — products for home use are sold.

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Hair care is named as —**
a) a hobby only  b) an income-generating career supporting livelihoods  c) a dying trade
**Answer: b) an income-generating career supporting livelihoods** — it supports livelihoods.
