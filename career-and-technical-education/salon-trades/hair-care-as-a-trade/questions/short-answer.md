# Hair care — clean, healthy, attractive hair as a trade — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Define hair care.**
**Answer:** The hygiene and cosmetology of head hair — keeping hair clean, healthy-looking and
attractive by different methods and tools.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Name where hair services are offered.**
**Answer:** Salons, barbershops and day spas (with products sold for home use).

**3. (Sub lesson 2) In what three ways does hair care differ?**
**Answer:** By hair type, by the processes applied, and by the individual's culture.
