# Hair care — clean, healthy, attractive hair as a trade — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) All hair is the same, so one care routine fits everyone.**
**Answer: False** — hair differs by type, process and culture; it shows human diversity.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Hair care uses different methods and tools.**
**Answer: True** — methods and tools vary with the hair and the process.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Day spas offer hair services.**
**Answer: True** — salons, barbershops and day spas are named.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Hair care cannot be a career.**
**Answer: False** — it is named an income-generating career.
