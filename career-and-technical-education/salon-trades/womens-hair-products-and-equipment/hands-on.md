# Hands-on — Women's hair — products and equipment

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes. Real trade practice, done safely and exactly as
the book teaches it.

## Salon recording visit

Visit a women's salon and record, in order: the steps of a service, the tools used, the
equipment present, and the products applied — the book's own recording task.

## Trolley stocking drill

From a mixed box (real or drawn items), stock a trolley in two shelves correctly against the
lists, timed, with a partner checking each item's shelf.
