# Women's hair — products and equipment — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Products in styling do what?**
a) Feed the client  b) Change hair's texture or shape, or hold a style in place  c) Clean the
floor
**Answer: b) Change hair's texture or shape, or hold a style in place** — texture/shape change
or holding the style.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Which is a product kind from the list?**
a) Mousses and activators  b) Scissors  c) Hood dryer
**Answer: a) Mousses and activators** — mousses and activators are products.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Heat protectors belong to —**
a) the equipment list  b) the product list  c) neither
**Answer: b) the product list** — heat protectors are products — against the heat tools.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) A hood dryer and a hand-held dryer are —**
a) products  b) equipment  c) decorations
**Answer: b) equipment** — both are drying equipment.

**5. (Sub lesson 2) Rollers come in —**
a) one size only  b) short, medium and long  c) left and right
**Answer: b) short, medium and long** — three lengths are listed.

**6. (Sub lesson 2) The three clip kinds named are —**
a) duckbill, double-pronged, single-pronged  b) big, bigger, biggest  c) metal, plastic, wood
**Answer: a) duckbill, double-pronged, single-pronged** — duckbill, double-pronged and single-
pronged clips.
