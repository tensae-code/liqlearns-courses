# Women's hair — products and equipment — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Gels and lotions are products.**
**Answer: True** — both are in the six product kinds.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Moisturizers change the salon's lighting.**
**Answer: False** — products change the hair's texture/shape or hold its style.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Feathers and ribbons are decorative equipment items.**
**Answer: True** — decorative feathers and ribbons close the equipment list.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Straighteners and curling tongs are setting tools with no heat.**
**Answer: False** — they are heat tools, like the dryers and heated rollers.
