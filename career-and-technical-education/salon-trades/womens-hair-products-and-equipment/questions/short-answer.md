# Women's hair — products and equipment — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the six product kinds.**
**Answer:** Lotions, mousses, activators, gels, moisturizers and heat protectors.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Name four heat items from the equipment list.**
**Answer:** Any four of: hand-held dryer, hood dryer, diffuser, nozzle, straighteners, curling
tongs, heated rollers.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) What three clip kinds are named?**
**Answer:** Duckbill, double-pronged and single-pronged clips.
