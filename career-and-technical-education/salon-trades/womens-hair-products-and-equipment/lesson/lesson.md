# Women's hair — products and equipment

## Do it first

A stylist's trolley rolls to the chair. Top shelf: the bottles and jars that will change the
hair or hold the style. Below: the heated tools, rollers, clips and pins that do the shaping.
Stock both shelves from memory — then check against the book's two lists.

## Sub lesson 1 — The products

Products change hair's texture or shape, or hold a style in place. The six kinds: lotions,
mousses, activators, gels, moisturizers and heat protectors. Note the last one — heat
protection stands in the product list because the equipment list is full of heat.

- Products: change texture/shape, or hold a style.
- Six kinds: lotions, mousses, activators, gels, moisturizers, heat protectors.

## Sub lesson 2 — The equipment

Equipment: hand-held dryer, hood dryer, diffuser and nozzle; round and flat brushes; rollers
secured with pins, in short, medium and long; pin-curl clips; straighteners; curling tongs;
heated rollers; combs and brushes; clips — duckbill, double-pronged, single-pronged; pins and
clamps; plus decorative feathers and ribbons. Pupils visit a women's salon and record its
steps, tools, equipment and products.

- Heat: hand/hood dryers, diffuser, nozzle, straighteners, tongs, heated rollers.
- Setting: rollers (short/medium/long), pin-curl clips, pins, clamps, sectioning clips of three
kinds.
- Finishing decoration: feathers and ribbons.

## Say it to your companion

Stock the trolley aloud for your companion: "Top shelf — lotion, mousse, activator, gel,
moisturizer, heat protector. Below — dryers, brushes, rollers, tongs…" Your companion names a
job; you hand over the product and the tool for it.

## Do It Yourself

1. Make the two-shelf trolley chart and fill both shelves.
2. Sort the equipment under heat / setting / decoration.
3. Visit a women's salon (the book's own activity) and record its steps, tools, equipment and
products.
4. Write why heat protectors earn a place in the product list.
