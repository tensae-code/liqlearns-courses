# Video script — Women's hair — products and equipment

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on
list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Know your trolley.
Top shelf, the products: lotion, mousse, activator,
gel, moisturizer — and heat protector.
They change the hair, or hold the style.
Below, the equipment: dryers and diffusers,
brushes round and flat, rollers long and short,
tongs, straighteners, clips and pins —
and feathers and ribbons for the finish.
A stocked trolley is a ready stylist.
