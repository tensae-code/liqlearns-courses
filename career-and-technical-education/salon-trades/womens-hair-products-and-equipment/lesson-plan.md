# Lesson plan — Women's hair — products and equipment

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** Salon Trades · **Lesson:** Women's hair
— products and equipment
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 4 — Salon Trades (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** products change hair's texture or shape or hold a style
in place — lotions, mousses, activators, gels, moisturizers and heat protectors — and the
equipment runs from hand and hood dryers to rollers, clips, pins and decorative feathers and
ribbons.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands after the women's technique lessons — the hands know the
techniques; now the trolley that serves them.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below,
the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written
as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**.
The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books;
on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as
the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its
source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead,
each opening when the one before it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub
Lessons (ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **The products** — Products change hair's texture or shape, or hold a style in place.
2. **The equipment** — Equipment: hand-held dryer, hood dryer, diffuser and nozzle; round and
flat brushes; rollers secured with pins, in short, medium and long; pin-curl clips;
straighteners; curling tongs; heated rollers; combs and brushes; clips — duckbill, double-
pronged, single-pronged; pins and clamps; plus decorative feathers and ribbons.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so
the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Women's hair — blow-drying well. Recommended, never forced.
- The other Salon Trades lessons stand next door in this same title.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what products do: change texture or shape, or hold a style in place.
2. Name the six product kinds.
3. Name the equipment families: dryers (hand-held, hood, diffuser, nozzle), brushes (round,
flat), rollers and heated rollers, straighteners and curling tongs, combs, clips (duckbill,
double-pronged, single-pronged), pins and clamps, pin-curl clips, rollers in short/medium/long,
decorative feathers and ribbons.
