# Hands-on — Skin care — looking after skin, and what cosmetics do

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes. Real trade practice, done safely and exactly as
the book teaches it.

## Facial sequence observation

Observe a facial treatment at a salon (with permission) and tick off which of the book's family
appears, in what order — steam, exfoliation, extraction, creams/lotions, mask/peel, massage.

## Emollient and sun practice

Practise the daily base: applying an appropriate emollient to dry skin on hands/arms, and plan
shade/covering for a midday outdoor task — the book's own daily practices, done properly.
