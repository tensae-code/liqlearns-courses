# Skin care — looking after skin, and what cosmetics do — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Define skin care with its base practices.**
**Answer:** The range of practices supporting skin integrity, enhancing appearance and
relieving skin conditions — nutrition, avoiding excessive sun, and appropriate emollients, plus
cosmetics for appearance.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) List the facial treatment family.**
**Answer:** Steam, exfoliation (physical and chemical), extraction, creams, lotions, masks,
peels and massage.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) What are cosmetics, and name two things they do.**
**Answer:** Mixtures of chemical compounds from natural or synthetic sources; any two of:
cleanse/protect body and skin, conceal blemishes, enhance features, add colour and fragrance,
change a face's appearance entirely.
