# Skin care — looking after skin, and what cosmetics do — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Avoiding excessive sun is part of skin care.**
**Answer: True** — it is one of the base practices.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Skin care has nothing to do with skin that is too dry or too moist.**
**Answer: False** — care of too-dry or too-moist skin is named in its reach.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Peels and masks are part of facial skin care.**
**Answer: True** — both are in the facial family.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Cosmetics can change a face's appearance entirely.**
**Answer: True** — that is one of their named uses.
