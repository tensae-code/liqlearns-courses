# Skin care — looking after skin, and what cosmetics do — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Skin care supports —**
a) only appearance  b) skin integrity, appearance, and relief of skin conditions  c) hair
growth
**Answer: b) skin integrity, appearance, and relief of skin conditions** — all three purposes
are named.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) The base practices include —**
a) nutrition, avoiding excessive sun, and appropriate emollients  b) daily bleaching  c) no
washing
**Answer: a) nutrition, avoiding excessive sun, and appropriate emollients** — those three are
the base.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Skin care also reaches into —**
a) wound healing and dermatitis prevention  b) car repair  c) hair cutting
**Answer: a) wound healing and dermatitis prevention** — wound healing and dermatitis
prevention are named contexts.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Which belongs to the facial family?**
a) Steam, exfoliation, extraction, masks and massage  b) Only soap and water  c) Shaving
**Answer: a) Steam, exfoliation, extraction, masks and massage** — the facial family is listed.

**5. (Sub lesson 2) Exfoliation can be —**
a) physical and chemical  b) only physical  c) neither
**Answer: a) physical and chemical** — physical and chemical exfoliation are both named.

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Cosmetics are —**
a) pure plant juices only  b) mixtures of chemical compounds from natural or synthetic sources
c) a kind of food
**Answer: b) mixtures of chemical compounds from natural or synthetic sources** — natural or
synthetic chemical compounds, mixed.
