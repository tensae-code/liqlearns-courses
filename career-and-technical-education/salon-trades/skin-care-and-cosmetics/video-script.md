# Video script — Skin care — looking after skin, and what cosmetics do

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on
list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Skin is the body's first wall. Care for it.
Feed it well. Do not bake it in the sun.
Soothe it with the right emollient.
In the salon: steam, exfoliation, extraction,
creams and masks, peels and massage — the facial.
And cosmetics? Mixtures, natural or made,
to cleanse, to conceal, to colour, to transform.
That is skin care — health first, beauty with it.
