# Lesson plan — Skin care — looking after skin, and what cosmetics do

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** Salon Trades · **Lesson:** Skin care —
looking after skin, and what cosmetics do
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 4 — Salon Trades (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** skin care is the range of practices that support skin
integrity, enhance appearance and relieve skin conditions — nutrition, avoiding excessive sun,
and appropriate emollients, plus cosmetics — facial treatments run steam, exfoliation,
extraction, creams, lotions, masks, peels and massage, and cosmetics are mixtures of chemical
compounds from natural or synthetic sources.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands at the salon title's end — hair and nails done; now the
skin, the salon's third surface.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead,

2. **Facials and cosmetics** — Facial skin care is the named treatment family — steam,

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Define skin care and its base practices: nutrition, avoiding excessive sun, appropriate
emollients, plus cosmetics for appearance.
2. Say skin care's wider reach: wound healing, radiation therapy and medication contexts; skin
too dry or too moist; dermatitis and injury prevention.
3. List the facial treatment family: steam, exfoliation (physical and chemical), extraction,
creams, lotions, masks, peels, massage.
4. Define cosmetics: mixtures of chemical compounds from natural or synthetic sources, used to
cleanse/protect, conceal blemishes, enhance features, add colour and fragrance, or change a
face's appearance entirely.

## If the learner is stuck

are the base, and the reach runs to wound healing and prevention.

creams/masks/peels treat, massage closes — the order is a treatment logic.
