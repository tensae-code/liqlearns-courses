# Hands-on — Women's hair — blow-drying well

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes. Real trade practice, done safely and exactly as
the book teaches it.

## Blow-dry practice

On a practice head, prepare fully (judge the hair, choose the product, name your problem plan
aloud), then blow-dry section by section with brush and dryer together, under a practitioner.

## Salon observation

Observe a professional blow-dry and note the preparation actually done before the switch, and
any mid-dry problem and its handling.
