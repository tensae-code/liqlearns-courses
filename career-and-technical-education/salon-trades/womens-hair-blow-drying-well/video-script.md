# Video script — Women's hair — blow-drying well

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on
list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A great blow-dry starts in silence — dryer off.
Feel the hair. Thick or fine? Dry or treated?
Choose the product this hair needs.
Ask: what could go wrong? Know your answer.
Now — switch on.
Brush rolled under the section. Dryer following.
The hair smooths as they pass.
Section by section. That is blow-drying well.
