# Women's hair — blow-drying well — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) List the three things done before the dryer switches on.**
**Answer:** Identify the condition and thickness of the hair; choose a product that will
support the blow-dry on that hair; know what problems may occur during drying and how to deal
with them.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Describe the brush-and-dryer motion.**
**Answer:** The brush rolls under a section of hair while the dryer follows it, and the hair
smooths as they pass.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Why is preparation called the first half of the skill?**
**Answer:** Because the hair's condition, the product and the problem plan are all decided
before the switch — the dry can only succeed on what preparation set up.
