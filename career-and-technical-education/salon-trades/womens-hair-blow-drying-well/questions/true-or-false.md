# Women's hair — blow-drying well — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Thickness of hair is part of what you identify first.**
**Answer: True** — condition and thickness are both identified.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Any product will support any hair's blow-dry.**
**Answer: False** — the product is chosen for that hair.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Problems during drying should be a surprise you meet unprepared.**
**Answer: False** — you must know in advance what may occur and how to deal with it.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Brush and dryer work together in one passing motion.**
**Answer: True** — the brush rolls under the section while the dryer follows it.
