# Women's hair — blow-drying well — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Blow-drying skill starts —**
a) when the dryer screams loudest  b) before the dryer switches on  c) after the style is
finished
**Answer: b) before the dryer switches on** — preparation is where the skill starts.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) First, identify —**
a) the client's age  b) the condition and thickness of the hair in hand  c) the dryer's brand
**Answer: b) the condition and thickness of the hair in hand** — condition and thickness of
this hair.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) The product chosen must —**
a) be the most expensive  b) support the blow-dry on that hair  c) smell strongest
**Answer: b) support the blow-dry on that hair** — the product supports this hair's blow-dry.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Before drying you must also know —**
a) the salon's history  b) what problems may occur during drying and how to deal with them  c)
tomorrow's clients
**Answer: b) what problems may occur during drying and how to deal with them** — problems and
their handling are prepared in advance.

**5. (Sub lesson 2) In the moving skill, the dryer —**
a) leads the way alone  b) follows the brush as it rolls under a section  c) stays pointed at
the ceiling
**Answer: b) follows the brush as it rolls under a section** — brush rolls under, dryer
follows, hair smooths.

**6. (Sub lesson 2) The work proceeds —**
a) all hair at once  b) section by section  c) only at the front
**Answer: b) section by section** — section by section over the whole head.
