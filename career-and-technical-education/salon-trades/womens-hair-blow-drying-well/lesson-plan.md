# Lesson plan — Women's hair — blow-drying well

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** Salon Trades · **Lesson:** Women's hair
— blow-drying well
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 4 — Salon Trades (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** blow-drying skill starts before the dryer switches on:
identify the condition and thickness of the hair in hand, choose a product that will support
the blow-dry on that hair, and know what problems may occur during drying and how to deal with
them.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands inside the women's techniques lesson — blow drying was
one of six; now it gets its own depth.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below,

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead,
each opening when the one before it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub
Lessons (ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **Before the dryer switches on** — Blow-drying skill starts before the dryer switches on:
(1) identify the condition and thickness of the hair in hand, (2) choose a product that will
support the blow-dry on that hair, and (3) know what hair problems may occur during drying and
how to deal with them.
2. **The moving skill** — With preparation done, the blow-dry itself is a two-handed moving
skill: the brush rolls under a section of hair while the dryer follows it, and the hair smooths
as the pair passes — the motion the GIF in this lesson shows, section by section, over the
whole head.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so
the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Do the three before-switch-on steps: identify condition and thickness; choose a supporting
product for that hair; know the problems that may occur and how to deal with them.
2. Work the moving skill: brush rolling under a section while the dryer follows, hair smoothing
as it passes.

## What you need
For practice: a hand dryer, a round brush, a practice head or model, and a supervising
practitioner. Paper and pencil.

## If the learner is stuck
- Switches on first → stop the hand: identify, choose, anticipate — three spoken checks before
any heat.
- Brush and dryer drift apart → they are one instrument in two hands; the smoothing happens
where they travel together.
