# Hair styling — reading the client before you cut — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) List the six factors weighed before styling.**
**Answer:** Previous chemical services, percentage of grey, the client's requirements, tools
and equipment, added hair, and how the style will be maintained.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Name the three texture grades and four face shapes.**
**Answer:** Textures: fine, medium, coarse. Face shapes (any four): oval, round, square,
oblong, heart, pear.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Name three growth patterns from the list.**
**Answer:** Any three of: cowlick, widow's peak, nape whorl, double crown, male-pattern
baldness.
