# Hair styling — reading the client before you cut — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Which is one of the six styling factors?**
a) The salon's rent  b) Previous chemical services  c) The client's car
**Answer: b) Previous chemical services** — previous chemical services heads the list.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Percentage of grey is checked because —**
a) it changes styling decisions  b) grey hair cuts faster  c) it sets the price only
**Answer: a) it changes styling decisions** — it is one of the factors weighed before styling.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) 'How the style will be maintained' is asked —**
a) after the cut  b) before styling, as a factor  c) never
**Answer: b) before styling, as a factor** — maintenance is factor six, weighed beforehand.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Hair texture is described as —**
a) soft, hard, rough  b) fine, medium, coarse  c) wet or dry
**Answer: b) fine, medium, coarse** — fine, medium and coarse are the texture grades.

**5. (Sub lesson 2) A cowlick and a widow's peak are examples of —**
a) face shapes  b) growth patterns  c) chemical services
**Answer: b) growth patterns** — both are growth patterns in the detail list.

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Which set is all face shapes from the list?**
a) Oval, round, square, oblong, heart, pear  b) Long, short, wide, thin  c) Big, small, medium
**Answer: a) Oval, round, square, oblong, heart, pear** — those six shapes are named.
