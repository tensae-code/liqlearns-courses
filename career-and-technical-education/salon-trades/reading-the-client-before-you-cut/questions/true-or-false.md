# Hair styling — reading the client before you cut — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) The client's own requirements are a styling factor.**
**Answer: True** — what the client wants is weighed with the rest.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Added hair is irrelevant to styling.**
**Answer: False** — added hair is one of the six factors.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Elasticity and porosity belong to reading hair condition.**
**Answer: True** — both are named under hair condition.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Male-pattern baldness is not a growth-pattern item.**
**Answer: False** — it is listed among the growth patterns.
