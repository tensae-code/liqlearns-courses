# Lesson plan — Hair styling — reading the client before you cut

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** Salon Trades · **Lesson:** Hair styling
— reading the client before you cut
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 4 — Salon Trades (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** before styling, weigh six factors — previous chemical
services, percentage of grey, the client's requirements, tools and equipment, added hair, and
how the style will be maintained — then read hair condition, texture, growth patterns, face
shape and the rest in detail.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands between the technique lessons: hands are learning; now
the eyes and judgement that must go before them.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below,
the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written
as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**.
The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books;
on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as
the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its
source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead,
each opening when the one before it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub
Lessons (ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **The six factors first** — Before styling, the stylist weighs: previous chemical services,
percentage of grey, the client's requirements, tools and equipment, added hair, and how the
style will be maintained.
2. **Reading hair and head in detail** — Then the stylist reads the client in detail: hair
condition (dry, greasy, normal, virgin, chemically treated; also elasticity and porosity), the
cut/style (uniform layer, one length, short or long graduation), temperature, texture (fine,
medium, coarse), length, density, growth patterns (cowlick, widow's peak, nape whorl, double
crown, male-pattern baldness), skin tone, face shape (oval, round, square, oblong, heart,
pear), head size, existing curl, and the client's lifestyle.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so
the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Men's hair — the four basic cutting techniques. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Recite the six styling factors.
2. Read the detailed list: hair condition (dry, greasy, normal, virgin, chemically treated;
elasticity; porosity), cut/style, temperature, texture (fine, medium, coarse), length, density,
growth patterns (cowlick, widow's peak, nape whorl, double crown, male-pattern baldness), skin
tone, face shape (oval, round, square, oblong, heart, pear), head size, existing curl,
lifestyle.

## If the learner is stuck
- Jumps to cutting → the six factors are a gate: no factor check, no styling.
- Drowns in the detail list → group it: hair (condition, texture, length, density, curl),
head/face (size, shape, skin tone, growth patterns), life (requirements, lifestyle,
maintenance).
