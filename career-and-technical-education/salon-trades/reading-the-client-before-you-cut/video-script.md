# Video script — Hair styling — reading the client before you cut

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on
list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Never cut blind. Read the client first.
What chemicals has this hair known?
How much grey? What does the client want?
What tools, what added hair — and who will maintain it?
Then read the hair itself: dry or greasy, fine or coarse.
The cowlick. The widow's peak. The shape of the face.
Six factors. A whole head of detail.
Now — and only now — pick up the scissors.
