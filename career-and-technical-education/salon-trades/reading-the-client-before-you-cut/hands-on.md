# Hands-on — Hair styling — reading the client before you cut

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes. Real trade practice, done safely and exactly as
the book teaches it.

## Consultation role-play

In pairs, run full consultations: one plays a client with a written secret card (grey %, a perm
last month, a cowlick, a heart-shaped face); the stylist must discover every relevant factor by
asking and looking, then propose a style that fits the findings.

## Salon shadowing

Shadow a stylist's consultations for an hour (with permission) and tally which of the book's
factors are actually checked aloud.
