# Video script — Men's hair — the four basic cutting techniques

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on
list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Four techniques. Every men's cut is built from them.
One: scissors over fingers — lift a section, cut the layer.
Two: scissors over comb — shorter hair, soft sides.
Three: clipper over comb — the barber's foundation.
The comb lifts; the clipper gives the shaved look.
Four: the blade on skin — the clean outline:
neck, ears, the edge of the fade.
Learn the four. You can cut.
