# Lesson plan — Men's hair — the four basic cutting techniques

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** Salon Trades · **Lesson:** Men's hair —
the four basic cutting techniques
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 4 — Salon Trades (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** hairstyling arranges hair to make a person attractive to
their own satisfaction, and men's cutting rests on four techniques: scissors over fingers,
scissors over comb, clipper over comb, and blade on skin.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the hair-care lesson — hair care is defined; now the
barber's four foundation moves.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below,
the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written
as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**.
The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books;
on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as

source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead,

1. **Styling and the scissor techniques** — Hairstyling arranges hair to make a person

2. **The clipper and blade techniques** — Technique 3, clipper over comb: the fundamental men's
technique — the comb lifting hair for the clipper, giving a shaved look, with clipper and comb
size chosen per head area.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Hair care — clean, healthy, attractive hair as a trade. Recommended, never
forced.
- The other Salon Trades lessons stand next door in this same title.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what hairstyling is and the ways it is done: washing, straightening, smoothening,
cutting, curling, dyeing, or a combination.
2. Perform/explain the four techniques and where each is used: scissors over fingers (layering
sections), scissors over comb (shorter hair, side shapes, softer finish), clipper over comb
(the fundamental men's technique, shaved look), blade on skin (outlining and finishing — fades,
tapers, perimeter, neck, bangs, ears).
3. Say that barbers work from style guides, including Ethiopia's own varied styles.

## What you need
For practice: comb, scissors and clippers only under a supervising practitioner; a practice
head or model where possible. Paper and pencil for written work.

## If the learner is stuck
- Mixes the two scissor techniques → the holder decides: fingers for sections, comb for shorter
hair and side shapes.
- Reaches for the blade early → the blade is a finisher: outline last.
