# Hands-on — Men's hair — the four basic cutting techniques

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes. Real trade practice, done safely and exactly as
the book teaches it.

## Technique practice on a model

On a practice head or model (never first on a person), work each technique slowly under a
practitioner: sections over fingers, scissors over comb on short areas, clipper over comb, and
— only with the practitioner's hand guiding — blade outlining.

## Barber observation

Observe a working barber and tally how many times each of the four techniques appears in one
real haircut; compare the tally with the lesson's claims about which is fundamental.
