# Men's hair — the four basic cutting techniques

## Do it first

Watch a barber in your memory: sometimes his fingers hold the hair and scissors snip above
them; sometimes a comb lifts and the clippers glide; sometimes a blade draws a clean edge at
the neck. Four hand-patterns. Name them as this lesson teaches them — then learn where each
belongs.

## Sub lesson 1 — Styling and the scissor techniques

Hairstyling arranges hair to make a person attractive to their own satisfaction — by washing,
straightening, smoothening, cutting, curling, dyeing or a combination — and barbers work from
style guides, including Ethiopia's own varied styles. Technique 1, scissors over fingers:
layering horizontal or vertical sections, held in the fingers. Technique 2, scissors over comb:
the same layering on shorter hair, defining side shapes, giving a softer finish.

- Styling: washing, straightening, smoothening, cutting, curling, dyeing, combinations.
- Barbers work from style guides; Ethiopia's styles included.
- Scissors over fingers: layering sections held in fingers.
- Scissors over comb: shorter hair, side shapes, softer finish.

## Sub lesson 2 — The clipper and blade techniques

Technique 3, clipper over comb: the fundamental men's technique — the comb lifting hair for the
clipper, giving a shaved look, with clipper and comb size chosen per head area. Technique 4,
blade on skin: outlining and finishing — for fades and tapers, the perimeter, the back of the
neck, the bangs and around the ears.

- Clipper over comb: fundamental men's technique; shaved look; sizes per head area.
- Blade on skin: outlining/finishing — fades, tapers, perimeter, neck, bangs, ears.

## Say it to your companion

Name the four techniques in order for your companion and give each one's place: "Fingers —
layering. Comb and scissors — short sides, soft finish. Clipper over comb — the shaved look.
Blade — the outline."

## Do It Yourself

1. Draw four panels, one per technique, showing hand, comb and tool positions.
2. Match ten described jobs (a fade's edge, a layered top, a side shape) to their technique.
3. Write which technique is called fundamental for men, and why it earns the name.
4. Practise comb positions in the air: lifting sections as the clipper would follow.
