# Men's hair — the four basic cutting techniques — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Dyeing and curling can be part of hairstyling.**
**Answer: True** — styling includes washing, straightening, smoothening, cutting, curling,
dyeing or a combination.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Scissors over comb is only for long hair.**
**Answer: False** — it is the same layering on shorter hair.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Clipper over comb gives a shaved look.**
**Answer: True** — the comb lifts the hair for the clipper, giving that look.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Blade on skin is used to layer the top.**
**Answer: False** — it outlines and finishes — perimeter, neck, bangs, ears.
