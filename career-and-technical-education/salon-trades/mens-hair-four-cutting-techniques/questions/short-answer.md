# Men's hair — the four basic cutting techniques — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the four basic techniques.**
**Answer:** Scissors over fingers; scissors over comb; clipper over comb; blade on skin.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Where does the blade work?**
**Answer:** Outlining and finishing: fades and tapers, the perimeter, the back of the neck, the
bangs and around the ears.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) How do scissors over fingers and scissors over comb differ?**
**Answer:** Both layer; over fingers works sections held in the fingers, over comb works
shorter hair, defines side shapes and gives a softer finish.
