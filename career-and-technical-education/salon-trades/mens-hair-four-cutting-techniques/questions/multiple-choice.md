# Men's hair — the four basic cutting techniques — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Hairstyling arranges hair to make a person —**
a) look like the barber  b) attractive to their own satisfaction  c) ready for school only
**Answer: b) attractive to their own satisfaction** — to their own satisfaction is the book's
phrase.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Scissors over fingers is used for —**
a) outlining the neck  b) layering horizontal or vertical sections held in the fingers  c)
shaving
**Answer: b) layering horizontal or vertical sections held in the fingers** — layering sections
held in the fingers.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Scissors over comb gives —**
a) a shaved look  b) layering on shorter hair, side shapes defined, a softer finish  c) tight
curls
**Answer: b) layering on shorter hair, side shapes defined, a softer finish** — shorter hair,
side shapes, softer finish.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) The fundamental men's technique is —**
a) blade on skin  b) clipper over comb  c) scissors over fingers
**Answer: b) clipper over comb** — clipper over comb is the fundamental men's technique.

**5. (Sub lesson 2) Clipper and comb size are chosen per —**
a) client's age  b) head area  c) day of week
**Answer: b) head area** — size follows the area of the head.

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Blade on skin is for —**
a) washing hair  b) outlining and finishing: fades, tapers, perimeter, neck, bangs, around the
ears  c) dyeing
**Answer: b) outlining and finishing: fades, tapers, perimeter, neck, bangs, around the ears**
— outlining and finishing is the blade's work.
