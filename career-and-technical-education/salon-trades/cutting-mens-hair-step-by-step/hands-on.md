# Hands-on — Men's hair — cutting a man's hair step by step

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes. Real trade practice, done safely and exactly as
the book teaches it.

## Full cut-along

Following the video-script's cut-along order on a practice head or model, under a practitioner:
complete all four steps in order, combing through after each line in Step 3 and finishing with
the floor swept.

## Timed observation

Time a real barber's cut and map the minutes onto the four steps; note where blending happens
and how the service closes.
