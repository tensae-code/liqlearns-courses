# Video script — Men's hair — cutting a man's hair step by step

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on
list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The whole cut, in four steps.
Step one: prepare.
Ask him: how short? How even? How will you style it?
Choose the cut. Set up. Clean hair, combed smooth.
Step two: clippers. Thumb and two fingers, firm.
Start at the base. Line the back. Then the sides.
Step three: the top. Dampen it. Trim. Comb after every line.
Blend the top. Trim the fringe.
Step four: blend and finish.
Melt the scissor work into the clipper work.
Neck. Sideburns. A final comb. A final wash.
And sweep the floor — before he leaves the chair.
