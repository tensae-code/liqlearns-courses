# Lesson plan — Men's hair — cutting a man's hair step by step

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** Salon Trades · **Lesson:** Men's hair —
cutting a man's hair step by step
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 4 — Salon Trades (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** cutting is simple with attention and time, in four
steps: prepare (ask, choose, set up, clean hair, detangle), clipper work (from the base of the
head up, back then sides), top and bangs (dampen, trim, blend), and blend and finish (blend the
joins, trim neck and sideburns, final comb, wash or spritz, sweep the floor).
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the techniques, factors and tools lessons —
everything converges into one complete cut.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below,
the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Recite the four steps with their contents.
2. Start correctly: ask the client how short, how even, and how he will style it; choose the
haircut; allow plenty of time; set up; start with clean, detangled hair.
3. Do clipper work in order: choose the attachment, hold firmly between thumb and first two
fingers, clip from the base of the head, line up the back, then the sides.
4. Finish completely: blend scissor cuts into clipper cuts, trim neck and sideburns, final
comb, wash or spritz, sweep the floor before the client leaves the chair.

## What you need
For practice: a full barber kit, a practice head or a willing model, and a supervising
practitioner. Paper and pencil for written work.
