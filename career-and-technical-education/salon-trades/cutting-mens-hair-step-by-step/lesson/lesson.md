# Men's hair — cutting a man's hair step by step

## Do it first

A client walks in. Write every stage until he walks out — the talk, the preparation, the
cutting order around the head, the finishing touches, even the floor. Then compare your order
with the book's four steps.

## Sub lesson 1 — Step 1 prepare, Step 2 clipper work

Cutting is presented as simple with attention and time, using scissors, clippers or both, after
asking the client how short, how even and how he will style it. Step 1, prepare: ask what he
wants, choose the haircut, allow plenty of time, set up the work area, start with clean hair,
detangle with a comb. Step 2, clipper work: choose the attachment, hold the clippers firmly
between thumb and first two fingers, clip from the base of the head, line up the back, then the
sides.

- Ask first: how short, how even, how styled.
- Prepare: choose the cut, time, set up, clean hair, detangle.
- Clipper grip: firm, between thumb and first two fingers.
- Clipper order: base of head → back lined up → sides.

## Sub lesson 2 — Step 3 top and bangs, Step 4 blend and finish

Step 3, top and bangs: dampen the top, trim it, comb through after each line, blend the top,
trim the bangs. Step 4, blend and finish: blend scissor cuts into clipper cuts, trim neck and
sideburns, comb a final time, wash or spritz once more — and sweep the floor before the client
leaves the chair. The swept floor is part of the service, not an afterthought.

- Top: dampen, trim, comb after each line, blend, trim bangs.
- Finish: blend the joins, trim neck and sideburns.
- Final: comb, wash or spritz — and sweep the floor before the client leaves.

## Say it to your companion

Call the four steps to your companion as a working barber: "Prepare… clipper work — base, back,
sides… top and bangs… blend and finish — and sweep the floor." Your companion interrupts with a
stage; you place it in its step.

## Do It Yourself

1. Write the four steps as a chair-side card with their contents.
2. Practise the clipper grip and the upward pass on a practice head.
3. List what 'prepare' includes, from memory, in order.
4. Say why the floor is swept before the client leaves, not after.
