# Men's hair — cutting a man's hair step by step — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) List the four steps in order.**
**Answer:** Prepare; clipper work; top and bangs; blend and finish.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Describe Step 2's order around the head.**
**Answer:** Choose the attachment, hold the clippers firmly between thumb and first two
fingers, clip from the base of the head, line up the back, then the sides.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) What does Step 4 finish, besides the hair?**
**Answer:** It combs a final time, washes or spritzes once more, and sweeps the floor before
the client leaves the chair.
