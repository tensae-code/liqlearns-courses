# Men's hair — cutting a man's hair step by step — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Before cutting, ask the client —**
a) only the price  b) how short, how even, and how he will style it  c) nothing; just begin
**Answer: b) how short, how even, and how he will style it** — those three questions open the
work.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Step 1 ends with hair that is —**
a) wet and tangled  b) clean and detangled with a comb  c) already cut
**Answer: b) clean and detangled with a comb** — start with clean hair, detangled.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Clippers are held —**
a) in the palm loosely  b) firmly between thumb and first two fingers  c) with both hands
**Answer: b) firmly between thumb and first two fingers** — that grip is the book's.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Clipper work clips first —**
a) the bangs  b) from the base of the head, then lines up the back, then the sides  c) the top
**Answer: b) from the base of the head, then lines up the back, then the sides** — base, back,
sides is the order.

**5. (Sub lesson 2) In Step 3 you comb through —**
a) only at the end  b) after each line  c) before dampening
**Answer: b) after each line** — comb through after each line.

**6. (Sub lesson 2) The final act of Step 4, before the client leaves the chair, includes —**
a) charging double  b) sweeping the floor  c) closing the shop
**Answer: b) sweeping the floor** — sweep the floor before the client leaves the chair.
