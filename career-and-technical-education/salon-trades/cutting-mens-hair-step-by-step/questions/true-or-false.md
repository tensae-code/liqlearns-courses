# Men's hair — cutting a man's hair step by step — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Plenty of time should be allowed for a cut.**
**Answer: True** — attention and time are the book's conditions.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Clipper work starts at the top of the head.**
**Answer: False** — it clips from the base of the head.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Blending scissor cuts into clipper cuts happens in Step 4.**
**Answer: True** — blend and finish is Step 4's work.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) The floor is swept after the client has gone home.**
**Answer: False** — before the client leaves the chair.
