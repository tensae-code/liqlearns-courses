# Video script — Men's hair — safety, hygiene and hair damage

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on
list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Beautiful hair, safely. That is the craft.
Wash: shampoo lifts sweat, oil, product away.
Condition: the cuticle smooths down, protected.
Colour opens the cuticle — and can leave hair dry and weak.
Perms rebuild the hair from inside — treat them gently.
Heat, repeated, breaks hair. Scalp troubles speak:
itch, fungus, a smell that stays for weeks.
A good stylist hears them. A good stylist does no harm.
