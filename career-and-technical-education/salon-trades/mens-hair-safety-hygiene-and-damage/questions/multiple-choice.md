# Men's hair — safety, hygiene and hair damage — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Shampoo with water removes —**
a) sweat, oil and product  b) the hair's colour always  c) the cuticle entirely
**Answer: a) sweat, oil and product** — sweat, oil and product are what washing removes.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Conditioner after shampooing —**
a) opens the cuticle wider  b) smooths the roughened cuticle and protects the hair  c) colours
the hair
**Answer: b) smooths the roughened cuticle and protects the hair** — it smooths the cuticle and
protects.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Colouring and bleaching require —**
a) cutting the hair first  b) opening the cuticle to add or remove pigment  c) no chemicals
**Answer: b) opening the cuticle to add or remove pigment** — pigment change requires opening
the cuticle.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Permed hair must be handled gently because —**
a) it is weaker after its internal structure was altered  b) it is longer  c) it is expensive
**Answer: a) it is weaker after its internal structure was altered** — relaxing/thermal
reconditioning alters internal structure, weakening it.

**5. (Sub lesson 2) A weeks-long odour from the scalp signals —**
a) good health  b) a scalp health problem  c) a new shampoo working
**Answer: b) a scalp health problem** — the book names it as a health-problem signal.

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Hair loss can come —**
a) only from shaving  b) by choice (shaving the head) or through illness and its treatment  c)
only from heat
**Answer: b) by choice (shaving the head) or through illness and its treatment** — choice or
illness are the book's two doors.
