# Men's hair — safety, hygiene and hair damage — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does shampoo remove, and what does conditioner then do?**
**Answer:** Shampoo with water removes sweat, oil and product; conditioner smooths the
roughened cuticle and protects the hair.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Why is permed hair handled gently?**
**Answer:** Perms and chemical straightening alter the hair's internal structure, leaving it
weaker.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Name three scalp troubles from the lesson.**
**Answer:** Any three of: mites, lice, follicle infections, fungus, allergic reactions.
