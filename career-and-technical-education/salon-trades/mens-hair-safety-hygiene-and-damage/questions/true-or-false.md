# Men's hair — safety, hygiene and hair damage — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Colouring chemicals can leave hair dry, weak and breakage-prone.**
**Answer: True** — that is the stated risk.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Conditioner is used before shampoo in the book's order.**
**Answer: False** — shampoo first, conditioner afterwards.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Oil is always good for rough hair and a dry scalp.**
**Answer: False** — the book says oil harms rough hair and dry scalp.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Mites, lice and fungus can all trouble the scalp.**
**Answer: True** — all are named among scalp troubles.
