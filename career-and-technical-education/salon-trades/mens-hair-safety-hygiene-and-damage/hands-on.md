# Hands-on — Men's hair — safety, hygiene and hair damage

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes. Real trade practice, done safely and exactly as
the book teaches it.

## Barber interview

Ask a local barber the book's question — what safety and hygiene measures do you practise when
styling? — and map each answer onto the five topics.

## Wash-station practice

Practise the wash sequence on a model head: shampoo worked through with water, rinse, then
conditioner — naming what each step does to the cuticle as you go.
