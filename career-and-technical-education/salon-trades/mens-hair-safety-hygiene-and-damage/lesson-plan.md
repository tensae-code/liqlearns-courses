# Lesson plan — Men's hair — safety, hygiene and hair damage

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** Salon Trades · **Lesson:** Men's hair —
safety, hygiene and hair damage
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 4 — Salon Trades (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** salon safety and hygiene in five topics: washing
(shampoo, then conditioner to smooth the cuticle), colouring and bleaching, perms and chemical
straightening, breakage and other damage including scalp troubles, and general hair loss.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the techniques lessons — cutting is learned; now
working without harming hair or scalp.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below,
the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written
as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**.
The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books;
on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as
the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its
source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead,
each opening when the one before it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub

2. **Perms, damage and hair loss** — (3) Perms and chemical straightening — relaxing or thermal

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Explain washing: shampoo with water removes sweat, oil and product; conditioner afterwards
smooths the roughened cuticle and protects the hair.
2. Explain colouring's risk: opening the cuticle to add/remove pigment can leave hair dry, weak
and breakage-prone.
3. Explain perms/straightening: altering the hair's internal structure; permed hair is weaker,
handled gently.
4. List damage causes and scalp troubles: chemical exposure, prolonged/repeated heat; oil
harming rough hair and dry scalp; mites, lice, follicle infections, fungus, allergic reactions;
a weeks-long odour signalling a scalp health problem.
5. Say hair loss can be by choice (shaving the head) or through illness and its treatment.
