# An Ethiopian Entrepreneur — Bethlehem Tilahun and Sole Rebels — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What did Bethlehem Tilahun see in her community?
a) No skills at all  b) Few jobs, but many unemployed people with remarkable artisan skills, largely unexploited  c) A foreign factory hiring
**Answer: b) Few jobs, but many unemployed people with remarkable artisan skills, largely unexploited** — skills present, jobs absent — that gap was the opportunity.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** She resolved to transform those skills into —
a) a school  b) a sustainable enterprise  c) a charity queue
**Answer: b) a sustainable enterprise** — an enterprise, sustainable — that was the resolve.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Sole Rebels was founded in —
a) 1994  b) 2004  c) 2014
**Answer: b) 2004** — 2004, with capital from her husband and immediate family.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Sole Rebels shoes are hand-crafted from —
a) imported leather only  b) recycled old tyres and locally sourced natural fibres such as jute and enset  c) plastic imports
**Answer: b) recycled old tyres and locally sourced natural fibres such as jute and enset** — local and recycled materials are the product's base.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The book characterises the entrepreneur as —
a) a cautious clerk  b) the visionary and dreamer in us — high need for achievement, energetic, a moderate risk taker  c) someone who avoids all risk
**Answer: b) the visionary and dreamer in us — high need for achievement, energetic, a moderate risk taker** — that is the general characterisation after the story.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Sole Rebels is described as —
a) a local stall only  b) one of Africa's most recognisable footwear manufacturers, a sustainable world-class enterprise  c) a government office
**Answer: b) one of Africa's most recognisable footwear manufacturers, a sustainable world-class enterprise** — world-class and sustainable, built from a local observation.
