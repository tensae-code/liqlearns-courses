# An Ethiopian Entrepreneur — Bethlehem Tilahun and Sole Rebels — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Sole Rebels shoes are sold in over 30 countries.
**Answer: True** — the book gives over 30 countries.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Tilahun's start-up capital came from a foreign bank loan.
**Answer: False** — it was sourced from her husband and immediate family.
