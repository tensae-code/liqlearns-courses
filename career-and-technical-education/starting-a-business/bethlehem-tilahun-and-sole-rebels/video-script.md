# Video script — An Ethiopian Entrepreneur — Bethlehem Tilahun and Sole Rebels

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

In Zenebework, in Addis Ababa,
Bethlehem Tilahun saw something.
Few jobs. But skilled hands everywhere.
Artisan skills — unexploited.

In 2004 she acted.
Capital from her husband and family.
She gathered the gifted makers.
Sole Rebels was founded.
Shoes from recycled old tyres.
From jute. From enset.

Today those shoes sell in over thirty countries.
A world-class enterprise — from a local observation.

That is the entrepreneur.
A visionary. A dreamer.
High need for achievement. A moderate risk taker.

Now you. What skill near you is waiting?
