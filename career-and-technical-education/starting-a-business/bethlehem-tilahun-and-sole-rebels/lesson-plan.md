# Lesson plan — An Ethiopian Entrepreneur — Bethlehem Tilahun and Sole Rebels

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** Starting a Business · **Lesson:** An Ethiopian Entrepreneur — Bethlehem Tilahun and Sole Rebels
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 10 — Starting a Business (CURRICULUM.md / TITLES.md ladder).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Bethlehem Tilahun saw unemployed neighbours with remarkable artisan skills, and built those skills into Sole Rebels — hand-crafted shoes from recycled tyres and local fibres, sold in over 30 countries.
**This lesson's own level:** It stands on the definitions lesson before it: this is the definition walking — an opportunity perceived in one Addis Ababa community, resources organised, an enterprise grown.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Retell the Sole Rebels story: the seeing, the founding, the growth.
2. Name the materials Sole Rebels shoes are made from.
3. Say where its start-up capital came from.
4. Give the book's general characterisation of the entrepreneur.
