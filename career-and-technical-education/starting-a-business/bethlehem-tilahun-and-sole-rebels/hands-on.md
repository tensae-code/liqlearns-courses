# Hands-on — An Ethiopian Entrepreneur — Bethlehem Tilahun and Sole Rebels

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Your community's unexploited skill
List three skills in your own community that are underused, as Tilahun found hers. For one of them, write: what product, made from what local material, sold to whom?

## Tell the story onward
Retell the Sole Rebels story aloud to a companion in under two minutes — the seeing, the 2004 founding, the materials, the 30 countries — without reading. Your companion checks the four beats.
