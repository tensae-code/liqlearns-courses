# Video script — Types of Entrepreneurship — Small Business, Startup, Large Company, Social

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Four kinds of venture. Choose yours first.
One — the small business.
One shop. No plan to become a chain.
Funded mostly from the founder's own pocket.
Two — the scalable startup.
Starts tiny — a seed of an idea.
Investors come in. It is built to grow.
Three — inside a large company.
A new venture, growing inside what exists.
Like mango juice, inside the avocado-oil park.
Four — social entrepreneurship.
Built to solve a community problem.
Mission first. Service, not profit.

Now you. Which kind would yours be?
