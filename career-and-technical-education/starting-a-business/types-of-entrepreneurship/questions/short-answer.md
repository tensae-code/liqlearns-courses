# Types of Entrepreneurship — Small Business, Startup, Large Company, Social — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give two examples of small businesses from the lesson.
**Answer:** Any two of: shops, hairdressers, bakers, restaurants, retail store owners (the single grocery store).

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Retell the mango case in one or two sentences.
**Answer:** In Ethiopia's agro-processing parks, where investors already process avocado oil, an entrepreneur spots high demand for mango juice and brings that processing idea to the investor — a new venture inside the existing company.

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** What is the defining difference of social entrepreneurship?
**Answer:** Its mission: solving a community problem / bringing social change, organised for serving society rather than for profit.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What is a scalable startup's defining intention?
**Answer:** Long-term evolution — it is built to grow, though it begins very small, often as the seed of an idea, with outside investors a common feature.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Why is the mango-juice venture not a small business?
**Answer:** It is not a founder's single independent shop built from nothing; it is a new business entity created inside an existing large company (the agro-processing operation).
