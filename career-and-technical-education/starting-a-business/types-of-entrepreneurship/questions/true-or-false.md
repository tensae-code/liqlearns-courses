# Types of Entrepreneurship — Small Business, Startup, Large Company, Social — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A scalable startup begins on a very small scale, often as just the seed of an idea.
**Answer: True** — small start, growth intention.

**2. (Sub lesson 4)** A social enterprise may generate some income to expand its social services.
**Answer: True** — income in service of the mission is allowed.
