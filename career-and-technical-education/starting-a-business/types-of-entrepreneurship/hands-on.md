# Hands-on — Types of Entrepreneurship — Small Business, Startup, Large Company, Social

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Sort your locality's ventures
List six ventures you know. Sort each into one of the four types, writing the intention or mission that proves the sort. Any venture you cannot sort, investigate: ask what its founder intends.

## Argue the startup case
Take one small business near you. Argue in writing: could it have been built as a scalable startup instead? What would have to change — investors, intention, product?
