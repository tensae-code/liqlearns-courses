# Lesson plan — Entrepreneurship — the Words and the Idea

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** Starting a Business · **Lesson:** Entrepreneurship — the Words and the Idea
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 10 — Starting a Business (CURRICULUM.md / TITLES.md ladder).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** entrepreneurship is the process of action an entrepreneur undertakes to establish an enterprise — spotting an opportunity, gathering resources, bearing the risk, and being rewarded with profit if it succeeds.
**This lesson's own level:** It stands on everything before it in this course: a trade learned, employability built — this title's first lesson names the person who turns a skill into an enterprise of their own.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an
image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture
of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does
with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and
while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is
dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the
student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph,
or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be
shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Define entrepreneurship and entrepreneur in the book's words.
2. Say what income and investment each mean.
3. Explain 'calculated risk' from the lesson's framing.
4. Tell the short definition of the entrepreneur (perceive, organise, exploit).

## What you need
Paper and a pencil.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks entrepreneur means any shopkeeper → return to the definition: seeing an opportunity, gathering resources, bearing risk. The venturing and the risk are what make the word.
