# Hands-on — Entrepreneurship — the Words and the Idea

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Spot three gaps
Walk your market or street (in person or in memory). Write three gaps — needs not well met. For each, write what resources a venture would need to gather to meet it.

## Risk, calculated
Take one gap from your list. Write the risks in starting a venture on it, and beside each, what the entrepreneur could do to calculate and reduce it — information, small start, a backup plan.
