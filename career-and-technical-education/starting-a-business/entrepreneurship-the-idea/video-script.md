# Video script — Entrepreneurship — the Words and the Idea

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A word for a whole way of working.
Entrepreneurship — the process of action
an entrepreneur takes to establish an enterprise.

And the entrepreneur?
One who sees an opportunity in the market.
Gathers resources. Creates a venture.
Bears the risk.
And is rewarded with profit, if it succeeds.

It is a creative activity.
Building something from practically nothing.
Seeking opportunities. Taking calculated risks.

Now you. What gap do you see in your market?
