# Entrepreneurship — the Words and the Idea — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Entrepreneurship is —
a) any paid job  b) the process of action an entrepreneur undertakes to establish his or her enterprise  c) a government grant
**Answer: b) the process of action an entrepreneur undertakes to establish his or her enterprise** — that is the book's key-word definition.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** An entrepreneur bears the risk and is rewarded with —
a) a fixed salary  b) profit, if the venture succeeds  c) nothing ever
**Answer: b) profit, if the venture succeeds** — risk carried, profit if it succeeds.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Investment means —
a) money spent on consumption  b) an asset or item acquired with the goal of generating income or appreciation  c) a tax
**Answer: b) an asset or item acquired with the goal of generating income or appreciation** — income or appreciation is its goal.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In the short definition, the entrepreneur —
a) perceives an opportunity, organises the resources, and exploits it  b) waits for instructions  c) copies a neighbour blindly
**Answer: a) perceives an opportunity, organises the resources, and exploits it** — perceive, organise, exploit.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Entrepreneurship is also described as —
a) a creative activity — building something from practically nothing  b) pure luck  c) an exam subject only
**Answer: a) a creative activity — building something from practically nothing** — creativity and calculated risk are in its framing.

**6. (Sub lesson 1)** Entrepreneurship takes risks that are —
a) never taken  b) calculated  c) hidden from everyone
**Answer: b) calculated** — an attitude of mind that seeks opportunities and takes calculated risks.
