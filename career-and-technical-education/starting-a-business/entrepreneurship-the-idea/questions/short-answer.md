# Entrepreneurship — the Words and the Idea — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What three things does the entrepreneur do with an opportunity?
**Answer:** Sees it in the market, gathers resources and creates/grows a venture to meet the need (short form: perceives it, organises the resources, exploits it).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does 'calculated' add to 'risk' in this lesson?
**Answer:** The risk is sought and weighed as an attitude of mind — opportunities are looked for and risks taken deliberately, not blindly.
