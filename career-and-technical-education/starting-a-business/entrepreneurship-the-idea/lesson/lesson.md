# Entrepreneurship — the Words and the Idea

## Do it first

Look at your own market or street with fresh eyes. Where is something missing — something people need that nobody there is providing well? Write the gap you see. That seeing is where this lesson begins.

## Sub lesson 1 — The key words

**Entrepreneurship** is the **process of action an entrepreneur undertakes to establish his or her enterprise**.

An **entrepreneur** is the person who **sees an opportunity in the market, gathers his or her resources, creates and grows a business venture to meet those needs, bears the risk** — and is **rewarded with profit if the venture succeeds**.

Two base words sit under both: **income** — money earned from work or received from investments; and **investment** — an asset or item acquired with the goal of generating income or appreciation.

Entrepreneurship is also introduced as a **creative activity**: creating and building something from practically nothing — an attitude of mind that seeks opportunities and takes **calculated risks**.

## Sub lesson 2 — The definitions, stated fully

Entrepreneurship is known as '**self-employment of any sort**': the activities necessary to create or carry on an enterprise where not all markets are well established or clearly defined, and parts of the production function are not completely known. Restated plainly: it is the process of action to establish an enterprise, and a creative activity.

And the entrepreneur, in the shorter definition: **someone who perceives an opportunity, organises the resources needed for exploiting that opportunity, and exploits it**.

Hold the pair together: the entrepreneur perceives and organises; entrepreneurship is the whole process of doing it.
