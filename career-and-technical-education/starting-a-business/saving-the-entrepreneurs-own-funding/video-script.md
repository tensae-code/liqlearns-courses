# Video script — Saving — the Entrepreneur's Own Funding

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Income arrives. It splits in two.
One part is spent — consumption.
The other part is saved.
Saving is the part of income not spent.
A sacrifice today — for a better standard tomorrow.

And saving starts a chain.
Save regularly. Invest. Develop the enterprise.
Your own funds — the simplest, safest way to start.
Saving leads to investment.
Investment leads to growth in value.

Now you. Save your first portion today.
