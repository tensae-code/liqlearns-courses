# Saving — the Entrepreneur's Own Funding

## Do it first

Take any income you have seen handled — a wage, a market day's takings. When it arrives, what are its two possible fates? Write them. The whole lesson lives in the split you just wrote.

## Sub lesson 1 — What saving is

**Saving is the part of income which is not spent on consumption.**

It is a **sacrifice of current consumption** — giving up spending now — **to increase the living standard and fulfil daily requirements in the future**.

Every income splits at the same fork: the part consumed today, and the part saved for tomorrow.

## Sub lesson 2 — Saving, investment, enterprise — the chain

Entrepreneurship development is related to **income, savings and investment in a chain**: **save regularly → invest → develop the enterprise**. Or, from the other end: find a business idea and additional funding sources.

Using **personal funds** to start or expand a small business is presented as the **most attractive, simplest and possibly safest** method. The entrepreneur **saves a portion of the income from the enterprise** — because **saving leads to investment, and to increment in value**.

The chain closes the course: a trade learned, a plan written, and the first funding raised not from a lender's desk but from the entrepreneur's own saved portion, added to regularly.
