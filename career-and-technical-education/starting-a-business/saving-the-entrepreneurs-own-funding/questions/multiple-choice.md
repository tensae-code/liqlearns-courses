# Saving — the Entrepreneur's Own Funding — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Saving is —
a) all income spent carefully  b) the part of income which is not spent on consumption  c) money borrowed
**Answer: b) the part of income which is not spent on consumption** — not spent on consumption — that is the definition.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Saving is a sacrifice of current consumption in order to —
a) punish yourself  b) increase the living standard and fulfil daily requirements in the future  c) impress neighbours
**Answer: b) increase the living standard and fulfil daily requirements in the future** — the future is what the sacrifice buys.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The chain runs —
a) spend → borrow → close  b) save regularly → invest → develop the enterprise  c) earn → hide → forget
**Answer: b) save regularly → invest → develop the enterprise** — income, savings, investment, enterprise.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Using personal funds to start or expand a small business is presented as —
a) the riskiest method  b) the most attractive, simplest and possibly safest method  c) impossible
**Answer: b) the most attractive, simplest and possibly safest method** — the book's own ranking.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The entrepreneur saves a portion of —
a) other people's income  b) the income from the enterprise  c) the tax money
**Answer: b) the income from the enterprise** — a portion of the enterprise's own income, saved regularly.

**6. (Sub lesson 1)** Saving fulfils its purpose in —
a) the same day only  b) the future — a better living standard and daily requirements met  c) never
**Answer: b) the future — a better living standard and daily requirements met** — the sacrifice is of current consumption, for the future.
