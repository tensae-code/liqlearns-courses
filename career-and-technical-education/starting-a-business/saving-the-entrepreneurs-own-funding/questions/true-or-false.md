# Saving — the Entrepreneur's Own Funding — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Saving leads to investment and to increment in value.
**Answer: True** — that is the chain's engine.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Saving means spending everything now and hoping for more later.
**Answer: False** — saving is precisely the part NOT spent on consumption.
