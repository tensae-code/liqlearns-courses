# Saving — the Entrepreneur's Own Funding — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Define saving in the book's words.
**Answer:** The part of income which is not spent on consumption.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give the two routes into enterprise funding the lesson names.
**Answer:** Save regularly, invest and develop the enterprise from your own funds — or find a business idea and additional funding sources.
