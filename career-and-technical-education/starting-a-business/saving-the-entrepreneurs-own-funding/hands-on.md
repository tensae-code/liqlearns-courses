# Hands-on — Saving — the Entrepreneur's Own Funding

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Start the chain
Set your own saving portion — a fixed part of any income you receive, however small. Put the first portion aside today, in a container or account you do not touch for daily spending, and write the chain above it: save → invest → develop.

## Price your start
Take your business plan's financial section. Write the start-up amount it needs. At your saving portion per week, calculate how many weeks of saving reach it — that number is your funding timeline.
