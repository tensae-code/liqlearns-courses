# Video script — Kinds of Entrepreneurs — by Development and by Motivation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Sort the entrepreneurs two ways.
By how they treat the new:
The innovator — creates something new.
The imitator — copies what the innovator made.
The fabian — follows the old ways, avoids risk.
The drone — refuses even to copy,
and struggles to survive.

By what drives them:
The pure — satisfaction and status.
The induced — started by government support.
Most small entrepreneurs are here.
The motivated — self-fulfilment, new products, profit.

Now you. Which kind are you becoming?
