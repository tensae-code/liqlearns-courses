# Kinds of Entrepreneurs — by Development and by Motivation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Order the four development kinds from most open to new methods to most closed.
**Answer:** Innovating (creates new) → imitative (copies new) → fabian (follows old ways) → drone (refuses even to copy).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What motivates the pure entrepreneur?
**Answer:** Psychological, economical and ethical considerations — satisfaction in work or status.
