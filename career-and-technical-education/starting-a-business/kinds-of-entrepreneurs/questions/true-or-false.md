# Kinds of Entrepreneurs — by Development and by Motivation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Imitative entrepreneurs innovate new products themselves.
**Answer: False** — they copy or adopt suitable innovations made by the innovators.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Motivation is the main force promoting the entrepreneur's effort.
**Answer: True** — the book frames the second sorting on exactly that.
