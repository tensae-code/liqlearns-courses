# Kinds of Entrepreneurs — by Development and by Motivation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An innovating entrepreneur —
a) copies others  b) sees the opportunity to introduce a new technique, product or market, resulting in something new  c) refuses all change
**Answer: b) sees the opportunity to introduce a new technique, product or market, resulting in something new** — creating the new is the innovator's mark.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A fabian entrepreneur —
a) adopts every innovation at once  b) follows set procedures, customs and traditions, risk-averse, unwilling to introduce or adopt new methods  c) is a beginner
**Answer: b) follows set procedures, customs and traditions, risk-averse, unwilling to introduce or adopt new methods** — conviction and caution keep the fabian on the old path.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A drone entrepreneur —
a) grows fastest  b) refuses to copy or use opportunities, sticking to set practices, struggling to survive rather than grow  c) works only seasonally
**Answer: b) refuses to copy or use opportunities, sticking to set practices, struggling to survive rather than grow** — refusal plus survival-struggle is the drone's mark.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The induced entrepreneur is started by —
a) family pressure only  b) government policy reforms — assistance, incentives and facilities  c) a dream
**Answer: b) government policy reforms — assistance, incentives and facilities** — policy support induces this kind.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Most small-scale entrepreneurs belong to which motivation kind?
a) Pure  b) Induced  c) Motivated
**Answer: b) Induced** — the book places most small-scale entrepreneurs among the induced.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The motivated entrepreneur is driven by —
a) the desire for self-fulfilment, making and marketing new products, rewarded by profit  b) status alone  c) avoiding customers
**Answer: a) the desire for self-fulfilment, making and marketing new products, rewarded by profit** — self-fulfilment through new products.
