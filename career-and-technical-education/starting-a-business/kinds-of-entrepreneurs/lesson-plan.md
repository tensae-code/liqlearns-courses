# Lesson plan — Kinds of Entrepreneurs — by Development and by Motivation

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** Starting a Business · **Lesson:** Kinds of Entrepreneurs — by Development and by Motivation
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 10 — Starting a Business (CURRICULUM.md / TITLES.md ladder).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** entrepreneurs sort two ways: by how they treat new methods — innovating, imitative, fabian, drone — and by what motivates them — pure, induced, motivated.
**This lesson's own level:** It stands on the types-of-entrepreneurship lesson: that one sorted ventures; this one sorts the people who run them.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an
image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture
of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does
with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and
while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is
dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the
student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph,
or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be
shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the four kinds by economic development, with each one's mark.
2. Tell a fabian from a drone.
3. Name the three kinds by motivation.
4. Say which motivation category holds most small-scale entrepreneurs.

## If the learner is stuck
- Mixes fabian and drone → both refuse the new, but differently: the fabian follows the old ways by conviction and caution; the drone refuses even to copy what comes to him, and struggles to survive rather than grow.
