# The Business Plan — Money, Marketing, Backup Plan and Licence — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The marketing plan covers —
a) target market, product/service, pricing strategy, promotional strategy and place of delivery  b) only advertising  c) the staff uniform
**Answer: a) target market, product/service, pricing strategy, promotional strategy and place of delivery** — five parts, all named.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Banks and microfinance institutions lend —
a) to any plan  b) only when the business is profitable, on a clear financial plan  c) without reading plans
**Answer: b) only when the business is profitable, on a clear financial plan** — a clear, profitable financial plan is required.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The management plan is —
a) the owner's profile — education and business experience — so lenders can judge capacity  b) a staff timetable  c) a stock list
**Answer: a) the owner's profile — education and business experience — so lenders can judge capacity** — lenders judge the person through it.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A contingency plan states —
a) the conditions under which you will close, and the alternative business if the first plan fails  b) next year's prices  c) the opening party
**Answer: a) the conditions under which you will close, and the alternative business if the first plan fails** — close conditions plus the alternative.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Without a licence, an enterprise —
a) gets a discount  b) may not be allowed to start  c) starts anyway
**Answer: b) may not be allowed to start** — registration and licence are in the supporting documents.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** The template's three sections are —
a) cover, diary, songs  b) title page, main contents, annex of supporting documents  c) index, glossary, maps
**Answer: b) title page, main contents, annex of supporting documents** — that is Table 7.1's shape.
