# The Business Plan — Money, Marketing, Backup Plan and Licence — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the marketing plan's five parts.
**Answer:** Target market; product/service; pricing strategy; promotional strategy; place of delivery.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What two things must a contingency plan contain?
**Answer:** The conditions under which you will close the business, and the alternative business prepared if the first plan fails.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What must the title page of the plan carry?
**Answer:** The business name, address, registration date and owner (Table 7.1's template).
