# Hands-on — The Business Plan — Money, Marketing, Backup Plan and Licence

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Draft a real one-page business plan
Using the template, draft your complete simple business plan on one page for the enterprise you chose: title information, summary, mission, situation, product, marketing, finance, management, contingency — and list the registration and licence documents in the annex.

## Be the lender
Swap plans with a partner. Read theirs as a microfinance lender: is the financial plan clear? Does it show profit? Does the management profile show capacity? Give your lending decision and your reasons, in writing.
