# Lesson plan — The Business Plan — Money, Marketing, Backup Plan and Licence

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** Starting a Business · **Lesson:** The Business Plan — Money, Marketing, Backup Plan and Licence
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 10 — Starting a Business (CURRICULUM.md / TITLES.md ladder).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the plan's remaining components — marketing, finance, management, contingency and supporting documents — and the book's template, from which you write your own simple one-page plan.
**This lesson's own level:** It stands directly on the business-plan lesson before it: same document, second half. Together the two lessons hold the whole plan.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an
image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture
of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does
with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and
while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is
dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the
student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph,
or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be
shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Before this lesson

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the marketing plan's five parts and the financial plan's content.
2. Say why lenders require a clear financial plan.
3. Write a management profile and a contingency plan's two parts.
4. Lay out a simple business plan on the book's template, title page to annex.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil, your first-parts draft from the previous lesson.

## If the learner is stuck
- Skips the contingency plan as negative → it is the component that decides in advance when to close and what to do next. A founder who writes it can survive a failure; one who refuses meets the same failure unprepared.
