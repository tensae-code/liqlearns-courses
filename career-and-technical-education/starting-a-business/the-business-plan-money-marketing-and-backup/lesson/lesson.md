# The Business Plan — Money, Marketing, Backup Plan and Licence

## Do it first

Take the enterprise from the last lesson. Answer quickly: who will buy from it, what will you charge, and what happens if it fails? Write your rough answers — the lesson gives each one its proper component.

## Sub lesson 1 — Marketing plan and financial plan

**Marketing plan** — the **target market, product/service, pricing strategy, promotional strategy and place of delivery**. Who buys, what they buy, at what price, told how, delivered where.

**Financial plan** — the **expected income and expenses**. This one has teeth: **microfinance institutions and banks require a clear financial plan, and lend only when the business is profitable**. A muddy money section does not just weaken the plan — it closes the lender's door.

## Sub lesson 2 — Management, contingency, and supporting documents

**Management plan** — the **owner's profile**: education and business experience, so lenders can judge **capacity** — can this person actually run this?

**Contingency plan** — the **conditions under which you will close the business**, and the **alternative business prepared if the first plan fails**. Writing the failure conditions in advance is not pessimism; it is the plan for surviving failure.

**Supporting documents** — the enterprise must be **registered and licensed**: **without a licence it may not be allowed to start**.

## Sub lesson 3 — The template — write your own plan

The book's template (its Table 7.1) gives the working shape:
