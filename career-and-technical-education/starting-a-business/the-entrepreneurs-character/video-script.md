# Video script — The Entrepreneur's Character — Seven Characteristics

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Seven characteristics. One entrepreneur.
One — passion. Care deeply about the work.
Money matters — but it comes second.
Two — self-confidence. Believe you can overcome.
Three — risk-taking. Accept the possible loss.
It is a natural part of reaching goals.
Four — creativity. New ways to solve problems.
Five — visionary. See the future, and work toward it.
Six — motivation. The inner drive. Self-starting.
Seven — personal effectiveness.
Time managed. Pressure handled. Self known.

Now you. Which of the seven is yours already?
