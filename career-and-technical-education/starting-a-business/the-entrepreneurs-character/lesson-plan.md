# Lesson plan — The Entrepreneur's Character — Seven Characteristics

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** Starting a Business · **Lesson:** The Entrepreneur's Character — Seven Characteristics
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 10 — Starting a Business (CURRICULUM.md / TITLES.md ladder).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** seven characteristics carry a successful entrepreneur: passion, self-confidence, risk-taking, creativity, being visionary, motivation, and personal effectiveness.
**This lesson's own level:** It stands on the kinds-of-entrepreneurs lesson: that one sorts entrepreneurs from outside; this one builds the character from inside.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an
image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture
of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does
with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and
while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is
dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the
student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph,
or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be
shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of this same title, in ladder order — and, for the whole title, the trades and career lessons earlier in the course. Recommended, never forced.
- A learner may jump in here directly; this lesson stands alone.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name all seven characteristics.
2. Say where money ranks for the passionate entrepreneur.
3. Define risk-taking in the book's words.
4. Tell creativity in business from creativity in art.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the learner does the opening task in lesson/lesson.md, before any explanation.
2. **The explanation** — the sub lessons in order (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE: a picture of a young trader wearing the seven characteristics as seven labelled badges — the student points to the badge each described action shows.
3. **Say it to your companion** — in lesson/lesson.md.
   - ADD VIDEO: the script in video-script.md, filmed — told in the super-simple voice (made later, when videos are made).
4. **Questions** — from questions/: multiple-choice, true-or-false, short-answer — served at the learner's level and teaching-mix (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** — the doing block at the end of lesson/lesson.md.

## Add-ons for this lesson (the full list)
- IMAGE (described slot only) — the seven badges on one trader — the student matches action to badge.
- VIDEO — the video-script filmed (made later) → the student tells the lesson's main idea along with it.

A slot the builder cannot fill yet stays in the plan as a slot — the
plan is the build sheet, so nothing the lesson needs gets forgotten.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks creativity means art → the book's creativity is problem-solving: new and better ways that have not occurred to others. A cheaper delivery route is creativity here.
