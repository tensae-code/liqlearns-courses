# Hands-on — The Entrepreneur's Character — Seven Characteristics

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Evidence hunt in your own life
Take your three achievements from Do it first. Under each, write which of the seven characteristics carried it, with the moment that proves it.

## Build the weakest one
Score yourself honestly on the seven. Choose the weakest and write a two-week practice for it — for personal effectiveness, a timed daily plan; for self-confidence, one small kept promise a day.
