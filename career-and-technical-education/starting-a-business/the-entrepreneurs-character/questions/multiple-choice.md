# The Entrepreneur's Character — Seven Characteristics — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** For the passionate entrepreneur, money is —
a) the only goal  b) secondary to achieving their goals — they care more about what they are doing  c) unimportant in every way
**Answer: b) secondary to achieving their goals — they care more about what they are doing** — income matters, but it is secondary.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Self-confidence means believing —
a) problems will never come  b) you can overcome the problems that come, and are worthy of success  c) others will solve everything
**Answer: b) you can overcome the problems that come, and are worthy of success** — problems are certain; belief is the characteristic.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Risk-taking is —
a) gambling blindly  b) assuming responsibility for the loss that may occur from the future's unexpected possibilities  c) avoiding all decisions
**Answer: b) assuming responsibility for the loss that may occur from the future's unexpected possibilities** — responsibility for possible loss is the definition.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Creativity in an entrepreneur means —
a) painting and poetry only  b) finding innovative ways to solve problems — new and better ways others had not thought of  c) copying exactly
**Answer: b) finding innovative ways to solve problems — new and better ways others had not thought of** — business creativity is problem-solving.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A visionary entrepreneur —
a) dreams without working  b) creates a vision of the future they want and works to achieve it, setting short- and long-term goals  c) never sets goals
**Answer: b) creates a vision of the future they want and works to achieve it, setting short- and long-term goals** — vision plus goals plus work.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Personal effectiveness includes —
a) managing time well and handling personal business efficiently under pressure  b) ignoring first impressions  c) hiding your weaknesses from yourself
**Answer: a) managing time well and handling personal business efficiently under pressure** — time, pressure, first impressions, self-knowledge.
