# The Entrepreneur's Character — Seven Characteristics — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the seven characteristics in order.
**Answer:** Passion; self-confidence; risk-taking; creativity; visionary; motivation; personal effectiveness.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the visionary do with problems?
**Answer:** Sees problems as opportunities (and sets short- and long-term goals toward the vision).
