# The Entrepreneur's Character — Seven Characteristics — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Successful entrepreneurs accept risk as a natural part of achieving goals.
**Answer: True** — their self-confidence carries them through it.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Motivation must come from a boss or teacher.
**Answer: False** — the entrepreneur must be self-motivated — a self-starter.
