# The Entrepreneur's Character — Seven Characteristics

## Do it first

First, the book's own opening activity: list three major achievements of your own life, and share them — with a companion, or on paper. Notice what carried you in each: wanting it, believing, daring, inventing, driving. Those are this lesson's words.

## Sub lesson 1 — Passion, self-confidence, risk-taking

1. **Passion** — commitment and deep care for the work give the best chance of success. Entrepreneurs typically care **more about what they are doing than how much money they might make** — income matters, but it is **secondary** to achieving their goals.

2. **Self-confidence** — every entrepreneur encounters problems and must **believe they can overcome them**, and that they are worthy of success.

3. **Risk-taking** — **assuming responsibility for the loss that may occur** from the future's unexpected possibilities. Where most people avoid risk, successful entrepreneurs **accept it as a natural part of achieving goals** — their self-confidence carrying them through.

## Sub lesson 2 — Creativity, visionary, motivation, personal effectiveness

4. **Creativity** — not necessarily painting or poetry, but **finding innovative ways to solve problems**, always looking for new and better ways that have not occurred to others.

5. **Visionary** — creating a **vision of the future they want** and working to achieve it; seeing problems as opportunities; setting both **short- and long-term goals**.

6. **Motivation** — the **inner drive** that ignites and sustains behaviour to satisfy needs. The potential entrepreneur must be **self-motivated** — a self-starter who sees and pursues opportunities.

7. **Personal effectiveness** — managing time well and handling personal business efficiently **under pressure**; attending to **first impressions** (personal appearance, communication skills); and knowing one's own **strengths and weaknesses**.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the one thing this lesson teaches, in your own words: seven characteristics carry a successful entrepreneur: passion, self-confidence, risk-taking, creativity, being visionary, motivation, and personal effectiveness. Your companion checks you against the lesson, then swaps roles.

## Do It Yourself

1. Write the seven characteristics from memory, numbered.
2. Write money's rank under passion, in one line.
3. Write your strongest and weakest, with one evidence line each.
