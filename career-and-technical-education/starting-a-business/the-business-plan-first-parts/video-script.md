# Video script — The Business Plan — Summary, Mission, Situation and Product

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

An idea in your head is not a plan.
A business plan is a document.
Written. Formal.
It holds your goals.
Your methods for reaching them.
And the time it will take.

Its first part is the summary.
One to three pages. The whole plan, clear.
A lender reads this first — make the message plain.
Then mission and vision — your purpose and drive.
Then the situation — strengths, weaknesses,
opportunities, threats.
Then the product — what you will offer the market.

Now you. Choose your enterprise, and begin.
