# Hands-on — The Business Plan — Summary, Mission, Situation and Product

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Draft the first parts for your enterprise
For the enterprise you chose, write: a half-page executive summary with one clear message; a mission/vision statement carrying start date, founders, employees, location, facilities and product; and a four-part situational analysis — strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, threats, honestly.

## Test the summary
Give only your executive summary to a partner. They must be able to say, after one reading: what the business is, who it serves, and why it can work. If they cannot, rewrite until they can.
