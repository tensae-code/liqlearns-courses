# Lesson plan — The Business Plan — Summary, Mission, Situation and Product

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** Starting a Business · **Lesson:** The Business Plan — Summary, Mission, Situation and Product
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 10 — Starting a Business (CURRICULUM.md / TITLES.md ladder).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a business plan is the formal written document of a business's goals, its methods, and its timeline — and its first components are the executive summary, the mission/vision, the situational analysis, and the product or service.
**This lesson's own level:** It stands on the trades and the career each learner selected earlier in this course: the plan is written for that very trade, as the book's activity directs.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an
image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture
of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does
with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and
while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is
dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the
student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph,
or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be
shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Define a business plan: goals, methods, time.
2. Say what the executive summary is and why clarity rules it.
3. List what the mission/vision statement carries.
4. Run a situational analysis in four parts, and state a product/service plainly.

## If the learner is stuck
- Writes the executive summary as decoration → it is the lender's quick read of the whole plan. If the summary is vague, the plan is unread beneath it; clarity is the component's whole job.
