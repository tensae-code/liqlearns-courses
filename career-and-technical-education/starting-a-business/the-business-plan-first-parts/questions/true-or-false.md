# The Business Plan — Summary, Mission, Situation and Product — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An unwritten idea in the founder's head counts as a business plan.
**Answer: False** — a plan is a formal written document.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The executive summary must carry a clear message because a lender reads it to understand the business quickly.
**Answer: True** — that is why clarity rules the component.
