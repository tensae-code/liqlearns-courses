# The Business Plan — Summary, Mission, Situation and Product — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the first four components of the business plan.
**Answer:** Executive summary; mission/vision statement; situational analysis; product/service.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Write the four parts of a situational analysis.
**Answer:** Internal strengths; internal weaknesses; external opportunities; external threats.
