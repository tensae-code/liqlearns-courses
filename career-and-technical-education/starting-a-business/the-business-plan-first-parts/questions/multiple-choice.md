# The Business Plan — Summary, Mission, Situation and Product — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A business plan is a formal written document containing —
a) only the business name  b) the goals of the business, the methods for achieving them, and the time for achieving them  c) a list of competitors
**Answer: b) the goals of the business, the methods for achieving them, and the time for achieving them** — goals, methods, time.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The plan is written for —
a) any business in general  b) the one enterprise the learner selects from the trades they know  c) the teacher's business
**Answer: b) the one enterprise the learner selects from the trades they know** — select one enterprise and start it properly.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The executive summary is —
a) a one-to-three-page overview of the whole plan, which a lender reads to understand the business quickly  b) the longest chapter  c) a price list
**Answer: a) a one-to-three-page overview of the whole plan, which a lender reads to understand the business quickly** — clarity rules it because the lender reads it first.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The mission/vision statement carries —
a) only a slogan  b) the purpose and drive, with start date, founders, employees, location, facilities and products/services  c) only the budget
**Answer: b) the purpose and drive, with start date, founders, employees, location, facilities and products/services** — purpose plus the practical facts.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** A situational analysis sets —
a) internal strengths and weaknesses against external opportunities and threats  b) prices against competitors only  c) the staff rota
**Answer: a) internal strengths and weaknesses against external opportunities and threats** — four sides, honestly read.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** The product/service component states —
a) what the business will offer the market  b) the owner's hobbies  c) next year's weather
**Answer: a) what the business will offer the market** — the offer, named plainly.
