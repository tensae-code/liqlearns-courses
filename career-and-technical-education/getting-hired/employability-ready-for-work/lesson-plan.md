# Lesson plan — Employability — Ready to Be Hired, and to Stay Hired

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** Getting Hired · **Lesson:** Employability — Ready to Be Hired, and to Stay Hired
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 9 — Getting Hired (CURRICULUM.md / TITLES.md ladder).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** employability is the mix of knowledge, skills, attitudes and presentation — and, deeper, of skills AND behaviours — that makes a person able to get a job, keep it, progress, and re-enter work after change.
**This lesson's own level:** This lesson climbs in depth: the first sub lesson is the Grade 7 book's four supports; the second and third are the Grade 8 book's deeper treatment of the same idea — behaviours beside skills, and the ILO framing of a whole working life. The depth structure is stated in levels.md.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what employability is, and name its four supports.
2. Explain why technical skills alone do not guarantee success.
3. Name the core behaviours: honesty, reliability, punctuality, attendance, loyalty.
4. Tell the ILO list: secure, retain, progress, cope with change, re-enter.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks employability means only exam knowledge → point at the second sub lesson: the behaviours list. A worker who is skilled but late, absent and dishonest is not employable for long.
