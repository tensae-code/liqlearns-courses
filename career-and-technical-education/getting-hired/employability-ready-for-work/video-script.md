# Video script — Employability — Ready to Be Hired, and to Stay Hired

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

To be employable is to be ready.
Ready to be hired. Ready to stay hired.

You stand on four supports.
Knowledge — what you know.
Skills — what you can do.
Attitude — how you approach things.
Presentation — how you show it all.

But skills alone are not enough.
You need behaviours too.
Honesty. Reliability. Punctuality.
Showing up. Loyalty.

The most employable people keep learning.
They get the job. They keep the job.
They progress. They cope with change.
And if they lose a job, they can enter again.

Now you. Which support will you build first?
