# Employability — Ready to Be Hired, and to Stay Hired — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Employability rests on four supports. Which list is right?
a) Knowledge, skills, attitudes, presentation  b) Money, luck, family, age  c) Tools, uniform, transport, food
**Answer: a) Knowledge, skills, attitudes, presentation** — the four supports are knowledge, skills, attitudes and presentation.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Presentation means —
a) wrapping gifts  b) how you communicate your capabilities, orally and in writing, to employers  c) wearing the most expensive clothes
**Answer: b) how you communicate your capabilities, orally and in writing, to employers** — presentation is how you show what you know and can do.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Grade 8 book insists employability skills are —
a) technical skills only  b) skills AND behaviours necessary for every job  c) exam marks only
**Answer: b) skills AND behaviours necessary for every job** — skills alone, without behaviours, do not guarantee success.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is a core employability behaviour named in the lesson?
a) Honesty and punctuality  b) Arriving late  c) Keeping knowledge secret
**Answer: a) Honesty and punctuality** — honesty, reliability, punctuality, attendance and loyalty are named.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** On the ILO framing, employability skills help a worker to —
a) secure and retain a job, progress, cope with change, and re-enter if laid off  b) never change jobs  c) avoid learning anything new
**Answer: a) secure and retain a job, progress, cope with change, and re-enter if laid off** — that is the ILO list.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Employers want people who —
a) stop learning once hired  b) keep learning and adapting, and communicate effectively  c) only work alone
**Answer: b) keep learning and adapting, and communicate effectively** — lifelong learning and communication are what employers want.
