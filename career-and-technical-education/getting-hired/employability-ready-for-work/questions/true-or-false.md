# Employability — Ready to Be Hired, and to Stay Hired — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Attitude — how you approach things — is one of the four supports of employability.
**Answer: True** — knowledge, skills, attitudes, presentation.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Having only technical skills guarantees career success.
**Answer: False** — the book says plainly it does not — behaviours are needed too.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Employability matters only at the moment of first hiring.
**Answer: False** — it covers retaining, progressing, coping with change and re-entering across a working life.
