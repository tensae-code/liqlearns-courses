# Employability — Ready to Be Hired, and to Stay Hired — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the four supports of employability.
**Answer:** Knowledge, skills, attitudes and presentation.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name three core employability behaviours.
**Answer:** Any three of: honesty, reliability, punctuality, attendance, loyalty.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What can an employable worker do after being laid off?
**Answer:** Secure another job and enter the labour market again more easily (the ILO framing).
