# Hands-on — Employability — Ready to Be Hired, and to Stay Hired

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Rate your four supports
Draw the four supports. Under each, write one honest line: strong, growing, or weak — with one piece of evidence from your own life.

## Behaviour audit for one week
For one week, track three behaviours daily: punctuality, attendance (did you show up where you promised?), and finishing what you started. Tally them at week's end.
