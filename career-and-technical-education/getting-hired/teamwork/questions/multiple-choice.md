# Teamwork — Working as Part of a Team — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A team is —
a) any crowd of people  b) a group of interdependent individuals working toward a common goal, with clearly defined roles  c) people waiting together
**Answer: b) a group of interdependent individuals working toward a common goal, with clearly defined roles** — common goal plus clear roles make a team.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which list gives the four characteristics of an effective team?
a) Cohesion, communication, commitment, accountability  b) Speed, silence, secrecy, strength  c) Height, age, wealth, luck
**Answer: a) Cohesion, communication, commitment, accountability** — those four hold a team together.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Communication in a team helps to —
a) create confusion  b) overcome obstacles, resolve conflict and avoid confusion  c) end the common goal
**Answer: b) overcome obstacles, resolve conflict and avoid confusion** — and it defines the purpose, which increases cohesion.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Debo and wonfel are —
a) kinds of seed  b) neighbours pooling labour for farm work in rotation  c) types of tax
**Answer: b) neighbours pooling labour for farm work in rotation** — today your field, tomorrow mine.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Edir is —
a) a farming tool  b) an informal organisation established to solve social and economic problems  c) a school subject
**Answer: b) an informal organisation established to solve social and economic problems** — edir is the community standing together against trouble.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Teamwork in this lesson is presented as —
a) a foreign idea  b) already native to rural Ethiopia, in debo, wonfel and edir  c) only for factories
**Answer: b) already native to rural Ethiopia, in debo, wonfel and edir** — the book bridges from the classroom team to those community practices.
