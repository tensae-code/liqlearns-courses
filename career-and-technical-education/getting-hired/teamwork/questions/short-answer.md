# Teamwork — Working as Part of a Team — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How do communication and cohesion feed each other?
**Answer:** Communication defines the team's purpose so there is a common goal, and having a common goal itself increases cohesion.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give one difference between debo/wonfel and edir.
**Answer:** Debo and wonfel pool neighbours' labour for agricultural work in rotation; edir is an organisation for solving social and economic problems.
