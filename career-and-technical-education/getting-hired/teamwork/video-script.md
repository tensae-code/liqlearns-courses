# Video script — Teamwork — Working as Part of a Team

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A team is not a crowd.
A team works together, toward one common goal.
Each person has a clear role.

Four things hold a team together.
Cohesion — the group sticks.
Communication — the group understands.
Commitment — the group stays focused.
Accountability — everyone takes part.

Ethiopia already knows this way.
Debo and wonfel — neighbours work each field in turn.
Edir — the community solves problems together.

Now you. Name your team's common goal.
