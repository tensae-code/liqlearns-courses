# Hands-on — Teamwork — Working as Part of a Team

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Run a four-characteristic job
In a small group, do one real job together (clean a shared place, prepare a display, organise materials). Before starting, name the common goal aloud and give each person a role. Afterwards, score yourselves on cohesion, communication, commitment and accountability.

## Ask about debo and edir
Ask an elder at home: how does debo or wonfel rotation work here, and what does our edir do? Write the answers in the elder's own order.
