# Lesson plan — Teamwork — Working as Part of a Team

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** Getting Hired · **Lesson:** Teamwork — Working as Part of a Team
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 9 — Getting Hired (CURRICULUM.md / TITLES.md ladder).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** an effective team has cohesion, communication, commitment and accountability around a common goal — and in Ethiopia that way of working already lives in debo, wonfel and edir.
**This lesson's own level:** It stands on the eight employability skills, where teamwork is skill number 3; this is the Grade 8 book's deeper lesson on that one skill.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an
image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture
of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does
with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and
while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is
dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the
student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph,
or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be
shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what teamwork and a team are.
2. Name the four characteristics of an effective team.
3. Explain how communication and cohesion feed each other.
4. Say what debo, wonfel and edir each are.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. A group to work with helps.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the learner does the opening task in lesson/lesson.md, before any explanation.
2. **The explanation** — the sub lessons in order (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE: a picture of a team ring with the four characteristics as four joined sections and a shared goal at the centre — the student names which characteristic a struggling team is missing.
   - ADD IMAGE: a picture of neighbours working one family's field together in rotation, with an edir meeting inset — the student says whose field is next in the rotation.
3. **Say it to your companion** — in lesson/lesson.md.
   - ADD VIDEO: the script in video-script.md, filmed — told in the super-simple voice (made later, when videos are made).
4. **Questions** — from questions/: multiple-choice, true-or-false, short-answer — served at the learner's level and teaching-mix (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** — the doing block at the end of lesson/lesson.md.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks a team is just people standing together → ask for the common goal and the roles. No shared goal and no clear roles means a crowd, not a team.
