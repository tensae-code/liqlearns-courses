## Sub lesson 1 — The effective team

**Teamwork** is the collaborative effort of a group to achieve a **common goal** or complete a task in the most effective and efficient way. A **team** is a group of interdependent individuals working toward that goal, uniting their resources, with **clearly defined roles** so everyone has a clear purpose.

An effective team holds four interrelated characteristics:

- **Cohesion** — the group sticks together; strong cohesion is positively related to performance.
- **Communication** — to overcome obstacles, resolve conflict and avoid confusion — and to define the team's purpose, so there IS a common goal, which itself increases cohesion.
- **Commitment** — members stay focused on the common goal.
- **Accountability** — milestones are reached and all members participate, which increases commitment.

Building teamwork skills improves results and communication — listening, taking instruction, offering support and ideas — and gives more chances to move forward in a career.

## Sub lesson 2 — Teamwork at home — debo, wonfel and edir

Teamwork is already native to rural Ethiopia.

**Debo** and **wonfel** are the practice of neighbours pooling their labour to perform agricultural activities **in rotation** — today your field, tomorrow mine.

**Edir** is the informal organisation established to solve **social and economic problems** — the community standing together when trouble comes.

The classroom team and the employer's team are not foreign ideas. They are the same way of working you already live inside.
