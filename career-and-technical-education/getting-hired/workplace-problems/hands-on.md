# Hands-on — Workplace Problems — Conflict, Communication, Discrimination, Harassment, Dissatisfaction, Gossip

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Name that problem
Write six small scenes of your own — one per problem — without naming the problem. Swap with a partner: each names the other's six and gives the counter for each.

## Fix the dissatisfaction list
Take the causes of job dissatisfaction. For a workplace you know, mark which causes are present, and match each to its improvement from the lesson.
