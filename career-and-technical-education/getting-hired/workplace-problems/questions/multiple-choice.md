# Workplace Problems — Conflict, Communication, Discrimination, Harassment, Dissatisfaction, Gossip — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Conflict between individuals at work arises from —
a) identical opinions  b) differences in thought process, attitudes, understanding and interests  c) too much free time only
**Answer: b) differences in thought process, attitudes, understanding and interests** — difference is its root — and it is often unavoidable.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Discrimination is avoided by —
a) hiring only one group  b) giving equal opportunity to all employees  c) ignoring it
**Answer: b) giving equal opportunity to all employees** — equal opportunity is the book's counter.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Great communication in an organisation raises —
a) morale, productivity and commitment  b) confusion  c) gossip
**Answer: a) morale, productivity and commitment** — open lines quickly create trust, too.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Harassment is —
a) one polite request  b) systematic and/or continued unwanted and annoying actions, including physical threats  c) any disagreement
**Answer: b) systematic and/or continued unwanted and annoying actions, including physical threats** — continued and unwanted is the mark of it.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Job dissatisfaction can be caused by —
a) poor conditions, overwork, low pay and lack of recognition  b) too much job security  c) clear communication
**Answer: a) poor conditions, overwork, low pay and lack of recognition** — those are the book's causes.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Workplace gossip damages —
a) only the building  b) morale, trust, respect and relationships  c) nothing
**Answer: b) morale, trust, respect and relationships** — and it can result in poor performance.
