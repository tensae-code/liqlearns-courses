# Workplace Problems — Conflict, Communication, Discrimination, Harassment, Dissatisfaction, Gossip — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the six common workplace problems.
**Answer:** Conflict; poor communication; discrimination; harassment; job dissatisfaction; gossip.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give three ways harassment is prevented.
**Answer:** Any three of: creating awareness; being a role model; following up on misbehaving employees; open communication; mutual respect; coaching.
