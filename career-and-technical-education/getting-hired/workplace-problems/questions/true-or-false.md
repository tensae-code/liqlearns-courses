# Workplace Problems — Conflict, Communication, Discrimination, Harassment, Dissatisfaction, Gossip — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Discrimination means the unfair or harmful treatment of people based on race, gender, age, ethnicity or disability.
**Answer: True** — that is its definition in the lesson.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Gossip means being unhappy with your own job.
**Answer: False** — that is job dissatisfaction — the book's own review swaps these two definitions; taught corrected here.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Serious harassment cases can be resolved through a legal process.
**Answer: True** — prevention first; serious cases go legal.
