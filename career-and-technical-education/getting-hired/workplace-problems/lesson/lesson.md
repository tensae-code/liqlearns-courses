# Workplace Problems — Conflict, Communication, Discrimination, Harassment, Dissatisfaction, Gossip

## Do it first

Think of any group that works together — a class committee counts. Name one thing that went wrong between people in it. Hold it in mind; by the end, you will be able to give it its proper name.

## Sub lesson 1 — Conflict, poor communication, discrimination

**Conflict** — a clash between individuals arising from differences in thought process, attitudes, understanding and interests. It is often unavoidable when people of different backgrounds, ideas and experiences work together — which is why handling it, not wishing it away, is the skill.

**Poor communication** — communication is the exchange of information and ideas in an organisation. Great communication raises **morale, productivity and commitment**; managers who build open lines quickly create trust, and employees who communicate well with colleagues and bosses remain valuable assets. Poor communication is the same channel, broken.

**Discrimination** — the unfair or harmful treatment of people and groups based on **race, gender, age, ethnicity, disability** and the like. It is avoided by giving **equal opportunity to all employees**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Harassment, job dissatisfaction, gossip

**Harassment** — systematic and/or continued unwanted and annoying actions of one party or group, including physical threats. It is prevented by creating awareness, being a role model, following up on misbehaving employees, open communication, mutual respect and coaching — with serious cases resolved through a **legal process**.

**Job dissatisfaction** — unhappy or negative feelings about work or the work environment, caused by poor conditions, overwork, low pay, lack of advancement, the employer's behaviour and lack of recognition. It is improved by job security, compensation, increased salary, better communication and a safe working environment.

**Workplace gossip** — informal communication shared about colleagues, coworkers or supervisors. It damages **morale, trust, respect and relationships** and can result in poor performance. It is countered by being a role model, focusing on the solution and creating an open working culture.

## Say it to your companion

Your companion checks you against the lesson, then swaps roles.
