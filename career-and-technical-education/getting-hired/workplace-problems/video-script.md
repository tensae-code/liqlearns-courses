# Video script — Workplace Problems — Conflict, Communication, Discrimination, Harassment, Dissatisfaction, Gossip

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Six problems live in workplaces.
One — conflict. People clash. It happens.
Two — poor communication. The channel breaks.
Three — discrimination. Unfair treatment. Never right.
Four — harassment. Unwanted actions, again and again.
Five — job dissatisfaction. Unhappy with the work itself.
Six — gossip. Talk that damages trust.

Each one has a counter.
Open communication. Equal opportunity.
Respect. A safe, fair workplace.

Now you. Name the six, in order.
