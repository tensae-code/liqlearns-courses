# Hands-on — Employee and Employer — and Solving Problems at Work

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Label your locality's pairs
Pick three workplaces near you. For each, name the employer and one employee, and say what specific job the employee was hired to do.

## Solve one real problem on paper
Take one small real problem (at school, home or work). Write it through the three moves: identify it exactly, list possible solution paths, choose and state the appropriate action — and name which of the eight feeding skills you used most.
