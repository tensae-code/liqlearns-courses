# Video script — Employee and Employer — and Solving Problems at Work

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Who is who at work?
The employer hires. The employee is hired.
Application first. Interview next. Then — employee.

And at work, problems come.
Problem-solving has three moves.
Identify the problem.
Develop possible solutions.
Take the right action — calmly.

Remember Latamo.
He talked. He encouraged. He documented warnings.
When conflict broke out, he stopped it at once.
He found the real reason with all the staff.
Then a written warning — the final step.
And everyone agreed: a peaceful workplace.

Now you. Name the employer and employee in one shop you know.
