# Lesson plan — Employee and Employer — and Solving Problems at Work

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** Getting Hired · **Lesson:** Employee and Employer — and Solving Problems at Work
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 9 — Getting Hired (CURRICULUM.md / TITLES.md ladder).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** an employee is hired by an employer after application and interview; and once at work, problem-solving — identify the problem, develop solutions, act — is the skill that handles difficult situations calmly, as the Latamo story shows.
**This lesson's own level:** It stands on the interview lessons' world from the other side: this Grade 8 lesson names the two parties to a job, then the problem-solving skill the workplace runs on.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Tell an employee from an employer, with the hiring path between them.
2. Give the three moves of problem-solving.
3. Name at least four skills that feed problem-solving.
4. Retell the Latamo story's steps in order, and say why the written warning could be issued.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil.

## If the learner is stuck
