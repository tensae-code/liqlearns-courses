# Employee and Employer — and Solving Problems at Work — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An employee is —
a) the one who hires  b) an individual hired by an employer to do a specific job, after application and interview  c) any customer
**Answer: b) an individual hired by an employer to do a specific job, after application and interview** — hired after application and interview.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these can be an employer?
a) Only a government office  b) An organisation, company, shop, supermarket — or an individual  c) Only a factory
**Answer: b) An organisation, company, shop, supermarket — or an individual** — the book's list runs from institutions down to an individual.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Problem-solving is the process of —
a) identifying a problem, developing possible solution paths, and taking the appropriate course of action  b) waiting for problems to pass  c) blaming the nearest person
**Answer: a) identifying a problem, developing possible solution paths, and taking the appropriate course of action** — identify, develop paths, act.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is one of the skills that feed problem-solving?
a) Active listening and decision making  b) Gossip  c) Arriving late
**Answer: a) Active listening and decision making** — the list: listening, analysis, research, creativity, communication, dependability, decision making, team-building.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Why could Latamo issue a written warning letter?
a) He was angry  b) A verbal warning was already documented, so the written warning was the final step before termination  c) It was Legesse's first mistake
**Answer: b) A verbal warning was already documented, so the written warning was the final step before termination** — documentation made the final step stand.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** The real reason under the floor conflict was —
a) a broken tool  b) harassment — Legesse touching Miss Sajitu's hair without permission  c) a pay dispute
**Answer: b) harassment — Legesse touching Miss Sajitu's hair without permission** — the staff discussion uncovered it.
