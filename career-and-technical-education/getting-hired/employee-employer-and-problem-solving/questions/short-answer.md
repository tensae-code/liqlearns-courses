# Employee and Employer — and Solving Problems at Work — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three moves of problem-solving.
**Answer:** Identify the problem; develop possible solution paths; take the appropriate course of action.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give four skills that feed problem-solving.
**Answer:** Any four of: active listening, analysis, research, creativity, communication, dependability, decision making, team-building.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why did Latamo gather all the staff after stopping the conflict?
**Answer:** To discuss and uncover the real reason — the harassment behind the disagreement — together.
