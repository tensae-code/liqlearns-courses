# Lesson plan — The Eight Employability Skills

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** Getting Hired · **Lesson:** The Eight Employability Skills
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 9 — Getting Hired (CURRICULUM.md / TITLES.md ladder).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the eight employability skills — personal development, communication, teamwork, problem solving, initiative, using new technologies, planning and organising, and self-management — are the non-technical skills every job needs.
**This lesson's own level:** It stands on the employability lesson before it: that lesson says what employability is; this one names its eight working parts.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an
image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture
of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does
with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and
while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is
dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the
student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph,
or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be
shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name all eight employability skills.
2. Say what each skill means, in one line each.
3. Give a daily practice for planning and organising.
4. Say where self-management begins.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the learner does the opening task in lesson/lesson.md, before any explanation.
2. **The explanation** — the sub lessons in order (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE: a picture of an eight-spoke wheel, one employability skill and a small work scene on each spoke — the student points to the spoke a given job wish names.
   - ADD AUDIO: a voice reading the eight skills aloud in order — the student counts them off on their fingers.
3. **Say it to your companion** — in lesson/lesson.md.
   - ADD VIDEO: the script in video-script.md, filmed — told in the super-simple voice (made later, when videos are made).
4. **Questions** — from questions/: multiple-choice, true-or-false, short-answer — served at the learner's level and teaching-mix (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** — the doing block at the end of lesson/lesson.md.

## If the learner is stuck
- Cannot hold eight apart → anchor each to a scene: initiative is the one who starts; self-management is the one who stays calm and knows themselves; planning is the timetable on the wall.
