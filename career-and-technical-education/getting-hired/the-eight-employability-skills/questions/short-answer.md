# The Eight Employability Skills — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is problem solving, as an employability skill?
**Answer:** Determining why a problem is happening and how to resolve it.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the last four of the eight skills.
**Answer:** Initiative; ability to use new technologies; planning and organising; self-management.
