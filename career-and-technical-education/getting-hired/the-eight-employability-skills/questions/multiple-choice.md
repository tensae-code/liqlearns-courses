# The Eight Employability Skills — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Employability skills are —
a) skills for one trade only  b) non-technical skills needed to get and keep employment, useful across every sector  c) skills only managers need
**Answer: b) non-technical skills needed to get and keep employment, useful across every sector** — they are non-technical and travel across sectors.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Personal development means —
a) setting goals, assessing your skills against career requirements, and filling the gap  b) buying new clothes  c) waiting to be chosen
**Answer: a) setting goals, assessing your skills against career requirements, and filling the gap** — assess, compare, fill the gap.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Communication includes —
a) only speaking  b) transmitting and receiving messages accurately, including listening  c) only writing letters
**Answer: b) transmitting and receiving messages accurately, including listening** — listening is part of communication, in verbal and non-verbal forms.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Initiative means —
a) acting or taking charge before others do  b) waiting for instructions forever  c) copying a neighbour
**Answer: a) acting or taking charge before others do** — initiative acts first.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Planning and organising can be practised through —
a) a daily timetable or a written study plan  b) guessing each morning  c) doing tasks in any order
**Answer: a) a daily timetable or a written study plan** — a timetable is the book's own practice.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Self-management begins from —
a) knowing your manager  b) knowing yourself — potential, strengths, weaknesses, likes, values and feelings  c) owning a phone
**Answer: b) knowing yourself — potential, strengths, weaknesses, likes, values and feelings** — self-management starts with self-knowledge.
