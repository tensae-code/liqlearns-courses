# The Eight Employability Skills

## Do it first

Read these job wishes: 'wanted — a worker who listens well, works with others, and finishes on time.' Which of those can a school certificate prove? Which must a person show by how they act? Keep the question open.

## Sub lesson 1 — The first four skills

**Employability skills** are the non-technical skills, knowledge and understandings needed to **get and keep employment**, useful across every sector — essential skills like communication, qualities like patience and commitment, values like respect and trust.

The first four of the eight:

1. **Personal development** — setting goals, assessing your skills and qualities, comparing them with career requirements, and finding and filling the gap.
2. **Communication** — transmitting and receiving messages accurately — spoken, written, by movement or signs — including listening; in verbal and non-verbal forms.
3. **Teamwork** — working harmoniously with others in groups to accomplish something.
4. **Problem solving** — determining why a problem is happening and how to resolve it.

## Sub lesson 2 — The last four skills

5. **Initiative** — acting or taking charge before others do.
6. **Ability to use new technologies** — familiarising yourself with the technology available and using it productively.
7. **Planning and organising** — identifying tasks, prioritising, scheduling and completing on time — practised through a daily timetable or a written study plan.
8. **Self-management** — regulating your emotions, thoughts and behaviour, beginning from **knowing yourself**: your potential, strengths, weaknesses, likes, values and feelings.

Eight skills, none of them tied to one trade — that is why they travel with you into every job.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the one thing this lesson teaches, in your own words: the eight employability skills — personal development, communication, teamwork, problem solving, initiative, using new technologies, planning and organising, and self-management — are the non-technical skills every job needs. Your companion checks you against the lesson, then swaps roles.

3. Circle the weakest one and write the practice you will use for it.
