# Video script — The Eight Employability Skills

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Eight skills. Every employer wants them.
One — personal development. Know your gap, and fill it.
Two — communication. Speak, write, and listen well.
Three — teamwork. Work in harmony with others.
Four — problem solving. Find why, then fix it.
Five — initiative. Act before others do.
Six — new technologies. Learn them, use them well.
Seven — planning and organising. Finish on time.
Eight — self-management. Know yourself. Govern yourself.

Now you. Which of the eight is your strongest?
