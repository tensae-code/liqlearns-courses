# Hands-on — The Eight Employability Skills

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Skill-spotting in a real workplace
Watch a real worker for ten minutes (at home, in a shop, on a farm). Write down every moment you see one of the eight skills being used, and name the skill.

## Your gap list
Do personal development on yourself: list your skills and qualities, list what your chosen career requires, and write the gap — then one step that would fill it.
