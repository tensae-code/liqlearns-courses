# Preparing for Your Interview — Traps and the Six-Step Checklist

## Do it first

List everything that could go wrong for you in an interview — being nervous, dressing wrong, not knowing what to say. Write your honest list. The lesson will name its seven; compare them at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Why applicants fail — the seven traps

Candidates fail interviews for identifiable reasons — traps you can see coming:

1. **Inability to express oneself clearly** — poor voice, grammar mistakes.
2. **Poor personal appearance.**
3. **Not being prepared** for the interview.
4. **Uncertainty about future goals** and career plans.
5. **Asking no questions** — or poor questions — about the position.
6. **Lack of confidence** — nervousness, failing to meet the interviewer's eye.
7. **Overemphasis on money** — being too interested in salary.

Read them as a warning list, not a fate: every trap on it has preparation as its counter.

## Sub lesson 2 — The six-step preparation checklist

Preparation starts by knowing **what you want to achieve** from the interview. Then work the six-item checklist:

1. **Know the job.**
2. **Match what is required with what you possess.**
3. **Know the employer** — the company.
4. **Study your CV** and know everything in it.
5. **Practise interviewing** — the **mock interview**: rehearsing answers to likely questions before the actual interview.
6. **Know what to wear and what to bring.**

The checklist and the trap list are the same lesson seen from two sides: preparation (checklist item 5) answers trap 3; knowing your goals (the starting point) answers trap 4; studying your CV and the job answers traps 1 and 5; the mock interview builds the confidence that answers trap 6.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the one thing this lesson teaches, in your own words: applicants fail interviews for seven named reasons — and preparation is a six-item checklist that ends in a mock interview, so none of the seven catches you. Your companion checks you against the lesson, then swaps roles.

## Do It Yourself

1. Write the seven traps from memory.
2. Write the six checklist items from memory, in order.
3. Beside each of three traps, write the checklist item or starting point that counters it.
