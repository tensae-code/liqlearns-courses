# Preparing for Your Interview — Traps and the Six-Step Checklist — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Asking no questions about the position can help you fail an interview.
**Answer: True** — it is trap 5 — asking nothing, or poor questions.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Preparation is only about clothes.
**Answer: False** — the checklist runs from knowing the job to the mock interview; clothes are item 6 of 6.
