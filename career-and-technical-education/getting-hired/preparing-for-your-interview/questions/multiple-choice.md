# Preparing for Your Interview — Traps and the Six-Step Checklist — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which is a reason applicants fail interviews?
a) Knowing the job too well  b) Overemphasis on money — being too interested in salary  c) Thanking the interviewer
**Answer: b) Overemphasis on money — being too interested in salary** — it is trap 7.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Failing to meet the interviewer's eye belongs to which trap?
a) Poor appearance  b) Lack of confidence  c) Asking poor questions
**Answer: b) Lack of confidence** — nervousness and no eye contact are confidence failures.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The preparation checklist begins, before its six items, by —
a) buying new clothes  b) knowing what you want to achieve from the interview  c) printing extra CVs
**Answer: b) knowing what you want to achieve from the interview** — purpose first, then the six items.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A mock interview is —
a) a fake job offer  b) practising interviewing — rehearsing answers to likely questions before the actual interview  c) an interview with no questions
**Answer: b) practising interviewing — rehearsing answers to likely questions before the actual interview** — rehearsal before the real thing.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Checklist item 4 is —
a) know the employer  b) study your CV and know everything in it  c) know what to wear
**Answer: b) study your CV and know everything in it** — your CV must never surprise you in the room.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Checklist item 2 is —
a) know what to wear  b) match what is required with what you possess  c) thank the interviewer
**Answer: b) match what is required with what you possess** — matching requirements to what you possess is item 2.
