# Preparing for Your Interview — Traps and the Six-Step Checklist — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** List the six checklist items in order.
**Answer:** Know the job; match what is required with what you possess; know the employer; study your CV; practise interviewing (the mock interview); know what to wear and what to bring.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name four of the seven failure reasons.
**Answer:** Any four of: inability to express oneself clearly; poor personal appearance; not being prepared; uncertainty about future goals; asking no/poor questions; lack of confidence; overemphasis on money.
