# Hands-on — Preparing for Your Interview — Traps and the Six-Step Checklist

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Run the full mock interview
Do the real rehearsal: a partner interviews you with likely questions for a job you want, in interview clothes, with your CV in hand. The partner scores you against the seven traps — any trap spotted gets rehearsed again on the spot.

## Build your checklist card
Write the six checklist items on a card. For a real or imagined interview date, tick each item only when it is truly done — CV studied, employer researched, clothes and items to bring laid out.
