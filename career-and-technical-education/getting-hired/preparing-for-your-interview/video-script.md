# Video script — Preparing for Your Interview — Traps and the Six-Step Checklist

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Why do applicants fail?
Seven reasons. All of them visible in advance.
Speaking unclearly. Poor appearance.
Not preparing. No clear goals.
No questions. No confidence.
And chasing the money in the room.

Now the counter — a checklist of six.
Know the job.
Match what is required with what you have.
Know the employer.
Study your CV.
Practise — a mock interview.
Know what to wear, and what to bring.

Prepare, and the traps stay empty.

Now you. Work your checklist.
