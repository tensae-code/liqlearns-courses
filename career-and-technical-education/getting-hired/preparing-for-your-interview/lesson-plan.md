# Lesson plan — Preparing for Your Interview — Traps and the Six-Step Checklist

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** Getting Hired · **Lesson:** Preparing for Your Interview — Traps and the Six-Step Checklist
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 9 — Getting Hired (CURRICULUM.md / TITLES.md ladder).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** applicants fail interviews for seven named reasons — and preparation is a six-item checklist that ends in a mock interview, so none of the seven catches you.
**This lesson's own level:** It stands on the interview lesson before it, and it is the top of the Getting Hired ladder: everything in this title — CV, letter, tips — lands in this one prepared conversation.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an
image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture
of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does
with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and
while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is
dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the
student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph,
or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be
shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. List the seven reasons applicants fail interviews.
2. Work the six-step preparation checklist in order.
3. Say what a mock interview is and why it sits inside the checklist.
4. Match at least three traps to the checklist item that counters them.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil, your CV, and a partner for the mock interview.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the learner does the opening task in lesson/lesson.md, before any explanation.
2. **The explanation** — the sub lessons in order (lesson/lesson.md).

## If the learner is stuck
- Treats the mock interview as play → it is checklist item 5, the rehearsal that turns traps 1, 5 and 6 (unclear expression, poor questions, no confidence) into practised answers. No mock, no counter.
