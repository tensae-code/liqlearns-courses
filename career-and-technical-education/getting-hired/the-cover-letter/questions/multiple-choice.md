# The Cover Letter — Persuading Them to Read Your CV — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A cover letter is sent —
a) instead of a CV  b) along with the CV when applying for a job  c) after you are hired
**Answer: b) along with the CV when applying for a job** — letter and CV travel together.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The main purpose of a cover letter is to —
a) replace the CV's content  b) support the CV's content and persuade the reader to open the CV  c) hide weak experience
**Answer: b) support the CV's content and persuade the reader to open the CV** — persuading the reader to open the CV is its main purpose.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The first move of the worked example is to —
a) close with kind regards  b) name the position applied for, as advertised  c) list every hobby
**Answer: b) name the position applied for, as advertised** — the job is named first.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In move 3, the writer states —
a) her salary demand  b) strong interest and the fruitful results she can produce  c) her complaints about school
**Answer: b) strong interest and the fruitful results she can produce** — move 3 gives the employer the value.

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** The cover letter is also called —
a) a job application letter  b) a receipt  c) a timetable
**Answer: a) a job application letter** — cover letter and job application letter are the same document.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The worked example's writer is —
a) a bank manager  b) a Grade 7 completer with practical skills in gardening and maintaining home appliances such as stoves  c) a foreign graduate
**Answer: b) a Grade 7 completer with practical skills in gardening and maintaining home appliances such as stoves** — that is the writer the example introduces.
