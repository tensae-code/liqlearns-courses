# The Cover Letter — Persuading Them to Read Your CV — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A cover letter briefly introduces the writer's work history, professional skills and personal interest in the job.
**Answer: True** — that is its content, briefly given.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A good cover letter repeats the whole CV word for word.
**Answer: False** — it introduces and persuades; the CV carries the detail.
