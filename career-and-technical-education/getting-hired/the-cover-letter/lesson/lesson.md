# The Cover Letter — Persuading Them to Read Your CV

## Do it first

Imagine your CV lands on a desk in a pile of twenty. What one short letter, lying on top of it, would make the reader pick yours up first? Write your guess in two sentences.

## Sub lesson 1 — What a cover letter is and does

A **cover letter** (job application letter) is sent **along with the CV** when applying for a job.

It briefly introduces the job seeker's **work history, professional skills and personal interest in the job**. Its **main purpose is to support the CV's content and persuade the reader to open the CV**.

The CV is the evidence. The cover letter is the voice that says: here is who I am, here is the job I want, and here is why my evidence is worth your next five minutes.

## Sub lesson 2 — The worked example — the letter's four moves

The book's worked example answers an advertised vacancy, and it moves in four steps:

1. **Name the position applied for** — as advertised (on a website or in a newspaper).
2. **Introduce the writer** — in the example, a Grade 7 completer wanting to improve her working skill, with practical skills in gardening and in maintaining home appliances such as stoves, learned beyond school.
3. **State strong interest and the fruitful results she can produce** — what the employer will gain.
4. **Close politely** — with kind regards.

Notice the order: the job first, then the writer, then the value to the employer, then the polite close. Write yours in the same order.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the one thing this lesson teaches, in your own words: a cover letter goes with your CV: it introduces you, names the job, shows your interest and fit — and its main purpose is to persuade the reader to open the CV. Your companion checks you against the lesson, then swaps roles.

## Do It Yourself

1. Write the four moves as four headings.
2. Draft two or three sentences under each heading for a job you want.
3. Check the finished letter against your CV: everything the letter claims must be provable in the CV.
