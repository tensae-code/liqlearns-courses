# Video script — The Cover Letter — Persuading Them to Read Your CV

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Your CV carries the facts.
But facts in a pile need a voice.
That voice is the cover letter.

It goes on top of your CV.
It names the job you are applying for.
It introduces you — briefly.
It states your interest, and what you can produce.
And it closes politely.

Its purpose is one thing:
to persuade the reader to open your CV.

Now you. Write the letter that opens yours.
