# Lesson plan — The Cover Letter — Persuading Them to Read Your CV

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** Getting Hired · **Lesson:** The Cover Letter — Persuading Them to Read Your CV
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 9 — Getting Hired (CURRICULUM.md / TITLES.md ladder).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a cover letter goes with your CV: it introduces you, names the job, shows your interest and fit — and its main purpose is to persuade the reader to open the CV.
**This lesson's own level:** It stands on the CV lesson before it: the CV carries the facts; the cover letter is the short, human introduction wrapped around it.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an
image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture
of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does
with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and
while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is
dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the
student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph,
or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be
shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what a cover letter is and its main purpose.
2. Name the four moves of the worked example, in order.
3. Draft a cover letter for a real or practice vacancy.
4. Keep the letter brief and tied to the CV beneath it.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil, your CV from the previous lesson, and a vacancy to answer.

## If the learner is stuck
- Writes a second CV instead of a letter → the letter introduces and persuades; the lists live in the CV. If a line appears in both word for word, cut it from the letter and replace it with interest and value.
