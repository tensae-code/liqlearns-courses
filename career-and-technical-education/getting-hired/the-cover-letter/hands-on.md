# Hands-on — The Cover Letter — Persuading Them to Read Your CV

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Write a real cover letter
Take one real or practice vacancy. Write the full cover letter for it in the four moves, on one page, with your own true skills in move 2 and the results you can produce in move 3.

## Read it as the employer
Swap letters with a partner. Read theirs as a busy employer for thirty seconds, then answer: would you open the CV under it? Say why, naming the move that worked or failed.
