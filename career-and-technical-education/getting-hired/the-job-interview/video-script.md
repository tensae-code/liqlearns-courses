# Video script — The Job Interview — Your Conversation with an Employer

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

An interview is a conversation with a purpose.
The employer asks. The candidate answers.
The employer is choosing the right person.
And the candidate is choosing too.

Your CV cannot show everything.
The interview shows how you speak and think.

Seven questions come again and again.
Tell us about yourself.
Why should we hire you?
What are your weaknesses?

Answer with four steps.
Know the job description.
Give specific examples.
Handle the follow-ups.
Tie every answer to the job.

And at the end — thank the interviewer.

Now you. Answer question one, by the steps.
