# Hands-on — The Job Interview — Your Conversation with an Employer

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Rehearse a real interview
With a partner as interviewer, run a real mock interview: the interviewer asks three of the common seven; you answer each by the four steps. Swap roles. Afterwards the interviewer names your strongest answer and your weakest, with reasons.

## Prepare your seven
Write your own answer to each of the common seven questions for a job you actually want — each answer containing one specific example from your real experience.
