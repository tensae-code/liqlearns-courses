# The Job Interview — Your Conversation with an Employer — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In a job interview, the interviewer is —
a) the candidate  b) the employer questioning the candidate to select the right person  c) a doctor
**Answer: b) the employer questioning the candidate to select the right person** — employer questions; candidate answers.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** An employer interviews to learn what a CV cannot show —
a) your handwriting  b) the knowledge, skills and qualities you show in person — how you speak, think and carry yourself  c) your family's income
**Answer: b) the knowledge, skills and qualities you show in person — how you speak, think and carry yourself** — the live conversation reveals what paper cannot.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is one of the common seven questions?
a) 'What is your mother's name?'  b) 'Why should we hire you?'  c) 'Can you pay a fee?'
**Answer: b) 'Why should we hire you?'** — it is question 4 of the common seven.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Step 2 of the answering method is —
a) give specific examples — past experience in concrete actions  b) memorise a speech  c) avoid all examples
**Answer: a) give specific examples — past experience in concrete actions** — concrete past actions show future intention.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** At the end of the interview you should —
a) leave silently  b) ask when results will be announced, express your interest, and thank the interviewer  c) demand the salary first
**Answer: b) ask when results will be announced, express your interest, and thank the interviewer** — those are the book's three closing moves.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** 'What are your weaknesses?' is —
a) not a real interview question  b) one of the common seven questions  c) a question to refuse
**Answer: b) one of the common seven questions** — it is the seventh common question — answer it by the four steps.
