# The Job Interview — Your Conversation with an Employer — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** List the four steps of the answering method.
**Answer:** Know the job description; give specific examples; handle follow-up questions; tie every answer to the job and the organisation.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name three of the common seven questions.
**Answer:** Any three of: tell us about yourself; your greatest strengths; how did you hear about this job; why should we hire you; why do you want this job; what can you do for us that others cannot; your weaknesses.
