# Video script — Your CV — Writing Your Achievements for an Employer

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

An employer cannot see inside you.
So you send a document. Your CV.
Curriculum vitae — the story of your achievements.

It carries three things.
Who you are, and how to reach you.
Your education and qualifications.
Your experience and achievements.

Look at the sample.
Skills from home count — gardening, poultry, helping.
Hobbies go in. And a reference who knows you.

Then go deeper.
Stay up to date — keep growing.
And fit your CV to the job.
Match its requirements with your true strengths.
Never invent. Just show the match clearly.

Now you. Write your first CV.
