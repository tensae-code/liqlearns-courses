# Hands-on — Your CV — Writing Your Achievements for an Employer

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Write your real CV
Using the sample's shape, write your own complete CV on one page: personal information, skill highlights, education, hobbies, and a reference who has agreed to be named.

## Interview a worker, build their CV
Interview a real worker at a local work site about their work and achievements, and build that person's CV from what they tell you — the Grade 7 unit's own assignment.
