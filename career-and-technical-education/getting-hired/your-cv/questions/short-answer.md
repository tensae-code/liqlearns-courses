# Your CV — Writing Your Achievements for an Employer — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three things a CV carries.
**Answer:** Personal information; qualifications and education; work experience and other achievements.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A job advert asks for poultry experience and reliability. Your CV lists both, buried at the bottom. What does Tip 2 tell you to do?
**Answer:** Reorder and word the CV in line with the job's requirements so the matching achievements — poultry experience and reliability — are easy for the employer to see.
