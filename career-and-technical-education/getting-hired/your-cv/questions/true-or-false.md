# Your CV — Writing Your Achievements for an Employer — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Skill highlights in the sample CV include gardening and poultry practised at home.
**Answer: True** — home and community skills are its listed highlights.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Tailoring a CV allows you to claim experience you do not have.
**Answer: False** — tailoring orders and words true achievements to match the job; inventing is not tailoring.
