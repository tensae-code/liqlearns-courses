# Lesson plan — Your CV — Writing Your Achievements for an Employer

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** Getting Hired · **Lesson:** Your CV — Writing Your Achievements for an Employer
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 9 — Getting Hired (CURRICULUM.md / TITLES.md ladder).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a CV is the detailed document of your achievements that an employer reads — and, deeper, a strong CV is tailored: rewritten to match the requirements of the one job you are applying for.
**This lesson's own level:** Sub lesson 1 is the Grade 7 book's CV and its printed sample; sub lesson 2 is the Grade 8 book's deeper move — stay up to date, and fit the CV to the job description.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what a CV is and what its three parts carry.
2. Lay out a simple CV in the sample's shape, with a reference.
3. Explain Tip 1 — what 'be up to date' shows an employer.
4. Tailor a CV to a job description without inventing anything.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. A real or practice job advertisement helps for sub lesson 2.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the learner does the opening task in lesson/lesson.md, before any explanation.
2. **The explanation** — the sub lessons in order (lesson/lesson.md).

## If the learner is stuck
- Says 'I have no experience' → return to the sample: gardening, poultry and family support at home are its skill highlights. Honest home and community work is experience; unpaid is not unreal.
