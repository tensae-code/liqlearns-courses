# Lesson plan — Getting Hired — Soft Skills, Contacts, and Thinking Like the Employer

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** Getting Hired · **Lesson:** Getting Hired — Soft Skills, Contacts, and Thinking Like the Employer
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 9 — Getting Hired (CURRICULUM.md / TITLES.md ladder).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the Grade 8 book's remaining hiring tips: grow the soft skills employers now prize, build a network of contacts, think in benefits the employer gains, use social media wisely for vacancies, and prepare smartly.
**This lesson's own level:** It stands on the CV lesson, which carried Tips 1–2; this lesson carries Tips 3–7 of the same Grade 8 sequence, at the same deeper level.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an
image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture
of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does
with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and
while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is
dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the
student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph,
or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be
shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say how a pupil develops soft skills (Tip 3).
2. Say what a professional network does for a career (Tip 4).
3. Tell a benefit from a feature, in the employer's sense (Tip 5).
4. Name where vacancies now appear (Tip 6) and what to research before an interview (Tip 7).

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. A phone with social media helps for Tip 6, if you have one.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the learner does the opening task in lesson/lesson.md, before any explanation.
2. **The explanation** — the sub lessons in order (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE: a picture in three panels — an employer reading an application, a phone showing vacancy posts, a candidate reading about a company before an interview — the student matches each panel to its tip.
   - ADD AUDIO: a voice reading Tips 3–7 as a checklist — the student marks each tip done, started, or not yet.
3. **Say it to your companion** — in lesson/lesson.md.
   - ADD VIDEO: the script in video-script.md, filmed — told in the super-simple voice (made later, when videos are made).
4. **Questions** — from questions/: multiple-choice, true-or-false, short-answer — served at the learner's level and teaching-mix (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** — the doing block at the end of lesson/lesson.md.

## If the learner is stuck
- Confuses benefit and feature → a feature is about you ('I am reliable'); a benefit is what the employer gains ('your shop opens on time every day because I am reliable'). Translate every feature into its benefit.
