# Getting Hired — Soft Skills, Contacts, and Thinking Like the Employer — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A pupil who lacks soft skills develops them by —
a) waiting  b) engaging in volunteering and community projects  c) avoiding group work
**Answer: b) engaging in volunteering and community projects** — volunteering and community projects are the book's route.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Tip 4 says relationships with people who might be your next employers —
a) do not matter  b) take a career in the right direction  c) should be avoided
**Answer: b) take a career in the right direction** — networks develop a career and reach goals.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In Tip 5, a 'benefit' is —
a) your personal strength  b) what the employer gains by hiring you  c) your hobby
**Answer: b) what the employer gains by hiring you** — benefits are the employer's gains; features are your strengths.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Before an interview, Tip 7 says to research the employer's —
a) products and services, working environment and areas of business operation  b) owner's family  c) competitors' secrets
**Answer: a) products and services, working environment and areas of business operation** — research the target employer thoroughly on those three.

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** Tip 3 names which soft skills as more critical than ever?
a) Typing and filing  b) Teamwork and time management  c) Singing and drawing
**Answer: b) Teamwork and time management** — those two are named in Tip 3.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Tip 6 says job announcements and hiring have moved onto —
a) only printed boards  b) social media  c) only radio
**Answer: b) social media** — social media has become a source of vacancies.
