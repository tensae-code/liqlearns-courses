Five more tips for getting hired.
Tip three — grow your soft skills.
Teamwork. Time management. Employers prize them.
Grow them in volunteering and community work.
Tip four — use your contacts.
Introduce yourself. Learn from experts.
Networks carry careers.
Tip five — think like the employer.
Your strengths are features.
What the employer gains — those are benefits.
Tip six — vacancies live on social media now.
Look there, wisely.
Tip seven — prepare smartly.
Research the employer before the interview.

Now you. Which tip will you act on first?
