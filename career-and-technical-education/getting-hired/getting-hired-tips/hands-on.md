# Hands-on — Getting Hired — Soft Skills, Contacts, and Thinking Like the Employer

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Build your contact list
Write the names of five people or organisations who could guide you toward work — a practitioner, a teacher, an organisation, an expert. Beside each, write how you will introduce yourself.

## Vacancy hunt, wisely
Find three real current vacancies through social media or notice boards. For each, write the job, the employer, and the requirements — and one thing you researched about that employer.
