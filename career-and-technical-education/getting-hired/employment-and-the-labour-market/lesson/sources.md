Book gap named by the placements: Unit 6's learning outcomes promise defining self-employment's forms and distinguishing national vs local markets, but the sole-trader / partnership / private-limited-company forms appear only inside the Unit Summary (printed pp. 151–152), never in the body, and the urban-vs-rural labour-market contrast appears only as a review question. This lesson teaches only what the body teaches — employment's two kinds and the local/national market — and does not invent the missing forms. No teacher-guide placements exist for these units: both teacher guides end inside their Unit 4 (the Grade 7 guide at printed p. 42, the Grade 8 guide at printed p. 57), so this lesson claims no guide evidence and stands on the student book alone.
