# Video script — Employment and the Labour Market

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Employment means work that earns a living.
Productive work. Paid in money, or paid in kind.

You can work for an employer.
The employer pays your salary.
Or you can work for yourself.
Your customers pay you directly.
Working for yourself gives freedom.
You set your hours. You choose the work.
You can even create jobs for others.

Where do workers and employers meet?
In the labour market.
Your town's jobs are your local market.
The whole country's jobs are the national market.

Now you. Name one worker of each kind.
