# Employment and the Labour Market — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Self-employment can create a job for yourself and for others.
**Answer: True** — the book lists creating jobs for yourself and others among its advantages.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The national labour market covers only your own town.
**Answer: False** — the national market covers the whole country; your town is the local market.
