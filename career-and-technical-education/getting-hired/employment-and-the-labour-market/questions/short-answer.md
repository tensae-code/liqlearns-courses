# Employment and the Labour Market — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the two kinds of employment.
**Answer:** Salary/wage employment (working for an employer) and self-employment (working for yourself).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words, what happens in a labour market?
**Answer:** Employers looking for people with knowledge and skills meet workers who are looking for jobs, so each side can find the other.
