# Employment and the Labour Market — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A person producing useful products and receiving income in money or in kind is —
a) on holiday  b) employed  c) retired
**Answer: b) employed** — that is the book's definition of employment.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Working for yourself, earning directly from the people who buy your output, is —
a) salary employment  b) wage employment  c) self-employment
**Answer: c) self-employment** — self-employment pays you from your customers, not from a boss.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Which is an advantage of self-employment named in the lesson?
a) A boss sets your hours  b) Setting your own hours and choosing your work  c) Never needing customers
**Answer: b) Setting your own hours and choosing your work** — freedom and flexibility are self-employment's advantages.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The labour market is —
a) a market that sells workers  b) the mechanism where employers and employees meet for employment  c) a government office
**Answer: b) the mechanism where employers and employees meet for employment** — employers seek skills there; skilled people seek jobs there.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Jobs you can apply for without travelling far — in your own town — are your —
a) national market  b) local labour market  c) foreign market
**Answer: b) local labour market** — local means near enough not to travel far.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is an expanding job area in Ethiopia in the lesson?
a) Flower plantations and sugar factories  b) Moon mining  c) Deep-sea fishing fleets
**Answer: a) Flower plantations and sugar factories** — the book instances plantations, farming, sugar, roads, telecom and electricity.
