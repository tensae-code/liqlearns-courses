# Hands-on — Employment and the Labour Market

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Sort your street
List every working person on your street or in your market that you can name. Beside each, write E (an employer pays them) or S (they employ themselves). Count which kind is bigger near you.

## Map your local labour market
On one page, draw your town. Mark the workplaces a person could apply to without travelling far — farms, shops, factories, offices, construction sites. That map is your local labour market.
