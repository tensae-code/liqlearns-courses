# Video script — Voluntary Work — Helping Freely, Gaining Experience

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Some work is not paid. It is given.
Voluntary work means giving your time.
Giving your energy. Giving your talent.
Freely. Of your own free will.
Young people rebuild an elder's house.
A doctor treats people for free.

And the giver gains too.
New skills. Real experience. Confidence.
New friends. A stronger community.
Employers notice volunteers.
They see commitment. They see loyalty.

Now you. What could you give, freely?
