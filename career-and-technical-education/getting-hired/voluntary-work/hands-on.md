# Hands-on — Voluntary Work — Helping Freely, Gaining Experience

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes.

## Find the volunteers near you
List three voluntary works happening in your locality — who does them, who they help, and what skill each one uses or builds.

## Give two hours
Choose one real voluntary task near you — helping an elder, cleaning a shared place, supporting a community event — and do it for two hours. Afterwards write what you gave and what you gained.
