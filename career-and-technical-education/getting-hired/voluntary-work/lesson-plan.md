# Lesson plan — Voluntary Work — Helping Freely, Gaining Experience

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** Getting Hired · **Lesson:** Voluntary Work — Helping Freely, Gaining Experience
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 9 — Getting Hired (CURRICULUM.md / TITLES.md ladder).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** voluntary work is giving your time, energy and talent of your own free will, free of charge — and it pays you back in experience, confidence and an employer's trust.
**This lesson's own level:** It stands on the employability idea that comes next in this title: voluntary work is one of the ways a learner builds real experience before anyone hires them.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what voluntary work is, in the book's words.
2. Give two of the book's examples of voluntary work.
3. List at least four benefits voluntary work gives back.
4. Say why an employer values a volunteer.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. A community group, elder or organisation near you that could use help.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the learner does the opening task in lesson/lesson.md, before any explanation.
2. **The explanation** — the sub lessons in order (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE: a picture of three volunteer scenes in one strip — a house being repaired, a free clinic check, a home visit — the student names what each volunteer gives: time, energy or talent.
   - ADD AUDIO: a voice reading the benefits list aloud — the student marks the two benefits they want most.
3. **Say it to your companion** — in lesson/lesson.md.
   - ADD VIDEO: the script in video-script.md, filmed — told in the super-simple voice (made later, when videos are made).
4. **Questions** — from questions/: multiple-choice, true-or-false, short-answer — served at the learner's level and teaching-mix (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** — the doing block at the end of lesson/lesson.md.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks unpaid means worthless → walk the benefits list and ask which one a first job would pay money for if it could: experience. Volunteering buys it without a wage.
