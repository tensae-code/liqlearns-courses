# Voluntary Work — Helping Freely, Gaining Experience — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Voluntary work is giving your time, energy and talent —
a) for a high wage  b) of your own free will, free of charge  c) only when ordered
**Answer: b) of your own free will, free of charge** — free will and free of charge are both in the definition.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which is one of the book's examples of voluntary work?
a) A paid shop assistant  b) Young people rebuilding elders' houses  c) A factory owner hiring staff
**Answer: b) Young people rebuilding elders' houses** — rebuilding elders' houses is the book's first example.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is a benefit of voluntary work?
a) A guaranteed salary  b) Real experience useful for employment  c) Skipping school
**Answer: b) Real experience useful for employment** — experience is one of the listed benefits.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Employers read voluntary service as a sign of —
a) laziness  b) commitment, loyalty and desire to contribute  c) needing money
**Answer: b) commitment, loyalty and desire to contribute** — that is how the book says employers read it.

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** Which three things does a volunteer give?
a) Money, rent and transport  b) Time, energy and talent  c) Exams, marks and certificates
**Answer: b) Time, energy and talent** — time, energy and talent are the three gifts named.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Voluntary work can build self-confidence by —
a) applying school learning in real life  b) avoiding all people  c) paying a fee
**Answer: a) applying school learning in real life** — applying what school taught is the book's confidence route.
