# Voluntary Work — Helping Freely, Gaining Experience — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A doctor giving free hospital service is doing voluntary work.
**Answer: True** — it is one of the book's own examples.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Voluntary work gives the volunteer nothing at all.
**Answer: False** — it gives satisfaction, skills, experience, confidence, friends and a part in community development.
