# Voluntary Work — Helping Freely, Gaining Experience — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give two examples of voluntary work from the lesson.
**Answer:** Any two of: rebuilding elders' houses; doctors' free hospital service; lawyers volunteering at legal centres for low-income people; youth supporting HIV patients at home.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name three benefits a volunteer gains.
**Answer:** Any three of: personal satisfaction; using or developing skills; real experience for employment; self-confidence; meeting people and making friends; taking part in community development.
