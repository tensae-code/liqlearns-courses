# Careers — the Path of Your Working Life — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson — a short, single-skill lesson with no sub lessons (his ruling 2026-11-09). Its whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can define a career as the long connected path toward life goals, give the book's four examples, and list local jobs with the job they want and why.

Practice deepens inside the one skill: first naming careers and local jobs; then explaining how jobs connect and advance over time; then judging which local jobs can grow into careers.
