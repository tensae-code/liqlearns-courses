# Hands-on — Careers — the Path of Your Working Life

Real looking, real asking — with your feet and your voice, not only your eyes.

## Walk your locality (in person, with an adult where needed)
List every job you pass on one real street or market row: who does it, where, with what tools. Bring back at least ten jobs. This is field observation — the way this course learns trades: go and look at the real practitioner.

## Ask one worker three questions
Ask a person in a career you admire: What was your first job? What did you learn next? What is your job now? Write their three answers as a rising path, like the picture.

## If it is hard
If walking is hard, do the walk from memory with a companion who knows the street, and ask your three questions to a worker at your own home or school gate.
