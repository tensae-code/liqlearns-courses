# Lesson plan — Careers — the Path of Your Working Life

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** Careers — the Path of Your Working Life
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 1 — First Steps (CURRICULUM.md / TITLES.md ladder).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a career is the long, connected path of jobs, positions and work a person builds toward their life goals.
**This lesson's own level:** It stands on the learner's own world — the jobs they see every day in their locality. It needs nothing else.

## Sub lessons
This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short, single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09).

## Before this lesson
- No lesson is required first. The next lesson, Job vs Career, sharpens the difference this lesson opens.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what a career is, in their own words.
2. Give the book's career examples: director, teacher, doctor, hairstylist.
3. List the jobs in their own locality and name the job they want, and why.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks a career is the same as today's job → hold that thought for the next lesson: one job is one rung; the career is the whole climb.
- Cannot name local jobs → walk the street in the mind: farm, shop, school, clinic, workshop, office.
