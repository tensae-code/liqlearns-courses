# Careers — the Path of Your Working Life

## Do it first

Name the job you want when you grow up. Say why you want it.

Now list the jobs you can see in your own locality — the work people around you do every day. Keep your list. This whole course starts from that list.

## A career is a long path

A **career** is the long path of progress a person follows toward their life goals, across their education and their working life.

It is not one single piece of work. A career is the **connected series of jobs a person holds, positions they earn, and work they accomplish over a long period**. A person chooses a field, and then advances in that field over time.

Working as a **director**, a **teacher**, a **doctor** or a **hairstylist** are careers. Each one is a path: you learn, you start, you grow, you take on more.

[Picture 11801 — a career as a rising path]

Look at the path in the picture: first job, next job, senior role. No one starts at the top of the path. A career is the walking of it, step after step, toward the life you want.
