# Careers — the Path of Your Working Life — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. ** Name two careers named in the lesson.
**Answer: Any two of: director, teacher, doctor, hairstylist.**

**2. ** What two things should you list about your own locality at the start?
**Answer: The job you want and why you want it, and the jobs that exist in your locality.**

**3. ** In your own words, what makes a series of jobs a career?
**Answer: They are connected over a long period, chosen by field, and the person advances in them toward life goals.**
