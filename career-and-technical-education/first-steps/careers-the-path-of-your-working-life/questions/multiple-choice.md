# Careers — the Path of Your Working Life — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** A career is best described as —
a) one day's paid work  b) the long path of progress toward your life goals across education and working life  c) a single school subject
**Answer: b) the long path of progress toward your life goals across education and working life**

**2. ** Which of these is given as an example of a career?
a) Director  b) A one-hour errand  c) A lunch break
**Answer: a) Director**

**3. ** A career is built from —
a) one job only  b) a connected series of jobs, positions and work over a long period  c) luck alone
**Answer: b) a connected series of jobs, positions and work over a long period**

**4. ** A career is advanced by —
a) staying in one place forever  b) choosing a field and advancing in it over time  c) avoiding education
**Answer: b) choosing a field and advancing in it over time**
