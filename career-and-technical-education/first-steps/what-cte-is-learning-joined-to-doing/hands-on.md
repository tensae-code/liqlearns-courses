# Hands-on — What CTE Is — Learning Joined to Doing

Real tools, real trades — hold the thing while you name what it takes to use it well.

## Hold a real tool and split its skill
Take one real tool at home — a hoe, a needle, a hammer, a cooking ladle. List: (a) what you must KNOW to use it well (book hand), (b) what your HANDS must practise (tool hand). Show both lists to an adult who uses that tool and ask what you missed.

## Watch a workplace for ten minutes
Stand where you may watch a real workplace for ten minutes (a workshop, a stall, a salon). Write down every moment you see learning (measuring, reading, counting, checking) inside the doing. Bring back at least three moments.
