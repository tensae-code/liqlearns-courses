# Lesson plan — What CTE Is — Learning Joined to Doing

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** What CTE Is — Learning Joined to Doing
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 1 — First Steps (CURRICULUM.md / TITLES.md ladder).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** CTE joins academic learning to technical doing: specific career skills, workplace competencies, learned in a real-world context.
**This lesson's own level:** It stands on Job vs Career: the learner knows what a career is; now they learn the kind of education built to lead to one.

## Sub lessons
This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short, single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09).

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Job vs Career — One Step or the Whole Climb. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what CTE stands for and what it combines.
2. Name the three workplace competencies: knowledge, skills, attitudes.
3. Say what CTE prepares learners for: further education and employment.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil; one real tool from home (any trade tool) to hold while learning, if one is at hand.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks CTE is only doing, no books → point at the two hands: drop either hand and the join fails; the trades in this course all need measuring, reading and counting.
- Thinks CTE is only for one sector → it prepares learners for employment across economic sectors.
