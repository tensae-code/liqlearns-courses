# What CTE Is — Learning Joined to Doing — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. ** Name the three workplace competencies CTE introduces.
**Answer: Knowledge, skills and attitudes.**

**2. ** In your own words, what does CTE join together?
**Answer: Learning (academic education) joined to practical doing (technical education) — specific career skills learned in a real-world context.**

**3. ** Give one example of a technical skill and the academic learning behind it.
**Answer: Example form: measuring and mixing for a trade (technical doing) rests on number and reading learning (academic). Any pairing that joins a hands-on task skill to its classroom learning is accepted.**
