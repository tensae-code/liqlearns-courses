# What CTE Is — Learning Joined to Doing — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** CTE combines —
a) academic education with technical education  b) sport with music  c) reading with sleeping
**Answer: a) academic education with technical education**

**2. ** Academic education has as its purpose —
a) learning  b) selling  c) building
**Answer: a) learning**

**3. ** Technical education teaches —
a) practical skills for performing a task  b) only theory  c) only history
**Answer: a) practical skills for performing a task**

**4. ** Workplace competencies in CTE are —
a) money, land and tools  b) knowledge, skills and attitudes  c) age, height and strength
**Answer: b) knowledge, skills and attitudes**
