# What CTE Is — Learning Joined to Doing — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. ** CTE prepares learners only for further education, never for employment.
**Answer: False** — it prepares learners for further education AND for employment.

**2. ** CTE skills are taught in a real-world, applied context.
**Answer: True**

**3. ** CTE teaches specific, technology-based, career-oriented skills.
**Answer: True**
