# Lesson plan — Why CTE Matters — Skills That Lead to Work

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** Why CTE Matters — Skills That Lead to Work
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 1 — First Steps (CURRICULUM.md / TITLES.md ladder).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** CTE matters twice over: it builds the country's skilled manpower, and it leads the learner to real work, real problems and further study.
**This lesson's own level:** It stands on What CTE Is: the learner knows the definition; now they learn why it is worth doing.

## Sub lessons
This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short, single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09).

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: What CTE Is — Learning Joined to Doing. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name CTE fields from the book's list.
2. State the national benefit: skilled manpower and socioeconomic development.
3. State the learner benefits: real problems, employment, hands-on experience, a base for higher education.

## If the learner is stuck
- Lists fields but no benefits → separate the two questions: WHERE does CTE work (fields) and WHAT does it do (benefits)?
- Thinks benefit is only a job → add the other arrows: own business, further study, and solving real problems where you live.
