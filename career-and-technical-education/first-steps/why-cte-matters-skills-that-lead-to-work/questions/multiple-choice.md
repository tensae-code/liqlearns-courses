# Why CTE Matters — Skills That Lead to Work — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** Nationally, CTE builds —
a) skilled manpower and socioeconomic development  b) only buildings  c) only exams
**Answer: a) skilled manpower and socioeconomic development**

**2. ** For the learner, CTE connects classroom learning to —
a) solving real problems  b) forgetting school  c) avoiding work
**Answer: a) solving real problems**

**3. ** Which is a CTE field named in the lesson?
a) Pottery  b) Astronomy research only  c) None — CTE has no fields
**Answer: a) Pottery**

**4. ** Hands-on job experience in CTE makes learners —
a) less engaged  b) more engaged workers  c) unable to study further
**Answer: b) more engaged workers**
