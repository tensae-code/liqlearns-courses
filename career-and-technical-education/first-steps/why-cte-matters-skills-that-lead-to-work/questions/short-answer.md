# Why CTE Matters — Skills That Lead to Work — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. ** Name four CTE fields from the lesson's list.
**Answer: Any four of: agriculture, construction, pottery, leather work, metal work, pharmacy, crafts, hotel service, business.**

**2. ** Give two benefits of CTE for the learner (not the country).
**Answer: Any two of: connects classroom learning to solving real problems; opens employment and reduces unemployment; gives hands-on job experience that makes more engaged workers; serves as the base for higher education and future career development.**

**3. ** What does CTE build for the country as a whole?
**Answer: Skilled manpower and socioeconomic development.**
