# Why CTE Matters — Skills That Lead to Work

## Do it first

Look at your locality list once more. Beside each job, write what breaks, runs out, or goes undone in your town if nobody learns that work. That empty space is why this subject exists.

## A wide field

CTE spans many fields of real work: **agriculture, construction, pottery, leather work, metal work, pharmacy, crafts, hotel service and business** — and more. Wherever skilled hands earn a living, CTE is there.

## What it does for the country

Nationally, CTE **builds skilled manpower** — a country full of people who can really do the work — **and socioeconomic development** with it. A country cannot develop faster than its people's skills.

## What it does for you

For the learner, CTE:

- **connects classroom learning to solving real problems** — the lesson meets the street;
- **opens employment and reduces unemployment**;
- **gives hands-on job experience**, and that experience makes **more engaged workers**;
- **serves as the base for higher education and future career development**.
