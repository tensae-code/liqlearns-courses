# Hands-on — Why CTE Matters — Skills That Lead to Work

Real evidence from your own streets — count the work before you claim its value.

## Count the trades on one street
Walk one street or market row (with an adult where needed) and tally how many workplaces belong to the lesson's fields: agriculture-linked, construction, pottery, leather, metal, pharmacy, crafts, hotel/food service, business. Bring back the tally — your locality's CTE map in numbers.

## Find the gap
Ask one shopkeeper or worker: "What skilled work does this area need that nobody here does well?" Write the answer down word for word. That gap is a career opening, in the worker's own words.
