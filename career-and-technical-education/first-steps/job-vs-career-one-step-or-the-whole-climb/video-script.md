# Video script — Job vs Career — One Step or the Whole Climb

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

One rung is a job.
You stand on it now. It pays. Then it ends.

The whole ladder is a career.
Rung after rung, you climb. You learn. You rise.

Health-aid, then nurse — a career.
Clerk, then manager — a career.

Even work with no pay can be a career,
if you walk it for a long time.
