# Job vs Career — One Step or the Whole Climb

## Do it first

Three workers. Decide quickly — is each one a *job* or a *career*?

1. A health-aid who is training and growing, on the way to becoming a nurse.
2. A grade 7 physics teacher, in that post now.
3. A clerk who, over the years, grows into a personnel manager.

Keep your answers. The lesson will test them.

## One rung, or the whole ladder?

A **job** is the paid activity done at a particular time. It can be short-term, or part-time. It is one step.

A **career** is the long-term journey those jobs add up to. It is chosen with passion, with a goal: grow your skills, advance, or move on to the next step. It is the whole climb.

|  | Job | Career |
|---|---|---|
| How long? | At a particular time; may be short-term or part-time | Long-term |
| What is it? | One paid activity | The journey many jobs add up to |
| Chosen how? | Taken for now | Chosen by passion, to grow and advance |

## Test your first answers

1. Health-aid → nurse: a **career** — one growing path over time.
2. A grade 7 physics teacher, in that post: a **job** — the step held at this time (it can sit inside a teaching career).
3. Clerk → personnel manager: a **career** — jobs adding up, with advancement.

One more rule from the book: work does not have to be paid to count toward a career. **Unpaid work, schoolwork, homemaking and volunteering, pursued over time, can also constitute a career.**

## Say it to your companion

Say it in one breath each: "A job is…" and "A career is…" Then give your companion one new example of each, from your own locality list.
