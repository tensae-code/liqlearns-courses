# Job vs Career — One Step or the Whole Climb — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. ** Give one difference between a job and a career from the lesson's table.
**Answer: A job is paid activity at a particular time (possibly short-term or part-time); a career is the long-term, passion-chosen journey those jobs add up to, aimed at growing skills and advancing.**

**2. ** Why is the ladder a good picture for this lesson?
**Answer: One rung is one job; the whole ladder is the career the jobs add up to.**
