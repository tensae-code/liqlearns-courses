# Job vs Career — One Step or the Whole Climb — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** A job is —
a) the paid activity done at a particular time  b) a whole lifetime path  c) a school building
**Answer: a) the paid activity done at a particular time**

**2. ** "Health-aid now, nurse later" as one growing path is —
a) a job only  b) a career  c) neither
**Answer: b) a career** — the worked statements sort growing paths as careers.

**3. ** A career is chosen by —
a) chance only  b) passion, with a goal of growing skills to advance or move on  c) age alone
**Answer: b) passion, with a goal of growing skills to advance or move on**

**4. ** Unpaid work pursued over time, such as volunteering, can be —
a) part of a career  b) never work  c) a job that pays
**Answer: a) part of a career**
