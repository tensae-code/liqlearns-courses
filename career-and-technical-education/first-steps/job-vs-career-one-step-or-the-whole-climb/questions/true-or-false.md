# Job vs Career — One Step or the Whole Climb — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. ** A job can be short-term or part-time.
**Answer: True**

**2. ** A career is only one single paid activity.
**Answer: False** — a career is the long-term journey jobs add up to.

**3. ** Homemaking pursued over time can constitute a career.
**Answer: True**
