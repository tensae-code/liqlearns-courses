# Job vs Career — One Step or the Whole Climb — matching

Match each item on the left with the right item on the right. Write the letter beside the number.

**1. ** A grade 7 physics teacher (one post, now) — a
**2. ** Clerk growing into personnel manager over years — b
**3. ** Health-aid growing into nurse over years — c

Right side (in order a, b, c …): a) a job — one step, done at a particular time; b) a career — the whole climb; c) a career — the whole climb

**Answer key:** 1—a, 2—b, 3—c (each left item matches the right item with its own letter, as listed).
