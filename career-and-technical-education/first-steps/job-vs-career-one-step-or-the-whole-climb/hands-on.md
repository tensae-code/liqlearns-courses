# Hands-on — Job vs Career — One Step or the Whole Climb

Real sorting, with real local work — paper, feet and questions.

## Build a real ladder on paper
Draw a ladder with at least five rungs. On each rung write one real job from your locality, in the order a person might hold them, bottom to top. Label the bottom rung "first job" and the whole ladder with the career name.

## Interview check
Ask one working adult: "Is what you do today your job, or your career — or both?" Write their answer in one sentence and say whether you agree, and why.
