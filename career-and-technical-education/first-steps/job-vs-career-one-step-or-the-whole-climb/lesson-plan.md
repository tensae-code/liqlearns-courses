# Lesson plan — Job vs Career — One Step or the Whole Climb

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** Job vs Career — One Step or the Whole Climb
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 1 — First Steps (CURRICULUM.md / TITLES.md ladder).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a job is one paid step at a particular time; a career is the whole long climb those steps add up to.
**This lesson's own level:** It stands on Careers — the Path of Your Working Life: the learner can already say what a career is; now they separate it from a single job.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Careers — the Path of Your Working Life. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the job/career contrast in their own words.
2. Sort the three worked statements (health-aid → nurse; grade 7 physics teacher; clerk → personnel manager) into career or job.
3. Say that unpaid work, schoolwork, homemaking and volunteering pursued over time can also constitute a career.

## If the learner is stuck
- Calls every paid activity a career → ask: is it one step at one time, or the whole journey? One step = job.
- Thinks unpaid work cannot be a career → the book's own rule: pursued over time, volunteering, homemaking and schoolwork can constitute a career.
