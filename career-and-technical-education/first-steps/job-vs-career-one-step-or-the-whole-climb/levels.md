# Job vs Career — One Step or the Whole Climb — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson — a short, single-skill lesson with no sub lessons (his ruling 2026-11-09). Its whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can define job and career, sort the three worked statements correctly, and state that unpaid work pursued over time can constitute a career.

Practice deepens inside the one skill: first sorting the book's worked statements; then sorting their own locality list; then harder corners — a job that sits inside a career, and unpaid work that is a career.
