# Video script — How This Course Works — Choose, Make, Sell, Get Hired

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This course is a road with four stops.

First: choose. Learn what a career is. Pick yours well.
Next: make. Learn a real trade with your hands — safely, step by step.
Next: sell. Turn the skill into a business. Count profit honestly.
Last: get hired. CV, interview, workplace skills — and a business plan for your own trade.

Learn a trade with your hands — and turn it into work. That is CTE.
