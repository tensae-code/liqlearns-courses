# Hands-on — How This Course Works — Choose, Make, Sell, Get Hired

Real planning on real paper — your own road, drawn from your own locality.

## Draw your road with real names
On one large sheet, draw the four stops. Under Make, write real workplaces from your locality for each trade family — the actual farm, salon, tailor, copy shop, builder and food business you could visit. A road with real names is a plan; a road with none is a poster.

## Prove the first stop
Write your current career shortlist (even if it is three maybes) at the Choose stop, and one observable next step under it with a date — for example, "ask the tailor near school my three questions by Friday". Do that step before you call this lesson finished.
