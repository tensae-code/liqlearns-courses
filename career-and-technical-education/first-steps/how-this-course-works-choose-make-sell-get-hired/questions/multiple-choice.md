# How This Course Works — Choose, Make, Sell, Get Hired — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** The arc of this course, in order, is —
a) choose → make → sell → get hired  b) get hired → choose → sleep  c) sell → choose → make
**Answer: a) choose → make → sell → get hired**

**2. ** Every unit in the books closes with —
a) a song only  b) a unit summary and unit review questions  c) nothing
**Answer: b) a unit summary and unit review questions**

**3. ** The recurring activity pattern in the books sends the learner to —
a) observe the real local practitioner  b) memorise without looking  c) avoid workplaces
**Answer: a) observe the real local practitioner**

**4. ** Inside this course, learners climb by —
a) school calendar only  b) levels — when they are ready and have proved the step  c) age alone
**Answer: b) levels — when they are ready and have proved the step**
