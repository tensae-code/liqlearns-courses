# How This Course Works — Choose, Make, Sell, Get Hired

## Do it first

You are going to learn real trades in this course. Before any trade: guess the order. Must you learn a trade before you choose it? Can you sell a skill you have not learned? Can you plan a business before you can do the work? Argue your order with a companion — then check it against the road below.

## The road

Everything in this course comes from government textbooks — this part from Ethiopia's CTE books. The books walk **one deliberate arc**, and this course keeps it:

**Choose → Make → Sell → Get Hired**

1. **Choose.** First, what a career is, and how to choose one well — that is this title and the next.
2. **Make.** Then hands-on trades you can start yourself: in the Grade 7 book, farm trades, the salon, and the sewing room; in the Grade 8 book, reception and copying, building and retail, and food, cleaning and events. Every trade is taught the same honest way: name the trade, show its tools, give the procedure step by step — then go and observe the real local practitioner.
3. **Sell.** The trades turn into livelihoods: starting a micro or small business, choosing products and markets, and working out profit.
4. **Get hired.** And the employability road runs beside it: recognising opportunities, voluntary work, the employability skills, your CV and cover letter, workplace problems, the job interview — and, at the end of the Grade 8 book, a business plan written for the very trade you chose at its start.

Inside the course you climb by **levels, not grades**: you move up when you are ready and have proved the step, not when a calendar says so. You may also jump in higher — the ladder is recommended, never forced.

## How every unit works

Each book unit works the same way, so you always know where you stand. It **opens with Learning Outcomes and Key Words**, teaches through **numbered local-life Activities** ("list the jobs in your locality", "visit a poultry site", "ask the barber near your school") and **Brainstorming Questions**, and **closes with a Unit Summary and Unit Review Questions**.

Teaching in this course is active (teacher guide — evidence): brainstorm first, work in pairs and small groups, visit real sites, practise for real — and adapt the activity to your own place and your own needs. The teacher facilitates; the learner does.

> **Tip:** The one exception to "everything here comes from the books": a tip in a box like this is from us, the people making LiqLearns. Tips are ours; the lessons are the books'.

## Say it to your companion

Walk your companion down the road: choose, make, sell, get hired — one sentence per stage, naming one real trade or task in each. Then swap: your companion walks it, and you check the order.

1. Draw the road as four stops on one page.
2. Under "Make", write the three Grade 7 trade families (farm, salon, sewing) and the three Grade 8 trade families (reception and copying; building and retail; food, cleaning and events).
3. Put a star on the stop where you stand today, and write what proving that step will look like for you.
