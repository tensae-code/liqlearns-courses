# Lesson plan — How This Course Works — Choose, Make, Sell, Get Hired

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** How This Course Works — Choose, Make, Sell, Get Hired
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 1 — First Steps (CURRICULUM.md / TITLES.md ladder).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** this course walks one arc — choose a career, learn the trade hands-on, sell it or get hired, plan a business on it — and every unit works the same way: outcomes, activities, summary, review.
**This lesson's own level:** It stands on Why CTE Matters: the learner knows the fields and benefits; now they see the whole road before walking it.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an
