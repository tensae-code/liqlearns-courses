# Career and Technical Education course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

CTE is the vocational course: real, sellable, hands-on trades
plus the career skills around them. The first stocked part is
built from Ethiopia's CTE books for Grades 7 and 8, and the
books' own arc is the path: **choose a career → learn the skill
properly and safely → be employable → plan a business on it.**

**First Steps → Choosing a Career → Farm Trades → Salon Trades →
Garment and Cloth → Reception and Copying → Building and Retail
→ Food, Cleaning and Events → Getting Hired → Starting a
Business**

That ladder IS the path order (recommended, never forced — a
learner can jump in higher; the "start here instead" path points
down this ladder). Inside the course the organisation is by
**levels, not grades**: both school years' lessons sit inside
the one title at rising depth wherever a title spans both books
(as in Choosing a Career and Getting Hired).

## The titles

- `first-steps/` — what CTE is, why it matters, and how this
  course works (choose → make → sell → get hired).
- `choosing-a-career/` — career vs job, the areas of CTE,
  choosing well: research, evaluate, discuss, decide; soft
  skills, creative thinking, problem solving, your personal
  profile.
- `farm-trades/` — gardening, dairy, cattle fattening, poultry,
  beekeeping — and starting a micro or small business on them.
- `salon-trades/` — hair care for men and women, nail care, skin
  care, and the salon's hygiene line. (Teacher evidence covers
  only the unit's opening — the Grade 7 guide is truncated.)
- `garment-and-cloth/` — garment making, the sewing machine and
  the tailor's tools, colouring cloth, needle crafts.
- `reception-and-copying/` — self-employment, the receptionist's
  skills, Ethiopian hospitality, the photocopier trade, and
  running an enterprise activity.
- `building-and-retail/` — construction, carpentry, painting,
  tyre-pressure checking, workplace safety, and retail business.
- `food-cleaning-and-events/` — hospitality and events, food
  preparation and Ethiopian cultural foods, customer service,
  and cleaning services.
- `getting-hired/` — employment and the labour market,
  voluntary work, employability, your CV and cover letter,
  workplace problems, and the job interview.
- `starting-a-business/` — entrepreneurship: the idea, the
  kinds of entrepreneurs, the business plan, and saving.
