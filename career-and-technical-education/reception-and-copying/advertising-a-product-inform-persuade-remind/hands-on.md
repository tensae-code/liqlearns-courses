# Hands-on — Advertising a product — inform, persuade, remind

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes. Machine work follows the book's own steps exactly, with a teacher or practitioner present as the books direct.

## One product, three adverts
For your product, draft three short adverts: one that informs (what it is), one that persuades (why choose it), one that reminds (still here, still good). Read them aloud.

## Site visit report (teacher guide — evidence)
Visit a local small enterprise selling a product. Note how it advertises — signs, speech, social media — and write a visit report.
