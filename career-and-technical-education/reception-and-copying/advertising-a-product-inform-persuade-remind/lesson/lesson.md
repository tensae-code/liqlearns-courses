Say what you already think, out loud or on paper. If parts will not come yet, that is exactly what this lesson is for.

## Sub lesson 1 — What advertising is and its three objectives

**Product advertising** is paid promotional communication meant to induce purchase, through television, radio, print media, websites, social media and billboards.

Its three objectives are:

1. **Inform** — creating awareness of brands, products, services and ideas.
2. **Persuade**.
3. **Remind**.

Selling skills — the "muscles" of sales professionals — are built by experience, coaching and training, and refined across a whole career.
