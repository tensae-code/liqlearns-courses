# Advertising a product — inform, persuade, remind — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Product advertising is —
a) free news reporting  b) a product's price tag  c) paid promotional communication meant to induce purchase
**Answer: c) paid promotional communication meant to induce purchase.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Advertising travels through —
a) only shop signs  b) television, radio, print, websites, social media and billboards  c) only word of mouth
**Answer: b) television, radio, print, websites, social media and billboards.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The three objectives are —
a) inform, persuade, remind  b) buy, sell, count  c) make, pack, deliver
**Answer: a) inform, persuade, remind.**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** To inform means —
a) reminding old customers only  b) lowering the price  c) creating awareness of brands, products, services and ideas
**Answer: c) creating awareness of brands, products, services and ideas.**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** Selling skills are built by —
a) one lesson  b) experience, coaching and training  c) birth only
**Answer: b) experience, coaching and training.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** (teacher guide — evidence) Pupils learn advertising by —
a) visiting a local small enterprise selling a product, and writing a visit report  b) staying in class only  c) watching television only
**Answer: a) visiting a local small enterprise selling a product, and writing a visit report.**
