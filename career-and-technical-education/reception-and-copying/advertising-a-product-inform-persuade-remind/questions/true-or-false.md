# Advertising a product — inform, persuade, remind — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** (teacher guide — evidence) Pupils learn advertising by visiting a local small enterprise selling a product, and writing a visit report.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Product advertising is free news reporting.
**Answer: False** — paid promotional communication meant to induce purchase.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** To inform means creating awareness of brands, products, services and ideas.
**Answer: True.**
