# Hands-on — Looking after a photocopier — cleaning it properly

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes. Machine work follows the book's own steps exactly, with a teacher or practitioner present as the books direct.

## Cleaning drill
With a machine switched off (or a model): moisten a soft cotton cloth 70/30, wring it so nothing drips, wipe the exterior and trays away from the entry areas. Check: no paper towels, no spraying.

## Visit and report (teacher guide — evidence)
Visit an office to observe a copier at work and write a report — what was copied, how it was cleaned, what you noticed.
