# Looking after a photocopier — cleaning it properly

## Do it first

Before any explanation, try this in your own words: clean a photocopier with a soft cotton cloth moistened with 70% isopropyl alcohol and 30% water, wiping away from entry areas — never spray liquid onto the machine.

Say what you already think, out loud or on paper. If parts will not come yet, that is exactly what this lesson is for.

## Sub lesson 1 — How to clean it

Regular cleaning keeps the machine working:

- Light wiping of the exterior.
- Regular wiping of the bypass and exit trays.
- Always wipe **away from entry areas**, so dirt does not fall into the paper path.
- The cleaning cloth must be a **clean, soft, dust-free cotton cloth, moistened** — no free liquid, no dripping — with **70% isopropyl alcohol and 30% water**.
- **Never** fibrous paper towels.
- **Never** spray liquid directly onto the machine.

## Sub lesson 2 — (teacher guide — evidence) Visits, inclusion and the no-machine fallback

The guide's office site visit to watch a copier work carries an explicit inclusion instruction — make the possible facilities for students with disabilities — followed by a written report from every pupil. Where a maintenance shop exists locally, pupils watch a photocopier being maintained there. Where no photocopier exists in the locality at all, the fallback is to show a picture or a video of one.

## Say it to your companion

Say the main points back without looking: clean a photocopier with a soft cotton cloth moistened with 70% isopropyl alcohol and 30% water, wiping away from entry areas — never spray liquid onto the machine. Then name the steps or parts in order.

## Do It Yourself

1. **Cleaning drill.** With a machine switched off (or a model): moisten a soft cotton cloth 70/30, wring it so nothing drips, wipe the exterior and trays away from the entry areas. Check: no paper towels, no spraying.
2. **Visit and report (teacher guide — evidence).** Visit an office to observe a copier at work and write a report — what was copied, how it was cleaned, what you noticed.
