# Video script — Looking after a photocopier — cleaning it properly

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Soft cotton cloth. Seventy alcohol, thirty water. No dripping.
Wipe away from the openings.
Never paper towels. Never spray the machine.
