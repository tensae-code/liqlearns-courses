# Looking after a photocopier — cleaning it properly — fill in the blank

Fill in the blank with the right words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The cleaning cloth should be moistened with ______.
**Answer: 70% isopropyl alcohol and 30% water.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** (teacher guide — evidence) If no photocopier exists locally, the fallback is ______.
**Answer: show a picture or a video of one.**
