# Looking after a photocopier — cleaning it properly — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Wipe trays —
a) towards the entry slot  b) with a dry paper towel  c) away from entry areas, so dirt does not fall into the paper path
**Answer: c) away from entry areas, so dirt does not fall into the paper path.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The cleaning cloth should be moistened with —
a) oil  b) 70% isopropyl alcohol and 30% water  c) pure water, dripping wet
**Answer: b) 70% isopropyl alcohol and 30% water.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Never use for cleaning —
a) fibrous paper towels  b) a soft cotton cloth  c) a dust-free cloth
**Answer: a) fibrous paper towels.**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Liquid must never be —
a) put on the cloth first  b) mixed 70/30  c) sprayed directly onto the machine
**Answer: c) sprayed directly onto the machine.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** (teacher guide — evidence) If no photocopier exists locally, the fallback is —
a) buy one for the class  b) show a picture or a video of one  c) cancel the lesson forever
**Answer: b) show a picture or a video of one.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** (teacher guide — evidence) After the office visit, every pupil produces —
a) a written report  b) a copy of a document  c) a repair bill
**Answer: a) a written report.**
