# Looking after a photocopier — cleaning it properly — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** (teacher guide — evidence) If no photocopier exists locally, the fallback is show a picture or a video of one.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Wipe trays towards the entry slot.
**Answer: False** — away from entry areas, so dirt does not fall into the paper path.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Liquid must never be sprayed directly onto the machine.
**Answer: True.**
