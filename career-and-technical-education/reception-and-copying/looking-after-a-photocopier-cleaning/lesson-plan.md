# Lesson plan — Looking after a photocopier — cleaning it properly

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** Reception and Copying · **Lesson:** Looking after a photocopier — cleaning it properly
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 6 — Reception and Copying (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** clean a photocopier with a soft cotton cloth moistened with 70% isopropyl alcohol and 30% water, wiping away from entry areas — never spray liquid onto the machine.
**This lesson's own level:** It stands on Using a Photocopier — the Six Steps; now keeping that machine working.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Answer: Wipe trays away from entry areas, so dirt does not fall into the paper path.
2. Answer: The cleaning cloth should be moistened with 70% isopropyl alcohol and 30% water.
3. Answer: Never use for cleaning fibrous paper towels.
4. Answer: Liquid must never be sprayed directly onto the machine.
5. Answer: (teacher guide — evidence) If no photocopier exists locally, the fallback is show a picture or a video of one.
