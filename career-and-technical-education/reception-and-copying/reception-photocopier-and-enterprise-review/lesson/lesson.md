## Sub lesson 1 — What this review covers

This is the unit's assessment set, as the book files it:

- True/False on reception-desk manning and the professional welcome.
- Fill-ins on the traditional view of marketing and promotion.
- Multiple choice covering copier problems and servicing, the term "promotion" for marketing communications, receptionist-interview preparation, and what makes a good receptionist.
- Written answers on diagnosing copier problems, the steps of using a photocopier, and the steps of product selection.

Work it after the lessons of this title. The questions in questions/ are built from this title's lessons, in the review's own spirit.
