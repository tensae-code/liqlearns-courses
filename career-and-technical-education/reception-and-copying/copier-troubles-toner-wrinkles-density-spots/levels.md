# Copier troubles — toner, wrinkles, density and spots — sub lessons and depth

This is the depth record of the lesson, re-expressed as sub lessons. A learner passes a sub lesson by its questions (the gate pool is that sub lesson's whole question set — see lesson-plan.md), then moves on; earlier sub lessons are revisited in mixed practice.

## Sub lesson 1 — Toner and wrinkles
Pass mark: the learner can answer this sub lesson's questions from the lesson — in particular: replacing the cartridge; worn feed/exit rollers, worn trays, humidity or fuser assemblies.

## Sub lesson 2 — Density and spots
Pass mark: the learner can answer this sub lesson's questions from the lesson — in particular: disturbed density controls — reset the density levels; smudges or debris on the mirror or glass; a defect in the drum — replace the drum.
