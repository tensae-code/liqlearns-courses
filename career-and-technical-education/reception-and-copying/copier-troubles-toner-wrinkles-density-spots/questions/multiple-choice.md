# Copier troubles — toner, wrinkles, density and spots — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Cartridge problems are resolved by —
a) adding water  b) wiping the glass  c) replacing the cartridge
**Answer: c) replacing the cartridge.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Wrinkled pages can trace to —
a) a new cartridge  b) worn feed/exit rollers, worn trays, humidity or fuser assemblies  c) a full paper tray
**Answer: b) worn feed/exit rollers, worn trays, humidity or fuser assemblies.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Copies too light or too dark usually mean —
a) disturbed density controls — reset the density levels  b) the drum must be replaced  c) the paper is wrong
**Answer: a) disturbed density controls — reset the density levels.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Dots in the same place on every copy point to —
a) a drum defect  b) low toner  c) smudges or debris on the mirror or glass
**Answer: c) smudges or debris on the mirror or glass.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Random spots throughout point to —
a) humidity  b) a defect in the drum — replace the drum  c) dirty glass
**Answer: b) a defect in the drum — replace the drum.**
