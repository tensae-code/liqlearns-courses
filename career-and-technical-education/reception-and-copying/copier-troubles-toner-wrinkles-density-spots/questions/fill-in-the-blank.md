# Copier troubles — toner, wrinkles, density and spots — fill in the blank

Fill in the blank with the right words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Wrinkled pages can trace to ______.
**Answer: worn feed/exit rollers, worn trays, humidity or fuser assemblies.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Random spots throughout point to ______.
**Answer: a defect in the drum — replace the drum.**
