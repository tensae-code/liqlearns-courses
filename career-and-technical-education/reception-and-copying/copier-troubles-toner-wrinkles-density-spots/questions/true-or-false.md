# Copier troubles — toner, wrinkles, density and spots — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Cartridge problems are resolved by replacing the cartridge.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Cartridge problems are resolved by adding water.
**Answer: False** — replacing the cartridge.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Dots in the same place on every copy point to smudges or debris on the mirror or glass.
**Answer: True.**
