# Video script — Copier troubles — toner, wrinkles, density and spots

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Faded copies? The cartridge. Replace it.
Wrinkled? Rollers, trays, damp, fuser.
Too light, too dark? Reset the density.
Same-place spots: clean the glass. Random spots: the drum.
