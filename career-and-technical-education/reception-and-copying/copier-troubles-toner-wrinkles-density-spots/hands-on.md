# Hands-on — Copier troubles — toner, wrinkles, density and spots

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes. Machine work follows the book's own steps exactly, with a teacher or practitioner present as the books direct.

## Fault-matching drill
Lay out four faulty copies — faded (toner), wrinkled, too dark, spotty in the same place. For each: name the cause and the fix from the lesson, in order.

## Density reset drill
On a copier or its diagram, find the density control and practise returning it to its middle setting — the fix for light/dark copies.
