This is the depth record of the lesson, re-expressed as sub lessons. A learner passes a sub lesson by its questions (the gate pool is that sub lesson's whole question set — see lesson-plan.md), then moves on; earlier sub lessons are revisited in mixed practice.

## Sub lesson 1 — Paper jams
Pass mark: the learner can answer this sub lesson's questions from the lesson — in particular: paper jam; wrong paper size or bad loading, and paper dust; removing the sheet, reloading the right size aligned, wiping feed tires and vacuuming dust.

## Sub lesson 2 — Lines on paper
