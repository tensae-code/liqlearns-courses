# Copier troubles — paper jams and lines on the copy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The most common copier error is —
a) spots  b) wrinkles  c) paper jam
**Answer: c) paper jam.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Jams come from —
a) cold weather only  b) wrong paper size or bad loading, and paper dust  c) too much toner only
**Answer: b) wrong paper size or bad loading, and paper dust.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The jam fix includes —
a) removing the sheet, reloading the right size aligned, wiping feed tires and vacuuming dust  b) tearing the sheet out in pieces  c) adding more paper on top
**Answer: a) removing the sheet, reloading the right size aligned, wiping feed tires and vacuuming dust.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Large vertical lines point to —
a) dirty hands  b) low paper  c) a drum-blade fault
**Answer: c) a drum-blade fault.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Lines can also come from —
a) old documents  b) foreign substances on scanner glass or mirrors  c) a noisy room
**Answer: b) foreign substances on scanner glass or mirrors.**
