# Lesson plan — Copier troubles — paper jams and lines on the copy

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** Reception and Copying · **Lesson:** Copier troubles — paper jams and lines on the copy
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 6 — Reception and Copying (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** paper jams come from wrong size or bad loading — remove, reload aligned, clean — and lines on copies come from dirty glass or mirrors, drum, developer or fuser faults.
**This lesson's own level:** It stands on Looking After a Photocopier; now diagnosing its two most visible faults.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Answer: The most common copier error is paper jam.
2. Answer: Jams come from wrong paper size or bad loading, and paper dust.
3. Answer: The jam fix includes removing the sheet, reloading the right size aligned, wiping feed tires and vacuuming dust.
4. Answer: Large vertical lines point to a drum-blade fault.
5. Answer: Lines can also come from foreign substances on scanner glass or mirrors.
