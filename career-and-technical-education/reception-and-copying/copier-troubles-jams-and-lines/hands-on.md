# Hands-on — Copier troubles — paper jams and lines on the copy

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes. Machine work follows the book's own steps exactly, with a teacher or practitioner present as the books direct.

## Jam drill
On a switched-off machine or model: find the jammed sheet, draw it out slowly in one steady pull — never torn. Reload the right size, aligned. Say the dust-cleaning steps.

## Diagnose the line
You are given three faulty copies: one with a thick vertical line, one with a thin line, one clean but from dirty glass (line repeats in the same place). Match each to its likely cause from the lesson.
