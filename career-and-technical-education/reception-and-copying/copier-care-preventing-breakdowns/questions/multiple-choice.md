# Copier care — the habits that prevent breakdowns — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Prevention includes —
a) using any damp paper  b) never cleaning the glass  c) using better paper and loading it correctly
**Answer: c) using better paper and loading it correctly.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Waste toner should be —
a) poured on paper  b) emptied  c) left forever
**Answer: b) emptied.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Part of prevention is knowing —
a) when to check with a technician  b) how to ignore faults  c) how to hide breakdowns
**Answer: a) when to check with a technician.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Daily care includes —
a) spraying water inside  b) using fibrous towels  c) cleaning the copier glass regularly
**Answer: c) cleaning the copier glass regularly.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Dust and debris must be kept from —
a) the customer's documents  b) the ink cartridge head  c) the office desk only
**Answer: b) the ink cartridge head.**
