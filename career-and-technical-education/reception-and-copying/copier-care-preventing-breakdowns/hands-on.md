# Hands-on — Copier care — the habits that prevent breakdowns

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes. Machine work follows the book's own steps exactly, with a teacher or practitioner present as the books direct.

## Make the wall chart
Write the seven prevention habits as a checklist and pin it (on paper) beside a copier or its picture. Tick each habit as you perform it once.

## Office observation
Visit a real office, observe a copier at work, and report which prevention habits you saw in use — and which you did not.
