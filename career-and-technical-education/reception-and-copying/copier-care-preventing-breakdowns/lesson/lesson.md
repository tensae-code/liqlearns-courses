# Copier care — the habits that prevent breakdowns

## Do it first

Before any explanation, try this in your own words: prevention habits — shut off, warm up, clean glass, better paper, correct loading, empty waste toner, know when to call a technician — plus daily care.

Say what you already think, out loud or on paper. If parts will not come yet, that is exactly what this lesson is for.

## Sub lesson 1 — Prevention habits

- Shut the copier off properly.
- Warm it up.
- Clean the glass.
- Use better paper.
- Load paper correctly.
- Empty waste toner.
- Know when to check with a technician.

## Sub lesson 2 — Daily care

- Use quality products.
- Clean the copier glass regularly.
- Keep dust and debris from the ink cartridge head.
- Clear dust from the machine.

The unit caps this with a visit to a real office to observe a copier at work and report.

## Say it to your companion

Say the main points back without looking: prevention habits — shut off, warm up, clean glass, better paper, correct loading, empty waste toner, know when to call a technician — plus daily care. Then name the steps or parts in order.

## Do It Yourself

1. **Make the wall chart.** Write the seven prevention habits as a checklist and pin it (on paper) beside a copier or its picture. Tick each habit as you perform it once.
2. **Office observation.** Visit a real office, observe a copier at work, and report which prevention habits you saw in use — and which you did not.
