# Choosing your market — the target market — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson — a short, single-skill lesson with no sub lessons (his ruling 2026-11-09). Its whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state and use what the lesson teaches — a target market is one part of the total market whose members share buying geography, buying power, demographics and incomes — categorised by age, location, income and lifestyle.

Practice deepens inside the one skill: first naming the parts or factors, then applying them to a new example, then judging a worked choice against all of them at once.
