# Hands-on — Choosing your market — the target market

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes. Machine work follows the book's own steps exactly, with a teacher or practitioner present as the books direct.

## Ring your target
Describe a busy market scene for your product. Ring (in words) one group — its age, location, income and lifestyle — and say why that group, not everyone, is your target.

## Reach test
For your ringed group, name one place and one time you can actually reach them with your selling. If you cannot name one, the target is too wide.
