## The target market

A **target market** is one part of the total market whose members share buying geography, buying power, demographics and incomes.

Target markets are generally categorised by **age, location, income and lifestyle**.

Defining one lets a business aim its sales and marketing efforts at the customers it can actually reach and connect with.

## Do It Yourself

1. **Ring your target.** Describe a busy market scene for your product. Ring (in words) one group — its age, location, income and lifestyle — and say why that group, not everyone, is your target.
2. **Reach test.** For your ringed group, name one place and one time you can actually reach them with your selling. If you cannot name one, the target is too wide.
