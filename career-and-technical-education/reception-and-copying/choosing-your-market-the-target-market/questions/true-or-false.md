# Choosing your market — the target market — true or false

Say true or false.

**1.** Members of a target market share buying geography, buying power, demographics and incomes.
**Answer: True.**

**2.** Target markets are categorised by colour and height.
**Answer: False** — age, location, income and lifestyle.

**3.** One way target markets are categorised is by lifestyle.
**Answer: True.**
