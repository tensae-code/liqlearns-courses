# Choosing your market — the target market — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Members of a target market share —
a) the same surname  b) the same school  c) buying geography, buying power, demographics and incomes
**Answer: c) buying geography, buying power, demographics and incomes.**

**2.** One way target markets are categorised is by —
a) luck  b) lifestyle  c) height
**Answer: b) lifestyle.**

**3.** A target market is —
a) one part of the total market sharing buying geography, power, demographics and incomes  b) everyone in the country  c) only other businesses
**Answer: a) one part of the total market sharing buying geography, power, demographics and incomes.**

**4.** Target markets are categorised by —
a) colour and height  b) luck  c) age, location, income and lifestyle
**Answer: c) age, location, income and lifestyle.**

**5.** Defining a target market lets a business —
a) raise prices always  b) aim sales and marketing at customers it can reach and connect with  c) sell to nobody in particular
**Answer: b) aim sales and marketing at customers it can reach and connect with.**
