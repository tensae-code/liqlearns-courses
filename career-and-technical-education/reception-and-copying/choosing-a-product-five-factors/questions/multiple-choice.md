# Choosing a product — demand, funding, materials, return, people — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The five factors are —
a) price, colour, weather and luck  b) demand only  c) demand, funding, raw materials, return and qualified personnel
**Answer: c) demand, funding, raw materials, return and qualified personnel.**

**2.** Reducing wastage keeps —
a) demand low  b) manufacturing cost minimal  c) funding high
**Answer: b) manufacturing cost minimal.**

**3.** The product with the greatest chance of success is —
a) the most-needed product  b) the rarest product  c) the most expensive product
**Answer: a) the most-needed product.**

**4.** Funding must cover —
a) only the first day  b) only raw materials  c) development, production, promotion, marketing and distribution
**Answer: c) development, production, promotion, marketing and distribution.**

**5.** For raw materials, consider —
a) only colour  b) source, quality to be achieved, and quantity needed  c) only price
**Answer: b) source, quality to be achieved, and quantity needed.**

**6.** Qualified personnel help by —
a) reducing wastage, keeping manufacturing cost minimal  b) increasing wastage  c) avoiding marketing
**Answer: a) reducing wastage, keeping manufacturing cost minimal.**

**7.** One of the five factors is —
a) the seller's mood  b) the weather  c) optimum return on investment
**Answer: c) optimum return on investment.**
