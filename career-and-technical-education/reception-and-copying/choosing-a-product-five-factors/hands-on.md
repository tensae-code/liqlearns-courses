# Hands-on — Choosing a product — demand, funding, materials, return, people

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes. Machine work follows the book's own steps exactly, with a teacher or practitioner present as the books direct.

## Score a product on five scales
Take one product idea. Score it 1–5 on each factor: demand, funding, materials, return, people. A low score names the work to do before starting.

## Cut the wastage
List three places your chosen product could waste materials. For each, name the skilled action that prevents it — competent manpower is factor 5 in action.
