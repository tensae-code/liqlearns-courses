# Choosing a product — demand, funding, materials, return, people — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson — a short, single-skill lesson with no sub lessons (his ruling 2026-11-09). Its whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can state and use what the lesson teaches — five factors govern product selection: frequency of demand, adequate funding, raw materials, optimum return on investment, and qualified personnel.

Practice deepens inside the one skill: first naming the parts or factors, then applying them to a new example, then judging a worked choice against all of them at once.
