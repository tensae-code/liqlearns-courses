# Video script — Choosing a product — demand, funding, materials, return, people

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Is it needed often? That is demand.
Is there funding? Are the materials there?
Will it return more than it costs?
And do skilled people run it? Five factors. Check them all.
