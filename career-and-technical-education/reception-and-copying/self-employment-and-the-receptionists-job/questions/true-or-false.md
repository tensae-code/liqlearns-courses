# Self-employment — and the receptionist's job — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Self-employment means working for yourself, earning directly from buyers.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Self-employment means working for an employer for a wage.
**Answer: False** — working for yourself, earning directly from buyers.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is a receptionist's task? setting appointments and sorting mail.
**Answer: True.**
