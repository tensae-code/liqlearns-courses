# Self-employment — and the receptionist's job — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Self-employment means —
a) working for an employer for a wage  b) working without earning  c) working for yourself, earning directly from buyers
**Answer: c) working for yourself, earning directly from buyers.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A self-employed person's income comes directly from —
a) the government only  b) the people who buy their output  c) an employer's payroll only
**Answer: b) the people who buy their output.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A receptionist is also called a —
a) front desk clerk  b) photocopier  c) housekeeper
**Answer: a) front desk clerk.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is a receptionist's task?
a) cooking meals  b) repairing tyres  c) setting appointments and sorting mail
**Answer: c) setting appointments and sorting mail.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** In Ethiopia, the reception career is tied mostly to —
a) mining  b) hotel services  c) farming
**Answer: b) hotel services.**
