# Video script — Self-employment — and the receptionist's job

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Work for yourself — that is self-employment.
Your income comes from the people who buy your work.
At the front desk, the receptionist greets, answers, files, directs.
In Ethiopia, that desk is mostly in a hotel.
