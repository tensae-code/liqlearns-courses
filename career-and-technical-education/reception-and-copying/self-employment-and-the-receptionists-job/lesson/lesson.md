# Self-employment — and the receptionist's job

## Do it first

Before any explanation, try this in your own words: self-employment is working for yourself, earning directly from buyers — and a receptionist is the front-desk worker who sets appointments, files, answers and directs.

Say what you already think, out loud or on paper. If parts will not come yet, that is exactly what this lesson is for.

## Sub lesson 1 — Self-employment

**Self-employment** is working for yourself — generating income directly from the people who buy your output — rather than for an employer paying a wage.

## Sub lesson 2 — The receptionist's job

A **receptionist (front desk clerk)** holds an office/administrative support post:

- Setting appointments
- Filing
- Record keeping
- Answering inquiries and calls
- Directing visitors
- Sorting mail

In Ethiopia the reception career is tied mostly to **hotel services**.

## Say it to your companion

Say the main points back without looking: self-employment is working for yourself, earning directly from buyers — and a receptionist is the front-desk worker who sets appointments, files, answers and directs. Then name the steps or parts in order.

## Do It Yourself

1. **Run a front desk for ten minutes.** Set a table as a front desk: appointment book, file, mail tray, phone (or a phone-shaped object). A partner arrives as a visitor: greet them, answer an inquiry, direct them, and record the appointment.
2. **List the desk's jobs.** Walk to a real reception desk if one is near (school office, hotel). List every task you see done there and compare with the lesson's six.
