# Lesson plan — Self-employment — and the receptionist's job

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** Reception and Copying · **Lesson:** Self-employment — and the receptionist's job
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 6 — Reception and Copying (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** self-employment is working for yourself, earning directly from buyers — and a receptionist is the front-desk worker who sets appointments, files, answers and directs.
**This lesson's own level:** It stands on nothing but offices, hotels and the learner's own world — the first lesson of this title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Answer: Self-employment means working for yourself, earning directly from buyers.
2. Answer: A self-employed person's income comes directly from the people who buy their output.
3. Answer: A receptionist is also called a front desk clerk.
4. Answer: Which is a receptionist's task? setting appointments and sorting mail.
5. Answer: In Ethiopia, the reception career is tied mostly to hotel services.
