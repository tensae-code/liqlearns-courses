# Hands-on — Self-employment — and the receptionist's job

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes. Machine work follows the book's own steps exactly, with a teacher or practitioner present as the books direct.

## Run a front desk for ten minutes
Set a table as a front desk: appointment book, file, mail tray, phone (or a phone-shaped object). A partner arrives as a visitor: greet them, answer an inquiry, direct them, and record the appointment.

## List the desk's jobs
Walk to a real reception desk if one is near (school office, hotel). List every task you see done there and compare with the lesson's six.
