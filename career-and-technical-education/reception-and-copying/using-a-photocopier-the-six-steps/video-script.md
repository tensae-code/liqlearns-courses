# Video script — Using a photocopier — the six steps

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Power on. If it sleeps, press copy to wake it.
Document face-down on the glass. Close the cover.
Set the copies. Set the colour. Choose the paper.
Press copy — and out it slides.
