# Using a photocopier — the six steps

## Do it first

Before any explanation, try this in your own words: the six steps of using a photocopier, in order: power on, document face-down on the glass, set copies, set colour, select paper size, press copy.

Say what you already think, out loud or on paper. If parts will not come yet, that is exactly what this lesson is for.

## Sub lesson 1 — The six steps

1. **Plug in and switch on** at the power button (face or side of the machine). If the machine is on but silent, it is in sleep mode — press "copy" to wake it.
2. **Lift the cover, lay the document face-down on the glass**, aligned to the guide marks, and close the cover.
3. **Set the number of copies** on the digital display with the plus/minus keys.
4. **Set colour preference** through menu → settings.
5. **Select paper size** — standard is 8.5 × 11 inches; special sizes go in the appropriate tray.
6. **Press the copy (start) button.**

## Sub lesson 2 — (teacher guide — evidence) Assessed as a demonstration

The guide prepares this lesson with a field visit to watch a shop copying documents. Assessment then puts pupils in small groups to show how to load paper and make a single copy, make multiple copies, name a problem that can happen with a photocopier, and say how to fix it.

## Say it to your companion

Say the main points back without looking: the six steps of using a photocopier, in order: power on, document face-down on the glass, set copies, set colour, select paper size, press copy. Then name the steps or parts in order.

## Do It Yourself

1. **Copying drill — single and multiple.** On a real copier (or a drawn one, step by step aloud): load paper, place a document face-down, make one copy. Reset and make three copies. Say each of the six steps as you do it.
2. **Wake the machine.** Find a copier in sleep mode: wake it with the copy button, as the book directs, without switching it off and on.
