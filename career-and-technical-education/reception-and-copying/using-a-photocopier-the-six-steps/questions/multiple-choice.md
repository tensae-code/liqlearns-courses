# Using a photocopier — the six steps — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Step 1 is —
a) press copy  b) set the number of copies  c) plug in and switch on at the power button
**Answer: c) plug in and switch on at the power button.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A machine that is on but silent is —
a) empty of toner  b) in sleep mode — press "copy" to wake it  c) broken
**Answer: b) in sleep mode — press "copy" to wake it.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The document is laid —
a) face-down on the glass, aligned to the guide marks  b) face-up on top of the cover  c) in the paper tray
**Answer: a) face-down on the glass, aligned to the guide marks.**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The number of copies is set —
a) on the power button  b) in the menu only  c) on the digital display with plus/minus keys
**Answer: c) on the digital display with plus/minus keys.**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** Standard paper size is —
a) 5 × 5 inches  b) 8.5 × 11 inches  c) A0
**Answer: b) 8.5 × 11 inches.**

**6. (Sub lesson 1)** The final step is —
a) press the copy (start) button  b) lift the cover  c) switch off
**Answer: a) press the copy (start) button.**

**7. (Sub lesson 2)** (teacher guide — evidence) The assessment asks groups to —
a) write an essay only  b) repair the machine  c) demonstrate making single and multiple copies
**Answer: c) demonstrate making single and multiple copies.**

**8. (Sub lesson 2)** (teacher guide — evidence) Before the demonstration, pupils prepare by —
a) buying a copier  b) a field visit to watch a shop copying documents  c) memorising a list
**Answer: b) a field visit to watch a shop copying documents.**
