# Hands-on — Using a photocopier — the six steps

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes. Machine work follows the book's own steps exactly, with a teacher or practitioner present as the books direct.

## Copying drill — single and multiple
On a real copier (or a drawn one, step by step aloud): load paper, place a document face-down, make one copy. Reset and make three copies. Say each of the six steps as you do it.

## Wake the machine
Find a copier in sleep mode: wake it with the copy button, as the book directs, without switching it off and on.
