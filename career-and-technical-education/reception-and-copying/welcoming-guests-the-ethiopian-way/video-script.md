# Video script — Welcoming guests the Ethiopian way — greetings and the coffee ceremony

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Welcome the guest warmly. Shake hands.
The guest sits first — the host stands.
Ask after family, after work, after life.
Then coffee, poured from the jebena. Tea, if coffee is declined.
