# Welcoming guests the Ethiopian way — greetings and the coffee ceremony — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In Ethiopian hospitality, hosts —
a) sit first and let guests stand  b) never greet guests  c) do not sit before their guests
**Answer: c) do not sit before their guests.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The proper conversation opening asks after —
a) politics only  b) family, children, work and life  c) the guest's money
**Answer: b) family, children, work and life.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Guests should read these customs as —
a) respect inside a conservative, modest culture  b) a demand for payment  c) a formality with no meaning
**Answer: a) respect inside a conservative, modest culture.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The core cultural custom of gathering is —
a) the tea dance  b) a market day  c) the coffee ceremony
**Answer: c) the coffee ceremony.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** If coffee is politely declined, the host serves —
a) water only  b) tea  c) nothing
**Answer: b) tea.**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** (teacher guide — evidence) Pupils learn the greetings by —
a) practising them in pairs  b) reading only  c) watching one video
**Answer: a) practising them in pairs.**

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** (teacher guide — evidence) Pupils observe, on their way to school —
a) a photocopier  b) a tyre shop  c) a traditional coffee ceremony
**Answer: c) a traditional coffee ceremony.**
