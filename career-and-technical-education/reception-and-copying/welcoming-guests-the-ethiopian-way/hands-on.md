# Hands-on — Welcoming guests the Ethiopian way — greetings and the coffee ceremony

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes. Machine work follows the book's own steps exactly, with a teacher or practitioner present as the books direct.

## Practise the greeting in pairs (teacher guide — evidence)
Pair up and practise: warm greeting, handshake, guest seated first, then ask after family, work and life. Start from the greeting methods of your own village and compare.

## Observe a coffee ceremony
Watch a traditional coffee ceremony (at home or on the way to school). Note the order of serving, who is served first, and how the pour is done. Report to the class.
