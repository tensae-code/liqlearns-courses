# Lesson plan — Welcoming guests the Ethiopian way — greetings and the coffee ceremony

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** Reception and Copying · **Lesson:** Welcoming guests the Ethiopian way — greetings and the coffee ceremony
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 6 — Reception and Copying (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Ethiopian hospitality: warm greetings and handshakes, guests given priority, hosts not sitting before guests, and the coffee ceremony at the centre.
**This lesson's own level:** It stands on the reception-skills lessons; now the Ethiopian way those skills live in.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Answer: In Ethiopian hospitality, hosts do not sit before their guests.
2. Answer: The proper conversation opening asks after family, children, work and life.
3. Answer: Guests should read these customs as respect inside a conservative, modest culture.
4. Answer: The core cultural custom of gathering is the coffee ceremony.
5. Answer: If coffee is politely declined, the host serves tea.
