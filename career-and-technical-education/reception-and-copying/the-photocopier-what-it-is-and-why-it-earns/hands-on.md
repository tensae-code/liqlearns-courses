# Hands-on — The photocopier — what it is and why it earns money

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes. Machine work follows the book's own steps exactly, with a teacher or practitioner present as the books direct.

## Watch a copy being made
Visit a copy shop or office: watch one document copied start to finish. Note where the original goes, face up or down, and where the copy comes out.

## Count the customers
Stand (with permission) near a copy service for fifteen minutes and count customers and pages. That count is the business case in numbers.
