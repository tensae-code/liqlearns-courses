Say what you already think, out loud or on paper. If parts will not come yet, that is exactly what this lesson is for.

## Sub lesson 1 — What a photocopier is

A **photocopy** is a copy of printed material made by forming an image with light, usually on an electrically charged surface.

The photocopier is one of the most critical machines in an office. An estimated five in ten people have never handled one.

## Sub lesson 2 — Why it is a business

Across much of the country, running a photocopy service is itself a young people's small-business opportunity — easy to start and repairable when damaged — alongside office secretaries doing copying as part of their work.

## Do It Yourself

1. **Watch a copy being made.** Visit a copy shop or office: watch one document copied start to finish. Note where the original goes, face up or down, and where the copy comes out.
2. **Count the customers.** Stand (with permission) near a copy service for fifteen minutes and count customers and pages. That count is the business case in numbers.
