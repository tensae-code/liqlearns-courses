# The photocopier — what it is and why it earns money — sub lessons and depth

This is the depth record of the lesson, re-expressed as sub lessons. A learner passes a sub lesson by its questions (the gate pool is that sub lesson's whole question set — see lesson-plan.md), then moves on; earlier sub lessons are revisited in mixed practice.

## Sub lesson 1 — What a photocopier is
Pass mark: the learner can answer this sub lesson's questions from the lesson — in particular: light, usually on an electrically charged surface; one of the most critical machines in an office; five in ten.

## Sub lesson 2 — Why it is a business
Pass mark: the learner can answer this sub lesson's questions from the lesson — in particular: a young people's small-business opportunity, easy to start and repairable when damaged; office secretaries as part of their work.
