# The photocopier — what it is and why it earns money — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A photocopy is made by forming an image with —
a) ink pens by hand  b) heat alone  c) light, usually on an electrically charged surface
**Answer: c) light, usually on an electrically charged surface.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The photocopier is described as —
a) a toy  b) one of the most critical machines in an office  c) rarely useful
**Answer: b) one of the most critical machines in an office.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** About how many people have never handled a photocopier?
a) five in ten  b) one in ten  c) nine in ten
**Answer: a) five in ten.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Running a photocopy service is —
a) impossible for young people  b) only for large companies  c) a young people's small-business opportunity, easy to start and repairable when damaged
**Answer: c) a young people's small-business opportunity, easy to start and repairable when damaged.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Alongside copy shops, copying is also done by —
a) only farmers  b) office secretaries as part of their work  c) only teachers
**Answer: b) office secretaries as part of their work.**
