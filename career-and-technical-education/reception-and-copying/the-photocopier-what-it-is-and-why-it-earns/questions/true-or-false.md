# The photocopier — what it is and why it earns money — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Running a photocopy service is a young people's small-business opportunity, easy to start and repairable when damaged.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A photocopy is made by forming an image with ink pens by hand.
**Answer: False** — light, usually on an electrically charged surface.
