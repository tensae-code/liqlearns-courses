# Hands-on — Reception skills — people skills, pressure, and office computers

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes. Machine work follows the book's own steps exactly, with a teacher or practitioner present as the books direct.

## Take criticism gracefully
In pairs: one gives a small, fair criticism of a desk task ("the file was in the wrong tray"), the other receives it with a nod, a thank-you, and a correction — no argument. Swap.

## Pressure queue drill
Five visitors arrive at once with different needs. Decide the order you will serve them and say why — that decision, made calmly, is working under pressure.
