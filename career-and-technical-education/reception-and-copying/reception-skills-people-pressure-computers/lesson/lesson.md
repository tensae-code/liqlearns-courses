# Reception skills — people skills, pressure, and office computers

## Do it first

Before any explanation, try this in your own words: three more receptionist skills: interpersonal skills, working under pressure, and basic computer knowledge.

Say what you already think, out loud or on paper. If parts will not come yet, that is exactly what this lesson is for.

## Sub lesson 1 — Interpersonal skills

Interpersonal skill goes beyond communication: collaborating easily, giving and receiving criticism gracefully, staying above petty office politics. Friendliness and likability are named as core to the role.

## Sub lesson 2 — Pressure and computers

**Working under pressure** means handling resource, time, difficulty and surprise constraints outside your control.

**Basic computer knowledge** means email, Word and Excel, plus operating photocopiers and phone systems.

## Say it to your companion

Say the main points back without looking: three more receptionist skills: interpersonal skills, working under pressure, and basic computer knowledge. Then name the steps or parts in order.

## Do It Yourself

1. **Take criticism gracefully.** In pairs: one gives a small, fair criticism of a desk task ("the file was in the wrong tray"), the other receives it with a nod, a thank-you, and a correction — no argument. Swap.
2. **Pressure queue drill.** Five visitors arrive at once with different needs. Decide the order you will serve them and say why — that decision, made calmly, is working under pressure.
