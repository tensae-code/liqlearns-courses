# Reception skills — people skills, pressure, and office computers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Interpersonal skill includes —
a) joining petty office politics  b) avoiding all collaboration  c) giving and receiving criticism gracefully
**Answer: c) giving and receiving criticism gracefully.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Named as core to the receptionist's role are —
a) speed only  b) friendliness and likability  c) silence and distance
**Answer: b) friendliness and likability.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Working under pressure means handling constraints of —
a) resource, time, difficulty and surprise  b) only weather  c) only money
**Answer: a) resource, time, difficulty and surprise.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Basic computer knowledge for a receptionist includes —
a) only games  b) only drawing  c) email, Word and Excel
**Answer: c) email, Word and Excel.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A receptionist should also be able to operate —
a) farm machinery  b) photocopiers and phone systems  c) heavy lorries
**Answer: b) photocopiers and phone systems.**
