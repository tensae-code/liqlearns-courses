# Lesson plan — Reception skills — communicating, serving, juggling, welcoming

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** Reception and Copying · **Lesson:** Reception skills — communicating, serving, juggling, welcoming
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 6 — Reception and Copying (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** four basic receptionist skills: clear communication, customer service, multitasking and prioritising, and welcoming body language.
**This lesson's own level:** It stands on Self-Employment — and the Receptionist's Job; now the skills the desk demands.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.
