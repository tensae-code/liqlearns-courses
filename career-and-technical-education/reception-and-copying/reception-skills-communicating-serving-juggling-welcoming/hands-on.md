# Hands-on — Reception skills — communicating, serving, juggling, welcoming

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes. Machine work follows the book's own steps exactly, with a teacher or practitioner present as the books direct.

## The juggle drill
Three partners at once: one phones (speaking), one walks in, one holds a question on paper. Practise: greet the walker-in with a smile and raised hand, ask the caller politely to hold, answer in priority order. Swap roles.

## Group discussion (teacher guide — evidence)
In a group, discuss: why does making visitors feel welcome matter? List three questions a receptionist may ask a visitor. Show three pieces of welcoming body language.
