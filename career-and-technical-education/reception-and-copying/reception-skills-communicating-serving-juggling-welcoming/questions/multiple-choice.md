# Reception skills — communicating, serving, juggling, welcoming — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Clear written and verbal communication makes the workday —
a) longer for no reason  b) impossible  c) productive
**Answer: c) productive.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Customer service means being —
a) fast and rude  b) pleasant, accommodating and attentive  c) silent and distant
**Answer: b) pleasant, accommodating and attentive.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Sometimes the whole job is —
a) a smile and the right answer  b) a long form  c) a locked door
**Answer: a) a smile and the right answer.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Multitasking at the desk means juggling —
a) only paperwork  b) only cleaning  c) holds, several phone conversations and the person who just walked in
**Answer: c) holds, several phone conversations and the person who just walked in.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Welcoming body language includes —
a) crossed arms and silence  b) smiles, nods and handshakes  c) turning away
**Answer: b) smiles, nods and handshakes.**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** (teacher guide — evidence) In the guide's assessment, the teacher —
a) observes the group discussion and records performance  b) does all the talking  c) leaves the room
**Answer: a) observes the group discussion and records performance.**

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** (teacher guide — evidence) The welcome procedure's quality must be —
a) different every hour  b) kept secret from other staff  c) consistent
**Answer: c) consistent.**
