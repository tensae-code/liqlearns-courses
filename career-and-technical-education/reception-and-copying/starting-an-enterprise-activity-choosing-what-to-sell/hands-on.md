# Hands-on — Starting an enterprise activity — choosing what to sell

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes. Machine work follows the book's own steps exactly, with a teacher or practitioner present as the books direct.

## Five-pupil product debate (teacher guide — evidence)
In a group of five: choose a product, say how it will be sold, list the resources needed, select the target market, choose advertising ways and places. Present to the class for appreciation and discussion.

## First-contact test
Hold up your chosen product (or its drawing). A partner, seeing it for the first time, says what it is, who it is for, and why they would buy — the product is the first contact, so it must speak clearly.
