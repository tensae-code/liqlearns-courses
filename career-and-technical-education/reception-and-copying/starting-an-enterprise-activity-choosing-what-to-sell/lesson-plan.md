# Lesson plan — Starting an enterprise activity — choosing what to sell

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** Reception and Copying · **Lesson:** Starting an enterprise activity — choosing what to sell
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 6 — Reception and Copying (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** an enterprise activity raises money, group or individual — and choosing the appropriate product or service is the building block of every venture.
**This lesson's own level:** It stands on the reception and copier trades; now turning a skill into an enterprise.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Answer: An enterprise activity is an activity that raises money.
2. Answer: An example of a group enterprise activity is baking cakes or washing cars.
3. Answer: Choosing the product is called the building block of every venture.
4. Answer: The product is the business's most important and visible first contact with buyers.
5. Answer: Product selection must stay current because customers' needs change faster than ever.
