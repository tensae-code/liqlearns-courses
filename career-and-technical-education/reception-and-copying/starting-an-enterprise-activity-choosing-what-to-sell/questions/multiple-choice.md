# Starting an enterprise activity — choosing what to sell — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An enterprise activity is —
a) an activity that costs money for fun  b) a school exam  c) an activity that raises money
**Answer: c) an activity that raises money.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** An example of a group enterprise activity is —
a) reading a book  b) baking cakes or washing cars  c) making cards alone
**Answer: b) baking cakes or washing cars.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Choosing the product is called —
a) the building block of every venture  b) an optional extra  c) the last step
**Answer: a) the building block of every venture.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The product is the business's —
a) least important part  b) secret  c) most important and visible first contact with buyers
**Answer: c) most important and visible first contact with buyers.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Product selection must stay current because —
a) trends do not matter  b) customers' needs change faster than ever  c) customers never change
**Answer: b) customers' needs change faster than ever.**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** (teacher guide — evidence) Start-up resources include —
a) financial, human, educational, physical and emotional resources  b) only money  c) only buildings
**Answer: a) financial, human, educational, physical and emotional resources.**

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** (teacher guide — evidence) The debate groups have —
a) two pupils  b) the whole school  c) five pupils
**Answer: c) five pupils.**
