# Video script — Poultry — choosing the best birds and eggs

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on
list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Choose well, or feed badly all year.
The hen: does she sit well? Does she guard her chicks?
The grower: fast — keep. Slow, small, late — cull.
The egg: average size. Sound shell. No cracks.
Broad at the bottom, sharp at the tip.
The chick: strong body, straight legs, bright eyes,
keen to eat and drink.
Six checks. Use them all.
