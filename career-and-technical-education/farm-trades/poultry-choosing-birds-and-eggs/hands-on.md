# Hands-on — Poultry — choosing the best birds and eggs

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes. Real trade practice, done safely and exactly as
the book teaches it.

## Egg judging practice

With a keeper's eggs, practise the selection: average size by comparison, shell checked by eye
and gentle touch, shape checked — broad bottom, sharp tip. Rejected eggs go back to the eating
tray, as the keeper directs.

## Chick check

Where day-old chicks are available, observe (without handling beyond the keeper's direction)
condition, legs, eyes, and keenness to eat and drink, and record the pass/fail pattern.
