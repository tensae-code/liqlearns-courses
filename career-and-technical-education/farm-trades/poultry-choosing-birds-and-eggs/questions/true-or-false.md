# Poultry — choosing the best birds and eggs — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Disease resistance is bred by keeping birds that survive outbreaks.**
**Answer: True** — breed from the survivors.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Surplus cocks should all be kept for breeding.**
**Answer: False** — surplus cocks are culled at slaughter age.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) A cracked egg can still be chosen for hatching.**
**Answer: False** — sound shell, no cracks, is the rule.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) A chick keen to eat and drink passes that part of the quality check.**
**Answer: True** — keenness to eat and drink is a named sign.
