# Poultry — choosing the best birds and eggs — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) List the six selection criteria.**
**Answer:** Good hatching ability, good mothering, high growth rate, egg selection for
hatching, day-old chick quality, disease resistance.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Describe the egg you would choose for hatching.**
**Answer:** Average size, sound shell with no deformity or cracks, broader bottom with a
sharper tip.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Which growers are kept, and which culled?**
**Answer:** Fast growers are kept; poor growers, dwarfs, late maturers and surplus cocks are
culled at slaughter age.
