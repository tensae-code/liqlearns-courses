# Poultry — choosing the best birds and eggs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A hen with good hatching ability —**
a) lays the biggest eggs  b) incubates well  c) crows loudly
**Answer: b) incubates well** — incubating well is the criterion.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Good mothering in a hen means —**
a) protecting chicks and escaping predators  b) laying daily  c) being largest
**Answer: a) protecting chicks and escaping predators** — protection of chicks is mothering.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Birds to cull at slaughter age include —**
a) fast growers  b) poor growers, dwarfs, late maturers and surplus cocks  c) good mothers
**Answer: b) poor growers, dwarfs, late maturers and surplus cocks** — those four are culled;
fast growers are kept.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) A hatching egg should have —**
a) any size, any shell  b) average size and a sound shell with no deformity or cracks  c) a
cracked shell
**Answer: b) average size and a sound shell with no deformity or cracks** — average size, sound
shell, no deformity or cracks.

**5. (Sub lesson 2) The shape of a good hatching egg is —**
a) round like a ball  b) broader bottom with a sharper tip  c) long and thin
**Answer: b) broader bottom with a sharper tip** — broad bottom, sharp tip.

**6. (Sub lesson 2) A good day-old chick is —**
a) sleepy and unwilling to eat  b) in good body condition, no leg or eye deformity, keen to eat
and drink  c) the smallest in the box
**Answer: b) in good body condition, no leg or eye deformity, keen to eat and drink** —
condition, sound legs/eyes, and keenness define it.
