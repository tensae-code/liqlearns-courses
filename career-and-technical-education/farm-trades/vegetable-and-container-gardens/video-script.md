# Video script — Gardening — vegetable gardens and container gardens

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on
list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

No field? No problem.
First, the ground garden: choose the site. Plan. Prepare the soil.
Choose seeds. Plant. Nurture. Harvest.
Cabbage. Carrots. Potatoes — to eat, to share, to sell.
Now the container garden: a bottle, a pot, a barrel.
Carrots and tomatoes grow deep — give them a big container.
But remember: containers dry fast. Water them often.
