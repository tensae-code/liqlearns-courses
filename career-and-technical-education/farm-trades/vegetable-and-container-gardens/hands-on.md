# Hands-on — Gardening — vegetable gardens and container gardens

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes. Real trade practice, done safely and exactly as
the book teaches it.

## Start a container

Cut and fill a bottle, pot or barrel with soil, following your teacher's or family's way of
preparing it. Plant one vegetable from the book's container list and label it with its name and
the date.

## School garden group work

In groups, garden the school compound or cultivate named vegetables — cabbage, carrots,
potatoes are the guide's examples — at home or in the school compound (teacher guide —
evidence, placement 10).
