# Lesson plan — Gardening — vegetable gardens and container gardens

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** Farm Trades · **Lesson:** Gardening —
vegetable gardens and container gardens
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Farm Trades (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a vegetable garden runs the full chain from choosing the
site to harvest, and a container garden grows deep-rooted vegetables in bottles, pots and
barrels where there is no ground.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the Gardening lesson before it — the learner knows
what a garden is; now the two ways to have one.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below,
the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written
as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**.
The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books;
on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as
the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its
source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead,

2. **The container garden** — A container garden grows deep-rooted vegetables in bottles, pots,

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Gardening — growing plants as a business. Recommended, never forced.
- The other Farm Trades lessons stand next door in this same title.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Run the vegetable-garden chain in order: site, plan, soil, seeds, plant, nurture, harvest.
2. Say how a home garden supplements a family's food, income and health care.
3. Name deep-rooted vegetables that grow in containers, and say why bigger containers succeed
more easily.
4. Say why containers need more frequent watering, and what drip irrigation on a timer does.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. A bottle, pot or barrel and some soil for the hands-on.

## If the learner is stuck
- Forgets the chain's order → walk it as a story: you cannot nurture what you have not planted,
and you cannot plant before the soil is ready.
- Waters containers like a field → containers hold little soil and dry fast; frequent watering
is the rule.
