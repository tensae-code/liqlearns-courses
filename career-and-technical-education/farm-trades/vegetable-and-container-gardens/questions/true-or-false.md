# Gardening — vegetable gardens and container gardens — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) The vegetable-garden chain ends at planting.**
**Answer: False** — it runs on through nurturing to harvest.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) A vegetable garden can be in a raised bed.**
**Answer: True** — in the ground or in a raised bed.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Tomatoes and carrots can grow in containers.**
**Answer: True** — both are on the book's container list.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Container gardens never need watering.**
**Answer: False** — they need more frequent watering.
