# Gardening — vegetable gardens and container gardens — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is the first step of the vegetable-garden chain?**
a) Harvest  b) Choose the site  c) Sell the crop
**Answer: b) Choose the site** — the chain begins with choosing the site.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Which vegetables does the book name from a vegetable garden?**
a) Cabbage, carrots and potatoes  b) Only flowers  c) Rice and coffee only
**Answer: a) Cabbage, carrots and potatoes** — those three are named.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) A home garden supplements a family's —**
a) food, income and health care  b) debts  c) school fees
**Answer: a) food, income and health care** — that is what it supplements, on little money,
space or talent.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Which of these can be a container for a container garden?**
a) A bottle, pot or barrel  b) A shoe only  c) A book
**Answer: a) A bottle, pot or barrel** — bottles, pots, barrels and bought planters are named.

**5. (Sub lesson 2) Why do bigger containers succeed more easily?**
a) They hold more soil and room for deep roots  b) They are heavier to steal  c) They need no
water
**Answer: a) They hold more soil and room for deep roots** — deep-rooted vegetables need room;
bigger containers give it.

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Containers need —**
a) less watering than ground beds  b) more frequent watering  c) no watering at all
**Answer: b) more frequent watering** — containers dry faster; drip irrigation on a timer is
ideal.
