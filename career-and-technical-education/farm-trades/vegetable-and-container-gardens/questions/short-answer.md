# Gardening — vegetable gardens and container gardens — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Write the vegetable-garden chain in order.**
**Answer:** Choose the site, plan, prepare the soil, choose seeds and plants, plant, nurture to
harvest.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Name four deep-rooted vegetables the book lists for containers.**
**Answer:** Any four of: beans, beets, carrots, cucumbers, garlic, lettuce, peas, peppers,
potatoes, spinach, squash, tomatoes.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Why is drip irrigation on a timer good for containers?**
**Answer:** Because containers need water more frequently, and the timer delivers it regularly
without forgetting.
