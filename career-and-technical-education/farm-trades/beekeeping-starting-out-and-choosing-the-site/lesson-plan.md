# Lesson plan — Beekeeping — starting out and choosing the site

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** Farm Trades · **Lesson:** Beekeeping —
starting out and choosing the site
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Farm Trades (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** four start-up steps — establish the site, obtain
colonies, prepare equipment, materials and tools, clear and fence the apiary — and site
selection, balancing the bees' needs against the sites available, decides success.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the colony lesson — the bees are known; now housing
them as a keeper.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below,
the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written
as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**.
The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books;
on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as
the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its
source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead,
each opening when the one before it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub
Lessons (ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **The four start-up steps** — Four start-up steps: (1) establish the beekeeping site, (2)
obtain colonies of honeybees, (3) prepare the basic equipment, materials and tools, and (4)
clear and fence the apiary site.
2. **Choosing the site** — Site selection decides success: productive beekeeping needs a
suitable area located and evaluated — balancing the bees' needs against the sites available —
to save time, energy and money.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so
the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Beekeeping — the colony: queen, drones and workers. Recommended, never
forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Recite the four start-up steps.
2. Say why site selection decides success: a suitable area located and evaluated saves time,
energy and money.
3. Tell traditional from modern hives in the book's picture.

## If the learner is stuck
- Wants bees before a site → the steps are a gate order: no colonies until their ground is
established.
- Judges a site by looks → judge by the balance: bees' needs against what the site offers.
