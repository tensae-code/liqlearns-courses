# Video script — Beekeeping — starting out and choosing the site

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on
list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Start right, or struggle always.
One: choose the site — the bees' home ground.
Two: get your colonies.
Three: prepare your equipment and tools.
Four: clear the ground. Fence it.
The site decides almost everything.
Choose it like the bees would: food near, water near,
people safe, keeper able to come and go.
Time, energy, money — a good site saves all three.
