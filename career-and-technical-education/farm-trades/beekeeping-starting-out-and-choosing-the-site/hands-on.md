# Hands-on — Beekeeping — starting out and choosing the site

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes. Real trade practice, done safely and exactly as
the book teaches it.

## Apiary site evaluation

Evaluate a real candidate site with a local beekeeper: forage, water, shade, distance from
houses and paths. Record the verdict against the lesson's balance.

## Fence and clear plan

Plan the clearing and fencing of an apiary corner on paper: where the fence runs, where hives
stand, where the keeper enters.
