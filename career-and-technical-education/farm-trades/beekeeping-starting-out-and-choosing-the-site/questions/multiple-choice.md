# Beekeeping — starting out and choosing the site — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Step 1 of starting beekeeping is —**
a) buy honey jars  b) establish the beekeeping site  c) sell the first honey
**Answer: b) establish the beekeeping site** — the site comes first.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Step 2 is —**
a) obtain colonies of honeybees  b) clear the site  c) build a shop
**Answer: a) obtain colonies of honeybees** — colonies are obtained second.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) The last of the four steps is —**
a) prepare equipment  b) clear and fence the apiary site  c) choose the site
**Answer: b) clear and fence the apiary site** — clearing and fencing closes the four.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Site selection is said to decide —**
a) the price of honey  b) success — a suitable area located and evaluated saves time, energy
and money  c) the bees' colour
**Answer: b) success — a suitable area located and evaluated saves time, energy and money** — a
good site saves time, energy and money.

**5. (Sub lesson 2) Choosing a site balances —**
a) the bees' needs against the sites available  b) two kinds of honey  c) buyers against
sellers
**Answer: a) the bees' needs against the sites available** — bees' needs vs available sites.

**6. (Sub lesson 2) The book pictures which hives side by side?**
a) Plastic and glass  b) Traditional and modern hives  c) No hives
**Answer: b) Traditional and modern hives** — traditional and modern hives are pictured
together.
