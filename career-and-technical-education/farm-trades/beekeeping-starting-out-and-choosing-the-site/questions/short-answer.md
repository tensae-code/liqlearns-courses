# Beekeeping — starting out and choosing the site — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) List the four start-up steps in order.**
**Answer:** Establish the beekeeping site; obtain colonies; prepare the basic equipment,
materials and tools; clear and fence the apiary site.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Why does site selection decide success?**
**Answer:** Productive beekeeping needs a suitable area located and evaluated; a good site
saves time, energy and money.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) What two kinds of hive does the book picture together?**
**Answer:** Traditional and modern hives.
