# Beekeeping — starting out and choosing the site — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Equipment is prepared before colonies are obtained.**
**Answer: False** — the order is: site, colonies, equipment, clear and fence.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Clearing and fencing the apiary is part of starting.**
**Answer: True** — it is step 4.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Any site will do if the bees are strong.**
**Answer: False** — site selection decides success.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Evaluating a site can save time, energy and money.**
**Answer: True** — that is the book's reason for care in choosing.
