# Poultry — keeping the flock healthy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) You should never buy birds —**
a) from a neighbour you know  b) from live-bird markets or unknown sources, especially during
outbreaks  c) at all
**Answer: b) from live-bird markets or unknown sources, especially during outbreaks** — markets
and unknown sources are the banned doors.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) New or gifted birds are quarantined for —**
a) two days  b) two weeks, observed, before joining the flock  c) a year
**Answer: b) two weeks, observed, before joining the flock** — two weeks of observation is the
rule.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Disease enters a flock through —**
a) contaminated materials and sick birds  b) clean water  c) sunlight
**Answer: a) contaminated materials and sick birds** — those are the entry roads.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Ammonia builds up in a poultry house when —**
a) birds are vaccinated  b) ventilation is poor; it comes from droppings  c) feed is rich
**Answer: b) ventilation is poor; it comes from droppings** — droppings plus poor ventilation
make ammonia.

**5. (Sub lesson 2) Ammonia causes —**
a) better laying  b) respiratory disorders: sneezing, running eyes, discharges  c) stronger
shells
**Answer: b) respiratory disorders: sneezing, running eyes, discharges** — the respiratory
signs are listed.

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Non-infectious diseases include —**
a) viral infections  b) feed deficiencies and toxicity  c) parasites
**Answer: b) feed deficiencies and toxicity** — deficiencies and toxicity are the non-
infectious pair.
