# Poultry — keeping the flock healthy — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) State the buying and quarantine rules together.**
**Answer:** Never buy from live-bird markets or unknown sources (especially in outbreaks);
quarantine new or gifted birds for two weeks, observed, before they join the flock.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) (Teacher guide) Name the four transmission routes.**
**Answer:** Contact, droplet, airborne and vector-borne.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Name the control set for family flocks.**
**Answer:** Improved management and nutrition (hygiene, housing, flock structure, young-chick
care, feeding), plus vaccination, deworming and external-parasite treatment.
