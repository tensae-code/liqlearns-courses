# Poultry — keeping the flock healthy — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) (Teacher guide) Diseases can travel by contact, droplets, air and
vectors.**
**Answer: True** — the guide's key names contact, droplet, airborne and vector-borne routes.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) A gifted bird can join the flock the same day.**
**Answer: False** — gifted birds are quarantined two weeks like any new bird.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Good ventilation prevents the ammonia respiratory disorders.**
**Answer: True** — ventilation is the prevention named.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Control rests only on medicines.**
**Answer: False** — it rests first on improved management and nutrition, plus vaccination,
deworming and parasite treatment.
