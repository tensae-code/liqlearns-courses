# Hands-on — Poultry — keeping the flock healthy

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes. Real trade practice, done safely and exactly as
the book teaches it.

## Quarantine set-up

Set up (or map) a real quarantine space apart from a flock: separate housing, its own feed and
water, and a two-week observation note sheet — the book's rule made physical.

## Ventilation check

With a keeper, inspect a poultry house's airflow: where air enters, where it leaves, whether
ammonia can be smelled at bird height. Report against the lesson.
