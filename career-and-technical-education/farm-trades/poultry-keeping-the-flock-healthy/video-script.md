# Video script — Poultry — keeping the flock healthy

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on
list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Disease walks in. Lock the roads.
Never buy from the live-bird market. Never from strangers.
A new bird? Two weeks alone. Watch her.
Air the house — droppings make ammonia,
and ammonia makes birds sneeze and weep.
Feed well. Keep clean. Vaccinate. Deworm.
Contact, droplet, air, insect — four roads.
Block them all.
