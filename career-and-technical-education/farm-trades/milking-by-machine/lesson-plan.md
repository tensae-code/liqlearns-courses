# Lesson plan — Dairy — milking by machine

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** Farm Trades · **Lesson:** Dairy —
milking by machine
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Farm Trades (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** machine milking attaches teat cups to the teats; the
cups alternate between vacuum and normal air pressure to draw the milk out, and the milk is
filtered and cooled into a bulk storage tank.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the hand-milking lesson — the same job, done by
pressure instead of fingers.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below,
the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written
as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**.
The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books;
on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as
the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its
source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say how machine milking draws milk: teat cups alternating vacuum and normal air pressure.
2. Follow the milk's path: cups, filtering, cooling, bulk storage tank.
3. Say what fully automatic systems do.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks the machine pulls like a hand → replace fingers with air: it is the alternation of
vacuum and normal pressure that draws.
- Forgets the milk's path → cups, filter, cooling, tank — four stops, in that order.
