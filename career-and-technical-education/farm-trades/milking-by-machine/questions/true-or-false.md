# Dairy — milking by machine — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Teat cups stay at one constant pressure.**
**Answer: False** — they alternate between vacuum and normal air pressure.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Machine milking draws milk out of the teat.**
**Answer: True** — that is what the alternation does.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Milk goes straight from the cups into bottles.**
**Answer: False** — it is filtered and cooled into a bulk storage tank.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Cooling the milk happens before bulk storage.**
**Answer: True** — filtered and cooled before entering the tank.
