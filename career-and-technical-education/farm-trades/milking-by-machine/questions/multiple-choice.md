# Dairy — milking by machine — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Machine milking attaches what to the teats?**
a) Bandages  b) Teat cups  c) Buckets
**Answer: b) Teat cups** — teat cups do the machine's work on the teat.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) The cups draw milk out by alternating —**
a) hot and cold water  b) vacuum and normal air pressure  c) light and dark
**Answer: b) vacuum and normal air pressure** — the pressure alternation draws the milk.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) The machine's pressure change does the work of —**
a) the farmer's legs  b) the milker's fingers  c) the cow's tail
**Answer: b) the milker's fingers** — it replaces the pinch-and-squeeze of the hand.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Before the bulk tank, the milk is —**
a) heated and coloured  b) filtered and cooled  c) sold
**Answer: b) filtered and cooled** — filtered and cooled, then into the tank.

**5. (Sub lesson 2) The large storage vessel is called a —**
a) bulk storage tank  b) milk bottle  c) cream cup
**Answer: a) bulk storage tank** — bulk storage tank.

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Fully automatic systems milk —**
a) only on Sundays  b) without human labour  c) only goats
**Answer: b) without human labour** — that is their step beyond ordinary machine milking.
