# Dairy — milking by machine — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) How does a teat cup milk a cow without fingers?**
**Answer:** The cups alternate between vacuum and normal air pressure, and that alternation
draws the milk out.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Follow machine-milked milk to its storage.**
**Answer:** It is filtered and cooled, then enters a large bulk storage tank.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) What do fully automatic systems remove?**
**Answer:** Human labour — they milk without it.
