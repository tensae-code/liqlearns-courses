# Hands-on — Dairy — milking by machine

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes. Real trade practice, done safely and exactly as
the book teaches it.

## Dairy visit

On a visit to a dairy that uses machine milking (or from a keeper's description), trace the
milk's real path from cup to tank and compare it with the book's path, step by step.

## Pressure demonstration

With a drinking straw and a cup of water, feel how changed air pressure moves liquid — a simple
feel for the vacuum idea behind the teat cups. Teacher-led; water only.
