# Dairy — milking by machine

## Do it first

A machine has no fingers to pinch and squeeze. So how can it take milk from a teat? Write your
guess — air? pulling? pushing? — then read how the book's machine actually does it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Cups, vacuum and air

Machine milking attaches teat cups to the cow's teats. The cups alternate between vacuum and
normal air pressure, and that alternation draws the milk out — the machine's pressure change
does the work the milker's fingers do by hand.

- Teat cups attach to the teats.
- Vacuum and normal air pressure alternate in the cups.
- The alternation draws the milk out.

## Sub lesson 2 — Filtered, cooled, stored

The milk the machine draws is filtered and cooled before entering a large bulk storage tank.
Fully automatic systems go one step further: they milk without human labour at all.

- Milk is filtered, then cooled.
- It enters a large bulk storage tank.
- Fully automatic systems milk without human labour.
