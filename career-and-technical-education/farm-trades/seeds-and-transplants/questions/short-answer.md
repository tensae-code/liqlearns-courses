# Gardening — seeds or seedlings? Starting your plants — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Why might a gardener choose seed over transplants?**
**Answer:** More varieties exist as seed and seed costs less.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Write the two rules of transplanting.**
**Answer:** Harden seedlings off with lengthening full-sun periods for about a week; plant at
the same depth they grew in the container (tomatoes slightly deeper).

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Name two root crops that do not transplant well.**
**Answer:** Any two of: carrots, radishes, beets.
