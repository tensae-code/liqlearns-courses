# Gardening — seeds or seedlings? Starting your plants — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Transplanting is essential for slow plants that cannot mature from a direct
sowing in one season.**
**Answer: True** — that is why the practice exists.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Seed usually costs more than transplants.**
**Answer: False** — seed costs less.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Carrots, radishes and beets transplant well.**
**Answer: False** — root crops such as these do not transplant well.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Hardening off takes about a week of lengthening sun.**
**Answer: True** — longer periods in full sun each day for about a week.
