# Gardening — seeds or seedlings? Starting your plants — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A seed is —**
a) the small hard part a new plant grows from  b) a young plant in a tray  c) a kind of soil
**Answer: a) the small hard part a new plant grows from** — that is the book's definition.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Two advantages of seed are —**
a) faster harvest and bigger plants  b) more varieties and lower cost  c) no watering needed
**Answer: b) more varieties and lower cost** — more varieties exist as seed, and seed costs
less.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Transplanting brings the harvest sooner because —**
a) seedlings are already growing when they are moved  b) the soil becomes richer  c) rain comes
earlier
**Answer: a) seedlings are already growing when they are moved** — the plants start ahead, so
the harvest comes sooner.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Hardening off means —**
a) keeping seedlings in the dark  b) giving seedlings longer periods in full sun each day for
about a week  c) watering only once a week
**Answer: b) giving seedlings longer periods in full sun each day for about a week** — the sun
periods lengthen over about a week.

**5. (Sub lesson 2) A transplant is planted —**
a) much deeper than it grew  b) at the same depth it grew in its container  c) on top of the
soil
**Answer: b) at the same depth it grew in its container** — same depth is the rule.

**6. (Sub lesson 2) The exception planted slightly deeper is —**
a) carrots  b) beets  c) tomatoes
**Answer: c) tomatoes** — tomatoes are the named exception.
