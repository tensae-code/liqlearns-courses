# Hands-on — Gardening — seeds or seedlings? Starting your plants

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes. Real trade practice, done safely and exactly as
the book teaches it.

## Transplant a real seedling

After a week of hardening off, lift a seedling from its container, set it into a prepared hole
at the same depth it grew, and firm the soil in around it — the GIF shows the real motion.
Water it as your teacher directs.

## Seed-versus-tray costing

Price a seed packet and a seedling tray for the same vegetable at a local seller. Work out
which gives more plants for the money, and what the tray buys you instead: time.
