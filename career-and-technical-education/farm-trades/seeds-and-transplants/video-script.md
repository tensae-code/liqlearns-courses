# Video script — Gardening — seeds or seedlings? Starting your plants

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on
list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A seed is small and hard — and a whole plant sleeps inside it.
Seed is cheaper. More kinds come as seed.
But a seedling in a tray is already growing.
Move it to the garden, and your harvest comes sooner.
First, harden it off: more sun each day, for about a week.
Then plant it at the same depth it grew —
except the tomato. The tomato goes a little deeper.
