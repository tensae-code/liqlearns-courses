# Gardening — growing plants as a business — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) In one sentence, what is horticulture?**
**Answer:** The art and science of growing flowers, fruits, vegetables and shrubs in gardens
and orchards — the plant-only branch of agriculture.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Name three uses plants are grown for.**
**Answer:** Any three of: flowers/leaves/appearance, food, dyes, medicine, cosmetics.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Name a medicinal herb used in your own locality and what it is used for.**
**Answer:** The learner's own local herb, named with its local use (answers vary by locality).
