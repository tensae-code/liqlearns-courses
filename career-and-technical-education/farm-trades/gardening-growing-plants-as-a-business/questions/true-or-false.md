# Gardening — growing plants as a business — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Horticulture covers animals as well as plants.**
**Answer: False** — it deals with plants only; animals belong to agriculture as a whole.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Shrubs can be part of horticulture.**
**Answer: True** — flowers, fruits, vegetables and shrubs are all named.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Gardening means laying out and caring for a plot.**
**Answer: True** — that is its practice.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Plants are grown only for food.**
**Answer: False** — also for appearance, dyes, medicine and cosmetics.
