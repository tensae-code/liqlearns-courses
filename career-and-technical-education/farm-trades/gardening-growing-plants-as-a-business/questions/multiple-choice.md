# Gardening — growing plants as a business — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What does horticulture deal with?**
a) Plants only  b) Animals only  c) Machines
**Answer: a) Plants only** — horticulture is the branch of agriculture that deals with plants
only.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Horticulture is the art and science of growing —**
a) cattle and goats  b) flowers, fruits, vegetables and shrubs  c) cars and tools
**Answer: b) flowers, fruits, vegetables and shrubs** — that is the book's own list.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) How is agriculture as a whole different from horticulture?**
a) It is smaller  b) It covers crops and animals too  c) It has no plants
**Answer: b) It covers crops and animals too** — agriculture covers both; horticulture is
plants only.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Gardening is —**
a) laying out and caring for a plot to grow plants  b) selling animals at market  c) building a
house
**Answer: a) laying out and caring for a plot to grow plants** — that is the practice of
horticulture.

**5. (Sub lesson 2) Which of these is a reason plants are grown?**
a) For dyes, medicine and cosmetics  b) For making rain  c) For feeding cars
**Answer: a) For dyes, medicine and cosmetics** — the book names appearance, food, dyes,
medicine and cosmetics.

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Where is horticulture practised?**
a) In gardens and orchards  b) Only in offices  c) Only at sea
**Answer: a) In gardens and orchards** — gardens and orchards are its places.
