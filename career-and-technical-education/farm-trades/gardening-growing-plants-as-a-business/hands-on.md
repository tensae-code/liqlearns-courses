# Hands-on — Gardening — growing plants as a business

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes. Real trade practice, done safely and exactly as
the book teaches it.

## Observe a real garden

Visit a working garden near your school, as the guide directs (teacher guide — evidence). Watch
how horticulture is actually practised there, then present what you observed back to your
group.

## Local herb hunt

With an elder or a knowledgeable neighbour, identify the medicinal herbs used in your locality.
Record each herb's local name and what it is used for. Do not taste or use any herb — naming
and recording only.
