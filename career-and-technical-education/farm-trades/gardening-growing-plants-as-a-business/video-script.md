# Video script — Gardening — growing plants as a business

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on
list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Here is a garden.
Flowers. Fruits. Vegetables. Shrubs.
Growing all of them well — that is horticulture.
Horticulture is plants only.
Agriculture is bigger: plants and animals too.
Now the work: lay out a plot. Care for it. That is gardening.
Food to eat. Flowers to look at. Herbs for medicine.
A cared-for plot can feed a family — and earn money too.
