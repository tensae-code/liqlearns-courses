# Beekeeping — running a good apiary — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A poor apiary site leads to —**
a) sweeter honey  b) people and animals stung and honey stolen  c) quieter bees
**Answer: b) people and animals stung and honey stolen** — those are the book's warnings.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) The site must be fenced or hedged to —**
a) keep bees in all day  b) keep people and animals away from the hives  c) look tidy
**Answer: b) keep people and animals away from the hives** — fencing protects people and
animals — and the bees.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Hives should stand —**
a) beside the main road  b) away from dwellings, roads and public areas  c) inside the school
yard
**Answer: b) away from dwellings, roads and public areas** — distance from dwellings, roads and
public areas is the rule.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) The sun rule for an apiary is —**
a) full sun all day  b) shade in the hot part of the day, morning sun  c) no sun at all
**Answer: b) shade in the hot part of the day, morning sun** — midday shade with morning sun.

**5. (Sub lesson 2) Fresh water for bees can be —**
a) only a river  b) a river, pond, or even a dripping tap  c) sea water
**Answer: b) a river, pond, or even a dripping tap** — even a dripping tap counts.

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Against theft the book offers —**
a) a lockable bee house, with wall holes for the bees  b) a dog only  c) moving hives nightly
**Answer: a) a lockable bee house, with wall holes for the bees** — the lockable bee house is
the book's option.
