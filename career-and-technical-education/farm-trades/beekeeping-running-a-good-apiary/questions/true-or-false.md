# Beekeeping — running a good apiary — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) The site should be hard to reach so bees are undisturbed.**
**Answer: False** — it must be easy to reach for regular checks.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Strong wind shelter and moving air are both wanted.**
**Answer: True** — shelter from strong wind while keeping air moving — both.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Smoke and fire near an apiary are harmless.**
**Answer: False** — bees are kept away from smoke, fire and unfriendly neighbours.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Rich bee flora nearby is a site-choice factor.**
**Answer: True** — flora, sunrise, wind direction, clean dry ground and windbreaks are all
weighed.
