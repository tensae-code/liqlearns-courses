# Beekeeping — running a good apiary — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Give four rules for where hives stand.**
**Answer:** Any four of: easy to reach for checks; fenced/hedged from people and animals; away
from dwellings, roads and public areas; midday shade with morning sun; sheltered from strong
wind with air moving; near fresh water.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) How does a lockable bee house still work for bees?**
**Answer:** Wall holes let the bees come and go while the house stays locked against theft.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Name three wider factors weighed in site choice.**
**Answer:** Any three of: sunrise, wind direction, freedom from pollution and bee disease, dry
open ground, windbreaks, rich bee flora nearby.
