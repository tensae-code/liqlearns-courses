# Video script — Beekeeping — running a good apiary

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on
list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Where the hives stand decides everything.
Easy for the keeper to reach.
Fenced from children and cattle.
Far from roads and houses.
Shade at noon. Sun in the morning.
Shelter from the wind — but let the air move.
Water near: a river, a pond, even a dripping tap.
Lock the bee house. Mind the neighbours.
A good site is a quiet promise to the bees.
