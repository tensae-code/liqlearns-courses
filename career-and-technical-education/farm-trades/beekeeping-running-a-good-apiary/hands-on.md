# Hands-on — Beekeeping — running a good apiary

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes. Real trade practice, done safely and exactly as
the book teaches it.

## Apiary audit

Audit a working apiary with its keeper against the full rule list, rule by rule, and agree the
single improvement that would help most.

## Water point set-up

Where an apiary lacks near water, set up a simple water point with a keeper's guidance — the
book allows even a dripping tap — placed so bees drink without troubling people.
