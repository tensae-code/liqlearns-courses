# Hands-on — Poultry — chickens for eggs, meat and income

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes. Real trade practice, done safely and exactly as
the book teaches it.

## Poultry farm observation

Observe a local poultry farm and list its activities — the student book's own activity; the
guide adds the site-visit method (teacher guide — evidence, placement 11). Report the
activities in order.

## Household flock count

Count and record a household flock you may visit: hens, cocks, chicks, eggs in a week. Turn the
count into the two incomes: regular and flexible.
