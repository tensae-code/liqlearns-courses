# Poultry — chickens for eggs, meat and income — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Why are chickens a good first livestock for a poor family?**
**Answer:** They are the smallest livestock investment a village family can make, and they
supply protein and income.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Tell the two poultry incomes apart.**
**Answer:** Eggs give small regular income; live birds give flexible cash when needed.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Name the three things poultry keeping supports across Ethiopia.**
**Answer:** Poverty alleviation, nutrition and food security.
