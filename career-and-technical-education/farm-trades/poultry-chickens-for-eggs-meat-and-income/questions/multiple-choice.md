# Poultry — chickens for eggs, meat and income — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Chickens are called —**
a) the hardest livestock to start  b) the smallest livestock investment a village family can
make  c) a rich family's animal
**Answer: b) the smallest livestock investment a village family can make** — smallest
investment is the book's phrase.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Eggs give a family —**
a) small regular income  b) one huge payment  c) no income
**Answer: a) small regular income** — eggs are the regular income.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Live birds give —**
a) flexible cash when needed  b) daily milk  c) wool
**Answer: a) flexible cash when needed** — a bird sold when money is needed.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Small-scale poultry production meets a family's —**
a) animal-nutrition demand  b) building needs  c) transport needs
**Answer: a) animal-nutrition demand** — nutrition is what it meets.

**5. (Sub lesson 2) Poultry keeping supports —**
a) poverty alleviation, nutrition and food security  b) only sport  c) only ceremonies
**Answer: a) poverty alleviation, nutrition and food security** — those three are named.

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Poultry keeping is found in Ethiopia in —**
a) rural areas only  b) rural, urban and semi-urban areas  c) cities only
**Answer: b) rural, urban and semi-urban areas** — all three settings are named.
