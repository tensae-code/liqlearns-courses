# Poultry — chickens for eggs, meat and income — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Chickens are rare in rural households.**
**Answer: False** — they are almost universal there.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) A live bird can be sold when a family suddenly needs cash.**
**Answer: True** — that is the flexible income.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Poultry gives protein as well as income.**
**Answer: True** — it supplies protein and income.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Poultry keeping has no part in food security.**
**Answer: False** — it supports food security, nutrition and poverty alleviation.
