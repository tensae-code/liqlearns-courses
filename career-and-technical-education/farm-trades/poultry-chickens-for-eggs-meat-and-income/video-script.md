# Video script — Poultry — chickens for eggs, meat and income

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on
list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The smallest livestock — and maybe the hardest working.
A hen asks little. She gives much.
Eggs: small money, often.
A live bird: money when the need knocks.
Protein for the family. Income for the house.
In villages, in towns, in cities —
chickens fight poverty, feed children, guard food security.
Start small. Start with chickens.
