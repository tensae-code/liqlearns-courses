# Gardening — keeping plants healthy: pests, disease and safe spraying — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) The first defence of a garden is —**
a) strong chemical sprays  b) healthy plants: adequate sun, water and fertility  c) a high
fence
**Answer: b) healthy plants: adequate sun, water and fertility** — healthy plants resist
attack.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Flowering plants along the bed help because they —**
a) attract beneficial insects  b) scare the rain  c) feed the pests
**Answer: a) attract beneficial insects** — beneficial insects are drawn by the flowers.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Seed and transplants should be chosen —**
a) disease- and pest-resistant seed, clean and healthy transplants  b) the cheapest only  c) by
colour
**Answer: a) disease- and pest-resistant seed, clean and healthy transplants** — resistant,
clean and healthy are the book's words.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Pesticides in the book's order are —**
a) the first step  b) the chemical last resort  c) never mentioned
**Answer: b) the chemical last resort** — they come after prevention.

**5. (Sub lesson 2) Before spraying, the gardener must wear —**
a) gloves, face mask and eye protection  b) a hat only  c) nothing special
**Answer: a) gloves, face mask and eye protection** — that is the safety rule, exactly as
taught.

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Why is the protective gear required?**
a) Pesticides harm human health  b) It looks professional  c) It keeps clothes clean
**Answer: a) Pesticides harm human health** — the book gives the health reason.
