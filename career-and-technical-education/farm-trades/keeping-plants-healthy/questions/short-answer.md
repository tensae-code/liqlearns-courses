# Gardening — keeping plants healthy: pests, disease and safe spraying — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name three first defences that are not chemicals.**
**Answer:** Any three of: adequate sun, water and fertility; flowering plants attracting
beneficial insects; resistant seed; clean, healthy transplants.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) State the spraying safety rule and its reason.**
**Answer:** Gloves, face mask and eye protection must be worn, because pesticides harm human
health.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) When are pesticides used in the book's order?**
**Answer:** As the chemical last resort, when prevention is not enough.
