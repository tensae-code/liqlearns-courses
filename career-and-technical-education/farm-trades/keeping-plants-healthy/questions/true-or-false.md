# Gardening — keeping plants healthy: pests, disease and safe spraying — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Healthy plants resist attack better than weak plants.**
**Answer: True** — strength is the first defence.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Beneficial insects are the gardener's enemies.**
**Answer: False** — flowering plants attract them as helpers.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Spraying without a mask is fine for a short job.**
**Answer: False** — the rule is gloves, mask and eye protection, because pesticides harm
health.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Pesticides may be sprayed on soil or plants.**
**Answer: True** — the book says soil or plants.
