# Video script — Gardening — keeping plants healthy: pests, disease and safe spraying

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on
list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A strong plant fights back.
Give it sun. Give it water. Feed the soil.
Plant flowers — they bring insects that help you.
Choose seed that resists disease.
Only when all that is not enough, comes the spray — the last resort.
And then: gloves on. Mask on. Eyes covered.
Pesticides harm human health. The book's rule. Never skip it.
