# Hands-on — Gardening — keeping plants healthy: pests, disease and safe spraying

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes. Real trade practice, done safely and exactly as
the book teaches it.

## Beneficial-insect planting

Plant or mark the flowering plants along a real bed's edge — the book's way of drawing
beneficial insects to the garden's defence.

## Safety-gear check

With an adult, lay out the spraying safety kit — gloves, face mask, eye protection — and check
each item is present and sound. No spraying is done in this activity; the kit check itself is
the practice.
