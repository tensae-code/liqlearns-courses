# Lesson plan — Gardening — starting your garden in eight steps

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** Farm Trades · **Lesson:** Gardening —
starting your garden in eight steps
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Farm Trades (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the eight start-up steps — decide, locate, plan beds,
arrange tools, choose seeds or transplants, plant with care, nurture, enjoy and sell — and
garden care as the gardener's most important duty.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on all the gardening lessons before it — tools, seeds
and transplants are known; now the whole start, in order.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below,
the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written
as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**.
The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books;
on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as
the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its
source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead,
each opening when the one before it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub
Lessons (ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **The eight steps** — The start-up sequence: (1) decide what to grow, (2) choose the
location, (3) plan the garden beds, (4) arrange the right tools, (5) choose the right seeds or
transplants, (6) plant with care, (7) nurture the garden, (8) enjoy and sell the harvest.
2. **Care — the most important duty** — Garden care is named the gardener's most important
duty, because the yield depends on it.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so
the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Gardening — seeds or seedlings? Starting your plants. Recommended, never
forced.
- The other Farm Trades lessons stand next door in this same title.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Recite the eight steps in order.
2. Say why garden care is called the gardener's most important duty.
3. Plan for seasons when rain does not come as expected.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. A real plot — school or home — for the practical.

## If the learner is stuck
- Steps out of order → anchor them to the plot: nothing can be planted before the beds exist on
paper and the tools are in hand.
- Thinks step 8 is the point of it all → without step 7 there is no step 8 worth having.
