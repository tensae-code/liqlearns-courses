# Gardening — starting your garden in eight steps — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) List the eight steps in order.**
**Answer:** Decide what to grow; choose the location; plan the garden beds; arrange the right
tools; choose the right seeds or transplants; plant with care; nurture the garden; enjoy and
sell the harvest.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Why is care the gardener's most important duty?**
**Answer:** Because the yield depends on it.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) What weather must a gardener prepare for?**
**Answer:** Seasons when rain does not come as expected.
