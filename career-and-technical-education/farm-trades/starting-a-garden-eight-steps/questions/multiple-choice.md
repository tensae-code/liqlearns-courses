# Gardening — starting your garden in eight steps — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is step 1 of starting a garden?**
a) Plant with care  b) Decide what to grow  c) Sell the harvest
**Answer: b) Decide what to grow** — deciding what to grow comes first.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Step 3 is —**
a) plan the garden beds  b) arrange the tools  c) nurture the garden
**Answer: a) plan the garden beds** — beds are planned after the location is chosen.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Step 5 is —**
a) choose the location  b) choose the right seeds or transplants  c) enjoy and sell
**Answer: b) choose the right seeds or transplants** — seeds or transplants are chosen after
tools are arranged.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Garden care is called —**
a) an optional extra  b) the gardener's most important duty  c) the seller's job
**Answer: b) the gardener's most important duty** — the yield depends on it.

**5. (Sub lesson 2) The yield of a garden depends most on —**
a) its care  b) its fence colour  c) its name
**Answer: a) its care** — care decides the yield.

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Care includes preparing for —**
a) visitors  b) seasons when rain does not come as expected  c) market day only
**Answer: b) seasons when rain does not come as expected** — failed or late rain must be
planned for.
