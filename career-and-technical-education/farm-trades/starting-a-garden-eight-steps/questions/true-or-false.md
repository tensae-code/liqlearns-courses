# Gardening — starting your garden in eight steps — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Planting with care is step 6 of the eight.**
**Answer: True** — after tools and seeds/transplants are ready.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) You should arrange tools after planting.**
**Answer: False** — arranging the right tools is step 4, before planting.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) A planted garden needs no more work until harvest.**
**Answer: False** — nurturing is step 7 and care is the most important duty.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Enjoying and selling the harvest is the last step.**
**Answer: True** — step 8 closes the sequence.
