# Video script — Gardening — starting your garden in eight steps

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on
list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Eight steps, one garden.
One: decide what to grow.
Two: choose the place. Three: plan the beds.
Four: arrange your tools. Five: choose seeds or seedlings.
Six: plant with care. Seven: nurture the garden.
Eight: enjoy the harvest — and sell it.
Remember step seven. Care is the gardener's greatest duty.
The yield depends on it.
