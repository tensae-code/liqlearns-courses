# Hands-on — Gardening — starting your garden in eight steps

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes. Real trade practice, done safely and exactly as
the book teaches it.

## Plant a real school garden

In groups of five, plant a real school vegetable and flower garden — the unit's own practical,
also in the pest lesson. Follow the eight steps on the real plot, in order.

## Step audit

Take an existing garden — home or school — and audit it against the eight steps: which steps
were done well, which were skipped, and what did the skipping cost?
