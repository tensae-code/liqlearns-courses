# Hands-on — Dairy — the breeding and lactation cycle

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes. Real trade practice, done safely and exactly as
the book teaches it.

## Keeper's calendar

Ask a dairy keeper to show or describe their breeding and calving calendar. Compare its timing
logic with the book's cycle.

## Manure-to-field walk

With a keeper's permission, follow where dairy waste actually goes on a real farm and note how
it is stored and used on the fields.
