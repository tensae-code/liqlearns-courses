# Video script — Dairy — the breeding and lactation cycle

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on
list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

No calf — no milk. That is the dairy law.
The cow grows. She is bred. She calves.
Now the milk comes — lactation begins.
Month by month, it slowly falls.
So the farmer times the next breeding against the fall.
Her daughters grow up to take the old cows' places.
And even the waste works: onto the fields,
feeding the soil that feeds the herd.
