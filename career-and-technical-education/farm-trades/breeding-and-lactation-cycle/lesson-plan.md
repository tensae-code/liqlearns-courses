# Lesson plan — Dairy — the breeding and lactation cycle

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** Farm Trades · **Lesson:** Dairy — the
breeding and lactation cycle
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Farm Trades (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a dairy cow gives no milk until she calves, so breeding
timing sets the farm's milk output; lactation starts at calving and steadily declines, female
calves replace older cows, and managed waste becomes fertiliser.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the housing-and-feeding lesson — the herd is housed
and fed; now the cycle that makes the milk at all.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below,
the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written
as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead,
each opening when the one before it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub
Lessons (ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **The cycle — calving starts the milk** — A dairy cow's life is a cycle of pregnancy and
lactation beginning at puberty.
2. **Lactation's curve — and waste that is not waste** — Lactation — milk secretion from the
mammary glands — starts at calving, and production steadily declines while milking continues;
farmers time the next breeding against this lactation curve.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Trace the cycle: puberty, breeding, calving, lactation beginning; production steadily
declining while milking continues.
2. Say why timing the first and every later breeding sets the farm's milk output.
3. Say what female calves are raised for: replacements for unproductive older cows.
4. Say what well-managed dairy waste becomes: fertiliser building soil organic matter and
fertility.
