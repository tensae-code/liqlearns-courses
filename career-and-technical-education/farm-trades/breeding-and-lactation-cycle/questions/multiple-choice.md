# Dairy — the breeding and lactation cycle — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A dairy cow gives milk —**
a) from birth  b) only after she calves  c) only in winter
**Answer: b) only after she calves** — no calving, no milk.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Breeding timing matters because it —**
a) sets the farm's milk output  b) changes the milk's colour  c) is traditional only
**Answer: a) sets the farm's milk output** — first and later breedings set output.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Female calves are raised as —**
a) pets  b) replacements for unproductive older cows  c) sale-only animals
**Answer: b) replacements for unproductive older cows** — the herd renews itself from its
female calves.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Lactation begins —**
a) at puberty  b) at calving  c) at weaning
**Answer: b) at calving** — milk secretion starts at calving.

**5. (Sub lesson 2) While milking continues, production —**
a) rises forever  b) steadily declines  c) stays flat
**Answer: b) steadily declines** — the lactation curve declines steadily.

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Properly managed dairy waste becomes —**
a) a pollution problem only  b) excellent fertiliser building soil organic matter and fertility
c) animal feed
**Answer: b) excellent fertiliser building soil organic matter and fertility** — its nutrients
build the soil.
