# Dairy — the breeding and lactation cycle — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) The dairy cycle of pregnancy and lactation begins at puberty.**
**Answer: True** — that is where the book starts the cycle.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) A cow's milk output has nothing to do with breeding.**
**Answer: False** — timing breeding sets the output — she gives no milk until she calves.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Lactation means milk secretion from the mammary glands.**
**Answer: True** — that is its definition here.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Dairy waste must always be thrown away unused.**
**Answer: False** — managed, it is excellent fertiliser.
