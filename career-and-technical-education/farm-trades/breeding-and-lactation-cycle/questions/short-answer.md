# Dairy — the breeding and lactation cycle — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Why does the timing of breeding set a farm's milk output?**
**Answer:** Because a cow gives no milk until she calves, so when she is bred decides when —
and how steadily — milk flows.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) What happens to milk production as lactation goes on?**
**Answer:** It steadily declines while milking continues.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) What is dairy waste good for, properly managed?**
**Answer:** Its nutrients make excellent fertiliser that builds soil organic matter and
fertility.
