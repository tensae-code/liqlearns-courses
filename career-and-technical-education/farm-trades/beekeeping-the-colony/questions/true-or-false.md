# Beekeeping — the colony: queen, drones and workers — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Royal jelly and propolis are bee products.**
**Answer: True** — both are on the book's list.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) A hive is managed bee by bee, not as a colony.**
**Answer: False** — bees are social; the colony is managed as a unit.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Drones are driven out in the dry season.**
**Answer: True** — when food is no longer plentiful, out they go.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Workers can number up to 40,000 in the main honey season.**
**Answer: True** — that is the book's peak figure.
