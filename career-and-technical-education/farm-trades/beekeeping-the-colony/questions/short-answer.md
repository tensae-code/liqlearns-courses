# Beekeeping — the colony: queen, drones and workers — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What is beekeeping, and name three of its products.**
**Answer:** Managing honeybees in hives for pollination and products — any three of: honey,
wax, royal jelly, propolis, pollen, live bees and queens.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Give the three sorts with their numbers.**
**Answer:** Queen: one per colony. Drones: about 500 when food is plentiful. Workers: up to
40,000 in the main honey season.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) What is the drones' job, and what happens to them in the dry season?**
**Answer:** Their job is mating with a young queen; in the dry season they are driven out.
