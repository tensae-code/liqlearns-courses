# Beekeeping — the colony: queen, drones and workers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Beekeeping is also called —**
a) apiculture  b) aquaculture  c) sericulture
**Answer: a) apiculture** — apiculture is its other name.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Bees are kept for pollination and for —**
a) milk and wool  b) honey and other products: wax, royal jelly, propolis, pollen  c) silk
**Answer: b) honey and other products: wax, royal jelly, propolis, pollen** — the product list
is the book's.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Bees are described as social because —**
a) they live alone  b) the colony works together  c) they are friendly to handle
**Answer: b) the colony works together** — the colony works together.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) A colony has how many queens?**
a) One  b) Five  c) Hundreds
**Answer: a) One** — one queen, the mother of the colony.

**5. (Sub lesson 2) At peak season the queen lays up to —**
a) 100 eggs a day  b) 1,000 eggs a day  c) 10,000 eggs a day
**Answer: b) 1,000 eggs a day** — up to 1,000 a day at peak.

**6. (Sub lesson 2) The workers are —**
a) males who mate  b) females who cannot lay eggs and do all the hive's work  c) young queens
**Answer: b) females who cannot lay eggs and do all the hive's work** — workers are non-laying
females doing all the work.
