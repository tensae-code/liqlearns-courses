# Video script — Beekeeping — the colony: queen, drones and workers

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on
list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

One hive. Three kinds of bee.
The queen: only one. The mother of them all.
A thousand eggs a day, in the fat season.
The drones: five hundred gentlemen. Their one job is mating.
When the dry season comes — out they go.
The workers: forty thousand daughters.
They cannot lay. They do everything else.
That is a colony. That is beekeeping.
