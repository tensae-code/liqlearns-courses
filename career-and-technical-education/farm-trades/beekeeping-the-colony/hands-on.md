# Hands-on — Beekeeping — the colony: queen, drones and workers

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes. Real trade practice, done safely and exactly as
the book teaches it.

## Hive-side observation

Observe a hive from a safe distance with a beekeeper: workers coming and going, any drones
visible at the entrance in season. No hive is opened in this activity except by the beekeeper,
in full protection.

## Honey-product survey

At a market, list the bee products actually sold — honey, wax and any others from the book's
list — with their local prices.
