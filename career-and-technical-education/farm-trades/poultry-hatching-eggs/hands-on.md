# Hands-on — Poultry — hatching eggs: the broody hen and the incubator

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes. Real trade practice, done safely and exactly as
the book teaches it.

## Nest set-up

Set up a nest area as the book directs (for a real sitting or as a model): feed and water
placed near, and in dry conditions slightly damp soil under the nest — arranged with a keeper.

## Incubator inspection

Where an incubator exists locally, inspect it with its operator: capacity, heat source, and how
the thermostat is checked. Report the three facts.
