# Poultry — hatching eggs: the broody hen and the incubator — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A hen's hatching record matters when choosing her for natural incubation.**
**Answer: True** — a good hatching and mothering record is required.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Eggs are fertile the moment a rooster is introduced.**
**Answer: False** — fertility comes about four days after he joins the hens.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Humidity has no part in hatching.**
**Answer: False** — dry regions manage the nest to hold 60–80% humidity.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Incubators come in only one size.**
**Answer: False** — they are machines of varying capacity.
