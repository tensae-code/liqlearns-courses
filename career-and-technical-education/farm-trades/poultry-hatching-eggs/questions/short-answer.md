# Poultry — hatching eggs: the broody hen and the incubator — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Describe the right broody hen.**
**Answer:** Large, healthy, preferably vaccinated, broody, with a good hatching and mothering
record.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) How is humidity held in a dry region's nest?**
**Answer:** Slightly damp soil under the nest helps hold humidity at 60–80%.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) What does an incubator's thermostat do?**
**Answer:** It holds the temperature constant within one degree.
