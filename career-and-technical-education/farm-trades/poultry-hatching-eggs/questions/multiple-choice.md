# Poultry — hatching eggs: the broody hen and the incubator — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) The natural-incubation hen should be —**
a) any hen at all  b) large, healthy, preferably vaccinated, broody, with a good hatching and
mothering record  c) the youngest hen
**Answer: b) large, healthy, preferably vaccinated, broody, with a good hatching and mothering
record** — that full description is the book's hen.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Eggs become fertile about —**
a) four days after the rooster joins the hens  b) four weeks after laying  c) never without a
machine
**Answer: a) four days after the rooster joins the hens** — about four days is the book's
timing.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Feed and water are placed near the sitting hen so —**
a) she gains weight for sale  b) she stays in condition and the eggs do not cool  c) the chicks
can practise
**Answer: b) she stays in condition and the eggs do not cool** — condition for her, warmth for
the eggs.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) In dry regions, damp soil under the nest holds humidity at —**
a) 10–20%  b) 60–80%  c) 100%
**Answer: b) 60–80%** — 60–80% is the book's range.

**5. (Sub lesson 2) An incubator may be heated by —**
a) electricity, gas or kerosene  b) sunlight only  c) burning feathers
**Answer: a) electricity, gas or kerosene** — those three heat sources are named.

**6. (Sub lesson 2) The thermostat holds incubator temperature constant within —**
a) ten degrees  b) one degree  c) a whole season
**Answer: b) one degree** — within one degree.
