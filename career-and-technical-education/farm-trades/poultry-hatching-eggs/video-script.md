# Video script — Poultry — hatching eggs: the broody hen and the incubator

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on
list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Two ways to hatch an egg.
First, the hen: big, healthy, broody, proven.
Four days after the cock joins — the eggs are fertile.
Food and water beside her, so the eggs never cool.
Dry land? Damp soil under the nest.
Second, the machine: the incubator.
Electric, gas or kerosene heat —
and a thermostat holding it steady, within one degree.
