# Lesson plan — Poultry — hatching eggs: the broody hen and the incubator

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** Farm Trades · **Lesson:** Poultry —
hatching eggs: the broody hen and the incubator
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Farm Trades (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** natural incubation uses a large, healthy, preferably
vaccinated broody hen with a good record — eggs fertile about four days after the rooster
joins, feed and water near her, damp soil in dry regions for 60–80% humidity — while artificial
incubators hold temperature within one degree by thermostat.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the choosing-eggs lesson — the right eggs are chosen;
now hatching them, two ways.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Choose the natural-incubation hen: large, healthy, preferably vaccinated, broody, with good
hatching and mothering record.
2. Say when eggs become fertile: about four days after the rooster joins the hens.
3. Keep the sitting hen in condition: feed and water placed near her so the eggs do not cool.
4. Hold humidity the dry-region way: slightly damp soil under the nest, for 60–80%.
5. Describe the artificial incubator: varying capacity, heated by electricity, gas or kerosene,
thermostat holding temperature within one degree.

## If the learner is stuck
- Forgets the hen's record → a broody hen is chosen like a worker is hired: by proven hatching
and mothering.

thermostat.
