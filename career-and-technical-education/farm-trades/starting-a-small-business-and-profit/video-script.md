# Video script — Starting a small business — and knowing your profit

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on
list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

You have a skill. Now make it a business.
Sell what people want. Treat customers well.
Stand where buyers can reach you.
Know your resources: things, people, money.
Plan how your goods travel.
Know your making and your marketing.
Price it fairly: costs, plus profit.
And keep the book — income minus cost.
P equals I minus C. That number tells the truth.
