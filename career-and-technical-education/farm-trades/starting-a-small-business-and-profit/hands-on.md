# Hands-on — Starting a small business — and knowing your profit

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes. Real trade practice, done safely and exactly as
the book teaches it.

## Market price survey

At a market, record the selling price of one farm product and ask a seller (politely) its main
costs. Work the margin with P = I − C from what you learn.

## One-week real ledger

Keep a real one-week ledger for a household or school micro-activity — eggs sold, vegetables
sold — recording every income and cost daily, and close the week with the formula.
