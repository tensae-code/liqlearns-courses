# Starting a small business — and knowing your profit — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Business idea 1 is to offer —**
a) the cheapest thing possible  b) a product or service customers actually want, with good
customer relationships  c) only what you enjoy making
**Answer: b) a product or service customers actually want, with good customer relationships** —
want and relationships open the seven.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Your location should be —**
a) hidden from rivals  b) accessible to buyers  c) in another town
**Answer: b) accessible to buyers** — buyers must be able to reach you.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Employees, hives and cash are examples of —**
a) customers  b) resources: human, physical and financial  c) costs only
**Answer: b) resources: human, physical and financial** — they are the resource examples —
human, physical, financial.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Distribution means —**
a) how the product reaches buyers: deliver yourself or through others by local transport  b)
the shop's decoration  c) hiring staff
**Answer: a) how the product reaches buyers: deliver yourself or through others by local
transport** — deliver yourself or through others.

**5. (Sub lesson 2) A reasonable price covers —**
a) costs only  b) costs plus profit  c) whatever rivals charge
**Answer: b) costs plus profit** — price covers costs plus profit.

**6. (Sub lesson 3) Income 800, costs 650. The profit is —**
a) 1,450  b) 150, by P = I − C  c) a loss
**Answer: b) 150, by P = I − C** — 800 − 650 = 150.
