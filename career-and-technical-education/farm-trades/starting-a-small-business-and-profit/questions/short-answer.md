# Starting a small business — and knowing your profit — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) List the three kinds of resources with one example each.**
**Answer:** Physical (e.g. hives, seeds, land, a sewing machine), human (employees), financial
(cash).

**2. (Sub lesson 2) What must a reasonable price cover?**
**Answer:** Costs plus profit.

**3. (Sub lesson 3) State the profit formula and what decides loss against profit.**
**Answer:** Profit = income − cost (P = I − C); income below cost is a loss, above it a profit.
