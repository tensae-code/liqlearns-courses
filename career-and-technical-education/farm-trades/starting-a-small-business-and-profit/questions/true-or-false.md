# Starting a small business — and knowing your profit — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Customer relationships are part of the business ideas, not an extra.**
**Answer: True** — idea 1 pairs the wanted product with good relationships.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) A sewing machine is a financial resource.**
**Answer: False** — it is a physical resource; cash is the financial example.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Distribution can be done through others by local transport.**
**Answer: True** — self or others are both planned options.

**4. (Sub lesson 3) If income is below cost, the result is a profit.**
**Answer: False** — income below cost is a loss.
