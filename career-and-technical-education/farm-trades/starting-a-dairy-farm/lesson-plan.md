# Lesson plan — Dairy — starting your own dairy farm

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** Farm Trades · **Lesson:** Dairy —
starting your own dairy farm
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Farm Trades (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** before establishing a dairy farm, arrange six things:
space and shelter, improved breeds, fodder management, daily farm management, disease control,
and reproduction/breeding management.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on all the dairy lessons — milk, milking, hygiene,
housing, feeding and the cycle are known; now starting, as a checklist.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below,
the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written
as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**.
The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books;
on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as
the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its
source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead,
each opening when the one before it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub
Lessons (ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **The six requirements — place, animals, feed** — Before establishing a dairy farm, arrange:
(1) space and shelter for the animals, (2) improved dairy breeds — local breeds can also be
used — and (3) fodder management.
2. **The six requirements — management, health, breeding** — Then: (4) daily farm management,
(5) disease control and management, and (6) reproduction/breeding management.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so
the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Recite the six start-up requirements in order.
2. Apply the checklist to a real or imagined farm and find its gaps.

feed.
