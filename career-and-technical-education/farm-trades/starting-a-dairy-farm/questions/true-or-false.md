# Dairy — starting your own dairy farm — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) The six requirements are arranged before establishing the farm.**
**Answer: True** — they are the before-you-start list.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Only improved breeds may ever be used.**
**Answer: False** — local breeds can also be used.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Disease control can wait until animals fall sick.**
**Answer: False** — it is a start-up requirement, arranged in advance.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Breeding management is one of the six requirements.**
**Answer: True** — requirement 6.
