# Dairy — starting your own dairy farm — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) List the six requirements in order.**
**Answer:** Space and shelter; improved dairy breeds; fodder management; daily farm management;
disease control and management; reproduction/breeding management.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Why is fodder management on the before-starting list?**
**Answer:** Animals must have their feed arranged from the first day — feeding is the farm's
largest ongoing work and cost.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Which requirement keeps the herd renewing itself?**
**Answer:** Reproduction/breeding management (requirement 6).
