# Dairy — starting your own dairy farm — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Requirement 1 is —**
a) disease control  b) space and shelter for the animals  c) daily management
**Answer: b) space and shelter for the animals** — place comes first.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Requirement 2 concerns breeds —**
a) improved dairy breeds; local breeds can also be used  b) only imported breeds  c) no breeds,
any animal
**Answer: a) improved dairy breeds; local breeds can also be used** — improved preferred, local
also usable.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Requirement 3 is —**
a) fodder management  b) breeding management  c) marketing
**Answer: a) fodder management** — what the animals eat is arranged third.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Requirement 4 is —**
a) daily farm management  b) space  c) fodder
**Answer: a) daily farm management** — the daily running of the farm.

**5. (Sub lesson 2) Requirement 5 is —**
a) disease control and management  b) shelter  c) selling milk
**Answer: a) disease control and management** — disease control and management.

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Requirement 6 is —**
a) reproduction/breeding management  b) fodder management  c) milk cooling
**Answer: a) reproduction/breeding management** — breeding management closes the six.
