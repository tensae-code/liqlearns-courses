# Hands-on — Dairy — starting your own dairy farm

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes. Real trade practice, done safely and exactly as
the book teaches it.

## Start-up site visit

Walk a site proposed for a small dairy and judge it against requirement 1 (space and shelter)
and requirement 3 (fodder): what is already there, what must be built or planted?

## Interview a starter

Ask someone who started dairying what they arranged first and what they wish they had arranged
sooner. Map their answers onto the six.
