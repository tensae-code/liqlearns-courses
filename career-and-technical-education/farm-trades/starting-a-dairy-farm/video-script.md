# Video script — Dairy — starting your own dairy farm

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on
list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Six things before the first cow.
One: space and shelter.
Two: good breeds — improved, or local.
Three: fodder — what they will eat.
Four: daily management.
Five: disease control.
Six: breeding management.
Arrange all six first.
Then — and only then — the herd arrives.
