# Gardening — the essential tools — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Loppers and pruning shears do the same family of job — cutting growth.**
**Answer: True** — both are pruning/cutting tools, loppers for bigger growth.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) A hand trowel is a soil-working tool.**
**Answer: True** — it is in the pictured set for working soil at hand scale.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) The sickle is named for digging holes.**
**Answer: False** — the sickle clears land; the hoe digs.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Every locality may have its own traditional tools to list.**
**Answer: True** — the lesson sends pupils to list their own locality's tools with their uses.
