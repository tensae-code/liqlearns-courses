# Gardening — the essential tools — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Which tool protects the gardener's hands?**
a) Gloves  b) Rake  c) Loppers
**Answer: a) Gloves** — gloves are the hand protection in the set.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Pruning shears are also called —**
a) secateurs  b) trowels  c) rakes
**Answer: a) secateurs** — the book gives both names.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Which pair cuts growth back?**
a) Fork and spade  b) Pruning shears and loppers  c) Rake and hoe
**Answer: b) Pruning shears and loppers** — shears and loppers are the cutting pair.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) The traditional tool for digging is —**
a) the sickle  b) the hoe  c) the rake
**Answer: b) the hoe** — the hoe digs; the sickle clears.

**5. (Sub lesson 2) The sickle is the traditional tool for —**
a) watering  b) clearing land  c) storing seeds
**Answer: b) clearing land** — clearing land is its named job.

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Table 3.1's set holds how many tools, counting the traditional hand hoe?**
a) Nine  b) Three  c) Twenty
**Answer: a) Nine** — gloves, shears, loppers, fork, trowel, spade, rake, hoe, traditional hand
hoe.
