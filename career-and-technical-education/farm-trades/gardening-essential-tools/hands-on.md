# Hands-on — Gardening — the essential tools

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes. Real trade practice, done safely and exactly as
the book teaches it.

## Tool-to-job matching

With real tools laid out safely (or drawings of them), take turns: one learner names a garden
job, the other picks the tool and explains the pick. Tools are carried and handled only as the
supervising adult directs.

## Locality tool survey

Ask a gardener in your locality which traditional tools they use and what each is for. Bring
the list back and compare it with the book's hoe and sickle.
