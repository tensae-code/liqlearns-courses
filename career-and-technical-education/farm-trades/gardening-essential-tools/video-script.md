# Video script — Gardening — the essential tools

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on
list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Before you plant, prepare your tools.
Gloves for your hands.
Shears and loppers to cut back growth.
Fork, trowel, spade and hoe to work the soil.
A rake to level the ground.
And the old tools of the village: the hoe that digs,
the sickle that clears the land.
Nine tools. Know each one. Know its job.
