# Lesson plan — Dairy — housing and feeding the herd

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** Farm Trades · **Lesson:** Dairy —
housing and feeding the herd
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Farm Trades (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** modern farms group animals by age, needs, reproductive
status and production; housing must give feed and water access and protection from heat and
cold; feeding is the largest cost, with diets formulated per animal.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the milking and clean-milk lessons — the herd behind
the milk now gets its house and its feed.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below,
the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written
as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**.
The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books;
on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as
the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its
source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say how modern farms group animals: by age, nutritional needs, reproductive status and milk
production — the lactating herd managed most intensively.
2. State housing's duties: access to feed and water, protection from heat and cold; shade (and
fans) against heat stress, which cuts fertility and milk yield.
3. Say feeding's place in the budget: the largest cost.
4. Say how pasture producers and large operations feed: maintained, rotated grazing; diets
formulated per animal; a nutritionist on large operations; every cow eating its full ration.

- Sees housing as just a roof → housing is feed access, water access, heat protection and cold
