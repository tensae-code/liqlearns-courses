# Dairy — housing and feeding the herd — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the four ways animals are grouped for management.**
**Answer:** By age, nutritional needs, reproductive status and milk production.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Why is shade essential in dairy housing?**
**Answer:** Heat stress cuts fertility and milk yield; shade (and fans in barns) protects
against it.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Who may a large operation hire to formulate diets?**
**Answer:** A nutritionist.
