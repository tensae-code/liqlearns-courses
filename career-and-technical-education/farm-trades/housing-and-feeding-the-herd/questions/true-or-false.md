# Dairy — housing and feeding the herd — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Shade matters because heat stress cuts fertility and milk yield.**
**Answer: True** — so shade — and fans in barns — are provided.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Housing should keep animals warm, but cold can never kill.**
**Answer: False** — housing must stay warm enough not to kill in cold.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Diets are the same for every cow, whatever her state.**
**Answer: False** — diets are formulated per animal's growth, production and reproductive
state.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) A cow must eat her full ration for the plan to work.**
**Answer: True** — a ration not eaten feeds no milk.
