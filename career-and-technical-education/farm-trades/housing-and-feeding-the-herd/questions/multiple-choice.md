# Dairy — housing and feeding the herd — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Animals are grouped by —**
a) colour and height  b) age, nutritional needs, reproductive status and milk production  c)
name
**Answer: b) age, nutritional needs, reproductive status and milk production** — those four are
the management units.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Which herd is managed most intensively?**
a) The dry herd  b) The lactating herd  c) Retired cows
**Answer: b) The lactating herd** — the milk-producing herd gets the closest management.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Housing must protect animals from —**
a) heat and cold  b) music  c) visitors
**Answer: a) heat and cold** — both extremes are housing's duty, with feed and water access.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Heat stress does what?**
a) Raises fertility  b) Cuts fertility and milk yield  c) Changes coat colour
**Answer: b) Cuts fertility and milk yield** — that is why shade and fans matter.

**5. (Sub lesson 2) The largest cost in dairying is —**
a) housing paint  b) feeding  c) transport
**Answer: b) feeding** — feeding is the largest cost.

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Pasture-based producers must —**
a) never move cattle  b) maintain grazing land and rotate grazing  c) buy all feed
**Answer: b) maintain grazing land and rotate grazing** — maintained, rotated grazing is their
way.
