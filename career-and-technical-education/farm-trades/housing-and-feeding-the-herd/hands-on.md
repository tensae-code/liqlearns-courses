# Hands-on — Dairy — housing and feeding the herd

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes. Real trade practice, done safely and exactly as
the book teaches it.

## Barn audit

Visit or picture a real dairy shelter and audit it: can every animal reach feed and water? Is
there shade? Warmth against cold nights? Report each point against the lesson.

## Ration observation

Watch a feeding: is the ration measured per animal, and does each cow finish hers? Note what
the keeper does about a cow that does not.
