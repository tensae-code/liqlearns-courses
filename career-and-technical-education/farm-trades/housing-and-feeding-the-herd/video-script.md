# Video script — Dairy — housing and feeding the herd

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on
list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A herd is not one crowd. It is groups.
Young and old. Milking and waiting. Each group, its own care.
The milking herd gets the closest care of all.
Their house must feed them, water them, shade them.
Heat steals milk and calves — so give shade.
Cold can kill — so give warmth.
And feed: the biggest cost of all.
Measure it for each cow. And see that she eats it all.
