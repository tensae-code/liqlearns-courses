# Fattening — choosing the site, the shelter and the animal — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) What two things must a fattening site provide?**
**Answer:** Feeds locally plentiful, and grass for grazing all year.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) List the five selection criteria.**
**Answer:** Age, sex, disposition, constitution and vigour, and breed.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Name the constitution signs of a good fattener.**
**Answer:** Big heart girth, well-sprung ribs, full chest and strong appetite.
