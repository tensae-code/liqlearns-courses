# Fattening — choosing the site, the shelter and the animal — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A good fattening site has —**
a) feeds locally plentiful and grass for grazing all year  b) a river port  c) many shops
**Answer: a) feeds locally plentiful and grass for grazing all year** — local feed and year-
round grass decide the site.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Which is on the book's local-feeds list?**
a) Rice straw and corn stover  b) Ice and sand  c) Imported cake only
**Answer: a) Rice straw and corn stover** — rice straw, corn stover, copra meal, rice bran,
molasses are named.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) In fattening housing, what is essential?**
a) Shade  b) Carpet  c) Music
**Answer: a) Shade** — shade is essential; housing shields from heat and cold extremes.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) The preferred age for a fattening animal is —**
a) six months  b) two to three years  c) ten years
**Answer: b) two to three years** — two to three years is preferred.

**5. (Sub lesson 2) Steers are preferred because they —**
a) gain faster and handle easier than heifers  b) eat nothing  c) work harder
**Answer: a) gain faster and handle easier than heifers** — faster gain, easier handling.

**6. (Sub lesson 2) A restless animal fattens badly because it —**
a) wastes energy  b) sleeps too much  c) drinks too much
**Answer: a) wastes energy** — restlessness wastes the energy that should become meat.
