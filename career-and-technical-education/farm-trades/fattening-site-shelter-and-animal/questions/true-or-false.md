# Fattening — choosing the site, the shelter and the animal — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Molasses and rice bran are named as locally plentiful feeds to look for.**
**Answer: True** — both are on the book's site list.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Housing for fattening needs no shade in a hot place.**
**Answer: False** — shade is essential.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Younger animals feed for a longer time than older ones.**
**Answer: True** — younger feed longer; older fatten faster — 2–3 years balances it.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Improved breeds gain quicker on more feed than native animals.**
**Answer: False** — quicker on LESS feed is the book's claim.
