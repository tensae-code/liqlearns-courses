# Lesson plan — Fattening — choosing the site, the shelter and the animal

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** Farm Trades · **Lesson:** Fattening —
choosing the site, the shelter and the animal
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Farm Trades (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** choose a site where feeds are locally plentiful with
year-round grazing, housing that shields from heat and cold with shade essential, and animals
by five criteria: age, sex, disposition, constitution and vigour, and breed.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the fattening lesson before it — fattening is
defined; now choosing where, what shelter, and which animal.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below,
the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written
as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**.
The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books;
on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as
the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its
source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead,

## Before this lesson

- The other Farm Trades lessons stand next door in this same title.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Choose a fattening site: feeds locally plentiful (rice straw, corn stover, copra meal, rice
bran, molasses) with grass for grazing all year.
2. State housing's duties for fattening: shield from extreme heat and cold; shade essential.
3. Judge an animal by the five criteria, with the details: age two to three years preferred;
steers gain faster and handle easier; quiet disposition; big heart girth, well-sprung ribs,
full chest, strong appetite; improved breeds gain quicker on less feed.

## If the learner is stuck
- Chooses by looks alone → force the five criteria, one by one; 'beautiful' is not among them.
- Forgets disposition → a restless animal burns its feed as energy; quiet is profit.
