# Fattening — choosing the site, the shelter and the animal

## Do it first

At a market, two steers stand side by side at the same price. One will fatten fast and sell
well; the other will eat your money. What do your eyes and hands check before you choose? List
your checks — the book's five criteria are waiting.

## Sub lesson 1 — Site and housing

Choose a site where feeds are locally plentiful — rice straw, corn stover, copra meal, rice
bran, molasses are the book's examples — with grass for grazing all year. Housing must shield
cattle from extreme heat and cold, and shade is essential.

- Site: local feeds plentiful + grazing grass all year.
- Named local feeds: rice straw, corn stover, copra meal, rice bran, molasses.
- Housing shields from extreme heat and cold; shade essential.

## Sub lesson 2 — Choosing the animal — five criteria

Age: two to three years preferred — younger animals feed longer, older fatten faster. Sex:
steers (castrated males) gain faster and handle easier than heifers. Disposition: a lively but
quiet, easily handled steer fattens best; restless animals waste energy. Constitution and
vigour: big heart girth, well-sprung ribs, full chest, strong appetite. Breed: improved breeds
gain quicker on less feed than native animals.

- Age 2–3 years preferred.
- Steers gain faster, handle easier than heifers.
- Quiet but lively disposition; restless wastes energy.
- Constitution: big heart girth, well-sprung ribs, full chest, strong appetite.
- Improved breeds: quicker gain on less feed.

## Say it to your companion

Walk your companion through a market choice: site first (feed? grass?), shelter (shade?), then
the animal — all five criteria, in order, with the steer details.

## Do It Yourself

1. Write a site scorecard: local feeds listed, year-round grass yes/no, shade possible yes/no.
2. Draw a steer and label the constitution checks: heart girth, ribs, chest, appetite.
3. Rank three described animals by the five criteria and defend the winner.
4. Memorise the five criteria as one hand: age, sex, disposition, constitution, breed.
