# Hands-on — Fattening — choosing the site, the shelter and the animal

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes. Real trade practice, done safely and exactly as
the book teaches it.

## Market judging

At a market or with a keeper, judge real animals against the five criteria card — age estimate,
sex, disposition watched quietly, constitution points, breed — without handling animals except
as their keeper directs.

## Site survey

Survey a proposed fattening site: list the feeds actually plentiful nearby, check grazing
through the year, and mark where shade stands or must be made.
