# Video script — Fattening — choosing the site, the shelter and the animal

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on
list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Choose the place first.
Feed must be near: straw, stover, bran, molasses.
Grass must last all year.
The shelter must shade them — shade is not a luxury.
Now the animal. Two to three years old.
A steer: he gains fast and handles easy.
Quiet, but lively. Big heart, springy ribs, full chest.
Hungry for his feed. A good breed.
Five checks. Make them all.
