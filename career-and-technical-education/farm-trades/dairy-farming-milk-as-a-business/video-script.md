# Video script — Dairy farming — milk as a business

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on
list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Milk is a business.
The cow gives it — and the goat, the sheep, the camel too.
The farm produces the milk.
The factory changes it: yogurt, cheese, butter.
Big dairying pays only where people will buy,
or where milk becomes food that keeps.
And the small farmer? Morning milk, carried to town.
A little money, every day.
