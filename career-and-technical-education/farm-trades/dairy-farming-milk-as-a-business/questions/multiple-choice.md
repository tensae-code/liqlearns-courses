# Dairy farming — milk as a business — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A dairy is the business of —**
a) harvesting and processing animal milk  b) growing vegetables  c) making cloth
**Answer: a) harvesting and processing animal milk** — that is the book's definition.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Dairy milk comes mostly from cows, and also from —**
a) goats, sheep and camels  b) horses and dogs  c) chickens
**Answer: a) goats, sheep and camels** — those three are named.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) A dairy factory does what to milk?**
a) Produces it from cows  b) Processes it into products such as yogurt, cheese and butter  c)
Sells the cows
**Answer: b) Processes it into products such as yogurt, cheese and butter** — the farm
produces; the factory processes.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Large-scale dairying only pays where —**
a) land is free  b) durable products are made or a paying market exists  c) many workers live
**Answer: b) durable products are made or a paying market exists** — milk spoils; scale needs
products or a market.

**5. (Sub lesson 2) Small farmers earn side income by —**
a) taking morning milk to town  b) selling their farms  c) buying factories
**Answer: a) taking morning milk to town** — the morning-milk trip is the book's picture.

**6. (Sub lesson 2) (Teacher guide) Cattle kept only for milk are called —**
a) beef-only  b) dairy-only  c) dual-purpose
**Answer: b) dairy-only** — dairy-only is the by-purpose name in the guide's key.
