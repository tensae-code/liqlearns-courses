# Dairy farming — milk as a business — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Tell a dairy farm from a dairy factory.**
**Answer:** The farm produces the milk; the factory processes it into products such as yogurt,
cheese and butter.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) How does a small farmer earn side income from dairying?**
**Answer:** By taking morning milk to town for sale.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) (Teacher guide) Name the three cattle types by production purpose.**
**Answer:** Beef-only, dairy-only and dual-purpose.
