# Dairy farming — milk as a business — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A dairy can be a section of a mixed farm.**
**Answer: True** — it need not be a dedicated farm.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Camels can be dairy animals.**
**Answer: True** — goats, sheep and camels are named beside cows.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Large-scale dairying pays anywhere, market or not.**
**Answer: False** — it only pays where durable products are made or a paying market exists.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) (Teacher guide) Dual-purpose cattle are kept for both milk and meat.**
**Answer: True** — that is what dual-purpose means in the guide's key: beef, dairy, dual-
purpose.
