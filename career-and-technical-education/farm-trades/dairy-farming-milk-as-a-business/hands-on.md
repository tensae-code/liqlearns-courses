# Hands-on — Dairy farming — milk as a business

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes. Real trade practice, done safely and exactly as
the book teaches it.

## Milk-chain interview

Interview a local milk seller or dairy keeper: which animals, how much milk a day, where it is
sold, what is made from what is left. Report the chain back in order.

## Visit a cattle site

Visit a cattle-farming site in the locality to observe how the activities are performed — the
guide's site-visit method (teacher guide — evidence, placement 11); a local guest speaker is
the alternative voice.
