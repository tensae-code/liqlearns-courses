# Video script — Beekeeping — the beekeeper's kit

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on
list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Carry the right kit, or do not open the hive.
The veil. The suit. They stop most stings.
The smoker: a little smoke, and the bees grow calm —
control, in your hand.
The hive tool works the hive itself.
Then harvest tools: extractor, presser, sieve, fork.
And the wax extractor for the wax.
Eleven tools. Know them. Name them. Use them.
