# Hands-on — Beekeeping — the beekeeper's kit

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes. Real trade practice, done safely and exactly as
the book teaches it.

## Kit inspection

With a beekeeper, inspect the real kit: try the veil's fit (without approaching bees), feel the
hive tool's edge, watch the smoker lit and puffed by the keeper. Record each tool's local name
if it has one.

## Smoker drill

Under a beekeeper's direction, practise lighting a smoker and giving steady puffs — the control
move the GIF shows — away from any hive until the keeper approves the technique.
