# Beekeeping — the beekeeper's kit — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Which items protect the beekeeper?**
a) Veil and protective clothing  b) Honey sieve and fork  c) Queen excluder
**Answer: a) Veil and protective clothing** — the veil and clothing are the protection.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Protective clothing does what?**
a) Prevents most stings  b) Attracts bees  c) Makes honey
**Answer: a) Prevents most stings** — it prevents most stings — the text's own reasoning.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Smoke is used because it —**
a) kills the bees  b) gives some control over the bees  c) feeds the bees
**Answer: b) gives some control over the bees** — smoke gives the keeper some control.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) The hive tool is for —**
a) working the hives  b) sieving honey  c) wearing on the head
**Answer: a) working the hives** — the hive tool works the hives.

**5. (Sub lesson 2) Which tool serves wax?**
a) Wax extractor  b) Veil  c) Smoker
**Answer: a) Wax extractor** — the wax extractor is the wax tool.

**6. (Sub lesson 2) The uncapping fork and honey sieve belong to —**
a) protection  b) the honey harvest tools  c) site fencing
**Answer: b) the honey harvest tools** — they work the honey at harvest.
