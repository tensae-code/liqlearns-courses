# Beekeeping — the beekeeper's kit — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A sprayer is part of Table 3.2's kit.**
**Answer: True** — sprayer is pictured in the table.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Protective clothing prevents every possible sting.**
**Answer: False** — it prevents most stings — the book's exact claim.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) The queen excluder is worn by the beekeeper.**
**Answer: False** — it is a hive part in the kit, not clothing.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Table 3.2 lists eleven tools in all.**
**Answer: True** — veil, clothing, smoker, sprayer, hive tool, excluder, extractor, presser,
wax extractor, sieve, uncapping fork.
