# Beekeeping — the beekeeper's kit — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name five tools from Table 3.2.**
**Answer:** Any five of: veil, protective clothing, smoker, sprayer, hive tool, queen excluder,
honey extractor, honey presser, wax extractor, honey sieve, uncapping fork.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) State the text's reasoning for clothing, smoke and the hive tool.**
**Answer:** Protective clothing prevents most stings; smoke gives some control over the bees;
the hive tool works the hives.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Which tools work the honey harvest?**
**Answer:** Honey extractor, honey presser, honey sieve and uncapping fork.
