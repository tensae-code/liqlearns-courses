# Hands-on — Dairy — clean milk and keeping it fresh

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes. Real trade practice, done safely and exactly as
the book teaches it.

## Hand-washing drill

Practise the milker's wash: hands washed thoroughly with soap before any milking role-play or
real milking, containers and (on a model or with the keeper's direction) teats prepared —
hygiene as a habit, not a paragraph.

## Locality preservation report

Ask milk sellers and households how milk is preserved where you live, and compare each answer
with the book's two: water-cooling and refrigeration.
