# Lesson plan — Dairy — clean milk and keeping it fresh

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** Farm Trades · **Lesson:** Dairy — clean
milk and keeping it fresh
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Farm Trades (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** hygiene guards all milking — wash hands, containers and
teats, keep hair and dirt out, because contaminated milk carries diseases, some deadly — and
cooling is the main preservation, slowing bacterial growth.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on both milking lessons — milk is being drawn; now
keeping it safe and keeping it fresh.

- Recommended first: Dairy — milking by hand. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the hygiene rule: the milker washes hands, containers and teats, and keeps hair and
dirt out of the milk.
2. Say why: contaminated milk carries diseases, some deadly.
3. Say how cooling preserves milk and how the refrigerator works: it slows bacterial growth.
4. Describe the old water-cooling method: continuously pumped cold underground water in a
cooling barrel.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Soap and water for the hand-washing practice.
