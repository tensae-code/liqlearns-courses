# Dairy — clean milk and keeping it fresh — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) The milker must wash —**
a) hands, containers and teats  b) only the bucket  c) the barn floor
**Answer: a) hands, containers and teats** — all three are named.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) What must be kept out of the milk?**
a) Hair and dirt  b) Air bubbles  c) Foam
**Answer: a) Hair and dirt** — hair and dirt are the named dangers.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Contaminated milk is dangerous because it —**
a) tastes sour  b) carries diseases, some deadly  c) is too cold
**Answer: b) carries diseases, some deadly** — disease is the reason for the rule.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) The main way milk is preserved is —**
a) boiling it dry  b) cooling  c) salting
**Answer: b) cooling** — cooling extends its storage life.

**5. (Sub lesson 2) Before electricity, milk was cooled with —**
a) ice from mountains only  b) continuously pumped cold underground water in a cooling barrel
c) open air
**Answer: b) continuously pumped cold underground water in a cooling barrel** — the cooling
barrel and underground water are the book's old way.

**6. (Sub lesson 2) A refrigerator preserves milk because cold —**
a) kills all taste  b) slows bacterial growth  c) adds nutrients
**Answer: b) slows bacterial growth** — cold slows the bacteria.
