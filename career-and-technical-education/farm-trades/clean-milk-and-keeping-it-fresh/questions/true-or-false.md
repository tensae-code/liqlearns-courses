# Dairy — clean milk and keeping it fresh — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Hygiene matters only for hand milking.**
**Answer: False** — it guards both hand and machine milking.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Diseases carried by contaminated milk can be deadly.**
**Answer: True** — the book says some are deadly.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Cooling extends milk's storage life until use or transport.**
**Answer: True** — that is preservation's purpose here.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) The refrigerator works by adding chemicals to milk.**
**Answer: False** — it works by cold, slowing bacterial growth.
