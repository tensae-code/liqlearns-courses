# Dairy — clean milk and keeping it fresh — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) State the milker's hygiene rule in full.**
**Answer:** Wash hands, containers and teats, and keep hair and dirt out of the milk.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) How was milk cooled before electricity?**
**Answer:** With continuously pumped cold underground water in a cooling barrel.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Why does refrigeration keep milk longer?**
**Answer:** The cold slows bacterial growth.
