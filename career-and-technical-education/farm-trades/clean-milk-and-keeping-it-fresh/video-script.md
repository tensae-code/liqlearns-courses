# Video script — Dairy — clean milk and keeping it fresh

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on
list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Milk leaves the udder clean. Keep it clean.
Wash your hands. Wash the containers. Wash the teats.
Keep hair out. Keep dirt out.
Dirty milk carries disease — some of it deadly.
Then keep it cold.
Once, cold water ran through a barrel, day and night.
Today, the refrigerator hums — and the cold slows the bacteria.
Clean, then cold. That is the whole rule.
