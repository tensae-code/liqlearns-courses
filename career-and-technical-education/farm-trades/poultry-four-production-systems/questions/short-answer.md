# Poultry — the four production systems — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the four systems in order, loosest to tightest.**
**Answer:** Free-range extensive, backyard extensive, semi-intensive, intensive.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) How does semi-intensive mix the two sides?**
**Answer:** Birds are confined to a fenced area with shelter — neither fully free nor fully
housed.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) List three advantages of chickens.**
**Answer:** Any three of: short generation interval, high productivity, easy transport and
sale, low unit value, few religious taboos, fit with crop–livestock farming.
