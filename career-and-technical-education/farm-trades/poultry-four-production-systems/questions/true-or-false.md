# Poultry — the four production systems — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) In free-range extensive keeping, shelters are required by the system.**
**Answer: False** — shelters are optional; birds nest in the bush.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Backyard birds get a handful of grain morning and evening.**
**Answer: True** — that handful marks the backyard system.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Semi-intensive is found only in cities.**
**Answer: False** — it is common in urban, semi-urban and rural areas.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Intensive keeping costs more capital than the loose systems.**
**Answer: True** — medium-to-large enterprises run it, at more capital.
