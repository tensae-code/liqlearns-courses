# Poultry — the four production systems — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Free-range extensive birds —**
a) live in cages  b) are unconfined, scavenging widely, nesting in the bush  c) are fed three
times daily indoors
**Answer: b) are unconfined, scavenging widely, nesting in the bush** — unconfined and
scavenging defines it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Backyard extensive birds are —**
a) housed at night, free-ranging by day, given a handful of grain morning and evening  b) never
housed  c) kept in cages
**Answer: a) housed at night, free-ranging by day, given a handful of grain morning and
evening** — night house, day range, handful of grain.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) The traditional system is defended because it —**
a) uses imported feed  b) makes best use of local resources  c) needs most capital
**Answer: b) makes best use of local resources** — local resources are its defence.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Semi-intensive birds live —**
a) anywhere at all  b) confined to a fenced area with shelter  c) in the bush
**Answer: b) confined to a fenced area with shelter** — fenced area with shelter.

**5. (Sub lesson 2) Intensive production means birds are —**
a) fully confined in houses or cages, wholly dependent on the owner  b) free all day  c) kept
by villages only
**Answer: a) fully confined in houses or cages, wholly dependent on the owner** — full
confinement, more capital, total dependence.

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Which is one of chickens' advantages?**
a) Long generation interval  b) Short generation interval and high productivity  c) High unit
value
**Answer: b) Short generation interval and high productivity** — short generation and high
productivity are listed.
