# Hands-on — Poultry — the four production systems

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes. Real trade practice, done safely and exactly as
the book teaches it.

## System survey

Walk your locality (or interview keepers) and classify every flock you find into one of the
four systems, recording the evidence: housing, fencing, feeding.

## Home practice

Practise poultry keeping at home or in the school compound, whichever is convenient — the
guide's practical engagement (teacher guide — evidence, placement 11) — under the system your
place actually runs.
