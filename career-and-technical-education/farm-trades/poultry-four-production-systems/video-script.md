# Video script — Poultry — the four production systems

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on
list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Four ways to keep a chicken.
One: free — she scavenges the valley, sleeps in the bush.
Two: the backyard — a house at night, freedom by day,
a handful of grain, morning and evening.
Three: the fence — a run, a shelter, a mix of both.
Four: intensive — houses and cages, everything provided,
everything paid for.
Choose your system. Know its costs. Know its gifts.
