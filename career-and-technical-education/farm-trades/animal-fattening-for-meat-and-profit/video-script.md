# Video script — Animal fattening — feeding animals for meat and profit

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on
list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Feed. Rest. Grow. That is fattening.
The animal stays in one place. It does no farm work.
It eats, continuously, for a set time —
and turns feed into meat. Good meat. Much meat.
Ethiopia does it three ways:
the old way, after the ploughing;
the by-product way, on maize and soy;
and the Hararghe way.
People need protein. Demand climbs. Fattening answers.
