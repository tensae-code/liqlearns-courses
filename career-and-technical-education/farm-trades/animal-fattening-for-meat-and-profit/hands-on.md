# Hands-on — Animal fattening — feeding animals for meat and profit

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes. Real trade practice, done safely and exactly as
the book teaches it.

## Fattening site visit

Visit a fattening site in the locality to observe how the activities are performed — the
guide's site-visit method (teacher guide — evidence, placement 11); a local guest speaker is
the alternative.

## Condition comparison

From a keeper's herd or market visit, compare a working animal's condition with a fattening
animal's, and note the feeding and rest differences that explain it.
