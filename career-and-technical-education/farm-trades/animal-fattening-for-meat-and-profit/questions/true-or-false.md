# Animal fattening — feeding animals for meat and profit — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Demand for meat rises yearly while supply lags.**
**Answer: True** — that gap is the career opening.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Fattening animals should work hard to build muscle for meat.**
**Answer: False** — they do no farm work and move little.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Feedlots belong to the by-product-based system.**
**Answer: True** — the book includes feedlots there.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) (Teacher guide) Breed is one of the keyed selection criteria.**
**Answer: True** — age, sex, disposition, constitution and vigour, and breed are keyed.
