# Animal fattening — feeding animals for meat and profit — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Fattening is feeding animals —**
a) once a week while they work  b) continuously for a set period, kept in one place  c) only on
market days
**Answer: b) continuously for a set period, kept in one place** — continuous feeding for a set
period in one place.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) A fattening animal does —**
a) heavy farm work  b) no farm work and little movement  c) long daily walks
**Answer: b) no farm work and little movement** — rest turns feed into meat.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) The goal of fattening is —**
a) the greatest quantity of high-quality meat  b) faster ploughing  c) more milk
**Answer: a) the greatest quantity of high-quality meat** — meat, in quantity and quality.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) In the traditional system, oxen are sold —**
a) before ploughing, in top condition  b) after the ploughing season, in poor condition, fed
grass and some cereals  c) as calves
**Answer: b) after the ploughing season, in poor condition, fed grass and some cereals** —
after ploughing, in poor condition, is the traditional picture.

**5. (Sub lesson 2) The by-product-based system raises cattle on —**
a) grass only  b) maize, soy and other feeds, including feedlots  c) milk only
**Answer: b) maize, soy and other feeds, including feedlots** — maize, soy and other feeds;
feedlots belong here.

**6. (Sub lesson 2) The third Ethiopian system is named —**
a) Hararghe  b) Addis  c) Nile
**Answer: a) Hararghe** — the Hararghe fattening system.
