# Animal fattening — feeding animals for meat and profit — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Define fattening in one sentence.**
**Answer:** Feeding animals continuously for a set period, kept in one place with no farm work
and little movement, so they yield the greatest quantity of high-quality meat.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Name Ethiopia's three fattening systems.**
**Answer:** The traditional system, the by-product-based system, and the Hararghe fattening
system.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Why is fattening presented as a profitable career?**
**Answer:** Humans need meat's protein, demand rises yearly and supply lags, so fattening
offers a profitable, fast return.
