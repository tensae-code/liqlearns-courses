# Lesson plan — Animal fattening — feeding animals for meat and profit

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** Farm Trades · **Lesson:** Animal
fattening — feeding animals for meat and profit
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Farm Trades (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** fattening is feeding animals continuously for a set
period, kept in one place with no farm work and little movement, so they yield the greatest
quantity of high-quality meat — in Ethiopia's three systems: traditional, by-product-based, and
Hararghe.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the dairy lessons — cattle are known from the milk
side; now the meat side.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below,
the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written
as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**.
The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books;
on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as
the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its
source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead,
each opening when the one before it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub
Lessons (ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **What fattening is — and why it pays** — Fattening is feeding animals continuously for a
set period, kept in one place with no farm work and little movement, so they yield the greatest
quantity of high-quality meat.
2. **Ethiopia's three systems** — The traditional system: oxen sold after the ploughing season,
in poor condition, fed grass and some cereals.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Define fattening exactly: continuous feeding, set period, one place, no farm work, little
movement, greatest quantity of high-quality meat.
2. Say why fattening is offered as a career: meat's protein, rising demand, lagging supply,
profitable fast return.
3. Name Ethiopia's three systems and tell them apart.
4. Recite the selection criteria (teacher guide key): age, sex, disposition, constitution and
vigour, breed.

## If the learner is stuck
- Confuses fattening with ordinary keeping → the marks are: continuous feeding, a set period,
one place, no work.
- Mixes the systems → anchor each to its feed and timing: after-ploughing grass (traditional),
maize/soy feedlots (by-product), Hararghe (named third).
