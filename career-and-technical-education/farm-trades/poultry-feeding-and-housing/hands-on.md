# Hands-on — Poultry — feeding and housing your flock

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes. Real trade practice, done safely and exactly as
the book teaches it.

## Feed collection survey

Gather or list actual samples of local chicken feeds and sort them under the seven sources;
note which source your locality lacks and what replaces it.

## Housing improvement

Choose one real flock's housing and make one improvement toward the four requirements — more
ventilation, better predator proofing — as the keeper directs.
