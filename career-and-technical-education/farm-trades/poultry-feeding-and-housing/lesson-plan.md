# Lesson plan — Poultry — feeding and housing your flock

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** Farm Trades · **Lesson:** Poultry —
feeding and housing your flock
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Farm Trades (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** egg production tracks feed and nutrient intake; chicken
feeds come from seven local sources; and housing — from inside the family house to a purpose-
built shelter — must always provide space, ventilation, light, and protection from weather and
predators.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the choosing-birds lesson — good birds chosen; now
feeding and housing them.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below,
the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written
as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**.
The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books;
on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as
the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its
source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say the feed rule: egg production tracks feed and nutrient intake.
2. Name the feed sources: household cooking waste, cereals and cereal by-products, roots and
tubers, oilseeds, trees and shrubs, animal proteins, commercially prepared feed.
3. State housing's four non-negotiables: space, ventilation, light, protection from weather and
predators.
4. Say the range of housing: inside the family house (even under a bed, against theft) to
purpose-built shelter.

## If the learner is stuck
- Treats housing as a building type → judge any shelter by the four functions, not by how it
looks.
- Forgets a feed source → chant the seven as a shopping list: waste, cereals, roots, oilseeds,
greens, animal protein, bought feed.
