# Video script — Poultry — feeding and housing your flock

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on
list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Feed the hen, and she feeds you.
Eggs follow feed — remember that.
Kitchen waste. Cereals. Roots. Oilseeds.
Greens from trees and shrubs. Animal protein. Bought feed.
Seven sources. Use your locality's mix.
And the house? Four things, always:
space, air, light — and protection
from weather and from predators.
Even under a bed counts — if the four are there.
