# Poultry — feeding and housing your flock — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Roots, tubers and oilseeds can feed chickens.**
**Answer: True** — both are named sources.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) A poorly fed hen lays the same as a well-fed one.**
**Answer: False** — egg production tracks feed and nutrient intake.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) A beautiful house without ventilation passes the book's test.**
**Answer: False** — ventilation is one of the four always-required things.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Trees and shrubs are among the feed sources.**
**Answer: True** — trees and shrubs are named.
