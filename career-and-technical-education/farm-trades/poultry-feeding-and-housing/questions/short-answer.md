# Poultry — feeding and housing your flock — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name four sources of chicken feed.**
**Answer:** Any four of: household cooking waste, cereals and cereal by-products, roots and
tubers, oilseeds, trees and shrubs, animal proteins, commercially prepared feed.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) State housing's four requirements.**
**Answer:** Space, ventilation, light, and protection from weather and predators.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) How far does the housing range run?**
**Answer:** From inside the family house (even under a bed, against theft) to purpose-built
shelter.
