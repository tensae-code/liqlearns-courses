# Poultry — feeding and housing your flock — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Egg production tracks —**
a) the weather only  b) feed and nutrient intake  c) the cock's crowing
**Answer: b) feed and nutrient intake** — feed and nutrients drive laying.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Which is a named chicken-feed source?**
a) Household cooking waste  b) Plastic  c) Sand
**Answer: a) Household cooking waste** — cooking waste is a source, with six others.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Cereals reach chickens also as —**
a) cereal by-products  b) cereal boxes  c) cereal adverts
**Answer: a) cereal by-products** — cereals and cereal by-products are both named.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Housing must always provide —**
a) space, ventilation, light, and protection from weather and predators  b) only a roof  c) a
lock only
**Answer: a) space, ventilation, light, and protection from weather and predators** — the four
are non-negotiable.

**5. (Sub lesson 2) The simplest housing named is —**
a) a palace  b) inside the family house, even under a bed, against theft  c) a cage on a roof
**Answer: b) inside the family house, even under a bed, against theft** — under a bed, against
theft, is the book's own example.

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Protection in the four means protection from —**
a) weather and predators  b) noise  c) sunlight for laying
**Answer: a) weather and predators** — weather and predators are the named dangers.
