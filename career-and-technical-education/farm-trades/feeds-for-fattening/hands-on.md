# Hands-on — Fattening — feeds: concentrates and roughages

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes. Real trade practice, done safely and exactly as
the book teaches it.

## Feed-sample sorting

Lay out real local feed samples (or labelled drawings) and sort them by the definitions —
protein/carbohydrate-rich against fibre-rich — defending each placement aloud.

## Keeper's ration interview

Ask a fattener what their ration is made of, which parts are bought and which are by-products,
and whether supplements are added. Map the answers onto the two families.
