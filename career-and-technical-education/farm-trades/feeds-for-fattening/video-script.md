# Video script — Fattening — feeds: concentrates and roughages

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on
list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Two families of feed.
Concentrates: rich in protein, rich in energy.
Barley is the king of the lot-feeding grains.
Oats? Mix it — alone, it is not enough.
Roughages: full of fibre.
Pasture. Hay — grass mowed and dried for winter.
Silage — green feed kept by fermentation.
Mix the families. Add the vitamins and minerals.
That is a ration.
