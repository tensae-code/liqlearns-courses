# Fattening — feeds: concentrates and roughages — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Concentrates are high in —**
a) fibre  b) protein or carbohydrate  c) water
**Answer: b) protein or carbohydrate** — that is their definition.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) The best lot-feeding grain named is —**
a) oats  b) barley  c) rice
**Answer: b) barley** — barley is named the best.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Oats alone is —**
a) perfect  b) imperfect, so it is mixed with other grains  c) forbidden
**Answer: b) imperfect, so it is mixed with other grains** — oats is mixed with other grains.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Roughages are high in —**
a) fibre  b) protein  c) sugar
**Answer: a) fibre** — fibre defines roughages.

**5. (Sub lesson 2) Hay is —**
a) green herbage fermented  b) mowed, dried forage  c) a kind of grain
**Answer: b) mowed, dried forage** — mowed and dried; the main winter nutrient source.

**6. (Sub lesson 2) Silage is preserved by —**
a) sun-drying  b) controlled fermentation  c) freezing
**Answer: b) controlled fermentation** — high-moisture herbage, preserved by controlled
fermentation.
