# Fattening — feeds: concentrates and roughages — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Wheat, sorghum and maize can all be lot-feeding grains.**
**Answer: True** — all are named beside barley and oats.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Home-mixed rations need no supplements.**
**Answer: False** — they need vitamin and mineral supplements.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Pasture is a concentrate.**
**Answer: False** — pasture is a roughage — high in fibre.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Silage can be made from forage or grain crops.**
**Answer: True** — both are named.
