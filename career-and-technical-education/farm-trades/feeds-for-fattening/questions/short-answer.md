# Fattening — feeds: concentrates and roughages — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Give the two feed families and their definitions.**
**Answer:** Concentrates — high in protein or carbohydrate; roughages — high in fibre (pasture,
hay, silage).

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Define hay and silage, one line each.**
**Answer:** Hay: mowed, dried forage. Silage: high-moisture herbage preserved by controlled
fermentation.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) What must be added to home-mixed rations?**
**Answer:** Vitamin and mineral supplements.
