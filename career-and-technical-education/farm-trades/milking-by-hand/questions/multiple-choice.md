# Dairy — milking by hand — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Milk is made in —**
a) the stomach  b) the mammary glands  c) the horns
**Answer: b) the mammary glands** — milk is the nutrient-rich food made in the mammary glands.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Locally, hand milking is done —**
a) once a week, outdoors  b) twice a day, indoors, morning and evening  c) only at noon
**Answer: b) twice a day, indoors, morning and evening** — twice daily, indoors, is the book's
picture.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) During milking the cow is —**
a) left wandering  b) tied or held in a stanchion in the barn  c) taken to market
**Answer: b) tied or held in a stanchion in the barn** — tied or stanchioned in the barn.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Both hand methods begin by —**
a) pinching the top of the teat shut to trap milk  b) pulling the teat hard  c) washing the
bucket
**Answer: a) pinching the top of the teat shut to trap milk** — trapping the milk is the shared
first move.

**5. (Sub lesson 2) In the squeeze method, after the pinch —**
a) the fingers slide down  b) the other fingers squeeze so milk squirts from the tip  c) the
hand lets go
**Answer: b) the other fingers squeeze so milk squirts from the tip** — squeeze with the other
fingers; milk squirts out.

**6. (Sub lesson 2) In the slide method, the fingers —**
a) slide down the teat, pushing milk down and out  b) stay still  c) pinch the tip only
**Answer: a) slide down the teat, pushing milk down and out** — the slide pushes the milk down
and out.
