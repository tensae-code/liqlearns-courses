# Dairy — milking by hand — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Milking can be done by hand or by machine.**
**Answer: True** — both are named; hand is the local way.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Milking happens once a day, at midday.**
**Answer: False** — twice a day — morning and evening.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) The pinch at the top of the teat traps milk in its lower part.**
**Answer: True** — that trapped milk is then squeezed or pushed out.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) The two hand methods are exactly the same movement.**
**Answer: False** — one squeezes, the other slides; only the opening pinch is shared.
