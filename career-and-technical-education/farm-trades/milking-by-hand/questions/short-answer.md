# Dairy — milking by hand — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Where and when is hand milking done locally?**
**Answer:** Indoors, twice a day — morning and evening — with the cow tied or held in a
stanchion in the barn.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Describe the pinch-and-squeeze method.**
**Answer:** Pinch the top of the teat shut between fingers and thumb to trap milk in the lower
part, then squeeze with the other fingers so milk squirts out of the tip into the bucket.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) How does the slide method differ?**
**Answer:** Instead of squeezing in place, the fingers slide down the teat, pushing the milk
down and out.
