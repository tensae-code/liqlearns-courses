# Lesson plan — Dairy — milking by hand

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** Farm Trades · **Lesson:** Dairy —
milking by hand
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Farm Trades (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** hand milking works the teat two ways — pinch the top
shut and squeeze so milk squirts out, or pinch the top shut and slide the fingers down — done
indoors, twice a day, morning and evening.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the Dairy farming lesson — the learner knows the
business; now the hands-on skill at its centre.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below,
the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written
as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**.
The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books;
on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as
the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its
source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead,
each opening when the one before it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub
Lessons (ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **Milk and milking** — Milk is the nutrient-rich food made in the mammary glands.
2. **The two hand methods** — Method one — pinch and squeeze: pinch the top of the teat shut
between fingers and thumb, to trap milk in its lower part; then squeeze with the other fingers,
so milk squirts out of the tip into the bucket.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Dairy farming — milk as a business. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what milk and milking are.
2. Say where and when hand milking is done locally: indoors, twice a day, the cow tied or held
in a stanchion.
3. Perform the two hand methods in words and in practice: pinch-and-squeeze, pinch-and-slide.

## What you need
For practice: a cow and milking place arranged by a supervising adult, a bucket, and water for
washing hands. Paper and a pencil for the written work.

## If the learner is stuck
- Pulls the teat instead of trapping milk → go back to the secret: nothing comes out well until
the top is pinched shut.
- Loses rhythm → slow count aloud: pinch, squeeze, release — steady, like a heartbeat.
