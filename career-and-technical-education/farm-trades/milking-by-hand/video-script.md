# Video script — Dairy — milking by hand

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on
list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Hands washed. Bucket ready. The cow stands quiet.
Morning and evening — milking time.
Watch the hand. First, pinch the top of the teat shut.
The milk is trapped below.
Now squeeze — the milk squirts into the bucket.
Again: pinch, squeeze. Pinch, squeeze.
Or pinch — and slide your fingers down,
pushing the milk out.
Two methods. One rule: trap the milk first.
