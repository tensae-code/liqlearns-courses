# Hands-on — Dairy — milking by hand

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes. Real trade practice, done safely and exactly as
the book teaches it.

## Milking practice

Under a supervising adult, at a real milking: wash hands first (the clean-milk rule), then milk
using the pinch-and-squeeze method, changing to pinch-and-slide, keeping the rhythm steady so
milk jets into the bucket. The animal is handled exactly as its keeper directs.

## Rhythm drill on a model

Practise the pinch-trap-squeeze sequence in the air or on a soft model teat until the rhythm is
even — trap, squeeze, release; trap, squeeze, release — as the GIF shows.
