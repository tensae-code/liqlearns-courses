# Your Personal Profile — Introducing Yourself on Paper — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A personal profile is —
a) a long life story  b) a short introduction setting out who a person is, plus their qualities, education, experience and skills  c) a price list
**Answer: b) a short introduction setting out who a person is, plus their qualities, education, experience and skills**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Before hiring, employers use a profile to judge —
a) your handwriting only  b) whether you have the skills, knowledge and experience the job needs  c) your family's wealth
**Answer: b) whether you have the skills, knowledge and experience the job needs**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Move 1 of writing a profile is —
a) list every hobby since childhood  b) describe yourself briefly — name, address, age, sex as the purpose requires  c) exaggerate your skills
**Answer: b) describe yourself briefly — name, address, age, sex as the purpose requires**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A good profile must stay —
a) long and flowery  b) short and concise, so it can be read quickly  c) secret
**Answer: b) short and concise, so it can be read quickly**
