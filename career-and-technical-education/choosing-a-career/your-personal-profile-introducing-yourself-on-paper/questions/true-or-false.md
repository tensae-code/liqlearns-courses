# Your Personal Profile — Introducing Yourself on Paper — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** "DON'T exaggerate details" is one of the profile rules.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Cooking, translating, singing and writing can be profile skills.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A profile should use flowery language to impress.
**Answer: False** — the rule is: don't use flowery language; be direct to the point.
