# Your Personal Profile — Introducing Yourself on Paper — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the three moves of writing a job profile, in order.
**Answer: (1) Describe yourself briefly; (2) state your skills and abilities; (3) include education and experience.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Besides jobs, where does a profile introduce you?
**Answer: In personal relationships.**
