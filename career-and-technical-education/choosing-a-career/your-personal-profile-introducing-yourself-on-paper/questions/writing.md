# Your Personal Profile — Introducing Yourself on Paper — writing

Write as directed. Your teacher (or the app) checks the writing against the lesson's rules.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Write move 2 for yourself: state your skills and abilities — at least two strengths and one character statement (for example hard-working, diligent, cooperative).
**Done when: two named strengths plus one character statement, stated briefly.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Write move 3 for yourself: your education and any experience, including voluntary work.
**Done when: schooling level plus any task experience, even voluntary, stated briefly.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Proofread a partner's three moves against the DOs and DON'Ts card and mark every breach you find.
**Done when: each breach correctly tied to a DON'T (flowery, redundant, exaggerated, too long) or a missing DO.**
