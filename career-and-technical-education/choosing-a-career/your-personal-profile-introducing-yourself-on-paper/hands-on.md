# Hands-on — Your Personal Profile — Introducing Yourself on Paper

Real paper, real checking — write it, submit it, get it checked, exactly as the guide's method runs it.

## Write and submit for checking
Write your full profile on real paper in the three moves. Submit it to a teacher, elder or companion playing checker, who marks it against the DOs and DON'Ts card and hands it back with the breaches named. Rewrite once, clean. This checked submission is the guide's own method (teacher guide — evidence).

## Fill a real form by hand, in step with the motion
Draw the form's three sections on paper with a ruler. Fill section 1 only; stop. Fill section 2; stop. Fill section 3. Working section by section — never all at once — is the hand skill this lesson's GIF shows, practised with your own pen.
