# Video script — Your Personal Profile — Introducing Yourself on Paper

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Your profile is you, on one short page.

Move one: who you are. Name, place, school.
Move two: what you can do. Skills. Character.
Move three: what you have learned and done.

Keep it short. Keep it true. Keep it direct.
Do proofread. Don't exaggerate.

Now write yours — section by section.
