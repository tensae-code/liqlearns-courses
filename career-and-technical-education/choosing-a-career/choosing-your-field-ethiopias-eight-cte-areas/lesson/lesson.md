## Two streams, eight areas

Secondary CTE splits into **two streams**:

- The **Natural Sciences stream** — it studies the **physical world**. Its areas: **manufacturing, agriculture, construction, information technology, health sciences**.
- The **Social Sciences stream** — it studies **society and relationships**. Its areas: **business sciences, language and social sciences, performing and visual arts**.

That is **eight occupational areas in all**. Each area holds its own sub-specialisations, and the areas are **studied in depth in Grades 11–12**.
