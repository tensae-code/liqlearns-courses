# Hands-on — Choosing Your Field — Ethiopia's Eight CTE Areas

Real doors in your real town — go and find which areas actually stand near you.

## Door hunt in your locality
On paper, list the eight areas. Walk your locality (with an adult where needed) and find one real workplace for as many areas as you can — a workshop, a farm, a building site, a clinic, a shop office, a musician. Photograph nothing; write address-by-description and the work done. Report honestly which doors have no workplace near you.

## Ask behind one door
Choose one workplace from your hunt and ask its worker: "What area and sub-area is your work?" Write their answer and compare it with the map in this lesson.
