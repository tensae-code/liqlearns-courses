# Lesson plan — Choosing Your Field — Ethiopia's Eight CTE Areas

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** Choosing a Career · **Lesson:** Choosing Your Field — Ethiopia's Eight CTE Areas
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 2 — Choosing a Career (CURRICULUM.md / TITLES.md ladder).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** secondary CTE splits into two streams holding eight occupational areas in all, studied in depth in Grades 11–12.
**This lesson's own level:** It stands on First Steps: the learner knows what CTE is; now they see its map of fields before touring each one.

## Sub lessons
This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short, single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09).

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: What CTE Is — Learning Joined to Doing. Recommended, never forced. The next three lessons tour the eight areas.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the two streams and what each studies.
2. Place all eight areas in their streams.
3. Say when the areas are studied in depth: Grades 11–12.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil.

## If the learner is stuck
- Mixes the streams → anchor by the question: does it study the physical world (Natural) or society and relationships (Social)?
- Tries to memorise sub-areas here → this lesson is the map only; each area gets its tour in the next lessons.
