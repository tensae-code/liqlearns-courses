# Choosing Your Field — Ethiopia's Eight CTE Areas — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** Secondary CTE has how many occupational areas in all?
a) Five  b) Eight  c) Twelve
**Answer: b) Eight**

**2. ** Which stream studies the physical world?
a) Social Sciences  b) Natural Sciences  c) Neither
**Answer: b) Natural Sciences**

**3. ** Health sciences belongs to the —
a) Natural Sciences stream  b) Social Sciences stream  c) No stream
**Answer: a) Natural Sciences stream**

**4. ** The eight areas are studied in depth in —
a) Grades 1–2  b) Grades 11–12  c) No grades
**Answer: b) Grades 11–12**
