# Hands-on — CTE Areas — Making and Building

Real materials in your real hands — trace one good from raw to finished, and audit one real building.

## Raw-to-finished trace
Pick one finished good at home. Find or name its raw material. Write the chain in order — material, processing steps, tools, finished good — then ask the person who made, sold or repaired such a good whether your chain is right. Correct it in a different colour.

## Four-stage building audit
Stand outside a real building (with an adult where needed). Find visible evidence of each construction sub-area: one electrical fitting, one plumbing fitting, one carpentry joint or fitting, one finishing surface. Sketch each in a four-box grid, labelled by sub-area.
