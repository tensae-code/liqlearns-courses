# Lesson plan — CTE Areas — Making and Building

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** Choosing a Career · **Lesson:** CTE Areas — Making and Building
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 2 — Choosing a Career (CURRICULUM.md / TITLES.md ladder).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** manufacturing turns raw materials into finished goods, and construction builds and finishes the built world — four sub-areas each.
**This lesson's own level:** It stands on Choosing Your Field: the learner has the eight-door map; now they tour its first two doors in depth.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an
image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture
of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does
with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and
while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is
dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the
student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph,
or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be
shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees
from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before
it is proved:

1. **Manufacturing — raw material in, finished goods out** — Sugarcane into sugar, cotton into a jacket; the four sub-areas: metal, automotive, textile and leather garment, wood-work.
2. **Construction — building, wiring, plumbing, finishing** — Planning to demolition; the four sub-areas: electricity, plumbing, carpentry, building finishing works.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Choosing Your Field — Ethiopia's Eight CTE Areas. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Define manufacturing and give both worked examples.
2. Name manufacturing's four sub-areas with one product each.
3. Name construction's span (planning to demolition) and its four sub-areas.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil; one raw material and one finished good from home to hold side by side (for example, cloth and a garment).
