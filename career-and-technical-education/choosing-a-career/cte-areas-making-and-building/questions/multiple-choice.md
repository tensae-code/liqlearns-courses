# CTE Areas — Making and Building — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Manufacturing turns
a) finished goods into raw material  b) raw materials into finished goods  c) money into land
**Answer: b) raw materials into finished goods**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Gold and silversmith jewellery belongs to
a) metal manufacturing  b) plumbing  c) wood-work
**Answer: a) metal manufacturing**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Building and repairing vehicles — engines, brakes, painting — is
a) textile work  b) automotive technology  c) carpentry
**Answer: b) automotive technology**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Plastering, tiling and painting a finished building is
a) plumbing  b) building finishing works  c) electricity
**Answer: b) building finishing works**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Laying and repairing water pipes, tanks and taps is
a) plumbing  b) carpentry  c) demolishing
**Answer: a) plumbing**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Construction's work runs from planning to
a) planting  b) finally demolishing  c) selling clothes
**Answer: b) finally demolishing**
