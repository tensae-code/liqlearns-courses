# CTE Areas — Making and Building — sub lessons and depth

This is the depth record for the lesson, re-expressed as sub lessons. Each sub lesson opens when the one before it is proved (gate pool: the sub lesson's whole question set).

## Sub lesson 1 — Manufacturing — raw material in, finished goods out
Pass mark: the learner can define manufacturing, give both worked examples, and name its four sub-areas with a product each.

## Sub lesson 2 — Construction — building, wiring, plumbing, finishing
Pass mark: the learner can state construction's full span and name its four sub-areas with their work.
