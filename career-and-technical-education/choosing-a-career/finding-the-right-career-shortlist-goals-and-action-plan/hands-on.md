# Hands-on — Finding the Right Career — Shortlist, Goals and Action Plan

Real paper, real people — cut a real shortlist and ask a real worker, then write the real plan.

## Cut the real shortlist on lined paper
On lined paper, write your master list in full. Cross out by exploring, not by mood: beside each cut, write the basic information that cut it. Stop at five-to-ten, then narrow to two-to-five. Keep the crossed-out list — it is evidence your choice was earned (the guide's paper-shortlist method, teacher guide — evidence).

## Firsthand interview and plan
Interview one person already in a shortlisted career (or host a guest speaker with your group): qualifications needed, market demand, opportunities, one hard truth. Then write your A-to-D action plan on one page, goals beside it, and read it aloud to your group for their questions.
