# Video script — Finding the Right Career — Shortlist, Goals and Action Plan

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Seven steps to the right career.

List everything. Explore it — ten, five, fewer.
Narrow to a shortlist you can hold in one hand.
Ask the people who DO those careers. Listen firsthand.
Choose the one that promises most joy in the work.
Set your goals — near ones, far ones.
Write the plan: A, B, C, D — your road, on paper.

And remember: you may change your mind. A plan guides; it does not chain.
