# Finding the Right Career — Shortlist, Goals and Action Plan — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Step 1 combines every self-assessment list into —
a) a diary  b) one master list of occupations to explore  c) a CV
**Answer: b) one master list of occupations to explore**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Exploring cuts the master list to —
a) five to ten options  b) one option  c) fifty options
**Answer: a) five to ten options**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Narrowing leaves a shortlist of —
a) twenty  b) two to five  c) none
**Answer: b) two to five**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In-depth information comes best from —
a) rumours  b) people already in those careers, asked for firsthand knowledge  c) nobody
**Answer: b) people already in those careers, asked for firsthand knowledge**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Long-term goals run at roughly —
a) three to five years  b) three to five days  c) thirty years exactly
**Answer: a) three to five years**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** A career action plan is —
a) a roadmap document from point A to B to C to D  b) a shopping list  c) an exam
**Answer: a) a roadmap document from point A to B to C to D**
