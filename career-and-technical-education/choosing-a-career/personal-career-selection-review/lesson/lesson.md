# Personal Career Selection — Review

## Do it first

Lay out your three papers from this title side by side: your personal profile, your shortlist, and your action plan. If any paper is missing, this review will find the gap — that is what a review is for. Make the missing paper first.

## The four doors, once more

[Picture 11817 — the four steps as four doors]

1. **Research** — list ALL careers actually available near you, comprehensively, before judging.
2. **Evaluate** — compare against resources, knowledge, skills and experience; narrow to five with qualifications.
3. **Discuss** — chosen against non-chosen, with peers; a favourite may honourably fall here.
4. **Decide** — ONE career that fits — then the finer funnel (master list → 5–10 → shortlist 2–5), firsthand information, goals (long-term roughly 3–5 years; short-term 6 months–3 years), and the **A-to-D action plan**.

Behind the doors stand the title's supports: the **eight CTE areas in two streams**, the **soft skills** that travel everywhere (with creative thinking and the five problem-solving steps), and your **personal profile**, written in three moves and checked against the DOs and DON'Ts.

The review questions for this lesson are filed from the books' own Unit 1 review set (Grade 8) and verified against the teacher guides' answer keys (teacher guide — evidence): true/false on doing every career vs listing local opportunities; fill-ins for the definitions of career selection and career researching; multiple choice on what researching, narrowing, information-gathering and goal-setting each accomplish.

## Say it to your companion

Walk your companion through your own four doors: your list's count, your five, your discussion's survivor, your decision. They check each claim against your papers.

## Do It Yourself

1. Answer the full question set for this lesson without your papers. Mark honestly.
2. Re-open your papers and correct every miss in another colour, naming the lesson each correction comes from.
3. Finish with the title's product on one page: chosen career, one long-term goal, one short-term goal, and stops A–D.
