# Lesson plan — Personal Career Selection — Review

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** Choosing a Career · **Lesson:** Personal Career Selection — Review
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 2 — Choosing a Career (CURRICULUM.md / TITLES.md ladder). The title's assessment lesson.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the whole choosing method, proved: research, evaluation, discussion, decision, the funnel, goals, the plan — plus the areas, soft skills and profile it stands on.
**This lesson's own level:** It stands on every Choosing a Career lesson before it; it teaches nothing new — it proves what the title taught, as the books' own unit reviews do.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an
image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture
of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does
with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and
while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is
dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the
student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph,
or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be
shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short, single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09).

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: all of Choosing a Career, in ladder order. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Answer the unit's review set across all four kinds (true/false, fill-in, multiple choice, written).
2. Show a decided career and a written action plan as the title's final evidence.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil; the learner's own shortlist, profile and action plan from the earlier lessons.

## If the learner is stuck
- Fails the funnel numbers → return to Finding the Right Career, Sub lesson 1: all → 5–10 → 2–5, out loud, three times.
- Has answers but no plan → the plan IS the title's product; draft A-to-D before this lesson counts as passed.
