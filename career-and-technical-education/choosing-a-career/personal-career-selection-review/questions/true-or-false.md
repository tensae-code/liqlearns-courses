# Personal Career Selection — Review — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. ** You must do every career you research.
**Answer: False** — research lists comprehensively; selection chooses one.

**2. ** Career researching means listing local opportunities before judging them.
**Answer: True**

**3. ** A personal profile should be long and flowery.
**Answer: False** — short, concise, direct — and proofread.
