# Personal Career Selection — Review — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer. Every question checks the Choosing a Career lessons.

**1. ** Researching career opportunities accomplishes —
a) a comprehensive list of careers available in your locality  b) an instant choice  c) a finished CV
**Answer: a) a comprehensive list of careers available in your locality**

**2. ** Narrowing a list accomplishes —
a) a shortlist of two to five from the explored options  b) a longer list  c) no decision
**Answer: a) a shortlist of two to five from the explored options**

**3. ** Gathering in-depth information means —
a) guessing harder  b) asking people already in the career for firsthand knowledge  c) reading nothing
**Answer: b) asking people already in the career for firsthand knowledge**

**4. ** Goal-setting in this unit divides goals into —
a) rich and poor  b) long-term (roughly 3–5 years) and short-term (6 months–3 years)  c) mine and yours
**Answer: b) long-term (roughly 3–5 years) and short-term (6 months–3 years)**

**5. ** Soft skills differ from hard skills because they —
a) belong to one job only  b) travel to every career  c) need no practice
**Answer: b) travel to every career**

**6. ** Ethiopia's CTE areas number —
a) eight, in two streams  b) three  c) twenty
**Answer: a) eight, in two streams**
