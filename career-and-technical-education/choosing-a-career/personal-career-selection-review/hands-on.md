# Hands-on — Personal Career Selection — Review

Real papers, real proof — the title's products checked like real work is checked.

## The three-paper audit
Exchange profile, shortlist and action plan with a companion. Audit theirs like an employer and a checker in one: profile against the DOs and DON'Ts card; shortlist against the funnel numbers; plan against A-to-D with both goal horizons. Sign and date the audit. Return, correct, re-submit — the checked-submission habit, applied to the whole title.

## Defend your decision in a real conversation
Present your decided career to a working adult. They ask Sarkabo's question: why this one alone? Answer with your sums, your research or your firsthand interview — evidence, not wishes. Write down their verdict in their words.
