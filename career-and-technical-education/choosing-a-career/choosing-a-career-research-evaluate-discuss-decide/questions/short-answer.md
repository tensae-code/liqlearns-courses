# Choosing a Career — Research, Evaluate, Discuss, Decide — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How does the guide run Step 1 in class, and with what timing? (teacher guide — evidence)
**Answer: Brainstorm local career options for 2–3 minutes and reflect on the list; then work individually and in groups of four to six (discussion 5–7 minutes).**

**2. (Sub lesson 4)** What is the Sarkabo story's lesson in one sentence?
**Answer: Focus after comparison beats doing everything badly — he spread himself across crops, poultry and a shop, found he could not manage all profitably, and committed to poultry alone.**
**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Evaluation aims to understand what, instead of fixating on one career?
**Answer: A collection of careers in detail — compared against resources and the knowledge, skills and experience each requires.**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** In discussion, what three things do pupils do with the shortlisted careers?
**Answer: Say what they liked about each, compare chosen against non-chosen, and test the requirements and possibilities of a shared interest (learning new job tracks from peers).**
