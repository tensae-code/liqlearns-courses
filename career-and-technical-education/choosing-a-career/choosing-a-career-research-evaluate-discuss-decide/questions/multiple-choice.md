# Choosing a Career — Research, Evaluate, Discuss, Decide — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In Step 1, the local career list must be —
a) short — only careers you already like  b) comprehensive — ALL careers actually available in your area  c) copied from another town
**Answer: b) comprehensive — ALL careers actually available in your area**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Animal production, crop production, manufacturing, construction and services are —
a) the local career families named for researching  b) school subjects only  c) streams
**Answer: a) the local career families named for researching**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Evaluation compares careers against —
a) their colours  b) availability of resources, and the knowledge, skills and experience each requires  c) their names
**Answer: b) availability of resources, and the knowledge, skills and experience each requires**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The evaluation drill narrows the class list to —
a) fifty careers  b) five careers, with the qualification each demands  c) one career at once
**Answer: b) five careers, with the qualification each demands**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** At the discussion step, a preferred choice —
a) can never change  b) may be dropped — and dropping it is part of the process  c) must be hidden
**Answer: b) may be dropped — and dropping it is part of the process**

**6. (Sub lesson 4)** Sarkabo finally committed to —
a) doing everything at once  b) poultry production alone  c) closing his farm
**Answer: b) poultry production alone**
