# Video script — Choosing a Career — Research, Evaluate, Discuss, Decide

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Choosing is a method. Four steps.

One: research. List EVERY career near you. No judging yet.
Two: evaluate. Five careers left. What does each one demand?
Three: discuss. Compare. Listen. A favourite may fall here — let it.
Four: decide. ONE career.

Sarkabo tried everything, and his sums said no.
He kept the poultry — and thrived on focus.
