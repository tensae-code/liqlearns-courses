# Hands-on — Choosing a Career — Research, Evaluate, Discuss, Decide

Real lists from real streets, real sums — research with your feet, decide with numbers.

## Build the real locality list
In your group of three, walk or recall street by street and list every career actually practised in your area, grouped under animal production, crop production, manufacturing, construction and services. Count each family. A family with zero entries means more research, not a finished list.

## Do Sarkabo's sums on a real small venture
Pick one tiny real venture you can cost honestly (for example, keeping a few chickens). Write its income and its costs for one month, from real local prices you ask for. Subtract. Would adding a second and third venture at the same time help or break it? Write the verdict your sums give — not the one you wanted.
