# Lesson plan — Choosing a Career — Research, Evaluate, Discuss, Decide

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** Choosing a Career · **Lesson:** Choosing a Career — Research, Evaluate, Discuss, Decide
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 2 — Choosing a Career (CURRICULUM.md / TITLES.md ladder). The Grade 8 book's four-step choosing method.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** choose by method, not by mood: research everything near you, evaluate it honestly, discuss it openly, then decide on one — as Sarkabo learned.
**This lesson's own level:** It stands on the eight-areas tour and the personal profile: the learner knows the fields and can present themselves; now they choose, by the Grade 8 book's steps.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees
from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before
it is proved:

1. **Step 1 — Research what exists near you** — List ALL careers actually available in your area, comprehensively, before judging any.
2. **Step 2 — Evaluate and compare** — Compare the list against resources, knowledge, skills and experience; narrow to five with their qualifications.
3. **Step 3 — Discuss it with others** — Say what you liked, compare chosen against non-chosen, learn new tracks — a favourite may be dropped here.
4. **Step 4 — Decide on one (the Sarkabo story)** — Select ONE career that fits — focus after comparison beats doing everything badly.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Choosing Your Field — Ethiopia's Eight CTE Areas. Recommended, never forced. The finer seven-step method follows in Finding the Right Career.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Build a comprehensive local career list in a group of three.
2. Evaluate to five careers with each one's qualification.
3. Discuss chosen against non-chosen, ready to drop a favourite.
4. Decide on one career and retell why Sarkabo did.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil; companions to research and discuss with (the book's groups of three; the guide's groups of four to six for discussion).

## If the learner is stuck
- Lists only liked careers in Step 1 → comprehensively means ALL careers actually available, judged later; a disliked career on the list costs nothing and teaches much.
- Refuses to drop a favourite in Step 3 → dropping at discussion is the process working, not failing — Sarkabo dropped four ventures and kept his livelihood.
