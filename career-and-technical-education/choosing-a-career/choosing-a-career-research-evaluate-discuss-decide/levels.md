# Choosing a Career — Research, Evaluate, Discuss, Decide — sub lessons and depth

This is the depth record for the lesson, re-expressed as sub lessons. Each sub lesson opens when the one before it is proved (gate pool: the sub lesson's whole question set).

## Sub lesson 1 — Step 1 — Research what exists near you
Pass mark: the learner can build a comprehensive local list in a group of three, across the five named families.

## Sub lesson 2 — Step 2 — Evaluate and compare
Pass mark: the learner can narrow to five careers and state each one's qualification against resources/knowledge/skills/experience.

## Sub lesson 3 — Step 3 — Discuss it with others
Pass mark: the learner can run a real comparison discussion and accept a drop as part of the process.

## Sub lesson 4 — Step 4 — Decide on one (the Sarkabo story)
Pass mark: the learner can decide on one career and retell the Sarkabo story with its sums and its moral.
