# What a Career Is — and What Career Selection Means — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. ** Unpaid work can be part of a career.
**Answer: True**

**2. ** Career selection means accepting the first job offered, without choosing.
**Answer: False** — selection IS the choosing of the occupation.

**3. ** This lesson recalls the Grade 7 career/job distinction as its starting point.
**Answer: True**
