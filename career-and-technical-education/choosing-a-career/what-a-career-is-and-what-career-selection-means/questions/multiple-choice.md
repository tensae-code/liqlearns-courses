# What a Career Is — and What Career Selection Means — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. ** In the Grade 8 climb, a career covers —
a) only one paid job  b) all the roles of a life — education, training, and paid and unpaid work — over a significant period  c) only school
**Answer: b) all the roles of a life — education, training, and paid and unpaid work — over a significant period**

**2. ** Career selection is —
a) choosing that occupation (your career)  b) being given any job  c) finishing school
**Answer: a) choosing that occupation (your career)**

**3. ** Career selection is harder in the 21st century partly because —
a) there are no careers  b) mass media shapes personal choices  c) schools closed
**Answer: b) mass media shapes personal choices**

**4. ** A career gives, over time —
a) progress and prosperity  b) nothing  c) only tiredness
**Answer: a) progress and prosperity**
