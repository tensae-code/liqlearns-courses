# What a Career Is — and What Career Selection Means — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. ** List the life roles a career covers.
**Answer: Education, training, and paid and unpaid work, held over a significant period.**

**2. ** Why must career selection be done carefully, in one sentence?
**Answer: Because the choice shapes a significant period of your life and your progress and prosperity — and modern influences such as mass media can push the choice off course.**

**3. ** How is this definition wider than "one job"?
**Answer: One job is a single role at a time; a career covers all the roles of a life — learning, training, paid and unpaid work — across a significant period.**
