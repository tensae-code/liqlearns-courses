# Lesson plan — What a Career Is — and What Career Selection Means

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** Choosing a Career · **Lesson:** What a Career Is — and What Career Selection Means
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 2 — Choosing a Career (CURRICULUM.md / TITLES.md ladder). The Grade 8 climb of this title, standing on First Steps.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a career covers all the roles of a life over a significant period, and career selection is the choosing of that occupation — a choice mass media now pushes on.
**This lesson's own level:** It stands on First Steps: the learner already knows career vs job from the Grade 7 book; this lesson is the Grade 8 book's wider restatement, and the doorway into the choosing method.

## Sub lessons
This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short, single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09).

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the whole First Steps title, especially Job vs Career. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Define career in the wider sense: education, training, paid and unpaid work over a significant period.
2. Define career selection.
3. Name why selection is harder now: mass media shapes personal choices.

## If the learner is stuck
- Narrows career back to one paid job → spread the strip: student and trainee are roles too, and so is unpaid community work.
- Thinks selection is something done to you → selection is YOUR choosing of the occupation; the next lessons give the method.
