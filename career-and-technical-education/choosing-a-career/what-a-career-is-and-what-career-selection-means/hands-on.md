# Hands-on — What a Career Is — and What Career Selection Means

Real roles, real people — your strip checked against a real working life.

## Build your strip from a real week
Keep a one-week role diary on paper: every role you actually hold, with hours beside each. Total the hours. Your career, today, is already wider than one job — your own numbers prove it.

## Check the strip against an elder's life
Ask an older worker to name the roles of their whole working life in order. Draw their strip. Count how many roles were unpaid, and how many were training. Bring back the count.
