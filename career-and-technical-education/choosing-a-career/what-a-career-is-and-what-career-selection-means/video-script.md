# Video script — What a Career Is — and What Career Selection Means

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A career is bigger than a job.

It is your learning. Your training.
Your paid work. Your unpaid work.
All of it, over many years — growing, prospering.

Choosing it is called career selection.
Choose carefully — the loudest voice is not always yours.
This title teaches you how.
