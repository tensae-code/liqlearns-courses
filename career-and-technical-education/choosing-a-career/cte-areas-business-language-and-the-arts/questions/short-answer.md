# CTE Areas — Business, Language, Society and the Arts — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name business science's six parts.
**Answer: Accounting and finance; marketing; banking; insurance; office management; hotel and tourism.**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** How do performers and visual artists differ in how they express opinion, emotion and taste?
**Answer: Performers use voice and body before an audience; visual artists do it through photography, painting, sculpting and drawing.**
