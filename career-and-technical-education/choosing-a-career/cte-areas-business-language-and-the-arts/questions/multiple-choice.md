# CTE Areas — Business, Language, Society and the Arts — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Recording and analysing financial information for decisions is —
a) marketing  b) accounting and finance  c) insurance
**Answer: b) accounting and finance**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Reducing money risks from fire and accidents is —
a) banking  b) insurance  c) office management
**Answer: b) insurance**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The study of human origins and culture is —
a) anthropology  b) translation  c) banking
**Answer: a) anthropology**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Story writing, application writing and translation belong to —
a) language use  b) insurance  c) manufacturing
**Answer: a) language use**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** A visual artist expresses ideas through —
a) only voice  b) photography, painting, sculpting and drawing  c) banking
**Answer: b) photography, painting, sculpting and drawing**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Kirar and masinqo are —
a) bank forms  b) musical instruments named in the arts area  c) farm tools
**Answer: b) musical instruments named in the arts area**
