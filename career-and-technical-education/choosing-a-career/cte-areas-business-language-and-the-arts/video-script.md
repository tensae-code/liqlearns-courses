# Video script — CTE Areas — Business, Language, Society and the Arts

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The people stream.

Business: count it, sell it, bank it, insure it,
run its office, welcome its guests.

Language and society: translate, write,
serve the community, know who we are.

Arts: sing it, play it, paint it, carve it.
Voice and body — or a made thing the eye can hold.
