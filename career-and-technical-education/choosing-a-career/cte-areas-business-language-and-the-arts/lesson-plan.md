# Lesson plan — CTE Areas — Business, Language, Society and the Arts

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** Choosing a Career · **Lesson:** CTE Areas — Business, Language, Society and the Arts
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 2 — Choosing a Career (CURRICULUM.md / TITLES.md ladder).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the Social Sciences stream's three areas: business science's six parts, language and social sciences, and performing and visual arts.
**This lesson's own level:** It stands on Technology, Farming and Health: the Natural Sciences doors are toured; these are the stream's other three doors.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an
image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture
of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does
with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and
while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is
dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the
student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph,
or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be
shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees
from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before
it is proved:

1. **Business science — selling, counting, protecting** — Accounting and finance, marketing, banking, insurance, office management, hotel and tourism.
2. **Language and social sciences** — Language use, social work and community development, anthropology, citizenship.
3. **Performing and visual arts** — Music, dance, theatre, film, painting and sculpture — voice and body before an audience, or image and object.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: CTE Areas — Technology, Farming and Health. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name business science's six parts with their work.
2. Name language and social sciences' coverage, including anthropology's meaning.
3. Distinguish performers from visual artists and name the instruments listed.

## If the learner is stuck
- Puts hotel and tourism under manufacturing → hotels sell service, not goods from raw material; the book places hotel and tourism inside business science.
- Mixes performer and visual artist → ask what meets the audience: voice and body live (performer), or a made thing — photo, painting, sculpture, drawing (visual artist).
