# CTE Areas — Business, Language, Society and the Arts

## Do it first

Stand, in your mind, on your busiest market street. Name every kind of work that is about *people dealing with people* — selling, counting money, guarding money, welcoming strangers, translating, performing. That street is this lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Business science — selling, counting, protecting

**Business** is about **what goods and services to sell, and how to make them available and sell them**. Its parts:

- **Accounting and finance** — recording, keeping and analysing financial information for decisions.
- **Marketing** — what to produce, and placing, selling and promoting it.
- **Banking** — keeping, withdrawing and borrowing money.
- **Insurance** — reducing money risks from fire, accidents and the like.
- **Office management** — planning office activities and keeping records systematically.
- **Hotel and tourism** — managing hotel and tourism services.

## Sub lesson 2 — Language and social sciences

This area covers **language use** (story and application writing, translation), **social work and community development**, **anthropology** (the study of human origins and culture) and **citizenship**.

## Sub lesson 3 — Performing and visual arts

This area covers **music and dance, theatre, film, painting and sculpture**, and instruments such as **kirar, masinqo, flute, guitar, drums and piano**.

The split inside it: **performers** use **voice and body before an audience** to express opinion, emotion and taste; **visual artists** do it through **photography, painting, sculpting and drawing**.

## Say it to your companion

Your companion calls a job — insurer, translator, masinqo player, hotel manager, sculptor, community worker. You name its area (and, for business, its part). Six jobs, no misses.
