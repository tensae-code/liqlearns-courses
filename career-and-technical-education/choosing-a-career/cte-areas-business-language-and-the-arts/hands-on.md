# Hands-on — CTE Areas — Business, Language, Society and the Arts

Real street, real artists — audit the people-stream where it actually works.

## Six-shopfront street audit
Walk one real commercial street (with an adult where needed). For each business part, find its real shopfront or write "absent": an accountant/bookkeeper, a seller doing marketing, a bank or agent, an insurer, an office, a hotel/guesthouse. Bring back the street's business map.

## Meet one artist properly
Watch or meet one local performer or visual artist. Name their instruments or materials exactly (kirar? drums? paint? stone?). Ask what training the work took. Write the answer in their words.
