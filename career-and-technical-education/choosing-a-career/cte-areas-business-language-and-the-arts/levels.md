# CTE Areas — Business, Language, Society and the Arts — sub lessons and depth

This is the depth record for the lesson, re-expressed as sub lessons. Each sub lesson opens when the one before it is proved (gate pool: the sub lesson's whole question set).

## Sub lesson 1 — Business science — selling, counting, protecting
Pass mark: the learner can name business science's six parts and each part's work.

## Sub lesson 2 — Language and social sciences
Pass mark: the learner can name the area's coverage and define anthropology.

## Sub lesson 3 — Performing and visual arts
Pass mark: the learner can distinguish performers from visual artists and name the listed instruments.
