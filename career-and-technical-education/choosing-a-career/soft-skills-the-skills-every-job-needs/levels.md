# Soft Skills — the Skills Every Job Needs — sub lessons and depth

This is the depth record for the lesson, re-expressed as sub lessons. Each sub lesson opens when the one before it is proved (gate pool: the sub lesson's whole question set).

## Sub lesson 1 — What soft skills are — in me and between us
Pass mark: the learner can define soft skills, contrast them with hard skills, and sort examples personal vs interpersonal.

## Sub lesson 2 — Creative thinking — seeing it a new way
Pass mark: the learner can describe creative thinking, its triggers, and its four practices.

## Sub lesson 3 — Problem solving, step by step
Pass mark: the learner can run the five steps in order and retell the book-cover worked example.
