# Hands-on — Soft Skills — the Skills Every Job Needs

Real problems, real books, real hands — solve one real cover problem and practise one real new-seeing.

## Cover a real book the problem-solving way
Take an exercise book whose cover is failing. Run all five steps out loud, in order, writing each step down. Choose your material (paper, plastic, cloth) by the cause you found — dirt, water, tearing — apply it with your own hands (measure, cut, fold, fit), then check after real use: did it survive a wet bag or a week of handling? Report the check honestly.

## New-seeing drill with a real object
Take one ordinary object (a pencil, a cup, a bottle top). List three new uses or views of it in five minutes — the pencil redrawn three ways, as tool, lever and artwork, is the model. Show your list to a companion and let them pick the most surprising one; defend it.

## If it is hard
If covering is hard, work as a pair: one pair of hands measures and holds, the other cuts and folds — interpersonal skill practised inside the task itself.
