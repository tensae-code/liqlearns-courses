# Video script — Soft Skills — the Skills Every Job Needs

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Hard skills fix one job.
Soft skills travel to every job.

In me: deciding, noticing, wondering.
Between us: speaking, listening, teaming.

See it a new way — aha!
Then solve it, step by step:
know it, find its cause, list ways,
pick the best, check it worked.
