# Soft Skills — the Skills Every Job Needs

## Do it first

Two workers, equally good with their tools. One is trusted, on time, easy to work with, and calm when things break. The other is none of those. A boss may hire only one. Which one — and what exactly does the winner have that the loser lacks? List those things. You have just listed this lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — What soft skills are — in me and between us

**Soft skills** are the **general personal attributes that shape how a person relates to others and performs at work**: self-awareness, time management, communication, teamwork, good manners, respect, self-confidence, listening, collaboration.

Unlike **hard skills**, they are **not tied to one job** — repairing a car is the hard-skill example. Soft skills **travel to every career**.

They split two ways:

- **Personal soft skills** sit *inside one person*: decision-making, flexibility, attention to detail, curiosity — for example, making your own study plan.
- **Interpersonal soft skills** work *between people*: communication, collaboration, teamwork, listening, time management — for example, completing a group assignment together.

## Sub lesson 2 — Creative thinking — seeing it a new way

**Creative thinking** means **seeing something in a new way**: a new approach to a problem, a fresh solution to a conflict — thinking outside the usual way until the **"aha" moment** arrives. Mobile phones, and every everyday technology from kitchen knives to sewing machines, are its products.

**Unsolved problems and curiosity trigger it.** It is practised by: examining things carefully; dropping bias and old assumptions; observing and asking questions; and presenting and communicating the new idea so others understand and accept it.

## Sub lesson 3 — Problem solving, step by step

**Problem solving is a method, not a guess.** Five steps:

1. **Know the problem.**
2. **Identify its cause.**
3. **Develop alternative solutions.**
4. **Select and apply the best one.**
5. **Check whether it worked.**

The worked example: covering exercise books. Paper covers dirty and tear when wet; plastic covers do not — so plastic is the selected solution. And the two skills feed each other: **problems spark ideas, and ideas solve problems.**

## Say it to your companion

Give your companion one personal and one interpersonal soft skill of your own, with evidence. Then walk them through the five problem-solving steps on a real small problem of theirs.

## Do It Yourself

1. Sort eight soft skills from this lesson into two columns: in me / between us.
2. Take one unsolved problem in your home or school. Write steps 1 and 2 (problem, cause) in one sentence each.
3. Write two alternative solutions (step 3) — force the second one to be a new way of seeing, not a repeat of the first.
