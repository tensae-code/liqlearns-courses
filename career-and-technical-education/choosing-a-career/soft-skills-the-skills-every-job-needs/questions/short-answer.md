# Soft Skills — the Skills Every Job Needs — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** How do creative thinking and problem solving feed each other?
**Answer: Problems spark ideas, and ideas solve problems — each feeds the other.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name three practices that grow creative thinking from the lesson.
**Answer: Any three of: examining things carefully; dropping bias and old assumptions; observing and asking questions; presenting and communicating the new idea so others understand and accept it.**
