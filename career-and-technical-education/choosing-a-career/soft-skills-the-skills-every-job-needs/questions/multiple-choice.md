# Soft Skills — the Skills Every Job Needs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Repairing a car is the lesson's example of a —
a) soft skill  b) hard skill  c) no skill
**Answer: b) hard skill**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Making your own study plan is an example of a —
a) personal soft skill  b) interpersonal soft skill  c) hard skill
**Answer: a) personal soft skill**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Creative thinking is triggered by —
a) solved problems only  b) unsolved problems and curiosity  c) sleep only
**Answer: b) unsolved problems and curiosity**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The "aha" moment is —
a) giving up  b) the moment a new way of seeing arrives  c) a lunch break
**Answer: b) the moment a new way of seeing arrives**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The FIRST step of problem solving is —
a) apply a solution  b) know the problem  c) check the result
**Answer: b) know the problem**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** In the book-cover example, plastic covers were selected because —
a) they are prettier  b) paper covers dirty and tear when wet, plastic covers do not  c) they cost more
**Answer: b) paper covers dirty and tear when wet, plastic covers do not**
