# Lesson plan — Soft Skills — the Skills Every Job Needs

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** Choosing a Career · **Lesson:** Soft Skills — the Skills Every Job Needs
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 2 — Choosing a Career (CURRICULUM.md / TITLES.md ladder).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** soft skills are the personal attributes that travel to every career — and two of them, creative thinking and problem solving, work as a pair.
**This lesson's own level:** It stands on the eight-areas tour: the learner knows the hard fields; now they learn the skills no single field owns.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an
image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture
of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does
with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and
while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is
dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the
student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph,
or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be
shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees
from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before
it is proved:

1. **What soft skills are — in me and between us** — General personal attributes; soft vs hard; personal vs interpersonal, with examples.
2. **Creative thinking — seeing it a new way** — A new approach, the "aha" moment, what triggers it and how it is practised.
3. **Problem solving, step by step** — Five steps: know, find the cause, alternatives, select and apply, check — on the book-cover example.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the three CTE-areas tour lessons. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Define soft skills and split them personal vs interpersonal, with examples.
2. Describe creative thinking, its triggers and its practice.
3. Run the five problem-solving steps on the book-cover example.

## If the learner is stuck
- Calls teamwork personal because "I do it" → the test is where the skill lives: between people = interpersonal, even when you supply your half.
- Jumps to a solution in problem solving → back to step 1: can you state the problem and its cause in one sentence each? No solving before knowing.
