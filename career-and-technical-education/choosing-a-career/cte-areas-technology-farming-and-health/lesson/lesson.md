## Sub lesson 1 — Information technology and computer science

**IT/CS** **creates, processes, stores, secures and exchanges electronic data**, using computers, networks, devices, and software and hardware design.

Its three sub-areas:

- **Computer maintenance and networking** — repairing faults and failures; connecting computers to share data.
- **Website design** — building a named site where a person or organisation's information can be visited.
- **Computer graphics design** — blending words, images, colour, typography and sound into attractive messages for publishing, advertising and television.

## Sub lesson 2 — Agriculture — crops, animals and the land

**Agriculture** is **cultivating plants and raising livestock**: **crop production**, **animal production** such as poultry, fattening and flower cultivation, and **protecting natural resources — forestry, soil fertility and erosion protection** — using **both traditional and modern technologies**.

## Sub lesson 3 — Health sciences — caring for people

**Health science** applies science to health: **medicine, nutrition, personal and community health, childcare and reproductive health**. It uses **health technologies — needles, syringes, X-ray, ultrasound, testing tools** — in **hospitals, clinics and health centres**. Its professionals are **doctors, nurses, laboratory technicians and pharmacists**.

These professionals matter urgently: the damage **HIV-AIDS and COVID-19** do to families, incomes and the country is the book's own reason given for honouring this work.
