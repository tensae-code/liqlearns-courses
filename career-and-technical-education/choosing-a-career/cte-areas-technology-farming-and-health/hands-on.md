# Hands-on — CTE Areas — Technology, Farming and Health

Real workplaces, real tools — audit all three doors where you actually live.

## Three-door workplace audit
Find one real example of each door near you: a phone/computer repairer or school computer room; a working farm plot; a clinic or health post. For each, write the tools you can see and the sub-area or field they prove. No example near you? Write that honestly — absence is data.

## Traditional/modern farm split
List every farming method you can observe on one real plot or farm visit, in two columns: traditional / modern. Ask the farmer which column is growing, and why. Write their answer in their words.
