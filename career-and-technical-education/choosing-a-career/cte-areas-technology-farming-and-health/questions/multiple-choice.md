# CTE Areas — Technology, Farming and Health — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Connecting computers so they share data is —
a) website design  b) computer maintenance and networking  c) graphics design
**Answer: b) computer maintenance and networking**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Blending words, images, colour, typography and sound into attractive messages is —
a) computer graphics design  b) networking  c) crop production
**Answer: a) computer graphics design**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Protecting soil fertility and preventing erosion belongs to —
a) health sciences  b) agriculture (protecting natural resources)  c) website design
**Answer: b) agriculture (protecting natural resources)**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Flower cultivation is part of —
a) animal production  b) agriculture  c) neither
**Answer: b) agriculture**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** X-ray and ultrasound are —
a) farm tools  b) health technologies  c) building tools
**Answer: b) health technologies**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Laboratory technicians and pharmacists are professionals of —
a) health sciences  b) agriculture  c) manufacturing
**Answer: a) health sciences**
