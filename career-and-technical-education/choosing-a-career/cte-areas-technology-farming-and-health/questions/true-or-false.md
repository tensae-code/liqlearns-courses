# CTE Areas — Technology, Farming and Health — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Agriculture uses both traditional and modern technologies.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Health science work happens only in hospitals, never in clinics or health centres.
**Answer: False** — it happens in hospitals, clinics and health centres.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** IT/CS includes securing electronic data, not only creating it.
**Answer: True**
