# Lesson plan — CTE Areas — Technology, Farming and Health

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** Choosing a Career · **Lesson:** CTE Areas — Technology, Farming and Health
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 2 — Choosing a Career (CURRICULUM.md / TITLES.md ladder).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** three Natural Sciences doors: computers and data, crops/animals/land, and the science of caring for people's health.
**This lesson's own level:** It stands on Making and Building: two doors toured; three more Natural Sciences doors follow here.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an
image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture
of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does
with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and
while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is
dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the
student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph,
or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be
shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees
from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before
it is proved:

1. **Information technology and computer science** — Creating, processing, storing, securing and exchanging electronic data; maintenance/networking, website design, graphics design.
2. **Agriculture — crops, animals and the land** — Crop production, animal production, and protecting natural resources, traditional and modern.
3. **Health sciences — caring for people** — Medicine, nutrition and health work with health technologies in hospitals, clinics and health centres.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: CTE Areas — Making and Building. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name IT/CS's work and its three sub-areas.
2. State what agriculture covers, including protecting natural resources.
3. Name health sciences' fields, technologies, workplaces and four professions.

## If the learner is stuck
- Mixes website design with graphics design → a website is a place visited for information; graphics is the attractive message itself (publishing, advertising, television).
- Forgets natural-resource protection inside agriculture → farming that eats its own soil ends itself; protection is part of the area, not an extra.
