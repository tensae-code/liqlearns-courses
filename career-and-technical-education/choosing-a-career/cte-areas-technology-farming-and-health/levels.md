# CTE Areas — Technology, Farming and Health — sub lessons and depth

This is the depth record for the lesson, re-expressed as sub lessons. Each sub lesson opens when the one before it is proved (gate pool: the sub lesson's whole question set).

## Sub lesson 1 — Information technology and computer science
Pass mark: the learner can state IT/CS's five data verbs and name its three sub-areas.

## Sub lesson 2 — Agriculture — crops, animals and the land
Pass mark: the learner can state agriculture's coverage — crops, animals, resource protection — and its two technology kinds.

## Sub lesson 3 — Health sciences — caring for people
Pass mark: the learner can name health sciences' fields, technologies, workplaces and four professions.
