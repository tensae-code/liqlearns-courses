# Lesson plan — Health and Safety at Work — the Law's List and Your Own Duties

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** Building and Retail · **Lesson:** Health and Safety at Work — the Law's List and Your Own Duties
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 7 — Building and Retail (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** general workplace health and safety: what the law's list covers — temperature, trip hazards, smoking, pollution, fire, toilets, equipment, disabled people's rights, working hours — and the worker's own part in it.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Concrete-Work Safety and Painting Safely: the learner knows trade-specific safety; now the general rules of any workplace.

## Sub lessons
1. **The law's list — what safety law covers** — temperature, hazards, smoking, pollution, fire, toilets, equipment, rights, hours
2. **Your own part — the worker's duties** — the right equipment for the task, and knowing where help lives

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Concrete-Work Safety and Painting Safely. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what workplace safety and health law is for.
2. List the law's coverage areas.
3. List the worker's own duties: right equipment, right clothing, knowing the extinguisher and first aid kit.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil.

## If the learner is stuck
- Memorises the law list but skips own duties → the book's second half is the point: law guards the building; the worker's equipment choices guard the worker.
- Treats 'safety equipment' as one fixed set → every duty in the book is matched to a task: gloves for toxic/sharp, safe shoes for slippery/lifting, hard hat where objects fall.
