# Hands-on — Health and Safety at Work — the Law's List and Your Own Duties

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes — real trade practice, done safely.

## The two-list walk

Take both lists into one real workplace (a workshop, a shop, a school). Tick what the law's list covers there, and mark what is missing. Then do the worker's list: can you point to the extinguisher and the first aid kit without searching? Write down where they are.

## Match duty to task

Write five tasks — pouring concrete, sanding a wall, lifting crates, driving to deliver goods, cleaning with chemicals. Beside each, write the worker-duty equipment the book assigns to it.
