# Video script — Health and Safety at Work — the Law's List and Your Own Duties

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Safety at work has two halves.

First, **the law** — laws to protect health and safety at work. Temperature. Trip hazards. Smoking. Fire. Toilets. Equipment. Rights of disabled people. Working hours.

Second, **your part** — the right equipment for THIS task. Know the extinguisher. Know the first aid kit.

Hard hat where things fall. Gloves for poison and sharp edges. Eye protection where eyes are at risk.

The law guards the workplace. You guard yourself.
