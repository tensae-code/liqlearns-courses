# Health and Safety at Work — the Law's List and Your Own Duties — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name four areas the law covers, from four different corners of the list.
**Answer:** Any four of: workplace temperature; trip hazards/clothing snags; smoking; pollution and fire hazards; toilets per person; safety equipment; rights of disabled people; daily working hours.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** State the pattern behind the worker's duties in one sentence.
**Answer:** Every duty matches the risk of the actual task: the proper equipment, clothing and shoes for THIS job, plus knowing where the extinguishers and first aid kits are.
