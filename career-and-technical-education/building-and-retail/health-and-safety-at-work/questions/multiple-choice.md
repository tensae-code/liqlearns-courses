# Health and Safety at Work — the Law's List and Your Own Duties — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Workplace safety and health is —
a) the set of laws made to protect people's health and safety at work  b) the worker's private concern only  c) a poster in the manager's office
**Answer: a) the set of laws made to protect people's health and safety at work.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which items are on the law's list?
a) temperature, trip hazards, smoking, pollution and fire hazards, toilets per person, safety equipment, disabled people's rights, daily working hours  b) only working hours and wages  c) only fire hazards and hard hats
**Answer: a) temperature, trip hazards, smoking, pollution and fire hazards, toilets per person, safety equipment, disabled people's rights, daily working hours.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** How strict safety laws are —
a) varies by country  b) is identical everywhere  c) does not matter
**Answer: a) varies by country.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The worker's own part includes knowing —
a) where the fire extinguishers and first aid kits are  b) next month's rota  c) the manager's password
**Answer: a) where the fire extinguishers and first aid kits are.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Gloves are the book's duty for —
a) cold weather only  b) carrying paper  c) toxic substances or sharp objects
**Answer: c) toxic substances or sharp objects.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Safe shoes are worn —
a) on slippery surfaces and for lifting  b) only in the office  c) only when driving
**Answer: a) on slippery surfaces and for lifting.**
