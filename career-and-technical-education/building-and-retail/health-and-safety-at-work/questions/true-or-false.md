# Health and Safety at Work — the Law's List and Your Own Duties — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The rights of disabled people are part of what workplace safety law covers.
**Answer: True** — the book lists them among the law's areas.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** One fixed set of equipment serves every task safely.
**Answer: False** — each duty is matched to the task's risk — hard hat where objects fall, gloves for toxic or sharp work, and so on.
