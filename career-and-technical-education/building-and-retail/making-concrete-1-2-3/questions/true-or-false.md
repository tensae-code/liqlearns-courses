# Making Concrete — Materials and the 1:2:3 Mix — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Leave the water out and you still have concrete.
**Answer: False** — all four materials are needed; without water the cement never wakes and binds.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Making bricks and blocks is difficult and only experts can do it.
**Answer: False** — the book stresses the making is easy — profit turns on standard size and consistent quality.
