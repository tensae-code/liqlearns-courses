# Making Concrete — Materials and the 1:2:3 Mix — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Concrete for blocks is made of —
a) powdered Portland cement, water, sand and gravel  b) clay, straw and water  c) cement and water only
**Answer: a) powdered Portland cement, water, sand and gravel.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The powder that binds the mix is —
a) gravel  b) sand  c) Portland cement
**Answer: c) Portland cement.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Which list has all four materials?
a) cement, sand, timber, water  b) water, gravel, clay, straw  c) cement, water, sand, gravel
**Answer: c) cement, water, sand, gravel.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The standard mix ratio 1:2:3 means —
a) 3 parts cement, 2 parts sand, 1 part stone  b) 1 part water, 2 parts cement, 3 parts sand  c) 1 part cement, 2 parts sand, 3 parts stone
**Answer: c) 1 part cement, 2 parts sand, 3 parts stone.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** For 6 buckets of stone, the ratio needs —
a) 1 bucket of cement and 2 of sand  b) 6 buckets of cement and 6 of sand  c) 2 buckets of cement and 4 of sand
**Answer: c) 2 buckets of cement and 4 of sand.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Profit in block making depends on —
a) standard size and consistent quality  b) making the blocks as fast as possible  c) using a different size each week
**Answer: a) standard size and consistent quality.**
