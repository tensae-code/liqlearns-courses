# Making Concrete — Materials and the 1:2:3 Mix — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is each material's job in the batch?
**Answer:** Cement binds; sand and stone give body; water wakes the cement and lets the mix be worked and poured.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A maker uses 1:2:3 for one batch and guesses the next. What goes wrong for his business?
**Answer:** His blocks change from batch to batch; buyers cannot trust the size or quality, and profit depends on standard size and consistent quality.
