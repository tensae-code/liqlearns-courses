# Hands-on — Making Concrete — Materials and the 1:2:3 Mix

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes — real trade practice, done safely.

## Count the ratio dry

With cups, count out the ratio using dry sand and small gravel only: 1 cup 'cement' (use flour or ash-free dry soil labelled clearly as a stand-in — never real cement for practice), 2 cups sand, 3 cups gravel. Lay the cups in a row and say the ratio aloud. Then double the batch: how many cups of each now?

## Judge a block

Look at two blocks in a real wall or stack (look only). Are they the same size? The same even grey? Write which maker you would buy from, and why — using the words standard size and consistent quality.
