# Hands-on — Bricks and Blocks — Concrete Block, Concrete Brick, Clay Brick

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes — real trade practice, done safely.

## Look at a real wall

Find a blocket wall near you. Count the cavities in one block by looking (do not climb or pull at the wall). Sketch one block and label its cavities and its smooth cast side.

## Sort by eye

Draw the three units from the lesson picture. Under each, write: family (concrete or clay), hollow or solid, and what it is made of.
