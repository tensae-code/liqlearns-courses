# Video script — Bricks and Blocks — Concrete Block, Concrete Brick, Clay Brick

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Three units in a row. Learn their names.

The big hollow one — a **concrete block**. A blocket. Cast with cavities, used for walls.

The small solid grey one — a **concrete brick**. Cement and sand, moulded and cured.

Coloured like clay? It can still be concrete. Colour does not decide.

The red-brown one — a **clay brick**, the second family.

Hollow block. Solid concrete brick. Clay brick. Three units, two families.
