# Bricks and Blocks — Concrete Block, Concrete Brick, Clay Brick — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A concrete block is —
a) a solid lump of pure clay  b) a hollow precast building unit used mainly for walls  c) a wooden mould for bricks
**Answer: b) a hollow precast building unit used mainly for walls.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The hollow spaces inside a concrete block are called —
a) cores of sand  b) joints  c) cavities
**Answer: c) cavities.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A concrete brick is made of —
a) cement and aggregate, usually sand, formed in moulds and cured  b) timber and nails  c) clay only, with no cement
**Answer: a) cement and aggregate, usually sand, formed in moulds and cured.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Clay bricks belong to —
a) the concrete family  b) the second family of brick construction  c) the timber family
**Answer: b) the second family of brick construction.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** To tell the families apart, the surest guide is —
a) its colour alone  b) its price  c) the material the unit is made of
**Answer: c) the material the unit is made of.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** A small solid unit coloured red-brown could still be —
a) a concrete brick coloured to resemble clay  b) a blocket  c) always a clay brick
**Answer: a) a concrete brick coloured to resemble clay.**
