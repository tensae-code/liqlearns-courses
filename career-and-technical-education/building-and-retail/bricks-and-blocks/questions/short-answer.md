# Bricks and Blocks — Concrete Block, Concrete Brick, Clay Brick — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How do you recognise a concrete block at a glance?
**Answer:** It is a large precast unit with one or more hollow cavities, its sides cast smooth or with a design.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the two families of brick construction in the book.
**Answer:** Concrete (blocks and bricks) and clay (clay bricks).
