# Lesson plan — Bricks and Blocks — Concrete Block, Concrete Brick, Clay Brick

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** Building and Retail · **Lesson:** Bricks and Blocks — Concrete Block, Concrete Brick, Clay Brick
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 7 — Building and Retail (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the two families of brick construction — hollow concrete blocks and solid concrete bricks, and clay bricks — and what each is made of.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Construction and Carpentry — the Building Trades and Their Key Words: the learner knows the blocket by name; now they learn its family.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Construction and Carpentry — the Building Trades and Their Key Words. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Tell a concrete block from a concrete brick from a clay brick by looking.
2. Say what each is made of.
3. Say what the hollow cavities in a concrete block are for in a wall.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil; if possible, one real block or brick to look at (look only — no lifting heavy blocks).

## If the learner is stuck
- Calls every unit a brick → size and hollowness decide first: big and hollow = block; small and solid = brick — then ask which family.
- Fooled by a coloured concrete brick → the book warns of exactly this: mineral colours make concrete resemble clay. Material, not colour, decides.
