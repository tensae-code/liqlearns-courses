# Hands-on — Opening a Retail Business — the Six Costs to Budget

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes — real trade practice, done safely.

## Price a real street

Pick one shop on a street you know. For each of the six costs, write your best estimate or the question you would ask the owner — deposit? monthly rent? power bill? A budget begins as six honest questions.

## The overlooked-cost check

Take any shop-opening plan (yours or a classmate's). Hunt for marketing: logo, cards, website, opening promotion. If it is missing, add it — the book says this is the cost that sinks plans by being forgotten.
