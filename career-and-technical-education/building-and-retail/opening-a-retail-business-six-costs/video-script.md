# Video script — Opening a Retail Business — the Six Costs to Budget

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Retail: buy from wholesalers, sell to consumers, keep the profit.

But opening costs money six ways. **Location** — deposit and remodelling, where the feet walk past.

**Rent** — monthly, by size and area. Dearest where the traffic is.

**Utilities** — power and gas, by price, space and climate.

**Insurance** — property, income, liability, crime. **Employees** — wages, benefits, training, payroll.

And **marketing** — logo, cards, website, opening offers. The one everybody forgets. Do not forget it.
