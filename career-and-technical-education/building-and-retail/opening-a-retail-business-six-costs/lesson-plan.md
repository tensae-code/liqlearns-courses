# Lesson plan — Opening a Retail Business — the Six Costs to Budget

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** Building and Retail · **Lesson:** Opening a Retail Business — the Six Costs to Budget
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 7 — Building and Retail (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the six start-up costs every new retail budget must face: location, rent, utilities, insurance, employee costs and marketing.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the title's opening key words, where retail was defined: now what it costs to open a retail business. Payment lessons follow.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an
image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture
of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does
with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and
while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is
dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the
student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph,
or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be
shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
1. **Place costs — location, rent, utilities** — the costs of where the shop is
2. **People and protection costs — insurance, employees, marketing** — insurance, staff costs, and the overlooked one

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Construction and Carpentry — the Building Trades and Their Key Words (retail is defined there). Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Define retail trade in one line.
2. List the six costs and what each covers.
3. Name the cost people most often overlook.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil.

1. **Do it first** — the learner budgets a dream shop in their head before the six costs are named (lesson/lesson.md).
2. **The explanation** — retail trade defined, then the six costs a–f in the book's order, ending with the overlooked one (lesson/lesson.md).

## If the learner is stuck
- Budgets only for stock and rent → walk the six a–f: location, rent, utilities, insurance, employees, marketing. Stock is not even on this list — these are the costs around the stock.
- Thinks online shops escape location costs → the book translates them: website production and hosting are the online location cost.
