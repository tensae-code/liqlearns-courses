# Opening a Retail Business — the Six Costs to Budget — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What three things set the size of the utilities bill?
**Answer:** Area prices, the size of the space, and the climate.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A budget covers location, rent, utilities, insurance and staff — and the shop fails to attract a single customer. Which cost was missed, and why did it matter?
**Answer:** Marketing — logo, cards, website and opening promotions are how customers learn the shop exists; without it the other five costs were paid for a shop nobody heard about.
