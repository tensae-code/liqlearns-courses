# Opening a Retail Business — the Six Costs to Budget — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retail trade —
a) buys from wholesalers and manufacturers and sells to final consumers for profit  b) sells only to other shops  c) makes goods in a factory
**Answer: a) buys from wholesalers and manufacturers and sells to final consumers for profit.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The location cost includes —
a) a deposit plus remodel/customising funds  b) the owner's salary  c) the first month's stock only
**Answer: a) a deposit plus remodel/customising funds.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Rent is charged —
a) monthly, by square footage and area value  b) once, at opening  c) by the number of customers
**Answer: a) monthly, by square footage and area value.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Insurance for a retail business covers —
a) only fire damage  b) only the owner's health  c) property, income, liability and crime
**Answer: c) property, income, liability and crime.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Employee costs include, beyond wages —
a) the employees' home rents  b) nothing else  c) benefits, plus training, payroll and timecards
**Answer: c) benefits, plus training, payroll and timecards.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The cost people most often overlook is —
a) rent  b) marketing  c) insurance
**Answer: b) marketing.**
