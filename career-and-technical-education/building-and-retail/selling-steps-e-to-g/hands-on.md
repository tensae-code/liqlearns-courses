# Hands-on — Selling — Handling Objections, Closing, Follow-Up

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes — real trade practice, done safely.

## Answer three objections

Take the product from the last lesson's script. Write the three hardest objections a customer could raise, and for each, a clarifying question followed by an answer that eliminates it — the E step done properly, not argued.

## Write the follow-up

Draft the follow-up for the same sale: the message or visit after delivery that (1) checks satisfaction and (2) strengthens the relationship. Two purposes, two sentences at least — one for each.
