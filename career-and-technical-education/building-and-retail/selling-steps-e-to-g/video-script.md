# Video script — Selling — Handling Objections, Closing, Follow-Up

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Three steps finish the sale.

**E — Handling objections**: clarify every doubt, eliminate every objection to buying.

**F — Closing**: ask for the order. Plainly. At the right moment.

**G — Follow-up**: check their satisfaction. Build the relationship. Repeat business is born here.

And the staircase is a circle — G bends back to A. The satisfied customer brings the next one.
