# Selling — Handling Objections, Closing, Follow-Up — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does clarifying come before eliminating in step E?
**Answer:** Because you cannot answer a doubt you have not understood exactly — clarification names the real objection, and only then can it be eliminated.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give E, F and G in order with one line each.
**Answer:** E handling objections — clarify doubts and eliminate every objection; F closing — ask the customer to place the order; G follow-up — ensure satisfaction and build the repeat-business relationship.
