# Selling — Handling Objections, Closing, Follow-Up — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Step E, handling objections, means —
a) changing the subject  b) clarifying the customer's doubts and questions and eliminating every objection to buying  c) arguing until the customer gives in
**Answer: b) clarifying the customer's doubts and questions and eliminating every objection to buying.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** An objection is best read as —
a) information about what still stands between the customer and the order  b) an insult to the seller  c) the end of the sale
**Answer: a) information about what still stands between the customer and the order.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Step F, closing, is —
a) locking the shop  b) asking the customer to place the order  c) writing the receipt afterwards
**Answer: b) asking the customer to place the order.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Follow-up ensures —
a) customer satisfaction  b) that the customer cannot return goods  c) that staff can rest
**Answer: a) customer satisfaction.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Follow-up builds the relationship that brings —
a) higher prices  b) new staff  c) repeat business
**Answer: c) repeat business.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The book draws the selling process as —
a) a ladder climbed once  b) a dead end at the order  c) a cycle — follow-up loops back toward new prospects
**Answer: c) a cycle — follow-up loops back toward new prospects.**
