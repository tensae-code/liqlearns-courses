# Lesson plan — Selling — Handling Objections, Closing, Follow-Up

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** Building and Retail · **Lesson:** Selling — Handling Objections, Closing, Follow-Up
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 7 — Building and Retail (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the last three steps of the seven-step selling process: E handling objections, F closing, and G follow-up — the step that loops back to repeat business.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Selling — the Retail Process and Selling Steps A–D: the staircase's first four steps are climbed; this lesson finishes it.

## Sub lessons

2. **G — follow-up, and the loop** — ensuring satisfaction and building repeat business

## If the learner is stuck
- Fights objections → E is clarifying first: understand the doubt exactly, then eliminate it. Arguing adds a second objection (the seller) to the first.
- Treats closing as pushy → in the book's order, closing is simply ASKING for the order after the objections are gone. The steps earn the ask.
