# Selling — Handling Objections, Closing, Follow-Up

## Do it first

Your presentation went well — and the customer still hesitates: 'Too expensive. Will it last? My brother says another shop is cheaper.' Write what a good seller does with each doubt. Then read how the book closes the staircase: E, F, G.

## Sub lesson 1 — E and F — objections cleared, the order asked

**E. Handling objections** — **clarifying the customer's doubts and questions and eliminating every objection to buying**. An objection is not an attack; it is information about what still stands between the customer and the order. Each doubt is clarified, then answered, until none remains.

**F. Closing** — **the customer is asked to place the order**. After A–E have done their work, the sale is completed by the asking: plainly, at the right moment.

## Sub lesson 2 — G — follow-up, and the loop

**G. Follow-up** — **following up with customers to ensure satisfaction and build the relationship that brings repeat business**.

The book draws the whole selling process as a **cycle**: follow-up is not the end of the road but the bend in it — a satisfied, remembered customer returns, and brings the next prospect with them. The staircase climbs A to G; the business lives on the loop.

## Say it to your companion

Give your companion E, F and G with their contents, then explain the cycle: how follow-up bends back to prospecting.
