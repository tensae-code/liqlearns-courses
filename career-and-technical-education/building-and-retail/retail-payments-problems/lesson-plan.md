# Lesson plan — Retail Payments — Why Collecting Payment Goes Wrong

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** Building and Retail · **Lesson:** Retail Payments — Why Collecting Payment Goes Wrong
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 7 — Building and Retail (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** payment collection as a driver of retail growth — and its three named troubles: distance, ever-changing orders, and the card reader a shop needs.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Opening a Retail Business — the Six Costs to Budget: the shop is open; now the money coming in has its own problems.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks payment problems mean dishonest customers → the book's three troubles are structural: distance, changing orders, missing equipment. No villain is required for payments to fail.
- Skips the importance line → the lesson's first fact is the frame: collection drives growth and profitability, and is underestimated. The troubles matter because the stakes are high.
