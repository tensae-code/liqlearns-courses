# Hands-on — Retail Payments — Why Collecting Payment Goes Wrong

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes — real trade practice, done safely.

## Audit a shop's till

With permission, ask one shopkeeper (or observe from your own buying): how do customers pay here? Has a payment ever failed — a distant order, a changed order, a card with no reader? Write the story in three lines, matched to the book's three troubles.

## Design the fix list

For each trouble, write the question a new shop must answer before opening: How will distant customers pay us? How will we handle orders that change? What equipment must we have on day one?
