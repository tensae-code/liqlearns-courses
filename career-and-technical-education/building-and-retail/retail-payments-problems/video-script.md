# Video script — Retail Payments — Why Collecting Payment Goes Wrong

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The sale is agreed. The money has not moved. That gap is **payment collection**.

It drives growth and profit — and shops underestimate it.

Three troubles live in the gap. **Distance** — cash and card stumble over long-distance orders.

**Changing orders** — every customer's payment is different, undefined, shifting.

**The card reader** — no reader, no card payments.

Next lesson: the payment options themselves, and what each one offers.
