# Retail Payments — Why Collecting Payment Goes Wrong — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Payment collection is usually given too much attention by new shops.
**Answer: False** — the book calls it often UNDERestimated.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** One fixed payment routine can cover every customer's order.
**Answer: False** — orders are undefined or constantly change from customer to customer — that is the second trouble.
