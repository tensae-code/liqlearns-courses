# Retail Payments — Why Collecting Payment Goes Wrong — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why do the three troubles matter so much?
**Answer:** Because payment collection itself drives growth and profitability — failures at the till are failures of growth, not small annoyances.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the three troubles with one line each.
**Answer:** Distance — cash/card may fail for long-distance orders; changing orders — payments are undefined or keep changing per customer; equipment — card payments need a card reader the shop may not have.
