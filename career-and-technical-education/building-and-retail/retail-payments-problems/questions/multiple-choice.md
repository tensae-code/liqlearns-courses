# Retail Payments — Why Collecting Payment Goes Wrong — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Payment collection is described as —
a) only the cashier's concern  b) a minor detail of shop work  c) a very important yet often underestimated driver of retail growth and profitability
**Answer: c) a very important yet often underestimated driver of retail growth and profitability.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Shops lose growth at the till when —
a) their prices are fair  b) their shelves are well stocked  c) money agreed does not arrive cleanly
**Answer: c) money agreed does not arrive cleanly.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Getting paid is —
a) the customer's problem alone  b) a system, not an afterthought  c) luck
**Answer: b) a system, not an afterthought.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Trouble 1 is that cash or card transactions may not work for —
a) long-distance orders  b) regulars at the counter  c) small purchases
**Answer: a) long-distance orders.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Trouble 2 is payment orders that are —
a) always identical  b) always in cash  c) undefined or constantly changing from customer to customer
**Answer: c) undefined or constantly changing from customer to customer.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Accepting card payments needs —
a) a second cashier  b) a card reader  c) a bigger shop
**Answer: b) a card reader.**
