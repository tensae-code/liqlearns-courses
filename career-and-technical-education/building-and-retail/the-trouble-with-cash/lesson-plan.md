# Lesson plan — The Trouble with Cash — Six Problems

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** Building and Retail · **Lesson:** The Trouble with Cash — Six Problems
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 7 — Building and Retail (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the book's six-count charge-sheet against cash payment: slowness, time eaten, robbery risk, leakage, processing burden, and paper money's short costly life.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Ways Customers Can Pay — Cash, Card, Mobile, Transfer: cash looked easiest there; this lesson counts its costs.

## Sub lessons
1. **Time and customers — the slow, eating payment** — payment delay, collection time, processing time
2. **Security and the notes themselves** — robbery targets, leakage, and paper money's short life

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Ways Customers Can Pay — Cash, Card, Mobile, Transfer. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. List the six problems of cash payment.
2. Explain the security pair: carrying risk and leakage.
3. Explain the time pair: collection time and processing time.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil.

## If the learner is stuck
- Recites 'cash is easiest' as a refutation → both lessons are the book's: easiest at the counter (Table 3.4) AND six problems in use. Holding both is the skill.
- Merges the two time problems → they are distinct counts in the book: collecting/dealing eats service time; checking/processing takes too much time. Keep both.
