# Video script — The Trouble with Cash — Six Problems

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Cash is easy at the counter. Then the bill arrives — six problems.

It can take **a week** to settle. Collecting it **eats time** owed to customers. Checking it takes **too much time**.

Cashiers carrying it are **robbery targets**. Large amounts **leak** — missing, stolen, paid as bribes.

And the notes themselves? **Short-lived and costly to print**.

Easy to accept. Expensive to live on. That is why shops choose a mix.
