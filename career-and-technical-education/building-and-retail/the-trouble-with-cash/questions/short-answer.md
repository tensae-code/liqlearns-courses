# The Trouble with Cash — Six Problems — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the two time problems and how they differ.
**Answer:** Collecting and dealing with cash eats time that should serve customers; checking and processing cash takes too much time. One steals service time at the counter, the other consumes back-room hours.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** List all six problems in the book's order.
**Answer:** Payment can take up to a week; collecting/dealing eats service time; carrying cash risks robbery; large cash handling leaks (missing, stolen, bribes); checking/processing takes too much time; paper money is short-lived and costly to print.
