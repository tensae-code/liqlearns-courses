# The Trouble with Cash — Six Problems — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Cash payment can take up to —
a) an hour  b) a month, always  c) a week — leaving customers dissatisfied
**Answer: c) a week — leaving customers dissatisfied.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Collecting and dealing with cash eats time that should —
a) be spent counting stock  b) belong to the owner alone  c) serve end users — the customers
**Answer: c) serve end users — the customers.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Checking and processing cash —
a) is done by the bank free  b) is instant  c) takes too much time
**Answer: c) takes too much time.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Cashiers loaded with cash are —
a) robbery targets  b) perfectly safe  c) insured automatically
**Answer: a) robbery targets.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Large cash handling leaks because some cash —
a) is always banked daily  b) goes missing, is stolen, or is used for bribes  c) evaporates
**Answer: b) goes missing, is stolen, or is used for bribes.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Paper money itself —
a) lasts forever  b) has a short lifetime and is costly to print  c) costs nothing to replace
**Answer: b) has a short lifetime and is costly to print.**
