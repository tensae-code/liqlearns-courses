# The Trouble with Cash — Six Problems — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Two of the book's six counts against cash are about time.
**Answer: True** — collection time and processing time are separate counts.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Cash leaves a clear trace, so leakage is rare.
**Answer: False** — the book's count is the opposite: untraced cash goes missing, is stolen, or is used for bribes.
