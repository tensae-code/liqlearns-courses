# Hands-on — The Trouble with Cash — Six Problems

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes — real trade practice, done safely.

## The six-count audit

Take one real or imagined cash-only shop. Walk the six counts and write, for each, whether it bites that shop and how hard. Finish with a verdict: which single other payment option from the last lesson would answer its worst count?

## Count the cost of counting

Time yourself counting and re-counting a stack of paper slips as 'notes' three times, checking each against a list. That small chore, scaled to a shop's day, is Problem 5 made visible.
