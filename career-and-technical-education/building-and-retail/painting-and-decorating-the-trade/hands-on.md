# Hands-on — Painting and Decorating — a Trade That Protects and Beautifies

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes — real trade practice, done safely.

## Surface audit

Find three surfaces — a wall, a door, a metal object. For each, write: is it coated? What is the coating protecting it from (weather, erosion, mould, rust)? What would one uncoated year do to it?

## Brief a painter

Following the teacher guide's workplace-briefing pattern: prepare four questions you would ask a working painter — about choosing a surface for a finish, preparing it, selecting materials, and technique. If you can ask a real painter (with an adult), do; otherwise answer them from this lesson and Painting Tools.
