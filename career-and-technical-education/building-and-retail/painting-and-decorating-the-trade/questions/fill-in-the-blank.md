# Painting and Decorating — a Trade That Protects and Beautifies — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The professional who applies the finishes is the painter and ______.
**Answer: decorator.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Weather damage, erosion, ______ and rust — the four harms.
**Answer: mould.**
