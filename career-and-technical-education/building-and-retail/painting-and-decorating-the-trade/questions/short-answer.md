# Painting and Decorating — a Trade That Protects and Beautifies — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the surfaces the painter-decorator works on.
**Answer:** Walls, ceilings and surfaces of residential, commercial and industrial buildings.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give the double job in one sentence, with an example.
**Answer:** Painting protects surfaces (e.g. a gate coated against rust) and makes them more attractive (e.g. a freshly painted wall) — protection and beauty in one trade.
