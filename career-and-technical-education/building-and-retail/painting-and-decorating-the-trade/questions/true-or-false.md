# Painting and Decorating — a Trade That Protects and Beautifies — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Painting is a modern invention of the last century.
**Answer: False** — the book calls it a practice as old as people's wish to improve their environment.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A coating protects a metal surface against rust.
**Answer: True** — rust is one of the four harms in the book's protection list.
