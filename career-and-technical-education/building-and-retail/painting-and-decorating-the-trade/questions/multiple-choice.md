# Painting and Decorating — a Trade That Protects and Beautifies — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Painting is —
a) building a wall from blocks  b) sandpapering a surface smooth  c) coating a surface with the chemicals called paint
**Answer: c) coating a surface with the chemicals called paint.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The painter and decorator applies —
a) paint, varnish, wallpaper and other finishes and special coatings  b) concrete, mortar and blocks  c) tyres, tubes and valves
**Answer: a) paint, varnish, wallpaper and other finishes and special coatings.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The trade works on buildings that are —
a) only residential  b) only temporary  c) residential, commercial and industrial
**Answer: c) residential, commercial and industrial.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Paint's practical job is protecting surfaces from —
a) weather damage, erosion, mould and rust  b) being looked at  c) dust settling overnight only
**Answer: a) weather damage, erosion, mould and rust.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The decorative half of the job is —
a) making surfaces cheaper  b) making surfaces more attractive  c) making surfaces waterproof
**Answer: b) making surfaces more attractive.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The book presents the trade as blending —
a) creativity with practical skill  b) colour with smell  c) speed with low cost
**Answer: a) creativity with practical skill.**
