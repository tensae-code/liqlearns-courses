# Video script — Painting and Decorating — a Trade That Protects and Beautifies

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Painting** — coating a surface with paint. An old practice, a living trade.

The **painter and decorator** applies paint, varnish, wallpaper and special coatings — in homes, businesses, factories.

The job is double. First, **protection**: against weather, erosion, mould, rust.

Second, **beauty**: surfaces made more attractive.

Creativity in one hand, practical skill in the other. That is the trade.
