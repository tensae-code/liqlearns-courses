# Painting and Decorating — a Trade That Protects and Beautifies

## Do it first

A new wall stands unpainted through one rainy season, then another. Rain soaks it. Sun cracks it. Mould creeps. A metal gate nearby begins to rust. Write what the wall needed — then read what the painter's trade actually does.

## Sub lesson 1 — Painting and the painter-decorator

**Painting is coating a surface with the chemicals called paint** — a practice as old as people's wish to improve their environment.

The professional is the **painter and decorator**, who applies paint, varnish, wallpaper and other finishes and special coatings to walls, ceilings and surfaces of residential, commercial and industrial buildings — homes, businesses and factories alike.

## Sub lesson 2 — The double job — protection and beauty

The work is double. Its practical half: **protecting surfaces from weather damage, erosion, mould and rust**. A coated surface sheds what an uncoated surface suffers.

Its decorative half: **making surfaces more attractive**. The book presents painting and decorating as a dedicated skill for life, blending creativity with practical skill — the trade needs both hands: one that protects, one that beautifies.
