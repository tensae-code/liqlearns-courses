# Lesson plan — Painting and Decorating — a Trade That Protects and Beautifies

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** Building and Retail · **Lesson:** Painting and Decorating — a Trade That Protects and Beautifies
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 7 — Building and Retail (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** what painting and decorating is: a professional trade with a double job — protecting surfaces from weather, erosion, mould and rust, and making them more attractive.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the carpentry lessons of this title: the learner has built and fitted; now the finishing trade that protects the work.

## Sub lessons
1. **Painting and the painter-decorator** — coating a surface, and the professional who does it
2. **The double job — protection and beauty** — what paint defends against, and what it adds

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Carpentry Safety — the learner should already think protection-first. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Define painting and the painter-decorator's work.
2. Name the finishes the trade applies.
3. State the trade's double job: practical protection and decoration.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks painting is decoration only → the book puts protection first in the double job: weather, erosion, mould, rust are stopped by the coating, not by the colour.
- Cannot name the finishes → paint, varnish, wallpaper, other finishes and special coatings — five words, counted on fingers.
