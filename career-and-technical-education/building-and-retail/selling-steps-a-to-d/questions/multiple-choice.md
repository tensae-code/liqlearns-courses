# Selling — the Retail Process and Selling Steps A–D — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The retail process includes —
a) developing value propositions and learning customer preferences  b) only hiring staff  c) only setting up the shop
**Answer: a) developing value propositions and learning customer preferences.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Establishing retail networks and ______ is part of the retail process.
a) supply chains  b) warehouses for other shops  c) lotteries
**Answer: a) supply chains.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The selling process sits —
a) inside the retail process — the path one sale walks  b) after the shop closes  c) before any shop exists
**Answer: a) inside the retail process — the path one sale walks.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Step A, prospecting, is —
a) the first meeting  b) identifying potential customers  c) collecting information before the call
**Answer: b) identifying potential customers.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Step B, pre-approach, is —
a) asking for the order  b) collecting information about the customer and understanding them before the sales call  c) showing the product
**Answer: b) collecting information about the customer and understanding them before the sales call.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Step D, presentation, must show —
a) why the shop needs the money  b) every feature the factory built in  c) how the product will satisfy the customer's needs and add value to their life
**Answer: c) how the product will satisfy the customer's needs and add value to their life.**
