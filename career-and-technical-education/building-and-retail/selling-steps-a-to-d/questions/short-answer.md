# Selling — the Retail Process and Selling Steps A–D — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** List the five movements of the retail process.
**Answer:** Developing value propositions; learning customer preferences; establishing retail networks and supply chains; getting customers for the merchandise; setting up stores and filling them with merchandise.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does pre-approach come before approach?
**Answer:** Because the seller must collect information and understand the customer BEFORE the first meeting — arriving already knowing is what makes the approach and presentation land.
