# Video script — Selling — the Retail Process and Selling Steps A–D

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Selling is a staircase of seven steps. Today, the first four.

Around it, the whole **retail process**: value offered, preferences learned, networks built, customers won, stores stocked.

**A — Prospecting**: find who might buy. **B — Pre-approach**: learn about them before you meet.

**C — Approach**: the first meeting. **D — Presentation**: show how the product meets THEIR needs and adds value to THEIR life.

Steps E to G — the next lesson climbs them.
