# Lesson plan — Selling — the Retail Process and Selling Steps A–D

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** Building and Retail · **Lesson:** Selling — the Retail Process and Selling Steps A–D
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 7 — Building and Retail (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the retail process as a whole, and the first four steps of the seven-step selling process: prospecting, pre-approach, approach and presentation.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the payment lessons: the shop can take money; now how a sale is actually made, step by step.

## If the learner is stuck
- Jumps straight to presentation → A and B happen before any meeting: prospects found, information gathered. Skipping them is selling blind.
- Presents features instead of needs → the book's D test: how the product satisfies the CUSTOMER's needs and adds value to THEIR life. Rewrite every feature sentence as a need sentence.
