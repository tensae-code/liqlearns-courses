# Hands-on — Selling — the Retail Process and Selling Steps A–D

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes — real trade practice, done safely.

## Script A–D for one product

Choose one product (a phone, a pair of shoes, a sack of teff). Write the four steps as a script: three prospects (A), three facts you would learn about one of them (B), your first sentence at the meeting (C), and a presentation that speaks to the customer's needs, not the product's parts (D).

## Catch a real approach

Recall a time a seller approached you well or badly. Write which step you witnessed and judge it against the book: at C, was it a real meeting? At D, did they speak to your needs or only the product's features?
