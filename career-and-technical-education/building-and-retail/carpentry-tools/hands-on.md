# Hands-on — Carpentry Tools — Hand Tools and Power Tools

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes — real trade practice, done safely.

## The one-question sort

List ten tools from a home or workshop you know. Beside each, answer the one question — hand or motor? — and write its family. Check your list against the book's pictured families.

## Spot the cord

In the lesson picture or a real workshop (looking only), find three power tools by their cords and three hand tools by their lack of one. Write the evidence you used for each call.
