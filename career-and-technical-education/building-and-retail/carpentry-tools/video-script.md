# Video script — Carpentry Tools — Hand Tools and Power Tools

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Two families on one bench. One question sorts them: **where does the power come from?**

**Hand tools** — your arm is the motor. Saws, hammer, plane, chisels. Stop your arm, the tool stops.

**Power tools** — electricity is the motor. Drills, sanders, powered saws. Your hand guides; the machine keeps moving.

Same jobs, different power. And different power means different safety — that is the next lesson.
