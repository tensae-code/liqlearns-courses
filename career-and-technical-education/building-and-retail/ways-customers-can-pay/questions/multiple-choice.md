# Ways Customers Can Pay — Cash, Card, Mobile, Transfer — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Cash is described as —
a) the rarest form  b) the most common and easiest form of payment  c) a payment only banks accept
**Answer: b) the most common and easiest form of payment.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Credit cards lead customers to —
a) always pay late  b) avoid large purchases  c) make more frequent or larger purchases
**Answer: c) make more frequent or larger purchases.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Cards let customers make large purchases —
a) without any equipment at the shop  b) safely  c) only in person at a bank
**Answer: b) safely.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Mobile payments are described in the book's table —
a) as suitable only for banks  b) as always free of all cost  c) in the same terms as credit cards
**Answer: c) in the same terms as credit cards.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Electronic bank transfers receive large payments —
a) only after a week  b) without paying fees, quicker and more convenient than cash or checks  c) with the highest fees of all options
**Answer: b) without paying fees, quicker and more convenient than cash or checks.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Who must decide which payment types to accept?
a) the customers, by vote  b) the wholesaler  c) the small-business owner
**Answer: c) the small-business owner.**
