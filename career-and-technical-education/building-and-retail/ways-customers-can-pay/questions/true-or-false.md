# Ways Customers Can Pay — Cash, Card, Mobile, Transfer — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Many customers expect a shop to accept cash.
**Answer: True** — the book says so — refusing cash refuses the payment most people carry.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** One payment method is right for every shop.
**Answer: False** — every method has merits and demerits — the owner must decide the mix for their own business.
