# Ways Customers Can Pay — Cash, Card, Mobile, Transfer — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What double advantage does the book give cards?
**Answer:** Customers make more frequent or larger purchases, and they can make large purchases safely.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A shop takes only cash. Name two advantages from the table it is giving up.
**Answer:** Any two: cards' more frequent/larger purchases and safe large purchases; mobile's same card-like promise; transfers' fee-free, quicker large payments.
