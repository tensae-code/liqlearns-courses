# Hands-on — Ways Customers Can Pay — Cash, Card, Mobile, Transfer

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes — real trade practice, done safely.

## Build a payment mix

For one imagined shop (a small grocery), choose which of the four options to accept on day one, and write the book's advantage as your reason for each one you take — and your reason for any you leave out.

## Pay four ways on paper

Write a 100-birr sale four times: paid in cash, by card, by phone, by transfer. Under each, write what the shop gains and what it must have ready (till, reader, phone, bank account).
