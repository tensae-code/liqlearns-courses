# Video script — Ways Customers Can Pay — Cash, Card, Mobile, Transfer

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Four ways to pay. Four advantages.

**Cash** — most common, easiest. Customers expect it.

**Cards** — bigger, more frequent purchases, made safely.

**Mobile** — the book gives it the same promise as cards.

**Transfers** — large payments, no fees, quicker than cash or cheques.

Every method has merits and demerits. The owner must choose — on purpose.
