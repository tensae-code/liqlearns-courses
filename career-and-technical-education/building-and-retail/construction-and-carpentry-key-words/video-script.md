# Video script — Construction and Carpentry — the Building Trades and Their Key Words

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

One street. A wall going up — that is **construction**.

The grey block in the wall? A **blocket** — the standard building block.

The worker fitting the wooden door — a **carpenter**. Carpentry is cutting, working and joining timber.

Air inside a tyre, measured — **tyre pressure**. Right pressure, safe road.

The shop selling to families — **retail**. And every sale ends in **payment**: money agreed and handed over.

These trades employ most young workers. Learn them well, and you can earn.
