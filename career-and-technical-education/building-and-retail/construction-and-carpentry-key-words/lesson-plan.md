# Lesson plan — Construction and Carpentry — the Building Trades and Their Key Words

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** Building and Retail · **Lesson:** Construction and Carpentry — the Building Trades and Their Key Words
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 7 — Building and Retail (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the five key words of the building-and-retail unit — construction, blocket, carpentry, tyre pressure, retail and payment — and why these trades matter in Ethiopia.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on nothing but the learner's own street: they have seen walls going up, doors being fitted and shops selling. It opens the Building and Retail title.

## Sub lessons
1. **The key words of the building trades** — what construction, blocket, carpentry and tyre pressure mean
2. **Retail, payment, and why these trades matter** — what retail and payment mean, and the place of these trades in Ethiopia

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- This lesson opens the title — start here if the building trades are new to you. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Define construction, carpentry, retail and payment in your own words.
2. Say what a blocket is and what tyre pressure does.
3. Say why construction and carpentry matter for young people in Ethiopia.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil; a walk past one building site or workshop if one is near.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the learner names every trade they can see in one street scene before any definition is given (lesson/lesson.md).
2. **The explanation** — the five key words, one by one, each tied to something the learner has seen; then why the trades matter (lesson/lesson.md).

3. **Say it to your companion** — the five key words from memory, each with one example from your own street (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD VIDEO: the script in video-script.md, filmed — the lesson in the super-simple voice (made later, when videos are made).
4. **Questions** — from questions/: served at the learner's level and teaching-mix (levels.md).

6. **Do It Yourself** — a key-word hunt on a real street or market (lesson/lesson.md).

## If the learner is stuck
- Mixes carpentry with all construction → carpentry is the timber part: cutting, working and joining wood. Construction is the whole building work.
- Cannot say why the trades matter → return to the street list: count how many of the workers seen are in building or selling. The number itself is the answer.
