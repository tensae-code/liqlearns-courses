# Construction and Carpentry — the Building Trades and Their Key Words

## Do it first

Walk one street in your mind — or in real life. A wall is going up. A door frame is being fitted. A shop is selling soap. Write down every kind of work you can see. Keep the list: this lesson names each one.

## Sub lesson 1 — The key words of the building trades

**Construction** is the creation or building of something. When a wall, a house or a bridge is made where nothing stood before, that is construction.

The standard building block of construction here is the **concrete masonry unit** — the rectangular block used for walls, known locally as the **blocket**.

**Carpentry** is the art and trade of cutting, working and joining timber. The carpenter makes and fits the wooden parts of a building: framing, doors, windows and staircases.

**Tyre pressure** is the measurement of the air inside a tyre. The right pressure keeps a tyre wearing evenly and gripping the road — a small measurement that carries people's safety.

## Sub lesson 2 — Retail, payment, and why these trades matter

**Retail** is selling consumer goods or services to customers through channels of distribution, to earn a profit. The shop on your street that sells to the families around it is retail.

**Payment** is the agreed transfer of money, goods or services in exchange for goods or services. No payment, no trade — every sale in this title ends in a payment, and later lessons show how payments can go wrong and how to handle them well.

Why do these trades matter so much? Construction and carpentry are interconnected trades, and together they employ the majority of youth in developing countries. For a country like Ethiopia they are crucial: houses, schools, shops and workshops all begin with someone who can build.

## Say it to your companion

Say the five key words to your companion — construction, blocket, carpentry, tyre pressure, retail — and add payment. For each one, give an example from your own street, not from this page.

3. Write one sentence: which of these trades would you most like to learn, and why?
