# Construction and Carpentry — the Building Trades and Their Key Words — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two things a carpenter makes or fits.
**Answer:** Any two of: framing, doors, windows, staircases (also walls, floors and doorframes in the trade's wider list).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why is learning a building trade a real chance for a young person?
**Answer:** Because construction and carpentry employ the majority of youth in developing countries and are crucial to a country like Ethiopia — houses, schools and shops all need builders.
