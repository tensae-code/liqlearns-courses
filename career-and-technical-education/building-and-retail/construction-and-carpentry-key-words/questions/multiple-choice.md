# Construction and Carpentry — the Building Trades and Their Key Words — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Construction is —
a) selling goods to customers  b) measuring air in a tyre  c) the creation or building of something
**Answer: c) the creation or building of something.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A blocket is —
a) a tool for smoothing concrete  b) a wooden door frame  c) a concrete masonry unit — the standard rectangular building block
**Answer: c) a concrete masonry unit — the standard rectangular building block.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Carpentry is the trade of —
a) mixing cement and gravel  b) selling goods for profit  c) cutting, working and joining timber
**Answer: c) cutting, working and joining timber.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Retail means —
a) building walls from blocks  b) selling consumer goods or services to customers for profit  c) joining timber by hand
**Answer: b) selling consumer goods or services to customers for profit.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Payment is —
a) a list of goods in a shop window  b) the agreed transfer of money, goods or services in exchange for goods or services  c) the air inside a tyre
**Answer: b) the agreed transfer of money, goods or services in exchange for goods or services.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Construction and carpentry matter in Ethiopia because —
a) they need no tools at all  b) only a few people ever do them  c) they are interconnected trades employing the majority of youth in developing countries
**Answer: c) they are interconnected trades employing the majority of youth in developing countries.**
