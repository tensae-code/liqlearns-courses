# Construction and Carpentry — the Building Trades and Their Key Words — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tyre pressure keeps a tyre wearing evenly and gripping the road.
**Answer: True** — the right air pressure does both — that is why it is checked.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A shop selling soap to the families around it is doing retail.
**Answer: True** — selling consumer goods to customers for profit is retail.
