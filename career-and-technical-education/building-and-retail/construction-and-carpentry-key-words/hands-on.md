# Hands-on — Construction and Carpentry — the Building Trades and Their Key Words

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes — real trade practice, done safely.

## Name the trades on your street

Walk a short way — or picture your walk to school or market. List every place where someone is building, joining wood, checking tyres or selling. Match each one to a key word from the lesson.

## Ask a builder one question

With an adult's permission, ask someone at a building site or workshop: what did you make today? Listen for the key words in their answer — blocket, timber, measure — and write down two you hear.
