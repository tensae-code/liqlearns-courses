# Hands-on — Checking Tyre Pressure — Placard, Gauge, Inflate, Cap

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes — real trade practice, done safely.

## Do the real check (with an adult)

At a real vehicle with an adult and a pen gauge, run the six steps on one tyre: read the placard values first and write them down; cap off; gauge on firmly; read; inflate at the air hose if low and recheck; cap on. Record the before and after readings — the teacher guide's pattern is readings recorded, at a visit arranged for everyone.

## Hear the hiss

During the same practice, deliberately seat the gauge at a slight angle once (adult supervising) and listen: the hiss is the gauge telling you the seat is wrong. Reseat it squarely until the hiss stops and the reading holds.
