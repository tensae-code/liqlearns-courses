# Video script — Checking Tyre Pressure — Placard, Gauge, Inflate, Cap

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Six steps check a tyre. Placard to cap.

**One** — read the right pressures: the placard inside the front door, or the manual. Front and rear can differ.

**Two** — cap off. **Three** — gauge on, pushed firmly. **Four** — read it.

**Five** — low? Add air, and check again. **Six** — cap back on.

Hissing? The gauge is not seated. Push it square.

A pen gauge of your own, and readings written down. That is the habit.
