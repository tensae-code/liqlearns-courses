# Lesson plan — Checking Tyre Pressure — Placard, Gauge, Inflate, Cap

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** Building and Retail · **Lesson:** Checking Tyre Pressure — Placard, Gauge, Inflate, Cap
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 7 — Building and Retail (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the six-step tyre-pressure check — read the placard, cap off, gauge on, read, inflate and recheck if low, cap back on — and how the pen gauge works and speaks.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Tyre Pressure — Why the Right Air Keeps Tyres Alive and People Safe: the learner knows the stakes; now the procedure, in the book's step order.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Tyre Pressure — Why the Right Air Keeps Tyres Alive and People Safe. Recommended, never forced.
- Real-vehicle practice is done with an adult at a real vehicle or service station.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Give the six steps in order.
2. Say where correct pressures are found, and that front and rear often differ.
3. Use the gauge's own signals: firm seating, the hissing warning, and its ±3 psi precision.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil for learning; for hands-on, an adult, a real vehicle and a pen gauge at a service station or garage (the teacher guide's visit pattern).

## If the learner is stuck
- Skips the placard and guesses a pressure → front and rear often differ and every vehicle sets its own values: the placard or manual is step 1 precisely so the target is known before the gauge comes out.
- Reads through a hiss → a hiss means the seat is leaking: not seated tight, or the angle is wrong. Reseat; never record a hissing reading.
