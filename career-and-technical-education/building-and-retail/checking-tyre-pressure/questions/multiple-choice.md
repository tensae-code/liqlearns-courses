# Checking Tyre Pressure — Placard, Gauge, Inflate, Cap — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Step 1 reads the correct pressures from —
a) a guess based on tyre size  b) the tyre placard (usually inside a front door opening) or the owner's manual  c) the tyre's colour
**Answer: b) the tyre placard (usually inside a front door opening) or the owner's manual.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Front and rear pressures —
a) are always identical  b) often differ — read both  c) never matter
**Answer: b) often differ — read both.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The six steps in order are —
a) cap on, placard, inflate, read, gauge off, drive  b) placard, cap off, gauge on, read, inflate-and-recheck if low, cap on  c) gauge on, cap off, inflate, placard, read, cap on
**Answer: b) placard, cap off, gauge on, read, inflate-and-recheck if low, cap on.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The gauge the book recommends buying is —
a) a pen-shaped pneumatic/mechanical gauge  b) a digital racing gauge  c) any stick found near the car
**Answer: a) a pen-shaped pneumatic/mechanical gauge.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A typical mechanical gauge is precise to —
a) exactly zero error  b) ±3 psi (21 kPa)  c) ±30 psi
**Answer: b) ±3 psi (21 kPa).**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** A hissing sound from the gauge means —
a) it is not seated tight, or its angle needs adjusting  b) the tyre is full  c) the gauge is broken forever
**Answer: a) it is not seated tight, or its angle needs adjusting.**
