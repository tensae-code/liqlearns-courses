# Checking Tyre Pressure — Placard, Gauge, Inflate, Cap — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does the cap appear at both step 2 and step 6?
**Answer:** Because the job opens and closes at the valve: the cap comes off to reach the valve for the gauge and hose, and goes back on to protect the valve when the check is done.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Your gauge hisses and the reading jumps around. What do you do, and why?
**Answer:** Reseat the gauge squarely and firmly on the valve — a hiss means it is not seated tight or the angle is wrong, so the reading cannot be trusted until the seat is sealed.
