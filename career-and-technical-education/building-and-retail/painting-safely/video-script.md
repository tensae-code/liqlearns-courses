# Video script — Painting Safely — Warnings, Masks, and the Lead Rule

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Painting has rules. Learn them before the tin opens.

**Warn** — fence, barrier or signs. People must know.

**Mask** — dust masks when sanding. **Eyes** — protection at all times.

**Lead** — NEVER sand it. It can cause cancer.

**Sparks** — never grind where painting is happening.

**Labels** — read every paint label, and obey it.
