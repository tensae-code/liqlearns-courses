# Hands-on — Painting Safely — Warnings, Masks, and the Lead Rule

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes — real trade practice, done safely.

## Plan a safe job

Choose one real room or wall. Write its safety plan rule by rule: where the barrier or sign goes, when masks are worn, what eye protection, how you will treat any old paint of unknown make-up (the lead rule), and which labels you will read first.

## Spot the dangers

Return to the Do-it-first scene. List its dangers again and match each one to the book rule that answers it. Any danger left unmatched is a question to bring to a teacher.
