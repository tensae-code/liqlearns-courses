# Painting Safely — Warnings, Masks, and the Lead Rule — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Who is the warning set-up for, and when is it put up?
**Answer:** For the people around the work — it is set up before and during coating work so others know the risk and keep clear.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** State the two absolute rules and the reason inside each.
**Answer:** Never sand materials containing lead (it can cause cancer); never grind where painting is in progress (grinding sparks are a fire hazard near paint work).
