# Painting Safely — Warnings, Masks, and the Lead Rule — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Before and during coating work, others are warned by —
a) a fence or barrier, or signs  b) shouting once at the start  c) a note left afterwards
**Answer: a) a fence or barrier, or signs.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** When sanding, wear —
a) a hard hat only  b) nothing — work outdoors instead  c) dust masks
**Answer: c) dust masks.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Eye protection during painting work is worn —
a) only while spraying  b) only by the person sanding  c) at all times
**Answer: c) at all times.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Materials containing lead must —
a) be sanded with a mask on  b) be sanded only outdoors  c) never be sanded — lead can cause cancer
**Answer: c) never be sanded — lead can cause cancer.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Grinding must never happen where painting is in progress because —
a) grinders dislike painters  b) grinding throws sparks — a fire hazard near paint work  c) grinding is too noisy
**Answer: b) grinding throws sparks — a fire hazard near paint work.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Every type of paint's label must be —
a) read and obeyed  b) kept for the shop's records  c) read only if the paint is new
**Answer: a) read and obeyed.**
