# Video script — Painting Tools — Applicators and Abrasives

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Two families in the painter's kit.

**Applicators** — brush for corners, roller for walls, tray for dipping, dusting brush before, putty knife for holes, hammer for proud nails.

**Abrasives** — sandpaper for smoothing, on a block for control; wire brush takes rust; scraper takes old paint off.

Drop sheets, gloves, dust masks protect. Turpentine and kerosene clean.

Prepare well, and the finish lasts.
