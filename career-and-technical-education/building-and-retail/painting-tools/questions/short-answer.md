# Painting Tools — Applicators and Abrasives — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does the dusting brush come before any applicator?
**Answer:** It removes dust and dirt before painting — paint laid on dust is wasted, so the surface is cleaned first.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the protection items in the table and what they protect.
**Answer:** Drop sheets, gloves and dust masks — they protect the painter and the surroundings while the work goes on.
