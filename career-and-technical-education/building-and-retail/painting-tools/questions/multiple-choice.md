# Painting Tools — Applicators and Abrasives — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The brush is for —
a) removing rust  b) large walls  c) smaller areas, corners and narrow spaces
**Answer: c) smaller areas, corners and narrow spaces.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The paint roller is for —
a) larger areas, and texture painting  b) filling small holes  c) scraping old paint
**Answer: a) larger areas, and texture painting.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The putty knife does what?
a) dips the roller in paint  b) removes dust before painting  c) applies fillers to small holes
**Answer: c) applies fillers to small holes.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Sanding is done with —
a) the dusting brush  b) a wire brush  c) sandpaper, in many types
**Answer: c) sandpaper, in many types.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A sanding block is —
a) a tray for sandpaper sheets  b) a block of solid abrasive stone  c) sandpaper wrapped round a block, for purchase and control
**Answer: c) sandpaper wrapped round a block, for purchase and control.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Rust on metal is removed with —
a) a wire brush  b) a scraper  c) a putty knife
**Answer: a) a wire brush.**
