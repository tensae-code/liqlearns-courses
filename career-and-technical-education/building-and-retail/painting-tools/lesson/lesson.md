# Painting Tools — Applicators and Abrasives

## Do it first

Ten tools lie on a sheet: two brushes, a hammer, a roller, a tray, a putty knife, papers and a block, a wire brush, a scraper. Half put paint ON. Half get the surface READY. Sort them by instinct — then check against the book's table.

## Sub lesson 1 — Applicators — the tools that put paint on

The **brush** paints smaller areas, corners and narrow spaces — where a roller cannot go, the brush goes.

The **hammer** belongs in the painting kit for punching and pushing nails in before painting — a nail standing proud would ruin a finish, so it is dealt with first.

The **paint roller** covers larger areas, and does texture painting. Its partner is the **tray**, for dipping the roller. The **dusting brush** removes dust and dirt before painting — paint laid on dust is paint wasted. The **putty knife** applies fillers to small holes, so the surface is whole before the coat goes on.

## Sub lesson 2 — Abrasives — the tools that prepare the surface

**Sandpaper** smooths surfaces — the process is called **sanding** — and comes in many types. A **sanding block** is sandpaper wrapped round a block, giving purchase and control while sanding.

The **wire brush** removes rust on metal. The **scraper** scrapes old paint off before re-painting. Preparation is half the trade: the finish can only be as good as the surface under it.

Protection and cleaning close the table: **drop sheets, gloves and dust masks** protect the painter and the surroundings, and **turpentine and kerosene** clean the tools afterwards.
