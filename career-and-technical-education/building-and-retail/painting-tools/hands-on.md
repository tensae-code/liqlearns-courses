# Hands-on — Painting Tools — Applicators and Abrasives

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes — real trade practice, done safely.

## Name-and-match drill

Write the ten tool names on slips and their jobs on other slips; shuffle and match. The two traps to watch: dusting brush (BEFORE painting, removes dust) and hammer (nails punched in BEFORE the coat).

## The preparation sequence

Put the preparation tools in working order for repainting an old metal gate: scraper (old paint off), wire brush (rust off), sandpaper (smooth), dusting brush (dust off) — then name the applicator you would finish with, and why.
