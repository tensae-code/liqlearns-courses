# Tyre Pressure — Why the Right Air Keeps Tyres Alive and People Safe

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — The promise of correct pressure

Maintaining proper tyre pressure gives two things at once: **vehicles long life and passengers safety**.

The mechanism for the first: **correct inflation prevents the accelerated wear that forces premature replacement**. A tyre at its right pressure rolls as it was designed to roll, and lasts. The tyre is not a detail — pressure is the cheapest maintenance a vehicle has.

## Sub lesson 2 — The danger of under-inflation

An **under-inflated** tyre is worked too hard by every turn of the road. It wears faster — the early replacement the book warns of.

Worse: under-inflated tyres **generate excessively high heat**, and that heat can **weaken the tyre to the point of failure**. Read the chain in order: too little air → too much wear and heat → a weakened tyre → failure, possibly at speed, with passengers aboard. That is why the next lesson's six-step check matters.
