# Video script — Tyre Pressure — Why the Right Air Keeps Tyres Alive and People Safe

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Air is a safety device. Its name is **tyre pressure**.

Keep it correct, and two things follow: the tyre lives long — no early replacement — and passengers ride safe.

Let it fall, and the chain starts: soft tyre, fast wear, **excessive heat**.

Heat weakens the tyre. A weakened tyre can **fail**.

Next lesson: the six-step check that stops the chain before it starts.
