# Hands-on — Tyre Pressure — Why the Right Air Keeps Tyres Alive and People Safe

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes — real trade practice, done safely.

## Read a tyre by eye (look only)

Look at parked vehicles' tyres (do not touch). A tyre bulging at the bottom and squashed wider where it meets the road is showing low air. Sketch a healthy tyre's shape and a soft tyre's shape side by side, and label the difference.

## Tell the chain

Write the under-inflation chain as five arrows on paper — too little air → … → failure — and under each arrow, one line saying why the next step follows.
