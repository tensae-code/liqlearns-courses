# Tyre Pressure — Why the Right Air Keeps Tyres Alive and People Safe — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Maintaining proper tyre pressure gives —
a) cheaper fuel only  b) a quieter engine  c) vehicles long life and passengers safety
**Answer: c) vehicles long life and passengers safety.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Correct inflation prevents —
a) all punctures forever  b) the need to check pressure  c) the accelerated wear that forces premature replacement
**Answer: c) the accelerated wear that forces premature replacement.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A correctly inflated tyre —
a) needs replacing sooner  b) rolls as designed and lasts  c) generates excessive heat
**Answer: b) rolls as designed and lasts.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Under-inflated tyres generate —
a) extra grip that saves them  b) cooling airflow  c) excessively high heat
**Answer: c) excessively high heat.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Excessive heat in a soft tyre can —
a) weaken the tyre to the point of failure  b) strengthen the rubber  c) repair small cuts
**Answer: a) weaken the tyre to the point of failure.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The under-inflation chain in order is —
a) low air → better grip → long life  b) too little air → fast wear and heat → weakened tyre → failure  c) failure → heat → wear → low air
**Answer: b) too little air → fast wear and heat → weakened tyre → failure.**
