# Tyre Pressure — Why the Right Air Keeps Tyres Alive and People Safe — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tyre pressure is the cheapest maintenance a vehicle has.
**Answer: True** — keeping air correct prevents early replacement — the lesson's framing of the book's point.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A soft tyre is only a comfort problem.
**Answer: False** — it is a wear problem and a safety problem — heat can weaken the tyre to failure.
