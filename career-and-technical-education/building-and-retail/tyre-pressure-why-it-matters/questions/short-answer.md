# Tyre Pressure — Why the Right Air Keeps Tyres Alive and People Safe — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What are the two promises of correct pressure?
**Answer:** Long life for the vehicle (its tyres) and safety for the passengers.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does this lesson come before the checking procedure?
**Answer:** Because the check only matters if you know what it prevents — early replacement through wear, and failure through heat. The reason gives the six steps their weight.
