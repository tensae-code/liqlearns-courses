# Construction, Carpentry, Tyres and Retail — Review Questions — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1.** Write the book's answer: the raw materials of concrete, with the standard ratio.
**Answer:** Powdered Portland cement, water, sand and gravel — mixed 1:2:3 (1 cement, 2 sand, 3 stone) with adequate water.

**2.** Write the book's answer: the steps of checking tyre pressure.
**Answer:** Read the placard or manual values; unscrew the valve cap; push the gauge firmly onto the valve; read the pressure; if low, inflate to the correct level and recheck; screw the cap back on.

**3.** Write the book's answer: three problems of cash payment.
**Answer:** Any three of: payment can take up to a week; collecting/dealing eats service time; carrying cash risks robbery; large cash handling leaks (missing, stolen, bribes); checking/processing takes too much time; paper money is short-lived and costly to print.

**4.** Write the book's answer: two carpentry skills and two hand tools.
**Answer:** Skills, any two of framing, staircases, windows/doors/siding, roofing, trim and hardware. Hand tools, any two of saws, hammer, plane, chisels.
