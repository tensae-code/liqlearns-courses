# Construction, Carpentry, Tyres and Retail — Review Questions — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or words.

**1.** Tyre pressure is read ______ (before driving heats the tyre), against the placard values.
**Answer: cold.**

**2.** The instrument that measures tyre pressure is the pressure ______.
**Answer: gauge.**

**3.** ______ business buys from wholesalers and manufacturers and sells to final consumers for profit.
**Answer: Retail.**

**4.** ______ is coating a surface with paint; ______ smooths a surface with sandpaper.
**Answer: Painting; sanding.**
