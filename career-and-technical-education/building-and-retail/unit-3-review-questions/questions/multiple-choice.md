# Construction, Carpentry, Tyres and Retail — Review Questions — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Concrete's ingredients are —
a) cement, water, sand and gravel  b) cement and clay only  c) sand, gravel and timber
**Answer: a) cement, water, sand and gravel.**

**2.** The payment type cheapest to set up is —
a) credit cards  b) bank transfers  c) cash — it needs no reader or account
**Answer: c) cash — it needs no reader or account.**

**3.** Decorating a surface is the skill of —
a) carpentry  b) painting and decorating  c) concrete work
**Answer: b) painting and decorating.**
