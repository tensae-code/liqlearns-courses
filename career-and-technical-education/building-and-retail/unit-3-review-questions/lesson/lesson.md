## The study sheet — what the book's review tests

**Concrete and tools.** Concrete is cement, water, sand and gravel in the 1:2:3 ratio (1 cement, 2 sand, 3 stone). The trowel — misprinted 'Towel' in Table 3.1 — digs, applies and smooths small amounts of material. Carpentry is cutting, shaping and installing timber; its skills run framing, staircases, windows, doors, siding, roofing, trim and hardware. Painting is coating a surface; sanding smooths with sandpaper.

**Tyres and payments.** Tyre pressure is checked cold with a pen gauge against the placard or manual: placard, cap off, gauge on, read, inflate and recheck if low, cap on. Cash is the payment type cheapest to set up — it needs no reader or account — yet the book lists six problems with it, from the week's delay to leakage. Credit cards' advantage: more frequent or larger purchases, made safely.

**Selling.** The seven steps: A prospecting, B pre-approach, C approach, D presentation, E handling objections, F closing, G follow-up. The book's review also tests squared diagonals (a checked square's diagonals are equal — the written/true-false corner carried from the unit's construction content), the definition of retail business (buying from wholesalers/manufacturers and selling to final consumers for profit), and the tyre-pressure instrument's name (the pressure gauge).
