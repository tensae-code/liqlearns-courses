# Hands-on — Construction, Carpentry, Tyres and Retail — Review Questions

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes — real trade practice, done safely.

## Self-marked test pass

Take all the questions in questions/ with the lessons closed. Mark yourself honestly against the answers given. For every miss, write the lesson it belongs to — that list is your revision plan.

## Teach one answer

Choose the written answer you found hardest (concrete's materials, the tyre procedure, or cash's problems) and teach it aloud in two minutes, as the teacher guide's presentation pattern asks. Teaching exposes the gap the test only marks.
