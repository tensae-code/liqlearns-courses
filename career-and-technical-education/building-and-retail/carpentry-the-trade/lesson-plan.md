# Lesson plan — Carpentry — the Trade and Its Basic Skills

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** Building and Retail · **Lesson:** Carpentry — the Trade and Its Basic Skills
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 7 — Building and Retail (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** what carpentry is and the carpenter's basic skill set — framing, staircases, windows, doors, siding, roofing, trim and hardware, measuring, cutting and joining.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the title's opening lessons: the learner knows carpentry as timber work; now the trade's full skill set.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an
image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture
of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does
with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and
while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is
dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the
student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph,
or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be
shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
1. **What carpentry is** — the skilled trade of cutting, shaping and installing building materials
2. **The basic skill set** — framing, staircases, windows, doors, siding, roofing, trim and hardware

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Construction and Carpentry — the Building Trades and Their Key Words. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Define carpentry as a skilled trade and name where its materials are installed.
2. List the basic skills: framing, staircases, windows, doors, siding, roofing, trim, hardware.
3. Place measuring, cutting and joining at the trade's heart.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil.

1. **Do it first** — the learner lists everything wooden in one room before the skill set is shown (lesson/lesson.md).
2. **The explanation** — the trade defined, then the basic skill set in the book's order (lesson/lesson.md).

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks carpentry = making furniture only → return to the definition: cutting, shaping and INSTALLING building materials — buildings, ships, bridges, formwork.
- Lists skills randomly → group them as the book does: framing; staircases; windows/doors/siding; roofing; trim/doors/stairs/hardware — over the base of measure-cut-join.
