# Hands-on — Carpentry — the Trade and Its Basic Skills

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes — real trade practice, done safely.

## Audit one building

Walk through one building and tick the skills you can spot: a framed wall, a staircase, a fitted window, a door with its hardware, trim, a roof. Write one line per skill found — where is it, and what is it made of?

## Measure, mark, (do not) cut

Measure a door or window frame with a tape and write its width and height. Carpenters measure twice in real work; measure yours twice and check the numbers agree. No cutting in this practice — measuring is the skill being trained.
