**Carpentry** — cutting, shaping, installing. A skilled trade in timber.

Its work stands in buildings, ships, bridges, even the moulds for concrete.

The skills: framing walls and floors. Building staircases. Fitting windows, doors, siding. Roofing. Trim and hardware.

And under everything: measure, cut, join. Measure, cut, join.
