# Carpentry — the Trade and Its Basic Skills — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Carpentry is the skilled trade of —
a) selling timber in a shop  b) cutting, shaping and installing building materials  c) mixing and pouring concrete
**Answer: b) cutting, shaping and installing building materials.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Carpentry work appears in —
a) only picture frames  b) buildings, ships, timber bridges and concrete formwork  c) only kitchen furniture
**Answer: b) buildings, ships, timber bridges and concrete formwork.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The three verbs at the trade's heart are —
a) cutting, shaping, installing  b) buying, selling, delivering
**Answer: a) cutting, shaping, installing.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** General framing covers —
a) door handles and hinges only  b) walls, floors and doorframes  c) roofs only
**Answer: b) walls, floors and doorframes.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Which list is part of the basic skill set?
a) building staircases; installing windows, doors and siding; installing roofing systems  b) painting walls; mixing concrete; checking tyres
**Answer: a) building staircases; installing windows, doors and siding; installing roofing systems.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The foundation skill under all carpentry is —
a) buying the cheapest timber  b) estimating by eye  c) measuring, cutting and joining wood or wood substitutes
**Answer: c) measuring, cutting and joining wood or wood substitutes.**
