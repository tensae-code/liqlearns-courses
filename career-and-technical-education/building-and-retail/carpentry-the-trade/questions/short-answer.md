# Carpentry — the Trade and Its Basic Skills — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why is formwork — the mould concrete is poured into — carpentry work?
**Answer:** Because carpentry is cutting, shaping and installing building materials, and formwork is timber shaped and installed to hold concrete — the book names concrete formwork among carpentry's fields.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** List the basic skill set in the book's order.
**Answer:** General framing (walls, floors, doorframes); building staircases; installing windows, doors and siding; installing roofing systems; fitting and installing trim, doors, stairs and hardware — on the foundation of measuring, cutting and joining.
