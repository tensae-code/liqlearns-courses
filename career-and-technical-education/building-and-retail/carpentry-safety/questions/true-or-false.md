# Carpentry Safety — Glasses, Hearing Protection and Protective Gear — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A face shield can serve as the eye protection the book requires.
**Answer: True** — the rule reads: safety glasses or goggles, OR a face shield.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Booties and hats are part of the book's protective set.
**Answer: True** — both are named in the book's list.
