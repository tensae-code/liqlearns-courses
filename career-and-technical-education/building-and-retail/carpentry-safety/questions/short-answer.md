# Carpentry Safety — Glasses, Hearing Protection and Protective Gear — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why must hearing protection be matched to the shop's noise rather than one standard pair for everywhere?
**Answer:** Because shops differ in noise level and frequency — a hand-tool bench and a powered-saw room are not the same noise, and the protection must fit the noise you are actually in.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A pupil wears gloves only and says he is protected. Answer him from the book.
**Answer:** The book's set is seven items guarding different exposures — eyes and ears have their own standing rules, and face, body, breathing, head and feet each have their gear. Gloves alone leave the rest unguarded.
