# Carpentry Safety — Glasses, Hearing Protection and Protective Gear — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the carpentry shop, your eyes must wear —
a) ordinary sunglasses  b) safety glasses or goggles, or a face shield  c) nothing, if you are careful
**Answer: b) safety glasses or goggles, or a face shield.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Hearing protection must be —
a) worn only for hand tools  b) the same for every shop and task  c) matched to the noise level and frequency of the woodworking shop
**Answer: c) matched to the noise level and frequency of the woodworking shop.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The eye rule applies —
a) only for beginners  b) only when a machine is loud  c) always, in every wood-work task
**Answer: c) always, in every wood-work task.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which list is the book's full protective set?
a) apron, sandals and a scarf  b) gloves, gowns/coats, face shields or masks, eye protection, resuscitation masks, hats and booties  c) gloves and a hat only
**Answer: b) gloves, gowns/coats, face shields or masks, eye protection, resuscitation masks, hats and booties.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Resuscitation masks appear in the book's list as —
a) part of the protective set for the shop  b) a kind of dust mask for sanding only  c) a first-aid item unrelated to carpentry
**Answer: a) part of the protective set for the shop.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The gear list works as —
a) a system: each item guards a different exposure  b) a menu: pick whichever item you like
**Answer: a) a system: each item guards a different exposure.**
