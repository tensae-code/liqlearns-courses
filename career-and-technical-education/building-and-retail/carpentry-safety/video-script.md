# Video script — Carpentry Safety — Glasses, Hearing Protection and Protective Gear

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Wood work throws chips, dust and noise at you. The book's rules are short.

**Eyes** — safety glasses, goggles, or a face shield. Always.

**Ears** — protection matched to the shop's noise: its level and its frequency.

And the full set: gloves. Gowns or coats. Face shields or masks. Eye protection. Resuscitation masks. Hats. Booties.

Gear first. Tools second. Every time.
