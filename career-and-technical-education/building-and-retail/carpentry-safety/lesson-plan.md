# Lesson plan — Carpentry Safety — Glasses, Hearing Protection and Protective Gear

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** Building and Retail · **Lesson:** Carpentry Safety — Glasses, Hearing Protection and Protective Gear
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 7 — Building and Retail (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the carpentry shop's safety rules — eye protection always, hearing protection matched to the noise, and the full protective set the book lists.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Carpentry Tools: the learner can sort hand from power tools; this lesson is the safety gate before handling either family.

## Sub lessons
1. **Eyes and ears — the two standing rules** — safety glasses or a face shield; hearing protection matched to the noise
2. **The full protective set** — the book's complete list of protective materials

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Carpentry Tools — Hand Tools and Power Tools. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the eye rule: safety glasses or goggles, or a face shield.
2. State the hearing rule: protection matched to the noise level and frequency of the shop.
3. List the book's full protective set without missing an item.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. No tool handling is required by this lesson.

## If the learner is stuck
- Wears glasses only for power tools → the book's word is ALWAYS: chips and dust fly in hand work too.
- Recites a partial gear list → the set has seven entries; count them on fingers: gloves, gowns/coats, face shields/masks, eye protection, resuscitation masks, hats, booties.
