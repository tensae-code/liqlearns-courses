# Hands-on — Carpentry Safety — Glasses, Hearing Protection and Protective Gear

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes — real trade practice, done safely.

## Present the rules

Following the book's own class activity: write the shop rules as a one-page poster — eye rule, hearing rule, full gear list — and present it aloud as if to a new pupil joining the shop. Teaching it is the test of knowing it.

## Match gear to exposure

Draw a worker figure and draw a line from each gear item to the body part it guards. Any item you cannot place is the one to re-read in the lesson.
