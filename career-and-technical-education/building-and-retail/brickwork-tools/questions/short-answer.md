# Brickwork Tools — Float, Trowel, Wheelbarrow, Tape, Shovel — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Float or trowel: which smooths a whole fresh surface, and which places little amounts of mortar?
**Answer:** The float smooths the surface; the trowel places and smooths small amounts of material such as mortar.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the five tools of Table 3.1 and one job for each.
**Answer:** Float — smooths concrete; trowel — applies/smooths small amounts of mortar; wheelbarrow — carries loads on one wheel; tape measure — measures size and distance; shovel — digs and moves bulk material.
