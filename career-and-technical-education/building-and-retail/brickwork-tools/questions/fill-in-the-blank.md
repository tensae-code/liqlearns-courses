# Brickwork Tools — Float, Trowel, Wheelbarrow, Tape, Shovel — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Mortar is placed in small exact amounts with the ______.
**Answer: trowel.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The wheelbarrow is pushed and guided by two ______ at the rear.
**Answer: handles.**
