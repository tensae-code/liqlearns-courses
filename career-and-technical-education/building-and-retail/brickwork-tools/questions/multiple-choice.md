# Brickwork Tools — Float, Trowel, Wheelbarrow, Tape, Shovel — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The tool that finishes a concrete surface smooth is the —
a) tape measure  b) trowel  c) concrete float
**Answer: c) concrete float.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A trowel is used for —
a) carrying loads around a site  b) measuring long distances  c) digging, applying, smoothing or moving small amounts of viscous or particulate material
**Answer: c) digging, applying, smoothing or moving small amounts of viscous or particulate material.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A tape measure is —
a) a flat board for smoothing  b) a one-wheeled vehicle  c) a flexible ruler for measuring size and distance
**Answer: c) a flexible ruler for measuring size and distance.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A wheelbarrow is —
a) a one-wheeled hand vehicle pushed and guided by two rear handles  b) a flexible measuring ruler  c) a two-wheeled cart pulled by an animal
**Answer: a) a one-wheeled hand vehicle pushed and guided by two rear handles.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A shovel is for —
a) smoothing finished concrete  b) digging, lifting and moving bulk material such as soil, gravel and sand  c) measuring a wall
**Answer: b) digging, lifting and moving bulk material such as soil, gravel and sand.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Which pair moves material around the site?
a) float and tape measure  b) trowel and tape measure  c) wheelbarrow and shovel
**Answer: c) wheelbarrow and shovel.**
