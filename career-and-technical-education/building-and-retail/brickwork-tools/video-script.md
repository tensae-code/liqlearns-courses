# Video script — Brickwork Tools — Float, Trowel, Wheelbarrow, Tape, Shovel

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Five tools build the wall. Learn them by their jobs.

The **float** — smooths fresh concrete flat.

The **trowel** — small blade, big work: digs, applies, smooths mortar, little by little.

The **tape measure** — a flexible ruler. Measure, do not guess.

The **wheelbarrow** — one wheel, two handles, carries the load.

The **shovel** — digs and lifts the bulk: sand, gravel, soil.

Five tools. Five jobs. Say them back.
