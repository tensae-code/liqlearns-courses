# Brickwork Tools — Float, Trowel, Wheelbarrow, Tape, Shovel

## Do it first

Five tools lie on a building site: a flat float, a small pointed blade on a handle, a one-wheeled barrow, a flexible ruler in a case, a spade. Before you read on: which one smooths a fresh concrete surface? Which one measures the wall? Guess all five.

## Sub lesson 1 — Smoothing and measuring — float, trowel, tape measure

The **concrete float** finishes a concrete surface smooth. When fresh concrete has been laid, the float is the tool that levels and smooths its face.

The **trowel** is the small hand tool for digging, applying, smoothing or moving small amounts of viscous or particulate material — mortar above all. It is the bricklayer's constant companion: a little mortar, placed exactly, again and again. (The book's table prints this tool's name as 'Towel' — that is a misprint; the tool described is a trowel, and this lesson teaches it by its true name.)

The **tape measure** is a flexible ruler used to measure size and distance. Walls, blocks and mortar joints all live or die by measure — the tape is how a builder knows, instead of guessing.

## Sub lesson 2 — Moving and digging — wheelbarrow and shovel

The **wheelbarrow** is a small hand-propelled vehicle with one wheel, pushed and guided by two handles at the rear. It is how sand, stone, mortar and blocks travel around a site — one wheel, two handles, the load between.

The **shovel** digs, lifts and moves bulk material — soil, coal, gravel, snow, sand, ore. Where the trowel moves spoonfuls, the shovel moves loads: it fills the wheelbarrow and feeds the mix.

## From the teacher guide (evidence)

The teacher guide (evidence) assesses this trade at a real workplace: learners are taken to where the work is done and briefed on building basic structures, using tools and equipment correctly, protective measures and constructing brick walls — then ask about anything unclear. The hands-on activity in this lesson follows that pattern: observe at a real site, then ask.

## Say it to your companion

Name the five tools to your companion and give each one's job in a few words — float, trowel, tape measure, wheelbarrow, shovel. End with the warning: which tool's name does the book misprint?

## Do It Yourself

1. Draw the five tools in a row and label each with its job.
2. Measure one block or brick three ways (length, width, height) and write the numbers.
3. Write one sentence: which tool moves spoonfuls, and which moves loads?
