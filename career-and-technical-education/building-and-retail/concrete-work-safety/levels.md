# Concrete-Work Safety — Protecting Skin, Head, Eyes, Ears, Feet and Back — sub lessons and depth

This file is the depth record for the lesson, re-expressed as its sub lessons (levels, not grades — the learner climbs when ready).

## Sub lesson 1 — Skin, head and eyes — the concrete dangers

Pass mark: the learner can state the skin danger and the wash-at-once rule, the hard-hat rules, and the eye rules including no contact lenses. The gate draws from this sub lesson's whole question set in questions/.

## Sub lesson 2 — Ears, feet and back — hearing, boots and the safe lift

Pass mark: the learner can state the ear, foot and back protections, and describe the safe lift in order. The gate draws from this sub lesson's whole question set in questions/.
