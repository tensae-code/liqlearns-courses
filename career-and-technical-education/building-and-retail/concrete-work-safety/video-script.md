# Video script — Concrete-Work Safety — Protecting Skin, Head, Eyes, Ears, Feet and Back

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Concrete work can hurt you. Protect six things.

**Skin** — wet cement burns, and the pain comes hours later. Wash it off at once.

**Head** — an approved hard hat. Never drilled, never baked on a car's back shelf, never steel.

**Eyes** — shatterproof protection, always. No contact lenses on the job.

**Ears** — hearing loss has no cure. Earplugs in.

**Feet** — steel toes against crush and puncture; rubber boots in wet concrete.

**Back** — straight back, bent knees, load close, lift with the legs. Turn with your feet. Never twist.
