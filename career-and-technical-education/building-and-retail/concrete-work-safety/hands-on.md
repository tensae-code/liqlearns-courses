# Hands-on — Concrete-Work Safety — Protecting Skin, Head, Eyes, Ears, Feet and Back

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes — real trade practice, done safely.

## Practise the safe lift (light load only)

Use an empty cardboard box. Practise the book's lift five times: back straight, knees bent, box close, lift with the legs; set it down, pivot your feet to turn — never twist. A partner watches and calls out any twist.

## The hard-hat inspection

If a hard hat is available to look at (an adult's, not worn for play), check it against the book's rules: any drill holes? cracks or damage? Has it been living on a car's rear shelf? Write the verdict: fit for the site, or not — and why.
