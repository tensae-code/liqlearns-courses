# Concrete-Work Safety — Protecting Skin, Head, Eyes, Ears, Feet and Back — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A steel hard hat is a good choice because it is strong.
**Answer: False** — never steel — steel conducts electricity. The hat must be approved and cared for.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Noise-induced hearing loss can be cured later.
**Answer: False** — the book is plain: no cure exists — that is why earplugs are worn.
