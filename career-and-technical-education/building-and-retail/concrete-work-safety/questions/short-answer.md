# Concrete-Work Safety — Protecting Skin, Head, Eyes, Ears, Feet and Back — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does the book ban contact lenses on the job, alongside requiring eye protection?
**Answer:** Eyes must have shatterproof safety protection at all times, and the book's rule is plain: no contact lenses on the job.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give the foot protection rules and say what each guards against.
**Answer:** Steel-toed safety boots guard against falling objects, crushing and punctures; waterproof rubber boots are for standing in wet concrete.
