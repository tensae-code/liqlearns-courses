# Concrete-Work Safety — Protecting Skin, Head, Eyes, Ears, Feet and Back — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Wet concrete and cement mixtures can cause —
a) only a mild itch that passes  b) no harm at all if you are careful  c) irritation and chemical burns up to third-degree, and serious eye damage
**Answer: c) irritation and chemical burns up to third-degree, and serious eye damage.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Cement on your skin must be —
a) left until it starts to hurt  b) wiped off at the end of the day  c) washed off at once
**Answer: c) washed off at once.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A hard hat on site must be —
a) steel, for strength  b) any cap that fits  c) approved and cared for — never drilled, never stored on a car's rear shelf
**Answer: c) approved and cared for — never drilled, never stored on a car's rear shelf.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** For noise on site, the book's protection is —
a) self-fitting earplugs  b) cotton wool balls  c) nothing — you get used to it
**Answer: a) self-fitting earplugs.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Standing in wet concrete, wear —
a) waterproof rubber boots  b) ordinary shoes  c) sandals, so you can wash your feet
**Answer: a) waterproof rubber boots.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The safe lift is —
a) back bent, load at arm's length, twist to turn  b) knees straight, lift with the back  c) back straight, knees bent, load close, lift with the legs, pivot the feet
**Answer: c) back straight, knees bent, load close, lift with the legs, pivot the feet.**
