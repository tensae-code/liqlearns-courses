# Concrete-Work Safety — Protecting Skin, Head, Eyes, Ears, Feet and Back — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Cement burns hurt only ______ later — so contact is washed off at once.
**Answer: hours.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** To turn while carrying a load, ______ the feet — never twist the back.
**Answer: pivot.**
