# Colouring cloth — nine techniques — sub lessons and depth

This is the depth record of the lesson, re-expressed as sub lessons. A learner passes a sub lesson by its questions (the gate pool is that sub lesson's whole question set — see lesson-plan.md), then moves on; earlier sub lessons are revisited in mixed practice.

## Sub lesson 1 — Hand techniques
Pass mark: the learner can answer this sub lesson's questions from the lesson — in particular: the dipped end of a pencil or an ear bud; a thin 00 liner brush or outliner; when no brushes exist.

## Sub lesson 2 — Stamp, stencil and spray
Pass mark: the learner can answer this sub lesson's questions from the lesson — in particular: cut potato or other vegetables; stops colour soaking through; cover nose and mouth against spray chemicals.

## Sub lesson 3 — Sun colouring
Pass mark: the learner can answer this sub lesson's questions from the lesson — in particular: faint highlighted impressions; in full sun.
