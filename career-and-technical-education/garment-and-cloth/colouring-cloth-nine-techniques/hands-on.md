# Hands-on — Colouring cloth — nine techniques

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes. Machine work follows the book's own steps exactly, with a teacher or practitioner present as the books direct.

## Dot drill (the book's practice)
Practise dot colouring with an ear bud: dip, print, lift — a row of even dots, then a flower made of dots.

## Make a stamp (the book's practice)
Cut a potato stamp (with a teacher's help with the knife), press it into colour, and print a repeating pattern. Then finger-colour a second cloth in class, as the book sets.

## One-stroke petal
Load a flat brush with two shades side by side and sweep one petal in a single stroke. If you go back over it, start again — one stroke only.
