# Colouring cloth — nine techniques — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Dot colouring is printed with —
a) a spray bottle  b) a stencil  c) the dipped end of a pencil or an ear bud
**Answer: c) the dipped end of a pencil or an ear bud.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Outliner painting uses —
a) a potato stamp  b) a thin 00 liner brush or outliner  c) fingers only
**Answer: b) a thin 00 liner brush or outliner.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Finger colouring is used —
a) when no brushes exist  b) only on paper  c) to wash fabric
**Answer: a) when no brushes exist.**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** One-stroke colouring paints a petal or leaf —
a) in ten strokes  b) with a spray  c) in a single stroke, with a flat brush loaded with one or more shades
**Answer: c) in a single stroke, with a flat brush loaded with one or more shades.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A home block stamp can be made from —
a) metal sheet  b) cut potato or other vegetables  c) glass
**Answer: b) cut potato or other vegetables.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Inside a T-shirt during stencil colouring, a hard board —
a) stops colour soaking through  b) makes the colour brighter  c) dries the paint
**Answer: a) stops colour soaking through.**

**7. (Sub lesson 2)** When spray colouring, you must —
a) close your eyes only  b) work in the dark  c) cover nose and mouth against spray chemicals
**Answer: c) cover nose and mouth against spray chemicals.**

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** In sun colouring, the covered shapes remain as —
a) stitches  b) faint highlighted impressions  c) dark holes
**Answer: b) faint highlighted impressions.**

**9. (Sub lesson 3)** Sun colouring is dried —
a) in full sun  b) in a freezer  c) under water
**Answer: a) in full sun.**
