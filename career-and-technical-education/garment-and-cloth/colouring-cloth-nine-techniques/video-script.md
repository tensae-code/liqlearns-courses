# Video script — Colouring cloth — nine techniques

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Nine ways to put colour on cloth.
Dot with an ear bud. Outline with a fine brush.
Press a potato block. Lift a stencil carefully.
Spray — nose and mouth covered.
One stroke paints a petal. The sun prints a leaf.
