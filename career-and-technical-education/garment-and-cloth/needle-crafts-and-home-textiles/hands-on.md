# Hands-on — Needle crafts — handmade textiles and selling them

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes. Machine work follows the book's own steps exactly, with a teacher or practitioner present as the books direct.

## Photograph your locality (the book's task)
Photograph needle/textile crafts from your own locality, as the book sets. Name each craft, what it decorates, and who might buy it.

## Plan one product
Choose one home textile you could make — a table cover, say. List its materials, its cost, who would buy it, and how a social-media profile would find those buyers.
