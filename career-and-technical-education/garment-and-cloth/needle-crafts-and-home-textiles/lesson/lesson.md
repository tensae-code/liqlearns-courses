# Needle crafts — handmade textiles and selling them

## Do it first

Before any explanation, try this in your own words: needle/textile crafts are made by hand with needle and thread for decoration — and home textiles are a real business with named advantages.

Say what you already think, out loud or on paper. If parts will not come yet, that is exactly what this lesson is for.

## Sub lesson 1 — Needle crafts and home textiles

**Needle/textile crafts** are made by hand with needle and thread on a textile product, for decorative and attractive purposes.

**Home textiles** — furnishing materials, curtains, carpets, kitchen items, table covers, bedspreads, bath linen — form a significant part of the garment market.

## Sub lesson 2 — The home-textile business — its advantages

- Consistent revenue as a full-time opportunity.
- Small daily expenditure yielding high returns.
- The chance to grow a small business into a national brand.
- Easy customer-building through a social-media profile.
- Easy contact with wholesalers and suppliers.
- A long-term, never-ending market.
