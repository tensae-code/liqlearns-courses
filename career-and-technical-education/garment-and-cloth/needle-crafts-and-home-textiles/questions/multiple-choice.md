# Needle crafts — handmade textiles and selling them — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Needle crafts are made —
a) by machine only, for strength  b) with paint, for colour  c) by hand with needle and thread, for decorative purposes
**Answer: c) by hand with needle and thread, for decorative purposes.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which is a home textile?
a) a cooking pot  b) a bedspread  c) a plough
**Answer: b) a bedspread.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Home textiles form —
a) a significant part of the garment market  b) no part of any market  c) only an export market
**Answer: a) a significant part of the garment market.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A home-textile business advantage is —
a) guaranteed riches in a day  b) no customers needed  c) small daily expenditure yielding high returns
**Answer: c) small daily expenditure yielding high returns.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Customers can be built easily through —
a) hiding the products  b) a social-media profile  c) never speaking to anyone
**Answer: b) a social-media profile.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The home-textile market is described as —
a) long-term and never-ending  b) ending next year  c) only seasonal
**Answer: a) long-term and never-ending.**
