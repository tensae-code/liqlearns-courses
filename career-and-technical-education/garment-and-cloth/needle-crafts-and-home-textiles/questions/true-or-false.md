# Needle crafts — handmade textiles and selling them — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Needle crafts are made by hand with needle and thread, for decorative purposes.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A home-textile business advantage is guaranteed riches in a day.
