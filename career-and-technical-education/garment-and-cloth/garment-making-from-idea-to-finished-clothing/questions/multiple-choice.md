# Garment making — from idea to finished clothing — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A garment is —
a) a sewing tool  b) a kind of fabric  c) a piece of clothing
**Answer: c) a piece of clothing.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Garment making runs from —
a) washing to ironing only  b) the idea or design concept to the finished product  c) the shop to the customer only
**Answer: b) the idea or design concept to the finished product.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The first step in the process is —
a) choose the fabric  b) cut  c) package for delivery
**Answer: a) choose the fabric.**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The last step in the process is —
a) design  b) stitch  c) package for delivery
**Answer: c) package for delivery.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The tailor's saying is —
a) sew fast, check never  b) measure ten times, then cut  c) cut first, measure later
**Answer: b) measure ten times, then cut.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Why measure ten times before cutting?
a) A wrong cut cannot be undone  b) Fabric is always cheap  c) Measuring is the finished product
**Answer: a) A wrong cut cannot be undone.**
