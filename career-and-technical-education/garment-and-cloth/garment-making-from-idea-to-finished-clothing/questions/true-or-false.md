# Garment making — from idea to finished clothing — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** The tailor's saying is measure ten times, then cut.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A garment is a sewing tool.
**Answer: False** — a piece of clothing.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The last step in the process is package for delivery.
**Answer: True.**
