# Hands-on — Garment making — from idea to finished clothing

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes. Machine work follows the book's own steps exactly, with a teacher or practitioner present as the books direct.

## Walk the eight stations
Lay eight papers on the floor in order — fabric, design, cut, stitch, sew, check, press, package. Walk from one to the next, saying at each station what happens there and what goes wrong if it is skipped.

## Measure ten times
Take a piece of scrap cloth. Measure one line on it, then measure it again nine more times before you are allowed to mark it. Feel why the saying exists.
