# Video script — Garment making — from idea to finished clothing

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A garment starts as an idea.
Choose the fabric. Design. Cut.
Stitch. Sew. Check. Press. Package.
Measure ten times — then cut.
That is garment making, idea to finished clothing.
