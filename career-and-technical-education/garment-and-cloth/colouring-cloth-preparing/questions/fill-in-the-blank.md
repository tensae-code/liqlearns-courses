# Colouring cloth — decorating fabric, and preparing to do it — fill in the blank

Fill in the blank with the right words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Coloured cloth decorates ______.
**Answer: clothing and home furnishings such as curtains and tablecloths.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The colouring area should be ______.
**Answer: a flat, smooth surface such as a table or counter.**
