# Colouring cloth — decorating fabric, and preparing to do it — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Textile colouring applies —
a) stitches to a seam  b) buttons to a shirt  c) specialised colour to a cloth surface
**Answer: c) specialised colour to a cloth surface.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Coloured cloth decorates —
a) only shoes  b) clothing and home furnishings such as curtains and tablecloths  c) only school books
**Answer: b) clothing and home furnishings such as curtains and tablecloths.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Natural and synthetic fabrics —
a) take colour differently  b) take colour identically  c) cannot be coloured
**Answer: a) take colour differently.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Pre-washing fabric removes —
a) all colour forever  b) the need to iron  c) dirt, shrinkage, chemicals and starch
**Answer: c) dirt, shrinkage, chemicals and starch.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The colouring area should be —
a) held in the hands  b) a flat, smooth surface such as a table or counter  c) a sloping floor
**Answer: b) a flat, smooth surface such as a table or counter.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Colour is chosen by —
a) the fabric kind and the wearer's need  b) chance  c) the cheapest price only
**Answer: a) the fabric kind and the wearer's need.**
