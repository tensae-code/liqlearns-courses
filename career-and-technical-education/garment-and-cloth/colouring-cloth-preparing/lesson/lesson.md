# Colouring cloth — decorating fabric, and preparing to do it

## Do it first

Before any explanation, try this in your own words: textile colouring applies specialised colour to a cloth surface to decorate clothing and home furnishings — and four preparation guidelines come before any colour.

Say what you already think, out loud or on paper. If parts will not come yet, that is exactly what this lesson is for.

## Sub lesson 1 — What textile colouring is

**Textile (fabric) colouring** applies specialised colour to a cloth surface — a graphic art of artistic composition on fabric.

It decorates clothing and home furnishings such as curtains and tablecloths, making cloth attractive and opening creative expression.

## Sub lesson 2 — Prepare in four guidelines

1. **Choose the appropriate fabric** — natural and synthetic kinds take colour differently, and the right fabric decides success.
2. **Prepare the fabric** — pre-wash to remove dirt, shrinkage, chemicals and starch, then dry and iron.
3. **Set up the colouring area** on a flat, smooth surface such as a table or counter.
4. **Set and select the colour**, chosen by the fabric kind and the wearer's need.

Then — and only then — the actual colouring begins.

## Do It Yourself

1. **Prepare a cloth properly.** Pre-wash a small cloth, dry it, iron it flat, and lay it on a flat smooth table. Say the four guidelines aloud as you complete each one.
2. **Test two fabrics.** Put one drop of colour on a natural-fibre scrap and one on a synthetic scrap. Compare how each takes the colour — this is why guideline 1 exists.
