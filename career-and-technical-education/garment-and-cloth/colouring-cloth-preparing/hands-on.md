# Hands-on — Colouring cloth — decorating fabric, and preparing to do it

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes. Machine work follows the book's own steps exactly, with a teacher or practitioner present as the books direct.

## Prepare a cloth properly
Pre-wash a small cloth, dry it, iron it flat, and lay it on a flat smooth table. Say the four guidelines aloud as you complete each one.

## Test two fabrics
Put one drop of colour on a natural-fibre scrap and one on a synthetic scrap. Compare how each takes the colour — this is why guideline 1 exists.
