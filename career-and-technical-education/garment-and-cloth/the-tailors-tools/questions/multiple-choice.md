# The tailor's tools — measuring, marking, cutting, pressing — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A measuring tape shows —
a) only centimetres  b) only inches  c) centimetres on one side, inches on the other
**Answer: c) centimetres on one side, inches on the other.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Tailor's chalk is used for —
a) pressing fabric  b) removable fabric marking  c) cutting fabric
**Answer: b) removable fabric marking.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** If tailor's chalk is missing, the book allows —
a) ordinary teaching chalk as a substitute  b) a pen that never washes out  c) no marking at all
**Answer: a) ordinary teaching chalk as a substitute.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Needle size follows —
a) the colour of the fabric  b) the price of the machine  c) fabric weight — lighter fabric, smaller needle
**Answer: c) fabric weight — lighter fabric, smaller needle.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** For knits, the better thread is —
a) no thread  b) polyester  c) cotton only
**Answer: b) polyester.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** A thimble —
a) is the pitted finger cup that pushes a hand needle safely  b) holds pins  c) smooths fabric
**Answer: a) is the pitted finger cup that pushes a hand needle safely.**

**7. (Sub lesson 2)** Lubrication oil —
a) marks fabric removably  b) holds fabric in place  c) reduces friction between moving machine parts
**Answer: c) reduces friction between moving machine parts.**
