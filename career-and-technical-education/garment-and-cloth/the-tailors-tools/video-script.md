# Video script — The tailor's tools — measuring, marking, cutting, pressing

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Measure with the tape. Mark with chalk.
Cut with scissors. Hold with pins.
Press with the iron, cloth between.
The right tool for each job — that is the tailor's table.
