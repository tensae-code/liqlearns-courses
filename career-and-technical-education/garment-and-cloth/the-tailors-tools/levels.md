# The tailor's tools — measuring, marking, cutting, pressing — sub lessons and depth

This is the depth record of the lesson, re-expressed as sub lessons. A learner passes a sub lesson by its questions (the gate pool is that sub lesson's whole question set — see lesson-plan.md), then moves on; earlier sub lessons are revisited in mixed practice.

## Sub lesson 1 — Measure, mark and cut
Pass mark: the learner can answer this sub lesson's questions from the lesson — in particular: centimetres on one side, inches on the other; removable fabric marking; ordinary teaching chalk as a substitute.

## Sub lesson 2 — Hold, press and thread
Pass mark: the learner can answer this sub lesson's questions from the lesson — in particular: fabric weight — lighter fabric, smaller needle; polyester; is the pitted finger cup that pushes a hand needle safely.
