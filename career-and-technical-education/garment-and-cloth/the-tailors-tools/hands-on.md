# Hands-on — The tailor's tools — measuring, marking, cutting, pressing

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes. Machine work follows the book's own steps exactly, with a teacher or practitioner present as the books direct.

## Chalk a star
As the book sets: chalk-draw a five-pointed star on cloth. Measure it first, mark with tailor's chalk (or teaching chalk), and check the points are even.

## Thread a needle three ways
Thread a hand needle by eye, then with a needle threader, then help a partner. Name which thread you would pick for a woven cloth and which for a knit, and why.

## Pin and press drill
Pin two scrap pieces together along a chalk line, keeping pins inside the line. Press a wrinkled scrap with an iron and press cloth — no scorch marks allowed.
