# Hands-on — Colouring cloth — the kit, and caring for coloured fabric

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes. Machine work follows the book's own steps exactly, with a teacher or practitioner present as the books direct.

## Lay out the kit
Arrange brushes, sponge, toothbrush, water container, covering, apron, palette and towels around a cloth. Call each item's job before you begin colouring.

## Write a care tag
For a coloured cloth you made, write its care tag: wash inside out, no direct sunlight for drying, no hot water, hand-wash or machine as its kind requires.
