# Colouring cloth — the kit, and caring for coloured fabric

## Do it first

Before any explanation, try this in your own words: the colouring kit — brushes, sponges, water container, covering, apron, palette, towels — and the after-care rules that preserve coloured fabric.

Say what you already think, out loud or on paper. If parts will not come yet, that is exactly what this lesson is for.

## Sub lesson 1 — The colouring kit

- **Colour brushes** — flat and round, various sizes.
- **Foam brushes / sponges**.
- **Toothbrushes** — for spraying.
- **A water container** — for mixing colour or soaking cloth.
- **Plastic covering** — to protect the work surface.
- **An apron** — against colour drops.
- **A colour palette** — for mixing.
- **Paper towels** — for cleaning.

## Sub lesson 2 — After-care

After-care preserves the work:

- Some coloured fabrics **hand-wash only**; others machine-wash — check which.
- Always wash **inside out**.
- **Never dry in direct sunlight.**
- **Never soak or wash in hot water.**

## Say it to your companion

Say the main points back without looking: the colouring kit — brushes, sponges, water container, covering, apron, palette, towels — and the after-care rules that preserve coloured fabric. Then name the steps or parts in order.

## Do It Yourself

1. **Lay out the kit.** Arrange brushes, sponge, toothbrush, water container, covering, apron, palette and towels around a cloth. Call each item's job before you begin colouring.
2. **Write a care tag.** For a coloured cloth you made, write its care tag: wash inside out, no direct sunlight for drying, no hot water, hand-wash or machine as its kind requires.
