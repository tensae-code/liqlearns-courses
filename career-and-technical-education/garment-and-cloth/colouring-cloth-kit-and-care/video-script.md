# Video script — Colouring cloth — the kit, and caring for coloured fabric

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Brushes, sponges, a palette, an apron.
Cover the table before colour touches cloth.
Afterwards: wash inside out.
No hot water. No direct sun. The work lasts.
