# Colouring cloth — the kit, and caring for coloured fabric — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Toothbrushes in the colouring kit are for —
a) mixing on the palette  b) protecting the table  c) spraying
**Answer: c) spraying.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The plastic covering protects —
a) your clothes  b) the work surface  c) the finished cloth from washing
**Answer: b) the work surface.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The apron protects against —
a) colour drops  b) sunlight  c) hot water
**Answer: a) colour drops.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Coloured fabric is always washed —
a) in hot water  b) in direct sunlight  c) inside out
**Answer: c) inside out.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Coloured fabric must never be dried —
a) on a line  b) in direct sunlight  c) in shade
**Answer: b) in direct sunlight.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Coloured fabric must never be soaked or washed —
a) in hot water  b) in cold water  c) inside out
**Answer: a) in hot water.**
