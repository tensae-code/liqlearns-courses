# Hands-on — Sewing — hand needle and machine

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes. Machine work follows the book's own steps exactly, with a teacher or practitioner present as the books direct.

## Hand-stitch a seam
Thread a hand needle, knot the thread, and stitch two scrap cloth pieces together with even stitches. Keep the line straight and the spacing even.

## Compare hand and machine
If a machine is available, look at a machine-sewn seam beside your hand seam. Name what is the same (thread joining fabric) and what differs (speed, evenness).
