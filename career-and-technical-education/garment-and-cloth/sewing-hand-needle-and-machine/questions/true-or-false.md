# Sewing — hand needle and machine — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Before modern machines, Ethiopian sewing was all by hand.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Sewing stitches fabrics together with glue and paper.
**Answer: False** — thread and needle.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Before modern machines, Ethiopian sewing was all by hand.
**Answer: True.**
