# Hands-on — The sewing machine — its parts and what each does

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes. Machine work follows the book's own steps exactly, with a teacher or practitioner present as the books direct.

## Name the parts on a real machine
Stand at a sewing machine (or the labelled picture). Point to each part as its name is called: spool pin, hand-wheel, tension dial, take-up lever, presser foot, feed dog, needle. Then swap — you call, a partner points.

## Follow one stitch
Turn the hand-wheel slowly by hand and watch: needle down and up, take-up lever moving with it, feed dog pulling. Say the sequence aloud three times.
