# Lesson plan — The sewing machine — its parts and what each does

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** Garment and Cloth · **Lesson:** The sewing machine — its parts and what each does
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 5 — Garment and Cloth (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the nineteen parts of a sewing machine, each doing one job, working in sequence — knowing them part by part is what makes operation learnable.
**This lesson's own level:** It stands on Sewing — Hand Needle and Machine; now the machine opened up, part by part.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Answer: The spool pin holds the thread spool.
2. Answer: The bobbin winder stopper stops winding at capacity.
3. Answer: The thread take-up lever moves up and down with the needle, thread passing through it.
4. Answer: The stitch width dial controls zigzag width.
5. Answer: The hand-wheel raises and lowers the needle, on the right side.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil, plus the real things named in hands-on.md where they are available. Machine-safety content is taught exactly as the books teach it — a real machine is used only with the book's own steps and a teacher or practitioner present, as the books direct.
