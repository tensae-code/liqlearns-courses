# The sewing machine — its parts and what each does — sub lessons and depth

This is the depth record of the lesson, re-expressed as sub lessons. A learner passes a sub lesson by its questions (the gate pool is that sub lesson's whole question set — see lesson-plan.md), then moves on; earlier sub lessons are revisited in mixed practice.

## Sub lesson 1 — Thread and winding parts
Pass mark: the learner can answer this sub lesson's questions from the lesson — in particular: holds the thread spool; stops winding at capacity; moves up and down with the needle, thread passing through it.

## Sub lesson 2 — Controls
Pass mark: the learner can answer this sub lesson's questions from the lesson — in particular: zigzag width; raises and lowers the needle, on the right side; sews backwards while pressed.

## Sub lesson 3 — Needle, foot and feed
Pass mark: the learner can answer this sub lesson's questions from the lesson — in particular: holds fabric in place; pulls fabric forward; forms the stitch.
