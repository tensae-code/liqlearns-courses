# The sewing machine — its parts and what each does

## Do it first

Before any explanation, try this in your own words: the nineteen parts of a sewing machine, each doing one job, working in sequence — knowing them part by part is what makes operation learnable.

Say what you already think, out loud or on paper. If parts will not come yet, that is exactly what this lesson is for.

## Sub lesson 1 — Thread and winding parts

Each part does one job, and the parts work in sequence:

- **Spool pin** — holds the thread spool.
- **Bobbin winder spindle** — holds the bobbin while winding.
- **Bobbin winder stopper** — stops winding at capacity.
- **Bobbin winder thread guide** — guides thread for winding.
- **Thread tension dial** — controls top-thread tension.
- **Thread take-up lever** — moves up and down with the needle; thread passes through it.

## Sub lesson 2 — Controls

- **Stitch width dial** — controls zigzag width.
- **Pattern selector dial** — sets the stitch pattern.
- **Stitch length dial** — controls stitch length.
- **Reverse stitch lever** — sews backwards while pressed.
- **Hand-wheel** — raises and lowers the needle; on the right side.
- **Power switch** — switches the machine on and off.

## Sub lesson 3 — Needle, foot and feed

- **Needle clamp screw** — holds the needle.
- **Needle** — forms the stitch.
- **Presser foot** — holds fabric in place.
- **Bobbin cover and its release button** — covers the bobbin.
- **Feed dog** — pulls fabric forward.
- **Needle plate** — under needle and presser foot; helps move fabric forward.

Watch one stitch: needle and take-up lever move together, the feed dog pulls the fabric forward — the parts only make sense in coordinated motion.

## Say it to your companion

Say the main points back without looking: the nineteen parts of a sewing machine, each doing one job, working in sequence — knowing them part by part is what makes operation learnable. Then name the steps or parts in order.

## Do It Yourself

1. **Name the parts on a real machine.** Stand at a sewing machine (or the labelled picture). Point to each part as its name is called: spool pin, hand-wheel, tension dial, take-up lever, presser foot, feed dog, needle. Then swap — you call, a partner points.
2. **Follow one stitch.** Turn the hand-wheel slowly by hand and watch: needle down and up, take-up lever moving with it, feed dog pulling. Say the sequence aloud three times.
