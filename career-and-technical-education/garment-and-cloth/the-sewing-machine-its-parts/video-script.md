# Video script — The sewing machine — its parts and what each does

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Nineteen parts. Each does one job.
The spool pin holds the thread.
The needle forms the stitch.
The presser foot holds the fabric. The feed dog pulls it forward.
Learn the parts, and the machine can be learned.
