# The sewing machine — its parts and what each does — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The spool pin —
a) holds the needle  b) pulls fabric forward  c) holds the thread spool
**Answer: c) holds the thread spool.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The bobbin winder stopper —
a) holds fabric in place  b) stops winding at capacity  c) sets the stitch pattern
**Answer: b) stops winding at capacity.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The thread take-up lever —
a) moves up and down with the needle, thread passing through it  b) cuts the fabric  c) holds the bobbin while winding
**Answer: a) moves up and down with the needle, thread passing through it.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The stitch width dial controls —
a) top-thread tension  b) winding capacity  c) zigzag width
**Answer: c) zigzag width.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The hand-wheel —
a) holds the thread spool  b) raises and lowers the needle, on the right side  c) sews backwards while pressed
**Answer: b) raises and lowers the needle, on the right side.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The reverse stitch lever —
a) sews backwards while pressed  b) sets the stitch pattern  c) controls stitch length
**Answer: a) sews backwards while pressed.**

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** The presser foot —
a) forms the stitch  b) stops bobbin winding  c) holds fabric in place
**Answer: c) holds fabric in place.**

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** The feed dog —
a) covers the bobbin  b) pulls fabric forward  c) holds the needle
**Answer: b) pulls fabric forward.**

**9. (Sub lesson 3)** The needle —
a) forms the stitch  b) pulls fabric forward  c) controls zigzag width
**Answer: a) forms the stitch.**
