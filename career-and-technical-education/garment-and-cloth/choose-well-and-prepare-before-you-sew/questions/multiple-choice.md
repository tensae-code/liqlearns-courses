# Garment making — choose well and prepare before you sew — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The garment's quality is determined by —
a) the price of the shop  b) the speed of sewing  c) the material's quality
**Answer: c) the material's quality.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Fabric weight and stretch must fit —
a) the colour of the thread  b) the clothing planned  c) any clothing at all
**Answer: b) the clothing planned.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Quality tools give —
a) an attractive, professional result  b) a faster mistake  c) no difference
**Answer: a) an attractive, professional result.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Garment making is never done —
a) by hand  b) in advance  c) by chance
**Answer: c) by chance.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The unforgiving steps are —
a) pricing, selling and packaging  b) measuring, cutting and ironing  c) washing, drying and folding
**Answer: b) measuring, cutting and ironing.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Before starting, you should —
a) assort all materials and tools in advance  b) start cutting at once  c) buy fabric afterwards
**Answer: a) assort all materials and tools in advance.**
