# Lesson plan — Garment making — choose well and prepare before you sew

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** Garment and Cloth · **Lesson:** Garment making — choose well and prepare before you sew
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 5 — Garment and Cloth (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** three preparation rules decide a garment's final quality: the fabric, quality tools, and deliberate preparation before the unforgiving steps.
**This lesson's own level:** It stands on The Tailor's Tools; now the judgement before any sewing starts.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Answer: The garment's quality is determined by the material's quality.
2. Answer: Fabric weight and stretch must fit the clothing planned.
3. Answer: Quality tools give an attractive, professional result.
4. Answer: Garment making is never done by chance.
5. Answer: The unforgiving steps are measuring, cutting and ironing.

## What you need
