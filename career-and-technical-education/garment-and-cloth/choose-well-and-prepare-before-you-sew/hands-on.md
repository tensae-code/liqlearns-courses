# Hands-on — Garment making — choose well and prepare before you sew

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes. Machine work follows the book's own steps exactly, with a teacher or practitioner present as the books direct.

## The preparation bench
Before touching scissors: lay out fabric, pattern or plan, thread, pins, chalk, tape and iron in order. Check the fabric's weight and stretch against the garment planned. Only then begin.

## Spot the unforgiving moment
In a practice run, call out "unforgiving!" each time you reach measuring, cutting or ironing — and slow down at exactly those moments.
