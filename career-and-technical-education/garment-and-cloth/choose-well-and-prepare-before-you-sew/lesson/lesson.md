# Garment making — choose well and prepare before you sew

## Do it first

Before any explanation, try this in your own words: three preparation rules decide a garment's final quality: the fabric, quality tools, and deliberate preparation before the unforgiving steps.

Say what you already think, out loud or on paper. If parts will not come yet, that is exactly what this lesson is for.

## Sub lesson 1 — Three rules that decide quality

**Rule 1 — Fabric matters.** The material's quality determines the garment's quality, and its weight and stretch must fit the clothing planned.

**Rule 2 — Use proper, quality sewing tools and equipment** for an attractive, professional result.

**Rule 3 — Prepare deliberately.** Garment making is never done by chance: organise mentally and physically beforehand, assort all materials and tools in advance, and keep tools in their proper places.

## Sub lesson 2 — The unforgiving steps

Give full attention at the steps where damage cannot be corrected: **measuring, cutting and ironing**.

A mis-measure becomes a wrong cut. A wrong cut cannot be sewn back into whole cloth. A scorching iron ruins fabric in a second. Prepare first, then work — slowly where it cannot be undone.

## Say it to your companion

Say the main points back without looking: three preparation rules decide a garment's final quality: the fabric, quality tools, and deliberate preparation before the unforgiving steps. Then name the steps or parts in order.

## Do It Yourself

1. **The preparation bench.** Before touching scissors: lay out fabric, pattern or plan, thread, pins, chalk, tape and iron in order. Check the fabric's weight and stretch against the garment planned. Only then begin.
2. **Spot the unforgiving moment.** In a practice run, call out "unforgiving!" each time you reach measuring, cutting or ironing — and slow down at exactly those moments.
