# About the Career and Technical Education course

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in this
course comes from government textbooks — this part is built from
Ethiopia's own Career and Technical Education (CTE) books for
Grades 7 and 8, read in full, page by page. The one exception is
the **Tip boxes**: a tip in a box is from us, the people making
LiqLearns. Tips are ours; the lessons are the books'.

## Where the course comes from

CTE is the vocational course: it teaches real, sellable,
hands-on trades and the career skills around them. The books'
own arc IS the course design and is preserved: **choose a career
→ learn the skill properly and safely → be employable → plan a
business on it.** Inside the course the path is organised by
**levels, not grades** — the school grades the books come from
appear only in each lesson's sources (provenance), never as the
course's organisation. Every procedure is kept as a doing-lesson
with its steps in the book's order.

Both guides are **teacher evidence only**: their lesson
structure, methods, assessment designs and answer keys shape and
verify the build, and their additions are labelled as teacher
evidence in every lesson they touch.

**Book errors are taught corrected**, with the error noted in
the lesson sources — including the Grade 7 book's twice-printed
styling-factors list (cited once) and its two Unit 3 review
questions whose answers exist only in the teacher guide's key
(cited as such), and the Grade 8 book's "Towel" that is a
trowel, its doubled figure numbers, and its Unit 5 outcome that
promises health-and-safety teaching no Unit 5 section delivers
(the review question on it is filed as assessment, and the gap
is stated, never papered over).

**Acceptance reckoning:** this part of the course is taught in
1 country (Ethiopia) from its own student books, except any
lesson whose sources state it stands on teacher-guide evidence
only — those are counted nowhere, and say so.
