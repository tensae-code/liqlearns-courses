# Video script — Cleaning Floors — Detergents, Water, and How Often

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Floors are cared for **by type**. Vacuum. Dust. Or a lightly **dampened** mop.

The nevers: **no soap detergents** — they film the floor dull. **No over-wetting. No wax** — too slippery.

Marks? Dilute **water and vinegar**. And **no water at all on wood**.

Damp mopping — the mop slightly moistened, wrung out. Weekly, it is the deep clean.

Busy floors: sweep every one to three days, mop weekly. Walked-on dirt grinds the finish away.
