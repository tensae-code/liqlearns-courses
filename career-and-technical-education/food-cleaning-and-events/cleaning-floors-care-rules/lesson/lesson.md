# Cleaning Floors — Detergents, Water, and How Often

## Do it first

How are the floors where you live actually cleaned — what touches them, how often, with what in the water? Write the honest routine. Then read the book's care rules and count how many your routine breaks.

## Sub lesson 1 — Floor care rules — methods and nevers

Floor care by type: **vacuum, dust, or wipe with a lightly dampened mop.** The nevers are as important as the methods: **never soap-based detergents** — they leave a **dull film**; **avoid over-wetting**; **no wax polish** — it is **too slippery**.

**Marks and stains come off with a dilute water-and-vinegar solution.** And the water rule by floor type: **water is NOT to be used on wood, laminated bamboo or similar flooring.** One floor's method is another floor's damage — type decides, always.

## Sub lesson 2 — Damp mopping and the rhythm

**Damp mopping is cleaning with a mop slightly moistened and wrung out** — damp, not wet. **A thorough weekly damp mopping with proper solution gives the deep clean.** Traditional damp mopping suits many floors; **flat mops suit daily dust**.

The rhythm for **high-traffic floors — kitchens, dining areas, bathrooms: swept or vacuumed every one to three days, and mopped weekly** — because **walked-on dirt damages the finish**. Dirt left under feet is ground in; frequency is protection, not fussiness.
