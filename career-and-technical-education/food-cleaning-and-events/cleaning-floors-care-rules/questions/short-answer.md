# Cleaning Floors — Detergents, Water, and How Often — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give the four nevers of floor care with one reason each.
**Answer:** No soap-based detergents (dull film); no over-wetting; no wax polish (too slippery); no water on wood/laminated bamboo floors (type damage).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which floors count as high-traffic in the book, and what rhythm do they get?
**Answer:** Kitchens, dining areas and bathrooms — swept or vacuumed every one to three days and mopped weekly.
