# Cleaning Floors — Detergents, Water, and How Often — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Floors are cared for by —
a) vacuuming, dusting, or wiping with a lightly dampened mop  b) waxing weekly  c) soaking with soapy water
**Answer: a) vacuuming, dusting, or wiping with a lightly dampened mop.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Soap-based detergents are banned because they —
a) cost too much  b) smell strongly  c) leave a dull film
**Answer: c) leave a dull film.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Wax polish is not used because it is —
a) too expensive  b) too slippery  c) too dull
**Answer: b) too slippery.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Damp mopping means cleaning with a mop —
a) completely dry  b) soaking wet  c) slightly moistened and wrung out
**Answer: c) slightly moistened and wrung out.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The thorough weekly damp mopping with proper solution gives —
a) a light dusting  b) the daily clean  c) the deep clean
**Answer: c) the deep clean.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** High-traffic floors are swept or vacuumed —
a) once a month  b) only when visibly dirty  c) every one to three days, and mopped weekly
**Answer: c) every one to three days, and mopped weekly.**
