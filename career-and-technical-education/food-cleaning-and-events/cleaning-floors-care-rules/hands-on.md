# Hands-on — Cleaning Floors — Detergents, Water, and How Often

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes — real trade practice, done safely.

## Write two floor care plans

Take two real floors of different types (for example a tiled kitchen and a wooden room). Write each one's care plan from the rules: method, what never touches it, the stain answer, and its rhythm. The plans must differ — type decides.

## The dull-film experiment (observation)

If a soap-detergent floor is known to you, compare its look with a damp-mopped one after drying: the film the book names is visible as dullness. Write the comparison in two lines. (Observation only — do not re-clean anyone's floor unasked.)
