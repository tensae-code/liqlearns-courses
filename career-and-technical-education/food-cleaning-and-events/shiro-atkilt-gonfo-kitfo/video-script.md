# Video script — Ethiopian Foods — Shiro, Atkilt, Gonfo, Kitfo

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Four dishes complete the set.

**Shiro** — chickpea powder, slow-cooked in the red pepper sauce. Soupy wot, or thick.

**Atkiltwot** — cabbage, carrots, potatoes, simmered in a light sauce.

**Gonfo** — barley porridge, stirred without stopping, shaped round with a hole for its butter-and-pepper sauce.

**Kitfo** — minced beef in mitmita and spiced butter, with injera or kocho.

Four names. Four makings. The cultural table is set.
