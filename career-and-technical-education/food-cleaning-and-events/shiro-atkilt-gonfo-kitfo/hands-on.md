# Hands-on — Ethiopian Foods — Shiro, Atkilt, Gonfo, Kitfo

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes — real trade practice, done safely.

## Write the menu card

Write a seller's menu card for the four dishes: each name, its base ingredient, its sauce, and how it is served — all from the book's descriptions. A menu card is the dish translated for a customer who has never met it.

## The gonfo stir (dry practice)

Practise the continuous-stirring motion with a wooden spoon in an empty bowl: steady circles, no resting, keeping an even rhythm for one full minute. The motion — not the cooking — is the skill being trained here; real gonfo is made with an adult.
