# Ethiopian Foods — Shiro, Atkilt, Gonfo, Kitfo — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Shiro is made from —
a) teff flour  b) barley flour  c) chickpea powder, sometimes with lentils and beans
**Answer: c) chickpea powder, sometimes with lentils and beans.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Shiro is slow-cooked with —
a) plain water  b) the spicy red pepper sauce  c) a light cabbage sauce
**Answer: b) the spicy red pepper sauce.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Atkiltwot is —
a) minced beef in butter  b) cabbage, carrots and potatoes simmered together in a light sauce  c) a stiff porridge
**Answer: b) cabbage, carrots and potatoes simmered together in a light sauce.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Gonfo is stirred —
a) after it cools  b) continuously, with a wooden spoon  c) only at the start
**Answer: b) continuously, with a wooden spoon.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Gonfo's hole in the middle holds —
a) its dipping sauce of butter and red pepper, or pulses  b) a boiled egg  c) extra porridge
**Answer: a) its dipping sauce of butter and red pepper, or pulses.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Kitfo is marinated in —
a) red pepper sauce and oil  b) turmeric and water  c) mitmita and herb-and-spice-infused butter
**Answer: c) mitmita and herb-and-spice-infused butter.**
