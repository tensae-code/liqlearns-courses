# Ethiopian Foods — Shiro, Atkilt, Gonfo, Kitfo — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Contrast shiro and atkiltwot in one sentence each.
**Answer:** Shiro: a chickpea-powder dish slow-cooked with spicy red pepper sauce. Atkiltwot: cabbage, carrots and potatoes simmered together in a light sauce.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Describe kitfo exactly as the book does.
**Answer:** Minced raw beef marinated in mitmita (a chili-powder spice blend) and herb-and-spice-infused butter, served with injera and/or kocho.
