# Hands-on — Hand Washing — the Ten Steps

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes — real trade practice, done safely.

## The class drill (the book's own practice)

At a real sink, run the ten steps slowly in order, saying each step aloud as you do it — three full runs. Runs two and three: a partner reads the steps and watches for the skipped ones (thumbs and cupped fingers are the usual misses).

## Teach it

Teach the ten steps to one person — a sibling, a classmate — using only your hands and voice, no notes. Their clean run at the sink is your result: hand hygiene spreads person to person.
