# Video script — Hand Washing — the Ten Steps

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Hand hygiene** — soap and water against viruses, bacteria, dirt and grease. Ten steps.

Wet. Soap — enough for a good lather.

Palms. Backs, hand over hand. Fingers interlinked. Fingers cupped. **Thumbs** — do not skip them. Palms rubbed with fingers.

Rinse until clean. **Dry** — before you touch anything. Wet hands re-contaminate.

Ten steps. Every surface. Every time.
