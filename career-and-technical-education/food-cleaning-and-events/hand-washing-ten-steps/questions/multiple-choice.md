# Hand Washing — the Ten Steps — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Hand washing removes —
a) only visible dirt  b) viruses, bacteria and microorganisms, dirt, grease and other harmful substances  c) only grease
**Answer: b) viruses, bacteria and microorganisms, dirt, grease and other harmful substances.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Drying is part of the process because —
a) towels are cheap  b) wet hands re-contaminate easily  c) dry hands look better
**Answer: b) wet hands re-contaminate easily.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Hand washing uses —
a) sanitiser only  b) water alone, always  c) soap and water
**Answer: c) soap and water.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Step 2 is —
a) rinse with water  b) dry the hands  c) apply enough liquid soap for a good lather
**Answer: c) apply enough liquid soap for a good lather.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Step 7 is —
a) interlink the fingers  b) clean the thumbs  c) cup the fingers
**Answer: b) clean the thumbs.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The rub steps are —
a) steps 1–2 only  b) steps 3–8: palms, backs, interlinked fingers, cupped fingers, thumbs, palms with fingers  c) the whole ten
**Answer: b) steps 3–8: palms, backs, interlinked fingers, cupped fingers, thumbs, palms with fingers.**
