# Hand Washing — the Ten Steps — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A wash that ends with wet hands touching surfaces has kept its full value.
**Answer: False** — wet hands re-contaminate easily — drying before touching any object is step 10.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Step 5 interlinks the fingers and step 6 cups them.
**Answer: True** — the sequence runs palms (3), backs (4), interlink (5), cup (6), thumbs (7), palms-with-fingers (8).
