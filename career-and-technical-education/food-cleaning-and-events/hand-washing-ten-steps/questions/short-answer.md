# Hand Washing — the Ten Steps — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does the book build drying into the definition itself?
**Answer:** Because the process is not complete while hands stay wet: wet hands re-contaminate easily, so drying protects everything the wash achieved.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Recite all ten steps in order.
**Answer:** Wet hands; apply enough liquid soap for a good lather; rub palms together; rub the backs, interchanging hands; interlink the fingers; cup the fingers; clean the thumbs; rub palms with the fingers; rinse until clean; dry before touching any object.
