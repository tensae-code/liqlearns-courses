# Lesson plan — Hand Washing — the Ten Steps

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** Food, Cleaning and Events · **Lesson:** Hand Washing — the Ten Steps
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 8 — Food, Cleaning and Events (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** hand washing as the book teaches it: soap and water against viruses, bacteria, dirt and grease, in ten named steps — with drying counted as part of the process.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Cleaning Safely and the whole food half of the title: hands touch food, surfaces and guests; this is the hygiene gate for all of it.

## Sub lessons
1. **What hand washing is — and why drying counts** — soap and water against microbes and dirt; wet hands re-contaminate
2. **The ten steps** — wet, soap, the six rubs, rinse, dry

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Cleaning Safely — PPE and How a Cleaner Presents. Recommended, never forced.
- This lesson is practised at a real sink, as the book's class drill does.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what hand washing removes, and why drying belongs to the process.
2. Give the ten steps in order.
3. Practise the rub sequence: palms, backs, interlinked fingers, cupped fingers, thumbs, palms-with-fingers.

## What you need
A sink, water, liquid soap and a clean way to dry hands — this lesson is done standing at the tap.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the learner washes their hands their usual way and times it before the ten steps are shown (lesson/lesson.md).
2. **The explanation** — what hand washing is and removes, then the ten steps in order, rub by rub (lesson/lesson.md).

## If the learner is stuck
- Rinses after one quick palm-rub → steps 3–8 are six distinct rubs covering six surfaces. Slow the drill down; each step gets its name said aloud.
- Touches the tap or towel rail with wet hands → step 10 exists precisely here: dry BEFORE touching any object, because wet hands re-contaminate easily.
