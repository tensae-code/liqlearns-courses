# Video script — A Clean Work Area — the Four Business Benefits

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A clean workplace is **more than a sparkling building**.

Keep the work area neat and orderly, and four returns follow.

**One** — staff stay safe and healthy. **Two** — productivity rises.

**Three** — the facility's professional image improves. **Four** — it pays for itself in the long run.

Not a chore. An investment with four returns.
