# A Clean Work Area — the Four Business Benefits — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A clean workplace is —
a) a building cleaned once, deeply  b) only shiny floors  c) more than a sparkling building — it is keeping the work area neat and orderly
**Answer: c) more than a sparkling building — it is keeping the work area neat and orderly.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The book's claim is about —
a) the cleaner's private habit  b) housekeeping taste  c) a business practice with returns
**Answer: c) a business practice with returns.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Not one of the four benefits is —
a) productivity rising  b) professional image improving  c) 'it looks nice' as a bare claim
**Answer: c) 'it looks nice' as a bare claim.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Benefit (1) is —
a) money is saved  b) the image improves  c) staff members stay safe and healthy
**Answer: c) staff members stay safe and healthy.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Benefit (3) is —
a) the professional image of the facility improves  b) productivity is boosted  c) staff stay healthy
**Answer: a) the professional image of the facility improves.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Benefit (4) is that cleanliness proves —
a) free  b) cost-effective for the business in the long run  c) unnecessary
**Answer: b) cost-effective for the business in the long run.**
