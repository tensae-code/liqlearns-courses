# A Clean Work Area — the Four Business Benefits — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Keeping the work area neat and orderly is the practice the benefits hang on.
**Answer: True** — the verb is keeping — a habit, not an event.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The four benefits run: safety/health, productivity, image, long-run cost.
**Answer: True** — that is the book's order on printed p. 102.
