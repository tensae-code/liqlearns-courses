# A Clean Work Area — the Four Business Benefits — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does the book open with 'more than a sparkling building'?
**Answer:** Because the shine is only the surface; the substance is the kept, orderly work area and the business returns it produces.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** List the four benefits in order.
**Answer:** Staff stay safe and healthy; employee productivity is boosted; the facility's professional image improves; it proves cost-effective in the long run.
