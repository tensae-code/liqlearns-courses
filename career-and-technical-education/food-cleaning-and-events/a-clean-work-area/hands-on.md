# Hands-on — A Clean Work Area — the Four Business Benefits

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes — real trade practice, done safely.

## Before-and-after plan

Choose one real work area (a kitchen bench, a classroom corner, a shop counter). Write its 'before' honestly, then its improvement plan: what is removed, what is ordered, in what sequence — and which of the four benefits each change buys. If you may, carry out the plan and photograph or sketch the after.

## Persuade the owner

Write a one-minute speech to a sceptical owner who calls cleaning 'lost time'. Use all four benefits, each with one concrete example from their own business type. Lost time is the claim; the four returns are the answer.
