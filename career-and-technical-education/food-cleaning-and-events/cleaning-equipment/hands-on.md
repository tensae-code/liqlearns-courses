# Hands-on — Cleaning Equipment — the Cleaner's Kit

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes — real trade practice, done safely.

## Kit census

With permission, census one real cleaning cupboard or kit against the book's list: present, missing, or substituted (what stands in for a missing item?). Write the gap list a buyer would need.

## Pack for a job

Three jobs are called: a carpeted office, a tiled bathroom block, a hotel bedroom. For each, write the kit subset you would carry from the full list — the vacuum's attachment choice is part of the answer for the first.
