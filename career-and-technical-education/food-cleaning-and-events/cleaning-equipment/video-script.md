# Video script — Cleaning Equipment — the Cleaner's Kit

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Pack the cleaner's kit.

**Vacuum**, with attachments for hardwood and carpet. **Mop and bucket**. A **bucket for supplies**.

**Dusters** — long and short. **Dustpan and broom**. **Paper towels**.

And the picture adds: toilet paper and dispensers, trash cans and liners.

Tools that clean, supplies that are consumed, bins that contain. Pack all three, or the shift stops.
