# Cleaning Equipment — the Cleaner's Kit — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The common kit includes a vacuum cleaner —
a) for carpets only  b) with no attachments  c) with attachments for hardwood and carpet
**Answer: c) with attachments for hardwood and carpet.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The bucket or container in the kit is for —
a) storing the vacuum  b) carrying supplies  c) mopping only
**Answer: b) carrying supplies.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Dusters come in the kit as —
a) wet and dry  b) long and short  c) one size only
**Answer: b) long and short.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The sample kit picture adds —
a) a second vacuum  b) a floor polisher  c) toilet paper and dispensers, trash cans and trash-can liners
**Answer: c) toilet paper and dispensers, trash cans and trash-can liners.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Trash-can liners belong to the kit's —
a) chemistry half  b) containment half — containing the dirt removed  c) mechanical half
**Answer: b) containment half — containing the dirt removed.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** A kit stocked with tools only can fail because —
a) tools are too heavy  b) customers dislike tools  c) consumables run out mid-shift
**Answer: c) consumables run out mid-shift.**
