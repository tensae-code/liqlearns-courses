# Cleaning Equipment — the Cleaner's Kit — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Mop and bucket, dustpan and broom, and paper towels are all in the common kit.
**Answer: True** — each is named in the book's list.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Toilet paper and dispensers are part of the book's sample kit picture.
**Answer: True** — they are named among its additions.
