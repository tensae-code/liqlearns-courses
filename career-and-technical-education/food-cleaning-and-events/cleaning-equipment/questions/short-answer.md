# Cleaning Equipment — the Cleaner's Kit — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** List the common kit in full.
**Answer:** Vacuum cleaner with attachments for hardwood and carpet; bucket or container to carry supplies; mop and bucket; dusters, long and short; dustpan and broom; paper towels.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** State the kit's halves as the lesson frames them.
**Answer:** The tools that clean (vacuum, mop, dusters, dustpan and broom), the supplies that are consumed (paper towels, toilet paper), and containment (trash cans and liners) — with the supply bucket carrying the chemistry to the work.
