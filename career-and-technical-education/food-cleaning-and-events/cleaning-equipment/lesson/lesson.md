# Cleaning Equipment — the Cleaner's Kit

## Do it first

You are kitting out a new cleaner for their first shift at a small hotel. Write the packing list — everything their hands and back will need. Then compare with the book's common kit and its sample picture, item by item.

## Sub lesson 1 — The common kit

The common kit: **vacuum cleaner with attachments for hardwood and carpet; a bucket or container to carry supplies; mop and bucket; dusters, long and short; dustpan and broom; and paper towels**.

Read the kit as the blend from the cleaning-science lesson made portable: the vacuum and mop supply the mechanical action, the supply bucket carries the chemistry to the work, and the dusters, dustpan and towels finish what the big tools start.

## Sub lesson 2 — The sample kit picture's additions

The book's **sample kit picture** adds the consumables-and-containment side: **toilet paper and dispensers, trash cans and trash-can liners**.

A working kit is therefore two halves: the tools that clean, and the supplies that get consumed and the bins that contain the dirt removed. Stock both halves, or the shift stops mid-job.

## Say it to your companion

List the common kit for your companion, then the sample picture's additions, and finish with the two-halves principle — three halves, counting containment.
