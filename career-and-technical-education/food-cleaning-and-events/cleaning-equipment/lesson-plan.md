# Lesson plan — Cleaning Equipment — the Cleaner's Kit

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** Food, Cleaning and Events · **Lesson:** Cleaning Equipment — the Cleaner's Kit
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 8 — Food, Cleaning and Events (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the common cleaning kit: vacuum cleaner with attachments, supply bucket, mop and bucket, dusters long and short, dustpan and broom, paper towels — and the book's sample kit picture's extras.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Cleaning Services: the learner knows the business's scope; now the kit that carries it out.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an

## Sub lessons
1. **The common kit** — vacuum with attachments, buckets, mop, dusters, dustpan and broom, paper towels
2. **The sample kit picture's additions** — toilet paper and dispensers, trash cans and liners

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Cleaning Services — What a Commercial Cleaning Company Does. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. List the common kit items.
2. Say what the vacuum's attachments are for.
3. Recall the sample kit picture's additions: toilet paper and dispensers, trash cans and liners.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil; the census works on any real cleaning cupboard, looking only with permission.

## If the learner is stuck
- Forgets the attachments → they are in the book's entry: attachments FOR hardwood and carpet — one machine, surfaces swapped by attachment.
- Lists tools only → the sample picture's half (consumables and containment) is what runs out mid-shift. Both halves are the kit.
