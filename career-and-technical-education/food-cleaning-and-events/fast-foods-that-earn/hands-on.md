# Hands-on — Fast Foods That Earn — Chips, Sambusa, Biscuits

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes — real trade practice, done safely.

## Prepare one fast food and report (adult present)

Following the book's own activity: with an adult, prepare ONE of the three foods — chips, sambusa or biscuits — from local ingredients, hands washed first. Write the report the book asks for: ingredients, steps, what you would charge, and what you would do differently next batch.

## Fold the triangle (paper practice)

Practise the sambusa fold with a paper square and a spoonful of dry beans as 'filling': fold to a triangle, seal the edges, keep the filling in. Three shapes to try: triangle, cone, half-moon. Paper first — the fold learned cheap.
