# Fast Foods That Earn — Chips, Sambusa, Biscuits — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why are potatoes a smart base for a seller's food, on the book's account?
**Answer:** Potatoes are locally available and rich in vitamins, especially vitamin C — a staple crop that fries into a fast food people buy.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the three fast foods and one fact about each.
**Answer:** Chips — potato-based, rich in vitamin C; sambusa — spiced potato/onion/pea filling in a folded pastry, fried or baked; biscuits — flour, sugar, fat, low moisture.
