# Fast Foods That Earn — Chips, Sambusa, Biscuits — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Potato fries (chips) have as main ingredient —
a) wheat flour  b) chickpeas  c) potatoes — rich in vitamins, especially vitamin C
**Answer: c) potatoes — rich in vitamins, especially vitamin C.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A sambusa is —
a) a boiled dumpling  b) a fried or baked pastry with a spicy filling  c) a raw salad wrap
**Answer: b) a fried or baked pastry with a spicy filling.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The sambusa filling in the book is —
a) cheese and herbs  b) spiced potatoes, onions and peas  c) minced beef only
**Answer: b) spiced potatoes, onions and peas.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Biscuits are made of —
a) teff and butter  b) potatoes and peas  c) wheat flour, sugar and fat
**Answer: c) wheat flour, sugar and fat.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Biscuits differ from bread and cakes by —
a) their colour  b) their low moisture content  c) being eaten raw
**Answer: b) their low moisture content.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** All three fast foods share this business virtue:
a) they need imported ingredients  b) they can be prepared from locally available ingredients  c) they need factory machinery
**Answer: b) they can be prepared from locally available ingredients.**
