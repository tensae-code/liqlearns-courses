# Fast Foods That Earn — Chips, Sambusa, Biscuits — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Sambusa shapes include triangle, cone and half-moon.
**Answer: True** — all three shapes are named in the book.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Low moisture helps biscuits keep as stock.
**Answer: True** — dry keeping is built into the food — the contrast with fresh goods is the point.
