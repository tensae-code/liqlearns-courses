# Fast Foods That Earn — Chips, Sambusa, Biscuits

## Do it first

Three foods sell at every school gate and bus stop. If you had to earn from ONE food next week — made by your own hands, from market ingredients — which would you pick, and what would you need? Write your pick and its ingredients. The book's three earners follow.

## Sub lesson 1 — Chips and sambusa

**Potato fries (chips):** potatoes are the main ingredient — and potatoes are **rich in vitamins, especially vitamin C**. A staple crop becomes a seller's tray of chips.

**Sambusa (sanbusa):** a **fried or baked pastry with a spicy filling of spiced potatoes, onions and peas**, folded in **triangular, cone or half-moon shapes**. The fold is the craft: the shape seals the filling in.

## Sub lesson 2 — Biscuits — and the earning point

**Biscuits** are made of **wheat flour, sugar and fat**, and they differ from bread and cakes by their **low moisture content** — dry keeping is built into the food itself.

The earning point covers all three: **all can be prepared from locally available ingredients**. No imports, no rare equipment — the business starts from the market you already have.

## Say it to your companion
