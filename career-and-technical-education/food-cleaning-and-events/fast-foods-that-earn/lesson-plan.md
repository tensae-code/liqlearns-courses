# Lesson plan — Fast Foods That Earn — Chips, Sambusa, Biscuits

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** Food, Cleaning and Events · **Lesson:** Fast Foods That Earn — Chips, Sambusa, Biscuits
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 8 — Food, Cleaning and Events (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** three fast foods taught as income activities: potato fries (chips), sambusa, and biscuits — each makeable from locally available ingredients.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the cultural-foods lessons: the same kitchen skills, aimed at earning.

## Sub lessons
1. **Chips and sambusa** — potato fries, and the filled pastry
2. **Biscuits — and the earning point** — wheat flour, sugar, fat — and low moisture

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Ethiopian Foods — Injera and Wat, and Shiro, Atkilt, Gonfo, Kitfo. Recommended, never forced.
- All frying/baking practice is with an adult.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Give each food's main ingredient(s) and form.
2. Say what makes biscuits different from bread and cakes.
3. State the business point: all three from locally available ingredients.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil; preparation practice only with an adult, after hand washing.

## If the learner is stuck
- Calls any fried potato dish 'chips' vaguely → the book's entry is exact: potato fries, main ingredient potatoes, rich in vitamins especially C.
- Cannot say how biscuits differ → not sweetness, not size: LOW MOISTURE content, against bread and cakes.
