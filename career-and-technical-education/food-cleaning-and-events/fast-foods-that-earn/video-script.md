# Video script — Fast Foods That Earn — Chips, Sambusa, Biscuits

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Three foods earn. All from your own market.

**Chips** — potatoes, rich in vitamin C, fried into a seller's tray.

**Sambusa** — spiced potato, onion and pea, folded into a pastry triangle and fried or baked.

**Biscuits** — flour, sugar, fat — and **low moisture**, so they keep.

Local ingredients. Your hands. A tray that sells.
