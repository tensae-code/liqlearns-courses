# Hands-on — Fresh Foods — Raw Foods and Healthy Snacks

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes — real trade practice, done safely.

## Make the snack mix (adult present, hands washed)

Wash hands first (the ten steps, Hand Washing lesson). With an adult, mix nuts and dried fruit into a snack mix; portion it into small clean bags or cups as a seller would. Note: it is non-perishable — write on your plan how long stock like this can be held compared with a raw fruit plate.

## Build a raw plate

Arrange a raw plate from the book's list — fruit and raw vegetables together — washed and presented as a seller would present it. Photograph or sketch it and label every item with its list name.
