# Fresh Foods — Raw Foods and Healthy Snacks — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Canned fruit appears in the book's fresh-foods section.
**Answer: True** — the section lists canned fruit and lower-sodium canned vegetables among its fresh foods.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Non-perishable stock can be held without spoiling — a seller's advantage.
**Answer: True** — that keeping quality is why the mix suits a small business shelf.
