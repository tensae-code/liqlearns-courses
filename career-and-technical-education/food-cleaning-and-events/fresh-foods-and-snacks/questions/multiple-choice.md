# Fresh Foods — Raw Foods and Healthy Snacks — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Fresh foods eaten raw include —
a) only garden vegetables  b) apples, oranges and bananas; raisins; canned fruit  c) only cooked fruits
**Answer: b) apples, oranges and bananas; raisins; canned fruit.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Vegetables eaten raw in the book's list are —
a) potatoes and onions  b) cabbage and kale, cooked  c) carrots, peppers, avocado, small cucumbers, tomatoes
**Answer: c) carrots, peppers, avocado, small cucumbers, tomatoes.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Bagged washed lettuce or salads are —
a) a cooked snack  b) not food service  c) part of the raw fresh foods
**Answer: c) part of the raw fresh foods.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The snack mix of nuts and dried fruit is —
a) a cooked meal  b) healthy and non-perishable  c) healthy but spoils in a day
**Answer: b) healthy and non-perishable.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Roasted chickpeas are —
a) a drink ingredient  b) a raw food  c) a cooked snack made from a staple pulse
**Answer: c) a cooked snack made from a staple pulse.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Apples are paired, in the snack list, with —
a) honey only  b) peanut butter  c) cheese
**Answer: b) peanut butter.**
