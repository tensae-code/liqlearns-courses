# Fresh Foods — Raw Foods and Healthy Snacks — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name five raw vegetables or salad items from the book.
**Answer:** Any five of: carrots, peppers, avocado, small cucumbers, tomatoes, lower-sodium canned vegetables, bagged washed lettuce or salads.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** List the three cooked-snack makings.
**Answer:** Nuts and dried fruit as a snack mix; roasted chickpeas; apples with peanut butter.
