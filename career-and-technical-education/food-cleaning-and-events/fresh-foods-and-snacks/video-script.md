# Video script — Fresh Foods — Raw Foods and Healthy Snacks

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Fresh food, two halves.

**Eaten raw**: apples, oranges, bananas. Raisins. Canned fruit. Carrots, peppers, avocado, cucumbers, tomatoes. Washed salads, ready in the bag.

**Made into snacks**: nuts and dried fruit — a mix that does not spoil. Roasted chickpeas. Apples with peanut butter.

Freshness is the preparation. Keeping quality is the business.
