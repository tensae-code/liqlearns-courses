# Cleaning — Removing Dirt, and Chemistry Plus Mechanics — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Cleaning is the process of —
a) removing unwanted substances from an object or environment  b) moving dirt to a hidden place  c) covering dirt with scent
**Answer: a) removing unwanted substances from an object or environment.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The unwanted substances include —
a) dirt, infectious agents and other impurities  b) only visible dust  c) only food waste
**Answer: a) dirt, infectious agents and other impurities.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The four contexts of cleaning are —
a) commercial, domestic, personal and environmental  b) daily, weekly, monthly and yearly  c) hotel, school, church and market
**Answer: a) commercial, domestic, personal and environmental.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Cleaning is taught as a blend of —
a) speed and strength  b) chemistry and mechanics  c) water and time
**Answer: b) chemistry and mechanics.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The chemical families named include —
a) paints and varnishes  b) soaps only  c) detergents, solvents, acids, alkalis, polishes, waxes
**Answer: c) detergents, solvents, acids, alkalis, polishes, waxes.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The third leg of the blend, beside product and action, is —
a) the cleaner's mood  b) the conditions at the time  c) the price of the product
**Answer: b) the conditions at the time.**
