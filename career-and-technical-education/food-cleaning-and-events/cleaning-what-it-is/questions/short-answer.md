# Cleaning — Removing Dirt, and Chemistry Plus Mechanics — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give the book's definition of cleaning in full.
**Answer:** Cleaning is the process of removing unwanted substances — dirt, infectious agents and other impurities — from an object or environment.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A cleaner scrubs a greasy pot with plain cold water and fails. Analyse it by the blend.
**Answer:** The mechanics were present (scrubbing) but the chemistry was missing — no detergent to loosen grease — and the conditions (cold water on grease) worked against both. Add the chemical leg and the same action succeeds.
