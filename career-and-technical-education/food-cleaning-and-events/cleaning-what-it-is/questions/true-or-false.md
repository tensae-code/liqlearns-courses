# Cleaning — Removing Dirt, and Chemistry Plus Mechanics — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The four contexts differ in scale and requirements.
**Answer: True** — a hotel corridor and a kitchen sink are the same process at different scales — that difference makes cleaning a trade.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The right chemical alone, with no mechanical action, is the whole of cleaning.
**Answer: False** — the chemical interacts WITH the mechanical action of the equipment on the surface, under the conditions — all three together.
