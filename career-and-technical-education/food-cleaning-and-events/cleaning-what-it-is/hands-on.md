# Hands-on — Cleaning — Removing Dirt, and Chemistry Plus Mechanics

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes — real trade practice, done safely.

## Split three jobs by the blend

Take three cleaning jobs you know — a greasy pot, a dusty room, a stained shirt. For each, write: the chemical (what loosened), the mechanics (what lifted), and the conditions (time, heat, surface). Any job where one leg is missing is a job done the hard way — say which leg.

## Context survey

List one cleaning task in each context — commercial, domestic, personal, environmental — from your own week. Beside each, write its scale and its special requirement. The differences you find are the book's point.
