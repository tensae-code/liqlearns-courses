# Video script — Cleaning — Removing Dirt, and Chemistry Plus Mechanics

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Cleaning** — removing unwanted substances: dirt, infectious agents, impurities. From an object, or a whole environment.

Four contexts: **commercial** — offices, hotels. **Domestic** — kitchens, houses. **Personal**. **Environmental**. Same process, different scales.

And it works as a blend: **chemistry** — detergents, solvents, acids, alkalis — meeting **mechanics** — the brush, the mop, the machine.

Product, action, conditions. All three decide. That is the science under the trade.
