# Lesson plan — Cleaning — Removing Dirt, and Chemistry Plus Mechanics

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** Food, Cleaning and Events · **Lesson:** Cleaning — Removing Dirt, and Chemistry Plus Mechanics
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 8 — Food, Cleaning and Events (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** what cleaning is — removing unwanted substances from an object or environment, across commercial, domestic, personal and environmental work — and how it works: cleaning chemistry meeting mechanical action.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Hospitality — Welcoming Guests as an Industry, where cleaning was defined beside hospitality: now the cleaning half of the title in full.

## Sub lessons
1. **What cleaning is — and where it happens** — removing unwanted substances, in four contexts
2. **How cleaning works — chemistry plus mechanics** — product, action and conditions together

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Define cleaning in the book's words.
2. Name the four contexts and how they differ.
3. Explain the blend: chemical product + mechanical action + conditions.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil.

## If the learner is stuck
- Defines cleaning as 'making things look nice' → the book's definition names infectious agents: cleaning is health work — dirt AND disease agents removed.
- Thinks the chemical does it all → blend means blend: chemical interacts WITH mechanical action, under conditions. One leg alone is not the process.
