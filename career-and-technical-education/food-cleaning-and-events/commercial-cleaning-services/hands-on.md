# Hands-on — Cleaning Services — What a Commercial Cleaning Company Does

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes — real trade practice, done safely.

## Draft a service scope

Draft the cleaning scope for one real building you know (a school, a guesthouse, an office): routine list, deep-clean list, periodic items — modelled on the book's scope. Present it as a one-page offer a company could sign.

## Depths audit

Walk one building in your mind and mark five cleaning jobs R (routine), D (deep) or P (periodic) using the book's scope. Any job you cannot class is one to look up in the lesson again.
