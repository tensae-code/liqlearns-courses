# Cleaning Services — What a Commercial Cleaning Company Does — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Commercial cleaning companies are —
a) contracted to clean a variety of premises  b) owned by the premises they clean  c) only for private houses
**Answer: a) contracted to clean a variety of premises.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The book's pictured service kinds are —
a) window washing only  b) cooking and serving  c) bed dressing, floor cleaning and toilet cleaning
**Answer: c) bed dressing, floor cleaning and toilet cleaning.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** They clean using —
a) one method for all premises  b) varied methods, chemicals and equipment  c) water alone
**Answer: b) varied methods, chemicals and equipment.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The routine scope includes —
a) floors, tiles, partition and internal walls, suspended ceilings, lighting, furniture and windows  b) only floors and windows  c) only kitchens
**Answer: a) floors, tiles, partition and internal walls, suspended ceilings, lighting, furniture and windows.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Deep cleans cover —
a) only bedrooms  b) sanitary conveniences and washing facilities, kitchens and dining areas  c) only offices
**Answer: b) sanitary conveniences and washing facilities, kitchens and dining areas.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The periodic range runs —
a) from daily dusting to weekly mopping  b) from real-estate property cleans to building-project aftermath  c) from windows to telephones only
**Answer: b) from real-estate property cleans to building-project aftermath.**
