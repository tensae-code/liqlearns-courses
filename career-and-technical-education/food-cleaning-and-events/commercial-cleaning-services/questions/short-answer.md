# Cleaning Services — What a Commercial Cleaning Company Does — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three pictured kinds and the means the company uses.
**Answer:** Bed dressing, floor cleaning and toilet cleaning — using varied methods, chemicals and equipment.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give the scope in its three depths.
**Answer:** Routine: all internal general cleaning — floors, tiles, walls, ceilings, lighting, furniture, windows. Deep: sanitary conveniences, washing facilities, kitchens, dining areas. Periodic/other: consumables, feminine hygiene facilities, telephone cleaning, property cleans to project aftermath.
