# Video script — Cleaning Services — What a Commercial Cleaning Company Does

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A **commercial cleaning company** is contracted to clean other people's premises.

The pictures show its kinds: beds dressed, floors cleaned, toilets cleaned — by varied methods, chemicals and equipment.

The scope is wide: floors, tiles, walls, ceilings, lighting, furniture, windows — the routine clean.

Deep cleans for kitchens, dining areas and sanitary rooms. Consumables. Telephones. Periodic work.

From a property prepared for sale to the aftermath of a building project — cleaning is a business with a scope.
