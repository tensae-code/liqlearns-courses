# Lesson plan — Cleaning Services — What a Commercial Cleaning Company Does

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** Food, Cleaning and Events · **Lesson:** Cleaning Services — What a Commercial Cleaning Company Does
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 8 — Food, Cleaning and Events (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** commercial cleaning as a business: the premises and scope a contracted company covers — routine internal cleaning, deep cleans of sanitary and kitchen areas, consumables, and periodic work from property cleans to project aftermath.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on A Clean Work Area: the learner knows why clean pays; now the business that sells cleaning itself.

## Sub lessons
1. **The contracted company and its pictured work** — bed dressing, floor cleaning, toilet cleaning — by varied methods
2. **The scope — routine, deep, periodic** — internal routine cleaning, deep cleans, consumables and periodic work

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: A Clean Work Area — the Four Business Benefits. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what a commercial cleaning company is contracted to do.
2. List the routine scope and the deep-clean scope.
3. Name the pictured service kinds: bed dressing, floor cleaning, toilet cleaning.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks the scope is 'floors and toilets' → the book's routine list alone has seven areas (floors, tiles, walls, ceilings, lighting, furniture, windows) before deep cleans and periodic work begin.
- Confuses deep cleans with routine → deep cleans are the sanitary, kitchen and dining depths, done periodically and thoroughly; routine is the general internal round.
