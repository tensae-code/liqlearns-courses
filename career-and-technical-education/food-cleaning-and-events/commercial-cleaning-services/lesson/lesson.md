# Cleaning Services — What a Commercial Cleaning Company Does

## Do it first

An office block hires an outside company to clean it. Write the full job list you think the company signs for — every room, every surface, every kind of clean. The book's scope is wider than most guesses; compare as you read.

## Sub lesson 1 — The contracted company and its pictured work

**Commercial cleaning companies are contracted to clean a variety of premises** — the book's pictured kinds are **bed dressing, floor cleaning and toilet cleaning** — **using varied methods, chemicals and equipment**.

Contracted is the business word: the cleaning is sold as a service to premises the company does not own. The next sub lesson is what the contract actually covers.

## Sub lesson 2 — The scope — routine, deep, periodic

The scope covers **all internal general and routine cleaning**: **floors, tiles, partition and internal walls, suspended ceilings, lighting, furniture and windows**.

Then the **deep cleans of sanitary conveniences and washing facilities, kitchens and dining areas**, plus **consumables and feminine hygiene facilities, telephone cleaning and other periodic cleaning** — ranging **from real-estate property cleans to building-project aftermath**. A cleaning company sells depths, not just hours: the daily routine, the periodic deep clean, and the one-off rescue.
