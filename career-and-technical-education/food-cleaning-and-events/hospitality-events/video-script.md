# Video script — Hospitality Events — Weddings, Meetings, Promotions, Conferences, Fundraisers

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Five gatherings, five purposes.

**Weddings, business meetings, celebration dinners** — run by planners, hotel owners, banquet and restaurant managers, for client, guests and host.

**Promotions** — a sport or activity shown to its market; participation rises; the sponsor shines second.

**Conferences** — plan, review, discuss, inform, choose, teach.

**Fundraisers** — dinners, bingo nights, auctions, awards: money raised by gathering well.

And before any of them: a **risk audit**. Name what could go wrong — then plan for it.
