# Hands-on — Hospitality Events — Weddings, Meetings, Promotions, Conferences, Fundraisers

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes — real trade practice, done safely.

## Plan a real local event

In the guide's pattern: choose an event actually run in your area. Plan it on paper — the tasks, a deadline for each, and the possible health-safety points. Present your plan to a partner group and take their questions.

## Risk-audit one event

Take the same event and write its risk audit: list everything undesirable that could occur — harm to people, damage to the organisation — and one planned response for each. Five risks minimum.
