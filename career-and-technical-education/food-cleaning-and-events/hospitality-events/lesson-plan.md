# Lesson plan — Hospitality Events — Weddings, Meetings, Promotions, Conferences, Fundraisers

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** Food, Cleaning and Events · **Lesson:** Hospitality Events — Weddings, Meetings, Promotions, Conferences, Fundraisers
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 8 — Food, Cleaning and Events (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the types of hospitality events — weddings, business meetings, celebration dinners and fundraisings, promotional events and conferences — who runs them, and what each kind is for.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Hospitality — Welcoming Guests as an Industry: the learner knows the industry; events are its gathering work.

## If the learner is stuck
- Lists events without purposes → each type has its own 'for': promotions raise participation, conferences plan/review/teach, fundraisers raise money. Purpose is the classification.
- Treats the risk audit as paperwork → the guide's definition is practical: a plan exists BEFORE the undesirable thing happens. No plan, no audit.
