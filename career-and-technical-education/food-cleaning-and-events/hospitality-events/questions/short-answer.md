# Hospitality Events — Weddings, Meetings, Promotions, Conferences, Fundraisers — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What do conferences gather participants to do? Name four purposes.
**Answer:** Any four of: plan; review progress; discuss issues; circulate information; select committee personnel; impart new knowledge.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give two risks for a celebration dinner and the planned response to each.
**Answer:** Any two sensible pairs, e.g. food spoiling in heat → serve promptly and keep dishes covered/cool; overcrowding at the door → a managed entry with space kept clear. The pattern is the point: risk named, plan ready before it happens.
