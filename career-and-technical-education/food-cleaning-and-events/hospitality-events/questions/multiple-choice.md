# Hospitality Events — Weddings, Meetings, Promotions, Conferences, Fundraisers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Hospitality events include —
a) weddings, business meetings, celebration dinners and fundraisings  b) only hotel openings  c) only weddings
**Answer: a) weddings, business meetings, celebration dinners and fundraisings.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Events are run by —
a) the local council only  b) the guests themselves  c) event planners, hotel owners, banquet-room managers or restaurant owners
**Answer: c) event planners, hotel owners, banquet-room managers or restaurant owners.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Promotional events exist to —
a) promote a sport or recreation activity to a target market and raise participation  b) raise money for charity  c) train new staff
**Answer: a) promote a sport or recreation activity to a target market and raise participation.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A risk audit is —
a) identifying and assessing all risks so a plan exists for any undesirable occurrence  b) a list of invited guests  c) a check of the event's accounts
**Answer: a) identifying and assessing all risks so a plan exists for any undesirable occurrence.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A risk audit protects against occurrences that could —
a) raise the event's cost slightly  b) make the event dull  c) harm people or damage the organisation
**Answer: c) harm people or damage the organisation.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Fundraising events include —
a) dinner functions, bingo/card nights, charity auctions and awards evenings  b) only government galas  c) only church collections
**Answer: a) dinner functions, bingo/card nights, charity auctions and awards evenings.**
