# Hospitality Events — Weddings, Meetings, Promotions, Conferences, Fundraisers — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** At a promotional event, the sponsor is promoted first and the activity second.
**Answer: False** — it is the other way: the activity is promoted to its market; the sponsor is promoted second.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The risk audit is named in the student book's types-of-events section.
**Answer: False** — it is teacher-guide evidence — the guide adds it; the student book's section does not name it.
