# Customer Service in Food Business — the Five Points

## Do it first

Two food businesses, same food. In one you return weekly; in the other you never went back. The food was the same — so what differed? Write the five things the good place did. The book fixes five points; compare yours.

## Sub lesson 1 — What customer service is

**Customer service is the provision of service to customers before, during and after delivery.** Its span is the whole relationship, not the moment of the sale.

And its success depends on the people: **employees who can adjust themselves to the personality of the guest**. The book's verdict is blunt: **great service can make or break a food preparation business** — which is why it fixes the practice into five points.

## Sub lesson 2 — The five points

**(i) Do it right from the start. (ii) Do not make customers wait. (iii) Fix problems immediately. (iv) Use customer comment cards. (v) Incorporate technology.**

Read them as a timeline of one visit: the first contact done right (i); the guest's time respected (ii); the failure — when it comes — repaired on the spot, not promised away (iii); the guest's own words collected (iv); and the tools of the age put to work (v). Five points, memorised in order, are the standard a food worker carries into every shift.

## Say it to your companion

Define customer service for your companion — span and human condition — then give the five points numbered, in order.
