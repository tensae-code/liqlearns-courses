# Video script — Customer Service in Food Business — the Five Points

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Service is **before, during and after** delivery — the whole relationship.

And it hangs on people who adjust to the guest in front of them.

The five points. **One** — right from the start. **Two** — never make them wait.

**Three** — fix problems immediately. **Four** — comment cards: hear the guest's own words.

**Five** — incorporate technology.

Great service makes the business. Poor service breaks it. Five points decide which.
