# Hands-on — Customer Service in Food Business — the Five Points

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes — real trade practice, done safely.

## Design the comment card

Design a customer comment card for one food business: five questions at most, answerable in one minute, with space for one free sentence. Pilot it on a family member 'as a guest' and record what their answers teach you.

## Fix it immediately — role-play

With a partner: they are a guest whose order arrived wrong and late. You have ninety seconds to (a) fix the problem immediately and (b) keep points (i) and (ii) alive for the next guest watching. Swap roles and judge each other against the five points.
