# Customer Service in Food Business — the Five Points — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Customer service spans —
a) only the moment of sale  b) only after a complaint  c) before, during and after delivery
**Answer: c) before, during and after delivery.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Service success depends on employees who —
a) follow one script for everyone  b) are the fastest cooks  c) can adjust themselves to the personality of the guest
**Answer: c) can adjust themselves to the personality of the guest.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Great service can —
a) only matter in hotels  b) make or break a food preparation business  c) matter little beside the food
**Answer: b) make or break a food preparation business.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Point (i) is —
a) do it right from the start  b) use comment cards  c) incorporate technology
**Answer: a) do it right from the start.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Point (iii) is —
a) do not make customers wait  b) do it right from the start  c) fix problems immediately
**Answer: c) fix problems immediately.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Point (iv) is —
a) use customer comment cards  b) fix problems immediately  c) incorporate technology
**Answer: a) use customer comment cards.**
