# Customer Service in Food Business — the Five Points — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Customer service ends when the food is delivered.
**Answer: False** — it continues after delivery — the span is before, during and after.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Point (ii) — do not make customers wait — is about respecting the guest's time.
**Answer: True** — that is its place in the visit's timeline.
