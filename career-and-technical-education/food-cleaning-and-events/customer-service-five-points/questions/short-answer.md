# Customer Service in Food Business — the Five Points — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does the book make personality-adjustment the condition of service success?
**Answer:** Because every guest differs; employees who adjust themselves to the guest's personality deliver the service that guest needs, and that service is what makes or breaks the business.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Recite the five points in order.
**Answer:** (i) Do it right from the start; (ii) do not make customers wait; (iii) fix problems immediately; (iv) use customer comment cards; (v) incorporate technology.
