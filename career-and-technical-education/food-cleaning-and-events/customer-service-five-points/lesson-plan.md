# Lesson plan — Customer Service in Food Business — the Five Points

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** Food, Cleaning and Events · **Lesson:** Customer Service in Food Business — the Five Points
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 8 — Food, Cleaning and Events (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the five points of proper customer service in a food business: do it right from the start, don't make customers wait, fix problems immediately, use comment cards, incorporate technology.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the whole food half of this title: the food is made; this lesson serves it well.

## Sub lessons
1. **What customer service is** — before, during and after — and the human condition
2. **The five points** — right from the start, no waiting, fix it now, comment cards, technology

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Serving Food — Counter Service and Table Service. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Define customer service by its span: before, during and after delivery.
2. List the five points in order.
3. Say what success depends on: employees adjusting to the guest's personality.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil; a blank comment card to design in hands-on.

## If the learner is stuck
- Defines service as 'being polite at the till' → the book's span is before, during AND after delivery, and its measure is the five operational points, not a smile alone.
- Recites points out of order → the book numbers them (i)–(v); the order is a timeline of one visit. Rebuild it as the visit: start, wait, problem, feedback, tools.
