# Hands-on — Hospitality, Food Preparation and Cleaning — Review Questions

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes — real trade practice, done safely.

## Self-marked test pass

Take all the questions in questions/ with the lessons closed. Mark yourself against the answers given. For every miss, write the lesson it belongs to — that list is your revision plan.

## Write one written answer as a teacher

Choose the written answer on PPE use or on cleaning equipment and write it as a model answer for a younger learner: short sentences, the book's items, no extras. Teaching form is the deepest check of knowing.
