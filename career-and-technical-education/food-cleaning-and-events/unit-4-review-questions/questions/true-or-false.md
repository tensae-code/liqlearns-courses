# Hospitality, Food Preparation and Cleaning — Review Questions — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1.** Event managers carry responsibility for health and safety at their events.
**Answer: True** — the risk-audit teaching and the review agree: organisers plan for what could harm people.

**2.** A cleaner's dress for office cleaning must follow set appearance rules — clean, pressed, fitting clothing and closed shoes.
**Answer: True** — the appearance rules of Cleaning Safely apply at work, offices included.

**3.** Fresh food is always served cooked.
**Answer: False** — the book lists fresh foods eaten raw — fruits, raw vegetables, salads — alongside cooked snacks.
