# Hospitality, Food Preparation and Cleaning — Review Questions — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The best description of cleaning skills for a hired cleaner is —
a) a certificate framed on the wall  b) the practical skills of the cleaning work itself — methods, equipment and agents used well  c) speed alone
**Answer: b) the practical skills of the cleaning work itself — methods, equipment and agents used well.**

**2.** Which item is NOT a kitchen tool of Table 4.1?
a) a mesh strainer  b) a tyre pressure gauge  c) kitchen tongs
**Answer: b) a tyre pressure gauge.**

**3.** Guest comfort in hospitality rests first on —
a) the size of the building  b) the host receiving the guest with goodwill  c) the price of the room
**Answer: b) the host receiving the guest with goodwill.**
