# Hospitality, Food Preparation and Cleaning — Review Questions — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or words.

**1.** The business of providing food at sites and venues is food preparation service — ______ by its common name.
**Answer: catering.**

**2.** The kitchen tool for cutting and scoring, with a serrated kind for slicing bread, is the ______.
**Answer: knife.**

**3.** Weddings, meetings, conferences and fundraisers are gathered as hospitality ______.
**Answer: events.**

**4.** A detergent bought as a liquid is a ______ detergent — one of the seven agent families in liquid form.
**Answer: liquid.**
