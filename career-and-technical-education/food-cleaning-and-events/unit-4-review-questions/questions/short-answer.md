# Hospitality, Food Preparation and Cleaning — Review Questions — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1.** Write the book's answer: the types of hospitality events.
**Answer:** Weddings, business meetings, celebration dinners and fundraisings; promotional events; and conferences — run by event planners, hotel owners, banquet-room managers or restaurant owners.

**2.** Write the book's answer: the components of a balanced diet.
**Answer:** Vitamins, minerals and antioxidants; carbohydrates (including starches and fibre); protein; and healthy fats — drawn from fruits, vegetables, grains, dairy and protein foods.

**3.** Write the book's answer: proper use of PPE in cleaning work.
**Answer:** Wear the equipment that minimises exposure to the job's hazards: gloves, safety glasses and shoes, earplugs or muffs, hard hats, respirators, coveralls, vests or full body suits — the right items for the chemical, physical, electrical and mechanical hazards present.

**4.** Write the book's answer: the cleaning equipment kit.
**Answer:** Vacuum cleaner with attachments for hardwood and carpet; bucket or container for supplies; mop and bucket; dusters, long and short; dustpan and broom; paper towels — with the sample kit's toilet paper and dispensers, trash cans and liners.
