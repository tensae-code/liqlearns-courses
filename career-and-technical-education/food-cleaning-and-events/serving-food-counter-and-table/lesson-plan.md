# Lesson plan — Serving Food — Counter Service and Table Service

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** Food, Cleaning and Events · **Lesson:** Serving Food — Counter Service and Table Service
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 8 — Food, Cleaning and Events (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** food preparation service — what the business provides and where — and its two service forms: counter service and table service, and where each is common.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Hospitality — Welcoming Guests as an Industry: food and drink service was one of its fields; now the service itself.

## Sub lessons
1. **Food preparation service — the business** — providing food at sites and venues, from hot meals to buffets
2. **The two service forms** — counter service and table service, and where each lives

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Hospitality — Welcoming Guests as an Industry. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Define food preparation service and its range, from hot meals to buffet platters.
2. Distinguish counter service from table service precisely.
3. Name where each form is common.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil.

## If the learner is stuck
- Calls any delivered-to-table meal 'table service' → the book puts 'picked up there OR delivered to the table' inside COUNTER service: the form is decided by where the order is placed.
- Forgets the business's range → from hot gourmet meals on-site to buffet platters, at sites from hospitals to film sets: range is half the definition.
