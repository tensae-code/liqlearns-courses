## Sub lesson 1 — Food preparation service — the business

**Food preparation** is the business of **providing food services at a remote site or at hotels, hospitals, bars, parks, filming and entertainment sites and event venues**.

Its range runs wide: anything from **hot gourmet meals prepared on-site** to **buffet food in chafing dishes and party platters**. The business follows the eating to wherever the eaters are — that is what makes it a service trade, not only a kitchen trade.

## Sub lesson 2 — The two service forms

**Counter service:** food is **ordered at the counter** and **picked up there or delivered to the table**. It is common in **fast food restaurants, pubs and bars**.

**Table service:** food is **ordered at the table and served there by waiters and waitresses**. It is common in **most restaurants**. The difference is where the ordering happens and who travels — the customer to the counter, or the server to the table.
