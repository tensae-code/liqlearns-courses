# Serving Food — Counter Service and Table Service — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Food preparation service follows the eating to where the eaters are.
**Answer: True** — that is why the book lists remote sites and venues — it is a service trade.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** If you ordered at the counter and the food was brought to your table, that was table service.
**Answer: False** — by the book's definition it is counter service — the order was placed at the counter.
