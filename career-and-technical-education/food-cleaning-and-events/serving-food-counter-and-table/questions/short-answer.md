# Serving Food — Counter Service and Table Service — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name four sites or venues the business serves.
**Answer:** Any four of: remote sites, hotels, hospitals, bars, parks, filming sites, entertainment sites, event venues.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** State the one deciding question between the two forms, and answer it for each.
**Answer:** Where is the food ordered (and who travels)? Counter service: ordered at the counter — the customer travels to order. Table service: ordered at the table — the server travels to the customer.
