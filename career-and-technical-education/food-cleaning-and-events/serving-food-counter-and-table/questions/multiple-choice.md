# Serving Food — Counter Service and Table Service — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Food preparation service provides food at —
a) only private homes  b) only restaurants with kitchens  c) remote sites and venues such as hotels, hospitals, bars, parks and event venues
**Answer: c) remote sites and venues such as hotels, hospitals, bars, parks and event venues.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Its range runs from —
a) raw ingredients only  b) sandwiches only  c) hot gourmet meals prepared on-site to buffet food in chafing dishes and party platters
**Answer: c) hot gourmet meals prepared on-site to buffet food in chafing dishes and party platters.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Filming and entertainment sites are —
a) among the places food preparation service serves  b) served only by hotels  c) never served by caterers
**Answer: a) among the places food preparation service serves.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In counter service, food is —
a) ordered by phone only  b) ordered at the counter, and picked up there or delivered to the table  c) ordered at the table and served there
**Answer: b) ordered at the counter, and picked up there or delivered to the table.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Table service is common in —
a) pubs and bars  b) most restaurants  c) fast food restaurants
**Answer: b) most restaurants.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Counter service is common in —
a) wedding halls only  b) hospitals only  c) fast food restaurants, pubs and bars
**Answer: c) fast food restaurants, pubs and bars.**
