# Hands-on — Serving Food — Counter Service and Table Service

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes — real trade practice, done safely.

## Classify your eating places

List every place you have eaten outside home. Mark each C (counter) or T (table) using the one question: where did you order, and who travelled? Any hybrid (ordered at counter, delivered to table) belongs to counter service by the book's definition — say why in one line.

## Serve both ways (role-play)

With a partner and pretend dishes, run one order both ways: first counter service (they come to you, order, collect), then table service (you go to their 'table', take the order, serve it there). Note which form asks more of the server and which asks more of the customer.
