# Video script — Serving Food — Counter Service and Table Service

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Food service is a business that travels — hotels, hospitals, parks, film sets, events.

Hot meals cooked on site. Buffets in chafing dishes. Party platters. Wherever the eaters are.

Two forms serve it. **Counter service** — order at the counter, collect there or have it brought over. Fast food, pubs, bars.

**Table service** — order at your table; the waiter brings it there. Most restaurants.

Who travels — customer or server? That one question names the form.
