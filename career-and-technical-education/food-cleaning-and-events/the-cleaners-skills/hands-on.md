# Hands-on — The Cleaner's Skills — People, Body, Numbers, Detail

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes — real trade practice, done safely.

## The four-skills self-audit

Rate yourself honestly on the four possessions (strong / growing / weak) with one piece of evidence each — a moment you communicated well, a physical task you handled, a label you read correctly, a detail you caught. Choose the weakest and write one practice for it this week.

## Spot the detail

In one room, hunt like a cleaner: find five details an ordinary glance misses (a dusty top edge, a marked switch plate, a cobwebbed corner). Write them as a detail-list. Attention to detail is trainable — this hunt is the training.
