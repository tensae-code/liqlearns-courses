# Video script — The Cleaner's Skills — People, Body, Numbers, Detail

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Good news at the door: **no specific qualifications or experience** are needed to start as a cleaner.

But good cleaners are made of four things.

**People skills** — clients met face to face. **Physical abilities** — the work is bodily.

**Numeracy and literacy** — labels, measures, records. **Attention to detail** — the missed corner is the verdict.

Open door. High standard. That is the trade.
