# Lesson plan — The Cleaner's Skills — People, Body, Numbers, Detail

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** Food, Cleaning and Events · **Lesson:** The Cleaner's Skills — People, Body, Numbers, Detail
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 8 — Food, Cleaning and Events (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** what a good cleaner possesses: good interpersonal and communication skills, physical abilities, good numeracy and literacy, and a high level of attention to detail — with no specific qualifications or experience needed to start.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the cleaning lessons before it: the learner can do the work; now what makes a cleaner good at it — and employable.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the
exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an
image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture
of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does
with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and
while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is
dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the
student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph,
or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be
shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
1. **Open entry — no qualifications demanded** — starting as a cleaner needs no specific qualifications or experience
2. **The four possessions of a good cleaner** — people skills, physical abilities, numeracy and literacy, attention to detail

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Cleaning Services and Cleaning Equipment. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the entry fact: no specific qualifications or experience needed to start.
2. List the four skill possessions.
3. Match each skill to its moment in a cleaner's day.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil.

## If the learner is stuck
- Reads 'no qualifications' as 'no standards' → the very next lines set the standard: four possessions define the GOOD cleaner. Entry is open; excellence is specified.
- Treats numeracy/literacy as school subjects only → in the cleaner's day they are dilutions measured, labels obeyed, stock counted, records written.
