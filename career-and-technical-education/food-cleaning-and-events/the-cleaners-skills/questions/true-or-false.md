# The Cleaner's Skills — People, Body, Numbers, Detail — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The book names what a GOOD cleaner possesses immediately after the entry fact.
**Answer: True** — the four possessions follow the open-door statement on the same pages.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Physical abilities are one of the four possessions.
**Answer: True** — the work is bodily, all shift long.
