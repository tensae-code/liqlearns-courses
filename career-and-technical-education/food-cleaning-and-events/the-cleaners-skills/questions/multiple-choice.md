# The Cleaner's Skills — People, Body, Numbers, Detail — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** To start as a cleaner, the book requires —
a) a cleaning certificate  b) no specific qualifications or experience  c) two years of experience
**Answer: b) no specific qualifications or experience.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Open entry matters because —
a) this course serves school leavers choosing sellable work  b) employers ask nothing  c) cleaning needs no skill
**Answer: a) this course serves school leavers choosing sellable work.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Open entry does NOT mean —
a) that a beginner can be hired  b) that anyone is automatically good at the work  c) that training is pointless to offer
**Answer: b) that anyone is automatically good at the work.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The four possessions are —
a) speed, strength, silence and height  b) a certificate, a uniform, tools and transport  c) interpersonal/communication skills, physical abilities, numeracy and literacy, attention to detail
**Answer: c) interpersonal/communication skills, physical abilities, numeracy and literacy, attention to detail.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Numeracy and literacy serve the cleaner in —
a) only in examinations  b) measures, labels, lists and records  c) nothing in daily work
**Answer: b) measures, labels, lists and records.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Attention to detail is judged by —
a) how loud the vacuum is  b) how fast the shift ends  c) the missed corner — the last uncleaned edge
**Answer: c) the missed corner — the last uncleaned edge.**
