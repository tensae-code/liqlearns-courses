# The Cleaner's Skills — People, Body, Numbers, Detail — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** State the entry fact and what follows it, in one breath.
**Answer:** No specific qualifications or experience are needed to start as a cleaner — but a good cleaner possesses the four skills the book lists.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Match each possession to its moment in a cleaner's day.
**Answer:** Interpersonal/communication — speaking with the client; physical abilities — carrying the work; numeracy and literacy — diluting, labelling, recording; attention to detail — catching the last missed corner.
