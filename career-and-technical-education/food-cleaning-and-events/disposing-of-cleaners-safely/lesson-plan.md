# Lesson plan — Disposing of Cleaners Safely — Drain or Trash

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** Food, Cleaning and Events · **Lesson:** Disposing of Cleaners Safely — Drain or Trash
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 8 — Food, Cleaning and Events (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** safe disposal of household cleaners by product type: water-soluble liquids, gels and powders down the drain with running water; detergents and prewash likewise; solid products in the trash.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Cleaning Agents — the Seven Families: the learner knows the substances; this lesson ends their life safely.

## Sub lessons
1. **Down the drain — with running water** — water-soluble liquids, gels and powders; detergents and prewash
2. **In the trash — the solid products** — soap scouring pads, sticks and the like

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Cleaning Agents — the Seven Families. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Sort disposal by type: drain vs trash.
2. State the running-water condition for drain disposal.
3. Apply the rule to the named examples: liquids/gels/powders, detergents/prewash, soap scouring pads and sticks.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Labels are read only; no products are poured for practice.

## If the learner is stuck
- Pours everything down the drain → solids are the counterexample: pads and sticks go in the TRASH. The question is dissolving, not convenience.
- Drops the condition → drain disposal is WITH RUNNING WATER. The water is part of the rule, not a garnish.
