# Disposing of Cleaners Safely — Drain or Trash — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** State the drain rule in full.
**Answer:** Most liquid, gel and powder water-soluble household cleaning products can go down the drain with running water just after use; detergents and prewash materials likewise go down the drain.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Sort these three and justify each: a liquid detergent's dregs; a used soap scouring pad; a soluble washing powder.
**Answer:** Liquid detergent — drain (water-soluble liquid; detergents go down the drain with running water). Scouring pad — trash (a solid product). Washing powder — drain (water-soluble powder, with running water).
