# Disposing of Cleaners Safely — Drain or Trash — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Water-soluble liquid, gel and powder products go —
a) down the drain with running water, just after use  b) in the trash  c) in a closed bottle kept at home
**Answer: a) down the drain with running water, just after use.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Detergents and prewash materials go —
a) down the drain  b) back on the shelf  c) in the trash
**Answer: a) down the drain.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The condition on drain disposal is —
a) with the bottle rinsed in a bucket first  b) only at night  c) with running water
**Answer: c) with running water.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Soap scouring pads go —
a) in the recycling  b) in the trash  c) down the drain
**Answer: b) in the trash.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Cleaning sticks and similar solids go —
a) down the drain with water  b) in the trash  c) in a drain left overnight
**Answer: b) in the trash.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The one sorting question is —
a) does it dissolve in water?  b) is it expensive?  c) is it a liquid?
**Answer: a) does it dissolve in water?.**
