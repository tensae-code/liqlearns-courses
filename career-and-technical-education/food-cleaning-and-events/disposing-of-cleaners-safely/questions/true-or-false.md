# Disposing of Cleaners Safely — Drain or Trash — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A water-soluble powder is drain-fit because what dissolves in water can leave by water.
**Answer: True** — that is the rule's own logic.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Every finished cleaning product may go down the drain if enough water is used.
**Answer: False** — solid products — pads, sticks and the like — go in the trash, not the drain.
