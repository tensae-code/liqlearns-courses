# Video script — Disposing of Cleaners Safely — Drain or Trash

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Finished products have two roads out. One question chooses: **does it dissolve in water?**

Liquids, gels, soluble powders — **down the drain, with running water**, just after use. Detergents and prewash go the same way.

Solids — scouring pads, sticks — **in the trash**. They do not dissolve; the drain is not their road.

Water-soluble by water. Solid by bin. Sort every product, every time.
