# Hands-on — Disposing of Cleaners Safely — Drain or Trash

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes — real trade practice, done safely.

## Write the home disposal card

List every cleaning product in your home (labels read only) and write its disposal road beside it — DRAIN (with running water) or TRASH — using the dissolving question. Keep the card where the products live; it is a working document, not homework decoration.

## Sort the shelf (dry drill)

On paper, sort ten products: liquid detergent, washing powder, a gel cleaner, a soap scouring pad, a prewash liquid, a cleaning stick, a soluble powder, a solid soap block for scouring, a liquid floor cleaner, a used scouring stick. Mark D or T and give the reason in three words each.
