# Disposing of Cleaners Safely — Drain or Trash

## Do it first

Three products are finished: a liquid detergent bottle with dregs, a powder box, a used soap scouring pad. Where does each go — sink or bin? Write your calls. The book's rule sorts by product type; check yours against it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Down the drain — with running water

**Most liquid, gel and powder water-soluble household cleaning products can go down the drain with running water, just after use.**

**Detergents and prewash materials likewise go down the drain.** The condition travels with the rule: **with running water** — the water carries and dilutes as the product leaves. A product is drain-fit when it is water-soluble: what dissolves in water can leave by water.

## Sub lesson 2 — In the trash — the solid products

**Most solid products — soap scouring pads, sticks and the like — go in the trash.** Solids do not dissolve; the drain is not their road.

The sort is therefore one question, asked of every finished product: **does it dissolve in water?** Liquids, gels and soluble powders: drain, with running water. Solids: trash. Two roads, one question.

## Say it to your companion

Give your companion both roads: the drain list with its condition, the trash list, and the one question that sorts any product.
