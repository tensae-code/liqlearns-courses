# Video script — Cleaning Safely — PPE and How a Cleaner Presents

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**PPE** — equipment worn to cut your exposure to hazards: chemical, physical, electrical, mechanical.

The kit: gloves. Safety glasses and shoes. Earplugs or muffs. Hard hats. Respirators. Coveralls, vests, full body suits.

And the cleaner **presents** safely too: clothing clean, pressed, fitting. Closed shoes — no open toes.

Hair neat; long hair tied back near equipment and food. Nothing worn may interfere with the machine.

Protected AND presentable. Both are the job.
