# Cleaning Safely — PPE and How a Cleaner Presents — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** PPE is equipment worn to —
a) identify staff to customers  b) keep uniforms clean  c) minimise exposure to hazards causing serious injury and illness
**Answer: c) minimise exposure to hazards causing serious injury and illness.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The hazard families are —
a) hot, cold, wet and dry  b) only chemical  c) chemical, physical, electrical and mechanical
**Answer: c) chemical, physical, electrical and mechanical.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The book's PPE kit includes —
a) gloves, safety glasses and shoes, earplugs or muffs, hard hats, respirators, coveralls, vests and full body suits  b) a helmet and boots only  c) only gloves and an apron
**Answer: a) gloves, safety glasses and shoes, earplugs or muffs, hard hats, respirators, coveralls, vests and full body suits.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A cleaner's clothing must be —
a) any clothes, if old  b) clean, pressed, in good condition and fitting appropriately  c) loose and trailing, for comfort
**Answer: b) clean, pressed, in good condition and fitting appropriately.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Open-toe shoes for cleaning work are —
a) required in summer  b) not worn — and high heels are not recommended  c) the cleaner's choice
**Answer: b) not worn — and high heels are not recommended.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Long hair, when operating equipment or working in food and beverage, is —
a) covered by any hat  b) tied back and away from the face  c) left loose
**Answer: b) tied back and away from the face.**
