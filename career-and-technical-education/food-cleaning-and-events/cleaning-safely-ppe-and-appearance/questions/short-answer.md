# Cleaning Safely — PPE and How a Cleaner Presents — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why is the kit a system rather than a single item?
**Answer:** Because hazards come in four families — chemical, physical, electrical, mechanical — and each kit item answers a different exposure; the job decides which items are worn.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give the hair rules in full.
**Answer:** Hair, sideburns, moustache and beard neat and groomed, with no extreme artificial colours; long hair tied back and away from the face when operating equipment or working in food and beverage.
