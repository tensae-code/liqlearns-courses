# Lesson plan — Cleaning Safely — PPE and How a Cleaner Presents

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** Food, Cleaning and Events · **Lesson:** Cleaning Safely — PPE and How a Cleaner Presents
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 8 — Food, Cleaning and Events (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** personal protective equipment for cleaning work — what PPE is and its full kit — and the book's appearance rules for a cleaner: clothing, shoes, hair, and equipment-safe dress.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the cleaning lessons before it: the learner can clean a floor; this lesson protects the cleaner doing it.

## Sub lessons
1. **PPE — equipment that minimises exposure** — the definition, the hazard families, the kit
2. **Appearance — how a cleaner presents** — clothing, socks and shoes, hair — and the equipment clauses

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Define PPE and the hazard families it guards against.
2. List the PPE kit items.
3. Give the appearance rules: clothing, socks, shoes, hair — and the equipment clauses.

## If the learner is stuck
- Treats PPE as 'gloves' → the book's kit is nine entries across four hazard families. Count them; the job decides the subset, the kit defines the universe.
- Reads appearance as fashion rules → two clauses tie dress directly to safety: long hair tied back near equipment/food, and clothing must not interfere with operating equipment.
