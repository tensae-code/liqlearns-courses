# Hands-on — Cleaning Safely — PPE and How a Cleaner Presents

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes — real trade practice, done safely.

## Kit check

Take one real cleaning situation (home or school). List the hazards present by family — chemical, physical, electrical, mechanical — then write the PPE items from the book's kit that answer them. Any hazard with no answer is a stop-and-ask point, not a start point.

## The presentation check

Write the appearance rules as a mirror checklist (clothing / socks and shoes / hair / equipment clauses) and run it on a partner role-playing a cleaner arriving for a shift. Correct kindly, item by item, from the book.
