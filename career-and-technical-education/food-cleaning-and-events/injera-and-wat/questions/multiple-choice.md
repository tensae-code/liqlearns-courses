# Ethiopian Foods — Injera and Wat, and How Wat Is Built — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Injera is —
a) a sour, spongy round bread made from teff flour  b) a roasted barley loaf  c) a flat wheat pancake with no sourness
**Answer: a) a sour, spongy round bread made from teff flour.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Injera's colour — white, red or mixed — follows —
a) the teff variety it is made from  b) the wat served on it  c) how long it cooks
**Answer: a) the teff variety it is made from.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Injera is eaten —
a) with wat  b) only alone  c) only at breakfast
**Answer: a) with wat.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A wat begins with —
a) the meat, browned first  b) a large amount of chopped onion, simmered soft  c) red pepper in hot oil
**Answer: b) a large amount of chopped onion, simmered soft.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** For a milder yellow wat, the cook adds —
a) yellow food colour  b) extra butter only  c) turmeric instead of red pepper
**Answer: c) turmeric instead of red pepper.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Prepared food offered for sale must be —
a) as cheap as possible  b) free of bad substances, fairly priced, and inside the regulations governing food sellers  c) sold only at markets
**Answer: b) free of bad substances, fairly priced, and inside the regulations governing food sellers.**
