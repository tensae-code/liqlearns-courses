# Ethiopian Foods — Injera and Wat, and How Wat Is Built — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Describe injera in one full sentence.
**Answer:** Injera is the sour, spongy round bread made from teff flour, its colour (white, red or mixed) following the teff variety, eaten with wat.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Build a beef wat in order, step by step.
**Answer:** Simmer a large amount of chopped onion soft; add butter or vegetable oil; add red pepper for a spicy red wat; then add the beef (the main ingredient) and cook the wat through.
