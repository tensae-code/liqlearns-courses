# Video script — Ethiopian Foods — Injera and Wat, and How Wat Is Built

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Injera** — sour, spongy, round. Made from **teff**, white, red or mixed, as the teff itself is.

On it, **wat** — and wat is built in order.

Onion first — a large amount, simmered soft. Then butter or oil.

Red pepper for the spicy red wat. Turmeric for the mild yellow. Neither, for vegetable stew.

Then the main: meat, legumes, or vegetables.

And the seller's rules: clean food, fair price, inside the regulations.
