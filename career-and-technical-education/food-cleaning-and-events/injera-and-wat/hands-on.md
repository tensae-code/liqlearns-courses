# Hands-on — Ethiopian Foods — Injera and Wat, and How Wat Is Built

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes — real trade practice, done safely.

## Watch or help a wat being built (adult's kitchen)

With an adult cooking, observe a real wat from the first onion. Tick the book's order as it happens — onion, fat, colour, main — and note the moment the colour goes in: red or yellow? Afterwards, write the order from memory and check it.

## The injera swirl (observation)

If injera is being made near you, watch the batter swirled onto the mitad and the lid lifted. Write three observations: the spiral's path, the bubbles as it cooks, the sponginess when lifted. If no mitad is near, study the GIF and answer from it.
