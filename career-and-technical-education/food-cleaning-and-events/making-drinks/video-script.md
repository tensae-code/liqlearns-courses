# Video script — Making Drinks — Hot Drinks and Soft Drinks

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Four hot drinks are **skills**, says the book: fresh ginger tea. Fruit tea. Coffee. Hot lemon.

Make them well — strength right, temperature right, poured steady.

Cold drinks are mixtures: dry or fresh ingredients with water, home-made or factory-made.

Syrup into carbonated water. Or the slow way — **lacto-fermentation**.

Small inputs, real care. A drinks business can start on a tray.
