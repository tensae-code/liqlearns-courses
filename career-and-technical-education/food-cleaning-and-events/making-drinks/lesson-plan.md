# Lesson plan — Making Drinks — Hot Drinks and Soft Drinks

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** Food, Cleaning and Events · **Lesson:** Making Drinks — Hot Drinks and Soft Drinks
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 8 — Food, Cleaning and Events (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the drinks a food business prepares: the four hot drinks of the book's Table 4.2 — ginger tea, fruit tea, coffee, hot lemon — and how cold soft drinks are made.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Balanced Meals: drinks complete the meal the kitchen serves.

## Sub lessons
1. **Hot drinks — Table 4.2** — ginger tea, fruit tea, coffee, hot lemon
2. **Cold drinks — soft drinks and how they are made** — mixing with water, syrup with carbonated water, lacto-fermentation

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Balanced Meals — Nutrients and the Five Food Groups. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the four hot drinks needing preparation skill.
2. Give the three ways soft drinks are made.
3. Practise the serving pour carefully (hands-on, with warm — never boiling — water and an adult).

## What you need
Paper and a pencil; hands-on needs a kettle of WARM water, a jug and cups, and an adult present. No boiling water in learner practice.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the learner lists every drink they could serve a guest right now before the table is shown (lesson/lesson.md).
2. **The explanation** — Table 4.2's four hot drinks, then the soft-drink makings in the book's three ways (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD GIF [GIF 11974]: a hot drink being poured from a kettle to a cup in a steady stream, the cup filled without spilling — the student watch the stream stay thin and steady — control, not speed, is the serving skill.
3. **Say it to your companion** — the four hot drinks and the three soft-drink ways (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD VIDEO: the script in video-script.md, filmed — the lesson in the super-simple voice (made later, when videos are made).
4. **Questions** — from questions/: served at the learner's level and teaching-mix (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** — planning the drinks for one small gathering, hot and cold (lesson/lesson.md).

## If the learner is stuck
- Lists 'tea, coffee' vaguely → Table 4.2 is exact: FRESH GINGER tea, FRUIT tea, coffee, hot lemon. The adjectives are part of the entries.
- Thinks soft drinks are only factory products → the book: factory-made OR home-made, by mixing — a starting business lives in the home-made half.
