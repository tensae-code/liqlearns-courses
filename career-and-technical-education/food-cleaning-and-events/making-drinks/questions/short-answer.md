# Making Drinks — Hot Drinks and Soft Drinks — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does the book call these drinks skills rather than afterthoughts?
**Answer:** Each has its making — strength, timing, serving temperature — and a food business is judged by its drinks as surely as by its food.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give the book's three makings of soft drinks.
**Answer:** Mixing dry or fresh ingredients with water; mixing syrup or dry ingredients with carbonated water; and lacto-fermentation.
