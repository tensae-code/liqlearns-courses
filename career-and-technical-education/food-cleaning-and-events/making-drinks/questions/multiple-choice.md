# Making Drinks — Hot Drinks and Soft Drinks — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Table 4.2's hot drinks are —
a) coffee and tea only  b) fresh ginger tea, fruit tea, coffee and hot lemon  c) hot chocolate, coffee, tea and milk
**Answer: b) fresh ginger tea, fruit tea, coffee and hot lemon.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The hot drinks are named as needing —
a) imported ingredients  b) preparation skill  c) expensive machines
**Answer: b) preparation skill.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Hot lemon is —
a) a cold drink  b) a kind of soup  c) one of the four skilled hot drinks
**Answer: c) one of the four skilled hot drinks.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Soft drinks are made by —
a) mixing dry or fresh ingredients with water  b) freezing fruit juice solid  c) distilling alcohol
**Answer: a) mixing dry or fresh ingredients with water.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Soft drinks may be —
a) factory-made or home-made  b) only home-made  c) only factory-made
**Answer: a) factory-made or home-made.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** One book way of making a soft drink is —
a) adding yeast to bread dough  b) boiling milk  c) mixing syrup or dry ingredients with carbonated water
**Answer: c) mixing syrup or dry ingredients with carbonated water.**
