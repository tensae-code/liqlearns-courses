# Hands-on — Making Drinks — Hot Drinks and Soft Drinks

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes — real trade practice, done safely.

## The steady pour (adult present, warm water only)

With an adult and WARM — never boiling — water in a kettle or jebena-shaped pot, practise pouring a thin steady stream into small cups without spilling: three cups in a row, each filled evenly. This is the ceremony pour as a serving skill; control, not speed, is the mark.

## Make a home soft drink

Mix one cold drink from dry or fresh ingredients and water (for example a fruit drink from fresh fruit, water and a little sugar — an adult present for any cutting). Serve it to a taster and record their verdict: too weak, too strong, or right? Adjust once and serve again.
