# Balanced Meals — Nutrients and the Five Food Groups

## Do it first

Write everything you ate yesterday. Now sort it: which foods built you, which gave energy, which protected you? Keep the piles — the book's five food groups and nutrient list will sort the same food more exactly.

## Sub lesson 1 — What a balanced diet includes

A **healthy balanced diet includes vitamins, minerals and antioxidants; carbohydrates — including starches and fibre; protein; and healthy fats**.

Read the list as four shelves: the protectors (vitamins, minerals, antioxidants), the energy shelf (carbohydrates — starches for fuel, fibre for the works), the builders (protein), and healthy fats. A meal plan that empties one shelf is not balanced, however full the others are.

## Sub lesson 2 — The five food groups — and the cost of imbalance

The nutrients are drawn from **five food groups: fruits, vegetables, grains, dairy and protein foods**.

Why it matters: **a balanced diet supplies what the body needs to work effectively; without it, the body is more prone to disease, infection, fatigue and low performance.** Balance is not politeness toward food — it is maintenance of the worker. The teacher guide opens this same lesson with the nutrient frame and sends pupils to a local food business to see balanced food produced for real (see the teacher note).
