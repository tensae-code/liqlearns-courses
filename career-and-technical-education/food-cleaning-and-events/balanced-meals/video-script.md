# Video script — Balanced Meals — Nutrients and the Five Food Groups

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Balance is maintenance for the body.

The nutrients: **vitamins, minerals, antioxidants**. **Carbohydrates** — starches and fibre. **Protein**. **Healthy fats**.

They come from five groups: **fruits, vegetables, grains, dairy, protein foods**.

Miss the balance, and the body pays: disease, infection, fatigue, low performance.

Feed all the shelves. The worker runs on them.
