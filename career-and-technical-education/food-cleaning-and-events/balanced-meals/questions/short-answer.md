# Balanced Meals — Nutrients and the Five Food Groups — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** List the four nutrient shelves as the lesson groups them.
**Answer:** Protectors — vitamins, minerals, antioxidants; energy — carbohydrates (starches and fibre); builders — protein; and healthy fats.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the four costs of imbalance.
**Answer:** Disease, infection, fatigue and low performance.
