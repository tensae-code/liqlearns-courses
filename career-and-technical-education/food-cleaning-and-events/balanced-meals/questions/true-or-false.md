# Balanced Meals — Nutrients and the Five Food Groups — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A meal heavy in one group but missing another can still be unbalanced.
**Answer: True** — balance is coverage of every shelf and group — size does not fix a missing group.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The guide's evidence for this lesson is complete.
**Answer: False** — the guide file ends mid-lesson inside this lesson — its evidence here is partial, and is labelled so.
