# Balanced Meals — Nutrients and the Five Food Groups — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A healthy balanced diet includes —
a) vitamins, minerals and antioxidants; carbohydrates; protein; and healthy fats  b) only fruits and water  c) only protein and fats
**Answer: a) vitamins, minerals and antioxidants; carbohydrates; protein; and healthy fats.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Carbohydrates in the diet include —
a) only sugars  b) starches and fibre  c) only proteins
**Answer: b) starches and fibre.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The 'builders' shelf of the lesson is —
a) antioxidants  b) fibre  c) protein
**Answer: c) protein.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The five food groups are —
a) fruits, meats, sweets, drinks and snacks  b) grains, oils, salts, sugars and water  c) fruits, vegetables, grains, dairy and protein foods
**Answer: c) fruits, vegetables, grains, dairy and protein foods.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Without a balanced diet the body is more prone to —
a) needing less sleep  b) disease, infection, fatigue and low performance  c) growing too fast
**Answer: b) disease, infection, fatigue and low performance.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** A balanced diet supplies —
a) only energy for sport  b) only fullness  c) what the body needs to work effectively
**Answer: c) what the body needs to work effectively.**
