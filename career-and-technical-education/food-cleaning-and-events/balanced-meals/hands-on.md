# Hands-on — Balanced Meals — Nutrients and the Five Food Groups

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes — real trade practice, done safely.

## Visit a food business (the guide's pattern)

Following the teacher guide: visit a local small enterprise producing food items, in a group, and write a group visit report — which food groups does the business serve? Which nutrients does its food carry? Where is its balance strong, and where thin?

## Balance one day

Take yesterday's real eating (from Do It First) and re-plan it on paper so all five groups appear and all four nutrient shelves are served. Change as little as possible — balance is usually an addition, not a rebuild.
