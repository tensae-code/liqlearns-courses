# Lesson plan — Balanced Meals — Nutrients and the Five Food Groups

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** Food, Cleaning and Events · **Lesson:** Balanced Meals — Nutrients and the Five Food Groups
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 8 — Food, Cleaning and Events (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** what a balanced diet carries — vitamins, minerals, antioxidants, carbohydrates, protein and healthy fats — drawn from the five food groups, and what the body loses without it.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Kitchen Tools: the learner can equip a kitchen; now what the kitchen should produce.

## Sub lessons
1. **What a balanced diet includes** — vitamins, minerals, antioxidants, carbohydrates, protein, healthy fats
2. **The five food groups — and the cost of imbalance** — fruits, vegetables, grains, dairy, protein foods

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Kitchen Tools — Names, Jobs, and the Essential Kit. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. List the nutrient families a healthy balanced diet includes.
2. Name the five food groups.
3. State what happens without balance: disease, infection, fatigue, low performance.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil.

## If the learner is stuck
- Recites groups but cannot map nutrients → drill shelf↔group pairs: protein group→protein; grains→carbohydrates; fruits/vegetables→vitamins, minerals, antioxidants, fibre.
- Thinks balance means large amounts → balance is coverage: every shelf served. A mountain of one group is an unbalanced meal at any size.
