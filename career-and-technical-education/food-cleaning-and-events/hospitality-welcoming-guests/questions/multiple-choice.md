# Hospitality — Welcoming Guests as an Industry — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Hospitality is —
a) the relationship between a guest and a host, where the host receives the guest with goodwill  b) selling food to travellers  c) any job in a hotel
**Answer: a) the relationship between a guest and a host, where the host receives the guest with goodwill.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The host receives the guest —
a) with a bill first  b) only after payment  c) with goodwill
**Answer: c) with goodwill.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Hospitality includes the reception and entertainment of —
a) only family members  b) guests, visitors or strangers  c) only paying customers
**Answer: b) guests, visitors or strangers.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The hospitality industry covers —
a) only hotels and bars  b) farming and construction  c) lodging, food and drink service, event planning, theme parks, travel and tourism
**Answer: c) lodging, food and drink service, event planning, theme parks, travel and tourism.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The industry offers school leavers jobs —
a) only abroad  b) helping customers meet leisure and recreational needs  c) only in management
**Answer: b) helping customers meet leisure and recreational needs.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Cleaning, in the book's definition, is —
a) wiping tables after meals only  b) sweeping floors in the morning  c) removing dirt and preventing food residues that decompose, grow disease-causing organisms or produce toxins
**Answer: c) removing dirt and preventing food residues that decompose, grow disease-causing organisms or produce toxins.**
