# Hospitality — Welcoming Guests as an Industry — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which three ideas must a definition of hospitality carry?
**Answer:** A relationship between guest and host; the host receiving the guest; and goodwill in the receiving.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name four fields of the hospitality industry.
**Answer:** Any four of: lodging; food and drink service; event planning; theme parks; travel and tourism.
