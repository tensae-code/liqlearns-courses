# Video script — Hospitality — Welcoming Guests as an Industry

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A guest arrives. A host receives them — **with goodwill**. That relationship is **hospitality**.

As an industry it is wide: lodging, food and drink, events, theme parks, travel and tourism.

Hotels, restaurants, bars, agencies — jobs for school leavers, serving people's leisure.

And its second hand is **cleaning**: dirt removed, food residues stopped before they breed disease.

Welcome warmly. Keep it clean. That is this title.
