# Lesson plan — Hospitality — Welcoming Guests as an Industry

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** Food, Cleaning and Events · **Lesson:** Hospitality — Welcoming Guests as an Industry
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 8 — Food, Cleaning and Events (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** what hospitality is — the guest–host relationship of goodwill — and the industry it names: lodging, food and drink, events, theme parks, travel and tourism, plus the book's definition of cleaning.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on nothing but the learner's own experience of being a guest and a host; it opens the Food, Cleaning and Events title.

## Sub lessons
1. **Hospitality — the guest–host relationship** — goodwill between guest and host
2. **Hospitality — the industry, and cleaning beside it** — the broad service category, its jobs, and the cleaning definition

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Define hospitality as a relationship, in the book's words.
2. List the industry's fields.
3. Say what cleaning is, in the book's definition, and why it stands beside hospitality.

## What you need

## If the learner is stuck
- Defines hospitality as 'hotels' → hotels are one field of the industry; the definition itself is the guest–host relationship received with goodwill.
- Treats cleaning as separate drudgery → the book defines it beside hospitality for a reason: residues breed disease-causing organisms; cleanliness is part of the welcome.
