# Hospitality — Welcoming Guests as an Industry

## Do it first

Remember two arrivals: once you were received so warmly you relaxed at once; once you stood unwelcomed while nobody looked up. What did the good host DO? Write it down — the book's definition of hospitality is hiding in your memory.

## Sub lesson 1 — Hospitality — the guest–host relationship

**Hospitality is the relationship between a guest and a host, in which the host receives the guest with goodwill** — the reception and entertainment of guests, visitors or strangers.

Every word earns its place: it is a *relationship*, not a transaction; the host *receives* — the welcome is an action; and it is done *with goodwill* — the warmth is part of the work, not decoration on it.

## Sub lesson 2 — Hospitality — the industry, and cleaning beside it

As an **industry**, hospitality is the broad service category of **lodging, food and drink service, event planning, theme parks, travel and tourism** — hotels, tourism agencies, restaurants and bars. It offers middle-level school leavers jobs helping customers meet their leisure and recreational needs, and good event management strengthens collaboration with stakeholders and partners.

Beside it stands **cleaning**, defined in the same breath: **removing dirt and preventing the food residues that decompose, grow disease-causing organisms or produce toxins**. Welcome and cleanliness are one trade's two hands — a guest received into dirt has not been received at all.
