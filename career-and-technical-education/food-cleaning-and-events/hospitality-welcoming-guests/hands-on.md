# Hands-on — Hospitality — Welcoming Guests as an Industry

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes — real trade practice, done safely.

## Map your town's hospitality

List every hospitality workplace in your area: lodging, food and drink, events, travel. Beside each, write the job a school leaver could do there. This is the title's job map for your own place.

## The goodwill audit

Take the warm-arrival memory from Do It First and list the host's actions one by one. Under each, write which word of the definition it shows: relationship, reception, or goodwill.
