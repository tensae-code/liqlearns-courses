# Hands-on — Kitchen Tools — Names, Jobs, and the Essential Kit

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes — real trade practice, done safely.

## Sort the kit — the guide's assessment

In the teacher guide's pattern (groups of five, mixed): sort the eleven entries into electrical and non-electrical piles, state the purpose of each tool, and prepare a summary report of your sort. Present the report to another group.

## Home-kitchen census

List the tools in your home kitchen. Mark each: in Table 4.1, or not? general-purpose or specialised? The census shows how the kit varies with cooking style — the book's own point, proven at home.
