# Kitchen Tools — Names, Jobs, and the Essential Kit — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What splits utensils into two kinds, and what is the book's example of each?
**Answer:** Purpose-range: general-purpose (a chef's knife, for many foods) and specialised (egg separator, apple corer — one job each).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name six entries of Table 4.1 with their jobs.
**Answer:** Any six of: knives — cutting/scoring (serrated for slicing bread); cutting boards — hygiene, easier cleaning; pasta strainer; spice grinder — coffee beans and whole spices; mesh strainer — velvet stocks/sauces; tongs — turning food, reaching into the oven; coffee maker; blenders — sauces, juices, soups; mixer — dough and batter; electric grill; oven — floor coil and roof broiler coil.
