# Kitchen Tools — Names, Jobs, and the Essential Kit — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A kitchen utensil is —
a) a kind of ingredient  b) any machine in a kitchen  c) a small hand-held tool for food preparation
**Answer: c) a small hand-held tool for food preparation.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A chef's knife is the book's example of —
a) a specialised utensil  b) an electrical utensil  c) a general-purpose utensil
**Answer: c) a general-purpose utensil.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** An egg separator and an apple corer are —
a) general-purpose utensils  b) not utensils at all  c) specialised utensils — one job each
**Answer: c) specialised utensils — one job each.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The serrated bread knife is for —
a) grinding spices  b) slicing  c) straining stocks
**Answer: b) slicing.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The mesh strainer gives stocks and sauces —
a) a smoky flavour  b) a velvet texture  c) extra heat
**Answer: b) a velvet texture.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Kitchen tongs are for —
a) turning food in the pan and reaching into the oven  b) mixing dough  c) measuring ingredients
**Answer: a) turning food in the pan and reaching into the oven.**
