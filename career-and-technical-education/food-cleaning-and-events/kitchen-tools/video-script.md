# Video script — Kitchen Tools — Names, Jobs, and the Essential Kit

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A **utensil** is a small hand tool for food: cutting, heating, baking, grinding, mixing, blending, measuring.

Some serve everything — the chef's knife. Some serve one thing — the egg separator.

The essential kit: knives and boards. Strainers — pasta and mesh. Spice grinder. Tongs.

Coffee maker. Blender for sauces and soups. Mixer for dough. Electric grill. The oven, coil below and broiler above.

Name the job, and your hand finds the tool.
