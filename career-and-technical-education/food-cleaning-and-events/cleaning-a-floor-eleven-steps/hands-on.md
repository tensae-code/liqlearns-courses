# Hands-on — Cleaning a Floor — the Eleven Steps

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes — real trade practice, done safely.

## Clean one floor by the eleven steps

With permission for the space, clean one real floor following the steps exactly, ticking each on a written list as you complete it. Note where you nearly broke order (most learners nearly mop before sweeping, or skip the final rinse) — the note is the learning.

## The backward walk

Practise step 6's movement dry (no water): with a dry mop, work a room from the far end toward the door, moving backward, feet always on the un-mopped side. The geometry — never trapping yourself in a wet corner — is the skill.
