# Cleaning a Floor — the Eleven Steps — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Step 5: dip and wring the mop until ______, not sopping.
**Answer: damp.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Step 8: rinse the mop in the ______ bucket, dunking up and down, then wring.
**Answer: rinse.**
