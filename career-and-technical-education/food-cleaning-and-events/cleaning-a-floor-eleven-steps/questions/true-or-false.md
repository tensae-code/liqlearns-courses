# Cleaning a Floor — the Eleven Steps — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Hotter water cleans better and quicker — the buckets are filled hot.
**Answer: True** — step 4's own note in the book.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Mop and bucket are stored as soon as the mopping ends, wet or dry.
**Answer: False** — step 11: rinse them and store only when dry — and the floor dries completely before walking.
