# Cleaning a Floor — the Eleven Steps — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Step 1 is —
a) sweep the floor  b) fill the buckets  c) choose a mop and bucket matched to the floor type
**Answer: c) choose a mop and bucket matched to the floor type.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** For textured ceramic tile, the book matches —
a) a string/strip mop  b) a dry dust mop  c) a sponge mop
**Answer: a) a string/strip mop.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Step 3 — sweep or vacuum — comes —
a) after the first mopping  b) only if the floor looks dirty  c) before the mop touches the floor, thoroughly
**Answer: c) before the mop touches the floor, thoroughly.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** While mopping, you move —
a) backward, always standing on the un-mopped area  b) in circles  c) forward, onto the clean floor
**Answer: a) backward, always standing on the un-mopped area.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Stubborn spots (step 7) get —
a) rapid back-and-forth rubbing with downward pressure  b) extra detergent poured on  c) a wire brush
**Answer: a) rapid back-and-forth rubbing with downward pressure.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The final rinse (step 10) uses —
a) fresh hot rinse water only  b) cold water with detergent  c) the wash bucket's water
**Answer: a) fresh hot rinse water only.**
