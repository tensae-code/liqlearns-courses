# Cleaning a Floor — the Eleven Steps — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give steps 1–5 in order.
**Answer:** Choose mop and bucket matched to floor type; choose a cleaner designed for the flooring; sweep or vacuum first, thoroughly; fill buckets with hot water, detergent in the wash bucket; dip and wring the mop until damp.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give steps 6–11 in order.
**Answer:** Mop from one end to the other moving backward; stop for stubborn spots with rapid downward rubbing; rinse-dunk the mop and wring out dirty water; continue section by section watching both waters; final rinse with fresh hot water only; let everything dry — tools stored dry, floor dry before walking.
