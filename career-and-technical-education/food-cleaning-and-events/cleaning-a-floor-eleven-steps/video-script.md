# Video script — Cleaning a Floor — the Eleven Steps

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Eleven steps clean a floor. In order.

**One** — mop and bucket matched to the floor. **Two** — cleaner made for that flooring. **Three** — sweep first, thoroughly.

**Four** — hot water in both buckets; detergent in the wash bucket. **Five** — dip, and wring to **damp**.

**Six** — mop backward, standing always on the un-mopped floor. **Seven** — stubborn spots: fast rubs, downward pressure.

**Eight** — rinse-dunk the mop, wring the dirty water out. **Nine** — section by section, watch both waters.

**Ten** — final rinse, fresh hot water only. **Eleven** — everything dries. Floor, mop, bucket.

Finished means dry.
