# Lesson plan — Cleaning Agents — the Seven Families

**Course:** Career and Technical Education · **Title:** Food, Cleaning and Events · **Lesson:** Cleaning Agents — the Seven Families
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 8 — Food, Cleaning and Events (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** cleaning agents (hard-surface cleaners) — what they are, their forms, and their seven families: water, detergents, abrasives, degreasers, acid cleaners, organic solvents, and other cleaning agents.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Cleaning Equipment: kit in hand; now the chemistry it carries — the chemistry leg of the blend, classified.

## Sub lessons
1. **What cleaning agents are** — hard-surface cleaners in liquid, powder, spray and granule forms
2. **The seven families** — water, detergents, abrasives, degreasers, acid cleaners, organic solvents, others

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Cleaning — Removing Dirt, and Chemistry Plus Mechanics (agents are its chemical leg). Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Define cleaning agents and their forms.
2. List the seven families.
3. Match an agent family to the dirt it answers.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. No chemicals are handled in this lesson's work — labels are read, bottles are not opened.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the learner lists every cleaning substance in their home before the families are named (lesson/lesson.md).
2. **The explanation** — agents defined with their forms, then the seven families in the book's order (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 11984]: a picture of seven agent bottles and tubs in a row — water, detergents, abrasives, degreasers, acid cleaners, organic solvents, other agents — with the dirt each removes beneath — the student cover the family names and identify each bottle by the dirt beneath it.
3. **Say it to your companion** — the seven families from memory, in order (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD VIDEO: the script in video-script.md, filmed — the lesson in the super-simple voice (made later, when videos are made).
4. **Questions** — from questions/: served at the learner's level and teaching-mix (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** — classifying a real home's agents into the families (labels read only) (lesson/lesson.md).

## If the learner is stuck
- Memorises families but cannot use them → drill dirt→family, never family→definition alone: grease→degreaser, deposit→acid cleaner, scouring→abrasive.
- Surprised water is family one → water is an agent in the classification: the base every cleaning uses. The book lists it first deliberately.
