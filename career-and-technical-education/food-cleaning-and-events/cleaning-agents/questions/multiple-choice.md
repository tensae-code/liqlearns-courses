# Cleaning Agents — the Seven Families — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Cleaning agents are substances used to remove —
a) only grease  b) only bacteria  c) dirt, dust, stains, bad smells and clutter from surfaces
**Answer: c) dirt, dust, stains, bad smells and clutter from surfaces.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Their usual forms are —
a) liquids, powders, sprays or granules  b) only blocks and bars  c) only liquids
**Answer: a) liquids, powders, sprays or granules.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Cleaning agents are also called —
a) surface polishes only  b) soft-soap cleaners  c) hard-surface cleaners
**Answer: c) hard-surface cleaners.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The first family in the classification is —
a) detergents  b) acid cleaners  c) water
**Answer: c) water.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Grease is the target of —
a) abrasives  b) organic solvents only  c) degreasers
**Answer: c) degreasers.**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The seven families end with —
a) waxes  b) other cleaning agents  c) polishes
**Answer: b) other cleaning agents.**
