# Cleaning Agents — the Seven Families — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An agent is the chemistry leg of the cleaning blend, bottled.
**Answer: True** — the blend's chemical product, classified by family in this lesson.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The families are: water, detergents, abrasives, degreasers, acid cleaners, organic solvents, and other cleaning agents.
**Answer: True** — that is the book's classification, in order.
