# Cleaning Agents — the Seven Families — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give the book's definition of cleaning agents.
**Answer:** Substances — usually liquids, powders, sprays or granules — used to remove dirt, dust, stains, bad smells and clutter from surfaces (hard-surface cleaners).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** List all seven families in order.
**Answer:** Water; detergents; abrasives; degreasers; acid cleaners; organic solvents; and other cleaning agents.
