# Video script — Cleaning Agents — the Seven Families

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

**Cleaning agents** — the substances that remove dirt, dust, stains, smells and clutter. Liquids, powders, sprays, granules.

Seven families stock the shelf. **Water** — the base. **Detergents** — the everyday washers.

**Abrasives** scour. **Degreasers** cut grease. **Acid cleaners** dissolve deposits. **Organic solvents** dissolve what water cannot.

And the seventh family — **all the others** the trade uses.

Name the dirt first. The family follows.
