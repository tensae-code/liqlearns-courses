# Hands-on — Cleaning Agents — the Seven Families

The lesson with your hands, not only your eyes — real trade practice, done safely.

## Classify by label (read only)

With an adult and WITHOUT opening anything, read the labels of cleaning products at home and sort each into one of the seven families by its label and purpose. Any product you cannot class goes in family seven — then check whether the lesson gives you a better home for it.

## Dirt-first drill

A partner names a dirt — kitchen grease, limescale-like deposit, a scuffed floor mark, general dust. You answer with the family you would reach for and why. Four rounds, then swap. Dirt first, family second — the professional order.
