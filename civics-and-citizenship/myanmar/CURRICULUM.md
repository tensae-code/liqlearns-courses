# Civics and Citizenship — Myanmar — curriculum

One course for Myanmar inside the Civics and Citizenship
folder, built from Myanmar's own government textbooks:
Morality and Civics (စာရိတ္တနှင့်ပြည်သူ့နီတိ), the Ministry
of Education series whose every book runs the same four
learning values — Social Ethics, Rights and
Responsibilities, Discipline (and Law), Peaceful
Coexistence — across four learning areas: oneself,
others/family, community (village/neighbourhood) and the
natural environment (world). Student books and teacher
guides for Grades 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 7 and 10 are in hand, as
filed in the two placements filings for this build
(`myanmar-placements.md` and `myanmar-placements-b.md` —
see ABOUT.md for the full book list, the verdicts, and
every label carried exactly as filed). The organisation
is by **levels, not grades**: a learner's place is found
by what they prove. The path is recommended, never
forced — a learner can jump in higher; the "start here
instead" hints point down this ladder. The grades the
books come from appear only in provenance (ABOUT.md and
each lesson's `lesson/sources.md`).

The ladder follows Myanmar's own books' progression, as
the two filings' ladder sketches lay it out, reconciled
into one ladder: Grades 1–2 sit at Level 1, Grades 3–6
at Level 2 (the first filing's marks), and the second
filing's Grade 7 book sits above at Level 3 and its
Grade 10 book at Level 4 — the same four-chapter frame
deepened at every rung, in the series' own order.

## Level 1 — Character at Home and School — 35 lessons

Level 1 rests on the Grade 1 and Grade 2 books and their teacher guides: the series begins with character in the family — helping parents, honesty, trustworthiness, empathy, the pupil's duty — and moves it into school life (discipline, politeness at school, the returned purse) before any rule is named as law. Lessons: My Family · Helping Parents · Politeness at Home · Not Lying · Regret and Saying Sorry · Fulfilling Duty · Following the Code · The Child's Honesty · Polite Dealing · Trustworthiness · Empathy · Knowing Fairness · The Pupil's Duty · Loving the Country · Morning Greetings · Beautifying Our Environment · Social Ethics · Rights and Responsibilities · Discipline · Peaceful Coexistence · Respecting Parents and Teachers · The Duties of Sons and Daughters · Caring for Others · Diligence · Caring for the School · School Discipline · Politeness at School · Benefit of Honesty · Honesty Returning What Is Found · Good Friends · Empathy and Understanding · Keeping a Fair Mind · The Duty That Fits One's Age · A Peaceful World · A Clean Environment.

## Level 2 — The Four Values Made Civic — 54 lessons

Level 2 rests on the Grade 3, Grade 4 and Grade 6 books and their guides: Grade 3 adds ICT conduct, public goods and one's role and responsibility; Grade 4 opens with fairness and names our rights; Grade 6 completes the primary frame with the school council, the importance of a country's rules and laws, and peace and unity studied as subjects. Lessons: Caring for Younger ones · Polite Guest · Using Ict Properly · Trustworthy Person · Finding Joy in Fairness · Needs to Understand · Hard Work with An Objective · Contributing to Others · Good Friendship · Keeping the Rule · Good Manners in the Street · One's Role and Responsibility · Caring for Public Goods · Cherishing the Motherland · Appreciating Nature · Clean Environment · Social Ethics as a Learning Value · Discipline as a Learning Value · Right and Responsibility · Peaceful Living · The Fair-Minded Children · The Right Way · Perseverance · Working Together for All · Helping with Goodwill · Honour of Honesty · Politeness at Table · Following the Rules · Duty Wherever Placed · Our Rights · Thinking for Oneself · One Who Knows Duty and Takes Responsibility · Unity and Friendship · Harmony · Protecting the Earth · Preserving Nature · Speaking the Truth · Uprightness Programme · Rights and Responsibilities as a Learning Value · Peaceful Coexistence as a Learning Value · Good Manners at Home · Respect and Duty · Honesty and Uprightness · Peaceful Family · Introduction to Rights and Responsibilities · Rights and Responsibilities at Home · Students' Rights and Responsibilities · Our Beloved Country · School and Classroom Rules · The School Council · Importance of Rules and Law · Introduction to Peace · Valuing Peace · Advantages of Unity.

## Level 3 — The Frame at Secondary School — 15 lessons

Level 3 rests on the Grade 7 book and its guide: the pupil meets the series' four-chapter frame in its secondary form — manners and empathy, children's educational rights and duties, rule-respecting citizenship and community affairs, and living together in peace. Lessons: Being Polite at School · Honest Students · Living Uprightly · Empathy · Children's Right to Education · Fulfilling Your Duties · Being Dependable · Citizens Who Respect the Rules · Community Affairs · Asking for Help · Living in Peace · Patience and Tolerance · Helping Brings Peace · Working Together for Peace · Taking Responsibility for Oneself, Others and Society.

## Level 4 — The Frame Completed — Rights, Law and the Union — 13 lessons

Level 4 rests on the Grade 10 book and its guide: at Grade 10 the same four-chapter frame turns fully civic — human rights, the Child Rights Law, elections and taxes, and peace for the Union — the frame's most explicitly civic statement in the filings. Lessons: Social Ethics and Social Rules · Mutual Trust · Respecting Elders and People with Disabilities · Diligence for the Good of Humanity · Generosity · Human Rights · The Child Rights Law · What to Know About Elections · What to Know About Taxes · A Peaceful Social Environment · Those Who Work for Union Peace · The Union and Peace · Rights and Responsibilities — the Whole Frame.
