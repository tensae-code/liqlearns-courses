# Civics and Citizenship — Myanmar course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

Civics belongs to the country: this course is Myanmar's
own — built from Myanmar's Morality and Civics books
(Ministry of Education, Republic of the Union of
Myanmar, Burmese, with the Grade 3 book read in its
English translation and the Grade 3 and Grade 6 teacher
guides read in English, exactly as filed). Inside the
course the organisation is by **levels, not grades**:
the titles below ARE the levels' titles, in path order —
what the learner proves, step by step. The grades the
books come from live only in each lesson's sources.

The path's own logic is Myanmar's, from the two filings'
ladder sketches, reconciled into one ladder: every book
in the series runs the same four-chapter frame — Social
Ethics, Rights and Responsibilities, Discipline and Law,
Peaceful Coexistence — carried across oneself, family,
community and the natural world, so the citizen is built
in the same order at every grade, with the content
deepened each year. Grades 1–2 sit at Level 1, Grades
3–6 at Level 2, Grade 7 at Level 3 and Grade 10 at
Level 4. That ladder IS the path order (recommended,
never forced — a learner can jump in higher; the "start
here instead" hints point down this ladder).

The coined lesson names in the filings were deduped
before building (see CURRICULUM.md, "Where the build
stands"): 146 placements stock 117
lessons. Every placement stocks into exactly one
lesson, and each placement's provenance row (book +
printed page, exactly as filed) is in that lesson's
`lesson/sources.md`. Unit folders are the series' own
four learning values; each value runs through every
level, as Myanmar's own books return to the same four
chapters year by year.
