# Rights and Responsibilities as a Learning Value — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Teaching our rights held together with duty wherever one is placed.  b) it has nothing to do with how Myanmar governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Teaching our rights held together with duty wherever one is placed.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Teaching our rights held together with duty wherever one is placed.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Myanmar
**Answer: a) Teaching our rights held together with duty wherever one is placed.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
