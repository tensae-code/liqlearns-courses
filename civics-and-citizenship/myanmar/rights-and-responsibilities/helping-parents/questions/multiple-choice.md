# Helping Parents — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Helping parents and elders as the child's duty at home.  b) it has nothing to do with how Myanmar governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Helping parents and elders as the child's duty at home.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Helping parents and elders as the child's duty at home.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Myanmar
**Answer: a) Helping parents and elders as the child's duty at home.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
