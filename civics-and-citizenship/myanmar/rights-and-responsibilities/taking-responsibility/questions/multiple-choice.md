# One Who Knows Duty and Takes Responsibility — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Knowing one's duty and taking responsibility for it.  b) it has nothing to do with how Myanmar governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Knowing one's duty and taking responsibility for it.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Knowing one's duty and taking responsibility for it.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Myanmar
**Answer: a) Knowing one's duty and taking responsibility for it.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
