# Children's Right to Education — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The child's right to learn and to be educated.  b) it has nothing to do with how Myanmar governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The child's right to learn and to be educated.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) The child's right to learn and to be educated.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Myanmar
**Answer: a) The child's right to learn and to be educated.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
