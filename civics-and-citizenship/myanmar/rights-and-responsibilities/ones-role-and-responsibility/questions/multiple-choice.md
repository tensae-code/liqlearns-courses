# One's Role and Responsibility — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) One's own role and responsibility.  b) it has nothing to do with how Myanmar governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) One's own role and responsibility.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) One's own role and responsibility.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Myanmar
**Answer: a) One's own role and responsibility.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
