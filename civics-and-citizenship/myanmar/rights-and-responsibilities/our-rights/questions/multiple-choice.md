# Our Rights — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The rights we hold.  b) it has nothing to do with how Myanmar governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The rights we hold.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) The rights we hold.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Myanmar
**Answer: a) The rights we hold.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
