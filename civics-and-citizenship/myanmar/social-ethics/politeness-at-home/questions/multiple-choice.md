# Politeness at Home — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Civil, polite conduct inside the home.  b) it has nothing to do with how Myanmar governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Civil, polite conduct inside the home.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Civil, polite conduct inside the home.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Myanmar
**Answer: a) Civil, polite conduct inside the home.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
