# Speaking the Truth — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Speaking the truth.  b) it has nothing to do with how Myanmar governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Speaking the truth.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Speaking the truth.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Myanmar
**Answer: a) Speaking the truth.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
