# Respect and Duty — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Respect practised as a duty — mutual respect between parents and children and between siblings.  b) it has nothing to do with how Myanmar governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Respect practised as a duty — mutual respect between parents and children and between siblings.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Respect practised as a duty — mutual respect between parents and children and between siblings.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Myanmar
**Answer: a) Respect practised as a duty — mutual respect between parents and children and between siblings.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
