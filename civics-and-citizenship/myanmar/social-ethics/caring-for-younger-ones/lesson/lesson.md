# Caring for Younger ones

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Caring for Younger ones. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Myanmar's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Caring carefully for younger children, at home and at school, through the story of Maung Maung and Thi Thi (read first-hand).

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Caring carefully for younger children, at home and at school, through the story of Maung Maung and Thi Thi (read first-hand). Give one real example from your own life or from the Myanmar you live in, and name the lesson's key words: caring, for, younger. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
