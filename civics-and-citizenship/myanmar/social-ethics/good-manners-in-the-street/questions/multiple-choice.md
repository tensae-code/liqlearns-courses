# Good Manners in the Street — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Manners in public places.  b) it has nothing to do with how Myanmar governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Manners in public places.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Manners in public places.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Myanmar
**Answer: a) Manners in public places.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
