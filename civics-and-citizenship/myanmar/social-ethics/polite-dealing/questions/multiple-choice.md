# Polite Dealing — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Polite, warm dealing with other people.  b) it has nothing to do with how Myanmar governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Polite, warm dealing with other people.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Polite, warm dealing with other people.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Myanmar
**Answer: a) Polite, warm dealing with other people.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
