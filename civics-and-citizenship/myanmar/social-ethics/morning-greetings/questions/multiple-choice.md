Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The auspicious (မင်္ဂလာ) greeting and the good start to the day.  b) it has nothing to do with how Myanmar governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
