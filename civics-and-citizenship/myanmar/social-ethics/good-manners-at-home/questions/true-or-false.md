# Good Manners at Home — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Good manners at home — polite behaviour and polite speech in the family.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that Good Manners at Home — and that it has no connection to the rest of Myanmar's story.
