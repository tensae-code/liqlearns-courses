# Good Manners at Home — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Good manners at home — polite behaviour and polite speech in the family.  b) it has nothing to do with how Myanmar governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Good manners at home — polite behaviour and polite speech in the family.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Good manners at home — polite behaviour and polite speech in the family.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Myanmar
**Answer: a) Good manners at home — polite behaviour and polite speech in the family.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
