# Good Manners at Home

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Good Manners at Home. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Myanmar's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Good manners at home — polite behaviour and polite speech in the family.

Polite behaviours and polite verbal communication at home, applied in daily life.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Good manners at home — polite behaviour and polite speech in the family. Give one real example from your own life or from the Myanmar you live in, and name the lesson's key words: good, manners, home. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
