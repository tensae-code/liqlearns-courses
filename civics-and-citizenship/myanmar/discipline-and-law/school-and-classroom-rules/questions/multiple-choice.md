# School and Classroom Rules — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The rules of the school and of the classroom.  b) it has nothing to do with how Myanmar governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The rules of the school and of the classroom.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) The rules of the school and of the classroom.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Myanmar
**Answer: a) The rules of the school and of the classroom.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
