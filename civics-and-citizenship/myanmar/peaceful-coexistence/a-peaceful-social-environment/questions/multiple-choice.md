# A Peaceful Social Environment — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) What makes the social environment peaceful.  b) it has nothing to do with how Myanmar governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) What makes the social environment peaceful.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?

**Answer: a) What makes the social environment peaceful.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
