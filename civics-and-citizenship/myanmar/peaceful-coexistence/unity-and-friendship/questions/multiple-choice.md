# Unity and Friendship — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Living united and friendly together.  b) it has nothing to do with how Myanmar governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Living united and friendly together.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Living united and friendly together.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Myanmar
**Answer: a) Living united and friendly together.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
