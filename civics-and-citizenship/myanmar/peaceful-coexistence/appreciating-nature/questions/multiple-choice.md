# Appreciating Nature — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Appreciating the beauty of nature.  b) it has nothing to do with how Myanmar governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Appreciating the beauty of nature.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Appreciating the beauty of nature.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Myanmar
**Answer: a) Appreciating the beauty of nature.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
