# Those Who Work for Union Peace — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The people and bodies who make peace in the Union.  b) it has nothing to do with how Myanmar governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The people and bodies who make peace in the Union.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) The people and bodies who make peace in the Union.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Myanmar
**Answer: a) The people and bodies who make peace in the Union.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
