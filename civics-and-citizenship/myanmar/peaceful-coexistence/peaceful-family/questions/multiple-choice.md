# Peaceful Family — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Admirable behaviours and practising love within the family.  b) it has nothing to do with how Myanmar governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Admirable behaviours and practising love within the family.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Admirable behaviours and practising love within the family.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Myanmar
**Answer: a) Admirable behaviours and practising love within the family.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
