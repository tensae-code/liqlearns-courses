# Harmony

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Harmony. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Myanmar's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Reconciliation and harmony as a source of happiness.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Reconciliation and harmony as a source of happiness. Give one real example from your own life or from the Myanmar you live in, and name the lesson's key words: harmony. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: Reconciliation and harmony as a source of happiness.
