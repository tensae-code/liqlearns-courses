# Loving the Country — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Loving one's country.  b) it has nothing to do with how Myanmar governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Loving one's country.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Loving one's country.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Myanmar
**Answer: a) Loving one's country.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
