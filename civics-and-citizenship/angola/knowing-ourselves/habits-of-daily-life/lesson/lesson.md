# Habits of Daily Life

## Do it first

List three things you do every day without thinking. Mark each one: keep it, or improve it. Say how you would improve one of them.

## Sub lesson 1 — Our habits

A habit is what I do again and again until it feels normal — in waking, eating, working, resting and living with others.

## Sub lesson 2 — Improving my habits

I can think about my own habits, judge them, and change one poor habit into a better one, step by step.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: my daily habits shape my life — I can look at them honestly, keep the good ones and improve the poor ones. Give one real example from your own life in Angola, and name the lesson's key words: habits, daily life, improvement. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Portuguese as well as in English — Angola's own books teach Educação Moral e Cívica in Portuguese, for life at home and in the community, not only for the classroom.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: my daily habits shape my life — I can look at them honestly, keep the good ones and improve the poor ones.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
