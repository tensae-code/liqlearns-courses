# Habits of Daily Life — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** My daily habits shape my life — i can look at them honestly, keep the good ones and improve the poor ones.

**2.** What does the part "Improving my habits" teach?
**Answer:** I can think about my own habits, judge them, and change one poor habit into a better one, step by step.
