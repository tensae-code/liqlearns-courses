# Habits of Daily Life — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) my daily habits shape my life — I can look at them honestly, keep the good ones and improve the poor ones  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — Our habits?
a) A habit is what I do again and again until it feels normal — in waking, eating, working, resting and living with others.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
