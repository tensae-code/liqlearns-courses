# Video script — Habits of Daily Life

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
My daily habits shape my life — i can look at them honestly, keep the good ones and improve the poor ones.

Our habits. A habit is what I do again and again until it feels normal — in waking, eating, working, resting and living with others.

Improving my habits. I can think about my own habits, judge them, and change one poor habit into a better one, step by step.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
