# Video script — Qualities for Social Life

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A good citizen carries moral qualities into daily life — and i can judge my own conduct each day and do more at home.

The qualities of a good citizen. Qualities such as honesty, respect, responsibility and kindness are what make living together possible.

Judging my own conduct. Each day I can look at what I did, name where I fell short, and choose a better act tomorrow — including doing more at home.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
