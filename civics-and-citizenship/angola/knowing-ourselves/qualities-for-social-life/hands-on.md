# Hands-on — Qualities for Social Life

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name one quality a good citizen needs. Then judge yesterday honestly: where did you show it, and where did you miss it?

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: a good citizen carries moral qualities into daily life — and I can judge my own conduct each day and do more at home. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
