# Qualities for Social Life — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A good citizen carries moral qualities into daily life — and i can judge my own conduct each day and do more at home.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a citizen's own home, school or community in Angola.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Angola and lived there.
