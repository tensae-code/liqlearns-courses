# Qualities for Social Life

## Do it first

Name one quality a good citizen needs. Then judge yesterday honestly: where did you show it, and where did you miss it?

## Sub lesson 1 — The qualities of a good citizen

Qualities such as honesty, respect, responsibility and kindness are what make living together possible.

## Sub lesson 2 — Judging my own conduct

Each day I can look at what I did, name where I fell short, and choose a better act tomorrow — including doing more at home.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: a good citizen carries moral qualities into daily life — and I can judge my own conduct each day and do more at home. Give one real example from your own life in Angola, and name the lesson's key words: qualities, good citizen, conduct. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Portuguese as well as in English — Angola's own books teach Educação Moral e Cívica in Portuguese, for life at home and in the community, not only for the classroom.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: a good citizen carries moral qualities into daily life — and I can judge my own conduct each day and do more at home.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
