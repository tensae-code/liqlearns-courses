# Video script — Human Values

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Human values guide how we live — values, habits and beliefs together shape what a person and a community hold dear.

What human values are. Values are the things we hold to be good and worth protecting — in ourselves, in others and in our community.

Values, habits and beliefs. What we value shows in what we habitually do and what we believe; the three travel together.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
