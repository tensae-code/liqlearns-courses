# Human Values

## Do it first

Name one thing your family holds dear and would never give up. Say how it shows in what your family does each week.

## Sub lesson 1 — What human values are

Values are the things we hold to be good and worth protecting — in ourselves, in others and in our community.

## Sub lesson 2 — Values, habits and beliefs

What we value shows in what we habitually do and what we believe; the three travel together.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: human values guide how we live — values, habits and beliefs together shape what a person and a community hold dear. Give one real example from your own life in Angola, and name the lesson's key words: values, beliefs, habits. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Portuguese as well as in English — Angola's own books teach Educação Moral e Cívica in Portuguese, for life at home and in the community, not only for the classroom.

3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
