# Human Values — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Human values guide how we live — values, habits and beliefs together shape what a person and a community hold dear.

**2.** What does the part "Values, habits and beliefs" teach?
**Answer:** What we value shows in what we habitually do and what we believe; the three travel together.
