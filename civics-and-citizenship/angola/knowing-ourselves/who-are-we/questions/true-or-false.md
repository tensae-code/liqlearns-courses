# Who Are We? — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Knowing myself and knowing each other go together — 'who am i?' is answered alongside 'who are we?'.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a citizen's own home, school or community in Angola.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Angola and lived there.
