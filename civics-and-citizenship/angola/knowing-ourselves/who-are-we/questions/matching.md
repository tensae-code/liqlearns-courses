# Who Are We? — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** Who am I? — a)

**2.** Me and the others — b)


Meanings:
a) I learn who I am by looking honestly at myself — my qualities, my feelings and my acts.
b) We come to know ourselves with each other: I learn about me as I learn about the people around me.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
