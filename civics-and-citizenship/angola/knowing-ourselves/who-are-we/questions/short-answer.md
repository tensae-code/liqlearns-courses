# Who Are We? — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Knowing myself and knowing each other go together — 'who am i?' is answered alongside 'who are we?'.

**2.** What does the part "Me and the others" teach?
**Answer:** We come to know ourselves with each other: I learn about me as I learn about the people around me.
