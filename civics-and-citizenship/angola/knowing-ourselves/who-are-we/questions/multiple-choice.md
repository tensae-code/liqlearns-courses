# Who Are We? — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) knowing myself and knowing each other go together — 'who am I?' is answered alongside 'who are we?'  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — Who am I??
a) I learn who I am by looking honestly at myself — my qualities, my feelings and my acts.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
