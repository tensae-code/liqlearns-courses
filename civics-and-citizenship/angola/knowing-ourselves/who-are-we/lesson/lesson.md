# Who Are We?

## Do it first

Tell a partner two true things about yourself. Then listen and repeat back two true things about them. That exchange is this lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Who am I?

I learn who I am by looking honestly at myself — my qualities, my feelings and my acts.

## Sub lesson 2 — Me and the others

We come to know ourselves with each other: I learn about me as I learn about the people around me.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: knowing myself and knowing each other go together — 'who am I?' is answered alongside 'who are we?'. Give one real example from your own life in Angola, and name the lesson's key words: self-knowledge, each other, identity. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Portuguese as well as in English — Angola's own books teach Educação Moral e Cívica in Portuguese, for life at home and in the community, not only for the classroom.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: knowing myself and knowing each other go together — 'who am I?' is answered alongside 'who are we?'.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
