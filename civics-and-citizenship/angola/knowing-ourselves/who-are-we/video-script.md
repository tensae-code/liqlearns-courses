# Video script — Who Are We?

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Knowing myself and knowing each other go together — 'who am i?' is answered alongside 'who are we?'.

Who am I?. I learn who I am by looking honestly at myself — my qualities, my feelings and my acts.

Me and the others. We come to know ourselves with each other: I learn about me as I learn about the people around me.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
