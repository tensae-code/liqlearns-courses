# Who Am I and Who Are the Others — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Each person is alike and different at once — knowing my heritages, my family and other families is how i learn who i am and who the others are.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a citizen's own home, school or community in Angola.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Angola and lived there.
