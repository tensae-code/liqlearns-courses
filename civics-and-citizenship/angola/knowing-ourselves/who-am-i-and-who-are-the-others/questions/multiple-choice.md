# Who Am I and Who Are the Others — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) each person is alike and different at once — knowing my heritages, my family and other families is how I learn who I am and who the others are  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — Alike and different?
a) We share much with others and differ from them too; naming both is the start of knowing ourselves.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
