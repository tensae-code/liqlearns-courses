# Who Am I and Who Are the Others — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Each person is alike and different at once — knowing my heritages, my family and other families is how i learn who i am and who the others are.

**2.** What does the part "My heritages and my family" teach?
**Answer:** What I receive from my family and my past is part of who I am, and other families hand down their own heritages.
