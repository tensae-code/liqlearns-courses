# Who Am I and Who Are the Others — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** Alike and different — a)

**2.** My heritages and my family — b)


Meanings:
a) We share much with others and differ from them too; naming both is the start of knowing ourselves.
b) What I receive from my family and my past is part of who I am, and other families hand down their own heritages.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
