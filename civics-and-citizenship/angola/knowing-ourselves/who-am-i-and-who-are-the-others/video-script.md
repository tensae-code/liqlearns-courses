# Video script — Who Am I and Who Are the Others

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Each person is alike and different at once — knowing my heritages, my family and other families is how i learn who i am and who the others are.

Alike and different. We share much with others and differ from them too; naming both is the start of knowing ourselves.

My heritages and my family. What I receive from my family and my past is part of who I am, and other families hand down their own heritages.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
