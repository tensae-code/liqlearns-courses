# Puberty and Growing

## Do it first

Name two changes of puberty and say for each whether it happens to boys, girls, or both. Name one trusted adult you could ask about growing.

## Sub lesson 1 — What puberty is

Puberty is the stage when a child's body changes toward adulthood, each person at their own pace.

## Sub lesson 2 — Growing well through it

The changes — including menstruation and sperm — are natural and complex; sport, cleanliness and trusted adults help.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: puberty is the body's growing into adulthood — changes such as menstruation and sperm production are natural, and sport and care help a young person through them. Give one real example from your own life in Angola, and name the lesson's key words: puberty, growing, body. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Portuguese as well as in English — Angola's own books teach Educação Moral e Cívica in Portuguese, for life at home and in the community, not only for the classroom.
