# Puberty and Growing — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Puberty is the body's growing into adulthood — changes such as menstruation and sperm production are natural, and sport and care help a young person through them.

**2.** What does the part "Growing well through it" teach?
**Answer:** The changes — including menstruation and sperm — are natural and complex; sport, cleanliness and trusted adults help.
