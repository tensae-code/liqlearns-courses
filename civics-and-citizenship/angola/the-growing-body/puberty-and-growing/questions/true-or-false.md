# Puberty and Growing — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Puberty is the body's growing into adulthood — changes such as menstruation and sperm production are natural, and sport and care help a young person through them.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a citizen's own home, school or community in Angola.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Angola and lived there.
