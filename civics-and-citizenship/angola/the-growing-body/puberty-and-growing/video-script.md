# Video script — Puberty and Growing

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Puberty is the body's growing into adulthood — changes such as menstruation and sperm production are natural, and sport and care help a young person through them.

What puberty is. Puberty is the stage when a child's body changes toward adulthood, each person at their own pace.

Growing well through it. The changes — including menstruation and sperm — are natural and complex; sport, cleanliness and trusted adults help.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
