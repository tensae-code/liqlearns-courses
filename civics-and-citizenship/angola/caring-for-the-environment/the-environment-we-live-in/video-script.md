# Video script — The Environment We Live In

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The environment is humanity's heritage — water is a precious good, and an ecological mind keeps our land clean for everyone.

The environment as heritage. The natural environment belongs to all humanity, held in trust for those who come after us.

Water, ecology and clean land. Ecology teaches how living things hang together; water is precious, and cleaning our land is everyone's work.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
