# The Environment We Live In

## Do it first

Name one precious thing in the environment near your home. Say one act that wastes it and one act that protects it.

## Sub lesson 1 — The environment as heritage

The natural environment belongs to all humanity, held in trust for those who come after us.

## Sub lesson 2 — Water, ecology and clean land

Ecology teaches how living things hang together; water is precious, and cleaning our land is everyone's work.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the environment is humanity's heritage — water is a precious good, and an ecological mind keeps our land clean for everyone. Give one real example from your own life in Angola, and name the lesson's key words: environment, water, ecology, heritage. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Portuguese as well as in English — Angola's own books teach Educação Moral e Cívica in Portuguese, for life at home and in the community, not only for the classroom.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: the environment is humanity's heritage — water is a precious good, and an ecological mind keeps our land clean for everyone.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
