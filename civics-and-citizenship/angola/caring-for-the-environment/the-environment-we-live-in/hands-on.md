# Hands-on — The Environment We Live In

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name one precious thing in the environment near your home. Say one act that wastes it and one act that protects it.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the environment is humanity's heritage — water is a precious good, and an ecological mind keeps our land clean for everyone. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
