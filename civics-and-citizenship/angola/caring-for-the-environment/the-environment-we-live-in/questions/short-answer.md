# The Environment We Live In — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** The environment is humanity's heritage — water is a precious good, and an ecological mind keeps our land clean for everyone.

**2.** What does the part "Water, ecology and clean land" teach?
**Answer:** Ecology teaches how living things hang together; water is precious, and cleaning our land is everyone's work.
