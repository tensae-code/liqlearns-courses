# The Environment We Live In — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) the environment is humanity's heritage — water is a precious good, and an ecological mind keeps our land clean for everyone  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — The environment as heritage?
a) The natural environment belongs to all humanity, held in trust for those who come after us.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
