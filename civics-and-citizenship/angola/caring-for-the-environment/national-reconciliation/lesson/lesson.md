# National Reconciliation

## Do it first

Say what reconciliation means in your own words. Then name one small act at school or home that builds peace instead of division.

## Sub lesson 1 — Reconciliation in Angola

After division and conflict, reconciliation means Angolans choosing to live as one people again.

## Sub lesson 2 — A better Angola, a better world

Building a better Angola to live in — in peace and fairness — is Angola's part in building a better world.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: national reconciliation heals a country — Angolans building a better Angola to live in are also building a better world. Give one real example from your own life in Angola, and name the lesson's key words: reconciliation, peace, Angola. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Portuguese as well as in English — Angola's own books teach Educação Moral e Cívica in Portuguese, for life at home and in the community, not only for the classroom.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: national reconciliation heals a country — Angolans building a better Angola to live in are also building a better world.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
