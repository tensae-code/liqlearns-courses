# Hands-on — National Reconciliation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Say what reconciliation means in your own words. Then name one small act at school or home that builds peace instead of division.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: national reconciliation heals a country — Angolans building a better Angola to live in are also building a better world. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
