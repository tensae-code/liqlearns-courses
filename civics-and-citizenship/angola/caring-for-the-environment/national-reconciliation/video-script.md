# Video script — National Reconciliation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
National reconciliation heals a country — angolans building a better angola to live in are also building a better world.

Reconciliation in Angola. After division and conflict, reconciliation means Angolans choosing to live as one people again.

A better Angola, a better world. Building a better Angola to live in — in peace and fairness — is Angola's part in building a better world.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
