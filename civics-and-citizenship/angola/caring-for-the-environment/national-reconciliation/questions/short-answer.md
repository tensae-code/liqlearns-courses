# National Reconciliation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** National reconciliation heals a country — angolans building a better angola to live in are also building a better world.

**2.** What does the part "A better Angola, a better world" teach?
**Answer:** Building a better Angola to live in — in peace and fairness — is Angola's part in building a better world.
