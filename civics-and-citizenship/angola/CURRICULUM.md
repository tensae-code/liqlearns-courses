# Civics and Citizenship — Angola course — CURRICULUM (the path)

One course for Angola inside the Civics and Citizenship folder,
organised by **levels, not grades**: a learner climbs when they
have proved the level, not when a school calendar says so. The
school grades the books come from (Grades 5–6) appear only in
each lesson's sources (provenance), never as the course's
organisation. The path follows Angola's own books' order: the
5ª Classe book opens the course — self and others, family rights
and children's rights, democratic living-together, health and
road safety, school as social progress, Angola's languages,
civil registration as identity, and national reconciliation —
and the 6ª Classe book deepens every circle: self and mutual
knowledge, cultural identity, the human person with rights and
duties under the Angolan Constitution, democracy built through
dialogue and tolerance, the growing body and health, just
relations with others, and care for the environment.

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in this
course comes from government textbooks — this country's own
Educação Moral e Cívica books — except the **Tip boxes**: a tip
in a box is from us, the people making LiqLearns. Tips are ours;
the lessons are the books'. See ABOUT.md for the full book list
and the acceptance reckoning.

## The levels (the path, in order)

1. **Knowing Ourselves** — who I am and who the others are, our
   daily habits, the moral qualities of the good citizen, human
   values; deepened by self and mutual knowledge. 5 lessons, in
   the title `knowing-ourselves/`.
2. **Identity and Cultural Diversity** — my country, my cultural
   and linguistic identity and the many languages of Angola;
   then the origins of our culture and our ancestors' customs,
   values and beliefs. 3 lessons, in the title
   `identity-and-cultural-diversity/`.
3. **The Human Person** — families' rights, children's rights,
   registration as the right to a name, nationality and
   citizenship; then the human person free and responsible —
   rights and duties under the Angolan Constitution, emotions,
   freedom and responsibility. 6 lessons, in the title
   `the-human-person/`.
4. **Democracy in My Country** — the rules of living together in
   a democratic society; then writing our own class rules,
   building democracy through dialogue, democratic habits and
   values, and tolerance. 5 lessons, in the title
   `democracy-in-my-country/`.
5. **Growing and Health** — diseases and collective health,
   self-care and the dangers around us; road traffic and safety;
   then puberty and growing, growing with health and HIV/AIDS
   prevention. 4 lessons, in the titles `the-growing-body/` (1),
   `growing-with-health/` (2) and `living-with-others/` (1 —
   road traffic, filed at this level).
6. **Living With Others** — school as a source of social
   progress; then being a social being, respect given and
   received, and justice versus injustice. 3 lessons, in the
   title `living-with-others/`.
7. **Caring for the Environment and Reconciliation** —
   environmental education: the environment we live in, water as
   a precious good, cleaning our land; and, as the 5ª book's own
   closing theme, national reconciliation and building a better
   Angola and a better world. 2 lessons, in the title
   `caring-for-the-environment/`.

**Totals: 28 lessons** (Level 1: 5 · Level 2: 3 · Level 3: 6 ·
Level 4: 5 · Level 5: 4 · Level 6: 3 · Level 7: 2 — all from
two student books, both COMPLETE; see ABOUT.md).
