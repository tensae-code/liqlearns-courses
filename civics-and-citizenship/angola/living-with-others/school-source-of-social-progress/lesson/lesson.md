# School: Source of Social Progress

## Do it first

Name one thing your school gives the whole neighbourhood, not just pupils. Then name one problem in your school environment and one fix pupils can help with.

## Sub lesson 1 — Education as progress

What a school teaches lifts not only the pupil but the family, the community and the country.

## Sub lesson 2 — Caring for the school

The school environment is shared: caring for it, and naming its problems honestly, is part of learning.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: school is a source of social progress — education and instruction move a whole society forward, so we care for the school environment and face its problems. Give one real example from your own life in Angola, and name the lesson's key words: school, progress, education. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Portuguese as well as in English — Angola's own books teach Educação Moral e Cívica in Portuguese, for life at home and in the community, not only for the classroom.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: school is a source of social progress — education and instruction move a whole society forward, so we care for the school environment and face its problems.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
