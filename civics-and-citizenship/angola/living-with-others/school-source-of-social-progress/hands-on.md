# Hands-on — School: Source of Social Progress

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name one thing your school gives the whole neighbourhood, not just pupils. Then name one problem in your school environment and one fix pupils can help with.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: school is a source of social progress — education and instruction move a whole society forward, so we care for the school environment and face its problems. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
