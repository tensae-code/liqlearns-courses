# Video script — School: Source of Social Progress

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
School is a source of social progress — education and instruction move a whole society forward, so we care for the school environment and face its problems.

Education as progress. What a school teaches lifts not only the pupil but the family, the community and the country.

Caring for the school. The school environment is shared: caring for it, and naming its problems honestly, is part of learning.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
