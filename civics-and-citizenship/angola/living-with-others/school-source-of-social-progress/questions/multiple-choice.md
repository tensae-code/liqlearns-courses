# School: Source of Social Progress — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) school is a source of social progress — education and instruction move a whole society forward, so we care for the school environment and face its problems  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — Education as progress?
a) What a school teaches lifts not only the pupil but the family, the community and the country.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
