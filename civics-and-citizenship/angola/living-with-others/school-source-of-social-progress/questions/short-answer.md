# School: Source of Social Progress — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** School is a source of social progress — education and instruction move a whole society forward, so we care for the school environment and face its problems.

**2.** What does the part "Caring for the school" teach?
**Answer:** The school environment is shared: caring for it, and naming its problems honestly, is part of learning.
