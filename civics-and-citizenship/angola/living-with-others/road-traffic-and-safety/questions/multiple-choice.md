# Road Traffic and Safety — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) the road has its elements — pedestrians, vehicles and traffic signs — and pedestrians and drivers each hold rights and duties there  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — The elements of traffic?
a) Traffic is made of pedestrians, vehicles and signs; each sign speaks to everyone on the road.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
