# Road Traffic and Safety — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** The road has its elements — pedestrians, vehicles and traffic signs — and pedestrians and drivers each hold rights and duties there.

**2.** What does the part "Rights and duties on the road" teach?
**Answer:** Pedestrian and driver, motorist, motorcyclist and cyclist — each has rights on the road and duties that keep it safe.
