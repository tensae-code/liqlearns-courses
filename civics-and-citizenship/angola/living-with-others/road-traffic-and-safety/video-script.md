# Video script — Road Traffic and Safety

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The road has its elements — pedestrians, vehicles and traffic signs — and pedestrians and drivers each hold rights and duties there.

The elements of traffic. Traffic is made of pedestrians, vehicles and signs; each sign speaks to everyone on the road.

Rights and duties on the road. Pedestrian and driver, motorist, motorcyclist and cyclist — each has rights on the road and duties that keep it safe.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
