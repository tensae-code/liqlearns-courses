# Hands-on — Road Traffic and Safety

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Walk (in your mind) from home to school. Name every traffic sign or danger you pass, and say what a pedestrian must do at each.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the road has its elements — pedestrians, vehicles and traffic signs — and pedestrians and drivers each hold rights and duties there. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
