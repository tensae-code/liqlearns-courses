# I Am a Social Being

## Do it first

Name one person you will meet today. Say one respectful act you will do toward them, and how you hope respect comes back.

## Sub lesson 1 — My behaviour toward others

No one lives alone: what I do reaches others, and what they do reaches me.

## Sub lesson 2 — Respecting and being respected

Social feelings grow where respect runs both ways — I respect others, and I am respected in return.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: I am a social being — my behaviour toward others, respect given and received, is how social life is built. Give one real example from your own life in Angola, and name the lesson's key words: social being, respect, others. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Portuguese as well as in English — Angola's own books teach Educação Moral e Cívica in Portuguese, for life at home and in the community, not only for the classroom.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: I am a social being — my behaviour toward others, respect given and received, is how social life is built.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
