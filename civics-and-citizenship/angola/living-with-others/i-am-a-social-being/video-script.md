# Video script — I Am a Social Being

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
I am a social being — my behaviour toward others, respect given and received, is how social life is built.

My behaviour toward others. No one lives alone: what I do reaches others, and what they do reaches me.

Respecting and being respected. Social feelings grow where respect runs both ways — I respect others, and I am respected in return.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
