# I Am a Social Being — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) I am a social being — my behaviour toward others, respect given and received, is how social life is built  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — My behaviour toward others?
a) No one lives alone: what I do reaches others, and what they do reaches me.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
