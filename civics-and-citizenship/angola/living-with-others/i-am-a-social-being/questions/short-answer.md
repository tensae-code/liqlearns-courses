# I Am a Social Being — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** I am a social being — my behaviour toward others, respect given and received, is how social life is built.

**2.** What does the part "Respecting and being respected" teach?
**Answer:** Social feelings grow where respect runs both ways — I respect others, and I am respected in return.
