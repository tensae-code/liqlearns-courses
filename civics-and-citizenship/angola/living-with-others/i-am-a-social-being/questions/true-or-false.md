# I Am a Social Being — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** I am a social being — my behaviour toward others, respect given and received, is how social life is built.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a citizen's own home, school or community in Angola.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Angola and lived there.
