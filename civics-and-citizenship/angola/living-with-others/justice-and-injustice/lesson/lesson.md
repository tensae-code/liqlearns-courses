# Justice and Injustice

## Do it first

Give one example of a just act and one of an unjust act at school. Say what makes each one just or unjust.

## Sub lesson 1 — Telling just from unjust

A just act treats a person fairly; an unjust act takes from them what is theirs by right.

## Sub lesson 2 — Living together justly

Communities hold together when people treat each other justly in ordinary dealings, every day.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: just acts and unjust acts can be told apart — living together well means treating others justly and naming injustice when it appears. Give one real example from your own life in Angola, and name the lesson's key words: justice, injustice, fairness. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Portuguese as well as in English — Angola's own books teach Educação Moral e Cívica in Portuguese, for life at home and in the community, not only for the classroom.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: just acts and unjust acts can be told apart — living together well means treating others justly and naming injustice when it appears.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
