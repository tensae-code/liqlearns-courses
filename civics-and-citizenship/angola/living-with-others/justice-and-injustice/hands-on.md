# Hands-on — Justice and Injustice

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Give one example of a just act and one of an unjust act at school. Say what makes each one just or unjust.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: just acts and unjust acts can be told apart — living together well means treating others justly and naming injustice when it appears. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
