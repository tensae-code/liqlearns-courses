# Justice and Injustice — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) just acts and unjust acts can be told apart — living together well means treating others justly and naming injustice when it appears  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — Telling just from unjust?
a) A just act treats a person fairly; an unjust act takes from them what is theirs by right.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
