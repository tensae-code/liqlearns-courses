# Justice and Injustice — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Just acts and unjust acts can be told apart — living together well means treating others justly and naming injustice when it appears.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a citizen's own home, school or community in Angola.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Angola and lived there.
