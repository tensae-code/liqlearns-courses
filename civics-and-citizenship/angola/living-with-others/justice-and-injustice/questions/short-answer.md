# Justice and Injustice — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Just acts and unjust acts can be told apart — living together well means treating others justly and naming injustice when it appears.

**2.** What does the part "Living together justly" teach?
**Answer:** Communities hold together when people treat each other justly in ordinary dealings, every day.
