# Video script — Justice and Injustice

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Just acts and unjust acts can be told apart — living together well means treating others justly and naming injustice when it appears.

Telling just from unjust. A just act treats a person fairly; an unjust act takes from them what is theirs by right.

Living together justly. Communities hold together when people treat each other justly in ordinary dealings, every day.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
