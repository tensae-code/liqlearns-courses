# Video script — Tolerance Makes a Good Democrat

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Tolerance is a democratic virtue — when i am tolerant of people who differ from me, i can be a good democrat.

Tolerance and intolerance. Tolerance lets different people live together in peace; intolerance pushes others out and harms everyone.

Tolerance as a virtue. Putting up with difference calmly — and respecting it — is part of what makes a good democrat.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
