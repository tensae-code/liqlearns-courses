# Tolerance Makes a Good Democrat — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Tolerance is a democratic virtue — when i am tolerant of people who differ from me, i can be a good democrat.

**2.** What does the part "Tolerance as a virtue" teach?
**Answer:** Putting up with difference calmly — and respecting it — is part of what makes a good democrat.
