# Tolerance Makes a Good Democrat — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Tolerance is a democratic virtue — when i am tolerant of people who differ from me, i can be a good democrat.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a citizen's own home, school or community in Angola.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Angola and lived there.
