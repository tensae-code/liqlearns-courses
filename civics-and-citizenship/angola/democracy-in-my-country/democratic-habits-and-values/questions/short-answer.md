# Democratic Habits and Values — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Democracy lives in habits and values practised daily — respect, fairness, listening and keeping shared rules.

**2.** What does the part "Democratic habits" teach?
**Answer:** Values become real as habits: listening, waiting your turn, accepting an agreed decision.
