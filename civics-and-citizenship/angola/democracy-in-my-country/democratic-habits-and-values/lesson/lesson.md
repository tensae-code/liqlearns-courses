# Democratic Habits and Values

## Do it first

Name one democratic value and one daily habit that shows it. Watch for that habit today and note where you saw it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Democratic values

Values such as respect for others, fairness and equal worth hold a democracy up.

## Sub lesson 2 — Democratic habits

Values become real as habits: listening, waiting your turn, accepting an agreed decision.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: democracy lives in habits and values practised daily — respect, fairness, listening and keeping shared rules. Give one real example from your own life in Angola, and name the lesson's key words: values, habits, democracy. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Portuguese as well as in English — Angola's own books teach Educação Moral e Cívica in Portuguese, for life at home and in the community, not only for the classroom.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: democracy lives in habits and values practised daily — respect, fairness, listening and keeping shared rules.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
