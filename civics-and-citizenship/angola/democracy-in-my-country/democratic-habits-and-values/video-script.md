# Video script — Democratic Habits and Values

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Democracy lives in habits and values practised daily — respect, fairness, listening and keeping shared rules.

Democratic values. Values such as respect for others, fairness and equal worth hold a democracy up.

Democratic habits. Values become real as habits: listening, waiting your turn, accepting an agreed decision.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
