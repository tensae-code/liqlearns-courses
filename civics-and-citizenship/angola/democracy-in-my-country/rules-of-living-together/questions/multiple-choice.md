# Rules of Living Together — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) a democratic society lives by shared rules of living together — rules that protect everyone and bind everyone equally  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — Rules for living together?
a) Living together needs agreed rules so that each person's rights and safety are protected.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
