# Rules of Living Together — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** A democratic society lives by shared rules of living together — rules that protect everyone and bind everyone equally.

**2.** What does the part "Rules in a democratic society" teach?
**Answer:** In a democracy the rules are made to serve everyone and apply to everyone, not only to the weak.
