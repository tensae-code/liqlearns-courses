# Rules of Living Together

## Do it first

Name one rule that helps your class or street live together well. Say who it protects and what would happen without it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Rules for living together

Living together needs agreed rules so that each person's rights and safety are protected.

## Sub lesson 2 — Rules in a democratic society

In a democracy the rules are made to serve everyone and apply to everyone, not only to the weak.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: a democratic society lives by shared rules of living together — rules that protect everyone and bind everyone equally. Give one real example from your own life in Angola, and name the lesson's key words: rules, living together, democracy. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Portuguese as well as in English — Angola's own books teach Educação Moral e Cívica in Portuguese, for life at home and in the community, not only for the classroom.

2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: a democratic society lives by shared rules of living together — rules that protect everyone and bind everyone equally.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
