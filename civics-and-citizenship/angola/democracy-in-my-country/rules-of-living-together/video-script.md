# Video script — Rules of Living Together

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A democratic society lives by shared rules of living together — rules that protect everyone and bind everyone equally.

Rules for living together. Living together needs agreed rules so that each person's rights and safety are protected.

Rules in a democratic society. In a democracy the rules are made to serve everyone and apply to everyone, not only to the weak.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
