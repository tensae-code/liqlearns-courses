# Video script — Building Democracy Through Dialogue

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

This lesson is about one thing.
I help build democracy in my country — and dialogue, truly listening and speaking together, is one of its main tools.

Building democracy in Angola. Democracy is built by ordinary citizens in ordinary places, not only by leaders in the capital.

Dialogue: meaning and value. Dialogue means speaking and listening to understand; it settles differences without force.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
