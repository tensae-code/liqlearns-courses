# Hands-on — We Write Our Class Rules

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Propose one rule for your class. Say why it is fair, who it protects, and how the class should agree it.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: democracy can be practised in the classroom — pupils can draft their own class rules together and live by what they agreed. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
