# We Write Our Class Rules — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Democracy can be practised in the classroom — pupils can draft their own class rules together and live by what they agreed.

**2.** What does the part "Living by what we agreed" teach?
**Answer:** Rules we made ourselves are rules we understand — and keeping them is democracy practised daily.
