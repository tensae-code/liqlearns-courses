# We Write Our Class Rules — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) democracy can be practised in the classroom — pupils can draft their own class rules together and live by what they agreed  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — Drafting our own rules?
a) A class can write its own regulation: proposing, discussing and agreeing the rules together.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
