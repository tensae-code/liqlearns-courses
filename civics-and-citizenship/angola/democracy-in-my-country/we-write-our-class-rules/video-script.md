# Video script — We Write Our Class Rules

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Democracy can be practised in the classroom — pupils can draft their own class rules together and live by what they agreed.

Drafting our own rules. A class can write its own regulation: proposing, discussing and agreeing the rules together.

Living by what we agreed. Rules we made ourselves are rules we understand — and keeping them is democracy practised daily.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
