# We Write Our Class Rules

## Do it first

Propose one rule for your class. Say why it is fair, who it protects, and how the class should agree it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Drafting our own rules

A class can write its own regulation: proposing, discussing and agreeing the rules together.

## Sub lesson 2 — Living by what we agreed

Rules we made ourselves are rules we understand — and keeping them is democracy practised daily.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: democracy can be practised in the classroom — pupils can draft their own class rules together and live by what they agreed. Give one real example from your own life in Angola, and name the lesson's key words: class rules, participation, democracy. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Portuguese as well as in English — Angola's own books teach Educação Moral e Cívica in Portuguese, for life at home and in the community, not only for the classroom.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: democracy can be practised in the classroom — pupils can draft their own class rules together and live by what they agreed.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
