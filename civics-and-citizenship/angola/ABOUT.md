# About the Civics and Citizenship — Angola course

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in this
course comes from government textbooks — this country's own
Educação Moral e Cívica books (Manuais da 5.ª e 6.ª Classe,
Ministério da Educação). The one exception is the **Tip
boxes**: a tip in a box is from us, the people making
LiqLearns. Tips are ours; the lessons are the books'.

## How this course is organised

This is ONE course for Angola inside the Civics and
Citizenship folder (one course per country). Inside it, the
organisation is by **levels, not grades**: a learner's place is
found by what they prove, and each level stands on the ones
before it as a recommended-first path — never a forced gate.
The school grades the books come from (Grades 5–6) appear only
in each lesson's `lesson/sources.md`, as provenance.

The course has **seven levels**, which are the two books' own
order — the 5ª Classe book opens the course and the 6ª Classe
book deepens every circle:

1. **Level 1 — Knowing Ourselves** (5ª opens; 6ª deepens into
   self and mutual knowledge). 5 lessons, in the title
   `knowing-ourselves/`.
2. **Level 2 — Identity and Cultural Diversity** (5ª: my
   country, my cultural and linguistic identity; 6ª: origins of
   our culture, ancestors' customs, values and beliefs).
   3 lessons, in the title `identity-and-cultural-diversity/`.
3. **Level 3 — The Human Person** (5ª: families' rights,
   children's rights, registration as identity; 6ª: rights and
   duties under the Angolan Constitution, emotions, freedom and
   responsibility). 6 lessons, in the title `the-human-person/`.
4. **Level 4 — Democracy in My Country** (5ª: rules of living
   together; 6ª: class rules, dialogue, democratic habits and
   values, tolerance). 5 lessons, in the title
   `democracy-in-my-country/`.
5. **Level 5 — Growing and Health** (5ª: diseases and
   collective health, road traffic and safety; 6ª: puberty,
   health and HIV/AIDS prevention). 4 lessons, in the titles
   `the-growing-body/`, `growing-with-health/` and
   `living-with-others/` (road traffic, filed at this level).
6. **Level 6 — Living With Others** (5ª: school as social
   progress; 6ª: the social being, respect, justice versus
   injustice). 3 lessons, in the title `living-with-others/`.
7. **Level 7 — Caring for the Environment and
   Reconciliation** (6ª: environmental education; 5ª's own
   closing theme: national reconciliation). 2 lessons, in the
   title `caring-for-the-environment/`.

**Angola's own way:** Moral e Cívica is taught as Temas that
start from self-knowledge and Angolan cultural identity, move
to the human person with rights and duties under the Angolan
Constitution and to building democracy through dialogue and
tolerance, then to the growing body, health, just relations
with others, and care for the environment. The 5ª book adds its
own closing theme: national reconciliation in Angola.

**Course-intro video script** (written here, made later; the
books' own shape, walked in order):

- *From knowing yourself to reconciling a country:* "Start with
  who you are and who the others are. Learn your culture and
  Angola's many languages. Stand as a human person — with
  rights, duties, a name and a nationality. Practise democracy:
  rules we write together, dialogue, tolerance. Grow and stay
  healthy. Live justly with others. Care for the land — and
  reconcile, building a better Angola. That is Angola's
  Educação Moral e Cívica."
