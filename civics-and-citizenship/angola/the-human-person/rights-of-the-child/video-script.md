# Video script — Rights of the Child

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Every child has rights — to care, protection, schooling and a name — simply because the child is a child.

The rights of the child. A child holds rights from birth: to be cared for, protected, taught and heard.

Rights in daily life. Those rights are real in ordinary places — at home, at school and in the street — not only in books.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
