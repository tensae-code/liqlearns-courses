# Rights of the Child — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Every child has rights — to care, protection, schooling and a name — simply because the child is a child.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a citizen's own home, school or community in Angola.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Angola and lived there.
