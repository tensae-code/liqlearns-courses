# Rights of the Child — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Every child has rights — to care, protection, schooling and a name — simply because the child is a child.

**2.** What does the part "Rights in daily life" teach?
**Answer:** Those rights are real in ordinary places — at home, at school and in the street — not only in books.
