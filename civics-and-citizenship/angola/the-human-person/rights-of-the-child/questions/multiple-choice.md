# Rights of the Child — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) every child has rights — to care, protection, schooling and a name — simply because the child is a child  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — The rights of the child?
a) A child holds rights from birth: to be cared for, protected, taught and heard.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
