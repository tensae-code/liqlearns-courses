# Video script — Emotions

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Emotions are part of being human — naming my feelings and affective states helps me understand myself and live with others.

What emotions are. Feelings such as joy, anger, fear and sadness come to every person; they are signals, not orders.

My affective states. Noticing and naming how I feel is the first step to handling it well with others.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
