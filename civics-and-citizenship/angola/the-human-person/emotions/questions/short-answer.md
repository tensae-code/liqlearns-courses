# Emotions — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Emotions are part of being human — naming my feelings and affective states helps me understand myself and live with others.

**2.** What does the part "My affective states" teach?
**Answer:** Noticing and naming how I feel is the first step to handling it well with others.
