# Emotions — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) emotions are part of being human — naming my feelings and affective states helps me understand myself and live with others  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — What emotions are?
a) Feelings such as joy, anger, fear and sadness come to every person; they are signals, not orders.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
