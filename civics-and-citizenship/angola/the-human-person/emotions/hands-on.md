# Hands-on — Emotions

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name how you feel right now in one word. Then say what happened that brought that feeling, and what you will do with it.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: emotions are part of being human — naming my feelings and affective states helps me understand myself and live with others. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
