# Emotions

## Do it first

Name how you feel right now in one word. Then say what happened that brought that feeling, and what you will do with it.

## Sub lesson 1 — What emotions are

Feelings such as joy, anger, fear and sadness come to every person; they are signals, not orders.

## Sub lesson 2 — My affective states

Noticing and naming how I feel is the first step to handling it well with others.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: emotions are part of being human — naming my feelings and affective states helps me understand myself and live with others. Give one real example from your own life in Angola, and name the lesson's key words: emotions, feelings, self. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Portuguese as well as in English — Angola's own books teach Educação Moral e Cívica in Portuguese, for life at home and in the community, not only for the classroom.

## Do It Yourself

3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
