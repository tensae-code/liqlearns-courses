# Video script — Registration: Name, Nationality and Citizenship

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Civil registration is an element of identity — every child has the right to a name and a nationality, and registration makes citizenship real on paper.

The right to a name and nationality. Every child has the right to a name and to a nationality from birth.

Why registration matters. Registering a birth records who the child is; without it, the child's identity and citizenship are hard to prove.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
