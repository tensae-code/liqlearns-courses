# Registration: Name, Nationality and Citizenship — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) civil registration is an element of identity — every child has the right to a name and a nationality, and registration makes citizenship real on paper  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — The right to a name and nationality?
a) Every child has the right to a name and to a nationality from birth.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
