# Registration: Name, Nationality and Citizenship — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Civil registration is an element of identity — every child has the right to a name and a nationality, and registration makes citizenship real on paper.

**2.** What does the part "Why registration matters" teach?
**Answer:** Registering a birth records who the child is; without it, the child's identity and citizenship are hard to prove.
