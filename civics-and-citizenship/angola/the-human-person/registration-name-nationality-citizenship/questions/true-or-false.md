# Registration: Name, Nationality and Citizenship — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Civil registration is an element of identity — every child has the right to a name and a nationality, and registration makes citizenship real on paper.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a citizen's own home, school or community in Angola.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Angola and lived there.
