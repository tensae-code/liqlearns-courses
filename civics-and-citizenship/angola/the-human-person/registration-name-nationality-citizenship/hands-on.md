# Hands-on — Registration: Name, Nationality and Citizenship

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Say your full name aloud. Then say: what paper proves who you are, and where would a family go to register a new baby?

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: civil registration is an element of identity — every child has the right to a name and a nationality, and registration makes citizenship real on paper. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
