# Registration: Name, Nationality and Citizenship

## Do it first

Say your full name aloud. Then say: what paper proves who you are, and where would a family go to register a new baby?

## Sub lesson 1 — The right to a name and nationality

Every child has the right to a name and to a nationality from birth.

## Sub lesson 2 — Why registration matters

Registering a birth records who the child is; without it, the child's identity and citizenship are hard to prove.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: civil registration is an element of identity — every child has the right to a name and a nationality, and registration makes citizenship real on paper. Give one real example from your own life in Angola, and name the lesson's key words: registration, name, nationality, citizenship. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Portuguese as well as in English — Angola's own books teach Educação Moral e Cívica in Portuguese, for life at home and in the community, not only for the classroom.
