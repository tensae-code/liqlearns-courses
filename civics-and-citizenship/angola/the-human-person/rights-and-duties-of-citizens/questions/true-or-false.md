# Rights and Duties of Citizens — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A human being holds rights and duties together — the angolan constitution guarantees rights to angolans, and my acts as a social being carry duties alongside them.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a citizen's own home, school or community in Angola.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Angola and lived there.
