# Rights and Duties of Citizens — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** A human being holds rights and duties together — the angolan constitution guarantees rights to angolans, and my acts as a social being carry duties alongside them.

**2.** What does the part "Duties alongside rights" teach?
**Answer:** Rights come with duties: what I do affects others, so I act as a social being, responsible for my acts.
