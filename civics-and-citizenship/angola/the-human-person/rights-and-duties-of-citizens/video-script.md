# Video script — Rights and Duties of Citizens

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A human being holds rights and duties together — the angolan constitution guarantees rights to angolans, and my acts as a social being carry duties alongside them.

Rights guaranteed. Angolans hold rights guaranteed by their Constitution — to live, learn, speak and be treated with dignity.

Duties alongside rights. Rights come with duties: what I do affects others, so I act as a social being, responsible for my acts.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
