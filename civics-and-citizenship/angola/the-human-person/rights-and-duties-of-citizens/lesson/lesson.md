# Rights and Duties of Citizens

## Do it first

Name one right you hold and one duty that goes with it. Say what happens to others if you keep the right but drop the duty.

## Sub lesson 1 — Rights guaranteed

Angolans hold rights guaranteed by their Constitution — to live, learn, speak and be treated with dignity.

## Sub lesson 2 — Duties alongside rights

Rights come with duties: what I do affects others, so I act as a social being, responsible for my acts.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: a human being holds rights and duties together — the Angolan Constitution guarantees rights to Angolans, and my acts as a social being carry duties alongside them. Give one real example from your own life in Angola, and name the lesson's key words: rights, duties, Constitution, citizen. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Portuguese as well as in English — Angola's own books teach Educação Moral e Cívica in Portuguese, for life at home and in the community, not only for the classroom.
