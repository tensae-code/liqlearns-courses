# Rights of Families — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Families have rights and needs — a family's quality of life, its habits and its place in angola matter and must be respected.

**2.** What does the part "The family in Angola" teach?
**Answer:** Angolan families, with their own habits and ways, are the first home of the citizen and deserve protection.
