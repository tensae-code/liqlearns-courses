# Rights of Families — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) families have rights and needs — a family's quality of life, its habits and its place in Angola matter and must be respected  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — The family and its needs?
a) Every family needs what lets it live well — care, shelter, food, schooling and respect.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
