# Rights of Families — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Families have rights and needs — a family's quality of life, its habits and its place in angola matter and must be respected.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a citizen's own home, school or community in Angola.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Angola and lived there.
