# Hands-on — Rights of Families

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
List three things your family needs to live well. Say which are met easily and which are hard, and who should help.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: families have rights and needs — a family's quality of life, its habits and its place in Angola matter and must be respected. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
