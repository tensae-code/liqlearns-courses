# Rights of Families

## Do it first

List three things your family needs to live well. Say which are met easily and which are hard, and who should help.

## Sub lesson 1 — The family and its needs

Every family needs what lets it live well — care, shelter, food, schooling and respect.

## Sub lesson 2 — The family in Angola

Angolan families, with their own habits and ways, are the first home of the citizen and deserve protection.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: families have rights and needs — a family's quality of life, its habits and its place in Angola matter and must be respected. Give one real example from your own life in Angola, and name the lesson's key words: family rights, needs, Angola. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Portuguese as well as in English — Angola's own books teach Educação Moral e Cívica in Portuguese, for life at home and in the community, not only for the classroom.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: families have rights and needs — a family's quality of life, its habits and its place in Angola matter and must be respected.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
