# Video script — Rights of Families

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Families have rights and needs — a family's quality of life, its habits and its place in angola matter and must be respected.

The family and its needs. Every family needs what lets it live well — care, shelter, food, schooling and respect.

The family in Angola. Angolan families, with their own habits and ways, are the first home of the citizen and deserve protection.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
