# Freedom and Responsibility — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Freedom means real choices — and every choice carries responsibility for what follows from it.

**2.** What does the part "Choices, decisions, responsibility" teach?
**Answer:** A free choice is still mine to answer for: deciding well means owning the results of what I chose.
