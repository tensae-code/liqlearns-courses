# Freedom and Responsibility — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) freedom means real choices — and every choice carries responsibility for what follows from it  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — The meaning of freedom?
a) To be free is to be able to choose — between acts, words and paths.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
