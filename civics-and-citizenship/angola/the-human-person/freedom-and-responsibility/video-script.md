# Video script — Freedom and Responsibility

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Freedom means real choices — and every choice carries responsibility for what follows from it.

The meaning of freedom. To be free is to be able to choose — between acts, words and paths.

Choices, decisions, responsibility. A free choice is still mine to answer for: deciding well means owning the results of what I chose.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
