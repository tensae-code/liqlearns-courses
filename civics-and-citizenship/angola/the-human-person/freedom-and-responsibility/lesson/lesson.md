# Freedom and Responsibility

## Do it first

Tell of one choice you made this week. Say what else you could have chosen, and what followed from the choice you made.

## Sub lesson 1 — The meaning of freedom

To be free is to be able to choose — between acts, words and paths.

## Sub lesson 2 — Choices, decisions, responsibility

A free choice is still mine to answer for: deciding well means owning the results of what I chose.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: freedom means real choices — and every choice carries responsibility for what follows from it. Give one real example from your own life in Angola, and name the lesson's key words: freedom, choice, responsibility. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Portuguese as well as in English — Angola's own books teach Educação Moral e Cívica in Portuguese, for life at home and in the community, not only for the classroom.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: freedom means real choices — and every choice carries responsibility for what follows from it.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
