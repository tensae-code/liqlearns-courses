# Hands-on — Diseases and Collective Health

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name one illness people catch from each other and one habit that stops it spreading. Say whose health your habit protects besides your own.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: health is collective as well as personal — caring for myself in sickness and health, and watching for the dangers around us, protects everyone; human life is each person's own good. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
