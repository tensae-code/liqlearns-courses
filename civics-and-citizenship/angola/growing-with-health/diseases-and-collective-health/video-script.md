# Video script — Diseases and Collective Health

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Health is collective as well as personal — caring for myself in sickness and health, and watching for the dangers around us, protects everyone; human life is each person's own good.

Our growing body and self-care. As our bodies grow we learn to care for ourselves — in health and when disease comes.

Dangers around us and collective health. Some dangers threaten the whole community; guarding against them together keeps everyone's life safe.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
