# Diseases and Collective Health — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Health is collective as well as personal — caring for myself in sickness and health, and watching for the dangers around us, protects everyone; human life is each person's own good.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a citizen's own home, school or community in Angola.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Angola and lived there.
