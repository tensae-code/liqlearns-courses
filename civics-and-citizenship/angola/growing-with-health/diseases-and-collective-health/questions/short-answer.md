# Diseases and Collective Health — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Health is collective as well as personal — caring for myself in sickness and health, and watching for the dangers around us, protects everyone; human life is each person's own good.

**2.** What does the part "Dangers around us and collective health" teach?
**Answer:** Some dangers threaten the whole community; guarding against them together keeps everyone's life safe.
