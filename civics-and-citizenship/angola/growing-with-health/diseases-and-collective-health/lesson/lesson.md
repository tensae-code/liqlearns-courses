# Diseases and Collective Health

## Do it first

Name one illness people catch from each other and one habit that stops it spreading. Say whose health your habit protects besides your own.

## Sub lesson 1 — Our growing body and self-care

As our bodies grow we learn to care for ourselves — in health and when disease comes.

## Sub lesson 2 — Dangers around us and collective health

Some dangers threaten the whole community; guarding against them together keeps everyone's life safe.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: health is collective as well as personal — caring for myself in sickness and health, and watching for the dangers around us, protects everyone; human life is each person's own good. Give one real example from your own life in Angola, and name the lesson's key words: health, disease, prevention. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Portuguese as well as in English — Angola's own books teach Educação Moral e Cívica in Portuguese, for life at home and in the community, not only for the classroom.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: health is collective as well as personal — caring for myself in sickness and health, and watching for the dangers around us, protects everyone; human life is each person's own good.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
