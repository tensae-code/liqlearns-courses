# Video script — My Health

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
My health is mine to care for — caring for the body, preventing illness, hygiene in puberty, and knowing what hiv/aids is and how to prevent it.

Care and prevention. Health is kept by daily care of the body and by preventing illness before it starts.

Hygiene in puberty and HIV/AIDS. Puberty asks for new hygiene habits; and HIV/AIDS — what it means and how it is prevented — must be known plainly.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
