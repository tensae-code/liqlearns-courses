# My Health

## Do it first

List your morning body-care steps in order. Then state, in one sentence each, what HIV/AIDS is and one way it is prevented.

## Sub lesson 1 — Care and prevention

Health is kept by daily care of the body and by preventing illness before it starts.

## Sub lesson 2 — Hygiene in puberty and HIV/AIDS

Puberty asks for new hygiene habits; and HIV/AIDS — what it means and how it is prevented — must be known plainly.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: my health is mine to care for — caring for the body, preventing illness, hygiene in puberty, and knowing what HIV/AIDS is and how to prevent it. Give one real example from your own life in Angola, and name the lesson's key words: health, hygiene, HIV/AIDS, prevention. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Portuguese as well as in English — Angola's own books teach Educação Moral e Cívica in Portuguese, for life at home and in the community, not only for the classroom.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: my health is mine to care for — caring for the body, preventing illness, hygiene in puberty, and knowing what HIV/AIDS is and how to prevent it.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
