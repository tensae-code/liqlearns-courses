# My Health — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** My health is mine to care for — caring for the body, preventing illness, hygiene in puberty, and knowing what hiv/aids is and how to prevent it.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a citizen's own home, school or community in Angola.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Angola and lived there.
