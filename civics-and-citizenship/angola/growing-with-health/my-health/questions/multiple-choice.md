# My Health — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) my health is mine to care for — caring for the body, preventing illness, hygiene in puberty, and knowing what HIV/AIDS is and how to prevent it  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — Care and prevention?
a) Health is kept by daily care of the body and by preventing illness before it starts.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
