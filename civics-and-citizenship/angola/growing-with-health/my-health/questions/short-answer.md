# My Health — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** My health is mine to care for — caring for the body, preventing illness, hygiene in puberty, and knowing what hiv/aids is and how to prevent it.

**2.** What does the part "Hygiene in puberty and HIV/AIDS" teach?
**Answer:** Puberty asks for new hygiene habits; and HIV/AIDS — what it means and how it is prevented — must be known plainly.
