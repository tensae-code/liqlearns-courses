# Civics and Citizenship — Angola course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

Civics belongs to the country: this course is Angola's own —
built from Angola's Educação Moral e Cívica student books for
the 5ª and 6ª Classes (Ministério da Educação, Portuguese,
2021). Inside the course the organisation is by **levels, not
grades**: the titles below ARE the levels, in path order — what
the learner proves, step by step. The grades the books come
from live only in each lesson's sources.

The path's own logic is the books': the 5ª book opens with self
and others and closes with national reconciliation; the 6ª book
deepens the same circles — self-knowledge, cultural identity,
the human person with rights and duties, democracy practised
through dialogue and tolerance, the growing body, just
relations, and the environment. That ladder IS the path order
(recommended, never forced — a learner can jump in higher; the
"start here instead" hints point down this ladder).

## The titles

- `knowing-ourselves/` — Level 1. Who am I and who are the
  others (differences and similarities, heritages, families) ·
  habits of daily life · qualities for social life (the good
  citizen's moral qualities, judging my own conduct) · human
  values · who are we? (self and mutual knowledge, 6ª).
- `identity-and-cultural-diversity/` — Level 2. My country, my
  cultural and linguistic identity (the many languages of
  Angola) · origins of our culture · customs, values and beliefs
  of our ancestors (twins in tradition, family festivals).
- `the-human-person/` — Level 3. Rights of families · rights of
  the child · registration: name, nationality and citizenship ·
  rights and duties of citizens (under the Angolan Constitution)
  · emotions · freedom and responsibility.
- `democracy-in-my-country/` — Level 4. Rules of living together
  in a democratic society · we write our class rules · building
  democracy through dialogue · democratic habits and values ·
  tolerance makes a good democrat.
- `the-growing-body/` — Level 5 (first title). Puberty and
  growing (menstruation and sperm, sport in puberty).
- `growing-with-health/` — Level 5 (second title). Diseases and
  collective health · my health (body care, hygiene in puberty,
  HIV/AIDS: meaning and prevention).
- `living-with-others/` — Levels 5–6. Road traffic and safety
  (Level 5: pedestrian, vehicles, signs, rights and duties) ·
  school: source of social progress (Level 6) · I am a social
  being (respect) · justice and injustice.
- `caring-for-the-environment/` — Level 7. The environment we
  live in (heritage, ecology, water, cleaning our land) ·
  national reconciliation (building a better Angola and world).
