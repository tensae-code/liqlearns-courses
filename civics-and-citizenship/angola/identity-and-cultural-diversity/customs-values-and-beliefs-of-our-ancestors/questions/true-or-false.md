# Customs, Values and Beliefs of Our Ancestors — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Our ancestors handed down customs, values and beliefs — in tradition, even twins have their place, and family festivals keep the handing-down alive.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a citizen's own home, school or community in Angola.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Angola and lived there.
