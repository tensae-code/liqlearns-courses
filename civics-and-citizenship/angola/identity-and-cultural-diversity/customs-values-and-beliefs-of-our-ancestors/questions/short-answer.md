# Customs, Values and Beliefs of Our Ancestors — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Our ancestors handed down customs, values and beliefs — in tradition, even twins have their place, and family festivals keep the handing-down alive.

**2.** What does the part "Tradition and family festivals" teach?
**Answer:** Traditional ways — including how twins are regarded in tradition — and family festivals keep that inheritance shared and alive.
