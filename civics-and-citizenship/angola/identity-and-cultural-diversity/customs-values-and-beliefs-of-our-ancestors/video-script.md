# Video script — Customs, Values and Beliefs of Our Ancestors

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

This lesson is about one thing.
Our ancestors handed down customs, values and beliefs — in tradition, even twins have their place, and family festivals keep the handing-down alive.

What the ancestors handed down. Customs, values and beliefs passed from grandparents to children carry a people's memory and guidance.

Tradition and family festivals. Traditional ways — including how twins are regarded in tradition — and family festivals keep that inheritance shared and alive.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
