# Customs, Values and Beliefs of Our Ancestors

## Do it first

Ask at home about one custom your grandparents kept. Write what it was and what value it protected.

## Sub lesson 1 — What the ancestors handed down

Customs, values and beliefs passed from grandparents to children carry a people's memory and guidance.

## Sub lesson 2 — Tradition and family festivals

Traditional ways — including how twins are regarded in tradition — and family festivals keep that inheritance shared and alive.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: our ancestors handed down customs, values and beliefs — in tradition, even twins have their place, and family festivals keep the handing-down alive. Give one real example from your own life in Angola, and name the lesson's key words: ancestors, customs, tradition, festivals. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Portuguese as well as in English — Angola's own books teach Educação Moral e Cívica in Portuguese, for life at home and in the community, not only for the classroom.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.

3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
