# Video script — My Country, My Cultural and Linguistic Identity

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Angola is a country of many languages — my cultural and linguistic identity is part of who i am, and i take part in my community's cultural life.

The many languages of Angola. Angola speaks in many languages; each language carries a people's way of seeing and living.

Taking part in cultural life. I belong to my culture by taking part in it — its language, its gatherings and its life in my community.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
