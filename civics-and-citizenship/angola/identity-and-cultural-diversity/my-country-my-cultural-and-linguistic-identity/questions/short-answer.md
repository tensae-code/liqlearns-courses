# My Country, My Cultural and Linguistic Identity — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Angola is a country of many languages — my cultural and linguistic identity is part of who i am, and i take part in my community's cultural life.

**2.** What does the part "Taking part in cultural life" teach?
**Answer:** I belong to my culture by taking part in it — its language, its gatherings and its life in my community.
