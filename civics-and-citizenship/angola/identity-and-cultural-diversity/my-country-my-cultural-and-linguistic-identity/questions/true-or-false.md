# My Country, My Cultural and Linguistic Identity — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Angola is a country of many languages — my cultural and linguistic identity is part of who i am, and i take part in my community's cultural life.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a citizen's own home, school or community in Angola.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Angola and lived there.
