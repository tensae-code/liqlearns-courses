# My Country, My Cultural and Linguistic Identity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) Angola is a country of many languages — my cultural and linguistic identity is part of who I am, and I take part in my community's cultural life  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — The many languages of Angola?
a) Angola speaks in many languages; each language carries a people's way of seeing and living.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
