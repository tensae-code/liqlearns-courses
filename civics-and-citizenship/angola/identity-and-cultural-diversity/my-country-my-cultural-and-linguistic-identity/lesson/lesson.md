# My Country, My Cultural and Linguistic Identity

## Do it first

Say a greeting in your home language, then in another Angolan language you know. Name one way you take part in your community's cultural life.

## Sub lesson 1 — The many languages of Angola

Angola speaks in many languages; each language carries a people's way of seeing and living.

## Sub lesson 2 — Taking part in cultural life

I belong to my culture by taking part in it — its language, its gatherings and its life in my community.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Angola is a country of many languages — my cultural and linguistic identity is part of who I am, and I take part in my community's cultural life. Give one real example from your own life in Angola, and name the lesson's key words: languages, identity, culture, Angola. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Portuguese as well as in English — Angola's own books teach Educação Moral e Cívica in Portuguese, for life at home and in the community, not only for the classroom.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: Angola is a country of many languages — my cultural and linguistic identity is part of who I am, and I take part in my community's cultural life.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
