# Hands-on — Origins of Our Culture

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name one custom from your home and say where you think it comes from. Then name one custom of another Angolan people you have seen or heard of.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: our culture has origins worth knowing — and becoming aware of cultural diversity makes us richer, not divided. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
