# Video script — Origins of Our Culture

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Our culture has origins worth knowing — and becoming aware of cultural diversity makes us richer, not divided.

Where our culture comes from. Angolan culture grew from the peoples, lands and histories of this country over a long time.

Aware of diversity. Cultures differ; noticing and respecting that diversity is a strength for a country of many peoples.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
