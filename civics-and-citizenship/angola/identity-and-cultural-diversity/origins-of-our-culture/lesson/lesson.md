# Origins of Our Culture

## Do it first

Name one custom from your home and say where you think it comes from. Then name one custom of another Angolan people you have seen or heard of.

## Sub lesson 1 — Where our culture comes from

Angolan culture grew from the peoples, lands and histories of this country over a long time.

## Sub lesson 2 — Aware of diversity

Cultures differ; noticing and respecting that diversity is a strength for a country of many peoples.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: our culture has origins worth knowing — and becoming aware of cultural diversity makes us richer, not divided. Give one real example from your own life in Angola, and name the lesson's key words: culture, origins, diversity. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Portuguese as well as in English — Angola's own books teach Educação Moral e Cívica in Portuguese, for life at home and in the community, not only for the classroom.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: our culture has origins worth knowing — and becoming aware of cultural diversity makes us richer, not divided.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
