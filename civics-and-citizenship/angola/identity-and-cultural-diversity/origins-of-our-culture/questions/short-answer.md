# Origins of Our Culture — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Our culture has origins worth knowing — and becoming aware of cultural diversity makes us richer, not divided.

**2.** What does the part "Aware of diversity" teach?
**Answer:** Cultures differ; noticing and respecting that diversity is a strength for a country of many peoples.
