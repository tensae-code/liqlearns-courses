# Origins of Our Culture — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) our culture has origins worth knowing — and becoming aware of cultural diversity makes us richer, not divided  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — Where our culture comes from?
a) Angolan culture grew from the peoples, lands and histories of this country over a long time.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
