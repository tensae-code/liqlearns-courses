# Communicating and Working in a Team

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Communicating and Working in a Team. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Moldova's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## Sub lesson 1 — Communication styles

The communication styles the book names — passive, aggressive, passive-aggressive and assertive — and how to tell them apart.

## Sub lesson 2 — Nonverbal communication

What we say without words — gesture, posture, face and distance — and how it agrees with or betrays our words.

## Sub lesson 3 — Working in a team and taking decisions

How a team with a common goal, cohesion and consensus takes decisions together.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Communication styles (passive, aggressive, passive-aggressive, assertive), nonverbal communication and how a team with a common goal, cohesion and consensus takes decisions. Give one real example from your own life or from the Moldova you live in, and name the lesson's key words: communicating, and, working. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
