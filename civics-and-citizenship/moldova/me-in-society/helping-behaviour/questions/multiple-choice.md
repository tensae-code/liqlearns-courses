Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Prosocial behaviour — helping, volunteering and solidarity — and how it is learned and practised.  b) it has nothing to do with how Moldova governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Prosocial behaviour — helping, volunteering and solidarity — and how it is learned and practised.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
