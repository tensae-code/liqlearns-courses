# About the Civics and Citizenship — Moldova course

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in
this course comes from government textbooks — this country's
own Educație Civică (Civic Education) books. The one
exception is the **Tip boxes**: a tip in a box is from us,
the people making LiqLearns. Tips are ours; the lessons are
the books'.

## How this course is organised

This is ONE course for Moldova inside the Civics and
Citizenship folder (one course per country). Inside it, the
organisation is by **levels, not grades**: a learner's place
is found by what they prove, and each level stands on the
ones before it as a recommended-first path — never a forced
gate. The school grades the books come from appear only in
each lesson's `lesson/sources.md`, as provenance.

Moldova's own way, preserved whole, as filed: civics is
taught as one spiral subject — the person in society, then
the democratic state, then health as a personal and social
value, then career — with every theme in the Grade 9 book
built the same way: a motto, a problem-situation, the base
text, a dictionary box, and Reflectez/Acționez (reflect/act)
tasks. The Grade 10 book turns from the group to the person
and the law: identity and dignity first, then law as the
person's protection — justice, national and European law,
the citizen before the law, civil law. The Grade 11 book
applies the law to adult life — family law, spouses'
property, labour law, fiscal law — and closes the civics
course with civil society, elections and Moldova's place in
Europe. Parallel language editions (Romanian and Russian)
are placed once, with both editions named, as filed.

The course as built has **three stocked levels**, from the
filing for this build (`moldova-placements-b.md` — 0 census
records; 6 link leads, all naming the Educație Civică
manuals for Grades 9, 10 and 11 in Romanian and Russian
parallel editions, none in the census; 3 distinct books read
— the Romanian editions read in full; the Russian editions
spot-checked: cover, contents and spreads match the Romanian
books page for page, same 128 pp and same contents, so
placed once with both editions named; source: official
ministry resource portal eduresurse.gov.md, no-login direct
PDFs; 63 placements in all, stocking 63 lessons — the coined
lesson names were deduped before building: every coined name
was already distinct, so no lesson is stocked by more than
one placement, and every placement stocks into exactly one
lesson):

1. **Level 1 — The Person as a Social Being (27 lessons).**
   Communication and team work, conflict within the rules,
   the family as first group and what families do, parents
   and children, values and helping behaviour; democracy and
   taking part, pluralism and civil society, rights,
   freedoms and responsibilities as one system, citizenship
   of Moldova and of the European Union, Moldova's European
   path, national values in a multicultural world; people,
   risk and cohesion, careless behaviour, passions and a
   healthy life, the right to health care, health insurance,
   first aid, overcoming hard times; work and professions in
   Moldova, choosing a career, presenting myself, my
   resources, my career project, producer and consumer,
   public goods and services.
2. **Level 2 — Identity, Dignity and the Law That Protects
   (18 lessons).** Identity and personality, personal and
   national dignity, relating to others, between
   generations, communication in a couple; justice and law,
   national and European law, the citizen and the law, civil
   law; health and culture, food and health, life, values
   and bioethics, today's social problems, HIV, AIDS and
   tuberculosis; professional success, chances for work and
   business, small and big business, a quality way of life.
3. **Level 3 — The Adult Citizen — Family, Law and Europe
   (18 lessons).** The family in society, family traditions,
   values and ethical norms, the citizen and civic conduct;
   family law, spouses' property, labour law, fiscal law,
   civil society and elections, Moldova and the European
   organisations; health as culture, life and conflicts of
   values, bioethics of existence, health protection law;
   ways to grow, personal and professional success, business
   — chances and fears, business ethics.

**Course-intro video script** (written here, made later;
the books' own shape, walked in order):

- *Moldova's own order:* "First, the person as a social
  being: how we communicate and work in a team, how conflict
  stays within the rules, the family — our first group — and
  the values that guide us and teach us to help. Then the
  democratic society: democracy and taking part, pluralism
  and civil society, rights, freedoms and responsibilities
  working as one system, citizenship of Moldova and of the
  European Union, and Moldova's European path. Then life and
  health as values every citizen guards, and the first steps
  into a career. The second book goes deeper into the
  person: identity, dignity, the generations, and the law
  that protects the person — justice, national and European
  law, civil law. The last book makes it adult: the family
  in society, family law, labour law, taxes, civil society
  and elections, Moldova among the European organisations —
  and honest business as a civic value."

## Honesty notes (course level)

- **What the filing does and does not contain.** The filing
  contains 3 distinct read books (the Educație Civică
  student books for Grades 9, 10 and 11, Romanian editions

  spot-checked and named), 63 placements, and the books' own
  contents maps. The filing does not contain any other

  therefore covers exactly the three books' own progression,
  Grade 9 first and Grade 11 last, as filed.

  name the Educație Civică manuals for Grades 9, 10 and 11
