# Family Traditions — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Family traditions and their role in identity and continuity.  b) it has nothing to do with how Moldova governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Family traditions and their role in identity and continuity.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Family traditions and their role in identity and continuity.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Moldova
**Answer: a) Family traditions and their role in identity and continuity.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
