# The Family in Society

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: The Family in Society. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Moldova's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

The family and society — how the family sits inside the wider community.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The family and society — how the family sits inside the wider community. Give one real example from your own life or from the Moldova you live in, and name the lesson's key words: the, family, in. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: The family and society — how the family sits inside the wider community.
