# Identity and Personality

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Identity and Personality. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Moldova's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

What identity and personality are and how a person comes to know who they are.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: What identity and personality are and how a person comes to know who they are. Give one real example from your own life or from the Moldova you live in, and name the lesson's key words: identity, and, personality. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
