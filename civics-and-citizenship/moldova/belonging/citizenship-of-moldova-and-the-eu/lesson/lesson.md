# Citizenship of Moldova and of the European Union

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Citizenship of Moldova and of the European Union. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Moldova's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## Sub lesson 1 — Citizenship of the Republic of Moldova

Who is a citizen of the Republic of Moldova, and how citizenship is gained and lost.

## Sub lesson 2 — Citizenship of the European Union

What EU citizenship adds on top of national citizenship.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Who is a citizen of the Republic of Moldova, how citizenship is gained and lost, and what EU citizenship adds. Give one real example from your own life or from the Moldova you live in, and name the lesson's key words: citizenship, of, moldova. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
