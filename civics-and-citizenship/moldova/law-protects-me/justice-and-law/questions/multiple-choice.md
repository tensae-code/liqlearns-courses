# Justice and Law — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Justice and law — what law is for and how it differs from custom and morality.  b) it has nothing to do with how Moldova governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Justice and law — what law is for and how it differs from custom and morality.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Justice and law — what law is for and how it differs from custom and morality.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Moldova
**Answer: a) Justice and law — what law is for and how it differs from custom and morality.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
