# Rights, Freedoms and Responsibilities

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Rights, Freedoms and Responsibilities. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Moldova's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## Sub lesson 1 — Rights, freedoms and responsibilities as one system

How people's rights, freedoms and responsibilities work together as one system, each holding the others up.

## Sub lesson 2 — When rights may be restricted

When some rights and freedoms may lawfully be restricted, and what keeps a restriction lawful.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Rights, freedoms and responsibilities as one system, and when some rights may lawfully be restricted. Give one real example from your own life or from the Moldova you live in, and name the lesson's key words: rights, freedoms, and. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: Rights, freedoms and responsibilities as one system, and when some rights may lawfully be restricted.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
