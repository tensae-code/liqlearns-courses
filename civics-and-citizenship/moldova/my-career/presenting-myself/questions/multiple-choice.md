# Presenting Myself — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Personal promotion — presenting one's abilities, CV and interview conduct.  b) it has nothing to do with how Moldova governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Personal promotion — presenting one's abilities, CV and interview conduct.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Personal promotion — presenting one's abilities, CV and interview conduct.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Moldova
**Answer: a) Personal promotion — presenting one's abilities, CV and interview conduct.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
