# A Quality Way of Life — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) A quality way of life as the guarantee of professional success.  b) it has nothing to do with how Moldova governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) A quality way of life as the guarantee of professional success.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) A quality way of life as the guarantee of professional success.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Moldova
**Answer: a) A quality way of life as the guarantee of professional success.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
