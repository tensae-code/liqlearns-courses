# Public Goods and Services

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Public Goods and Services. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Moldova's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Public goods and services — what they are, who provides them and why they matter.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Public goods and services — what they are, who provides them and why they matter. Give one real example from your own life or from the Moldova you live in, and name the lesson's key words: public, goods, and. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
