# Public Goods and Services — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Public goods and services — what they are, who provides them and why they matter.  b) it has nothing to do with how Moldova governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Public goods and services — what they are, who provides them and why they matter.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Public goods and services — what they are, who provides them and why they matter.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Moldova
**Answer: a) Public goods and services — what they are, who provides them and why they matter.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
