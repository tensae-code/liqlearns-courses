# A Multicultural World and National Values — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Living in a multicultural world while keeping national values, traditions, customs and folk dress.  b) it has nothing to do with how Moldova governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Living in a multicultural world while keeping national values, traditions, customs and folk dress.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Living in a multicultural world while keeping national values, traditions, customs and folk dress.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Moldova
**Answer: a) Living in a multicultural world while keeping national values, traditions, customs and folk dress.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
