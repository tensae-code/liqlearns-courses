# Health as Culture — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Health as an element of culture and personal responsibility.  b) it has nothing to do with how Moldova governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Health as an element of culture and personal responsibility.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Health as an element of culture and personal responsibility.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Moldova
**Answer: a) Health as an element of culture and personal responsibility.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
