# Food and Health — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Food culture and its effect on health.  b) it has nothing to do with how Moldova governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Food culture and its effect on health.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Food culture and its effect on health.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Moldova
**Answer: a) Food culture and its effect on health.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
