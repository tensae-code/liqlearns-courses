# First Aid — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Premedical first aid — what to do before professional help arrives.  b) it has nothing to do with how Moldova governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Premedical first aid — what to do before professional help arrives.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Premedical first aid — what to do before professional help arrives.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Moldova
**Answer: a) Premedical first aid — what to do before professional help arrives.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
