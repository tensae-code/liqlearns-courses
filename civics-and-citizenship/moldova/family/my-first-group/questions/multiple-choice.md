# The Family — My First Group — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The family as the first group a person belongs to, its place in society and what membership of it means.  b) it has nothing to do with how Moldova governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The family as the first group a person belongs to, its place in society and what membership of it means.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

a) The family as the first group a person belongs to, its place in society and what membership of it means.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Moldova
**Answer: a) The family as the first group a person belongs to, its place in society and what membership of it means.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
