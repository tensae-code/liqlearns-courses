# What Families Do — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The family's functions (care, upbringing, support, economic) and the responsibilities that go with them.  b) it has nothing to do with how Moldova governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The family's functions (care, upbringing, support, economic) and the responsibilities that go with them.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**Answer: a) The family's functions (care, upbringing, support, economic) and the responsibilities that go with them.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
