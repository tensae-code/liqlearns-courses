# Democracy and Taking Part — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) What democracy is and how citizens participate, in the world and in the Republic of Moldova.  b) it has nothing to do with how Moldova governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) What democracy is and how citizens participate, in the world and in the Republic of Moldova.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) What democracy is and how citizens participate, in the world and in the Republic of Moldova.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Moldova
**Answer: a) What democracy is and how citizens participate, in the world and in the Republic of Moldova.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
