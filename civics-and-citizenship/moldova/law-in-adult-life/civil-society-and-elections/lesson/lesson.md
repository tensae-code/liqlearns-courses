# Civil Society and Elections

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Civil Society and Elections. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Moldova's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

What civil society does in practice — organisations, campaigns and citizens acting together.

## Sub lesson 2 — Elections in Moldova

How elections are organised and run in Moldova — the Central Electoral Commission's information and education campaigns, and voting, including the mobile ballot box.
