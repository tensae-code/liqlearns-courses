# Civics and Citizenship — Moldova course — CURRICULUM (the path)

One course for Moldova inside the Civics and Citizenship
folder, organised by **levels, not grades**: a learner climbs
when they have proved the level, not when a school calendar
says so. The school grades the books come from appear only in
each lesson's sources (provenance), never as the course's
organisation. The path follows Moldova's own order as its
books lay it out, from the filing's ladder sketch
(`moldova-placements-b.md` — 0 census records; 6 link leads,
all naming the Educație Civică manuals for Grades 9, 10 and
11 in Romanian and Russian parallel editions, none in the
census; 3 distinct books read — the Romanian editions read in
full, the Russian editions spot-checked and placed once with
both editions named): civics is taught as one spiral subject —
the person in society, then the democratic state, then health
as a personal and social value, then career — deepening book
by book until the last book applies the law to adult life.
The filing stocks 63 placements into 63 lessons: the coined
lesson names were deduped before building — every coined name
was already distinct, no two placements feed the same lesson —
and every placement stocks into exactly one lesson.

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in
this course comes from government textbooks — this country's
own Educație Civică books — except the **Tip boxes**: a tip
in a box is from us, the people making LiqLearns. Tips are
ours; the lessons are the books'. See ABOUT.md for the full
book list and the acceptance reckoning. **All 63 lessons stand
on student books; no lesson in this course stands on a teacher
guide or a programme.**

## The levels (the path, in order)

1. **The Person as a Social Being** — the earliest book in hand starts from the pupil's own relations and widens circle by circle: communication and team work, conflict within the rules, the family as the first group, values and helping behaviour; then the democratic society — democracy and taking part, pluralism and civil society, rights-freedoms-responsibilities as one system, citizenship of Moldova and of the European Union, Moldova's European path and its national values in a multicultural world; then life and health as personal and social values; then personal development and career. 27 lessons, in 8 title(s): `me-in-society/` (4 lessons); `family/` (3 lessons); `democratic-society/` (2 lessons); `rights-and-duties/` (1 lesson); `belonging/` (1 lesson); `moldova-in-the-world/` (2 lessons); `life-and-health/` (7 lessons); `my-career/` (7 lessons).
2. **Identity, Dignity and the Law That Protects** — the middle book turns from the group to the person and the law: identity and personality, personal and national dignity, relations with others, across generations and in couples; then law as the person's protection — justice and law, national and European law, the citizen and the law, civil law; health as culture, food and health, bioethics and today's social problems, HIV/AIDS and tuberculosis; professional success and business. 18 lessons, in 4 title(s): `me-and-my-identity/` (5 lessons); `law-protects-me/` (4 lessons); `life-and-health/` (5 lessons); `my-career/` (4 lessons).
3. **The Adult Citizen — Family, Law and Europe** — the last book in hand applies civics to the adult citizen the pupil is about to become: the family in society, family traditions, values and ethical norms, the citizen and civic conduct; then the law of adult life — family law, spouses' property, labour law, fiscal law, civil society and elections, Moldova and the European organisations; health as culture, conflicts of values, bioethics and health-protection law; personal growth and business ethics. 18 lessons, in 4 title(s): `family-and-society/` (4 lessons); `law-in-adult-life/` (6 lessons); `life-and-health/` (4 lessons); `my-career/` (4 lessons).

**Totals: 63 lessons** (Level 1: 27 · Level 2: 18 · Level 3: 18 — from three read student books: *Educație Civică — Manual pentru clasa a IX-a* (Univers Educațional, for the Ministerul Educației al Republicii Moldova, 2014, ISBN 978-9975-9544-1-9, Romanian, with its Russian parallel edition, COMPLETE), *Educație civică: Manual pentru clasa a X-a* (ministry-approved manual, Ministerul Educației al Republicii Moldova, ISBN 978-9975-67-841-4, Romanian, with its Russian parallel edition, COMPLETE), and *Educație civică: Manual pentru clasa a XI-a* (ministry-approved manual, Ministerul Educației al Republicii Moldova, ISBN 978-9975-67-903-9, Romanian, with its Russian parallel edition, COMPLETE); 63 placements stocking 63 lessons).

## Where the build stands

  synthesis sections (Evaluare / Sinteză și evaluare) are

  programme, and no UNREAD book. Nothing else is in the
