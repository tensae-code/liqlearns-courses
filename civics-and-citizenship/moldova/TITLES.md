# Civics and Citizenship — Moldova course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

Civics belongs to the country: this course is Moldova's own —
built from Moldova's Educație Civică (Civic Education)
student books for Grades 9, 10 and 11 (Romanian editions,
with their Russian parallel editions named). Inside the
course the organisation is by **levels, not grades**: the
titles below ARE the levels' titles, in path order — what
the learner proves, step by step. The grades the books come
from live only in each lesson's sources.

The path's own logic is Moldova's, from the filing's ladder
sketch: the Grade 9 book starts from the pupil's own
relations — the person as a social being: communication,
team work, conflict within the rules, the family as first
group, values and helping behaviour — then widens to the
democratic society (democracy, pluralism, civil society,
rights-freedoms-responsibilities as one system, Moldovan
and EU citizenship, Moldova's European path), then life and
health as personal and social values, then personal
development and career. The Grade 10 book deepens the
person — identity, personality and dignity, relations
across generations and in couples — and introduces law
systematically, as the instrument that protects the person:
justice, national and European law, the citizen and the
law, civil law; then health and contemporary social
problems; then professional success and business. The
Grade 11 book applies civics to the adult citizen the pupil
is about to become: the family in society and civic
conduct; the law of adult life — family law, spouses'
property, labour law, fiscal law; civil society and
elections; Moldova and the European organisations;
bioethics and health-protection law; personal growth and
business ethics. That ladder IS the path order (recommended,
never forced — a learner can jump in higher; the "start here
instead" hints point down this ladder).
