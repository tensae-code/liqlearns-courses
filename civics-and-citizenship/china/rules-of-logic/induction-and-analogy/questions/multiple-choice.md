Pick the one right answer.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Induction and analogy — their methods and limits; analogy and its methods in depth.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in China
**Answer: a) Induction and analogy — their methods and limits; analogy and its methods in depth.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
