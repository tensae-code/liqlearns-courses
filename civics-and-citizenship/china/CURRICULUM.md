# Civics and Citizenship — China — curriculum

One course for China inside the Civics and Citizenship
folder, built from China's own government textbooks: the
senior-high Ideological and Political Education
(思想政治) books of the People's Education Press,
compiled under the Ministry of Education — the four
Compulsory books, the three Elective Compulsory books,
and the Xi Jinping Thought Student Reader — all Chinese
medium, all filed at senior high (filed grade-12), as
filed in the placements filing for this build
(`china-placements.md` — see ABOUT.md for the full book
list, the verdicts, and every label carried exactly as
filed). The organisation is by **levels, not grades**: a
learner's place is found by what they prove. The path is
recommended, never forced — a learner can jump in
higher; the "start here instead" hints point down this
ladder. The grades the books come from appear only in
provenance (ABOUT.md and each lesson's
`lesson/sources.md`).

The ladder follows China's own books' progression, as
the filing's ladder sketch lays it out: China's civics
is history-first — the ladder opens with origins (how
socialism arose, came to China, and took its Chinese
form), then the citizen's economic world, then the
political core (Party leadership, the people as masters,
rule of law), then the worldview level (philosophy,
values, culture), then the three electives as applied
extensions (the world, everyday law, clear thinking),
and closes with the capstone reader that re-presents
the whole system as one guiding thought.

## Level 1 — The Road to Socialism — 5 lessons

Level 1 rests on Compulsory 1, Chinese Socialism: how socialism arose as a science out of the succession of class societies, how it came to China through the New Democratic Revolution and the founding of the socialist system, how Reform and Opening Up opened the road of socialism with Chinese characteristics, and how the story arrives at the new era — the Chinese Dream of national rejuvenation and the guiding thought of the new era, closed by the book's own inquiry projects on China's road. Lessons: How Socialism Became a Science · Socialism in China · Socialism with Chinese Characteristics · The New Era and the Chinese Dream · The Guiding Thought of the New Era.

## Level 2 — The Economic Order and the People's Livelihood — 4 lessons

Level 2 rests on Compulsory 2, Economy and Society: economic citizenship taught inside the socialist market economy frame — public ownership as the mainstay with diverse forms of ownership developing together, the market's decisive role balanced by the government's role, the new development philosophy and a modernised economic system, and distribution and social security as the people's livelihood guarantees. Lessons: Ownership of the Means of Production · The Socialist Market Economy · How China Develops · Income Distribution and Social Security.

## Level 3 — Party Leadership, the People as Masters, and Rule of Law — 9 lessons

Level 3 rests on Compulsory 3, Politics and Rule of Law — the core civics volume of the set: the political order taught as a single integrated whole, Party leadership, the people as masters, and law-based governance presented as organically unified; the people's congress system as the fundamental political system, and multiparty cooperation, regional ethnic autonomy and grassroots self-governance each given their full place; then comprehensively advancing law-based governance — a law-based country, government and society, worked through scientific legislation, strict enforcement, impartial justice and universal observance of law. Lessons: Why the Party Leads · The Party's Nature and Purpose · Upholding Party Leadership · The Nature of the State · The People's Congress System · China's Basic Political Systems · Law as the Way of Governance · Building a Law-Based Country · How Law-Based Governance Works.

## Level 4 — Worldview, Values and Culture — 9 lessons

Level 4 rests on Compulsory 4, Philosophy and Culture: Marxist philosophy taught as the citizen's worldview and method — the material world and its laws, dialectics, where knowledge comes from, how society works, and the value of a life chosen standing with the people's interests — and culture taught as inheritance of fine traditional Chinese culture, critical learning from foreign cultures, and confidence in socialist culture. Lessons: What Philosophy Is · The Material World · Dialectics · Where Knowledge Comes From · How Society Works · The Value of a Life · What Culture Is · Cultures of the World · Developing Socialist Culture.

## Level 5 — The Electives — the World, Law in Life, and Clear Thinking — 32 lessons

Level 5 rests on the three elective compulsory books, filed together at this level. Elective 1, Contemporary International Politics and Economy, teaches the world from China's standpoint: forms of state compared, multipolarisation, peace and development as the era's themes, China's diplomacy and the community of a shared future for mankind, economic globalisation, and the international organisations with China's place in them. Elective 2, Law and Life, teaches law as life law — the Civil Code in daily situations: personal and property rights, contract and tort, marriage and inheritance, the worker's and founder's legal toolkits, and how disputes are settled from mediation to court. Elective 3, Logic and Thinking, teaches clear thinking as a citizen skill: concepts, judgements and inference, then dialectical and creative thinking, each unit closing with an inquiry on correcting real reasoning errors. Lessons: Forms of State · How States Are Structured · Multipolarisation · Peace and Development · China's Diplomacy · Entering Economic Globalisation · Economic Globalisation and China · The Main International Organisations · China and International Organisations · Learning Civil Law from Life · Protecting Property Rights · Contracts and Good Faith · Tort and the Limits of Rights · Growing Up in a Harmonious Family · Marriage in Law · The Wise Worker · Starting a Business Honestly · Many Ways to Settle a Dispute · Justice Through Litigation · Entering the World of Thought · The Point of Logic · What Scientific Thinking Is · Grasping Concepts Accurately · Using Judgements Correctly · Deductive Reasoning · Induction and Analogy · Analysis and Synthesis · Quality and Quantity · How Understanding Develops · Thinking by Association · Exploring Many Roads · Thinking Ahead.

## Level 6 — The Guiding Thought — the Capstone — 8 lessons

Level 6 rests on the Xi Jinping Thought Student Reader, Senior High: a single capstone reader that organises the whole subject around one guiding thought — its origin in the new era, its goals, the Party as leading force, the people as standpoint, the five-sphere overall layout ("Five-in-One"), the four-pronged strategic layout ("Four Comprehensives"), national security and diplomacy, closing on the pupil's own resolve. Lessons: The Thought of the New Era · The Goal of Rejuvenation · The Party as Leading Force · The People as the Standpoint · The Five-Sphere Layout · The Four-Pronged Strategy · Security for Rejuvenation · Peaceful Development and Diplomacy.
