# The Goal of Rejuvenation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The goal and task — socialist modernisation and the great rejuvenation of the Chinese nation.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that The Goal of Rejuvenation — and that it has no connection to the rest of China's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside China's own course, in its own order.
