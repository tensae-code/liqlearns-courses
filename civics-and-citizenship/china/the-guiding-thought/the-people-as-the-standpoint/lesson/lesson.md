# The People as the Standpoint

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: The People as the Standpoint. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what China's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

The fundamental standpoint — putting the people at the centre.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The fundamental standpoint — putting the people at the centre. Give one real example from your own life or from the China you live in, and name the lesson's key words: the, people, as. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
