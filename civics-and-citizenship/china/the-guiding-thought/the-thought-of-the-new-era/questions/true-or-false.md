# The Thought of the New Era — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Xi Jinping Thought as guiding thought — the new era that produced it and how it leads the new era; firming the "Four Confidences" and resolving to be a person of the new era.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that The Thought of the New Era — and that it has no connection to the rest of China's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside China's own course, in its own order.
