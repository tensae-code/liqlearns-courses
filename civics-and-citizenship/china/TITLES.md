# Civics and Citizenship — China course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

Civics belongs to the country: this course is China's
own — built from China's senior-high Ideological and
Political Education (思想政治) books (People's
Education Press, compiled under the Ministry of
Education, Chinese, exactly as filed). Inside the
course the organisation is by **levels, not grades**:
the titles below ARE the levels' titles, in path
order — what the learner proves, step by step. The
grades the books come from live only in each lesson's
sources.

The path's own logic is China's, from the filing's
ladder sketch: history-first civics — the ladder opens
with origins, how socialism arose, came to China, and
took its Chinese form, ending in the new era's guiding
thought and the rejuvenation goal, and everything
later presupposes this story; then the citizen's
economic world (ownership, the market and government,
development, distribution and social security); then
the political core and the ladder's centre (who leads,
in whose name, through which institutions, and how
law governs — the most purely civic level); then the
worldview level (Marxist philosophy as method, values
and the choice of a life, cultural inheritance and
confidence); then the electives as applied extensions
in three directions — the world, everyday law, and
disciplined thought — taken after the compulsory core;
and the capstone, the whole system re-presented as one
guiding thought with its layouts, security and
diplomacy, closing on the pupil's own resolve. That
ladder IS the path order (recommended, never forced —
a learner can jump in higher; the "start here instead"
hints point down this ladder).

The coined lesson names in the filing were deduped
before building (see CURRICULUM.md, "Where the build
stands"): 158 placements stock 67
lessons. Every placement stocks into exactly one
lesson, and each placement's provenance row (book +
printed page, exactly as filed) is in that lesson's
`lesson/sources.md`. Unit folders are the filing's own
coined titles.
