# Exploring Many Roads — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Divergent and convergent thinking, and reverse thinking; reverse thinking — its meaning and use.  b) it has nothing to do with how China governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Divergent and convergent thinking, and reverse thinking; reverse thinking — its meaning and use.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

a) Divergent and convergent thinking, and reverse thinking; reverse thinking — its meaning and use.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in China
**Answer: a) Divergent and convergent thinking, and reverse thinking; reverse thinking — its meaning and use.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
