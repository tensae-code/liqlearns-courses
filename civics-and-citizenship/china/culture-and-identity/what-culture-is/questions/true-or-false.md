# What Culture Is — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The connotation and functions of culture; correctly understanding traditional Chinese culture — its strengths and its historical limits; carrying forward fine traditional Chinese culture and the national spirit centred on patriotism.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that What Culture Is — and that it has no connection to the rest of China's story.
