# Cultures of the World — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The nationality and diversity of cultures, cultural exchange and blending, and the correct attitude to foreign cultures.  b) it has nothing to do with how China governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The nationality and diversity of cultures, cultural exchange and blending, and the correct attitude to foreign cultures.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

a) The nationality and diversity of cultures, cultural exchange and blending, and the correct attitude to foreign cultures.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in China
**Answer: a) The nationality and diversity of cultures, cultural exchange and blending, and the correct attitude to foreign cultures.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
