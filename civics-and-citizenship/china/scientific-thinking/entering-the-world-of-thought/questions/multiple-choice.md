# Entering the World of Thought — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) What thinking is, its features and its forms; the forms of thinking and their features in depth.  b) it has nothing to do with how China governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) What thinking is, its features and its forms; the forms of thinking and their features in depth.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) What thinking is, its features and its forms; the forms of thinking and their features in depth.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in China
**Answer: a) What thinking is, its features and its forms; the forms of thinking and their features in depth.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
