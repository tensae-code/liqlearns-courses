# Lesson plan — Forms of State

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — China · **Title:** The World of States · **Lesson:** Forms of State
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 5 — The Electives — the World, Law in Life, and Clear Thinking (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** What a state is — its nature and functions; forms of government — how state power is organised; political parties and interest groups in different systems; the unitary and composite forms compared in depth. This lesson stands on a student book (see lesson/sources.md).
**This lesson's own level:** Level 5 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: What a state is — its nature and functions; forms of government — how state power is organised; political parties and interest groups in different systems; the unitary and composite forms compared in depth.

3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the learner's own life in China and the book's own teaching, as the book itself works.
