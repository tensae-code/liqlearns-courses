Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Xi Jinping Thought on Socialism with Chinese Characteristics for a New Era, its core content and status as guiding thought; project work comparing China's road with other roads and arguing that direction decides the road and the road decides destiny.

**2.** This lesson teaches that The Guiding Thought of the New Era — and that it has no connection to the rest of China's story.
