# Why the Party Leads — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The political forces in China before 1949 and why they failed, as the historical case for Party leadership; the Party leading the people to stand up, grow rich and become strong across the revolutionary, construction and reform eras.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that Why the Party Leads — and that it has no connection to the rest of China's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside China's own course, in its own order.
