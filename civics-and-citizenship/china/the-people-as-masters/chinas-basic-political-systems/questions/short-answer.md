# China's Basic Political Systems — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** The system of multiparty cooperation and political consultation under Party leadership, and the CPPCC; the system of regional ethnic autonomy and national unity; the system of grassroots mass self-governance — villagers' and residents' committees as direct democracy in daily life.
