# About the Civics and Citizenship — China course

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in
this course comes from government textbooks — this country's
own Ideological and Political Education (思想政治)
books.
The one exception is the **Tip boxes**: a tip in a box is
from us, the people making LiqLearns. Tips are ours; the
lessons are the books'.

## How this course is organised

This is ONE course for China inside the Civics and
Citizenship folder (one course per country). Inside it, the
organisation is by **levels, not grades**: a learner's place
is found by what they prove, and each level stands on the
ones before it as a recommended-first path — never a forced
gate. The school grades the books come from appear only in
each lesson's `lesson/sources.md`, as provenance.

China's civics-type teaching in hand is the senior-high
Ideological and Political Education set, as filed: the four
Compulsory books, the three Elective Compulsory books, and
the Xi Jinping Thought Student Reader — all People's
Education Press, compiled under the Ministry of Education,
all filed at senior high (filed grade-12), all in Chinese.
The filing's own summary of how the set was read: 8 records
/ 8 distinct books — 3 read in place from phase3-staging
(text layer, full extraction), 5 fetched from their
recorded source (the kyohwang/ChinaTextbook GitHub mirror
of the People's Education Press editions) after the
grab.py tree route returned HTTP 404; each fetched copy
verified by cover + contents against the record title
before reading. Filings are structural summaries in
English only.

China's own way, preserved whole, as filed: history-first
civics — the political system is taught as the outcome of
a staged history of social formations and of China's own
revolutionary and reform path, ending in the current
guiding thought and the national-rejuvenation goal.
Economic citizenship is taught inside the socialist market
economy frame — public ownership as the mainstay, the
market's decisive role balanced by government action, and
distribution/social security as the people's livelihood
guarantees. The political order is taught as a single
integrated whole — Party leadership, the people as
masters, and law-based governance presented as organically
unified, with the institutions (people's congresses,
multiparty cooperation, regional ethnic autonomy,
grassroots self-governance) each given a full lesson.
Marxist philosophy is taught as the citizen's worldview
and method, and culture as inheritance of fine traditional
Chinese culture, critical learning from foreign cultures,
and confidence in socialist culture. The world is taught
from China's standpoint — state forms compared,
multipolarisation and peace-and-development as the era's
themes, China's diplomacy and the community of a shared
future for mankind as the closing frame. Law is taught as
life law — the Civil Code in daily situations. Clear
thinking is taught as a citizen skill. And a single
capstone reader organises the whole subject around one
guiding thought — its origin in the new era, its goals,
the Party as leading force, the people as standpoint, the
five-sphere layout, the four-pronged strategy, national
security and diplomacy.
