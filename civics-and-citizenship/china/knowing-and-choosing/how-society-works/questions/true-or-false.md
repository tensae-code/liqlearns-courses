# How Society Works — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The essence of social history — social being determines social consciousness; the development of social history through its basic contradictions and reforms; the subject of social history — the masses of the people as makers of history.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that How Society Works — and that it has no connection to the rest of China's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside China's own course, in its own order.
