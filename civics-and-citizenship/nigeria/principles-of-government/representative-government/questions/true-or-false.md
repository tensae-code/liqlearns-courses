# Representative Government — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Representative government means citizens govern through people they choose, and political participation is how citizens take part.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that representative Government — and that it has no connection to the rest of Nigeria's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Nigeria's own course, in its own order.
