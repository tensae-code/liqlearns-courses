# Representative Government — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Representative government means citizens govern through people they choose, and political participation is how citizens take part.  b) it has nothing to do with how Nigeria governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Representative government means citizens govern through people they choose, and political participation is how citizens take part.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Representative government means citizens govern through people they choose, and political participation is how citizens take part.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Nigeria
**Answer: a) Representative government means citizens govern through people they choose, and political participation is how citizens take part.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
