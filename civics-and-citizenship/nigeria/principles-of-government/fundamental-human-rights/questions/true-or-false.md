# Fundamental Human Rights — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Fundamental human rights are the citizen's built-in protections, and access to information stands among them.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that fundamental Human Rights — and that it has no connection to the rest of Nigeria's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Nigeria's own course, in its own order.
