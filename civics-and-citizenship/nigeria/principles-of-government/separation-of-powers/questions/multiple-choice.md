# Separation of Powers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Separation of powers divides government work among the arms, with checks and balances so each arm can restrain the others.  b) it has nothing to do with how Nigeria governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Separation of powers divides government work among the arms, with checks and balances so each arm can restrain the others.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Separation of powers divides government work among the arms, with checks and balances so each arm can restrain the others.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Nigeria
**Answer: a) Separation of powers divides government work among the arms, with checks and balances so each arm can restrain the others.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
