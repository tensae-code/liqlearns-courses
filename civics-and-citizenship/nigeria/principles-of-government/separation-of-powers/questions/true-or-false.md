# Separation of Powers — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Separation of powers divides government work among the arms, with checks and balances so each arm can restrain the others.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that separation of Powers — and that it has no connection to the rest of Nigeria's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Nigeria's own course, in its own order.
