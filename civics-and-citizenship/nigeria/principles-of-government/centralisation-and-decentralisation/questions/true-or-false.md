# Centralisation and Decentralisation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Centralisation gathers power at the centre, decentralisation spreads it outward, and delegated legislation is law-making power lent to another body.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that centralisation and Decentralisation — and that it has no connection to the rest of Nigeria's story.
