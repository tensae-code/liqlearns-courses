# Centralisation and Decentralisation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Centralisation gathers power at the centre, decentralisation spreads it outward, and delegated legislation is law-making power lent to another body.  b) it has nothing to do with how Nigeria governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Centralisation gathers power at the centre, decentralisation spreads it outward, and delegated legislation is law-making power lent to another body.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

a) Centralisation gathers power at the centre, decentralisation spreads it outward, and delegated legislation is law-making power lent to another body.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Nigeria
**Answer: a) Centralisation gathers power at the centre, decentralisation spreads it outward, and delegated legislation is law-making power lent to another body.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
