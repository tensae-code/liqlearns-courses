# The Rule of Law — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The rule of law is the first principle limiting government: the law rules over everyone, rulers and ruled alike, and no one is above it.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that the Rule of Law — and that it has no connection to the rest of Nigeria's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Nigeria's own course, in its own order.
