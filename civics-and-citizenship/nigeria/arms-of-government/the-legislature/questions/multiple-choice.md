# The Legislature and Bills — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The legislature is the arm that makes law, and a bill moves through set stages in it before it becomes law.  b) it has nothing to do with how Nigeria governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The legislature is the arm that makes law, and a bill moves through set stages in it before it becomes law.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) The legislature is the arm that makes law, and a bill moves through set stages in it before it becomes law.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Nigeria
**Answer: a) The legislature is the arm that makes law, and a bill moves through set stages in it before it becomes law.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
