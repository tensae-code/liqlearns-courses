# The Executive — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The executive is the arm of government that carries out policy and runs the day-to-day administration of the state.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that the Executive — and that it has no connection to the rest of Nigeria's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Nigeria's own course, in its own order.
