# The Executive

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: the Executive. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Nigeria's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

The executive is the arm of government that carries out policy and runs the day-to-day administration of the state.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The executive is the arm of government that carries out policy and runs the day-to-day administration of the state. Give one real example from your own life or from the Nigeria you live in, and name the lesson's key words: the, executive. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: The executive is the arm of government that carries out policy and runs the day-to-day administration of the state.
