# The Judiciary — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The judiciary is the arm that interprets the law and settles disputes between citizens, and between citizens and the state.  b) it has nothing to do with how Nigeria governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The judiciary is the arm that interprets the law and settles disputes between citizens, and between citizens and the state.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) The judiciary is the arm that interprets the law and settles disputes between citizens, and between citizens and the state.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Nigeria
**Answer: a) The judiciary is the arm that interprets the law and settles disputes between citizens, and between citizens and the state.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
