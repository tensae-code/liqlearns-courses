# The Constitution — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A constitution is the body of rules that establishes a state, distributes its powers and limits what government may do.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that the Constitution — and that it has no connection to the rest of Nigeria's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Nigeria's own course, in its own order.
