# The Constitution — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) A constitution is the body of rules that establishes a state, distributes its powers and limits what government may do.  b) it has nothing to do with how Nigeria governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) A constitution is the body of rules that establishes a state, distributes its powers and limits what government may do.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) A constitution is the body of rules that establishes a state, distributes its powers and limits what government may do.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Nigeria
**Answer: a) A constitution is the body of rules that establishes a state, distributes its powers and limits what government may do.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
