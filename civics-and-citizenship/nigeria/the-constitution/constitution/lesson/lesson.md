# The Constitution

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: the Constitution. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Nigeria's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

A constitution is the body of rules that establishes a state, distributes its powers and limits what government may do.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: A constitution is the body of rules that establishes a state, distributes its powers and limits what government may do. Give one real example from your own life or from the Nigeria you live in, and name the lesson's key words: the, constitution. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: A constitution is the body of rules that establishes a state, distributes its powers and limits what government may do.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
