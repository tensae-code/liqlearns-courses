# Constitutionalism — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Constitutionalism means government itself is bound by the constitution — power is limited and exercised according to agreed rules.  b) it has nothing to do with how Nigeria governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Constitutionalism means government itself is bound by the constitution — power is limited and exercised according to agreed rules.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Constitutionalism means government itself is bound by the constitution — power is limited and exercised according to agreed rules.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Nigeria
**Answer: a) Constitutionalism means government itself is bound by the constitution — power is limited and exercised according to agreed rules.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
