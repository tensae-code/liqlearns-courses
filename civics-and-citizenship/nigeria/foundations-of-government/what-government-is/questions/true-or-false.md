# What Government Is — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Government is the machinery through which a state is ruled: this lesson teaches what government is, its characteristics and functions, and why government is studied as a subject.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that what Government Is — and that it has no connection to the rest of Nigeria's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Nigeria's own course, in its own order.
