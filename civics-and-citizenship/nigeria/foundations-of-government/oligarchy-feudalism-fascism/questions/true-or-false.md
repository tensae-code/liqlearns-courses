# Oligarchy, Feudalism, Fascism and Totalitarianism — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Oligarchy, feudalism, fascism and totalitarianism are forms of rule by the few or by force, where the many have no real say.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that oligarchy, Feudalism, Fascism and Totalitarianism — and that it has no connection to the rest of Nigeria's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Nigeria's own course, in its own order.
