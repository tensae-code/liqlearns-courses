# Oligarchy, Feudalism, Fascism and Totalitarianism

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: oligarchy, Feudalism, Fascism and Totalitarianism. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Nigeria's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Oligarchy, feudalism, fascism and totalitarianism are forms of rule by the few or by force, where the many have no real say.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Oligarchy, feudalism, fascism and totalitarianism are forms of rule by the few or by force, where the many have no real say. Give one real example from your own life or from the Nigeria you live in, and name the lesson's key words: oligarchy, feudalism. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: Oligarchy, feudalism, fascism and totalitarianism are forms of rule by the few or by force, where the many have no real say.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
