# Political Culture and Political Socialisation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Political culture is the pattern of attitudes a people hold toward politics, and political socialisation is how citizens are formed into those attitudes.  b) it has nothing to do with how Nigeria governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Political culture is the pattern of attitudes a people hold toward politics, and political socialisation is how citizens are formed into those attitudes.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

a) Political culture is the pattern of attitudes a people hold toward politics, and political socialisation is how citizens are formed into those attitudes.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Nigeria
**Answer: a) Political culture is the pattern of attitudes a people hold toward politics, and political socialisation is how citizens are formed into those attitudes.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
