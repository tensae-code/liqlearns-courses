# Power, Authority and Legitimacy — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Power, authority and legitimacy are the basic concepts that make rule accepted: power can compel, authority is power recognised as right, and legitimacy is the people's acceptance of that rule.
**Answer: True**
