# Power, Authority and Legitimacy — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** Power, authority and legitimacy are the basic concepts that make rule accepted: power can compel, authority is power recognised as right, and legitimacy is the people's acceptance of that rule.
