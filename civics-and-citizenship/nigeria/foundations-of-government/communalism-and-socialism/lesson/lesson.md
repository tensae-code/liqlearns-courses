# Communalism and Socialism

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: communalism and Socialism. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Nigeria's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Communalism and socialism are two ways of organising society and the economy, holding property and production in common in different degrees.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Communalism and socialism are two ways of organising society and the economy, holding property and production in common in different degrees. Give one real example from your own life or from the Nigeria you live in, and name the lesson's key words: communalism, and. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
