# Sovereignty — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Sovereignty is the state's supreme power — the final say over its own affairs — and this lesson introduces democracy alongside it.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that sovereignty — and that it has no connection to the rest of Nigeria's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Nigeria's own course, in its own order.
