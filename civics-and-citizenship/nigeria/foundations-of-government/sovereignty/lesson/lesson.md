# Sovereignty

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: sovereignty. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Nigeria's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Sovereignty is the state's supreme power — the final say over its own affairs — and this lesson introduces democracy alongside it.
