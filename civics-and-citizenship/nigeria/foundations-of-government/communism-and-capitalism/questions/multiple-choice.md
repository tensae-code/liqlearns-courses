# Communism and Capitalism — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Communism and capitalism are opposite economic-political systems: one holds the means of production in common, the other leaves them in private hands.  b) it has nothing to do with how Nigeria governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Communism and capitalism are opposite economic-political systems: one holds the means of production in common, the other leaves them in private hands.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
