# Political Parties

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: political Parties. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Nigeria's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## Sub lesson 1 — What a party is and how parties are organised

A political party is an organised group that seeks power to govern, and parties are organised in their own set ways.

## Sub lesson 2 — What parties do, and their types

Parties have functions in the state's life, and they come in different types.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: A political party is an organised group that seeks power to govern; parties have their own organisation, functions and types. Give one real example from your own life or from the Nigeria you live in, and name the lesson's key words: political, parties. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
