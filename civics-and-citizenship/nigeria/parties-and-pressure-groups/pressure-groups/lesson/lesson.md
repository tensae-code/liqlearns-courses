# Pressure Groups

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — What pressure groups are, and their types

A pressure group seeks to influence government without seeking to govern itself, and pressure groups come in different types.

## Sub lesson 2 — How pressure groups work — and how they differ from parties

Pressure groups have their own functions and modes of operation, and they differ from political parties, which seek power itself.
