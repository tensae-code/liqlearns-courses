# Party Systems and the Manifesto — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) A party system is the pattern of parties competing in a state, and a manifesto is a party's public promise of what it will do.  b) it has nothing to do with how Nigeria governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
