# Party Systems and the Manifesto

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: party Systems and the Manifesto. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Nigeria's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

A party system is the pattern of parties competing in a state, and a manifesto is a party's public promise of what it will do.
