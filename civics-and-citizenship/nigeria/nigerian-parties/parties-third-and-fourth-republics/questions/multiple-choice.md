# Parties in the Third and Fourth Republics — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The Third Republic (1989–1993) and the Fourth Republic from 1999 each had their own parties and party politics.  b) it has nothing to do with how Nigeria governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The Third Republic (1989–1993) and the Fourth Republic from 1999 each had their own parties and party politics.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

a) The Third Republic (1989–1993) and the Fourth Republic from 1999 each had their own parties and party politics.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Nigeria
**Answer: a) The Third Republic (1989–1993) and the Fourth Republic from 1999 each had their own parties and party politics.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
