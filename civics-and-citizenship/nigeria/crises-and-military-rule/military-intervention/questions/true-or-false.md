# Military Intervention in Politics — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Soldiers intervene in politics for reasons that can be named, and Nigeria's interventions came in a sequence that can be followed step by step.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that military Intervention in Politics — and that it has no connection to the rest of Nigeria's story.
