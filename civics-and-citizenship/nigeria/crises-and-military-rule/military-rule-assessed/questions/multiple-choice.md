# Military Rule Assessed — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Military governments in Nigeria had their own structure, their achievements and their failures, and intervention can be prevented.  b) it has nothing to do with how Nigeria governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Military governments in Nigeria had their own structure, their achievements and their failures, and intervention can be prevented.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Military governments in Nigeria had their own structure, their achievements and their failures, and intervention can be prevented.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Nigeria
**Answer: a) Military governments in Nigeria had their own structure, their achievements and their failures, and intervention can be prevented.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
