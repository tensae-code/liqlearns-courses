# Electoral Crises in Nigeria

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: electoral Crises in Nigeria. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Nigeria's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

The election crises of 1964, 1979, 1983 and 1993 each broke something in Nigeria's politics, and each teaches what an election must not become.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The election crises of 1964, 1979, 1983 and 1993 each broke something in Nigeria's politics, and each teaches what an election must not become. Give one real example from your own life or from the Nigeria you live in, and name the lesson's key words: electoral, crises. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
