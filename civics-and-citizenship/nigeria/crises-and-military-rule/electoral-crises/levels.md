# Electoral Crises in Nigeria — depth

This lesson is ONE lesson with no sub lessons: it teaches a single thing, whole. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: The election crises of 1964, 1979, 1983 and 1993 each broke something in Nigeria's politics, and each teaches what an election must not become. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
