# Military Government

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: military Government. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Nigeria's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Military government is rule by soldiers who take power by force, and it differs from civilian rule in how it governs and how it can be removed.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Military government is rule by soldiers who take power by force, and it differs from civilian rule in how it governs and how it can be removed. Give one real example from your own life or from the Nigeria you live in, and name the lesson's key words: military, government. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
