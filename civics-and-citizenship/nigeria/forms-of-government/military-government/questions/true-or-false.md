# Military Government — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Military government is rule by soldiers who take power by force, and it differs from civilian rule in how it governs and how it can be removed.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that military Government — and that it has no connection to the rest of Nigeria's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Nigeria's own course, in its own order.
