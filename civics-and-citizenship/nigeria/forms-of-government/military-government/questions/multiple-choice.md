# Military Government — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Military government is rule by soldiers who take power by force, and it differs from civilian rule in how it governs and how it can be removed.  b) it has nothing to do with how Nigeria governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Military government is rule by soldiers who take power by force, and it differs from civilian rule in how it governs and how it can be removed.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

a) Military government is rule by soldiers who take power by force, and it differs from civilian rule in how it governs and how it can be removed.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Nigeria
**Answer: a) Military government is rule by soldiers who take power by force, and it differs from civilian rule in how it governs and how it can be removed.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
