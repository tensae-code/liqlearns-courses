# Unitary Government — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** In a unitary form of government, all power sits at the centre, and local authorities exercise only what the centre gives them.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that unitary Government — and that it has no connection to the rest of Nigeria's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Nigeria's own course, in its own order.
