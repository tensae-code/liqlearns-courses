# The Presidential System

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: the Presidential System. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Nigeria's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

In the presidential system the executive is separately elected, holds a fixed term, and stands outside the legislature.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: In the presidential system the executive is separately elected, holds a fixed term, and stands outside the legislature. Give one real example from your own life or from the Nigeria you live in, and name the lesson's key words: the, presidential. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
