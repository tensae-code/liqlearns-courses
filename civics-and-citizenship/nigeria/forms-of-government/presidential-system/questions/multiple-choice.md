# The Presidential System — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) In the presidential system the executive is separately elected, holds a fixed term, and stands outside the legislature.  b) it has nothing to do with how Nigeria governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) In the presidential system the executive is separately elected, holds a fixed term, and stands outside the legislature.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) In the presidential system the executive is separately elected, holds a fixed term, and stands outside the legislature.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Nigeria
**Answer: a) In the presidential system the executive is separately elected, holds a fixed term, and stands outside the legislature.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
