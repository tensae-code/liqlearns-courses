# Federal Government — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Federalism divides power between a central government and the governments of the units, each supreme in its own sphere.  b) it has nothing to do with how Nigeria governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Federalism divides power between a central government and the governments of the units, each supreme in its own sphere.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Federalism divides power between a central government and the governments of the units, each supreme in its own sphere.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Nigeria
**Answer: a) Federalism divides power between a central government and the governments of the units, each supreme in its own sphere.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
