# Federal Government — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Federalism divides power between a central government and the governments of the units, each supreme in its own sphere.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that federal Government — and that it has no connection to the rest of Nigeria's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Nigeria's own course, in its own order.
