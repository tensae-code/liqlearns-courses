# The Parliamentary System — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) In the parliamentary or cabinet system the executive sits in the legislature and governs only while it keeps the legislature's confidence.  b) it has nothing to do with how Nigeria governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) In the parliamentary or cabinet system the executive sits in the legislature and governs only while it keeps the legislature's confidence.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

a) In the parliamentary or cabinet system the executive sits in the legislature and governs only while it keeps the legislature's confidence.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Nigeria
**Answer: a) In the parliamentary or cabinet system the executive sits in the legislature and governs only while it keeps the legislature's confidence.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
