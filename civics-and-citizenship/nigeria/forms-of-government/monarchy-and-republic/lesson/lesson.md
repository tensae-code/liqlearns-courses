# Monarchy and Republicanism

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: monarchy and Republicanism. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Nigeria's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Monarchy and republicanism are opposite answers to who heads the state: a hereditary ruler, or a head chosen by the people.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Monarchy and republicanism are opposite answers to who heads the state: a hereditary ruler, or a head chosen by the people. Give one real example from your own life or from the Nigeria you live in, and name the lesson's key words: monarchy, and. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
