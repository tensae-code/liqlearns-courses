# The Emergence of Federalism in Nigeria — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Federalism emerged in Nigeria because its size and diversity forced a divided bargain, built step by step through constitutional conferences.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that the Emergence of Federalism in Nigeria — and that it has no connection to the rest of Nigeria's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Nigeria's own course, in its own order.
