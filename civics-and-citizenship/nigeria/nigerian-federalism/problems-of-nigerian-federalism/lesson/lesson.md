# Problems of Nigerian Federalism

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: problems of Nigerian Federalism. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Nigeria's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Nigerian federalism carries standing problems: revenue allocation, minority issues, state creation, inter-ethnic rivalry and secession.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Nigerian federalism carries standing problems: revenue allocation, minority issues, state creation, inter-ethnic rivalry and secession. Give one real example from your own life or from the Nigeria you live in, and name the lesson's key words: problems, of. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
