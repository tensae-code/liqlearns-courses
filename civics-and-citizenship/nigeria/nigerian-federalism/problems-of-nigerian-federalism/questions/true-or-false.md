# Problems of Nigerian Federalism — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Nigerian federalism carries standing problems: revenue allocation, minority issues, state creation, inter-ethnic rivalry and secession.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that problems of Nigerian Federalism — and that it has no connection to the rest of Nigeria's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Nigeria's own course, in its own order.
