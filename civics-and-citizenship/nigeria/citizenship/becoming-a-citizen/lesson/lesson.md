# Becoming a Citizen

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: becoming a Citizen. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Nigeria's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## Sub lesson 1 — How citizenship is acquired

Citizenship is acquired in set ways — this part names the ways a person becomes a citizen.

## Sub lesson 2 — Types of citizenship

There are different types of citizenship a person can hold, and this part tells them apart.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Citizenship is acquired in set ways, and there are different types of citizenship a person can hold. Give one real example from your own life or from the Nigeria you live in, and name the lesson's key words: becoming, a. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
