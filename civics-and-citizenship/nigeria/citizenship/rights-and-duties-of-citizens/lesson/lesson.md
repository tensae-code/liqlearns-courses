# Rights and Duties of Citizens

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: rights and Duties of Citizens. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Nigeria's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## Sub lesson 1 — Rights, duties and obligations

A citizen holds rights, duties and obligations together: the rights protect the citizen, and the duties and obligations are what the citizen owes in return.

## Sub lesson 2 — The citizen and the alien

A citizen differs from an alien: the alien lives in the state without holding the citizen's full place in it.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: A citizen holds rights, duties and obligations together, and a citizen differs from an alien, who does not hold the citizen's full place in the state. Give one real example from your own life or from the Nigeria you live in, and name the lesson's key words: rights, and. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
