# OPEC and the United Nations

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: oPEC and the United Nations. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Nigeria's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## Sub lesson 1 — OPEC

OPEC is the organisation of the oil-exporting countries, and Nigeria belongs to it.

## Sub lesson 2 — The United Nations

The United Nations Organisation works worldwide, through its specialised agencies.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: OPEC organises the oil-exporting countries Nigeria belongs to, and the United Nations Organisation works worldwide through its specialised agencies. Give one real example from your own life or from the Nigeria you live in, and name the lesson's key words: opec, and. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: OPEC organises the oil-exporting countries Nigeria belongs to, and the United Nations Organisation works worldwide through its specialised agencies.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
