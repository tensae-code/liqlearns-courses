# Leadership and Followership — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Leadership has types and a choice to be made well, and followership is the citizen's counterpart to leadership.  b) it has nothing to do with how Nigeria governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Leadership has types and a choice to be made well, and followership is the citizen's counterpart to leadership.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Leadership has types and a choice to be made well, and followership is the citizen's counterpart to leadership.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Nigeria
**Answer: a) Leadership has types and a choice to be made well, and followership is the citizen's counterpart to leadership.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
