# Leadership and Followership — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Leadership has types and a choice to be made well, and followership is the citizen's counterpart to leadership.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that leadership and Followership — and that it has no connection to the rest of Nigeria's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Nigeria's own course, in its own order.
