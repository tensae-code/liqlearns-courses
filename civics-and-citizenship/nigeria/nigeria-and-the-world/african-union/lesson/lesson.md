# The OAU and the African Union

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: the OAU and the African Union. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Nigeria's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

The Organisation of African Unity was Africa's continental body, and the African Union is its successor.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The Organisation of African Unity was Africa's continental body, and the African Union is its successor. Give one real example from your own life or from the Nigeria you live in, and name the lesson's key words: the, oau. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
