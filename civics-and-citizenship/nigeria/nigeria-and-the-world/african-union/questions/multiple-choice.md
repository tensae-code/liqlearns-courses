# The OAU and the African Union — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The Organisation of African Unity was Africa's continental body, and the African Union is its successor.  b) it has nothing to do with how Nigeria governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The Organisation of African Unity was Africa's continental body, and the African Union is its successor.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) The Organisation of African Unity was Africa's continental body, and the African Union is its successor.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Nigeria
**Answer: a) The Organisation of African Unity was Africa's continental body, and the African Union is its successor.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
