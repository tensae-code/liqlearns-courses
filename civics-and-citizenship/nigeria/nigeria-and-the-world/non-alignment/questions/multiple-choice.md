# Non-Alignment — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Non-alignment means refusing to join either great-power bloc, and the Non-Aligned Movement has its own place in Nigeria's foreign policy.  b) it has nothing to do with how Nigeria governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Non-alignment means refusing to join either great-power bloc, and the Non-Aligned Movement has its own place in Nigeria's foreign policy.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**Answer: a) Non-alignment means refusing to join either great-power bloc, and the Non-Aligned Movement has its own place in Nigeria's foreign policy.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
