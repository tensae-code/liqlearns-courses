# Non-Alignment — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Non-alignment means refusing to join either great-power bloc, and the Non-Aligned Movement has its own place in Nigeria's foreign policy.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that non-Alignment — and that it has no connection to the rest of Nigeria's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Nigeria's own course, in its own order.
