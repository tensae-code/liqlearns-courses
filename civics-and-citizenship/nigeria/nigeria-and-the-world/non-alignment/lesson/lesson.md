# Non-Alignment

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: non-Alignment. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Nigeria's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Non-alignment means refusing to join either great-power bloc, and the Non-Aligned Movement has its own place in Nigeria's foreign policy.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Non-alignment means refusing to join either great-power bloc, and the Non-Aligned Movement has its own place in Nigeria's foreign policy. Give one real example from your own life or from the Nigeria you live in, and name the lesson's key words: non-alignment. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
