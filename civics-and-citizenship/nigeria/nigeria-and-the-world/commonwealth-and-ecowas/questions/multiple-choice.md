# The Commonwealth and ECOWAS — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The Commonwealth of Nations links Nigeria with its member states, and ECOWAS is Nigeria's own regional body in West Africa.  b) it has nothing to do with how Nigeria governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The Commonwealth of Nations links Nigeria with its member states, and ECOWAS is Nigeria's own regional body in West Africa.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

a) The Commonwealth of Nations links Nigeria with its member states, and ECOWAS is Nigeria's own regional body in West Africa.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Nigeria
**Answer: a) The Commonwealth of Nations links Nigeria with its member states, and ECOWAS is Nigeria's own regional body in West Africa.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
