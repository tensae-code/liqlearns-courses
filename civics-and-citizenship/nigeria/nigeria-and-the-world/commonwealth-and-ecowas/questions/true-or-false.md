# The Commonwealth and ECOWAS — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Commonwealth of Nations links Nigeria with its member states, and ECOWAS is Nigeria's own regional body in West Africa.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that the Commonwealth and ECOWAS — and that it has no connection to the rest of Nigeria's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Nigeria's own course, in its own order.
