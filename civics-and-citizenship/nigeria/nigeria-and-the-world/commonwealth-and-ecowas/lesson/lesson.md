# The Commonwealth and ECOWAS

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: the Commonwealth and ECOWAS. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Nigeria's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Commonwealth of Nations

The Commonwealth of Nations is the worldwide body that links Nigeria with its fellow member states.

## Sub lesson 2 — ECOWAS

ECOWAS is Nigeria's own regional body in West Africa.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The Commonwealth of Nations links Nigeria with its member states, and ECOWAS is Nigeria's own regional body in West Africa. Give one real example from your own life or from the Nigeria you live in, and name the lesson's key words: the, commonwealth. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
