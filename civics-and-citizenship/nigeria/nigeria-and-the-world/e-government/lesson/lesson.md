# E-Government

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: e-Government. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Nigeria's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

E-government is government carried on electronically, with its own operations, advantages and disadvantages in Nigeria.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: E-government is government carried on electronically, with its own operations, advantages and disadvantages in Nigeria. Give one real example from your own life or from the Nigeria you live in, and name the lesson's key words: e-government. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
