# E-Government — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) E-government is government carried on electronically, with its own operations, advantages and disadvantages in Nigeria.  b) it has nothing to do with how Nigeria governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) E-government is government carried on electronically, with its own operations, advantages and disadvantages in Nigeria.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) E-government is government carried on electronically, with its own operations, advantages and disadvantages in Nigeria.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Nigeria
**Answer: a) E-government is government carried on electronically, with its own operations, advantages and disadvantages in Nigeria.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
