# E-Government — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** E-government is government carried on electronically, with its own operations, advantages and disadvantages in Nigeria.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that e-Government — and that it has no connection to the rest of Nigeria's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Nigeria's own course, in its own order.
