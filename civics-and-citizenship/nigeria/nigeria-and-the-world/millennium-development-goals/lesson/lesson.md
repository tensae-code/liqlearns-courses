# The Millennium Development Goals

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: the Millennium Development Goals. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Nigeria's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

The Millennium Development Goals were the world's shared targets to 2015 — why they were needed, and what they achieved.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The Millennium Development Goals were the world's shared targets to 2015 — why they were needed, and what they achieved. Give one real example from your own life or from the Nigeria you live in, and name the lesson's key words: the, millennium. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
