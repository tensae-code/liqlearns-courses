# The Millennium Development Goals — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Millennium Development Goals were the world's shared targets to 2015 — why they were needed, and what they achieved.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that the Millennium Development Goals — and that it has no connection to the rest of Nigeria's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Nigeria's own course, in its own order.
