# Finding My Interests — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The search for one's personal interests.  b) it has nothing to do with how El Salvador governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The search for one's personal interests.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) The search for one's personal interests.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in El Salvador
**Answer: a) The search for one's personal interests.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
