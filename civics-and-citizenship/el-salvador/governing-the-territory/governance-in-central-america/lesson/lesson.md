# Governing the Territory in Central America

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Governing the Territory in Central America. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what El Salvador's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Governance of the Central American territory — who decides and how.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Governance of the Central American territory — who decides and how. Give one real example from your own life or from the El Salvador you live in, and name the lesson's key words: governing, the, territory. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
