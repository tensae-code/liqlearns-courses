# Democracy and the Rule of Law — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Democracy, the rule of law and what they imply for society.  b) it has nothing to do with how El Salvador governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Democracy, the rule of law and what they imply for society.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Democracy, the rule of law and what they imply for society.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in El Salvador
**Answer: a) Democracy, the rule of law and what they imply for society.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
