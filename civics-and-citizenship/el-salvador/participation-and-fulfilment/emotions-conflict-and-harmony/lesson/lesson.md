# Emotions, Conflict Resolution and Living in Harmony

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Emotions, Conflict Resolution and Living in Harmony. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what El Salvador's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## Sub lesson 1 — Managing emotions

Managing one's emotions, the inner work of this lesson. Managing emotions for conflict resolution and harmonious coexistence.

## Sub lesson 2 — Conflict resolution and harmonious coexistence

Using that management to resolve conflicts and live in harmony (convivencia armónica).

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Managing emotions for conflict resolution and harmonious coexistence. Give one real example from your own life or from the El Salvador you live in, and name the lesson's key words: emotions, conflict, resolution. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
