# Water and the Governments That Guard It

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Water and the Governments That Guard It. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what El Salvador's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## Sub lesson 1 — Water resources

Water resources (recursos hídricos) — the shared resource at the centre of this lesson. Water resources and the role of local, national and regional governments in guarding them.

## Sub lesson 2 — The governments that guard water

The role of local, national and regional governments in guarding water resources.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Water resources and the role of local, national and regional governments in guarding them. Give one real example from your own life or from the El Salvador you live in, and name the lesson's key words: water, and, the. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: Water resources and the role of local, national and regional governments in guarding them.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
