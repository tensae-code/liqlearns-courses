# Civics and Citizenship — El Salvador course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

Civics belongs to the country: this course is El Salvador's
own — built from El Salvador's *Ciudadanía y Valores*
(Citizenship and Values) student books for Grades 5, 6, 7,
8, 9 and 1.° año de bachillerato (Grade 10), all in
Spanish. Inside the course the organisation is by
**levels, not grades**: the titles below ARE the levels'
titles, in path order — what the learner proves, step by
step. The grades the books come from live only in each
lesson's sources.

The path's own logic is El Salvador's, from the filing's
ladder sketch: the series' own grade order is the ladder;
every book repeats the same arc — territory and history
first, citizenship and rights last — at rising depth. So
Level 1 (Grade 5) opens with governing the territory, then
participation week by week (interests, emotions, conflict,
diversity, intercultural coexistence, local and national
participation), and closes with children's rights, road,
environmental and digital citizenship. Level 2 (Grade 6)
climbs the same arc one turn higher, Level 3 (Grade 7)
turns to interculturality, rights mechanisms and public
spaces, Level 4 (Grade 8) adds the political organisation
of territory and women's rights in history, Level 5
(Grade 9) turns adult — rights defended, governance
without borders, El Salvador's own tax system — and
Level 6 (bachillerato) crowns the ladder with the
democratic system whole and personal fulfilment. That
ladder IS the path order (recommended, never forced — a
learner can jump in higher; the "start here instead"
hints point down this ladder).

Four unit folders hold lessons at two levels, because the
books themselves reuse the same unit names at rising
depth: `governing-the-territory/` (Levels 1–2),
`rights-in-history/` (Levels 4–5),
`interculturality-and-rights/` (Levels 3 and 5) and
`digital-and-environmental-citizenship/` (Levels 4–5).
Each lesson's own level is stated in its lesson plan and
levels file. One coined lesson name is used by two books —
`digital-and-environmental-citizenship/environmental-citizenship`
(Grade 8 and Grade 9) — and was deduped into ONE lesson at
Level 4 with one sub lesson per book (see ABOUT.md).
