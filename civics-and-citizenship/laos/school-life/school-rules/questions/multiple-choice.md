# School Rules — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The school's rules and why they bind everyone.  b) it has nothing to do with how Laos governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The school's rules and why they bind everyone.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) The school's rules and why they bind everyone.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Laos
**Answer: a) The school's rules and why they bind everyone.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
