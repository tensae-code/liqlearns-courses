Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Taking part properly in the movements and activities of school life; taking part in school activities; taking part properly in school life.  b) it has nothing to do with how Laos governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Taking part properly in the movements and activities of school life; taking part in school activities; taking part properly in school life.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**Answer: a) Taking part properly in the movements and activities of school life; taking part in school activities; taking part properly in school life.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
