# I Am a Grade 1 Pupil — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The new pupil's identity and place; the new pupil's identity and place, taught in pictures — going to school and greeting the teacher.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that I Am a Grade 1 Pupil — and that it has no connection to the rest of Laos's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Laos's own course, in its own order.
