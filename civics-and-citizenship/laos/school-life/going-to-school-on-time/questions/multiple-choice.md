# Going to School on Time — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Punctuality from the first year.  b) it has nothing to do with how Laos governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Punctuality from the first year.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Punctuality from the first year.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Laos
**Answer: a) Punctuality from the first year.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
