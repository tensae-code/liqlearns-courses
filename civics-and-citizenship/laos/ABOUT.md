# About the Civics and Citizenship — Laos course

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in
this course comes from government textbooks — this country's
own ຄຸນສົມບັດສຶກສາ (Moral Education) and
ສຶກສາພົນລະເມືອງ (Civic Education) books, including the
Moral Education part of its combined Moral Education / World
Around Us volumes. The one exception is the **Tip boxes**: a
tip in a box is from us, the people making LiqLearns. Tips
are ours; the lessons are the books'.

## How this course is organised

This is ONE course for Laos inside the Civics and
Citizenship folder (one course per country). Inside it, the
organisation is by **levels, not grades**: a learner's place
is found by what they prove, and each level stands on the
ones before it as a recommended-first path — never a forced
gate. The school grades the books come from appear only in
each lesson's `lesson/sources.md`, as provenance.

The course as built has **six stocked levels** (Levels
1–6), from the filing for this build
(`laos-placements.md` — 30 records in the group targets,
Laos share: 24 books read and 6 records UNREAD-pending).
422 placements in all, stocking 244 lessons — the coined
lesson names were deduped before building (see
CURRICULUM.md, "Where the build stands"): every placement
stocks into exactly one lesson, and every placement's
provenance row is in that lesson's `lesson/sources.md`.

**How the books were read, exactly as filed:** the books
are Lao-language; the PDFs carry legacy-font text layers
that do not extract, so reads were visual — cover,
preface/introduction, full contents, and lesson openings
(objectives + content summaries) read as page images, except
where a header states the text layer extracts. Teacher
guides are placed as TEACHER evidence of what is taught,
per the filing. Every book's verdict states exactly how far
its read went; all 24 verdicts are PARTIAL, so placements
are at lesson level from the verified contents, exactly as
filed.

**What the filing does and does not contain, stated
plainly:** the filing contains 24 book headers and 422
placement lines, an UNREAD-pending section naming 6
records, and a closing ladder sketch. It contains no
COMPLETE verdict — every book is PARTIAL, as filed above.
It names no staged-pending file for Laos beyond the
phantom-in-tree / phantom-push fetch notes carried in the
book headers themselves (see "Gaps, labels and partial
reads" below). Anything not stated in the filing is not
claimed here.
