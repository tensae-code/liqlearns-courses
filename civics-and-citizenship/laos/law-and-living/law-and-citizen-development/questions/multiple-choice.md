# Law and Citizen Development — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) How law forms and develops the citizen.  b) it has nothing to do with how Laos governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) How law forms and develops the citizen.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) How law forms and develops the citizen.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Laos
**Answer: a) How law forms and develops the citizen.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
