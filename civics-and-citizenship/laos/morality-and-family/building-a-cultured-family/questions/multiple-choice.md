# Building a Cultured Family — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The family built on culture and good conduct.  b) it has nothing to do with how Laos governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The family built on culture and good conduct.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) The family built on culture and good conduct.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Laos
**Answer: a) The family built on culture and good conduct.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
