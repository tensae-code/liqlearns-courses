# Saving Money and Things — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Thrift with money and belongings.  b) it has nothing to do with how Laos governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Thrift with money and belongings.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Thrift with money and belongings.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Laos
**Answer: a) Thrift with money and belongings.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
