# Diligence and Endurance — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Effort that endures.  b) it has nothing to do with how Laos governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Effort that endures.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Effort that endures.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Laos
**Answer: a) Effort that endures.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
