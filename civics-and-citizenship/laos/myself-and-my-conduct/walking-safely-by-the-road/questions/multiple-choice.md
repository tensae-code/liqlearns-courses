# Walking Safely by the Road — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) A child walks on the correct side and edge of the road; the right way to walk the road.  b) it has nothing to do with how Laos governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) A child walks on the correct side and edge of the road; the right way to walk the road.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) A child walks on the correct side and edge of the road; the right way to walk the road.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Laos
**Answer: a) A child walks on the correct side and edge of the road; the right way to walk the road.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
