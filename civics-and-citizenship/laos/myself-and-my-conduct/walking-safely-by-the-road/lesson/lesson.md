# Walking Safely by the Road

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Walking Safely by the Road. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Laos's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## Walking correctly along the road edge

A child walks on the correct side and edge of the road. Do it now: name one place in your own home, school or town where you can see this, and say what happens there.

## Walking along the road correctly

The right way to walk the road. Do it now: name one place in your own home, school or town where you can see this, and say what happens there.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: A child walks on the correct side and edge of the road; the right way to walk the road. Give one real example from your own life or from the Laos you live in, and name the lesson's key words: walking safely by the road. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: A child walks on the correct side and edge of the road; the right way to walk the road.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
