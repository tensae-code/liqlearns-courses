Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?

**Answer: a) Admitting fault and turning.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Admitting fault and turning.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Laos
**Answer: a) Admitting fault and turning.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
