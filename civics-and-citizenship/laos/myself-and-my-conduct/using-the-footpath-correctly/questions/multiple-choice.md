# Using the Footpath Correctly — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Walking the right way on the path.  b) it has nothing to do with how Laos governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Walking the right way on the path.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Walking the right way on the path.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Laos
**Answer: a) Walking the right way on the path.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
