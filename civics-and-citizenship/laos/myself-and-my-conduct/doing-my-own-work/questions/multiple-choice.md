# Doing My Own Work — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) A child does their own tasks themselves instead of leaving them to others; doing one's own work oneself; a child does their own tasks themselves, shown in classroom and kitchen pictures.  b) it has nothing to do with how Laos governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) A child does their own tasks themselves instead of leaving them to others; doing one's own work oneself; a child does their own tasks themselves, shown in classroom and kitchen pictures.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) A child does their own tasks themselves instead of leaving them to others; doing one's own work oneself; a child does their own tasks themselves, shown in classroom and kitchen pictures.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Laos
**Answer: a) A child does their own tasks themselves instead of leaving them to others; doing one's own work oneself; a child does their own tasks themselves, shown in classroom and kitchen pictures.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
