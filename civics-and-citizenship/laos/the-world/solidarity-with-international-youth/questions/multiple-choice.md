# Solidarity with International Youth — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Friendship with young people of other countries.  b) it has nothing to do with how Laos governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Friendship with young people of other countries.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Friendship with young people of other countries.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Laos
**Answer: a) Friendship with young people of other countries.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
