# Local Governing Authority — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Who governs the locality and by what right.  b) it has nothing to do with how Laos governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Who governs the locality and by what right.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Who governs the locality and by what right.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Laos
**Answer: a) Who governs the locality and by what right.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
