# Village Administration — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The village's own governing body and the respect owed to it.  b) it has nothing to do with how Laos governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The village's own governing body and the respect owed to it.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) The village's own governing body and the respect owed to it.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Laos
**Answer: a) The village's own governing body and the respect owed to it.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
