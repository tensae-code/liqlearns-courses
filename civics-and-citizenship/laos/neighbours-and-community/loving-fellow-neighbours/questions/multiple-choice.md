# Loving Fellow Neighbours — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Love for the neighbours of one's own village.  b) it has nothing to do with how Laos governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Love for the neighbours of one's own village.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Love for the neighbours of one's own village.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Laos
**Answer: a) Love for the neighbours of one's own village.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
