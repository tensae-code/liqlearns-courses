# Politeness and Neatness — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Courteous, orderly bearing.  b) it has nothing to do with how Laos governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Courteous, orderly bearing.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Courteous, orderly bearing.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Laos
**Answer: a) Courteous, orderly bearing.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
