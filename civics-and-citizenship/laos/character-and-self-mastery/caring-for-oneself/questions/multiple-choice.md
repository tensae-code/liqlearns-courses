# Caring for Oneself — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Looking after one's own person.  b) it has nothing to do with how Laos governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Looking after one's own person.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Looking after one's own person.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Laos
**Answer: a) Looking after one's own person.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
