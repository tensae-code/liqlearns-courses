# Honesty to Self and Others — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Truthfulness inward and outward.  b) it has nothing to do with how Laos governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Truthfulness inward and outward.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Truthfulness inward and outward.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Laos
**Answer: a) Truthfulness inward and outward.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
