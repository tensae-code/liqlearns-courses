# The Value of Living — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) A life has value when it is lived with moral quality and within the law.  b) it has nothing to do with how Laos governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) A life has value when it is lived with moral quality and within the law.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) A life has value when it is lived with moral quality and within the law.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Laos
**Answer: a) A life has value when it is lived with moral quality and within the law.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
