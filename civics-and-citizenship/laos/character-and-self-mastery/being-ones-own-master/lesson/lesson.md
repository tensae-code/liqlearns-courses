# Being Ones Own Master

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Being Ones Own Master. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Laos's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## Being one's own master

Self-mastery shown in study, in work and in daily life, and how to judge it in oneself and others. Do it now: name one place in your own home, school or town where you can see this, and say what happens there.
