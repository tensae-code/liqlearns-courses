# Simple Living — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Living simply as a virtue, modelled on President Souphanouvong weaving his own cloth.  b) it has nothing to do with how Laos governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Living simply as a virtue, modelled on President Souphanouvong weaving his own cloth.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Living simply as a virtue, modelled on President Souphanouvong weaving his own cloth.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Laos
**Answer: a) Living simply as a virtue, modelled on President Souphanouvong weaving his own cloth.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
