# Self Confidence

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Self Confidence. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Laos's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Trusting one's own capacity.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Trusting one's own capacity. Give one real example from your own life or from the Laos you live in, and name the lesson's key words: self confidence. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: Trusting one's own capacity.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
