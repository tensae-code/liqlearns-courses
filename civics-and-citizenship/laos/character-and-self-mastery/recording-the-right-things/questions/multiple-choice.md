# Recording the Right Things — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Keeping account of right conduct — telling the right things from the wrong in daily life, at school, at home and in society.  b) it has nothing to do with how Laos governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Keeping account of right conduct — telling the right things from the wrong in daily life, at school, at home and in society.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Keeping account of right conduct — telling the right things from the wrong in daily life, at school, at home and in society.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Laos
**Answer: a) Keeping account of right conduct — telling the right things from the wrong in daily life, at school, at home and in society.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
