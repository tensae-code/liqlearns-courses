# Recording the Right Things

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Recording the Right Things. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Laos's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## Recording the right things

Keeping account of right conduct — telling the right things from the wrong in daily life, at school, at home and in society. Do it now: name one place in your own home, school or town where you can see this, and say what happens there.
