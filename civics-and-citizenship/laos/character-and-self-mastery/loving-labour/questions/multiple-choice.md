# Loving Labour — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Love of work.  b) it has nothing to do with how Laos governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Love of work.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Love of work.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Laos
**Answer: a) Love of work.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
