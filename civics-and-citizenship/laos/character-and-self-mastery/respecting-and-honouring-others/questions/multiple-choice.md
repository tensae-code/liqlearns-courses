# Respecting and Honouring Others — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Respect and honour given to other people.  b) it has nothing to do with how Laos governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Respect and honour given to other people.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Respect and honour given to other people.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Laos
**Answer: a) Respect and honour given to other people.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
