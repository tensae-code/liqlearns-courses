# Human Relations — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Relating well to other people.  b) it has nothing to do with how Laos governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Relating well to other people.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Relating well to other people.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Laos
**Answer: a) Relating well to other people.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
