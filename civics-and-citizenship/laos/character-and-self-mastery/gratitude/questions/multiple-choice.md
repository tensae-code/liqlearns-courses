# Gratitude — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Knowing and returning goodness.  b) it has nothing to do with how Laos governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Knowing and returning goodness.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Knowing and returning goodness.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Laos
**Answer: a) Knowing and returning goodness.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
