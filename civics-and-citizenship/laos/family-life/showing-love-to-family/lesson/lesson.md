# Showing Love to Family

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Showing Love to Family. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Laos's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## Showing love to family members

Love shown, not only felt. Do it now: name one place in your own home, school or town where you can see this, and say what happens there.

## Showing love to family members

Love expressed, not only felt. Do it now: name one place in your own home, school or town where you can see this, and say what happens there.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Love shown, not only felt; love expressed, not only felt. Give one real example from your own life or from the Laos you live in, and name the lesson's key words: showing love to family. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: Love shown, not only felt; love expressed, not only felt.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
