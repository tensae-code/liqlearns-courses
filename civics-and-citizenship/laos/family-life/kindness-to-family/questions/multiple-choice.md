# Kindness to Family — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Gentle kindness to those at home.  b) it has nothing to do with how Laos governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Gentle kindness to those at home.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Gentle kindness to those at home.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Laos
**Answer: a) Gentle kindness to those at home.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
