# Gratitude to Parents and Grandparents — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Thankfulness across three generations.  b) it has nothing to do with how Laos governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Thankfulness across three generations.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?

**Answer: a) Thankfulness across three generations.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
