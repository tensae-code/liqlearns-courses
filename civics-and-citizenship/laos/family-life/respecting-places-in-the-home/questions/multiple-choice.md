# Respecting Places in the Home — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Treating the home's places with respect.  b) it has nothing to do with how Laos governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Treating the home's places with respect.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Treating the home's places with respect.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Laos
**Answer: a) Treating the home's places with respect.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
