# Saving Learning Things Helps Family — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Thrift with school things as help to the household.  b) it has nothing to do with how Laos governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Thrift with school things as help to the household.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?

**Answer: a) Thrift with school things as help to the household.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
