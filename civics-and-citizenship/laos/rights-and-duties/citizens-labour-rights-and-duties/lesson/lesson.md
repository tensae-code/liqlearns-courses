# Citizens' Labour Rights and Duties

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Citizens' Labour Rights and Duties. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Laos's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

The rights a citizen holds at work and the duties that go with them.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The rights a citizen holds at work and the duties that go with them. Give one real example from your own life or from the Laos you live in, and name the lesson's key words: citizens labour rights and duties. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
