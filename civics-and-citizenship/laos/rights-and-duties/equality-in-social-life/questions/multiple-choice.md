# Equality in Social Life — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Equal rights across the fields of social life.  b) it has nothing to do with how Laos governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Equal rights across the fields of social life.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Equal rights across the fields of social life.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Laos
**Answer: a) Equal rights across the fields of social life.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
