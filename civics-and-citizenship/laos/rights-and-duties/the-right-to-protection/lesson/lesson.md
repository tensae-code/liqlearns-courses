# The Right to Protection

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: The Right to Protection. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Laos's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Give one real example from your own life or from the Laos you live in, and name the lesson's key words: the right to protection. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
