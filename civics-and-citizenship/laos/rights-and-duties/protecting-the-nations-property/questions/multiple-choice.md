# Protecting the Nation's Property — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The nation's wealth is held in common and guarded by every citizen.  b) it has nothing to do with how Laos governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The nation's wealth is held in common and guarded by every citizen.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) The nation's wealth is held in common and guarded by every citizen.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Laos
**Answer: a) The nation's wealth is held in common and guarded by every citizen.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
