# Mission of Lao Youth — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The mission of Lao youth in developing the republic and bringing it up to date.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that Mission of Lao Youth — and that it has no connection to the rest of Laos's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Laos's own course, in its own order.
