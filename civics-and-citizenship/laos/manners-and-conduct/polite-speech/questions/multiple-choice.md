# Polite Speech — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Courteous words in daily talk.  b) it has nothing to do with how Laos governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Courteous words in daily talk.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Courteous words in daily talk.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Laos
**Answer: a) Courteous words in daily talk.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
