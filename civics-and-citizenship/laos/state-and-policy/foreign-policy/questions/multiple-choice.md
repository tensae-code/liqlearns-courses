# Foreign Policy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) How Laos conducts itself toward other countries.  b) it has nothing to do with how Laos governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) How Laos conducts itself toward other countries.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) How Laos conducts itself toward other countries.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Laos
**Answer: a) How Laos conducts itself toward other countries.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
