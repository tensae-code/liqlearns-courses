# Sharing Things with Friends — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?

**Answer: a) Sharing directed to friends; dividing one's things.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**Answer: a) Sharing directed to friends; dividing one's things.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
