# Behaviour in Public Places — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) In public places, moral norms mean order and consideration among strangers — queuing, quiet where quiet is due, care for public things, help for those who need it.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) The norms walk ends at the widest everyday field: the public place, where the child's conduct touches people outside every group he belongs to.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
