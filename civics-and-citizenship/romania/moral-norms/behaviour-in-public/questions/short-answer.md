# Behaviour in Public Places — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** In public places, moral norms mean order and consideration among strangers — queuing, quiet where quiet is due, care for public things, help for those who need it.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** The norms walk ends at the widest everyday field: the public place, where the child's conduct touches people outside every group he belongs to.
