# Behaviour in Public Places — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** In public places, moral norms mean order and consideration among strangers — queuing, quiet where quiet is due, care for public things, help for those who need it.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a child's own home, school or community in Romania.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Romania and lived there.
