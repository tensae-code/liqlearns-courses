# Video script — Behaviour in Public Places

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
In public places, moral norms mean order and consideration among strangers — queuing, quiet where quiet is due, care for public things, help for those who need it.

Public places — streets, transport, shops, parks, cinemas — are shared with strangers, and norms make sharing possible: queue and wait your turn, keep quiet where quiet is due, give seats to those who need them, keep public things clean and unbroken, cross and travel by the rules. Consideration for people you will never meet is citizenship in its everyday clothes.

The norms walk ends at the widest everyday field: the public place, where the child's conduct touches people outside every group he belongs to.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
