# Video script — Behaviour in the Family

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Everyday moral life begins in the family: respect for parents and elders, help with the home's work, truth and kindness among its members.

The first field of moral norms is the family. Behaviour in the family means: respect and gratitude toward parents and elders, help with the work of the home, truth spoken inside it, kindness among brothers and sisters, and care when someone is ill or tired. The family is where norms are first learned — and where they are practised every single day.

The book walks the norms through life's places in widening order — family first, as Grade 3's groups began with the family too.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
