# Hands-on — Behaviour in the Family

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Do one piece of your home's work today before being asked, and speak one word of thanks to whoever carries the home most. Note how the family answers.

## Show it with real things
Take this lesson into one real act this week: do, make, visit, ask or help in a way that puts Behaviour in the Family into practice in your own community in Romania — then write what happened and what the lesson would say about it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
