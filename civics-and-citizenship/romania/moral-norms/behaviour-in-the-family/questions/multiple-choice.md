# Behaviour in the Family — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) Everyday moral life begins in the family: respect for parents and elders, help with the home's work, truth and kindness among its members.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) The book walks the norms through life's places in widening order — family first, as Grade 3's groups began with the family too.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
