# Behaviour at School — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) At school, moral norms mean respect for teachers and classmates, honesty in work, care for the school's things and keeping its rules.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) School norms join the two earlier circles: the care for things of Grade 3 and the learning group, now as the everyday conduct of the pupil-citizen.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
