# Behaviour at School — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** At school, moral norms mean respect for teachers and classmates, honesty in work, care for the school's things and keeping its rules.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a child's own home, school or community in Romania.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Romania and lived there.
