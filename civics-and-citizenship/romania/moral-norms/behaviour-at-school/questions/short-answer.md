# Behaviour at School — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** At school, moral norms mean respect for teachers and classmates, honesty in work, care for the school's things and keeping its rules.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** School norms join the two earlier circles: the care for things of Grade 3 and the learning group, now as the everyday conduct of the pupil-citizen.
