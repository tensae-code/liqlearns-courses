# Video script — What Moral Norms Do

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Moral norms are the rules of conduct that values give rise to — they tell us, case by case, how to behave, and they hold common life together.

Values become conduct through moral norms: the unwritten and written rules that say, in each kind of situation, what is to be done — greet, queue, tell the truth, help, keep quiet here, speak up there. Norms are values made practical. Without them, common life would collide at every step; with them, strangers can live together in trust and order.

This unit is the hinge of the Grade 4 book: values (Unit 3) become norms (Unit 4), and norms are then walked through the places of everyday life — family, school, friends, public places.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
