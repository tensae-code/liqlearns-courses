# What Moral Norms Do — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Moral norms are the rules of conduct that values give rise to — they tell us, case by case, how to behave, and they hold common life together.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** This unit is the hinge of the Grade 4 book: values (Unit 3) become norms (Unit 4), and norms are then walked through the places of everyday life — family, school, friends, public places.
