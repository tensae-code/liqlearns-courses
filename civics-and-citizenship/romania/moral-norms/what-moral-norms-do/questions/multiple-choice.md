# What Moral Norms Do — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) Moral norms are the rules of conduct that values give rise to — they tell us, case by case, how to behave, and they hold common life together.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) This unit is the hinge of the Grade 4 book: values (Unit 3) become norms (Unit 4), and norms are then walked through the places of everyday life — family, school, friends, public places.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
