# Video script — Prosocial and Antisocial Behaviour

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Behaviour that helps society is prosocial; behaviour that harms it is antisocial — and behaviour can change, by will, practice and help.

Conduct has a social measure: prosocial behaviour helps — cooperating, helping, protecting, building; antisocial behaviour harms — aggression, destruction, deceit, exclusion. The book's hopeful close to the unit: behaviour can change. A person who sees his antisocial acts, wills otherwise, practises the better way and accepts help can become prosocial — no one is sentenced to his worst acts.

Change is the unit's last word on norms: norms are not only for judging others — they are the path by which one's own behaviour is remade.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
