# Prosocial and Antisocial Behaviour — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Behaviour that helps society is prosocial; behaviour that harms it is antisocial — and behaviour can change, by will, practice and help.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Change is the unit's last word on norms: norms are not only for judging others — they are the path by which one's own behaviour is remade.
