# Video script — Behaviour with Friends

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Among friends, moral norms mean loyalty, truth, fairness in play and help in need — friendship kept by conduct, not only by feeling.

Friendship lives by norms: be loyal — stand by your friend; be truthful — no tales behind backs; be fair — in games, in turns, in sharing; help in need — when a friend is stuck, sad or in trouble. A friend who breaks these norms breaks the friendship itself, slowly or at once. Good conduct among friends is how a child first practises faithfulness.

Friends were met in Grade 3 as a small group; here the group gets its moral rule-book, written as everyday behaviour.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
