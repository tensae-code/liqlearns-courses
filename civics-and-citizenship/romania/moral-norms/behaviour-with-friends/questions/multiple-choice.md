# Behaviour with Friends — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) Among friends, moral norms mean loyalty, truth, fairness in play and help in need — friendship kept by conduct, not only by feeling.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Friends were met in Grade 3 as a small group; here the group gets its moral rule-book, written as everyday behaviour.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
