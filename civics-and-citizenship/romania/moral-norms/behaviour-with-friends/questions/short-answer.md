# Behaviour with Friends — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Among friends, moral norms mean loyalty, truth, fairness in play and help in need — friendship kept by conduct, not only by feeling.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Friends were met in Grade 3 as a small group; here the group gets its moral rule-book, written as everyday behaviour.
