# Video script — Rights and Responsibilities

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Rights stand together with responsibilities — my rights end where another's begin, and holding rights means answering for how I use them.

The course closes by pairing its two great words: rights and responsibilities. My rights are real, and so are everyone else's: my freedom ends where another's rights begin. Holding rights brings responsibilities — to respect others' rights, to use my own well, to do my duties at home, at school and in the community. The citizen the two books have formed holds both together, and that is where the course ends.

Grade 3 ended with rights and duties inside small groups; Grade 4 ends with rights and responsibilities at the scale of all children — the same pairing, at the widest circle, closing the pair of books.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
