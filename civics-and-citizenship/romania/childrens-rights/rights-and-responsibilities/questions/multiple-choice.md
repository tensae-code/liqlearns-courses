# Rights and Responsibilities — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) Rights stand together with responsibilities — my rights end where another's begin, and holding rights means answering for how I use them.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Grade 3 ended with rights and duties inside small groups; Grade 4 ends with rights and responsibilities at the scale of all children — the same pairing, at the widest circle, closing the pair of books.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
