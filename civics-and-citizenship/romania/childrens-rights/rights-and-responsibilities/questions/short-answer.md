# Rights and Responsibilities — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Rights stand together with responsibilities — my rights end where another's begin, and holding rights means answering for how I use them.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Grade 3 ended with rights and duties inside small groups; Grade 4 ends with rights and responsibilities at the scale of all children — the same pairing, at the widest circle, closing the pair of books.
