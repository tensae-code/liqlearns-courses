# All Children Have Rights — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) Children's rights are universal — they belong to every child, everywhere, without distinction.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) From 'I have rights' to 'all children have rights' — the widening circle one last time, now to every child on earth.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
