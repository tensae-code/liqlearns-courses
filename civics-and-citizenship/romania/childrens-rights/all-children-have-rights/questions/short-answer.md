# All Children Have Rights — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Children's rights are universal — they belong to every child, everywhere, without distinction.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** From 'I have rights' to 'all children have rights' — the widening circle one last time, now to every child on earth.
