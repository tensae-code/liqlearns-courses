# Video script — All Children Have Rights

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Children's rights are universal — they belong to every child, everywhere, without distinction.

The rights of the child are universal: they belong to all children — every country, every language, rich or poor, girl or boy, well or ill. No child holds more rights than another, and no child can be left outside them. Universality is what makes them rights of the child, not privileges of some children. The book teaches the child to see every other child as a fellow holder of the same rights.

From 'I have rights' to 'all children have rights' — the widening circle one last time, now to every child on earth.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
