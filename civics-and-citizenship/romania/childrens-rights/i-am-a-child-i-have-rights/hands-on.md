# Hands-on — I Am a Child, I Have Rights

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name three rights you hold as a child. For each, name who must respect it — family, school, the state — and one sign that it is respected in your life.

## Show it with real things
Take this lesson into one real act this week: do, make, visit, ask or help in a way that puts I Am a Child, I Have Rights into practice in your own community in Romania — then write what happened and what the lesson would say about it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
