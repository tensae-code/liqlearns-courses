# I Am a Child, I Have Rights — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) A child is a holder of rights — being a child means certain rights belong to you simply because you are a child.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Rights arrive last in the two books' climb, as their capstone: the person formed through traits, relations, belonging, values and norms now stands as a holder of rights.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
