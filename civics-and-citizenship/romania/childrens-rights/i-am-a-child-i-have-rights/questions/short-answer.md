# I Am a Child, I Have Rights — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** A child is a holder of rights — being a child means certain rights belong to you simply because you are a child.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Rights arrive last in the two books' climb, as their capstone: the person formed through traits, relations, belonging, values and norms now stands as a holder of rights.
