# I Am a Child, I Have Rights — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A child is a holder of rights — being a child means certain rights belong to you simply because you are a child.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a child's own home, school or community in Romania.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Romania and lived there.
