# My Country — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** My country — Romania — is the wider home that holds all its localities and all its people together.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** The widening circles of the two books meet here: person, things, nature, groups, locality — and now the country itself, the circle the rest of the course will fill in.
