# My Country — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) My country — Romania — is the wider home that holds all its localities and all its people together.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) The widening circles of the two books meet here: person, things, nature, groups, locality — and now the country itself, the circle the rest of the course will fill in.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
