# Lesson plan — My Country

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Romania · **Title:** Belonging · **Lesson:** My Country
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — Belonging, Moral Values and Norms, and the Rights of the Child (CURRICULUM.md).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: My country — Romania — is the wider home that holds all its localities and all its people together.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street, school or community in Romania.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the learner's own life in Romania and the book's own teaching, as Romania's own books teach civics in widening circles: the person first — moral traits taught as pairs of opposites — then relations with things, with animals and plants, and with other people; then belonging, moral values and norms in everyday life, closing with the universal rights of the child.
