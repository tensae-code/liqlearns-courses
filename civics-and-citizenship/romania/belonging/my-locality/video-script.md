# Video script — My Locality

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
My locality is the place I belong to first — my town or village — and knowing and valuing it is the start of belonging.

The Grade 4 book widens the circle to belonging, and belonging starts near: my locality — the town or village where I live. To know your locality is to know its places, its people, its life; to value it is to care for it as your own first wider home. The citizen's love of country grows from this small, known ground.

Grade 4's Unit 1 revises the Grade 3 book (named, not re-placed); the new climb opens here, with the locality — the largest place a young child can still know street by street.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
