# My Locality — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** My locality is the place I belong to first — my town or village — and knowing and valuing it is the start of belonging.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a child's own home, school or community in Romania.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Romania and lived there.
