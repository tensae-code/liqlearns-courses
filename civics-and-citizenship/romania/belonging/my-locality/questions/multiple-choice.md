# My Locality — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) My locality is the place I belong to first — my town or village — and knowing and valuing it is the start of belonging.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Grade 4's Unit 1 revises the Grade 3 book (named, not re-placed); the new climb opens here, with the locality — the largest place a young child can still know street by street.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
