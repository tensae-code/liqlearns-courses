# My Locality — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** My locality is the place I belong to first — my town or village — and knowing and valuing it is the start of belonging.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Grade 4's Unit 1 revises the Grade 3 book (named, not re-placed); the new climb opens here, with the locality — the largest place a young child can still know street by street.
