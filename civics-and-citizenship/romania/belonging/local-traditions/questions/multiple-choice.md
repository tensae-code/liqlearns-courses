# Local Traditions — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) Local traditions are the customs a community hands down — feasts, crafts, songs and ways — and they bind the locality together.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Traditions are the locality's memory; the book places them right after the locality itself, because a place is its people and their handed-down life, not only its streets.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
