# Local Traditions — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Local traditions are the customs a community hands down — feasts, crafts, songs and ways — and they bind the locality together.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Traditions are the locality's memory; the book places them right after the locality itself, because a place is its people and their handed-down life, not only its streets.
