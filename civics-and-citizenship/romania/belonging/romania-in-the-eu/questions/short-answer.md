# Romania in the European Union — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Romania is a member of the European Union — belonging to the country and belonging to the Union stand together.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** The belonging unit closes with the widest circle the book draws: the country placed among countries, in the Union it chose to join.
