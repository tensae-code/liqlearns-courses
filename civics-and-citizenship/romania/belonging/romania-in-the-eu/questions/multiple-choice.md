# Romania in the European Union — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) Romania is a member of the European Union — belonging to the country and belonging to the Union stand together.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) The belonging unit closes with the widest circle the book draws: the country placed among countries, in the Union it chose to join.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
