# Romania in the European Union

## Do it first

Name one thing that Romania's membership of the European Union makes possible for its people — travel, study, work, trade. Say how it connects your locality to other countries.

## What the book teaches

Romania's belonging does not stop at its borders: Romania is a member of the European Union, a union of European countries that work together — travelling, trading, studying and deciding many things in common. A Romanian is also a European citizen. The two belongings stand together: the country inside the Union, as the locality stands inside the country.

## The lesson in full

The belonging unit closes with the widest circle the book draws: the country placed among countries, in the Union it chose to join.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Romania is a member of the European Union — belonging to the country and belonging to the Union stand together. Give one real example from your own life in Romania, and name the lesson's key words: European Union, membership, European citizen, belonging. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Romania's own books teach civics in widening circles: the person first — moral traits taught as pairs of opposites — then relations with things, with animals and plants, and with other people; then belonging, moral values and norms in everyday life, closing with the universal rights of the child. When a lesson asks you to look at your own life and your own place, really look: your own life is this course's first text.
