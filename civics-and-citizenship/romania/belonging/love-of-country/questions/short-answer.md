# Love of Country — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Love of country is a civic feeling — caring for Romania, being proud of its good, and wishing and working for its betterment.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Feeling follows knowledge in the book's order: locality, traditions, country, symbols — and then the love that all that knowledge is meant to grow.
