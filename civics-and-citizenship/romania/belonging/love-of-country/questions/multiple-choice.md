# Love of Country — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) Love of country is a civic feeling — caring for Romania, being proud of its good, and wishing and working for its betterment.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Feeling follows knowledge in the book's order: locality, traditions, country, symbols — and then the love that all that knowledge is meant to grow.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
