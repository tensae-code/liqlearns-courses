# Video script — The Country's Symbols

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The country's insignia — its flag, its anthem and its coat of arms — stand for Romania, and citizens treat them with respect.

A country is recognised by its insignia: the flag, with its colours; the national anthem, sung at solemn moments; and the coat of arms, borne on official acts and buildings. These symbols stand for the whole country and everyone in it. Citizens treat them with respect — standing for the anthem, honouring the flag — because respecting the symbol is respecting the country it stands for.

Symbols make the wide circle visible: a child cannot see a whole country, but he can see its flag, hear its anthem, and recognise its arms.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
