# The Country's Symbols — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** The country's insignia — its flag, its anthem and its coat of arms — stand for Romania, and citizens treat them with respect.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Symbols make the wide circle visible: a child cannot see a whole country, but he can see its flag, hear its anthem, and recognise its arms.
