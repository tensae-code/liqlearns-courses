# The Country's Symbols — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The country's insignia — its flag, its anthem and its coat of arms — stand for Romania, and citizens treat them with respect.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a child's own home, school or community in Romania.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Romania and lived there.
