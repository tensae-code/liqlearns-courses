# The Country's Symbols — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) The country's insignia — its flag, its anthem and its coat of arms — stand for Romania, and citizens treat them with respect.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Symbols make the wide circle visible: a child cannot see a whole country, but he can see its flag, hear its anthem, and recognise its arms.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
