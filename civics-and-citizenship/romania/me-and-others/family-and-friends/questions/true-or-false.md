# Family and Friends — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The first small groups we belong to are the family and the group of friends, and each has its own relations inside it.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a child's own home, school or community in Romania.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Romania and lived there.
