# Family and Friends — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) The first small groups we belong to are the family and the group of friends, and each has its own relations inside it.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Groups come before rules in the book's order: first belong, know the group from inside — then, two lessons later, learn its rules, rights and duties.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
