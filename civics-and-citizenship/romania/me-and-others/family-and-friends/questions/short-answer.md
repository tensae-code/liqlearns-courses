# Family and Friends — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** The first small groups we belong to are the family and the group of friends, and each has its own relations inside it.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Groups come before rules in the book's order: first belong, know the group from inside — then, two lessons later, learn its rules, rights and duties.
