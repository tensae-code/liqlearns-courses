# Hands-on — Group Rules, Rights and Duties

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Take one group you belong to. Write one right its members have, one duty they carry, and one rule that protects both. Check your three against the group's real life.

## Show it with real things
Take this lesson into one real act this week: do, make, visit, ask or help in a way that puts Group Rules, Rights and Duties into practice in your own community in Romania — then write what happened and what the lesson would say about it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
