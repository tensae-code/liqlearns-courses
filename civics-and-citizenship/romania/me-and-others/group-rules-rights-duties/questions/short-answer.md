# Group Rules, Rights and Duties — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Small groups live by rules, and inside them every member holds both rights and duties.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** The Grade 3 book ends at the citizen's foundation: rules, rights and duties inside the small groups a child knows — the same pattern the Grade 4 book will widen to the country and to all children.
