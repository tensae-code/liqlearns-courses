# Group Rules, Rights and Duties — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) Small groups live by rules, and inside them every member holds both rights and duties.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) The Grade 3 book ends at the citizen's foundation: rules, rights and duties inside the small groups a child knows — the same pattern the Grade 4 book will widen to the country and to all children.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
