# Video script — Group Rules, Rights and Duties

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Small groups live by rules, and inside them every member holds both rights and duties.

A group that lasts has rules — agreed ways of behaving that protect everyone in it. Inside the group's rules, each member has rights: to be heard, to be treated fairly, to take part. And each member has duties: to keep the rules, to do their share, to respect the others' rights. Rights and duties come as a pair — that pairing is the seed of all citizenship.

The Grade 3 book ends at the citizen's foundation: rules, rights and duties inside the small groups a child knows — the same pattern the Grade 4 book will widen to the country and to all children.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
