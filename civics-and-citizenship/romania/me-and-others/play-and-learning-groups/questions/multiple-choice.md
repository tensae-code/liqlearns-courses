# Play Groups and Learning Groups — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) The play group and the learning group are small groups with their own ways of relating — fair play in one, help and patience in the other.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Play and learning are the child's two public worlds; the book teaches that each world works only when its members relate rightly inside it.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
