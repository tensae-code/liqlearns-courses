# Play Groups and Learning Groups — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** The play group and the learning group are small groups with their own ways of relating — fair play in one, help and patience in the other.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Play and learning are the child's two public worlds; the book teaches that each world works only when its members relate rightly inside it.
