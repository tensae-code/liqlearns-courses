# Video script — Play Groups and Learning Groups

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The play group and the learning group are small groups with their own ways of relating — fair play in one, help and patience in the other.

A child also belongs to a play group and a learning group. The play group holds together by fair play: taking turns, keeping the game's rules, including everyone. The learning group — the class, the study circle — holds together by help: explaining, waiting, sharing what you understood. Each group asks its members for relations that fit its purpose.

Play and learning are the child's two public worlds; the book teaches that each world works only when its members relate rightly inside it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
