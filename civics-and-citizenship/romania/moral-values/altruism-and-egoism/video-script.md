# Video script — Altruism and Egoism

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Altruism puts others' good alongside or before one's own; egoism thinks only of oneself — the pair decides how a person shares life with others.

Altruism is care for others' good: sharing, helping, giving time and things, sometimes putting another's need first. Egoism is its opposite: only oneself matters, others are tools or obstacles. A community can only live on altruism — families, classes and villages run on people who give. The book teaches the child to recognise both, in stories and in himself.

Altruism is where the values unit turns outward: good and evil weighed, then the self measured against others.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
