# Altruism and Egoism — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — altruism puts others' good alongside or before one's own; egoism thinks only of oneself — the pair decides how a person shares life with others — in their own words, give one real example from their own life in Romania, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or community situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
