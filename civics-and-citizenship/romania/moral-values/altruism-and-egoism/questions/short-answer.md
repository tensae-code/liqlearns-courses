# Altruism and Egoism — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Altruism puts others' good alongside or before one's own; egoism thinks only of oneself — the pair decides how a person shares life with others.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Altruism is where the values unit turns outward: good and evil weighed, then the self measured against others.
