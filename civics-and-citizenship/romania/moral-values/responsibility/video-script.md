# Video script — Responsibility

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Responsibility answers for one's own acts and duties — against irresponsibility, which shrugs and blames others.

Responsibility means answering: for your acts, your words, your duties and their consequences. The responsible person does what is theirs to do, admits mistakes, and repairs what they broke. Irresponsibility shrugs — it was not me, it does not matter, someone else will do it. Every trust ever given — a task, a pet, a group, one day a country — rests on responsibility.

Responsibility closes the values unit because it is the value that carries all the others into action: a value you will not answer for is only a word.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
