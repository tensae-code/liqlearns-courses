# Honesty — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) Honesty says and does what is true — in words, in work and in dealings — against dishonesty, which deceives.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Grade 3 taught sincerity, truth in speech; Grade 4 widens it to honesty, truth in all dealings — the same virtue at the wider circle.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
