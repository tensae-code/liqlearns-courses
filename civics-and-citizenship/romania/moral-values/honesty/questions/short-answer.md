# Honesty — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Honesty says and does what is true — in words, in work and in dealings — against dishonesty, which deceives.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Grade 3 taught sincerity, truth in speech; Grade 4 widens it to honesty, truth in all dealings — the same virtue at the wider circle.
