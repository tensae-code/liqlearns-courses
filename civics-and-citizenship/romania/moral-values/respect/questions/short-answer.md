# Respect — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Respect honours every person's worth — against disrespect — and it is owed to all, not only to the powerful.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** The filing itself marks this lesson as respect deepened from Grade 3 — the ladder climbing the same virtue a second time, at the wider circle.
