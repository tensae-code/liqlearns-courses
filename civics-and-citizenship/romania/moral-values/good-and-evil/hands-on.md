# Hands-on — Good and Evil

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Take one story you know well. Point to the good acts and the evil acts in it, and say what each one built or broke. Then weigh one act of your own the same way.

## Show it with real things
Take this lesson into one real act this week: do, make, visit, ask or help in a way that puts Good and Evil into practice in your own community in Romania — then write what happened and what the lesson would say about it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
