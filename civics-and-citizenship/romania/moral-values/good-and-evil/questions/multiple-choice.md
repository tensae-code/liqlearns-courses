# Good and Evil — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) Good and evil are the basic moral pair — good builds and helps, evil harms and destroys, and every act can be weighed between them.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) The pair-method of the two books reaches its root here: all the earlier pairs — kindness, honesty, respect — are branches of this one.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
