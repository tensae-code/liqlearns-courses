# Good and Evil — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Good and evil are the basic moral pair — good builds and helps, evil harms and destroys, and every act can be weighed between them.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** The pair-method of the two books reaches its root here: all the earlier pairs — kindness, honesty, respect — are branches of this one.
