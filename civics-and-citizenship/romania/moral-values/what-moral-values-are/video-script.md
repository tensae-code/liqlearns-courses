# Video script — What Moral Values Are

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Moral values are the goods a person and a community hold as right and worthy — they guide how we judge and how we act.

After belonging, the book turns to moral values: the goods held as right and worthy — good itself, honesty, respect, responsibility and the rest of this unit. A value guides twice: it helps us judge acts (this is good, that is bad) and it steers our own conduct. Values are learned from family, school and community, and they show in what a person actually does.

Grade 3 taught single traits as pairs of opposites; Grade 4 now gathers them under their proper name — values — before the next unit turns them into norms for everyday life.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
