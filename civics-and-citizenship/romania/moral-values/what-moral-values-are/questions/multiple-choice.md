# What Moral Values Are — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) Moral values are the goods a person and a community hold as right and worthy — they guide how we judge and how we act.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Grade 3 taught single traits as pairs of opposites; Grade 4 now gathers them under their proper name — values — before the next unit turns them into norms for everyday life.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
