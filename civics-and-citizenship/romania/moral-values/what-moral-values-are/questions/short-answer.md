# What Moral Values Are — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Moral values are the goods a person and a community hold as right and worthy — they guide how we judge and how we act.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Grade 3 taught single traits as pairs of opposites; Grade 4 now gathers them under their proper name — values — before the next unit turns them into norms for everyday life.
