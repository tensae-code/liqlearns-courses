# Modesty and Trust — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) Modesty and trust — in oneself and in others — stand against showing off and against distrust.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) This closes the book's first unit: the person's moral traits, each one a pair of opposites — kindness, courage, respect, sincerity, modesty and trust, each against its shadow.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
