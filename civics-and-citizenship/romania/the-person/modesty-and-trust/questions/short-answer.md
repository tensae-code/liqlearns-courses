# Modesty and Trust — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Modesty and trust — in oneself and in others — stand against showing off and against distrust.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** This closes the book's first unit: the person's moral traits, each one a pair of opposites — kindness, courage, respect, sincerity, modesty and trust, each against its shadow.
