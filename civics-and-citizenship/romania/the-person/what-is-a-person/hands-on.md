# Hands-on — What Is a Person

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Look at the people around you — at home, in class — and say of each one, quietly: a person, like me. Then say what makes you a person: what you think, feel and choose. Keep your list; the lesson names what you found.

## Show it with real things
Take this lesson into one real act this week: do, make, visit, ask or help in a way that puts What Is a Person into practice in your own community in Romania — then write what happened and what the lesson would say about it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
