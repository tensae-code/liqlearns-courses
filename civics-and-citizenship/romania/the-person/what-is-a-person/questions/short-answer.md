# What Is a Person — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** A person is oneself and every other human being — civic education begins by knowing what a person is, starting with your own person.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** The book opens the whole course here: before things, before animals, before groups, the child meets the person — first himself, then every other. Everything later in the course is built on this one recognition.
