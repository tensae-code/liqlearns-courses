# What Is a Person — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) A person is oneself and every other human being — civic education begins by knowing what a person is, starting with your own person.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) The book opens the whole course here: before things, before animals, before groups, the child meets the person — first himself, then every other. Everything later in the course is built on this one recognition.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
