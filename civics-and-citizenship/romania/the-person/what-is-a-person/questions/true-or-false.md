# What Is a Person — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A person is oneself and every other human being — civic education begins by knowing what a person is, starting with your own person.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a child's own home, school or community in Romania.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Romania and lived there.
