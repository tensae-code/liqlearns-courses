# Respect and Sincerity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) Respect against disrespect and sincerity against insincerity are moral traits a person practises in daily conduct.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Like every trait in this book, respect and sincerity are taught against their opposites, so the learner can recognise disrespect and insincerity when they appear — in others and in himself.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
