# Respect and Sincerity — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Respect against disrespect and sincerity against insincerity are moral traits a person practises in daily conduct.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Like every trait in this book, respect and sincerity are taught against their opposites, so the learner can recognise disrespect and insincerity when they appear — in others and in himself.
