# Video script — Respect and Sincerity

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Respect against disrespect and sincerity against insincerity are moral traits a person practises in daily conduct.

Respect treats every person as someone who matters — listening, greeting, taking care not to offend. Its opposite, disrespect, ignores and belittles. Sincerity says what is true and means what it says; insincerity hides and pretends. Both traits are practised in small daily acts, at home and at school, and other people feel them at once.

Like every trait in this book, respect and sincerity are taught against their opposites, so the learner can recognise disrespect and insincerity when they appear — in others and in himself.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
