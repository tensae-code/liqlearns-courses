# Video script — Kindness and Courage

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Moral traits come in pairs of opposites, and a person chooses: kindness against unkindness, courage against cowardice.

A person's character shows in moral traits — the qualities that make conduct good or bad. The book teaches them as pairs: kindness or unkindness, courage or cowardice. Kindness treats others gently and helps; unkindness hurts. Courage does the right thing even when it is hard or frightening; cowardice gives up. The traits are choices, made again in every situation.

Romania's own way runs through this whole book: every trait is taught as a pair of opposites, through stories and situations, so the child learns to name both sides and choose the good one.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
