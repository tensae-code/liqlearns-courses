# Kindness and Courage — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Moral traits come in pairs of opposites, and a person chooses: kindness against unkindness, courage against cowardice.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Romania's own way runs through this whole book: every trait is taught as a pair of opposites, through stories and situations, so the child learns to name both sides and choose the good one.
