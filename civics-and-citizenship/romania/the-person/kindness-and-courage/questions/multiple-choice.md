# Kindness and Courage — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) Moral traits come in pairs of opposites, and a person chooses: kindness against unkindness, courage against cowardice.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Romania's own way runs through this whole book: every trait is taught as a pair of opposites, through stories and situations, so the child learns to name both sides and choose the good one.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
