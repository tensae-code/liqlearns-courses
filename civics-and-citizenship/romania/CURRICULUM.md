# Civics and Citizenship — Romania course — CURRICULUM (the path)

One course for Romania inside the Civics and Citizenship
folder, organised by **levels, not grades**: a learner
climbs when they have proved the level, not when a school
calendar says so. The school grades the books come from
(Grades 3 and 4) appear only in each lesson's sources
(provenance), never as the course's organisation. The
path follows Romania's own books' order: primary civics
starts from the person and moves outward in widening
circles of relation — me as a person with moral traits,
then my relations with things, then with animals and
plants, then with other people — and the second book
widens the circle from the person to belonging — my
locality, its traditions, my country and its symbols,
Romania in the European Union — then to moral values and
moral norms in everyday life (family, school, friends,
public places), and ends with the universal rights of
the child. That ladder IS the placements file's ladder
sketch: the Grade 3 book sits at Level 1 and the Grade 4
book at Level 2.

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything
in this course comes from government textbooks — this
country's own Educație Civică (Civic Education) student
books (for the Ministerul Educației) — except the **Tip
boxes**: a tip in a box is from us, the people making
LiqLearns. Tips are ours; the lessons are the books'.
See ABOUT.md for the full book list and the acceptance
reckoning.

## The levels (the path, in order)

1. **Level 1 — The Person and My Relations** — what a
   person is; moral traits taught as pairs of opposites
   (kindness or unkindness, courage or cowardice; respect
   or disrespect, sincerity or insincerity; modesty or
   lack of modesty, trust or distrust); then relations
   in widening circles: with things (what things are,
   the things that express me, property and care), with
   animals and plants (what they are, attention and
   care, gentleness and respect, compassion), and with
   other people (family and friends, play and learning
   groups, group rules with rights and duties)
   (`the-person/`, `me-and-things/`, `me-and-nature/`,
   `me-and-others/`). 14 lessons.
2. **Level 2 — Belonging, Moral Values and Norms, and
   the Rights of the Child** — belonging (my locality,
   local traditions, my country, the country's symbols,
   love of country, Romania in the European Union); then
   moral values (what they are, good and evil, altruism
   and egoism, honesty, respect, responsibility); then
   moral norms in everyday life (what moral norms do,
   behaviour in the family, at school, with friends, in
   public places, prosocial and antisocial behaviour and
   changing behaviour); closing with the universal
   rights of the child (I am a child, I have rights; all
   children have rights; rights and responsibilities)
   (`belonging/`, `moral-values/`, `moral-norms/`,
   `childrens-rights/`). 21 lessons.

**Totals: 35 lessons, 35 placements stocked** (Level 1:
14 · Level 2: 21 — all from two student books:
*Educație Civică* for Grades 3 and 4 (Aramis Print,
2021, and Grup Editorial Litera, year not printed on
the pages read, both for the Ministerul Educației,
Romanian, both COMPLETE as filed); see ABOUT.md).

*Standing note on the count: the two books' 35
placements coin 35 distinct lesson names — no name is
coined twice — so no dedupe merge arises. 35 placements,
35 lessons, every placement stocked exactly once.*

  every lesson stands on Romania's *Educație Civică*

  PDFs via their Wayback captures, as filed; this build
