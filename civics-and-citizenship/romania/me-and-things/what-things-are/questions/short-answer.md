# What Things Are — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Things are the objects people make and use, and people need things to live, learn, play and work.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** The widening circle of the Grade 3 book moves one step out: from the person himself to the nearest world he handles every day — the world of things.
