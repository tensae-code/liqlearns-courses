# What Things Are — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) Things are the objects people make and use, and people need things to live, learn, play and work.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) The widening circle of the Grade 3 book moves one step out: from the person himself to the nearest world he handles every day — the world of things.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
