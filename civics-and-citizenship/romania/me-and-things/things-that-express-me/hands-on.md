# Hands-on — The Things That Express Me

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Choose three of your things that express you most. Say what each one says about you. Then look at a friend's things and guess what they express — and ask if you guessed right.

## Show it with real things
Take this lesson into one real act this week: do, make, visit, ask or help in a way that puts The Things That Express Me into practice in your own community in Romania — then write what happened and what the lesson would say about it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
