# The Things That Express Me — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Some things express the person who owns them — clothes, toys and games, books, collections, and one's own room.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** The book reads a child's own world — the wardrobe, the toy box, the bookshelf, the collection, the room — as the child's first self-portrait.
