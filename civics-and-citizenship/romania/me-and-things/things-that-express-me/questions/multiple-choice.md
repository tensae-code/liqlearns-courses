# The Things That Express Me — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) Some things express the person who owns them — clothes, toys and games, books, collections, and one's own room.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) The book reads a child's own world — the wardrobe, the toy box, the bookshelf, the collection, the room — as the child's first self-portrait.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
