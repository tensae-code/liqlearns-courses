# Video script — The Things That Express Me

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Some things express the person who owns them — clothes, toys and games, books, collections, and one's own room.

Some things are more than useful: they express the person. The clothes you choose, your toys and games, the books you keep, the collection you build, your own room arranged your way — each one says something about who you are and what you love. Other people's things express them the same way, which is why their things deserve respect too.

The book reads a child's own world — the wardrobe, the toy box, the bookshelf, the collection, the room — as the child's first self-portrait.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
