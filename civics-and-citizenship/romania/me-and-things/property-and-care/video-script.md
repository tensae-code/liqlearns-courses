# Video script — Property and Care for Things

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Things belong to someone — property — and the right attitude toward things is care, against negligence.

Things belong to people: that belonging is property — mine, yours, the family's, the school's, everyone's. Because things are needed and express their owners, the right attitude toward them is care: use them properly, keep them, repair them. Its opposite is negligence — carelessness that breaks, loses and wastes what someone needs.

Care for things is where the young citizen's responsibility first becomes visible: in what his hands do to the objects of daily life, his own and other people's.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
