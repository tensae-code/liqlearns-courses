# Hands-on — Property and Care for Things

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Pick one thing of yours that you have neglected — a book, a toy, a tool — and care for it now: clean it, mend it, put it in its place. Then do the same for one thing that belongs to everyone at school.

## Show it with real things
Take this lesson into one real act this week: do, make, visit, ask or help in a way that puts Property and Care for Things into practice in your own community in Romania — then write what happened and what the lesson would say about it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
