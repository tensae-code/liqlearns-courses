# Property and Care for Things — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) Things belong to someone — property — and the right attitude toward things is care, against negligence.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Care for things is where the young citizen's responsibility first becomes visible: in what his hands do to the objects of daily life, his own and other people's.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
