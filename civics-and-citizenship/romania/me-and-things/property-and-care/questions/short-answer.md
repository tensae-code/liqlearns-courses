# Property and Care for Things — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Things belong to someone — property — and the right attitude toward things is care, against negligence.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Care for things is where the young citizen's responsibility first becomes visible: in what his hands do to the objects of daily life, his own and other people's.
