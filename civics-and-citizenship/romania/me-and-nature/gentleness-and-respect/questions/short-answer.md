# Gentleness and Respect for Living Things — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Living things are to be treated with gentleness and respect, against brutality and disrespect.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** The same word — respect — that the first unit taught between people returns here between the child and living things: one virtue, widening circles.
