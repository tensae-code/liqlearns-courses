# What Animals and Plants Are — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Animals and plants are living beings, and people need them — for food, for work, for the life of the world around us.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a child's own home, school or community in Romania.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Romania and lived there.
