# What Animals and Plants Are — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Animals and plants are living beings, and people need them — for food, for work, for the life of the world around us.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** This opens the book's third unit: our relations with animals and plants, taught with the same pairs-of-opposites method as the person's own traits.
