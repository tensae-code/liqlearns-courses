# What Animals and Plants Are — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) Animals and plants are living beings, and people need them — for food, for work, for the life of the world around us.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) This opens the book's third unit: our relations with animals and plants, taught with the same pairs-of-opposites method as the person's own traits.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
