# Video script — What Animals and Plants Are

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Animals and plants are living beings, and people need them — for food, for work, for the life of the world around us.

The circle widens again: from things to living beings. Animals and plants are alive — they grow, they feel, they can suffer and die. People need them: plants give food, shade, wood and clean air; animals give food, help and companionship. Because they are living and needed, how we treat them matters.

This opens the book's third unit: our relations with animals and plants, taught with the same pairs-of-opposites method as the person's own traits.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
