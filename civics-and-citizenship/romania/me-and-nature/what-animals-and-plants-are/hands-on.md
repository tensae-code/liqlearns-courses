# Hands-on — What Animals and Plants Are

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name the animals and plants you met today — at home, on the way, near school. For each one, say what it gives people, and what people owe it.

## Show it with real things
Take this lesson into one real act this week: do, make, visit, ask or help in a way that puts What Animals and Plants Are into practice in your own community in Romania — then write what happened and what the lesson would say about it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
