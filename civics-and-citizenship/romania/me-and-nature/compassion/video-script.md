# Video script — Compassion for Animals and Plants

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Compassion, love and courage govern our relations with animals and plants — against lack of compassion, hatred and fear.

Compassion feels with a living being that suffers — the hurt animal, the wilting plant — and moves to help. Love cares for living things as goods in themselves. Courage faces the animal or the situation that frightens us calmly, without cruelty and without panic. Their opposites — lack of compassion, hatred and fear — close the heart or turn it against the living world.

This closes the nature unit at its deepest point: not only hands that care, but a heart that feels with living things — the bridge from nature to other people, the book's final unit.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
