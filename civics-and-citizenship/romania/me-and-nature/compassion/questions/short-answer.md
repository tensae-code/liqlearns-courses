# Compassion for Animals and Plants — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Compassion, love and courage govern our relations with animals and plants — against lack of compassion, hatred and fear.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** This closes the nature unit at its deepest point: not only hands that care, but a heart that feels with living things — the bridge from nature to other people, the book's final unit.
