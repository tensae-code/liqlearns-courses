# Hands-on — Compassion for Animals and Plants

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Think of an animal or plant you have seen suffering or struggling. Say what it needed and what compassion would do. If you can do it today, do it.

## Show it with real things
Take this lesson into one real act this week: do, make, visit, ask or help in a way that puts Compassion for Animals and Plants into practice in your own community in Romania — then write what happened and what the lesson would say about it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
