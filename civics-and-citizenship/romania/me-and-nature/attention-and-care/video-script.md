# Video script — Attention and Care for Animals and Plants

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Toward plants and animals the right attitudes are attention and care, against inattention and indifference.

Living beings near us need attention: noticing the dry plant, the hungry animal, the bird in winter. Attention becomes care — watering, feeding, sheltering, protecting. The opposites are inattention, which does not see, and indifference, which sees and does nothing. The book teaches the child to see, and then to act.

Attention first, then care: the order itself is the teaching — most harm to living things begins not in cruelty but in not noticing.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
