# Attention and Care for Animals and Plants — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Toward plants and animals the right attitudes are attention and care, against inattention and indifference.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Attention first, then care: the order itself is the teaching — most harm to living things begins not in cruelty but in not noticing.
