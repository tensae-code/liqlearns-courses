# Attention and Care for Animals and Plants — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) Toward plants and animals the right attitudes are attention and care, against inattention and indifference.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Attention first, then care: the order itself is the teaching — most harm to living things begins not in cruelty but in not noticing.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
