# About the Civics and Citizenship — France course

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in this
course comes from government textbooks — this country's own
Enseignement moral et civique (EMC) book for the Lycée. The one
exception is the **Tip boxes**: a tip in a box is from us, the
people making LiqLearns. Tips are ours; the lessons are the
books'.

## How this course is organised

This is ONE course for France inside the Civics and Citizenship
folder (one course per country). Inside it, the organisation is by
**levels, not grades**: a learner's place is found by what they
prove, and each level stands on the ones before it as a
recommended-first path — never a forced gate. The school grades
the book comes from (2de, 1re, Terminale) appear only in each
lesson's `lesson/sources.md`, as provenance.

The course has **three levels**, which are the book's own three
axes, in the official programme's order:

1. **Level 1 — Rights, Freedoms and Responsibility** (the Seconde
   axis). The citizen's legal frame first: the rule of law, public
   order, freedoms (expression, information and pluralism),
   laïcité and pluralism, secularisation and the hierarchy of
   values, and environmental rights and responsibility. 8 lessons,
   in the title `rights-freedoms-and-responsibility/`.
2. **Level 2 — Cohesion and Diversity in a Democracy** (the
   Première axis). What holds a diverse society together:
   nationality and citizenship, pluralism as a republican
   principle, decentralisation, defence and national security,
   fighting discrimination, equality of women and men, the figures
   of social hatred, and solidarity and fraternity. 8 lessons, in
   the title `cohesion-and-diversity-in-a-democracy/`.
3. **Level 3 — Democratic Life: Debate and Deliberation** (the
   Terminale axis). How democratic decisions are actually debated
   and taken: the culture of organised discussion, public opinion,
   the digital society, European law, international law,
   legitimacy, consensus and majority, active citizenship, and
   participatory democracy. 8 lessons, in the title
   `democratic-life-debate-and-deliberation/`.

**France's own way:** EMC lycée is a resource manual built on the
official programme's three axes — rights/freedoms/responsibility
in Seconde, cohesion and diversity in Première, democratic debate
and deliberation in Terminale — taught pearl by pearl from real
texts, cases and questions, with laïcité as a distinctively French
thread through the rights axis. This course preserves that way:
each lesson is one chapter of the manual, taught from its opening
'De quoi est il question' lesson, in our own words.

**Course-intro video script** (written here, made later; the
book's own shape, walked in order):

- *Three axes, one citizen:* "Start with the frame: the rule of
  law, your freedoms, laïcité, and your responsibility for the
  environment. Then what holds us together: citizenship,
  pluralism, equality, solidarity. Then how we decide: discuss,
  judge opinion, face the digital world, and take part. That is
  France's EMC — rights first, cohesion next, deliberation last."

## The way of the book (its own shape, preserved)

**Manuel Libre d'EMC, Lycée** — a libre resource manual, not a
paged textbook: 379 HTML 'perles' (about 600 including media, by
its own contents page) in 3 axes × 8 chapters. Each chapter opens
with a 'De quoi est il question' lesson — what is at stake — and
continues with the chapter's own resource files: texts, videos
and question sets. Teaching runs pearl by pearl from real texts,
cases and questions. This course stocks each chapter from its
opening lesson, exactly as filed; the remaining pearls of each
chapter are its resources, inventoried by count in the contents
map above. *(Student book.)*
