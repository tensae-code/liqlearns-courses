# The Rule of Law — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** Law made by national representation — a)

**2.** Founded on the vote — and not a synonym of democracy — b)


Meanings:
a) In France, law is made by the national representation — the elected representatives of the people — not by one person's order.
b) The whole arrangement rests on the popular will expressed by vote, yet État de droit is not simply a synonym of democracy.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
