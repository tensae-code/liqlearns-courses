# The Rule of Law — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** In france the rule of law (l'état de droit) means law is made by the people's national representation, guarded by the conseil constitutionnel, applied by the courts, and founded on the popular will expressed by vote — and it is not simply a synonym of democracy.

**2.** What does the part "Guarded and applied" teach?
**Answer:** The Conseil constitutionnel guards the law against the Constitution, and the courts apply the law to real cases.
