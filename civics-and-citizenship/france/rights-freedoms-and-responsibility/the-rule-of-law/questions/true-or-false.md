# The Rule of Law — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** In france the rule of law (l'état de droit) means law is made by the people's national representation, guarded by the conseil constitutionnel, applied by the courts, and founded on the popular will expressed by vote — and it is not simply a synonym of democracy.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a citizen's own home, school or street in France.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from the public life of France and lived there.
