# Video script — Public Order

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Public order (l'ordre public) is the frame within which freedoms are exercised — the shared safety and calm that let everyone's freedom exist at the same time.

Freedoms need a frame. Each person's freedom ends where it would destroy another's — public order is the shared frame that keeps freedoms from colliding. Without the frame, the loudest or strongest freedom would swallow the rest.

Safety and calm as common goods. Public order protects the safety of people and property and the calm of shared places, so daily life can go on. Streets, schools and gatherings can only be used freely when they are safe.

Limits inside the frame. When a freedom is limited for public order, the limit itself must stay inside the law — the frame binds the authorities too. Public order is a legal idea, not a blank permission to forbid.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
