# Public Order — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Public order (l'ordre public) is the frame within which freedoms are exercised — the shared safety and calm that let everyone's freedom exist at the same time.

**2.** What does the part "Safety and calm as common goods" teach?
**Answer:** Public order protects the safety of people and property and the calm of shared places, so daily life can go on.
