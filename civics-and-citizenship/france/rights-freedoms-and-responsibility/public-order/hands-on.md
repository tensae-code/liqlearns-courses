# Hands-on — Public Order

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Picture a busy street, a market and a demonstration sharing one town on one day. Write down what has to hold so that all three can happen safely. This lesson names that frame: public order.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: public order (l'ordre public) is the frame within which freedoms are exercised — the shared safety and calm that let everyone's freedom exist at the same time. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
