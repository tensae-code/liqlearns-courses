# Freedom of Expression — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** The freedom and its foundations — a)

**2.** Its limits — b)


Meanings:
a) Freedom of expression lets every person speak, write and publish their thoughts — democracy cannot work if citizens cannot say what they think.
b) The law limits expression where it attacks others — incitement to hatred, defamation and similar harms are not protected by the freedom.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
