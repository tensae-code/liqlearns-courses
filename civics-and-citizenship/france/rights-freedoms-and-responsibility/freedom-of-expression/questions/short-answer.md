# Freedom of Expression — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Freedom of expression is the freedom to say, write and publish what one thinks — a foundation of democracy — and it has limits set by law.

**2.** What does the part "Why democracy needs it" teach?
**Answer:** Elections, debate and a free press all presuppose that opinions can be expressed and criticised in public.
