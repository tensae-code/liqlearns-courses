# Freedom of Expression — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) freedom of expression is the freedom to say, write and publish what one thinks — a foundation of democracy — and it has limits set by law  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — The freedom and its foundations?
a) Freedom of expression lets every person speak, write and publish their thoughts — democracy cannot work if citizens cannot say what they think.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first sub lesson teaches.
