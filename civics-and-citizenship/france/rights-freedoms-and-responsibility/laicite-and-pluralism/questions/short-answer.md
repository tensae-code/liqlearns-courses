# Laïcité and Pluralism — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Laïcité is france's democratic principle of separation between religions and the state — another name for tolerance — set by the 1905 separation law, written into the constitutional texts, and applied at school.

**2.** What does the part "The 1905 law and the constitutional texts" teach?
**Answer:** The law of 1905 separated the churches and the state, and laïcité stands in France's constitutional texts as a principle of the Republic.
