# Laïcité and Pluralism — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** A democratic principle — another name for tolerance — a)

**2.** Laïcité at school, and religious signs — b)


Meanings:
a) Laïcité means the state recognises no religion and favours none, so every belief can exist freely and equally.
b) At school laïcité means a neutral space where pupils of every belief learn together; the question of religious signs is treated inside that frame.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
