# Development and Ecological Transition — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Development must now answer an ecological question: how a society grows and organises its life without destroying the environment its citizens have rights in — the ecological transition.

**2.** What does the part "The ecological transition" teach?
**Answer:** Transition means changing energy, transport, building and production so development respects environmental limits.
