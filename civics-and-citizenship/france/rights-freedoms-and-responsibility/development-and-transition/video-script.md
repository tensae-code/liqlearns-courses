# Video script — Development and Ecological Transition

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Development must now answer an ecological question: how a society grows and organises its life without destroying the environment its citizens have rights in — the ecological transition.

Development as a rights question. How a society develops decides who enjoys its rights — to work, to housing, to a healthy environment — and who is left out. Development is therefore a civic question, not only an economic one.

The ecological transition. Transition means changing energy, transport, building and production so development respects environmental limits. It is a change of model, carried over years, not a single measure.

Responsibility shared. Citizens, businesses and public powers each carry part of the transition — the axis's theme of responsibility applied to the planet. The chapter closes the Seconde axis where it began: rights exercised responsibly.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
