# Hands-on — Development and Ecological Transition

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
List two things your town needs more of — housing, transport, jobs — and one environmental cost each could carry. This lesson asks how both columns are held together: development and transition.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: development must now answer an ecological question: how a society grows and organises its life without destroying the environment its citizens have rights in — the ecological transition. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
