# Freedom of Information and Pluralism — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** Free information — a)

**2.** A condition of democracy — b)


Meanings:
a) Citizens must be able to seek and receive information freely — without that, every other freedom is blind.
b) The manual teaches free information and media pluralism as conditions of democracy, not decorations on it.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
