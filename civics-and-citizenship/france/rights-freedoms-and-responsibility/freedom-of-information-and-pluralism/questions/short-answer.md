# Freedom of Information and Pluralism — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Freedom of information and a plurality of media are conditions of democracy — citizens can only choose freely if they can learn freely from more than one source.

**2.** What does the part "Pluralism of the media" teach?
**Answer:** Pluralism means a real diversity of media and viewpoints, so no single power owns the public's picture of the world.
