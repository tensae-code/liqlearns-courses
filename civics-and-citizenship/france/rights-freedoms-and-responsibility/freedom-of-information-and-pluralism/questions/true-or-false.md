# Freedom of Information and Pluralism — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Freedom of information and a plurality of media are conditions of democracy — citizens can only choose freely if they can learn freely from more than one source.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a citizen's own home, school or street in France.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from the public life of France and lived there.
