# Video script — Freedom of Information and Pluralism

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Freedom of information and a plurality of media are conditions of democracy — citizens can only choose freely if they can learn freely from more than one source.

Free information. Citizens must be able to seek and receive information freely — without that, every other freedom is blind. Voting, debating and judging all depend on knowing.

Pluralism of the media. Pluralism means a real diversity of media and viewpoints, so no single power owns the public's picture of the world. Different outlets checking each other is part of how errors surface.

A condition of democracy. The manual teaches free information and media pluralism as conditions of democracy, not decorations on it. Where information is monopolised, democratic choice becomes theatre.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
