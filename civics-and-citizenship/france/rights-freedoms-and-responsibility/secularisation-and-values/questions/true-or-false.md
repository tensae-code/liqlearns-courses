# Secularisation and the Hierarchy of Values — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Secularisation is the long change by which religion's place in society shrinks and values once ordered by religion come to be ordered by society itself — raising the question of how a plural society ranks its values.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a citizen's own home, school or street in France.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from the public life of France and lived there.
