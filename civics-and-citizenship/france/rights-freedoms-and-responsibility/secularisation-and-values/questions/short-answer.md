# Secularisation and the Hierarchy of Values — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Secularisation is the long change by which religion's place in society shrinks and values once ordered by religion come to be ordered by society itself — raising the question of how a plural society ranks its values.

**2.** What does the part "Values re-ordered" teach?
**Answer:** As secularisation proceeds, the hierarchy of values — what a society puts first — is debated and decided socially, not handed down by one religion.
