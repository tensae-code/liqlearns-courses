# Video script — Secularisation and the Hierarchy of Values

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Secularisation is the long change by which religion's place in society shrinks and values once ordered by religion come to be ordered by society itself — raising the question of how a plural society ranks its values.

What secularisation is. Secularisation names the process by which religious authority over society's life weakens over time. Beliefs remain, but they stop organising the whole of common life.

Values re-ordered. As secularisation proceeds, the hierarchy of values — what a society puts first — is debated and decided socially, not handed down by one religion. Different citizens rank values differently, and the ranking itself becomes a public question.

Living plural. In a secularised, plural society, the shared frame must hold for believers of every religion and for non-believers alike. That is why this chapter stands in the rights axis beside laïcité.
