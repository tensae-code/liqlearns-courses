# Hands-on — Secularisation and the Hierarchy of Values

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name one rule of daily life that once came from religion and now comes from law or custom — school holidays, marriage, Sunday rest. This lesson studies that long change: secularisation.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: secularisation is the long change by which religion's place in society shrinks and values once ordered by religion come to be ordered by society itself — raising the question of how a plural society ranks its values. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
