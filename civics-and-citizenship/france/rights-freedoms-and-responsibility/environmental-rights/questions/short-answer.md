# Environmental Rights — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** The environment carries rights and duties: every person has the right to live in a balanced environment that respects health, and a responsibility to protect it.

**2.** What does the part "Responsibility matches the right" teach?
**Answer:** Rights over the environment come with responsibility: each person and each power must take care not to damage what all depend on.
