# Environmental Rights — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** A right to a healthy environment — a)

**2.** Rights held for the future — b)


Meanings:
a) Environmental rights recognise that living in a balanced, health-respecting environment is a right, not a favour.
b) Environmental rights look forward: today's choices bind the conditions in which future citizens will live.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
