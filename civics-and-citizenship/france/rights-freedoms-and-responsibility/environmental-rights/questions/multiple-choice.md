# Environmental Rights — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) the environment carries rights and duties: every person has the right to live in a balanced environment that respects health, and a responsibility to protect it  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — A right to a healthy environment?
a) Environmental rights recognise that living in a balanced, health-respecting environment is a right, not a favour.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first sub lesson teaches.
