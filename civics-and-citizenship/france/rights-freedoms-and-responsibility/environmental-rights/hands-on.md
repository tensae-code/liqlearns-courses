# Hands-on — Environmental Rights

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name one place near you — a river, a wood, a clean street — that your health depends on. Write who can damage it and who must protect it. This lesson names both sides: environmental rights and responsibility.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the environment carries rights and duties: every person has the right to live in a balanced environment that respects health, and a responsibility to protect it. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
