# Video script — Environmental Rights

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The environment carries rights and duties: every person has the right to live in a balanced environment that respects health, and a responsibility to protect it.

A right to a healthy environment. Environmental rights recognise that living in a balanced, health-respecting environment is a right, not a favour. France carries this in its Charter for the Environment, joined to the constitutional block.

Responsibility matches the right. Rights over the environment come with responsibility: each person and each power must take care not to damage what all depend on. Prevention comes before repair — damage to shared nature is often irreversible.

Rights held for the future. Environmental rights look forward: today's choices bind the conditions in which future citizens will live. That is why the environment stands in the axis of rights and responsibility together.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
