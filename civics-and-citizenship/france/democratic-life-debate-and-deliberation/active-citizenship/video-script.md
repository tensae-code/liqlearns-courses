# Video script — Active Citizenship

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Active citizenship is citizenship practised: engaging in political life, knowing the offices citizens hold — mayor, deputy, senator, minister — and keeping those responsibilities open to all, even as engagement professionalises.

Engagement as a real activity. Political engagement — joining, campaigning, standing, serving — is citizenship in its active form. Voting is its minimum, not its whole.

Knowing the offices. The active citizen knows the offices and what each does: mayor, deputy, senator, minister. You cannot aim at, judge or question an office you cannot name.

Open to all, against professionalisation. Engagement tends to professionalise — a political class forms — and democracy answers by keeping responsibilities open to all citizens. The chapter's closing question: who gets to be active, and how the door stays open.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
