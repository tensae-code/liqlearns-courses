# Active Citizenship — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Active citizenship is citizenship practised: engaging in political life, knowing the offices citizens hold — mayor, deputy, senator, minister — and keeping those responsibilities open to all, even as engagement professionalises.

**2.** What does the part "Knowing the offices" teach?
**Answer:** The active citizen knows the offices and what each does: mayor, deputy, senator, minister.
