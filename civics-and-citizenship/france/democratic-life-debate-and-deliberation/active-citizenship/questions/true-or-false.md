# Active Citizenship — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Active citizenship is citizenship practised: engaging in political life, knowing the offices citizens hold — mayor, deputy, senator, minister — and keeping those responsibilities open to all, even as engagement professionalises.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a citizen's own home, school or street in France.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from the public life of France and lived there.
