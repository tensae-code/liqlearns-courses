# Lesson plan — Active Citizenship

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — France · **Title:** Democratic Life: Debate and Deliberation · **Lesson:** Active Citizenship
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Democratic Life: Debate and Deliberation (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** active citizenship is citizenship practised: engaging in political life, knowing the offices citizens hold — mayor, deputy, senator, minister — and keeping those responsibilities open to all, even as engagement professionalises.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — Democratic Life: Debate and Deliberation — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Engagement as a real activity** — Political engagement — joining, campaigning, standing, serving — is citizenship in its active form.
2. **Knowing the offices** — The active citizen knows the offices and what each does: mayor, deputy, senator, minister.
3. **Open to all, against professionalisation** — Engagement tends to professionalise — a political class forms — and democracy answers by keeping responsibilities open to all citizens.

Gate pool: the prove-it check for each sub lesson is at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson — drawn from this lesson's question files (questions/).
