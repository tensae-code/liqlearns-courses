# International Law — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** International law is the frame within which states deliberate and bind themselves — treaties, organisations and rules that no single state commands, yet all can invoke.

**2.** What does the part "Made by consent, kept by interest" teach?
**Answer:** States make international law by agreeing to it, and keep it because breaking faith isolates and costs them.
