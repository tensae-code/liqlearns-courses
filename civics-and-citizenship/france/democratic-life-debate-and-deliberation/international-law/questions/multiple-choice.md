# International Law — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) international law is the frame within which states deliberate and bind themselves — treaties, organisations and rules that no single state commands, yet all can invoke  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — A frame between states?
a) International law organises relations between states: treaties, diplomacy and international organisations.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first sub lesson teaches.
