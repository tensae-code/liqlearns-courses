# Video script — International Law

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
International law is the frame within which states deliberate and bind themselves — treaties, organisations and rules that no single state commands, yet all can invoke.

A frame between states. International law organises relations between states: treaties, diplomacy and international organisations. It is deliberation continued by legal means — this axis's theme at world scale.

Made by consent, kept by interest. States make international law by agreeing to it, and keep it because breaking faith isolates and costs them. Its authority is consensual, which is both its limit and its legitimacy.

The citizen's horizon widens. Rights and protections also live at this level — the international texts stand behind many national rights. The course's last legal layer: from the commune to the world, law frames deliberation at every level.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
