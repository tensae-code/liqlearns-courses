# Hands-on — International Law

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Imagine two countries disputing a border with no law above either of them. Write what could settle it besides force. This lesson names that frame: international law.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: international law is the frame within which states deliberate and bind themselves — treaties, organisations and rules that no single state commands, yet all can invoke. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
