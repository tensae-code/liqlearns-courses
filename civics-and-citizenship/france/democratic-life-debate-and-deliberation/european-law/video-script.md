# Video script — European Law

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
European law is the law of the european union — built by treaties between its member states, standing above national law in its fields, and raising the debated question of what europe is for.

Law built by treaties. European law rests on the treaties the member states signed together, creating common institutions and common rules. France is an author of this law, not only its subject.

Sovereignty debated. How much sovereignty states pool in Europe is a standing democratic debate — the chapter teaches the debate, not a verdict. Every treaty step re-opens the question of who decides.

What Europe is for. Peace between its peoples, shared prosperity, common rules for a shared market and shared problems — the chapter asks what Europe is for. The citizen of Terminale is also a European citizen, with rights at that level too.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
