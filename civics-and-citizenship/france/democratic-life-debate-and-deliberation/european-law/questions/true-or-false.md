# European Law — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** European law is the law of the european union — built by treaties between its member states, standing above national law in its fields, and raising the debated question of what europe is for.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a citizen's own home, school or street in France.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from the public life of France and lived there.
