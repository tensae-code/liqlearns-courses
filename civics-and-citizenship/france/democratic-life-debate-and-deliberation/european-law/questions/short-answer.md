# European Law — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** European law is the law of the european union — built by treaties between its member states, standing above national law in its fields, and raising the debated question of what europe is for.

**2.** What does the part "Sovereignty debated" teach?
**Answer:** How much sovereignty states pool in Europe is a standing democratic debate — the chapter teaches the debate, not a verdict.
