# Hands-on — The Digital Society

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Take one striking claim you saw online this week. Before believing it, write three checks you could run on it. This lesson is about the world that claim travels in: the digital society.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the digital society moves public debate online — where disinformation and conspiracy thinking spread fast, and the citizen's defence is critical distance: checking sources before believing or sharing. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
