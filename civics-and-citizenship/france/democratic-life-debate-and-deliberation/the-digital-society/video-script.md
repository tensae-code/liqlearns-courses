# Video script — The Digital Society

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The digital society moves public debate online — where disinformation and conspiracy thinking spread fast, and the citizen's defence is critical distance: checking sources before believing or sharing.

Debate moves online. Public discussion now happens largely online: fast, wide, and open to everyone — its strengths and its dangers come from the same openness. The Terminale axis's discussion culture must now survive in this arena.

Disinformation and conspiracy thinking. Disinformation is false content spread deliberately; conspiracy thinking reads every event as a hidden plot's proof. Both exploit the wish for a simple story; both corrode shared facts.

Keeping critical distance. The defence is method: check the source, compare outlets, wait before sharing — critical distance as a daily habit. A democracy can debate anything except a world where facts themselves dissolve.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
