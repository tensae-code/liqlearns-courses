# The Digital Society — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** The digital society moves public debate online — where disinformation and conspiracy thinking spread fast, and the citizen's defence is critical distance: checking sources before believing or sharing.

**2.** What does the part "Disinformation and conspiracy thinking" teach?
**Answer:** Disinformation is false content spread deliberately; conspiracy thinking reads every event as a hidden plot's proof.
