# Hands-on — Public Opinion

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Ask ten people one question and announce the result as 'what everyone thinks'. This lesson asks what you actually measured: public opinion.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: public opinion is what a society is taken to think — measured by polls that can mirror or distort it, increasingly made on social networks, and always shadowed by conformism. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
