# Public Opinion — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Public opinion is what a society is taken to think — measured by polls that can mirror or distort it, increasingly made on social networks, and always shadowed by conformism.

**2.** What does the part "Polls: thermometers or distorting mirrors" teach?
**Answer:** Polls measure opinion and can distort it: wording, samples and timing bend the result.
