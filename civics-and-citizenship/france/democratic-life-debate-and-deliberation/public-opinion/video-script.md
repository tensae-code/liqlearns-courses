# Video script — Public Opinion

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Public opinion is what a society is taken to think — measured by polls that can mirror or distort it, increasingly made on social networks, and always shadowed by conformism.

Defining public opinion. Public opinion is the shared judgement attributed to a public on the questions of common life. It is a real force — governments watch it — yet no one has ever seen it directly.

Polls: thermometers or distorting mirrors. Polls measure opinion and can distort it: wording, samples and timing bend the result. The critical citizen reads a poll the way they read an argument — asking how it was made.

Networks, conformism, and a world opinion. Social networks now factory-make opinion at speed; conformism pressures each person to think with the crowd; and opinion increasingly forms at world scale. The chapter ends asking whether a world public opinion is being born.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
