# Lesson plan — Public Opinion

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — France · **Title:** Democratic Life: Debate and Deliberation · **Lesson:** Public Opinion
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Democratic Life: Debate and Deliberation (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** public opinion is what a society is taken to think — measured by polls that can mirror or distort it, increasingly made on social networks, and always shadowed by conformism.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — Democratic Life: Debate and Deliberation — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Defining public opinion** — Public opinion is the shared judgement attributed to a public on the questions of common life.
2. **Polls: thermometers or distorting mirrors** — Polls measure opinion and can distort it: wording, samples and timing bend the result.
3. **Networks, conformism, and a world opinion** — Social networks now factory-make opinion at speed; conformism pressures each person to think with the crowd; and opinion increasingly forms at world scale.

Gate pool: the prove-it check for each sub lesson is at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson — drawn from this lesson's question files (questions/).
