# Legitimacy, Consensus and Majority — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A democratic decision binds everyone when it is legitimate — taken by the right procedure — and majority rule, the usual procedure, works only when the minority accepts it and consensus is sought where it can be found.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a citizen's own home, school or street in France.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from the public life of France and lived there.
