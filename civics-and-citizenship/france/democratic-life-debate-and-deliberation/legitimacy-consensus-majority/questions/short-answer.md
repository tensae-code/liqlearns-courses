# Legitimacy, Consensus and Majority — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** A democratic decision binds everyone when it is legitimate — taken by the right procedure — and majority rule, the usual procedure, works only when the minority accepts it and consensus is sought where it can be found.

**2.** What does the part "Majority rule and the minority" teach?
**Answer:** Democracy decides by majority — and protects the minority, whose turn to persuade and win may come at the next vote.
