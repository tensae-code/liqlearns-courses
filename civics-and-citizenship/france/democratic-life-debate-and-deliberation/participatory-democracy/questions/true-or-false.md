# Participatory Democracy — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Participatory democracy takes citizens into decisions beyond the vote — consulted, associated and heard between elections — completing representative democracy rather than replacing it.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a citizen's own home, school or street in France.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from the public life of France and lived there.
