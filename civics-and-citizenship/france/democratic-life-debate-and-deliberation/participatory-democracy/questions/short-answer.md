# Participatory Democracy — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Participatory democracy takes citizens into decisions beyond the vote — consulted, associated and heard between elections — completing representative democracy rather than replacing it.

**2.** What does the part "What participation adds" teach?
**Answer:** Decisions gain local knowledge, legitimacy and acceptance when those affected help shape them.
