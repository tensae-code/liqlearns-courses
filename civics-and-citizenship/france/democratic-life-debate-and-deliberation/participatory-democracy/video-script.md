# Video script — Participatory Democracy

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Participatory democracy takes citizens into decisions beyond the vote — consulted, associated and heard between elections — completing representative democracy rather than replacing it.

Beyond the vote. Citizens can take part between elections: public debates, consultations, participatory budgets and citizens' assemblies. Participation widens the discussion culture of this axis into institutions.

What participation adds. Decisions gain local knowledge, legitimacy and acceptance when those affected help shape them. People support what they helped build — participation is cohesion applied to decisions.

Its place in the Republic. Participatory democracy completes the representative system: representatives decide, citizens inform, test and enrich the decision. The course ends where citizenship becomes a practice, not a status.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
