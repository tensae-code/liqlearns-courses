# Hands-on — The Culture of Organised Discussion

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Take one question your group disagrees on. Write the rules that would let you discuss it without shouting or splitting. This lesson is about exactly those rules: the culture of organised discussion.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: democracy decides by organised discussion: citizens who know what discussing is, keep the ethics of discussion, hear plural views, and vote — debating even the hardest questions, from the death penalty to the ecological question. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
