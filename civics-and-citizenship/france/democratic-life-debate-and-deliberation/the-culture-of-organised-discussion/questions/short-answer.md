# The Culture of Organised Discussion — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Democracy decides by organised discussion: citizens who know what discussing is, keep the ethics of discussion, hear plural views, and vote — debating even the hardest questions, from the death penalty to the ecological question.

**2.** What does the part "Pluralism, parties and the duty to vote" teach?
**Answer:** Discussion lives inside pluralism: political parties, named in the 1958 Constitution, organise the choices; the citizen's part ends in the duty to vote.
