# The Culture of Organised Discussion — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Democracy decides by organised discussion: citizens who know what discussing is, keep the ethics of discussion, hear plural views, and vote — debating even the hardest questions, from the death penalty to the ecological question.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a citizen's own home, school or street in France.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from the public life of France and lived there.
