# Video script — The Culture of Organised Discussion

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Democracy decides by organised discussion: citizens who know what discussing is, keep the ethics of discussion, hear plural views, and vote — debating even the hardest questions, from the death penalty to the ecological question.

What discussing is, and its ethics. Discussion is the exchange of reasons under shared rules: each speaks, each listens, arguments answer arguments. Its ethics — honesty, listening, no force but reasons — is what separates debate from quarrel.

Pluralism, parties and the duty to vote. Discussion lives inside pluralism: political parties, named in the 1958 Constitution, organise the choices; the citizen's part ends in the duty to vote. Parties turn millions of opinions into decidable options.

Debating the hardest questions. The manual practises discussion on real hard cases — the death penalty, the ecological question — where values collide. If discussion can hold there, it can hold anywhere in democratic life.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
