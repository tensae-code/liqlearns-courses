# Civics and Citizenship — France course — CURRICULUM (the path)

One course for France inside the Civics and Citizenship folder,
organised by **levels, not grades**: a learner climbs when they
have proved the level, not when a school calendar says so. The
school grades the book comes from (2de, 1re, Terminale) appear
only in each lesson's sources (provenance), never as the course's
organisation. The path follows the book's own order — the
official EMC programme's three axes: rights, freedoms and
responsibility first (the citizen's legal frame), then cohesion
and diversity (what holds a diverse society together), then
democratic life as debate and deliberation (how decisions are
actually debated and taken), as France's *Manuel Libre
d'Enseignement moral et civique* for the Lycée lays it out.

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in this
course comes from government textbooks — this country's own
Enseignement moral et civique book for the Lycée — except the
**Tip boxes**: a tip in a box is from us, the people making
LiqLearns. Tips are ours; the lessons are the books'. See
ABOUT.md for the full book list, the contents map, and the
acceptance reckoning.

## The levels (the path, in order)

1. **Rights, Freedoms and Responsibility** — the rule of law,
   public order, freedom of expression, laïcité and pluralism,
   freedom of information and pluralism, secularisation and the
   hierarchy of values, environmental rights, and development
   and ecological transition — the citizen's legal frame first,
   as the official programme orders it. 8 lessons, in the title
   `rights-freedoms-and-responsibility/`.
2. **Cohesion and Diversity in a Democracy** — nationality and
   citizenship, pluralism, decentralisation, defence and
   national security, fighting discrimination, equality of women
   and men, figures of social hatred, and solidarity and
   fraternity — what holds a diverse society together. 8 lessons,
   in the title `cohesion-and-diversity-in-a-democracy/`.
3. **Democratic Life: Debate and Deliberation** — the culture of
   organised discussion, public opinion, the digital society,
   European law, international law, legitimacy, consensus and
   majority, active citizenship, and participatory democracy —
   how democratic decisions are actually debated and taken.
   8 lessons, in the title
   `democratic-life-debate-and-deliberation/`.

**Totals: 24 lessons** (Level 1: 8 · Level 2: 8 · Level 3: 8 —
all from one student book).
