# Hands-on — Solidarity and Fraternity

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name one time this month when someone carried a cost that was not theirs — a shared meal, a collection, a tax-funded service. This lesson names that bond: solidarity and fraternity.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: solidarity and fraternity — the third word of the Republic's motto — mean citizens carry one another: through public solidarity organised by law and through the everyday fraternity of people who treat each other as equals. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
