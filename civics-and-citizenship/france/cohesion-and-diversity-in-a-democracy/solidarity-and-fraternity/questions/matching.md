# Solidarity and Fraternity — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** Fraternity, the motto's third word — a)

**2.** Solidarity practised — b)


Meanings:
a) Liberty and equality are joined by fraternity: citizens are not strangers but members of one another's lives.
b) Everyday solidarity — associations, mutual help, volunteering — is the same value in citizens' own hands.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
