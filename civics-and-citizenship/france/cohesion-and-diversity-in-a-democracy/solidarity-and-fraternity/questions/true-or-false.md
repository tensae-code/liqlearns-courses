# Solidarity and Fraternity — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Solidarity and fraternity — the third word of the republic's motto — mean citizens carry one another: through public solidarity organised by law and through the everyday fraternity of people who treat each other as equals.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a citizen's own home, school or street in France.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from the public life of France and lived there.
