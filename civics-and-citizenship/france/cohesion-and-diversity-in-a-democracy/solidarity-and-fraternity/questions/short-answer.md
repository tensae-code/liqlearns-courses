# Solidarity and Fraternity — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Solidarity and fraternity — the third word of the republic's motto — mean citizens carry one another: through public solidarity organised by law and through the everyday fraternity of people who treat each other as equals.

**2.** What does the part "Solidarity organised" teach?
**Answer:** Public solidarity — social protection, public services, help in hardship — is fraternity organised by law and funded in common.
