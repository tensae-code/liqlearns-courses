# Video script — Solidarity and Fraternity

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Solidarity and fraternity — the third word of the republic's motto — mean citizens carry one another: through public solidarity organised by law and through the everyday fraternity of people who treat each other as equals.

Fraternity, the motto's third word. Liberty and equality are joined by fraternity: citizens are not strangers but members of one another's lives. The motto's three words stand or fall together.

Solidarity organised. Public solidarity — social protection, public services, help in hardship — is fraternity organised by law and funded in common. It turns a value into a system that holds when goodwill alone would fail.

Solidarity practised. Everyday solidarity — associations, mutual help, volunteering — is the same value in citizens' own hands. The chapter closes the Première axis: cohesion is fraternity at work.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
