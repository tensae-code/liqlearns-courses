# Lesson plan — Solidarity and Fraternity

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — France · **Title:** Cohesion and Diversity in a Democracy · **Lesson:** Solidarity and Fraternity
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — Cohesion and Diversity in a Democracy (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** solidarity and fraternity — the third word of the Republic's motto — mean citizens carry one another: through public solidarity organised by law and through the everyday fraternity of people who treat each other as equals.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — Cohesion and Diversity in a Democracy — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Fraternity, the motto's third word** — Liberty and equality are joined by fraternity: citizens are not strangers but members of one another's lives.
2. **Solidarity organised** — Public solidarity — social protection, public services, help in hardship — is fraternity organised by law and funded in common.
3. **Solidarity practised** — Everyday solidarity — associations, mutual help, volunteering — is the same value in citizens' own hands.

Gate pool: the prove-it check for each sub lesson is at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson — drawn from this lesson's question files (questions/).
