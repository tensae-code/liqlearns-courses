# Nationality and Citizenship — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** Acquiring nationality — a)

**2.** What being French means — a debated question — b)


Meanings:
a) French nationality is acquired in set ways — by birth in the cases the law defines, and by naturalisation for those who ask and qualify.
b) Beyond the legal bond, what being French means is debated: language, values, history, choice.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
