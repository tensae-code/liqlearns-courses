# Nationality and Citizenship — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** French nationality is the legal bond to france — acquired by birth or by naturalisation — and citizenship is the civic life that flows from it: rights, duties, the possible loss of civic rights, and the debated question of what being french means.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a citizen's own home, school or street in France.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from the public life of France and lived there.
