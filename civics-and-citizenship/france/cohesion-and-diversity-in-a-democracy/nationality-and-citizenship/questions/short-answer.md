# Nationality and Citizenship — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** French nationality is the legal bond to france — acquired by birth or by naturalisation — and citizenship is the civic life that flows from it: rights, duties, the possible loss of civic rights, and the debated question of what being french means.

**2.** What does the part "Citizenship: rights, duties, and their loss" teach?
**Answer:** The citizen holds civic rights — voting first among them — and owes civic duties; civic rights can be lost in the cases the law provides.
