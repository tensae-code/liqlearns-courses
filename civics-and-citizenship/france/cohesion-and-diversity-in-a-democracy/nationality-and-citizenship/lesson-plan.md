# Lesson plan — Nationality and Citizenship

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — France · **Title:** Cohesion and Diversity in a Democracy · **Lesson:** Nationality and Citizenship
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — Cohesion and Diversity in a Democracy (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** French nationality is the legal bond to France — acquired by birth or by naturalisation — and citizenship is the civic life that flows from it: rights, duties, the possible loss of civic rights, and the debated question of what being French means.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — Cohesion and Diversity in a Democracy — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Acquiring nationality** — French nationality is acquired in set ways — by birth in the cases the law defines, and by naturalisation for those who ask and qualify.
2. **Citizenship: rights, duties, and their loss** — The citizen holds civic rights — voting first among them — and owes civic duties; civic rights can be lost in the cases the law provides.
3. **What being French means — a debated question** — Beyond the legal bond, what being French means is debated: language, values, history, choice.

Gate pool: the prove-it check for each sub lesson is at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson — drawn from this lesson's question files (questions/).
