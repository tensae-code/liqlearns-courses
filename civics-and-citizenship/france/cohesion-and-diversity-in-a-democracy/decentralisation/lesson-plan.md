# Lesson plan — Decentralisation

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — France · **Title:** Cohesion and Diversity in a Democracy · **Lesson:** Decentralisation
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — Cohesion and Diversity in a Democracy (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** decentralisation organises the Republic close to its citizens: communes, départements and régions hold real powers of their own, decided by elected local councils.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — Cohesion and Diversity in a Democracy — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The Republic close to citizens** — Decentralisation brings public decisions closer to the people they concern.
2. **Communes, départements, régions** — France is organised in territorial levels — the commune, the département and the région — each with its elected council and its own competences.
3. **Elected and responsible** — Local powers are exercised by elected councils, answerable to local voters — decentralisation is democracy at walking distance.

Gate pool: the prove-it check for each sub lesson is at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson — drawn from this lesson's question files (questions/).
