# Hands-on — Decentralisation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name one decision taken about your street by your town, and one taken in Paris. This lesson explains why both levels exist and how the nearer one works: decentralisation.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: decentralisation organises the Republic close to its citizens: communes, départements and régions hold real powers of their own, decided by elected local councils. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
