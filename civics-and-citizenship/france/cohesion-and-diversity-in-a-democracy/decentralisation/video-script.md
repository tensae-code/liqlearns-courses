# Video script — Decentralisation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Decentralisation organises the republic close to its citizens: communes, départements and régions hold real powers of their own, decided by elected local councils.

The Republic close to citizens. Decentralisation brings public decisions closer to the people they concern. A commune knows its streets in a way a distant ministry cannot.

Communes, départements, régions. France is organised in territorial levels — the commune, the département and the région — each with its elected council and its own competences. The levels stack; each does what it is best placed to do.

Elected and responsible. Local powers are exercised by elected councils, answerable to local voters — decentralisation is democracy at walking distance. It widens citizenship: the citizen meets public power first in their own town.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
