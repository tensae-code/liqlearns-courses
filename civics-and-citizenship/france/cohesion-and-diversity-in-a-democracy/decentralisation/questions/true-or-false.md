# Decentralisation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Decentralisation organises the republic close to its citizens: communes, départements and régions hold real powers of their own, decided by elected local councils.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a citizen's own home, school or street in France.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from the public life of France and lived there.
