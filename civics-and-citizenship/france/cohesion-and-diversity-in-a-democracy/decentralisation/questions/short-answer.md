# Decentralisation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Decentralisation organises the republic close to its citizens: communes, départements and régions hold real powers of their own, decided by elected local councils.

**2.** What does the part "Communes, départements, régions" teach?
**Answer:** France is organised in territorial levels — the commune, the département and the région — each with its elected council and its own competences.
