# Fighting Discrimination — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) discrimination — treating a person unequally because of who they are — is forbidden by law, and building an inclusive society means removing the barriers that keep people out  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — What discrimination is?
a) Discrimination is unequal treatment based on who a person is — origin, sex, disability, beliefs and the other grounds the law names.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first sub lesson teaches.
