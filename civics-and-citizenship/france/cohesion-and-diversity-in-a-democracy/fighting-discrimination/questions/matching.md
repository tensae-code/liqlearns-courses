# Fighting Discrimination — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** What discrimination is — a)

**2.** An inclusive society — b)


Meanings:
a) Discrimination is unequal treatment based on who a person is — origin, sex, disability, beliefs and the other grounds the law names.
b) An inclusive society removes barriers — in school, work and public life — so every person can take part fully.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
