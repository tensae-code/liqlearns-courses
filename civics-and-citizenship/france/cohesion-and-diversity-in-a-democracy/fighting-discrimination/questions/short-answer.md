# Fighting Discrimination — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Discrimination — treating a person unequally because of who they are — is forbidden by law, and building an inclusive society means removing the barriers that keep people out.

**2.** What does the part "Forbidden by law" teach?
**Answer:** The law forbids discrimination and gives victims ways to answer it.
