# Video script — Fighting Discrimination

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Discrimination — treating a person unequally because of who they are — is forbidden by law, and building an inclusive society means removing the barriers that keep people out.

What discrimination is. Discrimination is unequal treatment based on who a person is — origin, sex, disability, beliefs and the other grounds the law names. It denies equal citizenship in practice while promising it on paper.

Forbidden by law. The law forbids discrimination and gives victims ways to answer it. Naming the grounds in law is how a republic makes equality enforceable.

An inclusive society. An inclusive society removes barriers — in school, work and public life — so every person can take part fully. Inclusion is the positive side of the same chapter: not only refusing exclusion, but building access.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
