# Hands-on — Fighting Discrimination

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Think of a door — a job, a school, a club — that opens for some and not for others, for reasons that have nothing to do with ability. This lesson names that wrong and its answer: fighting discrimination, building an inclusive society.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: discrimination — treating a person unequally because of who they are — is forbidden by law, and building an inclusive society means removing the barriers that keep people out. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
