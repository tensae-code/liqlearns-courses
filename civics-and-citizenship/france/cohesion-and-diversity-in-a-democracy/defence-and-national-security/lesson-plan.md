# Lesson plan — Defence and National Security

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — France · **Title:** Cohesion and Diversity in a Democracy · **Lesson:** Defence and National Security
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — Cohesion and Diversity in a Democracy (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** national defence and security are a shared civic responsibility: the Republic protects its territory and people, and citizens — through national service and civic duties — take their part in that protection.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — Cohesion and Diversity in a Democracy — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Why a republic defends itself** — Defence protects the territory, the population and the Republic's independence — the conditions in which civic life exists at all.
2. **A shared responsibility** — National security is shared: the armed forces, public institutions and citizens contribute, each in their place.
3. **Defence inside democracy** — In a republic, defence stays under democratic authority and law — protection never suspends citizenship.

Gate pool: the prove-it check for each sub lesson is at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson — drawn from this lesson's question files (questions/).
