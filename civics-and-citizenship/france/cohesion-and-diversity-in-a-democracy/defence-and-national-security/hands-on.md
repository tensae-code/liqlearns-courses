# Hands-on — Defence and National Security

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write down who protects your country, and what an ordinary citizen's part in that protection could be. This lesson treats defence as a citizen's subject, not only a soldier's: defence and national security.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: national defence and security are a shared civic responsibility: the Republic protects its territory and people, and citizens — through national service and civic duties — take their part in that protection. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
