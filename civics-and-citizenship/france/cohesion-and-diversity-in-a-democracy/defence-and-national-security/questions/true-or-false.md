# Defence and National Security — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** National defence and security are a shared civic responsibility: the republic protects its territory and people, and citizens — through national service and civic duties — take their part in that protection.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a citizen's own home, school or street in France.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from the public life of France and lived there.
