# Defence and National Security — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) national defence and security are a shared civic responsibility: the Republic protects its territory and people, and citizens — through national service and civic duties — take their part in that protection  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — Why a republic defends itself?
a) Defence protects the territory, the population and the Republic's independence — the conditions in which civic life exists at all.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first sub lesson teaches.
