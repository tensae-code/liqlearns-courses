# Defence and National Security — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** National defence and security are a shared civic responsibility: the republic protects its territory and people, and citizens — through national service and civic duties — take their part in that protection.

**2.** What does the part "A shared responsibility" teach?
**Answer:** National security is shared: the armed forces, public institutions and citizens contribute, each in their place.
