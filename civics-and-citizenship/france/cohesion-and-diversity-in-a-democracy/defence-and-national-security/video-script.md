# Video script — Defence and National Security

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
National defence and security are a shared civic responsibility: the republic protects its territory and people, and citizens — through national service and civic duties — take their part in that protection.

Why a republic defends itself. Defence protects the territory, the population and the Republic's independence — the conditions in which civic life exists at all. Without security, every other right stands on sand.

A shared responsibility. National security is shared: the armed forces, public institutions and citizens contribute, each in their place. France marks this bond in the citizen's path — the defence and citizenship day is its best-known sign.

Defence inside democracy. In a republic, defence stays under democratic authority and law — protection never suspends citizenship. The chapter holds both ideas at once: strong protection, democratic control.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
