# Video script — Equality of Women and Men

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Equality between women and men is a principle of the republic that must hold in law, in work and in political life — and the distance between the principle and the facts is the chapter's subject.

Equality in law. Women and men hold the same rights in French law — the principle is constitutional and complete on paper. The chapter starts from that proclaimed equality.

Equality in work. In working life the facts lag: pay gaps and unequal careers persist, and law and policy push against them. Measuring the gap is the first step the chapter teaches.

Equality in political life. Political equality advanced by deliberate rules — parity in elections is France's named instrument for sharing seats. Representation is where the Première axis's cohesion is tested in public.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
