# Equality of Women and Men — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Equality between women and men is a principle of the republic that must hold in law, in work and in political life — and the distance between the principle and the facts is the chapter's subject.

**2.** What does the part "Equality in work" teach?
**Answer:** In working life the facts lag: pay gaps and unequal careers persist, and law and policy push against them.
