# Equality of Women and Men — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Equality between women and men is a principle of the republic that must hold in law, in work and in political life — and the distance between the principle and the facts is the chapter's subject.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a citizen's own home, school or street in France.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from the public life of France and lived there.
