# Equality of Women and Men — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** Equality in law — a)

**2.** Equality in political life — b)


Meanings:
a) Women and men hold the same rights in French law — the principle is constitutional and complete on paper.
b) Political equality advanced by deliberate rules — parity in elections is France's named instrument for sharing seats.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
