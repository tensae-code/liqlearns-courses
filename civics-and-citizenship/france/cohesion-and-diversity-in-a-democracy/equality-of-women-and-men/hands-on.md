# Hands-on — Equality of Women and Men

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
List three jobs and three public offices, and ask who usually holds them around you. This lesson compares that picture with the Republic's promise: equality of women and men.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: equality between women and men is a principle of the Republic that must hold in law, in work and in political life — and the distance between the principle and the facts is the chapter's subject. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
