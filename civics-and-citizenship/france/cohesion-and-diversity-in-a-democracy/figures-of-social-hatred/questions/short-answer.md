# Figures of Social Hatred — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Social hatred — racism, antisemitism and the related hatreds aimed at groups — attacks the republic's cohesion at its root, and citizens answer it by naming it, refusing it and standing with those it targets.

**2.** What does the part "Why it attacks cohesion" teach?
**Answer:** Hatred aimed at a group tells some citizens they do not belong — it strikes the fraternity the Republic promises.
