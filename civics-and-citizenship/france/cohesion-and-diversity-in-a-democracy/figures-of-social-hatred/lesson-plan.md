# Lesson plan — Figures of Social Hatred

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — France · **Title:** Cohesion and Diversity in a Democracy · **Lesson:** Figures of Social Hatred
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — Cohesion and Diversity in a Democracy (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** social hatred — racism, antisemitism and the related hatreds aimed at groups — attacks the Republic's cohesion at its root, and citizens answer it by naming it, refusing it and standing with those it targets.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — Cohesion and Diversity in a Democracy — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The forms hatred takes** — Social hatred appears in named figures — racism, antisemitism and related hatreds — each with its own history and targets.
2. **Why it attacks cohesion** — Hatred aimed at a group tells some citizens they do not belong — it strikes the fraternity the Republic promises.
3. **Answering it** — The answer is civic: refuse the hatred, apply the law that bounds it, and stand with those targeted.

Gate pool: the prove-it check for each sub lesson is at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson — drawn from this lesson's question files (questions/).
