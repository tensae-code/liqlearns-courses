# Video script — Figures of Social Hatred

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Social hatred — racism, antisemitism and the related hatreds aimed at groups — attacks the republic's cohesion at its root, and citizens answer it by naming it, refusing it and standing with those it targets.

The forms hatred takes. Social hatred appears in named figures — racism, antisemitism and related hatreds — each with its own history and targets. Naming the form precisely is how the chapter begins to answer it.

Why it attacks cohesion. Hatred aimed at a group tells some citizens they do not belong — it strikes the fraternity the Republic promises. A society cannot cohere while some members are marked as outsiders.

Answering it. The answer is civic: refuse the hatred, apply the law that bounds it, and stand with those targeted. The chapter closes the loop with discrimination: hatred is discrimination's loudest voice.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
