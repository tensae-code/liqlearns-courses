# Hands-on — Figures of Social Hatred

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Recall a slogan or joke aimed not at what someone did but at what someone is. This lesson studies that move and its forms: the figures of social hatred.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: social hatred — racism, antisemitism and the related hatreds aimed at groups — attacks the Republic's cohesion at its root, and citizens answer it by naming it, refusing it and standing with those it targets. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
