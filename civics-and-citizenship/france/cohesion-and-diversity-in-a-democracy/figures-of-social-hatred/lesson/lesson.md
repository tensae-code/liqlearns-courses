# Figures of Social Hatred

## Do it first

Recall a slogan or joke aimed not at what someone did but at what someone is. This lesson studies that move and its forms: the figures of social hatred.

## Sub lesson 1 — The forms hatred takes

Social hatred appears in named figures — racism, antisemitism and related hatreds — each with its own history and targets.

Naming the form precisely is how the chapter begins to answer it.

## Sub lesson 2 — Why it attacks cohesion

Hatred aimed at a group tells some citizens they do not belong — it strikes the fraternity the Republic promises.

A society cannot cohere while some members are marked as outsiders.

## Sub lesson 3 — Answering it

The answer is civic: refuse the hatred, apply the law that bounds it, and stand with those targeted.

The chapter closes the loop with discrimination: hatred is discrimination's loudest voice.
