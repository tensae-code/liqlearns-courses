# Pluralism — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** Diversity as a fact and a principle — a)

**2.** Coexistence organised — b)


Meanings:
a) Opinions, beliefs and ways of life are genuinely diverse — pluralism takes that diversity as a principle to protect, not a problem to erase.
b) Pluralism needs rules of coexistence: freedoms for each, limits where one freedom would crush another, and debate to settle the rest.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
