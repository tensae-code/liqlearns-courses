# Pluralism — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Pluralism — the real diversity of opinions, beliefs and ways of life — is a principle of the republic: the republic does not impose one opinion, it organises the coexistence of many.

**2.** What does the part "No imposed opinion" teach?
**Answer:** The Republic imposes no official opinion or belief; public powers stay neutral between citizens' convictions.
