# Pluralism — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) pluralism — the real diversity of opinions, beliefs and ways of life — is a principle of the Republic: the Republic does not impose one opinion, it organises the coexistence of many  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — Diversity as a fact and a principle?
