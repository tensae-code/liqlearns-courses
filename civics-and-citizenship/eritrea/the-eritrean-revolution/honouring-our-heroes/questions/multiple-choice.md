# Honoring Our Heroes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The heroes and martyrs of the struggle and how they are remembered.  b) it has nothing to do with how Eritrea governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The heroes and martyrs of the struggle and how they are remembered.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) The heroes and martyrs of the struggle and how they are remembered.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Eritrea
**Answer: a) The heroes and martyrs of the struggle and how they are remembered.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
