# Honoring Our Independence — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Independence as the revolution's outcome and how it is celebrated.  b) it has nothing to do with how Eritrea governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Independence as the revolution's outcome and how it is celebrated.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Independence as the revolution's outcome and how it is celebrated.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Eritrea
**Answer: a) Independence as the revolution's outcome and how it is celebrated.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
