# Wrongful Acts — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Wrongful acts and their consequences for the doer and others.  b) it has nothing to do with how Eritrea governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Wrongful acts and their consequences for the doer and others.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Wrongful acts and their consequences for the doer and others.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Eritrea
**Answer: a) Wrongful acts and their consequences for the doer and others.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
