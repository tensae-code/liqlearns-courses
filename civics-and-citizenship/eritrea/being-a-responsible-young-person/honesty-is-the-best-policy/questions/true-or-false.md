# Honesty Is the Best Policy — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Honesty as a rule of life, taught through story.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that Honesty Is the Best Policy — and that it has no connection to the rest of Eritrea's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Eritrea's own course, in its own order.
