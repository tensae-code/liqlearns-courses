# Who Am I? — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Describing yourself, your family, school and life timeline as the start of responsibility.  b) it has nothing to do with how Eritrea governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Describing yourself, your family, school and life timeline as the start of responsibility.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Describing yourself, your family, school and life timeline as the start of responsibility.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Eritrea
**Answer: a) Describing yourself, your family, school and life timeline as the start of responsibility.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
