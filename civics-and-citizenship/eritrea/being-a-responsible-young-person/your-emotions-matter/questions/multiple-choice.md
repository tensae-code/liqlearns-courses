# Your Emotions Matter — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Naming and managing emotions.  b) it has nothing to do with how Eritrea governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Naming and managing emotions.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Naming and managing emotions.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Eritrea
**Answer: a) Naming and managing emotions.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
