# Friendship — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Qualities of good friendship and choosing friends well.  b) it has nothing to do with how Eritrea governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Qualities of good friendship and choosing friends well.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Qualities of good friendship and choosing friends well.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Eritrea
**Answer: a) Qualities of good friendship and choosing friends well.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
