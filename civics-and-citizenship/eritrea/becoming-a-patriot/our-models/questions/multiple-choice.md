# Our Models — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Model Eritreans whose lives show patriotism.  b) it has nothing to do with how Eritrea governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Model Eritreans whose lives show patriotism.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Model Eritreans whose lives show patriotism.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Eritrea
**Answer: a) Model Eritreans whose lives show patriotism.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
