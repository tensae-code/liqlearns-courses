# Young Patriots — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) How young people show patriotism through work and service.  b) it has nothing to do with how Eritrea governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) How young people show patriotism through work and service.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) How young people show patriotism through work and service.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Eritrea
**Answer: a) How young people show patriotism through work and service.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
