# Being a Good and Active Citizen — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The good and active citizen at home, school and in the community.  b) it has nothing to do with how Eritrea governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The good and active citizen at home, school and in the community.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) The good and active citizen at home, school and in the community.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Eritrea
**Answer: a) The good and active citizen at home, school and in the community.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
