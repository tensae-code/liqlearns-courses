# Cultural Unity — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Culture as what unites a diverse people ('united we stand, divided we fall').
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that Cultural Unity — and that it has no connection to the rest of Eritrea's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Eritrea's own course, in its own order.
