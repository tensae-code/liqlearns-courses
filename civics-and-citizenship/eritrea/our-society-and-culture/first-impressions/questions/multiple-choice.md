# First Impressions — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) First impressions, difference and not judging people wrongly.  b) it has nothing to do with how Eritrea governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) First impressions, difference and not judging people wrongly.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) First impressions, difference and not judging people wrongly.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Eritrea
**Answer: a) First impressions, difference and not judging people wrongly.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
