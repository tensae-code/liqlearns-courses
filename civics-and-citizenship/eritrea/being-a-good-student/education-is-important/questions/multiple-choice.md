# Education Is Important — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Why education matters for the person and the country.  b) it has nothing to do with how Eritrea governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Why education matters for the person and the country.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Why education matters for the person and the country.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Eritrea
**Answer: a) Why education matters for the person and the country.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
