# Civics and Citizenship — Eritrea course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

Civics belongs to the country: this course is Eritrea's own —
built from Eritrea's Citizenship Education books for Grades
6 and 8 (Ministry of Education, State of Eritrea, in
English). Inside the course the organisation is by **levels,
not grades**: the titles below ARE the levels' titles, in
path order — what the learner proves, step by step. The
grades the books come from live only in each lesson's
sources.

The path's own logic is Eritrea's, from the filing's ladder
sketch: the books open with the child's own character
(responsibility, self-control, honesty) and life as a
student, because Eritrea's spiral always starts with the
young person; the circle widens to family, traditions,
diversity, religious harmony and shared culture; then comes
the national story — revolution, liberation, referendum, the
state's principles and government, patriotism and symbols;
and the course closes on rules, rights paired with duties,
right/wrong consequences, and the good, active citizen. Both
books run the same nine-unit spiral, the Grade 8 book at
greater depth, so each title below holds its Grade 6 lessons
first and its Grade 8 lessons after them, in the books' own
order. That ladder IS the path order (recommended, never
forced — a learner can jump in higher; the "start here
instead" hints point down this ladder).
