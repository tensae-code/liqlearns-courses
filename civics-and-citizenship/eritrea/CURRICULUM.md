# Civics and Citizenship — Eritrea course — CURRICULUM (the path)

One course for Eritrea inside the Civics and Citizenship
folder, organised by **levels, not grades**: a learner climbs
when they have proved the level, not when a school calendar
says so. The school grades the books come from appear only in
each lesson's sources (provenance), never as the course's
organisation. The path follows Eritrea's own order as its
books lay it out — the same nine-unit spiral in both books:
character first (the responsible young person, the good
student), then school, culture, the Revolution and the birth
of the state, patriotism, symbols, rules and rights, and
finally the good, active citizen. The Grade 8 book deepens
the Grade 6 spiral — self-control and honesty replace general
responsibility, world revolutions set up the Eritrean one,
and the course ends on right/wrong consequences and the
active citizen. The filings for this build
(`eritrea-placements.md` — 2 records / 2 distinct books, both
student books, both COMPLETE) stock 60 placements into 58
lessons: the coined lesson names were deduped before
building — two lessons are stocked by two placements each
(the Grade 6 and Grade 8 passes of the same teaching: the
national principles, and the national holidays) — and every
placement stocks into exactly one lesson.

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in
this course comes from government textbooks — this country's
own Citizenship Education books — except the **Tip boxes**:
a tip in a box is from us, the people making LiqLearns. Tips
are ours; the lessons are the books'. See ABOUT.md for the
full book list and the acceptance reckoning. **All 58 lessons
stand on student books; no lesson in this course stands on a
teacher guide or a programme.**

## The levels (the path, in order)

1. **The Responsible Young Person and the Good Student** — the books open with the child's own character (responsibility, self-control, honesty) and life as a student, because Eritrea's spiral always starts with the young person. 17 lessons, in 2 title(s): `being-a-responsible-young-person/` (8 lessons); `being-a-good-student/` (9 lessons).
2. **Our Society and Culture** — the circle widens to family, traditions, diversity, religious harmony and shared culture. 8 lessons, in 1 title(s): `our-society-and-culture/` (8 lessons).
3. **The National Story — Revolution, State, Patriotism and Symbols** — the national story: revolution, liberation, referendum, the state's principles and government, patriotism and symbols. 23 lessons, in 4 title(s): `the-eritrean-revolution/` (6 lessons); `the-state-of-eritrea/` (7 lessons); `becoming-a-patriot/` (7 lessons); `national-symbols-and-holidays/` (3 lessons).
4. **Rules, Rights and the Good, Active Citizen** — the course closes on rules, rights paired with duties, right/wrong consequences, and the good, active citizen. 10 lessons, in 3 title(s): `rights-and-duties/` (1 lessons); `rules-rights-and-responsibilities/` (4 lessons); `being-a-good-citizen/` (5 lessons).

**Totals: 58 lessons** (Level 1: 17 · Level 2: 8 · Level 3: 23 · Level 4: 10 — from two read student
books: *Citizenship Education*, Grade 6 (Ministry of
Education, State of Eritrea, 2014, ISBN 99948-1-743-4,
COMPLETE) and *Citizenship Education*, Grade 8 (Ministry of
Education, State of Eritrea, 2015, ISBN 99948-1-745-0,
COMPLETE); 60 placements stocking 58 lessons).
