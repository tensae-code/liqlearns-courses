# The School Forms Eco-Citizens — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "Identity and being Haitian" teach?
**Answer:** Identity is approached several ways in the programme's catch-up notions; at its centre stands being Haitian in a democratic context — citizen conscience and democratic citizenship.

**2.** In your own words, what does the part "The school and the eco-citizen" teach?
**Answer:** The school has a named role in forming eco-citizens: citizens who carry their citizenship into care for their environment.

**3.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** The school forms eco-citizens, and identity is where it starts — being haitian in a democratic context, with a citizen conscience.
