# The School Forms Eco-Citizens — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Identity is approached several ways in the programme's catch-up notions; at its centre stands being Haitian in a democratic context — citizen conscience and democratic citizenship.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The school has a named role in forming eco-citizens: citizens who carry their citizenship into care for their environment.
**Answer: True**

**3.** This lesson teaches that the school forms eco-citizens, and identity is where it starts — being Haitian in a democratic context, with a citizen conscience — and that it has no connection to the rest of Haiti's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Haiti's own course, in its own order.
