# The School Forms Eco-Citizens — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — Identity and being Haitian?
a) Identity is approached several ways in the programme's catch-up notions; at its centre stands being Haitian in a democratic context — citizen conscience and democratic citizenship.  b) it has nothing to do with how Haiti governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Identity is approached several ways in the programme's catch-up notions; at its centre stands being Haitian in a democratic context — citizen conscience and democratic citizenship.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — The school and the eco-citizen?
a) The school has a named role in forming eco-citizens: citizens who carry their citizenship into care for their environment.  b) it has nothing to do with how Haiti governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The school has a named role in forming eco-citizens: citizens who carry their citizenship into care for their environment.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**3.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) the school forms eco-citizens, and identity is where it starts — being Haitian in a democratic context, with a citizen conscience.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Haiti
**Answer: a) the school forms eco-citizens, and identity is where it starts — being Haitian in a democratic context, with a citizen conscience.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
