# Video script — The School Forms Eco-Citizens

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The school forms eco-citizens, and identity is where it starts — being haitian in a democratic context, with a citizen conscience.

Identity is approached several ways in the programme's catch-up notions; at its centre stands being Haitian in a democratic context — citizen conscience and democratic citizenship.

The school has a named role in forming eco-citizens: citizens who carry their citizenship into care for their environment.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
