# The School Forms Eco-Citizens

## Do it first

Say what makes you Haitian, in one sentence. This lesson joins two of the programme's themes: who you are, and the school that forms you into an eco-citizen.

## Sub lesson 1 — Identity and being Haitian

Identity is approached several ways in the programme's catch-up notions; at its centre stands being Haitian in a democratic context — citizen conscience and democratic citizenship.

## Sub lesson 2 — The school and the eco-citizen

The school has a named role in forming eco-citizens: citizens who carry their citizenship into care for their environment.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the school forms eco-citizens, and identity is where it starts — being Haitian in a democratic context, with a citizen conscience. Give one real example from your own life or from the Haiti you live in, and name the lesson's key words: identity, eco-citizen, school, democratic citizenship. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: the school forms eco-citizens, and identity is where it starts — being Haitian in a democratic context, with a citizen conscience.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
