# Lesson plan — The School Forms Eco-Citizens

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Haiti · **Title:** Forming Citizens · **Lesson:** The School Forms Eco-Citizens
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — The Republic and the Citizen (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the school forms eco-citizens, and identity is where it starts — being Haitian in a democratic context, with a citizen conscience. This lesson stands on a teacher programme alone — evidence only, labelled as such.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Identity and being Haitian** — Identity is approached several ways in the programme's catch-up notions; at its centre stands being Haitian in a democratic context — citizen conscience and democratic citizenship.
2. **The school and the eco-citizen** — The school has a named role in forming eco-citizens: citizens who carry their citizenship into care for their environment.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
