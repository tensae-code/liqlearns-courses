# Hands-on — The School Forms Eco-Citizens

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Say what makes you Haitian, in one sentence. This lesson joins two of the programme's themes: who you are, and the school that forms you into an eco-citizen.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the school forms eco-citizens, and identity is where it starts — being Haitian in a democratic context, with a citizen conscience. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
