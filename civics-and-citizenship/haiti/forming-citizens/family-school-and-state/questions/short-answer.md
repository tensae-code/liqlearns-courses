# Family, School and State — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "Three formers of the citizen" teach?
**Answer:** The programme's progression grid gives a whole theme to the contribution of family, school and state — with religion named alongside them — in forming citizens.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** Citizens are formed, not born — the family, the school and the state (and religion) each contribute their part to forming them.
