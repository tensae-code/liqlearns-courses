# Family, School and State — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — Three formers of the citizen?
a) The programme's progression grid gives a whole theme to the contribution of family, school and state — with religion named alongside them — in forming citizens.  b) it has nothing to do with how Haiti governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The programme's progression grid gives a whole theme to the contribution of family, school and state — with religion named alongside them — in forming citizens.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) citizens are formed, not born — the family, the school and the state (and religion) each contribute their part to forming them.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Haiti
**Answer: a) citizens are formed, not born — the family, the school and the state (and religion) each contribute their part to forming them.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
