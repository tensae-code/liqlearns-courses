# Family, School and State — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The programme's progression grid gives a whole theme to the contribution of family, school and state — with religion named alongside them — in forming citizens.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that citizens are formed, not born — the family, the school and the state (and religion) each contribute their part to forming them — and that it has no connection to the rest of Haiti's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Haiti's own course, in its own order.
