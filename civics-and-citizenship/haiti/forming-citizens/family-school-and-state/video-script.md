# Video script — Family, School and State

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Citizens are formed, not born — the family, the school and the state (and religion) each contribute their part to forming them.

The programme's progression grid gives a whole theme to the contribution of family, school and state — with religion named alongside them — in forming citizens.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
