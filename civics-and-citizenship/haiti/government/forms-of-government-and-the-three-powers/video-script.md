# Video script — Forms of Government and the Three Powers

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Countries are governed in different forms, and haiti's three powers can be compared with how other states arrange theirs — across the caribbean, the americas, europe, africa and asia.

The programme teaches the different forms of government found in the world, so the pupil can place Haiti's form among them.

Haiti's three powers are compared with how other states work — in the Caribbean, the Americas, Europe, Africa and Asia.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
