# Forms of Government and the Three Powers — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "Forms of government in the world" teach?
**Answer:** The programme teaches the different forms of government found in the world, so the pupil can place Haiti's form among them.

**2.** In your own words, what does the part "Haiti's three powers, compared" teach?
**Answer:** Haiti's three powers are compared with how other states work — in the Caribbean, the Americas, Europe, Africa and Asia.

**3.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** Countries are governed in different forms, and haiti's three powers can be compared with how other states arrange theirs — across the caribbean, the americas, europe, africa and asia.
