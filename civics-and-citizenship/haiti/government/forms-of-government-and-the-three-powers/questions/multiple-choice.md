# Forms of Government and the Three Powers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — Forms of government in the world?
a) The programme teaches the different forms of government found in the world, so the pupil can place Haiti's form among them.  b) it has nothing to do with how Haiti governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The programme teaches the different forms of government found in the world, so the pupil can place Haiti's form among them.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — Haiti's three powers, compared?
a) Haiti's three powers are compared with how other states work — in the Caribbean, the Americas, Europe, Africa and Asia.  b) it has nothing to do with how Haiti governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Haiti's three powers are compared with how other states work — in the Caribbean, the Americas, Europe, Africa and Asia.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

a) countries are governed in different forms, and Haiti's three powers can be compared with how other states arrange theirs — across the Caribbean, the Americas, Europe, Africa and Asia.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Haiti
**Answer: a) countries are governed in different forms, and Haiti's three powers can be compared with how other states arrange theirs — across the Caribbean, the Americas, Europe, Africa and Asia.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
