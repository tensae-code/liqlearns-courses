# Forms of Government and the Three Powers — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The programme teaches the different forms of government found in the world, so the pupil can place Haiti's form among them.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Haiti's three powers are compared with how other states work — in the Caribbean, the Americas, Europe, Africa and Asia.
**Answer: True**

**3.** This lesson teaches that countries are governed in different forms, and Haiti's three powers can be compared with how other states arrange theirs — across the Caribbean, the Americas, Europe, Africa and Asia — and that it has no connection to the rest of Haiti's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Haiti's own course, in its own order.
