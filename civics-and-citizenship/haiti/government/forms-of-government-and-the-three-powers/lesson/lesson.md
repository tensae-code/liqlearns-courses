# Forms of Government and the Three Powers

## Do it first

Name one other country and say how you think it is ruled. This lesson compares forms of government — and Haiti's three powers against the world's.

## Sub lesson 1 — Forms of government in the world

The programme teaches the different forms of government found in the world, so the pupil can place Haiti's form among them.

## Sub lesson 2 — Haiti's three powers, compared

Haiti's three powers are compared with how other states work — in the Caribbean, the Americas, Europe, Africa and Asia.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: countries are governed in different forms, and Haiti's three powers can be compared with how other states arrange theirs — across the Caribbean, the Americas, Europe, Africa and Asia. Give one real example from your own life or from the Haiti you live in, and name the lesson's key words: forms of government, three powers, comparison. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
