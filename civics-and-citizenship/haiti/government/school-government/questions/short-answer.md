# School Government — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "What a school government is" teach?
**Answer:** The school government is taught as a concept with an organisation, an importance and a functioning of its own — including the practical techniques for installing one: ballot boxes, ballots, candidates.

**2.** In your own words, what does the part "Democracy at school" teach?
**Answer:** Through the school government, democracy is practised at school, with participative structures for making the rules of school life together.

**3.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** A school can practise democracy itself — the gouvernement scolaire gives pupils a real, organised share in the rules of school life.
