# School Government — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The school government is taught as a concept with an organisation, an importance and a functioning of its own — including the practical techniques for installing one: ballot boxes, ballots, candidates.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Through the school government, democracy is practised at school, with participative structures for making the rules of school life together.
**Answer: True**

**3.** This lesson teaches that a school can practise democracy itself — the gouvernement scolaire gives pupils a real, organised share in the rules of school life — and that it has no connection to the rest of Haiti's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Haiti's own course, in its own order.
