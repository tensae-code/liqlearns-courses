# School Government — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — What a school government is?
a) The school government is taught as a concept with an organisation, an importance and a functioning of its own — including the practical techniques for installing one: ballot boxes, ballots, candidates.  b) it has nothing to do with how Haiti governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The school government is taught as a concept with an organisation, an importance and a functioning of its own — including the practical techniques for installing one: ballot boxes, ballots, candidates.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — Democracy at school?
a) Through the school government, democracy is practised at school, with participative structures for making the rules of school life together.  b) it has nothing to do with how Haiti governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Through the school government, democracy is practised at school, with participative structures for making the rules of school life together.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**3.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) a school can practise democracy itself — the gouvernement scolaire gives pupils a real, organised share in the rules of school life.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Haiti
**Answer: a) a school can practise democracy itself — the gouvernement scolaire gives pupils a real, organised share in the rules of school life.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
