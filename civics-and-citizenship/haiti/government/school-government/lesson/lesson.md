# School Government

## Do it first

If your class could vote on one rule of school life, what would it be? This lesson is about the school government — democracy practised where you learn.

## Sub lesson 1 — What a school government is

The school government is taught as a concept with an organisation, an importance and a functioning of its own — including the practical techniques for installing one: ballot boxes, ballots, candidates.

## Sub lesson 2 — Democracy at school

Through the school government, democracy is practised at school, with participative structures for making the rules of school life together.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: a school can practise democracy itself — the gouvernement scolaire gives pupils a real, organised share in the rules of school life. Give one real example from your own life or from the Haiti you live in, and name the lesson's key words: school government, gouvernement scolaire, democracy at school. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
