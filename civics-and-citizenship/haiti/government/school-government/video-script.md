# Video script — School Government

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A school can practise democracy itself — the gouvernement scolaire gives pupils a real, organised share in the rules of school life.

The school government is taught as a concept with an organisation, an importance and a functioning of its own — including the practical techniques for installing one: ballot boxes, ballots, candidates.

Through the school government, democracy is practised at school, with participative structures for making the rules of school life together.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
