# Governance and Public Ethics — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — Governance and good governance?
a) Governance, and good governance, are defined and characterised in a state — what steering a country is, and what makes the steering good.  b) it has nothing to do with how Haiti governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Governance, and good governance, are defined and characterised in a state — what steering a country is, and what makes the steering good.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — Public ethics?
a) Good governance is tied to public ethics: the link between the two is demonstrated, not just asserted — ethics is governance's working condition.  b) it has nothing to do with how Haiti governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Good governance is tied to public ethics: the link between the two is demonstrated, not just asserted — ethics is governance's working condition.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**3.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) governance is how a state is steered, good governance is steering it well — and public ethics is what makes the difference.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Haiti
**Answer: a) governance is how a state is steered, good governance is steering it well — and public ethics is what makes the difference.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
