# Governance and Public Ethics — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Governance, and good governance, are defined and characterised in a state — what steering a country is, and what makes the steering good.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Good governance is tied to public ethics: the link between the two is demonstrated, not just asserted — ethics is governance's working condition.
**Answer: True**

**3.** This lesson teaches that governance is how a state is steered, good governance is steering it well — and public ethics is what makes the difference — and that it has no connection to the rest of Haiti's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Haiti's own course, in its own order.
