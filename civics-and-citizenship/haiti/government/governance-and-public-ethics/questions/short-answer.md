# Governance and Public Ethics — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "Governance and good governance" teach?
**Answer:** Governance, and good governance, are defined and characterised in a state — what steering a country is, and what makes the steering good.

**2.** In your own words, what does the part "Public ethics" teach?
**Answer:** Good governance is tied to public ethics: the link between the two is demonstrated, not just asserted — ethics is governance's working condition.

**3.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** Governance is how a state is steered, good governance is steering it well — and public ethics is what makes the difference.
