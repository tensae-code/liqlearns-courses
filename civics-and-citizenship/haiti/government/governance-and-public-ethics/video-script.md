# Video script — Governance and Public Ethics

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Governance is how a state is steered, good governance is steering it well — and public ethics is what makes the difference.

Governance, and good governance, are defined and characterised in a state — what steering a country is, and what makes the steering good.

Good governance is tied to public ethics: the link between the two is demonstrated, not just asserted — ethics is governance's working condition.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
