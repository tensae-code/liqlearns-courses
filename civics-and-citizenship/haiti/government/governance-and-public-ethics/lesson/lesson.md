# Governance and Public Ethics

## Do it first

Say what you think makes a leader good or bad at running public things. This lesson gives that question its proper names: governance, good governance, public ethics.

## Sub lesson 1 — Governance and good governance

Governance, and good governance, are defined and characterised in a state — what steering a country is, and what makes the steering good.

## Sub lesson 2 — Public ethics

Good governance is tied to public ethics: the link between the two is demonstrated, not just asserted — ethics is governance's working condition.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: governance is how a state is steered, good governance is steering it well — and public ethics is what makes the difference. Give one real example from your own life or from the Haiti you live in, and name the lesson's key words: governance, good governance, public ethics. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
