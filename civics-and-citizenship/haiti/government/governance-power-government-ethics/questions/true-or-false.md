# Governance: Power, Government and Ethics — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The NS2 progression grid's second theme treats governance as power, government and ethics together: who holds power, how government works, and the ethics that must govern both.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that governance holds three things together — power, government, and ethics — and the NS2 grid teaches them as one theme — and that it has no connection to the rest of Haiti's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Haiti's own course, in its own order.
