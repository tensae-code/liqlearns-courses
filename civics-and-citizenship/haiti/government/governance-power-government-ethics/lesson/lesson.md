# Governance: Power, Government and Ethics

## Do it first

Say which matters more in running a country: power, organisation, or honesty. This lesson refuses to choose — it teaches the three as one.

The NS2 progression grid's second theme treats governance as power, government and ethics together: who holds power, how government works, and the ethics that must govern both.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: governance holds three things together — power, government, and ethics — and the NS2 grid teaches them as one theme. Give one real example from your own life or from the Haiti you live in, and name the lesson's key words: governance, power, government, ethics. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
