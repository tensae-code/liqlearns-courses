# Hands-on — Governance: Power, Government and Ethics

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Say which matters more in running a country: power, organisation, or honesty. This lesson refuses to choose — it teaches the three as one.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: governance holds three things together — power, government, and ethics — and the NS2 grid teaches them as one theme. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
