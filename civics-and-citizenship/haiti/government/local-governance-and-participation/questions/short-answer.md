# Local Governance and Participation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "Local governance through participation" teach?
**Answer:** The NS2 progression grid's third theme is local governance through the citizens' participation: the nearest level of government runs on the people who live under it taking part.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** Governance is also local — the citizen's participation is how local governance works, close to home.
