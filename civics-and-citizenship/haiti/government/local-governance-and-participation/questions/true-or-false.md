# Local Governance and Participation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The NS2 progression grid's third theme is local governance through the citizens' participation: the nearest level of government runs on the people who live under it taking part.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that governance is also local — the citizen's participation is how local governance works, close to home — and that it has no connection to the rest of Haiti's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Haiti's own course, in its own order.
