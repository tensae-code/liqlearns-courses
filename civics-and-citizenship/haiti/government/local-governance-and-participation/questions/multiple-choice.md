# Local Governance and Participation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — Local governance through participation?
a) The NS2 progression grid's third theme is local governance through the citizens' participation: the nearest level of government runs on the people who live under it taking part.  b) it has nothing to do with how Haiti governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The NS2 progression grid's third theme is local governance through the citizens' participation: the nearest level of government runs on the people who live under it taking part.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) governance is also local — the citizen's participation is how local governance works, close to home.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Haiti
**Answer: a) governance is also local — the citizen's participation is how local governance works, close to home.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
