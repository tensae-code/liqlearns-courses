# Local Governance and Participation

## Do it first

Name one decision taken close to your home that affects your street. Who took it, and were people like you part of it? This lesson is about that level of power.

The NS2 progression grid's third theme is local governance through the citizens' participation: the nearest level of government runs on the people who live under it taking part.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: governance is also local — the citizen's participation is how local governance works, close to home. Give one real example from your own life or from the Haiti you live in, and name the lesson's key words: local governance, participation. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: governance is also local — the citizen's participation is how local governance works, close to home.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
