# Hands-on — Local Governance and Participation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name one decision taken close to your home that affects your street. Who took it, and were people like you part of it? This lesson is about that level of power.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: governance is also local — the citizen's participation is how local governance works, close to home. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
