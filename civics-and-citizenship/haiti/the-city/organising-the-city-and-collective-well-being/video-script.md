# Video script — Organising the City and Collective Well-Being

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The city is organised, not accidental — organising it for collective well-being is everyone's part in building a new haiti.

Organising the city and collective well-being in Haiti are taught together: the shared place arranged for the shared good.

Building a new Haiti is named as everyone's part — the city's organisation is the citizen's work, not only the authorities'.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
