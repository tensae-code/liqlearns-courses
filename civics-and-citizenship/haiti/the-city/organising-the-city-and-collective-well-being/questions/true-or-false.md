# Organising the City and Collective Well-Being — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Organising the city and collective well-being in Haiti are taught together: the shared place arranged for the shared good.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Building a new Haiti is named as everyone's part — the city's organisation is the citizen's work, not only the authorities'.
**Answer: True**

**3.** This lesson teaches that the city is organised, not accidental — organising it for collective well-being is everyone's part in building a new Haiti — and that it has no connection to the rest of Haiti's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Haiti's own course, in its own order.
