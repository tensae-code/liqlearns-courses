# Organising the City and Collective Well-Being — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "The organised city" teach?
**Answer:** Organising the city and collective well-being in Haiti are taught together: the shared place arranged for the shared good.

**2.** In your own words, what does the part "Everyone's part in a new Haiti" teach?
**Answer:** Building a new Haiti is named as everyone's part — the city's organisation is the citizen's work, not only the authorities'.

**3.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** The city is organised, not accidental — organising it for collective well-being is everyone's part in building a new haiti.
