# Organising the City and Collective Well-Being — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — The organised city?
a) Organising the city and collective well-being in Haiti are taught together: the shared place arranged for the shared good.  b) it has nothing to do with how Haiti governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Organising the city and collective well-being in Haiti are taught together: the shared place arranged for the shared good.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — Everyone's part in a new Haiti?
a) Building a new Haiti is named as everyone's part — the city's organisation is the citizen's work, not only the authorities'.  b) it has nothing to do with how Haiti governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Building a new Haiti is named as everyone's part — the city's organisation is the citizen's work, not only the authorities'.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**3.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) the city is organised, not accidental — organising it for collective well-being is everyone's part in building a new Haiti.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Haiti
**Answer: a) the city is organised, not accidental — organising it for collective well-being is everyone's part in building a new Haiti.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
