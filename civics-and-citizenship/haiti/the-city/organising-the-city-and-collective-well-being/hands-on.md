# Hands-on — Organising the City and Collective Well-Being

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Describe your town as it is, then as it could be if it were organised for everyone's well-being. This lesson is about the road between the two.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the city is organised, not accidental — organising it for collective well-being is everyone's part in building a new Haiti. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
