# Organising the City and Collective Well-Being

## Do it first

Describe your town as it is, then as it could be if it were organised for everyone's well-being. This lesson is about the road between the two.

## Sub lesson 1 — The organised city

Organising the city and collective well-being in Haiti are taught together: the shared place arranged for the shared good.

## Sub lesson 2 — Everyone's part in a new Haiti

Building a new Haiti is named as everyone's part — the city's organisation is the citizen's work, not only the authorities'.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the city is organised, not accidental — organising it for collective well-being is everyone's part in building a new Haiti. Give one real example from your own life or from the Haiti you live in, and name the lesson's key words: city, collective well-being, new Haiti. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: the city is organised, not accidental — organising it for collective well-being is everyone's part in building a new Haiti.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
