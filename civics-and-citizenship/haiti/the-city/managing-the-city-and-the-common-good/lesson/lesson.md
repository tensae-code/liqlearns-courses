# Managing the City and the Common Good

## Do it first

Name one public thing — a road, a school, a water point — and say who suffers when it is badly managed. This lesson follows that suffering to its cause.

## Sub lesson 1 — Managing public things

Managing the city is managing public things for the common good — the standard every manager of the shared world is judged by.

## Sub lesson 2 — Bad management feeds poverty

The programme states the consequence plainly: bad management of the Haitian state's goods feeds poverty.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: public things must be managed for the common good — and the programme names the cost when they are not: bad management of the Haitian state's goods feeds poverty. Give one real example from your own life or from the Haiti you live in, and name the lesson's key words: common good, public management, poverty. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
