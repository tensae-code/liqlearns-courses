# Hands-on — Managing the City and the Common Good

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name one public thing — a road, a school, a water point — and say who suffers when it is badly managed. This lesson follows that suffering to its cause.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: public things must be managed for the common good — and the programme names the cost when they are not: bad management of the Haitian state's goods feeds poverty. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
