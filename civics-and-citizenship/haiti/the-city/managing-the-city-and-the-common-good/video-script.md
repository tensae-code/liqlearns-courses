# Video script — Managing the City and the Common Good

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

This lesson is about one thing.
Public things must be managed for the common good — and the programme names the cost when they are not: bad management of the haitian state's goods feeds poverty.

Managing the city is managing public things for the common good — the standard every manager of the shared world is judged by.

The programme states the consequence plainly: bad management of the Haitian state's goods feeds poverty.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
