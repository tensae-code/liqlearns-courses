# Managing the City and the Common Good — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "Managing public things" teach?
**Answer:** Managing the city is managing public things for the common good — the standard every manager of the shared world is judged by.

**2.** In your own words, what does the part "Bad management feeds poverty" teach?
**Answer:** The programme states the consequence plainly: bad management of the Haitian state's goods feeds poverty.

**3.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** Public things must be managed for the common good — and the programme names the cost when they are not: bad management of the haitian state's goods feeds poverty.
