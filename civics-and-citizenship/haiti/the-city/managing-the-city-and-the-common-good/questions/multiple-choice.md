# Managing the City and the Common Good — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — Managing public things?
a) Managing the city is managing public things for the common good — the standard every manager of the shared world is judged by.  b) it has nothing to do with how Haiti governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Managing the city is managing public things for the common good — the standard every manager of the shared world is judged by.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — Bad management feeds poverty?
a) The programme states the consequence plainly: bad management of the Haitian state's goods feeds poverty.  b) it has nothing to do with how Haiti governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The programme states the consequence plainly: bad management of the Haitian state's goods feeds poverty.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

a) public things must be managed for the common good — and the programme names the cost when they are not: bad management of the Haitian state's goods feeds poverty.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Haiti
**Answer: a) public things must be managed for the common good — and the programme names the cost when they are not: bad management of the Haitian state's goods feeds poverty.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
