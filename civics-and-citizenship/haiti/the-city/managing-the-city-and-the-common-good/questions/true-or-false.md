# Managing the City and the Common Good — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Managing the city is managing public things for the common good — the standard every manager of the shared world is judged by.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The programme states the consequence plainly: bad management of the Haitian state's goods feeds poverty.
**Answer: True**

**3.** This lesson teaches that public things must be managed for the common good — and the programme names the cost when they are not: bad management of the Haitian state's goods feeds poverty — and that it has no connection to the rest of Haiti's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Haiti's own course, in its own order.
