# Hands-on — Living Together: Tolerance and Respect

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name one belief, not your own, that you must live beside peacefully. This lesson is about that living beside — and the old text Haiti's programme reads for it.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: living together in Haiti rests on tolerance and respect inside a secular republic — laïcité — read from texts that include Voltaire's treatise on tolerance. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
