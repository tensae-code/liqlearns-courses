# Living Together: Tolerance and Respect

## Do it first

Name one belief, not your own, that you must live beside peacefully. This lesson is about that living beside — and the old text Haiti's programme reads for it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Tolerance within laïcité

Living together in Haiti is taught as tolerance and respect within laïcité — the secular frame that lets different beliefs share one republic.

## Sub lesson 2 — Reading tolerance

The teaching is read from texts, including Voltaire's treatise on tolerance — the pupil meets the argument in an author's own work.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: living together in Haiti rests on tolerance and respect inside a secular republic — laïcité — read from texts that include Voltaire's treatise on tolerance. Give one real example from your own life or from the Haiti you live in, and name the lesson's key words: living together, tolerance, respect, laïcité, Voltaire. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
