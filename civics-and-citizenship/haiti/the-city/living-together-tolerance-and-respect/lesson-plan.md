# Lesson plan — Living Together: Tolerance and Respect

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Haiti · **Title:** The City · **Lesson:** Living Together: Tolerance and Respect
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Ethics, Rights and the City (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** living together in Haiti rests on tolerance and respect inside a secular republic — laïcité — read from texts that include Voltaire's treatise on tolerance. This lesson stands on a teacher programme alone — evidence only, labelled as such.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Tolerance within laïcité** — Living together in Haiti is taught as tolerance and respect within laïcité — the secular frame that lets different beliefs share one republic.
2. **Reading tolerance** — The teaching is read from texts, including Voltaire's treatise on tolerance — the pupil meets the argument in an author's own work.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
