# Citizen Participation

## Do it first

Name one public thing in your town that got better because ordinary people took part. This lesson is about that taking part.

Citizen participation in promoting citizenship is taught as a concept first, then in its application — what it is, and how it is done.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: citizen participation is the citizen taking part in promoting citizenship itself — a concept the programme defines and then applies. Give one real example from your own life or from the Haiti you live in, and name the lesson's key words: citizen participation, taking part. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: citizen participation is the citizen taking part in promoting citizenship itself — a concept the programme defines and then applies.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
