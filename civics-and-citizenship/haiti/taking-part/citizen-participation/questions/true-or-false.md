# Citizen Participation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Citizen participation in promoting citizenship is taught as a concept first, then in its application — what it is, and how it is done.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that citizen participation is the citizen taking part in promoting citizenship itself — a concept the programme defines and then applies — and that it has no connection to the rest of Haiti's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Haiti's own course, in its own order.
