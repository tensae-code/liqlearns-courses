# Citizen Participation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — Participation, concept and application?
a) Citizen participation in promoting citizenship is taught as a concept first, then in its application — what it is, and how it is done.  b) it has nothing to do with how Haiti governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Citizen participation in promoting citizenship is taught as a concept first, then in its application — what it is, and how it is done.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) citizen participation is the citizen taking part in promoting citizenship itself — a concept the programme defines and then applies.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Haiti
**Answer: a) citizen participation is the citizen taking part in promoting citizenship itself — a concept the programme defines and then applies.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
