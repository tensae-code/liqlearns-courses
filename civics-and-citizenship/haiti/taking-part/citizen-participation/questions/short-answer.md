# Citizen Participation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "Participation, concept and application" teach?
**Answer:** Citizen participation in promoting citizenship is taught as a concept first, then in its application — what it is, and how it is done.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** Citizen participation is the citizen taking part in promoting citizenship itself — a concept the programme defines and then applies.
