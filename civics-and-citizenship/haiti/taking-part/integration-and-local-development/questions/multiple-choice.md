# Integration and Local Development — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — Taking part in local development?
a) The NS3 progression grid's second theme is citizens' integration and participation in local development — belonging made practical, where you live.  b) it has nothing to do with how Haiti governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The NS3 progression grid's second theme is citizens' integration and participation in local development — belonging made practical, where you live.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) citizens develop their own place by integrating and taking part in its local development — the NS3 grid gives participation a theme of its own.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Haiti
**Answer: a) citizens develop their own place by integrating and taking part in its local development — the NS3 grid gives participation a theme of its own.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
