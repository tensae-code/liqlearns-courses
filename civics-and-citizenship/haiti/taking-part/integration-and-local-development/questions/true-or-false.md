# Integration and Local Development — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The NS3 progression grid's second theme is citizens' integration and participation in local development — belonging made practical, where you live.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that citizens develop their own place by integrating and taking part in its local development — the NS3 grid gives participation a theme of its own — and that it has no connection to the rest of Haiti's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Haiti's own course, in its own order.
