# Integration and Local Development

## Do it first

Name one thing your town or neighbourhood needs, and one way ordinary people could help build it. This lesson is about exactly that help.

The NS3 progression grid's second theme is citizens' integration and participation in local development — belonging made practical, where you live.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: citizens develop their own place by integrating and taking part in its local development — the NS3 grid gives participation a theme of its own. Give one real example from your own life or from the Haiti you live in, and name the lesson's key words: local development, integration, participation. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: citizens develop their own place by integrating and taking part in its local development — the NS3 grid gives participation a theme of its own.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
