# Video script — Democracy as Citizen Participation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Democracy, in the ns3 programme's closing themes, is citizen participation itself — integration, participation and the exercise of citizenship understood together.

The NS3 grid closes with democracy understood as citizen participation, and with comprehending integration, participation and the exercise of citizenship as one practice.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
