# Democracy as Citizen Participation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — Participation as democracy?
a) The NS3 grid closes with democracy understood as citizen participation, and with comprehending integration, participation and the exercise of citizenship as one practice.  b) it has nothing to do with how Haiti governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations

**Answer: a) democracy, in the NS3 programme's closing themes, is citizen participation itself — integration, participation and the exercise of citizenship understood together.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
