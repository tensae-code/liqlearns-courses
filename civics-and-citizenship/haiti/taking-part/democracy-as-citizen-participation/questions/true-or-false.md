# Democracy as Citizen Participation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The NS3 grid closes with democracy understood as citizen participation, and with comprehending integration, participation and the exercise of citizenship as one practice.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that democracy, in the NS3 programme's closing themes, is citizen participation itself — integration, participation and the exercise of citizenship understood together — and that it has no connection to the rest of Haiti's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Haiti's own course, in its own order.
