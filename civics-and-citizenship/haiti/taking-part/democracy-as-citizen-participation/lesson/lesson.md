# Democracy as Citizen Participation

## Do it first

Say what democracy is, without using the word vote. This lesson's answer: citizens taking part.

The NS3 grid closes with democracy understood as citizen participation, and with comprehending integration, participation and the exercise of citizenship as one practice.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: democracy, in the NS3 programme's closing themes, is citizen participation itself — integration, participation and the exercise of citizenship understood together. Give one real example from your own life or from the Haiti you live in, and name the lesson's key words: democracy, participation, exercise of citizenship. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: democracy, in the NS3 programme's closing themes, is citizen participation itself — integration, participation and the exercise of citizenship understood together.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
