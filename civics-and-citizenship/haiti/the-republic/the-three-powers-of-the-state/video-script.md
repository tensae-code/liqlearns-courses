# Video script — The Three Powers of the State

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The state's power is divided into three constituted powers, each with its own principles, mission and limits — none may swallow the others.

The programme teaches the three constituted powers of the state: their principles, their missions, and how each one functions.

Each power is delimited — it has a boundary. Knowing where one power ends and another begins is how the citizen sees that the division is real.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
