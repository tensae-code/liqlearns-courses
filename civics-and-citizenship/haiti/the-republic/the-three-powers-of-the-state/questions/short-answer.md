# The Three Powers of the State — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "The three powers and their missions" teach?
**Answer:** The programme teaches the three constituted powers of the state: their principles, their missions, and how each one functions.

**2.** In your own words, what does the part "Where each power stops" teach?
**Answer:** Each power is delimited — it has a boundary. Knowing where one power ends and another begins is how the citizen sees that the division is real.

**3.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** The state's power is divided into three constituted powers, each with its own principles, mission and limits — none may swallow the others.
