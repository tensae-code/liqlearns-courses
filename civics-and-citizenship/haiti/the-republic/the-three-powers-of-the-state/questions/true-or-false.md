# The Three Powers of the State — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The programme teaches the three constituted powers of the state: their principles, their missions, and how each one functions.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Each power is delimited — it has a boundary. Knowing where one power ends and another begins is how the citizen sees that the division is real.
**Answer: True**

**3.** This lesson teaches that the state's power is divided into three constituted powers, each with its own principles, mission and limits — none may swallow the others — and that it has no connection to the rest of Haiti's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Haiti's own course, in its own order.
