# The Three Powers of the State — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — The three powers and their missions?
a) The programme teaches the three constituted powers of the state: their principles, their missions, and how each one functions.  b) it has nothing to do with how Haiti governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The programme teaches the three constituted powers of the state: their principles, their missions, and how each one functions.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — Where each power stops?
a) Each power is delimited — it has a boundary. Knowing where one power ends and another begins is how the citizen sees that the division is real.  b) it has nothing to do with how Haiti governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Each power is delimited — it has a boundary. Knowing where one power ends and another begins is how the citizen sees that the division is real.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**3.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) the state's power is divided into three constituted powers, each with its own principles, mission and limits — none may swallow the others.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Haiti
**Answer: a) the state's power is divided into three constituted powers, each with its own principles, mission and limits — none may swallow the others.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
