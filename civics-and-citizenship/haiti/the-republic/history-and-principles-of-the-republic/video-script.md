# Video script — History and Principles of the Republic

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A republic is a way of organising a country — haiti's republic has a history, principles and values, among them the separation of powers.

The programme follows the history of the republic as a form of political organisation — how this way of ruling a country arose and what it replaced.

The Republic stands on principles and values the citizen must know, including the separation of powers — no single power holding the whole state.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
