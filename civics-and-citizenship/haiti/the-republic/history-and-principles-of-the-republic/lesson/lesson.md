# History and Principles of the Republic

## Do it first

Say what you think the word republic means, and name one rule you think a republic must keep. This lesson traces where the idea comes from and what it stands for.

## Sub lesson 1 — The republic through history

The programme follows the history of the republic as a form of political organisation — how this way of ruling a country arose and what it replaced.

## Sub lesson 2 — Principles and values of the Republic

The Republic stands on principles and values the citizen must know, including the separation of powers — no single power holding the whole state.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: a republic is a way of organising a country — Haiti's Republic has a history, principles and values, among them the separation of powers. Give one real example from your own life or from the Haiti you live in, and name the lesson's key words: republic, principles, values, separation of powers. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
