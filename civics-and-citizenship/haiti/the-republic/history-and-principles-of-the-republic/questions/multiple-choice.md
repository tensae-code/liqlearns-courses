# History and Principles of the Republic — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — The republic through history?
a) The programme follows the history of the republic as a form of political organisation — how this way of ruling a country arose and what it replaced.  b) it has nothing to do with how Haiti governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The programme follows the history of the republic as a form of political organisation — how this way of ruling a country arose and what it replaced.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — Principles and values of the Republic?
a) The Republic stands on principles and values the citizen must know, including the separation of powers — no single power holding the whole state.  b) it has nothing to do with how Haiti governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The Republic stands on principles and values the citizen must know, including the separation of powers — no single power holding the whole state.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**3.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) a republic is a way of organising a country — Haiti's Republic has a history, principles and values, among them the separation of powers.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Haiti
**Answer: a) a republic is a way of organising a country — Haiti's Republic has a history, principles and values, among them the separation of powers.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
