# History and Principles of the Republic — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The programme follows the history of the republic as a form of political organisation — how this way of ruling a country arose and what it replaced.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The Republic stands on principles and values the citizen must know, including the separation of powers — no single power holding the whole state.
**Answer: True**

**3.** This lesson teaches that a republic is a way of organising a country — Haiti's Republic has a history, principles and values, among them the separation of powers — and that it has no connection to the rest of Haiti's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Haiti's own course, in its own order.
