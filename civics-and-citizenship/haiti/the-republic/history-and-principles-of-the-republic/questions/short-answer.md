# History and Principles of the Republic — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "The republic through history" teach?
**Answer:** The programme follows the history of the republic as a form of political organisation — how this way of ruling a country arose and what it replaced.

**2.** In your own words, what does the part "Principles and values of the Republic" teach?
**Answer:** The Republic stands on principles and values the citizen must know, including the separation of powers — no single power holding the whole state.

**3.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** A republic is a way of organising a country — haiti's republic has a history, principles and values, among them the separation of powers.
