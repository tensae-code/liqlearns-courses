# Hands-on — The Citizenship Crisis

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name one way you see people losing trust in each other or in institutions around you. This lesson names that sickness the way Haiti's own programme names it.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Haiti's programmes name a crisis of citizenship openly — collapsed social values, and crises of responsibility, leadership and institutions — and ask pupils to understand it and help resolve it. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
