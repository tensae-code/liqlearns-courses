# The Citizenship Crisis

## Do it first

Name one way you see people losing trust in each other or in institutions around you. This lesson names that sickness the way Haiti's own programme names it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Naming the crisis

The crisis of citizenship shows itself as a collapse of social values, and as crises of responsibility, of leadership, and of institutions.

## Sub lesson 2 — Working to resolve it

The pupil is not only told to understand the crisis but to try to resolve it — the crisis is the reason citizenship education exists.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Haiti's programmes name a crisis of citizenship openly — collapsed social values, and crises of responsibility, leadership and institutions — and ask pupils to understand it and help resolve it. Give one real example from your own life or from the Haiti you live in, and name the lesson's key words: crisis of citizenship, values, responsibility, leadership. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
