# The Citizenship Crisis — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The crisis of citizenship shows itself as a collapse of social values, and as crises of responsibility, of leadership, and of institutions.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The pupil is not only told to understand the crisis but to try to resolve it — the crisis is the reason citizenship education exists.
**Answer: True**

**3.** This lesson teaches that Haiti's programmes name a crisis of citizenship openly — collapsed social values, and crises of responsibility, leadership and institutions — and ask pupils to understand it and help resolve it — and that it has no connection to the rest of Haiti's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Haiti's own course, in its own order.
