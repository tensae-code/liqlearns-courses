# The Citizenship Crisis — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "Naming the crisis" teach?
**Answer:** The crisis of citizenship shows itself as a collapse of social values, and as crises of responsibility, of leadership, and of institutions.

**2.** In your own words, what does the part "Working to resolve it" teach?
**Answer:** The pupil is not only told to understand the crisis but to try to resolve it — the crisis is the reason citizenship education exists.

**3.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** Haiti's programmes name a crisis of citizenship openly — collapsed social values, and crises of responsibility, leadership and institutions — and ask pupils to understand it and help resolve it.
