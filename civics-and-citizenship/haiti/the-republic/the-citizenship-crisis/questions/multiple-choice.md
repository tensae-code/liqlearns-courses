# The Citizenship Crisis — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — Naming the crisis?
a) The crisis of citizenship shows itself as a collapse of social values, and as crises of responsibility, of leadership, and of institutions.  b) it has nothing to do with how Haiti governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The crisis of citizenship shows itself as a collapse of social values, and as crises of responsibility, of leadership, and of institutions.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — Working to resolve it?
a) The pupil is not only told to understand the crisis but to try to resolve it — the crisis is the reason citizenship education exists.  b) it has nothing to do with how Haiti governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The pupil is not only told to understand the crisis but to try to resolve it — the crisis is the reason citizenship education exists.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**3.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Haiti's programmes name a crisis of citizenship openly — collapsed social values, and crises of responsibility, leadership and institutions — and ask pupils to understand it and help resolve it.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Haiti
**Answer: a) Haiti's programmes name a crisis of citizenship openly — collapsed social values, and crises of responsibility, leadership and institutions — and ask pupils to understand it and help resolve it.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
