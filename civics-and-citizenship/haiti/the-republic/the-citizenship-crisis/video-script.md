# Video script — The Citizenship Crisis

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Haiti's programmes name a crisis of citizenship openly — collapsed social values, and crises of responsibility, leadership and institutions — and ask pupils to understand it and help resolve it.

The crisis of citizenship shows itself as a collapse of social values, and as crises of responsibility, of leadership, and of institutions.

The pupil is not only told to understand the crisis but to try to resolve it — the crisis is the reason citizenship education exists.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
