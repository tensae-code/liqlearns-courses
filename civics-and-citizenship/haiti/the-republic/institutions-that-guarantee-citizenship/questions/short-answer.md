# Institutions That Guarantee Citizenship — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "The guarantors" teach?
**Answer:** The programme names the institutions that guarantee citizenship: justice, the Office de la Protection du Citoyen (OPC), parliament, and the organisations of civil society — each with its part in protecting the citizen.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** Citizenship is protected by named institutions — justice, the office de la protection du citoyen (opc), parliament, and civil-society organisations.
