# Institutions That Guarantee Citizenship — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The programme names the institutions that guarantee citizenship: justice, the Office de la Protection du Citoyen (OPC), parliament, and the organisations of civil society — each with its part in protecting the citizen.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that citizenship is protected by named institutions — justice, the Office de la Protection du Citoyen (OPC), parliament, and civil-society organisations — and that it has no connection to the rest of Haiti's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Haiti's own course, in its own order.
