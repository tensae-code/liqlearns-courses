# Institutions That Guarantee Citizenship — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — The guarantors?
a) The programme names the institutions that guarantee citizenship: justice, the Office de la Protection du Citoyen (OPC), parliament, and the organisations of civil society — each with its part in protecting the citizen.  b) it has nothing to do with how Haiti governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The programme names the institutions that guarantee citizenship: justice, the Office de la Protection du Citoyen (OPC), parliament, and the organisations of civil society — each with its part in protecting the citizen.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) citizenship is protected by named institutions — justice, the Office de la Protection du Citoyen (OPC), parliament, and civil-society organisations.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Haiti
**Answer: a) citizenship is protected by named institutions — justice, the Office de la Protection du Citoyen (OPC), parliament, and civil-society organisations.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
