The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

This lesson is about one thing.
Citizenship is protected by named institutions — justice, the office de la protection du citoyen (opc), parliament, and civil-society organisations.

The programme names the institutions that guarantee citizenship: justice, the Office de la Protection du Citoyen (OPC), parliament, and the organisations of civil society — each with its part in protecting the citizen.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
