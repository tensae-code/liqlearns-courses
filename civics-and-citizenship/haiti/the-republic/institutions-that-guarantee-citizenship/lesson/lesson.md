# Institutions That Guarantee Citizenship

## Do it first

If your rights were trampled on tomorrow, where would you go? This lesson names the institutions whose job is to protect the citizen.

The programme names the institutions that guarantee citizenship: justice, the Office de la Protection du Citoyen (OPC), parliament, and the organisations of civil society — each with its part in protecting the citizen.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: citizenship is protected by named institutions — justice, the Office de la Protection du Citoyen (OPC), parliament, and civil-society organisations. Give one real example from your own life or from the Haiti you live in, and name the lesson's key words: OPC, justice, parliament, civil society, protection. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
