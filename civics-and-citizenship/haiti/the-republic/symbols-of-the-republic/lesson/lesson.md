# Symbols of the Republic

## Do it first

Draw or describe one symbol of Haiti's Republic from memory. This lesson is about knowing the symbols by which the Republic is recognised — and being able to present them.

The programme teaches the pupil to identify the symbols of the Republic and to present them — to say what each one is and what it stands for.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the Republic is recognised by its symbols — a citizen knows them, can name them, and can present what they stand for. Give one real example from your own life or from the Haiti you live in, and name the lesson's key words: symbols, republic, recognition. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: the Republic is recognised by its symbols — a citizen knows them, can name them, and can present what they stand for.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
