# Symbols of the Republic — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The programme teaches the pupil to identify the symbols of the Republic and to present them — to say what each one is and what it stands for.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that the Republic is recognised by its symbols — a citizen knows them, can name them, and can present what they stand for — and that it has no connection to the rest of Haiti's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Haiti's own course, in its own order.
