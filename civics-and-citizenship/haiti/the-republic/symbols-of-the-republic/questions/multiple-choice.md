# Symbols of the Republic — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — Knowing the symbols?
a) The programme teaches the pupil to identify the symbols of the Republic and to present them — to say what each one is and what it stands for.  b) it has nothing to do with how Haiti governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The programme teaches the pupil to identify the symbols of the Republic and to present them — to say what each one is and what it stands for.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) the Republic is recognised by its symbols — a citizen knows them, can name them, and can present what they stand for.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Haiti
**Answer: a) the Republic is recognised by its symbols — a citizen knows them, can name them, and can present what they stand for.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
