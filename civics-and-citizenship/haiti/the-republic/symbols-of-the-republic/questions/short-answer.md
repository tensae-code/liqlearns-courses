# Symbols of the Republic — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "Knowing the symbols" teach?
**Answer:** The programme teaches the pupil to identify the symbols of the Republic and to present them — to say what each one is and what it stands for.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** The republic is recognised by its symbols — a citizen knows them, can name them, and can present what they stand for.
