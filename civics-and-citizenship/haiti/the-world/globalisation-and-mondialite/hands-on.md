# Hands-on — Globalisation and Mondialité

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name one thing in your daily life that comes from far away. This lesson asks what the world's closeness does — to the world, and to Haiti.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: globalisation and mondialité are not the same word for the same thing — the programme defines both, tells them apart, and analyses globalisation's effects in Haiti. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
