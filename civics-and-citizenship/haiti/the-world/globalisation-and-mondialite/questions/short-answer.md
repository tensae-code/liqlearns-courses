# Globalisation and Mondialité — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "Two words, told apart" teach?
**Answer:** Globalisation and mondialité are each defined, and the difference between them is taught — precision about the world's interconnection.

**2.** In your own words, what does the part "Globalisation's effects in Haiti" teach?
**Answer:** The analysis comes home: globalisation's effects in Haiti are examined, so the world citizen judges the process from where they stand.

**3.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** Globalisation and mondialité are not the same word for the same thing — the programme defines both, tells them apart, and analyses globalisation's effects in haiti.
