# Globalisation and Mondialité

## Do it first

Name one thing in your daily life that comes from far away. This lesson asks what the world's closeness does — to the world, and to Haiti.

## Sub lesson 1 — Two words, told apart

Globalisation and mondialité are each defined, and the difference between them is taught — precision about the world's interconnection.

## Sub lesson 2 — Globalisation's effects in Haiti

The analysis comes home: globalisation's effects in Haiti are examined, so the world citizen judges the process from where they stand.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: globalisation and mondialité are not the same word for the same thing — the programme defines both, tells them apart, and analyses globalisation's effects in Haiti. Give one real example from your own life or from the Haiti you live in, and name the lesson's key words: globalisation, mondialité, Haiti. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
