# Video script — Globalisation and Mondialité

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Globalisation and mondialité are not the same word for the same thing — the programme defines both, tells them apart, and analyses globalisation's effects in haiti.

Globalisation and mondialité are each defined, and the difference between them is taught — precision about the world's interconnection.

The analysis comes home: globalisation's effects in Haiti are examined, so the world citizen judges the process from where they stand.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
