# Video script — Rights and Social Justice

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

This lesson is about one thing.
The universal declaration of human rights is read and commented in class — and citizenship is tied to social justice, in values and in behaviour.

The Universal Declaration of Human Rights is read and commented — the pupil meets the text itself, not only a summary of it.

The programme links citizenship to social justice, through the values and behaviours of citizenship education: a citizen's rights imply a fairer society.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
