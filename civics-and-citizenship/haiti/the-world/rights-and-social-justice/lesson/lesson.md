# Rights and Social Justice

## Do it first

Name one right you believe belongs to every human being, everywhere. This lesson reads the document that declares them.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Universal Declaration, read

The Universal Declaration of Human Rights is read and commented — the pupil meets the text itself, not only a summary of it.

## Sub lesson 2 — Citizenship and social justice

The programme links citizenship to social justice, through the values and behaviours of citizenship education: a citizen's rights imply a fairer society.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the Universal Declaration of Human Rights is read and commented in class — and citizenship is tied to social justice, in values and in behaviour. Give one real example from your own life or from the Haiti you live in, and name the lesson's key words: human rights, Universal Declaration, social justice. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
