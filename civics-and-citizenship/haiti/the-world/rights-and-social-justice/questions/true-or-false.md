# Rights and Social Justice — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Universal Declaration of Human Rights is read and commented — the pupil meets the text itself, not only a summary of it.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The programme links citizenship to social justice, through the values and behaviours of citizenship education: a citizen's rights imply a fairer society.
**Answer: True**

**3.** This lesson teaches that the Universal Declaration of Human Rights is read and commented in class — and citizenship is tied to social justice, in values and in behaviour — and that it has no connection to the rest of Haiti's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Haiti's own course, in its own order.
