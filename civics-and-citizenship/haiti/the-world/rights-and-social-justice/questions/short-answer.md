# Rights and Social Justice — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "The Universal Declaration, read" teach?
**Answer:** The Universal Declaration of Human Rights is read and commented — the pupil meets the text itself, not only a summary of it.

**2.** In your own words, what does the part "Citizenship and social justice" teach?
**Answer:** The programme links citizenship to social justice, through the values and behaviours of citizenship education: a citizen's rights imply a fairer society.

**3.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** The universal declaration of human rights is read and commented in class — and citizenship is tied to social justice, in values and in behaviour.
