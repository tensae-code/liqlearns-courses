# Rights and Social Justice — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — The Universal Declaration, read?
a) The Universal Declaration of Human Rights is read and commented — the pupil meets the text itself, not only a summary of it.  b) it has nothing to do with how Haiti governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The Universal Declaration of Human Rights is read and commented — the pupil meets the text itself, not only a summary of it.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — Citizenship and social justice?
a) The programme links citizenship to social justice, through the values and behaviours of citizenship education: a citizen's rights imply a fairer society.  b) it has nothing to do with how Haiti governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The programme links citizenship to social justice, through the values and behaviours of citizenship education: a citizen's rights imply a fairer society.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**3.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) the Universal Declaration of Human Rights is read and commented in class — and citizenship is tied to social justice, in values and in behaviour.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Haiti
**Answer: a) the Universal Declaration of Human Rights is read and commented in class — and citizenship is tied to social justice, in values and in behaviour.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
