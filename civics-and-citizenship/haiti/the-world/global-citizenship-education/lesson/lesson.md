# Global Citizenship Education

## Do it first

Say whether a Haitian citizen should care about people in countries they will never see. This lesson is the programme's answer.

## Sub lesson 1 — Approaches to world citizenship

World citizenship education is approached two ways in the programme: the Enlightenment approach and the post-modern approach.

## Sub lesson 2 — Domains and foundations

The programme lays out the great thematic domains of citizenship education and its foundations — the ground the world citizen stands on.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: citizenship education reaches the whole world — the programme teaches world citizenship education through its approaches, its great thematic domains and its foundations. Give one real example from your own life or from the Haiti you live in, and name the lesson's key words: world citizenship, Enlightenment, post-modern. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
