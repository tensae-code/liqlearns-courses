# Hands-on — Global Citizenship Education

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Say whether a Haitian citizen should care about people in countries they will never see. This lesson is the programme's answer.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: citizenship education reaches the whole world — the programme teaches world citizenship education through its approaches, its great thematic domains and its foundations. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
