# Global Citizenship Education — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — Approaches to world citizenship?
a) World citizenship education is approached two ways in the programme: the Enlightenment approach and the post-modern approach.  b) it has nothing to do with how Haiti governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) World citizenship education is approached two ways in the programme: the Enlightenment approach and the post-modern approach.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — Domains and foundations?
a) The programme lays out the great thematic domains of citizenship education and its foundations — the ground the world citizen stands on.  b) it has nothing to do with how Haiti governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The programme lays out the great thematic domains of citizenship education and its foundations — the ground the world citizen stands on.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**3.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) citizenship education reaches the whole world — the programme teaches world citizenship education through its approaches, its great thematic domains and its foundations.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Haiti
**Answer: a) citizenship education reaches the whole world — the programme teaches world citizenship education through its approaches, its great thematic domains and its foundations.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
