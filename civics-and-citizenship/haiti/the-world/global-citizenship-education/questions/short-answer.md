# Global Citizenship Education — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "Approaches to world citizenship" teach?
**Answer:** World citizenship education is approached two ways in the programme: the Enlightenment approach and the post-modern approach.

**2.** In your own words, what does the part "Domains and foundations" teach?
**Answer:** The programme lays out the great thematic domains of citizenship education and its foundations — the ground the world citizen stands on.

**3.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** Citizenship education reaches the whole world — the programme teaches world citizenship education through its approaches, its great thematic domains and its foundations.
