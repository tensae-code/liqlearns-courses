# Global Citizenship Education — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** World citizenship education is approached two ways in the programme: the Enlightenment approach and the post-modern approach.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The programme lays out the great thematic domains of citizenship education and its foundations — the ground the world citizen stands on.
**Answer: True**

**3.** This lesson teaches that citizenship education reaches the whole world — the programme teaches world citizenship education through its approaches, its great thematic domains and its foundations — and that it has no connection to the rest of Haiti's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Haiti's own course, in its own order.
