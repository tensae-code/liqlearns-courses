# Video script — Global Citizenship Education

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

This lesson is about one thing.
Citizenship education reaches the whole world — the programme teaches world citizenship education through its approaches, its great thematic domains and its foundations.

World citizenship education is approached two ways in the programme: the Enlightenment approach and the post-modern approach.

The programme lays out the great thematic domains of citizenship education and its foundations — the ground the world citizen stands on.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
