# The Haitian Constitution

## Do it first

Say what you think a constitution is, in one sentence. This lesson presents Haiti's constitution — and the long line of constitutions behind it.

## Sub lesson 1 — What a constitution is

The programme presents and defines the constitution: what kind of law it is, and what it does for a country.

## Sub lesson 2 — Haiti's constitutions, 1805–1987

Haiti's constitutional history runs from 1805 to 1987; the pupil studies that history, the constitution in force, and why the constitution matters to a citizen.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: a constitution is the country's founding law — Haiti's runs from 1805 to 1987, and the citizen knows the constitution in force and why it matters to them. Give one real example from your own life or from the Haiti you live in, and name the lesson's key words: constitution, 1805, 1987, founding law. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
