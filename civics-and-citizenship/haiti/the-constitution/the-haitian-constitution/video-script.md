# Video script — The Haitian Constitution

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A constitution is the country's founding law — haiti's runs from 1805 to 1987, and the citizen knows the constitution in force and why it matters to them.

The programme presents and defines the constitution: what kind of law it is, and what it does for a country.

Haiti's constitutional history runs from 1805 to 1987; the pupil studies that history, the constitution in force, and why the constitution matters to a citizen.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
