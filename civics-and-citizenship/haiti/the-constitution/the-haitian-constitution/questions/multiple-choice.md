# The Haitian Constitution — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — What a constitution is?
a) The programme presents and defines the constitution: what kind of law it is, and what it does for a country.  b) it has nothing to do with how Haiti governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The programme presents and defines the constitution: what kind of law it is, and what it does for a country.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — Haiti's constitutions, 1805–1987?
a) Haiti's constitutional history runs from 1805 to 1987; the pupil studies that history, the constitution in force, and why the constitution matters to a citizen.  b) it has nothing to do with how Haiti governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Haiti's constitutional history runs from 1805 to 1987; the pupil studies that history, the constitution in force, and why the constitution matters to a citizen.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**3.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) a constitution is the country's founding law — Haiti's runs from 1805 to 1987, and the citizen knows the constitution in force and why it matters to them.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Haiti
**Answer: a) a constitution is the country's founding law — Haiti's runs from 1805 to 1987, and the citizen knows the constitution in force and why it matters to them.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
