# The Haitian Constitution — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "What a constitution is" teach?
**Answer:** The programme presents and defines the constitution: what kind of law it is, and what it does for a country.

**2.** In your own words, what does the part "Haiti's constitutions, 1805–1987" teach?
**Answer:** Haiti's constitutional history runs from 1805 to 1987; the pupil studies that history, the constitution in force, and why the constitution matters to a citizen.

**3.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** A constitution is the country's founding law — haiti's runs from 1805 to 1987, and the citizen knows the constitution in force and why it matters to them.
