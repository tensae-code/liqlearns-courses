# The Haitian Constitution — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The programme presents and defines the constitution: what kind of law it is, and what it does for a country.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Haiti's constitutional history runs from 1805 to 1987; the pupil studies that history, the constitution in force, and why the constitution matters to a citizen.
**Answer: True**

**3.** This lesson teaches that a constitution is the country's founding law — Haiti's runs from 1805 to 1987, and the citizen knows the constitution in force and why it matters to them — and that it has no connection to the rest of Haiti's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Haiti's own course, in its own order.
