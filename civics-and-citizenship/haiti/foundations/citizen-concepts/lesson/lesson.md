# Citizen Concepts

## Do it first

Name one time you helped your street, your school or your town. This lesson gives names to what you were doing — and to the bigger ideas it belongs to.

## Sub lesson 1 — Responsibility, engagement and solidarity

Civic responsibility is answering for your part in the common life; citizen engagement is taking part in it; citizen solidarity is standing with other citizens, near and far.

## Sub lesson 2 — Democracy, nationality and world citizenship

Participative democracy means citizens do not only vote — they take part. Nationality is the legal bond to one's country, and world citizenship widens the citizen's concern to the whole world.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: a citizen works with a small set of big ideas — civic responsibility, participative democracy, citizen engagement, world citizenship, nationality, and citizen solidarity. Give one real example from your own life or from the Haiti you live in, and name the lesson's key words: civic responsibility, participative democracy, engagement, solidarity, nationality. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
