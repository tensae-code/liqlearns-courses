# Hands-on — Citizen Concepts

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name one time you helped your street, your school or your town. This lesson gives names to what you were doing — and to the bigger ideas it belongs to.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: a citizen works with a small set of big ideas — civic responsibility, participative democracy, citizen engagement, world citizenship, nationality, and citizen solidarity. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
