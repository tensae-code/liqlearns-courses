# Citizen Concepts — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "Responsibility, engagement and solidarity" teach?
**Answer:** Civic responsibility is answering for your part in the common life; citizen engagement is taking part in it; citizen solidarity is standing with other citizens, near and far.

**2.** In your own words, what does the part "Democracy, nationality and world citizenship" teach?
**Answer:** Participative democracy means citizens do not only vote — they take part. Nationality is the legal bond to one's country, and world citizenship widens the citizen's concern to the whole world.

**3.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** A citizen works with a small set of big ideas — civic responsibility, participative democracy, citizen engagement, world citizenship, nationality, and citizen solidarity.
