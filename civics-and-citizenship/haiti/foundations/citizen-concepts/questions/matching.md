# Citizen Concepts — matching

Match each part of the lesson with its teaching.

**1.** Responsibility, engagement and solidarity — a)
**2.** Democracy, nationality and world citizenship — b)


Meanings:
a) Civic responsibility is answering for your part in the common life; citizen engagement is taking part in it; citizen solidarity is standing with other citizens, near and far.
b) Participative democracy means citizens do not only vote — they take part. Nationality is the legal bond to one's country, and world citizenship widens the citizen's concern to the whole world.

**Answer key:** 1. a 2. b
