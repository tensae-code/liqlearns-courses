# Citizen Concepts — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — Responsibility, engagement and solidarity?
a) Civic responsibility is answering for your part in the common life; citizen engagement is taking part in it; citizen solidarity is standing with other citizens, near and far.  b) it has nothing to do with how Haiti governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Civic responsibility is answering for your part in the common life; citizen engagement is taking part in it; citizen solidarity is standing with other citizens, near and far.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — Democracy, nationality and world citizenship?
a) Participative democracy means citizens do not only vote — they take part. Nationality is the legal bond to one's country, and world citizenship widens the citizen's concern to the whole world.  b) it has nothing to do with how Haiti governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Participative democracy means citizens do not only vote — they take part. Nationality is the legal bond to one's country, and world citizenship widens the citizen's concern to the whole world.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**3.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) a citizen works with a small set of big ideas — civic responsibility, participative democracy, citizen engagement, world citizenship, nationality, and citizen solidarity.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Haiti
**Answer: a) a citizen works with a small set of big ideas — civic responsibility, participative democracy, citizen engagement, world citizenship, nationality, and citizen solidarity.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
