# Lesson plan — Citizen Concepts

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Haiti · **Title:** Foundations · **Lesson:** Citizen Concepts
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — The Republic and the Citizen (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a citizen works with a small set of big ideas — civic responsibility, participative democracy, citizen engagement, world citizenship, nationality, and citizen solidarity. This lesson stands on a teacher programme alone — evidence only, labelled as such.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Responsibility, engagement and solidarity** — Civic responsibility is answering for your part in the common life; citizen engagement is taking part in it; citizen solidarity is standing with other citizens, near and far.
2. **Democracy, nationality and world citizenship** — Participative democracy means citizens do not only vote — they take part. Nationality is the legal bond to one's country, and world citizenship widens the citizen's concern to the whole world.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
