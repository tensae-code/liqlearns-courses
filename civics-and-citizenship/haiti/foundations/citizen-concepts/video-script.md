# Video script — Citizen Concepts

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A citizen works with a small set of big ideas — civic responsibility, participative democracy, citizen engagement, world citizenship, nationality, and citizen solidarity.

Civic responsibility is answering for your part in the common life; citizen engagement is taking part in it; citizen solidarity is standing with other citizens, near and far.

Participative democracy means citizens do not only vote — they take part. Nationality is the legal bond to one's country, and world citizenship widens the citizen's concern to the whole world.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
