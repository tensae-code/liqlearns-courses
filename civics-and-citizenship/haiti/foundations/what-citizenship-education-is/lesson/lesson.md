# What Citizenship Education Is

## Do it first

Write down, in your own words, what you think the word citizenship means, and one thing a good citizen does. This lesson starts from the definitions themselves.

## Sub lesson 1 — Education, citizenship, citizenship education

The programme defines its three key words first: education, citizenship, and citizenship education itself — looked at two ways, the legal-political way (the citizen's place in the state) and the psychological way (the citizen's own sense of belonging and duty).

## Sub lesson 2 — Building a new Haiti

Citizenship education is not taught for its own sake: its role is to form the citizens who will build a new Haiti — a country whose people know their rights, do their duties, and take part.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: citizenship education is the teaching that forms a citizen — what education is, what citizenship is, and how the two together can build a new Haiti. Give one real example from your own life or from the Haiti you live in, and name the lesson's key words: citizenship education, citizenship, education. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
