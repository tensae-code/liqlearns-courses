# Hands-on — What Citizenship Education Is

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write down, in your own words, what you think the word citizenship means, and one thing a good citizen does. This lesson starts from the definitions themselves.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: citizenship education is the teaching that forms a citizen — what education is, what citizenship is, and how the two together can build a new Haiti. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
