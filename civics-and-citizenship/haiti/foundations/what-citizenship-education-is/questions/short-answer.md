# What Citizenship Education Is — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "Education, citizenship, citizenship education" teach?
**Answer:** The programme defines its three key words first: education, citizenship, and citizenship education itself — looked at two ways, the legal-political way (the citizen's place in the state) and the psychological way (the citizen's own sense of belonging and duty).

**2.** In your own words, what does the part "Building a new Haiti" teach?
**Answer:** Citizenship education is not taught for its own sake: its role is to form the citizens who will build a new Haiti — a country whose people know their rights, do their duties, and take part.

**3.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** Citizenship education is the teaching that forms a citizen — what education is, what citizenship is, and how the two together can build a new haiti.
