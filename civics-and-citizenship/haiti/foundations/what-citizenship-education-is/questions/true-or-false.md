# What Citizenship Education Is — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The programme defines its three key words first: education, citizenship, and citizenship education itself — looked at two ways, the legal-political way (the citizen's place in the state) and the psychological way (the citizen's own sense of belonging and duty).
**Answer: True**

**2.** Citizenship education is not taught for its own sake: its role is to form the citizens who will build a new Haiti — a country whose people know their rights, do their duties, and take part.
**Answer: True**

**3.** This lesson teaches that citizenship education is the teaching that forms a citizen — what education is, what citizenship is, and how the two together can build a new Haiti — and that it has no connection to the rest of Haiti's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Haiti's own course, in its own order.
