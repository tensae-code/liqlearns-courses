# What Citizenship Education Is — matching

Match each part of the lesson with its teaching.

**1.** Education, citizenship, citizenship education — a)
**2.** Building a new Haiti — b)


Meanings:
a) The programme defines its three key words first: education, citizenship, and citizenship education itself — looked at two ways, the legal-political way (the citizen's place in the state) and the psychological way (the citizen's own sense of belonging and duty).
b) Citizenship education is not taught for its own sake: its role is to form the citizens who will build a new Haiti — a country whose people know their rights, do their duties, and take part.

**Answer key:** 1. a 2. b
