# Video script — What Citizenship Education Is

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Citizenship education is the teaching that forms a citizen — what education is, what citizenship is, and how the two together can build a new haiti.

The programme defines its three key words first: education, citizenship, and citizenship education itself — looked at two ways, the legal-political way (the citizen's place in the state) and the psychological way (the citizen's own sense of belonging and duty).

Citizenship education is not taught for its own sake: its role is to form the citizens who will build a new Haiti — a country whose people know their rights, do their duties, and take part.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
