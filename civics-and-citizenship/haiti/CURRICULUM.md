# Civics and Citizenship — Haiti course — CURRICULUM (the path)

One course for Haiti inside the Civics and Citizenship
folder, organised by **levels, not grades**: a learner climbs
when they have proved the level, not when a school calendar
says so. The school grades the books come from appear only in
each lesson's sources (provenance), never as the course's
organisation. The path follows Haiti's own order as its
programmes lay it out — the MENFP programmes' own four-year
climb: Republic and citizen → constitution and governance →
world and environment → ethics and the city. The two filings
are combined into this one course: the main filing
(`haiti-placements.md`, the five MENFP secondary programmes)
carries all 46 placements; the second filing
(`haiti-placements-b.md`) is a record-only programme filing
with no placement lines, carried in ABOUT.md exactly as
filed. Five lessons are taught in two programmes at once —
the 2015 operational programme (NS1) and the 2019 NS2 minimal
programme teach the same identity, government and
school-government lessons — so those five lessons sit at
Level 1, where the 2015 programme first teaches them, and
carry both programmes' provenance rows; NS2's deeper return
to them is named in ABOUT.md.

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in
this course comes from government textbooks — this country's
own Éducation à la citoyenneté programmes — except the
**Tip boxes**: a tip in a box is from us, the people making
LiqLearns. Tips are ours; the lessons are the books'. See
ABOUT.md for the full book list, the record-only programme,
and the acceptance reckoning. **This whole course rests on
teacher programmes alone — there is no Haitian pupil civics
textbook in hand — and it is labelled guides-only
throughout.**

## The levels (the path, in order)

1. **The Republic and the Citizen** — what citizenship
   education is and the citizen concepts; the rights and
   duties of the Haitian citizen; the Republic — its history
   and principles, its symbols, its three powers, the
   institutions that guarantee citizenship, and the
   citizenship crisis named openly; who forms citizens
   (family, school, state; the school forming eco-citizens);
   then, from the 2015 operational programme's fuller NS1
   year, identity and the duty of memory, forms of government
   and governance with public ethics, elections and types of
   vote, the school government, a culture of peace with the
   security institutions and freedom–equality–equity, citizen
   participation, and environmental culture with climate
   change. 22 lessons, in ten titles: `foundations/`
   (2 lessons), `rights-and-duties/` (1 lesson),
   `the-republic/` (5 lessons), `forming-citizens/`
   (2 lessons), `identity/` (2 lessons), `government/`
   (3 lessons), `elections/` (1 lesson), `peace/`
   (3 lessons), `taking-part/` (1 lesson),
   `eco-citizenship/` (2 lessons).
2. **Constitution and Governance** — the Haitian
   constitution (what a constitution is; Haiti's
   constitutions 1805–1987; the constitution in force); a
   culture of peace and non-violence in a democratic society;
   governance as power, government and ethics together;
   local governance through citizens' participation; and
   electoral practices as the exercise of citizens' rights.
   5 lessons, in four titles: `the-constitution/`
   (1 lesson), `peace/` (1 lesson), `government/`
   (2 lessons), `elections/` (1 lesson).
3. **The World, the Environment and Taking Part** — world
   citizenship education and globalisation told apart from
   mondialité; the Universal Declaration of Human Rights and
   social justice; eco-citizenship and climate change with
   pollution; and taking part — integration in local
   development, and democracy itself understood as citizen
   participation. 7 lessons, in three titles: `the-world/`
   (3 lessons), `eco-citizenship/` (2 lessons),
   `taking-part/` (2 lessons).
4. **Ethics, Rights and the City** — bioethics and its moral
   principles; the three generations of human rights, judged
   against their ineffectiveness in Haiti; the ethics of
   responsibility and the diverse principles of justice; and
   the city — organising it for collective well-being,
   living together in tolerance and respect within laïcité,
   and managing it for the common good. 7 lessons, in three
   titles: `ethics/` (3 lessons), `human-rights/`
   (1 lesson), `the-city/` (3 lessons).

**Totals: 41 lessons** (Level 1: 22 · Level 2: 5 · Level 3:
7 · Level 4: 7 — from five teacher programmes: the four 2019
Programmes à compétences minimales, NS1–NS4, and the 2015
Programme Pédagogique Opérationnel for NS1; 46 placements
stocking 41 lessons — five lessons are stocked by two
programmes each).
