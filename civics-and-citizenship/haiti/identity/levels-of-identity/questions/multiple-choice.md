# Levels of Identity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — Personal, social, national, transnational?
a) Identity's components and attributes run in layers: the personal/citizen layer (rights, duties), the social layer (class, category), the national layer, and the transnational layer beyond the country's borders.  b) it has nothing to do with how Haiti governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Identity's components and attributes run in layers: the personal/citizen layer (rights, duties), the social layer (class, category), the national layer, and the transnational layer beyond the country's borders.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
