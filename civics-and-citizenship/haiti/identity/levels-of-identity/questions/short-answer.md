# Levels of Identity — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "Personal, social, national, transnational" teach?
**Answer:** Identity's components and attributes run in layers: the personal/citizen layer (rights, duties), the social layer (class, category), the national layer, and the transnational layer beyond the country's borders.

**2.** In your own words, what does the part "Identity put to work" teach?
**Answer:** Identity is not taught as a label to admire: it is mobilised — used — for a culture of peace, for citizen participation, and for tolerance.

**3.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** Identity has layers — personal and citizen identity (rights and duties), social identity (class and category), national identity, and transnational identity — and knowing them serves peace, participation and tolerance.
