# Levels of Identity — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Identity's components and attributes run in layers: the personal/citizen layer (rights, duties), the social layer (class, category), the national layer, and the transnational layer beyond the country's borders.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Identity is not taught as a label to admire: it is mobilised — used — for a culture of peace, for citizen participation, and for tolerance.
**Answer: True**

**3.** This lesson teaches that identity has layers — personal and citizen identity (rights and duties), social identity (class and category), national identity, and transnational identity — and knowing them serves peace, participation and tolerance — and that it has no connection to the rest of Haiti's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Haiti's own course, in its own order.
