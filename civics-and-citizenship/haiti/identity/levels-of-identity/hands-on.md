# Hands-on — Levels of Identity

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write four words that say who you are: one about you alone, one about your place among people, one about your country, one about you in the world. This lesson names those four layers.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: identity has layers — personal and citizen identity (rights and duties), social identity (class and category), national identity, and transnational identity — and knowing them serves peace, participation and tolerance. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
