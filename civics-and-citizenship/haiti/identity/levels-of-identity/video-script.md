# Video script — Levels of Identity

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Identity has layers — personal and citizen identity (rights and duties), social identity (class and category), national identity, and transnational identity — and knowing them serves peace, participation and tolerance.

Identity's components and attributes run in layers: the personal/citizen layer (rights, duties), the social layer (class, category), the national layer, and the transnational layer beyond the country's borders.

Identity is not taught as a label to admire: it is mobilised — used — for a culture of peace, for citizen participation, and for tolerance.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
