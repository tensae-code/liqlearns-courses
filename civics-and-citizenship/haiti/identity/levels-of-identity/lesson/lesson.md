# Levels of Identity

## Do it first

Write four words that say who you are: one about you alone, one about your place among people, one about your country, one about you in the world. This lesson names those four layers.

## Sub lesson 1 — Personal, social, national, transnational

Identity's components and attributes run in layers: the personal/citizen layer (rights, duties), the social layer (class, category), the national layer, and the transnational layer beyond the country's borders.

## Sub lesson 2 — Identity put to work

Identity is not taught as a label to admire: it is mobilised — used — for a culture of peace, for citizen participation, and for tolerance.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: identity has layers — personal and citizen identity (rights and duties), social identity (class and category), national identity, and transnational identity — and knowing them serves peace, participation and tolerance. Give one real example from your own life or from the Haiti you live in, and name the lesson's key words: identity, personal, social, national, transnational. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
