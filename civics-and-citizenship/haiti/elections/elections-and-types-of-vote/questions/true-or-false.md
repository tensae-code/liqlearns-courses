# Elections and Types of Vote — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Elections are set up by organising bodies — a permanent or provisional electoral council — working under the electoral law and decree.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The programme teaches the typology of votes: the direct, universal vote, and the indirect vote — two different roads from the citizen to the office.
**Answer: True**

**3.** This lesson teaches that elections are set up by named bodies under electoral law, and a vote can be direct and universal or indirect — the citizen knows the machinery that counts their voice — and that it has no connection to the rest of Haiti's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Haiti's own course, in its own order.
