# Elections and Types of Vote — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "Who organises elections" teach?
**Answer:** Elections are set up by organising bodies — a permanent or provisional electoral council — working under the electoral law and decree.

**2.** In your own words, what does the part "Types of vote" teach?
**Answer:** The programme teaches the typology of votes: the direct, universal vote, and the indirect vote — two different roads from the citizen to the office.

**3.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** Elections are set up by named bodies under electoral law, and a vote can be direct and universal or indirect — the citizen knows the machinery that counts their voice.
