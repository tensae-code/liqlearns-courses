# Video script — Elections and Types of Vote

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Elections are set up by named bodies under electoral law, and a vote can be direct and universal or indirect — the citizen knows the machinery that counts their voice.

Elections are set up by organising bodies — a permanent or provisional electoral council — working under the electoral law and decree.

The programme teaches the typology of votes: the direct, universal vote, and the indirect vote — two different roads from the citizen to the office.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
