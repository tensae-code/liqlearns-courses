# Electoral Practices and Citizens' Rights

## Do it first

Say what an election is for, in one sentence. This lesson ties the practice of elections to the rights of the citizens who use them.

Electoral practices and the exercise of citizens' rights in a democracy form the NS2 grid's fourth theme: elections defined, and the typology of voting taught.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: elections are the citizen's right in action — the programme defines elections and teaches the typology of voting as the exercise of citizens' rights in a democracy. Give one real example from your own life or from the Haiti you live in, and name the lesson's key words: elections, voting, citizens' rights, democracy. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
