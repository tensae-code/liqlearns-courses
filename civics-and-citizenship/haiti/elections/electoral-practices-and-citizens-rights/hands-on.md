# Hands-on — Electoral Practices and Citizens' Rights

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Say what an election is for, in one sentence. This lesson ties the practice of elections to the rights of the citizens who use them.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: elections are the citizen's right in action — the programme defines elections and teaches the typology of voting as the exercise of citizens' rights in a democracy. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
