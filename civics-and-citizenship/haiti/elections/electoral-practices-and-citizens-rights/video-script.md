This lesson is about one thing.
Elections are the citizen's right in action — the programme defines elections and teaches the typology of voting as the exercise of citizens' rights in a democracy.

Electoral practices and the exercise of citizens' rights in a democracy form the NS2 grid's fourth theme: elections defined, and the typology of voting taught.
