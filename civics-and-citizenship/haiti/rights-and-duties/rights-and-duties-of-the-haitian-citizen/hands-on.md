# Hands-on — Rights and Duties of the Haitian Citizen

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
List one right you believe every Haitian has, and one duty you believe every Haitian owes. This lesson checks both lists against the constitution.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the Haitian citizen holds rights recognised by the constitution and owes duties in return — the two sides are learned together, from the constitutional texts themselves. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
