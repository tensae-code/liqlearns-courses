# Rights and Duties of the Haitian Citizen

## Do it first

List one right you believe every Haitian has, and one duty you believe every Haitian owes. This lesson checks both lists against the constitution.

## Sub lesson 1 — Your duties as a citizen

The programme asks the pupil to master their duties as a Haitian citizen — the obligations that come with belonging to the Republic.

## Sub lesson 2 — Your rights under the constitution

Against the duties stand the rights the constitution recognises, read from the constitutional provisions and from the declarations of the rights of man.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the Haitian citizen holds rights recognised by the constitution and owes duties in return — the two sides are learned together, from the constitutional texts themselves. Give one real example from your own life or from the Haiti you live in, and name the lesson's key words: rights, duties, constitution. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: the Haitian citizen holds rights recognised by the constitution and owes duties in return — the two sides are learned together, from the constitutional texts themselves.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
