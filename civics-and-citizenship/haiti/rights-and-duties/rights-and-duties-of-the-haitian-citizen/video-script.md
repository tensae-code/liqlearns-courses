# Video script — Rights and Duties of the Haitian Citizen

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The haitian citizen holds rights recognised by the constitution and owes duties in return — the two sides are learned together, from the constitutional texts themselves.

The programme asks the pupil to master their duties as a Haitian citizen — the obligations that come with belonging to the Republic.

Against the duties stand the rights the constitution recognises, read from the constitutional provisions and from the declarations of the rights of man.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
