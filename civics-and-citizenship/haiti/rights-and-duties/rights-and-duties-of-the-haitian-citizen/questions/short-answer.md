# Rights and Duties of the Haitian Citizen — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "Your duties as a citizen" teach?
**Answer:** The programme asks the pupil to master their duties as a Haitian citizen — the obligations that come with belonging to the Republic.

**2.** In your own words, what does the part "Your rights under the constitution" teach?
**Answer:** Against the duties stand the rights the constitution recognises, read from the constitutional provisions and from the declarations of the rights of man.

**3.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** The haitian citizen holds rights recognised by the constitution and owes duties in return — the two sides are learned together, from the constitutional texts themselves.
