# Rights and Duties of the Haitian Citizen — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The programme asks the pupil to master their duties as a Haitian citizen — the obligations that come with belonging to the Republic.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Against the duties stand the rights the constitution recognises, read from the constitutional provisions and from the declarations of the rights of man.
**Answer: True**

**3.** This lesson teaches that the Haitian citizen holds rights recognised by the constitution and owes duties in return — the two sides are learned together, from the constitutional texts themselves — and that it has no connection to the rest of Haiti's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Haiti's own course, in its own order.
