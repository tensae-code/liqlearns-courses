# Rights and Duties of the Haitian Citizen — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — Your duties as a citizen?
a) The programme asks the pupil to master their duties as a Haitian citizen — the obligations that come with belonging to the Republic.  b) it has nothing to do with how Haiti governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The programme asks the pupil to master their duties as a Haitian citizen — the obligations that come with belonging to the Republic.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — Your rights under the constitution?
a) Against the duties stand the rights the constitution recognises, read from the constitutional provisions and from the declarations of the rights of man.  b) it has nothing to do with how Haiti governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Against the duties stand the rights the constitution recognises, read from the constitutional provisions and from the declarations of the rights of man.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

a) the Haitian citizen holds rights recognised by the constitution and owes duties in return — the two sides are learned together, from the constitutional texts themselves.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Haiti
**Answer: a) the Haitian citizen holds rights recognised by the constitution and owes duties in return — the two sides are learned together, from the constitutional texts themselves.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
