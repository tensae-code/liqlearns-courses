# Rights and Duties of the Haitian Citizen — matching

Match each part of the lesson with its teaching.

**1.** Your duties as a citizen — a)
**2.** Your rights under the constitution — b)


Meanings:
a) The programme asks the pupil to master their duties as a Haitian citizen — the obligations that come with belonging to the Republic.
b) Against the duties stand the rights the constitution recognises, read from the constitutional provisions and from the declarations of the rights of man.

**Answer key:** 1. a 2. b
