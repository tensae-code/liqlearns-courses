# Climate Change and Pollution

## Do it first

Name one kind of pollution you can see from your door. This lesson classifies what you see — and asks who warms the climate, and by how much.

## Sub lesson 1 — Warming, and who causes it

Climate warming is presented and evaluated: the elements that cause it, and developed countries' share of greenhouse-gas emissions, are named.

## Sub lesson 2 — Pollution: types, agents, strategies

Pollution is taught by its typology and its agents, and the programme closes with strategies against warming and pollution together.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: climate warming has causes that can be named — including developed countries' share of greenhouse-gas emissions — and pollution has types and agents; against both, the programme teaches strategies. Give one real example from your own life or from the Haiti you live in, and name the lesson's key words: climate warming, greenhouse gas, pollution. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
