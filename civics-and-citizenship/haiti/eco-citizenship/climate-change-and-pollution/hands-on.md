# Hands-on — Climate Change and Pollution

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name one kind of pollution you can see from your door. This lesson classifies what you see — and asks who warms the climate, and by how much.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: climate warming has causes that can be named — including developed countries' share of greenhouse-gas emissions — and pollution has types and agents; against both, the programme teaches strategies. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
