# Video script — Climate Change and Pollution

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Climate warming has causes that can be named — including developed countries' share of greenhouse-gas emissions — and pollution has types and agents; against both, the programme teaches strategies.

Climate warming is presented and evaluated: the elements that cause it, and developed countries' share of greenhouse-gas emissions, are named.

Pollution is taught by its typology and its agents, and the programme closes with strategies against warming and pollution together.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
