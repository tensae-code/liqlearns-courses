# Climate Change and Pollution — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Climate warming is presented and evaluated: the elements that cause it, and developed countries' share of greenhouse-gas emissions, are named.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Pollution is taught by its typology and its agents, and the programme closes with strategies against warming and pollution together.
**Answer: True**

**3.** This lesson teaches that climate warming has causes that can be named — including developed countries' share of greenhouse-gas emissions — and pollution has types and agents; against both, the programme teaches strategies — and that it has no connection to the rest of Haiti's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Haiti's own course, in its own order.
