# Climate Change and Pollution — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — Warming, and who causes it?
a) Climate warming is presented and evaluated: the elements that cause it, and developed countries' share of greenhouse-gas emissions, are named.  b) it has nothing to do with how Haiti governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Climate warming is presented and evaluated: the elements that cause it, and developed countries' share of greenhouse-gas emissions, are named.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — Pollution: types, agents, strategies?
a) Pollution is taught by its typology and its agents, and the programme closes with strategies against warming and pollution together.  b) it has nothing to do with how Haiti governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Pollution is taught by its typology and its agents, and the programme closes with strategies against warming and pollution together.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**3.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) climate warming has causes that can be named — including developed countries' share of greenhouse-gas emissions — and pollution has types and agents; against both, the programme teaches strategies.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Haiti
**Answer: a) climate warming has causes that can be named — including developed countries' share of greenhouse-gas emissions — and pollution has types and agents; against both, the programme teaches strategies.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
