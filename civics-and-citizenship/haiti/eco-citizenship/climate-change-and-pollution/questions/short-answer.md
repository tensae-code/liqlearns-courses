# Climate Change and Pollution — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "Warming, and who causes it" teach?
**Answer:** Climate warming is presented and evaluated: the elements that cause it, and developed countries' share of greenhouse-gas emissions, are named.

**2.** In your own words, what does the part "Pollution: types, agents, strategies" teach?
**Answer:** Pollution is taught by its typology and its agents, and the programme closes with strategies against warming and pollution together.

**3.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** Climate warming has causes that can be named — including developed countries' share of greenhouse-gas emissions — and pollution has types and agents; against both, the programme teaches strategies.
