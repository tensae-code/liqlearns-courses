# Hands-on — Eco-Citizenship

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name one small act — an eco-geste — that protects the place you live. This lesson builds a whole citizenship out of such acts.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: an eco-citizen knows their rights and duties toward their own surroundings — eco-gestes, care for the environment, and teams promoting sustainable development. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
