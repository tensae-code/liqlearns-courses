# Video script — Eco-Citizenship

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
An eco-citizen knows their rights and duties toward their own surroundings — eco-gestes, care for the environment, and teams promoting sustainable development.

Eco-citizenship is defined and tied to the environment; its daily form is the eco-geste — the small, repeatable act of care.

The citizen holds rights and duties toward their surroundings, and works in teams for promoting sustainable development.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
