# Eco-Citizenship — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "Eco-citizenship and eco-gestes" teach?
**Answer:** Eco-citizenship is defined and tied to the environment; its daily form is the eco-geste — the small, repeatable act of care.

**2.** In your own words, what does the part "Rights, duties and teams" teach?
**Answer:** The citizen holds rights and duties toward their surroundings, and works in teams for promoting sustainable development.

**3.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** An eco-citizen knows their rights and duties toward their own surroundings — eco-gestes, care for the environment, and teams promoting sustainable development.
