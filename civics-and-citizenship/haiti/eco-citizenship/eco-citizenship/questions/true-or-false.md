# Eco-Citizenship — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Eco-citizenship is defined and tied to the environment; its daily form is the eco-geste — the small, repeatable act of care.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The citizen holds rights and duties toward their surroundings, and works in teams for promoting sustainable development.
**Answer: True**

**3.** This lesson teaches that an eco-citizen knows their rights and duties toward their own surroundings — eco-gestes, care for the environment, and teams promoting sustainable development — and that it has no connection to the rest of Haiti's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Haiti's own course, in its own order.
