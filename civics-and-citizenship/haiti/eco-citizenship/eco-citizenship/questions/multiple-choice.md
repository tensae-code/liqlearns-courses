# Eco-Citizenship — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — Eco-citizenship and eco-gestes?
a) Eco-citizenship is defined and tied to the environment; its daily form is the eco-geste — the small, repeatable act of care.  b) it has nothing to do with how Haiti governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Eco-citizenship is defined and tied to the environment; its daily form is the eco-geste — the small, repeatable act of care.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — Rights, duties and teams?
a) The citizen holds rights and duties toward their surroundings, and works in teams for promoting sustainable development.  b) it has nothing to do with how Haiti governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The citizen holds rights and duties toward their surroundings, and works in teams for promoting sustainable development.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**3.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) an eco-citizen knows their rights and duties toward their own surroundings — eco-gestes, care for the environment, and teams promoting sustainable development.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Haiti
**Answer: a) an eco-citizen knows their rights and duties toward their own surroundings — eco-gestes, care for the environment, and teams promoting sustainable development.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
