# Eco-Citizenship

## Do it first

Name one small act — an eco-geste — that protects the place you live. This lesson builds a whole citizenship out of such acts.

## Sub lesson 1 — Eco-citizenship and eco-gestes

Eco-citizenship is defined and tied to the environment; its daily form is the eco-geste — the small, repeatable act of care.

## Sub lesson 2 — Rights, duties and teams

The citizen holds rights and duties toward their surroundings, and works in teams for promoting sustainable development.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: an eco-citizen knows their rights and duties toward their own surroundings — eco-gestes, care for the environment, and teams promoting sustainable development. Give one real example from your own life or from the Haiti you live in, and name the lesson's key words: eco-citizenship, eco-gestes, environment, sustainable development. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: an eco-citizen knows their rights and duties toward their own surroundings — eco-gestes, care for the environment, and teams promoting sustainable development.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
