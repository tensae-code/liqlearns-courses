# Hands-on — Climate Change

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name one way the weather or the sea has changed where you live. This lesson gives that change its name and asks what can be done.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: climate warming is defined, its consequences are identified and evaluated, and the citizen learns there are strategies against its effects. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
