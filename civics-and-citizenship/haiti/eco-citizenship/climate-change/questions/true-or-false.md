# Climate Change — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Climate warming is defined and presented; its consequences are identified and evaluated.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The programme asks for a strategy against warming's effects — the citizen answers a measured problem with a planned response.
**Answer: True**

**3.** This lesson teaches that climate warming is defined, its consequences are identified and evaluated, and the citizen learns there are strategies against its effects — and that it has no connection to the rest of Haiti's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Haiti's own course, in its own order.
