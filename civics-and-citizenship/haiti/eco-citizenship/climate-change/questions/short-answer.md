# Climate Change — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "Warming and its consequences" teach?
**Answer:** Climate warming is defined and presented; its consequences are identified and evaluated.

**2.** In your own words, what does the part "Strategies against its effects" teach?
**Answer:** The programme asks for a strategy against warming's effects — the citizen answers a measured problem with a planned response.

**3.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** Climate warming is defined, its consequences are identified and evaluated, and the citizen learns there are strategies against its effects.
