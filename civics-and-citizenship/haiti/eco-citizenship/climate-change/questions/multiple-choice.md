# Climate Change — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — Warming and its consequences?
a) Climate warming is defined and presented; its consequences are identified and evaluated.  b) it has nothing to do with how Haiti governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Climate warming is defined and presented; its consequences are identified and evaluated.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
