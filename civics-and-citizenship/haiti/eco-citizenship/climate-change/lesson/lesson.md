# Climate Change

## Do it first

Name one way the weather or the sea has changed where you live. This lesson gives that change its name and asks what can be done.

## Sub lesson 1 — Warming and its consequences

Climate warming is defined and presented; its consequences are identified and evaluated.

## Sub lesson 2 — Strategies against its effects

The programme asks for a strategy against warming's effects — the citizen answers a measured problem with a planned response.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: climate warming is defined, its consequences are identified and evaluated, and the citizen learns there are strategies against its effects. Give one real example from your own life or from the Haiti you live in, and name the lesson's key words: climate warming, consequences, strategy. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: climate warming is defined, its consequences are identified and evaluated, and the citizen learns there are strategies against its effects.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
