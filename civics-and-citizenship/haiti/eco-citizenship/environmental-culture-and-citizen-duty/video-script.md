# Video script — Environmental Culture and Citizen Duty

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The citizen owes the environment care — environmental culture names the citizen's responsibility, rights and duties toward their surroundings, and the teams that promote sustainable development.

Environmental culture has fundamental elements: the citizen's responsibility toward the environment, and the citizen's rights and duties toward their surroundings.

The programme forms teams for promoting sustainable development and eco-cleanliness — care for the environment organised, not left to chance.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
