# Hands-on — Environmental Culture and Citizen Duty

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Look at the ground, water and air nearest your home. What state are they in, and whose job is that? This lesson says: partly yours.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the citizen owes the environment care — environmental culture names the citizen's responsibility, rights and duties toward their surroundings, and the teams that promote sustainable development. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
