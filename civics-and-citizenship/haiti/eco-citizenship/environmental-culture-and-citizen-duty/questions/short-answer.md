# Environmental Culture and Citizen Duty — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "Environmental culture" teach?
**Answer:** Environmental culture has fundamental elements: the citizen's responsibility toward the environment, and the citizen's rights and duties toward their surroundings.

**2.** In your own words, what does the part "Teams for sustainable development" teach?
**Answer:** The programme forms teams for promoting sustainable development and eco-cleanliness — care for the environment organised, not left to chance.

**3.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** The citizen owes the environment care — environmental culture names the citizen's responsibility, rights and duties toward their surroundings, and the teams that promote sustainable development.
