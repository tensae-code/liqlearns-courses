# Environmental Culture and Citizen Duty — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Environmental culture has fundamental elements: the citizen's responsibility toward the environment, and the citizen's rights and duties toward their surroundings.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The programme forms teams for promoting sustainable development and eco-cleanliness — care for the environment organised, not left to chance.
**Answer: True**

**3.** This lesson teaches that the citizen owes the environment care — environmental culture names the citizen's responsibility, rights and duties toward their surroundings, and the teams that promote sustainable development — and that it has no connection to the rest of Haiti's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Haiti's own course, in its own order.
