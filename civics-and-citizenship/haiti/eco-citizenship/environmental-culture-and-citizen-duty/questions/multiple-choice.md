# Environmental Culture and Citizen Duty — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — Environmental culture?
a) Environmental culture has fundamental elements: the citizen's responsibility toward the environment, and the citizen's rights and duties toward their surroundings.  b) it has nothing to do with how Haiti governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Environmental culture has fundamental elements: the citizen's responsibility toward the environment, and the citizen's rights and duties toward their surroundings.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — Teams for sustainable development?
a) The programme forms teams for promoting sustainable development and eco-cleanliness — care for the environment organised, not left to chance.  b) it has nothing to do with how Haiti governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The programme forms teams for promoting sustainable development and eco-cleanliness — care for the environment organised, not left to chance.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**Answer: a) the citizen owes the environment care — environmental culture names the citizen's responsibility, rights and duties toward their surroundings, and the teams that promote sustainable development.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
