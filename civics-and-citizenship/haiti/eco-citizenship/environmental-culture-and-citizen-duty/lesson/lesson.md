# Environmental Culture and Citizen Duty

## Do it first

Look at the ground, water and air nearest your home. What state are they in, and whose job is that? This lesson says: partly yours.

## Sub lesson 1 — Environmental culture

Environmental culture has fundamental elements: the citizen's responsibility toward the environment, and the citizen's rights and duties toward their surroundings.

## Sub lesson 2 — Teams for sustainable development

The programme forms teams for promoting sustainable development and eco-cleanliness — care for the environment organised, not left to chance.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the citizen owes the environment care — environmental culture names the citizen's responsibility, rights and duties toward their surroundings, and the teams that promote sustainable development. Give one real example from your own life or from the Haiti you live in, and name the lesson's key words: environment, sustainable development, eco-cleanliness, duty. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: the citizen owes the environment care — environmental culture names the citizen's responsibility, rights and duties toward their surroundings, and the teams that promote sustainable development.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
