# Culture of Peace and Conflict Resolution

## Do it first

Think of a quarrel you have seen end well, without blows or lasting hatred. What made it end well? This lesson builds a whole culture out of that answer.

## Sub lesson 1 — A culture of peace

The concept of a culture of peace rests on respect for the other, practised through socio-educative activities that build mutual respect.

## Sub lesson 2 — Resolving conflicts

The programme teaches strategies for resolving conflict situations — the quarrel is met with a method, not with force.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: a culture of peace is built on respect for the other — and conflicts, when they come, have strategies for resolving them without violence. Give one real example from your own life or from the Haiti you live in, and name the lesson's key words: culture of peace, respect, conflict resolution. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
