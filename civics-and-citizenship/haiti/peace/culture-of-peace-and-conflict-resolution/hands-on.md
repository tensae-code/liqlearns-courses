# Hands-on — Culture of Peace and Conflict Resolution

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Think of a quarrel you have seen end well, without blows or lasting hatred. What made it end well? This lesson builds a whole culture out of that answer.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: a culture of peace is built on respect for the other — and conflicts, when they come, have strategies for resolving them without violence. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
