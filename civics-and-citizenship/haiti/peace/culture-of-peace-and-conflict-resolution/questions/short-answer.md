# Culture of Peace and Conflict Resolution — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "A culture of peace" teach?
**Answer:** The concept of a culture of peace rests on respect for the other, practised through socio-educative activities that build mutual respect.

**2.** In your own words, what does the part "Resolving conflicts" teach?
**Answer:** The programme teaches strategies for resolving conflict situations — the quarrel is met with a method, not with force.

**3.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** A culture of peace is built on respect for the other — and conflicts, when they come, have strategies for resolving them without violence.
