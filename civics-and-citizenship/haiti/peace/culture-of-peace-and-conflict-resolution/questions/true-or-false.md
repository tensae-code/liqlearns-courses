# Culture of Peace and Conflict Resolution — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The concept of a culture of peace rests on respect for the other, practised through socio-educative activities that build mutual respect.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The programme teaches strategies for resolving conflict situations — the quarrel is met with a method, not with force.
**Answer: True**

**3.** This lesson teaches that a culture of peace is built on respect for the other — and conflicts, when they come, have strategies for resolving them without violence — and that it has no connection to the rest of Haiti's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Haiti's own course, in its own order.
