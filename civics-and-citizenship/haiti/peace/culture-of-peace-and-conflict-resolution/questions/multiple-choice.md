# Culture of Peace and Conflict Resolution — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — A culture of peace?
a) The concept of a culture of peace rests on respect for the other, practised through socio-educative activities that build mutual respect.  b) it has nothing to do with how Haiti governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The concept of a culture of peace rests on respect for the other, practised through socio-educative activities that build mutual respect.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — Resolving conflicts?
a) The programme teaches strategies for resolving conflict situations — the quarrel is met with a method, not with force.  b) it has nothing to do with how Haiti governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The programme teaches strategies for resolving conflict situations — the quarrel is met with a method, not with force.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**3.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) a culture of peace is built on respect for the other — and conflicts, when they come, have strategies for resolving them without violence.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Haiti
**Answer: a) a culture of peace is built on respect for the other — and conflicts, when they come, have strategies for resolving them without violence.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
