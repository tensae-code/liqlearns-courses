# Video script — Culture of Peace and Conflict Resolution

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A culture of peace is built on respect for the other — and conflicts, when they come, have strategies for resolving them without violence.

The concept of a culture of peace rests on respect for the other, practised through socio-educative activities that build mutual respect.

The programme teaches strategies for resolving conflict situations — the quarrel is met with a method, not with force.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
