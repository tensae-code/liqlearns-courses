# Security Institutions — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — Haiti's security institutions?
a) The programme teaches the pupil to identify Haiti's security institutions and the links between them — and to judge their initiatives and decisions, as a citizen should.  b) it has nothing to do with how Haiti governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The programme teaches the pupil to identify Haiti's security institutions and the links between them — and to judge their initiatives and decisions, as a citizen should.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Haiti has named security institutions, linked to each other — the citizen can identify them and judge their initiatives and decisions.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Haiti
**Answer: a) Haiti has named security institutions, linked to each other — the citizen can identify them and judge their initiatives and decisions.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
