# Security Institutions — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "Haiti's security institutions" teach?
**Answer:** The programme teaches the pupil to identify Haiti's security institutions and the links between them — and to judge their initiatives and decisions, as a citizen should.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** Haiti has named security institutions, linked to each other — the citizen can identify them and judge their initiatives and decisions.
