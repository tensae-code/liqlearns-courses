# Hands-on — Security Institutions

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name who keeps people safe in your town, and who watches over them. This lesson maps Haiti's security institutions and how they link.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Haiti has named security institutions, linked to each other — the citizen can identify them and judge their initiatives and decisions. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
