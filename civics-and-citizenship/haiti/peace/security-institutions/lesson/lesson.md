# Security Institutions

## Do it first

Name who keeps people safe in your town, and who watches over them. This lesson maps Haiti's security institutions and how they link.

The programme teaches the pupil to identify Haiti's security institutions and the links between them — and to judge their initiatives and decisions, as a citizen should.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Haiti has named security institutions, linked to each other — the citizen can identify them and judge their initiatives and decisions. Give one real example from your own life or from the Haiti you live in, and name the lesson's key words: security institutions, safety, judgement. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: Haiti has named security institutions, linked to each other — the citizen can identify them and judge their initiatives and decisions.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
