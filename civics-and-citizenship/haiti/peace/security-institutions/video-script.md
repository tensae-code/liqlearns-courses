# Video script — Security Institutions

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Haiti has named security institutions, linked to each other — the citizen can identify them and judge their initiatives and decisions.

The programme teaches the pupil to identify Haiti's security institutions and the links between them — and to judge their initiatives and decisions, as a citizen should.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
