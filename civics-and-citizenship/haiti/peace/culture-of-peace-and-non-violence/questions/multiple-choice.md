Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — Peace as a democratic culture?
a) The NS2 progression grid gives its first theme to a culture of peace and non-violence in a democratic society — peace as the working culture of democracy, not only the absence of war.  b) it has nothing to do with how Haiti governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
