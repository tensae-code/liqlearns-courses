# Culture of Peace and Non-Violence — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "Peace as a democratic culture" teach?
**Answer:** The NS2 progression grid gives its first theme to a culture of peace and non-violence in a democratic society — peace as the working culture of democracy, not only the absence of war.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** In a democratic society, peace is a working culture — non-violence is how a democracy handles its disagreements.
