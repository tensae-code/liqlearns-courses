# Culture of Peace and Non-Violence — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The NS2 progression grid gives its first theme to a culture of peace and non-violence in a democratic society — peace as the working culture of democracy, not only the absence of war.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that in a democratic society, peace is a working culture — non-violence is how a democracy handles its disagreements — and that it has no connection to the rest of Haiti's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Haiti's own course, in its own order.
