# Video script — Culture of Peace and Non-Violence

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
In a democratic society, peace is a working culture — non-violence is how a democracy handles its disagreements.

The NS2 progression grid gives its first theme to a culture of peace and non-violence in a democratic society — peace as the working culture of democracy, not only the absence of war.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
