# Culture of Peace and Non-Violence

## Do it first

Say one disagreement a whole country can have without violence. This lesson names the culture that makes that possible.

The NS2 progression grid gives its first theme to a culture of peace and non-violence in a democratic society — peace as the working culture of democracy, not only the absence of war.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: in a democratic society, peace is a working culture — non-violence is how a democracy handles its disagreements. Give one real example from your own life or from the Haiti you live in, and name the lesson's key words: culture of peace, non-violence, democracy. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: in a democratic society, peace is a working culture — non-violence is how a democracy handles its disagreements.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
