# Freedom, Equality, Equity — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "Freedom, exercised autonomously" teach?
**Answer:** The programme teaches the types of freedom and their application in Haiti, and asks the pupil to exercise their own freedom autonomously.

**2.** In your own words, what does the part "Equality and equity" teach?
**Answer:** Equality is to be promoted — and beyond it, a culture of equity in the community: fairness that looks at real situations, not only at sameness.

**3.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** Freedom is exercised with autonomy, equality is promoted, and equity — fairness in the community — is a culture a citizen helps build.
