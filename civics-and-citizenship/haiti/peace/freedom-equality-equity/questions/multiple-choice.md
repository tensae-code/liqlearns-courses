# Freedom, Equality, Equity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — Freedom, exercised autonomously?
a) The programme teaches the types of freedom and their application in Haiti, and asks the pupil to exercise their own freedom autonomously.  b) it has nothing to do with how Haiti governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The programme teaches the types of freedom and their application in Haiti, and asks the pupil to exercise their own freedom autonomously.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — Equality and equity?
a) Equality is to be promoted — and beyond it, a culture of equity in the community: fairness that looks at real situations, not only at sameness.  b) it has nothing to do with how Haiti governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Equality is to be promoted — and beyond it, a culture of equity in the community: fairness that looks at real situations, not only at sameness.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**3.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) freedom is exercised with autonomy, equality is promoted, and equity — fairness in the community — is a culture a citizen helps build.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Haiti
**Answer: a) freedom is exercised with autonomy, equality is promoted, and equity — fairness in the community — is a culture a citizen helps build.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
