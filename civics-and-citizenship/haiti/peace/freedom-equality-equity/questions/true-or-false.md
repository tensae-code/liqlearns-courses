# Freedom, Equality, Equity — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The programme teaches the types of freedom and their application in Haiti, and asks the pupil to exercise their own freedom autonomously.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Equality is to be promoted — and beyond it, a culture of equity in the community: fairness that looks at real situations, not only at sameness.
**Answer: True**

**3.** This lesson teaches that freedom is exercised with autonomy, equality is promoted, and equity — fairness in the community — is a culture a citizen helps build — and that it has no connection to the rest of Haiti's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Haiti's own course, in its own order.
