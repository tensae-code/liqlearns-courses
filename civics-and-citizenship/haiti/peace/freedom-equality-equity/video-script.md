# Video script — Freedom, Equality, Equity

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Freedom is exercised with autonomy, equality is promoted, and equity — fairness in the community — is a culture a citizen helps build.

The programme teaches the types of freedom and their application in Haiti, and asks the pupil to exercise their own freedom autonomously.

Equality is to be promoted — and beyond it, a culture of equity in the community: fairness that looks at real situations, not only at sameness.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
