# Freedom, Equality, Equity

## Do it first

Say what freedom means to you, and whether treating everyone exactly the same is always fair. This lesson separates three ideas that are often tangled.

## Sub lesson 1 — Freedom, exercised autonomously

The programme teaches the types of freedom and their application in Haiti, and asks the pupil to exercise their own freedom autonomously.

## Sub lesson 2 — Equality and equity

Equality is to be promoted — and beyond it, a culture of equity in the community: fairness that looks at real situations, not only at sameness.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: freedom is exercised with autonomy, equality is promoted, and equity — fairness in the community — is a culture a citizen helps build. Give one real example from your own life or from the Haiti you live in, and name the lesson's key words: freedom, equality, equity. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: freedom is exercised with autonomy, equality is promoted, and equity — fairness in the community — is a culture a citizen helps build.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
