# Lesson plan — The Three Generations of Human Rights

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Haiti · **Title:** Human Rights · **Lesson:** The Three Generations of Human Rights
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Ethics, Rights and the City (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** human rights came in three generations — freedoms and political rights; social, economic and cultural rights; and solidarity rights — and the programme judges their ineffectiveness in Haiti honestly. This lesson stands on a teacher programme alone — evidence only, labelled as such.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Three generations of rights** — The first generation is freedoms and political rights; the second, social, economic and cultural rights; the third, solidarity rights — peace, development, a healthy environment, and the common heritage of humanity.
2. **Rights on paper, rights in Haiti** — The programme names the ineffectiveness of human rights in Haiti — the gap between the generations of rights as declared and as lived.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
