# Video script — The Three Generations of Human Rights

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Human rights came in three generations — freedoms and political rights; social, economic and cultural rights; and solidarity rights — and the programme judges their ineffectiveness in haiti honestly.

The first generation is freedoms and political rights; the second, social, economic and cultural rights; the third, solidarity rights — peace, development, a healthy environment, and the common heritage of humanity.

The programme names the ineffectiveness of human rights in Haiti — the gap between the generations of rights as declared and as lived.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
