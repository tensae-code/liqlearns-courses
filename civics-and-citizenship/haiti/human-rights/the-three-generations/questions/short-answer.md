# The Three Generations of Human Rights — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "Three generations of rights" teach?
**Answer:** The first generation is freedoms and political rights; the second, social, economic and cultural rights; the third, solidarity rights — peace, development, a healthy environment, and the common heritage of humanity.

**2.** In your own words, what does the part "Rights on paper, rights in Haiti" teach?
**Answer:** The programme names the ineffectiveness of human rights in Haiti — the gap between the generations of rights as declared and as lived.

**3.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** Human rights came in three generations — freedoms and political rights; social, economic and cultural rights; and solidarity rights — and the programme judges their ineffectiveness in haiti honestly.
