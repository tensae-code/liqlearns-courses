# The Three Generations of Human Rights — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — Three generations of rights?
a) The first generation is freedoms and political rights; the second, social, economic and cultural rights; the third, solidarity rights — peace, development, a healthy environment, and the common heritage of humanity.  b) it has nothing to do with how Haiti governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The first generation is freedoms and political rights; the second, social, economic and cultural rights; the third, solidarity rights — peace, development, a healthy environment, and the common heritage of humanity.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — Rights on paper, rights in Haiti?

**Answer: a) The programme names the ineffectiveness of human rights in Haiti — the gap between the generations of rights as declared and as lived.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
