# The Three Generations of Human Rights — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The first generation is freedoms and political rights; the second, social, economic and cultural rights; the third, solidarity rights — peace, development, a healthy environment, and the common heritage of humanity.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The programme names the ineffectiveness of human rights in Haiti — the gap between the generations of rights as declared and as lived.
**Answer: True**

**3.** This lesson teaches that human rights came in three generations — freedoms and political rights; social, economic and cultural rights; and solidarity rights — and the programme judges their ineffectiveness in Haiti honestly — and that it has no connection to the rest of Haiti's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Haiti's own course, in its own order.
