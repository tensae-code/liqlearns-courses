# Civics and Citizenship — Haiti course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

Civics belongs to the country: this course is Haiti's own —
built from Haiti's MENFP Éducation à la citoyenneté teacher
programmes (all French; the four 2019 minimal programmes and
the 2015 operational programme, all COMPLETE as filed).
Inside the course the organisation is by **levels, not
grades**: the titles below ARE the levels' titles, in path
order — what the learner proves, step by step. The grades
the books come from live only in each lesson's sources.

The path's own logic is Haiti's, from the main filing's
ladder sketch: the MENFP programmes' own four-year climb —
Republic and citizen first (NS1), because a citizen must know
what the Republic is and what crisis it faces before its
machinery; then constitution and governance (NS2); then the
world and the environment (NS3); and last ethics and the city
(NS4). The 2015 operational programme teaches the same NS1
year more fully, so its extra themes (identity, elections,
peace, eco-citizenship) sit inside Level 1 with the NS1
minimal programme's themes, and its five lessons that NS2
also teaches are stocked once, at Level 1, with both
programmes' rows. That ladder IS the path order (recommended,
never forced — a learner can jump in higher; the "start here
instead" hints point down this ladder). Every lesson in this
course stands on a teacher programme — evidence only,
acceptance 0, labelled in every lesson's sources.

## The titles

**Level 1 — the Republic and the citizen** (NS1: the 2019
minimal programme and the fuller 2015 operational programme):

- `foundations/` — Level 1. What Citizenship Education Is
  (education, citizenship and citizenship education defined
  two ways, and their role in building a new Haiti) ·
  Citizen Concepts (civic responsibility, participative
  democracy, citizen engagement, world citizenship,
  nationality, citizen solidarity — each defined).
- `rights-and-duties/` — Level 1. Rights and Duties of the
  Haitian Citizen (duties mastered, rights read from the
  constitutional provisions and the declarations of the
  rights of man).
- `the-republic/` — Level 1. History and Principles of the
  Republic (the republic as a form of organisation; its
  principles and values, including the separation of powers)
  · Symbols of the Republic (identifying and presenting the
  Republic's symbols) · The Three Powers of the State (the
  three constituted powers — principles, missions,
  functioning, delimitation) · Institutions That Guarantee
  Citizenship (justice, the OPC, parliament, civil-society
  organisations) · The Citizenship Crisis (the collapse of
  social values and the crises of responsibility, leadership
  and institutions — understood, and to be resolved).
- `forming-citizens/` — Level 1. Family, School and State
  (the contribution of family, school, state and religion in
  forming citizens) · The School Forms Eco-Citizens (the
  school's role in forming eco-citizens; identity — being
  Haitian in a democratic context, citizen conscience).
- `identity/` — Level 1 (taught by both the 2015 programme
  and NS2; stocked here at first teaching). Levels of
  Identity (personal/citizen, social, national and
  transnational identity, mobilised for peace, participation
  and tolerance) · Memory and the Duty of Memory (individual,
  collective, historical and social memory; the duty of
  memory in the organisation of a state).
- `government/` — Level 1 (taught by both the 2015 programme
  and NS2; stocked here at first teaching). Forms of
  Government and the Three Powers (the world's forms of
  government; Haiti's three powers compared across the
  Caribbean, the Americas, Europe, Africa and Asia) ·
  Governance and Public Ethics (governance and good
  governance characterised; the link between public ethics
  and good governance) · School Government (the gouvernement
  scolaire — concept, organisation, installation techniques,
  democracy at school).
- `elections/` — Level 1 (2015 programme). Elections and
  Types of Vote (how elections are set up; the electoral
  council, law and decree; direct/universal and indirect
  votes).
- `peace/` — Level 1 (2015 programme). Culture of Peace and
  Conflict Resolution (respect for the other; strategies for
  resolving conflicts) · Security Institutions (Haiti's
  security institutions, their links, judging their
  decisions) · Freedom, Equality, Equity (types of freedom
  and their application in Haiti; promoting equality and a
  culture of equity).
- `taking-part/` — Level 1 (2015 programme). Citizen
  Participation (participation in promoting citizenship —
  the concept and its application).
- `eco-citizenship/` — Level 1 (2015 programme).
  Environmental Culture and Citizen Duty (environmental
  culture's elements; rights and duties toward your
  surroundings; teams for sustainable development) · Climate
  Change (warming defined, consequences evaluated, a
  strategy against its effects).

**Level 2 — constitution and governance** (NS2, the 2019
minimal programme):

- `the-constitution/` — Level 2. The Haitian Constitution
  (the constitution presented and defined; Haiti's
  constitutional history 1805–1987; the constitution in
  force and why it matters to a citizen).
- `peace/` — Level 2. Culture of Peace and Non-Violence
  (peace and non-violence as the working culture of a
  democratic society — progression-grid theme 1).
- `government/` — Level 2. Governance: Power, Government
  and Ethics (governance as the three together — grid
  theme 2) · Local Governance and Participation (local
  governance through citizens' participation — grid
  theme 3).
- `elections/` — Level 2. Electoral Practices and Citizens'
  Rights (elections defined; the typology of voting; the
  exercise of citizens' rights in a democracy — grid
  theme 4).

**Level 3 — the world, the environment and taking part**
(NS3, the 2019 minimal programme):

- `the-world/` — Level 3. Global Citizenship Education
  (Enlightenment and post-modern approaches; the great
  thematic domains and foundations) · Globalisation and
  Mondialité (the two defined and told apart; globalisation's
  effects in Haiti) · Rights and Social Justice (the
  Universal Declaration of Human Rights read and commented;
  citizenship and social justice).
- `eco-citizenship/` — Level 3. Eco-Citizenship
  (eco-citizenship defined; eco-gestes; rights and duties
  toward your surroundings; teams for sustainable
  development) · Climate Change and Pollution (warming's
  causes, developed countries' share of emissions;
  pollution's typology and agents; strategies against both).
- `taking-part/` — Level 3. Integration and Local
  Development (citizens' integration and participation in
  local development — grid theme 2) · Democracy as Citizen
  Participation (democracy understood as participation;
  integration, participation and the exercise of citizenship
  — grid themes 3–4).

**Level 4 — ethics, rights and the city** (NS4, the 2019
minimal programme):

- `ethics/` — Level 4. Bioethics and Moral Principles
  (bioethics presented; beneficence against ambient
  relativism; minimal ethics and autonomy) · Ethics of
  Responsibility (inequalities of power and knowledge;
  parental, professional and hierarchical responsibility;
  answering for consequences) · Principles of Justice (the
  diverse principles; individualism and communitarianism;
  respect for differences; responsibilities toward future
  generations).
- `human-rights/` — Level 4. The Three Generations of Human
  Rights (freedoms and political rights; social, economic
  and cultural rights; solidarity rights — and the
  ineffectiveness of human rights in Haiti).
- `the-city/` — Level 4. Organising the City and Collective
  Well-Being (the city organised for the common well-being;
  everyone's part in building a new Haiti) · Living
  Together: Tolerance and Respect (tolerance and respect
  within laïcité, read from texts including Voltaire) ·
  Managing the City and the Common Good (managing public
  things; bad management of the state's goods feeds poverty).
