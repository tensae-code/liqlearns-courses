# Principles of Justice

## Do it first

Say what a fair society owes one person, and what it owes everyone together. This lesson shows those two answers pulling against each other — by design.

## Sub lesson 1 — Diverse principles of justice

The principles of justice are diverse: individualism and communitarianism are the two great pulls, and respect for differences is taught between them.

## Sub lesson 2 — Future generations

Responsibility does not stop at the living: the programme extends it to future generations — justice owed to people not yet born.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: justice has more than one principle — individualism and communitarianism pull different ways, respect for differences holds them together, and responsibility reaches to future generations. Give one real example from your own life or from the Haiti you live in, and name the lesson's key words: justice, individualism, communitarianism, future generations. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
