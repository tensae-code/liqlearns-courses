# Principles of Justice — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — Diverse principles of justice?
a) The principles of justice are diverse: individualism and communitarianism are the two great pulls, and respect for differences is taught between them.  b) it has nothing to do with how Haiti governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The principles of justice are diverse: individualism and communitarianism are the two great pulls, and respect for differences is taught between them.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — Future generations?
a) Responsibility does not stop at the living: the programme extends it to future generations — justice owed to people not yet born.  b) it has nothing to do with how Haiti governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Responsibility does not stop at the living: the programme extends it to future generations — justice owed to people not yet born.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**3.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) justice has more than one principle — individualism and communitarianism pull different ways, respect for differences holds them together, and responsibility reaches to future generations.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Haiti
**Answer: a) justice has more than one principle — individualism and communitarianism pull different ways, respect for differences holds them together, and responsibility reaches to future generations.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
