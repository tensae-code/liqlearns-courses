# Principles of Justice — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The principles of justice are diverse: individualism and communitarianism are the two great pulls, and respect for differences is taught between them.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Responsibility does not stop at the living: the programme extends it to future generations — justice owed to people not yet born.
**Answer: True**

**3.** This lesson teaches that justice has more than one principle — individualism and communitarianism pull different ways, respect for differences holds them together, and responsibility reaches to future generations — and that it has no connection to the rest of Haiti's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Haiti's own course, in its own order.
