# Principles of Justice — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "Diverse principles of justice" teach?
**Answer:** The principles of justice are diverse: individualism and communitarianism are the two great pulls, and respect for differences is taught between them.

**2.** In your own words, what does the part "Future generations" teach?
**Answer:** Responsibility does not stop at the living: the programme extends it to future generations — justice owed to people not yet born.

**3.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** Justice has more than one principle — individualism and communitarianism pull different ways, respect for differences holds them together, and responsibility reaches to future generations.
