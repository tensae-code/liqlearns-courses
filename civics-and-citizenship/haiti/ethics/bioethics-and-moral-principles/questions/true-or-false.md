# Bioethics and Moral Principles — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Bioethics is presented against ambient relativism — the air of 'anything goes' — and sets against it the principle of beneficence: do good to the person concerned.
**Answer: True**

**2.** A minimal ethics and the principle of autonomy complete the lesson: the person's own say over their life is a principle, not a preference.
**Answer: True**

**3.** This lesson teaches that bioethics asks what may rightly be done with life — against ambient relativism it sets the principle of beneficence, a minimal ethics, and the principle of autonomy — and that it has no connection to the rest of Haiti's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Haiti's own course, in its own order.
