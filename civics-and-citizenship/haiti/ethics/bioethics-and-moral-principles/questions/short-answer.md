# Bioethics and Moral Principles — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "Beneficence against relativism" teach?
**Answer:** Bioethics is presented against ambient relativism — the air of 'anything goes' — and sets against it the principle of beneficence: do good to the person concerned.

**2.** In your own words, what does the part "Autonomy and minimal ethics" teach?
**Answer:** A minimal ethics and the principle of autonomy complete the lesson: the person's own say over their life is a principle, not a preference.

**3.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** Bioethics asks what may rightly be done with life — against ambient relativism it sets the principle of beneficence, a minimal ethics, and the principle of autonomy.
