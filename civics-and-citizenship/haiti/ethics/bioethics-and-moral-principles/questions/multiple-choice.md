# Bioethics and Moral Principles — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — Beneficence against relativism?
a) Bioethics is presented against ambient relativism — the air of 'anything goes' — and sets against it the principle of beneficence: do good to the person concerned.  b) it has nothing to do with how Haiti governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Bioethics is presented against ambient relativism — the air of 'anything goes' — and sets against it the principle of beneficence: do good to the person concerned.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — Autonomy and minimal ethics?

**Answer: a) A minimal ethics and the principle of autonomy complete the lesson: the person's own say over their life is a principle, not a preference.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**Answer: a) bioethics asks what may rightly be done with life — against ambient relativism it sets the principle of beneficence, a minimal ethics, and the principle of autonomy.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
