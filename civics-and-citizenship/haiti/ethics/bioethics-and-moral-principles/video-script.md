# Video script — Bioethics and Moral Principles

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Bioethics asks what may rightly be done with life — against ambient relativism it sets the principle of beneficence, a minimal ethics, and the principle of autonomy.

Bioethics is presented against ambient relativism — the air of 'anything goes' — and sets against it the principle of beneficence: do good to the person concerned.

A minimal ethics and the principle of autonomy complete the lesson: the person's own say over their life is a principle, not a preference.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
