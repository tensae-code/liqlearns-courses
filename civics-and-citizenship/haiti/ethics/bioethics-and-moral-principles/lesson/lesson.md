# Bioethics and Moral Principles

## Do it first

Say whether 'everyone decides for themselves what is right' is enough when doctors and scientists work on human life. This lesson is bioethics' answer.

## Sub lesson 1 — Beneficence against relativism

Bioethics is presented against ambient relativism — the air of 'anything goes' — and sets against it the principle of beneficence: do good to the person concerned.

## Sub lesson 2 — Autonomy and minimal ethics

A minimal ethics and the principle of autonomy complete the lesson: the person's own say over their life is a principle, not a preference.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: bioethics asks what may rightly be done with life — against ambient relativism it sets the principle of beneficence, a minimal ethics, and the principle of autonomy. Give one real example from your own life or from the Haiti you live in, and name the lesson's key words: bioethics, beneficence, autonomy, relativism. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
