# Ethics of Responsibility

## Do it first

Name one person whose mistakes hurt more than yours would, because of the place they hold. This lesson is about why — and about answering for consequences.

## Sub lesson 1 — Unequal responsibility

The ethics of responsibility begins from inequalities of power and knowledge: the more you can do and know, the more you answer for.

## Sub lesson 2 — Kinds of responsibility, one ending

Parental, professional and hierarchical responsibility are distinguished — and all end in answering for the consequences of your responsibilities.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: responsibility is unequal because power and knowledge are unequal — parental, professional and hierarchical responsibility all end the same way: answering for the consequences. Give one real example from your own life or from the Haiti you live in, and name the lesson's key words: responsibility, consequences, power, knowledge. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
