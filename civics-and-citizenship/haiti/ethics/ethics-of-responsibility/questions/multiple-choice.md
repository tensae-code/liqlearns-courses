# Ethics of Responsibility — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — Unequal responsibility?
a) The ethics of responsibility begins from inequalities of power and knowledge: the more you can do and know, the more you answer for.  b) it has nothing to do with how Haiti governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The ethics of responsibility begins from inequalities of power and knowledge: the more you can do and know, the more you answer for.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — Kinds of responsibility, one ending?
a) Parental, professional and hierarchical responsibility are distinguished — and all end in answering for the consequences of your responsibilities.  b) it has nothing to do with how Haiti governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Parental, professional and hierarchical responsibility are distinguished — and all end in answering for the consequences of your responsibilities.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**3.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) responsibility is unequal because power and knowledge are unequal — parental, professional and hierarchical responsibility all end the same way: answering for the consequences.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Haiti
**Answer: a) responsibility is unequal because power and knowledge are unequal — parental, professional and hierarchical responsibility all end the same way: answering for the consequences.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
