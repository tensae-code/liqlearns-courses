# Ethics of Responsibility — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The ethics of responsibility begins from inequalities of power and knowledge: the more you can do and know, the more you answer for.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Parental, professional and hierarchical responsibility are distinguished — and all end in answering for the consequences of your responsibilities.
**Answer: True**

**3.** This lesson teaches that responsibility is unequal because power and knowledge are unequal — parental, professional and hierarchical responsibility all end the same way: answering for the consequences — and that it has no connection to the rest of Haiti's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Haiti's own course, in its own order.
