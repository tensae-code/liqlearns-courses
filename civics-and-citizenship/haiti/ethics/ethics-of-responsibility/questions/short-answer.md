# Ethics of Responsibility — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "Unequal responsibility" teach?
**Answer:** The ethics of responsibility begins from inequalities of power and knowledge: the more you can do and know, the more you answer for.

**2.** In your own words, what does the part "Kinds of responsibility, one ending" teach?
**Answer:** Parental, professional and hierarchical responsibility are distinguished — and all end in answering for the consequences of your responsibilities.

**3.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** Responsibility is unequal because power and knowledge are unequal — parental, professional and hierarchical responsibility all end the same way: answering for the consequences.
