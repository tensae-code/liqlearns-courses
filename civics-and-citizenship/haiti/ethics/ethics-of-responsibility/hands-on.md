# Hands-on — Ethics of Responsibility

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name one person whose mistakes hurt more than yours would, because of the place they hold. This lesson is about why — and about answering for consequences.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: responsibility is unequal because power and knowledge are unequal — parental, professional and hierarchical responsibility all end the same way: answering for the consequences. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
