# Video script — Ethics of Responsibility

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Responsibility is unequal because power and knowledge are unequal — parental, professional and hierarchical responsibility all end the same way: answering for the consequences.

The ethics of responsibility begins from inequalities of power and knowledge: the more you can do and know, the more you answer for.

Parental, professional and hierarchical responsibility are distinguished — and all end in answering for the consequences of your responsibilities.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
