# Our school — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) our school as the child's community  b) it has nothing to do with how Mongolia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) our school as the child's community** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) our school as the child's community  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Mongolia
**Answer: a) our school as the child's community** — That is what this lesson teaches.
