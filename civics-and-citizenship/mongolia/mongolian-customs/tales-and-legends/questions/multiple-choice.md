# Mongolian tales and legends — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) tales and legends as carriers of the culture's values  b) it has nothing to do with how Mongolia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) tales and legends as carriers of the culture's values** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) tales and legends as carriers of the culture's values  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Mongolia
**Answer: a) tales and legends as carriers of the culture's values** — That is what this lesson teaches.
