# Celebrate the New Year beautifully — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Tsagaan Sar greetings and New Year customs  b) it has nothing to do with how Mongolia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Tsagaan Sar greetings and New Year customs** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Tsagaan Sar greetings and New Year customs  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Mongolia
**Answer: a) Tsagaan Sar greetings and New Year customs** — That is what this lesson teaches.
