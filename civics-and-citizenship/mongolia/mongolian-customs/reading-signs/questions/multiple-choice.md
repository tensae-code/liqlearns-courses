# Reading Signs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) the Mongolian way of examining and reading signs  b) it has nothing to do with how Mongolia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) the Mongolian way of examining and reading signs** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) the Mongolian way of examining and reading signs  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Mongolia
**Answer: a) the Mongolian way of examining and reading signs** — That is what this lesson teaches.
