# The meaning and symbolism of Mongolian clothing — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) what Mongolian dress means and symbolises  b) it has nothing to do with how Mongolia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) what Mongolian dress means and symbolises** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) what Mongolian dress means and symbolises  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Mongolia
**Answer: a) what Mongolian dress means and symbolises** — That is what this lesson teaches.
