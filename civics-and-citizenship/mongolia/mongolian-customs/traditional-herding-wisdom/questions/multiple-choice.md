# The Traditional Wisdom of Herding — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) herding knowledge handed down as wisdom  b) it has nothing to do with how Mongolia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations

**Answer: a) herding knowledge handed down as wisdom** — That is what this lesson teaches.
