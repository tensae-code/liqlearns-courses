# Being responsible and working together — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) why one must be responsible and work with others  b) it has nothing to do with how Mongolia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) why one must be responsible and work with others** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) why one must be responsible and work with others  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Mongolia
**Answer: a) why one must be responsible and work with others** — That is what this lesson teaches.
