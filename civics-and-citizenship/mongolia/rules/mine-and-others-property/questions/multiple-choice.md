# Mine and others' property — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) respecting what is mine and what belongs to others  b) it has nothing to do with how Mongolia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) respecting what is mine and what belongs to others** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) respecting what is mine and what belongs to others  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Mongolia
**Answer: a) respecting what is mine and what belongs to others** — That is what this lesson teaches.
