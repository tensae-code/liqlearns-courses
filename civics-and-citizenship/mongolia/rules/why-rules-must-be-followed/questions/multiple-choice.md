# Why must rules and regulations be followed? — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) the reasons rules and regulations bind everyone  b) it has nothing to do with how Mongolia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) the reasons rules and regulations bind everyone** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) the reasons rules and regulations bind everyone  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Mongolia
**Answer: a) the reasons rules and regulations bind everyone** — That is what this lesson teaches.
