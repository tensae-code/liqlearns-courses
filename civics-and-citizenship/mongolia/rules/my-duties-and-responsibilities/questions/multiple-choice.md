# My duties and responsibilities — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) the child's own duties and responsibilities  b) it has nothing to do with how Mongolia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) the child's own duties and responsibilities** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) the child's own duties and responsibilities  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Mongolia
**Answer: a) the child's own duties and responsibilities** — That is what this lesson teaches.
