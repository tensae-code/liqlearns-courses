# Let us keep our family lineage record — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) writing and keeping the family's lineage record  b) it has nothing to do with how Mongolia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) writing and keeping the family's lineage record** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) writing and keeping the family's lineage record  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Mongolia
**Answer: a) writing and keeping the family's lineage record** — That is what this lesson teaches.
