# My kin

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: My kin. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Mongolia's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: naming the child's relatives — father's side (авга) and mother's side (нагац) — on a family tree (read first-hand on the chapter opener) Give one real example from your own life or from the Mongolia you live in, and name the lesson's key words: my, kin. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
