# Let us respect father and mother — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) respecting father and mother as the child's first civic duty  b) it has nothing to do with how Mongolia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) respecting father and mother as the child's first civic duty** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) respecting father and mother as the child's first civic duty  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Mongolia
**Answer: a) respecting father and mother as the child's first civic duty** — That is what this lesson teaches.
