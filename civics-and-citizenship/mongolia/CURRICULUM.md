# Civics and Citizenship — Mongolia — curriculum

One course for Mongolia inside the Civics and Citizenship
folder, built from Mongolia's own government textbooks:
the Иргэний боловсрол (Civic Education) student books for
Grades 1–5 (Ministry of Education, Culture, Science and
Sports, Mongolia, 2018) and the Иргэний ёс зүйн боловсрол
(Civic Ethics Education) student books for Grades 6–7
(Ministry of Education and Science, Mongolia, 2020), all
Mongolian, all PARTIAL as filed — every book read from
the Ministry's econtent.edu.mn flipbook edition, its
recorded source (see ABOUT.md). The organisation is by
**levels, not grades**: a learner's place is found by
what they prove. The path is recommended, never forced —
a learner can jump in higher; the "start here instead"
hints point down this ladder. The grades the books come
from appear only in provenance (ABOUT.md and each
lesson's `lesson/sources.md`).

The ladder follows Mongolia's own books' progression:
the series "Би — Монгол улсын иргэн" (I am a citizen of
Mongolia) starts with greetings, parents, the teacher and
the ger — citizenship as manners and belonging at home —
widens circle by circle through lineage, harmony, custom
and nature to the state flag, equal rights and how the
state serves citizens, closes the primary arc by
systematising the customs and naming rules, duties and
responsibilities as one set, and then, under its new
name Civic Ethics, turns inward to self-knowledge,
self-correction and the fruits of actions.

## Where the build stands

- **Course frame — BUILT** (this CURRICULUM, TITLES.md,
  ABOUT.md, and the folder README entry).
- **Levels 1–4 — BUILT**: 108 lessons, stocked from 108
  placements in the seven read books (Grades 1–7, all
  student books).
- **Not built, named plainly**: every book's verdict is
  PARTIAL, exactly as filed — cover, imprint (Grades
  6–7), contents and chapter openers were read first-hand
  from the Ministry's econtent.edu.mn flipbook renders;
  no book was read page by page, so placements are from
  the verified contents. The books' review/conclusion
  chapters are named in the contents maps in ABOUT.md
  but not placed, as filed. The GitHub tree grab returned
  404 for every Mongolian record — the PDFs are not in
  the tree; they may sit in Releases, which the tree grab
  cannot see, so absence from GitHub is NOT proven and no
  phantom push is claimed. No Mongolian PDF was staged in
  this wave — the flipbook serves page images, not a
  stageable PDF file — and this build staged and
  downloaded nothing (DISK HOLD in force). The filing
  names no UNREAD book and no teacher guide or programme:
  all seven records are student books, read as far as
  their PARTIAL verdicts state. Nothing else is in the
  course, and no lesson pretends to one (see ABOUT.md).
