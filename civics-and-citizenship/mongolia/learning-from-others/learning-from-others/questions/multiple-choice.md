# Learning from others — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) seeking the fault in oneself, not in others; forgiving others generously; learning from others' knowledge, supporting their right deeds and rejoicing in their success without envy  b) it has nothing to do with how Mongolia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) seeking the fault in oneself, not in others; forgiving others generously; learning from others' knowledge, supporting their right deeds and rejoicing in their success without envy** — That is what this lesson teaches.

a) seeking the fault in oneself, not in others; forgiving others generously; learning from others' knowledge, supporting their right deeds and rejoicing in their success without envy  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Mongolia
**Answer: a) seeking the fault in oneself, not in others; forgiving others generously; learning from others' knowledge, supporting their right deeds and rejoicing in their success without envy** — That is what this lesson teaches.
