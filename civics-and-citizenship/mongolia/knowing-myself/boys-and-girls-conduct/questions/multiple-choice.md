# Examining the Conduct of Boys and Girls — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) observing and judging children's own conduct  b) it has nothing to do with how Mongolia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) observing and judging children's own conduct** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) observing and judging children's own conduct  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Mongolia
**Answer: a) observing and judging children's own conduct** — That is what this lesson teaches.
