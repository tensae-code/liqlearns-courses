# Life's purpose — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) knowing life's purpose and reaching toward it  b) it has nothing to do with how Mongolia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) knowing life's purpose and reaching toward it** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) knowing life's purpose and reaching toward it  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Mongolia
**Answer: a) knowing life's purpose and reaching toward it** — That is what this lesson teaches.
