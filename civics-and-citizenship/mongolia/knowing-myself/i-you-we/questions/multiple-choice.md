# I, You, We — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) the self, the other and the group  b) it has nothing to do with how Mongolia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) the self, the other and the group** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) the self, the other and the group  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Mongolia
**Answer: a) the self, the other and the group** — That is what this lesson teaches.
