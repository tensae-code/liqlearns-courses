# Civics and Citizenship — Mongolia course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

Civics belongs to the country: this course is Mongolia's
own — built from Mongolia's Иргэний боловсрол (Civic
Education) books for Grades 1–5 and Иргэний ёс зүйн
боловсрол (Civic Ethics Education) books for Grades 6–7
(Ministry of Education, Culture, Science and Sports /
Ministry of Education and Science, Mongolia, Mongolian),
all PARTIAL as filed. Inside the course the organisation
is by **levels, not grades**: the titles below ARE the
levels' titles, in path order — what the learner proves,
step by step. The grades the books come from live only in
each lesson's sources.

The path's own logic is Mongolia's, from the filing's
ladder sketch: the series "Би — Монгол улсын иргэн" (I am
a citizen of Mongolia) opens with the child's own conduct
— greetings, respect for parents and teachers, the ger,
the five livestock — and only at Grade 2 names the
customs, the flag and the anthem. Belonging then widens
from lineage to the nation (state symbols in depth, equal
rights, collective decisions, how the state serves
citizens, pupil self-government, fairness), the primary
arc closes by systematising the customs (symbolism,
measures, heritage) and naming rules, duties and
responsibilities as one set beside the local state
organisations, and at Grade 6 the subject renames itself
Civic Ethics and turns inward — self-knowledge,
self-correction, learning from others, the fruits of
actions — ending in national script and pride. That
ladder IS the path order (recommended, never forced — a
learner can jump in higher; the "start here instead"
hints point down this ladder).

The coined lesson names in the filing were deduped
before building, by lesson name: no coined name is used
by more than one placement, so 108 placements stock 108
lessons, each lesson carrying exactly one placement.
Every placement stocks into exactly one lesson, and each
placement's provenance row (book + printed page, exactly
as filed) is in that lesson's `lesson/sources.md`. Unit
folders are the filing's own theme names; a theme can
run through several levels, as Mongolia's own books
return to the same themes year by year.
