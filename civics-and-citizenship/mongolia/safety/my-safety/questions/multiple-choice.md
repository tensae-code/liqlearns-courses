# My safety — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) keeping oneself safe  b) it has nothing to do with how Mongolia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) keeping oneself safe** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) keeping oneself safe  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Mongolia
**Answer: a) keeping oneself safe** — That is what this lesson teaches.
