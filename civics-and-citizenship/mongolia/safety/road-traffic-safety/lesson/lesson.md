# Let us keep road traffic safety

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Let us keep road traffic safety. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Mongolia's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: road safety as a shared duty Give one real example from your own life or from the Mongolia you live in, and name the lesson's key words: let, us. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
