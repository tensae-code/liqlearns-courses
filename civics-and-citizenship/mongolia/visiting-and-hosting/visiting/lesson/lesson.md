# The custom of visiting

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: The custom of visiting. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Mongolia's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

visiting has its own custom in every country; the guest is honoured from welcome to farewell, and the one who sends off is also a guest (read first-hand on the chapter opener)

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: visiting has its own custom in every country; the guest is honoured from welcome to farewell, and the one who sends off is also a guest (read first-hand on the chapter opener) Give one real example from your own life or from the Mongolia you live in, and name the lesson's key words: the, custom.
