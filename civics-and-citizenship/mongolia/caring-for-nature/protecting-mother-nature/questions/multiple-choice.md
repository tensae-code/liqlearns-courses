# Let's protect mother nature — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) protecting nature as the citizen's care for the homeland  b) it has nothing to do with how Mongolia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) protecting nature as the citizen's care for the homeland** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) protecting nature as the citizen's care for the homeland  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Mongolia
**Answer: a) protecting nature as the citizen's care for the homeland** — That is what this lesson teaches.
