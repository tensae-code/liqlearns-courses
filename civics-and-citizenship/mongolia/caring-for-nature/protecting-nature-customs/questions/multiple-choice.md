# Customs of Protecting Nature — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) nature protection as inherited custom  b) it has nothing to do with how Mongolia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) nature protection as inherited custom** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) nature protection as inherited custom  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Mongolia
**Answer: a) nature protection as inherited custom** — That is what this lesson teaches.
