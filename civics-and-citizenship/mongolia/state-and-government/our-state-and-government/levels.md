# Our state and government — depth

This lesson is ONE lesson with no sub lessons: it teaches a single thing, whole. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: in a democratic country citizens themselves elect and form the state and government, which protect citizens; citizens pay taxes that form the state budget, spent for the people's welfare; supporting the elected government and obeying its laws and rules is for everyone's own good (read first-hand on the chapter opener) — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
