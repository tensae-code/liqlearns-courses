# Our state and government

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Our state and government. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Mongolia's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

in a democratic country citizens themselves elect and form the state and government, which protect citizens; citizens pay taxes that form the state budget, spent for the people's welfare; supporting the elected government and obeying its laws and rules is for everyone's own good (read first-hand on the chapter opener)

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: in a democratic country citizens themselves elect and form the state and government, which protect citizens; citizens pay taxes that form the state budget, spent for the people's welfare; supporting the elected government and obeying its laws and rules is for everyone's own good (read first-hand on the chapter opener) Give one real example from your own life or from the Mongolia you live in, and name the lesson's key words: our, state. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
