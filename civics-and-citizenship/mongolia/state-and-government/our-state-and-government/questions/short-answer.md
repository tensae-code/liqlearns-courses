# Our state and government — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** in a democratic country citizens themselves elect and form the state and government, which protect citizens; citizens pay taxes that form the state budget, spent for the people's welfare; supporting the elected government and obeying its laws and rules is for everyone's own good (read first-hand on the chapter opener)
