# Valuing a centred, upright character — depth

This lesson is ONE lesson with no sub lessons: it teaches a single thing, whole. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: uprightness and fairness as the most precious character; not swelling with praise nor sinking under blame — and the Mongolian principle that hidden praise and open blame are both good — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
