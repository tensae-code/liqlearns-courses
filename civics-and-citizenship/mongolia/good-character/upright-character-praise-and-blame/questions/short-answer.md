# Valuing a centred, upright character — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** uprightness and fairness as the most precious character; not swelling with praise nor sinking under blame — and the Mongolian principle that hidden praise and open blame are both good

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** uprightness and fairness as the most precious character; not swelling with praise nor sinking under blame — and the Mongolian principle that hidden praise and open blame are both good
