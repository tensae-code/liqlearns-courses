# What is pupil self-government? — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) pupils governing their own affairs at school  b) it has nothing to do with how Mongolia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) pupils governing their own affairs at school** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) pupils governing their own affairs at school  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Mongolia
**Answer: a) pupils governing their own affairs at school** — That is what this lesson teaches.
