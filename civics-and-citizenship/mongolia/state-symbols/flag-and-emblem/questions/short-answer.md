# State flag and emblem — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** the state flag symbolises independence, sovereignty and good fortune; the Great White Banner (Их цагаан туг) of nine white yak-tail banners, kept in the State Palace and honoured at Naadam; colours and the number nine as symbols (read first-hand on the chapter opener)
