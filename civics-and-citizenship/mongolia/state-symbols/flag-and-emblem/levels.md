# State flag and emblem — depth

This lesson is ONE lesson with no sub lessons: it teaches a single thing, whole. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: the state flag symbolises independence, sovereignty and good fortune; the Great White Banner (Их цагаан туг) of nine white yak-tail banners, kept in the State Palace and honoured at Naadam; colours and the number nine as symbols (read first-hand on the chapter opener) — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
