# The harm of bad deeds — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) bad deeds bring harm and bad consequences  b) it has nothing to do with how Mongolia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) bad deeds bring harm and bad consequences** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) bad deeds bring harm and bad consequences  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Mongolia
**Answer: a) bad deeds bring harm and bad consequences** — That is what this lesson teaches.
