# Correcting Wrong Deeds

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Correcting Wrong Deeds. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Mongolia's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

correcting wrong actions of body, speech and mind, and striving not to fall into wrong thoughts, not to speak bad words and not to do wrong deeds

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: correcting wrong actions of body, speech and mind, and striving not to fall into wrong thoughts, not to speak bad words and not to do wrong deeds Give one real example from your own life or from the Mongolia you live in, and name the lesson's key words: correcting, wrong. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
