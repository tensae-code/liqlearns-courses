# Lesson plan — Correcting Wrong Deeds

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Mongolia · **Title:** Good and Bad Deeds · **Lesson:** Correcting Wrong Deeds
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Knowing Yourself: Civic Ethics (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** correcting wrong actions of body, speech and mind, and striving not to fall into wrong thoughts, not to speak bad words and not to do wrong deeds This lesson stands on a student book.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Before this lesson
- Stands in path order after **Controlling and correcting one's actions** — do that lesson first if this one feels hard (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: correcting wrong actions of body, speech and mind, and striving not to fall into wrong thoughts, not to speak bad words and not to do wrong deeds
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, school or town — or, for this book's lessons, from the public life of Mongolia they live in.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the learner's own life in Mongolia and the book's own teaching, as the book itself works.
