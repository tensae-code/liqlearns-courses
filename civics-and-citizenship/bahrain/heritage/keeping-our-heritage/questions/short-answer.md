# Keeping Our Heritage — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Preserving heritage is a responsibility — heritage is part of the citizen's identity and of the environment he lives in, and each generation must guard what it inherited for the next.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Responsibility means the heritage's survival is placed in the citizen's own hands.
