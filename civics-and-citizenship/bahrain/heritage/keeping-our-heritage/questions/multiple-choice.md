# Keeping Our Heritage — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) Preserving heritage is a responsibility — heritage is part of the citizen's identity and of the environment he lives in, and each generation must guard what it inherited for the next.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Responsibility means the heritage's survival is placed in the citizen's own hands.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
