# Video script — Keeping Our Heritage

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Preserving heritage is a responsibility — heritage is part of the citizen's identity and of the environment he lives in, and each generation must guard what it inherited for the next.

Heritage is what a people inherits: its places, crafts, stories and ways. The book teaches preserving it as a responsibility, because heritage is part of identity — lose it, and the citizen loses a piece of who he is.

Heritage is also part of the environment the citizen lives in, so guarding it is daily work, not ceremony: respect it, learn it, hand it on whole.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
