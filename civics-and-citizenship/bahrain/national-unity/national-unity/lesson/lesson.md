# National Unity

## Do it first

List the different groups that make up Bahrain's people as you know them. Beside the list, write what holds them together as one homeland. Keep both; the lesson founds unity on them

## What the book teaches

The final preparatory book opens with national unity: what it means for a people of many origins and sects to be one nation. Its foundations in Bahrain are a shared homeland, social peace, and development that includes all.

## The lesson in full

Lesson 1 itself opens with the rule-of-law document 'no one is above the law': unity is not sameness — it is different people held in one homeland by a law that stands above every one of them alike.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: national unity is Bahrain's people standing as one homeland — across its multi-ethnic, multi-sect society — founded on social peace, shared development and respect for the law, where no one is above the law. Give one real example from your own life in Bahrain, and name the lesson's key words: unity, homeland, peace, law. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Bahrain's own books teach civics by building the citizen from his own society outward — society and the modern state first, then living together, then the citizen facing the world and guarding his homeland. When a lesson asks you to look at your own community, really look: your community is this course's first text.
