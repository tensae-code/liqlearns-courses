# National Unity — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** National unity is bahrain's people standing as one homeland — across its multi-ethnic, multi-sect society — founded on social peace, shared development and respect for the law, where no one is above the law.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Unity is founded on peace, shared development and a law above all persons.
