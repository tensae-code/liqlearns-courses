# Video script — National Unity

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
National unity is bahrain's people standing as one homeland — across its multi-ethnic, multi-sect society — founded on social peace, shared development and respect for the law, where no one is above the law.

The final preparatory book opens with national unity: what it means for a people of many origins and sects to be one nation. Its foundations in Bahrain are a shared homeland, social peace, and development that includes all.

Lesson 1 itself opens with the rule-of-law document 'no one is above the law': unity is not sameness — it is different people held in one homeland by a law that stands above every one of them alike.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
