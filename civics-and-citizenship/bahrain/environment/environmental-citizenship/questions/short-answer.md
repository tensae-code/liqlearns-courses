# Environmental Citizenship — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Environmental citizenship is the citizen's duties toward the environment — the land, sea and air of bahrain are part of the homeland, and caring for them is a citizen's duty, not a hobby.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** The environment is homeland too — the duty to it is a citizen's duty.
