# Environmental Citizenship

## Do it first

List three harms the environment around you in Bahrain suffers, and one act that answers each. Keep your list; the lesson names the duty behind the acts

## What the book teaches

Citizenship does not stop at people. Environmental citizenship extends the citizen's duties to the environment he lives in: Bahrain's land, its sea and shores, its air and water.

## The lesson in full

The book teaches those duties as part of belonging: the homeland the citizen loves includes its nature, and the generation that dirties or wastes it steals from the generations after it.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: environmental citizenship is the citizen's duties toward the environment — the land, sea and air of Bahrain are part of the homeland, and caring for them is a citizen's duty, not a hobby. Give one real example from your own life in Bahrain, and name the lesson's key words: environment, citizenship, duty, nature. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Bahrain's own books teach civics by building the citizen from his own society outward — society and the modern state first, then living together, then the citizen facing the world and guarding his homeland. When a lesson asks you to look at your own community, really look: your community is this course's first text.
