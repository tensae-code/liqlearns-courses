# Video script — Environmental Citizenship

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Environmental citizenship is the citizen's duties toward the environment — the land, sea and air of bahrain are part of the homeland, and caring for them is a citizen's duty, not a hobby.

Citizenship does not stop at people. Environmental citizenship extends the citizen's duties to the environment he lives in: Bahrain's land, its sea and shores, its air and water.

The book teaches those duties as part of belonging: the homeland the citizen loves includes its nature, and the generation that dirties or wastes it steals from the generations after it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
