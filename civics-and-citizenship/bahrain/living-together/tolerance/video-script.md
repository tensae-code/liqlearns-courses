# Video script — Tolerance

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Tolerance is accepting difference within the one society — bearing with people whose views, ways or beliefs differ from yours without hatred or exclusion — and it holds shared life together.

People in one society differ — in opinion, custom, taste and belief. Tolerance is the virtue that lets difference exist without breaking the society: accepting the other as a fellow citizen and neighbour.

The book teaches tolerance as strength, not weakness: the tolerant citizen is secure enough in his own way to let others live theirs, and the society that practises it keeps its peace.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
