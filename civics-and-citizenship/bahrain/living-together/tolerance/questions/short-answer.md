# Tolerance — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Tolerance is accepting difference within the one society — bearing with people whose views, ways or beliefs differ from yours without hatred or exclusion — and it holds shared life together.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Tolerance accepts difference; it does not require agreeing with it.
