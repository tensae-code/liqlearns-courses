# Tolerance — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) Tolerance is accepting difference within the one society — bearing with people whose views, ways or beliefs differ from yours without hatred or exclusion — and it holds shared life together.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Tolerance accepts difference; it does not require agreeing with it.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
