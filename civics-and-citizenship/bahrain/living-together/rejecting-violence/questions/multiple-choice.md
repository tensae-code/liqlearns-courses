# Rejecting Violence — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) Violence is to be rejected — renounced in school, in the family and in society — because it solves nothing and wounds the whole shared life; the citizen's way is dialogue and law, not force.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Rejecting violence is the negative side of every virtue in this level: dialogue, tolerance and law all refuse it.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
