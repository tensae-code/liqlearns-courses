# Rejecting Violence — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Violence is to be rejected — renounced in school, in the family and in society — because it solves nothing and wounds the whole shared life; the citizen's way is dialogue and law, not force.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Rejecting violence is the negative side of every virtue in this level: dialogue, tolerance and law all refuse it.
