# Video script — Rejecting Violence

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Violence is to be rejected — renounced in school, in the family and in society — because it solves nothing and wounds the whole shared life; the citizen's way is dialogue and law, not force.

To reject violence is to renounce it entirely: in school, where it poisons learning; in the family, where it breaks trust; and in society, where it tears the shared life the citizen is being formed to protect.

The Grade 8 book closes its living-together climb here: the pupil who has learned dialogue, tolerance and institutions is asked to refuse the opposite of them all — force — wherever it appears.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
