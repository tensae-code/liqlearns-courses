# Dialogue — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Dialogue — talking through differences calmly, listening and answering — is a citizen's skill for living with people who disagree, and it can be learned and practised.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Listening is half of dialogue; a dialogue of one voice is a speech.
