# Dialogue — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) Dialogue — talking through differences calmly, listening and answering — is a citizen's skill for living with people who disagree, and it can be learned and practised.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Listening is half of dialogue; a dialogue of one voice is a speech.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
