# Video script — Dialogue

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Dialogue — talking through differences calmly, listening and answering — is a citizen's skill for living with people who disagree, and it can be learned and practised.

Dialogue is more than talking: it is speaking and listening in turn, with the aim of understanding and reaching what is right or agreed, instead of merely winning.

The book teaches dialogue as a skill of citizenship because a society of different people survives by it: families, classes, neighbours and a whole country settle their differences by dialogue or suffer them unsettled.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
