# Social Institutions and Citizenship Values — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) The social institutions — family, school and the bodies of shared life — carry the values of citizenship, teaching them by how they treat people every day.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Values live inside institutions; weaken the institution and the value loses its carrier.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
