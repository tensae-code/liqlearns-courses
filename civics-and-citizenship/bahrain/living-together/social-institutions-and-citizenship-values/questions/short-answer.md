# Social Institutions and Citizenship Values — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** The social institutions — family, school and the bodies of shared life — carry the values of citizenship, teaching them by how they treat people every day.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Values live inside institutions; weaken the institution and the value loses its carrier.
