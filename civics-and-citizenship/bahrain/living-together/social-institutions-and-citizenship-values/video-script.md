# Video script — Social Institutions and Citizenship Values

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The social institutions — family, school and the bodies of shared life — carry the values of citizenship, teaching them by how they treat people every day.

Citizenship values are not learned from speeches alone. They are carried by social institutions: the family that teaches care, the school that teaches fairness and cooperation, and the other bodies where people live and work together.

The lesson binds the two together — institutions and values — so the pupil sees where his values came from and what his own part will be in carrying them to the next generation.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
