# Social Institutions and Citizenship Values — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — the social institutions — family, school and the bodies of shared life — carry the values of citizenship, teaching them by how they treat people every day. — in their own words, give one real example from their own life in Bahrain, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or community situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
