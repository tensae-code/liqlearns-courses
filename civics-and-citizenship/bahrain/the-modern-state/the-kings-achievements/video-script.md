# Video script — The King's Achievements

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The main achievements realised in king hamad's reign — the works and changes of his rule that the citizen can name — are the second half of the king's lesson.

A reign is known by its works. The book gathers the main achievements realised in King Hamad's reign — what was built, reformed and opened in the Kingdom under his rule.

The pupil learns to name those achievements concretely, as a citizen names the works of his own country: not praise in the abstract, but the record of a reign he can point to in the Bahrain around him.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
