# The King's Achievements

## Do it first

Name two achievements of King Hamad's reign that you can see in Bahrain today. Keep your list; the lesson completes it

## What the book teaches

A reign is known by its works. The book gathers the main achievements realised in King Hamad's reign — what was built, reformed and opened in the Kingdom under his rule.

## The lesson in full

The pupil learns to name those achievements concretely, as a citizen names the works of his own country: not praise in the abstract, but the record of a reign he can point to in the Bahrain around him.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the main achievements realised in King Hamad's reign — the works and changes of his rule that the citizen can name — are the second half of the King's lesson. Give one real example from your own life in Bahrain, and name the lesson's key words: achievements, reign, reform, kingdom. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Bahrain's own books teach civics by building the citizen from his own society outward — society and the modern state first, then living together, then the citizen facing the world and guarding his homeland. When a lesson asks you to look at your own community, really look: your community is this course's first text.
