# Video script — Reform and the Democratic March

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
King hamad's reform and modernisation project, and the democratic march that followed it, resounded through the whole kingdom — this study dossier traces that echo.

This part is a study dossier (ملف), not an ordinary lesson: it gathers how the reform, modernisation and democratic-march project echoed through Bahrain — in its institutions, its public life and its people.

The pupil studies the echo itself: a reform announced at the top becomes real only as it resounds through the society, and the dossier asks the citizen to hear where it reached and what it changed.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
