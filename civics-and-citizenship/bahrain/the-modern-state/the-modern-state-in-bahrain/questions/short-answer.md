# The Modern State in Bahrain — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Bahrain is a modern state — a state with institutions, laws and a government that serves the whole country, and the pupil can say how that state arose and what it consists of.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** The state is met through its institutions in daily life before it is studied as an idea.
