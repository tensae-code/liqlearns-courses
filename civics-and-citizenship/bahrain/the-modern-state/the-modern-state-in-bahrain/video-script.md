# Video script — The Modern State in Bahrain

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Bahrain is a modern state — a state with institutions, laws and a government that serves the whole country, and the pupil can say how that state arose and what it consists of.

The modern state is the form Bahrain takes as a country: a state standing on institutions rather than on persons, ruling by law, and providing the services and order a whole society needs.

The lesson shows how that modern state arose in Bahrain and what it consists of, so the pupil meets the state not as a distant power but as the organised form of his own society's life.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
