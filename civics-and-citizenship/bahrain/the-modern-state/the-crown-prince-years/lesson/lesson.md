# The Crown Prince Years

## Do it first

Write what you know of King Hamad's life before he became King, and one thing from his first years of rule. Keep both; the lesson orders them

## What the book teaches

Before his reign, King Hamad served as crown prince — years of preparation in which he learned the state he would one day lead. The book teaches those years as the foundation of the reign.

## The lesson in full

The lesson then follows his first years in rule, when the direction of the reign was set. A citizen who knows his King's road to the throne understands the state he lives in as a story with a beginning he can tell.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: King Hamad bin Isa Al Khalifa's years as crown prince and his first years of rule prepared and began the reign the pupil lives under — the book teaches that story as part of the citizen's own history. Give one real example from your own life in Bahrain, and name the lesson's key words: king, crown prince, reign, rule. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Bahrain's own books teach civics by building the citizen from his own society outward — society and the modern state first, then living together, then the citizen facing the world and guarding his homeland. When a lesson asks you to look at your own community, really look: your community is this course's first text.
