# Video script — The Crown Prince Years

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
King hamad bin isa al khalifa's years as crown prince and his first years of rule prepared and began the reign the pupil lives under — the book teaches that story as part of the citizen's own history.

Before his reign, King Hamad served as crown prince — years of preparation in which he learned the state he would one day lead. The book teaches those years as the foundation of the reign.

The lesson then follows his first years in rule, when the direction of the reign was set. A citizen who knows his King's road to the throne understands the state he lives in as a story with a beginning he can tell.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
