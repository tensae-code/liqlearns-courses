# Civics and Citizenship — Bahrain course — CURRICULUM (the path)

One course for Bahrain inside the Civics and Citizenship
folder, organised by **levels, not grades**: a learner
climbs when they have proved the level, not when a school
calendar says so. The school grades the books come from
(Grades 7, 8 and 9, the three preparatory grades) appear
only in each lesson's sources (provenance), never as the
course's organisation. The path follows Bahrain's own
books' order: the preparatory Citizenship Education run
builds the citizen from his own society outward — what
Bahraini society is made of and how it developed, the
modern state and the King's reign and reform project,
then law and human rights, and Bahrain among the nations;
then the society the citizen lives in — its culture, its
women, law in daily life, dialogue, tolerance, children's
rights, violence refused; and finally national unity and
the citizen facing the world, closing with the homeland's
safety and continuing march. That ladder IS the
placements files' ladder sketches, reconciled into one:
the Grade 4 primary book sits at Level 1 in the sketch
but is UNREAD, so Level 1 is sketched, not stocked; the
Grade 7 book sits at Level 2, the Grade 8 book at Level
3, and the Grade 9 book at Level 4.

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything
in this course comes from government textbooks — this
country's own التربية للمواطنة (Citizenship Education)
student books (Ministry of Education, Kingdom of
Bahrain) — except the **Tip boxes**: a tip in a box is
from us, the people making LiqLearns. Tips are ours; the
lessons are the books'. See ABOUT.md for the full book
list and the acceptance reckoning.

## The levels (the path, in order)

1. **Level 1 — sketched, not stocked.** The ladder
   sketch places the census's Citizenship Education
   book for Grade 4 Primary at this level. That book is
   RECORD-ONLY — UNREAD (no copy could be obtained by
   either route tried; see ABOUT.md), so no lesson
   stands here and nothing from it is claimed.
2. **Level 2 — Bahraini Society, the Modern State, Law
   and Rights** — the elements of Bahraini society and
   its features; how our society grows; the modern
   state in Bahrain; the Crown Prince years; the King's
   achievements; the reform-and-democratic-march study
   dossier; Bahrain a state of law; human rights; the
   rights-and-duties study dossier; Bahrain's
   international relations; and Bahrain's Arab belonging
   (`bahraini-society/`, `the-modern-state/`,
   `law-and-rights/`, `bahrain-and-the-world/`).
   11 lessons.
3. **Level 3 — Culture, Society and Living Together** —
   popular culture in Bahrain; women in Bahraini
   society; law in our lives; dialogue; social
   institutions and citizenship values; tolerance;
   children's rights; and rejecting violence
   (`our-culture/`, `our-society/`, `law-and-rights/`,
   `living-together/`). 8 lessons.
4. **Level 4 — National Unity, the Citizen and the
   World, and the Homeland's Safety** — national unity;
   the citizen and democracy; keeping our heritage;
   environmental citizenship; work; development; facing
   the knowledge gap; media and globalisation;
   solidarity and international cooperation;
   international humanitarian law; dialogue of
   civilisations; violence and sabotage in our
   homeland; protecting the safety of our homeland; and
   our homeland triumphs and continues its march
   (`national-unity/`, `democracy/`, `heritage/`,
   `environment/`, `work-and-development/`,
   `the-citizen-and-the-world/`, `homeland-safety/`).
   14 lessons.

**Totals: 33 lessons, 33 placements stocked** (Level 2:
11 · Level 3: 8 · Level 4: 14 — all from three student
books: *Citizenship Education* for the three preparatory
grades (Ministry of Education, Kingdom of Bahrain,
Arabic, editions of 2021, 2021 and 2017, all COMPLETE as
filed); Level 1 sketched, not stocked — its Grade 4
book is UNREAD; see ABOUT.md).

*Standing note on the count: the three read books' 33
placements coin 33 distinct lesson names — no name is
coined twice — so no dedupe merge arises. 33 placements,
33 lessons, every placement stocked exactly once.*
