# The Citizen and Democracy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) The citizen has real roles in the democratic system — taking part, choosing, speaking and watching over public life — and by performing them he consolidates democracy, making it firm in his country.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Consolidating democracy is the citizen's work, repeated, not a founder's work done once.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
