# The Citizen and Democracy — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** The citizen has real roles in the democratic system — taking part, choosing, speaking and watching over public life — and by performing them he consolidates democracy, making it firm in his country.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Consolidating democracy is the citizen's work, repeated, not a founder's work done once.
