# Video script — The Citizen and Democracy

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The citizen has real roles in the democratic system — taking part, choosing, speaking and watching over public life — and by performing them he consolidates democracy, making it firm in his country.

Democracy is not a machine that runs itself. The book teaches the citizen's roles in the democratic system: the acts by which ordinary people take part in ruling their country and hold its public life to account.

Those roles matter because democracy is consolidated — made firm and lasting — by citizens performing them. A democracy whose citizens stop taking part weakens; one whose citizens act grows roots.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
