# Video script — Popular Culture in Bahrain

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Bahrain's popular culture — the folk culture its people live and hand down, its crafts, tales, occasions and ways — is part of the kingdom's identity and the citizen's inheritance.

Popular culture is the culture of the people themselves: what they make, tell, sing, celebrate and hand down outside official halls. In Bahrain it carries the memory of pearl divers, craftsmen, sailors and families.

The lesson teaches its forms and its place in the Kingdom's identity: a citizen who knows his people's culture knows a part of himself that no institution gave him and none can take away.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
