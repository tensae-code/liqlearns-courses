## What the book teaches

Popular culture is the culture of the people themselves: what they make, tell, sing, celebrate and hand down outside official halls. In Bahrain it carries the memory of pearl divers, craftsmen, sailors and families.

## The lesson in full

The lesson teaches its forms and its place in the Kingdom's identity: a citizen who knows his people's culture knows a part of himself that no institution gave him and none can take away.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Bahrain's popular culture — the folk culture its people live and hand down, its crafts, tales, occasions and ways — is part of the Kingdom's identity and the citizen's inheritance. Give one real example from your own life in Bahrain, and name the lesson's key words: culture, folk, heritage, identity. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Bahrain's own books teach civics by building the citizen from his own society outward — society and the modern state first, then living together, then the citizen facing the world and guarding his homeland. When a lesson asks you to look at your own community, really look: your community is this course's first text.
