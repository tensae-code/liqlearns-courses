# Popular Culture in Bahrain — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Bahrain's popular culture — the folk culture its people live and hand down, its crafts, tales, occasions and ways — is part of the kingdom's identity and the citizen's inheritance.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Popular culture is handed down by living it, not only by recording it.
