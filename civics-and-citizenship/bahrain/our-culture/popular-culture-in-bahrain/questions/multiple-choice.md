# Popular Culture in Bahrain — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) Bahrain's popular culture — the folk culture its people live and hand down, its crafts, tales, occasions and ways — is part of the kingdom's identity and the citizen's inheritance.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Popular culture is handed down by living it, not only by recording it.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
