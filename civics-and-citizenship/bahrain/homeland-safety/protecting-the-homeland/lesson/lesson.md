# Protecting the Safety of Our Homeland

## Do it first

List three ways an ordinary citizen — not a soldier or policeman — protects his homeland's safety. Keep your list; the lesson gathers the citizen's share

## What the book teaches

A homeland's safety is not guarded by its institutions alone. The book teaches protecting the safety of our homeland as a shared duty: the state guards, and the citizen guards with it — by vigilance, by lawfulness, and by refusing to shelter harm.

## The lesson in full

The pupil's share is real at his own scale: care for public things, respect for the law, and loyalty to the homeland's peace. Safety kept by all is safety that lasts.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: protecting the safety of our homeland is a shared duty — the homeland's security and peace are guarded by its institutions and by every citizen's vigilance, lawfulness and refusal of harm. Give one real example from your own life in Bahrain, and name the lesson's key words: safety, protection, homeland, duty. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Bahrain's own books teach civics by building the citizen from his own society outward — society and the modern state first, then living together, then the citizen facing the world and guarding his homeland. When a lesson asks you to look at your own community, really look: your community is this course's first text.
