# Protecting the Safety of Our Homeland — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Protecting the safety of our homeland is a shared duty — the homeland's security and peace are guarded by its institutions and by every citizen's vigilance, lawfulness and refusal of harm.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** The citizen's share of protection is vigilance, lawfulness and refusal of harm.
