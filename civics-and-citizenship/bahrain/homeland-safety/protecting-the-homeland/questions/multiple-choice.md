# Protecting the Safety of Our Homeland — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) Protecting the safety of our homeland is a shared duty — the homeland's security and peace are guarded by its institutions and by every citizen's vigilance, lawfulness and refusal of harm.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) The citizen's share of protection is vigilance, lawfulness and refusal of harm.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
