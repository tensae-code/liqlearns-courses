# Video script — Protecting the Safety of Our Homeland

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Protecting the safety of our homeland is a shared duty — the homeland's security and peace are guarded by its institutions and by every citizen's vigilance, lawfulness and refusal of harm.

A homeland's safety is not guarded by its institutions alone. The book teaches protecting the safety of our homeland as a shared duty: the state guards, and the citizen guards with it — by vigilance, by lawfulness, and by refusing to shelter harm.

The pupil's share is real at his own scale: care for public things, respect for the law, and loyalty to the homeland's peace. Safety kept by all is safety that lasts.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
