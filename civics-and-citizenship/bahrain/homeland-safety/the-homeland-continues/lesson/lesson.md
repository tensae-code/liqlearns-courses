# Our Homeland Triumphs and Continues Its March

## Do it first

Write the course backwards in one line: where did it start, and where has it brought you? Keep your line; the closing lesson gathers the whole march

## What the book teaches

The course ends where a citizen's course should: with the homeland continuing. Our homeland triumphs — it overcomes violence, sabotage and danger — and continues its march forward, in development, unity and peace.

## The lesson in full

This closing lesson is one of national confidence and continuity: the pupil who began, three books ago, by naming the elements of his society now stands as a citizen of a homeland that endures — and his own life becomes part of its march.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: our homeland triumphs and continues its march — the closing lesson of the whole preparatory course: Bahrain endures, overcomes its dangers, and goes forward, and the citizen walks with it. Give one real example from your own life in Bahrain, and name the lesson's key words: homeland, triumph, continuity, march. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Bahrain's own books teach civics by building the citizen from his own society outward — society and the modern state first, then living together, then the citizen facing the world and guarding his homeland. When a lesson asks you to look at your own community, really look: your community is this course's first text.
