# Video script — Our Homeland Triumphs and Continues Its March

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Our homeland triumphs and continues its march — the closing lesson of the whole preparatory course: bahrain endures, overcomes its dangers, and goes forward, and the citizen walks with it.

The course ends where a citizen's course should: with the homeland continuing. Our homeland triumphs — it overcomes violence, sabotage and danger — and continues its march forward, in development, unity and peace.

This closing lesson is one of national confidence and continuity: the pupil who began, three books ago, by naming the elements of his society now stands as a citizen of a homeland that endures — and his own life becomes part of its march.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
