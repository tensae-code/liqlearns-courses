# Video script — Violence and Sabotage in Our Homeland

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Violence and sabotage in the homeland are named and rejected — attacks on people and destruction of the country's own works wound bahrain itself, and the citizen refuses them utterly.

The book turns home with an exclamation: violence and sabotage in our homeland! Violence wounds people; sabotage destroys the works the homeland built for everyone — and both are done, terribly, inside the country they wound.

The lesson names and rejects them without softening: there is no cause that makes attacking one's own people's safety or works a citizen's act. The pupil who rejected violence at Grade 8 now refuses it at the scale of the homeland.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
