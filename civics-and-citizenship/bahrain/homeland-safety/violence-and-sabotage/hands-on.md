# Hands-on — Violence and Sabotage in Our Homeland

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write what sabotage of a public work — a school, a road, a power line — actually destroys for ordinary people. Keep your account; the lesson names the act for what it is

## Show it with real things
Take this lesson into one real act this week: do, make, visit, ask or help in a way that puts Violence and Sabotage in Our Homeland into practice in your own community in Bahrain — then write what happened and what the lesson would say about it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
