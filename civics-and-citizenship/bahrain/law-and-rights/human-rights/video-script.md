# Video script — Human Rights

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Human rights are the rights every person holds simply as a human being, and the citizen has a place in them — as a holder of rights and as one who respects the rights of others.

Human rights belong to every person by being human, before any office or favour grants them. The book teaches what they are: the standing of each person to live, learn, be treated fairly and be respected.

The citizen's place in them is double: he holds these rights himself, and he lives among others who hold the same rights — so respecting them is part of citizenship, not an extra.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
