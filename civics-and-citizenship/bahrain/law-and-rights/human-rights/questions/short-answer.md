# Human Rights — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Human rights are the rights every person holds simply as a human being, and the citizen has a place in them — as a holder of rights and as one who respects the rights of others.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Rights are summarised here from the book's teaching; no legal text is transcribed.
