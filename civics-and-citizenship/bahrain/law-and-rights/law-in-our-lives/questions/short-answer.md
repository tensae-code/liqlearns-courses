# Law in Our Lives — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** The law is present in the citizen's everyday life — at home, at school, on the road and in the market — not only in courts, and knowing where it meets daily life is part of living as a citizen.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Law in daily life is the Level 2 state of law seen at street level.
