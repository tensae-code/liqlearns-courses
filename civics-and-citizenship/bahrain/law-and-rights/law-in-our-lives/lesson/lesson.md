# Law in Our Lives

## Do it first

Count the laws or rules that have already touched your day today — road, school, shop, home. Keep your count; the lesson finds the law in ordinary life

## What the book teaches

The Grade 8 book brings the law down from the state to the street: law in our lives is where the law meets the citizen's everyday life — the rules that order traffic, protect buyers and sellers, guard children and keep shared life safe.

## The lesson in full

Meeting the law daily changes how a citizen stands: the law is not a distant threat but the agreed frame of common life, and respecting it in small things is citizenship practised every day.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the law is present in the citizen's everyday life — at home, at school, on the road and in the market — not only in courts, and knowing where it meets daily life is part of living as a citizen. Give one real example from your own life in Bahrain, and name the lesson's key words: law, daily life, rules, citizen. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Bahrain's own books teach civics by building the citizen from his own society outward — society and the modern state first, then living together, then the citizen facing the world and guarding his homeland. When a lesson asks you to look at your own community, really look: your community is this course's first text.
