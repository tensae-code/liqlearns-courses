# Video script — Law in Our Lives

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The law is present in the citizen's everyday life — at home, at school, on the road and in the market — not only in courts, and knowing where it meets daily life is part of living as a citizen.

The Grade 8 book brings the law down from the state to the street: law in our lives is where the law meets the citizen's everyday life — the rules that order traffic, protect buyers and sellers, guard children and keep shared life safe.

Meeting the law daily changes how a citizen stands: the law is not a distant threat but the agreed frame of common life, and respecting it in small things is citizenship practised every day.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
