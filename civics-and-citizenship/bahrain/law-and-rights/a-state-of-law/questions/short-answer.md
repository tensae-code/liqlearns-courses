# A State of Law — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** The kingdom of bahrain is a state of law — the law stands above every person and office, and the rule of law is the frame of the whole kingdom.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** The law binds the powerful and the ordinary citizen by the same measure.
