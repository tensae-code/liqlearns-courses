Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) The kingdom of bahrain is a state of law — the law stands above every person and office, and the rule of law is the frame of the whole kingdom.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
