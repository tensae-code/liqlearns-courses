# Video script — A State of Law

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The kingdom of bahrain is a state of law — the law stands above every person and office, and the rule of law is the frame of the whole kingdom.

A state of law means the law rules, not persons: government, institutions and citizens alike stand under the law, and no one is placed above it. Bahrain is taught here as exactly such a state.

For the pupil, the rule of law is the frame that makes every other lesson in this course hold: rights can be promised, and duties required, because a law above all persons guards them both.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
