# Children's Rights — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Children hold their own rights — to care, protection, learning and a name and family — and the pupil, himself a child, learns them as rights he already holds.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Children's rights are summarised here from the book's teaching; no legal text is transcribed.
