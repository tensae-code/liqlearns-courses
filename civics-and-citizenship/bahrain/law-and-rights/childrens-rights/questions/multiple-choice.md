# Children's Rights — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) Children hold their own rights — to care, protection, learning and a name and family — and the pupil, himself a child, learns them as rights he already holds.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Children's rights are summarised here from the book's teaching; no legal text is transcribed.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
