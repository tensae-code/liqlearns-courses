# Video script — Children's Rights

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Children hold their own rights — to care, protection, learning and a name and family — and the pupil, himself a child, learns them as rights he already holds.

Children's rights are the human rights of the youngest citizens, stated for their stage of life: the right to be cared for and protected, to learn, to health, and to grow up in a family and a name of their own.

The pupil meets this lesson as its subject: these are not rights for other children far away, but his own — and knowing them teaches him both to claim his standing and to respect every other child's.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
