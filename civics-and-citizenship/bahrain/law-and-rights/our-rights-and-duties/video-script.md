# Video script — Our Rights and Our Duties

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Our rights and our duties are the two sides of citizenship held together — a citizen claims his rights and performs his duties, and neither side stands without the other.

This study dossier (ملف) sets rights and duties side by side. Rights are what the citizen may claim; duties are what the citizen owes. The book refuses to teach one without the other.

Held together, they make the citizen whole: a person who only claims becomes a burden, a person who only owes becomes a subject — the citizen of Bahrain is taught to carry both sides at once.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
