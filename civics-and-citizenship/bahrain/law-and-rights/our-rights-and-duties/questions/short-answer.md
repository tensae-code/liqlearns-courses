# Our Rights and Our Duties — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Our rights and our duties are the two sides of citizenship held together — a citizen claims his rights and performs his duties, and neither side stands without the other.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Every right in the dossier is taught beside the duty that keeps it alive for everyone.
