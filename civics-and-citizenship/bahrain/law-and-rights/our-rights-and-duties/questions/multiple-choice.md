# Our Rights and Our Duties — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) Our rights and our duties are the two sides of citizenship held together — a citizen claims his rights and performs his duties, and neither side stands without the other.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Every right in the dossier is taught beside the duty that keeps it alive for everyone.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
