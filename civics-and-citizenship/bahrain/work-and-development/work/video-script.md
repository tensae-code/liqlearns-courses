# Video script — Work

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Work is the basis of the homeland's development and the ground of the citizen's valued place in society — by work the country is built and the worker holds his standing.

Nothing in a homeland develops by itself. The book teaches work as the basis of the homeland's development: every road, school, boat and office stands because someone worked.

Work also grounds the citizen's own standing: the worker holds a valued place in society because he carries a share of the whole. The pupil is taught to honour work — his future work included — in all its honest forms.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
