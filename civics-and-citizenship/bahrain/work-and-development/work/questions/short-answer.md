# Work — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Work is the basis of the homeland's development and the ground of the citizen's valued place in society — by work the country is built and the worker holds his standing.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
