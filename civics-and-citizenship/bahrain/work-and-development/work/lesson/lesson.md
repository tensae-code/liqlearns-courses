# Work

## Do it first

Name three kinds of work that build Bahrain, and what each one adds to the country. Keep your list; the lesson founds development on work

## What the book teaches

Nothing in a homeland develops by itself. The book teaches work as the basis of the homeland's development: every road, school, boat and office stands because someone worked.

## The lesson in full

Work also grounds the citizen's own standing: the worker holds a valued place in society because he carries a share of the whole. The pupil is taught to honour work — his future work included — in all its honest forms.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: work is the basis of the homeland's development and the ground of the citizen's valued place in society — by work the country is built and the worker holds his standing. Give one real example from your own life in Bahrain, and name the lesson's key words: work, development, homeland, standing. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Bahrain's own books teach civics by building the citizen from his own society outward — society and the modern state first, then living together, then the citizen facing the world and guarding his homeland. When a lesson asks you to look at your own community, really look: your community is this course's first text.
