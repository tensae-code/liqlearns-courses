# Video script — Development

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Development is the homeland's growth in people's lives — in learning, health, work and welfare — pursued so it lasts, including the sustainable development goals the book names among its objectives.

Development is more than building: it is the growth of a whole society's life — its people's learning, health, work and welfare rising together. The book teaches it after work because work is its engine.

Among the lesson's objectives the book names the sustainable development goals: development that lasts, meeting this generation's needs without stealing the next generation's share of land, sea and resources.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
