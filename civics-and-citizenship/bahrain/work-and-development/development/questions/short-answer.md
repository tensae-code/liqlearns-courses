# Development — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Development is the homeland's growth in people's lives — in learning, health, work and welfare — pursued so it lasts, including the sustainable development goals the book names among its objectives.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Sustainable development is development that can continue — the goals are named among the book's own objectives.
