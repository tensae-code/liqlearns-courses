# Development — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Development is the homeland's growth in people's lives — in learning, health, work and welfare — pursued so it lasts, including the sustainable development goals the book names among its objectives.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a child's own home, school or community in Bahrain.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Bahrain and lived there.
