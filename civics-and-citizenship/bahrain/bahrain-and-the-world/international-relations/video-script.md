# Video script — Bahrain's International Relations

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The kingdom of bahrain has relations with the world's states and organisations — friendships, agreements and cooperation that place the small kingdom in the wide world.

No state lives alone. Bahrain's international relations are its ties with other states and with international organisations — ties of friendship, trade, security and shared work.

The lesson shows the pupil his Kingdom acting in the world: sending and receiving, agreeing and cooperating. A citizen who knows his country's relations knows where his homeland stands among nations.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
