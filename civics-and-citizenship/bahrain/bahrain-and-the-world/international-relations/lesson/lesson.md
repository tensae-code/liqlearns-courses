# Bahrain's International Relations

## Do it first

Name two countries or organisations Bahrain works with, and one thing they do together. Keep your list; the lesson widens it

## What the book teaches

No state lives alone. Bahrain's international relations are its ties with other states and with international organisations — ties of friendship, trade, security and shared work.

## The lesson in full

The lesson shows the pupil his Kingdom acting in the world: sending and receiving, agreeing and cooperating. A citizen who knows his country's relations knows where his homeland stands among nations.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the Kingdom of Bahrain has relations with the world's states and organisations — friendships, agreements and cooperation that place the small Kingdom in the wide world. Give one real example from your own life in Bahrain, and name the lesson's key words: relations, world, cooperation, states. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Bahrain's own books teach civics by building the citizen from his own society outward — society and the modern state first, then living together, then the citizen facing the world and guarding his homeland. When a lesson asks you to look at your own community, really look: your community is this course's first text.
