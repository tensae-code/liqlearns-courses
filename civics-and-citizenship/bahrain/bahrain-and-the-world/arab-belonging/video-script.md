# Video script — Bahrain's Arab Belonging

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Bahrain belongs to the arab world — by language, history and shared life — and that arab belonging is part of the bahraini citizen's own identity.

Belonging is the feeling and the fact of being part of a larger whole. Bahrain's Arab belonging is its place in the Arab world: the Arabic language, a shared history, and a common life with the Arab peoples around it.

The Grade 7 book closes here for a reason: the citizen who began with his own society ends by knowing the wider family his country belongs to — the Arab world in which Bahrain stands.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
