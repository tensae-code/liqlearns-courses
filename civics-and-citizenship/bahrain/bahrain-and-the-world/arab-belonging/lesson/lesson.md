# Bahrain's Arab Belonging

## Do it first

List three things Bahrain shares with the other Arab countries — language, customs, occasions, history. Keep your list; the lesson names the belonging

## What the book teaches

Belonging is the feeling and the fact of being part of a larger whole. Bahrain's Arab belonging is its place in the Arab world: the Arabic language, a shared history, and a common life with the Arab peoples around it.

## The lesson in full

The Grade 7 book closes here for a reason: the citizen who began with his own society ends by knowing the wider family his country belongs to — the Arab world in which Bahrain stands.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Bahrain belongs to the Arab world — by language, history and shared life — and that Arab belonging is part of the Bahraini citizen's own identity. Give one real example from your own life in Bahrain, and name the lesson's key words: belonging, arab, identity, language. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Bahrain's own books teach civics by building the citizen from his own society outward — society and the modern state first, then living together, then the citizen facing the world and guarding his homeland. When a lesson asks you to look at your own community, really look: your community is this course's first text.
