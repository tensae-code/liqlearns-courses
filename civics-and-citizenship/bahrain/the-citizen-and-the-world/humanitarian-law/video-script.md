# Video script — International Humanitarian Law

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
International humanitarian law binds even war: it exists to limit the effects of wars — protecting those who do not fight and restraining how wars are fought — and committing to it is a duty the book teaches.

Even war has law. International humanitarian law is the body of law that limits the effects of armed conflict: protecting civilians, the wounded and prisoners, and restraining the means of fighting.

The book teaches commitment to it as part of the citizen's world education: humanity does not end where conflict begins, and the pupil learns that law's reach extends to the hardest place of all.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
