# International Humanitarian Law — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** International humanitarian law binds even war: it exists to limit the effects of wars — protecting those who do not fight and restraining how wars are fought — and committing to it is a duty the book teaches.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a child's own home, school or community in Bahrain.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Bahrain and lived there.
