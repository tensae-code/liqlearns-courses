# International Humanitarian Law — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** International humanitarian law binds even war: it exists to limit the effects of wars — protecting those who do not fight and restraining how wars are fought — and committing to it is a duty the book teaches.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Humanitarian law is summarised here from the book's teaching; no legal text is transcribed.
