# Media and Globalisation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** In the age of globalisation the media reaches every citizen everywhere, changed utterly in speed and reach — and the citizen needs vigilance and insight to meet it without being swept away.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Vigilance means asking who sent a message and what it wants, before believing it.
