# Media and Globalisation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) In the age of globalisation the media reaches every citizen everywhere, changed utterly in speed and reach — and the citizen needs vigilance and insight to meet it without being swept away.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Vigilance means asking who sent a message and what it wants, before believing it.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
