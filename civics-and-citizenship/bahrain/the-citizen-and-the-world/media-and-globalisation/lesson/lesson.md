# Media and Globalisation

## Do it first

For one day, note three messages the media brings you from outside Bahrain. Beside each, write: who sent it, and what it wants me to think or buy. Keep your notes; the lesson teaches the vigilance behind them

## What the book teaches

Globalisation binds the world into one flow of goods, news and images, and the media is its voice in every home. The book teaches how media has changed in the age of globalisation — faster, wider, and present everywhere.

## The lesson in full

Against that flood the citizen's tools are vigilance and insight: asking who speaks, what they want, and whether it is true, before believing or passing it on. The lesson forms a reader and watcher, not a sponge.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: in the age of globalisation the media reaches every citizen everywhere, changed utterly in speed and reach — and the citizen needs vigilance and insight to meet it without being swept away. Give one real example from your own life in Bahrain, and name the lesson's key words: media, globalisation, vigilance, insight. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Bahrain's own books teach civics by building the citizen from his own society outward — society and the modern state first, then living together, then the citizen facing the world and guarding his homeland. When a lesson asks you to look at your own community, really look: your community is this course's first text.
