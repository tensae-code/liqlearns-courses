# Video script — Media and Globalisation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
In the age of globalisation the media reaches every citizen everywhere, changed utterly in speed and reach — and the citizen needs vigilance and insight to meet it without being swept away.

Globalisation binds the world into one flow of goods, news and images, and the media is its voice in every home. The book teaches how media has changed in the age of globalisation — faster, wider, and present everywhere.

Against that flood the citizen's tools are vigilance and insight: asking who speaks, what they want, and whether it is true, before believing or passing it on. The lesson forms a reader and watcher, not a sponge.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
