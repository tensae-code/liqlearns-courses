# Hands-on — Media and Globalisation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
For one day, note three messages the media brings you from outside Bahrain. Beside each, write: who sent it, and what it wants me to think or buy. Keep your notes; the lesson teaches the vigilance behind them

## Show it with real things
Take this lesson into one real act this week: do, make, visit, ask or help in a way that puts Media and Globalisation into practice in your own community in Bahrain — then write what happened and what the lesson would say about it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
