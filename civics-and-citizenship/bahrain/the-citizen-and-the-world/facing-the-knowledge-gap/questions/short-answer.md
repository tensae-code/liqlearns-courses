# Facing the Knowledge Gap — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** The citizen faces the world's challenges, first among them the scientific and technological gap between countries — the distance between those who produce knowledge and those who only consume it — and his answer is learning.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** The gap is measured in who produces knowledge, not only in who owns devices.
