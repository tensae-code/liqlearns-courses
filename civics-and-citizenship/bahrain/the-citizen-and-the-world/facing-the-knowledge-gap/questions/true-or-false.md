# Facing the Knowledge Gap — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The citizen faces the world's challenges, first among them the scientific and technological gap between countries — the distance between those who produce knowledge and those who only consume it — and his answer is learning.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a child's own home, school or community in Bahrain.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Bahrain and lived there.
