# Facing the Knowledge Gap — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) The citizen faces the world's challenges, first among them the scientific and technological gap between countries — the distance between those who produce knowledge and those who only consume it — and his answer is learning.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
