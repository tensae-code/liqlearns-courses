# Video script — Facing the Knowledge Gap

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The citizen faces the world's challenges, first among them the scientific and technological gap between countries — the distance between those who produce knowledge and those who only consume it — and his answer is learning.

Countries are unequal in knowledge: some produce science and technology, others depend on them. That scientific and technological gap is the first world challenge the book sets before the citizen.

The citizen's answer is not complaint but learning: a generation that studies science and masters technology narrows the gap for its country. The challenge is set before the pupil because he is the one who will answer it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
