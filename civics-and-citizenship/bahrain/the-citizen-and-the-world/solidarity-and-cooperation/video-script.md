# Video script — Solidarity and International Cooperation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Peoples and states practise solidarity and cooperation across borders — standing with others in hardship and working together on shared problems — and they have built mechanisms to carry that work.

Solidarity is standing with others in their hardship as if it touched you; international cooperation is states working together on what none can do alone. The book teaches both as the world's way of answering shared problems.

It also teaches the mechanisms they created — the bodies and agreements through which solidarity and cooperation actually happen — so the pupil sees goodwill given an organised form.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
