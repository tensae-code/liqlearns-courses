# Solidarity and International Cooperation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Peoples and states practise solidarity and cooperation across borders — standing with others in hardship and working together on shared problems — and they have built mechanisms to carry that work.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Solidarity is a feeling; cooperation is its organised form, with mechanisms of its own.
