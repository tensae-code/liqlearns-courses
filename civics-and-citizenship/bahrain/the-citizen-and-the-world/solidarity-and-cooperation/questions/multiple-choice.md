# Solidarity and International Cooperation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) Peoples and states practise solidarity and cooperation across borders — standing with others in hardship and working together on shared problems — and they have built mechanisms to carry that work.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Solidarity is a feeling; cooperation is its organised form, with mechanisms of its own.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
