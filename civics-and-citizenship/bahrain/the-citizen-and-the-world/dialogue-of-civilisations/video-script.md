# Video script — Dialogue of Civilisations

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Civilisations meet best in dialogue — knowing each other, speaking and listening across difference — whose importance and conditions the book teaches as the way to prevent misunderstanding and conflict.

Civilisations are the great cultures of peoples, and they meet whether they choose to or not. Dialogue of civilisations is their meeting by speech and listening instead of by prejudice and clash.

The book teaches its importance and its conditions — real knowledge of the other, respect, and honest speech — because dialogue done properly prevents the misunderstanding from which conflicts grow. The Grade 8 lesson on dialogue returns here at the scale of the world.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
