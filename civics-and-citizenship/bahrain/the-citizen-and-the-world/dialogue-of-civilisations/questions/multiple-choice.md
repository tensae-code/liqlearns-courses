# Dialogue of Civilisations — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) Civilisations meet best in dialogue — knowing each other, speaking and listening across difference — whose importance and conditions the book teaches as the way to prevent misunderstanding and conflict.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Dialogue at world scale keeps the same conditions as dialogue at home: knowledge, respect, honest speech.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
