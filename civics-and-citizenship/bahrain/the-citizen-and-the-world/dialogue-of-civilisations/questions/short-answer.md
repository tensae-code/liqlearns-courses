# Dialogue of Civilisations — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Civilisations meet best in dialogue — knowing each other, speaking and listening across difference — whose importance and conditions the book teaches as the way to prevent misunderstanding and conflict.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Dialogue at world scale keeps the same conditions as dialogue at home: knowledge, respect, honest speech.
