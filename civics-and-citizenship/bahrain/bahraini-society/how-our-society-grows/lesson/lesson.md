# How Our Society Grows

## Do it first

Ask an older person in your family how your town or neighbourhood in Bahrain has changed in their lifetime. Write down two changes. Keep them; the lesson places them

## What the book teaches

Societies are not frozen. Bahraini society developed step by step — in how its people live, work, learn and gather — and each generation inherits a society shaped by the one before it.

## The lesson in full

The lesson follows that development in the Kingdom of Bahrain so the pupil sees his own society as something alive and growing, whose next chapter his own generation will write.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Bahraini society has developed over time — it grew and changed through the Kingdom's history, and the society of today is the result of that development. Give one real example from your own life in Bahrain, and name the lesson's key words: development, change, generations, growth. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Bahrain's own books teach civics by building the citizen from his own society outward — society and the modern state first, then living together, then the citizen facing the world and guarding his homeland. When a lesson asks you to look at your own community, really look: your community is this course's first text.
