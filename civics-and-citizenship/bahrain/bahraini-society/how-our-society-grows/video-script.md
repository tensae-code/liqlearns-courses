# Video script — How Our Society Grows

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Bahraini society has developed over time — it grew and changed through the kingdom's history, and the society of today is the result of that development.

Societies are not frozen. Bahraini society developed step by step — in how its people live, work, learn and gather — and each generation inherits a society shaped by the one before it.

The lesson follows that development in the Kingdom of Bahrain so the pupil sees his own society as something alive and growing, whose next chapter his own generation will write.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
