# How Our Society Grows — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Bahraini society has developed over time — it grew and changed through the kingdom's history, and the society of today is the result of that development.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Development is change over time in the whole society, not in one person alone.
