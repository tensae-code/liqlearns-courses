# Video script — The Elements of Our Society

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Bahraini society is made of elements that can be named — its people and groups, its land and institutions, its shared life — and it has features that mark it as this society and no other.

A society is not a crowd. It is people bound together by a shared life on one land, and Bahraini society has elements a citizen can name: who its people are, the families and groups they form, the institutions that serve them, and the land and sea they share.

The lesson also names the society's features — the marks that distinguish it. To know your society's elements and features is the citizen's first knowledge: you cannot serve a society you cannot describe.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
