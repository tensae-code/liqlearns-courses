# The Elements of Our Society — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — Bahraini society is made of elements that can be named — its people and groups, its land and institutions, its shared life — and it has features that mark it as this society and no other. — in their own words, give one real example from their own life in Bahrain, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or community situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
