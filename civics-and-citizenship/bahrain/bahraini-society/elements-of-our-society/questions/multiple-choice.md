# The Elements of Our Society — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) Bahraini society is made of elements that can be named — its people and groups, its land and institutions, its shared life — and it has features that mark it as this society and no other.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Bahraini society is examined first, before the state, law or the world — the pupil's own society is the textbook's first text.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
