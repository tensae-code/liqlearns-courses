# The Elements of Our Society — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Bahraini society is made of elements that can be named — its people and groups, its land and institutions, its shared life — and it has features that mark it as this society and no other.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Bahraini society is examined first, before the state, law or the world — the pupil's own society is the textbook's first text.
