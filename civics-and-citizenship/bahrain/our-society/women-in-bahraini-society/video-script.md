# Video script — Women in Bahraini Society

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Women hold a real and honoured role in bahraini society — in the family, in work, in learning and in public life — and the book teaches that role as part of the society's own strength.

Bahraini society is built by its women and men together. The book teaches women's role across the society: raising and teaching the family, working and building, learning and leading.

The pupil learns to see that role plainly and honour it: a society that recognises what its women do understands its own strength, and a citizen respects the people who carry half his world.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
