# Women in Bahraini Society

## Do it first

Name three roles women carry in your family and community in Bahrain. Keep your list; the lesson sets it in the whole society

## What the book teaches

Bahraini society is built by its women and men together. The book teaches women's role across the society: raising and teaching the family, working and building, learning and leading.

## The lesson in full

The pupil learns to see that role plainly and honour it: a society that recognises what its women do understands its own strength, and a citizen respects the people who carry half his world.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: women hold a real and honoured role in Bahraini society — in the family, in work, in learning and in public life — and the book teaches that role as part of the society's own strength. Give one real example from your own life in Bahrain, and name the lesson's key words: women, society, role, family. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Bahrain's own books teach civics by building the citizen from his own society outward — society and the modern state first, then living together, then the citizen facing the world and guarding his homeland. When a lesson asks you to look at your own community, really look: your community is this course's first text.
