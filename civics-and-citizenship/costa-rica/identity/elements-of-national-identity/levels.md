# Elements of National Identity — depth

This lesson is ONE lesson with no sub lessons: it teaches a single thing, whole. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: The Costa Rican nationality and the national symbols one by one — flag, coat of arms, anthem, guaria morada, Guanacaste tree, carreta típica, marimba, yigüirro, white-tailed deer, torch, Los Crestones, manatee, pre-Columbian spheres, National Theatre, coffee and more. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
