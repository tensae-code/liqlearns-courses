# The Community

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: The Community. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Costa Rica's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Characteristics and types of communities (rural and urban), urban-space problems, elements for studying a community, its historical origin and its geography and economic activities.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Characteristics and types of communities (rural and urban), urban-space problems, elements for studying a community, its historical origin and its geography and economic activities. Give one real example from your own life or from the Costa Rica you live in, and name the lesson's key words: community. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
