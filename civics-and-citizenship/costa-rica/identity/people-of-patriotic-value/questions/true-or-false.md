# People of Patriotic Value — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Men and women of patriotic value, including Pablo Presbere and Juan Rafael Mora Porras.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that People of Patriotic Value — and that it has no connection to the rest of Costa Rica's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Costa Rica's own course, in its own order.
