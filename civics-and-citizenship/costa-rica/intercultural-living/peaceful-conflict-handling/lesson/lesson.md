# Handling Conflicts Peacefully

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Handling Conflicts Peacefully. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Costa Rica's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Peaceful handling of conflicts in an intercultural society, diversity in cultural and artistic expression, and discrimination in the institutional sphere.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Peaceful handling of conflicts in an intercultural society, diversity in cultural and artistic expression, and discrimination in the institutional sphere. Give one real example from your own life or from the Costa Rica you live in, and name the lesson's key words: handling, conflicts, peacefully. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
