# What Road Safety Is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) What road safety is and its characteristics.  b) it has nothing to do with how Costa Rica governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) What road safety is and its characteristics.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) What road safety is and its characteristics.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Costa Rica
**Answer: a) What road safety is and its characteristics.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
