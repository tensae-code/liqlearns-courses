Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Communal institutions and organisations, communal organisation as a safe environment, and safety in educational institutions.  b) it has nothing to do with how Costa Rica governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
