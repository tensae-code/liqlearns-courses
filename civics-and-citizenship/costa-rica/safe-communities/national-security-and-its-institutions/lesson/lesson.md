# National Security and Its Institutions

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: National Security and Its Institutions. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Costa Rica's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

The institutions responsible for national security, the role of the mass media, causes and consequences of insecurity, and the Juvenile Criminal Justice Law.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The institutions responsible for national security, the role of the mass media, causes and consequences of insecurity, and the Juvenile Criminal Justice Law. Give one real example from your own life or from the Costa Rica you live in, and name the lesson's key words: national, security, institutions. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
