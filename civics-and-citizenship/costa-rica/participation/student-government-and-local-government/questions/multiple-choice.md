# Student Government and Local Government — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The contrasts and similarities between student government and local government.  b) it has nothing to do with how Costa Rica governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The contrasts and similarities between student government and local government.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) The contrasts and similarities between student government and local government.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Costa Rica
**Answer: a) The contrasts and similarities between student government and local government.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
