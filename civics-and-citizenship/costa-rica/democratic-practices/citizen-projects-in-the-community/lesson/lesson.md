# Citizen Projects in the Community

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Citizen Projects in the Community. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Costa Rica's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Turning democratic attitudes into projects the young citizen can actually carry out in their community.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Turning democratic attitudes into projects the young citizen can actually carry out in their community. Give one real example from your own life or from the Costa Rica you live in, and name the lesson's key words: citizen, projects, community. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
