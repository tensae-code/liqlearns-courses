# Why Democracy Matters

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Why Democracy Matters. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Costa Rica's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Democracy's importance for society's development, and democracy against dictatorship.
