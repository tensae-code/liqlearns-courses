# Social Christianity and Fascism

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Social Christianity and Fascism. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Costa Rica's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## Sub lesson 1 — Social Christianity

Social Christianity — solidarity, human dignity, preference for the poorest — as the book teaches it.

## Sub lesson 2 — Fascism

Fascism — authoritarian nationalism, the state over the individual — as the book teaches it.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Social Christianity (solidarity, human dignity, preference for the poorest) and fascism (authoritarian nationalism, the state over the individual) as contrasting ideologies. Give one real example from your own life or from the Costa Rica you live in, and name the lesson's key words: social, christianity, fascism. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
