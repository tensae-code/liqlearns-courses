# Education Policy — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Education policy goals — quality for all, universal coverage, financing and better schools.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that Education Policy — and that it has no connection to the rest of Costa Rica's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Costa Rica's own course, in its own order.
