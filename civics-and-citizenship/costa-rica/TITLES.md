# Civics and Citizenship — Costa Rica course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

Civics belongs to the country: this course is Costa Rica's
own — built from Costa Rica's *Educación Cívica* student
books for Grade 7 (sétimo año), Grade 8 (octavo año) and
Grade 9 (noveno año) and the shared *Nuevo material de
Educación Cívica* for Grades 10–11 (décimo y undécimo),
all in Spanish, all from CONED / Universidad Estatal a
Distancia (UNED). Inside the course the organisation is by
**levels, not grades**: the titles below ARE the levels'
titles, in path order — what the learner proves, step by
step. The grades the books come from live only in each
lesson's sources.

The path's own logic is Costa Rica's, from the filing's
ladder sketch: the CONED series' own widening circle —
safety → identity → rights and participation → the
political system → the citizen practising democracy. So
Level 1 (Grade 7) begins as the safety of the places the
young citizen actually lives: community, road and
emergency. Level 2 (Grade 8) turns to who I am (and who
Costa Rica is, symbol by symbol), gender rebuilt for
equity under the law, and living with other cultures in
one society. Level 3 (Grade 9) practises participation in
student and local government, teaches rights in three
generations with Costa Rican protection, and defends
democracy by horizontal and vertical control. Level 4
(the Décimo año chapters of the shared book) measures the
world's regimes and ideologies against Costa Rica's own
institutions, electoral system and parties. Level 5 (the
Undécimo año chapters) closes with the state guaranteeing
equal opportunity in health, education and work, and the
young citizen's own democratic attitudes, leadership,
deliberation and negotiation. That ladder IS the path
order (recommended, never forced — a learner can jump in
higher; the "start here instead" hints point down this
ladder).

No unit folder is reused across levels in this course:
each of the fourteen titles sits at exactly one level,
and each lesson's own level is stated in its lesson plan
and levels file. No coined lesson name is used twice in
the filing, so the dedupe changed nothing: 77 placements
stock 77 lessons (see ABOUT.md).
