Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?

**Answer: a) The laws in force for gender equity — the convention eliminating discrimination against women, the Political Constitution, the Childhood and Adolescence Code (Law 7739) and the Law Penalising Violence against Women.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
