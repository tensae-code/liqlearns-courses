# Laws for Gender Equity and Equality — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** The laws in force for gender equity — the convention eliminating discrimination against women, the Political Constitution, the Childhood and Adolescence Code (Law 7739) and the Law Penalising Violence against Women.
