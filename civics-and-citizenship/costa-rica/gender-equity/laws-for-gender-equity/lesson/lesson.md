# Laws for Gender Equity and Equality

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Laws for Gender Equity and Equality. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Costa Rica's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

The laws in force for gender equity — the convention eliminating discrimination against women, the Political Constitution, the Childhood and Adolescence Code (Law 7739) and the Law Penalising Violence against Women.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The laws in force for gender equity — the convention eliminating discrimination against women, the Political Constitution, the Childhood and Adolescence Code (Law 7739) and the Law Penalising Violence against Women. Give one real example from your own life or from the Costa Rica you live in, and name the lesson's key words: laws, for, gender. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
