# Everyday Life and Co-Responsibility

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Everyday Life and Co-Responsibility. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Costa Rica's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Daily relations within and between genders — life as a couple, co-responsibility in raising children, mixed projects, and stereotypes, sexism and discrimination in everyday life.
