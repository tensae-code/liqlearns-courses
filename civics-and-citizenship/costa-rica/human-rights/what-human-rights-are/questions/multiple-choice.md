# What Human Rights Are — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The concept and principles of human rights.  b) it has nothing to do with how Costa Rica governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The concept and principles of human rights.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) The concept and principles of human rights.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Costa Rica
**Answer: a) The concept and principles of human rights.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
