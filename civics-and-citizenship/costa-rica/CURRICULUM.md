# Civics and Citizenship — Costa Rica course — CURRICULUM (the path)

One course for Costa Rica inside the Civics and
Citizenship folder, organised by **levels, not grades**: a
learner climbs when they have proved the level, not when a
school calendar says so. The school grades the books come
from appear only in each lesson's sources (provenance),
never as the course's organisation. The path follows Costa
Rica's own order as its books lay it out, from the filing's
ladder sketch (`costa-rica-placements.md` — 4 records / 4
distinct books, all student books, all Spanish: the CONED /
Universidad Estatal a Distancia (UNED) *Educación Cívica*
anthologies for Grade 7 (sétimo año), Grade 8 (octavo año)
and Grade 9 (noveno año), and the shared *Nuevo material de
Educación Cívica* for Grades 10–11 (décimo y undécimo),
2015): the CONED series' own widening circle — safety →
identity → rights and participation → the political system
→ the citizen practising democracy. The filing stocks 77
placements into 77 lessons: the coined lesson names were
deduped before building — every coined name in the filing
was already distinct, no name is used twice, and every
placement stocks into exactly one lesson. Self-assessment
exercises and answer keys in the books are inventory-only,
not placed, as filed; the shared Grades 10–11 book is built
largely as guided readings with exercises, and those
exercises are the teaching vehicle and are placed as such,
as filed.

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in
this course comes from government textbooks — this
country's own Educación Cívica books — except the
**Tip boxes**: a tip in a box is from us, the people
making LiqLearns. Tips are ours; the lessons are the
books'. See ABOUT.md for the full book list and the
acceptance reckoning. **All 77 lessons stand on student
books; no lesson in this course stands on a teacher guide
or a programme.**

## The levels (the path, in order)

1. **Safe Communities, the Road and Emergencies** — The Grade 7 book's three units: safe communities first (what Educación Cívica studies and the citizen's responsibilities, citizen security, national security and its institutions, and building safe environments), then road culture (what road safety is, road safety in Costa Rica, the Traffic Law, responsible drivers and traffic signs, Costa Rica's road system, and peaceful living on the road), and last risk and disasters (what disaster prevention is, natural and human-made events and their risk, the emergency law and the National Emergencies Commission, and risk management in the community and at school). 16 lessons, in 3 title(s): `safe-communities/` (4 lessons); `road-culture/` (6 lessons); `risk-and-disasters/` (6 lessons).
2. **Identity, Gender Equity and Intercultural Living** — The Grade 8 book's three units: identity first (the main identity concepts, the educational institution and its conflict-resolution mechanisms, the community and its communal life and traditions, the elements of national identity symbol by symbol, and people of patriotic value), then gender identity rebuilt for equity (the working concepts, gender identities, the socialising agents, the laws in force for gender equity, everyday life and co-responsibility, and citizenship against sexism), and last intercultural living (the main concepts, the laws on rights and duties, intercultural enrichment, and handling conflicts peacefully). 17 lessons, in 3 title(s): `identity/` (7 lessons); `gender-equity/` (6 lessons); `intercultural-living/` (4 lessons).
3. **Participation, Human Rights and Democracy** — The Grade 9 book's three chapters: participation first (the basic concepts, student elections and other forms of participation at school, student government against local government, the young adult in the community, development associations and groups, and the local citizen participation mechanisms), then human rights (the basic concepts, what human rights are, the Universal Declaration and the three generations, the protecting laws and mechanisms, rights in Costa Rica's own Constitution, and collective rights and the state), and last democracy (the basic concepts, why democracy matters, its Greek and Declaration origins, contemporary democracy in Costa Rica, horizontal and vertical political control, and the intermediary organisations). 21 lessons, in 3 title(s): `participation/` (7 lessons); `human-rights/` (7 lessons); `democracy/` (7 lessons).
4. **Political Regimes, Costa Rican Democracy and Elections** — The shared Grades 10–11 book's first three chapters (the Décimo año chapters): the world's political regimes and ideologies first (what a political regime is, authoritarianism and populism, democracy and its characteristics, liberalism, socialism and anarchism, social Christianity and fascism), then Costa Rica's own democracy (the political system and its key concepts, the public institutions — municipalities and the abolition of the army among them — political communication and agendas, and the challenges and public accountability), and last elections (the electoral system and electoral culture, the elective offices and who may stand, suffrage and the direct mechanisms, political parties, and campaigns, propaganda and government programmes). 14 lessons, in 3 title(s): `political-regimes/` (5 lessons); `costa-rican-democracy/` (4 lessons); `elections/` (5 lessons).
5. **Equal Opportunity and Democratic Practices** — The shared Grades 10–11 book's last two chapters (the Undécimo año chapters): the state as guarantor of equal opportunity first (the state and public policy and their key concepts, health policy — La Caja and the EBAIS clinics — education policy, work and employment policy, and the state's challenges of poverty and school dropout), and last the young citizen's own democratic practices (democratic values against anti-values, citizen projects in the community, democratic leadership, and deliberation and negotiation, closing the book). 9 lessons, in 2 title(s): `equal-opportunity/` (5 lessons); `democratic-practices/` (4 lessons).

**Totals: 77 lessons** (Level 1: 16 · Level 2: 17 · Level 3: 21 · Level 4: 14 · Level 5: 9 — from four read student books: *Educación cívica — sétimo año*, *Educación Cívica — Octavo año*, *Educación cívica — noveno año* and *Nuevo material de Educación Cívica — Niveles décimo y undécimo* (CONED / Universidad Estatal a Distancia (UNED), Spanish, no ISBN printed in any book, years not printed on the title pages of the first three and 2015 for the fourth, all COMPLETE as filed); 77 placements stocking 77 lessons).
