# Lesson plan — Challenges and Public Accountability

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Costa Rica · **Title:** Costa Rican Democracy · **Lesson:** Challenges and Public Accountability
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Political Regimes, Costa Rican Democracy and Elections (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The challenges of Costa Rica's institutional organisation, the Constitution as the system's base, and the mechanisms of public accountability (rendición de cuentas). This lesson stands on a student book.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Before this lesson
- Stands in path order after **Political Communication and Agendas** — do that lesson first if this one feels hard (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: The challenges of Costa Rica's institutional organisation, the Constitution as the system's base, and the mechanisms of public accountability (rendición de cuentas).
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, school or town — or, for this book's lessons, from the public life of Costa Rica they live in.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the learner's own life in Costa Rica and the book's own teaching, as the book itself works.
