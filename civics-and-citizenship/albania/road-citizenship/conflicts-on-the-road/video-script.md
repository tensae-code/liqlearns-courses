# Video script — Conflicts on the Road

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Conflicts happen on the road too — between travellers in a hurry and in each other's way — and they are resolved without harm: by calm, by rules, and by giving way where giving way is right.

The road squeezes strangers together at speed: the blocked pavement, the disputed crossing, the near miss. Tempers rise fastest exactly where calm matters most.

The lesson carries the community's conflict teaching onto the road: stop, stay calm, let the rules and courtesy decide, and give way where giving way prevents harm. No journey is worth an injury — and no traveller wins a fight with the road.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
