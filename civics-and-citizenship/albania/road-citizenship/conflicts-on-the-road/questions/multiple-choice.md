# Conflicts on the Road — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) conflicts happen on the road too — between travellers in a hurry and in each other's way — and they are resolved without harm: by calm, by rules, and by giving way where giving way is right  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Road conflicts are taught as the community's conflict lesson in a new place — the same peaceful solving, under the road's extra danger of speed and harm.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
