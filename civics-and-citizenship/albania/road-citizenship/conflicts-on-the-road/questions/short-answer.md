# Conflicts on the Road — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Conflicts happen on the road too — between travellers in a hurry and in each other's way — and they are resolved without harm: by calm, by rules, and by giving way where giving way is right.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Road conflicts are taught as the community's conflict lesson in a new place — the same peaceful solving, under the road's extra danger of speed and harm.
