# Means of Transport

## Do it first

Name every means of transport you have used this month. For each, say what it cost, what it gave, and what it asked of the street.

## Choosing how to move

People move in many ways: on foot, by bicycle, by bus and shared transport, by car. The lesson lays the means side by side — what each costs, what each gives, what each asks of the traveller and of the place.

Choosing is part of citizenship: the short walk needs no vehicle at all; the bus carries many with one engine; every choice touches the street, the air and the other travellers. A citizen chooses with eyes open.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the means of transport — walking, bicycle, bus, car and more — are the citizen's choices for moving, each with its own costs, benefits and duties. Give one real example from your own life in Albania, and name the lesson's key words: transport, choice, bus, walking. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Albania's own book teaches civics by doing — family decisions, class rules, a mock campaign, a kiosk game. When a lesson asks you to practise something, really practise it with real people, even in a small way: the practising is the teaching.
