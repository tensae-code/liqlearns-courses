# Video script — Means of Transport

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The means of transport — walking, bicycle, bus, car and more — are the citizen's choices for moving, each with its own costs, benefits and duties.

People move in many ways: on foot, by bicycle, by bus and shared transport, by car. The lesson lays the means side by side — what each costs, what each gives, what each asks of the traveller and of the place.

Choosing is part of citizenship: the short walk needs no vehicle at all; the bus carries many with one engine; every choice touches the street, the air and the other travellers. A citizen chooses with eyes open.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
