# Means of Transport — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** The means of transport — walking, bicycle, bus, car and more — are the citizen's choices for moving, each with its own costs, benefits and duties.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Means of transport are taught here as choices a citizen makes — comparing costs and effects — not as a list of vehicles to name.
