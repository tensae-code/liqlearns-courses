# Means of Transport — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) the means of transport — walking, bicycle, bus, car and more — are the citizen's choices for moving, each with its own costs, benefits and duties  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Means of transport are taught here as choices a citizen makes — comparing costs and effects — not as a list of vehicles to name.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
