# Means of Transport — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The means of transport — walking, bicycle, bus, car and more — are the citizen's choices for moving, each with its own costs, benefits and duties.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a child's own home, school or community in Albania.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Albania and lived there.
