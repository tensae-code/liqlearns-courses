# Risk and Information on the Road — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** The road speaks through signs and information — reading them, and judging risk before it reaches you, is how a citizen stays safe in public space.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Risk and information close the road chapter together: judging danger early, and reading the signs and signals that carry the road's information.
