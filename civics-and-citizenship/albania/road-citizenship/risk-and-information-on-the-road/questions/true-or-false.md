# Risk and Information on the Road — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The road speaks through signs and information — reading them, and judging risk before it reaches you, is how a citizen stays safe in public space.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a child's own home, school or community in Albania.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Albania and lived there.
