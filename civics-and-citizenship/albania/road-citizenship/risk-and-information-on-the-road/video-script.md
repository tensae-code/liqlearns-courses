# Video script — Risk and Information on the Road

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The road speaks through signs and information — reading them, and judging risk before it reaches you, is how a citizen stays safe in public space.

Risk is danger before it happens: the fast corner, the hidden crossing, the place where vehicles turn. The citizen on the road learns to see risk early, while there is still time to choose.

Information is the road's own language: signs, signals, markings and lights that warn, direct and protect. Reading them is a skill like reading words — the chapter closes by making the child a reader of the road itself.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
