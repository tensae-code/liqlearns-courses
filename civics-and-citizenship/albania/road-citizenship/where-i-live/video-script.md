# Video script — Where I Live

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The place where you live — its streets, spaces and shared rules — is your first public world, and a citizen knows it, uses it well and cares for it.

The road chapter opens at home: the place where you live. Its streets and pavements, its crossings and corners, its shared spaces — this is the ground where the child is first a citizen in public.

Knowing your place is the start: which streets lead where, where it is safe to cross, which spaces are shared and what rules keep them usable for everyone. The citizen of the road begins as a knower of their own ground.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
