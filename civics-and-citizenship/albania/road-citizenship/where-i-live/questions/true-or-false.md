# Where I Live — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The place where you live — its streets, spaces and shared rules — is your first public world, and a citizen knows it, uses it well and cares for it.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a child's own home, school or community in Albania.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Albania and lived there.
