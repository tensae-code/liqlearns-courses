# Where I Live — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) the place where you live — its streets, spaces and shared rules — is your first public world, and a citizen knows it, uses it well and cares for it  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Road citizenship is taught here starting from knowledge of your own place — its streets, spaces and shared rules — before any journey is made.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
