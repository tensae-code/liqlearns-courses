# Where I Live — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** The place where you live — its streets, spaces and shared rules — is your first public world, and a citizen knows it, uses it well and cares for it.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Road citizenship is taught here starting from knowledge of your own place — its streets, spaces and shared rules — before any journey is made.
