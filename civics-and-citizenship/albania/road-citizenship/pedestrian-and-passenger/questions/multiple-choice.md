# Pedestrian and Passenger — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) as a pedestrian and as a passenger you hold two roles on the road, and each role has its own rights and its own duties  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Pedestrian and passenger are taught as two roles of one citizen — each with rights and duties of its own — because the child lives both every week.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
