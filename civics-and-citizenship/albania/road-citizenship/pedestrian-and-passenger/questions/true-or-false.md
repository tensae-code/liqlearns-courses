# Pedestrian and Passenger — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** As a pedestrian and as a passenger you hold two roles on the road, and each role has its own rights and its own duties.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a child's own home, school or community in Albania.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Albania and lived there.
