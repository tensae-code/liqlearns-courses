# Pedestrian and Passenger — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** As a pedestrian and as a passenger you hold two roles on the road, and each role has its own rights and its own duties.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Pedestrian and passenger are taught as two roles of one citizen — each with rights and duties of its own — because the child lives both every week.
