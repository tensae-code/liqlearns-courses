# Video script — On the Road to School

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The road to school is the child's daily journey through public space — to be travelled safely and considerately, as a citizen among other citizens.

Almost every school day begins with a journey: on foot, by bus, by bicycle, with family. The lesson treats that journey as citizenship in motion — you share the road with everyone else travelling theirs.

Safely means knowing the dangers of your own route and meeting them prepared; considerately means your journey does not become someone else's danger — no pushing at crossings, no blocking pavements, care at every corner.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
