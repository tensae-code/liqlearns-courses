# On the Road to School

## Do it first

Describe your road to school step by step: where you cross, where you wait, where the danger points are. Say what you do at each danger point.

## The daily journey

Almost every school day begins with a journey: on foot, by bus, by bicycle, with family. The lesson treats that journey as citizenship in motion — you share the road with everyone else travelling theirs.

Safely means knowing the dangers of your own route and meeting them prepared; considerately means your journey does not become someone else's danger — no pushing at crossings, no blocking pavements, care at every corner.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the road to school is the child's daily journey through public space — to be travelled safely and considerately, as a citizen among other citizens. Give one real example from your own life in Albania, and name the lesson's key words: road, school, safety, journey. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Albania's own book teaches civics by doing — family decisions, class rules, a mock campaign, a kiosk game. When a lesson asks you to practise something, really practise it with real people, even in a small way: the practising is the teaching.
