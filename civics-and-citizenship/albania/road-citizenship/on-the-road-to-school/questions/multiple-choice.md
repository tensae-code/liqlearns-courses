# On the Road to School — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) the road to school is the child's daily journey through public space — to be travelled safely and considerately, as a citizen among other citizens  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) The road to school is taught as the child's own daily piece of public space — the route where road citizenship is practised every day, not on special occasions.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
