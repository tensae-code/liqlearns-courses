# On the Road to School — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The road to school is the child's daily journey through public space — to be travelled safely and considerately, as a citizen among other citizens.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a child's own home, school or community in Albania.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Albania and lived there.
