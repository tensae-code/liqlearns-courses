# On the Road to School — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** The road to school is the child's daily journey through public space — to be travelled safely and considerately, as a citizen among other citizens.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** The road to school is taught as the child's own daily piece of public space — the route where road citizenship is practised every day, not on special occasions.
