# Video script — Power and Responsibility

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Power and responsibility belong together — power is the ability to decide and act for others, and it is only right when it carries its responsibility with it.

Power means your decisions reach other people: a leader's choice changes the group's day. The lesson teaches that this reach is exactly why power must carry responsibility — an answer owed for what the power does.

Power without responsibility is the danger the chapter warns against at the child's scale: the class leader who favours friends, the older child who orders and never explains. Where power answers for itself, people can trust it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
