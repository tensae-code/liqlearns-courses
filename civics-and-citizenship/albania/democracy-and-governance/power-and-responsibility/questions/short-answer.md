# Power and Responsibility — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Power and responsibility belong together — power is the ability to decide and act for others, and it is only right when it carries its responsibility with it.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Power is taught here as inseparable from responsibility — the pair is the lesson; power alone, unanswerable, is named as the wrong thing.
