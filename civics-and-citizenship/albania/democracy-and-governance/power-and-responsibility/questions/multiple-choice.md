# Power and Responsibility — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) power and responsibility belong together — power is the ability to decide and act for others, and it is only right when it carries its responsibility with it  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Power is taught here as inseparable from responsibility — the pair is the lesson; power alone, unanswerable, is named as the wrong thing.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
