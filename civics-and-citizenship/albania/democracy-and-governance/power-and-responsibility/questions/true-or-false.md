# Power and Responsibility — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Power and responsibility belong together — power is the ability to decide and act for others, and it is only right when it carries its responsibility with it.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a child's own home, school or community in Albania.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Albania and lived there.
