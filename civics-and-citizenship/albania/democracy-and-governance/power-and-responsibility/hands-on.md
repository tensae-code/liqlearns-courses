# Hands-on — Power and Responsibility

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name someone with a little power over your day — a class leader, an older sibling, a team captain. Say one decision they made and the answer they owed for it.

## Show it with real things
Take a small power for one task today (leading a game, dividing a shared snack fairly) and afterwards give your account: what you decided, why, and who it affected. Let the group judge.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
