# Local Self-Government

## Do it first

Name the local unit you live in, if you know it. Name two things it decides that touch your family's week.

## Governing your own place

Not every decision should travel far away. Local self-government means the community's own affairs — its streets, services and local plans — are decided by the place's own chosen bodies, close to the people they affect.

Albania is organised in local units, and the lesson teaches what they are for: decisions taken near home, by people the community knows and can question, about the things the community knows best.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: local self-government means the people of a place govern their own local affairs through their own local units — deciding near home, about the things nearest home. Give one real example from your own life in Albania, and name the lesson's key words: local self-government, local unit, community, decisions. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Albania's own book teaches civics by doing — family decisions, class rules, a mock campaign, a kiosk game. When a lesson asks you to practise something, really practise it with real people, even in a small way: the practising is the teaching.
