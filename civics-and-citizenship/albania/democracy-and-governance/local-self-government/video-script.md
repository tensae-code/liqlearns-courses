# Video script — Local Self-Government

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Local self-government means the people of a place govern their own local affairs through their own local units — deciding near home, about the things nearest home.

Not every decision should travel far away. Local self-government means the community's own affairs — its streets, services and local plans — are decided by the place's own chosen bodies, close to the people they affect.

Albania is organised in local units, and the lesson teaches what they are for: decisions taken near home, by people the community knows and can question, about the things the community knows best.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
