# Local Self-Government — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Local self-government means the people of a place govern their own local affairs through their own local units — deciding near home, about the things nearest home.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a child's own home, school or community in Albania.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Albania and lived there.
