# Local Self-Government — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Local self-government means the people of a place govern their own local affairs through their own local units — deciding near home, about the things nearest home.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Local self-government is taught here as government by the place itself — its own units deciding its own affairs — the step between community governance and national elections.
