# Local Self-Government — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) local self-government means the people of a place govern their own local affairs through their own local units — deciding near home, about the things nearest home  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Local self-government is taught here as government by the place itself — its own units deciding its own affairs — the step between community governance and national elections.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
