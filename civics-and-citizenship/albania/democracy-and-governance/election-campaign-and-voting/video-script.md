# Video script — Election Campaign and Voting

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
An election campaign is how candidates present themselves and their plans so voters can choose — and voting is the act that turns the choice into a decision.

Before anyone votes, there is a campaign: candidates say who they are, what they promise, and why they should be chosen. Voters listen, compare and question — the campaign is the choosing made public.

Then comes voting: each voter marks their choice, the votes are counted, and the result stands as the people's decision. The book prepares this as a class activity, because a campaign and a vote are learned by doing them.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
