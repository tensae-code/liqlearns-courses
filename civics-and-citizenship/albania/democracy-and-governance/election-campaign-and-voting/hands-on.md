# Hands-on — Election Campaign and Voting

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Imagine your class must choose a representative. Say what a fair campaign would need: time to speak, promises stated, questions allowed. Then say what the vote itself needs to be fair.

## Show it with real things
Run a mini-campaign in your group: two candidates present a small plan, the group questions them, then everyone votes and the votes are counted aloud. Note what made it feel fair or unfair.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
