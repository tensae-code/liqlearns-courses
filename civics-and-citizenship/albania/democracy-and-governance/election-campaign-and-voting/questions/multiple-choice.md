# Election Campaign and Voting — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) an election campaign is how candidates present themselves and their plans so voters can choose — and voting is the act that turns the choice into a decision  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) The campaign and the vote are taught as one lesson in two acts — presenting and questioning first, then the counted choice — prepared as a class activity.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
