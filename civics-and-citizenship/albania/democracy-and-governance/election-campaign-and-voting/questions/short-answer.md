# Election Campaign and Voting — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** An election campaign is how candidates present themselves and their plans so voters can choose — and voting is the act that turns the choice into a decision.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** The campaign and the vote are taught as one lesson in two acts — presenting and questioning first, then the counted choice — prepared as a class activity.
