# Elections and Representation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Elections are how the voters' choice becomes representation — the winners carry the people's voice into the bodies that decide, and answer for it.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Representation is taught here as what an election produces — a person carrying the voters' voice into the deciding bodies — not as a prize the winner keeps.
