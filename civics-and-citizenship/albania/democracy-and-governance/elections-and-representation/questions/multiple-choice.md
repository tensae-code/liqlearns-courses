# Elections and Representation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) elections are how the voters' choice becomes representation — the winners carry the people's voice into the bodies that decide, and answer for it  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Representation is taught here as what an election produces — a person carrying the voters' voice into the deciding bodies — not as a prize the winner keeps.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
