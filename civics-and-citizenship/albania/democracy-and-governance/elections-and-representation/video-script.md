# Video script — Elections and Representation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Elections are how the voters' choice becomes representation — the winners carry the people's voice into the bodies that decide, and answer for it.

A community is too big for everyone to sit in every decision. So voters choose representatives: people who sit in the deciding bodies in the voters' place, carrying their interests and views.

Representation is the bridge the election builds. It holds only while the representative truly carries the voters' voice — and the next election is where the voters judge whether they did.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
