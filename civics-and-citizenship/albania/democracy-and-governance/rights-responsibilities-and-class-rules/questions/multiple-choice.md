# Rights, Responsibilities and Class Rules — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) in the classroom — the child's first public world — rights come with responsibilities, and class rules are the first law: they protect everyone's rights equally  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Class rules are taught here as the child's first law — agreed rules that protect rights — joining rights and responsibilities into one lesson.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
