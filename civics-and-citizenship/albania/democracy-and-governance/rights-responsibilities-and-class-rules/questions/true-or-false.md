# Rights, Responsibilities and Class Rules — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** In the classroom — the child's first public world — rights come with responsibilities, and class rules are the first law: they protect everyone's rights equally.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a child's own home, school or community in Albania.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Albania and lived there.
