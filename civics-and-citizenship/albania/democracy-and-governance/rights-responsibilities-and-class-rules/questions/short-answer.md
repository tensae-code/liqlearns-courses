# Rights, Responsibilities and Class Rules — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** In the classroom — the child's first public world — rights come with responsibilities, and class rules are the first law: they protect everyone's rights equally.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Class rules are taught here as the child's first law — agreed rules that protect rights — joining rights and responsibilities into one lesson.
