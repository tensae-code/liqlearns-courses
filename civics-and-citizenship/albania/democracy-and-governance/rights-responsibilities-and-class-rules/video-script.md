# Video script — Rights, Responsibilities and Class Rules

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
In the classroom — the child's first public world — rights come with responsibilities, and class rules are the first law: they protect everyone's rights equally.

A right is what you may claim: to learn, to speak, to be safe, to be treated fairly. A responsibility is what the same world asks back: to let others learn, speak, be safe and be treated fairly too.

Class rules are where this becomes real. Agreed and kept by the class, they are the child's first law — not the teacher's weapon, but the class's own protection of everyone's rights, applying the same to all.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
