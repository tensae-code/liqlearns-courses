# Rights, Responsibilities and Class Rules — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — in the classroom — the child's first public world — rights come with responsibilities, and class rules are the first law: they protect everyone's rights equally — in their own words, give one real example from their own life in Albania, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or community situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
