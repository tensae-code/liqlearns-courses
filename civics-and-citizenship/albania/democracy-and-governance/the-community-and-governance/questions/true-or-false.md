# The Community and Governance — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Governance is how a community is organised and led — who decides, by what right, and for whose good — and every community, from class to country, has some form of it.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a child's own home, school or community in Albania.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Albania and lived there.
