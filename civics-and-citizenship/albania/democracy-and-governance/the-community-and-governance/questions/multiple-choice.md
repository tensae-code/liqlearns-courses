# The Community and Governance — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) governance is how a community is organised and led — who decides, by what right, and for whose good — and every community, from class to country, has some form of it  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Governance is taught here starting from the child's own community — who decides the shared things there — before any national institutions are named.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
