# The Community and Governance — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Governance is how a community is organised and led — who decides, by what right, and for whose good — and every community, from class to country, has some form of it.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Governance is taught here starting from the child's own community — who decides the shared things there — before any national institutions are named.
