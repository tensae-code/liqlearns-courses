# Video script — The Community and Governance

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Governance is how a community is organised and led — who decides, by what right, and for whose good — and every community, from class to country, has some form of it.

Governance means the steering of a community: the decisions about shared things — roads, water, safety, schools — and the people and bodies who make and carry out those decisions.

The lesson asks the child to look at their own place with new eyes: which decisions here are governed, and by whom? A community that understands its own governance can take part in it; one that never looks cannot.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
