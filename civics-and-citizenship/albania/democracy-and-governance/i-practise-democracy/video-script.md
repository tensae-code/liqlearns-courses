# Video script — I Practise Democracy

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Democracy is something you practise, not only something you study — the child exercises it in class and community: speaking, listening, voting, accepting decisions, answering for power.

The governance chapter closes by handing the whole subject to the learner: I practise democracy. Every piece of the chapter becomes an act — voice in the family, rights and rules in class, a campaign, a vote, power with responsibility.

Practising is how the habits set. A child who has run a fair class vote and accepted a result they disliked has learned more democracy than one who only read its definition.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
