# I Practise Democracy — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Democracy is something you practise, not only something you study — the child exercises it in class and community: speaking, listening, voting, accepting decisions, answering for power.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a child's own home, school or community in Albania.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Albania and lived there.
