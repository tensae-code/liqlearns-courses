# Video script — Democracy Begins in the Family

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Democracy begins in the family — where a child first gets a voice, is treated fairly, and shares in decisions, the habits of democracy are learned before any election.

Democracy is usually pictured in parliaments, but the book starts it at the kitchen table: a family where children are heard, where rules are fair, where decisions are explained — that family is teaching democracy every day.

The habits are the same ones a country needs: speaking and being listened to, accepting a shared decision you did not choose, treating each member as counting for one. Practised at home, they are ready when the citizen meets them in public.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
