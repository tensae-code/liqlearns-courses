# Democracy Begins in the Family — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Democracy begins in the family — where a child first gets a voice, is treated fairly, and shares in decisions, the habits of democracy are learned before any election.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a child's own home, school or community in Albania.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Albania and lived there.
