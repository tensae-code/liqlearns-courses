# Democracy Begins in the Family — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Democracy begins in the family — where a child first gets a voice, is treated fairly, and shares in decisions, the habits of democracy are learned before any election.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Democracy is taught here as beginning before politics — in the family's voice, fairness and shared decisions — and the governance chapter builds from there.
