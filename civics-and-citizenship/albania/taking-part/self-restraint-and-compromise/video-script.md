# Video script — Self-Restraint and Compromise

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Self-restraint — holding yourself back when you could push — and compromise — meeting in the middle — are marks of a citizen: the community works because its people can do both.

Self-restraint is strength used quietly: you want the last word, the biggest share, your own way — and you hold back, because the group matters too. The book names it a mark of citizenship, a sign that shows what kind of citizen you are.

Compromise is what self-restraint makes possible between people: each side gives a little, and the group reaches a decision everyone can live with. Without compromise, every disagreement becomes a fight somebody must lose completely.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
