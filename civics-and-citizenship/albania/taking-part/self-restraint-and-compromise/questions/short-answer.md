# Self-Restraint and Compromise — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Self-restraint — holding yourself back when you could push — and compromise — meeting in the middle — are marks of a citizen: the community works because its people can do both.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Self-restraint and compromise are taught here as signs — visible marks — of citizenship in the community, not only private good manners.
