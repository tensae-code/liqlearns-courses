# Self-Restraint and Compromise — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Self-restraint — holding yourself back when you could push — and compromise — meeting in the middle — are marks of a citizen: the community works because its people can do both.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a child's own home, school or community in Albania.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Albania and lived there.
