# Video script — I Have a Dream — Making the Change

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A citizen can dream of a change their community needs and take the first steps toward it — the community chapter closes with the child's own dream of making a difference.

The chapter on the community ends by turning to the learner: what would you change? A dream here is not a wish to escape — it is a picture of your own place made better, held clearly enough to work toward.

The lesson joins the dream to everything before it: you know your family, your community, its groups and leaders, its conflicts and its volunteers. Now name the change you want, and the first small step that is yours to take.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
