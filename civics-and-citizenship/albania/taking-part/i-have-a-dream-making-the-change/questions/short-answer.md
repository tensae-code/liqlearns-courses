# I Have a Dream — Making the Change — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** A citizen can dream of a change their community needs and take the first steps toward it — the community chapter closes with the child's own dream of making a difference.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** The dream is taught here as a citizen's starting point — a pictured change plus a first step — closing the community chapter by handing it to the learner.
