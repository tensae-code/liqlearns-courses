# I Have a Dream — Making the Change — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) a citizen can dream of a change their community needs and take the first steps toward it — the community chapter closes with the child's own dream of making a difference  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) The dream is taught here as a citizen's starting point — a pictured change plus a first step — closing the community chapter by handing it to the learner.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
