# I Have a Dream — Making the Change — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A citizen can dream of a change their community needs and take the first steps toward it — the community chapter closes with the child's own dream of making a difference.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a child's own home, school or community in Albania.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Albania and lived there.
