# The Community and Education — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** The community and education hold each other up: the community builds and supports the school, and the school educates the community's children into its future citizens.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Community and education are taught here as one connected system — the community sustains the school, and the school returns citizens to the community.
