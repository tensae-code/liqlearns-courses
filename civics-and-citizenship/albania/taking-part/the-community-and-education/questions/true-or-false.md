# The Community and Education — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The community and education hold each other up: the community builds and supports the school, and the school educates the community's children into its future citizens.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a child's own home, school or community in Albania.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Albania and lived there.
