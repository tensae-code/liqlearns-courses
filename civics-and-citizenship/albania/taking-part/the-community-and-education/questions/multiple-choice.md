# The Community and Education — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) the community and education hold each other up: the community builds and supports the school, and the school educates the community's children into its future citizens  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Community and education are taught here as one connected system — the community sustains the school, and the school returns citizens to the community.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
