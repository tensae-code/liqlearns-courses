# Video script — The Community and Education

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The community and education hold each other up: the community builds and supports the school, and the school educates the community's children into its future citizens.

A school does not stand alone. The community around it provides the children, the teachers, the building, the care — and expects the school to give back educated, capable young citizens.

The connection runs both ways, and the lesson makes the child part of it: how you learn and behave at school is the community's business, and the community's support — families valuing learning, elders encouraging pupils — is the school's strength.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
