# Video script — Voluntary Work

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Voluntary work is work you give freely for the community — unpaid, chosen — and it gives back: a better place, new skills, and the trust of the people around you.

A volunteer is not hired and not forced: they see something the community needs — a clean park, help for an elder, a hand at an event — and they give their work freely.

The lesson counts what voluntary work gives back. The community gains the work itself. The volunteer gains practice, skills, and something money cannot buy: the knowledge that their place is better because they were in it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
