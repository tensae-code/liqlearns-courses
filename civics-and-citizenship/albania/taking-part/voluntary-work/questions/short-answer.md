# Voluntary Work — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Voluntary work is work you give freely for the community — unpaid, chosen — and it gives back: a better place, new skills, and the trust of the people around you.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Voluntary work is taught here by its two-way gain: the community receives the work, and the volunteer receives skills and standing in return.
