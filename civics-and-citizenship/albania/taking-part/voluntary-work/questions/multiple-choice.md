# Voluntary Work — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) voluntary work is work you give freely for the community — unpaid, chosen — and it gives back: a better place, new skills, and the trust of the people around you  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Voluntary work is taught here by its two-way gain: the community receives the work, and the volunteer receives skills and standing in return.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
