# Being an Active Group Member — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** A citizen does not stand by and watch — being an active part of your group and community means joining in, speaking up and doing your share.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Being active is taught here as the opposite of being a bystander — the lesson names standing aside as the thing to unlearn.
