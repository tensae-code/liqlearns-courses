# Being an Active Group Member — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A citizen does not stand by and watch — being an active part of your group and community means joining in, speaking up and doing your share.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a child's own home, school or community in Albania.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Albania and lived there.
