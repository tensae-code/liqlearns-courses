# Being an Active Group Member — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) a citizen does not stand by and watch — being an active part of your group and community means joining in, speaking up and doing your share  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Being active is taught here as the opposite of being a bystander — the lesson names standing aside as the thing to unlearn.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
