# Video script — Being an Active Group Member

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A citizen does not stand by and watch — being an active part of your group and community means joining in, speaking up and doing your share.

There are two ways to belong to a group: on the list, or in the work. The active member is in the work — present, taking part, offering ideas, doing their share of the jobs the group needs.

The lesson is aimed at the bystander habit: watching others clean the street, organise the game, solve the problem. A community of bystanders stays weak; a community of active members can change itself.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
