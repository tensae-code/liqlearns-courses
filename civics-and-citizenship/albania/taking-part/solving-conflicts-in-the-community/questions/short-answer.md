# Solving Conflicts in the Community — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Conflicts in a community are solved by talking, listening and finding a way both sides can accept — peacefully, without harm and without winners who crush losers.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Conflict-solving is taught here as a community skill with steps — speak, listen, find the way both sides accept — not as leaving it to the strongest.
