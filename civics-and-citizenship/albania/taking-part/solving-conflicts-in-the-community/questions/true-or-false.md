# Solving Conflicts in the Community — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Conflicts in a community are solved by talking, listening and finding a way both sides can accept — peacefully, without harm and without winners who crush losers.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a child's own home, school or community in Albania.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Albania and lived there.
