# Video script — Solving Conflicts in the Community

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Conflicts in a community are solved by talking, listening and finding a way both sides can accept — peacefully, without harm and without winners who crush losers.

Where people live together, conflicts come: over space, over noise, over turns, over words. The lesson teaches that a conflict is a problem to solve together, not a battle to win.

The way is the one the book practises: each side says what happened and what it needs, each side listens, and together they look for the solution both can accept. Solved this way, a conflict can leave neighbours closer than before.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
