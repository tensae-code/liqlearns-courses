# My Region in the World — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Your home region sits inside a wide world — connected by roads, trade, people and news — and the book closes by placing the child's own region in that world.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a child's own home, school or community in Albania.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Albania and lived there.
