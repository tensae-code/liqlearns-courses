# My Region in the World — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) your home region sits inside a wide world — connected by roads, trade, people and news — and the book closes by placing the child's own region in that world  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) The closing lesson places the home region inside the world — its connections outward and inward — completing the course's widening circle from family to world.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
