# My Region in the World — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Your home region sits inside a wide world — connected by roads, trade, people and news — and the book closes by placing the child's own region in that world.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** The closing lesson places the home region inside the world — its connections outward and inward — completing the course's widening circle from family to world.
