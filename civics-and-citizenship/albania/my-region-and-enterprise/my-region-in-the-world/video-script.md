# Video script — My Region in the World

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Your home region sits inside a wide world — connected by roads, trade, people and news — and the book closes by placing the child's own region in that world.

After the family, the community, the town's government and the region's enterprises, the book takes the last step outward: the region itself is a member of a bigger whole — the country, and beyond it, the world.

The connections are real and daily: goods travel in and out, people move, languages and news arrive. To know your region in the world is to know both what it gives outward and what it receives — the widening circle of the whole course, completed.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
