# My Region in the World

## Do it first

Name three things in your home that came from outside your region, and one thing your region sends out to the world. Say how each one travelled.

## The small place in the big world

After the family, the community, the town's government and the region's enterprises, the book takes the last step outward: the region itself is a member of a bigger whole — the country, and beyond it, the world.

The connections are real and daily: goods travel in and out, people move, languages and news arrive. To know your region in the world is to know both what it gives outward and what it receives — the widening circle of the whole course, completed.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: your home region sits inside a wide world — connected by roads, trade, people and news — and the book closes by placing the child's own region in that world. Give one real example from your own life in Albania, and name the lesson's key words: region, world, connections, trade. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Albania's own book teaches civics by doing — family decisions, class rules, a mock campaign, a kiosk game. When a lesson asks you to practise something, really practise it with real people, even in a small way: the practising is the teaching.
