# Regional Resources for Enterprise

## Do it first

Name five resources of your home region: something it grows, something it makes, a skill its people have, a place it is known for, and one more. Say which could feed an enterprise.

## What your region gives

An enterprise does not start from nothing; it starts from what is at hand. A region's resources are its starting capital: what its land grows, what its people know how to make, what its places offer.

The lesson teaches the child to inventory their own region: name its products, its crafts, its natural gifts, its skilled people. Enterprise in the home region begins by seeing the region as rich in means, not poor in chances.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: every home region has resources — its land, products, skills and people — and those resources are the means from which the region's enterprises are built. Give one real example from your own life in Albania, and name the lesson's key words: region, resources, enterprise, products. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Albania's own book teaches civics by doing — family decisions, class rules, a mock campaign, a kiosk game. When a lesson asks you to practise something, really practise it with real people, even in a small way: the practising is the teaching.
