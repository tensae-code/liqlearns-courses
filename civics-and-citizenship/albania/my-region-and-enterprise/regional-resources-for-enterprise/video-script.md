# Video script — Regional Resources for Enterprise

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Every home region has resources — its land, products, skills and people — and those resources are the means from which the region's enterprises are built.

An enterprise does not start from nothing; it starts from what is at hand. A region's resources are its starting capital: what its land grows, what its people know how to make, what its places offer.

The lesson teaches the child to inventory their own region: name its products, its crafts, its natural gifts, its skilled people. Enterprise in the home region begins by seeing the region as rich in means, not poor in chances.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
