# Regional Resources for Enterprise — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Every home region has resources — its land, products, skills and people — and those resources are the means from which the region's enterprises are built.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Regional resources are taught as the means for enterprise — the region's land, products, skills and people are the starting material of its businesses.
