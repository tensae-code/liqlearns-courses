# Regional Resources for Enterprise — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Every home region has resources — its land, products, skills and people — and those resources are the means from which the region's enterprises are built.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a child's own home, school or community in Albania.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Albania and lived there.
