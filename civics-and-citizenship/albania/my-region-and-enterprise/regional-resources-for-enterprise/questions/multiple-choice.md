# Regional Resources for Enterprise — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) every home region has resources — its land, products, skills and people — and those resources are the means from which the region's enterprises are built  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Regional resources are taught as the means for enterprise — the region's land, products, skills and people are the starting material of its businesses.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
