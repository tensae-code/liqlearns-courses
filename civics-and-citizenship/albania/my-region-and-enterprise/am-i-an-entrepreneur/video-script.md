# Video script — Am I an Entrepreneur?

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
An entrepreneur is a person who sees a need, gathers what is needed, and starts something that serves people — and the chapter asks the child directly: could that person be you?.

Enterprise begins with seeing: a thing people need that nobody is providing well — a service, a product, a small business. The entrepreneur is the one who does not only see it, but starts: organising, risking a little, serving real customers.

The lesson turns the question on the learner: Am I an entrepreneur? It looks for the signs in the child — noticing needs, enjoying making and organising, not fearing a first try — and treats enterprise as something learnable, not a gift for a lucky few.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
