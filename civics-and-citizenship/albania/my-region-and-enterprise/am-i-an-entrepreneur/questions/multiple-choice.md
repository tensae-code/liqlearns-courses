# Am I an Entrepreneur? — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) an entrepreneur is a person who sees a need, gathers what is needed, and starts something that serves people — and the chapter asks the child directly: could that person be you?  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) The entrepreneur is taught here through the child's own question — Am I one? — with enterprise presented as learnable, beginning from noticing a need.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
