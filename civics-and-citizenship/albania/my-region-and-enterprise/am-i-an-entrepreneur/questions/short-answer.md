# Am I an Entrepreneur? — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** An entrepreneur is a person who sees a need, gathers what is needed, and starts something that serves people — and the chapter asks the child directly: could that person be you?.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** The entrepreneur is taught here through the child's own question — Am I one? — with enterprise presented as learnable, beginning from noticing a need.
