# Am I an Entrepreneur? — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** An entrepreneur is a person who sees a need, gathers what is needed, and starts something that serves people — and the chapter asks the child directly: could that person be you?.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a child's own home, school or community in Albania.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Albania and lived there.
