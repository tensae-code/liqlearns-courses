# The Hot-Dog Kiosk Game — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) running a kiosk — even in a game — teaches enterprise whole: you buy what you need, you sell to real customers, and the money left over tells you how you did  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) The kiosk game is taught as a two-lesson simulation — buying first, then selling and serving — so the whole circle of enterprise is practised, not described.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
