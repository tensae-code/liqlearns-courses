# The Hot-Dog Kiosk Game — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Running a kiosk — even in a game — teaches enterprise whole: you buy what you need, you sell to real customers, and the money left over tells you how you did.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** The kiosk game is taught as a two-lesson simulation — buying first, then selling and serving — so the whole circle of enterprise is practised, not described.
