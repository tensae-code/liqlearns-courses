# The Hot-Dog Kiosk Game — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Running a kiosk — even in a game — teaches enterprise whole: you buy what you need, you sell to real customers, and the money left over tells you how you did.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a child's own home, school or community in Albania.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Albania and lived there.
