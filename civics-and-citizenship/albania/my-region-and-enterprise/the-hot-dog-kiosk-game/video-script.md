# Video script — The Hot-Dog Kiosk Game

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Running a kiosk — even in a game — teaches enterprise whole: you buy what you need, you sell to real customers, and the money left over tells you how you did.

The game opens a kiosk that sells hot dogs, and the first work is buying: bread, sausages, sauces — enough to sell, not so much that it spoils. Every purchase is a decision made with limited money, exactly as real sellers decide.

Then the kiosk opens. Customers come with wants and with money; you serve them well, take payment, give change, and keep the kiosk clean and quick. At the end you count: what came in, what went out, what is left — the game's honest score.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
