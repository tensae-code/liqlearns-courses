# Video script — Entrepreneurs Solve Problems

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Entrepreneurs solve problems — a business is an answer to something that bothers people — and the project teaches it whole: find a real problem, then solve it.

The project begins by hunting: a real problem, near enough to touch — something in the school, the street, the home that is broken, missing, slow or unfair. A vague bother is not enough; the problem must be named so clearly that a solution can be checked against it.

Then the solving: plan the answer, gather what the region gives, try it on the real problem, and look honestly at whether it worked. The two lessons together are the entrepreneur's whole motion — from a problem seen to a problem answered.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
