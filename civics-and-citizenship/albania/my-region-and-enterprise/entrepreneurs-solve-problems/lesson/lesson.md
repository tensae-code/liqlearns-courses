# Entrepreneurs Solve Problems

## Do it first

Name one real problem near you — at school, in your street, at home — in one clear sentence. Say who it bothers and how you know.

## Sub lesson 1 — Finding a real problem

The project begins by hunting: a real problem, near enough to touch — something in the school, the street, the home that is broken, missing, slow or unfair. A vague bother is not enough; the problem must be named so clearly that a solution can be checked against it.

## Sub lesson 2 — Solving it — plan and act

Then the solving: plan the answer, gather what the region gives, try it on the real problem, and look honestly at whether it worked. The two lessons together are the entrepreneur's whole motion — from a problem seen to a problem answered.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: entrepreneurs solve problems — a business is an answer to something that bothers people — and the project teaches it whole: find a real problem, then solve it. Give one real example from your own life in Albania, and name the lesson's key words: entrepreneur, problem, solution, project. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Albania's own book teaches civics by doing — family decisions, class rules, a mock campaign, a kiosk game. When a lesson asks you to practise something, really practise it with real people, even in a small way: the practising is the teaching.
