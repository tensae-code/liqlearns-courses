# Entrepreneurs Solve Problems — depth

This lesson has two sub lessons, in order: 1. Finding a real problem; 2. Solving it — plan and act. Pass mark: the learner proves each sub lesson in order — each prove-it check is at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson — and can state the lesson's one thing — entrepreneurs solve problems — a business is an answer to something that bothers people — and the project teaches it whole: find a real problem, then solve it — in their own words, give one real example from their own life in Albania, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or community situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
