# Entrepreneurs Solve Problems — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Entrepreneurs solve problems — a business is an answer to something that bothers people — and the project teaches it whole: find a real problem, then solve it.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a child's own home, school or community in Albania.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Albania and lived there.
