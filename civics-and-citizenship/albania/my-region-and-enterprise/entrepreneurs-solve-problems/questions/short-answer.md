# Entrepreneurs Solve Problems — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Entrepreneurs solve problems — a business is an answer to something that bothers people — and the project teaches it whole: find a real problem, then solve it.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** The project is taught in two lessons — finding a real problem, then solving it — because enterprise is the whole motion, not only the idea.
