# The Family — the First Social Group — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The family is the most important social group a child belongs to — the first group, where belonging, care and learning to live with others begin.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a child's own home, school or community in Albania.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Albania and lived there.
