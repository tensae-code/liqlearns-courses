# The Family — the First Social Group — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** The family is the most important social group a child belongs to — the first group, where belonging, care and learning to live with others begin.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** The family is taught here as a social group — people joined together — not only as the people who live in one house.
