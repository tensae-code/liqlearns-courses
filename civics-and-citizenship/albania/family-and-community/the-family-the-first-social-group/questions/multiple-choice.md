# The Family — the First Social Group — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) the family is the most important social group a child belongs to — the first group, where belonging, care and learning to live with others begin  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) The family is taught here as a social group — people joined together — not only as the people who live in one house.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
