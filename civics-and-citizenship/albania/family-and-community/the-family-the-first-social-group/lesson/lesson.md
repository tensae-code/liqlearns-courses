# The Family — the First Social Group

## Do it first

Name every person in your family. For each one, say one thing that person does for the family — and one thing you do.

## The first group you belong to

Before school, before any club or team, every child already belongs to a group: the family. The book calls the family the most important social group, because it is the group that is always there — the people who feed you, teach you, protect you and know you best.

A social group is people joined together who share life and look out for one another. The family is the child's first one, and everything later — class, community, country — is learned first in miniature at home: sharing, waiting your turn, helping, being helped.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the family is the most important social group a child belongs to — the first group, where belonging, care and learning to live with others begin. Give one real example from your own life in Albania, and name the lesson's key words: family, social group, belonging, care. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Albania's own book teaches civics by doing — family decisions, class rules, a mock campaign, a kiosk game. When a lesson asks you to practise something, really practise it with real people, even in a small way: the practising is the teaching.
