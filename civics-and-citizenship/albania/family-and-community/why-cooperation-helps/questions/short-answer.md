# Why Cooperation Helps — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Cooperation — working together on purpose — gets more done, better, than people working alone, and it makes the group stronger while it works.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Cooperation is taught here by its usefulness — what it gains for the group — not only as being nice to others.
