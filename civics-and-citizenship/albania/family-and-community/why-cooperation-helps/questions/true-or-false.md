# Why Cooperation Helps — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Cooperation — working together on purpose — gets more done, better, than people working alone, and it makes the group stronger while it works.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a child's own home, school or community in Albania.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Albania and lived there.
