# Video script — Why Cooperation Helps

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Cooperation — working together on purpose — gets more done, better, than people working alone, and it makes the group stronger while it works.

Cooperation is not just being near other people; it is joining your effort with theirs on the same job. A wall one child cannot move, five children move together. A class project shared is finished sooner and better.

The lesson names the usefulness plainly: cooperating people share strength, share ideas, and finish with something no one of them could have made alone — and they end the job closer than they began it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
