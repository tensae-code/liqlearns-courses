# Group Members and the Leader — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) every group has members with parts to play and a leader who guides them — and a leader's job is to serve the group's purpose, not to rule over it  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) The leader is taught here as a role inside the group — guiding and organising — the same kind of role as every member's, only pointed at the whole group.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
