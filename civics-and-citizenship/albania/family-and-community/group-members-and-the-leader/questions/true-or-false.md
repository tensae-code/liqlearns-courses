# Group Members and the Leader — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Every group has members with parts to play and a leader who guides them — and a leader's job is to serve the group's purpose, not to rule over it.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a child's own home, school or community in Albania.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Albania and lived there.
