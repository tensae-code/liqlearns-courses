# Group Members and the Leader — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Every group has members with parts to play and a leader who guides them — and a leader's job is to serve the group's purpose, not to rule over it.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** The leader is taught here as a role inside the group — guiding and organising — the same kind of role as every member's, only pointed at the whole group.
