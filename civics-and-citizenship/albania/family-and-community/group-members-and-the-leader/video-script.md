# Video script — Group Members and the Leader

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Every group has members with parts to play and a leader who guides them — and a leader's job is to serve the group's purpose, not to rule over it.

A group is not a crowd. Its members each have a role — a part they are counted on to play — and together the roles make the group work. When a member does not play their part, the whole group feels the gap.

The leader is the member who guides: organising, deciding when the group must choose, and keeping everyone moving toward the group's purpose. The lesson teaches the leader as the group's guide and helper, chosen for the work — not a boss to fear.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
