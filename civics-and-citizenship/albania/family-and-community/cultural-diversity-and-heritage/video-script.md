# Video script — Cultural Diversity and Heritage

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Communities hold many cultures side by side — different languages, foods, songs and customs — and the heritage they hand down is a treasure to respect, not a reason to divide.

Diversity means difference that lives side by side. In one community, families may speak differently, cook differently, celebrate differently. The lesson teaches that this variety is the community's richness.

Heritage is what the people before us handed down — stories, crafts, songs, buildings, ways of doing things. Respecting other people's heritage, and caring for your own, keeps the whole community's treasure safe.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
