# Cultural Diversity and Heritage

## Do it first

Name one custom, food or song from your own family's heritage, and one from a family different from yours. Say what each one celebrates.

## Many cultures, one community

Diversity means difference that lives side by side. In one community, families may speak differently, cook differently, celebrate differently. The lesson teaches that this variety is the community's richness.

Heritage is what the people before us handed down — stories, crafts, songs, buildings, ways of doing things. Respecting other people's heritage, and caring for your own, keeps the whole community's treasure safe.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: communities hold many cultures side by side — different languages, foods, songs and customs — and the heritage they hand down is a treasure to respect, not a reason to divide. Give one real example from your own life in Albania, and name the lesson's key words: diversity, heritage, culture, respect. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Albania's own book teaches civics by doing — family decisions, class rules, a mock campaign, a kiosk game. When a lesson asks you to practise something, really practise it with real people, even in a small way: the practising is the teaching.
