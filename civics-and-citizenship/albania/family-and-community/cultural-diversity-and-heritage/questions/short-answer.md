# Cultural Diversity and Heritage — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Communities hold many cultures side by side — different languages, foods, songs and customs — and the heritage they hand down is a treasure to respect, not a reason to divide.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Diversity and heritage are taught together here: difference among living people, and the treasure handed down by those before them — both are to be respected.
