# Cultural Diversity and Heritage — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) communities hold many cultures side by side — different languages, foods, songs and customs — and the heritage they hand down is a treasure to respect, not a reason to divide  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Diversity and heritage are taught together here: difference among living people, and the treasure handed down by those before them — both are to be respected.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
