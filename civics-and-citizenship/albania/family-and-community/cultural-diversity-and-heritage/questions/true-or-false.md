# Cultural Diversity and Heritage — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Communities hold many cultures side by side — different languages, foods, songs and customs — and the heritage they hand down is a treasure to respect, not a reason to divide.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a child's own home, school or community in Albania.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Albania and lived there.
