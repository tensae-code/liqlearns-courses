# What the Family Does

## Do it first

List three things your family did for you today before you even noticed — a meal, a lesson, a safe place. Say who did each one.

## The family's jobs

A function is a job a thing does. The family does many jobs at once: it feeds and shelters its members, keeps them safe, teaches children how to speak, behave and live with others, and comforts them when life is hard.

These jobs are not only for the family itself. When families raise children who can cooperate, work and respect others, the whole society gains — the family's functions reach far beyond its own door.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the family has jobs to do — functions — for its members and for society: it cares, teaches, protects and raises children into members of the community. Give one real example from your own life in Albania, and name the lesson's key words: family, functions, care, society. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Albania's own book teaches civics by doing — family decisions, class rules, a mock campaign, a kiosk game. When a lesson asks you to practise something, really practise it with real people, even in a small way: the practising is the teaching.
