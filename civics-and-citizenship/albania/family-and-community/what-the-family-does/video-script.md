# Video script — What the Family Does

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The family has jobs to do — functions — for its members and for society: it cares, teaches, protects and raises children into members of the community.

A function is a job a thing does. The family does many jobs at once: it feeds and shelters its members, keeps them safe, teaches children how to speak, behave and live with others, and comforts them when life is hard.

These jobs are not only for the family itself. When families raise children who can cooperate, work and respect others, the whole society gains — the family's functions reach far beyond its own door.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
