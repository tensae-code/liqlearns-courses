# What the Family Does — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) the family has jobs to do — functions — for its members and for society: it cares, teaches, protects and raises children into members of the community  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) The family's functions are taught here as serving two directions at once: its own members, and the society around it.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
