# What the Family Does — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** The family has jobs to do — functions — for its members and for society: it cares, teaches, protects and raises children into members of the community.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** The family's functions are taught here as serving two directions at once: its own members, and the society around it.
