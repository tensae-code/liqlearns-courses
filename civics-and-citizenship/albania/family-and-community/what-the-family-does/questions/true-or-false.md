# What the Family Does — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The family has jobs to do — functions — for its members and for society: it cares, teaches, protects and raises children into members of the community.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a child's own home, school or community in Albania.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Albania and lived there.
