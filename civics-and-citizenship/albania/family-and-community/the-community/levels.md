# The Community — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — a community is the wider group of people who live and share life around you — neighbours, school, local people — and the child belongs to it too — in their own words, give one real example from their own life in Albania, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or community situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
