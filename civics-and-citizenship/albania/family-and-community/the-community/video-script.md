# Video script — The Community

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A community is the wider group of people who live and share life around you — neighbours, school, local people — and the child belongs to it too.

Step outside your family's door and you are in the community: the neighbours on your street, the people at your school, the shopkeepers, the elders, the other children. A community is the people who share a place and a life together.

Belonging to a community means you are known there and you have a part there. The lesson asks you to find your own community: who is in it, where it meets, and what its people share.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
