# The Community — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) a community is the wider group of people who live and share life around you — neighbours, school, local people — and the child belongs to it too  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) The community is taught here as the circle just wider than the family — near enough that its people know the child by name.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
