# The Community — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A community is the wider group of people who live and share life around you — neighbours, school, local people — and the child belongs to it too.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a child's own home, school or community in Albania.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Albania and lived there.
