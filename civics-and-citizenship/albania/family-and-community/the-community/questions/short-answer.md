# The Community — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** A community is the wider group of people who live and share life around you — neighbours, school, local people — and the child belongs to it too.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** The community is taught here as the circle just wider than the family — near enough that its people know the child by name.
