# Civil Society Organisations — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Civil society organisations: what they are, how they differ from government bodies — and researching the ones in the pupil's own area.  b) it has nothing to do with how Egypt governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Civil society organisations: what they are, how they differ from government bodies — and researching the ones in the pupil's own area.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

a) Civil society organisations: what they are, how they differ from government bodies — and researching the ones in the pupil's own area.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Egypt
**Answer: a) Civil society organisations: what they are, how they differ from government bodies — and researching the ones in the pupil's own area.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
