# Civil Society Organisations

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: civil Society Organisations. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Egypt's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Voluntary work usually travels through organisations. This lesson teaches civil society organisations: what they are, and how they differ from government bodies — and then sends the learner out, as the book does, to research the ones working in their own area.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Civil society organisations: what they are, how they differ from government bodies — and researching the ones in the pupil's own area. Give one real example from your own life or from the Egypt you live in, and name the lesson's key words: civil, society. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
