# Participation in Egyptian Society

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: participation in Egyptian Society. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Egypt's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Now the general teaching is measured against home ground. This lesson teaches political participation in Egyptian society as it actually stands — the real level and shape of the pupil's own country's participation, not the ideal of it.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Political participation in Egyptian society as it actually stands. Give one real example from your own life or from the Egypt you live in, and name the lesson's key words: participation, in. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: Political participation in Egyptian society as it actually stands.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
