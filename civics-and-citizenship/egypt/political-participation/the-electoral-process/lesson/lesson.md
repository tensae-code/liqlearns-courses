# The Electoral Process

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: the Electoral Process. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Egypt's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Voting is participation's sharpest instrument, and it runs as a process. This lesson teaches the electoral process step by step, the way the book walks it — so the learner who will soon vote knows the road their ballot travels, from the process's opening to its close.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The electoral process, step by step. Give one real example from your own life or from the Egypt you live in, and name the lesson's key words: the, electoral. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
