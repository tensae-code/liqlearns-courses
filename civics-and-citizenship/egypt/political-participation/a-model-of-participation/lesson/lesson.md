# A Model of Participation

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: a Model of Participation. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Egypt's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

The book — and with it this course — closes with a model: political participation gathered into a pattern the pupil can follow in their own life. This lesson teaches that closing model, the point where four levels of teaching become one citizen's practice.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The book's closing model of political participation, for the pupil to follow. Give one real example from your own life or from the Egypt you live in, and name the lesson's key words: a, model. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
