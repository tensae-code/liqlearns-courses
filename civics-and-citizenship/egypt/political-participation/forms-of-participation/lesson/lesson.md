# Forms of Participation

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: forms of Participation. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Egypt's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Participation wears many forms. This lesson teaches them as the book ranges them — from voting, the form every citizen knows first, through party work, to community work where politics meets daily life. The ladder of forms is the ladder of a citizen's growing part.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The forms of political participation, from voting to party and community work. Give one real example from your own life or from the Egypt you live in, and name the lesson's key words: forms, of. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: The forms of political participation, from voting to party and community work.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
