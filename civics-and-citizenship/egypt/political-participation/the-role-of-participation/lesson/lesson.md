# The Role of Participation

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: the Role of Participation. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Egypt's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Why does participation matter to the state itself? This lesson teaches the role political participation plays in the life of the state: it is how the governed speak to the government, how legitimacy is renewed, and how a constitution on paper becomes a living order.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The role political participation plays in the life of the state. Give one real example from your own life or from the Egypt you live in, and name the lesson's key words: the, role. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
