# The Role of Participation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The role political participation plays in the life of the state.  b) it has nothing to do with how Egypt governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The role political participation plays in the life of the state.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) The role political participation plays in the life of the state.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Egypt
**Answer: a) The role political participation plays in the life of the state.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
