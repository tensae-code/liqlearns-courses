# Obstacles to Participation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The obstacles that hold participation back.  b) it has nothing to do with how Egypt governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The obstacles that hold participation back.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) The obstacles that hold participation back.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Egypt
**Answer: a) The obstacles that hold participation back.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
