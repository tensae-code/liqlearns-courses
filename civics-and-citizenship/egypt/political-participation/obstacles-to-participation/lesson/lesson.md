# Obstacles to Participation

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: obstacles to Participation. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Egypt's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

If participation is so central, why is it often thin? This lesson teaches the obstacles that hold participation back — the things that keep citizens from the part the earlier lessons described — naming them plainly is the first step past them.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The obstacles that hold participation back. Give one real example from your own life or from the Egypt you live in, and name the lesson's key words: obstacles, to. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
