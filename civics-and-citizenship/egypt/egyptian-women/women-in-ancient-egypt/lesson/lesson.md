# Women in Ancient Egypt

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: women in Ancient Egypt. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Egypt's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

The book's chapter on the Egyptian woman opens where Egypt opens: in the ancient era. This lesson teaches the place the Egyptian woman held then — the starting point of a journey the chapter follows era by era, so the learner sees how far the road runs.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The place of the Egyptian woman in the ancient era. Give one real example from your own life or from the Egypt you live in, and name the lesson's key words: women, in. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: The place of the Egyptian woman in the ancient era.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
