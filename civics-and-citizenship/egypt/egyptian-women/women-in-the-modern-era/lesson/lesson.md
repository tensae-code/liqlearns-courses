# Women in the Modern Era

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: women in the Modern Era. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Egypt's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

In the modern era the Egyptian woman's journey speeds up. The book follows her from the 1919 generation — the women who stepped into public protest and public life — into work and the open life of society. This lesson teaches that turn: the era in which the struggle named in the chapter's title becomes visible.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The Egyptian woman in the modern era, from the 1919 generation to work and public life. Give one real example from your own life or from the Egypt you live in, and name the lesson's key words: women, in. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
