# How Society Empowers Women

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: how Society Empowers Women. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Egypt's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Empowerment is not something women win entirely alone. This lesson teaches the role of society itself in empowering women — what families, schools, workplaces and the wider public do, or fail to do, to open the road the earlier lessons traced.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The role of society in empowering women. Give one real example from your own life or from the Egypt you live in, and name the lesson's key words: how, society. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: The role of society in empowering women.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
