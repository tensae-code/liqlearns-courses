# The National Council for Women — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The National Council for Women and its competences.  b) it has nothing to do with how Egypt governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The National Council for Women and its competences.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) The National Council for Women and its competences.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Egypt
**Answer: a) The National Council for Women and its competences.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
