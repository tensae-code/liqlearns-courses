# The National Council for Women

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: the National Council for Women. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Egypt's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Egypt gave the woman's cause a national body of its own: the National Council for Women. This lesson teaches what that council is and what its competences are — the institution that carries the claim the chapter's history built.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The National Council for Women and its competences. Give one real example from your own life or from the Egypt you live in, and name the lesson's key words: the, national. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
