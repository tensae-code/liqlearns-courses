# Women in the Middle Ages

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: women in the Middle Ages. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Egypt's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

The journey continues into the medieval era. This lesson teaches the Egyptian woman's place in that age — what her life, standing and work looked like between the ancient world of the first lesson and the modern age that follows.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The Egyptian woman's place in the medieval era. Give one real example from your own life or from the Egypt you live in, and name the lesson's key words: women, in. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: The Egyptian woman's place in the medieval era.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
