# How Citizenship Is Gained — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The conditions under which citizenship is attained.  b) it has nothing to do with how Egypt governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The conditions under which citizenship is attained.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) The conditions under which citizenship is attained.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Egypt
**Answer: a) The conditions under which citizenship is attained.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
