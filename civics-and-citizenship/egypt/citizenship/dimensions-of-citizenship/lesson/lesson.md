# Dimensions of Citizenship

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: dimensions of Citizenship. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Egypt's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Citizenship is one bond, but it shows itself in several dimensions. The book names them — the legal dimension, the social dimension, the cultural dimension and more — so the learner stops picturing citizenship as a paper status and starts seeing it as a whole way of belonging that runs through law, society and culture at once.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The dimensions of citizenship — legal, social, cultural and more. Give one real example from your own life or from the Egypt you live in, and name the lesson's key words: dimensions, of. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: The dimensions of citizenship — legal, social, cultural and more.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
