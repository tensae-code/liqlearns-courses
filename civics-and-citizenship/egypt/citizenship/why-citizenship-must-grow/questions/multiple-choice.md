# Why Citizenship Must Grow — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The importance of developing citizenship in society.  b) it has nothing to do with how Egypt governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The importance of developing citizenship in society.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**Answer: a) The importance of developing citizenship in society.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
