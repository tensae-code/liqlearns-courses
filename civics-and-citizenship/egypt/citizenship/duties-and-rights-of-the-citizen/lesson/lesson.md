# Duties and Rights of the Citizen

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: duties and Rights of the Citizen. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Egypt's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## Sub lesson 1 — The citizen's duties

The duties the citizen owes — one full side of the citizen's balance sheet.

## Sub lesson 2 — The citizen's rights

The rights the citizen holds — held together with the duties: no right without a matching duty.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The citizen's duties and rights, held together: no right without a matching duty. Give one real example from your own life or from the Egypt you live in, and name the lesson's key words: duties, and. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
