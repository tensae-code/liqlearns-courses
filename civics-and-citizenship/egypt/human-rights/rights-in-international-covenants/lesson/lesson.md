# Rights in International Covenants

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: rights in International Covenants. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Egypt's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

In the modern age the world's states wrote human rights into international covenants and declarations. This lesson teaches those instruments the way the book presents them — summarised by instrument and by theme — so the learner can name where a right is written down beyond their own country's borders.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Human rights in the international covenants and declarations, summarised by instrument and theme. Give one real example from your own life or from the Egypt you live in, and name the lesson's key words: rights, in. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
