# Kinds of Rights — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The categories of rights: civil and political rights, headed by the right to life; economic, social and cultural rights; and collective rights.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that kinds of Rights — and that it has no connection to the rest of Egypt's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Egypt's own course, in its own order.
