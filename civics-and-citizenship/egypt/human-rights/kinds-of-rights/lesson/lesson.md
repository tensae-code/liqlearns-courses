# Kinds of Rights

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: kinds of Rights. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Egypt's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## Sub lesson 1 — Civil and political rights

Civil and political rights, headed by the right to life.

## Sub lesson 2 — Economic, social and cultural rights

Economic, social and cultural rights — the rights of livelihood, welfare and culture.

## Sub lesson 3 — Collective rights

Collective rights — the rights held by groups and peoples together, not by one person alone.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The categories of rights: civil and political rights, headed by the right to life; economic, social and cultural rights; and collective rights. Give one real example from your own life or from the Egypt you live in, and name the lesson's key words: kinds, of. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
