# What Human Rights Are

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: what Human Rights Are. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Egypt's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Human rights are the rights a person holds simply by being human. The book does not start with modern paperwork: it starts with the concept itself, and with its first sources in the old civilisations — the idea is far older than the declarations that later wrote it down.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The concept of human rights and their first sources in the old civilisations. Give one real example from your own life or from the Egypt you live in, and name the lesson's key words: what, human. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
