# NGOs and Human Rights

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: nGOs and Human Rights. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Egypt's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

States and councils are not the only guardians of human rights. Non-governmental organisations — NGOs — spread knowledge of rights and defend them where they are threatened. This lesson teaches the role they play, closing the book's human-rights chapter with the citizen's own organisations at work.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The role of non-governmental organisations in spreading and defending human rights. Give one real example from your own life or from the Egypt you live in, and name the lesson's key words: ngos, and. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
