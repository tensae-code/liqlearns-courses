# What Democracy Is

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: what Democracy Is. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Egypt's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Democracy is the rule of the people — but the book is careful to place it among its neighbours. This lesson teaches what democracy is through its relation to the concepts around it: freedom, equality and participation. Democracy is not any one of them; it is the system in which they meet.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Democracy, and its relation to neighbouring concepts: freedom, equality and participation. Give one real example from your own life or from the Egypt you live in, and name the lesson's key words: what, democracy. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
