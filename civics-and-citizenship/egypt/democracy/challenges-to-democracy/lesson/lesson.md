# Challenges to Democracy

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: challenges to Democracy. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Egypt's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Democracy is not self-running. This lesson teaches the challenges and obstacles it faces — and examines, critically, even the tools meant to bring the people closer to power: semi-direct democracy's referendum and recall, weighed for what they can and cannot do.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The challenges and obstacles democracy faces — including semi-direct democracy's tools, referendum and recall, examined critically. Give one real example from your own life or from the Egypt you live in, and name the lesson's key words: challenges, to. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
