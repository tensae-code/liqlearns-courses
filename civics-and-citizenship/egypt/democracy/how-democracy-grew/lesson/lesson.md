# How Democracy Grew

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: how Democracy Grew. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Egypt's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

The word itself carries its history: demos, the people, and kratos, rule — Athens' invention. This lesson follows the idea of democracy as it emerges and develops, from Athens and Aristotle's thinking to the modern age, when it became the standard against which governments are judged.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The emergence and development of the idea of democracy, from Athens (demos, kratos) and Aristotle to the modern age. Give one real example from your own life or from the Egypt you live in, and name the lesson's key words: how, democracy. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
