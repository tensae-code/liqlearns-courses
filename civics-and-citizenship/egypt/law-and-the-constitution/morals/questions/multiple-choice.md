# Morals — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) What morals are, and how they govern conduct before any law does.  b) it has nothing to do with how Egypt governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) What morals are, and how they govern conduct before any law does.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) What morals are, and how they govern conduct before any law does.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Egypt
**Answer: a) What morals are, and how they govern conduct before any law does.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
