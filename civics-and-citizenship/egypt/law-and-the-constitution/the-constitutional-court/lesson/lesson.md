# The Constitutional Court

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: the Constitutional Court. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Egypt's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

A constitution needs a guardian. In Egypt that guardian is the Supreme Constitutional Court. This lesson teaches how the court is formed and what its competences are — the body that measures laws against the constitution and speaks when they clash.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The Supreme Constitutional Court: its formation and its competences. Give one real example from your own life or from the Egypt you live in, and name the lesson's key words: the, constitutional. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
