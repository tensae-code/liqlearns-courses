# Law, Morals and Religion

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: law, Morals and Religion. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Egypt's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Three orderings govern a person's conduct: the rules of law, the principles of morals, and the ordering of religion. This lesson teaches how they relate — where they overlap, where each one rules alone, and why a citizen needs to tell them apart.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The relation between the rules of law and the principles of morals and the ordering of religion. Give one real example from your own life or from the Egypt you live in, and name the lesson's key words: law, morals. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
