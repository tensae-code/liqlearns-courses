# The Digital Citizen

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: the Digital Citizen. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Egypt's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## Sub lesson 1 — The traits of the digital citizen

Respecting dialogue and intellectual property, guarding personal data, and respecting others' privacy and cultures.

## Sub lesson 2 — The skills of the digital citizen

The skills the digital citizen must gain, so the traits become habits and not just words.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The traits of the digital citizen — respecting dialogue and intellectual property, guarding personal data, respecting others' privacy and cultures — and the skills he must gain. Give one real example from your own life or from the Egypt you live in, and name the lesson's key words: the, digital. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: The traits of the digital citizen — respecting dialogue and intellectual property, guarding personal data, respecting others' privacy and cultures — and the skills he must gain.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
