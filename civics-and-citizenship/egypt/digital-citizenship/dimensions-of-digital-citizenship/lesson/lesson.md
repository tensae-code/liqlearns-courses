# Dimensions of Digital Citizenship

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: dimensions of Digital Citizenship. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Egypt's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Digital citizenship, like citizenship itself, has dimensions. The book runs them from the ground up: digital access and digital literacy first — can you get online, and can you use what you find — through to digital law and digital security at the top. A citizen is only as strong online as the weakest of these dimensions.

## Say it to your companion

Give one real example from your own life or from the Egypt you live in, and name the lesson's key words: dimensions, of. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
