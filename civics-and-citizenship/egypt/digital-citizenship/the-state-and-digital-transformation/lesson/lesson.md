# The State and Digital Transformation

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: the State and Digital Transformation. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Egypt's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

The digital citizen does not act alone: the state has a role in digital transformation, and Egypt's book names its own programme — Digital Egypt. The lesson closes the section the way the book does, with the pupils themselves at work: an activity in which they map the dimensions of digital citizenship onto the transformation happening around them.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The state's role in digital transformation — Digital Egypt — and a pupil activity mapping the dimensions of digital citizenship. Give one real example from your own life or from the Egypt you live in, and name the lesson's key words: the, state. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
