# How Parties Arose

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: how Parties Arose. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Egypt's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Parties were not always there. This lesson teaches their origin and rise — how political parties emerged as people's part in government grew, and organisation became the way scattered opinion turns into a force.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The origin and rise of political parties. Give one real example from your own life or from the Egypt you live in, and name the lesson's key words: how, parties. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
