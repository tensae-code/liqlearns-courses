# Parties in Egypt

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: parties in Egypt. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Egypt's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

The chapter lands, as this book always lands, in Egypt. This lesson teaches political parties in Egypt themselves — how they emerged and how they developed — the country's own party history, told as the record of its citizens organising to take part.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Political parties in Egypt: their emergence and development. Give one real example from your own life or from the Egypt you live in, and name the lesson's key words: parties, in. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: Political parties in Egypt: their emergence and development.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
