# Hands-on — Cultures and Peace

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Go back to your first lesson's note: one thing a people of Guatemala teaches. Add one more value now, and say how it could stop a harm you have met this level. This lesson is the foundation, deepened.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Guatemala's cultures are the course's standing foundation — their own values are peace resources, returned to and deepened in every básico year. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the community life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
