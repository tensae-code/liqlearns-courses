# Video script — Cultures and Peace

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Guatemala's cultures are the course's standing foundation — their own values are peace resources, returned to and deepened in every básico year.

Every básico guide opens with the same introduction: the learning elements of the diverse cultures of Guatemala. At segundo básico the teaching deepens: the cultures' own values are peace resources — the materials peace is built from, carried inside the country's peoples themselves.

Diligent work, protection of all that has life, care for territory and nature, ancestral knowledge: these are not only things to know about. They are things to build with.

At tercero básico the same introduction stands again at the head of the guide: Guatemala's cultures' learning elements as the course's foundation. The repetition is the teaching — each year of básico begins again from the peoples of Guatemala before it names that year's violences.

So this lesson stands where the ladder turns: it closes Level 3's protection of named people, and it is the ground Level 4's new violences are answered from.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
