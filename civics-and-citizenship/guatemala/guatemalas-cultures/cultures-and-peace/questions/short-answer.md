# Cultures and Peace — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name two of the cultures' values or elements named as peace resources.
**Answer:** Any two, for example: diligent work (mitijul); protecting all that has life; care for territory and nature; ancestral knowledge.

**2.** Why does this lesson stand at the turn of the ladder?
**Answer:** It closes Level 3's protection of named people and is the ground from which Level 4's new violences are answered.
