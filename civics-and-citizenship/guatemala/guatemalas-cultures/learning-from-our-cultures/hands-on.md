# Hands-on — Learning from Our Cultures

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name the peoples of Guatemala you know. For one of them, write down one thing its people know or value that everyone could learn from. This lesson gathers the learning elements of Guatemala's cultures — the ground the whole course stands on.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Guatemala's citizen formation stands on the learning elements of its own peoples — Maya, Xinka, Garífuna and Ladino: their territory and nature, their ancestral knowledge and their community values. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the community life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
