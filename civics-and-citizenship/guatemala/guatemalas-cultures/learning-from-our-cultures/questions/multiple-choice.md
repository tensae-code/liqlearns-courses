# Learning from Our Cultures — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which peoples' learning elements ground Guatemala's citizen formation?
a) the Maya, Xinka, Garífuna and Ladino peoples  b) only the people of the capital city  c) peoples of other countries only  d) no peoples — rules alone ground it
**Answer: a) the Maya, Xinka, Garífuna and Ladino peoples** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is mitijul, as the guides teach it?
a) a Maya value of diligent work  b) a kind of school uniform  c) a government office  d) a type of exam
**Answer: a) a Maya value of diligent work** — That is what this lesson teaches.
