## Sub lesson 1 — Guatemala's peoples
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Guatemala is a country of peoples: Maya, Xinka, Garífuna and Ladino. Citizen formation in básico begins here — not with rules, but with the peoples who live in the country and what each of them teaches. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — What each culture teaches
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The guides name the learning elements of the diverse cultures of Guatemala: territory and nature — the land a people lives from and cares for; ancestral knowledge — what the elders and the generations before have learned and handed down; and community values — the ways a people holds together and treats its members. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
