## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Guatemala's peoples** — Guatemala is a country of peoples: Maya, Xinka, Garífuna and Ladino. Citizen formation in básico begins here — not with rules, but with the peoples who live in the country and what each of them teaches.
2. **What each culture teaches** — The guides name the learning elements of the diverse cultures of Guatemala: territory and nature — the land a people lives from and cares for; ancestral knowledge — what the elders and the generations before have learned and handed down; and community values — the ways a people holds together and treats its members.
3. **The ground of citizen formation** — Citizen formation, in Guatemala's own way, grows out of its cultures. A citizen is formed by learning from the peoples of their own country — their knowledge, their values, their care for land and life.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Guatemala's citizen formation stands on the learning elements of its own peoples — Maya, Xinka, Garífuna and Ladino: their territory and nature, their ancestral knowledge and their community values.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or town — or, for this course's lessons, from the school and community life they live in.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the learner's own school, family and community life, as the guides' learning experiences work.
