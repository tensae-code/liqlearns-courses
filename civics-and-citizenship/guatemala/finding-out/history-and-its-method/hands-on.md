# Hands-on — History and Its Method

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Two people tell you different versions of the same event last year. How do you find the truth? History faces that problem at the scale of centuries. This lesson gives its method.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: History knows the past by a scientific method: posing questions, seeking sources, criticising and comparing them, and only then interpreting — so the past is known by evidence, not by legend. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the community life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
