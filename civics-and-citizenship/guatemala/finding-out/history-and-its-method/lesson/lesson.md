# History and Its Method

## Do it first

Two people tell you different versions of the same event last year. How do you find the truth? History faces that problem at the scale of centuries. This lesson gives its method.

## Sub lesson 1 — The historian's method

History's method of investigation runs in order: a question about the past; the search for sources that bear on it; criticism of those sources — who made them, when, why; comparison between them; and interpretation, the reasoned account built only on what survives the criticism.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: History knows the past by a scientific method: posing questions, seeking sources, criticising and comparing them, and only then interpreting — so the past is known by evidence, not by legend. Give one real example from your own life or from the Guatemala you live in, and name the lesson's key words: method, source criticism, interpretation. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
