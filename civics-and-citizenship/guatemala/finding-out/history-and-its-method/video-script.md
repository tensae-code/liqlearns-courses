# Video script — History and Its Method

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
History knows the past by a scientific method: posing questions, seeking sources, criticising and comparing them, and only then interpreting — so the past is known by evidence, not by legend.

History's method of investigation runs in order: a question about the past; the search for sources that bear on it; criticism of those sources — who made them, when, why; comparison between them; and interpretation, the reasoned account built only on what survives the criticism.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
