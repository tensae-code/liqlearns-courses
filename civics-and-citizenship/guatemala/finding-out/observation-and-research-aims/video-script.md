# Video script — Observation and Research Aims

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Observation is the social sciences' first technique: looking carefully and recording what is seen. Every investigation then needs objectives — clear statements of what it seeks to find out, set step by step before the work begins.

Observation means looking with a purpose: watching people, places and events carefully, and recording what is seen without inventing or skipping. It is a basic technique of the social sciences because society must first be seen clearly before it can be understood.

An investigation without objectives wanders. The objectives say, step by step, what the investigation wants to achieve: first the general aim, then the specific aims that lead to it. They are set before collecting information, and the whole work is checked against them at the end.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
