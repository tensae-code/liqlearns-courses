# Video script — The Social Sciences

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The social sciences study the human being in society — history, geography, sociology, economics and their sisters — separate disciplines that hold together because their subject is one: people living together.

The social sciences are the disciplines that study the human being in society: history studies our past, geography our land, sociology our living together, economics our work and exchange, and more besides. Each has its own questions and methods, yet they hold together — a society cannot be understood by one of them alone.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
