# The Social Sciences

## Do it first

Your town could be studied by its past, its land, its money, its customs. Four studies, one town. This lesson introduces the family of sciences that study society.

## Sub lesson 1 — Studying society

The social sciences are the disciplines that study the human being in society: history studies our past, geography our land, sociology our living together, economics our work and exchange, and more besides. Each has its own questions and methods, yet they hold together — a society cannot be understood by one of them alone.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The social sciences study the human being in society — history, geography, sociology, economics and their sisters — separate disciplines that hold together because their subject is one: people living together. Give one real example from your own life or from the Guatemala you live in, and name the lesson's key words: social sciences, discipline, society. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
