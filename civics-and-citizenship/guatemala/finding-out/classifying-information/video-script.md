# Video script — Classifying Information

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Investigating is seeking new knowledge; once information is found, classifying it — sorting it by kind, theme or source — turns a pile of notes into knowledge that can be used.

Classification is ordering information by a chosen criterion — by theme, by kind of source, by date — so each piece can be found, compared and used. Unclassified information is a heap; classified, it becomes the investigator's working material.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
