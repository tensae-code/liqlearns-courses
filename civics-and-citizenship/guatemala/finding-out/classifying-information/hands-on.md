# Hands-on — Classifying Information

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Empty a mixed drawer onto the table, then sort it into piles that belong together. You have just done this lesson's work: classification.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Investigating is seeking new knowledge; once information is found, classifying it — sorting it by kind, theme or source — turns a pile of notes into knowledge that can be used. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the community life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
