# Classifying Information

## Do it first

Empty a mixed drawer onto the table, then sort it into piles that belong together. You have just done this lesson's work: classification.

## Sub lesson 1 — Sorting what was found

Classification is ordering information by a chosen criterion — by theme, by kind of source, by date — so each piece can be found, compared and used. Unclassified information is a heap; classified, it becomes the investigator's working material.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Investigating is seeking new knowledge; once information is found, classifying it — sorting it by kind, theme or source — turns a pile of notes into knowledge that can be used. Give one real example from your own life or from the Guatemala you live in, and name the lesson's key words: classify, criterion, investigate. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
