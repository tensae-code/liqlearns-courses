# Video script — Selecting and Recording Information

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A social investigation selects its information on purpose: first objectives and a work plan, then choosing what fits the question and recording it in order, so nothing needed is lost.

Selection begins before gathering: set the investigation's objectives and a work plan — what will be sought, where, and how it will be recorded. The plan keeps the investigator from drowning in information that does not serve the question.

Recording is writing down what was selected, in order, with its source, so it can be found again, checked and used in the report. A note without its source is information half lost.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
