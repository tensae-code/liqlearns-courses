# Hands-on — Selecting and Recording Information

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
You have one bag for a market trip and a list in your head. Choosing what goes in, and writing the list, is this lesson's skill — applied to information.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: A social investigation selects its information on purpose: first objectives and a work plan, then choosing what fits the question and recording it in order, so nothing needed is lost. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the community life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
