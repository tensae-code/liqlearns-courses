# Video script — Historical Sources

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
To interpret historical facts and social problems we use sources — documents, testimonies, objects and images left by the past or gathered about the present — and each source must be asked who made it and why.

A source is anything that gives information about the past or a social problem: written documents, spoken testimony, photographs, buildings, objects. The historian and the social investigator interpret facts by comparing sources, not by trusting one alone.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
