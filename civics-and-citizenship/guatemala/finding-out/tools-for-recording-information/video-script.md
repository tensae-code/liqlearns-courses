# Video script — Tools for Recording Information

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
An investigation gathers information with tools — notebooks, cards, recordings, tables — chosen and used in order, so what is gathered is recorded completely and can be trusted later.

The tools for registering information — the notebook and the card, the questionnaire and the recording, the table and the file — are how an investigation keeps what it gathers. Gathering (recopilar) and recording go together: every item noted with its source, at the moment it is found.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
