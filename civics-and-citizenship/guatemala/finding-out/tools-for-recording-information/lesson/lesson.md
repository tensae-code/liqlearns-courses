# Tools for Recording Information

## Do it first

You interview three elders about your town's history with nothing to write on. By evening, half is gone. This lesson is about the tools that stop knowledge escaping.

## Sub lesson 1 — Gathering and recording

The tools for registering information — the notebook and the card, the questionnaire and the recording, the table and the file — are how an investigation keeps what it gathers. Gathering (recopilar) and recording go together: every item noted with its source, at the moment it is found.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: An investigation gathers information with tools — notebooks, cards, recordings, tables — chosen and used in order, so what is gathered is recorded completely and can be trusted later. Give one real example from your own life or from the Guatemala you live in, and name the lesson's key words: recording tool, gather (recopilar), register. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
