# Video script — Social Investigation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Social investigation studies a real situation to identify its problems and solve them — and it works from the sources of information the social sciences use: documents, observation, testimony and data.

Social investigation begins in a real situation: something in society that needs understanding. It studies the situation in order to identify the problem exactly — its causes and its people — because a problem well identified is already on the way to its solution.

The investigation draws on the social sciences' sources of information: written documents and statistics, direct observation, and the testimony of the people who live the situation. Each source is chosen for the question asked.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
