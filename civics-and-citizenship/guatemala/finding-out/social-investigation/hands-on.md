# Hands-on — Social Investigation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Your school has a problem — say, pupils arriving late. Guessing helps no one. This lesson shows how to investigate a social problem properly.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Social investigation studies a real situation to identify its problems and solve them — and it works from the sources of information the social sciences use: documents, observation, testimony and data. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the community life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
