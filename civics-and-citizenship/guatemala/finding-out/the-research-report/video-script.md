# Video script — The Research Report

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The research report is the investigation's final written form — presenting the work in its elements, and closing with conclusions: the synthesis of the explanations, where the findings answer the guiding questions.

The research report gathers the whole investigation — theme, questions, method, findings — in an ordered written document. Its conclusions are not one more summary: they are the synthesis of the explanations, the point where everything found is drawn together to answer the questions the work began with.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
