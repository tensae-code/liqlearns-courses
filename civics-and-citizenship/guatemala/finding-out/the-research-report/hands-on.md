# Hands-on — The Research Report

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
An investigation no one can read might as well not exist. This lesson builds its final form: the report, ending in conclusions.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: The research report is the investigation's final written form — presenting the work in its elements, and closing with conclusions: the synthesis of the explanations, where the findings answer the guiding questions. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the community life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
