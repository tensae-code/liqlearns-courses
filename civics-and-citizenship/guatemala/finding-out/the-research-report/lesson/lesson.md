# The Research Report

## Do it first

An investigation no one can read might as well not exist. This lesson builds its final form: the report, ending in conclusions.

## Sub lesson 1 — The report and its conclusions

The research report gathers the whole investigation — theme, questions, method, findings — in an ordered written document. Its conclusions are not one more summary: they are the synthesis of the explanations, the point where everything found is drawn together to answer the questions the work began with.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The research report is the investigation's final written form — presenting the work in its elements, and closing with conclusions: the synthesis of the explanations, where the findings answer the guiding questions. Give one real example from your own life or from the Guatemala you live in, and name the lesson's key words: research report, conclusion, synthesis. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
