# Video script — Written Research Reports

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A written research report presents an investigation in order: the theme, the background, the guiding questions — and then what was found — so others can follow, check and use the work.

The written report opens with the theme — what was investigated — and its background: what was already known and why the question matters. Then come the guiding questions, the questions that steered the whole investigation and that the report must answer.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
