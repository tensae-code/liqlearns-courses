# Video script — Workers' Rights

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Workers hold rights established in Guatemala's Constitution itself — to fair pay, limited hours, rest and safe conditions — so that dignity at work is not a favour from employers but a right of every worker.

Guatemala's Constitution establishes the workers' rights: just remuneration, regulated working hours, weekly rest and holidays, protection for working mothers and minors, and safe conditions. Being constitutional, these rights stand above any contract or custom that would deny them.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
