# Workers' Rights

## Do it first

A right is stronger than a request. This lesson lists what Guatemalan workers hold by right, in the Constitution's own keeping.

## Sub lesson 1 — Rights in the Constitution

Guatemala's Constitution establishes the workers' rights: just remuneration, regulated working hours, weekly rest and holidays, protection for working mothers and minors, and safe conditions. Being constitutional, these rights stand above any contract or custom that would deny them.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Workers hold rights established in Guatemala's Constitution itself — to fair pay, limited hours, rest and safe conditions — so that dignity at work is not a favour from employers but a right of every worker. Give one real example from your own life or from the Guatemala you live in, and name the lesson's key words: workers' rights, remuneration, constitution. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
