# Video script — Child Labour

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Child labour is economic activity done by children and young people — work that takes their childhood, schooling and health — and it is named as a wrong against them, to be prevented, not accepted.

Child labour is work done by children and young people in economic activities — in fields, markets, workshops and homes — often heavy, dangerous or long, stealing school and rest. The book names it plainly as a wrong against children, against their rights to education, health and play.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
