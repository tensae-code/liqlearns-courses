# Child Labour — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Child labour is economic work by children that steals school, health and rest — a wrong against them.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Work never harms children, whatever it is and however long.
**Answer: False** — Child labour is economic work by children that steals school, health and rest — a wrong against them.
