# Work in Guatemala

## Do it first

Count the working people you pass on one street: shop, stall, workshop, street seller. Which of them have contracts, holidays, protection? This lesson looks at work in Guatemala as it is.

## Sub lesson 1 — Work and its kinds

Work is effort that produces: physical effort of the hands, mental effort of thought, spiritual effort of care and creation. Guatemala's workers do all three — in fields, factories, offices, markets and homes.

## Sub lesson 2 — The informal economy

The informal economy is work outside the formal system: unregistered businesses and workers without contracts, social security or the law's protection. It feeds millions of Guatemalan families — and leaves its workers exposed, which is why it is named in a course about dignity at work.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Work is human effort — physical, mental and spiritual — that produces; in Guatemala much of it happens in the informal economy, outside registration and protection, where its workers labour without the rights the formal world promises. Give one real example from your own life or from the Guatemala you live in, and name the lesson's key words: work, informal economy, social security. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
