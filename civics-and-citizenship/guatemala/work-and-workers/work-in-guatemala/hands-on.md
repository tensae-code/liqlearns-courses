# Hands-on — Work in Guatemala

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Count the working people you pass on one street: shop, stall, workshop, street seller. Which of them have contracts, holidays, protection? This lesson looks at work in Guatemala as it is.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Work is human effort — physical, mental and spiritual — that produces; in Guatemala much of it happens in the informal economy, outside registration and protection, where its workers labour without the rights the formal world promises. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the community life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
