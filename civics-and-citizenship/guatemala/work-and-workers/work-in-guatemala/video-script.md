# Video script — Work in Guatemala

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Work is human effort — physical, mental and spiritual — that produces; in Guatemala much of it happens in the informal economy, outside registration and protection, where its workers labour without the rights the formal world promises.

Work is effort that produces: physical effort of the hands, mental effort of thought, spiritual effort of care and creation. Guatemala's workers do all three — in fields, factories, offices, markets and homes.

The informal economy is work outside the formal system: unregistered businesses and workers without contracts, social security or the law's protection. It feeds millions of Guatemalan families — and leaves its workers exposed, which is why it is named in a course about dignity at work.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
