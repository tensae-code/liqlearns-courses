# Video script — Unions and Workers' Movements

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Workers and peasants organised — in unions and movements — to demand labour and social rights together: fair wages, hours, and safety among their demands, because one voice alone is easily ignored and many together are heard.

Unions (sindicalismo) are workers organised by trade or workplace; with peasants' movements beside them, they pressed labour and social demands no single worker could win: fair pay, limited hours, rest — and safety at work among them. Organisation turned individual weakness into collective strength.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
