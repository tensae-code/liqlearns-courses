# Unions and Workers' Movements — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Unions and workers' movements organised to win labour and social demands — safety among them.
**Answer: True**

**2.** A single worker alone has the same power to change conditions as an organised union.
**Answer: False** — Unions and workers' movements organised to win labour and social demands — safety among them.
