# Unions and Workers' Movements

## Do it first

Try to lift something heavy alone; then with three friends. Workers learned the same lesson about their demands. This lesson tells how they organised.

## Sub lesson 1 — Organising to be heard

Unions (sindicalismo) are workers organised by trade or workplace; with peasants' movements beside them, they pressed labour and social demands no single worker could win: fair pay, limited hours, rest — and safety at work among them. Organisation turned individual weakness into collective strength.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Workers and peasants organised — in unions and movements — to demand labour and social rights together: fair wages, hours, and safety among their demands, because one voice alone is easily ignored and many together are heard. Give one real example from your own life or from the Guatemala you live in, and name the lesson's key words: union, labour demand, collective. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
