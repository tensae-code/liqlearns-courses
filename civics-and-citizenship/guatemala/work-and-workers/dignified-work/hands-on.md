# Hands-on — Dignified Work

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Imagine two workshops: in one, workers have water, light, rest and safe tools; in the other, none of these. This lesson says which one the law and dignity demand.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Work is a right, and it must be dignified: fair conditions for the worker, with minimum hygiene and safety at work, so no one's health or life is the price of their wage. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the community life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
