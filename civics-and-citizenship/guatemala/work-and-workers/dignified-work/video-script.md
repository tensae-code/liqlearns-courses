# Video script — Dignified Work

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Work is a right, and it must be dignified: fair conditions for the worker, with minimum hygiene and safety at work, so no one's health or life is the price of their wage.

Work is every person's right — and not any work at any price. Dignified work means conditions worthy of a person: fair hours and pay, rest, and respect, so that working builds a life instead of consuming it.

Minimum hygiene and safety at work are part of dignity: clean water and air, safe tools and machines, protection against dangers of the job. An unsafe workplace injures the worker to enrich the work — the opposite of dignified.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
