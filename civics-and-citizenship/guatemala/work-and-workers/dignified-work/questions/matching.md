# Dignified Work — matching

Match each word with its meaning.

**1.** dignified work — a)

**2.** hygiene at work — b)

**3.** safety at work — c)


Meanings:
a) work under conditions worthy of a person
b) cleanliness conditions that protect workers' health
c) protection against the dangers of a job

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b; 3. c
