# Dignified Work — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What does this lesson teach about work as a right, with conditions?
**Answer:** Work is a right, and dignified work means fair conditions worthy of a person.

**2.** What does this lesson teach about hygiene and safety?
**Answer:** Hygiene and safety minimums — clean water, safe tools, protection — are part of dignified work.
