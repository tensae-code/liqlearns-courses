# Video script — Colonial Government

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The colonies were governed from Spain through a chain of authorities — king, councils, viceroys and local officials — and the Church governed beside them, through bishoprics and parishes in every town.

Colonial government was organised from Spain: the king and his councils at the top, viceroys and captains-general ruling whole regions, and governors, magistrates and town councils below. Every authority answered upward to the crown, not to the peoples governed.

The Church was part of colonial government, not beside it: bishoprics divided the territory, parishes reached every town, and the Church kept registers, taught, and enforced the official religion among the peoples.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
