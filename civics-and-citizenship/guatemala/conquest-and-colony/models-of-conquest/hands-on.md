# Hands-on — Models of Conquest

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Two teachers can run the same class very differently. Two empires ran conquest differently too. This lesson compares their models.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Not every European power conquered the same way: the Spanish model of settlement and rule, the Portuguese model centred on Brazil and trade, and other models besides — comparing them shows conquest had shapes, not one shape. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the community life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
