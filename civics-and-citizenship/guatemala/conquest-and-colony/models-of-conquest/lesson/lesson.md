# Models of Conquest

## Do it first

Two teachers can run the same class very differently. Two empires ran conquest differently too. This lesson compares their models.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Spanish and Portuguese models

Spain conquered by founding cities, ruling territory and putting the peoples under its administration and church. Portugal, in Brazil, built its model first around trade posts and the extraction of products, settling the land in its own way.

## Sub lesson 2 — Other models

Other European powers followed still other models — trading companies, coastal factories, settler colonies — each shaping differently the lands and peoples they seized. Comparing models shows what was common to all conquest: land and peoples taken by force.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Not every European power conquered the same way: the Spanish model of settlement and rule, the Portuguese model centred on Brazil and trade, and other models besides — comparing them shows conquest had shapes, not one shape. Give one real example from your own life or from the Guatemala you live in, and name the lesson's key words: model of conquest, settlement, trade post. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
