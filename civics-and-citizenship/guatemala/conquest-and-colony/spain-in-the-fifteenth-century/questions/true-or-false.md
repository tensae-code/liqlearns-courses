# Spain in the Fifteenth Century — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Spain formed from the joined kingdoms of Castilla and Aragón on the eve of the voyages.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Spain had always been one single kingdom since ancient times.
**Answer: False** — Spain formed from the joined kingdoms of Castilla and Aragón on the eve of the voyages.
