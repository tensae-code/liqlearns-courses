# Hands-on — Spain in the Fifteenth Century

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Join two puzzle pieces or two ropes into one. Spain itself was joined like that just before Columbus sailed. This lesson shows the joining.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: The Spain that launched the voyages was newly formed of kingdoms — Castilla and Aragón above all — joined under one crown on the very eve of the crossing to America. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the community life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
