# Video script — Spain in the Fifteenth Century

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The Spain that launched the voyages was newly formed of kingdoms — Castilla and Aragón above all — joined under one crown on the very eve of the crossing to America.

Spain was not always one kingdom. In the fifteenth century the crowns of Castilla and Aragón were joined by marriage and rule, making a stronger monarchy — and it was this newly united Spain that backed the voyage of 1492.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
