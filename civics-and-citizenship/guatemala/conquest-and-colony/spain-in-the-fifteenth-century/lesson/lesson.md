# Spain in the Fifteenth Century

## Do it first

Join two puzzle pieces or two ropes into one. Spain itself was joined like that just before Columbus sailed. This lesson shows the joining.

## Sub lesson 1 — Kingdoms joined

Spain was not always one kingdom. In the fifteenth century the crowns of Castilla and Aragón were joined by marriage and rule, making a stronger monarchy — and it was this newly united Spain that backed the voyage of 1492.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The Spain that launched the voyages was newly formed of kingdoms — Castilla and Aragón above all — joined under one crown on the very eve of the crossing to America. Give one real example from your own life or from the Guatemala you live in, and name the lesson's key words: Castilla, Aragón, monarchy. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
