# The Conquest Process

## Do it first

Think of how a whole school year changes a class — not in one day, but step by step. The conquest happened the same way, step by step, and this lesson walks the process.

## Sub lesson 1 — How the conquest proceeded

After the first landings came expeditions into the mainland. The conquerors advanced with steel, horses and firearms, made alliances with peoples who were enemies of the great powers, and were helped terribly by new diseases. One by one the capitals fell, and Spanish rule was imposed over the peoples of America.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The conquest of America was a process, not one battle: expeditions, alliances, wars, disease and the fall of peoples' capitals, region by region, over decades. Give one real example from your own life or from the Guatemala you live in, and name the lesson's key words: conquest, expedition, alliance. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
