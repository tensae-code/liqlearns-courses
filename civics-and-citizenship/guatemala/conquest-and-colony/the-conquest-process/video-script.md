# Video script — The Conquest Process

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The conquest of America was a process, not one battle: expeditions, alliances, wars, disease and the fall of peoples' capitals, region by region, over decades.

After the first landings came expeditions into the mainland. The conquerors advanced with steel, horses and firearms, made alliances with peoples who were enemies of the great powers, and were helped terribly by new diseases. One by one the capitals fell, and Spanish rule was imposed over the peoples of America.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
