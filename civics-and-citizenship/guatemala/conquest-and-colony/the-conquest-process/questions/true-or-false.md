# The Conquest Process — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The conquest proceeded by expeditions, alliances, war and disease, region by region, over decades.
**Answer: True**

**2.** America was conquered in a single battle in 1492.
**Answer: False** — The conquest proceeded by expeditions, alliances, war and disease, region by region, over decades.
