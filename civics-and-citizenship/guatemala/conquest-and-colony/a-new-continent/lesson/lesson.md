# A New Continent — America

## Do it first

Set out for a shop by a new street and discover a place you never knew existed. The European voyages were like that, at the scale of an ocean. This lesson tells why they sailed — and what they met.

## Sub lesson 1 — Why they sailed

Europe wanted the goods of Asia — spices, silk, gold — by a cheaper, safer route than the blocked land paths. Kings and merchants backed sailors willing to cross unknown seas to find that route.

## Sub lesson 2 — The meeting

What the sailors met was not an empty land but a continent of peoples, cities and cultures — new to the Europeans, ancient to those who lived there. Everything in this title follows from that meeting of worlds.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Europeans launched their explorations seeking new routes to the riches of the East — and met a continent that was new to them: America, already full of peoples with their own worlds. Give one real example from your own life or from the Guatemala you live in, and name the lesson's key words: exploration, route, meeting of worlds. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
