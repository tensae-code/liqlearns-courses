# Video script — A New Continent — America

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Europeans launched their explorations seeking new routes to the riches of the East — and met a continent that was new to them: America, already full of peoples with their own worlds.

Europe wanted the goods of Asia — spices, silk, gold — by a cheaper, safer route than the blocked land paths. Kings and merchants backed sailors willing to cross unknown seas to find that route.

What the sailors met was not an empty land but a continent of peoples, cities and cultures — new to the Europeans, ancient to those who lived there. Everything in this title follows from that meeting of worlds.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
