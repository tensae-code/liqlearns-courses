# Hands-on — A New Continent — America

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Set out for a shop by a new street and discover a place you never knew existed. The European voyages were like that, at the scale of an ocean. This lesson tells why they sailed — and what they met.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Europeans launched their explorations seeking new routes to the riches of the East — and met a continent that was new to them: America, already full of peoples with their own worlds. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the community life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
