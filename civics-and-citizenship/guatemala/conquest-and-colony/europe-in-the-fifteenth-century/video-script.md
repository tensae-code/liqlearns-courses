# Video script — Europe in the Fifteenth Century

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
In the 1400s Europe was changing: old certainties loosened, trade and cities grew, and the Renaissance — its great cultural movement — revived learning and art; out of that restless Europe the great voyages came.

Fifteenth-century Europe was a world of kingdoms and growing trade, hungry for the spices and goods of the East and pressed by the powers that blocked the old routes. That hunger, with new ships and knowledge, pushed Europeans toward the sea.

The Renaissance was Europe's cultural awakening: a rebirth of learning, art and curiosity about the world, looking back to Greece and Rome and forward to new knowledge. It formed the minds that would question, measure and explore.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
