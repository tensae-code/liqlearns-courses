# Europe in the Fifteenth Century — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Europe in the 1400s was hungry for eastern trade and pressed toward the sea.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Europe in the 1400s was still and unchanged, with no interest in the wider world.
**Answer: False** — Europe in the 1400s was hungry for eastern trade and pressed toward the sea.
