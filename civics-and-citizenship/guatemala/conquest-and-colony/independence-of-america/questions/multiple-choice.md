# Independence of America — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What did the Acta de Independencia of 1823 found?
a) The Provincias Unidas del Centro de América  b) The Spanish viceroyalty  c) The United States  d) The Inca empire restored
**Answer: a) The Provincias Unidas del Centro de América** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What did independence largely leave standing?
a) Land in few hands and old inequalities  b) Spanish rule itself  c) All colonial laws word for word  d) Nothing — everything changed
**Answer: a) Land in few hands and old inequalities** — That is what this lesson teaches.
