# Independence of America — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What does this lesson teach about the independences?
**Answer:** Each American region won independence in its own process — Haiti, South America, Brazil and the rest.

**2.** What does this lesson teach about what independence changed — and what it left?
**Answer:** Independence changed governments but left land, inequality and the peoples' place largely unchanged.
