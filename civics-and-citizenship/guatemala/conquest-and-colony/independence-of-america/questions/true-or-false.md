# Independence of America — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Each American region won independence in its own process — Haiti, South America, Brazil and the rest.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Independence instantly made everyone in America equal and free.
**Answer: False** — Each American region won independence in its own process — Haiti, South America, Brazil and the rest.
