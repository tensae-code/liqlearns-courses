# Video script — Consequences of Colonisation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Colonisation changed America in every sphere — land, work, government, population — and imposed its religion on the peoples of the New Continent; those consequences are still being lived and answered today.

Colonisation's consequences ran through everything: the peoples' lands and labour were taken, their populations fell terribly, their governments were replaced, their economies were bent to serve Spain. No sphere of American life stood outside it.

Among the deepest consequences was religion: the colonisers imposed their faith on the peoples of the New Continent, building churches over sacred places and forbidding the old beliefs — which survived, hidden and mixed, inside the new.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
