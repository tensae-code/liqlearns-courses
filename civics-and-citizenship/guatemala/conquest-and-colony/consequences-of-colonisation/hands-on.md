# Hands-on — Consequences of Colonisation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Drop a stone in water and watch the rings reach the edge. Colonisation was the stone; this lesson traces the rings, sphere by sphere.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Colonisation changed America in every sphere — land, work, government, population — and imposed its religion on the peoples of the New Continent; those consequences are still being lived and answered today. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the community life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
