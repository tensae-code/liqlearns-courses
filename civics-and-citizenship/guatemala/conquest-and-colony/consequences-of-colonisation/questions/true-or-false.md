# Consequences of Colonisation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Colonisation's consequences touched land, labour, population, government and economy — every sphere.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Colonisation changed only who collected the taxes.
**Answer: False** — Colonisation's consequences touched land, labour, population, government and economy — every sphere.
