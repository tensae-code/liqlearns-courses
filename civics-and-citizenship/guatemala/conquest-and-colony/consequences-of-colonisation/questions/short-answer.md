# Consequences of Colonisation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What does this lesson teach about in every sphere?
**Answer:** Colonisation's consequences touched land, labour, population, government and economy — every sphere.

**2.** What does this lesson teach about the religious consequence?
**Answer:** Colonisation imposed the colonisers' religion on America's peoples, whose own beliefs survived within and beneath it.
