# Consequences of Colonisation

## Do it first

Drop a stone in water and watch the rings reach the edge. Colonisation was the stone; this lesson traces the rings, sphere by sphere.

## Sub lesson 1 — In every sphere

Colonisation's consequences ran through everything: the peoples' lands and labour were taken, their populations fell terribly, their governments were replaced, their economies were bent to serve Spain. No sphere of American life stood outside it.

## Sub lesson 2 — The religious consequence

Among the deepest consequences was religion: the colonisers imposed their faith on the peoples of the New Continent, building churches over sacred places and forbidding the old beliefs — which survived, hidden and mixed, inside the new.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Colonisation changed America in every sphere — land, work, government, population — and imposed its religion on the peoples of the New Continent; those consequences are still being lived and answered today. Give one real example from your own life or from the Guatemala you live in, and name the lesson's key words: consequence, imposition, sphere. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
