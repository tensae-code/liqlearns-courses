# Video script — The Colonial Era

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
For three centuries America lived under colonial rule: power and wealth drew the colonisers, and systems like the mita put indigenous people at the service of the Spaniards' mines and works.

The colonial era is the long period when Spain ruled its American lands as colonies. What drew and held the colonisers was power and wealth — silver, gold, land and labour — drawn out of the colonies for the crown and the settlers.

Among the systems of forced labour stood the mita: turns of indigenous people obliged to work for the Spaniards, above all in the mines. It shows the colony's underside — wealth for some, built on the forced work of the peoples.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
