# The Colonial Era — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What does this lesson teach about power and wealth?
**Answer:** The colonial era was three centuries of Spanish rule, drawn by power and the colonies' wealth.

**2.** What does this lesson teach about the mita?
**Answer:** The mita was forced turns of indigenous labour, above all in the mines, for the Spaniards.
