# Hands-on — The Colonial Era

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Imagine your town ruled for 300 years by officials sent from across the sea, and its people made to work for them. That was the colonial era — this lesson opens it.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: For three centuries America lived under colonial rule: power and wealth drew the colonisers, and systems like the mita put indigenous people at the service of the Spaniards' mines and works. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the community life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
