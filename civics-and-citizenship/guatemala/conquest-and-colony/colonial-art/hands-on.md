# Hands-on — Colonial Art and Music

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Listen for a church bell or a traditional song: its style crossed an ocean, but local hands made it ours. This lesson is about colonial art and music.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Across the three colonial centuries America made its own art — churches, painting, sculpture and music like that of Gaspar Fernández — European forms filled with American hands, materials and feeling. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the community life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
