# Video script — Colonial Art and Music

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Across the three colonial centuries America made its own art — churches, painting, sculpture and music like that of Gaspar Fernández — European forms filled with American hands, materials and feeling.

Colonial art filled the churches and cities Spain built: painting, sculpture and architecture in European styles, executed largely by indigenous and mestizo artists whose hands and eyes changed what they made into something American.

Music sounded in every colonial church and festival. Gaspar Fernández, composer and chapel master, stands as a name for the era's music — European sacred forms composed and performed in America, in America's cathedrals.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
