# Video script — Colonial Cities of America

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
America's colonial cities share a pattern — a central plaza with church and government around it, streets in a grid — laid over older worlds: Cuzco, the Incas' political and ceremonial capital, became a colonial city on Inca foundations.

Colonial cities across America repeat one pattern: the plaza at the centre, the church and the seats of government on its sides, streets laid in a grid, and the powerful living nearest the centre. The pattern itself taught who ruled.

Cuzco was the Incas' political and ceremonial capital long before Spain. The colonial city was built over and from it — Inca walls carrying colonial houses — so one city holds both worlds in its stones.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
