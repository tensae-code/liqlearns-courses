# Video script — Columbus's Voyages — and the Exchange

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Cristóbal Colón crossed the Atlantic seeking another route to Asia and reached America instead; his voyages opened an exchange of products in both directions that changed eating and farming on both sides of the ocean.

Colón sailed west for Spain in 1492, sure he would reach Asia by sea. He reached the islands and coasts of America instead, and returned with the news; further voyages followed, and Europe understood a whole continent stood in the way of its route.

The meeting moved products both ways: from America went maize, potato, tomato, cacao and more; from Europe came wheat, horses, cattle and other animals and plants. Both worlds' tables and fields were changed forever.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
