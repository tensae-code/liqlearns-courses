# Columbus's Voyages — and the Exchange

## Do it first

Name one food your family eats that grows here, and one that came from far away long ago. This lesson is about the voyages that started that worldwide exchange.

## Sub lesson 1 — The voyages

Colón sailed west for Spain in 1492, sure he would reach Asia by sea. He reached the islands and coasts of America instead, and returned with the news; further voyages followed, and Europe understood a whole continent stood in the way of its route.

## Sub lesson 2 — Products exchanged

The meeting moved products both ways: from America went maize, potato, tomato, cacao and more; from Europe came wheat, horses, cattle and other animals and plants. Both worlds' tables and fields were changed forever.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Cristóbal Colón crossed the Atlantic seeking another route to Asia and reached America instead; his voyages opened an exchange of products in both directions that changed eating and farming on both sides of the ocean. Give one real example from your own life or from the Guatemala you live in, and name the lesson's key words: Cristóbal Colón, exchange, cacao. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
