# Columbus's Voyages — and the Exchange — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Where did Columbus think his westward route would take him?
a) To Asia  b) To America, which he knew  c) To Africa  d) To the South Pole
**Answer: a) To Asia** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which product travelled from America to Europe?
a) Maize (and potato, tomato, cacao)  b) Wheat  c) Horses  d) Cattle
**Answer: a) Maize (and potato, tomato, cacao)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
