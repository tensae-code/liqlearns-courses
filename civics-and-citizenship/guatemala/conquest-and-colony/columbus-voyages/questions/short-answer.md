# Columbus's Voyages — and the Exchange — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What does this lesson teach about the voyages?
**Answer:** Columbus sailed west in 1492 seeking Asia and reached America; more voyages followed.

**2.** What does this lesson teach about products exchanged?
**Answer:** The exchange carried America's maize, potato and cacao to Europe, and Europe's wheat and animals to America.
