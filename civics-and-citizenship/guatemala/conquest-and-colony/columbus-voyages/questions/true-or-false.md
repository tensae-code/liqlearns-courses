# Columbus's Voyages — and the Exchange — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Columbus sailed west in 1492 seeking Asia and reached America; more voyages followed.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Columbus set out knowing America was there and aiming for it.
**Answer: False** — Columbus sailed west in 1492 seeking Asia and reached America; more voyages followed.
