# Hands-on — The Middle Ages

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Between the ancient world and ours stands a long middle age. This lesson crosses it — invasions, an eastern empire, and a new faith.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: The Middle Ages are the thousand years between Rome's fall and the modern age: peoples invaded and remade Europe, the Byzantine Empire kept Rome's east alive, and Islam arose and spread a civilisation of its own. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the community life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
