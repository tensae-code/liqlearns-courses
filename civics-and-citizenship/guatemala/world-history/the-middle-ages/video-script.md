# Video script — The Middle Ages

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The Middle Ages are the thousand years between Rome's fall and the modern age: peoples invaded and remade Europe, the Byzantine Empire kept Rome's east alive, and Islam arose and spread a civilisation of its own.

As Rome's western power fell, peoples the Romans called invaders moved across Europe and founded the kingdoms that became its nations. In the east, the Byzantine Empire — Rome's continuation, centred on Constantinople — preserved Roman law and learning for a thousand years.

In Arabia arose Islam, a faith that spread with extraordinary speed across the Middle East, North Africa and into Spain — building a civilisation of cities, science and trade that medieval Europe both fought and learned from.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
