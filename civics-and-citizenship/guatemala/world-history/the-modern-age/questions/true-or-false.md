# The Modern Age — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Modern Age's spirit: Renaissance and Humanism, multiplied by the printing press.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Books were common and cheap for everyone before the printing press.
**Answer: False** — The Modern Age's spirit: Renaissance and Humanism, multiplied by the printing press.
