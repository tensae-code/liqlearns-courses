# Video script — The Contemporary Age

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The Contemporary Age — our own — opens with the French Revolution's blow for liberty and equality, and runs through the nineteenth century's new ideas: liberalism, socialism and a sociocultural world remade by industry and education.

The French Revolution overthrew the old order in France in the name of liberty, equality and fraternity — proclaiming the rights of man and the citizen. Its ideas crossed every border and mark the opening of the Contemporary Age.

Europe's nineteenth century argued over new political and economic ideas — liberalism's freedoms and markets, socialism's answer in the name of workers and equality — while industry, science and culture (its sociocultural development) remade daily life.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
