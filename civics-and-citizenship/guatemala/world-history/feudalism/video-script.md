# Video script — Feudalism

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
When Rome's central power fell, Europe organised itself as feudalism: land held in exchange for service, lords and vassals bound by vassalage, and serfs working the land as the system's organising element.

Feudalism organised society around land and loyalty: a lord granted land (a fief) to a vassal, who owed service and fidelity in return — that bond is vassalage. At the base, serfs worked the lord's land, bound to it, and their labour — serfdom — was the element that organised the whole system.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
