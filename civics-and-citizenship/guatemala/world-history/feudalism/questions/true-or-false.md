# Feudalism — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Feudalism bound lords and vassals by vassalage over land, with serfs' labour as its organising base.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Feudalism was a strong central government ruling Europe from one capital.
**Answer: False** — Feudalism bound lords and vassals by vassalage over land, with serfs' labour as its organising base.
