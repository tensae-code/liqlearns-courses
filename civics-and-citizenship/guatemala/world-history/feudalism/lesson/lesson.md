# Feudalism

## Do it first

No king can protect your village; the local lord can — for a price paid in loyalty and work. That bargain, multiplied across Europe, is this lesson: feudalism.

## Sub lesson 1 — The feudal system

Feudalism organised society around land and loyalty: a lord granted land (a fief) to a vassal, who owed service and fidelity in return — that bond is vassalage. At the base, serfs worked the lord's land, bound to it, and their labour — serfdom — was the element that organised the whole system.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: When Rome's central power fell, Europe organised itself as feudalism: land held in exchange for service, lords and vassals bound by vassalage, and serfs working the land as the system's organising element. Give one real example from your own life or from the Guatemala you live in, and name the lesson's key words: feudalism, vassalage, serf. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
