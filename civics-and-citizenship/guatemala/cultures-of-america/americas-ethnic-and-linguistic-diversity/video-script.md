# Video script — America's Ethnic and Linguistic Diversity

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
America is a continent of many peoples and many tongues: indigenous peoples, descendants of Europeans and Africans, and mixtures of all — speaking hundreds of languages beside the colonial ones.

America's diversity is ethnic and linguistic at once: peoples with their own territories and identities, and languages by the hundred — the original tongues, the languages brought from Europe and Africa, and new mixtures. This diversity is the continent's wealth and its standing task of respect.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
