# America's Ethnic and Linguistic Diversity — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** America holds many peoples and hundreds of languages — a diversity to know and respect.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Everyone in America speaks one language and belongs to one people.
**Answer: False** — America holds many peoples and hundreds of languages — a diversity to know and respect.
