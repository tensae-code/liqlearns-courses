# America's Ethnic and Linguistic Diversity

## Do it first

Count the languages spoken in your own classroom or street. Now multiply that by a continent. This lesson celebrates America's diversity of peoples and languages.

## Sub lesson 1 — Many peoples, many languages

America's diversity is ethnic and linguistic at once: peoples with their own territories and identities, and languages by the hundred — the original tongues, the languages brought from Europe and Africa, and new mixtures. This diversity is the continent's wealth and its standing task of respect.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: America is a continent of many peoples and many tongues: indigenous peoples, descendants of Europeans and Africans, and mixtures of all — speaking hundreds of languages beside the colonial ones. Give one real example from your own life or from the Guatemala you live in, and name the lesson's key words: ethnic diversity, linguistic diversity, indigenous peoples. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
