# Hands-on — American Expressions

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name one song, dish or celebration that could only come from your region. This lesson is about those expressions across America — and why the world protects them.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: America's cultural expressions — its music, dances, foods, crafts and celebrations — are the living identity of its peoples, and states and international organisations protect them: Guatemala ratified UNESCO's protection in 2006. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the community life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
