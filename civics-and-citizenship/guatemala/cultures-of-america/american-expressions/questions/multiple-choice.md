# American Expressions — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What do America's cultural expressions carry?
a) Each people's history and identity  b) Nothing important  c) Only entertainment for tourists  d) The government's orders
**Answer: a) Each people's history and identity** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** When did Guatemala ratify UNESCO's heritage safeguarding?
a) In 2006  b) In 1492  c) In 1821  d) It never did
**Answer: a) In 2006** — That is what this lesson teaches.
