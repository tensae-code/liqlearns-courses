# American Expressions — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** America's cultural expressions carry each people's history and identity, kept alive by their communities.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Cultural expressions are just parties, with no history or identity in them.
**Answer: False** — America's cultural expressions carry each people's history and identity, kept alive by their communities.
