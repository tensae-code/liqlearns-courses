# Hands-on — Cultural Activities and Institutions

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name the biggest celebration of your town's year. Who organises it, and who helps pay for it? This lesson is about celebrations — and the institutions behind them.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: A region's cultural activities — its events, festivals and celebrations — keep its culture alive in public, and institutions that promote culture, like Guatemala's Ministerio de Cultura y Deportes, exist to support them. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the community life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
