# Video script — Cultural Activities and Institutions

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A region's cultural activities — its events, festivals and celebrations — keep its culture alive in public, and institutions that promote culture, like Guatemala's Ministerio de Cultura y Deportes, exist to support them.

Cultural activities are a region's living calendar: patronal festivals, dances, fairs, concerts and commemorations where a community performs who it is. They teach the young, gather the old, and carry identity from year to year.

Culture also has institutions: Guatemala's Ministerio de Cultura y Deportes is the state body charged with promoting culture and sport — supporting activities, protecting heritage and opening culture to all.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
