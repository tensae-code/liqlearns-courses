# Cultural Activities and Institutions

## Do it first

Name the biggest celebration of your town's year. Who organises it, and who helps pay for it? This lesson is about celebrations — and the institutions behind them.

## Sub lesson 1 — Cultural activities

Cultural activities are a region's living calendar: patronal festivals, dances, fairs, concerts and commemorations where a community performs who it is. They teach the young, gather the old, and carry identity from year to year.

## Sub lesson 2 — Institutions that promote culture

Culture also has institutions: Guatemala's Ministerio de Cultura y Deportes is the state body charged with promoting culture and sport — supporting activities, protecting heritage and opening culture to all.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: A region's cultural activities — its events, festivals and celebrations — keep its culture alive in public, and institutions that promote culture, like Guatemala's Ministerio de Cultura y Deportes, exist to support them. Give one real example from your own life or from the Guatemala you live in, and name the lesson's key words: cultural activity, institution (cultural), heritage. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
