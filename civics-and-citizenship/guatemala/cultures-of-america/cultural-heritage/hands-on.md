# Hands-on — Cultural Heritage

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Your grandmother's recipe exists nowhere on paper, yet it is an inheritance. This lesson widens that idea to whole peoples: cultural heritage, tangible and intangible.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Cultural heritage is what we received from the past and must hand on: world heritage in UNESCO's sense — places and works of all humanity — and intangible heritage that cannot be touched: theatre, traditions, living practices. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the community life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
