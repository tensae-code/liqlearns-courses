# Video script — Cultural Heritage

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Cultural heritage is what we received from the past and must hand on: world heritage in UNESCO's sense — places and works of all humanity — and intangible heritage that cannot be touched: theatre, traditions, living practices.

Patrimonio cultural is the inheritance of the past: monuments, sites, works and knowledge received from those before us. UNESCO's world heritage names the places and works that belong, in value, to all humanity — and charges every country with protecting its share.

Not all heritage can be touched. Intangible cultural heritage lives in practice: theatre and dance, festivals, crafts, oral traditions — carried in people, not in stone, and lost the day a community stops practising them.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
