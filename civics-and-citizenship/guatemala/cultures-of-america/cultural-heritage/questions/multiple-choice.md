# Cultural Heritage — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Intangible heritage differs from other heritage because…
a) It lives in practices, not objects — theatre, traditions, crafts  b) It is worth less  c) It is always written in books  d) It cannot be lost
**Answer: a) It lives in practices, not objects — theatre, traditions, crafts** — That is what this lesson teaches.
