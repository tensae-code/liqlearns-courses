# Cultural Heritage — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What does this lesson teach about heritage of humanity?
**Answer:** Cultural heritage is what the past hands us; world heritage is its part belonging to all humanity.

**2.** What does this lesson teach about intangible heritage?
**Answer:** Intangible heritage — theatre, traditions, living practices — lives in people and dies if unpractised.
