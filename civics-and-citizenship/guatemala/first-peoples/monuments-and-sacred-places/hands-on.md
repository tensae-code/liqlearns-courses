# Hands-on — Monuments and Sacred Places

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name one old or special place near you that everyone agrees must not be damaged. This lesson is about the far older places America's first peoples left, and why they matter.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: The ancient peoples left monuments, great architecture and sacred places — Teotihuacan among them — and that heritage is ours to know and protect. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the community life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
