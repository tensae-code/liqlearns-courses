# Monuments and Sacred Places

## Do it first

Name one old or special place near you that everyone agrees must not be damaged. This lesson is about the far older places America's first peoples left, and why they matter.

## Sub lesson 1 — What they built

America's ancient peoples raised pyramids, temples, plazas and whole ceremonial cities — Teotihuacan, with its great pyramids, is one of the most famous. These monuments show the knowledge, organisation and faith of the peoples who built them.

## Sub lesson 2 — Heritage to protect

A sacred place is where a people meets what it holds holy; a monument is its memory in stone. Protecting them protects the history of the whole continent — once destroyed, they cannot be rebuilt as they were.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The ancient peoples left monuments, great architecture and sacred places — Teotihuacan among them — and that heritage is ours to know and protect. Give one real example from your own life or from the Guatemala you live in, and name the lesson's key words: monument, sacred place, heritage. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
