# Video script — Monuments and Sacred Places

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The ancient peoples left monuments, great architecture and sacred places — Teotihuacan among them — and that heritage is ours to know and protect.

America's ancient peoples raised pyramids, temples, plazas and whole ceremonial cities — Teotihuacan, with its great pyramids, is one of the most famous. These monuments show the knowledge, organisation and faith of the peoples who built them.

A sacred place is where a people meets what it holds holy; a monument is its memory in stone. Protecting them protects the history of the whole continent — once destroyed, they cannot be rebuilt as they were.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
