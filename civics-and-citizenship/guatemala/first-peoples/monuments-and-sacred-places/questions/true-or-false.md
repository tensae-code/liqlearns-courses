# Monuments and Sacred Places — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The ancient peoples left pyramids, temples and ceremonial cities — Teotihuacan among the most famous.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Old monuments are just piles of stone with nothing to teach.
**Answer: False** — The ancient peoples left pyramids, temples and ceremonial cities — Teotihuacan among the most famous.
