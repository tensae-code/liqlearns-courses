# Monuments and Sacred Places — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Teotihuacan is remembered in this lesson as…
a) One of the ancient peoples' great ceremonial cities  b) A Spanish colonial port  c) A modern capital  d) A natural volcano
**Answer: a) One of the ancient peoples' great ceremonial cities** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Why must monuments and sacred places be protected?
a) Once destroyed, the history they carry cannot be rebuilt  b) So tourists only can see them  c) Because they are made of gold  d) They need no protection
**Answer: a) Once destroyed, the history they carry cannot be rebuilt** — That is what this lesson teaches.
