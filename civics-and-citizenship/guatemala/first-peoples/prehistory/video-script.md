The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Prehistory runs from the first humans, some 200,000 years ago, to about 3,500 BC: the Palaeolithic of nomadic hunter-gatherers, the Neolithic, when people settled and farmed, and the Age of Metals that closed it.

The Palaeolithic was the long age of nomads: small bands hunting and gathering, moving with the seasons. The Neolithic changed everything — sedentarisation: people settled in one place, farmed, kept animals and built villages. Last came the Age of Metals, when copper, bronze and iron tools replaced stone and the prehistoric world approached history.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
