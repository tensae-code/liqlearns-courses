# Prehistory — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Prehistory runs Palaeolithic nomads, then Neolithic settlement and farming, then the Age of Metals.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Prehistory is a short time just before our grandparents.
**Answer: False** — Prehistory runs Palaeolithic nomads, then Neolithic settlement and farming, then the Age of Metals.
