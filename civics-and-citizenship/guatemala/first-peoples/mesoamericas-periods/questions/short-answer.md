# Mesoamerica's Periods — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What does this lesson teach about preclassic and classic?
**Answer:** The Preclassic raised the first centres and the Olmecs; the Classic was the age of great cities like Tikal.

**2.** What does this lesson teach about the postclassic?
**Answer:** The Postclassic followed the Classic decline and raised new powers, among them the Aztecs.
