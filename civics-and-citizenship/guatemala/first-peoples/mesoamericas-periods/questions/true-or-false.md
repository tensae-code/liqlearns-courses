# Mesoamerica's Periods — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Preclassic raised the first centres and the Olmecs; the Classic was the age of great cities like Tikal.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The Aztecs were the first people of Mesoamerica.
**Answer: False** — The Preclassic raised the first centres and the Olmecs; the Classic was the age of great cities like Tikal.
