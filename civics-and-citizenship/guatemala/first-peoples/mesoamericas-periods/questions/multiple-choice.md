# Mesoamerica's Periods — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which people raised the earliest great culture of the Preclassic?
a) The Olmecs  b) The Aztecs  c) The Incas  d) The Spanish
**Answer: a) The Olmecs** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Tikal, of the Great Jaguar, belongs to which period?
a) The Classic  b) The Preclassic  c) The Postclassic  d) The colonial era
**Answer: a) The Classic** — That is what this lesson teaches.
