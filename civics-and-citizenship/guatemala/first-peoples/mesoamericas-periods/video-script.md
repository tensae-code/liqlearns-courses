# Video script — Mesoamerica's Periods

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Mesoamerican history is told in periods: the Preclassic, when farming peoples and the Olmecs rose; the Classic, the age of great cities like Tikal of the Great Jaguar; and the Postclassic, early and late, down to the Aztecs.

In the Preclassic period, farming villages grew into the first ceremonial centres, and the Olmecs raised the earliest great culture. In the Classic period that followed, city-states flourished — Tikal, remembered by its Great Jaguar, among the greatest Maya cities.

The Postclassic, early and late, came after the Classic cities declined: new powers rose, among them the Aztecs in Mexico, and it was into this late world that the Spanish arrived.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
