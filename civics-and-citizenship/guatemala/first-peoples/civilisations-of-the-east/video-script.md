# Video script — Civilisations of the East

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The East raised its own great civilisations — India, with its Hinduism among the world's oldest living religions, and ancient China, whose commerce and culture influenced all Asia under its early dynasties.

India's civilisation grew along its great rivers, with cities, trade and deep religious life. Hinduism, among the oldest religions still lived, shaped its thought about life, duty and the divine across thousands of years.

Ancient China built a lasting civilisation of farming, writing, craft and rule by dynasties. Under its second dynasty its commercial and cultural influence spread — goods, techniques and ideas travelling the roads of Asia.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
