# Hands-on — Civilisations of the East

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
The first civilisations were not only in the west. Turn the globe the other way: this lesson visits India and China.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: The East raised its own great civilisations — India, with its Hinduism among the world's oldest living religions, and ancient China, whose commerce and culture influenced all Asia under its early dynasties. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the community life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
