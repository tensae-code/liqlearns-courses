# Civilisations of the East

## Do it first

The first civilisations were not only in the west. Turn the globe the other way: this lesson visits India and China.

## Sub lesson 1 — India and Hinduism

India's civilisation grew along its great rivers, with cities, trade and deep religious life. Hinduism, among the oldest religions still lived, shaped its thought about life, duty and the divine across thousands of years.

## Sub lesson 2 — Ancient China

Ancient China built a lasting civilisation of farming, writing, craft and rule by dynasties. Under its second dynasty its commercial and cultural influence spread — goods, techniques and ideas travelling the roads of Asia.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The East raised its own great civilisations — India, with its Hinduism among the world's oldest living religions, and ancient China, whose commerce and culture influenced all Asia under its early dynasties. Give one real example from your own life or from the Guatemala you live in, and name the lesson's key words: Hinduism, dynasty, the East (Oriente). Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
