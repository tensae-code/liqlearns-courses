# Video script — Mesoamerica

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Mesoamerica is a shared cultural territory — southern and central Mexico, Guatemala and their neighbours — whose peoples held much in common: farming, calendars, cities and beliefs, while each people kept its own language and ways.

Mesoamerica is not a country: it is the wide territory where many peoples shared one cultural base — maize farming, great stone cities, calendars, markets and similar beliefs about the world.

The peoples of Mesoamerica were alike and different at once: they traded, borrowed ideas and built in similar ways, yet each people — Maya, and their neighbours — kept its own language, rulers and customs.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
