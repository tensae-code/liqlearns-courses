# Mesoamerica — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What does this lesson teach about a shared territory?
**Answer:** Mesoamerica is the territory where many peoples shared one cultural base — farming, cities, calendars and beliefs.

**2.** What does this lesson teach about common and apart?
**Answer:** Mesoamerica's peoples shared a culture yet each kept its own language, rulers and customs.
