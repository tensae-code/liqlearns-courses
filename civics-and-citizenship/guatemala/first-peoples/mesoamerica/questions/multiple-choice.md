# Mesoamerica — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is Mesoamerica?
a) A shared cultural territory, not a single country  b) One single empire with one king  c) A modern Guatemalan department  d) An ocean beside America
**Answer: a) A shared cultural territory, not a single country** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What did Mesoamerica's peoples hold in common?
a) Farming, cities, calendars, markets and beliefs  b) One single language only  c) Nothing at all  d) European customs
**Answer: a) Farming, cities, calendars, markets and beliefs** — That is what this lesson teaches.
