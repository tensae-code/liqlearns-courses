# Hands-on — Mesoamerica

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name three things the towns around yours share — a food, a market day, a celebration — and one thing your town does differently. This lesson is about a whole region that shared a culture in that same way.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Mesoamerica is a shared cultural territory — southern and central Mexico, Guatemala and their neighbours — whose peoples held much in common: farming, calendars, cities and beliefs, while each people kept its own language and ways. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the community life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
