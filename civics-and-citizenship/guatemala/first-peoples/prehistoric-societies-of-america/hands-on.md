# Hands-on — Prehistoric Societies of America

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
List everything your family ate yesterday. Beside each item, write whether someone hunted or gathered it, grew it, or bought it. This lesson is about the peoples who lived by the first way only.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Before writing and cities, America's first peoples lived as hunter-gatherers: the Palaeolithic age of hunting and gathering, then the Mesolithic, when that life slowly began to change. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the community life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
