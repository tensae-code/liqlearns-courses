# Prehistoric Societies of America — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** How did Palaeolithic people get their food?
a) By hunting, fishing and gathering wild plants  b) By farming large fields  c) By buying it in markets  d) By herding cattle only
**Answer: a) By hunting, fishing and gathering wild plants** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the Mesolithic?
a) The transition between hunting-gathering and settled life  b) The age of metal cities  c) A modern century  d) The age of writing
**Answer: a) The transition between hunting-gathering and settled life** — That is what this lesson teaches.
