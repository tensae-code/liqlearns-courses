# Prehistoric Societies of America — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What does this lesson teach about palaeolithic life?
**Answer:** In the Palaeolithic, people lived by hunting, fishing and gathering, moving with the seasons.

**2.** What does this lesson teach about the mesolithic change?
**Answer:** The Mesolithic is the transition age between hunting-gathering life and settled farming life.
