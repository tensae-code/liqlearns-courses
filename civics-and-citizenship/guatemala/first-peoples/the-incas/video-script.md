# Video script — The Incas

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The Incas built a great people in the Andes, ruled from Cuzco; their own account tells that the Sun, Inti, and the Moon, Quilla, sent their children to found it.

Far south of Mesoamerica, in the Andes mountains, the Incas built one of America's greatest peoples, with Cuzco as their capital and centre. Their roads, terraces and organisation held a vast territory together.

The Incas told that Inti, the Sun, and Quilla, the Moon, sent their son and daughter to the earth to found Cuzco and teach people to live well. An origin account like this tells a people who they are and why their city is sacred.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
