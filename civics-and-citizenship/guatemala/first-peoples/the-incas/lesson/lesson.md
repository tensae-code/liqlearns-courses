# The Incas

## Do it first

Every family has a story of how it began. Ask for yours tonight. This lesson tells how the Incas explained their own beginning.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Inca people and Cuzco

Far south of Mesoamerica, in the Andes mountains, the Incas built one of America's greatest peoples, with Cuzco as their capital and centre. Their roads, terraces and organisation held a vast territory together.

## Sub lesson 2 — Their origin account

The Incas told that Inti, the Sun, and Quilla, the Moon, sent their son and daughter to the earth to found Cuzco and teach people to live well. An origin account like this tells a people who they are and why their city is sacred.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The Incas built a great people in the Andes, ruled from Cuzco; their own account tells that the Sun, Inti, and the Moon, Quilla, sent their children to found it. Give one real example from your own life or from the Guatemala you live in, and name the lesson's key words: Incas, Inti, Quilla. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
