# The Incas — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What was the Incas' capital?
a) Cuzco  b) Tikal  c) Madrid  d) Lake Titicaca's shore town only
**Answer: a) Cuzco** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** In the Incas' origin account, who sent their children to found the city?
a) Inti the Sun and Quilla the Moon  b) The Great Jaguar  c) The king of Spain  d) The Aztec emperor
**Answer: a) Inti the Sun and Quilla the Moon** — That is what this lesson teaches.
