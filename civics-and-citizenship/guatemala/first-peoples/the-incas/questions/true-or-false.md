# The Incas — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Incas built a great Andean people ruled from their capital, Cuzco.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The Incas lived in Mesoamerica beside the Maya.
**Answer: False** — The Incas built a great Andean people ruled from their capital, Cuzco.
