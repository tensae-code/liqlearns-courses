# Hands-on — The Incas

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Every family has a story of how it began. Ask for yours tonight. This lesson tells how the Incas explained their own beginning.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: The Incas built a great people in the Andes, ruled from Cuzco; their own account tells that the Sun, Inti, and the Moon, Quilla, sent their children to found it. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the community life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
