# Hands-on — The First Civilisations

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Why is your town where it is? Follow the water. The first civilisations did the same at the scale of history. This lesson visits them one by one.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: A civilisation is a society with cities, organisation and shared culture — and the first ones arose beside great rivers: Mesopotamia between its two rivers, Egypt under its Pharaoh, then the Creto-Mycenaean world, classical Greece, and Rome, republic then empire. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the community life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
