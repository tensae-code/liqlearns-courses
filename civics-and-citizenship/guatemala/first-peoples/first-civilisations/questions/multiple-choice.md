# The First Civilisations — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Where did the first civilisations arise?
a) Beside great rivers  b) On high snowy mountains  c) On desert islands  d) In the far north ice
**Answer: a) Beside great rivers** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** How did Rome's Republic differ from its Empire?
a) Power was shared among institutions in the Republic, concentrated in the Empire  b) The Republic had an emperor; the Empire had none  c) They were identical  d) The Empire was a village
**Answer: a) Power was shared among institutions in the Republic, concentrated in the Empire** — That is what this lesson teaches.
