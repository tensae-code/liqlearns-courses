# The First Civilisations — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What does this lesson teach about what a civilisation is?
**Answer:** A civilisation has cities, government, crafts and shared culture — and the first arose beside great rivers.

**2.** What does this lesson teach about rome — republic and empire?
**Answer:** Rome shared power among institutions in the Republic and concentrated it in the Empire.
