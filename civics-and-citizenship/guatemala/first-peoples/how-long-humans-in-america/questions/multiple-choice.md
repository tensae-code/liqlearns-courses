# How Long Humans Have Been in America — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** How do we know humans have been in America a very long time?
a) From dated remains such as tools, hearths and bones  b) From modern newspapers  c) From the names of capitals  d) We cannot know anything about it
**Answer: a) From dated remains such as tools, hearths and bones** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Who were America's first peoples descended from?
a) The people who reached America in remote times  b) The Spanish colonisers  c) Modern tourists  d) No one — they appeared suddenly
**Answer: a) The people who reached America in remote times** — That is what this lesson teaches.
