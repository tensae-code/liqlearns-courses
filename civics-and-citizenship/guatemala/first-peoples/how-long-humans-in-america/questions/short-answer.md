# How Long Humans Have Been in America — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What does this lesson teach about a very old arrival?
**Answer:** People reached America many thousands of years ago, long before its named empires.

**2.** What does this lesson teach about how we know?
**Answer:** We know humans are ancient in America from dated remains — tools, hearths, bones and settlements.
