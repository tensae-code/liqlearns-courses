# How Long Humans Have Been in America

## Do it first

Ask the oldest person you know how old your town or village is. Then think: how would anyone know about people who lived thousands of years before any writing? This lesson answers that.

## Sub lesson 1 — A very old arrival

The human species is far older than any American city. People spread across the world and reached America in remote times, long before the peoples and empires the history books name — and their descendants became America's first peoples.

## Sub lesson 2 — How we know

Antiquity is proved by evidence: stone tools, hearths, bones and buried settlements. Scientists study and date these remains, and each dated remain pushes the known arrival of humans in America further back or confirms it.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Human beings reached America many thousands of years ago: we know their antiquity from the remains they left — tools, bones and settlements that scientists date. Give one real example from your own life or from the Guatemala you live in, and name the lesson's key words: antiquity, remains, evidence. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
