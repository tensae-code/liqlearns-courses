# Hands-on — How Long Humans Have Been in America

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Ask the oldest person you know how old your town or village is. Then think: how would anyone know about people who lived thousands of years before any writing? This lesson answers that.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Human beings reached America many thousands of years ago: we know their antiquity from the remains they left — tools, bones and settlements that scientists date. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the community life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
