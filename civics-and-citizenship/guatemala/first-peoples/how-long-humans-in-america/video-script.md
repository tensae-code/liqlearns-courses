# Video script — How Long Humans Have Been in America

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Human beings reached America many thousands of years ago: we know their antiquity from the remains they left — tools, bones and settlements that scientists date.

The human species is far older than any American city. People spread across the world and reached America in remote times, long before the peoples and empires the history books name — and their descendants became America's first peoples.

Antiquity is proved by evidence: stone tools, hearths, bones and buried settlements. Scientists study and date these remains, and each dated remain pushes the known arrival of humans in America further back or confirms it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
