# Violence in the Family

## Do it first

Describe, without names, a home where people feel safe, and one thing that makes it safe. This lesson names the opposite — violence inside the family — and how family life is built without it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Recognising violence inside the family

Violencia intrafamiliar is violence inside the family: harm done by family members to family members, inside the home that should protect them.

The experience teaches pupils to recognise it — harm at home is still harm, and the closeness of family does not make it right.

## Sub lesson 2 — Building family life without it

Against family violence the course builds family life without it: homes where people are treated with the respect every person is owed, where conflicts are settled without harm.

The family's own values — the community values of Guatemala's peoples, learned at the start of this course — are the building materials: care, diligent work for one another, and protection of all that has life, beginning at home.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: violence inside the family can be recognised — and family life can be built without it. Give one real example from your own life or from the Guatemala you live in, and name the lesson's key words: violencia intrafamiliar, violence in the family, family life without violence, respect at home. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
