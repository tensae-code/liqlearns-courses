# Hands-on — Violence in the Family

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Describe, without names, a home where people feel safe, and one thing that makes it safe. This lesson names the opposite — violence inside the family — and how family life is built without it.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: violence inside the family can be recognised — and family life can be built without it. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the community life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
