# Lesson plan — Violence in the Family

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Guatemala · **Title:** Peace and Nonviolence · **Lesson:** Violence in the Family
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — Peace and Nonviolence (CURRICULUM.md).

**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).
**Footing:** teacher-guide evidence only — every placement for this lesson is marked TEACHER (see lesson/sources.md).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Recognising violence inside the family** — Violencia intrafamiliar is violence inside the family: harm done by family members to family members, inside the home that should protect them.
2. **Building family life without it** — Against family violence the course builds family life without it: homes where people are treated with the respect every person is owed, where conflicts are settled without harm.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: violence inside the family can be recognised — and family life can be built without it.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or town — or, for this course's lessons, from the school and community life they live in.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the learner's own school, family and community life, as the guides' learning experiences work.
