# Video script — Violence in the Family

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Violence inside the family can be recognised — and family life can be built without it.

Violencia intrafamiliar is violence inside the family: harm done by family members to family members, inside the home that should protect them.

The experience teaches pupils to recognise it — harm at home is still harm, and the closeness of family does not make it right.

Against family violence the course builds family life without it: homes where people are treated with the respect every person is owed, where conflicts are settled without harm.

The family's own values — the community values of Guatemala's peoples, learned at the start of this course — are the building materials: care, diligent work for one another, and protection of all that has life, beginning at home.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
