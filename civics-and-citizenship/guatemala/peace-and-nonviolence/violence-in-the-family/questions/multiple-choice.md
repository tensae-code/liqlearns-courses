# Violence in the Family — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is violencia intrafamiliar?
a) violence inside the family  b) violence between countries  c) violence only at school  d) a family celebration
**Answer: a) violence inside the family** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What makes family life without violence?
a) treating every person with respect and settling conflicts without harm  b) never speaking to each other  c) letting the strongest rule  d) hiding every conflict
**Answer: a) treating every person with respect and settling conflicts without harm** — That is what this lesson teaches.
