# Violence in the Family — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Where does violencia intrafamiliar happen, and between whom?
**Answer:** Inside the family, at home — harm done by family members to family members.

**2.** Name one building material of family life without violence from this course.
**Answer:** For example: the community values of Guatemala's peoples — care, diligent work for one another, protecting all that has life.
