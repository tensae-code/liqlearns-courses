# What Violence Is — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — What violence is
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Violence is harm done to people. The first learning experience of the básico course stops and names it plainly, before any of its particular forms: a pupil who can name violence can begin to refuse it. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — Forms and roots
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The experience works through violence's forms and its roots: where violence comes from, what feeds it, and how it grows when nobody names it. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 3 — The culture of peace as its opposite
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Against violence the guides set its opposite: a culture of peace — the way of living together that the whole básico course is named for: "Juntos construimos la paz" — together we build peace. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
