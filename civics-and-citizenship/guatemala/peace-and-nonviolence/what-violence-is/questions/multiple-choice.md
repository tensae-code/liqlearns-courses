# What Violence Is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the opposite of violence in the guides' teaching?
a) a culture of peace  b) a culture of silence  c) stronger punishment alone  d) ignoring it until it stops
**Answer: a) a culture of peace** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Why does the course name violence's roots, not only its acts?
a) because peace is built by changing what violence grows from  b) roots do not matter at all  c) only acts can ever be named  d) to excuse every violent act
**Answer: a) because peace is built by changing what violence grows from** — That is what this lesson teaches.
