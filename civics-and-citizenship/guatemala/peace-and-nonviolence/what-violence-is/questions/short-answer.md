# What Violence Is — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name two forms violence can take.
**Answer:** Any two, for example: acts that hurt the body; hurtful words; treating people badly or leaving them out.

**2.** What is a culture of peace?
**Answer:** A way of living together — values, habits and ways of treating people — that stands against violence, learned and practised.
