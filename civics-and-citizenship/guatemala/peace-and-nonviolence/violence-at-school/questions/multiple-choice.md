# Violence at School — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the school's answer to school violence, in this lesson?
a) coexistence and prevention  b) expelling everyone involved without a hearing  c) ignoring it  d) more violence to frighten pupils
**Answer: a) coexistence and prevention** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is violencia escolar?
a) violence at school — harm between the people of the school  b) a school holiday  c) a kind of school uniform  d) homework given at school
**Answer: a) violence at school — harm between the people of the school** — That is what this lesson teaches.
