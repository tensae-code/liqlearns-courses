# Hands-on — Violence at School

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write one thing that makes a school feel unsafe, and one thing that makes it feel safe. This lesson names school violence and the school's own answer to it.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: school violence is named and prevented — the school's answer is coexistence: living and learning together in peace. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the community life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
