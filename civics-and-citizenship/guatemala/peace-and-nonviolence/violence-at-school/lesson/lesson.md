# Violence at School

## Do it first

Write one thing that makes a school feel unsafe, and one thing that makes it feel safe. This lesson names school violence and the school's own answer to it.

Violencia escolar is violence at school: harm between the people of the school, in the place where pupils come to learn.

The school's answer, as the experience teaches it, is coexistence and prevention: a school that lives together in peace — convivencia — and stops violence before it grows, rather than only answering it after. Prevention and coexistence close the first básico guide's arc: from what violence is, through children, family and school, to a culture of peace practised where pupils spend their days.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: school violence is named and prevented — the school's answer is coexistence: living and learning together in peace. Give one real example from your own life or from the Guatemala you live in, and name the lesson's key words: violencia escolar, school violence, coexistence, convivencia, prevention. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
