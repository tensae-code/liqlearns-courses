# Video script — Violence at School

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
School violence is named and prevented — the school's answer is coexistence: living and learning together in peace.

Violencia escolar is violence at school: harm between the people of the school, in the place where pupils come to learn.

The school's answer, as the experience teaches it, is coexistence and prevention: a school that lives together in peace — convivencia — and stops violence before it grows, rather than only answering it after. Prevention and coexistence close the first básico guide's arc: from what violence is, through children, family and school, to a culture of peace practised where pupils spend their days.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
