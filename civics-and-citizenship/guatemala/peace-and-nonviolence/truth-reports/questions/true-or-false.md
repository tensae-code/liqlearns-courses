# Truth Reports After Armed Conflict — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Truth reports establish officially what happened in an armed conflict, so the past is named and not repeated.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Peace after war requires never speaking of what happened.
**Answer: False** — Truth reports establish officially what happened in an armed conflict, so the past is named and not repeated.
