# Truth Reports After Armed Conflict

## Do it first

After a long quarrel in a family, someone must say plainly what happened before peace can hold. Whole countries do the same. This lesson is about truth reports.

## Sub lesson 1 — Reckoning with the past

A truth report is an official investigation after armed conflict: gathering testimony and evidence to establish what happened — the killings, the disappeared, the causes — and publishing it for the whole society. Guatemala lived its armed conflict and produced its own truth reports, naming the suffering so it is not repeated.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Countries that lived armed conflict have sought the truth afterwards in official reports — truth reports that name what happened and who suffered — and Guatemala's own reckoning after its conflict stands among them. Give one real example from your own life or from the Guatemala you live in, and name the lesson's key words: truth report, armed conflict, testimony. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
