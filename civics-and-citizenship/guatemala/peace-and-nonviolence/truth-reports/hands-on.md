# Hands-on — Truth Reports After Armed Conflict

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
After a long quarrel in a family, someone must say plainly what happened before peace can hold. Whole countries do the same. This lesson is about truth reports.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Countries that lived armed conflict have sought the truth afterwards in official reports — truth reports that name what happened and who suffered — and Guatemala's own reckoning after its conflict stands among them. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the community life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
