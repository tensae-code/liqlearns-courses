# Video script — Truth Reports After Armed Conflict

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Countries that lived armed conflict have sought the truth afterwards in official reports — truth reports that name what happened and who suffered — and Guatemala's own reckoning after its conflict stands among them.

A truth report is an official investigation after armed conflict: gathering testimony and evidence to establish what happened — the killings, the disappeared, the causes — and publishing it for the whole society. Guatemala lived its armed conflict and produced its own truth reports, naming the suffering so it is not repeated.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
