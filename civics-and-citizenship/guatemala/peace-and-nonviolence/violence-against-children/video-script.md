# Video script — Violence against Children

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Violence against children is named and refused — and guatemala's cultural values are the answer the course builds against it.

The second learning experience names violencia infantil — violence against children: the harms children meet from the people and places around them.

A child who is hurt is not to blame for the harm. Naming the violence is the first step to stopping it, and the course teaches pupils to name it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
