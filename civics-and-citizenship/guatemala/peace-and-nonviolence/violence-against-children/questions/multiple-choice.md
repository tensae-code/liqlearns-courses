# Violence against Children — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** How does the course answer violence against children?
a) with the cultural values of Guatemala's peoples  b) by blaming the child  c) by keeping it secret  d) by answering harm with harm
**Answer: a) with the cultural values of Guatemala's peoples** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Who is to blame when a child is hurt by violence?
a) not the child — the harm is the wrong  b) always the child  c) no one at all  d) the child's friends
**Answer: a) not the child — the harm is the wrong** — That is what this lesson teaches.
