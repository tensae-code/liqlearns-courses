# Hands-on — Violence against Children

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write down one way a child can be hurt by the people around them, and one value from your own family's or people's way of life that protects children. This lesson puts the two side by side.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: violence against children is named and refused — and Guatemala's cultural values are the answer the course builds against it. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the community life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
