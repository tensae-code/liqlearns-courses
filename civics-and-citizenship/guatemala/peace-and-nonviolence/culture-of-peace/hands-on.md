# Hands-on — Culture of Peace

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Remember a quarrel that ended well. What did people do — shout more, or talk? This lesson is about the whole way of life that chooses the talking: the culture of peace.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: A culture of peace is built on values, attitudes and behaviours of respect for life and dignity — Guatemala builds it after 36 years of internal war; dialogue is its tool against violence, and peace itself is the value people aspire to most, lived where conflicts arise. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the community life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
