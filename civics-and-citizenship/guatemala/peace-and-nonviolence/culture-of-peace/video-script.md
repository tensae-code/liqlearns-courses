# Video script — Culture of Peace

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A culture of peace is built on values, attitudes and behaviours of respect for life and dignity — Guatemala builds it after 36 years of internal war; dialogue is its tool against violence, and peace itself is the value people aspire to most, lived where conflicts arise.

Guatemala lived 36 years of internal armed conflict. The culture of peace is the answer built afterwards: not only no fighting, but values, attitudes and behaviours — respect for life, for dignity, for others' rights — practised in family, school and community until peace becomes the culture.

Where violence appears, the culture of peace answers with dialogue: speaking and listening to solve conflicts in the family, at school and in the community, instead of blows or silence. Dialogue is the tool; the conflict is faced, named and worked through together.

The book returns to the culture of peace at its end: peace is the value people aspire to most, and it is not waited for — it is lived, in the places where conflicts arise, by people who practise its values daily.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
