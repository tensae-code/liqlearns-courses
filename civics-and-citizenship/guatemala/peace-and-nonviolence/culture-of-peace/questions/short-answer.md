# Culture of Peace — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What does this lesson teach about after the war, peace as culture?
**Answer:** The culture of peace is values, attitudes and behaviours of respect for life and dignity, built after Guatemala's 36-year war.

**2.** What does this lesson teach about peace lived at the book's close?
**Answer:** Peace is lived daily where conflicts arise; it is the value people aspire to most.
