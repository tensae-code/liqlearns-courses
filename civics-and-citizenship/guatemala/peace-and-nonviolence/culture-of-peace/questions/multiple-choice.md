# Culture of Peace — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The culture of peace is only the absence of fighting. True or false as the book teaches?
a) False — it is values, attitudes and behaviours of respect practised daily  b) True — nothing more is needed  c) False — it is only about armies  d) True — peace needs no values
**Answer: a) False — it is values, attitudes and behaviours of respect practised daily** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What tool does the culture of peace use where violence appears?
a) Dialogue  b) Silence  c) Revenge  d) Avoiding the person forever
**Answer: a) Dialogue** — That is what this lesson teaches.
