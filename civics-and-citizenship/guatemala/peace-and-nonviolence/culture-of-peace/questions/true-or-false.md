# Culture of Peace — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The culture of peace is values, attitudes and behaviours of respect for life and dignity, built after Guatemala's 36-year war.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Peace means only that nobody is fighting right now.
**Answer: False** — The culture of peace is values, attitudes and behaviours of respect for life and dignity, built after Guatemala's 36-year war.
