# Video script — Violence over Identity

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
No person may be harmed for who they are — violence against people because of sexual orientation or gender identity is named, and respect for every person is the answer.

The final experience of the final básico guide names violencia contra personas debido a orientaciones sexuales e identidad de género — violence against people because of their sexual orientation or gender identity: harm aimed at a person for who they are.

It closes the peace-building arc at the point where pupils are about to leave básico: the circle of protection, widened year by year, reaches every person.

The answer is the course's foundation, stated whole: respect for every person. The dignity the rights frame gives each person does not stop at identity — a person harmed for who they are is owed the same naming, refusal and protection as every person in every lesson before this one.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
