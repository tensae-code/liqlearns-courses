## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Violence against people because of sexual orientation or gender identity** — The final experience of the final básico guide names violencia contra personas debido a orientaciones sexuales e identidad de género — violence against people because of their sexual orientation or gender identity: harm aimed at a person for who they are.
2. **Respect for every person** — The answer is the course's foundation, stated whole: respect for every person. The dignity the rights frame gives each person does not stop at identity — a person harmed for who they are is owed the same naming, refusal and protection as every person in every lesson before this one.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: no person may be harmed for who they are — violence against people because of sexual orientation or gender identity is named, and respect for every person is the answer.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or town — or, for this course's lessons, from the school and community life they live in.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the learner's own school, family and community life, as the guides' learning experiences work.
