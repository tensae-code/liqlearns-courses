# Violence over Identity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** For what is the person harmed, in this lesson's violence?
a) for who they are — their sexual orientation or gender identity  b) for breaking a school rule  c) for their school marks  d) for arriving late
**Answer: a) for who they are — their sexual orientation or gender identity** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the course's answer, stated whole?
a) respect for every person  b) respect for some people only  c) silence about the harm  d) answering violence with violence
**Answer: a) respect for every person** — That is what this lesson teaches.
