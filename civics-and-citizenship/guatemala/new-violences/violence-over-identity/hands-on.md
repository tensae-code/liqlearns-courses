# Hands-on — Violence over Identity

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write the rule this whole course has been building, in your own words: who may be harmed, and who may not. This last lesson tests that rule at its hardest point.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: no person may be harmed for who they are — violence against people because of sexual orientation or gender identity is named, and respect for every person is the answer. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the community life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
