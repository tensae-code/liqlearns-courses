# Video script — Cyberviolence

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Violence can travel through screens — cyberviolence and cyberbullying have actors, myths, forms, and ways to prevent them.

The experience begins with the actores cibernéticos — the cyber actors: the people who take part in violence online, in their roles — and with the mitos sobre el acoso escolar cibernético, the myths about school cyberbullying: the false beliefs that let it hide, such as treating online harm as not real harm.

Harm done through a screen is real harm to a real person. Naming the myths clears the way to naming the thing itself.

Next the experience works through the tipos y modalidades — the types and forms of cyberbullying and cyberviolence: the ways harm is done through messages, images, accounts and networks.

Each form is a form of the same violence the course has named from its first lesson — carried by new tools, answered by the same refusal.

The experience closes with cómo prevenir la violencia cibernética — how to prevent cyberviolence: what pupils, schools and families do so the harm does not happen, and so it stops when it starts.

Prevention online follows the course's standing pattern: name it, refuse it, protect the person harmed, and build the culture of peace in the digital place too.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
