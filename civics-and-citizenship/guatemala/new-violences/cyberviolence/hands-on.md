# Hands-on — Cyberviolence

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write one harmful thing that can be done to a person through a phone or computer, without touching them. This lesson names that whole world: violencia cibernética.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: violence can travel through screens — cyberviolence and cyberbullying have actors, myths, forms, and ways to prevent them. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the community life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
