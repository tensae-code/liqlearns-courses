# Violence Revisited

## Do it first

Read your Level 2 note on what violence is. Add what you know now: one form and one actor you did not name then. This lesson meets violence again, deeper.

## Sub lesson 1 — Violence at tercero básico depth

The final básico guide opens, as the first did, with La violencia — violence itself. Met again at tercero básico depth, the teaching widens: violence's forms, and its actors — who does it, who allows it, who can stop it.

The return is deliberate: a pupil about to leave básico meets the first lesson's question with three years of named violences behind them — against children, in families, at school, against women, elders, people with disabilities, through racism.

## Sub lesson 2 — The school's culture of peace

The answer is the same, now carried by the pupil: the school's culture of peace — the living-together the guides build year by year, which the oldest básico pupils are ready to uphold themselves, not only receive.
