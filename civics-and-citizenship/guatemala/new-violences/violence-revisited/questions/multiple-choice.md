# Violence Revisited — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What widens when violence is met again at tercero básico?
a) its forms — and its actors: who does it, who allows it, who can stop it  b) nothing — the lesson is a copy with nothing added  c) only its spelling  d) only punishment is discussed
**Answer: a) its forms — and its actors: who does it, who allows it, who can stop it** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Who carries the school's culture of peace at tercero básico?
a) the pupils themselves, not only the teachers  b) no one  c) only visitors  d) only the youngest pupils
**Answer: a) the pupils themselves, not only the teachers** — That is what this lesson teaches.
