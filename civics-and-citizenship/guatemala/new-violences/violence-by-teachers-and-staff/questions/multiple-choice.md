# Violence by Teachers and Staff — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Why is naming violence by teachers and staff especially important?
a) because authority can hide it — and no position makes harm right  b) because it never happens  c) because pupils have no rights at school  d) because directors are above the rules they teach
**Answer: a) because authority can hide it — and no position makes harm right** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What right stands against this violence?
a) the pupil's right to be treated with dignity at school  b) the right to leave school forever  c) no right at all  d) the right to answer harm with harm
**Answer: a) the pupil's right to be treated with dignity at school** — That is what this lesson teaches.
