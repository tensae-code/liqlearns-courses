# Video script — Violence by Teachers and Staff

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Teachers and school directors can also do violence — and every pupil has the right to be treated with dignity at school.

The second experience of the final guide turns to la violencia de docentes y directivos escolares — violence exercised by teachers and school directors: harm done by the very people charged with the school's care.

Naming it matters most of all the violences in the course, because authority can hide it: the lesson teaches that no position makes harm right.

Against it stands the pupil's right: to be treated with dignity at school — the same dignity the course's rights frame gives every person, held also against the school's own adults.

A school whose adults are bound by the culture of peace is a school where authority itself answers to the rights it teaches.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
