# Violence by Teachers and Staff

## Do it first

Write what a pupil should be able to expect from every adult at school. This lesson names what happens when the adults themselves are the ones who harm.

## Sub lesson 1 — Violence exercised by teachers and school directors

The second experience of the final guide turns to la violencia de docentes y directivos escolares — violence exercised by teachers and school directors: harm done by the very people charged with the school's care.

Naming it matters most of all the violences in the course, because authority can hide it: the lesson teaches that no position makes harm right.

## Sub lesson 2 — The pupil's right to dignity at school

Against it stands the pupil's right: to be treated with dignity at school — the same dignity the course's rights frame gives every person, held also against the school's own adults.

A school whose adults are bound by the culture of peace is a school where authority itself answers to the rights it teaches.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: teachers and school directors can also do violence — and every pupil has the right to be treated with dignity at school. Give one real example from your own life or from the Guatemala you live in, and name the lesson's key words: violencia de docentes, violence by teachers, school directors, dignity at school, pupil's right. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
