# Video script — Discrimination and HIV

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
People living with HIV and AIDS face discrimination on top of illness — and the answer taught is human rights: HIV and AIDS explained plainly, the discrimination named, and rights defended, as Amnesty International defends them.

HIV is a virus that attacks the body's defences; AIDS is the advanced illness it can cause. People living with them suffer a second burden: discrimination — rejection at work, school and even in care — born of fear and ignorance. The book answers with human rights: the epidemic is fought with medicine and with rights together, as Amnesty International's work shows.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
