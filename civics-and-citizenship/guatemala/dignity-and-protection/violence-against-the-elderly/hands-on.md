# Hands-on — Violence against the Elderly

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name one older person in your family or community and one thing they are owed. This lesson names what happens when the opposite is done to them — and how it is prevented.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: an elderly person can be a victim of family violence — its types and risk factors can be named, and it can be prevented and fought. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the community life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
