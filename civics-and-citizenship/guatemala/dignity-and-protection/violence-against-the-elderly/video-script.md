# Video script — Violence against the Elderly

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
An elderly person can be a victim of family violence — its types and risk factors can be named, and it can be prevented and fought.

The second experience names el adulto mayor — the elderly person — as a victim of family violence: the same violencia intrafamiliar of the first guide, now met by the oldest members of the family.

The lesson works through its types: the forms this violence takes against an elderly person.

The experience names the risk factors: the conditions that make violence against an elderly person more likely — the situations in which an older person is left unprotected and unheard.

Naming risk factors is practical knowledge: a family and a community that can see the risk can move before the harm.

The experience closes with prevención y lucha — prevention and the fight against violence toward the elderly: protecting older people, hearing them, and refusing the harm.

In the values of Guatemala's peoples, elders carry ancestral knowledge — what the generations learned and handed down. Protecting them protects the community's own memory.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
