# Violence against the Elderly — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What are risk factors, in this lesson?
**Answer:** The conditions that make violence against an elderly person more likely — situations where an older person is left unprotected and unheard.

**2.** How does the experience close?
**Answer:** With prevention and the fight against violence toward the elderly — protecting older people and hearing them.
