# Lesson plan — Violence against the Elderly

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Guatemala · **Title:** Dignity and Protection · **Lesson:** Violence against the Elderly
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Dignity and Protection (CURRICULUM.md).

**Footing:** teacher-guide evidence only — every placement for this lesson is marked TEACHER (see lesson/sources.md).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The elderly person as victim of family violence** — The second experience names el adulto mayor — the elderly person — as a victim of family violence: the same violencia intrafamiliar of the first guide, now met by the oldest members of the family.
2. **Risk factors** — The experience names the risk factors: the conditions that make violence against an elderly person more likely — the situations in which an older person is left unprotected and unheard.
3. **Prevention and the fight against it** — The experience closes with prevención y lucha — prevention and the fight against violence toward the elderly: protecting older people, hearing them, and refusing the harm.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
