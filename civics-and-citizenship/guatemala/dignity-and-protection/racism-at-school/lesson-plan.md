## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Racism at school** — The last experience of the second guide names violencia por racismo escolar — violence through school racism: pupils harmed because of the people, language or culture they come from.
2. **Building an intercultural school** — The answer the experience builds is an intercultural school: a school where the peoples of Guatemala meet as equals, where each culture is learned from — as this course's first lesson taught — and no pupil is worth less for who they are.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: racism at school is violence — naming it is how an intercultural school, where every people belongs, is built.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or town — or, for this course's lessons, from the school and community life they live in.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the learner's own school, family and community life, as the guides' learning experiences work.
