# Racism at School

## Do it first

Write one way a pupil could be treated worse at school because of the people they come from — and why it is wrong. This lesson names that harm: racism at school.

## Sub lesson 1 — Racism at school

The last experience of the second guide names violencia por racismo escolar — violence through school racism: pupils harmed because of the people, language or culture they come from.

In a country of peoples — Maya, Xinka, Garífuna and Ladino — racism strikes at the course's very ground: the cultures whose values citizen formation is built from.

## Sub lesson 2 — Building an intercultural school

The answer the experience builds is an intercultural school: a school where the peoples of Guatemala meet as equals, where each culture is learned from — as this course's first lesson taught — and no pupil is worth less for who they are.

Naming racism is the school's first move; building the intercultural school is its standing answer.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: racism at school is violence — naming it is how an intercultural school, where every people belongs, is built. Give one real example from your own life or from the Guatemala you live in, and name the lesson's key words: racismo escolar, racism at school, intercultural school, equality of peoples. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
