# Racism at School — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is school racism, in this lesson?
a) violence — pupils harmed because of the people, language or culture they come from  b) a fair way to group pupils  c) a harmless joke by definition  d) something schools may ignore
**Answer: a) violence — pupils harmed because of the people, language or culture they come from** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What kind of school does the lesson build against racism?
a) an intercultural school, where every people belongs as equals  b) a school for one people only  c) a school with no cultures in it  d) a school that hides its pupils' peoples
**Answer: a) an intercultural school, where every people belongs as equals** — That is what this lesson teaches.
