# Racism at School — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Racism at school is a form of violence.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In an intercultural school, pupils are worth less for the people they come from.
**Answer: False** — In an intercultural school the peoples meet as equals; no pupil is worth less for who they are.
