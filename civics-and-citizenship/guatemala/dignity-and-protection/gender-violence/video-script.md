# Video script — Gender Violence

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Gender violence — above all violence against indigenous women — has forms and causes that can be named, and it can be prevented.

The second básico guide opens by turning to violence against specific people. Its first experience names violencia de género — gender violence — and within it, violence against indigenous women, who meet violence both as women and as indigenous people.

The experience teaches its forms and its causes: how this violence is done, and what feeds it — the treating of women, and of indigenous people, as worth less.

What has causes can be prevented. The course's answer follows its whole arc: name the violence, refuse the idea that any person is worth less, and build the culture of peace from the values of Guatemala's peoples — in which every person, and every woman of every people, is owed protection and respect.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
