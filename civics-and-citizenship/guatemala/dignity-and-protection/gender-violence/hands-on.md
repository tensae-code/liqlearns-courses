# Hands-on — Gender Violence

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write one reason a person might be treated worse because they are a woman, and why that reason fails. This lesson names gender violence — and, at its centre, violence against indigenous women.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: gender violence — above all violence against indigenous women — has forms and causes that can be named, and it can be prevented. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the community life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
