# Gender Violence — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Whose protection stands at the centre of this lesson?
a) indigenous women  b) only school directors  c) only visitors to Guatemala  d) no one in particular
**Answer: a) indigenous women** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does preventing gender violence begin from?
a) refusing the idea that any person is worth less  b) treating it as private and unspeakable  c) blaming the women harmed  d) waiting for it to end by itself
**Answer: a) refusing the idea that any person is worth less** — That is what this lesson teaches.
