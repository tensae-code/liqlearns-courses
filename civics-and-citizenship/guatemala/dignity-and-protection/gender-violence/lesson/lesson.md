# Gender Violence

## Do it first

Write one reason a person might be treated worse because they are a woman, and why that reason fails. This lesson names gender violence — and, at its centre, violence against indigenous women.

## Sub lesson 1 — Gender violence and violence against indigenous women

The second básico guide opens by turning to violence against specific people. Its first experience names violencia de género — gender violence — and within it, violence against indigenous women, who meet violence both as women and as indigenous people.

The experience teaches its forms and its causes: how this violence is done, and what feeds it — the treating of women, and of indigenous people, as worth less.

## Sub lesson 2 — Prevention

What has causes can be prevented. The course's answer follows its whole arc: name the violence, refuse the idea that any person is worth less, and build the culture of peace from the values of Guatemala's peoples — in which every person, and every woman of every people, is owed protection and respect.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: gender violence — above all violence against indigenous women — has forms and causes that can be named, and it can be prevented. Give one real example from your own life or from the Guatemala you live in, and name the lesson's key words: violencia de género, gender violence, violence against indigenous women, prevention. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
