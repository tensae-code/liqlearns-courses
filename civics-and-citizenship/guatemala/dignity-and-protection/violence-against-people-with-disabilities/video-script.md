# Video script — Violence against People with Disabilities

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
People with disabilities are owed more support, not violence — violence against them is named and refused.

The third experience names violencia contra los discapacitados — violence against people with disabilities: harm done to people because of, or careless of, their disability.

Its case carries the lesson's whole teaching in its title: "Personas con capacidades diferentes necesitan más apoyo" — people with different capacities need more support. The owed answer to difference is support; violence is its betrayal.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
