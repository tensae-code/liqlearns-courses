# Violence against People with Disabilities — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What are people with different capacities owed, in the guides' case?
a) more support  b) more violence  c) less schooling  d) to be left out
**Answer: a) more support** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is violence against people with disabilities?
a) harm done to people because of, or careless of, their disability  b) any help given to them  c) a kind of medical treatment  d) a school subject
**Answer: a) harm done to people because of, or careless of, their disability** — That is what this lesson teaches.
