# Natural Disasters — Earthquakes — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What does this lesson teach about disasters and earthquakes?
**Answer:** Earthquakes are sudden shakings from movements inside the Earth, among the most feared natural disasters.

**2.** What does this lesson teach about measuring quakes; effects; chile 2010?
**Answer:** A quake's effects depend on its measured strength and on the land, buildings and preparation where it strikes — as Chile 2010 showed.
