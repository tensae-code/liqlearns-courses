# Natural Disasters — Earthquakes — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Earthquakes are sudden shakings from movements inside the Earth, among the most feared natural disasters.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Two places hit by the same earthquake always suffer exactly the same damage.
**Answer: False** — Earthquakes are sudden shakings from movements inside the Earth, among the most feared natural disasters.
