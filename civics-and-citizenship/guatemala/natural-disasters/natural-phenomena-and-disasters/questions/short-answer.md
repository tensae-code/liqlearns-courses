# Natural Phenomena and Disasters — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What does this lesson teach about phenomena: weather, water, earth?
**Answer:** Phenomena come from weather and water, from inside the Earth, and from slopes that slide.

**2.** What does this lesson teach about disasters provoked by humans?
**Answer:** Human-provoked disasters, like oil spills, can be prevented by those who cause them.
