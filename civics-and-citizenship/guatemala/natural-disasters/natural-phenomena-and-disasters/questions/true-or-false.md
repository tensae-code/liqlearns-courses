# Natural Phenomena and Disasters — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Phenomena come from weather and water, from inside the Earth, and from slopes that slide.
**Answer: True**

**2.** All disasters are natural; humans never cause any.
**Answer: False** — Phenomena come from weather and water, from inside the Earth, and from slopes that slide.
