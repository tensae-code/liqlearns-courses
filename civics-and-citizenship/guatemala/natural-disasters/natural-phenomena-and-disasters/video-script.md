# Video script — Natural Phenomena and Disasters

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A natural phenomenon becomes a disaster when it strikes where people live: weather and water phenomena, phenomena inside the Earth, landslides — and some disasters are provoked by human beings themselves, like oil spills.

Natural phenomena are the Earth's own events: meteorological and hydrological ones — storms, floods, droughts — and phenomena from the interior of the Earth — earthquakes and volcanoes. Landslides (deslaves), where soaked or shaken slopes give way, join the list of what communities must know.

Not every disaster is natural: human beings provoke disasters too — oil spills poisoning seas and coasts among them. The difference matters: what humans cause, humans can prevent.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
