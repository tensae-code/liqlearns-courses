# Disaster Management

## Do it first

A fire drill feels silly until the day of smoke. This lesson is about managing disasters before, during and after — instead of merely suffering them.

## Sub lesson 1 — Prevention and emergency

Disaster management (manejo de desastres) is the organised work around disasters: prevention — building safely, protecting slopes and rivers, educating the community — and response when an emergency, a sudden dangerous event, demands immediate action. Management turns fate into a task with steps.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Disasters are managed: prevention builds the conditions that keep phenomena from becoming catastrophes, and when an emergency comes — a sudden event demanding immediate action — the prepared community knows what to do. Give one real example from your own life or from the Guatemala you live in, and name the lesson's key words: disaster management, emergency, prevention. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
