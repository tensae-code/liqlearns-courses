# Video script — Disaster Management

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Disasters are managed: prevention builds the conditions that keep phenomena from becoming catastrophes, and when an emergency comes — a sudden event demanding immediate action — the prepared community knows what to do.

Disaster management (manejo de desastres) is the organised work around disasters: prevention — building safely, protecting slopes and rivers, educating the community — and response when an emergency, a sudden dangerous event, demands immediate action. Management turns fate into a task with steps.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
