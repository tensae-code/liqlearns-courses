# Hands-on — Disaster Management

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
A fire drill feels silly until the day of smoke. This lesson is about managing disasters before, during and after — instead of merely suffering them.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Disasters are managed: prevention builds the conditions that keep phenomena from becoming catastrophes, and when an emergency comes — a sudden event demanding immediate action — the prepared community knows what to do. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the community life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
