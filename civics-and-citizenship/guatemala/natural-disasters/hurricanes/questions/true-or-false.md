# Hurricanes — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A hurricane is a violent wind storm turning like a whirlwind, bringing wind, rain and flood to coasts.
**Answer: True**

**2.** A hurricane is only heavy rain, with no dangerous wind.
**Answer: False** — A hurricane is a violent wind storm turning like a whirlwind, bringing wind, rain and flood to coasts.
