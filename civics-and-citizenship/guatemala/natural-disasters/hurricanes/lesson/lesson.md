# Hurricanes

## Do it first

Stir water in a bowl and watch it spin. Now imagine that spin made of wind, wider than your whole department. This lesson is about hurricanes.

## Sub lesson 1 — Winds that turn

Hurricanes form over warm ocean waters and turn like giant whirlwinds: winds spiralling at great speed around a calm eye. When they reach land they bring the storm's three harms — tearing wind, torrential rain and flood — and coasts in their path must prepare and evacuate in time.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: A hurricane is a violent storm of winds turning like a whirlwind around a calm centre, born over warm seas — bringing destructive wind, rain and flooding to the coasts it strikes. Give one real example from your own life or from the Guatemala you live in, and name the lesson's key words: hurricane, eye (of a hurricane), evacuate. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
