# Video script — Hurricanes

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A hurricane is a violent storm of winds turning like a whirlwind around a calm centre, born over warm seas — bringing destructive wind, rain and flooding to the coasts it strikes.

Hurricanes form over warm ocean waters and turn like giant whirlwinds: winds spiralling at great speed around a calm eye. When they reach land they bring the storm's three harms — tearing wind, torrential rain and flood — and coasts in their path must prepare and evacuate in time.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
