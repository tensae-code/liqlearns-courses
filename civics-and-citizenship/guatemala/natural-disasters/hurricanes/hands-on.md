# Hands-on — Hurricanes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Stir water in a bowl and watch it spin. Now imagine that spin made of wind, wider than your whole department. This lesson is about hurricanes.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: A hurricane is a violent storm of winds turning like a whirlwind around a calm centre, born over warm seas — bringing destructive wind, rain and flooding to the coasts it strikes. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the community life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
