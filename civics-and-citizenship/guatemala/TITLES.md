# Civics and Citizenship — Guatemala course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

Civics belongs to the country: this course is Guatemala's own —
built from Guatemala's Ciencias Sociales y Formación Ciudadana
básico teacher guides (Grades 7–9, MINEDUC, "Juntos construimos
la paz" — together we build peace), with the primary student
books (Grades 5–6, MINEDUC 2025) standing before them in the
ladder as a sketch only (their secured assets are DAMAGED
pushes — truncated uploads, unopenable; 0 placements). Inside
the course the organisation is by **levels, not grades**: the
titles below ARE the path, in order — what the learner proves,
step by step. The grades the books come from live only in each
lesson's sources.

The path's own logic is the guides': the circle of protection
widens year by year. First the ground — Guatemala's own cultures
and the pupil's rights and duties — then the violences of the
child's immediate world (against children, in the family, at
school), then violence against named people (women and
indigenous women, the elderly, people with disabilities) and
racism at school, and last the violences of the digital and
institutional world (teachers and staff, cyberviolence, violence
over identity). Every violence is answered the same way: name
it, refuse it, and build the culture of peace from the values of
Guatemala's peoples. That ladder IS the path order (recommended,
never forced — a learner can jump in higher; the "start here
instead" hints point down this ladder).

## The titles

**Level 1 — the primary years** (Ciencias Sociales y Formación
Ciudadana student books, Grades 5–6, MINEDUC 2025; **sketched,
not stocked** — both books UNREAD, their secured assets DAMAGED
pushes, so no title and no lesson is built for this level).

**Levels 2–4 — the básico guides** (Ciencias Sociales y
Formación Ciudadana I–III teacher guides, Grades 7–9;
teacher-guide evidence only throughout):

- `guatemalas-cultures/` — Levels 2–4, the standing foundation.
  Learning from Our Cultures (Level 2: the learning elements of
  the Maya, Xinka, Garífuna and Ladino peoples — territory and
  nature, ancestral knowledge, community values) · Cultures and
  Peace (Level 3, deepened at Level 4: the cultures' own values
  as peace resources, returned to at the head of every básico
  guide).
- `rights-and-duties/` — Level 2. Rights and Duties in School
  (individual and collective rights; the pupil's duties and
  rights at school and in society — the course's competence
  frame).
- `peace-and-nonviolence/` — Level 2. What Violence Is (its
  forms and roots, and the culture of peace as its opposite) ·
  Violence against Children (answered with cultural values) ·
  Violence in the Family (recognising it, and family life
  without it) · Violence at School (coexistence and prevention).
- `dignity-and-protection/` — Level 3. Gender Violence (above
  all violence against indigenous women) · Violence against the
  Elderly (types, risk factors, prevention) · Violence against
  People with Disabilities (people with different capacities
  need more support) · Racism at School (building an
  intercultural school).
- `new-violences/` — Level 4. Violence Revisited (forms and
  actors, at tercero básico depth) · Violence by Teachers and
  Staff (the pupil's right to dignity at school) ·
  Cyberviolence (cyber actors, myths, types and forms,
  prevention) · Violence over Identity (violence against people
  because of sexual orientation or gender identity, and respect
  for every person).
