# Civics and Citizenship — Guatemala course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

Civics belongs to the country: this course is Guatemala's own —
built from Guatemala's Ciencias Sociales y Formación Ciudadana
books: the primary student books (Grades 5–6, MINEDUC 2025;
repaired and read in full 2026-11-25) at Level 1, and the básico
teacher guides (Grades 7–9, MINEDUC, "Juntos construimos la
paz" — together we build peace) at Levels 2–4. Inside the course
the organisation is by **levels, not grades**: the titles below
ARE the path, in order — what the learner proves, step by step.
The grades the books come from live only in each lesson's
sources.

Level 1's own logic is the primary books': the combined subject
taught whole — the social-science strand (land, peoples,
history, population, work, disasters, research) in its own
titles, never folded silently into the citizen strand, and the
citizen strand (rights, duties, cultures, peace) stocked into
the standing titles the básico guides later build on. Levels
2–4 then widen the circle of protection year by year, as the
guides lay it out. That ladder IS the path order (recommended,
never forced — a learner can jump in higher; the "start here
instead" hints point down this ladder).
