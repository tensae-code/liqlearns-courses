# The Second World War and America

## Do it first

A storm far away can still flood your river. This lesson traces how a world war fought across the oceans changed America.

## Sub lesson 1 — Repercussions in America

Though the great battles were fought in Europe, Africa and Asia, the Second World War's repercussions crossed to America: trade broken and rebuilt, goods scarce then demanded, governments aligned, and the United States emerging as the world's strongest power beside the continent.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The Second World War shook the whole planet, and its repercussions reached America — its economies, its politics and its place in the world were all changed by a war fought mostly elsewhere. Give one real example from your own life or from the Guatemala you live in, and name the lesson's key words: repercussion, Second World War, alignment. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
