# Hands-on — The Second World War and America

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
A storm far away can still flood your river. This lesson traces how a world war fought across the oceans changed America.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: The Second World War shook the whole planet, and its repercussions reached America — its economies, its politics and its place in the world were all changed by a war fought mostly elsewhere. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the community life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
