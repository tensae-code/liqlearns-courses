# The Second World War and America — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The Second World War affected America mainly by…
a) Its repercussions — trade, scarcity and a new balance of power  b) Battles in every American capital  c) Not at all  d) Making it a colony again
**Answer: a) Its repercussions — trade, scarcity and a new balance of power** — That is what this lesson teaches.
