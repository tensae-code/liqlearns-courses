# The Second World War and America — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Second World War's repercussions — trade, scarcity, alignment, US power — reached all America.
**Answer: True**

**2.** A war fought on other continents has no effects in America.
**Answer: False** — The Second World War's repercussions — trade, scarcity, alignment, US power — reached all America.
