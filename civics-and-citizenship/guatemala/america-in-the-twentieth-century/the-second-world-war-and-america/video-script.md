# Video script — The Second World War and America

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The Second World War shook the whole planet, and its repercussions reached America — its economies, its politics and its place in the world were all changed by a war fought mostly elsewhere.

Though the great battles were fought in Europe, Africa and Asia, the Second World War's repercussions crossed to America: trade broken and rebuilt, goods scarce then demanded, governments aligned, and the United States emerging as the world's strongest power beside the continent.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
