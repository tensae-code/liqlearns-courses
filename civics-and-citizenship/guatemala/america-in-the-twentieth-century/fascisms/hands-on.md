# Hands-on — The Era of the Fascisms

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
After crisis came men promising order at any price. This lesson names what they built: the fascisms — and why democracies must recognise them.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Between the world wars, fascisms rose in Europe — movements and regimes that exalted the nation and one leader, silenced all opposition, and prepared the road to the Second World War. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the community life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
