# The Era of the Fascisms

## Do it first

After crisis came men promising order at any price. This lesson names what they built: the fascisms — and why democracies must recognise them.

## Sub lesson 1 — The era of the fascisms

Fascism is an authoritarian ideology and regime: one party, one leader, the nation exalted above the person, opponents persecuted, rights abolished. In Italy, Germany and elsewhere the fascisms of the interwar years turned crisis and humiliation into dictatorship — and Europe toward war.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Between the world wars, fascisms rose in Europe — movements and regimes that exalted the nation and one leader, silenced all opposition, and prepared the road to the Second World War. Give one real example from your own life or from the Guatemala you live in, and name the lesson's key words: fascism, dictatorship, interwar. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
