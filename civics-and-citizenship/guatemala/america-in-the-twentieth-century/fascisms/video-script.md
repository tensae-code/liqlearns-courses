# Video script — The Era of the Fascisms

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Between the world wars, fascisms rose in Europe — movements and regimes that exalted the nation and one leader, silenced all opposition, and prepared the road to the Second World War.

Fascism is an authoritarian ideology and regime: one party, one leader, the nation exalted above the person, opponents persecuted, rights abolished. In Italy, Germany and elsewhere the fascisms of the interwar years turned crisis and humiliation into dictatorship — and Europe toward war.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
