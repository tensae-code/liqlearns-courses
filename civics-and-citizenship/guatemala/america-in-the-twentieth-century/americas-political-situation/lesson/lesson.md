# America's Political Situation

## Do it first

When a school's rules stop fitting its pupils, pressure to change them builds. Whole countries feel that pressure. This lesson reads America's political situation in the 1900s.

## Sub lesson 1 — Reform pressing for change

America's political situation in the century was movement: societies grown unequal and governed by narrow orders felt the need for reform, and in country after country that need drove change — new parties, new constitutions, revolutions and reactions.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Across twentieth-century America, the need for reform drove political change in many countries — peoples pressing against old orders, and governments changing, peacefully or by force, in answer. Give one real example from your own life or from the Guatemala you live in, and name the lesson's key words: reform, political situation, constitution. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
