# Video script — America's Political Situation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Across twentieth-century America, the need for reform drove political change in many countries — peoples pressing against old orders, and governments changing, peacefully or by force, in answer.

America's political situation in the century was movement: societies grown unequal and governed by narrow orders felt the need for reform, and in country after country that need drove change — new parties, new constitutions, revolutions and reactions.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
