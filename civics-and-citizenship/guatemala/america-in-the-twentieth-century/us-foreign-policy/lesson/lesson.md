# The United States and Its Foreign Policy

## Do it first

A big neighbour's decisions reach your street even if you never meet them. This lesson studies the biggest neighbour of all, and how its policy reached into America.

## Sub lesson 1 — Consolidation and expansion

Through the nineteenth century the United States consolidated its union and expanded its territory west and south. As it grew stronger, its foreign policy — how it deals with other countries — weighed more and more on the Americas.

## Sub lesson 2 — Interventions

Expansion brought interventions: the separation of Texas from Mexico in the 1820s–30s was an early sign of a long pattern in which the United States pressed, occupied or decided in its neighbours' affairs — a pattern Latin America has lived with ever since.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The United States grew by consolidating and expanding — and its foreign policy reached into America with interventions from the nineteenth century on, Texas in 1823 among the early signs, shaping its neighbours' histories. Give one real example from your own life or from the Guatemala you live in, and name the lesson's key words: foreign policy, intervention, expansion. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
