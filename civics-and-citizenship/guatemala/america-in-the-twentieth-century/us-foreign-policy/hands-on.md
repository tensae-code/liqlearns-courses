# Hands-on — The United States and Its Foreign Policy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
A big neighbour's decisions reach your street even if you never meet them. This lesson studies the biggest neighbour of all, and how its policy reached into America.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: The United States grew by consolidating and expanding — and its foreign policy reached into America with interventions from the nineteenth century on, Texas in 1823 among the early signs, shaping its neighbours' histories. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the community life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
