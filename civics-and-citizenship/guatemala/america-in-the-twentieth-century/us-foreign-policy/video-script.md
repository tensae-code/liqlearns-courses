# Video script — The United States and Its Foreign Policy

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The United States grew by consolidating and expanding — and its foreign policy reached into America with interventions from the nineteenth century on, Texas in 1823 among the early signs, shaping its neighbours' histories.

Through the nineteenth century the United States consolidated its union and expanded its territory west and south. As it grew stronger, its foreign policy — how it deals with other countries — weighed more and more on the Americas.

Expansion brought interventions: the separation of Texas from Mexico in the 1820s–30s was an early sign of a long pattern in which the United States pressed, occupied or decided in its neighbours' affairs — a pattern Latin America has lived with ever since.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
