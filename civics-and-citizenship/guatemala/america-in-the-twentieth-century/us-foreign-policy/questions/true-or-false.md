# The United States and Its Foreign Policy — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The United States consolidated and expanded, and its foreign policy weighed ever more on America.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The United States never intervened in its American neighbours' affairs.
**Answer: False** — The United States consolidated and expanded, and its foreign policy weighed ever more on America.
