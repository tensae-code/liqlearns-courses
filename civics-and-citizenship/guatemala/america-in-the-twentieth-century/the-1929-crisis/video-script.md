# Video script — The Crisis of 1929

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
In 1929 the world's economy crashed: banks and markets collapsed from the United States outward — falling on a Europe already ruined by the First World War — and ordinary people everywhere paid in lost work and hunger.

The economic crisis of 1929 began with collapse in the United States — banks failing, production stopping, millions losing work — and spread through a world economy already chained together. Europe, ruined and indebted from the First World War, was hit at its weakest; the crisis became the Great Depression of the 1930s.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
