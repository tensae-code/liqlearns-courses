# The Crisis of 1929 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The 1929 crisis crashed the world economy, falling hardest on a Europe ruined by the First World War.
**Answer: True**

**2.** An economic crash in one country harms only that country.
**Answer: False** — The 1929 crisis crashed the world economy, falling hardest on a Europe ruined by the First World War.
