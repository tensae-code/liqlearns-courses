# The Crisis of 1929 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Europe was hit so hard by 1929 because it was already…
a) Ruined and indebted by the First World War  b) The world's richest untouched region  c) Unconnected to world trade  d) At war in 1929 itself
**Answer: a) Ruined and indebted by the First World War** — That is what this lesson teaches.
