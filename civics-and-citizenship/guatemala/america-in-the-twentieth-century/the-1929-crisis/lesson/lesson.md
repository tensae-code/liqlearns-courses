# The Crisis of 1929

## Do it first

One market falls in one city — and a farmer on another continent cannot sell his crop. This lesson traces the crash of 1929 and its worldwide chain.

## Sub lesson 1 — The crash and its world

The economic crisis of 1929 began with collapse in the United States — banks failing, production stopping, millions losing work — and spread through a world economy already chained together. Europe, ruined and indebted from the First World War, was hit at its weakest; the crisis became the Great Depression of the 1930s.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: In 1929 the world's economy crashed: banks and markets collapsed from the United States outward — falling on a Europe already ruined by the First World War — and ordinary people everywhere paid in lost work and hunger. Give one real example from your own life or from the Guatemala you live in, and name the lesson's key words: economic crisis, Great Depression, debt. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
