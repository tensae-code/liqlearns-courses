# Conflicts of the Twentieth Century

## Do it first

The 1900s are called the twentieth century. This lesson opens its hardest chapter: the century's conflicts, beginning with a world war.

## Sub lesson 1 — An age of conflicts

The 1900s brought conflicts and problems at a world scale: empires competing, nations arming, and wars that drew in country after country. Understanding the century starts by facing that its great events were conflicts — and that their costs were paid by ordinary people everywhere.

## Sub lesson 2 — The First World War

The First World War (1914–1918) set Europe's great powers and their allies against each other across the world. It redrew maps, exhausted empires, and planted the seeds of the century's later conflicts.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The twentieth century was an age of great conflicts — the First World War among them — whose problems and wars reached every continent, America included. Give one real example from your own life or from the Guatemala you live in, and name the lesson's key words: twentieth century, world war, conflict. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
