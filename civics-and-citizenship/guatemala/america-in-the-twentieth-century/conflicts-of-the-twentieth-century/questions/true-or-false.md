# Conflicts of the Twentieth Century — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The twentieth century's great conflicts reached every continent and were paid for by ordinary people.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The twentieth century was a century of peace with no world wars.
**Answer: False** — The twentieth century's great conflicts reached every continent and were paid for by ordinary people.
