# Hands-on — Conflicts of the Twentieth Century

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
The 1900s are called the twentieth century. This lesson opens its hardest chapter: the century's conflicts, beginning with a world war.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: The twentieth century was an age of great conflicts — the First World War among them — whose problems and wars reached every continent, America included. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the community life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
