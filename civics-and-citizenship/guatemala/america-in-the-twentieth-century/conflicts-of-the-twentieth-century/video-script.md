# Video script — Conflicts of the Twentieth Century

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The twentieth century was an age of great conflicts — the First World War among them — whose problems and wars reached every continent, America included.

The 1900s brought conflicts and problems at a world scale: empires competing, nations arming, and wars that drew in country after country. Understanding the century starts by facing that its great events were conflicts — and that their costs were paid by ordinary people everywhere.

The First World War (1914–1918) set Europe's great powers and their allies against each other across the world. It redrew maps, exhausted empires, and planted the seeds of the century's later conflicts.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
