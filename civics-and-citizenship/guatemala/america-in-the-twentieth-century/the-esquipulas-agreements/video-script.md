This lesson is about one thing.
In 1986 Central America's presidents signed the Esquipulas agreements — choosing peace by agreement over war: a regional decision that conflicts would be settled by negotiation, elections and reconciliation.

Central America in the 1980s was torn by wars. At Esquipulas in 1986 its presidents agreed a procedure for a firm and lasting peace: negotiation instead of arms, democratisation, and reconciliation in each country. Esquipulas stands as proof that agreement is a real alternative to war.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
