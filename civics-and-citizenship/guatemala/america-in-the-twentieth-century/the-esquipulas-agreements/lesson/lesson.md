# The Esquipulas Agreements

## Do it first

Two fighting neighbours sign a paper promising to talk instead. Whole countries did that at Esquipulas. This lesson tells how Central America chose agreement.

## Sub lesson 1 — Peace by agreement, 1986

Central America in the 1980s was torn by wars. At Esquipulas in 1986 its presidents agreed a procedure for a firm and lasting peace: negotiation instead of arms, democratisation, and reconciliation in each country. Esquipulas stands as proof that agreement is a real alternative to war.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: In 1986 Central America's presidents signed the Esquipulas agreements — choosing peace by agreement over war: a regional decision that conflicts would be settled by negotiation, elections and reconciliation. Give one real example from your own life or from the Guatemala you live in, and name the lesson's key words: agreement, reconciliation, negotiation. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
