# Imperialism and Colonialism

## Do it first

The colonial era seemed finished in America — then Europe's powers seized most of Africa and much of Asia in a generation. This lesson names that new wave: imperialism.

## Sub lesson 1 — The new scramble

Imperialism is a state extending its power over other peoples and lands; colonialism is the system of ruling them as colonies. In the late 1800s Britain and France led a scramble that divided nearly all Africa and much of Asia between European empires — for raw materials, markets and prestige.

Give one real example from your own life or from the Guatemala you live in, and name the lesson's key words: imperialism, colonialism, raw materials.
