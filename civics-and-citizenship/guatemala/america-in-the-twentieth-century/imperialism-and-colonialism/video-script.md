This lesson is about one thing.
At the end of the nineteenth century the great powers — Britain and France foremost — raced to seize colonies across Africa and Asia: imperialism, the extension of a state's power over other peoples, with colonialism as its rule on the ground.

Imperialism is a state extending its power over other peoples and lands; colonialism is the system of ruling them as colonies. In the late 1800s Britain and France led a scramble that divided nearly all Africa and much of Asia between European empires — for raw materials, markets and prestige.
