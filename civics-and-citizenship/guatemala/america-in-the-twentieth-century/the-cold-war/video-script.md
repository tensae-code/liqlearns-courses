# Video script — The Cold War

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
After 1945 the world divided between two superpowers — the United States and the Soviet Union — in a Cold War: a struggle of ideology, arms and influence that never became direct war between them, yet marked every country, down to the divided Olympics of Moscow 1980.

The Cold War set the capitalist West, led by the United States, against the communist East, led by the Soviet Union: alliances, arms races, and contests for influence in every continent — Latin America among them. It was 'cold' because the two giants never fought each other directly, fearing nuclear war.

The Cold War showed even in sport: the 1980 Moscow Olympics were boycotted by the United States and allies — a sign that the division ran through every field of life, not only armies and diplomacy.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
