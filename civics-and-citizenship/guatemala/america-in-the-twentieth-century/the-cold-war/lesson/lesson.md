# The Cold War

## Do it first

Two giants glare at each other for forty years, never quite fighting, while every smaller country feels the glare. This lesson is the Cold War.

## Sub lesson 1 — A divided world

The Cold War set the capitalist West, led by the United States, against the communist East, led by the Soviet Union: alliances, arms races, and contests for influence in every continent — Latin America among them. It was 'cold' because the two giants never fought each other directly, fearing nuclear war.

## Sub lesson 2 — Its signs — Moscow 1980

The Cold War showed even in sport: the 1980 Moscow Olympics were boycotted by the United States and allies — a sign that the division ran through every field of life, not only armies and diplomacy.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: After 1945 the world divided between two superpowers — the United States and the Soviet Union — in a Cold War: a struggle of ideology, arms and influence that never became direct war between them, yet marked every country, down to the divided Olympics of Moscow 1980. Give one real example from your own life or from the Guatemala you live in, and name the lesson's key words: Cold War, superpower, boycott. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
