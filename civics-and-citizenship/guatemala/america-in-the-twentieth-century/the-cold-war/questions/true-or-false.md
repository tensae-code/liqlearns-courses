# The Cold War — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Cold War divided the world between the US-led West and the Soviet-led East, without direct war between them.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The Cold War touched only the two superpowers and no one else.
**Answer: False** — The Cold War divided the world between the US-led West and the Soviet-led East, without direct war between them.
