# The Cold War — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What does this lesson teach about a divided world?
**Answer:** The Cold War divided the world between the US-led West and the Soviet-led East, without direct war between them.

**2.** What does this lesson teach about its signs — moscow 1980?
**Answer:** The boycotted Moscow Olympics of 1980 showed the Cold War dividing even sport.
