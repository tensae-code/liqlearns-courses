# Video script — Forms of Subjection

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Peoples can be subjected in more than one form — by conquest, by economic control, and by authoritarianism at home: rule that silences the people it governs. Naming the forms is the first defence against them.

Subjection (sometimiento) is a people or person placed under another's power against their will. Its forms run from foreign domination to authoritarianism — government that concentrates power, silences opposition and rules without the people's consent — a form Latin America has known from inside its own states.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
