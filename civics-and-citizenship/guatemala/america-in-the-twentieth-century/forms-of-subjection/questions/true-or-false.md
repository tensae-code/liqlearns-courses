# Forms of Subjection — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Subjection places a people under another's power; authoritarianism is its home-grown form — rule without consent.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Only foreign armies can subject a people; its own government never can.
**Answer: False** — Subjection places a people under another's power; authoritarianism is its home-grown form — rule without consent.
