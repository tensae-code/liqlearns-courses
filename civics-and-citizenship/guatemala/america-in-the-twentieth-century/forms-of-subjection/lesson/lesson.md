# Forms of Subjection

## Do it first

A person can be controlled by chains — or by fear, or by an empty purse. Powers control peoples the same several ways. This lesson names the forms of subjection.

## Sub lesson 1 — Subjection and authoritarianism

Subjection (sometimiento) is a people or person placed under another's power against their will. Its forms run from foreign domination to authoritarianism — government that concentrates power, silences opposition and rules without the people's consent — a form Latin America has known from inside its own states.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Peoples can be subjected in more than one form — by conquest, by economic control, and by authoritarianism at home: rule that silences the people it governs. Naming the forms is the first defence against them. Give one real example from your own life or from the Guatemala you live in, and name the lesson's key words: subjection (sometimiento), authoritarianism, consent. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
