# Hands-on — The Cuban Revolution

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
An island near a giant changes its government against the giant's wishes. This lesson tells Cuba's revolution and why it shook America.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: In Cuba, a revolution overthrew the United States-backed government that had ruled since 1952 — and set the island on a new, socialist course that reshaped the whole continent's politics. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the community life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
