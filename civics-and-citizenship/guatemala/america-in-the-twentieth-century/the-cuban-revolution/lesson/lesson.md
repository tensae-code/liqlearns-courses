# The Cuban Revolution

## Do it first

An island near a giant changes its government against the giant's wishes. This lesson tells Cuba's revolution and why it shook America.

## Sub lesson 1 — Revolution against the backed government

Cuba was governed from 1952 by a regime backed by the United States. The Cuban revolution fought that government and overthrew it, bringing a revolutionary government that broke with the United States and chose socialism — a turning point for all Latin America.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: In Cuba, a revolution overthrew the United States-backed government that had ruled since 1952 — and set the island on a new, socialist course that reshaped the whole continent's politics. Give one real example from your own life or from the Guatemala you live in, and name the lesson's key words: revolution, regime, socialism. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
