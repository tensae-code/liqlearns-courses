# Video script — The Cuban Revolution

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
In Cuba, a revolution overthrew the United States-backed government that had ruled since 1952 — and set the island on a new, socialist course that reshaped the whole continent's politics.

Cuba was governed from 1952 by a regime backed by the United States. The Cuban revolution fought that government and overthrew it, bringing a revolutionary government that broke with the United States and chose socialism — a turning point for all Latin America.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
