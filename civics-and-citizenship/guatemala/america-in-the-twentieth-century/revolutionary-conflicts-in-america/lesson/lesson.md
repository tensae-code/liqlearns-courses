# Revolutionary Conflicts in America

## Do it first

When a country's crisis cannot be settled by talking, some reach for revolution. This lesson surveys America's revolutionary conflicts — and their costs.

## Sub lesson 1 — Crises and revolutionary processes

Across the continent, deep crises — inequality, dictatorship, exclusion — broke into revolutionary processes: armed and political struggles to overturn the order. Each country's process differed, but all left marks: dead and disappeared, changed states, and memories their peoples still work through.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Many American countries lived serious political crises and revolutionary processes in the twentieth century — struggles over power and the social order that marked their peoples for generations. Give one real example from your own life or from the Guatemala you live in, and name the lesson's key words: political crisis, revolutionary process, dictatorship. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
