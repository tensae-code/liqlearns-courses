# Hands-on — Revolutionary Conflicts in America

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
When a country's crisis cannot be settled by talking, some reach for revolution. This lesson surveys America's revolutionary conflicts — and their costs.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Many American countries lived serious political crises and revolutionary processes in the twentieth century — struggles over power and the social order that marked their peoples for generations. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the community life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
