# Video script — Revolutionary Conflicts in America

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Many American countries lived serious political crises and revolutionary processes in the twentieth century — struggles over power and the social order that marked their peoples for generations.

Across the continent, deep crises — inequality, dictatorship, exclusion — broke into revolutionary processes: armed and political struggles to overturn the order. Each country's process differed, but all left marks: dead and disappeared, changed states, and memories their peoples still work through.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
