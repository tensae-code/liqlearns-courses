# Video script — Transport — Ways and Means

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Transport moves people and goods by ways and means that differ by country — roads and rails, rivers and air — and Guatemala has its own answers, like the moto-taxis that carry its towns.

The ways (vías) are the paths — roads, railways, rivers, sea lanes, air routes; the means are the vehicles that use them. Every country combines them by its geography and wealth, and a country's transport is a map of how its people actually live and trade.

Guatemala's moto-taxis — motorcycle taxis carrying passengers through towns and city edges — are local transport invented by need: cheap, nimble where buses and cars do not serve, and part of daily Guatemalan movement.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
