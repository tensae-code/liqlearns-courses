# Hands-on — Transport — Ways and Means

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Count the kinds of transport you have ridden in your life. This lesson orders them — ways and means — and lands in Guatemala's own streets.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Transport moves people and goods by ways and means that differ by country — roads and rails, rivers and air — and Guatemala has its own answers, like the moto-taxis that carry its towns. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the community life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
