# Transport — Ways and Means — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What does this lesson teach about ways and means?
**Answer:** Transport joins ways (roads, rivers, air routes) with means (vehicles) — combined differently in each country.

**2.** What does this lesson teach about moto-taxis in guatemala?
**Answer:** Moto-taxis are Guatemala's own local transport — cheap and nimble where other means do not serve.
