# River Transport and Ports — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What does this lesson teach about transport on rivers?
**Answer:** River transport moves people and goods along navigable rivers, growing trade on their banks.

**2.** What does this lesson teach about ports, then and now?
**Answer:** Ports — where ships load and unload — have been first-order places since antiquity.
