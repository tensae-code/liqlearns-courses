# Hands-on — River Transport and Ports

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Before roads, there were rivers. Follow one in your mind to the sea: boats, landings, a port. This lesson travels it.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Rivers were humanity's first highways: river transport moves people and goods on the water, and ports — where ships load and unload — have held a first-order role since antiquity; the world's greatest ports move its trade today. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the community life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
