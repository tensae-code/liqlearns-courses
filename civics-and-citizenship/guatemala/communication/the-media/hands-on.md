# Hands-on — The Media

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Tell a secret to one friend; then announce something to your whole school. The second act is what this lesson studies: communication to the many — the media.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: The media are channels of communication — press, radio, television and digital media — and the mass media join millions of people across any distance with the same message at the same time. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the community life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
