# Video script — The Media

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The media are channels of communication — press, radio, television and digital media — and the mass media join millions of people across any distance with the same message at the same time.

Communication needs channels. The media are the channels built for the many: newspapers, radio, television and now digital networks. The mass media carry one message to millions at once, across any distance — informing, entertaining and shaping what whole societies talk about.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
