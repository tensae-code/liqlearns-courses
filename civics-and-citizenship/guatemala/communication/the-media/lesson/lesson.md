# The Media

## Do it first

Tell a secret to one friend; then announce something to your whole school. The second act is what this lesson studies: communication to the many — the media.

## Sub lesson 1 — Channels and masses

Communication needs channels. The media are the channels built for the many: newspapers, radio, television and now digital networks. The mass media carry one message to millions at once, across any distance — informing, entertaining and shaping what whole societies talk about.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The media are channels of communication — press, radio, television and digital media — and the mass media join millions of people across any distance with the same message at the same time. Give one real example from your own life or from the Guatemala you live in, and name the lesson's key words: media, mass media, channel. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
