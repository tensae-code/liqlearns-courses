# Video script — Human Development

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Human development is the opportunities a country gives its people — to live long and healthy, to learn, to earn a decent life — and it differs country by country across America.

Human development measures what a country's people can actually do and be: the opportunities the country gives its population — health, education, a decent standard of living. A rich country that gives few opportunities develops its people less than its wealth promises.

Set side by side, America's countries stand at very different levels of human development — the fruit of their histories, resources and choices. The comparison is a mirror: it shows each country what its people still lack in opportunity.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
