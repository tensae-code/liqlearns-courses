# Hands-on — Human Development

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Two children with the same cleverness: one has a school, a clinic and books; the other has none. Their lives will differ. This lesson names that difference: human development.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Human development is the opportunities a country gives its people — to live long and healthy, to learn, to earn a decent life — and it differs country by country across America. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the community life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
