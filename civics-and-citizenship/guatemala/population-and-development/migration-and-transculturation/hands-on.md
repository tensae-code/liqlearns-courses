# Hands-on — Migration and Transculturation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Has anyone in your family moved to another town or country? What did they take with them, and what new things did they learn? This lesson is about movement — and mixing.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: People move — within Latin America and beyond it — seeking work, safety and a better life; and where peoples meet, cultures mix: that exchange and blending of cultures is transculturation. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the community life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
