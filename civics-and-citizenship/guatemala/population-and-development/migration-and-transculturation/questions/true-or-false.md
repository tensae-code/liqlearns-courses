# Migration and Transculturation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Migration — people moving for work, safety or hope — changes both the place left and the place reached.
**Answer: True**

**2.** When peoples meet, their cultures stay completely separate and unchanged.
**Answer: False** — Migration — people moving for work, safety or hope — changes both the place left and the place reached.
