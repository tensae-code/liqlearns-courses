# Migration and Transculturation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What does this lesson teach about population movements?
**Answer:** Migration — people moving for work, safety or hope — changes both the place left and the place reached.

**2.** What does this lesson teach about transculturation?
**Answer:** Transculturation is cultures exchanging and mixing into new forms where peoples meet.
