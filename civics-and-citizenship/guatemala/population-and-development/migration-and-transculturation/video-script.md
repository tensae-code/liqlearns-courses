# Video script — Migration and Transculturation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
People move — within Latin America and beyond it — seeking work, safety and a better life; and where peoples meet, cultures mix: that exchange and blending of cultures is transculturation.

Migration is people changing where they live — from country to city, country to country — driven by work, land, violence or hope. Latin America's history is written in such movements, and every movement changes both the place left and the place reached.

Where peoples live together, cultures do not merely sit side by side: they exchange, mix and create new forms — foods, music, words, customs. That living exchange is transculturation, and it has made Latin America's cultures what they are.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
