# Globalisation — matching

Match each word with its meaning.

**1.** globalisation — a)

**2.** interdependence — b)

**3.** border — c)


Meanings:
a) the growing interdependence of all countries
b) depending on each other mutually
c) the line between countries — crossed daily by goods and news

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b; 3. c
