# Globalisation

## Do it first

Read the labels on things in your home: how many countries touched them before they reached you? That web is this lesson: globalisation.

## Sub lesson 1 — Growing interdependence

Globalisation is the process, growing since the 1960s, by which countries depend ever more on each other: products made across three continents, news crossing the world in seconds, money and culture in constant movement. No country now lives sealed from the rest.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Since the 1960s the world has grown ever more interdependent — goods, money, information and culture crossing borders constantly: that growing web of connections is globalisation. Give one real example from your own life or from the Guatemala you live in, and name the lesson's key words: globalisation, interdependence, border. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
