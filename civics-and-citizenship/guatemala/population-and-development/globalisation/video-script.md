# Video script — Globalisation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Since the 1960s the world has grown ever more interdependent — goods, money, information and culture crossing borders constantly: that growing web of connections is globalisation.

Globalisation is the process, growing since the 1960s, by which countries depend ever more on each other: products made across three continents, news crossing the world in seconds, money and culture in constant movement. No country now lives sealed from the rest.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
