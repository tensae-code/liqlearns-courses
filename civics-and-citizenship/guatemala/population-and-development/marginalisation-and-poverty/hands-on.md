# Hands-on — Marginalisation and Poverty

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Picture your town as a table where decisions and goods are shared. Who sits at the edge, or not at the table at all? This lesson names their condition: marginalisation.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Marginalisation pushes people and communities to society's edge, denied its goods and chances — its socioeconomic form is its most visible evidence — and in Guatemala poverty, sharper than in developed countries, is marginalisation's daily face. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the community life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
