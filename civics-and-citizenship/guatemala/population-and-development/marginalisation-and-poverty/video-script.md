# Video script — Marginalisation and Poverty

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Marginalisation pushes people and communities to society's edge, denied its goods and chances — its socioeconomic form is its most visible evidence — and in Guatemala poverty, sharper than in developed countries, is marginalisation's daily face.

Marginalisation (marginación) is being pushed to the margin: communities and people left outside development's benefits — without services, voice or opportunity. Its socioeconomic form — poverty of income, housing, health and schooling — is its most visible evidence.

Poverty is lacking what a decent life needs. In Guatemala it is deep and wide — sharper, as in other developing countries, in the countryside and among indigenous peoples — and it is the daily face of marginalisation that policy and solidarity must answer.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
