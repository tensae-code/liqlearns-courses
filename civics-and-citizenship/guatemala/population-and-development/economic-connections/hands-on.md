# Hands-on — Economic Connections Between Countries

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
If your town's market joined with the next town's market under one set of rules, trading would grow easier. Countries do exactly that. This lesson shows how.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Countries connect economically through trade treaties and regional groupings: agreements that let goods move between them under shared rules, tying each country's production to its neighbours'. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the community life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
