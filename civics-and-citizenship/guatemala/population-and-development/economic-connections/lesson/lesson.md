# Economic Connections Between Countries

## Do it first

If your town's market joined with the next town's market under one set of rules, trading would grow easier. Countries do exactly that. This lesson shows how.

## Sub lesson 1 — Treaties and groupings

An economic connection is a working tie between countries: a trade treaty sets the rules for exchanging goods, and regional groupings join neighbouring economies so their products, and sometimes their people, move more freely within the group.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Countries connect economically through trade treaties and regional groupings: agreements that let goods move between them under shared rules, tying each country's production to its neighbours'. Give one real example from your own life or from the Guatemala you live in, and name the lesson's key words: trade treaty, regional grouping, exports.
