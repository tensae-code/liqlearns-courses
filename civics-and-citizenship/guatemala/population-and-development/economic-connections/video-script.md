# Video script — Economic Connections Between Countries

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Countries connect economically through trade treaties and regional groupings: agreements that let goods move between them under shared rules, tying each country's production to its neighbours'.

An economic connection is a working tie between countries: a trade treaty sets the rules for exchanging goods, and regional groupings join neighbouring economies so their products, and sometimes their people, move more freely within the group.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
