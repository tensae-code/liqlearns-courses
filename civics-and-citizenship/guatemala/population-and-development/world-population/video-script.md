# Video script — World Population

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The world's population is counted in thousands of millions and is spread with very uneven density — and population only means something set against quality of life, as the world map of human development shows.

The world's population runs to thousands of millions. Population density — people per square kilometre — varies enormously: crowded deltas and cities against near-empty deserts, tundras and forests. Density, not the raw total, is what a place feels.

Numbers alone do not tell how people live. Set population against quality of life — the world human-development map shows countries of similar size living at wholly different levels — and the count becomes a question about opportunities, not just heads.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
