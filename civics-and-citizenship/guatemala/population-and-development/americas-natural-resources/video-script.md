# Video script — America's Natural Resources

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Natural resources are what nature gives without human making — water, forests, soils, minerals — and America's regions differ greatly in them, as its forest production alone shows region by region.

A natural resource comes from nature without human intervention: the river's water, the forest's timber, the soil's fertility, the mountain's minerals. Societies live by using them — wisely, they last; wastefully, they run out.

America's resources differ by region: the book's table of forest production by region shows one resource alone varying hugely across the continent. Each region's development starts from the resources it actually has.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
