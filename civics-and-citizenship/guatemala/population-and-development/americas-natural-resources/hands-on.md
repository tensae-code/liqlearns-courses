# Hands-on — America's Natural Resources

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Look around your home and separate: what did people make, and what did nature give? This lesson is about nature's half — America's natural resources.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Natural resources are what nature gives without human making — water, forests, soils, minerals — and America's regions differ greatly in them, as its forest production alone shows region by region. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the community life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
