# Video script — Productive and Economic Activities

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
People meet their essential needs — food, clothing, shelter — through productive and economic activities: farming, making, trading and serving, each turning resources into what life requires.

Productive activities create the goods life needs: the farmer's crop, the maker's craft, the builder's house. Economic activities move and exchange them: transport, trade, services. Together they are how a society feeds, clothes and houses itself.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
