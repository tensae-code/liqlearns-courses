# Hands-on — Productive and Economic Activities

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Trace one meal backwards: who grew it, who carried it, who sold it, who cooked it? This lesson names that whole chain of activities.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: People meet their essential needs — food, clothing, shelter — through productive and economic activities: farming, making, trading and serving, each turning resources into what life requires. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the community life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
