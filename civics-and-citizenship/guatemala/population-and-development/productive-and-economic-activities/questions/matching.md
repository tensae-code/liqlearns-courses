# Productive and Economic Activities — matching

Match each word with its meaning.

**1.** productive activity — a)

**2.** economic activity — b)

**3.** essential needs — c)


Meanings:
a) work that creates goods — farming, making, building
b) work that moves, trades or serves goods
c) what life requires: food, clothing, shelter

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b; 3. c
