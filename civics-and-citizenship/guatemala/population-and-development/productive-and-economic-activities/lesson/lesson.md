# Productive and Economic Activities

## Do it first

Trace one meal backwards: who grew it, who carried it, who sold it, who cooked it? This lesson names that whole chain of activities.

## Sub lesson 1 — Meeting our needs

Productive activities create the goods life needs: the farmer's crop, the maker's craft, the builder's house. Economic activities move and exchange them: transport, trade, services. Together they are how a society feeds, clothes and houses itself.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: People meet their essential needs — food, clothing, shelter — through productive and economic activities: farming, making, trading and serving, each turning resources into what life requires. Give one real example from your own life or from the Guatemala you live in, and name the lesson's key words: productive activity, economic activity, essential needs. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
