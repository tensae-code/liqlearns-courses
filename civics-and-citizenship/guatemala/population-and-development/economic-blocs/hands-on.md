# Hands-on — Economic Blocs

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Market stalls that join into one market gain a louder voice. Countries do the same. This lesson is about their blocs.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Inside globalisation, countries form economic blocs — groups that integrate their economies under shared rules — to trade and negotiate with the world together, stronger than each alone. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the community life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
