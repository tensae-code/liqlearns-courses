# Video script — Economic Blocs

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Inside globalisation, countries form economic blocs — groups that integrate their economies under shared rules — to trade and negotiate with the world together, stronger than each alone.

An economic bloc is a group of countries that integrate their economies: freer trade inside, common rules, joint negotiation outside. Blocs are globalisation organised — countries answering a connected world by connecting formally with their partners.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
