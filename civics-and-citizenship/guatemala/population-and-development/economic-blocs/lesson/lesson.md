# Economic Blocs

## Do it first

Market stalls that join into one market gain a louder voice. Countries do the same. This lesson is about their blocs.

## Sub lesson 1 — Formed inside globalisation

An economic bloc is a group of countries that integrate their economies: freer trade inside, common rules, joint negotiation outside. Blocs are globalisation organised — countries answering a connected world by connecting formally with their partners.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Inside globalisation, countries form economic blocs — groups that integrate their economies under shared rules — to trade and negotiate with the world together, stronger than each alone. Give one real example from your own life or from the Guatemala you live in, and name the lesson's key words: economic bloc, integration, member. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
