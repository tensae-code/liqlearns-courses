# America's Population Today — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** America's population is distributed…
a) Unevenly — dense in cities and favoured lands, sparse in hard lands  b) Perfectly evenly everywhere  c) Only on the coasts  d) Only in one country
**Answer: a) Unevenly — dense in cities and favoured lands, sparse in hard lands** — That is what this lesson teaches.
