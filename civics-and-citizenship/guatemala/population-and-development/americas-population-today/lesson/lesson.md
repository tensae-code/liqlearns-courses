# America's Population Today

## Do it first

Why is your town where it is — water, road, flat land? Whole continents settle by the same logic. This lesson counts America's people and maps where they gather.

## Sub lesson 1 — Counting the continent

America's population today runs to hundreds of millions of people, measured by bodies like UNFPA, the United Nations Population Fund. Counting people — how many, of what ages, growing how fast — is the first fact every plan for schools, food and health stands on.

## Sub lesson 2 — How it is distributed

Population is distributed unevenly: dense in great cities and favoured lands, sparse where the land is hard — high mountains, deserts, deep forest. The distribution map of America is a map of water, climate and work as much as of people.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: America's population today is counted in hundreds of millions (UNFPA figures) and is not spread evenly: it concentrates in cities and fertile, coastal lands, and thins across mountains, deserts and forests. Give one real example from your own life or from the Guatemala you live in, and name the lesson's key words: population, distribution, UNFPA. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
