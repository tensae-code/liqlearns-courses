# Video script — America's Population Today

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
America's population today is counted in hundreds of millions (UNFPA figures) and is not spread evenly: it concentrates in cities and fertile, coastal lands, and thins across mountains, deserts and forests.

America's population today runs to hundreds of millions of people, measured by bodies like UNFPA, the United Nations Population Fund. Counting people — how many, of what ages, growing how fast — is the first fact every plan for schools, food and health stands on.

Population is distributed unevenly: dense in great cities and favoured lands, sparse where the land is hard — high mountains, deserts, deep forest. The distribution map of America is a map of water, climate and work as much as of people.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
