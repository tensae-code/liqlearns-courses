# Hands-on — Tourism and Development

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
If a visitor came to your town for one day, what would you show them, and who would earn from their visit? That is this lesson: tourism as development.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Tourism is travelling to know places — and for Guatemala it is development waiting: visitors come from abroad (receptive tourism) to its lands and cultures, yet much of that potential is still unused. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the community life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
