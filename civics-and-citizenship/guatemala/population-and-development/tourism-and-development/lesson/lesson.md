# Tourism and Development

## Do it first

If a visitor came to your town for one day, what would you show them, and who would earn from their visit? That is this lesson: tourism as development.

## Sub lesson 1 — Tourism and Guatemala's potential

Tourism is travelling to know other places. Receptive tourism — visitors arriving from other countries — brings income to hotels, guides, markets and whole towns. Guatemala, rich in landscapes, archaeology and living cultures, receives tourists, but the book is plain: much of its potential is still unused.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Tourism is travelling to know places — and for Guatemala it is development waiting: visitors come from abroad (receptive tourism) to its lands and cultures, yet much of that potential is still unused. Give one real example from your own life or from the Guatemala you live in, and name the lesson's key words: tourism, receptive tourism, potential. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
