# Video script — Tourism and Development

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Tourism is travelling to know places — and for Guatemala it is development waiting: visitors come from abroad (receptive tourism) to its lands and cultures, yet much of that potential is still unused.

Tourism is travelling to know other places. Receptive tourism — visitors arriving from other countries — brings income to hotels, guides, markets and whole towns. Guatemala, rich in landscapes, archaeology and living cultures, receives tourists, but the book is plain: much of its potential is still unused.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
