# Waters — Rivers and Lakes

## Do it first

Pour water on sloped ground and watch where it runs and where it pools. Rivers and lakes work exactly so, at the scale of a continent. This lesson follows the water.

## Sub lesson 1 — Surface currents

Rivers and streams are surface currents: water running over the land from high ground to low, gathering as it goes, until it reaches a lake or the sea. They water the lands they cross and carry people and goods.

## Sub lesson 2 — Lakes

A lake is water gathered and held in a hollow of the relief — fed by rivers and rain, and giving water, fish and transport to the peoples around its shores.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: America's waters gather and flow: rivers run as surface currents from the heights to the seas, and lakes hold water collected in the hollows of the relief. Give one real example from your own life or from the Guatemala you live in, and name the lesson's key words: surface current, lake, water source. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
