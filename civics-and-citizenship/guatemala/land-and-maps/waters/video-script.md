# Video script — Waters — Rivers and Lakes

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
America's waters gather and flow: rivers run as surface currents from the heights to the seas, and lakes hold water collected in the hollows of the relief.

Rivers and streams are surface currents: water running over the land from high ground to low, gathering as it goes, until it reaches a lake or the sea. They water the lands they cross and carry people and goods.

A lake is water gathered and held in a hollow of the relief — fed by rivers and rain, and giving water, fish and transport to the peoples around its shores.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
