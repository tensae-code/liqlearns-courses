# Hands-on — Landforms — Mountains and Volcanoes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Look out at the land around you and name every different shape it takes: high, flat, hollow. This lesson names those forms across all America.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: The land's relief takes forms — mountains and cordilleras, volcanoes, valleys and plains — spread across America's whole territory, shaping where people live and travel. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the community life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
