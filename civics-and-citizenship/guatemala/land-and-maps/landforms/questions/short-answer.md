# Landforms — Mountains and Volcanoes — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What does this lesson teach about forms of relief?
**Answer:** Relief is the land's shape; America's runs from cordilleras to coastal plains, shaping life in each place.

**2.** What does this lesson teach about mountains and volcanoes?
**Answer:** Mountains chain into cordilleras; volcanoes are built by eruptions from the Earth's interior.
