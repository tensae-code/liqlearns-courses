# Video script — Landforms — Mountains and Volcanoes

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The land's relief takes forms — mountains and cordilleras, volcanoes, valleys and plains — spread across America's whole territory, shaping where people live and travel.

Relief is the shape of the Earth's surface in a place. Across America's great territorial extension the relief runs from high cordilleras and single mountains down through plateaus and valleys to coastal plains — and each form shapes climate, farming and settlement.

Mountains are the land's great heights, often chained in cordilleras. Volcanoes are mountains of a special kind — built by eruptions from the Earth's interior, and Central America's chain of them marks the land Guatemala stands on.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
