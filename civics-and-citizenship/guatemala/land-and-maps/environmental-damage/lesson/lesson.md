# Environmental Damage — and Protecting the Environment

## Do it first

Walk outside and find one place hurt by rubbish, smoke or dirty water, and one place that is clean and cared for. This lesson names the causes of the damage — and the protection.

## Sub lesson 1 — Causes and consequences

Environmental deterioration is the wearing away of the natural world around us. Its causes are human acts: pollution of water, air and soil, felling and burning. Its consequences return to us — sickness, lost crops, disappearing animals and water that cannot be drunk.

## Sub lesson 2 — Protecting the environment

The book's workshop turns the teaching into action: protecting the environment means stopping the damage at its causes — disposing of waste properly, caring for water and trees — in the home, the school and the community.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The environment deteriorates through human causes — pollution of air, water and soil among them — and the consequences fall on everyone's health and life; protecting the environment is the answer, and it begins with each person's acts. Give one real example from your own life or from the Guatemala you live in, and name the lesson's key words: pollution, deterioration, environment. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
