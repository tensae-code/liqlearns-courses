# Video script — Environmental Damage — and Protecting the Environment

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The environment deteriorates through human causes — pollution of air, water and soil among them — and the consequences fall on everyone's health and life; protecting the environment is the answer, and it begins with each person's acts.

Environmental deterioration is the wearing away of the natural world around us. Its causes are human acts: pollution of water, air and soil, felling and burning. Its consequences return to us — sickness, lost crops, disappearing animals and water that cannot be drunk.

The book's workshop turns the teaching into action: protecting the environment means stopping the damage at its causes — disposing of waste properly, caring for water and trees — in the home, the school and the community.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
