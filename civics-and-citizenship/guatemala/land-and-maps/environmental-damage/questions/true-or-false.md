# Environmental Damage — and Protecting the Environment — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Pollution and other human acts deteriorate the environment, and the consequences fall on health and life.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Environmental damage harms nature but never harms people.
**Answer: False** — Pollution and other human acts deteriorate the environment, and the consequences fall on health and life.
