# Environmental Damage — and Protecting the Environment — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these is a cause of environmental deterioration?
a) Pollution of water, air and soil  b) Planting trees  c) Caring for rivers  d) Cleaning a schoolyard
**Answer: a) Pollution of water, air and soil** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Who do the consequences of environmental damage fall on?
a) Everyone — health and life depend on the environment  b) No one  c) Only people far away  d) Only animals
**Answer: a) Everyone — health and life depend on the environment** — That is what this lesson teaches.
