# Environmental Damage — and Protecting the Environment — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What does this lesson teach about causes and consequences?
**Answer:** Pollution and other human acts deteriorate the environment, and the consequences fall on health and life.

**2.** What does this lesson teach about protecting the environment?
**Answer:** Protecting the environment means acting on the causes of damage, at home, school and community.
