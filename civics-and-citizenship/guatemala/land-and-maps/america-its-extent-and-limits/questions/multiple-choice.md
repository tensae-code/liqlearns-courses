# America — Its Extent and Limits — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does a continent's extent tell you?
a) How far it reaches from one end to the other  b) Only its capital city  c) Only its weather  d) The names of its rulers
**Answer: a) How far it reaches from one end to the other** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Why do we learn America's limits first?
a) So every place inside it can be located against its borders  b) To memorise ocean names for their own sake  c) Because countries have no borders  d) So maps are not needed
**Answer: a) So every place inside it can be located against its borders** — That is what this lesson teaches.
