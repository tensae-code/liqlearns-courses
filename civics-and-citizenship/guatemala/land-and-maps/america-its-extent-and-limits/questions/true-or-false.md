# America — Its Extent and Limits — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** America's extent is the distance the continent covers from its far north to its far south.
**Answer: True**

**2.** America's limits are only the borders between its countries.
**Answer: False** — America's extent is the distance the continent covers from its far north to its far south.
