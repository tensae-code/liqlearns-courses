# America — Its Extent and Limits — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What does this lesson teach about the continent's extent?
**Answer:** America's extent is the distance the continent covers from its far north to its far south.

**2.** What does this lesson teach about its limits?
**Answer:** America's limits are the oceans and lands that border the continent on every side.
