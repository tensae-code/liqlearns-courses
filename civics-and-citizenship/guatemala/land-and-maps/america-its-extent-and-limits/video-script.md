# Video script — America — Its Extent and Limits

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
America is one great continent: knowing its extent — how far it reaches — and its limits — what borders it — is the first step to locating every place in it.

America stretches from far northern lands to far southern lands, one of the largest continents on Earth. Its extent is the distance it covers from one end to the other, and the first explorers measured it voyage by voyage as they met coasts they had never seen.

A limit is where a land ends: oceans on its sides, and other lands or seas at its ends. Naming America's limits — the waters and lands that border it — fixes the continent on the map of the world before any country inside it is studied.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
