# Hands-on — America — Its Extent and Limits

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Draw a long line on paper for a continent. Mark where it begins and where it ends, and name what lies beyond each end. This lesson measures America itself.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: America is one great continent: knowing its extent — how far it reaches — and its limits — what borders it — is the first step to locating every place in it. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the community life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
