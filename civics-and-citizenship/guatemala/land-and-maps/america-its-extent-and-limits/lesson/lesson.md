# America — Its Extent and Limits

## Do it first

Draw a long line on paper for a continent. Mark where it begins and where it ends, and name what lies beyond each end. This lesson measures America itself.

## Sub lesson 1 — The continent's extent

America stretches from far northern lands to far southern lands, one of the largest continents on Earth. Its extent is the distance it covers from one end to the other, and the first explorers measured it voyage by voyage as they met coasts they had never seen.

## Sub lesson 2 — Its limits

A limit is where a land ends: oceans on its sides, and other lands or seas at its ends. Naming America's limits — the waters and lands that border it — fixes the continent on the map of the world before any country inside it is studied.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: America is one great continent: knowing its extent — how far it reaches — and its limits — what borders it — is the first step to locating every place in it. Give one real example from your own life or from the Guatemala you live in, and name the lesson's key words: extent, limits, continent. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
