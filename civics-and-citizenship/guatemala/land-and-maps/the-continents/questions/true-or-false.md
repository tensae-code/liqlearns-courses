# The Continents — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The continents are America, Europe, Africa, Asia, Oceania and Antarctica — each with its extent and limits.
**Answer: True**

**2.** There are only three continents: America, Europe and Africa.
**Answer: False** — The continents are America, Europe, Africa, Asia, Oceania and Antarctica — each with its extent and limits.
