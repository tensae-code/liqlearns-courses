# Hands-on — The Continents

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name every continent before looking at any map. Then check yourself. This lesson walks the Earth's great lands one by one.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: The Earth's land is divided into continents — America, Europe, Africa, Asia, Oceania and Antarctica — each with its own extent and limits, to be known one by one. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the community life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
