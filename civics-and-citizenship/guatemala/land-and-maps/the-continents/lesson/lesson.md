# The Continents

## Do it first

Name every continent before looking at any map. Then check yourself. This lesson walks the Earth's great lands one by one.

## Sub lesson 1 — The continents one by one

America reaches from Arctic to Antarctic seas; Europe is its smaller western neighbour across the Atlantic; Africa stands between ocean and ocean below Europe; Asia is the largest of all; Oceania scatters its islands across the Pacific; and Antarctica, the ice continent, closes the list. Each continent has its extent and its limits, as America does.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The Earth's land is divided into continents — America, Europe, Africa, Asia, Oceania and Antarctica — each with its own extent and limits, to be known one by one. Give one real example from your own life or from the Guatemala you live in, and name the lesson's key words: continent, Oceania, Antarctica. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
