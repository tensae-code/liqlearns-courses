# Video script — The Continents

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The Earth's land is divided into continents — America, Europe, Africa, Asia, Oceania and Antarctica — each with its own extent and limits, to be known one by one.

America reaches from Arctic to Antarctic seas; Europe is its smaller western neighbour across the Atlantic; Africa stands between ocean and ocean below Europe; Asia is the largest of all; Oceania scatters its islands across the Pacific; and Antarctica, the ice continent, closes the list. Each continent has its extent and its limits, as America does.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
