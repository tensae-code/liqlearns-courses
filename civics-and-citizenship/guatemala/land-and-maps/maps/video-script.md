# Video script — Maps

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A map is a partial representation of the Earth — a drawing of a territory on a flat surface, with signs and a scale — and reading one means turning those signs back into the real land.

No map shows everything: a map represents part of the Earth on a flat surface, choosing what to show — borders, rivers, towns, roads — and shrinking it by a scale so a whole territory fits on one sheet.

To read a map, use its signs: the legend explains the symbols, the cardinal points give direction, and the scale turns centimetres on paper into real distance on the land.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
