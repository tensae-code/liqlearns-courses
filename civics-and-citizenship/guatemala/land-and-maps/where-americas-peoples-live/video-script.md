# Video script — Where America's Peoples Live

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
America's peoples live spread across some 15,000 kilometres of continent — country by country, each with its capital and its languages, from the far north to the far south.

From north to south, America is a chain of countries across about 15,000 km. The book's table sets each country beside its capital and its languages, so the continent can be read as peoples in places, not empty land.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
