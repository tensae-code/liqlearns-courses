# Hands-on — Where America's Peoples Live

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name every American country you can, north to south. This lesson fills the continent in — where each people lives, its capital and its tongue.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: America's peoples live spread across some 15,000 kilometres of continent — country by country, each with its capital and its languages, from the far north to the far south. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the community life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
