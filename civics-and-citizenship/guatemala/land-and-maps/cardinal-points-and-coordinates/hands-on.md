# Hands-on — Cardinal Points and Coordinates

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Stand and point to where the sun rises. That is east. This lesson turns that pointing into a system that can find any place on the planet.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: The cardinal points — north, south, east, west — and the geographic coordinates of latitude and longitude let us locate any place on Earth exactly. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the community life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
