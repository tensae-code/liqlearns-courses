This lesson is about one thing.
The cardinal points — north, south, east, west — and the geographic coordinates of latitude and longitude let us locate any place on Earth exactly.

The cardinal points are the four great directions: north and south along the Earth's axis, east where the sun rises and west where it sets. Every other direction is found from these four.

Geographic coordinates fix a place by two numbers: latitude, its distance north or south of the equator, and longitude, its distance east or west of the prime meridian. Together they give every point on Earth its own address.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
