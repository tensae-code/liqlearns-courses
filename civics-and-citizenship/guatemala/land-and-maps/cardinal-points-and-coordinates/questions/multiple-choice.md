# Cardinal Points and Coordinates — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** From which direction does the sun rise?
a) East  b) North  c) South  d) West
**Answer: a) East** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What do latitude and longitude together give a place?
a) Its exact location on Earth  b) Its population  c) Its language  d) Its weather tomorrow
**Answer: a) Its exact location on Earth** — That is what this lesson teaches.
