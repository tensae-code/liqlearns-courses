# Cardinal Points and Coordinates — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The cardinal points are north, south, east and west — the four directions all others are found from.
**Answer: True**

**2.** North is the direction the sun rises in.
**Answer: False** — The cardinal points are north, south, east and west — the four directions all others are found from.
