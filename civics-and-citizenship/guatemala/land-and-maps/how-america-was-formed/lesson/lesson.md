# How America Was Formed

## Do it first

Build a small landscape of earth or clay: a hill, a flat, a hollow for water. The real continent was shaped on a scale beyond imagining. This lesson tells how.

## Sub lesson 1 — The continent's formation

The geographic formation of America is the story of its land: great masses that became the north and the south, joined at Central America, with their mountain chains, plains and coasts raised and shaped over geological time.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: America's land itself has a formation: the continent took its shape over immense time as its masses, mountains and coasts were formed — the stage on which all its history stands. Give one real example from your own life or from the Guatemala you live in, and name the lesson's key words: formation, relief, geological time. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
