# Video script — How America Was Formed

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
America's land itself has a formation: the continent took its shape over immense time as its masses, mountains and coasts were formed — the stage on which all its history stands.

The geographic formation of America is the story of its land: great masses that became the north and the south, joined at Central America, with their mountain chains, plains and coasts raised and shaped over geological time.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
