# How America Was Formed — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** America was formed over geological time — northern and southern masses joined at Central America, with their relief.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The continent has always had exactly today's shape, unchanged forever.
**Answer: False** — America was formed over geological time — northern and southern masses joined at Central America, with their relief.
