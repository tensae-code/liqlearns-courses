# Solidarity

## Do it first

Remember a time someone stood by you when you could not stand alone — and a time you did it for another. This lesson names that bond: solidarity.

## Sub lesson 1 — A social being

No one grows alone: we are born into families and societies, learn language and life from others, and need others at every age. Solidarity is that truth turned into conduct — feeling another's need as a call to stand beside them.

## Sub lesson 2 — Living together in solidarity

Convivencia solidaria — living together in solidarity — is the society built on that bond: harmony instead of inequality and exclusion, where each person's dignity is guarded by all. It is the opposite of every exclusion this course names.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The human being is a social being, born and growing in society — and solidarity is living that truth: standing with others in their need, so society is built on harmony, not on inequality and exclusion. Give one real example from your own life or from the Guatemala you live in, and name the lesson's key words: solidarity, social being, convivencia. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
