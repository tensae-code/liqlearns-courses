# Video script — Solidarity

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The human being is a social being, born and growing in society — and solidarity is living that truth: standing with others in their need, so society is built on harmony, not on inequality and exclusion.

No one grows alone: we are born into families and societies, learn language and life from others, and need others at every age. Solidarity is that truth turned into conduct — feeling another's need as a call to stand beside them.

Convivencia solidaria — living together in solidarity — is the society built on that bond: harmony instead of inequality and exclusion, where each person's dignity is guarded by all. It is the opposite of every exclusion this course names.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
