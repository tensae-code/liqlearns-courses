# Hands-on — Solidarity

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Remember a time someone stood by you when you could not stand alone — and a time you did it for another. This lesson names that bond: solidarity.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: The human being is a social being, born and growing in society — and solidarity is living that truth: standing with others in their need, so society is built on harmony, not on inequality and exclusion. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the community life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
