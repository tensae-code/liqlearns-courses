# Duties in the Family — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Every family member holds obligations — care and provision from parents; respect, study and help from children.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Only parents have obligations in a family; children have none.
**Answer: False** — Every family member holds obligations — care and provision from parents; respect, study and help from children.
