# Duties in the Family

## Do it first

List what each person in your home does for the home. That list is this lesson: the obligations of the family.

## Sub lesson 1 — Obligations of each member

Inside the family, obligations are shared by place and age: parents hold the duties of care, provision and guidance; children hold duties of respect, study and help suited to their years. When each member carries their part, the family protects and raises everyone in it.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: A family is a group living together, and every member has obligations in it — parents to care and provide, children to help and respect — so the home works for everyone in it. Give one real example from your own life or from the Guatemala you live in, and name the lesson's key words: obligation, family, provision. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: A family is a group living together, and every member has obligations in it — parents to care and provide, children to help and respect — so the home works for everyone in it.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
