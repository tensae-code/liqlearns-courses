# Hands-on — Duties in the Family

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
List what each person in your home does for the home. That list is this lesson: the obligations of the family.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: A family is a group living together, and every member has obligations in it — parents to care and provide, children to help and respect — so the home works for everyone in it. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the community life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
