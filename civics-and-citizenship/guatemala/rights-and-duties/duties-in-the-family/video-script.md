# Video script — Duties in the Family

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A family is a group living together, and every member has obligations in it — parents to care and provide, children to help and respect — so the home works for everyone in it.

Inside the family, obligations are shared by place and age: parents hold the duties of care, provision and guidance; children hold duties of respect, study and help suited to their years. When each member carries their part, the family protects and raises everyone in it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
