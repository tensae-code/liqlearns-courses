# Children's Rights — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the Convention on the Rights of the Child?
a) The first international instrument gathering all children's rights  b) A school rule book  c) A Guatemalan tax law  d) A list of duties only
**Answer: a) The first international instrument gathering all children's rights** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does Guatemala's Ley de Protección Integral protect?
a) Children and adolescents, integrally  b) Only adults  c) Only property  d) Only drivers
**Answer: a) Children and adolescents, integrally** — That is what this lesson teaches.
