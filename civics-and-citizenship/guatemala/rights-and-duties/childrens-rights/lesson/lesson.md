# Children's Rights

## Do it first

List three things every child should always have — and three things no one should ever do to a child. This lesson shows those lists written into law, for the whole world and for Guatemala.

## Sub lesson 1 — The rights of children and young people

Children are not small adults without rights: they hold rights of their own — to life, a name, education, health, protection and participation — because they are still growing and need special care.

## Sub lesson 2 — The Convention and Guatemala's law

The Convention on the Rights of the Child is the international agreement that gathers all these rights in one instrument, the first to do so. Guatemala adds its own shield: the Ley de Protección Integral de la Niñez y Adolescencia, the law of integral protection of children and adolescents.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Children and young people have their own rights, gathered in the Convention on the Rights of the Child — the first international instrument holding them all — and protected in Guatemala by the Ley de Protección Integral de la Niñez y Adolescencia. Give one real example from your own life or from the Guatemala you live in, and name the lesson's key words: Convention on the Rights of the Child, children's rights, protection law. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
