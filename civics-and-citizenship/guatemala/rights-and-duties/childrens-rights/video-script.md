# Video script — Children's Rights

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Children and young people have their own rights, gathered in the Convention on the Rights of the Child — the first international instrument holding them all — and protected in Guatemala by the Ley de Protección Integral de la Niñez y Adolescencia.

Children are not small adults without rights: they hold rights of their own — to life, a name, education, health, protection and participation — because they are still growing and need special care.

The Convention on the Rights of the Child is the international agreement that gathers all these rights in one instrument, the first to do so. Guatemala adds its own shield: the Ley de Protección Integral de la Niñez y Adolescencia, the law of integral protection of children and adolescents.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
