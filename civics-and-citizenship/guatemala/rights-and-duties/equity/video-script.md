# Video script — Equity

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Equity is justice in giving: treating people equally does not always mean giving everyone the same — equity gives each person what they need, and gender equity means women and men have the same opportunities.

Equity joins equality with justice: each person receives what they need to truly have the same chance. The book's workshop practises seeing situations through equity — who needs what, so that no one is left behind by a sameness that only looks fair.

Gender equity applies the same justice between women and men: equal opportunity to study, work, decide and be respected, against customs that give one sex more chances than the other.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
