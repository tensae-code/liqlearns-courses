# Equity

## Do it first

Imagine two children, one tall and one short, both trying to see over a fence. Giving both the same box is equal; giving the shorter child a taller box is fair. This lesson is about that fairness — equity.

## Sub lesson 1 — Equity as justice

Equity joins equality with justice: each person receives what they need to truly have the same chance. The book's workshop practises seeing situations through equity — who needs what, so that no one is left behind by a sameness that only looks fair.

## Sub lesson 2 — Gender equity

Gender equity applies the same justice between women and men: equal opportunity to study, work, decide and be respected, against customs that give one sex more chances than the other.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Equity is justice in giving: treating people equally does not always mean giving everyone the same — equity gives each person what they need, and gender equity means women and men have the same opportunities. Give one real example from your own life or from the Guatemala you live in, and name the lesson's key words: equity, equality, gender equity. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
