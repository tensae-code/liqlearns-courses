# Hands-on — Equity

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Imagine two children, one tall and one short, both trying to see over a fence. Giving both the same box is equal; giving the shorter child a taller box is fair. This lesson is about that fairness — equity.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Equity is justice in giving: treating people equally does not always mean giving everyone the same — equity gives each person what they need, and gender equity means women and men have the same opportunities. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the community life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
