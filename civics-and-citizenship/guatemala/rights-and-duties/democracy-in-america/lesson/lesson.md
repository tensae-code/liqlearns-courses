# Democracy in America

## Do it first

Your class must choose between two games. Hands go up; the majority decides; the minority accepts and plays. That small act is this lesson's great word: democracy.

## Sub lesson 1 — Government of the people

Democracy comes from Greek: demos, the people, and kratos, government. It is the form of government in which power belongs to the people, who exercise it by choosing their rulers in elections and by the rights that let them speak, organise and oversee.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Democracy means government of the people — from the Greek demos, the people, and kratos, government or power — and as a form of government in America it rests on citizens choosing and watching over their rulers. Give one real example from your own life or from the Guatemala you live in, and name the lesson's key words: democracy, demos, kratos. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
