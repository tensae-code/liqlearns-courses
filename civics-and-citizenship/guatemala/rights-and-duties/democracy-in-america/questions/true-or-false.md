# Democracy in America — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Democracy — demos, the people; kratos, government — is the people's power, exercised by elections and rights.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Democracy means one ruler deciding everything for the people.
**Answer: False** — Democracy — demos, the people; kratos, government — is the people's power, exercised by elections and rights.
