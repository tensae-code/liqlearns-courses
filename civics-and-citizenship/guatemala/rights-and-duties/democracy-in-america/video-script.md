This lesson is about one thing.
Democracy means government of the people — from the Greek demos, the people, and kratos, government or power — and as a form of government in America it rests on citizens choosing and watching over their rulers.

Democracy comes from Greek: demos, the people, and kratos, government. It is the form of government in which power belongs to the people, who exercise it by choosing their rulers in elections and by the rights that let them speak, organise and oversee.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
