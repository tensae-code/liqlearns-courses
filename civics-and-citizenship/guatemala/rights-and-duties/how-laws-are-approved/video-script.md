This lesson is about one thing.
In Guatemala, laws are approved by the Legislative power — the Congress of the Republic: a proposal is presented, debated and voted, and only then becomes a law that binds everyone.

The power to make laws is the Legislative power, held by the Congress of the Republic of Guatemala. A law begins as an initiative, is studied and debated by the deputies, is voted — and, approved and sanctioned, becomes binding on all. Knowing the path is knowing how the rules that govern us are born.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
