# Video script — Economic, Social and Cultural Rights

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Economic, social and cultural rights are the rights that give people an appropriate standard of living — work, health, education, housing, food and culture — the material ground on which all other rights stand.

Beside the civil and political rights stand the economic, social and cultural rights: to work and fair conditions, to health, to education, to housing and food, to take part in cultural life. Together they promise every person an appropriate standard of living.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
