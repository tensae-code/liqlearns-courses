# Economic, Social and Cultural Rights

## Do it first

A person may vote freely but be hungry, unschooled and unhoused. This lesson is about the rights that fill that gap: the rights to live decently.

## Sub lesson 1 — Rights to a decent life

Beside the civil and political rights stand the economic, social and cultural rights: to work and fair conditions, to health, to education, to housing and food, to take part in cultural life. Together they promise every person an appropriate standard of living.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Economic, social and cultural rights are the rights that give people an appropriate standard of living — work, health, education, housing, food and culture — the material ground on which all other rights stand. Give one real example from your own life or from the Guatemala you live in, and name the lesson's key words: economic, social and cultural rights, standard of living, civil rights. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
