# Economic, Social and Cultural Rights — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Economic, social and cultural rights promise an appropriate standard of living — work, health, education, housing, culture.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Rights are only about voting and speaking; living conditions are not rights.
**Answer: False** — Economic, social and cultural rights promise an appropriate standard of living — work, health, education, housing, culture.
