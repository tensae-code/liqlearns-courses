# Citizenship — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** At what age does a Guatemalan gain citizenship?
a) 18  b) 16  c) 21  d) At birth with no change
**Answer: a) 18** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Un Techo Para Mi País is an example of…
a) Citizenship in action — organised young people serving others  b) A tax office  c) A political party only  d) A construction company for profit
**Answer: a) Citizenship in action — organised young people serving others** — That is what this lesson teaches.
