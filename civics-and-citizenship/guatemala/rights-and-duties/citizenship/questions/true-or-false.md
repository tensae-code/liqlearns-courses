# Citizenship — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Citizenship comes at majority — 18 in Guatemala — with labour, civic and quality-of-life rights, and the vote.
**Answer: True**

**Answer: False** — Citizenship comes at majority — 18 in Guatemala — with labour, civic and quality-of-life rights, and the vote.
