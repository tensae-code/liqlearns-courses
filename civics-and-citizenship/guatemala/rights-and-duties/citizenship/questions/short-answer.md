# Citizenship — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What does this lesson teach about citizenship at majority?
**Answer:** Citizenship comes at majority — 18 in Guatemala — with labour, civic and quality-of-life rights, and the vote.

**2.** What does this lesson teach about the functions of citizenship?
**Answer:** The functions of citizenship are what citizens do: vote, oversee, defend the constitution, contribute and participate.
