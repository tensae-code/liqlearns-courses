# Hands-on — Citizenship

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name one thing adults in your town may do that children may not — and one thing good adults do for their town without being paid. Both halves are this lesson: citizenship.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Citizenship is the quality and right a person gains at majority — 18 in Guatemala — carrying labour, civic and quality-of-life rights and the vote; full citizenship is commitment to country and community, and citizens prove it in action, like the young people of Un Techo Para Mi País. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the community life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
