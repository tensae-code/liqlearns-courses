# Hands-on — Rights and Duties in School

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Make two lists. On one side, write what you have a right to at school. On the other, write what is your duty at school. This lesson is the course's frame: rights and duties, held together.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: every person holds individual and collective rights, and a pupil carries duties and rights together — at school and in society. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the community life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
