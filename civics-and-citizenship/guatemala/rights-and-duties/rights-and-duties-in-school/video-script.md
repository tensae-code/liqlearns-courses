# Video script — Rights and Duties in School

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Every person holds individual and collective rights, and a pupil carries duties and rights together — at school and in society.

Some rights belong to a person alone — individual rights: they protect each single person. Some rights belong to people together — collective rights: they protect a people or a community as a whole.

The course's competence frame names both kinds together: a citizen knows their own rights, and the rights they hold with others.

At school the two sides meet every day. A pupil has rights at school — and duties at school: the things they owe their teachers, their classmates and the school itself.

Rights without duties would leave everyone unprotected; duties without rights would leave the pupil without a voice. The frame holds them as one pair.

What is true at school is true in society: each person's duties and rights run side by side, at school and in society alike.

This frame stands behind everything that follows in the course: when the lessons name a violence, they name a right being broken — and a duty calling people to protect it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
