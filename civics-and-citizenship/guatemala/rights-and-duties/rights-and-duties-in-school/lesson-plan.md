## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Individual and collective rights** — Some rights belong to a person alone — individual rights: they protect each single person. Some rights belong to people together — collective rights: they protect a people or a community as a whole.
2. **Duties and rights at school** — At school the two sides meet every day. A pupil has rights at school — and duties at school: the things they owe their teachers, their classmates and the school itself.
3. **Duties and rights in society** — What is true at school is true in society: each person's duties and rights run side by side, at school and in society alike.
