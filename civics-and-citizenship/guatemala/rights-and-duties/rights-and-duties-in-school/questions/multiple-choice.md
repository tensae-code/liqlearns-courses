# Rights and Duties in School — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What two kinds of rights does the course's frame name?
a) individual rights and collective rights  b) school rights and home rights only  c) old rights and new rights  d) rights for teachers only
**Answer: a) individual rights and collective rights** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** How do duties and rights stand at school?
a) together — a pupil carries both at once  b) duties only, with no rights  c) rights only, with no duties  d) neither — school has no duties or rights
**Answer: a) together — a pupil carries both at once** — That is what this lesson teaches.
