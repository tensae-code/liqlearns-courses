# Citizen Participation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Participation is taking part and being part — rights bound to responsibilities, including serving and defending the homeland.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Voting once is the whole of citizen participation.
**Answer: False** — Participation is taking part and being part — rights bound to responsibilities, including serving and defending the homeland.
