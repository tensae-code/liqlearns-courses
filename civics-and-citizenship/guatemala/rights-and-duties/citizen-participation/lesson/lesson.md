# Citizen Participation

## Do it first

A team is not its eleven players only; it is everyone who shows up. This lesson is about showing up for your society: participation.

## Sub lesson 1 — Taking part, being part

Citizen participation is more than voting: it is taking part — in decisions, organisations and public life — and being part, a member who belongs and answers for the whole. Rights and responsibilities travel together in it, as the Constitution binds them: among the citizen's duties stand serving and defending the homeland.

## Sub lesson 2 — Community activities

Community activities are participation at home scale: neighbours organising to clean, build, celebrate, protect and decide together. In them the community stops being a crowd of houses and becomes a body that acts.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Participation is taking part and being part of society: citizens participate in elections, in community activities where the community acts together — serving and defending the homeland among the constitutional duties that bind rights to responsibilities. Give one real example from your own life or from the Guatemala you live in, and name the lesson's key words: participation, community activity, responsibility. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
