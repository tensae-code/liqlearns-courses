# Hands-on — Citizen Participation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
A team is not its eleven players only; it is everyone who shows up. This lesson is about showing up for your society: participation.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Participation is taking part and being part of society: citizens participate in elections, in community activities where the community acts together — serving and defending the homeland among the constitutional duties that bind rights to responsibilities. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the community life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
