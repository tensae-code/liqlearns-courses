# Video script — Citizen Participation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Participation is taking part and being part of society: citizens participate in elections, in community activities where the community acts together — serving and defending the homeland among the constitutional duties that bind rights to responsibilities.

Citizen participation is more than voting: it is taking part — in decisions, organisations and public life — and being part, a member who belongs and answers for the whole. Rights and responsibilities travel together in it, as the Constitution binds them: among the citizen's duties stand serving and defending the homeland.

Community activities are participation at home scale: neighbours organising to clean, build, celebrate, protect and decide together. In them the community stops being a crowd of houses and becomes a body that acts.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
