# Hands-on — Paying Taxes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Your school's roof, the road to your town, the health post: who paid for them? This lesson follows the money: taxes.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Taxes are the state's most important income — the money citizens contribute so the state can serve everyone — and tax culture is the citizen's commitment to that common wellbeing. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the community life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
