# Video script — Paying Taxes

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Taxes are the state's most important income — the money citizens contribute so the state can serve everyone — and tax culture is the citizen's commitment to that common wellbeing.

A tax is a contribution the law requires from citizens and businesses. Gathered together, taxes are the state's most important income — from them come schools, hospitals, roads and every public service no family could buy alone.

Tax culture is the citizen's side of the bargain: knowing why taxes exist, paying them honestly, and watching that they are well used. The book's project — an information leaflet on tax culture — teaches citizens to spread that commitment.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
