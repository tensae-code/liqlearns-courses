# Paying Taxes — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Taxes are citizens' contributions and the state's most important income, funding public services.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Taxes vanish; they pay for nothing anyone can see.
**Answer: False** — Taxes are citizens' contributions and the state's most important income, funding public services.
