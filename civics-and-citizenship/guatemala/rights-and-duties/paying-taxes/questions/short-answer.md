# Paying Taxes — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What does this lesson teach about what taxes are for?
**Answer:** Taxes are citizens' contributions and the state's most important income, funding public services.

**2.** What does this lesson teach about tax culture?
**Answer:** Tax culture is knowing, honestly paying and watching over taxes as a commitment to everyone's wellbeing.
