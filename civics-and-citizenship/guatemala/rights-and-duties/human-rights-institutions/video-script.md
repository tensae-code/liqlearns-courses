# Video script — Human Rights Institutions

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Across America there are institutions and organisations whose work is to watch over human rights — and above the national ones stands the Inter-American Court of Human Rights, the region's court for those principles.

Rights need guardians. Human rights institutions — national offices, ombudsmen and organisations across America — exist to watch that governments and people respect the rights of all, and to act when those rights are broken.

Above the national guardians stands a regional one: the Inter-American Court of Human Rights, which watches over the principles of human rights across the continent and judges cases where people have not found justice at home. It is the region's court of last resort for human rights.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
