# Civics and Citizenship — Switzerland course — CURRICULUM (the path)

One course for Switzerland inside the Civics and Citizenship
folder, organised by **levels, not grades**: a learner climbs
when they have proved the level, not when a school calendar
says so. The school grades the books come from appear only in
each lesson's sources (provenance), never as the course's
organisation. The path follows the books' own order: the one
Graubünden unit *Sorge oder Zwang? / Assistenza o coercizione?
/ Agid u sforz?* (Care or coercion?) — first at Sekundarstufe I,
the pupil reader's five cases with the Sek I companion, then at
Sekundarstufe II, the same cases re-worked from the full
documentary record in the Sek II companion.

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in
this course comes from government textbooks — this country's
own Graubünden teaching unit on coercive welfare measures —
except the **Tip boxes**: a tip in a box is from us, the people
making LiqLearns. Tips are ours; the lessons are the books'.
See ABOUT.md for the full book list, the phantom-push records,
the UNREAD Romansh Sek I companion, and the acceptance
reckoning.

## The levels (the path, in order)

1. **Care or Coercion? at Sekundarstufe I** — what coercive
   welfare measures were and the law behind them (including
   the Confederation's measures and Pro Juventute's 'children
   of the road' action, about 600 victims); how the unit is
   taught at Sek I (source work, Lehrplan 21 competences, roots
   in cantonal poor law); then the five cases in the reader's
   own order — the Albin family, Uschi Waser, Cornelia Studer,
   Florian Branger, Ruedi Hofer — and the closing question,
   whether amends can be made (rehabilitation, the official
   apologies of 2010/2013/2017, solidarity contributions).
   8 lessons, in one title: `care-or-coercion/` (8 lessons).
2. **Care or Coercion? at Sekundarstufe II** — the same five
   cases re-worked from the full documentary record: the
   guardianship authority's decisions and the Piccolo
   Consiglio's 1955 considerations, Pro Juventute's own files
   and Dr Siegfried's letters, the home's own records, the 1932
   press and Loosli's critique, Hofer's own proposal and the
   state's apology as a document — judging the machinery of
   the welfare state, and whether amends can be made. 1 new
   lesson (Teaching the Unit at Sek II), in the same title
   `care-or-coercion/`; 5 Level 1 lessons are deepened at this
   level (The Albin Family · Uschi Waser — Children of the
   Road · Behind the Facades of a Home · Florian Branger —
   Guilt and Internment · Can Amends Be Made?).

**Totals: 9 lessons** (Level 1: 8 · Level 2: 1 new lesson, with
5 Level 1 lessons deepened — 19 placements stocked in all:
7 pupil-reader placements, 6 Sek I companion placements,
6 Sek II companion placements).

*Standing note on the shape: Switzerland's in-hand civics set
is this single cantonal unit only — there is no general Swiss
civics textbook in the census for this country, so the course
in hand is one unit deep; the ladder has two levels because
the books themselves come in Sek I and Sek II forms.*
