# Civics and Citizenship — Switzerland course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

Civics belongs to the country: this course is Switzerland's
own — built from the Kanton Graubünden teaching unit *Sorge
oder Zwang? / Assistenza o coercizione? / Agid u sforz?* (Care
or coercion?, Lehrmittel Graubünden, 1st edition 2020): the
pupil reader (Italian edition, STUDENT, COMPLETE) and its two
teacher companions (Sek I, German; Sek II, Italian — TEACHER,
evidence). Inside the course the organisation is by **levels,
not grades**: the title below is the unit the books themselves
form, in path order — what the learner proves, step by step.
The grades the books come from live only in each lesson's
sources.

The path's own logic is the unit's: first the measures and the
law behind them, then five true cases in the reader's own
order — a family dissolved, a child of the road moved 22
times, a home's facades, a man interned again and again, a
child passed from place to place — then the closing question,
whether amends can be made; and one level up, the same cases
judged again from the full documentary record. That ladder IS
the path order (recommended, never forced — a learner can jump
in higher; the "start here instead" hints point down this
ladder).

## The titles

**Levels 1–2 — one unit, two depths** (the Graubünden RZG/ERG
unit on coercive welfare measures):

- `care-or-coercion/` — Levels 1–2. When the State Steps In
  (what coercive welfare measures were, the legal instruments
  behind them, the 'children of the road' action, and the
  questions the unit asks) · Teaching the Unit at Sek I (how
  the unit is taught at Sek I — source work with the reader,
  Lehrplan 21 competences, roots in cantonal poor law;
  teacher-guide evidence only) · The Albin Family (eight
  children, poverty, illness and moral judgement — the
  authorities decide the parents cannot raise them, told
  through the social worker's file notes; deepened at Level 2
  by the guardianship decisions and the Piccolo Consiglio's
  1955 considerations) · Uschi Waser — Children of the Road
  (a Yenish victim of Pro Juventute's action, 22 placements,
  and whom the judge believed; deepened at Level 2 by Pro
  Juventute's own documentation and Dr Siegfried's letters) ·
  Behind the Facades of a Home (Cornelia Studer's memoirs of
  life inside a children's home — humiliation and hard labour
  — and her 2016 reckoning; deepened at Level 2 by the home's
  own records) · Florian Branger — Guilt and Internment (a man
  interned again and again for offences and 'way of life' —
  his guilt or the system's?; deepened at Level 2 by the 1932
  press, Loosli's critique and the cemetery inquiry) · Ruedi
  Hofer (passed from place to place, seriously injured,
  exploited and abused — and when a bad time ends) · Can
  Amends Be Made? (rehabilitation, the official apologies —
  Widmer-Schlumpf 2010, Sommaruga 2013, Parolini 2017 —
  solidarity contributions, and working through the past;
  deepened at Level 2 by Hofer's own proposal and the state's
  apology as document D9) · Teaching the Unit at Sek II (how
  the unit is taught at Sek II — didactic principles, the
  history at greater depth, and judging the machinery of the
  welfare state from the full record; teacher-guide evidence
  only).
