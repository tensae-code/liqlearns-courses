Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's first teaching — A victim of the children of the road?
a) Uschi Waser was a victim of Pro Juventute's 'children of the road' action against Yenish families, which removed children from their families because of their travelling way of life.  b) it has nothing to do with rights or the state  c) it was invented outside Switzerland  d) it matters only in examinations
