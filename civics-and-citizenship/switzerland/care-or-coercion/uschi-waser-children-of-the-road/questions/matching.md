# Uschi Waser — Children of the Road — matching

Match each part of the lesson with its teaching.

**1.** A victim of the children of the road — a)

**2.** Twenty-two placements — b)

**3.** Whom did the judge believe? — c)


Meanings:
a) Uschi Waser was a victim of Pro Juventute's 'children of the road' action against Yenish families, which removed children from their families because of their travelling way of life.
b) Waser's life was shaped by 22 placements — moved from place to place, never given the stable life the measures claimed to provide; the Sek I teaching presents her as the defenceless victim of the action, in the context of the family dissolutions of the early 1970s.
c) The reader asks 'A chi credette il giudice?' — whom did the judge believe? At Sek II depth the case is worked from Pro Juventute's own summary documentation and Dr Siegfried's letters as sources.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b; 3. c
