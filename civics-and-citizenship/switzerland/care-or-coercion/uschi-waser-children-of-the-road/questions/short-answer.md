# Uschi Waser — Children of the Road — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "A victim of the children of the road" teach?
**Answer:** Uschi Waser was a victim of Pro Juventute's 'children of the road' action against Yenish families, which removed children from their families because of their travelling way of life.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** Uschi Waser, a Yenish victim of Pro Juventute's 'children of the road' action, was moved through 22 placements in search of a stable life that the system never gave her — and when it came to judgement, the judge believed the authorities.
