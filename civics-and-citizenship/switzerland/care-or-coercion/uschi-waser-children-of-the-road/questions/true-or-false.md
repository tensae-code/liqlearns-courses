# Uschi Waser — Children of the Road — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Waser's life was shaped by 22 placements — moved from place to place, never given the stable life the measures claimed to provide; the Sek I teaching presents her as the defenceless victim of the action, in the context of the family dissolutions of the early 1970s.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that Uschi Waser, a Yenish victim of Pro Juventute's 'children of the road' action, was moved through 22 placements in search of a stable life that the system never gave her — and when it came to judgement, the judge believed the authorities — and that it has no connection to the rest of this unit's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson stands inside the one Graubünden unit, case by case.
