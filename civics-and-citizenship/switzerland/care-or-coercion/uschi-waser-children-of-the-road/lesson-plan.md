# Lesson plan — Uschi Waser — Children of the Road

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Switzerland · **Title:** Care or Coercion? · **Lesson:** Uschi Waser — Children of the Road
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 (deepened at Level 2) — Care or Coercion? (CURRICULUM.md).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **A victim of the children of the road** — Uschi Waser was a victim of Pro Juventute's 'children of the road' action against Yenish families, which removed children from their families because of their travelling way of life.
2. **Twenty-two placements** — Waser's life was shaped by 22 placements — moved from place to place, never given the stable life the measures claimed to provide; the Sek I teaching presents her as the defenceless victim of the action, in the context of the family dissolutions of the early 1970s.
3. **Whom did the judge believe?** — The reader asks 'A chi credette il giudice?' — whom did the judge believe? At Sek II depth the case is worked from Pro Juventute's own summary documentation and Dr Siegfried's letters as sources.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
