# Hands-on — Uschi Waser — Children of the Road

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write down what a child needs to stay in one place: one home, one school, people who stay. Now count what happens to that list if the child is moved again and again. Uschi Waser was moved 22 times — this lesson follows her case.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Uschi Waser, a Yenish victim of Pro Juventute's 'children of the road' action, was moved through 22 placements in search of a stable life that the system never gave her — and when it came to judgement, the judge believed the authorities. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
