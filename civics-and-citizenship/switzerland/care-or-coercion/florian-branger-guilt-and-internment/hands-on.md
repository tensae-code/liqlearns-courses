# Hands-on — Florian Branger — Guilt and Internment

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write two columns: 'his fault' and 'the system's fault'. As you work this lesson, put every fact about Florian Branger into one column or the other — and notice which column the authorities of his day used, and which one they never opened.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Florian Branger was interned again and again in a work institution for his offences and his 'way of life' — his case asks how far that was guilt by his own fault, and how far the fault of the system that kept interning him. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
