# Florian Branger — Guilt and Internment — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "In open sea — and interned" teach?
**Answer:** Branger's story — told under the heading 'in open sea, and interned' — is of a man repeatedly interned in a work institution for offences and for his way of life.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** Florian Branger was interned again and again in a work institution for his offences and his 'way of life' — his case asks how far that was guilt by his own fault, and how far the fault of the system that kept interning him.
