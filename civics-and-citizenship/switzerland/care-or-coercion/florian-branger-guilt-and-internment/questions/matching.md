# Florian Branger — Guilt and Internment — matching

Match each part of the lesson with its teaching.

**1.** In open sea — and interned — a)

**2.** Punishment or welfare? — b)

**3.** Press, critics and the cemetery inquiry — c)


Meanings:
a) Branger's story — told under the heading 'in open sea, and interned' — is of a man repeatedly interned in a work institution for offences and for his way of life.
b) The Sek I teaching works the case between punishment and welfare: which norms were applied to men like Branger, and how guilt should be judged when the two are mixed.
c) At Sek II depth the case adds the public argument of the time: a 1932 newspaper article in the NZZ, C. A. Loosli's critique, and the inquiry into the work institution's cemetery.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b; 3. c
