# Florian Branger — Guilt and Internment — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Sek I teaching works the case between punishment and welfare: which norms were applied to men like Branger, and how guilt should be judged when the two are mixed.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that Florian Branger was interned again and again in a work institution for his offences and his 'way of life' — his case asks how far that was guilt by his own fault, and how far the fault of the system that kept interning him — and that it has no connection to the rest of this unit's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson stands inside the one Graubünden unit, case by case.
