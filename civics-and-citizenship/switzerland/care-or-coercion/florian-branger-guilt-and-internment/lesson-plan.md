# Lesson plan — Florian Branger — Guilt and Internment

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Switzerland · **Title:** Care or Coercion? · **Lesson:** Florian Branger — Guilt and Internment
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 (deepened at Level 2) — Care or Coercion? (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Florian Branger was interned again and again in a work institution for his offences and his 'way of life' — his case asks how far that was guilt by his own fault, and how far the fault of the system that kept interning him.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 (deepened at Level 2) — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **In open sea — and interned** — Branger's story — told under the heading 'in open sea, and interned' — is of a man repeatedly interned in a work institution for offences and for his way of life.
2. **Punishment or welfare?** — The Sek I teaching works the case between punishment and welfare: which norms were applied to men like Branger, and how guilt should be judged when the two are mixed.
3. **Press, critics and the cemetery inquiry** — At Sek II depth the case adds the public argument of the time: a 1932 newspaper article in the NZZ, C. A. Loosli's critique, and the inquiry into the work institution's cemetery.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
