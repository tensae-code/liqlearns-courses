# When the State Steps In — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's first teaching — What coercive welfare measures were?
a) Coercive welfare measures were official measures, presented as help, by which authorities intervened in people's lives: children were taken from their parents and placed in homes, institutions or other families, and adults were interned.  b) it has nothing to do with rights or the state  c) it was invented outside Switzerland  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Coercive welfare measures were official measures, presented as help, by which authorities intervened in people's lives: children were taken from their parents and placed in homes, institutions or other families, and adults were interned.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
