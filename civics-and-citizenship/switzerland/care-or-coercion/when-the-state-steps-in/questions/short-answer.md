# When the State Steps In — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "What coercive welfare measures were" teach?
**Answer:** Coercive welfare measures were official measures, presented as help, by which authorities intervened in people's lives: children were taken from their parents and placed in homes, institutions or other families, and adults were interned.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** In Graubünden, welfare authorities once stepped into people's lives with coercive measures — taking children from families, placing them in homes and institutions and interning adults — violating fundamental rights while oversight failed; this lesson asks when the state may intervene at all.
