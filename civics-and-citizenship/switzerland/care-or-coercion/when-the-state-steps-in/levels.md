# When the State Steps In — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — What coercive welfare measures were
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Coercive welfare measures were official measures, presented as help, by which authorities intervened in people's lives: children were taken from their parents and placed in homes, institutions or other families, and adults were interned. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — The law behind it and the children of the road
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Behind the measures stood legal instruments, including measures of the Confederation, and actions such as Pro Juventute's 'children of the road', which took about 600 children from Yenish families. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 3 — The questions this unit asks
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files.
