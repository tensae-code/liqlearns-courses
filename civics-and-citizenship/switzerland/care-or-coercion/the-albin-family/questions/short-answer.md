# The Albin Family — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "Eight children and no family" teach?
**Answer:** The Albins were a married couple with eight children; poverty, illness and moral judgement of their way of life brought the authorities into their home.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** The Albin family — a married couple with eight children, hit by poverty, illness and moral judgement — lost their children because the authorities decided the parents could not raise them.
