# The Albin Family — matching

Match each part of the lesson with its teaching.

**1.** Eight children and no family — a)

**2.** The authorities decide — b)

**3.** The decisions at full depth — c)


Meanings:
a) The Albins were a married couple with eight children; poverty, illness and moral judgement of their way of life brought the authorities into their home.
b) The authorities decided the parents could not raise their children; the case is told through the social worker's own file notes, and the Sek I teaching works it as family dissolution and out-of-family placement, with document work D1–D5.
c) At Sek II depth the case is re-worked from the guardianship authority's own decisions, with documents to D7, including the Piccolo Consiglio's considerations of 1955.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b; 3. c
