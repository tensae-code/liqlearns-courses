# The Albin Family — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The authorities decided the parents could not raise their children; the case is told through the social worker's own file notes, and the Sek I teaching works it as family dissolution and out-of-family placement, with document work D1–D5.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that the Albin family — a married couple with eight children, hit by poverty, illness and moral judgement — lost their children because the authorities decided the parents could not raise them — and that it has no connection to the rest of this unit's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson stands inside the one Graubünden unit, case by case.
