# The Albin Family — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's first teaching — Eight children and no family?
a) The Albins were a married couple with eight children; poverty, illness and moral judgement of their way of life brought the authorities into their home.  b) it has nothing to do with rights or the state  c) it was invented outside Switzerland  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The Albins were a married couple with eight children; poverty, illness and moral judgement of their way of life brought the authorities into their home.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) the Albin family — a married couple with eight children, hit by poverty, illness and moral judgement — lost their children because the authorities decided the parents could not raise them  b) welfare measures never touched anyone's rights  c) citizens should never question an authority's file  d) every case in Graubünden ended well
**Answer: a) the Albin family — a married couple with eight children, hit by poverty, illness and moral judgement — lost their children because the authorities decided the parents could not raise them** — That is what this lesson teaches.
