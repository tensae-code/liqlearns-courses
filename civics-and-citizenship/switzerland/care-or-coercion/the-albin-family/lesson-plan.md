# Lesson plan — The Albin Family

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Switzerland · **Title:** Care or Coercion? · **Lesson:** The Albin Family
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 (deepened at Level 2) — Care or Coercion? (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the Albin family — a married couple with eight children, hit by poverty, illness and moral judgement — lost their children because the authorities decided the parents could not raise them.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 (deepened at Level 2) — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Eight children and no family** — The Albins were a married couple with eight children; poverty, illness and moral judgement of their way of life brought the authorities into their home.
2. **The authorities decide** — The authorities decided the parents could not raise their children; the case is told through the social worker's own file notes, and the Sek I teaching works it as family dissolution and out-of-family placement, with document work D1–D5.
3. **The decisions at full depth** — At Sek II depth the case is re-worked from the guardianship authority's own decisions, with documents to D7, including the Piccolo Consiglio's considerations of 1955.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
