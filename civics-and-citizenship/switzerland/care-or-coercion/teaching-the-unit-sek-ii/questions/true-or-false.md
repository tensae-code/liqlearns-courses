# Teaching the Unit at Sek II — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The companion's introduction gives the history of coercive welfare measures at greater depth: abuse and exploitation inside the measures, and authorities failing in their duties.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that at Sek II the same five cases are re-worked from the full documentary record — council decisions, welfare-organisation files, press criticism and the state's own apology — so older pupils judge the machinery of the welfare state, not only the stories — and that it has no connection to the rest of this unit's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson stands inside the one Graubünden unit, case by case.
