# Teaching the Unit at Sek II — matching

Match each part of the lesson with its teaching.

**1.** Didactic principles at Sek II — a)

**2.** The history at greater depth — b)

**3.** Judging the machinery — c)


Meanings:
a) The Sek II companion opens with its didactic principles and competence orientation: older pupils work the same cases at greater depth, from fuller sources, towards their own judgement of the system.
b) The companion's introduction gives the history of coercive welfare measures at greater depth: abuse and exploitation inside the measures, and authorities failing in their duties.
c) Worked whole, the Sek II cases let pupils judge the machinery of the welfare state: council decisions, Pro Juventute's files, the press of the time, and the apology documents.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b; 3. c
