# Teaching the Unit at Sek II — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "Didactic principles at Sek II" teach?
**Answer:** The Sek II companion opens with its didactic principles and competence orientation: older pupils work the same cases at greater depth, from fuller sources, towards their own judgement of the system.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** At Sek II the same five cases are re-worked from the full documentary record — council decisions, welfare-organisation files, press criticism and the state's own apology — so older pupils judge the machinery of the welfare state, not only the stories.
