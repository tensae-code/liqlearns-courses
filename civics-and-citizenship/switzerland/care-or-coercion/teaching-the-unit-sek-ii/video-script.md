# Video script — Teaching the Unit at Sek II

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
At Sek II the same five cases are re-worked from the full documentary record — council decisions, welfare-organisation files, press criticism and the state's own apology — so older pupils judge the machinery of the welfare state, not only the stories.

The Sek II companion opens with its didactic principles and competence orientation: older pupils work the same cases at greater depth, from fuller sources, towards their own judgement of the system.

The principle is progression without replacement: the stories stay, but the evidence doubles — more documents per case, harder documents, and the state's own later words among them.

The companion's introduction gives the history of coercive welfare measures at greater depth: abuse and exploitation inside the measures, and authorities failing in their duties.

At this depth the failures are named as failures of duty, not misfortunes: oversight existed on paper and did not protect. That naming is what separates the Sek II history from a summary of sad stories.

Worked whole, the Sek II cases let pupils judge the machinery of the welfare state: council decisions, Pro Juventute's files, the press of the time, and the apology documents.

The unit's arc completes itself here: Level 1 asked whether help became coercion in five lives; Level 2 asks how a whole apparatus — offices, organisations, courts, press — produced, defended and finally answered for it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
