# Hands-on — Teaching the Unit at Sek II

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Take one case you already know from Level 1 — the Albins, Waser, Studer, Branger or Hofer. Write what you judged from the story alone. Then ask: what would you need to see to judge the offices, not just the outcome? This lesson is about the teaching that hands pupils exactly that record.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: at Sek II the same five cases are re-worked from the full documentary record — council decisions, welfare-organisation files, press criticism and the state's own apology — so older pupils judge the machinery of the welfare state, not only the stories. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
