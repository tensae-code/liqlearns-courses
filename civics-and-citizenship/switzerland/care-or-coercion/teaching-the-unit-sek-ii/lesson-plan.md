# Lesson plan — Teaching the Unit at Sek II

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Switzerland · **Title:** Care or Coercion? · **Lesson:** Teaching the Unit at Sek II
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — Care or Coercion? (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** at Sek II the same five cases are re-worked from the full documentary record — council decisions, welfare-organisation files, press criticism and the state's own apology — so older pupils judge the machinery of the welfare state, not only the stories.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Didactic principles at Sek II** — The Sek II companion opens with its didactic principles and competence orientation: older pupils work the same cases at greater depth, from fuller sources, towards their own judgement of the system.
2. **The history at greater depth** — The companion's introduction gives the history of coercive welfare measures at greater depth: abuse and exploitation inside the measures, and authorities failing in their duties.
3. **Judging the machinery** — Worked whole, the Sek II cases let pupils judge the machinery of the welfare state: council decisions, Pro Juventute's files, the press of the time, and the apology documents.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
