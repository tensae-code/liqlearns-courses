# Lesson plan — Can Amends Be Made?

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Switzerland · **Title:** Care or Coercion? · **Lesson:** Can Amends Be Made?
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 (deepened at Level 2) — Care or Coercion? (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** amends for coercive welfare injustice come in three parts — rehabilitation, official apologies and solidarity contributions, together with working through the past — and whether they can ever be enough stays the unit's open closing question.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 (deepened at Level 2) — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Is amends possible?** — The reader closes Hofer's case and the whole unit with the question 'È possibile fare ammenda?' — is it possible to make amends for this injustice?
2. **Apologies and solidarity contributions** — Reparation, as the unit tells it, includes rehabilitation and official apologies — Federal Councillor Widmer-Schlumpf in 2010, Federal Councillor Sommaruga in 2013, and Graubünden councillor Parolini in 2017 — together with solidarity contributions to the victims.
3. **The apology as a document** — At Sek II depth the Hofer case closes with Hofer's own proposal and the state's apology itself as a source — Federal Councillor Sommaruga's apology stands as document D9 — with working through the past as the third element of reparation.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
