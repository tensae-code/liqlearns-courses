# Can Amends Be Made? — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Reparation, as the unit tells it, includes rehabilitation and official apologies — Federal Councillor Widmer-Schlumpf in 2010, Federal Councillor Sommaruga in 2013, and Graubünden councillor Parolini in 2017 — together with solidarity contributions to the victims.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that amends for coercive welfare injustice come in three parts — rehabilitation, official apologies and solidarity contributions, together with working through the past — and whether they can ever be enough stays the unit's open closing question — and that it has no connection to the rest of this unit's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson stands inside the one Graubünden unit, case by case.
