# Can Amends Be Made? — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "Is amends possible?" teach?
**Answer:** The reader closes Hofer's case and the whole unit with the question 'È possibile fare ammenda?' — is it possible to make amends for this injustice?

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** Amends for coercive welfare injustice come in three parts — rehabilitation, official apologies and solidarity contributions, together with working through the past — and whether they can ever be enough stays the unit's open closing question.
