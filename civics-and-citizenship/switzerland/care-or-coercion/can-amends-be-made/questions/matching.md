# Can Amends Be Made? — matching

Match each part of the lesson with its teaching.

**1.** Is amends possible? — a)

**2.** Apologies and solidarity contributions — b)

**3.** The apology as a document — c)


Meanings:
a) The reader closes Hofer's case and the whole unit with the question 'È possibile fare ammenda?' — is it possible to make amends for this injustice?
b) Reparation, as the unit tells it, includes rehabilitation and official apologies — Federal Councillor Widmer-Schlumpf in 2010, Federal Councillor Sommaruga in 2013, and Graubünden councillor Parolini in 2017 — together with solidarity contributions to the victims.
c) At Sek II depth the Hofer case closes with Hofer's own proposal and the state's apology itself as a source — Federal Councillor Sommaruga's apology stands as document D9 — with working through the past as the third element of reparation.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b; 3. c
