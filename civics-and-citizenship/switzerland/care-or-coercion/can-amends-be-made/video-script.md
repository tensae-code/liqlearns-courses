# Video script — Can Amends Be Made?

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Amends for coercive welfare injustice come in three parts — rehabilitation, official apologies and solidarity contributions, together with working through the past — and whether they can ever be enough stays the unit's open closing question.

The reader closes Hofer's case and the whole unit with the question 'È possibile fare ammenda?' — is it possible to make amends for this injustice?

The question is honestly open: the harm — childhoods, families, years — cannot be handed back. Everything in this lesson is measured against that impossibility, not against a clean settlement.

Reparation, as the unit tells it, includes rehabilitation and official apologies — Federal Councillor Widmer-Schlumpf in 2010, Federal Councillor Sommaruga in 2013, and Graubünden councillor Parolini in 2017 — together with solidarity contributions to the victims.

An apology from the state's own mouth does what no payment alone can: it names the measures as injustice, officially, where the files once named the victims as the problem. The contributions stand beside the words as the material part of the amends.

At Sek II depth the Hofer case closes with Hofer's own proposal and the state's apology itself as a source — Federal Councillor Sommaruga's apology stands as document D9 — with working through the past as the third element of reparation.

Pupils end the unit as they began it, with a document — but this time the document is the state judging itself. Working through the past, the unit's own existence included, is presented as part of the amends, not as an extra.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
