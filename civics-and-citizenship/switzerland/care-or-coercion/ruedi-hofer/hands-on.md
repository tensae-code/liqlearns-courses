# Hands-on — Ruedi Hofer

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Think of everything a child carries when moved to a new place: belongings, memories, injuries, hopes. Write what of that the receiving place ever sees on paper. Ruedi Hofer was passed on many times — this lesson follows what was carried, and what was done to him, from place to place.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Ruedi Hofer was passed from place to place as a placed child — seriously injured, exploited and abused along the way — and his story asks when a bad time finally ends. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
