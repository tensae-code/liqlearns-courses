# Lesson plan — Ruedi Hofer

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Switzerland · **Title:** Care or Coercion? · **Lesson:** Ruedi Hofer
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — Care or Coercion? (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Ruedi Hofer was passed from place to place as a placed child — seriously injured, exploited and abused along the way — and his story asks when a bad time finally ends.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Passed from place to place** — Hofer's life, as the reader tells it, is a life of being passed from place to place — a placed child with no place that was his own.
2. **Injured, exploited and abused** — The Sek I teaching names what happened to Hofer in his placements: he was seriously injured, exploited and abused as a placed child — and it notes what Hofer himself values, his dog.
3. **When a bad time ends** — The reader follows Hofer's story to the question of its ending: when does a bad time finally end for a person the system passed on?

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
