# Ruedi Hofer — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "Passed from place to place" teach?
**Answer:** Hofer's life, as the reader tells it, is a life of being passed from place to place — a placed child with no place that was his own.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** Ruedi Hofer was passed from place to place as a placed child — seriously injured, exploited and abused along the way — and his story asks when a bad time finally ends.
