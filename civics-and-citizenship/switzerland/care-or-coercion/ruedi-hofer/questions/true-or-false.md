# Ruedi Hofer — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Sek I teaching names what happened to Hofer in his placements: he was seriously injured, exploited and abused as a placed child — and it notes what Hofer himself values, his dog.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that Ruedi Hofer was passed from place to place as a placed child — seriously injured, exploited and abused along the way — and his story asks when a bad time finally ends — and that it has no connection to the rest of this unit's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson stands inside the one Graubünden unit, case by case.
