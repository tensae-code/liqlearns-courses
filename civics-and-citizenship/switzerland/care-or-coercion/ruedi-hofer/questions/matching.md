# Ruedi Hofer — matching

Match each part of the lesson with its teaching.

**1.** Passed from place to place — a)

**2.** Injured, exploited and abused — b)

**3.** When a bad time ends — c)


Meanings:
a) Hofer's life, as the reader tells it, is a life of being passed from place to place — a placed child with no place that was his own.
b) The Sek I teaching names what happened to Hofer in his placements: he was seriously injured, exploited and abused as a placed child — and it notes what Hofer himself values, his dog.
c) The reader follows Hofer's story to the question of its ending: when does a bad time finally end for a person the system passed on?

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b; 3. c
