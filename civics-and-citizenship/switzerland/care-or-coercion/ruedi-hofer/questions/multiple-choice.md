# Ruedi Hofer — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's first teaching — Passed from place to place?
a) Hofer's life, as the reader tells it, is a life of being passed from place to place — a placed child with no place that was his own.  b) it has nothing to do with rights or the state  c) it was invented outside Switzerland  d) it matters only in examinations
