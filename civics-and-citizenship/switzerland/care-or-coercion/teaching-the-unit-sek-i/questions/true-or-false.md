# Teaching the Unit at Sek I — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The unit is competence-oriented under Lehrplan 21: pupils practise reading sources, weighing perspectives and forming a reasoned judgement about rights and state power.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that at Sek I this unit is taught by source work: pupils read the authorities' own documents case by case and judge for themselves, under the competences of Lehrplan 21, with the subject's roots in cantonal poor law as the teacher's background — and that it has no connection to the rest of this unit's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson stands inside the one Graubünden unit, case by case.
