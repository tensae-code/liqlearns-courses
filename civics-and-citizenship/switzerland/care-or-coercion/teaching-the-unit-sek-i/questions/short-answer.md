# Teaching the Unit at Sek I — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "Working with the reader" teach?
**Answer:** The Sek I companion organises the teaching around source work with the pupil reader: each case is worked through its own documents (D1–D5 per case) before any judgement is made.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** At Sek I this unit is taught by source work: pupils read the authorities' own documents case by case and judge for themselves, under the competences of Lehrplan 21, with the subject's roots in cantonal poor law as the teacher's background.
