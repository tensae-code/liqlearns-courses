# Lesson plan — Teaching the Unit at Sek I

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Switzerland · **Title:** Care or Coercion? · **Lesson:** Teaching the Unit at Sek I
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — Care or Coercion? (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** at Sek I this unit is taught by source work: pupils read the authorities' own documents case by case and judge for themselves, under the competences of Lehrplan 21, with the subject's roots in cantonal poor law as the teacher's background.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Working with the reader** — The Sek I companion organises the teaching around source work with the pupil reader: each case is worked through its own documents (D1–D5 per case) before any judgement is made.
2. **Lehrplan 21 competences** — The unit is competence-oriented under Lehrplan 21: pupils practise reading sources, weighing perspectives and forming a reasoned judgement about rights and state power.
3. **Roots in poor law and new research** — The teacher's background traces the measures to their roots in cantonal poor law, sets out what recent research has shown, and records the arguments welfare professionals themselves made at the time.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
