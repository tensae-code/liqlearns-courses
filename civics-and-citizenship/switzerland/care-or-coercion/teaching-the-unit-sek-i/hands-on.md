# Hands-on — Teaching the Unit at Sek I

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Take any official note about a person — a file note, a report, a decision. Read it twice: first for what it says, then for what it does not say and whose voice is missing. That second reading is how this unit is taught at Sek I, and this lesson shows how.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: at Sek I this unit is taught by source work: pupils read the authorities' own documents case by case and judge for themselves, under the competences of Lehrplan 21, with the subject's roots in cantonal poor law as the teacher's background. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
