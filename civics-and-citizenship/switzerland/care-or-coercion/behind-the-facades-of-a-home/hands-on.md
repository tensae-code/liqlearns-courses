# Hands-on — Behind the Facades of a Home

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Picture a children's home from outside: clean windows, a tidy yard, a good name in the village. Now write what an outsider cannot see from that picture. Cornelia Studer lived behind that facade — this lesson reads her account of what was inside.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: behind the orderly facades of a children's home, Cornelia Studer's memoirs tell of humiliation and hard labour — and in 2016 she returned to reckon with the institution that raised her. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
