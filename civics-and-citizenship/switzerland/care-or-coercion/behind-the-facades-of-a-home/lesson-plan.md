# Lesson plan — Behind the Facades of a Home

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Switzerland · **Title:** Care or Coercion? · **Lesson:** Behind the Facades of a Home
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 (deepened at Level 2) — Care or Coercion? (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** behind the orderly facades of a children's home, Cornelia Studer's memoirs tell of humiliation and hard labour — and in 2016 she returned to reckon with the institution that raised her.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 (deepened at Level 2) — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Life behind the facades** — Cornelia Studer's memoirs tell life inside a children's home from the child's side — what the daily order felt like to the children who lived under it.
2. **Humiliation and hard labour** — The Sek I teaching names what the memoirs show: behind the facades stood humiliation and hard labour for the children placed in the home.
3. **The home's records and the reckoning** — At Sek II depth the case is worked from the home's own records and memories together (documents to D11), and the reader follows Studer's reckoning with the institution in 2016.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
