# Hands-on — Environmental Deterioration

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Look out of the nearest window, or picture your street. Name one way the environment there is worse than it should be — dirtied water, cut trees, fouled air, wasted land. This lesson takes that one sight and sets it among the world's forms of environmental deterioration.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the environment is deteriorating in many forms, its effects reach every state, and it closes the book's ladder as the challenge no citizen and no state faces alone. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the news of the world. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
