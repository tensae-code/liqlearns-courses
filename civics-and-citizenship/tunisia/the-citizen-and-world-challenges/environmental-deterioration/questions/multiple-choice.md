# Environmental Deterioration — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these is a form of environmental deterioration in this lesson?
a) water dirtied, air fouled, trees and land destroyed  b) a new road opened  c) a school built  d) rain in its season
**Answer: a) water dirtied, air fouled, trees and land destroyed** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Why does the book close with this challenge?
a) its effects cross borders — no state faces it alone; it belongs to the whole international community  b) it is the smallest challenge  c) it concerns one village only  d) it has no effects on people
**Answer: a) its effects cross borders — no state faces it alone; it belongs to the whole international community** — That is what this lesson teaches.
