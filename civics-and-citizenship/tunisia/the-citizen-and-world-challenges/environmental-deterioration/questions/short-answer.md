# Environmental Deterioration — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name three forms of environmental deterioration.
**Answer:** Any three: water dirtied; air fouled; trees destroyed; land destroyed or wasted; ground degraded.

**2.** In your own words, why is this a challenge for the international community and not one state?
**Answer:** Because its effects cross borders and reach every state's health, food, water and livelihood — no state faces it alone.
