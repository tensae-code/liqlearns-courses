# Lesson plan — Environmental Deterioration

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Tunisia · **Title:** The Citizen and World Challenges · **Lesson:** Environmental Deterioration
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — The Citizen and World Challenges (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the environment is deteriorating in many forms, its effects reach every state, and it closes the book's ladder as the challenge no citizen and no state faces alone.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The forms of environmental deterioration** — Environmental deterioration takes forms: water dirtied, air fouled, trees and land destroyed, ground wasted — the shared environment worsening in ways that can be named and counted.
2. **Its effects** — Its effects reach the lives built on that environment: health, food, water and livelihood — and they cross borders, so no state meets them inside its own fence alone.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## If the learner is stuck
- Treats the environment as scenery → the lesson's effects are on lives: water, food, health and land. For every form named, ask what it does to the people who live from that environment.
- Go back to the one thing: the environment is deteriorating in many forms, its effects reach every state, and it closes the book's ladder as the challenge no citizen and no state faces alone. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
