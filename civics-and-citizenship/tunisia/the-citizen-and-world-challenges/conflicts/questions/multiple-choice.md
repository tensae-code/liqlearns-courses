# Conflicts — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the first step to understanding a conflict, in this lesson?
a) sorting it by its form — who is in it, and over what  b) choosing a side at once  c) counting weapons only  d) ignoring its causes
**Answer: a) sorting it by its form — who is in it, and over what** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** On whom do a conflict's effects fall, in this lesson?
a) on people first, then states, then the international community  b) on the fighters only  c) on no one outside the battlefield  d) on one state only, ever
**Answer: a) on people first, then states, then the international community** — That is what this lesson teaches.
