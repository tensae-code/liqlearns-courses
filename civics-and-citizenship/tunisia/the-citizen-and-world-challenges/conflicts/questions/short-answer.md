# Conflicts — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name two forms a conflict can take.
**Answer:** Any two: between states; inside a state; over land, power, resources or identity; open war; tension not yet broken into war.

**2.** Why does the lesson make the citizen study effects?
**Answer:** Because the effects are the true size of a conflict — they show everything it harms, from people's lives and homes to the international community.
