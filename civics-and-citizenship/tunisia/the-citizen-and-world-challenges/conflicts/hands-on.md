# Hands-on — Conflicts

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name one conflict in the world you have heard of. Now ask: who is fighting, over what, and who else is harmed besides the fighters? Hold those answers — this lesson sorts conflicts by their forms and follows their effects.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: conflicts in the world take many forms, and their effects fall on people, states and the whole international community the citizen belongs to. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the news of the world. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
