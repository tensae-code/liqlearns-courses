# Poverty — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these is a form of poverty in this lesson?
a) lacking schooling, health care or clean water, not money alone  b) owning few luxury goods  c) living in a small town  d) working with your hands
**Answer: a) lacking schooling, health care or clean water, not money alone** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Why is poverty placed among the world challenges?
a) its effects spread across health, learning, work and a society's development  b) it exists in only one village  c) it is easily ended by one household  d) it affects nothing beyond one person
**Answer: a) its effects spread across health, learning, work and a society's development** — That is what this lesson teaches.
