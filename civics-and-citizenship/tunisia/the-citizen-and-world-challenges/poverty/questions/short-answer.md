# Poverty — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name three forms of poverty from this lesson.
**Answer:** Any three: lack of income; lack of food; lack of health care; lack of schooling; lack of clean water; lack of a secure home; lack of a voice in decisions.

**2.** Trace one effect: how does one form of poverty produce another?
**Answer:** For example: poverty that closes schooling limits work; limited work keeps income low; low income closes health care — each form producing effects in the others.
