# Poverty — matching

Match each word with its meaning.

**1.** poverty — a)

**2.** forms of poverty — b)

**3.** effects of poverty — c)


Meanings:
a) lacking what a life needs — income, food, care, schooling, water, a voice
b) the many lacks poverty takes, beyond money alone
c) what poverty does to health, learning, work and a society's development

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b; 3. c
