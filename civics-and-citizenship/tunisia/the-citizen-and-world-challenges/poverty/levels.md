# Poverty — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — The forms of poverty
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Poverty takes forms: the lack of income is only the first. Poverty is also the lack of what a life needs — food, health care, schooling, clean water, a secure home — and the lack of a voice in the decisions that govern a life. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — Its effects
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Poverty's effects spread: on health, on learning, on work, on the family, and on a society's ability to develop — each form of poverty producing effects in the others. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
