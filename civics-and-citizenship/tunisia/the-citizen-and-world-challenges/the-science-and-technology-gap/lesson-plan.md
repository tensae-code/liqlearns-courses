# Lesson plan — The Science and Technology Gap

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Tunisia · **Title:** The Citizen and World Challenges · **Lesson:** The Science and Technology Gap
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — The Citizen and World Challenges (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a scientific and technological gap divides states — in its forms and its effects — and joining the information society is how a state answers it.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The forms of the gap** — The gap appears in forms: states differ in their science — their research, their laboratories, their trained people — and in their technology — the tools, networks and industries built on that science.
2. **Its effects** — The gap has effects: it shapes what a state can produce, how its people learn and work, and how much weight its voice carries among states.
3. **Joining the information society** — The lesson closes on the answer: joining the information society — connecting a state's people to the world's knowledge and networks — matters because it is how the gap is narrowed.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
