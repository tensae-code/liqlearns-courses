# Hands-on — The Science and Technology Gap

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
List the technology your household used today — phone, light, water, transport, a screen. Now imagine a place where most of it is missing. The distance between the two lists is this lesson: the science and technology gap between states.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: a scientific and technological gap divides states — in its forms and its effects — and joining the information society is how a state answers it. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the news of the world. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
