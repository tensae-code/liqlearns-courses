# Video script — The Science and Technology Gap

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A scientific and technological gap divides states — in its forms and its effects — and joining the information society is how a state answers it.

The gap appears in forms: states differ in their science — their research, their laboratories, their trained people — and in their technology — the tools, networks and industries built on that science.

One state produces knowledge and technology; another must buy both, or go without. That difference is the gap's shape.

The gap has effects: it shapes what a state can produce, how its people learn and work, and how much weight its voice carries among states.

Left alone, the gap widens itself — the state with more science makes more technology, and the distance grows.

The lesson closes on the answer: joining the information society — connecting a state's people to the world's knowledge and networks — matters because it is how the gap is narrowed.

For the citizen, the point is practical: information is not a luxury added after development; it is one of the roads development travels.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
