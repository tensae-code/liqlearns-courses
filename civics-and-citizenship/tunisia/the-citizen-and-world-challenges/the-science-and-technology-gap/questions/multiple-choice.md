# The Science and Technology Gap — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** In what forms does the science and technology gap appear?
a) states differ in research and trained people, and in the tools, networks and industries built on science  b) states differ only in flag colours  c) states differ only in population  d) states differ only in climate
**Answer: a) states differ in research and trained people, and in the tools, networks and industries built on science** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Why does joining the information society matter, in this lesson?
a) it is how the gap between states is narrowed  b) it ends all need for schools  c) it makes technology unnecessary  d) it removes every other challenge
**Answer: a) it is how the gap between states is narrowed** — That is what this lesson teaches.
