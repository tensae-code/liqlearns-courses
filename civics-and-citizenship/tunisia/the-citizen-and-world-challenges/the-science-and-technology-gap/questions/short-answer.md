# The Science and Technology Gap — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Describe the gap in your own words.
**Answer:** States differ in science — research, laboratories, trained people — and in technology — tools, networks, industries; one state produces knowledge while another must buy it or go without.

**2.** Name one effect of the gap that is not a device.
**Answer:** It shapes what a state can produce, how its people learn and work, or how much weight its voice carries among states.
