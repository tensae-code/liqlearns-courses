# Differences in Democratic Practice — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is a difference in democratic practice?
**Answer:** Two states both practising democracy but arranging it differently — elections, representation and institutions in different forms.

**2.** Name two things that shift when the form of practice shifts.
**Answer:** How citizens take part, and how power is checked (how decisions are made also shifts).
