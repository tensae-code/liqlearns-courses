# Differences in Democratic Practice — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson study first?
a) the forms the difference in democratic practice takes  b) one country's constitution only  c) how to abolish elections  d) the oldest democracy in history only
**Answer: a) the forms the difference in democratic practice takes** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What follows from differences in democratic practice?
a) consequences for how citizens take part and how power is checked  b) nothing — form changes nothing  c) the end of all democracy  d) identical results in every state
**Answer: a) consequences for how citizens take part and how power is checked** — That is what this lesson teaches.
