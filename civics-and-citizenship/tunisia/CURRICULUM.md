# Civics and Citizenship — Tunisia course — CURRICULUM (the path)

One course for Tunisia inside the Civics and Citizenship folder,
organised by **levels, not grades**: a learner climbs when they
have proved the level, not when a school calendar says so. The
school grades the books come from appear only in each lesson's
sources (provenance), never as the course's organisation. The path
follows Tunisia's own order as its books lay it out: the sketched
lower level works in interest-axes on the pupil's own social
environment, and the stocked top level runs entirely outward —
from the world inward to the citizen's rights and back out to
world challenges.

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in this
course comes from government textbooks — this country's own Civic
Education book — except the **Tip boxes**: a tip in a box is from
us, the people making LiqLearns. Tips are ours; the lessons are
the books'. See ABOUT.md for the full book list, the PARTIAL G8
scan whose civics unit is absent, and the acceptance reckoning.

## The levels (the path, in order)

1. **The Pupil's Social Environment (sketched, not stocked)** —
   the G8 Social Subjects civics unit: interest-axes on positive
   interaction with the social environment, citizenship values,
   shared living, civilised conduct and human-rights principles.
   Known only from the combined book's preface and cover in this
   wave; the unit itself was absent from the scan, so the level
   is sketched, not stocked. **0 lessons.**
2. **The Citizen in the World** — the international community and
   its principles; the citizen as holder of rights at the
   international level (human rights basics and protection,
   universality and particularity, rights and sustainable
   development); and the citizen facing current international
   challenges (differences in democratic practice, the science
   and technology gap, poverty, conflicts, environmental
   deterioration). 9 lessons, in three titles:
   `the-international-community/` (1 lesson) then
   `rights-at-the-international-level/` (3 lessons) then
   `the-citizen-and-world-challenges/` (5 lessons).

**Totals: 9 lessons** (Level 1: 0 — sketched, not stocked ·
Level 2: 9 — all from one student book).
