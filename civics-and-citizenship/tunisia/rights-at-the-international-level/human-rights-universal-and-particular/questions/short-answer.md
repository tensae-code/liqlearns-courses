# Human Rights — Universal and Particular — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** State the universal claim in your own words.
**Answer:** Human rights belong to every person everywhere, simply as a human being; no place or condition removes them.

**2.** Why must a citizen hold both sides of this question?
**Answer:** Because rights are claimed universally but lived in particular places — judging how the claim is honoured in a real place is the citizen's work.
