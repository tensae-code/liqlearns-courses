# Human Rights — Universal and Particular — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the universality of human rights claim?
a) that rights belong to every person, everywhere, as a human being  b) that rights belong only to citizens of one state  c) that each place invents its own rights from nothing  d) that rights stop at national borders
**Answer: a) that rights belong to every person, everywhere, as a human being** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is particularity, in this lesson?
a) the particular history, culture and ways of each place, which universal rights meet  b) a person's private property  c) a rule that cancels all rights  d) the smallest detail of a document
**Answer: a) the particular history, culture and ways of each place, which universal rights meet** — That is what this lesson teaches.
