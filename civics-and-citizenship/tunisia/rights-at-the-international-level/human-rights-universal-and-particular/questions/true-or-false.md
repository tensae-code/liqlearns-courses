# Human Rights — Universal and Particular — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A right that stopped at a border would be a local privilege, not a human right.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Particularity means universal rights simply disappear in each place.
**Answer: False** — The lesson holds both sides: the claim meets the place and must be judged there.
