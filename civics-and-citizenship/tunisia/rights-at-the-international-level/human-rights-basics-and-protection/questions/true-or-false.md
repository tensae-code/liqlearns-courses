# Human Rights — Basics and Protection — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Charters and organisations are among the mechanisms that protect human rights.
**Answer: True**

**2.** In this book, a right is fully known once its name is memorised, with no need to know what protects it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson joins each right to its protection — document and body.
