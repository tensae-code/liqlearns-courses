# Human Rights — Basics and Protection — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson study after the basic concepts of human rights?
a) the mechanisms that protect them  b) how rights can be removed  c) the history of one country only  d) how to write a charter alone
**Answer: a) the mechanisms that protect them** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** How does the book teach this lesson?
a) by working through charters and organisation documents  b) by memorising dates only  c) by stories with no documents  d) by exposition with nothing to read
**Answer: a) by working through charters and organisation documents** — That is what this lesson teaches.
