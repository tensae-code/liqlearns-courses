# Human Rights — Basics and Protection — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What two things must a citizen know about a human right, in this lesson?
**Answer:** Its basic concept — what the right is — and the mechanism that protects it: the charter that states it and the organisation that watches over it.

**2.** What documents does the lesson work through?
**Answer:** Charters and organisation documents — read, used and evaluated.
