# Hands-on — Human Rights — Basics and Protection

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name one right you believe every person has, wherever they live. Say why you think no one may take it away. This lesson starts from those basic concepts and follows the rights upward, to the charters and organisations that protect them.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: human rights rest on basic concepts every citizen should know, and they are protected by mechanisms — charters and organisations — at the international level. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the news of the world. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
