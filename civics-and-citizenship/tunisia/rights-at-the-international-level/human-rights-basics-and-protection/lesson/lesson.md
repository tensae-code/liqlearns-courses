# Human Rights — Basics and Protection

## Do it first

Name one right you believe every person has, wherever they live. Say why you think no one may take it away. This lesson starts from those basic concepts and follows the rights upward, to the charters and organisations that protect them.

## Sub lesson 1 — The basic concepts of human rights

Human rights are the rights a person holds as a human being. The lesson starts from their basic concepts — what these rights are and what makes them rights — before asking who protects them.

The book works the concepts from documents: the learner reads the charters and texts, uses them, and evaluates them, rather than receiving the ideas as bare statements.

## Sub lesson 2 — The mechanisms that protect them

Rights are protected by mechanisms at the international level: charters that state them, and organisations charged with watching over them.

To know a right, in this book, is also to know its protection — which document declares it and which body stands behind it.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: human rights rest on basic concepts every citizen should know, and they are protected by mechanisms — charters and organisations — at the international level. Give one real example from your own life or from the world you live in, and name the lesson's key words: human rights, basic concepts, charters, protection mechanisms, international organisations. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
