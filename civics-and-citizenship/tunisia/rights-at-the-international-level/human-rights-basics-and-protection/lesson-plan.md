# Lesson plan — Human Rights — Basics and Protection

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Tunisia · **Title:** Rights at the International Level · **Lesson:** Human Rights — Basics and Protection
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — Rights at the International Level (CURRICULUM.md).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The basic concepts of human rights** — Human rights are the rights a person holds as a human being. The lesson starts from their basic concepts — what these rights are and what makes them rights — before asking who protects them.
2. **The mechanisms that protect them** — Rights are protected by mechanisms at the international level: charters that state them, and organisations charged with watching over them.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## If the learner is stuck
- Treats protection as automatic → go back to the lesson's two halves: concepts say what the rights are; mechanisms — charters and organisations — are what protect them. A right with no mechanism is a claim, not yet a protection.
- Go back to the one thing: human rights rest on basic concepts every citizen should know, and they are protected by mechanisms — charters and organisations — at the international level. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
