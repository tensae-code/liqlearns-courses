# Hands-on — Human Rights and Sustainable Development

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Think of one improvement in your town — a road, a school, clean water. Ask: will it still serve your younger brother or sister in twenty years, and did it respect everyone's rights while it was built? Those two questions are this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: sustainable development is development that lasts — across its fields, economic, social and environmental — and human rights are a factor of that development, not a decoration on it. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the news of the world. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
