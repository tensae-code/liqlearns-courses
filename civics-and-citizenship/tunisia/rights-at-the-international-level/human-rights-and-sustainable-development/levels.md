# Human Rights and Sustainable Development — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — What sustainable development is
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Sustainable development is development that can last: it meets present needs without destroying the ability of those who come after to meet theirs. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — Its fields
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Sustainable development works across fields that hold together: the economic field (livelihoods and work), the social field (people's lives and services), and the environmental field (the land, water and air development depends on). — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 3 — Human rights as a factor of development
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Human rights are a factor of development itself: development done with people's rights respected — their participation, their dignity, their claims heard — is the development that lasts. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
