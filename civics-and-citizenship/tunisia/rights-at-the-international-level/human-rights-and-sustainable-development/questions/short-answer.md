# Human Rights and Sustainable Development — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the fields of sustainable development as this lesson gives them.
**Answer:** The economic field, the social field and the environmental field — held together, since a gain in one bought by wrecking another is not sustainable.

**2.** In your own words, why are rights a factor of development?
**Answer:** Because development that respects people's rights — participation, dignity, claims heard — is the development that lasts; rights are part of what development is made of.
