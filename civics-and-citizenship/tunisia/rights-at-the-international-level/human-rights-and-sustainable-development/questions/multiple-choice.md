# Human Rights and Sustainable Development — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What makes development 'sustainable', in this lesson?
a) it can last — meeting present needs without destroying the future's ability to meet its own  b) it is built very quickly  c) it spends the most money  d) it uses the newest machines only
**Answer: a) it can last — meeting present needs without destroying the future's ability to meet its own** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** How do human rights stand toward sustainable development in this lesson?
a) they are a factor of the development itself  b) they are a reward given after development  c) they have nothing to do with it  d) they are an obstacle to it
**Answer: a) they are a factor of the development itself** — That is what this lesson teaches.
