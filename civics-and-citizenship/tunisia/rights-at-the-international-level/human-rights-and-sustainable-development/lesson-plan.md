## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **What sustainable development is** — Sustainable development is development that can last: it meets present needs without destroying the ability of those who come after to meet theirs.
2. **Its fields** — Sustainable development works across fields that hold together: the economic field (livelihoods and work), the social field (people's lives and services), and the environmental field (the land, water and air development depends on).
3. **Human rights as a factor of development** — Human rights are a factor of development itself: development done with people's rights respected — their participation, their dignity, their claims heard — is the development that lasts.
