# The International Community and Its Principles — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the four working principles of the international community.
**Answer:** Independence and non-interference in states' internal affairs; equality between states; international cooperation; and the peaceful settlement of disputes.

**2.** How does the book build the idea of the international community?
**Answer:** From a timeline of its organisations — organisation after organisation — until the community itself can be studied.
