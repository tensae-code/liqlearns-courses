# The International Community and Its Principles — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the international community?
a) the community of states and the organisations they have built together  b) one powerful state ruling the others  c) a single world government that replaces states  d) a market where states only trade
**Answer: a) the community of states and the organisations they have built together** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these is one of the community's working principles?
a) the peaceful settlement of disputes  b) interference in other states' internal affairs  c) rule of the largest state over the rest  d) settling disputes by force
**Answer: a) the peaceful settlement of disputes** — That is what this lesson teaches.
