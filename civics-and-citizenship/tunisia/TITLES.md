# Civics and Citizenship — Tunisia course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

Civics belongs to the country: this course is Tunisia's own —
built from Tunisia's Civic Education book for the 3rd year of
secondary school, Letters stream (student book, Arabic), with the
G8 Social Subjects book's civics unit standing before it in the
ladder as a sketch only (its unit was absent from the scan —
PARTIAL, 0 placements). Inside the course the organisation is by
**levels, not grades**: the titles below sit inside Level 2, in
path order — what the learner proves, step by step. The grades
the books come from live only in each lesson's sources.

The path's own logic is Tunisia's: at the top of its ladder
Tunisia teaches civics entirely outward-facing — the pupil is
placed inside the international community first, then given
rights at the international level, then set against world
challenges; every lesson is worked from documents
(أقرأ / أستثمر وأقيّم — read, use, evaluate) rather than
exposition. That ladder IS the path order (recommended, never
forced — a learner can jump in higher; the "start here instead"
hints point down this ladder).

## The titles

**Level 1 — the pupil's social environment** (Social Subjects,
8th year Basic; **sketched, not stocked** — the civics unit was
absent from the scan, so no title and no lesson is built for
this level).

**Level 2 — the citizen in the world** (Civic Education, 3rd
Year Letters student book):

- `the-international-community/` — Level 2, first title. The
  International Community and Its Principles (what the
  international community is, built from a timeline of its
  organisations, then its working principles — independence and
  non-interference, equality between states, international
  cooperation, peaceful settlement of disputes).
- `rights-at-the-international-level/` — Level 2, second title.
  Human Rights — Basics and Protection (basic concepts, then the
  charters and organisations that protect them) · Human Rights —
  Universal and Particular (the universal claim and its meeting
  with local particularity) · Human Rights and Sustainable
  Development (the concept, its fields, and rights as a factor
  of development).
- `the-citizen-and-world-challenges/` — Level 2, third title.
  Differences in Democratic Practice (the forms the difference
  takes, and what follows) · The Science and Technology Gap
  (its forms, its effects, and joining the information society)
  · Poverty (its forms and its effects) · Conflicts (their forms
  and their effects) · Environmental Deterioration (its forms
  and its effects, closing the book's outward ladder).
