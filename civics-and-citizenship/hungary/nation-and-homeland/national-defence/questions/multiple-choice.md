# National Defence — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) National defence and the citizen's part in it.  b) it has nothing to do with how Hungary governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) National defence and the citizen's part in it.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) National defence and the citizen's part in it.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Hungary
**Answer: a) National defence and the citizen's part in it.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
