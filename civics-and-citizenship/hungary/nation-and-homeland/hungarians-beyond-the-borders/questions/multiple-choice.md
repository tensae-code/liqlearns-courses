# Hungarians Beyond the Borders — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Hungarians living beyond the borders and their ties to Hungary.  b) it has nothing to do with how Hungary governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Hungarians living beyond the borders and their ties to Hungary.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Hungarians living beyond the borders and their ties to Hungary.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Hungary
**Answer: a) Hungarians living beyond the borders and their ties to Hungary.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
