# Civics and Citizenship — Hungary course — CURRICULUM (the path)

One course for Hungary inside the Civics and
Citizenship folder, organised by **levels, not grades**: a
learner climbs when they have proved the level, not when a
school calendar says so. The school grades the books come
from appear only in each lesson's sources (provenance),
never as the course's organisation. The path follows
Hungary's own order as its books lay it out, from the
filing's ladder sketch (`hungary-placements-b.md`):
Hungary's two civics books form a spiral — the Grade 8
book walks the widening circles (family, household money,
settlement, nation, the state, everyday administration,
consumer protection, the great public systems), and the
Grade 12 book revisits the same circles at adult depth for
the school-leaving citizen, adding freedom and
responsibility, the state organ by organ, and a heavy
practical strand (enterprise, banks, household finance,
official business). The filing stocks 88 placements into
85 lessons: the coined lesson names were deduped before
building, by lesson name — every placement stocks into
exactly one lesson. The Grade 8 book's closing
*Mindennapi pénzügyeink* practical-exercise section and
the Grade 12 book's *Feladattár* (task bank) are apparatus,
named in the filing's contents maps, not placed, as filed.

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in
this course comes from government textbooks — this
country's own *Állampolgári ismeretek* (Citizenship
Knowledge) books — except the **Tip boxes**: a tip in a
box is from us, the people making LiqLearns. Tips are
ours; the lessons are the books'. See ABOUT.md for the
full book list and the acceptance reckoning. **All 85
lessons stand on student books; no lesson in this course
stands on a teacher guide or a programme — the filing
contains none.**

## The levels (the path, in order)

1. **The Widening Circles — Family, Settlement, Nation and State** — The Grade 8 book (*Állampolgári ismeretek 8.*): the family and what it does, growing up in it, having children and the family's own history — then the family's money (running a household, the family budget, saving), the learner's own settlement (its features, its history and values, its local government, developing it, and a project letter to the mayor), the nation and the homeland (national identity, cultural heritage and the country's image, the nationalities and their rights, Hungarians beyond the borders, national defence, globalisation and Europe), the Hungarian state's basic institutions (citizens' rights and duties, elections, lawmaking and government, the administration of justice, the state's economic role, sharing public burdens), everyday administration (official business, preparing for adult tasks, career orientation, the world of work), the conscious consumer (sustainability, conscious shopping, consumer protection) and the great public systems (public education, health care, social care). Three lessons climb on into the Grade 12 book inside themselves — National Defence, Consumer Protection and Public Education each carry their Grade 12 step as a second sub lesson. 34 lessons, in 8 title(s): `the-family/` (4 lessons); `household-money/` (3 lessons); `my-settlement/` (5 lessons); `nation-and-homeland/` (6 lessons); `the-state/` (6 lessons); `everyday-administration/` (4 lessons); `the-conscious-consumer/` (3 lessons); `the-great-systems/` (3 lessons).
2. **The School-Leaving Citizen — the Same Circles at Adult Depth** — The Grade 12 book (*Állampolgári ismeretek 12.*): the same circles at adult depth — the family again (its models and roles, partnership and marriage, family planning and demography), nation and state (national consciousness, the nation-state, citizenship, Hungary and the Hungarians abroad, international integration), freedom and responsibility (human rights, democracy, electoral systems, parties, the Fundamental Law, the legal order, civil organisations), the Hungarian state organ by organ (the republic and its president, legislation, the executive, the judiciary, the control institutions, local governments) — then the practical adult strand: enterprise (the market economy, competition and monopoly, forms of enterprise, the business plan), banks and money (financial markets, the two-tier banking system, inflation, borrowing and loans), the state in the economy (macroeconomic indicators, the state's economic tasks, public finances, the tax system, state debt, social insurance and family support), household finance (family and household, income, spending, financial planning, bank account and card, savings, insurance), everyday business (citizen documents, public administration, the labour market, taking a job, documents and contracts) and consumer and environment (protecting competition, warranty and guarantee, environmental protection, the conscious consumer). 51 lessons, in 10 title(s): `the-family/` (3 lessons); `nation-and-state/` (5 lessons); `freedom-and-responsibility/` (7 lessons); `the-hungarian-state/` (6 lessons); `enterprise/` (4 lessons); `banks-and-money/` (4 lessons); `the-state-in-the-economy/` (6 lessons); `household-finance/` (7 lessons); `everyday-business/` (5 lessons); `consumer-and-environment/` (4 lessons).

**Totals: 85 lessons** (Level 1: 34 · Level 2: 51 — from two read books: *Állampolgári ismeretek 8.* and *Állampolgári ismeretek 12.* (Oktatási Hivatal / Education Office, Hungarian, both student books, both COMPLETE as filed); 88 placements stocking 85 lessons).

  ABOUT.md, and the folder README entry).
- **Levels 1–2 — BUILT**: 85 lessons, stocked from 88

  student's books).
- **Not built, named plainly**: the Grade 8 book's
  *Mindennapi pénzügyeink* practical exercises (printed
  pp. 85–92) and its appendix (p. 93), and the Grade 12
  book's *Feladattár* (task bank and érettségi practice,
  pp. 91+) are apparatus — named in the filing's contents

  teacher guide, no programme, and no UNREAD book.
  Nothing else is in the course, and no lesson pretends
  to one (see ABOUT.md).
