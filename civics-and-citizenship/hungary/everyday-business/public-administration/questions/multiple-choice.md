# Public Administration — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Public administration and doing business at offices.  b) it has nothing to do with how Hungary governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Public administration and doing business at offices.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Public administration and doing business at offices.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Hungary
**Answer: a) Public administration and doing business at offices.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
