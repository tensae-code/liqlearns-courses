# Family History — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Family history — researching and valuing one's own family's past.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that Family History — and that it has no connection to the rest of Hungary's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Hungary's own course, in its own order.
