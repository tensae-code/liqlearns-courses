# Savings — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Savings and their forms.  b) it has nothing to do with how Hungary governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Savings and their forms.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Savings and their forms.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Hungary
**Answer: a) Savings and their forms.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
