# Social Care — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Social provision — support for families, the unemployed and people with disabilities, including the compulsory community service (IKSZ) for secondary pupils.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that Social Care — and that it has no connection to the rest of Hungary's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Hungary's own course, in its own order.
