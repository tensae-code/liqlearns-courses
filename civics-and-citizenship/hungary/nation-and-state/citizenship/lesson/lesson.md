# Citizenship

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Citizenship. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Hungary's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Citizenship — who is a Hungarian citizen and what it carries.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Citizenship — who is a Hungarian citizen and what it carries. Give one real example from your own life or from the Hungary you live in, and name the lesson's key words: citizenship. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
