# Hungary and Hungarians Abroad — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Hungary and the Hungarians beyond the borders.  b) it has nothing to do with how Hungary governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Hungary and the Hungarians beyond the borders.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Hungary and the Hungarians beyond the borders.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Hungary
**Answer: a) Hungary and the Hungarians beyond the borders.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
