# International Integration — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) International integration — Hungary in the EU and world organisations.  b) it has nothing to do with how Hungary governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) International integration — Hungary in the EU and world organisations.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) International integration — Hungary in the EU and world organisations.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Hungary
**Answer: a) International integration — Hungary in the EU and world organisations.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
