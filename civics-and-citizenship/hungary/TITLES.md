# Civics and Citizenship — Hungary course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

Civics belongs to the country: this course is Hungary's
own — built from Hungary's *Állampolgári ismeretek*
(Citizenship Knowledge) books (Oktatási Hivatal /
Education Office, Hungarian): the student books for
Grade 8 and Grade 12, both read in full and COMPLETE as
filed. Inside the course the organisation is by
**levels, not grades**: the titles below ARE the levels'
titles, in path order — what the learner proves, step by
step. The grades the books come from live only in each
lesson's sources.

The path's own logic is Hungary's, from the filing's
ladder sketch: Hungary's own two civics books form a
spiral. The Grade 8 book moves from the family outward —
household money, the home settlement and its council,
nation and homeland duties, the state's basic
institutions, everyday administration, consumer
protection and the big public systems — and every lesson
opens with a picture and a quotation from Hungary's
Fundamental Law (Alaptörvény), so the constitution frames
even family and household topics. The Grade 12 book
explicitly deepens the Grade 8 circles for the
school-leaving citizen: family and nation again, then
freedom and responsibility, the Hungarian state organ by
organ, and a heavy practical strand — enterprise, banks,
the state's economic role, household finance, official
business and consumer and environmental protection, all
aimed at the érettségi and adult life. So Level 1 is the
Grade 8 turn of the spiral and Level 2 is the Grade 12
turn. That ladder IS the path order (recommended, never
forced — a learner can jump in higher; the "start here
instead" hints point down this ladder).

The coined lesson names in the filing were deduped
before building, by lesson name: 88 placements stock 85
lessons. Three coined names are used by both books —
National Defence, Consumer Protection and Public
Education — and each stocks into exactly one lesson,
which sits at Level 1 (its Grade 8 home) and climbs to
its Grade 12 step as a second sub lesson. Every
placement stocks into exactly one lesson, and each
placement's provenance row (book + printed page, exactly
as filed) is in that lesson's `lesson/sources.md`. Unit
folders are the filing's own theme names; a theme folder
can hold lessons from more than one level (each lesson's
own level is stated in its lesson plan and levels file) —
`the-family/` holds both turns of the spiral, as the
books return to the family.
