Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The history and cultural values of one's home place.  b) it has nothing to do with how Hungary governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The history and cultural values of one's home place.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) The history and cultural values of one's home place.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Hungary
**Answer: a) The history and cultural values of one's home place.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
