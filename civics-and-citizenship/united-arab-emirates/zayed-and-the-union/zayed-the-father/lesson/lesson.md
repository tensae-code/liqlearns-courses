# Zayed the Father

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Zayed the Father. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what United Arab Emirates's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Sheikh Zayed as founder and father of the nation, the raising of the flag, and the union of the seven emirates on 2 December 1971

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Sheikh Zayed as founder and father of the nation, the raising of the flag, and the union of the seven emirates on 2 December 1971 Give one real example from your own life or from the United Arab Emirates you live in, and name the lesson's key words: zayed, the, father. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: Sheikh Zayed as founder and father of the nation, the raising of the flag, and the union of the seven emirates on 2 December 1971
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
