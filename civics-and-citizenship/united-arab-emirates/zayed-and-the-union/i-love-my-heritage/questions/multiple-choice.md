# I Love my Heritage — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Emirati traditional dress and heritage foods as things to know, wear, taste and be proud of  b) it has nothing to do with how United Arab Emirates governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Emirati traditional dress and heritage foods as things to know, wear, taste and be proud of** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Emirati traditional dress and heritage foods as things to know, wear, taste and be proud of  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in United Arab Emirates
**Answer: a) Emirati traditional dress and heritage foods as things to know, wear, taste and be proud of** — That is what this lesson teaches.
