# Growing Into Good Character — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) How people can change and grow through experience and learning until good character is theirs  b) it has nothing to do with how United Arab Emirates governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) How people can change and grow through experience and learning until good character is theirs** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) How people can change and grow through experience and learning until good character is theirs  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in United Arab Emirates
**Answer: a) How people can change and grow through experience and learning until good character is theirs** — That is what this lesson teaches.
