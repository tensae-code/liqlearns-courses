# A School for Everyone — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Auditing your own school for diversity and proposing how it can include everyone  b) it has nothing to do with how United Arab Emirates governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Auditing your own school for diversity and proposing how it can include everyone** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Auditing your own school for diversity and proposing how it can include everyone  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in United Arab Emirates
**Answer: a) Auditing your own school for diversity and proposing how it can include everyone** — That is what this lesson teaches.
