# Tolerance and Diversity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Respecting other people's cultures and values, with a reading of the Universal Declaration of Human Rights  b) it has nothing to do with how United Arab Emirates governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Respecting other people's cultures and values, with a reading of the Universal Declaration of Human Rights** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Respecting other people's cultures and values, with a reading of the Universal Declaration of Human Rights  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in United Arab Emirates
**Answer: a) Respecting other people's cultures and values, with a reading of the Universal Declaration of Human Rights** — That is what this lesson teaches.
