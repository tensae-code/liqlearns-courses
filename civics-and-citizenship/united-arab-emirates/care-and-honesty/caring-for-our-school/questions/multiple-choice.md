# Caring for Our School — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Keeping the classroom and school clean, tidy and safe as shared care  b) it has nothing to do with how United Arab Emirates governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Keeping the classroom and school clean, tidy and safe as shared care** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Keeping the classroom and school clean, tidy and safe as shared care  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in United Arab Emirates
**Answer: a) Keeping the classroom and school clean, tidy and safe as shared care** — That is what this lesson teaches.
