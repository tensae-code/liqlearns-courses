# Opening Day — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Opening the exhibition, reviewing it, and learning about other cultures to grow cultural competence  b) it has nothing to do with how United Arab Emirates governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Opening the exhibition, reviewing it, and learning about other cultures to grow cultural competence** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Opening the exhibition, reviewing it, and learning about other cultures to grow cultural competence  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in United Arab Emirates
**Answer: a) Opening the exhibition, reviewing it, and learning about other cultures to grow cultural competence** — That is what this lesson teaches.
