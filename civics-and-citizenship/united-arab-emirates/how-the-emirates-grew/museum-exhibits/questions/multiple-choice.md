# Museum Exhibits — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) What an exhibit is, from an Egyptian papyrus to the class's own virtual museum  b) it has nothing to do with how United Arab Emirates governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) What an exhibit is, from an Egyptian papyrus to the class's own virtual museum** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) What an exhibit is, from an Egyptian papyrus to the class's own virtual museum  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in United Arab Emirates
**Answer: a) What an exhibit is, from an Egyptian papyrus to the class's own virtual museum** — That is what this lesson teaches.
