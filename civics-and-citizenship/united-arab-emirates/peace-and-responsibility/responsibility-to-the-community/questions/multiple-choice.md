# Responsibility to the Community — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Social responsibility — caring for the community beyond yourself  b) it has nothing to do with how United Arab Emirates governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Social responsibility — caring for the community beyond yourself** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Social responsibility — caring for the community beyond yourself  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in United Arab Emirates
**Answer: a) Social responsibility — caring for the community beyond yourself** — That is what this lesson teaches.
