# Acting Responsibly — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) How to act responsibly and recognise what your choices do to others  b) it has nothing to do with how United Arab Emirates governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) How to act responsibly and recognise what your choices do to others** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) How to act responsibly and recognise what your choices do to others  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in United Arab Emirates
**Answer: a) How to act responsibly and recognise what your choices do to others** — That is what this lesson teaches.
