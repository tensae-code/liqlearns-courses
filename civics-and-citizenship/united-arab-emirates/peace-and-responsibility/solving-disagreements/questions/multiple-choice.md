# Solving Disagreements — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Resolving disagreements at home and at school  b) it has nothing to do with how United Arab Emirates governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Resolving disagreements at home and at school** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Resolving disagreements at home and at school  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in United Arab Emirates
**Answer: a) Resolving disagreements at home and at school** — That is what this lesson teaches.
