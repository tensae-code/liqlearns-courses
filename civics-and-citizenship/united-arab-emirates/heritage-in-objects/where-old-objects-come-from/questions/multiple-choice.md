# Where Old Objects Come From — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Talking about artefacts and judging the sources they come from  b) it has nothing to do with how United Arab Emirates governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Talking about artefacts and judging the sources they come from** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Talking about artefacts and judging the sources they come from  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in United Arab Emirates
**Answer: a) Talking about artefacts and judging the sources they come from** — That is what this lesson teaches.
