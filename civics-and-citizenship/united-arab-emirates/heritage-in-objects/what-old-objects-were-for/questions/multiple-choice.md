# What Old Objects Were for — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Describing historical pieces and working out what each was used for  b) it has nothing to do with how United Arab Emirates governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Describing historical pieces and working out what each was used for** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Describing historical pieces and working out what each was used for  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in United Arab Emirates
**Answer: a) Describing historical pieces and working out what each was used for** — That is what this lesson teaches.
