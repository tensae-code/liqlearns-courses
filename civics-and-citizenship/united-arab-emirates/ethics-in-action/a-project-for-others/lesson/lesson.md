# A Project for Others

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: A Project for Others. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what United Arab Emirates's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## Sub lesson 1 — Choosing the action

Choosing the direct action the project will take for others in the community.

## Sub lesson 2 — Planning the steps

Setting out the practical steps the project needs, in order.

## Sub lesson 3 — Inviting the community in

Inviting community members — including National Council members, as the book names them — to the school.

## Sub lesson 4 — Running and sharing the project

Running the project and sharing what was done and learned.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Ethics in Action (الأخلاق فعلاً) — the pupil plans and runs a real community project: choosing the direct action, the practical steps, inviting community members (including National Council members) to school, and sharing the project Give one real example from your own life or from the United Arab Emirates you live in, and name the lesson's key words: a, project, for. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: Ethics in Action (الأخلاق فعلاً) — the pupil plans and runs a real community project: choosing the direct action, the practical steps, inviting community members (including National Council members) to school, and sharing the project
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
