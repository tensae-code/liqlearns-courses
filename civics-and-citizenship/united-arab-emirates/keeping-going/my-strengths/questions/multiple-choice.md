# My Strengths — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Naming your own strengths and using them to get past difficulties  b) it has nothing to do with how United Arab Emirates governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Naming your own strengths and using them to get past difficulties** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Naming your own strengths and using them to get past difficulties  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in United Arab Emirates
**Answer: a) Naming your own strengths and using them to get past difficulties** — That is what this lesson teaches.
