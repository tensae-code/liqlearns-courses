Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Sticking with a hard project to the end and presenting the finished art project to parents  b) it has nothing to do with how United Arab Emirates governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
