# What Trade Is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) What trade is, and why the land and sea trade routes mattered  b) it has nothing to do with how United Arab Emirates governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) What trade is, and why the land and sea trade routes mattered** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) What trade is, and why the land and sea trade routes mattered  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in United Arab Emirates
**Answer: a) What trade is, and why the land and sea trade routes mattered** — That is what this lesson teaches.
