# Civics and Citizenship — United Arab Emirates course — CURRICULUM (the path)

One course for the United Arab Emirates inside the Civics and
Citizenship folder, organised by **levels, not grades**: a
learner climbs when they have proved the level, not when a
school calendar says so. The school grades the books come
from appear only in each lesson's sources (provenance),
never as the course's organisation. The path follows the
United Arab Emirates' own order as its books lay it out,
from the two filings' ladder sketches
(`united-arab-emirates-placements.md` — 7 records / 7
distinct books, Grades 1–6 — and
`united-arab-emirates-placements-b.md` — the Grade 8 Moral
Education student book, Volume 3), reconciled into one
ladder: the first filing's five levels stand as Levels 1–5,
and the second filing's middle-school book — which its own
sketch placed after the Grades 1–6 volumes and before the
Grades 9–12 volumes — stands as Level 6. The UAE Moral
Education programme runs on four strands (Character and
Morals; the Individual and Society; Cultural Studies; Civic
Studies), and the ladder follows the books' own widening
circle: care and honesty first, heritage as story and
object, character in action, then trade, peace and
settlement, then the nation itself — its health, its
diverse society, respect and tolerance — and finally
conflict faced directly and ethics put into action. The
filings stock 71 placements into 66 lessons: the coined
lesson names were deduped before building — the five
Grade 6 *How the Emirates Grew* lessons are stocked by two
placements each (the Grade 6 teacher guide, term 2, and the
Grade 6 student book, volume 2, teach the same unit) — and
every placement stocks into exactly one lesson.

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in
this course comes from government textbooks — this
country's own Moral Education and Social Studies and
National Education books — except the **Tip boxes**: a tip
in a box is from us, the people making LiqLearns. Tips are
ours; the lessons are the books'. See ABOUT.md for the full
book list and the acceptance reckoning. **26 of the 66
lessons stand on student books; 40 lessons stand on
teacher-guide evidence only and count 0 in the acceptance
reckoning, labelled as such in their sources.**

## The levels (the path, in order)

1. **Care, Honesty and the Work Around You** — The youngest child's circle: caring for yourself, others and your school, telling the truth and finding honesty in stories, and the work people do around you — your grandfathers' professions and the job you want when you grow up (Grade 1 books). 7 lessons, in 2 title(s): `care-and-honesty/` (5 lessons); `work-in-my-country/` (2 lessons).
2. **Belonging — Stories, Zayed and the Union, and Old Objects** — Belonging arrives as story and object: the tales grandparents tell and the UAE's heritage inside them, Zayed as father of the union and the days and heritage a child loves, and real artefacts a child can describe, source and explain (Grades 1–2). 13 lessons, in 3 title(s): `heritage-in-stories/` (5 lessons); `zayed-and-the-union/` (3 lessons); `heritage-in-objects/` (5 lessons).
3. **Character in Action — Keeping Going and Good Character** — Character in action: perseverance and flexibility first — working hard, naming your strengths, finishing and showing a project — then the named virtues that build a moral character: patience, forgiveness, upright words and deeds, and growing into good character (Grades 2 and 5). 10 lessons, in 2 title(s): `keeping-going/` (5 lessons); `moral-character/` (5 lessons).
4. **The Widening World — Trade, Peace and Settlement** — The widening world: how trade and travel made Emirati culture (routes, camels, caravanserais, ports), how disagreements are settled peacefully and responsibility reaches the community, and how families and tribes settled the land from pearls to oil (Grades 4–5). 15 lessons, in 3 title(s): `trade-travel-and-culture/` (5 lessons); `peace-and-responsibility/` (5 lessons); `family-and-settlement-in-the-emirates/` (5 lessons).
5. **The Nation Itself — Health, a Diverse Society, Respect and Tolerance** — The oldest primary pupil studies the nation itself: his own health as a responsibility, how the UAE grew into the diverse, inclusive society it is today (taught through its museums and a class exhibition), and how respect and tolerance hold that society together (Grade 6). 15 lessons, in 3 title(s): `my-health-my-responsibility/` (5 lessons); `how-the-emirates-grew/` (5 lessons); `respect-and-tolerance/` (5 lessons).
6. **Conflict and Ethics in Action** — The middle-school volume's printed body: conflict faced directly — internal conflict and external conflict, managing each, and resolving disagreements — and then ethics in action, a real community project the pupil plans and runs (Grade 8). 6 lessons, in 2 title(s): `dealing-with-conflict/` (5 lessons); `ethics-in-action/` (1 lesson).

**Totals: 66 lessons** (Level 1: 7 · Level 2: 13 · Level 3: 10 · Level 4: 15 · Level 5: 15 · Level 6: 6 — from eight read books: *Moral Education, Grade One, Term Two, trial edition* (student book, Ministry of Education, United Arab Emirates, 2017–2018, Arabic, COMPLETE), *Social Studies and National Education, Teacher's Guide, Grade One, Volume Two* (teacher guide, 2023–2024, Arabic, PARTIAL as filed), *Moral Education, Teacher's Guide, Grade Two, Term Two, trial edition* (teacher guide, 2017–2018, Arabic, COMPLETE), *Moral Education, Teacher's Guide, Grade Four, Term Two, trial edition* (teacher guide, 2017–2018, Arabic, COMPLETE), *Moral Education, Teacher's Guide, Grade Five, Term Two, trial edition* (teacher guide, 2017–2018, Arabic, COMPLETE), *Moral Education, Teacher's Guide, Grade Six, Term Two, trial edition* (teacher guide, 2017–2018, Arabic, COMPLETE), *Moral Education, Student Book, Grade Six, Volume Two, third edition* (student book, 2020–2021, Arabic, COMPLETE for the two units this volume contains), and *Moral Education, Student Book, Grade 8, Volume 3* (student book, third edition, 2020–2021, Arabic, COMPLETE for the printed volume); 71 placements stocking 66 lessons).

  Education, United Arab Emirates).

  Grade 8 volume 3's Units 1–4 are delivered only through
  the smart-learning application and are not in the printed

  Moral Education volumes for Grades 9–12 named in the
  second filing's leads are UNREAD and not yet in the
  course (see ABOUT.md). Nothing else is in the course, and
  no lesson pretends to one.
