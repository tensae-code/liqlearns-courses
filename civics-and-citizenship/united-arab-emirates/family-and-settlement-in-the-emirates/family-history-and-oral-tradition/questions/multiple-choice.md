# Family History and Oral Tradition — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Genealogy and oral tradition, practised by interviewing a member of the pupil's own family  b) it has nothing to do with how United Arab Emirates governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Genealogy and oral tradition, practised by interviewing a member of the pupil's own family** — That is what this lesson teaches.

a) Genealogy and oral tradition, practised by interviewing a member of the pupil's own family  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in United Arab Emirates
**Answer: a) Genealogy and oral tradition, practised by interviewing a member of the pupil's own family** — That is what this lesson teaches.
