# Reading Artefacts of Settlement — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Exploring artefacts and guessing their uses to read how people lived and settled  b) it has nothing to do with how United Arab Emirates governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Exploring artefacts and guessing their uses to read how people lived and settled** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Exploring artefacts and guessing their uses to read how people lived and settled  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in United Arab Emirates
**Answer: a) Exploring artefacts and guessing their uses to read how people lived and settled** — That is what this lesson teaches.
