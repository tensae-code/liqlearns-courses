# Writing Our Own Stories — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Children make and tell their own story, becoming carriers of the heritage themselves  b) it has nothing to do with how United Arab Emirates governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Children make and tell their own story, becoming carriers of the heritage themselves** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Children make and tell their own story, becoming carriers of the heritage themselves  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in United Arab Emirates
**Answer: a) Children make and tell their own story, becoming carriers of the heritage themselves** — That is what this lesson teaches.
