# About the Civics and Citizenship — Papua New Guinea course

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in
this course comes from government textbooks — this country's
own Citizenship & Christian Values Education (CCVE), Character
and Social Development (CSD), Legal Studies and Political
Science books. The one exception is the **Tip boxes**: a tip
in a box is from us, the people making LiqLearns. Tips are
ours; the lessons are the books'.

## How this course is organised

This is ONE course for Papua New Guinea inside the Civics and
Citizenship folder (one course per country). Inside it, the
organisation is by **levels, not grades**: a learner's place
is found by what they prove, and each level stands on the
ones before it as a recommended-first path — never a forced
gate. The school grades the books come from appear only in
each lesson's `lesson/sources.md`, as provenance.

**FOOTING — guides only, stated plainly, exactly as filed:**
Papua New Guinea's whole set in this wave is TEACHER GUIDES.
No pupil books are in hand for CCVE, Character and Social
Development, Legal Studies or Political Science. Every
placement in the filing is therefore marked TEACHER and rests
on guide evidence only; guides are never a substitute for a
missing pupil-book row, and every level of this course is
labelled guides-only.

**CCVE combined track, as filed:** each CCVE guide has 4
strands — (1) Civic Identities, Principles and Systems,
(2) Christian Values and Principles, (3) Citizenship and
Society, (4) Governance and Leadership. Strand 2 (the
Christian strand) is NAMED in every contents map and NEVER
placed. Strands 1, 3 and 4 are placed. At senior levels the
subject is renamed Christian & Citizenship Values Education
and its Strand 1 is the Christian strand — likewise named,
never placed; the civic strands are placed, with their
Christian framing noted as the book's own where the filing
notes it. For the CSD guides, Strand 2's health-behaviour
topics are health-subject content — named in the contents
maps, not placed into the civics course, except the
laws/policies, relationships, communication and conflict
topics the filing places.

The course as built has **five stocked levels** (Levels 1, 2,
4, 5 and 6; Level 3 is reserved, not stocked — see below),
from the filing for this build (`papua-new-guinea-placements.md`
— 20 records / 20 distinct books, all teacher guides). 178
placements in all, stocking 174 lessons — the coined lesson
names were deduped before building (see CURRICULUM.md,
"Where the build stands"): every placement stocks into
exactly one lesson, and every placement's provenance row is
in that lesson's `lesson/sources.md`.
