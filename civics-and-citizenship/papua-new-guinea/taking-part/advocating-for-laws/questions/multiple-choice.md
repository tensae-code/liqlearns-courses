# Advocating for Laws — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Citizens advocating for laws and policies and bringing about change.  b) it has nothing to do with how Papua New Guinea governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Citizens advocating for laws and policies and bringing about change.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Citizens advocating for laws and policies and bringing about change.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Papua New Guinea
**Answer: a) Citizens advocating for laws and policies and bringing about change.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
