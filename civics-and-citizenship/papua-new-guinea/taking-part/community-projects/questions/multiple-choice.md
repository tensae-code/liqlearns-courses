# Community Projects — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Planning community projects and advocating for community needs.  b) it has nothing to do with how Papua New Guinea governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Planning community projects and advocating for community needs.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Planning community projects and advocating for community needs.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Papua New Guinea
**Answer: a) Planning community projects and advocating for community needs.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
