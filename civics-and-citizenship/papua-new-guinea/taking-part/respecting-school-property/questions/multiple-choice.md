# Respecting School Property — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Respect for school property, praise and accepting correction.  b) it has nothing to do with how Papua New Guinea governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Respect for school property, praise and accepting correction.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Respect for school property, praise and accepting correction.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Papua New Guinea
**Answer: a) Respect for school property, praise and accepting correction.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
