# Rules and Peace — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Peace and harmony, and the consequences when rules are broken.  b) it has nothing to do with how Papua New Guinea governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Peace and harmony, and the consequences when rules are broken.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Peace and harmony, and the consequences when rules are broken.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Papua New Guinea
**Answer: a) Peace and harmony, and the consequences when rules are broken.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
