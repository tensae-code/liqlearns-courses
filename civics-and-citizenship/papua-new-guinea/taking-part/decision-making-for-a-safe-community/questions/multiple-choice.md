# Decision Making for a Safe Community — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Evidence-based responsible decision-making for a safe community.  b) it has nothing to do with how Papua New Guinea governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Evidence-based responsible decision-making for a safe community.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**Answer: a) Evidence-based responsible decision-making for a safe community.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
