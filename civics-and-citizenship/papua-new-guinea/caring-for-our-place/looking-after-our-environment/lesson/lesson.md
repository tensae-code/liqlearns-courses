# Looking After Our Environment

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Looking After Our Environment. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Papua New Guinea's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Community participation through rubbish, environment and a class community project.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Community participation through rubbish, environment and a class community project. Give one real example from your own life or from the Papua New Guinea you live in, and name the lesson's key words: looking after our. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: Community participation through rubbish, environment and a class community project.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
