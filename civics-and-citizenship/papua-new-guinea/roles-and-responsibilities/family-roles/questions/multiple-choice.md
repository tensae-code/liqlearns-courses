# Family Roles — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Everyone's role at home, including the child's own responsibilities.  b) it has nothing to do with how Papua New Guinea governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Everyone's role at home, including the child's own responsibilities.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Everyone's role at home, including the child's own responsibilities.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Papua New Guinea
**Answer: a) Everyone's role at home, including the child's own responsibilities.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
