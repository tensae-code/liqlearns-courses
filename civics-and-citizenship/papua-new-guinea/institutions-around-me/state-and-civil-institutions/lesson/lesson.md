# State and Civil Institutions

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: State and Civil Institutions. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Papua New Guinea's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

How a state institution (school, health centre, police station) and civil institutions work in the community.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: How a state institution (school, health centre, police station) and civil institutions work in the community. Give one real example from your own life or from the Papua New Guinea you live in, and name the lesson's key words: state civil institutions. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
