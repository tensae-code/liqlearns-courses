# Caring for Things — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Caring for family property and for state and civil institutions.  b) it has nothing to do with how Papua New Guinea governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Caring for family property and for state and civil institutions.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Caring for family property and for state and civil institutions.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Papua New Guinea
**Answer: a) Caring for family property and for state and civil institutions.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
