# Civil Institutions — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Family, church and clan/tribe as the civil institutions the child belongs to.  b) it has nothing to do with how Papua New Guinea governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Family, church and clan/tribe as the civil institutions the child belongs to.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Family, church and clan/tribe as the civil institutions the child belongs to.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Papua New Guinea
**Answer: a) Family, church and clan/tribe as the civil institutions the child belongs to.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
