# I Am a Citizen — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Characteristics of a citizen, basic individual rights, and the child's jobs at home, school and community.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that I Am a Citizen — and that it has no connection to the rest of Papua New Guinea's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Papua New Guinea's own course, in its own order.
