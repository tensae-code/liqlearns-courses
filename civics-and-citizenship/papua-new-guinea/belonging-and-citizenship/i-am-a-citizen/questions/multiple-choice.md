# I Am a Citizen — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Characteristics of a citizen, basic individual rights, and the child's jobs at home, school and community.  b) it has nothing to do with how Papua New Guinea governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Characteristics of a citizen, basic individual rights, and the child's jobs at home, school and community.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Characteristics of a citizen, basic individual rights, and the child's jobs at home, school and community.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Papua New Guinea
**Answer: a) Characteristics of a citizen, basic individual rights, and the child's jobs at home, school and community.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
