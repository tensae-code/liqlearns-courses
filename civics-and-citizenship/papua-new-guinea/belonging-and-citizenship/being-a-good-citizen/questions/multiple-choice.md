# Being a Good Citizen — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Good citizenship defined through the pupil's own duties and the class captain's job.  b) it has nothing to do with how Papua New Guinea governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Good citizenship defined through the pupil's own duties and the class captain's job.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Good citizenship defined through the pupil's own duties and the class captain's job.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Papua New Guinea
**Answer: a) Good citizenship defined through the pupil's own duties and the class captain's job.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
