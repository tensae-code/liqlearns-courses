# Being a Good Citizen

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Being a Good Citizen. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Papua New Guinea's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Good citizenship defined through the pupil's own duties and the class captain's job.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Good citizenship defined through the pupil's own duties and the class captain's job. Give one real example from your own life or from the Papua New Guinea you live in, and name the lesson's key words: being good citizen. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: Good citizenship defined through the pupil's own duties and the class captain's job.
