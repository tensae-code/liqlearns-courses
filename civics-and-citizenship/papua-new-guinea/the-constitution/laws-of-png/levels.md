# Laws of PNG — depth

This lesson is ONE lesson with no sub lessons: it teaches a single thing, whole. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: The laws as stated in the Constitution (summarised by topic, not transcribed), constitutional supremacy, emergency laws, what Local-Level Governments and provincial governments may legislate, delegated legislation, and the Underlying Law including Customary Law and Common Law and Equity. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
