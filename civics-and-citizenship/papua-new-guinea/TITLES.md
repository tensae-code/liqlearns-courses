# Civics and Citizenship — Papua New Guinea course — TITLES (titles from the bottom)

The same ladder as CURRICULUM.md, written as the list of unit
folders. Each unit is a theme (a "title") in Papua New Guinea's
own course; a unit's lessons sit at the levels the filing placed
them at, and each entry says what the unit holds and why it sits
at that level. Levels are the organisation; the school grades
the books come from appear only in each lesson's sources.
Level 3 is reserved and not stocked (the Grades 3–6 CCVE guides
are UNREAD — see ABOUT.md), so no unit entry appears at Level 3.
