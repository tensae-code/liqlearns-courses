# Manners and Care of Property — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The citizen's abilities and potentials shown in manners, care of property and not littering, fighting or cheating.  b) it has nothing to do with how Papua New Guinea governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The citizen's abilities and potentials shown in manners, care of property and not littering, fighting or cheating.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
