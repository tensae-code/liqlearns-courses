# Responsibility and Harmony — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Responsibility and harmony practised at home and school.  b) it has nothing to do with how Papua New Guinea governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Responsibility and harmony practised at home and school.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Responsibility and harmony practised at home and school.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Papua New Guinea
**Answer: a) Responsibility and harmony practised at home and school.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
