# Kindness and Its Opposites — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Kindness, joy and friendliness named against their opposites so children can tell them apart.  b) it has nothing to do with how Papua New Guinea governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Kindness, joy and friendliness named against their opposites so children can tell them apart.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Kindness, joy and friendliness named against their opposites so children can tell them apart.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Papua New Guinea
**Answer: a) Kindness, joy and friendliness named against their opposites so children can tell them apart.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
