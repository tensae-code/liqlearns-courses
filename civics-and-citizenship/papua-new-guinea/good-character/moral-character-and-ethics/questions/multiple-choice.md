# Moral Character and Ethics — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Moral character and ethical conduct of the individual citizen.  b) it has nothing to do with how Papua New Guinea governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Moral character and ethical conduct of the individual citizen.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Moral character and ethical conduct of the individual citizen.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Papua New Guinea
**Answer: a) Moral character and ethical conduct of the individual citizen.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
