# Time and Self Management

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Time and Self Management. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Papua New Guinea's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Managing time and resources and advocating for your own needs.
