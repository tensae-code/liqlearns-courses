# Principles of Socialism — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** What socialism is — equal ownership of the factors of production, allocation for individual and greater social needs, central planning, and its defining maxim (very short fragment only).
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that Principles of Socialism — and that it has no connection to the rest of Papua New Guinea's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Papua New Guinea's own course, in its own order.
