# Hands-on — The Law and You

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: The Law and You. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Papua New Guinea's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Family values and responsibilities, the two legally recognised marriages in PNG (customary and statutory), how each kind of marriage ends (custom, custody and property; National Court dissolution, maintenance and custody), and types of contract (oral, written, part-oral/part-written). Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
