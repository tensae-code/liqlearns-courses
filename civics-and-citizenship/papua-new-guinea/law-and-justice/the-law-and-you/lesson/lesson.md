# The Law and You

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: The Law and You. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Papua New Guinea's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Family values and responsibilities, the two legally recognised marriages in PNG (customary and statutory), how each kind of marriage ends (custom, custody and property; National Court dissolution, maintenance and custody), and types of contract (oral, written, part-oral/part-written).

Give one real example from your own life or from the Papua New Guinea you live in, and name the lesson's key words: law you. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
