# The Law and You — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Family values and responsibilities, the two legally recognised marriages in PNG (customary and statutory), how each kind of marriage ends (custom, custody and property; National Court dissolution, maintenance and custody), and types of contract (oral, written, part-oral/part-written).
