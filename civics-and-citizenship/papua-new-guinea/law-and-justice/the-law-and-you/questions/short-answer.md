# The Law and You — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** Family values and responsibilities, the two legally recognised marriages in PNG (customary and statutory), how each kind of marriage ends (custom, custody and property; National Court dissolution, maintenance and custody), and types of contract (oral, written, part-oral/part-written).

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** Family values and responsibilities, the two legally recognised marriages in PNG (customary and statutory), how each kind of marriage ends (custom, custody and property; National Court dissolution, maintenance and custody), and types of contract (oral, written, part-oral/part-written).
