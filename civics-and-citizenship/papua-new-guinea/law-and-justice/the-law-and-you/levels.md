# The Law and You — depth

This lesson is ONE lesson with no sub lessons: it teaches a single thing, whole. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: Family values and responsibilities, the two legally recognised marriages in PNG (customary and statutory), how each kind of marriage ends (custom, custody and property; National Court dissolution, maintenance and custody), and types of contract (oral, written, part-oral/part-written). — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
