# Natural Resources and the Law

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Natural Resources and the Law. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Papua New Guinea's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Ownership of land under custom, ownership of natural resources (mining, petroleum, forestry, fisheries), customary landowners' participation and spin-off benefits, sustainable development, and pollution case studies.
