# Protection of the Law — depth

This lesson is ONE lesson with no sub lessons: it teaches a single thing, whole. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: Criminal versus civil law, the rights engaged when police apprehend you (the Constitution's protection of the person, summarised by topic), apprehended/arrested/detained/charged distinguished, minor versus serious offences, steps when a wrong is done to you by a person, a company or the State, and a hypothetical human-rights case study of a police raid. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
