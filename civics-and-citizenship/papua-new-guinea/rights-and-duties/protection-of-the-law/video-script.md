# Video script — Protection of the Law

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Criminal versus civil law, the rights engaged when police apprehend you (the Constitution's protection of the person, summarised by topic), apprehended/arrested/detained/charged distinguished, minor versus serious offences, steps when a wrong is done to you by a person, a company or the State, and a hypothetical human-rights case study of a police raid.
