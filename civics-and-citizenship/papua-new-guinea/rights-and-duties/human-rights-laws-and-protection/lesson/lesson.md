# Human Rights Laws and Protection

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Human Rights Laws and Protection. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Papua New Guinea's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Human-rights laws, policies and practices, and protecting the rights of women and girls, people with disabilities and marginalised groups.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Human-rights laws, policies and practices, and protecting the rights of women and girls, people with disabilities and marginalised groups. Give one real example from your own life or from the Papua New Guinea you live in, and name the lesson's key words: human rights laws. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
