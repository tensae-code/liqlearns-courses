# Value of Rule of Law — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Why the rule of law matters.  b) it has nothing to do with how Papua New Guinea governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Why the rule of law matters.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Why the rule of law matters.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Papua New Guinea
**Answer: a) Why the rule of law matters.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
