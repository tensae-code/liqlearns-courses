# Enforcing Classroom Rules — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Rules are practised, enforced, and breaking them brings discipline and penalties.  b) it has nothing to do with how Papua New Guinea governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Rules are practised, enforced, and breaking them brings discipline and penalties.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Rules are practised, enforced, and breaking them brings discipline and penalties.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Papua New Guinea
**Answer: a) Rules are practised, enforced, and breaking them brings discipline and penalties.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
