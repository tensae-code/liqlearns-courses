# Choosing and Knowing Leaders — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Leaders are chosen and are found in community, home, church and school.  b) it has nothing to do with how Papua New Guinea governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Leaders are chosen and are found in community, home, church and school.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Leaders are chosen and are found in community, home, church and school.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Papua New Guinea
**Answer: a) Leaders are chosen and are found in community, home, church and school.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
