# Serving the Marginalised — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Servant leadership measured by how it serves the least privileged.  b) it has nothing to do with how Papua New Guinea governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Servant leadership measured by how it serves the least privileged.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Servant leadership measured by how it serves the least privileged.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Papua New Guinea
**Answer: a) Servant leadership measured by how it serves the least privileged.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
