# Honesty and Care

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Honesty and Care. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Papua New Guinea's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Principles of good governance beginning with honesty and care for people and environment.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Principles of good governance beginning with honesty and care for people and environment. Give one real example from your own life or from the Papua New Guinea you live in, and name the lesson's key words: honesty care. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: Principles of good governance beginning with honesty and care for people and environment.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
