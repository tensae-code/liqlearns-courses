# Parents and Family — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) What parents should and should not do, seen from the child's civic identity.  b) it has nothing to do with how Papua New Guinea governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) What parents should and should not do, seen from the child's civic identity.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) What parents should and should not do, seen from the child's civic identity.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Papua New Guinea
**Answer: a) What parents should and should not do, seen from the child's civic identity.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
