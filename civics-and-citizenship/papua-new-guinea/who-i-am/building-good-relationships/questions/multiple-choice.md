# Building Good Relationships — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Good relationships are built at home first, then in the community.  b) it has nothing to do with how Papua New Guinea governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Good relationships are built at home first, then in the community.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Good relationships are built at home first, then in the community.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Papua New Guinea
**Answer: a) Good relationships are built at home first, then in the community.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
