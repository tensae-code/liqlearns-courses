# My Family and Extended Family — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Family and extended family shape identity, beliefs and values.  b) it has nothing to do with how Papua New Guinea governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Family and extended family shape identity, beliefs and values.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Family and extended family shape identity, beliefs and values.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Papua New Guinea
**Answer: a) Family and extended family shape identity, beliefs and values.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
