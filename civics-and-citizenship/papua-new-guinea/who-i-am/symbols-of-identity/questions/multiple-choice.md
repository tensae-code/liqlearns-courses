# Symbols of Identity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Personal symbols, then the national and provincial flags and the Bird of Paradise emblem.  b) it has nothing to do with how Papua New Guinea governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Personal symbols, then the national and provincial flags and the Bird of Paradise emblem.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Personal symbols, then the national and provincial flags and the Bird of Paradise emblem.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Papua New Guinea
**Answer: a) Personal symbols, then the national and provincial flags and the Bird of Paradise emblem.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
