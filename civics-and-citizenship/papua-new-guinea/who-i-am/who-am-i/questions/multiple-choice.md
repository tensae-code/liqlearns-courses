Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The child names themselves, their family and their place in the community; Identity through family name and place of origin, and manners that change by group.  b) it has nothing to do with how Papua New Guinea governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
