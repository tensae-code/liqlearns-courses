# Who Am I — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The child names themselves, their family and their place in the community; Identity through family name and place of origin, and manners that change by group.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that Who Am I — and that it has no connection to the rest of Papua New Guinea's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Papua New Guinea's own course, in its own order.
