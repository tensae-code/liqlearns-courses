# Christian Community Groups — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Community groups as a form of civic identity and how they are built and represented.  b) it has nothing to do with how Papua New Guinea governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Community groups as a form of civic identity and how they are built and represented.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Community groups as a form of civic identity and how they are built and represented.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Papua New Guinea
**Answer: a) Community groups as a form of civic identity and how they are built and represented.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
