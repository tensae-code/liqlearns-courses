# Christian and Civic Identities — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) How Christian identity is formed and represented, as the senior-primary form of civic identity.  b) it has nothing to do with how Papua New Guinea governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) How Christian identity is formed and represented, as the senior-primary form of civic identity.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) How Christian identity is formed and represented, as the senior-primary form of civic identity.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Papua New Guinea
**Answer: a) How Christian identity is formed and represented, as the senior-primary form of civic identity.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
