# Civics and Citizenship — Papua New Guinea course — CURRICULUM (the path)

One course for Papua New Guinea inside the Civics and Citizenship folder,
organised by **levels, not grades**: a learner climbs when they
have proved the level, not when a school calendar says so. The
school grades the books come from appear only in each lesson's
sources (provenance), never as the course's organisation. The path
follows the books' own arcs, as sketched at the end of the filing:
the child's identity, family and first rules first (the Prep and
Grade 1 CCVE guides), widening to groups and influences (Grade 2),
then — across a gap the filing labels partial, because the
Grades 3–6 CCVE guides are UNREAD in this wave — citizens across
political systems and good governance (Grades 7–8), rights,
accountability and character at national scale (Grades 9–10 CCVE
and CSD), and at the top government systems, the Constitution and
PNG in the world (Grades 11–12, with the 2008 Legal Studies guide
kept whole and labelled as the older generation).

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in this
course comes from government textbooks — this country's own
Citizenship & Christian Values Education (CCVE), Character and
Social Development (CSD), Legal Studies and Political Science
books — except the **Tip boxes**: a tip in a box is from us, the
people making LiqLearns. Tips are ours; the lessons are the
books'. See ABOUT.md for the full book list, the guides-only
footing of every level, the UNREAD books, and the acceptance
reckoning.

**Guides-only footing — every level:** Papua New Guinea's whole
set in this wave is TEACHER GUIDES. No pupil books are in hand
for CCVE, Character and Social Development, Legal Studies or
Political Science. Every level below therefore rests on
teacher-guide evidence only and counts 0 in the acceptance
reckoning; every lesson's sources say so.

## The levels (the path, in order)

1. **Myself, My Family and First Rules** — the Prep and Grade 1 CCVE guides — the child's identity, family, clan/tribe, manners, the institutions in their own community, and house/family/school rules. PNG's own progression starts with the self and the home, taught through affective (feeling/attitude) objectives first. 35 lessons (38 placements filed at the level).

2. **Belonging to Groups and Choosing Good Influences** — the Grade 2 CCVE guide — identity as belonging to groups and a positive versus negative self-image, social influences good and bad ("Just say NO!"), what state and civil institutions give the community, and school citizenship. 16 lessons (16 placements filed at the level).

4. **Citizens Across Political Systems and Good Governance** — the Grade 7 and Grade 8 CCVE guides — senior primary renames the subject Christian & Citizenship Values Education and compares citizens' roles across systems (including communist societies), with good governance, equitable distribution, PNG's leadership code and PNG's own development performance. 20 lessons (20 placements filed at the level).

5. **Rights, Accountability and Character at National Scale** — the Grade 9–10 CCVE guides and Grade 9–10 CSD guides — junior secondary adds rights and human-rights organisations, the rights of marginalised people, accountability and transparency in political governance, economic governance judged by PNG's own economic performance, servant and steward leadership, civic justice, and character topics (bullying, gender-based violence, the youth homebrew problem) at national scale. 39 lessons (40 placements filed at the level).

6. **Government Systems, the Constitution and PNG in the World** — the Grade 11–12 CCVE, CSD and Political Science guides and the Grades 11–12 Legal Studies guide (2008, older generation, kept whole and labelled) — senior secondary completes the spine: government systems (communism, socialism, democracy, monarchy, republic), PNG's Constitution and its laws, courts, human rights across systems, administrative governance and corruption, and PNG in the Pacific and the world. 64 lessons (64 placements filed at the level).

3. **Reserved — not stocked.** Level 3 is reserved for the
   unread Grades 4–6 CCVE guides, as the filing's ladder sketch
   states. The CCVE teacher guides for Grades 3, 4, 5 and 6
   (Books 4–7 in the filing) are RECORD-ONLY — UNREAD, with
   0 placements each; they are named in ABOUT.md. The ladder
   between Level 2 and Level 4 is therefore evidenced at its
   ends only and is labelled **partial**, exactly as filed.

**Totals: 174 lessons, 178 placements stocked** (Level 1: 35
lessons · Level 2: 16 lessons · Level 4: 20 lessons · Level 5:
39 lessons · Level 6: 64 lessons).
