# Civics and Citizenship — Greece course — CURRICULUM (the path)

One course for Greece inside the Civics and Citizenship folder,
organised by **levels, not grades**: a learner climbs when they
have proved the level, not when a school calendar says so. The
school grades the books come from appear only in each lesson's
sources (provenance), never as the course's organisation. The
path follows Greece's own order as its two books lay it out:
first the individual and the state (the Gymnasium book's civics
strand), then outward to the European Union and the
international community, then the Lykeio Πολιτική Παιδεία —
society, state and economy as one system, and at its centre the
free, responsible and active citizen.

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in this
course comes from government textbooks — this country's own
Social and Political Education and Political Education books —
except the **Tip boxes**: a tip in a box is from us, the people
making LiqLearns. Tips are ours; the lessons are the books'.
See ABOUT.md for the full book list, the phantom-push record of
both books, Book 1's combined-track footing, and the
acceptance reckoning.

## The levels (the path, in order)

1. **The Individual and the State** — what the state is and
   the citizen's relation to it; forms of government and the
   Constitution; elections, political parties and the mass
   media; the functions of the state; public administration;
   and rights paired with obligations. 6 lessons, in one
   title: `the-individual-and-the-state/` (6 lessons).
2. **Greece, Europe and the World** — the European Union and
   the individual within it; the international community and
   Greece's place in it. 2 lessons, in one title:
   `greece-europe-and-the-world/` (2 lessons).
3. **Society, the State and the Economy, and the Free,
   Responsible and Active Citizen** — the human being as
   social, economic and political being (the polis and the
   citizen, the political art); society from agrarian to
   information; the economy, entrepreneurship and innovation,
   money and banks; the institutions and values of Greek
   society (the Greek Orthodox tradition, the family,
   education, freedom); the political community's ladder
   (Municipality, Region, State, European Union),
   socialisation and political socialisation, migration and
   refugees, and social problems; and the free, responsible
   and active citizen, decision-making, and sustainable
   development. 13 lessons, in four titles:
   `society-state-and-economy/` (5 lessons),
   `institutions-and-values-of-greek-society/` (1),
   `the-political-community/` (4),
   `the-free-responsible-and-active-citizen/` (3). Inside this
   level the lessons are grouped by the book's own themes;
   chapter order is preserved inside each title and every
   lesson's sources carry its chapter number.

**Totals: 21 lessons** (Level 1: 6 · Level 2: 2 · Level 3: 13
— all from two student books).
