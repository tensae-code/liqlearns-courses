# Video script — Sustainable Development

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Sustainable development meets people's needs without spending the natural world the next generations need — by saving natural resources and turning to renewable energy sources.

Human life rests on the natural environment: its air, water, land and life are the base every economy and city stands on.

Harming that base is not an outside matter — it is harm to the conditions of our own life.

Development models differ: some spend nature for quick gain, and sustainable development is the model that develops without consuming its own base.

Comparing models by what they leave behind is the lesson's test.

Saving natural resources and turning to renewable energy sources — sun, wind, water — are the practical heart of the model.

What a citizen saves and chooses daily is the model becoming real.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
