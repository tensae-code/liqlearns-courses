# Sustainable Development — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** The natural environment and the human being; Development models; Saving resources and renewable energy.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a citizen of Greece?
**Answer:** Because sustainable development meets people's needs without spending the natural world the next generations need — by saving natural resources and turning to renewable energy sources — and a citizen who understands it can take their real place in that shared life.
