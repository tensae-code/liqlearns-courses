# Sustainable Development — matching

Match each word with its meaning.

**1.** The natural environment and the human being — a)

**2.** Development models — b)

**3.** Saving resources and renewable energy — c)


Meanings:
a) Human life rests on the natural environment: its air, water, land and life are the base every economy and city stands on.
b) Development models differ: some spend nature for quick gain, and sustainable development is the model that develops without consuming its own base.
c) Saving natural resources and turning to renewable energy sources — sun, wind, water — are the practical heart of the model.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b; 3. c
