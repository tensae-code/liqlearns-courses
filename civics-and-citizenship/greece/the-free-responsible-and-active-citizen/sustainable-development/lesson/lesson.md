# Sustainable Development

## Do it first

List three things your household uses that come straight from nature — water, wood, fuel. Mark which could run out. This lesson names the way of growing that does not run them out: sustainable development.

## Sub lesson 1 — The natural environment and the human being

Human life rests on the natural environment: its air, water, land and life are the base every economy and city stands on.

Harming that base is not an outside matter — it is harm to the conditions of our own life.

## Sub lesson 2 — Development models

Development models differ: some spend nature for quick gain, and sustainable development is the model that develops without consuming its own base.

Comparing models by what they leave behind is the lesson's test.

## Sub lesson 3 — Saving resources and renewable energy

Saving natural resources and turning to renewable energy sources — sun, wind, water — are the practical heart of the model.

What a citizen saves and chooses daily is the model becoming real.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: sustainable development meets people's needs without spending the natural world the next generations need — by saving natural resources and turning to renewable energy sources. Give one real example from your own life or from the Greece you live in, and name the lesson's key words: sustainable development, natural resources, renewable energy. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
