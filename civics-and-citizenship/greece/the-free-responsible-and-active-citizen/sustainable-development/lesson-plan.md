# Lesson plan — Sustainable Development

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Greece · **Title:** The Free, Responsible and Active Citizen · **Lesson:** Sustainable Development
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Society, the State and the Economy, and the Free, Responsible and Active Citizen (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** sustainable development meets people's needs without spending the natural world the next generations need — by saving natural resources and turning to renewable energy sources.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — Society, the State and the Economy, and the Free, Responsible and Active Citizen — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The natural environment and the human being** — Human life rests on the natural environment: its air, water, land and life are the base every economy and city stands on.
2. **Development models** — Development models differ: some spend nature for quick gain, and sustainable development is the model that develops without consuming its own base.
3. **Saving resources and renewable energy** — Saving natural resources and turning to renewable energy sources — sun, wind, water — are the practical heart of the model.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
