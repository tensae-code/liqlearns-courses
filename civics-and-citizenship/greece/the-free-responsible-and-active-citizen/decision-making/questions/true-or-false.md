# Decision-Making — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of an ordinary citizen in Greece.
**Answer: False** — Decisions — individual and collective — are made well by criteria: naming the options, weighing them against values and rules, and keeping the collective interest in view.

**2.** Some decisions are individual — one person's to make; others are collective — a group's, a community's, a state's — and the two kinds ask different care.
**Answer: True**
