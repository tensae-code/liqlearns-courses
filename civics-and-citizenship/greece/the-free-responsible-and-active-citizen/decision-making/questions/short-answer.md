# Decision-Making — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Individual and collective decisions; Criteria, values and rules; The collective interest.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a citizen of Greece?
**Answer:** Because decisions — individual and collective — are made well by criteria: naming the options, weighing them against values and rules, and keeping the collective interest in view — and a citizen who understands it can take their real place in that shared life.
