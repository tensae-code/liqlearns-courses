# Decision-Making — matching

Match each word with its meaning.

**1.** Individual and collective decisions — a)

**2.** Criteria, values and rules — b)

**3.** The collective interest — c)


Meanings:
a) Some decisions are individual — one person's to make; others are collective — a group's, a community's, a state's — and the two kinds ask different care.
b) Right decisions are tested against criteria: the facts, the values at stake, and the rules that bind the deciders.
c) In collective decisions the measure is the collective interest — the good of the whole the decision binds — not the advantage of the strongest part.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b; 3. c
