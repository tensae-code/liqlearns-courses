# Decision-Making — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) decisions — individual and collective — are made well by criteria: naming the options, weighing them against values and rules, and keeping the collective interest in view  b) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary citizens  c) that citizens need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and Greece has no way of its own
**Answer: a) decisions — individual and collective — are made well by criteria: naming the options, weighing them against values and rules, and keeping the collective interest in view** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Criteria, values and rules” is right?
a) Right decisions are tested against criteria: the facts, the values at stake, and the rules that bind the deciders.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no citizen ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Greece's book
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
