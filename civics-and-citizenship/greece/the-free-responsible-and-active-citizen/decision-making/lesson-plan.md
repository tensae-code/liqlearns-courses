# Lesson plan — Decision-Making

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Greece · **Title:** The Free, Responsible and Active Citizen · **Lesson:** Decision-Making
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Society, the State and the Economy, and the Free, Responsible and Active Citizen (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** decisions — individual and collective — are made well by criteria: naming the options, weighing them against values and rules, and keeping the collective interest in view.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — Society, the State and the Economy, and the Free, Responsible and Active Citizen — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Individual and collective decisions** — Some decisions are individual — one person's to make; others are collective — a group's, a community's, a state's — and the two kinds ask different care.
2. **Criteria, values and rules** — Right decisions are tested against criteria: the facts, the values at stake, and the rules that bind the deciders.
3. **The collective interest** — In collective decisions the measure is the collective interest — the good of the whole the decision binds — not the advantage of the strongest part.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
