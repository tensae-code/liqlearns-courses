## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Free** — The free citizen thinks and chooses for themselves, under the law and in respect of others' freedom.
2. **Responsible and active** — The responsible citizen answers for their choices; the active citizen takes part — in the community, in decisions, in common work.
3. **Fields of action** — The book practises the axiom in named fields: environmental education, consumer education and health education — the citizen caring for the environment, choosing wisely as a consumer, and guarding health.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
