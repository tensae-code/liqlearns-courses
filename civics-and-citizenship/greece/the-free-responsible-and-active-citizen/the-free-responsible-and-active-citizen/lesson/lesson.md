# The Free, Responsible and Active Citizen

## Do it first

Describe, in three words, the citizen you want to be at voting age. This lesson gives the course's own three words — free, responsible, active — and the fields where they are practised.

## Sub lesson 1 — Free

The free citizen thinks and chooses for themselves, under the law and in respect of others' freedom.

Freedom here is a capacity to be grown, not only a right to be claimed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Responsible and active

The responsible citizen answers for their choices; the active citizen takes part — in the community, in decisions, in common work.

The three words are one citizen: freedom without responsibility decays, responsibility without action sleeps.

## Sub lesson 3 — Fields of action

The book practises the axiom in named fields: environmental education, consumer education and health education — the citizen caring for the environment, choosing wisely as a consumer, and guarding health.

Across its eleven hours, the chapter turns the axiom into habits a learner can actually build.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the citizen this course builds is free, responsible and active — and proves it in fields of action that include environmental education, consumer education and health education. Give one real example from your own life or from the Greece you live in, and name the lesson's key words: free citizen, responsible citizen, active citizen. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
