# The Free, Responsible and Active Citizen — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of an ordinary citizen in Greece.
**Answer: False** — The citizen this course builds is free, responsible and active — and proves it in fields of action that include environmental education, consumer education and health education.

**2.** The free citizen thinks and chooses for themselves, under the law and in respect of others' freedom.
**Answer: True**
