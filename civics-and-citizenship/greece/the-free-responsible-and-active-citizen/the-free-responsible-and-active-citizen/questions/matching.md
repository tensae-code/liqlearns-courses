# The Free, Responsible and Active Citizen — matching

Match each word with its meaning.

**1.** Free — a)

**2.** Responsible and active — b)

**3.** Fields of action — c)


Meanings:
a) The free citizen thinks and chooses for themselves, under the law and in respect of others' freedom.
b) The responsible citizen answers for their choices; the active citizen takes part — in the community, in decisions, in common work.
c) The book practises the axiom in named fields: environmental education, consumer education and health education — the citizen caring for the environment, choosing wisely as a consumer, and guarding health.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b; 3. c
