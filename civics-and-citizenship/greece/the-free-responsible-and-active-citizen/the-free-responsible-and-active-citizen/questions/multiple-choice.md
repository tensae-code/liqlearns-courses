# The Free, Responsible and Active Citizen — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) the citizen this course builds is free, responsible and active — and proves it in fields of action that include environmental education, consumer education and health education  b) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary citizens  c) that citizens need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and Greece has no way of its own
**Answer: a) the citizen this course builds is free, responsible and active — and proves it in fields of action that include environmental education, consumer education and health education** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Responsible and active” is right?
a) The responsible citizen answers for their choices; the active citizen takes part — in the community, in decisions, in common work.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no citizen ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Greece's book
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
