# The Free, Responsible and Active Citizen — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Free; Responsible and active; Fields of action.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a citizen of Greece?
**Answer:** Because the citizen this course builds is free, responsible and active — and proves it in fields of action that include environmental education, consumer education and health education — and a citizen who understands it can take their real place in that shared life.
