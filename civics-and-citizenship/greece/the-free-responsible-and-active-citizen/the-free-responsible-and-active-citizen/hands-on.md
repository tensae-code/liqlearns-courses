# Hands-on — The Free, Responsible and Active Citizen

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Describe, in three words, the citizen you want to be at voting age. This lesson gives the course's own three words — free, responsible, active — and the fields where they are practised.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the citizen this course builds is free, responsible and active — and proves it in fields of action that include environmental education, consumer education and health education. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
