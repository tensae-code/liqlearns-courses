# Video script — Socialisation and Political Socialisation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A person becomes a member of society — and a citizen — through socialisation and political socialisation, whose first agents are the family, the school and education.

Socialisation is the process by which a person learns the ways, values and roles of their society and becomes its member.

It begins at birth and never wholly ends.

Political socialisation is the political part of that learning: how a person comes to know the state, form views, and take part as a citizen.

Its lessons can be deliberate — or absorbed without noticing, which is why they must be seen clearly.

The family is the first agent, the school and education the second great one — with peers, media and public life adding their lessons.

Naming the agents lets a citizen choose their teachers instead of only inheriting them.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
