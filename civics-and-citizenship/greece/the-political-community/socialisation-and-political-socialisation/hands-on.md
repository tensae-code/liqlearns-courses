# Hands-on — Socialisation and Political Socialisation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
List where you learned how to greet, how to queue, how to vote one day, and what your country is. This lesson names the process behind your list, and the agents who carry it.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: a person becomes a member of society — and a citizen — through socialisation and political socialisation, whose first agents are the family, the school and education. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
