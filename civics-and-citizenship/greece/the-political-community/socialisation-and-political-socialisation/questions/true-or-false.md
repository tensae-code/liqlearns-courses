# Socialisation and Political Socialisation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of an ordinary citizen in Greece.
**Answer: False** — A person becomes a member of society — and a citizen — through socialisation and political socialisation, whose first agents are the family, the school and education.

**2.** Socialisation is the process by which a person learns the ways, values and roles of their society and becomes its member.
**Answer: True**
