# Socialisation and Political Socialisation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) a person becomes a member of society — and a citizen — through socialisation and political socialisation, whose first agents are the family, the school and education  b) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary citizens  c) that citizens need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and Greece has no way of its own
**Answer: a) a person becomes a member of society — and a citizen — through socialisation and political socialisation, whose first agents are the family, the school and education** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Political socialisation” is right?
a) Political socialisation is the political part of that learning: how a person comes to know the state, form views, and take part as a citizen.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no citizen ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Greece's book
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
