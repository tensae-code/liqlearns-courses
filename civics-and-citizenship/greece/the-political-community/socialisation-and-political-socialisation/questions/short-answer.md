# Socialisation and Political Socialisation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Socialisation; Political socialisation; The agents: family, school and education.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a citizen of Greece?
**Answer:** Because a person becomes a member of society — and a citizen — through socialisation and political socialisation, whose first agents are the family, the school and education — and a citizen who understands it can take their real place in that shared life.
