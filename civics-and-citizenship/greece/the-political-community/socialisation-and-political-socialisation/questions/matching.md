# Socialisation and Political Socialisation — matching

Match each word with its meaning.

**1.** Socialisation — a)

**2.** Political socialisation — b)

**3.** The agents: family, school and education — c)


Meanings:
a) Socialisation is the process by which a person learns the ways, values and roles of their society and becomes its member.
b) Political socialisation is the political part of that learning: how a person comes to know the state, form views, and take part as a citizen.
c) The family is the first agent, the school and education the second great one — with peers, media and public life adding their lessons.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b; 3. c
