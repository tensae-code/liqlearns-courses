# Hands-on — Social Problems

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name one problem in your town that no single family caused and no single family can fix. This lesson gives such problems their method: define them, find their causes, weigh their consequences, and tackle them.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: a social problem is a condition that harms many people and calls for common action — it has causes and consequences that can be named, and it can be tackled, as poverty and unemployment show. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
