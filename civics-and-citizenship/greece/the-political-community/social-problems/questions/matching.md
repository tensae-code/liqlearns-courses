# Social Problems — matching

Match each word with its meaning.

**1.** Defining a social problem — a)

**2.** Causes and consequences — b)

**3.** Tackling them — poverty and unemployment — c)


Meanings:
a) A social problem is a harmful condition affecting many people, recognised as calling for common action — not merely a private trouble.
b) Social problems have causes that can be traced and consequences that spread — to families, to the economy, to trust in common life.
c) Poverty and unemployment are the book's standing examples: conditions to be tackled by policy, work, education and solidarity together.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b; 3. c
