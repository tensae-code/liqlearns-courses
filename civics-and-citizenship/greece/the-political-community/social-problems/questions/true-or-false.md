# Social Problems — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of an ordinary citizen in Greece.
**Answer: False** — A social problem is a condition that harms many people and calls for common action — it has causes and consequences that can be named, and it can be tackled, as poverty and unemployment show.

**2.** A social problem is a harmful condition affecting many people, recognised as calling for common action — not merely a private trouble.
**Answer: True**
