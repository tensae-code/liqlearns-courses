# Social Problems — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Defining a social problem; Causes and consequences; Tackling them — poverty and unemployment.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a citizen of Greece?
**Answer:** Because a social problem is a condition that harms many people and calls for common action — it has causes and consequences that can be named, and it can be tackled, as poverty and unemployment show — and a citizen who understands it can take their real place in that shared life.
