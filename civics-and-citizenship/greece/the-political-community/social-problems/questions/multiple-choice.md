# Social Problems — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) a social problem is a condition that harms many people and calls for common action — it has causes and consequences that can be named, and it can be tackled, as poverty and unemployment show  b) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary citizens  c) that citizens need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and Greece has no way of its own
**Answer: a) a social problem is a condition that harms many people and calls for common action — it has causes and consequences that can be named, and it can be tackled, as poverty and unemployment show** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Causes and consequences” is right?
a) Social problems have causes that can be traced and consequences that spread — to families, to the economy, to trust in common life.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no citizen ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Greece's book
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
