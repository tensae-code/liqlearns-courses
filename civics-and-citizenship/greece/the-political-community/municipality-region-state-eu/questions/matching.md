# Municipality, Region, State and the European Union — matching

Match each word with its meaning.

**1.** The Municipality — local society — a)

**2.** The Region and the State — b)

**3.** The European Union — a wider common polity — c)


Meanings:
a) The first rung is the Municipality: the political community of local society, closest to daily life.
b) The Region gathers the wider society between locality and nation, and the State is the full political community of the country.
c) Above the state stands the European Union as a wider common polity its member states share.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b; 3. c
