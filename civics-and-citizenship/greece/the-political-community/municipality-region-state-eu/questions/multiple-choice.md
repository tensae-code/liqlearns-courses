# Municipality, Region, State and the European Union — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) the political community is a ladder: the Municipality (local society), the Region (wider society), the State, and the European Union as a wider common polity  b) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary citizens  c) that citizens need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and Greece has no way of its own
**Answer: a) the political community is a ladder: the Municipality (local society), the Region (wider society), the State, and the European Union as a wider common polity** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “The Region and the State” is right?
a) The Region gathers the wider society between locality and nation, and the State is the full political community of the country.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no citizen ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Greece's book
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
