# Municipality, Region, State and the European Union — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of an ordinary citizen in Greece.
**Answer: False** — The political community is a ladder: the municipality (local society), the region (wider society), the state, and the european union as a wider common polity.

**2.** The first rung is the Municipality: the political community of local society, closest to daily life.
**Answer: True**
