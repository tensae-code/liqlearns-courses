# Municipality, Region, State and the European Union — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** The Municipality — local society; The Region and the State; The European Union — a wider common polity.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a citizen of Greece?
**Answer:** Because the political community is a ladder: the Municipality (local society), the Region (wider society), the State, and the European Union as a wider common polity — and a citizen who understands it can take their real place in that shared life.
