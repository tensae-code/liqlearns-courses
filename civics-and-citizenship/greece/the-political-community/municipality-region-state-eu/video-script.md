# Video script — Municipality, Region, State and the European Union

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The political community is a ladder: the municipality (local society), the region (wider society), the state, and the european union as a wider common polity.

The first rung is the Municipality: the political community of local society, closest to daily life.

Its matters are the street, the school building, the water — the shared life you can walk through.

The Region gathers the wider society between locality and nation, and the State is the full political community of the country.

Each rung handles what its width fits: nearer matters nearer home, common matters in common.

Above the state stands the European Union as a wider common polity its member states share.

The ladder does not replace the lower rungs — it completes them.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
