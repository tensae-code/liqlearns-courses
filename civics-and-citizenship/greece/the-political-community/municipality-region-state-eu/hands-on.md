# Hands-on — Municipality, Region, State and the European Union

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Draw four circles around your home: your neighbourhood's council, your region, your country, and Europe. This lesson builds that ladder rung by rung — the political community at every width.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the political community is a ladder: the Municipality (local society), the Region (wider society), the State, and the European Union as a wider common polity. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
