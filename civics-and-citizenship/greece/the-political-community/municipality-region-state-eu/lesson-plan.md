# Lesson plan — Municipality, Region, State and the European Union

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Greece · **Title:** The Political Community · **Lesson:** Municipality, Region, State and the European Union
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Society, the State and the Economy, and the Free, Responsible and Active Citizen (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the political community is a ladder: the Municipality (local society), the Region (wider society), the State, and the European Union as a wider common polity.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — Society, the State and the Economy, and the Free, Responsible and Active Citizen — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The Municipality — local society** — The first rung is the Municipality: the political community of local society, closest to daily life.
2. **The Region and the State** — The Region gathers the wider society between locality and nation, and the State is the full political community of the country.
3. **The European Union — a wider common polity** — Above the state stands the European Union as a wider common polity its member states share.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: the political community is a ladder: the Municipality (local society), the Region (wider society), the State, and the European Union as a wider common polity.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or town — or, for this book's lessons, from the public life of Greece they live in.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
