# Municipality, Region, State and the European Union

## Do it first

Draw four circles around your home: your neighbourhood's council, your region, your country, and Europe. This lesson builds that ladder rung by rung — the political community at every width.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Municipality — local society

The first rung is the Municipality: the political community of local society, closest to daily life.

Its matters are the street, the school building, the water — the shared life you can walk through.

## Sub lesson 2 — The Region and the State

The Region gathers the wider society between locality and nation, and the State is the full political community of the country.

Each rung handles what its width fits: nearer matters nearer home, common matters in common.

## Sub lesson 3 — The European Union — a wider common polity

Above the state stands the European Union as a wider common polity its member states share.

The ladder does not replace the lower rungs — it completes them.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the political community is a ladder: the Municipality (local society), the Region (wider society), the State, and the European Union as a wider common polity. Give one real example from your own life or from the Greece you live in, and name the lesson's key words: Municipality, Region, State, European Union, political community. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
