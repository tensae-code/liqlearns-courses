# Migration and Refugees — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Categories and causes of migration
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Migration takes categories — by reason, by distance, by whether it is chosen or forced — and its causes push and pull: hardship and danger push, work and safety pull. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — Social and economic consequences
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Migration changes both ends of the journey: the place left loses and gains, the place reached gains workers and neighbours and must organise their welcome. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 3 — Refugees and the Schengen agreement
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Refugees are people fleeing danger, and they stand under special protection; the Schengen agreement governs movement across much of Europe without internal border checks. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
