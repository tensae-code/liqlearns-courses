**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Greece · **Title:** The Political Community · **Lesson:** Migration and Refugees

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Categories and causes of migration** — Migration takes categories — by reason, by distance, by whether it is chosen or forced — and its causes push and pull: hardship and danger push, work and safety pull.
2. **Social and economic consequences** — Migration changes both ends of the journey: the place left loses and gains, the place reached gains workers and neighbours and must organise their welcome.
3. **Refugees and the Schengen agreement** — Refugees are people fleeing danger, and they stand under special protection; the Schengen agreement governs movement across much of Europe without internal border checks.
