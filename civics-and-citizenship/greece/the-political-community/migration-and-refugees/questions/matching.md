# Migration and Refugees — matching

Match each word with its meaning.

**1.** Categories and causes of migration — a)

**2.** Social and economic consequences — b)

**3.** Refugees and the Schengen agreement — c)


Meanings:
a) Migration takes categories — by reason, by distance, by whether it is chosen or forced — and its causes push and pull: hardship and danger push, work and safety pull.
b) Migration changes both ends of the journey: the place left loses and gains, the place reached gains workers and neighbours and must organise their welcome.
c) Refugees are people fleeing danger, and they stand under special protection; the Schengen agreement governs movement across much of Europe without internal border checks.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b; 3. c
