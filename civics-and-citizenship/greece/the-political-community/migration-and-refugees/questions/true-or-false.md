# Migration and Refugees — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of an ordinary citizen in Greece.
**Answer: False** — Migration has categories and causes and carries social and economic consequences for both the places left and the places reached — and refugees, people fleeing danger, stand under special protection, with the schengen agreement governing movement in much of europe.

**2.** Migration takes categories — by reason, by distance, by whether it is chosen or forced — and its causes push and pull: hardship and danger push, work and safety pull.
**Answer: True**
