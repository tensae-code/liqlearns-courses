# Migration and Refugees — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Categories and causes of migration; Social and economic consequences; Refugees and the Schengen agreement.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a citizen of Greece?
**Answer:** Because migration has categories and causes and carries social and economic consequences for both the places left and the places reached — and refugees, people fleeing danger, stand under special protection, with the Schengen agreement governing movement in much of Europe — and a citizen who understands it can take their real place in that shared life.
