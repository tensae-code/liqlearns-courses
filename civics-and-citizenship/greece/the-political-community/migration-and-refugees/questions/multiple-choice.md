# Migration and Refugees — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) migration has categories and causes and carries social and economic consequences for both the places left and the places reached — and refugees, people fleeing danger, stand under special protection, with the Schengen agreement governing movement in much of Europe  b) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary citizens  c) that citizens need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and Greece has no way of its own
**Answer: a) migration has categories and causes and carries social and economic consequences for both the places left and the places reached — and refugees, people fleeing danger, stand under special protection, with the Schengen agreement governing movement in much of Europe** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Social and economic consequences” is right?
a) Migration changes both ends of the journey: the place left loses and gains, the place reached gains workers and neighbours and must organise their welcome.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no citizen ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Greece's book
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
