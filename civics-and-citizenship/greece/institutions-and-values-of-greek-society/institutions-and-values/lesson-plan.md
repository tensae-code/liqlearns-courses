# Lesson plan — Institutions and Values of Greek Society

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Greece · **Title:** Institutions and Values of Greek Society · **Lesson:** Institutions and Values of Greek Society
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Society, the State and the Economy, and the Free, Responsible and Active Citizen (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Greek society is held together by its institutions and values — among them the Greek Orthodox tradition, the family as the cell of individual and collective life, education, and freedom.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — Society, the State and the Economy, and the Free, Responsible and Active Citizen — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Institutions and values** — An institution is a lasting, organised form of common life; values are what a society holds good and passes on through its institutions.
2. **Religion, the family and education** — The book names the Greek Orthodox tradition's place in Greek society, the family as the cell of individual and collective life, and education as the institution that forms each new generation.
3. **Freedom** — Freedom stands among the society's core values — the space in which persons and institutions alike can live and grow.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
