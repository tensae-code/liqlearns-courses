# Institutions and Values of Greek Society — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Institutions and values; Religion, the family and education; Freedom.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a citizen of Greece?
**Answer:** Because Greek society is held together by its institutions and values — among them the Greek Orthodox tradition, the family as the cell of individual and collective life, education, and freedom — and a citizen who understands it can take their real place in that shared life.
