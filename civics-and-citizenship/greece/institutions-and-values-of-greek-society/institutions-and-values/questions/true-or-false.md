# Institutions and Values of Greek Society — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of an ordinary citizen in Greece.
**Answer: False** — Greek society is held together by its institutions and values — among them the greek orthodox tradition, the family as the cell of individual and collective life, education, and freedom.

**2.** An institution is a lasting, organised form of common life; values are what a society holds good and passes on through its institutions.
**Answer: True**
