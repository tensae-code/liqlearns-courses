# Institutions and Values of Greek Society — matching

Match each word with its meaning.

**1.** Institutions and values — a)

**2.** Religion, the family and education — b)

**3.** Freedom — c)


Meanings:
a) An institution is a lasting, organised form of common life; values are what a society holds good and passes on through its institutions.
b) The book names the Greek Orthodox tradition's place in Greek society, the family as the cell of individual and collective life, and education as the institution that forms each new generation.
c) Freedom stands among the society's core values — the space in which persons and institutions alike can live and grow.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b; 3. c
