# The European Union

## Do it first

Find Greece on a map of Europe. Write down one thing Greece decides alone and one thing European countries decide together — trade, travel, or clean seas. This lesson names that shared level: the European Union.

## Sub lesson 1 — What the European Union is

The European Union is a union of European states that pool work on matters they handle better together.

It grew from cooperation after war into a lasting common framework of law and institutions.

## Sub lesson 2 — Greece in the Union

Greece is a member state: it takes part in the Union's decisions and applies its common rules.

Membership brings both a voice in common decisions and duties under them.

## Sub lesson 3 — The citizen of the Union

A Greek citizen is also a citizen of the European Union, with rights that reach across its member states.

Europe is therefore part of a Greek citizen's own political home, not a foreign extra.
