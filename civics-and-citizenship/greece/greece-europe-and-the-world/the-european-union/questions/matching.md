# The European Union — matching

Match each word with its meaning.

**1.** What the European Union is — a)

**2.** Greece in the Union — b)

**3.** The citizen of the Union — c)


Meanings:
a) The European Union is a union of European states that pool work on matters they handle better together.
b) Greece is a member state: it takes part in the Union's decisions and applies its common rules.
c) A Greek citizen is also a citizen of the European Union, with rights that reach across its member states.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b; 3. c
