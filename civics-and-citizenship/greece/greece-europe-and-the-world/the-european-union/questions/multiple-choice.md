# The European Union — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) the European Union is a union of European states that work together on common matters, and a Greek citizen is also a citizen of the Union  b) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary citizens  c) that citizens need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and Greece has no way of its own
**Answer: a) the European Union is a union of European states that work together on common matters, and a Greek citizen is also a citizen of the Union** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Greece in the Union” is right?
a) Greece is a member state: it takes part in the Union's decisions and applies its common rules.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no citizen ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Greece's book
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
