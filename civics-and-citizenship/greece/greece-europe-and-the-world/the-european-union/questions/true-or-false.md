# The European Union — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of an ordinary citizen in Greece.
**Answer: False** — The european union is a union of european states that work together on common matters, and a greek citizen is also a citizen of the union.

**2.** The European Union is a union of European states that pool work on matters they handle better together.
**Answer: True**
