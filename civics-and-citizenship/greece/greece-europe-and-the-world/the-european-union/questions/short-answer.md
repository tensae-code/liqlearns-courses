# The European Union — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** What the European Union is; Greece in the Union; The citizen of the Union.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a citizen of Greece?
**Answer:** Because the European Union is a union of European states that work together on common matters, and a Greek citizen is also a citizen of the Union — and a citizen who understands it can take their real place in that shared life.
