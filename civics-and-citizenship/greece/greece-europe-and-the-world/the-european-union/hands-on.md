# Hands-on — The European Union

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Find Greece on a map of Europe. Write down one thing Greece decides alone and one thing European countries decide together — trade, travel, or clean seas. This lesson names that shared level: the European Union.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the European Union is a union of European states that work together on common matters, and a Greek citizen is also a citizen of the Union. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
