# Lesson plan — The European Union

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Greece · **Title:** Greece, Europe and the World · **Lesson:** The European Union
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — Greece, Europe and the World (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the European Union is a union of European states that work together on common matters, and a Greek citizen is also a citizen of the Union.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — Greece, Europe and the World — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **What the European Union is** — The European Union is a union of European states that pool work on matters they handle better together.
2. **Greece in the Union** — Greece is a member state: it takes part in the Union's decisions and applies its common rules.
3. **The citizen of the Union** — A Greek citizen is also a citizen of the European Union, with rights that reach across its member states.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks joining the EU ended the Greek state → Greece remains a state with its own constitution and government; the Union is a common level its member states built and work through together.
- Go back to the one thing: the European Union is a union of European states that work together on common matters, and a Greek citizen is also a citizen of the Union. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
