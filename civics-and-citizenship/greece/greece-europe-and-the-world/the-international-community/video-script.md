# Video script — The International Community

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
States live together in an international community, joined by organisations, agreements and common rules, and greece takes its place in that community.

No state lives in isolation: the international community is the whole of states in relation with one another.

Relations run through diplomacy, agreements and everyday exchange.

States build international organisations to work on common matters — peace, health, trade, and the protection of rights.

Through them, small and large states can act together on what none can settle alone.

Greece takes part in the international community as a member of its organisations and a party to its agreements.

The citizen's world therefore reaches from the municipality to the whole community of states.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
