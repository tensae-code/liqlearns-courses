# The International Community — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of an ordinary citizen in Greece.
**Answer: False** — States live together in an international community, joined by organisations, agreements and common rules, and greece takes its place in that community.

**2.** No state lives in isolation: the international community is the whole of states in relation with one another.
**Answer: True**
