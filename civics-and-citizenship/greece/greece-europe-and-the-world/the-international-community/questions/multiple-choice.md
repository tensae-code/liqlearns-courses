# The International Community — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) states live together in an international community, joined by organisations, agreements and common rules, and Greece takes its place in that community  b) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary citizens  c) that citizens need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and Greece has no way of its own
**Answer: a) states live together in an international community, joined by organisations, agreements and common rules, and Greece takes its place in that community** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “International organisations” is right?
a) States build international organisations to work on common matters — peace, health, trade, and the protection of rights.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no citizen ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Greece's book
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
