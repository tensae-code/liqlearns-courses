# The International Community — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** States living together; International organisations; Greece's place.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a citizen of Greece?
**Answer:** Because states live together in an international community, joined by organisations, agreements and common rules, and Greece takes its place in that community — and a citizen who understands it can take their real place in that shared life.
