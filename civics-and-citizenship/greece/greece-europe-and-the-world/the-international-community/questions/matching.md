# The International Community — matching

Match each word with its meaning.

**1.** States living together — a)

**2.** International organisations — b)

**3.** Greece's place — c)


Meanings:
a) No state lives in isolation: the international community is the whole of states in relation with one another.
b) States build international organisations to work on common matters — peace, health, trade, and the protection of rights.
c) Greece takes part in the international community as a member of its organisations and a party to its agreements.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b; 3. c
