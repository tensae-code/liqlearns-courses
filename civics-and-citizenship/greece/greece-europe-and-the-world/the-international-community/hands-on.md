# Hands-on — The International Community

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name one problem no country can solve alone — a warming sea, a pandemic, a war's refugees. This lesson looks outward: how states live together and work together beyond their borders.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: states live together in an international community, joined by organisations, agreements and common rules, and Greece takes its place in that community. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
