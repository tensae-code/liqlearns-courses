# Civics and Citizenship — Greece course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

Civics belongs to the country: this course is Greece's own —
built from Greece's Social and Political Education student book
for the Gymnasium (Greek, 2019 edition (V3), COMPLETE for the
civics parts, combined track) and its Πολιτική Παιδεία
(Political Education) student book for the Lykeio (Greek, 2014
edition (V5), COMPLETE). Inside the course the organisation is
by **levels, not grades**: the titles below ARE the levels'
titles, in path order — what the learner proves, step by step.
The grades the books come from live only in each lesson's
sources.

The path's own logic is Greece's: first the individual meets
the state — its forms, its Constitution, its elections, its
functions, its administration, and the citizen's rights with
their obligations — then the circle widens to the European
Union and the international community; then the Lykeio course
rebuilds the whole field as one system — society, state and
economy together, Greek society's institutions and values, the
political community from Municipality to European Union — and
places at its centre the free, responsible and active citizen.
That ladder IS the path order (recommended, never forced — a
learner can jump in higher; the "start here instead" hints
point down this ladder).

## The titles

**Level 1 — the individual and the state** (Book 1, ΜΕΡΟΣ Β΄,
Ενότητα II):

- `the-individual-and-the-state/` — Level 1 (Ch. 7–12). The
  Individual and the State (what the state is and the citizen's
  relation to it) · Regimes and the Constitution (forms of
  government and the highest law) · Elections, Parties and
  Media (choosing rulers, organising choice, informing — and
  influencing — citizens) · Functions of the State (making,
  carrying out and judging under laws) · Administration (the
  machinery citizens actually meet) · Rights and Obligations
  (what the citizen may claim and what the citizen owes).

**Level 2 — Greece, Europe and the world** (Book 1, Ενότητες
III–IV):

- `greece-europe-and-the-world/` — Level 2 (Ch. 13–14). The
  European Union (the union Greece belongs to, and EU
  citizenship) · The International Community (states living and
  working together, and Greece's place among them).

**Level 3 — society, the state and the economy, and the free,
responsible and active citizen** (Book 2, Πολιτική Παιδεία,
grouped by the book's own themes; chapter numbers in each
lesson's sources):

- `society-state-and-economy/` — Level 3, first title
  (Ch. 1, 2, 4, 10, 11). Society, the State and the Economy
  (the human being as social, economic and political being;
  the polis, the citizen, the political art) · Society — From
  Agrarian to Information (society's changing forms) · The
  Economy (needs, goods, the consumer, enterprises) ·
  Entrepreneurship and Innovation (the entrepreneur,
  competitiveness, outward orientation) · Money and Banks
  (from barter to the money economy and its modern forms).
- `institutions-and-values-of-greek-society/` — Level 3,
  second title (Ch. 3). Institutions and Values of Greek
  Society (the Greek Orthodox tradition, the family as the
  cell of individual and collective life, education, freedom).
- `the-political-community/` — Level 3, third title
  (Ch. 5, 6, 12, 13). Municipality, Region, State and the
  European Union (the political community's ladder) ·
  Socialisation and Political Socialisation (how a member and
  a citizen are formed; family, school and education) ·
  Migration and Refugees (categories, causes, consequences;
  refugees and the Schengen agreement) · Social Problems
  (defining, causing, and tackling them; poverty and
  unemployment).
- `the-free-responsible-and-active-citizen/` — Level 3,
  fourth title (Ch. 7, 8, 9). The Free, Responsible and Active
  Citizen (the citizen's axiom, practised through
  environmental, consumer and health education) ·
  Decision-Making (individual and collective decisions,
  criteria, values and rules, the collective interest) ·
  Sustainable Development (the natural environment,
  development models, saving resources, renewable energy).
