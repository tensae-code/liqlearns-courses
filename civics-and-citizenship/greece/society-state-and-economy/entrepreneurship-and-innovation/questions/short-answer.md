# Entrepreneurship and Innovation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** The entrepreneur and the enterprise; Innovation; Competitiveness and outward orientation.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a citizen of Greece?
**Answer:** Because entrepreneurship turns an idea into an enterprise, and innovation — doing things in new and better ways — makes enterprises competitive and outward-looking — and a citizen who understands it can take their real place in that shared life.
