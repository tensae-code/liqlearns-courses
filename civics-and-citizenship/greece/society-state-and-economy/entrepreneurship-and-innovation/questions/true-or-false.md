# Entrepreneurship and Innovation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of an ordinary citizen in Greece.
**Answer: False** — Entrepreneurship turns an idea into an enterprise, and innovation — doing things in new and better ways — makes enterprises competitive and outward-looking.

**2.** The entrepreneur is the person who organises an enterprise — taking an idea, gathering resources, and carrying the risk of making it real.
**Answer: True**
