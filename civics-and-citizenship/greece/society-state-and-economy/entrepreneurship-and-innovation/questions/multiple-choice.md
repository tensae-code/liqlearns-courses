# Entrepreneurship and Innovation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) entrepreneurship turns an idea into an enterprise, and innovation — doing things in new and better ways — makes enterprises competitive and outward-looking  b) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary citizens  c) that citizens need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and Greece has no way of its own
**Answer: a) entrepreneurship turns an idea into an enterprise, and innovation — doing things in new and better ways — makes enterprises competitive and outward-looking** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Innovation” is right?
a) Innovation is doing things in new ways — a new product, a new method, a new market — not only inventing, but improving.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no citizen ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Greece's book
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
