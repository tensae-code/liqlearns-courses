# Entrepreneurship and Innovation — matching

Match each word with its meaning.

**1.** The entrepreneur and the enterprise — a)

**2.** Innovation — b)

**3.** Competitiveness and outward orientation — c)


Meanings:
a) The entrepreneur is the person who organises an enterprise — taking an idea, gathering resources, and carrying the risk of making it real.
b) Innovation is doing things in new ways — a new product, a new method, a new market — not only inventing, but improving.
c) An enterprise that innovates can compete — at home and beyond its borders; outward orientation means looking to wider markets, not only the street it stands on.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b; 3. c
