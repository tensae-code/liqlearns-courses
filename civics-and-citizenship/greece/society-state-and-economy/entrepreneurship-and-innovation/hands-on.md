# Hands-on — Entrepreneurship and Innovation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Think of a small business near you. Write down: what need it serves, and one new thing it could do better than its rivals. This lesson names the two forces in your notes: entrepreneurship and innovation.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: entrepreneurship turns an idea into an enterprise, and innovation — doing things in new and better ways — makes enterprises competitive and outward-looking. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
