# Lesson plan — Entrepreneurship and Innovation

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Greece · **Title:** Society, the State and the Economy · **Lesson:** Entrepreneurship and Innovation
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Society, the State and the Economy, and the Free, Responsible and Active Citizen (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** entrepreneurship turns an idea into an enterprise, and innovation — doing things in new and better ways — makes enterprises competitive and outward-looking.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — Society, the State and the Economy, and the Free, Responsible and Active Citizen — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The entrepreneur and the enterprise** — The entrepreneur is the person who organises an enterprise — taking an idea, gathering resources, and carrying the risk of making it real.
2. **Innovation** — Innovation is doing things in new ways — a new product, a new method, a new market — not only inventing, but improving.
3. **Competitiveness and outward orientation** — An enterprise that innovates can compete — at home and beyond its borders; outward orientation means looking to wider markets, not only the street it stands on.
