# Hands-on — Society, the State and the Economy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Watch yourself for one morning: you live with others, you use and exchange things, and you follow shared rules. This lesson shows those are one life seen three ways — society, economy and the state — starting from Aristotle's polis.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the human being is at once a social, an economic and a political being — the maker of the polis, a citizen of it, and a practitioner of the political art. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
