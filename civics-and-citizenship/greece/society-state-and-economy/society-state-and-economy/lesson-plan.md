## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The human being: social, economic, political** — A human being lives with others (social), provides for life's needs with others (economic), and shares in ruling and being ruled (political).
2. **The polis and the citizen** — From Aristotle, the book takes the polis: the self-governing community, and the citizen as the one who shares in its common life.
3. **The political art and the politician** — Politics is an art practised for the common good, and the politician is judged by its qualities — serving the whole, not the self.
