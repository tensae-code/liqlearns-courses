# Society, the State and the Economy — matching

Match each word with its meaning.

**1.** The human being: social, economic, political — a)

**2.** The polis and the citizen — b)

**3.** The political art and the politician — c)


Meanings:
a) A human being lives with others (social), provides for life's needs with others (economic), and shares in ruling and being ruled (political).
b) From Aristotle, the book takes the polis: the self-governing community, and the citizen as the one who shares in its common life.
c) Politics is an art practised for the common good, and the politician is judged by its qualities — serving the whole, not the self.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b; 3. c
