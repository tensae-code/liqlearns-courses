# Society, the State and the Economy — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of an ordinary citizen in Greece.
**Answer: False** — The human being is at once a social, an economic and a political being — the maker of the polis, a citizen of it, and a practitioner of the political art.

**2.** A human being lives with others (social), provides for life's needs with others (economic), and shares in ruling and being ruled (political).
**Answer: True**
