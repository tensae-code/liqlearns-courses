# Society, the State and the Economy — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** The human being: social, economic, political; The polis and the citizen; The political art and the politician.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a citizen of Greece?
**Answer:** Because the human being is at once a social, an economic and a political being — the maker of the polis, a citizen of it, and a practitioner of the political art — and a citizen who understands it can take their real place in that shared life.
