# Society, the State and the Economy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) the human being is at once a social, an economic and a political being — the maker of the polis, a citizen of it, and a practitioner of the political art  b) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary citizens  c) that citizens need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and Greece has no way of its own
**Answer: a) the human being is at once a social, an economic and a political being — the maker of the polis, a citizen of it, and a practitioner of the political art** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “The polis and the citizen” is right?
a) From Aristotle, the book takes the polis: the self-governing community, and the citizen as the one who shares in its common life.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no citizen ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Greece's book
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
