# Lesson plan — Society — From Agrarian to Information

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Greece · **Title:** Society, the State and the Economy · **Lesson:** Society — From Agrarian to Information
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Society, the State and the Economy, and the Free, Responsible and Active Citizen (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** societies change their form across history — from agrarian society to the information and knowledge society — and each form shapes how people live, work and learn.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — Society, the State and the Economy, and the Free, Responsible and Active Citizen — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Agrarian society** — In agrarian society, most people work the land, live in small communities, and knowledge travels slowly, face to face.
2. **The road of change** — Society's form changes as production and communication change — industry, cities and mass media each remake work and community.
3. **The information and knowledge society** — In the information and knowledge society, information moves instantly and knowledge itself is a main resource and a main form of work.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
