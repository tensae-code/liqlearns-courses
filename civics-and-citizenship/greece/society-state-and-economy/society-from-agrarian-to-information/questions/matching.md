# Society — From Agrarian to Information — matching

Match each word with its meaning.

**1.** Agrarian society — a)

**2.** The road of change — b)

**3.** The information and knowledge society — c)


Meanings:
a) In agrarian society, most people work the land, live in small communities, and knowledge travels slowly, face to face.
b) Society's form changes as production and communication change — industry, cities and mass media each remake work and community.
c) In the information and knowledge society, information moves instantly and knowledge itself is a main resource and a main form of work.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b; 3. c
