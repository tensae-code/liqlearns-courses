# Society — From Agrarian to Information — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of an ordinary citizen in Greece.
**Answer: False** — Societies change their form across history — from agrarian society to the information and knowledge society — and each form shapes how people live, work and learn.

**2.** In agrarian society, most people work the land, live in small communities, and knowledge travels slowly, face to face.
**Answer: True**
