# Society — From Agrarian to Information — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Agrarian society; The road of change; The information and knowledge society.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a citizen of Greece?
**Answer:** Because societies change their form across history — from agrarian society to the information and knowledge society — and each form shapes how people live, work and learn — and a citizen who understands it can take their real place in that shared life.
