# Hands-on — Society — From Agrarian to Information

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Ask an older person in your family how news, work and shopping were done when they were young. Write down what has changed. This lesson gives that change its map: the forms society takes across time.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: societies change their form across history — from agrarian society to the information and knowledge society — and each form shapes how people live, work and learn. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
