# Video script — Society — From Agrarian to Information

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Societies change their form across history — from agrarian society to the information and knowledge society — and each form shapes how people live, work and learn.

In agrarian society, most people work the land, live in small communities, and knowledge travels slowly, face to face.

Land, season and family set the shape of life.

Society's form changes as production and communication change — industry, cities and mass media each remake work and community.

Change in the tools of life becomes change in the life itself.

In the information and knowledge society, information moves instantly and knowledge itself is a main resource and a main form of work.

Learning, in such a society, never finishes — which is why this course treats the citizen as a learner for life.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
