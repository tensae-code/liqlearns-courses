# Money and Banks — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** From barter to the money economy; Properties and functions of money; Modern forms and banks.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a citizen of Greece?
**Answer:** Because money grew out of barter as the common measure and means of exchange, it works because of its properties and functions, and it takes modern forms held and moved through banks — and a citizen who understands it can take their real place in that shared life.
