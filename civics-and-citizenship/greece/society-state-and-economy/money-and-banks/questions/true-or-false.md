# Money and Banks — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of an ordinary citizen in Greece.
**Answer: False** — Money grew out of barter as the common measure and means of exchange, it works because of its properties and functions, and it takes modern forms held and moved through banks.

**2.** Barter — goods for goods — fails when the two sides do not want what each other has; the money economy solves that with a common means of exchange.
**Answer: True**
