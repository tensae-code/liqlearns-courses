# Money and Banks — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) money grew out of barter as the common measure and means of exchange, it works because of its properties and functions, and it takes modern forms held and moved through banks  b) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary citizens  c) that citizens need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and Greece has no way of its own
**Answer: a) money grew out of barter as the common measure and means of exchange, it works because of its properties and functions, and it takes modern forms held and moved through banks** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Properties and functions of money” is right?
a) Money works because it is accepted by all, measurable, divisible and lasting; its functions are to measure value, to serve as a means of exchange, and to store value.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no citizen ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Greece's book
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
