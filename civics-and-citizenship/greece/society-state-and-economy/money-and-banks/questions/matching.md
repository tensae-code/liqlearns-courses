# Money and Banks — matching

Match each word with its meaning.

**1.** From barter to the money economy — a)

**2.** Properties and functions of money — b)

**3.** Modern forms and banks — c)


Meanings:
a) Barter — goods for goods — fails when the two sides do not want what each other has; the money economy solves that with a common means of exchange.
b) Money works because it is accepted by all, measurable, divisible and lasting; its functions are to measure value, to serve as a means of exchange, and to store value.
c) Modern money takes forms beyond coins and notes — deposits and electronic payment — and banks hold, move and lend it.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b; 3. c
