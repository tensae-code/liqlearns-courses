## Sub lesson 1 — From barter to the money economy
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Barter — goods for goods — fails when the two sides do not want what each other has; the money economy solves that with a common means of exchange. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — Properties and functions of money
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Money works because it is accepted by all, measurable, divisible and lasting; its functions are to measure value, to serve as a means of exchange, and to store value. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
