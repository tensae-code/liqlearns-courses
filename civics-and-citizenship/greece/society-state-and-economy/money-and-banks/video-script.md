# Video script — Money and Banks

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Money grew out of barter as the common measure and means of exchange, it works because of its properties and functions, and it takes modern forms held and moved through banks.

Barter — goods for goods — fails when the two sides do not want what each other has; the money economy solves that with a common means of exchange.

Money lets any good be exchanged for any other, through one shared middle term.

Money works because it is accepted by all, measurable, divisible and lasting; its functions are to measure value, to serve as a means of exchange, and to store value.

Test any would-be money against those properties and the failures show at once.

Modern money takes forms beyond coins and notes — deposits and electronic payment — and banks hold, move and lend it.

The bank is therefore part of every citizen's economic life, and understanding it is part of citizenship.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
