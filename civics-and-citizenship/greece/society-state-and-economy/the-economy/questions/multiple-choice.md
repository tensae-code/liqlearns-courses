# The Economy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) the economy answers one main problem — human needs are many, the goods to meet them are limited — through consumers choosing and enterprises producing  b) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary citizens  c) that citizens need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and Greece has no way of its own
**Answer: a) the economy answers one main problem — human needs are many, the goods to meet them are limited — through consumers choosing and enterprises producing** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Needs, goods and the consumer” is right?
a) Needs call for goods to satisfy them, and the consumer is the person whose choices among goods guide much of economic life.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no citizen ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Greece's book
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
