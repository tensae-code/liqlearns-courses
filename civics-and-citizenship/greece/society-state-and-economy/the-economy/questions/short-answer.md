# The Economy — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** The main economic problem; Needs, goods and the consumer; Enterprises.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a citizen of Greece?
**Answer:** Because the economy answers one main problem — human needs are many, the goods to meet them are limited — through consumers choosing and enterprises producing — and a citizen who understands it can take their real place in that shared life.
