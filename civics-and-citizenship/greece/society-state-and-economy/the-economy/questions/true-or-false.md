# The Economy — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of an ordinary citizen in Greece.
**Answer: False** — The economy answers one main problem — human needs are many, the goods to meet them are limited — through consumers choosing and enterprises producing.

**2.** Human needs are many and growing, while the goods and resources to meet them are limited — that gap is the main economic problem.
**Answer: True**
