# The Economy — matching

Match each word with its meaning.

**1.** The main economic problem — a)

**2.** Needs, goods and the consumer — b)

**3.** Enterprises — c)


Meanings:
a) Human needs are many and growing, while the goods and resources to meet them are limited — that gap is the main economic problem.
b) Needs call for goods to satisfy them, and the consumer is the person whose choices among goods guide much of economic life.
c) Enterprises organise production: they combine resources to make the goods and services consumers need.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b; 3. c
