# The Economy

## Do it first

List everything your household needed this week, then what it cost. This lesson names the gap between the two lists: the main economic problem, and the actors who work on it — consumers and enterprises.

## Sub lesson 1 — The main economic problem

Human needs are many and growing, while the goods and resources to meet them are limited — that gap is the main economic problem.

Every economic choice is an answer to it: what to produce, how, and for whom.

## Sub lesson 2 — Needs, goods and the consumer

Needs call for goods to satisfy them, and the consumer is the person whose choices among goods guide much of economic life.

The book names the consumer model plainly so citizens can see their own part in it.

## Sub lesson 3 — Enterprises

Enterprises organise production: they combine resources to make the goods and services consumers need.

A healthy economy needs enterprises that produce well and consumers who choose with open eyes.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the economy answers one main problem — human needs are many, the goods to meet them are limited — through consumers choosing and enterprises producing. Give one real example from your own life or from the Greece you live in, and name the lesson's key words: needs, goods, consumer, enterprise. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
