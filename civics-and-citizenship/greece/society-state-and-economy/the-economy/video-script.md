# Video script — The Economy

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The economy answers one main problem — human needs are many, the goods to meet them are limited — through consumers choosing and enterprises producing.

Human needs are many and growing, while the goods and resources to meet them are limited — that gap is the main economic problem.

Every economic choice is an answer to it: what to produce, how, and for whom.

Needs call for goods to satisfy them, and the consumer is the person whose choices among goods guide much of economic life.

The book names the consumer model plainly so citizens can see their own part in it.

Enterprises organise production: they combine resources to make the goods and services consumers need.

A healthy economy needs enterprises that produce well and consumers who choose with open eyes.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
