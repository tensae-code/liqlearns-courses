# Hands-on — The Economy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
List everything your household needed this week, then what it cost. This lesson names the gap between the two lists: the main economic problem, and the actors who work on it — consumers and enterprises.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the economy answers one main problem — human needs are many, the goods to meet them are limited — through consumers choosing and enterprises producing. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
