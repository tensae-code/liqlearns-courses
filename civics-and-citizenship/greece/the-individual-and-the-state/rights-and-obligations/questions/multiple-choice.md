# Rights and Obligations — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) citizens hold rights the state must respect, and obligations they owe in return — the two travel together  b) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary citizens  c) that citizens need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and Greece has no way of its own
**Answer: a) citizens hold rights the state must respect, and obligations they owe in return — the two travel together** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Obligations of the citizen” is right?
a) Citizens also owe obligations: to obey the law, respect the rights of others, defend the country and contribute to common costs.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no citizen ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Greece's book
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
