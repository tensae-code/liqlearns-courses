# Rights and Obligations — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Rights of the citizen; Obligations of the citizen; Rights and obligations together.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a citizen of Greece?
**Answer:** Because citizens hold rights the state must respect, and obligations they owe in return — the two travel together — and a citizen who understands it can take their real place in that shared life.
