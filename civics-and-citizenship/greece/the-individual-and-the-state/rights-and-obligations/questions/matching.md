# Rights and Obligations — matching

Match each word with its meaning.

**1.** Rights of the citizen — a)

**2.** Obligations of the citizen — b)

**3.** Rights and obligations together — c)


Meanings:
a) Citizens hold rights — to life, freedom, education, expression and more — that the state must respect and protect.
b) Citizens also owe obligations: to obey the law, respect the rights of others, defend the country and contribute to common costs.
c) A healthy state keeps the pair in balance: rights claimed with responsibility, obligations demanded with respect for rights.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b; 3. c
