# Rights and Obligations — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of an ordinary citizen in Greece.
**Answer: False** — Citizens hold rights the state must respect, and obligations they owe in return — the two travel together.

**2.** Citizens hold rights — to life, freedom, education, expression and more — that the state must respect and protect.
**Answer: True**
