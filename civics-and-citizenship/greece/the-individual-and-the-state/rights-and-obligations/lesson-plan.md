## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Rights of the citizen** — Citizens hold rights — to life, freedom, education, expression and more — that the state must respect and protect.
2. **Obligations of the citizen** — Citizens also owe obligations: to obey the law, respect the rights of others, defend the country and contribute to common costs.
3. **Rights and obligations together** — A healthy state keeps the pair in balance: rights claimed with responsibility, obligations demanded with respect for rights.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks citizenship is rights only → rights without obligations cannot hold: my right is protected when others do their duty, and the same is true in reverse.
- Go back to the one thing: citizens hold rights the state must respect, and obligations they owe in return — the two travel together. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
