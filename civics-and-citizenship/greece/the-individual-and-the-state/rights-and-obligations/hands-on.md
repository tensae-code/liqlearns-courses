# Hands-on — Rights and Obligations

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Make two lists about yourself as a citizen: what you may claim (to learn, to speak, to be safe) and what you owe (to obey the law, to respect others, to contribute). This lesson shows the two lists belong to one citizenship.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: citizens hold rights the state must respect, and obligations they owe in return — the two travel together. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
