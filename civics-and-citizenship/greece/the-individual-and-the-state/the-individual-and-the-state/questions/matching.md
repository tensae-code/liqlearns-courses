# The Individual and the State — matching

Match each word with its meaning.

**1.** What the state is — a)

**2.** The citizen's relation to the state — b)

**3.** Why the relation matters — c)


Meanings:
a) The state is an organised political community: people living together under common rules and common institutions.
b) A citizen is a member of the state, not a stranger to it: the state protects the citizen, and the citizen takes part in and supports the common life.
c) When citizens understand the state, they can use its institutions, judge them, and improve them instead of standing outside them.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b; 3. c
