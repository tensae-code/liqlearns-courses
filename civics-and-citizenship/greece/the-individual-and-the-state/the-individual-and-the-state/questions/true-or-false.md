# The Individual and the State — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of an ordinary citizen in Greece.
**Answer: False** — A state (a politeia) is an organised political community, and a citizen belongs to it with a real relation to it — not as a bystander, but as a member.

**2.** The state is an organised political community: people living together under common rules and common institutions.
**Answer: True**
