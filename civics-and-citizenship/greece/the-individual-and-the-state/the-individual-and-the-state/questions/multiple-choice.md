# The Individual and the State — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) a state (a Politeia) is an organised political community, and a citizen belongs to it with a real relation to it — not as a bystander, but as a member  b) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary citizens  c) that citizens need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and Greece has no way of its own
**Answer: a) a state (a Politeia) is an organised political community, and a citizen belongs to it with a real relation to it — not as a bystander, but as a member** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “The citizen's relation to the state” is right?
a) A citizen is a member of the state, not a stranger to it: the state protects the citizen, and the citizen takes part in and supports the common life.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no citizen ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Greece's book
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
