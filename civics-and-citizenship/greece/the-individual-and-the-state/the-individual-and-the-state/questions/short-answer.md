# The Individual and the State — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** What the state is; The citizen's relation to the state; Why the relation matters.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a citizen of Greece?
**Answer:** Because a state (a Politeia) is an organised political community, and a citizen belongs to it with a real relation to it — not as a bystander, but as a member — and a citizen who understands it can take their real place in that shared life.
