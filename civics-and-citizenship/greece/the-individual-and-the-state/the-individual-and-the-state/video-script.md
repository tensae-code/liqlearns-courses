# Video script — The Individual and the State

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A state (a politeia) is an organised political community, and a citizen belongs to it with a real relation to it — not as a bystander, but as a member.

The state is an organised political community: people living together under common rules and common institutions.

It is not just a building or an office — it is the organised form of a people's shared life.

A citizen is a member of the state, not a stranger to it: the state protects the citizen, and the citizen takes part in and supports the common life.

Belonging runs both ways — protection and services one way, participation and duty the other.

When citizens understand the state, they can use its institutions, judge them, and improve them instead of standing outside them.

That understanding is the starting point for every lesson that follows in this level.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
