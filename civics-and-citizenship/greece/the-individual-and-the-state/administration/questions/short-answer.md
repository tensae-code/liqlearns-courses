# Administration — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** What administration is; How administration should work; The citizen and the office.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a citizen of Greece?
**Answer:** Because public administration is the organised machinery that carries the state's decisions into everyday life — the offices, services and officials citizens actually meet — and a citizen who understands it can take their real place in that shared life.
