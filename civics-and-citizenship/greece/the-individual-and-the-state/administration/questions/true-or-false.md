# Administration — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of an ordinary citizen in Greece.
**Answer: False** — Public administration is the organised machinery that carries the state's decisions into everyday life — the offices, services and officials citizens actually meet.

**2.** Administration is the state's organised services — ministries, local offices and public bodies — working under the law.
**Answer: True**
