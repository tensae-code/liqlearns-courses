# Administration — matching

Match each word with its meaning.

**1.** What administration is — a)

**2.** How administration should work — b)

**3.** The citizen and the office — c)


Meanings:
a) Administration is the state's organised services — ministries, local offices and public bodies — working under the law.
b) Administration must serve citizens lawfully and equally, following set procedures rather than favour or whim.
c) Most citizens meet the state through administration, so its fairness is where the state is judged daily.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b; 3. c
