# Administration — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) public administration is the organised machinery that carries the state's decisions into everyday life — the offices, services and officials citizens actually meet  b) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary citizens  c) that citizens need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and Greece has no way of its own
**Answer: a) public administration is the organised machinery that carries the state's decisions into everyday life — the offices, services and officials citizens actually meet** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “How administration should work” is right?
a) Administration must serve citizens lawfully and equally, following set procedures rather than favour or whim.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no citizen ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Greece's book
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
