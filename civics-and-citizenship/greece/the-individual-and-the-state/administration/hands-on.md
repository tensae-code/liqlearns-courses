# Hands-on — Administration

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Think of the last official paper your family needed — a certificate, a permit, an enrolment. Write down which office gave it and what the official did. This lesson names that machinery: the administration.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: public administration is the organised machinery that carries the state's decisions into everyday life — the offices, services and officials citizens actually meet. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
