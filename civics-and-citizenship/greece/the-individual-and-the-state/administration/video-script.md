# Video script — Administration

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Public administration is the organised machinery that carries the state's decisions into everyday life — the offices, services and officials citizens actually meet.

Administration is the state's organised services — ministries, local offices and public bodies — working under the law.

It turns general decisions into individual acts: a licence granted, a school staffed, a benefit paid.

Administration must serve citizens lawfully and equally, following set procedures rather than favour or whim.

A citizen who knows the procedure can ask for their case to be handled by the rule, not by connection.

Most citizens meet the state through administration, so its fairness is where the state is judged daily.

Polite, lawful treatment at the counter is the state keeping its promise in person.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
