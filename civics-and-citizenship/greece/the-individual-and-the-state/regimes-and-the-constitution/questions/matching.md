# Regimes and the Constitution — matching

Match each word with its meaning.

**1.** Forms of government — a)

**2.** The Constitution — b)

**3.** Constitution and citizen — c)


Meanings:
a) A politeuma is the form a state's government takes — who holds power, how they get it, and how they may use it.
b) The Constitution is the state's highest law: it organises the institutions, divides power, and protects the citizen's basic rights.
c) Because the Constitution limits power, it is the citizen's shield as well as the state's plan.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b; 3. c
