# Regimes and the Constitution — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of an ordinary citizen in Greece.
**Answer: False** — Countries are ruled under different forms of government (politeumata), and a constitution is the highest set of rules that organises the state and limits its power.

**2.** A politeuma is the form a state's government takes — who holds power, how they get it, and how they may use it.
**Answer: True**
