# Regimes and the Constitution — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) countries are ruled under different forms of government (politeumata), and a constitution is the highest set of rules that organises the state and limits its power  b) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary citizens  c) that citizens need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and Greece has no way of its own
**Answer: a) countries are ruled under different forms of government (politeumata), and a constitution is the highest set of rules that organises the state and limits its power** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “The Constitution” is right?
a) The Constitution is the state's highest law: it organises the institutions, divides power, and protects the citizen's basic rights.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no citizen ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Greece's book
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
