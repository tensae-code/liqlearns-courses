# Functions of the State — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** The law-making function; The executive function; The judging function.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a citizen of Greece?
**Answer:** Because the state does its work through distinct functions — making laws, carrying them out, and judging disputes under them — and a citizen who understands it can take their real place in that shared life.
