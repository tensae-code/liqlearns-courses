# Functions of the State — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) the state does its work through distinct functions — making laws, carrying them out, and judging disputes under them  b) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary citizens  c) that citizens need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and Greece has no way of its own
**Answer: a) the state does its work through distinct functions — making laws, carrying them out, and judging disputes under them** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “The executive function” is right?
a) A second function carries the laws out: governing day to day, running services, and keeping the country safe.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no citizen ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Greece's book
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
