# Lesson plan — Elections, Parties and Media

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Greece · **Title:** The Individual and the State · **Lesson:** Elections, Parties and Media
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — The Individual and the State (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** elections let citizens choose their rulers, political parties organise choices and programmes, and the mass media carry information — and influence — between citizens and power.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Elections** — Elections are how citizens choose who will govern and can remove them peacefully at the next vote.
2. **Political parties** — Parties gather people who share a programme for the country, present candidates, and organise the choice elections offer.
3. **The mass media** — The mass media (MME) inform citizens about public life — and also influence opinion by what they show and how they show it.
