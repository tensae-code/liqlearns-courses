# Elections, Parties and Media — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the parts of this lesson, in order.
**Answer:** Elections; Political parties; The mass media.

**2.** Why does this lesson matter to a citizen of Greece?
**Answer:** Because elections let citizens choose their rulers, political parties organise choices and programmes, and the mass media carry information — and influence — between citizens and power — and a citizen who understands it can take their real place in that shared life.
