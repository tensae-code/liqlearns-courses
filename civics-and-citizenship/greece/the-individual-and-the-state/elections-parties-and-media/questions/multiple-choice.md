# Elections, Parties and Media — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the one thing this lesson teaches?
a) elections let citizens choose their rulers, political parties organise choices and programmes, and the mass media carry information — and influence — between citizens and power  b) that this topic concerns only officials and never ordinary citizens  c) that citizens need know nothing about it  d) that it is the same in every country and Greece has no way of its own
**Answer: a) elections let citizens choose their rulers, political parties organise choices and programmes, and the mass media carry information — and influence — between citizens and power** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement about “Political parties” is right?
a) Parties gather people who share a programme for the country, present candidates, and organise the choice elections offer.  b) it has nothing to do with this lesson  c) it is a private matter no citizen ever meets  d) it was invented by LiqLearns, not taught in Greece's book
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
