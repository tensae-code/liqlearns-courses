# Elections, Parties and Media — matching

Match each word with its meaning.

**1.** Elections — a)

**2.** Political parties — b)

**3.** The mass media — c)


Meanings:
a) Elections are how citizens choose who will govern and can remove them peacefully at the next vote.
b) Parties gather people who share a programme for the country, present candidates, and organise the choice elections offer.
c) The mass media (MME) inform citizens about public life — and also influence opinion by what they show and how they show it.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b; 3. c
