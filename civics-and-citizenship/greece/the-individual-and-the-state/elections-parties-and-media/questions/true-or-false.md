# Elections, Parties and Media — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** This lesson's teaching has nothing to do with the life of an ordinary citizen in Greece.
**Answer: False** — Elections let citizens choose their rulers, political parties organise choices and programmes, and the mass media carry information — and influence — between citizens and power.

**2.** Elections are how citizens choose who will govern and can remove them peacefully at the next vote.
**Answer: True**
