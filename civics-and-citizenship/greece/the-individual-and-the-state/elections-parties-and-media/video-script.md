# Video script — Elections, Parties and Media

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Elections let citizens choose their rulers, political parties organise choices and programmes, and the mass media carry information — and influence — between citizens and power.

Elections are how citizens choose who will govern and can remove them peacefully at the next vote.

A real election offers a real choice, honestly counted.

Parties gather people who share a programme for the country, present candidates, and organise the choice elections offer.

Without parties, voters would face scattered individuals instead of clear alternatives.

The mass media (MME) inform citizens about public life — and also influence opinion by what they show and how they show it.

A careful citizen compares sources and separates reporting from persuasion.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
