# Video script — Science and Technical Progress

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Science and technical development change society, and the young generation's part is to learn, master and direct that change for the homeland.

The Grade 7 course closes with change itself: how scientific and technical development affects society.

The question is handed to the pupil's own generation: this change will be led by the young now in school.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
