# Environmental Citizenship — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The citizen owes the environment care: knowing renewable from non-renewable energy, how Syria produces its electricity, and acting before forests shrink and burn.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a child's own home, school or community in Syria.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Syria and lived there.
