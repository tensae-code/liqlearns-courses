# Video script — Environmental Citizenship

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The citizen owes the environment care: knowing renewable from non-renewable energy, how Syria produces its electricity, and acting before forests shrink and burn.

The final unit faces forward, and it opens with the environment.

Knowledge turns into duty in the lesson's closing case study: a forest that is shrinking and burning.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
