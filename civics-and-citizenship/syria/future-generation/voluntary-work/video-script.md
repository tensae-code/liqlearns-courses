# Video script — Voluntary Work

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Voluntary work is the citizen giving time freely — a planned campaign can change a neighbourhood and a school, and you can picture both before and after.

Volunteering is the future generation's giving practised now: time and work offered freely, in the pupil's own neighbourhood and school.

The book's own move is to picture both places after the campaign: the neighbourhood and the school as they would look if the pupils acted.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
