# Voluntary Work — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Voluntary work is the citizen giving time freely — a planned campaign can change a neighbourhood and a school, and you can picture both before and after.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Volunteering is the future generation's giving practised now: time and work offered freely, in the pupil's own neighbourhood and school.
