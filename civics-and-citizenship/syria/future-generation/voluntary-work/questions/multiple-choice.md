# Voluntary Work — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) Voluntary work is the citizen giving time freely — a planned campaign can change a neighbourhood and a school, and you can picture both before and after.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Volunteering is the future generation's giving practised now: time and work offered freely, in the pupil's own neighbourhood and school.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
