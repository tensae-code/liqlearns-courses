# Video script — Problems of the Young

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Young people face real problems, and a pupil's duty includes standing by a classmate who suffers one — the term's project takes up smoking and how to warn against it.

The forward-looking unit does not pretend the young are safe.

The lesson's centre is duty toward the other: what a pupil owes a classmate who suffers one of these problems.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
