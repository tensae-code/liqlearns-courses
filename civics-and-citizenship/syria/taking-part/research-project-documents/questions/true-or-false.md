# Research Project: Gathering Documents — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A research project gathers real documents with classmates and presents them — here, documents on violations of international law: occupation of others' land, displacement of inhabitants and settlements.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a child's own home, school or community in Syria.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Syria and lived there.
