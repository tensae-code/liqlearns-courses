# Research Project: Gathering Documents

## Do it first

With classmates, plan a document collection: choose the topic, list what documents would prove it, and decide where each document will come from and how you will present it.

## What the book teaches

The first term closes with work, not a test: a research project done with classmates. The class collects documents on violations of international law — occupation of others' land, displacement of inhabitants, and settlements — and presents them at school and on the school site.

## The lesson in full

The lesson inside the project is a civic skill: a claim is only as strong as its documents. Pupils learn to gather evidence, order it, and show it to others — the citizen's way of speaking about the homeland's cause with proof in hand.
