# Video script — Research Project: Gathering Documents

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A research project gathers real documents with classmates and presents them — here, documents on violations of international law: occupation of others' land, displacement of inhabitants and settlements.

The first term closes with work, not a test: a research project done with classmates.

The lesson inside the project is a civic skill: a claim is only as strong as its documents.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
