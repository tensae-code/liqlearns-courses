The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

This lesson is about one thing.
A wall magazine made with classmates — posters and pictures — can teach a whole school: here, the dangers of smoking and advice to stay away from it.

The second term, like the first, closes in work: a research project done with classmates.

Its subject is the danger studied in the unit: the dangers of smoking, with advice to stay away from it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
