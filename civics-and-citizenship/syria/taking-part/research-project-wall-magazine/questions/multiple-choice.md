# Research Project: A Wall Magazine — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) A wall magazine made with classmates — posters and pictures — can teach a whole school: here, the dangers of smoking and advice to stay away from it.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) The second term, like the first, closes in work: a research project done with classmates.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
