# Civics and Citizenship — Syria course — CURRICULUM (the path)

One course for Syria inside the Civics and Citizenship
folder, organised by **levels, not grades**: a learner
climbs when they have proved the level, not when a school
calendar says so. The school grades the books come from
(Grade 7 and Grade 10, first year secondary) appear only
in each lesson's sources (provenance), never as the
course's organisation. The path follows Syria's own
books' order: the Grade 7 book climbs from the pupil's
own self-esteem — family, friends, community — straight
up to the state (society's organisation, human rights,
the powers of the state, the homeland's protectors), then
deepens belonging into duty (loving, protecting and
giving to the homeland, keeping one's pledge), and
closes facing forward (environment, volunteering, the
problems of the young, science). The Grade 10 book then
climbs the same spine at an older pupil's depth: the
self again (decision-making, social awareness,
initiative, public opinion), the citizen's frame opened
out (political thought, the state's pillars, systems of
government, economic systems), the widening communities
(the Arab community, the international community, human
heritage, the adversary idea as Syria frames it), and
sustainable development closing in the pupil's own
project for change and public health. That ladder IS the
placements files' ladder sketches, reconciled into one:
the first file files the Grade 7 units at Levels 1–4;
the second file sketches the Grade 7 book as its Level 1
and the Grade 10 book as its Level 2 in its own unit
order — reconciled, the Grade 10 book follows the Grade
7 book whole at Levels 5–8.

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything
in this course comes from government textbooks — this
country's own التربية الوطنية (National Education)
student books (National Center for Curriculum
Development (NCCD), Syrian Arab Republic) — except the
**Tip boxes**: a tip in a box is from us, the people
making LiqLearns. Tips are ours; the lessons are the
books'. See ABOUT.md for the full book list and the
acceptance reckoning.

## The levels (the path, in order)

1. **Level 1 — Developing Myself** — I value myself; I
   love my family; I respect my friends; I connect with
   my community (`developing-myself/`). 4 lessons.
2. **Level 2 — Rights and Duties** — organising
   society's affairs; respecting and protecting human
   rights; the powers of the state; protectors of the
   homeland; and the first-term research project,
   gathering documents (`rights-and-duties/`,
   `taking-part/`). 5 lessons.
3. **Level 3 — Homeland and Citizen** — my homeland
   that I love; my homeland that I protect; springs of
   giving; keeping my word (`homeland-and-citizen/`).
   4 lessons.
4. **Level 4 — The Future Generation** — environmental
   citizenship; voluntary work; problems of the young;
   science and technical progress; and the second-term
   research project, a wall magazine
   (`future-generation/`, `taking-part/`). 5 lessons.
5. **Level 5 — Developing the Self** — decision-making;
   social awareness; initiative; public opinion
   (`developing-the-self/`). 4 lessons.
6. **Level 6 — Rights and Duties: the State and Its
   Systems** — political thought; the state and its
   pillars; systems of government; how economic systems
   developed (`rights-and-duties/`). 4 lessons.
7. **Level 7 — Homeland and Citizen: the Wider World**
   — the Arab community; the international community;
   human heritage; the Arab citizen and Zionist thought
   (`homeland-and-citizen/`). 4 lessons.
8. **Level 8 — Sustainable Development** — national
   policy and the environment; the problems of poverty;
   my project for change; public health
   (`sustainable-development/`). 4 lessons.

**Totals: 34 lessons, 34 placements stocked** (Level 1:
4 · Level 2: 5 · Level 3: 4 · Level 4: 5 · Level 5: 4 ·
Level 6: 4 · Level 7: 4 · Level 8: 4 — all from two
student books: *National Education* for Grade 7 (NCCD,
Arabic, no year printed, COMPLETE as filed) and
*National Education* for Grade 10, first year secondary
(Ministry of Education, NCCD developed-curriculum
edition, Arabic, 2021–2022, COMPLETE as filed); see
ABOUT.md).

*Standing note on the count: the two books' 34
placements coin 34 distinct lesson names — no name is
coined twice — so no dedupe merge arises. 34 placements,
34 lessons, every placement stocked exactly once.*
