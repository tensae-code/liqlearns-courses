# Video script — National Policy and the Environment

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The state sets a national policy toward the environment — protecting it by law and plan — and the citizen owes the environment his share of that protection.

The younger book taught environmental citizenship as the pupil's own duty.

Policy and duty meet in the middle.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
