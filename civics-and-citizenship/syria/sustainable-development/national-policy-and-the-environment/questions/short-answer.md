# National Policy and the Environment — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** The state sets a national policy toward the environment — protecting it by law and plan — and the citizen owes the environment his share of that protection.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** The younger book taught environmental citizenship as the pupil's own duty.
