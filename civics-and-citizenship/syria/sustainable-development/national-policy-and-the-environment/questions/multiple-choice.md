# National Policy and the Environment — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) The state sets a national policy toward the environment — protecting it by law and plan — and the citizen owes the environment his share of that protection.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) The younger book taught environmental citizenship as the pupil's own duty.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
