# The Problems of Poverty — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) Poverty is a community problem, not only a private one — the citizen must understand its problems to stand with his society against them.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Poverty is usually seen through one family's door.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
