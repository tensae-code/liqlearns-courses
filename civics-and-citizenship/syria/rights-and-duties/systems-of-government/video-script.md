# Video script — Systems of Government

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
States organise rule in different systems of government — how rule is arranged decides how citizens are governed and how they take part.

The pillars say what a state is; this lesson asks how it is ruled.

The lesson is comparative: the pupil meets more than his own country's arrangement, so his own system becomes visible as one choice among systems, with its own shape and reasons.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
