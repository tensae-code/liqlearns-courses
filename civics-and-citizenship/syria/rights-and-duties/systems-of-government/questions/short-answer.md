# Systems of Government — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** States organise rule in different systems of government — how rule is arranged decides how citizens are governed and how they take part.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** The pillars say what a state is; this lesson asks how it is ruled.
