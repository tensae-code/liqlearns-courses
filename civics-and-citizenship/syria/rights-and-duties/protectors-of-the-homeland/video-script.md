# Video script — Protectors of the Homeland

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The homeland is guarded by protectors whose work makes ordinary life — home, school and work — possible.

The unit on rights and duties closes with those who guard the homeland.

The lesson asks the pupil to see protection as a duty carried by real people, and to understand what it makes possible: children in school, families at home, work and travel in safety.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
