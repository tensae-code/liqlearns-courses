# Video script — Respecting and Protecting Human Rights

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Every human being holds rights from birth — all people are born free and equal in dignity and rights — and further rights as a member of society, with no discrimination among children.

Some rights are held from birth, as a human being: all people are born free and equal in dignity and rights.

Rights are not only held; they are to be respected and protected — by the state, by society and by each citizen toward every other.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
