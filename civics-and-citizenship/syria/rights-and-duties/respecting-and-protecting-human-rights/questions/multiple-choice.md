# Respecting and Protecting Human Rights — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) Every human being holds rights from birth — all people are born free and equal in dignity and rights — and further rights as a member of society, with no discrimination among children.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Some rights are held from birth, as a human being: all people are born free and equal in dignity and rights.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
