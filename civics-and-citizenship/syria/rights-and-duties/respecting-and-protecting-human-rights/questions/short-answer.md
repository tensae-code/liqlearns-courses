# Respecting and Protecting Human Rights — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Every human being holds rights from birth — all people are born free and equal in dignity and rights — and further rights as a member of society, with no discrimination among children.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Some rights are held from birth, as a human being: all people are born free and equal in dignity and rights.
