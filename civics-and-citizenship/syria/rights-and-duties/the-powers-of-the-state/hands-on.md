# Hands-on — The Powers of the State

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name three big jobs a state must do, and write beside each one which part of the state you think does it. Keep the list; the lesson names the powers

## Show it with real things
Take this lesson into one real act this week: do, make, visit, ask or help in a way that puts The Powers of the State into practice in your own community in Syria — then write what happened and what the lesson would say about it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
