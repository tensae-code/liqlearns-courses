# The Powers of the State — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) The state works through distinct powers, each with its own job — and through them the state guarantees rights such as education for all its children.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) The state does its work through distinct powers, and each power has its own job.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
