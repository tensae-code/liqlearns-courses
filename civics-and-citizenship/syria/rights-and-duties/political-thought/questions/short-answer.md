# Political Thought — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Political thought is the store of ideas about power and the state that stands behind rights and duties — knowing the ideas explains the frame.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Rights and duties do not float in the air; they rest on ideas.
