# The State and Its Pillars — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** A state stands on pillars — people, territory and authority — and thinkers have explained in different ways how states arose.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** What is a state made of? The lesson answers with its pillars: a people, a territory, and an authority over them.
