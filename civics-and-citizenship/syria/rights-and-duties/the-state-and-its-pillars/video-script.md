# Video script — The State and Its Pillars

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A state stands on pillars — people, territory and authority — and thinkers have explained in different ways how states arose.

What is a state made of? The lesson answers with its pillars: a people, a territory, and an authority over them.

Around that frame the lesson sets the theories of how states arose — the different explanations given for the first pillar joining the others.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
