# The Arab Community — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) The Arab community is the Syrian citizen's first wider belonging — beyond the homeland, a larger community of shared language, history and ties.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it

a) The older book's third unit widens the circle in steps, and the first step is the nearest: the Arab community.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
