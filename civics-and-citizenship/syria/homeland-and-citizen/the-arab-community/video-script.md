# Video script — The Arab Community

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The Arab community is the Syrian citizen's first wider belonging — beyond the homeland, a larger community of shared language, history and ties.

The older book's third unit widens the circle in steps, and the first step is the nearest: the Arab community.

The lesson teaches the citizen to hold two belongings at once without confusion: Syria is his homeland, and the Arab community is the larger house his homeland stands in.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
