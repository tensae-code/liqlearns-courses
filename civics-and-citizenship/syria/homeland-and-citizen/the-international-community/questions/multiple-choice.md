# The International Community — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) Beyond the Arab world stands the international community — the states and peoples of the whole world, joined in shared bodies and shared laws.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) The circle widens to its full width: beyond the Arab world stands the international community — all the states and peoples of the world, joined in shared bodies and bound by shared laws and dealings..  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
