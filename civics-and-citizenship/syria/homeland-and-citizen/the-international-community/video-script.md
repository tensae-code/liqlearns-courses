This lesson is about one thing.
Beyond the Arab world stands the international community — the states and peoples of the whole world, joined in shared bodies and shared laws.

The circle widens to its full width: beyond the Arab world stands the international community — all the states and peoples of the world, joined in shared bodies and bound by shared laws and dealings..

The citizen formed by this course now stands at the widest view the books give: his homeland inside the Arab community, and both inside the world.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
