# Springs of Giving — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Giving to the homeland is a spring that never runs dry — the homeland honours martyrdom and its martyrs, and the pupil answers with a pledge of his own.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** The homeland gives to the citizen; the citizen gives back.
