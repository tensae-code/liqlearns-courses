# Springs of Giving — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) Giving to the homeland is a spring that never runs dry — the homeland honours martyrdom and its martyrs, and the pupil answers with a pledge of his own.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) The homeland gives to the citizen; the citizen gives back.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
