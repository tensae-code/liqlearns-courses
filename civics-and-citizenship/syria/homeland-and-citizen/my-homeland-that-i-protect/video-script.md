# Video script — My Homeland That I Protect

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Love of the land is handed down generation after generation, and protecting the homeland — with resistance named as a basic element in recovering rights — is the citizen's duty.

Love of the land is not invented by each generation; it is handed down, generation after generation, and each generation receives the homeland as a trust.

Protection is taught plainly: resistance is named as a basic element in recovering rights.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
