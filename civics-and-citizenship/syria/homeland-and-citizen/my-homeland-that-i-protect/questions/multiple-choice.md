# My Homeland That I Protect — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) Love of the land is handed down generation after generation, and protecting the homeland — with resistance named as a basic element in recovering rights — is the citizen's duty.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Love of the land is not invented by each generation; it is handed down, generation after generation, and each generation receives the homeland as a trust.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
