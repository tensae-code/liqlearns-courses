# My Homeland That I Protect — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Love of the land is handed down generation after generation, and protecting the homeland — with resistance named as a basic element in recovering rights — is the citizen's duty.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Love of the land is not invented by each generation; it is handed down, generation after generation, and each generation receives the homeland as a trust.
