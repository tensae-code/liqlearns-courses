# Hands-on — My Homeland That I Love

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write six acts a pupil might do this week. Sort them into two columns: acts that show strong belonging, and acts that show weak belonging. Keep the sorted list; the lesson works the same sort

## Show it with real things
Take this lesson into one real act this week: do, make, visit, ask or help in a way that puts My Homeland That I Love into practice in your own community in Syria — then write what happened and what the lesson would say about it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
