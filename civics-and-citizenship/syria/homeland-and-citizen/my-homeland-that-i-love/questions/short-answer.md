# My Homeland That I Love — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** The third unit turns from the citizen's frame to the citizen's heart: national belonging, the bond between citizen and homeland.
