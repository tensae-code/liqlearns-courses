# My Homeland That I Love — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) National belonging is the bond between citizen and homeland — and it is measured in acts: some acts strengthen it and others weaken it.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
