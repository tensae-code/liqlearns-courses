# Video script — My Homeland That I Love

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
National belonging is the bond between citizen and homeland — and it is measured in acts: some acts strengthen it and others weaken it.

The third unit turns from the citizen's frame to the citizen's heart: national belonging, the bond between citizen and homeland.

The book's own exercise is a sorting one: acts that show strong belonging are separated from acts that show weak belonging.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
