# Keeping My Word — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) Keeping your pledge — loyalty to your word — is a citizen's duty: a homeland is built by people whose word can be trusted.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) The unit on the homeland closes small and personal: the pledge.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
