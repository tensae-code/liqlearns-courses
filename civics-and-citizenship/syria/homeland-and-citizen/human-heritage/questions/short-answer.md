# Human Heritage — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Human heritage (التراث الإنساني) is a shared inheritance from all peoples' past — and guarding it is part of the citizen's duty.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Heritage is what the past hands to the present.
