# I Value Myself — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Self-esteem is the picture you hold of yourself — knowing your own worth is the first civic skill, and family and community help shape that picture.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Self-esteem is the picture you hold of yourself: what you believe you are worth and what you believe you can do.
