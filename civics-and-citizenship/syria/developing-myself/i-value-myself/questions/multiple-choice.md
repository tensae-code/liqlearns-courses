# I Value Myself — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) Self-esteem is the picture you hold of yourself — knowing your own worth is the first civic skill, and family and community help shape that picture.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Self-esteem is the picture you hold of yourself: what you believe you are worth and what you believe you can do.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
