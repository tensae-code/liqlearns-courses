# Video script — I Value Myself

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Self-esteem is the picture you hold of yourself — knowing your own worth is the first civic skill, and family and community help shape that picture.

Self-esteem is the picture you hold of yourself: what you believe you are worth and what you believe you can do.

That picture is not made alone.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
