# I Connect with My Community — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) Connecting with your community means taking part in the life around you — knowing its people, joining its work and sharing its occasions.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) The first unit closes at the widest circle it reaches: the surrounding community.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
