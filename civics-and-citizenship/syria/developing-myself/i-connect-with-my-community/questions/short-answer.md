# I Connect with My Community — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Connecting with your community means taking part in the life around you — knowing its people, joining its work and sharing its occasions.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** The first unit closes at the widest circle it reaches: the surrounding community.
