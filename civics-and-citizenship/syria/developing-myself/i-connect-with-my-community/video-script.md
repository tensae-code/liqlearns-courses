# Video script — I Connect with My Community

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Connecting with your community means taking part in the life around you — knowing its people, joining its work and sharing its occasions.

The first unit closes at the widest circle it reaches: the surrounding community.

The pupil who has valued himself, loved his family and respected his friends now steps outward: the community is where those three lessons are practised on people who are not kin and not chosen friends.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
