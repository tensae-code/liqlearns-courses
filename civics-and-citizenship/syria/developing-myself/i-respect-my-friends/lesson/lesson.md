# I Respect My Friends

## Do it first

Think of one ordinary school scene with a friend — sharing, disagreeing, waiting your turn. Write what respect looked like in that scene, or what it should have looked like.

## What the book teaches

The circle widens again: from family to friends. Respect among friends is not taught as a slogan but in everyday school scenes — the small, ordinary moments where a pupil either honours a friend or fails to.

## The lesson in full

To respect a friend is to treat his worth as real as your own: listen when he speaks, be fair when you share or compete, and refuse the easy laugh at his expense. The lesson holds these scenes up one by one and asks the pupil to judge them — and then to judge his own.
