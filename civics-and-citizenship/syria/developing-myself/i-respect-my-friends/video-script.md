# Video script — I Respect My Friends

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Respect among friends is shown in everyday acts — listening, fairness and kindness in ordinary school scenes.

The circle widens again: from family to friends.

To respect a friend is to treat his worth as real as your own: listen when he speaks, be fair when you share or compete, and refuse the easy laugh at his expense.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
