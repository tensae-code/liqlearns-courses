# Initiative — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) Initiative (المبادرة) is starting useful action yourself instead of waiting to be told — the citizen who begins.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Initiative — المبادرة — is starting useful action oneself instead of waiting.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
