# Public Opinion — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) Public opinion is what a society thinks together — it forms through talk and media, and the citizen meets it critically, not blindly.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) A society does not only act together; it thinks together.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
