# Hands-on — Public Opinion

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Take one thing 'everyone says' in your school or town. Write where you first heard it, who repeats it, and whether you have any ground for it besides the repeating

## Show it with real things
Take this lesson into one real act this week: do, make, visit, ask or help in a way that puts Public Opinion into practice in your own community in Syria — then write what happened and what the lesson would say about it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
