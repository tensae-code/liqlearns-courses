# Video script — Public Opinion

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Public opinion is what a society thinks together — it forms through talk and media, and the citizen meets it critically, not blindly.

A society does not only act together; it thinks together.

The citizen's duty toward it is double: respect it, because it is his society's voice; and meet it critically, because a crowd can repeat an error as easily as a truth.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
