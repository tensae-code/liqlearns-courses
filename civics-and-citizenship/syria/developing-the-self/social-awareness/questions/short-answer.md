# Social Awareness — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Social awareness is reading your own society clearly — seeing its life and your place in it — before you act in it.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Before the older pupil can act on society, he must be able to read it.
