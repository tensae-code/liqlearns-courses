# Social Awareness — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) Social awareness is reading your own society clearly — seeing its life and your place in it — before you act in it.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Before the older pupil can act on society, he must be able to read it.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
