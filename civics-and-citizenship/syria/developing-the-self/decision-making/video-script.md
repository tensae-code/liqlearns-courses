# Video script — Decision-Making

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A decision is made in steps — gather the grounds, weigh the options, then take the decision — and this is the first civic skill of the older pupil.

The older book opens where the younger one opened — inside the pupil — but with a harder skill: how a decision is made.

The book calls this the first civic skill because everything after it depends on it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
