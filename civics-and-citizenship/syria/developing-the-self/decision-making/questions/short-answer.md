# Decision-Making — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** A decision is made in steps — gather the grounds, weigh the options, then take the decision — and this is the first civic skill of the older pupil.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** The older book opens where the younger one opened — inside the pupil — but with a harder skill: how a decision is made.
