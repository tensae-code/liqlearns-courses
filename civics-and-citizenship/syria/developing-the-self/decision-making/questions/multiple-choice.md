# Decision-Making — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) A decision is made in steps — gather the grounds, weigh the options, then take the decision — and this is the first civic skill of the older pupil.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) The older book opens where the younger one opened — inside the pupil — but with a harder skill: how a decision is made.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
