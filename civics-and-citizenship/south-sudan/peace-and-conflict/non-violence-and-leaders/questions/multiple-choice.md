# Non-Violence and Leaders — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Non-violence and the leaders who practise it.  b) it has nothing to do with how South Sudan governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Non-violence and the leaders who practise it.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Non-violence and the leaders who practise it.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in South Sudan
**Answer: a) Non-violence and the leaders who practise it.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
