# Peace-Making — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Peace-making and the terms associated with peace.  b) it has nothing to do with how South Sudan governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Peace-making and the terms associated with peace.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Peace-making and the terms associated with peace.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in South Sudan
**Answer: a) Peace-making and the terms associated with peace.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
