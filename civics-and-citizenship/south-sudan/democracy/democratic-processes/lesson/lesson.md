# Democratic Processes

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Democratic Processes. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what South Sudan's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Democratic processes — voting, paying taxes, upholding the rule of law, standing for leadership and reporting law-breakers — as things every citizen does.
