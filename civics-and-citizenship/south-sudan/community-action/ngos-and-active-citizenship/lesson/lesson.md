# NGOs and Active Citizenship

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: NGOs and Active Citizenship. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what South Sudan's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

The role of non-governmental organisations in civil society and how NGOs promote active citizenship.
