# What Citizenship Is — depth

This lesson is ONE lesson with no sub lessons: it teaches a single thing, whole. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: Citizenship as the legal right to belong to a country; South Sudanese citizenship by birth to a South Sudanese mother or father, naturalisation, dual citizenship; the duties of citizens (defend the country, promote democracy, good governance and the rule of law, avoid violence and end divisions, protect public funds, combat corruption, take part in elections and referenda); nationalism and patriotism. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
