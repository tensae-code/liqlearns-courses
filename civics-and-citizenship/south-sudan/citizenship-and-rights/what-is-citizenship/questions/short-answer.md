# What Citizenship Is — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** Citizenship as the legal right to belong to a country; South Sudanese citizenship by birth to a South Sudanese mother or father, naturalisation, dual citizenship; the duties of citizens (defend the country, promote democracy, good governance and the rule of law, avoid violence and end divisions, protect public funds, combat corruption, take part in elections and referenda); nationalism and patriotism.
