# What Citizenship Is — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Citizenship as the legal right to belong to a country; South Sudanese citizenship by birth to a South Sudanese mother or father, naturalisation, dual citizenship; the duties of citizens (defend the country, promote democracy, good governance and the rule of law, avoid violence and end divisions, protect public funds, combat corruption, take part in elections and referenda); nationalism and patriotism.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that What Citizenship Is — and that it has no connection to the rest of South Sudan's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside South Sudan's own course, in its own order.
