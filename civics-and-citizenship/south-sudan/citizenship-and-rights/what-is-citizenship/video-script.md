# Video script — What Citizenship Is

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Citizenship as the legal right to belong to a country; South Sudanese citizenship by birth to a South Sudanese mother or father, naturalisation, dual citizenship; the duties of citizens (defend the country, promote democracy, good governance and the rule of law, avoid violence and end divisions, protect public funds, combat corruption, take part in elections and referenda); nationalism and patriotism.
