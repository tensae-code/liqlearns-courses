# Legal and Human Rights — National and International — depth

This lesson is ONE lesson with no sub lessons: it teaches a single thing, whole. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: Creation of political parties, elections in South Sudan, roles of Parliament, the judiciary and court system (appeal and high courts, police in court operations), roles of citizens in their government, civil rights versus human rights, and civic leadership in promoting peace. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
