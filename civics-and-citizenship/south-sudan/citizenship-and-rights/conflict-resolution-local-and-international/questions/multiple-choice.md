# Conflict Resolution — Local and International — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Conflict resolution from the local level to the international level.  b) it has nothing to do with how South Sudan governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Conflict resolution from the local level to the international level.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Conflict resolution from the local level to the international level.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in South Sudan
**Answer: a) Conflict resolution from the local level to the international level.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
