# Labour Law — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The labour law of South Sudan.  b) it has nothing to do with how South Sudan governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The labour law of South Sudan.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) The labour law of South Sudan.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in South Sudan
**Answer: a) The labour law of South Sudan.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
