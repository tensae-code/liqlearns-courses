# Rights of Employees and Employers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The rights and responsibilities of employees and of employers.  b) it has nothing to do with how South Sudan governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The rights and responsibilities of employees and of employers.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) The rights and responsibilities of employees and of employers.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in South Sudan
**Answer: a) The rights and responsibilities of employees and of employers.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
