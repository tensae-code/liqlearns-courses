# Parliament and Forms of Government — depth

This lesson is ONE lesson with no sub lessons: it teaches a single thing, whole. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: The National Government, the Parliament of South Sudan (National Assembly) — its characteristics and functions; state-level government (legislative and executive organs), local governments, forms of government in other parts of the world, and Dr John Garang's contribution to the Republic. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
