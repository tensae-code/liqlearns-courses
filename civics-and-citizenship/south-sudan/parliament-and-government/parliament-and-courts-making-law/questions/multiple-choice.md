# Parliament and the Courts Making Law — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) How parliament and the courts together make law.  b) it has nothing to do with how South Sudan governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) How parliament and the courts together make law.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) How parliament and the courts together make law.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in South Sudan
**Answer: a) How parliament and the courts together make law.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
