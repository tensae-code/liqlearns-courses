# Organs of Government — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The government organs of the Republic of South Sudan: the national government, the state government and local government.  b) it has nothing to do with how South Sudan governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The government organs of the Republic of South Sudan: the national government, the state government and local government.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) The government organs of the Republic of South Sudan: the national government, the state government and local government.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in South Sudan
**Answer: a) The government organs of the Republic of South Sudan: the national government, the state government and local government.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
