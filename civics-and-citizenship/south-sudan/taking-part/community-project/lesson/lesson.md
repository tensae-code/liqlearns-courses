# Community Project

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Community Project. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what South Sudan's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Secondary 1 project

The first community project: opportunities in a community, the project cycle and project management steps, requirements to participate, evidencing a project's impact, and communication in community projects — as the Secondary 1 books teach it.

## Sub lesson 2 — The Secondary 2 project

The year's community project at Secondary 2: running a community project for active citizenship (the student book's worked example is planting trees) — opportunities, aims and objectives, and the project's impacts.

## Sub lesson 3 — The Secondary 3 project

The year's community project at Secondary 3, climbed one level higher.
