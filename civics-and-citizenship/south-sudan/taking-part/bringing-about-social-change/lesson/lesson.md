# Bringing About Social Change

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Bringing About Social Change. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what South Sudan's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Social change and its indicators, strategies of voluntary organisations and individuals, the contributions of NGOs and international organisations, and activities that unite people in peaceful co-existence.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Social change and its indicators, strategies of voluntary organisations and individuals, the contributions of NGOs and international organisations, and activities that unite people in peaceful co-existence. Give one real example from your own life or from the South Sudan you live in, and name the lesson's key words: bringing, about, social. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.

3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
