# Civics and Citizenship — Nepal course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

Civics belongs to the country: this course is Nepal's own —
built from Nepal's Moral Education student books for Class 7
and Class 8 (Curriculum Development Centre, English, each with
a Nepali parallel edition). Inside the course the organisation
is by **levels, not grades**: the titles below are the books'
own five units, and the lessons inside them climb the two
levels in path order — what the learner proves, step by step.
The grades the books come from live only in each lesson's
sources.

The path's own logic is the books': both books run the same
five-unit frame — character development, human values, civil
rights and duties, community life style and diversity,
discipline and positive thinking. The Class 7 book walks it
first, teaching through named-character stories (Devi Ram);
the Class 8 book walks the same frame one step up, with the
civic weight raised — from rights to the duty of citizens,
social work, and peace and friendship in a diverse community
(Aaitaman, Misrilal, Ganga). That ladder IS the path order
(recommended, never forced — a learner can jump in anywhere;
the "start here instead" hints point down this ladder).

## The titles

- `character/` — Levels 1–2. Character development: Level 1
  (building good character — Devi Ram, a path finder; the
  common essence of religions; unity); Level 2 (building good
  character deepened — Aaitaman, pride of the village; being a
  good example; positive thinking).
- `human-values/` — Levels 1–2. Human values and norms:
  Level 1 (helping others; respecting all work; honesty);
  Level 2 (true friendship; my real identity; universal
  relationship).
- `rights-and-duties/` — Levels 1–2. Civil duties and
  responsibilities, then civil rights and duties: Level 1
  (our rights; moral responsibility; learning from elders);
  Level 2 (the duty of citizens; my duty; social work).
- `community-life/` — Levels 1–2. Community life style and
  diversity: Level 1 (the importance of education; moral
  practice; respecting others and oneself); Level 2 (peace
  and friendship; effort that changes a community; unity in
  diversity).
- `discipline/` — Levels 1–2. Discipline and positive
  thinking: Level 1 (becoming good; advantages of
  discipline; as you sow so you reap); Level 2 (regret and
  learning; inner eyes; moderation).
