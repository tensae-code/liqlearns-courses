# Civics and Citizenship — Nepal course — CURRICULUM (the path)

One course for Nepal inside the Civics and Citizenship folder,
organised by **levels, not grades**: a learner climbs when they
have proved the level, not when a school calendar says so. The
school grades the books come from (Classes 7 and 8) appear only
in each lesson's sources (provenance), never as the course's
organisation. The path follows Nepal's own books' order: the
Class 7 Moral Education book first — character built through
named-character stories, then human values and norms, rights
with moral responsibility, community life in diversity, and
discipline with positive thinking — then the Class 8 book,
which repeats the same five-unit frame one step up, with the
civic weight raised: the duty of citizens, social work, peace
and friendship in a diverse community, and moderation.

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in
this course comes from government textbooks — this country's
own Moral Education books (Classes 7 and 8, Curriculum
Development Centre) — except the **Tip boxes**: a tip in a box
is from us, the people making LiqLearns. Tips are ours; the
lessons are the books'. See ABOUT.md for the full book list,
the parallel Nepali editions, the record-only Scribd lead, and
the acceptance reckoning.

## The levels (the path, in order)

1. **Character, Values and First Civic Duties** — the Class 7
   book's whole climb, in its own five titles: character
   (`character/` — building good character through Devi Ram's
   story, the common essence of religions, unity), human
   values (`human-values/` — helping others, respecting all
   work, honesty), rights and duties (`rights-and-duties/` —
   our rights, moral responsibility, learning from elders),
   community life (`community-life/` — the importance of
   education, moral practice, respecting others and oneself),
   and discipline (`discipline/` — becoming good, advantages
   of discipline, as you sow so you reap). 15 lessons.
2. **The Citizen's Duty in the Community** — the Class 8 book
   in the same five titles, one step up: character deepened
   (building good character's second story, Aaitaman; being a
   good example; positive thinking), human values widened
   (true friendship, my real identity, universal
   relationship), rights turned to duties (`rights-and-duties/`
   — the duty of citizens, my duty, social work through
   Misrilal's life), community life in diversity
   (`community-life/` — peace and friendship, effort that
   changes a community, unity in diversity), and discipline
   matured (`discipline/` — regret and learning, inner eyes,
   moderation). 14 new lessons, and Building Good Character
   deepened by its second sub lesson.

**Totals: 29 lessons, 30 placements stocked** (Level 1: 15
lessons · Level 2: 14 new lessons + 1 lesson deepened — from
two student books: Moral Education, Class 7 (CDC, 2017 revised
edition, COMPLETE) and Moral Education, Class 8 (CDC, 2018
revised edition, COMPLETE), each placed once with its 2076 BS
Nepali parallel edition named; see ABOUT.md).

*Standing note on the count: the two books coin one lesson
name twice — character/building-good-character (Devi Ram in
Class 7, Aaitaman in Class 8). Those two placements stock into
ONE lesson, Building Good Character, as its two sub lessons in
ladder order. That is the course's only dedupe merge: 30
placements, 29 lessons, every placement stocked exactly once.*
