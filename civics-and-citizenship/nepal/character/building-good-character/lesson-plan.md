# Lesson plan — Building Good Character

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Nepal · **Title:** Character · **Lesson:** Building Good Character
**Place in the curriculum:** opens Level 1 — Character, Values and First Civic Duties, and is deepened at Level 2 — The Citizen's Duty in the Community (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** good character is something you build — and the way to build it is to follow people who already live it, the way Devi Ram and Aaitaman are followed in their own places.
**This lesson's own levels:** it opens the course at Level 1 with Devi Ram's story, and its second part stands at Level 2 with Aaitaman's — the same lesson climbed twice, as Nepal's two books climb it (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Devi Ram, a path finder** — Devi Ram is held up as a path finder — a person whose way of living shows others the road to good character. The lesson asks you to watch what such a person does, name the character in it, and take the same path yourself.
2. **Aaitaman, pride of the village** — Aaitaman is the pride of his village because his character is good and fair, and he is ready for the welfare of others. One step up from Devi Ram: character is no longer only your own road — it is something a whole village can point to and follow.

Gate pool: the prove-it check for each sub lesson is at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson — drawn from this lesson's question files (questions/).

## Before this lesson
- This is the first lesson of the course. Nothing is recommended before it. Its second part is recommended after the Level 1 lessons — never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: good character is something you build — and the way to build it is to follow people who already live it, the way Devi Ram and Aaitaman are followed in their own places.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street, school or community in Nepal.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the learner's own life in Nepal and the books' own teaching, as Nepal's Moral Education books work through named-character stories, the behaviours they model, and discussion.
