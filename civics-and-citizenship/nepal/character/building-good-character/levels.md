# Building Good Character — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Devi Ram, a path finder
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Devi Ram is held up as a path finder — a person whose way of living shows others the road to good character. The lesson asks you to watch what such a person does, name the character in it, and take the same path yourself. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — Aaitaman, pride of the village
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Aaitaman is the pride of his village because his character is good and fair, and he is ready for the welfare of others. One step up from Devi Ram: character is no longer only your own road — it is something a whole village can point to and follow. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
