# Building Good Character

## Do it first

Think of one person you know whose way of living you would like to copy. Say their name, and name one thing they do that shows good character.

## Sub lesson 1 — Devi Ram, a path finder

Devi Ram is held up as a path finder — a person whose way of living shows others the road to good character. The lesson asks you to watch what such a person does, name the character in it, and take the same path yourself.

## Sub lesson 2 — Aaitaman, pride of the village

Aaitaman is the pride of his village because his character is good and fair, and he is ready for the welfare of others. One step up from Devi Ram: character is no longer only your own road — it is something a whole village can point to and follow.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: good character is something you build — and the way to build it is to follow people who already live it, the way Devi Ram and Aaitaman are followed in their own places. Give one real example from your own life in Nepal, and name the lesson's key words: character, example, path finder, village. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Nepal's own books teach Moral Education through named people — a story first, then the behaviour it models, then discussion. When a lesson names a person, picture that person's day, not only their name: the story is the teaching.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: good character is something you build — and the way to build it is to follow people who already live it, the way Devi Ram and Aaitaman are followed in their own places.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
