# Video script — Building Good Character

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Good character is something you build — and the way to build it is to follow people who already live it, the way Devi Ram and Aaitaman are followed in their own places.

Devi Ram, a path finder. Devi Ram is held up as a path finder — a person whose way of living shows others the road to good character. The lesson asks you to watch what such a person does, name the character in it, and take the same path yourself.

Aaitaman, pride of the village. Aaitaman is the pride of his village because his character is good and fair, and he is ready for the welfare of others. One step up from Devi Ram: character is no longer only your own road — it is something a whole village can point to and follow.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
