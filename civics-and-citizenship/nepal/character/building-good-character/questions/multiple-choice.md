# Building Good Character — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) good character is something you build — and the way to build it is to follow people who already live it, the way Devi Ram and Aaitaman are followed in their own places  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the books' teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — Devi Ram, a path finder?
a) Devi Ram is held up as a path finder — a person whose way of living shows others the road to good character. The lesson asks you to watch what such a person does, name the character in it, and take the same path yourself.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
