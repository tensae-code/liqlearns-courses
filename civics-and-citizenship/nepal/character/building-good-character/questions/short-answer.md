# Building Good Character — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Good character is something you build — and the way to build it is to follow people who already live it, the way Devi Ram and Aaitaman are followed in their own places.

**2.** What does the part "Aaitaman, pride of the village" teach?
**Answer:** Aaitaman is the pride of his village because his character is good and fair, and he is ready for the welfare of others. One step up from Devi Ram: character is no longer only your own road — it is something a whole village can point to and follow.
