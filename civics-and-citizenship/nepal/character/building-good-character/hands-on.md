# Hands-on — Building Good Character

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Think of one person you know whose way of living you would like to copy. Say their name, and name one thing they do that shows good character.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: good character is something you build — and the way to build it is to follow people who already live it, the way Devi Ram and Aaitaman are followed in their own places. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
