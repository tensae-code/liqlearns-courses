# Being a Good Example — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Make yourself an example for others — live so that people who watch you learn the good way from what you do.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** The learner becomes the model: others learn the good way by watching what you do, not only by hearing you.
