# Being a Good Example — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) make yourself an example for others — live so that people who watch you learn the good way from what you do  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) The learner becomes the model: others learn the good way by watching what you do, not only by hearing you.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
