# Unity — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Unity is a strength of character — people who stand together can do what no one of them could do alone.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Unity is taught here as a character strength — a settled part of the person, not only a way to win an argument.
