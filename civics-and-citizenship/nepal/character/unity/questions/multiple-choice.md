# Unity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) unity is a strength of character — people who stand together can do what no one of them could do alone  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Unity is taught here as a character strength — a settled part of the person, not only a way to win an argument.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
