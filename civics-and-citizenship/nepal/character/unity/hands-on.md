# Hands-on — Unity

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Try to snap one thin stick. Then try to snap a bundle of the same sticks tied together. Feel the difference unity makes.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: unity is a strength of character — people who stand together can do what no one of them could do alone. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
