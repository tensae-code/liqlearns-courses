# The Common Essence of Religions — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) under their different names and ways, religions share one essence about how a person should live and treat others — and religious conduct and social conduct belong together  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) The lesson joins religious conduct with social conduct — belief and daily behaviour are one teaching, not two.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
