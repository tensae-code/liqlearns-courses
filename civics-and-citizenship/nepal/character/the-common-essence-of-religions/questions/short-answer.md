# The Common Essence of Religions — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Under their different names and ways, religions share one essence about how a person should live and treat others — and religious conduct and social conduct belong together.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** The lesson joins religious conduct with social conduct — belief and daily behaviour are one teaching, not two.
