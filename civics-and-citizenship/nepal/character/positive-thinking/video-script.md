# Video script — Positive Thinking

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Positive thinking is not only a mood — it joins learning and doing: think positively, learn, and then act.

The book's lesson title is a three-step order: think positively, learn, and do. Positive thinking comes first because the way you name a difficulty decides whether you face it or flee from it.

But thinking alone is not the lesson. The positive thought must pass into learning — finding out, practising, asking — and learning must pass into doing. A positive thinker who never acts has stopped at the first step of three.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
