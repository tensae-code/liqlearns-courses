# Positive Thinking — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Positive thinking is not only a mood — it joins learning and doing: think positively, learn, and then act.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Positive thinking is a three-step order in the book: think positively, learn, and do — all three, in that order.
