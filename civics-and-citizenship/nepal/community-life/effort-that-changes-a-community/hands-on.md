# Hands-on — Effort That Changes a Community

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name one thing in your community that is better because one person made an effort. Say who started it.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: one person's effort can change a whole community — Ganga's effort shows that community life improves when someone starts and keeps going. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
