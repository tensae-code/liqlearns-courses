# Effort That Changes a Community — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) one person's effort can change a whole community — Ganga's effort shows that community life improves when someone starts and keeps going  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

a) Ganga's effort is the book's proof that one person's sustained effort can improve a whole community's life.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
