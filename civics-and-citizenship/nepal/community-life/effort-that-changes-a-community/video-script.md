# Video script — Effort That Changes a Community

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
One person's effort can change a whole community — Ganga's effort shows that community life improves when someone starts and keeps going.

This lesson's teacher is Ganga, whose effort improved the life of her community. The book holds her up to make one point plainly: communities do not only change by orders from above — they change when one person makes an effort and keeps going.

Effort is the bridge between wishing and change. Everyone can wish the path were swept, the water cleaner, the school repaired. Ganga's lesson is that the one who starts — and continues — is the one whose community actually changes.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
