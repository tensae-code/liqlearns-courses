# Effort That Changes a Community — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — one person's effort can change a whole community — Ganga's effort shows that community life improves when someone starts and keeps going — in their own words, give one real example from their own life in Nepal, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or community situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
