# Respecting Others and Oneself — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Respect for others and respect for yourself are one habit — you cannot truly have one without the other.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Respect for others and self-respect are one habit — neither half truly stands without the other.
