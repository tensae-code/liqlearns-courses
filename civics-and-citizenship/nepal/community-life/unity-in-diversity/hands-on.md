# Hands-on — Unity in Diversity

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name the members of one group you belong to — family, class or team. Say one way you differ, and one thing that holds you together.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: different members can stand as one family — the story of five brothers teaches unity among people who are not the same. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
