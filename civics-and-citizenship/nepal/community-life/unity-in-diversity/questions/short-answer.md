# Unity in Diversity — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Different members can stand as one family — the story of five brothers teaches unity among people who are not the same.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** The five brothers are different members standing as one family — unity added to diversity, not sameness replacing it.
