# Moral Practice — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Morality is not only something you know — it is something you practise every day, in small acts, until it becomes your habit.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Morality must be practised daily in small acts — knowing the good without doing it is not yet morality lived.
