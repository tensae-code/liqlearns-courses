# Moral Practice — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) morality is not only something you know — it is something you practise every day, in small acts, until it becomes your habit  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Morality must be practised daily in small acts — knowing the good without doing it is not yet morality lived.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
