# Peace and Friendship — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Peace and friendship are how a diverse community lives — people who differ can still live as friends, in peace.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Peace is difference handled with friendship — a diverse community lives by peace and friendship, not by sameness.
