# Peace and Friendship — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Peace and friendship are how a diverse community lives — people who differ can still live as friends, in peace.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a child's own home, school or community in Nepal.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Nepal and lived there.
