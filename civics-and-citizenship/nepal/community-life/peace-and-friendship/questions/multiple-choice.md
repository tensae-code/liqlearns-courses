# Peace and Friendship — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) peace and friendship are how a diverse community lives — people who differ can still live as friends, in peace  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Peace is difference handled with friendship — a diverse community lives by peace and friendship, not by sameness.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
