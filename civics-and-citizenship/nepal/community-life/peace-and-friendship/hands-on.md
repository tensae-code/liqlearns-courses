# Hands-on — Peace and Friendship

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name one person different from you — in language, custom or belief — who is your friend, or could be. Say what keeps the friendship peaceful.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: peace and friendship are how a diverse community lives — people who differ can still live as friends, in peace. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
