# Video script — The Importance of Education

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Education matters for the whole community, not only for the one who learns — a community of educated people lives better together.

This lesson opens the book's unit on community life, and it begins with education — because education is one of the ways a community rises. What one child learns does not stay inside that child: it goes home, it helps the family, it serves the neighbours.

An educated community can read, reckon, judge and build together. That is why education matters in community life: it is not a private prize for the clever, but a common good that lifts everyone it touches.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
