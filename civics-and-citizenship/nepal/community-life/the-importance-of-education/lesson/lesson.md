# The Importance of Education

## Do it first

Name one thing you have learned at school that you have already used to help someone at home.

## Why education matters

This lesson opens the book's unit on community life, and it begins with education — because education is one of the ways a community rises. What one child learns does not stay inside that child: it goes home, it helps the family, it serves the neighbours.

An educated community can read, reckon, judge and build together. That is why education matters in community life: it is not a private prize for the clever, but a common good that lifts everyone it touches.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: education matters for the whole community, not only for the one who learns — a community of educated people lives better together. Give one real example from your own life in Nepal, and name the lesson's key words: education, community, learning, life. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Nepal's own books teach Moral Education through named people — a story first, then the behaviour it models, then discussion. When a lesson names a person, picture that person's day, not only their name: the story is the teaching.
