# The Importance of Education — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Education matters for the whole community, not only for the one who learns — a community of educated people lives better together.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Education is taught as a community good — what one learner gains is carried home to family and neighbours.
