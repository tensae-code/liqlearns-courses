# The Importance of Education — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) education matters for the whole community, not only for the one who learns — a community of educated people lives better together  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Education is taught as a community good — what one learner gains is carried home to family and neighbours.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
