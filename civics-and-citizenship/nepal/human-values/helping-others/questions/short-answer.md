# Helping Others — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Helping others is a human value you practise with your own hands — when someone near you needs help, giving it is what a person does.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Help is taught as something done with your hands — real acts for real people near you, not kind thoughts alone.
