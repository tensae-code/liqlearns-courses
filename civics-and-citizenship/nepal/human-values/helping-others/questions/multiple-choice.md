# Helping Others — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) helping others is a human value you practise with your own hands — when someone near you needs help, giving it is what a person does  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Help is taught as something done with your hands — real acts for real people near you, not kind thoughts alone.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
