# My Real Identity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) your real identity is who you truly are — deeper than appearances, names or what others happen to see first  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Real identity lies beyond appearances — it is the character and values that make a person themselves.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
