# Video script — My Real Identity

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Your real identity is who you truly are — deeper than appearances, names or what others happen to see first.

Appearances are the first thing people see — face, clothes, size, wealth — but they are not who a person is. This lesson teaches the learner to know their real identity: the character, values and purposes that make them themselves.

Knowing your real identity steadies you. A person who knows who they truly are is not blown about by every opinion, and does not mistake a label for a self. Identity known honestly is the ground that good character is built on.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
