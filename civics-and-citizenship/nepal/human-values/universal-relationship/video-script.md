# Video script — Universal Relationship

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
You are related to all people, not only to your own circle — every human being stands in relationship with you.

A person's closest relationships are with family and friends — but the lesson teaches that relationship does not stop at that circle. Each of us stands in a universal relationship: bound to all people, near and far, known and unknown.

What happens to distant people touches us, and what we do touches them — through trade, through the shared earth, through our common humanity. To know your universal relationship is to widen your care to the size of the world.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
