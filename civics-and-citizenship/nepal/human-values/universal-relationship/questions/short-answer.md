# Universal Relationship — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** You are related to all people, not only to your own circle — every human being stands in relationship with you.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Relationship does not stop at family and friends — every person stands in a universal relationship with all people.
