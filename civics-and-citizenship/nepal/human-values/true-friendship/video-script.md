# Video script — True Friendship

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
True companionship is a human value — a true friend stands with you in good times and hard ones, and you stand with them.

The book calls true friends 'true soul mates' — companions whose bond is deeper than convenience. This lesson teaches true companionship as a human value: a friendship that holds in hard times, not only in easy ones.

A true friend tells you the truth, stands beside you when others drift away, and rejoices at your good without envy. And the value runs both ways: to have a true friend, be one — standing with your friend is the same lesson from the other side.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
