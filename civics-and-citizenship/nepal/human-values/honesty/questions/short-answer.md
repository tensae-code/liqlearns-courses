# Honesty — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Honesty is a norm of life — tell the truth and act truly, not only when someone is watching, but as your settled way of living.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Honesty is taught as a norm of life — the settled rule of every day, not a show for watching eyes.
