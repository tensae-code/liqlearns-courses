# Honesty — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) honesty is a norm of life — tell the truth and act truly, not only when someone is watching, but as your settled way of living  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Honesty is taught as a norm of life — the settled rule of every day, not a show for watching eyes.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
