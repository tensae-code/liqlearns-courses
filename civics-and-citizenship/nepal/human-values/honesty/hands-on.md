# Hands-on — Honesty

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Think of one small moment today when you could have hidden the truth. Say out loud what the honest thing to say or do was.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: honesty is a norm of life — tell the truth and act truly, not only when someone is watching, but as your settled way of living. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
