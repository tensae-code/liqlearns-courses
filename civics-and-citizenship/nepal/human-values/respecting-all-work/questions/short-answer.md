# Respecting All Work — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Every honest occupation has dignity — the farmer, the teacher, the cleaner and the driver all do work the community needs, and all deserve respect.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** No honest occupation is beneath respect — the lesson names the dignity of various occupations, all of them.
