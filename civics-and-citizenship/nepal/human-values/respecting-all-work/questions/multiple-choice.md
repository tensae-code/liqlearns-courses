# Respecting All Work — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) every honest occupation has dignity — the farmer, the teacher, the cleaner and the driver all do work the community needs, and all deserve respect  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) No honest occupation is beneath respect — the lesson names the dignity of various occupations, all of them.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
