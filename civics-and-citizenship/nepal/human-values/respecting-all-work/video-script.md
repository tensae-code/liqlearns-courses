# Video script — Respecting All Work

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Every honest occupation has dignity — the farmer, the teacher, the cleaner and the driver all do work the community needs, and all deserve respect.

A community lives by many occupations: people grow food, teach children, build, carry, clean, heal, drive and mend. The lesson teaches that these various occupations all carry dignity, because each one serves a need that everyone shares.

Respecting all work means never looking down on a person for the job they do. The one who sweeps the street and the one who sits in an office both give their labour to the common life — and labour honestly done is worthy of respect, whoever does it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
