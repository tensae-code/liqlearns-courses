# Video script — My Duty

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Duty becomes real when you name it as your own — 'my duty' is the duty you have taken hold of yourself.

The lesson before this one named the duty of citizens in general. This lesson brings it home to one person: my duty. A duty that belongs to everybody in general can be dodged by anybody; a duty named as mine cannot.

Naming your duty means saying, in your own words, what you yourself owe — at home, at school, in the community — and owning it. The lesson asks the learner to stop speaking of duty as other people's business and take hold of their own share by name.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
