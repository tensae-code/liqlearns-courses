# My Duty — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) duty becomes real when you name it as your own — 'my duty' is the duty you have taken hold of yourself  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Duty becomes real when it is named as your own — 'my duty' is duty taken hold of personally, not left to everyone in general.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
