# My Duty — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Duty becomes real when you name it as your own — 'my duty' is the duty you have taken hold of yourself.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Duty becomes real when it is named as your own — 'my duty' is duty taken hold of personally, not left to everyone in general.
