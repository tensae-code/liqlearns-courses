# My Duty — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Duty becomes real when you name it as your own — 'my duty' is the duty you have taken hold of yourself.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a child's own home, school or community in Nepal.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Nepal and lived there.
