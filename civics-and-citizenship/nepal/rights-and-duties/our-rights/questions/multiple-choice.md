# Our Rights — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) every person holds rights — things that are theirs by being a person, which others must respect  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Rights belong to a person by being a person — they are not gifts others can take away at will.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
