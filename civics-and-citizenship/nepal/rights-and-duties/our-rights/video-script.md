# Video script — Our Rights

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Every person holds rights — things that are theirs by being a person, which others must respect.

A right is something that belongs to a person simply because they are a person. This lesson opens the book's unit on civil duties and responsibilities by starting where it must start: with the rights each of us holds.

Rights are not gifts that others may take away when they please. They are ours — and because they are ours, other people must respect them, just as we must respect the same rights in them. The next lessons take up the other side of the coin: responsibility and duty.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
