# Video script — The Duty of Citizens

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Citizens owe duties to their country and community — being a citizen is not only holding rights, it is carrying your share.

The Class 7 book taught rights first, then moral responsibility. The Class 8 book raises the civic weight: this lesson names the duty of citizens — what the members of a country owe to that country and to the community they share.

A citizen who only claims and never carries is a passenger, not a citizen. Duties — respecting the law and the rights of others, serving the community, answering when the country calls — are the citizen's share of the common life.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
