# The Duty of Citizens — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) citizens owe duties to their country and community — being a citizen is not only holding rights, it is carrying your share  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Citizenship is carrying your share: duties owed to country and community stand beside the citizen's rights.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
