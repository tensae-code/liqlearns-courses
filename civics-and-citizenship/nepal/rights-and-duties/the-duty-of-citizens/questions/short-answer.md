# The Duty of Citizens — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Citizens owe duties to their country and community — being a citizen is not only holding rights, it is carrying your share.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Citizenship is carrying your share: duties owed to country and community stand beside the citizen's rights.
