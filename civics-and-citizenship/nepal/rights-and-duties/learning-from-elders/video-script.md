# Video script — Learning from Elders

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Parents and elders guide us in our duties — listening to their guidance is part of how a young person learns to do right.

The book teaches this lesson through a father's guidance: the older generation handing the younger one its duties. Parents and elders have walked the road before; their guidance is experience turned into direction.

Taking guidance is itself a duty of the young. To listen carefully, to ask, and to follow good guidance is how a child learns what no book alone can teach — how duties look in a real life, in a real family.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
