# Learning from Elders — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Parents and elders guide us in our duties — listening to their guidance is part of how a young person learns to do right.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Guidance from parents and elders is how the young learn their duties — listening to it is itself a duty.
