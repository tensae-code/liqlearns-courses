# Video script — Moral Responsibility

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Rights come with a second side — moral responsibility: what you ought to do for others and for what is right.

The lesson before this one taught the rights a person holds. This lesson turns the coin over: alongside every right stands a moral responsibility — the things a person ought to do, for others and for what is right.

Moral responsibility is not forced from outside; it is the inner ought. I ought to respect the rights I claim for myself when they belong to my neighbour. I ought to answer for my acts. A person who holds their rights and carries their responsibilities is a whole citizen in the making.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
