# Moral Responsibility — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Rights come with a second side — moral responsibility: what you ought to do for others and for what is right.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Moral responsibility is the other side of rights — the inner ought that answers to every right a person holds.
