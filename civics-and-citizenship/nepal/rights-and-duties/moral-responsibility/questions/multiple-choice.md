# Moral Responsibility — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) rights come with a second side — moral responsibility: what you ought to do for others and for what is right  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Moral responsibility is the other side of rights — the inner ought that answers to every right a person holds.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
