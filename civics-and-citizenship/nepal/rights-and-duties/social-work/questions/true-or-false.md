# Social Work — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Misrilal's life shows what civic duty looks like in a person — a true social worker spends himself for the good of the community.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a child's own home, school or community in Nepal.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Nepal and lived there.
