# Hands-on — Social Work

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name one act of social work you have seen in your own community — someone serving others without being paid. Say what it changed.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Misrilal's life shows what civic duty looks like in a person — a true social worker spends himself for the good of the community. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
