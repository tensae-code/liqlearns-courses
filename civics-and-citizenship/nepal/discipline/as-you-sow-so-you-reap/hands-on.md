# Hands-on — As You Sow, So You Reap

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Think of one result in your life right now, good or bad. Trace it back: what act was the seed it grew from?

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: actions bring their own consequences — as you sow, so you reap: good acts grow good results, and careless or harmful acts grow theirs. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
