# As You Sow, So You Reap — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) actions bring their own consequences — as you sow, so you reap: good acts grow good results, and careless or harmful acts grow theirs  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Actions are seeds and consequences are the harvest — good acts grow good results, harmful acts grow theirs.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
