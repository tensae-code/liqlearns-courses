# As You Sow, So You Reap — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Actions bring their own consequences — as you sow, so you reap: good acts grow good results, and careless or harmful acts grow theirs.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Actions are seeds and consequences are the harvest — good acts grow good results, harmful acts grow theirs.
