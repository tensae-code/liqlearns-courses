# Video script — As You Sow, So You Reap

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Actions bring their own consequences — as you sow, so you reap: good acts grow good results, and careless or harmful acts grow theirs.

The lesson teaches with a farmer's picture. A farmer who sows good seed and tends it reaps a good harvest; a farmer who sows weeds, or nothing, reaps accordingly. Actions work the same way: every act is a seed, and its consequence is the harvest.

This is not a threat — it is how life is built. The person who sows honesty reaps trust; the person who sows laziness reaps want. Knowing that your harvest grows from your own sowing puts your future in your own hands.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
