# Hands-on — Inner Eyes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Close your eyes for a moment and look at your own day honestly. Name one thing you did well, and one thing you would change.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: see yourself honestly with your inner eyes — self-examination is how you know what in you must change. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
