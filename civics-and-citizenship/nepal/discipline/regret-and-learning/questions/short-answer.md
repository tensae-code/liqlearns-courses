# Regret and Learning — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Regret is a teacher — after wrongdoing, feeling regret honestly is how a person learns and turns.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Regret honestly faced teaches — it is the clear sight of a wrong act, and the start of turning from it.
