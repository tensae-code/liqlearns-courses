# Moderation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Excess in anything destroys — moderation keeps even good things good, by holding them in measure.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Excess in anything destroys — even good things turn harmful past their measure; moderation holds the measure.
