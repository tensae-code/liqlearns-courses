# Video script — Moderation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Excess in anything destroys — moderation keeps even good things good, by holding them in measure.

The book's lesson title is its whole teaching: excess of anything is destructive. Even good things — food, rest, play, speech, even work — turn harmful when they pass their measure. Moderation is the discipline of holding things at their right measure.

The moderate person is not a person of half-heartedness; they are a person of measure. They know how much is enough, and stop there — so the good things of life stay good, and nothing they love is destroyed by too much of itself.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
