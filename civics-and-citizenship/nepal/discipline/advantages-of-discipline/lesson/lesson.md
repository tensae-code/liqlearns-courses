# Advantages of Discipline

## Do it first

Name one part of your day that runs in order — and one part that does not. Say what the ordered part gains you.

## What discipline gains a person

Discipline is keeping order in your own life: doing what should be done, when it should be done, in the way it should be done. This lesson teaches its advantages — what discipline gains the person who has it.

The disciplined person wastes less time, keeps their promises, and is trusted by others. Their strength is not spent on chaos and repair, so it is free for learning, work and helping. Discipline feels like a cost at first; the lesson shows it is really a gain.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: discipline pays — the person who keeps order in their own life gains time, trust and strength for everything else. Give one real example from your own life in Nepal, and name the lesson's key words: discipline, advantages, order, gains. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Nepal's own books teach Moral Education through named people — a story first, then the behaviour it models, then discussion. When a lesson names a person, picture that person's day, not only their name: the story is the teaching.
