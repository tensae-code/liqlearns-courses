# Video script — Advantages of Discipline

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Discipline pays — the person who keeps order in their own life gains time, trust and strength for everything else.

Discipline is keeping order in your own life: doing what should be done, when it should be done, in the way it should be done. This lesson teaches its advantages — what discipline gains the person who has it.

The disciplined person wastes less time, keeps their promises, and is trusted by others. Their strength is not spent on chaos and repair, so it is free for learning, work and helping. Discipline feels like a cost at first; the lesson shows it is really a gain.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
