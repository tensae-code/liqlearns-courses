# Advantages of Discipline — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) discipline pays — the person who keeps order in their own life gains time, trust and strength for everything else  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Discipline gains a person time, trust and free strength — its advantages, not only its effort.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
