# Advantages of Discipline — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Discipline pays — the person who keeps order in their own life gains time, trust and strength for everything else.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Discipline gains a person time, trust and free strength — its advantages, not only its effort.
