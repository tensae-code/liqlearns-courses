# Video script — Becoming Good

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Becoming good is a choice you make yourself — 'I become good' starts with your own decision, not with being forced.

The book's lesson speaks in the first person: I become good. This lesson teaches that goodness is not something poured into a person from outside — it begins with the person's own choice to become good.

A choice is only the start; it must be carried by acts. But without the choice, the acts are only obedience. The learner who says 'I become good' and means it has taken the first and hardest step: deciding, themselves, what kind of person to be.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
