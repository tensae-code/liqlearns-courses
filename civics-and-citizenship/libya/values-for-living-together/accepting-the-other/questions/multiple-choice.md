# Accepting the Other — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Loving your friends whatever their colour, sex, language or people, and wanting to learn many languages and talk with the world.  b) it has nothing to do with how Libya governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Loving your friends whatever their colour, sex, language or people, and wanting to learn many languages and talk with the world.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Loving your friends whatever their colour, sex, language or people, and wanting to learn many languages and talk with the world.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Libya
**Answer: a) Loving your friends whatever their colour, sex, language or people, and wanting to learn many languages and talk with the world.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
