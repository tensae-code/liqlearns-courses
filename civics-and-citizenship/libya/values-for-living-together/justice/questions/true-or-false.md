# Justice — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The value of justice, and the difference between justice and equality — through the story of Ahmed, a pupil with Down syndrome given extra minutes in a test.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that justice — and that it has no connection to the rest of Libya's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Libya's own course, in its own order.
