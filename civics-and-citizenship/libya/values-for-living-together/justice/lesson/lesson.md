# Justice

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: justice. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Libya's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

The value of justice, and the difference between justice and equality — through the story of Ahmed, a pupil with Down syndrome given extra minutes in a test.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The value of justice, and the difference between justice and equality — through the story of Ahmed, a pupil with Down syndrome given extra minutes in a test. Give one real example from your own life or from the Libya you live in, and name the lesson's key words: justice. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
