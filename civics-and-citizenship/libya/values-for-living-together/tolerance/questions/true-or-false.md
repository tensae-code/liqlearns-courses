# Tolerance — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Tolerance is the glue between peoples: rejecting violence, extremism and hate speech, and settling quarrels — through the story of two sisters fighting over an orange.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that tolerance — and that it has no connection to the rest of Libya's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Libya's own course, in its own order.
