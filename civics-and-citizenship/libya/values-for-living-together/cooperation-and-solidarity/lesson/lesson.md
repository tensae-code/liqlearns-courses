# Cooperation and Solidarity

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: cooperation and Solidarity. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Libya's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

What solidarity and cooperation mean and how they differ, matched against scenes of help — the displaced, the soldier, the doctor, the person in a wheelchair, the fire.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: What solidarity and cooperation mean and how they differ, matched against scenes of help — the displaced, the soldier, the doctor, the person in a wheelchair, the fire. Give one real example from your own life or from the Libya you live in, and name the lesson's key words: cooperation, solidarity. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself
