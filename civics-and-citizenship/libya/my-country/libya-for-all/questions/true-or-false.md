# Libya for All — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The country’s regions and their products — the cities of the south, coast and mountains and what each makes and grows — one homeland belonging to everyone.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that libya for All — and that it has no connection to the rest of Libya's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Libya's own course, in its own order.
