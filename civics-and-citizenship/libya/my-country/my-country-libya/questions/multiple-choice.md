# My Country Libya — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Knowing your homeland: its wealth, its customs and traditions, and its cities great and small.  b) it has nothing to do with how Libya governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Knowing your homeland: its wealth, its customs and traditions, and its cities great and small.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Knowing your homeland: its wealth, its customs and traditions, and its cities great and small.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Libya
**Answer: a) Knowing your homeland: its wealth, its customs and traditions, and its cities great and small.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
