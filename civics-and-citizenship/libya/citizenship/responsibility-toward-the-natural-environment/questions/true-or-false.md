# Responsibility toward the Natural Environment — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Responsibility for the natural world, through a story of power cuts, carbon emissions and a warming climate.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that responsibility toward the Natural Environment — and that it has no connection to the rest of Libya's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Libya's own course, in its own order.
