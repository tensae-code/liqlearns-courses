# My Homeland — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The homeland as the ground of belonging.  b) it has nothing to do with how Libya governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The homeland as the ground of belonging.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) The homeland as the ground of belonging.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Libya
**Answer: a) The homeland as the ground of belonging.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
