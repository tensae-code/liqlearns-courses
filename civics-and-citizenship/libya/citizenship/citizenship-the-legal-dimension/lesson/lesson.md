# Citizenship — the Legal Dimension

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: citizenship — the Legal Dimension. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Libya's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Among the pillars of the legal dimension are holding the nationality, enjoying rights while doing duties, and equality before the law — through the case of a fourteen-year-old driving his father’s car.

Give one real example from your own life or from the Libya you live in, and name the lesson's key words: citizenship, legal. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
