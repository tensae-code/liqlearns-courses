# Citizenship — the Administrative Dimension

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: citizenship — the Administrative Dimension. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Libya's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

The administration serves the citizens and orders their lives — through the case of a pupil whose late civil registration nearly cost him his school place.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The administration serves the citizens and orders their lives — through the case of a pupil whose late civil registration nearly cost him his school place. Give one real example from your own life or from the Libya you live in, and name the lesson's key words: citizenship, administrative. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
