# Citizenship — the Administrative Dimension — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The administration serves the citizens and orders their lives — through the case of a pupil whose late civil registration nearly cost him his school place.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that citizenship — the Administrative Dimension — and that it has no connection to the rest of Libya's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Libya's own course, in its own order.
