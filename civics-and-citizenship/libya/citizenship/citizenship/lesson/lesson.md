# Citizenship

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: citizenship. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Libya's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## Sub lesson 1 — The five definitions

The book’s five definitions of citizenship.

## Sub lesson 2 — What citizenship rests on

Citizenship rests on the bond between rights and duties, on belonging and loyalty, and on the controls of the law.
