# Citizenship — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Citizenship, in the book’s five definitions, rests on the bond between rights and duties, on belonging and loyalty, and on the controls of the law.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that citizenship — and that it has no connection to the rest of Libya's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Libya's own course, in its own order.
