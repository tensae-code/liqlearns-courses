# Citizenship — the Social Dimension — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Citizenship lived socially: sorting your conduct by its sphere — family, school, society.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that citizenship — the Social Dimension — and that it has no connection to the rest of Libya's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Libya's own course, in its own order.
