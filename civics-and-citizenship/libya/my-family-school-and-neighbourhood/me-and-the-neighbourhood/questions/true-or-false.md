# Me and the Neighbourhood — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Responsibility in your quarter: helping the elderly, the disabled and the poor, respecting neighbours’ privacy and not disturbing them — the neighbourhood as a fabric of warm human relations, not just a place.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that me and the Neighbourhood — and that it has no connection to the rest of Libya's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Libya's own course, in its own order.
