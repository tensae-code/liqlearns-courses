# Me and My School — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The school is the second environment after home and a small society with its own rules — and basic education is the child’s right, free and compulsory, as the child-rights convention’s article on education provides, in summary.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that me and My School — and that it has no connection to the rest of Libya's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Libya's own course, in its own order.
