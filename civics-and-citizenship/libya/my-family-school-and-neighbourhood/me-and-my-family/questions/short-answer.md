# Me and My Family — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** The family is society’s basic group, bound by kinship: it cares, brings up, passes on values and meets material needs — and in it the child learns solidarity, cooperation, equality, rights and duties.
