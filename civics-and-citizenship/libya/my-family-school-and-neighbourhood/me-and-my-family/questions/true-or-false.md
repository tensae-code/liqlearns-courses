# Me and My Family — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The family is society’s basic group, bound by kinship: it cares, brings up, passes on values and meets material needs — and in it the child learns solidarity, cooperation, equality, rights and duties.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that me and My Family — and that it has no connection to the rest of Libya's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Libya's own course, in its own order.
