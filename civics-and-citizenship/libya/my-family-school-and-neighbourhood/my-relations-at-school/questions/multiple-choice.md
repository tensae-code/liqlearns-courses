# My Relations at School — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The school as a miniature society and a fabric of human relations, and the pupil’s conduct inside it.  b) it has nothing to do with how Libya governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The school as a miniature society and a fabric of human relations, and the pupil’s conduct inside it.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) The school as a miniature society and a fabric of human relations, and the pupil’s conduct inside it.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Libya
**Answer: a) The school as a miniature society and a fabric of human relations, and the pupil’s conduct inside it.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
