# I Protect the Environment — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) How we protect the environment: endangered animals, pollution of water, soil and air, and nature reserves.  b) it has nothing to do with how Libya governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) How we protect the environment: endangered animals, pollution of water, soil and air, and nature reserves.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) How we protect the environment: endangered animals, pollution of water, soil and air, and nature reserves.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Libya
**Answer: a) How we protect the environment: endangered animals, pollution of water, soil and air, and nature reserves.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
