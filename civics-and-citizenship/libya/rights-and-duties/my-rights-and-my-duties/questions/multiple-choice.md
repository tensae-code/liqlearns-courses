# My Rights and My Duties — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Knowing your rights, knowing your duties, and the bond between them.  b) it has nothing to do with how Libya governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Knowing your rights, knowing your duties, and the bond between them.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Knowing your rights, knowing your duties, and the bond between them.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Libya
**Answer: a) Knowing your rights, knowing your duties, and the bond between them.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
