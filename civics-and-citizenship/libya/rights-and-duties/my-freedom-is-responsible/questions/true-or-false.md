# My Freedom Is Responsible — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** What freedom means, that freedom must be responsible, and the benefits of responsible freedom.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that my Freedom Is Responsible — and that it has no connection to the rest of Libya's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Libya's own course, in its own order.
