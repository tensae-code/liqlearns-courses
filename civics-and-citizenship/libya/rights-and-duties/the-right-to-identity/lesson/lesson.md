# The Right to Identity

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: the Right to Identity. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Libya's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

The child’s right to a name and a nationality from birth, as the convention’s identity article provides, in summary.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The child’s right to a name and a nationality from birth, as the convention’s identity article provides, in summary. Give one real example from your own life or from the Libya you live in, and name the lesson's key words: right, identity. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
