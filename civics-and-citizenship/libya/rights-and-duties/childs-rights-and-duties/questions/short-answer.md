# The Child's Rights and Duties — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** A right is the power to do or not do a thing and a duty is what must be done; the 1989 Convention on the Rights of the Child is the first binding instrument joining civil, political, cultural, social and economic rights.
