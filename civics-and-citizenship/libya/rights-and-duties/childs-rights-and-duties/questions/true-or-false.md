# The Child's Rights and Duties — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A right is the power to do or not do a thing and a duty is what must be done; the 1989 Convention on the Rights of the Child is the first binding instrument joining civil, political, cultural, social and economic rights.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that the Child's Rights and Duties — and that it has no connection to the rest of Libya's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Libya's own course, in its own order.
