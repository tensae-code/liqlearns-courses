# My Responsibility toward Society

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: my Responsibility toward Society. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Libya's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

What society is made of, what responsibility toward it means, and the rules for bearing it.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: What society is made of, what responsibility toward it means, and the rules for bearing it. Give one real example from your own life or from the Libya you live in, and name the lesson's key words: responsibility, society. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: What society is made of, what responsibility toward it means, and the rules for bearing it.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
