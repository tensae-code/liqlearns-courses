# I Am a Human

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: i Am a Human. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Libya's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## Sub lesson 1 — Knowing yourself

Your name, your family, your talents and your strengths — the things that make you you.

## Sub lesson 2 — Alike and different

Each person shares some traits with others and differs in others; both are part of being human.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Knowing yourself: your name, your family, your talents and your strengths — and that each person shares some traits with others and differs in others. Give one real example from your own life or from the Libya you live in, and name the lesson's key words: i, am. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: Knowing yourself: your name, your family, your talents and your strengths — and that each person shares some traits with others and differs in others.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
