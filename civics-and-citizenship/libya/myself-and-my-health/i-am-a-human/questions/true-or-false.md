# I Am a Human — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Knowing yourself: your name, your family, your talents and your strengths — and that each person shares some traits with others and differs in others.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that i Am a Human — and that it has no connection to the rest of Libya's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Libya's own course, in its own order.
