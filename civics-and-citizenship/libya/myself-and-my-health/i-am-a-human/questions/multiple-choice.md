Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Knowing yourself: your name, your family, your talents and your strengths — and that each person shares some traits with others and differs in others.  b) it has nothing to do with how Libya governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
