# My Cleanliness — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** What cleanliness is and why it matters — at home, at school and in the street — and the responsibility each person bears for it.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** What cleanliness is and why it matters — at home, at school and in the street — and the responsibility each person bears for it.
