# My Bodily Safety

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: my Bodily Safety. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Libya's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## Sub lesson 1 — Keeping the body safe

Keeping the body safe, with traffic accidents and the seat belt as its sharpest case.

## Sub lesson 2 — Safety at a citizen’s depth

Keeping the body safe from dangers, including using modern technology without harm.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Keeping your body safe from dangers — with traffic accidents and the seat belt as the sharpest case, and modern technology used without harm. Give one real example from your own life or from the Libya you live in, and name the lesson's key words: bodily, safety. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: Keeping your body safe from dangers — with traffic accidents and the seat belt as the sharpest case, and modern technology used without harm.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
