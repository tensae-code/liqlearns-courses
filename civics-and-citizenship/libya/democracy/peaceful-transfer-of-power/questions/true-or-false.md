# Peaceful Transfer of Power — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The conditions and rules of peaceful alternation: a constitution, an election law guaranteeing integrity, free and impartial media, real political pluralism, respect for commitments, and social awareness.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that peaceful Transfer of Power — and that it has no connection to the rest of Libya's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Libya's own course, in its own order.
