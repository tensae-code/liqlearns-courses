# Peaceful Transfer of Power

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: peaceful Transfer of Power. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Libya's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

The conditions and rules of peaceful alternation: a constitution, an election law guaranteeing integrity, free and impartial media, real political pluralism, respect for commitments, and social awareness.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The conditions and rules of peaceful alternation: a constitution, an election law guaranteeing integrity, free and impartial media, real political pluralism, respect for commitments, and social awareness. Give one real example from your own life or from the Libya you live in, and name the lesson's key words: peaceful, transfer. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
