# Peaceful Transfer of Power — depth

This lesson is ONE lesson with no sub lessons: it teaches a single thing, whole. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: The conditions and rules of peaceful alternation: a constitution, an election law guaranteeing integrity, free and impartial media, real political pluralism, respect for commitments, and social awareness. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
