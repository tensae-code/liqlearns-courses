# Democracy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?

**Answer: a) What democracy is.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) What democracy is.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Libya
**Answer: a) What democracy is.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
