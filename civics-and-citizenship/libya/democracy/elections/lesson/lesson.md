# Elections

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: elections. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Libya's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## Sub lesson 1 — How an election runs

How an election runs, through the story of Ahmed’s father going to vote — the voter card, the polling station, the secrecy of the ballot.

## Sub lesson 2 — Why elections

Elections are the best mechanism for choosing a representative.
