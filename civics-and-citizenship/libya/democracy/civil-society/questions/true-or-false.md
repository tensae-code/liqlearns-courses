# Civil Society — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Civil society as part of the democratic state: it gathers all society’s components, encourages citizens to take part, and guards social peace.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that civil Society — and that it has no connection to the rest of Libya's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Libya's own course, in its own order.
