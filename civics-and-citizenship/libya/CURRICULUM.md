# Civics and Citizenship — Libya course — CURRICULUM (the path)

One course for Libya inside the Civics and Citizenship
folder, organised by **levels, not grades**: a learner climbs
when they have proved the level, not when a school calendar
says so. The school grades the books come from appear only in
each lesson's sources (provenance), never as the course's
organisation. The path follows Libya's own order as its books
lay it out — citizenship built as widening "I" statements:
the child's own person and health first, then family, school
and neighbourhood, then the environment, then the homeland,
then the values that let people live together, then rights
and duties named outright, then citizenship taken apart into
its dimensions, and at the top democracy in practice, closing
with two workshops in which the class itself votes. The
filing for this build (`libya-placements.md` — 6 records /
6 distinct books, all read in full; 53 placements in all,
from six distinct student books read) stocks 49 lessons:
the filing's coined lesson names were deduped before
building — the Grade 6 book repeats the Grade 4 health
lessons and the neighbourhood lesson at a citizen's depth,
so those parallel placements stock into the same lessons as
their parts — and every placement stocks into exactly one
lesson.

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in
this course comes from government textbooks — this country's
own National Education books, أنا المواطن (I Am the
Citizen) — except the **Tip boxes**: a tip in a box is from
us, the people making LiqLearns. Tips are ours; the lessons
are the books'. See ABOUT.md for the full book list and the
acceptance reckoning. **Every lesson in this course stands
on a student book; no lesson rests on a teacher guide.**

## The levels (the path, in order)

1. **Myself and My Health** — the course opens with the
   child's own person: I am a human, my cleanliness, my
   nutrition, my bodily safety — and the same three health
   lessons return at a citizen's depth as this level's own
   deeper parts. 4 lessons, in one title:
   `myself-and-my-health/` (4 lessons).
2. **My Family, School and Neighbourhood** — the first
   circles: family, school and neighbourhood, revisited as
   responsibilities in the family and relations at school.
   5 lessons, in one title:
   `my-family-school-and-neighbourhood/` (5 lessons).
3. **My Environment** — the natural world: what the
   environment is, and how the child protects it.
   2 lessons, in one title: `my-environment/` (2 lessons).
4. **My Country** — the turn to the homeland: my country
   Libya, and Libya for all its regions and people.
   2 lessons, in one title: `my-country/` (2 lessons).
5. **Values for Living Together** — the values of
   coexistence: equality, justice, solidarity and
   cooperation, tolerance, accepting the other, and the
   culture of dialogue, closing with difference and
   agreement. 7 lessons, in one title:
   `values-for-living-together/` (7 lessons).
6. **Rights and Duties** — rights named and bounded: my
   rights and my duties and responsible freedom, then a
   full run on the child's rights under the 1989 Convention
   on the Rights of the Child and the duties that answer
   them. 11 lessons, in one title: `rights-and-duties/`
   (11 lessons).
7. **Citizenship** — citizenship taken apart into its
   dimensions — belonging, social, administrative, legal,
   political — with freedom, voluntary work, cultural life
   and environmental responsibility. 10 lessons, in one
   title: `citizenship/` (10 lessons).
8. **Democracy** — democracy in practice: participation in
   public life, peaceful transfer of power, civil society,
   women's participation, elections, and two workshops
   where the class itself votes. 8 lessons, in one title:
   `democracy/` (8 lessons).

**Totals: 49 lessons** (Level 1: 4 · Level 2: 5 · Level 3:
2 · Level 4: 2 · Level 5: 7 · Level 6: 11 · Level 7: 10 ·
Level 8: 8 — from six read student books: the أنا المواطن
(I Am the Citizen) National Education activity books for
Grades 4–9 of Basic Education, Centre for Educational
Curricula and Pedagogical Research, Ministry of Education,
State of Libya, school year 1440–1441 AH / 2019–2020, in
Arabic, all COMPLETE as filed; 53 placements stocking 49
lessons).
