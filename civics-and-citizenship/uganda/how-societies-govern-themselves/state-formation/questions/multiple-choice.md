# State Formation in East Africa — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What makes a state centralised?
a) power is gathered in one leader and one government, as in Buganda  b) every clan rules itself with no centre at all  c) there is no leader of any kind  d) only visitors may decide matters
**Answer: a) power is gathered in one leader and one government, as in Buganda** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** How were non-centralised societies led?
a) by clans, elders and councils, with no single ruler  b) by one king and his army  c) they were not led at all  d) by colonial officials
**Answer: a) by clans, elders and councils, with no single ruler** — That is what this lesson teaches.
