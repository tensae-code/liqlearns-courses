# State Formation in East Africa — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Contrast a centralised state with a non-centralised society.
**Answer:** A centralised state gathers power in one leader and government (like Buganda under its kabaka); a non-centralised society spreads authority among clans, elders and councils with no single ruler.

**2.** Who were the Bachwezi, in this lesson?
**Answer:** An early dynasty remembered in the region's traditions as rulers whose organised realm shows state formation beginning in East Africa.
