# Mato Oput — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Sincerity — speaking the truth plainly — is one of Mato Oput's guiding principles.
**Answer: True**

**2.** After Mato Oput, the two clans remain enemies for life.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite.
