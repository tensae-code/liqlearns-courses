# Mato Oput — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What kind of justice is Mato Oput?
a) restorative justice — it restores relations between clans  b) justice that only imprisons  c) justice decided by fighting  d) justice that hides the truth
**Answer: a) restorative justice — it restores relations between clans** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does drinking together at the ceremony seal?
a) restored relations — the two clans can live together again  b) a business contract  c) a new quarrel  d) the end of all customs
**Answer: a) restored relations — the two clans can live together again** — That is what this lesson teaches.
