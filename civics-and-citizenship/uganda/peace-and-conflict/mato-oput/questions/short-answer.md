# Mato Oput — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name Mato Oput's guiding principles.
**Answer:** Respect, sincerity, forgiveness, reconciliation, and solving problems through discussion.

**2.** Why is the drink bitter, and what does sharing it mean?
**Answer:** It tastes of the bitterness of the wrong that was done; drinking together means both sides leave that bitterness behind and relations are restored.
