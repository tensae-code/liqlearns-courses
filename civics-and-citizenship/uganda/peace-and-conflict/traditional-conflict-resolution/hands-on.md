# Hands-on — Traditional Conflict Resolution

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Think of a quarrel you have seen settled without anyone going to court — elders talking, families meeting, an apology accepted. Say how it ended. This lesson names the ways peoples have done that for generations.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: East Africa's traditions settle conflict without courts first — by mediation, adjudication, reconciliation, arbitration and negotiation — and after mass violations, transitional justice rebuilds a broken society. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
