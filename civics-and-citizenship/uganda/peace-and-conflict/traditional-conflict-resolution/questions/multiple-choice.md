# Traditional Conflict Resolution — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** In mediation, who finds the agreement?
a) the two sides themselves, helped by a trusted third person  b) a judge whose ruling cannot be questioned  c) the stronger side alone  d) no one — the quarrel is left alone
**Answer: a) the two sides themselves, helped by a trusted third person** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is transitional justice for?
a) moving a society from mass violence to peace — truth, accountability, repair and rebuilt trust  b) punishing a whole people forever  c) forgetting everything that happened  d) one family's private quarrel
**Answer: a) moving a society from mass violence to peace — truth, accountability, repair and rebuilt trust** — That is what this lesson teaches.
