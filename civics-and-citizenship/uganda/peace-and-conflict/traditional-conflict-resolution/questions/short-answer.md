# Traditional Conflict Resolution — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the five traditional mechanisms and say who decides in each.
**Answer:** Mediation (sides decide, helper guides), adjudication (elders or council judge), reconciliation (sides restored to right relations), arbitration (chosen judge decides, accepted in advance), negotiation (sides bargain directly).

**2.** Why does a society need transitional justice after mass violations?
**Answer:** Because the harm is too large for ordinary settlement: truth must be told, wrongdoers held to account, harm repaired and trust rebuilt so people can live together again.
