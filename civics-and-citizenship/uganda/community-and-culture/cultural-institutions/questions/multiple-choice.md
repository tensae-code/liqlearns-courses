# Cultural Institutions — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What do cultural institutions do?
a) keep a people's culture alive — guarding customs, sites and ceremonies  b) sell land to visitors  c) replace schools  d) stop all ceremonies
**Answer: a) keep a people's culture alive — guarding customs, sites and ceremonies** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** How can culture earn a living?
a) cultural sites draw visitors, and crafts, music and guiding earn money  b) by hiding customs from everyone  c) by closing cultural sites  d) it can never earn anything
**Answer: a) cultural sites draw visitors, and crafts, music and guiding earn money** — That is what this lesson teaches.
