# Cultural Institutions — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is a cultural site, in your own words?
**Answer:** A place — such as a palace, shrine, tomb or gathering place — where a people's history and identity are kept and remembered.

**2.** Give one example of culture as work.
**Answer:** For example: guiding visitors at a cultural site, or making and selling crafts, music and dance performances.
