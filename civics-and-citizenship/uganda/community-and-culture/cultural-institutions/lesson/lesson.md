# Cultural Institutions

## Do it first

Name one custom, ceremony, craft or place in your community that visitors come to see or hear about. Keep it in mind — this lesson is about the institutions that keep such things alive.

## Cultural Institutions

Every people in East Africa keeps its culture through cultural institutions: the kingships, chiefdoms, clan leaderships and councils of elders that guard customs, settle questions of tradition and lead ceremonies. Kenya, Tanzania and Uganda each have their own, with their own names and ways.

These institutions look after cultural sites — the palaces, shrines, tombs, hills and gathering places where a people's history is kept. A site is not just an old place: it is where the community remembers who it is.

Culture is also a livelihood. Cultural sites draw visitors; crafts, music, dance and storytelling earn money; young people find work as guides, makers and performers. The lesson's point is double: respect the institutions that keep culture alive, and see that culture, cared for well, can feed the people who keep it.
