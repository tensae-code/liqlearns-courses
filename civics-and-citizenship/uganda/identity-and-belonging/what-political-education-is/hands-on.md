# Hands-on — What Political Education Is

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Say your full name out loud. Now say your clan, and where your family comes from. You have just touched the starting point of this lesson: knowing who you are is where belonging begins.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: political education is learning how to live well in a civic society — and it starts with who you are: your name, your clan, your tribe and your roots. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
