# What Political Education Is — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is political education?
**Answer:** It is learning how to live well in a civic society — what a good member of society does, following models of good leadership.

**2.** Why does this lesson start with your name, clan and roots?
**Answer:** Because civic belonging starts with identity: knowing who you are and where you come from is the ground a citizen stands on.
