# What Political Education Is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does history study?
a) the past — how people lived, moved and were ruled long ago  b) a single family's shopping list  c) only future inventions  d) the weather tomorrow
**Answer: a) the past — how people lived, moved and were ruled long ago** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does political education teach?
a) how to live well in a civic society, with good leadership as a model  b) how to win every argument  c) how to avoid other people  d) only dates and battles
**Answer: a) how to live well in a civic society, with good leadership as a model** — That is what this lesson teaches.
