# Hands-on — Roles in the Family

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name one job you do in your family. Now name one job your mother or father does, and one a grandparent or elder does. Hold the three side by side — this lesson is about how a family's work is shared.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: everyone in a family has a role — men, women and children each carry part of the family's life — and those roles are changing over time. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
