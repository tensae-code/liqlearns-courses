# Roles in the Family — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Give two examples of roles different family members carry.
**Answer:** For example: a parent provides and guides; a mother or father cooks and cares; children fetch water, help at home and learn at school — any two true examples from the lesson.

**2.** Name one way family roles are changing.
**Answer:** For example: girls and boys both go to school, women work outside the home, and men take up more care of children.
