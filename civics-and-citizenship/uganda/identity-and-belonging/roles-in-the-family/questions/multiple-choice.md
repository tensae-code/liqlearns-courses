# Roles in the Family — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Who carries roles in a family?
a) men, women and children — each carries part of the family's life  b) only the father  c) only the children  d) only visitors
**Answer: a) men, women and children — each carries part of the family's life** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is happening to family roles over time?
a) they are changing — work is shared in new ways  b) they ended long ago  c) they can never change  d) they matter less every year
**Answer: a) they are changing — work is shared in new ways** — That is what this lesson teaches.
