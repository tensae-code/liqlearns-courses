# Video script — Roles in the Family

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Everyone in a family has a role — men, women and children each carry part of the family's life — and those roles are changing over time.

In East African families, different people carry different roles. Men, women and children each have work that is theirs: providing, caring, cooking, farming, fetching, learning, guiding — the family's life is held up by all of them together.

No role stands alone. When one person's part is missing, the others feel it at once — that is why each role is respected, and why children learn their part early.

These roles are also changing. Work that once belonged to one person alone is now shared in new ways: girls and boys both go to school, women work outside the home, and men take up more of the care of children. The lesson asks learners to notice both what stays and what changes — and to value the people in every role.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
