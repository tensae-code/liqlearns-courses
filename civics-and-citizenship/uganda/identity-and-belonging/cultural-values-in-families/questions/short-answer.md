# Cultural Values in Families — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why is the family called the source of moral being?
**Answer:** Because a child first learns right from wrong, discipline and values like honesty and respect at home, before any school.

**2.** What place does marriage hold in this lesson?
**Answer:** It is an institution to be respected: it joins families, raises children in a stable home and carries traditions forward.
