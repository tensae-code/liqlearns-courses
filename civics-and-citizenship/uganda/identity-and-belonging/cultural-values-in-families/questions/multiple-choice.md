# Cultural Values in Families — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Where is moral being first learned?
a) in the family  b) at the market  c) only from books  d) from strangers
**Answer: a) in the family** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** How does this lesson teach children to be treated in family decisions?
a) as persons whose good matters and whose view deserves a hearing  b) as people who must never be told anything  c) as the family's property  d) as adults with no guidance
**Answer: a) as persons whose good matters and whose view deserves a hearing** — That is what this lesson teaches.
