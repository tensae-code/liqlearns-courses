# Hands-on — Cultural Values in Families

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Think of one rule your family lives by — something you were taught at home about how to behave. Say it out loud. This lesson is about where lessons like that come from, and why the family is their first school.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the family is where moral being and discipline are first learned — through respect for traditions and marriage, and through children's rights being heard in family decisions. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
