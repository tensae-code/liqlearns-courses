# Telling Our Story with the Map — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** In the handbook's game, what do pupils travel across?
a) the map of Slovenia  b) a foreign country  c) a river only  d) a timetable
**Answer: a) the map of Slovenia** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What happens at each stop of the journey?
a) a story of that place is told  b) nothing  c) a test is taken  d) the map is folded away
**Answer: a) a story of that place is told** — That is what this lesson teaches.
