# Telling Our Story with the Map — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, how does the map game work?
**Answer:** Pupils travel the map of Slovenia, and at each place the journey reaches, they tell that place's story.

**2.** What does the game change a map into?
**Answer:** Something you can tell — place by place, story by story — not just something you look at.
