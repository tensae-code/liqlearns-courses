# Hands-on — Telling Our Story with the Map

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Spread out an imaginary map in front of you. Put your finger on one place, then travel it to another. At each stop, ask: what story belongs to this place? Hold that game in mind as you read.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: telling our story with the map — travelling the map of Slovenia in a game, and telling the story of each place you reach. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
