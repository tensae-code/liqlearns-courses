# Slovenia Place by Place — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, how is Slovenia known in this lesson?
**Answer:** Place by place, through its own tales and legends — each place on the map with a story of its own.

**2.** Name two places from the lesson and the story or figure that belongs to each.
**Answer:** Any two true pairs from the lesson, such as Triglav–Zlatorog, Ljubljana–its dragon, Postojna–the cave, or the mountains–Kekec.
