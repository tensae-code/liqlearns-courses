# Slovenia Place by Place

## Do it first

Name as many places in Slovenia as you can — a mountain, a town, a cave, a lake. For each one, ask whether a story lives there. Hold your list as you read: this lesson walks the country through its stories.

## The country's own tales, place by place

The handbook's second half is a store of suggested stories and legends, arranged place by place, for the teacher to tell. The country is taught the way a child can love it: through its tales.

In the mountains there is Kekec, and on Triglav the tale of Zlatorog, the golden-horned chamois. Martin Krpan carries his story across the land, and Peter Klepec belongs to Osilnica. Kralj Matjaž — King Matjaž — sleeps in the tales of Koroška.

The places themselves carry stories too: the Postojna cave, Bled, Ljubljana and its dragon, the Soča, Lipica, Idrija, Cerknica, Ptuj, Celje, Pohorje and the rest of the handbook's list — each place on the map with a tale of its own, so the whole country can be travelled in stories.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Slovenia place by place — the country is known through its own tales and legends, one story for each of its places. Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood, and name the lesson's key words: tales, legends, Kekec, Zlatorog, Triglav, Martin Krpan, Postojna, Bled, Ljubljana. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
