# Lesson plan — Slovenia Place by Place

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Slovenia · **Title:** Our Homeland · **Lesson:** Slovenia Place by Place
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — Our Homeland (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Slovenia place by place — the country is known through its own tales and legends, one story for each of its places.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced). Every lesson in this course stands on teacher-handbook evidence only (see lesson/sources.md).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short, single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09).

## Before this lesson
- The lesson before it in path order — Telling Our Story with the Map — is recommended first (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Slovenia place by place — the country is known through its own tales and legends, one story for each of its places.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson's scenes are the learner's own home, school and neighbourhood.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the learner does the opening act of lesson/lesson.md before any explanation.
2. **The explanation** — the lesson's parts in order (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE: a picture of a map of Slovenia dotted with small story scenes — a boy in the mountains, a golden-horned chamois on a peak, a dragon by a river town, a cave entrance — the student points to two scenes and says which place each belongs to.
3. **Say it to your companion** — the learner explains the lesson's one thing and gives a real example (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD VIDEO: the script in video-script.md, filmed (made later, when videos are made).
4. **Questions** — from questions/: multiple-choice, true-or-false, short-answer, fill-in-the-blank, matching — served at the learner's level and teaching-mix (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** — the closing doing block (lesson/lesson.md).

## If the learner is stuck
- Can name places but no stories → pair each place with its tale from the lesson, one by one: Triglav–Zlatorog, Ljubljana–the dragon, Postojna–the cave.
- Go back to the one thing: Slovenia place by place — the country is known through its own tales and legends, one story for each of its places. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
- This is the last lesson of the course as built; its next door is the learner's own telling — retell the country's stories from the map, start to finish.
