# Hands-on — Slovenia Place by Place

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name as many places in Slovenia as you can — a mountain, a town, a cave, a lake. For each one, ask whether a story lives there. Hold your list as you read: this lesson walks the country through its stories.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Slovenia place by place — the country is known through its own tales and legends, one story for each of its places. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
