# Civics and Citizenship — Slovenia course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

Civics belongs to the country: this course is Slovenia's own —
built from Slovenia's *Slovenija, moja domovina — Priročnik*
(Slovenia, My Homeland — Handbook), the teacher handbook for
Domovinska in državljanska kultura in etika (Homeland and Civic
Culture and Ethics) in the first triad of primary school.
Inside the course the organisation is by **levels, not
grades**: the titles below sit inside Level 1, in path order —
what the learner proves, step by step. The grade the book
comes from lives only in each lesson's sources.

The path's own logic is the handbook's: identity and home
first, the state's symbols and days next, the whole country
last — homeland and civic culture taught to the youngest pupils
as first steps into the world of feelings. That ladder IS the
path order (recommended, never forced — a learner can jump in
higher; the "start here instead" hints point down this ladder).

## The titles

**Level 1 — first steps into the homeland** (*Slovenija, moja
domovina* teacher handbook; teacher-handbook evidence only
throughout):

- `this-is-us/` — Level 1, first title. Who We Are (the class
  as a community and the groups that join us) · Here We Are at
  Home (our place on the map of Slovenia, our school, the
  Slovenian language and the bilingual areas).
- `our-symbols/` — Level 1, second title. Our Anthem (what a
  hymn is, and that France Prešeren wrote Slovenia's, learned
  by listening and singing) · Our Flag and Coat of Arms (the
  flag's colours and coat of arms, and how the flag flies on
  holidays).
- `our-holidays/` — Level 1, third title. Prešeren Day
  (8 February, the Slovenian cultural holiday) · Statehood Day
  (25 June — Slovenia is a young state with its own birthday) ·
  Independence and Unity Day (26 December — how Slovenians
  decided for their own state) · We Celebrate (what a holiday
  is, and how holidays are marked at home and at school).
- `our-homeland/` — Level 1, fourth title. Telling Our Story
  with the Map (a didactic game — travel the country and tell
  the stories of its places) · Slovenia Place by Place (the
  country through its own tales and legends — Kekec, Zlatorog
  on Triglav, Martin Krpan, the Postojna cave, Bled,
  Ljubljana's dragon and the other regional stories).
