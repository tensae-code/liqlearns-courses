# About the Civics and Citizenship — Slovenia course

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in this
course comes from government textbooks — this country's own
Domovinska in državljanska kultura in etika (Homeland and Civic
Culture and Ethics) handbook for the first triad of primary
school. The one exception is the **Tip boxes**: a tip in a box
is from us, the people making LiqLearns. Tips are ours; the
lessons are the books'.

## How this course is organised

This is ONE course for Slovenia inside the Civics and
Citizenship folder (one course per country). Inside it, the
organisation is by **levels, not grades**: a learner's place is
found by what they prove, and each level stands on the ones
before it as a recommended-first path — never a forced gate.
The school grade the book comes from appears only in each
lesson's `lesson/sources.md`, as provenance.

The course as built has **one level**:

1. **Level 1 — first steps into the homeland.** Slovenia's only
   civics item in hand teaches homeland and civic culture to the
   youngest pupils as "first steps into the world of feelings":
   who we are and where we are at home, then the state's symbols
   (anthem, flag and coat of arms), then the year's Slovenian
   holidays (Prešeren Day, Statehood Day, Independence and Unity
   Day, and celebrating itself), and last the whole country
   known place by place through its own tales and legends. All
   ten lessons stand on a **teacher handbook alone** — the pupil
   booklet it serves is not in hand — so every lesson is
   labelled TEACHER throughout and counts for **0** in the
   acceptance reckoning below.

**Course-intro video script** (written here, made later; the
book's own shape, walked in order):

- *First steps into the homeland:* "Start with who we are and
  where we are at home. Then our symbols — the anthem, the flag
  and the coat of arms. Then our holidays through the year.
  Last, travel the whole country in its stories, place by
  place."
