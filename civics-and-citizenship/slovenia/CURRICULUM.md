# Civics and Citizenship — Slovenia course — CURRICULUM (the path)

One course for Slovenia inside the Civics and Citizenship
folder, organised by **levels, not grades**: a learner climbs
when they have proved the level, not when a school calendar
says so. The school grade the book comes from appears only in
each lesson's sources (provenance), never as the course's
organisation. The path follows the handbook's own order —
identity and home first, the state's symbols and days next, the
whole country last, exactly as the placements file's ladder
sketch lays it out.

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in this
course comes from government textbooks — this country's own
Domovinska in državljanska kultura in etika (Homeland and Civic
Culture and Ethics) handbook — except the **Tip boxes**: a tip
in a box is from us, the people making LiqLearns. Tips are
ours; the lessons are the books'. See ABOUT.md for the full
book list, the teacher-handbook-only footing of this level, and
the acceptance reckoning.

## The levels (the path, in order)

1. **First Steps into the Homeland** — who we are and where we
   are at home; our symbols — the anthem, the flag and the coat
   of arms; our holidays — Prešeren Day (8 February), Statehood
   Day (25 June), Independence and Unity Day (26 December),
   and celebrating itself; and our homeland known place by
   place through its stories, ending in the map game and the
   country's tales. 10 lessons, in four titles: `this-is-us/`
   (2 lessons) then `our-symbols/` (2 lessons) then
   `our-holidays/` (4 lessons) then `our-homeland/`
   (2 lessons). *(Teacher-handbook evidence only — every
   lesson's sources say so; the pupil booklet is not in hand.)*

**Totals: 10 lessons** (Level 1: 10 — all from one teacher
handbook, acceptance 0).
