# Hands-on — Here We Are at Home

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Think of the place where you live. Picture your home, then your school, then the streets between them. Now picture that whole place as one small part of a bigger country. Hold that picture as you read.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: where we are at home — our place on the map of Slovenia, our school, and the Slovenian language we speak. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
