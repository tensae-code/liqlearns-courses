# Video script — Here We Are at Home

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Where we are at home — our place on the map of slovenia, our school, and the slovenian language we speak.

Every pupil is at home somewhere: in a place that has a name, that stands somewhere on the map of Slovenia. Finding your place on the map is finding where you are at home.

Our school is part of our place — the building where our class meets, in our own town or village. School is one of the first parts of the homeland a child can point to.

At home and at school we speak Slovenian. The handbook also names the bilingual areas — the parts of the country where children grow up with two languages side by side. Language is part of where we are at home.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
