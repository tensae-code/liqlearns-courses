# Here We Are at Home

## Do it first

Think of the place where you live. Picture your home, then your school, then the streets between them. Now picture that whole place as one small part of a bigger country. Hold that picture as you read.

## Our place, our school, our language

Every pupil is at home somewhere: in a place that has a name, that stands somewhere on the map of Slovenia. Finding your place on the map is finding where you are at home.

Our school is part of our place — the building where our class meets, in our own town or village. School is one of the first parts of the homeland a child can point to.

At home and at school we speak Slovenian. The handbook also names the bilingual areas — the parts of the country where children grow up with two languages side by side. Language is part of where we are at home.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: where we are at home — our place on the map of Slovenia, our school, and the Slovenian language we speak. Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood, and name the lesson's key words: home, place, map, school, Slovenian language, bilingual areas. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: where we are at home — our place on the map of Slovenia, our school, and the Slovenian language we speak.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
