# Hands-on — Who We Are

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Look around your class or your group. Name the people you learn with, one by one. Now name the groups you belong to — your class, your family, your place. Hold this in mind as you read: this lesson is about who we are, together.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: who we are — we belong to a class and to the groups that join us, and together we are part of Slovenia. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
