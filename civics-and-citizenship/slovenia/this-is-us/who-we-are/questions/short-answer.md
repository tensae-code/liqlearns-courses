# Who We Are — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, who are we?
**Answer:** We are the people in our class and our groups — each of us ourselves, and all of us together part of Slovenia.

**2.** Name two groups you belong to.
**Answer:** Any two real groups from the learner's own life, such as their class and their family, or their family and their place.
