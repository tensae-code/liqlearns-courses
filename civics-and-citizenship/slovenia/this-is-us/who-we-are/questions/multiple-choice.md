# Who We Are — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is a class, in this lesson?
a) a community of children and their teacher who learn together  b) a single child alone  c) a map of a country  d) a holiday
**Answer: a) a community of children and their teacher who learn together** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What joins us to other people?
a) the groups we belong to  b) staying apart  c) forgetting names  d) singing alone
**Answer: a) the groups we belong to** — That is what this lesson teaches.
