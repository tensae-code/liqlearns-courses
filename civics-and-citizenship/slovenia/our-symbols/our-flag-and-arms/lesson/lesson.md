# Our Flag and Coat of Arms

## Do it first

Picture a flag flying above a school on a special day. Look at it in your mind: its colours, and the small shield pictured on it. Hold that picture as you read.

## The flag and the shield on it

A country's flag is a sign everyone can read from far away: these colours, in this order, mean this country. The Slovenian flag has its own colours, known to every pupil.

On the flag sits the coat of arms — a small shield with the country's own picture on it. The arms are the country's mark, carried on the flag and used wherever the state shows itself.

The handbook teaches when the flag flies: on holidays the flag goes up at school and at home, so the country's special days can be seen as well as known.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: our flag — the Slovenian flag carries its own colours and the coat of arms, and it flies on the country's holidays. Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood, and name the lesson's key words: flag, colours, coat of arms, shield, holidays. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
