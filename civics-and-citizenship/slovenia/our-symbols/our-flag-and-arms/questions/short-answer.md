# Our Flag and Coat of Arms — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is a coat of arms?
**Answer:** A small shield with the country's own picture on it — the country's mark, carried on the flag.

**2.** Why does the flag go up on holidays?
**Answer:** So the country's special days can be seen as well as known — the flying flag marks the day.
