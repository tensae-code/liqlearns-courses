# Hands-on — Our Flag and Coat of Arms

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Picture a flag flying above a school on a special day. Look at it in your mind: its colours, and the small shield pictured on it. Hold that picture as you read.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: our flag — the Slovenian flag carries its own colours and the coat of arms, and it flies on the country's holidays. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
