# Our Anthem — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is an anthem?
a) a country's own song  b) a school bell  c) a folk dance only  d) a map
**Answer: a) a country's own song** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Who wrote Slovenia's anthem?
a) France Prešeren  b) a visiting singer  c) the class teacher  d) no one knows
**Answer: a) France Prešeren** — That is what this lesson teaches.
