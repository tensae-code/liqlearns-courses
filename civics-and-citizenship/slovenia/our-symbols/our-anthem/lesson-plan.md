**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — Our Symbols (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** our anthem — a hymn is a country's own song, and Slovenia's was written by the poet France Prešeren.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced). Every lesson in this course stands on teacher-handbook evidence only (see lesson/sources.md).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: our anthem — a hymn is a country's own song, and Slovenia's was written by the poet France Prešeren.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson's scenes are the learner's own home, school and neighbourhood.

- Thinks an anthem is any favourite song → ask who the song belongs to: one singer, or the whole country?
- Go back to the one thing: our anthem — a hymn is a country's own song, and Slovenia's was written by the poet France Prešeren. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
