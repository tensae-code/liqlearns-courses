# Our Anthem

## Do it first

Stand up straight, the way people stand when a country's song is played. Think of a song that belongs not to one person but to a whole country. Hold that thought as you read.

## A song that belongs to the whole country

A hymn — an anthem — is a special song: the song of a whole country. People stand for it, listen to it, and sing it together at the country's important moments.

Slovenia's anthem was written by France Prešeren, Slovenia's greatest poet. The words the whole country sings come from his poetry.

The handbook teaches the anthem the way young pupils meet it: first listening to it, then singing it — learning by ear and by voice what the country's song is.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: our anthem — a hymn is a country's own song, and Slovenia's was written by the poet France Prešeren. Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood, and name the lesson's key words: anthem, hymn, France Prešeren, listening, singing. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
