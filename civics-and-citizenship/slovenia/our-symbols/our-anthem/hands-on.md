# Hands-on — Our Anthem

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Stand up straight, the way people stand when a country's song is played. Think of a song that belongs not to one person but to a whole country. Hold that thought as you read.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: our anthem — a hymn is a country's own song, and Slovenia's was written by the poet France Prešeren. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
