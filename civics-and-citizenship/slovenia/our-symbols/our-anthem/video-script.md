# Video script — Our Anthem

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Our anthem — a hymn is a country's own song, and slovenia's was written by the poet france prešeren.

A hymn — an anthem — is a special song: the song of a whole country. People stand for it, listen to it, and sing it together at the country's important moments.

Slovenia's anthem was written by France Prešeren, Slovenia's greatest poet. The words the whole country sings come from his poetry.

The handbook teaches the anthem the way young pupils meet it: first listening to it, then singing it — learning by ear and by voice what the country's song is.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
