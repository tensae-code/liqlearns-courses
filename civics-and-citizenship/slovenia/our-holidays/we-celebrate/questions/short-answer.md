# We Celebrate — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is celebrating?
**Answer:** Marking a special day in its own ways — gathering, singing, programmes, the flag — to show that the day matters.

**2.** Give one way a holiday is marked at school and one way at home.
**Answer:** At school: a programme, singing or the flag flying. At home: the family gathering and preparing special food. Any real pair from the lesson is right.
