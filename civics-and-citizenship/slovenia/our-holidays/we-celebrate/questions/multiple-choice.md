# We Celebrate — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is a holiday?
a) a special day set apart from ordinary days  b) any ordinary school day  c) a day with no meaning  d) a long night
**Answer: a) a special day set apart from ordinary days** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Where are holidays marked, in this lesson?
a) at home and at school  b) nowhere  c) only abroad  d) only in books
**Answer: a) at home and at school** — That is what this lesson teaches.
