# Video script — We Celebrate

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Celebrating — a holiday is a special day set apart, and holidays are marked at home and at school in their own ways.

A holiday is a day set apart from ordinary days. Work and lessons stop or change, and the day is given to what it honours — a person, a day in the country's story, or a season of the year.

Holidays are marked at home and at school, each in its own way: families gather and prepare special food; schools hold programmes, sing, and fly the flag. The same day can be celebrated in both places, differently.

The handbook's unit on celebrating gathers the year's days together: after learning the holidays one by one, the pupil learns what celebrating itself is — how a community shows that a day matters.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
