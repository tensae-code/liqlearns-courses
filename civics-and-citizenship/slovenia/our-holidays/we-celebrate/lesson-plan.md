# Lesson plan — We Celebrate

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Slovenia · **Title:** Our Holidays · **Lesson:** We Celebrate
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — Our Holidays (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** celebrating — a holiday is a special day set apart, and holidays are marked at home and at school in their own ways.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced). Every lesson in this course stands on teacher-handbook evidence only (see lesson/sources.md).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. ## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: celebrating — a holiday is a special day set apart, and holidays are marked at home and at school in their own ways.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson's scenes are the learner's own home, school and neighbourhood.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks a holiday is only a day off → ask what the day honours and how people show it matters, at home and at school.
- Go back to the one thing: celebrating — a holiday is a special day set apart, and holidays are marked at home and at school in their own ways. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
