# Statehood Day

## Do it first

Think of your own birthday: the day you count your years from. Now think of a country having a birthday of its own. Hold that thought as you read.

## The young state's birthday

25 June is Statehood Day — dan državnosti. It is the birthday of the Slovenian state: the day Slovenia counts its life as its own state from.

Slovenia is a young state. The grown-ups around a young pupil are older than the state itself — the country in its present form began within living memory.

The handbook teaches this day simply, the way a young child can hold it: our country has a birthday, and on 25 June we mark the day our state began.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: 25 June is Statehood Day — Slovenia is a young state, and this is its birthday. Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood, and name the lesson's key words: 25 June, Statehood Day, young state, birthday. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
