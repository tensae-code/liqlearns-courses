# Statehood Day — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is Statehood Day?
**Answer:** 25 June — the birthday of the Slovenian state, the day Slovenia counts its life as its own state from.

**2.** Why can we say Slovenia is a young state?
**Answer:** Because its present form began within living memory — many grown-ups are older than the state itself.
