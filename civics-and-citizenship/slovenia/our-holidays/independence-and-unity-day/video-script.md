# Video script — Independence and Unity Day

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
26 december is independence and unity day — the day slovenians decided, together, for their own state.

26 December is Independence and Unity Day — dan samostojnosti in enotnosti. It remembers how Slovenians decided for their own state.

The day's name carries two things: independence — standing as our own state — and unity — the people deciding together, as one. The decision was shared, and the day honours the sharing as much as the decision.

The handbook places this December day beside the June birthday of the state: first the people decide together, then the state begins — and a young pupil learns both days as one story in two parts.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
