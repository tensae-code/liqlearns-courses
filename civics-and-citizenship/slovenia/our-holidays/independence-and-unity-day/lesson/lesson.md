# Independence and Unity Day

## Do it first

Think of a moment when a whole group decides something together — everyone asked, everyone answering, one decision made by all. Hold that picture as you read.

## The day of the shared decision

26 December is Independence and Unity Day — dan samostojnosti in enotnosti. It remembers how Slovenians decided for their own state.

The day's name carries two things: independence — standing as our own state — and unity — the people deciding together, as one. The decision was shared, and the day honours the sharing as much as the decision.

The handbook places this December day beside the June birthday of the state: first the people decide together, then the state begins — and a young pupil learns both days as one story in two parts.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: 26 December is Independence and Unity Day — the day Slovenians decided, together, for their own state. Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood, and name the lesson's key words: 26 December, independence, unity, decision, own state. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
