# Independence and Unity Day — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is Independence and Unity Day?
**Answer:** 26 December — the day that remembers how Slovenians decided, together and as one, for their own state.

**2.** What two things does the day's name carry?
**Answer:** Independence — standing as our own state — and unity — the people deciding together.
