# Independence and Unity Day — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** When is Independence and Unity Day?
a) 26 December  b) 25 June  c) 8 February  d) 1 May
**Answer: a) 26 December** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does the day remember?
a) Slovenians deciding together for their own state  b) a poet's birthday  c) the start of school  d) a harvest
**Answer: a) Slovenians deciding together for their own state** — That is what this lesson teaches.
