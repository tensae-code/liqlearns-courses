# Hands-on — Prešeren Day

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Think of a day when your school does not do ordinary lessons but does something special — a performance, poems, songs. Ask yourself what that day is honouring. Hold that question as you read.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: 8 February is Slovenia's cultural holiday — the day the country honours France Prešeren, its greatest poet, and celebrates its culture. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
