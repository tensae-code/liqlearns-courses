# Prešeren Day

## Do it first

Think of a day when your school does not do ordinary lessons but does something special — a performance, poems, songs. Ask yourself what that day is honouring. Hold that question as you read.

## The day of the poet and of culture

8 February is the Slovenian cultural holiday — Prešeren Day. It is named for France Prešeren, the poet who wrote Slovenia's anthem and whom Slovenia honours as its greatest poet.

A cultural holiday celebrates culture itself: poems, stories, songs, theatre — the things a people makes and passes on. On this day schools and towns mark the day with cultural programmes.

The handbook puts this day among the year's holidays a young pupil learns first: the country stops ordinary work to honour the man of words, and the words themselves.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: 8 February is Slovenia's cultural holiday — the day the country honours France Prešeren, its greatest poet, and celebrates its culture. Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood, and name the lesson's key words: 8 February, cultural holiday, Prešeren, culture, poetry. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
