# Prešeren Day — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is Prešeren Day?
**Answer:** Slovenia's cultural holiday on 8 February, honouring France Prešeren and celebrating Slovenian culture.

**2.** How is culture celebrated on this day?
**Answer:** With cultural programmes — poems, stories, songs and performances at school and in towns.
