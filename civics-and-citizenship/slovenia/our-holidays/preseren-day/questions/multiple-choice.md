# Prešeren Day — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** When is Prešeren Day?
a) 8 February  b) 25 June  c) 26 December  d) 1 January
**Answer: a) 8 February** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does the cultural holiday celebrate?
a) Slovenia's culture and its greatest poet  b) only the weather  c) a sports match  d) the end of winter only
**Answer: a) Slovenia's culture and its greatest poet** — That is what this lesson teaches.
