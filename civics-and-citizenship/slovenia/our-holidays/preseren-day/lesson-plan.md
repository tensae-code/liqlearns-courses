# Lesson plan — Prešeren Day

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Slovenia · **Title:** Our Holidays · **Lesson:** Prešeren Day
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — Our Holidays (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** 8 February is Slovenia's cultural holiday — the day the country honours France Prešeren, its greatest poet, and celebrates its culture.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced). Every lesson in this course stands on teacher-handbook evidence only (see lesson/sources.md).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. ## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: 8 February is Slovenia's cultural holiday — the day the country honours France Prešeren, its greatest poet, and celebrates its culture.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson's scenes are the learner's own home, school and neighbourhood.

- Mixes this day with the state's birthday → ask who the day honours: a poet and culture itself, not the founding of the state.
- Go back to the one thing: 8 February is Slovenia's cultural holiday — the day the country honours France Prešeren, its greatest poet, and celebrates its culture. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
