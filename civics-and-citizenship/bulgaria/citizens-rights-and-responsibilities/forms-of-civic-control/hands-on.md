# Hands-on — Forms of Civic Control

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Follow one decision by your municipality or school. List every form of civic control a citizen could use on it, in order from lightest to strongest.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: citizens control power between elections too: by voting, media scrutiny, petitions, protest, courts, transparency requests and organised watching. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
