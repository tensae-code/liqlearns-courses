# Forms of Civic Control — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Citizens control power between elections too: by voting, media scrutiny, petitions, protest, courts, transparency requests and organised watching.

**2.** What does the part "Organised watching" teach?
**Answer:** Control works best organised: associations, media and initiatives that follow budgets, decisions and promises, publish what they find and keep asking until answered.
