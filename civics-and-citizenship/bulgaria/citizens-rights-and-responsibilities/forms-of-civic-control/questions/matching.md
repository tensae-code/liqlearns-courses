# Forms of Civic Control — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** Control at and between elections — a)

**2.** Organised watching — b)


Meanings:
a) Elections are the great control, but not the only one: citizens also control power through questions, petitions, lawful protest, court actions and demands for public information.
b) Control works best organised: associations, media and initiatives that follow budgets, decisions and promises, publish what they find and keep asking until answered.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
