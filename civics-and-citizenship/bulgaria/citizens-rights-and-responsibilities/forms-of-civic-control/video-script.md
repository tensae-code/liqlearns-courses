# Video script — Forms of Civic Control

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Citizens control power between elections too: by voting, media scrutiny, petitions, protest, courts, transparency requests and organised watching.

Control at and between elections. Elections are the great control, but not the only one: citizens also control power through questions, petitions, lawful protest, court actions and demands for public information.

Organised watching. Control works best organised: associations, media and initiatives that follow budgets, decisions and promises, publish what they find and keep asking until answered.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
