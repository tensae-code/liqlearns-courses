# Video script — Me and My Civic Position

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A civic position is a stance a citizen takes on public life — formed from knowledge and values, stated openly and defended with reasons.

Forming a civic position. A position is built, not inherited whole: learn the facts, weigh the values at stake, hear the other side, then stand somewhere you can defend.

Stating and defending it. The citizen states their position openly, argues it with reasons, listens to counter-arguments and may change it when the evidence changes — firmness is not the same as deafness.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
