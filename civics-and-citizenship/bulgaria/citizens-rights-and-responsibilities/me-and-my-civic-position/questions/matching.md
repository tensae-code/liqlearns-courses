# Me and My Civic Position — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** Forming a civic position — a)

**2.** Stating and defending it — b)


Meanings:
a) A position is built, not inherited whole: learn the facts, weigh the values at stake, hear the other side, then stand somewhere you can defend.
b) The citizen states their position openly, argues it with reasons, listens to counter-arguments and may change it when the evidence changes — firmness is not the same as deafness.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
