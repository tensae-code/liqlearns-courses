# Me and My Civic Position — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) a civic position is a stance a citizen takes on public life — formed from knowledge and values, stated openly and defended with reasons  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — Forming a civic position?
a) A position is built, not inherited whole: learn the facts, weigh the values at stake, hear the other side, then stand somewhere you can defend.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
