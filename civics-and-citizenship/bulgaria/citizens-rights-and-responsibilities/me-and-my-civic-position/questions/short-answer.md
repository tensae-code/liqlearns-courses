# Me and My Civic Position — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** A civic position is a stance a citizen takes on public life — formed from knowledge and values, stated openly and defended with reasons.

**2.** What does the part "Stating and defending it" teach?
**Answer:** The citizen states their position openly, argues it with reasons, listens to counter-arguments and may change it when the evidence changes — firmness is not the same as deafness.
