# Video script — Private and Public Spheres

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Life has a private sphere the state must not enter and a public sphere we share — and the two constantly shape each other.

The private and the public. The private sphere is home, family, conscience and personal life, protected from intrusion. The public sphere is the shared world of streets, schools, media and politics, open to all.

How the spheres interact. Private life forms the people who act in public; public decisions reach back into private life. Drawing the line — what the state may regulate and what it must leave alone — is permanent democratic work.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
