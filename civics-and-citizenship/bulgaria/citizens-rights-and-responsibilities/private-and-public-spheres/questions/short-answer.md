# Private and Public Spheres — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Life has a private sphere the state must not enter and a public sphere we share — and the two constantly shape each other.

**2.** What does the part "How the spheres interact" teach?
**Answer:** Private life forms the people who act in public; public decisions reach back into private life. Drawing the line — what the state may regulate and what it must leave alone — is permanent democratic work.
