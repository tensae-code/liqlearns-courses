# Private and Public Spheres — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) life has a private sphere the state must not enter and a public sphere we share — and the two constantly shape each other  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — The private and the public?
a) The private sphere is home, family, conscience and personal life, protected from intrusion. The public sphere is the shared world of streets, schools, media and politics, open to all.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
