# Private and Public Spheres — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** The private and the public — a)

**2.** How the spheres interact — b)


Meanings:
a) The private sphere is home, family, conscience and personal life, protected from intrusion. The public sphere is the shared world of streets, schools, media and politics, open to all.
b) Private life forms the people who act in public; public decisions reach back into private life. Drawing the line — what the state may regulate and what it must leave alone — is permanent democratic work.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
