# Hands-on — Private and Public Spheres

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
List five parts of your day. Mark each private or public, then find one where a public rule reaches into private life and judge whether it should.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: life has a private sphere the state must not enter and a public sphere we share — and the two constantly shape each other. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
