# Private and Public Spheres

## Do it first

List five parts of your day. Mark each private or public, then find one where a public rule reaches into private life and judge whether it should.

## Sub lesson 1 — The private and the public

The private sphere is home, family, conscience and personal life, protected from intrusion. The public sphere is the shared world of streets, schools, media and politics, open to all.

## Sub lesson 2 — How the spheres interact

Private life forms the people who act in public; public decisions reach back into private life. Drawing the line — what the state may regulate and what it must leave alone — is permanent democratic work.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: life has a private sphere the state must not enter and a public sphere we share — and the two constantly shape each other. Give one real example from your own life in Bulgaria, and name the lesson's key words: private sphere, public sphere, privacy, state. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Bulgarian as well as in English — Bulgaria's own book teaches Гражданско образование (Civic Education) in Bulgarian, for life in Bulgarian society, not only for the classroom.
