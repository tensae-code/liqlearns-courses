# Mechanisms Protecting Human Rights — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Rights protect people only where mechanisms enforce them: courts, ombudsmen, treaty bodies and commissions that hear complaints and order repair.

**2.** What does the part "International mechanisms" teach?
**Answer:** Beyond the state stand the international mechanisms: treaty committees and the European Court of Human Rights, reached when national protection has been tried and has failed.
