# Mechanisms Protecting Human Rights — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** National mechanisms — a)

**2.** International mechanisms — b)


Meanings:
a) At home the citizen turns to the courts, the ombudsman and the bodies that guard equality and rights — each with its own door, procedure and power to repair a violation.
b) Beyond the state stand the international mechanisms: treaty committees and the European Court of Human Rights, reached when national protection has been tried and has failed.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
