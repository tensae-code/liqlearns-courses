# Mechanisms Protecting Human Rights — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) rights protect people only where mechanisms enforce them: courts, ombudsmen, treaty bodies and commissions that hear complaints and order repair  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — National mechanisms?
a) At home the citizen turns to the courts, the ombudsman and the bodies that guard equality and rights — each with its own door, procedure and power to repair a violation.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
