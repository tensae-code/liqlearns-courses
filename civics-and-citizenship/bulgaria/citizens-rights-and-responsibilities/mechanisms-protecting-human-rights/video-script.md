# Video script — Mechanisms Protecting Human Rights

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Rights protect people only where mechanisms enforce them: courts, ombudsmen, treaty bodies and commissions that hear complaints and order repair.

National mechanisms. At home the citizen turns to the courts, the ombudsman and the bodies that guard equality and rights — each with its own door, procedure and power to repair a violation.

International mechanisms. Beyond the state stand the international mechanisms: treaty committees and the European Court of Human Rights, reached when national protection has been tried and has failed.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
