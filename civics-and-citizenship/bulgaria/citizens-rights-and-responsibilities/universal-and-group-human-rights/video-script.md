# Video script — Universal and Group Human Rights

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Some human rights belong to every person simply as a person, and some protect people as members of groups — both kinds are human rights.

Universal human rights. Universal rights — life, freedom, dignity, equality before the law — belong to every human being everywhere, by birth, not by grant of any state.

Collective and group-specific rights. Some rights protect people together: minorities' language and culture, peoples' self-determination, a healthy environment. They guard what a person can only hold with their group.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
