# Universal and Group Human Rights — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Some human rights belong to every person simply as a person, and some protect people as members of groups — both kinds are human rights.

**2.** What does the part "Collective and group-specific rights" teach?
**Answer:** Some rights protect people together: minorities' language and culture, peoples' self-determination, a healthy environment. They guard what a person can only hold with their group.
