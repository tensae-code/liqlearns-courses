# Universal and Group Human Rights — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** Universal human rights — a)

**2.** Collective and group-specific rights — b)


Meanings:
a) Universal rights — life, freedom, dignity, equality before the law — belong to every human being everywhere, by birth, not by grant of any state.
b) Some rights protect people together: minorities' language and culture, peoples' self-determination, a healthy environment. They guard what a person can only hold with their group.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
