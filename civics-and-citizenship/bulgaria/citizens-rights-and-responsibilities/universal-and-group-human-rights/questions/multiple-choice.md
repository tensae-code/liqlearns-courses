# Universal and Group Human Rights — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) some human rights belong to every person simply as a person, and some protect people as members of groups — both kinds are human rights  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — Universal human rights?
a) Universal rights — life, freedom, dignity, equality before the law — belong to every human being everywhere, by birth, not by grant of any state.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
