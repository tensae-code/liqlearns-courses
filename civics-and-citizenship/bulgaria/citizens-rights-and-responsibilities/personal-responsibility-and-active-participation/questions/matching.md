# Personal Responsibility and Active Participation — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** Personal responsibility — a)

**2.** Active participation as responsibility lived — b)


Meanings:
a) Responsibility means owning our share: our acts, our omissions and their effects on the shared life. The responsible citizen asks what is mine to do before asking whose fault it is.
b) Participation is responsibility in action — joining, helping, watching, voting and speaking. Where citizens withdraw, public life is left to whoever does not.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
