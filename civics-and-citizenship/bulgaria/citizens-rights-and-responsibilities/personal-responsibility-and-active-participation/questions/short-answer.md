# Personal Responsibility and Active Participation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Public life improves where citizens accept personal responsibility for it and take part actively instead of waiting for others.

**2.** What does the part "Active participation as responsibility lived" teach?
**Answer:** Participation is responsibility in action — joining, helping, watching, voting and speaking. Where citizens withdraw, public life is left to whoever does not.
