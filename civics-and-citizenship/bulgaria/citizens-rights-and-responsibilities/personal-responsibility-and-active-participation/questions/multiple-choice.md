# Personal Responsibility and Active Participation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) public life improves where citizens accept personal responsibility for it and take part actively instead of waiting for others  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — Personal responsibility?
a) Responsibility means owning our share: our acts, our omissions and their effects on the shared life. The responsible citizen asks what is mine to do before asking whose fault it is.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
