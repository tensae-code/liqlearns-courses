# Video script — Personal Responsibility and Active Participation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Public life improves where citizens accept personal responsibility for it and take part actively instead of waiting for others.

Personal responsibility. Responsibility means owning our share: our acts, our omissions and their effects on the shared life. The responsible citizen asks what is mine to do before asking whose fault it is.

Active participation as responsibility lived. Participation is responsibility in action — joining, helping, watching, voting and speaking. Where citizens withdraw, public life is left to whoever does not.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
