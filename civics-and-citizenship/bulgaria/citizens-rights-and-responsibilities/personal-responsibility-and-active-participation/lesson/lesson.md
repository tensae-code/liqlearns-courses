# Personal Responsibility and Active Participation

## Do it first

Name one public problem you usually blame on others. Write your share of it honestly, then one act of participation that is yours to do this month.

## Sub lesson 1 — Personal responsibility

Responsibility means owning our share: our acts, our omissions and their effects on the shared life. The responsible citizen asks what is mine to do before asking whose fault it is.

## Sub lesson 2 — Active participation as responsibility lived

Participation is responsibility in action — joining, helping, watching, voting and speaking. Where citizens withdraw, public life is left to whoever does not.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: public life improves where citizens accept personal responsibility for it and take part actively instead of waiting for others. Give one real example from your own life in Bulgaria, and name the lesson's key words: responsibility, participation, public life, initiative. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Bulgarian as well as in English — Bulgaria's own book teaches Гражданско образование (Civic Education) in Bulgarian, for life in Bulgarian society, not only for the classroom.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: public life improves where citizens accept personal responsibility for it and take part actively instead of waiting for others.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
