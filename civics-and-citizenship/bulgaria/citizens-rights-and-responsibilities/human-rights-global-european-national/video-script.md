# Video script — Human Rights — Global, European and National

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Human rights are realised at three levels at once: global declarations and treaties, european instruments and courts, and the national constitution and laws.

The global and European levels. At the global level the UN declarations and treaties name the rights; at the European level the Council of Europe and the European Union give them courts and instruments a citizen can actually reach.

The national level. At home, the constitution and laws turn the same rights into daily protection — in Bulgaria's courts, administration and public life. The levels support each other; none replaces the citizen's own claim.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
