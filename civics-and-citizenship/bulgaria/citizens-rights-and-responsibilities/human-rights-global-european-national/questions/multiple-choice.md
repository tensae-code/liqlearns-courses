# Human Rights — Global, European and National — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) human rights are realised at three levels at once: global declarations and treaties, European instruments and courts, and the national constitution and laws  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — The global and European levels?
a) At the global level the UN declarations and treaties name the rights; at the European level the Council of Europe and the European Union give them courts and instruments a citizen can actually reach.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
