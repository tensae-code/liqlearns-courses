# Human Rights — Global, European and National — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Human rights are realised at three levels at once: global declarations and treaties, european instruments and courts, and the national constitution and laws.

**2.** What does the part "The national level" teach?
**Answer:** At home, the constitution and laws turn the same rights into daily protection — in Bulgaria's courts, administration and public life. The levels support each other; none replaces the citizen's own claim.
