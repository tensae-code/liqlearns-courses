# Human Rights — Global, European and National — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** The global and European levels — a)

**2.** The national level — b)


Meanings:
a) At the global level the UN declarations and treaties name the rights; at the European level the Council of Europe and the European Union give them courts and instruments a citizen can actually reach.
b) At home, the constitution and laws turn the same rights into daily protection — in Bulgaria's courts, administration and public life. The levels support each other; none replaces the citizen's own claim.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
