# Hands-on — Human Rights — Global, European and National

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Take one right — expression, education or a fair trial. Trace it through all three levels: where it is named globally, protected in Europe, and guaranteed at home.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: human rights are realised at three levels at once: global declarations and treaties, European instruments and courts, and the national constitution and laws. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
