# Human Rights — Global, European and National

## Do it first

Take one right — expression, education or a fair trial. Trace it through all three levels: where it is named globally, protected in Europe, and guaranteed at home.

## Sub lesson 1 — The global and European levels

At the global level the UN declarations and treaties name the rights; at the European level the Council of Europe and the European Union give them courts and instruments a citizen can actually reach.

## Sub lesson 2 — The national level

At home, the constitution and laws turn the same rights into daily protection — in Bulgaria's courts, administration and public life. The levels support each other; none replaces the citizen's own claim.

## Say it to your companion
