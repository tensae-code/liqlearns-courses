# Justice, Rights and Responsibilities — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) justice means each person receives what is due to them; citizens' rights and responsibilities are justice written into shared life  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — The idea of justice?
a) Justice gives each their due: equal treatment for equals, fair shares, honest rules applied the same way to all, and repair when someone is wronged.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
