# Justice, Rights and Responsibilities — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** The idea of justice — a)

**2.** Rights with responsibilities — b)


Meanings:
a) Justice gives each their due: equal treatment for equals, fair shares, honest rules applied the same way to all, and repair when someone is wronged.
b) Rights protect what is due to us; responsibilities are what is due from us. A citizen holds both together — claiming rights while refusing duties breaks the justice they claim.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
