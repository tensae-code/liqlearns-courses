# Justice, Rights and Responsibilities — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Justice means each person receives what is due to them; citizens' rights and responsibilities are justice written into shared life.

**2.** What does the part "Rights with responsibilities" teach?
**Answer:** Rights protect what is due to us; responsibilities are what is due from us. A citizen holds both together — claiming rights while refusing duties breaks the justice they claim.
