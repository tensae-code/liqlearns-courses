# Hands-on — Justice, Rights and Responsibilities

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Take one shared thing — class time, a family chore rota, a team place. Judge its sharing by justice: who gets what, is it their due, and what duty goes with each right?

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: justice means each person receives what is due to them; citizens' rights and responsibilities are justice written into shared life. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
