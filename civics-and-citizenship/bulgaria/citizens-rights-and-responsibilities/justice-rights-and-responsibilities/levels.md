# Justice, Rights and Responsibilities — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — The idea of justice
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Justice gives each their due: equal treatment for equals, fair shares, honest rules applied the same way to all, and repair when someone is wronged. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — Rights with responsibilities
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Rights protect what is due to us; responsibilities are what is due from us. A citizen holds both together — claiming rights while refusing duties breaks the justice they claim. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
