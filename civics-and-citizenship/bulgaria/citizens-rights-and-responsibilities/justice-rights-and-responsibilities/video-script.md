# Video script — Justice, Rights and Responsibilities

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Justice means each person receives what is due to them; citizens' rights and responsibilities are justice written into shared life.

The idea of justice. Justice gives each their due: equal treatment for equals, fair shares, honest rules applied the same way to all, and repair when someone is wronged.

Rights with responsibilities. Rights protect what is due to us; responsibilities are what is due from us. A citizen holds both together — claiming rights while refusing duties breaks the justice they claim.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
