# Justice, Rights and Responsibilities

## Do it first

Take one shared thing — class time, a family chore rota, a team place. Judge its sharing by justice: who gets what, is it their due, and what duty goes with each right?

## Sub lesson 1 — The idea of justice

Justice gives each their due: equal treatment for equals, fair shares, honest rules applied the same way to all, and repair when someone is wronged.

## Sub lesson 2 — Rights with responsibilities

Rights protect what is due to us; responsibilities are what is due from us. A citizen holds both together — claiming rights while refusing duties breaks the justice they claim.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: justice means each person receives what is due to them; citizens' rights and responsibilities are justice written into shared life. Give one real example from your own life in Bulgaria, and name the lesson's key words: justice, rights, responsibilities, fairness. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Bulgarian as well as in English — Bulgaria's own book teaches Гражданско образование (Civic Education) in Bulgarian, for life in Bulgarian society, not only for the classroom.
