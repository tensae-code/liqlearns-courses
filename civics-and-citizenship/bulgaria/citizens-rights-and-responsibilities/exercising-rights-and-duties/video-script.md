# Video script — Exercising Rights and Duties

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Rights and duties exist only when exercised: citizens use their rights lawfully and do their duties without waiting to be forced.

Exercising rights. To exercise a right is to use it: speak, assemble, vote, claim, appeal. A right never used fades; a right used lawfully and with respect for others' rights strengthens everyone's.

Doing duties. Duties — obeying the law, paying taxes, defending others' rights, taking part — are done as a citizen's own act, not only under threat of punishment.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
