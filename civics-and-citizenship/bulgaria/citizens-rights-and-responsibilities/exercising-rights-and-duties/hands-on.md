# Hands-on — Exercising Rights and Duties

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Audit your own week: name one right you exercised, one duty you did, and one of each you let pass. Plan the two you will exercise this week.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: rights and duties exist only when exercised: citizens use their rights lawfully and do their duties without waiting to be forced. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
