# Exercising Rights and Duties — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) rights and duties exist only when exercised: citizens use their rights lawfully and do their duties without waiting to be forced  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — Exercising rights?
a) To exercise a right is to use it: speak, assemble, vote, claim, appeal. A right never used fades; a right used lawfully and with respect for others' rights strengthens everyone's.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
