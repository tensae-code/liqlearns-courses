# Exercising Rights and Duties — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Rights and duties exist only when exercised: citizens use their rights lawfully and do their duties without waiting to be forced.

**2.** What does the part "Doing duties" teach?
**Answer:** Duties — obeying the law, paying taxes, defending others' rights, taking part — are done as a citizen's own act, not only under threat of punishment.
