# Exercising Rights and Duties — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** Exercising rights — a)

**2.** Doing duties — b)


Meanings:
a) To exercise a right is to use it: speak, assemble, vote, claim, appeal. A right never used fades; a right used lawfully and with respect for others' rights strengthens everyone's.
b) Duties — obeying the law, paying taxes, defending others' rights, taking part — are done as a citizen's own act, not only under threat of punishment.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
