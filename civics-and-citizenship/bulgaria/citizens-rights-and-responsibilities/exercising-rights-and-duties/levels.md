# Exercising Rights and Duties — sub lessons and depth

## Sub lesson 1 — Exercising rights
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: To exercise a right is to use it: speak, assemble, vote, claim, appeal. A right never used fades; a right used lawfully and with respect for others' rights strengthens everyone's. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — Doing duties
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Duties — obeying the law, paying taxes, defending others' rights, taking part — are done as a citizen's own act, not only under threat of punishment. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
