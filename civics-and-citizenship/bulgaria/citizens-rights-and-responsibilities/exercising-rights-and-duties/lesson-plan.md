## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Exercising rights** — To exercise a right is to use it: speak, assemble, vote, claim, appeal. A right never used fades; a right used lawfully and with respect for others' rights strengthens everyone's.
2. **Doing duties** — Duties — obeying the law, paying taxes, defending others' rights, taking part — are done as a citizen's own act, not only under threat of punishment.
