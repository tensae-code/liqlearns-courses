# Exercising Rights and Duties

## Do it first

Audit your own week: name one right you exercised, one duty you did, and one of each you let pass. Plan the two you will exercise this week.

## Sub lesson 1 — Exercising rights

To exercise a right is to use it: speak, assemble, vote, claim, appeal. A right never used fades; a right used lawfully and with respect for others' rights strengthens everyone's.

## Sub lesson 2 — Doing duties

Duties — obeying the law, paying taxes, defending others' rights, taking part — are done as a citizen's own act, not only under threat of punishment.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: rights and duties exist only when exercised: citizens use their rights lawfully and do their duties without waiting to be forced. Give one real example from your own life in Bulgaria, and name the lesson's key words: rights, duties, exercise, lawfulness. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Bulgarian as well as in English — Bulgaria's own book teaches Гражданско образование (Civic Education) in Bulgarian, for life in Bulgarian society, not only for the classroom.
