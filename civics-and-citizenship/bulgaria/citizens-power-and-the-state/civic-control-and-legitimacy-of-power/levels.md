## Sub lesson 1 — Citizens as the corrective
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: In a modern constitutional democracy the citizens are the corrective of power: they assess governance, seek responsibility when the public interest is harmed, and can insist that power and its structures stay inside strict legal frames and work effectively for the citizens. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — Forms of control and participation
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Free elections express the citizens' will and legitimise the civilised change of power. Beyond elections, participation takes three forms — direct participation, civic control and public control: referendum, civic initiative, European citizens' initiative, petitions and signals, and the ombudsman's law are among their legal instruments. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
