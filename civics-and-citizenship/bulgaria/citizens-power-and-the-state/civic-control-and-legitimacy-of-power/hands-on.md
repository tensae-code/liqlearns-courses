# Hands-on — Civic Control and Legitimacy of Power

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Follow one decision of your municipality. Write the evaluation a citizen could give it, and name the legal form — petition, signal, referendum, ombudsman — that fits your judgement.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: power is legitimate when citizens control it: they evaluate the governors, demand accountability, sanction unlawful decisions, and renew power through free elections. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
