# Civic Control and Legitimacy of Power — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** Citizens as the corrective — a)

**2.** Forms of control and participation — b)


Meanings:
a) In a modern constitutional democracy the citizens are the corrective of power: they assess governance, seek responsibility when the public interest is harmed, and can insist that power and its structures stay inside strict legal frames and work effectively for the citizens.
b) Free elections express the citizens' will and legitimise the civilised change of power. Beyond elections, participation takes three forms — direct participation, civic control and public control: referendum, civic initiative, European citizens' initiative, petitions and signals, and the ombudsman's law are among their legal instruments.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
