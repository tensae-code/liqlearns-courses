# Civic Control and Legitimacy of Power — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Power is legitimate when citizens control it: they evaluate the governors, demand accountability, sanction unlawful decisions, and renew power through free elections.

**2.** What does the part "Forms of control and participation" teach?
**Answer:** Free elections express the citizens' will and legitimise the civilised change of power. Beyond elections, participation takes three forms — direct participation, civic control and public control: referendum, civic initiative, European citizens' initiative, petitions and signals, and the ombudsman's law are among their legal instruments.
