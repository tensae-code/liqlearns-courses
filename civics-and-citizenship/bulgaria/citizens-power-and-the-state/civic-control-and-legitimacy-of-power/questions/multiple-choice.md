# Civic Control and Legitimacy of Power — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) power is legitimate when citizens control it: they evaluate the governors, demand accountability, sanction unlawful decisions, and renew power through free elections  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — Citizens as the corrective?
a) In a modern constitutional democracy the citizens are the corrective of power: they assess governance, seek responsibility when the public interest is harmed, and can insist that power and its structures stay inside strict legal frames and work effectively for the citizens.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
