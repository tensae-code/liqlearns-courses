# Civic Control and Legitimacy of Power

## Do it first

Follow one decision of your municipality. Write the evaluation a citizen could give it, and name the legal form — petition, signal, referendum, ombudsman — that fits your judgement.

## Sub lesson 1 — Citizens as the corrective

In a modern constitutional democracy the citizens are the corrective of power: they assess governance, seek responsibility when the public interest is harmed, and can insist that power and its structures stay inside strict legal frames and work effectively for the citizens.

## Sub lesson 2 — Forms of control and participation

Free elections express the citizens' will and legitimise the civilised change of power. Beyond elections, participation takes three forms — direct participation, civic control and public control: referendum, civic initiative, European citizens' initiative, petitions and signals, and the ombudsman's law are among their legal instruments.
