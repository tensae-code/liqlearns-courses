# Video script — Civic Control and Legitimacy of Power

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Power is legitimate when citizens control it: they evaluate the governors, demand accountability, sanction unlawful decisions, and renew power through free elections.

Citizens as the corrective. In a modern constitutional democracy the citizens are the corrective of power: they assess governance, seek responsibility when the public interest is harmed, and can insist that power and its structures stay inside strict legal frames and work effectively for the citizens.

Forms of control and participation. Free elections express the citizens' will and legitimise the civilised change of power. Beyond elections, participation takes three forms — direct participation, civic control and public control: referendum, civic initiative, European citizens' initiative, petitions and signals, and the ombudsman's law are among their legal instruments.
