# My Civic Project for Positive Change — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** Choosing and naming — a)

**2.** Planning, doing, presenting — b)


Meanings:
a) A project is a form of work and inquiry that solves set tasks inside a set time. First choose the problem, theme and field — a volunteer action, a social project, an initiative — and brainstorm a name that gathers every idea before they are split into sub-themes.
b) Build the schedule and name who is responsible for each activity; gather the materials and information; decide the form of presentation — report, film, poster, interview. The responsible members then work independently to the schedule, and the project closes with presentation, summary and reflection on what went well and what can improve.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
