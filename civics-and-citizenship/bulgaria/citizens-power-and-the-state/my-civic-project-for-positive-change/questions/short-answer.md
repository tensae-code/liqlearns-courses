# My Civic Project for Positive Change — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** A civic project turns a public problem into positive change through six phases: choose the problem, name the project, plan the schedule and responsibilities, do the work, present it, and learn from feedback.

**2.** What does the part "Planning, doing, presenting" teach?
**Answer:** Build the schedule and name who is responsible for each activity; gather the materials and information; decide the form of presentation — report, film, poster, interview. The responsible members then work independently to the schedule, and the project closes with presentation, summary and reflection on what went well and what can improve.
