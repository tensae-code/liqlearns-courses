# My Civic Project for Positive Change — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A civic project turns a public problem into positive change through six phases: choose the problem, name the project, plan the schedule and responsibilities, do the work, present it, and learn from feedback.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a citizen's own home, school or community in Bulgaria.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Bulgaria and lived there.
