# Hands-on — My Civic Project for Positive Change

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Pick one problem in your school or street. Write its project name and the six phases as one line each, with a real name beside every task.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: a civic project turns a public problem into positive change through six phases: choose the problem, name the project, plan the schedule and responsibilities, do the work, present it, and learn from feedback. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
