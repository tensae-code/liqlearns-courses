# Video script — My Civic Project for Positive Change

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A civic project turns a public problem into positive change through six phases: choose the problem, name the project, plan the schedule and responsibilities, do the work, present it, and learn from feedback.

Choosing and naming. A project is a form of work and inquiry that solves set tasks inside a set time. First choose the problem, theme and field — a volunteer action, a social project, an initiative — and brainstorm a name that gathers every idea before they are split into sub-themes.

Planning, doing, presenting. Build the schedule and name who is responsible for each activity; gather the materials and information; decide the form of presentation — report, film, poster, interview. The responsible members then work independently to the schedule, and the project closes with presentation, summary and reflection on what went well and what can improve.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
