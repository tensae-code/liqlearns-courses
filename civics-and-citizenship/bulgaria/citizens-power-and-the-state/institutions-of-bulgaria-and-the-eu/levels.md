## Sub lesson 1 — Bulgaria's constitutional system
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The Constitution of 12 July 1991 fixes the principles of the state, civil society and economic relations: a republic with parliamentary government, one unitary state with no autonomous territorial formations, and a political regime of liberal democracy. The National Assembly is the only legislative body, elected for four years, and it also controls the executive and votes the state budget. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — Unitary state, federation, confederation
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: A unitary state is governed by one state power and its local organs answer to the centre. A federation is a lasting union of states that keep their independence under common federal organs; a confederation is a temporary union of sovereign states for set goals, founded on international treaties. Bulgaria is a unitary state. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
