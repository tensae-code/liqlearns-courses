# Hands-on — Institutions of Bulgaria and the European Union

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
List Bulgaria's four constitutional organs from memory. Beside each, write one thing it did this year that reached your own street.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Bulgaria is a unitary parliamentary republic whose Constitution sets the National Assembly, the Council of Ministers, the judiciary and the president, and the same citizen also lives inside the institutions of the European Union. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
