# Institutions of Bulgaria and the European Union — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Bulgaria is a unitary parliamentary republic whose Constitution sets the National Assembly, the Council of Ministers, the judiciary and the president, and the same citizen also lives inside the institutions of the European Union.

**2.** What does the part "Unitary state, federation, confederation" teach?
**Answer:** A unitary state is governed by one state power and its local organs answer to the centre. A federation is a lasting union of states that keep their independence under common federal organs; a confederation is a temporary union of sovereign states for set goals, founded on international treaties. Bulgaria is a unitary state.
