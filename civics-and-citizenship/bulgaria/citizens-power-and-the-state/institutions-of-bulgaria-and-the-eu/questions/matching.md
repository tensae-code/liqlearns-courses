# Institutions of Bulgaria and the European Union — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** Bulgaria's constitutional system — a)

**2.** Unitary state, federation, confederation — b)


Meanings:
a) The Constitution of 12 July 1991 fixes the principles of the state, civil society and economic relations: a republic with parliamentary government, one unitary state with no autonomous territorial formations, and a political regime of liberal democracy. The National Assembly is the only legislative body, elected for four years, and it also controls the executive and votes the state budget.
b) A unitary state is governed by one state power and its local organs answer to the centre. A federation is a lasting union of states that keep their independence under common federal organs; a confederation is a temporary union of sovereign states for set goals, founded on international treaties. Bulgaria is a unitary state.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
