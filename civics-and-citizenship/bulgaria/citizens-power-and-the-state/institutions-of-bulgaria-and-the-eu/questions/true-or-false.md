# Institutions of Bulgaria and the European Union — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Bulgaria is a unitary parliamentary republic whose Constitution sets the National Assembly, the Council of Ministers, the judiciary and the president, and the same citizen also lives inside the institutions of the European Union.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a citizen's own home, school or community in Bulgaria.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Bulgaria and lived there.
