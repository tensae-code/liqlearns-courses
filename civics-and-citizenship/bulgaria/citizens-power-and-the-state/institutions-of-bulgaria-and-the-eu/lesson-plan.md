## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Bulgaria's constitutional system** — The Constitution of 12 July 1991 fixes the principles of the state, civil society and economic relations: a republic with parliamentary government, one unitary state with no autonomous territorial formations, and a political regime of liberal democracy. The National Assembly is the only legislative body, elected for four years, and it also controls the executive and votes the state budget.
2. **Unitary state, federation, confederation** — A unitary state is governed by one state power and its local organs answer to the centre. A federation is a lasting union of states that keep their independence under common federal organs; a confederation is a temporary union of sovereign states for set goals, founded on international treaties. Bulgaria is a unitary state.
