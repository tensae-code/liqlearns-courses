# Me, the Institutions, Europe — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Values in a painting
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Study Bosch's Haywain triptych and hunt for the European Union's fundamental values — or their violation — inside it. Say what associations the picture raises for civic education: greed, folly and conflict against dignity, freedom and solidarity. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — Europe on the map
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Read the map of Europe: name the member states of the European Union and their capitals, and say which you would visit and why. Knowing the map is knowing the extent of your own citizenship. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
