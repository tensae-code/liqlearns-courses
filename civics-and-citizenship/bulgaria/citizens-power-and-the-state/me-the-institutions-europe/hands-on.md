# Hands-on — Me, the Institutions, Europe

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name the EU's member states you can from memory, then check the map. Pick one capital and find which EU institution, if any, sits there.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: I am a citizen of Bulgaria and of the European Union at once: the Union's fundamental values are the test for what I see around me, and its member states and capitals are my wider home. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
