# Me, the Institutions, Europe — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** I am a citizen of Bulgaria and of the European Union at once: the Union's fundamental values are the test for what I see around me, and its member states and capitals are my wider home.

**2.** What does the part "Europe on the map" teach?
**Answer:** Read the map of Europe: name the member states of the European Union and their capitals, and say which you would visit and why. Knowing the map is knowing the extent of your own citizenship.
