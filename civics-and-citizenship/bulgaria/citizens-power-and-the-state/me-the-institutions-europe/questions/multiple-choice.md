# Me, the Institutions, Europe — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) I am a citizen of Bulgaria and of the European Union at once: the Union's fundamental values are the test for what I see around me, and its member states and capitals are my wider home  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
