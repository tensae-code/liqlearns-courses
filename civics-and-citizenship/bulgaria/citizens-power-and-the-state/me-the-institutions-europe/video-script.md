# Video script — Me, the Institutions, Europe

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
I am a citizen of Bulgaria and of the European Union at once: the Union's fundamental values are the test for what I see around me, and its member states and capitals are my wider home.

Values in a painting. Study Bosch's Haywain triptych and hunt for the European Union's fundamental values — or their violation — inside it. Say what associations the picture raises for civic education: greed, folly and conflict against dignity, freedom and solidarity.

Europe on the map. Read the map of Europe: name the member states of the European Union and their capitals, and say which you would visit and why. Knowing the map is knowing the extent of your own citizenship.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
