# Lesson plan — Society and Power

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Bulgaria · **Title:** citizens-power-and-the-state · **Lesson:** Society and Power

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Power as a relation** — Power relations appear at every level of living together: they grow from the human will to reach goals and from unequal abilities and resources, and they order the conflicts that clashing interests create. Power is an intersubjective relation, mediated by values both sides hold significant.
2. **The state and power's deformations** — Guaranteeing public order is impossible without the state — political power — and politics is the mechanism that forms and distributes it. Power deforms when it is treated as an end in itself: coercion and violence, abuse of power, restricted freedom and broken equality, solidarity and justice.
