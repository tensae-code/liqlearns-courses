# Society and Power — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Power as a relation
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Power relations appear at every level of living together: they grow from the human will to reach goals and from unequal abilities and resources, and they order the conflicts that clashing interests create. Power is an intersubjective relation, mediated by values both sides hold significant. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — The state and power's deformations
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Guaranteeing public order is impossible without the state — political power — and politics is the mechanism that forms and distributes it. Power deforms when it is treated as an end in itself: coercion and violence, abuse of power, restricted freedom and broken equality, solidarity and justice. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
