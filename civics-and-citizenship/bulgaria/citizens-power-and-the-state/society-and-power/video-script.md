# Video script — Society and Power

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Power lives in every shared life: it is a relation between people with interests and values, the state is its public instrument, and it turns dangerous when it becomes an end in itself.

Power as a relation. Power relations appear at every level of living together: they grow from the human will to reach goals and from unequal abilities and resources, and they order the conflicts that clashing interests create. Power is an intersubjective relation, mediated by values both sides hold significant.

The state and power's deformations. Guaranteeing public order is impossible without the state — political power — and politics is the mechanism that forms and distributes it. Power deforms when it is treated as an end in itself: coercion and violence, abuse of power, restricted freedom and broken equality, solidarity and justice.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
