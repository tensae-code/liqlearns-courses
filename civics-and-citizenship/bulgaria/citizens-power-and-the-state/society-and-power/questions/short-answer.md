# Society and Power — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Power lives in every shared life: it is a relation between people with interests and values, the state is its public instrument, and it turns dangerous when it becomes an end in itself.

**2.** What does the part "The state and power's deformations" teach?
**Answer:** Guaranteeing public order is impossible without the state — political power — and politics is the mechanism that forms and distributes it. Power deforms when it is treated as an end in itself: coercion and violence, abuse of power, restricted freedom and broken equality, solidarity and justice.
