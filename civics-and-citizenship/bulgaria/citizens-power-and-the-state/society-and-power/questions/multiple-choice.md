# Society and Power — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) power lives in every shared life: it is a relation between people with interests and values, the state is its public instrument, and it turns dangerous when it becomes an end in itself  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — Power as a relation?
a) Power relations appear at every level of living together: they grow from the human will to reach goals and from unequal abilities and resources, and they order the conflicts that clashing interests create. Power is an intersubjective relation, mediated by values both sides hold significant.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
