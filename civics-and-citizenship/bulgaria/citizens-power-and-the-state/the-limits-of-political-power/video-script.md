# Video script — The Limits of Political Power

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Political power has limits, and the citizen stands at its triangle's open corner: active civic control is the limit that works before disobedience becomes the answer.

The triangle of power. Fill in the institutions that form the so-called triangle of power in Bulgaria — the three corners where public power is concentrated — and learn the abbreviations of the institutions and laws a citizen meets daily, from the European Commission to the Access to Public Information Act.

Where control is missing. Ask at which corner of the triangle a possibility for active civic control could be added, and who could exercise it. Then judge honestly: what would provoke citizens en masse to disobedience, and in which sphere of Bulgarian life is civic activity most lacking?

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
