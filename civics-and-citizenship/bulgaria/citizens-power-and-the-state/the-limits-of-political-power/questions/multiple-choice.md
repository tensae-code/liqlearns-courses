# The Limits of Political Power — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) political power has limits, and the citizen stands at its triangle's open corner: active civic control is the limit that works before disobedience becomes the answer  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — The triangle of power?
a) Fill in the institutions that form the so-called triangle of power in Bulgaria — the three corners where public power is concentrated — and learn the abbreviations of the institutions and laws a citizen meets daily, from the European Commission to the Access to Public Information Act.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
