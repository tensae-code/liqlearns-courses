# The Limits of Political Power — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** The triangle of power — a)

**2.** Where control is missing — b)


Meanings:
a) Fill in the institutions that form the so-called triangle of power in Bulgaria — the three corners where public power is concentrated — and learn the abbreviations of the institutions and laws a citizen meets daily, from the European Commission to the Access to Public Information Act.
b) Ask at which corner of the triangle a possibility for active civic control could be added, and who could exercise it. Then judge honestly: what would provoke citizens en masse to disobedience, and in which sphere of Bulgarian life is civic activity most lacking?

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
