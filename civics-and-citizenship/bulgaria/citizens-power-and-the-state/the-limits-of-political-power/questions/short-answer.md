# The Limits of Political Power — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Political power has limits, and the citizen stands at its triangle's open corner: active civic control is the limit that works before disobedience becomes the answer.

**2.** What does the part "Where control is missing" teach?
**Answer:** Ask at which corner of the triangle a possibility for active civic control could be added, and who could exercise it. Then judge honestly: what would provoke citizens en masse to disobedience, and in which sphere of Bulgarian life is civic activity most lacking?
