# Lesson plan — The Limits of Political Power

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Bulgaria · **Title:** citizens-power-and-the-state · **Lesson:** The Limits of Political Power
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Power, the Economy and Global Problems (CURRICULUM.md).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The triangle of power** — Fill in the institutions that form the so-called triangle of power in Bulgaria — the three corners where public power is concentrated — and learn the abbreviations of the institutions and laws a citizen meets daily, from the European Commission to the Access to Public Information Act.
2. **Where control is missing** — Ask at which corner of the triangle a possibility for active civic control could be added, and who could exercise it. Then judge honestly: what would provoke citizens en masse to disobedience, and in which sphere of Bulgarian life is civic activity most lacking?

Gate pool: the prove-it check for each sub lesson is at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson — drawn from this lesson's question files (questions/).
