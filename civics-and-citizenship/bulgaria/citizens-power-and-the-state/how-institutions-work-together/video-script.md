# Video script — How Institutions Work Together

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Institutions interact across local, national and European levels, and the first step of civic skill is knowing which office is elected, which is appointed, and which institution owns a problem.

Elected or appointed. Sort the offices by their mechanism: deputy, mayor, MEP, president and municipal councillor are elected; prime minister, chief prosecutor, ombudsman and regional governor reach office by appointment or election by another body. The mechanism decides to whom each office answers.

Matching statements to institutions. Read statements from different institutions — a ring road needing national funding, stray dogs needing a municipal campaign, a draft law, a European arrest warrant — and name the institution behind each, and whether interaction is possible at local, national or European level.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
