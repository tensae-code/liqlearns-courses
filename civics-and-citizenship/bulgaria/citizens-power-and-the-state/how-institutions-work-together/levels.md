# How Institutions Work Together — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Elected or appointed
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Sort the offices by their mechanism: deputy, mayor, MEP, president and municipal councillor are elected; prime minister, chief prosecutor, ombudsman and regional governor reach office by appointment or election by another body. The mechanism decides to whom each office answers. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — Matching statements to institutions
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Read statements from different institutions — a ring road needing national funding, stray dogs needing a municipal campaign, a draft law, a European arrest warrant — and name the institution behind each, and whether interaction is possible at local, national or European level. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
