# How Institutions Work Together — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Institutions interact across local, national and European levels, and the first step of civic skill is knowing which office is elected, which is appointed, and which institution owns a problem.

**2.** What does the part "Matching statements to institutions" teach?
**Answer:** Read statements from different institutions — a ring road needing national funding, stray dogs needing a municipal campaign, a draft law, a European arrest warrant — and name the institution behind each, and whether interaction is possible at local, national or European level.
