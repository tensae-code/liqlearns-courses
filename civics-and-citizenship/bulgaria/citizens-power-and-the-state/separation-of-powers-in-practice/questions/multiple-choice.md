# Separation of Powers in Practice — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) the legislative, executive and judicial powers are separated so that each limits the others, and every public problem has an owner — a person, local power or central power — who must take responsibility  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — Three powers, three symbols?
a) Match each power to its symbol and defend the match: the law paragraph for those who make the rules, the scales for those who judge, the instruments of enforcement for those who execute. Separation is the guarantee of political freedom.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
