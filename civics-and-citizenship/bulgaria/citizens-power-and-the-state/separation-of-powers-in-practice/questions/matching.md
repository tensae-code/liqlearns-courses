# Separation of Powers in Practice — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** Three powers, three symbols — a)

**2.** Who takes responsibility — b)


Meanings:
a) Match each power to its symbol and defend the match: the law paragraph for those who make the rules, the scales for those who judge, the instruments of enforcement for those who execute. Separation is the guarantee of political freedom.
b) Work real cases and decide for each whether intervention is needed and by whom: the individual person, the local power, or the central power. State power is a necessary instrument for solving public problems — and in a democracy no one may be punished without legal grounds.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
