## Sub lesson 1 — Three powers, three symbols
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Match each power to its symbol and defend the match: the law paragraph for those who make the rules, the scales for those who judge, the instruments of enforcement for those who execute. Separation is the guarantee of political freedom. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Work real cases and decide for each whether intervention is needed and by whom: the individual person, the local power, or the central power. State power is a necessary instrument for solving public problems — and in a democracy no one may be punished without legal grounds. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
