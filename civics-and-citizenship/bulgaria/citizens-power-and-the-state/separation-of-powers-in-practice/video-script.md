# Video script — Separation of Powers in Practice

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The legislative, executive and judicial powers are separated so that each limits the others, and every public problem has an owner — a person, local power or central power — who must take responsibility.

Three powers, three symbols. Match each power to its symbol and defend the match: the law paragraph for those who make the rules, the scales for those who judge, the instruments of enforcement for those who execute. Separation is the guarantee of political freedom.

Who takes responsibility. Work real cases and decide for each whether intervention is needed and by whom: the individual person, the local power, or the central power. State power is a necessary instrument for solving public problems — and in a democracy no one may be punished without legal grounds.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
