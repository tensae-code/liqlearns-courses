# Hands-on — Partnership in Governance

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
With two or three others, write five basic rules for a place you share, as if they were laws. Then check: who makes, who carries out, who judges a breach?

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: a community governs itself in partnership when its members together write the basic rules, give them the force of laws, and share the power those rules create. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
