# Video script — Partnership in Governance

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A community governs itself in partnership when its members together write the basic rules, give them the force of laws, and share the power those rules create.

A new beginning. Shipwrecked on an uninhabited island or stranded on an alien planet, survivors must found their common life: each group drafts ten basic rules, equal to laws, that everyone must keep — and answers what problems arise when there are no rules at all.

Rules compared. Compare the two groups' rules: what is alike, what differs, and what explains it — including whether the survivors' ages change the rules. Decide what resource of power you will use to regulate relations, and whether you will apply the separation of powers.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
