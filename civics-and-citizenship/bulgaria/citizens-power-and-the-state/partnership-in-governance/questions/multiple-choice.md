# Partnership in Governance — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) a community governs itself in partnership when its members together write the basic rules, give them the force of laws, and share the power those rules create  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — A new beginning?
a) Shipwrecked on an uninhabited island or stranded on an alien planet, survivors must found their common life: each group drafts ten basic rules, equal to laws, that everyone must keep — and answers what problems arise when there are no rules at all.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
