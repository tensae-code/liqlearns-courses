# Partnership in Governance — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** A new beginning — a)

**2.** Rules compared — b)


Meanings:
a) Shipwrecked on an uninhabited island or stranded on an alien planet, survivors must found their common life: each group drafts ten basic rules, equal to laws, that everyone must keep — and answers what problems arise when there are no rules at all.
b) Compare the two groups' rules: what is alike, what differs, and what explains it — including whether the survivors' ages change the rules. Decide what resource of power you will use to regulate relations, and whether you will apply the separation of powers.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
