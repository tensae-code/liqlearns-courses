# Partnership in Governance — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** A community governs itself in partnership when its members together write the basic rules, give them the force of laws, and share the power those rules create.

**2.** What does the part "Rules compared" teach?
**Answer:** Compare the two groups' rules: what is alike, what differs, and what explains it — including whether the survivors' ages change the rules. Decide what resource of power you will use to regulate relations, and whether you will apply the separation of powers.
