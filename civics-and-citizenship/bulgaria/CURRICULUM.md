# Civics and Citizenship — Bulgaria course — CURRICULUM (the path)

One course for Bulgaria inside the Civics and Citizenship
folder, organised by **levels, not grades**: a learner climbs
when they have proved the level, not when a school calendar
says so. The school grade the book comes from (Grade 11)
appears only in each lesson's sources (provenance), never as
the course's organisation. The path follows Bulgaria's own
book's order: citizens, politics and democracy first; then
citizens, rights and responsibilities; then identities and
differences in society. A fourth level, for Grade 12, is
pending — its book is UNREAD-pending under the disk hold.

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in
this course comes from government textbooks — this country's
own Civic Education book (Гражданско образование) — except
the **Tip boxes**: a tip in a box is from us, the people
making LiqLearns. Tips are ours; the lessons are the books'.
See ABOUT.md for the full book list, the Grade 12
UNREAD-pending label, and the acceptance reckoning.

## The levels (the path, in order)

1. **Citizens, Politics and Democracy** — freedom and power,
   the citizen, democracy and why it matters, politics and
   civic culture, good versus bad politics, civil society and
   civic participation in practice, media and democracy, the
   pupil and the school in democratic citizenship, and active
   citizens in focus. 11 lessons, in the title
   `citizens-politics-and-democracy/`.
2. **Citizens, Rights and Responsibilities** — the idea of
   justice, universal and group human rights, private and
   public spheres, human rights realised at global, European
   and national level and the mechanisms that protect them,
   exercising rights and duties, my civic position, personal
   responsibility and active participation, and forms of
   civic control. 9 lessons, in the title
   `citizens-rights-and-responsibilities/`.
3. **Identities and Differences in Society** — identity and
   social groups, the groups we belong to, majorities and
   minorities, the minorities around us, discrimination,
   recognising discrimination, protection from discrimination,
   the state and minorities, policies for minorities, and
   resolving conflicts with constructive communication.
   10 lessons, in the title
   `identities-and-differences-in-society/`.
4. **Grade 12 — PENDING.** Гражданско образование за
   12. клас (Педагог 6, 2024): the book is UNREAD-pending
   under the disk hold; its level will be sketched from its
   own contents when it is fetched after the hold lifts.
   0 lessons — sketched, not stocked.

**Totals: 30 lessons** (Level 1: 11 · Level 2: 9 · Level 3:
10 — all from one student book, COMPLETE by image read, its
assembled PDF preserved from page images and its staging
deferred under the disk hold; Level 4 pending, UNREAD — see
ABOUT.md).
