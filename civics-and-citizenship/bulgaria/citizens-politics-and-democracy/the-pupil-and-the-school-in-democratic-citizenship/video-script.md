# Video script — The Pupil and the School in Democratic Citizenship

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The school is the pupil's first political community: democratic citizenship is practised there daily, not only studied.

The school as a small democracy. Pupils hold rights and duties at school, meet rules they can question fairly, elect representatives and learn that decisions can be discussed, voted and reviewed.

The pupil's part. The pupil's part is to take part: in class decisions, pupil bodies and school life — respectfully, with reasons, accepting fair results and defending others' rights as their own.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
