# Hands-on — The Pupil and the School in Democratic Citizenship

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Map your school as a democracy: name one right you hold there, one rule, one way pupils take part in decisions, and one change you would argue for fairly.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the school is the pupil's first political community: democratic citizenship is practised there daily, not only studied. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
