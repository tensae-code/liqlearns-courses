# The Pupil and the School in Democratic Citizenship — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) the school is the pupil's first political community: democratic citizenship is practised there daily, not only studied  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — The school as a small democracy?
a) Pupils hold rights and duties at school, meet rules they can question fairly, elect representatives and learn that decisions can be discussed, voted and reviewed.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
