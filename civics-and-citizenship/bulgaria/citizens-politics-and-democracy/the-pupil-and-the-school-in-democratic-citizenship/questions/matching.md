# The Pupil and the School in Democratic Citizenship — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** The school as a small democracy — a)

**2.** The pupil's part — b)


Meanings:
a) Pupils hold rights and duties at school, meet rules they can question fairly, elect representatives and learn that decisions can be discussed, voted and reviewed.
b) The pupil's part is to take part: in class decisions, pupil bodies and school life — respectfully, with reasons, accepting fair results and defending others' rights as their own.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
