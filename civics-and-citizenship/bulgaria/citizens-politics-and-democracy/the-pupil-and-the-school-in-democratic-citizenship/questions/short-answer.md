# The Pupil and the School in Democratic Citizenship — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** The school is the pupil's first political community: democratic citizenship is practised there daily, not only studied.

**2.** What does the part "The pupil's part" teach?
**Answer:** The pupil's part is to take part: in class decisions, pupil bodies and school life — respectfully, with reasons, accepting fair results and defending others' rights as their own.
