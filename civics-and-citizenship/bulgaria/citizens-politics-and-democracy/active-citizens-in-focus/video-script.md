# Video script — Active Citizens in Focus

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Active citizens are ordinary people who saw a public problem, organised others and stayed with it until something changed.

What active citizens do. The cases show a pattern: notice a problem, learn about it, gather people, act lawfully and publicly, and persist through slow answers and setbacks.

What we learn from the cases. Active citizenship is a skill, not a temperament: it can be learned from real cases — what worked, what failed, and what the citizens changed in their approach.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
