# Hands-on — Active Citizens in Focus

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Pick one active citizen you know of, near or far. Tell their case in five steps: problem, learning, people, action, result. Name the step you could copy first.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: active citizens are ordinary people who saw a public problem, organised others and stayed with it until something changed. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
