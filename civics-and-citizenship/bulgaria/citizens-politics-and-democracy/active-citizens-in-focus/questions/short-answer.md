# Active Citizens in Focus — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Active citizens are ordinary people who saw a public problem, organised others and stayed with it until something changed.

**2.** What does the part "What we learn from the cases" teach?
**Answer:** Active citizenship is a skill, not a temperament: it can be learned from real cases — what worked, what failed, and what the citizens changed in their approach.
