## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **What civil society is** — Between the family and the state stands civil society: voluntary associations, charities, clubs, initiatives and movements where citizens join freely around shared concerns.
2. **Civic participation** — Participation means acting on public life: voting, joining, petitioning, volunteering, protesting lawfully and speaking in public — the channels through which civil society moves power.

Gate pool: the prove-it check for each sub lesson is at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson — drawn from this lesson's question files (questions/).
