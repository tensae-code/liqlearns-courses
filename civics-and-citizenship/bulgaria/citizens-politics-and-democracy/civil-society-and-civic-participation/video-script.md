# Video script — Civil Society and Civic Participation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Civil society is citizens organised freely outside the state — associations, initiatives and movements — and participation is how citizens act through it.

What civil society is. Between the family and the state stands civil society: voluntary associations, charities, clubs, initiatives and movements where citizens join freely around shared concerns.

Civic participation. Participation means acting on public life: voting, joining, petitioning, volunteering, protesting lawfully and speaking in public — the channels through which civil society moves power.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
