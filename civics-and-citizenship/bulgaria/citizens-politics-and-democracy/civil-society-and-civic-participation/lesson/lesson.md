# Civil Society and Civic Participation

## Do it first

List three groups in your town that are civil society, not state and not business. For one of them, say what concern it organises around and how a new member joins in.

## Sub lesson 1 — What civil society is

Between the family and the state stands civil society: voluntary associations, charities, clubs, initiatives and movements where citizens join freely around shared concerns.

## Sub lesson 2 — Civic participation

Participation means acting on public life: voting, joining, petitioning, volunteering, protesting lawfully and speaking in public — the channels through which civil society moves power.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: civil society is citizens organised freely outside the state — associations, initiatives and movements — and participation is how citizens act through it. Give one real example from your own life in Bulgaria, and name the lesson's key words: civil society, participation, associations, volunteering. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Bulgarian as well as in English — Bulgaria's own book teaches Гражданско образование (Civic Education) in Bulgarian, for life in Bulgarian society, not only for the classroom.
