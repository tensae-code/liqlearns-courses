# Civil Society and Civic Participation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Civil society is citizens organised freely outside the state — associations, initiatives and movements — and participation is how citizens act through it.

**2.** What does the part "Civic participation" teach?
**Answer:** Participation means acting on public life: voting, joining, petitioning, volunteering, protesting lawfully and speaking in public — the channels through which civil society moves power.
