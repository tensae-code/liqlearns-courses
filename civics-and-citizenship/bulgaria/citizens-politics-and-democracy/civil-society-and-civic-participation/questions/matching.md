# Civil Society and Civic Participation — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** What civil society is — a)

**2.** Civic participation — b)


Meanings:
a) Between the family and the state stands civil society: voluntary associations, charities, clubs, initiatives and movements where citizens join freely around shared concerns.
b) Participation means acting on public life: voting, joining, petitioning, volunteering, protesting lawfully and speaking in public — the channels through which civil society moves power.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
