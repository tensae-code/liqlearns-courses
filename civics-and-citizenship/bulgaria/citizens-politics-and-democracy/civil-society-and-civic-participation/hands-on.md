# Hands-on — Civil Society and Civic Participation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
List three groups in your town that are civil society, not state and not business. For one of them, say what concern it organises around and how a new member joins in.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: civil society is citizens organised freely outside the state — associations, initiatives and movements — and participation is how citizens act through it. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
