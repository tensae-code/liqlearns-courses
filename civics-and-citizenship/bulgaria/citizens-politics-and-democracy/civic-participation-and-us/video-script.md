# Video script — Civic Participation and Us

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Civic participation is not for other people: our own small, regular acts of taking part are what participation is made of.

Participation starts with us. Every act of participation counts at our scale too: speaking in a meeting, joining a clean-up, signing, voting where we may, and helping decide in the groups we belong to.

Finding our own form. People take part differently — time, voice, skill, money, presence. The lesson's task is to find the form that fits us and do it regularly, not once.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
