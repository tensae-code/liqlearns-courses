# Civic Participation and Us — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) civic participation is not for other people: our own small, regular acts of taking part are what participation is made of  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — Participation starts with us?
a) Every act of participation counts at our scale too: speaking in a meeting, joining a clean-up, signing, voting where we may, and helping decide in the groups we belong to.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
