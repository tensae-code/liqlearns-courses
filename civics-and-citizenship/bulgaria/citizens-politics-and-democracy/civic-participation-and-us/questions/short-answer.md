# Civic Participation and Us — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Civic participation is not for other people: our own small, regular acts of taking part are what participation is made of.

**2.** What does the part "Finding our own form" teach?
**Answer:** People take part differently — time, voice, skill, money, presence. The lesson's task is to find the form that fits us and do it regularly, not once.
