# Civic Participation and Us — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** Participation starts with us — a)

**2.** Finding our own form — b)


Meanings:
a) Every act of participation counts at our scale too: speaking in a meeting, joining a clean-up, signing, voting where we may, and helping decide in the groups we belong to.
b) People take part differently — time, voice, skill, money, presence. The lesson's task is to find the form that fits us and do it regularly, not once.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
