# Hands-on — Civic Participation and Us

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Choose one real issue at your school. Do one act of participation about it this week — speak, write, join or organise — and note what happened because you did.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: civic participation is not for other people: our own small, regular acts of taking part are what participation is made of. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
