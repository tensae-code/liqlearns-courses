# Civic Participation and Us

## Do it first

Choose one real issue at your school. Do one act of participation about it this week — speak, write, join or organise — and note what happened because you did.

## Sub lesson 1 — Participation starts with us

Every act of participation counts at our scale too: speaking in a meeting, joining a clean-up, signing, voting where we may, and helping decide in the groups we belong to.

## Sub lesson 2 — Finding our own form

People take part differently — time, voice, skill, money, presence. The lesson's task is to find the form that fits us and do it regularly, not once.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: civic participation is not for other people: our own small, regular acts of taking part are what participation is made of. Give one real example from your own life in Bulgaria, and name the lesson's key words: participation, initiative, responsibility, action. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Bulgarian as well as in English — Bulgaria's own book teaches Гражданско образование (Civic Education) in Bulgarian, for life in Bulgarian society, not only for the classroom.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: civic participation is not for other people: our own small, regular acts of taking part are what participation is made of.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
