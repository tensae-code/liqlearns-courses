# Good Politics, Bad Politics — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Good politics serves the common good openly and lawfully; bad politics serves private gain, hides its acts and breaks its own rules.

**2.** What does the part "Marks of bad politics" teach?
**Answer:** Bad politics hides decisions, buys loyalty, lies to the public, punishes critics and treats public money and power as private property.
