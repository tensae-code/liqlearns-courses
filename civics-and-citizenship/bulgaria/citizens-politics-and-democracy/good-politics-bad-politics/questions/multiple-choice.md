# Good Politics, Bad Politics — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) good politics serves the common good openly and lawfully; bad politics serves private gain, hides its acts and breaks its own rules  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — Marks of good politics?
a) Good politics aims at the common good, explains itself in public, keeps the law, respects opponents and can be checked and corrected by citizens.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
