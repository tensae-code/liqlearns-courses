# Lesson plan — Good Politics, Bad Politics

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Bulgaria · **Title:** citizens-politics-and-democracy · **Lesson:** Good Politics, Bad Politics
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — Citizens, Politics and Democracy (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** good politics serves the common good openly and lawfully; bad politics serves private gain, hides its acts and breaks its own rules.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — Citizens, Politics and Democracy — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Marks of good politics** — Good politics aims at the common good, explains itself in public, keeps the law, respects opponents and can be checked and corrected by citizens.
2. **Marks of bad politics** — Bad politics hides decisions, buys loyalty, lies to the public, punishes critics and treats public money and power as private property.

Gate pool: the prove-it check for each sub lesson is at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson — drawn from this lesson's question files (questions/).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: good politics serves the common good openly and lawfully; bad politics serves private gain, hides its acts and breaks its own rules.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street, school or community in Bulgaria.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the learner's own life in Bulgaria and the book's own teaching, as Bulgaria's Civic Education book works from the citizen's own public life, outward.
