# Video script — Good Politics, Bad Politics

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Good politics serves the common good openly and lawfully; bad politics serves private gain, hides its acts and breaks its own rules.

Marks of good politics. Good politics aims at the common good, explains itself in public, keeps the law, respects opponents and can be checked and corrected by citizens.

Marks of bad politics. Bad politics hides decisions, buys loyalty, lies to the public, punishes critics and treats public money and power as private property.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
