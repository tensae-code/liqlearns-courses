# Hands-on — Good Politics, Bad Politics

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Take two real political acts from the news. Test each against the marks: common good or private gain, open or hidden, lawful or law-breaking. Give your verdict and your evidence.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: good politics serves the common good openly and lawfully; bad politics serves private gain, hides its acts and breaks its own rules. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
