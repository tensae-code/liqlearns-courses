# Good Politics, Bad Politics

## Do it first

Take two real political acts from the news. Test each against the marks: common good or private gain, open or hidden, lawful or law-breaking. Give your verdict and your evidence.

## Sub lesson 1 — Marks of good politics

Good politics aims at the common good, explains itself in public, keeps the law, respects opponents and can be checked and corrected by citizens.

## Sub lesson 2 — Marks of bad politics

Bad politics hides decisions, buys loyalty, lies to the public, punishes critics and treats public money and power as private property.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: good politics serves the common good openly and lawfully; bad politics serves private gain, hides its acts and breaks its own rules. Give one real example from your own life in Bulgaria, and name the lesson's key words: good politics, common good, corruption, accountability. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Bulgarian as well as in English — Bulgaria's own book teaches Гражданско образование (Civic Education) in Bulgarian, for life in Bulgarian society, not only for the classroom.
