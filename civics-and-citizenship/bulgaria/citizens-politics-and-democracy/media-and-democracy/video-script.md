# Video script — Media and Democracy

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Free media keep democracy alive by informing citizens and watching power — and citizens keep media honest by checking what they read.

Media as democracy's watchdog. Free and plural media inform the public, expose abuse, give opponents a voice and let citizens judge power between elections. Where media are captured, that watch fails.

Reading media critically. Not everything published is true. The democratic reader checks the source, compares reports, separates fact from opinion and refuses to pass on what they have not verified.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
