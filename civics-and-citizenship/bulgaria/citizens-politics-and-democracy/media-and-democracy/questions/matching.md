# Media and Democracy — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** Media as democracy's watchdog — a)

**2.** Reading media critically — b)


Meanings:
a) Free and plural media inform the public, expose abuse, give opponents a voice and let citizens judge power between elections. Where media are captured, that watch fails.
b) Not everything published is true. The democratic reader checks the source, compares reports, separates fact from opinion and refuses to pass on what they have not verified.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
