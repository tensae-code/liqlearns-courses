# Media and Democracy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) free media keep democracy alive by informing citizens and watching power — and citizens keep media honest by checking what they read  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — Media as democracy's watchdog?
a) Free and plural media inform the public, expose abuse, give opponents a voice and let citizens judge power between elections. Where media are captured, that watch fails.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
