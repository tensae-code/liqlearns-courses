# Media and Democracy — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Free media keep democracy alive by informing citizens and watching power — and citizens keep media honest by checking what they read.

**2.** What does the part "Reading media critically" teach?
**Answer:** Not everything published is true. The democratic reader checks the source, compares reports, separates fact from opinion and refuses to pass on what they have not verified.
