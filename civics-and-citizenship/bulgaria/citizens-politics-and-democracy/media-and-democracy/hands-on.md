# Hands-on — Media and Democracy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Take one news story and find it in two different outlets. Note what agrees, what differs, and one claim you still cannot verify. Say which report you trust more, and why.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: free media keep democracy alive by informing citizens and watching power — and citizens keep media honest by checking what they read. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
