# Media and Democracy — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Media as democracy's watchdog
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Free and plural media inform the public, expose abuse, give opponents a voice and let citizens judge power between elections. Where media are captured, that watch fails. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — Reading media critically
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Not everything published is true. The democratic reader checks the source, compares reports, separates fact from opinion and refuses to pass on what they have not verified. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
