# Democracy — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** What democracy is — a)

**2.** How democracy works — b)


Meanings:
a) In a democracy the people are the source of power: they elect representatives in free elections, decisions follow the majority while minorities stay protected, and no ruler stands above the law.
b) Democracy works through elections, separated powers, free media, courts and citizens who watch, question and take part between elections as well as on the day.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
