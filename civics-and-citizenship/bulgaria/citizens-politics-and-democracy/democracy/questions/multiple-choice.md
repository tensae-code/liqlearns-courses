# Democracy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) democracy is rule by the people: citizens choose their rulers, power is limited by law, and every voice counts  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — What democracy is?
a) In a democracy the people are the source of power: they elect representatives in free elections, decisions follow the majority while minorities stay protected, and no ruler stands above the law.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
