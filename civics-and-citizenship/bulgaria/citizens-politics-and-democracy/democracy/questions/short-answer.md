# Democracy — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Democracy is rule by the people: citizens choose their rulers, power is limited by law, and every voice counts.

**2.** What does the part "How democracy works" teach?
**Answer:** Democracy works through elections, separated powers, free media, courts and citizens who watch, question and take part between elections as well as on the day.
