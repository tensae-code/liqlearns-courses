# Video script — Democracy

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Democracy is rule by the people: citizens choose their rulers, power is limited by law, and every voice counts.

What democracy is. In a democracy the people are the source of power: they elect representatives in free elections, decisions follow the majority while minorities stay protected, and no ruler stands above the law.

How democracy works. Democracy works through elections, separated powers, free media, courts and citizens who watch, question and take part between elections as well as on the day.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
