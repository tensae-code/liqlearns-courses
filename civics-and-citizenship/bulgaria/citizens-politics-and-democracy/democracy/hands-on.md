# Hands-on — Democracy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Hold a two-minute class vote on a real small choice. Count openly, follow the result, and say one protection the losing side still keeps.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: democracy is rule by the people: citizens choose their rulers, power is limited by law, and every voice counts. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
