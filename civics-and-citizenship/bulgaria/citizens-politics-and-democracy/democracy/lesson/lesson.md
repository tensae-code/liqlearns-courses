# Democracy

## Do it first

Hold a two-minute class vote on a real small choice. Count openly, follow the result, and say one protection the losing side still keeps.

## Sub lesson 1 — What democracy is

In a democracy the people are the source of power: they elect representatives in free elections, decisions follow the majority while minorities stay protected, and no ruler stands above the law.

## Sub lesson 2 — How democracy works

Democracy works through elections, separated powers, free media, courts and citizens who watch, question and take part between elections as well as on the day.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: democracy is rule by the people: citizens choose their rulers, power is limited by law, and every voice counts. Give one real example from your own life in Bulgaria, and name the lesson's key words: democracy, elections, majority, rule of law. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Bulgarian as well as in English — Bulgaria's own book teaches Гражданско образование (Civic Education) in Bulgarian, for life in Bulgarian society, not only for the classroom.
