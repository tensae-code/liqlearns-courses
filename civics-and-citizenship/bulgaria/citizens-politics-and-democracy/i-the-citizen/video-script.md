# Video script — I, the Citizen

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A citizen is not only a passport holder: a citizen is a member of a political community who holds rights, carries duties and takes part.

Who the citizen is. The citizen belongs to a state and a community, holds rights the community guarantees, and carries duties toward it — membership is legal and lived at the same time.

The citizen in daily life. Citizenship shows in daily acts: obeying fair laws, respecting others, taking part in decisions and caring for the shared place — not only on election day.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
