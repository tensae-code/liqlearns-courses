# I, the Citizen

## Do it first

Introduce yourself as a citizen: say where you belong, one right you hold, one duty you carry, and one way you took part in a shared decision this month.

## Sub lesson 1 — Who the citizen is

The citizen belongs to a state and a community, holds rights the community guarantees, and carries duties toward it — membership is legal and lived at the same time.

## Sub lesson 2 — The citizen in daily life

Citizenship shows in daily acts: obeying fair laws, respecting others, taking part in decisions and caring for the shared place — not only on election day.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: a citizen is not only a passport holder: a citizen is a member of a political community who holds rights, carries duties and takes part. Give one real example from your own life in Bulgaria, and name the lesson's key words: citizen, citizenship, rights, duties. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Bulgarian as well as in English — Bulgaria's own book teaches Гражданско образование (Civic Education) in Bulgarian, for life in Bulgarian society, not only for the classroom.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: a citizen is not only a passport holder: a citizen is a member of a political community who holds rights, carries duties and takes part.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
