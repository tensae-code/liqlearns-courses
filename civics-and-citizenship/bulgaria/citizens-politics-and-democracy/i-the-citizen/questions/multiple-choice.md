# I, the Citizen — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) a citizen is not only a passport holder: a citizen is a member of a political community who holds rights, carries duties and takes part  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — Who the citizen is?
a) The citizen belongs to a state and a community, holds rights the community guarantees, and carries duties toward it — membership is legal and lived at the same time.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
