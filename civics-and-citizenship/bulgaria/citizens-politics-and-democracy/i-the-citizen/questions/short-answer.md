# I, the Citizen — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** A citizen is not only a passport holder: a citizen is a member of a political community who holds rights, carries duties and takes part.

**2.** What does the part "The citizen in daily life" teach?
**Answer:** Citizenship shows in daily acts: obeying fair laws, respecting others, taking part in decisions and caring for the shared place — not only on election day.
