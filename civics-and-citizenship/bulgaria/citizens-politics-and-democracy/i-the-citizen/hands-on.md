# Hands-on — I, the Citizen

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Introduce yourself as a citizen: say where you belong, one right you hold, one duty you carry, and one way you took part in a shared decision this month.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: a citizen is not only a passport holder: a citizen is a member of a political community who holds rights, carries duties and takes part. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
