# Politics and Civic Culture — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** What politics is — a)

**2.** Civic culture — b)


Meanings:
a) Politics is the public work of deciding how we live together: who decides, by what rules, and for whose good. It is not only parties and parliaments — it begins wherever a group must choose together.
b) Civic culture is the knowledge, values and habits citizens bring to politics: interest in public life, tolerance, trust, readiness to take part and to accept fair results.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
