# Politics and Civic Culture — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Politics is how a community decides together, and civic culture — what citizens know, value and do — decides how well those decisions go.

**2.** What does the part "Civic culture" teach?
**Answer:** Civic culture is the knowledge, values and habits citizens bring to politics: interest in public life, tolerance, trust, readiness to take part and to accept fair results.
