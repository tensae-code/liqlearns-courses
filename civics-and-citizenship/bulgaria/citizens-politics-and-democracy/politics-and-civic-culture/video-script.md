# Video script — Politics and Civic Culture

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Politics is how a community decides together, and civic culture — what citizens know, value and do — decides how well those decisions go.

What politics is. Politics is the public work of deciding how we live together: who decides, by what rules, and for whose good. It is not only parties and parliaments — it begins wherever a group must choose together.

Civic culture. Civic culture is the knowledge, values and habits citizens bring to politics: interest in public life, tolerance, trust, readiness to take part and to accept fair results.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
