# Hands-on — Politics and Civic Culture

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Watch one public decision this week — at school, at home or in the news. Name who decided, by what rule, and one civic habit that made it go well or badly.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: politics is how a community decides together, and civic culture — what citizens know, value and do — decides how well those decisions go. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
