# Politics and Civic Culture

## Do it first

Watch one public decision this week — at school, at home or in the news. Name who decided, by what rule, and one civic habit that made it go well or badly.

## Sub lesson 1 — What politics is

Politics is the public work of deciding how we live together: who decides, by what rules, and for whose good. It is not only parties and parliaments — it begins wherever a group must choose together.

## Sub lesson 2 — Civic culture

Civic culture is the knowledge, values and habits citizens bring to politics: interest in public life, tolerance, trust, readiness to take part and to accept fair results.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: politics is how a community decides together, and civic culture — what citizens know, value and do — decides how well those decisions go. Give one real example from your own life in Bulgaria, and name the lesson's key words: politics, civic culture, participation, tolerance. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Bulgarian as well as in English — Bulgaria's own book teaches Гражданско образование (Civic Education) in Bulgarian, for life in Bulgarian society, not only for the classroom.
