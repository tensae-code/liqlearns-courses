# Why Democracy Matters — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Democracy matters because it protects freedom and dignity, lets errors be corrected peacefully, and gives every citizen a share in power.

**2.** What does the part "Why it beats the alternatives" teach?
**Answer:** Where citizens cannot remove rulers peacefully, errors harden into oppression. Democracy's slow arguments are its strength: they let a society correct itself without violence.
