# Why Democracy Matters — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** What democracy protects — a)

**2.** Why it beats the alternatives — b)


Meanings:
a) Democracy protects freedom, dignity and rights: people may speak, organise and live without fear, and power that harms them can be challenged in the open.
b) Where citizens cannot remove rulers peacefully, errors harden into oppression. Democracy's slow arguments are its strength: they let a society correct itself without violence.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
