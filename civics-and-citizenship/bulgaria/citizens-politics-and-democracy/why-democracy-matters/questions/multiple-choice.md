# Why Democracy Matters — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) democracy matters because it protects freedom and dignity, lets errors be corrected peacefully, and gives every citizen a share in power  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — What democracy protects?
a) Democracy protects freedom, dignity and rights: people may speak, organise and live without fear, and power that harms them can be challenged in the open.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
