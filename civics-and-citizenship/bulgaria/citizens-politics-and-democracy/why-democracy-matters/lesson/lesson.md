# Why Democracy Matters

## Do it first

Name one problem in your town. Say how citizens could get it fixed in a democracy, step by step, and what would block the same fix where people have no vote.

## Sub lesson 1 — What democracy protects

Democracy protects freedom, dignity and rights: people may speak, organise and live without fear, and power that harms them can be challenged in the open.

## Sub lesson 2 — Why it beats the alternatives

Where citizens cannot remove rulers peacefully, errors harden into oppression. Democracy's slow arguments are its strength: they let a society correct itself without violence.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: democracy matters because it protects freedom and dignity, lets errors be corrected peacefully, and gives every citizen a share in power. Give one real example from your own life in Bulgaria, and name the lesson's key words: democracy, freedom, dignity, participation. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
