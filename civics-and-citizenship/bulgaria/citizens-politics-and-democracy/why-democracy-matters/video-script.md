# Video script — Why Democracy Matters

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Democracy matters because it protects freedom and dignity, lets errors be corrected peacefully, and gives every citizen a share in power.

What democracy protects. Democracy protects freedom, dignity and rights: people may speak, organise and live without fear, and power that harms them can be challenged in the open.

Why it beats the alternatives. Where citizens cannot remove rulers peacefully, errors harden into oppression. Democracy's slow arguments are its strength: they let a society correct itself without violence.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
