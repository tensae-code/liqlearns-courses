## Sub lesson 1 — Freedom and the free person
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Freedom is both absence of constraint and self-realisation: the free person acts by their own reason and will, and every person holds natural, inalienable rights no power may take away. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — Power, values and the philosophers
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Power must serve freedom, not crush it. Hegel, Kant, Rousseau, Sartre and Locke each teach how freedom is grounded — in reason, autonomy, the social contract, choice and natural rights — and democracy's values are freedom, equality, justice and solidarity. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
