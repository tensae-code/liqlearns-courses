# Video script — Freedom and Power

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Freedom and power belong together: a free person has human rights, and democracy rests on freedom, equality, justice and solidarity.

Freedom and the free person. Freedom is both absence of constraint and self-realisation: the free person acts by their own reason and will, and every person holds natural, inalienable rights no power may take away.

Power, values and the philosophers. Power must serve freedom, not crush it. Hegel, Kant, Rousseau, Sartre and Locke each teach how freedom is grounded — in reason, autonomy, the social contract, choice and natural rights — and democracy's values are freedom, equality, justice and solidarity.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
