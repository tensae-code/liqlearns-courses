# Freedom and Power — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** Freedom and the free person — a)

**2.** Power, values and the philosophers — b)


Meanings:
a) Freedom is both absence of constraint and self-realisation: the free person acts by their own reason and will, and every person holds natural, inalienable rights no power may take away.
b) Power must serve freedom, not crush it. Hegel, Kant, Rousseau, Sartre and Locke each teach how freedom is grounded — in reason, autonomy, the social contract, choice and natural rights — and democracy's values are freedom, equality, justice and solidarity.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
