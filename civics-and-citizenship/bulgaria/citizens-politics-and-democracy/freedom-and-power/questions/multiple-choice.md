# Freedom and Power — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) freedom and power belong together: a free person has human rights, and democracy rests on freedom, equality, justice and solidarity  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — Freedom and the free person?
a) Freedom is both absence of constraint and self-realisation: the free person acts by their own reason and will, and every person holds natural, inalienable rights no power may take away.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
