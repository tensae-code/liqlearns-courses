# Freedom and Power — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Freedom and power belong together: a free person has human rights, and democracy rests on freedom, equality, justice and solidarity.

**2.** What does the part "Power, values and the philosophers" teach?
**Answer:** Power must serve freedom, not crush it. Hegel, Kant, Rousseau, Sartre and Locke each teach how freedom is grounded — in reason, autonomy, the social contract, choice and natural rights — and democracy's values are freedom, equality, justice and solidarity.
