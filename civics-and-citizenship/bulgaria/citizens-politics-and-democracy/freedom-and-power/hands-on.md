# Hands-on — Freedom and Power

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Think of one choice you made freely today and one rule that limited a choice. Say what made the first free, and whether the limit protected someone's rights or only blocked you.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: freedom and power belong together: a free person has human rights, and democracy rests on freedom, equality, justice and solidarity. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
