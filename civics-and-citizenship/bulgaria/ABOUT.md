# About the Civics and Citizenship — Bulgaria course

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in this
course comes from government textbooks — this country's own
Civic Education book (Гражданско образование, Grade 11). The one
exception is the **Tip boxes**: a tip in a box is from us, the
people making LiqLearns. Tips are ours; the lessons are the
books'.

## How this course is organised

This is ONE course for Bulgaria inside the Civics and
Citizenship folder (one course per country). Inside it, the
organisation is by **levels, not grades**: a learner's place is
found by what they prove, and each level stands on the ones
before it as a recommended-first path — never a forced gate.
The school grade the book comes from (Grade 11) appears only in
each lesson's `lesson/sources.md`, as provenance.

The course has **three stocked levels**, because exactly one
Bulgaria civics book has been read in hand — the Grade 11 Civic
Education student book. A fourth level, for Grade 12, is
sketched, not stocked: its book is UNREAD-pending under the
disk hold (see the book list below):

1. **Level 1 — Citizens, Politics and Democracy** (11 lessons,
   in the title `citizens-politics-and-democracy/`). Freedom
   and power, the citizen, democracy and why it matters, civic
   culture, good versus bad politics, civil society and
   participation, media, and the school as a place of
   democratic citizenship.
2. **Level 2 — Citizens, Rights and Responsibilities**
   (9 lessons, in the title
   `citizens-rights-and-responsibilities/`). Justice, universal
   and group human rights, private and public spheres, rights
   realised and protected at global, European and national
   level, exercising rights and duties, my civic position,
   personal responsibility, and forms of civic control.
3. **Level 3 — Identities and Differences in Society**
   (10 lessons, in the title
   `identities-and-differences-in-society/`). Identity and
   social groups, majorities and minorities, discrimination
   recognised and answered, the state and minorities, policies
   for minorities, and resolving conflicts.

**Bulgaria's own way:** Гражданско образование in Grade 11 is
a lesson-numbered course (each Тема built from НЗ
new-knowledge and ПО practical-experience lessons) that starts
philosophically — freedom and power through Hegel, Kant,
Rousseau, Sartre and Locke — then works through democracy and
civic culture, rights with their protection mechanisms at
global/European/national level, and identities, minorities and
discrimination in Bulgarian society.

**Course-intro video script** (written here, made later; the
book's own shape, walked in order):

- *Freedom first, then rights, then living together:* "Start
  with freedom and power, and what a citizen is. Learn what
  democracy is and why it matters. Then your rights — universal
  and group rights, protected at home, in Europe and in the
  world — and the duties that go with them. Last, who we are:
  identity, majorities and minorities, discrimination named
  and answered, and conflicts resolved by talking well. That
  is Bulgaria's Civic Education."
