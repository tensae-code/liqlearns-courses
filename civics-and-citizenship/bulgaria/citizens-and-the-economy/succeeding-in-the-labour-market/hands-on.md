# Hands-on — Succeeding in the Labour Market

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write a five-line presentation of yourself to an employer that is completely true and still strong. Then list what a dishonest version would add — and what it would risk.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: to succeed on the labour market, know how to find the right job and present yourself honestly and well — because hidden information poisons hiring, through adverse selection before the contract and moral hazard after it. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
