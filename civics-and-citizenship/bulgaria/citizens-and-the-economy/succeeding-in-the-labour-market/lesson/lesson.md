# Succeeding in the Labour Market

## Do it first

Write a five-line presentation of yourself to an employer that is completely true and still strong. Then list what a dishonest version would add — and what it would risk.

## Sub lesson 1 — Finding and presenting

Participation starts with knowing how to search for and find a suitable job, and the lesson's main goal is practical: understand how to present yourself well to an employer — your real abilities, stated truly and in the order that serves the work.

## Sub lesson 2 — Asymmetric information

Sort real cases: the candidate who hides a gambling problem and two dismissals at the interview; the driver who siphons the firm's diesel; the colleague who stays silent; the applicant who invents years of accounting experience. Which show adverse selection — hidden truth before hiring — and which moral hazard — hidden conduct after it?

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: to succeed on the labour market, know how to find the right job and present yourself honestly and well — because hidden information poisons hiring, through adverse selection before the contract and moral hazard after it. Give one real example from your own life in Bulgaria, and name the lesson's key words: labour market, interview, adverse selection, moral hazard. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Bulgarian as well as in English — Bulgaria's own book teaches Гражданско образование (Civic Education) in Bulgarian, for life in Bulgarian society, not only for the classroom.
