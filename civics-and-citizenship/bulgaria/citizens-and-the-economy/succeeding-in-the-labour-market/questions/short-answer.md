# Succeeding in the Labour Market — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** To succeed on the labour market, know how to find the right job and present yourself honestly and well — because hidden information poisons hiring, through adverse selection before the contract and moral hazard after it.

**2.** What does the part "Asymmetric information" teach?
**Answer:** Sort real cases: the candidate who hides a gambling problem and two dismissals at the interview; the driver who siphons the firm's diesel; the colleague who stays silent; the applicant who invents years of accounting experience. Which show adverse selection — hidden truth before hiring — and which moral hazard — hidden conduct after it?
