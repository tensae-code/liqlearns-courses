# Succeeding in the Labour Market — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** To succeed on the labour market, know how to find the right job and present yourself honestly and well — because hidden information poisons hiring, through adverse selection before the contract and moral hazard after it.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a citizen's own home, school or community in Bulgaria.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Bulgaria and lived there.
