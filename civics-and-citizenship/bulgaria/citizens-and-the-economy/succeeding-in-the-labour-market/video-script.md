# Video script — Succeeding in the Labour Market

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
To succeed on the labour market, know how to find the right job and present yourself honestly and well — because hidden information poisons hiring, through adverse selection before the contract and moral hazard after it.

Finding and presenting. Participation starts with knowing how to search for and find a suitable job, and the lesson's main goal is practical: understand how to present yourself well to an employer — your real abilities, stated truly and in the order that serves the work.

Asymmetric information. Sort real cases: the candidate who hides a gambling problem and two dismissals at the interview; the driver who siphons the firm's diesel; the colleague who stays silent; the applicant who invents years of accounting experience. Which show adverse selection — hidden truth before hiring — and which moral hazard — hidden conduct after it?

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
