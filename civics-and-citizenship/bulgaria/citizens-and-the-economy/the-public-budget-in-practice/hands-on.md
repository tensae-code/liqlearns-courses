# Hands-on — The Public Budget in Practice

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Take the budget figures of any year you can find. Split three taxes into direct and indirect and compute the bigger group's share. Say in one sentence whether you find it fair.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: reading a real budget table is a civic skill: split Bulgaria's tax revenues into direct and indirect, compute their shares, and argue whether the balance between them is fair. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
