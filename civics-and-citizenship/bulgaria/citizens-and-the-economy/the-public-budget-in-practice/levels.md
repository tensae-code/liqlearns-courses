## Sub lesson 1 — Bulgaria's revenues on the table
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. ## Sub lesson 2 — The fairness debate
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Some economists would collect everything indirectly: consumption taxes reward saving and growth, and everyone who draws from the common pot pays in. Others answer that indirect taxes offend social justice, because poorer and vulnerable groups spend a larger share of their income on consumption. Choose a thesis, defend it, and judge Bulgaria's balance. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
