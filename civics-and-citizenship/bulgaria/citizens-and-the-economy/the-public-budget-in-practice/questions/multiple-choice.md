# The Public Budget in Practice — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — Bulgaria's revenues on the table?
a) Use the table from the State Budget Law of the Republic of Bulgaria for 2020: total revenues, aid and donations of about 26 billion leva, of which tax revenues about 23 billion — corporate tax, personal income tax, VAT, excises, customs and others.
