# The Public Budget in Practice — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Reading a real budget table is a civic skill: split Bulgaria's tax revenues into direct and indirect, compute their shares, and argue whether the balance between them is fair.

**2.** What does the part "The fairness debate" teach?
**Answer:** Some economists would collect everything indirectly: consumption taxes reward saving and growth, and everyone who draws from the common pot pays in. Others answer that indirect taxes offend social justice, because poorer and vulnerable groups spend a larger share of their income on consumption. Choose a thesis, defend it, and judge Bulgaria's balance.
