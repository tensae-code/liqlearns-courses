# Sustainable Social and Economic Development — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Four indicators
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Match each indicator to its aspect: GDP per head at purchasing-power parity to society's capacity to produce goods and services; life expectancy to the quality and accessibility of healthcare; mean years of schooling to education's quality and reach; the Gini index to inequality in income distribution. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — Measuring real countries
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Work the table of sixteen countries — from Luxembourg and Singapore to Burundi and the Central African Republic — and see how the indicators separate: rich in output yet unequal, long-lived yet poor, schooled yet short-lived. Economic development and societal development are not the same thing, and sustainability asks whether either can continue. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
