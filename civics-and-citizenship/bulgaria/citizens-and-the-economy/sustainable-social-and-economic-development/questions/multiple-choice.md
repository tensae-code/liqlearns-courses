# Sustainable Social and Economic Development — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) a country's development is more than its wealth: it is measured by GDP per head, life expectancy, years of schooling and the Gini index together — and judged by whether it can last  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — Four indicators?
