# Sustainable Social and Economic Development — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** A country's development is more than its wealth: it is measured by GDP per head, life expectancy, years of schooling and the Gini index together — and judged by whether it can last.

**2.** What does the part "Measuring real countries" teach?
**Answer:** Work the table of sixteen countries — from Luxembourg and Singapore to Burundi and the Central African Republic — and see how the indicators separate: rich in output yet unequal, long-lived yet poor, schooled yet short-lived. Economic development and societal development are not the same thing, and sustainability asks whether either can continue.
