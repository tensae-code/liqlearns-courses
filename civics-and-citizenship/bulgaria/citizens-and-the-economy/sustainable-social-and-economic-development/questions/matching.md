# Sustainable Social and Economic Development — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** Four indicators — a)

**2.** Measuring real countries — b)


Meanings:
a) Match each indicator to its aspect: GDP per head at purchasing-power parity to society's capacity to produce goods and services; life expectancy to the quality and accessibility of healthcare; mean years of schooling to education's quality and reach; the Gini index to inequality in income distribution.
b) Work the table of sixteen countries — from Luxembourg and Singapore to Burundi and the Central African Republic — and see how the indicators separate: rich in output yet unequal, long-lived yet poor, schooled yet short-lived. Economic development and societal development are not the same thing, and sustainability asks whether either can continue.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
