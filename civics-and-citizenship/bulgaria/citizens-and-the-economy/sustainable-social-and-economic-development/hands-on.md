# Hands-on — Sustainable Social and Economic Development

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Choose two countries you know something about. Rank them by the four indicators from memory, then say which single indicator you would improve first in each — and why.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: a country's development is more than its wealth: it is measured by GDP per head, life expectancy, years of schooling and the Gini index together — and judged by whether it can last. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
