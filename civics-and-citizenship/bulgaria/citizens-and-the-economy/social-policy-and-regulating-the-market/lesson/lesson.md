# Social Policy and Regulating the Market

## Do it first

Take two workers — a dentist earning well part-time and a driver earning half as much full-time with three children. Argue the case for helping the second under each idea of fairness.

## Sub lesson 1 — Why the state steps in

To raise social justice the state has built instruments that supplement and correct the market mechanism's results — insurance funds and institutions that arrange and guarantee citizens' insurance rights. The social function is one of the state's basic functions, and social policy holds an important place among state policies.

## Sub lesson 2 — Two ideas of fairness

Fairness at the start: everyone begins from one starting line with equal chances, and the race decides the finish — society helps equalise chances at the career's beginning. Fairness at the finish: since not everyone can start equal, society helps those who fare worse during their careers, so that all finish at an equally high standard of living. Argue both, with life examples.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the market alone does not deliver social justice, so the state regulates it and runs social policy — and citizens must choose their idea of fairness: equal chances at the start, or an equal standard at the finish. Give one real example from your own life in Bulgaria, and name the lesson's key words: social policy, social justice, regulation, insurance. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Bulgarian as well as in English — Bulgaria's own book teaches Гражданско образование (Civic Education) in Bulgarian, for life in Bulgarian society, not only for the classroom.
