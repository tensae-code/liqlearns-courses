# Social Policy and Regulating the Market — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The market alone does not deliver social justice, so the state regulates it and runs social policy — and citizens must choose their idea of fairness: equal chances at the start, or an equal standard at the finish.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a citizen's own home, school or community in Bulgaria.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Bulgaria and lived there.
