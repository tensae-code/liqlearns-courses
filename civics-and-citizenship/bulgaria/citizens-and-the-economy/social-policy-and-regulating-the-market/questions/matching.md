# Social Policy and Regulating the Market — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** Why the state steps in — a)

**2.** Two ideas of fairness — b)


Meanings:
a) To raise social justice the state has built instruments that supplement and correct the market mechanism's results — insurance funds and institutions that arrange and guarantee citizens' insurance rights. The social function is one of the state's basic functions, and social policy holds an important place among state policies.
b) Fairness at the start: everyone begins from one starting line with equal chances, and the race decides the finish — society helps equalise chances at the career's beginning. Fairness at the finish: since not everyone can start equal, society helps those who fare worse during their careers, so that all finish at an equally high standard of living. Argue both, with life examples.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
