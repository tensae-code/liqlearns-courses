# Social Policy and Regulating the Market — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** The market alone does not deliver social justice, so the state regulates it and runs social policy — and citizens must choose their idea of fairness: equal chances at the start, or an equal standard at the finish.

**2.** What does the part "Two ideas of fairness" teach?
**Answer:** Fairness at the start: everyone begins from one starting line with equal chances, and the race decides the finish — society helps equalise chances at the career's beginning. Fairness at the finish: since not everyone can start equal, society helps those who fare worse during their careers, so that all finish at an equally high standard of living. Argue both, with life examples.
