## Sub lesson 2 — Two ideas of fairness
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Fairness at the start: everyone begins from one starting line with equal chances, and the race decides the finish — society helps equalise chances at the career's beginning. Fairness at the finish: since not everyone can start equal, society helps those who fare worse during their careers, so that all finish at an equally high standard of living. Argue both, with life examples. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
