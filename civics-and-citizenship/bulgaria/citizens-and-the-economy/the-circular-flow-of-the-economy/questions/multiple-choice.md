# The Circular Flow of the Economy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) households and firms keep the economy turning: households sell labour and buy goods, firms hire labour and sell goods, and two flows — the real flow and the money flow — run in opposite directions between them  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — Two actors, two markets?
a) The simplified model divides economic subjects into households and firms, meeting on two markets only: the markets for goods and the markets for labour. **Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
