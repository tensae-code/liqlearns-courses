# The Circular Flow of the Economy — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Households and firms keep the economy turning: households sell labour and buy goods, firms hire labour and sell goods, and two flows — the real flow and the money flow — run in opposite directions between them.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a citizen's own home, school or community in Bulgaria.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Bulgaria and lived there.
