# The Circular Flow of the Economy — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Households and firms keep the economy turning: households sell labour and buy goods, firms hire labour and sell goods, and two flows — the real flow and the money flow — run in opposite directions between them.

**2.** What does the part "Reading the scheme" teach?
**Answer:** Read the circular-flow scheme and complete the model: firms can hire the factors they need only on the markets, and households can buy the goods they need only on the markets. Civil society, as part of the model, watches that firms do not pollute and use natural resources responsibly, by the principles of sustainable development.
