# The Circular Flow of the Economy — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** Two actors, two markets — a)

**2.** Reading the scheme — b)


Meanings:
a) The simplified model divides economic subjects into households and firms, meeting on two markets only: the markets for goods and the markets for labour. Labour is owned by households; land and capital are owned and managed by firms, which are wholly owned by households.
b) Read the circular-flow scheme and complete the model: firms can hire the factors they need only on the markets, and households can buy the goods they need only on the markets. Civil society, as part of the model, watches that firms do not pollute and use natural resources responsibly, by the principles of sustainable development.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
