# Hands-on — The Circular Flow of the Economy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Draw the circular flow for your own household: one arrow of labour out, one arrow of money in, one arrow of spending out, one arrow of goods in. Label the real and the money flow.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: households and firms keep the economy turning: households sell labour and buy goods, firms hire labour and sell goods, and two flows — the real flow and the money flow — run in opposite directions between them. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
