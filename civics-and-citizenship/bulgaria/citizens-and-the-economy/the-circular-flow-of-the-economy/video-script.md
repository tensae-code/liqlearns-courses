# Video script — The Circular Flow of the Economy

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Households and firms keep the economy turning: households sell labour and buy goods, firms hire labour and sell goods, and two flows — the real flow and the money flow — run in opposite directions between them.

Two actors, two markets. The simplified model divides economic subjects into households and firms, meeting on two markets only: the markets for goods and the markets for labour. Labour is owned by households; land and capital are owned and managed by firms, which are wholly owned by households.

Reading the scheme. Read the circular-flow scheme and complete the model: firms can hire the factors they need only on the markets, and households can buy the goods they need only on the markets. Civil society, as part of the model, watches that firms do not pollute and use natural resources responsibly, by the principles of sustainable development.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
