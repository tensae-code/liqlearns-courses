# The Circular Flow of the Economy — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Two actors, two markets
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The simplified model divides economic subjects into households and firms, meeting on two markets only: the markets for goods and the markets for labour. Labour is owned by households; land and capital are owned and managed by firms, which are wholly owned by households. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — Reading the scheme
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Read the circular-flow scheme and complete the model: firms can hire the factors they need only on the markets, and households can buy the goods they need only on the markets. Civil society, as part of the model, watches that firms do not pollute and use natural resources responsibly, by the principles of sustainable development. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
