# The Labour Market and Public Policies — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** On the labour market the worker sells what is their own — their labour — freely and without coercion, the wage forms where supply meets demand, and public policy steps in where this market's special features hurt people.

**2.** What does the part "A market unlike others" teach?
**Answer:** The traded resource is heterogeneous — every worker is unique, and the quality of labour depends on education, qualification, experience, motivation and health. What is traded is time, given over by a labour contract that creates rights but also obligations. Wages form by supply and demand, tied to the demand for the goods that labour produces — and these features breed problems that call for public policy.
