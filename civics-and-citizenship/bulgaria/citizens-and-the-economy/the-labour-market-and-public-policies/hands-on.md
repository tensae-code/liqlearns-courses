# Hands-on — The Labour Market and Public Policies

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write your own labour as an offer: what you can do, what it is worth, and what would raise its price. Then name one rule the state sets on this market and who it protects.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: on the labour market the worker sells what is their own — their labour — freely and without coercion, the wage forms where supply meets demand, and public policy steps in where this market's special features hurt people. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
