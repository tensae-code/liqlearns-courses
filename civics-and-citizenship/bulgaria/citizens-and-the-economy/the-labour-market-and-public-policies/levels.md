# The Labour Market and Public Policies — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Labour as the worker's property
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Labour markets are markets for the factor labour: sellers — the workers — offer their labour to buyers — the employers. Because labour is the workers' private property, they decide how much to offer, at what price and to whom; the Constitution states expressly that no one may be forced into labour and workers freely choose their profession and workplace. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — A market unlike others
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The traded resource is heterogeneous — every worker is unique, and the quality of labour depends on education, qualification, experience, motivation and health. What is traded is time, given over by a labour contract that creates rights but also obligations. Wages form by supply and demand, tied to the demand for the goods that labour produces — and these features breed problems that call for public policy. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
