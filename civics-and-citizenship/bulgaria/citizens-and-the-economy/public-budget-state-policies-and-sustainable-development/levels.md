# Public Budget, State Policies and Sustainable Development — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Taxes and the budget
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: State spending is financed from state revenues and from debt, and most revenue comes from taxes — compulsory, non-refundable payments imposed by general rule. Direct taxes are collected straight from the liable persons on income and property; indirect taxes are charged by sellers into prices and passed to the budget, and they also steer behaviour, as with alcohol, cigarettes and fuels. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — The budget as a public plan
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The public budget is the advance account of the revenues and spending of all state institutions for a set period; its basis is the State Budget Law, drawn up and proposed by the government each year and voted by parliament. Through it the state conducts its economic, financial and social policy — to be judged through the prism of sustainable development. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
