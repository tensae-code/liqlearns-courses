# Public Budget, State Policies and Sustainable Development — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** Taxes and the budget — a)

**2.** The budget as a public plan — b)


Meanings:
a) State spending is financed from state revenues and from debt, and most revenue comes from taxes — compulsory, non-refundable payments imposed by general rule. Direct taxes are collected straight from the liable persons on income and property; indirect taxes are charged by sellers into prices and passed to the budget, and they also steer behaviour, as with alcohol, cigarettes and fuels.
b) The public budget is the advance account of the revenues and spending of all state institutions for a set period; its basis is the State Budget Law, drawn up and proposed by the government each year and voted by parliament. Through it the state conducts its economic, financial and social policy — to be judged through the prism of sustainable development.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
