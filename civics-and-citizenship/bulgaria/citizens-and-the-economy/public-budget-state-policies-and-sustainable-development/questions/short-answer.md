# Public Budget, State Policies and Sustainable Development — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** The public budget is how the state pays for its policies: taxes — direct and indirect — and debt fill it, parliament votes it, and sustainable development is the measure of whether the policies it funds are wise.

**2.** What does the part "The budget as a public plan" teach?
**Answer:** The public budget is the advance account of the revenues and spending of all state institutions for a set period; its basis is the State Budget Law, drawn up and proposed by the government each year and voted by parliament. Through it the state conducts its economic, financial and social policy — to be judged through the prism of sustainable development.
