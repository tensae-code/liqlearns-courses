# Public Budget, State Policies and Sustainable Development — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The public budget is how the state pays for its policies: taxes — direct and indirect — and debt fill it, parliament votes it, and sustainable development is the measure of whether the policies it funds are wise.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a citizen's own home, school or community in Bulgaria.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Bulgaria and lived there.
