# Lesson plan — Public Budget, State Policies and Sustainable Development

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Bulgaria · **Title:** citizens-and-the-economy · **Lesson:** Public Budget, State Policies and Sustainable Development

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Taxes and the budget** — State spending is financed from state revenues and from debt, and most revenue comes from taxes — compulsory, non-refundable payments imposed by general rule. Direct taxes are collected straight from the liable persons on income and property; indirect taxes are charged by sellers into prices and passed to the budget, and they also steer behaviour, as with alcohol, cigarettes and fuels.
2. **The budget as a public plan** — The public budget is the advance account of the revenues and spending of all state institutions for a set period; its basis is the State Budget Law, drawn up and proposed by the government each year and voted by parliament. Through it the state conducts its economic, financial and social policy — to be judged through the prism of sustainable development.
