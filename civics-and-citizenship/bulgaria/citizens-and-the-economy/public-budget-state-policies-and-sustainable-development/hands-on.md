# Hands-on — Public Budget, State Policies and Sustainable Development

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Follow one lev of tax: from a shop receipt at home, through the budget, to one public thing it helps pay for on your street. Name each step.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the public budget is how the state pays for its policies: taxes — direct and indirect — and debt fill it, parliament votes it, and sustainable development is the measure of whether the policies it funds are wise. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
