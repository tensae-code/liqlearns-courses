# Hands-on — Property and Contracts in Today's Market Economy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Argue Locke's side for two minutes — property as the fruit of labour — then argue against tying political weight to property for two minutes. Say which argument survives.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Locke's three fundamental rights — life, freedom and property — still frame today's market economy, and the citizen must be able to argue both sides: how far property's protection should go, and whether political weight should follow wealth. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
