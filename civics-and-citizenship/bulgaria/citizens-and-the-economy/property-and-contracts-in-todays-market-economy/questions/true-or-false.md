# Property and Contracts in Today's Market Economy — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Locke's three fundamental rights — life, freedom and property — still frame today's market economy, and the citizen must be able to argue both sides: how far property's protection should go, and whether political weight should follow wealth.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a citizen's own home, school or community in Bulgaria.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Bulgaria and lived there.
