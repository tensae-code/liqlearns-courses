# Property and Contracts in Today's Market Economy — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Locke's three fundamental rights — life, freedom and property — still frame today's market economy, and the citizen must be able to argue both sides: how far property's protection should go, and whether political weight should follow wealth.

**2.** What does the part "Locke debated" teach?
**Answer:** Locke counts life, freedom and property as the three fundamental rights, property being the natural continuation of life and freedom, to be protected with the state's full strictness. Debate it: should attacks on property be treated as strictly as attacks on life — and should citizens' political rights be proportional to their property, as Locke argued from labour?
