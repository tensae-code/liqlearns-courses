# Property and Contracts in Today's Market Economy — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** Athens and the vote — a)

**2.** Locke debated — b)


Meanings:
a) In mid-4th-century Athens, of 260,000 people only about 40,000 met the requirements to vote — male, adult, not enslaved, not foreign — and some 5,000, mostly wealthy owners, actually attended and decided. Compute the share that ruled, and judge whether it was just.
b) Locke counts life, freedom and property as the three fundamental rights, property being the natural continuation of life and freedom, to be protected with the state's full strictness. Debate it: should attacks on property be treated as strictly as attacks on life — and should citizens' political rights be proportional to their property, as Locke argued from labour?

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
