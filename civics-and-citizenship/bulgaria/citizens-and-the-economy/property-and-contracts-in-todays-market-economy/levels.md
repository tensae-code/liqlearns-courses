## Sub lesson 1 — Athens and the vote
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: In mid-4th-century Athens, of 260,000 people only about 40,000 met the requirements to vote — male, adult, not enslaved, not foreign — and some 5,000, mostly wealthy owners, actually attended and decided. Compute the share that ruled, and judge whether it was just. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — Locke debated
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Locke counts life, freedom and property as the three fundamental rights, property being the natural continuation of life and freedom, to be protected with the state's full strictness. Debate it: should attacks on property be treated as strictly as attacks on life — and should citizens' political rights be proportional to their property, as Locke argued from labour? — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
