# Property, Contracts and the Market Economy

## Do it first

List three things your household owns and one contract it lives by. Say which of your rights would remain exactly the same if you owned nothing at all.

## Sub lesson 1 — Property and the free citizen

In modern market economies the means of production are mostly private property, and relations are formally ordered by contracts. In ancient Greece only landowners could take part in political life; owning property was the condition for belonging to civil society, and only a few percent of Athenians decided.

## Sub lesson 2 — Property as a right, not a condition

In today's developed democracies property is accepted as a basic human right, yet it may not be demanded for participation in political life, because political self-determination is also a basic right, owed unconditionally to every citizen — even though citizens with more property still usually carry more weight in society.
