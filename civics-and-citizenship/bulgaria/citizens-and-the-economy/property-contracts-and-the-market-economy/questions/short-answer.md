# Property, Contracts and the Market Economy — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Property and contracts hold a market society together: property is a basic human right — but never a ticket one must hold to have political rights — and the contract is the basic civic bond between members of society.

**2.** What does the part "Property as a right, not a condition" teach?
**Answer:** In today's developed democracies property is accepted as a basic human right, yet it may not be demanded for participation in political life, because political self-determination is also a basic right, owed unconditionally to every citizen — even though citizens with more property still usually carry more weight in society.
