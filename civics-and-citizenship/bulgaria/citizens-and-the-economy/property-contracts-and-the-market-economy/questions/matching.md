# Property, Contracts and the Market Economy — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** Property and the free citizen — a)

**2.** Property as a right, not a condition — b)


Meanings:
a) In modern market economies the means of production are mostly private property, and relations are formally ordered by contracts. In ancient Greece only landowners could take part in political life; owning property was the condition for belonging to civil society, and only a few percent of Athenians decided.
b) In today's developed democracies property is accepted as a basic human right, yet it may not be demanded for participation in political life, because political self-determination is also a basic right, owed unconditionally to every citizen — even though citizens with more property still usually carry more weight in society.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
