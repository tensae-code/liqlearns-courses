# Property, Contracts and the Market Economy — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Property and contracts hold a market society together: property is a basic human right — but never a ticket one must hold to have political rights — and the contract is the basic civic bond between members of society.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a citizen's own home, school or community in Bulgaria.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Bulgaria and lived there.
