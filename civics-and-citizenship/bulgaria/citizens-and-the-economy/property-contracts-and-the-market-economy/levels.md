## Sub lesson 1 — Property and the free citizen
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: In modern market economies the means of production are mostly private property, and relations are formally ordered by contracts. In ancient Greece only landowners could take part in political life; owning property was the condition for belonging to civil society, and only a few percent of Athenians decided. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — Property as a right, not a condition
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: In today's developed democracies property is accepted as a basic human right, yet it may not be demanded for participation in political life, because political self-determination is also a basic right, owed unconditionally to every citizen — even though citizens with more property still usually carry more weight in society. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
