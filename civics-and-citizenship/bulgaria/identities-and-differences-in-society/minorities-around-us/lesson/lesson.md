# Minorities Around Us

## Do it first

Spend a day noticing: list the differences you actually meet — languages heard, customs seen, faiths practised. For one, write what you learned by asking rather than assuming.

## Sub lesson 1 — Who is around us

Bulgarian society holds many communities — by language, faith, origin and way of life. Meeting them as neighbours and classmates replaces distant labels with real people.

## Sub lesson 2 — Living together in fact

Living together is daily work: greeting, trading, studying and celebrating side by side, with curiosity about difference instead of fear of it.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: minorities are not abstractions: they are the neighbours, classmates and fellow citizens around us, with their own languages, faiths and customs. Give one real example from your own life in Bulgaria, and name the lesson's key words: minorities, neighbours, diversity, coexistence. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Bulgarian as well as in English — Bulgaria's own book teaches Гражданско образование (Civic Education) in Bulgarian, for life in Bulgarian society, not only for the classroom.
