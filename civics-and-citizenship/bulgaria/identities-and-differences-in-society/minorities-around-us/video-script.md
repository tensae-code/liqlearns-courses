# Video script — Minorities Around Us

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Minorities are not abstractions: they are the neighbours, classmates and fellow citizens around us, with their own languages, faiths and customs.

Who is around us. Bulgarian society holds many communities — by language, faith, origin and way of life. Meeting them as neighbours and classmates replaces distant labels with real people.

Living together in fact. Living together is daily work: greeting, trading, studying and celebrating side by side, with curiosity about difference instead of fear of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
