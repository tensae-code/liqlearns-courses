# Minorities Around Us — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) minorities are not abstractions: they are the neighbours, classmates and fellow citizens around us, with their own languages, faiths and customs  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — Who is around us?
a) Bulgarian society holds many communities — by language, faith, origin and way of life. Meeting them as neighbours and classmates replaces distant labels with real people.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
