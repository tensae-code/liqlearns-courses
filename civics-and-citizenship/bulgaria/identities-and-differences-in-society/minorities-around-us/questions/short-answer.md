# Minorities Around Us — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Minorities are not abstractions: they are the neighbours, classmates and fellow citizens around us, with their own languages, faiths and customs.

**2.** What does the part "Living together in fact" teach?
**Answer:** Living together is daily work: greeting, trading, studying and celebrating side by side, with curiosity about difference instead of fear of it.
