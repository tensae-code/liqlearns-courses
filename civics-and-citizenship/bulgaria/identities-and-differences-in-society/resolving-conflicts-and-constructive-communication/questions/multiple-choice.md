# Resolving Conflicts and Constructive Communication — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) conflicts are resolved without violence when people communicate constructively: listen fully, name interests not insults, and build an agreement both sides can keep  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — How conflicts grow and settle?
a) Conflicts grow by misunderstanding, insult and taking sides blindly; they settle when each side's real interest is heard and the quarrel is separated from the persons.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
