# Resolving Conflicts and Constructive Communication — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Conflicts are resolved without violence when people communicate constructively: listen fully, name interests not insults, and build an agreement both sides can keep.

**2.** What does the part "Constructive communication" teach?
**Answer:** Constructive talk listens to understand, speaks of one's own needs without attacking, checks what was heard, and proposes rather than accuses. It is a learnable skill, practised line by line.
