# Resolving Conflicts and Constructive Communication — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** How conflicts grow and settle — a)

**2.** Constructive communication — b)


Meanings:
a) Conflicts grow by misunderstanding, insult and taking sides blindly; they settle when each side's real interest is heard and the quarrel is separated from the persons.
b) Constructive talk listens to understand, speaks of one's own needs without attacking, checks what was heard, and proposes rather than accuses. It is a learnable skill, practised line by line.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
