# Hands-on — Resolving Conflicts and Constructive Communication

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Replay a small quarrel you know. Rewrite its three worst lines constructively — listen, name the interest, propose — and say what changes when they are spoken that way.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: conflicts are resolved without violence when people communicate constructively: listen fully, name interests not insults, and build an agreement both sides can keep. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
