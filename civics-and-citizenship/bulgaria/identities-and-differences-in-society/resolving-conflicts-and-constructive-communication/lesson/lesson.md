# Resolving Conflicts and Constructive Communication

## Do it first

Replay a small quarrel you know. Rewrite its three worst lines constructively — listen, name the interest, propose — and say what changes when they are spoken that way.

## Sub lesson 1 — How conflicts grow and settle

Conflicts grow by misunderstanding, insult and taking sides blindly; they settle when each side's real interest is heard and the quarrel is separated from the persons.

## Sub lesson 2 — Constructive communication

Constructive talk listens to understand, speaks of one's own needs without attacking, checks what was heard, and proposes rather than accuses. It is a learnable skill, practised line by line.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: conflicts are resolved without violence when people communicate constructively: listen fully, name interests not insults, and build an agreement both sides can keep. Give one real example from your own life in Bulgaria, and name the lesson's key words: conflict resolution, communication, listening, agreement. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Bulgarian as well as in English — Bulgaria's own book teaches Гражданско образование (Civic Education) in Bulgarian, for life in Bulgarian society, not only for the classroom.
