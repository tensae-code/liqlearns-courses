# Policies for Minorities — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — What minority policies do
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Policies turn duties into programmes: mother-tongue learning support, cultural preservation, equal access to work and services, and inclusion of minorities in decisions about them. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — Judging a policy
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: A policy is judged by its results in real lives and by whether the minority itself was heard in making it. A programme done to a minority, without it, usually misses. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
