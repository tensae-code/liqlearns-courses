# Policies for Minorities — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) minority policies are the state's practical programmes — in education, language, culture, work and inclusion — and they are judged by whether minorities' real lives improve  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — What minority policies do?
a) Policies turn duties into programmes: mother-tongue learning support, cultural preservation, equal access to work and services, and inclusion of minorities in decisions about them.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
