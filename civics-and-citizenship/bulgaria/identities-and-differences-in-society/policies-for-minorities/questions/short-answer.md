# Policies for Minorities — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Minority policies are the state's practical programmes — in education, language, culture, work and inclusion — and they are judged by whether minorities' real lives improve.

**2.** What does the part "Judging a policy" teach?
**Answer:** A policy is judged by its results in real lives and by whether the minority itself was heard in making it. A programme done to a minority, without it, usually misses.
