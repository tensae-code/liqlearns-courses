# Policies for Minorities — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** What minority policies do — a)

**2.** Judging a policy — b)


Meanings:
a) Policies turn duties into programmes: mother-tongue learning support, cultural preservation, equal access to work and services, and inclusion of minorities in decisions about them.
b) A policy is judged by its results in real lives and by whether the minority itself was heard in making it. A programme done to a minority, without it, usually misses.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
