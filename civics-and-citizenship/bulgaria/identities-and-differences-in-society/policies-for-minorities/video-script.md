# Video script — Policies for Minorities

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Minority policies are the state's practical programmes — in education, language, culture, work and inclusion — and they are judged by whether minorities' real lives improve.

What minority policies do. Policies turn duties into programmes: mother-tongue learning support, cultural preservation, equal access to work and services, and inclusion of minorities in decisions about them.

Judging a policy. A policy is judged by its results in real lives and by whether the minority itself was heard in making it. A programme done to a minority, without it, usually misses.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
