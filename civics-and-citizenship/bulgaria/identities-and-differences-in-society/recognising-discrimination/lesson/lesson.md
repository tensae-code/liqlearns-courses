# Recognising Discrimination

## Do it first

Take three scenes from school or street life. Run the recognition test on each: mark, treatment, fair reason or none. Record your verdict and evidence for each.

## Sub lesson 1 — The recognition test

Ask: what happened, what mark was in play, and would the treatment change if the mark changed? Unequal treatment without a fair, relevant reason is the signal.

## Sub lesson 2 — Signs in daily life

Refusals, exclusions, unequal rules, mocking and doors that open for some and not others — recognised early, discrimination can be named, questioned and stopped before it hardens.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: discrimination is recognised by testing treatment: would this person be treated the same if only the group mark changed?. Give one real example from your own life in Bulgaria, and name the lesson's key words: discrimination, recognition, evidence, equality. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Bulgarian as well as in English — Bulgaria's own book teaches Гражданско образование (Civic Education) in Bulgarian, for life in Bulgarian society, not only for the classroom.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: discrimination is recognised by testing treatment: would this person be treated the same if only the group mark changed?.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
