# Video script — Recognising Discrimination

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Discrimination is recognised by testing treatment: would this person be treated the same if only the group mark changed?.

The recognition test. Ask: what happened, what mark was in play, and would the treatment change if the mark changed? Unequal treatment without a fair, relevant reason is the signal.

Signs in daily life. Refusals, exclusions, unequal rules, mocking and doors that open for some and not others — recognised early, discrimination can be named, questioned and stopped before it hardens.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
