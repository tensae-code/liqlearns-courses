# Recognising Discrimination — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) discrimination is recognised by testing treatment: would this person be treated the same if only the group mark changed?  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — The recognition test?
a) Ask: what happened, what mark was in play, and would the treatment change if the mark changed? Unequal treatment without a fair, relevant reason is the signal.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
