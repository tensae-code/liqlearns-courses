# Recognising Discrimination — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Discrimination is recognised by testing treatment: would this person be treated the same if only the group mark changed?.

**2.** What does the part "Signs in daily life" teach?
**Answer:** Refusals, exclusions, unequal rules, mocking and doors that open for some and not others — recognised early, discrimination can be named, questioned and stopped before it hardens.
