# Protection from Discrimination

## Do it first

A classmate is excluded for a group mark. Plan the protection: what the victim can do, where they can complain, and what you as a bystander do in the first minute.

## Sub lesson 1 — Legal protection

Anti-discrimination law forbids unequal treatment on protected marks and gives the victim routes: complaint to the equality commission, the courts, and bodies that must answer.

## Sub lesson 2 — Our own protection work

Protection is also social: witnesses who speak, friends who stand beside the victim, schools and workplaces that act on the first sign. Silence protects the discriminator.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the law protects people from discrimination: the victim can complain, the equality body and the courts can act, and bystanders can refuse to go along. Give one real example from your own life in Bulgaria, and name the lesson's key words: protection, equality law, complaint, bystander. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Bulgarian as well as in English — Bulgaria's own book teaches Гражданско образование (Civic Education) in Bulgarian, for life in Bulgarian society, not only for the classroom.
