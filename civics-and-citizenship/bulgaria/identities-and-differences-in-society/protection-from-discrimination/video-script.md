# Video script — Protection from Discrimination

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The law protects people from discrimination: the victim can complain, the equality body and the courts can act, and bystanders can refuse to go along.

Legal protection. Anti-discrimination law forbids unequal treatment on protected marks and gives the victim routes: complaint to the equality commission, the courts, and bodies that must answer.

Our own protection work. Protection is also social: witnesses who speak, friends who stand beside the victim, schools and workplaces that act on the first sign. Silence protects the discriminator.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
