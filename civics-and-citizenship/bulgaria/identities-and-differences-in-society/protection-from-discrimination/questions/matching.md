# Protection from Discrimination — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** Legal protection — a)

**2.** Our own protection work — b)


Meanings:
a) Anti-discrimination law forbids unequal treatment on protected marks and gives the victim routes: complaint to the equality commission, the courts, and bodies that must answer.
b) Protection is also social: witnesses who speak, friends who stand beside the victim, schools and workplaces that act on the first sign. Silence protects the discriminator.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
