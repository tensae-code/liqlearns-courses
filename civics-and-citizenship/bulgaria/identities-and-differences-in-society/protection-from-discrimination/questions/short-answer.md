# Protection from Discrimination — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** The law protects people from discrimination: the victim can complain, the equality body and the courts can act, and bystanders can refuse to go along.

**2.** What does the part "Our own protection work" teach?
**Answer:** Protection is also social: witnesses who speak, friends who stand beside the victim, schools and workplaces that act on the first sign. Silence protects the discriminator.
