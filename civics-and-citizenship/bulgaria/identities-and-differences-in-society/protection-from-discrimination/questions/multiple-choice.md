# Protection from Discrimination — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) the law protects people from discrimination: the victim can complain, the equality body and the courts can act, and bystanders can refuse to go along  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — Legal protection?
a) Anti-discrimination law forbids unequal treatment on protected marks and gives the victim routes: complaint to the equality commission, the courts, and bodies that must answer.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
