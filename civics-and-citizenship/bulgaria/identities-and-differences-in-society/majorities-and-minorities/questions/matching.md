# Majorities and Minorities — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** Majority and minority — a)

**2.** The democratic test — b)


Meanings:
a) Majorities and minorities form wherever people differ and are counted: in a class vote, a town, a country. The same person can be majority in one count and minority in another.
b) Majority rule decides; minority rights limit. A democracy is judged not by how winners celebrate but by whether losers and minorities keep their dignity, rights and chance to become the majority.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
