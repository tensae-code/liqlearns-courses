# Majorities and Minorities — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** A majority is the larger part and a minority the smaller — by number, language, faith or origin — and democracy is tested by how the majority treats the minority.

**2.** What does the part "The democratic test" teach?
**Answer:** Majority rule decides; minority rights limit. A democracy is judged not by how winners celebrate but by whether losers and minorities keep their dignity, rights and chance to become the majority.
