# Video script — Majorities and Minorities

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A majority is the larger part and a minority the smaller — by number, language, faith or origin — and democracy is tested by how the majority treats the minority.

Majority and minority. Majorities and minorities form wherever people differ and are counted: in a class vote, a town, a country. The same person can be majority in one count and minority in another.

The democratic test. Majority rule decides; minority rights limit. A democracy is judged not by how winners celebrate but by whether losers and minorities keep their dignity, rights and chance to become the majority.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
