# Majorities and Minorities — sub lessons and depth

## Sub lesson 1 — Majority and minority
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Majorities and minorities form wherever people differ and are counted: in a class vote, a town, a country. The same person can be majority in one count and minority in another. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — The democratic test
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Majority rule decides; minority rights limit. A democracy is judged not by how winners celebrate but by whether losers and minorities keep their dignity, rights and chance to become the majority. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
