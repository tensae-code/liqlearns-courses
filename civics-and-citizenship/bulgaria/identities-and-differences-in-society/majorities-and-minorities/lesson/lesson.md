# Majorities and Minorities

## Do it first

Recall a vote you lost. List what you kept despite losing — voice, rights, next chance — then say which of those a real minority must never lose.

## Sub lesson 1 — Majority and minority

Majorities and minorities form wherever people differ and are counted: in a class vote, a town, a country. The same person can be majority in one count and minority in another.

## Sub lesson 2 — The democratic test

Majority rule decides; minority rights limit. A democracy is judged not by how winners celebrate but by whether losers and minorities keep their dignity, rights and chance to become the majority.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: a majority is the larger part and a minority the smaller — by number, language, faith or origin — and democracy is tested by how the majority treats the minority. Give one real example from your own life in Bulgaria, and name the lesson's key words: majority, minority, democracy, rights. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Bulgarian as well as in English — Bulgaria's own book teaches Гражданско образование (Civic Education) in Bulgarian, for life in Bulgarian society, not only for the classroom.
