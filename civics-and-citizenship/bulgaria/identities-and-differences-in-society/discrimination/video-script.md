# Video script — Discrimination

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Discrimination is unequal, unjust treatment of a person because of a group they belong to — not because of anything they have done.

What discrimination is. Discrimination treats a person worse for their origin, language, faith, gender, disability or other group mark. It judges the group instead of the person, and it wounds dignity and opportunity together.

Direct and hidden forms. Discrimination can be direct — a refusal stated openly — or hidden inside rules and habits that look neutral but push one group down. Both forms are unjust.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
