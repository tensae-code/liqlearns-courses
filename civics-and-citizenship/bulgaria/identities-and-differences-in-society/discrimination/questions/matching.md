# Discrimination — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** What discrimination is — a)

**2.** Direct and hidden forms — b)


Meanings:
a) Discrimination treats a person worse for their origin, language, faith, gender, disability or other group mark. It judges the group instead of the person, and it wounds dignity and opportunity together.
b) Discrimination can be direct — a refusal stated openly — or hidden inside rules and habits that look neutral but push one group down. Both forms are unjust.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
