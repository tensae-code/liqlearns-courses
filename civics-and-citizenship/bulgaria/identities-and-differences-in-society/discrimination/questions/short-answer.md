# Discrimination — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Discrimination is unequal, unjust treatment of a person because of a group they belong to — not because of anything they have done.

**2.** What does the part "Direct and hidden forms" teach?
**Answer:** Discrimination can be direct — a refusal stated openly — or hidden inside rules and habits that look neutral but push one group down. Both forms are unjust.
