# Discrimination — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) discrimination is unequal, unjust treatment of a person because of a group they belong to — not because of anything they have done  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — What discrimination is?
a) Discrimination treats a person worse for their origin, language, faith, gender, disability or other group mark. It judges the group instead of the person, and it wounds dignity and opportunity together.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
