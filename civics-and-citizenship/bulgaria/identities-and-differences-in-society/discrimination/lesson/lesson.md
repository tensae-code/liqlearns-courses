# Discrimination

## Do it first

Judge three cases: a shop refusing a customer, a rule nobody explains, a joke everyone laughs at. Say which are discrimination, what mark is used, and who is harmed.

## Sub lesson 1 — What discrimination is

Discrimination treats a person worse for their origin, language, faith, gender, disability or other group mark. It judges the group instead of the person, and it wounds dignity and opportunity together.

## Sub lesson 2 — Direct and hidden forms

Discrimination can be direct — a refusal stated openly — or hidden inside rules and habits that look neutral but push one group down. Both forms are unjust.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: discrimination is unequal, unjust treatment of a person because of a group they belong to — not because of anything they have done. Give one real example from your own life in Bulgaria, and name the lesson's key words: discrimination, equality, dignity, prejudice. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Bulgarian as well as in English — Bulgaria's own book teaches Гражданско образование (Civic Education) in Bulgarian, for life in Bulgarian society, not only for the classroom.
