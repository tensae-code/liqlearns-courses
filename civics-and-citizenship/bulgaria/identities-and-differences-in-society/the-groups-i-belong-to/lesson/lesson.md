# The Groups I Belong To

## Do it first

Draw your group map: six groups you belong to, one norm from each, and one place where two of them pull against each other. Say how you would judge it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Mapping our groups

Our groups differ in kind: some we are born into, some we join, some join us by place or circumstance. Each gives belonging, norms and a part of our identity.

## Sub lesson 2 — When groups pull apart

Group loyalties can conflict: what one group expects, another forbids. The mature citizen sees the pull clearly, judges by wider values and stays honest in every group.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: each of us belongs to many groups at once — family, class, town, language, faith, interests — and their demands sometimes pull in different directions. Give one real example from your own life in Bulgaria, and name the lesson's key words: groups, belonging, loyalty, identity. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Bulgarian as well as in English — Bulgaria's own book teaches Гражданско образование (Civic Education) in Bulgarian, for life in Bulgarian society, not only for the classroom.
