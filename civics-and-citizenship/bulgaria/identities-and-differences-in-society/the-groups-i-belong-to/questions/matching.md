# The Groups I Belong To — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** Mapping our groups — a)

**2.** When groups pull apart — b)


Meanings:
a) Our groups differ in kind: some we are born into, some we join, some join us by place or circumstance. Each gives belonging, norms and a part of our identity.
b) Group loyalties can conflict: what one group expects, another forbids. The mature citizen sees the pull clearly, judges by wider values and stays honest in every group.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
