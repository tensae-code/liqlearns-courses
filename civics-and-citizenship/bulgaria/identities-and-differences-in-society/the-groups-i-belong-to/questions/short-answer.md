# The Groups I Belong To — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Each of us belongs to many groups at once — family, class, town, language, faith, interests — and their demands sometimes pull in different directions.

**2.** What does the part "When groups pull apart" teach?
**Answer:** Group loyalties can conflict: what one group expects, another forbids. The mature citizen sees the pull clearly, judges by wider values and stays honest in every group.
