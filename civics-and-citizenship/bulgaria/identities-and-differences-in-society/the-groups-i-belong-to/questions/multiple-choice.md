# The Groups I Belong To — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) each of us belongs to many groups at once — family, class, town, language, faith, interests — and their demands sometimes pull in different directions  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — Mapping our groups?
a) Our groups differ in kind: some we are born into, some we join, some join us by place or circumstance. Each gives belonging, norms and a part of our identity.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
