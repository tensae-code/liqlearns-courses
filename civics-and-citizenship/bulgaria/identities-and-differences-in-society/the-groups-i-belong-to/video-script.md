# Video script — The Groups I Belong To

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Each of us belongs to many groups at once — family, class, town, language, faith, interests — and their demands sometimes pull in different directions.

Mapping our groups. Our groups differ in kind: some we are born into, some we join, some join us by place or circumstance. Each gives belonging, norms and a part of our identity.

When groups pull apart. Group loyalties can conflict: what one group expects, another forbids. The mature citizen sees the pull clearly, judges by wider values and stays honest in every group.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
