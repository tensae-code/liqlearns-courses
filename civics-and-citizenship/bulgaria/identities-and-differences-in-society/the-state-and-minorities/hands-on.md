# Hands-on — The State and Minorities

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Audit one public service — a school office, a clinic, a municipal desk. Ask: could every minority citizen use it fully and equally? Name one barrier and one fix.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the state owes its minorities equal citizenship: protection of their rights, language and culture, and a real share in public life. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
