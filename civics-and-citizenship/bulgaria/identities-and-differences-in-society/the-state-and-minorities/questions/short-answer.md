# The State and Minorities — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** The state owes its minorities equal citizenship: protection of their rights, language and culture, and a real share in public life.

**2.** What does the part "Equal citizenship in practice" teach?
**Answer:** Equal citizenship is measured in practice: documents, schooling, courts, media and office open on equal terms — not in declarations alone.
