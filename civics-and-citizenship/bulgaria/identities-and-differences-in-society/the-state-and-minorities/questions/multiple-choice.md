# The State and Minorities — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) the state owes its minorities equal citizenship: protection of their rights, language and culture, and a real share in public life  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — The state's duties?
a) The state must protect minorities from discrimination and violence, respect their language, faith and culture, and keep public services and justice equally open to them.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
