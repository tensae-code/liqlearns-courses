# The State and Minorities — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** The state's duties — a)

**2.** Equal citizenship in practice — b)


Meanings:
a) The state must protect minorities from discrimination and violence, respect their language, faith and culture, and keep public services and justice equally open to them.
b) Equal citizenship is measured in practice: documents, schooling, courts, media and office open on equal terms — not in declarations alone.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
