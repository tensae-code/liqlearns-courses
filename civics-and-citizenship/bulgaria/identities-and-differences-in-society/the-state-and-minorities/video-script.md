# Video script — The State and Minorities

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The state owes its minorities equal citizenship: protection of their rights, language and culture, and a real share in public life.

The state's duties. The state must protect minorities from discrimination and violence, respect their language, faith and culture, and keep public services and justice equally open to them.

Equal citizenship in practice. Equal citizenship is measured in practice: documents, schooling, courts, media and office open on equal terms — not in declarations alone.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
