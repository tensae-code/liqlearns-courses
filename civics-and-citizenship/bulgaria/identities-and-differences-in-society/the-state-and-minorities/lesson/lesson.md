# The State and Minorities

## Do it first

Audit one public service — a school office, a clinic, a municipal desk. Ask: could every minority citizen use it fully and equally? Name one barrier and one fix.

## Sub lesson 1 — The state's duties

The state must protect minorities from discrimination and violence, respect their language, faith and culture, and keep public services and justice equally open to them.

## Sub lesson 2 — Equal citizenship in practice

Equal citizenship is measured in practice: documents, schooling, courts, media and office open on equal terms — not in declarations alone.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the state owes its minorities equal citizenship: protection of their rights, language and culture, and a real share in public life. Give one real example from your own life in Bulgaria, and name the lesson's key words: state, minorities, equal citizenship, rights. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Bulgarian as well as in English — Bulgaria's own book teaches Гражданско образование (Civic Education) in Bulgarian, for life in Bulgarian society, not only for the classroom.
