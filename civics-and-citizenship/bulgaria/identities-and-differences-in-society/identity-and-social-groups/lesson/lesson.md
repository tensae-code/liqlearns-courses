# Identity and Social Groups

## Do it first

Write your identity in five lines: one given, one chosen, one from a group, one you are growing into, and one you refuse. Say which group shaped each.

## Sub lesson 1 — What identity is

Identity joins what is given — birth, language, place — with what is chosen and built: beliefs, work, commitments. It answers the question who am I, in our own words.

## Sub lesson 2 — Social groups form identity

Families, peers, communities and nations shape identity by their norms, stories and expectations. Belonging gives identity its materials; the person still composes their own.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: identity is who we understand ourselves to be, formed inside the social groups we belong to — and every person carries several identities at once. Give one real example from your own life in Bulgaria, and name the lesson's key words: identity, social groups, belonging, self. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Bulgarian as well as in English — Bulgaria's own book teaches Гражданско образование (Civic Education) in Bulgarian, for life in Bulgarian society, not only for the classroom.
