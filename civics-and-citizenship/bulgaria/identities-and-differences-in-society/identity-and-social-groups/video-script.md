# Video script — Identity and Social Groups

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Identity is who we understand ourselves to be, formed inside the social groups we belong to — and every person carries several identities at once.

What identity is. Identity joins what is given — birth, language, place — with what is chosen and built: beliefs, work, commitments. It answers the question who am I, in our own words.

Social groups form identity. Families, peers, communities and nations shape identity by their norms, stories and expectations. Belonging gives identity its materials; the person still composes their own.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
