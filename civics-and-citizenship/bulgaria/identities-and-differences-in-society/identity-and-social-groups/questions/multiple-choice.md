# Identity and Social Groups — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) identity is who we understand ourselves to be, formed inside the social groups we belong to — and every person carries several identities at once  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — What identity is?
a) Identity joins what is given — birth, language, place — with what is chosen and built: beliefs, work, commitments. It answers the question who am I, in our own words.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
