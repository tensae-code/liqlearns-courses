# Identity and Social Groups — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Identity is who we understand ourselves to be, formed inside the social groups we belong to — and every person carries several identities at once.

**2.** What does the part "Social groups form identity" teach?
**Answer:** Families, peers, communities and nations shape identity by their norms, stories and expectations. Belonging gives identity its materials; the person still composes their own.
