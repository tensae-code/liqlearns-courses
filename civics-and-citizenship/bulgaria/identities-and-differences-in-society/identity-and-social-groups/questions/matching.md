# Identity and Social Groups — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** What identity is — a)

**2.** Social groups form identity — b)


Meanings:
a) Identity joins what is given — birth, language, place — with what is chosen and built: beliefs, work, commitments. It answers the question who am I, in our own words.
b) Families, peers, communities and nations shape identity by their norms, stories and expectations. Belonging gives identity its materials; the person still composes their own.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
