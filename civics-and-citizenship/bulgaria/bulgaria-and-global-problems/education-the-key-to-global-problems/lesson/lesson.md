# Education — the Key to Global Problems

## Do it first

List five reasons a child near you might miss school, and one remedy for each that costs nothing. Then draw your own circular economy for one object you own.

## Sub lesson 1 — Why children are out of school

Name ten reasons millions of children worldwide do not attend school or lack access to education. In poor countries the low level of education is set by economic institutions that give parents no incentive and political institutions that do not make governments build, finance and maintain schools — and the price is potential talent working unwillingly in poverty.

## Sub lesson 2 — Responsible consumption

Comment on the circular economy scheme, part of Europe's climate policies, against the traditional model whose waste poisons air, water, sea and soil. Research the EU's long-term climate strategy: education here is not only schooling but the knowledge that changes how a whole society consumes.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: education is the key that opens global problems: nations that fail to educate pay for it in wasted talent, and responsible consumption with a circular economy is education applied to things. Give one real example from your own life in Bulgaria, and name the lesson's key words: education, global problems, circular economy, responsible consumption. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Bulgarian as well as in English — Bulgaria's own book teaches Гражданско образование (Civic Education) in Bulgarian, for life in Bulgarian society, not only for the classroom.
