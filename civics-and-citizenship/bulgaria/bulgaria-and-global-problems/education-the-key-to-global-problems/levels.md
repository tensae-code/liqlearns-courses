# Education — the Key to Global Problems — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Why children are out of school
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Name ten reasons millions of children worldwide do not attend school or lack access to education. In poor countries the low level of education is set by economic institutions that give parents no incentive and political institutions that do not make governments build, finance and maintain schools — and the price is potential talent working unwillingly in poverty. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
