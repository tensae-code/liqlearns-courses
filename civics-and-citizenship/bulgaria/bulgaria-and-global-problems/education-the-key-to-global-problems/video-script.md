# Video script — Education — the Key to Global Problems

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Education is the key that opens global problems: nations that fail to educate pay for it in wasted talent, and responsible consumption with a circular economy is education applied to things.

Why children are out of school. Name ten reasons millions of children worldwide do not attend school or lack access to education. In poor countries the low level of education is set by economic institutions that give parents no incentive and political institutions that do not make governments build, finance and maintain schools — and the price is potential talent working unwillingly in poverty.

Responsible consumption. Comment on the circular economy scheme, part of Europe's climate policies, against the traditional model whose waste poisons air, water, sea and soil. Research the EU's long-term climate strategy: education here is not only schooling but the knowledge that changes how a whole society consumes.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
