# Education — the Key to Global Problems — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Education is the key that opens global problems: nations that fail to educate pay for it in wasted talent, and responsible consumption with a circular economy is education applied to things.

**2.** What does the part "Responsible consumption" teach?
**Answer:** Comment on the circular economy scheme, part of Europe's climate policies, against the traditional model whose waste poisons air, water, sea and soil. Research the EU's long-term climate strategy: education here is not only schooling but the knowledge that changes how a whole society consumes.
