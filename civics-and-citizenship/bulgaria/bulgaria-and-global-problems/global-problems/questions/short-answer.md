# Global Problems — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Globalisation has bound the world into one interdependent system, and its problems are global too: inequality, exploitation, climate change and migration, which no state can solve alone.

**2.** What does the part "Globalisation's other face" teach?
**Answer:** Drawn into global processes unprepared, states and regions face growing inequality between poor and rich countries, unfair and inhuman labour conditions in low-wage countries, exploitation of people and natural resources, and climate change. Polarisation grows between those who enjoy globalisation's goods and those who cannot — and migration rises, for economic, political, cultural and now ecological reasons.
