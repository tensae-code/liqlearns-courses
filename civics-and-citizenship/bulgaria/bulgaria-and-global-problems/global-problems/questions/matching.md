# Global Problems — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** Globalisation's open horizons — a)

**2.** Globalisation's other face — b)


Meanings:
a) Globalisation — driven by transport, telecommunications and information technology — shrinks time and distance: finance moves virtually, trade turns world-wide, people, goods and capital move freely, and access to information, other cultures and self-realisation opens without limit. It cannot be stopped by anyone's will; it sets the frame of modern life.
b) Drawn into global processes unprepared, states and regions face growing inequality between poor and rich countries, unfair and inhuman labour conditions in low-wage countries, exploitation of people and natural resources, and climate change. Polarisation grows between those who enjoy globalisation's goods and those who cannot — and migration rises, for economic, political, cultural and now ecological reasons.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
