# Global Problems — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) globalisation has bound the world into one interdependent system, and its problems are global too: inequality, exploitation, climate change and migration, which no state can solve alone  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — Globalisation's open horizons?
a) Globalisation — driven by transport, telecommunications and information technology — shrinks time and distance: finance moves virtually, trade turns world-wide, people, goods and capital move freely, and access to information, other cultures and self-realisation opens without limit. It cannot be stopped by anyone's will; it sets the frame of modern life.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
