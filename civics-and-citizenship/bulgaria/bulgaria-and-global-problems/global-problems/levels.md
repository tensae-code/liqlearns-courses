# Global Problems — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Globalisation's open horizons
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Globalisation — driven by transport, telecommunications and information technology — shrinks time and distance: finance moves virtually, trade turns world-wide, people, goods and capital move freely, and access to information, other cultures and self-realisation opens without limit. It cannot be stopped by anyone's will; it sets the frame of modern life. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — Globalisation's other face
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Drawn into global processes unprepared, states and regions face growing inequality between poor and rich countries, unfair and inhuman labour conditions in low-wage countries, exploitation of people and natural resources, and climate change. Polarisation grows between those who enjoy globalisation's goods and those who cannot — and migration rises, for economic, political, cultural and now ecological reasons. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
