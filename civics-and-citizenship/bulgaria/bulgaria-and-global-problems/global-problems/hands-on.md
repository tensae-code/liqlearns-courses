# Hands-on — Global Problems

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name one global problem that has already touched your town. Trace its chain: where it began, how it travelled, and where it reached you.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: globalisation has bound the world into one interdependent system, and its problems are global too: inequality, exploitation, climate change and migration, which no state can solve alone. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
