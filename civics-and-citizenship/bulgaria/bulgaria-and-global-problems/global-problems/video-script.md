# Video script — Global Problems

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Globalisation has bound the world into one interdependent system, and its problems are global too: inequality, exploitation, climate change and migration, which no state can solve alone.

Globalisation's open horizons. Globalisation — driven by transport, telecommunications and information technology — shrinks time and distance: finance moves virtually, trade turns world-wide, people, goods and capital move freely, and access to information, other cultures and self-realisation opens without limit. It cannot be stopped by anyone's will; it sets the frame of modern life.

Globalisation's other face. Drawn into global processes unprepared, states and regions face growing inequality between poor and rich countries, unfair and inhuman labour conditions in low-wage countries, exploitation of people and natural resources, and climate change. Polarisation grows between those who enjoy globalisation's goods and those who cannot — and migration rises, for economic, political, cultural and now ecological reasons.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
