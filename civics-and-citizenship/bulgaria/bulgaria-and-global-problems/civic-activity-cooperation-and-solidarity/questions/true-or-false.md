# Civic Activity, Cooperation and Solidarity — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Behind every migration statistic is a person: solidarity begins by hearing real stories and telling voluntary migration — chosen for work and independence — from forced migration, driven by war, poverty and danger.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a citizen's own home, school or community in Bulgaria.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Bulgaria and lived there.
