# Civic Activity, Cooperation and Solidarity — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** Five real stories — a)

**2.** Voluntary or forced — b)


Meanings:
a) A Romanian cleaner in Vienna earning three times more but uninsured; a Hungarian waiter in Italy building an independent life; a young Sudanese man fleeing civil war and poverty; a Moroccan woman picking strawberries fourteen hours a day in Spain, illegally and in debt to a smuggler; a Dutch woman whose family spans four countries. Find each one's motive.
b) Mark each story's migration voluntary or forced, and defend the mark. The exercise trains the exact judgement solidarity needs: the same border crossing can be a free choice, an economic necessity, or a flight for one's life — and cooperation with each person starts from knowing which.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
