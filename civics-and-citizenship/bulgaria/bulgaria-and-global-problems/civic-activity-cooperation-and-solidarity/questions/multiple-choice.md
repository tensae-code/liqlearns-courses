# Civic Activity, Cooperation and Solidarity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) behind every migration statistic is a person: solidarity begins by hearing real stories and telling voluntary migration — chosen for work and independence — from forced migration, driven by war, poverty and danger  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — Five real stories?
a) A Romanian cleaner in Vienna earning three times more but uninsured; a Hungarian waiter in Italy building an independent life; a young Sudanese man fleeing civil war and poverty; a Moroccan woman picking strawberries fourteen hours a day in Spain, illegally and in debt to a smuggler; a Dutch woman whose family spans four countries. Find each one's motive.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
