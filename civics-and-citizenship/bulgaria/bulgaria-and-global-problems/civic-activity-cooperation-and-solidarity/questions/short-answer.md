# Civic Activity, Cooperation and Solidarity — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Behind every migration statistic is a person: solidarity begins by hearing real stories and telling voluntary migration — chosen for work and independence — from forced migration, driven by war, poverty and danger.

**2.** What does the part "Voluntary or forced" teach?
**Answer:** Mark each story's migration voluntary or forced, and defend the mark. The exercise trains the exact judgement solidarity needs: the same border crossing can be a free choice, an economic necessity, or a flight for one's life — and cooperation with each person starts from knowing which.
