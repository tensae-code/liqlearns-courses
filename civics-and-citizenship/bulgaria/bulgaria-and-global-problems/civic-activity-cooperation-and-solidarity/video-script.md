# Video script — Civic Activity, Cooperation and Solidarity

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Behind every migration statistic is a person: solidarity begins by hearing real stories and telling voluntary migration — chosen for work and independence — from forced migration, driven by war, poverty and danger.

Five real stories. A Romanian cleaner in Vienna earning three times more but uninsured; a Hungarian waiter in Italy building an independent life; a young Sudanese man fleeing civil war and poverty; a Moroccan woman picking strawberries fourteen hours a day in Spain, illegally and in debt to a smuggler; a Dutch woman whose family spans four countries. Find each one's motive.

Voluntary or forced. Mark each story's migration voluntary or forced, and defend the mark. The exercise trains the exact judgement solidarity needs: the same border crossing can be a free choice, an economic necessity, or a flight for one's life — and cooperation with each person starts from knowing which.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
