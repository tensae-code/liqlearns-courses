# Civic Activity, Cooperation and Solidarity — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Five real stories
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: A Romanian cleaner in Vienna earning three times more but uninsured; a Hungarian waiter in Italy building an independent life; a young Sudanese man fleeing civil war and poverty; a Moroccan woman picking strawberries fourteen hours a day in Spain, illegally and in debt to a smuggler; a Dutch woman whose family spans four countries. Find each one's motive. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — Voluntary or forced
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Mark each story's migration voluntary or forced, and defend the mark. The exercise trains the exact judgement solidarity needs: the same border crossing can be a free choice, an economic necessity, or a flight for one's life — and cooperation with each person starts from knowing which. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
