# Hands-on — Civic Activity, Cooperation and Solidarity

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Interview — really or in imagination — one person who moved town or country. Write their motive in two lines and mark the move voluntary or forced, with your reason.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: behind every migration statistic is a person: solidarity begins by hearing real stories and telling voluntary migration — chosen for work and independence — from forced migration, driven by war, poverty and danger. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
