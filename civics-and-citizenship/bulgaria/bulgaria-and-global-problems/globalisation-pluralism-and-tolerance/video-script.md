# Video script — Globalisation, Pluralism and Tolerance

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Living in a globalised world takes pluralism and tolerance: many peoples, views and ways of life inside one global village, where each person is also a citizen of the world.

Words for a wired world. Explain in your own words: the global village, the citizen of the world, the global player, globalisation itself. Then name three leading countries of world industry and say by which criteria — economic, political, ecological — you chose them, and three main causes that drive globalisation.

Milestones on a timeline. Match the events to their years and judge which belong to globalisation's process: the first intercontinental flight, the first home television, the first container ships, the first industrial robot, the first personal computer, the world computer network, Facebook, the first smartphone, the first mobile message, the first digital currency, the first drone delivery.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
