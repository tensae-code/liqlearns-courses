# Globalisation, Pluralism and Tolerance — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Living in a globalised world takes pluralism and tolerance: many peoples, views and ways of life inside one global village, where each person is also a citizen of the world.

**2.** What does the part "Milestones on a timeline" teach?
**Answer:** Match the events to their years and judge which belong to globalisation's process: the first intercontinental flight, the first home television, the first container ships, the first industrial robot, the first personal computer, the world computer network, Facebook, the first smartphone, the first mobile message, the first digital currency, the first drone delivery.
