# Globalisation, Pluralism and Tolerance — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) living in a globalised world takes pluralism and tolerance: many peoples, views and ways of life inside one global village, where each person is also a citizen of the world  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — Words for a wired world?
a) Explain in your own words: the global village, the citizen of the world, the global player, globalisation itself. Then name three leading countries of world industry and say by which criteria — economic, political, ecological — you chose them, and three main causes that drive globalisation.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
