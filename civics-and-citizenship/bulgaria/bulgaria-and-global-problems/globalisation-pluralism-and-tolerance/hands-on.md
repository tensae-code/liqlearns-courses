# Hands-on — Globalisation, Pluralism and Tolerance

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write your own definitions of 'global village' and 'citizen of the world' in one sentence each. Then name one thing in your room that proves the first, and one duty that proves the second.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: living in a globalised world takes pluralism and tolerance: many peoples, views and ways of life inside one global village, where each person is also a citizen of the world. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
