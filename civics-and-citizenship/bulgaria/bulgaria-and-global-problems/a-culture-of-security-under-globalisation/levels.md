## Sub lesson 1 — The seventeen goals
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Recall the Sustainable Development Goals in order: no poverty; zero hunger; good health; quality education; gender equality; clean water and sanitation; renewable energy; decent work and economic growth; innovation and infrastructure; reduced inequalities; sustainable cities and communities; responsible consumption; climate action; life below water; life on land; peace and justice; partnerships for the goals. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — One proposal for each
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Write one proposal of your own for achieving each of the seventeen goals. The exercise is the culture of security in miniature: safety, health, justice and a livable planet are built from concrete, nameable steps — not waited for. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
