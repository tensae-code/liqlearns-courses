# Hands-on — A Culture of Security under Globalisation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Take the three goals closest to your town's life. Write one proposal for each that a school class could actually carry out this term.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: security in a globalised world is a culture, not only a force: the seventeen Sustainable Development Goals are its shared programme, and every citizen can propose a step toward each. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
