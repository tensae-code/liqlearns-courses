# A Culture of Security under Globalisation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) security in a globalised world is a culture, not only a force: the seventeen Sustainable Development Goals are its shared programme, and every citizen can propose a step toward each  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — The seventeen goals?
a) Recall the Sustainable Development Goals in order: no poverty; zero hunger; good health; quality education; gender equality; clean water and sanitation; renewable energy; decent work and economic growth; innovation and infrastructure; reduced inequalities; sustainable cities and communities; responsible consumption; climate action; life below water; life on land; peace and justice; partnerships for the goals.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
