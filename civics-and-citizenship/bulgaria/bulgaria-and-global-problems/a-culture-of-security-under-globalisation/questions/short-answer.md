# A Culture of Security under Globalisation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Security in a globalised world is a culture, not only a force: the seventeen Sustainable Development Goals are its shared programme, and every citizen can propose a step toward each.

**2.** What does the part "One proposal for each" teach?
**Answer:** Write one proposal of your own for achieving each of the seventeen goals. The exercise is the culture of security in miniature: safety, health, justice and a livable planet are built from concrete, nameable steps — not waited for.
