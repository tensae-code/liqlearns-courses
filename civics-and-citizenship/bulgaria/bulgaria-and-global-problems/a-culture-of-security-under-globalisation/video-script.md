# Video script — A Culture of Security under Globalisation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Security in a globalised world is a culture, not only a force: the seventeen Sustainable Development Goals are its shared programme, and every citizen can propose a step toward each.

The seventeen goals. Recall the Sustainable Development Goals in order: no poverty; zero hunger; good health; quality education; gender equality; clean water and sanitation; renewable energy; decent work and economic growth; innovation and infrastructure; reduced inequalities; sustainable cities and communities; responsible consumption; climate action; life below water; life on land; peace and justice; partnerships for the goals.

One proposal for each. Write one proposal of your own for achieving each of the seventeen goals. The exercise is the culture of security in miniature: safety, health, justice and a livable planet are built from concrete, nameable steps — not waited for.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
