# A Culture of Security under Globalisation

## Do it first

Take the three goals closest to your town's life. Write one proposal for each that a school class could actually carry out this term.

## Sub lesson 1 — The seventeen goals

Recall the Sustainable Development Goals in order: no poverty; zero hunger; good health; quality education; gender equality; clean water and sanitation; renewable energy; decent work and economic growth; innovation and infrastructure; reduced inequalities; sustainable cities and communities; responsible consumption; climate action; life below water; life on land; peace and justice; partnerships for the goals.

## Sub lesson 2 — One proposal for each

Write one proposal of your own for achieving each of the seventeen goals. The exercise is the culture of security in miniature: safety, health, justice and a livable planet are built from concrete, nameable steps — not waited for.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: security in a globalised world is a culture, not only a force: the seventeen Sustainable Development Goals are its shared programme, and every citizen can propose a step toward each. Give one real example from your own life in Bulgaria, and name the lesson's key words: security, sustainable development goals, globalisation, peace. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Bulgarian as well as in English — Bulgaria's own book teaches Гражданско образование (Civic Education) in Bulgarian, for life in Bulgarian society, not only for the classroom.
