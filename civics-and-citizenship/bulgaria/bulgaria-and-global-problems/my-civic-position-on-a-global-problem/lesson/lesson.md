# My Civic Position on a Global Problem

## Do it first

Write your civic position on one global problem in five lines: the problem, the future you want, the project you propose, your own task in it.

## Sub lesson 1 — A look into the future

By 2050 the world will look very different. Imagine the changes — and the problems travelling with them — in transport, energy, the food industry, telecommunications, trade, financial services, industrial production and healthcare, at local, regional and global level, and choose the problem your position addresses.

## Sub lesson 2 — The plan-scheme

Build the project's plan-scheme as in Theme 1: the participants with a project leader, the theme, the materials you will rely on and who researches and summarises them, the form of presentation — presentation, reportage, film, interview, poster, photographs, games — and the preliminary preparation, who takes which task.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: a civic position is not an opinion but a plan: imagine the world of 2050, choose one global problem, and build the project that states what you will do about it. Give one real example from your own life in Bulgaria, and name the lesson's key words: civic position, global problem, project, future. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Bulgarian as well as in English — Bulgaria's own book teaches Гражданско образование (Civic Education) in Bulgarian, for life in Bulgarian society, not only for the classroom.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: a civic position is not an opinion but a plan: imagine the world of 2050, choose one global problem, and build the project that states what you will do about it.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
