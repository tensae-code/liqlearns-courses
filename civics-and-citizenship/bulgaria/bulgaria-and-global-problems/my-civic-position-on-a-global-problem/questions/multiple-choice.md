# My Civic Position on a Global Problem — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) a civic position is not an opinion but a plan: imagine the world of 2050, choose one global problem, and build the project that states what you will do about it  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — A look into the future?
a) By 2050 the world will look very different. Imagine the changes — and the problems travelling with them — in transport, energy, the food industry, telecommunications, trade, financial services, industrial production and healthcare, at local, regional and global level, and choose the problem your position addresses.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
