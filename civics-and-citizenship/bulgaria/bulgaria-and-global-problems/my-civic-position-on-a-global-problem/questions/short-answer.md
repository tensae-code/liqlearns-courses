# My Civic Position on a Global Problem — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** A civic position is not an opinion but a plan: imagine the world of 2050, choose one global problem, and build the project that states what you will do about it.

**2.** What does the part "The plan-scheme" teach?
**Answer:** Build the project's plan-scheme as in Theme 1: the participants with a project leader, the theme, the materials you will rely on and who researches and summarises them, the form of presentation — presentation, reportage, film, interview, poster, photographs, games — and the preliminary preparation, who takes which task.
