# Hands-on — My Civic Position on a Global Problem

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write your civic position on one global problem in five lines: the problem, the future you want, the project you propose, your own task in it.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: a civic position is not an opinion but a plan: imagine the world of 2050, choose one global problem, and build the project that states what you will do about it. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
