# Video script — My Civic Position on a Global Problem

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A civic position is not an opinion but a plan: imagine the world of 2050, choose one global problem, and build the project that states what you will do about it.

A look into the future. By 2050 the world will look very different. Imagine the changes — and the problems travelling with them — in transport, energy, the food industry, telecommunications, trade, financial services, industrial production and healthcare, at local, regional and global level, and choose the problem your position addresses.

The plan-scheme. Build the project's plan-scheme as in Theme 1: the participants with a project leader, the theme, the materials you will rely on and who researches and summarises them, the form of presentation — presentation, reportage, film, interview, poster, photographs, games — and the preliminary preparation, who takes which task.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
