# Nature and Culture under Globalisation — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Nature and the limits of exploitation
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: A century of excessive industrialisation has destroyed the environment and steadily shrunk natural resources; overconsumption threatens the Earth itself — global waste pollution, harmful production lowering air quality, shrinking water resources, warming with rising seas, extreme weather, dying species and shortages of food and water. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — One system, two kinds of law
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The Earth is a whole interconnected system governed by ecological and by social laws, because people are part of it. The ruling linear model — extract resources, process them into final goods, discard — treats nature as inexhaustible; pressed against the planet's limits, it cannot be sustainable, green growth notwithstanding. Gandhi's measure stands over the lesson: the Earth has enough for everyone's need, not for everyone's greed. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
