# Nature and Culture under Globalisation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Nature has limits that globalised overconsumption is pressing against: the Earth is one interconnected system, and a culture of tolerance, solidarity and cooperation is the human answer to living inside it.

**2.** What does the part "One system, two kinds of law" teach?
**Answer:** The Earth is a whole interconnected system governed by ecological and by social laws, because people are part of it. The ruling linear model — extract resources, process them into final goods, discard — treats nature as inexhaustible; pressed against the planet's limits, it cannot be sustainable, green growth notwithstanding. Gandhi's measure stands over the lesson: the Earth has enough for everyone's need, not for everyone's greed.
