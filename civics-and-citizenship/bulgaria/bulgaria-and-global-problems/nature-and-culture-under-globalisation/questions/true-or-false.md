# Nature and Culture under Globalisation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Nature has limits that globalised overconsumption is pressing against: the Earth is one interconnected system, and a culture of tolerance, solidarity and cooperation is the human answer to living inside it.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a citizen's own home, school or community in Bulgaria.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Bulgaria and lived there.
