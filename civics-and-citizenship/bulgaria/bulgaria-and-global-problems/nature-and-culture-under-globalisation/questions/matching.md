# Nature and Culture under Globalisation — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** Nature and the limits of exploitation — a)

**2.** One system, two kinds of law — b)


Meanings:
a) A century of excessive industrialisation has destroyed the environment and steadily shrunk natural resources; overconsumption threatens the Earth itself — global waste pollution, harmful production lowering air quality, shrinking water resources, warming with rising seas, extreme weather, dying species and shortages of food and water.
b) The Earth is a whole interconnected system governed by ecological and by social laws, because people are part of it. The ruling linear model — extract resources, process them into final goods, discard — treats nature as inexhaustible; pressed against the planet's limits, it cannot be sustainable, green growth notwithstanding. Gandhi's measure stands over the lesson: the Earth has enough for everyone's need, not for everyone's greed.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
