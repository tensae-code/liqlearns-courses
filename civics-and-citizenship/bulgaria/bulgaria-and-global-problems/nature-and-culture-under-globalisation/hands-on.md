# Hands-on — Nature and Culture under Globalisation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Audit one day of your household's consumption: three things taken from nature, three thrown back at it. Mark where the linear chain could bend into a circle.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: nature has limits that globalised overconsumption is pressing against: the Earth is one interconnected system, and a culture of tolerance, solidarity and cooperation is the human answer to living inside it. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
