# Video script — Nature and Culture under Globalisation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Nature has limits that globalised overconsumption is pressing against: the Earth is one interconnected system, and a culture of tolerance, solidarity and cooperation is the human answer to living inside it.

Nature and the limits of exploitation. A century of excessive industrialisation has destroyed the environment and steadily shrunk natural resources; overconsumption threatens the Earth itself — global waste pollution, harmful production lowering air quality, shrinking water resources, warming with rising seas, extreme weather, dying species and shortages of food and water.

One system, two kinds of law. The Earth is a whole interconnected system governed by ecological and by social laws, because people are part of it. The ruling linear model — extract resources, process them into final goods, discard — treats nature as inexhaustible; pressed against the planet's limits, it cannot be sustainable, green growth notwithstanding. Gandhi's measure stands over the lesson: the Earth has enough for everyone's need, not for everyone's greed.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
