## Sub lesson 1 — What the millennials fear
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The World Economic Forum's Global Shapers Survey asked over 31,000 people aged 18–35: nearly half named climate change and the destruction of nature the biggest global problem, over 78% would change their way of life to protect the environment, and 91% agreed people are to blame for climate change. The ten problems run from climate through escalating conflicts and wars, inequality and discrimination, poverty, religious conflicts, corruption, food and water security, education access, safety, and lack of economic opportunity. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
