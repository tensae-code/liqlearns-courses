# Video script — Together We Can Do More

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The young generation has already ranked the world's problems — climate first — and the course ends where citizenship begins: choose one problem of national significance and carry out its project with like-minded partners.

What the millennials fear. The World Economic Forum's Global Shapers Survey asked over 31,000 people aged 18–35: nearly half named climate change and the destruction of nature the biggest global problem, over 78% would change their way of life to protect the environment, and 91% agreed people are to blame for climate change. The ten problems run from climate through escalating conflicts and wars, inequality and discrimination, poverty, religious conflicts, corruption, food and water security, education access, safety, and lack of economic opportunity.

Choosing and carrying out. Choose a theme for your project — solving a social, ecological, demographic or health problem of national significance — and realise it with the help of like-minded people. Together we can do more is the book's last word: the citizen leaves school with a problem chosen, partners found, and a plan in hand.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
