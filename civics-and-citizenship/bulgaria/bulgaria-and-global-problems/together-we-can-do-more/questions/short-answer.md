# Together We Can Do More — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** The young generation has already ranked the world's problems — climate first — and the course ends where citizenship begins: choose one problem of national significance and carry out its project with like-minded partners.

**2.** What does the part "Choosing and carrying out" teach?
**Answer:** Choose a theme for your project — solving a social, ecological, demographic or health problem of national significance — and realise it with the help of like-minded people. Together we can do more is the book's last word: the citizen leaves school with a problem chosen, partners found, and a plan in hand.
