# Together We Can Do More — matching

**2.** Choosing and carrying out — b)

a) The World Economic Forum's Global Shapers Survey asked over 31,000 people aged 18–35: nearly half named climate change and the destruction of nature the biggest global problem, over 78% would change their way of life to protect the environment, and 91% agreed people are to blame for climate change. Together we can do more is the book's last word: the citizen leaves school with a problem chosen, partners found, and a plan in hand.
