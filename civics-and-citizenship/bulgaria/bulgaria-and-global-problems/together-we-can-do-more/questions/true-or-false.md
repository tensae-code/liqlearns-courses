# Together We Can Do More — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The young generation has already ranked the world's problems — climate first — and the course ends where citizenship begins: choose one problem of national significance and carry out its project with like-minded partners.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a citizen's own home, school or community in Bulgaria.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Bulgaria and lived there.
