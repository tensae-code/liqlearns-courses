# Hands-on — Together We Can Do More

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Rank the ten problems for your own town, honestly. Then write the first message you would send to two like-minded partners to start the project on your number one.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the young generation has already ranked the world's problems — climate first — and the course ends where citizenship begins: choose one problem of national significance and carry out its project with like-minded partners. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
