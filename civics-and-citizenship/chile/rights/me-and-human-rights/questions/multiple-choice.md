# Me and Human Rights — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** In what three places does each person stand with human rights?
a) holder of their own rights, witness of others' rights, and defender of both  b) only as a holder, never as anything else  c) only as a judge of other people  d) as owner of other people's rights
**Answer: a) holder of their own rights, witness of others' rights, and defender of both** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does a defender of rights do?
a) speaks, helps, reports, and stands with the person whose right is harmed  b) looks away whenever a right is harmed  c) waits for someone with a title to act first  d) defends only their own rights, never others'
**Answer: a) speaks, helps, reports, and stands with the person whose right is harmed** — That is what this lesson teaches.
