# Me and Human Rights — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why does the lesson call each of us a witness of rights?
**Answer:** Because we see other people's rights respected or harmed every day — at home, at school, in the street — and whether we look away is part of how rights live or die around us.

**2.** Does defending rights need a title or an office? Why not?
**Answer:** No — it begins with refusing to treat another person's harm as none of our business, and with the ways open to us: speaking, helping, reporting, standing with the person harmed.
