# Me and Human Rights — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Human rights concern only people in faraway countries.
**Answer: False** — Each person relates to human rights here and now — as holder of their own, witness of others', and defender of both.

**2.** A right you cannot name is hard to claim when you need it.
**Answer: True**
