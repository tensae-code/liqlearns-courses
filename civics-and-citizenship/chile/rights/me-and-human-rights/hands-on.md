# Hands-on — Me and Human Rights

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write three short lines about yourself: one right you hold, one time you saw someone else's right harmed or respected, and one thing you could do if you saw a right being harmed. This lesson gives those three lines their names: holder, witness, defender.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: each person stands in three places at once with human rights: holder of their own, witness of others', and defender of both. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
