# How the State Defends Our Rights — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** How does the state defend human rights in practice?
a) by guaranteeing access to justice — a real way to claim a right when it is harmed  b) by printing the rights in a book no one reads  c) by punishing people before hearing them  d) by keeping the courts closed to ordinary people
**Answer: a) by guaranteeing access to justice — a real way to claim a right when it is harmed** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does access to justice mean?
a) a real, open road to claim a right: to be heard, present your case, and have it decided fairly  b) only the rich can use the courts  c) a promise that is never kept  d) deciding cases by who shouts loudest
**Answer: a) a real, open road to claim a right: to be heard, present your case, and have it decided fairly** — That is what this lesson teaches.
