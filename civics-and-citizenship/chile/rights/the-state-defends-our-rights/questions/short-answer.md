# How the State Defends Our Rights — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why is access to justice called the state's defence of rights 'in practice'?
**Answer:** Because it is where a harmed right is actually heard, decided and repaired — not in speeches, but in what happens to the person who was harmed.

**2.** What three things does access to justice give the person claiming a right?
**Answer:** To be heard, to present their case, and to have it decided fairly.
