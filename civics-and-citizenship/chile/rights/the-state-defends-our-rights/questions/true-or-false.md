# How the State Defends Our Rights — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A right on paper protects a person even when there is no way to claim it.
**Answer: False** — A right protects no one until there is a real road to claim it — that road is access to justice, guaranteed by the state.

**2.** The road to justice must be open to everyone.
**Answer: True**
