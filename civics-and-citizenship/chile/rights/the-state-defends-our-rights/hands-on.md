# Hands-on — How the State Defends Our Rights

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Think of one unfair thing you have seen — someone cheated, hurt, or shut out. Write what happened, and then write where that person could turn for justice. This lesson follows that road: how access to justice is guaranteed.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the state defends our human rights in practice by guaranteeing access to justice — a real way to claim a right when it is harmed. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
