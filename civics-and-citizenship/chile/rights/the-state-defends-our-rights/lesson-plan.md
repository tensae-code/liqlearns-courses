# Lesson plan — How the State Defends Our Rights

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Chile · **Title:** Rights · **Lesson:** How the State Defends Our Rights
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — Rights (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the state defends our human rights in practice by guaranteeing access to justice — a real way to claim a right when it is harmed.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Rights need a defender** — Human rights belong to every person — and the state is charged with defending them, not as a favour but as its duty.
2. **Access to justice** — Access to justice means a real, open road to claim a right: to be heard, to present your case, and to have it decided fairly.
3. **Justice in practice** — When a right is harmed, justice in practice is the claim being heard and answered: the wrong examined, decided, and repaired as far as it can be.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
