## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **What a development model is** — A development model is the way a country chooses to grow: what it produces, how it works and trades, and what it treats as wealth and as cost.
2. **Models in daily life** — The model reaches the kitchen table: the work available, the prices charged, the journeys taken and the goods on the shelf are the model, lived daily.
3. **Models and climate change** — Models also meet the climate: how a country produces, travels and consumes shapes what it emits and how exposed it stands to a changing climate.
