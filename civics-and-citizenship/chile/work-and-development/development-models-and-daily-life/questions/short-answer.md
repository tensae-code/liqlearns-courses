# Development Models and Daily Life — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why should a citizen be able to name the development model they live in?
**Answer:** Because the model decides the work, prices, journeys and goods of their daily life — naming it is the first step to judging it.

**2.** What are the model's 'two mirrors' at the end of this lesson?
**Answer:** Daily life and the climate: how the country grows is reflected in the citizen's ordinary day and in the changing climate alike.
