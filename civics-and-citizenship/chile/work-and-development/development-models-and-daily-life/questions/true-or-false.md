# Development Models and Daily Life — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A development model is only an abstract idea for economists.
**Answer: False** — A model is lived daily — it decides what is produced, how people work and travel — and it shapes the climate too.

**2.** How a country produces, travels and consumes shapes how exposed it stands to climate change.
**Answer: True**
