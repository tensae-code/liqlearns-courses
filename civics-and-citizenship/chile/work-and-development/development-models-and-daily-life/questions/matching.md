# Development Models and Daily Life — matching

Match each word with its meaning.

**1.** development model — a)

**2.** daily life — b)

**3.** climate change — c)


Meanings:
a) the way a country chooses to grow — what it produces, how it works, what it counts as wealth and cost
b) the ordinary day — work, food, journeys, rest — where big choices are actually lived
c) the changing climate that production, travel and consumption shape, and that shapes them back

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b; 3. c
