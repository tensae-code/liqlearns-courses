# Development Models and Daily Life — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is a development model?
a) the way a country chooses to grow: what it produces, how it works and trades, and what it treats as wealth and as cost  b) an idea with no effect on real life  c) only the weather forecast  d) a drawing of a factory
**Answer: a) the way a country chooses to grow: what it produces, how it works and trades, and what it treats as wealth and as cost** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** How do development models affect daily life?
a) the work available, the prices charged, the journeys taken and the goods on the shelf are the model, lived daily  b) they never reach ordinary people  c) only by changing the calendar  d) daily life is untouched by how a country grows
**Answer: a) the work available, the prices charged, the journeys taken and the goods on the shelf are the model, lived daily** — That is what this lesson teaches.
