# Labour Rights — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — What labour rights are
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Labour rights are the worker's human rights at work: fair pay, safe conditions, rest, and treatment with dignity — the person does not stop being a person when work begins. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — How labour rights are protected
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Labour rights are protected by law that sets the floor, by inspection that checks it, and by workers organising together to claim what the law promises. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 3 — When rights are not respected
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: When a labour right is not respected, the worker is not without road: the claim, the organisation and the institutions exist so the wrong can be named and answered. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
