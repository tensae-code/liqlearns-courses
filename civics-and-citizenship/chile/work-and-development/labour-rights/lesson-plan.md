## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **What labour rights are** — Labour rights are the worker's human rights at work: fair pay, safe conditions, rest, and treatment with dignity — the person does not stop being a person when work begins.
2. **How labour rights are protected** — Labour rights are protected by law that sets the floor, by inspection that checks it, and by workers organising together to claim what the law promises.
3. **When rights are not respected** — When a labour right is not respected, the worker is not without road: the claim, the organisation and the institutions exist so the wrong can be named and answered.
