# Labour Rights — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the three protections of labour rights in this lesson.
**Answer:** Law that sets the floor, inspection that checks it, and workers organising together to claim what the law promises.

**2.** What road stands open when a labour right is not respected?
**Answer:** The claim, the organisation and the institutions — the wrong can be named and answered; it is access to justice at the workplace door.
