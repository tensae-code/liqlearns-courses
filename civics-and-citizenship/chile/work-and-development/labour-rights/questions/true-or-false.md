# Labour Rights — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A labour right is a gift from the employer, withdrawable at pleasure.
**Answer: False** — Labour rights belong to the worker as rights — a gift can be withdrawn at pleasure; a right can be claimed.

**2.** Law without inspection sleeps — inspection is one of the protections of labour rights.
**Answer: True**
