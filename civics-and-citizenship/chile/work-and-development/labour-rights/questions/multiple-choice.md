# Labour Rights — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What are labour rights?
a) the worker's human rights at work: fair pay, safe conditions, rest, and treatment with dignity  b) favours an employer may give or withdraw at pleasure  c) rights that stop at the workplace door  d) rules that protect only the employer
**Answer: a) the worker's human rights at work: fair pay, safe conditions, rest, and treatment with dignity** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** How are labour rights protected?
a) by law that sets the floor, by inspection that checks it, and by workers organising together  b) by hoping employers are kind  c) they are not protected at all  d) only by each worker alone, in silence
**Answer: a) by law that sets the floor, by inspection that checks it, and by workers organising together** — That is what this lesson teaches.
