# Labour Rights — matching

Match each word with its meaning.

**1.** labour rights — a)

**2.** inspection — b)

**3.** organising — c)


Meanings:
a) the worker's human rights at work — pay, safety, rest, dignity
b) the public checking that workplaces keep the law's floor
c) workers joining together to claim what the law promises

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b; 3. c
