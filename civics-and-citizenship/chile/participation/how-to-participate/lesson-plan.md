# Lesson plan — How to Participate in Chile's Democracy

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Chile · **Title:** Participation · **Lesson:** How to Participate in Chile's Democracy
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — Participation (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a citizen participates in Chile's democracy through real channels — and participation is how social problems get solved.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The channels of participation** — Participation has channels: voting and elections, local organisations, public meetings, consultations and the community's own bodies — each is a door into shared decisions.
2. **Using a channel well** — A channel works when it is used well: inform yourself, join with others, state the problem clearly, and follow the matter until it is answered.
3. **Participation solves problems** — The unit's own question stands behind this lesson: participation matters because social problems are solved by the people who take part, not by waiting.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
