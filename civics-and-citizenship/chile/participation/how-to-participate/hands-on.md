# Hands-on — How to Participate in Chile's Democracy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name one problem in your street or school that bothers many people. Write who could act on it, and one channel — a vote, a meeting, an organisation, a petition — by which people like you could press for it to be solved. This lesson walks those channels.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: a citizen participates in Chile's democracy through real channels — and participation is how social problems get solved. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
