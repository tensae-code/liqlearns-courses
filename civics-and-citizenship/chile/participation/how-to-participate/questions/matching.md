# How to Participate in Chile's Democracy — matching

Match each word with its meaning.

**1.** participation — a)

**2.** channel — b)

**3.** consultation — c)


Meanings:
a) taking part in the shared decisions and life of the country

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b; 3. c
