# How to Participate in Chile's Democracy — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Participation means only voting in elections.
**Answer: False** — Voting is one channel among several — organisations, meetings, consultations and community bodies are also doors into shared decisions.

**2.** Organised citizens with a clear ask are how problems actually move.
**Answer: True**
