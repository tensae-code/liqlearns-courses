# How to Participate in Chile's Democracy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is a channel of participation?
a) a real way in — voting, local organisations, public meetings, consultations — by which citizens take part in shared decisions  b) a television station  c) a private diary  d) a door that is always locked
**Answer: a) a real way in — voting, local organisations, public meetings, consultations — by which citizens take part in shared decisions** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** How is a channel used well?
a) inform yourself, join with others, state the problem clearly, and follow the matter until it is answered  b) shout once and never return  c) act entirely alone and tell no one  d) wait for the problem to solve itself
**Answer: a) inform yourself, join with others, state the problem clearly, and follow the matter until it is answered** — That is what this lesson teaches.
