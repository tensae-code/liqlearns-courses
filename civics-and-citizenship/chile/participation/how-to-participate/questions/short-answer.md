# How to Participate in Chile's Democracy — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name three channels of participation.
**Answer:** Any three of: voting and elections, local organisations, public meetings, consultations, the community's own bodies.

**2.** Why does this course put participation first in the graduating year?
**Answer:** Because participation is how social problems get solved — by the people who take part, not by waiting — and every later lesson is a field for practising it.
