# Video script — How to Participate in Chile's Democracy

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A citizen participates in chile's democracy through real channels — and participation is how social problems get solved.

Participation has channels: voting and elections, local organisations, public meetings, consultations and the community's own bodies — each is a door into shared decisions.

Knowing the doors is the first step: a citizen who cannot name the channels cannot use them, however much they care.

A channel works when it is used well: inform yourself, join with others, state the problem clearly, and follow the matter until it is answered.

One voice is easy to file away; organised citizens, with a clear ask and patience, are how problems actually move.

The unit's own question stands behind this lesson: participation matters because social problems are solved by the people who take part, not by waiting.

Every later lesson in this level — the gaps, the media, inclusion, work — is a field where this same taking-part is practised.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
