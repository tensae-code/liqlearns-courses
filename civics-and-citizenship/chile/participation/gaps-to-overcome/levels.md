# Gaps to Overcome to Participate — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Gaps of access
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Some citizens cannot reach the channels: distance, cost, time, disability or paperwork shut doors that stand open for others. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — Gaps of information
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Some citizens do not know: that a decision is coming, that a channel exists, or how to use it — decisions pass them by in silence. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 3 — Gaps of voice
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Some citizens are heard less: their word carries less weight because of poverty, origin, gender, age or where they live. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
