# Gaps to Overcome to Participate — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why is access called the first gap to close?
**Answer:** Because a channel that cannot be reached — by distance, cost, time, disability or paperwork — is, for that citizen, not a channel at all.

**2.** What does closing the voice gap mean, in the lesson's words?
**Answer:** Democracy keeping its own promise: that each citizen's word counts, not only the words of the already-powerful.
