# Gaps to Overcome to Participate — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What are the three gaps to overcome to participate?
a) gaps of access, of information, and of voice  b) gaps of height, weight and age  c) there are no gaps — all participate equally already  d) gaps that can never be named
**Answer: a) gaps of access, of information, and of voice** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is a gap of information?
a) citizens do not know a decision is coming, that a channel exists, or how to use it — decisions pass them by  b) citizens know too much  c) information is printed in too many colours  d) everyone is always fully informed
**Answer: a) citizens do not know a decision is coming, that a channel exists, or how to use it — decisions pass them by** — That is what this lesson teaches.
