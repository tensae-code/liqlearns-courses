# Gaps to Overcome to Participate — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The channels of participation stand equally open to everyone in practice.
**Answer: False** — Access, information and voice are unequally held — those are the gaps this lesson names, so they can be overcome.

**2.** Without information, even an open door of participation is invisible.
**Answer: True**
