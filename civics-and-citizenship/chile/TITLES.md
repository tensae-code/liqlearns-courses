# Civics and Citizenship — Chile course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

Civics belongs to the country: this course is Chile's own —
built from Chile's Educación Ciudadana student book for 3° y 4°
Medio (one combined volume, Spanish, COMPLETE). Inside the
course the organisation is by **levels, not grades**: the titles
below ARE the levels' titles, in path order — what the learner
proves, step by step. The grades the book comes from live only
in each lesson's sources.

The path's own logic is Chile's: the whole book is built as
questions a citizen must answer, and its two years form one arc
— first what the democratic state is (democracy's purpose,
citizenship, rights defended by the state, territory, citizens
and the market), then the graduating citizen acting in it
(participation, media, inclusion, labour rights and development
models). That ladder IS the path order (recommended, never
forced — a learner can jump in higher; the "start here instead"
hints point down this ladder).

## The titles

**Level 1 — what the democratic state is** (Educación
Ciudadana, 3° Medio half of the combined student book):

- `democracy/` — Level 1, first title (Unidad 1: ¿Cuál es el
  propósito de la democracia?). What Democracy Is For (how
  democracy has changed from Athens to today, as the road into
  what democracy is for) · Being a Citizen in a Democracy (what
  it means to be a citizen in a democracy).
- `rights/` — Level 1, second title (Unidad 2: ¿Cómo defiende
  el Estado nuestros derechos humanos?). How the State Defends
  Our Rights (how access to justice is guaranteed, as the
  state's defence of human rights in practice) · Me and Human
  Rights (the pupil's own relation to human rights — holder,
  witness and defender).
- `state-and-territory/` — Level 1, third title (Unidad 3:
  Estado y territorio). What Territory Means (what "territory"
  means for a state and its people) · How Territories Change
  (how territories are transformed — by people, economy and
  the state).
- `citizens-and-the-market/` — Level 1, fourth title (Unidad 4:
  Ciudadanos y mercado: ¿cómo pueden colaborar?). Economic
  Decisions in Society (how economic decisions are taken in
  society) · The Economy and Public Policy (the relation
  between the economy and public policies).

**Level 2 — the graduating citizen acting in it** (Educación
Ciudadana, 4° Medio half of the combined student book):

- `participation/` — Level 2, first title (Unidad 1: ¿Por qué
  es importante participar para resolver problemas sociales?).
  How to Participate in Chile's Democracy (the channels and
  their use) · Gaps to Overcome to Participate (the gaps —
  inequalities of access, information and voice — that must be
  overcome to participate).
- `media-and-democracy/` — Level 2, second title (Unidad 2:
  ¿Cómo se relacionan los medios de comunicación con la
  democracia?). The Role of the Media (the role of the media
  in a democracy) · Media Risks and Chances for Democracy (the
  risks and opportunities that media and ICT bring to
  democracy).
- `inclusive-democracy/` — Level 2, third title (Unidad 3:
  ¿Cómo construir una democracia más inclusiva?). Principles of
  Democracy (the principles that guide democracy) · An
  Inclusive Territory (advancing toward an inclusive territory
  — who is left out of places and decisions, and how that
  changes).
- `work-and-development/` — Level 2, fourth title (Unidad 4:
  Derechos laborales y modelos de desarrollo, ¿cómo se
  relacionan?). Labour Rights (how labour rights are
  protected) · Development Models and Daily Life (how
  development models affect daily life and climate change).
