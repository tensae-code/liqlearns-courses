# Economic Decisions in Society — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Choosing with limited means
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Every economic decision is a choice with limited means: money, time and goods are finite, so choosing one thing means giving up another. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — Decisions affect others
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: No economic decision stays private in its effects: a family's, a firm's or a state's spending creates work, prices, shortages or chances for others. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 3 — Deciding together in society
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Because decisions affect everyone, society also decides together: through the market, where buyers and sellers meet, and through public choices the citizens share. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
