# Hands-on — Economic Decisions in Society

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write down the last three things money was spent on in your home. For each, write who decided, and who else was affected by the decision. This lesson widens that page: how economic decisions are taken in society.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: economic decisions are taken in society, not alone: what each person, family, firm and the state chooses affects the others. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
