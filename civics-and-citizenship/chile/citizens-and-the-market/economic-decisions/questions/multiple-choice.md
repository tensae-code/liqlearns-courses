# Economic Decisions in Society — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Why is every economic decision a choice?
a) because means — money, time, goods — are limited, so choosing one thing means giving up another  b) because decisions are always easy  c) because money is unlimited  d) because someone else always chooses for us
**Answer: a) because means — money, time, goods — are limited, so choosing one thing means giving up another** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** How do economic decisions travel through society?
a) one person's, family's, firm's or state's spending creates work, prices, shortages or chances for others  b) they affect only the person who takes them  c) they disappear the moment they are taken  d) only the state's decisions affect anyone
**Answer: a) one person's, family's, firm's or state's spending creates work, prices, shortages or chances for others** — That is what this lesson teaches.
