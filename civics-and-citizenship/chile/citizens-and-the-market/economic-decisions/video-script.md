# Video script — Economic Decisions in Society

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Economic decisions are taken in society, not alone: what each person, family, firm and the state chooses affects the others.

Every economic decision is a choice with limited means: money, time and goods are finite, so choosing one thing means giving up another.

That giving-up has a name in the citizen's thinking: what we renounce when we choose — seeing it clearly is the first skill of deciding well.

No economic decision stays private in its effects: a family's, a firm's or a state's spending creates work, prices, shortages or chances for others.

Society is the web those effects travel along — a decision taken in one home is felt, sooner or later, in other homes.

Because decisions affect everyone, society also decides together: through the market, where buyers and sellers meet, and through public choices the citizens share.

The citizen meets the economy in both places — as a buyer with a purse, and as a member of the public that decides in common.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
