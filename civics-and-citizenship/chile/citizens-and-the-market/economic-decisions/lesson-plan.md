# Lesson plan — Economic Decisions in Society

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Chile · **Title:** Citizens and the Market · **Lesson:** Economic Decisions in Society
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — Citizens and the Market (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** economic decisions are taken in society, not alone: what each person, family, firm and the state chooses affects the others.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Choosing with limited means** — Every economic decision is a choice with limited means: money, time and goods are finite, so choosing one thing means giving up another.
2. **Decisions affect others** — No economic decision stays private in its effects: a family's, a firm's or a state's spending creates work, prices, shortages or chances for others.
3. **Deciding together in society** — Because decisions affect everyone, society also decides together: through the market, where buyers and sellers meet, and through public choices the citizens share.
