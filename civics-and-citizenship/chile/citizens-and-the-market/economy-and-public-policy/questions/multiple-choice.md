# The Economy and Public Policy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is a public policy?
a) a decision the state takes for the common life — education, health, work, taxes — paid from common funds  b) a private firm's advertising plan  c) a decision no one pays for  d) a rule that affects no one
**Answer: a) a decision the state takes for the common life — education, health, work, taxes — paid from common funds** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** How does the economy shape public policy?
a) work, unemployment, prices, poverty and growth press on the state and set what its policies must answer  b) it never influences the state  c) only by sending letters  d) the economy obeys policies but never shapes them
**Answer: a) work, unemployment, prices, poverty and growth press on the state and set what its policies must answer** — That is what this lesson teaches.
