# The Economy and Public Policy — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Describe the circle this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** The economy presses on the state — work, prices, poverty, growth — and the state answers with policies; those policies then change economic life in turn, and the circle begins again.

**2.** Why must a policy 'read' the economy it lands in?
**Answer:** Because a policy that ignores the economic life around it does not reach the people it was meant for — it answers a situation that is not the real one.
