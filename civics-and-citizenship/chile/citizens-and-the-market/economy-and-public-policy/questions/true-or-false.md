# The Economy and Public Policy — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Public policies have no effect on economic life.
**Answer: False** — Policies change economic life in turn: taxes, spending, rules and services alter what people and firms can do, earn and choose.

**2.** A public policy is also an economic decision, taken in everyone's name.
**Answer: True**
