# Lesson plan — The Economy and Public Policy

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Chile · **Title:** Citizens and the Market · **Lesson:** The Economy and Public Policy
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — Citizens and the Market (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the economy and public policies shape each other: the state answers economic life with policies, and those policies change economic life in turn.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **What public policy is** — Public policies are the decisions the state takes for the common life — on education, health, work, prices, taxes and more — paid from common funds.
2. **The economy shapes policy** — The state reads economic life before it acts: work and unemployment, prices, poverty and growth press on the state and set what its policies must answer.
3. **Policy shapes the economy** — In turn, policies change economic life: taxes, spending, rules and services alter what people and firms can do, earn and choose.
