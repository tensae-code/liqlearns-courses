# Video script — The Economy and Public Policy

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The economy and public policies shape each other: the state answers economic life with policies, and those policies change economic life in turn.

Public policies are the decisions the state takes for the common life — on education, health, work, prices, taxes and more — paid from common funds.

A policy is an economic decision too, taken in everyone's name: it chooses, with limited means, like every decision in the last lesson.

The state reads economic life before it acts: work and unemployment, prices, poverty and growth press on the state and set what its policies must answer.

A policy that ignores the economy it lands in is a letter sent to a wrong address — it arrives, but it does not reach anyone.

In turn, policies change economic life: taxes, spending, rules and services alter what people and firms can do, earn and choose.

So the circle closes — economy to policy, policy to economy — and the citizen's task is to judge the whole circle, not one arrow of it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
