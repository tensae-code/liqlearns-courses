# What Democracy Is For — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** How did citizens decide in Athens?
a) they met and decided the city's affairs together, face to face  b) one king decided everything alone  c) only soldiers were allowed to speak  d) decisions were bought and sold
**Answer: a) they met and decided the city's affairs together, face to face** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is democracy for?
a) so that the people themselves govern and decide their shared affairs  b) so that no one ever has to decide anything  c) so that the strongest person always wins  d) so that laws never change
**Answer: a) so that the people themselves govern and decide their shared affairs** — That is what this lesson teaches.
