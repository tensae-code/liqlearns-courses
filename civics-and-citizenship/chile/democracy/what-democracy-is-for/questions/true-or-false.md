# What Democracy Is For — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Democracy has stayed exactly the same since Athens.
**Answer: False** — Democracy has changed across the centuries — more people count as citizens, and representatives decide in the citizens' name in large countries.

**2.** The big idea of Athens that survives is that the people are the source of power.
**Answer: True**
