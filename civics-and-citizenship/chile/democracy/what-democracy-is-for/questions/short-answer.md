# What Democracy Is For — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name two ways democracy has changed since Athens.
**Answer:** More people count as citizens, and in large countries citizens choose representatives to decide in their name instead of all meeting face to face.

**2.** In your own words, what is democracy for?
**Answer:** Democracy is for governing ourselves together: deciding shared affairs, protecting each person's dignity, and correcting mistakes without violence.
