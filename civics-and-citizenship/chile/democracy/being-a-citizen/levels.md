# Being a Citizen in a Democracy — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Who is a citizen
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: A citizen is a full member of the country: the country recognises them, and they belong to its shared life, not as a guest but as one of its own. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — Rights and duties together
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The citizen holds rights the country must respect — and carries duties toward the country and toward other citizens. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 3 — Taking part as a citizen
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Being a citizen is practised: informing yourself, giving your opinion, voting, and taking part in the community's life are how citizenship is lived. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
