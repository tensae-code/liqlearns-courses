# Being a Citizen in a Democracy — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A citizen holds rights but carries no duties at all.
**Answer: False** — Rights and duties come together: the citizen holds rights the country must respect and carries duties toward the country and other citizens.

**2.** Taking part in the community's life is part of being a citizen.
**Answer: True**
