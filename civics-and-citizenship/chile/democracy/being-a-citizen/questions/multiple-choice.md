# Being a Citizen in a Democracy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What makes someone a citizen in a democracy?
a) full membership of the country — holding rights, carrying duties, and belonging to its shared life  b) owning the most property in town  c) being the oldest person in the family  d) never questioning anything
**Answer: a) full membership of the country — holding rights, carrying duties, and belonging to its shared life** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** How is citizenship lived, day to day?
a) by informing yourself, giving your opinion, voting, and taking part in the community's life  b) by staying silent about every shared affair  c) by waiting for others to decide everything  d) by avoiding every duty
**Answer: a) by informing yourself, giving your opinion, voting, and taking part in the community's life** — That is what this lesson teaches.
