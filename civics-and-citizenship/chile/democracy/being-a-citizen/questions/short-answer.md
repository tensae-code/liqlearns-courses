# Being a Citizen in a Democracy — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why do rights and duties belong together?
**Answer:** Because my rights are safe when others do their duties, and my duties protect everyone else's rights — each side holds the other up.

**2.** Give two ways a citizen takes part in the life of the country.
**Answer:** Any two of: informing yourself, giving your opinion, voting, taking part in the community's life.
