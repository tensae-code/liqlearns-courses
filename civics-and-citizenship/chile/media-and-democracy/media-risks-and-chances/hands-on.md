# Hands-on — Media Risks and Chances for Democracy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write one true thing you learned from a screen this week, and one thing you saw on a screen that turned out to be false or twisted. Keep both in front of you: this lesson is about exactly those two columns — risks and opportunities.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: media and ICT bring democracy both risks and opportunities — the citizen's task is to take the chances and guard against the risks. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
