# Lesson plan — Media Risks and Chances for Democracy

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Chile · **Title:** Media and Democracy · **Lesson:** Media Risks and Chances for Democracy
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — Media and Democracy (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** media and ICT bring democracy both risks and opportunities — the citizen's task is to take the chances and guard against the risks.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The risks media and ICT bring** — The risks are real: falsehood travels fast, voices can be bought or drowned, echo rooms shut out other views, and private life can be exposed.
2. **The opportunities they bring** — The opportunities are just as real: any citizen can inform themselves, publish, organise and be heard far beyond their own street.
3. **The citizen's handling** — The citizen's task is double: take the opportunities — inform yourself, speak, organise — and guard against the risks — check before believing or sharing.
