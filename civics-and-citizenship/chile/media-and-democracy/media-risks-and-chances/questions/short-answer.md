# Media Risks and Chances for Democracy — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name two risks media and ICT bring to democracy.
**Answer:** Any two of: falsehood travels fast; voices can be bought or drowned; echo rooms shut out other views; private life can be exposed.

**2.** What is the citizen's double task with media and ICT?
**Answer:** To take the opportunities — inform yourself, speak, organise — and to guard against the risks — check before believing or sharing.
