# Media Risks and Chances for Democracy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson hold about media and ICT?
a) they bring democracy both risks and opportunities — the citizen takes the chances and guards against the risks  b) they are simply good for democracy in every way  c) they are simply bad and must be abandoned  d) they change nothing for democracy
**Answer: a) they bring democracy both risks and opportunities — the citizen takes the chances and guards against the risks** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Name one opportunity media and ICT bring.
a) any citizen can inform themselves, publish, organise and be heard far beyond their own street  b) only officials can speak now  c) citizens know less than before  d) no one can organise anymore
**Answer: a) any citizen can inform themselves, publish, organise and be heard far beyond their own street** — That is what this lesson teaches.
