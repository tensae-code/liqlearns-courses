# Lesson plan — The Role of the Media

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Chile · **Title:** Media and Democracy · **Lesson:** The Role of the Media
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — Media and Democracy (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the media's role in a democracy is to inform the citizens, watch those who hold power, and carry the public conversation.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The media inform** — Democracy runs on informed citizens: the media gather what happens — in the government, the courts, the streets — and carry it to everyone.
2. **The media watch power** — The media also watch those who hold power: asking, checking and publishing is how wrongdoing and failure come to light between elections.
3. **The media carry the conversation** — The media carry the public conversation: different voices, arguments and letters meet there, and citizens hear each other through them.

3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
