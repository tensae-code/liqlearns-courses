# The Role of the Media — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the media's role in a democracy?
a) to inform the citizens, watch those who hold power, and carry the public conversation  b) only to entertain and advertise  c) to decide the laws themselves  d) to keep citizens from knowing anything
**Answer: a) to inform the citizens, watch those who hold power, and carry the public conversation** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Why must the media carry news to everyone?
a) because without it each citizen would know only their own street, and shared decisions would be taken in the dark  b) because news is a decoration  c) because citizens must never think for themselves  d) it does not matter whether citizens know anything
**Answer: a) because without it each citizen would know only their own street, and shared decisions would be taken in the dark** — That is what this lesson teaches.
