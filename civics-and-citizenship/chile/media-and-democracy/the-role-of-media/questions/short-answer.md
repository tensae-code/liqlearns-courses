# The Role of the Media — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the media's three roles in this lesson.
**Answer:** To inform the citizens, to watch those who hold power, and to carry the public conversation.

**2.** How do the media help democracy between elections?
**Answer:** By asking, checking and publishing — wrongdoing and failure come to light between elections, not only at election time.
