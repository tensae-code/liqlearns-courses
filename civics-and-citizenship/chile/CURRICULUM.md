# Civics and Citizenship — Chile course — CURRICULUM (the path)

One course for Chile inside the Civics and Citizenship folder,
organised by **levels, not grades**: a learner climbs when they
have proved the level, not when a school calendar says so. The
school grades the books come from appear only in each lesson's
sources (provenance), never as the course's organisation. The
path follows Chile's own order as its one combined book lays it
out: first what the democratic state is — democracy's purpose,
citizenship, rights defended by the state, territory, citizens
and the market — then the graduating citizen acting in it —
participation, media, a more inclusive democracy, labour rights
and development models.

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in this
course comes from government textbooks — this country's own
Educación Ciudadana book — except the **Tip boxes**: a tip in a
box is from us, the people making LiqLearns. Tips are ours; the
lessons are the books'. See ABOUT.md for the full book list, the
record-only teacher-guide leads, and the acceptance reckoning.

## The levels (the path, in order)

1. **What the Democratic State Is** — the purpose of democracy
   and being a citizen in it; the state defending human rights
   (access to justice; the pupil's own relation to rights as
   holder, witness and defender); what territory means and how
   territories change; and citizens and the market (economic
   decisions in society; the economy and public policy).
   8 lessons, in four titles: `democracy/` (2 lessons),
   `rights/` (2), `state-and-territory/` (2),
   `citizens-and-the-market/` (2).
2. **The Graduating Citizen Acting in It** — how to participate
   in Chile's democracy and the gaps to overcome (access,
   information, voice); the role of the media and the risks and
   chances media and ICT bring; the principles of democracy and
   advancing toward an inclusive territory; and labour rights
   and how development models affect daily life and the
   climate. 8 lessons, in four titles: `participation/`
   (2 lessons), `media-and-democracy/` (2),
   `inclusive-democracy/` (2), `work-and-development/` (2).

**Totals: 16 lessons** (Level 1: 8 · Level 2: 8 — all from one
combined student book).
