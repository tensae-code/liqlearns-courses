# An Inclusive Territory — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Who is left out
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Some people are left out of places and decisions: by distance, by cost, by steps they cannot climb, by language, by being treated as if they did not belong. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — Why places exclude
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Places and decisions exclude by design and by habit: a building, a timetable, a rule or a custom can shut a door without anyone deciding to shut it. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 3 — Changing toward inclusion
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Advancing toward an inclusive territory means changing both: the places — so all can enter and move — and the decisions — so all are heard in them. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
