# Lesson plan — An Inclusive Territory

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Chile · **Title:** Inclusive Democracy · **Lesson:** An Inclusive Territory
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — Inclusive Democracy (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** advancing toward an inclusive territory means finding who is left out of places and decisions — and changing the places and the decisions.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Who is left out** — Some people are left out of places and decisions: by distance, by cost, by steps they cannot climb, by language, by being treated as if they did not belong.
2. **Why places exclude** — Places and decisions exclude by design and by habit: a building, a timetable, a rule or a custom can shut a door without anyone deciding to shut it.
3. **Changing toward inclusion** — Advancing toward an inclusive territory means changing both: the places — so all can enter and move — and the decisions — so all are heard in them.
