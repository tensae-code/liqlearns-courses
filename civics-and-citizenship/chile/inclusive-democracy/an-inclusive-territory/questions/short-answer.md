# An Inclusive Territory — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name three ways people are left out of places and decisions.
**Answer:** Any three of: distance, cost, steps they cannot climb, language, being treated as if they did not belong.

**2.** What two things must change for a territory to become inclusive?
**Answer:** The places — so all can enter and move — and the decisions — so all are heard in them.
