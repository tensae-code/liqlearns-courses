# An Inclusive Territory — matching

Match each word with its meaning.

**1.** inclusive — a)

**2.** left out — b)

**3.** territory — c)


Meanings:
a) built so that everyone can enter, move, and be heard — no one left out
b) shut out of a place or a decision, by design or by habit
c) the ground a community shares — its places and the decisions taken over them

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b; 3. c
