# An Inclusive Territory — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does advancing toward an inclusive territory mean?
a) finding who is left out of places and decisions — and changing the places and the decisions  b) drawing a prettier map  c) moving the left-out people further away  d) declaring inclusion without changing anything
**Answer: a) finding who is left out of places and decisions — and changing the places and the decisions** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** How do places come to exclude people?
a) by design and by habit — a building, a timetable, a rule or a custom can shut a door without anyone deciding to shut it  b) places never exclude anyone  c) only by locked doors with guards  d) exclusion is natural and can never change
**Answer: a) by design and by habit — a building, a timetable, a rule or a custom can shut a door without anyone deciding to shut it** — That is what this lesson teaches.
