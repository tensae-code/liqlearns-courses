# An Inclusive Territory — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** If no one decided to shut a door, the exclusion cannot be changed.
**Answer: False** — Exclusion by habit and design is still made by people — and what was built excluding can be rebuilt including.

**2.** The test of inclusion is to ask the person who was left out whether the door is open now.
**Answer: True**
