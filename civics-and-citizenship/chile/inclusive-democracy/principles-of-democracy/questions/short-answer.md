# Principles of Democracy — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the principles that guide democracy in this lesson.
**Answer:** The people's rule (power comes from the people), equality, freedom, and respect for rights.

**2.** By what is a democracy judged, at the end of this lesson?
**Answer:** By whether its principles reach the last citizen, not only the first — that is inclusion.
