# Principles of Democracy — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Democracy is nothing but a counting of votes.
**Answer: False** — Voting is the machinery; the principles — the people's rule, equality, freedom, respect for rights — are what the machinery is for.

**2.** Governments hold power in trust, for a time, under rules the people can change.
**Answer: True**
