# Principles of Democracy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What guides democracy, in this lesson?
a) its principles — the people's rule, equality, freedom, and respect for rights  b) only the wishes of the strongest  c) whatever the government finds convenient  d) nothing — democracy has no guides
**Answer: a) its principles — the people's rule, equality, freedom, and respect for rights** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Why do equality and freedom need each other?
a) equality without freedom is a silent sameness; freedom without equality is a loud few and a quiet many  b) they do not — only one of them matters  c) freedom means some citizens' words count double  d) equality means no one may speak
**Answer: a) equality without freedom is a silent sameness; freedom without equality is a loud few and a quiet many** — That is what this lesson teaches.
