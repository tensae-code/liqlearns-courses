# What Territory Means — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is territory, for a state?
a) the defined ground — land and the space that goes with it — within which the state exists  b) any land anyone walks on, anywhere  c) only the capital city  d) a drawing that means nothing in real life
**Answer: a) the defined ground — land and the space that goes with it — within which the state exists** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Why does territory matter to citizens, not only to map-makers?
a) because it is where the people live their common life and where the state's authority runs  b) because maps are pretty to look at  c) because citizens must memorise every border  d) it does not matter to citizens at all
**Answer: a) because it is where the people live their common life and where the state's authority runs** — That is what this lesson teaches.
