# What Territory Means — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the three ways this lesson says to see the same ground.
**Answer:** As the state's base, as the people's home, and as the reach of the law (where the state's authority runs).

**2.** Why can there be no state without territory?
**Answer:** Because a state must exist somewhere: the defined ground within which it exists is one of the things a state is made of.
