# Lesson plan — What Territory Means

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Chile · **Title:** State and Territory · **Lesson:** What Territory Means
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — State and Territory (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** territory is the ground of the state — the land, and the space, where a people lives and where the state's authority runs.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Territory as the state's ground** — A state stands on territory: the defined ground — land and the space that goes with it — within which the state exists.
2. **Territory and its people** — Territory is not empty: it is where the people live, work, travel and bury their dead — the shared ground of a common life.
3. **Authority over territory** — Within its territory the state's authority runs: its laws apply, its institutions act, and it answers for what happens there.
