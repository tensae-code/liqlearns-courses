# Hands-on — How Territories Change

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Think of one place you know that looks different from ten years ago — a street, a field, a shore. Write what changed it. This lesson sorts the changers into three: people, the economy, and the state.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: territories are not fixed: they are transformed — by people, by the economy, and by the state. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
