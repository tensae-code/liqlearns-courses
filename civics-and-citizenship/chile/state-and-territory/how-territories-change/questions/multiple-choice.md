# How Territories Change — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which three forces transform territories?
a) people, the economy, and the state  b) only the weather  c) only foreign countries  d) nothing — territories never change
**Answer: a) people, the economy, and the state** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** How does the economy transform a territory?
a) work and trade — mines, ports, farms, factories, markets — pull people and roads toward them and remake regions  b) the economy never touches the ground  c) only by printing money  d) by erasing all towns at once
**Answer: a) work and trade — mines, ports, farms, factories, markets — pull people and roads toward them and remake regions** — That is what this lesson teaches.
