# How Territories Change — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Give one way people transform territory.
**Answer:** Any one of: settling, building, farming, travelling, and leaving places — the ground takes the shape of their lives.

**2.** How does the state transform territory?
**Answer:** By its laws, its public works, its borders, and the way it organises and names its regions.
