# National Integrity — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** National integrity — النزاهة — is the citizen's own honesty raised to a national value: public life kept clean because the people in it refuse crooked dealing.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** National integrity (النزاهة) is the personal form of the rule of law — law binds the state from outside, integrity binds the citizen from inside.
