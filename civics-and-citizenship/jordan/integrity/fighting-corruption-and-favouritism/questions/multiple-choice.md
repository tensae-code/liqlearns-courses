# Fighting Corruption and Favouritism — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) fighting corruption and favouritism — الفساد والمحسوبية — is integrity at war: refusing the bribe, the stolen public right, and the job or favour given by connection instead of by merit.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
