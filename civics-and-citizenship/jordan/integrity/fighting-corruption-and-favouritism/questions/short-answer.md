# Fighting Corruption and Favouritism — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Fighting corruption and favouritism — الفساد والمحسوبية — is integrity at war: refusing the bribe, the stolen public right, and the job or favour given by connection instead of by merit.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Corruption and favouritism steal what belongs to all or to the deserving; the citizen fights them first at the small offices of daily life.
