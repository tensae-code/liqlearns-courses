# Civics and Citizenship — Jordan course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

Civics belongs to the country: this course is Jordan's own —
built from Jordan's التربية الوطنية والمدنية (National and
Civic Education) student books for Grades 9 and 10
(National Centre for Curriculum Development, Arabic, 2024,
adopted from 2024/2025). Inside the course the organisation
is by **levels, not grades**: the titles below are the
units the placements coin from the books' own units, and
the lessons inside them climb the five levels in path
order — what the learner proves, step by step. The grades
the books come from live only in each lesson's sources.

The path's own logic is the books': start from Jordanian
society itself — its make-up, its culture, its live issues —
with the pupil's own community as the first text; build
citizenship as an identity before a mechanism; then
participation, then the frame of law, constitution and
rights; then Jordan placed — its heritage and its Arab and
world relations and future; and last the citizen's own
conduct, integrity first. That ladder IS the path order
(recommended, never forced — a learner can jump in anywhere;
the "start here instead" hints point down this ladder).
Every lesson stands on student books — taught in 1 country
(Jordan); see ABOUT.md for the phantom-push record carried
for all three books.

## The titles

- `my-society/` — Level 1. Grade 9's opening unit
  (Jordanian Society): what Jordanian society is and its
  characteristics, Jordanian national culture, and the
  live issues of my society. It sits first because the
  book itself starts there — the pupil's own community is
  the textbook's first text.
- `citizenship-and-identity/` — Level 1. Grade 10's
  opening lessons: what national and civic education is,
  my Jordanian identity, citizenship, and serving the
  flag. It sits beside `my-society/` at Level 1 because
  identity is the second half of the citizen's starting
  ground — society without, belonging within — and the
  books build it before any mechanism.
- `taking-part/` — Level 2. Participation, from both
  books: democracy and elections (each one lesson with
  two sub lessons — the Grade 9 treatment, then the Grade
  10 deepening), civil society organisations, voluntary
  work, participation in public life, and political
  parties. It sits second because the citizen formed at
  Level 1 is now asked to act.
- `the-state-of-law/` — Level 3. Grade 9's State of Law
  unit: the rule of law, and accountability. It sits at
  Level 3 because participation needs its frame — law
  above persons, officials answering — before the
  constitution lesson states the frame's foundation.
- `the-constitution/` — Level 3. One lesson: the Jordanian
  constitution and the rule of law — the independent Arab
  state, sovereignty belonging to the nation. It sits
  inside Level 3 as the frame's highest statement.
- `human-rights/` — Level 3. Grade 10 Term 2's Human
  Rights unit: the international bill of human rights,
  then human rights in Jordan. It closes Level 3 because
  rights are what the frame of law and constitution
  exists to hold — the world's bill first, then its
  Jordanian holding.
- `heritage/` — Level 4. Heritage from both ends of the
  set: Grade 9's closing unit (Jordanian national
  heritage, its models, its museums) and Grade 10 Term
  2's National Heritage unit (national heritage whole,
  world heritage and UNESCO). It sits at Level 4 because
  the placed citizen now meets what the homeland has
  inherited and must keep.
- `jordan-in-the-world/` — Level 4. Jordan placed among
  nations: Jordanian–Arab relations, Jordan's
  international relations, and Jordan of the future. It
  shares Level 4 with heritage because both are the
  homeland seen whole — what it holds from the past, and
  where it stands and is heading among the world's
  states.
- `integrity/` — Level 5. Grade 10 Term 2's Integrity and
  Fighting Corruption unit: national integrity, and
  fighting corruption and favouritism. It sits at the top
  because it is the rule of law turned inward — the
  personal form of the Level 3 frame, practised by the
  citizen himself.
- `life-skills/` — Level 5. The books' closing unit:
  media content read critically, and behaviour. It closes
  the ladder because the course that began with society
  ends with the one part of the state every pupil already
  governs — their own conduct.
