# Issues of My Society

## Do it first

Name one live issue people talk about in your community — something many people say needs fixing. Write down who it affects and who could act on it.

## Live issues, examined

Every society has live issues — questions its people are actively facing. The lesson takes up issues from Jordanian society itself and examines them the citizen's way: what the issue is, whom it touches, and why it matters to the whole society and not only to those it touches first.

## A citizen's business

An issue of your society is not somebody else's problem. Because the pupil belongs to Jordanian society, its issues are part of the pupil's own business — first to understand them clearly, and in time to play a part in answering them.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: a society's live issues are a citizen's business: the pupil learns to name the issues Jordanian society faces and to examine them as someone who belongs to that society. Give one real example from your own life in Jordan, and name the lesson's key words: issues, society, citizen, problems. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Jordan's own books teach civics by starting from Jordanian society itself — its make-up, its culture, its live issues — and only then widening to participation, law and heritage. When a lesson asks you to look at your own community, really look: your community is this course's first text.
