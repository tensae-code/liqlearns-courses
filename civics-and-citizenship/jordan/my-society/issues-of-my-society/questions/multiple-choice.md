# Issues of My Society — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) a society's live issues are a citizen's business: the pupil learns to name the issues Jordanian society faces and to examine them as someone who belongs to that society.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) The Grade 9 course examines live issues from Jordanian society in its very first unit — society's problems are placed in the pupil's hands from the start.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
