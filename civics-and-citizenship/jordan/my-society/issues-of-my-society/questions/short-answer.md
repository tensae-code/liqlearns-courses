# Issues of My Society — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** A society's live issues are a citizen's business: the pupil learns to name the issues jordanian society faces and to examine them as someone who belongs to that society.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** The Grade 9 course examines live issues from Jordanian society in its very first unit — society's problems are placed in the pupil's hands from the start.
