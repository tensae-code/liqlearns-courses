# Jordanian National Culture — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) a nation's culture is what its people create, practise and hand on together — and Jordanian national culture is the shared way of life that carries Jordan's identity.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) National culture is taught as something carried — practised and handed on — not as a museum label.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
