# Jordanian National Culture — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** A nation's culture is what its people create, practise and hand on together — and jordanian national culture is the shared way of life that carries jordan's identity.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** National culture is taught as something carried — practised and handed on — not as a museum label.
