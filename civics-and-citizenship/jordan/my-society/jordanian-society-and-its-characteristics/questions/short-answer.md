# Jordanian Society and Its Characteristics — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Jordanian society is the society the learner already lives in — and it has characteristics that can be named: who composes it, what its people share, and what holds it together.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Jordanian society is examined first, before participation, law or heritage — the pupil's own community is the textbook's first text.
