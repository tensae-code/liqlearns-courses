# Video script — Jordanian Society and Its Characteristics

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Jordanian society is the society the learner already lives in — and it has characteristics that can be named: who composes it, what its people share, and what holds it together.

The course starts where Jordan's book starts: with Jordanian society itself. A society is the people who live together in one homeland, bound by a shared life — and Jordanian society is that shared life as Jordanians live it, in its cities, villages and camps, its families and its neighbourhoods.

The lesson names the characteristics that mark Jordanian society: what its people hold in common, the ties between its members, and the values its life runs on. To know your society's characteristics is to be able to say what makes its people one society and not a crowd of strangers.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
