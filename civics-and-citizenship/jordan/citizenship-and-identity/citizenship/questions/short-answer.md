# Citizenship — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Citizenship is the bond between the jordanian and his state — the standing that joins a person to the homeland with rights on one side and duties on the other.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Citizenship is taught as a bond — the standing that joins the Jordanian to his state — not as a mere status word.
