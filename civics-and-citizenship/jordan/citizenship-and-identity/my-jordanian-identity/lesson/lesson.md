# My Jordanian Identity

## Do it first

Write down three things that make you Jordanian — things about your homeland, its people and its story that are part of who you are.

## What composes an identity

An identity is the answer to 'who am I?' at the scale of a nation. The lesson sets out what composes the Jordanian identity: belonging to the Jordanian homeland, sharing its people's life and culture, and standing inside its story — so that 'Jordanian' names something real in the pupil, not only a word on a document.

## Identity before mechanism

Jordan's book builds citizenship as an identity before it builds it as a mechanism. The pupil first knows who they are and where they belong; the rights, duties and institutions taught later in the course attach to that belonging.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: my Jordanian identity is what makes me a Jordanian — the belonging, the shared history and culture, and the homeland that compose who I am as a member of this nation. Give one real example from your own life in Jordan, and name the lesson's key words: identity, belonging, homeland, Jordanian. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Jordan's own books teach civics by starting from Jordanian society itself — its make-up, its culture, its live issues — and only then widening to participation, law and heritage. When a lesson asks you to look at your own community, really look: your community is this course's first text.
