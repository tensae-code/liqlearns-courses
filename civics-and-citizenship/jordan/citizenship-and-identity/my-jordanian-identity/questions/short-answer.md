# My Jordanian Identity — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** My jordanian identity is what makes me a jordanian — the belonging, the shared history and culture, and the homeland that compose who i am as a member of this nation.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Citizenship is built as an identity before it is built as a mechanism — the homeland, in the unit's framing, gathers and does not divide.
