# Serving the Flag

## Do it first

Describe Jordan's flag as exactly as you can — its colours and what stands on it. Then say one way a pupil can serve what that flag stands for.

## The flag and what it stands for

The flag is the homeland's sign: where it is raised, Jordan is present. Serving the flag therefore means more than saluting a cloth — it means honouring, in act, the homeland the flag stands for, its people and its standing among nations.

## The pupil's own act of service

The lesson makes flag service the pupil's own act: a form of national service a school pupil can already perform — respect for the symbol, and the conduct that honours it: standing for the homeland in study, in behaviour and in readiness to serve it. Service begins where the pupil stands, not at some later age.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: serving the flag is the pupil's own act of national service — honouring the homeland's symbol by serving the homeland it stands for. Give one real example from your own life in Jordan, and name the lesson's key words: flag, service, homeland, honour. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Jordan's own books teach civics by starting from Jordanian society itself — its make-up, its culture, its live issues — and only then widening to participation, law and heritage. When a lesson asks you to look at your own community, really look: your community is this course's first text.
