# Serving the Flag — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Serving the flag is the pupil's own act of national service — honouring the homeland's symbol by serving the homeland it stands for.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Flag service is taught as the pupil's own present act of national service, not a ceremony watched from outside.
