# Jordan's International Relations

## Do it first

Find Jordan on a world map. Name two far countries Jordan has relations with, and one reason a small state needs friends far away as well as near.

## Beyond the Arab world

The second lesson of the unit widens the circle to its full width: Jordan's international relations beyond the Arab world — the homeland's ties with states across the world, and its place in the community of nations.

## A state among states

To have international relations is to act as a state among states: exchanging representatives, joining the world's bodies, and defending the homeland's interests and standing by peaceful, lawful means. The pupil completes here the widening that began with their own street: society, nation, Arab world, world.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Jordan's international relations are the homeland's ties beyond the Arab world — how a state of Jordan's place and size stands, speaks and acts among all the nations of the world. Give one real example from your own life in Jordan, and name the lesson's key words: international relations, world, state, nations. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Jordan's own books teach civics by starting from Jordanian society itself — its make-up, its culture, its live issues — and only then widening to participation, law and heritage. When a lesson asks you to look at your own community, really look: your community is this course's first text.
