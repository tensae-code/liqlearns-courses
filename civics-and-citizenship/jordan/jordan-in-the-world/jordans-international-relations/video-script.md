# Video script — Jordan's International Relations

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Jordan's international relations are the homeland's ties beyond the arab world — how a state of jordan's place and size stands, speaks and acts among all the nations of the world.

The second lesson of the unit widens the circle to its full width: Jordan's international relations beyond the Arab world — the homeland's ties with states across the world, and its place in the community of nations.

To have international relations is to act as a state among states: exchanging representatives, joining the world's bodies, and defending the homeland's interests and standing by peaceful, lawful means. The pupil completes here the widening that began with their own street: society, nation, Arab world, world.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
