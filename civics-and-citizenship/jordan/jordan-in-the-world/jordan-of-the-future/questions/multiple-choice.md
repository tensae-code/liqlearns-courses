# Jordan of the Future — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) Jordan of the future is the homeland the pupils will inherit and build — its challenges read honestly, tied to its geography, its Arab belonging and its national role.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) The future is read from real grounds — geography, Arab belonging, national role — and charged to the pupils who will build it.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
