# Hands-on — Jordan of the Future

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write three sentences about the Jordan you want to live in as an adult — one about its land and resources, one about its place among the Arabs, one about its people's life.

## Show it with real things
Take this lesson into one real act this week: do, make, visit, ask or help in a way that puts Jordan of the Future into practice in your own community in Jordan — then write what happened and what the lesson would say about it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
