# Video script — Jordanian–Arab Relations

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Jordan's relations with the arab world are the homeland's nearest circle outward — ties of language, history and belonging joining jordan to its arab sister states.

The second term turns outward. Jordan does not stand alone in the world: its first circle is the Arab world — the peoples who share its language, much of its history, and its belonging. The lesson teaches Jordan's relations with the Arab world as that nearest circle: what joins them and how the relations are carried.

Arab belonging was already part of the pupil's identity in Level 1; here it becomes policy and relation — the homeland acting among its sister states. The pupil learns to read Jordan's place on the map as a member of a family of nations, before the next lesson widens the view to the whole world.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
