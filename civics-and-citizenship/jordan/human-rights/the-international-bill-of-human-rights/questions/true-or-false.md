# The International Bill of Human Rights — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The international bill of human rights is the world's own written promise about every person's rights — opening with the principle that all people are born free and equal in dignity and rights.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a child's own home, school or community in Jordan.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Jordan and lived there.
