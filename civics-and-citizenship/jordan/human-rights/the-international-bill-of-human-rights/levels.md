# The International Bill of Human Rights — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — the international bill of human rights is the world's own written promise about every person's rights — opening with the principle that all people are born free and equal in dignity and rights. — in their own words, give one real example from their own life in Jordan, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or community situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
