# Human Rights in Jordan — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Human rights in jordan are the same universal rights as they are held and protected in the homeland — the pupil learns how jordan holds them and what protects them.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a child's own home, school or community in Jordan.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Jordan and lived there.
