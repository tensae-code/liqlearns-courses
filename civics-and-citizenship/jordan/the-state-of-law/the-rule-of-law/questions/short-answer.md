# The Rule of Law — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** The rule of law means the state itself is ruled by law: law stands above persons, and every act of power must answer to it.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** The state is ruled by law — law binds the rulers as it binds the ruled; the unit itself is named 'The State of Law'.
