# The Rule of Law — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) the rule of law means the state itself is ruled by law: law stands above persons, and every act of power must answer to it.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) The state is ruled by law — law binds the rulers as it binds the ruled; the unit itself is named 'The State of Law'.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
