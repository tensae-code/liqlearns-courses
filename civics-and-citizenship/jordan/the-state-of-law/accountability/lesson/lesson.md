# Accountability

## Do it first

A school spends money collected for a trip and the trip never happens. List the questions the pupils and parents may rightly ask, and who must answer them.

## Questioning and accounting

The lesson joins two words: المساءلة — questioning — and المحاسبة — calling to account. Together they name the practice by which anyone who holds power or trust must answer for it: explain what was done, show by what right, and bear the result of the answer.

## The rule of law at work

Accountability is the rule of law with a face: law does not only bind officials in the abstract; it gives the community the standing to ask, and obliges the official to reply. A state of law without accountability would be a promise no one could enforce.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: accountability — المساءلة والمحاسبة — is how officials answer for what they do: those who hold power can be questioned, and their acts can be called to account. Give one real example from your own life in Jordan, and name the lesson's key words: accountability, questioning, officials, answer. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Jordan's own books teach civics by starting from Jordanian society itself — its make-up, its culture, its live issues — and only then widening to participation, law and heritage. When a lesson asks you to look at your own community, really look: your community is this course's first text.
