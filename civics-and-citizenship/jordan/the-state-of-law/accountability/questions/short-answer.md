# Accountability — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Accountability — المساءلة والمحاسبة — is how officials answer for what they do: those who hold power can be questioned, and their acts can be called to account.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Accountability joins questioning (المساءلة) and calling to account (المحاسبة) — officials answer for what they do.
