# Accountability — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) accountability — المساءلة والمحاسبة — is how officials answer for what they do: those who hold power can be questioned, and their acts can be called to account.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Accountability joins questioning (المساءلة) and calling to account (المحاسبة) — officials answer for what they do.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
