# Video script — Accountability

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Accountability — المساءلة والمحاسبة — is how officials answer for what they do: those who hold power can be questioned, and their acts can be called to account.

The lesson joins two words: المساءلة — questioning — and المحاسبة — calling to account. Together they name the practice by which anyone who holds power or trust must answer for it: explain what was done, show by what right, and bear the result of the answer.

Accountability is the rule of law with a face: law does not only bind officials in the abstract; it gives the community the standing to ask, and obliges the official to reply. A state of law without accountability would be a promise no one could enforce.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
