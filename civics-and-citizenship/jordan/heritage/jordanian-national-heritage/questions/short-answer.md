# Jordanian National Heritage — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Jordanian national heritage is what the homeland has inherited — the places, works, customs and memory handed down — and knowing what it consists of is the first step to keeping it.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Heritage is an inheritance held in trust — received, guarded and handed on; that trust is why it stands in a civics course.
