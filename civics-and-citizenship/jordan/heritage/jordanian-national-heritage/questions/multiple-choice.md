# Jordanian National Heritage — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) Jordanian national heritage is what the homeland has inherited — the places, works, customs and memory handed down — and knowing what it consists of is the first step to keeping it.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Heritage is an inheritance held in trust — received, guarded and handed on; that trust is why it stands in a civics course.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
