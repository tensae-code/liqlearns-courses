# Museums Keep Heritage — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Museums keep heritage: they collect, guard, study and show the nation's inheritance so that what time would scatter, the whole people can still meet.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Museums collect, guard, study and show the inheritance — and preservation is a citizen's concern, not the staff's alone.
