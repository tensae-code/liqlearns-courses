# Museums Keep Heritage — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) museums keep heritage: they collect, guard, study and show the nation's inheritance so that what time would scatter, the whole people can still meet.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Museums collect, guard, study and show the inheritance — and preservation is a citizen's concern, not the staff's alone.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
