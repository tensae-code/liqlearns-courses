# World Heritage and UNESCO — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** World heritage is the part of peoples' inheritance that belongs to all humanity's story — and unesco is the world body charged with helping to protect it, with heritage cities such as damascus as worked examples.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a child's own home, school or community in Jordan.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Jordan and lived there.
