# Hands-on — World Heritage and UNESCO

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name one place anywhere in the world that you think all humanity should help protect. Say why it belongs to everyone's story, not only its own country's.

## Show it with real things
Take this lesson into one real act this week: do, make, visit, ask or help in a way that puts World Heritage and UNESCO into practice in your own community in Jordan — then write what happened and what the lesson would say about it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
