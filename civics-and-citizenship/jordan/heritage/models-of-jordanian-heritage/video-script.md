# Video script — Models of Jordanian Heritage

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Models of jordanian heritage are the chosen examples through which the pupil meets the homeland's inheritance face to face — heritage learned from real instances, not from a definition alone.

A definition tells what heritage is; a model shows it. This lesson works through chosen models of Jordanian national heritage — real instances the pupil can picture, visit or point to — so that the inheritance of the previous lesson gains faces and places.

Taken together, the models teach the range of the inheritance: built and made things, places and practices, across the homeland's regions and ages. The pupil who has met the models can recognise heritage when they meet it unnamed — in their own town first of all.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
