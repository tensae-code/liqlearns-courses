# Video script — The Jordanian Constitution and the Rule of Law

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Jordan's constitution is the state's highest law: it founds an independent arab state whose sovereignty belongs to the nation, and it sets the sovereignty of law over the whole of public life.

The Grade 10 book joins the constitution and the rule of law in one lesson. The constitution is the law above laws: it establishes the state — Jordan an independent Arab state — and declares where sovereignty lies: sovereignty belongs to the nation, the source from which all authority in the state proceeds.

From that foundation the lesson draws the sovereignty of law: because the constitution stands highest, every authority in the state acts under law, and the citizen's rights and the state's powers are both read from the same written source. The constitution is thus not a ceremony text but the working frame of the citizen's whole public life.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
