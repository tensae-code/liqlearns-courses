# The Jordanian Constitution and the Rule of Law — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Jordan's constitution is the state's highest law: it founds an independent arab state whose sovereignty belongs to the nation, and it sets the sovereignty of law over the whole of public life.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a child's own home, school or community in Jordan.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Jordan and lived there.
