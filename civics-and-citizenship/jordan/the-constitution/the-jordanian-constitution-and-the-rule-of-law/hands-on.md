# Hands-on — The Jordanian Constitution and the Rule of Law

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Say what a country would need written down before any other laws could be made — who holds power, where it comes from, what no law may break. You are describing a constitution's work.

## Show it with real things
Take this lesson into one real act this week: do, make, visit, ask or help in a way that puts The Jordanian Constitution and the Rule of Law into practice in your own community in Jordan — then write what happened and what the lesson would say about it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
