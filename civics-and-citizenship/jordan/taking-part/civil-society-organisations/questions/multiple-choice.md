# Civil Society Organisations — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) civil society organisations are the bodies citizens form between themselves and the state — associations and institutions through which people act together on their shared concerns.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Civil society is placed between citizen and state — the organisations citizens form themselves are part of political participation, not an extra to it.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
