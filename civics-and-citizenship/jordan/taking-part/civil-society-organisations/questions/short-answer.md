# Civil Society Organisations — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Civil society organisations are the bodies citizens form between themselves and the state — associations and institutions through which people act together on their shared concerns.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Civil society is placed between citizen and state — the organisations citizens form themselves are part of political participation, not an extra to it.
