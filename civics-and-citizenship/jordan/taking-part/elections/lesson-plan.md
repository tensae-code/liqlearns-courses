## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Elections — the citizen's instrument** — An election is how a democracy turns the people's will into chosen representatives. The Grade 9 lesson teaches elections as the citizen's instrument: the tool by which an ordinary citizen, with one vote, shares in deciding who governs.
2. **I and the ballot box** — The Grade 10 lesson defines elections, weighs their importance, and puts the pupil in front of the ballot box itself: the moment where citizenship becomes an act. The pupil is placed where a voter stands.

Gate pool: the prove-it check for each sub lesson is at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson — drawn from this lesson's question files (questions/).
