# Elections — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Elections are the citizen's instrument: the act by which citizens choose who represents them — defined, weighed, and finally faced at the ballot box itself.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Elections are taught twice: as the citizen's instrument (Grade 9), then face to face with the ballot box — 'I and the Ballot Box' (Grade 10).
