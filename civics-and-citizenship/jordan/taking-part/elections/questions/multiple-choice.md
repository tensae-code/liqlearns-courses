# Elections — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) elections are the citizen's instrument: the act by which citizens choose who represents them — defined, weighed, and finally faced at the ballot box itself.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Elections are taught twice: as the citizen's instrument (Grade 9), then face to face with the ballot box — 'I and the Ballot Box' (Grade 10).  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
