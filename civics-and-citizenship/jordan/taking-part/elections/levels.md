# Elections — depth

This lesson has two sub lessons, in order: 1. Elections — the citizen's instrument; 2. I and the ballot box. Pass mark: the learner proves each sub lesson in order — each prove-it check is at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson — and can state the lesson's one thing — elections are the citizen's instrument: the act by which citizens choose who represents them — defined, weighed, and finally faced at the ballot box itself. — in their own words, give one real example from their own life in Jordan, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or community situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
