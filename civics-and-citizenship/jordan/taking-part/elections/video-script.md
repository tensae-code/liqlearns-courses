# Video script — Elections

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Elections are the citizen's instrument: the act by which citizens choose who represents them — defined, weighed, and finally faced at the ballot box itself.

An election is how a democracy turns the people's will into chosen representatives. The Grade 9 lesson teaches elections as the citizen's instrument: the tool by which an ordinary citizen, with one vote, shares in deciding who governs.

The Grade 10 lesson — 'I and the Ballot Box' — defines elections, weighs their importance, and puts the pupil in front of the ballot box itself: the moment where citizenship becomes an act. The pupil is not only told what an election is; they are placed where a voter stands.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
