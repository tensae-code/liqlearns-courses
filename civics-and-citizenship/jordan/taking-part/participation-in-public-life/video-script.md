# Video script — Participation in Public Life

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Participation in public life is active citizenship in act — a civilised behaviour, and both a right and a duty of every citizen, without which active citizenship is not achieved.

The Grade 10 book states the lesson's core plainly: active citizenship is not achieved without participation in public life. A citizen who only holds a status but never takes part has the name of citizenship without its life.

Participation is taught as two things at once: a right — no one may bar the citizen from public life — and a duty — the citizen owes public life their share. It is also a civilised behaviour: taking part peaceably, in the open, by the forms the community lives by.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
