# Participation in Public Life — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Participation in public life is active citizenship in act — a civilised behaviour, and both a right and a duty of every citizen, without which active citizenship is not achieved.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Participation in public life is a right and a duty at once — and active citizenship is not achieved without it.
