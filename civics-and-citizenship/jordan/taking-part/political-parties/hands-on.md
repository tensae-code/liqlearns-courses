# Hands-on — Political Parties

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Say what a group of citizens would need if they wanted to act together in politics for years — a name, shared aims, members, a way of choosing leaders. You have just described a party's bones.

## Show it with real things
Take this lesson into one real act this week: do, make, visit, ask or help in a way that puts Political Parties into practice in your own community in Jordan — then write what happened and what the lesson would say about it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
