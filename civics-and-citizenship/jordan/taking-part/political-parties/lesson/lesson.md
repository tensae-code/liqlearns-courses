# Political Parties

## Do it first

Say what a group of citizens would need if they wanted to act together in politics for years — a name, shared aims, members, a way of choosing leaders. You have just described a party's bones.

## What a political party is

A political party gathers citizens who share a view of how the country should be run, organises them, and seeks to carry its programme into public life — presenting candidates, arguing its case, and answering for its record. Parties turn scattered opinion into organised participation.

## Jordan's parties and their history

The book teaches Jordan's own parties: national and nationalist in character, and with a history the pupil can date — the first Jordanian political party was founded in 1927. Party life in Jordan is thus almost as old as the state itself, and the pupil meets it as part of the homeland's own story.

Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Jordan's own books teach civics by starting from Jordanian society itself — its make-up, its culture, its live issues — and only then widening to participation, law and heritage.
