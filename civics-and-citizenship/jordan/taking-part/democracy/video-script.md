# Video script — Democracy

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Democracy is the people's rule of themselves — the door into political participation, where citizens share in deciding how they are governed.

Democracy means the people govern themselves: the decisions that rule the community are made with the people's share in them, not handed down without them. The Grade 9 book teaches it first, as the door into political participation — everything else in the unit, elections, organisations and volunteering, is a way through that door.

The Grade 10 book returns to democracy and deepens it: the pupil who met it as the door into participation now meets it again with citizenship and identity already in hand, and sees it as part of the bond between the Jordanian and his state — the form of public life the whole course is building toward.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
