# Democracy — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Democracy is the people's rule of themselves — the door into political participation, where citizens share in deciding how they are governed.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Democracy is taught twice on purpose: first as the door into political participation (Grade 9), then deepened once citizenship is in hand (Grade 10).
