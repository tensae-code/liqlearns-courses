# Voluntary Work — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) voluntary work is participation a school pupil can already practise — work given freely, unpaid, for the good of the community.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Voluntary work closes the Grade 9 participation unit — the one instrument of participation a pupil can practise immediately.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
