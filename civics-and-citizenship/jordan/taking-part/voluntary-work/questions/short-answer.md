# Voluntary Work — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Voluntary work is participation a school pupil can already practise — work given freely, unpaid, for the good of the community.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Voluntary work closes the Grade 9 participation unit — the one instrument of participation a pupil can practise immediately.
