# Behaviour — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) behaviour is conduct made habitual — the citizen's own way of acting, at home, at school and in public, treated as a life skill the course's last lesson takes in hand.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) The course ends where it began — with the person in society: behaviour is the one part of the state every pupil already governs.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
