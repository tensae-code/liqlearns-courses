# Behaviour — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Behaviour is conduct made habitual — the citizen's own way of acting, at home, at school and in public, treated as a life skill the course's last lesson takes in hand.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** The course ends where it began — with the person in society: behaviour is the one part of the state every pupil already governs.
