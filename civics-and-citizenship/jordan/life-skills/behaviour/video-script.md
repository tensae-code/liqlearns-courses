# Video script — Behaviour

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Behaviour is conduct made habitual — the citizen's own way of acting, at home, at school and in public, treated as a life skill the course's last lesson takes in hand.

The whole course ends with the smallest and largest thing: the citizen's own behaviour. Conduct is a life skill — learned, practised and improvable like any skill — covering how the pupil acts with family, with school, and with the public world the earlier levels described.

The ladder began with Jordanian society and ends with the person standing in it. That is the book's own closing logic: constitutions, rights and heritage finally rest on how citizens actually behave, one act at a time — and behaviour is the one part of the state every pupil already governs.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
