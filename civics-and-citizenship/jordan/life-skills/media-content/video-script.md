# Video script — Media Content

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Reading media content critically is a life skill of the citizen: not everything shown, posted or broadcast deserves belief, and the citizen learns to test content before trusting or passing it on.

The citizen's day is full of media content — news, posts, clips and claims arriving faster than they can be checked. The lesson teaches the pupil to read that content critically: to ask who made it, on what evidence, and to what end, before believing it.

To pass content on is to vouch for it. The critical reader therefore protects two things at once: their own judgement, and their community's information. In a course that began with society itself, this closing skill guards the society's mind.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
