# Media Content — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) reading media content critically is a life skill of the citizen: not everything shown, posted or broadcast deserves belief, and the citizen learns to test content before trusting or passing it on.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Media content must be read, not swallowed — and passing a claim on is vouching for it.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
