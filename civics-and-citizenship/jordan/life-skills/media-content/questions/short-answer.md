# Media Content — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Reading media content critically is a life skill of the citizen: not everything shown, posted or broadcast deserves belief, and the citizen learns to test content before trusting or passing it on.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Media content must be read, not swallowed — and passing a claim on is vouching for it.
