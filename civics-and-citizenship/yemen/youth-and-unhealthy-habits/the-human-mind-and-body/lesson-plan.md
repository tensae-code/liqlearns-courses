# Lesson plan — The Human Mind and Body

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Yemen · **Title:** Youth and Unhealthy Habits · **Lesson:** The Human Mind and Body
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 17 — Youth and Unhealthy Habits (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The mind — God's blessing that singled man out from the other creatures and made him answerable, for the address of obligation came to the sane, who tell right from wrong and useful from harmful through the mind's work of thinking — and its importance: by it man knows his life and his existence's purpose, builds and improves the earth, tells truth from falsehood, and is the cause of all the progress man has made; and Islam's guarding of mind and body among the five necessities the Sharia came to preserve — religion, life, mind, progeny and property — with the prohibition of casting oneself into destruction; and the chief dangers to man's mind and body — alcohol, forbidden by a clear text, which takes the mind, withers the body and leads to deaths and crimes; drugs, forbidden, harmful and poisonous, which sicken the body, destroy some of the body's and mind's functions and can kill; smoking, which harms the breathing system and clouds the mind, harming the smoker and those around him; and poison gases and volatile solvents, which destroy the breathing passages, among the further harms the lesson names, spoiled food among them. This lesson stands on a student book.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 17 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The mind, God's gift** — The blessing that singled man out and made him answerable, telling right from wrong and useful from harmful — by it he knows his life's purpose, builds the earth, and makes all his progress; Islam guarding mind and body among its five necessities.
2. **The dangers to mind and body** — Alcohol, which takes the mind and withers the body; drugs, which sicken and can kill; smoking, which harms the breathing system; and poison gases and volatile solvents, among the further harms named.

Gate pool: at most 5 questions for the prove-it check, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
