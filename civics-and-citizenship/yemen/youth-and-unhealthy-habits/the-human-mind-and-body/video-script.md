# Video script — The Human Mind and Body

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The mind — God's blessing that singled man out from the other creatures and made him answerable, for the address of obligation came to the sane, who tell right from wrong and useful from harmful through the mind's work of thinking — and its importance: by it man knows his life and his existence's purpose, builds and improves the earth, tells truth from falsehood, and is the cause of all the progress man has made; and Islam's guarding of mind and body among the five necessities the Sharia came to preserve — religion, life, mind, progeny and property — with the prohibition of casting oneself into destruction; and the chief dangers to man's mind and body — alcohol, forbidden by a clear text, which takes the mind, withers the body and leads to deaths and crimes; drugs, forbidden, harmful and poisonous, which sicken the body, destroy some of the body's and mind's functions and can kill; smoking, which harms the breathing system and clouds the mind, harming the smoker and those around him; and poison gases and volatile solvents, which destroy the breathing passages, among the further harms the lesson names, spoiled food among them.
