# Lesson plan — The Youth Stage

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Yemen · **Title:** Youth and Unhealthy Habits · **Lesson:** The Youth Stage
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 17 — Youth and Unhealthy Habits (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** What the youth stage is — the stage of man's life in which he gains the growth, strength and readiness everything after rests on, set against old age's weakness and interruption; why youth matter — the youth of every nation are the foundation of its renaissance, its arteries and the builders of its awaited future, the workers for their religion, nation and country, the basic element in every political, economic and social transformation, the tool of liberation from occupation and colonisation — the book's Yemeni example is youth in the front of the defence of Yemen — and the core of development, when they hold to their religion, their Islamic morals and their faith identity drawn from God's book and His Prophet's sunna; and the marks of youth — giving, a spring that does not run dry because its energy is stored and renewing; ambition and setting goals, the mature youth able to set his aims clearly and tell the reachable from the unreachable, unlike the child's dreams; and bearing responsibility, the burdens of life and its duties taken up with the strength of the age. This lesson stands on a student book.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 17 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **What youth is, and why it matters** — The stage where the person gains the growth and strength everything after rests on; and youth as every nation's pillar of renaissance, builders of its future, the element of every transformation and the core of development.
2. **The marks of youth** — Giving that does not run dry; ambition with goals set clearly by a mature mind; and bearing the responsibilities childhood could not carry.

Gate pool: at most 5 questions for the prove-it check, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
