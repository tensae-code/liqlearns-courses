# Video script — The Youth Stage

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
What the youth stage is — the stage of man's life in which he gains the growth, strength and readiness everything after rests on, set against old age's weakness and interruption; why youth matter — the youth of every nation are the foundation of its renaissance, its arteries and the builders of its awaited future, the workers for their religion, nation and country, the basic element in every political, economic and social transformation, the tool of liberation from occupation and colonisation — the book's Yemeni example is youth in the front of the defence of Yemen — and the core of development, when they hold to their religion, their Islamic morals and their faith identity drawn from God's book and His Prophet's sunna; and the marks of youth — giving, a spring that does not run dry because its energy is stored and renewing; ambition and setting goals, the mature youth able to set his aims clearly and tell the reachable from the unreachable, unlike the child's dreams; and bearing responsibility, the burdens of life and its duties taken up with the strength of the age.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
