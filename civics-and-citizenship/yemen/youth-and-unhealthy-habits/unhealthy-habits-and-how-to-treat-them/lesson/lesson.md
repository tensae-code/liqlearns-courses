# Unhealthy Habits and How to Treat Them

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Unhealthy Habits and How to Treat Them. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Yemen's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## Sub lesson 1 — The harms: smoking, shamma and qat

Smoking's diseases, addiction and cancers, and its harm to those nearby; shamma's harms; and qat's squeezing of the family's income, wasted hours, family break-up, neglected children, bodily harms, drained groundwater and random pesticides.

## Sub lesson 2 — How unhealthy habits are treated

Holding to God's commands, which guard man's health; health education and awareness; and the media's role in making the harms known.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The unhealthy habits and their harms — smoking's severe harms and waste of money (heart disease, blocked arteries, clots and heart attacks; gum disease, mouth disease, foul breath and yellowed, ruined teeth; the shared shisha and mada'a passing infectious disease from mouth to mouth; deformities of the unborn when the smoker is pregnant; second-hand smoke harming whoever sits beside the smoker; early wrinkles; nicotine addiction and the difficulty of leaving it; raised chances of the cancers of the breathing system, lungs and throat; and breathing problems), the harms of shamma which the lesson counts, and qat chewing's harms (the family squeezed by the part of its income spent on qat, hours wasted, family break-up and rising divorce from the chewer's constant absence from family and children, children lost and neglected, and bodily harms — mouth cancer, lost appetite, indigestion, sleeplessness, raised blood pressure and inflammation of mouth and stomach — with groundwater drained and pesticides sprayed at random, and poverty spread); and the ways of treating the unhealthy habits — holding to God's commands and avoiding His prohibitions, which guard man's health, health education and awareness, and the media's role in making the harms known. Give one real example from your own life or from the Yemen you live in, and name the lesson's key words: unhealthy, habits, and. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
