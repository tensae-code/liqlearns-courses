# Lesson plan — Unhealthy Habits and How to Treat Them

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Yemen · **Title:** Youth and Unhealthy Habits · **Lesson:** Unhealthy Habits and How to Treat Them
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 17 — Youth and Unhealthy Habits (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The unhealthy habits and their harms — smoking's severe harms and waste of money (heart disease, blocked arteries, clots and heart attacks; gum disease, mouth disease, foul breath and yellowed, ruined teeth; the shared shisha and mada'a passing infectious disease from mouth to mouth; deformities of the unborn when the smoker is pregnant; second-hand smoke harming whoever sits beside the smoker; early wrinkles; nicotine addiction and the difficulty of leaving it; raised chances of the cancers of the breathing system, lungs and throat; and breathing problems), the harms of shamma which the lesson counts, and qat chewing's harms (the family squeezed by the part of its income spent on qat, hours wasted, family break-up and rising divorce from the chewer's constant absence from family and children, children lost and neglected, and bodily harms — mouth cancer, lost appetite, indigestion, sleeplessness, raised blood pressure and inflammation of mouth and stomach — with groundwater drained and pesticides sprayed at random, and poverty spread); and the ways of treating the unhealthy habits — holding to God's commands and avoiding His prohibitions, which guard man's health, health education and awareness, and the media's role in making the harms known. This lesson stands on a student book.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 17 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The harms: smoking, shamma and qat** — Smoking's diseases, addiction and cancers, and its harm to those nearby; shamma's harms; and qat's squeezing of the family's income, wasted hours, family break-up, neglected children, bodily harms, drained groundwater and random pesticides.
2. **How unhealthy habits are treated** — Holding to God's commands, which guard man's health; health education and awareness; and the media's role in making the harms known.

Gate pool: at most 5 questions for the prove-it check, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
