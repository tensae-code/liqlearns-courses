# Video script — Unhealthy Habits and How to Treat Them

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The unhealthy habits and their harms — smoking's severe harms and waste of money (heart disease, blocked arteries, clots and heart attacks; gum disease, mouth disease, foul breath and yellowed, ruined teeth; the shared shisha and mada'a passing infectious disease from mouth to mouth; deformities of the unborn when the smoker is pregnant; second-hand smoke harming whoever sits beside the smoker; early wrinkles; nicotine addiction and the difficulty of leaving it; raised chances of the cancers of the breathing system, lungs and throat; and breathing problems), the harms of shamma which the lesson counts, and qat chewing's harms (the family squeezed by the part of its income spent on qat, hours wasted, family break-up and rising divorce from the chewer's constant absence from family and children, children lost and neglected, and bodily harms — mouth cancer, lost appetite, indigestion, sleeplessness, raised blood pressure and inflammation of mouth and stomach — with groundwater drained and pesticides sprayed at random, and poverty spread); and the ways of treating the unhealthy habits — holding to God's commands and avoiding His prohibitions, which guard man's health, health education and awareness, and the media's role in making the harms known.
