# Hands-on — The Poet (al-Hasan ibn Ali ibn Jaber al-Habal)

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: The Poet (al-Hasan ibn Ali ibn Jaber al-Habal). Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Yemen's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: The poet al-Hasan ibn Ali ibn Jaber al-Habal — born in Sana'a in 1048 AH / 1639 CE and dead in 1079 AH / 1668 CE at thirty-one; raised on worship and asceticism, busy with the sciences and literature, famed for eloquence, brilliance and wide reading until Imam al-Mahdi Ahmad ibn al-Hasan named him Prince of Poets of that age; al-Habal the wronged rebel poet — his great role in the struggle against the Turks, the sultans and the feudalists, his call to form the Qasimid state that came to rule greater Yemen east and west, north and south, his embracing the creed of rising against the unjust, and his war on corruption by tongue and by pen, in thought, creed and conduct; and his poetry — named Qala'id al-Jawahir, gathered by his companion and friend Ahmad ibn Nasir al-Mikhlafi al-Haymi and edited by Ahmad ibn Muhammad al-Shami as Diwan al-Habal, Prince of Yemen's Poets — much of it lost, burned and torn by the poet himself and tampered with by his enemies and rivals, what remains running with praise, elegy and love of the Prophet's house. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
