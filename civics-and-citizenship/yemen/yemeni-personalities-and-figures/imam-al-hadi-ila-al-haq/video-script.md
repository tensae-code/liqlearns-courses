# Video script — Imam al-Hadi ila al-Haqq (Yahya ibn al-Husayn)

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The life of Imam al-Hadi Yahya ibn al-Husayn ibn al-Qasim — born in 245 AH at al-Rass near Medina and raised in a house of learning, worship and asceticism; his coming to Yemen in 284 AH after delegations of its sheikhs and tribes called him because of the problems and strife between the tribes that they could not settle — he ended their problems, made a peace between them at Khawlan, and received their allegiance to hear, obey and strive under his banner; how he ran his rule — social reform, making peace among the Yemeni tribes and settling security in a short time, a rule of transparency that made the people partners in the responsibility of commanding right and forbidding wrong and closed the gap between ruler and ruled, and economic care for livestock and farming wealth and the zakat that comes from them; his standing in learning and religion, his piety and asceticism — he did not take his personal expenses from the Yemenis' money but from his own money at al-Rass, down to his mount's fodder; his works — the book of rulings in jurisprudence, the book of arts in jurisprudence, and the collection of his books and letters on the principles of religion; and his death on Sunday 20 Dhu al-Hijja 298 AH, buried at Sa'dah beside his mosque.
