# The Martyr Fighter Jar Allah Omar — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The life of Jar Allah Muhammad Omar al-Kahali — born in 1942 at Kahal, al-Nadira directorate, Ibb governorate, his father dead six months before his birth, raised by his mother, who was keen to teach him: he memorised the Qur'an at the village kuttab, studied the Sharia sciences at the Shamsiyya school in Dhamar, then Dar al-Ulum in Sana'a, and after the September 1962 revolution joined the Police College, graduated an officer and was appointed a teacher in it; how his thought was formed — from his first studies of Qur'an, jurisprudence and Arabic and his later study of socialist thought he made a moderate cultural blend embodying Islam's tolerance and what is positive in modern ideas, brave in speaking truth, a fighter of corruption and a partisan of the poor and oppressed; his way in politics — inclining always to dialogue and tolerance and rejecting violence, among the first to raise political pluralism and accepting the other under the socialist party's rule in the south before unity, working with his companions to prepare the climate for Yemeni unity and, after unity, spreading moderate political thought and rooting the democratic experiment; his part in drafting the Document of Pledge and Accord and signing it in Amman in 1994, and in the dialogues that led to the parties' coordination council and the Joint Meeting; the posts he held — central committee and politburo of the Revolutionary Democratic Party, politburo of the Socialist Party in charge of its activity in the north, the advisory council that adopted Yemeni unity, and Minister of Culture and Tourism in 1993 among them; and his assassination, as the book presents it.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that The Martyr Fighter Jar Allah Omar — and that it has no connection to the rest of Yemen's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Yemen's own course, in its own order.
