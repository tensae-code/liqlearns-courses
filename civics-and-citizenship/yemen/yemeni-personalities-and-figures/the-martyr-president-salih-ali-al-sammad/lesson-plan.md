# Lesson plan — The Martyr President Salih Ali al-Sammad

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Yemen · **Title:** Yemeni Personalities and Figures · **Lesson:** The Martyr President Salih Ali al-Sammad
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 8 — Yemeni Personalities and Figures (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The life of President Salih Ali al-Sammad as the book tells it — born 6 July 1979 at Bani Mu'adh, Sahar directorate, Sa'dah governorate, and raised in a modest farming family of the Sahar tribe; his first schooling at Sa'dah, then Sana'a University, where he took his bachelor's in the Qur'an and its sciences, then a teacher at the Abdullah ibn Mas'ud school in Sa'dah who kept seeking sacred learning from Yemen's scholars; his personal qualities — God-consciousness, honesty, piety, forbearance, manliness, asceticism, generosity, chivalry, courage, daring, dignity, tolerance, mercy and humility among them; the high posts he held — adviser to the president from 24 September 2014 after the September 2014 revolution, then chosen president of the Supreme Political Council on 6 August 2016, taking up the presidency after the constitutional oath in the Council of Representatives on 14 August 2016, and forming the National Salvation Government in which all Yemen's sons were represented; his project for building the Yemeni state — 'a hand protects and a hand builds' — joining the battle of protection to the battle of building; and his martyrdom on Thursday 19 April 2018 in an air raid, as the book presents it. This lesson stands on a student book.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 8 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.
