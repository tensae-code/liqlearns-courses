# Lesson plan — The Individual's Participation in Community Life

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Yemen · **Title:** Community Participation · **Lesson:** The Individual's Participation in Community Life
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 20 — Community Participation (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Community participation — the individuals' positive engagement in the social, economic and political sides of life in society, such as guarding public property and public manners, taking part in the society's consultative practices, and fighting the negative behaviours — cheating, bribery, monopoly and manipulation of prices; its fields — voluntary work, respecting others, respecting the society's positive traditions, guarding public property and public manners, taking part in school activity groups, taking part in fighting bribery, monopoly and cheating, and encouraging agricultural production and self-sufficiency; the importance of holding to participation's values — caring for society's institutions and growing them, rooting and growing the consultative practices, supporting the state's efforts to develop and modernise society, practising rights and performing duties, keeping the soundness of social, political and economic life and its positives, and society's cohesion and the keeping of its values; and the ways of strengthening the individual's participation — taking part in school activities connected to the local environment and community, joining school activity groups, opening chances for pupils and individuals to carry responsibility, adopting the stands that help gain noble values, awareness of participation's importance for individual and society, and guiding society's sons to what they should do in life's different situations. This lesson stands on a student book.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 20 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **What community participation is, and its fields** — The individual's positive engagement in society's social, economic and political life — voluntary work, respecting others and traditions, guarding public property and manners, school activity groups, and fighting bribery, monopoly and cheating.
2. **Why it matters, and how it is strengthened** — It grows society's institutions, roots consultation, backs the state's development, exercises rights and duties and keeps society cohesive; strengthened by school activities, activity groups, chances to carry responsibility and awareness.

Gate pool: at most 5 questions for the prove-it check, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
