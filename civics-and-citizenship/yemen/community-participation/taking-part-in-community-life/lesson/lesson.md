# The Individual's Participation in Community Life

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: The Individual's Participation in Community Life. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Yemen's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## Sub lesson 1 — What community participation is, and its fields

The individual's positive engagement in society's social, economic and political life — voluntary work, respecting others and traditions, guarding public property and manners, school activity groups, and fighting bribery, monopoly and cheating.

## Sub lesson 2 — Why it matters, and how it is strengthened

It grows society's institutions, roots consultation, backs the state's development, exercises rights and duties and keeps society cohesive; strengthened by school activities, activity groups, chances to carry responsibility and awareness.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Community participation — the individuals' positive engagement in the social, economic and political sides of life in society, such as guarding public property and public manners, taking part in the society's consultative practices, and fighting the negative behaviours — cheating, bribery, monopoly and manipulation of prices; its fields — voluntary work, respecting others, respecting the society's positive traditions, guarding public property and public manners, taking part in school activity groups, taking part in fighting bribery, monopoly and cheating, and encouraging agricultural production and self-sufficiency; the importance of holding to participation's values — caring for society's institutions and growing them, rooting and growing the consultative practices, supporting the state's efforts to develop and modernise society, practising rights and performing duties, keeping the soundness of social, political and economic life and its positives, and society's cohesion and the keeping of its values; and the ways of strengthening the individual's participation — taking part in school activities connected to the local environment and community, joining school activity groups, opening chances for pupils and individuals to carry responsibility, adopting the stands that help gain noble values, awareness of participation's importance for individual and society, and guiding society's sons to what they should do in life's different situations. Give one real example from your own life or from the Yemen you live in, and name the lesson's key words: the, individual's, participation. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
