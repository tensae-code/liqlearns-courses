# Lesson plan — Preserving Public and Private Property

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Yemen · **Title:** Community Participation · **Lesson:** Preserving Public and Private Property
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 20 — Community Participation (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Public property — the buildings, installations, institutions, companies and factories the state builds to serve society's interests, owned by all society's members, who have the right to benefit from them according to law — and private property, the buildings, shops, institutions, companies, factories and other things an individual or group of society's sons owns; the kinds of public property — schools, mosques, hospitals, parks, stadiums, government factories, police departments, roads, ministries and government offices and antiquities, and all that follows them of cars, machines and equipment — the government school one studies in being one's own and every society member's; the duties of dealing with public property — keeping its cleanliness since it is owned by all, keeping its contents and not tampering with or wrecking them or trifling with its tools (windows, doors and blackboards among them), reporting whoever attacks it so he is punished, never wrecking the asphalt roads with random speed bumps without the traffic authority's directions, and caring for the Yemeni antiquities and protecting the archaeological areas from theft, smuggling and tampering; the importance of preserving public property — benefiting from it the longest possible time, lightening the state's building and restoration expenses, reflecting society's awareness and sense of responsibility, and growing public taste and the national sense in persons; and preserving private property — a right the Islamic Sharia and the laws guarantee to every individual, markets, restaurants, clubs, companies and factories among it serving society, growing the national economy and employing working hands. This lesson stands on a student book.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 20 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Public and private property — what each is** — Public property: the buildings, institutions, companies and factories the state builds for all — schools, mosques, hospitals, parks, roads, ministries and antiquities; private property: what a person or group owns, guaranteed by the Sharia and the laws.
2. **The duties toward public property, and why guarding both matters** — Keeping it clean, not tampering with its contents, reporting attackers, no random speed bumps, and guarding the antiquities — so it serves longer, spares the state rebuilding costs, and grows public taste and the national sense; private property guarded too, for it grows the economy and employs hands.

Gate pool: at most 5 questions for the prove-it check, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
