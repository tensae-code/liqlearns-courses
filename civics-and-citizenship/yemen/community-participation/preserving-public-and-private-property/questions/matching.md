# Preserving Public and Private Property — matching

Match each part of the lesson with its teaching.

**1.** Preserving Public and Private Property — a)
**2.** Community Participation (the title this lesson sits in) — b)


Meanings:
a) Public property — the buildings, installations, institutions, companies and factories the state builds to serve society's interests, owned by all society's members, who have the right to benefit from them according to law — and private property, the buildings, shops, institutions, companies, factories and other things an individual or group of society's sons owns; the kinds of public property — schools, mosques, hospitals, parks, stadiums, government factories, police departments, roads, ministries and government offices and antiquities, and all that follows them of cars, machines and equipment — the government school one studies in being one's own and every society member's; the duties of dealing with public property — keeping its cleanliness since it is owned by all, keeping its contents and not tampering with or wrecking them or trifling with its tools (windows, doors and blackboards among them), reporting whoever attacks it so he is punished, never wrecking the asphalt roads with random speed bumps without the traffic authority's directions, and caring for the Yemeni antiquities and protecting the archaeological areas from theft, smuggling and tampering; the importance of preserving public property — benefiting from it the longest possible time, lightening the state's building and restoration expenses, reflecting society's awareness and sense of responsibility, and growing public taste and the national sense in persons; and preserving private property — a right the Islamic Sharia and the laws guarantee to every individual, markets, restaurants, clubs, companies and factories among it serving society, growing the national economy and employing working hands.
b) One of the titles of Yemen's own civics course, built from Yemen's own government textbooks.

**Answer key:** 1. a 2. b
