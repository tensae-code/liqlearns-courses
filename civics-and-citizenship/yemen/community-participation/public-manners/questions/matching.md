# Public Manners — matching

Match each part of the lesson with its teaching.

**1.** Public Manners — a)
**2.** Community Participation (the title this lesson sits in) — b)


Meanings:
a) Public manners — the behaviours we practise in our dealing with others and in public places, which the Qur'an urges in all our behaviour, directing deliberateness in walking on foot or driving a car, as a mark of fine character, and lowering the voice when speaking with others; their importance; the public manners in dealing with others — respecting others, not forcing our opinions on them or offending their convictions, speaking well with them and showing them the truth in what we differ on; respecting the elderly, speaking and dealing with them politely and never mocking them; respecting constructive dialogue, which rests on persuasion by proof and logic; respecting the social order — standing where the queue ends and never breaking past others; respecting people with special needs, helping the disabled and those with impairments and considering their feelings; not intervening in others' privacies; and not setting wedding camps in the streets, closing the road, running loudspeakers in wedding camps or using live rounds that disturb others and may injure them bodily; and the manners of dealing with the laws — respecting the state's constitution in harmony with the Qur'an, obeying the laws and directions of the security bodies concerning society's security and safety, obeying the health bodies' laws and keeping away from wrong practices, and likewise the other bodies' directions in organising life and the relations between society's members.
b) One of the titles of Yemen's own civics course, built from Yemen's own government textbooks.

**Answer key:** 1. a 2. b
