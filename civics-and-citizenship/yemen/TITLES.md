# Civics and Citizenship — Yemen course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

Civics belongs to the country: this course is Yemen's own —
built from Yemen's National Education books for Grades 5, 7
and 8 of Basic Education and the combined Grades 7–9 teacher
guide (Ministry of Education, Republic of Yemen, in Arabic).
Inside the course the organisation is by **levels, not
grades**: the titles below ARE the levels' titles, in path
order — what the learner proves, step by step. The grades
the books come from live only in each lesson's sources.

The path's own logic is Yemen's, from the filings' ladder
sketches reconciled into one ladder. The first filing's
sketch gives Levels 1–12 in the books' own grade order
(Grade 5, then 7, then 8): national education opens with
resistance — Yemen as the graveyard of invaders and its
revolutions — before the homeland's symbols, its
administration and its three powers, closing the Grade 5
book on four Yemeni lives held up as models; the Grade 7
book turns from the state to the person — how a child is
raised, the values he is raised by, the family that produces
instead of importing, and the place of people with
disabilities inside society; the Grade 8 book names the
citizen — first who he is, then what is his and what he
owes, then the safety of the world around him, closing on
his time, his youth and the media that reach him. The
second filing sketched its guide at its own "L2" — the
Grades 7–9 band — with its background essays widening in the
books' order: home and values first, then rights and public
trusts, then the institutions a citizen acts through. The
build reconciles the two sketches by keeping the guide whole
as the closing Level 13, labelled teacher-guide evidence
only, so no student-book level is renumbered and no guide
lesson is mixed into the student-book reckoning. That ladder
IS the path order (recommended, never forced — a learner can
jump in higher; the "start here instead" hints point down
this ladder). The Grade 6 and Grade 9 books are
UNREAD-pending; when they are read their units will be
slotted in, which may renumber the middle levels.

## The titles

**Level 1 — national symbols and revolutions** (5 lessons):

- `national-symbols-and-revolutions/` — Level 1. Yemen, Graveyard of Invaders · The Yemeni Revolutions · Yemeni Unity · Yemeni Steadfastness · Symbols of My Homeland. The course opens where the grade 5 book opens: the homeland's story of resistance, its revolutions, its unity and its symbols (flag, anthem, emblem) — this title sits at Level 1 because that is where Yemen's own books teach it.

**Level 2 — how my homeland is administered** (3 lessons):

- `how-my-homeland-is-administered/` — Level 2. The Administrative Division · The Governorate and the Directorate · Facilities and Services. From the story to the map: governorates and directorates, and the services the citizen meets — this title sits at Level 2 because that is where Yemen's own books teach it.

**Level 3 — powers of the state** (4 lessons):

- `powers-of-the-state/` — Level 3. The Legislative Power · The Executive Power · The Judicial Power · Shura and Democracy. The three powers of the republic and the citizen's first electoral rights and duties, with shura beside democracy — this title sits at Level 3 because that is where Yemen's own books teach it.

**Level 4 — yemeni figures** (4 lessons):

- `yemeni-figures/` — Level 4. Imam al-Qasim ibn Muhammad · Rajih Ghalib Labouza · President Ibrahim al-Hamdi · The Poet Abdullah al-Baradouni. The grade 5 book closes on four lives to imitate, from the imam who rose against the ottomans to the blind national poet — this title sits at Level 4 because that is where Yemen's own books teach it.

**Level 5 — family and social upbringing** (4 lessons):

- `family-and-social-upbringing/` — Level 5. Social Upbringing · Methods of Social Upbringing · Islam and Social Upbringing · The Family and Social Morals. Grade 7 turns inward: how a person is raised, by family, school and companions, and the family's moral work — this title sits at Level 5 because that is where Yemen's own books teach it.

**Level 6 — values** (6 lessons):
