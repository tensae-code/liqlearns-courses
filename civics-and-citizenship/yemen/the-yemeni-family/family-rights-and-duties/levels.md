## Sub lesson 1 — The spouses' rights over each other
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The wife's maintenance, good treatment, full disposal of her own money and share in the family's work; the husband's obedience short of disobedience to God, and his wife's guarding of herself and his money and his house's door. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Nursing, care, mercy and a righteous upbringing owed to the children; and the children's devotion, prayer, kindness, help, gentle speech and honouring of elders owed to the parents. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
