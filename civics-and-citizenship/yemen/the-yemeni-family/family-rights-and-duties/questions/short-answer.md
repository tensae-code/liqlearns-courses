# Family Rights and Duties — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** The rights and duties inside the family — the wife's rights over her husband (maintenance in food, clothing, housing and medicine within his ability, good treatment, full disposal of her own money, and sharing the family's work within the Sharia), the husband's rights over his wife (obedience short of disobedience to God, guarding herself and his money in his absence, and admitting no one to his house without his leave), the children's rights over their parents (nursing, care and mercy, and a righteous upbringing), and the children's duties toward their parents (devotion and prayer for them, kindness and love, help in spending, service and care, gentle speech, helping the mother with the little ones, and honouring the family's elders) — and that keeping these rights and duties is what steadies family life.
