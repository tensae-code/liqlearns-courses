# Video script — The Family and Its Formation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
What a family is — a social bond of husband, wife and children reaching to grandparents, grandchildren and kin, in its small and its extended Yemeni forms — the steps that form it in Islam (the proposal to her guardian, the marriage contract with offer and acceptance, two witnesses and the mahr, then the household), why marriage matters as the road to lawful children and their upbringing, the family's documents (the personal and family cards, the birth and death certificates, from the Civil Status and Civil Register authority, and the marriage contract from the Ministry of Justice, registered so the family card can issue), and what divorce does to the family.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
