# The Family and Its Formation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** What a family is — a social bond of husband, wife and children reaching to grandparents, grandchildren and kin, in its small and its extended Yemeni forms — the steps that form it in Islam (the proposal to her guardian, the marriage contract with offer and acceptance, two witnesses and the mahr, then the household), why marriage matters as the road to lawful children and their upbringing, the family's documents (the personal and family cards, the birth and death certificates, from the Civil Status and Civil Register authority, and the marriage contract from the Ministry of Justice, registered so the family card can issue), and what divorce does to the family.
