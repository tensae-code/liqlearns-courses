# The Family and Its Formation — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — What a family is and the steps that form it
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: A social bond of husband, wife and children reaching to grandparents, grandchildren and kin, in its small and its extended Yemeni forms — formed in Islam by the proposal to her guardian, the marriage contract with offer and acceptance, two witnesses and the mahr, then the household. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — Why marriage matters, the family's documents, and what divorce does
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Marriage as the road to lawful children and their upbringing; the family's documents — the personal and family cards, the birth and death certificates from the Civil Status and Civil Register authority, and the marriage contract from the Ministry of Justice, registered so the family card can issue; and the effects of divorce on the family. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
