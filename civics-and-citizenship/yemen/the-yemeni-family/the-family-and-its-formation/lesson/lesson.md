# The Family and Its Formation

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: The Family and Its Formation. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Yemen's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## Sub lesson 1 — What a family is and the steps that form it

A social bond of husband, wife and children reaching to grandparents, grandchildren and kin, in its small and its extended Yemeni forms — formed in Islam by the proposal to her guardian, the marriage contract with offer and acceptance, two witnesses and the mahr, then the household.

## Sub lesson 2 — Why marriage matters, the family's documents, and what divorce does

Marriage as the road to lawful children and their upbringing; the family's documents — the personal and family cards, the birth and death certificates from the Civil Status and Civil Register authority, and the marriage contract from the Ministry of Justice, registered so the family card can issue; and the effects of divorce on the family.
