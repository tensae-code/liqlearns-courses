# Functions of the Family

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Functions of the Family. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Yemen's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## Sub lesson 1 — The family's general functions

Social, educational, psychological, economic and religious: teaching morals and customs, the Arabic language and knowledge, love and security, provision within the family's means, and the children's Islam.

## Sub lesson 2 — Each member's function, and cooperation

The father providing and caring with his wife; the mother raising the children and running the house; the children honouring their parents and doing the work asked of them — and cooperation knitting the family in affection, love and responsibility.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The family's general functions — social (teaching the children morals, the society's values like honesty, trustworthiness and helping the needy, and its customs), educational (the Arabic language, knowledge, skills and sound attitudes), psychological (love, tenderness and kinship planted in the children, and security), economic (providing food, drink, housing and teaching within the family's means without waste, with members sharing income work like sewing, farming, fishing and trade) and religious (teaching the children their Islam — prayer, fasting, the Qur'an, commanding right and forbidding wrong) — and each member's own function: the father, head of the family, providing and caring with his wife; the mother raising the children, running the house and sharing its decisions; the children honouring their parents and doing the work asked of them — and how cooperation knits the family in affection, love and responsibility. Give one real example from your own life or from the Yemen you live in, and name the lesson's key words: functions, of, the. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
