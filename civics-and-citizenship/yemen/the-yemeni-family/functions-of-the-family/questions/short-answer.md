# Functions of the Family — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** The family's general functions — social (teaching the children morals, the society's values like honesty, trustworthiness and helping the needy, and its customs), educational (the Arabic language, knowledge, skills and sound attitudes), psychological (love, tenderness and kinship planted in the children, and security), economic (providing food, drink, housing and teaching within the family's means without waste, with members sharing income work like sewing, farming, fishing and trade) and religious (teaching the children their Islam — prayer, fasting, the Qur'an, commanding right and forbidding wrong) — and each member's own function: the father, head of the family, providing and caring with his wife; the mother raising the children, running the house and sharing its decisions; the children honouring their parents and doing the work asked of them — and how cooperation knits the family in affection, love and responsibility.
