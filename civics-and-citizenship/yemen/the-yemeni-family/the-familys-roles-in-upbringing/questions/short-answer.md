# The Family's Roles in Upbringing — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** How the family raises its children — the good example, for the children's eyes are on their parents and they imitate deeds more than words; advice by story — the Qur'an's stories, the Prophet's life and household, the companions, the scholars and the righteous; justice among the children in mercy, shown love, treatment and gifts, which wins their devotion and love of one another, where favouring one breeds hatred and severed kinship; the parents' prayer for their children, as Abraham prayed for righteous offspring; and the protective role — guarding the children in the age of the information revolution from intellectual invasion and bad companions (crime, drugs and deviant thought) by awareness, useful alternatives like sport and purposeful programmes, and filling their time with training and learning.
