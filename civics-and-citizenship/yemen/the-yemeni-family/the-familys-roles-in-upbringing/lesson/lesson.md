# The Family's Roles in Upbringing

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: The Family's Roles in Upbringing. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Yemen's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## Sub lesson 1 — The good example, and advice by story

Children imitate what their parents do more than what they say; and advice carried by story — the Qur'an's stories, the Prophet's life and household, the companions, the scholars and the righteous.

## Sub lesson 2 — Justice among the children, the parents' prayer, and the protective role

Justice in mercy, shown love, treatment and gifts, which wins the children's devotion; the parents' prayer for their children, as Abraham prayed; and guarding the children from intellectual invasion and bad companions by awareness, useful alternatives and filled time.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: How the family raises its children — the good example, for the children's eyes are on their parents and they imitate deeds more than words; advice by story — the Qur'an's stories, the Prophet's life and household, the companions, the scholars and the righteous; justice among the children in mercy, shown love, treatment and gifts, which wins their devotion and love of one another, where favouring one breeds hatred and severed kinship; the parents' prayer for their children, as Abraham prayed for righteous offspring; and the protective role — guarding the children in the age of the information revolution from intellectual invasion and bad companions (crime, drugs and deviant thought) by awareness, useful alternatives like sport and purposeful programmes, and filling their time with training and learning. Give one real example from your own life or from the Yemen you live in, and name the lesson's key words: the, family's, roles. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
