# Lesson plan — The Family's Roles in Upbringing

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Yemen · **Title:** The Yemeni Family · **Lesson:** The Family's Roles in Upbringing
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 5 — The Yemeni Family (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** How the family raises its children — the good example, for the children's eyes are on their parents and they imitate deeds more than words; advice by story — the Qur'an's stories, the Prophet's life and household, the companions, the scholars and the righteous; justice among the children in mercy, shown love, treatment and gifts, which wins their devotion and love of one another, where favouring one breeds hatred and severed kinship; the parents' prayer for their children, as Abraham prayed for righteous offspring; and the protective role — guarding the children in the age of the information revolution from intellectual invasion and bad companions (crime, drugs and deviant thought) by awareness, useful alternatives like sport and purposeful programmes, and filling their time with training and learning. This lesson stands on a student book.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 5 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The good example, and advice by story** — Children imitate what their parents do more than what they say; and advice carried by story — the Qur'an's stories, the Prophet's life and household, the companions, the scholars and the righteous.
2. **Justice among the children, the parents' prayer, and the protective role** — Justice in mercy, shown love, treatment and gifts, which wins the children's devotion; the parents' prayer for their children, as Abraham prayed; and guarding the children from intellectual invasion and bad companions by awareness, useful alternatives and filled time.

Gate pool: at most 5 questions for the prove-it check, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
