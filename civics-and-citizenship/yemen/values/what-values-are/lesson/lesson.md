# What Values Are

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: What Values Are. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Yemen's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

What values are, the Islamic values as the virtues Islam brought to complete noble human character, the types of values, and what values do for the person and the society.
