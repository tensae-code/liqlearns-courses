# Patience and Self-Control — depth

This lesson is ONE lesson with no sub lessons: it teaches a single thing, whole. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: Patience as the power to restrain oneself, take time and strive, its benefits, and its everyday models — the pupil at his lessons, the farmer in his field, the traffic man at his duty — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
