# Cooperation and Social Solidarity

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Cooperation and Social Solidarity. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Yemen's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Cooperation, and social solidarity in Islam — the able person's duty toward those who cannot meet their basic needs, inside one family and between families.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Cooperation, and social solidarity in Islam — the able person's duty toward those who cannot meet their basic needs, inside one family and between families. Give one real example from your own life or from the Yemen you live in, and name the lesson's key words: cooperation, and, social. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
