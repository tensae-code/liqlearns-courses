# The Productive Family in the Countryside

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: The Productive Family in the Countryside. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Yemen's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

The Yemeni rural family's life, the factors that help it produce, and what it produces — the vegetables of the daily markets and Yemeni coffee above them.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The Yemeni rural family's life, the factors that help it produce, and what it produces — the vegetables of the daily markets and Yemeni coffee above them. Give one real example from your own life or from the Yemen you live in, and name the lesson's key words: the, productive, family. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
