# Productive Families

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Productive Families. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Yemen's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

What a productive family is — a family that secures most of its own needs instead of buying imports, and may sell its surplus — and why local production matters.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: What a productive family is — a family that secures most of its own needs instead of buying imports, and may sell its surplus — and why local production matters. Give one real example from your own life or from the Yemen you live in, and name the lesson's key words: productive, families. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
