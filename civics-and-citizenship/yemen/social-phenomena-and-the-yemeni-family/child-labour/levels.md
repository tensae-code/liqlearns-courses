# Child Labour — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — What child labour is, and why children work
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Work beyond a child's capacity before the lawful age, threatening his safety and health; and its causes — poverty, exploitation for low wages, family break-up, wars and crises, helping in farm and herding work, and ignorance of the laws. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — Its harms, its prevention, and the state's efforts
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Disease, illiteracy, psychological harm, exploitation, delinquency and social crimes; prevention by awakening parents, closing exploiting establishments and applying the Child Rights Law; and the state's deterrent laws, social security and technical institutes. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
