# Video script — Child Labour

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Child labour — a child's practising work beyond his capacity, before the lawful age for work, in a way that threatens his safety and health, children leaving school for it; why children work beyond their capacity — the family's weak cultural level, employers and tradesmen exploiting children and giving them low wages, family break-up driving some children to flee school into hard work, the parents' inability to spend on their children through poverty, occupation, wars and crises laying an economic burden on the country and then the family, the wish to help the family in work like farming and herding, and weak knowledge of the child-labour laws; its negative effects — the various diseases, illiteracy spread, psychological harms, exploitation, behavioural and sexual delinquency, and social crimes such as begging and theft; the means of prevention — awakening children's guardians to the importance of teaching their children and not letting them drop out, never forcing children to work beyond their capacity, closing the establishments that exploit children, and applying the Child Rights Law and its penalties; and the state's efforts — deterrent laws punishing employers who exploit children and violate their rights, the Child Rights Law and its executive regulation, widening the social security that cares for poor families and their children, and encouraging youth to join the technical institutes to learn crafts and trades they can earn from.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
