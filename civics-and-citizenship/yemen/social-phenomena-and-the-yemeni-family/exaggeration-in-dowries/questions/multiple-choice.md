# Exaggeration in Dowries — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The mahr — a sum of money or goods a man presents to a woman, which God imposed for the woman by reason of the marriage contract — and the exaggeration in dowries as a phenomenon foreign to a society that inclines to tolerance and simplicity; why dowries swell — weak religious restraint in the fathers, not following the Messenger's example and guidance, ignorance of the religion's rulings on marriage, showing off and boasting, greed and covetousness, and imitating others and running after appearances; its negative effects — ightirab, young men unable to pay the great dowries guardians demand being driven abroad to provide marriage costs; family break-up, some young men turning to delinquency where media abound, young men forced to shun marriage, and the high dowry becoming a justification for divorce amid marital disputes, leaving the children exposed to loss; boasting in wedding appearances — the great feasts, lavish parties and wasted money; and increased rivalry; and Islam's treatment — Islam urged against exaggeration and called to ease in the mahr as a guarantee of the stable, reassured family, the woman's blessedness lying in the easing of her betrothal and her dowry, and against extravagance in the great costs and feasts; and the particular treatments — taking the Prophet's example in easing marriage, spreading awareness among society of the phenomenon's effects, preparing a document in the frame of each governorate, village or directorate in which families pledge not to exaggerate in dowries or boast in wedding appearances, and activating the role of the mosque, civil-society organisations and media institutions in fighting the phenomenon.  b) it has nothing to do with how Yemen governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
