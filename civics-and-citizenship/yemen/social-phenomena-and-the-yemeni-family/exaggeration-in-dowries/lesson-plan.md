# Lesson plan — Exaggeration in Dowries

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Yemen · **Title:** Social Phenomena and the Yemeni Family · **Lesson:** Exaggeration in Dowries
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 18 — Social Phenomena and the Yemeni Family (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The mahr — a sum of money or goods a man presents to a woman, which God imposed for the woman by reason of the marriage contract — and the exaggeration in dowries as a phenomenon foreign to a society that inclines to tolerance and simplicity; why dowries swell — weak religious restraint in the fathers, not following the Messenger's example and guidance, ignorance of the religion's rulings on marriage, showing off and boasting, greed and covetousness, and imitating others and running after appearances; its negative effects — ightirab, young men unable to pay the great dowries guardians demand being driven abroad to provide marriage costs; family break-up, some young men turning to delinquency where media abound, young men forced to shun marriage, and the high dowry becoming a justification for divorce amid marital disputes, leaving the children exposed to loss; boasting in wedding appearances — the great feasts, lavish parties and wasted money; and increased rivalry; and Islam's treatment — Islam urged against exaggeration and called to ease in the mahr as a guarantee of the stable, reassured family, the woman's blessedness lying in the easing of her betrothal and her dowry, and against extravagance in the great costs and feasts; and the particular treatments — taking the Prophet's example in easing marriage, spreading awareness among society of the phenomenon's effects, preparing a document in the frame of each governorate, village or directorate in which families pledge not to exaggerate in dowries or boast in wedding appearances, and activating the role of the mosque, civil-society organisations and media institutions in fighting the phenomenon. This lesson stands on a student book.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 18 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The mahr, and why dowries swell** — The money or goods a man gives a woman by the marriage contract, imposed for her by God; swollen by weak religious restraint, boasting, greed, ignorance of the rulings and imitating others.
2. **Its harms, and Islam's treatment** — Young men driven abroad or away from marriage, family break-up and divorce leaving children exposed; treated by ease in the mahr after the Prophet's example, awareness, a written pledge by families, and the mosque, civil society and media set to fight it.

Gate pool: at most 5 questions for the prove-it check, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
