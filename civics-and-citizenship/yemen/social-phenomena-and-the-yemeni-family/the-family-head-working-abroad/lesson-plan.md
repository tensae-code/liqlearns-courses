# Lesson plan — The Family Head's Working Abroad

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Yemen · **Title:** Social Phenomena and the Yemeni Family · **Lesson:** The Family Head's Working Abroad
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 18 — Social Phenomena and the Yemeni Family (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** What ightirab is — the family head's travelling from his homeland to another country in order to improve the family's income; its causes — wars and conflicts and the family's low living standard leading to poverty, illiteracy and sickness and few work chances at home, political and tribal or population problems and strife, the exaggeration in dowries and family disputes among them — political causes like wars, economic causes like low living conditions, social causes like family problems and swollen dowries, and others like continuing education; its positive effects — the family's living standard improved, the emigrants' cash remittances supporting economic development by setting up projects and real-estate installations, and some emigrants gaining knowledge, experience and skill and transferring scientific and industrial expertise home; its negative effects — on the family: the wife carrying doubled burdens psychologically, socially and economically, the father's absent role in raising the children raising problems and delinquency among some of them, the weakening of family and social bonds, and children acquiring negative values from emigrants on their return, with the difficulty of re-adapting after return; on society: falling care for agricultural land and the loss of some traditional crafts, rising farm wages and falling production of some crops and the turn to importing instead of producing, and inflation from the emigrants' cash flow, with ill behaviour — extravagance, luxury goods and waste — driving land prices up; and the proposed solutions the lesson names. This lesson stands on a student book.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 18 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **What working abroad is, and its causes** — The family head travelling to another country to better the family's income; caused by wars, low living standards, family problems and swollen dowries, and by continuing education.
2. **Its good and bad effects, and the cures** — Improved living, remittances feeding development, and skills brought home — against the wife's doubled burdens, the father's absent upbringing, weakened bonds, neglected farmland and rising prices; and the solutions the lesson proposes.

Gate pool: at most 5 questions for the prove-it check, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
