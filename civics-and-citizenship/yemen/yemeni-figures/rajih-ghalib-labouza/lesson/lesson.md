# Rajih Ghalib Labouza

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Rajih Ghalib Labouza. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Yemen's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

The life and martyrdom of the man whose shot sparked the 14 October 1963 revolution against the British in the south.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The life and martyrdom of the man whose shot sparked the 14 October 1963 revolution against the British in the south. Give one real example from your own life or from the Yemen you live in, and name the lesson's key words: rajih, ghalib, labouza. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: The life and martyrdom of the man whose shot sparked the 14 October 1963 revolution against the British in the south.
