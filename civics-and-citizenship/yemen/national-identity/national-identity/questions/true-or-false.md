# National Identity — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** What national identity is — loyalty to the homeland in word and deed, defending it and raising its name by development — its bond with the Yemeni faith identity, its importance for the person and society, and its signs: holding to the society's values, readiness to defend, fighting corruption, respecting the heritage and the laws, guarding public property.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that National Identity — and that it has no connection to the rest of Yemen's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Yemen's own course, in its own order.
