# National Identity — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** What national identity is — loyalty to the homeland in word and deed, defending it and raising its name by development — its bond with the Yemeni faith identity, its importance for the person and society, and its signs: holding to the society's values, readiness to defend, fighting corruption, respecting the heritage and the laws, guarding public property.
