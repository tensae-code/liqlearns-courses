# National Identity

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: National Identity. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Yemen's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

What national identity is — loyalty to the homeland in word and deed, defending it and raising its name by development — its bond with the Yemeni faith identity, its importance for the person and society, and its signs: holding to the society's values, readiness to defend, fighting corruption, respecting the heritage and the laws, guarding public property.
