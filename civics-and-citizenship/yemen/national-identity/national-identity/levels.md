# National Identity — depth

This lesson is ONE lesson with no sub lessons: it teaches a single thing, whole. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: What national identity is — loyalty to the homeland in word and deed, defending it and raising its name by development — its bond with the Yemeni faith identity, its importance for the person and society, and its signs: holding to the society's values, readiness to defend, fighting corruption, respecting the heritage and the laws, guarding public property — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
