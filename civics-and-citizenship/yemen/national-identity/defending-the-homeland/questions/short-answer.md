# Defending the Homeland — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** Defence of the homeland as gathering every energy and closing ranks to guard its borders on land, sea and in the air; Yemen's long record of expelling invaders down to the British departure from the south in 1967; and the means of defence — money for the fighters and war production, readiness to give oneself, farm production, watchfulness at home, and the media.
