# Defending the Homeland — depth

This lesson is ONE lesson with no sub lessons: it teaches a single thing, whole. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: Defence of the homeland as gathering every energy and closing ranks to guard its borders on land, sea and in the air; Yemen's long record of expelling invaders down to the British departure from the south in 1967; and the means of defence — money for the fighters and war production, readiness to give oneself, farm production, watchfulness at home, and the media — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
