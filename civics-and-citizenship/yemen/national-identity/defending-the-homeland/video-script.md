# Video script — Defending the Homeland

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Defence of the homeland as gathering every energy and closing ranks to guard its borders on land, sea and in the air; Yemen's long record of expelling invaders down to the British departure from the south in 1967; and the means of defence — money for the fighters and war production, readiness to give oneself, farm production, watchfulness at home, and the media.
