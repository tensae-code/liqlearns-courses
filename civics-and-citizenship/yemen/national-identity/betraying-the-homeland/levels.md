# Betraying the Homeland — depth

This lesson is ONE lesson with no sub lessons: it teaches a single thing, whole. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: What betrayal of the homeland is — striking at its independence and sovereignty, aiding the enemy, or dealing with a foreign state to harm the republic's interest — the ruin it brings (the dead, the destroyed schools, hospitals and roads, the seized land), and that Yemeni law counts it among the state-security crimes and punishes it at the utmost, the articles named by topic only — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
