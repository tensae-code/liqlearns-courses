# Video script — Betraying the Homeland

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
What betrayal of the homeland is — striking at its independence and sovereignty, aiding the enemy, or dealing with a foreign state to harm the republic's interest — the ruin it brings (the dead, the destroyed schools, hospitals and roads, the seized land), and that Yemeni law counts it among the state-security crimes and punishes it at the utmost, the articles named by topic only.
