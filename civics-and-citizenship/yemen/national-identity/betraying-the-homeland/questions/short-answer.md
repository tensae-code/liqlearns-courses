# Betraying the Homeland — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** What betrayal of the homeland is — striking at its independence and sovereignty, aiding the enemy, or dealing with a foreign state to harm the republic's interest — the ruin it brings (the dead, the destroyed schools, hospitals and roads, the seized land), and that Yemeni law counts it among the state-security crimes and punishes it at the utmost, the articles named by topic only.
