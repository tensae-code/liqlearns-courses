# Independence and Sovereignty

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Independence and Sovereignty. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Yemen's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## Sub lesson 1 — Independence

The homeland free of any foreign force, master of its land, sea and air and self-sufficient.

## Sub lesson 2 — Sovereignty

The state's decisions taken from a purely national vision, answerable to no outside direction — with the signs of both, the factors that win them, and the danger of letting them slip.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Independence — the homeland free of any foreign force, master of its land, sea and air and self-sufficient — and sovereignty — the state's decisions taken from a purely national vision, answerable to no outside direction — their signs, the factors that win them, and the danger of letting them slip. Give one real example from your own life or from the Yemen you live in, and name the lesson's key words: independence, and, sovereignty. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
