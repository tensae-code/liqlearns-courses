This lesson is about one thing.
Independence — the homeland free of any foreign force, master of its land, sea and air and self-sufficient — and sovereignty — the state's decisions taken from a purely national vision, answerable to no outside direction — their signs, the factors that win them, and the danger of letting them slip.
