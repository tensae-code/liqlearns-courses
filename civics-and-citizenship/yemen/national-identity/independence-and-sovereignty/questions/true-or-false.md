# Independence and Sovereignty — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Independence — the homeland free of any foreign force, master of its land, sea and air and self-sufficient — and sovereignty — the state's decisions taken from a purely national vision, answerable to no outside direction — their signs, the factors that win them, and the danger of letting them slip.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that Independence and Sovereignty — and that it has no connection to the rest of Yemen's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Yemen's own course, in its own order.
