# Independence and Sovereignty — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** Independence — the homeland free of any foreign force, master of its land, sea and air and self-sufficient — and sovereignty — the state's decisions taken from a purely national vision, answerable to no outside direction — their signs, the factors that win them, and the danger of letting them slip.
