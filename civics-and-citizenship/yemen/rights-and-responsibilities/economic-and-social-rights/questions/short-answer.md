# Economic and Social Rights — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** The economic rights — work, ownership, and investing and trading one's money short of usury, for men and women alike, and what their denial costs (idleness and halted building); and the social rights — social care for the needy, education guaranteed by the state, health care in its hospitals and centres, and the rights of women and children, among them the woman's ownership, inheritance, learning and work as the book presents them.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** The economic rights — work, ownership, and investing and trading one's money short of usury, for men and women alike, and what their denial costs (idleness and halted building); and the social rights — social care for the needy, education guaranteed by the state, health care in its hospitals and centres, and the rights of women and children, among them the woman's ownership, inheritance, learning and work as the book presents them.
