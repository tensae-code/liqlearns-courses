# Economic and Social Rights — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The economic rights — work, ownership, and investing and trading one's money short of usury, for men and women alike, and what their denial costs (idleness and halted building); and the social rights — social care for the needy, education guaranteed by the state, health care in its hospitals and centres, and the rights of women and children, among them the woman's ownership, inheritance, learning and work as the book presents them.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that Economic and Social Rights — and that it has no connection to the rest of Yemen's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Yemen's own course, in its own order.
