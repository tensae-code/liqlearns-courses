# Economic and Social Rights — matching

Match each part of the lesson with its teaching.

**1.** Economic and Social Rights — a)
**2.** Rights and Responsibilities (the title this lesson sits in) — b)


Meanings:
a) The economic rights — work, ownership, and investing and trading one's money short of usury, for men and women alike, and what their denial costs (idleness and halted building); and the social rights — social care for the needy, education guaranteed by the state, health care in its hospitals and centres, and the rights of women and children, among them the woman's ownership, inheritance, learning and work as the book presents them.
b) One of the titles of Yemen's own civics course, built from Yemen's own government textbooks.

**Answer key:** 1. a 2. b
