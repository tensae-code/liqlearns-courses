# Video script — Economic and Social Rights

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The economic rights — work, ownership, and investing and trading one's money short of usury, for men and women alike, and what their denial costs (idleness and halted building); and the social rights — social care for the needy, education guaranteed by the state, health care in its hospitals and centres, and the rights of women and children, among them the woman's ownership, inheritance, learning and work as the book presents them.
