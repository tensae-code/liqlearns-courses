# Rights and Responsibilities — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** A right as what family and society owe the person in services and care so he can do his duties, and a responsibility as all he must answer for in word and deed; the seven families of rights — personal, political, economic, social, cultural, educational, health — and the kinds of responsibility: religious, political, economic and social.
