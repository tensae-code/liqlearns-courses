# Rights and Responsibilities — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A right as what family and society owe the person in services and care so he can do his duties, and a responsibility as all he must answer for in word and deed; the seven families of rights — personal, political, economic, social, cultural, educational, health — and the kinds of responsibility: religious, political, economic and social.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that Rights and Responsibilities — and that it has no connection to the rest of Yemen's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Yemen's own course, in its own order.
