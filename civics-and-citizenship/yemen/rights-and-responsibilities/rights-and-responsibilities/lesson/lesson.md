# Rights and Responsibilities

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Rights and Responsibilities. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Yemen's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## Sub lesson 1 — Rights and their seven families

A right as what family and society owe the person in services and care so he can do his duties, and the seven families of rights: personal, political, economic, social, cultural, educational, health.

## Sub lesson 2 — Responsibility and its kinds

A responsibility as all he must answer for in word and deed, and its kinds: religious, political, economic and social.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: A right as what family and society owe the person in services and care so he can do his duties, and a responsibility as all he must answer for in word and deed; the seven families of rights — personal, political, economic, social, cultural, educational, health — and the kinds of responsibility: religious, political, economic and social. Give one real example from your own life or from the Yemen you live in, and name the lesson's key words: rights, and, responsibilities. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
