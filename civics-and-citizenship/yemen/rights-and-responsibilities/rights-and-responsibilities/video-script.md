# Video script — Rights and Responsibilities

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A right as what family and society owe the person in services and care so he can do his duties, and a responsibility as all he must answer for in word and deed; the seven families of rights — personal, political, economic, social, cultural, educational, health — and the kinds of responsibility: religious, political, economic and social.
