# Lesson plan — The Citizen's Responsibilities

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Yemen · **Title:** Rights and Responsibilities · **Lesson:** The Citizen's Responsibilities
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 14 — Rights and Responsibilities (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The citizen's responsibilities in four fields — religious: every person, farmer, worker, trader, official, soldier, doctor, pupil, doing his own work sincerely where he stands; political: advising those in office, voting only for the upright, standing with the nation's causes; economic: earning diligently, boycotting the enemy's goods as the book directs, saving and investing lawfully, paying what he owes; social: providing for one's family, kindness to kin and neighbours, help for the poor and the martyrs' families, guarding public facilities, paying zakat. This lesson stands on a student book.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 14 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).
