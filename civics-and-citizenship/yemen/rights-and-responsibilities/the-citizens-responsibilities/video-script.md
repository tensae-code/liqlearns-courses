# Video script — The Citizen's Responsibilities

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The citizen's responsibilities in four fields — religious: every person, farmer, worker, trader, official, soldier, doctor, pupil, doing his own work sincerely where he stands; political: advising those in office, voting only for the upright, standing with the nation's causes; economic: earning diligently, boycotting the enemy's goods as the book directs, saving and investing lawfully, paying what he owes; social: providing for one's family, kindness to kin and neighbours, help for the poor and the martyrs' families, guarding public facilities, paying zakat.
