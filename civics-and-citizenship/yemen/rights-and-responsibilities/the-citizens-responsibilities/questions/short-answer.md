# The Citizen's Responsibilities — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** The citizen's responsibilities in four fields — religious: every person, farmer, worker, trader, official, soldier, doctor, pupil, doing his own work sincerely where he stands; political: advising those in office, voting only for the upright, standing with the nation's causes; economic: earning diligently, boycotting the enemy's goods as the book directs, saving and investing lawfully, paying what he owes; social: providing for one's family, kindness to kin and neighbours, help for the poor and the martyrs' families, guarding public facilities, paying zakat.
