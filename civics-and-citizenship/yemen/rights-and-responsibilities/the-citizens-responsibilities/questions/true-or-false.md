# The Citizen's Responsibilities — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The citizen's responsibilities in four fields — religious: every person, farmer, worker, trader, official, soldier, doctor, pupil, doing his own work sincerely where he stands; political: advising those in office, voting only for the upright, standing with the nation's causes; economic: earning diligently, boycotting the enemy's goods as the book directs, saving and investing lawfully, paying what he owes; social: providing for one's family, kindness to kin and neighbours, help for the poor and the martyrs' families, guarding public facilities, paying zakat.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that The Citizen's Responsibilities — and that it has no connection to the rest of Yemen's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Yemen's own course, in its own order.
