# Video script — Personal Rights

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The personal rights and why they give the citizen a secure life: the right to security over oneself and one's property, the inviolability of the home — no entry or search except by a court's order — the right to freedom, no arrest without lawful cause, and the right to litigation, with its courtroom guarantees: equality of the parties, the accused innocent until proven guilty, and punishment personal to the offender, in summary.
