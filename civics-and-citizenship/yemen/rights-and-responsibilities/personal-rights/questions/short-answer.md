# Personal Rights — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** The personal rights and why they give the citizen a secure life: the right to security over oneself and one's property, the inviolability of the home — no entry or search except by a court's order — the right to freedom, no arrest without lawful cause, and the right to litigation, with its courtroom guarantees: equality of the parties, the accused innocent until proven guilty, and punishment personal to the offender, in summary.
