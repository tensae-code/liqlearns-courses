# Personal Rights — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The personal rights and why they give the citizen a secure life: the right to security over oneself and one's property, the inviolability of the home — no entry or search except by a court's order — the right to freedom, no arrest without lawful cause, and the right to litigation, with its courtroom guarantees: equality of the parties, the accused innocent until proven guilty, and punishment personal to the offender, in summary.
