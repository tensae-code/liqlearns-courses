# Personal Rights — depth

This lesson is ONE lesson with no sub lessons: it teaches a single thing, whole. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: The personal rights and why they give the citizen a secure life: the right to security over oneself and one's property, the inviolability of the home — no entry or search except by a court's order — the right to freedom, no arrest without lawful cause, and the right to litigation, with its courtroom guarantees: equality of the parties, the accused innocent until proven guilty, and punishment personal to the offender, in summary — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
