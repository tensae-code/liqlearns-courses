# Video script — Political and Cultural Rights

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The political rights — choosing rulers in free and honest elections, standing and voting for councils and the presidency, forming and joining associations and unions, and speaking one's opinion through the media within the homeland's constants, as the book frames them — and the cultural rights: authorship and publishing, invention and creativity, freedom of research, and the author's ownership of his book.
