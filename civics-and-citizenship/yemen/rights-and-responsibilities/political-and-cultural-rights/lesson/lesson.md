# Political and Cultural Rights

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Political and Cultural Rights. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Yemen's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## Sub lesson 1 — Political rights

Choosing rulers in free and honest elections, standing and voting for councils and the presidency, forming and joining associations and unions, and speaking one's opinion through the media within the homeland's constants, as the book frames them.

## Sub lesson 2 — Cultural rights

Authorship and publishing, invention and creativity, freedom of research, and the author's ownership of his book.

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The political rights — choosing rulers in free and honest elections, standing and voting for councils and the presidency, forming and joining associations and unions, and speaking one's opinion through the media within the homeland's constants, as the book frames them — and the cultural rights: authorship and publishing, invention and creativity, freedom of research, and the author's ownership of his book. Give one real example from your own life or from the Yemen you live in, and name the lesson's key words: political, and, cultural. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
