# Political and Cultural Rights — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** The political rights — choosing rulers in free and honest elections, standing and voting for councils and the presidency, forming and joining associations and unions, and speaking one's opinion through the media within the homeland's constants, as the book frames them — and the cultural rights: authorship and publishing, invention and creativity, freedom of research, and the author's ownership of his book.
