# Political and Cultural Rights — matching

Match each part of the lesson with its teaching.

**1.** Political and Cultural Rights — a)
**2.** Rights and Responsibilities (the title this lesson sits in) — b)


Meanings:
a) The political rights — choosing rulers in free and honest elections, standing and voting for councils and the presidency, forming and joining associations and unions, and speaking one's opinion through the media within the homeland's constants, as the book frames them — and the cultural rights: authorship and publishing, invention and creativity, freedom of research, and the author's ownership of his book.
b) One of the titles of Yemen's own civics course, built from Yemen's own government textbooks.

**Answer key:** 1. a 2. b
