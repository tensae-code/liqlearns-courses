# Political and Cultural Rights — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The political rights — choosing rulers in free and honest elections, standing and voting for councils and the presidency, forming and joining associations and unions, and speaking one's opinion through the media within the homeland's constants, as the book frames them — and the cultural rights: authorship and publishing, invention and creativity, freedom of research, and the author's ownership of his book.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that Political and Cultural Rights — and that it has no connection to the rest of Yemen's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Yemen's own course, in its own order.
