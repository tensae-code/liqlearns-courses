# Lesson plan — Shura and Democracy

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Yemen · **Title:** Powers of the State · **Lesson:** Shura and Democracy
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Powers of the State (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Shura — consultation — as an Islamic principle of government, with Queen Bilqis of Sheba as its Yemeni exemplar, and democracy as the people's rule through free ballot, and the benefits of each. This lesson stands on a student book.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

Gate pool: at most 5 questions for the prove-it check, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
