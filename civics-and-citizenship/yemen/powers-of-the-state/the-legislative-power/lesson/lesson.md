## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: The Legislative Power. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Yemen's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

The Council of Representatives and its competences: passing laws, approving the government's programme and the budget, watching the government.
