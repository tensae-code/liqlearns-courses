# The Legislative Power — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** The Council of Representatives and its competences — passing laws, approving the government's programme and the budget, watching the government — and the citizen's electoral rights and duties: voting at eighteen and standing at twenty-five, as the book states.
