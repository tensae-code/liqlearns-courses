# The Judicial Power

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: The Judicial Power. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Yemen's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

The courts and their work of justice.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The courts and their work of justice. Give one real example from your own life or from the Yemen you live in, and name the lesson's key words: the, judicial, power. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
