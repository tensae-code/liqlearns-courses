# The Media — depth

This lesson is ONE lesson with no sub lessons: it teaches a single thing, whole. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: The media — satellite channels, the internet, radio and press — and their power to inform and educate; their good side (news, learning, awareness, skills) and their dangers as the book presents them (habits and values against the nation's identity, dulled creativity, isolation, violence, wasted time, a consumer culture carried to the developing world); and protection — holding to one's faith and values, and filling time with reading, languages, sport and family — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
