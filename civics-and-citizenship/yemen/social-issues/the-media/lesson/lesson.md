# The Media

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: The Media. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Yemen's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

The media — satellite channels, the internet, radio and press — and their power to inform and educate; their good side (news, learning, awareness, skills) and their dangers as the book presents them (habits and values against the nation's identity, dulled creativity, isolation, violence, wasted time, a consumer culture carried to the developing world); and protection — holding to one's faith and values, and filling time with reading, languages, sport and family.
