# The Media — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** The media — satellite channels, the internet, radio and press — and their power to inform and educate; their good side (news, learning, awareness, skills) and their dangers as the book presents them (habits and values against the nation's identity, dulled creativity, isolation, violence, wasted time, a consumer culture carried to the developing world); and protection — holding to one's faith and values, and filling time with reading, languages, sport and family.
