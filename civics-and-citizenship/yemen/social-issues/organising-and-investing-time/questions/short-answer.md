# Organising and Investing Time — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** Time as one of man's greatest resources, for which he will be asked; the importance of organising it; the time-wasters, outer (other people) and inner (oneself); and investing time by a daily plan — time set for each task, priorities first, appointments kept, and distance from the wasters.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
