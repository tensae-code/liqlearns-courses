# Video script — Organising and Investing Time

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Time as one of man's greatest resources, for which he will be asked; the importance of organising it; the time-wasters, outer (other people) and inner (oneself); and investing time by a daily plan — time set for each task, priorities first, appointments kept, and distance from the wasters.
