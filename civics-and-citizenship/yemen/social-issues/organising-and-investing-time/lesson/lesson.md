# Organising and Investing Time

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Organising and Investing Time. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Yemen's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Time as one of man's greatest resources, for which he will be asked; the importance of organising it; the time-wasters, outer (other people) and inner (oneself); and investing time by a daily plan — time set for each task, priorities first, appointments kept, and distance from the wasters.
