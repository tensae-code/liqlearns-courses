# Video script — Summer Centres and Youth

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The summer centres — state or community centres that fill pupils' and youths' summer holiday, the best investment of free time — their aims (a balanced personality, stronger belonging, talents discovered, responsibility learned) and their activities, sport, swimming, computing, languages, arts, first aid and Qur'an study among them; and the state's care for youth, from memorisation centres and sports clubs to women's education centres.
