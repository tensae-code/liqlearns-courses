# Summer Centres and Youth — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** The summer centres — state or community centres that fill pupils' and youths' summer holiday, the best investment of free time — their aims (a balanced personality, stronger belonging, talents discovered, responsibility learned) and their activities, sport, swimming, computing, languages, arts, first aid and Qur'an study among them; and the state's care for youth, from memorisation centres and sports clubs to women's education centres.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
