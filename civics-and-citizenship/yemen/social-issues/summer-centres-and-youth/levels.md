# Summer Centres and Youth — depth

This lesson is ONE lesson with no sub lessons: it teaches a single thing, whole. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: The summer centres — state or community centres that fill pupils' and youths' summer holiday, the best investment of free time — their aims (a balanced personality, stronger belonging, talents discovered, responsibility learned) and their activities, sport, swimming, computing, languages, arts, first aid and Qur'an study among them; and the state's care for youth, from memorisation centres and sports clubs to women's education centres — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
