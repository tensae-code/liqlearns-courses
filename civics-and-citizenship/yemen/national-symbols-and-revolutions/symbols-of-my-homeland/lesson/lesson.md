# Symbols of My Homeland

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Symbols of My Homeland. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Yemen's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## Sub lesson 1 — The flag and the anthem

The national flag and the meaning of its colours, and the national anthem sung at the morning line.

## Sub lesson 2 — The emblem of the republic

The republic's emblem of the eagle — the third symbol of the homeland.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The symbols of the homeland: the national flag and the meaning of its colours, the national anthem sung at the morning line, and the republic's emblem of the eagle. Give one real example from your own life or from the Yemen you live in, and name the lesson's key words: symbols, of, my. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: The symbols of the homeland: the national flag and the meaning of its colours, the national anthem sung at the morning line, and the republic's emblem of the eagle.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
