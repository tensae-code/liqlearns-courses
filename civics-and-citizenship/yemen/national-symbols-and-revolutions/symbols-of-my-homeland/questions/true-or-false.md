# Symbols of My Homeland — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The symbols of the homeland: the national flag and the meaning of its colours, the national anthem sung at the morning line, and the republic's emblem of the eagle.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that Symbols of My Homeland — and that it has no connection to the rest of Yemen's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Yemen's own course, in its own order.
