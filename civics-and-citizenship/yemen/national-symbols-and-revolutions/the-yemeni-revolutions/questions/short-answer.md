# The Yemeni Revolutions — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** What a revolution is, and the Yemeni revolutions from al-Qasim's rising against the first Ottoman occupation to 26 September 1962, 14 October 1963 and the later risings the book lists — their causes and their aims: liberation, a just republican order, unity.
