# The Yemeni Revolutions — depth

This lesson is ONE lesson with no sub lessons: it teaches a single thing, whole. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: What a revolution is, and the Yemeni revolutions from al-Qasim's rising against the first Ottoman occupation to 26 September 1962, 14 October 1963 and the later risings the book lists — their causes and their aims: liberation, a just republican order, unity — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
