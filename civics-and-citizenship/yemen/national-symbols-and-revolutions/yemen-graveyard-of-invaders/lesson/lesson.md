# Yemen, Graveyard of Invaders

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Yemen, Graveyard of Invaders. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Yemen's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Why Yemen is called the invaders' graveyard — the Ottoman defeat after the battle of Shahara and every failed occupation since, as the book tells it.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Why Yemen is called the invaders' graveyard — the Ottoman defeat after the battle of Shahara and every failed occupation since, as the book tells it. Give one real example from your own life or from the Yemen you live in, and name the lesson's key words: yemen, graveyard, of. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
