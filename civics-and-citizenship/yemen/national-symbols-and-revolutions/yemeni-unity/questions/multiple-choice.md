# Yemeni Unity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The unity of Yemen as the homeland's great achievement.  b) it has nothing to do with how Yemen governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The unity of Yemen as the homeland's great achievement.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) The unity of Yemen as the homeland's great achievement.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Yemen
**Answer: a) The unity of Yemen as the homeland's great achievement.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
