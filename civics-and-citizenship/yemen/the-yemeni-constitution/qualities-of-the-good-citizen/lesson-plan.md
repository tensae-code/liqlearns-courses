# Lesson plan — Qualities of the Good Citizen

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Yemen · **Title:** The Yemeni Constitution · **Lesson:** Qualities of the Good Citizen
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 7 — The Yemeni Constitution (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Love of the homeland as belonging and pride in its history and civilisation made deed, not slogans — the teacher teaching sincerely, the soldier guarding, the official serving, the doctor healing, the minister and the president serving the people's interests, the cleaner proud of his work, the mother raising her children and working, the trader and factory owner without cheating, exploitation or monopoly, and the pupil studying sincerely; who the good citizen is — every Yemeni living on the republic's land or outside it, holding its nationality, enjoying the rights the constitution guarantees, performing his duties to the homeland, keeping its laws and ready to defend it in any place; and two of his marks — guarding public property (the installations and funds owned by all the homeland's sons — schools, universities, hospitals, health centres, public parks, roads, mosques, ministries, sports stadiums and police stations — using them in a civilised way, protecting them from tampering and never staying silent at an attack on them) and constructive criticism, the civilised behaviour of alerting the responsible bodies, institutions and persons to violations touching the homeland's and the citizen's interest, by press articles, interviews, letters, complaints and social media, without leaving the bounds of the homeland's and the citizen's interest. This lesson stands on a student book.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 7 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Love of the homeland, and who the good citizen is** — Belonging made deed, not slogan — every worker, from teacher to pupil, doing his work sincerely; and the good citizen defined: enjoying the constitution's rights, performing his duties, keeping the laws and ready to defend his homeland anywhere.
2. **Guarding public property, and constructive criticism** — The installations and funds owned by all — schools, hospitals, parks, roads, mosques — used in a civilised way and defended from tampering; and criticism that alerts the responsible bodies to violations, by press, letters, complaints and social media, within the bounds of good manners.

Gate pool: at most 5 questions for the prove-it check, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
