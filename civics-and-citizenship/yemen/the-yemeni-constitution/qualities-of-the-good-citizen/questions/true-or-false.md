# Qualities of the Good Citizen — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Love of the homeland as belonging and pride in its history and civilisation made deed, not slogans — the teacher teaching sincerely, the soldier guarding, the official serving, the doctor healing, the minister and the president serving the people's interests, the cleaner proud of his work, the mother raising her children and working, the trader and factory owner without cheating, exploitation or monopoly, and the pupil studying sincerely; who the good citizen is — every Yemeni living on the republic's land or outside it, holding its nationality, enjoying the rights the constitution guarantees, performing his duties to the homeland, keeping its laws and ready to defend it in any place; and two of his marks — guarding public property (the installations and funds owned by all the homeland's sons — schools, universities, hospitals, health centres, public parks, roads, mosques, ministries, sports stadiums and police stations — using them in a civilised way, protecting them from tampering and never staying silent at an attack on them) and constructive criticism, the civilised behaviour of alerting the responsible bodies, institutions and persons to violations touching the homeland's and the citizen's interest, by press articles, interviews, letters, complaints and social media, without leaving the bounds of the homeland's and the citizen's interest.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that Qualities of the Good Citizen — and that it has no connection to the rest of Yemen's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Yemen's own course, in its own order.
