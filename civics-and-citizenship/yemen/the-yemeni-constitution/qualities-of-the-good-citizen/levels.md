# Qualities of the Good Citizen — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Love of the homeland, and who the good citizen is
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Belonging made deed, not slogan — every worker, from teacher to pupil, doing his work sincerely; and the good citizen defined: enjoying the constitution's rights, performing his duties, keeping the laws and ready to defend his homeland anywhere. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — Guarding public property, and constructive criticism
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The installations and funds owned by all — schools, hospitals, parks, roads, mosques — used in a civilised way and defended from tampering; and criticism that alerts the responsible bodies to violations, by press, letters, complaints and social media, within the bounds of good manners. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
