Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Defence of religion and homeland and military service; paying the general taxes; keeping national unity and the state's secrets; and respecting the laws and abiding by their rulings. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
