# Lesson plan — Foundations of the Yemeni State in the Yemeni Constitution

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Yemen · **Title:** The Yemeni Constitution · **Lesson:** Foundations of the Yemeni State in the Yemeni Constitution
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 7 — The Yemeni Constitution (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** What a constitution is — the basic document that shows the form of the state's rule, fixes the competences of the legislative, judicial and executive powers, orders the relations between them and states the citizen's rights and duties — and what a law is, the rules issued to organise a matter, flowing from the constitution as the elections law flows from the constitutional right to vote, stand and vote in referendums; and the republic's constitutional foundations — political (the Republic of Yemen an Arab, Islamic, sovereign state; Islam the state's religion and Arabic its official language; the Islamic Sharia the source of all legislation; the political system built on pluralism; the peaceful transfer of power), economic (freedom of economic activity for the good of individual and society, freedom of private property, and the natural wealth — oil, gas, gold mines and the other minerals — owned by the state and used for the public good), social and cultural (equal opportunity for all citizens without discrimination in rights and duties, the family as society's foundation on religion and morals, society built on justice and freedom, women as men's full partners with the rights of their duties, and the encouragement of scientific and artistic invention and creativity), and of national defence (the state alone creates the armed forces, police and general security, and no individual or group may raise forces of its own; the armed forces belong to the people and their task is guarding the republic from outside attack and attempts at invasion and colonisation; the police a disciplined body serving the people and keeping internal security, public order and public morals; and the armed forces, police and security may never be harnessed for an individual, group or party) — the articles named by topic and summarised, never transcribed. This lesson stands on a student book.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 7 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **What a constitution is, and what a law is** — The constitution as the basic document fixing the powers' competences, their relations and the citizen's rights and duties; and the law as the rules flowing from it, as the elections law flows from the right to vote and stand.
2. **The constitution's foundations** — Political, economic, social and cultural, and of national defence — a sovereign Arab Islamic republic under the Sharia, pluralism and peaceful transfer of power; free activity, private property and state-owned natural wealth; equal opportunity, the family as society's base and women as men's partners; and the state alone raising forces that belong to the people.

Gate pool: at most 5 questions for the prove-it check, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
