# Social Upbringing — depth

This lesson is ONE lesson with no sub lessons: it teaches a single thing, whole. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: What social upbringing is and why it matters — training the person in independence and self-reliance and giving him his society's language, manners, values and customs — and the institutions that do it: family, school, companions and media among them — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
