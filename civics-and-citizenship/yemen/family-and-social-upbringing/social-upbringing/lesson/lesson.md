# Social Upbringing

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Social Upbringing. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Yemen's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

What social upbringing is and why it matters — training the person in independence and self-reliance and giving him his society's language, manners, values and customs — and the institutions that do it: family, school, companions and media among them.
