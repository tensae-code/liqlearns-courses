# Social Upbringing — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** What social upbringing is and why it matters — training the person in independence and self-reliance and giving him his society's language, manners, values and customs — and the institutions that do it: family, school, companions and media among them.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that Social Upbringing — and that it has no connection to the rest of Yemen's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Yemen's own course, in its own order.
