# Methods of Social Upbringing

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Methods of Social Upbringing. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Yemen's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Family harmony and the methods parents use — gentleness and equality against verbal and physical violence and over-indulgence — and the school's part in a child's upbringing.
