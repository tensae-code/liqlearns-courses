# The Tribe and Social Peace — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** The tribe as peacemaker — Yemeni society as one of the Arab and Islamic world's great conservative models, where tribal urf still does the largest work of settling the problems between individuals and groups: blood-feuds inside the tribe and outside it, disputes over agricultural and housing land, family problems and social problems; and the Yemeni tribe's marks — it takes the initiative in settling problems, guards the foundations of social peace with great care, answers to protect the homeland's higher interests, lightens the suffering of some families, and closes ranks and stands together against fate's blows and dangers.
