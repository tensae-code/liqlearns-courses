# The Tribe and Social Peace — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The tribe as peacemaker — Yemeni society as one of the Arab and Islamic world's great conservative models, where tribal urf still does the largest work of settling the problems between individuals and groups: blood-feuds inside the tribe and outside it, disputes over agricultural and housing land, family problems and social problems; and the Yemeni tribe's marks — it takes the initiative in settling problems, guards the foundations of social peace with great care, answers to protect the homeland's higher interests, lightens the suffering of some families, and closes ranks and stands together against fate's blows and dangers.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that The Tribe and Social Peace — and that it has no connection to the rest of Yemen's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Yemen's own course, in its own order.
