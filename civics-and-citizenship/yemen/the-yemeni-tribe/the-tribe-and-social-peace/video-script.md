# Video script — The Tribe and Social Peace

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The tribe as peacemaker — Yemeni society as one of the Arab and Islamic world's great conservative models, where tribal urf still does the largest work of settling the problems between individuals and groups: blood-feuds inside the tribe and outside it, disputes over agricultural and housing land, family problems and social problems; and the Yemeni tribe's marks — it takes the initiative in settling problems, guards the foundations of social peace with great care, answers to protect the homeland's higher interests, lightens the suffering of some families, and closes ranks and stands together against fate's blows and dangers.
