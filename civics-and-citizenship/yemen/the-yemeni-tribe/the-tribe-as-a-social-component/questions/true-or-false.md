# The Tribe as a Social Component — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** What a tribe is — a group of people bound by ties of blood and descent, mostly living in one stretch of land, with a social body that helps solve their problems and binds them in mutual support, solidarity and cooperation — the second component of society after the family, the basic building block of Yemeni society, whose members hold to its habits, traditions and urf; why tribes arose — God's will that mankind be made peoples and tribes to know one another, and man's nature as a social being who prefers life in a group, for security and for meeting his needs; and the Yemeni tribesman's praised traits — chivalry, manliness, daring, generosity, courage, loyalty, honesty, and pardoning when able.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that The Tribe as a Social Component — and that it has no connection to the rest of Yemen's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Yemen's own course, in its own order.
