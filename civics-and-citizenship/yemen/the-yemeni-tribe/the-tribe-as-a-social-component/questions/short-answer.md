# The Tribe as a Social Component — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** What a tribe is — a group of people bound by ties of blood and descent, mostly living in one stretch of land, with a social body that helps solve their problems and binds them in mutual support, solidarity and cooperation — the second component of society after the family, the basic building block of Yemeni society, whose members hold to its habits, traditions and urf; why tribes arose — God's will that mankind be made peoples and tribes to know one another, and man's nature as a social being who prefers life in a group, for security and for meeting his needs; and the Yemeni tribesman's praised traits — chivalry, manliness, daring, generosity, courage, loyalty, honesty, and pardoning when able.
