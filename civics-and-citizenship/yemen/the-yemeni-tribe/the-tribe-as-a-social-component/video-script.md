# Video script — The Tribe as a Social Component

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
What a tribe is — a group of people bound by ties of blood and descent, mostly living in one stretch of land, with a social body that helps solve their problems and binds them in mutual support, solidarity and cooperation — the second component of society after the family, the basic building block of Yemeni society, whose members hold to its habits, traditions and urf; why tribes arose — God's will that mankind be made peoples and tribes to know one another, and man's nature as a social being who prefers life in a group, for security and for meeting his needs; and the Yemeni tribesman's praised traits — chivalry, manliness, daring, generosity, courage, loyalty, honesty, and pardoning when able.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
