# The Tribe as a Social Component

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: The Tribe as a Social Component. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Yemen's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## Sub lesson 1 — What a tribe is

A group of people bound by ties of blood and descent, mostly in one stretch of land, whose social body solves their problems and binds them in mutual support — the second component of society after the family and Yemeni society's basic building block.

## Sub lesson 2 — Why tribes arose, and the tribesman's traits

God's will that mankind be peoples and tribes who know one another, and man's social nature, needing the group for security and his needs; and the praised traits — chivalry, manliness, daring, generosity, courage, loyalty, honesty, and pardoning when able.
