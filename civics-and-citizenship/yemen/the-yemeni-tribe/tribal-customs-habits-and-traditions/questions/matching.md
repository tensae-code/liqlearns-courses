# The Tribe's Customs, Its Habits and Traditions — matching

Match each part of the lesson with its teaching.

**1.** The Tribe's Customs, Its Habits and Traditions — a)
**2.** The Yemeni Tribe (the title this lesson sits in) — b)


Meanings:
a) The tribe's urf — what its people have agreed among themselves for settling the questions that face them and serving the tribe's and its members' interests, counted a part of divine and statutory law, some of it general to all tribes and some each tribe's own — above all al-ghurm, the whole tribe sharing the loss of any member struck by one of fate's calamities, and al-nakf, the rallying call of one tribe, or of all tribes when the homeland is attacked, to meet and take a single stand; and the habits and traditions — al-hajr, the bull or goat an aggressor must present after an attack or quarrel, its cost set by the size of the wrong as the sheikh or the tribe's wise men rule; hospitality, honouring the guest and receiving him well; the white flag hung in a high place for a glorious deed that deserves praise, and the black flag, its opposite, for a shameful one that deserves blame and punishment.
b) One of the titles of Yemen's own civics course, built from Yemen's own government textbooks.

**Answer key:** 1. a 2. b
