# Video script — The Tribe's Customs, Its Habits and Traditions

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The tribe's urf — what its people have agreed among themselves for settling the questions that face them and serving the tribe's and its members' interests, counted a part of divine and statutory law, some of it general to all tribes and some each tribe's own — above all al-ghurm, the whole tribe sharing the loss of any member struck by one of fate's calamities, and al-nakf, the rallying call of one tribe, or of all tribes when the homeland is attacked, to meet and take a single stand; and the habits and traditions — al-hajr, the bull or goat an aggressor must present after an attack or quarrel, its cost set by the size of the wrong as the sheikh or the tribe's wise men rule; hospitality, honouring the guest and receiving him well; the white flag hung in a high place for a glorious deed that deserves praise, and the black flag, its opposite, for a shameful one that deserves blame and punishment.
