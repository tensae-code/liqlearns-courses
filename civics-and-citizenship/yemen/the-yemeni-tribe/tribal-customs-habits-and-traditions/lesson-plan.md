# Lesson plan — The Tribe's Customs, Its Habits and Traditions

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Yemen · **Title:** The Yemeni Tribe · **Lesson:** The Tribe's Customs, Its Habits and Traditions
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 6 — The Yemeni Tribe (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The tribe's urf — what its people have agreed among themselves for settling the questions that face them and serving the tribe's and its members' interests, counted a part of divine and statutory law, some of it general to all tribes and some each tribe's own — above all al-ghurm, the whole tribe sharing the loss of any member struck by one of fate's calamities, and al-nakf, the rallying call of one tribe, or of all tribes when the homeland is attacked, to meet and take a single stand; and the habits and traditions — al-hajr, the bull or goat an aggressor must present after an attack or quarrel, its cost set by the size of the wrong as the sheikh or the tribe's wise men rule; hospitality, honouring the guest and receiving him well; the white flag hung in a high place for a glorious deed that deserves praise, and the black flag, its opposite, for a shameful one that deserves blame and punishment. This lesson stands on a student book.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 6 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The urf: al-ghurm and al-nakf** — What the tribe's people have agreed for settling their questions, counted part of divine and statutory law — al-ghurm, the whole tribe sharing a stricken member's loss, and al-nakf, the rallying call to a single stand when the tribe or the homeland is attacked.
2. **Habits and traditions: al-hajr, hospitality, and the two flags** — Al-hajr, the bull or goat an aggressor must present, priced by the wrong as the sheikh rules; hospitality to the guest; the white flag for a glorious deed and the black flag for a shameful one.

Gate pool: at most 5 questions for the prove-it check, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
