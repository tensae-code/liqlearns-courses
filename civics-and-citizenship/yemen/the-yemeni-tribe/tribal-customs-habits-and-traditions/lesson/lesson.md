# The Tribe's Customs, Its Habits and Traditions

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: The Tribe's Customs, Its Habits and Traditions. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Yemen's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## Sub lesson 1 — The urf: al-ghurm and al-nakf

What the tribe's people have agreed for settling their questions, counted part of divine and statutory law — al-ghurm, the whole tribe sharing a stricken member's loss, and al-nakf, the rallying call to a single stand when the tribe or the homeland is attacked.

## Sub lesson 2 — Habits and traditions: al-hajr, hospitality, and the two flags

Al-hajr, the bull or goat an aggressor must present, priced by the wrong as the sheikh rules; hospitality to the guest; the white flag for a glorious deed and the black flag for a shameful one.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The tribe's urf — what its people have agreed among themselves for settling the questions that face them and serving the tribe's and its members' interests, counted a part of divine and statutory law, some of it general to all tribes and some each tribe's own — above all al-ghurm, the whole tribe sharing the loss of any member struck by one of fate's calamities, and al-nakf, the rallying call of one tribe, or of all tribes when the homeland is attacked, to meet and take a single stand; and the habits and traditions — al-hajr, the bull or goat an aggressor must present after an attack or quarrel, its cost set by the size of the wrong as the sheikh or the tribe's wise men rule; hospitality, honouring the guest and receiving him well; the white flag hung in a high place for a glorious deed that deserves praise, and the black flag, its opposite, for a shameful one that deserves blame and punishment. Give one real example from your own life or from the Yemen you live in, and name the lesson's key words: the, tribe's, customs,. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
