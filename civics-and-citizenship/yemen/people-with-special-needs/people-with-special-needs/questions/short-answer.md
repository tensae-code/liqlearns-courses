# People with Special Needs — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** Who people with special needs are — those who differ from the average in mental, physical, sensory, language or learning ability, the gifted among them — the kinds of disability, and guarding against it by care in pregnancy, vaccination and road safety.
