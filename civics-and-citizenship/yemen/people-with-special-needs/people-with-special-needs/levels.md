# People with Special Needs — depth

This lesson is ONE lesson with no sub lessons: it teaches a single thing, whole. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: Who people with special needs are — those who differ from the average in mental, physical, sensory, language or learning ability, the gifted among them — the kinds of disability, and guarding against it by care in pregnancy, vaccination and road safety — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
