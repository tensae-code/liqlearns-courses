# People with Special Needs

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: People with Special Needs. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Yemen's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Who people with special needs are — those who differ from the average in mental, physical, sensory, language or learning ability, the gifted among them — the kinds of disability, and guarding against it by care in pregnancy, vaccination and road safety.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Who people with special needs are — those who differ from the average in mental, physical, sensory, language or learning ability, the gifted among them — the kinds of disability, and guarding against it by care in pregnancy, vaccination and road safety. Give one real example from your own life or from the Yemen you live in, and name the lesson's key words: people, with, special. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
