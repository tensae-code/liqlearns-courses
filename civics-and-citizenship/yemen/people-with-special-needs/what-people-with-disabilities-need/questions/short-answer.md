# What People with Disabilities Need — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** What people with disabilities need — for the mobility-disabled, lifts and homes, schools, public buildings and transport fitted to their movement — and the forms of solidarity with them.
