# What People with Disabilities Need — depth

This lesson is ONE lesson with no sub lessons: it teaches a single thing, whole. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: What people with disabilities need — for the mobility-disabled, lifts and homes, schools, public buildings and transport fitted to their movement — and the forms of solidarity with them — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
