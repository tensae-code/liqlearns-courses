# Video script — Civil Defence

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The civil defence as the body charged with protecting people and property from fires, disasters and wars — relieving the stricken, training volunteers, fighting fires, evacuation, searching for the trapped, checking safety conditions and guiding citizens; why fires start; keeping the home safe from fire; using an extinguisher; and first aid for a burn — cut off the air, cool with water, do not strip the burned clothes, no creams or powders, and get the casualty to hospital.
