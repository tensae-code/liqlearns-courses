# Civil Defence — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** The civil defence as the body charged with protecting people and property from fires, disasters and wars — relieving the stricken, training volunteers, fighting fires, evacuation, searching for the trapped, checking safety conditions and guiding citizens; why fires start; keeping the home safe from fire; using an extinguisher; and first aid for a burn — cut off the air, cool with water, do not strip the burned clothes, no creams or powders, and get the casualty to hospital.
