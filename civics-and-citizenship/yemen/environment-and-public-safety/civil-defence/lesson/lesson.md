# Civil Defence

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Civil Defence. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Yemen's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## Sub lesson 1 — The civil defence and its work

The body charged with protecting people and property from fires, disasters and wars: relieving the stricken, training volunteers, fighting fires, evacuation, searching for the trapped, checking safety conditions and guiding citizens.

## Sub lesson 2 — Fire safety and first aid for a burn

Why fires start, keeping the home safe from fire, using an extinguisher, and first aid for a burn: cut off the air, cool with water, do not strip the burned clothes, no creams or powders, and get the casualty to hospital.
