# Environmental Health — depth

This lesson is ONE lesson with no sub lessons: it teaches a single thing, whole. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: Environmental health as the soundness and safety of the surroundings one lives in — home, quarter, school, workplace — free of pollutants; man himself as the first spoiler of his environment; and the means of keeping it healthy, from clean water tanks, covered rubbish and aired rooms at home to designated dumps, safe food and protected water sources in town and country, and beautifying both with trees, gardens and clean streets — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
