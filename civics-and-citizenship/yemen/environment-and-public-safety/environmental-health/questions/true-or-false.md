# Environmental Health — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Environmental health as the soundness and safety of the surroundings one lives in — home, quarter, school, workplace — free of pollutants; man himself as the first spoiler of his environment; and the means of keeping it healthy, from clean water tanks, covered rubbish and aired rooms at home to designated dumps, safe food and protected water sources in town and country, and beautifying both with trees, gardens and clean streets.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that Environmental Health — and that it has no connection to the rest of Yemen's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Yemen's own course, in its own order.
