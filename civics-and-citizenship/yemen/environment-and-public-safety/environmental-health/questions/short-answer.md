# Environmental Health — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** Environmental health as the soundness and safety of the surroundings one lives in — home, quarter, school, workplace — free of pollutants; man himself as the first spoiler of his environment; and the means of keeping it healthy, from clean water tanks, covered rubbish and aired rooms at home to designated dumps, safe food and protected water sources in town and country, and beautifying both with trees, gardens and clean streets.
