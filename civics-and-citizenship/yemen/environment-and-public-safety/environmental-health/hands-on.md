# Hands-on — Environmental Health

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Environmental Health. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Yemen's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Environmental health as the soundness and safety of the surroundings one lives in — home, quarter, school, workplace — free of pollutants; man himself as the first spoiler of his environment; and the means of keeping it healthy, from clean water tanks, covered rubbish and aired rooms at home to designated dumps, safe food and protected water sources in town and country, and beautifying both with trees, gardens and clean streets. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
