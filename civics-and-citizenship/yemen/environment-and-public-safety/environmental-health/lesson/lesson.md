# Environmental Health

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Environmental Health. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Yemen's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Environmental health as the soundness and safety of the surroundings one lives in — home, quarter, school, workplace — free of pollutants; man himself as the first spoiler of his environment; and the means of keeping it healthy, from clean water tanks, covered rubbish and aired rooms at home to designated dumps, safe food and protected water sources in town and country, and beautifying both with trees, gardens and clean streets.
