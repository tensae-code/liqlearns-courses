# Islam and the Environment — depth

This lesson is ONE lesson with no sub lessons: it teaches a single thing, whole. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: Islam's standing rules of cleanliness as protection against disease — the personal directives (body, hands, head, mouth, clothes) and the environmental ones: preserve the earth, keep homes, mosques and roads clean, remove harm from the road, plant green spaces, and use water sparingly — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
