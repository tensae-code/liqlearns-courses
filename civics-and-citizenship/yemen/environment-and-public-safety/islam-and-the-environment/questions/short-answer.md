# Islam and the Environment — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** Islam's standing rules of cleanliness as protection against disease — the personal directives (body, hands, head, mouth, clothes) and the environmental ones: preserve the earth, keep homes, mosques and roads clean, remove harm from the road, plant green spaces, and use water sparingly.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
