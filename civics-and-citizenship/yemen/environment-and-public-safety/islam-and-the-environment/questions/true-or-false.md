# Islam and the Environment — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Islam's standing rules of cleanliness as protection against disease — the personal directives (body, hands, head, mouth, clothes) and the environmental ones: preserve the earth, keep homes, mosques and roads clean, remove harm from the road, plant green spaces, and use water sparingly.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that Islam and the Environment — and that it has no connection to the rest of Yemen's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Yemen's own course, in its own order.
