# Islam and the Environment

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Islam and the Environment. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Yemen's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Islam's standing rules of cleanliness as protection against disease — the personal directives (body, hands, head, mouth, clothes) and the environmental ones: preserve the earth, keep homes, mosques and roads clean, remove harm from the road, plant green spaces, and use water sparingly.

Give one real example from your own life or from the Yemen you live in, and name the lesson's key words: islam, and, the. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
