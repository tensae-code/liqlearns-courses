# Video script — Security and Public Safety

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Security as preventing the emergencies that can strike persons, society or property, and public safety as a better life in ordinary conditions; the safety rules of home (gas and electricity shut off, matches, medicines and sharp tools away from children, a first-aid box, extinguishers), of the car (maintenance, care in fog and rain, seat belts, children in the back), of school (no crowding, no harmful play) and of the road, with the traffic signs pictured.
