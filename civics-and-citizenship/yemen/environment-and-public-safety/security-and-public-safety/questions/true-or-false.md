# Security and Public Safety — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Security as preventing the emergencies that can strike persons, society or property, and public safety as a better life in ordinary conditions; the safety rules of home (gas and electricity shut off, matches, medicines and sharp tools away from children, a first-aid box, extinguishers), of the car (maintenance, care in fog and rain, seat belts, children in the back), of school (no crowding, no harmful play) and of the road, with the traffic signs pictured.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that Security and Public Safety — and that it has no connection to the rest of Yemen's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Yemen's own course, in its own order.
