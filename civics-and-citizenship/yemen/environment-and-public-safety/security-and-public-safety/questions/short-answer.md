# Security and Public Safety — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** Security as preventing the emergencies that can strike persons, society or property, and public safety as a better life in ordinary conditions; the safety rules of home (gas and electricity shut off, matches, medicines and sharp tools away from children, a first-aid box, extinguishers), of the car (maintenance, care in fog and rain, seat belts, children in the back), of school (no crowding, no harmful play) and of the road, with the traffic signs pictured.
