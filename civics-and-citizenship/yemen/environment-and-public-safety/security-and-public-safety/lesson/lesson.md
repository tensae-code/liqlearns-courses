# Security and Public Safety

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Security and Public Safety. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Yemen's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## Sub lesson 1 — Safety at home, in the car and at school

The safety rules of home (gas and electricity shut off, matches, medicines and sharp tools away from children, a first-aid box, extinguishers), of the car (maintenance, care in fog and rain, seat belts, children in the back) and of school (no crowding, no harmful play).

## Sub lesson 2 — Safety on the road

The safety rules of the road, with the traffic signs pictured — security as preventing emergencies, public safety as a better life in ordinary conditions.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Security as preventing the emergencies that can strike persons, society or property, and public safety as a better life in ordinary conditions; the safety rules of home (gas and electricity shut off, matches, medicines and sharp tools away from children, a first-aid box, extinguishers), of the car (maintenance, care in fog and rain, seat belts, children in the back), of school (no crowding, no harmful play) and of the road, with the traffic signs pictured. Give one real example from your own life or from the Yemen you live in, and name the lesson's key words: security, and, public. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
