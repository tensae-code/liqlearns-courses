# Hands-on — Security and Public Safety

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Security and Public Safety. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Yemen's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Security as preventing the emergencies that can strike persons, society or property, and public safety as a better life in ordinary conditions; the safety rules of home (gas and electricity shut off, matches, medicines and sharp tools away from children, a first-aid box, extinguishers), of the car (maintenance, care in fog and rain, seat belts, children in the back), of school (no crowding, no harmful play) and of the road, with the traffic signs pictured. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
