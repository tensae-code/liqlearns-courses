# Lesson plan — Arab-Islamic Political and Military Integration

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Yemen · **Title:** Arab-Islamic Integration · **Lesson:** Arab-Islamic Political and Military Integration
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 19 — Arab-Islamic Integration (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Political integration — the Islamic states' cooperation and coordination in taking the unified political stands that serve the Islamic nation's interests and its fateful causes, Palestine among them, the Muslims' strength lying in their unity and integration and their weakness in their fragmentation and abandoning their faith identity; and military integration — the Islamic states' cooperation in facing the outside dangers that threaten them through forming joint Islamic defence forces, of the utmost importance because the great Arab and Islamic homeland commands a distinguished geographic place among the world's continents, overlooking the most important water straits — Bab al-Mandab, the Suez Canal, the Strait of Hormuz and the Strait of Gibraltar, and the Red and Mediterranean seas and the Arab Gulf — which made it the object of outside states' coveting for control of those straits; and the means of achieving military integration — beginning by planting a military jihad creed embodying the faith identity and strengthening belonging to the principles of right, justice, freedom and dignity, setting one military policy between the Islamic states, caring for the joint qualification and training of the armed forces' members, forming joint military units charged with defending the nation from the dangers, and founding a shared, developed military industry. This lesson stands on a student book.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 19 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. ## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Political integration** — The Islamic states coordinating unified political stands for the nation's fateful causes, Palestine at their head — their strength in unity, their weakness in fragmentation.
2. **Military integration and its means** — Joint Islamic defence forces against outside dangers, urgent for a homeland commanding Bab al-Mandab, Suez, Hormuz and Gibraltar — reached by a shared military creed and policy, joint training, joint units and a shared military industry.

Gate pool: at most 5 questions for the prove-it check, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
