# Arab-Islamic Political and Military Integration — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** Political integration — the Islamic states' cooperation and coordination in taking the unified political stands that serve the Islamic nation's interests and its fateful causes, Palestine among them, the Muslims' strength lying in their unity and integration and their weakness in their fragmentation and abandoning their faith identity; and military integration — the Islamic states' cooperation in facing the outside dangers that threaten them through forming joint Islamic defence forces, of the utmost importance because the great Arab and Islamic homeland commands a distinguished geographic place among the world's continents, overlooking the most important water straits — Bab al-Mandab, the Suez Canal, the Strait of Hormuz and the Strait of Gibraltar, and the Red and Mediterranean seas and the Arab Gulf — which made it the object of outside states' coveting for control of those straits; and the means of achieving military integration — beginning by planting a military jihad creed embodying the faith identity and strengthening belonging to the principles of right, justice, freedom and dignity, setting one military policy between the Islamic states, caring for the joint qualification and training of the armed forces' members, forming joint military units charged with defending the nation from the dangers, and founding a shared, developed military industry.
