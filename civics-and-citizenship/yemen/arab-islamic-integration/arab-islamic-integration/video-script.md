# Video script — Arab-Islamic Integration

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
What Arab-Islamic integration means — the cooperation of the Arab and Islamic homelands' states among themselves, each state contributing what it has of raw materials, money or manpower, across the cultural, economic, educational, health and military fields, a thought born of the Arab homeland's and Islamic world's variety of climate, natural and human wealth and religious, cultural and value heritage; and its factors — cultural: the Qur'an, the source of knowledge and culture and the methodology the nation must move by, whose guidance can carry it past division and fragmentation, and the Arabic language, the Qur'an's language, the effective means of carrying thought and feeling between the Arab-Islamic nation's sons, whose keeping and developing is a religious duty; historical: a past carrying models of the nation's great sons who spread right, justice, asceticism and virtue and left a scientific and moral heritage that is all mankind's pride, able to revive the spirit of integration and unity; social: the Arabs' likeness in their social make-up and most customs and traditions — cooperation, honouring the neighbour and keeping the family's cohesion — a strong spur to strengthen the social bonds; and natural: one geographic homeland whose varied environments and climates complete one another, which the invaders were keen to cut into statelets so as to seize and plunder its goods.
