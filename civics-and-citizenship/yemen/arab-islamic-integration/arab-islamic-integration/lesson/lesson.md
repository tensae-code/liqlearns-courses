# Arab-Islamic Integration

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Arab-Islamic Integration. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Yemen's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## Sub lesson 1 — What Arab-Islamic integration means

The Arab and Islamic states cooperating, each contributing what it has of raw materials, money or manpower, across the cultural, economic, educational, health and military fields.

## Sub lesson 2 — Its factors

Cultural — the Qur'an and the Arabic language; historical — a shared heritage of models of right and justice; social — peoples alike in cooperation, neighbourliness and family cohesion; and natural — one varied geographic homeland the invaders cut into statelets.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: What Arab-Islamic integration means — the cooperation of the Arab and Islamic homelands' states among themselves, each state contributing what it has of raw materials, money or manpower, across the cultural, economic, educational, health and military fields, a thought born of the Arab homeland's and Islamic world's variety of climate, natural and human wealth and religious, cultural and value heritage; and its factors — cultural: the Qur'an, the source of knowledge and culture and the methodology the nation must move by, whose guidance can carry it past division and fragmentation, and the Arabic language, the Qur'an's language, the effective means of carrying thought and feeling between the Arab-Islamic nation's sons, whose keeping and developing is a religious duty; historical: a past carrying models of the nation's great sons who spread right, justice, asceticism and virtue and left a scientific and moral heritage that is all mankind's pride, able to revive the spirit of integration and unity; social: the Arabs' likeness in their social make-up and most customs and traditions — cooperation, honouring the neighbour and keeping the family's cohesion — a strong spur to strengthen the social bonds; and natural: one geographic homeland whose varied environments and climates complete one another, which the invaders were keen to cut into statelets so as to seize and plunder its goods. Give one real example from your own life or from the Yemen you live in, and name the lesson's key words: arab-islamic, integration. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
