# Video script — Arab-Islamic Economic Integration

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
What Arab-Islamic economic integration means — the states exchanging their varied resources among themselves, the states where human resources are available and capital is lacking — Egypt and Sudan are the book's examples — supplying the other states with workers and receiving money in return, and likewise in the other fields: agriculture, livestock and fish wealth, mineral wealth and energy, so that all can be benefited from; its importance; and the steps to achieving it — a sincere political will in the Arab and Muslim rulers and a sense of responsibility toward their peoples' interests, removing the customs barriers between the states, cooperating in carrying out economic development projects and protecting them as this integration's infrastructure, exchanging Arab and Islamic manufactures and finding them a place before foreign manufactures, and preparing the climate for the Arab and Islamic economic cadres from across the world and gathering them to benefit from their accumulated experience in running the integration's projects.
