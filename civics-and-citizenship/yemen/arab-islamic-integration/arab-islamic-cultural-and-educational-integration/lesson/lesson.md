# Arab-Islamic Cultural and Educational Integration

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Arab-Islamic Cultural and Educational Integration. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Yemen's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## Sub lesson 1 — Culture and upbringing — what the words mean

Culture as the practical and intellectual yield of the Sharia texts and the nation's inherited values; upbringing as the activity that raises the human being in every side of his personality.

## Sub lesson 2 — What this integration is, and how it is reached

Cooperation of the means of upbringing and culture to raise the citizen's level — reviving faith brotherhood, foiling the intellectual invasion and renewing pride — by one Islamic educational philosophy, a strengthened identity, and the nation's leading models.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: What culture is, in its Arab sense — the practical and intellectual yield arising from studying the Sharia texts and the value and moral inheritance the nation gained by experience — and what upbringing is, the individual and social activity aiming to raise the human being in every side of his personality; what Arab-Islamic cultural and educational integration is — the cooperation and coordination between the means of upbringing and culture at the level of the Arab homeland and the Islamic world, leading to raising the citizen's scientific and cultural level, and so fixing the nation's identity and course; its importance — reviving the spirit of faith brotherhood and solidarity among the Arab and Islamic nation's sons, foiling the attempts of the intellectual invasion that seeks to replace Islamic culture with Western culture carrying much that demolishes Islamic values and principles, and reviving the spirit of pride in belonging to the nation; and the ways of achieving it — adopting one comprehensive Islamic educational philosophy built on the Qur'an and its view of man, the universe and life, freed from dependence on Western education, strengthening the faith and national identity in the nation's sons, and bringing forward the leading models in the nation's history. Give one real example from your own life or from the Yemen you live in, and name the lesson's key words: arab-islamic, cultural, and. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
