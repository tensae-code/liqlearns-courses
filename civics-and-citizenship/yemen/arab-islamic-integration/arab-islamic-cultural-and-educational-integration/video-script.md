# Video script — Arab-Islamic Cultural and Educational Integration

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
What culture is, in its Arab sense — the practical and intellectual yield arising from studying the Sharia texts and the value and moral inheritance the nation gained by experience — and what upbringing is, the individual and social activity aiming to raise the human being in every side of his personality; what Arab-Islamic cultural and educational integration is — the cooperation and coordination between the means of upbringing and culture at the level of the Arab homeland and the Islamic world, leading to raising the citizen's scientific and cultural level, and so fixing the nation's identity and course; its importance — reviving the spirit of faith brotherhood and solidarity among the Arab and Islamic nation's sons, foiling the attempts of the intellectual invasion that seeks to replace Islamic culture with Western culture carrying much that demolishes Islamic values and principles, and reviving the spirit of pride in belonging to the nation; and the ways of achieving it — adopting one comprehensive Islamic educational philosophy built on the Qur'an and its view of man, the universe and life, freed from dependence on Western education, strengthening the faith and national identity in the nation's sons, and bringing forward the leading models in the nation's history.
