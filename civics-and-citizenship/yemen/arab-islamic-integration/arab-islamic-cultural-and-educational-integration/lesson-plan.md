# Lesson plan — Arab-Islamic Cultural and Educational Integration

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Yemen · **Title:** Arab-Islamic Integration · **Lesson:** Arab-Islamic Cultural and Educational Integration
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 19 — Arab-Islamic Integration (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** What culture is, in its Arab sense — the practical and intellectual yield arising from studying the Sharia texts and the value and moral inheritance the nation gained by experience — and what upbringing is, the individual and social activity aiming to raise the human being in every side of his personality; what Arab-Islamic cultural and educational integration is — the cooperation and coordination between the means of upbringing and culture at the level of the Arab homeland and the Islamic world, leading to raising the citizen's scientific and cultural level, and so fixing the nation's identity and course; its importance — reviving the spirit of faith brotherhood and solidarity among the Arab and Islamic nation's sons, foiling the attempts of the intellectual invasion that seeks to replace Islamic culture with Western culture carrying much that demolishes Islamic values and principles, and reviving the spirit of pride in belonging to the nation; and the ways of achieving it — adopting one comprehensive Islamic educational philosophy built on the Qur'an and its view of man, the universe and life, freed from dependence on Western education, strengthening the faith and national identity in the nation's sons, and bringing forward the leading models in the nation's history. This lesson stands on a student book.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 19 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. ## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Culture and upbringing — what the words mean** — Culture as the practical and intellectual yield of the Sharia texts and the nation's inherited values; upbringing as the activity that raises the human being in every side of his personality.
2. **What this integration is, and how it is reached** — Cooperation of the means of upbringing and culture to raise the citizen's level — reviving faith brotherhood, foiling the intellectual invasion and renewing pride — by one Islamic educational philosophy, a strengthened identity, and the nation's leading models.

Gate pool: at most 5 questions for the prove-it check, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
