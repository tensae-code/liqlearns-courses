# Facilities and Services — depth

This lesson is ONE lesson with no sub lessons: it teaches a single thing, whole. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: The service facilities and what each ministry gives the citizen — education, health and population, and the interior's security, civil register, passports and civil defence — and the pupil's duty to guard them, beginning with his own school — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
