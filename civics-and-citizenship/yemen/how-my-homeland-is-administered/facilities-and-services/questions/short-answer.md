# Facilities and Services — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** The service facilities and what each ministry gives the citizen — education, health and population, and the interior's security, civil register, passports and civil defence — and the pupil's duty to guard them, beginning with his own school.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** The service facilities and what each ministry gives the citizen — education, health and population, and the interior's security, civil register, passports and civil defence — and the pupil's duty to guard them, beginning with his own school.
