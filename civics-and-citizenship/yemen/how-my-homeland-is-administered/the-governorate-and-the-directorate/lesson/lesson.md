# The Governorate and the Directorate

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: The Governorate and the Directorate. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Yemen's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## Sub lesson 1 — The governorate and its governor

The governorate and the governor's chief responsibilities: carrying out the republic's decisions and laws, serving the citizens and solving their problems, keeping security.

## Sub lesson 2 — The directorate and its director

The directorate below the governorate and its director's same charge, with the directorates of al-Dhale'e and al-Mahwit governorates as the worked examples.
