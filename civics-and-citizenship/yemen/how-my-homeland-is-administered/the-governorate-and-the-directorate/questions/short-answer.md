# The Governorate and the Directorate — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** The governor's chief responsibilities and the directorate director's — carrying out the republic's decisions and laws, serving the citizens and solving their problems, keeping security — with the directorates of al-Dhale'e and al-Mahwit governorates as the worked examples.
