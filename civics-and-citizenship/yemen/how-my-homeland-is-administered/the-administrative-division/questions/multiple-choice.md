Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) How the homeland is divided into governorates and below, and what the division is for.  b) it has nothing to do with how Yemen governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
