# Civics and Citizenship — Yemen course — CURRICULUM (the path)

One course for Yemen inside the Civics and Citizenship
folder, organised by **levels, not grades**: a learner climbs
when they have proved the level, not when a school calendar
says so. The school grades the books come from appear only in
each lesson's sources (provenance), never as the course's
organisation. The path follows Yemen's own order as its books
lay it out — the Grade 5 book first: the homeland's story of
resistance, its revolutions, unity and symbols, then its
administration and its three powers, closing on four Yemeni
lives; then the Grade 6 book: the family that forms the
citizen and the tribe that stands behind him, his
constitution, and four more Yemeni lives; then the Grade 7
book, which turns from the state to the person — upbringing,
values, the productive family, and people with special needs;
then the Grade 8 book, which names the citizen — identity,
rights family by family answered by responsibilities, the
safety of the world around him, and his own time, youth and
media; then the Grade 9 book, which faces the young citizen
himself — his youth and its unhealthy habits, the social
phenomena pressing on his family, the whole Arab-Islamic
homeland's integration, and his own part in community life.
The combined Grades 7–9 teacher guide's background essays
stand whole at the end as a labelled evidence-only level, in
that filing's own order. The filings for this build
(`yemen-placements.md` — 5 records / 5 distinct books, 78
placements from five read student books — and
`yemen-placements-b.md` — the combined Grades 7–9 teacher
guide, 8 placements, TEACHER evidence) stock 86 lessons in
all: every placement names its own coined lesson, the coined
names were deduped before building (no two placements shared
a lesson), and every placement stocks into exactly one
lesson.

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in
this course comes from government textbooks — this country's
own National Education books — except the **Tip boxes**: a
tip in a box is from us, the people making LiqLearns. Tips
are ours; the lessons are the books'. See ABOUT.md for the
full book list and the acceptance reckoning. **Seventy-eight
lessons stand on student books; eight stand on teacher-guide
evidence only and count 0 in the acceptance reckoning, as
ABOUT.md states.** The Grade 6 and Grade 9 student books were
UNREAD-pending under the 2026-11-17 disk hold in the first
build; they were fetched, read in full and built in the
2026-11-22 unread-queue phase, and their units are slotted
into the ladder in grade order exactly as the first filing's
ladder sketch warned — the middle levels were renumbered
(Grade 7's units moved from Levels 5–8 to 9–12, Grade 8's
from 9–12 to 13–16, and the teacher guide from Level 13 to
Level 21). Yemen's unread list is now empty.

## The levels (the path, in order)

1. **National Symbols and Revolutions** — the course opens where the Grade 5 book opens: the homeland's story of resistance, its revolutions, its unity and its symbols (flag, anthem, emblem). 5 lessons, in one title: `national-symbols-and-revolutions/` (5 lessons).
2. **How My Homeland Is Administered** — from the story to the map: governorates and directorates, and the services the citizen meets. 3 lessons, in one title: `how-my-homeland-is-administered/` (3 lessons).
3. **Powers of the State** — the three powers of the republic and the citizen's first electoral rights and duties, with shura beside democracy. 4 lessons, in one title: `powers-of-the-state/` (4 lessons).
4. **Yemeni Figures** — the Grade 5 book closes on four lives to imitate, from the imam who rose against the Ottomans to the blind national poet. 4 lessons, in one title: `yemeni-figures/` (4 lessons).
5. **The Yemeni Family** — Grade 6 begins with the family: how it is formed by marriage, its functions, its roles in raising children, and the rights and duties inside it. 4 lessons, in one title: `the-yemeni-family/` (4 lessons).
6. **The Yemeni Tribe** — the second belonging: the tribe as a social component, its urf, habits and traditions, and its peacemaking. 3 lessons, in one title: `the-yemeni-tribe/` (3 lessons).
7. **The Yemeni Constitution** — the citizen handed his constitution: what it is, the state's foundations in it, his rights and duties in it, and the good citizen's qualities. 3 lessons, in one title: `the-yemeni-constitution/` (3 lessons).
8. **Yemeni Personalities and Figures** — the Grade 6 book closes on four more lives: the imam who made peace among the tribes, the president who built, the fighter for dialogue, and the rebel poet. 4 lessons, in one title: `yemeni-personalities-and-figures/` (4 lessons).
9. **Family and Social Upbringing** — Grade 7 turns inward: how a person is raised, by family, school and companions, and the family's moral work. 4 lessons, in one title: `family-and-social-upbringing/` (4 lessons).
10. **Values** — the virtues that upbringing plants: honesty, patience, sincerity in work, cooperation, order and responsibility. 6 lessons, in one title: `values/` (6 lessons).
11. **Productive Families and Self-Sufficiency** — the family at work, in village and city, and the homeland that needs no imports. 4 lessons, in one title: `productive-families-and-self-sufficiency/` (4 lessons).
12. **People with Special Needs** — who people with special needs are, what they need, the institutions that serve them, and their full place in society. 4 lessons, in one title: `people-with-special-needs/` (4 lessons).
13. **National Identity** — Grade 8 names the bond itself: identity, independence and sovereignty, defence, and betrayal as its opposite. 4 lessons, in one title: `national-identity/` (4 lessons).
14. **Rights and Responsibilities** — the citizen's rights family by family, answered by his responsibilities in the same four fields. 5 lessons, in one title: `rights-and-responsibilities/` (5 lessons).
15. **Environment and Public Safety** — the citizen as guardian of his surroundings: environmental health, everyday safety, civil defence, and Islam's cleanliness rules. 4 lessons, in one title: `environment-and-public-safety/` (4 lessons).
16. **Social Issues** — the young citizen's own life: his time, his summer, and the media that reach him. 3 lessons, in one title: `social-issues/` (3 lessons).
17. **Youth and Unhealthy Habits** — Grade 9 faces the young citizen himself: his youth stage, his mind and body, and the habits — smoking, shamma, qat — that can ruin both, with their treatment. 3 lessons, in one title: `youth-and-unhealthy-habits/` (3 lessons).
18. **Social Phenomena and the Yemeni Family** — the phenomena pressing on the family: children at work beyond their capacity, the family head working abroad, and dowries exaggerated past bearing. 3 lessons, in one title: `social-phenomena-and-the-yemeni-family/` (3 lessons).
19. **Arab-Islamic Integration** — the eyes lifted to the whole Arab-Islamic homeland: what integration is and its factors, then its economic, cultural-educational and political-military fields. 4 lessons, in one title: `arab-islamic-integration/` (4 lessons).
20. **Community Participation** — the course's student-book ladder closes where the citizen acts: taking part in community life, public manners, preserving public and private property, and fighting bribery, cheating and monopoly. 4 lessons, in one title: `community-participation/` (4 lessons).
21. **Teacher-Guide Background — Evidence Only** — the combined Grades 7–9 teacher guide's background essays, kept whole and labelled as evidence only — home and values first, then rights and public trusts, then the institutions a citizen acts through, in the guide filing's own order. 8 lessons, in 6 titles: `family-and-society/` (1 lesson), `rights-and-duties/` (2 lessons), `character-and-values/` (1 lesson), `homeland-and-world/` (2 lessons), `law-and-public-life/` (1 lesson), `taking-part/` (1 lesson).
