# Care, Empathy and Sharing

ℹ️ *Info: this lesson teaches one thing — care, empathy and sharing. Tap a part and do it; the explanation is inside each part.*

## Do it first

Show the lesson's teaching with your own body and things: act out, draw, or arrange objects for this — care, empathy and sharing.

## Sub lesson 1 — Care, empathy and sharing

Care, empathy and sharing — noticing others, feeling with them, and sharing what you have.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: care, empathy and sharing. Give one real example from your own life in Vietnam, and name the lesson's key words: care, empathy, sharing. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Vietnamese as well as in English — Vietnam's own books teach Đạo đức (Ethics), Giáo dục công dân (Civic Education) and Giáo dục Kinh tế và Pháp luật (Economics and Law) in Vietnamese, through pictures, stories and situations, for life at home and at school, not only for the classroom.
