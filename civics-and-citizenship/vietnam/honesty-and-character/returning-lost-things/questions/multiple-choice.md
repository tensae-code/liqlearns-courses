# Returning Lost Things — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) finding something lost and returning it to the person who lost it  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) finding something lost and returning it to the person who lost it  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
