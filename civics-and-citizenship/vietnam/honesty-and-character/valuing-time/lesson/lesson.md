# Valuing Time

ℹ️ *Info: this lesson teaches one thing — valuing time. Tap a part and do it; the explanation is inside each part.*

## Do it first

Show the lesson's teaching with your own body and things: act out, draw, or arrange objects for this — valuing time.

## Sub lesson 1 — Valuing time

Valuing time — time is precious; using it well and never wasting it.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: valuing time. Give one real example from your own life in Vietnam, and name the lesson's key words: valuing, time. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Vietnamese as well as in English — Vietnam's own books teach Đạo đức (Ethics), Giáo dục công dân (Civic Education) and Giáo dục Kinh tế và Pháp luật (Economics and Law) in Vietnamese, through pictures, stories and situations, for life at home and at school, not only for the classroom.
