# Protecting What Is Right and Good

ℹ️ *Info: this lesson teaches one thing — protecting what is right and good. Tap a part and do it; the explanation is inside each part.*

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — Protecting what is right and good

Protecting what is right and good — standing up for the right thing.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: protecting what is right and good. Give one real example from your own life in Vietnam, and name the lesson's key words: protecting, what, is, right. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Vietnamese as well as in English — Vietnam's own books teach Đạo đức (Ethics), Giáo dục công dân (Civic Education) and Giáo dục Kinh tế và Pháp luật (Economics and Law) in Vietnamese, through pictures, stories and situations, for life at home and at school, not only for the classroom.
