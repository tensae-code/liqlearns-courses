# Living with Ideals

ℹ️ *Info: this lesson teaches one thing — living with ideals. Tap a part and do it; the explanation is inside each part.*

## Do it first

Show the lesson's teaching with your own body and things: act out, draw, or arrange objects for this — living with ideals.

## Sub lesson 1 — Living with ideals

Living with ideals — what an ideal is, why a life needs one, and the ideals of vietnamese youth (the book opens with hồ chí minh's stated wish and the diary of đặng thuỳ trâm, in summary).

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: living with ideals. Give one real example from your own life in Vietnam, and name the lesson's key words: living, with, ideals. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Vietnamese as well as in English — Vietnam's own books teach Đạo đức (Ethics), Giáo dục công dân (Civic Education) and Giáo dục Kinh tế và Pháp luật (Economics and Law) in Vietnamese, through pictures, stories and situations, for life at home and at school, not only for the classroom.
