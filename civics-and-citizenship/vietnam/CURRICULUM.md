# Civics and Citizenship — Vietnam course — CURRICULUM (the path)

One course for Vietnam inside the Civics and Citizenship folder, organised by **levels, not grades**: a learner climbs when they have proved the level, not when a school calendar says so. The school grades the books come from appear only in each lesson's sources (provenance), never as the course's organisation. The path follows Vietnam's own books' progression, as confirmed at the end of the filing: primary school teaches Đạo đức (Ethics) in widening circles — the body, the family, the school, the homeland; lower secondary teaches Giáo dục công dân (Civic Education) as the citizen's formation — virtues deepened, heritage, then rights, duties and law named sphere by sphere; upper secondary teaches Giáo dục Kinh tế và Pháp luật (Economics and Law) with the citizen meeting law as a system — and for this course only its civics/law parts are placed, its economics spans named in every contents map and never placed.

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in this course comes from government textbooks — this country's own Đạo đức, Giáo dục công dân and Giáo dục Kinh tế và Pháp luật books — except the **Tip boxes**: a tip in a box is from us, the people making LiqLearns. Tips are ours; the lessons are the books'. See ABOUT.md for the full book list, every gap and label carried exactly as filed, and the acceptance reckoning.

## The levels (the path, in order)

1. **Ethics in Primary School** — the child's own conduct first — the body, the family, the school — one small duty or virtue per lesson, exactly as Vietnam's Đạo đức (Ethics) books for Grades 1–5 teach it. 71 lessons standing at this level (72 placements filed at it; Protecting the Environment also carries its Grade 8 part and stands here at its earliest filed level). 71 lessons.

2. **Civic Education in Lower Secondary** — the named citizen subject, Giáo dục công dân (Civic Education) for Grades 6–9 — the virtues deepen, the homeland and its heritage enter, and rights, duties and the law are named sphere by sphere. 41 lessons standing at this level (42 placements filed at it; Freedom of Business and the Duty to Pay Tax also carries its Grade 12 part and stands here at its earliest filed level). 41 lessons.

3. **Law and the Citizen in Upper Secondary** — Giáo dục Kinh tế và Pháp luật (Economics and Law) for Grades 10–12, civics/law parts only — what law is, the Constitution, the state organ by organ, rights taught as equalities, democratic rights and freedoms, and the law of family, work, property and the world. 47 lessons. 47 lessons.
