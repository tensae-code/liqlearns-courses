# Keeping My Hands Clean — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) keeping your hands clean — why hands must be washed, and how a child keeps them clean through the day  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
