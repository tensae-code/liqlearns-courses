# Being Tidy and Orderly — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) being tidy and orderly — a place for your things, at home and at school  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) being tidy and orderly — a place for your things, at home and at school  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
