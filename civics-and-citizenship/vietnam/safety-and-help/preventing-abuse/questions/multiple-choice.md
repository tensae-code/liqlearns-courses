# Preventing Abuse — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) preventing abuse — knowing your body's safety rules and telling a trusted adult (the Grade 1 telling of the book's safety close)  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) preventing abuse — the older primary child's fuller telling: danger signs, refusal, escape and telling (the Grade 5 telling)  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
