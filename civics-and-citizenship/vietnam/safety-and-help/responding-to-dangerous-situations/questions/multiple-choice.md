# Responding to Dangerous Situations — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) responding to dangerous situations — keeping calm, escaping, and calling for help  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
