# Keeping School and Class Property

ℹ️ *Info: this lesson teaches one thing — looking after the school's and class's property. Tap a part and do it; the explanation is inside each part.*

## Do it first

Show the lesson's teaching with your own body and things: act out, draw, or arrange objects for this — looking after the school's and class's property.

## Sub lesson 1 — Looking after the school's and class's property

Looking after the school's and class's property — what belongs to everyone is cared for by everyone.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: looking after the school's and class's property. Give one real example from your own life in Vietnam, and name the lesson's key words: keeping, school, class, property. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Vietnamese as well as in English — Vietnam's own books teach Đạo đức (Ethics), Giáo dục công dân (Civic Education) and Giáo dục Kinh tế và Pháp luật (Economics and Law) in Vietnamese, through pictures, stories and situations, for life at home and at school, not only for the classroom.
