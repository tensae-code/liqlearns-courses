# Learning Actively on My Own

ℹ️ *Info: this lesson teaches one thing — active, self-directed learning. Tap a part and do it; the explanation is inside each part.*

## Do it first

Show the lesson's teaching with your own body and things: act out, draw, or arrange objects for this — active, self-directed learning.

## Sub lesson 1 — Active, self-directed learning

Active, self-directed learning — studying willingly and energetically, not under pressure.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: active, self-directed learning. Give one real example from your own life in Vietnam, and name the lesson's key words: learning, actively, own. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Vietnamese as well as in English — Vietnam's own books teach Đạo đức (Ethics), Giáo dục công dân (Civic Education) and Giáo dục Kinh tế và Pháp luật (Economics and Law) in Vietnamese, through pictures, stories and situations, for life at home and at school, not only for the classroom.
