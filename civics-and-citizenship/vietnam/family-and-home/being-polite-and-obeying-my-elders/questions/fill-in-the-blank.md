# Being Polite and Obeying My Elders — fill in the blank

Fill the gap with the lesson's own words.

**1.** This lesson teaches ______.
**Answer:** being polite and obedient to grandparents, parents and older siblings — the child's manners at home.

**2.** The lesson's key words include ______.
**Answer:** being, polite, obeying, elders.
