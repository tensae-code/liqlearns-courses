Preserving cultural heritage — what heritage is, and what a pupil can do to protect it.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: preserving cultural heritage. Give one real example from your own life in Vietnam, and name the lesson's key words: preserving, cultural, heritage. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Vietnamese as well as in English — Vietnam's own books teach Đạo đức (Ethics), Giáo dục công dân (Civic Education) and Giáo dục Kinh tế và Pháp luật (Economics and Law) in Vietnamese, through pictures, stories and situations, for life at home and at school, not only for the classroom.
