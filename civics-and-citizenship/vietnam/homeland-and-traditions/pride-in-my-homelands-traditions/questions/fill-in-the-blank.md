# Pride in My Homeland's Traditions — fill in the blank

Fill the gap with the lesson's own words.

**1.** This lesson teaches ______.
**Answer:** pride in the homeland's traditions — its cultural traditions and its tradition of patriotism and resisting invasion, and deeds that keep them alive (the book opens at the Lim festival and its quan họ singing).

**2.** The lesson's key words include ______.
**Answer:** pride, homelands, traditions.
