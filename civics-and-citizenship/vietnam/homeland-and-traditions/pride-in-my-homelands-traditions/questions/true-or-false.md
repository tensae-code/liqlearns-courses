# Pride in My Homeland's Traditions — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** This lesson teaches: pride in the homeland's traditions — its cultural traditions and its tradition of patriotism and resisting invasion, and deeds that keep them alive (the book opens at the Lim festival and its quan họ singing).
**Answer: True** — that is the lesson's teaching, in the book's own summary.

**2.** This lesson has nothing to do with life in Vietnam.
**Answer: False** — every part comes from Vietnam's own textbooks and is worked from the learner's own life in Vietnam.
