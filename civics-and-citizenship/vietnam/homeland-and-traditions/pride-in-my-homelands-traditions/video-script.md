This lesson is about one thing.
Pride in the homeland's traditions.

Pride in the homeland's traditions. Pride in the homeland's traditions — its cultural traditions and its tradition of patriotism and resisting invasion, and deeds that keep them alive (the book opens at the lim festival and its quan họ singing).

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
