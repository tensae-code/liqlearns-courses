# Gratitude to Workers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) gratitude to workers — seeing what working people give us, and thanking them in words and deeds  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?

**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
