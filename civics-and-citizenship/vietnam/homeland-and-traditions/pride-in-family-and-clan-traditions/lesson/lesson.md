# Pride in Family and Clan Traditions

ℹ️ *Info: this lesson teaches one thing — pride in family and clan traditions. Tap a part and do it; the explanation is inside each part.*

## Do it first

Show the lesson's teaching with your own body and things: act out, draw, or arrange objects for this — pride in family and clan traditions.

## Sub lesson 1 — Pride in family and clan traditions

Pride in family and clan traditions — what a family's traditions are (the book's example: the đặng clan of sơn la and its love of learning), why they matter, and keeping and developing them.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: pride in family and clan traditions. Give one real example from your own life in Vietnam, and name the lesson's key words: pride, family, clan, traditions. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Vietnamese as well as in English — Vietnam's own books teach Đạo đức (Ethics), Giáo dục công dân (Civic Education) and Giáo dục Kinh tế và Pháp luật (Economics and Law) in Vietnamese, through pictures, stories and situations, for life at home and at school, not only for the classroom.
