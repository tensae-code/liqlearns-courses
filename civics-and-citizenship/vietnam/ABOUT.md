# About the Civics and Citizenship — Vietnam course

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in this course comes from government textbooks — this country's own Đạo đức (Ethics), Giáo dục công dân (Civic Education) and Giáo dục Kinh tế và Pháp luật (Economics and Law) books. The one exception is the **Tip boxes**: a tip in a box is from us, the people making LiqLearns. Tips are ours; the lessons are the books'.

## How this course is organised

This is ONE course for Vietnam inside the Civics and Citizenship folder (one course per country). Inside it, the organisation is by **levels, not grades**: a learner's place is found by what they prove, and each level stands on the ones before it as a recommended-first path — never a forced gate. The school grades the books come from appear only in each lesson's `lesson/sources.md`, as provenance.

The course as built has **three stocked levels** (Levels 1–3), from the filing for this build (`vietnam-placements.md` — 15 records, 15 books read, no records unread). 162 placements in all, stocking 159 lessons — the coined lesson names were deduped before building (see CURRICULUM.md, "Where the build stands"): every placement stocks into exactly one lesson, and every placement's provenance row is in that lesson's `lesson/sources.md`.

**How the books were read, exactly as filed:** the books are Vietnamese-language and image-only (no text layer). The 9 Ethics and Civic Education books were read from their full OCR text — RapidOCR over the publisher's page images, the method that produced the OCR files held with the books (this machine's tesseract carries no Vietnamese traineddata). The 6 Economics and Law books were read from rendered page images — covers/imprints, prefaces and full contents, with law-part openings — alongside RapidOCR of the Grade 10 volume. Every book's verdict states exactly how far its read went; all 15 verdicts are PARTIAL, so placements are at lesson level from the verified contents, exactly as filed.

**What the filing does and does not contain, stated plainly:** the filing contains 15 book headers and 162 placement lines, a closed unread queue, and a closing ladder sketch. It contains no COMPLETE verdict — every book is PARTIAL, as filed. Legal and constitutional teaching is named and summarised throughout, never transcribed beyond very short fragments. The Economics and Law books' economics spans are named in their contents maps and never placed.
