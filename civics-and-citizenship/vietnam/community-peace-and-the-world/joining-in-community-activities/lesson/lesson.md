## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — Taking an active part in community activities

Taking an active part in community activities — the citizen in the neighbourhood, not only at home.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: taking an active part in community activities. Give one real example from your own life in Vietnam, and name the lesson's key words: joining, community, activities. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Vietnamese as well as in English — Vietnam's own books teach Đạo đức (Ethics), Giáo dục công dân (Civic Education) and Giáo dục Kinh tế và Pháp luật (Economics and Law) in Vietnamese, through pictures, stories and situations, for life at home and at school, not only for the classroom.
