# Video script — Protecting the Environment

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Protecting the living environment.

Protecting the living environment. Protecting the living environment — the child's part in keeping the world around them clean and alive (the primary telling).

Protecting the environment and natural resources. Protecting the environment and natural resources — the citizen's environmental duty, at national scale (the grade 8 telling).

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
