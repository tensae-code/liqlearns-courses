# Caribbean Festivals Overseas

## Do it first

Name one Caribbean festival celebrated outside the Caribbean. This lesson asks where it came from and why it is still celebrated.

Caribbean festivals overseas — their origins, purposes and activities, and why they are still celebrated.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Caribbean festivals overseas — their origins, purposes and activities, and why they are still celebrated. Give one real example from your own life or from the Jamaica you live in, and name the lesson's key words: caribbean, festivals, overseas. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself
