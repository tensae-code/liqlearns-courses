# Honouring the Name That I Bear

## Do it first

Say what your school's name stands for. This lesson is about honouring the name you bear — your school's symbols, rituals and reputation.

School rituals, symbols and reputation continued through the ISSA Championships case study — respect, loyalty and school pride.
