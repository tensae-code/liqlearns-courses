# Hands-on — Honouring the Name That I Bear

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Say what your school's name stands for. This lesson is about honouring the name you bear — your school's symbols, rituals and reputation.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: School rituals, symbols and reputation continued through the ISSA Championships case study — respect, loyalty and school pride. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
