# Hands-on — Protocol at Rituals and Ceremonies

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Remember one formal ceremony you have seen and how people behaved. This lesson teaches the protocol — the right procedures and behaviour — at rituals and ceremonies.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Protocol and procedures at national and community rituals and ceremonies, and the behaviour they call for. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
