# Hands-on — Cultural Retentions

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name one custom in Jamaica today that came from long ago. This lesson is about cultural retentions — what a people keeps — and how to help keep it.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Identifying cultural retentions in Jamaica and the Caribbean, how they shape values and attitudes, and practical ways to contribute to retaining Jamaican culture. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
