# Caribbean Festival Characters

## Do it first

Name one festival character you know, such as a Jonkonnu character. This lesson is about the characters that make Caribbean festivals uniquely Caribbean.

Being uniquely Caribbean — festivals across the Caribbean and the festival characters (e.g. Jonkonnu) that come from the colonial and ancestral past.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Being uniquely Caribbean — festivals across the Caribbean and the festival characters (e.g. Jonkonnu) that come from the colonial and ancestral past. Give one real example from your own life or from the Jamaica you live in, and name the lesson's key words: caribbean, festival, characters. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
