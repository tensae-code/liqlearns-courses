# National Symbols and National Awards

## Do it first

Name one national symbol of Jamaica and say what a National Award is given for. This lesson joins the two: the symbols of the nation and the honours it gives.

National symbols and the National Awards given to celebrate persons' achievements and contributions to the nation.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: National symbols and the National Awards given to celebrate persons' achievements and contributions to the nation. Give one real example from your own life or from the Jamaica you live in, and name the lesson's key words: national, symbols, national, awards. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
