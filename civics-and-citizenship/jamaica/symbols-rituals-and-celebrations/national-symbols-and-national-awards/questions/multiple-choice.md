# National Symbols and National Awards — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) National symbols and the National Awards given to celebrate persons' achievements and contributions to the nation.  b) it has nothing to do with how Jamaica governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations

a) National symbols and the National Awards given to celebrate persons' achievements and contributions to the nation.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Jamaica
**Answer: a) National symbols and the National Awards given to celebrate persons' achievements and contributions to the nation.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
