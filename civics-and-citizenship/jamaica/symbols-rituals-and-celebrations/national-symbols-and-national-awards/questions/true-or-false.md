# National Symbols and National Awards — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** National symbols and the National Awards given to celebrate persons' achievements and contributions to the nation.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that national symbols and the National Awards given to celebrate persons' achievements and contributions to the nation. — and that it has no connection to the rest of Jamaica's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Jamaica's own course, in its own order.
