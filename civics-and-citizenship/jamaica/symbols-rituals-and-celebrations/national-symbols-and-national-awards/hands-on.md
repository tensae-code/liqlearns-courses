# Hands-on — National Symbols and National Awards

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name one national symbol of Jamaica and say what a National Award is given for. This lesson joins the two: the symbols of the nation and the honours it gives.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: National symbols and the National Awards given to celebrate persons' achievements and contributions to the nation. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
