# Sharing Our Success With Others

## Do it first

Name one contribution Jamaica has made to the world's culture. This lesson is about sharing our success — and devising your own contribution to nation-building.

Jamaica's role in global cultural affairs, the contributions of nation builders, and devising your own contribution to nation-building.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Jamaica's role in global cultural affairs, the contributions of nation builders, and devising your own contribution to nation-building. Give one real example from your own life or from the Jamaica you live in, and name the lesson's key words: sharing, success, with, others. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
