# Life Is One Big Road

## Do it first

Name one sign or place-name in your community that guides people. This lesson — Life Is One Big Road — reads community signs and symbols as guides for life's journey.

Community signs, names and symbols as a metaphor for life's journey — identity, aliases and the guidance signs we meet.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Community signs, names and symbols as a metaphor for life's journey — identity, aliases and the guidance signs we meet. Give one real example from your own life or from the Jamaica you live in, and name the lesson's key words: life, one, big, road. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
