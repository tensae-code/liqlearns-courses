# Standing On Ceremony — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Values in the symbols, rituals and celebrations of national emblems, national awards and honours and uniformed groups; protocol and celebrating achievement and service.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that values in the symbols, rituals and celebrations of national emblems, national awards and honours and uniformed groups; protocol and celebrating achievement and service. — and that it has no connection to the rest of Jamaica's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Jamaica's own course, in its own order.
