# Celebrate Me Celebrate My School

## Do it first

Name one thing your school is known for. This lesson is about how a school's rituals and symbols build its reputation — and yours.

How rituals and symbols build a school's reputation, using the ISSA Boys and Girls Championships as case study, and how school reputation reflects on the nation.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: How rituals and symbols build a school's reputation, using the ISSA Boys and Girls Championships as case study, and how school reputation reflects on the nation. Give one real example from your own life or from the Jamaica you live in, and name the lesson's key words: celebrate, celebrate, school. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: How rituals and symbols build a school's reputation, using the ISSA Boys and Girls Championships as case study, and how school reputation reflects on the nation.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
