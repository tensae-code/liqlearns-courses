# Hands-on — Celebrate Me Celebrate My School

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name one thing your school is known for. This lesson is about how a school's rituals and symbols build its reputation — and yours.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: How rituals and symbols build a school's reputation, using the ISSA Boys and Girls Championships as case study, and how school reputation reflects on the nation. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
