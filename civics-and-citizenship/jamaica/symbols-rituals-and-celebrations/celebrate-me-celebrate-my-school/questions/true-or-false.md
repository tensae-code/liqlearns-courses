# Celebrate Me Celebrate My School — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** How rituals and symbols build a school's reputation, using the ISSA Boys and Girls Championships as case study, and how school reputation reflects on the nation.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that how rituals and symbols build a school's reputation, using the ISSA Boys and Girls Championships as case study, and how school reputation reflects on the nation. — and that it has no connection to the rest of Jamaica's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Jamaica's own course, in its own order.
