# Celebrations at School

## Do it first

Name one celebration at your school and one symbol of your school. This lesson asks why they matter and how to show them respect.

The significance of the school's symbols and rituals, showing respect for them, and why celebrations matter.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The significance of the school's symbols and rituals, showing respect for them, and why celebrations matter. Give one real example from your own life or from the Jamaica you live in, and name the lesson's key words: celebrations, school. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: The significance of the school's symbols and rituals, showing respect for them, and why celebrations matter.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
