# Symbols Rituals and Celebrations Around Us

## Do it first

Point out one symbol and one celebration you know — at home, in your parish or in the nation. This lesson gathers the symbols, rituals and celebrations all around you.

Family, community, parish and national symbols, rituals and ceremonies, and the contributions of national heroes, heroine and cultural icons.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Family, community, parish and national symbols, rituals and ceremonies, and the contributions of national heroes, heroine and cultural icons. Give one real example from your own life or from the Jamaica you live in, and name the lesson's key words: symbols, rituals, celebrations, around. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: Family, community, parish and national symbols, rituals and ceremonies, and the contributions of national heroes, heroine and cultural icons.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
