# Share the Vision Reap the Rewards

## Do it first

Name one nation builder of Jamaica. This lesson — Share the Vision, Reap the Rewards — is about their contributions, and how you can add yours.

Appreciating nation builders' contributions and devising ways to contribute to nation-building.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Appreciating nation builders' contributions and devising ways to contribute to nation-building. Give one real example from your own life or from the Jamaica you live in, and name the lesson's key words: share, vision, reap, rewards. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: Appreciating nation builders' contributions and devising ways to contribute to nation-building.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
