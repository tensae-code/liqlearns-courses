# Hands-on — Honouring Contributors

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name one person who has contributed to your community or your country. This lesson is about honouring contributors — and behaving well when we do.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: People who contributed locally and nationally, ways children can contribute, and appropriate behaviour at formal functions honouring contributors. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
