# Honouring Contributors

## Do it first

Name one person who has contributed to your community or your country. This lesson is about honouring contributors — and behaving well when we do.

People who contributed locally and nationally, ways children can contribute, and appropriate behaviour at formal functions honouring contributors.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: People who contributed locally and nationally, ways children can contribute, and appropriate behaviour at formal functions honouring contributors. Give one real example from your own life or from the Jamaica you live in, and name the lesson's key words: honouring, contributors. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: People who contributed locally and nationally, ways children can contribute, and appropriate behaviour at formal functions honouring contributors.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
