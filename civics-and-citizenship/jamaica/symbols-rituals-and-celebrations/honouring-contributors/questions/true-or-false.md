# Honouring Contributors — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** People who contributed locally and nationally, ways children can contribute, and appropriate behaviour at formal functions honouring contributors.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that people who contributed locally and nationally, ways children can contribute, and appropriate behaviour at formal functions honouring contributors. — and that it has no connection to the rest of Jamaica's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Jamaica's own course, in its own order.
