# Civic and Cultural Celebrations

## Do it first

Name one civic celebration and one cultural celebration in your community. This lesson asks what they mean and how you can take part.

Kinds of civic and cultural celebrations in the community, the meaning of their rituals, belonging, and ways children can contribute to them.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Kinds of civic and cultural celebrations in the community, the meaning of their rituals, belonging, and ways children can contribute to them. Give one real example from your own life or from the Jamaica you live in, and name the lesson's key words: civic, cultural, celebrations. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: Kinds of civic and cultural celebrations in the community, the meaning of their rituals, belonging, and ways children can contribute to them.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
