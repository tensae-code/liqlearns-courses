# Celebrations at Home and in the Community

## Do it first

Name one celebration at home and one in your community, and say how each began. This lesson is about keeping celebrations alive.

Home and community celebrations, how they evolved, and the need for continuity.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Home and community celebrations, how they evolved, and the need for continuity. Give one real example from your own life or from the Jamaica you live in, and name the lesson's key words: celebrations, home, community. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: Home and community celebrations, how they evolved, and the need for continuity.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
