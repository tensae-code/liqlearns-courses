# Hands-on — Celebrations at Home and in the Community

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name one celebration at home and one in your community, and say how each began. This lesson is about keeping celebrations alive.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Home and community celebrations, how they evolved, and the need for continuity. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
