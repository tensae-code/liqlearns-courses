# Civics and Citizenship — Jamaica course — CURRICULUM (the path)

One course for Jamaica inside the Civics and Citizenship
folder, organised by **levels, not grades**: a learner climbs
when they have proved the level, not when a school calendar
says so. The school grades the books come from appear only in
each lesson's sources (provenance), never as the course's
organisation. The path follows Jamaica's own order as its
National Standards Curriculum guides lay it out — three
recurring themes (identity, culture and heritage; symbols,
rituals and celebrations; rights, responsibilities and
stewardship) spiralling from the child's self to governance
and leadership. The filing for this build
(`jamaica-placements.md`) carries all 49 placements from two
curriculum guides, and every placement stocks into exactly
one lesson, so the course holds 49 lessons.

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in
this course comes from government textbooks — this country's
own Civics Education curriculum guides — except the **Tip
boxes**: a tip in a box is from us, the people making
LiqLearns. Tips are ours; the lessons are the books'. See
ABOUT.md for the full book list and the acceptance
reckoning. **This whole course rests on curriculum guides
alone — no Jamaica student book was in hand — and it is
labelled guides-only throughout.**

## The levels (the path, in order)

1. **Self, Community and First Symbols** — personal
   identity, community and group culture, and being
   Jamaican; the symbols, rituals and celebrations around
   the child, civic and cultural celebrations, and
   honouring contributors; environmental stewardship and
   disaster preparation, and the rights and responsibilities
   of the child. 8 lessons, in three titles:
   `identity-culture-and-heritage/` (3 lessons),
   `symbols-rituals-and-celebrations/` (3 lessons),
   `rights-responsibilities-and-stewardship/` (2 lessons).
2. **School, Community Builders and the Caribbean Citizen**
   — the school and the learner's identity, community
   builders, and the Caribbean citizen; celebrations at
   school, at home and in the community, and national
   symbols and National Awards; rights with leadership and
   the environment, and government and the people.
   8 lessons, in three titles:
   `identity-culture-and-heritage/` (3 lessons),
   `symbols-rituals-and-celebrations/` (3 lessons),
   `rights-responsibilities-and-stewardship/` (2 lessons).
3. **Jamaica in the World — Sport, Festivals and
   Stewardship** — Jamaica in world sport, sport as national
   identity, and global citizenship through sport; Caribbean
   festivals overseas, festival characters, and cultural
   retentions; caring for the environment, and stewardship
   and national development. 8 lessons, in three titles:
   `identity-culture-and-heritage/` (3 lessons),
   `symbols-rituals-and-celebrations/` (3 lessons),
   `rights-responsibilities-and-stewardship/` (2 lessons).
4. **Keeping Self, Respect and Rights in Action** — keeping
   self in a changing context and valuing heritage through
   the Free Villages, and respect due; celebrating the
   school and honouring the name it bears; rights in action
   through Emancipation and the heroes Bogle and Gordon,
   and valuing cultural heritage. 8 lessons, in three
   titles: `identity-culture-and-heritage/` (3 lessons),
   `symbols-rituals-and-celebrations/` (2 lessons),
   `rights-responsibilities-and-stewardship/` (3 lessons).
5. **Independence, Unity and Social Justice** — growing up
   Jamaican, unity and prosperity, and the islands'
   uniqueness in one regional identity; life's road in
   community signs, protocol at ceremonies, and standing on
   ceremony; a neighbour's rights, standing together for
   the region's environment, and social justice in the
   community. 9 lessons, in three titles:
   `identity-culture-and-heritage/` (3 lessons),
   `symbols-rituals-and-celebrations/` (3 lessons),
   `rights-responsibilities-and-stewardship/` (3 lessons).
6. **Destiny, Nation-Building, Governance and Leadership**
   — every person's right to decide their own destiny,
   national identity documents, and the local self become
   global; sharing success and sharing the nation builders'
   vision; governance in the community, bigging up Jamaica
   through Bustamante and Manley, and leadership and legacy
   to the world. 8 lessons, in three titles:
   `identity-culture-and-heritage/` (3 lessons),
   `symbols-rituals-and-celebrations/` (2 lessons),
   `rights-responsibilities-and-stewardship/` (3 lessons).

**Totals: 49 lessons** (Level 1: 8 · Level 2: 8 · Level 3:
8 · Level 4: 8 · Level 5: 9 · Level 6: 8 — from two
curriculum guides: the National Standards Curriculum
Grades 4-6 and Grades 7-9 Civics Education guides, Ministry
of Education and Youth, Jamaica, 2022, both COMPLETE as
filed; 49 placements stocking 49 lessons).
