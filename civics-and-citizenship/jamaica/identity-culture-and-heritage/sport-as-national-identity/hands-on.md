# Hands-on — Sport As National Identity

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Remember one time Jamaica did well at a sporting event and how people felt. This lesson is about sport as a symbol of who Jamaica is.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Jamaica's performance at national and international sporting events as a symbol of cultural identity and positive self-image. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
