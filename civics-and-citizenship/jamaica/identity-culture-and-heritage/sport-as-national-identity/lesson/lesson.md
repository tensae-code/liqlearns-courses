# Sport As National Identity

## Do it first

Remember one time Jamaica did well at a sporting event and how people felt. This lesson is about sport as a symbol of who Jamaica is.

Jamaica's performance at national and international sporting events as a symbol of cultural identity and positive self-image.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Jamaica's performance at national and international sporting events as a symbol of cultural identity and positive self-image. Give one real example from your own life or from the Jamaica you live in, and name the lesson's key words: sport, as, national, identity. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
