# Valuing Heritage Yours and Mine — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Cultural heritage and identity through a Free Village example (Sturge Town, Maidstone or Sligoville) — self-reliance, cooperation and group cohesion.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that cultural heritage and identity through a Free Village example (Sturge Town, Maidstone or Sligoville) — self-reliance, cooperation and group cohesion. — and that it has no connection to the rest of Jamaica's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Jamaica's own course, in its own order.
