# Valuing Heritage Yours and Mine

## Do it first

Name one thing your family or community has kept from the past. This lesson is about valuing heritage — yours and mine — through a Free Village's story.

Cultural heritage and identity through a Free Village example (Sturge Town, Maidstone or Sligoville) — self-reliance, cooperation and group cohesion.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Cultural heritage and identity through a Free Village example (Sturge Town, Maidstone or Sligoville) — self-reliance, cooperation and group cohesion. Give one real example from your own life or from the Jamaica you live in, and name the lesson's key words: valuing, heritage, yours, mine. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: Cultural heritage and identity through a Free Village example (Sturge Town, Maidstone or Sligoville) — self-reliance, cooperation and group cohesion.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
