# Community Builders

## Do it first

Name one person in your community who teaches others a skill or a good way of living. This lesson is about community builders — and your own contribution.

The community's role in developing good citizens, community builders who pass on skills, values and attitudes, and judging your own contribution.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The community's role in developing good citizens, community builders who pass on skills, values and attitudes, and judging your own contribution. Give one real example from your own life or from the Jamaica you live in, and name the lesson's key words: community, builders. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: The community's role in developing good citizens, community builders who pass on skills, values and attitudes, and judging your own contribution.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
