# Hands-on — Community Builders

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name one person in your community who teaches others a skill or a good way of living. This lesson is about community builders — and your own contribution.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: The community's role in developing good citizens, community builders who pass on skills, values and attitudes, and judging your own contribution. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
