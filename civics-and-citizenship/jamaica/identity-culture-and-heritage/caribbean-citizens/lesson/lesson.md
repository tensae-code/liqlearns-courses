# Caribbean Citizens

## Do it first

Name one thing Jamaica shares with other Caribbean countries. This lesson is about yourself as a Caribbean citizen, not only a Jamaican one.

Yourself as a Caribbean citizen shaped by common historical experiences and cultural practices across the region.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Yourself as a Caribbean citizen shaped by common historical experiences and cultural practices across the region. Give one real example from your own life or from the Jamaica you live in, and name the lesson's key words: caribbean, citizens. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
