# Caribbean Citizens — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Yourself as a Caribbean citizen shaped by common historical experiences and cultural practices across the region.  b) it has nothing to do with how Jamaica governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Yourself as a Caribbean citizen shaped by common historical experiences and cultural practices across the region.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

a) Yourself as a Caribbean citizen shaped by common historical experiences and cultural practices across the region.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Jamaica
**Answer: a) Yourself as a Caribbean citizen shaped by common historical experiences and cultural practices across the region.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
