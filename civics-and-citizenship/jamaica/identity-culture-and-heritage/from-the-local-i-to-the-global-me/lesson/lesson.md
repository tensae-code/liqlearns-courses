# From the Local I to the Global Me

## Do it first

Name one way a Jamaican can affect the wider world. This lesson follows the climb from the local I to the global me.

The impact of Jamaica and its citizens on the global community.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The impact of Jamaica and its citizens on the global community. Give one real example from your own life or from the Jamaica you live in, and name the lesson's key words: from, local, global. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: The impact of Jamaica and its citizens on the global community.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
