# Unity and Prosperity

## Do it first

Name one way Jamaicans of different backgrounds work as one people. This lesson — Unity and Prosperity — is about diversity held together, and your part in Vision 2030.

Ethnic diversity in pre-independent Jamaica against group cohesion after independence; teamwork, patriotism and the citizen's role in Vision 2030.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Ethnic diversity in pre-independent Jamaica against group cohesion after independence; teamwork, patriotism and the citizen's role in Vision 2030. Give one real example from your own life or from the Jamaica you live in, and name the lesson's key words: unity, prosperity. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
