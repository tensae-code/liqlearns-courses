# Hands-on — Unity and Prosperity

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name one way Jamaicans of different backgrounds work as one people. This lesson — Unity and Prosperity — is about diversity held together, and your part in Vision 2030.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Ethnic diversity in pre-independent Jamaica against group cohesion after independence; teamwork, patriotism and the citizen's role in Vision 2030. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
