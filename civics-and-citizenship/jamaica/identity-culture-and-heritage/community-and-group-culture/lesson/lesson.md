# Community and Group Culture

## Do it first

Name the people and places that make up your community, and one way they work together. This lesson is about the community as a place people build together.

What a community is, living and working together in harmony, cultural events that hold communities together, and contributing to community building.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: What a community is, living and working together in harmony, cultural events that hold communities together, and contributing to community building. Give one real example from your own life or from the Jamaica you live in, and name the lesson's key words: community, group, culture. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: What a community is, living and working together in harmony, cultural events that hold communities together, and contributing to community building.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
