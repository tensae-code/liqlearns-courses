# Hands-on — Community and Group Culture

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name the people and places that make up your community, and one way they work together. This lesson is about the community as a place people build together.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: What a community is, living and working together in harmony, cultural events that hold communities together, and contributing to community building. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
