# Respect Due

## Do it first

Name one person you show respect to and one way you show it. This lesson is about respect due — to yourself, to others, to property, to time and to authority.

Respect for self, peers, property, time and those in authority, and building relationships on mutual respect.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Respect for self, peers, property, time and those in authority, and building relationships on mutual respect. Give one real example from your own life or from the Jamaica you live in, and name the lesson's key words: respect, due. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: Respect for self, peers, property, time and those in authority, and building relationships on mutual respect.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
