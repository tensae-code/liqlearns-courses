# Hands-on — Global Citizenship Through Sport

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name one way sport brings people of different countries together. This lesson is about global citizenship learned through sport.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Global citizenship through interaction of cultures at international sporting events — tolerance, respect for other cultures, and sport's role in unity and world peace. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
