# Global Citizenship Through Sport

## Do it first

Name one way sport brings people of different countries together. This lesson is about global citizenship learned through sport.

Global citizenship through interaction of cultures at international sporting events — tolerance, respect for other cultures, and sport's role in unity and world peace.

Give one real example from your own life or from the Jamaica you live in, and name the lesson's key words: global, citizenship, through, sport. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
