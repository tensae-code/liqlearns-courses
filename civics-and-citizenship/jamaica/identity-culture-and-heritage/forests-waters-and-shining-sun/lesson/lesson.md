# Forests Waters and Shining Sun

## Do it first

Name one thing that makes your island — or your part of the Caribbean — unlike any other. This lesson is about each island's uniqueness inside a shared regional identity.

How each Caribbean island's uniqueness contributes to regional identity, and keeping your own uniqueness and cultural identity.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: How each Caribbean island's uniqueness contributes to regional identity, and keeping your own uniqueness and cultural identity. Give one real example from your own life or from the Jamaica you live in, and name the lesson's key words: forests, waters, shining, sun. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: How each Caribbean island's uniqueness contributes to regional identity, and keeping your own uniqueness and cultural identity.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
