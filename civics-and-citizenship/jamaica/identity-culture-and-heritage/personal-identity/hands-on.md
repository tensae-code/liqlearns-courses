# Hands-on — Personal Identity

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name three things that make you you — one from your family, one from your customs, one that is yours alone. This lesson starts where Jamaica's own course starts: with who you are.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Defining and valuing your own identity through uniqueness, family roots and customs, and group identity (e.g. Maroons, Rastafari) as part of being Jamaican. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
