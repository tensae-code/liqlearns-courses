# Personal Identity

## Do it first

Name three things that make you you — one from your family, one from your customs, one that is yours alone. This lesson starts where Jamaica's own course starts: with who you are.

Defining and valuing your own identity through uniqueness, family roots and customs, and group identity (e.g. Maroons, Rastafari) as part of being Jamaican.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Defining and valuing your own identity through uniqueness, family roots and customs, and group identity (e.g. Maroons, Rastafari) as part of being Jamaican. Give one real example from your own life or from the Jamaica you live in, and name the lesson's key words: personal, identity. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
