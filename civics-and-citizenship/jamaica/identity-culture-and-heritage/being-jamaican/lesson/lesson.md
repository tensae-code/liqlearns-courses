# Being Jamaican

## Do it first

Say what being Jamaican means to you, in your own words. This lesson is about Jamaicaness — the feelings and qualities that make a Jamaican identity.

Feelings and positive attributes of "Jamaicaness" and "smaddyness" as a template for personal identity.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Feelings and positive attributes of "Jamaicaness" and "smaddyness" as a template for personal identity. Give one real example from your own life or from the Jamaica you live in, and name the lesson's key words: being, jamaican. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: Feelings and positive attributes of "Jamaicaness" and "smaddyness" as a template for personal identity.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
