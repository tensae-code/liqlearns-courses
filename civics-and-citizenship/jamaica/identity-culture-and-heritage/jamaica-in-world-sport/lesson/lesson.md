# Jamaica in World Sport

## Do it first

Name one Jamaican who has represented Jamaica in world sport. This lesson is about what Jamaica gives the world through sport — and the values it shows.

Jamaica's contribution to the world through international sport (football, cricket, track and field) and the "good" citizenship values that build a positive profile of self and country.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Jamaica's contribution to the world through international sport (football, cricket, track and field) and the "good" citizenship values that build a positive profile of self and country. Give one real example from your own life or from the Jamaica you live in, and name the lesson's key words: jamaica, world, sport. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
