# Every Man Has A Right

## Do it first

Say what success means to you. This lesson — 'Every Man Has a Right to Decide His Own Destiny' — is about success, its values, and your right to choose your path.

Appreciating individual, community and national success and the values of success — cooperation, tolerance, teamwork and work ethics.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Appreciating individual, community and national success and the values of success — cooperation, tolerance, teamwork and work ethics. Give one real example from your own life or from the Jamaica you live in, and name the lesson's key words: every, man, has, right. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: Appreciating individual, community and national success and the values of success — cooperation, tolerance, teamwork and work ethics.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
