Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The history and image of your school, the members of the school community, and valuing your own identity and talents as part of a group.  b) it has nothing to do with how Jamaica governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
