# Hands-on — My School and My Identity

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name your school, one thing from its history, and one talent of yours it helps you grow. This lesson is about your school and who you are inside it.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: The history and image of your school, the members of the school community, and valuing your own identity and talents as part of a group. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
