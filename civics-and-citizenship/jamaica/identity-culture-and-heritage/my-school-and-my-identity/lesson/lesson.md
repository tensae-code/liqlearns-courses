# My School and My Identity

## Do it first

Name your school, one thing from its history, and one talent of yours it helps you grow. This lesson is about your school and who you are inside it.

The history and image of your school, the members of the school community, and valuing your own identity and talents as part of a group.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The history and image of your school, the members of the school community, and valuing your own identity and talents as part of a group. Give one real example from your own life or from the Jamaica you live in, and name the lesson's key words: school, identity. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: The history and image of your school, the members of the school community, and valuing your own identity and talents as part of a group.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
