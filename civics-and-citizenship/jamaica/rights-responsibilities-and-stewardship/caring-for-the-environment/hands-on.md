# Hands-on — Caring For the Environment

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name one way people harm the environment and one way they protect it. This lesson is about good stewardship, in Jamaica and through the world's organisations.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Good stewardship of the environment and the international organisations working to reduce pollution, destruction and waste of resources. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
