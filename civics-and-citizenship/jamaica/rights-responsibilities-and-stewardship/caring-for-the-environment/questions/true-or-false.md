# Caring For the Environment — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Good stewardship of the environment and the international organisations working to reduce pollution, destruction and waste of resources.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that good stewardship of the environment and the international organisations working to reduce pollution, destruction and waste of resources. — and that it has no connection to the rest of Jamaica's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Jamaica's own course, in its own order.
