# Caring For the Environment

## Do it first

Name one way people harm the environment and one way they protect it. This lesson is about good stewardship, in Jamaica and through the world's organisations.

Good stewardship of the environment and the international organisations working to reduce pollution, destruction and waste of resources.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Good stewardship of the environment and the international organisations working to reduce pollution, destruction and waste of resources. Give one real example from your own life or from the Jamaica you live in, and name the lesson's key words: caring, for, environment. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: Good stewardship of the environment and the international organisations working to reduce pollution, destruction and waste of resources.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
