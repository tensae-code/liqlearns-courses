# Get Up Stand Up

## Do it first

Name one right that people were once denied in Jamaica's past. This lesson — Get Up, Stand Up — teaches rights through Emancipation.

How restriction of rights hinders responsible participation, using Emancipation as case study; rights can be violated and must be protected.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: How restriction of rights hinders responsible participation, using Emancipation as case study; rights can be violated and must be protected. Give one real example from your own life or from the Jamaica you live in, and name the lesson's key words: get, up, stand, up. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: How restriction of rights hinders responsible participation, using Emancipation as case study; rights can be violated and must be protected.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
