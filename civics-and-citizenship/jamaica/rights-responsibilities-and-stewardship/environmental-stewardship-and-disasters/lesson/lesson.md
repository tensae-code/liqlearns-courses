# Environmental Stewardship and Disasters

## Do it first

Look around your home or school and name one way people care for the environment — and one danger, like a hurricane, you must prepare for. This lesson is about that care and that preparation.

Responsibilities toward the environment, preparing for natural disasters such as hurricanes, and protecting community natural resources.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Responsibilities toward the environment, preparing for natural disasters such as hurricanes, and protecting community natural resources. Give one real example from your own life or from the Jamaica you live in, and name the lesson's key words: environmental, stewardship, disasters. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: Responsibilities toward the environment, preparing for natural disasters such as hurricanes, and protecting community natural resources.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
