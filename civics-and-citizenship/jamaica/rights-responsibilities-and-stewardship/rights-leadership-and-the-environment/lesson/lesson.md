# Rights Leadership and the Environment

## Do it first

Name one right you hold and one way a leader should behave. This lesson joins rights, leadership and care for the environment into one responsibility.

Your rights with responsible behaviour, recognising and responding to rights violations, leadership and governance practices for the greater good, and sustainable environmental practice.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Your rights with responsible behaviour, recognising and responding to rights violations, leadership and governance practices for the greater good, and sustainable environmental practice. Give one real example from your own life or from the Jamaica you live in, and name the lesson's key words: rights, leadership, environment. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: Your rights with responsible behaviour, recognising and responding to rights violations, leadership and governance practices for the greater good, and sustainable environmental practice.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
