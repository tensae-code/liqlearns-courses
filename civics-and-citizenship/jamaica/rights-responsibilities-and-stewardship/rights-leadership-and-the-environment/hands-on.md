# Hands-on — Rights Leadership and the Environment

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name one right you hold and one way a leader should behave. This lesson joins rights, leadership and care for the environment into one responsibility.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Your rights with responsible behaviour, recognising and responding to rights violations, leadership and governance practices for the greater good, and sustainable environmental practice. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
