# Rights and Responsibilities of the Child

## Do it first

Name one right you have as a child and one responsibility that goes with it. This lesson puts the two together.

Children's rights and their protection (with the Ananda Alert project), consequences of violating rights, obeying rules and laws, and making informed decisions.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Children's rights and their protection (with the Ananda Alert project), consequences of violating rights, obeying rules and laws, and making informed decisions. Give one real example from your own life or from the Jamaica you live in, and name the lesson's key words: rights, responsibilities, child. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: Children's rights and their protection (with the Ananda Alert project), consequences of violating rights, obeying rules and laws, and making informed decisions.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
