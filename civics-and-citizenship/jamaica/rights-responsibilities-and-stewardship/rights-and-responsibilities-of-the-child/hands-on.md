# Hands-on — Rights and Responsibilities of the Child

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name one right you have as a child and one responsibility that goes with it. This lesson puts the two together.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Children's rights and their protection (with the Ananda Alert project), consequences of violating rights, obeying rules and laws, and making informed decisions. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
