# Unity in the Community Social Justice

## Do it first

Name one thing in your community that is not fair, and one gift of yours that could help. This lesson is about social justice — and using your gifts in a crisis.

The elements, principles and components of social justice, explored through the arts, and using your gifts to help others in times of crisis.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The elements, principles and components of social justice, explored through the arts, and using your gifts to help others in times of crisis. Give one real example from your own life or from the Jamaica you live in, and name the lesson's key words: unity, community, social, justice. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: The elements, principles and components of social justice, explored through the arts, and using your gifts to help others in times of crisis.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
