# Hands-on — Unity in the Community Social Justice

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name one thing in your community that is not fair, and one gift of yours that could help. This lesson is about social justice — and using your gifts in a crisis.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: The elements, principles and components of social justice, explored through the arts, and using your gifts to help others in times of crisis. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
