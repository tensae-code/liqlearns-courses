# One Han' Caan Clap — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Respecting your neighbour's rights — collaboration, cooperation, unity, fairness and equity, and areas of regional integration in the Caribbean.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that respecting your neighbour's rights — collaboration, cooperation, unity, fairness and equity, and areas of regional integration in the Caribbean. — and that it has no connection to the rest of Jamaica's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Jamaica's own course, in its own order.
