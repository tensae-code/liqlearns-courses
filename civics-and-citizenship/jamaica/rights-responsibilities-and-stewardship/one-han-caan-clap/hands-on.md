# Hands-on — One Han' Caan Clap

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Say the proverb 'One han' caan clap' in your own words. This lesson is about your neighbour's rights — and why no one achieves anything alone.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Respecting your neighbour's rights — collaboration, cooperation, unity, fairness and equity, and areas of regional integration in the Caribbean. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
