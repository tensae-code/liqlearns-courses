# One Han' Caan Clap

## Do it first

Say the proverb 'One han' caan clap' in your own words. This lesson is about your neighbour's rights — and why no one achieves anything alone.

Respecting your neighbour's rights — collaboration, cooperation, unity, fairness and equity, and areas of regional integration in the Caribbean.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Respecting your neighbour's rights — collaboration, cooperation, unity, fairness and equity, and areas of regional integration in the Caribbean. Give one real example from your own life or from the Jamaica you live in, and name the lesson's key words: one, han, caan, clap. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: Respecting your neighbour's rights — collaboration, cooperation, unity, fairness and equity, and areas of regional integration in the Caribbean.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
