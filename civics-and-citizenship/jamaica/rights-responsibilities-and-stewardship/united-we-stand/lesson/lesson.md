# United We Stand

## Do it first

Name one environmental problem the whole Caribbean shares. This lesson — United We Stand, Divided We Fall — is about character, integrity and protecting the region's environment together.

Character and integrity through common Caribbean environmental problems and the role of environmental protection agencies in the region.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Character and integrity through common Caribbean environmental problems and the role of environmental protection agencies in the region. Give one real example from your own life or from the Jamaica you live in, and name the lesson's key words: united, we, stand. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: Character and integrity through common Caribbean environmental problems and the role of environmental protection agencies in the region.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
