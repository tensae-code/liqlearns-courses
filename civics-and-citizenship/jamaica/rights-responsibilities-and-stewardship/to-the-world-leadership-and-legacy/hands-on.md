# Hands-on — To the World Leadership and Legacy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name one quality of a wholesome leader. This lesson — To the World — is about leadership and legacy, taught through Marcus Garvey and the challenges of nation-building.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: The responsibilities of leaders such as Marcus Garvey, the challenges of nation-building and of wholesome leadership at local, national and international level. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
