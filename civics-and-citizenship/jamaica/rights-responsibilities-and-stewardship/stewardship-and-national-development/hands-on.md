# Hands-on — Stewardship and National Development

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name one small act of care that helps your whole country. This lesson shows how one person's stewardship and civic pride build national development.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: How individual stewardship and civic pride affect the cultural and national environment and national development, including ethical behaviour and safety with technology. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
