# Hands-on — Wake Up and Live

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name one National Hero of Jamaica. This lesson — Wake Up and Live — teaches rights and responsibility through Paul Bogle and George William Gordon.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Rights, violations and responsible participation through the case studies of National Heroes Paul Bogle and George William Gordon. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
