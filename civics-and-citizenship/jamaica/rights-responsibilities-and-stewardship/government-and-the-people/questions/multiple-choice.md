# Government and the People — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The interdependent relationship between government and the people ("before you have a government you must have the people"), civic pride, care for property and coexisting.  b) it has nothing to do with how Jamaica governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations

**Answer: a) The interdependent relationship between government and the people ("before you have a government you must have the people"), civic pride, care for property and coexisting.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
