# Government and the People — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The interdependent relationship between government and the people ("before you have a government you must have the people"), civic pride, care for property and coexisting.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that the interdependent relationship between government and the people ("before you have a government you must have the people"), civic pride, care for property and coexisting. — and that it has no connection to the rest of Jamaica's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Jamaica's own course, in its own order.
