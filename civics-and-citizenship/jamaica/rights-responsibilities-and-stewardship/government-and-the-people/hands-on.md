# Hands-on — Government and the People

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Say in one sentence what a government is for. This lesson starts from Jamaica's own order: before you have a government, you must have the people.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: The interdependent relationship between government and the people ("before you have a government you must have the people"), civic pride, care for property and coexisting. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
