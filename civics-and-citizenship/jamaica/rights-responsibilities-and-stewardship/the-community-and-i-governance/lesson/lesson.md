# The Community and I Governance

## Do it first

Name one decision in your school or community that was made well, and say why. This lesson — The Community and I — is about good governance and your share in it.

Good governance and citizenship, collective responsibility, social justice and peace, and contributing to governance in school, community and region.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Good governance and citizenship, collective responsibility, social justice and peace, and contributing to governance in school, community and region. Give one real example from your own life or from the Jamaica you live in, and name the lesson's key words: community, governance. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: Good governance and citizenship, collective responsibility, social justice and peace, and contributing to governance in school, community and region.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
