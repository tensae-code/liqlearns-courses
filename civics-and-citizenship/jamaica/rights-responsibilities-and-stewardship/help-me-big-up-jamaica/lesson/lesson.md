# Help Me Big Up Jamaica

## Do it first

Name one thing Alexander Bustamante or Norman Manley did for Jamaica. This lesson — Help Me Big Up Jamaica — is about their contributions and keeping their legacies alive.

The contributions of National Heroes Bustamante and Manley to national development, preserving their legacies, and using national colours and symbols to promote development.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The contributions of National Heroes Bustamante and Manley to national development, preserving their legacies, and using national colours and symbols to promote development. Give one real example from your own life or from the Jamaica you live in, and name the lesson's key words: help, big, up, jamaica. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: The contributions of National Heroes Bustamante and Manley to national development, preserving their legacies, and using national colours and symbols to promote development.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
