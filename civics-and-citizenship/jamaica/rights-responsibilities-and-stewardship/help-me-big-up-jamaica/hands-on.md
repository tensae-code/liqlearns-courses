# Hands-on — Help Me Big Up Jamaica

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name one thing Alexander Bustamante or Norman Manley did for Jamaica. This lesson — Help Me Big Up Jamaica — is about their contributions and keeping their legacies alive.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: The contributions of National Heroes Bustamante and Manley to national development, preserving their legacies, and using national colours and symbols to promote development. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
