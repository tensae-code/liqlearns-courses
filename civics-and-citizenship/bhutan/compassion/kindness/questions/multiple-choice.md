# Kindness — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Being kind in word and act  b) it has nothing to do with how Bhutan governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Being kind in word and act** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Being kind in word and act  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Bhutan
**Answer: a) Being kind in word and act** — That is what this lesson teaches.
