# The Executive — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The executive and its responsibilities  b) it has nothing to do with how Bhutan governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The executive and its responsibilities** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) The executive and its responsibilities  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Bhutan
**Answer: a) The executive and its responsibilities** — That is what this lesson teaches.
