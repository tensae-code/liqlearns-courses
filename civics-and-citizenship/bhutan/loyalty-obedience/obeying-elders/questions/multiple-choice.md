# Obeying Elders — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Obedience to parents, teachers and elders as loyalty  b) it has nothing to do with how Bhutan governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Obedience to parents, teachers and elders as loyalty** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Obedience to parents, teachers and elders as loyalty  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Bhutan
**Answer: a) Obedience to parents, teachers and elders as loyalty** — That is what this lesson teaches.
