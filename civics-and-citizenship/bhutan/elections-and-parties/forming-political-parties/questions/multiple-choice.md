# Forming Political Parties — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) How political parties are formed and what they do  b) it has nothing to do with how Bhutan governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) How political parties are formed and what they do** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) How political parties are formed and what they do  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Bhutan
**Answer: a) How political parties are formed and what they do** — That is what this lesson teaches.
