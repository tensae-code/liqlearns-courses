# The Aims of Learning Respect

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: The Aims of Learning Respect. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Bhutan's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

The child will express the need to value and appreciate respect for other people, the importance of respecting elders, parents and teachers, and give examples of respectful speech, acts and behaviours at home, in public places, in the community and in schools

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The child will express the need to value and appreciate respect for other people, the importance of respecting elders, parents and teachers, and give examples of respectful speech, acts and behaviours at home, in public places, in the community and in schools Give one real example from your own life or from the Bhutan you live in, and name the lesson's key words: the, the aims of learning respect. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
