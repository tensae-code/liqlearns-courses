# The Aims of Learning Respect — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The child will express the need to value and appreciate respect for other people, the importance of respecting elders, parents and teachers, and give examples of respectful speech, acts and behaviours at home, in public places, in the community and in schools  b) it has nothing to do with how Bhutan governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The child will express the need to value and appreciate respect for other people, the importance of respecting elders, parents and teachers, and give examples of respectful speech, acts and behaviours at home, in public places, in the community and in schools** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) The child will express the need to value and appreciate respect for other people, the importance of respecting elders, parents and teachers, and give examples of respectful speech, acts and behaviours at home, in public places, in the community and in schools  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Bhutan
**Answer: a) The child will express the need to value and appreciate respect for other people, the importance of respecting elders, parents and teachers, and give examples of respectful speech, acts and behaviours at home, in public places, in the community and in schools** — That is what this lesson teaches.
