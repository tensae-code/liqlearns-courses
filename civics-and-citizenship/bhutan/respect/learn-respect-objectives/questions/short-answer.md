# The Aims of Learning Respect — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** The child will express the need to value and appreciate respect for other people, the importance of respecting elders, parents and teachers, and give examples of respectful speech, acts and behaviours at home, in public places, in the community and in schools
