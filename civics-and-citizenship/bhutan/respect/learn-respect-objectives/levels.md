# The Aims of Learning Respect — depth

This lesson is ONE lesson with no sub lessons: it teaches a single thing, whole. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: The child will express the need to value and appreciate respect for other people, the importance of respecting elders, parents and teachers, and give examples of respectful speech, acts and behaviours at home, in public places, in the community and in schools — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
