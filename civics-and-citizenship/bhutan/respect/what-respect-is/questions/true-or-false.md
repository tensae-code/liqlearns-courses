# What Respect Is — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Respect is a basic moral value — being polite in speech, courteous to others, admiring people of integrity and courage; we respect parents for their care, teachers for their teaching, and the school cleaner for his or her contribution
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that What Respect Is — and that it has no connection to the rest of Bhutan's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Bhutan's own course, in its own order.
