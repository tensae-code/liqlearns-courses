# What Respect Is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Respect is a basic moral value — being polite in speech, courteous to others, admiring people of integrity and courage; we respect parents for their care, teachers for their teaching, and the school cleaner for his or her contribution  b) it has nothing to do with how Bhutan governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Respect is a basic moral value — being polite in speech, courteous to others, admiring people of integrity and courage; we respect parents for their care, teachers for their teaching, and the school cleaner for his or her contribution** — That is what this lesson teaches.
