# What Respect Is — depth

This lesson is ONE lesson with no sub lessons: it teaches a single thing, whole. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: Respect is a basic moral value — being polite in speech, courteous to others, admiring people of integrity and courage; we respect parents for their care, teachers for their teaching, and the school cleaner for his or her contribution — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
