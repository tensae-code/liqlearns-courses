# Lesson plan — Values Beyond the Classroom

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Bhutan · **Title:** Values Foundations · **Lesson:** Values Beyond the Classroom
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — Values at Home and School (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Values are promoted both through separate values-education instructional lessons and through cross-curricular/integrated lessons, so the values are lived across the school day, not only in the VE period This lesson stands on a teacher guide alone — teacher-guide evidence only, acceptance 0, labelled as such (see lesson/sources.md).
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Before this lesson
- Stands in path order after **How a Values Lesson Is Built** — do that lesson first if this one feels hard (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Values are promoted both through separate values-education instructional lessons and through cross-curricular/integrated lessons, so the values are lived across the school day, not only in the VE period
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, school or town — or, for this book's lessons, from the public life of Bhutan they live in.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the learner's own life in Bhutan and the book's own teaching, as the book itself works.
