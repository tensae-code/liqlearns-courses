# Hands-on — Values and Gross National Happiness

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Values and Gross National Happiness. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Bhutan's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Values define the self-beliefs that guide and motivate a person's actions, behaviour, thoughts and speech (Gilligan 1993; Schwartz 1994 cited); values education is a core aspect of education and of the Gross National Happiness philosophy, and schools lay the foundation for balanced outcomes of head, hands and heart Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
