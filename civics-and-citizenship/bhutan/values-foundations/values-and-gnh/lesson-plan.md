# Lesson plan — Values and Gross National Happiness

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Bhutan · **Title:** Values Foundations · **Lesson:** Values and Gross National Happiness
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — Values at Home and School (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Values define the self-beliefs that guide and motivate a person's actions, behaviour, thoughts and speech (Gilligan 1993; Schwartz 1994 cited); values education is a core aspect of education and of the Gross National Happiness philosophy, and schools lay the foundation for balanced outcomes of head, hands and heart This lesson stands on a teacher guide alone — teacher-guide evidence only, acceptance 0, labelled as such (see lesson/sources.md).
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.
