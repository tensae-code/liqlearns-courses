# Values and Gross National Happiness

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Values and Gross National Happiness. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Bhutan's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.
