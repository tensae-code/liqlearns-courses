# About the Civics and Citizenship — Bhutan course

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in
this course comes from government textbooks — this country's
own Values and Citizenship Education and Bhutan Civics
books.
The one exception is the **Tip boxes**: a tip in a box is
from us, the people making LiqLearns. Tips are ours; the
lessons are the books'.

## How this course is organised

This is ONE course for Bhutan inside the Civics and
Citizenship folder (one course per country). Inside it, the
organisation is by **levels, not grades**: a learner's place
is found by what they prove, and each level stands on the
ones before it as a recommended-first path — never a forced
gate. The school grades the books come from appear only in
each lesson's `lesson/sources.md`, as provenance.

Bhutan's own way, preserved whole, as filed: citizenship
begins as five core values — Respect, Responsibility,
Compassion, Integrity, Loyalty — practised by a young child
beyond the classroom: each VCE lesson pairs a story or poem
with a "value practice" the child ticks off against their
own current behaviour, and the same five-value ladder
repeats at every class (PP, I, II, III) with new stories, so
a value is revisited yearly at a higher level. The teachers'
handbooks ground the whole subject in Gross National
Happiness — values education is named in the Class III
handbook as "one of the core aspects of education and the
philosophy of GNH", aiming at the balanced growth of head,
hands and heart. The senior civics is taught as the working
of Bhutan's democratic constitutional monarchy —
constitution first, then parties and elections, then the
organs of government, closing with Gross National Happiness
as a governance principle — and the Classes XI–XII book
turns theoretical (society, state and nation; forms of
government) before returning to Bhutan's own settlement: a
democratic constitutional monarchy with an apolitical civil
service.

The course as built has **five stocked levels**, from the
two filings for this build (`bhutan-placements.md` — 6
records / 6 distinct books, of which 2 read and 4 UNREAD;
and `bhutan-placements-b.md` — 35 link leads triaged / 10

**Course-intro video script** (written here, made later;
the books' own shape, walked in order):

- *Bhutan's own order:* "First, values at home and school —
  what values are and how they join Gross National
  Happiness, the five values and their parts, and then the
  values themselves practised week by week: respect,
  courtesy and gratitude; punctuality, determination and
  duty; generosity, friendliness, sharing, care and
  kindness; honesty and trustworthiness; obedience growing
  into loyalty. Then the Constitution, rights and duties —
  how Bhutan's Constitution was made, its features, and the
  fundamental rights and duties of citizens. Then elections
  and parties — how parties form and how elections run.
  Then the government — Parliament, the executive, the
  judiciary, the constitutional bodies, local government,
  and Gross National Happiness with good governance. Last,
  the state, monarchy and public service — society, state
  and nation, forms of government, what a constitution is,
  the monarch's role, the Prime Minister and the Council of
  Ministers, the principles of state policy, and the civil
  service."
