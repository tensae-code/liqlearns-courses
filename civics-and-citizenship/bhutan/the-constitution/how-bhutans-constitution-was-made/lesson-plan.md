**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — The Constitution, Rights and Duties (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The evolution of the monarchy, devolution of power in 1998, public consultation and the making of the Constitution This lesson stands on a student book (some of its parts are teacher-guide evidence — see lesson/sources.md).
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. ## Before this lesson

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: The evolution of the monarchy, devolution of power in 1998, public consultation and the making of the Constitution
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, school or town — or, for this book's lessons, from the public life of Bhutan they live in.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the learner's own life in Bhutan and the book's own teaching, as the book itself works.
