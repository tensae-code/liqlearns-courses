# Civics and Citizenship — Bhutan course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

Civics belongs to the country: this course is Bhutan's
own — built from Bhutan's Values and Citizenship
Education workbooks and handbooks for Classes PP–III
(Royal Education Council, 2017) and its Bhutan Civics
coursebooks and teachers' guides for Classes IX & X and
XI & XII (DCPD, Ministry of Education, 2022 reprints),
all English, all COMPLETE as filed. Inside the course
the organisation is by **levels, not grades**: the titles
below ARE the levels' titles, in path order — what the
learner proves, step by step. The grades the books come
from live only in each lesson's sources.

The path's own logic is Bhutan's, from the filings'
ladder sketches, reconciled into one ladder: the VCE
books teach five core values — Respect, Responsibility,
Compassion, Integrity, Loyalty — as weekly practice for
the youngest children, grounded in Gross National
Happiness; the Classes IX–X coursebook then opens the
citizen's machinery with the Constitution, rights and
duties, moves to elections and parties, then to the
working government (legislature, executive, judiciary,
constitutional bodies, local government, GNH and good
governance); and the Classes XI–XII coursebook closes
with the theory of the state, the monarch's role, the
Prime Minister and Council of Ministers, state policy
principles and the civil service. That ladder IS the
path order (recommended, never forced — a learner can
jump in higher; the "start here instead" hints point
down this ladder).

The coined lesson names in the filings were deduped
before building, by lesson name: the two filings' unit
names for the same strand were merged (the first
filing's `integrity/` and `loyalty/` are the second
filing's `integrity-honesty/` and `loyalty-obedience/`),
and every placement stocks into exactly one lesson —
124 placements stocking 50 lessons. Each placement's
provenance row (book + printed page, exactly as filed)
is in that lesson's `lesson/sources.md`. Unit folders
are the filings' own theme names; a theme can run
through several levels, as Bhutan's own books return to
the same themes class by class.
