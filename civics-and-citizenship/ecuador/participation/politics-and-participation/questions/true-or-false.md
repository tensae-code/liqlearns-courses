# Politics and Participation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Politics is the field citizens act in together, and participation is how they act in it: the guide teaches politics and participation as one subject.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that politics and participation — and that it has no connection to the rest of Ecuador's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Ecuador's own course, in its own order.
