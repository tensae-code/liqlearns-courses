# Coexistence and Conflict

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: coexistence and conflict. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Ecuador's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Coexistence is living with others, and conflict belongs to it: the guide teaches living together through case-study work on living with others and handling conflict.
