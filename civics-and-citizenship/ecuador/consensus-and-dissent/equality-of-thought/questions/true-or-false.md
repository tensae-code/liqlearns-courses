# Equality of Thought — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Consensus and dissent begin with respecting how others think: the right to equality guarantees respect for different ways of thinking.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that equality of thought — and that it has no connection to the rest of Ecuador's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Ecuador's own course, in its own order.
