# Equality of Thought — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Consensus and dissent begin with respecting how others think: the right to equality guarantees respect for different ways of thinking.  b) it has nothing to do with how Ecuador governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Consensus and dissent begin with respecting how others think: the right to equality guarantees respect for different ways of thinking.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
