# Building Consensus

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: building consensus. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Ecuador's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Consensus is built in everyday spaces: the book's challenge is to generate consensus in your own class on something that helps.
