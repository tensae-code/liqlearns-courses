# Citizen Deliberation

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: citizen deliberation. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Ecuador's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Citizen deliberation is a democratic process and a mechanism that legitimises political power socially: power decided through citizens' reasoning carries social legitimacy.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Citizen deliberation is a democratic process and a mechanism that legitimises political power socially: power decided through citizens' reasoning carries social legitimacy. Give one real example from your own life or from the Ecuador you live in, and name the lesson's key words: citizen, deliberation. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
