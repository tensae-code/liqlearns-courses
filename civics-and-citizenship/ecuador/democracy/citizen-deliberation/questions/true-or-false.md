# Citizen Deliberation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Citizen deliberation is a democratic process and a mechanism that legitimises political power socially: power decided through citizens' reasoning carries social legitimacy.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that citizen deliberation — and that it has no connection to the rest of Ecuador's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Ecuador's own course, in its own order.
