# The Citizen in Modern Democracies

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: the citizen in modern democracies. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Ecuador's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## Sub lesson 1 — The direct citizen

In direct democracy the citizen decides: the role is to take part in the decision itself.

## Sub lesson 2 — The representative citizen

In representative democracy the citizen chooses and oversees: the role is to elect representatives and hold them to account.

## Sub lesson 3 — The deliberative citizen

In deliberative democracy the citizen reasons in public: the role is to deliberate — to argue, listen and decide together.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The citizen has a role in each modern democracy — direct, representative and deliberative — and the role differs with the form. Give one real example from your own life or from the Ecuador you live in, and name the lesson's key words: citizen, modern. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
