# Consensus and Dissent — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Social consensus and dissent are worked out through deliberation in everyday spaces, and that deliberation is itself an exercise of citizenship.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that consensus and dissent — and that it has no connection to the rest of Ecuador's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Ecuador's own course, in its own order.
