# Consensus and Dissent

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: consensus and dissent. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Ecuador's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Social consensus and dissent are worked out through deliberation in everyday spaces, and that deliberation is itself an exercise of citizenship.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Social consensus and dissent are worked out through deliberation in everyday spaces, and that deliberation is itself an exercise of citizenship. Give one real example from your own life or from the Ecuador you live in, and name the lesson's key words: consensus, dissent. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
