# The Universal Declaration of Human Rights — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Human rights are the rights every person holds, proclaimed for all in the Universal Declaration of Human Rights of 10 December 1948 — yet violations persist despite it, and the book sets the learner to analyse a real case of a rights violation.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that the universal declaration of human rights — and that it has no connection to the rest of Ecuador's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Ecuador's own course, in its own order.
