# Equality of Citizens — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Citizen equality is the natural and juridical condition of the human being: equal by nature, and held equal by law.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that equality of citizens — and that it has no connection to the rest of Ecuador's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Ecuador's own course, in its own order.
