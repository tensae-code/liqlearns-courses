# Equality of Citizens — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Citizen equality is the natural and juridical condition of the human being: equal by nature, and held equal by law.  b) it has nothing to do with how Ecuador governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Citizen equality is the natural and juridical condition of the human being: equal by nature, and held equal by law.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
