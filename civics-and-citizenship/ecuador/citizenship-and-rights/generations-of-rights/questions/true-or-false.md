# Generations of Rights — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Rights came in generations: civil and political rights first, then social rights, then the rights of solidarity, and a newest generation after them.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that generations of rights — and that it has no connection to the rest of Ecuador's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Ecuador's own course, in its own order.
