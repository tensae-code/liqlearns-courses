# Milestones Toward Human Rights — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The events that led to the declaration of human rights form a chain: the Carta Magna of 1215, the Declaration of Rights of 1689, United States independence in 1776, and the French Revolution of 1789.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that milestones toward human rights — and that it has no connection to the rest of Ecuador's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Ecuador's own course, in its own order.
