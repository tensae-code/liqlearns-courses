# Milestones Toward Human Rights

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: milestones toward human rights. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Ecuador's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## Sub lesson 1 — The early limits on power

The Carta Magna of 1215 and the Declaration of Rights of 1689 each limited a ruler's power and wrote down rights that could be claimed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Independence and revolution

United States independence in 1776 and the French Revolution of 1789 carried the claim further: rights belong to people, and peoples may govern themselves.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The events that led to the declaration of human rights form a chain: the Carta Magna of 1215, the Declaration of Rights of 1689, United States independence in 1776, and the French Revolution of 1789. Give one real example from your own life or from the Ecuador you live in, and name the lesson's key words: milestones, toward. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
