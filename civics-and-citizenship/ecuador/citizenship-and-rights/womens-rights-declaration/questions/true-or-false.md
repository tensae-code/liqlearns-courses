# The Women's Rights Declaration — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Women took part in the French Revolution yet their rights were not recognised — the Declaration of the Rights of Woman and of the Citizen answered that exclusion.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that the women's rights declaration — and that it has no connection to the rest of Ecuador's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Ecuador's own course, in its own order.
