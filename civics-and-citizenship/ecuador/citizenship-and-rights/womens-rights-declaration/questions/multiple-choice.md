# The Women's Rights Declaration — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Women took part in the French Revolution yet their rights were not recognised — the Declaration of the Rights of Woman and of the Citizen answered that exclusion.  b) it has nothing to do with how Ecuador governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
