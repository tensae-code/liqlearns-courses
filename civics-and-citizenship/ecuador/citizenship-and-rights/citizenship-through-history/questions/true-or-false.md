# Citizenship Through History — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Citizenship and human rights have a historical, social and political evolution: from the armed citizen of Athens through Magna Carta, 1776 and 1789, to the generations of rights.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that citizenship through history — and that it has no connection to the rest of Ecuador's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Ecuador's own course, in its own order.
