# Government and Media Power — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Government administers the State's resources, and power over public attention has shifted: social networks have taken power from the traditional media.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that government and media power — and that it has no connection to the rest of Ecuador's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Ecuador's own course, in its own order.
