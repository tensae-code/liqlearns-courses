# Forms of Government Compared — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Democracy differs from aristocracy and monarchy in who holds power: in a democracy the citizens hold it, in an aristocracy a few hold it, and in a monarchy one person holds it.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that forms of government compared — and that it has no connection to the rest of Ecuador's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Ecuador's own course, in its own order.
