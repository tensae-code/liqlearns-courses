# Forms of Government Compared — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** Democracy differs from aristocracy and monarchy in who holds power: in a democracy the citizens hold it, in an aristocracy a few hold it, and in a monarchy one person holds it.
