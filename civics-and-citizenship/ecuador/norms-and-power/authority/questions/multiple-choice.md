# Authority — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Authority is legitimate power: the guide teaches authority against mere power — power that is recognised as rightful.  b) it has nothing to do with how Ecuador governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Authority is legitimate power: the guide teaches authority against mere power — power that is recognised as rightful.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
