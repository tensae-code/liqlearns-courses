# Authority

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: authority. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Ecuador's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Authority is legitimate power: the guide teaches authority against mere power — power that is recognised as rightful.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Authority is legitimate power: the guide teaches authority against mere power — power that is recognised as rightful. Give one real example from your own life or from the Ecuador you live in, and name the lesson's key words: authority, ecuador. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
