# Norms

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: norms. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Ecuador's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Norms are the social and legal rules that order life together, and they bind: the guide teaches what norms are and why they hold.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Norms are the social and legal rules that order life together, and they bind: the guide teaches what norms are and why they hold. Give one real example from your own life or from the Ecuador you live in, and name the lesson's key words: norms, ecuador. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
