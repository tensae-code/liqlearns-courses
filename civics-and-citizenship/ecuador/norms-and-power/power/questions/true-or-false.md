# Power — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Power takes forms and is exercised over others: the guide teaches what power is, its forms, and how it is exercised.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that power — and that it has no connection to the rest of Ecuador's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Ecuador's own course, in its own order.
