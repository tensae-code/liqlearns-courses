# The Buen Vivir Regime — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Buen Vivir regime — Sumak Kawsay — is Ecuador's constitutional order of living well, in harmony with community and nature.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that the buen vivir regime — and that it has no connection to the rest of Ecuador's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Ecuador's own course, in its own order.
