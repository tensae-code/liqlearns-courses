# Participation in School Life — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Citizens practise participation at school through student governments: school life is a first field of citizen participation.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that participation in school life — and that it has no connection to the rest of Ecuador's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Ecuador's own course, in its own order.
