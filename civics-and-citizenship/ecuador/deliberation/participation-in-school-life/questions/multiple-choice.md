Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Citizens practise participation at school through student governments: school life is a first field of citizen participation.  b) it has nothing to do with how Ecuador governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
