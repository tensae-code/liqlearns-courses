# Deliberative and Social Democracy — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Deliberative democracy decides through public reasoning, and social democracy empowers citizens — it puts real capacity to take part in citizens' hands.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that deliberative and social democracy — and that it has no connection to the rest of Ecuador's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Ecuador's own course, in its own order.
