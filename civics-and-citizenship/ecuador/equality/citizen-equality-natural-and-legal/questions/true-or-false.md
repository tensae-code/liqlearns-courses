# Citizen Equality, Natural and Legal — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Citizen equality is a natural and a legal condition of the human being: people are equal by nature, and the law recognises and holds that equality.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that citizen equality, natural and legal — and that it has no connection to the rest of Ecuador's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Ecuador's own course, in its own order.
