# Citizen Equality, Natural and Legal

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: citizen equality, natural and legal. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Ecuador's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Citizen equality is a natural and a legal condition of the human being: people are equal by nature, and the law recognises and holds that equality.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Citizen equality is a natural and a legal condition of the human being: people are equal by nature, and the law recognises and holds that equality. Give one real example from your own life or from the Ecuador you live in, and name the lesson's key words: citizen, equality. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
