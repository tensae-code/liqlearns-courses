# Parity and the Optional Vote — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Ecuador's constitution guarantees parity — the paritary representation of women and men (Article 65) — and sets who may choose whether to vote (Article 62: optional vote for ages 16–18, people over 65, military, police, and persons with disability).
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that parity and the optional vote — and that it has no connection to the rest of Ecuador's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Ecuador's own course, in its own order.
