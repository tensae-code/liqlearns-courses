# Parity and the Optional Vote

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: parity and the optional vote. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Ecuador's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## Sub lesson 1 — Parity

Article 65 of the Constitution guarantees parity: women and men are represented on equal terms in the bodies citizens elect.

## Sub lesson 2 — The optional vote

Article 62 sets who may choose whether to vote: young people aged 16 to 18, people over 65, members of the military and police, and persons with disability.
