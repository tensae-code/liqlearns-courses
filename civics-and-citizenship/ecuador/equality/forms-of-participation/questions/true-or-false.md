# Forms of Participation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A citizen participates in many ways: voting, joining a political party, taking part in social organisations, peaceful protest, and contacting representatives.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that forms of participation — and that it has no connection to the rest of Ecuador's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Ecuador's own course, in its own order.
