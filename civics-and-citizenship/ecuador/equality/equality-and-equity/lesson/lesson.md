# Equality and Equity

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: equality and equity. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Ecuador's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## Sub lesson 1 — Equality and equity

Equality treats people as equals; equity asks what each person actually needs and faces, so treating different situations identically can keep them unequal.

## Sub lesson 2 — Formal and material equality

Formal equality is equality before the law as written; material equality is equality in real conditions — in income, schooling, health and opportunity.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Equality is not equity: formal equality is equality before the law, while material equality is equality of real conditions — and equity answers real conditions, not only the written rule. Give one real example from your own life or from the Ecuador you live in, and name the lesson's key words: equality, equity. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
