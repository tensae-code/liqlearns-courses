# Equality and Equity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Equality is not equity: formal equality is equality before the law, while material equality is equality of real conditions — and equity answers real conditions, not only the written rule.  b) it has nothing to do with how Ecuador governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
