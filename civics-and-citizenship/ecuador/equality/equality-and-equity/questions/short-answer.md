# Equality and Equity — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** Equality is not equity: formal equality is equality before the law, while material equality is equality of real conditions — and equity answers real conditions, not only the written rule.
