# The National State and Social Control

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: the national state and social control. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Ecuador's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

The national state has a political organisation and functions, and mechanisms of social control that hold it to its course.

## Say it to your companion

Give one real example from your own life or from the Ecuador you live in, and name the lesson's key words: national, state. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
