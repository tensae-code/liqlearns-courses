# Republicanism and Other Forms — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Democratic organisation takes the republican form, and republicanism is understood in contrast with other forms of political organisation.  b) it has nothing to do with how Ecuador governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Democratic organisation takes the republican form, and republicanism is understood in contrast with other forms of political organisation.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Democratic organisation takes the republican form, and republicanism is understood in contrast with other forms of political organisation.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Ecuador
**Answer: a) Democratic organisation takes the republican form, and republicanism is understood in contrast with other forms of political organisation.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
