# First Elections and Accountability — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Ecuador's first presidential elections were held in 1830–31, in the time of Rocafuerte and Flores, and accountability is a citizen right: Article 88 of the Constitution gives the citizen the right to demand rendición de cuentas — an account — from power.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that first elections and accountability — and that it has no connection to the rest of Ecuador's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Ecuador's own course, in its own order.
