# First Elections and Accountability — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** Ecuador's first presidential elections were held in 1830–31, in the time of Rocafuerte and Flores, and accountability is a citizen right: Article 88 of the Constitution gives the citizen the right to demand rendición de cuentas — an account — from power.
