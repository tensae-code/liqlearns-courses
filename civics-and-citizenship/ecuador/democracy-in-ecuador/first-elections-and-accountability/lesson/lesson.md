# First Elections and Accountability

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: first elections and accountability. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Ecuador's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## Sub lesson 1 — The first elections

Ecuador's first presidential elections, in 1830–31, belong to the republic's founding years of Rocafuerte and Flores.

## Sub lesson 2 — Accountability as a right

Article 88 makes accountability a citizen's right: a citizen may demand that those who hold power render an account of what they have done with it.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Ecuador's first presidential elections were held in 1830–31, in the time of Rocafuerte and Flores, and accountability is a citizen right: Article 88 of the Constitution gives the citizen the right to demand rendición de cuentas — an account — from power. Give one real example from your own life or from the Ecuador you live in, and name the lesson's key words: first, elections. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
