# History and Exclusion — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Ecuador's democracy grew through exclusion and struggle: its historical evolution is marked by social processes and political limitations, and by the exclusion of social groups from effective participation.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that history and exclusion — and that it has no connection to the rest of Ecuador's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Ecuador's own course, in its own order.
