# The Intercultural Society — Building a Real Democracy — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The intercultural society is the construction of a real democracy in a plurinational country: democracy is real when every nationality and culture in the country counts in it.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that the intercultural society — building a real democracy — and that it has no connection to the rest of Ecuador's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Ecuador's own course, in its own order.
