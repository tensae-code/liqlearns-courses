# The People as Legitimator of Power — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The people legitimise political power by writing constitutions: Ecuador's constituent assemblies — the first in 1830, four in all to 2008 — produced 20 constitutions.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that the people as legitimator of power — and that it has no connection to the rest of Ecuador's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Ecuador's own course, in its own order.
