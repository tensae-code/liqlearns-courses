# The People as Legitimator of Power

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: the people as legitimator of power. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Ecuador's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

The people legitimise political power by writing constitutions: Ecuador's constituent assemblies — the first in 1830, four in all to 2008 — produced 20 constitutions.
