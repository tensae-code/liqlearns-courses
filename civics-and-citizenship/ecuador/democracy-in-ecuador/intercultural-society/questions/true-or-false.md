# The Intercultural Society — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Interculturality is the base of Ecuador's democracy: Ecuador is home to more than 14 indigenous nationalities, and the 2008 Constitution recognises a pluricultural, multiethnic Ecuador — a real democracy is built on that recognition.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that the intercultural society — and that it has no connection to the rest of Ecuador's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Ecuador's own course, in its own order.
