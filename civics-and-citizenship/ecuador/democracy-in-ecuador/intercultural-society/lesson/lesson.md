# The Intercultural Society

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: the intercultural society. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Ecuador's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Interculturality is the base of Ecuador's democracy: Ecuador is home to more than 14 indigenous nationalities, and the 2008 Constitution recognises a pluricultural, multiethnic Ecuador — a real democracy is built on that recognition.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Interculturality is the base of Ecuador's democracy: Ecuador is home to more than 14 indigenous nationalities, and the 2008 Constitution recognises a pluricultural, multiethnic Ecuador — a real democracy is built on that recognition. Give one real example from your own life or from the Ecuador you live in, and name the lesson's key words: intercultural, society. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
