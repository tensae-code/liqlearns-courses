# Republicanism in Ecuador — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The republic is the people's form of government in Ecuador: republicanism runs from 1859 — the first republican president, Francisco Robles, with the republic restored in 1884 — to the democratic republic of 2008.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that republicanism in ecuador — and that it has no connection to the rest of Ecuador's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Ecuador's own course, in its own order.
