# Constitutional Rights and Guarantees

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: constitutional rights and guarantees. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Ecuador's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

The Constitution promises rights and gives guarantees: constitutional rights are what the Constitution promises, and guarantees are how the promise is enforced.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The Constitution promises rights and gives guarantees: constitutional rights are what the Constitution promises, and guarantees are how the promise is enforced. Give one real example from your own life or from the Ecuador you live in, and name the lesson's key words: constitutional, rights. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
