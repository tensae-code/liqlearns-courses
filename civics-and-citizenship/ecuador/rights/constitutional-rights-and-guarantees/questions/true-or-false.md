# Constitutional Rights and Guarantees — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Constitution promises rights and gives guarantees: constitutional rights are what the Constitution promises, and guarantees are how the promise is enforced.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that constitutional rights and guarantees — and that it has no connection to the rest of Ecuador's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Ecuador's own course, in its own order.
