# Rights and Responsibilities — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Rights and responsibilities belong together, and the guide works them through women's rights, gender stereotypes and equality between man and woman, with the Constitution of Ecuador as the reference.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that rights and responsibilities — and that it has no connection to the rest of Ecuador's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Ecuador's own course, in its own order.
