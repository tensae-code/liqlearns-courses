# Equality and Diversity — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Equality and diversity belong together: citizens are equal, and they are different — the guide teaches holding both at once.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that equality and diversity — and that it has no connection to the rest of Ecuador's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Ecuador's own course, in its own order.
