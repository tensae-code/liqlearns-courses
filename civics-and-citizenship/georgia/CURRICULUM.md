# Civics and Citizenship — Georgia course — CURRICULUM (the path)

One course for Georgia inside the Civics and Citizenship folder,
organised by **levels, not grades**: a learner climbs when they
have proved the level, not when a school calendar says so. The
school grade the book comes from (Grade 7) appears only in each
lesson's sources (provenance), never as the course's
organisation. The path follows Georgia's own book's order:
citizenship taught outwards from the community the child
actually lives in — relations, rights and participation in the
community and the school first; law, safety and tolerance
inside that circle; health, heritage and money last.

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in this
course comes from government textbooks — this country's own
Citizenship book (მოქალაქეობა) — except the **Tip boxes**: a tip
in a box is from us, the people making LiqLearns. Tips are ours;
the lessons are the books'. See ABOUT.md for the full book list
and the acceptance reckoning.

## The levels (the path, in order)

1. **The Community I Live In** — the only level the books in
   hand reach (higher Georgia grades' citizenship books are not
   in hand, so the ladder stands at one level, honestly). The
   community I live in first — relations around me, my place in
   society, participation as my right, what forms my
   personality, leadership and ethics — with rights and law
   inside that circle (human rights, law and justice, the
   person and the law, juveniles and crime) and living together
   (tolerance, diversity around me, a safe environment); then
   my school (school culture, a democratic school, we at
   school, self-assessment and confidence, communicating well,
   bullying, conflicts around us, keeping everyone safe); then
   healthy life (caring for physical health, early marriage,
   addiction); then heritage (my culture is my wealth, caring
   for nature and heritage); and last work and money (choosing
   a profession, the market, volunteering, planning resources).
   29 lessons, in seven titles: `my-community/` (5 lessons),
   `rights-and-law/` (4), `living-together/` (3), `my-school/`
   (8), `healthy-life/` (3), `heritage/` (2) and
   `work-and-money/` (4).

**Totals: 29 lessons** (Level 1: 29 — all from one student
book, COMPLETE; its teacher's book stands as evidence only —
see ABOUT.md).
