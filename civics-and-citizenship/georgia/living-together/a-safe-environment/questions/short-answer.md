# A Safe Environment — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** A safe environment can be recognised and built — at home, at school and in the street.

**2.** What does the part "Building a safe environment" teach?
**Answer:** Safety is built: by care, by rules kept together, and by speaking up when something is unsafe.
