# A Safe Environment — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) a safe environment can be recognised and built — at home, at school and in the street  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — Recognising safety and danger?
a) A safe environment is one where people are protected from harm; learning to spot danger is the first step.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
