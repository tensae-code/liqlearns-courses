# Video script — A Safe Environment

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A safe environment can be recognised and built — at home, at school and in the street.

Recognising safety and danger. A safe environment is one where people are protected from harm; learning to spot danger is the first step.

Building a safe environment. Safety is built: by care, by rules kept together, and by speaking up when something is unsafe.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
