# Hands-on — A Safe Environment

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Walk one place you know — your home, school or street — in your mind. Name one thing that keeps it safe and one thing that could make it safer.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: a safe environment can be recognised and built — at home, at school and in the street. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
