# A Safe Environment

## Do it first

Walk one place you know — your home, school or street — in your mind. Name one thing that keeps it safe and one thing that could make it safer.

## Sub lesson 1 — Recognising safety and danger

A safe environment is one where people are protected from harm; learning to spot danger is the first step.

## Sub lesson 2 — Building a safe environment

Safety is built: by care, by rules kept together, and by speaking up when something is unsafe.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: a safe environment can be recognised and built — at home, at school and in the street. Give one real example from your own life in Georgia, and name the lesson's key words: safety, environment, protection, home and school. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Georgian as well as in English — Georgia's own book teaches Citizenship (მოქალაქეობა) in Georgian, for life at home and in the community, not only for the classroom.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: a safe environment can be recognised and built — at home, at school and in the street.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
