# Video script — Tolerance

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Tolerance means respecting people whose views, faith or way of life differ from my own.

Respecting different views and faiths. People around me hold different views and faiths; tolerance respects them even when I do not share them.

Respecting different ways of life. A different way of life is not a threat: tolerance lets different people live side by side in peace.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
