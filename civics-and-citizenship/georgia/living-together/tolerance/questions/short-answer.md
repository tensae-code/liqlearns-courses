# Tolerance — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Tolerance means respecting people whose views, faith or way of life differ from my own.

**2.** What does the part "Respecting different ways of life" teach?
**Answer:** A different way of life is not a threat: tolerance lets different people live side by side in peace.
