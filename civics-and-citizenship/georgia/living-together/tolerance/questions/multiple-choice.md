# Tolerance — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) tolerance means respecting people whose views, faith or way of life differ from my own  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — Respecting different views and faiths?
a) People around me hold different views and faiths; tolerance respects them even when I do not share them.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
