# Tolerance — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** Respecting different views and faiths — a)

**2.** Respecting different ways of life — b)


Meanings:
a) People around me hold different views and faiths; tolerance respects them even when I do not share them.
b) A different way of life is not a threat: tolerance lets different people live side by side in peace.
