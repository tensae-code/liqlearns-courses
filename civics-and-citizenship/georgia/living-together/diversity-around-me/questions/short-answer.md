# Diversity Around Me — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** The diversity of people and cultures around me is a fact to value, not to fear.

**2.** What does the part "Valuing diversity" teach?
**Answer:** Diversity is wealth: different people bring different knowledge, skills and ways of seeing.
