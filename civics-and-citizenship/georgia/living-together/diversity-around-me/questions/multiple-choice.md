# Diversity Around Me — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) the diversity of people and cultures around me is a fact to value, not to fear  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — Seeing the diversity around me?
a) The people around me differ in language, culture, custom and belief — that diversity is simply there, everywhere.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
