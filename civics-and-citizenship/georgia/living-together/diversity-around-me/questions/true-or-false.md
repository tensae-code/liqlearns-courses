# Diversity Around Me — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The diversity of people and cultures around me is a fact to value, not to fear.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a citizen's own home, school or community in Georgia.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Georgia and lived there.
