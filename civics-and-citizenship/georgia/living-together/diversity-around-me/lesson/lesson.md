# Diversity Around Me

## Do it first

Look around your street or school. Name three differences between people you see — and one good thing each difference brings.

## Sub lesson 1 — Seeing the diversity around me

The people around me differ in language, culture, custom and belief — that diversity is simply there, everywhere.

## Sub lesson 2 — Valuing diversity

Diversity is wealth: different people bring different knowledge, skills and ways of seeing.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the diversity of people and cultures around me is a fact to value, not to fear. Give one real example from your own life in Georgia, and name the lesson's key words: diversity, culture, value, difference. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Georgian as well as in English — Georgia's own book teaches Citizenship (მოქალაქეობა) in Georgian, for life at home and in the community, not only for the classroom.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: the diversity of people and cultures around me is a fact to value, not to fear.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
