# Video script — Diversity Around Me

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The diversity of people and cultures around me is a fact to value, not to fear.

Seeing the diversity around me. The people around me differ in language, culture, custom and belief — that diversity is simply there, everywhere.

Valuing diversity. Diversity is wealth: different people bring different knowledge, skills and ways of seeing.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
