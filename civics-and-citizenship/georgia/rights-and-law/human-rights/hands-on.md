# Hands-on — Human Rights

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name one right you have. Say where it is written down, and one way it protects you or someone you know.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: human rights belong to every person — they are written down in laws and declarations, and they protect everyone. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
