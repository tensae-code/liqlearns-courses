# Video script — Human Rights

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Human rights belong to every person — they are written down in laws and declarations, and they protect everyone.

What human rights are. Human rights are the basic rights every person has simply by being human.

Where they are written and how they protect. Human rights are written down in documents such as the Universal Declaration and the Convention on the Rights of the Child, and they protect every person against unfair treatment.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
