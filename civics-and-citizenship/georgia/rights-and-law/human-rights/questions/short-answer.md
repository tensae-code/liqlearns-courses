# Human Rights — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Human rights belong to every person — they are written down in laws and declarations, and they protect everyone.

**2.** What does the part "Where they are written and how they protect" teach?
**Answer:** Human rights are written down in documents such as the Universal Declaration and the Convention on the Rights of the Child, and they protect every person against unfair treatment.
