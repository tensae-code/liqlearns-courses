# Human Rights

## Do it first

Name one right you have. Say where it is written down, and one way it protects you or someone you know.

## Sub lesson 1 — What human rights are

Human rights are the basic rights every person has simply by being human.

## Sub lesson 2 — Where they are written and how they protect

Human rights are written down in documents such as the Universal Declaration and the Convention on the Rights of the Child, and they protect every person against unfair treatment.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: human rights belong to every person — they are written down in laws and declarations, and they protect everyone. Give one real example from your own life in Georgia, and name the lesson's key words: human rights, protection, declaration, every person. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
