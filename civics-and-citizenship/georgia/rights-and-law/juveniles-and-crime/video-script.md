This lesson is about one thing.
When a minor breaks the law there are consequences — and every young person is responsible for their own acts.

What happens when a minor breaks the law. A minor who breaks the law faces the law's response, shaped for young people — with protection as well as consequences.

Responsibility for my acts. Being young does not remove responsibility: each person answers for what they choose to do.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
