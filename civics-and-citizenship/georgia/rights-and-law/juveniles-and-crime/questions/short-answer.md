# Juveniles and Crime — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** When a minor breaks the law there are consequences — and every young person is responsible for their own acts.

**2.** What does the part "Responsibility for my acts" teach?
**Answer:** Being young does not remove responsibility: each person answers for what they choose to do.
