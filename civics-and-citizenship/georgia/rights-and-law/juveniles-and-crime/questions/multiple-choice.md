# Juveniles and Crime — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) when a minor breaks the law there are consequences — and every young person is responsible for their own acts  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — What happens when a minor breaks the law?
a) A minor who breaks the law faces the law's response, shaped for young people — with protection as well as consequences.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
