# The Person and the Law — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Before the law, every person has rights and duties — and responsibility for what they do.

**2.** What does the part "Responsibility in everyday situations" teach?
**Answer:** In everyday situations — at home, at school, in the street — a person answers for their own acts.
