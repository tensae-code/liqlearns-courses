# The Person and the Law — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) before the law, every person has rights and duties — and responsibility for what they do  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — Rights and duties before the law?
a) The law gives every person rights and lays duties on every person at the same time.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
