# Video script — The Person and the Law

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Before the law, every person has rights and duties — and responsibility for what they do.

Rights and duties before the law. The law gives every person rights and lays duties on every person at the same time.

Responsibility in everyday situations. In everyday situations — at home, at school, in the street — a person answers for their own acts.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
