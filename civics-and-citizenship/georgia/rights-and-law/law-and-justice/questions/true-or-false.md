# Law and Justice — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Rules become law so that life together is ordered — and an outcome is just when it is fair for everyone it touches.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a citizen's own home, school or community in Georgia.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Georgia and lived there.
