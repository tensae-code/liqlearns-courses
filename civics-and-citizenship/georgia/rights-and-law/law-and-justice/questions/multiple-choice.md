# Law and Justice — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) rules become law so that life together is ordered — and an outcome is just when it is fair for everyone it touches  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — How rules become law?
a) A community's rules become law when they are agreed, written down and applied to everyone.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
