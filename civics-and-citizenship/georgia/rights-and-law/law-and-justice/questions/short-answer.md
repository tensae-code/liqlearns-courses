# Law and Justice — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Rules become law so that life together is ordered — and an outcome is just when it is fair for everyone it touches.

**2.** What does the part "What makes an outcome just" teach?
**Answer:** An outcome is just when it is fair — the same measure for everyone, with each side heard.
