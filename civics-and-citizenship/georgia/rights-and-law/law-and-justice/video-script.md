# Video script — Law and Justice

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Rules become law so that life together is ordered — and an outcome is just when it is fair for everyone it touches.

How rules become law. A community's rules become law when they are agreed, written down and applied to everyone.

What makes an outcome just. An outcome is just when it is fair — the same measure for everyone, with each side heard.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
