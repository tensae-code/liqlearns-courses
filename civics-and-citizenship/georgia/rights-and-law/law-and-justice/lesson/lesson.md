# Law and Justice

## Do it first

Name one rule at school. Say how it could become a law — and what a just outcome would look like if someone broke it.

## Sub lesson 1 — How rules become law

A community's rules become law when they are agreed, written down and applied to everyone.

## Sub lesson 2 — What makes an outcome just

An outcome is just when it is fair — the same measure for everyone, with each side heard.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: rules become law so that life together is ordered — and an outcome is just when it is fair for everyone it touches. Give one real example from your own life in Georgia, and name the lesson's key words: law, justice, fairness, rules. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Georgian as well as in English — Georgia's own book teaches Citizenship (მოქალაქეობა) in Georgian, for life at home and in the community, not only for the classroom.
