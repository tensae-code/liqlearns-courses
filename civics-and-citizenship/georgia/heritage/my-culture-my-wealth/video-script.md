# Video script — My Culture Is My Wealth

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
My own culture is wealth — knowing and valuing Georgian cultural heritage makes it mine to keep.

Knowing my heritage. Georgian cultural heritage — its language, songs, buildings, crafts and customs — is known first by learning it.

Valuing my heritage. What I value, I keep: treasuring my culture is what hands it on alive.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
