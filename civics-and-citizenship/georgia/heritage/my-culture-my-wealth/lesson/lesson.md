# My Culture Is My Wealth

## Do it first

Name three parts of Georgian cultural heritage you know. Say what each one is — and one way you can value and keep it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Knowing my heritage

Georgian cultural heritage — its language, songs, buildings, crafts and customs — is known first by learning it.

## Sub lesson 2 — Valuing my heritage

What I value, I keep: treasuring my culture is what hands it on alive.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: my own culture is wealth — knowing and valuing Georgian cultural heritage makes it mine to keep. Give one real example from your own life in Georgia, and name the lesson's key words: culture, heritage, Georgia, wealth. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Georgian as well as in English — Georgia's own book teaches Citizenship (მოქალაქეობა) in Georgian, for life at home and in the community, not only for the classroom.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: my own culture is wealth — knowing and valuing Georgian cultural heritage makes it mine to keep.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
