# My Culture Is My Wealth — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** My own culture is wealth — knowing and valuing Georgian cultural heritage makes it mine to keep.

**2.** What does the part "Valuing my heritage" teach?
**Answer:** What I value, I keep: treasuring my culture is what hands it on alive.
