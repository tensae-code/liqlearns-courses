# My Culture Is My Wealth — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) my own culture is wealth — knowing and valuing Georgian cultural heritage makes it mine to keep  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — Knowing my heritage?
a) Georgian cultural heritage — its language, songs, buildings, crafts and customs — is known first by learning it.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
