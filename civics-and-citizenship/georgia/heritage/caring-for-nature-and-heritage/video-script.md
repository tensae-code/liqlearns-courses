# Video script — Caring for Nature and Heritage

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Protecting the natural environment and cultural heritage is caring for the country itself.

Caring for nature. The natural environment — land, water, forests — is the country's own wealth, protected by everyone's care.

Caring for cultural heritage. Old buildings, monuments and traditions survive only if each generation protects them.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
