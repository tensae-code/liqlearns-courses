# Caring for Nature and Heritage

## Do it first

Name one natural place and one heritage place near you. Say one threat to each — and one act of care you can do for each.

## Sub lesson 1 — Caring for nature

The natural environment — land, water, forests — is the country's own wealth, protected by everyone's care.

## Sub lesson 2 — Caring for cultural heritage

Old buildings, monuments and traditions survive only if each generation protects them.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: protecting the natural environment and cultural heritage is caring for the country itself. Give one real example from your own life in Georgia, and name the lesson's key words: nature, heritage, environment, protection. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Georgian as well as in English — Georgia's own book teaches Citizenship (მოქალაქეობა) in Georgian, for life at home and in the community, not only for the classroom.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: protecting the natural environment and cultural heritage is caring for the country itself.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
