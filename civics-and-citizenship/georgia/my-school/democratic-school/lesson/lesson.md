# A Democratic School

## Do it first

Name one decision in your school that pupils could share in. Say how a fair pupil vote on it would run.

## Sub lesson 1 — Pupil voice and elections

In a democratic school, pupils have a voice: they speak, they stand, and they vote in real elections.

## Sub lesson 2 — Shared decisions

Decisions that touch everyone are made with everyone — discussed openly and decided fairly.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: democracy can be practised inside school — through pupil voice, elections and shared decisions. Give one real example from your own life in Georgia, and name the lesson's key words: democracy, elections, pupil voice, decisions. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Georgian as well as in English — Georgia's own book teaches Citizenship (მოქალაქეობა) in Georgian, for life at home and in the community, not only for the classroom.
