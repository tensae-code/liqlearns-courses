# Video script — A Democratic School

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Democracy can be practised inside school — through pupil voice, elections and shared decisions.

Pupil voice and elections. In a democratic school, pupils have a voice: they speak, they stand, and they vote in real elections.

Shared decisions. Decisions that touch everyone are made with everyone — discussed openly and decided fairly.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
