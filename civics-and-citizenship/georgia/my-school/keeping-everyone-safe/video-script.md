# Video script — Keeping Everyone Safe

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Protecting my own safety and the safety of those around me is something I can do every day.

Protecting my own safety. I protect myself by noticing danger, keeping safety rules, and asking for help in time.

Protecting others' safety. Others' safety is also my care: warning, helping, and never leaving someone in danger alone.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
