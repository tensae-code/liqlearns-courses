# Keeping Everyone Safe — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Protecting my own safety and the safety of those around me is something I can do every day.

**2.** What does the part "Protecting others' safety" teach?
**Answer:** Others' safety is also my care: warning, helping, and never leaving someone in danger alone.
