# Keeping Everyone Safe — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) protecting my own safety and the safety of those around me is something I can do every day  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — Protecting my own safety?
a) I protect myself by noticing danger, keeping safety rules, and asking for help in time.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
