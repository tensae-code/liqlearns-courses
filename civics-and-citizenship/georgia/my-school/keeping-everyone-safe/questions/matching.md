# Keeping Everyone Safe — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** Protecting my own safety — a)

**2.** Protecting others' safety — b)


Meanings:
a) I protect myself by noticing danger, keeping safety rules, and asking for help in time.
b) Others' safety is also my care: warning, helping, and never leaving someone in danger alone.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
