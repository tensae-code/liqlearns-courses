# Keeping Everyone Safe

## Do it first

Name one danger on your way to school. Say how you protect yourself from it — and how you could protect a younger pupil from it too.

## Sub lesson 1 — Protecting my own safety

I protect myself by noticing danger, keeping safety rules, and asking for help in time.

## Sub lesson 2 — Protecting others' safety

Others' safety is also my care: warning, helping, and never leaving someone in danger alone.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: protecting my own safety and the safety of those around me is something I can do every day. Give one real example from your own life in Georgia, and name the lesson's key words: safety, protection, help, others. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Georgian as well as in English — Georgia's own book teaches Citizenship (მოქალაქეობა) in Georgian, for life at home and in the community, not only for the classroom.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: protecting my own safety and the safety of those around me is something I can do every day.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
