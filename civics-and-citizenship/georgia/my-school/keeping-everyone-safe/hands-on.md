# Hands-on — Keeping Everyone Safe

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name one danger on your way to school. Say how you protect yourself from it — and how you could protect a younger pupil from it too.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: protecting my own safety and the safety of those around me is something I can do every day. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
