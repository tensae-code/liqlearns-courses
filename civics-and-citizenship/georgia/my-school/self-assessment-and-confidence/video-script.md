# Video script — Self-Assessment and Self-Confidence

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Honest self-assessment — knowing what I can and cannot yet do — is the ground of real self-confidence.

Assessing myself honestly. Self-assessment means judging my own work truthfully: what I did well, and what I still cannot do.

Confidence that is grounded. Real confidence grows from honest assessment and practice — not from pretending.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
