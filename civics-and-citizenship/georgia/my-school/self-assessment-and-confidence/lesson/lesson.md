# Self-Assessment and Self-Confidence

## Do it first

Pick one school task you did recently. Assess it honestly: one strength, one weakness. Then say what you will practise next.

## Sub lesson 1 — Assessing myself honestly

Self-assessment means judging my own work truthfully: what I did well, and what I still cannot do.

## Sub lesson 2 — Confidence that is grounded

Real confidence grows from honest assessment and practice — not from pretending.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: honest self-assessment — knowing what I can and cannot yet do — is the ground of real self-confidence. Give one real example from your own life in Georgia, and name the lesson's key words: self-assessment, confidence, honesty, growth. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Georgian as well as in English — Georgia's own book teaches Citizenship (მოქალაქეობა) in Georgian, for life at home and in the community, not only for the classroom.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: honest self-assessment — knowing what I can and cannot yet do — is the ground of real self-confidence.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
