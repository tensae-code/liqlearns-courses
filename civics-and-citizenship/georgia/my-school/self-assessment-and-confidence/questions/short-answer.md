# Self-Assessment and Self-Confidence — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Honest self-assessment — knowing what I can and cannot yet do — is the ground of real self-confidence.

**2.** What does the part "Confidence that is grounded" teach?
**Answer:** Real confidence grows from honest assessment and practice — not from pretending.
