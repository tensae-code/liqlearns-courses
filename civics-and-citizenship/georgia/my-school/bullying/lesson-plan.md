# Lesson plan — Bullying

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Georgia · **Title:** my-school · **Lesson:** Bullying
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — The Community I Live In (CURRICULUM.md).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Recognising bullying** — Bullying is not a single quarrel: it is repeated, deliberate harm, where one side is stronger.
2. **What victims and bystanders can do** — A victim can tell and seek help; a bystander who speaks up or stands with the victim weakens the bullying.

Gate pool: the prove-it check for each sub lesson is at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson — drawn from this lesson's question files (questions/).
