# Bullying — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Bullying is repeated harm by someone stronger — it can be recognised, and victims and bystanders can act.

**2.** What does the part "What victims and bystanders can do" teach?
**Answer:** A victim can tell and seek help; a bystander who speaks up or stands with the victim weakens the bullying.
