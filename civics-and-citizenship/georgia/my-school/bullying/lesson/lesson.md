# Bullying

## Do it first

Take a scene: a pupil is mocked every day by a stronger group. Name the signs that this is bullying — then say what the victim can do, and what you as a bystander can do.

## Sub lesson 1 — Recognising bullying

Bullying is not a single quarrel: it is repeated, deliberate harm, where one side is stronger.

## Sub lesson 2 — What victims and bystanders can do

A victim can tell and seek help; a bystander who speaks up or stands with the victim weakens the bullying.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: bullying is repeated harm by someone stronger — it can be recognised, and victims and bystanders can act. Give one real example from your own life in Georgia, and name the lesson's key words: bullying, bystander, victim, help. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Georgian as well as in English — Georgia's own book teaches Citizenship (მოქალაქეობა) in Georgian, for life at home and in the community, not only for the classroom.
