# Video script — Bullying

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Bullying is repeated harm by someone stronger — it can be recognised, and victims and bystanders can act.

Recognising bullying. Bullying is not a single quarrel: it is repeated, deliberate harm, where one side is stronger.

What victims and bystanders can do. A victim can tell and seek help; a bystander who speaks up or stands with the victim weakens the bullying.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
