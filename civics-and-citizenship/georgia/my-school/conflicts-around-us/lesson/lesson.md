# Conflicts Around Us

## Do it first

Name one conflict you have seen at school. Say what each side wanted — then describe a peaceful outcome both sides could accept.

## Sub lesson 1 — Where conflicts come from

Conflicts arise when needs, views or interests clash — in class, in the yard, in the community.

## Sub lesson 2 — Resolving conflict peacefully

A conflict can be settled by talking, listening, and finding an outcome both sides can accept — without force.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: conflicts arise in school and community — and they can be resolved peacefully. Give one real example from your own life in Georgia, and name the lesson's key words: conflict, resolution, peace, negotiation. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Georgian as well as in English — Georgia's own book teaches Citizenship (მოქალაქეობა) in Georgian, for life at home and in the community, not only for the classroom.
