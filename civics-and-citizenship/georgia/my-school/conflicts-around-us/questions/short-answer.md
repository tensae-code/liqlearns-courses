# Conflicts Around Us — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Conflicts arise in school and community — and they can be resolved peacefully.

**2.** What does the part "Resolving conflict peacefully" teach?
**Answer:** A conflict can be settled by talking, listening, and finding an outcome both sides can accept — without force.
