# Conflicts Around Us — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) conflicts arise in school and community — and they can be resolved peacefully  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — Where conflicts come from?
a) Conflicts arise when needs, views or interests clash — in class, in the yard, in the community.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
