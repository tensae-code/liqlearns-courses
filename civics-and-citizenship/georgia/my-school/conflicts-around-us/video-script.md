# Video script — Conflicts Around Us

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Conflicts arise in school and community — and they can be resolved peacefully.

Where conflicts come from. Conflicts arise when needs, views or interests clash — in class, in the yard, in the community.

Resolving conflict peacefully. A conflict can be settled by talking, listening, and finding an outcome both sides can accept — without force.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
