# School Culture — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) a school's culture — its norms, traditions and climate — is what makes it a community  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — Norms and traditions?
a) Every school has norms and traditions: how we greet, how we work, how we celebrate and solve problems.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
