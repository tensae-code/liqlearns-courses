# School Culture — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** A school's culture — its norms, traditions and climate — is what makes it a community.

**2.** What does the part "The school's climate" teach?
**Answer:** The climate of a school — whether people feel respected and safe — is made by everyone's daily behaviour.
