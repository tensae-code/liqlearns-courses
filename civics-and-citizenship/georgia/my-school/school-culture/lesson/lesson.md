# School Culture

## Do it first

Name one norm and one tradition of your school. Say how each one helps — or hurts — the school's climate.

## Sub lesson 1 — Norms and traditions

Every school has norms and traditions: how we greet, how we work, how we celebrate and solve problems.

## Sub lesson 2 — The school's climate

The climate of a school — whether people feel respected and safe — is made by everyone's daily behaviour.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: a school's culture — its norms, traditions and climate — is what makes it a community. Give one real example from your own life in Georgia, and name the lesson's key words: school culture, norms, traditions, climate. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Georgian as well as in English — Georgia's own book teaches Citizenship (მოქალაქეობა) in Georgian, for life at home and in the community, not only for the classroom.
