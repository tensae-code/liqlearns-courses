# Video script — School Culture

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A school's culture — its norms, traditions and climate — is what makes it a community.

Norms and traditions. Every school has norms and traditions: how we greet, how we work, how we celebrate and solve problems.

The school's climate. The climate of a school — whether people feel respected and safe — is made by everyone's daily behaviour.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
