# We at School — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** My place at school — a)

**2.** My part at school — b)


Meanings:
a) Every pupil belongs at school — with a place in its classes, groups and common life.
b) Belonging means taking part: each pupil contributes something to the life everyone shares.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
