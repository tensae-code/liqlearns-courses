# We at School — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) each pupil has a place and a part in the school's common life  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — My place at school?
a) Every pupil belongs at school — with a place in its classes, groups and common life.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
