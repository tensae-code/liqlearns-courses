# We at School

## Do it first

Name your place at school — your class, your group. Then name one part you play in the school's common life, and one more you could take.

## Sub lesson 1 — My place at school

Every pupil belongs at school — with a place in its classes, groups and common life.

## Sub lesson 2 — My part at school

Belonging means taking part: each pupil contributes something to the life everyone shares.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: each pupil has a place and a part in the school's common life. Give one real example from your own life in Georgia, and name the lesson's key words: school, belonging, participation, community. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Georgian as well as in English — Georgia's own book teaches Citizenship (მოქალაქეობა) in Georgian, for life at home and in the community, not only for the classroom.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: each pupil has a place and a part in the school's common life.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
