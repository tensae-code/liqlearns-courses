# Video script — Communicating Well

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Communication is verbal and nonverbal — and listening is half of it.

Words and more than words. We communicate with words, and also nonverbally — with face, hands, posture and silence.

Listening well. Listening — hearing the other person fully before answering — is what makes communication work.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
