# Communicating Well — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) communication is verbal and nonverbal — and listening is half of it  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — Words and more than words?
a) We communicate with words, and also nonverbally — with face, hands, posture and silence.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
