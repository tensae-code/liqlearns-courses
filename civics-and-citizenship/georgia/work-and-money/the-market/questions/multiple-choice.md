# The Market — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) in the market, buyers and sellers meet — price and choice order the community's economic life  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — Buyers, sellers and price?
a) Sellers offer goods, buyers choose and pay; the price is where their decisions meet.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
