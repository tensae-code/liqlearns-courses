# The Market — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** In the market, buyers and sellers meet — price and choice order the community's economic life.

**2.** What does the part "Choice in the market" teach?
**Answer:** Buyers choose by need, quality and price — and sellers answer with what they offer.
