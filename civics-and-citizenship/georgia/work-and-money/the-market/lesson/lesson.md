# The Market

## Do it first

Visit a market or shop in your mind. Name one thing sold, its price, and two choices a buyer makes there. Say what decides the buyer's choice.

## Sub lesson 1 — Buyers, sellers and price

Sellers offer goods, buyers choose and pay; the price is where their decisions meet.

## Sub lesson 2 — Choice in the market

Buyers choose by need, quality and price — and sellers answer with what they offer.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: in the market, buyers and sellers meet — price and choice order the community's economic life. Give one real example from your own life in Georgia, and name the lesson's key words: market, price, buyers, sellers. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Georgian as well as in English — Georgia's own book teaches Citizenship (მოქალაქეობა) in Georgian, for life at home and in the community, not only for the classroom.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: in the market, buyers and sellers meet — price and choice order the community's economic life.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
