# Video script — The Market

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
In the market, buyers and sellers meet — price and choice order the community's economic life.

Buyers, sellers and price. Sellers offer goods, buyers choose and pay; the price is where their decisions meet.

Choice in the market. Buyers choose by need, quality and price — and sellers answer with what they offer.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
