# Volunteering — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) volunteering is a free choice to serve the community without pay  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — A free choice?
a) No one is forced to volunteer: it is chosen freely, from one's own will to help.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
