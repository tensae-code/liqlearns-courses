# Volunteering — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** A free choice — a)

**2.** Serving without pay — b)


Meanings:
a) No one is forced to volunteer: it is chosen freely, from one's own will to help.
b) The volunteer gives time and work to the community and receives no pay — the community's good is the reward.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
