# Volunteering — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Volunteering is a free choice to serve the community without pay.

**2.** What does the part "Serving without pay" teach?
**Answer:** The volunteer gives time and work to the community and receives no pay — the community's good is the reward.
