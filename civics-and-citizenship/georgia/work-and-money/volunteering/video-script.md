# Video script — Volunteering

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Volunteering is a free choice to serve the community without pay.

A free choice. No one is forced to volunteer: it is chosen freely, from one's own will to help.

Serving without pay. The volunteer gives time and work to the community and receives no pay — the community's good is the reward.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
