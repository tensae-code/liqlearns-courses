# Volunteering

## Do it first

Name one job your community needs done. Say how a volunteer could do it — and what makes it volunteering and not paid work.

## Sub lesson 1 — A free choice

No one is forced to volunteer: it is chosen freely, from one's own will to help.

## Sub lesson 2 — Serving without pay

The volunteer gives time and work to the community and receives no pay — the community's good is the reward.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: volunteering is a free choice to serve the community without pay. Give one real example from your own life in Georgia, and name the lesson's key words: volunteering, community, service, free choice. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Georgian as well as in English — Georgia's own book teaches Citizenship (მოქალაქეობა) in Georgian, for life at home and in the community, not only for the classroom.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: volunteering is a free choice to serve the community without pay.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
