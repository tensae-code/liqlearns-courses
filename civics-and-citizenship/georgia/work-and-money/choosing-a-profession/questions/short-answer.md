# Choosing a Profession — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Choosing a profession is choosing a future — by interests, abilities and study routes.

**2.** What does the part "Study routes to a profession" teach?
**Answer:** Every profession has a road: subjects to study, training to take, steps to climb.
