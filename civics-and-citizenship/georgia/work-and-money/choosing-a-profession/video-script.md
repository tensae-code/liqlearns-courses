# Video script — Choosing a Profession

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Choosing a profession is choosing a future — by interests, abilities and study routes.

Interests and abilities. A good choice starts from what interests me and what I am able to do and learn.

Study routes to a profession. Every profession has a road: subjects to study, training to take, steps to climb.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
