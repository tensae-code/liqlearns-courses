# Choosing a Profession

## Do it first

Name one profession that interests you. Say what abilities it needs — and the study route that leads to it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Interests and abilities

A good choice starts from what interests me and what I am able to do and learn.

## Sub lesson 2 — Study routes to a profession

Every profession has a road: subjects to study, training to take, steps to climb.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: choosing a profession is choosing a future — by interests, abilities and study routes. Give one real example from your own life in Georgia, and name the lesson's key words: profession, future, interests, abilities. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Georgian as well as in English — Georgia's own book teaches Citizenship (მოქალაქეობა) in Georgian, for life at home and in the community, not only for the classroom.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: choosing a profession is choosing a future — by interests, abilities and study routes.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
