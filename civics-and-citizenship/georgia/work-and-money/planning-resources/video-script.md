# Video script — Planning Resources

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Resources are planned and distributed by needs first — wants after, and the budget kept.

Needs and wants. Planning starts by telling needs from wants: needs come first when resources are shared out.

Budgeting in the community. A budget is a plan for resources — what comes in, what goes out, and what is kept for later.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
