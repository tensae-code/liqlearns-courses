# Planning Resources — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Resources are planned and distributed by needs first — wants after, and the budget kept.

**2.** What does the part "Budgeting in the community" teach?
**Answer:** A budget is a plan for resources — what comes in, what goes out, and what is kept for later.
