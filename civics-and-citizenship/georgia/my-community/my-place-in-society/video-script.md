# Video script — My Place in Society

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Belonging is a right — every person has the right to have their place in society and to feel a full member of it.

Belonging as a right. Having a place in society is not a favour; it is a right every person holds.

Feeling a full member. A person feels a full member of society when they are accepted, heard and able to take part — as the Convention on the Rights of the Child protects for children.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
