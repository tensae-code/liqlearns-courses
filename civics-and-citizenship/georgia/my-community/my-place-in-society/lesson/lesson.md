# My Place in Society

## Do it first

Think of a group you belong to. Say one thing that makes you feel a full member of it — and one thing that would make someone new feel they belong.

## Sub lesson 1 — Belonging as a right

Having a place in society is not a favour; it is a right every person holds.

## Sub lesson 2 — Feeling a full member

A person feels a full member of society when they are accepted, heard and able to take part — as the Convention on the Rights of the Child protects for children.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: belonging is a right — every person has the right to have their place in society and to feel a full member of it. Give one real example from your own life in Georgia, and name the lesson's key words: belonging, society, rights, membership. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Georgian as well as in English — Georgia's own book teaches Citizenship (მოქალაქეობა) in Georgian, for life at home and in the community, not only for the classroom.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: belonging is a right — every person has the right to have their place in society and to feel a full member of it.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
