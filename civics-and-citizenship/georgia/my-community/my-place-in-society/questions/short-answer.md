# My Place in Society — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Belonging is a right — every person has the right to have their place in society and to feel a full member of it.

**2.** What does the part "Feeling a full member" teach?
**Answer:** A person feels a full member of society when they are accepted, heard and able to take part — as the Convention on the Rights of the Child protects for children.
