# My Place in Society — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** Belonging as a right — a)

**2.** Feeling a full member — b)


Meanings:
a) Having a place in society is not a favour; it is a right every person holds.
b) A person feels a full member of society when they are accepted, heard and able to take part — as the Convention on the Rights of the Child protects for children.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
