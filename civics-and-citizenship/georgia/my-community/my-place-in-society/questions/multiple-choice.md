# My Place in Society — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) belonging is a right — every person has the right to have their place in society and to feel a full member of it  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — Belonging as a right?
a) Having a place in society is not a favour; it is a right every person holds.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
