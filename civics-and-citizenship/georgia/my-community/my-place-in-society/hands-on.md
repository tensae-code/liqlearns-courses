# Hands-on — My Place in Society

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Think of a group you belong to. Say one thing that makes you feel a full member of it — and one thing that would make someone new feel they belong.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: belonging is a right — every person has the right to have their place in society and to feel a full member of it. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
