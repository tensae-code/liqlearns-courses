# What Forms My Personality — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** My personality is formed by the people, groups and experiences around me — family, friends, school and community.

**2.** What does the part "The experiences that form me" teach?
**Answer:** What happens to me — what I practise, suffer and overcome — also forms who I become.
