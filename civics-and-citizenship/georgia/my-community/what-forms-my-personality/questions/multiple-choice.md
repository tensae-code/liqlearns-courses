# What Forms My Personality — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) my personality is formed by the people, groups and experiences around me — family, friends, school and community  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — The people and groups that form me?
a) Family, friends, teachers and the groups I belong to all shape my character and identity.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
