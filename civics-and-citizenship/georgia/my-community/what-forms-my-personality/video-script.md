# Video script — What Forms My Personality

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
My personality is formed by the people, groups and experiences around me — family, friends, school and community.

The people and groups that form me. Family, friends, teachers and the groups I belong to all shape my character and identity.

The experiences that form me. What happens to me — what I practise, suffer and overcome — also forms who I become.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
