# What Forms My Personality

## Do it first

Name one person, one group and one experience that have formed you. Say how each one changed you.

## Sub lesson 1 — The people and groups that form me

Family, friends, teachers and the groups I belong to all shape my character and identity.

## Sub lesson 2 — The experiences that form me

What happens to me — what I practise, suffer and overcome — also forms who I become.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: my personality is formed by the people, groups and experiences around me — family, friends, school and community. Give one real example from your own life in Georgia, and name the lesson's key words: personality, character, identity, influence. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Georgian as well as in English — Georgia's own book teaches Citizenship (მოქალაქეობა) in Georgian, for life at home and in the community, not only for the classroom.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: my personality is formed by the people, groups and experiences around me — family, friends, school and community.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
