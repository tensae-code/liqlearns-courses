# Video script — Leadership and Ethics

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Leadership is service — a leader owes the group honesty, fairness and care.

Leadership as service. A leader's job is to serve the group, not to be served by it.

The ethics a leader owes. A leader owes the group a culture of honesty, fairness and respect — and answers for their decisions.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
