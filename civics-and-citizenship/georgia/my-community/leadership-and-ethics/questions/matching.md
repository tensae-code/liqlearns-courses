# Leadership and Ethics — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** Leadership as service — a)

**2.** The ethics a leader owes — b)


Meanings:
a) A leader's job is to serve the group, not to be served by it.
b) A leader owes the group a culture of honesty, fairness and respect — and answers for their decisions.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
