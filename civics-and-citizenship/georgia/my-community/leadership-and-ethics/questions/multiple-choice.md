# Leadership and Ethics — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) leadership is service — a leader owes the group honesty, fairness and care  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — Leadership as service?
a) A leader's job is to serve the group, not to be served by it.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
