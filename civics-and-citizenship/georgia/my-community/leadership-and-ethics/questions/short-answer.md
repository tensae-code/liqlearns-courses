# Leadership and Ethics — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Leadership is service — a leader owes the group honesty, fairness and care.

**2.** What does the part "The ethics a leader owes" teach?
**Answer:** A leader owes the group a culture of honesty, fairness and respect — and answers for their decisions.
