# Leadership and Ethics

## Do it first

Think of a leader you know — in class, in a team, in the community. Name one thing they do that serves the group, and one duty they owe it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Leadership as service

A leader's job is to serve the group, not to be served by it.

## Sub lesson 2 — The ethics a leader owes

A leader owes the group a culture of honesty, fairness and respect — and answers for their decisions.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: leadership is service — a leader owes the group honesty, fairness and care. Give one real example from your own life in Georgia, and name the lesson's key words: leadership, service, ethics, responsibility. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Georgian as well as in English — Georgia's own book teaches Citizenship (მოქალაქეობა) in Georgian, for life at home and in the community, not only for the classroom.
