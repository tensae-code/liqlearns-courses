# Hands-on — Participation Is My Right

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name one shared space in your community — a yard, a park, a street. Say one decision about it you could take part in, and how.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: taking part in my community's shared spaces and decisions is my right and my duty as a citizen. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
