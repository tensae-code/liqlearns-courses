# Video script — Participation Is My Right

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Taking part in my community's shared spaces and decisions is my right and my duty as a citizen.

Participation as a right. Every citizen, children included, has the right to take part in the community's shared spaces and decisions.

Participation as a duty. Taking part is also a duty: the children who acted to save their yard's trees were doing what citizens do — caring for what is shared.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
