# Participation Is My Right

## Do it first

Name one shared space in your community — a yard, a park, a street. Say one decision about it you could take part in, and how.

## Sub lesson 1 — Participation as a right

Every citizen, children included, has the right to take part in the community's shared spaces and decisions.

## Sub lesson 2 — Participation as a duty

Taking part is also a duty: the children who acted to save their yard's trees were doing what citizens do — caring for what is shared.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: taking part in my community's shared spaces and decisions is my right and my duty as a citizen. Give one real example from your own life in Georgia, and name the lesson's key words: participation, community, right, duty. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Georgian as well as in English — Georgia's own book teaches Citizenship (მოქალაქეობა) in Georgian, for life at home and in the community, not only for the classroom.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: taking part in my community's shared spaces and decisions is my right and my duty as a citizen.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
