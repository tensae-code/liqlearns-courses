# Participation Is My Right — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) taking part in my community's shared spaces and decisions is my right and my duty as a citizen  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — Participation as a right?
a) Every citizen, children included, has the right to take part in the community's shared spaces and decisions.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
