# Participation Is My Right — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Taking part in my community's shared spaces and decisions is my right and my duty as a citizen.

**2.** What does the part "Participation as a duty" teach?
**Answer:** Taking part is also a duty: the children who acted to save their yard's trees were doing what citizens do — caring for what is shared.
