# Participation Is My Right — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** Participation as a right — a)

**2.** Participation as a duty — b)


Meanings:
a) Every citizen, children included, has the right to take part in the community's shared spaces and decisions.
b) Taking part is also a duty: the children who acted to save their yard's trees were doing what citizens do — caring for what is shared.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
