# Relations Around Me

## Do it first

Name three people you relate to every day. For each one, say one thing that shapes your relation — and one respectful thing you can do for them today.

## Sub lesson 1 — What shapes relations

Character, views, goals and social position shape how people relate to each other.

## Sub lesson 2 — Building respectful relations

Respect and empathy are practised: listening, understanding the other person, and treating them well builds strong relations.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the relations around me are shaped by character, views, goals and social position — and respectful, empathetic relations are built, not found. Give one real example from your own life in Georgia, and name the lesson's key words: relations, respect, empathy, character. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Georgian as well as in English — Georgia's own book teaches Citizenship (მოქალაქეობა) in Georgian, for life at home and in the community, not only for the classroom.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: the relations around me are shaped by character, views, goals and social position — and respectful, empathetic relations are built, not found.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
