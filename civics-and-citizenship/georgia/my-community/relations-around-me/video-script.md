# Video script — Relations Around Me

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The relations around me are shaped by character, views, goals and social position — and respectful, empathetic relations are built, not found.

What shapes relations. Character, views, goals and social position shape how people relate to each other.

Building respectful relations. Respect and empathy are practised: listening, understanding the other person, and treating them well builds strong relations.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
