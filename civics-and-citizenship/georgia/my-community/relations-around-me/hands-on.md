# Hands-on — Relations Around Me

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name three people you relate to every day. For each one, say one thing that shapes your relation — and one respectful thing you can do for them today.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the relations around me are shaped by character, views, goals and social position — and respectful, empathetic relations are built, not found. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
