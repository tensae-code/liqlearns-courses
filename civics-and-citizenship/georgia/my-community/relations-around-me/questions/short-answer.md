# Relations Around Me — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** The relations around me are shaped by character, views, goals and social position — and respectful, empathetic relations are built, not found.

**2.** What does the part "Building respectful relations" teach?
**Answer:** Respect and empathy are practised: listening, understanding the other person, and treating them well builds strong relations.
