# Relations Around Me — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) the relations around me are shaped by character, views, goals and social position — and respectful, empathetic relations are built, not found  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — What shapes relations?
a) Character, views, goals and social position shape how people relate to each other.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
