# Video script — Early Marriage

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Early marriage carries serious risks and consequences — it is taught as a protection topic.

What early marriage is and its risks. Marriage while still a child or young teenager interrupts schooling and carries health and life risks.

Consequences and protection. Its consequences reach education, health and the whole future; knowing them is how young people are protected.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
