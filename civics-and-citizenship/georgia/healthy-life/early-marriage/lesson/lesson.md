# Early Marriage

## Do it first

List what a pupil gives up when schooling stops early for marriage — then say who a young person at risk can turn to for protection.

## Sub lesson 1 — What early marriage is and its risks

Marriage while still a child or young teenager interrupts schooling and carries health and life risks.

## Sub lesson 2 — Consequences and protection

Its consequences reach education, health and the whole future; knowing them is how young people are protected.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: early marriage carries serious risks and consequences — it is taught as a protection topic. Give one real example from your own life in Georgia, and name the lesson's key words: early marriage, protection, risks, schooling. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Georgian as well as in English — Georgia's own book teaches Citizenship (მოქალაქეობა) in Georgian, for life at home and in the community, not only for the classroom.
