# Early Marriage — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Early marriage carries serious risks and consequences — it is taught as a protection topic.

**2.** What does the part "Consequences and protection" teach?
**Answer:** Its consequences reach education, health and the whole future; knowing them is how young people are protected.
