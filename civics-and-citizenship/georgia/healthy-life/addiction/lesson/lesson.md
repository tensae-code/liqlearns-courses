# Addiction

## Do it first

Describe how a habit can grow into dependence, step by step. Then name two ways to avoid the first step, and where a person with dependence can find help.

## Sub lesson 1 — How addiction forms

Dependence forms gradually: a habit becomes a need the person struggles to stop.

## Sub lesson 2 — Avoiding and treating addiction

Addiction is avoided by refusing the first steps and choosing a healthy life; a person with dependence needs help and treatment, not shame.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: addiction is a dependence that forms step by step — it can be avoided, and it can be treated. Give one real example from your own life in Georgia, and name the lesson's key words: addiction, dependence, prevention, treatment. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
