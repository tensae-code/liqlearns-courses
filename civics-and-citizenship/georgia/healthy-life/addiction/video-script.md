# Video script — Addiction

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Addiction is a dependence that forms step by step — it can be avoided, and it can be treated.

How addiction forms. Dependence forms gradually: a habit becomes a need the person struggles to stop.

Avoiding and treating addiction. Addiction is avoided by refusing the first steps and choosing a healthy life; a person with dependence needs help and treatment, not shame.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
