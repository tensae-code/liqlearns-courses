# Addiction — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Addiction is a dependence that forms step by step — it can be avoided, and it can be treated.

**2.** What does the part "Avoiding and treating addiction" teach?
**Answer:** Addiction is avoided by refusing the first steps and choosing a healthy life; a person with dependence needs help and treatment, not shame.
