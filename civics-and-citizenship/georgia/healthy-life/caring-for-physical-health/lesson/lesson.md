# Caring for Physical Health

## Do it first

Write your day as it is: movement, food, rest. Mark one habit that keeps your health and one you will change this week.

## Sub lesson 1 — Movement, food and rest

The body needs movement, varied food and enough rest — every day, not sometimes.

## Sub lesson 2 — Habits that keep health

Health is kept by habits: cleanliness, regular days, and avoiding what harms the body.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: physical health is cared for daily — through movement, food, rest and good habits. Give one real example from your own life in Georgia, and name the lesson's key words: health, movement, food, habits. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Georgian as well as in English — Georgia's own book teaches Citizenship (მოქალაქეობა) in Georgian, for life at home and in the community, not only for the classroom.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: physical health is cared for daily — through movement, food, rest and good habits.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
