# Caring for Physical Health — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Physical health is cared for daily — through movement, food, rest and good habits.

**2.** What does the part "Habits that keep health" teach?
**Answer:** Health is kept by habits: cleanliness, regular days, and avoiding what harms the body.
