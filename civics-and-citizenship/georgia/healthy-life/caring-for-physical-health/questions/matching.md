# Caring for Physical Health — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** Movement, food and rest — a)

**2.** Habits that keep health — b)


Meanings:
a) The body needs movement, varied food and enough rest — every day, not sometimes.
b) Health is kept by habits: cleanliness, regular days, and avoiding what harms the body.
