# Video script — Caring for Physical Health

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Physical health is cared for daily — through movement, food, rest and good habits.

Movement, food and rest. The body needs movement, varied food and enough rest — every day, not sometimes.

Habits that keep health. Health is kept by habits: cleanliness, regular days, and avoiding what harms the body.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
