# Civics and Citizenship — Georgia course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

Civics belongs to the country: this course is Georgia's own —
built from Georgia's Citizenship student book for Grade 7
(მოქალაქეობა 7, Klio, Georgian, 2019), with its teacher's book
as labelled evidence. Inside the course the organisation is by
**levels, not grades**: the titles below sit inside the course's
one level, in path order — what the learner proves, step by
step. The grade the book comes from lives only in each lesson's
sources.

The path's own logic is the book's: citizenship taught outwards
from the community the child actually lives in — the community
first (relations, belonging, participation), with rights, law
and living together inside that circle; then the school
community; then healthy life; then protecting nature and
cultural heritage; and last the community's economic relations.
Higher Georgia grades' citizenship books are not in hand, so
the ladder stands at one level, honestly. That ladder IS the
path order (recommended, never forced — a learner can jump in
anywhere; the "start here instead" hints point down this
ladder).

## The titles

**Level 1 — The Community I Live In** (the course's one level):

- `my-community/` — Level 1. The community I live in: relations
  around me, my place in society, participation as my right,
  what forms my personality, leadership and ethics.
- `rights-and-law/` — Level 1. Rights and law inside the
  community circle: human rights, law and justice, the person
  and the law, juveniles and crime.
- `living-together/` — Level 1. Living together: tolerance,
  diversity around me, a safe environment.
- `my-school/` — Level 1. The school community: school culture,
  a democratic school, we at school, self-assessment and
  confidence, communicating well, bullying, conflicts around
  us, keeping everyone safe.
- `healthy-life/` — Level 1. Healthy life: caring for physical
  health, early marriage, addiction.
- `heritage/` — Level 1. Protecting nature and cultural
  heritage: my culture is my wealth, caring for nature and
  heritage.
- `work-and-money/` — Level 1. Economic relations in the
  community: choosing a profession, the market, volunteering,
  planning resources.
