# Education for Everyone — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) learning means acquiring knowledge; getting an education is necessary for every human being and benefits every person.  b) it has nothing to do with how Pakistan governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) learning means acquiring knowledge; getting an education is necessary for every human being and benefits every person.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) learning means acquiring knowledge; getting an education is necessary for every human being and benefits every person.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Pakistan
**Answer: a) learning means acquiring knowledge; getting an education is necessary for every human being and benefits every person.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
