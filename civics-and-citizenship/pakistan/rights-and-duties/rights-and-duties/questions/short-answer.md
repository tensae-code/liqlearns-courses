# Rights and Duties — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** all human beings are equal and hold rights in society — to life, religious freedom, education, housing and employment; and duties in return — caring for parents, respecting teachers, fulfilling the rights of neighbours and others, respecting every person's religion, race and family.
