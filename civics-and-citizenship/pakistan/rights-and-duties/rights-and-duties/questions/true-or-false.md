# Rights and Duties — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** all human beings are equal and hold rights in society — to life, religious freedom, education, housing and employment; and duties in return — caring for parents, respecting teachers, fulfilling the rights of neighbours and others, respecting every person's religion, race and family.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that Rights and Duties — and that it has no connection to the rest of Pakistan's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Pakistan's own course, in its own order.
