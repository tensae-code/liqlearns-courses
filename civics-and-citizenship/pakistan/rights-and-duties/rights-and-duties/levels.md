# Rights and Duties — depth

This lesson has sub lessons (listed in lesson-plan.md): The rights every person holds · The duties owed in return. Each sub lesson is visible from the start and locked ahead; each opens by proving the previous one — a prove-it check of at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, picked as hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson. On a fail: review the questions and answers just done, then reset with another set. No relocks ever. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: all human beings are equal and hold rights in society — to life, religious freedom, education, housing and employment; and duties in return — caring for parents, respecting teachers, fulfilling the rights of neighbours and others, respecting every person's religion, race and family. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example, part by part.
