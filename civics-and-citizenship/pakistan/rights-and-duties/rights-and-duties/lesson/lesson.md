# Rights and Duties

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Rights and Duties. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Pakistan's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## The rights every person holds

All human beings are equal and hold rights in society — to life, religious freedom, education, housing and employment. all human beings are equal and hold rights in society — to life, religious freedom, education, housing and employment; and duties in return — caring for parents, respecting teachers, fulfilling the rights of neighbours and others, respecting every person's religion, race and family.
