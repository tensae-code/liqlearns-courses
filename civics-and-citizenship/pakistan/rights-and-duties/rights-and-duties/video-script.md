# Video script — Rights and Duties

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
all human beings are equal and hold rights in society — to life, religious freedom, education, housing and employment; and duties in return — caring for parents, respecting teachers, fulfilling the rights of neighbours and others, respecting every person's religion, race and family.
