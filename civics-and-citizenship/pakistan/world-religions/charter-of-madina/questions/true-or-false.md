# Charter of Madina — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** the Mithaq-e-Madina named in the book as a peace agreement (امن کا معاہدہ) — the Prophet's covenant ordering a mixed city's life together, taught to minority-faith pupils as part of Islam's civic teaching (named and summarised, not transcribed).
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that Charter of Madina — and that it has no connection to the rest of Pakistan's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Pakistan's own course, in its own order.
