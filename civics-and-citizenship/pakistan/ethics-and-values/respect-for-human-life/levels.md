# Respect for Human Life — depth

This lesson is ONE lesson with no sub lessons: it teaches a single thing, whole. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: all religions teach protecting and respecting your own life and others'; the practical form — get treatment when ill, keep the body clean, bathe daily, wash hands before and after food, brush teeth daily, wear clean clothes, take a morning walk. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
