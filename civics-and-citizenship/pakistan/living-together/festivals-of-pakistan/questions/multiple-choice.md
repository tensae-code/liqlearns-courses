# Festivals of Pakistan — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Eid-ul-Adha, Easter, Holi and Nowruz as the festivals of one country.  b) it has nothing to do with how Pakistan governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Eid-ul-Adha, Easter, Holi and Nowruz as the festivals of one country.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Eid-ul-Adha, Easter, Holi and Nowruz as the festivals of one country.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Pakistan
**Answer: a) Eid-ul-Adha, Easter, Holi and Nowruz as the festivals of one country.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
