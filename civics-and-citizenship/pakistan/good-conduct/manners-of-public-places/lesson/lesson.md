# Manners of Public Places

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Manners of Public Places. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Pakistan's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

conduct in public places (ذاکٹر بیگ ہسپتال پارک — doctor's, bank, hospital, park named) as civic manners.

conduct at the railway station, the bus stand, the airport and the bazaar/market, place by place.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: conduct in public places (ذاکٹر بیگ ہسپتال پارک — doctor's, bank, hospital, park named) as civic manners. Give one real example from your own life or from the Pakistan you live in, and name the lesson's key words: manners, of, public, places. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
