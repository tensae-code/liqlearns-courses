Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) community and state defined; state and government distinguished; why government is needed.  b) it has nothing to do with how Pakistan governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) community and state defined; state and government distinguished; why government is needed.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
