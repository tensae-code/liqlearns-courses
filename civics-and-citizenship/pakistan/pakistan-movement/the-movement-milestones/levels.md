# The Movement Milestones — depth

This lesson is ONE lesson with no sub lessons: it teaches a single thing, whole. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: the road by its dated milestones — the Aligarh Movement, the All-India Muslim League 1906, the Lucknow Pact 1916, the Khilafat Movement 1919, the Lahore Resolution 1940 and the 3 June 1947 plan. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
