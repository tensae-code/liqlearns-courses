# The Movement Milestones — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** the road by its dated milestones — the Aligarh Movement, the All-India Muslim League 1906, the Lucknow Pact 1916, the Khilafat Movement 1919, the Lahore Resolution 1940 and the 3 June 1947 plan.
