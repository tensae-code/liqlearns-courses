# The Movement Milestones — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) the road by its dated milestones — the Aligarh Movement, the All-India Muslim League 1906, the Lucknow Pact 1916, the Khilafat Movement 1919, the Lahore Resolution 1940 and the 3 June 1947 plan.  b) it has nothing to do with how Pakistan governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) the road by its dated milestones — the Aligarh Movement, the All-India Muslim League 1906, the Lucknow Pact 1916, the Khilafat Movement 1919, the Lahore Resolution 1940 and the 3 June 1947 plan.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
