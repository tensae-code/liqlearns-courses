# Ideology of Pakistan — depth

This lesson is ONE lesson with no sub lessons: it teaches a single thing, whole. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: "ideology" defined (with Dr. George's and the World Encyclopaedia's definitions quoted), then Pakistan's ideology stated — an ideological state founded on Islam, whose principles are Islam's own; the Quaid-e-Azam and Allama Iqbal presented as the ideology's two voices. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
