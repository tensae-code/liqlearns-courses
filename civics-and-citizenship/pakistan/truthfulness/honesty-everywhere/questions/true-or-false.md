# Honesty Everywhere — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** a 20-page chapter on one virtue — honesty and its importance, honesty at home, honesty at school, truthfulness, always speaking truthfully, confessing with compassion (شفقت کا اعتراف).
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that Honesty Everywhere — and that it has no connection to the rest of Pakistan's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Pakistan's own course, in its own order.
