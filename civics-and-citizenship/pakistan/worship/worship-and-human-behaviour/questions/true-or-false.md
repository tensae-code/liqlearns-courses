# Worship and Human Behaviour — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** the effects of places of worship and of worship itself on human behaviour (عبادت گاہوں اور عبادات کے انسانی رویوں پر اثرات), and social welfare through worship (سماجی بہبود اور عبادات), religion by religion — Buddhism, Zoroastrianism, Sikhism and Islam among the heads.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that Worship and Human Behaviour — and that it has no connection to the rest of Pakistan's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Pakistan's own course, in its own order.
