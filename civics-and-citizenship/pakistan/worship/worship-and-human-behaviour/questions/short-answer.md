# Worship and Human Behaviour — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** the effects of places of worship and of worship itself on human behaviour (عبادت گاہوں اور عبادات کے انسانی رویوں پر اثرات), and social welfare through worship (سماجی بہبود اور عبادات), religion by religion — Buddhism, Zoroastrianism, Sikhism and Islam among the heads.
