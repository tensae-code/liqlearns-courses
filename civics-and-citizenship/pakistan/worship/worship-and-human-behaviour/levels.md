# Worship and Human Behaviour — depth

This lesson is ONE lesson with no sub lessons: it teaches a single thing, whole. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: the effects of places of worship and of worship itself on human behaviour (عبادت گاہوں اور عبادات کے انسانی رویوں پر اثرات), and social welfare through worship (سماجی بہبود اور عبادات), religion by religion — Buddhism, Zoroastrianism, Sikhism and Islam among the heads. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
