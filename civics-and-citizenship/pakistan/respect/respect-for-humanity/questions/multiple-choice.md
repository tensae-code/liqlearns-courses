# Respect for Humanity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) respect owed to the human being as such.  b) it has nothing to do with how Pakistan governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) respect owed to the human being as such.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) respect owed to the human being as such.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Pakistan
**Answer: a) respect owed to the human being as such.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
