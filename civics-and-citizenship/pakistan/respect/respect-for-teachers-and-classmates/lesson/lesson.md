# Respect for Teachers and Classmates

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Respect for Teachers and Classmates. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Pakistan's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

teachers hold the rank of parents and give children the wealth of knowledge; classmates are like siblings to each other, so always respect the teacher; the education a teacher gives helps a person succeed in life.

respect for teachers and classmates as a standing value.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: teachers hold the rank of parents and give children the wealth of knowledge; classmates are like siblings to each other, so always respect the teacher; the education a teacher gives helps a person succeed in life. Give one real example from your own life or from the Pakistan you live in, and name the lesson's key words: respect, for, teachers, and. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
