# Foreign Policy and World Bodies — depth

This lesson is ONE lesson with no sub lessons: it teaches a single thing, whole. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: the meaning of foreign policy, the factors determining Pakistan's, its salient features and Pakistan's relations with other states; Pakistan's role in the Organisation of Islamic Cooperation and the Economic Cooperation Organisation; and the United Nations, its organs, and the UN's role in implementing international. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
