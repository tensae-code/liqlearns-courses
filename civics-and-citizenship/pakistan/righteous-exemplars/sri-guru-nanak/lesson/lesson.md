# Sri Guru Nanak

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Sri Guru Nanak. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Pakistan's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

born 14 April 1469 at Talwandi (now Nankana Sahib, Punjab, Pakistan); learned Sanskrit and Persian; his saying that there is no Hindu and no Muslim — all must walk the path God shows; founded Kartarpur, where he spent his last days and died 22 September 1539; the langar (free kitchen) as service.
