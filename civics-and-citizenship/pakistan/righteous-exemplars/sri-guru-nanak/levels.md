# Sri Guru Nanak — depth

This lesson is ONE lesson with no sub lessons: it teaches a single thing, whole. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: born 14 April 1469 at Talwandi (now Nankana Sahib, Punjab, Pakistan); learned Sanskrit and Persian; his saying that there is no Hindu and no Muslim — all must walk the path God shows; founded Kartarpur, where he spent his last days and died 22 September 1539; the langar (free kitchen) as service. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
