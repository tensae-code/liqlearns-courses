# Sri Guru Nanak — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** born 14 April 1469 at Talwandi (now Nankana Sahib, Punjab, Pakistan); learned Sanskrit and Persian; his saying that there is no Hindu and no Muslim — all must walk the path God shows; founded Kartarpur, where he spent his last days and died 22 September 1539; the langar (free kitchen) as service.
