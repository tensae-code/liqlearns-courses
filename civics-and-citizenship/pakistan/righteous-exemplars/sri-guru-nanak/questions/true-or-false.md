# Sri Guru Nanak — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** born 14 April 1469 at Talwandi (now Nankana Sahib, Punjab, Pakistan); learned Sanskrit and Persian; his saying that there is no Hindu and no Muslim — all must walk the path God shows; founded Kartarpur, where he spent his last days and died 22 September 1539; the langar (free kitchen) as service.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that Sri Guru Nanak — and that it has no connection to the rest of Pakistan's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Pakistan's own course, in its own order.
