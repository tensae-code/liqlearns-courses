Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Ibrahim's life as a journey of preaching one God — leaving his people, travelling with Hazrat Sara and Hazrat Lut, settling in Palestine, then Egypt, and the building of the Kaaba with Hazrat Ismail.

**2.** This lesson teaches that Ibrahim the Journey — and that it has no connection to the rest of Pakistan's story.
