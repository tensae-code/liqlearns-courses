# Ibrahim the Journey — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** Ibrahim's life as a journey of preaching one God — leaving his people, travelling with Hazrat Sara and Hazrat Lut, settling in Palestine, then Egypt, and the building of the Kaaba with Hazrat Ismail.
