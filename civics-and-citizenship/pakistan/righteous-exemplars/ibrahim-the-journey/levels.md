# Ibrahim the Journey — depth

This lesson is ONE lesson with no sub lessons: it teaches a single thing, whole. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: Ibrahim's life as a journey of preaching one God — leaving his people, travelling with Hazrat Sara and Hazrat Lut, settling in Palestine, then Egypt, and the building of the Kaaba with Hazrat Ismail. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
