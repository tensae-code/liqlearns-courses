# Ibrahim the Journey

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Ibrahim the Journey. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Pakistan's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Ibrahim's life as a journey of preaching one God — leaving his people, travelling with Hazrat Sara and Hazrat Lut, settling in Palestine, then Egypt, and the building of the Kaaba with Hazrat Ismail.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Ibrahim's life as a journey of preaching one God — leaving his people, travelling with Hazrat Sara and Hazrat Lut, settling in Palestine, then Egypt, and the building of the Kaaba with Hazrat Ismail. Give one real example from your own life or from the Pakistan you live in, and name the lesson's key words: ibrahim, journey. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: Ibrahim's life as a journey of preaching one God — leaving his people, travelling with Hazrat Sara and Hazrat Lut, settling in Palestine, then Egypt, and the building of the Kaaba with Hazrat Ismail.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
