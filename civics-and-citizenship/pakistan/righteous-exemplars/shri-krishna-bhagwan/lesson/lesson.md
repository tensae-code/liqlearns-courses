# Shri Krishna Bhagwan

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Shri Krishna Bhagwan. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Pakistan's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Krishna born at Mathura to Vasudev and Devaki; known as a teacher through the Bhagavad Gita's teachings; famed for good thoughts, the lesson of love for the world, reforming people, skill in arts and music, and open-hearted help for the poor and needy.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Krishna born at Mathura to Vasudev and Devaki; known as a teacher through the Bhagavad Gita's teachings; famed for good thoughts, the lesson of love for the world, reforming people, skill in arts and music, and open-hearted help for the poor and needy. Give one real example from your own life or from the Pakistan you live in, and name the lesson's key words: shri, krishna, bhagwan. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
