# Shri Krishna Bhagwan — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** Krishna born at Mathura to Vasudev and Devaki; known as a teacher through the Bhagavad Gita's teachings; famed for good thoughts, the lesson of love for the world, reforming people, skill in arts and music, and open-hearted help for the poor and needy.
