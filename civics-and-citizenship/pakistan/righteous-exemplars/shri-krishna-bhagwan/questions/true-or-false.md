# Shri Krishna Bhagwan — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Krishna born at Mathura to Vasudev and Devaki; known as a teacher through the Bhagavad Gita's teachings; famed for good thoughts, the lesson of love for the world, reforming people, skill in arts and music, and open-hearted help for the poor and needy.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that Shri Krishna Bhagwan — and that it has no connection to the rest of Pakistan's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Pakistan's own course, in its own order.
