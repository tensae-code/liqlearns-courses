# Jesus — depth

This lesson is ONE lesson with no sub lessons: it teaches a single thing, whole. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: Jesus, called Hazrat Isa and Ibn-e-Maryam in Islam, born at Bethlehem about two thousand years ago to Hazrat Maryam; Christianity's holy book is the Bible (Injil); his teachings — love God most, honour parents, care for neighbours, do not hoard wealth — and his miracles of healing. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
