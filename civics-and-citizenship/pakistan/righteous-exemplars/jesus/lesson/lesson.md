# Jesus

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Jesus. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Pakistan's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Jesus, called Hazrat Isa and Ibn-e-Maryam in Islam, born at Bethlehem about two thousand years ago to Hazrat Maryam; Christianity's holy book is the Bible (Injil); his teachings — love God most, honour parents, care for neighbours, do not hoard wealth — and his miracles of healing.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Jesus, called Hazrat Isa and Ibn-e-Maryam in Islam, born at Bethlehem about two thousand years ago to Hazrat Maryam; Christianity's holy book is the Bible (Injil); his teachings — love God most, honour parents, care for neighbours, do not hoard wealth — and his miracles of healing. Give one real example from your own life or from the Pakistan you live in, and name the lesson's key words: jesus. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
