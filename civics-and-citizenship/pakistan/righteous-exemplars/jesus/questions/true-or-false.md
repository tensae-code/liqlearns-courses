# Jesus — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Jesus, called Hazrat Isa and Ibn-e-Maryam in Islam, born at Bethlehem about two thousand years ago to Hazrat Maryam; Christianity's holy book is the Bible (Injil); his teachings — love God most, honour parents, care for neighbours, do not hoard wealth — and his miracles of healing.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that Jesus — and that it has no connection to the rest of Pakistan's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Pakistan's own course, in its own order.
