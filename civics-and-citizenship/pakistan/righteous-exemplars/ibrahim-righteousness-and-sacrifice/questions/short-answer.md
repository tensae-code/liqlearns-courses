# Ibrahim Righteousness and Sacrifice — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** righteousness defined as truthfulness, honesty and trustworthiness in word and act, shown in Ibrahim's whole life; and his readiness to sacrifice his son at God's command as the summit of obedience.
