# Ibrahim Righteousness and Sacrifice — depth

This lesson is ONE lesson with no sub lessons: it teaches a single thing, whole. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: righteousness defined as truthfulness, honesty and trustworthiness in word and act, shown in Ibrahim's whole life; and his readiness to sacrifice his son at God's command as the summit of obedience. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
