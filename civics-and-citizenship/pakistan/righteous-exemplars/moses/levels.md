# Moses — depth

This lesson is ONE lesson with no sub lessons: it teaches a single thing, whole. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: Moses, raised in Pharaoh's palace, led his people out of Egypt as Pharaoh drowned in the sea; God gave him commands in the Torah — summarised for children as worship God alone, honour father and mother, do not steal, do not bear false witness against a neighbour. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
