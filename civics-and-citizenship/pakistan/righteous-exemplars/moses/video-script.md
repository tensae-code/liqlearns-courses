# Video script — Moses

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Moses, raised in Pharaoh's palace, led his people out of Egypt as Pharaoh drowned in the sea; God gave him commands in the Torah — summarised for children as worship God alone, honour father and mother, do not steal, do not bear false witness against a neighbour.
