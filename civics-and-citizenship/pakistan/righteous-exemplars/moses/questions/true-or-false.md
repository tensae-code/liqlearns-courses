# Moses — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Moses, raised in Pharaoh's palace, led his people out of Egypt as Pharaoh drowned in the sea; God gave him commands in the Torah — summarised for children as worship God alone, honour father and mother, do not steal, do not bear false witness against a neighbour.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that Moses — and that it has no connection to the rest of Pakistan's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Pakistan's own course, in its own order.
