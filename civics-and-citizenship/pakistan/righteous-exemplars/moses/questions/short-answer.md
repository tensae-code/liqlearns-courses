# Moses — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** Moses, raised in Pharaoh's palace, led his people out of Egypt as Pharaoh drowned in the sea; God gave him commands in the Torah — summarised for children as worship God alone, honour father and mother, do not steal, do not bear false witness against a neighbour.
