# Christianity — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Christianity began with Hazrat Isa (Jesus); the Bible in two parts, Old and New Testaments; Christian beliefs and Jesus's teachings — love God above all, love your neighbour as yourself, do good work, honour mother and father, love even enemies.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that Christianity — and that it has no connection to the rest of Pakistan's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Pakistan's own course, in its own order.
