# Christianity

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Christianity. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Pakistan's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Christianity began with Hazrat Isa (Jesus); the Bible in two parts, Old and New Testaments; Christian beliefs and Jesus's teachings — love God above all, love your neighbour as yourself, do good work, honour mother and father, love even enemies.
