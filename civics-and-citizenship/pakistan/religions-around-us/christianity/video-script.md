The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

This lesson is about one thing.
Christianity began with Hazrat Isa (Jesus); the Bible in two parts, Old and New Testaments; Christian beliefs and Jesus's teachings — love God above all, love your neighbour as yourself, do good work, honour mother and father, love even enemies.
