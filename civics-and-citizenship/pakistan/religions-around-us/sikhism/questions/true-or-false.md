# Sikhism — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** most of the world's Sikhs' holiest places are in Pakistan — Guru Nanak born at Nankana Sahib; the Guru Granth Sahib (in Punjabi) as the holy book; kirtan and selfless service (seva) as beliefs; respect owed to every religion without distinction.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that Sikhism — and that it has no connection to the rest of Pakistan's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Pakistan's own course, in its own order.
