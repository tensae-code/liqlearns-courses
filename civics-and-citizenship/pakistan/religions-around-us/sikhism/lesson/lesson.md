# Sikhism

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Sikhism. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Pakistan's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

most of the world's Sikhs' holiest places are in Pakistan — Guru Nanak born at Nankana Sahib; the Guru Granth Sahib (in Punjabi) as the holy book; kirtan and selfless service (seva) as beliefs; respect owed to every religion without distinction.
