# Judaism

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Judaism. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Pakistan's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Hazrat Musa (Moses) received the Torah (Torait) as guidance for the Jews; its holy book is called Tanakh, of which the Torah scroll is the first part, in Hebrew; the Ten Commandments named as its core commands.
