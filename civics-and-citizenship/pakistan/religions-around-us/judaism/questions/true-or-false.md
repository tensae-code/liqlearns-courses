Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Hazrat Musa (Moses) received the Torah (Torait) as guidance for the Jews; its holy book is called Tanakh, of which the Torah scroll is the first part, in Hebrew; the Ten Commandments named as its core commands.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that Judaism — and that it has no connection to the rest of Pakistan's story.
