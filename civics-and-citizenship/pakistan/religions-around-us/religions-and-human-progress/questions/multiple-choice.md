# Religions and Human Progress — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) what the religions have contributed to humanity's development.  b) it has nothing to do with how Pakistan governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) what the religions have contributed to humanity's development.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) what the religions have contributed to humanity's development.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Pakistan
**Answer: a) what the religions have contributed to humanity's development.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
