# Social Welfare and Character

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Social Welfare and Character. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Pakistan's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

every religion commands social welfare — helping the poor, the needy and orphans financially so their needs are met, feeding the hungry — and builds character; good character is presented as religion's social fruit.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: every religion commands social welfare — helping the poor, the needy and orphans financially so their needs are met, feeding the hungry — and builds character; good character is presented as religion's social fruit. Give one real example from your own life or from the Pakistan you live in, and name the lesson's key words: social, welfare, and, character. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
