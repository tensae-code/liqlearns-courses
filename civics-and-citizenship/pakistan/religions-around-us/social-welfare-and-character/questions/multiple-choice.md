Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) every religion commands social welfare — helping the poor, the needy and orphans financially so their needs are met, feeding the hungry — and builds character; good character is presented as religion's social fruit.  b) it has nothing to do with how Pakistan governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
