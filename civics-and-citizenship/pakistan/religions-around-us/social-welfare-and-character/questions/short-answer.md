# Social Welfare and Character — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** every religion commands social welfare — helping the poor, the needy and orphans financially so their needs are met, feeding the hungry — and builds character; good character is presented as religion's social fruit.
