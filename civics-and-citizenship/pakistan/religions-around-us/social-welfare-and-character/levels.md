# Social Welfare and Character — depth

This lesson is ONE lesson with no sub lessons: it teaches a single thing, whole. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: every religion commands social welfare — helping the poor, the needy and orphans financially so their needs are met, feeding the hungry — and builds character; good character is presented as religion's social fruit. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
