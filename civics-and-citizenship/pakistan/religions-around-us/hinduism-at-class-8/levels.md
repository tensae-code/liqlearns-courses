# Hinduism at Class 8 — depth

This lesson is ONE lesson with no sub lessons: it teaches a single thing, whole. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: Hinduism as a religion of tolerance, love and sacrifice; worship of one God (Ishwar) in the mandir; Ram, Krishna, Vishnu and the Trimurti — Brahma, Vishnu, Mahesh — and the four Vedas (Rig, Sama, Yajur, Atharva) named. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
