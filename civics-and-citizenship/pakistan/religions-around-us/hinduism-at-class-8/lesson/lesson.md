# Hinduism at Class 8

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Hinduism at Class 8. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Pakistan's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Hinduism as a religion of tolerance, love and sacrifice; worship of one God (Ishwar) in the mandir; Ram, Krishna, Vishnu and the Trimurti — Brahma, Vishnu, Mahesh — and the four Vedas (Rig, Sama, Yajur, Atharva) named.
