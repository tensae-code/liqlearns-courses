# Hinduism at Class 8 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** Hinduism as a religion of tolerance, love and sacrifice; worship of one God (Ishwar) in the mandir; Ram, Krishna, Vishnu and the Trimurti — Brahma, Vishnu, Mahesh — and the four Vedas (Rig, Sama, Yajur, Atharva) named.
