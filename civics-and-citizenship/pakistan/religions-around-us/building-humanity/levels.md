# Building Humanity — depth

This lesson is ONE lesson with no sub lessons: it teaches a single thing, whole. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: through a school story (Michael, Dada Jan and a school programme on religions) why religions arose — the world's religions and their law together build humanity; Professor John Joseph's question — if law is made by humans, why do people not obey it? — opens the inquiry. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
