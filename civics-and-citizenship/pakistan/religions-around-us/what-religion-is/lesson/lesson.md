# What Religion is

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: What Religion is. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Pakistan's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

religion teaches human beings how to live a good life; the world holds many religions, and they teach that the Creator of the universe is one God, who made the moon, stars, sun, fruits and mountains, and is to be worshipped alone.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: religion teaches human beings how to live a good life; the world holds many religions, and they teach that the Creator of the universe is one God, who made the moon, stars, sun, fruits and mountains, and is to be worshipped alone. Give one real example from your own life or from the Pakistan you live in, and name the lesson's key words: what, religion, is. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.

3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
