# Purposes of Religion — depth

This lesson is ONE lesson with no sub lessons: it teaches a single thing, whole. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: seven numbered purposes, from the interpretation of life's occasions (توضیح) to religion as a power of social control and a teacher of humility, contentment and good character. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
