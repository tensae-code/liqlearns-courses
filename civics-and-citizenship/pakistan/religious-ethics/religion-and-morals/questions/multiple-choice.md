# Religion and Morals — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) religion is a heartfelt belief whose teachings, practised firmly, keep a person moral; good morals cannot be separated from religion.  b) it has nothing to do with how Pakistan governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) religion is a heartfelt belief whose teachings, practised firmly, keep a person moral; good morals cannot be separated from religion.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) religion is a heartfelt belief whose teachings, practised firmly, keep a person moral; good morals cannot be separated from religion.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Pakistan
**Answer: a) religion is a heartfelt belief whose teachings, practised firmly, keep a person moral; good morals cannot be separated from religion.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
