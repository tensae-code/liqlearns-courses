# Religion and Morals — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** religion is a heartfelt belief whose teachings, practised firmly, keep a person moral; good morals cannot be separated from religion.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** religion is a heartfelt belief whose teachings, practised firmly, keep a person moral; good morals cannot be separated from religion.
