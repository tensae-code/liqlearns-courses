# Respect for All Life — depth

This lesson is ONE lesson with no sub lessons: it teaches a single thing, whole. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: human life is a trust from God, to be protected and never wasted; all religions teach caring for the rights of others — parents, siblings, relatives, neighbours; and the care extends to animals and plants — watering plants, feeding animals and birds, protecting them from disease. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
