# Respect for All Life — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** human life is a trust from God, to be protected and never wasted; all religions teach caring for the rights of others — parents, siblings, relatives, neighbours; and the care extends to animals and plants — watering plants, feeding animals and birds, protecting them from disease.
