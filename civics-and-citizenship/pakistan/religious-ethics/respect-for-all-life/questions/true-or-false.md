# Respect for All Life — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** human life is a trust from God, to be protected and never wasted; all religions teach caring for the rights of others — parents, siblings, relatives, neighbours; and the care extends to animals and plants — watering plants, feeding animals and birds, protecting them from disease.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that Respect for All Life — and that it has no connection to the rest of Pakistan's story.
