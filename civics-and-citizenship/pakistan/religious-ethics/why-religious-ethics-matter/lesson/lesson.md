# Why Religious Ethics Matter

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Why Religious Ethics Matter. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Pakistan's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

all the world's religions rest on moral foundations and teach doing good and avoiding evil — honesty, service, truthfulness, keeping promises, punctuality, respecting elders and good conduct; bad acts (lying, oppression, breaking promises, backbiting) are named as what ethics forbids.

Give one real example from your own life or from the Pakistan you live in, and name the lesson's key words: why, religious, ethics, matter. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
