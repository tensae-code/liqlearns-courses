# Why Religious Ethics Matter — depth

This lesson is ONE lesson with no sub lessons: it teaches a single thing, whole. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: all the world's religions rest on moral foundations and teach doing good and avoiding evil — honesty, service, truthfulness, keeping promises, punctuality, respecting elders and good conduct; bad acts (lying, oppression, breaking promises, backbiting) are named as what ethics forbids. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
