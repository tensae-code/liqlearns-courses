# Why Religious Ethics Matter — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** all the world's religions rest on moral foundations and teach doing good and avoiding evil — honesty, service, truthfulness, keeping promises, punctuality, respecting elders and good conduct; bad acts (lying, oppression, breaking promises, backbiting) are named as what ethics forbids.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that Why Religious Ethics Matter — and that it has no connection to the rest of Pakistan's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Pakistan's own course, in its own order.
