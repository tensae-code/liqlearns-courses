# Why Religious Ethics Matter — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** all the world's religions rest on moral foundations and teach doing good and avoiding evil — honesty, service, truthfulness, keeping promises, punctuality, respecting elders and good conduct; bad acts (lying, oppression, breaking promises, backbiting) are named as what ethics forbids.
