# Human Rights History and Types — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) the history of human rights and their kinds.  b) it has nothing to do with how Pakistan governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) the history of human rights and their kinds.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) the history of human rights and their kinds.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Pakistan
**Answer: a) the history of human rights and their kinds.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
