# Constitution and Minority Rights — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** the 1973 Constitution of the Islamic Republic of Pakistan secured the minorities' rights and religious freedom, and Quaid-e-Azam's 11 August address promised minorities complete freedom in Pakistan — the stated reason this book teaches every religion with respect (summarised from the preface, not transcribed).
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that Constitution and Minority Rights — and that it has no connection to the rest of Pakistan's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Pakistan's own course, in its own order.
