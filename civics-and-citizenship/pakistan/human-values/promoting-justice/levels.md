# Promoting Justice — depth

This lesson is ONE lesson with no sub lessons: it teaches a single thing, whole. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: ways of promoting justice in society; injustice (discrimination, prejudice) defined; identifying injustices in Pakistani society; the requirements of justice and activities that produce it locally, nationally and globally. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
