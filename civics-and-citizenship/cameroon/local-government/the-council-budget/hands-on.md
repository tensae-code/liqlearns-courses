# Hands-on — The Council Budget

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Give a household one month of money on one page: what comes in, what each thing costs, what is left. A council does the same at the scale of a town. This lesson is the council budget.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the council budget is the council's money planned in the open — what comes in, what it is used for — and civic spirit is what makes citizens care that it is managed well. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in the village. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
