# Video script — The Council Budget

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The council budget is the council's money planned in the open — what comes in, what it is used for — and civic spirit is what makes citizens care that it is managed well.

What the council budget is — and its use. The budget is the council's planned statement of its resources and its spending for a period — the tool that turns money into services in an order citizens can follow. A budget is a promise in numbers: each line says what the community will get for its money.

Civic spirit — and managing the budget well. Civic spirit is the citizen's care for the common good — and it is why the budget must be managed well, spent on what it promised, and accounted for. Good management is not a favour from officials; it is what the citizens' own civic spirit demands and watches.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
