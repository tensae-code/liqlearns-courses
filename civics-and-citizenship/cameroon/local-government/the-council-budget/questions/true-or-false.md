# The Council Budget — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The council budget is the council's money planned in the open — what comes in, what it is used for — and civic spirit is what makes citizens care that it is managed well.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a citizen's own home, school or village in Cameroon.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Cameroon and lived there.
