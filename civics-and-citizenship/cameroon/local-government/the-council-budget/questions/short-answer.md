# The Council Budget — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** The council budget is the council's money planned in the open — what comes in, what it is used for — and civic spirit is what makes citizens care that it is managed well.

**2.** What does the part "Civic spirit — and managing the budget well" teach?
**Answer:** Civic spirit is the citizen's care for the common good — and it is why the budget must be managed well, spent on what it promised, and accounted for.
