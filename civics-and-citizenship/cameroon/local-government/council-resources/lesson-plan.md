# Lesson plan — Resources of the Council

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Cameroon · **Title:** Local Government · **Lesson:** Resources of the Council
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Local Government (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a council can only serve with resources: they come from known sources — above all the taxes of its own citizens — which is why every citizen must pay council taxes honestly.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — Local Government — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Council resources and their sources** — A council's resources are the money and means it may use, and they come from known sources — its own revenues, grants and the taxes of its area.
2. **Why every citizen must pay council taxes** — Council taxes are every citizen's share in the services all enjoy — paying them is a duty of citizenship, because without them the council can do nothing.

Gate pool: the prove-it check for each sub lesson is at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson — drawn from this lesson's question files (questions/).
