# Hands-on — Resources of the Council

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
A council promises a road, a market, clean water. Ask the hard question: with what? This lesson follows the money in — the resources of the council and where they come from.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: a council can only serve with resources: they come from known sources — above all the taxes of its own citizens — which is why every citizen must pay council taxes honestly. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in the village. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
