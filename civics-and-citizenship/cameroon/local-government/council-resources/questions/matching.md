# Resources of the Council — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** Council resources and their sources — a)

**2.** Why every citizen must pay council taxes — b)


Meanings:
a) A council's resources are the money and means it may use, and they come from known sources — its own revenues, grants and the taxes of its area.
b) Council taxes are every citizen's share in the services all enjoy — paying them is a duty of citizenship, because without them the council can do nothing.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
