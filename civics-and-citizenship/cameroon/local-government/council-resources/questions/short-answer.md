# Resources of the Council — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** A council can only serve with resources: they come from known sources — above all the taxes of its own citizens — which is why every citizen must pay council taxes honestly.

**2.** What does the part "Why every citizen must pay council taxes" teach?
**Answer:** Council taxes are every citizen's share in the services all enjoy — paying them is a duty of citizenship, because without them the council can do nothing.
