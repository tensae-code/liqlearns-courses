# Resources of the Council — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) a council can only serve with resources: they come from known sources — above all the taxes of its own citizens — which is why every citizen must pay council taxes honestly  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — Council resources and their sources?
a) A council's resources are the money and means it may use, and they come from known sources — its own revenues, grants and the taxes of its area.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first sub lesson teaches.
