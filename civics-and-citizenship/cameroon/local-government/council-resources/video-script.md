# Video script — Resources of the Council

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A council can only serve with resources: they come from known sources — above all the taxes of its own citizens — which is why every citizen must pay council taxes honestly.

Council resources and their sources. A council's resources are the money and means it may use, and they come from known sources — its own revenues, grants and the taxes of its area. A source that is known can be checked; the book teaches citizens to know where their council's money should come from.

Why every citizen must pay council taxes. Council taxes are every citizen's share in the services all enjoy — paying them is a duty of citizenship, because without them the council can do nothing. The tax is the lesson's circle closing: citizens fund the council, and the council serves the citizens. Break either side and both fail.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
