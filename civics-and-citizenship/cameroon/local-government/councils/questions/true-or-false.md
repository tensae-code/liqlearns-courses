# Councils — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A council is the elected local body that runs a community's own affairs — cameroon has types of councils, and each carries defined functions for the people of its area.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a citizen's own home, school or village in Cameroon.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Cameroon and lived there.
