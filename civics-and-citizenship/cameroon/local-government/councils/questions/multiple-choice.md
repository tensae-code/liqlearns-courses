# Councils — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) a council is the elected local body that runs a community's own affairs — Cameroon has types of councils, and each carries defined functions for the people of its area  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — What a council is?
a) A council is the body chosen to manage the affairs of a local community — its own people deciding its own local matters.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first sub lesson teaches.
