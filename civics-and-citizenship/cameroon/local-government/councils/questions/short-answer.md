# Councils — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** A council is the elected local body that runs a community's own affairs — cameroon has types of councils, and each carries defined functions for the people of its area.

**2.** What does the part "The types of councils in Cameroon" teach?
**Answer:** Cameroon has types of councils, matching the types of communities they serve.
