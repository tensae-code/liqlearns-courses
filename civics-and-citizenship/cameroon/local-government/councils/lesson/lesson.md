# Councils

## Do it first

Your street needs a repaired road and a cleaned market. Who decides, and who pays? Not the distant capital first — this lesson's answer is the council, the citizen's first state.

## Sub lesson 1 — What a council is

A council is the body chosen to manage the affairs of a local community — its own people deciding its own local matters.

The council is where the state becomes a neighbour: the first public authority a citizen can walk to.

## Sub lesson 2 — The types of councils in Cameroon

Cameroon has types of councils, matching the types of communities they serve.

Each type fits its area's size and needs; the pupil learns to name the council that serves his own place.

## Sub lesson 3 — The functions of a council

A council carries defined functions — the local services, works and order of its area.

Functions are the council's job description: what citizens may rightly demand of it is written in them.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: a council is the elected local body that runs a community's own affairs — Cameroon has types of councils, and each carries defined functions for the people of its area. Give one real example from your own life in Cameroon, and name the lesson's key words: council, types, functions. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in your own language as well as in English — Cameroon's own book teaches citizenship for life at home and in the village, not only for the classroom.
