# Video script — Councils

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A council is the elected local body that runs a community's own affairs — cameroon has types of councils, and each carries defined functions for the people of its area.

What a council is. A council is the body chosen to manage the affairs of a local community — its own people deciding its own local matters. The council is where the state becomes a neighbour: the first public authority a citizen can walk to.

The types of councils in Cameroon. Cameroon has types of councils, matching the types of communities they serve. Each type fits its area's size and needs; the pupil learns to name the council that serves his own place.

The functions of a council. A council carries defined functions — the local services, works and order of its area. Functions are the council's job description: what citizens may rightly demand of it is written in them.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
