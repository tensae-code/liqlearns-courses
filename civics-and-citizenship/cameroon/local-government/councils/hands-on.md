# Hands-on — Councils

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Your street needs a repaired road and a cleaned market. Who decides, and who pays? Not the distant capital first — this lesson's answer is the council, the citizen's first state.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: a council is the elected local body that runs a community's own affairs — Cameroon has types of councils, and each carries defined functions for the people of its area. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in the village. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
