# Lesson plan — Councils

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Cameroon · **Title:** Local Government · **Lesson:** Councils
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Local Government (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a council is the elected local body that runs a community's own affairs — Cameroon has types of councils, and each carries defined functions for the people of its area.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — Local Government — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. ## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **What a council is** — A council is the body chosen to manage the affairs of a local community — its own people deciding its own local matters.
2. **The types of councils in Cameroon** — Cameroon has types of councils, matching the types of communities they serve.
3. **The functions of a council** — A council carries defined functions — the local services, works and order of its area.

Gate pool: the prove-it check for each sub lesson is at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson — drawn from this lesson's question files (questions/).
