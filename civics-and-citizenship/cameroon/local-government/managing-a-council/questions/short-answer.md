# The Management of a Council — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** A council is managed in the open: it has an organisation chart, its officials carry defined duties, the municipal council is composed in a known way, and its positions are gained by election, not by favour.

**2.** What does the part "Composition — and how positions are gained" teach?
**Answer:** The municipal council is composed of members in a known number and manner, and its positions are gained through election by the people of the area.
