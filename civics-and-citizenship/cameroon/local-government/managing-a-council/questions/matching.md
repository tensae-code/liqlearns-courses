# The Management of a Council — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** The organisation chart and officials' duties — a)

**2.** Composition — and how positions are gained — b)


Meanings:
a) A council works from an organisation chart — its services and posts in order — and each official, from the mayor down, carries defined duties.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
