# The Management of a Council — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) a council is managed in the open: it has an organisation chart, its officials carry defined duties, the municipal council is composed in a known way, and its positions are gained by election, not by favour  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — The organisation chart and officials' duties?
a) A council works from an organisation chart — its services and posts in order — and each official, from the mayor down, carries defined duties.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first sub lesson teaches.
