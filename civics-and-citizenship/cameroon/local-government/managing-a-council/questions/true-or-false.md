# The Management of a Council — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A council is managed in the open: it has an organisation chart, its officials carry defined duties, the municipal council is composed in a known way, and its positions are gained by election, not by favour.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a citizen's own home, school or village in Cameroon.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Cameroon and lived there.
