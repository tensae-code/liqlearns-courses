# Lesson plan — The Management of a Council

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Cameroon · **Title:** Local Government · **Lesson:** The Management of a Council
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Local Government (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a council is managed in the open: it has an organisation chart, its officials carry defined duties, the municipal council is composed in a known way, and its positions are gained by election, not by favour.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — Local Government — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The organisation chart and officials' duties** — A council works from an organisation chart — its services and posts in order — and each official, from the mayor down, carries defined duties.
2. **Composition — and how positions are gained** — The municipal council is composed of members in a known number and manner, and its positions are gained through election by the people of the area.

Gate pool: the prove-it check for each sub lesson is at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson — drawn from this lesson's question files (questions/).
