# Hands-on — The Management of a Council

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Take the council of the last lesson and open its doors. Who sits where, who signs what, and how did each person get there? This lesson is the management of a council.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: a council is managed in the open: it has an organisation chart, its officials carry defined duties, the municipal council is composed in a known way, and its positions are gained by election, not by favour. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in the village. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
