# Video script — The Management of a Council

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A council is managed in the open: it has an organisation chart, its officials carry defined duties, the municipal council is composed in a known way, and its positions are gained by election, not by favour.

The organisation chart and officials' duties. A council works from an organisation chart — its services and posts in order — and each official, from the mayor down, carries defined duties. The chart is the citizen's map: it shows who answers for what, so nothing is everyone's job and therefore no one's.

Composition — and how positions are gained. The municipal council is composed of members in a known number and manner, and its positions are gained through election by the people of the area. Election is the root of the council's right to act: its authority rises from the voters, and returns to them.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
