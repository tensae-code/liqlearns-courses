# The Day-to-Day Life of the Council and the Quality of Life of the Population — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The council proves itself daily: the services it provides — water, markets, roads, records and the rest — are its purpose, and they are what raise the quality of life in its area.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a citizen's own home, school or village in Cameroon.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Cameroon and lived there.
