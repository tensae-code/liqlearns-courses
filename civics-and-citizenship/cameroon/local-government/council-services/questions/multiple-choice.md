# The Day-to-Day Life of the Council and the Quality of Life of the Population — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) the council proves itself daily: the services it provides — water, markets, roads, records and the rest — are its purpose, and they are what raise the quality of life in its area  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — The services a council provides — and their purpose?
a) Day by day the council provides services — each one existing for a purpose in the population's life, from markets and roads to civil records.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first sub lesson teaches.
