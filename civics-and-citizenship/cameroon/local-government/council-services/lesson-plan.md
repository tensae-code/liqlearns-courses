# Lesson plan — The Day-to-Day Life of the Council and the Quality of Life of the Population

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Cameroon · **Title:** Local Government · **Lesson:** The Day-to-Day Life of the Council and the Quality of Life of the Population
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Local Government (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the council proves itself daily: the services it provides — water, markets, roads, records and the rest — are its purpose, and they are what raise the quality of life in its area.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — Local Government — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The services a council provides — and their purpose** — Day by day the council provides services — each one existing for a purpose in the population's life, from markets and roads to civil records.
2. **The importance of the council area** — The council area — the community the council serves — matters because it is the scale at which services meet real lives.

Gate pool: the prove-it check for each sub lesson is at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson — drawn from this lesson's question files (questions/).
