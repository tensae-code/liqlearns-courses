# Hands-on — The Day-to-Day Life of the Council and the Quality of Life of the Population

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
List everything your household used today that a council touches: the road, the market, the water point, a birth certificate. This lesson counts those services — the day-to-day life of the council.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the council proves itself daily: the services it provides — water, markets, roads, records and the rest — are its purpose, and they are what raise the quality of life in its area. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in the village. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
