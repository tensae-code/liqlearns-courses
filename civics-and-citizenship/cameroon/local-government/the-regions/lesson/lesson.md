# The Regional Organisation of Cameroon

## Do it first

Draw Cameroon's ladder under your feet: family, village, council — and now the wide level above the council. This last lesson names it: the regional organisation of Cameroon.

## Sub lesson 1 — What a region is in Cameroon

A region is one of the large divisions of Cameroon — a wide area grouping many councils under one regional organisation.

The region is the council's bigger brother: too large for a market or a street, right-sized for what councils cannot do alone.

## Sub lesson 2 — The regions' organisation and officials' duties

Each region is organised with its own officials, whose duties are defined, so the region's work can be followed and questioned.

As with the council's chart, defined duties are the citizen's handle on power.

## Sub lesson 3 — Region versus former province

The region stands where the former province stood — the book sets the two side by side so the pupil can tell what changed and what continued.

Cameroon's ladder ends here in the books in hand: family, school, village, council, region — the citizen's first state, complete.
