# Lesson plan — The Regional Organisation of Cameroon

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Cameroon · **Title:** Local Government · **Lesson:** The Regional Organisation of Cameroon
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Local Government (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** above the councils stand Cameroon's regions: a region is a large division of the country with its own organisation and officials with defined duties — the successor, in the book's account, of the former provinces.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — Local Government — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **What a region is in Cameroon** — A region is one of the large divisions of Cameroon — a wide area grouping many councils under one regional organisation.
2. **The regions' organisation and officials' duties** — Each region is organised with its own officials, whose duties are defined, so the region's work can be followed and questioned.
3. **Region versus former province** — The region stands where the former province stood — the book sets the two side by side so the pupil can tell what changed and what continued.

Gate pool: the prove-it check for each sub lesson is at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson — drawn from this lesson's question files (questions/).
