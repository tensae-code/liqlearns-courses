# Hands-on — The Regional Organisation of Cameroon

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Draw Cameroon's ladder under your feet: family, village, council — and now the wide level above the council. This last lesson names it: the regional organisation of Cameroon.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: above the councils stand Cameroon's regions: a region is a large division of the country with its own organisation and officials with defined duties — the successor, in the book's account, of the former provinces. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in the village. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
