# The Regional Organisation of Cameroon — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Above the councils stand cameroon's regions: a region is a large division of the country with its own organisation and officials with defined duties — the successor, in the book's account, of the former provinces.

**2.** What does the part "The regions' organisation and officials' duties" teach?
**Answer:** Each region is organised with its own officials, whose duties are defined, so the region's work can be followed and questioned.
