# The Regional Organisation of Cameroon — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** What a region is in Cameroon — a)

**2.** The regions' organisation and officials' duties — b)

a) A region is one of the large divisions of Cameroon — a wide area grouping many councils under one regional organisation.
