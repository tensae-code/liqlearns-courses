# The Regional Organisation of Cameroon — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Above the councils stand cameroon's regions: a region is a large division of the country with its own organisation and officials with defined duties — the successor, in the book's account, of the former provinces.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a citizen's own home, school or village in Cameroon.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Cameroon and lived there.
