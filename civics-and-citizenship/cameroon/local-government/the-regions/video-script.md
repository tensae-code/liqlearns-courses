# Video script — The Regional Organisation of Cameroon

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Above the councils stand cameroon's regions: a region is a large division of the country with its own organisation and officials with defined duties — the successor, in the book's account, of the former provinces.

What a region is in Cameroon. A region is one of the large divisions of Cameroon — a wide area grouping many councils under one regional organisation. The region is the council's bigger brother: too large for a market or a street, right-sized for what councils cannot do alone.

The regions' organisation and officials' duties. Each region is organised with its own officials, whose duties are defined, so the region's work can be followed and questioned. As with the council's chart, defined duties are the citizen's handle on power.

Region versus former province. The region stands where the former province stood — the book sets the two side by side so the pupil can tell what changed and what continued. Cameroon's ladder ends here in the books in hand: family, school, village, council, region — the citizen's first state, complete.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
