# Civics and Citizenship — Cameroon course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

Civics belongs to the country: this course is Cameroon's own —
built from Cameroon's Citizenship Education books (Books 1–2 in
one ANUCAM volume, student book, English), placed at chapter
level from the volume's complete table of contents (PARTIAL —
contents-only read; see ABOUT.md). Inside the course the
organisation is by **levels, not grades**: the titles below ARE
the levels, in path order — what the learner proves, step by
step. The grades the book comes from (secondary Books 1–2) live
only in each lesson's sources.

The path's own logic is the book's: start inside the family —
marriage, divorce and parenthood taught head-on — widen through
clan and tribe to human dignity and rights, learn the school as
an institution, then move out to the village and its traditional
authorities, and end at the council, the citizen's first state,
and the regions above it. That ladder IS the path order
(recommended, never forced — a learner can jump in higher; the
"start here instead" hints point down this ladder).

## The titles

- `the-family/` — Level 1. The family (what it is, its types,
  what unites or harms it) · marriage (forms, certificate,
  conditions, spouses' duties) · the rights and duties of a
  child in the family · the responsibilities of parents (the
  mother as first educator, parent–child communication) ·
  divorce (meaning, causes, consequences, the child's part in
  family peace) · family protection (the measures and the
  services responsible).
- `belonging/` — Level 2 (first title). The extension of the
  family — clan, tribe and ethnic group, Cameroon's major
  ethnic groups, and the dangers of tribalism.
- `rights/` — Level 2 (second title). The dignity of the human
  being (dignified and undignified behaviour, the child's
  fundamental rights) · the notion of human rights (the texts,
  abuse cases, the institutions that monitor them).
- `school-life/` — Level 2 (third title). How to start a school
  day (the eve and the morning) · from the home to the school
  (road parts and signs, responsible road use, accident causes)
  · the school institution (types, staff, student documents) ·
  how a school institution works (staff duties, councils,
  support services, internal rules) · the school environment
  (elements, pollution, school heritage) · school guidance and
  vocational counselling (series and branches, end-of-cycle
  certificates).
- `the-village/` — Level 3. The village (types, building
  materials, telling Cameroonian villages apart) · the
  traditional organisation of the village (hierarchical
  institutions, competence, powers of traditional authorities)
  · traditional social values (advantages, disadvantages,
  enhancing the positive ones) · the village in the economic
  life of the country (production, problems, harm to the
  environment) · the restoration of the village (developing
  traditional culture, the village's importance).
- `local-government/` — Level 4. Councils (what a council is,
  Cameroon's types, their functions) · the management of a
  council (organisation chart, officials' duties, composition,
  election) · resources of the council (sources, why every
  citizen pays council taxes) · the council budget (civic
  spirit, the budget's use, managing it well) · the day-to-day
  life of the council and the quality of life of the population
  (its services and their purpose) · the regional organisation
  of Cameroon (what a region is, its organisation and officials,
  region versus former province).
