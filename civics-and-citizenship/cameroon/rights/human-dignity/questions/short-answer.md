# The Dignity of the Human Being — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Every human being has dignity — worth that no one gives and no one may take away — shown in dignified behaviour, refused by undignified behaviour, and standing behind the child's fundamental rights.

**2.** What does the part "Dignified and undignified behaviour" teach?
**Answer:** Behaviour is dignified when it respects the worth of oneself and others, and undignified when it shames, degrades or treats a person as a thing.
