# The Dignity of the Human Being — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** What human dignity is — a)

**2.** Dignified and undignified behaviour — b)


Meanings:
a) Human dignity is the worth every person has simply by being human — it is not earned, bought or granted.
b) Behaviour is dignified when it respects the worth of oneself and others, and undignified when it shames, degrades or treats a person as a thing.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
