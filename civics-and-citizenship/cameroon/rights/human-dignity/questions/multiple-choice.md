# The Dignity of the Human Being — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) every human being has dignity — worth that no one gives and no one may take away — shown in dignified behaviour, refused by undignified behaviour, and standing behind the child's fundamental rights  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — What human dignity is?
a) Human dignity is the worth every person has simply by being human — it is not earned, bought or granted.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first sub lesson teaches.
