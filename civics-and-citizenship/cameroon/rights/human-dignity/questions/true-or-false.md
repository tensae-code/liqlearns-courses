# The Dignity of the Human Being — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Every human being has dignity — worth that no one gives and no one may take away — shown in dignified behaviour, refused by undignified behaviour, and standing behind the child's fundamental rights.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a citizen's own home, school or village in Cameroon.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Cameroon and lived there.
