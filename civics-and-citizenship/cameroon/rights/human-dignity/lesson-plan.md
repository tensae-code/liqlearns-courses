# Lesson plan — The Dignity of the Human Being

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Cameroon · **Title:** Rights · **Lesson:** The Dignity of the Human Being
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — Belonging, Rights and School Life (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** every human being has dignity — worth that no one gives and no one may take away — shown in dignified behaviour, refused by undignified behaviour, and standing behind the child's fundamental rights.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — Belonging, Rights and School Life — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **What human dignity is** — Human dignity is the worth every person has simply by being human — it is not earned, bought or granted.
2. **Dignified and undignified behaviour** — Behaviour is dignified when it respects the worth of oneself and others, and undignified when it shames, degrades or treats a person as a thing.
3. **The child's fundamental rights** — Because the child is a human being, the child holds fundamental rights that flow from dignity itself.

Gate pool: the prove-it check for each sub lesson is at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson — drawn from this lesson's question files (questions/).

3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
