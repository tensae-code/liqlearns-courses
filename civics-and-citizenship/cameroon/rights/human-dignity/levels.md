## Sub lesson 1 — What human dignity is
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Human dignity is the worth every person has simply by being human — it is not earned, bought or granted. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — Dignified and undignified behaviour
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Behaviour is dignified when it respects the worth of oneself and others, and undignified when it shames, degrades or treats a person as a thing. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
