# Hands-on — The Dignity of the Human Being

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Look at the youngest child you know, and at the oldest elder. Ask what makes each of them worth respect before they have done anything at all. This lesson gives that worth its name: dignity.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: every human being has dignity — worth that no one gives and no one may take away — shown in dignified behaviour, refused by undignified behaviour, and standing behind the child's fundamental rights. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in the village. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
