# Video script — The Dignity of the Human Being

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Every human being has dignity — worth that no one gives and no one may take away — shown in dignified behaviour, refused by undignified behaviour, and standing behind the child's fundamental rights.

What human dignity is. Human dignity is the worth every person has simply by being human — it is not earned, bought or granted. Because dignity comes with being human, it belongs to the child exactly as it belongs to the elder and the chief.

Dignified and undignified behaviour. Behaviour is dignified when it respects the worth of oneself and others, and undignified when it shames, degrades or treats a person as a thing. The lesson teaches pupils to judge conduct — their own first — by this measure.

The child's fundamental rights. Because the child is a human being, the child holds fundamental rights that flow from dignity itself. Rights are not gifts from adults; they are dignity written as claims the child can stand on.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
