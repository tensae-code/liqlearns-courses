# Lesson plan — The Notion of Human Rights

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Cameroon · **Title:** Rights · **Lesson:** The Notion of Human Rights
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — Belonging, Rights and School Life (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** human rights are the claims every person holds by being human — they are written in known texts, they are abused in known ways, and named institutions watch over respect for them.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **What human rights are** — Human rights are the basic claims of every human being — to life, freedom, fairness and respect — held by all, everywhere.
2. **The texts that contain them** — Human rights are written down in known texts — declarations and laws that pupils can name and point to.
3. **Abuse — and the institutions that watch** — Rights are abused when they are violated — and named institutions monitor respect for human rights and receive complaints.

Gate pool: the prove-it check for each sub lesson is at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson — drawn from this lesson's question files (questions/).
