# Hands-on — The Notion of Human Rights

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name one thing no one may rightly do to you, even if they are stronger. Now ask where that protection is written down, and who watches that it is kept. This lesson answers: human rights, their texts, and their guardians.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: human rights are the claims every person holds by being human — they are written in known texts, they are abused in known ways, and named institutions watch over respect for them. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in the village. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
