# Video script — The Notion of Human Rights

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Human rights are the claims every person holds by being human — they are written in known texts, they are abused in known ways, and named institutions watch over respect for them.

What human rights are. Human rights are the basic claims of every human being — to life, freedom, fairness and respect — held by all, everywhere. They grow out of dignity: because every person has worth, every person holds rights.

The texts that contain them. Human rights are written down in known texts — declarations and laws that pupils can name and point to. A right written in a text can be quoted back to power; that is why the texts matter and why the book teaches them.

Abuse — and the institutions that watch. Rights are abused when they are violated — and named institutions monitor respect for human rights and receive complaints. Knowing who watches is part of holding a right: a violation reported to the right institution can be stopped.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
