# The Notion of Human Rights — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Human rights are the claims every person holds by being human — they are written in known texts, they are abused in known ways, and named institutions watch over respect for them.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a citizen's own home, school or village in Cameroon.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Cameroon and lived there.
