# The Notion of Human Rights — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Human rights are the claims every person holds by being human — they are written in known texts, they are abused in known ways, and named institutions watch over respect for them.

**2.** What does the part "The texts that contain them" teach?
**Answer:** Human rights are written down in known texts — declarations and laws that pupils can name and point to.
