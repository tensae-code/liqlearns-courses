# Family Protection

## Do it first

Imagine a family in trouble — a child not in school, a parent ill, no food in the house. Who outside the family can step in? This lesson names the protection that exists and the services that carry it.

## Sub lesson 1 — What family protection is

Family protection is the set of measures that guard the family — its children, its health, its unity and its means of living.

Protection is the community and the state standing behind the family, so that a family's hard season does not destroy it.

## Sub lesson 2 — The services responsible — and the measures

Named services are responsible for family protection, and they act through real measures — help, supervision and support for families in need.

Knowing which service to turn to is itself protection: a family that knows where help lives is already less alone.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: families are protected, not left alone: family protection means the measures that guard the family, and named services are responsible for carrying them out. Give one real example from your own life in Cameroon, and name the lesson's key words: family protection, services, measures. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in your own language as well as in English — Cameroon's own book teaches citizenship for life at home and in the village, not only for the classroom.
