# Family Protection — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Families are protected, not left alone: family protection means the measures that guard the family, and named services are responsible for carrying them out.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a citizen's own home, school or village in Cameroon.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Cameroon and lived there.
