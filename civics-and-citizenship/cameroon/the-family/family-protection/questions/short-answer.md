# Family Protection — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Families are protected, not left alone: family protection means the measures that guard the family, and named services are responsible for carrying them out.

**2.** What does the part "The services responsible — and the measures" teach?
**Answer:** Named services are responsible for family protection, and they act through real measures — help, supervision and support for families in need.
