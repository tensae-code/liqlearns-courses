# Family Protection — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** What family protection is — a)

**2.** The services responsible — and the measures — b)


Meanings:
a) Family protection is the set of measures that guard the family — its children, its health, its unity and its means of living.
b) Named services are responsible for family protection, and they act through real measures — help, supervision and support for families in need.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
