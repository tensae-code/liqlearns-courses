# Family Protection — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) families are protected, not left alone: family protection means the measures that guard the family, and named services are responsible for carrying them out  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — What family protection is?
a) Family protection is the set of measures that guard the family — its children, its health, its unity and its means of living.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first sub lesson teaches.
