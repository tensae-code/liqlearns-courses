# Hands-on — Family Protection

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Imagine a family in trouble — a child not in school, a parent ill, no food in the house. Who outside the family can step in? This lesson names the protection that exists and the services that carry it.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: families are protected, not left alone: family protection means the measures that guard the family, and named services are responsible for carrying them out. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in the village. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
