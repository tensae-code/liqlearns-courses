# Video script — Family Protection

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Families are protected, not left alone: family protection means the measures that guard the family, and named services are responsible for carrying them out.

What family protection is. Family protection is the set of measures that guard the family — its children, its health, its unity and its means of living. Protection is the community and the state standing behind the family, so that a family's hard season does not destroy it.

The services responsible — and the measures. Named services are responsible for family protection, and they act through real measures — help, supervision and support for families in need. Knowing which service to turn to is itself protection: a family that knows where help lives is already less alone.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
