# The Rights and Duties of a Child in the Family — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A child in the family holds real rights — and carries real duties: the child matters greatly in an african family, and the parent–child relationship works when rights are respected and duties are done.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a citizen's own home, school or village in Cameroon.
