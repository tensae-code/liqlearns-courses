# The Rights and Duties of a Child in the Family — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** A child in the family holds real rights — and carries real duties: the child matters greatly in an african family, and the parent–child relationship works when rights are respected and duties are done.

**2.** What does the part "The child's rights — and what blocks them" teach?
**Answer:** A child has rights inside the family — to care, to schooling, to be heard and protected — and those rights can be blocked when adults neglect or misuse them.
