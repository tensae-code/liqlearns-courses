# Marriage — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) marriage is the recognised union of spouses — it has forms, it is proved by a marriage certificate, it holds only when its conditions are met, and it gives the spouses duties to each other  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — What marriage is and its forms?
a) Marriage is the union of two spouses recognised by law and custom, and it comes in more than one form.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first sub lesson teaches.
