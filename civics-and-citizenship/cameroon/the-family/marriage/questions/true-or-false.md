# Marriage — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Marriage is the recognised union of spouses — it has forms, it is proved by a marriage certificate, it holds only when its conditions are met, and it gives the spouses duties to each other.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a citizen's own home, school or village in Cameroon.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Cameroon and lived there.
