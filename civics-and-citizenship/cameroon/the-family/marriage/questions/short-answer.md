# Marriage — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Marriage is the recognised union of spouses — it has forms, it is proved by a marriage certificate, it holds only when its conditions are met, and it gives the spouses duties to each other.

**2.** What does the part "The certificate and the conditions" teach?
**Answer:** A marriage certificate is the written proof of the marriage, and a marriage is recognised only when its conditions are fulfilled.
