# Hands-on — Marriage

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Think of a married couple you know. Ask yourself: how do people know they are married — what paper proves it, and what promises stand behind it? This lesson answers those questions.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: marriage is the recognised union of spouses — it has forms, it is proved by a marriage certificate, it holds only when its conditions are met, and it gives the spouses duties to each other. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in the village. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
