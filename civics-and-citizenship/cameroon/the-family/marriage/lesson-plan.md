## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **What marriage is and its forms** — Marriage is the union of two spouses recognised by law and custom, and it comes in more than one form.
2. **The certificate and the conditions** — A marriage certificate is the written proof of the marriage, and a marriage is recognised only when its conditions are fulfilled.
3. **The spouses' duties — and failed marriage** — Married spouses owe each other faithfulness, help and respect; when those duties collapse, a marriage can fail.
