# Video script — Divorce

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Divorce is the legal ending of a marriage — it has causes, it carries consequences for the family and above all for the child, and the child's own part is to work for peace in the family.

What divorce means — and its causes. Divorce is the recognised ending of a marriage, and it comes from causes the book names plainly — broken duties, quarrels that never settle, faithlessness and neglect. A marriage rarely breaks in one day; the causes grow where the spouses' duties were left undone.

Consequences for family and child. Divorce scatters the household and falls hardest on the child, who can lose care, schooling and the daily presence of a parent. That is why the book teaches divorce as a grave matter: the adults end a union, but the child pays the largest share.

The child's part in family peace. The child has a part in family peace — respect, honesty and conduct that does not feed quarrels at home. A child cannot hold a marriage together alone, but a child's behaviour can guard the peace of the house day by day.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
