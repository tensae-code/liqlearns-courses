# Hands-on — Divorce

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Think of a family that has split apart, if you know one. Ask: what broke, who was hurt most, and what could have kept the peace? This lesson teaches divorce honestly — its meaning, its causes, and its cost.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: divorce is the legal ending of a marriage — it has causes, it carries consequences for the family and above all for the child, and the child's own part is to work for peace in the family. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in the village. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
