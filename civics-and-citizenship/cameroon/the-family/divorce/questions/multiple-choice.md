# Divorce — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) divorce is the legal ending of a marriage — it has causes, it carries consequences for the family and above all for the child, and the child's own part is to work for peace in the family  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — What divorce means — and its causes?
a) Divorce is the recognised ending of a marriage, and it comes from causes the book names plainly — broken duties, quarrels that never settle, faithlessness and neglect.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first sub lesson teaches.
