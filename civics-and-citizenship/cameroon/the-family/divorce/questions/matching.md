# Divorce — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** What divorce means — and its causes — a)

**2.** Consequences for family and child — b)


Meanings:
a) Divorce is the recognised ending of a marriage, and it comes from causes the book names plainly — broken duties, quarrels that never settle, faithlessness and neglect.
b) Divorce scatters the household and falls hardest on the child, who can lose care, schooling and the daily presence of a parent.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
