# Divorce — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Divorce is the legal ending of a marriage — it has causes, it carries consequences for the family and above all for the child, and the child's own part is to work for peace in the family.

**2.** What does the part "Consequences for family and child" teach?
**Answer:** Divorce scatters the household and falls hardest on the child, who can lose care, schooling and the daily presence of a parent.
