# Divorce — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Divorce is the legal ending of a marriage — it has causes, it carries consequences for the family and above all for the child, and the child's own part is to work for peace in the family.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a citizen's own home, school or village in Cameroon.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Cameroon and lived there.
