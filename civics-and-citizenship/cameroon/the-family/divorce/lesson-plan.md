# Lesson plan — Divorce

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Cameroon · **Title:** The Family · **Lesson:** Divorce
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — The Family (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** divorce is the legal ending of a marriage — it has causes, it carries consequences for the family and above all for the child, and the child's own part is to work for peace in the family.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — The Family — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **What divorce means — and its causes** — Divorce is the recognised ending of a marriage, and it comes from causes the book names plainly — broken duties, quarrels that never settle, faithlessness and neglect.
2. **Consequences for family and child** — Divorce scatters the household and falls hardest on the child, who can lose care, schooling and the daily presence of a parent.
3. **The child's part in family peace** — The child has a part in family peace — respect, honesty and conduct that does not feed quarrels at home.

Gate pool: the prove-it check for each sub lesson is at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson — drawn from this lesson's question files (questions/).
