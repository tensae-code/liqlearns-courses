# The Responsibilities of Parents — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** Responsible parenthood — a)

**2.** The father's and the mother's duties — b)


Meanings:
a) Responsible parenthood means having children you can care for, and then truly caring for them — feeding, clothing, schooling and guiding them.
b) Father and mother each carry duties in the home, and the mother stands as the child's first educator — the first teacher of words, manners and values.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
