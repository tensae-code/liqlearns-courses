# The Responsibilities of Parents — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Parents carry the family's first responsibilities: responsible parenthood, the father's duties and the mother's duties — the mother as the child's first educator — held together by real parent–child communication.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a citizen's own home, school or village in Cameroon.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Cameroon and lived there.
