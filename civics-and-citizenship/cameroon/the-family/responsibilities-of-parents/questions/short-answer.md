# The Responsibilities of Parents — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Parents carry the family's first responsibilities: responsible parenthood, the father's duties and the mother's duties — the mother as the child's first educator — held together by real parent–child communication.

**2.** What does the part "The father's and the mother's duties" teach?
**Answer:** Father and mother each carry duties in the home, and the mother stands as the child's first educator — the first teacher of words, manners and values.
