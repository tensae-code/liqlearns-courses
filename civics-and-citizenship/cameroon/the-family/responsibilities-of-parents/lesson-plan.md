## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Responsible parenthood** — Responsible parenthood means having children you can care for, and then truly caring for them — feeding, clothing, schooling and guiding them.
2. **The father's and the mother's duties** — Father and mother each carry duties in the home, and the mother stands as the child's first educator — the first teacher of words, manners and values.
3. **Parent–child communication** — Parents and children must truly talk and listen to each other — communication is how duties are explained and trust is kept.

Gate pool: the prove-it check for each sub lesson is at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson — drawn from this lesson's question files (questions/).
