# Hands-on — The Responsibilities of Parents

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Watch a parent or guardian for one morning. Count the things they do before anyone thanks them: feeding, preparing, teaching, correcting. This lesson names that work — the responsibilities of parents.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: parents carry the family's first responsibilities: responsible parenthood, the father's duties and the mother's duties — the mother as the child's first educator — held together by real parent–child communication. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in the village. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
