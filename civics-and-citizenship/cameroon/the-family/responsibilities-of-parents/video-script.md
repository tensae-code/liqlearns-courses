# Video script — The Responsibilities of Parents

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Parents carry the family's first responsibilities: responsible parenthood, the father's duties and the mother's duties — the mother as the child's first educator — held together by real parent–child communication.

Responsible parenthood. Responsible parenthood means having children you can care for, and then truly caring for them — feeding, clothing, schooling and guiding them. Parenthood in the book is a duty before it is a joy: the child did not choose to come, so the parents owe the care.

The father's and the mother's duties. Father and mother each carry duties in the home, and the mother stands as the child's first educator — the first teacher of words, manners and values. Long before school, the child learns by watching the parents; what the home teaches first, the school builds on.

Parent–child communication. Parents and children must truly talk and listen to each other — communication is how duties are explained and trust is kept. A home where no one listens raises children who stop speaking; the book teaches talk as a parental duty, not a favour.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
