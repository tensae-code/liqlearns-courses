# The Family — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** A family is the first group a child belongs to — people joined together who owe each other care and support, and whose unity helps the child while their quarrels harm the child.

**2.** What does the part "The types of family" teach?
**Answer:** Families take different forms — a small household of parents and children, or a wide household of many relatives together.
