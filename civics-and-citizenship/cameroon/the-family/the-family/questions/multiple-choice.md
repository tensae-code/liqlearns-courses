# The Family — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) a family is the first group a child belongs to — people joined together who owe each other care and support, and whose unity helps the child while their quarrels harm the child  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — What a family is?
a) A family is a group of people joined by birth, marriage or care, living as one household and bound to each other.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first sub lesson teaches.
