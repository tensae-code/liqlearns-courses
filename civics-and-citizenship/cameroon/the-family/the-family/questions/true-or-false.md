# The Family — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A family is the first group a child belongs to — people joined together who owe each other care and support, and whose unity helps the child while their quarrels harm the child.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a citizen's own home, school or village in Cameroon.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Cameroon and lived there.
