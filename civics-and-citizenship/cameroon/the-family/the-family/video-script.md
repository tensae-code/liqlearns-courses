# Video script — The Family

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A family is the first group a child belongs to — people joined together who owe each other care and support, and whose unity helps the child while their quarrels harm the child.

What a family is. A family is a group of people joined by birth, marriage or care, living as one household and bound to each other. It is the first society a child ever meets: before school, before the village, the child learns belonging inside the family.

The types of family. Families take different forms — a small household of parents and children, or a wide household of many relatives together. The form can differ from house to house; what makes it a family is the bond and the duties, not the size.

What unites or harms it — and our duties. Love, respect and shared work unite a family; neglect and quarrels harm it — and the child and the family owe each other duties both ways. The child owes respect and help; the family owes the child care and protection. Each side keeps the other strong.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
