# Lesson plan — The Family

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Cameroon · **Title:** The Family · **Lesson:** The Family
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — The Family (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a family is the first group a child belongs to — people joined together who owe each other care and support, and whose unity helps the child while their quarrels harm the child.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — The Family — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **What a family is** — A family is a group of people joined by birth, marriage or care, living as one household and bound to each other.
2. **The types of family** — Families take different forms — a small household of parents and children, or a wide household of many relatives together.
3. **What unites or harms it — and our duties** — Love, respect and shared work unite a family; neglect and quarrels harm it — and the child and the family owe each other duties both ways.
