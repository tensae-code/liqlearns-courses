# From the Home to the School — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** The road to school must be used responsibly: the pupil knows the road's parts and signs, crosses and walks with care, and knows that road accidents come from causes that care can prevent.

**2.** What does the part "Responsible road use" teach?
**Answer:** Responsible road use means walking facing care, crossing where and how one should, and never playing on the carriageway.
