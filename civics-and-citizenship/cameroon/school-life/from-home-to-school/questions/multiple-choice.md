# From the Home to the School — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) the road to school must be used responsibly: the pupil knows the road's parts and signs, crosses and walks with care, and knows that road accidents come from causes that care can prevent  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — The parts of the road and its signs?
a) A road has parts — carriageway, pavement or verge, crossings — and signs that speak to everyone who uses it.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first sub lesson teaches.
