# From the Home to the School — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The road to school must be used responsibly: the pupil knows the road's parts and signs, crosses and walks with care, and knows that road accidents come from causes that care can prevent.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a citizen's own home, school or village in Cameroon.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Cameroon and lived there.
