# Video script — From the Home to the School

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The road to school must be used responsibly: the pupil knows the road's parts and signs, crosses and walks with care, and knows that road accidents come from causes that care can prevent.

The parts of the road and its signs. A road has parts — carriageway, pavement or verge, crossings — and signs that speak to everyone who uses it. A pupil who can read the road's parts and signs can be read by the road's other users: drivers see a child who behaves predictably.

Responsible road use. Responsible road use means walking facing care, crossing where and how one should, and never playing on the carriageway. Most road rules for children are one rule in many shapes: be seen, be predictable, stay out of the vehicles' path.

Causes and damage of road accidents. Road accidents come from named causes — speed, carelessness, disobeyed signs — and their damage falls on bodies, families and goods. Teaching the causes is the prevention: an accident with a known cause is an accident a careful pupil can help avoid.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
