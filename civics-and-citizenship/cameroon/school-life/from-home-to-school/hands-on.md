# Hands-on — From the Home to the School

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Walk your road to school in your mind. Name its parts, its crossings, its dangers. This lesson teaches that road — its signs, its responsible use, and why accidents happen.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the road to school must be used responsibly: the pupil knows the road's parts and signs, crosses and walks with care, and knows that road accidents come from causes that care can prevent. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in the village. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
