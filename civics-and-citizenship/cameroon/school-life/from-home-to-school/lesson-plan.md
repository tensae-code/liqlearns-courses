# Lesson plan — From the Home to the School

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Cameroon · **Title:** School Life · **Lesson:** From the Home to the School
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — Belonging, Rights and School Life (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the road to school must be used responsibly: the pupil knows the road's parts and signs, crosses and walks with care, and knows that road accidents come from causes that care can prevent.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The parts of the road and its signs** — A road has parts — carriageway, pavement or verge, crossings — and signs that speak to everyone who uses it.
2. **Responsible road use** — Responsible road use means walking facing care, crossing where and how one should, and never playing on the carriageway.
3. **Causes and damage of road accidents** — Road accidents come from named causes — speed, carelessness, disobeyed signs — and their damage falls on bodies, families and goods.

Gate pool: the prove-it check for each sub lesson is at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson — drawn from this lesson's question files (questions/).
