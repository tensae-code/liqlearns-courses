# Video script — The School Institution

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A school is an institution, not just a building: it has types, it is run by known staff, the student holds documents that prove his place in it, and its environment must be protected.

What a school institution is — and its types. A school institution is an organised body for teaching, with a structure and a purpose — and schools come in types, by level and by kind. Calling the school an institution teaches the pupil that it runs on roles and rules, not on whoever shouts loudest.

Staff and the student's documents. Known staff run the school — head, teachers and others, each with a role — and the student holds documents, such as his school identity, that prove his place. Documents protect the pupil: his record and identity card say who he is and what he has done at school.

Protecting the school environment. The school's environment — its buildings, grounds and materials — belongs to all its pupils, present and future, and must be protected. A pupil who damages his school damages his own place of learning and the place of those who come after him.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
