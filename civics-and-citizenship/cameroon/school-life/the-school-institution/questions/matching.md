# The School Institution — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** What a school institution is — and its types — a)

**2.** Staff and the student's documents — b)


Meanings:
a) A school institution is an organised body for teaching, with a structure and a purpose — and schools come in types, by level and by kind.
b) Known staff run the school — head, teachers and others, each with a role — and the student holds documents, such as his school identity, that prove his place.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
