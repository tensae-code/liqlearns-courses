# The School Institution — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** A school is an institution, not just a building: it has types, it is run by known staff, the student holds documents that prove his place in it, and its environment must be protected.

**2.** What does the part "Staff and the student's documents" teach?
**Answer:** Known staff run the school — head, teachers and others, each with a role — and the student holds documents, such as his school identity, that prove his place.
