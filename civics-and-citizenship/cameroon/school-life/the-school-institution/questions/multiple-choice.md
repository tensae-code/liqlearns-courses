# The School Institution — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) a school is an institution, not just a building: it has types, it is run by known staff, the student holds documents that prove his place in it, and its environment must be protected  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — What a school institution is — and its types?
a) A school institution is an organised body for teaching, with a structure and a purpose — and schools come in types, by level and by kind.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first sub lesson teaches.
