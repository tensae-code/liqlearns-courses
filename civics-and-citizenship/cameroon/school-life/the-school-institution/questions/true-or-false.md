# The School Institution — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A school is an institution, not just a building: it has types, it is run by known staff, the student holds documents that prove his place in it, and its environment must be protected.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a citizen's own home, school or village in Cameroon.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Cameroon and lived there.
