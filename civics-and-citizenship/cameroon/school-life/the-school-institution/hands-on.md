# Hands-on — The School Institution

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name the adults at your school and what each one does. Then name the paper that proves you are a pupil there. This lesson puts those pieces together: the school as an institution.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: a school is an institution, not just a building: it has types, it is run by known staff, the student holds documents that prove his place in it, and its environment must be protected. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in the village. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
