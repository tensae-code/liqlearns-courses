# Lesson plan — The School Institution

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Cameroon · **Title:** School Life · **Lesson:** The School Institution
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — Belonging, Rights and School Life (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a school is an institution, not just a building: it has types, it is run by known staff, the student holds documents that prove his place in it, and its environment must be protected.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — Belonging, Rights and School Life — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **What a school institution is — and its types** — A school institution is an organised body for teaching, with a structure and a purpose — and schools come in types, by level and by kind.
2. **Staff and the student's documents** — Known staff run the school — head, teachers and others, each with a role — and the student holds documents, such as his school identity, that prove his place.
3. **Protecting the school environment** — The school's environment — its buildings, grounds and materials — belongs to all its pupils, present and future, and must be protected.

Gate pool: the prove-it check for each sub lesson is at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson — drawn from this lesson's question files (questions/).

3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
