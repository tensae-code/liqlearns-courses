# Lesson plan — How to Start a School Day

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Cameroon · **Title:** School Life · **Lesson:** How to Start a School Day
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — Belonging, Rights and School Life (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a school day starts the evening before: the pupil prepares at night and acts in order in the morning, so that the day's learning begins on time and unhurried.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — Belonging, Rights and School Life — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The eve of a school day** — On the evening before, the pupil prepares: lessons reviewed, books and materials packed, clothes made ready.
2. **The morning of a school day** — In the morning the pupil rises in time, washes, eats, dresses and leaves early enough to arrive without hurry.

Gate pool: the prove-it check for each sub lesson is at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson — drawn from this lesson's question files (questions/).

3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
