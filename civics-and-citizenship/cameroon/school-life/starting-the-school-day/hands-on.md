# Hands-on — How to Start a School Day

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Think of your last rushed morning — the forgotten book, the late arrival. Now think of a calm one. This lesson teaches the difference: a school day is started the eve before, not at the gate.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: a school day starts the evening before: the pupil prepares at night and acts in order in the morning, so that the day's learning begins on time and unhurried. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in the village. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
