# Video script — How to Start a School Day

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A school day starts the evening before: the pupil prepares at night and acts in order in the morning, so that the day's learning begins on time and unhurried.

The eve of a school day. On the evening before, the pupil prepares: lessons reviewed, books and materials packed, clothes made ready. Half the morning's peace is bought the night before; preparation is the pupil's first responsibility.

The morning of a school day. In the morning the pupil rises in time, washes, eats, dresses and leaves early enough to arrive without hurry. Each morning act has its order and its hour; a pupil who keeps them starts learning before the first lesson begins.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
