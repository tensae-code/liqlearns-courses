# How to Start a School Day — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A school day starts the evening before: the pupil prepares at night and acts in order in the morning, so that the day's learning begins on time and unhurried.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a citizen's own home, school or village in Cameroon.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Cameroon and lived there.
