# How to Start a School Day — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) a school day starts the evening before: the pupil prepares at night and acts in order in the morning, so that the day's learning begins on time and unhurried  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — The eve of a school day?
a) On the evening before, the pupil prepares: lessons reviewed, books and materials packed, clothes made ready.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first sub lesson teaches.
