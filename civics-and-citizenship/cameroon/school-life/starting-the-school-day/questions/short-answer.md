# How to Start a School Day — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** A school day starts the evening before: the pupil prepares at night and acts in order in the morning, so that the day's learning begins on time and unhurried.

**2.** What does the part "The morning of a school day" teach?
**Answer:** In the morning the pupil rises in time, washes, eats, dresses and leaves early enough to arrive without hurry.
