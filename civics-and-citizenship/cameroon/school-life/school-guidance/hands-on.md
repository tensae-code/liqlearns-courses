# Hands-on — School Guidance and Vocational Counselling

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Imagine standing where two paths divide — one toward one kind of study and work, one toward another. Who helps you choose, and by what signs? This lesson answers: school guidance and vocational counselling.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: a secondary pupil does not drift: guidance and vocational counselling show him the series and branches open to him, the certificates each path leads to, and help him choose with his future in view. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in the village. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
