# School Guidance and Vocational Counselling — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A secondary pupil does not drift: guidance and vocational counselling show him the series and branches open to him, the certificates each path leads to, and help him choose with his future in view.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a citizen's own home, school or village in Cameroon.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Cameroon and lived there.
