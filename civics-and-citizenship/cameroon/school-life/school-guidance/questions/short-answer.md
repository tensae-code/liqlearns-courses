# School Guidance and Vocational Counselling — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** A secondary pupil does not drift: guidance and vocational counselling show him the series and branches open to him, the certificates each path leads to, and help him choose with his future in view.

**2.** What does the part "End-of-cycle certificates — and why guidance matters" teach?
**Answer:** Each path leads toward end-of-cycle certificates, the written proof that opens the next door — and guidance matters because a well-chosen path fits the pupil's strengths to his future work.
