# School Guidance and Vocational Counselling

## Do it first

Imagine standing where two paths divide — one toward one kind of study and work, one toward another. Who helps you choose, and by what signs? This lesson answers: school guidance and vocational counselling.

## Sub lesson 1 — The series and branches open to a pupil

Secondary school opens into series and branches — different paths of study a pupil may enter, each with its own subjects and demands.

A pupil cannot choose a path he has never been shown; guidance begins by laying the whole map out.

## Sub lesson 2 — End-of-cycle certificates — and why guidance matters

Each path leads toward end-of-cycle certificates, the written proof that opens the next door — and guidance matters because a well-chosen path fits the pupil's strengths to his future work.

The counsellor's work is fitting: the right pupil, on the right branch, toward the right certificate and calling.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: a secondary pupil does not drift: guidance and vocational counselling show him the series and branches open to him, the certificates each path leads to, and help him choose with his future in view. Give one real example from your own life in Cameroon, and name the lesson's key words: guidance, vocational counselling, branches, certificates. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in your own language as well as in English — Cameroon's own book teaches citizenship for life at home and in the village, not only for the classroom.
