# Lesson plan — School Guidance and Vocational Counselling

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Cameroon · **Title:** School Life · **Lesson:** School Guidance and Vocational Counselling
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — Belonging, Rights and School Life (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a secondary pupil does not drift: guidance and vocational counselling show him the series and branches open to him, the certificates each path leads to, and help him choose with his future in view.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — Belonging, Rights and School Life — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The series and branches open to a pupil** — Secondary school opens into series and branches — different paths of study a pupil may enter, each with its own subjects and demands.
2. **End-of-cycle certificates — and why guidance matters** — Each path leads toward end-of-cycle certificates, the written proof that opens the next door — and guidance matters because a well-chosen path fits the pupil's strengths to his future work.

Gate pool: the prove-it check for each sub lesson is at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson — drawn from this lesson's question files (questions/).
