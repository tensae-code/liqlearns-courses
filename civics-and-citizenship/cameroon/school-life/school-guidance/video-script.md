# Video script — School Guidance and Vocational Counselling

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A secondary pupil does not drift: guidance and vocational counselling show him the series and branches open to him, the certificates each path leads to, and help him choose with his future in view.

The series and branches open to a pupil. Secondary school opens into series and branches — different paths of study a pupil may enter, each with its own subjects and demands. A pupil cannot choose a path he has never been shown; guidance begins by laying the whole map out.

End-of-cycle certificates — and why guidance matters. Each path leads toward end-of-cycle certificates, the written proof that opens the next door — and guidance matters because a well-chosen path fits the pupil's strengths to his future work. The counsellor's work is fitting: the right pupil, on the right branch, toward the right certificate and calling.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
