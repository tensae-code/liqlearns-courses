# The School Environment — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** The school environment is everything around the learning — its grounds, air, water and buildings — and it is kept by fighting pollution and protecting the school's heritage.

**2.** What does the part "Pollution in the school" teach?
**Answer:** Pollution — litter, dirty water, smoke and noise — damages the school environment and the health of everyone in it.
