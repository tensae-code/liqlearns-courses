# The School Environment — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The school environment is everything around the learning — its grounds, air, water and buildings — and it is kept by fighting pollution and protecting the school's heritage.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a citizen's own home, school or village in Cameroon.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Cameroon and lived there.
