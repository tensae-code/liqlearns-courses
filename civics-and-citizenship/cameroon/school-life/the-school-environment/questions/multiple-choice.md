# The School Environment — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) the school environment is everything around the learning — its grounds, air, water and buildings — and it is kept by fighting pollution and protecting the school's heritage  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — The elements of the school environment?
a) The school environment is made of elements — the grounds, the plants, the buildings, the water and air of the place.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first sub lesson teaches.
