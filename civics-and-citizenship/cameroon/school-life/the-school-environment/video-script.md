# Video script — The School Environment

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The school environment is everything around the learning — its grounds, air, water and buildings — and it is kept by fighting pollution and protecting the school's heritage.

The elements of the school environment. The school environment is made of elements — the grounds, the plants, the buildings, the water and air of the place. Naming the elements is the first act of care: you cannot protect what you have never noticed.

Pollution in the school. Pollution — litter, dirty water, smoke and noise — damages the school environment and the health of everyone in it. Most school pollution is made by the school's own users, which means its own users can end it.

Protecting school heritage. The school's heritage — its buildings, trees, equipment and good name built over years — must be protected and handed on intact. Heritage is what earlier pupils left for you; protecting it is how you leave the same gift behind you.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
