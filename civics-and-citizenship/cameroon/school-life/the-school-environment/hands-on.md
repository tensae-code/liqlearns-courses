# Hands-on — The School Environment

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Step outside your classroom in your mind and list everything you see: trees, yard, water point, walls, litter. All of it teaches. This lesson is about that environment — its elements, its pollution, and its protection.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the school environment is everything around the learning — its grounds, air, water and buildings — and it is kept by fighting pollution and protecting the school's heritage. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in the village. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
