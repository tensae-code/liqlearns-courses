# The School Environment

## Do it first

Step outside your classroom in your mind and list everything you see: trees, yard, water point, walls, litter. All of it teaches. This lesson is about that environment — its elements, its pollution, and its protection.

## Sub lesson 1 — The elements of the school environment

The school environment is made of elements — the grounds, the plants, the buildings, the water and air of the place.

Naming the elements is the first act of care: you cannot protect what you have never noticed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Pollution in the school

Pollution — litter, dirty water, smoke and noise — damages the school environment and the health of everyone in it.

Most school pollution is made by the school's own users, which means its own users can end it.

## Sub lesson 3 — Protecting school heritage

The school's heritage — its buildings, trees, equipment and good name built over years — must be protected and handed on intact.

Heritage is what earlier pupils left for you; protecting it is how you leave the same gift behind you.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the school environment is everything around the learning — its grounds, air, water and buildings — and it is kept by fighting pollution and protecting the school's heritage. Give one real example from your own life in Cameroon, and name the lesson's key words: school environment, pollution, heritage. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in your own language as well as in English — Cameroon's own book teaches citizenship for life at home and in the village, not only for the classroom.

2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: the school environment is everything around the learning — its grounds, air, water and buildings — and it is kept by fighting pollution and protecting the school's heritage.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
