# Video script — How a School Institution Works

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A school works through people doing defined duties: its staff each carry a role, its councils decide together, its support services help the pupils, and its internal rules hold the whole together.

Staff duties. Each member of staff — head, teachers, and the rest — carries defined duties, and the school works when each duty is done. Defined duties mean a pupil always knows who to ask, and a failure can always be traced and repaired.

School councils and support services. The school's councils bring its members together to decide, and its support services — health, guidance and others — help pupils beyond the classroom. A school is not run by orders alone; its councils deliberate and its services catch the pupils the classroom cannot reach.

Internal rules. The school's internal rules state what is expected of everyone inside it, and they bind staff and pupils alike. Rules known by all and applied to all are what make the institution fair instead of arbitrary.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
