# How a School Institution Works — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** A school works through people doing defined duties: its staff each carry a role, its councils decide together, its support services help the pupils, and its internal rules hold the whole together.

**2.** What does the part "School councils and support services" teach?
**Answer:** The school's councils bring its members together to decide, and its support services — health, guidance and others — help pupils beyond the classroom.
