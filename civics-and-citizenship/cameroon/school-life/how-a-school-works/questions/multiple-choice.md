# How a School Institution Works — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) a school works through people doing defined duties: its staff each carry a role, its councils decide together, its support services help the pupils, and its internal rules hold the whole together  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — Staff duties?
a) Each member of staff — head, teachers, and the rest — carries defined duties, and the school works when each duty is done.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first sub lesson teaches.
