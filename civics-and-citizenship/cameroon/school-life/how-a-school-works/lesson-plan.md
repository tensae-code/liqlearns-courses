# Lesson plan — How a School Institution Works

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Cameroon · **Title:** School Life · **Lesson:** How a School Institution Works
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — Belonging, Rights and School Life (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a school works through people doing defined duties: its staff each carry a role, its councils decide together, its support services help the pupils, and its internal rules hold the whole together.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — Belonging, Rights and School Life — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Staff duties** — Each member of staff — head, teachers, and the rest — carries defined duties, and the school works when each duty is done.
2. **School councils and support services** — The school's councils bring its members together to decide, and its support services — health, guidance and others — help pupils beyond the classroom.
3. **Internal rules** — The school's internal rules state what is expected of everyone inside it, and they bind staff and pupils alike.

Gate pool: the prove-it check for each sub lesson is at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson — drawn from this lesson's question files (questions/).
