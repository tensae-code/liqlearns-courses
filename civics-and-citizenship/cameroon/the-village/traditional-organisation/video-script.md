# Video script — The Traditional Organisation of the Village

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The village is governed traditionally by a hierarchy of institutions — chiefs and councils with defined competence — and traditional authorities hold real powers that the book names.

The village's hierarchical institutions. The village is organised in a hierarchy of traditional institutions — from the family head up through quarter and village authorities. Hierarchy here means order, not vanity: each level settles what the level below cannot.

Their competence and the powers of traditional authorities. Each institution has its competence — the matters it may judge and decide — and traditional authorities hold named powers within it. A power with a known limit is authority; a power without one is mere force. The book teaches the limits with the powers.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
