# The Traditional Organisation of the Village — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The village is governed traditionally by a hierarchy of institutions — chiefs and councils with defined competence — and traditional authorities hold real powers that the book names.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a citizen's own home, school or village in Cameroon.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Cameroon and lived there.
