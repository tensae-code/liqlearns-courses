# The Traditional Organisation of the Village — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** The village is governed traditionally by a hierarchy of institutions — chiefs and councils with defined competence — and traditional authorities hold real powers that the book names.

**2.** What does the part "Their competence and the powers of traditional authorities" teach?
**Answer:** Each institution has its competence — the matters it may judge and decide — and traditional authorities hold named powers within it.
