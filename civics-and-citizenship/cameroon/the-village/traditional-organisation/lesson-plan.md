# Lesson plan — The Traditional Organisation of the Village

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Cameroon · **Title:** The Village · **Lesson:** The Traditional Organisation of the Village
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — The Village (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the village is governed traditionally by a hierarchy of institutions — chiefs and councils with defined competence — and traditional authorities hold real powers that the book names.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — The Village — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The village's hierarchical institutions** — The village is organised in a hierarchy of traditional institutions — from the family head up through quarter and village authorities.
2. **Their competence and the powers of traditional authorities** — Each institution has its competence — the matters it may judge and decide — and traditional authorities hold named powers within it.

Gate pool: the prove-it check for each sub lesson is at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson — drawn from this lesson's question files (questions/).
