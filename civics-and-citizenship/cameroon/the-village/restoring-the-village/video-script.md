# Video script — The Restoration of the Village

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The village is worth restoring: developing its traditional culture and keeping its place in the country's life, so that what the village holds is not lost as the country changes.

Developing traditional culture. Restoring the village means developing its traditional culture — its crafts, festivals, skills and stories — as living wealth, not museum pieces. A culture that is practised and improved keeps its people rooted while the country moves.

The village's importance. The village matters to the whole country — as producer, as keeper of culture, and as the home most citizens' belonging starts from. Book 2's village section closes here: the village restored is the foundation the council and the region are built on.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
