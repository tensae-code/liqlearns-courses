# Hands-on — The Restoration of the Village

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Imagine a village whose young people have all left and whose crafts no one practises. What exactly has been lost? This lesson teaches the restoration of the village — why it matters and what it restores.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the village is worth restoring: developing its traditional culture and keeping its place in the country's life, so that what the village holds is not lost as the country changes. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in the village. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
