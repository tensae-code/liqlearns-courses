# The Restoration of the Village — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** The village is worth restoring: developing its traditional culture and keeping its place in the country's life, so that what the village holds is not lost as the country changes.

**2.** What does the part "The village's importance" teach?
**Answer:** The village matters to the whole country — as producer, as keeper of culture, and as the home most citizens' belonging starts from.
