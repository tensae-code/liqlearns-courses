# The Restoration of the Village — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) the village is worth restoring: developing its traditional culture and keeping its place in the country's life, so that what the village holds is not lost as the country changes  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — Developing traditional culture?
a) Restoring the village means developing its traditional culture — its crafts, festivals, skills and stories — as living wealth, not museum pieces.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first sub lesson teaches.
