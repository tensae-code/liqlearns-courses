# The Restoration of the Village — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The village is worth restoring: developing its traditional culture and keeping its place in the country's life, so that what the village holds is not lost as the country changes.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a citizen's own home, school or village in Cameroon.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Cameroon and lived there.
