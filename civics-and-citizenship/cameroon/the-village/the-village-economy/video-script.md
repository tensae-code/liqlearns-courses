# Video script — The Village in the Economic Life of the Country

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The village feeds the country: its production techniques and economic activities supply food and goods far beyond its borders — while it faces real problems, and some of its activities harm the environment it depends on.

Production techniques and economic activities. Village production — farming, craft and trade — uses techniques handed down and improved, and its activities reach well beyond the village itself. The country's towns eat because villages produce; the book gives the village its true economic weight.

The village's problems — and activities harmful to the environment. The village faces problems — poor roads, low prices, scarce tools — and some activities, like destructive clearing, harm the environment the village lives from. Naming both together is the lesson's honesty: the village is the country's strength and still needs the country's help, and its own care.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
