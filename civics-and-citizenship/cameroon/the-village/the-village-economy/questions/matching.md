# The Village in the Economic Life of the Country — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** Production techniques and economic activities — a)

**2.** The village's problems — and activities harmful to the environment — b)


Meanings:
a) Village production — farming, craft and trade — uses techniques handed down and improved, and its activities reach well beyond the village itself.
b) The village faces problems — poor roads, low prices, scarce tools — and some activities, like destructive clearing, harm the environment the village lives from.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
