# The Village in the Economic Life of the Country — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) the village feeds the country: its production techniques and economic activities supply food and goods far beyond its borders — while it faces real problems, and some of its activities harm the environment it depends on  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — Production techniques and economic activities?
a) Village production — farming, craft and trade — uses techniques handed down and improved, and its activities reach well beyond the village itself.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first sub lesson teaches.
