# The Village in the Economic Life of the Country — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The village feeds the country: its production techniques and economic activities supply food and goods far beyond its borders — while it faces real problems, and some of its activities harm the environment it depends on.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a citizen's own home, school or village in Cameroon.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Cameroon and lived there.
