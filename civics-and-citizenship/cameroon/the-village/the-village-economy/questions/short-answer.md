# The Village in the Economic Life of the Country — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** The village feeds the country: its production techniques and economic activities supply food and goods far beyond its borders — while it faces real problems, and some of its activities harm the environment it depends on.

**2.** What does the part "The village's problems — and activities harmful to the environment" teach?
**Answer:** The village faces problems — poor roads, low prices, scarce tools — and some activities, like destructive clearing, harm the environment the village lives from.
