# The Village in the Economic Life of the Country

## Do it first

Follow a bag of food from a village farm to a town market. Every hand it passes through is this lesson: the village in the economic life of the country.

## Sub lesson 1 — Production techniques and economic activities

Village production — farming, craft and trade — uses techniques handed down and improved, and its activities reach well beyond the village itself.

The country's towns eat because villages produce; the book gives the village its true economic weight.

## Sub lesson 2 — The village's problems — and activities harmful to the environment

The village faces problems — poor roads, low prices, scarce tools — and some activities, like destructive clearing, harm the environment the village lives from.

Naming both together is the lesson's honesty: the village is the country's strength and still needs the country's help, and its own care.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the village feeds the country: its production techniques and economic activities supply food and goods far beyond its borders — while it faces real problems, and some of its activities harm the environment it depends on. Give one real example from your own life in Cameroon, and name the lesson's key words: village economy, production, environment. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in your own language as well as in English — Cameroon's own book teaches citizenship for life at home and in the village, not only for the classroom.
