# Hands-on — The Village in the Economic Life of the Country

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Follow a bag of food from a village farm to a town market. Every hand it passes through is this lesson: the village in the economic life of the country.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the village feeds the country: its production techniques and economic activities supply food and goods far beyond its borders — while it faces real problems, and some of its activities harm the environment it depends on. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in the village. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
