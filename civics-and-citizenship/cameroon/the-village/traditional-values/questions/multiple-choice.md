# Traditional Social Values — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) a community lives by traditional social values — the customs of respect, solidarity and honesty handed down — which carry advantages and disadvantages, and whose positive side can be deliberately strengthened  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — What traditional values are?
a) Traditional social values are the standards of conduct a community hands down — respect for elders, solidarity, hospitality, honesty and the rest.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first sub lesson teaches.
