# Traditional Social Values — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A community lives by traditional social values — the customs of respect, solidarity and honesty handed down — which carry advantages and disadvantages, and whose positive side can be deliberately strengthened.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a citizen's own home, school or village in Cameroon.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Cameroon and lived there.
