# Traditional Social Values — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** What traditional values are — a)

**2.** Their advantages and disadvantages — b)


Meanings:
a) Traditional social values are the standards of conduct a community hands down — respect for elders, solidarity, hospitality, honesty and the rest.
b) Traditional values bind a community and guide the young — but a value can also carry a disadvantage when it is kept past its usefulness or applied unjustly.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
