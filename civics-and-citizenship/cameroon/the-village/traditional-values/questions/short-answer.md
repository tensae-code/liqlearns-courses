# Traditional Social Values — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** A community lives by traditional social values — the customs of respect, solidarity and honesty handed down — which carry advantages and disadvantages, and whose positive side can be deliberately strengthened.

**2.** What does the part "Their advantages and disadvantages" teach?
**Answer:** Traditional values bind a community and guide the young — but a value can also carry a disadvantage when it is kept past its usefulness or applied unjustly.
