## Sub lesson 1 — What traditional values are
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Traditional social values are the standards of conduct a community hands down — respect for elders, solidarity, hospitality, honesty and the rest. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — Their advantages and disadvantages
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Traditional values bind a community and guide the young — but a value can also carry a disadvantage when it is kept past its usefulness or applied unjustly. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 3 — Enhancing the positive ones
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The positive values can be deliberately enhanced — practised, taught and honoured — so the community keeps their strength and sheds their harm. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
