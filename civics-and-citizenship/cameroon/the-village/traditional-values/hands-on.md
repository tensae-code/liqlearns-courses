# Hands-on — Traditional Social Values

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name one custom your elders praise — how guests are received, how elders are greeted, how neighbours help at harvest. This lesson weighs such values: what they give, where they can harm, and how to keep the good.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: a community lives by traditional social values — the customs of respect, solidarity and honesty handed down — which carry advantages and disadvantages, and whose positive side can be deliberately strengthened. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in the village. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
