# Video script — Traditional Social Values

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A community lives by traditional social values — the customs of respect, solidarity and honesty handed down — which carry advantages and disadvantages, and whose positive side can be deliberately strengthened.

What traditional values are. Traditional social values are the standards of conduct a community hands down — respect for elders, solidarity, hospitality, honesty and the rest. They are the village's unwritten curriculum: taught at home and in gatherings long before any school lesson.

Their advantages and disadvantages. Traditional values bind a community and guide the young — but a value can also carry a disadvantage when it is kept past its usefulness or applied unjustly. The book does not worship or discard the values; it weighs them, which is the pupil's own work too.

Enhancing the positive ones. The positive values can be deliberately enhanced — practised, taught and honoured — so the community keeps their strength and sheds their harm. A value that is practised on purpose survives; a value left to habit alone fades or twists.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
