## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **What a village is** — A village is a small settled community — homes, families and shared life in one place, under its own order.
2. **Village types and building materials** — Villages take types — by their layout, their people's work and their place — and they are built from the materials their land provides.
3. **Telling Cameroonian villages apart** — Cameroon's village types can be told apart by those signs — layout, work, materials and setting.

Gate pool: the prove-it check for each sub lesson is at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson — drawn from this lesson's question files (questions/).
