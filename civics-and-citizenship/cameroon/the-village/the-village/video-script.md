# Video script — The Village

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A village is a settled community smaller than a town, with its own life and order — villages come in types, are built from the materials of their place, and cameroon's village types can be told apart.

What a village is. A village is a small settled community — homes, families and shared life in one place, under its own order. The village is the first community beyond the family in Cameroon's book: Book 2 opens where Book 1's family widens.

Village types and building materials. Villages take types — by their layout, their people's work and their place — and they are built from the materials their land provides. Roof, wall and fence tell you where you are: materials are the land's own signature on the village.

Telling Cameroonian villages apart. Cameroon's village types can be told apart by those signs — layout, work, materials and setting. A pupil who can read those signs can arrive in an unknown village and already understand half of what he sees.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
