# Hands-on — The Village

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Picture the village you know best, or one you have visited. Name its houses, its centre, its paths. This lesson teaches what a village is — its types, its building materials, and how Cameroonian villages differ.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: a village is a settled community smaller than a town, with its own life and order — villages come in types, are built from the materials of their place, and Cameroon's village types can be told apart. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in the village. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
