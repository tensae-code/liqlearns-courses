# The Village — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) a village is a settled community smaller than a town, with its own life and order — villages come in types, are built from the materials of their place, and Cameroon's village types can be told apart  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — What a village is?
a) A village is a small settled community — homes, families and shared life in one place, under its own order.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first sub lesson teaches.
