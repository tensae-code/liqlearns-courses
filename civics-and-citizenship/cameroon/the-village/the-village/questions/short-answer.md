# The Village — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** A village is a settled community smaller than a town, with its own life and order — villages come in types, are built from the materials of their place, and cameroon's village types can be told apart.

**2.** What does the part "Village types and building materials" teach?
**Answer:** Villages take types — by their layout, their people's work and their place — and they are built from the materials their land provides.
