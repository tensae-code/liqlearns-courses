# The Village — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A village is a settled community smaller than a town, with its own life and order — villages come in types, are built from the materials of their place, and cameroon's village types can be told apart.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a citizen's own home, school or village in Cameroon.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Cameroon and lived there.
