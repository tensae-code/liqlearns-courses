# Video script — The Extension of the Family

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The family widens into clan, tribe and ethnic group — cameroon's people belong to many groups, each with its own heritage — and tribalism, putting your group against the nation, is the danger that widening must refuse.

Clan, tribe and ethnic group. Beyond the household stand the clan (families joined by a common ancestor), the tribe, and the ethnic group — each wider circle of belonging. A Cameroonian belongs to all these circles at once; none of them cancels the family, each one extends it.

Cameroon's major ethnic groups. Cameroon holds many major ethnic groups, each with its own language, customs and homeland inside one country. The book teaches the pupil to know the country's groups as neighbours in one nation, not as strangers.

The dangers of tribalism. Tribalism — favouring your own group against others and against the nation — divides the country and breeds injustice and conflict. Belonging is a gift; tribalism is belonging turned into a weapon. The lesson keeps the first and refuses the second.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
