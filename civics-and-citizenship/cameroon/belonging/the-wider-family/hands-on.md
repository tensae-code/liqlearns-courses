# Hands-on — The Extension of the Family

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Start with your family, then name the wider group your family belongs to, then one group different from yours that you know. This lesson walks that widening — clan, tribe, ethnic group — and names its danger.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the family widens into clan, tribe and ethnic group — Cameroon's people belong to many groups, each with its own heritage — and tribalism, putting your group against the nation, is the danger that widening must refuse. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in the village. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
