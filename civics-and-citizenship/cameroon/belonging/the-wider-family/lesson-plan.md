# Lesson plan — The Extension of the Family

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Cameroon · **Title:** Belonging · **Lesson:** The Extension of the Family
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — Belonging, Rights and School Life (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the family widens into clan, tribe and ethnic group — Cameroon's people belong to many groups, each with its own heritage — and tribalism, putting your group against the nation, is the danger that widening must refuse.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — Belonging, Rights and School Life — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Clan, tribe and ethnic group** — Beyond the household stand the clan (families joined by a common ancestor), the tribe, and the ethnic group — each wider circle of belonging.
2. **Cameroon's major ethnic groups** — Cameroon holds many major ethnic groups, each with its own language, customs and homeland inside one country.
3. **The dangers of tribalism** — Tribalism — favouring your own group against others and against the nation — divides the country and breeds injustice and conflict.

Gate pool: the prove-it check for each sub lesson is at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson — drawn from this lesson's question files (questions/).
