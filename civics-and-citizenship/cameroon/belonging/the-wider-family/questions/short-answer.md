# The Extension of the Family — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** The family widens into clan, tribe and ethnic group — cameroon's people belong to many groups, each with its own heritage — and tribalism, putting your group against the nation, is the danger that widening must refuse.

**2.** What does the part "Cameroon's major ethnic groups" teach?
**Answer:** Cameroon holds many major ethnic groups, each with its own language, customs and homeland inside one country.
