# The Extension of the Family — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The family widens into clan, tribe and ethnic group — cameroon's people belong to many groups, each with its own heritage — and tribalism, putting your group against the nation, is the danger that widening must refuse.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a citizen's own home, school or village in Cameroon.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Cameroon and lived there.
