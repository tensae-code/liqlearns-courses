# Civics and Citizenship — Cameroon course — CURRICULUM (the path)

One course for Cameroon inside the Civics and Citizenship folder,
organised by **levels, not grades**: a learner climbs when they
have proved the level, not when a school calendar says so. The
school grades the book comes from (secondary Books 1–2) appear
only in each lesson's sources (provenance), never as the course's
organisation. The path follows the book's own order — Cameroon's
Citizenship Education starts inside the family, widens through
clan and tribe to human dignity and rights, goes to school as an
institution, and in its second book moves to the village, its
traditional authorities, and the council as the citizen's first
state, ending at the regions.

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in this
course comes from government textbooks — this country's own
Citizenship Education books — except the **Tip boxes**: a tip in
a box is from us, the people making LiqLearns. Tips are ours; the
lessons are the books'. See ABOUT.md for the full book list, the
PARTIAL (contents-only) footing of the whole course, and the
acceptance reckoning.

## The levels (the path, in order)

1. **The Family** — what a family is and its types, marriage and
   its certificate, the rights and duties of a child in the
   family, the responsibilities of parents, divorce taught
   head-on, and family protection — the course starts where the
   citizen starts. 6 lessons, in the title `the-family/`.
2. **Belonging, Rights and School Life** — the family widened:
   clan, tribe and ethnic groups, and the dangers of tribalism;
   then the dignity of the human being and the notion of human
   rights; then school life whole — starting the school day, the
   road from home to school, the school as an institution, how a
   school works, the school environment, and guidance toward a
   calling. 9 lessons, in the titles `belonging/` (1), `rights/`
   (2) and `school-life/` (6).
3. **The Village** — what a village is and Cameroon's village
   types, its traditional organisation and the powers of
   traditional authorities, traditional social values weighed,
   the village in the economic life of the country, and the
   restoration of the village. 5 lessons, in the title
   `the-village/`.
4. **Local Government** — councils and their types and functions,
   the management of a council, council resources and why every
   citizen pays council taxes, the council budget and civic
   spirit, the council's day-to-day services and the quality of
   life, and the regional organisation of Cameroon. 6 lessons,
   in the title `local-government/`.

**Totals: 26 lessons** (Level 1: 6 · Level 2: 9 · Level 3: 5 ·
Level 4: 6 — all from one student book in two books, placed at
chapter level from its contents: PARTIAL, contents-only — see
ABOUT.md).
