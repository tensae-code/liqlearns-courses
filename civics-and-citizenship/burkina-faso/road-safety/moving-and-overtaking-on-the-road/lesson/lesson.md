# Moving and Overtaking on the Road

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Moving and Overtaking on the Road. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Burkina Faso's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Moving and overtaking by bicycle or on foot on the public road.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Moving and overtaking by bicycle or on foot on the public road. Give one real example from your own life or from the Burkina Faso you live in, and name the lesson's key words: moving, overtaking, on. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: Moving and overtaking by bicycle or on foot on the public road.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
