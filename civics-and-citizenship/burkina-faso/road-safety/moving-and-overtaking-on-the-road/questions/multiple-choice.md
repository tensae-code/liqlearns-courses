Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Moving and overtaking by bicycle or on foot on the public road.  b) it has nothing to do with how Burkina Faso governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Moving and overtaking by bicycle or on foot on the public road.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Moving and overtaking by bicycle or on foot on the public road.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Burkina Faso
**Answer: a) Moving and overtaking by bicycle or on foot on the public road.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
