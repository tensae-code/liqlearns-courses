# Moving on Foot — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Moving about on foot safely.  b) it has nothing to do with how Burkina Faso governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Moving about on foot safely.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Moving about on foot safely.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Burkina Faso
**Answer: a) Moving about on foot safely.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
