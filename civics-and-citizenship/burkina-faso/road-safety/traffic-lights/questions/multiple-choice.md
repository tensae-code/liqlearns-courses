# Traffic Lights — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The traffic light and obeying it.  b) it has nothing to do with how Burkina Faso governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The traffic light and obeying it.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) The traffic light and obeying it.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Burkina Faso
**Answer: a) The traffic light and obeying it.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
