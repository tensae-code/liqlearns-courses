# The Stop and the Traffic Lights — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Naming the stop sign and traffic lights, what to do at each, and why they must be obeyed.  b) it has nothing to do with how Burkina Faso governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Naming the stop sign and traffic lights, what to do at each, and why they must be obeyed.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Naming the stop sign and traffic lights, what to do at each, and why they must be obeyed.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Burkina Faso
**Answer: a) Naming the stop sign and traffic lights, what to do at each, and why they must be obeyed.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
