# Picking Up Rubbish and Using Bins

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Picking Up Rubbish and Using Bins. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Burkina Faso's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Picking up rubbish and using the bins.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Picking up rubbish and using the bins. Give one real example from your own life or from the Burkina Faso you live in, and name the lesson's key words: picking, up, rubbish. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: Picking up rubbish and using the bins.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
