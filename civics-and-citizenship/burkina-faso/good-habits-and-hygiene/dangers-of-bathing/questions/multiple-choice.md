# The Dangers of Bathing — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The dangers of bathing in unsafe water.  b) it has nothing to do with how Burkina Faso governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The dangers of bathing in unsafe water.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) The dangers of bathing in unsafe water.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Burkina Faso
**Answer: a) The dangers of bathing in unsafe water.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
