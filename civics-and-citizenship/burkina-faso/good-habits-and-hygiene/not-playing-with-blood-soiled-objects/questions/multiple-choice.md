# Not Playing with Blood-Soiled Objects — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Never playing with objects soiled by blood.  b) it has nothing to do with how Burkina Faso governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Never playing with objects soiled by blood.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Never playing with objects soiled by blood.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Burkina Faso
**Answer: a) Never playing with objects soiled by blood.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
