# Mutual Help and Solidarity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Mutual help and solidarity.  b) it has nothing to do with how Burkina Faso governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Mutual help and solidarity.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Mutual help and solidarity.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Burkina Faso
**Answer: a) Mutual help and solidarity.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
