# Order and Politeness — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Order and politeness as individual and social virtues.  b) it has nothing to do with how Burkina Faso governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Order and politeness as individual and social virtues.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Order and politeness as individual and social virtues.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Burkina Faso
**Answer: a) Order and politeness as individual and social virtues.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
