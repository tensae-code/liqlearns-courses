Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Elementary citizenship — what a citizen is and the everyday rules of citizenship.  b) it has nothing to do with how Burkina Faso governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Elementary citizenship — what a citizen is and the everyday rules of citizenship.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
