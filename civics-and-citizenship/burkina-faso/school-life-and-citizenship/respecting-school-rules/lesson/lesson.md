# Respecting School Rules

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Respecting School Rules. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Burkina Faso's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Respecting the school's and class's internal rules.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Respecting the school's and class's internal rules. Give one real example from your own life or from the Burkina Faso you live in, and name the lesson's key words: respecting, school, rules. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
