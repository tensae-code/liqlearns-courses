# Respecting School Rules — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Respecting the school's and class's internal rules.  b) it has nothing to do with how Burkina Faso governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Respecting the school's and class's internal rules.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Respecting the school's and class's internal rules.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Burkina Faso
**Answer: a) Respecting the school's and class's internal rules.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
