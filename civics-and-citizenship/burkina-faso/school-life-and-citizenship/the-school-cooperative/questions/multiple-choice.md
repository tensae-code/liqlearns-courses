# The School Cooperative — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The school cooperative and the spirit of cooperation.  b) it has nothing to do with how Burkina Faso governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The school cooperative and the spirit of cooperation.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) The school cooperative and the spirit of cooperation.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Burkina Faso
**Answer: a) The school cooperative and the spirit of cooperation.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
