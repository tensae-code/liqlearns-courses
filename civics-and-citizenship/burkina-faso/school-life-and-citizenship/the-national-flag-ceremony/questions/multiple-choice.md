# The National Flag Ceremony — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) How to behave at the raising and lowering of the national flag.  b) it has nothing to do with how Burkina Faso governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) How to behave at the raising and lowering of the national flag.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) How to behave at the raising and lowering of the national flag.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Burkina Faso
**Answer: a) How to behave at the raising and lowering of the national flag.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
