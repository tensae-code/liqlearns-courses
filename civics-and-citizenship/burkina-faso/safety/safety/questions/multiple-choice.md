# Safety and Security — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Safety and security.  b) it has nothing to do with how Burkina Faso governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Safety and security.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Safety and security.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Burkina Faso
**Answer: a) Safety and security.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
