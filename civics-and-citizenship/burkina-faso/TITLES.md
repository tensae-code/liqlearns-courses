# Civics and Citizenship — Burkina Faso course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

Civics belongs to the country: this course is Burkina
Faso's own — built from Burkina Faso's Éducation Civique
et Morale books (MENAPLN, French), five teacher guides
and teacher fiche sets COMPLETE as filed, all on TEACHER
footing. Inside the course the organisation is by
**levels, not grades**: the titles below ARE the levels'
titles, in path order — what the learner proves, step by
step. The grades the books come from (CP1, CP2, CE1,
CM2) live only in each lesson's sources.

The path's own logic is Burkina Faso's, reconciled from
the two filings' ladder sketches: the first filing's
five thematic CP levels (hygiene → virtues → school life
→ rights and duties → eco-citizenship and road safety),
with the second filing's grade ladder laid over them —
CE1 at Level 2 and CM2 at Level 5, exactly where its own
placement marks put them; its CE2 and CM1 rungs are
fetched with filings pending and stock nothing. That
ladder IS the path order (recommended, never forced — a
learner can jump in higher; the "start here instead"
hints point down this ladder).

The coined lesson names in the filings were deduped
before building, by level and lesson name: 149
placements stock 83 lessons, and every placement
stocks into exactly one lesson. Four coined names recur
at two levels (CE1 at Level 2, CM2 at Level 5) and stand
as two lessons each, the CM2 lesson under a deepened
name from its own filing line. Each placement's
provenance row (book + printed page, exactly as filed)
is in that lesson's `lesson/sources.md`. Unit folders
are the filings' own theme names; a theme can run
through several levels, as Burkina Faso's own books
return to the same strands year by year.
