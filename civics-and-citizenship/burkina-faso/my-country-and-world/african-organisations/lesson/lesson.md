# African Organisations

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: African Organisations. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Burkina Faso's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

African organisations — the country in its continent.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: African organisations — the country in its continent. Give one real example from your own life or from the Burkina Faso you live in, and name the lesson's key words: african, organisations. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
