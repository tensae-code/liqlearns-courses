# Equality of Girls and Boys — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Girls and boys doing the same tasks in their living environment — equality practised, not just stated.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that Equality of Girls and Boys — and that it has no connection to the rest of Burkina Faso's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Burkina Faso's own course, in its own order.
