# Belonging to a Family and the Birth Certificate — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Belonging to a family and the birth certificate as the child's first civic document.  b) it has nothing to do with how Burkina Faso governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Belonging to a family and the birth certificate as the child's first civic document.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Belonging to a family and the birth certificate as the child's first civic document.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Burkina Faso
**Answer: a) Belonging to a family and the birth certificate as the child's first civic document.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
