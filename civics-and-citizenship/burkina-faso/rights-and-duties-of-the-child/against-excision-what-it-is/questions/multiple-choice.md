# Against Excision — What It Is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) What excision is and its consequences.  b) it has nothing to do with how Burkina Faso governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) What excision is and its consequences.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) What excision is and its consequences.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Burkina Faso
**Answer: a) What excision is and its consequences.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
