# The Right to a Name, Food and Education — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The child's right to a name and first name, to food and to education.  b) it has nothing to do with how Burkina Faso governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The child's right to a name and first name, to food and to education.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) The child's right to a name and first name, to food and to education.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Burkina Faso
**Answer: a) The child's right to a name and first name, to food and to education.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
