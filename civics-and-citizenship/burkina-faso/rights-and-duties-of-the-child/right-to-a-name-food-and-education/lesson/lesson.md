# The Right to a Name, Food and Education

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: The Right to a Name, Food and Education. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Burkina Faso's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## The Right to a Name and First Name

The child's right to a name and a first name. The child's right to a name and first name, to food and to education.

## The Right to Food

The child's right to food. The child's right to a name and first name, to food and to education.

## The Right to Education

The child's right to education. The child's right to a name and first name, to food and to education.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The child's right to a name and first name, to food and to education. Give one real example from your own life or from the Burkina Faso you live in, and name the lesson's key words: right, to, name. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: The child's right to a name and first name, to food and to education.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
