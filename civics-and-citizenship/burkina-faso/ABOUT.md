# About the Civics and Citizenship — Burkina Faso course

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in
this course comes from government textbooks — this country's
own Éducation Civique et Morale
books.
The one exception is the **Tip boxes**: a tip in a box is
from us, the people making LiqLearns. Tips are ours; the
lessons are the books'.

## How this course is organised

This is ONE course for Burkina Faso inside the Civics and
Citizenship folder (one course per country). Inside it, the
organisation is by **levels, not grades**: a learner's place
is found by what they prove, and each level stands on the
ones before it as a recommended-first path — never a forced
gate. The school grades the books come from (CP1, CP2, CE1,
CM2) appear only in each lesson's `lesson/sources.md`, as
provenance.

Burkina Faso's civics-type course is Éducation Civique et
Morale (ECM — Civic and Moral Education), the MENAPLN
subject taught through ministry preparation fiches and
teacher guides. Burkina Faso's own way, preserved whole,
as filed: ECM at CP level is taught as short practical
fiches where civic and moral teaching is inseparable from
hygiene, road safety and care of plants — citizenship
begins as habits of the body, respect for people, and care
for the shared place, with the flag, class elections and
the school cooperative as the child's first civic
institutions, and the fight against excision taught
already at this level. At CE1, civics is conduct first —
road safety, hygiene, habits and virtues — with
citizenship and children's rights arriving inside the same
fiche sequence, all taught through the ministry's standard
preparation-fiche form. By CM2 the same fiche form carries
the citizen's world — the country itself, patriotism,
civic rights, African organisations — while keeping the
conduct strand (family, solidarity, social practices)
beside it.

The filings state what they do and do not contain, and
this course carries only what the filings state. Stated
plainly at the head of everything below: **the whole
in-hand Burkina Faso set is TEACHER footing** — the first
filing's three records are teacher preparation materials
for CP1/CP2 (fiches and a guide), and the second filing's
two read books are teacher fiches for CE1 and CM2. **No
pupil books are in the census for this country**, so no
lesson in this course stands on a student book: every
lesson stands on teacher-guide/fiches evidence only, at
acceptance 0, labelled as such in its sources. The second
filing's CE2 and CM1 fiche sets were fetched and their
filings are pending — they are not read into this course
and stock nothing. Its two remaining leads are
record-only (news of the 2026 programme reform, non-book)
and stock nothing.

**Course-intro video script** (written here, made later;
the books' own shape, walked in order):

- *Burkina Faso's own order:* "First, the body and the
  shared place: keeping your body, clothes, teeth and
  eyes clean, the latrines and the faecal peril, safe
  food and drinking water, a swept classroom and a clean
  schoolyard. Then the virtues — respect, obedience,
  tolerance, forgiveness, mutual help — widened by the
  CE1 course to road safety, school life and everyday
  citizenship, and the rights of the child. Then the
  child's first civic institutions: school rules, class
  leaders chosen by election, the school cooperative and
  the flag. Then your rights and duties: your family and
  your birth certificate, your name, protection, equality
  of girls and boys, and the fight against excision.
  Last, acting in the wider world: caring for plants,
  fighting plastic waste and bush fires, the road and its
  code — and, at CM2, Burkina Faso itself, patriotism,
  civic rights, privacy, African organisations, and man
  and society. The CE2 and CM1 fiche sets are still to be
  filed: they are named in this course's About page, not
  filled in."

## The acceptance reckoning

- **Student books count — none in this course.** No
  pupil book for Burkina Faso is in the census in either
  filing, so no lesson stands on a student book and no
  lesson carries a student-book acceptance line.
- **Teacher guides and teacher fiches are evidence
  only.** All 83 of the 83 lessons stand on teacher
  guides or teacher fiches alone (the CP2 fiches, 30
  placements; the CP1 fiches, 25; the CP1 guide, 37;
  the CE1 fiches, 30; the CM2 fiches, 27) — each such
  lesson's `lesson/sources.md` carries "**Acceptance
  count: taught in 0 countries — this lesson stands on
  teacher-guide evidence only (Burkina Faso), named
  here, counted nowhere.**" The whole course therefore
  stands at acceptance 0, labelled, exactly as both
  filings state: the whole in-hand Burkina Faso set is
  TEACHER footing, so the levels count as guides-only
  in the acceptance reckoning.
- **The pending and record-only items count for
  nothing and stock nothing.** The CE2 and CM1 fiche
  sets (fetched, filings pending) and the 2 record-only
  reform-news leads carry 0 placements each.
