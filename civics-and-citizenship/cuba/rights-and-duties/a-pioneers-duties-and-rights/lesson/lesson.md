# A Pioneer's Duties and Rights

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: A Pioneer's Duties and Rights. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Cuba's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

A pioneer's duties to the homeland — study as the first duty, preparing to defend the patria, respecting the patriotic symbols, caring for social and personal property, solidarity and the defence of peace — together with the school regulations every pioneer obeys.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: A pioneer's duties to the homeland — study as the first duty, preparing to defend the patria, respecting the patriotic symbols, caring for social and personal property, solidarity and the defence of peace — together with the school regulations every pioneer obeys. Give one real example from your own life or from the Cuba you live in, and name the lesson's key words: a, pioneers, duties. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
