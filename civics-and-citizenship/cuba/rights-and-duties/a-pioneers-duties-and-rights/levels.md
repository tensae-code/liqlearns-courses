# A Pioneer's Duties and Rights — depth

This lesson is ONE lesson with no sub lessons: it teaches a single thing, whole. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: A pioneer's duties to the homeland — study as the first duty, preparing to defend the patria, respecting the patriotic symbols, caring for social and personal property, solidarity and the defence of peace — together with the school regulations every pioneer obeys. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
