# A Pioneer's Duties and Rights — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A pioneer's duties to the homeland — study as the first duty, preparing to defend the patria, respecting the patriotic symbols, caring for social and personal property, solidarity and the defence of peace — together with the school regulations every pioneer obeys.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that A Pioneer's Duties and Rights — and that it has no connection to the rest of Cuba's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Cuba's own course, in its own order.
