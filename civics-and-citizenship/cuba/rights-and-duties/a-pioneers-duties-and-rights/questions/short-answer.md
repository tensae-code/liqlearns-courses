# A Pioneer's Duties and Rights — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** A pioneer's duties to the homeland — study as the first duty, preparing to defend the patria, respecting the patriotic symbols, caring for social and personal property, solidarity and the defence of peace — together with the school regulations every pioneer obeys.
