# Everyone's Rights to A Dignified Life — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** The fundamental rights for a dignified life — work, education, health and recreation, and women's rights — enjoyed without discrimination of race or sex; human rights as a lived reality in Cuba, including the rights of the child.
