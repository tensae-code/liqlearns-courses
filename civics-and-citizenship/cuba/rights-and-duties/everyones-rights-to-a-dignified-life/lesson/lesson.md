# Everyone's Rights to A Dignified Life

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Everyone's Rights to A Dignified Life. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Cuba's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

The fundamental rights for a dignified life — work, education, health and recreation, and women's rights — enjoyed without discrimination of race or sex; human rights as a lived reality in Cuba, including the rights of the child.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The fundamental rights for a dignified life — work, education, health and recreation, and women's rights — enjoyed without discrimination of race or sex; human rights as a lived reality in Cuba, including the rights of the child. Give one real example from your own life or from the Cuba you live in, and name the lesson's key words: everyones, rights, to. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
