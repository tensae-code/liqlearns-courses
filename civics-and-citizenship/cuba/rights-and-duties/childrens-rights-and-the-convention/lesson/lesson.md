# Children's Rights and the Convention

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Children's Rights and the Convention. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Cuba's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Children's main rights under the Convention on the Rights of the Child and what they mean for Cuban children.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Children's main rights under the Convention on the Rights of the Child and what they mean for Cuban children. Give one real example from your own life or from the Cuba you live in, and name the lesson's key words: childrens, rights, and. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
