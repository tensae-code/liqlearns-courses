# Keeping Youth Problems Out of our Community — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The responsibility of families, schools and pupils to keep youth problems from spreading in their community and country.  b) it has nothing to do with how Cuba governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The responsibility of families, schools and pupils to keep youth problems from spreading in their community and country.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**Answer: a) The responsibility of families, schools and pupils to keep youth problems from spreading in their community and country.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
