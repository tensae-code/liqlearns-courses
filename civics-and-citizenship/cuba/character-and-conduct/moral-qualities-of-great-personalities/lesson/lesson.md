# Moral Qualities of Great Personalities

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Moral Qualities of Great Personalities. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Cuba's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

The moral qualities and values of great personalities at local, national and regional level, held up as examples to follow.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The moral qualities and values of great personalities at local, national and regional level, held up as examples to follow. Give one real example from your own life or from the Cuba you live in, and name the lesson's key words: moral, qualities, of. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
