# The Constitution the Law of Laws — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Constitution as the "law of laws"; unwritten rules of conduct vs written laws that bind everyone; equality before the law — no discrimination, equal pay for equal work, violations sanctioned; the law also protects natural resources and the environment.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that The Constitution the Law of Laws — and that it has no connection to the rest of Cuba's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Cuba's own course, in its own order.
