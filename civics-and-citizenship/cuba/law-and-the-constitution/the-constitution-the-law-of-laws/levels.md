# The Constitution the Law of Laws — depth

This lesson is ONE lesson with no sub lessons: it teaches a single thing, whole. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: The Constitution as the "law of laws"; unwritten rules of conduct vs written laws that bind everyone; equality before the law — no discrimination, equal pay for equal work, violations sanctioned; the law also protects natural resources and the environment. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
