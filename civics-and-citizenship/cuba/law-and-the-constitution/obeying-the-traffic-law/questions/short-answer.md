# Obeying the Traffic Law — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** The traffic code as a law everyone obeys — the pedestrian's duties on the street and respect for the traffic police who keep road users safe.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** The traffic code as a law everyone obeys — the pedestrian's duties on the street and respect for the traffic police who keep road users safe.
