# Socialist Legality

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Socialist Legality. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Cuba's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Keeping socialist legality — the constitutional and popular backing for the rule of law in Cuba and every citizen's duty to uphold it.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Keeping socialist legality — the constitutional and popular backing for the rule of law in Cuba and every citizen's duty to uphold it. Give one real example from your own life or from the Cuba you live in, and name the lesson's key words: socialist, legality. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
