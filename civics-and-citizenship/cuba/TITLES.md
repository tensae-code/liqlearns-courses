# Civics and Citizenship — Cuba course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

Civics belongs to the country: this course is Cuba's
own — built from Cuba's Educación Ciudadana, Educación
Moral y Ciudadana and Cultura Política books (Ministerio
de Educación, Cuba / Editorial Pueblo y Educación,
Spanish), all COMPLETE as filed, with five curriculum
programmes RECORD-ONLY. Inside the course the
organisation is by **levels, not grades**: the titles
below ARE the levels' titles, in path order — what the
learner proves, step by step. The grades the books come
from live only in each lesson's sources.

The path's own logic is Cuba's, from the filings'
ladder sketches, reconciled into one ladder: citizenship
starts from the child's own family and pioneer
organisation and is read straight out of the 2019
Constitution; the Grade 6 book adds children's rights,
pioneer participation, conduct and Poder Popular at
primary depth at the same opening level; secondary opens
with the citizen's morality and Martí's ethics, reads
the Constitution as a values document, and teaches
human rights together with a culture of peace; Grades
8–9 work the legal system and the politically organised
society; and Cultura Política is taught from primary
documents — pupils read Marx, Engels, Lenin, Martí,
Bolívar and Fidel in the original — closing with Cuba's
most recent history. That ladder IS the path order
(recommended, never forced — a learner can jump in
higher; the "start here instead" hints point down this
ladder).

The coined lesson names in the filings were deduped
before building, by lesson name: no coined name is used
by more than one placement across the two filings, so
126 placements stock 126 lessons, each lesson carrying
exactly one placement. Every placement stocks into
exactly one lesson, and each placement's provenance row
(book + printed page, exactly as filed) is in that
lesson's `lesson/sources.md`. Unit folders are the
filings' own theme names; a theme can run through
several levels, as Cuba's own books return to the same
themes year by year.
