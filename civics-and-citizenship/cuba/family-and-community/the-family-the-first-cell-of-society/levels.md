# The Family the First Cell of Society — depth

This lesson is ONE lesson with no sub lessons: it teaches a single thing, whole. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: The family as society's fundamental cell; the Constitution gives everyone the right to found a family and sets parents' duties to feed, respect and protect their children and children's duty to respect and protect their parents and older adults; each member's responsibilities at home and equality in housework. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
