# The Family the First Cell of Society — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** The family as society's fundamental cell; the Constitution gives everyone the right to found a family and sets parents' duties to feed, respect and protect their children and children's duty to respect and protect their parents and older adults; each member's responsibilities at home and equality in housework.
