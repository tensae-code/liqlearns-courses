# The Family the First Cell of Society

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: The Family the First Cell of Society. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Cuba's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

The family as society's fundamental cell; the Constitution gives everyone the right to found a family and sets parents' duties to feed, respect and protect their children and children's duty to respect and protect their parents and older adults; each member's responsibilities at home and equality in housework.
