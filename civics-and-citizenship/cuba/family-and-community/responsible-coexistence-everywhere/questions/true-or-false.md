# Responsible Coexistence Everywhere — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Morality for responsible citizen coexistence — how to behave and resolve friction in the family, the school, the locality and society.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that Responsible Coexistence Everywhere — and that it has no connection to the rest of Cuba's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Cuba's own course, in its own order.
