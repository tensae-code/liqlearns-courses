# Neighbours and Friendship in the Barrio — depth

This lesson is ONE lesson with no sub lessons: it teaches a single thing, whole. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: The families of the neighbourhood are treated as family — respect, cooperation between households and care for shared surroundings; friendship as honesty, sincerity and solidarity, told apart from false friendship. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
