# The Popular Council — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The Consejo Popular and the part played in it by the school, the family and the pioneers.  b) it has nothing to do with how Cuba governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The Consejo Popular and the part played in it by the school, the family and the pioneers.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) The Consejo Popular and the part played in it by the school, the family and the pioneers.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Cuba
**Answer: a) The Consejo Popular and the part played in it by the school, the family and the pioneers.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
