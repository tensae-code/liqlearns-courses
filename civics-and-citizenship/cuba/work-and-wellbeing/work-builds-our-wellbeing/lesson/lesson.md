# Work Builds our Wellbeing

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Work Builds our Wellbeing. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Cuba's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Work as the source of all material and spiritual wealth; every kind of work is necessary and honoured; workers' duties and rights guaranteed by law; women's incorporation into work as social justice; voluntary, socially useful work.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Work as the source of all material and spiritual wealth; every kind of work is necessary and honoured; workers' duties and rights guaranteed by law; women's incorporation into work as social justice; voluntary, socially useful work. Give one real example from your own life or from the Cuba you live in, and name the lesson's key words: work, builds, our. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: Work as the source of all material and spiritual wealth; every kind of work is necessary and honoured; workers' duties and rights guaranteed by law; women's incorporation into work as social justice; voluntary, socially useful work.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
