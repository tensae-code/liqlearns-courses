# Work Builds our Wellbeing — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Work as the source of all material and spiritual wealth; every kind of work is necessary and honoured; workers' duties and rights guaranteed by law; women's incorporation into work as social justice; voluntary, socially useful work.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that Work Builds our Wellbeing — and that it has no connection to the rest of Cuba's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Cuba's own course, in its own order.
