# Video script — Cuba's National Symbols

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

This lesson is about one thing.
Cuba's national symbols under the symbols law and the Constitution — the lone-star flag, the Bayamo anthem and the royal-palm shield, their parts and meanings, and their correct use (hoisting, lowering, escorting and folding the flag, standing at attention); the nation's other emblems (bird, flower, tree).
