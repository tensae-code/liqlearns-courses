# Cuba's National Symbols — depth

This lesson is ONE lesson with no sub lessons: it teaches a single thing, whole. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: Cuba's national symbols under the symbols law and the Constitution — the lone-star flag, the Bayamo anthem and the royal-palm shield, their parts and meanings, and their correct use (hoisting, lowering, escorting and folding the flag, standing at attention); the nation's other emblems (bird, flower, tree). — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
