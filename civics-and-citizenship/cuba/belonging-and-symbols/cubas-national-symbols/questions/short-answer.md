# Cuba's National Symbols — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** Cuba's national symbols under the symbols law and the Constitution — the lone-star flag, the Bayamo anthem and the royal-palm shield, their parts and meanings, and their correct use (hoisting, lowering, escorting and folding the flag, standing at attention); the nation's other emblems (bird, flower, tree).
