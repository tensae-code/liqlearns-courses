# What Makes the Cuban Nation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** What makes the Cuban nation — its territory, its people, the Spanish language and its living traditions and customs, learned as part of belonging to the patria.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that What Makes the Cuban Nation — and that it has no connection to the rest of Cuba's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Cuba's own course, in its own order.
