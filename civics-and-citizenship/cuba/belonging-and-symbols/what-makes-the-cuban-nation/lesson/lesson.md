# What Makes the Cuban Nation

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: What Makes the Cuban Nation. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Cuba's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

What makes the Cuban nation — its territory, its people, the Spanish language and its living traditions and customs, learned as part of belonging to the patria.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: What makes the Cuban nation — its territory, its people, the Spanish language and its living traditions and customs, learned as part of belonging to the patria. Give one real example from your own life or from the Cuba you live in, and name the lesson's key words: what, makes, the. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
