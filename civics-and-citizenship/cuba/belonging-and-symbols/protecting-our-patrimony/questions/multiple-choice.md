# Protecting our Patrimony — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Pupils, families and schools protecting the patrimony.  b) it has nothing to do with how Cuba governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Pupils, families and schools protecting the patrimony.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Pupils, families and schools protecting the patrimony.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Cuba
**Answer: a) Pupils, families and schools protecting the patrimony.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
