# Fidel and Marti on Liberation

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Fidel and Marti on Liberation. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Cuba's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

The liberation tradition in its own words — Fidel's reflections and speeches, Martí's homage to Bolívar, and Bolívar's Angostura Address and Jamaica Letter.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The liberation tradition in its own words — Fidel's reflections and speeches, Martí's homage to Bolívar, and Bolívar's Angostura Address and Jamaica Letter. Give one real example from your own life or from the Cuba you live in, and name the lesson's key words: fidel, and, marti. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
