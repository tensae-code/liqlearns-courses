# Lives of the Founders

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Lives of the Founders. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Cuba's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

The lives of the personalities the subject studies — Bolívar, Marx, Engels, Martí, Lenin, Fidel Castro and Che Guevara — as the human doorway into their ideas.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The lives of the personalities the subject studies — Bolívar, Marx, Engels, Martí, Lenin, Fidel Castro and Che Guevara — as the human doorway into their ideas. Give one real example from your own life or from the Cuba you live in, and name the lesson's key words: lives, of, the. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
