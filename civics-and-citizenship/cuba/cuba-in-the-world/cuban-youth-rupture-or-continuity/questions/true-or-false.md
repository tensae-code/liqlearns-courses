# Cuban Youth Rupture Or Continuity — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Cuban youth and their ideological-behavioural foundations in recent socialist construction — rupture or continuity?
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that Cuban Youth Rupture Or Continuity — and that it has no connection to the rest of Cuba's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Cuba's own course, in its own order.
