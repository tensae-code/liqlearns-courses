# Cuban Youth Rupture Or Continuity

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Cuban Youth Rupture Or Continuity. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Cuba's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Cuban youth and their ideological-behavioural foundations in recent socialist construction — rupture or continuity?

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Cuban youth and their ideological-behavioural foundations in recent socialist construction — rupture or continuity? Give one real example from your own life or from the Cuba you live in, and name the lesson's key words: cuban, youth, rupture. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
