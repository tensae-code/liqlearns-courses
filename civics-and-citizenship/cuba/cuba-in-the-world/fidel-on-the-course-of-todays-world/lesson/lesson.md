# Fidel on the Course of Todays World

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Fidel on the Course of Todays World. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Cuba's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

The course of today's world criticised from the Revolution's ideology — thirty-five texts of Fidel Castro.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The course of today's world criticised from the Revolution's ideology — thirty-five texts of Fidel Castro. Give one real example from your own life or from the Cuba you live in, and name the lesson's key words: fidel, on, the. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: The course of today's world criticised from the Revolution's ideology — thirty-five texts of Fidel Castro.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
