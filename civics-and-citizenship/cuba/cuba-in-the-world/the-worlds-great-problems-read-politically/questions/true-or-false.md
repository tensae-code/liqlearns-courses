# The Worlds Great Problems Read Politically — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The world's great problems read politically — development and underdevelopment, war and peace, poverty and the great social problems, migration, democracy and human rights, and the environment.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that The Worlds Great Problems Read Politically — and that it has no connection to the rest of Cuba's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Cuba's own course, in its own order.
