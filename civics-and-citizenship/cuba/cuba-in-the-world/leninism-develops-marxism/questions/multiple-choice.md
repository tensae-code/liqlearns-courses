# Leninism Develops Marxism — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Leninism as the tendency that develops Marxism.  b) it has nothing to do with how Cuba governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Leninism as the tendency that develops Marxism.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Leninism as the tendency that develops Marxism.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Cuba
**Answer: a) Leninism as the tendency that develops Marxism.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
