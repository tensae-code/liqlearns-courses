# Cuban Thought Meets Marxism

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Cuban Thought Meets Marxism. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Cuba's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.
