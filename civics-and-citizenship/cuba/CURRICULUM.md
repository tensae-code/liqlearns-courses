# Civics and Citizenship — Cuba — curriculum

One course for Cuba inside the Civics and Citizenship
folder, built from Cuba's own government textbooks:
Educación Ciudadana (Grade 5 teacher guide and Grade 6
student book), Educación Moral y Ciudadana (Grades 7–9
student books, with the Grade 8 teacher guide), and
Cultura Política (Grades 10–12 student books and study
compilations, with the Grade 11 teacher guide) — all
Spanish, all COMPLETE as filed, with five MINED
curriculum programmes standing RECORD-ONLY with 0
placements (see ABOUT.md for the full book list, the
reading routes, and every label carried exactly as
filed). The organisation is by **levels, not grades**: a
learner's place is found by what they prove. The path is
recommended, never forced — a learner can jump in
higher; the "start here instead" hints point down this
ladder. The grades the books come from appear only in
provenance (ABOUT.md and each lesson's
`lesson/sources.md`).

The ladder follows Cuba's own books' progression, as
the two filings' ladder sketches lay it out, reconciled
into one ladder: the Grade 6 book in the second filing
sits at Level 1 below the first filing's ladder, between
Grade 5 and Grade 7, exactly as both sketches file it.
Citizenship starts in the family and the barrio and with
the child's own rights (Level 1), widens to the
Constitution, the state and work (Level 2), turns to the
citizen's morality (Level 3), reads the 2019 Constitution
as a values document (Level 4), takes human rights
together with a culture of peace (Level 5), works the
legal system and the politically organised society
(Level 6), and closes with Cultura Política — the
Revolution's ideology founded in primary documents
(Level 7), its makers and today's world (Level 8), and
everything applied to Cuba's most recent history
(Level 9).

## Level 1 — Family, Rights and First Belonging — 31 lessons

Citizenship starts in the family and the barrio and with the child's own rights: the Grade 5 teacher guide's opening units (family as society's fundamental cell, neighbours and friendship, the patria and its symbols, the pioneer's duties and rights) and the Grade 6 student book's own order — children's rights under the Convention, pioneer participation and the citizen's duty, conduct at home, school and in society, the Constitution, Poder Popular and the circunscripción, and finally homeland, identity and Cuba in its region. Lessons: The Family the First Cell of Society · Neighbours and Friendship in the Barrio · Cuba's National Symbols · What Makes the Cuban Nation · A Pioneer's Duties and Rights · The Convention · Rights in Other Countries · The Convention in Cuba · What Socialism Won · Children and Democracy · Study Vocation Life Project · Why Citizen Education Matters · Behaviour at Home · Behaviour at School · Behaviour in Society · Health Norms with Others · The Law of Laws · Poder Popular and the Circunscripcion · The Popular Council · Defending Against Disasters · Cuba's Budget and Taxes · The Family and Wellbeing · Helping My Family Thrive · Road Safety · Contributing to Road Safety · Nationality and Patriotism · Personal and National Identity · Culture and Traditions · Our Region · Cooperation with Other Countries · The Blockade.

## Level 2 — The Constitution, the State and Work — 6 lessons

The Grade 5 teacher guide's second half: the Constitution as law of laws and the traffic law, the people choosing their leaders and the organisations of Cuban society, work as the source of wellbeing, and everyone's rights to a dignified life. Lessons: The Constitution the Law of Laws · Obeying the Traffic Law · The People Choose their Leaders · The Organisations of Cuban Society · Work Builds our Wellbeing · Everyone's Rights to A Dignified Life.

## Level 3 — The Cuban Citizen's Morality — 7 lessons

Grade 7 Unidad 1: what morality is, the history of civic formation in Cuban education, traditions and identity, citizen formation, Martí's ethics, the moral qualities of great personalities, and responsible coexistence in family, school, locality and society. Lessons: What Morality Is and Why Citizens Study It · How Civic Education Grew in Cuba · Traditions That Build Identity · Citizen Formation in Cuban Education · Martí's Ethics for Citizens · Moral Qualities of Great Personalities · Responsible Coexistence Everywhere.

## Level 4 — The 2019 Constitution and Its Values — 9 lessons

Grade 7 Unidad 2: who the Cubans are, Cuban constitutional history and the roots of the socialist Constitution, the 2019 Constitution and its values, nationality and citizenship, constitutional rights and duties, equality in daily life, defence of the patria, and the symbols and attributes of the patria at constitutional depth. Lessons: Who We Cubans Are · Cuba's Constitutional History · Roots of the Socialist Constitution · The 2019 Constitution and its Values · Nationality and Citizenship in Cuba · Constitutional Rights and Duties · Equality of Rights in Daily Life · Contributing to the Defence of the Patria · Symbols and Attributes of the Patria.

## Level 5 — Human Rights and a Culture of Peace — 6 lessons

Grade 7 Unidad 3: a short history of human rights in Cuba, the international human-rights instruments, women's challenges in today's world, children's rights and the Convention, the blockade as Cuba frames it, and the culture of peace. Lessons: Human Rights in Cuba A Short History · International Human Rights Instruments · Women's Challenges in Todays World · Children's Rights and the Convention · The Blockade as A Rights Violation · A Culture of Peace.

## Level 6 — Laws in Working Order and the Politically Organised Society — 26 lessons

Grades 8–9: the legal system in working order — socialist legality, the electoral law, the budget, the road safety code, illegality, family law and families' challenges — then the politically organised society itself (the political system, youth protagonism, the socialist state of law, mass and social organisations, defence and national security) and the world's problems. Lessons: Socialist Legality · The Electoral Law and the Vote · The Budget Without Distinction · The Road Safety Code · Illegality and its Consequences · Anti-Cuban Laws from Abroad · The Family the Fundamental Cell · Laws That Protect Families · Challenges Facing Cuban Families · Families Guard the Environment · Responsible Personal Action · The Adolescent in the Defence of the Revolution · Aesthetic Education and Integral Culture · Responsibility at Home and Prevention · Teaching the Laws of Cuba · Teaching Families and Community · Teaching Adolescent Responsibility · The Cuban Political System · Youth Protagonism in the Political System · The Socialist State of Law · Mass and Social Organisations · Defence and National Security · The Worlds Great Asymmetries · Youth Problems in Capitalist Countries · Keeping Youth Problems Out of our Community · Protecting our Patrimony.

## Level 7 — Political Culture Founded — 20 lessons

Grade 10: Cultura Política defined — the Revolution's ideology and political culture, the ideological struggle, Latin-American emancipatory thought, Marxism and Leninism and their other developers, Cuba's founding thought and symbols — read in primary documents (the 2012 Documentos compilation) beside the narrative text (2023). Lessons: Lives of the Founders · Marx and Engels in their Own Words · Lenin on Marxism · Fidel and Marti on Liberation · Work and Primitive Accumulation · The Manifesto and Imperialism · What Political Culture Is · Revolutionary Ideology and Political Culture · The Ideological Struggle Today · Latin American Emancipatory Thought · The Marxist Doctrine · Leninism Develops Marxism · Other Developers of Marxism · Cuba's Founding Thought · Symbols in the Revolutionary Ideology · Ideological Legacy of the Independence Heroes · Marti as Ideological Exponent · Anti-Imperialism as Patriotism · Cuban Thought Meets Marxism · Fidel as Symbol of the Ideology.

## Level 8 — The Ideology's Makers and Today's World — 10 lessons

Grade 11: the founding Cuban thinkers, Martí as a basic nucleus, the twentieth-century articulators and other ideologues, then Fidel, Chávez, Morales and Correa in their own words (the Documentos II compilation), with the teacher guide reading political culture and the recent contemporary epoch's great problems. Lessons: The Founding Cuban Thinkers · Marti the Basic Nucleus · Articulators of Cuban Revolutionary Thought · Other Ideologues of Cuban Thought · Fidel on the Course of Todays World · Latin American Leaders in their Own Words · Political Culture and the Recent Contemporary Epoch · Characteristics of Todays World · The Worlds Great Problems Read Politically · Formative Activities Beyond the Classroom.

## Level 9 — Cuba's Most Recent History Applied — 11 lessons

Grade 12: everything applied to Cuba's socialist construction in its most recent history — Che and Fidel's ideas in practice, the particularities and reconfiguration of socialist construction, changes in the political system and laws, foreign policy, US–Cuba relations, the updating of the socioeconomic model, and Cuban youth as rupture or continuity. Lessons: Political Culture in Recent Cuban History · Ideologies in the Recent Revolutionary Process · Ideological Struggle in Recent History · Che and Fidel on Building Socialism · Particularities of Cuban Socialist Construction · Reconfiguration and the Battle of Ideas · Changes in the Political System and Laws · Cuban Foreign Policy as Ideology · US Cuba Relations · Updating the Socioeconomic Model · Cuban Youth Rupture Or Continuity.

**Totals: 126 lessons** (Level 1: 31 · Level 2: 6 · Level 3: 7 · Level 4: 9 · Level 5: 6 · Level 6: 26 · Level 7: 20 · Level 8: 10 · Level 9: 11 — from sixteen books read as filed: eight student books, three teacher guides (Orientaciones metodológicas) whose lessons stand as labelled evidence only, and five curriculum programmes standing RECORD-ONLY with 0 placements; 126 placements stocking 126 lessons).
