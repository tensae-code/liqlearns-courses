# The Blockade — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The economic blockade imposed by the United States government, as the book presents its effects on Cuba.  b) it has nothing to do with how Cuba governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The economic blockade imposed by the United States government, as the book presents its effects on Cuba.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) The economic blockade imposed by the United States government, as the book presents its effects on Cuba.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Cuba
**Answer: a) The economic blockade imposed by the United States government, as the book presents its effects on Cuba.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
