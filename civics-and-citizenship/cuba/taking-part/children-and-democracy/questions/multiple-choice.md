# Children and Democracy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Children's participation and exercise of democracy through the pioneer organisation.  b) it has nothing to do with how Cuba governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Children's participation and exercise of democracy through the pioneer organisation.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Children's participation and exercise of democracy through the pioneer organisation.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Cuba
**Answer: a) Children's participation and exercise of democracy through the pioneer organisation.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
