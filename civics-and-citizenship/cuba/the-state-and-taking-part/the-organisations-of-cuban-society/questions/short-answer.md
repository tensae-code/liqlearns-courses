# The Organisations of Cuban Society — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** The mass and student organisations of Cuban society — the pioneers' own OPJM with its motto, school structure and elected leaders, the CDR and FMC, the Communist Party as leading political organisation and the UJC.
