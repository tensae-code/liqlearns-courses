# The People Choose their Leaders — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Sovereignty resides in the people, who elect their rulers freely; delegates answer to their electors in accountability assemblies.  b) it has nothing to do with how Cuba governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Sovereignty resides in the people, who elect their rulers freely; delegates answer to their electors in accountability assemblies.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Sovereignty resides in the people, who elect their rulers freely; delegates answer to their electors in accountability assemblies.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Cuba
**Answer: a) Sovereignty resides in the people, who elect their rulers freely; delegates answer to their electors in accountability assemblies.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
