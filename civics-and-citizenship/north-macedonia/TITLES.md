# Civics and Citizenship — North Macedonia course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

Civics belongs to the country: this course is North
Macedonia's own — built from North Macedonia's Civic
Education student book for Grade 9 and its gymnasium
Ethics student book (3rd year), both in Macedonian. Inside
the course the organisation is by **levels, not grades**:
the titles below ARE the levels' titles, in path order —
what the learner proves, step by step. The grades the
books come from live only in each lesson's sources.

The path's own logic is North Macedonia's, from the
filing's ladder sketch: the Grade 9 book opens with the
pupil's own civic identity (developing my traits,
ethics/values/humanity, career development), widens to
living with others — cooperation, differences, viewpoints,
conflict, interculturalism, social responsibility, civic
organisations, culture, religion and social media — then
turns to the citizen and the state (justice and the rule
of law, the legal state, elections, administration serving
citizens), and closes outward with the citizen and Europe.
The gymnasium Ethics book starts again at the root — what
ethics and morality are, life as the highest value, and
the building elements (good and evil, will, character,
conscience, the moral norm) — then ethics in the
religions, the modern teachings (utilitarianism,
consequentialism, care) and today's priorities, and ends
applied: bioethics and addiction, family and universal
ethics, work and business ethics, and ethics and
democracy, peace and human rights. That ladder IS the
path order (recommended, never forced — a learner can
jump in higher; the "start here instead" hints point down
this ladder).
