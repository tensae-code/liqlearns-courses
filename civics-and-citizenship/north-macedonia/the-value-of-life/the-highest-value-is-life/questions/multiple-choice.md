# The Highest Value Is Life — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Life as the highest value.  b) it has nothing to do with how North Macedonia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Life as the highest value.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Life as the highest value.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in North Macedonia
**Answer: a) Life as the highest value.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
