# Social Media and Citizens — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The influence of social media on citizens.  b) it has nothing to do with how North Macedonia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The influence of social media on citizens.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) The influence of social media on citizens.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in North Macedonia
**Answer: a) The influence of social media on citizens.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
