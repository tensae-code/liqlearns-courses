# Accepting and Respecting Differences — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Accepting and respecting differences.  b) it has nothing to do with how North Macedonia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Accepting and respecting differences.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Accepting and respecting differences.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in North Macedonia
**Answer: a) Accepting and respecting differences.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
