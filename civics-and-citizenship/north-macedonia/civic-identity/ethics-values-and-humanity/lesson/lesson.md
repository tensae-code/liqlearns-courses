# Ethics, Values and Humanity

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Ethics, Values and Humanity. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what North Macedonia's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## Sub lesson 1 — Ethicality

Ethicality — being ethical — as one strand of the citizen's inner compass.

## Sub lesson 2 — Values

The values a citizen holds, and how they steer the citizen's choices.

## Sub lesson 3 — Humanity

Humanity — treating every person as a person — as the third strand of that compass.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Ethicality, values and humanity as the citizen's inner compass. Give one real example from your own life or from the North Macedonia you live in, and name the lesson's key words: ethics, values, and. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
