# Lesson plan — Ethics, Values and Humanity

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — North Macedonia · **Title:** Civic Identity · **Lesson:** Ethics, Values and Humanity
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — Civic Identity (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Ethicality, values and humanity as the citizen's inner compass. This lesson stands on a student book.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Ethicality** — Ethicality — being ethical — as one strand of the citizen's inner compass.
2. **Values** — The values a citizen holds, and how they steer the citizen's choices.
3. **Humanity** — Humanity — treating every person as a person — as the third strand of that compass.

Gate pool: at most 5 questions for the prove-it check, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Stands in path order after **Developing My Own Traits** — do that lesson first if this one feels hard (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Ethicality, values and humanity as the citizen's inner compass.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, school or town — or, for this book's lessons, from the public life of North Macedonia they live in.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the learner's own life in North Macedonia and the book's own teaching, as the book itself works.
