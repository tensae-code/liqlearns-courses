# What Is Ethics — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) What ethics is.  b) it has nothing to do with how North Macedonia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) What ethics is.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) What ethics is.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in North Macedonia
**Answer: a) What ethics is.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
