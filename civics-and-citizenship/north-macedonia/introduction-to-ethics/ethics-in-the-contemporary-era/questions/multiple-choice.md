# Ethics in the Contemporary Era — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Ethics in the contemporary era.  b) it has nothing to do with how North Macedonia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Ethics in the contemporary era.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Ethics in the contemporary era.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in North Macedonia
**Answer: a) Ethics in the contemporary era.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
