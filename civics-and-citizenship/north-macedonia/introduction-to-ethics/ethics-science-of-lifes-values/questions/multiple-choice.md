# Ethics — the Science of Life's Values — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Ethics as the science of life's values.  b) it has nothing to do with how North Macedonia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Ethics as the science of life's values.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Ethics as the science of life's values.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in North Macedonia
**Answer: a) Ethics as the science of life's values.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
