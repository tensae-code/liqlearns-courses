# About the Civics and Citizenship — North Macedonia course

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in
this course comes from government textbooks — this country's
own Civic Education and Ethics books. The one
exception is the **Tip boxes**: a tip in a box is from us,
the people making LiqLearns. Tips are ours; the lessons are
the books'.

## How this course is organised

This is ONE course for North Macedonia inside the Civics and
Citizenship folder (one course per country). Inside it, the
organisation is by **levels, not grades**: a learner's place
is found by what they prove, and each level stands on the
ones before it as a recommended-first path — never a forced
gate. The school grades the books come from appear only in
each lesson's `lesson/sources.md`, as provenance.

North Macedonia's own way, preserved whole, as filed: the
Grade 9 Civic Education course is built as six themes that
move from the pupil's own civic identity and values, through
cooperation, difference and conflict in civil society, to
the citizen facing the state (justice, rule of law,
elections, administration) — and it ends, distinctively,
with the citizen and Europe: the EU and North Macedonia's
place in it. The gymnasium Ethics course (Temkov) is a full
moral philosophy course in Macedonian — from what ethics and
morality are, through the building elements (good and evil,
will, character, conscience, moral norm), ethics in the
religions, modern teachings (utilitarianism,
consequentialism, ethics of care), bioethics and addiction,
and out to social, economic and political ethics, closing
on democracy, peace and human rights — taught with songs,
cases and moral-dilemma exercises (the democracy lesson
opens from 'Give Peace a Chance').

The course as built has **seven stocked levels**, from the
filing for this build (`north-macedonia-placements.md` —
2 records / 2 distinct books. The filing's own header notes
that the assignment note said 4 records for this country,
but the group's targets file targets-af-b.json contains 2 —
Civic Education Grade 9 and Ethics Grade 12 — and those are
the 2 filed. 66 placements in all, stocking 66 lessons —
the coined lesson names were deduped before building: every
coined name was already distinct, so no lesson is stocked by
more than one placement, and every placement stocks into
exactly one lesson):

1. **Level 1 — Civic Identity (3 lessons).** Developing my
   own traits, ethics, values and humanity, and professional
   and career development (Grade 9 Civic Education, Тема 1).
2. **Level 2 — Living With Others in Civil Society
   (9 lessons).** Accepting and respecting differences,
   attitudes and viewpoints, conflicts and dealing with
   conflicts, interculturalism in civil society; social
   responsibility, civic organisations and advocacy groups;
   culture and cultural identity, the role of religion in
   civil society, and social media and citizens (Grade 9
   Civic Education, Темы 2–4).
3. **Level 3 — The Citizen and the State (4 lessons).**
   Justice and the rule of law, the legal state, elections
   and democratic processes, and administration in the
   service of citizens (Grade 9 Civic Education, Тема 5).
4. **Level 4 — The Citizen and Europe (2 lessons).** The
   European Union, and North Macedonia in the European Union
   (Grade 9 Civic Education, Тема 6).
5. **Level 5 — What Ethics Is, the Value of Life and Its
   Building Elements (20 lessons).** What ethics is, what
   morality is, ethics as the science of life's values,
   ethics in the contemporary era, ethical education; life
   and its value, the goals of life, life as the highest
   value, a positive stance toward life; good and evil,
   autonomy and heteronomy, will, character, intention and
   deed, choice of values, the moral norm, ethical
   evaluation, conscience, reason and ethical reckoning,
   and building an autonomous personality (Ethics, Parts
   I–III).
6. **Level 6 — Ethics in Religions, Modern Teachings and
   Today's Priorities (12 lessons).** Religion and ethics,
   the Ten Commandments, Christian ethics, the ethics of
   Islam, the ethics of other religions; utilitarianism,
   consequentialism, the ethics of care; the most important
   contemporary values, essential positive values,
   responsibility and duty, and negative moral phenomena
   (Ethics, Parts IV–VI).
7. **Level 7 — Ethics Applied — Bioethics, Society, Work
   and Politics (16 lessons).** Bioethics as a new leading
   discipline, ecological ethics, the ethics of health,
   addictions and drugs; I and the others, ethics in the
   family, universal ethics, avoiding and resolving
   conflicts, etiquette; work ethics and business ethics;
   ethics and democracy, peace, human rights, and writing
   an ethical essay (Ethics, Parts VII–X and the closing
   essay chapter).

**Course-intro video script** (written here, made later;
the books' own shape, walked in order):

- *North Macedonia's own order:* "First, your own civic
  identity: the traits you develop, the ethics, values and
  humanity that steer you, and the working life you are
  growing toward. Then living with others: accepting
  differences, holding viewpoints, handling conflicts,
  living interculturally, taking social responsibility, and
  the organisations citizens act through — with culture,
  religion and social media around it all. Then you face
  the state: justice and the rule of law, the legal state,
  elections, and an administration that serves citizens.
  And the first book closes looking outward, to Europe.
  The second book goes to the root: what ethics and
  morality are, why life is the highest value, and the
  building elements — good and evil, will, character,
  conscience, the moral norm. Then ethics in the religions,
  the modern teachings, and today's values and duties.
  Last, ethics at work in the world: the body and health,
  family and society, work and business, and democracy,
  peace and human rights — and you close by writing an
  ethical essay of your own."
