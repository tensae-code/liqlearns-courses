# The Ten Commandments — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The Ten Commandments as moral teaching.  b) it has nothing to do with how North Macedonia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The Ten Commandments as moral teaching.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) The Ten Commandments as moral teaching.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in North Macedonia
**Answer: a) The Ten Commandments as moral teaching.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
