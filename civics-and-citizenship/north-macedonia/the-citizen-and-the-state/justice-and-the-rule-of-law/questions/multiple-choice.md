# Justice and the Rule of Law — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Justice and the rule of law.  b) it has nothing to do with how North Macedonia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Justice and the rule of law.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Justice and the rule of law.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in North Macedonia
**Answer: a) Justice and the rule of law.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
