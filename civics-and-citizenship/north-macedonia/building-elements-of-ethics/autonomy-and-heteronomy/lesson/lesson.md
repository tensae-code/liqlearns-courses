# Autonomy and Heteronomy

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Autonomy and Heteronomy. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what North Macedonia's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## Sub lesson 1 — Ethical autonomy

Ethical autonomy — deciding for yourself what is right.

## Sub lesson 2 — Heteronomy

Heteronomy — being ruled from outside in your moral choices, the opposite of autonomy.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Ethical autonomy and heteronomy — deciding for yourself versus being ruled from outside. Give one real example from your own life or from the North Macedonia you live in, and name the lesson's key words: autonomy, and, heteronomy. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
