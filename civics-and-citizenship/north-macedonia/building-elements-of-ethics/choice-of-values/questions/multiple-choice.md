# Choice of Values — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Choosing one's values.  b) it has nothing to do with how North Macedonia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Choosing one's values.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Choosing one's values.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in North Macedonia
**Answer: a) Choosing one's values.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
