# Intention and Deed — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Intention and deed.  b) it has nothing to do with how North Macedonia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Intention and deed.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Intention and deed.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in North Macedonia
**Answer: a) Intention and deed.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
