# Peace — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Peace as an ethical value.  b) it has nothing to do with how North Macedonia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Peace as an ethical value.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Peace as an ethical value.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in North Macedonia
**Answer: a) Peace as an ethical value.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
