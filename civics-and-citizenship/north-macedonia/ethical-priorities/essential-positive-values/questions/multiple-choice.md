# Essential Positive Values — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The essential positive values.  b) it has nothing to do with how North Macedonia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The essential positive values.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) The essential positive values.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in North Macedonia
**Answer: a) The essential positive values.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
