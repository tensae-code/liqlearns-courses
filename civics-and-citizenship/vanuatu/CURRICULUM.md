# Civics and Citizenship — Vanuatu course — CURRICULUM (the path)

One course for Vanuatu inside the Civics and Citizenship
folder, organised by **levels, not grades**: a learner climbs
when they have proved the level, not when a school calendar
says so. The school grades the books come from appear only in
each lesson's sources (provenance), never as the course's
organisation. The path follows Vanuatu's own order as its
books lay it out — the citizen and their rights first, then
the machinery of democracy (the Constitution, the three arms,
Parliament, MPs and local government, including the
Malvatumauri National Council of Chiefs), then elections,
voting and the citizens and media who watch government, and
at the top the rules that keep elections fair, the inclusion
of everyone, good leaders, and a class election run by the
learners themselves. The filings for this build
(`vanuatu-placements.md` — 3 records / 3 distinct books, 32
placements; and `vanuatu-placements-b.md` — 14 link leads
triaged, 2 distinct new books read, 18 placements; 50
placements in all, from five distinct books read) stock 27
lessons: the filings' coined lesson names were deduped
before building — the student booklet, the teacher manual
and the student workbook are three holdings of the one
"Vanuatu Blong Yu" course, and the French stream's guide
carries parallel strands — so parallel placements for the
same lesson stock into that one lesson as its parts, and
every placement stocks into exactly one lesson.

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in
this course comes from government textbooks — this country's
own civic education books — except the **Tip boxes**: a tip
in a box is from us, the people making LiqLearns. Tips are
ours; the lessons are the books'. See ABOUT.md for the full
book list and the acceptance reckoning. **Seven of this
course's lessons rest on teacher guides alone and are
labelled guides-only throughout.**

## The levels (the path, in order)

1. **The Citizen and Belonging** — who a citizen of Vanuatu
   is and what makes a good one; Pacific belonging through
   place and community, the sea and the land (the ESD
   readers' teaching, on teacher-guide evidence); then
   rights and responsibilities together, human rights, and
   the Constitution's promise that all citizens are equal.
   8 lessons, in two titles: `belonging-and-citizenship/`
   (5 lessons), `rights-and-equality/` (3 lessons). **Three
   of this level's lessons — the ESD readers' three — rest
   on a teacher guide alone — evidence only, acceptance 0.**
2. **Democracy and How Vanuatu Is Governed** — what
   democracy is, independence and the three arms of
   government; Parliament, the work of an MP, local
   government and the Malvatumauri, and the whole system of
   government as one picture; and the French stream's two
   method-side lessons, on teacher-guide evidence: the
   active citizen at school, and teaching social citizenship
   across the curriculum. 8 lessons, in four titles:
   `democracy-and-the-constitution/` (2 lessons),
   `parliament-and-government/` (4 lessons),
   `belonging-and-citizenship/` (1 lesson), `taking-part/`
   (1 lesson). **Two of this level's lessons rest on a
   teacher guide alone — evidence only, acceptance 0.**
3. **Elections, Voting and Watching Government** — civil
   society and the media, transparency and accountability,
   and community action on a shared issue; what an election
   is; voting itself — your vote is your voice; and seven
   Ni-Vanuatu citizens saying why they vote. 6 lessons, in
   two titles: `taking-part/` (4 lessons),
   `elections-and-voting/` (2 lessons). **One of this
   level's lessons — community action — rests on a teacher
   guide alone — evidence only, acceptance 0.**
4. **Fair Elections, Inclusion and Leading** — candidates,
   programmes and campaigns; electoral offences and the
   courts that guard the vote; including everyone in a
   democracy; what makes a good leader; and the class
   election, the course's practical capstone. 5 lessons, in
   three titles: `elections-and-voting/` (2 lessons),
   `rights-and-equality/` (1 lesson), `taking-part/`
   (2 lessons). **One of this level's lessons — the class
   election — rests on a teacher guide alone — evidence
   only, acceptance 0.**

**Totals: 27 lessons** (Level 1: 8 · Level 2: 8 · Level 3:
6 · Level 4: 5 — from five read books: the *Vanuatu blong
yu* student booklet (Vanuatu Electoral Office and Department
of Curriculum Development, with UNDP VEEP, 2020, COMPLETE),
its Civic Education Teacher Manual (2020, COMPLETE as
TEACHER evidence), the Pacific-regional ESD Readers Teacher
Guide (UNESCO / University of the South Pacific, 2020,
PARTIAL — civics parts only, as TEACHER evidence), the
Civic Education Student Workbook (Vanuatu Electoral Office
and Curriculum Development Unit, with UNDP VEEP, 2020,
COMPLETE), and the French stream's *Citoyenneté Sociale*
teacher guide for Years 7–10 (SPC and the Ministry of
Education and Training, 2019 (Suva, 2018), COMPLETE as
TEACHER evidence); 50 placements stocking 27 lessons).
