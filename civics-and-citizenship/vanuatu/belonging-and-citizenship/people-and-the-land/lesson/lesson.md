# People and the Land

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: people and the Land. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Vanuatu's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Pacific people hold a special relationship with the land: elders pass traditional knowledge to the next generation, and local legends carry the values a community lives by.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Pacific people hold a special relationship with the land: elders pass traditional knowledge to the next generation, and local legends carry the values a community lives by. Give one real example from your own life or from the Vanuatu you live in, and name the lesson's key words: people, and. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: Pacific people hold a special relationship with the land: elders pass traditional knowledge to the next generation, and local legends carry the values a community lives by.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
