# People and the Land — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Pacific people hold a special relationship with the land: elders pass traditional knowledge to the next generation, and local legends carry the values a community lives by.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that people and the Land — and that it has no connection to the rest of Vanuatu's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Vanuatu's own course, in its own order.
