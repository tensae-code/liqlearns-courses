# People and the Sea — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) For Pacific people, who we are is bound up with the ocean: we owe the ocean and our ancestors care, and we hold it for the generations that come after us.  b) it has nothing to do with how Vanuatu governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) For Pacific people, who we are is bound up with the ocean: we owe the ocean and our ancestors care, and we hold it for the generations that come after us.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
