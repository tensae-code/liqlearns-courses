# People and the Sea — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** For Pacific people, who we are is bound up with the ocean: we owe the ocean and our ancestors care, and we hold it for the generations that come after us.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that people and the Sea — and that it has no connection to the rest of Vanuatu's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Vanuatu's own course, in its own order.
