# People and the Sea — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** For Pacific people, who we are is bound up with the ocean: we owe the ocean and our ancestors care, and we hold it for the generations that come after us.
