# Our Place and Community — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Pacific readers teach belonging through four ideas — community, exchange community, place and relationships — learned by asking about me, my family and my community, and by taking part with them.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that our Place and Community — and that it has no connection to the rest of Vanuatu's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Vanuatu's own course, in its own order.
