# Our Place and Community

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: our Place and Community. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Vanuatu's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

The Pacific readers teach belonging through four ideas — community, exchange community, place and relationships — learned by asking about me, my family and my community, and by taking part with them.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The Pacific readers teach belonging through four ideas — community, exchange community, place and relationships — learned by asking about me, my family and my community, and by taking part with them. Give one real example from your own life or from the Vanuatu you live in, and name the lesson's key words: our, place. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: The Pacific readers teach belonging through four ideas — community, exchange community, place and relationships — learned by asking about me, my family and my community, and by taking part with them.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
