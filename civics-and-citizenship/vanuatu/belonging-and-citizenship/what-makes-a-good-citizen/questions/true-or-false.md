# What Makes a Good Citizen? — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A good citizen is known by their qualities and conduct — the marks a citizen of Vanuatu carries in everyday life, learned by looking at illustrations and acting them out in class.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that what Makes a Good Citizen — and that it has no connection to the rest of Vanuatu's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Vanuatu's own course, in its own order.
