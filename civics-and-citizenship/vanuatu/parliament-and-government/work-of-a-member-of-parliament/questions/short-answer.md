# The Work of a Member of Parliament — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** An MP's work is to make laws, to elect the Prime Minister, to sit in the electoral college that elects the President, and to carry their constituency's needs to Parliament — and voters can vote an MP out.

**Answer:** An MP's work is to make laws, to elect the Prime Minister, to sit in the electoral college that elects the President, and to carry their constituency's needs to Parliament — and voters can vote an MP out.
