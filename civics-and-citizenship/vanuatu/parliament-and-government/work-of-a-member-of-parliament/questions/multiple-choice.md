# The Work of a Member of Parliament — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) An MP's work is to make laws, to elect the Prime Minister, to sit in the electoral college that elects the President, and to carry their constituency's needs to Parliament — and voters can vote an MP out.  b) it has nothing to do with how Vanuatu governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
