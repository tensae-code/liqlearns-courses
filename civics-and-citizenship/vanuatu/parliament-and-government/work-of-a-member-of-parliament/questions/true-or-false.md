# The Work of a Member of Parliament — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** An MP's work is to make laws, to elect the Prime Minister, to sit in the electoral college that elects the President, and to carry their constituency's needs to Parliament — and voters can vote an MP out.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that the Work of a Member of Parliament — and that it has no connection to the rest of Vanuatu's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Vanuatu's own course, in its own order.
