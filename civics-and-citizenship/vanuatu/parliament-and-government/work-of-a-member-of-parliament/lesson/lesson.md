# The Work of a Member of Parliament

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: the Work of a Member of Parliament. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Vanuatu's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

An MP's work is to make laws, to elect the Prime Minister, to sit in the electoral college that elects the President, and to carry their constituency's needs to Parliament — and voters can vote an MP out.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: An MP's work is to make laws, to elect the Prime Minister, to sit in the electoral college that elects the President, and to carry their constituency's needs to Parliament — and voters can vote an MP out. Give one real example from your own life or from the Vanuatu you live in, and name the lesson's key words: work, of. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
