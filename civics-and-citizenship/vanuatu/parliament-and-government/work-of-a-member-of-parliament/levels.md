# The Work of a Member of Parliament — depth

This lesson is ONE lesson with no sub lessons: it teaches a single thing, whole. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: An MP's work is to make laws, to elect the Prime Minister, to sit in the electoral college that elects the President, and to carry their constituency's needs to Parliament — and voters can vote an MP out. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
