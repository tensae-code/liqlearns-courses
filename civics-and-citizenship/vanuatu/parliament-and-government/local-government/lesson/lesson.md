# Local Government

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: local Government. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Vanuatu's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## Sub lesson 1 — Provinces and municipalities

Six Provincial Councils and three municipalities run local affairs: they are elected, they pass by-laws, chairmen and mayors lead them, and seats are reserved for women.

## Sub lesson 2 — The Malvatumauri — the chiefs' voice

The Malvatumauri National Council of Chiefs advises the government, carrying custom's voice into the state's decisions.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Below Parliament, Vanuatu is governed close to home: six Provincial Councils and three municipalities, elected, making by-laws under chairmen and mayors, with seats reserved for women — and the Malvatumauri National Council of Chiefs advising the government. Give one real example from your own life or from the Vanuatu you live in, and name the lesson's key words: local, government. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
