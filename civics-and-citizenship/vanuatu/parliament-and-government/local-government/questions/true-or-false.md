# Local Government — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Below Parliament, Vanuatu is governed close to home: six Provincial Councils and three municipalities, elected, making by-laws under chairmen and mayors, with seats reserved for women — and the Malvatumauri National Council of Chiefs advising the government.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that local Government — and that it has no connection to the rest of Vanuatu's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Vanuatu's own course, in its own order.
