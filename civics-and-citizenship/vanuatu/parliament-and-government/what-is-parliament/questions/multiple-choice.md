# What Is Parliament? — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Parliament is Vanuatu's law-making house in Port Vila: 52 Members of Parliament from 18 constituencies, elected for a four-year term — and when Parliament is dissolved, an election follows within 60 days under a caretaker government.  b) it has nothing to do with how Vanuatu governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Parliament is Vanuatu's law-making house in Port Vila: 52 Members of Parliament from 18 constituencies, elected for a four-year term — and when Parliament is dissolved, an election follows within 60 days under a caretaker government.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
