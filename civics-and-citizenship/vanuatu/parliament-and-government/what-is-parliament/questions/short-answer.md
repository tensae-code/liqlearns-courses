# What Is Parliament? — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** Parliament is Vanuatu's law-making house in Port Vila: 52 Members of Parliament from 18 constituencies, elected for a four-year term — and when Parliament is dissolved, an election follows within 60 days under a caretaker government.
