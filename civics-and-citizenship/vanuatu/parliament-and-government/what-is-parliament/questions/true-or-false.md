# What Is Parliament? — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Parliament is Vanuatu's law-making house in Port Vila: 52 Members of Parliament from 18 constituencies, elected for a four-year term — and when Parliament is dissolved, an election follows within 60 days under a caretaker government.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that what Is Parliament — and that it has no connection to the rest of Vanuatu's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Vanuatu's own course, in its own order.
