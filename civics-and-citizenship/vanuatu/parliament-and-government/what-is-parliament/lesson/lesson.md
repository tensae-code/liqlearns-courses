# What Is Parliament?

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: what Is Parliament. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Vanuatu's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## Sub lesson 1 — The house and its members

Parliament House stands in Port Vila, and 52 MPs sit in it, each sent by one of Vanuatu's 18 constituencies.

## Sub lesson 2 — Term, dissolution and the caretaker government

An MP's term runs four years; if Parliament is dissolved early, elections are held within 60 days while a caretaker government keeps the country running.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Parliament is Vanuatu's law-making house in Port Vila: 52 Members of Parliament from 18 constituencies, elected for a four-year term — and when Parliament is dissolved, an election follows within 60 days under a caretaker government. Give one real example from your own life or from the Vanuatu you live in, and name the lesson's key words: what, is. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
