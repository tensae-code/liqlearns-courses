# Our System of Government

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: our System of Government. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Vanuatu's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Vanuatu's whole system of government as one picture: the arms of the state, Parliament and the councils, at local, provincial and national level.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Vanuatu's whole system of government as one picture: the arms of the state, Parliament and the councils, at local, provincial and national level. Give one real example from your own life or from the Vanuatu you live in, and name the lesson's key words: our, system. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
