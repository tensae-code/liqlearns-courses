# Our System of Government — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Vanuatu's whole system of government as one picture: the arms of the state, Parliament and the councils, at local, provincial and national level.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that our System of Government — and that it has no connection to the rest of Vanuatu's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Vanuatu's own course, in its own order.
