# What Makes a Good Leader? — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** A good leader is judged by their qualities — and citizens learn to judge them, because the representatives they choose will carry these qualities, or fail to, in public life.
