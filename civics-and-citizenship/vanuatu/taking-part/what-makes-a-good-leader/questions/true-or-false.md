# What Makes a Good Leader? — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A good leader is judged by their qualities — and citizens learn to judge them, because the representatives they choose will carry these qualities, or fail to, in public life.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that what Makes a Good Leader — and that it has no connection to the rest of Vanuatu's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Vanuatu's own course, in its own order.
