# Why I Vote

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: why I Vote. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Vanuatu's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Seven real Ni-Vanuatu citizens say in their own words why they vote — for a voice, for representation, for services, and for the future — and their testimonies stand as this course's closing case studies on voting.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Seven real Ni-Vanuatu citizens say in their own words why they vote — for a voice, for representation, for services, and for the future — and their testimonies stand as this course's closing case studies on voting. Give one real example from your own life or from the Vanuatu you live in, and name the lesson's key words: why, i. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
