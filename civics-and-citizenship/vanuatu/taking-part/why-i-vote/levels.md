# Why I Vote — depth

This lesson is ONE lesson with no sub lessons: it teaches a single thing, whole. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: Seven real Ni-Vanuatu citizens say in their own words why they vote — for a voice, for representation, for services, and for the future — and their testimonies stand as this course's closing case studies on voting. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
