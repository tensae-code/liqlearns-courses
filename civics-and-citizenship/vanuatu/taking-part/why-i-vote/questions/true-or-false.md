# Why I Vote — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Seven real Ni-Vanuatu citizens say in their own words why they vote — for a voice, for representation, for services, and for the future — and their testimonies stand as this course's closing case studies on voting.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that why I Vote — and that it has no connection to the rest of Vanuatu's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Vanuatu's own course, in its own order.
