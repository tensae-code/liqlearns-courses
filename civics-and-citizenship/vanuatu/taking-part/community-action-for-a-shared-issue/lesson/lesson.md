# Community Action for a Shared Issue

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: community Action for a Shared Issue. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Vanuatu's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## Sub lesson 1 — Name the issue and decide together

The class identifies a sustainability issue in its own community and works through it together on a decision-making grid.

## Sub lesson 2 — Plan, act and reflect

Then the class plans collaborative action, carries it out, and reflects on what it did — action learning, the Pacific readers' way.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: A class can act as citizens on a real issue: name a sustainability issue in your own community, decide together with a decision-making grid, plan collaborative action, act, and reflect on what the action did. Give one real example from your own life or from the Vanuatu you live in, and name the lesson's key words: community, action. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: A class can act as citizens on a real issue: name a sustainability issue in your own community, decide together with a decision-making grid, plan collaborative action, act, and reflect on what the action did.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
