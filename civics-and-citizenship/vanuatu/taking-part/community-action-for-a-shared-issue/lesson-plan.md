# Lesson plan — Community Action for a Shared Issue

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Vanuatu · **Title:** Taking Part · **Lesson:** Community Action for a Shared Issue
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Elections, Voting and Watching Government (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** A class can act as citizens on a real issue: name a sustainability issue in your own community, decide together with a decision-making grid, plan collaborative action, act, and reflect on what the action did. This lesson stands on a teacher guide alone — evidence only, labelled as such.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Name the issue and decide together** — The class identifies a sustainability issue in its own community and works through it together on a decision-making grid.
2. **Plan, act and reflect** — Then the class plans collaborative action, carries it out, and reflects on what it did — action learning, the Pacific readers' way.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
