**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Transparency means government works in the open; accountability means it must answer for what it does — and civil society does the checking, on schools, transport, health and jobs, so democracy is exercised by both sides.  b) it has nothing to do with how Vanuatu governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
