# Transparency and Accountability — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Transparency means government works in the open; accountability means it must answer for what it does — and civil society does the checking, on schools, transport, health and jobs, so democracy is exercised by both sides.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that transparency and Accountability — and that it has no connection to the rest of Vanuatu's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Vanuatu's own course, in its own order.
