# Transparency and Accountability — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** Transparency means government works in the open; accountability means it must answer for what it does — and civil society does the checking, on schools, transport, health and jobs, so democracy is exercised by both sides.
