# Transparency and Accountability

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: transparency and Accountability. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Vanuatu's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## Sub lesson 1 — What the two words mean

Transparency: decisions and spending can be seen. Accountability: those who decide must explain themselves and face the result.

## Sub lesson 2 — Civil society does the checking

Citizens acting together check the government on the things daily life depends on — schools, transport, health and jobs — because democracy is exercised by both sides, not by government alone.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Transparency means government works in the open; accountability means it must answer for what it does — and civil society does the checking, on schools, transport, health and jobs, so democracy is exercised by both sides. Give one real example from your own life or from the Vanuatu you live in, and name the lesson's key words: transparency, and. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
