# The Active Citizen at School

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: the Active Citizen at School. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Vanuatu's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Citizenship is practised where a learner already lives: the French stream's guide teaches being an active citizen inside one's own school (être un citoyen actif au sein de mon école).

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Citizenship is practised where a learner already lives: the French stream's guide teaches being an active citizen inside one's own school (être un citoyen actif au sein de mon école). Give one real example from your own life or from the Vanuatu you live in, and name the lesson's key words: the, active. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
