# The Active Citizen at School — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Citizenship is practised where a learner already lives: the French stream's guide teaches being an active citizen inside one's own school (être un citoyen actif au sein de mon école).
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that the Active Citizen at School — and that it has no connection to the rest of Vanuatu's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Vanuatu's own course, in its own order.
