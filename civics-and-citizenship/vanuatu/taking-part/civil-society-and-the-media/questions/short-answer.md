# Civil Society and the Media — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** Between the citizen and the government stand civil society — NGOs, churches, women's, youth and disability groups and business — and the media, which watch the government and inform citizens; both must be read with care, because misinformation and disinformation travel the same channels.
