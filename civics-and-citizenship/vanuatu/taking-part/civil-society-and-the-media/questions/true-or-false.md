# Civil Society and the Media — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Between the citizen and the government stand civil society — NGOs, churches, women's, youth and disability groups and business — and the media, which watch the government and inform citizens; both must be read with care, because misinformation and disinformation travel the same channels.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that civil Society and the Media — and that it has no connection to the rest of Vanuatu's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Vanuatu's own course, in its own order.
