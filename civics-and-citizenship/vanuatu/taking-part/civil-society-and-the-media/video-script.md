# Video script — Civil Society and the Media

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

This lesson is about one thing.
Between the citizen and the government stand civil society — NGOs, churches, women's, youth and disability groups and business — and the media, which watch the government and inform citizens; both must be read with care, because misinformation and disinformation travel the same channels.
