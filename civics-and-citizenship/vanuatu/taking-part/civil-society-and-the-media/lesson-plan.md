# Lesson plan — Civil Society and the Media

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Vanuatu · **Title:** Taking Part · **Lesson:** Civil Society and the Media
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Elections, Voting and Watching Government (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Between the citizen and the government stand civil society — NGOs, churches, women's, youth and disability groups and business — and the media, which watch the government and inform citizens; both must be read with care, because misinformation and disinformation travel the same channels. This lesson stands on a student book, with teacher-guide parts labelled (TEACHER) in its sources.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Civil society** — Civil society is the organised life of citizens outside government — NGOs, churches, women's, youth and disability groups, business — and it holds its own place in Vanuatu's democracy.
2. **The media — and telling true from false** — The media watch government and inform citizens, social media included; the citizen's skill is telling misinformation and disinformation from reliable sources, and checking information before passing it on.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
