# The Class Election

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: the Class Election. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Vanuatu's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## Sub lesson 1 — Nominations and campaigns

The class election opens with nominations and campaign posters: candidates stand, and say why they should be chosen.

## Sub lesson 2 — Polling and counting

Then the class votes — polling — and counts the votes, and the result is declared, as in a real election.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The course ends by doing the thing itself: the class runs a full mock election — nominations, campaign posters, polling and counting — and practises democracy by running one. Give one real example from your own life or from the Vanuatu you live in, and name the lesson's key words: the, class. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
