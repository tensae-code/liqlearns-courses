# Your Vote Is Your Voice — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** Every citizen of Vanuatu aged 18 or over can vote — a right and a responsibility: register on the Electoral List when you turn 18 (registration closes six weeks before an election), carry your National ID Card, and vote once, in secret behind the voting screen, at your assigned polling station.
