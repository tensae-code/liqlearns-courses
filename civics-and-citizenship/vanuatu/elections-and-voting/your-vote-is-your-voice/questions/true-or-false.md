# Your Vote Is Your Voice — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Every citizen of Vanuatu aged 18 or over can vote — a right and a responsibility: register on the Electoral List when you turn 18 (registration closes six weeks before an election), carry your National ID Card, and vote once, in secret behind the voting screen, at your assigned polling station.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that your Vote Is Your Voice — and that it has no connection to the rest of Vanuatu's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Vanuatu's own course, in its own order.
