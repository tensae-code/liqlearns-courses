# Video script — Your Vote Is Your Voice

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

This lesson is about one thing.
Every citizen of Vanuatu aged 18 or over can vote — a right and a responsibility: register on the Electoral List when you turn 18 (registration closes six weeks before an election), carry your National ID Card, and vote once, in secret behind the voting screen, at your assigned polling station.
