# Your Vote Is Your Voice

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: your Vote Is Your Voice. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Vanuatu's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## Sub lesson 1 — Voting is your right and your responsibility

Every citizen 18 or over can vote: it is a right no one may take away and a responsibility a citizen takes up — starting with registering on the Electoral List and holding a National ID Card.

## Sub lesson 2 — Your vote, step by step

Register when you turn 18 — registration closes six weeks before an election — then vote once, at your assigned polling station, marking your ballot in secret behind the voting screen.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Every citizen of Vanuatu aged 18 or over can vote — a right and a responsibility: register on the Electoral List when you turn 18 (registration closes six weeks before an election), carry your National ID Card, and vote once, in secret behind the voting screen, at your assigned polling station. Give one real example from your own life or from the Vanuatu you live in, and name the lesson's key words: your, vote. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
