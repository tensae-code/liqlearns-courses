# Lesson plan — What Is an Election?

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Vanuatu · **Title:** Elections and Voting · **Lesson:** What Is an Election?
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Elections, Voting and Watching Government (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** An election is how citizens choose their representatives — national, provincial and municipal elections, and referendums — run under the Representation of the People Act by the Electoral Commission and the Electoral Office. ## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Choosing representatives** — An election is the citizens' act of choosing who represents them, at national, provincial and municipal level — and a referendum asks the people a question directly.
2. **Who runs Vanuatu's elections** — Elections run under the Representation of the People Act; the Electoral Commission and the Electoral Office organise them, as the course's worked election-day example shows.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
