# What Is an Election?

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: what Is an Election. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Vanuatu's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## Sub lesson 1 — Choosing representatives

An election is the citizens' act of choosing who represents them, at national, provincial and municipal level — and a referendum asks the people a question directly.

## Sub lesson 2 — Who runs Vanuatu's elections

Elections run under the Representation of the People Act; the Electoral Commission and the Electoral Office organise them, as the course's worked election-day example shows.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: An election is how citizens choose their representatives — national, provincial and municipal elections, and referendums — run under the Representation of the People Act by the Electoral Commission and the Electoral Office. Give one real example from your own life or from the Vanuatu you live in, and name the lesson's key words: what, is. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
