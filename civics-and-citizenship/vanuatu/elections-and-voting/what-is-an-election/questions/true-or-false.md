# What Is an Election? — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** An election is how citizens choose their representatives — national, provincial and municipal elections, and referendums — run under the Representation of the People Act by the Electoral Commission and the Electoral Office.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that what Is an Election — and that it has no connection to the rest of Vanuatu's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Vanuatu's own course, in its own order.
