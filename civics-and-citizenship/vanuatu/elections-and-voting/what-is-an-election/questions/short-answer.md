# What Is an Election? — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** An election is how citizens choose their representatives — national, provincial and municipal elections, and referendums — run under the Representation of the People Act by the Electoral Commission and the Electoral Office.
