# What Is an Election? — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) An election is how citizens choose their representatives — national, provincial and municipal elections, and referendums — run under the Representation of the People Act by the Electoral Commission and the Electoral Office.  b) it has nothing to do with how Vanuatu governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) An election is how citizens choose their representatives — national, provincial and municipal elections, and referendums — run under the Representation of the People Act by the Electoral Commission and the Electoral Office.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
