# Candidates, Programmes and Campaigns — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** To stand for Parliament a candidate must be 25 (21 for local councils); each candidate offers a programme, campaigns for 14 days — the campaign ends 48 hours before election day — and then the votes are counted and the winner declared.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that candidates, Programmes and Campaigns — and that it has no connection to the rest of Vanuatu's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Vanuatu's own course, in its own order.
