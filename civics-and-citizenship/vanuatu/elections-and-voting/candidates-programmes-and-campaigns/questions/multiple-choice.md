**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) To stand for Parliament a candidate must be 25 (21 for local councils); each candidate offers a programme, campaigns for 14 days — the campaign ends 48 hours before election day — and then the votes are counted and the winner declared.  b) it has nothing to do with how Vanuatu governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) To stand for Parliament a candidate must be 25 (21 for local councils); each candidate offers a programme, campaigns for 14 days — the campaign ends 48 hours before election day — and then the votes are counted and the winner declared.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
