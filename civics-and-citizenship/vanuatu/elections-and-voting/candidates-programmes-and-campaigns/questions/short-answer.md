# Candidates, Programmes and Campaigns — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** To stand for Parliament a candidate must be 25 (21 for local councils); each candidate offers a programme, campaigns for 14 days — the campaign ends 48 hours before election day — and then the votes are counted and the winner declared.
