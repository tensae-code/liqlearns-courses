# Candidates, Programmes and Campaigns

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: candidates, Programmes and Campaigns. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Vanuatu's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## Sub lesson 1 — Who can stand, and what they offer

A candidate for Parliament must be at least 25, and 21 for a local council; what a candidate offers the voters is their programme.

## Sub lesson 2 — The campaign, the count and the winner

Campaigning runs 14 days and ends 48 hours before election day; after the vote comes the counting, and the candidate with the votes wins.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: To stand for Parliament a candidate must be 25 (21 for local councils); each candidate offers a programme, campaigns for 14 days — the campaign ends 48 hours before election day — and then the votes are counted and the winner declared. Give one real example from your own life or from the Vanuatu you live in, and name the lesson's key words: candidates, programmes. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
