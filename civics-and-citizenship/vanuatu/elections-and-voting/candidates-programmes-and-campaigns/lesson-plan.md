# Lesson plan — Candidates, Programmes and Campaigns

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Vanuatu · **Title:** Elections and Voting · **Lesson:** Candidates, Programmes and Campaigns
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Fair Elections, Inclusion and Leading (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** To stand for Parliament a candidate must be 25 (21 for local councils); each candidate offers a programme, campaigns for 14 days — the campaign ends 48 hours before election day — and then the votes are counted and the winner declared. This lesson stands on a student book.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Who can stand, and what they offer** — A candidate for Parliament must be at least 25, and 21 for a local council; what a candidate offers the voters is their programme.
2. **The campaign, the count and the winner** — Campaigning runs 14 days and ends 48 hours before election day; after the vote comes the counting, and the candidate with the votes wins.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
