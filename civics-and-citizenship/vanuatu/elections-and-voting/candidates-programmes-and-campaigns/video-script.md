# Video script — Candidates, Programmes and Campaigns

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

This lesson is about one thing.
To stand for Parliament a candidate must be 25 (21 for local councils); each candidate offers a programme, campaigns for 14 days — the campaign ends 48 hours before election day — and then the votes are counted and the winner declared.
