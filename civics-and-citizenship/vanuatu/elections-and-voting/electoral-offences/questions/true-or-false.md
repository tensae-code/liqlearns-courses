# Electoral Offences — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Elections stay fair because breaking their rules is an offence: double voting, impersonation, bribery, treating, false statements, destroying ballot papers and breaking the ballot's secrecy are punished by fines or imprisonment — and a disputed election goes by petition to the Supreme Court within 21 days.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that electoral Offences — and that it has no connection to the rest of Vanuatu's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Vanuatu's own course, in its own order.
