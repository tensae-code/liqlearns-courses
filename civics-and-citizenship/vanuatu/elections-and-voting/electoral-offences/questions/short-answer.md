# Electoral Offences — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** Elections stay fair because breaking their rules is an offence: double voting, impersonation, bribery, treating, false statements, destroying ballot papers and breaking the ballot's secrecy are punished by fines or imprisonment — and a disputed election goes by petition to the Supreme Court within 21 days.
