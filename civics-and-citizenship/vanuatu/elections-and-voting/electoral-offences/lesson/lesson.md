# Electoral Offences

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: electoral Offences. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Vanuatu's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## Sub lesson 1 — The offences

The main electoral offences: voting twice, impersonating another voter, bribery, treating, false statements, destroying ballot papers, and breaking the secrecy of the ballot.

## Sub lesson 2 — What happens — and why voting still matters

Offences are punished with fines or imprisonment; complaints go through the courts, and an election petition reaches the Supreme Court within 21 days — the rules protect the vote, which is why voting matters.
