# Three Arms of Government

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: three Arms of Government. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Vanuatu's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## Sub lesson 1 — Independence and the people's sovereignty

On 30 July 1980 Vanuatu became independent, and national sovereignty — the final say — belongs to the people of Vanuatu.

## Sub lesson 2 — The three independent arms

Government is split three ways so no one part rules alone: the Legislature makes law, the Judiciary judges, and the Executive governs day to day.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Vanuatu became independent on 30 July 1980; sovereignty belongs to its people, and the state works through three independent arms — the Legislature, the Judiciary and the Executive. Give one real example from your own life or from the Vanuatu you live in, and name the lesson's key words: three, arms. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
