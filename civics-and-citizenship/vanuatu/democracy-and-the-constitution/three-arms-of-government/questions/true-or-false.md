# Three Arms of Government — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Vanuatu became independent on 30 July 1980; sovereignty belongs to its people, and the state works through three independent arms — the Legislature, the Judiciary and the Executive.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that three Arms of Government — and that it has no connection to the rest of Vanuatu's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Vanuatu's own course, in its own order.
