# What Is Democracy?

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: what Is Democracy. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Vanuatu's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## Sub lesson 1 — Rule by the people

Democracy means the people rule: Lincoln's words name it — government of the people, by the people, for the people.

## Sub lesson 2 — Vanuatu's representative democracy

Vanuatu's people rule through the representatives they choose, and the Constitution stands above all other law, guaranteeing rights and freedoms.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Democracy is rule by the people — of the people, by the people, for the people — and Vanuatu is a representative democracy, where the Constitution stands as supreme law guaranteeing rights and freedoms. Give one real example from your own life or from the Vanuatu you live in, and name the lesson's key words: what, is. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
