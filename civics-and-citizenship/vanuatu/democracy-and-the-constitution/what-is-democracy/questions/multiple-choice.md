# What Is Democracy? — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Democracy is rule by the people — of the people, by the people, for the people — and Vanuatu is a representative democracy, where the Constitution stands as supreme law guaranteeing rights and freedoms.  b) it has nothing to do with how Vanuatu governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Democracy is rule by the people — of the people, by the people, for the people — and Vanuatu is a representative democracy, where the Constitution stands as supreme law guaranteeing rights and freedoms.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
