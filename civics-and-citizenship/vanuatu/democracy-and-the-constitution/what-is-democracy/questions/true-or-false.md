# What Is Democracy? — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Democracy is rule by the people — of the people, by the people, for the people — and Vanuatu is a representative democracy, where the Constitution stands as supreme law guaranteeing rights and freedoms.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that what Is Democracy — and that it has no connection to the rest of Vanuatu's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Vanuatu's own course, in its own order.
