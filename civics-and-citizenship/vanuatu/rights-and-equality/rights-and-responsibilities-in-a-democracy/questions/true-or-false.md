# Rights and Responsibilities in a Democracy — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** In a democracy the two travel together: citizens hold rights, and the same citizens carry responsibilities that go with them.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that rights and Responsibilities in a Democracy — and that it has no connection to the rest of Vanuatu's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Vanuatu's own course, in its own order.
