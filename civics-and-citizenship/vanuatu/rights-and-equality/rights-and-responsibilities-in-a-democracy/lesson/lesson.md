# Rights and Responsibilities in a Democracy

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: rights and Responsibilities in a Democracy. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Vanuatu's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## Sub lesson 1 — The rights citizens hold

The rights a citizen of Vanuatu holds in its democracy — the freedoms and protections that are theirs as citizens.

## Sub lesson 2 — The responsibilities that go with them

Rights are matched by responsibilities: the duties a citizen carries toward the country and toward other citizens.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: In a democracy the two travel together: citizens hold rights, and the same citizens carry responsibilities that go with them. Give one real example from your own life or from the Vanuatu you live in, and name the lesson's key words: rights, and. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
