# Including Everyone in a Democracy

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: including Everyone in a Democracy. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Vanuatu's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## Sub lesson 1 — Equality, equity and including everyone

Equality gives everyone the same standing; equity — fairness — gives each what they need to take part; inclusion brings everyone into society's life.

## Sub lesson 2 — Inclusion as a working part of democracy

Women, youth, people with disabilities and minorities are not guests in a democracy: including them is part of how it works, every day.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: A democracy is not finished while anyone is left out: women, youth, people with disabilities and minorities are a working part of it — equality, equity (fairness) and social inclusion, in the French stream's words, égalité, équité et inclusion sociale. Give one real example from your own life or from the Vanuatu you live in, and name the lesson's key words: including, everyone. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
