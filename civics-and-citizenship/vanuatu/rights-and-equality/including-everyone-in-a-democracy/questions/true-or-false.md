# Including Everyone in a Democracy — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A democracy is not finished while anyone is left out: women, youth, people with disabilities and minorities are a working part of it — equality, equity (fairness) and social inclusion, in the French stream's words, égalité, équité et inclusion sociale.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that including Everyone in a Democracy — and that it has no connection to the rest of Vanuatu's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Vanuatu's own course, in its own order.
