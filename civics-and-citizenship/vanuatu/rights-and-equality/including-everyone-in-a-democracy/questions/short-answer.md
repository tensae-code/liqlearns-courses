# Including Everyone in a Democracy — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** A democracy is not finished while anyone is left out: women, youth, people with disabilities and minorities are a working part of it — equality, equity (fairness) and social inclusion, in the French stream's words, égalité, équité et inclusion sociale.
