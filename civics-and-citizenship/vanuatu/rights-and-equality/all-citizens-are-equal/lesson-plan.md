# Lesson plan — All Citizens Are Equal

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Vanuatu · **Title:** Rights and Equality · **Lesson:** All Citizens Are Equal
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — The Citizen and Belonging (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The Constitution gives every citizen the same fundamental rights without discrimination — women, youth, citizens with disabilities and rural citizens stand equal — and citizens help one another take part, by helping others register and vote and by proxy voting. This lesson stands on a student book.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Equal rights without discrimination** — The Constitution's fundamental rights belong to all citizens alike, without discrimination — equality of women, youth, disabled and rural citizens included.
2. **Helping everyone take part** — Equality is practised: citizens help others register and vote, and proxy voting lets a citizen who cannot attend still be counted.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
