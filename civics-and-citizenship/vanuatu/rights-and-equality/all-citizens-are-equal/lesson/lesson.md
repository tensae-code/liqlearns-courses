# All Citizens Are Equal

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: all Citizens Are Equal. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Vanuatu's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## Sub lesson 1 — Equal rights without discrimination

The Constitution's fundamental rights belong to all citizens alike, without discrimination — equality of women, youth, disabled and rural citizens included.

## Sub lesson 2 — Helping everyone take part

Equality is practised: citizens help others register and vote, and proxy voting lets a citizen who cannot attend still be counted.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The Constitution gives every citizen the same fundamental rights without discrimination — women, youth, citizens with disabilities and rural citizens stand equal — and citizens help one another take part, by helping others register and vote and by proxy voting. Give one real example from your own life or from the Vanuatu you live in, and name the lesson's key words: all, citizens. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
