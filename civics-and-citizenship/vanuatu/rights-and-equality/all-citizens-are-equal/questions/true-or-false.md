# All Citizens Are Equal — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Constitution gives every citizen the same fundamental rights without discrimination — women, youth, citizens with disabilities and rural citizens stand equal — and citizens help one another take part, by helping others register and vote and by proxy voting.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that all Citizens Are Equal — and that it has no connection to the rest of Vanuatu's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Vanuatu's own course, in its own order.
