# All Citizens Are Equal — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** The Constitution gives every citizen the same fundamental rights without discrimination — women, youth, citizens with disabilities and rural citizens stand equal — and citizens help one another take part, by helping others register and vote and by proxy voting.
