# Video script — Rights of Vulnerable People

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Vulnerable people — children and disabled people named first — hold rights that others must protect and promote.

Children are vulnerable rights-holders — their rights are protected and promoted by the adults and the state around them.

Disabled people are vulnerable rights-holders — their rights stand in full, and protecting them is a duty, not a kindness.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
