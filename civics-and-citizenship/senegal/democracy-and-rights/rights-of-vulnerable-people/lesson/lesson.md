# Rights of Vulnerable People

## Do it first

Name one right of a child and one of a disabled person, and say whose duty it is to guard each. This lesson closes the rights climb with the people least able to claim rights alone.

## Sub lesson 1 — Children as rights-holders

Children are vulnerable rights-holders — their rights are protected and promoted by the adults and the state around them.

## Sub lesson 2 — Disabled people as rights-holders

Disabled people are vulnerable rights-holders — their rights stand in full, and protecting them is a duty, not a kindness.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: vulnerable people — children and disabled people named first — hold rights that others must protect and promote. Give one real example from your own life or from the Senegal you live in, and name the lesson's key words: vulnerable people, personnes vulnérables, children, disabled people. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
