# Rights of Vulnerable People — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "Children as rights-holders" teach?
**Answer:** Children are vulnerable rights-holders — their rights are protected and promoted by the adults and the state around them.

**2.** In your own words, what does the part "Disabled people as rights-holders" teach?
**Answer:** Disabled people are vulnerable rights-holders — their rights stand in full, and protecting them is a duty, not a kindness.

**3.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** Vulnerable people — children and disabled people named first — hold rights that others must protect and promote.
