# Rights of Vulnerable People — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — Children as rights-holders?
a) Children are vulnerable rights-holders — their rights are protected and promoted by the adults and the state around them  b) it has nothing to do with how Senegal governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Children are vulnerable rights-holders — their rights are protected and promoted by the adults and the state around them.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — Disabled people as rights-holders?
a) Disabled people are vulnerable rights-holders — their rights stand in full, and protecting them is a duty, not a kindness  b) it has nothing to do with how Senegal governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Disabled people are vulnerable rights-holders — their rights stand in full, and protecting them is a duty, not a kindness.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**3.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) vulnerable people — children and disabled people named first — hold rights that others must protect and promote  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Senegal
**Answer: a) Vulnerable people — children and disabled people named first — hold rights that others must protect and promote.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
