# Rights of Vulnerable People — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Children are vulnerable rights-holders — their rights are protected and promoted by the adults and the state around them.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Disabled people are vulnerable rights-holders — their rights stand in full, and protecting them is a duty, not a kindness.
**Answer: True**

**3.** This lesson teaches that vulnerable people — children and disabled people named first — hold rights that others must protect and promote — and that it has no connection to the rest of Senegal's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Senegal's own course, in its own order.
