# Video script — Women's Rights and Parity

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Women's rights are protected and promoted — and parity, women's equal place in decision-making, is the question Senegal's course puts openly.

Women's rights are protected and promoted — named as rights, not favours.

Parity — women's equal place where decisions are made — and the strategies that promote women's rights are taught openly.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
