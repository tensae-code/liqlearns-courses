# Women's Rights and Parity — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Women's rights are protected and promoted — named as rights, not favours.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Parity — women's equal place where decisions are made — and the strategies that promote women's rights are taught openly.
**Answer: True**

**3.** This lesson teaches that women's rights are protected and promoted — and parity, women's equal place in decision-making, is the question Senegal's course puts openly — and that it has no connection to the rest of Senegal's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Senegal's own course, in its own order.
