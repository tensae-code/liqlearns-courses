# Women's Rights and Parity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — Protecting and promoting women's rights?
a) Women's rights are protected and promoted — named as rights, not favours  b) it has nothing to do with how Senegal governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Women's rights are protected and promoted — named as rights, not favours.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — Parity and how rights advance?
a) Parity — women's equal place where decisions are made — and the strategies that promote women's rights are taught openly  b) it has nothing to do with how Senegal governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Parity — women's equal place where decisions are made — and the strategies that promote women's rights are taught openly.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**3.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) women's rights are protected and promoted — and parity, women's equal place in decision-making, is the question Senegal's course puts openly  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Senegal
**Answer: a) Women's rights are protected and promoted — and parity, women's equal place in decision-making, is the question Senegal's course puts openly.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
