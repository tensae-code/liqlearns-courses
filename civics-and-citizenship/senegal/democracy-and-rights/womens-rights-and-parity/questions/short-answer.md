# Women's Rights and Parity — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "Protecting and promoting women's rights" teach?
**Answer:** Women's rights are protected and promoted — named as rights, not favours.

**2.** In your own words, what does the part "Parity and how rights advance" teach?
**Answer:** Parity — women's equal place where decisions are made — and the strategies that promote women's rights are taught openly.

**3.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** Women's rights are protected and promoted — and parity, women's equal place in decision-making, is the question Senegal's course puts openly.
