# Women's Rights and Parity

## Do it first

Name one place in public life where women's presence has changed in your lifetime, and say what helped it change. This lesson takes human rights to the half of the nation whose rights were longest denied.

## Sub lesson 1 — Protecting and promoting women's rights

Women's rights are protected and promoted — named as rights, not favours.

## Sub lesson 2 — Parity and how rights advance

Parity — women's equal place where decisions are made — and the strategies that promote women's rights are taught openly.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: women's rights are protected and promoted — and parity, women's equal place in decision-making, is the question Senegal's course puts openly. Give one real example from your own life or from the Senegal you live in, and name the lesson's key words: women's rights, droits de la femme, parity, parité. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
