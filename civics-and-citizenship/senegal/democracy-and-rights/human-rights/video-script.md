# Video script — Human Rights

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Human rights belong to every person — they are defined, they have a history, and they stand in founding texts a citizen can name.

Human rights are defined, and their history told — the rights every person holds simply as a person.

Human rights stand in fundamental texts — declarations, pacts, charters and treaties the citizen can name and read.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
