# Human Rights — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "What human rights are, and their history" teach?
**Answer:** Human rights are defined, and their history told — the rights every person holds simply as a person.

**2.** In your own words, what does the part "The founding texts" teach?
**Answer:** Human rights stand in fundamental texts — declarations, pacts, charters and treaties the citizen can name and read.

**3.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** Human rights belong to every person — they are defined, they have a history, and they stand in founding texts a citizen can name.
