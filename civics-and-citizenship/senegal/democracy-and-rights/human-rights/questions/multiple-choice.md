# Human Rights — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — What human rights are, and their history?
a) Human rights are defined, and their history told — the rights every person holds simply as a person  b) it has nothing to do with how Senegal governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Human rights are defined, and their history told — the rights every person holds simply as a person.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — The founding texts?
a) Human rights stand in fundamental texts — declarations, pacts, charters and treaties the citizen can name and read  b) it has nothing to do with how Senegal governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Human rights stand in fundamental texts — declarations, pacts, charters and treaties the citizen can name and read.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**3.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) human rights belong to every person — they are defined, they have a history, and they stand in founding texts a citizen can name  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Senegal
**Answer: a) Human rights belong to every person — they are defined, they have a history, and they stand in founding texts a citizen can name.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
