# Human Rights — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Human rights are defined, and their history told — the rights every person holds simply as a person.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Human rights stand in fundamental texts — declarations, pacts, charters and treaties the citizen can name and read.
**Answer: True**

**3.** This lesson teaches that human rights belong to every person — they are defined, they have a history, and they stand in founding texts a citizen can name — and that it has no connection to the rest of Senegal's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Senegal's own course, in its own order.
