# Rule of Law and Good Governance

## Do it first

Name one rule that binds even the powerful in Senegal, and say what would change if it vanished. This lesson opens the democracy-and-rights title at Level 3.

## Sub lesson 1 — The rule of law

In a state of law, power itself is held to the law — no one stands above the rules that bind everyone.

## Sub lesson 2 — Good governance

Good governance is the state's affairs run well — the companion foundation of a democratic state.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: a state of law (État de droit) and good governance are the foundations democracy stands on — power held to rules, and public affairs run well. Give one real example from your own life or from the Senegal you live in, and name the lesson's key words: rule of law, État de droit, good governance, bonne gouvernance. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
