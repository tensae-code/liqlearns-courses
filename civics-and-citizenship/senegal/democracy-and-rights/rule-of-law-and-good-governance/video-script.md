# Video script — Rule of Law and Good Governance

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A state of law (État de droit) and good governance are the foundations democracy stands on — power held to rules, and public affairs run well.

In a state of law, power itself is held to the law — no one stands above the rules that bind everyone.

Good governance is the state's affairs run well — the companion foundation of a democratic state.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
