# Rule of Law and Good Governance — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** In a state of law, power itself is held to the law — no one stands above the rules that bind everyone.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Good governance is the state's affairs run well — the companion foundation of a democratic state.
**Answer: True**

**3.** This lesson teaches that a state of law (État de droit) and good governance are the foundations democracy stands on — power held to rules, and public affairs run well — and that it has no connection to the rest of Senegal's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Senegal's own course, in its own order.
