# Rule of Law and Good Governance — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — The rule of law?
a) In a state of law, power itself is held to the law — no one stands above the rules that bind everyone  b) it has nothing to do with how Senegal governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) In a state of law, power itself is held to the law — no one stands above the rules that bind everyone.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — Good governance?
a) Good governance is the state's affairs run well — the companion foundation of a democratic state  b) it has nothing to do with how Senegal governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Good governance is the state's affairs run well — the companion foundation of a democratic state.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**3.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) a state of law (État de droit) and good governance are the foundations democracy stands on — power held to rules, and public affairs run well  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Senegal
**Answer: a) A state of law (État de droit) and good governance are the foundations democracy stands on — power held to rules, and public affairs run well.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
