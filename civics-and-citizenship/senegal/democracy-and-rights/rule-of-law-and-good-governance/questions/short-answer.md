# Rule of Law and Good Governance — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "The rule of law" teach?
**Answer:** In a state of law, power itself is held to the law — no one stands above the rules that bind everyone.

**2.** In your own words, what does the part "Good governance" teach?
**Answer:** Good governance is the state's affairs run well — the companion foundation of a democratic state.

**3.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** A state of law (État de droit) and good governance are the foundations democracy stands on — power held to rules, and public affairs run well.
