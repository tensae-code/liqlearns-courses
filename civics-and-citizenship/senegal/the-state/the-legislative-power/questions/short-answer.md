# The Legislative Power — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "The legislature's structures" teach?
**Answer:** The legislative power has structures — the assembly and the bodies that carry law-making.

**2.** In your own words, what does the part "How the legislature functions" teach?
**Answer:** The legislature functions by debating and making the laws the executive then carries out.

**3.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** The legislative power is the power that makes the laws — with structures and a functioning of its own.
