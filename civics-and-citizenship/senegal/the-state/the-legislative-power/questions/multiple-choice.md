# The Legislative Power — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — The legislature's structures?
a) The legislative power has structures — the assembly and the bodies that carry law-making  b) it has nothing to do with how Senegal governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The legislative power has structures — the assembly and the bodies that carry law-making.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — How the legislature functions?
a) The legislature functions by debating and making the laws the executive then carries out  b) it has nothing to do with how Senegal governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The legislature functions by debating and making the laws the executive then carries out.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**3.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) the legislative power is the power that makes the laws — with structures and a functioning of its own  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Senegal
**Answer: a) The legislative power is the power that makes the laws — with structures and a functioning of its own.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
