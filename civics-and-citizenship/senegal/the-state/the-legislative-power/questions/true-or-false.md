# The Legislative Power — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The legislative power has structures — the assembly and the bodies that carry law-making.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The legislature functions by debating and making the laws the executive then carries out.
**Answer: True**

**3.** This lesson teaches that the legislative power is the power that makes the laws — with structures and a functioning of its own — and that it has no connection to the rest of Senegal's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Senegal's own course, in its own order.
