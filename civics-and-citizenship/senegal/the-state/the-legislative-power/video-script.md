# Video script — The Legislative Power

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The legislative power is the power that makes the laws — with structures and a functioning of its own.

The legislative power has structures — the assembly and the bodies that carry law-making.

The legislature functions by debating and making the laws the executive then carries out.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
