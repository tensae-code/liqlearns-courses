# The Legislative Power

## Do it first

Name the body that makes Senegal's laws, and one law whose making you have heard discussed. This lesson takes the second of the state's three powers.

## Sub lesson 1 — The legislature's structures

The legislative power has structures — the assembly and the bodies that carry law-making.

## Sub lesson 2 — How the legislature functions

The legislature functions by debating and making the laws the executive then carries out.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the legislative power is the power that makes the laws — with structures and a functioning of its own. Give one real example from your own life or from the Senegal you live in, and name the lesson's key words: legislative power, pouvoir législatif, laws. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
