# The State — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — What the state is, and its types?
a) The state is defined, and its types distinguished — what kind of organised power it is  b) it has nothing to do with how Senegal governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The state is defined, and its types distinguished — what kind of organised power it is.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — The state's role and functions?
a) The state has a role and functions — the work it exists to do for the whole country  b) it has nothing to do with how Senegal governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The state has a role and functions — the work it exists to do for the whole country.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**3.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) the state is the organised political power of a country — it has types, a role and functions a citizen can name  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Senegal
**Answer: a) The state is the organised political power of a country — it has types, a role and functions a citizen can name.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
