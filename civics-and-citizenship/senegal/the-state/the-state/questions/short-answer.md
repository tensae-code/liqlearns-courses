# The State — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "What the state is, and its types" teach?
**Answer:** The state is defined, and its types distinguished — what kind of organised power it is.

**2.** In your own words, what does the part "The state's role and functions" teach?
**Answer:** The state has a role and functions — the work it exists to do for the whole country.

**3.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** The state is the organised political power of a country — it has types, a role and functions a citizen can name.
