# The State

## Do it first

Name three things only the state can do in Senegal, and say why a family or a quarter cannot do them. This lesson names the machinery the next three lessons take apart, power by power.

## Sub lesson 1 — What the state is, and its types

The state is defined, and its types distinguished — what kind of organised power it is.

## Sub lesson 2 — The state's role and functions

The state has a role and functions — the work it exists to do for the whole country.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the state is the organised political power of a country — it has types, a role and functions a citizen can name. Give one real example from your own life or from the Senegal you live in, and name the lesson's key words: state, État, definition, types, role, functions. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
