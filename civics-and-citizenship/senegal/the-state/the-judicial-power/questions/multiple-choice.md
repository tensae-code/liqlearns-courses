# The Judicial Power — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — The judiciary's structures?
a) The judicial power has structures — the courts and the bodies that carry judging  b) it has nothing to do with how Senegal governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The judicial power has structures — the courts and the bodies that carry judging.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — How the judiciary functions?
a) The judiciary functions by judging cases under the law, apart from the powers that make and carry out the laws  b) it has nothing to do with how Senegal governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The judiciary functions by judging cases under the law, apart from the powers that make and carry out the laws.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**3.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) the judicial power is the power that judges — the courts' structures and functioning guard the law itself  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Senegal
**Answer: a) The judicial power is the power that judges — the courts' structures and functioning guard the law itself.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
