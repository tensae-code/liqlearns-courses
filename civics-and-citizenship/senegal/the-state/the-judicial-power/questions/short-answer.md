# The Judicial Power — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "The judiciary's structures" teach?
**Answer:** The judicial power has structures — the courts and the bodies that carry judging.

**2.** In your own words, what does the part "How the judiciary functions" teach?
**Answer:** The judiciary functions by judging cases under the law, apart from the powers that make and carry out the laws.

**3.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** The judicial power is the power that judges — the courts' structures and functioning guard the law itself.
