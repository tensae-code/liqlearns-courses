# The Judicial Power — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The judicial power has structures — the courts and the bodies that carry judging.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The judiciary functions by judging cases under the law, apart from the powers that make and carry out the laws.
**Answer: True**

**3.** This lesson teaches that the judicial power is the power that judges — the courts' structures and functioning guard the law itself — and that it has no connection to the rest of Senegal's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Senegal's own course, in its own order.
