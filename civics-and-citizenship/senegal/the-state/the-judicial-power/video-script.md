# Video script — The Judicial Power

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The judicial power is the power that judges — the courts' structures and functioning guard the law itself.

The judicial power has structures — the courts and the bodies that carry judging.

The judiciary functions by judging cases under the law, apart from the powers that make and carry out the laws.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
