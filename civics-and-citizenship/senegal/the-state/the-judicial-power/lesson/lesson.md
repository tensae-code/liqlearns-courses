# The Judicial Power

## Do it first

Name a court in Senegal, and one kind of case that belongs in front of it. This lesson completes the state's three powers.

## Sub lesson 1 — The judiciary's structures

The judicial power has structures — the courts and the bodies that carry judging.

## Sub lesson 2 — How the judiciary functions

The judiciary functions by judging cases under the law, apart from the powers that make and carry out the laws.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the judicial power is the power that judges — the courts' structures and functioning guard the law itself. Give one real example from your own life or from the Senegal you live in, and name the lesson's key words: judicial power, pouvoir judiciaire, courts, justice. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
