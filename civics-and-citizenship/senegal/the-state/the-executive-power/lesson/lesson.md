# The Executive Power

## Do it first

Name who heads the executive in Senegal, and one act of government you have seen its work produce. This lesson takes the first of the state's three powers.

## Sub lesson 1 — The executive's structures

The executive power has structures — the offices and bodies that carry it.

## Sub lesson 2 — How the executive functions

The executive functions by carrying out decisions and running the state day by day.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the executive power is the power that carries out the laws and runs the state — with structures a citizen can name and a functioning a citizen can follow. Give one real example from your own life or from the Senegal you live in, and name the lesson's key words: executive power, pouvoir exécutif, structures, functioning. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
