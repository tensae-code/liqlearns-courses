# The Executive Power — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "The executive's structures" teach?
**Answer:** The executive power has structures — the offices and bodies that carry it.

**2.** In your own words, what does the part "How the executive functions" teach?
**Answer:** The executive functions by carrying out decisions and running the state day by day.

**3.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** The executive power is the power that carries out the laws and runs the state — with structures a citizen can name and a functioning a citizen can follow.
