# The Executive Power — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — The executive's structures?
a) The executive power has structures — the offices and bodies that carry it  b) it has nothing to do with how Senegal governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The executive power has structures — the offices and bodies that carry it.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — How the executive functions?
a) The executive functions by carrying out decisions and running the state day by day  b) it has nothing to do with how Senegal governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The executive functions by carrying out decisions and running the state day by day.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**3.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) the executive power is the power that carries out the laws and runs the state — with structures a citizen can name and a functioning a citizen can follow  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Senegal
**Answer: a) The executive power is the power that carries out the laws and runs the state — with structures a citizen can name and a functioning a citizen can follow.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
