# The Executive Power — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The executive power has structures — the offices and bodies that carry it.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The executive functions by carrying out decisions and running the state day by day.
**Answer: True**

**3.** This lesson teaches that the executive power is the power that carries out the laws and runs the state — with structures a citizen can name and a functioning a citizen can follow — and that it has no connection to the rest of Senegal's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Senegal's own course, in its own order.
