# Video script — The Executive Power

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The executive power is the power that carries out the laws and runs the state — with structures a citizen can name and a functioning a citizen can follow.

The executive power has structures — the offices and bodies that carry it.

The executive functions by carrying out decisions and running the state day by day.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
