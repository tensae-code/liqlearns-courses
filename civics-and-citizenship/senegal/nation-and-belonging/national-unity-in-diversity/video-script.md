# Video script — National Unity in Diversity

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The Senegalese nation holds unity and diversity together — one nation, many peoples and cultures.

The Senegalese nation is one — its unity is the shared belonging of all its people.

Senegal's unity holds inside ethnic and cultural diversity — difference is carried within the nation, not against it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
