# Hands-on — National Unity in Diversity

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name two cultures or languages of Senegal, and one thing that joins their people as one nation. This lesson holds the two sides of Senegal's nationhood together.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the Senegalese nation holds unity and diversity together — one nation, many peoples and cultures. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
