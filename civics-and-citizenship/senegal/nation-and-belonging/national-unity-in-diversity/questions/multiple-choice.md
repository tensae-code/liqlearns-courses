# National Unity in Diversity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — One nation, in unity?
a) The Senegalese nation is one — its unity is the shared belonging of all its people  b) it has nothing to do with how Senegal governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The Senegalese nation is one — its unity is the shared belonging of all its people.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — One nation, in diversity?
a) Senegal's unity holds inside ethnic and cultural diversity — difference is carried within the nation, not against it  b) it has nothing to do with how Senegal governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Senegal's unity holds inside ethnic and cultural diversity — difference is carried within the nation, not against it.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**3.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) the Senegalese nation holds unity and diversity together — one nation, many peoples and cultures  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Senegal
**Answer: a) The Senegalese nation holds unity and diversity together — one nation, many peoples and cultures.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
