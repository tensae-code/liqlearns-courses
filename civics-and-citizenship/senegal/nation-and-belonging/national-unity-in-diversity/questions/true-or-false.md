# National Unity in Diversity — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Senegalese nation is one — its unity is the shared belonging of all its people.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Senegal's unity holds inside ethnic and cultural diversity — difference is carried within the nation, not against it.
**Answer: True**

**3.** This lesson teaches that the Senegalese nation holds unity and diversity together — one nation, many peoples and cultures — and that it has no connection to the rest of Senegal's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Senegal's own course, in its own order.
