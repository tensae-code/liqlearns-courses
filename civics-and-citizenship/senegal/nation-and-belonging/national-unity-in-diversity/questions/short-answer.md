# National Unity in Diversity — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "One nation, in unity" teach?
**Answer:** The Senegalese nation is one — its unity is the shared belonging of all its people.

**2.** In your own words, what does the part "One nation, in diversity" teach?
**Answer:** Senegal's unity holds inside ethnic and cultural diversity — difference is carried within the nation, not against it.

**3.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** The Senegalese nation holds unity and diversity together — one nation, many peoples and cultures.
