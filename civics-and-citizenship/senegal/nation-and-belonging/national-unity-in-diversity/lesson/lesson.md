# National Unity in Diversity

## Do it first

Name two cultures or languages of Senegal, and one thing that joins their people as one nation. This lesson holds the two sides of Senegal's nationhood together.

## Sub lesson 1 — One nation, in unity

The Senegalese nation is one — its unity is the shared belonging of all its people.

## Sub lesson 2 — One nation, in diversity

Senegal's unity holds inside ethnic and cultural diversity — difference is carried within the nation, not against it.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the Senegalese nation holds unity and diversity together — one nation, many peoples and cultures. Give one real example from your own life or from the Senegal you live in, and name the lesson's key words: national unity, diversity, Senegalese nation. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
