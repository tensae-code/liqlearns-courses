# Homeland and Patriotism — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "Homeland and patriotism" teach?
**Answer:** The homeland is the country that is one's own, and patriotism is the attachment to it that shows in a citizen's acts.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** The homeland (patrie) is the country a citizen belongs to, and patriotism is the attachment a citizen bears it.
