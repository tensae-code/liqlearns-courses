# Homeland and Patriotism — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The homeland is the country that is one's own, and patriotism is the attachment to it that shows in a citizen's acts.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that the homeland (patrie) is the country a citizen belongs to, and patriotism is the attachment a citizen bears it — and that it has no connection to the rest of Senegal's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Senegal's own course, in its own order.
