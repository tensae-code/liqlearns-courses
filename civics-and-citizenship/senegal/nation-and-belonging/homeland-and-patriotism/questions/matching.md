# Homeland and Patriotism — matching

Match each part of the lesson with its teaching.

**1.** Homeland and patriotism — a)
**2.** The lesson's one thing — b)


Meanings:
a) The homeland is the country that is one's own, and patriotism is the attachment to it that shows in a citizen's acts.
