# Homeland and Patriotism — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — Homeland and patriotism?
a) The homeland is the country that is one's own, and patriotism is the attachment to it that shows in a citizen's acts  b) it has nothing to do with how Senegal governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The homeland is the country that is one's own, and patriotism is the attachment to it that shows in a citizen's acts.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) the homeland (patrie) is the country a citizen belongs to, and patriotism is the attachment a citizen bears it  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Senegal
**Answer: a) The homeland (patrie) is the country a citizen belongs to, and patriotism is the attachment a citizen bears it.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
