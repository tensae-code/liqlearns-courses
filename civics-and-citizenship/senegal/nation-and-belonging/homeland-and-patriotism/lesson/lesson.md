# Homeland and Patriotism

## Do it first

Say one thing you would do for your country without being asked, and why. This lesson names the feeling that holds a citizen to the homeland.

Homeland and patriotism are taught together: the patrie as the citizen's own country, and patriotism as the love and loyalty owed to it.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the homeland (patrie) is the country a citizen belongs to, and patriotism is the attachment a citizen bears it. Give one real example from your own life or from the Senegal you live in, and name the lesson's key words: homeland, patrie, patriotism, attachment. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: the homeland (patrie) is the country a citizen belongs to, and patriotism is the attachment a citizen bears it.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
