# Building the Senegalese Nation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — The origin and building of the Senegalese nation?
a) The Senegalese nation has an origin and was built through its evolution — a history the citizen learns in order to hold the nation's unity  b) it has nothing to do with how Senegal governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The Senegalese nation has an origin and was built through its evolution — a history the citizen learns in order to hold the nation's unity.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) the Senegalese nation was built — it has an origin and an evolution, and knowing them builds national-unity consciousness  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Senegal
**Answer: a) The Senegalese nation was built — it has an origin and an evolution, and knowing them builds national-unity consciousness.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
