# Building the Senegalese Nation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Senegalese nation has an origin and was built through its evolution — a history the citizen learns in order to hold the nation's unity.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that the Senegalese nation was built — it has an origin and an evolution, and knowing them builds national-unity consciousness — and that it has no connection to the rest of Senegal's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Senegal's own course, in its own order.
