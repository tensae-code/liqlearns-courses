# Building the Senegalese Nation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "The origin and building of the Senegalese nation" teach?

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** The Senegalese nation was built — it has an origin and an evolution, and knowing them builds national-unity consciousness.
