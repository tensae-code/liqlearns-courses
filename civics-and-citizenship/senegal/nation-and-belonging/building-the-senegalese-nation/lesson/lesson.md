# Building the Senegalese Nation

## Do it first

Ask what peoples and histories came together to make Senegal, and name two of them. This lesson opens Level 3 by returning to the nation — now as something built in history.

The construction of the Senegalese nation is taught through its origin and evolution; the 4e guide's taught lesson aims it at national-unity consciousness.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the Senegalese nation was built — it has an origin and an evolution, and knowing them builds national-unity consciousness. Give one real example from your own life or from the Senegal you live in, and name the lesson's key words: Senegalese nation, construction, origin, evolution, unity. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: the Senegalese nation was built — it has an origin and an evolution, and knowing them builds national-unity consciousness.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
