# What Citizenship Is — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Citizenship is the standing of a full member of the political community — the idea this whole course is built on.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that citizenship is the standing of a member of the nation — the idea on which the whole course is built — and that it has no connection to the rest of Senegal's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Senegal's own course, in its own order.
