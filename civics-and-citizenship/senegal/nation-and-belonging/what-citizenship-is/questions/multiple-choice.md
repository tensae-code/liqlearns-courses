# What Citizenship Is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — The idea of citizenship?
a) Citizenship is the standing of a full member of the political community — the idea this whole course is built on  b) it has nothing to do with how Senegal governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Citizenship is the standing of a full member of the political community — the idea this whole course is built on.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) citizenship is the standing of a member of the nation — the idea on which the whole course is built  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Senegal
**Answer: a) Citizenship is the standing of a member of the nation — the idea on which the whole course is built.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
