# Hands-on — The Nation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name three symbols of Senegal and say what each one stands for. This lesson widens the circle from the local community to the whole nation.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: a nation is the community a people forms together — and Senegal's nation has its own symbols that stand for it. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
