# The Nation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "What a nation is" teach?
**Answer:** A nation is a community of people joined by a shared life and a shared future — defined here in the programme's own terms.

**2.** In your own words, what does the part "Senegal's national symbols" teach?
**Answer:** Senegal's nation has symbols that stand for it, and a citizen learns to recognise them and say what they mean.

**3.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** A nation is the community a people forms together — and Senegal's nation has its own symbols that stand for it.
