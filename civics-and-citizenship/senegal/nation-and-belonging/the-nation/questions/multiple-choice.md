# The Nation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — What a nation is?
a) A nation is a community of people joined by a shared life and a shared future — defined here in the programme's own terms  b) it has nothing to do with how Senegal governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) A nation is a community of people joined by a shared life and a shared future — defined here in the programme's own terms.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — Senegal's national symbols?
a) Senegal's nation has symbols that stand for it, and a citizen learns to recognise them and say what they mean  b) it has nothing to do with how Senegal governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Senegal's nation has symbols that stand for it, and a citizen learns to recognise them and say what they mean.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**3.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) a nation is the community a people forms together — and Senegal's nation has its own symbols that stand for it  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Senegal
**Answer: a) A nation is the community a people forms together — and Senegal's nation has its own symbols that stand for it.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
