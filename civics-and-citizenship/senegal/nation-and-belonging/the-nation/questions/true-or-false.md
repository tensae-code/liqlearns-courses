# The Nation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A nation is a community of people joined by a shared life and a shared future — defined here in the programme's own terms.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Senegal's nation has symbols that stand for it, and a citizen learns to recognise them and say what they mean.
**Answer: True**

**3.** This lesson teaches that a nation is the community a people forms together — and Senegal's nation has its own symbols that stand for it — and that it has no connection to the rest of Senegal's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Senegal's own course, in its own order.
