# The Nation

## Do it first

Name three symbols of Senegal and say what each one stands for. This lesson widens the circle from the local community to the whole nation.

## Sub lesson 1 — What a nation is

A nation is a community of people joined by a shared life and a shared future — defined here in the programme's own terms.

## Sub lesson 2 — Senegal's national symbols

Senegal's nation has symbols that stand for it, and a citizen learns to recognise them and say what they mean.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: a nation is the community a people forms together — and Senegal's nation has its own symbols that stand for it. Give one real example from your own life or from the Senegal you live in, and name the lesson's key words: nation, symbols, Senegal, belonging. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: a nation is the community a people forms together — and Senegal's nation has its own symbols that stand for it.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
