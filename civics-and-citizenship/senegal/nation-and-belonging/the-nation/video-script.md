# Video script — The Nation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A nation is the community a people forms together — and Senegal's nation has its own symbols that stand for it.

A nation is a community of people joined by a shared life and a shared future — defined here in the programme's own terms.

Senegal's nation has symbols that stand for it, and a citizen learns to recognise them and say what they mean.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
