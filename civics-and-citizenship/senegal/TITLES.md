# Civics and Citizenship — Senegal course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

Civics belongs to the country: this course is Senegal's own —
built from Senegal's Ministère de l'Éducation Nationale
Éducation Civique teacher guides (all French; the 2012
all-levels programme guide COMPLETE as filed, and the 5e, 4e
and 3e pedagogical guides PARTIAL as filed). Inside the
course the organisation is by **levels, not grades**: the
titles below ARE the levels' titles, in path order — what the
learner proves, step by step. The grades the books come from
live only in each lesson's sources.

The path's own logic is Senegal's, from the filing's ladder
sketch: the course widens circle by circle — family, quarter,
village, school, then the decentralised local state, then
nation and citizenship, then the Republic's institutions and
human rights, ending with heritage, peace culture and
national challenges. Level 1 is the 6e opening (close world,
first local structures, nation, citizenship, rights and
duties); Level 2 is the 5e year (living in society, the
administered state, peace and solidarity); Level 3 is the 4e
year (the nation built, the state and its powers, democracy
and rights); Level 4 is the 3e ending (heritage, peace at
world scale, national challenges). That ladder IS the path
order (recommended, never forced — a learner can jump in
higher; the "start here instead" hints point down this
ladder). Every lesson in this course stands on teacher
guides — evidence only, acceptance 0, labelled in every
lesson's sources.

## The titles

**Level 1 — my close world, first local government and
belonging** (the programme guide's 6e order):

- `my-close-world/` — Level 1. The Family (society's basic
  cell: its forms, evolution and problems) · My Quarter
  (its history, description and organisation) · The Village
  (the rural counterpart: history, description,
  organisation) · My School (its history, internal rules,
  management council, school project and partnerships).
  These sit first because Senegal's own course starts with
  the worlds the child already lives in.
- `local-government/` — Level 1. The Rural Community (the
  first decentralised structure) · The District Commune
  (structure and functioning) · The Commune (its history
  and how it runs). The first step outward: the local state
  the close world lives inside.
- `nation-and-belonging/` — Level 1. The Nation (what a
  nation is; Senegal's national symbols) · Homeland and
  Patriotism (the patrie and the attachment owed it) ·
  What Citizenship Is (the notion the whole course builds
  on).
- `rights-and-duties/` — Level 1. Rights and Duties of the
  Citizen (the paired rights a citizen holds and duties a
  citizen owes).

**Level 2 — living in society, administering Senegal, peace
and solidarity** (the 5e year):

- `living-in-society/` — Level 2. Living in Society
  (respecting laws and rules, the duty of responsibility,
  love of work well done — stocked by Books 1 and 2, with
  the 5e guide's culture of peace and mediation) ·
  Citizenship and Solidarity (taking part in the life of
  the city, protecting the environment, individual and
  collective security).
- `local-government/` — Level 2. How Senegal Is Administered
  (the administrative organisation's evolution from 1960 to
  today) · The Arrondissement (structures and organisation)
  · The Department (structures and organisation) · The
  Region (the top local level). Level 1's decentralised
  communities are now met again from the state's side, one
  deconcentrated level at a time.
- `peace-and-solidarity/` — Level 2. Peace as Progress
  (peace as a factor of progress — stocked by Books 1 and
  2) · National Solidarity (its forms and manifestations —
  stocked by Books 1 and 2) · International Solidarity (its
  forms and manifestations).

**Level 3 — the nation, the state, democracy and rights**
(the 4e year):

- `nation-and-belonging/` — Level 3. Building the
  Senegalese Nation (its origin and evolution — stocked by
  Books 1 and 3) · National Unity in Diversity (one nation
  held inside ethnic and cultural diversity). Level 1's
  nation, returned to as something built in history.
- `the-state/` — Level 3. The State (definition, types,
  role and functions — stocked by Books 1 and 3) · The
  Executive Power (structures and functioning — stocked by
  Books 1 and 3) · The Legislative Power (structures and
  functioning) · The Judicial Power (structures and
  functioning).
- `democracy-and-rights/` — Level 3. Rule of Law and Good
  Governance (the democratic foundations) · Human Rights
  (definition, history, founding texts) · Women's Rights
  and Parity (protection and promotion; the parity
  question) · Rights of Vulnerable People (children and
  disabled people as rights-holders).

**Level 4 — heritage, peace at world scale and national
challenges** (the 3e year, as the programme itself ends):

- `heritage-and-environment/` — Level 4. Natural Heritage
  (typology and protection through the environment, forest,
  hygiene and water codes) · Historical and Cultural
  Heritage (typology, protection and promotion strategies).
- `peace-and-solidarity/` — Level 4. The Culture of Peace
  (tolerance, dialogue of cultures and religions, Senegal's
  Islamo-Christian dialogue — stocked by Books 1 and 4) ·
  African Solidarity and Integration (the African Union,
  ECOWAS — stocked by Books 1 and 4) · The United Nations
  System (solidarity at world scale). Level 2's peace and
  solidarity, widened to the continent and the world.
- `national-challenges/` — Level 4. Fighting Poverty
  (objectives, structures, results and perspectives; DSRP,
  AGETIP, PNLP) · Fighting Malaria and HIV (the national
  fight against malaria and HIV/AIDS) · Fighting Drugs
  (the fight against drugs and addiction). The course ends
  where Senegal's own programme ends: with the citizen
  facing the country's open problems.
