# The District Commune — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The district commune has a structure — a defined local body inside a larger commune.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The district commune functions by handling local affairs close to the people who live in the district.
**Answer: True**

**3.** This lesson teaches that the district commune is a decentralised structure inside a larger commune — with its own structure and its own functioning — and that it has no connection to the rest of Senegal's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Senegal's own course, in its own order.
