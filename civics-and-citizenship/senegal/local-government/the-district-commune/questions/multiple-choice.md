# The District Commune — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — The district commune's structure?
a) The district commune has a structure — a defined local body inside a larger commune  b) it has nothing to do with how Senegal governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The district commune has a structure — a defined local body inside a larger commune.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — How the district commune functions?
a) The district commune functions by handling local affairs close to the people who live in the district  b) it has nothing to do with how Senegal governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The district commune functions by handling local affairs close to the people who live in the district.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**3.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) the district commune is a decentralised structure inside a larger commune — with its own structure and its own functioning  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Senegal
**Answer: a) The district commune is a decentralised structure inside a larger commune — with its own structure and its own functioning.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
