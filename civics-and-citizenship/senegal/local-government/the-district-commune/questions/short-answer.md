# The District Commune — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "The district commune's structure" teach?
**Answer:** The district commune has a structure — a defined local body inside a larger commune.

**2.** In your own words, what does the part "How the district commune functions" teach?
**Answer:** The district commune functions by handling local affairs close to the people who live in the district.

**3.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** The district commune is a decentralised structure inside a larger commune — with its own structure and its own functioning.
