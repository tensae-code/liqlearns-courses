# The District Commune

## Do it first

Name the district you live in or nearest to you, and one thing decided there rather than in a bigger office. This lesson names the city counterpart of the rural community.

## Sub lesson 1 — The district commune's structure

The district commune has a structure — a defined local body inside a larger commune.

## Sub lesson 2 — How the district commune functions

The district commune functions by handling local affairs close to the people who live in the district.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the district commune is a decentralised structure inside a larger commune — with its own structure and its own functioning. Give one real example from your own life or from the Senegal you live in, and name the lesson's key words: district commune, commune d'arrondissement, decentralisation. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
