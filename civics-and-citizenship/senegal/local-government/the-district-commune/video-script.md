# Video script — The District Commune

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The district commune is a decentralised structure inside a larger commune — with its own structure and its own functioning.

The district commune has a structure — a defined local body inside a larger commune.

The district commune functions by handling local affairs close to the people who live in the district.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
