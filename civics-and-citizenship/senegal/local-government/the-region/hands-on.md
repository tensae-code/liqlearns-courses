# Hands-on — The Region

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name your region and its capital, and one thing that joins all its departments together. This lesson completes the climb of Senegal's local administration.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the region is the top local level — its structures and organisation crown both the deconcentrated state and the decentralised communities. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
