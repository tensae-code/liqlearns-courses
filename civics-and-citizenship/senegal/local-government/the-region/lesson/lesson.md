# The Region

## Do it first

Name your region and its capital, and one thing that joins all its departments together. This lesson completes the climb of Senegal's local administration.

## Sub lesson 1 — The region's structures

The region has structures — bodies at the widest local scale in Senegal.

## Sub lesson 2 — The region's organisation

The region is organised as the top local level, above the department and the arrondissement.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the region is the top local level — its structures and organisation crown both the deconcentrated state and the decentralised communities. Give one real example from your own life or from the Senegal you live in, and name the lesson's key words: region, région, top local level. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
