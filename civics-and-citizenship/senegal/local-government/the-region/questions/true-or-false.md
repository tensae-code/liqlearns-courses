# The Region — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The region has structures — bodies at the widest local scale in Senegal.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The region is organised as the top local level, above the department and the arrondissement.
**Answer: True**

**3.** This lesson teaches that the region is the top local level — its structures and organisation crown both the deconcentrated state and the decentralised communities — and that it has no connection to the rest of Senegal's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Senegal's own course, in its own order.
