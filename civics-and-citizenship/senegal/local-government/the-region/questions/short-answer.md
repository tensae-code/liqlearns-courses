# The Region — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "The region's structures" teach?
**Answer:** The region has structures — bodies at the widest local scale in Senegal.

**2.** In your own words, what does the part "The region's organisation" teach?
**Answer:** The region is organised as the top local level, above the department and the arrondissement.

**3.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** The region is the top local level — its structures and organisation crown both the deconcentrated state and the decentralised communities.
