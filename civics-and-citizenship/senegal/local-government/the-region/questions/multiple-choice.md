# The Region — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — The region's structures?
a) The region has structures — bodies at the widest local scale in Senegal  b) it has nothing to do with how Senegal governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The region has structures — bodies at the widest local scale in Senegal.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — The region's organisation?
a) The region is organised as the top local level, above the department and the arrondissement  b) it has nothing to do with how Senegal governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The region is organised as the top local level, above the department and the arrondissement.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**3.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) the region is the top local level — its structures and organisation crown both the deconcentrated state and the decentralised communities  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Senegal
**Answer: a) The region is the top local level — its structures and organisation crown both the deconcentrated state and the decentralised communities.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
