# The Department — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — The department's structures?
a) The department has structures — the state's offices and officers at department level  b) it has nothing to do with how Senegal governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The department has structures — the state's offices and officers at department level.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — The department's organisation?
a) The department is organised to carry the state's administration across a wider area than the arrondissement  b) it has nothing to do with how Senegal governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The department is organised to carry the state's administration across a wider area than the arrondissement.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**3.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) the department is the level of the deconcentrated state above the arrondissement — with its own structures and organisation  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Senegal
**Answer: a) The department is the level of the deconcentrated state above the arrondissement — with its own structures and organisation.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
