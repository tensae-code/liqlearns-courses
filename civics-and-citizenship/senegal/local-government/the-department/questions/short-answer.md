# The Department — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "The department's structures" teach?
**Answer:** The department has structures — the state's offices and officers at department level.

**2.** In your own words, what does the part "The department's organisation" teach?
**Answer:** The department is organised to carry the state's administration across a wider area than the arrondissement.

**3.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** The department is the level of the deconcentrated state above the arrondissement — with its own structures and organisation.
