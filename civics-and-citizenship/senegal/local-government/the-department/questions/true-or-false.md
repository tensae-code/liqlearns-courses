# The Department — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The department has structures — the state's offices and officers at department level.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The department is organised to carry the state's administration across a wider area than the arrondissement.
**Answer: True**

**3.** This lesson teaches that the department is the level of the deconcentrated state above the arrondissement — with its own structures and organisation — and that it has no connection to the rest of Senegal's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Senegal's own course, in its own order.
