# The Department

## Do it first

Name your department, and one matter handled there rather than in your arrondissement. This lesson climbs the next level of the deconcentrated state.

## Sub lesson 1 — The department's structures

The department has structures — the state's offices and officers at department level.

## Sub lesson 2 — The department's organisation

The department is organised to carry the state's administration across a wider area than the arrondissement.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the department is the level of the deconcentrated state above the arrondissement — with its own structures and organisation. Give one real example from your own life or from the Senegal you live in, and name the lesson's key words: department, département, deconcentration. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
