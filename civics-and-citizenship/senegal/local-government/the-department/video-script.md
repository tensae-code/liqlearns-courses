# Video script — The Department

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The department is the level of the deconcentrated state above the arrondissement — with its own structures and organisation.

The department has structures — the state's offices and officers at department level.

The department is organised to carry the state's administration across a wider area than the arrondissement.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
