# Hands-on — The Rural Community

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name the villages around one centre you know, and say one decision those villages must make together. This lesson takes the first step from the close world into the decentralised local state.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the rural community is the first decentralised structure — the local level where rural Senegal governs part of its own life. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
