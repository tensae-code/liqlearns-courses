# The Rural Community

## Do it first

Name the villages around one centre you know, and say one decision those villages must make together. This lesson takes the first step from the close world into the decentralised local state.

## Sub lesson 1 — The rural community's structure

The rural community has a structure — a defined body joining villages for their common affairs.

## Sub lesson 2 — How the rural community functions

The rural community functions by deciding and acting for its villages at the local level, the first level of decentralisation.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the rural community is the first decentralised structure — the local level where rural Senegal governs part of its own life. Give one real example from your own life or from the Senegal you live in, and name the lesson's key words: rural community, decentralisation, structure, functioning. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
