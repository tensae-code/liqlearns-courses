# The Rural Community — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — The rural community's structure?
a) The rural community has a structure — a defined body joining villages for their common affairs  b) it has nothing to do with how Senegal governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The rural community has a structure — a defined body joining villages for their common affairs.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — How the rural community functions?
a) The rural community functions by deciding and acting for its villages at the local level, the first level of decentralisation  b) it has nothing to do with how Senegal governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The rural community functions by deciding and acting for its villages at the local level, the first level of decentralisation.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**3.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) the rural community is the first decentralised structure — the local level where rural Senegal governs part of its own life  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Senegal
**Answer: a) The rural community is the first decentralised structure — the local level where rural Senegal governs part of its own life.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
