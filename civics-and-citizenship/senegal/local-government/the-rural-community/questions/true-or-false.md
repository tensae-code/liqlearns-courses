# The Rural Community — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The rural community has a structure — a defined body joining villages for their common affairs.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The rural community functions by deciding and acting for its villages at the local level, the first level of decentralisation.
**Answer: True**

**3.** This lesson teaches that the rural community is the first decentralised structure — the local level where rural Senegal governs part of its own life — and that it has no connection to the rest of Senegal's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Senegal's own course, in its own order.
