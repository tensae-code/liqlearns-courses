# The Rural Community — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "The rural community's structure" teach?
**Answer:** The rural community has a structure — a defined body joining villages for their common affairs.

**2.** In your own words, what does the part "How the rural community functions" teach?
**Answer:** The rural community functions by deciding and acting for its villages at the local level, the first level of decentralisation.

**3.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** The rural community is the first decentralised structure — the local level where rural Senegal governs part of its own life.
