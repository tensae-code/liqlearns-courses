# Video script — The Rural Community

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The rural community is the first decentralised structure — the local level where rural Senegal governs part of its own life.

The rural community has a structure — a defined body joining villages for their common affairs.

The rural community functions by deciding and acting for its villages at the local level, the first level of decentralisation.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
