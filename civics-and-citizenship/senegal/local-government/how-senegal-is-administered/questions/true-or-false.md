# How Senegal Is Administered — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Senegal's administrative organisation has evolved since independence in 1960, step by step, into the structures taught next.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that Senegal's administrative organisation has evolved from independence in 1960 to today — the local state the pupil meets was built step by step — and that it has no connection to the rest of Senegal's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Senegal's own course, in its own order.
