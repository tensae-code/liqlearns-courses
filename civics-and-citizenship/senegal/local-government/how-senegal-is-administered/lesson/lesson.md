# How Senegal Is Administered

## Do it first

Ask an older person what has changed in how your area is administered since they were young, and note one change. This lesson gives the history the next three lessons stand on.

The evolution of Senegal's administrative organisation from 1960 to the present is taught as the story behind the arrondissement, the department and the region.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Senegal's administrative organisation has evolved from independence in 1960 to today — the local state the pupil meets was built step by step. Give one real example from your own life or from the Senegal you live in, and name the lesson's key words: administration, Senegal, 1960, evolution. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: Senegal's administrative organisation has evolved from independence in 1960 to today — the local state the pupil meets was built step by step.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
