# Hands-on — How Senegal Is Administered

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Ask an older person what has changed in how your area is administered since they were young, and note one change. This lesson gives the history the next three lessons stand on.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Senegal's administrative organisation has evolved from independence in 1960 to today — the local state the pupil meets was built step by step. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
