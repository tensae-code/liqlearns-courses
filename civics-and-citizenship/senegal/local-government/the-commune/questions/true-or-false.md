# The Commune — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The commune has a history — it grew over time into the local community Senegal knows today.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The commune runs its own local affairs through its own bodies, close to its inhabitants.
**Answer: True**

**3.** This lesson teaches that the commune is a decentralised local community with a history — and a way of running its own affairs — and that it has no connection to the rest of Senegal's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Senegal's own course, in its own order.
