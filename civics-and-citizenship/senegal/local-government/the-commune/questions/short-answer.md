# The Commune — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "The commune's history" teach?
**Answer:** The commune has a history — it grew over time into the local community Senegal knows today.

**2.** In your own words, what does the part "How the commune runs" teach?
**Answer:** The commune runs its own local affairs through its own bodies, close to its inhabitants.

**3.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** The commune is a decentralised local community with a history — and a way of running its own affairs.
