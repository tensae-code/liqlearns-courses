# The Commune — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — The commune's history?
a) The commune has a history — it grew over time into the local community Senegal knows today  b) it has nothing to do with how Senegal governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The commune has a history — it grew over time into the local community Senegal knows today.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — How the commune runs?
a) The commune runs its own local affairs through its own bodies, close to its inhabitants  b) it has nothing to do with how Senegal governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The commune runs its own local affairs through its own bodies, close to its inhabitants.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**3.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) the commune is a decentralised local community with a history — and a way of running its own affairs  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Senegal
**Answer: a) The commune is a decentralised local community with a history — and a way of running its own affairs.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
