# Hands-on — The Commune

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name your commune, or the nearest one, and one service it gives the people who live in it. This lesson completes the first picture of Senegal's decentralised local structures.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the commune is a decentralised local community with a history — and a way of running its own affairs. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
