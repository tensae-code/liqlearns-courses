# The Commune

## Do it first

Name your commune, or the nearest one, and one service it gives the people who live in it. This lesson completes the first picture of Senegal's decentralised local structures.

## Sub lesson 1 — The commune's history

The commune has a history — it grew over time into the local community Senegal knows today.

## Sub lesson 2 — How the commune runs

The commune runs its own local affairs through its own bodies, close to its inhabitants.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the commune is a decentralised local community with a history — and a way of running its own affairs. Give one real example from your own life or from the Senegal you live in, and name the lesson's key words: commune, history, local government, decentralisation. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: the commune is a decentralised local community with a history — and a way of running its own affairs.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
