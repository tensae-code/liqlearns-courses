# Video script — The Arrondissement

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The arrondissement is a level of the deconcentrated state — the state's own administration placed close to the people.

The arrondissement has structures — the state's own local offices and officers.

The arrondissement is organised as the state's level close to the people, carrying its administration into local life.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
