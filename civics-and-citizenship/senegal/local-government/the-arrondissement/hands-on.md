# Hands-on — The Arrondissement

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name the arrondissement you belong to, or the nearest office of the state in your area. This lesson climbs one level of the deconcentrated state.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the arrondissement is a level of the deconcentrated state — the state's own administration placed close to the people. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
