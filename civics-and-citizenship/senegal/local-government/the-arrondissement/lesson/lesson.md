# The Arrondissement

## Do it first

Name the arrondissement you belong to, or the nearest office of the state in your area. This lesson climbs one level of the deconcentrated state.

## Sub lesson 1 — The arrondissement's structures

The arrondissement has structures — the state's own local offices and officers.

## Sub lesson 2 — The arrondissement's organisation

The arrondissement is organised as the state's level close to the people, carrying its administration into local life.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the arrondissement is a level of the deconcentrated state — the state's own administration placed close to the people. Give one real example from your own life or from the Senegal you live in, and name the lesson's key words: arrondissement, deconcentration, state administration. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: the arrondissement is a level of the deconcentrated state — the state's own administration placed close to the people.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
