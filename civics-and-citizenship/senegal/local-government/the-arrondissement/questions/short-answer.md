# The Arrondissement — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "The arrondissement's structures" teach?
**Answer:** The arrondissement has structures — the state's own local offices and officers.

**2.** In your own words, what does the part "The arrondissement's organisation" teach?
**Answer:** The arrondissement is organised as the state's level close to the people, carrying its administration into local life.

**3.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** The arrondissement is a level of the deconcentrated state — the state's own administration placed close to the people.
