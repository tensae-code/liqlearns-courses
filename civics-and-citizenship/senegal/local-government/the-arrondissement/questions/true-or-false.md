# The Arrondissement — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The arrondissement has structures — the state's own local offices and officers.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The arrondissement is organised as the state's level close to the people, carrying its administration into local life.
**Answer: True**

**3.** This lesson teaches that the arrondissement is a level of the deconcentrated state — the state's own administration placed close to the people — and that it has no connection to the rest of Senegal's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Senegal's own course, in its own order.
