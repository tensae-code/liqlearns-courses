# The Arrondissement — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — The arrondissement's structures?
a) The arrondissement has structures — the state's own local offices and officers  b) it has nothing to do with how Senegal governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The arrondissement has structures — the state's own local offices and officers.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — The arrondissement's organisation?
a) The arrondissement is organised as the state's level close to the people, carrying its administration into local life  b) it has nothing to do with how Senegal governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The arrondissement is organised as the state's level close to the people, carrying its administration into local life.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**3.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) the arrondissement is a level of the deconcentrated state — the state's own administration placed close to the people  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Senegal
**Answer: a) The arrondissement is a level of the deconcentrated state — the state's own administration placed close to the people.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
