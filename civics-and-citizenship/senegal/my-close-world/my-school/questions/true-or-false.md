# My School — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A school has a history and an organisation: its people, its places and its internal rules.
**Answer: True**

**2.** A school runs through its management council, its school project and its partnerships — bodies and plans the pupil can name and join.
**Answer: True**

**3.** This lesson teaches that a school is an organised community — it has a history, internal rules, a management council, a school project and partnerships — and that it has no connection to the rest of Senegal's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Senegal's own course, in its own order.
