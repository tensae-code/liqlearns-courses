# My School — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "How my school is organised" teach?
**Answer:** A school has a history and an organisation: its people, its places and its internal rules.

**2.** In your own words, what does the part "How my school runs" teach?
**Answer:** A school runs through its management council, its school project and its partnerships — bodies and plans the pupil can name and join.

**3.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** A school is an organised community — it has a history, internal rules, a management council, a school project and partnerships.
