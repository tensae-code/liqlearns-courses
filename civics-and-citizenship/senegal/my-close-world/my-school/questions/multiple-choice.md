# My School — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — How my school is organised?
a) A school has a history and an organisation: its people, its places and its internal rules  b) it has nothing to do with how Senegal governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) A school has a history and an organisation: its people, its places and its internal rules.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — How my school runs?
a) A school runs through its management council, its school project and its partnerships — bodies and plans the pupil can name and join  b) it has nothing to do with how Senegal governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) A school runs through its management council, its school project and its partnerships — bodies and plans the pupil can name and join.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**3.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) a school is an organised community — it has a history, internal rules, a management council, a school project and partnerships  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Senegal
**Answer: a) A school is an organised community — it has a history, internal rules, a management council, a school project and partnerships.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
