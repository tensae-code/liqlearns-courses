# Video script — My School

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A school is an organised community — it has a history, internal rules, a management council, a school project and partnerships.

A school has a history and an organisation: its people, its places and its internal rules.

A school runs through its management council, its school project and its partnerships — bodies and plans the pupil can name and join.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
