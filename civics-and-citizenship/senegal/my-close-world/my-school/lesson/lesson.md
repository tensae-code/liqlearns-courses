# My School

## Do it first

Name three rules of your school and say what each one protects. This lesson shows the school as the first organised public community a pupil takes part in.

## Sub lesson 1 — How my school is organised

A school has a history and an organisation: its people, its places and its internal rules.

## Sub lesson 2 — How my school runs

A school runs through its management council, its school project and its partnerships — bodies and plans the pupil can name and join.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: a school is an organised community — it has a history, internal rules, a management council, a school project and partnerships. Give one real example from your own life or from the Senegal you live in, and name the lesson's key words: school, rules, management council, school project. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: a school is an organised community — it has a history, internal rules, a management council, a school project and partnerships.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
