# Video script — The Family

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The family is society's basic cell — the child's first civic world — with its own forms, its evolution and its problems.

The family is the basic cell of society — the first community a child belongs to, before the quarter, the school and the nation.

Families take different forms and change over time, and the guide names the problems families face so pupils can face them honestly.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
