# The Family — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "The family, society's basic cell" teach?
**Answer:** The family is the basic cell of society — the first community a child belongs to, before the quarter, the school and the nation.

**2.** In your own words, what does the part "How families change, and the problems they face" teach?
**Answer:** Families take different forms and change over time, and the guide names the problems families face so pupils can face them honestly.

**3.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** The family is society's basic cell — the child's first civic world — with its own forms, its evolution and its problems.
