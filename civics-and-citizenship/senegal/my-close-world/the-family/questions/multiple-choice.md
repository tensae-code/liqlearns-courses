# The Family — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — The family, society's basic cell?
a) The family is the basic cell of society — the first community a child belongs to, before the quarter, the school and the nation  b) it has nothing to do with how Senegal governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The family is the basic cell of society — the first community a child belongs to, before the quarter, the school and the nation.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — How families change, and the problems they face?
a) Families take different forms and change over time, and the guide names the problems families face so pupils can face them honestly  b) it has nothing to do with how Senegal governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Families take different forms and change over time, and the guide names the problems families face so pupils can face them honestly.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**3.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) the family is society's basic cell — the child's first civic world — with its own forms, its evolution and its problems  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Senegal
**Answer: a) The family is society's basic cell — the child's first civic world — with its own forms, its evolution and its problems.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
