# The Family — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The family is the basic cell of society — the first community a child belongs to, before the quarter, the school and the nation.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Families take different forms and change over time, and the guide names the problems families face so pupils can face them honestly.
**Answer: True**

**3.** This lesson teaches that the family is society's basic cell — the child's first civic world — with its own forms, its evolution and its problems — and that it has no connection to the rest of Senegal's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Senegal's own course, in its own order.
