# The Family

## Do it first

Name the people in your family and one thing each person does for the others. This lesson starts where Senegal's own course starts: with the family, the first world a citizen belongs to.

## Sub lesson 1 — The family, society's basic cell

The family is the basic cell of society — the first community a child belongs to, before the quarter, the school and the nation.

## Sub lesson 2 — How families change, and the problems they face

Families take different forms and change over time, and the guide names the problems families face so pupils can face them honestly.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the family is society's basic cell — the child's first civic world — with its own forms, its evolution and its problems. Give one real example from your own life or from the Senegal you live in, and name the lesson's key words: family, society, forms, problems. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
