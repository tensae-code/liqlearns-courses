# Video script — My Quarter

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A quarter — the neighbourhood a child lives in — has a history, a shape that can be described, and an organisation that can be understood.

A quarter can be described: its history, its streets and places, and the people who live and work in it.

A quarter is organised — it has its own order, its recognised people and its shared life, and the pupil learns to name them.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
