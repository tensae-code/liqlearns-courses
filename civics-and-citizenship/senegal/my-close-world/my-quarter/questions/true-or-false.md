# My Quarter — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A quarter can be described: its history, its streets and places, and the people who live and work in it.
**Answer: True**

**2.** A quarter is organised — it has its own order, its recognised people and its shared life, and the pupil learns to name them.
**Answer: True**

**3.** This lesson teaches that a quarter — the neighbourhood a child lives in — has a history, a shape that can be described, and an organisation that can be understood — and that it has no connection to the rest of Senegal's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Senegal's own course, in its own order.
