# My Quarter — matching

Match each part of the lesson with its teaching.

**1.** Describing my quarter — a)
**2.** How my quarter is organised — b)


Meanings:
a) A quarter can be described: its history, its streets and places, and the people who live and work in it.
b) A quarter is organised — it has its own order, its recognised people and its shared life, and the pupil learns to name them.

**Answer key:** 1. a 2. b
