# My Quarter — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "Describing my quarter" teach?
**Answer:** A quarter can be described: its history, its streets and places, and the people who live and work in it.

**2.** In your own words, what does the part "How my quarter is organised" teach?
**Answer:** A quarter is organised — it has its own order, its recognised people and its shared life, and the pupil learns to name them.

**3.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** A quarter — the neighbourhood a child lives in — has a history, a shape that can be described, and an organisation that can be understood.
