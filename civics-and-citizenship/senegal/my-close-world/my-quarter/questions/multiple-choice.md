# My Quarter — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — Describing my quarter?
a) A quarter can be described: its history, its streets and places, and the people who live and work in it  b) it has nothing to do with how Senegal governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) A quarter can be described: its history, its streets and places, and the people who live and work in it.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — How my quarter is organised?
a) A quarter is organised — it has its own order, its recognised people and its shared life, and the pupil learns to name them  b) it has nothing to do with how Senegal governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) A quarter is organised — it has its own order, its recognised people and its shared life, and the pupil learns to name them.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**3.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) a quarter — the neighbourhood a child lives in — has a history, a shape that can be described, and an organisation that can be understood  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Senegal
**Answer: a) A quarter — the neighbourhood a child lives in — has a history, a shape that can be described, and an organisation that can be understood.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
