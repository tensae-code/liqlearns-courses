# My Quarter

## Do it first

Walk your quarter in your mind, street by street, and name three places and what happens in each. This lesson teaches you to describe and understand the quarter you live in.

## Sub lesson 1 — Describing my quarter

A quarter can be described: its history, its streets and places, and the people who live and work in it.

## Sub lesson 2 — How my quarter is organised

A quarter is organised — it has its own order, its recognised people and its shared life, and the pupil learns to name them.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: a quarter — the neighbourhood a child lives in — has a history, a shape that can be described, and an organisation that can be understood. Give one real example from your own life or from the Senegal you live in, and name the lesson's key words: quarter, neighbourhood, history, organisation. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: a quarter — the neighbourhood a child lives in — has a history, a shape that can be described, and an organisation that can be understood.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
