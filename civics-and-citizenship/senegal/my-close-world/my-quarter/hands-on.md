# Hands-on — My Quarter

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Walk your quarter in your mind, street by street, and name three places and what happens in each. This lesson teaches you to describe and understand the quarter you live in.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: a quarter — the neighbourhood a child lives in — has a history, a shape that can be described, and an organisation that can be understood. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
