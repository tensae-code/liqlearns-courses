# Hands-on — The Village

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name a village you know, or imagine one from what you have heard, and say who leads it and where people meet. This lesson looks at the village the way the last lesson looked at the quarter.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the village is the rural counterpart of the quarter — a community with its own history, description and organisation. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
