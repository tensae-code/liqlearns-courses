# The Village — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — Describing the village?
a) A village can be described: where it began, what stands in it, and who lives there  b) it has nothing to do with how Senegal governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) A village can be described: where it began, what stands in it, and who lives there.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — How the village is organised?
a) A village is organised — it has its own recognised order for deciding and for living together  b) it has nothing to do with how Senegal governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) A village is organised — it has its own recognised order for deciding and for living together.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**3.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) the village is the rural counterpart of the quarter — a community with its own history, description and organisation  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Senegal
**Answer: a) The village is the rural counterpart of the quarter — a community with its own history, description and organisation.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
