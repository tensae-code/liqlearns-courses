# The Village — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "Describing the village" teach?
**Answer:** A village can be described: where it began, what stands in it, and who lives there.

**2.** In your own words, what does the part "How the village is organised" teach?
**Answer:** A village is organised — it has its own recognised order for deciding and for living together.

**3.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** The village is the rural counterpart of the quarter — a community with its own history, description and organisation.
