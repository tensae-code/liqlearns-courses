# The Village — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A village can be described: where it began, what stands in it, and who lives there.
**Answer: True**

**2.** A village is organised — it has its own recognised order for deciding and for living together.
**Answer: True**

**3.** This lesson teaches that the village is the rural counterpart of the quarter — a community with its own history, description and organisation — and that it has no connection to the rest of Senegal's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Senegal's own course, in its own order.
