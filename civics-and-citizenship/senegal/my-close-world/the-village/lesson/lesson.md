# The Village

## Do it first

Name a village you know, or imagine one from what you have heard, and say who leads it and where people meet. This lesson looks at the village the way the last lesson looked at the quarter.

## Sub lesson 1 — Describing the village

A village can be described: where it began, what stands in it, and who lives there.

## Sub lesson 2 — How the village is organised

A village is organised — it has its own recognised order for deciding and for living together.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the village is the rural counterpart of the quarter — a community with its own history, description and organisation. Give one real example from your own life or from the Senegal you live in, and name the lesson's key words: village, rural community, history, organisation. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: the village is the rural counterpart of the quarter — a community with its own history, description and organisation.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
