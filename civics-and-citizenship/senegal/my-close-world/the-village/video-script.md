# Video script — The Village

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The village is the rural counterpart of the quarter — a community with its own history, description and organisation.

A village can be described: where it began, what stands in it, and who lives there.

A village is organised — it has its own recognised order for deciding and for living together.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
