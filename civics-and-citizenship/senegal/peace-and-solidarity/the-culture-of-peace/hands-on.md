# Hands-on — The Culture of Peace

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name one proverb or saying about peace that you know, and say what it teaches. This lesson returns to peace at Level 4 — now as a culture a whole country practises.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: a culture of peace is built — through tolerance, dialogue of cultures and religions, and pro-peace attitudes learned and practised. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
