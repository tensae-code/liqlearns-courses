# Video script — The Culture of Peace

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A culture of peace is built — through tolerance, dialogue of cultures and religions, and pro-peace attitudes learned and practised.

A culture of peace grows through tolerance and through dialogue between cultures and between religions.

Senegal's Islamo-Christian dialogue is the home example, and pro-peace attitudes are learned from religious peace texts, proverbs and legends.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
