# The Culture of Peace

## Do it first

Name one proverb or saying about peace that you know, and say what it teaches. This lesson returns to peace at Level 4 — now as a culture a whole country practises.

## Sub lesson 1 — Tolerance and dialogue of cultures and religions

A culture of peace grows through tolerance and through dialogue between cultures and between religions.

## Sub lesson 2 — Senegal's dialogue, and learning pro-peace attitudes

Senegal's Islamo-Christian dialogue is the home example, and pro-peace attitudes are learned from religious peace texts, proverbs and legends.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: a culture of peace is built — through tolerance, dialogue of cultures and religions, and pro-peace attitudes learned and practised. Give one real example from your own life or from the Senegal you live in, and name the lesson's key words: culture of peace, culture de la paix, tolerance, dialogue, Islamo-Christian dialogue. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: a culture of peace is built — through tolerance, dialogue of cultures and religions, and pro-peace attitudes learned and practised.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
