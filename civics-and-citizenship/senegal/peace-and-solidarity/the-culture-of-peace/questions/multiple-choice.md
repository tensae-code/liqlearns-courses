# The Culture of Peace — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — Tolerance and dialogue of cultures and religions?
a) A culture of peace grows through tolerance and through dialogue between cultures and between religions  b) it has nothing to do with how Senegal governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) A culture of peace grows through tolerance and through dialogue between cultures and between religions.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — Senegal's dialogue, and learning pro-peace attitudes?
a) Senegal's Islamo-Christian dialogue is the home example, and pro-peace attitudes are learned from religious peace texts, proverbs and legends  b) it has nothing to do with how Senegal governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Senegal's Islamo-Christian dialogue is the home example, and pro-peace attitudes are learned from religious peace texts, proverbs and legends.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**3.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) a culture of peace is built — through tolerance, dialogue of cultures and religions, and pro-peace attitudes learned and practised  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Senegal
**Answer: a) A culture of peace is built — through tolerance, dialogue of cultures and religions, and pro-peace attitudes learned and practised.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
