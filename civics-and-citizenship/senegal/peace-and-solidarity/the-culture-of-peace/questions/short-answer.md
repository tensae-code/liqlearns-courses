# The Culture of Peace — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "Tolerance and dialogue of cultures and religions" teach?
**Answer:** A culture of peace grows through tolerance and through dialogue between cultures and between religions.

**2.** In your own words, what does the part "Senegal's dialogue, and learning pro-peace attitudes" teach?
**Answer:** Senegal's Islamo-Christian dialogue is the home example, and pro-peace attitudes are learned from religious peace texts, proverbs and legends.

**3.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** A culture of peace is built — through tolerance, dialogue of cultures and religions, and pro-peace attitudes learned and practised.
