# The Culture of Peace — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A culture of peace grows through tolerance and through dialogue between cultures and between religions.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Senegal's Islamo-Christian dialogue is the home example, and pro-peace attitudes are learned from religious peace texts, proverbs and legends.
**Answer: True**

**3.** This lesson teaches that a culture of peace is built — through tolerance, dialogue of cultures and religions, and pro-peace attitudes learned and practised — and that it has no connection to the rest of Senegal's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Senegal's own course, in its own order.
