# The United Nations System

## Do it first

Name one job the United Nations does that no single country can do alone. This lesson completes the climb from national solidarity to the world.

The United Nations system is taught as the world level of solidarity, closing the widening circles of this title.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the United Nations system is solidarity at world scale — the level above the continent. Give one real example from your own life or from the Senegal you live in, and name the lesson's key words: United Nations, Nations Unies, world, solidarity. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: the United Nations system is solidarity at world scale — the level above the continent.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
