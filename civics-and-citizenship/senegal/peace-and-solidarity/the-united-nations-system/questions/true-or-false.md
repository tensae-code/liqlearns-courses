# The United Nations System — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The United Nations system joins the world's states in one system — solidarity at the widest scale there is.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that the United Nations system is solidarity at world scale — the level above the continent — and that it has no connection to the rest of Senegal's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Senegal's own course, in its own order.
