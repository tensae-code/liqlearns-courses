# The United Nations System — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "The United Nations system" teach?
**Answer:** The United Nations system joins the world's states in one system — solidarity at the widest scale there is.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** The United Nations system is solidarity at world scale — the level above the continent.
