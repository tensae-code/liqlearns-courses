# The United Nations System — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — The United Nations system?
a) The United Nations system joins the world's states in one system — solidarity at the widest scale there is  b) it has nothing to do with how Senegal governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The United Nations system joins the world's states in one system — solidarity at the widest scale there is.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) the United Nations system is solidarity at world scale — the level above the continent  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Senegal
**Answer: a) The United Nations system is solidarity at world scale — the level above the continent.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
