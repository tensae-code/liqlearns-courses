# National Solidarity

## Do it first

Name one moment when people across Senegal helped each other, and say what form the help took. This lesson names solidarity at the scale of the whole nation.

National solidarity is taught through its forms and its manifestations: the ways Senegal's people stand together as one country.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: national solidarity is the country's members holding together — it takes named forms and shows in named manifestations. Give one real example from your own life or from the Senegal you live in, and name the lesson's key words: solidarity, national, forms, manifestations. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
