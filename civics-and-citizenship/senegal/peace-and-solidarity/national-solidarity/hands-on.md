# Hands-on — National Solidarity

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name one moment when people across Senegal helped each other, and say what form the help took. This lesson names solidarity at the scale of the whole nation.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: national solidarity is the country's members holding together — it takes named forms and shows in named manifestations. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
