# National Solidarity — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "National solidarity and its forms" teach?
**Answer:** National solidarity joins the whole country: it takes forms and shows in manifestations the pupil learns to recognise.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** National solidarity is the country's members holding together — it takes named forms and shows in named manifestations.
