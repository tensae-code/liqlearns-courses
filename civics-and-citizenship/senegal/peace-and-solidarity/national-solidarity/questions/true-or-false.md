# National Solidarity — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** National solidarity joins the whole country: it takes forms and shows in manifestations the pupil learns to recognise.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that national solidarity is the country's members holding together — it takes named forms and shows in named manifestations — and that it has no connection to the rest of Senegal's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Senegal's own course, in its own order.
