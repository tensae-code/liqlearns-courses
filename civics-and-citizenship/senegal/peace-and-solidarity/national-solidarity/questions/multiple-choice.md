# National Solidarity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — National solidarity and its forms?
a) National solidarity joins the whole country: it takes forms and shows in manifestations the pupil learns to recognise  b) it has nothing to do with how Senegal governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) National solidarity joins the whole country: it takes forms and shows in manifestations the pupil learns to recognise.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) national solidarity is the country's members holding together — it takes named forms and shows in named manifestations  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Senegal
**Answer: a) National solidarity is the country's members holding together — it takes named forms and shows in named manifestations.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
