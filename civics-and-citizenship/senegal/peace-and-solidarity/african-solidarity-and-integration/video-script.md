# Video script — African Solidarity and Integration

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Africa's solidarity takes organised form — the African Union and ECOWAS join Senegal to its continent.

The African Union joins Africa's states in one continental body — solidarity organised at the scale of Africa.

ECOWAS (CEDEAO) joins West Africa's states — Senegal's own region, integrated for shared strength.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
