# African Solidarity and Integration

## Do it first

Name one thing Senegal shares with its ECOWAS neighbours that it could not do as well alone. This lesson widens solidarity to the continent.

## Sub lesson 1 — The African Union

The African Union joins Africa's states in one continental body — solidarity organised at the scale of Africa.

## Sub lesson 2 — ECOWAS

ECOWAS (CEDEAO) joins West Africa's states — Senegal's own region, integrated for shared strength.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Africa's solidarity takes organised form — the African Union and ECOWAS join Senegal to its continent. Give one real example from your own life or from the Senegal you live in, and name the lesson's key words: African Union, ECOWAS, CEDEAO, integration, Africa. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
