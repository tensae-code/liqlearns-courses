# African Solidarity and Integration — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — The African Union?
a) The African Union joins Africa's states in one continental body — solidarity organised at the scale of Africa  b) it has nothing to do with how Senegal governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The African Union joins Africa's states in one continental body — solidarity organised at the scale of Africa.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — ECOWAS?
a) ECOWAS (CEDEAO) joins West Africa's states — Senegal's own region, integrated for shared strength  b) it has nothing to do with how Senegal governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) ECOWAS (CEDEAO) joins West Africa's states — Senegal's own region, integrated for shared strength.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**3.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Africa's solidarity takes organised form — the African Union and ECOWAS join Senegal to its continent  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Senegal
**Answer: a) Africa's solidarity takes organised form — the African Union and ECOWAS join Senegal to its continent.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
