# African Solidarity and Integration — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The African Union joins Africa's states in one continental body — solidarity organised at the scale of Africa.
**Answer: True**

**2.** ECOWAS (CEDEAO) joins West Africa's states — Senegal's own region, integrated for shared strength.
**Answer: True**

**3.** This lesson teaches that Africa's solidarity takes organised form — the African Union and ECOWAS join Senegal to its continent — and that it has no connection to the rest of Senegal's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Senegal's own course, in its own order.
