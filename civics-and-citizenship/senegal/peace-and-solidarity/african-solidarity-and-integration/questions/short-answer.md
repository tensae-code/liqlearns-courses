# African Solidarity and Integration — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "The African Union" teach?
**Answer:** The African Union joins Africa's states in one continental body — solidarity organised at the scale of Africa.

**2.** In your own words, what does the part "ECOWAS" teach?
**Answer:** ECOWAS (CEDEAO) joins West Africa's states — Senegal's own region, integrated for shared strength.

**3.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** Africa's solidarity takes organised form — the African Union and ECOWAS join Senegal to its continent.
