# Peace as Progress

## Do it first

Name one thing your school or quarter could build in a year of peace that a quarrel would stop. This lesson opens the peace-and-solidarity title at Level 2.

Peace is taught as a factor of progress: the taught 5e lesson presents its expected results and activities around that one idea.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: peace is a factor of progress — a community at peace can build what a community in conflict cannot. Give one real example from your own life or from the Senegal you live in, and name the lesson's key words: peace, progress, paix. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: peace is a factor of progress — a community at peace can build what a community in conflict cannot.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
