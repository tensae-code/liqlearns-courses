# Peace as Progress — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Peace is a condition of progress — where peace holds, a community can build; where it breaks, building stops.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that peace is a factor of progress — a community at peace can build what a community in conflict cannot — and that it has no connection to the rest of Senegal's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Senegal's own course, in its own order.
