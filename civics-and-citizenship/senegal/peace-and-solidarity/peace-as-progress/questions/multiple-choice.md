# Peace as Progress — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — Peace, a factor of progress?
a) Peace is a condition of progress — where peace holds, a community can build; where it breaks, building stops  b) it has nothing to do with how Senegal governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Peace is a condition of progress — where peace holds, a community can build; where it breaks, building stops.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) peace is a factor of progress — a community at peace can build what a community in conflict cannot  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Senegal
**Answer: a) Peace is a factor of progress — a community at peace can build what a community in conflict cannot.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
