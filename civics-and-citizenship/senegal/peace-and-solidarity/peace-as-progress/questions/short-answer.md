# Peace as Progress — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "Peace, a factor of progress" teach?
**Answer:** Peace is a condition of progress — where peace holds, a community can build; where it breaks, building stops.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** Peace is a factor of progress — a community at peace can build what a community in conflict cannot.
