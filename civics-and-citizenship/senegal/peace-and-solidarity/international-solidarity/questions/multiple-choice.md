# International Solidarity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — International solidarity and its forms?
a) International solidarity joins countries in mutual help, and takes forms and manifestations of its own  b) it has nothing to do with how Senegal governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) International solidarity joins countries in mutual help, and takes forms and manifestations of its own.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) solidarity does not stop at the border — international solidarity joins countries as national solidarity joins citizens  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Senegal
**Answer: a) Solidarity does not stop at the border — international solidarity joins countries as national solidarity joins citizens.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
