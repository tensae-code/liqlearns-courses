# International Solidarity — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "International solidarity and its forms" teach?
**Answer:** International solidarity joins countries in mutual help, and takes forms and manifestations of its own.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** Solidarity does not stop at the border — international solidarity joins countries as national solidarity joins citizens.
