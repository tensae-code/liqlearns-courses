# International Solidarity — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** International solidarity joins countries in mutual help, and takes forms and manifestations of its own.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that solidarity does not stop at the border — international solidarity joins countries as national solidarity joins citizens — and that it has no connection to the rest of Senegal's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Senegal's own course, in its own order.
