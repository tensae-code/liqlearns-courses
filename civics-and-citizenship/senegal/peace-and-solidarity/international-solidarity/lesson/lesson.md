# International Solidarity

## Do it first

Name one way countries help each other, and say who gives and who receives. This lesson carries solidarity to the world scale for the first time.

International solidarity is taught through its forms and manifestations, widening the last lesson's idea beyond Senegal's borders.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: solidarity does not stop at the border — international solidarity joins countries as national solidarity joins citizens. Give one real example from your own life or from the Senegal you live in, and name the lesson's key words: international solidarity, forms, manifestations. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: solidarity does not stop at the border — international solidarity joins countries as national solidarity joins citizens.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
