# Historical and Cultural Heritage — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — The types of historical and cultural heritage?
a) Historical and cultural heritage has types — places, objects and practices handed down from the past  b) it has nothing to do with how Senegal governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Historical and cultural heritage has types — places, objects and practices handed down from the past.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — Protecting and promoting heritage?
a) Heritage is protected by care and law, and promoted by strategies that keep it living for the next generation  b) it has nothing to do with how Senegal governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Heritage is protected by care and law, and promoted by strategies that keep it living for the next generation.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**3.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) historical and cultural heritage is what the past hands down — it has types, it is protected, and it is promoted so it stays alive  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Senegal
**Answer: a) Historical and cultural heritage is what the past hands down — it has types, it is protected, and it is promoted so it stays alive.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
