# Historical and Cultural Heritage — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "The types of historical and cultural heritage" teach?
**Answer:** Historical and cultural heritage has types — places, objects and practices handed down from the past.

**2.** In your own words, what does the part "Protecting and promoting heritage" teach?
**Answer:** Heritage is protected by care and law, and promoted by strategies that keep it living for the next generation.

**3.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** Historical and cultural heritage is what the past hands down — it has types, it is protected, and it is promoted so it stays alive.
