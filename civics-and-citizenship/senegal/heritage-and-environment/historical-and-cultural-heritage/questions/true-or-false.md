# Historical and Cultural Heritage — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Historical and cultural heritage has types — places, objects and practices handed down from the past.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Heritage is protected by care and law, and promoted by strategies that keep it living for the next generation.
**Answer: True**

**3.** This lesson teaches that historical and cultural heritage is what the past hands down — it has types, it is protected, and it is promoted so it stays alive — and that it has no connection to the rest of Senegal's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Senegal's own course, in its own order.
