# Hands-on — Historical and Cultural Heritage

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name one historic place or one cultural practice of Senegal, and say how it is kept alive. This lesson turns from nature's inheritance to the inheritance of history and culture.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: historical and cultural heritage is what the past hands down — it has types, it is protected, and it is promoted so it stays alive. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
