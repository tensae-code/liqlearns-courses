# Historical and Cultural Heritage

## Do it first

Name one historic place or one cultural practice of Senegal, and say how it is kept alive. This lesson turns from nature's inheritance to the inheritance of history and culture.

## Sub lesson 1 — The types of historical and cultural heritage

Historical and cultural heritage has types — places, objects and practices handed down from the past.

## Sub lesson 2 — Protecting and promoting heritage

Heritage is protected by care and law, and promoted by strategies that keep it living for the next generation.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: historical and cultural heritage is what the past hands down — it has types, it is protected, and it is promoted so it stays alive. Give one real example from your own life or from the Senegal you live in, and name the lesson's key words: historical heritage, cultural heritage, patrimoine, promotion. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
