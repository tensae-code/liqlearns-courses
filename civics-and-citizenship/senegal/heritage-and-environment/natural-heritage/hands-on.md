# Hands-on — Natural Heritage

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name one natural place in Senegal that should still be there for your grandchildren, and say what threatens it. This lesson opens Level 4 with what the citizen inherits from nature.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: natural heritage is the country's inherited natural wealth — it has types, and it is protected through Senegal's own codes. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
