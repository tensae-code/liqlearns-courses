# Natural Heritage — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Natural heritage has types — the kinds of inherited natural wealth a country holds.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Natural heritage is protected through Senegal's own law — the environment, forest, hygiene and water codes.
**Answer: True**

**3.** This lesson teaches that natural heritage is the country's inherited natural wealth — it has types, and it is protected through Senegal's own codes — and that it has no connection to the rest of Senegal's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Senegal's own course, in its own order.
