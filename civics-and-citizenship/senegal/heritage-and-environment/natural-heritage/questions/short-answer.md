# Natural Heritage — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "The types of natural heritage" teach?
**Answer:** Natural heritage has types — the kinds of inherited natural wealth a country holds.

**2.** In your own words, what does the part "Protecting natural heritage" teach?
**Answer:** Natural heritage is protected through Senegal's own law — the environment, forest, hygiene and water codes.

**3.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** Natural heritage is the country's inherited natural wealth — it has types, and it is protected through Senegal's own codes.
