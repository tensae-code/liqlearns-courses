# Natural Heritage — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — The types of natural heritage?
a) Natural heritage has types — the kinds of inherited natural wealth a country holds  b) it has nothing to do with how Senegal governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Natural heritage has types — the kinds of inherited natural wealth a country holds.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — Protecting natural heritage?
a) Natural heritage is protected through Senegal's own law — the environment, forest, hygiene and water codes  b) it has nothing to do with how Senegal governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Natural heritage is protected through Senegal's own law — the environment, forest, hygiene and water codes.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**3.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) natural heritage is the country's inherited natural wealth — it has types, and it is protected through Senegal's own codes  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Senegal
**Answer: a) Natural heritage is the country's inherited natural wealth — it has types, and it is protected through Senegal's own codes.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
