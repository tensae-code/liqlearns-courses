# Natural Heritage

## Do it first

Name one natural place in Senegal that should still be there for your grandchildren, and say what threatens it. This lesson opens Level 4 with what the citizen inherits from nature.

## Sub lesson 1 — The types of natural heritage

Natural heritage has types — the kinds of inherited natural wealth a country holds.

## Sub lesson 2 — Protecting natural heritage

Natural heritage is protected through Senegal's own law — the environment, forest, hygiene and water codes.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: natural heritage is the country's inherited natural wealth — it has types, and it is protected through Senegal's own codes. Give one real example from your own life or from the Senegal you live in, and name the lesson's key words: natural heritage, patrimoine naturel, protection, codes. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
