# Hands-on — Rights and Duties of the Citizen

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name one right you hold and one duty you owe, and say how they fit together. This lesson gives the pair that every later lesson in the course uses.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: a citizen holds rights and owes duties together — neither half stands without the other. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
