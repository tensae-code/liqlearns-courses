# Rights and Duties of the Citizen

## Do it first

Name one right you hold and one duty you owe, and say how they fit together. This lesson gives the pair that every later lesson in the course uses.

## Sub lesson 1 — The citizen's rights

A citizen holds rights — standing and protections that belong to them as a member of the nation.

## Sub lesson 2 — The citizen's duties

A citizen owes duties — what the community may rightly ask of each of its members.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: a citizen holds rights and owes duties together — neither half stands without the other. Give one real example from your own life or from the Senegal you live in, and name the lesson's key words: rights, duties, citizen. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
