# Video script — Rights and Duties of the Citizen

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A citizen holds rights and owes duties together — neither half stands without the other.

A citizen holds rights — standing and protections that belong to them as a member of the nation.

A citizen owes duties — what the community may rightly ask of each of its members.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
