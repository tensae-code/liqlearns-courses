# Rights and Duties of the Citizen — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — The citizen's rights?
a) A citizen holds rights — standing and protections that belong to them as a member of the nation  b) it has nothing to do with how Senegal governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) A citizen holds rights — standing and protections that belong to them as a member of the nation.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — The citizen's duties?
a) A citizen owes duties — what the community may rightly ask of each of its members  b) it has nothing to do with how Senegal governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) A citizen owes duties — what the community may rightly ask of each of its members.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**3.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) a citizen holds rights and owes duties together — neither half stands without the other  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Senegal
**Answer: a) A citizen holds rights and owes duties together — neither half stands without the other.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
