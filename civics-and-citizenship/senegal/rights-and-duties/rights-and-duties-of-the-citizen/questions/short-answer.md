# Rights and Duties of the Citizen — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "The citizen's rights" teach?
**Answer:** A citizen holds rights — standing and protections that belong to them as a member of the nation.

**2.** In your own words, what does the part "The citizen's duties" teach?
**Answer:** A citizen owes duties — what the community may rightly ask of each of its members.

**3.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** A citizen holds rights and owes duties together — neither half stands without the other.
