# Rights and Duties of the Citizen — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A citizen holds rights — standing and protections that belong to them as a member of the nation.
**Answer: True**

**2.** A citizen owes duties — what the community may rightly ask of each of its members.
**Answer: True**

**3.** This lesson teaches that a citizen holds rights and owes duties together — neither half stands without the other — and that it has no connection to the rest of Senegal's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Senegal's own course, in its own order.
