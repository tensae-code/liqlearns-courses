# Civics and Citizenship — Senegal course — CURRICULUM (the path)

One course for Senegal inside the Civics and Citizenship
folder, organised by **levels, not grades**: a learner climbs
when they have proved the level, not when a school calendar
says so. The school grades the books come from appear only in
each lesson's sources (provenance), never as the course's
organisation. The path follows Senegal's own order as its
programme guide lays it out — the widening circle: close
world → decentralised local state → nation and citizenship →
living in society and the administered state → the nation's
building, the state's powers, democracy and rights →
heritage, peace at world scale and the national challenges.
The filing for this build (`senegal-placements-b.md`) carries
all 46 placements from four teacher guides. Eight lessons are
taught in two guides at once — the all-levels programme guide
(Book 1) and one of the pedagogical guides (Books 2–4) teach
the same lesson — so those eight lessons sit where the
programme guide first teaches them and carry both guides'
provenance rows.

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in
this course comes from government textbooks — this country's
own Éducation Civique guides — except the **Tip boxes**: a
tip in a box is from us, the people making LiqLearns. Tips
are ours; the lessons are the books'. See ABOUT.md for the
full book list and the acceptance reckoning. **This whole
course rests on teacher guides alone — no Senegal pupil book
was among the leads — and it is labelled guides-only
throughout.**

## The levels (the path, in order)

1. **My Close World, First Local Government and Belonging**
   — the family as society's basic cell; the quarter, the
   village and the school described and understood; the
   first decentralised structures (the rural community, the
   district commune, the commune); the nation and its
   symbols, homeland and patriotism, what citizenship is;
   and the citizen's rights and duties. 11 lessons, in four
   titles: `my-close-world/` (4 lessons),
   `local-government/` (3 lessons), `nation-and-belonging/`
   (3 lessons), `rights-and-duties/` (1 lesson).
2. **Living in Society, Administering Senegal, Peace and
   Solidarity** — living in society (laws and rules,
   responsibility, work well done) and citizenship and
   solidarity; Senegal's administrative evolution since
   1960 and its deconcentrated levels (the arrondissement,
   the department, the region); peace as progress, and
   national and international solidarity. 9 lessons, in
   three titles: `living-in-society/` (2 lessons),
   `local-government/` (4 lessons), `peace-and-solidarity/`
   (3 lessons).
3. **The Nation, the State, Democracy and Rights** — the
   building of the Senegalese nation and its unity in
   diversity; the state defined, and its executive,
   legislative and judicial powers; rule of law and good
   governance; human rights and their founding texts;
   women's rights and parity; the rights of vulnerable
   people. 10 lessons, in three titles:
   `nation-and-belonging/` (2 lessons), `the-state/`
   (4 lessons), `democracy-and-rights/` (4 lessons).
4. **Heritage, Peace at World Scale and National
   Challenges** — natural heritage and historical and
   cultural heritage, their types and their protection;
   the culture of peace, African solidarity and integration
   (the African Union, ECOWAS) and the United Nations
   system; and the national challenges: fighting poverty,
   fighting malaria and HIV, fighting drugs. 8 lessons, in
   three titles: `heritage-and-environment/` (2 lessons),
   `peace-and-solidarity/` (3 lessons),
   `national-challenges/` (3 lessons).

**Totals: 38 lessons** (Level 1: 11 · Level 2: 9 · Level 3:
10 · Level 4: 8 — from four teacher guides: the 2012
all-levels Guide d'usage des programmes, COMPLETE as filed,
and the PARTIAL pedagogical guides for 5e, 4e and 3e; 46
placements stocking 38 lessons — eight lessons are stocked
by two guides each).
