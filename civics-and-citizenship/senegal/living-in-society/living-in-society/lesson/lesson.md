# Living in Society

## Do it first

Name one rule at school or at home, and say what would happen in a week if nobody kept it. This lesson opens Level 2 with the habits that make shared life possible.

## Sub lesson 1 — Respecting laws and rules, responsibility, work well done

Living in society rests on respecting laws and rules, on the duty of responsibility, and on love of work well done.

## Sub lesson 2 — A culture of peace and mediation

The 5e guide builds this lesson to anchor a culture of peace and mediation in the pupil's own situations.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: living in society means respecting laws and rules, carrying the duty of responsibility, and loving work well done. Give one real example from your own life or from the Senegal you live in, and name the lesson's key words: living in society, laws, rules, responsibility, work well done. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: living in society means respecting laws and rules, carrying the duty of responsibility, and loving work well done.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
