# Hands-on — Living in Society

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name one rule at school or at home, and say what would happen in a week if nobody kept it. This lesson opens Level 2 with the habits that make shared life possible.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: living in society means respecting laws and rules, carrying the duty of responsibility, and loving work well done. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
