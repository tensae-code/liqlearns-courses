# Living in Society — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — Respecting laws and rules, responsibility, work well done?
a) Living in society rests on respecting laws and rules, on the duty of responsibility, and on love of work well done  b) it has nothing to do with how Senegal governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Living in society rests on respecting laws and rules, on the duty of responsibility, and on love of work well done.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — A culture of peace and mediation?
a) The 5e guide builds this lesson to anchor a culture of peace and mediation in the pupil's own situations  b) it has nothing to do with how Senegal governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The 5e guide builds this lesson to anchor a culture of peace and mediation in the pupil's own situations.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**3.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) living in society means respecting laws and rules, carrying the duty of responsibility, and loving work well done  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Senegal
**Answer: a) Living in society means respecting laws and rules, carrying the duty of responsibility, and loving work well done.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
