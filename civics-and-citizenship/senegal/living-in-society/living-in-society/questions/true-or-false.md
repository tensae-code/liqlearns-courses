# Living in Society — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Living in society rests on respecting laws and rules, on the duty of responsibility, and on love of work well done.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The 5e guide builds this lesson to anchor a culture of peace and mediation in the pupil's own situations.
**Answer: True**

**3.** This lesson teaches that living in society means respecting laws and rules, carrying the duty of responsibility, and loving work well done — and that it has no connection to the rest of Senegal's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Senegal's own course, in its own order.
