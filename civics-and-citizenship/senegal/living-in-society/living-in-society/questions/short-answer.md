# Living in Society — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "Respecting laws and rules, responsibility, work well done" teach?
**Answer:** Living in society rests on respecting laws and rules, on the duty of responsibility, and on love of work well done.

**2.** In your own words, what does the part "A culture of peace and mediation" teach?
**Answer:** The 5e guide builds this lesson to anchor a culture of peace and mediation in the pupil's own situations.

**3.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** Living in society means respecting laws and rules, carrying the duty of responsibility, and loving work well done.
