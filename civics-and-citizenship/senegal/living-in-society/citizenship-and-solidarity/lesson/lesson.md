# Citizenship and Solidarity

## Do it first

Name one thing people in your quarter do together that no one could do alone. This lesson turns citizenship outward, into solidarity with others.

## Sub lesson 1 — Taking part in the life of the city

A citizen takes part in the life of the city — its shared affairs are also theirs.

## Sub lesson 2 — Protecting the environment and our security

Solidarity shows in protecting the environment and in individual and collective security — guarding the life everyone shares.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: citizenship shows in solidarity — taking part in the life of the city, protecting the environment, and guarding individual and collective security. Give one real example from your own life or from the Senegal you live in, and name the lesson's key words: citizenship, solidarity, city, environment, security. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: citizenship shows in solidarity — taking part in the life of the city, protecting the environment, and guarding individual and collective security.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
