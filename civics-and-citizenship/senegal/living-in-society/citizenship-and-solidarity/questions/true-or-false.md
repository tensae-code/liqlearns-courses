# Citizenship and Solidarity — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A citizen takes part in the life of the city — its shared affairs are also theirs.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Solidarity shows in protecting the environment and in individual and collective security — guarding the life everyone shares.
**Answer: True**

**3.** This lesson teaches that citizenship shows in solidarity — taking part in the life of the city, protecting the environment, and guarding individual and collective security — and that it has no connection to the rest of Senegal's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Senegal's own course, in its own order.
