# Citizenship and Solidarity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — Taking part in the life of the city?
a) A citizen takes part in the life of the city — its shared affairs are also theirs  b) it has nothing to do with how Senegal governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) A citizen takes part in the life of the city — its shared affairs are also theirs.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — Protecting the environment and our security?
a) Solidarity shows in protecting the environment and in individual and collective security — guarding the life everyone shares  b) it has nothing to do with how Senegal governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Solidarity shows in protecting the environment and in individual and collective security — guarding the life everyone shares.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**3.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) citizenship shows in solidarity — taking part in the life of the city, protecting the environment, and guarding individual and collective security  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Senegal
**Answer: a) Citizenship shows in solidarity — taking part in the life of the city, protecting the environment, and guarding individual and collective security.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
