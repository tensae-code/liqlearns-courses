# Citizenship and Solidarity — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "Taking part in the life of the city" teach?
**Answer:** A citizen takes part in the life of the city — its shared affairs are also theirs.

**2.** In your own words, what does the part "Protecting the environment and our security" teach?
**Answer:** Solidarity shows in protecting the environment and in individual and collective security — guarding the life everyone shares.

**3.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** Citizenship shows in solidarity — taking part in the life of the city, protecting the environment, and guarding individual and collective security.
