# Video script — Citizenship and Solidarity

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Citizenship shows in solidarity — taking part in the life of the city, protecting the environment, and guarding individual and collective security.

A citizen takes part in the life of the city — its shared affairs are also theirs.

Solidarity shows in protecting the environment and in individual and collective security — guarding the life everyone shares.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
