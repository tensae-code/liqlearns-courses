# Hands-on — Fighting Poverty

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name one thing poverty stops a family from doing, and one programme or act meant to answer it. This lesson opens the final title: the citizen facing the country's open problems.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: fighting poverty is a national challenge with objectives, structures, results and perspectives — Senegal's own programmes against it can be named and judged. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
