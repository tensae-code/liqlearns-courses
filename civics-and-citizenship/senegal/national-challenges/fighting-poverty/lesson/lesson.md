# Fighting Poverty

## Do it first

Name one thing poverty stops a family from doing, and one programme or act meant to answer it. This lesson opens the final title: the citizen facing the country's open problems.

## Sub lesson 1 — Poverty, and how it is measured

Poverty is named and measured — a challenge cannot be fought until it is seen clearly.

## Sub lesson 2 — Senegal's programmes against poverty

The state's anti-poverty programmes — DSRP, AGETIP, PNLP — are taught with their objectives, structures, results and perspectives, to be judged by what they achieved.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: fighting poverty is a national challenge with objectives, structures, results and perspectives — Senegal's own programmes against it can be named and judged. Give one real example from your own life or from the Senegal you live in, and name the lesson's key words: poverty, pauvreté, DSRP, AGETIP, PNLP. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
