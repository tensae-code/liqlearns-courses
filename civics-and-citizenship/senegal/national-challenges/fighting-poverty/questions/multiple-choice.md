# Fighting Poverty — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — Poverty, and how it is measured?
a) Poverty is named and measured — a challenge cannot be fought until it is seen clearly  b) it has nothing to do with how Senegal governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Poverty is named and measured — a challenge cannot be fought until it is seen clearly.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — Senegal's programmes against poverty?
a) The state's anti-poverty programmes — DSRP, AGETIP, PNLP — are taught with their objectives, structures, results and perspectives, to be judged by what they achieved  b) it has nothing to do with how Senegal governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The state's anti-poverty programmes — DSRP, AGETIP, PNLP — are taught with their objectives, structures, results and perspectives, to be judged by what they achieved.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**3.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) fighting poverty is a national challenge with objectives, structures, results and perspectives — Senegal's own programmes against it can be named and judged  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Senegal
**Answer: a) Fighting poverty is a national challenge with objectives, structures, results and perspectives — Senegal's own programmes against it can be named and judged.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
