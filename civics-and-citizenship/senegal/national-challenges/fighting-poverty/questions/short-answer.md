# Fighting Poverty — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "Poverty, and how it is measured" teach?
**Answer:** Poverty is named and measured — a challenge cannot be fought until it is seen clearly.

**2.** In your own words, what does the part "Senegal's programmes against poverty" teach?
**Answer:** The state's anti-poverty programmes — DSRP, AGETIP, PNLP — are taught with their objectives, structures, results and perspectives, to be judged by what they achieved.

**3.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** Fighting poverty is a national challenge with objectives, structures, results and perspectives — Senegal's own programmes against it can be named and judged.
