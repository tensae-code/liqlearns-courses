# Fighting Poverty — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Poverty is named and measured — a challenge cannot be fought until it is seen clearly.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The state's anti-poverty programmes — DSRP, AGETIP, PNLP — are taught with their objectives, structures, results and perspectives, to be judged by what they achieved.
**Answer: True**

**3.** This lesson teaches that fighting poverty is a national challenge with objectives, structures, results and perspectives — Senegal's own programmes against it can be named and judged — and that it has no connection to the rest of Senegal's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Senegal's own course, in its own order.
