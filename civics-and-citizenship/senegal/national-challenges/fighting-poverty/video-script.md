# Video script — Fighting Poverty

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Fighting poverty is a national challenge with objectives, structures, results and perspectives — Senegal's own programmes against it can be named and judged.

Poverty is named and measured — a challenge cannot be fought until it is seen clearly.

The state's anti-poverty programmes — DSRP, AGETIP, PNLP — are taught with their objectives, structures, results and perspectives, to be judged by what they achieved.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
