# Fighting Drugs

## Do it first

Name one way drugs enter a young person's life, and one way a community keeps them out. This lesson closes the course where Senegal's own programme closes it: with the challenges the citizen must help answer.

The fight against drugs and addiction is taught as the last of the 3e programme's national challenges.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: drugs and addiction (toxicomanie) are a national challenge — fought by the state and refused by the citizen. Give one real example from your own life or from the Senegal you live in, and name the lesson's key words: drugs, drogue, addiction, toxicomanie. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: drugs and addiction (toxicomanie) are a national challenge — fought by the state and refused by the citizen.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
