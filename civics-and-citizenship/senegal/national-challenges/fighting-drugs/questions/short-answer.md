# Fighting Drugs — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "The fight against drugs and addiction" teach?
**Answer:** Drugs and addiction are fought as a national challenge — by the state's action and by citizens who refuse and protect.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** Drugs and addiction (toxicomanie) are a national challenge — fought by the state and refused by the citizen.
