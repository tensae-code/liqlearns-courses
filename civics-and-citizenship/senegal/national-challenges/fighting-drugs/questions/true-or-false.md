# Fighting Drugs — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Drugs and addiction are fought as a national challenge — by the state's action and by citizens who refuse and protect.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that drugs and addiction (toxicomanie) are a national challenge — fought by the state and refused by the citizen — and that it has no connection to the rest of Senegal's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Senegal's own course, in its own order.
