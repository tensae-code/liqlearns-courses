# Fighting Drugs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — The fight against drugs and addiction?
a) Drugs and addiction are fought as a national challenge — by the state's action and by citizens who refuse and protect  b) it has nothing to do with how Senegal governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Drugs and addiction are fought as a national challenge — by the state's action and by citizens who refuse and protect.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) drugs and addiction (toxicomanie) are a national challenge — fought by the state and refused by the citizen  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Senegal
**Answer: a) Drugs and addiction (toxicomanie) are a national challenge — fought by the state and refused by the citizen.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
