# Fighting Malaria and HIV

## Do it first

Name one act that protects your household from malaria, and one that protects people from HIV. This lesson takes the national challenges into health.

## Sub lesson 1 — Fighting malaria

Malaria is fought nationally and at home — the fight against the paludisme is a named national campaign.

## Sub lesson 2 — Fighting HIV/AIDS

HIV/AIDS is fought nationally and by each citizen's conduct — the fight against the VIH/SIDA stands beside the malaria fight.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: malaria and HIV/AIDS are fought nationally — the citizen's own conduct is part of the fight. Give one real example from your own life or from the Senegal you live in, and name the lesson's key words: malaria, paludisme, HIV, VIH/SIDA, health. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
