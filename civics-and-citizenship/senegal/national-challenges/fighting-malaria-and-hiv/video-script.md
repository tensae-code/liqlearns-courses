# Video script — Fighting Malaria and HIV

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Malaria and HIV/AIDS are fought nationally — the citizen's own conduct is part of the fight.

Malaria is fought nationally and at home — the fight against the paludisme is a named national campaign.

HIV/AIDS is fought nationally and by each citizen's conduct — the fight against the VIH/SIDA stands beside the malaria fight.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
