# Hands-on — Fighting Malaria and HIV

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name one act that protects your household from malaria, and one that protects people from HIV. This lesson takes the national challenges into health.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: malaria and HIV/AIDS are fought nationally — the citizen's own conduct is part of the fight. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
