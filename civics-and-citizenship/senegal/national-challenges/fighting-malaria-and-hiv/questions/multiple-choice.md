# Fighting Malaria and HIV — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — Fighting malaria?
a) Malaria is fought nationally and at home — the fight against the paludisme is a named national campaign  b) it has nothing to do with how Senegal governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Malaria is fought nationally and at home — the fight against the paludisme is a named national campaign.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching — Fighting HIV/AIDS?
a) HIV/AIDS is fought nationally and by each citizen's conduct — the fight against the VIH/SIDA stands beside the malaria fight  b) it has nothing to do with how Senegal governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) HIV/AIDS is fought nationally and by each citizen's conduct — the fight against the VIH/SIDA stands beside the malaria fight.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**3.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) malaria and HIV/AIDS are fought nationally — the citizen's own conduct is part of the fight  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Senegal
**Answer: a) Malaria and HIV/AIDS are fought nationally — the citizen's own conduct is part of the fight.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
