# Fighting Malaria and HIV — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "Fighting malaria" teach?
**Answer:** Malaria is fought nationally and at home — the fight against the paludisme is a named national campaign.

**2.** In your own words, what does the part "Fighting HIV/AIDS" teach?
**Answer:** HIV/AIDS is fought nationally and by each citizen's conduct — the fight against the VIH/SIDA stands beside the malaria fight.

**3.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** Malaria and HIV/AIDS are fought nationally — the citizen's own conduct is part of the fight.
