# Fighting Malaria and HIV — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Malaria is fought nationally and at home — the fight against the paludisme is a named national campaign.
**Answer: True**

**2.** HIV/AIDS is fought nationally and by each citizen's conduct — the fight against the VIH/SIDA stands beside the malaria fight.
**Answer: True**

**3.** This lesson teaches that malaria and HIV/AIDS are fought nationally — the citizen's own conduct is part of the fight — and that it has no connection to the rest of Senegal's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Senegal's own course, in its own order.
