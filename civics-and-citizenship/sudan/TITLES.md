# Civics and Citizenship — Sudan course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

Civics belongs to the country: this course is Sudan's own — built
from Sudan's History and National Education book for Grade 3 of
Basic Education (student book, Arabic). Inside the course the
organisation is by **levels, not grades**: the titles below sit
inside Level 1, in path order — what the learner proves, step by
step. The grade the book comes from lives only in each lesson's
sources.

The path's own logic is the book's: national education for a young
child is identity before institutions — the homeland first (what
"Sudan" means, how it was formed, the civilisation it built), and
the self as Sudanese at the book's close (nationality, traits and
morals, customs and traditions). That ladder IS the path order
(recommended, never forced — a learner can jump in higher; the
"start here instead" hints point down this ladder).

## The titles

**Level 1 — the homeland and the self as Sudanese** (History &
National Education, Grade 3 student book):

- `our-homeland-sudan/` — Level 1, first title. What Sudan Means
  (the name and the homeland it names) · How Sudan Was Formed (the
  land and peoples coming together as one country) · Sudan's
  Civilisation (civilisation and urban life as a source of pride
  in the homeland).
- `i-am-sudanese/` — Level 1, second title. Our Nationality
  ("Who are you? I am Sudanese" — nationality through a dialogue
  and a Sudanese identity card) · Our Traits and Morals (the
  qualities Sudanese people are known by, shown through Sudanese
  faces) · Our Customs and Traditions (Sudanese dress, food and
  ways, closing with the "Our Sudan" poem).
