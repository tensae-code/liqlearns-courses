# Civics and Citizenship — Sudan course — CURRICULUM (the path)

One course for Sudan inside the Civics and Citizenship folder,
organised by **levels, not grades**: a learner climbs when they
have proved the level, not when a school calendar says so. The
school grade the book comes from appears only in each lesson's
sources (provenance), never as the course's organisation. The path
follows the book's own order: the homeland first, and the self as
Sudanese last — identity before institutions, as Sudan's earliest
book in hand teaches it.

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in this
course comes from government textbooks — this country's own
History and National Education book — except the **Tip boxes**: a
tip in a box is from us, the people making LiqLearns. Tips are
ours; the lessons are the books'. See ABOUT.md for the full book
list, the combined-book contents map, and the acceptance
reckoning.

## The levels (the path, in order)

1. **Our Homeland Sudan → I Am Sudanese** — what "Sudan" means,
   how Sudan was formed, and Sudan's civilisation as a source of
   pride; then our nationality ("Who are you? I am Sudanese"),
   our traits and morals, and our customs and traditions, closing
   with the "Our Sudan" poem. 6 lessons, in two titles:
   `our-homeland-sudan/` (3 lessons) then `i-am-sudanese/`
   (3 lessons).

**Totals: 6 lessons** (Level 1: 6 — all from one student book).
