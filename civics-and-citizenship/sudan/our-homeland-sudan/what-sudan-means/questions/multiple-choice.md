# What Sudan Means — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the name “Sudan” name?
a) our homeland  b) a river only  c) a single town  d) a school subject
**Answer: a) our homeland** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the first step of belonging in this lesson?
a) learning what the homeland's name means  b) memorising a poem  c) drawing a map of the world  d) naming other continents
**Answer: a) learning what the homeland's name means** — That is what this lesson teaches.
