# What Sudan Means — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does “Sudan” mean?
**Answer:** It is the name of our homeland — the country, its land and its people, that we belong to.

**2.** Why does this course start with the homeland's name?
**Answer:** Because belonging starts with knowing the name of your homeland and what it stands for.
