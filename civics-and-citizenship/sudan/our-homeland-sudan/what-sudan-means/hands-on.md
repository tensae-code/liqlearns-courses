# Hands-on — What Sudan Means

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Say the word “Sudan” out loud. Now say what it points to: not just a word on a page, but a real homeland — the land and the people you belong to. Hold that in mind as you read.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the name “Sudan” names our homeland — the country we belong to. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
