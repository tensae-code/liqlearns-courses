# What Sudan Means

## Do it first

Say the word “Sudan” out loud. Now say what it points to: not just a word on a page, but a real homeland — the land and the people you belong to. Hold that in mind as you read.

## The name and the homeland

“Sudan” is a name with a meaning: it names our homeland.

When we say “Sudan”, we are not saying a sound — we are naming the country that is ours: its land, and the people who live in it together.

Learning what the name means is the first step of belonging: before anything else, a Sudanese child learns to say the homeland's name and know what it stands for.
