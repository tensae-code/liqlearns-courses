# Video script — What Sudan Means

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The name “Sudan” names our homeland — the country we belong to.

“Sudan” is a name with a meaning: it names our homeland.

When we say “Sudan”, we are not saying a sound — we are naming the country that is ours: its land, and the people who live in it together.

Learning what the name means is the first step of belonging: before anything else, a Sudanese child learns to say the homeland's name and know what it stands for.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
