# Hands-on — How Sudan Was Formed

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Think of the people who live in different parts of your homeland. They live in different places and ways — yet they are one country. Ask: how did the land and its peoples come together as one? That is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Sudan was formed as the land and its peoples came together as one country. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
