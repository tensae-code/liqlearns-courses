# How Sudan Was Formed

## Do it first

Think of the people who live in different parts of your homeland. They live in different places and ways — yet they are one country. Ask: how did the land and its peoples come together as one? That is this lesson.

## The land and peoples coming together

Sudan was formed — تكوين السودان — as one country: the land and the peoples in it coming together.

A country is not only land. It is land and people together, joined as one homeland.

So when we ask how Sudan was formed, we ask how this land and these peoples became the one country we call Sudan.

Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood, and name the lesson's key words: formed, land, peoples, one country. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
