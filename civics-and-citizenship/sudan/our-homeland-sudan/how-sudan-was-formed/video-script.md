# Video script — How Sudan Was Formed

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Sudan was formed as the land and its peoples came together as one country.

Sudan was formed — تكوين السودان — as one country: the land and the peoples in it coming together.

A country is not only land. It is land and people together, joined as one homeland.

So when we ask how Sudan was formed, we ask how this land and these peoples became the one country we call Sudan.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
