# How Sudan Was Formed — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** How was Sudan formed?
a) the land and its peoples came together as one country  b) one person built it alone  c) it was never formed  d) each village stayed separate forever
**Answer: a) the land and its peoples came together as one country** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What makes a country, in this lesson?
a) land and people together  b) land only, with no people  c) people only, with no land  d) a name only, with nothing behind it
**Answer: a) land and people together** — That is what this lesson teaches.
