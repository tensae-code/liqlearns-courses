# Video script — Sudan's Civilisation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Sudan has its own civilisation and city life, and it is a source of pride in our homeland.

Sudan has a civilisation of its own — حضارة ومدنية السودان: what its people built, made and learned over a long time, and the city life they live.

Civilisation is not only other countries' story. Our homeland has its own.

Knowing Sudan's civilisation gives a child pride in the homeland: the country we belong to has made real things, and we stand in that line.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
