# Sudan's Civilisation

## Do it first

Think of the towns and cities of your homeland, and the old things Sudanese people built and made long ago. Ask: what has Sudan itself made and built, that we can be proud of? That is this lesson.

## Civilisation and city life to be proud of

Sudan has a civilisation of its own — حضارة ومدنية السودان: what its people built, made and learned over a long time, and the city life they live.

Civilisation is not only other countries' story. Our homeland has its own.

Knowing Sudan's civilisation gives a child pride in the homeland: the country we belong to has made real things, and we stand in that line.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Sudan has its own civilisation and city life, and it is a source of pride in our homeland. Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood, and name the lesson's key words: civilisation, city life, pride. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
