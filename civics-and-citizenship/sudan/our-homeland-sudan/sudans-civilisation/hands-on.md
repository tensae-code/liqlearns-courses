# Hands-on — Sudan's Civilisation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Think of the towns and cities of your homeland, and the old things Sudanese people built and made long ago. Ask: what has Sudan itself made and built, that we can be proud of? That is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Sudan has its own civilisation and city life, and it is a source of pride in our homeland. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
