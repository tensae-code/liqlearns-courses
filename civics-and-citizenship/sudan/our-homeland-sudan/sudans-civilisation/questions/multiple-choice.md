# Sudan's Civilisation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach about Sudan?
a) it has its own civilisation and city life  b) it has nothing of its own  c) civilisation belongs only to other countries  d) city life is not part of Sudan
**Answer: a) it has its own civilisation and city life** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does knowing Sudan's civilisation give a child?
a) pride in the homeland  b) shame in the homeland  c) nothing at all  d) a reason to leave
**Answer: a) pride in the homeland** — That is what this lesson teaches.
