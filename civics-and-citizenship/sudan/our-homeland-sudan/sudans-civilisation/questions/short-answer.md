# Sudan's Civilisation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what is Sudan's civilisation?
**Answer:** What Sudanese people built, made and learned over a long time, together with the city life they live.

**2.** Why is Sudan's civilisation taught to young children?
**Answer:** So they know their homeland has made real things, and feel pride in belonging to it.
