# Lesson plan — Sudan's Civilisation

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Sudan · **Title:** Our Homeland Sudan · **Lesson:** Sudan's Civilisation
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — Our Homeland Sudan (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Sudan has its own civilisation and city life, and it is a source of pride in our homeland.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — it stands at the foot of Sudan's ladder, on nothing before it in this course except the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Before this lesson

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Sudan has its own civilisation and city life, and it is a source of pride in our homeland.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson's scenes are the learner's own home, school and neighbourhood.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the learner does the opening act of lesson/lesson.md before any explanation.
2. **The explanation** — the lesson's parts in order (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE: a picture of a Sudanese town scene — buildings, a market and people at work — standing for Sudan's civilisation and city life — the student points to the things in the picture that Sudanese people made and built.
3. **Say it to your companion** — the learner explains the lesson's one thing and gives a real example (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD VIDEO: the script in video-script.md, filmed (made later, when videos are made).
4. **Questions** — from questions/: multiple-choice, true-or-false, short-answer, fill-in-the-blank, matching — served at the learner's level and teaching-mix (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** — the closing doing block (lesson/lesson.md).

## If the learner is stuck
- Gives the words but no example → ask for the lesson happening once in their own home, school or street today; the example is the proof.
- Go back to the one thing: Sudan has its own civilisation and city life, and it is a source of pride in our homeland. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
