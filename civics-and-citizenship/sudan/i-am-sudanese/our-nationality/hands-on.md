# Hands-on — Our Nationality

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Someone asks you: “Who are you?” Practise the book's answer out loud: “I am Sudanese.” Now ask what shows it: a Sudanese person carries an identity card that says who they are. That is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: our nationality is Sudanese — “Who are you? I am Sudanese” — and it is carried on a Sudanese identity card. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
