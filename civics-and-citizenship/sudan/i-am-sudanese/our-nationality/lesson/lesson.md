# Our Nationality

## Do it first

Someone asks you: “Who are you?” Practise the book's answer out loud: “I am Sudanese.” Now ask what shows it: a Sudanese person carries an identity card that says who they are. That is this lesson.

## “Who are you? I am Sudanese”

Our nationality — جنسيتنا — is Sudanese.

The book teaches it as a dialogue: “Who are you?” — “I am Sudanese.” Nationality is the answer to who you are, as a member of your country.

And nationality is written down: the boy in the book has a Sudanese identity card. The card names the person and their nationality — who they are, on paper everyone can read.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: our nationality is Sudanese — “Who are you? I am Sudanese” — and it is carried on a Sudanese identity card. Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood, and name the lesson's key words: nationality, Sudanese, identity card. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
