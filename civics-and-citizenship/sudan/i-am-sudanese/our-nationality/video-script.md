# Video script — Our Nationality

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Our nationality is Sudanese — “Who are you? I am Sudanese” — and it is carried on a Sudanese identity card.

Our nationality — جنسيتنا — is Sudanese.

The book teaches it as a dialogue: “Who are you?” — “I am Sudanese.” Nationality is the answer to who you are, as a member of your country.

And nationality is written down: the boy in the book has a Sudanese identity card. The card names the person and their nationality — who they are, on paper everyone can read.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
