# Our Traits and Morals — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what are “our traits and morals”?
**Answer:** The qualities Sudanese people are known by, shown in their faces and in how they treat each other.

**2.** Where does the book show these traits?
**Answer:** In Sudanese faces — in its people and how they live with each other.
