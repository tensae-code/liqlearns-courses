# Our Traits and Morals — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Being Sudanese is also a way of being — traits and morals — not only a name and a card.
**Answer: True**

**2.** A people's traits and morals cannot be seen in how its people live with each other.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite.
