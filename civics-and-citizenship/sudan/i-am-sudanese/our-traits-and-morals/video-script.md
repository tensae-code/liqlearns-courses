# Video script — Our Traits and Morals

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Sudanese people are known by their traits and morals — the qualities that show in their faces and their conduct.

Our traits and morals — صفاتنا وأخلاقنا — are the qualities Sudanese people are known by.

The book shows them through Sudanese faces: you learn a people's traits by looking at its people and how they live with each other.

To be Sudanese is not only a name and a card. It is also a way of being: the traits and morals a Sudanese person carries into every meeting.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
