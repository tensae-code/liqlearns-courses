# Lesson plan — Our Traits and Morals

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Sudan · **Title:** I Am Sudanese · **Lesson:** Our Traits and Morals
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — I Am Sudanese (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Sudanese people are known by their traits and morals — the qualities that show in their faces and their conduct.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — it stands at the foot of Sudan's ladder, on nothing before it in this course except the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. ## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Sudanese people are known by their traits and morals — the qualities that show in their faces and their conduct.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson's scenes are the learner's own home, school and neighbourhood.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the learner does the opening act of lesson/lesson.md before any explanation.

4. **Questions** — from questions/: multiple-choice, true-or-false, short-answer, fill-in-the-blank, matching — served at the learner's level and teaching-mix (levels.md).

## If the learner is stuck
- Gives the words but no example → ask for the lesson happening once in their own home, school or street today; the example is the proof.
- Go back to the one thing: Sudanese people are known by their traits and morals — the qualities that show in their faces and their conduct. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
