# Our Traits and Morals

## Do it first

Look at the faces of people around you — at home, in the street. Ask: what qualities are Sudanese people known by? How do our traits and morals show in how people treat each other? That is this lesson.

## The qualities Sudanese people are known by

Our traits and morals — صفاتنا وأخلاقنا — are the qualities Sudanese people are known by.

The book shows them through Sudanese faces: you learn a people's traits by looking at its people and how they live with each other.

To be Sudanese is not only a name and a card. It is also a way of being: the traits and morals a Sudanese person carries into every meeting.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Sudanese people are known by their traits and morals — the qualities that show in their faces and their conduct. Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood, and name the lesson's key words: traits, morals, qualities. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
