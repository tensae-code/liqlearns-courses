# Hands-on — Our Traits and Morals

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Look at the faces of people around you — at home, in the street. Ask: what qualities are Sudanese people known by? How do our traits and morals show in how people treat each other? That is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Sudanese people are known by their traits and morals — the qualities that show in their faces and their conduct. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
