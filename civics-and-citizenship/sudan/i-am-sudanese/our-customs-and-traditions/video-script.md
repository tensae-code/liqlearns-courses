# Video script — Our Customs and Traditions

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Our customs and traditions — Sudanese dress, food and ways — are ours to know and keep, and the book closes with the “Our Sudan” poem.

Our customs and traditions — عاداتنا وتقاليدنا — are the ways Sudanese people live: the dress they wear, the food they eat, and the ways they do things together.

Customs are how belonging shows in daily life: you can see a people's traditions at the table, in clothing, and in how people greet and gather.

The book closes this unit — and the placed course — with the “Our Sudan” poem: belonging sung, after it has been named, formed, and lived.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
