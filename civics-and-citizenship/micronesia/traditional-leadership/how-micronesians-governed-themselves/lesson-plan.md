# Lesson plan — How Micronesians Governed Themselves

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Micronesia · **Title:** Traditional Leadership · **Lesson:** How Micronesians Governed Themselves
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — How Micronesians Governed Themselves (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** long before any written constitution, Micronesians governed themselves through traditional leadership systems — and authority in them was real, ordered and answerable.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Leadership across Micronesia** — Across Micronesia, communities were led by traditional leaders whose authority came from custom, kinship and standing — not from an election or a written law.
2. **How authority worked** — A traditional leader's authority worked through respect, obligation and the community watching: people followed because the leader held a recognised place and was expected to use it for the group.
3. **Why this comes first** — This course starts here on purpose: the FSM's modern government was built by people whose first experience of government was this traditional one.
