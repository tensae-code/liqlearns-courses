# How Micronesians Governed Themselves — matching

Match each part of the lesson with its teaching.

**1.** Leadership across Micronesia — a)

**2.** How authority worked — b)

**3.** Why this comes first — c)


Meanings:
a) Across Micronesia, communities were led by traditional leaders whose authority came from custom, kinship and standing — not from an election or a written law.
b) A traditional leader's authority worked through respect, obligation and the community watching: people followed because the leader held a recognised place and was expected to use it for the group.
c) This course starts here on purpose: the FSM's modern government was built by people whose first experience of government was this traditional one.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b; 3. c
