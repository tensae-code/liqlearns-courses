# How Micronesians Governed Themselves — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "Leadership across Micronesia" teach?
**Answer:** Across Micronesia, communities were led by traditional leaders whose authority came from custom, kinship and standing — not from an election or a written law.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** Long before any written constitution, Micronesians governed themselves through traditional leadership systems — and authority in them was real, ordered and answerable.
