# How Micronesians Governed Themselves — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A traditional leader's authority worked through respect, obligation and the community watching: people followed because the leader held a recognised place and was expected to use it for the group.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that long before any written constitution, Micronesians governed themselves through traditional leadership systems — and authority in them was real, ordered and answerable — and that it has no connection to the rest of the FSM's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside the FSM's one long story of self-government.
