# Hands-on — Themes in Traditional Leadership

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Look back at the three models you have met — Chuuk, Pohnpei and Yap. Write one thing all three share, and one thing where they differ. This lesson names the shared themes the book itself draws out.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: across Micronesia's different traditional systems, the same themes recur — how leaders gained power, how they held it, and how their power was checked. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
