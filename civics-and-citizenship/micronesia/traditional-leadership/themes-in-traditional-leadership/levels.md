## Sub lesson 1 — How leaders gained power
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Across the systems, leaders gained power through recognised paths — birth and lineage, titles, achievement and the community's acceptance working together in different mixes. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — How leaders held power
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Leaders held power by carrying their duties — providing, judging fairly, keeping the peace and honouring the obligations of their place. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 3 — How power was checked
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Traditional power was checked: by custom, by other ranks and leaders, by the community's respect being withdrawable, and by the duties built into the leader's place. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
