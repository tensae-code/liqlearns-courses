# Themes in Traditional Leadership — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "How leaders gained power" teach?
**Answer:** Across the systems, leaders gained power through recognised paths — birth and lineage, titles, achievement and the community's acceptance working together in different mixes.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** Across Micronesia's different traditional systems, the same themes recur — how leaders gained power, how they held it, and how their power was checked.
