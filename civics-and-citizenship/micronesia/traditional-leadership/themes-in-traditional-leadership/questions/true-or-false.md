# Themes in Traditional Leadership — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Leaders held power by carrying their duties — providing, judging fairly, keeping the peace and honouring the obligations of their place.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that across Micronesia's different traditional systems, the same themes recur — how leaders gained power, how they held it, and how their power was checked — and that it has no connection to the rest of the FSM's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside the FSM's one long story of self-government.
