# Themes in Traditional Leadership — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's first teaching — How leaders gained power?
a) Across the systems, leaders gained power through recognised paths — birth and lineage, titles, achievement and the community's acceptance working together in different mixes.  b) it has nothing to do with how the FSM governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Across the systems, leaders gained power through recognised paths — birth and lineage, titles, achievement and the community's acceptance working together in different mixes.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) across Micronesia's different traditional systems, the same themes recur — how leaders gained power, how they held it, and how their power was checked  b) government began only when foreigners arrived  c) citizens should never question leaders  d) all islands govern in exactly the same way
**Answer: a) across Micronesia's different traditional systems, the same themes recur — how leaders gained power, how they held it, and how their power was checked** — That is what this lesson teaches.
