# Themes in Traditional Leadership — matching

Match each part of the lesson with its teaching.

**1.** How leaders gained power — a)

**2.** How leaders held power — b)

**3.** How power was checked — c)


Meanings:
a) Across the systems, leaders gained power through recognised paths — birth and lineage, titles, achievement and the community's acceptance working together in different mixes.
b) Leaders held power by carrying their duties — providing, judging fairly, keeping the peace and honouring the obligations of their place.
c) Traditional power was checked: by custom, by other ranks and leaders, by the community's respect being withdrawable, and by the duties built into the leader's place.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b; 3. c
