# Lesson plan — The Chuuk Model

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Micronesia · **Title:** Traditional Leadership · **Lesson:** The Chuuk Model
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — How Micronesians Governed Themselves (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Chuuk's traditional leadership model — how authority was held and exercised in Chuuk's own way, as one of Micronesia's distinct systems.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Chuuk's way of leading** — Chuuk's traditional model organised leadership through its own ranks and relationships, fitted to Chuuk's islands and lineages.
2. **How a Chuuk leader held power** — In the Chuuk model, a leader's power rested on his place in the system and on being recognised by the people under him — authority had to be carried in the accepted way.
3. **What the model teaches** — The Chuuk model teaches that there is no single Micronesian way: each island group built leadership that fitted its own land and people.
