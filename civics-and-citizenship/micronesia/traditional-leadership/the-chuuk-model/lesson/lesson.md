# The Chuuk Model

## Do it first

Draw a simple chart of who leads in your own place: who is at the top, who is under them, and who do people go to first with a problem? Keep the chart — this lesson shows you Chuuk's own chart, drawn by custom.

## Sub lesson 1 — Chuuk's way of leading

Chuuk's traditional model organised leadership through its own ranks and relationships, fitted to Chuuk's islands and lineages.

It is one model among several in Micronesia — the course studies it on its own terms before comparing.

## Sub lesson 2 — How a Chuuk leader held power

In the Chuuk model, a leader's power rested on his place in the system and on being recognised by the people under him — authority had to be carried in the accepted way.

Power used outside the accepted way weakened the leader who used it: custom itself was the check.

## Sub lesson 3 — What the model teaches

The Chuuk model teaches that there is no single Micronesian way: each island group built leadership that fitted its own land and people.

That variety is why the later federation had to unite different traditions, not erase them.
