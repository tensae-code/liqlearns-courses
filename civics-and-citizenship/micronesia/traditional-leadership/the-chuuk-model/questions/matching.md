# The Chuuk Model — matching

Match each part of the lesson with its teaching.

**1.** Chuuk's way of leading — a)

**2.** How a Chuuk leader held power — b)

**3.** What the model teaches — c)


Meanings:
a) Chuuk's traditional model organised leadership through its own ranks and relationships, fitted to Chuuk's islands and lineages.
b) In the Chuuk model, a leader's power rested on his place in the system and on being recognised by the people under him — authority had to be carried in the accepted way.
c) The Chuuk model teaches that there is no single Micronesian way: each island group built leadership that fitted its own land and people.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b; 3. c
