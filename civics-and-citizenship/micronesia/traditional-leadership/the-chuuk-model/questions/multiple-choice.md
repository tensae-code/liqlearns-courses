# The Chuuk Model — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's first teaching — Chuuk's way of leading?
a) Chuuk's traditional model organised leadership through its own ranks and relationships, fitted to Chuuk's islands and lineages.  b) it has nothing to do with how the FSM governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Chuuk's traditional model organised leadership through its own ranks and relationships, fitted to Chuuk's islands and lineages.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Chuuk's traditional leadership model — how authority was held and exercised in Chuuk's own way, as one of Micronesia's distinct systems  b) government began only when foreigners arrived  c) citizens should never question leaders  d) all islands govern in exactly the same way
**Answer: a) Chuuk's traditional leadership model — how authority was held and exercised in Chuuk's own way, as one of Micronesia's distinct systems** — That is what this lesson teaches.
