# The Chuuk Model — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** In the Chuuk model, a leader's power rested on his place in the system and on being recognised by the people under him — authority had to be carried in the accepted way.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that Chuuk's traditional leadership model — how authority was held and exercised in Chuuk's own way, as one of Micronesia's distinct systems — and that it has no connection to the rest of the FSM's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside the FSM's one long story of self-government.
