# The Chuuk Model — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "Chuuk's way of leading" teach?
**Answer:** Chuuk's traditional model organised leadership through its own ranks and relationships, fitted to Chuuk's islands and lineages.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** Chuuk's traditional leadership model — how authority was held and exercised in Chuuk's own way, as one of Micronesia's distinct systems.
