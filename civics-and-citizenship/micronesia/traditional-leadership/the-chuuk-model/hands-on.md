# Hands-on — The Chuuk Model

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Draw a simple chart of who leads in your own place: who is at the top, who is under them, and who do people go to first with a problem? Keep the chart — this lesson shows you Chuuk's own chart, drawn by custom.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Chuuk's traditional leadership model — how authority was held and exercised in Chuuk's own way, as one of Micronesia's distinct systems. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
