# Lesson plan — The Pohnpei Model

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Micronesia · **Title:** Traditional Leadership · **Lesson:** The Pohnpei Model
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — How Micronesians Governed Themselves (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Pohnpei's traditional leadership model — a system of titles and ranks through which authority was ordered and shared.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **A government of titles and ranks** — Pohnpei's traditional model ordered leadership through titles and ranks: each title carried a known place, known duties and a known order of seniority.
2. **How titles ordered authority** — Authority in Pohnpei flowed through the title system — rising through titles meant rising in responsibility as well as honour.
3. **What the model teaches** — Pohnpei's model shows a traditional system can be highly organised — order does not need paper; it can live in titles everyone knows.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
