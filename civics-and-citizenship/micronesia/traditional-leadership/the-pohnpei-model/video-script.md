# Video script — The Pohnpei Model

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Pohnpei's traditional leadership model — a system of titles and ranks through which authority was ordered and shared.

Pohnpei's traditional model ordered leadership through titles and ranks: each title carried a known place, known duties and a known order of seniority.

Because the ranks were known to everyone, people knew who should act, who should be consulted and who should decide.

Authority in Pohnpei flowed through the title system — rising through titles meant rising in responsibility as well as honour.

The system joined leaders together instead of leaving power with one person alone: high titles depended on the ranks below them.

Pohnpei's model shows a traditional system can be highly organised — order does not need paper; it can live in titles everyone knows.

Hold this beside Chuuk's model: same need to govern, different machinery.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
