# The Pohnpei Model — matching

Match each part of the lesson with its teaching.

**1.** A government of titles and ranks — a)

**2.** How titles ordered authority — b)

**3.** What the model teaches — c)


Meanings:
a) Pohnpei's traditional model ordered leadership through titles and ranks: each title carried a known place, known duties and a known order of seniority.
b) Authority in Pohnpei flowed through the title system — rising through titles meant rising in responsibility as well as honour.
c) Pohnpei's model shows a traditional system can be highly organised — order does not need paper; it can live in titles everyone knows.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b; 3. c
