# The Pohnpei Model — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's first teaching — A government of titles and ranks?
a) Pohnpei's traditional model ordered leadership through titles and ranks: each title carried a known place, known duties and a known order of seniority.  b) it has nothing to do with how the FSM governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Pohnpei's traditional model ordered leadership through titles and ranks: each title carried a known place, known duties and a known order of seniority.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Pohnpei's traditional leadership model — a system of titles and ranks through which authority was ordered and shared  b) government began only when foreigners arrived  c) citizens should never question leaders  d) all islands govern in exactly the same way
**Answer: a) Pohnpei's traditional leadership model — a system of titles and ranks through which authority was ordered and shared** — That is what this lesson teaches.
