# The Pohnpei Model — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "A government of titles and ranks" teach?
**Answer:** Pohnpei's traditional model ordered leadership through titles and ranks: each title carried a known place, known duties and a known order of seniority.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** Pohnpei's traditional leadership model — a system of titles and ranks through which authority was ordered and shared.
