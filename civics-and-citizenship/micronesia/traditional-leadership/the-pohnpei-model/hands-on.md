# Hands-on — The Pohnpei Model

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
List any titles you know — in a family, a church, a school or a village. Beside each title write what its holder may do. This lesson is about a whole government built out of titles like those.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Pohnpei's traditional leadership model — a system of titles and ranks through which authority was ordered and shared. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
