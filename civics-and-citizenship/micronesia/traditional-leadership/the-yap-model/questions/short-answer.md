# The Yap Model — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "Yap's way of leading" teach?
**Answer:** Yap's traditional model gave leadership its own Yapese order — ranks, places and obligations fitted to Yap's villages and their relations with each other.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** Yap's traditional leadership model — Yap's own ordered way of holding and exercising authority.
