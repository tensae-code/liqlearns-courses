# The Yap Model — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A Yap leader's authority depended on holding his place in the accepted order and acting as that place required.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that Yap's traditional leadership model — Yap's own ordered way of holding and exercising authority — and that it has no connection to the rest of the FSM's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside the FSM's one long story of self-government.
