# The Yap Model — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's first teaching — Yap's way of leading?
a) Yap's traditional model gave leadership its own Yapese order — ranks, places and obligations fitted to Yap's villages and their relations with each other.  b) it has nothing to do with how the FSM governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Yap's traditional model gave leadership its own Yapese order — ranks, places and obligations fitted to Yap's villages and their relations with each other.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Yap's traditional leadership model — Yap's own ordered way of holding and exercising authority  b) government began only when foreigners arrived  c) citizens should never question leaders  d) all islands govern in exactly the same way
**Answer: a) Yap's traditional leadership model — Yap's own ordered way of holding and exercising authority** — That is what this lesson teaches.
