# The Yap Model — matching

Match each part of the lesson with its teaching.

**1.** Yap's way of leading — a)

**2.** How a Yap leader held power — b)

**3.** What the model teaches — c)


Meanings:
a) Yap's traditional model gave leadership its own Yapese order — ranks, places and obligations fitted to Yap's villages and their relations with each other.
b) A Yap leader's authority depended on holding his place in the accepted order and acting as that place required.
c) With Yap, the course has three models side by side: three proofs that Micronesia's peoples each built working governments of their own.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b; 3. c
