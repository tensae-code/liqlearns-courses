# Lesson plan — The Yap Model

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Micronesia · **Title:** Traditional Leadership · **Lesson:** The Yap Model
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — How Micronesians Governed Themselves (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Yap's traditional leadership model — Yap's own ordered way of holding and exercising authority.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Yap's way of leading** — Yap's traditional model gave leadership its own Yapese order — ranks, places and obligations fitted to Yap's villages and their relations with each other.
2. **How a Yap leader held power** — A Yap leader's authority depended on holding his place in the accepted order and acting as that place required.
3. **What the model teaches** — With Yap, the course has three models side by side: three proofs that Micronesia's peoples each built working governments of their own.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Yap's traditional leadership model — Yap's own ordered way of holding and exercising authority.

3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.
