# Video script — The Yap Model

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Yap's traditional leadership model — Yap's own ordered way of holding and exercising authority.

Yap's traditional model gave leadership its own Yapese order — ranks, places and obligations fitted to Yap's villages and their relations with each other.

Like Chuuk and Pohnpei, Yap governed itself fully before any outside government arrived; unlike them, it did so in its own distinct pattern.

A Yap leader's authority depended on holding his place in the accepted order and acting as that place required.

Stepping outside the order cost a leader the recognition that made his authority work.

With Yap, the course has three models side by side: three proofs that Micronesia's peoples each built working governments of their own.

The next lesson draws out what the three models share.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
