# Hands-on — The Congress of Micronesia

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Your municipalities each have a council. Now imagine all the islands sending chosen members to one council for the whole country. Write two questions only that big council could answer. This lesson is about the real council Micronesia built.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the Congress of Micronesia was the first islander legislature for the whole territory — and from it the campaign for independence was fought. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
