# Video script — The Congress of Micronesia

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The Congress of Micronesia was the first islander legislature for the whole territory — and from it the campaign for independence was fought.

The Congress of Micronesia brought elected islander representatives together in one legislature for the whole territory.

For the first time, Micronesians made laws and argued their future together, across all the island groups, in their own chamber.

Members reached the Congress by the people's vote — the municipal lesson in elections, now practised at territorial scale.

Voting for it taught a whole people to think of themselves as one electorate with a shared future to decide.

From the Congress, Micronesian leaders pressed the campaign for independence — arguing their people's case and preparing to govern themselves fully.

The Congress is the bridge: behind it the municipalities, ahead of it the Constitutional Convention.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
