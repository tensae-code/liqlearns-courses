# The Congress of Micronesia — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Members reached the Congress by the people's vote — the municipal lesson in elections, now practised at territorial scale.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that the Congress of Micronesia was the first islander legislature for the whole territory — and from it the campaign for independence was fought — and that it has no connection to the rest of the FSM's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside the FSM's one long story of self-government.
