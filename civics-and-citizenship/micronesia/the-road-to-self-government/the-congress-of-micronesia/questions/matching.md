# The Congress of Micronesia — matching

Match each part of the lesson with its teaching.

**1.** The first islander legislature — a)

**2.** Voting for the Congress — b)

**3.** The fight for independence — c)


Meanings:
a) The Congress of Micronesia brought elected islander representatives together in one legislature for the whole territory.
b) Members reached the Congress by the people's vote — the municipal lesson in elections, now practised at territorial scale.
c) From the Congress, Micronesian leaders pressed the campaign for independence — arguing their people's case and preparing to govern themselves fully.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b; 3. c
