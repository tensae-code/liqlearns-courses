# The Congress of Micronesia — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "The first islander legislature" teach?
**Answer:** The Congress of Micronesia brought elected islander representatives together in one legislature for the whole territory.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** The Congress of Micronesia was the first islander legislature for the whole territory — and from it the campaign for independence was fought.
