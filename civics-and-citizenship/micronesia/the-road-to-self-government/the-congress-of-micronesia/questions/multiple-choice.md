# The Congress of Micronesia — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's first teaching — The first islander legislature?
a) The Congress of Micronesia brought elected islander representatives together in one legislature for the whole territory.  b) it has nothing to do with how the FSM governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The Congress of Micronesia brought elected islander representatives together in one legislature for the whole territory.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) the Congress of Micronesia was the first islander legislature for the whole territory — and from it the campaign for independence was fought  b) government began only when foreigners arrived  c) citizens should never question leaders  d) all islands govern in exactly the same way
**Answer: a) the Congress of Micronesia was the first islander legislature for the whole territory — and from it the campaign for independence was fought** — That is what this lesson teaches.
