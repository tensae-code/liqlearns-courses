# Lesson plan — The Congress of Micronesia

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Micronesia · **Title:** The Road to Self-Government · **Lesson:** The Congress of Micronesia
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — The Road Back to Self-Government (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the Congress of Micronesia was the first islander legislature for the whole territory — and from it the campaign for independence was fought.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The first islander legislature** — The Congress of Micronesia brought elected islander representatives together in one legislature for the whole territory.
2. **Voting for the Congress** — Members reached the Congress by the people's vote — the municipal lesson in elections, now practised at territorial scale.
3. **The fight for independence** — From the Congress, Micronesian leaders pressed the campaign for independence — arguing their people's case and preparing to govern themselves fully.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
