# Hands-on — First Elections and Municipalities

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Imagine your town must choose its own leader for the first time by voting. Write: who may vote, who counts the votes, and what the winner may decide. This lesson tells how Micronesian municipalities actually took that first step.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: self-government came back step by step — and the first step was the municipalities, where islanders first elected their own magistrates, councils and judges. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
