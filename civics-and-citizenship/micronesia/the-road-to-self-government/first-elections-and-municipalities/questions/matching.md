# First Elections and Municipalities — matching

Match each part of the lesson with its teaching.

**1.** The first elections — a)

**2.** The municipality and its officers — b)

**3.** The first step back — c)


Meanings:
a) The first elections let islanders choose their own local leaders by vote — told in the book through Kosrae's and Etal's first elections.
b) Each municipality had its officers — a magistrate to lead it, a legislative council to make its local rules, and a municipal judge to settle its cases.
c) The municipalities matter beyond their size: they were the training ground where Micronesians practised elected self-government again.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b; 3. c
