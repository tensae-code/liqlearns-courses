# First Elections and Municipalities — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Each municipality had its officers — a magistrate to lead it, a legislative council to make its local rules, and a municipal judge to settle its cases.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that self-government came back step by step — and the first step was the municipalities, where islanders first elected their own magistrates, councils and judges — and that it has no connection to the rest of the FSM's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside the FSM's one long story of self-government.
