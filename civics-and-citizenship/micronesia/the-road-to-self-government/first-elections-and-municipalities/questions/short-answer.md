# First Elections and Municipalities — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "The first elections" teach?
**Answer:** The first elections let islanders choose their own local leaders by vote — told in the book through Kosrae's and Etal's first elections.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** Self-government came back step by step — and the first step was the municipalities, where islanders first elected their own magistrates, councils and judges.
