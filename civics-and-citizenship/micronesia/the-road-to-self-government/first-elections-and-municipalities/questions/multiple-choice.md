# First Elections and Municipalities — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's first teaching — The first elections?
a) The first elections let islanders choose their own local leaders by vote — told in the book through Kosrae's and Etal's first elections.  b) it has nothing to do with how the FSM governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The first elections let islanders choose their own local leaders by vote — told in the book through Kosrae's and Etal's first elections.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) self-government came back step by step — and the first step was the municipalities, where islanders first elected their own magistrates, councils and judges  b) government began only when foreigners arrived  c) citizens should never question leaders  d) all islands govern in exactly the same way
**Answer: a) self-government came back step by step — and the first step was the municipalities, where islanders first elected their own magistrates, councils and judges** — That is what this lesson teaches.
