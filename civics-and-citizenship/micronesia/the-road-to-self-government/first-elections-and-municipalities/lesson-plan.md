# Lesson plan — First Elections and Municipalities

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Micronesia · **Title:** The Road to Self-Government · **Lesson:** First Elections and Municipalities
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — The Road Back to Self-Government (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** self-government came back step by step — and the first step was the municipalities, where islanders first elected their own magistrates, councils and judges.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The first elections** — The first elections let islanders choose their own local leaders by vote — told in the book through Kosrae's and Etal's first elections.
2. **The municipality and its officers** — Each municipality had its officers — a magistrate to lead it, a legislative council to make its local rules, and a municipal judge to settle its cases.
3. **The first step back** — The municipalities matter beyond their size: they were the training ground where Micronesians practised elected self-government again.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
