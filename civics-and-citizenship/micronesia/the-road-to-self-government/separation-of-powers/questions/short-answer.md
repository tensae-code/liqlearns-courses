# Separation of Powers — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "The three powers named" teach?
**Answer:** Legislative power makes the laws, executive power carries the laws out, and judicial power judges cases under the laws.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** Government power is safer divided — legislative, executive and judicial power defined and separated, against the traditional joining of powers in one leader.
