# Separation of Powers — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Separated, each power limits the others: the law-maker cannot also be the judge of his own law, and the executor must answer to both.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that government power is safer divided — legislative, executive and judicial power defined and separated, against the traditional joining of powers in one leader — and that it has no connection to the rest of the FSM's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside the FSM's one long story of self-government.
