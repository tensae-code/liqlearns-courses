# Separation of Powers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's first teaching — The three powers named?
a) Legislative power makes the laws, executive power carries the laws out, and judicial power judges cases under the laws.  b) it has nothing to do with how the FSM governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Legislative power makes the laws, executive power carries the laws out, and judicial power judges cases under the laws.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) government power is safer divided — legislative, executive and judicial power defined and separated, against the traditional joining of powers in one leader  b) government began only when foreigners arrived  c) citizens should never question leaders  d) all islands govern in exactly the same way
**Answer: a) government power is safer divided — legislative, executive and judicial power defined and separated, against the traditional joining of powers in one leader** — That is what this lesson teaches.
