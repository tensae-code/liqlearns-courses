# Writing the Constitution — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's first teaching — The Constitutional Convention?
a) The Micronesian Constitutional Convention gathered the people's delegates to design the new nation's basic law.  b) it has nothing to do with how the FSM governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The Micronesian Constitutional Convention gathered the people's delegates to design the new nation's basic law.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) a free people must write its own rules — the Micronesian Constitutional Convention decided what the new constitution should say, and its delegates became the Founding Fathers of the FSM  b) government began only when foreigners arrived  c) citizens should never question leaders  d) all islands govern in exactly the same way
**Answer: a) a free people must write its own rules — the Micronesian Constitutional Convention decided what the new constitution should say, and its delegates became the Founding Fathers of the FSM** — That is what this lesson teaches.
