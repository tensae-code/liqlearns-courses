# Writing the Constitution — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "The Constitutional Convention" teach?
**Answer:** The Micronesian Constitutional Convention gathered the people's delegates to design the new nation's basic law.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** A free people must write its own rules — the Micronesian Constitutional Convention decided what the new constitution should say, and its delegates became the Founding Fathers of the FSM.
