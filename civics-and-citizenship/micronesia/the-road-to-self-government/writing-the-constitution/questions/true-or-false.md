# Writing the Constitution — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The delegates had to decide the great questions — how power would be divided between the nation and the states, what rights the people would keep, and how leaders would be checked.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that a free people must write its own rules — the Micronesian Constitutional Convention decided what the new constitution should say, and its delegates became the Founding Fathers of the FSM — and that it has no connection to the rest of the FSM's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside the FSM's one long story of self-government.
