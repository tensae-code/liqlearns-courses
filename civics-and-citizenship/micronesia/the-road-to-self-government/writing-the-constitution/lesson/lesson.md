# Writing the Constitution

## Do it first

If your class had to write the rules it will live under for fifty years, list the five rules you would fight hardest to include. This lesson tells how Micronesia's delegates faced exactly that task.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Constitutional Convention

The Micronesian Constitutional Convention gathered the people's delegates to design the new nation's basic law.

A convention is a founding meeting: its product is not an ordinary law but the constitution every later law must obey.

## Sub lesson 2 — Deciding what the constitution should say

The delegates had to decide the great questions — how power would be divided between the nation and the states, what rights the people would keep, and how leaders would be checked.

Their answers balanced the traditional past of Level 1 with the separated-powers design of this level.

## Sub lesson 3 — The Founding Fathers of the FSM

The book honours the men who founded the FSM — the leaders who carried the independence fight into the convention and signed the new order into being.

Remembering founders by name is part of citizenship: the federation is a made thing, made by known people at a known time.
