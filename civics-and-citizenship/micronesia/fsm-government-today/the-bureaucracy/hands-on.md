# Hands-on — The Bureaucracy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Think of the last form you or your family had to get stamped, signed or approved. Write whose desk it crossed and how long each step took. Those desks are this lesson's subject: the bureaucracy.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: behind the elected leaders stands the bureaucracy — the government's working offices — and in the FSM its work is pulled by personal and family ties. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
