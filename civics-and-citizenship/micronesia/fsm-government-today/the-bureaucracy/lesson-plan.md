# Lesson plan — The Bureaucracy

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Micronesia · **Title:** FSM Government Today · **Lesson:** The Bureaucracy
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — The FSM Government Today (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** behind the elected leaders stands the bureaucracy — the government's working offices — and in the FSM its work is pulled by personal and family ties.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **What the bureaucracy is** — The bureaucracy is the government's permanent working machinery — the offices and public servants who carry out the laws day by day.
2. **When politics is personal** — The book's warning is in its subtitle: in a small island society, officials and applicants are kin, neighbours and friends — politics becomes personal.
3. **The citizen's eye on the offices** — A citizen who understands the bureaucracy knows where a matter rests, what paper it needs, and when a delay is a system failing rather than a rule.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
