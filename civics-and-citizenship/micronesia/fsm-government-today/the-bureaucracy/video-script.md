# Video script — The Bureaucracy

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Behind the elected leaders stands the bureaucracy — the government's working offices — and in the FSM its work is pulled by personal and family ties.

The bureaucracy is the government's permanent working machinery — the offices and public servants who carry out the laws day by day.

Presidents and congresses change; the bureaucracy remains, and how it works decides how government actually feels to a citizen.

The book's warning is in its subtitle: in a small island society, officials and applicants are kin, neighbours and friends — politics becomes personal.

Personal ties can humanise an office, and can also bend it: the favour for a relative is the stranger's injustice.

A citizen who understands the bureaucracy knows where a matter rests, what paper it needs, and when a delay is a system failing rather than a rule.

Fair administration — the same rule for kin and stranger — is the standard this lesson sets.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
