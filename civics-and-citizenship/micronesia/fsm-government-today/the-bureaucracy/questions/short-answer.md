# The Bureaucracy — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "What the bureaucracy is" teach?
**Answer:** The bureaucracy is the government's permanent working machinery — the offices and public servants who carry out the laws day by day.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** Behind the elected leaders stands the bureaucracy — the government's working offices — and in the FSM its work is pulled by personal and family ties.
