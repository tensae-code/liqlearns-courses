# The Bureaucracy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's first teaching — What the bureaucracy is?
a) The bureaucracy is the government's permanent working machinery — the offices and public servants who carry out the laws day by day.  b) it has nothing to do with how the FSM governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The bureaucracy is the government's permanent working machinery — the offices and public servants who carry out the laws day by day.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) behind the elected leaders stands the bureaucracy — the government's working offices — and in the FSM its work is pulled by personal and family ties  b) government began only when foreigners arrived  c) citizens should never question leaders  d) all islands govern in exactly the same way
**Answer: a) behind the elected leaders stands the bureaucracy — the government's working offices — and in the FSM its work is pulled by personal and family ties** — That is what this lesson teaches.
