# The Bureaucracy — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The book's warning is in its subtitle: in a small island society, officials and applicants are kin, neighbours and friends — politics becomes personal.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that behind the elected leaders stands the bureaucracy — the government's working offices — and in the FSM its work is pulled by personal and family ties — and that it has no connection to the rest of the FSM's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside the FSM's one long story of self-government.
