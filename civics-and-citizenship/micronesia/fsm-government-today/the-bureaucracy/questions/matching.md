# The Bureaucracy — matching

Match each part of the lesson with its teaching.

**1.** What the bureaucracy is — a)

**2.** When politics is personal — b)

**3.** The citizen's eye on the offices — c)


Meanings:
a) The bureaucracy is the government's permanent working machinery — the offices and public servants who carry out the laws day by day.
b) The book's warning is in its subtitle: in a small island society, officials and applicants are kin, neighbours and friends — politics becomes personal.
c) A citizen who understands the bureaucracy knows where a matter rests, what paper it needs, and when a delay is a system failing rather than a rule.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b; 3. c
