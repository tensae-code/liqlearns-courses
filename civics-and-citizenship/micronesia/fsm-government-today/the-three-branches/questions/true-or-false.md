# The Three Branches — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The President heads the executive branch: carrying out the laws, running the national government's daily work and representing the FSM.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that the FSM national government works in three branches — the Congress makes the laws, the President carries them out, and the courts judge under them — and that it has no connection to the rest of the FSM's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside the FSM's one long story of self-government.
