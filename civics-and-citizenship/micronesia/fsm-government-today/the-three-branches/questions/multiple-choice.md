# The Three Branches — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's first teaching — The Congress — the legislative branch?
a) The FSM Congress is the national legislature: the people's elected representatives meeting to make the nation's laws.  b) it has nothing to do with how the FSM governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The FSM Congress is the national legislature: the people's elected representatives meeting to make the nation's laws.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) the FSM national government works in three branches — the Congress makes the laws, the President carries them out, and the courts judge under them  b) government began only when foreigners arrived  c) citizens should never question leaders  d) all islands govern in exactly the same way
**Answer: a) the FSM national government works in three branches — the Congress makes the laws, the President carries them out, and the courts judge under them** — That is what this lesson teaches.
