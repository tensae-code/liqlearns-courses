# The Three Branches — matching

Match each part of the lesson with its teaching.

**1.** The Congress — the legislative branch — a)

**2.** The President — the executive branch — b)

**3.** The courts — the judicial branch — c)


Meanings:
a) The FSM Congress is the national legislature: the people's elected representatives meeting to make the nation's laws.
b) The President heads the executive branch: carrying out the laws, running the national government's daily work and representing the FSM.
c) The courts form the judicial branch: judging cases, settling disputes about what the law means and whether it has been obeyed.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b; 3. c
