# The Three Branches — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "The Congress — the legislative branch" teach?
**Answer:** The FSM Congress is the national legislature: the people's elected representatives meeting to make the nation's laws.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** The FSM national government works in three branches — the Congress makes the laws, the President carries them out, and the courts judge under them.
