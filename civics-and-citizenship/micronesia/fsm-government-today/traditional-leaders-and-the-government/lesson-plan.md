# Lesson plan — Traditional Leaders and the Government

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Micronesia · **Title:** FSM Government Today · **Lesson:** Traditional Leaders and the Government
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — The FSM Government Today (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** traditional leaders did not vanish when the FSM was founded — they hold a real place inside today's government, and the two authorities must work together.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The place of traditional leaders today** — Traditional leaders keep recognised standing in the FSM's public life — their authority from Level 1 continues inside the modern state's world.
2. **Two authorities, one people** — Elected government rules by constitution and vote; traditional leadership rules by custom and standing — the same people live under both.
3. **When the two must work it out** — Where the two authorities disagree, the FSM must settle the matter without destroying either — that negotiation is a standing feature of its politics.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
