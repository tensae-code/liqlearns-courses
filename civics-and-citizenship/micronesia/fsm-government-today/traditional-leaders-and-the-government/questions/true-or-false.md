# Traditional Leaders and the Government — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Elected government rules by constitution and vote; traditional leadership rules by custom and standing — the same people live under both.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that traditional leaders did not vanish when the FSM was founded — they hold a real place inside today's government, and the two authorities must work together — and that it has no connection to the rest of the FSM's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside the FSM's one long story of self-government.
