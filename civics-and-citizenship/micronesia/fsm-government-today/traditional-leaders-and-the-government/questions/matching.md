# Traditional Leaders and the Government — matching

Match each part of the lesson with its teaching.

**1.** The place of traditional leaders today — a)

**2.** Two authorities, one people — b)

**3.** When the two must work it out — c)


Meanings:
a) Traditional leaders keep recognised standing in the FSM's public life — their authority from Level 1 continues inside the modern state's world.
b) Elected government rules by constitution and vote; traditional leadership rules by custom and standing — the same people live under both.
c) Where the two authorities disagree, the FSM must settle the matter without destroying either — that negotiation is a standing feature of its politics.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b; 3. c
