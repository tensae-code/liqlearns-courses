# Video script — Traditional Leaders and the Government

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Traditional leaders did not vanish when the FSM was founded — they hold a real place inside today's government, and the two authorities must work together.

Traditional leaders keep recognised standing in the FSM's public life — their authority from Level 1 continues inside the modern state's world.

The federation was built by people who held both inheritances, and its government makes room for both.

Elected government rules by constitution and vote; traditional leadership rules by custom and standing — the same people live under both.

Most days the two support each other: custom gives government roots, and government gives custom a protected place.

Where the two authorities disagree, the FSM must settle the matter without destroying either — that negotiation is a standing feature of its politics.

The citizen's part is respect for both doors, and knowing which door a matter truly belongs to.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
