# Hands-on — Traditional Leaders and the Government

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name a decision in your community where people would ask a traditional leader first, and one where they would go to an elected official. This lesson explains why both doors exist in the FSM — and how they relate.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: traditional leaders did not vanish when the FSM was founded — they hold a real place inside today's government, and the two authorities must work together. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
