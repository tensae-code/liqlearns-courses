# Video script — Checks and Balances

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The FSM's branches check each other so no branch rules alone — and the book is honest about where those checks strain.

Each branch holds tools against the others: laws can be blocked, actions reviewed, appointments and spending watched, judgments enforced or resisted through lawful channels.

A check is not an insult to the other branch — it is the design working: power answering to power so the people stay sovereign.

Checks and balances turn the separation of powers from three sealed boxes into one shared government, each part inside the others' sight.

The system's aim is the old Micronesian aim of Level 1: no leader unchecked — now written into a constitution.

The book names the problems honestly: checks can slow government, blur responsibility, or become weapons in personal and political fights.

Knowing where the strain falls is part of the lesson — a citizen defends the checks best by seeing their costs clearly.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
