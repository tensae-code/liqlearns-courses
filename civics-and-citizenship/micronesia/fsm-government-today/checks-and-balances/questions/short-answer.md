# Checks and Balances — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "How the branches check each other" teach?
**Answer:** Each branch holds tools against the others: laws can be blocked, actions reviewed, appointments and spending watched, judgments enforced or resisted through lawful channels.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** The FSM's branches check each other so no branch rules alone — and the book is honest about where those checks strain.
