# Hands-on — Checks and Balances

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name one person or group who can stop you doing something wrong at home or school, and one person you can stop in return. That two-way stopping is a check. This lesson maps the FSM's whole system of two-way stopping — and its problems.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the FSM's branches check each other so no branch rules alone — and the book is honest about where those checks strain. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
