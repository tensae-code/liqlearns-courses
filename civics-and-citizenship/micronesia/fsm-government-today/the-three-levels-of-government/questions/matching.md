# The Three Levels of Government — matching

Match each part of the lesson with its teaching.

**1.** The national level — a)

**2.** The state level — b)

**3.** The municipal level — c)


Meanings:
a) The national government serves the whole federation — the affairs the four states share and cannot handle island by island.
b) Each of the four states governs its own affairs through its own state government, close to its people's language, land and custom.
c) Municipal government is the closest level — the descendant of the municipalities of Level 2, handling the daily affairs of towns and villages.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b; 3. c
