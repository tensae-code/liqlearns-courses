# The Three Levels of Government — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Each of the four states governs its own affairs through its own state government, close to its people's language, land and custom.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that the FSM governs at three levels — national, state and municipal — and each level has its own work to do — and that it has no connection to the rest of the FSM's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside the FSM's one long story of self-government.
