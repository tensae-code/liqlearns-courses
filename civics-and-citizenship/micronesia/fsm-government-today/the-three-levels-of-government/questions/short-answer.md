# The Three Levels of Government — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "The national level" teach?
**Answer:** The national government serves the whole federation — the affairs the four states share and cannot handle island by island.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** The FSM governs at three levels — national, state and municipal — and each level has its own work to do.
