# The Three Levels of Government — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's first teaching — The national level?
a) The national government serves the whole federation — the affairs the four states share and cannot handle island by island.  b) it has nothing to do with how the FSM governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The national government serves the whole federation — the affairs the four states share and cannot handle island by island.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) the FSM governs at three levels — national, state and municipal — and each level has its own work to do  b) government began only when foreigners arrived  c) citizens should never question leaders  d) all islands govern in exactly the same way
**Answer: a) the FSM governs at three levels — national, state and municipal — and each level has its own work to do** — That is what this lesson teaches.
