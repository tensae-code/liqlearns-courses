# The Three Levels of Government

## Do it first

List three services you used this week — say a road, a school and a clinic. Beside each, guess which level of government runs it. This lesson gives you the FSM's real three-level map to check your guesses against.

## Sub lesson 1 — The national level

The national government serves the whole federation — the affairs the four states share and cannot handle island by island.

It is the level of the FSM Congress, the President and the national courts you will meet in this level's next lessons.

## Sub lesson 2 — The state level

Each of the four states governs its own affairs through its own state government, close to its people's language, land and custom.

State government is where the federation's promise is kept: unity without sameness.

## Sub lesson 3 — The municipal level

Municipal government is the closest level — the descendant of the municipalities of Level 2, handling the daily affairs of towns and villages.

The three levels are a ladder of closeness: municipal nearest daily life, national widest in reach, state between them.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the FSM governs at three levels — national, state and municipal — and each level has its own work to do. Give one real example from your own life or from the FSM you live in, and name the lesson's key words: three levels, national, state. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
