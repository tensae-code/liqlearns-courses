# Hands-on — The Three Levels of Government

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
List three services you used this week — say a road, a school and a clinic. Beside each, guess which level of government runs it. This lesson gives you the FSM's real three-level map to check your guesses against.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the FSM governs at three levels — national, state and municipal — and each level has its own work to do. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
