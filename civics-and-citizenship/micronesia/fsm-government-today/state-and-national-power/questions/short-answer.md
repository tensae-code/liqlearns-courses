# State and National Power — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "Sharing power between state and nation" teach?
**Answer:** The FSM divides power: some affairs belong to the national government, others remain with the states, and the constitution is the referee of the division.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** Power in the FSM is shared between the states and the national government — a division designed by learning from the Trust Territory's mistakes.
