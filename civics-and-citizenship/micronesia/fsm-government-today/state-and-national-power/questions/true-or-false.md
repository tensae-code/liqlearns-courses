# State and National Power — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Under the Trust Territory, power sat far away and centralised — decisions about islanders' lives were made over their heads.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that power in the FSM is shared between the states and the national government — a division designed by learning from the Trust Territory's mistakes — and that it has no connection to the rest of the FSM's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside the FSM's one long story of self-government.
