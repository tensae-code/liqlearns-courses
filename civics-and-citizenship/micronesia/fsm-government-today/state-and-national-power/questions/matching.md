# State and National Power — matching

Match each part of the lesson with its teaching.

**1.** Sharing power between state and nation — a)

**2.** The Trust Territory's mistakes — b)

**3.** The lesson built into the FSM — c)


Meanings:
a) The FSM divides power: some affairs belong to the national government, others remain with the states, and the constitution is the referee of the division.
b) Under the Trust Territory, power sat far away and centralised — decisions about islanders' lives were made over their heads.
c) The FSM's division of power is the correction: strong states, a limited national government, and the people's own levels close to home.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b; 3. c
