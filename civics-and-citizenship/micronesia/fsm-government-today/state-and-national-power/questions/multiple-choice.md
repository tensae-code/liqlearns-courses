# State and National Power — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's first teaching — Sharing power between state and nation?
a) The FSM divides power: some affairs belong to the national government, others remain with the states, and the constitution is the referee of the division.  b) it has nothing to do with how the FSM governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The FSM divides power: some affairs belong to the national government, others remain with the states, and the constitution is the referee of the division.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) power in the FSM is shared between the states and the national government — a division designed by learning from the Trust Territory's mistakes  b) government began only when foreigners arrived  c) citizens should never question leaders  d) all islands govern in exactly the same way
**Answer: a) power in the FSM is shared between the states and the national government — a division designed by learning from the Trust Territory's mistakes** — That is what this lesson teaches.
