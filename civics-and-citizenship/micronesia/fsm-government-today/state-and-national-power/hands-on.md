# Hands-on — State and National Power

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Two captains cannot steer one boat the same way at once. Write one job you would give the whole crew and one job you would leave to each small team. The FSM had to make exactly that division — this lesson tells how, and why the old way failed.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: power in the FSM is shared between the states and the national government — a division designed by learning from the Trust Territory's mistakes. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
