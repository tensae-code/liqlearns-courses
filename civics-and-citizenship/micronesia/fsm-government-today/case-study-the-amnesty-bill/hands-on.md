# Hands-on — Case Study: The Amnesty Bill

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Choose any rule change you have seen argued about — at school, in church or in your town. Write who proposed it, who argued each side, and who finally decided. Then read how the FSM did the same work on the Amnesty Bill.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the course ends by studying one real FSM law whole — the Amnesty Bill — so every branch, level and check met so far can be seen working on a single case. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
