# Video script — Case Study: The Amnesty Bill

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The course ends by studying one real FSM law whole — the Amnesty Bill — so every branch, level and check met so far can be seen working on a single case.

An amnesty forgives a defined set of past offences — the FSM Amnesty Bill proposed doing this as a matter of national law.

A bill is a proposed law: it has authors, reasons and opponents before it has any force.

Follow the bill through the machinery this level built: debated and voted in the legislative process, weighed by the executive, and argued against the constitution's limits.

Every earlier lesson appears here in working clothes — branches, checks, levels and citizens all touching one text.

A case study teaches what definitions cannot: timing, pressure, principle and compromise inside a real decision.

The course closes where citizenship lives — not in the chart of government, but in a real law, argued by real people, under the constitution the Founding Fathers wrote.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
