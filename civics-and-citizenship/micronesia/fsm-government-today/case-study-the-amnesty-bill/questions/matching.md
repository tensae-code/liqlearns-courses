# Case Study: The Amnesty Bill — matching

Match each part of the lesson with its teaching.

**1.** The bill and what it proposed — a)

**2.** The bill through the machinery — b)

**3.** What the case teaches — c)


Meanings:
a) An amnesty forgives a defined set of past offences — the FSM Amnesty Bill proposed doing this as a matter of national law.
b) Follow the bill through the machinery this level built: debated and voted in the legislative process, weighed by the executive, and argued against the constitution's limits.
c) A case study teaches what definitions cannot: timing, pressure, principle and compromise inside a real decision.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b; 3. c
