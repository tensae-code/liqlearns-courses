# Hands-on — The Courts and Traditional Justice

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Think of a wrong that was put right in your community without any court — an apology, a repayment, a reconciliation. Write what made it feel just. This lesson asks what happens when that older justice meets the FSM's courts.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the FSM Supreme Court stands at the head of the court system — and the book tests the courts against traditional justice in a real case study. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
