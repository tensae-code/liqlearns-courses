# Video script — The Courts and Traditional Justice

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The FSM Supreme Court stands at the head of the court system — and the book tests the courts against traditional justice in a real case study.

The FSM Supreme Court is the federation's highest court — the final judge of cases and of what the constitution and laws mean.

Below and beside it, the court system carries judicial power into the states and communities.

Traditional justice settles wrongs through custom — restoring relations, compensating harm and reconciling the parties, led by those custom appoints.

Its goal is a community made whole again, which is not always the same goal as a court's verdict.

The book's case study sets a real matter before both systems and watches how each handles it.

The lesson for a citizen: the FSM lives under two inheritances of justice, and wisdom is knowing what each is for.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
