# The Courts and Traditional Justice — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Traditional justice settles wrongs through custom — restoring relations, compensating harm and reconciling the parties, led by those custom appoints.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that the FSM Supreme Court stands at the head of the court system — and the book tests the courts against traditional justice in a real case study — and that it has no connection to the rest of the FSM's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside the FSM's one long story of self-government.
