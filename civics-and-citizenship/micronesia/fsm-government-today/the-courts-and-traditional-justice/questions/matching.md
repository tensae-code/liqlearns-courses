# The Courts and Traditional Justice — matching

Match each part of the lesson with its teaching.

**1.** The FSM Supreme Court — a)

**2.** Traditional justice — b)

a) The FSM Supreme Court is the federation's highest court — the final judge of cases and of what the constitution and laws mean.

c) The book's case study sets a real matter before both systems and watches how each handles it.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b; 3. c
