# Video script — Taking Part in Politics

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Government by the people only works when the people take part — voting, speaking, organising and watching those in power.

Voting chooses the representatives and, through them, the direction of the government — the act the municipalities first practised in Level 2.

A vote unused is a share of self-government handed to whoever bothers to use theirs.

Participation continues between elections: speaking on public questions, joining community action, and making one's view known to those in office.

A citizen who only votes is present one day in years; a participating citizen is present whenever the community decides.

Taking part includes watching: following what leaders do, asking for accounts, and using the checks the constitution gives the people.

Participation is the people's own check — the one all the written checks finally depend on.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
