# Taking Part in Politics — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "Voting — the citizen's first tool" teach?
**Answer:** Voting chooses the representatives and, through them, the direction of the government — the act the municipalities first practised in Level 2.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** Government by the people only works when the people take part — voting, speaking, organising and watching those in power.
