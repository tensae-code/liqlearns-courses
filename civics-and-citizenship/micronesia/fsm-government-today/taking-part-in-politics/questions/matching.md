# Taking Part in Politics — matching

Match each part of the lesson with its teaching.

**1.** Voting — the citizen's first tool — a)

**2.** Taking part between elections — b)

**3.** Watching those in power — c)


Meanings:
a) Voting chooses the representatives and, through them, the direction of the government — the act the municipalities first practised in Level 2.
b) Participation continues between elections: speaking on public questions, joining community action, and making one's view known to those in office.
c) Taking part includes watching: following what leaders do, asking for accounts, and using the checks the constitution gives the people.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b; 3. c
