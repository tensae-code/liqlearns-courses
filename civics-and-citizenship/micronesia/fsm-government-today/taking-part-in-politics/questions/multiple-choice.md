# Taking Part in Politics — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's first teaching — Voting — the citizen's first tool?
a) Voting chooses the representatives and, through them, the direction of the government — the act the municipalities first practised in Level 2.  b) it has nothing to do with how the FSM governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Voting chooses the representatives and, through them, the direction of the government — the act the municipalities first practised in Level 2.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) government by the people only works when the people take part — voting, speaking, organising and watching those in power  b) government began only when foreigners arrived  c) citizens should never question leaders  d) all islands govern in exactly the same way
**Answer: a) government by the people only works when the people take part — voting, speaking, organising and watching those in power** — That is what this lesson teaches.
