# Taking Part in Politics — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Participation continues between elections: speaking on public questions, joining community action, and making one's view known to those in office.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that government by the people only works when the people take part — voting, speaking, organising and watching those in power — and that it has no connection to the rest of the FSM's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside the FSM's one long story of self-government.
