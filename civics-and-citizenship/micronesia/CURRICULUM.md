# Civics and Citizenship — Micronesia course — CURRICULUM (the path)

One course for Micronesia (the Federated States of Micronesia)
inside the Civics and Citizenship folder, organised by **levels,
not grades**: a learner climbs when they have proved the level,
not when a school calendar says so. The school grades the books
come from appear only in each lesson's sources (provenance),
never as the course's organisation. The path follows
Micronesia's own order as its one book lays it out: one long
story of self-government — traditional leadership first, then
the road back through municipalities, the Congress and the
Constitution, then the FSM government today.

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in this
course comes from government textbooks — this country's own
Micronesian Government book — except the **Tip boxes**: a tip
in a box is from us, the people making LiqLearns. Tips are
ours; the lessons are the books'. See ABOUT.md for the full
book list, the combined-book scope, the record-only leads, and
the acceptance reckoning.

## The levels (the path, in order)

1. **How Micronesians Governed Themselves** — the traditional
   leadership systems found across Micronesia and how authority
   worked in them; the Chuuk model; the Pohnpei model, its
   titles and ranks; the Yap model; and the common themes — how
   leaders gained, held and were checked in their power.
   5 lessons, in one title: `traditional-leadership/`
   (5 lessons).
2. **The Road Back to Self-Government** — the first elections
   and the municipalities (magistrate, legislative council and
   municipal judge) as the first step to self-government;
   separation of powers; the Congress of Micronesia as the
   first islander legislature and the fight for independence;
   and the Micronesian Constitutional Convention and the
   Founding Fathers of the FSM. 4 lessons, in one title:
   `the-road-to-self-government/` (4 lessons).
3. **The FSM Government Today** — national, state and municipal
   government and what each level does; how power is shared
   between the states and the national government, with the
   lessons of the Trust Territory time; the three branches;
   checks and balances and where they strain; the FSM Supreme
   Court and traditional justice; the bureaucracy and the pull
   of personal ties; traditional leaders inside today's
   government; how citizens take part in politics; and a real
   law-making case studied whole — the FSM Amnesty Bill.
   9 lessons, in one title: `fsm-government-today/` (9 lessons).

**Totals: 18 lessons** (Level 1: 5 · Level 2: 4 · Level 3: 9 —
all from one student book).
