# Civics and Citizenship — Malaysia — curriculum

One course for Malaysia inside the Civics and Citizenship
folder, built from Malaysia's own government textbooks:
Pendidikan Moral (Moral Education), the subject taken by
non-Muslim pupils and the closest Malaysian equivalent of
a civics course — Tahun 4 (KSSR, Semakan 2017) and
Tingkatan 1–4 (KSSM) student books, all Malay, all
COMPLETE as filed, with the Tingkatan 5 student book
standing UNREAD-pending under the disk hold (see
ABOUT.md for the full book list, the reading routes, and
every label carried exactly as filed). The organisation
is by **levels, not grades**: a learner's place is found
by what they prove. The path is recommended, never
forced — a learner can jump in higher; the "start here
instead" hints point down this ladder. The grades and
forms the books come from appear only in provenance
(ABOUT.md and each lesson's `lesson/sources.md`).

The ladder follows Malaysia's own books' progression, as
the filing's ladder sketch lays it out: the child's first
society is the neighbour (Levels 1–2, the whole Tahun 4
year), then the secondary course's widening circles —
moral defined, self, family and friends (Level 3), moral
sources, community and society, rules and law (Level 4),
moral choice as one's own and the first step beyond
Malaysia (Level 5) — and finally the senior course's turn
to integrity, citizens' rights read from the Federal
Constitution, unity and sovereignty (Level 6). Level 7
is reserved for the final year, Tingkatan 5, which is
not yet read.

## Level 1 — The Neighbourhood — My First Society — 14 lessons

Tahun 4, units 1–7: the primary course's whole year is the Kejiranan (neighbourhood) theme, and its first half teaches the child to know the neighbours — their faiths, their needs, their ceremonies — and to meet them with care, help, duty, thanks, courtesy, respect and love. Civics here is the neighbourhood: the child's first society is the jiran (neighbour). Lessons: Knowing My Neighbours Faiths · Unity Among Neighbours · The Caring Attitude · Helping Each Other · Responsibility in Neighbourly Life · Carrying Out Neighbourly Duties · Valuing My Neighbours · Saying Thank You · Courtesy with Neighbours · Courtesy at Neighbours Ceremonies · Respecting My Neighbours · Respect is a Noble Practice · Loving My Neighbours · Love Drives Success.

## Level 2 — The Neighbourhood Deepened — Fairness, Honesty and Tolerance — 14 lessons

Tahun 4, units 8–14: the same neighbourhood circle deepened — fairness, courage to speak up politely, honesty, steady effort and diligence, consensus (muafakat), moderation (kesederhanaan) and tolerance (bertolak ansur) between neighbours. Lessons: Being Fair to Neighbours · Fairness Brings Peace · Bravery for the Neighbourhood · Speaking Up Politely · Honesty with Neighbours · Honesty Strengthens Relations · Steady Effort Brings Wellbeing · Diligence the Basis of Wellbeing · Consensus Brings Blessing · Working Together for Everyone's Good · Moderation Between Neighbours · Moderation in Dress · Tolerance a Praiseworthy Practice · Tolerance Settles Disputes.

## Level 3 — What Moral Is — Self, Family, School and First Law — 34 lessons

Tingkatan 1: the secondary course opens by defining moral itself against religion, community norms, rules and law; the moral person and why moral is learned; Malaysia's faiths side by side; one's own potential; family love and choosing friends; the caring school citizen, neighbourhood safety, public facilities and the 5R; the prudent teenager and the patriotic spirit; home etiquette; avoiding addiction; school rules; children's rights under the Child Act 2001; and corruption as the nation's enemy under the SPRM Act 2009. Lessons: What Moral Means · Good Right and Proper · The Moral Person · Why Be a Moral Person · Why Learn Moral · The Moral and Immoral Life · Malaysia's Faiths Side by Side · The Blessing of a Religious Life · Knowing My Own Potential · Kinds of Potential · Loving My Family · Why Family Love Matters · Choosing Friends Wisely · The Good of True Friendship · The Caring School Citizen · Making School a Good Place · Safety Where I Live · Guarding Our Neighbourhood · Public Facilities Are for All · Caring for Shared Facilities · Caring for God's Creation · The 5R in My Neighbourhood · The Prudent Teenager · The Patriotic Spirit · Etiquette at Home · Etiquette of Respecting Family · Avoiding Addiction · Smoking and the Law · Why School Rules Exist · What School Rules Build · The Rights of Children · The Child's Right to Grow · Corruption the Nations Enemy · Corruption Offences.

## Level 4 — Where Morals Come From — the Caring Society, Services and Rights — 29 lessons

Tingkatan 2: the sources of morals — religion, belief, community norms, customs and taboos, and the Rukun Negara as a shared moral guide; faith shaping character; duties to oneself and in the family; manners in friendship; neighbourly life; the caring society and people in need; public service agencies; protecting the environment and the bodies that guard nature; a healthy and safe life; courtesy in public places; cyber ethics and Malaysia's cyber laws; road rules against road laws; identity conflict; and the human rights of women, consumers, disabled people and workers. Lessons: Sources of Morals · Rukun Negara as a Moral Guide · Faith Shapes Character · Life Without a Moral Anchor · Responsibility to Myself · Keeping Myself Safe · My Duties in the Family · What Happens When Family Duties Fail · Good Manners in Friendship · Why Manners Keep Friendships · Living with Neighbours · Being a Good Neighbour · The Caring Society · People in Need Come First · Public Service Agencies · Valuing Public Services · Protecting Our Environment · Government Bodies That Guard Nature · A Healthy and Safe Life · Cleanliness and a Positive Mind · Public Places and Shared Facilities · Courtesy in Public Places · Ethics in Cyberspace · Cyber Laws of Malaysia · Road Rules and Road Laws · Identity Conflict · The Teenage Years and Identity · Rights of Women and Consumers · Rights of Disabled People and Workers.

## Level 5 — My Own Moral Choice — Traditions, Unity, Speech and Humanity — 28 lessons

Tingkatan 3: moral choice becomes the pupil's own, to be defended and practised; nature as God's gift; self-excellence; the family traditions of every community and conflict between friends; living united and its challenges; quality public service and client charters; the green earth; social responsibility; courtesy at ceremonies; child crime and its prevention; freedom of speech and its legal limits; the traditions of a plural society; and international humanitarian law — the ladder's first step beyond Malaysia. ## Level 6 — Integrity and the Nation — Rights, Unity and Sovereignty — 24 lessons

Tingkatan 4 (Bidang 5–7: Insan Bermoral, Jati Diri Moral, Moral dan Kenegaraan): community norms; noble character; justice and care as the principles of moral decisions; ethical use of ICT; integrity in the person and the family; being humane; citizens' rights and responsibilities read from the Federal Constitution, and their limits; unity and who builds it; ethical spending and the budget; the uniqueness of Malaysia's people as national wealth; and sovereignty under the constitutional monarchy as a shared citizen duty.
