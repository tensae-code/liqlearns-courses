# Courtesy with Neighbours — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Courtesy with neighbours, sung in the song of high courtesy (Amalan Hemah Tinggi) — polite speech keeps relations close.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that Courtesy with Neighbours — and that it has no connection to the rest of Malaysia's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Malaysia's own course, in its own order.
