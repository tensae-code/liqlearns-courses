# Carrying Out Neighbourly Duties — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Carrying out neighbourly responsibilities in real situations (watching a neighbour's pet fish) and the trust it builds.  b) it has nothing to do with how Malaysia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Carrying out neighbourly responsibilities in real situations (watching a neighbour's pet fish) and the trust it builds.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

a) Carrying out neighbourly responsibilities in real situations (watching a neighbour's pet fish) and the trust it builds.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Malaysia
**Answer: a) Carrying out neighbourly responsibilities in real situations (watching a neighbour's pet fish) and the trust it builds.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
