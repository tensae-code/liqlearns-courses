# Tolerance Settles Disputes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Tolerance as the settlement of neighbour disputes.  b) it has nothing to do with how Malaysia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Tolerance as the settlement of neighbour disputes.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Tolerance as the settlement of neighbour disputes.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Malaysia
**Answer: a) Tolerance as the settlement of neighbour disputes.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
