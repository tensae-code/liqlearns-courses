# Honesty Strengthens Relations — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Honesty tightens neighbourly relations.  b) it has nothing to do with how Malaysia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Honesty tightens neighbourly relations.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Honesty tightens neighbourly relations.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Malaysia
**Answer: a) Honesty tightens neighbourly relations.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
