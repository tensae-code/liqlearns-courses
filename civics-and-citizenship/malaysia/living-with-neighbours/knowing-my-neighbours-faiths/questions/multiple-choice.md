# Knowing My Neighbours Faiths — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Knowing the religious practices of one's neighbours — stealing is forbidden in every religion or belief.  b) it has nothing to do with how Malaysia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Knowing the religious practices of one's neighbours — stealing is forbidden in every religion or belief.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Knowing the religious practices of one's neighbours — stealing is forbidden in every religion or belief.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Malaysia
**Answer: a) Knowing the religious practices of one's neighbours — stealing is forbidden in every religion or belief.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
