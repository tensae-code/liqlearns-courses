# Global Society Norms

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Global Society Norms. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Malaysia's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

What the global society is and what its norms are — the behaviour of the world's people based on social rules that are accepted and shared together, understanding and respecting other cultures and communicating across them.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: What the global society is and what its norms are — the behaviour of the world's people based on social rules that are accepted and shared together, understanding and respecting other cultures and communicating across them. Give one real example from your own life or from the Malaysia you live in, and name the lesson's key words: global, society, norms. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
