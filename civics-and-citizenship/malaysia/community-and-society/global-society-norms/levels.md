# Global Society Norms — depth

This lesson is ONE lesson with no sub lessons: it teaches a single thing, whole. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: What the global society is and what its norms are — the behaviour of the world's people based on social rules that are accepted and shared together, understanding and respecting other cultures and communicating across them — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
