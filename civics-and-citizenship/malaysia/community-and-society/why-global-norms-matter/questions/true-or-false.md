# Why Global Norms Matter — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Why practising global-society norms matters — it lifts the world economy and technology, forms humane behaviour and upholds human rights and care for the earth — and what neglecting those norms brings: discrimination, crime and a threatened environment.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that Why Global Norms Matter — and that it has no connection to the rest of Malaysia's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Malaysia's own course, in its own order.
