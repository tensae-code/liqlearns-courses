# Why Global Norms Matter — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** Why practising global-society norms matters — it lifts the world economy and technology, forms humane behaviour and upholds human rights and care for the earth — and what neglecting those norms brings: discrimination, crime and a threatened environment.
