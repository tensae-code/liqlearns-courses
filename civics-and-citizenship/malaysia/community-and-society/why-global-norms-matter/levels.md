# Why Global Norms Matter — depth

This lesson is ONE lesson with no sub lessons: it teaches a single thing, whole. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: Why practising global-society norms matters — it lifts the world economy and technology, forms humane behaviour and upholds human rights and care for the earth — and what neglecting those norms brings: discrimination, crime and a threatened environment — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
