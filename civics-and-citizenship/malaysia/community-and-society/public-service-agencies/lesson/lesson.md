# Public Service Agencies

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Public Service Agencies. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Malaysia's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

What public service agencies are and Malaysia's own — courts, police, immigration, hospitals, the anti-corruption commission.
