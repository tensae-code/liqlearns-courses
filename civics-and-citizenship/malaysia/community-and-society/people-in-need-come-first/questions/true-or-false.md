# People in Need Come First — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Groups in need (the homeless, the poor, former addicts, former prisoners, people with AIDS) and why they deserve priority help and acceptance.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that People in Need Come First — and that it has no connection to the rest of Malaysia's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Malaysia's own course, in its own order.
