# Safety Where I Live — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Safety where one lives as everyone's worry and everyone's shared responsibility, not the guards' alone.  b) it has nothing to do with how Malaysia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Safety where one lives as everyone's worry and everyone's shared responsibility, not the guards' alone.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Safety where one lives as everyone's worry and everyone's shared responsibility, not the guards' alone.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Malaysia
**Answer: a) Safety where one lives as everyone's worry and everyone's shared responsibility, not the guards' alone.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
