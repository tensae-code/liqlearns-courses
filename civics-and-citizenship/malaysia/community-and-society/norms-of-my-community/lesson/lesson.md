# Norms of My Community

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Norms of My Community. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Malaysia's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Every ethnic community in Malaysia has its own norms, practised inside the group and respected across groups.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Every ethnic community in Malaysia has its own norms, practised inside the group and respected across groups. Give one real example from your own life or from the Malaysia you live in, and name the lesson's key words: norms, of, my. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: Every ethnic community in Malaysia has its own norms, practised inside the group and respected across groups.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
