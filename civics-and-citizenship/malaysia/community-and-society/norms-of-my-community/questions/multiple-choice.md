# Norms of My Community — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Every ethnic community in Malaysia has its own norms, practised inside the group and respected across groups.  b) it has nothing to do with how Malaysia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Every ethnic community in Malaysia has its own norms, practised inside the group and respected across groups.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Every ethnic community in Malaysia has its own norms, practised inside the group and respected across groups.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Malaysia
**Answer: a) Every ethnic community in Malaysia has its own norms, practised inside the group and respected across groups.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
