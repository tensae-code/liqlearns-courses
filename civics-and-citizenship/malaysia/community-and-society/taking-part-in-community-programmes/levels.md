# Taking Part in Community Programmes — depth

This lesson is ONE lesson with no sub lessons: it teaches a single thing, whole. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: How to take part in community programmes — register as a volunteer, give time and energy, donate basic needs, share knowledge and skill, promote the programme and encourage family and friends to join — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
