# Public Facilities Are for All — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Malaysia's public facilities and what each gives the community — hospital, LRT, Urban Transformation Centre, library, playground, community hall.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that Public Facilities Are for All — and that it has no connection to the rest of Malaysia's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Malaysia's own course, in its own order.
