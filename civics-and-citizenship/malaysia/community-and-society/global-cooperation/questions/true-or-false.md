# Global Cooperation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Examples of cooperation in the global society — student exchanges and scholarships in education, expert and innovation exchanges in science and technology, trade cooperation in the economy, and peace and safety missions.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that Global Cooperation — and that it has no connection to the rest of Malaysia's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Malaysia's own course, in its own order.
