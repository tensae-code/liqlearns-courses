# Being a Good Neighbour — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Being a courteous neighbour — respecting and tolerating neighbours whatever their attitude, keeping harmony.  b) it has nothing to do with how Malaysia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Being a courteous neighbour — respecting and tolerating neighbours whatever their attitude, keeping harmony.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Being a courteous neighbour — respecting and tolerating neighbours whatever their attitude, keeping harmony.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Malaysia
**Answer: a) Being a courteous neighbour — respecting and tolerating neighbours whatever their attitude, keeping harmony.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
