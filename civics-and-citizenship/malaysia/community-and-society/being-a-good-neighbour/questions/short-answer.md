# Being a Good Neighbour — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** Being a courteous neighbour — respecting and tolerating neighbours whatever their attitude, keeping harmony.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** Being a courteous neighbour — respecting and tolerating neighbours whatever their attitude, keeping harmony.
