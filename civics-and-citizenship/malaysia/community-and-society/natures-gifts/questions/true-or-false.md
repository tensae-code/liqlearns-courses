# Natures Gifts — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Nature as God's gift — bounty, blessing and grace, from nutritious fruit to caves as national heritage.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that Natures Gifts — and that it has no connection to the rest of Malaysia's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Malaysia's own course, in its own order.
