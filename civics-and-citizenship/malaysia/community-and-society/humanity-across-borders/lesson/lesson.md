# Humanity Across Borders

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Humanity Across Borders. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Malaysia's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Humane conduct for global wellbeing in action — protecting human rights and helping the less fortunate, care and concern for disaster victims, loving the environment worldwide and standing for world peace.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Humane conduct for global wellbeing in action — protecting human rights and helping the less fortunate, care and concern for disaster victims, loving the environment worldwide and standing for world peace. Give one real example from your own life or from the Malaysia you live in, and name the lesson's key words: humanity, across, borders. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: Humane conduct for global wellbeing in action — protecting human rights and helping the less fortunate, care and concern for disaster victims, loving the environment worldwide and standing for world peace.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
