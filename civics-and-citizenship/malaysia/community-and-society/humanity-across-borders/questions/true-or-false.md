# Humanity Across Borders — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Humane conduct for global wellbeing in action — protecting human rights and helping the less fortunate, care and concern for disaster victims, loving the environment worldwide and standing for world peace.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that Humanity Across Borders — and that it has no connection to the rest of Malaysia's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Malaysia's own course, in its own order.
