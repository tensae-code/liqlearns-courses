# Humane for Global Wellbeing — depth

This lesson is ONE lesson with no sub lessons: it teaches a single thing, whole. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: The traits of being humane for global wellbeing — valuing life, loving and respecting fellow creatures and their rights, empathy and sympathy for the world community, loving world peace and keeping the balance of the ecosystem — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
