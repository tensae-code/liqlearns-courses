# The 5R in My Neighbourhood — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The 5R programme in the neighbourhood — gotong-royong clean-ups and planting flowers to beautify the area.  b) it has nothing to do with how Malaysia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The 5R programme in the neighbourhood — gotong-royong clean-ups and planting flowers to beautify the area.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

a) The 5R programme in the neighbourhood — gotong-royong clean-ups and planting flowers to beautify the area.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Malaysia
**Answer: a) The 5R programme in the neighbourhood — gotong-royong clean-ups and planting flowers to beautify the area.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
