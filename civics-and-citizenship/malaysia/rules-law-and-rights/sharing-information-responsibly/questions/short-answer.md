# Sharing Information Responsibly — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** The proper way to use sources and spread information — check that it is true, get confirmation from those concerned, name the source, avoid sensitive issues and personal attacks, and never hack, plagiarise or add to what was said.
