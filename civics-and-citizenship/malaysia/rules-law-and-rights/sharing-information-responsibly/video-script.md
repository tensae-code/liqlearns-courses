# Video script — Sharing Information Responsibly

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

This lesson is about one thing.
The proper way to use sources and spread information — check that it is true, get confirmation from those concerned, name the source, avoid sensitive issues and personal attacks, and never hack, plagiarise or add to what was said.
