# Trustworthy Sources — depth

This lesson is ONE lesson with no sub lessons: it teaches a single thing, whole. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: Which sources and information are trustworthy for the wellbeing of all — research reports, encyclopaedias, government circulars, scholarly books and recognised websites and people in authority — and that false information harms society if it is not stopped — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
