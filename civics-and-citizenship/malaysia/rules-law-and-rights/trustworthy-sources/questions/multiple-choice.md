# Trustworthy Sources — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Which sources and information are trustworthy for the wellbeing of all — research reports, encyclopaedias, government circulars, scholarly books and recognised websites and people in authority — and that false information harms society if it is not stopped.  b) it has nothing to do with how Malaysia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Which sources and information are trustworthy for the wellbeing of all — research reports, encyclopaedias, government circulars, scholarly books and recognised websites and people in authority — and that false information harms society if it is not stopped.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
