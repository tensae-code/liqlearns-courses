# Trustworthy Sources — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** Which sources and information are trustworthy for the wellbeing of all — research reports, encyclopaedias, government circulars, scholarly books and recognised websites and people in authority — and that false information harms society if it is not stopped.
