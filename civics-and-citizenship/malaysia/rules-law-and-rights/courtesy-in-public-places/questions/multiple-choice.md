# Courtesy in Public Places — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Courteous behaviour in public — dress properly, bin litter, use facilities well; hedonistic behaviour named as its opposite.  b) it has nothing to do with how Malaysia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Courteous behaviour in public — dress properly, bin litter, use facilities well; hedonistic behaviour named as its opposite.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

a) Courteous behaviour in public — dress properly, bin litter, use facilities well; hedonistic behaviour named as its opposite.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Malaysia
**Answer: a) Courteous behaviour in public — dress properly, bin litter, use facilities well; hedonistic behaviour named as its opposite.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
