# Cyber Laws of Malaysia

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Cyber Laws of Malaysia. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Malaysia's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.
