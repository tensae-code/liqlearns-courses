# What Unethical Technology Use Brings

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: What Unethical Technology Use Brings. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Malaysia's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

The problems that follow when ICT ethics are ignored, and every party's shared responsibility for ethical use, including copyright.
