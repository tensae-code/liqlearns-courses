# Corruption the Nations Enemy

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Corruption the Nations Enemy. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Malaysia's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

What corruption (rasuah) is — a bribe (suapan) in money, gifts, posts, loans or services — under the Malaysian Anti-Corruption Commission Act 2009, with its street names named.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: What corruption (rasuah) is — a bribe (suapan) in money, gifts, posts, loans or services — under the Malaysian Anti-Corruption Commission Act 2009, with its street names named. Give one real example from your own life or from the Malaysia you live in, and name the lesson's key words: corruption, the, nations. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
