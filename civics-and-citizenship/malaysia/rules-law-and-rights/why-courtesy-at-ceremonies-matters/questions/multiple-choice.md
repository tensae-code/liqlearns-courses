# Why Courtesy at Ceremonies Matters — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) When courtesy is kept the ceremony runs smoothly; when it is not, everyone is discomforted.  b) it has nothing to do with how Malaysia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) When courtesy is kept the ceremony runs smoothly; when it is not, everyone is discomforted.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) When courtesy is kept the ceremony runs smoothly; when it is not, everyone is discomforted.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Malaysia
**Answer: a) When courtesy is kept the ceremony runs smoothly; when it is not, everyone is discomforted.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
