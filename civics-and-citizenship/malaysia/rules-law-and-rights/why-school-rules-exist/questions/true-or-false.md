# Why School Rules Exist — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** What school rules are for — order, harmony and smooth running — and what a school without rules looks like: dirt, sickness, fear, fighting, vandalism, bullying.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that Why School Rules Exist — and that it has no connection to the rest of Malaysia's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Malaysia's own course, in its own order.
