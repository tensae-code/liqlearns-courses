# Rights of Women and Consumers

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Rights of Women and Consumers. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Malaysia's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Human rights protected together — women's rights (education, work, promotion, protection, voice) and consumers' rights (basic needs, safe goods, information, choice).

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Human rights protected together — women's rights (education, work, promotion, protection, voice) and consumers' rights (basic needs, safe goods, information, choice). Give one real example from your own life or from the Malaysia you live in, and name the lesson's key words: rights, of, women. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
