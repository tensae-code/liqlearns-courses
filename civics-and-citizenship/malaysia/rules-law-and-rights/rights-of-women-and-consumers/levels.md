# Rights of Women and Consumers — depth

This lesson is ONE lesson with no sub lessons: it teaches a single thing, whole. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: Human rights protected together — women's rights (education, work, promotion, protection, voice) and consumers' rights (basic needs, safe goods, information, choice) — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
