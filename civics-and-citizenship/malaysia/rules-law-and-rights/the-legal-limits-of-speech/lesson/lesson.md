# The Legal Limits of Speech

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: The Legal Limits of Speech. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Malaysia's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

The legal limits on free speech in Malaysia, the acts that protect the right, and using the right channels so conflicts end courteously.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The legal limits on free speech in Malaysia, the acts that protect the right, and using the right channels so conflicts end courteously. Give one real example from your own life or from the Malaysia you live in, and name the lesson's key words: the, legal, limits. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
