# Road Rules and Road Laws

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Road Rules and Road Laws. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Malaysia's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

The difference between road rules (safety practices) and road laws (acts that compel, with penalties), with examples of each — licences, speed limits, seat belts, helmets.
