# Road Rules and Road Laws — depth

This lesson is ONE lesson with no sub lessons: it teaches a single thing, whole. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: The difference between road rules (safety practices) and road laws (acts that compel, with penalties), with examples of each — licences, speed limits, seat belts, helmets — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
