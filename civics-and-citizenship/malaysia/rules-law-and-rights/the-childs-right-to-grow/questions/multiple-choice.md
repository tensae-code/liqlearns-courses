# The Child's Right to Grow — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Children's development rights — the right to learn at school and to grow up healthy.  b) it has nothing to do with how Malaysia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Children's development rights — the right to learn at school and to grow up healthy.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Children's development rights — the right to learn at school and to grow up healthy.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Malaysia
**Answer: a) Children's development rights — the right to learn at school and to grow up healthy.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
