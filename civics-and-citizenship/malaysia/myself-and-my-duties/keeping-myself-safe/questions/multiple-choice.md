# Keeping Myself Safe — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Keeping oneself safe — not trusting strangers, not exposing personal details online, not walking alone at night.  b) it has nothing to do with how Malaysia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Keeping oneself safe — not trusting strangers, not exposing personal details online, not walking alone at night.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Keeping oneself safe — not trusting strangers, not exposing personal details online, not walking alone at night.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Malaysia
**Answer: a) Keeping oneself safe — not trusting strangers, not exposing personal details online, not walking alone at night.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
