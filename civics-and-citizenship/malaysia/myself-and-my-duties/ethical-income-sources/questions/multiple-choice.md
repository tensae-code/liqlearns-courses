# Ethical Income Sources — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The sources of money that are obtained ethically — salary, wages and bonus, bank savings, recognised shares and dividends, insurance, rent and property investment.  b) it has nothing to do with how Malaysia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
