# Managing Money Ethically — depth

This lesson is ONE lesson with no sub lessons: it teaches a single thing, whole. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: How to manage money ethically — plan income and spending prudently, save for emergencies and old age, join only recognised investment schemes, borrow only within one's means and repay loans steadily — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
