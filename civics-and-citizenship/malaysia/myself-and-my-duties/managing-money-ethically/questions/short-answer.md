# Managing Money Ethically — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** How to manage money ethically — plan income and spending prudently, save for emergencies and old age, join only recognised investment schemes, borrow only within one's means and repay loans steadily.
