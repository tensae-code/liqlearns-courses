# Kinds of Potential — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Kinds of potential — the creative mind, fluent speech, the gift for drawing.  b) it has nothing to do with how Malaysia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Kinds of potential — the creative mind, fluent speech, the gift for drawing.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Kinds of potential — the creative mind, fluent speech, the gift for drawing.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Malaysia
**Answer: a) Kinds of potential — the creative mind, fluent speech, the gift for drawing.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
