# The Limits of Rights — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Citizens' rights have limits so no one acts as they please; rights and duties must be guarded to keep Malaysian identity.  b) it has nothing to do with how Malaysia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Citizens' rights have limits so no one acts as they please; rights and duties must be guarded to keep Malaysian identity.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Citizens' rights have limits so no one acts as they please; rights and duties must be guarded to keep Malaysian identity.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Malaysia
**Answer: a) Citizens' rights have limits so no one acts as they please; rights and duties must be guarded to keep Malaysian identity.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
