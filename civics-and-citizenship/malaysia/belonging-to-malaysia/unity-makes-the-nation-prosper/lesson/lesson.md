# Unity Makes the Nation Prosper

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Unity Makes the Nation Prosper. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Malaysia's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Unity (perpaduan) as the uniting of society into one national identity and shared values.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Unity (perpaduan) as the uniting of society into one national identity and shared values. Give one real example from your own life or from the Malaysia you live in, and name the lesson's key words: unity, makes, the. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
