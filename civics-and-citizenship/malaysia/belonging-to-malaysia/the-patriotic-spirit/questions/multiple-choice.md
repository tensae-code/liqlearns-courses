# The Patriotic Spirit — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The patriotic spirit — pride in being Malaysian, guarding the nation's dignity, readiness to sacrifice and defend sovereignty.  b) it has nothing to do with how Malaysia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The patriotic spirit — pride in being Malaysian, guarding the nation's dignity, readiness to sacrifice and defend sovereignty.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
