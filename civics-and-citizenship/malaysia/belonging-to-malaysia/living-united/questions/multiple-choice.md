# Living United — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Living united (hidup bersatu padu) in multi-ethnic Malaysia — the practice that keeps the country peaceful and harmonious.  b) it has nothing to do with how Malaysia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Living united (hidup bersatu padu) in multi-ethnic Malaysia — the practice that keeps the country peaceful and harmonious.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
