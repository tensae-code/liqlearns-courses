# Malaysia Excels in the World's Eyes — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Examples of Malaysia's success that are a pride on the world stage — industry and food products reaching world markets, hosting world sport and world champions, the national astronaut programme and MEASAT satellites, world-class transport, ecotourism and UNESCO world heritage sites.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that Malaysia Excels in the World's Eyes — and that it has no connection to the rest of Malaysia's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Malaysia's own course, in its own order.
