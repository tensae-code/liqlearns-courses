# Malaysia Excels in the World's Eyes

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Malaysia Excels in the World's Eyes. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Malaysia's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Examples of Malaysia's success that are a pride on the world stage — industry and food products reaching world markets, hosting world sport and world champions, the national astronaut programme and MEASAT satellites, world-class transport, ecotourism and UNESCO world heritage sites.
