# Malaysia Excels in the World's Eyes — depth

This lesson is ONE lesson with no sub lessons: it teaches a single thing, whole. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: Examples of Malaysia's success that are a pride on the world stage — industry and food products reaching world markets, hosting world sport and world champions, the national astronaut programme and MEASAT satellites, world-class transport, ecotourism and UNESCO world heritage sites — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
