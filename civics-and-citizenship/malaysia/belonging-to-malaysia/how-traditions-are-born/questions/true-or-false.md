# How Traditions Are Born — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Today's traditions began as norms before they were accepted as tradition — the duty to stay proud of and keep them.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that How Traditions Are Born — and that it has no connection to the rest of Malaysia's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Malaysia's own course, in its own order.
