# Malaysia and the World — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** What international relations mean — the ties and interaction between the countries of the world, and between countries and international organisations — and Malaysia's foreign policy of protecting its sovereignty and interests while fostering understanding between nations.
