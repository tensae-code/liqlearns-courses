# Malaysia and the World — depth

This lesson is ONE lesson with no sub lessons: it teaches a single thing, whole. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: What international relations mean — the ties and interaction between the countries of the world, and between countries and international organisations — and Malaysia's foreign policy of protecting its sovereignty and interests while fostering understanding between nations — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
