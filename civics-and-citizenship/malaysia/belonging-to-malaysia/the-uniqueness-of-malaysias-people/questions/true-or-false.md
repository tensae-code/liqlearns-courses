# The Uniqueness of Malaysia's People — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Malaysia's chief treasure is the uniqueness of its people — every citizen's duty to defend and keep that diversity.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that The Uniqueness of Malaysia's People — and that it has no connection to the rest of Malaysia's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Malaysia's own course, in its own order.
