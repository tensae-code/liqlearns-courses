# The Uniqueness of Malaysia's People — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Malaysia's chief treasure is the uniqueness of its people — every citizen's duty to defend and keep that diversity.  b) it has nothing to do with how Malaysia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Malaysia's chief treasure is the uniqueness of its people — every citizen's duty to defend and keep that diversity.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

a) Malaysia's chief treasure is the uniqueness of its people — every citizen's duty to defend and keep that diversity.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Malaysia
**Answer: a) Malaysia's chief treasure is the uniqueness of its people — every citizen's duty to defend and keep that diversity.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
