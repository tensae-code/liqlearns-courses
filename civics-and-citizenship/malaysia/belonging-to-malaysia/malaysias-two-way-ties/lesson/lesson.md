# Malaysia's Two-Way Ties

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Malaysia's Two-Way Ties. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Malaysia's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Malaysia's bilateral relations with other countries — guarding sovereignty and human rights under international law, good political ties and leaders' visits, global trade, foreign investment and technology sharing, humanitarian help and cross-cultural sharing.
