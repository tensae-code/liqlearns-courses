# Growing Spiritual Values — depth

This lesson is ONE lesson with no sub lessons: it teaches a single thing, whole. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: How to grow spiritual values — making the teachings of one's religion or belief a guide to life, practising them daily, keeping good relations with others, caring for the environment and obeying and being good to one's parents — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
