# Doing My Duty with Integrity — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** How to carry out one's duty with integrity for the nation's building — refuse to give or take bribes, pay taxes and repay loans, obey the client charter and the laws, and foster integrity in every line of work.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that Doing My Duty with Integrity — and that it has no connection to the rest of Malaysia's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Malaysia's own course, in its own order.
