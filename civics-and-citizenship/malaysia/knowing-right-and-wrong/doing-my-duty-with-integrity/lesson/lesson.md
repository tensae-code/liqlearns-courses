# Doing My Duty with Integrity

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Doing My Duty with Integrity. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Malaysia's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

How to carry out one's duty with integrity for the nation's building — refuse to give or take bribes, pay taxes and repay loans, obey the client charter and the laws, and foster integrity in every line of work.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: How to carry out one's duty with integrity for the nation's building — refuse to give or take bribes, pay taxes and repay loans, obey the client charter and the laws, and foster integrity in every line of work. Give one real example from your own life or from the Malaysia you live in, and name the lesson's key words: doing, my, duty, with, integrity. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: How to carry out one's duty with integrity for the nation's building — refuse to give or take bribes, pay taxes and repay loans, obey the client charter and the laws, and foster integrity in every line of work.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
