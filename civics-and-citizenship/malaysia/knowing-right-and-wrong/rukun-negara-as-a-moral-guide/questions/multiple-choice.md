# Rukun Negara as a Moral Guide — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Customs and taboos (pantang larang, e.g. of the Melanau) and the Rukun Negara as shared moral references for all Malaysians.  b) it has nothing to do with how Malaysia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Customs and taboos (pantang larang, e.g. of the Melanau) and the Rukun Negara as shared moral references for all Malaysians.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
