# Why Be a Moral Person — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Why being a moral person matters — for oneself, for others and for the nation.  b) it has nothing to do with how Malaysia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Why being a moral person matters — for oneself, for others and for the nation.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Why being a moral person matters — for oneself, for others and for the nation.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Malaysia
**Answer: a) Why being a moral person matters — for oneself, for others and for the nation.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
