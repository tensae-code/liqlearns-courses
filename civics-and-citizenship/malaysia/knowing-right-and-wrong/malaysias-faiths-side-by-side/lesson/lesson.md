# Malaysia's Faiths Side by Side

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Malaysia's Faiths Side by Side. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Malaysia's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Malaysia's faiths side by side through their children — Islam, Hinduism, Buddhism, Christianity and Sikhism as practised at home and in worship.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Malaysia's faiths side by side through their children — Islam, Hinduism, Buddhism, Christianity and Sikhism as practised at home and in worship. Give one real example from your own life or from the Malaysia you live in, and name the lesson's key words: malaysia's, faiths, side. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
