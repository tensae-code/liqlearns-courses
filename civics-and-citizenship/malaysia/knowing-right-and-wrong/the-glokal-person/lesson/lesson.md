# The Glokal Person

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: The Glokal Person. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Malaysia's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

The traits of a glokal person — someone who can compete at global level while staying rooted in their own local identity: competitive, well-connected, strong in identity and skilled in information technology.
