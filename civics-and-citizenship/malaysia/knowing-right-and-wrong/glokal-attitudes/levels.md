# Glokal Attitudes — depth

This lesson is ONE lesson with no sub lessons: it teaches a single thing, whole. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: The attitudes of a glokal person — putting their own culture first, mastering many languages without abandoning the national language and mother tongue, preferring local products and guarding the country's good name abroad — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
