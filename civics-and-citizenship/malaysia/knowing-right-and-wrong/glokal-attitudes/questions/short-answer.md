# Glokal Attitudes — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** The attitudes of a glokal person — putting their own culture first, mastering many languages without abandoning the national language and mother tongue, preferring local products and guarding the country's good name abroad.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** The attitudes of a glokal person — putting their own culture first, mastering many languages without abandoning the national language and mother tongue, preferring local products and guarding the country's good name abroad.
