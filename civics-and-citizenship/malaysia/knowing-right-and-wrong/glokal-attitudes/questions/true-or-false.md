# Glokal Attitudes — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The attitudes of a glokal person — putting their own culture first, mastering many languages without abandoning the national language and mother tongue, preferring local products and guarding the country's good name abroad.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that Glokal Attitudes — and that it has no connection to the rest of Malaysia's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Malaysia's own course, in its own order.
