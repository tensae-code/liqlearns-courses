# Why Organisational Integrity Matters

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Why Organisational Integrity Matters. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Malaysia's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Why integrity matters in an organisation — it builds ethical behaviour, accountability, quality service and public trust — and what its absence brings: lost trust, falling quality and a damaged image.

Give one real example from your own life or from the Malaysia you live in, and name the lesson's key words: why, organisational, integrity, matters. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
