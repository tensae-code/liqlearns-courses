# Why Organisational Integrity Matters — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Why integrity matters in an organisation — it builds ethical behaviour, accountability, quality service and public trust — and what its absence brings: lost trust, falling quality and a damaged image.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that Why Organisational Integrity Matters — and that it has no connection to the rest of Malaysia's story.
