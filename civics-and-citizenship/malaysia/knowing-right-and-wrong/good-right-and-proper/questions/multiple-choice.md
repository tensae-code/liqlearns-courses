# Good Right and Proper — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The difference between behaviour that is good, behaviour that is right and behaviour that is proper.  b) it has nothing to do with how Malaysia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The difference between behaviour that is good, behaviour that is right and behaviour that is proper.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) The difference between behaviour that is good, behaviour that is right and behaviour that is proper.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Malaysia
**Answer: a) The difference between behaviour that is good, behaviour that is right and behaviour that is proper.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
