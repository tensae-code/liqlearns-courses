# Spiritual Values Shape Character

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Spiritual Values Shape Character. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Malaysia's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

The traits of a person who holds spiritual values — holding firmly to their religion or belief, fair, honest, brave in upholding truth, loving themselves, respecting others and grateful for life.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The traits of a person who holds spiritual values — holding firmly to their religion or belief, fair, honest, brave in upholding truth, loving themselves, respecting others and grateful for life. Give one real example from your own life or from the Malaysia you live in, and name the lesson's key words: spiritual, values, shape, character. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.

3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
