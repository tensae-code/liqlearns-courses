# Self Excellence — depth

This lesson is ONE lesson with no sub lessons: it teaches a single thing, whole. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: Self-excellence (kebestarian diri) — reason as God's gift to humankind, and the traits of the excellent self: mature, active, knowledgeable, diligent, independent — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
