# Self Excellence — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Self-excellence (kebestarian diri) — reason as God's gift to humankind, and the traits of the excellent self: mature, active, knowledgeable, diligent, independent.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that Self Excellence — and that it has no connection to the rest of Malaysia's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Malaysia's own course, in its own order.
