# Standing by a Moral Choice — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Why moral choices must be made, and that a moral choice must be defended and practised continuously.  b) it has nothing to do with how Malaysia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Why moral choices must be made, and that a moral choice must be defended and practised continuously.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Why moral choices must be made, and that a moral choice must be defended and practised continuously.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Malaysia
**Answer: a) Why moral choices must be made, and that a moral choice must be defended and practised continuously.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
