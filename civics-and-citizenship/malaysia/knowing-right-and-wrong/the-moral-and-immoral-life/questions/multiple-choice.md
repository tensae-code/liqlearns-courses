# The Moral and Immoral Life — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The moral way of life against the immoral way of life, and practising moral conduct daily.  b) it has nothing to do with how Malaysia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The moral way of life against the immoral way of life, and practising moral conduct daily.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) The moral way of life against the immoral way of life, and practising moral conduct daily.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Malaysia
**Answer: a) The moral way of life against the immoral way of life, and practising moral conduct daily.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
