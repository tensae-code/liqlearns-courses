# Integrity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Integrity — accountability and firmness in every layer of society, from the individual to the career.  b) it has nothing to do with how Malaysia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Integrity — accountability and firmness in every layer of society, from the individual to the career.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Integrity — accountability and firmness in every layer of society, from the individual to the career.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Malaysia
**Answer: a) Integrity — accountability and firmness in every layer of society, from the individual to the career.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
