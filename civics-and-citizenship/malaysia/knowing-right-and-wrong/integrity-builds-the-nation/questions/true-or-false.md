# Integrity Builds the Nation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Examples of integrity for the building of a nation — honest administration that fights corruption and guards state secrets, honest taxes and use of public facilities in the economy, and law enforcement with no exceptions in social life.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that Integrity Builds the Nation — and that it has no connection to the rest of Malaysia's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Malaysia's own course, in its own order.
