# Integrity Builds the Nation

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Integrity Builds the Nation. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Malaysia's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Examples of integrity for the building of a nation — honest administration that fights corruption and guards state secrets, honest taxes and use of public facilities in the economy, and law enforcement with no exceptions in social life.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Examples of integrity for the building of a nation — honest administration that fights corruption and guards state secrets, honest taxes and use of public facilities in the economy, and law enforcement with no exceptions in social life. Give one real example from your own life or from the Malaysia you live in, and name the lesson's key words: integrity, builds, the, nation. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
