# What Happens When Family Duties Fail — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The effects of neglecting family duties — quarrels, a damaged family name, a society that looks down, lost happiness.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that What Happens When Family Duties Fail — and that it has no connection to the rest of Malaysia's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Malaysia's own course, in its own order.
