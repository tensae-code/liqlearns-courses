# My Duties in the Family — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Each family member's responsibilities — safety, welfare, love, respect for elders, guarding the family name.  b) it has nothing to do with how Malaysia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Each family member's responsibilities — safety, welfare, love, respect for elders, guarding the family name.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Each family member's responsibilities — safety, welfare, love, respect for elders, guarding the family name.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Malaysia
**Answer: a) Each family member's responsibilities — safety, welfare, love, respect for elders, guarding the family name.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
