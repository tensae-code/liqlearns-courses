# Family Traditions Are Treasure — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** What a family tradition is — a practice inherited down the generations — with the Chinese family's reunion dinner as a worked example.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that Family Traditions Are Treasure — and that it has no connection to the rest of Malaysia's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Malaysia's own course, in its own order.
