# Family Traditions Are Treasure

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Family Traditions Are Treasure. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Malaysia's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

What a family tradition is — a practice inherited down the generations — with the Chinese family's reunion dinner as a worked example.

## Say it to your companion
