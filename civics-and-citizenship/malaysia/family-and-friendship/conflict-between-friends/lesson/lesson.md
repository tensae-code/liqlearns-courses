# Conflict Between Friends

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Conflict Between Friends. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Malaysia's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

The kinds of conflict in friendship — personality conflicts and ethnic, religious and cultural conflicts.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The kinds of conflict in friendship — personality conflicts and ethnic, religious and cultural conflicts. Give one real example from your own life or from the Malaysia you live in, and name the lesson's key words: conflict, between, friends. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
