# The Good of True Friendship — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The good of positive friendship — friends who correct, advise and push each other toward success.  b) it has nothing to do with how Malaysia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The good of positive friendship — friends who correct, advise and push each other toward success.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) The good of positive friendship — friends who correct, advise and push each other toward success.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Malaysia
**Answer: a) The good of positive friendship — friends who correct, advise and push each other toward success.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
