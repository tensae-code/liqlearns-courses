# Video script — Obeying the Norms

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Obeying the norms — what norms bind a citizen and the consequences of breaking them; the human being as God's creature, an individual and a social being — the ground of norms; what a norm is; the kinds of norms (religious, decency, courtesy and legal norms); acting in accordance with the norms in daily life.
