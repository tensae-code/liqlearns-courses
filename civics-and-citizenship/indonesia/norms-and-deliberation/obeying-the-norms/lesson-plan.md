# Lesson plan — Obeying the Norms

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Indonesia · **Title:** Norms and Deliberation · **Lesson:** Obeying the Norms
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 10 — Pancasila Deepened — Foundation, Worldview and Ideology (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Obeying the norms — what norms bind a citizen and the consequences of breaking them; the human being as God's creature, an individual and a social being — the ground of norms; what a norm is; the kinds of norms (religious, decency, courtesy and legal norms); acting in accordance with the norms in daily life. This lesson stands on a student book, with some parts on teacher-guide evidence marked TEACHER in lesson/sources.md (see there).
**This lesson's own level:** Level 10 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.
