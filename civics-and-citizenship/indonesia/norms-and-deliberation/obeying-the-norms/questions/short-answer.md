# Obeying the Norms — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** Obeying the norms — what norms bind a citizen and the consequences of breaking them; the human being as God's creature, an individual and a social being — the ground of norms; what a norm is; the kinds of norms (religious, decency, courtesy and legal norms); acting in accordance with the norms in daily life.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
