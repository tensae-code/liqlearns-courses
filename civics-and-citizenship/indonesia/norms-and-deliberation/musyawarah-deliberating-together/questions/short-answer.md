# Musyawarah Deliberating Together — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** Musyawarah — deliberating together to reach a decision everyone accepts — and why it matters in family, school and community; musyawarah for mufakat (consensus) — its benefits and the steps of a deliberation.
