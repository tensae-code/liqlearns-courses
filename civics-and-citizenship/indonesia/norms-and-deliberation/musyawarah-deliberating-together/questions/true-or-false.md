# Musyawarah Deliberating Together — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Musyawarah — deliberating together to reach a decision everyone accepts — and why it matters in family, school and community; musyawarah for mufakat (consensus) — its benefits and the steps of a deliberation.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that Musyawarah Deliberating Together — and that it has no connection to the rest of Indonesia's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Indonesia's own course, in its own order.
