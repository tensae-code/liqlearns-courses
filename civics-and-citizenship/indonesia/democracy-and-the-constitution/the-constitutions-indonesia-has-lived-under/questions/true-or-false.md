# The Constitutions Indonesia Has Lived Under — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The periods of Indonesia's constitutions — UUD 1945 (1945–49), the RIS constitution (1949–50), the provisional UUDS (1950–59), and UUD 1945 restored (1959–99).
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that The Constitutions Indonesia Has Lived Under — and that it has no connection to the rest of Indonesia's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Indonesia's own course, in its own order.
