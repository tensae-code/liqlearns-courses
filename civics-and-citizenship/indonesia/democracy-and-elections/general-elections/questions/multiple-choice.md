# General Elections — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) General elections (Pemilu) — how Indonesia chooses its leaders and representatives.  b) it has nothing to do with how Indonesia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) General elections (Pemilu) — how Indonesia chooses its leaders and representatives.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) General elections (Pemilu) — how Indonesia chooses its leaders and representatives.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Indonesia
**Answer: a) General elections (Pemilu) — how Indonesia chooses its leaders and representatives.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
