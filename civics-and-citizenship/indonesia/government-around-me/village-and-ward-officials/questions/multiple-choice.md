# Village and Ward Officials — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The village and urban-ward officials (perangkat desa/kelurahan) — who they are and what each does for the community.  b) it has nothing to do with how Indonesia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The village and urban-ward officials (perangkat desa/kelurahan) — who they are and what each does for the community.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) The village and urban-ward officials (perangkat desa/kelurahan) — who they are and what each does for the community.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Indonesia
**Answer: a) The village and urban-ward officials (perangkat desa/kelurahan) — who they are and what each does for the community.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
