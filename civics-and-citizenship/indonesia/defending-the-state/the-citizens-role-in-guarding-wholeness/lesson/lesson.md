# The Citizens Role in Guarding Wholeness

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: The Citizens Role in Guarding Wholeness. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Indonesia's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Citizens' participation in guarding the wholeness of the Pancasila ideology and the NKRI.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Citizens' participation in guarding the wholeness of the Pancasila ideology and the NKRI. Give one real example from your own life or from the Indonesia you live in, and name the lesson's key words: the citizens. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
