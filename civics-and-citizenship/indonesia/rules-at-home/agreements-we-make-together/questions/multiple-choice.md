# Agreements We Make Together — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Class agreements — rules we decide together (kesepakatan) and then keep together; making rules together for school and neighbourhood and carrying them out.  b) it has nothing to do with how Indonesia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
