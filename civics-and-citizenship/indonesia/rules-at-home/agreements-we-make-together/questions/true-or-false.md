# Agreements We Make Together — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Class agreements — rules we decide together (kesepakatan) and then keep together; making rules together for school and neighbourhood and carrying them out.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that Agreements We Make Together — and that it has no connection to the rest of Indonesia's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Indonesia's own course, in its own order.
