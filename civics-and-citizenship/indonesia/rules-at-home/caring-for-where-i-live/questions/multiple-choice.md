# Caring for Where I Live — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Caring for the home environment — cleaning and looking after the place you live.  b) it has nothing to do with how Indonesia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Caring for the home environment — cleaning and looking after the place you live.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Caring for the home environment — cleaning and looking after the place you live.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Indonesia
**Answer: a) Caring for the home environment — cleaning and looking after the place you live.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
