# Rules in My Family — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Every family has rules (waking, bathing, teeth, helping) and the child identifies the rules of her own home.  b) it has nothing to do with how Indonesia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Every family has rules (waking, bathing, teeth, helping) and the child identifies the rules of her own home.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Every family has rules (waking, bathing, teeth, helping) and the child identifies the rules of her own home.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Indonesia
**Answer: a) Every family has rules (waking, bathing, teeth, helping) and the child identifies the rules of her own home.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
