# Stories of Obeying Rules — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Telling and hearing stories of children who obey rules, and what happens when rules are broken.  b) it has nothing to do with how Indonesia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Telling and hearing stories of children who obey rules, and what happens when rules are broken.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Telling and hearing stories of children who obey rules, and what happens when rules are broken.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Indonesia
**Answer: a) Telling and hearing stories of children who obey rules, and what happens when rules are broken.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
