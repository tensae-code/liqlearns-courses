# My Attitude to Rules — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The child's own attitude to rules — obeying without being told twice, at home and in play.  b) it has nothing to do with how Indonesia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The child's own attitude to rules — obeying without being told twice, at home and in play.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) The child's own attitude to rules — obeying without being told twice, at home and in play.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Indonesia
**Answer: a) The child's own attitude to rules — obeying without being told twice, at home and in play.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
