# Keeping Tradition in a Global World — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?

**Answer: a) Committing to guard Indonesian tradition and culture while living globally.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?

**Answer: a) Committing to guard Indonesian tradition and culture while living globally.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
