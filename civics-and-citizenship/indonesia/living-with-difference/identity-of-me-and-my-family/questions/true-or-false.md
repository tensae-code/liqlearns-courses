# Identity of Me and My Family — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** My identity and my family's and friends' identities — culture, ethnic group, language, religion and belief protected by the state — named and respected.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that Identity of Me and My Family — and that it has no connection to the rest of Indonesia's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Indonesia's own course, in its own order.
