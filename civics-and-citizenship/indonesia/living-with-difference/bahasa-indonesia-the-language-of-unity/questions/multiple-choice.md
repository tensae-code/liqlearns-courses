# Bahasa Indonesia the Language of Unity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Bahasa Indonesia as the language of unity that lets hundreds of ethnic groups speak to each other; bahasa Indonesia as the language of unity in community life.  b) it has nothing to do with how Indonesia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Bahasa Indonesia as the language of unity that lets hundreds of ethnic groups speak to each other; bahasa Indonesia as the language of unity in community life.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

a) Bahasa Indonesia as the language of unity that lets hundreds of ethnic groups speak to each other; bahasa Indonesia as the language of unity in community life.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Indonesia
**Answer: a) Bahasa Indonesia as the language of unity that lets hundreds of ethnic groups speak to each other; bahasa Indonesia as the language of unity in community life.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
