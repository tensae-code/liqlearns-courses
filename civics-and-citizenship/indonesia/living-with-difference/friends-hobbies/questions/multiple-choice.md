# Friends Hobbies — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Friends have different hobbies and interests, and that difference is enjoyed, not mocked.  b) it has nothing to do with how Indonesia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Friends have different hobbies and interests, and that difference is enjoyed, not mocked.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Friends have different hobbies and interests, and that difference is enjoyed, not mocked.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Indonesia
**Answer: a) Friends have different hobbies and interests, and that difference is enjoyed, not mocked.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
