# Different But Together — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Physical and family differences among friends, and staying together across them.  b) it has nothing to do with how Indonesia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Physical and family differences among friends, and staying together across them.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Physical and family differences among friends, and staying together across them.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Indonesia
**Answer: a) Physical and family differences among friends, and staying together across them.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
