# The Wealth of My Ethnic Group

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: The Wealth of My Ethnic Group. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Indonesia's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

The riches of Indonesia's ethnic groups — houses, dress, food, arts — as national wealth.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The riches of Indonesia's ethnic groups — houses, dress, food, arts — as national wealth. Give one real example from your own life or from the Indonesia you live in, and name the lesson's key words: the wealth. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: The riches of Indonesia's ethnic groups — houses, dress, food, arts — as national wealth.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
