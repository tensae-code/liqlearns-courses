# Lesson plan — Diversity in the Frame of Bhinneka Tunggal Ika

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Indonesia · **Title:** Living With Difference · **Lesson:** Diversity in the Frame of Bhinneka Tunggal Ika
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 10 — Pancasila Deepened — Foundation, Worldview and Ideology (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The Indonesian nation's diversity held within the frame of Bhinneka Tunggal Ika (unity in diversity); the meaning of unity within diversity; unity across ethnic groups, religions, races and social groups in society; the factors that cause social change and diversity; challenges and attitudes toward cultural change at local, national and global levels; diversity held in the frame of Bhinneka Tunggal Ika (unity in diversity). This lesson stands on a student book, with some parts on teacher-guide evidence marked TEACHER in lesson/sources.md (see there).
**This lesson's own level:** Level 10 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.
