# Video script — Diversity in the Frame of Bhinneka Tunggal Ika

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The Indonesian nation's diversity held within the frame of Bhinneka Tunggal Ika (unity in diversity); the meaning of unity within diversity; unity across ethnic groups, religions, races and social groups in society; the factors that cause social change and diversity; challenges and attitudes toward cultural change at local, national and global levels; diversity held in the frame of Bhinneka Tunggal Ika (unity in diversity).
