# The Wealth of Indonesian Culture — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Indonesian culture across the islands as one shared wealth.  b) it has nothing to do with how Indonesia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Indonesian culture across the islands as one shared wealth.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Indonesian culture across the islands as one shared wealth.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Indonesia
**Answer: a) Indonesian culture across the islands as one shared wealth.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
