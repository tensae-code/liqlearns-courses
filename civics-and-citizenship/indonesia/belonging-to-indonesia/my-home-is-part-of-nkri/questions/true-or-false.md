# My Home Is Part of NKRI — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The place I live is part of the Unitary State of the Republic of Indonesia (NKRI) — my neighbourhood sits inside a province inside one country.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that My Home Is Part of NKRI — and that it has no connection to the rest of Indonesia's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Indonesia's own course, in its own order.
