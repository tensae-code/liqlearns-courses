# Gotong Royong for Unity and Bela Negara — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Gotong royong as the guard of unity and as everyday bela negara (defending the country), at home in the neighbourhood.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that Gotong Royong for Unity and Bela Negara — and that it has no connection to the rest of Indonesia's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Indonesia's own course, in its own order.
