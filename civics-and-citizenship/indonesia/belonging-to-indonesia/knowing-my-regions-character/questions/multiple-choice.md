# Knowing My Regions Character — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The characteristics of my region — its landscape, livelihoods and people.  b) it has nothing to do with how Indonesia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations

**Answer: a) The characteristics of my region — its landscape, livelihoods and people.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
