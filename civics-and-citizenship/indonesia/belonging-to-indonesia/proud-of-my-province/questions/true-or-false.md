# Proud of My Province — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Pride in my own province joined to respect for other provinces.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that Proud of My Province — and that it has no connection to the rest of Indonesia's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Indonesia's own course, in its own order.
