# Getting Rights Doing Duties — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Receiving my rights and doing my obligations as two sides of one life.  b) it has nothing to do with how Indonesia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Receiving my rights and doing my obligations as two sides of one life.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Receiving my rights and doing my obligations as two sides of one life.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Indonesia
**Answer: a) Receiving my rights and doing my obligations as two sides of one life.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
