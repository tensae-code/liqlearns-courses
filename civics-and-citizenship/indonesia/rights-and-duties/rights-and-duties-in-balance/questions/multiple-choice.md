# Rights and Duties in Balance — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Carrying out citizens' rights and obligations in balance.  b) it has nothing to do with how Indonesia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Carrying out citizens' rights and obligations in balance.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Carrying out citizens' rights and obligations in balance.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Indonesia
**Answer: a) Carrying out citizens' rights and obligations in balance.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
