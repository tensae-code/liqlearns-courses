# Violations of Rights and Denial of Duties — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** Cases of rights violations and duty-denial — the rights-duties relation, the causes of violation, and rights and duties as members of global society; violations of rights and denial of obligations measured against the Constitution; designing solution-minded ideas for rights-violation problems — the "generasi solutif" (solution generation) stance.
