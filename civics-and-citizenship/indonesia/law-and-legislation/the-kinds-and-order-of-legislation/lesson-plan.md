# Lesson plan — The Kinds and Order of Legislation

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Indonesia · **Title:** Law and Legislation · **Lesson:** The Kinds and Order of Legislation
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 11 — The Constitution and the Ladder of Laws (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The kinds and order of legislation — UUD 1945, TAP MPR, UU/Perppu, government regulation, presidential regulation, provincial and regency/city regional regulations; legal products and the hierarchy of legislation at full senior level, including harmonisation/synchronisation of regulations and Pancasila's relation to legislation. This lesson stands on a student book (see lesson/sources.md).
**This lesson's own level:** Level 11 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.
