# The Kinds and Order of Legislation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The kinds and order of legislation — UUD 1945, TAP MPR, UU/Perppu, government regulation, presidential regulation, provincial and regency/city regional regulations; legal products and the hierarchy of legislation at full senior level, including harmonisation/synchronisation of regulations and Pancasila's relation to legislation.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that The Kinds and Order of Legislation — and that it has no connection to the rest of Indonesia's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Indonesia's own course, in its own order.
