# The Kinds and Order of Legislation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** The kinds and order of legislation — UUD 1945, TAP MPR, UU/Perppu, government regulation, presidential regulation, provincial and regency/city regional regulations; legal products and the hierarchy of legislation at full senior level, including harmonisation/synchronisation of regulations and Pancasila's relation to legislation.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** The kinds and order of legislation — UUD 1945, TAP MPR, UU/Perppu, government regulation, presidential regulation, provincial and regency/city regional regulations; legal products and the hierarchy of legislation at full senior level, including harmonisation/synchronisation of regulations and Pancasila's relation to legislation.
