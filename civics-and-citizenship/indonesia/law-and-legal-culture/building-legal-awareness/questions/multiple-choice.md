# Building Legal Awareness — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Legal awareness — what it is, the purpose of law, and the sources of law.  b) it has nothing to do with how Indonesia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Legal awareness — what it is, the purpose of law, and the sources of law.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Legal awareness — what it is, the purpose of law, and the sources of law.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Indonesia
**Answer: a) Legal awareness — what it is, the purpose of law, and the sources of law.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
