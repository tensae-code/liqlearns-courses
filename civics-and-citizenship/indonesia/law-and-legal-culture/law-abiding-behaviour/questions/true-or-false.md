Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Law-abiding behaviour — law's relation to norms, the substance of norm enforcement, the divisions of law, and norms that realise justice based on Pancasila.

**2.** This lesson teaches that Law Abiding Behaviour — and that it has no connection to the rest of Indonesia's story.
