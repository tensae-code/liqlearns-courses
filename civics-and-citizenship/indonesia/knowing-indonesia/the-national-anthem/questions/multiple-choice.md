# The National Anthem — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Indonesia Raya as the national anthem, learned by singing and standing respectfully.  b) it has nothing to do with how Indonesia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Indonesia Raya as the national anthem, learned by singing and standing respectfully.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Indonesia Raya as the national anthem, learned by singing and standing respectfully.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Indonesia
**Answer: a) Indonesia Raya as the national anthem, learned by singing and standing respectfully.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
