# Learning From Heroes — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** Taking Indonesia's heroes as models of Pancasila behaviour; the formulators of Pancasila as exemplars — their conduct during the 1945 formulation taken as a model; modelling one's behaviour on Pancasila conduct shown by others.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
