# Video script — Learning From Heroes

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Taking Indonesia's heroes as models of Pancasila behaviour; the formulators of Pancasila as exemplars — their conduct during the 1945 formulation taken as a model; modelling one's behaviour on Pancasila conduct shown by others.
