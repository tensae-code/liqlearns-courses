# The Four Elements of the Subject — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** The subject's four-element architecture at Fase A — Pancasila (flag, anthem, Garuda symbols and sila, the formulators of Pancasila, applying its values at home), UUD NRI 1945 (rules in the family), Bhinneka Tunggal Ika (own and others' identities), NKRI — as the outcome map for grades 1–2; the Fase A outcome map restated for grade 2, tying each chapter to the four elements (Pancasila, UUD NRI 1945, Bhinneka Tunggal Ika, NKRI); the four-element outcome map at Fase B (grades 3–4), where identity, home-as-part-of-NKRI, school rules and rights/obligations enter; the Fase B learning-outcome flow (ATP) completed at grade 4, mapping the four elements across both Fase B years; the four-element outcome map at Fase C (grades 5–6), where the norm system and deliberation join the earlier elements; the Fase C outcome map completed at grade 6 — the four elements at their primary-school fullest (norms, rights and obligations in national life; provinces of the NKRI; deliberation); the four-element outcome map at Fase D as it lands in grade 8 — the UUD NRI 1945 element carrying the constitution and the hierarchy of legislation; the four-element outcome map at the close of Fase D — Pancasila, Constitution, Bhinneka Tunggal Ika and NKRI taught as one related system; the four-element outcome map at Fase E (senior secondary), where legal culture and the citizen's standing join the elements; the four-element outcome map at Fase F (grades 11–12) — constitutional history, harmony in diversity and the forms of the state; the four-element outcome map completed at the end of Fase F — the whole school course's outcomes in one frame.
