# Lesson plan — The Four Elements of the Subject

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Indonesia · **Title:** Knowing Indonesia · **Lesson:** The Four Elements of the Subject
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — Myself, My Friends and the Subject's Frame (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The subject's four-element architecture at Fase A — Pancasila (flag, anthem, Garuda symbols and sila, the formulators of Pancasila, applying its values at home), UUD NRI 1945 (rules in the family), Bhinneka Tunggal Ika (own and others' identities), NKRI — as the outcome map for grades 1–2; the Fase A outcome map restated for grade 2, tying each chapter to the four elements (Pancasila, UUD NRI 1945, Bhinneka Tunggal Ika, NKRI); the four-element outcome map at Fase B (grades 3–4), where identity, home-as-part-of-NKRI, school rules and rights/obligations enter; the Fase B learning-outcome flow (ATP) completed at grade 4, mapping the four elements across both Fase B years; the four-element outcome map at Fase C (grades 5–6), where the norm system and deliberation join the earlier elements; the Fase C outcome map completed at grade 6 — the four elements at their primary-school fullest (norms, rights and obligations in national life; provinces of the NKRI; deliberation); the four-element outcome map at Fase D as it lands in grade 8 — the UUD NRI 1945 element carrying the constitution and the hierarchy of legislation; the four-element outcome map at the close of Fase D — Pancasila, Constitution, Bhinneka Tunggal Ika and NKRI taught as one related system; the four-element outcome map at Fase E (senior secondary), where legal culture and the citizen's standing join the elements; the four-element outcome map at Fase F (grades 11–12) — constitutional history, harmony in diversity and the forms of the state; the four-element outcome map completed at the end of Fase F — the whole school course's outcomes in one frame. This lesson stands on teacher-guide evidence only (see lesson/sources.md) — acceptance 0, labelled.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.
