# Hands-on — How Pancasila Was Formulated

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: How Pancasila Was Formulated. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Indonesia's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: The history of Pancasila's formulation in 1945 — the sessions and speeches by which the five sila were agreed; the birth of Pancasila — the 1945 sessions and the speeches from which the five sila were born; the birth of Pancasila as history — the BPUPK sessions, the Panitia Sembilan (Committee of Nine) formulation, the proclamation and the stipulation of Pancasila as the foundation of the state; the dynamics of Pancasila's birth — the argument inside the BPUPK session, the formulation, and the ratification; the birth of Pancasila in the BPUPK (Investigating Committee) sessions of 1945; the Committee of Nine's formulation of Pancasila (the Jakarta Charter); the proclamation of independence and the stipulation of Pancasila as the foundation of the state. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
