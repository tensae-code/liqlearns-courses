# How Pancasila Was Formulated — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The history of Pancasila's formulation in 1945 — the sessions and speeches by which the five sila were agreed; the birth of Pancasila — the 1945 sessions and the speeches from which the five sila were born; the birth of Pancasila as history — the BPUPK sessions, the Panitia Sembilan (Committee of Nine) formulation, the proclamation and the stipulation of Pancasila as the foundation of the state; the dynamics of Pancasila's birth — the argument inside the BPUPK session, the formulation, and the ratification; the birth of Pancasila in the BPUPK (Investigating Committee) sessions of 1945; the Committee of Nine's formulation of Pancasila (the Jakarta Charter); the proclamation of independence and the stipulation of Pancasila as the foundation of the state.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that How Pancasila Was Formulated — and that it has no connection to the rest of Indonesia's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Indonesia's own course, in its own order.
