# Video script — How Pancasila Was Formulated

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The history of Pancasila's formulation in 1945 — the sessions and speeches by which the five sila were agreed; the birth of Pancasila — the 1945 sessions and the speeches from which the five sila were born; the birth of Pancasila as history — the BPUPK sessions, the Panitia Sembilan (Committee of Nine) formulation, the proclamation and the stipulation of Pancasila as the foundation of the state; the dynamics of Pancasila's birth — the argument inside the BPUPK session, the formulation, and the ratification; the birth of Pancasila in the BPUPK (Investigating Committee) sessions of 1945; the Committee of Nine's formulation of Pancasila (the Jakarta Charter); the proclamation of independence and the stipulation of Pancasila as the foundation of the state.
