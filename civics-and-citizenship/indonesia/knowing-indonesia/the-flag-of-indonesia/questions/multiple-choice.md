# The Flag of Indonesia — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The red-and-white flag as Indonesia's flag, its colours and respectful treatment.  b) it has nothing to do with how Indonesia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The red-and-white flag as Indonesia's flag, its colours and respectful treatment.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) The red-and-white flag as Indonesia's flag, its colours and respectful treatment.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Indonesia
**Answer: a) The red-and-white flag as Indonesia's flag, its colours and respectful treatment.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
