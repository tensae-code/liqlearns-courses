# Video script — Pancasila Values in Daily Life

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Practising the meaning of each sila in daily life at home, school and community; the meaning of each sila as it shows up in community life; attitudes and behaviour that show Pancasila practised in society; making Pancasila behaviour a habit in daily life; the values of the five sila are one interconnected goodness, practised as a whole in daily life and in society; learning to practise Pancasila deliberately — a personal programme, letters, and never discriminating between friends; inviting friends to practise Pancasila — through one's own work and through caring; applying Pancasila's values in the life of a junior-secondary pupil, sila by sila; the meaning of each of the five sila; applying Pancasila in the life of society; applying Pancasila in the life of the nation and the state.
