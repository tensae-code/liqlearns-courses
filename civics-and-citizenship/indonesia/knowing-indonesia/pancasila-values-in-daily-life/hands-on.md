# Hands-on — Pancasila Values in Daily Life

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Pancasila Values in Daily Life. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Indonesia's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Practising the meaning of each sila in daily life at home, school and community; the meaning of each sila as it shows up in community life; attitudes and behaviour that show Pancasila practised in society; making Pancasila behaviour a habit in daily life; the values of the five sila are one interconnected goodness, practised as a whole in daily life and in society; learning to practise Pancasila deliberately — a personal programme, letters, and never discriminating between friends; inviting friends to practise Pancasila — through one's own work and through caring; applying Pancasila's values in the life of a junior-secondary pupil, sila by sila; the meaning of each of the five sila; applying Pancasila in the life of society; applying Pancasila in the life of the nation and the state. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
