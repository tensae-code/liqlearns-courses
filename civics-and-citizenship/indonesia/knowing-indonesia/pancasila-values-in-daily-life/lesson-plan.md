# Lesson plan — Pancasila Values in Daily Life

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Indonesia · **Title:** Knowing Indonesia · **Lesson:** Pancasila Values in Daily Life
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Knowing Indonesia — Flag, Anthem, Garuda and Pancasila (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Practising the meaning of each sila in daily life at home, school and community; the meaning of each sila as it shows up in community life; attitudes and behaviour that show Pancasila practised in society; making Pancasila behaviour a habit in daily life; the values of the five sila are one interconnected goodness, practised as a whole in daily life and in society; learning to practise Pancasila deliberately — a personal programme, letters, and never discriminating between friends; inviting friends to practise Pancasila — through one's own work and through caring; applying Pancasila's values in the life of a junior-secondary pupil, sila by sila; the meaning of each of the five sila; applying Pancasila in the life of society; applying Pancasila in the life of the nation and the state. This lesson stands on a student book, with some parts on teacher-guide evidence marked TEACHER in lesson/sources.md (see there).
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.
