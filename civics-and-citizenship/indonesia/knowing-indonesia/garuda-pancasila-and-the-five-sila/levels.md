# Garuda Pancasila and the Five Sila — depth

This lesson is ONE lesson with no sub lessons: it teaches a single thing, whole. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: Garuda Pancasila as the state emblem, the motto Bhinneka Tunggal Ika on its ribbon, and the five sila matched to their symbols, learned through the song "Garuda Pancasila" and a clapping game — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
