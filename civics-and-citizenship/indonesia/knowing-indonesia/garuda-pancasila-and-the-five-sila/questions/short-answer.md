# Garuda Pancasila and the Five Sila — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** Garuda Pancasila as the state emblem, the motto Bhinneka Tunggal Ika on its ribbon, and the five sila matched to their symbols, learned through the song "Garuda Pancasila" and a clapping game.
