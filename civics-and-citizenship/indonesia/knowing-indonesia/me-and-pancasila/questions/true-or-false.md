# Me and Pancasila — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The pupil's own relation to Pancasila, examined personally.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that Me and Pancasila — and that it has no connection to the rest of Indonesia's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Indonesia's own course, in its own order.
