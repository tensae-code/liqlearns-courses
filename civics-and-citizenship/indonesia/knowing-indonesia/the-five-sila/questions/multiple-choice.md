# The Five Sila — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The wording of the five sila in order, recited and matched to daily life.  b) it has nothing to do with how Indonesia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The wording of the five sila in order, recited and matched to daily life.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) The wording of the five sila in order, recited and matched to daily life.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Indonesia
**Answer: a) The wording of the five sila in order, recited and matched to daily life.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
