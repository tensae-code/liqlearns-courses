# Pancasila as Worldview and Unifier — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Pancasila as the nation's way of life (pandangan hidup) and as the unifier of the nation, practised by pupils; pancasila as the nation's worldview lived personally.  b) it has nothing to do with how Indonesia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Pancasila as the nation's way of life (pandangan hidup) and as the unifier of the nation, practised by pupils; pancasila as the nation's worldview lived personally.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Pancasila as the nation's way of life (pandangan hidup) and as the unifier of the nation, practised by pupils; pancasila as the nation's worldview lived personally.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Indonesia
**Answer: a) Pancasila as the nation's way of life (pandangan hidup) and as the unifier of the nation, practised by pupils; pancasila as the nation's worldview lived personally.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
