# Expressing Opinion in Practice

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Expressing Opinion in Practice. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Indonesia's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Practising the expression of opinion responsibly.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Practising the expression of opinion responsibly. Give one real example from your own life or from the Indonesia you live in, and name the lesson's key words: expressing opinion. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
