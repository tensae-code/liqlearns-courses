# Civics and Citizenship — Indonesia course — CURRICULUM (the path)

One course for Indonesia inside the Civics and Citizenship folder,
organised by **levels, not grades**: a learner climbs when they
have proved the level, not when a school calendar says so. The
school grades the books come from appear only in each lesson's
sources (provenance), never as the course's organisation. The
path follows Indonesia's own books' widening circles, as
sketched at the ends of the filings: the child, her rules and
symbols, her neighbourhood, difference, belonging, rights, the
village state, norms and deliberation — then in secondary the
same four elements (Pancasila, UUD NRI 1945, Bhinneka Tunggal
Ika, NKRI) deepened year by year until the pupil exits as a
first-time voter.

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in this
course comes from government textbooks — this country's own
Pendidikan Pancasila books — except the **Tip boxes**: a tip in
a box is from us, the people making LiqLearns. Tips are ours;
the lessons are the books'. See ABOUT.md for the full book
list, every gap and label carried exactly as filed, and the
acceptance reckoning.

## The levels (the path, in order)

1. **Myself, My Friends and the Subject's Frame** — the grade-1 opening: myself, my friends, honest play; the Profil Pelajar Pancasila and the subject's four elements named by the teacher guides from the start. 5 lessons (6 placements filed at the level).

2. **Rules at Home, at School and Around Me** — rules in the family, in play, at school, and agreements we make together (grades 1–4): rules are the child's first law. 9 lessons (10 placements filed at the level).

3. **Knowing Indonesia — Flag, Anthem, Garuda and Pancasila** — flag, anthem, Garuda and the five sila, their symbols and meanings, the formulators and heroes, Pancasila's formulation history and its values practised at home (grades 1–6, the primary spine). 12 lessons (22 placements filed at the level).

4. **My Neighbourhood, My School and the Environment** — my neighbourhood, my school, caring for the environment, gotong royong in practice (grades 1–6). 9 lessons (11 placements filed at the level).

5. **Living with Difference** — hobbies, bodies, religions, ethnic and regional cultures, Bahasa Indonesia as unity's language, tolerance and preservation (grades 2–6, deepened in secondary). 14 lessons (15 placements filed at the level).

6. **Belonging to Indonesia — Home and Province in the NKRI** — my home and province inside the NKRI, unity as habit, gotong royong as bela negara (grades 3–6). 6 lessons (8 placements filed at the level).

7. **Rights and Duties** — hak dan kewajiban first named at home and school (grade 3), balanced through primary. 4 lessons (4 placements filed at the level).

8. **Government Around Me — the Village and Ward Officials** — the village and ward officials (grade 4): the first rung of the state, met as neighbours. 1 lessons (1 placements filed at the level).

9. **Norms and Deliberation** — the four kinds of norms, norms in national life, and musyawarah for mufakat (grades 5–6): primary school closes with deliberation. 6 lessons (9 placements filed at the level).

10. **Pancasila Deepened — Foundation, Worldview and Ideology** — Fase D opening (grades 7–8): Pancasila's formulation and values taught at argument level, norms obeyed, diversity in the frame of Bhinneka Tunggal Ika, the territory of the unitary state, and tradition and local wisdom preserved. 8 lessons (30 placements filed at the level).

11. **The Constitution and the Ladder of Laws** — grade 8: the 1945 Constitution's making and standing, the full hierarchy of legislation, Wawasan Nusantara. 10 lessons (11 placements filed at the level).

12. **The Four Elements as One System** — grade 9: Pancasila ↔ Constitution ↔ Bhinneka Tunggal Ika ↔ NKRI; citizens' rights read from the Constitution; freedom of expression in the information era; guarding territorial wholeness. 22 lessons (23 placements filed at the level).

13. **Legal Culture and the Citizen's Standing** — grade 10, Fase E: legal awareness and law-abiding culture; the citizen's position and tasks; the defence system; Indonesia among the nations. 13 lessons (18 placements filed at the level).

14. **Constitutions, Harmony and Conflict, and the Forms of the State** — grade 11, Fase F: the constitutions Indonesia has lived under and their amendment; harmony and conflict resolution in diversity; ATHG; forms of state, government and government system. 14 lessons (17 placements filed at the level).

15. **State Institutions, Elections and Indonesia in the World** — grade 12: the state institutions traced; the nation in global life under Pancasila's guidance; solution-generation against rights violations; the course ends with Pemilu and the first-time voter. 16 lessons (21 placements filed at the level).
