# Knowing My Friends — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Recognising friends' differences, including boys and girls and friends from other islands/regions, as the normal starting point of friendship.  b) it has nothing to do with how Indonesia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Recognising friends' differences, including boys and girls and friends from other islands/regions, as the normal starting point of friendship.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
