# Rules for Playing Together — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Games have rules everyone follows; playing honestly and accepting losing are part of playing together.  b) it has nothing to do with how Indonesia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Games have rules everyone follows; playing honestly and accepting losing are part of playing together.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Games have rules everyone follows; playing honestly and accepting losing are part of playing together.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Indonesia
**Answer: a) Games have rules everyone follows; playing honestly and accepting losing are part of playing together.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
