# Rules for Playing Together — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Games have rules everyone follows; playing honestly and accepting losing are part of playing together.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that Rules for Playing Together — and that it has no connection to the rest of Indonesia's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Indonesia's own course, in its own order.
