# History of Our School — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Every school has a history like a family does; pupils reconstruct theirs from elders, past pupils and records.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that History of Our School — and that it has no connection to the rest of Sri Lanka's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Sri Lanka's own course, in its own order.
