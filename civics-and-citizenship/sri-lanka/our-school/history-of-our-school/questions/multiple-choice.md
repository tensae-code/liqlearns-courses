# History of Our School — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Every school has a history like a family does; pupils reconstruct theirs from elders, past pupils and records.  b) it has nothing to do with how Sri Lanka governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Every school has a history like a family does; pupils reconstruct theirs from elders, past pupils and records.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Every school has a history like a family does; pupils reconstruct theirs from elders, past pupils and records.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Sri Lanka
**Answer: a) Every school has a history like a family does; pupils reconstruct theirs from elders, past pupils and records.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
