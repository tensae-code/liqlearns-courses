# School and Society Bond — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The school serves and is supported by the society around it; the bond is two-way.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that School and Society Bond — and that it has no connection to the rest of Sri Lanka's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Sri Lanka's own course, in its own order.
