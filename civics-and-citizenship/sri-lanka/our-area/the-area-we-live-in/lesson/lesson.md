# The Area We Live in

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: The Area We Live in. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Sri Lanka's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

The human and physical resources of one's home area and the administrative divisions over it (district, provincial secretary's division).

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The human and physical resources of one's home area and the administrative divisions over it (district, provincial secretary's division). Give one real example from your own life or from the Sri Lanka you live in, and name the lesson's key words: the area. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
