# Appreciating Local Things — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Valuing the products and heritage of one's own locality.  b) it has nothing to do with how Sri Lanka governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Valuing the products and heritage of one's own locality.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Valuing the products and heritage of one's own locality.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Sri Lanka
**Answer: a) Valuing the products and heritage of one's own locality.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
