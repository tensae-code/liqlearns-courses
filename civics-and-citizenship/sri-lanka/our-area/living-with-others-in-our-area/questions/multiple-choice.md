# Living with Others in Our Area — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Living in harmony with the people of one's area.  b) it has nothing to do with how Sri Lanka governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Living in harmony with the people of one's area.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Living in harmony with the people of one's area.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Sri Lanka
**Answer: a) Living in harmony with the people of one's area.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
