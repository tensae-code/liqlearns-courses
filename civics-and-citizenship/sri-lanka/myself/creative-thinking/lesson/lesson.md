# Creative Thinking

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Creative Thinking. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Sri Lanka's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Looking at phenomena from different angles.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Looking at phenomena from different angles. Give one real example from your own life or from the Sri Lanka you live in, and name the lesson's key words: creative thinking. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
