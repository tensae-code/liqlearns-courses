# Who Am I — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) A child enquires into their own identity, family and personal information as the start of knowing themselves.  b) it has nothing to do with how Sri Lanka governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) A child enquires into their own identity, family and personal information as the start of knowing themselves.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) A child enquires into their own identity, family and personal information as the start of knowing themselves.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Sri Lanka
**Answer: a) A child enquires into their own identity, family and personal information as the start of knowing themselves.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
