# Using Leisure Well

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Using Leisure Well. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Sri Lanka's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Using leisure for personality development through sport and hobbies.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Using leisure for personality development through sport and hobbies. Give one real example from your own life or from the Sri Lanka you live in, and name the lesson's key words: using leisure. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
