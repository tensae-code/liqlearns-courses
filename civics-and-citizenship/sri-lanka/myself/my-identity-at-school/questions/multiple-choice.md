# My Identity at School — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) One's identity, good qualities, skills, weaknesses and the opportunities school gives to grow.  b) it has nothing to do with how Sri Lanka governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) One's identity, good qualities, skills, weaknesses and the opportunities school gives to grow.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) One's identity, good qualities, skills, weaknesses and the opportunities school gives to grow.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Sri Lanka
**Answer: a) One's identity, good qualities, skills, weaknesses and the opportunities school gives to grow.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
