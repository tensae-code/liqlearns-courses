# Human Rights — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Human rights and the duties that accompany them.  b) it has nothing to do with how Sri Lanka governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Human rights and the duties that accompany them.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Human rights and the duties that accompany them.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Sri Lanka
**Answer: a) Human rights and the duties that accompany them.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
