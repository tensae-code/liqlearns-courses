# Protection of Human Rights at Grade 11 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The human-rights protection chapter returns at Year 11 as the book's close.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that Protection of Human Rights at Grade 11 — and that it has no connection to the rest of Sri Lanka's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Sri Lanka's own course, in its own order.
