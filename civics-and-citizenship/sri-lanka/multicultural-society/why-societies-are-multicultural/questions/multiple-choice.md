# Why Societies Are Multicultural — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) How societies come to be multicultural.  b) it has nothing to do with how Sri Lanka governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) How societies come to be multicultural.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) How societies come to be multicultural.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Sri Lanka
**Answer: a) How societies come to be multicultural.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
