# Buddhist Culture

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Buddhist Culture. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Sri Lanka's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

How Buddhists use the Buddha's teaching to build a good life — one community's culture studied from inside.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: How Buddhists use the Buddha's teaching to build a good life — one community's culture studied from inside. Give one real example from your own life or from the Sri Lanka you live in, and name the lesson's key words: buddhist culture. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
