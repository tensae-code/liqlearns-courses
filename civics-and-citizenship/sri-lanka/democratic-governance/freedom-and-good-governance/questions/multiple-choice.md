# Freedom and Good Governance — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Freedom defined through meeting economic and social needs; a sound economic standard, welfare and equal rights as conditions of good governance.  b) it has nothing to do with how Sri Lanka governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Freedom defined through meeting economic and social needs; a sound economic standard, welfare and equal rights as conditions of good governance.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
