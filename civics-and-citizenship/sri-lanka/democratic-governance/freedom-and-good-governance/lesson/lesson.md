# Freedom and Good Governance

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Freedom and Good Governance. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Sri Lanka's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Freedom defined through meeting economic and social needs; a sound economic standard, welfare and equal rights as conditions of good governance.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Freedom defined through meeting economic and social needs; a sound economic standard, welfare and equal rights as conditions of good governance. Give one real example from your own life or from the Sri Lanka you live in, and name the lesson's key words: freedom and. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
