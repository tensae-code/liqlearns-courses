# Franchise and Elections — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The franchise and how elections work as the citizen's instrument.  b) it has nothing to do with how Sri Lanka governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The franchise and how elections work as the citizen's instrument.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) The franchise and how elections work as the citizen's instrument.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Sri Lanka
**Answer: a) The franchise and how elections work as the citizen's instrument.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
