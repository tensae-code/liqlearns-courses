# Civics and Citizenship — Sri Lanka course — TITLES (titles from the bottom)

The same ladder as CURRICULUM.md, written as the list of unit
folders. Each unit is a theme (a "title") in Sri Lanka's own
course; a unit's lessons sit at the levels the filings placed
them at (a lesson stocked from more than one level stands at
its earliest filed level), and each entry says what the unit
holds and why it sits at that level. Levels are the
organisation; the school grades the books come from appear
only in each lesson's sources.
