# What Culture Is

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: What Culture Is. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Sri Lanka's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Culture has many scholarly definitions; the book works through them to what a people's culture is.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Culture has many scholarly definitions; the book works through them to what a people's culture is. Give one real example from your own life or from the Sri Lanka you live in, and name the lesson's key words: what culture. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
