# Building National Unity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Putting national unity first while protecting one's own cultural identity.  b) it has nothing to do with how Sri Lanka governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Putting national unity first while protecting one's own cultural identity.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Putting national unity first while protecting one's own cultural identity.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Sri Lanka
**Answer: a) Putting national unity first while protecting one's own cultural identity.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
