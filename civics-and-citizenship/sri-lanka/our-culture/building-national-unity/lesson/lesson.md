# Building National Unity

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Building National Unity. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Sri Lanka's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Putting national unity first while protecting one's own cultural identity.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Putting national unity first while protecting one's own cultural identity. Give one real example from your own life or from the Sri Lanka you live in, and name the lesson's key words: building national. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
