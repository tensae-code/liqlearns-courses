# National Symbols

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: National Symbols. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Sri Lanka's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Sri Lanka's national symbols — standing at attention for the National Anthem, the State emblem as the symbol of the state's rule.
