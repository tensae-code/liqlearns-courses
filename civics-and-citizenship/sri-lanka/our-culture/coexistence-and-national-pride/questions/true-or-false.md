# Coexistence and National Pride — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Living in coexistence while safeguarding every community's heritage grows national pride.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that Coexistence and National Pride — and that it has no connection to the rest of Sri Lanka's story.
