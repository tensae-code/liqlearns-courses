# Those Who Help Us — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The people and services of the area who help daily life, and valuing their work.  b) it has nothing to do with how Sri Lanka governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The people and services of the area who help daily life, and valuing their work.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) The people and services of the area who help daily life, and valuing their work.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Sri Lanka
**Answer: a) The people and services of the area who help daily life, and valuing their work.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
