# Inter Relationships — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Good inter-relationships with teachers, neighbours and others, and the benefits they bring.  b) it has nothing to do with how Sri Lanka governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Good inter-relationships with teachers, neighbours and others, and the benefits they bring.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Good inter-relationships with teachers, neighbours and others, and the benefits they bring.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Sri Lanka
**Answer: a) Good inter-relationships with teachers, neighbours and others, and the benefits they bring.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
