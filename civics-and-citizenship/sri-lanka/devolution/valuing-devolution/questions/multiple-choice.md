# Valuing Devolution — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Why power is shared with provinces and local bodies.  b) it has nothing to do with how Sri Lanka governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Why power is shared with provinces and local bodies.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Why power is shared with provinces and local bodies.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Sri Lanka
**Answer: a) Why power is shared with provinces and local bodies.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
