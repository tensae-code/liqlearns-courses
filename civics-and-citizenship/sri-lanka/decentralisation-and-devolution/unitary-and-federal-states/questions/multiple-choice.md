# Unitary and Federal States — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) How states are categorised by the sharing of power between central and local government.  b) it has nothing to do with how Sri Lanka governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) How states are categorised by the sharing of power between central and local government.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) How states are categorised by the sharing of power between central and local government.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Sri Lanka
**Answer: a) How states are categorised by the sharing of power between central and local government.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
