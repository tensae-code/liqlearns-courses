# Devolution of Power — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Devolution between the central government and local government bodies, and the provincial councils made under it.  b) it has nothing to do with how Sri Lanka governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Devolution between the central government and local government bodies, and the provincial councils made under it.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Devolution between the central government and local government bodies, and the provincial councils made under it.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Sri Lanka
**Answer: a) Devolution between the central government and local government bodies, and the provincial councils made under it.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
