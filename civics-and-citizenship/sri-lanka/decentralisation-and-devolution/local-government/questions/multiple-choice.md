# Local Government — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Local governments implementing administrative power close to the people.  b) it has nothing to do with how Sri Lanka governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Local governments implementing administrative power close to the people.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Local governments implementing administrative power close to the people.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Sri Lanka
**Answer: a) Local governments implementing administrative power close to the people.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
