# Vocational Training — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Finding the vocational training centres of your area, the courses they provide, and reading newspaper advertisements for them.  b) it has nothing to do with how Sri Lanka governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Finding the vocational training centres of your area, the courses they provide, and reading newspaper advertisements for them.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**Answer: a) Finding the vocational training centres of your area, the courses they provide, and reading newspaper advertisements for them.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
