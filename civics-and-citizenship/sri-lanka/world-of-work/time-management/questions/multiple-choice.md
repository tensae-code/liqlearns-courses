# Time Management — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Managing time properly at work.  b) it has nothing to do with how Sri Lanka governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Managing time properly at work.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Managing time properly at work.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Sri Lanka
**Answer: a) Managing time properly at work.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
