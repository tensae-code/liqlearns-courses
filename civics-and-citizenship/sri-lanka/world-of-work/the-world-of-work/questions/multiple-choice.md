# The World of Work — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Appreciating the diversity of work and preparing for working life.  b) it has nothing to do with how Sri Lanka governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Appreciating the diversity of work and preparing for working life.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Appreciating the diversity of work and preparing for working life.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Sri Lanka
**Answer: a) Appreciating the diversity of work and preparing for working life.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
