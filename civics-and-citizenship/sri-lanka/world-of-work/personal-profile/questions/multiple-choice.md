# Personal Profile — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Keeping a personal profile of your achievements and skills.  b) it has nothing to do with how Sri Lanka governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Keeping a personal profile of your achievements and skills.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Keeping a personal profile of your achievements and skills.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Sri Lanka
**Answer: a) Keeping a personal profile of your achievements and skills.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
