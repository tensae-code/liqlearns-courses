# Serving Customers Well — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Frequent accountability, live discussion, quick response and thinking from the customer's point of view as working virtues.  b) it has nothing to do with how Sri Lanka governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Frequent accountability, live discussion, quick response and thinking from the customer's point of view as working virtues.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**Answer: a) Frequent accountability, live discussion, quick response and thinking from the customer's point of view as working virtues.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
