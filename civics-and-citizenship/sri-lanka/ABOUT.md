# About the Civics and Citizenship — Sri Lanka course

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in
this course comes from government textbooks — this country's
own Civic Education books (with its Life Competencies and
Civic Education, Civics and Governance, Social Education and
Political Science books). The one exception is the
**Tip boxes**: a tip in a box is from us, the people making
LiqLearns. Tips are ours; the lessons are the books'.

## How this course is organised

This is ONE course for Sri Lanka inside the Civics and
Citizenship folder (one course per country). Inside it, the
organisation is by **levels, not grades**: a learner's place
is found by what they prove, and each level stands on the
ones before it as a recommended-first path — never a forced
gate. The school grades the books come from appear only in
each lesson's `lesson/sources.md`, as provenance.

The course as built has **seven stocked levels** (Levels
1–7), from the filings for this build
(`sri-lanka-placements.md` — 13 records / 11 distinct books,
two Tamil-medium books being parallel editions of English
books in the set; and `sri-lanka-placements-b.md` — 112 link
leads triaged, its books listed per header). 218 placements
in all, stocking 209 lessons — the coined lesson names were
deduped before building (see CURRICULUM.md, "Where the build
stands"): every placement stocks into exactly one lesson,
and every placement's provenance row is in that lesson's
`lesson/sources.md`.

**The two filings number their levels differently, stated
plainly:** the first filing numbers the modern band
thematically (L1–L6) with the older Tamil-medium generation
as L7; the second filing numbers its books by grade band
(L1 Grade 6 · L2 Grade 7 · L3 Grade 8 · L4 Grade 10 · L5
Grade 11). The build keeps every placement's filed level;
a lesson deduped across the filings stands at its earliest
filed level, and CURRICULUM.md reconciles the two sketches
into one ladder. Where this brief's expectations and a
filing disagreed, the filing was carried.

**How the books were read, exactly as filed:** the first
filing's books were read from GitHub tree grabs where the
file answered, otherwise from the record's own recorded
source URL; source-fetched books that were recorded as
pushed but 404 in the tree are phantom pushes — named in
the group log as library debts (the Grade 8 and Grade 9
Tamil-medium books and the older Tamil-medium books were
fetched complete from their Noolaham source after the tree
grab 404'd). The second filing's NIE teacher guides were
fetched from nie.lk. The filings name no individual
staged-pending file for Sri Lanka.
