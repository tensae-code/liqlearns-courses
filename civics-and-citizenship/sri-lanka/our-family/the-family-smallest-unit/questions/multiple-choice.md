# The Family Smallest Unit — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Society is a collection of family units; the family is the smallest social unit and a person belongs to one by birth.  b) it has nothing to do with how Sri Lanka governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Society is a collection of family units; the family is the smallest social unit and a person belongs to one by birth.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Society is a collection of family units; the family is the smallest social unit and a person belongs to one by birth.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Sri Lanka
**Answer: a) Society is a collection of family units; the family is the smallest social unit and a person belongs to one by birth.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
