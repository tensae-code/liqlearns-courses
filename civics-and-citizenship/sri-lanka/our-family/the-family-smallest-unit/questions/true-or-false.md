# The Family Smallest Unit — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Society is a collection of family units; the family is the smallest social unit and a person belongs to one by birth.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that The Family Smallest Unit — and that it has no connection to the rest of Sri Lanka's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Sri Lanka's own course, in its own order.
