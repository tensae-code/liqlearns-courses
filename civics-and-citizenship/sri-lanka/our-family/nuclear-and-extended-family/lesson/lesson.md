# Nuclear and Extended Family

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Nuclear and Extended Family. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Sri Lanka's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

The nuclear family (mother, father, children) and the extended family with relatives under one roof, both normal in Sri Lankan society.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The nuclear family (mother, father, children) and the extended family with relatives under one roof, both normal in Sri Lankan society. Give one real example from your own life or from the Sri Lanka you live in, and name the lesson's key words: nuclear and. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
