# Family Traditions

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Family Traditions. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Sri Lanka's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Traditions a family follows and the benefits they bring, handed down across generations.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Traditions a family follows and the benefits they bring, handed down across generations. Give one real example from your own life or from the Sri Lanka you live in, and name the lesson's key words: family traditions. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
