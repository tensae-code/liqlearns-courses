Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The family as the first society and the making of stable family units.  b) it has nothing to do with how Sri Lanka governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations

**Answer: a) The family as the first society and the making of stable family units.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
