# Duties Between Teachers and Students — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Duties run both ways between teachers and students and are carried out in daily school life.  b) it has nothing to do with how Sri Lanka governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Duties run both ways between teachers and students and are carried out in daily school life.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Duties run both ways between teachers and students and are carried out in daily school life.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Sri Lanka
**Answer: a) Duties run both ways between teachers and students and are carried out in daily school life.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
