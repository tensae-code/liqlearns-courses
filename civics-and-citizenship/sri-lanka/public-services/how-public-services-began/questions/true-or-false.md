# How Public Services Began — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Transport, postal/telecommunication and banking services began under British rule and grew with time.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that How Public Services Began — and that it has no connection to the rest of Sri Lanka's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Sri Lanka's own course, in its own order.
