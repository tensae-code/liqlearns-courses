# How Public Services Began — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Transport, postal/telecommunication and banking services began under British rule and grew with time.  b) it has nothing to do with how Sri Lanka governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Transport, postal/telecommunication and banking services began under British rule and grew with time.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Transport, postal/telecommunication and banking services began under British rule and grew with time.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Sri Lanka
**Answer: a) Transport, postal/telecommunication and banking services began under British rule and grew with time.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
