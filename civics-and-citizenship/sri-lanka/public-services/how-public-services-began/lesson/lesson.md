# How Public Services Began

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: How Public Services Began. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Sri Lanka's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Transport, postal/telecommunication and banking services began under British rule and grew with time.

## Say it to your companion
