# Duties of Service Institutions — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) What service institutions owe the public.  b) it has nothing to do with how Sri Lanka governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) What service institutions owe the public.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) What service institutions owe the public.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Sri Lanka
**Answer: a) What service institutions owe the public.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
