# Speaking Decently — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Decent, pleasant speech as civic conduct.  b) it has nothing to do with how Sri Lanka governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Decent, pleasant speech as civic conduct.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Decent, pleasant speech as civic conduct.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Sri Lanka
**Answer: a) Decent, pleasant speech as civic conduct.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
