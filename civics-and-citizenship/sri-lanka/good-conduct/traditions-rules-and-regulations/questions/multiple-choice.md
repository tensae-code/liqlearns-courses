# Traditions Rules and Regulations — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Respecting traditions and obeying rules and regulations in school and society.  b) it has nothing to do with how Sri Lanka governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Respecting traditions and obeying rules and regulations in school and society.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Respecting traditions and obeying rules and regulations in school and society.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Sri Lanka
**Answer: a) Respecting traditions and obeying rules and regulations in school and society.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
