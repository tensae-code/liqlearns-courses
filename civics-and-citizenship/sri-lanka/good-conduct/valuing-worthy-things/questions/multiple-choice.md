# Valuing Worthy Things — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Identifying and following worthy things practised in society.  b) it has nothing to do with how Sri Lanka governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Identifying and following worthy things practised in society.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Identifying and following worthy things practised in society.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Sri Lanka
**Answer: a) Identifying and following worthy things practised in society.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
