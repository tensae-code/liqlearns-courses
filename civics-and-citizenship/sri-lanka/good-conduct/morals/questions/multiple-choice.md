# Morals — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Morals are the human qualities that enrich person, society and life and form social relationships.  b) it has nothing to do with how Sri Lanka governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Morals are the human qualities that enrich person, society and life and form social relationships.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Morals are the human qualities that enrich person, society and life and form social relationships.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Sri Lanka
**Answer: a) Morals are the human qualities that enrich person, society and life and form social relationships.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
