# Morals

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Morals. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Sri Lanka's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Morals are the human qualities that enrich person, society and life and form social relationships.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Morals are the human qualities that enrich person, society and life and form social relationships. Give one real example from your own life or from the Sri Lanka you live in, and name the lesson's key words: morals. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: Morals are the human qualities that enrich person, society and life and form social relationships.
