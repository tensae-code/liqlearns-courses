Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Identifying the problems and challenges of contemporary society.  b) it has nothing to do with how Sri Lanka governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Identifying the problems and challenges of contemporary society.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?

**Answer: a) Identifying the problems and challenges of contemporary society.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
