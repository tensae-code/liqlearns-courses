# What Law and Justice Are — depth

This lesson is ONE lesson with no sub lessons: it teaches a single thing, whole. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: Law as the state's system of rights and obligations (Green) and the sovereign's command (Austin); justice as fair treatment through impartial enforcement; the factors that create justice — a fair trial, being heard, knowing the allegations. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
