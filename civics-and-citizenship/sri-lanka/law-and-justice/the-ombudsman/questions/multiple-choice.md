# The Ombudsman — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The Ombudsman's place among the institutions that guard the citizen.  b) it has nothing to do with how Sri Lanka governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The Ombudsman's place among the institutions that guard the citizen.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
