# Kandyan Law — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The law of the ancient Kandyan kingdom, still controlling marriage, divorce and heirship for Kandyan Sinhalese, with marriage also possible under the general law.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that Kandyan Law — and that it has no connection to the rest of Sri Lanka's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Sri Lanka's own course, in its own order.
