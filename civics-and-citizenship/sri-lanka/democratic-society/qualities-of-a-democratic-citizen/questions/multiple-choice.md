# Qualities of a Democratic Citizen — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The qualities a citizen needs for democracy to work.  b) it has nothing to do with how Sri Lanka governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The qualities a citizen needs for democracy to work.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) The qualities a citizen needs for democracy to work.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Sri Lanka
**Answer: a) The qualities a citizen needs for democracy to work.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
