# Democracy a Way of Living

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Democracy a Way of Living. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Sri Lanka's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Democracy is both a system of government and a way of living, and the way of living guarantees society's well-being.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Democracy is both a system of government and a way of living, and the way of living guarantees society's well-being. Give one real example from your own life or from the Sri Lanka you live in, and name the lesson's key words: democracy a. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: Democracy is both a system of government and a way of living, and the way of living guarantees society's well-being.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
