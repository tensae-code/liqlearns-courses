# Democracy a Way of Living — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Democracy is both a system of government and a way of living, and the way of living guarantees society's well-being.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that Democracy a Way of Living — and that it has no connection to the rest of Sri Lanka's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Sri Lanka's own course, in its own order.
