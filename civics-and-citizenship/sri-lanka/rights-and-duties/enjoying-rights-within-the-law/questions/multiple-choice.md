Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?

**Answer: a) Enjoying one's rights inside a legal framework while performing duties.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**Answer: a) Enjoying one's rights inside a legal framework while performing duties.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
