# Duties Related to Rights — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A member of society holds rights, and duties come with them; fulfil your duties for the benefit of society.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that Duties Related to Rights — and that it has no connection to the rest of Sri Lanka's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Sri Lanka's own course, in its own order.
