# My Rights and Duties at School — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) A student's rights in school and the duties that go with them.  b) it has nothing to do with how Sri Lanka governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) A student's rights in school and the duties that go with them.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) A student's rights in school and the duties that go with them.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Sri Lanka
**Answer: a) A student's rights in school and the duties that go with them.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
