# Civilisations and Political Thought

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Civilisations and Political Thought. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Sri Lanka's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

How the civilisations fed political knowledge — the origins of political thought in the world's cultures.
