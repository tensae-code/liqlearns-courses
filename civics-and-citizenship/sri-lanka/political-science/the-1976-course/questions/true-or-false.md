# The 1976 Course — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Sri Lanka's oldest civics-band book in this set is a university-entrance political science — the state, its origin theories, sovereignty, and state-and-society, examined for the National Certificate of Higher Education.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that The 1976 Course — and that it has no connection to the rest of Sri Lanka's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Sri Lanka's own course, in its own order.
