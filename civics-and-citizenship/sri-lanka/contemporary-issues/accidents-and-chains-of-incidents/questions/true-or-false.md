# Accidents and Chains of Incidents — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Disasters and accidents (the Kosgama armoury fire as case study) can occur as chains of incidents, natural and human-caused.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that Accidents and Chains of Incidents — and that it has no connection to the rest of Sri Lanka's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Sri Lanka's own course, in its own order.
