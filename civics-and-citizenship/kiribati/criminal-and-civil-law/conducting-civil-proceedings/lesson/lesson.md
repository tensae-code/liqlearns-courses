# Conducting Civil Proceedings

## Do it first

Money alone does not settle every wrong. If you won a civil case, what could the court actually order? This lesson teaches how civil proceedings are conducted — and the remedies at their end.

## Sub lesson 1 — Plaintiff, claimant and petitioner

The syllabus names the parties who bring civil proceedings — plaintiff, claimant and petitioner — and what each name means.

## Sub lesson 2 — Conduct and remedies

Civil proceedings are conducted in set steps, and the syllabus teaches the remedies commonly practised in Kiribati — the orders a court makes to answer a proven claim.
