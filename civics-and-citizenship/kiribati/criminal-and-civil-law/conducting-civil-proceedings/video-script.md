# Video script — Conducting Civil Proceedings

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A civil case is conducted by named parties — plaintiff, claimant, petitioner — and ends in remedies: what the court orders to put the wrong right, as commonly practised in Kiribati. This lesson stands on the KCSE Civic syllabus alone — evidence only, labelled as such.

The syllabus names the parties who bring civil proceedings — plaintiff, claimant and petitioner — and what each name means.

Civil proceedings are conducted in set steps, and the syllabus teaches the remedies commonly practised in Kiribati — the orders a court makes to answer a proven claim.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
