# Hands-on — Conducting Civil Proceedings

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Money alone does not settle every wrong. If you won a civil case, what could the court actually order? This lesson teaches how civil proceedings are conducted — and the remedies at their end.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: a civil case is conducted by named parties — plaintiff, claimant, petitioner — and ends in remedies: what the court orders to put the wrong right, as commonly practised in Kiribati. This lesson stands on the KCSE Civic syllabus alone — evidence only, labelled as such. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
