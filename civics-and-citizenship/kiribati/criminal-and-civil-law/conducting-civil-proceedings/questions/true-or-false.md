# Conducting Civil Proceedings — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Civil proceedings are conducted in set steps, and the syllabus teaches the remedies commonly practised in Kiribati — the orders a court makes to answer a proven claim.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that a civil case is conducted by named parties — plaintiff, claimant, petitioner — and ends in remedies: what the court orders to put the wrong right, as commonly practised in Kiribati. This lesson stands on the KCSE Civic syllabus alone — evidence only, labelled as such. — and that it has no connection to the rest of Kiribati's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Kiribati's own course, in its own order.
