# Conducting Civil Proceedings — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "Plaintiff, claimant and petitioner" teach?
**Answer:** The syllabus names the parties who bring civil proceedings — plaintiff, claimant and petitioner — and what each name means.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** A civil case is conducted by named parties — plaintiff, claimant, petitioner — and ends in remedies: what the court orders to put the wrong right, as commonly practised in Kiribati. This lesson stands on the KCSE Civic syllabus alone — evidence only, labelled as such.
