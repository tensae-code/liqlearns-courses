# Lesson plan — Conducting Civil Proceedings

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Kiribati · **Title:** Criminal and Civil Law · **Lesson:** Conducting Civil Proceedings
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Criminal and Civil Law (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a civil case is conducted by named parties — plaintiff, claimant, petitioner — and ends in remedies: what the court orders to put the wrong right, as commonly practised in Kiribati. This lesson stands on the KCSE Civic syllabus alone — evidence only, labelled as such.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Plaintiff, claimant and petitioner** — The syllabus names the parties who bring civil proceedings — plaintiff, claimant and petitioner — and what each name means.
2. **Conduct and remedies** — Civil proceedings are conducted in set steps, and the syllabus teaches the remedies commonly practised in Kiribati — the orders a court makes to answer a proven claim.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
