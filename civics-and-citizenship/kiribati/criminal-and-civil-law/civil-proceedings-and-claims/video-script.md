# Video script — Civil Proceedings and Claims

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Civil proceedings settle private wrongs: torts — harms one person does another — and the interests civil law protects. This lesson stands on the KCSE Civic syllabus alone — evidence only, labelled as such.

The syllabus teaches civil proceedings as they run in the Kiribati context — who sues, who answers, and what the court can do.

A tort is a civil wrong; the syllabus teaches torts and their types, and the interests civil law protects — person, property and reputation among them.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
