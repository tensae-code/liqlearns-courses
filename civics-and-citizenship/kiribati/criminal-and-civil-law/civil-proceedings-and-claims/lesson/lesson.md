# Civil Proceedings and Claims

## Do it first

Your neighbour's carelessness damages your canoe. No crime was intended — yet you have a real loss. What does the law offer you? This lesson teaches civil claims: the law's answer to private wrongs.

## Sub lesson 1 — Civil proceedings in the Kiribati context

The syllabus teaches civil proceedings as they run in the Kiribati context — who sues, who answers, and what the court can do.

## Sub lesson 2 — Torts and protected interests

A tort is a civil wrong; the syllabus teaches torts and their types, and the interests civil law protects — person, property and reputation among them.
