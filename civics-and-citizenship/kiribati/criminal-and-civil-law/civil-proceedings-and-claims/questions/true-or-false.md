# Civil Proceedings and Claims — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A tort is a civil wrong; the syllabus teaches torts and their types, and the interests civil law protects — person, property and reputation among them.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that civil proceedings settle private wrongs: torts — harms one person does another — and the interests civil law protects. This lesson stands on the KCSE Civic syllabus alone — evidence only, labelled as such. — and that it has no connection to the rest of Kiribati's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Kiribati's own course, in its own order.
