# Civil Proceedings and Claims — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "Civil proceedings in the Kiribati context" teach?
**Answer:** The syllabus teaches civil proceedings as they run in the Kiribati context — who sues, who answers, and what the court can do.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** Civil proceedings settle private wrongs: torts — harms one person does another — and the interests civil law protects. This lesson stands on the KCSE Civic syllabus alone — evidence only, labelled as such.
