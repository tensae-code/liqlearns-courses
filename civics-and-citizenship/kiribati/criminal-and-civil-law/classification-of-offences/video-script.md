The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

This lesson is about one thing.
Offences are classified: Kiribati sorts offences into classes — including a third, intermediate class — and the class decides how a case is handled. This lesson stands on the KCSE Civic syllabus alone — evidence only, labelled as such.

An offence is an act the law forbids and punishes; the syllabus defines it before sorting it.

The syllabus teaches the classes of offences in Kiribati and how offences are classified by the system — including the third intermediate class between the plainly minor and the plainly grave.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
