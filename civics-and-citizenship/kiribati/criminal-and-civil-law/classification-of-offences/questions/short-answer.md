# Classification of Offences — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "What an offence is" teach?
**Answer:** An offence is an act the law forbids and punishes; the syllabus defines it before sorting it.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** Offences are classified: Kiribati sorts offences into classes — including a third, intermediate class — and the class decides how a case is handled. This lesson stands on the KCSE Civic syllabus alone — evidence only, labelled as such.
