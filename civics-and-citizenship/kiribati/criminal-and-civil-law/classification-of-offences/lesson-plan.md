# Lesson plan — Classification of Offences

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Kiribati · **Title:** Criminal and Civil Law · **Lesson:** Classification of Offences
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Criminal and Civil Law (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** offences are classified: Kiribati sorts offences into classes — including a third, intermediate class — and the class decides how a case is handled. This lesson stands on the KCSE Civic syllabus alone — evidence only, labelled as such.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **What an offence is** — An offence is an act the law forbids and punishes; the syllabus defines it before sorting it.
2. **The classes of offences** — The syllabus teaches the classes of offences in Kiribati and how offences are classified by the system — including the third intermediate class between the plainly minor and the plainly grave.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
