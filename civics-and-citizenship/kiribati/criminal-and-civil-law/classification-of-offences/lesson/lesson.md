# Classification of Offences

## Do it first

Is every wrongdoing equally serious in law? A stolen bicycle and a violent attack — should the law treat them the same way? This lesson teaches how Kiribati classifies offences.

## Sub lesson 1 — What an offence is

An offence is an act the law forbids and punishes; the syllabus defines it before sorting it.

## Sub lesson 2 — The classes of offences

The syllabus teaches the classes of offences in Kiribati and how offences are classified by the system — including the third intermediate class between the plainly minor and the plainly grave.
