# Hands-on — Criminal and Civil Proceedings

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
A man steals a boat; a woman is owed money for fish she sold. One case is criminal, one civil. Which is which — and who brings each case? This lesson teaches the difference at the root of Year 11's law study.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: criminal law and civil law are different roads: criminal cases answer offences against the community, civil cases settle disputes between parties — and the criminal court, with its juries and assessors, is built for the first road. This lesson stands on the KCSE Civic syllabus alone — evidence only, labelled as such. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
