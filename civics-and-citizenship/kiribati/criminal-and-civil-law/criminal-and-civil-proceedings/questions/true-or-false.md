# Criminal and Civil Proceedings — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Juries and assessors take part in criminal proceedings; the syllabus teaches who they are and what they do.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that criminal law and civil law are different roads: criminal cases answer offences against the community, civil cases settle disputes between parties — and the criminal court, with its juries and assessors, is built for the first road. This lesson stands on the KCSE Civic syllabus alone — evidence only, labelled as such. — and that it has no connection to the rest of Kiribati's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Kiribati's own course, in its own order.
