# Criminal and Civil Proceedings — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "Criminal, non-criminal and civil cases" teach?
**Answer:** The syllabus sorts cases: criminal, non-criminal and civil, and teaches the difference between criminal and civil law.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** Criminal law and civil law are different roads: criminal cases answer offences against the community, civil cases settle disputes between parties — and the criminal court, with its juries and assessors, is built for the first road. This lesson stands on the KCSE Civic syllabus alone — evidence only, labelled as such.
