# Video script — Criminal and Civil Proceedings

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Criminal law and civil law are different roads: criminal cases answer offences against the community, civil cases settle disputes between parties — and the criminal court, with its juries and assessors, is built for the first road. This lesson stands on the KCSE Civic syllabus alone — evidence only, labelled as such.

The syllabus sorts cases: criminal, non-criminal and civil, and teaches the difference between criminal and civil law.

The criminal court has its own function and structure in the magistrates' and high courts; the syllabus teaches both levels.

Juries and assessors take part in criminal proceedings; the syllabus teaches who they are and what they do.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
