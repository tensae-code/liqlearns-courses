# Burden and Standard of Proof — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The burden and standard differ between criminal and civil cases: the criminal standard is the heavier one, because a person's liberty and name are at stake.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that who must prove, and how surely: the burden of proof says who carries the proving, the standard of proof says how convincing it must be — and both differ between criminal and civil cases. This lesson stands on the KCSE Civic syllabus alone — evidence only, labelled as such. — and that it has no connection to the rest of Kiribati's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Kiribati's own course, in its own order.
