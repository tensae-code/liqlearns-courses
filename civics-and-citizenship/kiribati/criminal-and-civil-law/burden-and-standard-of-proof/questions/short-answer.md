# Burden and Standard of Proof — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "Burden and standard defined" teach?
**Answer:** The syllabus defines burden of proof and standard of proof and teaches their components — who proves, and to what standard.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** Who must prove, and how surely: the burden of proof says who carries the proving, the standard of proof says how convincing it must be — and both differ between criminal and civil cases. This lesson stands on the KCSE Civic syllabus alone — evidence only, labelled as such.
