# Burden and Standard of Proof

## Do it first

You accuse someone of taking your property. Who must prove it — you, or must they prove they did not? And how certain must the court be? This lesson teaches the two ideas that answer those questions.

## Sub lesson 1 — Burden and standard defined

The syllabus defines burden of proof and standard of proof and teaches their components — who proves, and to what standard.

## Sub lesson 2 — Criminal against civil

The burden and standard differ between criminal and civil cases: the criminal standard is the heavier one, because a person's liberty and name are at stake.
