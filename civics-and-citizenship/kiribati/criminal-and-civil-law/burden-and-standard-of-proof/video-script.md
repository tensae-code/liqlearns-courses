This lesson is about one thing.
Who must prove, and how surely: the burden of proof says who carries the proving, the standard of proof says how convincing it must be — and both differ between criminal and civil cases. This lesson stands on the KCSE Civic syllabus alone — evidence only, labelled as such.

The syllabus defines burden of proof and standard of proof and teaches their components — who proves, and to what standard.

The burden and standard differ between criminal and civil cases: the criminal standard is the heavier one, because a person's liberty and name are at stake.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
