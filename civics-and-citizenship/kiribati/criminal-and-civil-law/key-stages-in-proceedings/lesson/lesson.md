# Key Stages in Proceedings

## Do it first

From an arrest to a verdict, what happens in between? List the steps you think a criminal case passes through, in order. This lesson teaches the critical stages — and the differences between them.

## Sub lesson 1 — The critical stages

The syllabus teaches the critical stages in all criminal proceedings, in their order.

## Sub lesson 2 — Differences between the stages

Each stage has its own purpose and its own actors; the syllabus teaches the differences between them, so a stage is never confused with the next.
