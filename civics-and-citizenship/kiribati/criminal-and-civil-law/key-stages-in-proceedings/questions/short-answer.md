# Key Stages in Proceedings — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "The critical stages" teach?
**Answer:** The syllabus teaches the critical stages in all criminal proceedings, in their order.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** Every criminal proceeding walks the same road in stages — and knowing the stages, and how they differ, is knowing how a case actually moves. This lesson stands on the KCSE Civic syllabus alone — evidence only, labelled as such.
