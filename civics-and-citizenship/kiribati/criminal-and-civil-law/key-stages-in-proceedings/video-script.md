# Video script — Key Stages in Proceedings

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Every criminal proceeding walks the same road in stages — and knowing the stages, and how they differ, is knowing how a case actually moves. This lesson stands on the KCSE Civic syllabus alone — evidence only, labelled as such.

The syllabus teaches the critical stages in all criminal proceedings, in their order.

Each stage has its own purpose and its own actors; the syllabus teaches the differences between them, so a stage is never confused with the next.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
