# Hands-on — Key Stages in Proceedings

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
From an arrest to a verdict, what happens in between? List the steps you think a criminal case passes through, in order. This lesson teaches the critical stages — and the differences between them.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: every criminal proceeding walks the same road in stages — and knowing the stages, and how they differ, is knowing how a case actually moves. This lesson stands on the KCSE Civic syllabus alone — evidence only, labelled as such. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
