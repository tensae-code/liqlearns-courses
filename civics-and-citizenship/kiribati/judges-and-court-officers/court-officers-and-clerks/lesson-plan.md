# Lesson plan — Court Officers and Clerks

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Kiribati · **Title:** Judges and Court Officers · **Lesson:** Court Officers and Clerks
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 5 — The People Who Run Justice (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a court runs on more than judges: court officers, clerks and registrars carry differing responsibilities, split between administrative and judicial parts — and their effectiveness decides whether justice is actually delivered. This lesson stands on the KCSE Civic syllabus alone — evidence only, labelled as such.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 5 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Differing responsibilities** — Court officers, court clerks and registrar officers hold differing responsibilities; the syllabus separates them into administrative and judicial parts.
2. **Effectiveness in delivering justice** — The syllabus judges these roles by their effectiveness in delivering justice: records kept, orders moved, people served — the court's promise kept in practice.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
