This lesson is about one thing.
A court runs on more than judges: court officers, clerks and registrars carry differing responsibilities, split between administrative and judicial parts — and their effectiveness decides whether justice is actually delivered. This lesson stands on the KCSE Civic syllabus alone — evidence only, labelled as such.

Court officers, court clerks and registrar officers hold differing responsibilities; the syllabus separates them into administrative and judicial parts.

The syllabus judges these roles by their effectiveness in delivering justice: records kept, orders moved, people served — the court's promise kept in practice.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
