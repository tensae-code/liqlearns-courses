# Court Officers and Clerks — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "Differing responsibilities" teach?
**Answer:** Court officers, court clerks and registrar officers hold differing responsibilities; the syllabus separates them into administrative and judicial parts.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** A court runs on more than judges: court officers, clerks and registrars carry differing responsibilities, split between administrative and judicial parts — and their effectiveness decides whether justice is actually delivered. This lesson stands on the KCSE Civic syllabus alone — evidence only, labelled as such.
