# Court Officers and Clerks — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The syllabus judges these roles by their effectiveness in delivering justice: records kept, orders moved, people served — the court's promise kept in practice.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that a court runs on more than judges: court officers, clerks and registrars carry differing responsibilities, split between administrative and judicial parts — and their effectiveness decides whether justice is actually delivered. This lesson stands on the KCSE Civic syllabus alone — evidence only, labelled as such. — and that it has no connection to the rest of Kiribati's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Kiribati's own course, in its own order.
