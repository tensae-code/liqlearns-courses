# Hands-on — Judicial Functions

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
What exactly does a judge produce at the end of a case — and what must be inside it for it to stand? This lesson teaches the judicial functions and the anatomy of a judgment.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: judicial officers do two main functions, administer justice in set dimensions, and end cases with a judgment built from named elements. This lesson stands on the KCSE Civic syllabus alone — evidence only, labelled as such. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
