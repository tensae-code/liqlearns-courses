# Video script — Judicial Functions

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Judicial officers do two main functions, administer justice in set dimensions, and end cases with a judgment built from named elements. This lesson stands on the KCSE Civic syllabus alone — evidence only, labelled as such.

The syllabus teaches the two main functions of judicial officers — the work that defines the office.

Judicial administration has dimensions the syllabus names; and a judgment or decision has elements — the parts that must be present for the decision to be complete.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
