# Judicial Functions

## Do it first

What exactly does a judge produce at the end of a case — and what must be inside it for it to stand? This lesson teaches the judicial functions and the anatomy of a judgment.

## Sub lesson 1 — The two main functions

The syllabus teaches the two main functions of judicial officers — the work that defines the office.

## Sub lesson 2 — Administration and the elements of a judgment

Judicial administration has dimensions the syllabus names; and a judgment or decision has elements — the parts that must be present for the decision to be complete.
