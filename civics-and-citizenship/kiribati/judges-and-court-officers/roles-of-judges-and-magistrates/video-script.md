# Video script — Roles of Judges and Magistrates

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Judges and magistrates hold their power from the law, not from themselves: the syllabus teaches where that power comes from, what their roles are, and where the limits of their decision-making lie. This lesson stands on the KCSE Civic syllabus alone — evidence only, labelled as such.

The syllabus names judges, judicial officers and magistrates, and where their power comes from — appointment under the law, not personal authority.

Their roles are to hear, decide and give judgment; and their decision-making power has limits, which the syllabus teaches as part of the role itself.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
