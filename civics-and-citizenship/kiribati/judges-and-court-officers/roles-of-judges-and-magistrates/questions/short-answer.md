# Roles of Judges and Magistrates — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "Judges, judicial officers and magistrates" teach?
**Answer:** The syllabus names judges, judicial officers and magistrates, and where their power comes from — appointment under the law, not personal authority.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** Judges and magistrates hold their power from the law, not from themselves: the syllabus teaches where that power comes from, what their roles are, and where the limits of their decision-making lie. This lesson stands on the KCSE Civic syllabus alone — evidence only, labelled as such.
