# Roles of Judges and Magistrates

## Do it first

A magistrate decides a case against a powerful family. What protects his decision — and what stops him deciding by favour? This lesson teaches the judge's and magistrate's power, role and limits.

## Sub lesson 1 — Judges, judicial officers and magistrates

The syllabus names judges, judicial officers and magistrates, and where their power comes from — appointment under the law, not personal authority.

## Sub lesson 2 — Roles and limits

Their roles are to hear, decide and give judgment; and their decision-making power has limits, which the syllabus teaches as part of the role itself.
