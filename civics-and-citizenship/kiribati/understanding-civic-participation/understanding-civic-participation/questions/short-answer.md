# Understanding Civic Participation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "The participation strand" teach?
**Answer:** Strand 3, Understand Civic Participation, is the junior course's explicit civics strand: the pupil learns what it means to take part in the community's life. Placed as filed and marked TEACHER.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** Civic participation — taking part in the life of your community — is the junior course's explicit civics strand: citizenship begins as joining in. This lesson stands on the Year 7 teacher guide alone — evidence only, labelled as such.
