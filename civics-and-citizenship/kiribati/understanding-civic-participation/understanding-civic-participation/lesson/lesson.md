# Understanding Civic Participation

## Do it first

List the ways people in your community take part together: meetings, clean-ups, church and village work, decisions. Which have you joined? The guide's third strand names this joining-in as civics itself.

Strand 3, Understand Civic Participation, is the junior course's explicit civics strand: the pupil learns what it means to take part in the community's life. Placed as filed and marked TEACHER.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: civic participation — taking part in the life of your community — is the junior course's explicit civics strand: citizenship begins as joining in. This lesson stands on the Year 7 teacher guide alone — evidence only, labelled as such. Give one real example from your own life or from the Kiribati you live in, and name the lesson's key words: civic participation, Strand 3. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
