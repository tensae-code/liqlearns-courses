# Hands-on — Understanding Civic Participation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
List the ways people in your community take part together: meetings, clean-ups, church and village work, decisions. Which have you joined? The guide's third strand names this joining-in as civics itself.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: civic participation — taking part in the life of your community — is the junior course's explicit civics strand: citizenship begins as joining in. This lesson stands on the Year 7 teacher guide alone — evidence only, labelled as such. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
