# Video script — The Bar, Lawyers and Legal Aid

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

This lesson is about one thing.
The citizen's helpers close the course: the bar and its lawyers — their functions, qualifications, regulation and code of ethics — and the paralegals and legal aid that bring law within everyone's reach. This lesson stands on the KCSE Civic syllabus alone — evidence only, labelled as such.

The syllabus teaches the bar and lawyers: their functions, the qualifications the work demands, how lawyers are regulated, and their code of ethics.

Paralegals and legal aid extend the law's help beyond those who can pay; the syllabus teaches their roles as the course's closing word — help for the citizen.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
