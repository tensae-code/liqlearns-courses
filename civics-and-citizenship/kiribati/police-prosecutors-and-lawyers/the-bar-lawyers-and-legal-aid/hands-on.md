# Hands-on — The Bar, Lawyers and Legal Aid

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
You face a court case and cannot pay a lawyer. Who can still stand beside you? This closing lesson teaches the bar, lawyers, paralegals and legal aid — the citizen's helpers.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the citizen's helpers close the course: the bar and its lawyers — their functions, qualifications, regulation and code of ethics — and the paralegals and legal aid that bring law within everyone's reach. This lesson stands on the KCSE Civic syllabus alone — evidence only, labelled as such. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
