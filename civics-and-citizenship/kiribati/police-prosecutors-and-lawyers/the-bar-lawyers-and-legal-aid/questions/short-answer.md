# The Bar, Lawyers and Legal Aid — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "The bar and lawyers" teach?
**Answer:** The syllabus teaches the bar and lawyers: their functions, the qualifications the work demands, how lawyers are regulated, and their code of ethics.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** The citizen's helpers close the course: the bar and its lawyers — their functions, qualifications, regulation and code of ethics — and the paralegals and legal aid that bring law within everyone's reach. This lesson stands on the KCSE Civic syllabus alone — evidence only, labelled as such.
