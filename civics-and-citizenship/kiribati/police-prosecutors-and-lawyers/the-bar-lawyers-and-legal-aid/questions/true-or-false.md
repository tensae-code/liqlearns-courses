# The Bar, Lawyers and Legal Aid — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Paralegals and legal aid extend the law's help beyond those who can pay; the syllabus teaches their roles as the course's closing word — help for the citizen.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that the citizen's helpers close the course: the bar and its lawyers — their functions, qualifications, regulation and code of ethics — and the paralegals and legal aid that bring law within everyone's reach. This lesson stands on the KCSE Civic syllabus alone — evidence only, labelled as such. — and that it has no connection to the rest of Kiribati's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Kiribati's own course, in its own order.
