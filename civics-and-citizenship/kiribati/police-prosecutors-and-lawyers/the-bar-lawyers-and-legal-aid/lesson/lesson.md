# The Bar, Lawyers and Legal Aid

## Do it first

You face a court case and cannot pay a lawyer. Who can still stand beside you? This closing lesson teaches the bar, lawyers, paralegals and legal aid — the citizen's helpers.

## Sub lesson 1 — The bar and lawyers

The syllabus teaches the bar and lawyers: their functions, the qualifications the work demands, how lawyers are regulated, and their code of ethics.

## Sub lesson 2 — Paralegals and legal aid

Paralegals and legal aid extend the law's help beyond those who can pay; the syllabus teaches their roles as the course's closing word — help for the citizen.
