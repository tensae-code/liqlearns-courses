# Roles of Police

## Do it first

The police can stop, question and arrest. What are those powers for — and what would your island look like without them? This lesson teaches the police's role, powers and functions.

## Sub lesson 1 — Police and their powers

The syllabus names police and police officers and teaches the police's significant powers in enforcing the law.

## Sub lesson 2 — Functions and effectiveness

Their critical functions keep peace and order; the syllabus also weighs their effectiveness in doing so — power judged by the peace it actually keeps.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the police hold significant powers to enforce the law — and the syllabus weighs both their critical functions and their effectiveness in keeping peace and order. Give one real example from your own life or from the Kiribati you live in, and name the lesson's key words: police, law enforcement, peace and order.
