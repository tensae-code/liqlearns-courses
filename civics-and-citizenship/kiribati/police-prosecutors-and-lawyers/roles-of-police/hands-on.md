# Hands-on — Roles of Police

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
The police can stop, question and arrest. What are those powers for — and what would your island look like without them? This lesson teaches the police's role, powers and functions.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the police hold significant powers to enforce the law — and the syllabus weighs both their critical functions and their effectiveness in keeping peace and order. This lesson stands on the KCSE Civic syllabus alone — evidence only, labelled as such. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
