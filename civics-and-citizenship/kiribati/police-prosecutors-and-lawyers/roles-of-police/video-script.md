This lesson is about one thing.
The police hold significant powers to enforce the law — and the syllabus weighs both their critical functions and their effectiveness in keeping peace and order. This lesson stands on the KCSE Civic syllabus alone — evidence only, labelled as such.

The syllabus names police and police officers and teaches the police's significant powers in enforcing the law.

Their critical functions keep peace and order; the syllabus also weighs their effectiveness in doing so — power judged by the peace it actually keeps.
