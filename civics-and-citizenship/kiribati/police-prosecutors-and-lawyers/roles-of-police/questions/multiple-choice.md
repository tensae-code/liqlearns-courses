Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's first teaching — Police and their powers?
a) The syllabus names police and police officers and teaches the police's significant powers in enforcing the law.  b) it has nothing to do with how Kiribati governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
