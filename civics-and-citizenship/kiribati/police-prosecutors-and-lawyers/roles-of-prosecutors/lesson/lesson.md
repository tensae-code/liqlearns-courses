# Roles of Prosecutors

## Do it first

In a criminal case, who speaks for the community against the accused — and what rules bind that speaker? This lesson teaches the prosecutor's role and its standards.

## Sub lesson 1 — Prosecution and the three functions

Prosecution is bringing a criminal case on the community's behalf; the syllabus teaches the prosecutor's three main functions.

## Sub lesson 2 — Ethical and professional standards

The prosecutor's role is bound by ethical and professional standards — the case must be brought fairly, not merely won. The syllabus teaches those standards as part of the role.
