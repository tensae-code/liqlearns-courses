# Roles of Prosecutors — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The prosecutor's role is bound by ethical and professional standards — the case must be brought fairly, not merely won. The syllabus teaches those standards as part of the role.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that the prosecutor brings the case for the community: three main functions, held to ethical and professional standards. This lesson stands on the KCSE Civic syllabus alone — evidence only, labelled as such. — and that it has no connection to the rest of Kiribati's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Kiribati's own course, in its own order.
