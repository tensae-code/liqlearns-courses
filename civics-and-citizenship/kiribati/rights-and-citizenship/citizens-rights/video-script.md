# Video script — Citizens' Rights

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A citizen of Kiribati holds rights as a citizen — and citizenship itself has a road in: how one becomes a citizen. This lesson stands on the KCSE Civic syllabus alone — evidence only, labelled as such.

The syllabus teaches citizenship and the rights that belong to citizens as citizens.

There are set ways a person becomes a citizen of Kiribati; the syllabus teaches them as part of knowing one's own status.

Among citizens' rights, the syllabus names a citizen's right that supports democratic government — the citizen's own part in keeping government democratic.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
