# Citizens' Rights — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's first teaching — Citizenship and citizens' rights?
a) The syllabus teaches citizenship and the rights that belong to citizens as citizens.  b) it has nothing to do with how Kiribati governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The syllabus teaches citizenship and the rights that belong to citizens as citizens.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) a citizen of Kiribati holds rights as a citizen — and citizenship itself has a road in: how one becomes a citizen. This lesson stands on the KCSE Civic syllabus alone — evidence only, labelled as such.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Kiribati
**Answer: a) a citizen of Kiribati holds rights as a citizen — and citizenship itself has a road in: how one becomes a citizen. This lesson stands on the KCSE Civic syllabus alone — evidence only, labelled as such.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
