# Citizens' Rights — matching

Match each part of the lesson with its teaching.

**1.** Citizenship and citizens' rights — a)

**2.** How one becomes a citizen — b)

**3.** A right that supports democracy — c)


Meanings:
a) The syllabus teaches citizenship and the rights that belong to citizens as citizens.
b) There are set ways a person becomes a citizen of Kiribati; the syllabus teaches them as part of knowing one's own status.
c) Among citizens' rights, the syllabus names a citizen's right that supports democratic government — the citizen's own part in keeping government democratic.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b; 3. c
