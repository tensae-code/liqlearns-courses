# Citizens' Rights

## Do it first

What makes you a citizen of Kiribati — and what could a citizen from another country do to become one? Write what you think, then check it against this lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Citizenship and citizens' rights

The syllabus teaches citizenship and the rights that belong to citizens as citizens.

## Sub lesson 2 — How one becomes a citizen

There are set ways a person becomes a citizen of Kiribati; the syllabus teaches them as part of knowing one's own status.

## Sub lesson 3 — A right that supports democracy

Among citizens' rights, the syllabus names a citizen's right that supports democratic government — the citizen's own part in keeping government democratic.
