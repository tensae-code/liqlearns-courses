# Fundamental Rights

## Do it first

Write down one thing you believe no one — not even the government — should be allowed to do to you. The Constitution of Kiribati protects a list of such things. This lesson teaches them.

## Sub lesson 1 — What a fundamental right is

A fundamental right is a right protected by the Constitution itself, held by the person and not granted by favour.

## Sub lesson 2 — Why fundamental rights matter

The syllabus teaches why fundamental rights matter, and names at least three rights protected under Kiribati's Constitution.

## Sub lesson 3 — Rights in a healthy family

Rights are not only for courts: the syllabus places fundamental rights inside a healthy family, where respect for each person's rights is practised daily.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: fundamental rights are the rights the Constitution itself protects — they matter because without them a citizen stands unprotected, and they have a place even in a healthy family.
