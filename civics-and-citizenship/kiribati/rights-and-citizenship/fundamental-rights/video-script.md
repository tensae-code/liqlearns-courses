# Video script — Fundamental Rights

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Fundamental rights are the rights the Constitution itself protects — they matter because without them a citizen stands unprotected, and they have a place even in a healthy family. This lesson stands on the KCSE Civic syllabus alone — evidence only, labelled as such.

A fundamental right is a right protected by the Constitution itself, held by the person and not granted by favour.

The syllabus teaches why fundamental rights matter, and names at least three rights protected under Kiribati's Constitution.

Rights are not only for courts: the syllabus places fundamental rights inside a healthy family, where respect for each person's rights is practised daily.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
