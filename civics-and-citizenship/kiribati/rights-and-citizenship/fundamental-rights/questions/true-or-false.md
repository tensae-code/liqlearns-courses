# Fundamental Rights — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Rights are not only for courts: the syllabus places fundamental rights inside a healthy family, where respect for each person's rights is practised daily.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that fundamental rights are the rights the Constitution itself protects — they matter because without them a citizen stands unprotected, and they have a place even in a healthy family. This lesson stands on the KCSE Civic syllabus alone — evidence only, labelled as such. — and that it has no connection to the rest of Kiribati's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Kiribati's own course, in its own order.
