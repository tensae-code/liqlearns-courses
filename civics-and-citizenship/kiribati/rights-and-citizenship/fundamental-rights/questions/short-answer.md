# Fundamental Rights — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "What a fundamental right is" teach?
**Answer:** A fundamental right is a right protected by the Constitution itself, held by the person and not granted by favour.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** Fundamental rights are the rights the Constitution itself protects — they matter because without them a citizen stands unprotected, and they have a place even in a healthy family. This lesson stands on the KCSE Civic syllabus alone — evidence only, labelled as such.
