# Fundamental Rights — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's first teaching — What a fundamental right is?
a) A fundamental right is a right protected by the Constitution itself, held by the person and not granted by favour.  b) it has nothing to do with how Kiribati governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) A fundamental right is a right protected by the Constitution itself, held by the person and not granted by favour.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
