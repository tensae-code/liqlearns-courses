# Hands-on — Traditional, Social and Spiritual Values

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name three values your family or community handed to you — things you were taught are right before you ever chose them. Write where each came from. The guide's second strand teaches those inheritances by name.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Kiribati's traditional, social and spiritual values are a teaching strand in their own right — the values a pupil inherits from family, community and faith. This lesson stands on the Year 7 teacher guide alone — evidence only, labelled as such. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
