# Traditional, Social and Spiritual Values

## Do it first

Name three values your family or community handed to you — things you were taught are right before you ever chose them. Write where each came from. The guide's second strand teaches those inheritances by name.

Strand 2, Traditional, Social and Spiritual Values, teaches Kiribati's traditional, social and spiritual values as a strand of the Year 7 course — the shared values that hold family and community together. Placed as filed and marked TEACHER.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Kiribati's traditional, social and spiritual values are a teaching strand in their own right — the values a pupil inherits from family, community and faith. This lesson stands on the Year 7 teacher guide alone — evidence only, labelled as such. Give one real example from your own life or from the Kiribati you live in, and name the lesson's key words: traditional values, social values, spiritual values, Strand 2. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
