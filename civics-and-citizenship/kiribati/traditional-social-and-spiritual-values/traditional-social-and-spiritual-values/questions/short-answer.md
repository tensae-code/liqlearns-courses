# Traditional, Social and Spiritual Values — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "The values strand" teach?
**Answer:** Strand 2, Traditional, Social and Spiritual Values, teaches Kiribati's traditional, social and spiritual values as a strand of the Year 7 course — the shared values that hold family and community together. Placed as filed and marked TEACHER.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** Kiribati's traditional, social and spiritual values are a teaching strand in their own right — the values a pupil inherits from family, community and faith. This lesson stands on the Year 7 teacher guide alone — evidence only, labelled as such.
