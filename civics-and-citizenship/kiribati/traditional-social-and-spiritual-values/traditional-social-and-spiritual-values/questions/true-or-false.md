# Traditional, Social and Spiritual Values — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Strand 2, Traditional, Social and Spiritual Values, teaches Kiribati's traditional, social and spiritual values as a strand of the Year 7 course — the shared values that hold family and community together. Placed as filed and marked TEACHER.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that Kiribati's traditional, social and spiritual values are a teaching strand in their own right — the values a pupil inherits from family, community and faith. This lesson stands on the Year 7 teacher guide alone — evidence only, labelled as such. — and that it has no connection to the rest of Kiribati's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Kiribati's own course, in its own order.
