# Video script — Traditional, Social and Spiritual Values

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Kiribati's traditional, social and spiritual values are a teaching strand in their own right — the values a pupil inherits from family, community and faith. This lesson stands on the Year 7 teacher guide alone — evidence only, labelled as such.

Strand 2, Traditional, Social and Spiritual Values, teaches Kiribati's traditional, social and spiritual values as a strand of the Year 7 course — the shared values that hold family and community together. Placed as filed and marked TEACHER.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
