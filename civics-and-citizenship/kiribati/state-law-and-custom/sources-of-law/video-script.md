# Video script — Sources of Law

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Law in Kiribati comes from named sources: the citizen who can identify, describe and explain each source — with examples — knows where any rule gets its force. This lesson stands on the KCSE Civic syllabus alone — evidence only, labelled as such.

The syllabus teaches learners to identify the sources of law in Kiribati — the places law comes from, including the Constitution, acts of the Maneaba ni Maungatabu, and custom.

Naming a source is not enough: the syllabus requires describing each source and explaining it with examples from Kiribati life, so the citizen can tell which source a given rule stands on.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
