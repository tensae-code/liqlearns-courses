# Sources of Law

## Do it first

A rule is quoted at you — at school, at home, in the village. Ask: where does this rule come from, and who made it binding? This lesson teaches Kiribati's answers: the sources of law.

## Sub lesson 1 — Identifying the sources

The syllabus teaches learners to identify the sources of law in Kiribati — the places law comes from, including the Constitution, acts of the Maneaba ni Maungatabu, and custom.

## Sub lesson 2 — Describing and explaining with examples

Naming a source is not enough: the syllabus requires describing each source and explaining it with examples from Kiribati life, so the citizen can tell which source a given rule stands on.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: law in Kiribati comes from named sources: the citizen who can identify, describe and explain each source — with examples — knows where any rule gets its force. This lesson stands on the KCSE Civic syllabus alone — evidence only, labelled as such. Give one real example from your own life or from the Kiribati you live in, and name the lesson's key words: sources of law, law. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
