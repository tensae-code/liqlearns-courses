# Sources of Law — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "Identifying the sources" teach?
**Answer:** The syllabus teaches learners to identify the sources of law in Kiribati — the places law comes from, including the Constitution, acts of the Maneaba ni Maungatabu, and custom.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** Law in Kiribati comes from named sources: the citizen who can identify, describe and explain each source — with examples — knows where any rule gets its force. This lesson stands on the KCSE Civic syllabus alone — evidence only, labelled as such.
