# Sources of Law — matching

Match each part of the lesson with its teaching.

**1.** Identifying the sources — a)

**2.** Describing and explaining with examples — b)


Meanings:
a) The syllabus teaches learners to identify the sources of law in Kiribati — the places law comes from, including the Constitution, acts of the Maneaba ni Maungatabu, and custom.
b) Naming a source is not enough: the syllabus requires describing each source and explaining it with examples from Kiribati life, so the citizen can tell which source a given rule stands on.
