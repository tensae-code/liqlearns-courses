# Hands-on — Law Making and the Maneaba

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Imagine your island with no laws at all for one week. Write three things that would go wrong first. This lesson shows the machine Kiribati built so that never happens: law-making in the Maneaba ni Maungatabu.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: laws are made in steps by the Maneaba ni Maungatabu, Kiribati's national parliament: a bill is proposed, passed, assented to, and becomes an act — and a country with no laws has no common protection. This lesson stands on the KCSE Civic syllabus alone — evidence only, labelled as such. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
