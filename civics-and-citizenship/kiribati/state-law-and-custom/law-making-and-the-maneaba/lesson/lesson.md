# Law Making and the Maneaba

## Do it first

Imagine your island with no laws at all for one week. Write three things that would go wrong first. This lesson shows the machine Kiribati built so that never happens: law-making in the Maneaba ni Maungatabu.

## Sub lesson 1 — Bills, assent and acts

A proposed law is a bill — private or government. Passed by the Maneaba ni Maungatabu and given assent, a bill becomes an act: law that binds everyone.

## Sub lesson 2 — The steps and the Maneaba's functions

The syllabus teaches the steps of law-making in order and the functions of the Maneaba ni Maungatabu, the national parliament where Kiribati's laws are made.

## Sub lesson 3 — Why law-making matters

The syllabus asks why law-making matters and what happens with no laws: without made law there is no shared rule to protect the weak, settle disputes or order common life.
