# Law Making and the Maneaba — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's first teaching — Bills, assent and acts?
a) A proposed law is a bill — private or government. Passed by the Maneaba ni Maungatabu and given assent, a bill becomes an act: law that binds everyone.  b) it has nothing to do with how Kiribati governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) A proposed law is a bill — private or government. Passed by the Maneaba ni Maungatabu and given assent, a bill becomes an act: law that binds everyone.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
