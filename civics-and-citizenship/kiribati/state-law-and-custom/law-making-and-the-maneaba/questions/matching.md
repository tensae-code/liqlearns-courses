# Law Making and the Maneaba — matching

Match each part of the lesson with its teaching.

**1.** Bills, assent and acts — a)

**2.** The steps and the Maneaba's functions — b)

**3.** Why law-making matters — c)


Meanings:
a) A proposed law is a bill — private or government. Passed by the Maneaba ni Maungatabu and given assent, a bill becomes an act: law that binds everyone.
b) The syllabus teaches the steps of law-making in order and the functions of the Maneaba ni Maungatabu, the national parliament where Kiribati's laws are made.
c) The syllabus asks why law-making matters and what happens with no laws: without made law there is no shared rule to protect the weak, settle disputes or order common life.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b; 3. c
