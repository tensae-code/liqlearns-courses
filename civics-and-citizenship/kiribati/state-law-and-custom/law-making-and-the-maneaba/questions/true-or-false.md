# Law Making and the Maneaba — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The syllabus asks why law-making matters and what happens with no laws: without made law there is no shared rule to protect the weak, settle disputes or order common life.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that laws are made in steps by the Maneaba ni Maungatabu, Kiribati's national parliament: a bill is proposed, passed, assented to, and becomes an act — and a country with no laws has no common protection. This lesson stands on the KCSE Civic syllabus alone — evidence only, labelled as such. — and that it has no connection to the rest of Kiribati's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Kiribati's own course, in its own order.
