# Law Making and the Maneaba — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "Bills, assent and acts" teach?
**Answer:** A proposed law is a bill — private or government. Passed by the Maneaba ni Maungatabu and given assent, a bill becomes an act: law that binds everyone.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** Laws are made in steps by the Maneaba ni Maungatabu, Kiribati's national parliament: a bill is proposed, passed, assented to, and becomes an act — and a country with no laws has no common protection. This lesson stands on the KCSE Civic syllabus alone — evidence only, labelled as such.
