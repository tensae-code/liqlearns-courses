# Video script — Government in Kiribati

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Kiribati is governed democratically: a head of state and a head of government, citizens with democratic rights, a government with set functions, and levels of government reaching from the nation to the island. This lesson stands on the KCSE Civic syllabus alone — evidence only, labelled as such.

Kiribati's government is democratic: power rests on the citizens' choice, and the syllabus begins by distinguishing the head of state from the head of government and naming what each does.

Citizenship carries democratic rights: the syllabus teaches citizenship and the citizen's democratic rights together with the citizen's role in government.

Government has set functions, and it works at levels — Kiribati's levels of government run from the national government down to local and island government, each with its part.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
