# Government in Kiribati — matching

Match each part of the lesson with its teaching.

**1.** Democratic government — a)

**2.** Citizenship and democratic rights — b)

**3.** Functions and levels of government — c)


Meanings:
a) Kiribati's government is democratic: power rests on the citizens' choice, and the syllabus begins by distinguishing the head of state from the head of government and naming what each does.
b) Citizenship carries democratic rights: the syllabus teaches citizenship and the citizen's democratic rights together with the citizen's role in government.
c) Government has set functions, and it works at levels — Kiribati's levels of government run from the national government down to local and island government, each with its part.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b; 3. c
