# Government in Kiribati — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "Democratic government" teach?
**Answer:** Kiribati's government is democratic: power rests on the citizens' choice, and the syllabus begins by distinguishing the head of state from the head of government and naming what each does.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** Kiribati is governed democratically: a head of state and a head of government, citizens with democratic rights, a government with set functions, and levels of government reaching from the nation to the island. This lesson stands on the KCSE Civic syllabus alone — evidence only, labelled as such.
