# Government in Kiribati — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Government has set functions, and it works at levels — Kiribati's levels of government run from the national government down to local and island government, each with its part.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that Kiribati is governed democratically: a head of state and a head of government, citizens with democratic rights, a government with set functions, and levels of government reaching from the nation to the island. This lesson stands on the KCSE Civic syllabus alone — evidence only, labelled as such. — and that it has no connection to the rest of Kiribati's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Kiribati's own course, in its own order.
