# Lesson plan — Government in Kiribati

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Kiribati · **Title:** State, Law and Custom · **Lesson:** Government in Kiribati
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — State, Law and Custom (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Kiribati is governed democratically: a head of state and a head of government, citizens with democratic rights, a government with set functions, and levels of government reaching from the nation to the island. This lesson stands on the KCSE Civic syllabus alone — evidence only, labelled as such.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Democratic government** — Kiribati's government is democratic: power rests on the citizens' choice, and the syllabus begins by distinguishing the head of state from the head of government and naming what each does.
2. **Citizenship and democratic rights** — Citizenship carries democratic rights: the syllabus teaches citizenship and the citizen's democratic rights together with the citizen's role in government.
3. **Functions and levels of government** — Government has set functions, and it works at levels — Kiribati's levels of government run from the national government down to local and island government, each with its part.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
