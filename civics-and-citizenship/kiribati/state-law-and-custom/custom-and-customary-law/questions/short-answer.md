# Custom and Customary Law — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "Defining custom and customary law" teach?
**Answer:** Custom is a people's established practice; customary law is custom that carries the force of law. The syllabus teaches both definitions plainly.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** Custom is what a people practises; customary law is custom recognised as binding — Kiribati teaches both, and the difference between them. This lesson stands on the KCSE Civic syllabus alone — evidence only, labelled as such.
