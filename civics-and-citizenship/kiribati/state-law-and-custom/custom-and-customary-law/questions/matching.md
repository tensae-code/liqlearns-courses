# Custom and Customary Law — matching

Match each part of the lesson with its teaching.

**1.** Defining custom and customary law — a)

**2.** Customs practised today — b)

**3.** The difference — c)


Meanings:
a) Custom is a people's established practice; customary law is custom that carries the force of law. The syllabus teaches both definitions plainly.
b) The syllabus names customs practised in Kiribati society today, so the teaching stays in the learner's own community, not in a book example from elsewhere.
c) Custom and customary law differ in force: a custom guides, customary law binds. Telling them apart is the lesson's test.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b; 3. c
