# Custom and Customary Law — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Custom and customary law differ in force: a custom guides, customary law binds. Telling them apart is the lesson's test.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that custom is what a people practises; customary law is custom recognised as binding — Kiribati teaches both, and the difference between them. This lesson stands on the KCSE Civic syllabus alone — evidence only, labelled as such. — and that it has no connection to the rest of Kiribati's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Kiribati's own course, in its own order.
