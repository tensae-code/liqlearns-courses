# Custom and Customary Law — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's first teaching — Defining custom and customary law?
a) Custom is a people's established practice; customary law is custom that carries the force of law. The syllabus teaches both definitions plainly.  b) it has nothing to do with how Kiribati governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Custom is a people's established practice; customary law is custom that carries the force of law. The syllabus teaches both definitions plainly.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
