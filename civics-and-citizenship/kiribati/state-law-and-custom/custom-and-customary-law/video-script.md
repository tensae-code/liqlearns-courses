# Video script — Custom and Customary Law

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Custom is what a people practises; customary law is custom recognised as binding — Kiribati teaches both, and the difference between them. This lesson stands on the KCSE Civic syllabus alone — evidence only, labelled as such.

Custom is a people's established practice; customary law is custom that carries the force of law. The syllabus teaches both definitions plainly.

The syllabus names customs practised in Kiribati society today, so the teaching stays in the learner's own community, not in a book example from elsewhere.

Custom and customary law differ in force: a custom guides, customary law binds. Telling them apart is the lesson's test.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
