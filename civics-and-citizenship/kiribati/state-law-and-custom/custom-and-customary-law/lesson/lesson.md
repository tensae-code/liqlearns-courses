# Custom and Customary Law

## Do it first

Name one custom practised in your community today. Now ask: is it simply what people do, or is it treated as a rule people must follow? This lesson teaches that exact difference.

## Sub lesson 1 — Defining custom and customary law

Custom is a people's established practice; customary law is custom that carries the force of law. The syllabus teaches both definitions plainly.

## Sub lesson 2 — Customs practised today

The syllabus names customs practised in Kiribati society today, so the teaching stays in the learner's own community, not in a book example from elsewhere.

## Sub lesson 3 — The difference

Custom and customary law differ in force: a custom guides, customary law binds. Telling them apart is the lesson's test.

## Say it to your companion
