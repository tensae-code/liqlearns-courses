This lesson is about one thing.
Democracy means the people are sovereign, and the Constitution splits their power among three arms of government that check and balance one another. This lesson stands on the KCSE Civic syllabus alone — evidence only, labelled as such.

Democracy rests on sovereignty of the people: government holds power from the citizens and answers to them.

The Constitution sets the functions of the three arms of government — law-making, executive and judicial — and gives each its own work.

Each arm checks and balances the others, so no single arm can rule alone: the syllabus teaches how each check works.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
