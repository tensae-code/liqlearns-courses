# Democratic Government System — matching

Match each part of the lesson with its teaching.

**1.** Democracy and sovereignty — a)

**2.** The three arms under the Constitution — b)

**3.** Checks and balances — c)


Meanings:
a) Democracy rests on sovereignty of the people: government holds power from the citizens and answers to them.
b) The Constitution sets the functions of the three arms of government — law-making, executive and judicial — and gives each its own work.
c) Each arm checks and balances the others, so no single arm can rule alone: the syllabus teaches how each check works.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b; 3. c
