# Democratic Government System — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "Democracy and sovereignty" teach?
**Answer:** Democracy rests on sovereignty of the people: government holds power from the citizens and answers to them.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** Democracy means the people are sovereign, and the Constitution splits their power among three arms of government that check and balance one another. This lesson stands on the KCSE Civic syllabus alone — evidence only, labelled as such.
