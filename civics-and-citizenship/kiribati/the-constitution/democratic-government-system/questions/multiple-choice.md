# Democratic Government System — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's first teaching — Democracy and sovereignty?
a) Democracy rests on sovereignty of the people: government holds power from the citizens and answers to them.  b) it has nothing to do with how Kiribati governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Democracy rests on sovereignty of the people: government holds power from the citizens and answers to them.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) democracy means the people are sovereign, and the Constitution splits their power among three arms of government that check and balance one another. This lesson stands on the KCSE Civic syllabus alone — evidence only, labelled as such.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Kiribati
**Answer: a) democracy means the people are sovereign, and the Constitution splits their power among three arms of government that check and balance one another. This lesson stands on the KCSE Civic syllabus alone — evidence only, labelled as such.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
