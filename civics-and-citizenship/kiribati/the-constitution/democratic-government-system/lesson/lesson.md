# Democratic Government System

## Do it first

If one office held all power — making laws, enforcing them and judging disputes — what could stop it abusing that power? Write your answer. This lesson shows the Constitution's answer: three arms, each checking the others.

## Sub lesson 1 — Democracy and sovereignty

Democracy rests on sovereignty of the people: government holds power from the citizens and answers to them.

## Sub lesson 2 — The three arms under the Constitution

The Constitution sets the functions of the three arms of government — law-making, executive and judicial — and gives each its own work.

## Sub lesson 3 — Checks and balances

Each arm checks and balances the others, so no single arm can rule alone: the syllabus teaches how each check works.
