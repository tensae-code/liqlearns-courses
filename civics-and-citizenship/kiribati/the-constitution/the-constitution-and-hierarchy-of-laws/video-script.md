# Video script — The Constitution and Hierarchy of Laws

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The Constitution is the supreme law of Kiribati: it stands at the top of a hierarchy of laws, and every other law must agree with it. This lesson stands on the KCSE Civic syllabus alone — evidence only, labelled as such.

A constitution is the founding law of a state: the syllabus teaches what it is, the features of Kiribati's Constitution, and the two forms a constitution can take.

Laws have sources and an order — a hierarchy. The syllabus teaches the source and hierarchy of laws so the citizen knows which law outranks which.

The Constitution is the supreme law: any law that conflicts with it gives way. That supremacy is what makes every other protection in this course hold.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
