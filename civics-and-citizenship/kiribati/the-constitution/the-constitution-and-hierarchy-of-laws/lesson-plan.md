# Lesson plan — The Constitution and Hierarchy of Laws

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Kiribati · **Title:** The Constitution · **Lesson:** The Constitution and Hierarchy of Laws
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — The Constitution, Rights and the Courts (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the Constitution is the supreme law of Kiribati: it stands at the top of a hierarchy of laws, and every other law must agree with it. This lesson stands on the KCSE Civic syllabus alone — evidence only, labelled as such.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **What a constitution is** — A constitution is the founding law of a state: the syllabus teaches what it is, the features of Kiribati's Constitution, and the two forms a constitution can take.
2. **Source and hierarchy of laws** — Laws have sources and an order — a hierarchy. The syllabus teaches the source and hierarchy of laws so the citizen knows which law outranks which.
3. **The supreme law** — The Constitution is the supreme law: any law that conflicts with it gives way. That supremacy is what makes every other protection in this course hold.
