# The Constitution and Hierarchy of Laws — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "What a constitution is" teach?
**Answer:** A constitution is the founding law of a state: the syllabus teaches what it is, the features of Kiribati's Constitution, and the two forms a constitution can take.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** The Constitution is the supreme law of Kiribati: it stands at the top of a hierarchy of laws, and every other law must agree with it. This lesson stands on the KCSE Civic syllabus alone — evidence only, labelled as such.
