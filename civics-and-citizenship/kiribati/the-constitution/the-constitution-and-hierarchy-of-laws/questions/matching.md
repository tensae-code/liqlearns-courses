# The Constitution and Hierarchy of Laws — matching

Match each part of the lesson with its teaching.

**1.** What a constitution is — a)

**2.** Source and hierarchy of laws — b)

**3.** The supreme law — c)


Meanings:
a) A constitution is the founding law of a state: the syllabus teaches what it is, the features of Kiribati's Constitution, and the two forms a constitution can take.
b) Laws have sources and an order — a hierarchy. The syllabus teaches the source and hierarchy of laws so the citizen knows which law outranks which.
c) The Constitution is the supreme law: any law that conflicts with it gives way. That supremacy is what makes every other protection in this course hold.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b; 3. c
