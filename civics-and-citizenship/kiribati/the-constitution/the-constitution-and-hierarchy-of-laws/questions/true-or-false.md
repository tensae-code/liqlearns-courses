# The Constitution and Hierarchy of Laws — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The Constitution is the supreme law: any law that conflicts with it gives way. That supremacy is what makes every other protection in this course hold.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that the Constitution is the supreme law of Kiribati: it stands at the top of a hierarchy of laws, and every other law must agree with it. This lesson stands on the KCSE Civic syllabus alone — evidence only, labelled as such. — and that it has no connection to the rest of Kiribati's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Kiribati's own course, in its own order.
