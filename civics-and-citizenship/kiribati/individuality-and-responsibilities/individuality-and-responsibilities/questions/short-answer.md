# Individuality and Responsibilities — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "The first strand" teach?
**Answer:** Strand 1 of the Year 7 Moral Education guide, Individuality and Responsibilities, runs weeks 1–12: the pupil's own character and duties, taught through weekly lesson plans. It is the guide's own teaching content, placed as filed and marked TEACHER.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** The year's first strand teaches the pupil's own character and duties: who I am, and what is mine to do (lesson plans, weeks 1–12). This lesson stands on the Year 7 teacher guide alone — evidence only, labelled as such.
