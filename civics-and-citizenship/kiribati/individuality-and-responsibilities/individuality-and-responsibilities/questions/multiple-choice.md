# Individuality and Responsibilities — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's first teaching — The first strand?
a) Strand 1 of the Year 7 Moral Education guide, Individuality and Responsibilities, runs weeks 1–12: the pupil's own character and duties, taught through weekly lesson plans. It is the guide's own teaching content, placed as filed and marked TEACHER.  b) it has nothing to do with how Kiribati governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Strand 1 of the Year 7 Moral Education guide, Individuality and Responsibilities, runs weeks 1–12: the pupil's own character and duties, taught through weekly lesson plans. It is the guide's own teaching content, placed as filed and marked TEACHER.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) the year's first strand teaches the pupil's own character and duties: who I am, and what is mine to do (lesson plans, weeks 1–12). This lesson stands on the Year 7 teacher guide alone — evidence only, labelled as such.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Kiribati
**Answer: a) the year's first strand teaches the pupil's own character and duties: who I am, and what is mine to do (lesson plans, weeks 1–12). This lesson stands on the Year 7 teacher guide alone — evidence only, labelled as such.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
