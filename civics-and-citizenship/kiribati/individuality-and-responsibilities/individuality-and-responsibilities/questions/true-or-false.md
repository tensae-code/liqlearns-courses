# Individuality and Responsibilities — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Strand 1 of the Year 7 Moral Education guide, Individuality and Responsibilities, runs weeks 1–12: the pupil's own character and duties, taught through weekly lesson plans. It is the guide's own teaching content, placed as filed and marked TEACHER.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that the year's first strand teaches the pupil's own character and duties: who I am, and what is mine to do (lesson plans, weeks 1–12). This lesson stands on the Year 7 teacher guide alone — evidence only, labelled as such. — and that it has no connection to the rest of Kiribati's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Kiribati's own course, in its own order.
