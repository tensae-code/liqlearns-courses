# Hands-on — Individuality and Responsibilities

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write two lists: 'who I am' (your character, as you honestly know it) and 'what is mine to do' (your duties this week). The Year 7 guide's first strand joins exactly these two lists.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the year's first strand teaches the pupil's own character and duties: who I am, and what is mine to do (lesson plans, weeks 1–12). This lesson stands on the Year 7 teacher guide alone — evidence only, labelled as such. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
