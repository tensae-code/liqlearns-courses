# Individuality and Responsibilities

## Do it first

Write two lists: 'who I am' (your character, as you honestly know it) and 'what is mine to do' (your duties this week). The Year 7 guide's first strand joins exactly these two lists.

Strand 1 of the Year 7 Moral Education guide, Individuality and Responsibilities, runs weeks 1–12: the pupil's own character and duties, taught through weekly lesson plans. It is the guide's own teaching content, placed as filed and marked TEACHER.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the year's first strand teaches the pupil's own character and duties: who I am, and what is mine to do (lesson plans, weeks 1–12). This lesson stands on the Year 7 teacher guide alone — evidence only, labelled as such. Give one real example from your own life or from the Kiribati you live in, and name the lesson's key words: individuality, responsibilities, Strand 1. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
