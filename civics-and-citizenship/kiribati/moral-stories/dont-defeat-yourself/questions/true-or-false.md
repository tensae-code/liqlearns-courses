# Don't Defeat Yourself — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Don't Defeat Yourself teaches that a person's own surrender is the deepest defeat: keep standing, keep trying, and the difficulty stays outside you instead of becoming you.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that do not defeat yourself: the person who gives up on himself has lost before anyone else could beat him. — and that it has no connection to the rest of Kiribati's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Kiribati's own course, in its own order.
