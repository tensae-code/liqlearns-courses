# Don't Defeat Yourself — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's first teaching — Not giving up on oneself?
a) Don't Defeat Yourself teaches that a person's own surrender is the deepest defeat: keep standing, keep trying, and the difficulty stays outside you instead of becoming you.  b) it has nothing to do with how Kiribati governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Don't Defeat Yourself teaches that a person's own surrender is the deepest defeat: keep standing, keep trying, and the difficulty stays outside you instead of becoming you.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) do not defeat yourself: the person who gives up on himself has lost before anyone else could beat him.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Kiribati
**Answer: a) do not defeat yourself: the person who gives up on himself has lost before anyone else could beat him.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
