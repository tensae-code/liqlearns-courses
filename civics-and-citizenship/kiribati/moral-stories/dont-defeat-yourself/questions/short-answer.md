# Don't Defeat Yourself — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "Not giving up on oneself" teach?
**Answer:** Don't Defeat Yourself teaches that a person's own surrender is the deepest defeat: keep standing, keep trying, and the difficulty stays outside you instead of becoming you.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** Do not defeat yourself: the person who gives up on himself has lost before anyone else could beat him.
