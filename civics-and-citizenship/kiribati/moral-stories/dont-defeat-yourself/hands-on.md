# Hands-on — Don't Defeat Yourself

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Think of a time you nearly gave up on something — learning, work, a duty. What would finishing have needed? Write it down. This closing text of the Year 7 readers teaches the refusal to give up on oneself.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: do not defeat yourself: the person who gives up on himself has lost before anyone else could beat him. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
