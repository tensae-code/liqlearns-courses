# Video script — Don't Defeat Yourself

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Do not defeat yourself: the person who gives up on himself has lost before anyone else could beat him.

Don't Defeat Yourself teaches that a person's own surrender is the deepest defeat: keep standing, keep trying, and the difficulty stays outside you instead of becoming you.

The filing carries this text from the book's contents page (the contents-verified read of Term Three); its provenance row below is carried exactly as filed.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
