# Honour in the Family — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "Bita's family and Erea" teach?
**Answer:** In Bita's family the first thing is to honour God, and from that flow love, honour and respect for one another. Erea, who had none of it, learns it from Bita's family and builds her own family on it — honour can be learned, and a family can be rebuilt on it.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** In Bita's family the first thing is to honour God, and from that flow love, honour and respect for one another — and Erea, who had none of it, learns it and builds her own family on it.
