# Honour in the Family — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** In Bita's family the first thing is to honour God, and from that flow love, honour and respect for one another. Erea, who had none of it, learns it from Bita's family and builds her own family on it — honour can be learned, and a family can be rebuilt on it.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that in Bita's family the first thing is to honour God, and from that flow love, honour and respect for one another — and Erea, who had none of it, learns it and builds her own family on it. — and that it has no connection to the rest of Kiribati's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Kiribati's own course, in its own order.
