# Honour in the Family

## Do it first

Think of a family where people speak to one another with honour, and one where they do not. Write one thing that is different in daily life between the two homes. This lesson's closing story of Term Two shows where honour in a family starts.

In Bita's family the first thing is to honour God, and from that flow love, honour and respect for one another. Erea, who had none of it, learns it from Bita's family and builds her own family on it — honour can be learned, and a family can be rebuilt on it.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: in Bita's family the first thing is to honour God, and from that flow love, honour and respect for one another — and Erea, who had none of it, learns it and builds her own family on it. Give one real example from your own life or from the Kiribati you live in, and name the lesson's key words: honour, family, respect, Bita, Erea. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
