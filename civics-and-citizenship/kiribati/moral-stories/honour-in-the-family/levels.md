# Honour in the Family — depth

This lesson is ONE lesson with no sub lessons: it teaches a single thing, whole. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: In Bita's family the first thing is to honour God, and from that flow love, honour and respect for one another. Erea, who had none of it, learns it from Bita's family and builds her own family on it — honour can be learned, and a family can be rebuilt on it. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
