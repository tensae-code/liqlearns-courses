# Video script — The Change That Brings Happiness

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A person can change: Teeta, a heavy smoker and drinker, sees his friend die of drink and tobacco, gives up smoking, alcohol and grog, and becomes a committed father — change of habit is the road to family happiness.

Teeta smoked and drank heavily. When his friend died of drink and tobacco, Teeta saw where his own road ended, and he gave up smoking, alcohol and grog. He became a committed father, and his family's happiness came from that change.

The story is by Vicky Claude, a named Kiribati author, and it teaches the way this course teaches: a village character, a turning point, a moral turn — not a rule or a definition.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
