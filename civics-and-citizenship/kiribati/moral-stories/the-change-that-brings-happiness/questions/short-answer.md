# The Change That Brings Happiness — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "Teeta's change" teach?
**Answer:** Teeta smoked and drank heavily. When his friend died of drink and tobacco, Teeta saw where his own road ended, and he gave up smoking, alcohol and grog. He became a committed father, and his family's happiness came from that change.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** A person can change: Teeta, a heavy smoker and drinker, sees his friend die of drink and tobacco, gives up smoking, alcohol and grog, and becomes a committed father — change of habit is the road to family happiness.
