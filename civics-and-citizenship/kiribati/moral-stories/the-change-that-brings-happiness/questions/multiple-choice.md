# The Change That Brings Happiness — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's first teaching — Teeta's change?
a) Teeta smoked and drank heavily. When his friend died of drink and tobacco, Teeta saw where his own road ended, and he gave up smoking, alcohol and grog. He became a committed father, and his family's happiness came from that change.  b) it has nothing to do with how Kiribati governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Teeta smoked and drank heavily. When his friend died of drink and tobacco, Teeta saw where his own road ended, and he gave up smoking, alcohol and grog. He became a committed father, and his family's happiness came from that change.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) a person can change: Teeta, a heavy smoker and drinker, sees his friend die of drink and tobacco, gives up smoking, alcohol and grog, and becomes a committed father — change of habit is the road to family happiness.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Kiribati
**Answer: a) a person can change: Teeta, a heavy smoker and drinker, sees his friend die of drink and tobacco, gives up smoking, alcohol and grog, and becomes a committed father — change of habit is the road to family happiness.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
