# The Change That Brings Happiness — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Teeta smoked and drank heavily. When his friend died of drink and tobacco, Teeta saw where his own road ended, and he gave up smoking, alcohol and grog. He became a committed father, and his family's happiness came from that change.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that a person can change: Teeta, a heavy smoker and drinker, sees his friend die of drink and tobacco, gives up smoking, alcohol and grog, and becomes a committed father — change of habit is the road to family happiness. — and that it has no connection to the rest of Kiribati's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Kiribati's own course, in its own order.
