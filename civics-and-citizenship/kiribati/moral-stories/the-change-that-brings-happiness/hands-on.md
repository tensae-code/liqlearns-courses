# Hands-on — The Change That Brings Happiness

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name one habit — in yourself or in a home you know — that makes family life harder. Now name what life in that home would look like if the habit stopped. Hold that picture: this lesson's story walks it.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: a person can change: Teeta, a heavy smoker and drinker, sees his friend die of drink and tobacco, gives up smoking, alcohol and grog, and becomes a committed father — change of habit is the road to family happiness. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
