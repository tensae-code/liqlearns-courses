# Think Before You Act — depth

This lesson is ONE lesson with no sub lessons: it teaches a single thing, whole. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: Thinking before you act means stopping, picturing what the act will do — to you and to others — and only then choosing. The act you choose after thinking is yours; the act done without thinking chooses you. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
