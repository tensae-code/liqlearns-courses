# Hands-on — Think Before You Act

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Recall something you did quickly that you wished afterwards you had thought about first. Write what the pause would have changed. This lesson — the first text of Term Three — teaches that pause.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: pause and think before you act — the space between wanting to do a thing and doing it is where character works. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
