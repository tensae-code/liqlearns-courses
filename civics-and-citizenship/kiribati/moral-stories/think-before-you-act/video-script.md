# Video script — Think Before You Act

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Pause and think before you act — the space between wanting to do a thing and doing it is where character works.

Thinking before you act means stopping, picturing what the act will do — to you and to others — and only then choosing. The act you choose after thinking is yours; the act done without thinking chooses you.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
