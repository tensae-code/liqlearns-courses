# Think Before You Act — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's first teaching — The pause before acting?
a) Thinking before you act means stopping, picturing what the act will do — to you and to others — and only then choosing. The act you choose after thinking is yours; the act done without thinking chooses you.  b) it has nothing to do with how Kiribati governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Thinking before you act means stopping, picturing what the act will do — to you and to others — and only then choosing. The act you choose after thinking is yours; the act done without thinking chooses you.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) pause and think before you act — the space between wanting to do a thing and doing it is where character works.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Kiribati
**Answer: a) pause and think before you act — the space between wanting to do a thing and doing it is where character works.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
