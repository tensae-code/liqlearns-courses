# Think Before You Act — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "The pause before acting" teach?
**Answer:** Thinking before you act means stopping, picturing what the act will do — to you and to others — and only then choosing. The act you choose after thinking is yours; the act done without thinking chooses you.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** Pause and think before you act — the space between wanting to do a thing and doing it is where character works.
