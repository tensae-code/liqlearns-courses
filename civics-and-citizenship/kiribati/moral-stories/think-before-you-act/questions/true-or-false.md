# Think Before You Act — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Thinking before you act means stopping, picturing what the act will do — to you and to others — and only then choosing. The act you choose after thinking is yours; the act done without thinking chooses you.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that pause and think before you act — the space between wanting to do a thing and doing it is where character works. — and that it has no connection to the rest of Kiribati's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Kiribati's own course, in its own order.
