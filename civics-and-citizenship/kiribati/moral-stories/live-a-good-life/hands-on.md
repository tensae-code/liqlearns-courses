# Hands-on — Live a Good Life

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write down what a good life looks like in your village or town — not riches, but the life people point to and say: that one lived well. List three things that person does. This lesson teaches what living a good life means.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: living a good life is a practice, not a wish — it is what a person does with their days, their family and their neighbours. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
