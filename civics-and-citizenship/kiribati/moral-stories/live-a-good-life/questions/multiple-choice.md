# Live a Good Life — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's first teaching — What a good life means?
a) A good life, as this text teaches it, is built from how a person treats family, neighbours and work each day — the small faithful acts, repeated, are the good life itself.  b) it has nothing to do with how Kiribati governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) A good life, as this text teaches it, is built from how a person treats family, neighbours and work each day — the small faithful acts, repeated, are the good life itself.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) living a good life is a practice, not a wish — it is what a person does with their days, their family and their neighbours.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Kiribati
**Answer: a) living a good life is a practice, not a wish — it is what a person does with their days, their family and their neighbours.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
