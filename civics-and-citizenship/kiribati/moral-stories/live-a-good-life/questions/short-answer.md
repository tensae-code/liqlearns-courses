# Live a Good Life — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "What a good life means" teach?
**Answer:** A good life, as this text teaches it, is built from how a person treats family, neighbours and work each day — the small faithful acts, repeated, are the good life itself.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** Living a good life is a practice, not a wish — it is what a person does with their days, their family and their neighbours.
