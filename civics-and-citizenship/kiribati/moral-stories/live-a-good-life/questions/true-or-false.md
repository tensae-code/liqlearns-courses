# Live a Good Life — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A good life, as this text teaches it, is built from how a person treats family, neighbours and work each day — the small faithful acts, repeated, are the good life itself.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that living a good life is a practice, not a wish — it is what a person does with their days, their family and their neighbours. — and that it has no connection to the rest of Kiribati's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Kiribati's own course, in its own order.
