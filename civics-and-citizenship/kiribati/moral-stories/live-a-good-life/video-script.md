# Video script — Live a Good Life

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Living a good life is a practice, not a wish — it is what a person does with their days, their family and their neighbours.

A good life, as this text teaches it, is built from how a person treats family, neighbours and work each day — the small faithful acts, repeated, are the good life itself.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
