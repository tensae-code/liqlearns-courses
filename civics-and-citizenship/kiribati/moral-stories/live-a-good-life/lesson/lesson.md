# Live a Good Life

## Do it first

Write down what a good life looks like in your village or town — not riches, but the life people point to and say: that one lived well. List three things that person does. This lesson teaches what living a good life means.

A good life, as this text teaches it, is built from how a person treats family, neighbours and work each day — the small faithful acts, repeated, are the good life itself.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: living a good life is a practice, not a wish — it is what a person does with their days, their family and their neighbours. Give one real example from your own life or from the Kiribati you live in, and name the lesson's key words: good life, character. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: living a good life is a practice, not a wish — it is what a person does with their days, their family and their neighbours.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
