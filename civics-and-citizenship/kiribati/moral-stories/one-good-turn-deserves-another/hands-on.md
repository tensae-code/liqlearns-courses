# Hands-on — One Good Turn Deserves Another

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Remember a time someone did you a good turn when they did not have to. Did you pass it on, or pay it back? Write down what happened next. This lesson's story teaches what grows from a good deed.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: kindness is returned: a good deed done to another comes back — one good turn deserves another. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
