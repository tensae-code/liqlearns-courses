# One Good Turn Deserves Another

## Do it first

Remember a time someone did you a good turn when they did not have to. Did you pass it on, or pay it back? Write down what happened next. This lesson's story teaches what grows from a good deed.

The story by Kuongalelei Edwin teaches that kindness is returned: a good deed done to another comes back, so doing good is never wasted — it builds the circle of people who do good to one another.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: kindness is returned: a good deed done to another comes back — one good turn deserves another. Give one real example from your own life or from the Kiribati you live in, and name the lesson's key words: kindness, good deed, Kuongalelei Edwin. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: kindness is returned: a good deed done to another comes back — one good turn deserves another.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
