# One Good Turn Deserves Another — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "Kindness returned" teach?
**Answer:** The story by Kuongalelei Edwin teaches that kindness is returned: a good deed done to another comes back, so doing good is never wasted — it builds the circle of people who do good to one another.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** Kindness is returned: a good deed done to another comes back — one good turn deserves another.
