# One Good Turn Deserves Another — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The story by Kuongalelei Edwin teaches that kindness is returned: a good deed done to another comes back, so doing good is never wasted — it builds the circle of people who do good to one another.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that kindness is returned: a good deed done to another comes back — one good turn deserves another. — and that it has no connection to the rest of Kiribati's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Kiribati's own course, in its own order.
