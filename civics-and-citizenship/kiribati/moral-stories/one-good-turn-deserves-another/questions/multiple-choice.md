# One Good Turn Deserves Another — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's first teaching — Kindness returned?
a) The story by Kuongalelei Edwin teaches that kindness is returned: a good deed done to another comes back, so doing good is never wasted — it builds the circle of people who do good to one another.  b) it has nothing to do with how Kiribati governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The story by Kuongalelei Edwin teaches that kindness is returned: a good deed done to another comes back, so doing good is never wasted — it builds the circle of people who do good to one another.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) kindness is returned: a good deed done to another comes back — one good turn deserves another.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Kiribati
**Answer: a) kindness is returned: a good deed done to another comes back — one good turn deserves another.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
