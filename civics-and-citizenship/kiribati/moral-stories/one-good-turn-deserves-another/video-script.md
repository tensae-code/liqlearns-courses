# Video script — One Good Turn Deserves Another

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Kindness is returned: a good deed done to another comes back — one good turn deserves another.

The story by Kuongalelei Edwin teaches that kindness is returned: a good deed done to another comes back, so doing good is never wasted — it builds the circle of people who do good to one another.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
