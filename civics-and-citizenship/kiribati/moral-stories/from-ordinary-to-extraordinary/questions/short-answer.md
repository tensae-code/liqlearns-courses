# From Ordinary to Extraordinary — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "The story's teaching" teach?
**Answer:** An ordinary person becomes extraordinary through character: what a person does faithfully, honestly and for others is what lifts an ordinary life into an extraordinary one.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** An ordinary person can become extraordinary through character — this is the opening text of the Year 7 course, and it sets the course's way: character is something a person builds.
