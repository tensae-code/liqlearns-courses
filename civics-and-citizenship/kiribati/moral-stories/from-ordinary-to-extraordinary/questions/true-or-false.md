# From Ordinary to Extraordinary — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** An ordinary person becomes extraordinary through character: what a person does faithfully, honestly and for others is what lifts an ordinary life into an extraordinary one.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that an ordinary person can become extraordinary through character — this is the opening text of the Year 7 course, and it sets the course's way: character is something a person builds. — and that it has no connection to the rest of Kiribati's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Kiribati's own course, in its own order.
