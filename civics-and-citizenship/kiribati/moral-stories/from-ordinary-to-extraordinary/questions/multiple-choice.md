# From Ordinary to Extraordinary — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's first teaching — The story's teaching?
a) An ordinary person becomes extraordinary through character: what a person does faithfully, honestly and for others is what lifts an ordinary life into an extraordinary one.  b) it has nothing to do with how Kiribati governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) An ordinary person becomes extraordinary through character: what a person does faithfully, honestly and for others is what lifts an ordinary life into an extraordinary one.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

a) an ordinary person can become extraordinary through character — this is the opening text of the Year 7 course, and it sets the course's way: character is something a person builds.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Kiribati
**Answer: a) an ordinary person can become extraordinary through character — this is the opening text of the Year 7 course, and it sets the course's way: character is something a person builds.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
