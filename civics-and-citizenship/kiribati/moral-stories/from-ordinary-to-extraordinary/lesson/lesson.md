# From Ordinary to Extraordinary

## Do it first

Think of someone you know who is ordinary in every outward way — no fame, no riches — yet people trust and respect them. Write down what they do, day by day, that earns that trust. This lesson's story is about exactly that road.

An ordinary person becomes extraordinary through character: what a person does faithfully, honestly and for others is what lifts an ordinary life into an extraordinary one.

The reader opens the whole Year 7 course with this text (Weeks 1–2 of Term One), so the course's first word to a young citizen is about character, not rules.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: an ordinary person can become extraordinary through character — this is the opening text of the Year 7 course, and it sets the course's way: character is something a person builds. Give one real example from your own life or from the Kiribati you live in, and name the lesson's key words: character, ordinary, extraordinary. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: an ordinary person can become extraordinary through character — this is the opening text of the Year 7 course, and it sets the course's way: character is something a person builds.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
