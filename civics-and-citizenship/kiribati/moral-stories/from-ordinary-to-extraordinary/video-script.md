# Video script — From Ordinary to Extraordinary

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
An ordinary person can become extraordinary through character — this is the opening text of the Year 7 course, and it sets the course's way: character is something a person builds.

An ordinary person becomes extraordinary through character: what a person does faithfully, honestly and for others is what lifts an ordinary life into an extraordinary one.

The reader opens the whole Year 7 course with this text (Weeks 1–2 of Term One), so the course's first word to a young citizen is about character, not rules.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
