# Hands-on — From Ordinary to Extraordinary

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Think of someone you know who is ordinary in every outward way — no fame, no riches — yet people trust and respect them. Write down what they do, day by day, that earns that trust. This lesson's story is about exactly that road.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: an ordinary person can become extraordinary through character — this is the opening text of the Year 7 course, and it sets the course's way: character is something a person builds. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
