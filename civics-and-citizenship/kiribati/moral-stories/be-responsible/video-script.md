# Video script — Be Responsible

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Responsibility is a habit: doing what is yours to do, and answering for what you have done.

Being responsible means taking your duties as your own: you do them without being chased, and when something goes wrong you answer for your part instead of hiding it. Practised daily, responsibility becomes who you are.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
