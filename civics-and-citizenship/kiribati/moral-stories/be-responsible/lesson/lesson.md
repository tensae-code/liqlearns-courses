# Be Responsible

## Do it first

List the things that are yours to do today — at home, at school, for others. Which of them would still be done if nobody checked? This lesson teaches responsibility as that habit.

Being responsible means taking your duties as your own: you do them without being chased, and when something goes wrong you answer for your part instead of hiding it. Practised daily, responsibility becomes who you are.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: responsibility is a habit: doing what is yours to do, and answering for what you have done. Give one real example from your own life or from the Kiribati you live in, and name the lesson's key words: responsibility, duty, habit. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: responsibility is a habit: doing what is yours to do, and answering for what you have done.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
