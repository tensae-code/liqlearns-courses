# Be Responsible — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "Responsibility as a habit" teach?
**Answer:** Being responsible means taking your duties as your own: you do them without being chased, and when something goes wrong you answer for your part instead of hiding it. Practised daily, responsibility becomes who you are.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** Responsibility is a habit: doing what is yours to do, and answering for what you have done.
