# Be Responsible — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Being responsible means taking your duties as your own: you do them without being chased, and when something goes wrong you answer for your part instead of hiding it. Practised daily, responsibility becomes who you are.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that responsibility is a habit: doing what is yours to do, and answering for what you have done. — and that it has no connection to the rest of Kiribati's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Kiribati's own course, in its own order.
