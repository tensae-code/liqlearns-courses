# Be Responsible — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's first teaching — Responsibility as a habit?
a) Being responsible means taking your duties as your own: you do them without being chased, and when something goes wrong you answer for your part instead of hiding it. Practised daily, responsibility becomes who you are.  b) it has nothing to do with how Kiribati governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Being responsible means taking your duties as your own: you do them without being chased, and when something goes wrong you answer for your part instead of hiding it. Practised daily, responsibility becomes who you are.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) responsibility is a habit: doing what is yours to do, and answering for what you have done.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Kiribati
**Answer: a) responsibility is a habit: doing what is yours to do, and answering for what you have done.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
