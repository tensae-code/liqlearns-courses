# Hands-on — An Alcoholic Father

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Without naming anyone, write down what changes in a home when a parent drinks heavily: money, peace, children's days. This lesson's text returns to the Term One theme and looks at it from the family's side.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: a father's drinking reaches the whole family — what one person drinks, the whole home pays for. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
