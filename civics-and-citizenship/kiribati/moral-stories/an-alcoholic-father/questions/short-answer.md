# An Alcoholic Father — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "What drinking does to a family" teach?
**Answer:** The text teaches what a father's drinking does to his family — the harm is not his alone. It is the Term One theme (the change that brings happiness) returned to, this time seen from inside the home that suffers it.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** A father's drinking reaches the whole family — what one person drinks, the whole home pays for.
