# An Alcoholic Father — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's first teaching — What drinking does to a family?
a) The text teaches what a father's drinking does to his family — the harm is not his alone. It is the Term One theme (the change that brings happiness) returned to, this time seen from inside the home that suffers it.  b) it has nothing to do with how Kiribati governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The text teaches what a father's drinking does to his family — the harm is not his alone. It is the Term One theme (the change that brings happiness) returned to, this time seen from inside the home that suffers it.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) a father's drinking reaches the whole family — what one person drinks, the whole home pays for.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Kiribati
**Answer: a) a father's drinking reaches the whole family — what one person drinks, the whole home pays for.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
