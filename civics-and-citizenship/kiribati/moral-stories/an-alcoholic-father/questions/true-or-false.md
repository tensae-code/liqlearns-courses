# An Alcoholic Father — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The text teaches what a father's drinking does to his family — the harm is not his alone. It is the Term One theme (the change that brings happiness) returned to, this time seen from inside the home that suffers it.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that a father's drinking reaches the whole family — what one person drinks, the whole home pays for. — and that it has no connection to the rest of Kiribati's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Kiribati's own course, in its own order.
