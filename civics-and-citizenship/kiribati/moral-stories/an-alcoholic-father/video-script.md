# Video script — An Alcoholic Father

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A father's drinking reaches the whole family — what one person drinks, the whole home pays for.

The text teaches what a father's drinking does to his family — the harm is not his alone. It is the Term One theme (the change that brings happiness) returned to, this time seen from inside the home that suffers it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
