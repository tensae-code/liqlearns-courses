# An Alcoholic Father

## Do it first

Without naming anyone, write down what changes in a home when a parent drinks heavily: money, peace, children's days. This lesson's text returns to the Term One theme and looks at it from the family's side.

The text teaches what a father's drinking does to his family — the harm is not his alone. It is the Term One theme (the change that brings happiness) returned to, this time seen from inside the home that suffers it.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: a father's drinking reaches the whole family — what one person drinks, the whole home pays for. Give one real example from your own life or from the Kiribati you live in, and name the lesson's key words: alcohol, father, family. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: a father's drinking reaches the whole family — what one person drinks, the whole home pays for.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
