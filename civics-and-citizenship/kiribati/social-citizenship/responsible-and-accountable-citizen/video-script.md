The strand ends at the responsible, accountable citizen: a citizen does their part and answers for it (Weeks 9–12). This lesson stands on the Year 8 teacher guide alone — evidence only, labelled as such.

The closing sub-strand (Weeks 9–12) teaches the responsible and accountable citizen as the strand's end: responsibility for one's duties, and accountability — answering openly for what one has done. Placed as filed and marked TEACHER.
