# Responsible and Accountable Citizen

## Do it first

Write what an accountable person does when something in their care goes wrong — and what an unaccountable person does instead. The Year 8 strand's end names the first one as the citizen.

The closing sub-strand (Weeks 9–12) teaches the responsible and accountable citizen as the strand's end: responsibility for one's duties, and accountability — answering openly for what one has done. Placed as filed and marked TEACHER.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the strand ends at the responsible, accountable citizen: a citizen does their part and answers for it (Weeks 9–12). This lesson stands on the Year 8 teacher guide alone — evidence only, labelled as such. Give one real example from your own life or from the Kiribati you live in, and name the lesson's key words: responsible citizen, accountable citizen, Strand 4. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
