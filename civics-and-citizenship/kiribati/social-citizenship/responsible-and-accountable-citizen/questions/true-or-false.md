# Responsible and Accountable Citizen — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The closing sub-strand (Weeks 9–12) teaches the responsible and accountable citizen as the strand's end: responsibility for one's duties, and accountability — answering openly for what one has done. Placed as filed and marked TEACHER.

**2.** This lesson teaches that the strand ends at the responsible, accountable citizen: a citizen does their part and answers for it (Weeks 9–12).
