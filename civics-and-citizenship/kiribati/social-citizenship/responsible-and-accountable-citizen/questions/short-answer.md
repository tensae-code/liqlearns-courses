# Responsible and Accountable Citizen — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "The accountable citizen" teach?
**Answer:** The closing sub-strand (Weeks 9–12) teaches the responsible and accountable citizen as the strand's end: responsibility for one's duties, and accountability — answering openly for what one has done. Placed as filed and marked TEACHER.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** The strand ends at the responsible, accountable citizen: a citizen does their part and answers for it (Weeks 9–12). This lesson stands on the Year 8 teacher guide alone — evidence only, labelled as such.
