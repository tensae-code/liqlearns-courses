# Video script — Ownership and Collaboration in the Community

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The community is partly yours: owning its problems and working together on them is the second circle of citizenship (Weeks 5–8). This lesson stands on the Year 8 teacher guide alone — evidence only, labelled as such.

Sub-Strand 4.2 (Weeks 5–8) teaches a sense of ownership and collaboration in the community: the community's goods and problems belong to its members, and they are carried by working together. Placed as filed and marked TEACHER.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
