# Hands-on — Ownership and Collaboration in the Community

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name one thing in your community that everyone uses and nobody owns — a path, a well, a meeting place. Who looks after it? This lesson teaches the citizen's answer: we do, together.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the community is partly yours: owning its problems and working together on them is the second circle of citizenship (Weeks 5–8). This lesson stands on the Year 8 teacher guide alone — evidence only, labelled as such. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
