# Ownership and Collaboration in the Community — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Sub-Strand 4.2 (Weeks 5–8) teaches a sense of ownership and collaboration in the community: the community's goods and problems belong to its members, and they are carried by working together. Placed as filed and marked TEACHER.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that the community is partly yours: owning its problems and working together on them is the second circle of citizenship (Weeks 5–8). This lesson stands on the Year 8 teacher guide alone — evidence only, labelled as such. — and that it has no connection to the rest of Kiribati's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Kiribati's own course, in its own order.
