# Ownership and Collaboration in the Community — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "Ownership and collaboration" teach?
**Answer:** Sub-Strand 4.2 (Weeks 5–8) teaches a sense of ownership and collaboration in the community: the community's goods and problems belong to its members, and they are carried by working together. Placed as filed and marked TEACHER.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** The community is partly yours: owning its problems and working together on them is the second circle of citizenship (Weeks 5–8). This lesson stands on the Year 8 teacher guide alone — evidence only, labelled as such.
