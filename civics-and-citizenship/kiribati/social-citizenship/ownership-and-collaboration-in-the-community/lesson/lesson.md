# Ownership and Collaboration in the Community

## Do it first

Name one thing in your community that everyone uses and nobody owns — a path, a well, a meeting place. Who looks after it? This lesson teaches the citizen's answer: we do, together.

Sub-Strand 4.2 (Weeks 5–8) teaches a sense of ownership and collaboration in the community: the community's goods and problems belong to its members, and they are carried by working together. Placed as filed and marked TEACHER.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the community is partly yours: owning its problems and working together on them is the second circle of citizenship (Weeks 5–8). This lesson stands on the Year 8 teacher guide alone — evidence only, labelled as such. Give one real example from your own life or from the Kiribati you live in, and name the lesson's key words: ownership, collaboration, community, Sub-Strand 4.2. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
