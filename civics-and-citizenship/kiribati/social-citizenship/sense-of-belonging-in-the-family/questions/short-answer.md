# Sense of Belonging in the Family — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "Belonging in the family" teach?
**Answer:** Sub-Strand 4.1 (Weeks 1–4) teaches the sense of belonging in the family as the first circle of citizenship — the family is where a person first belongs, is known, and owes something back. Placed as filed and marked TEACHER.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** Belonging in the family is the first circle of citizenship: before the village and the nation, a citizen first belongs at home (Weeks 1–4). This lesson stands on the Year 8 teacher guide alone — evidence only, labelled as such.
