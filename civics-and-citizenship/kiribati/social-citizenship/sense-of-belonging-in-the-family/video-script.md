# Video script — Sense of Belonging in the Family

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Belonging in the family is the first circle of citizenship: before the village and the nation, a citizen first belongs at home (Weeks 1–4). This lesson stands on the Year 8 teacher guide alone — evidence only, labelled as such.

Sub-Strand 4.1 (Weeks 1–4) teaches the sense of belonging in the family as the first circle of citizenship — the family is where a person first belongs, is known, and owes something back. Placed as filed and marked TEACHER.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
