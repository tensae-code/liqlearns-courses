# Sense of Belonging in the Family

## Do it first

Write what 'belonging' feels like in your family: who claims you, who you can call on, what you owe back. The Year 8 guide's Social Citizenship strand starts citizenship right there.

Sub-Strand 4.1 (Weeks 1–4) teaches the sense of belonging in the family as the first circle of citizenship — the family is where a person first belongs, is known, and owes something back. Placed as filed and marked TEACHER.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: belonging in the family is the first circle of citizenship: before the village and the nation, a citizen first belongs at home (Weeks 1–4). This lesson stands on the Year 8 teacher guide alone — evidence only, labelled as such. Give one real example from your own life or from the Kiribati you live in, and name the lesson's key words: belonging, family, social citizenship, Sub-Strand 4.1. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
