# Court Structure and Hierarchy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's first teaching — The court's officers named?
a) The syllabus first names the court and its heads: the Chief Justice, the Chief Registrar and the Chief Magistrate, and what each title means.  b) it has nothing to do with how Kiribati governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The syllabus first names the court and its heads: the Chief Justice, the Chief Registrar and the Chief Magistrate, and what each title means.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Kiribati's courts stand in a hierarchy, each with its function, headed in their own spheres by the Chief Justice, the Chief Registrar and the Chief Magistrate. This lesson stands on the KCSE Civic syllabus alone — evidence only, labelled as such.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Kiribati
**Answer: a) Kiribati's courts stand in a hierarchy, each with its function, headed in their own spheres by the Chief Justice, the Chief Registrar and the Chief Magistrate. This lesson stands on the KCSE Civic syllabus alone — evidence only, labelled as such.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
