# Court Structure and Hierarchy — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Kiribati's courts stand in a hierarchy; the syllabus teaches the order of the courts and the function of each court within it.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that Kiribati's courts stand in a hierarchy, each with its function, headed in their own spheres by the Chief Justice, the Chief Registrar and the Chief Magistrate. This lesson stands on the KCSE Civic syllabus alone — evidence only, labelled as such. — and that it has no connection to the rest of Kiribati's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Kiribati's own course, in its own order.
