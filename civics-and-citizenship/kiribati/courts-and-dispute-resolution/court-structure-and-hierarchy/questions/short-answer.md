# Court Structure and Hierarchy — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "The court's officers named" teach?
**Answer:** The syllabus first names the court and its heads: the Chief Justice, the Chief Registrar and the Chief Magistrate, and what each title means.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** Kiribati's courts stand in a hierarchy, each with its function, headed in their own spheres by the Chief Justice, the Chief Registrar and the Chief Magistrate. This lesson stands on the KCSE Civic syllabus alone — evidence only, labelled as such.
