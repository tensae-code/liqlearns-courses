This lesson is about one thing.
Kiribati's courts stand in a hierarchy, each with its function, headed in their own spheres by the Chief Justice, the Chief Registrar and the Chief Magistrate. This lesson stands on the KCSE Civic syllabus alone — evidence only, labelled as such.

The syllabus first names the court and its heads: the Chief Justice, the Chief Registrar and the Chief Magistrate, and what each title means.

Kiribati's courts stand in a hierarchy; the syllabus teaches the order of the courts and the function of each court within it.
