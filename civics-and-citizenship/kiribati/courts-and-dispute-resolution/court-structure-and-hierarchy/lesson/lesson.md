# Court Structure and Hierarchy

## Do it first

A dispute is too big for the island court. Where does it go next — and who stands at the top of the court system? Draw your guess as a ladder. This lesson teaches the real ladder.

## Sub lesson 1 — The court's officers named

The syllabus first names the court and its heads: the Chief Justice, the Chief Registrar and the Chief Magistrate, and what each title means.

## Sub lesson 2 — Hierarchy and function

Kiribati's courts stand in a hierarchy; the syllabus teaches the order of the courts and the function of each court within it.
