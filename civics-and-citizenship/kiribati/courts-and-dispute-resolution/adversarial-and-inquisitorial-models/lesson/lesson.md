# Adversarial and Inquisitorial Models

## Do it first

Two ways to find the truth in a dispute: let each side argue its case, or let the judge dig for the facts. Which do you trust more, and why? This lesson teaches both models — and the one Kiribati works by.

## Sub lesson 1 — The two models and their features

The syllabus teaches the two models of justice — adversarial and inquisitorial — and the features of each.

## Sub lesson 2 — How the adversarial system operates

The adversarial system operates as a contest between two sides before an impartial decision-maker; the syllabus teaches how it runs, since it is the model behind the proceedings taught in the rest of this course.
