This lesson is about one thing.
Justice has two great models: the adversarial, where two sides contest before a neutral judge, and the inquisitorial, where the court itself investigates — Kiribati's system is taught through the adversarial model. This lesson stands on the KCSE Civic syllabus alone — evidence only, labelled as such.

The syllabus teaches the two models of justice — adversarial and inquisitorial — and the features of each.

The adversarial system operates as a contest between two sides before an impartial decision-maker; the syllabus teaches how it runs, since it is the model behind the proceedings taught in the rest of this course.
