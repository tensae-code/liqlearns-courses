# Adversarial and Inquisitorial Models — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "The two models and their features" teach?
**Answer:** The syllabus teaches the two models of justice — adversarial and inquisitorial — and the features of each.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** Justice has two great models: the adversarial, where two sides contest before a neutral judge, and the inquisitorial, where the court itself investigates — Kiribati's system is taught through the adversarial model. This lesson stands on the KCSE Civic syllabus alone — evidence only, labelled as such.
