# Hands-on — Types of Courts

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Three disputes: a theft, an unpaid debt, a quarrel over land. Which court hears each? Write your answers; this lesson teaches how Kiribati sorts them.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: different disputes go to different courts: criminal, civil and lands courts each hold their own jurisdiction — the set of cases they may hear. This lesson stands on the KCSE Civic syllabus alone — evidence only, labelled as such. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
