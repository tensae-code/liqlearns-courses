# Types of Courts — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Kiribati has criminal, civil and lands courts among its types of courts; the syllabus teaches each court's jurisdiction and functions.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that different disputes go to different courts: criminal, civil and lands courts each hold their own jurisdiction — the set of cases they may hear. This lesson stands on the KCSE Civic syllabus alone — evidence only, labelled as such. — and that it has no connection to the rest of Kiribati's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Kiribati's own course, in its own order.
