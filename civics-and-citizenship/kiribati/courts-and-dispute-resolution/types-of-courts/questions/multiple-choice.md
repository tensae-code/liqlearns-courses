# Types of Courts — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's first teaching — Jurisdiction?
a) Jurisdiction is a court's authority to hear a kind of case. The syllabus teaches the word first, because the types of courts are sorted by it.  b) it has nothing to do with how Kiribati governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Jurisdiction is a court's authority to hear a kind of case. The syllabus teaches the word first, because the types of courts are sorted by it.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) different disputes go to different courts: criminal, civil and lands courts each hold their own jurisdiction — the set of cases they may hear. This lesson stands on the KCSE Civic syllabus alone — evidence only, labelled as such.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Kiribati
**Answer: a) different disputes go to different courts: criminal, civil and lands courts each hold their own jurisdiction — the set of cases they may hear. This lesson stands on the KCSE Civic syllabus alone — evidence only, labelled as such.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
