# Types of Courts — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does the part "Jurisdiction" teach?
**Answer:** Jurisdiction is a court's authority to hear a kind of case. The syllabus teaches the word first, because the types of courts are sorted by it.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** Different disputes go to different courts: criminal, civil and lands courts each hold their own jurisdiction — the set of cases they may hear. This lesson stands on the KCSE Civic syllabus alone — evidence only, labelled as such.
