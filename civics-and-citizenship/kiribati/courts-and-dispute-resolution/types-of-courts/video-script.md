This lesson is about one thing.
Different disputes go to different courts: criminal, civil and lands courts each hold their own jurisdiction — the set of cases they may hear. This lesson stands on the KCSE Civic syllabus alone — evidence only, labelled as such.

Jurisdiction is a court's authority to hear a kind of case. The syllabus teaches the word first, because the types of courts are sorted by it.

Kiribati has criminal, civil and lands courts among its types of courts; the syllabus teaches each court's jurisdiction and functions.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
