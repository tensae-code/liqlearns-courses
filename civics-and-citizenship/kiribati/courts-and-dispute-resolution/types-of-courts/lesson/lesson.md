# Types of Courts

## Do it first

Three disputes: a theft, an unpaid debt, a quarrel over land. Which court hears each? Write your answers; this lesson teaches how Kiribati sorts them.

## Sub lesson 1 — Jurisdiction

Jurisdiction is a court's authority to hear a kind of case. The syllabus teaches the word first, because the types of courts are sorted by it.

## Sub lesson 2 — Criminal, civil and lands courts

Kiribati has criminal, civil and lands courts among its types of courts; the syllabus teaches each court's jurisdiction and functions.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: different disputes go to different courts: criminal, civil and lands courts each hold their own jurisdiction — the set of cases they may hear. This lesson stands on the KCSE Civic syllabus alone — evidence only, labelled as such. Give one real example from your own life or from the Kiribati you live in, and name the lesson's key words: jurisdiction, criminal court, civil court, lands court. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
