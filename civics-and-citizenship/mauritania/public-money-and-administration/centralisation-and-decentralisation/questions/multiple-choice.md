# Centralisation and Decentralisation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Power held at the centre against power shared with the regions.  b) it has nothing to do with how Mauritania governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Power held at the centre against power shared with the regions.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Power held at the centre against power shared with the regions.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Mauritania
**Answer: a) Power held at the centre against power shared with the regions.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
