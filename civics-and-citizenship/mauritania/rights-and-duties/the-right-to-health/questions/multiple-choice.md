# The Right to Health — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The right to health, with prevention as part of it.  b) it has nothing to do with how Mauritania governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The right to health, with prevention as part of it.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) The right to health, with prevention as part of it.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Mauritania
**Answer: a) The right to health, with prevention as part of it.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
