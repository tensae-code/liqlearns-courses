# Corruption and Fighting It — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Corruption and why it must be fought.  b) it has nothing to do with how Mauritania governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Corruption and why it must be fought.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Corruption and why it must be fought.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Mauritania
**Answer: a) Corruption and why it must be fought.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
