# Public and Private Property — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Public property against private property, through the school's own things.  b) it has nothing to do with how Mauritania governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Public property against private property, through the school's own things.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Public property against private property, through the school's own things.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Mauritania
**Answer: a) Public property against private property, through the school's own things.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
