# The Municipal Councillor — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The councillor as the people's local representative.  b) it has nothing to do with how Mauritania governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The councillor as the people's local representative.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) The councillor as the people's local representative.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Mauritania
**Answer: a) The councillor as the people's local representative.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
