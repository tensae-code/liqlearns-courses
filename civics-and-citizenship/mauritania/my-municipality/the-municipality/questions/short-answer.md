# The Municipality — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** The prepared lesson introducing the municipality; The first naming of the municipality; What a municipality is and does — roads, primary schools, health centres, water, markets and cultural facilities, under the municipalities law the book cites — run by an elected council headed by the mayor; The municipal council elected for five years, the mayor and deputies, under the Interior Ministry's tutelage.
