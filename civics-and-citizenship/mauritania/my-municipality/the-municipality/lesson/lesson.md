# The Municipality

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: The Municipality. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Mauritania's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

The prepared lesson introducing the municipality; The first naming of the municipality; What a municipality is and does — roads, primary schools, health centres, water, markets and cultural facilities, under the municipalities law the book cites — run by an elected council headed by the mayor; The municipal council elected for five years, the mayor and deputies, under the Interior Ministry's tutelage. Do it now: name one place in your own home, school or town where you can see this, and say what happens there.
