# Video script — The Municipality

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The prepared lesson introducing the municipality; The first naming of the municipality; What a municipality is and does — roads, primary schools, health centres, water, markets and cultural facilities, under the municipalities law the book cites — run by an elected council headed by the mayor; The municipal council elected for five years, the mayor and deputies, under the Interior Ministry's tutelage.
