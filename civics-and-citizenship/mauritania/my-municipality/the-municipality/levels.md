# The Municipality — depth

This lesson is ONE lesson with no sub lessons: it teaches a single thing, whole. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files.

Pass mark: The prepared lesson introducing the municipality; The first naming of the municipality; What a municipality is and does — roads, primary schools, health centres, water, markets and cultural facilities, under the municipalities law the book cites — run by an elected council headed by the mayor; The municipal council elected for five years, the mayor and deputies, under the Interior Ministry's tutelage — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
