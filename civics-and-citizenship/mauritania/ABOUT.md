# About the Civics and Citizenship — Mauritania course

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in
this course comes from government textbooks — this country's
own التربية المدنية (Civic Education — "Awakening and Civic
Behaviour" in the first primary years) books. The one
exception is the **Tip boxes**: a tip in a box is from us, the
people making LiqLearns. Tips are ours; the lessons are the
books'.

## How this course is organised

This is ONE course for Mauritania inside the Civics and
Citizenship folder (one course per country). Inside it, the
organisation is by **levels, not grades**: a learner's place
is found by what they prove, and each level stands on the
ones before it as a recommended-first path — never a forced
gate. The school grades the books come from appear only in
each lesson's `lesson/sources.md`, as provenance.

The course as built has **ten stocked levels** (Levels
1–10), from the filing for this build
(`mauritania-placements.md` — 15 records / 15 distinct books,
all read in full). 332 placements in all, stocking
221 lessons — the coined lesson names were deduped
before building (see CURRICULUM.md, "Where the build
stands"): every placement stocks into exactly one lesson,
and every placement's provenance row is in that lesson's
`lesson/sources.md`.

**How the books were read, exactly as filed:** eight books
were grabbed from the GitHub tree or read in place from
phase3-staging; seven records were marked pushed but their
files were absent from the GitHub tree (phantom pushes —
library debts, named in the group log), and those seven were
fetched from the records' own source URLs (docs.bsimr.com,
the IPN digital library) and staged under the push-don't-delete
rule. The seven phantom-push books are Books 2, 6, 7, 12, 13,
14 and 15, each labelled in its header below exactly as
filed. All books are the Arabic editions of التربية المدنية
(Civic Education), Institut Pédagogique National (IPN),
Ministry of Education and Reform of the Education System,
Islamic Republic of Mauritania. The census titles describe
the series in French; the books themselves are Arabic.

**What the filing does and does not contain, stated
plainly:** the filing contains 15 book headers and 332
placement lines, plus a closing ladder sketch. It names no
UNREAD book — all 15 books are recorded read in full. It
contains one PARTIAL verdict (Book 9). It names assessment
and integration situations at the ends of units in Books 2,
5 and 8 (and the two integration situations in Book 2 at
pp. 61–62, the two assessment situations in Book 5 at
pp. 47–48, and the integration situations in Book 8 after
lessons 3, 5 and 15 and at pp. 77–78) as excluded by name,
and states that no glossary is placed. Anything not stated
in the filing is not claimed here.
