# Problems of Big Cities — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) What goes wrong as cities grow.  b) it has nothing to do with how Mauritania governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) What goes wrong as cities grow.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) What goes wrong as cities grow.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Mauritania
**Answer: a) What goes wrong as cities grow.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
