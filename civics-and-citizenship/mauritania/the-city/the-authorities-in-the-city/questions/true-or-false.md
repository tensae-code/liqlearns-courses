# The Authorities in the City — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The wali, the hakem and the chef de centre in the city.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that The Authorities in the City — and that it has no connection to the rest of Mauritania's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Mauritania's own course, in its own order.
