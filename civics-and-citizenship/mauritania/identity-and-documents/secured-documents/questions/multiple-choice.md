Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The National Agency for the Population Register and Secured Documents — the biometric identity card, passport, birth certificate and national number.  b) it has nothing to do with how Mauritania governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
