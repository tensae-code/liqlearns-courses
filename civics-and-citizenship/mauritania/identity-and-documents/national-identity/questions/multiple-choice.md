# National Identity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Identity resting on Islam and shared social and cultural bonds, carried in documents such as the identity card and passport.  b) it has nothing to do with how Mauritania governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Identity resting on Islam and shared social and cultural bonds, carried in documents such as the identity card and passport.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

a) Identity resting on Islam and shared social and cultural bonds, carried in documents such as the identity card and passport.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Mauritania
**Answer: a) Identity resting on Islam and shared social and cultural bonds, carried in documents such as the identity card and passport.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
