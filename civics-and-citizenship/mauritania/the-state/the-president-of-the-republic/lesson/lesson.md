# The President of the Republic

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: The President of the Republic. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Mauritania's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

The president's roles as the book lists them, in summary. Do it now: name one place in your own home, school or town where you can see this, and say what happens there.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The president's roles as the book lists them, in summary. Give one real example from your own life or from the Mauritania you live in, and name the lesson's key words: the president of the republic. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
