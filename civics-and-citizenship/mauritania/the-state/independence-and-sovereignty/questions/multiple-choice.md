# Independence and Sovereignty — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Independence of the person and of the state — sovereignty and a political system of one's own.  b) it has nothing to do with how Mauritania governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
