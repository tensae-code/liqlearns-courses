# The Mosque — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The mosque as a landmark of the neighbourhood and how to behave in it; The mosque in the neighbourhood; The prepared lesson on the mosque; The mosque.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that The Mosque — and that it has no connection to the rest of Mauritania's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Mauritania's own course, in its own order.
