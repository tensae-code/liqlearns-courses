# Public Opinion and Means of Expression — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) How public opinion forms and speaks.  b) it has nothing to do with how Mauritania governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) How public opinion forms and speaks.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) How public opinion forms and speaks.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Mauritania
**Answer: a) How public opinion forms and speaks.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
