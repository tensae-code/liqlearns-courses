# Rest and Sleep — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Sleep and rest as part of staying healthy; Rest and sleep keep the body healthy.  b) it has nothing to do with how Mauritania governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Sleep and rest as part of staying healthy; Rest and sleep keep the body healthy.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Sleep and rest as part of staying healthy; Rest and sleep keep the body healthy.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Mauritania
**Answer: a) Sleep and rest as part of staying healthy; Rest and sleep keep the body healthy.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
