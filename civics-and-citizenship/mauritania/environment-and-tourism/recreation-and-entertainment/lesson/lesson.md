# Recreation and Entertainment

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Recreation and Entertainment. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Mauritania's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Rest and amusement as needs, and using free time well. Do it now: name one place in your own home, school or town where you can see this, and say what happens there.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Rest and amusement as needs, and using free time well. Give one real example from your own life or from the Mauritania you live in, and name the lesson's key words: recreation and entertainment. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: Rest and amusement as needs, and using free time well.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
