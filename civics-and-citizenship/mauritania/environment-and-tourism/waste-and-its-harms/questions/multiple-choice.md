# Waste and its Harms — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Waste and the harm it does.  b) it has nothing to do with how Mauritania governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Waste and the harm it does.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Waste and the harm it does.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Mauritania
**Answer: a) Waste and the harm it does.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
