# The National Pedagogical Institute — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The institute that makes the school books.  b) it has nothing to do with how Mauritania governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The institute that makes the school books.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) The institute that makes the school books.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Mauritania
**Answer: a) The institute that makes the school books.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
