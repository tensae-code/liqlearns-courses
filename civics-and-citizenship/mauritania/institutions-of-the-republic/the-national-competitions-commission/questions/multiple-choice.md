# The National Competitions Commission — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The body that runs national examinations and competitions.  b) it has nothing to do with how Mauritania governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The body that runs national examinations and competitions.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) The body that runs national examinations and competitions.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Mauritania
**Answer: a) The body that runs national examinations and competitions.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
