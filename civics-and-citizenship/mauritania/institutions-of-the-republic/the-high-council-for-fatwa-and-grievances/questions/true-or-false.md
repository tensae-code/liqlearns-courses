# The High Council For Fatwa and Grievances — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Mauritania's own council for religious rulings and citizens' grievances.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that The High Council For Fatwa and Grievances — and that it has no connection to the rest of Mauritania's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Mauritania's own course, in its own order.
