# Litter and Litter Bins — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Litter as dirt that harms everyone; Putting litter in the bin; Not throwing litter.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that Litter and Litter Bins — and that it has no connection to the rest of Mauritania's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Mauritania's own course, in its own order.
