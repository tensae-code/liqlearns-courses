# Trees and Plants

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Trees and Plants. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Mauritania's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## Trees

What trees give and why they are cared for. Do it now: name one place in your own home, school or town where you can see this, and say what happens there.

## Plants

Plants around the child and their uses. Do it now: name one place in your own home, school or town where you can see this, and say what happens there.

## Trees and Plants

The place of trees and plants in the child's surroundings. Do it now: name one place in your own home, school or town where you can see this, and say what happens there.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: What trees give and why they are cared for; Plants around the child and their uses; The place of trees and plants in the child's surroundings; The prepared lesson on plants; Plants and their place around the home. Give one real example from your own life or from the Mauritania you live in, and name the lesson's key words: trees and plants. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: What trees give and why they are cared for; Plants around the child and their uses; The place of trees and plants in the child's surroundings; The prepared lesson on plants; Plants and their place around the home.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
