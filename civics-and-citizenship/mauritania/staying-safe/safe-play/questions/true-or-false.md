# Safe Play — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Playing without danger; Play as a child's right, only in the places made for it, never with fire, electricity or sharp tools.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that Safe Play — and that it has no connection to the rest of Mauritania's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Mauritania's own course, in its own order.
