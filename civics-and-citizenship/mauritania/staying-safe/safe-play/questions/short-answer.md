# Safe Play — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** Playing without danger; Play as a child's right, only in the places made for it, never with fire, electricity or sharp tools.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** Playing without danger; Play as a child's right, only in the places made for it, never with fire, electricity or sharp tools.
