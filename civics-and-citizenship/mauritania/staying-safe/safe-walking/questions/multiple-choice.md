# Safe Walking — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Walking safely along the road.  b) it has nothing to do with how Mauritania governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Walking safely along the road.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Walking safely along the road.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Mauritania
**Answer: a) Walking safely along the road.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
