# First Aid — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) First aid for the injured, in the book's own wording.  b) it has nothing to do with how Mauritania governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) First aid for the injured, in the book's own wording.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) First aid for the injured, in the book's own wording.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Mauritania
**Answer: a) First aid for the injured, in the book's own wording.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
