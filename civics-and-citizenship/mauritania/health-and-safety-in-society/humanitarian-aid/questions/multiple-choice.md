# Humanitarian Aid — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Aid given across borders in emergencies.  b) it has nothing to do with how Mauritania governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Aid given across borders in emergencies.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Aid given across borders in emergencies.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Mauritania
**Answer: a) Aid given across borders in emergencies.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
