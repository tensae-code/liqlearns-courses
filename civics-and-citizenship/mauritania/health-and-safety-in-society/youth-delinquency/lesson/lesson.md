# Youth Delinquency

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Youth Delinquency. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Mauritania's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Young people and the law. Do it now: name one place in your own home, school or town where you can see this, and say what happens there.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Young people and the law. Give one real example from your own life or from the Mauritania you live in, and name the lesson's key words: youth delinquency. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: Young people and the law.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
