# Justice and the Courts

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Justice and the Courts. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Mauritania's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Wilaya courts with their chambers and the courts of appeal. Do it now: name one place in your own home, school or town where you can see this, and say what happens there.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Wilaya courts with their chambers and the courts of appeal. Give one real example from your own life or from the Mauritania you live in, and name the lesson's key words: justice and the courts. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: Wilaya courts with their chambers and the courts of appeal.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
