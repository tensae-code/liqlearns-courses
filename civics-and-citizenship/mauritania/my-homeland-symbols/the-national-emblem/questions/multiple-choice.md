# The National Emblem — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Recognising the national emblem.  b) it has nothing to do with how Mauritania governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Recognising the national emblem.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Recognising the national emblem.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Mauritania
**Answer: a) Recognising the national emblem.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
