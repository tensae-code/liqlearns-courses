# My Homeland — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The child's own country named and loved first, before any institution; Naming and loving one's homeland; The prepared lesson on the homeland; The homeland.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that My Homeland — and that it has no connection to the rest of Mauritania's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Mauritania's own course, in its own order.
