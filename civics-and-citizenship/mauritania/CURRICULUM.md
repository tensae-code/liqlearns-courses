# Civics and Citizenship — Mauritania course — CURRICULUM (the path)

One course for Mauritania inside the Civics and Citizenship folder,
organised by **levels, not grades**: a learner climbs when they
have proved the level, not when a school calendar says so. The
school grades the books come from appear only in each lesson's
sources (provenance), never as the course's organisation. The
path follows Mauritania's own books' progression, as sketched
at the end of the filing: the youngest child's civics is taught
as "Awakening and Civic Behaviour" — the body, habits, safety
and surroundings first, then the homeland and its symbols, then
family, school and the places of the quarter, with the values
strand running through every year — then the municipality and
the territorial ladder, rights and identity, the republic's
machinery, elections and civil society, the working world of
money, professions, education and the city, and finally health,
the environment, heritage and Mauritania in the world.

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in this
course comes from government textbooks — this country's own
التربية المدنية (Civic Education) books — except the **Tip
boxes**: a tip in a box is from us, the people making LiqLearns.
Tips are ours; the lessons are the books'. See ABOUT.md for the
full book list, every gap and label carried exactly as filed,
and the acceptance reckoning.

## The levels (the path, in order)

1. **My Body, My Habits, Safety and My Surroundings** — the youngest books (Grades 1–2, reinforced in 3–4) teach the child's own body, habits, safety and nearest surroundings first: washing, the siwak, sleep, ponds, litter, water. 25 lessons (55 placements filed at the level).

2. **My Homeland and Its Symbols, Time and Feasts** — the same first books name the homeland and its symbols (flag, anthem, emblem, currency, motto) and the child's calendar of days, feasts and Independence Day. 10 lessons (27 placements filed at the level).

3. **My Family, My School and the Places Around Me** — the circle widens to family order and duties, the school as first institution (its order, cooperative, garden, parents' association) and the landmarks of the quarter (mosque, mahdhara, market, dispensary). 26 lessons (49 placements filed at the level).

4. **Values and Conduct** — the values strand runs through every year (tolerance, cooperation, solidarity/takaful, volunteering, compassion, discipline, moderation, equality) and is gathered here as the books repeat and deepen it. 22 lessons (43 placements filed at the level).

5. **My Municipality and How My Country Is Run Locally** — Grade 4 gives a whole year to the municipality and Grade 5 climbs the territorial ladder (centre, moughataa, wilaya, region): the first machinery of the state, met locally. 13 lessons (16 placements filed at the level).

6. **Rights, Identity and Protecting People and Public Money** — rights arrive in late primary (child-rights convention, freedoms) and are systematised in preparatory school: citizen's rights and duties, human rights, identity documents, and the fights against enslavement and corruption. 17 lessons (19 placements filed at the level).

7. **The State, the Republic's Institutions, Security and Administration** — the republic's machinery: president, parliament, government, courts, the watchdog councils and commissions, the security services, civil service, budget and land. 29 lessons (33 placements filed at the level).

8. **Elections, Civil Society, Media and Systems of Government** — taking part and judging power: elections and the ballot, peaceful alternation, parties, unions, media and propaganda, and the comparative study of states and constitutions that closes the course. 16 lessons (17 placements filed at the level).

9. **Money, Work, Education and the City** — the working world the books build from Grade 5 on: professions, the ouguiya and the banks, taxes and budgets, kinds of education, the internet and social media, and the city. 24 lessons (30 placements filed at the level).

10. **Health, Environment, Heritage and Mauritania in the World** — the widest circle: public health and humanitarian aid, the environment from pollution to the Banc d'Arguin, heritage and identity at full depth, and Mauritania among the world's organisations. 39 lessons (43 placements filed at the level).
