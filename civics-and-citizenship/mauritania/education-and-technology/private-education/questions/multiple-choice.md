# Private Education — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Education outside the state system.  b) it has nothing to do with how Mauritania governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Education outside the state system.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Education outside the state system.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Mauritania
**Answer: a) Education outside the state system.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
