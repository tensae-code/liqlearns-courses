# Social Media — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The social media platforms and their place in education and society.  b) it has nothing to do with how Mauritania governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The social media platforms and their place in education and society.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) The social media platforms and their place in education and society.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Mauritania
**Answer: a) The social media platforms and their place in education and society.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
