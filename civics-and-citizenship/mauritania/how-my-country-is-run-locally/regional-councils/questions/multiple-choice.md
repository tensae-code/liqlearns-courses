Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The region beside the municipality, and decentralisation.  b) it has nothing to do with how Mauritania governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The region beside the municipality, and decentralisation.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?

**Answer: a) The region beside the municipality, and decentralisation.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
