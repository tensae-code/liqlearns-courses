# Moderation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The middle way (الاعتدال) in conduct.  b) it has nothing to do with how Mauritania governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The middle way (الاعتدال) in conduct.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) The middle way (الاعتدال) in conduct.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Mauritania
**Answer: a) The middle way (الاعتدال) in conduct.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
