# Solidarity — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Mutual support inside the family and society; Solidarity practised; The guide's worked lesson on what solidarity means; What solidarity is, through the story of the lame man and the blind man; Mutual support inside the family; Mutual support in the quarter; Solidarity organised by society — zakat, health cover, social-security funds, Taazour and the health-insurance fund as the book names them.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that Solidarity — and that it has no connection to the rest of Mauritania's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Mauritania's own course, in its own order.
