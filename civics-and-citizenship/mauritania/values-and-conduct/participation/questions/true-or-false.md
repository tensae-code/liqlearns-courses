# Participation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Taking part at home, at school, in the neighbourhood and by volunteering.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that Participation — and that it has no connection to the rest of Mauritania's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Mauritania's own course, in its own order.
