# Respecting Others Opinions — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Hearing other views, in the book's spirit that one's own opinion may be right or wrong.  b) it has nothing to do with how Mauritania governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Hearing other views, in the book's spirit that one's own opinion may be right or wrong.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Hearing other views, in the book's spirit that one's own opinion may be right or wrong.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Mauritania
**Answer: a) Hearing other views, in the book's spirit that one's own opinion may be right or wrong.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
