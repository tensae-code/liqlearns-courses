# Organising Elections

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Organising Elections. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Mauritania's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Presidential, parliamentary and municipal elections, absolute majority, proportional representation and the second round, in summary. Do it now: name one place in your own home, school or town where you can see this, and say what happens there.
