# The Arab League — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The league of Arab states.  b) it has nothing to do with how Mauritania governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The league of Arab states.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) The league of Arab states.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Mauritania
**Answer: a) The league of Arab states.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
