# Forms of Government — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Hereditary against democratic rule, opened by comparing Britain and France.  b) it has nothing to do with how Mauritania governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Hereditary against democratic rule, opened by comparing Britain and France.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

a) Hereditary against democratic rule, opened by comparing Britain and France.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Mauritania
**Answer: a) Hereditary against democratic rule, opened by comparing Britain and France.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
