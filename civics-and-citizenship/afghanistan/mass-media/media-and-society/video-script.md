# Video script — Media and Society

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The media act on society — informing it, teaching it, joining it together — and society in turn shapes its media: each forms the other.

The media inform society — carrying news further and faster than any traveller. They teach it, joining distant people into one conversation, one set of shared facts and stories. And they can move it — for good or ill — which is why the relation runs both ways: a society gets, in part, the media it supports, demands and believes. The citizen who understands media and society holds a key to his own time.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
