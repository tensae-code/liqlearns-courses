# Hands-on — Media and Society

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name one thing your whole street or village learned from the media this month, and one way people's behaviour changed because of something the media carried. Keep both; the lesson is about that power and its other side.

## Show it with real things
Take this lesson into one real act this week: do, make, visit, ask or help in a way that puts Media and Society into practice in your own community in Afghanistan — then write what happened and what the lesson would say about it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
