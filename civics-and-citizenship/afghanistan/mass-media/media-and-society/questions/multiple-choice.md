# Media and Society — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) The media act on society — informing it, teaching it, joining it together — and society in turn shapes its media: each forms the other.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) The book closes its second year here: the citizen who knows the services around him now asks what the loudest of them does to the society it speaks to.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
