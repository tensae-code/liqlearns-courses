# Media and Society — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** The media act on society — informing it, teaching it, joining it together — and society in turn shapes its media: each forms the other.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** The book closes its second year here: the citizen who knows the services around him now asks what the loudest of them does to the society it speaks to.
