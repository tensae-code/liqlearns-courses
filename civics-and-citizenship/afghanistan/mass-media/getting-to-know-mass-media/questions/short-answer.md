# Getting to Know Mass Media — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** The mass media are the channels that carry news and messages to a whole people at once, and they have kinds a citizen can name and tell apart.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** After the institutions that serve the citizen's body and city, the book turns to the institutions that serve — and shape — his mind.
