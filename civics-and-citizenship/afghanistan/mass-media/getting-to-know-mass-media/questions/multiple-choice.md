# Getting to Know Mass Media — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) The mass media are the channels that carry news and messages to a whole people at once, and they have kinds a citizen can name and tell apart.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) After the institutions that serve the citizen's body and city, the book turns to the institutions that serve — and shape — his mind.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
