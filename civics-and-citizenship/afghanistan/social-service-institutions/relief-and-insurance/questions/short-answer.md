# Relief and Insurance — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** The relief organisation answers disaster — floods, earthquakes and war — and insurance spreads life's risks, covering property and liability so one blow does not ruin one family.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Relief and insurance are the book's paired answer to trouble: one acts after disaster strikes a whole place, the other prepares each holder before trouble strikes him.
