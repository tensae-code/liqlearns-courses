# Relief and Insurance — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The relief organisation answers disaster — floods, earthquakes and war — and insurance spreads life's risks, covering property and liability so one blow does not ruin one family.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a child's own home, school or community in Afghanistan.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Afghanistan and lived there.
