# Relief and Insurance — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) The relief organisation answers disaster — floods, earthquakes and war — and insurance spreads life's risks, covering property and liability so one blow does not ruin one family.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Relief and insurance are the book's paired answer to trouble: one acts after disaster strikes a whole place, the other prepares each holder before trouble strikes him.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
