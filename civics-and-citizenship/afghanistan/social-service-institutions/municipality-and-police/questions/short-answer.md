# Municipality and Police — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** The municipality (شاروالی) runs the city's services, the national police keep order and security, and the traffic police govern the roads — the institutions a citizen meets in his own street.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** The municipality is named in the book by its Afghan name, sharwali (شاروالی) — the city office every urban citizen knows.
