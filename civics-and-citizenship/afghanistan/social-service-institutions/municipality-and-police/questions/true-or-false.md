# Municipality and Police — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The municipality (شاروالی) runs the city's services, the national police keep order and security, and the traffic police govern the roads — the institutions a citizen meets in his own street.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a child's own home, school or community in Afghanistan.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Afghanistan and lived there.
