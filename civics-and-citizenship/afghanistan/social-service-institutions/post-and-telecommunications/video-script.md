# Video script — Post and Telecommunications

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The post carries letters and parcels inside and outside the country, and telecommunications — landline, mobile, fax and internet — carry voices and messages instantly: together they are the citizen's first social service institutions.

The post is the old network: it takes in letters and parcels and delivers them across the country and beyond its borders, so a person in one village can reach a person in another land. Telecommunications is the new network laid over it: the landline telephone, the mobile, the fax and the internet carry the voice and the written word at once. Both are social service institutions — organised services every citizen may use, run so that connection does not depend on wealth or place.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
