# Post and Telecommunications — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) The post carries letters and parcels inside and outside the country, and telecommunications — landline, mobile, fax and internet — carry voices and messages instantly: together they are the citizen's first social service institutions.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) The book opens its tour of social service institutions with the services that connect people: post and telecommunications.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
