# Post and Telecommunications — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** The post carries letters and parcels inside and outside the country, and telecommunications — landline, mobile, fax and internet — carry voices and messages instantly: together they are the citizen's first social service institutions.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** The book opens its tour of social service institutions with the services that connect people: post and telecommunications.
