# What Needs Do We Have — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Human needs are of two kinds — material needs, which nature, God's gift, supplies and which we must use reasonably and protect, and non-material needs: knowing oneself, knowing the world, knowing the Creator, and connection with other people.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Civics in Afghanistan begins not with the state but with the human being as a creature of needs — material and spiritual.
