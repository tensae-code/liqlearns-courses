# What Needs Do We Have — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Human needs are of two kinds — material needs, which nature, God's gift, supplies and which we must use reasonably and protect, and non-material needs: knowing oneself, knowing the world, knowing the Creator, and connection with other people.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a child's own home, school or community in Afghanistan.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Afghanistan and lived there.
