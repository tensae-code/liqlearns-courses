# Hands-on — What Needs Do We Have

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write down everything you needed today from the moment you woke up: food, water, clothing, shelter — and then the needs no hand can touch: to know, to understand, to be with other people. Keep both lists; the lesson sorts them.

## Show it with real things
Take this lesson into one real act this week: do, make, visit, ask or help in a way that puts What Needs Do We Have into practice in your own community in Afghanistan — then write what happened and what the lesson would say about it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
