# Video script — What Needs Do We Have

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Human needs are of two kinds — material needs, which nature, God's gift, supplies and which we must use reasonably and protect, and non-material needs: knowing oneself, knowing the world, knowing the Creator, and connection with other people.

The material needs come from nature, and nature is given, not made by us — so the book teaches using it reasonably and protecting it, not wasting or destroying the source of life. The non-material needs are just as real: a person needs to know himself, to know the world around him, to know his Creator, and to live connected with other people. A life that feeds only the first list starves the second.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
