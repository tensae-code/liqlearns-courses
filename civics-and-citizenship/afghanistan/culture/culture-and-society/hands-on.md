# Hands-on — Culture and Society

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Try to picture your village or street with its people but no shared pattern — no common language, no shared rules of greeting, work or marriage. Then picture the pattern with no people living it. Write what is missing each time. Keep both; the lesson joins the pair.

## Show it with real things
Take this lesson into one real act this week: do, make, visit, ask or help in a way that puts Culture and Society into practice in your own community in Afghanistan — then write what happened and what the lesson would say about it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
