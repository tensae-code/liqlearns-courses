# Culture and Society — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Culture and society hold each other up — society is the people living together, culture is the pattern they live by, and neither stands without the other.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a child's own home, school or community in Afghanistan.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Afghanistan and lived there.
