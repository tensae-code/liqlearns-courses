# Culture and Society — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) Culture and society hold each other up — society is the people living together, culture is the pattern they live by, and neither stands without the other.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) The book keeps the two words apart so they can be joined rightly: people make the society; the pattern they share is its culture.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
