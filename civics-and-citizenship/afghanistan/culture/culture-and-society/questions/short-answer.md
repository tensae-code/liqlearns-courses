# Culture and Society — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Culture and society hold each other up — society is the people living together, culture is the pattern they live by, and neither stands without the other.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** The book keeps the two words apart so they can be joined rightly: people make the society; the pattern they share is its culture.
