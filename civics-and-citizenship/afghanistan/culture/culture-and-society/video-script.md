# Video script — Culture and Society

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Culture and society hold each other up — society is the people living together, culture is the pattern they live by, and neither stands without the other.

Society names the people and their relations; culture names the pattern of behaviour, values and rules those people share. A society without culture could not hold together — its members would not know how to meet, work, marry or mourn. A culture without a society is only a memory in books. Each generation receives both together and hands both on, changed a little by its own living.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
