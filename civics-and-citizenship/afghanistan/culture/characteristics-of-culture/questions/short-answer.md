# Characteristics of Culture — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Culture has marks that can be named — the characteristics that distinguish one culture's way of life from another's and hold a people's pattern together.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** To study a culture, the book first teaches the student to recognise its characteristics — what makes this pattern this pattern.
