# Characteristics of Culture — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) Culture has marks that can be named — the characteristics that distinguish one culture's way of life from another's and hold a people's pattern together.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) To study a culture, the book first teaches the student to recognise its characteristics — what makes this pattern this pattern.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
