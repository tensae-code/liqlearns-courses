# Video script — What Is Culture

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Culture is the whole pattern of learned behaviour, values and rules a people lives by — everything a society teaches its members about how to live.

Culture is not one custom but the whole pattern: the behaviours a people learns and hands on, the values it holds about what is good and shameful, and the rules it lives by. It is learned, not inherited in the blood — a child takes it from the people around him, which is why peoples differ and why a person's own way of life feels natural to him. The Grade 10 course opens here because everything after it — groups, institutions, development — stands inside a culture.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
