# What Is Culture — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Culture is the whole pattern of learned behaviour, values and rules a people lives by — everything a society teaches its members about how to live.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a child's own home, school or community in Afghanistan.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Afghanistan and lived there.
