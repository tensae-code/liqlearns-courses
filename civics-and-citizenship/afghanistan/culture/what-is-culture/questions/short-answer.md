# What Is Culture — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Culture is the whole pattern of learned behaviour, values and rules a people lives by — everything a society teaches its members about how to live.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** The upper course turns sociological: culture, groups and institutions are now studied as systems, not only lived.
