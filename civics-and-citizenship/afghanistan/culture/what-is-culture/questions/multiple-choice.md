# What Is Culture — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) Culture is the whole pattern of learned behaviour, values and rules a people lives by — everything a society teaches its members about how to live.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) The upper course turns sociological: culture, groups and institutions are now studied as systems, not only lived.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
