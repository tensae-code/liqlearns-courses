# Video script — Cultural Contacts

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
When cultures meet, they act on each other — borrowing, exchanging, sometimes clashing — up to what the book names cultural invasion, one culture overwhelming another.

Cultures have never lived sealed. Travellers, traders, neighbours and conquerors carry ways across borders, and each meeting changes both sides a little: words are borrowed, tools adopted, tastes exchanged. But contact is not always equal. The book names its extreme plainly — cultural invasion, when a stronger culture floods and displaces a weaker one. The student who can tell exchange from invasion can judge his own time's meetings of cultures.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
