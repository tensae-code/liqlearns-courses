# Cultural Contacts — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) When cultures meet, they act on each other — borrowing, exchanging, sometimes clashing — up to what the book names cultural invasion, one culture overwhelming another.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Contact is as old as trade and travel; the book asks the student to judge its forms, from fair exchange to invasion.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
