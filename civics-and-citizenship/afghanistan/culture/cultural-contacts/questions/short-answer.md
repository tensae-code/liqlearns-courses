# Cultural Contacts — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** When cultures meet, they act on each other — borrowing, exchanging, sometimes clashing — up to what the book names cultural invasion, one culture overwhelming another.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Contact is as old as trade and travel; the book asks the student to judge its forms, from fair exchange to invasion.
