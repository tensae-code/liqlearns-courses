# Cultural Contacts — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** When cultures meet, they act on each other — borrowing, exchanging, sometimes clashing — up to what the book names cultural invasion, one culture overwhelming another.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a child's own home, school or community in Afghanistan.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Afghanistan and lived there.
