# Video script — Small Cultures

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Inside the larger culture live small cultures — subcultures with patterns of their own — and where patterns rub against each other, cultural conflict can arise.

No culture is one flat cloth. Within it, regions, trades, generations and communities keep patterns of their own — small cultures, or subcultures, sharing the large pattern while adding their own. Mostly the parts live peaceably inside the whole; but where a small culture's ways offend the larger pattern, or the larger pattern crushes the small one's, cultural conflict arises — and must be understood before it can be settled.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
