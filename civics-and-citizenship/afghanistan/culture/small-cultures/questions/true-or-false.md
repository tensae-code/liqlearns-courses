# Small Cultures — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Inside the larger culture live small cultures — subcultures with patterns of their own — and where patterns rub against each other, cultural conflict can arise.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a child's own home, school or community in Afghanistan.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Afghanistan and lived there.
