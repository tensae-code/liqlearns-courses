# Small Cultures — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) Inside the larger culture live small cultures — subcultures with patterns of their own — and where patterns rub against each other, cultural conflict can arise.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) The book's word is small cultures: the whole has parts, and each part — a region, a trade, a generation — carries a pattern of its own inside the shared one.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
