# Small Cultures — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Inside the larger culture live small cultures — subcultures with patterns of their own — and where patterns rub against each other, cultural conflict can arise.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** The book's word is small cultures: the whole has parts, and each part — a region, a trade, a generation — carries a pattern of its own inside the shared one.
