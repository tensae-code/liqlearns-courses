# The Social Group

## Do it first

Take the strongest group in your life and describe it as a stranger-scientist would: its members, its shared features, its rules, what holds it together. Keep the description; this lesson studies your own first groups with new eyes.

## What the book teaches

The social group, studied at secondary depth — the same group the first book introduced, now analysed as a building-block of society itself.

## The lesson in full

The first book taught the group from inside life: people with shared features and lasting relations. The upper course takes the same thing up as sociology: the group as a unit of society, with structure — members, relations, norms — that can be examined. Everything larger in society is built from groups joined to groups, so the analysis begins here.

When a lesson asks you to look at your own life, really look: your own needs, family, group and community in Afghanistan are this course's first text.
