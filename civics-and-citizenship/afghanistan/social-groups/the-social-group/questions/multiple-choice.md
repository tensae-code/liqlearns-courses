# The Social Group — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) The social group, studied at secondary depth — the same group the first book introduced, now analysed as a building-block of society itself.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) The course returns to the group at a higher turn: what Grade 7 met in daily life, Grade 10 examines as a system.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
