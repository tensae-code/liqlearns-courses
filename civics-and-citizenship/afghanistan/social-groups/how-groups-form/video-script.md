# Video script — How Groups Form

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Groups come together by steps that can be followed — people with a shared need or aim find each other, relations form, and a collection of persons becomes a group.

Formation begins where a shared need or aim draws people together. Then relations must form: members learn each other, roles appear, shared ways of working settle. Only then is a crowd a group. Knowing the steps matters practically — a teacher, an organiser or a leader who knows how groups form can build one deliberately instead of hoping one happens.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
