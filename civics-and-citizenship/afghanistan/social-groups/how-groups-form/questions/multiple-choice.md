# How Groups Form — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) Groups come together by steps that can be followed — people with a shared need or aim find each other, relations form, and a collection of persons becomes a group.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) A group is made, not found: the book follows its formation the way the first book followed a family's success.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
