# How Groups Form — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Groups come together by steps that can be followed — people with a shared need or aim find each other, relations form, and a collection of persons becomes a group.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** A group is made, not found: the book follows its formation the way the first book followed a family's success.
