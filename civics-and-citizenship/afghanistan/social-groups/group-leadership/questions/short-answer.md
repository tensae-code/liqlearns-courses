# Group Leadership — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Every formed group raises the question of leadership — who directs it, how a leader emerges, and what leadership inside a group is for.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** The book's order is itself the teaching: first the group, then its forming, then the leadership that formed groups call forth.
