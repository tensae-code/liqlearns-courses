# Group Leadership — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Every formed group raises the question of leadership — who directs it, how a leader emerges, and what leadership inside a group is for.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a child's own home, school or community in Afghanistan.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Afghanistan and lived there.
