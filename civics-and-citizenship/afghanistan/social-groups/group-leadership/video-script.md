# Video script — Group Leadership

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Every formed group raises the question of leadership — who directs it, how a leader emerges, and what leadership inside a group is for.

A group with shared work needs direction: someone to call members together, settle disputes, speak for the whole and carry its aim when energy flags. Leaders emerge in different ways — chosen, appointed, or rising by ability and trust. The lesson examines what the leader is for and what the group owes and may demand — leadership as a function inside the group, not a prize above it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
