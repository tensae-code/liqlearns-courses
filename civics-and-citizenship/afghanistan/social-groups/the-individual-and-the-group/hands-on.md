# Hands-on — The Individual and the Group

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name one habit or way of speaking you picked up from a group you belong to, and one thing you yourself have changed in that group, even a small thing. Keep both; the lesson is about exactly this two-way road.

## Show it with real things
Take this lesson into one real act this week: do, make, visit, ask or help in a way that puts The Individual and the Group into practice in your own community in Afghanistan — then write what happened and what the lesson would say about it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
