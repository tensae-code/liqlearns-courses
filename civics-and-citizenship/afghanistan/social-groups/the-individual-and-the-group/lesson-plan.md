# Lesson plan — The Individual and the Group

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Afghanistan · **Title:** Social Groups · **Lesson:** The Individual and the Group
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — Groups and Ways of Life in Afghanistan (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The group shapes the person and the person shapes the group, each acting on the other — for good, or, as the book warns with groups fallen into addiction and crime, for ill.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — Groups and Ways of Life in Afghanistan — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: The group shapes the person and the person shapes the group, each acting on the other — for good, or, as the book warns with groups fallen into addiction and crime, for ill.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street, school or community in Afghanistan.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the learner's own life in Afghanistan and the book's own teaching, as Afghanistan's own books teach civics starting from need — the human being and what he needs, the family that first meets those needs, then groups and the country's own three ways of life — before turning to education, economic life, the services and media a citizen lives among, and at last to culture, institutions and development studied as systems.
