# The Individual and the Group — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** The group shapes the person and the person shapes the group, each acting on the other — for good, or, as the book warns with groups fallen into addiction and crime, for ill.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** The influence runs both ways: no member only receives from his group, and no group is untouched by its members.
