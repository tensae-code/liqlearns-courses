# The Individual and the Group — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The group shapes the person and the person shapes the group, each acting on the other — for good, or, as the book warns with groups fallen into addiction and crime, for ill.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a child's own home, school or community in Afghanistan.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Afghanistan and lived there.
