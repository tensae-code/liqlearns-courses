# The Individual and the Group — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) The group shapes the person and the person shapes the group, each acting on the other — for good, or, as the book warns with groups fallen into addiction and crime, for ill.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) The influence runs both ways: no member only receives from his group, and no group is untouched by its members.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
