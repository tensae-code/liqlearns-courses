# Video script — What Is a Group

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A group is people with shared features and lasting mutual relations — from primary groups like family and friends to secondary groups formed around a purpose.

A group is more than people in one place. Its members share features — a home, an aim, a work, a place — and their relations with one another last; they know each other and count on each other. The nearest groups are primary: the family and close friends, bound by affection. Beyond them stand secondary groups, larger and formed around a purpose — a class, a team, a workplace — where relations are real but less close.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
