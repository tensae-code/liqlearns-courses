# What Is a Group — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) A group is people with shared features and lasting mutual relations — from primary groups like family and friends to secondary groups formed around a purpose.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) A crowd that gathers once and scatters is not yet a group: the book's group has shared features and relations that last.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
