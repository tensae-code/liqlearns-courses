# What Is a Group — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** A group is people with shared features and lasting mutual relations — from primary groups like family and friends to secondary groups formed around a purpose.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** A crowd that gathers once and scatters is not yet a group: the book's group has shared features and relations that last.
