# Why We Need a Family — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** The family raises the child for life in society and supports its members economically and emotionally — it is the first place a person is prepared for life among people.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** The family is where the needs of the first lesson are first met — the child is raised there for life in society.
