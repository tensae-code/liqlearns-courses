# Video script — Why We Need a Family

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The family raises the child for life in society and supports its members economically and emotionally — it is the first place a person is prepared for life among people.

No child raises himself. The family feeds and shelters its members, earns and provides for them, and carries them through sickness and trouble — its economic support. And it does what money cannot: it loves, comforts and steadies its members — its emotional support. Above all it raises the child step by step for life in society, so that the person who leaves the family can stand among people.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
