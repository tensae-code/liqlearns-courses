# Video script — The Successful Family

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A successful family is marked by affection, responsibility, cooperation, consultation and order — and by tolerating one another's views instead of imposing one's own opinion.

Affection holds the members together; responsibility means each does his part; cooperation means the work is shared; consultation means decisions are talked through, not handed down; and order means the family's life has a shape its members can rely on. The second part adds the hardest mark: tolerating one another's views — listening to an opinion you do not share without forcing your own on the other person.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
