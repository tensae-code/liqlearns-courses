# The Successful Family — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A successful family is marked by affection, responsibility, cooperation, consultation and order — and by tolerating one another's views instead of imposing one's own opinion.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a child's own home, school or community in Afghanistan.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Afghanistan and lived there.
