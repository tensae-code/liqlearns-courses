# The Successful Family — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** A successful family is marked by affection, responsibility, cooperation, consultation and order — and by tolerating one another's views instead of imposing one's own opinion.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Consultation — deciding together — is one of the book's own marks of the family that succeeds.
