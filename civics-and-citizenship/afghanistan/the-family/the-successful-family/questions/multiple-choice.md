# The Successful Family — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) A successful family is marked by affection, responsibility, cooperation, consultation and order — and by tolerating one another's views instead of imposing one's own opinion.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Consultation — deciding together — is one of the book's own marks of the family that succeeds.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
