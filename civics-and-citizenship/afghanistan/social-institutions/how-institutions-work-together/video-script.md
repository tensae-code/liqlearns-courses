# Video script — How Institutions Work Together

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Institutions depend on and complete one another — family, education, religion, economy and polity form one working whole, and a weakness in one is felt in all.

The family raises the child the school teaches; the school prepares the worker the economy employs; religion steadies the morals the law also guards; the polity protects them all and is staffed by people the others formed. That is the mutual relation of institutions: each hands its work to the next and receives the next's work in return. A society's institutions stand or sag together.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
