# How Institutions Work Together — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Institutions depend on and complete one another — family, education, religion, economy and polity form one working whole, and a weakness in one is felt in all.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** The book's word is mutual relations: no institution in the chapter stands alone, just as no group in the last chapter did.
