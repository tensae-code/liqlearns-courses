# Video script — What Is a Social Institution

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A social institution is an organised system of relations answering a basic need of society — the family and marriage first, then education and religion — giving members their roles and norms, with political institutions making law, keeping security, providing services and defending the whole.

An institution is not a building but a system: organised relations, roles and norms built around one of society's basic needs, lasting beyond any member's life. The book walks the great ones in order — family and marriage, education from cradle to grave, religion — then draws the general lesson: institutions hand each person his roles and the norms that govern them. The political institutions stand in summary at the end: law-making, security, services and defence.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
