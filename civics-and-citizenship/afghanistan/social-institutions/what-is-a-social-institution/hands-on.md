# Hands-on — What Is a Social Institution

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
List the needs your society must answer every single generation — children raised, the young taught, disputes settled, the land defended. Beside each, write the lasting arrangement that answers it. Keep the pairs; the lesson names what you have listed.

## Show it with real things
Take this lesson into one real act this week: do, make, visit, ask or help in a way that puts What Is a Social Institution into practice in your own community in Afghanistan — then write what happened and what the lesson would say about it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
