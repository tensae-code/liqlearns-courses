# What Is a Social Institution — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** A social institution is an organised system of relations answering a basic need of society — the family and marriage first, then education and religion — giving members their roles and norms, with political institutions making law, keeping security, providing services and defending the whole.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Institutions are groups grown permanent: where a basic need never goes away, society builds a lasting system to answer it.
