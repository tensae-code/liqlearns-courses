# What Is a Social Institution — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A social institution is an organised system of relations answering a basic need of society — the family and marriage first, then education and religion — giving members their roles and norms, with political institutions making law, keeping security, providing services and defending the whole.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a child's own home, school or community in Afghanistan.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Afghanistan and lived there.
