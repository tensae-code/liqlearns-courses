# Hands-on — How Village People Live

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Think of a job in your own place that no single family could finish alone — a canal to clear, a roof to raise, a harvest to bring in. Who would come, and how would it be organised? Keep your answer; the book gives this working-together its own name.

## Show it with real things
Take this lesson into one real act this week: do, make, visit, ask or help in a way that puts How Village People Live into practice in your own community in Afghanistan — then write what happened and what the lesson would say about it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
