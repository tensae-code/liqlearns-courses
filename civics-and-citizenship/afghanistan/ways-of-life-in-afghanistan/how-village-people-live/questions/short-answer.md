# How Village People Live — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Village people live by family farming and by collective work — hashar, neighbours working together on dams, bridges, canals and harvests — while facing shortages of land, water and tools.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Hashar (حشر) is the village's own institution in the book: the collective work by which big tasks no one family could do get done.
