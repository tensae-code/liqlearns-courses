# How Village People Live — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) Village people live by family farming and by collective work — hashar, neighbours working together on dams, bridges, canals and harvests — while facing shortages of land, water and tools.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Hashar (حشر) is the village's own institution in the book: the collective work by which big tasks no one family could do get done.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
