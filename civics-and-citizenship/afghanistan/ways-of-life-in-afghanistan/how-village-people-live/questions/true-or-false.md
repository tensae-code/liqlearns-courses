# How Village People Live — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Village people live by family farming and by collective work — hashar, neighbours working together on dams, bridges, canals and harvests — while facing shortages of land, water and tools.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a child's own home, school or community in Afghanistan.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Afghanistan and lived there.
