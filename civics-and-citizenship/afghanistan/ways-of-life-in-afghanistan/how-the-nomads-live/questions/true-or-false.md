# How the Nomads Live — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Afghanistan's nomads, the Kuchi, live a tent life built on herding, with the work of the tent divided among its members — and they face hardships in schooling and health that settled people do not.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a child's own home, school or community in Afghanistan.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Afghanistan and lived there.
