# How the Nomads Live — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Afghanistan's nomads, the Kuchi, live a tent life built on herding, with the work of the tent divided among its members — and they face hardships in schooling and health that settled people do not.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** The nomad way is one of Afghanistan's own three ways of life in the book: nomad, village and city.
