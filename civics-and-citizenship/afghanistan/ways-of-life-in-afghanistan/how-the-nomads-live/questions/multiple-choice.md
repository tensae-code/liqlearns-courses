# How the Nomads Live — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) Afghanistan's nomads, the Kuchi, live a tent life built on herding, with the work of the tent divided among its members — and they face hardships in schooling and health that settled people do not.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) The nomad way is one of Afghanistan's own three ways of life in the book: nomad, village and city.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
