# Video script — Urban Life in Afghanistan

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
City life in Afghanistan joins family life to wage work and services — and carries the city's own problems: electricity shortage, polluted air and overcrowding.

In the city the family earns by jobs and trades rather than by its own land, and it buys what the village family makes — food, water, power, schooling, care. In exchange the city offers work, services and chances the village lacks. But the book names the city's problems without flinching: electricity that fails, air polluted by traffic and smoke, and overcrowding as more people press into the same streets and houses.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
