# Urban Life in Afghanistan — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** City life in Afghanistan joins family life to wage work and services — and carries the city's own problems: electricity shortage, polluted air and overcrowding.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a child's own home, school or community in Afghanistan.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Afghanistan and lived there.
