# Urban Life in Afghanistan — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** City life in Afghanistan joins family life to wage work and services — and carries the city's own problems: electricity shortage, polluted air and overcrowding.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** The city is the third of the book's three ways of life in Afghanistan, after the nomad and the village.
