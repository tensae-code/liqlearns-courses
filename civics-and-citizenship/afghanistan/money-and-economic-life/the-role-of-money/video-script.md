# Video script — The Role of Money

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Money is the measure and the go-between of economic life — it lets work and goods be exchanged, valued and saved, and daily life runs on it.

Money lets a person sell his work in the morning and buy another's work in the evening, though the two never meet. It measures value, so unlike things can be compared and priced; it carries value forward, so today's earning can be saved for next season's need. That is money's role in our lives: not wealth itself, but the instrument by which economic life moves.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
