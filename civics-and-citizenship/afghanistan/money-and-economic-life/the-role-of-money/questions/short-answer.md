# The Role of Money — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Money is the measure and the go-between of economic life — it lets work and goods be exchanged, valued and saved, and daily life runs on it.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Without money every exchange would be a barter: the book shows money doing in daily life what direct swapping cannot.
