# The Role of Money — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) Money is the measure and the go-between of economic life — it lets work and goods be exchanged, valued and saved, and daily life runs on it.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Without money every exchange would be a barter: the book shows money doing in daily life what direct swapping cannot.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
