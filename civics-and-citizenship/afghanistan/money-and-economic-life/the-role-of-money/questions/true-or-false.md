# The Role of Money — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Money is the measure and the go-between of economic life — it lets work and goods be exchanged, valued and saved, and daily life runs on it.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a child's own home, school or community in Afghanistan.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Afghanistan and lived there.
