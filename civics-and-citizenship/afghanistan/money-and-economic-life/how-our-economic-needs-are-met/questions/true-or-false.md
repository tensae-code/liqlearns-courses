# How Our Economic Needs Are Met — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A household and a country meet their economic needs by work and production — earning, making and providing what people need to live.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a child's own home, school or community in Afghanistan.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Afghanistan and lived there.
