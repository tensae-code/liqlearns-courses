# How Our Economic Needs Are Met — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** A household and a country meet their economic needs by work and production — earning, making and providing what people need to live.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** The first book's needs return here as economic needs: the question is no longer what we need, but how work meets it.
