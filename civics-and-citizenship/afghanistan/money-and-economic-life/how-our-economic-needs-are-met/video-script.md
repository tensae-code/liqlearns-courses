# Video script — How Our Economic Needs Are Met

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A household and a country meet their economic needs by work and production — earning, making and providing what people need to live.

Needs do not meet themselves. Behind every meal and garment stands work: farming, making, carrying, trading, serving. A household earns by its members' work and provides what it cannot make by exchange; a country does the same at its own scale, producing what it can and trading for the rest. Economic life is the organised answer to the question the first book asked: what do we need?

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
