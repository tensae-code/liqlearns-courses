# Hands-on — How Our Economic Needs Are Met

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Follow one thing your family used today — bread, say — backwards: who made it, who grew its wheat, who carried it, who paid whom? Write the chain as far as you can. The lesson is about that chain.

## Show it with real things
Take this lesson into one real act this week: do, make, visit, ask or help in a way that puts How Our Economic Needs Are Met into practice in your own community in Afghanistan — then write what happened and what the lesson would say about it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
