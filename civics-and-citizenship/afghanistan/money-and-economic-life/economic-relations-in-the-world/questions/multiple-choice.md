# Economic Relations in the World — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) Countries trade and depend on one another — no country makes everything it needs, so economic relations bind the world's peoples together.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) The lesson widens the circle a last time in this book's economics: household, country, then the world.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
