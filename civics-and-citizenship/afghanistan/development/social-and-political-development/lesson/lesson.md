# Social and Political Development

## Do it first

Write one way the public life of your place works better than it did a generation ago, and one way it works worse. Keep both; the lesson takes development into society and politics together.

## What the book teaches

Development in society and politics — a people's social life and its political order maturing together, so institutions serve the whole society better.

## The lesson in full

Societies develop socially as their institutions widen — more children schooled, more disputes settled by law, more people taking part. They develop politically as their order matures: government that answers to the people it serves, institutions that outlast persons. The book treats the two as one subject, social-political development, because in real life neither moves far without the other.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: development in society and politics — a people's social life and its political order maturing together, so institutions serve the whole society better. Give one real example from your own life in Afghanistan, and name the lesson's key words: social-political development, political order. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Afghanistan's own books teach civics starting from need — the human being and what he needs, the family that first meets those needs, then groups and the country's own three ways of life — before turning to education, economic life, the services and media a citizen lives among, and at last to culture, institutions and development studied as systems. When a lesson asks you to look at your own life, really look: your own needs, family, group and community in Afghanistan are this course's first text.
