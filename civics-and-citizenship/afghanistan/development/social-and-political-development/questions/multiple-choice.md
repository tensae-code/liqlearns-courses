# Social and Political Development — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) Development in society and politics — a people's social life and its political order maturing together, so institutions serve the whole society better.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) The book joins the social and the political in one lesson: a society's life together and its government grow — or fail to grow — as one.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
