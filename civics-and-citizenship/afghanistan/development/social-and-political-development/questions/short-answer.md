# Social and Political Development — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Development in society and politics — a people's social life and its political order maturing together, so institutions serve the whole society better.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** The book joins the social and the political in one lesson: a society's life together and its government grow — or fail to grow — as one.
