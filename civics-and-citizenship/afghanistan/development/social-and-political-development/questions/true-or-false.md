# Social and Political Development — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Development in society and politics — a people's social life and its political order maturing together, so institutions serve the whole society better.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a child's own home, school or community in Afghanistan.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Afghanistan and lived there.
