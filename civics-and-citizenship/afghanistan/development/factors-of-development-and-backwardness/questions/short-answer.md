# Factors of Development and Backwardness — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Development has factors that move a country forward and factors that hold it back — and a citizen who can name both can judge his own country's road.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** The book asks the hard question directly: why do nations fall behind — and answers it with factors, not fate.
