# Hands-on — Cultural Development

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name one way your people's shared life is richer than your grandparents' — a school in the village, books, a craft revived — and one way it is poorer. Keep both; the lesson studies culture itself as something that develops.

## Show it with real things
Take this lesson into one real act this week: do, make, visit, ask or help in a way that puts Cultural Development into practice in your own community in Afghanistan — then write what happened and what the lesson would say about it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
