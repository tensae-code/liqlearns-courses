# Cultural Development — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Cultural development is the growth of a people's culture — its knowledge, arts and shared life deepening and spreading through the society.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** Development's first form in the book is cultural: before economies and states, a people's own pattern of life itself grows or stalls.
