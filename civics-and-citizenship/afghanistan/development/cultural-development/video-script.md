# Video script — Cultural Development

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Cultural development is the growth of a people's culture — its knowledge, arts and shared life deepening and spreading through the society.

A culture is not fixed. It develops as knowledge spreads, as arts and crafts are practised and taught, as more of the people share in the society's store of learning and making. Cultural development is that growth measured in the people's own pattern of life — the first form of development the book examines, because the other forms grow from it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
