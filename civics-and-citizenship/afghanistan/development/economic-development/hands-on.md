# Hands-on — Economic Development

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
List what your country would produce more of, and better, if its economy developed for twenty years — and what new work its people would do. Keep the list; the lesson studies the economy's development as one form of the whole.

## Show it with real things
Take this lesson into one real act this week: do, make, visit, ask or help in a way that puts Economic Development into practice in your own community in Afghanistan — then write what happened and what the lesson would say about it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
