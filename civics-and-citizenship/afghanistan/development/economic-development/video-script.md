# Video script — Economic Development

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Economic development is the growth of a country's economy — more and better production, work and provision for its people's needs.

An economy develops when it produces more and better — farms yielding more, workshops and industries growing, roads and power reaching further, and the people's needs met more fully than the generation before. It is the form of development most easily counted, which is why the book sets it in its place: one form among several, standing on the cultural and social-political ground the last lessons laid.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
