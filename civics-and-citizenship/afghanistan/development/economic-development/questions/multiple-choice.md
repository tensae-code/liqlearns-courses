# Economic Development — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) Economic development is the growth of a country's economy — more and better production, work and provision for its people's needs.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) The Grade 8 book's economic life returns at the top of the ladder as economic development: the same needs, now asked at the scale of a nation's future.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
