# Economic Development — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Economic development is the growth of a country's economy — more and better production, work and provision for its people's needs.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a child's own home, school or community in Afghanistan.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Afghanistan and lived there.
