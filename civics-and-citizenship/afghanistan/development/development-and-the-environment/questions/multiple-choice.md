# Development and the Environment — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) Development has an account with the natural world — it draws on nature's stores and can protect or exhaust them, so true development must reckon with the environment it depends on.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) The course in hand closes where its first lesson began: nature as the God-given source of material needs, to be used reasonably and protected — now at the scale of a nation's development.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
