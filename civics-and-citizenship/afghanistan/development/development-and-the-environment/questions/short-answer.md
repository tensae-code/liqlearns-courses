# Development and the Environment — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Development has an account with the natural world — it draws on nature's stores and can protect or exhaust them, so true development must reckon with the environment it depends on.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** The course in hand closes where its first lesson began: nature as the God-given source of material needs, to be used reasonably and protected — now at the scale of a nation's development.
