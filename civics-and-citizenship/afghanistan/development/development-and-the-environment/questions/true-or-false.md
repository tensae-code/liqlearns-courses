# Development and the Environment — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Development has an account with the natural world — it draws on nature's stores and can protect or exhaust them, so true development must reckon with the environment it depends on.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a child's own home, school or community in Afghanistan.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Afghanistan and lived there.
