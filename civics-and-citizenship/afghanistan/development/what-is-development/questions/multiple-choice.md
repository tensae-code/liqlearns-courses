# What Is Development — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) Development is a people's forward movement — growth and change in how a society lives, works and organises itself, judged as a whole and not by one number.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) The course in hand closes on development: after culture, groups and institutions, the student asks where the whole society is going.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
