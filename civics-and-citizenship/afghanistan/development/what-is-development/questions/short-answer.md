# What Is Development — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Development is a people's forward movement — growth and change in how a society lives, works and organises itself, judged as a whole and not by one number.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** The course in hand closes on development: after culture, groups and institutions, the student asks where the whole society is going.
