# What Is Development — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Development is a people's forward movement — growth and change in how a society lives, works and organises itself, judged as a whole and not by one number.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a child's own home, school or community in Afghanistan.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Afghanistan and lived there.
