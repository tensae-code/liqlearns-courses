# What Is Development — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — development is a people's forward movement — growth and change in how a society lives, works and organises itself, judged as a whole and not by one number — in their own words, give one real example from their own life in Afghanistan, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or community situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
