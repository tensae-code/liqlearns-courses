# Why We Need Education — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state the lesson's one thing — education and upbringing form the person — and the school's gifts are knowledge, moral upbringing and initiative: what a person knows, how he behaves, and his power to begin — in their own words, give one real example from their own life in Afghanistan, and answer the lesson's questions. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners: first the plain cases from the lesson, then cases where the lesson's opposite (its false friend) tempts the wrong answer, then the lesson applied in a new place — a new home, school or community situation the learner has not seen in the lesson.
