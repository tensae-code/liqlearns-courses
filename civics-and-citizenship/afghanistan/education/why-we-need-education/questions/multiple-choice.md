# Why We Need Education — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) Education and upbringing form the person — and the school's gifts are knowledge, moral upbringing and initiative: what a person knows, how he behaves, and his power to begin.  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) The book joins teaching and upbringing in one word-pair: to educate is to raise, not only to inform.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the lesson teaches.
