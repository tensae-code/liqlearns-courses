# Why We Need Education — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Education and upbringing form the person — and the school's gifts are knowledge, moral upbringing and initiative: what a person knows, how he behaves, and his power to begin.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** The book joins teaching and upbringing in one word-pair: to educate is to raise, not only to inform.
