# Video script — Why We Need Education

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Education and upbringing form the person — and the school's gifts are knowledge, moral upbringing and initiative: what a person knows, how he behaves, and his power to begin.

The book first says what education and upbringing mean, and gives their short history — people have always handed on what they know and how they live. The school is that handing-on made deliberate. Its gifts are three: knowledge, so the pupil knows the world truly; moral upbringing, so he behaves well among people; and initiative, so he can begin work himself instead of only waiting to be told.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
