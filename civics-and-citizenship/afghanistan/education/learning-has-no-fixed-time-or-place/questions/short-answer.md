# Learning Has No Fixed Time or Place — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Learning is not bound to one age or one classroom — a person can learn young and old, at school and outside it, all through life.

**2.** What does this lesson add?
**Answer:** The school's gifts do not expire: what education begins, a whole life can continue.
