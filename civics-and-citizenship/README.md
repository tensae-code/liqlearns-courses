# Civics and Citizenship — a folder of courses, one per country

Civics is not the same thing in every country: the laws, the
constitution, the institutions and the duties of a citizen belong to
the country itself. So this top-level entry is a **folder**, not one
world course: inside it there is **one course per country**, found by
searching the country.

Inside each country course, everything is organised by **levels** —
what the learner proves, step by step — never by school grade. The
school grades the books come from appear only in each lesson's
sources, as provenance.

## The courses in this folder

- **Ethiopia** — [`ethiopia/`](ethiopia/) · 384 lessons: the Moral
  Education opening levels (morals and manners, Grades 1–6 teacher
  guides), the young citizen's rights-and-law level, the Citizenship
  Education levels (Grades 7–10 student books with their guides), and
  a clearly-labelled older-generation section (Civics and Ethical
  Education, Grade 11, 2002 E.C.). See `ethiopia/ABOUT.md` for the
  full book list and the acceptance reckoning.
- **Sudan** — [`sudan/`](sudan/) · 6 lessons: Level 1 only — the
  homeland and the self as Sudanese, from Sudan's Grade 3 History
  and National Education student book (Arabic; its two
  national-education units placed, its history/geography units
  named, not placed). See `sudan/ABOUT.md` for the full book list
  and the acceptance reckoning.
- **Uganda** — [`uganda/`](uganda/) · 7 lessons: Levels 1–4 —
  identity and belonging, community and culture, peace and
  conflict resolution (Mato Oput), and how societies govern
  themselves, from Uganda's Senior One History and Political
  Education prototype student book (English; PARTIAL by
  combined-book scope, with its teacher's guide as labelled
  evidence; the Senior Four teacher's guide is record-only,
  UNREAD). See `uganda/ABOUT.md` for the full book list and the
  acceptance reckoning.
- **Slovenia** — [`slovenia/`](slovenia/) · 10 lessons: Level 1 only —
  first steps into the homeland (who we are and where we are at
  home, the anthem, the flag and coat of arms, the year's
  holidays, and the country place by place through its tales),
  from Slovenia's *Slovenija, moja domovina* teacher handbook
  for the first triad of primary school (Slovenian, 2012;
  teacher-handbook evidence only — the pupil booklet is not in
  hand). See `slovenia/ABOUT.md` for the full book list and the
  acceptance reckoning.
- **Tunisia** — [`tunisia/`](tunisia/) · 9 lessons: Level 2 only —
  the citizen in the world (the international community, rights at
  the international level, and world challenges), from Tunisia's
  Civic Education student book for 3rd-year Letters (Arabic,
  COMPLETE); Level 1 is sketched, not stocked — the G8 Social
  Subjects civics unit was absent from its scan (PARTIAL, 0
  placements). See `tunisia/ABOUT.md` for the full book list and
  the acceptance reckoning.
