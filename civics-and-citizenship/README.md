# Civics and Citizenship — a folder of courses, one per country

Civics is not the same thing in every country: the laws, the
constitution, the institutions and the duties of a citizen belong to
the country itself. So this top-level entry is a **folder**, not one
world course: inside it there is **one course per country**, found by
searching the country.

Inside each country course, everything is organised by **levels** —
what the learner proves, step by step — never by school grade. The
school grades the books come from appear only in each lesson's
sources, as provenance.

## The courses in this folder

- **Ethiopia** — [`ethiopia/`](ethiopia/) · 382 lessons: the Moral
  Education opening levels (morals and manners, Grades 1–6 teacher
  guides), the young citizen's rights-and-law level, the Citizenship
  Education levels (Grades 7–10 student books with their guides), and
  a clearly-labelled older-generation section (Civics and Ethical
  Education, Grade 11, 2002 E.C.). See `ethiopia/ABOUT.md` for the
  full book list and the acceptance reckoning.
