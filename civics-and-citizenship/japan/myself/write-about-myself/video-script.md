# Video script — Write About Myself

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
You can write your own profile — who you are now, and the person you want to become.

My profile now. Write about yourself for the new school years: your favourite play, food, song and book, and what you are good at.

The person I want to become. Write your dream and the person you want to become, so your profile points forward, not only back.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
