# Hands-on — Write About Myself

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write four lines about yourself: a favourite thing, a song or book you love, one thing you are good at, and the person you want to become.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: you can write your own profile — who you are now, and the person you want to become. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
