# Write About Myself

## Do it first

Write four lines about yourself: a favourite thing, a song or book you love, one thing you are good at, and the person you want to become.

## Sub lesson 1 — My profile now

Write about yourself for the new school years: your favourite play, food, song and book, and what you are good at.

## Sub lesson 2 — The person I want to become

Write your dream and the person you want to become, so your profile points forward, not only back.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: you can write your own profile — who you are now, and the person you want to become. Give one real example from your own life in Japan, and name the lesson's key words: profile, write, dream, become. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Japanese as well as in English — Japan's own books teach Moral Education (道徳, Doutoku) in Japanese, through stories and self-check pages, for life at home and at school, not only for the classroom.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: you can write your own profile — who you are now, and the person you want to become.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
