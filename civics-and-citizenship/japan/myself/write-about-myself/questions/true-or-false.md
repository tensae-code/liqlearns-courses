# Write About Myself — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** You can write your own profile — who you are now, and the person you want to become.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a child's own home, school or community in Japan.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Japan and lived there.
