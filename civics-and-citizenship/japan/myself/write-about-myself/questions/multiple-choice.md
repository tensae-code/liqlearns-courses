# Write About Myself — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) you can write your own profile — who you are now, and the person you want to become  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — My profile now?
a) Write about yourself for the new school years: your favourite play, food, song and book, and what you are good at.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
