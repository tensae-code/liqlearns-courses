# Write About Myself — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** You can write your own profile — who you are now, and the person you want to become.

**2.** What does the part "The person I want to become" teach?
**Answer:** Write your dream and the person you want to become, so your profile points forward, not only back.
