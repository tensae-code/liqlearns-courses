# Polish Myself — Honesty and Growth — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** A bright, sincere heart seeks new things, fixes its faults, and grows its strengths.

**2.** What does the part "Fix faults, grow strengths" teach?
**Answer:** Seek new things willingly; correct your shortcomings honestly, and grow your strengths on purpose.
