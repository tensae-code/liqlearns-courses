# Polish Myself — Honesty and Growth — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** Sincere, with a bright heart — a)

**2.** Fix faults, grow strengths — b)


Meanings:
a) Be sincere and honest with a bright heart — in words, in work, and in how you meet people.
b) Seek new things willingly; correct your shortcomings honestly, and grow your strengths on purpose.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
