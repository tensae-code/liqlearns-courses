# Polish Myself — Honesty and Growth

## Do it first

Write down one shortcoming you will work on and one strength you will grow this month. Say the first small act for each.

## Sub lesson 1 — Sincere, with a bright heart

Be sincere and honest with a bright heart — in words, in work, and in how you meet people.

## Sub lesson 2 — Fix faults, grow strengths

Seek new things willingly; correct your shortcomings honestly, and grow your strengths on purpose.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: a bright, sincere heart seeks new things, fixes its faults, and grows its strengths. Give one real example from your own life in Japan, and name the lesson's key words: sincere, bright, shortcomings, strengths. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Japanese as well as in English — Japan's own books teach Moral Education (道徳, Doutoku) in Japanese, through stories and self-check pages, for life at home and at school, not only for the classroom.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: a bright, sincere heart seeks new things, fixes its faults, and grows its strengths.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
