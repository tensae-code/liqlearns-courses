# Hands-on — Polish Myself — Honesty and Growth

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write down one shortcoming you will work on and one strength you will grow this month. Say the first small act for each.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: a bright, sincere heart seeks new things, fixes its faults, and grows its strengths. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
