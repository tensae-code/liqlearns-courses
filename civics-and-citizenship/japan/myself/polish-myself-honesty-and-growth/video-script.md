# Video script — Polish Myself — Honesty and Growth

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A bright, sincere heart seeks new things, fixes its faults, and grows its strengths.

Sincere, with a bright heart. Be sincere and honest with a bright heart — in words, in work, and in how you meet people.

Fix faults, grow strengths. Seek new things willingly; correct your shortcomings honestly, and grow your strengths on purpose.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
