# Video script — An Unpolished Jewel Shines Not

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Like a jewel, a person shines only when polished — notice the self you want to change, and show your own character.

The jewel and the polishing. The old teaching from the Book of Rites says a jewel unpolished does not shine — people, too, become bright only by being worked on.

The self I want to change. Notice honestly the self you want to change, polish that part, and let your own character show — no one else's shine can replace yours.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
