# Hands-on — An Unpolished Jewel Shines Not

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name one rough edge in yourself you want to polish. Say what polishing it looks like in one ordinary day this week, and do that day.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: like a jewel, a person shines only when polished — notice the self you want to change, and show your own character. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
