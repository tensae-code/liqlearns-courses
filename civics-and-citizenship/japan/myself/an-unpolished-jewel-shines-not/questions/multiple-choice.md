# An Unpolished Jewel Shines Not — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) like a jewel, a person shines only when polished — notice the self you want to change, and show your own character  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — The jewel and the polishing?
a) The old teaching from the Book of Rites says a jewel unpolished does not shine — people, too, become bright only by being worked on.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
