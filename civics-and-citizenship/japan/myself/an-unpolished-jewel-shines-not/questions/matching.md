# An Unpolished Jewel Shines Not — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** The jewel and the polishing — a)

**2.** The self I want to change — b)


Meanings:
a) The old teaching from the Book of Rites says a jewel unpolished does not shine — people, too, become bright only by being worked on.
b) Notice honestly the self you want to change, polish that part, and let your own character show — no one else's shine can replace yours.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
