# An Unpolished Jewel Shines Not — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Like a jewel, a person shines only when polished — notice the self you want to change, and show your own character.

**2.** What does the part "The self I want to change" teach?
**Answer:** Notice honestly the self you want to change, polish that part, and let your own character show — no one else's shine can replace yours.
