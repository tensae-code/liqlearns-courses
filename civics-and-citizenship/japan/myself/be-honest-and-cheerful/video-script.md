# Video script — Be Honest and Cheerful

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
An honest, open heart is a cheerful heart — say sorry, own your mistakes, and keep your temper.

The things I must never do. Never lie, never take others' things, never hit, bully, hide the truth or speak ill of others — an honest heart starts by refusing these.

Sorry, and a calmer face. Saying sorry brings relief — the child who blamed his younger brother feels it, and Koro the cricket, seeing his sulky face in a moonlit dewdrop, learns to apologise and control his temper.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
