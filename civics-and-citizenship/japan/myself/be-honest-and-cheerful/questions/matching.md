# Be Honest and Cheerful — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** The things I must never do — a)

**2.** Sorry, and a calmer face — b)


Meanings:
a) Never lie, never take others' things, never hit, bully, hide the truth or speak ill of others — an honest heart starts by refusing these.
b) Saying sorry brings relief — the child who blamed his younger brother feels it, and Koro the cricket, seeing his sulky face in a moonlit dewdrop, learns to apologise and control his temper.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
