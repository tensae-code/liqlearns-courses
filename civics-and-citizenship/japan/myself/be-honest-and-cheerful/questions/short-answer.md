# Be Honest and Cheerful — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** An honest, open heart is a cheerful heart — say sorry, own your mistakes, and keep your temper.

**2.** What does the part "Sorry, and a calmer face" teach?
**Answer:** Saying sorry brings relief — the child who blamed his younger brother feels it, and Koro the cricket, seeing his sulky face in a moonlit dewdrop, learns to apologise and control his temper.
