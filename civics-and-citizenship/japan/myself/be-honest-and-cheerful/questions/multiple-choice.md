# Be Honest and Cheerful — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) an honest, open heart is a cheerful heart — say sorry, own your mistakes, and keep your temper  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — The things I must never do?
a) Never lie, never take others' things, never hit, bully, hide the truth or speak ill of others — an honest heart starts by refusing these.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
