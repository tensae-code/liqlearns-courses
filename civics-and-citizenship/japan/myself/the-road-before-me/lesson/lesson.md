# The Road Before Me

## Do it first

Draw your road: mark one straight part, one winding part, one uphill part of your life this year. Say what new self you hope to meet at the end of it.

## Sub lesson 1 — The one road ahead

The poem's road runs on before me — straight, winding, uphill and down — and it is my road: looking at myself honestly is how I start walking it.

## Sub lesson 2 — Meeting my new self

Living my own life on that road, I meet a new self — who I become is made by how I walk.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: one road runs ahead of me — straight, winding, uphill and down — and walking it, I meet my new self. Give one real example from your own life in Japan, and name the lesson's key words: road, myself, grow, future. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Japanese as well as in English — Japan's own books teach Moral Education (道徳, Doutoku) in Japanese, through stories and self-check pages, for life at home and at school, not only for the classroom.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: one road runs ahead of me — straight, winding, uphill and down — and walking it, I meet my new self.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
