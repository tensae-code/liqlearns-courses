# The Road Before Me — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** The one road ahead — a)

**2.** Meeting my new self — b)


Meanings:
a) The poem's road runs on before me — straight, winding, uphill and down — and it is my road: looking at myself honestly is how I start walking it.
b) Living my own life on that road, I meet a new self — who I become is made by how I walk.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
