# The Road Before Me — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** One road runs ahead of me — straight, winding, uphill and down — and walking it, I meet my new self.

**2.** What does the part "Meeting my new self" teach?
**Answer:** Living my own life on that road, I meet a new self — who I become is made by how I walk.
