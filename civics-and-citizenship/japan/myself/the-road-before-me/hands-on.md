# Hands-on — The Road Before Me

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Draw your road: mark one straight part, one winding part, one uphill part of your life this year. Say what new self you hope to meet at the end of it.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: one road runs ahead of me — straight, winding, uphill and down — and walking it, I meet my new self. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
