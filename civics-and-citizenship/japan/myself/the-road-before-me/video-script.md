# Video script — The Road Before Me

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
One road runs ahead of me — straight, winding, uphill and down — and walking it, I meet my new self.

The one road ahead. The poem's road runs on before me — straight, winding, uphill and down — and it is my road: looking at myself honestly is how I start walking it.

Meeting my new self. Living my own life on that road, I meet a new self — who I become is made by how I walk.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
