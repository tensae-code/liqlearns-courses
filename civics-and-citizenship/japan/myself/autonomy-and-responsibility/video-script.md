# Video script — Autonomy and Responsibility

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
To grow as a person I must govern myself and take responsibility — judging with my own will.

Governing myself. Autonomy means judging with your own will: I decide, by my own thinking, what is right to do — not merely copying others.

Responsibility, to others and myself. To live and grow as a person, act with responsibility toward others and toward yourself — your acts reach both.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
