# Autonomy and Responsibility — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** To grow as a person I must govern myself and take responsibility — judging with my own will.

**2.** What does the part "Responsibility, to others and myself" teach?
**Answer:** To live and grow as a person, act with responsibility toward others and toward yourself — your acts reach both.
