# Autonomy and Responsibility — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) to grow as a person I must govern myself and take responsibility — judging with my own will  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — Governing myself?
a) Autonomy means judging with your own will: I decide, by my own thinking, what is right to do — not merely copying others.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
