# Autonomy and Responsibility — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** Governing myself — a)

**2.** Responsibility, to others and myself — b)


Meanings:
a) Autonomy means judging with your own will: I decide, by my own thinking, what is right to do — not merely copying others.
b) To live and grow as a person, act with responsibility toward others and toward yourself — your acts reach both.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
