# Autonomy and Responsibility

## Do it first

Make one decision today entirely by your own judgement. Write what you decided, why, and who it affects — including yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Governing myself

Autonomy means judging with your own will: I decide, by my own thinking, what is right to do — not merely copying others.

## Sub lesson 2 — Responsibility, to others and myself

To live and grow as a person, act with responsibility toward others and toward yourself — your acts reach both.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: to grow as a person I must govern myself and take responsibility — judging with my own will. Give one real example from your own life in Japan, and name the lesson's key words: autonomy, responsibility, will, judgement. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Japanese as well as in English — Japan's own books teach Moral Education (道徳, Doutoku) in Japanese, through stories and self-check pages, for life at home and at school, not only for the classroom.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: to grow as a person I must govern myself and take responsibility — judging with my own will.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
