# Video script — Polish Myself — Moderation and Courage

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Polishing myself starts with self-control, hope and courage, and acting on my own responsibility.

Moderation and self-control. Keep moderation and self-restraint in daily life — wanting, eating, playing and speaking all have their right measure.

Hope, courage, responsibility. Hold hope and courage without giving up, and act autonomously and responsibly — your choices are yours, and so are their results.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
