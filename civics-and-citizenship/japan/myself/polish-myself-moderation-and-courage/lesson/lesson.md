# Polish Myself — Moderation and Courage

## Do it first

Name one thing you will use in moderation today, and one hard thing you will not give up on. Say how you will answer for both choices yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Moderation and self-control

Keep moderation and self-restraint in daily life — wanting, eating, playing and speaking all have their right measure.

## Sub lesson 2 — Hope, courage, responsibility

Hold hope and courage without giving up, and act autonomously and responsibly — your choices are yours, and so are their results.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: polishing myself starts with self-control, hope and courage, and acting on my own responsibility. Give one real example from your own life in Japan, and name the lesson's key words: moderation, courage, hope, responsibility. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Japanese as well as in English — Japan's own books teach Moral Education (道徳, Doutoku) in Japanese, through stories and self-check pages, for life at home and at school, not only for the classroom.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: polishing myself starts with self-control, hope and courage, and acting on my own responsibility.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
