# Polish Myself — Moderation and Courage — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Polishing myself starts with self-control, hope and courage, and acting on my own responsibility.

**2.** What does the part "Hope, courage, responsibility" teach?
**Answer:** Hold hope and courage without giving up, and act autonomously and responsibly — your choices are yours, and so are their results.
