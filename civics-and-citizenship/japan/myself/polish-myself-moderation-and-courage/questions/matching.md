# Polish Myself — Moderation and Courage — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** Moderation and self-control — a)

**2.** Hope, courage, responsibility — b)


Meanings:
a) Keep moderation and self-restraint in daily life — wanting, eating, playing and speaking all have their right measure.
b) Hold hope and courage without giving up, and act autonomously and responsibly — your choices are yours, and so are their results.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
