# Hands-on — Think Well and Live with Moderation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Choose one thing you have decided to do this week. Say how you will finish it, and name one good point of yours you will grow while you do it.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: raising myself means thinking well, finishing what I decide, doing right with courage, staying honest, and growing my good points. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
