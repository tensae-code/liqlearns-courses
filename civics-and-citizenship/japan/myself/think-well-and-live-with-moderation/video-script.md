# Video script — Think Well and Live with Moderation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Raising myself means thinking well, finishing what I decide, doing right with courage, staying honest, and growing my good points.

Think, finish, be brave. Think well and live a moderate life; finish what you decide to do, to the very end; and do the right thing with courage, even when it is hard.

Honest, bright, growing. Be honest with a bright heart, and grow your good points — notice what you do well and make it stronger.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
