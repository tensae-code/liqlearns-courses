# Think Well and Live with Moderation

## Do it first

Choose one thing you have decided to do this week. Say how you will finish it, and name one good point of yours you will grow while you do it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Think, finish, be brave

Think well and live a moderate life; finish what you decide to do, to the very end; and do the right thing with courage, even when it is hard.

## Sub lesson 2 — Honest, bright, growing

Be honest with a bright heart, and grow your good points — notice what you do well and make it stronger.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: raising myself means thinking well, finishing what I decide, doing right with courage, staying honest, and growing my good points. Give one real example from your own life in Japan, and name the lesson's key words: moderation, persistence, courage, honesty. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Japanese as well as in English — Japan's own books teach Moral Education (道徳, Doutoku) in Japanese, through stories and self-check pages, for life at home and at school, not only for the classroom.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: raising myself means thinking well, finishing what I decide, doing right with courage, staying honest, and growing my good points.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
