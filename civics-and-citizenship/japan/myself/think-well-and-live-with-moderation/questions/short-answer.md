# Think Well and Live with Moderation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Raising myself means thinking well, finishing what I decide, doing right with courage, staying honest, and growing my good points.

**2.** What does the part "Honest, bright, growing" teach?
**Answer:** Be honest with a bright heart, and grow your good points — notice what you do well and make it stronger.
