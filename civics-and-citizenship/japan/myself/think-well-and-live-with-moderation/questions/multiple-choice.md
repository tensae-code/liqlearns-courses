# Think Well and Live with Moderation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) raising myself means thinking well, finishing what I decide, doing right with courage, staying honest, and growing my good points  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — Think, finish, be brave?
a) Think well and live a moderate life; finish what you decide to do, to the very end; and do the right thing with courage, even when it is hard.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
