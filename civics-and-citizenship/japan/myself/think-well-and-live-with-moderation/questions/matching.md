# Think Well and Live with Moderation — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** Think, finish, be brave — a)

**2.** Honest, bright, growing — b)


Meanings:
a) Think well and live a moderate life; finish what you decide to do, to the very end; and do the right thing with courage, even when it is hard.
b) Be honest with a bright heart, and grow your good points — notice what you do well and make it stronger.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
