# Video script — Tell Us About Yourself

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
This lesson is where you introduce yourself — your favourite things, what you are good at, what you want to learn, your treasure and your dream.

Say who I am. Introduce yourself the book's way: your favourite food and play, what you are good at, and what you want to learn.

My treasure and my dream. Name your treasure and your dream, and say the person you are becoming as you grow from Grade 1 to Grade 2.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
