# Hands-on — Tell Us About Yourself

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Say your name, then tell a partner four things about yourself: a favourite food, a favourite game, one thing you are good at, and one thing you want to learn.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: this lesson is where you introduce yourself — your favourite things, what you are good at, what you want to learn, your treasure and your dream. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
