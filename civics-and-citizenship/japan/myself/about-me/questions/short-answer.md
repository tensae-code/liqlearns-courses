# Tell Us About Yourself — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** This lesson is where you introduce yourself — your favourite things, what you are good at, what you want to learn, your treasure and your dream.

**2.** What does the part "My treasure and my dream" teach?
**Answer:** Name your treasure and your dream, and say the person you are becoming as you grow from Grade 1 to Grade 2.
