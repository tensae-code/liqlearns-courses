# Tell Us About Yourself

## Do it first

Say your name, then tell a partner four things about yourself: a favourite food, a favourite game, one thing you are good at, and one thing you want to learn.

## Sub lesson 1 — Say who I am

Introduce yourself the book's way: your favourite food and play, what you are good at, and what you want to learn.

## Sub lesson 2 — My treasure and my dream

Name your treasure and your dream, and say the person you are becoming as you grow from Grade 1 to Grade 2.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: this lesson is where you introduce yourself — your favourite things, what you are good at, what you want to learn, your treasure and your dream. Give one real example from your own life in Japan, and name the lesson's key words: myself, introduce, favourite, dream. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Japanese as well as in English — Japan's own books teach Moral Education (道徳, Doutoku) in Japanese, through stories and self-check pages, for life at home and at school, not only for the classroom.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: this lesson is where you introduce yourself — your favourite things, what you are good at, what you want to learn, your treasure and your dream.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
