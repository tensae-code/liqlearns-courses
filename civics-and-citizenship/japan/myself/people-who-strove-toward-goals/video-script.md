# Video script — People Who Strove Toward Goals

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Goals are reached by effort piled on effort — an astronaut, an inventor and an actress each prove it.

Effort piled on effort. Mukai Chiaki the astronaut, Toyoda Sakichi the inventor-industrialist, and Mori Mitsuko the actress each reached their goal by piling effort on effort, over years.

Three lives, one lesson. Their fields differ — space, machines, the stage — but the way is the same: a clear goal, and effort repeated until it carries.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
