# Hands-on — People Who Strove Toward Goals

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Choose one of the three strivers. Say their goal and two efforts they repeated. Then name your own goal and the effort you will repeat this week.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: goals are reached by effort piled on effort — an astronaut, an inventor and an actress each prove it. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
