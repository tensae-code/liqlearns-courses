# People Who Strove Toward Goals — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Goals are reached by effort piled on effort — an astronaut, an inventor and an actress each prove it.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a child's own home, school or community in Japan.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Japan and lived there.
