# People Who Strove Toward Goals — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** Effort piled on effort — a)

**2.** Three lives, one lesson — b)


Meanings:
a) Mukai Chiaki the astronaut, Toyoda Sakichi the inventor-industrialist, and Mori Mitsuko the actress each reached their goal by piling effort on effort, over years.
b) Their fields differ — space, machines, the stage — but the way is the same: a clear goal, and effort repeated until it carries.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
