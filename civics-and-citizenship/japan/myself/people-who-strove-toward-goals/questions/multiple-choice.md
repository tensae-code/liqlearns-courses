# People Who Strove Toward Goals — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) goals are reached by effort piled on effort — an astronaut, an inventor and an actress each prove it  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — Effort piled on effort?
a) Mukai Chiaki the astronaut, Toyoda Sakichi the inventor-industrialist, and Mori Mitsuko the actress each reached their goal by piling effort on effort, over years.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
