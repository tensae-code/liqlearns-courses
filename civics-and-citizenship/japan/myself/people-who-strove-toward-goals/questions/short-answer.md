# People Who Strove Toward Goals — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Goals are reached by effort piled on effort — an astronaut, an inventor and an actress each prove it.

**2.** What does the part "Three lives, one lesson" teach?
**Answer:** Their fields differ — space, machines, the stage — but the way is the same: a clear goal, and effort repeated until it carries.
