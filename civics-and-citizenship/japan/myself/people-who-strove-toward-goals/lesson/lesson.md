# People Who Strove Toward Goals

## Do it first

Choose one of the three strivers. Say their goal and two efforts they repeated. Then name your own goal and the effort you will repeat this week.

## Sub lesson 1 — Effort piled on effort

Mukai Chiaki the astronaut, Toyoda Sakichi the inventor-industrialist, and Mori Mitsuko the actress each reached their goal by piling effort on effort, over years.

## Sub lesson 2 — Three lives, one lesson

Their fields differ — space, machines, the stage — but the way is the same: a clear goal, and effort repeated until it carries.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: goals are reached by effort piled on effort — an astronaut, an inventor and an actress each prove it. Give one real example from your own life in Japan, and name the lesson's key words: goals, effort, strive, examples. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Japanese as well as in English — Japan's own books teach Moral Education (道徳, Doutoku) in Japanese, through stories and self-check pages, for life at home and at school, not only for the classroom.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: goals are reached by effort piled on effort — an astronaut, an inventor and an actress each prove it.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
