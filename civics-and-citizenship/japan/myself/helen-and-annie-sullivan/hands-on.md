# Hands-on — Helen and Annie Sullivan

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Teach someone one small thing today — a word, a skill, a game — with real patience. Notice what your learner needed from you to succeed.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: a great teacher can open the whole world to one learner — Annie Sullivan did it for Helen Keller. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
