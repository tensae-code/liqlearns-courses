# Video script — Helen and Annie Sullivan

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A great teacher can open the whole world to one learner — Annie Sullivan did it for Helen Keller.

The teacher who stayed. Annie Sullivan came to teach Helen Keller, who could neither see nor hear, and stayed — opening language, and with it the world, to her pupil.

Learning breaks through. With patience and invention, teacher and learner broke through silence and darkness together — effort on both sides made it possible.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
