# Helen and Annie Sullivan — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** A great teacher can open the whole world to one learner — Annie Sullivan did it for Helen Keller.

**2.** What does the part "Learning breaks through" teach?
**Answer:** With patience and invention, teacher and learner broke through silence and darkness together — effort on both sides made it possible.
