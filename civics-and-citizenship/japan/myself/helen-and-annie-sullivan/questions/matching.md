# Helen and Annie Sullivan — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** The teacher who stayed — a)

**2.** Learning breaks through — b)


Meanings:
a) Annie Sullivan came to teach Helen Keller, who could neither see nor hear, and stayed — opening language, and with it the world, to her pupil.
b) With patience and invention, teacher and learner broke through silence and darkness together — effort on both sides made it possible.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
