# Helen and Annie Sullivan — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) a great teacher can open the whole world to one learner — Annie Sullivan did it for Helen Keller  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — The teacher who stayed?
a) Annie Sullivan came to teach Helen Keller, who could neither see nor hear, and stayed — opening language, and with it the world, to her pupil.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
