# I Can Do It — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** Small goals, one step at a time — a)

**2.** From crying child to the top — b)


Meanings:
a) Takahashi Naoko set small daily goals and climbed them one step at a time, like a long staircase — that is how 'I can surely do it' becomes true.
b) Naoko began as a child who cried when she ran; step by step, the small goals carried her to the top of her sport.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
