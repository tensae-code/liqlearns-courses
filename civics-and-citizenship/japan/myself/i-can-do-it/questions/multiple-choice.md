# I Can Do It — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) big goals are climbed like a long staircase — one small daily goal at a time  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — Small goals, one step at a time?
a) Takahashi Naoko set small daily goals and climbed them one step at a time, like a long staircase — that is how 'I can surely do it' becomes true.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
