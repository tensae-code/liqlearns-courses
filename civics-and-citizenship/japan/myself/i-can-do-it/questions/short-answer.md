# I Can Do It — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Big goals are climbed like a long staircase — one small daily goal at a time.

**2.** What does the part "From crying child to the top" teach?
**Answer:** Naoko began as a child who cried when she ran; step by step, the small goals carried her to the top of her sport.
