# Video script — I Can Do It

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Big goals are climbed like a long staircase — one small daily goal at a time.

Small goals, one step at a time. Takahashi Naoko set small daily goals and climbed them one step at a time, like a long staircase — that is how 'I can surely do it' becomes true.

From crying child to the top. Naoko began as a child who cried when she ran; step by step, the small goals carried her to the top of her sport.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
