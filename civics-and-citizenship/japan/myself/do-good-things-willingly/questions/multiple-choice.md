# Do Good Things Willingly — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) when you think a thing is good, do it willingly — however small it is  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — Good things, however small?
a) Do the good thing you can see — pick up litter, leave the wall clean, offer your seat to an older person on the train — willingly, not because you were told.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
