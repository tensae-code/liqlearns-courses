# Do Good Things Willingly — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** Good things, however small — a)

**2.** Say no clearly, like Ponta — b)


Meanings:
a) Do the good thing you can see — pick up litter, leave the wall clean, offer your seat to an older person on the train — willingly, not because you were told.
b) Ponta refuses to follow his friend into the forbidden, dangerous hill and says so clearly — doing good also means refusing what is wrong, out loud.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
