# Do Good Things Willingly — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** When you think a thing is good, do it willingly — however small it is.

**2.** What does the part "Say no clearly, like Ponta" teach?
**Answer:** Ponta refuses to follow his friend into the forbidden, dangerous hill and says so clearly — doing good also means refusing what is wrong, out loud.
