# Hands-on — Do Good Things Willingly

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Think of one small good thing you can do today — at home, at school or on the road. Do it without being asked, then say why it was good.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: when you think a thing is good, do it willingly — however small it is. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
