# Video script — Do Good Things Willingly

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
When you think a thing is good, do it willingly — however small it is.

Good things, however small. Do the good thing you can see — pick up litter, leave the wall clean, offer your seat to an older person on the train — willingly, not because you were told.

Say no clearly, like Ponta. Ponta refuses to follow his friend into the forbidden, dangerous hill and says so clearly — doing good also means refusing what is wrong, out loud.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
