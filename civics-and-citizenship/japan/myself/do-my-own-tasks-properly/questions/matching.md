# Do My Own Tasks Properly — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** My tasks at school and home — a)

**2.** Small efforts pile up — b)


Meanings:
a) Do your own jobs properly — at school and at home, like the child whose own job is cleaning the bath, done every day without being told.
b) The Tortoise beats the Hare by never stopping, and Ninomiya Kinjiro's small daily efforts piled up into a whole life — steady effort wins.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
