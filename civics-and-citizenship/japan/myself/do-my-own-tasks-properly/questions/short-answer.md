# Do My Own Tasks Properly — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** The tasks that are yours to do, you do yourself — properly, and to the end.

**2.** What does the part "Small efforts pile up" teach?
**Answer:** The Tortoise beats the Hare by never stopping, and Ninomiya Kinjiro's small daily efforts piled up into a whole life — steady effort wins.
