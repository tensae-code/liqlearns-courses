# Do My Own Tasks Properly — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) the tasks that are yours to do, you do yourself — properly, and to the end  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — My tasks at school and home?
a) Do your own jobs properly — at school and at home, like the child whose own job is cleaning the bath, done every day without being told.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
