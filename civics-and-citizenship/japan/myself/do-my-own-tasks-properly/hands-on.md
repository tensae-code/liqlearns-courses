# Hands-on — Do My Own Tasks Properly

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name one job that is yours at home and one at school. Do the home one today without being asked, and check: did you finish it properly, to the end?

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the tasks that are yours to do, you do yourself — properly, and to the end. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
