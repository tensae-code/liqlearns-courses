# Video script — Do My Own Tasks Properly

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The tasks that are yours to do, you do yourself — properly, and to the end.

My tasks at school and home. Do your own jobs properly — at school and at home, like the child whose own job is cleaning the bath, done every day without being told.

Small efforts pile up. The Tortoise beats the Hare by never stopping, and Ninomiya Kinjiro's small daily efforts piled up into a whole life — steady effort wins.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
