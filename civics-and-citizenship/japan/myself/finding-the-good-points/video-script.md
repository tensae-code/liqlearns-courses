# Video script — Finding the Good Points

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Find the good points — yours and your friends': everyone has them, and naming them grows them.

My good points, my friends' good points. Everyone has splendid good points — you, and every one of your friends. Find them: the friend who is kind to everyone, the one who reads so many books, the one whose legs are fast. Teach each other your good points and write them in this book, in Grade 1 and in Grade 2.

Machiko, who loved manga. Hasegawa Machiko drew pictures from when she was small and was good at likenesses. She thought up Sazae-san, and kept drawing the manga everyone loves — through hard times too — because drawing was what she loved and was good at.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
