# Finding the Good Points — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** My good points, my friends' good points — a)

**2.** Machiko, who loved manga — b)


Meanings:
a) Everyone has splendid good points — you, and every one of your friends. Find them: the friend who is kind to everyone, the one who reads so many books, the one whose legs are fast. Teach each other your good points and write them in this book, in Grade 1 and in Grade 2.
b) Hasegawa Machiko drew pictures from when she was small and was good at likenesses. She thought up Sazae-san, and kept drawing the manga everyone loves — through hard times too — because drawing was what she loved and was good at.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
