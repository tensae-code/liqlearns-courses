# Finding the Good Points — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Find the good points — yours and your friends': everyone has them, and naming them grows them.

**2.** What does the part "Machiko, who loved manga" teach?
**Answer:** Hasegawa Machiko drew pictures from when she was small and was good at likenesses. She thought up Sazae-san, and kept drawing the manga everyone loves — through hard times too — because drawing was what she loved and was good at.
