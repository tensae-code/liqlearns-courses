# Finding the Good Points — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) find the good points — yours and your friends': everyone has them, and naming them grows them  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — My good points, my friends' good points?
a) Everyone has splendid good points — you, and every one of your friends. Find them: the friend who is kind to everyone, the one who reads so many books, the one whose legs are fast. Teach each other your good points and write them in this book, in Grade 1 and in Grade 2.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
