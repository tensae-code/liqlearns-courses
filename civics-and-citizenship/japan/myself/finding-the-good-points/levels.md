## Sub lesson 1 — My good points, my friends' good points
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Everyone has splendid good points — you, and every one of your friends. Find them: the friend who is kind to everyone, the one who reads so many books, the one whose legs are fast. Teach each other your good points and write them in this book, in Grade 1 and in Grade 2. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
