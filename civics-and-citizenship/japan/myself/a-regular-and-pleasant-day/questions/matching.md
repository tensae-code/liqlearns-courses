# A Regular and Pleasant Day — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** Tidy things, tidy day — a)

**2.** A good daily rhythm — b)


Meanings:
a) Keep your desk, locker and things tidy at school and at home, and check yourself on a chart — Ruppe, who cannot get up and scatters his schoolbag, shows what an untidy start costs.
b) Set your day's rhythm: rise early, eat breakfast, and sleep enough, so each day starts pleasant and in order.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
