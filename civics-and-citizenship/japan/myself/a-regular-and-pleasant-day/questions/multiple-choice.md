# A Regular and Pleasant Day — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) a regular, pleasant day is built by small habits — tidying your things and keeping a good daily rhythm  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — Tidy things, tidy day?
a) Keep your desk, locker and things tidy at school and at home, and check yourself on a chart — Ruppe, who cannot get up and scatters his schoolbag, shows what an untidy start costs.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
