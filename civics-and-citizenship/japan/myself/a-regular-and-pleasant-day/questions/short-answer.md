# A Regular and Pleasant Day — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** A regular, pleasant day is built by small habits — tidying your things and keeping a good daily rhythm.

**2.** What does the part "A good daily rhythm" teach?
**Answer:** Set your day's rhythm: rise early, eat breakfast, and sleep enough, so each day starts pleasant and in order.
