# Video script — A Regular and Pleasant Day

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A regular, pleasant day is built by small habits — tidying your things and keeping a good daily rhythm.

Tidy things, tidy day. Keep your desk, locker and things tidy at school and at home, and check yourself on a chart — Ruppe, who cannot get up and scatters his schoolbag, shows what an untidy start costs.

A good daily rhythm. Set your day's rhythm: rise early, eat breakfast, and sleep enough, so each day starts pleasant and in order.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
