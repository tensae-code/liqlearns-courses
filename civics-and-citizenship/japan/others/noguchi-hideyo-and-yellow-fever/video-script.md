# Video script — Noguchi Hideyo and Yellow Fever

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Noguchi Hideyo fought disease for the whole world — and the fight itself took his life.

The scientist who went out. Noguchi Hideyo, the world-famous medical scientist from Japan, travelled where yellow fever was, to fight it for people he would never meet.

Struck down in the fight. In the year illness struck him down, his work stood: a life spent against disease is a life spent for others.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
