# Noguchi Hideyo and Yellow Fever — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Noguchi Hideyo fought disease for the whole world — and the fight itself took his life.

**2.** What does the part "Struck down in the fight" teach?
**Answer:** In the year illness struck him down, his work stood: a life spent against disease is a life spent for others.
