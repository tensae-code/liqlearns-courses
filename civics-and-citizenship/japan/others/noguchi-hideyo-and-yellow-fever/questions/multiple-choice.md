# Noguchi Hideyo and Yellow Fever — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) Noguchi Hideyo fought disease for the whole world — and the fight itself took his life  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — The scientist who went out?
a) Noguchi Hideyo, the world-famous medical scientist from Japan, travelled where yellow fever was, to fight it for people he would never meet.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
