# Noguchi Hideyo and Yellow Fever — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** The scientist who went out — a)

**2.** Struck down in the fight — b)


Meanings:
a) Noguchi Hideyo, the world-famous medical scientist from Japan, travelled where yellow fever was, to fight it for people he would never meet.
b) In the year illness struck him down, his work stood: a life spent against disease is a life spent for others.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
