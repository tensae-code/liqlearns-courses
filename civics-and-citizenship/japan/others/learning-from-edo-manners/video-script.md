# Video script — Learning from Edo Manners

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The manners of Edo kept a crowded city comfortable for 300 years — make room for others, in body and in mind.

Manners for living together. Edo manners are the wisdom of a long peace for living comfortably together — small courtesies that keep shared life smooth.

Room on the road, room to sit. Pass carefully on a narrow road, and make room for others to sit — the same heart in both: leave space for the other person.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
