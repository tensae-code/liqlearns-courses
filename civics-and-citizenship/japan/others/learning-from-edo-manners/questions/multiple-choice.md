# Learning from Edo Manners — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) the manners of Edo kept a crowded city comfortable for 300 years — make room for others, in body and in mind  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — Manners for living together?
a) Edo manners are the wisdom of a long peace for living comfortably together — small courtesies that keep shared life smooth.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
