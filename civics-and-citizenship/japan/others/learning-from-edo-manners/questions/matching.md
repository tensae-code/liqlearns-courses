# Learning from Edo Manners — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** Manners for living together — a)

**2.** Room on the road, room to sit — b)


Meanings:
a) Edo manners are the wisdom of a long peace for living comfortably together — small courtesies that keep shared life smooth.
b) Pass carefully on a narrow road, and make room for others to sit — the same heart in both: leave space for the other person.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
