# Learning from Edo Manners — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** The manners of Edo kept a crowded city comfortable for 300 years — make room for others, in body and in mind.

**2.** What does the part "Room on the road, room to sit" teach?
**Answer:** Pass carefully on a narrow road, and make room for others to sit — the same heart in both: leave space for the other person.
