# Hands-on — Learning from Edo Manners

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Walk one narrow path or corridor today the Edo way: slow down, make room, let the other pass first. Then make room for someone to sit beside you.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the manners of Edo kept a crowded city comfortable for 300 years — make room for others, in body and in mind. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
