# Video script — The Meaning of an Act

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The heart cannot be seen, but consideration can — because consideration is an act done for another.

Close to another's feelings. Draw close to another's feelings: try to stand where they stand and feel what the moment is like for them.

Consideration is action. Miyazawa Shoji's poem teaches that the heart is invisible, but consideration (kokorozukai) is visible — it is action toward another person.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
