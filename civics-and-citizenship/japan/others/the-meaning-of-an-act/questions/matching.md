# The Meaning of an Act — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** Close to another's feelings — a)

**2.** Consideration is action — b)


Meanings:
a) Draw close to another's feelings: try to stand where they stand and feel what the moment is like for them.
b) Miyazawa Shoji's poem teaches that the heart is invisible, but consideration (kokorozukai) is visible — it is action toward another person.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
