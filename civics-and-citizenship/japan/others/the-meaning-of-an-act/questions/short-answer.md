# The Meaning of an Act — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** The heart cannot be seen, but consideration can — because consideration is an act done for another.

**2.** What does the part "Consideration is action" teach?
**Answer:** Miyazawa Shoji's poem teaches that the heart is invisible, but consideration (kokorozukai) is visible — it is action toward another person.
