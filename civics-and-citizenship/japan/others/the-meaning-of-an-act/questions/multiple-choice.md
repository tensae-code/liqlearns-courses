# The Meaning of an Act — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) the heart cannot be seen, but consideration can — because consideration is an act done for another  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — Close to another's feelings?
a) Draw close to another's feelings: try to stand where they stand and feel what the moment is like for them.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
