# Pleasant Behaviour and Greetings — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Greetings that connect hearts
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The day is full of greetings — good morning, hello, thank you, from teachers and from family. When you greet, your heart and the other person's heart connect; grow your own greeting tree with the greetings you want to be able to say. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — The island with no greetings
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Keita visits an island of monkeys where nobody greets anyone, and finds it silent and strange. Day by day his own cheerful greetings spread, until the whole island rings with them — and the world's countries each greet in their own way. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
