# Video script — Pleasant Behaviour and Greetings

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Pleasant behaviour begins with greetings — greet the people of your day warmly, and hearts connect.

Greetings that connect hearts. The day is full of greetings — good morning, hello, thank you, from teachers and from family. When you greet, your heart and the other person's heart connect; grow your own greeting tree with the greetings you want to be able to say.

The island with no greetings. Keita visits an island of monkeys where nobody greets anyone, and finds it silent and strange. Day by day his own cheerful greetings spread, until the whole island rings with them — and the world's countries each greet in their own way.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
