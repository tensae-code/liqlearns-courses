# Pleasant Behaviour and Greetings — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** Greetings that connect hearts — a)

**2.** The island with no greetings — b)


Meanings:
a) The day is full of greetings — good morning, hello, thank you, from teachers and from family. When you greet, your heart and the other person's heart connect; grow your own greeting tree with the greetings you want to be able to say.
b) Keita visits an island of monkeys where nobody greets anyone, and finds it silent and strange. Day by day his own cheerful greetings spread, until the whole island rings with them — and the world's countries each greet in their own way.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
