# Pleasant Behaviour and Greetings — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Pleasant behaviour begins with greetings — greet the people of your day warmly, and hearts connect.

**2.** What does the part "The island with no greetings" teach?
**Answer:** Keita visits an island of monkeys where nobody greets anyone, and finds it silent and strange. Day by day his own cheerful greetings spread, until the whole island rings with them — and the world's countries each greet in their own way.
