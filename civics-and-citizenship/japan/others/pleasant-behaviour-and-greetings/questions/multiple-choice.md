# Pleasant Behaviour and Greetings — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) pleasant behaviour begins with greetings — greet the people of your day warmly, and hearts connect  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — Greetings that connect hearts?
a) The day is full of greetings — good morning, hello, thank you, from teachers and from family. When you greet, your heart and the other person's heart connect; grow your own greeting tree with the greetings you want to be able to say.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
