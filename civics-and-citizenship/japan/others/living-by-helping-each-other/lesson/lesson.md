# Living by Helping Each Other

## Do it first

Think of one way someone helped your family, and one way your family helped someone else. Say both aloud, and thank one helper this week.

## Sub lesson 1 — The guide-dog family

Mikita's blind parents live with their guide dog Berna — a family that overcomes much, together, to live happily.

## Sub lesson 2 — Help given, help received

Helping is mutual: the dog guides, the family cares, neighbours support — everyone both gives and receives.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: we live by helping one another — a family, a dog and a community can carry each other through hard things. Give one real example from your own life in Japan, and name the lesson's key words: helping, mutual, guide dog, family. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Japanese as well as in English — Japan's own books teach Moral Education (道徳, Doutoku) in Japanese, through stories and self-check pages, for life at home and at school, not only for the classroom.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: we live by helping one another — a family, a dog and a community can carry each other through hard things.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
