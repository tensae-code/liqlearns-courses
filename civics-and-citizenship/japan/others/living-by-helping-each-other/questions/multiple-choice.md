# Living by Helping Each Other — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) we live by helping one another — a family, a dog and a community can carry each other through hard things  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — The guide-dog family?
a) Mikita's blind parents live with their guide dog Berna — a family that overcomes much, together, to live happily.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
