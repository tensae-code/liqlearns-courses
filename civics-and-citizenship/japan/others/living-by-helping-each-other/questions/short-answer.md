# Living by Helping Each Other — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** We live by helping one another — a family, a dog and a community can carry each other through hard things.

**2.** What does the part "Help given, help received" teach?
**Answer:** Helping is mutual: the dog guides, the family cares, neighbours support — everyone both gives and receives.
