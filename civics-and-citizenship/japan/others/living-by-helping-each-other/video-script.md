# Video script — Living by Helping Each Other

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
We live by helping one another — a family, a dog and a community can carry each other through hard things.

The guide-dog family. Mikita's blind parents live with their guide dog Berna — a family that overcomes much, together, to live happily.

Help given, help received. Helping is mutual: the dog guides, the family cares, neighbours support — everyone both gives and receives.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
