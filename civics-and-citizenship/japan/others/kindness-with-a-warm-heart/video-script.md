# Video script — Kindness with a Warm Heart

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Be kind with a warm heart — think what you can do for the people around you, and use your time for others too.

Your life is your time. Dr Hino'ohara Shigeaki, a doctor still working past one hundred, teaches that your life is the time you can use as your own. Do not spend it only on yourself: use it for other people too — that is truly living, by helping each other.

The wolf on the bridge. The wolf bullies the rabbit and the fox off his narrow bridge, until the bear simply lifts him up and sets him gently behind. The wolf tries it himself — and finds that letting others pass, kindly, feels far better than bullying ever did.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
