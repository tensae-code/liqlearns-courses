# Kindness with a Warm Heart

## Do it first

Do one kind thing today for someone around you — an older person, a younger child, a friend — chosen by you, before anyone asks.

## Sub lesson 1 — Your life is your time

Dr Hino'ohara Shigeaki, a doctor still working past one hundred, teaches that your life is the time you can use as your own. Do not spend it only on yourself: use it for other people too — that is truly living, by helping each other.

## Sub lesson 2 — The wolf on the bridge

The wolf bullies the rabbit and the fox off his narrow bridge, until the bear simply lifts him up and sets him gently behind. The wolf tries it himself — and finds that letting others pass, kindly, feels far better than bullying ever did.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: be kind with a warm heart — think what you can do for the people around you, and use your time for others too. Give one real example from your own life in Japan, and name the lesson's key words: kindness, warm heart, helping, bridge. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Japanese as well as in English — Japan's own books teach Moral Education (道徳, Doutoku) in Japanese, through stories and self-check pages, for life at home and at school, not only for the classroom.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: be kind with a warm heart — think what you can do for the people around you, and use your time for others too.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
