# Kindness with a Warm Heart — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** Your life is your time — a)

**2.** The wolf on the bridge — b)


Meanings:
a) Dr Hino'ohara Shigeaki, a doctor still working past one hundred, teaches that your life is the time you can use as your own. Do not spend it only on yourself: use it for other people too — that is truly living, by helping each other.
b) The wolf bullies the rabbit and the fox off his narrow bridge, until the bear simply lifts him up and sets him gently behind. The wolf tries it himself — and finds that letting others pass, kindly, feels far better than bullying ever did.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
