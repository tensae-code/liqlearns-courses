# Kindness with a Warm Heart — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Be kind with a warm heart — think what you can do for the people around you, and use your time for others too.

**2.** What does the part "The wolf on the bridge" teach?
**Answer:** The wolf bullies the rabbit and the fox off his narrow bridge, until the bear simply lifts him up and sets him gently behind. The wolf tries it himself — and finds that letting others pass, kindly, feels far better than bullying ever did.
