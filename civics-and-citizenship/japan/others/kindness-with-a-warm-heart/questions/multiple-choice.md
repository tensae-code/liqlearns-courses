# Kindness with a Warm Heart — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) be kind with a warm heart — think what you can do for the people around you, and use your time for others too  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — Your life is your time?
a) Dr Hino'ohara Shigeaki, a doctor still working past one hundred, teaches that your life is the time you can use as your own. Do not spend it only on yourself: use it for other people too — that is truly living, by helping each other.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
