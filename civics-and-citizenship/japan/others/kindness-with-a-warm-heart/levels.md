## Sub lesson 1 — Your life is your time
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Dr Hino'ohara Shigeaki, a doctor still working past one hundred, teaches that your life is the time you can use as your own. Do not spend it only on yourself: use it for other people too — that is truly living, by helping each other. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — The wolf on the bridge
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The wolf bullies the rabbit and the fox off his narrow bridge, until the bear simply lifts him up and sets him gently behind. The wolf tries it himself — and finds that letting others pass, kindly, feels far better than bullying ever did. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
