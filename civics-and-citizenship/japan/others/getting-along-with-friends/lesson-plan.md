## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **A friend halves sorrow and doubles joy** — The friendship poem teaches that a friend is someone who halves your sorrow and doubles your joy — Poppo the dove made friends through talking and through letters, and a friend beside you makes you want to try hard.
2. **The squirrel who couldn't swim** — The animals tell Squirrel he cannot come to the island because he cannot swim, and the island's games feel flat without him. The turtle carries him over on his back, the others say sorry — and everyone agrees the day is better with Squirrel there.

Gate pool: the prove-it check for each sub lesson is at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson — drawn from this lesson's question files (questions/).
