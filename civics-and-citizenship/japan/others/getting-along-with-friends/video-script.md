# Video script — Getting Along with Friends

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Get along with friends — share their joys and sorrows, and find a way for everyone to be included.

A friend halves sorrow and doubles joy. The friendship poem teaches that a friend is someone who halves your sorrow and doubles your joy — Poppo the dove made friends through talking and through letters, and a friend beside you makes you want to try hard.

The squirrel who couldn't swim. The animals tell Squirrel he cannot come to the island because he cannot swim, and the island's games feel flat without him. The turtle carries him over on his back, the others say sorry — and everyone agrees the day is better with Squirrel there.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
