# Getting Along with Friends — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Get along with friends — share their joys and sorrows, and find a way for everyone to be included.

**2.** What does the part "The squirrel who couldn't swim" teach?
**Answer:** The animals tell Squirrel he cannot come to the island because he cannot swim, and the island's games feel flat without him. The turtle carries him over on his back, the others say sorry — and everyone agrees the day is better with Squirrel there.
