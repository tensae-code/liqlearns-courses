# Getting Along with Friends — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) get along with friends — share their joys and sorrows, and find a way for everyone to be included  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — A friend halves sorrow and doubles joy?
a) The friendship poem teaches that a friend is someone who halves your sorrow and doubles your joy — Poppo the dove made friends through talking and through letters, and a friend beside you makes you want to try hard.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
