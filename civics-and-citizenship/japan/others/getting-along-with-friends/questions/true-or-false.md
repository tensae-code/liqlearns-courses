# Getting Along with Friends — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Get along with friends — share their joys and sorrows, and find a way for everyone to be included.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a child's own home, school or community in Japan.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from life in Japan and lived there.
