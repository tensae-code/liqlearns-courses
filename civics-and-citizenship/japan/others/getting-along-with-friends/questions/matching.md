# Getting Along with Friends — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** A friend halves sorrow and doubles joy — a)

**2.** The squirrel who couldn't swim — b)


Meanings:
a) The friendship poem teaches that a friend is someone who halves your sorrow and doubles your joy — Poppo the dove made friends through talking and through letters, and a friend beside you makes you want to try hard.
b) The animals tell Squirrel he cannot come to the island because he cannot swim, and the island's games feel flat without him. The turtle carries him over on his back, the others say sorry — and everyone agrees the day is better with Squirrel there.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
