# Sincerity Toward Everyone

## Do it first

Greet one person today whom you do not usually talk to. Speak sincerely — look at them, listen to their answer, and respond to what they actually said.

## Sub lesson 1 — A true heart for all

Sincerity (magokoro) is owed to everyone — family, friends, classmates and strangers alike; relating to others begins here.

## Sub lesson 2 — Sincerity you can see

A sincere heart shows in acts: listening properly, answering honestly, and treating each person as someone who matters.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: treat everyone with sincerity — a true heart toward every person, not only your friends. Give one real example from your own life in Japan, and name the lesson's key words: sincerity, magokoro, everyone, relating. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Japanese as well as in English — Japan's own books teach Moral Education (道徳, Doutoku) in Japanese, through stories and self-check pages, for life at home and at school, not only for the classroom.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: treat everyone with sincerity — a true heart toward every person, not only your friends.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
