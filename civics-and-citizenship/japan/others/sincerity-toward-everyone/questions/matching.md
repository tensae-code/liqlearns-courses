# Sincerity Toward Everyone — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** A true heart for all — a)

**2.** Sincerity you can see — b)


Meanings:
a) Sincerity (magokoro) is owed to everyone — family, friends, classmates and strangers alike; relating to others begins here.
b) A sincere heart shows in acts: listening properly, answering honestly, and treating each person as someone who matters.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
