# Sincerity Toward Everyone — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) treat everyone with sincerity — a true heart toward every person, not only your friends  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — A true heart for all?
a) Sincerity (magokoro) is owed to everyone — family, friends, classmates and strangers alike; relating to others begins here.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
