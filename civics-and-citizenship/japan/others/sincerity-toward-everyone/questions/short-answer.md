# Sincerity Toward Everyone — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Treat everyone with sincerity — a true heart toward every person, not only your friends.

**2.** What does the part "Sincerity you can see" teach?
**Answer:** A sincere heart shows in acts: listening properly, answering honestly, and treating each person as someone who matters.
