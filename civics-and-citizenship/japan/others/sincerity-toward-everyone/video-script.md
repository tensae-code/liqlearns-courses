# Video script — Sincerity Toward Everyone

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Treat everyone with sincerity — a true heart toward every person, not only your friends.

A true heart for all. Sincerity (magokoro) is owed to everyone — family, friends, classmates and strangers alike; relating to others begins here.

Sincerity you can see. A sincere heart shows in acts: listening properly, answering honestly, and treating each person as someone who matters.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
