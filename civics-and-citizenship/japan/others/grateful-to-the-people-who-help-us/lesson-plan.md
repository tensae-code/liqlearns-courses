# Lesson plan — Grateful to the People Who Help Us

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Japan · **Title:** others · **Lesson:** Grateful to the People Who Help Us
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — Myself, People, Life and Everyone (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** be grateful to the people who help us — notice them, thank them, and say it from the heart.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — Myself, People, Life and Everyone — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **So many thank-yous** — Many people look after us every day: the sixth-graders, grandmother, the person who cooks the school lunch, the crossing guard who keeps the walk to school safe. There are so many thank-yous to say.
2. **A thank-you card from the heart** — Make a thank-you card the book's way: write the name of the person, write your thanks from your heart, and write your own name and the date — a small card that carries a real feeling.

Gate pool: the prove-it check for each sub lesson is at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson — drawn from this lesson's question files (questions/).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: be grateful to the people who help us — notice them, thank them, and say it from the heart.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street, school or community in Japan.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the learner's own life in Japan and the book's own teaching, as Japan's Moral Education books work through short illustrated stories, songs and self-check pages.
