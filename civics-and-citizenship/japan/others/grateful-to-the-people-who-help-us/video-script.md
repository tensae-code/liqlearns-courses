# Video script — Grateful to the People Who Help Us

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Be grateful to the people who help us — notice them, thank them, and say it from the heart.

So many thank-yous. Many people look after us every day: the sixth-graders, grandmother, the person who cooks the school lunch, the crossing guard who keeps the walk to school safe. There are so many thank-yous to say.

A thank-you card from the heart. Make a thank-you card the book's way: write the name of the person, write your thanks from your heart, and write your own name and the date — a small card that carries a real feeling.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
