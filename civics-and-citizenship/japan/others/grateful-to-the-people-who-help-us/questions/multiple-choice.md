# Grateful to the People Who Help Us — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) be grateful to the people who help us — notice them, thank them, and say it from the heart  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — So many thank-yous?
a) Many people look after us every day: the sixth-graders, grandmother, the person who cooks the school lunch, the crossing guard who keeps the walk to school safe. There are so many thank-yous to say.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
