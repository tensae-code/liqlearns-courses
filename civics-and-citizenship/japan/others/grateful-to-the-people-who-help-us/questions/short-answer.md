# Grateful to the People Who Help Us — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Be grateful to the people who help us — notice them, thank them, and say it from the heart.

**2.** What does the part "A thank-you card from the heart" teach?
**Answer:** Make a thank-you card the book's way: write the name of the person, write your thanks from your heart, and write your own name and the date — a small card that carries a real feeling.
