# Grateful to the People Who Help Us — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** So many thank-yous — a)

**2.** A thank-you card from the heart — b)


Meanings:
a) Many people look after us every day: the sixth-graders, grandmother, the person who cooks the school lunch, the crossing guard who keeps the walk to school safe. There are so many thank-yous to say.
b) Make a thank-you card the book's way: write the name of the person, write your thanks from your heart, and write your own name and the date — a small card that carries a real feeling.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
