# Getting Along with Everyone — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** What should I do? — a)

**2.** The rabbit who thought again — b)


Meanings:
a) Picture scenes ask the question: children queueing, a shared toy, a game someone wants to join, a snowman built together. Some acts help everyone get along and some must never be done — learn to tell them apart and choose.
b) Read the little story of the rabbit, told in the open book's own pages, and think it through: what happened, what should the rabbit have done, and what would you do?

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
