# Getting Along with Everyone — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Get along with everyone — judge each moment and choose the act that lets everyone be together gladly.

**2.** What does the part "The rabbit who thought again" teach?
**Answer:** Read the little story of the rabbit, told in the open book's own pages, and think it through: what happened, what should the rabbit have done, and what would you do?
