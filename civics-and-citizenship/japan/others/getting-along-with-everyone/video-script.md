# Video script — Getting Along with Everyone

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Get along with everyone — judge each moment and choose the act that lets everyone be together gladly.

What should I do?. Picture scenes ask the question: children queueing, a shared toy, a game someone wants to join, a snowman built together. Some acts help everyone get along and some must never be done — learn to tell them apart and choose.

The rabbit who thought again. Read the little story of the rabbit, told in the open book's own pages, and think it through: what happened, what should the rabbit have done, and what would you do?

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
