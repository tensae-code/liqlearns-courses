# Hands-on — Connect with Others

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Do one kind act today chosen from the other person's standpoint — what they need, not what suits you. Then thank someone whose support you usually take for granted.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: connecting with others takes courtesy, kindness from their standpoint, trust, humility and gratitude. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
