# Video script — Connect with Others

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Connecting with others takes courtesy, kindness from their standpoint, trust, humility and gratitude.

Courtesy, kindness, trust. Be courteous and sincere; be kind by standing in the other's place; trust each other and learn from each other.

Humility and gratitude. Be humble and large-hearted, and be grateful for the support you give each other — connection is kept by thanks.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
