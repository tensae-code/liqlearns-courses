# Connect with Others

## Do it first

Do one kind act today chosen from the other person's standpoint — what they need, not what suits you. Then thank someone whose support you usually take for granted.

## Sub lesson 1 — Courtesy, kindness, trust

Be courteous and sincere; be kind by standing in the other's place; trust each other and learn from each other.

## Sub lesson 2 — Humility and gratitude

Be humble and large-hearted, and be grateful for the support you give each other — connection is kept by thanks.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: connecting with others takes courtesy, kindness from their standpoint, trust, humility and gratitude. Give one real example from your own life in Japan, and name the lesson's key words: courtesy, kindness, trust, gratitude. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Japanese as well as in English — Japan's own books teach Moral Education (道徳, Doutoku) in Japanese, through stories and self-check pages, for life at home and at school, not only for the classroom.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: connecting with others takes courtesy, kindness from their standpoint, trust, humility and gratitude.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
