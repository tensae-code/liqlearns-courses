# Connect with Others — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** Courtesy, kindness, trust — a)

**2.** Humility and gratitude — b)


Meanings:
a) Be courteous and sincere; be kind by standing in the other's place; trust each other and learn from each other.
b) Be humble and large-hearted, and be grateful for the support you give each other — connection is kept by thanks.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
