# Connect with Others — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Connecting with others takes courtesy, kindness from their standpoint, trust, humility and gratitude.

**2.** What does the part "Humility and gratitude" teach?
**Answer:** Be humble and large-hearted, and be grateful for the support you give each other — connection is kept by thanks.
