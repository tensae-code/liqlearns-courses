# Feeling Close to My Hometown — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Feel close to your hometown — its festivals, people and places are treasures its people guard together.

**2.** What does the part "The Gion Festival" teach?
**Answer:** A Kyoto boy practises the festival music for a month before the Gion Festival. He is scolded, tells his father he wants to quit — and his father tells him everyone has practised through scoldings, guarding this festival for a thousand years. On the day, riding the great float, he is glad he kept going.
