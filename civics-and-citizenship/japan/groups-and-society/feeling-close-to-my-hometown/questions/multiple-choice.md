# Feeling Close to My Hometown — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) feel close to your hometown — its festivals, people and places are treasures its people guard together  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — My town newspaper?
a) Make a newspaper of your town: its festivals and events, the look of its people, your own recommended place, and the town you hope for in the future. What you grow up in is your town's everyone's joy.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
