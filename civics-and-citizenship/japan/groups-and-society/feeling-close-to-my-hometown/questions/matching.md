# Feeling Close to My Hometown — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** My town newspaper — a)

**2.** The Gion Festival — b)


Meanings:
a) Make a newspaper of your town: its festivals and events, the look of its people, your own recommended place, and the town you hope for in the future. What you grow up in is your town's everyone's joy.
b) A Kyoto boy practises the festival music for a month before the Gion Festival. He is scolded, tells his father he wants to quit — and his father tells him everyone has practised through scoldings, guarding this festival for a thousand years. On the day, riding the great float, he is glad he kept going.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
