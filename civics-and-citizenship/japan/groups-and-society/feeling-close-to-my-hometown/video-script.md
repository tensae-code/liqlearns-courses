# Video script — Feeling Close to My Hometown

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Feel close to your hometown — its festivals, people and places are treasures its people guard together.

My town newspaper. Make a newspaper of your town: its festivals and events, the look of its people, your own recommended place, and the town you hope for in the future. What you grow up in is your town's everyone's joy.

The Gion Festival. A Kyoto boy practises the festival music for a month before the Gion Festival. He is scolded, tells his father he wants to quit — and his father tells him everyone has practised through scoldings, guarding this festival for a thousand years. On the day, riding the great float, he is glad he kept going.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
