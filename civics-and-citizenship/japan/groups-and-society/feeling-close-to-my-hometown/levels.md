# Feeling Close to My Hometown — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — My town newspaper
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Make a newspaper of your town: its festivals and events, the look of its people, your own recommended place, and the town you hope for in the future. What you grow up in is your town's everyone's joy. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — The Gion Festival
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: A Kyoto boy practises the festival music for a month before the Gion Festival. He is scolded, tells his father he wants to quit — and his father tells him everyone has practised through scoldings, guarding this festival for a thousand years. On the day, riding the great float, he is glad he kept going. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
