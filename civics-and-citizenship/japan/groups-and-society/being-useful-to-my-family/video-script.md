# Video script — Being Useful to My Family

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Think how to be useful to your family — the people who treasure you most — and let helping warm your heart.

The people who treasure me most. Your family are the people who treasure you most. Read their messages to you — and remember the sick day when mother kept changing the cooling sheet on your forehead and grandmother kept coming to look in on you.

What I can do for my family. A boy makes tea for his father, tired from work, and rubs his shoulders; a girl plays in the garden with her little brother so her mother can cook. Their thanks and smiles warm the helper's own heart too.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
