# Being Useful to My Family — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** The people who treasure me most — a)

**2.** What I can do for my family — b)


Meanings:
a) Your family are the people who treasure you most. Read their messages to you — and remember the sick day when mother kept changing the cooling sheet on your forehead and grandmother kept coming to look in on you.
b) A boy makes tea for his father, tired from work, and rubs his shoulders; a girl plays in the garden with her little brother so her mother can cook. Their thanks and smiles warm the helper's own heart too.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
