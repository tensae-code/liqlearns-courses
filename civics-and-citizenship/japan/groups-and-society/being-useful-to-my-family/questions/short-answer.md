# Being Useful to My Family — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Think how to be useful to your family — the people who treasure you most — and let helping warm your heart.

**2.** What does the part "What I can do for my family" teach?
**Answer:** A boy makes tea for his father, tired from work, and rubs his shoulders; a girl plays in the garden with her little brother so her mother can cook. Their thanks and smiles warm the helper's own heart too.
