# Being Useful to My Family — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) think how to be useful to your family — the people who treasure you most — and let helping warm your heart  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — The people who treasure me most?
a) Your family are the people who treasure you most. Read their messages to you — and remember the sick day when mother kept changing the cooling sheet on your forehead and grandmother kept coming to look in on you.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
