# Learning About Other Countries — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) learn about other countries — their things are already in your life, their athletes strive beside yours, and their greetings differ from yours  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — The Olympics and Paralympics?
a) At the Olympics and Paralympics the athletes of many countries strive together — a letter written from the Tokyo Games of 1964, and photographs of London 2012. The whole world meets, competes and cheers in one place.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
