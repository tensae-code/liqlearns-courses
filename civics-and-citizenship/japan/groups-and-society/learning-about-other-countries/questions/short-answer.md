# Learning About Other Countries — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Learn about other countries — their things are already in your life, their athletes strive beside yours, and their greetings differ from yours.

**2.** What does the part "Things and greetings from other countries" teach?
**Answer:** Look around you: clothes, food, music and games that came from other countries are everywhere. And each country greets in its own way — hello, namaste — as the book's world-greetings pages showed. The book closes by writing down what you learned in Moral Education, in Grade 1 and in Grade 2.
