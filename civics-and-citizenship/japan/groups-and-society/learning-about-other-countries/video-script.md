# Video script — Learning About Other Countries

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Learn about other countries — their things are already in your life, their athletes strive beside yours, and their greetings differ from yours.

The Olympics and Paralympics. At the Olympics and Paralympics the athletes of many countries strive together — a letter written from the Tokyo Games of 1964, and photographs of London 2012. The whole world meets, competes and cheers in one place.

Things and greetings from other countries. Look around you: clothes, food, music and games that came from other countries are everywhere. And each country greets in its own way — hello, namaste — as the book's world-greetings pages showed. The book closes by writing down what you learned in Moral Education, in Grade 1 and in Grade 2.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
