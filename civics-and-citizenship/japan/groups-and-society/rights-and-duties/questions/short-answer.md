# Rights and Duties — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Living together in society, each of us holds rights and fulfils duties — the two stand together.

**2.** What does the part "What is a duty" teach?
**Answer:** A duty is what each of us must fulfil while living together in society — rights are kept safe when duties are done.
