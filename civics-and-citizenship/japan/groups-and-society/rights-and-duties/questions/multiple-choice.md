# Rights and Duties — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) living together in society, each of us holds rights and fulfils duties — the two stand together  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — What is a right?
a) A right is what each person may rightly claim and have respected — yours, and equally everyone else's.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
