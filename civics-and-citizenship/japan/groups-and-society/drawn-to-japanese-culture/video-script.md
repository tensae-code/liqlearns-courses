# Video script — Drawn to Japanese Culture

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A person from far away can truly see your culture's beauty — and teach you to see it too.

Yakumo comes to Japan. Koizumi Yakumo — Lafcadio Hearn — came from across the sea and was drawn to Japanese culture, writing it down for the world.

Seeing the trees truly. Yakumo wrote of why Japan's trees are so beautiful to one who truly sees them — loving a culture begins with really looking.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
