# Hands-on — Drawn to Japanese Culture

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Look at one tree or one familiar place near your home as if for the first time. Say three beautiful things about it that you had stopped noticing.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: a person from far away can truly see your culture's beauty — and teach you to see it too. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
