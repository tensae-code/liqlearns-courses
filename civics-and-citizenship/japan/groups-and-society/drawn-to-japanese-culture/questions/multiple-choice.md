# Drawn to Japanese Culture — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) a person from far away can truly see your culture's beauty — and teach you to see it too  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — Yakumo comes to Japan?
a) Koizumi Yakumo — Lafcadio Hearn — came from across the sea and was drawn to Japanese culture, writing it down for the world.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
