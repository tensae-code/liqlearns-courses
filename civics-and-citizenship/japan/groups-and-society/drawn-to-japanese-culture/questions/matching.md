# Drawn to Japanese Culture — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** Yakumo comes to Japan — a)

**2.** Seeing the trees truly — b)


Meanings:
a) Koizumi Yakumo — Lafcadio Hearn — came from across the sea and was drawn to Japanese culture, writing it down for the world.
b) Yakumo wrote of why Japan's trees are so beautiful to one who truly sees them — loving a culture begins with really looking.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
