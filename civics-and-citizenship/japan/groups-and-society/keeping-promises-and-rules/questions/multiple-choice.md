# Keeping Promises and Rules — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) keep promises and rules — they guard everyone's safety and pleasant days, and shared things belong to everyone  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — Why promises and rules exist?
a) Talk about what would happen if there were no rules at all. The town, the road and the park are full of things everyone uses and no one person owns — use them carefully, keep your turn, share, and never dirty or break them.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
