# Keeping Promises and Rules — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** Why promises and rules exist — a)

**2.** The yellow bench — b)


Meanings:
a) Talk about what would happen if there were no rules at all. The town, the road and the park are full of things everyone uses and no one person owns — use them carefully, keep your turn, share, and never dirty or break them.
b) Takashi and Tetsuo fly their paper planes from the top of the park's yellow bench, and their muddy shoes cover it with dirt. Then a little girl sits down in the mud — and the boys see, too late, what a shared thing asks of everyone who uses it.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
