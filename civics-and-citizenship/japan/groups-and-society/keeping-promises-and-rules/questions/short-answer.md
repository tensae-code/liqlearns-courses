# Keeping Promises and Rules — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Keep promises and rules — they guard everyone's safety and pleasant days, and shared things belong to everyone.

**2.** What does the part "The yellow bench" teach?
**Answer:** Takashi and Tetsuo fly their paper planes from the top of the park's yellow bench, and their muddy shoes cover it with dirt. Then a little girl sits down in the mud — and the boys see, too late, what a shared thing asks of everyone who uses it.
