# Video script — Keeping Promises and Rules

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Keep promises and rules — they guard everyone's safety and pleasant days, and shared things belong to everyone.

Why promises and rules exist. Talk about what would happen if there were no rules at all. The town, the road and the park are full of things everyone uses and no one person owns — use them carefully, keep your turn, share, and never dirty or break them.

The yellow bench. Takashi and Tetsuo fly their paper planes from the top of the park's yellow bench, and their muddy shoes cover it with dirt. Then a little girl sits down in the mud — and the boys see, too late, what a shared thing asks of everyone who uses it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
