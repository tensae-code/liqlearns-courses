# Hands-on — Keeping Promises and Rules

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Keep one promise and one rule today on purpose — a turn, a queue, a shared thing used carefully — and notice how the day runs because of it.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: keep promises and rules — they guard everyone's safety and pleasant days, and shared things belong to everyone. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
