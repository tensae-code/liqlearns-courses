# My Role and Responsibility in the Group

## Do it first

Name a group you belong to and your role in it. Say what would fail if you did not do your part, then do that part fully this week.

## Sub lesson 1 — Every role holds the play up

In the school play, the lighting and props members shine as much as the actors — without one person doing their part, the play cannot stand.

## Sub lesson 2 — My role, my responsibility

Find your role in the group and fulfil it responsibly — the group's success is built from each member's kept part.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: a group stands only when every role is fulfilled — the unseen roles shine as much as the seen ones. Give one real example from your own life in Japan, and name the lesson's key words: role, responsibility, group, play. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Japanese as well as in English — Japan's own books teach Moral Education (道徳, Doutoku) in Japanese, through stories and self-check pages, for life at home and at school, not only for the classroom.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: a group stands only when every role is fulfilled — the unseen roles shine as much as the seen ones.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
