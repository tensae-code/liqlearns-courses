# My Role and Responsibility in the Group — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** Every role holds the play up — a)

**2.** My role, my responsibility — b)


Meanings:
a) In the school play, the lighting and props members shine as much as the actors — without one person doing their part, the play cannot stand.
b) Find your role in the group and fulfil it responsibly — the group's success is built from each member's kept part.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
