# My Role and Responsibility in the Group — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) a group stands only when every role is fulfilled — the unseen roles shine as much as the seen ones  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — Every role holds the play up?
a) In the school play, the lighting and props members shine as much as the actors — without one person doing their part, the play cannot stand.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
