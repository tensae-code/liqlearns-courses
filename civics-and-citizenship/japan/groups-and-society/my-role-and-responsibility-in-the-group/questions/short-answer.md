# My Role and Responsibility in the Group — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** A group stands only when every role is fulfilled — the unseen roles shine as much as the seen ones.

**2.** What does the part "My role, my responsibility" teach?
**Answer:** Find your role in the group and fulfil it responsibly — the group's success is built from each member's kept part.
