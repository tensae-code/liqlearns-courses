# Video script — My Role and Responsibility in the Group

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A group stands only when every role is fulfilled — the unseen roles shine as much as the seen ones.

Every role holds the play up. In the school play, the lighting and props members shine as much as the actors — without one person doing their part, the play cannot stand.

My role, my responsibility. Find your role in the group and fulfil it responsibly — the group's success is built from each member's kept part.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
