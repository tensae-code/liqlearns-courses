# Hands-on — My Role and Responsibility in the Group

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name a group you belong to and your role in it. Say what would fail if you did not do your part, then do that part fully this week.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: a group stands only when every role is fulfilled — the unseen roles shine as much as the seen ones. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
