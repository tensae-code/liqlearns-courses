# Video script — Connected to the World's People

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
More than seven billion people share one star — with awareness as a Japanese person, ask what you can do.

One star, seven billion. The world's peoples live connected on one planet; Sakamoto Ryoma's charge — that Japan must look to the world — opens the book's last theme.

What can I do. With awareness as a Japanese person, ask what you yourself can do for the world's peoples — connection begins with one person's chosen act.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
