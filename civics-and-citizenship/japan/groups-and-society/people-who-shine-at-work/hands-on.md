# Hands-on — People Who Shine at Work

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name three jobs people do in your town. For each one, say who it helps, and one careful thing that worker must do well.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: people shine in their work when they give it their whole heart — because other people's lives can depend on it. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
