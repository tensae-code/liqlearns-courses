# Video script — People Who Shine at Work

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
People shine in their work when they give it their whole heart — because other people's lives can depend on it.

Work that shines. Across many fields, people shine in their work — the heart surgeon prepares again and again, because a patient's life depends on the preparation.

What work means. Work is not only a job: done with care, it serves other people, and that service is what makes it shine.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
