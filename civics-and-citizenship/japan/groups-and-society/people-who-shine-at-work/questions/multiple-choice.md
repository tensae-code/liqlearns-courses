# People Who Shine at Work — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) people shine in their work when they give it their whole heart — because other people's lives can depend on it  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — Work that shines?
a) Across many fields, people shine in their work — the heart surgeon prepares again and again, because a patient's life depends on the preparation.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
