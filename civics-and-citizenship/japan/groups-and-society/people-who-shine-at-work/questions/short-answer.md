# People Who Shine at Work — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** People shine in their work when they give it their whole heart — because other people's lives can depend on it.

**2.** What does the part "What work means" teach?
**Answer:** Work is not only a job: done with care, it serves other people, and that service is what makes it shine.
