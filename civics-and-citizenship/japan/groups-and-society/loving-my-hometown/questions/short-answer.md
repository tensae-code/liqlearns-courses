# Loving My Hometown — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** My hometown is a treasure — its people, its life, its nature and its culture are mine to love and keep.

**2.** What does the part "Love that keeps" teach?
**Answer:** Love for a hometown shows in care: keeping its places clean, learning its stories, and passing them on.
