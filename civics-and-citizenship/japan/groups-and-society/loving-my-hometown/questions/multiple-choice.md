# Loving My Hometown — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) my hometown is a treasure — its people, its life, its nature and its culture are mine to love and keep  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — The treasures of home?
a) Your hometown's people, daily life, nature and culture are treasures — loving your hometown (furusato) starts by seeing them as such.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
