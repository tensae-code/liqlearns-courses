# Loving My Hometown — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** The treasures of home — a)

**2.** Love that keeps — b)


Meanings:
a) Your hometown's people, daily life, nature and culture are treasures — loving your hometown (furusato) starts by seeing them as such.
b) Love for a hometown shows in care: keeping its places clean, learning its stories, and passing them on.
