# Hands-on — Loving My Hometown

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name four treasures of your hometown: a person, a place in nature, a food or craft, and a story. Say one way you can care for one of them.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: my hometown is a treasure — its people, its life, its nature and its culture are mine to love and keep. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
