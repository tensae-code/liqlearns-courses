# Video script — Loving My Hometown

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
My hometown is a treasure — its people, its life, its nature and its culture are mine to love and keep.

The treasures of home. Your hometown's people, daily life, nature and culture are treasures — loving your hometown (furusato) starts by seeing them as such.

Love that keeps. Love for a hometown shows in care: keeping its places clean, learning its stories, and passing them on.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
