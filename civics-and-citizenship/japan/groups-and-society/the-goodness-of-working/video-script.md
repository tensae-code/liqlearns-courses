# Video script — The Goodness of Working

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Feel the goodness of working — work done with all your heart for others shines, and feels good.

My job. A child writes about her lunch duty: today she wiped the spilt milk properly and washed the cloth clean, the teacher thanked her, and she resolved to try again tomorrow. School duties and helping at home are real work too.

Kuma the bear postman. Kuma is the forest's postman. He delivers letters and parcels house by house, climbs the snowy mountain road to old Goat's house imagining his happy face, and even on days with no mail he talks with everyone — the forest waits for him gladly.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
