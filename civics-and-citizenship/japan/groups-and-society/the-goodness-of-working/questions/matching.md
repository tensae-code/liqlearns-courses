# The Goodness of Working — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** My job — a)

**2.** Kuma the bear postman — b)


Meanings:
a) A child writes about her lunch duty: today she wiped the spilt milk properly and washed the cloth clean, the teacher thanked her, and she resolved to try again tomorrow. School duties and helping at home are real work too.
b) Kuma is the forest's postman. He delivers letters and parcels house by house, climbs the snowy mountain road to old Goat's house imagining his happy face, and even on days with no mail he talks with everyone — the forest waits for him gladly.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
