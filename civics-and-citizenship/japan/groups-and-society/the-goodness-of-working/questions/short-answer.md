# The Goodness of Working — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Feel the goodness of working — work done with all your heart for others shines, and feels good.

**2.** What does the part "Kuma the bear postman" teach?
**Answer:** Kuma is the forest's postman. He delivers letters and parcels house by house, climbs the snowy mountain road to old Goat's house imagining his happy face, and even on days with no mail he talks with everyone — the forest waits for him gladly.
