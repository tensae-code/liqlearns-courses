# The Goodness of Working — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) feel the goodness of working — work done with all your heart for others shines, and feels good  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — My job?
a) A child writes about her lunch duty: today she wiped the spilt milk properly and washed the cloth clean, the teacher thanked her, and she resolved to try again tomorrow. School duties and helping at home are real work too.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
