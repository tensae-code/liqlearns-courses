# Laws and Rules for Living Together — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** One person in society — a)

**2.** Rules that let us live as people — b)


Meanings:
a) As one person living in society, you live among people of many standpoints — Fukuzawa Yukichi's teaching and the Hibi no Oshie point the same way: conduct yourself so shared life holds.
b) The laws and rules that must be kept are not chains: they are what lets people live as people, safely and fairly, side by side.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
