# Laws and Rules for Living Together — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) laws and rules exist so people of many standpoints can live as people, among people  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — One person in society?
a) As one person living in society, you live among people of many standpoints — Fukuzawa Yukichi's teaching and the Hibi no Oshie point the same way: conduct yourself so shared life holds.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
