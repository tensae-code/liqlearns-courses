# Laws and Rules for Living Together — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Laws and rules exist so people of many standpoints can live as people, among people.

**2.** What does the part "Rules that let us live as people" teach?
**Answer:** The laws and rules that must be kept are not chains: they are what lets people live as people, safely and fairly, side by side.
