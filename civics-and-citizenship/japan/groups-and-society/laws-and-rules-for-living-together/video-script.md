# Video script — Laws and Rules for Living Together

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Laws and rules exist so people of many standpoints can live as people, among people.

One person in society. As one person living in society, you live among people of many standpoints — Fukuzawa Yukichi's teaching and the Hibi no Oshie point the same way: conduct yourself so shared life holds.

Rules that let us live as people. The laws and rules that must be kept are not chains: they are what lets people live as people, safely and fairly, side by side.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
