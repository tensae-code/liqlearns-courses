# Hands-on — Laws and Rules for Living Together

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name three rules — at home, at school, on the road. For each, say whose safety or fairness it protects, including someone whose standpoint differs from yours.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: laws and rules exist so people of many standpoints can live as people, among people. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
