# Making School Life Fun

## Do it first

Ask one person who helps your school — a teacher, lunch staff, office staff, a caretaker — what their work is, and thank them for it.

## Sub lesson 1 — The people who help our school

Many people work for the school: teachers, the people of the lunch room and the office, caretakers. Find out what each of them does and what they think about as they work.

## Sub lesson 2 — Our school's treasures

It is everyone's school, so everyone makes it good together — talking together, keeping it clean together. Find your school's treasures: your favourite places, your favourite lessons, your happiest memories there.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: make school life fun — know the people who help the school, and build a good school together. Give one real example from your own life in Japan, and name the lesson's key words: school, helpers, treasures, together. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Japanese as well as in English — Japan's own books teach Moral Education (道徳, Doutoku) in Japanese, through stories and self-check pages, for life at home and at school, not only for the classroom.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: make school life fun — know the people who help the school, and build a good school together.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
