# Making School Life Fun — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) make school life fun — know the people who help the school, and build a good school together  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — The people who help our school?
a) Many people work for the school: teachers, the people of the lunch room and the office, caretakers. Find out what each of them does and what they think about as they work.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
