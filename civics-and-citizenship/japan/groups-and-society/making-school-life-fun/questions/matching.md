# Making School Life Fun — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** The people who help our school — a)

**2.** Our school's treasures — b)


Meanings:
a) Many people work for the school: teachers, the people of the lunch room and the office, caretakers. Find out what each of them does and what they think about as they work.
b) It is everyone's school, so everyone makes it good together — talking together, keeping it clean together. Find your school's treasures: your favourite places, your favourite lessons, your happiest memories there.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
