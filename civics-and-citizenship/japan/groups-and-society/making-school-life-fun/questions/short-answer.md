# Making School Life Fun — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Make school life fun — know the people who help the school, and build a good school together.

**2.** What does the part "Our school's treasures" teach?
**Answer:** It is everyone's school, so everyone makes it good together — talking together, keeping it clean together. Find your school's treasures: your favourite places, your favourite lessons, your happiest memories there.
