# Video script — Making School Life Fun

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Make school life fun — know the people who help the school, and build a good school together.

The people who help our school. Many people work for the school: teachers, the people of the lunch room and the office, caretakers. Find out what each of them does and what they think about as they work.

Our school's treasures. It is everyone's school, so everyone makes it good together — talking together, keeping it clean together. Find your school's treasures: your favourite places, your favourite lessons, your happiest memories there.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
