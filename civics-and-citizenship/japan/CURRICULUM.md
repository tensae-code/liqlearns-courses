# Civics and Citizenship — Japan course — CURRICULUM (the path)

One course for Japan inside the Civics and Citizenship folder,
organised by **levels, not grades**: a learner climbs when they
have proved the level, not when a school calendar says so. The
school grades the books come from (Grades 1–6) appear only in
each lesson's sources (provenance), never as the course's
organisation. The path follows Japan's own books' climb: one
six-year climb of the same four areas — self, others, life and
nature, society — the values restated at each band in older
stories and, by the last band, in the child's own argued words.
Japan's subject here is Moral Education (道徳, Doutoku), taught
entirely through short illustrated stories, songs and
self-check pages.

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in
this course comes from government textbooks — this country's
own Moral Education books (Watashitachi no Doutoku) — except
the **Tip boxes**: a tip in a box is from us, the people
making LiqLearns. Tips are ours; the lessons are the books'.
See ABOUT.md for the full book list, the Grades 1–2 PARTIAL
label, the UNREAD complete volume, and the acceptance
reckoning.

## The levels (the path, in order)

1. **Looking at Myself** — the youngest child's first content
   area: tell us about yourself; a regular and pleasant day;
   my own tasks done properly; good things done willingly; an
   honest and cheerful heart. 5 lessons, in the title
   `myself/`. *(Book 1 as filed is PARTIAL — only this first
   content area was in the file read; the band's other three
   areas are owed from the complete volume, UNREAD-pending
   under the disk hold — see ABOUT.md.)*
2. **Raising Myself and Relating to Others** — the four areas
   widen: write about myself; think well and live with
   moderation; the road before me; I can do it (Takahashi
   Naoko); sincerity toward everyone; people who shine at
   work; living by helping each other (the guide-dog family);
   loving my hometown; drawn to Japanese culture (Koizumi
   Yakumo). 9 lessons, in the titles `myself/` (4),
   `others/` (2) and `groups-and-society/` (3).
3. **Polish Myself, Connect, Cherish Life, Engage with
   Society** — the four areas as verbs: polish myself
   (moderation and courage; honesty and growth; people who
   strove toward goals; Helen and Annie Sullivan; autonomy
   and responsibility; an unpolished jewel shines not),
   connect with others (connect with others; Edo manners; the
   meaning of an act; Noguchi Hideyo), cherish life (one
   irreplaceable life; inheriting his feelings), engage with
   society (rights and duties; laws and rules for living
   together; my role and responsibility in the group;
   connected to the world's people). 16 lessons, in the
   titles `myself/` (6), `others/` (4), `cherishing-life/`
   (2) and `groups-and-society/` (4).

**Totals: 30 lessons** (Level 1: 5 · Level 2: 9 · Level 3:
16 — from three student books: Grades 3–4 and Grades 5–6
COMPLETE; Grades 1–2 PARTIAL as filed, its complete volume
UNREAD-pending under the disk hold; see ABOUT.md).
