# Civics and Citizenship — Japan course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

Civics belongs to the country: this course is Japan's own —
built from Japan's Moral Education student books,
Watashitachi no Doutoku for Grades 1–2, 3–4 and 5–6 (MEXT,
Japanese). Inside the course the organisation is by
**levels, not grades**: the titles below are the books' own
content areas, and the lessons inside them climb the three
levels in path order — what the learner proves, step by
step. The grades the books come from live only in each
lesson's sources.

The path's own logic is the books': one six-year climb of
the same four areas — self, others, life and nature,
society. The youngest book opens on the self alone (its
first content area, "Looking at myself" — the only area in
the file read; Book 1 is PARTIAL as filed, and its other
three areas are owed from the complete volume,
UNREAD-pending under the disk hold). The middle book widens
all four areas — raising myself, relating to others,
society, nature. The last book names the areas as verbs —
polish myself, connect with others, cherish life, engage
with society — and the values are argued in the child's own
words. That ladder IS the path order (recommended, never
forced — a learner can jump in anywhere; the "start here
instead" hints point down this ladder).

## The titles

- `myself/` — Levels 1–3. The self, at every band: Level 1
  (tell us about yourself, a regular and pleasant day, my
  own tasks done properly, good things done willingly, an
  honest and cheerful heart); Level 2 (write about myself,
  think well and live with moderation, the road before me,
  I can do it); Level 3 (polish myself — moderation and
  courage, polish myself — honesty and growth, people who
  strove toward goals, Helen and Annie Sullivan, autonomy
  and responsibility, an unpolished jewel shines not).
- `others/` — Levels 2–3. Relating and connecting: Level 2
  (sincerity toward everyone, living by helping each
  other); Level 3 (connect with others, learning from Edo
  manners, the meaning of an act, Noguchi Hideyo and yellow
  fever).
- `cherishing-life/` — Level 3. Cherishing life: one
  irreplaceable life, inheriting his feelings.
- `groups-and-society/` — Levels 2–3. Groups and society:
  Level 2 (people who shine at work, loving my hometown,
  drawn to Japanese culture); Level 3 (rights and duties,
  laws and rules for living together, my role and
  responsibility in the group, connected to the world's
  people).
