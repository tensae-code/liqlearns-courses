# Civics and Citizenship — Japan course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

Civics belongs to the country: this course is Japan's own —
built from Japan's Moral Education student books,
Watashitachi no Doutoku for Grades 1–2, 3–4 and 5–6 (MEXT,
Japanese), all three read in full and COMPLETE. Inside the
course the organisation is by **levels, not grades**: the
titles below are the books' own content areas, and the
lessons inside them climb the three levels in path order —
what the learner proves, step by step. The grades the books
come from live only in each lesson's sources.

The path's own logic is the books': one six-year climb of
the same four areas — self, others, life and nature,
society. The youngest book climbs all four in turn —
looking at myself, with people, touching life, together
with everyone — and closes with a review section (good
points, getting along, other countries). The middle book
widens all four areas — raising myself, relating to others,
society, nature. The last book names the areas as verbs —
polish myself, connect with others, cherish life, engage
with society — and the values are argued in the child's own
words. That ladder IS the path order (recommended, never
forced — a learner can jump in anywhere; the "start here
instead" hints point down this ladder).

## The titles

- `myself/` — Levels 1–3. The self, at every band: Level 1
  (tell us about yourself, a regular and pleasant day, my
  own tasks done properly, good things done willingly, an
  honest and cheerful heart, finding the good points);
  Level 2 (write about myself, think well and live with
  moderation, the road before me, I can do it); Level 3
  (polish myself — moderation and courage, polish myself —
  honesty and growth, people who strove toward goals, Helen
  and Annie Sullivan, autonomy and responsibility, an
  unpolished jewel shines not).
- `others/` — Levels 1–3. Relating and connecting: Level 1
  (pleasant behaviour and greetings, kindness with a warm
  heart, getting along with friends, grateful to the people
  who help us, getting along with everyone); Level 2
  (sincerity toward everyone, living by helping each
  other); Level 3 (connect with others, learning from Edo
  manners, the meaning of an act, Noguchi Hideyo and yellow
  fever).
- `cherishing-life/` — Levels 1 and 3. Life and nature:
  Level 1 (cherish life, palms to the sun, kind to living
  things, a fresh and open heart); Level 3 (one
  irreplaceable life, inheriting his feelings).
- `groups-and-society/` — Levels 1–3. Groups and society:
  Level 1 (keeping promises and rules, the goodness of
  working, being useful to my family, making school life
  fun, feeling close to my hometown, learning about other
  countries); Level 2 (people who shine at work, loving my
  hometown, drawn to Japanese culture); Level 3 (rights and
  duties, laws and rules for living together, my role and
  responsibility in the group, connected to the world's
  people).
