# Hands-on — A Fresh and Open Heart

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Find one beautiful or amazing thing near you today — the sky, the sea if you can see it, rain, a flower — and look at it properly, for a whole minute.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: meet the world's beauty with a fresh, open heart — nature's wonders refresh the heart that notices them. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
