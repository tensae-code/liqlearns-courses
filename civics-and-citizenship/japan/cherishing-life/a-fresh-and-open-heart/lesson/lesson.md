# A Fresh and Open Heart

## Do it first

Find one beautiful or amazing thing near you today — the sky, the sea if you can see it, rain, a flower — and look at it properly, for a whole minute.

## Sub lesson 1 — Wonderful things around me

Around you are nature, comfortable places and splendid things. When you meet them with a fresh, open heart, a refreshed feeling rises in you — go looking for the wonderful things near you.

## Sub lesson 2 — The sea, the sky, and my picture

The poem sings of the sea, its great blue waves rolling on and on. The sky wears many faces too. Write or draw the scene that made you feel 'beautiful' or 'amazing', in words or in a picture.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: meet the world's beauty with a fresh, open heart — nature's wonders refresh the heart that notices them. Give one real example from your own life in Japan, and name the lesson's key words: nature, wonder, beauty, refreshed heart. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

> **Tip:** Say the lesson's key words aloud once in Japanese as well as in English — Japan's own books teach Moral Education (道徳, Doutoku) in Japanese, through stories and self-check pages, for life at home and at school, not only for the classroom.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: meet the world's beauty with a fresh, open heart — nature's wonders refresh the heart that notices them.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
