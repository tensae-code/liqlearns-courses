# Video script — A Fresh and Open Heart

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Meet the world's beauty with a fresh, open heart — nature's wonders refresh the heart that notices them.

Wonderful things around me. Around you are nature, comfortable places and splendid things. When you meet them with a fresh, open heart, a refreshed feeling rises in you — go looking for the wonderful things near you.

The sea, the sky, and my picture. The poem sings of the sea, its great blue waves rolling on and on. The sky wears many faces too. Write or draw the scene that made you feel 'beautiful' or 'amazing', in words or in a picture.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
