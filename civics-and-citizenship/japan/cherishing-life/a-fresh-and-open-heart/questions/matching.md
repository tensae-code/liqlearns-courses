# A Fresh and Open Heart — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** Wonderful things around me — a)

**2.** The sea, the sky, and my picture — b)


Meanings:
a) Around you are nature, comfortable places and splendid things. When you meet them with a fresh, open heart, a refreshed feeling rises in you — go looking for the wonderful things near you.
b) The poem sings of the sea, its great blue waves rolling on and on. The sky wears many faces too. Write or draw the scene that made you feel 'beautiful' or 'amazing', in words or in a picture.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
