# A Fresh and Open Heart — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) meet the world's beauty with a fresh, open heart — nature's wonders refresh the heart that notices them  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — Wonderful things around me?
a) Around you are nature, comfortable places and splendid things. When you meet them with a fresh, open heart, a refreshed feeling rises in you — go looking for the wonderful things near you.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
