# A Fresh and Open Heart — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Meet the world's beauty with a fresh, open heart — nature's wonders refresh the heart that notices them.

**2.** What does the part "The sea, the sky, and my picture" teach?
**Answer:** The poem sings of the sea, its great blue waves rolling on and on. The sky wears many faces too. Write or draw the scene that made you feel 'beautiful' or 'amazing', in words or in a picture.
