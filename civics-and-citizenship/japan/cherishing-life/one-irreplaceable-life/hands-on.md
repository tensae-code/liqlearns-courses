# Hands-on — One Irreplaceable Life

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Sit quietly with one living thing — a tree, an insect, a pet — for two minutes. Say what is great about it, and why its one life matters.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: each of us has just one, irreplaceable, limited life — so respect life, know nature's greatness, and feel what is greater. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
