# One Irreplaceable Life — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) each of us has just one, irreplaceable, limited life — so respect life, know nature's greatness, and feel what is greater  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — Respect every life?
a) Respect the lives of yourself and others — because each life is one only, irreplaceable, and limited, it can never be treated lightly.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
