# One Irreplaceable Life — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** Respect every life — a)

**2.** Nature's greatness, and beyond — b)


Meanings:
a) Respect the lives of yourself and others — because each life is one only, irreplaceable, and limited, it can never be treated lightly.
b) Know nature's greatness, and feel that which is greater than ourselves — life sits inside something vast.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
