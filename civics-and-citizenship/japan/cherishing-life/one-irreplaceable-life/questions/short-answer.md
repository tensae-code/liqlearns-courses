# One Irreplaceable Life — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Each of us has just one, irreplaceable, limited life — so respect life, know nature's greatness, and feel what is greater.

**2.** What does the part "Nature's greatness, and beyond" teach?
**Answer:** Know nature's greatness, and feel that which is greater than ourselves — life sits inside something vast.
