# Video script — One Irreplaceable Life

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Each of us has just one, irreplaceable, limited life — so respect life, know nature's greatness, and feel what is greater.

Respect every life. Respect the lives of yourself and others — because each life is one only, irreplaceable, and limited, it can never be treated lightly.

Nature's greatness, and beyond. Know nature's greatness, and feel that which is greater than ourselves — life sits inside something vast.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
