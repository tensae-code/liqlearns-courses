## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **When do you feel alive?** — You feel that you are alive when your hand is warm, when your heart beats fast, when you eat something delicious, when you study happily. Life shows itself in these small moments — notice them.
2. **The grave visit and the baby hamsters** — In Kawai Masao's story, elephants cover their dead with grass and leaves and never forget them, and Naoto promises to visit his uncle's grave as his family visits his grandfather's. In the photo diary, a hamster mother carries her tiny babies ever so gently in her strong mouth, like treasure.

Gate pool: the prove-it check for each sub lesson is at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, picked as the hardest from the rank right under the wanted sub lesson — drawn from this lesson's question files (questions/).
