# Cherish Life — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** When do you feel alive? — a)

**2.** The grave visit and the baby hamsters — b)


Meanings:
a) You feel that you are alive when your hand is warm, when your heart beats fast, when you eat something delicious, when you study happily. Life shows itself in these small moments — notice them.
b) In Kawai Masao's story, elephants cover their dead with grass and leaves and never forget them, and Naoto promises to visit his uncle's grave as his family visits his grandfather's. In the photo diary, a hamster mother carries her tiny babies ever so gently in her strong mouth, like treasure.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
