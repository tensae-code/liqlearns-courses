# Cherish Life — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Cherish life — your one life and every life around you is precious and alive right now.

**2.** What does the part "The grave visit and the baby hamsters" teach?
**Answer:** In Kawai Masao's story, elephants cover their dead with grass and leaves and never forget them, and Naoto promises to visit his uncle's grave as his family visits his grandfather's. In the photo diary, a hamster mother carries her tiny babies ever so gently in her strong mouth, like treasure.
