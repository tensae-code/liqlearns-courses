# Cherish Life — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) cherish life — your one life and every life around you is precious and alive right now  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — When do you feel alive??
a) You feel that you are alive when your hand is warm, when your heart beats fast, when you eat something delicious, when you study happily. Life shows itself in these small moments — notice them.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
