# Video script — Cherish Life

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Cherish life — your one life and every life around you is precious and alive right now.

When do you feel alive?. You feel that you are alive when your hand is warm, when your heart beats fast, when you eat something delicious, when you study happily. Life shows itself in these small moments — notice them.

The grave visit and the baby hamsters. In Kawai Masao's story, elephants cover their dead with grass and leaves and never forget them, and Naoto promises to visit his uncle's grave as his family visits his grandfather's. In the photo diary, a hamster mother carries her tiny babies ever so gently in her strong mouth, like treasure.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
