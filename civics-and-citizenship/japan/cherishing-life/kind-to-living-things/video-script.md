# Video script — Kind to Living Things

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Be kind to living things — care for the creature in your charge to the end, and wonder at every small life.

Peace the polar bear. At Tobe Zoo a baby polar bear's mother would not raise him, so the keepers raised Peace themselves — the first time in Japan humans raised a polar bear. If you keep a pet, raise it with care for its whole life: that is what its life asks of you.

Fabre, who loved insects. The boy Fabre watched ants for hours, asking how they carry such big loads and find their way home. Grown up, he wrote the great Insect Book — and when an observation ended he thanked his insects and let them go home.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
