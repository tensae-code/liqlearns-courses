## Sub lesson 1 — Peace the polar bear
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: At Tobe Zoo a baby polar bear's mother would not raise him, so the keepers raised Peace themselves — the first time in Japan humans raised a polar bear. If you keep a pet, raise it with care for its whole life: that is what its life asks of you. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — Fabre, who loved insects
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The boy Fabre watched ants for hours, asking how they carry such big loads and find their way home.
