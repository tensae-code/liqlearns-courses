# Hands-on — Kind to Living Things

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Care for one living thing today — a plant, an insect you watch without harming, a pet — and do the care properly, the whole job.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: be kind to living things — care for the creature in your charge to the end, and wonder at every small life. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
