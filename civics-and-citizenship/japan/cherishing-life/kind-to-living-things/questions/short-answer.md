# Kind to Living Things — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** Be kind to living things — care for the creature in your charge to the end, and wonder at every small life.

**2.** What does the part "Fabre, who loved insects" teach?
**Answer:** The boy Fabre watched ants for hours, asking how they carry such big loads and find their way home. Grown up, he wrote the great Insect Book — and when an observation ended he thanked his insects and let them go home.
