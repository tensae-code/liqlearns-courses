# Kind to Living Things — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) be kind to living things — care for the creature in your charge to the end, and wonder at every small life  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — Peace the polar bear?
a) At Tobe Zoo a baby polar bear's mother would not raise him, so the keepers raised Peace themselves — the first time in Japan humans raised a polar bear. If you keep a pet, raise it with care for its whole life: that is what its life asks of you.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
