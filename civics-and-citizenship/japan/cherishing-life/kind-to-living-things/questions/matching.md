# Kind to Living Things — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** Peace the polar bear — a)

**2.** Fabre, who loved insects — b)


Meanings:
a) At Tobe Zoo a baby polar bear's mother would not raise him, so the keepers raised Peace themselves — the first time in Japan humans raised a polar bear. If you keep a pet, raise it with care for its whole life: that is what its life asks of you.
b) The boy Fabre watched ants for hours, asking how they carry such big loads and find their way home. Grown up, he wrote the great Insect Book — and when an observation ended he thanked his insects and let them go home.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
