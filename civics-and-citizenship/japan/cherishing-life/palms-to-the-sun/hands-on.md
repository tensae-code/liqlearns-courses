# Hands-on — Palms to the Sun

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Hold your palms up toward the sun or a bright light, the way the song says, and look at the red glow through your fingers.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: we are all alive — hold your palms to the sun: your life flows in you, and every living thing is a friend. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
