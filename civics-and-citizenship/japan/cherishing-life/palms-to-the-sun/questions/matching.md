# Palms to the Sun — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** We are all alive — a)

**2.** Your blood is flowing — b)


Meanings:
a) Yanase Takashi's song sings that earthworms and crickets, ants and dragonflies are all alive — and everyone who is alive is a friend. Nobody alive is alone in being alive.
b) Hold your palms to the sun and you can see your own blood flowing, bright red. That flowing blood is your life itself, warm and moving in your hands.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
