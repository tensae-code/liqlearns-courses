# Palms to the Sun — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** We are all alive — hold your palms to the sun: your life flows in you, and every living thing is a friend.

**2.** What does the part "Your blood is flowing" teach?
**Answer:** Hold your palms to the sun and you can see your own blood flowing, bright red. That flowing blood is your life itself, warm and moving in your hands.
