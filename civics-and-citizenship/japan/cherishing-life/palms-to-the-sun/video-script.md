# Video script — Palms to the Sun

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
We are all alive — hold your palms to the sun: your life flows in you, and every living thing is a friend.

We are all alive. Yanase Takashi's song sings that earthworms and crickets, ants and dragonflies are all alive — and everyone who is alive is a friend. Nobody alive is alone in being alive.

Your blood is flowing. Hold your palms to the sun and you can see your own blood flowing, bright red. That flowing blood is your life itself, warm and moving in your hands.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
