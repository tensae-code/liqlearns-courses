# Inheriting His Feelings — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) when someone you love is dying, you can carry on what they gave you — their feelings become yours to live  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these belongs to this lesson's first part — Visiting, every day?
a) A boy visits his grandfather — who may die within three months — every day, spending the limited time face to face.  b) a rule invented this morning with no source  c) the opposite of the lesson  d) nothing in the lesson covers it
**Answer: a)** — That is what the first part teaches.
