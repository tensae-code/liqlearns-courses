# Inheriting His Feelings — matching

Match each part with its teaching.

**1.** Visiting, every day — a)

**2.** Carrying on what he gave — b)


Meanings:
a) A boy visits his grandfather — who may die within three months — every day, spending the limited time face to face.
b) Inheriting his feelings means carrying forward what his grandfather gave him — the love and teaching do not end with the life.

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b
