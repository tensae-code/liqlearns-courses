# Inheriting His Feelings — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is this lesson's one thing, in your own words?
**Answer:** When someone you love is dying, you can carry on what they gave you — their feelings become yours to live.

**2.** What does the part "Carrying on what he gave" teach?
**Answer:** Inheriting his feelings means carrying forward what his grandfather gave him — the love and teaching do not end with the life.
