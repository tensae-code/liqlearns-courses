# Hands-on — Inheriting His Feelings

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Think of an older person who has taught you something. Write down what they gave you, and one way you will carry it on. Tell them, if you can.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: when someone you love is dying, you can carry on what they gave you — their feelings become yours to live. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, in your community. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
