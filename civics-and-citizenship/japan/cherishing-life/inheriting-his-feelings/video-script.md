# Video script — Inheriting His Feelings

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
When someone you love is dying, you can carry on what they gave you — their feelings become yours to live.

Visiting, every day. A boy visits his grandfather — who may die within three months — every day, spending the limited time face to face.

Carrying on what he gave. Inheriting his feelings means carrying forward what his grandfather gave him — the love and teaching do not end with the life.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
