# Lesson plan — Road Safety Education

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Dominican Republic · **Title:** Roads, Taxes and Environment · **Lesson:** Road Safety Education
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — My Country, My House (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The main points of road-safety education, researched by the pupils in teams; road-safety education for the responsible citizen; road education under the heading of responsible citizenship — living better through roads and taxes; road education inside a project on learning to live together and to contribute; road education as "safe routes" — a pillar of civism; road education for a safe environment and citizen responsibility; road education as citizenship in action — forging road responsibility in the final year. This lesson stands on a student book (see lesson/sources.md).
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.
