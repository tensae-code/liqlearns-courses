# A Culture of Saving

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: A Culture of Saving. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what the Dominican Republic's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

A culture of saving — actions to save light, water, money and school supplies, and helping the family not spend more than needed.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: A culture of saving — actions to save light, water, money and school supplies, and helping the family not spend more than needed. Give one real example from your own life or from the Dominican Republic you live in, and name the lesson's key words: a culture of. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: A culture of saving — actions to save light, water, money and school supplies, and helping the family not spend more than needed.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
