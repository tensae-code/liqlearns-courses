# Living Together on the Road — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Living together on the road — norms that save lives, in the street, at home and at school.  b) it has nothing to do with how the Dominican Republic governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Living together on the road — norms that save lives, in the street, at home and at school.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Living together on the road — norms that save lives, in the street, at home and at school.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in the Dominican Republic
**Answer: a) Living together on the road — norms that save lives, in the street, at home and at school.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
