# Lesson plan — Tax Education

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Dominican Republic · **Title:** Roads, Taxes and Environment · **Lesson:** Tax Education
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Citizens of Tomorrow — the Pillars (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Tax education — why citizens pay and what taxes build; tax education at second-year secondary depth; tax education as the pupil's own contribution — "I am part of the change"; tax education as "conscious taxes" — understanding what one pays; tax education as demonstrated citizen responsibility; tax education as citizenship in action — ethics and peaceful coexistence on the road and in the tax system. This lesson stands on a student book (see lesson/sources.md).
**This lesson's own level:** Level 4 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.
