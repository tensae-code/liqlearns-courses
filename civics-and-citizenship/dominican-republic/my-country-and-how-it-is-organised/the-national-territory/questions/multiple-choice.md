# The National Territory — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The national territory as part of the pupil's own identity.  b) it has nothing to do with how the Dominican Republic governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The national territory as part of the pupil's own identity.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) The national territory as part of the pupil's own identity.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in the Dominican Republic
**Answer: a) The national territory as part of the pupil's own identity.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
