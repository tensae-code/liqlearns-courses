# How Society Is Organised — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** How society is organised, met first through the child's own community and school; how society is organised, deepened from Grade 4 with the community's institutions.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that How Society Is Organised — and that it has no connection to the rest of the Dominican Republic's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside the Dominican Republic's own course, in its own order.
