# How Society Is Organised — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) How society is organised, met first through the child's own community and school; how society is organised, deepened from Grade 4 with the community's institutions.  b) it has nothing to do with how the Dominican Republic governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
