# The Dominican Constitution — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The Dominican Constitution read at the top of the ladder, after Grade 11's history of the constitutions.  b) it has nothing to do with how the Dominican Republic governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The Dominican Constitution read at the top of the ladder, after Grade 11's history of the constitutions.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) The Dominican Constitution read at the top of the ladder, after Grade 11's history of the constitutions.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in the Dominican Republic
**Answer: a) The Dominican Constitution read at the top of the ladder, after Grade 11's history of the constitutions.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
