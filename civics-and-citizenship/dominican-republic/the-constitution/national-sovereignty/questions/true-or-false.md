# National Sovereignty — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** National sovereignty — where the State's authority comes from; national sovereignty at the roots of the nation, with the emergence of the Dominican State and its Constitution.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that National Sovereignty — and that it has no connection to the rest of the Dominican Republic's story.
