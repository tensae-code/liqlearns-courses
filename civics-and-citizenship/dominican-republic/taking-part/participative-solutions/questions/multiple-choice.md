# Participative Solutions — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Participative solutions — conflicts settled with the participation of those involved.  b) it has nothing to do with how the Dominican Republic governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Participative solutions — conflicts settled with the participation of those involved.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

a) Participative solutions — conflicts settled with the participation of those involved.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in the Dominican Republic
**Answer: a) Participative solutions — conflicts settled with the participation of those involved.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
