# Civics and Citizenship — Dominican Republic — curriculum

One course for the Dominican Republic inside the Civics and
Citizenship folder, built from the Dominican Republic's own
government textbooks: the Ministerio de Educación de la
República Dominicana (MINERD) "Libro Abierto" Serie 1
guides for the subject Educación Moral, Cívica y Ética
Ciudadana — a student guide plus a teacher orientations
volume per grade, for Grades 4–6 of Primary and 1°–6° of
Secondary (census grades 4–12), all in Spanish, as filed
in the placements filing for this build
(`dominican-republic-placements.md` — see ABOUT.md for
the full book list, the verdicts, and every label
carried exactly as filed). The organisation is by
**levels, not grades**: a learner's place is found by
what they prove. The path is recommended, never forced
— a learner can jump in higher; the "start here instead"
hints point down this ladder. The grades the books come
from appear only in provenance (ABOUT.md and each
lesson's `lesson/sources.md`).

The ladder follows the Dominican Republic's own books'
progression, as the filing's ladder sketch lays it out:
each school year of the series is one level, because
that is the order the books themselves teach in. Every
student book is built as four learning projects, each
worked in four phases (explore / think / act / reflect),
so every concept is met inside a campaign or school
exhibition the pupils themselves organise. The nation is
introduced as "my country, my house"; the Constitution
opens the second year; primary ends turned outward to
the municipality and the world; secondary restates the
pillars for the "citizen of tomorrow", returns to the
roots of the nation, reads the constitutions' own
history, and closes with Dominican thought itself —
Duarte's constitutional project — and the graduate as
guardian of the nation's symbols, soil and sovereignty.
Alongside the student lessons at most levels stand the
teacher guides' own lessons, in the title Teaching the
Subject: the series' shared teacher doctrine and
project-by-project orientations. Those lessons are for
teachers, stand on teacher-guide evidence only, and
count 0 in the acceptance reckoning (see ABOUT.md).

## Level 1 — My Country, My House — 27 lessons

The first civics book: how society is organised, nation, State and government, the State as social, democratic and ruled by rights, the national territory, identity and values, the State and the Constitution met through a story, the powers of the State, civil and political rights and duties, the national symbols, road safety, living together on the road, a culture of saving, norms for happy coexistence, and living together though different — a culture of peace, and recognising violence and corruption. Citizenship begins as "my country, my house". Alongside it stand the Grade 4 teacher guide's own lessons on teaching the subject — what it is, its purposes, why and what for it is taught, the teacher as a model of integrity, the competency approach, the pedagogical principles, methodological suggestions, and orientations for the four projects (teacher-guide evidence only). Lessons: How Society Is Organised · Nation State and Government · A Social, Democratic State of Rights · The National Territory · Our Identity and Values · The State and the Constitution · The Powers of the State · Civil and Political Rights and Duties · The National Symbols · Road Safety Education · Living Together on the Road · A Culture of Saving · Norms for Happy Coexistence · Human Coexistence · A Culture of Peace and Managing Conflict · Values for Living Together · Violence and Corruption · Respect for Others and Harmonious Relations · What Moral Civic and Ethical Education Is · Purposes of Teaching the Subject · Why Teach Moral Civic and Ethical Education · What Teaching It Is for · The Teacher as a Model of Integrity · The Competency Approach · Pedagogical Principles and the Learning Guide · Methodological Suggestions · Orientations for the Four Projects.

## Level 2 — The Constitution Opens the Year — 19 lessons

The Constitution itself opens the year: fundamental rights in the Constitution, what the Legislative Power does, nationality and citizenship, the natural, historical and cultural heritage, government, the national symbols deepened, the child-protection law Ley 136-03, road and tax education, what traffic accidents cost the State, pollution, everyday risks to safety and health, and peace — a culture of peace, democratic solutions, and recognising social, family and youth violence — for a resilient community. Alongside it, the Grade 5 teacher guide's shared teacher doctrine and its orientations for the four projects (teacher-guide evidence only). Lessons: Fundamental Rights in the Constitution · What the Legislative Power Does · Nationality and Citizenship · Natural Historical and Cultural Heritage · Government · The Law That Protects Children (Ley 136-03) · Road and Tax Education · What Traffic Accidents Cost the State · The Impact of Pollution · Daily Situations That Risk Safety and Health · A Culture of Peace and Peaceful Solutions · Democratic Solutions · Social and Family Violence · Youth Violence · The Shared Teacher Doctrine · Orientations for Exploring the Constitution · Orientations for My Country My Rights and Duties · Orientations for Road Tax and Environment Strategies · Orientations for Peace and a Resilient Community.

## Level 3 — How Our Society Is Organised — Turned Outward — 15 lessons

Primary ends turned outward: the heritage of the world, how society is organised deepened, what the Judicial Power does, municipal organisation — the nearest level of the State — social and civil participation, tax rights and duties, road problems and tax education, fundamental rights and duties and fulfilling them, the national symbols at constitutional depth, values for life, and harmonious relations in the world. Alongside it, the Grade 6 teacher guide's shared teacher doctrine and its orientations for the four projects (teacher-guide evidence only). Lessons: World Heritage · What the Judicial Power Does · Municipal Organisation · Social and Civil Participation · Tax Rights and Duties · Road Problems and Tax Education · Fundamental Rights and Duties · Fulfilling Duties and Rights · Values for Life · A Culture of Peace and Conflict Situations · Harmonious Relations in the World · Orientations for Social Organisation and Children's Rights · Orientations for Deepening the Constitution · Orientations for Road and Tax Responsibility · Orientations for Peace and Global Harmony.

## Level 4 — Citizens of Tomorrow — the Pillars — 16 lessons

Secondary opens by naming the pupil a citizen of tomorrow and studying the pillars: rights and duties, national sovereignty, and the Constitution of the Dominican State with its powers and functions; then identity — the national symbols, dialogue, respect for oneself and others; then peace-building — road-safety and tax education, conflict resolution and a culture of peace, human values and harmonious coexistence; and guardianship of the planet — global warming, preserving natural resources, the social impact of pollution. Alongside it, the Grade 7 teacher guide's shared teacher doctrine, its orientations for the four projects, and the key actors in civic formation (teacher-guide evidence only). Lessons: Rights and Duties · National Sovereignty · The Dominican State and Its Powers · Dialogue · Respect for Oneself and Others · Tax Education · Conflict Resolution and a Culture of Peace · Human Values and Harmonious Coexistence · Global Warming · Preserving Natural Resources · The Social Impact of Pollution · Orientations for Citizens of Tomorrow · Orientations for Identity and Harmonious Coexistence · Orientations for Peace Builders · Orientations for Guardians of the Planet · The Key Actors in Civic Formation.

## Level 5 — Rights, Symbols and the Planet — 12 lessons

Fundamental rights in the Dominican Republic worked through values, conflict resolution and a culture of peace, and participative solutions; road-safety and tax education under the heading of responsible citizenship; the national symbols returned to, now with the laws that regulate their use; and the planet read through the global proposals of international organisations on environmental rights, actions for the environment joined to citizen duties, and the impact of pollution in the country and the world compared. Alongside it, the Grade 8 teacher guide's shared teacher doctrine, its orientations for the four projects, and its orientations for evaluation in the subject (teacher-guide evidence only). Lessons: Fundamental Rights in the Dominican Republic · Values Conflict Resolution and a Culture of Peace · Participative Solutions · The National Symbols and the Laws on Their Use · Global Proposals on Environmental Rights · Actions for the Environment and Citizen Duties · The Impact of Pollution in the Country and the World · Orientations for Rights, Values and Participation · Orientations for Responsible Citizenship · Orientations for the Symbols and the Laws on Their Use · Orientations for Guardians of the Planet: Acting for a Sustainable Future · Orientations for Evaluation in the Subject.

## Level 6 — Conscious Citizenship — Democracy as a State of Law — 9 lessons

Human rights joined to governance, economic development and democracy as a State of law; the use of natural resources in America and in the Dominican Republic; road and tax education as the pupil's own contribution — "I am part of the change"; the national symbols with their history and identity; and values that transform the school — human values, conflict solution and a culture of peace, and building healthy school environments resilient in the face of substance use. Alongside it, the Grade 9 teacher guide's shared teacher doctrine and its orientations for the four projects (teacher-guide evidence only). Lessons: Human Rights Governance and Democracy · Using Natural Resources in America and the Country · The National Symbols Their History and Identity · Human Values Conflict Resolution and a Culture of Peace · Healthy Resilient School Environments · Orientations for Conscious Citizenship · Orientations for Being Part of the Change · Orientations for Symbols History and Identity · Orientations for Values That Transform the School.

## Level 7 — Roots of the Nation — 11 lessons

The second cycle opens at the roots: national sovereignty with the emergence of the Dominican State and its Constitution, national identity, human rights; "safe routes and conscious taxes" — road and tax education, and protecting the environment inside that project; the pillars of the nation — the powers of the State, the national symbols, the State of law; and builders of harmony — democratic coexistence and a culture of peace, values and conflict resolution, listening and dialogue, respect for oneself and others. Alongside it, the Grade 10 teacher guide's shared teacher doctrine and its orientations for the four projects (teacher-guide evidence only). Lessons: National Identity · Human Rights · Protecting the Environment · The Rule of Law · Democratic Coexistence and a Culture of Peace · Values and Conflict Resolution · Listening and Dialogue · Orientations for Roots of the Nation · Orientations for Safe Routes and Conscious Taxes · Orientations for Pillars of the Nation · Orientations for Builders of Harmony.

## Level 8 — Constitutional History and Active Citizenship — 12 lessons

Constitutional history proper: how the Dominican State emerged, the Dominican constitutions across history and how they consecrate and guarantee fundamental rights, and the influence of the United States and French constitutions on them; road and tax education for a safe environment and demonstrated citizen responsibility; reform proposals, vulnerability and risk inside the project on symbols and active citizenship; and values and a culture of peace — dialogic solutions, the value of one's own and others' lives, situations of violence and vulnerability. Alongside it, the Grade 11 teacher guide's shared teacher doctrine and its orientations for the four projects (teacher-guide evidence only). Lessons: The Emergence of the Dominican State · The Dominican Constitutions and Fundamental Rights · Foreign Influences on the Dominican Constitutions · Reform Proposals Vulnerability and Risk · Values and a Culture of Peace · Dialogic Solutions · The Value of One's Own and Others' Lives · Violence and Vulnerability · Orientations for Constitution and Identity · Orientations for a Safe Environment and Responsibility · Orientations for Symbols and Active Citizenship · Orientations for Values and a Culture of Peace.

## Level 9 — Dominican Thought — Guardians of the Nation — 13 lessons

The final year turns to Dominican thought itself: the historical context of Dominican identity, democracy in the Dominican Republic, the Dominican Constitution, and Juan Pablo Duarte's constitutional project read between theoretical liberalism and practical conservatism; citizenship in action — road and tax education forging responsibility; guardians of the nation — the national symbols, and the nation's soil against deforestation, drought, pollution, overexploitation, waste, agrochemicals, and forest and mining exploitation; and human values joined to the pupils' own proposals for solving conflicts, with social and intrafamily violence returned to as a problem the graduate must answer. Alongside it, the Grade 12 teacher guide's shared teacher doctrine and its orientations for the four projects (teacher-guide evidence only). Lessons: The Historical Context of Dominican Identity · Democracy in the Dominican Republic · The Dominican Constitution · Duarte's Constitutional Project · Deforestation Drought Pollution and Waste · Agrochemicals and Their Environmental Impact · Forest and Mining Exploitation Risks · Human Values and Conflict Solution Proposals · Proposals for Solving Conflicts · Orientations for National Identity and Dominican Thought · Orientations for Citizenship in Action · Orientations for Guardians of the Nation · Orientations for Human Values and Conflict Solution.

  treatments, in the books' own order; the lesson sits

  filing order; single-part lessons stand whole, with
  no sub lessons.
