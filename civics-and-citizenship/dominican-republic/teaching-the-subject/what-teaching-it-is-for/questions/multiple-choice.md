# What Teaching It Is for — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) What it is for: building a conscious, participatory citizenship of people committed to their surroundings.  b) it has nothing to do with how the Dominican Republic governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) What it is for: building a conscious, participatory citizenship of people committed to their surroundings.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) What it is for: building a conscious, participatory citizenship of people committed to their surroundings.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in the Dominican Republic
**Answer: a) What it is for: building a conscious, participatory citizenship of people committed to their surroundings.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
