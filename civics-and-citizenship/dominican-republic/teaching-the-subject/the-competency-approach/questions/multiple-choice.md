# The Competency Approach — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The competency approach that organises the whole Libro Abierto series.  b) it has nothing to do with how the Dominican Republic governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The competency approach that organises the whole Libro Abierto series.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) The competency approach that organises the whole Libro Abierto series.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in the Dominican Republic
**Answer: a) The competency approach that organises the whole Libro Abierto series.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
