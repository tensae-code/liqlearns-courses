# Purposes of Teaching the Subject

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Purposes of Teaching the Subject. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what the Dominican Republic's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

The purposes of the subject: strengthening the teacher's role in patriotic, moral, civic and ethical formation.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The purposes of the subject: strengthening the teacher's role in patriotic, moral, civic and ethical formation. Give one real example from your own life or from the Dominican Republic you live in, and name the lesson's key words: purposes of teaching. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
