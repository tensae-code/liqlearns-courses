# Civics and Citizenship — Dominican Republic course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

Civics belongs to the country: this course is the
Dominican Republic's own — built from the Dominican Republic's own
government textbooks, the MINERD "Libro Abierto" Serie 1
guides for Educación Moral, Cívica y Ética Ciudadana
(Spanish, exactly as filed). Inside the course the
organisation is by **levels, not grades**: the titles
below ARE the levels' titles, in path order — what the
learner proves, step by step. The grades the books come
from live only in each lesson's sources.

The path's own logic is the Dominican Republic's, from the
filing's ladder sketch: the series teaches one school
year at a time, and each year is one level of this
ladder — "my country, my house" first; the Constitution
opening the second year; primary ending turned outward
to the municipality, participation and the world; the
pillars restated for secondary's "citizens of tomorrow";
rights worked through conflict and participation; human
rights joined to governance and democracy as a State of
law; the roots of the nation; the constitutions' own
history; and Dominican thought itself at the top, the
graduate proposing solutions as guardian of symbols,
soil and sovereignty. Two strands run the whole ladder
in the books' own way: the national symbols return at
almost every level, deepening each time, and road, tax
and environmental education are taught together as one
citizen responsibility in every book. That ladder IS
the path order (recommended, never forced — a learner
can jump in higher; the "start here instead" hints
point down this ladder).

The coined lesson names in the filing were deduped
before building (see CURRICULUM.md, "Where the build
stands"): 159 placements stock 129
lessons. Every placement stocks into exactly one
lesson, and each placement's provenance row (book +
printed page, exactly as filed) is in that lesson's
`lesson/sources.md`. Unit folders are the filing's own
coined titles. A lesson is listed under the first level
where its coined name appears; its later parts, from
later levels' books, are inside the lesson as sub
lessons.
