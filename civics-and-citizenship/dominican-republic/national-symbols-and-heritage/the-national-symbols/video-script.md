This lesson is about one thing.
The national symbols, valuing and respecting them in a school exhibition "Mi país, mi casa"; the national symbols deepened from Grade 4, inside a project on rights and duties; the national symbols at constitutional depth; the national symbols inside a project on identity and harmonious coexistence; the national symbols, with respect for them tied to civic conscience and citizen participation; the national symbols joined to active citizenship; the national symbols under the heading "guardians of the nation: symbols, soil and sovereignty".
