# Natural Historical and Cultural Heritage — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The natural, historical and cultural heritage the Constitution protects.  b) it has nothing to do with how the Dominican Republic governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The natural, historical and cultural heritage the Constitution protects.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) The natural, historical and cultural heritage the Constitution protects.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in the Dominican Republic
**Answer: a) The natural, historical and cultural heritage the Constitution protects.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
