# World Heritage — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The natural, historical and cultural heritage of the world, widening Grade 5's national heritage.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that World Heritage — and that it has no connection to the rest of the Dominican Republic's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside the Dominican Republic's own course, in its own order.
