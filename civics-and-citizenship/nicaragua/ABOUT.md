# About the Civics and Citizenship — Nicaragua course

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in
this course comes from government textbooks — this country's
own Convivencia y Civismo (Living Together and Civics) and
Convivencia y Desarrollo Rural (Living Together and Rural
Development) books. The one exception is the **Tip boxes**:
a tip in a box is from us, the people making LiqLearns.
Tips are ours; the lessons are the books'.

## How this course is organised

This is ONE course for Nicaragua inside the Civics and
Citizenship folder (one course per country). Inside it, the
organisation is by **levels, not grades**: a learner's place
is found by what they prove, and each level stands on the
ones before it as a recommended-first path — never a forced
gate. The school grades the books come from appear only in
each lesson's `lesson/sources.md`, as provenance.

Nicaragua's civics-type teaching in hand is the Ministerio
de Educación (MINED) set as filed: the Convivencia y
Civismo student books for Grades 2–6 of Primary, and the
Convivencia y Desarrollo Rural self-study modules of the
rural distance secondary (Educación Secundaria a Distancia
en el Campo) for Grades 7, 9 and 10 — 8 records / 8 distinct
books, all in Spanish, all student books or student modules.
The filing's own summary of how the set was read: the
primary Convivencia y Civismo books (Grades 2–6) and the
Grade 10 module were fetched from recorded sources (Wayback
captures of the MINED files and the MINED biblioteca) after
the GitHub tree route 404'd and the nicaraguaeduca host
returned 403; fetched byte sizes match the records exactly
where a size is on record, and the books are recorded as
pushed, so fetched copies were deleted after reading. The
Grade 7 module is UNHELD in the census: fetched from its
recorded MINED source, read, and STAGED under the
push-don't-delete rule. The Grade 9 module was fetched
complete from the mid-size-books-2 release after its
record's source proved to be only the MINED biblioteca
portal root. Glossary and bibliography sections are
inventory-only, not placed. Printed-page offsets differ
per book, as filed (each module's index is in printed
pages; the primary books' printed p. = PDFp − 4 in Grades
3–6, − 6 in Grade 2).

Nicaragua's own way, preserved whole, as filed:
Convivencia y Civismo starts from living together —
self-knowledge, emotions, solidarity and good manners in
home, school and community — and builds the citizen
outward: pride in the patria and its symbols, Nicaraguan
cultural identity, a first meeting with the Constitution
and children's rights, road safety, taxes, and working
for the common good. The rural distance-education modules
bind rights to the land — human rights and their
"restitution" in the countryside and in education, peasant
family values and women's protagonism, the rural
community's development, and love of Mother Earth. Grade 9
reads the Constitution title by title and adds the Labour
Code's protection of working children and adolescents;
Grade 10 opens with the ten life skills, then the Family
Code, then the tax law every citizen must meet, and closes
Mother Earth as rural tourism and entrepreneurship.

**Course-intro video script** (written here, made later;
the books' own shape, walked in order):

- *Nicaragua's own order:* "First, living together begins:
  accepting yourself as you are, your qualities,
  self-control and your emotions; solidarity, respect and
  honesty; collaborating at home, at school and in the
  community; and the norms and good manners that let us
  live better. Then my patria — its symbols, its coat of
  arms, its beautiful National Anthem, its heroes and its
  joyful patriotic fiestas — and the music, dance, dress,
  customs and traditions that make our national identity.
  Then being a citizen: a first meeting with the Political
  Constitution, human rights and the rights of the boy and
  the girl, a name and a nationality, and rights with
  duties under the Childhood and Adolescence Code. Then
  the road — walking and circulating safely, pedestrian
  zones, bicycles and transport — and protecting myself:
  healthy habits, danger noticed in time, the Red Cross.
  Then advertising, taxes and learning to buy, and
  organising for the common good. Year by year the same
  spine deepens: the carta magna and the Powers of the
  State, Central America's symbols and heroes, the
  Executive and Judicial powers, circulation and citizen
  security, the DGI, and the values book that closes
  primary — autonomy, solidarity, diversity of peoples and
  languages, gender focus and empowerment. Then the
  countryside: human rights and their restitution, peasant
  family values, rural development, and Mother Earth — her
  declaration, the national environmental strategy, energy
  in its forms and sources, water harvesting. Then the
  Constitution title by title, the Labour Code, the Family
  Code, the ten life skills, income tax and VAT — and at
  the top, rural tourism on Nicaragua's own routes, and
  the citizen as worker, taxpayer and community builder."

## The books (Nicaragua)

Book entries are named in full in each lesson's
`lesson/sources.md`. The set in hand for this build is the
filing's whole set: 8 distinct books — 5 primary student
books and 3 rural secondary student modules, all read.
176 placements in all, stocking 175 lessons (every
placement stocks into exactly one lesson). The filing
states what it does and does not contain, and this course
carries only what the filing states: verdicts are carried
exactly as filed — COMPLETE for Books 1, 3, 4, 5 and 6;
PARTIAL for Books 2, 7 and 8, whose civics parts are placed
in full and whose agricultural parts are named in their
contents maps below, not placed. A counting note, carried
plainly: the filing's per-book tallies sum to 175, but the
filing carries 176 placement lines — Book 5's tally reads
"33 placements" while 34 placement lines are filed under
it; this build stocks all 176 filed lines.

## Acceptance reckoning

Student books and student modules count; teacher guides
and programmes are evidence only, acceptance 0, labelled.
This filing names **no teacher guides and no programmes**
— all eight books are student books or student modules —
so every one of the 175 lessons stands on a student book
and counts: **acceptance 175 of 175 lessons**. No lesson
in this course stands at acceptance 0.
