# We Are All Equal — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) All persons are equal, and respecting others' rights is respecting yourself.  b) it has nothing to do with how Nicaragua governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) All persons are equal, and respecting others' rights is respecting yourself.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) All persons are equal, and respecting others' rights is respecting yourself.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Nicaragua
**Answer: a) All persons are equal, and respecting others' rights is respecting yourself.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
