# The Universal Declaration — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The Universal Declaration of Human Rights.  b) it has nothing to do with how Nicaragua governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The Universal Declaration of Human Rights.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) The Universal Declaration of Human Rights.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Nicaragua
**Answer: a) The Universal Declaration of Human Rights.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
