# The Powers in the Constitution — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) How the Constitution covers the powers that form the organisation of the State.  b) it has nothing to do with how Nicaragua governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) How the Constitution covers the powers that form the organisation of the State.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

a) How the Constitution covers the powers that form the organisation of the State.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Nicaragua
**Answer: a) How the Constitution covers the powers that form the organisation of the State.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
