# Children As Rights Holders

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Children As Rights Holders. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Nicaragua's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Girls and boys as social subjects and rights-holders, and respect for diversity.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Girls and boys as social subjects and rights-holders, and respect for diversity. Give one real example from your own life or from the Nicaragua you live in, and name the lesson's key words: children as rights. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
