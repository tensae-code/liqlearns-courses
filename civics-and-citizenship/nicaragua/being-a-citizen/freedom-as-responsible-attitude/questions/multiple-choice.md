# Freedom As Responsible Attitude — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Being free and able to decide, freedom as a responsible attitude, and the fable of the Wolf and the Dog.  b) it has nothing to do with how Nicaragua governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
