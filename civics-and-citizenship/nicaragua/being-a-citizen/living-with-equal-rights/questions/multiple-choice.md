# Living With Equal Rights — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Living with equality of rights, and the good father as its example.  b) it has nothing to do with how Nicaragua governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Living with equality of rights, and the good father as its example.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Living with equality of rights, and the good father as its example.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Nicaragua
**Answer: a) Living with equality of rights, and the good father as its example.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
