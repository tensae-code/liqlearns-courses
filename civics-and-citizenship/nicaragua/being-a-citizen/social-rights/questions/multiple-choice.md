# Social Rights — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Social rights.  b) it has nothing to do with how Nicaragua governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Social rights.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Social rights.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Nicaragua
**Answer: a) Social rights.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
