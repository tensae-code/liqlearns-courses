# Tax Law and Tax Power — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The tax law — tax or fiscal law, and the taxing power.  b) it has nothing to do with how Nicaragua governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The tax law — tax or fiscal law, and the taxing power.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) The tax law — tax or fiscal law, and the taxing power.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Nicaragua
**Answer: a) The tax law — tax or fiscal law, and the taxing power.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
