# Vanguard in Values — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Being a vanguard in values, with our family and community organised.  b) it has nothing to do with how Nicaragua governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Being a vanguard in values, with our family and community organised.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Being a vanguard in values, with our family and community organised.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Nicaragua
**Answer: a) Being a vanguard in values, with our family and community organised.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
