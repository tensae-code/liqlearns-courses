# Heroes Worthy of Imitation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Heroes and próceres worthy of imitation.  b) it has nothing to do with how Nicaragua governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Heroes and próceres worthy of imitation.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Heroes and próceres worthy of imitation.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Nicaragua
**Answer: a) Heroes and próceres worthy of imitation.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
