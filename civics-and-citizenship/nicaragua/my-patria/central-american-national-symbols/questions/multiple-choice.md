# Central American National Symbols — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The national symbols of the Central American countries.  b) it has nothing to do with how Nicaragua governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The national symbols of the Central American countries.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) The national symbols of the Central American countries.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Nicaragua
**Answer: a) The national symbols of the Central American countries.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
