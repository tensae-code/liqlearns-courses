# My Patria and Its Symbols — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** My homeland, the patriotic symbols that represent the nation — the coat of arms, the beautiful National Anthem and the other symbols — and respecting them.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that My Patria and Its Symbols — and that it has no connection to the rest of Nicaragua's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Nicaragua's own course, in its own order.
