# Civics and Citizenship — Nicaragua course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

Civics belongs to the country: this course is
Nicaragua's own — built from Nicaragua's own government
textbooks, the MINED Convivencia y Civismo books and
Convivencia y Desarrollo Rural modules (Spanish,
exactly as filed). Inside the course the organisation
is by **levels, not grades**: the titles below ARE the
levels' titles, in path order — what the learner
proves, step by step. The grades the books come from
live only in each lesson's sources.

The path's own logic is Nicaragua's, from the filing's
ladder sketch: a single spine from Grade 2 to Grade 10.
Living together begins with myself — my qualities, my
emotions, my manners — and the circle widens year by
year: my patria and its symbols, my national identity,
the Constitution and the citizen's rights, the road,
my health, the market and the tax office, and working
for the common good, which closes every primary book.
In the rural secondary modules the same spine turns to
the land: rights and their restitution in the
countryside, the Constitution read title by title, the
great codes, energy and Mother Earth, and at the top
the citizen as worker, taxpayer and community builder
— life skills, the Family Code, income tax and VAT,
rural tourism and entrepreneurship. That ladder IS the
path order (recommended, never forced — a learner can
jump in higher; the "start here instead" hints point
down this ladder).

The coined lesson names in the filing were deduped
before building (see CURRICULUM.md, "Where the build
stands"): 176 placements stock 175 lessons. Every
placement stocks into exactly one lesson, and each
placement's provenance row (book + printed page,
exactly as filed) is in that lesson's
`lesson/sources.md`. Unit folders are the filing's own
coined titles. A lesson is listed under the first level
where its coined name appears; its later parts, from
later levels' books, are inside the lesson as sub
lessons.
