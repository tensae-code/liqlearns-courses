# Behaviour on Transport — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Proper behaviour when travelling by collective, school and private transport.  b) it has nothing to do with how Nicaragua governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Proper behaviour when travelling by collective, school and private transport.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Proper behaviour when travelling by collective, school and private transport.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Nicaragua
**Answer: a) Proper behaviour when travelling by collective, school and private transport.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
