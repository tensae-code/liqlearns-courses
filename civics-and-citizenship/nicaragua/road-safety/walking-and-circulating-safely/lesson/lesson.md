# Walking and Circulating Safely

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Walking and Circulating Safely. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Nicaragua's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

The journey from home to school, circulating safely on public roads, and the traffic signs that guide and regulate us.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The journey from home to school, circulating safely on public roads, and the traffic signs that guide and regulate us. Give one real example from your own life or from the Nicaragua you live in, and name the lesson's key words: walking and circulating. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: The journey from home to school, circulating safely on public roads, and the traffic signs that guide and regulate us.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
