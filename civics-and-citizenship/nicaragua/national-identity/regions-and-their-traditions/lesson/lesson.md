# Regions and Their Traditions

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Regions and Their Traditions. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Nicaragua's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

The Pacific region, the Caribbean Coast, and the north and centre — their traditions and customs.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The Pacific region, the Caribbean Coast, and the north and centre — their traditions and customs. Give one real example from your own life or from the Nicaragua you live in, and name the lesson's key words: regions and their. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: The Pacific region, the Caribbean Coast, and the north and centre — their traditions and customs.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
