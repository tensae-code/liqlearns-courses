# Friends Cooperation and Helping — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Learning good things from good friends, liking to cooperate, and how pleasant it is to help others.  b) it has nothing to do with how Nicaragua governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Learning good things from good friends, liking to cooperate, and how pleasant it is to help others.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

a) Learning good things from good friends, liking to cooperate, and how pleasant it is to help others.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Nicaragua
**Answer: a) Learning good things from good friends, liking to cooperate, and how pleasant it is to help others.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
