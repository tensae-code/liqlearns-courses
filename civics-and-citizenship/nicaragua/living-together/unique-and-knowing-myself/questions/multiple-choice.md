# Unique and Knowing Myself — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) We are each unique, and knowing myself makes me better.  b) it has nothing to do with how Nicaragua governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) We are each unique, and knowing myself makes me better.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) We are each unique, and knowing myself makes me better.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Nicaragua
**Answer: a) We are each unique, and knowing myself makes me better.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
