# Rules and Good Manners — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Following norms and rules to live better — good behaviour, manners at home, at table and on visits, orderliness, and courtesy at school and in the classroom.  b) it has nothing to do with how Nicaragua governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Following norms and rules to live better — good behaviour, manners at home, at table and on visits, orderliness, and courtesy at school and in the classroom.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

a) Following norms and rules to live better — good behaviour, manners at home, at table and on visits, orderliness, and courtesy at school and in the classroom.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Nicaragua
**Answer: a) Following norms and rules to live better — good behaviour, manners at home, at table and on visits, orderliness, and courtesy at school and in the classroom.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
