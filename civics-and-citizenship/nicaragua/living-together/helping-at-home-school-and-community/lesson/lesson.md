# Helping At Home School and Community

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Helping At Home School and Community. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Nicaragua's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

How boys and girls collaborate at home, at school and in the community.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: How boys and girls collaborate at home, at school and in the community. Give one real example from your own life or from the Nicaragua you live in, and name the lesson's key words: helping at home. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: How boys and girls collaborate at home, at school and in the community.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
