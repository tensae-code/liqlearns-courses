# Health Habits — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Hygiene habits that keep your health from harm, and eating healthily.  b) it has nothing to do with how Nicaragua governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Hygiene habits that keep your health from harm, and eating healthily.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Hygiene habits that keep your health from harm, and eating healthily.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Nicaragua
**Answer: a) Hygiene habits that keep your health from harm, and eating healthily.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
