# Hands-on — Associations as Citizenship

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Citizenship is something you practise, not only something you hold. This lesson's practice is the association: what it is, how one is founded, and one worked example from start to finish.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: an association is citizens practising citizenship together — the law sets the steps to found one, and the lesson works a real example through. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
