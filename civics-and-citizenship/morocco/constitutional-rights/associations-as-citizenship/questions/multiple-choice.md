# Associations as Citizenship — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is an association?
a) a group of people joined by agreement for a shared purpose that is not profit  b) a company formed to make money  c) a government office  d) a political party's newspaper
**Answer: a) a group of people joined by agreement for a shared purpose that is not profit** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** How is an association founded?
a) by legal steps the law sets out, from the founding agreement to the declared association  b) by chance, with no steps  c) only by the King's order  d) only by foreign bodies
**Answer: a) by legal steps the law sets out, from the founding agreement to the declared association** — That is what this lesson teaches.
