# Lesson plan — Associations as Citizenship

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Morocco · **Title:** Constitutional Rights · **Lesson:** Associations as Citizenship
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — Constitutional Rights (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** an association is citizens practising citizenship together — the law sets the steps to found one, and the lesson works a real example through.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **What an association is** — An association (جمعية) is a group of people who join together, by agreement, for a shared purpose that is not profit.
2. **The legal steps to found one** — Founding an association follows legal steps: the law sets the road from the founding agreement to the declared association.
3. **A worked example** — The lesson closes with a worked example: one association followed through its founding, step by step.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
