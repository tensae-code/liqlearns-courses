# Associations as Citizenship

## Do it first

Citizenship is something you practise, not only something you hold. This lesson's practice is the association: what it is, how one is founded, and one worked example from start to finish.

## Sub lesson 1 — What an association is

An association (جمعية) is a group of people who join together, by agreement, for a shared purpose that is not profit.

Associations are where citizens act together in the country's daily life — the practice of citizenship in its plainest form.

## Sub lesson 2 — The legal steps to found one

Founding an association follows legal steps: the law sets the road from the founding agreement to the declared association.

The lesson walks the steps in order, so the citizen sees that founding one is a thing he can actually do.

## Sub lesson 3 — A worked example

The lesson closes with a worked example: one association followed through its founding, step by step.

The example gathers the whole strand: citizens, acting together under the law, are citizenship practised — the note this course ends on.
