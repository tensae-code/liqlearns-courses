# Civil and Political Rights — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The civil and political rights are the first family of rights in this course.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The covenants' rights have nothing to do with Morocco's laws.
**Answer: False** — The lesson sets the two side by side: the same family stands in Morocco's own laws.
