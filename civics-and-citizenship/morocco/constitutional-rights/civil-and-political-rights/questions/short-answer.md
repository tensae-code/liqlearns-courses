# Civil and Political Rights — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** How does the lesson work — what is its method?
**Answer:** It sets the international covenants' lists of rights against Morocco's own laws, right by right, working from the documents.

**2.** What does 'written twice' mean for the citizen's rights?
**Answer:** The rights stand once in the international covenants, for the world, and once in Morocco's own laws, at home.
