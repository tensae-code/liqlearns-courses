# Civil and Political Rights — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Where does the lesson read the civil and political rights first?
a) in the international covenants  b) in the newspapers  c) in the programme of a party  d) in the communal council's minutes
**Answer: a) in the international covenants** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does the lesson then find in Morocco's own laws?
a) the same family of rights, standing in the country's law too  b) no rights at all  c) only duties, no rights  d) rights for visitors only
**Answer: a) the same family of rights, standing in the country's law too** — That is what this lesson teaches.
