# Lesson plan — Civil and Political Rights

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Morocco · **Title:** Constitutional Rights · **Lesson:** Civil and Political Rights
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — Constitutional Rights (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** civil and political rights are the first family of rights — the lesson reads them in the international covenants, then finds the same rights in Morocco's own laws.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The rights in the international covenants** — The international covenants list the civil and political rights — the rights of the person as a citizen among citizens.
2. **The same rights in Morocco's laws** — Morocco's own laws carry the same family: the rights the covenants name stand in the country's law too.
3. **Covenant and law side by side** — The lesson's work is the setting-against: covenant list against Moroccan law, right by right.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
