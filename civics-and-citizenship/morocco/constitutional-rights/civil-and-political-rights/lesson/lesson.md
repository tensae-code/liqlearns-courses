# Civil and Political Rights

## Do it first

Write down three things you believe every person should be free to do and to be. This lesson takes a whole family of rights — the civil and political — and reads it in two places: the world's covenants and Morocco's laws.

## Sub lesson 1 — The rights in the international covenants

The international covenants list the civil and political rights — the rights of the person as a citizen among citizens.

The lesson reads the lists as the covenants give them, so the family of rights is met whole before it is met at home.

## Sub lesson 2 — The same rights in Morocco's laws

Morocco's own laws carry the same family: the rights the covenants name stand in the country's law too.

Reading the two side by side shows the citizen that his rights are written twice — once for the world, once at home.

## Sub lesson 3 — Covenant and law side by side

The lesson's work is the setting-against: covenant list against Moroccan law, right by right.

What matches, the citizen holds in both hands; the comparison itself is the lesson's method, as this book always works from documents.
