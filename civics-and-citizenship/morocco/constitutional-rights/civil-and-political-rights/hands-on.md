# Hands-on — Civil and Political Rights

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write down three things you believe every person should be free to do and to be. This lesson takes a whole family of rights — the civil and political — and reads it in two places: the world's covenants and Morocco's laws.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: civil and political rights are the first family of rights — the lesson reads them in the international covenants, then finds the same rights in Morocco's own laws. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
