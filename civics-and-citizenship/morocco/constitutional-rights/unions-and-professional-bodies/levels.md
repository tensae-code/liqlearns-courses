## Sub lesson 1 — What a union is
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: A union (نقابة) is the organisation workers join to defend their shared interests together — their pay, their conditions, their treatment at work. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 2 — What a professional organisation is
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: A professional organisation (منظمة مهنية) gathers the members of a profession — the people who practise the same work. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.

## Sub lesson 3 — How a union differs from a professional body
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The difference is the purpose: a union defends its members' interests; a professional body orders the profession its members share. — shown in the learner's own words and one real example.
