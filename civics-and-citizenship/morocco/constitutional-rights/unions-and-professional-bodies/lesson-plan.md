## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **What a union is** — A union (نقابة) is the organisation workers join to defend their shared interests together — their pay, their conditions, their treatment at work.
2. **What a professional organisation is** — A professional organisation (منظمة مهنية) gathers the members of a profession — the people who practise the same work.
3. **How a union differs from a professional body** — The difference is the purpose: a union defends its members' interests; a professional body orders the profession its members share.
