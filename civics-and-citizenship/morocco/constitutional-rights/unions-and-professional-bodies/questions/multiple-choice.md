# Unions and Professional Bodies — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is a union?
a) the organisation workers join to defend their shared interests together  b) a body that sets school exams  c) a government ministry  d) a group of employers only
**Answer: a) the organisation workers join to defend their shared interests together** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does a professional organisation gather?
a) the members of one profession — people who practise the same work  b) all workers of every trade in one town  c) only retired workers  d) the customers of a profession
**Answer: a) the members of one profession — people who practise the same work** — That is what this lesson teaches.
