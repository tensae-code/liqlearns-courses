# Unions and Professional Bodies — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A union and a professional body are the same thing under two names.
**Answer: False** — They differ in purpose: a union defends its members' interests; a professional body orders the profession its members share.

**2.** Workers together in a union speak with one stronger voice than one worker alone.
**Answer: True**
