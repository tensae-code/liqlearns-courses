# Unions and Professional Bodies

## Do it first

Two groups of working people: one bands together to defend its pay and conditions; the other gathers everyone in one trade. This lesson names both — and teaches you to tell them apart.

## Sub lesson 1 — What a union is

A union (نقابة) is the organisation workers join to defend their shared interests together — their pay, their conditions, their treatment at work.

One worker alone is easy to refuse; workers together, in a union, speak with one stronger voice.

## Sub lesson 2 — What a professional organisation is

A professional organisation (منظمة مهنية) gathers the members of a profession — the people who practise the same work.

Its care is the profession itself: who belongs to it, and how its members practise.

## Sub lesson 3 — How a union differs from a professional body

The difference is the purpose: a union defends its members' interests; a professional body orders the profession its members share.

The lesson sets the two side by side, so the citizen can name which body does what in working life.
