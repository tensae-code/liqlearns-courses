# The Press

## Do it first

This morning's news reached you somehow. This lesson looks at the people and the craft behind it: the press — a profession, with ethics, standing among your freedoms.

## Sub lesson 1 — The press as a profession

The press (الصحافة) is a profession: the work of gathering news and publishing it, done by people trained and answerable for that work.

It is not mere rumour-carrying: a profession has standards, and the press is judged by them.

## Sub lesson 2 — The ethics of the press

The press carries its own ethics — the rules of right conduct inside the craft, binding on those who practise it.

Ethics are what separate the press as a profession from anyone with something to say: the journalist answers for the truth of what he publishes.

## Sub lesson 3 — The press among the citizen's freedoms

Freedom of the press stands among the citizen's public freedoms, in the family this course has already met.

A free press serves every citizen: it carries the country's public life to him, so he can judge and take part.
