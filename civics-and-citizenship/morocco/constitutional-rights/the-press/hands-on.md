# Hands-on — The Press

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
This morning's news reached you somehow. This lesson looks at the people and the craft behind it: the press — a profession, with ethics, standing among your freedoms.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the press is a profession with its own ethics — and it stands among the citizen's freedoms, because a free press carries the country's public life. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
