# The Press — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the press, as this lesson teaches it?
a) a profession — the work of gathering and publishing news, done to standards  b) any rumour repeated in the street  c) a government announcement office only  d) a kind of court
**Answer: a) a profession — the work of gathering and publishing news, done to standards** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What separates the press as a profession from anyone with something to say?
a) its ethics — the journalist answers for the truth of what he publishes  b) its printing machines  c) its age  d) its price
**Answer: a) its ethics — the journalist answers for the truth of what he publishes** — That is what this lesson teaches.
