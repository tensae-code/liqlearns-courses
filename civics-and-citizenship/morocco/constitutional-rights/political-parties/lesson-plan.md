# Lesson plan — Political Parties

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Morocco · **Title:** Constitutional Rights · **Lesson:** Political Parties
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — Constitutional Rights (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a political party gathers citizens around a shared programme — parties arose in Morocco's own history, the law sets the rules for founding one, and their work is framing citizens for political life.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **What a political party is** — A political party is an organisation of citizens who share a political programme and work together to carry it into the country's life.
2. **How parties arose in Morocco** — Parties in Morocco have their own history: they arose in the country's political life and grew with it.
3. **Founding a party — and framing citizens** — Founding a party is not left to chance: the law sets rules for how a party is founded and works.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
