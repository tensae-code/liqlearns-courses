# Political Parties — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is a political party?
a) an organisation of citizens who share a political programme and work together to carry it out  b) a government office  c) a crowd gathered for one day  d) a court that judges politicians
**Answer: a) an organisation of citizens who share a political programme and work together to carry it out** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is a party's great task, in the book's own words?
a) framing citizens — forming them for political life  b) collecting taxes  c) running the courts  d) printing newspapers
**Answer: a) framing citizens — forming them for political life** — That is what this lesson teaches.
