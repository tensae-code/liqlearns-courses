# Political Parties

## Do it first

People who agree about how a country should be run often band together. This lesson names that band: the political party — what it is, where Morocco's parties came from, and how one is founded.

## Sub lesson 1 — What a political party is

A political party is an organisation of citizens who share a political programme and work together to carry it into the country's life.

A party is more than a crowd of supporters: it has members, a programme and a lasting shape.

## Sub lesson 2 — How parties arose in Morocco

Parties in Morocco have their own history: they arose in the country's political life and grew with it.

The lesson walks that arising in order, so today's parties are seen as the fruit of a road, not a sudden invention.

## Sub lesson 3 — Founding a party — and framing citizens

Founding a party is not left to chance: the law sets rules for how a party is founded and works.

And a party's great task, in this book's own words, is framing citizens (تأطير المواطنين) — forming them for political life, so they can take part with understanding.
