# Hands-on — Political Parties

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
People who agree about how a country should be run often band together. This lesson names that band: the political party — what it is, where Morocco's parties came from, and how one is founded.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: a political party gathers citizens around a shared programme — parties arose in Morocco's own history, the law sets the rules for founding one, and their work is framing citizens for political life. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
