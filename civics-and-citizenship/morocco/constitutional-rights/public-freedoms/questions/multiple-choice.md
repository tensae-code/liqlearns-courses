# Public Freedoms — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which of these is one of the public freedoms?
a) freedom of movement  b) freedom to break the law  c) freedom from all rules  d) freedom to take another's property
**Answer: a) freedom of movement** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What do the guarantees do for the public freedoms?
a) they protect them, so a freedom on paper is one the citizen can actually use  b) they replace them  c) they hide them from citizens  d) they apply them only abroad
**Answer: a) they protect them, so a freedom on paper is one the citizen can actually use** — That is what this lesson teaches.
