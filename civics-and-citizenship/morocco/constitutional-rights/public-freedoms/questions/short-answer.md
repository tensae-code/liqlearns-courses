# Public Freedoms — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the public freedoms this lesson names.
**Answer:** Freedom of association, of assembly, of the press and of movement, and their companions.

**2.** Why are the freedoms taught as one family?
**Answer:** Because together they make the open space in which citizens act in public — the doors of one room, not separate gifts.
