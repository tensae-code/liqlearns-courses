# Public Freedoms — matching

Match each word with its meaning.

**1.** public freedoms — a)

**2.** freedom of the press — b)

**3.** guarantees — c)


Meanings:
a) the freedoms citizens hold in their shared, public life
b) the freedom to publish, standing among the public freedoms
c) the protections in law that guard the freedoms

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b; 3. c
