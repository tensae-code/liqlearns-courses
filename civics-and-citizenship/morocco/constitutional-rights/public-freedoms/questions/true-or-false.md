# Public Freedoms — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Freedom of association and freedom of assembly are among the public freedoms Moroccans hold.
**Answer: True**

**2.** A freedom needs no guarantees — writing it down is enough.
**Answer: False** — The law carries guarantees that guard the freedoms; they stand between a freedom on paper and a freedom in use.
