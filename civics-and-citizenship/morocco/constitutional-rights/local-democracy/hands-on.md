# Hands-on — Local Democracy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Democracy is not only in the capital. This lesson comes home: to the commune, its council, and the public meetings where local affairs are decided.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: democracy also lives locally — the communal council runs the commune's affairs, holds named powers, and meets in public sessions the citizen can attend. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
