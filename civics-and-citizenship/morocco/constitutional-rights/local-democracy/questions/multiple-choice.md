# Local Democracy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which institution works local democracy?
a) the communal council  b) the courts  c) a political party  d) the press
**Answer: a) the communal council** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is special about the council's sessions?
a) they are public — citizens can attend and watch local affairs being decided  b) they are secret  c) they are held abroad  d) they are for members only, forever closed
**Answer: a) they are public — citizens can attend and watch local affairs being decided** — That is what this lesson teaches.
