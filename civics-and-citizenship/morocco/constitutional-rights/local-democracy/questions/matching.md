# Local Democracy — matching

Match each word with its meaning.

**1.** local democracy — a)

**2.** communal council — b)

**3.** public sessions — c)


Meanings:
a) the citizen's democracy at home, in his own commune
b) the body that runs the commune's affairs
c) the council's open sittings, which citizens can attend

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b; 3. c
