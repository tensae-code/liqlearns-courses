# Video script — Local Democracy

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Democracy also lives locally — the communal council runs the commune's affairs, holds named powers, and meets in public sessions the citizen can attend.

Local democracy (الديمقراطية المحلية) is the citizen's democracy at home: the affairs of his own commune decided close to where he lives.

It brings the state's decisions within walking distance of the people they touch.

The institution that works local democracy is the communal council — the body that runs the commune's affairs.

The council holds named powers over local life, and the lesson reads them as the law gives them.

The council meets in public sessions: its sittings are open, so citizens can watch their local affairs being decided.

Open sessions are the citizen's door into local democracy — he can attend, listen, and judge the work done in his name.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
