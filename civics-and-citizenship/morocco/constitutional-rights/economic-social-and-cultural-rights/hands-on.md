# Hands-on — Economic, Social and Cultural Rights

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Work, health, schooling, culture. This lesson takes the second family of rights — the economic, social and cultural — on the same road as the first: from the world's covenant to Morocco's law.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the second family of rights — economic, social and cultural — runs from the international covenant to Moroccan law, as the first family did. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
