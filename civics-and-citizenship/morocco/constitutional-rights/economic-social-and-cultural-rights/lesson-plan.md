# Lesson plan — Economic, Social and Cultural Rights

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Morocco · **Title:** Constitutional Rights · **Lesson:** Economic, Social and Cultural Rights
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — Constitutional Rights (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the second family of rights — economic, social and cultural — runs from the international covenant to Moroccan law, as the first family did.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The second rights family** — Beside the civil and political rights stands a second family: economic, social and cultural rights — the rights of work, of welfare and of culture.
2. **From the international covenant** — The international covenant for this family lists its rights for all countries, as the first family's covenant did.
3. **To Moroccan law** — Morocco's law carries the second family too: the rights named in the covenant stand in the country's own law.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
