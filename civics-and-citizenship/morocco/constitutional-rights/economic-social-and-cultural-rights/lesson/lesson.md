# Economic, Social and Cultural Rights

## Do it first

Work, health, schooling, culture. This lesson takes the second family of rights — the economic, social and cultural — on the same road as the first: from the world's covenant to Morocco's law.

## Sub lesson 1 — The second rights family

Beside the civil and political rights stands a second family: economic, social and cultural rights — the rights of work, of welfare and of culture.

The two families are companions: the first guards the citizen's voice, the second guards his living.

## Sub lesson 2 — From the international covenant

The international covenant for this family lists its rights for all countries, as the first family's covenant did.

The lesson reads the list from the covenant first, in the book's own document-first way.

## Sub lesson 3 — To Moroccan law

Morocco's law carries the second family too: the rights named in the covenant stand in the country's own law.

Covenant and law side by side, the citizen sees his economic, social and cultural rights written twice — for the world, and at home.
