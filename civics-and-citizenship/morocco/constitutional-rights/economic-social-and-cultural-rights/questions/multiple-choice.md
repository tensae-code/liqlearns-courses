# Economic, Social and Cultural Rights — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which family of rights is this lesson about?
a) the second family — economic, social and cultural rights  b) the first family — civil and political rights  c) the rights of states only  d) the rights of visitors only
**Answer: a) the second family — economic, social and cultural rights** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Where does the lesson read this family's rights first?
a) in the international covenant for this family  b) in a party programme  c) in the communal council's minutes  d) in the newspapers
**Answer: a) in the international covenant for this family** — That is what this lesson teaches.
