# Economic, Social and Cultural Rights — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** How do the two families of rights differ in what they guard?
**Answer:** The first family (civil and political) guards the citizen's voice; the second (economic, social and cultural) guards his living — work, welfare and culture.

**2.** What road does this lesson travel, and in what order?
**Answer:** From the international covenant's list of the family's rights to the same rights standing in Moroccan law — covenant first, then home law.
