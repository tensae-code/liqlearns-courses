# Civics and Citizenship — Morocco course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

Civics belongs to the country: this course is Morocco's own —
built from Morocco's Fadaa al-Ijtima'iyat (Social Studies Space)
book for Collège Year 2 (student book, Arabic), in its
Citizenship Education strand only, with the Collège Year 1
strand standing before it in the ladder as a sketch only (no
Year 1 book in hand — 0 placements). Inside the course the
organisation is by **levels, not grades**: the titles below sit
inside Level 2, in path order — what the learner proves, step
by step. The grades the books come from live only in each
lesson's sources.

The path's own logic is Morocco's: citizenship is taught from
the state's own papers — nearly every lesson works directly on
constitutional articles, laws and official documents (وثائق) —
and its ladder is institutions first (what the state is, its
constitution, king, parliament, government, courts), then the
citizen's rights and the bodies — parties, unions, press,
associations, local councils — through which citizens act. That
ladder IS the path order (recommended, never forced — a learner
can jump in higher; the "start here instead" hints point down
this ladder).

## The titles

**Level 1 — the year before** (Collège Year 1 citizenship
strand; **sketched, not stocked** — sketched by the series'
order only, so no title and no lesson is built for this level).

**Level 2 — the state, then your rights** (Fadaa al-Ijtima'iyat,
Collège Year 2 student book, Citizenship Education strand):

- `constitutional-institutions/` — Level 2, first title (Axis 1
  of the strand). The Components of the Moroccan State
  (territory, people, authority, and the state's symbols) · The
  Moroccan Constitution (what a constitution is, its place
  above other laws, the stages of Morocco's constitutions) ·
  The King (his place in the system of rule and his
  constitutional powers, read from the articles) · Parliament
  (the two houses, how members are chosen, legislation and
  oversight) · The Government (how it is formed, what it does,
  the prime minister's constitutional role) · The Judiciary
  (the principles it rests on, the kinds and levels of courts).
- `constitutional-rights/` — Level 2, second title (Axis 2 of
  the strand). Civil and Political Rights (the covenants' lists
  set against Morocco's laws) · Public Freedoms (association,
  assembly, press, movement — and the guarantees protecting
  them) · Political Parties (what a party is, how parties arose
  in Morocco, the rules for founding one, framing citizens) ·
  Unions and Professional Bodies (what a union is, and how it
  differs from a professional body) · The Press (a profession
  with its own ethics, among the citizen's freedoms) ·
  Economic, Social and Cultural Rights (the second rights
  family, from the covenant to Moroccan law) · Local Democracy
  (the communal council, its powers, its public sessions) ·
  Associations as Citizenship (what an association is, the
  legal steps to found one, a worked example).
