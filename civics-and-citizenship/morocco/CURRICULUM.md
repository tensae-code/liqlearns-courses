# Civics and Citizenship — Morocco course — CURRICULUM (the path)

One course for Morocco inside the Civics and Citizenship folder,
organised by **levels, not grades**: a learner climbs when they
have proved the level, not when a school calendar says so. The
school grades the books come from appear only in each lesson's
sources (provenance), never as the course's organisation. The
path follows Morocco's own order as its book lays it out: the
state's machinery first — components, constitution, king,
parliament, government, courts — then the citizen's rights and
the civic bodies (parties, unions, press, local councils,
associations) that make them real.

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in this
course comes from government textbooks — this country's own
Social Studies book, in its Citizenship Education strand —
except the **Tip boxes**: a tip in a box is from us, the people
making LiqLearns. Tips are ours; the lessons are the books'. See
ABOUT.md for the full book list, the combined-book scope, and
the acceptance reckoning.

## The levels (the path, in order)

1. **The Year Before (sketched, not stocked)** — the Collège
   Year 1 citizenship strand, sketched by the series' order
   only. No Collège Year 1 book is in hand this pass, so the
   level is sketched, not stocked. **0 lessons.**
2. **The State, Then Your Rights** — first the constitutional
   institutions: the components of the Moroccan state, the
   constitution, the King, Parliament, the Government and the
   Judiciary; then the constitutional rights: civil and
   political rights, public freedoms, political parties, unions
   and professional bodies, the press, economic, social and
   cultural rights, local democracy, and associations as
   citizenship practised. 14 lessons, in two titles:
   `constitutional-institutions/` (6 lessons) then
   `constitutional-rights/` (8 lessons).

**Totals: 14 lessons** (Level 1: 0 — sketched, not stocked ·
Level 2: 14 — all from one student book).
