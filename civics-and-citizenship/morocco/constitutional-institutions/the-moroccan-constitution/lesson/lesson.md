# The Moroccan Constitution

## Do it first

Write down three rules you live by — one at home, one at school, one in your street. Now ask: is there one rule above all the others? This lesson meets the law that stands above every other law.

## Sub lesson 1 — What a constitution is

A constitution is the basic law of a country: it sets out how the state is organised and what the citizen's rights are.

It is written as one document that the whole state works from, so everyone can read the rules of rule itself.

## Sub lesson 2 — The constitution above other laws

The constitution stands above all other laws: no ordinary law may go against it.

When a law and the constitution disagree, the constitution wins — that is what makes it the highest law of the country.

## Sub lesson 3 — Morocco's constitutions in stages

Morocco has not had one constitution forever: its constitutions have come in stages, each stage marking a step in the country's life.

The lesson walks the main stages in order, so the present constitution is seen as the latest step of a road, not a single sudden page.
