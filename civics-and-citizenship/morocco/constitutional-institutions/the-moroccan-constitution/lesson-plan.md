## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **What a constitution is** — A constitution is the basic law of a country: it sets out how the state is organised and what the citizen's rights are.
2. **The constitution above other laws** — The constitution stands above all other laws: no ordinary law may go against it.
3. **Morocco's constitutions in stages** — Morocco has not had one constitution forever: its constitutions have come in stages, each stage marking a step in the country's life.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
