# Hands-on — The Moroccan Constitution

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write down three rules you live by — one at home, one at school, one in your street. Now ask: is there one rule above all the others? This lesson meets the law that stands above every other law.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the constitution is the country's highest law — it stands above all other laws — and Morocco's constitutions have come in stages. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
