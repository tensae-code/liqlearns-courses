# The Moroccan Constitution — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is a constitution?
a) the basic law that sets out how the state is organised and what the citizen's rights are  b) a speech the government gives each year  c) a list of taxes and fines  d) the rule book of one political party
**Answer: a) the basic law that sets out how the state is organised and what the citizen's rights are** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** When an ordinary law goes against the constitution, what follows?
a) the constitution wins — the law cannot stand against it  b) the law wins, because it is newer  c) both stand, side by side  d) the courts ignore both
**Answer: a) the constitution wins — the law cannot stand against it** — That is what this lesson teaches.
