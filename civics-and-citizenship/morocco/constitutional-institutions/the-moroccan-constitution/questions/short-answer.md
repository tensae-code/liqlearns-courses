# The Moroccan Constitution — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why is the constitution called the highest law?
**Answer:** Because it stands above all other laws: no ordinary law may go against it, and when a law and the constitution disagree, the constitution wins.

**2.** What two big things does a constitution set out?
**Answer:** How the state is organised, and what the citizen's rights are.
