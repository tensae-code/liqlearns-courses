# Hands-on — Parliament

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Think of a big meeting that can make rules for a whole country. Who sits in it, and how did they get there? This lesson opens Morocco's Parliament.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Parliament is Morocco's law-making body — two houses, members chosen by the citizens, with the powers to make laws and to watch over the government. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
