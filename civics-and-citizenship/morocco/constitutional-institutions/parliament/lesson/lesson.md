# Parliament

## Do it first

Think of a big meeting that can make rules for a whole country. Who sits in it, and how did they get there? This lesson opens Morocco's Parliament.

## Sub lesson 1 — The two houses

Morocco's Parliament is made of two houses, not one: the two chambers sit together as the country's legislature.

Working in two houses means a law is looked at twice, by two bodies, before it stands.

## Sub lesson 2 — How members are chosen

The members of Parliament are chosen by the citizens — they sit in Parliament because the people put them there.

That is what makes Parliament the citizens' own house inside the state: its members speak for those who chose them.

## Sub lesson 3 — Legislation and oversight

Parliament's first power is legislation: it makes the country's laws.

Its second power is oversight: it watches the government, questions it and holds it to account for what it does.
