# Parliament — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** How many houses does Morocco's Parliament have?
a) two houses  b) one house  c) three houses  d) as many as the government wants
**Answer: a) two houses** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What are Parliament's two powers?
a) legislation and oversight — making laws and watching the government  b) taxing and spending  c) judging court cases and arresting offenders  d) appointing judges and commanding the army
**Answer: a) legislation and oversight — making laws and watching the government** — That is what this lesson teaches.
