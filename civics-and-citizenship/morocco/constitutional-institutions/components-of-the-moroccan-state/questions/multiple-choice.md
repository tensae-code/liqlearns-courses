# Components of the Moroccan State — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What are the three components of a state?
a) territory, people and authority  b) an army, money and roads  c) a flag, an anthem and a capital  d) cities, villages and farms
**Answer: a) territory, people and authority** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does authority give the state?
a) the power that governs the territory and the people  b) its land and borders  c) its population  d) its symbols and signs
**Answer: a) the power that governs the territory and the people** — That is what this lesson teaches.
