# Components of the Moroccan State — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A state can stand on territory and people alone, with no authority.
**Answer: False** — Authority is the third component: without it, territory and people are only a place and a crowd.

**2.** The symbols of a state stand for the whole state and are recognised by everyone.
**Answer: True**
