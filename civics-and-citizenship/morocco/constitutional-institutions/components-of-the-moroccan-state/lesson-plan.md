# Lesson plan — Components of the Moroccan State

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Morocco · **Title:** Constitutional Institutions · **Lesson:** Components of the Moroccan State
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — Constitutional Institutions (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a state stands on three components — its territory, its people and its authority — and Morocco's state is known by its own symbols.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Territory and people** — The first component is the territory: the land the state stands on, with its borders. A state without its own territory has nowhere to stand.
2. **Authority — the power that governs** — The third component is authority: the power that governs the territory and the people, makes the rules and keeps them.
3. **The symbols of the state** — A state also shows itself in symbols — signs that stand for the whole state and are recognised by everyone.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## If the learner is stuck
- Loses a component → set out three cards — territory, people, authority — and say what each one gives the state; a state missing one corner cannot stand.
- Go back to the one thing: a state stands on three components — its territory, its people and its authority — and Morocco's state is known by its own symbols. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
