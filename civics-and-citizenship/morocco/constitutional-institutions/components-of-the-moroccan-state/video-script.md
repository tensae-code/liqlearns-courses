# Video script — Components of the Moroccan State

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A state stands on three components — its territory, its people and its authority — and morocco's state is known by its own symbols.

The first component is the territory: the land the state stands on, with its borders. A state without its own territory has nowhere to stand.

The second component is the people: the population that lives on the territory and belongs to the state. Territory and people together give the state its ground and its members.

The third component is authority: the power that governs the territory and the people, makes the rules and keeps them.

Without authority, territory and people are only a place and a crowd. With it, they are a state — one body that can act, decide and answer for itself.

A state also shows itself in symbols — signs that stand for the whole state and are recognised by everyone.

Morocco's state has its own symbols, and the lesson reads them as part of the state's components: they mark the state out and gather its people around one sign.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
