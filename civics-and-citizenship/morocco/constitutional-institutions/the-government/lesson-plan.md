# Lesson plan — The Government

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Morocco · **Title:** Constitutional Institutions · **Lesson:** The Government
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — Constitutional Institutions (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the Government runs the country's affairs day by day — it is formed under the prime minister, whose role the constitution itself sets out.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **How the government is formed** — The Government is formed at the top of the state: it comes together under the prime minister, who leads it.
2. **What the government does** — The Government does the state's daily work: it carries the laws into practice and runs the public services of the country.
3. **The prime minister's constitutional role** — The prime minister's role is written in the constitution: he heads the Government and stands answerable for its work.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
