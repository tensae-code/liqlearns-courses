# Hands-on — The Government

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Laws on paper do not run a country by themselves. Someone must do the daily work. This lesson meets the body that does it: the Government.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the Government runs the country's affairs day by day — it is formed under the prime minister, whose role the constitution itself sets out. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
