# The Government — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Who leads the Government?
a) the prime minister  b) the head of the courts  c) the speaker chosen by the unions  d) no one — it leads itself
**Answer: a) the prime minister** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the Government's work?
a) the state's daily work — carrying the laws into practice and running public services  b) making the laws  c) judging court cases  d) writing the constitution
**Answer: a) the state's daily work — carrying the laws into practice and running public services** — That is what this lesson teaches.
