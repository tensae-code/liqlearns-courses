# The Government — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** How is the Government formed?
**Answer:** It comes together at the top of the state, under the prime minister who leads it; its members take charge of the different fields of the country's life.

**2.** How do Parliament and the Government meet in daily life?
**Answer:** Parliament makes the law and the Government works it — the two powers meet in the country's everyday life.
