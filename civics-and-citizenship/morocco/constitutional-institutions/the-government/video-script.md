# Video script — The Government

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The government runs the country's affairs day by day — it is formed under the prime minister, whose role the constitution itself sets out.

The Government is formed at the top of the state: it comes together under the prime minister, who leads it.

Its members take charge of the different fields of the country's life, each answerable for their part.

The Government does the state's daily work: it carries the laws into practice and runs the public services of the country.

Where Parliament makes the law, the Government works it — the two powers meet in the country's everyday life.

The prime minister's role is written in the constitution: he heads the Government and stands answerable for its work.

The lesson reads that role against the constitution's own text, so the Government is seen as the constitution arranges it, not as habit alone.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
