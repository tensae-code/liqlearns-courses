# The King — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Where does the King stand in Morocco's system of rule?
a) at its head, at the top of the state's authority  b) below Parliament, with no powers  c) outside the state altogether  d) as head of one political party
**Answer: a) at its head, at the top of the state's authority** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Where are the King's powers written?
a) in the constitution itself, article by article  b) nowhere — they are only custom  c) in the newspapers  d) in the programme of the government
**Answer: a) in the constitution itself, article by article** — That is what this lesson teaches.
