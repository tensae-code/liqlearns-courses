# The King — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** How does this lesson study the King's powers?
**Answer:** It reads them straight from the constitution's own articles — from the document, not from a summary.

**2.** What makes the King's place more than a matter of custom?
**Answer:** It is set by the constitution: the state's highest law itself places him at the head of the system of rule.
