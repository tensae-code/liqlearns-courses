# Hands-on — The King

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
List the people and bodies that hold power in a country — then put one name at the very top. This lesson looks at the top of Morocco's system: the King, and the powers the constitution gives him.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the King stands at the head of Morocco's system of rule, and his powers are written in the constitution itself. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
