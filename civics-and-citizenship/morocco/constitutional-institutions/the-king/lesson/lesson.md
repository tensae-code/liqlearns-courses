# The King

## Do it first

List the people and bodies that hold power in a country — then put one name at the very top. This lesson looks at the top of Morocco's system: the King, and the powers the constitution gives him.

## Sub lesson 1 — The King's place in the system of rule

Morocco's system of rule places the King at its head: he stands at the top of the state's authority.

His place is not a matter of custom alone — it is set by the constitution, the same document this course has just read.

## Sub lesson 2 — The King's constitutional powers

The constitution gives the King named powers — things he may do in the life of the state.

Each power is written down, so what the King does in the state can be pointed to, article by article.

## Sub lesson 3 — Reading the powers from the articles

The lesson reads the King's powers straight from the constitution's own articles, as this book always works: from the document, not from a summary.

Working this way, the pupil meets the King's role as the constitution states it — the state's own paper speaking for itself.
