# Hands-on — The Judiciary

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Two people disagree and cannot settle it between them. Where do they go? This lesson enters the courts — the state's justice power.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the Judiciary is the state's justice power — it rests on named principles, and its courts are arranged in kinds and levels. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
