# The Judiciary — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the Judiciary?
a) the state's justice power — its courts and judges  b) the law-making body  c) the government's daily administration  d) the press and its journalists
**Answer: a) the state's justice power — its courts and judges** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Why are the courts arranged in levels?
a) so a case can be looked at again by a higher court  b) so every town has the same court  c) so judges can choose their cases  d) so cases are never finished
**Answer: a) so a case can be looked at again by a higher court** — That is what this lesson teaches.
