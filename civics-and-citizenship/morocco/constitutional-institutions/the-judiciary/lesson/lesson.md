# The Judiciary

## Do it first

Two people disagree and cannot settle it between them. Where do they go? This lesson enters the courts — the state's justice power.

## Sub lesson 1 — The principles the judiciary rests on

Morocco's judiciary rests on principles that hold every court: justice is done under the law, by judges set apart for that work.

The principles come first in the lesson because they are what make a court's decision a judgment, not just an order.

## Sub lesson 2 — The kinds of courts

There is not one court for everything: Morocco's courts come in kinds, each kind hearing its own sort of case.

Knowing the kinds tells a citizen where a matter belongs when it comes before justice.

## Sub lesson 3 — The levels of courts

The courts are also arranged in levels, one above another, so a case can be looked at again by a higher court.

Kinds across, levels up: together they make the map of the judiciary that this lesson draws.
