# Rights and the Reservations Debate — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What question is asked about reservations in this lesson?
a) whether they offend equality  b) whether they cost too much  c) whether other countries copy them  d) whether they should cover sports teams
**Answer: a) whether they offend equality** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** How does the chapter teach rights here?
a) by arguing them through real, disputed cases  b) by listing them without discussion  c) by leaving them to the courts alone  d) by postponing them to a later book
**Answer: a) by arguing them through real, disputed cases** — That is what this lesson teaches.
