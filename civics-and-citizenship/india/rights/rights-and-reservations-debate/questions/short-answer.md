# Rights and the Reservations Debate — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** State the two meanings of equality that meet in the reservations debate.
**Answer:** Equality as exactly the same treatment for everyone, and equality as a fair chance for those whom history placed far behind.

**2.** Whose words on rights, quoted in the text, widen the argument beyond India?
**Answer:** The UN Secretary-General's words on rights.
