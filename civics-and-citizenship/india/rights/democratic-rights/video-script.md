# Video script — Democratic Rights

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The constitution gives citizens democratic rights, and those rights complete the frame built by the elections and institutions of the earlier chapters.

The Constitution gives citizens rights — claims that the state itself must respect.

Because they stand in the Constitution, no ordinary law or official order can simply take them away.

This chapter stands last in the first book on purpose: elections choose the rulers, institutions do the governing, and rights protect the citizen within both.

A democracy without protected rights would leave the citizen defenceless between elections.

Rights are not decorations: they are what a citizen can stand on when power is misused.

Knowing your rights is therefore part of being a citizen, not an extra.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
