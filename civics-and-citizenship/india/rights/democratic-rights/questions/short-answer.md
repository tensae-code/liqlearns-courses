# Democratic Rights — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is a constitutional right, in your own words?
**Answer:** A claim the citizen holds against power itself, written into the Constitution so that the state must respect it.

**2.** Name the three parts of the frame the first book builds, in order.
**Answer:** Elections that choose the rulers, institutions that govern, and rights that protect the citizen.
