# Democratic Rights — matching

Match each word with its meaning.

**1.** democratic rights — a)

**2.** Constitution — b)

**3.** citizen — c)


Meanings:
a) the rights the Constitution gives citizens, binding the state itself
b) the founding frame that guarantees rights
c) the person who holds rights and can stand on them

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b; 3. c
