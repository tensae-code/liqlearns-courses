# Democratic Rights — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Where do citizens' democratic rights stand?
a) in the Constitution  b) in the ruling party's manifesto  c) in a minister's speech  d) nowhere written
**Answer: a) in the Constitution** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Why does the rights chapter complete the first book's frame?
a) elections choose rulers and institutions govern, while rights protect the citizen within both  b) because it is the longest chapter  c) because rights replace elections  d) because institutions need no limits
**Answer: a) elections choose rulers and institutions govern, while rights protect the citizen within both** — That is what this lesson teaches.
