# Hands-on — Popular Struggles and Movements

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Think of a change people in your area wanted — a road repaired, a fee dropped, a wrong righted — when no election was near. Write: what could they actually do? This lesson studies the instruments this chapter names for exactly that moment.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: between elections, people press their demands through struggles and movements — democracy's own instruments for those the ballot alone does not reach. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
