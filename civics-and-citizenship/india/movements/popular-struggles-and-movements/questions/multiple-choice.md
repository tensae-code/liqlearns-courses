# Popular Struggles and Movements — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** When do struggles and movements mainly do their work?
a) between elections, when demands cannot wait  b) only on election day  c) never in a democracy  d) only in other countries
**Answer: a) between elections, when demands cannot wait** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** How does the chapter treat movements?
a) as democracy's own instruments  b) as crimes to be banned  c) as replacements for the constitution  d) as foreign imports
**Answer: a) as democracy's own instruments** — That is what this lesson teaches.
