# Popular Struggles and Movements — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is a movement, in the lesson's terms?
**Answer:** Many people gathered around a demand, keeping pressure on power until it is answered.

**2.** Why are struggles and movements called democracy's own instruments?
**Answer:** Because they let ordinary people press demands and shape what governments must answer, in the time between elections.
