# Video script — Popular Struggles and Movements

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Between elections, people press their demands through struggles and movements — democracy's own instruments for those the ballot alone does not reach.

Elections come at intervals, but people's demands do not wait for them.

Struggles and movements are how people press demands on power in the time between elections.

A struggle fights for a specific change; movements gather many people around a demand and keep pressure on until it is answered.

They are not a breakdown of democracy — the chapter treats them as democracy's own instruments.

Through struggles and movements, ordinary people — including those elections overlook — shape what governments must answer.

A citizen who understands them understands how democracy works between one vote and the next.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
