# Video script — Political Parties

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Political parties are democracy's working machinery — they contest elections, form governments and hold them to account, and this chapter studies them for themselves.

Parties have appeared all through the course — in constitutional design, electoral politics and the making of governments.

Here they are studied for themselves: what they are, what they do, and why democracies have them.

Parties contest elections, put forward policies, form governments when they win, and oppose and criticise when they lose.

They link the citizen to the government: a voter chooses not only a person but a party's programme.

The chapter places parties in the rise of democracies themselves: modern democracy and parties grew together.

Judging parties — their promises, their working, their hold on power — is part of judging a democracy.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
