# Political Parties — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** List three things political parties do.
**Answer:** Contest elections; put forward policies; form governments when they win; oppose and criticise when they lose (any three).

**2.** How do parties link the citizen to the government?
**Answer:** The voter chooses a party's programme as well as a person, so the election connects the citizen's choice to the government that follows.
