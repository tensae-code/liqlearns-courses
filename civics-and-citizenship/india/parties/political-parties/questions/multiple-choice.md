# Political Parties — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What do parties do when they lose an election?
a) oppose and criticise the government  b) disband at once  c) run the government anyway  d) stop all politics
**Answer: a) oppose and criticise the government** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does a voter choose when voting for a party's candidate?
a) a person and a party's programme  b) only a famous name  c) only a symbol with no meaning  d) nothing beyond the day
**Answer: a) a person and a party's programme** — That is what this lesson teaches.
