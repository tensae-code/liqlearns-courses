# Why Democracy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Why is a democratic government called legitimate?
a) because the people themselves chose it  b) because it never makes mistakes  c) because it is the strongest government  d) because its leaders are hereditary
**Answer: a) because the people themselves chose it** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** How does democracy handle social conflicts?
a) by discussion and decision instead of force  b) by banning all disagreement  c) by letting the strongest group win  d) by ignoring conflicts until they fade
**Answer: a) by discussion and decision instead of force** — That is what this lesson teaches.
