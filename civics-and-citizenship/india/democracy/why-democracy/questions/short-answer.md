# Why Democracy — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Give two reasons the lesson gives for preferring democracy.
**Answer:** It produces an accountable, responsive and legitimate government, and it handles social conflicts peacefully and can correct its own mistakes (any two).

**2.** Name one argument made against democracy in this lesson.
**Answer:** That it is slow, that leaders keep changing, or that ordinary people may not know what is best for them (any one).
