# Lesson plan — What Democracy Is

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — India · **Title:** Democracy · **Lesson:** What Democracy Is
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — Democracy and the Constitution Founded (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** democracy means rulers elected by the people, free and fair elections, one person one vote one value, and rule of law with respect for rights.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Rulers elected by the people** — In a democracy the rulers are elected by the people: the people choose who will govern them, and the government rules in their name.
2. **Free and fair elections, one vote one value** — Elections in a democracy must be free and fair, and every adult citizen's vote counts the same: one person, one vote, one value.
3. **Rule of law and respect for rights** — Democracy also means the rule of law: everyone, rulers included, is bound by the law, and the rights of citizens are respected.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks democracy just means having elections → elections alone are not enough: they must be free and fair, every vote must count equally, and the winners must still obey the law and respect rights.
- Go back to the one thing: democracy means rulers elected by the people, free and fair elections, one person one vote one value, and rule of law with respect for rights. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
