# What Democracy Is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Who rules in a democracy?
a) rulers elected by the people  b) one hereditary ruler who is never chosen  c) the richest person in the country  d) whoever commands the army
**Answer: a) rulers elected by the people** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does 'one person, one vote, one value' mean?
a) every citizen's vote counts the same  b) rich citizens get extra votes  c) only educated citizens may vote  d) the first vote cast counts double
**Answer: a) every citizen's vote counts the same** — That is what this lesson teaches.
