# What Democracy Is — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the four features of democracy this lesson builds, step by step.
**Answer:** Rulers elected by the people; free and fair elections; one person, one vote, one value; and rule of law with respect for rights.

**2.** Why is 'rulers elected by the people' placed first in the definition?
**Answer:** Because democracy is rule by the people: the people's choice is what makes a government democratic in the first place.
