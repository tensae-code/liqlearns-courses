# What Democracy Is — matching

Match each word with its meaning.

**1.** democracy — a)

**2.** free and fair elections — b)

**3.** rule of law — c)


Meanings:
a) rule by the people, through rulers they elect
b) elections anyone may contest and vote in honestly, with an honest count
c) everyone, rulers included, bound by the law

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b; 3. c
