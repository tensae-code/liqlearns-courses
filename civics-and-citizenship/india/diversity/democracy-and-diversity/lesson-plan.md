# Lesson plan — Democracy and Diversity

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — India · **Title:** Democracy and Diversity · **Lesson:** Democracy and Diversity
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — Democracy at Work (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** social diversity does not threaten democracy — democracy is the system that can accommodate linguistic and regional diversities by distributing power.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 2 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Diversity is a fact of social life** — Societies differ within themselves — by language, region, origin and more — and those differences are a fact, not a fault.
2. **Power distributed to accommodate diversity** — Power can be distributed so that linguistic and regional diversities are accommodated — given room and a share in governing.
3. **Why diversity does not threaten democracy** — Social diversity does not threaten democracy, because democracy gives every group a voice and a peaceful way to press its claims.
