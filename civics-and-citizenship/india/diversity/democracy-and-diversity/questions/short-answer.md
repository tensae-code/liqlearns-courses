# Democracy and Diversity — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** How can power be used to accommodate diversity?
**Answer:** By distributing it so that linguistic and regional groups have room and a share in governing.

**2.** Why is diversity a fact rather than a fault, in the lesson's terms?
**Answer:** Because societies always differ within themselves; the political question is how the system handles the differences, not whether they exist.
