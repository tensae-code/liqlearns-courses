# Democracy and Diversity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does democracy do with linguistic and regional diversity?
a) accommodates it by distributing power  b) suppresses it  c) ignores it completely  d) allows only one language
**Answer: a) accommodates it by distributing power** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What threatens democracy, in this lesson's argument?
a) refusing to accommodate diversity  b) the existence of many languages  c) regional differences  d) social differences of any kind
**Answer: a) refusing to accommodate diversity** — That is what this lesson teaches.
