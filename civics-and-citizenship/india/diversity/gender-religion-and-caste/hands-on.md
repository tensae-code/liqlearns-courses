# Hands-on — Gender, Religion and Caste

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write down three ways people around you are grouped — by the work men and women are expected to do, by religion, by caste. For each, note one way the grouping can turn unfair. This lesson studies exactly those three differences and what politics does with them.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: gender, religion and caste are three kinds of social difference that can become social division and inequality in India — and each is expressed in politics. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
