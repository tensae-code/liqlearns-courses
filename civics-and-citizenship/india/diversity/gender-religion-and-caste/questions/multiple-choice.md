# Gender, Religion and Caste — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which three differences does the chapter study?
a) gender, religion and caste  b) language, region and age  c) wealth, height and occupation  d) urban, rural and coastal
**Answer: a) gender, religion and caste** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** When does a social difference become a social division?
a) when it hardens into unequal treatment, one group above another  b) whenever people notice it  c) when it is written in a textbook  d) when groups celebrate festivals
**Answer: a) when it hardens into unequal treatment, one group above another** — That is what this lesson teaches.
