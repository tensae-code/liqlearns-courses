# Gender, Religion and Caste — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the three differences and say what the chapter asks about each.
**Answer:** Gender, religion and caste; for each, how it becomes social division and inequality in India, and how it is expressed in politics.

**2.** Why does the lesson want citizens to see how these differences are expressed in politics?
**Answer:** So that appeals and demands made in their name can be judged clearly rather than followed blindly.
