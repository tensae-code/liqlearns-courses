# The Two Houses of Parliament — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What are Parliament's two Houses?
a) the Council of States and the House of the People  b) the Senate and the Assembly of Elders  c) the High Court and the Supreme Court  d) the Cabinet and the Council of Ministers
**Answer: a) the Council of States and the House of the People** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which House is directly elected by the people?
a) the House of the People (Lok Sabha)  b) the Council of States (Rajya Sabha)  c) both Houses equally  d) neither House
**Answer: a) the House of the People (Lok Sabha)** — That is what this lesson teaches.
