# The Two Houses of Parliament — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name Parliament's two Houses and say which one the people elect directly.
**Answer:** The Council of States (Rajya Sabha) and the House of the People (Lok Sabha); the people elect the Lok Sabha directly.

**2.** What does a day in the Lok Sabha show the House is for?
**Answer:** Holding the government to account through questions and debate, and making the country's laws.
