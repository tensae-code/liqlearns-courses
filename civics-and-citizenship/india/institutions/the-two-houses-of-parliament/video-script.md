# Video script — The Two Houses of Parliament

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Parliament has two houses — the council of states and the house of the people — and law-making and questioning happen in their daily working, as a day in the lok sabha shows.

Electing rulers is only the start: democracy lives in how the institutions work every day between elections.

Parliament is the first of those institutions this course opens up.

Parliament has two Houses: the Council of States (Rajya Sabha) and the House of the People (Lok Sabha).

The House of the People is directly elected by the people; the Council of States represents the states.

The lesson walks through a day in the Lok Sabha: questions to ministers, debates, and the making of laws.

Watching one working day shows what the House is for — holding the government to account and making the country's laws.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
