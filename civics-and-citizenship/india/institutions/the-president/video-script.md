# Video script — The President

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The president is the formal head of the indian state, whose powers are exercised within the working institutions — on the advice of the elected government.

The President is the formal head of the Indian state — the office that represents the whole country.

Formal acts of the state are done in the President's name, even though the daily governing is done by the elected government.

The President's powers are real on paper, but they are exercised within the working institutions — through, and on the advice of, the Council of Ministers.

That is how the book presents the office: dignity and form at the top, political responsibility with the elected team.

The President is therefore not a rival government: the office stands inside the same frame as Parliament and the Council.

Understanding this place — formal head, elected government governing — is the lesson's one thing.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
