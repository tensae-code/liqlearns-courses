# The President — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is the President in India's institutions?
a) the formal head of the state  b) the head of the government team  c) the chief of the civil services  d) the leader of the opposition
**Answer: a) the formal head of the state** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** How are the President's powers exercised?
a) within the institutions, on the advice of the Council of Ministers  b) entirely at the President's personal will  c) by the army on the President's orders  d) they are never exercised at all
**Answer: a) within the institutions, on the advice of the Council of Ministers** — That is what this lesson teaches.
