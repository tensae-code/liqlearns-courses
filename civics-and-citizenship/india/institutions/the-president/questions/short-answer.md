# The President — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does 'formal head' mean here?
**Answer:** The President represents the whole country and state acts are done in the President's name, while the elected government does the daily governing.

**2.** Why is the President not a rival government?
**Answer:** Because the President's powers are exercised within the institutions, on the advice of the Council of Ministers, so political responsibility stays with the elected team.
