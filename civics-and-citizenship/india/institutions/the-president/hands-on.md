# Hands-on — The President

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Think of a ceremony you know where one person formally opens or blesses something that others actually organise. Write: what does the formal head do, and who does the daily work? This lesson places India's President in the same picture.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the President is the formal head of the Indian state, whose powers are exercised within the working institutions — on the advice of the elected government. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
