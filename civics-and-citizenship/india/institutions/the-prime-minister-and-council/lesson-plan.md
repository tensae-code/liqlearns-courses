# Lesson plan — The Prime Minister and the Council of Ministers

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — India · **Title:** Working of Institutions · **Lesson:** The Prime Minister and the Council of Ministers
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — Democracy and the Constitution Founded (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the political executive is a team — the Prime Minister and the Council of Ministers — assisted by the secretaries and the civil services.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The political executive** — Above the daily administration stands the political executive: the leaders the people's vote has put in charge.
2. **The Prime Minister and the Council** — The Prime Minister leads the Council of Ministers, the team that takes the government's big decisions together.
3. **Secretaries and the civil services** — The ministers are assisted by the secretaries and the civil services — the permanent officials who carry the work on from day to day.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
