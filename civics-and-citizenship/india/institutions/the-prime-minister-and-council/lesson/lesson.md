# The Prime Minister and the Council of Ministers

## Do it first

Think of a team you know — a sports team or a work crew. Write: who leads it, who does each job, and who does the steady daily work that keeps it running? This lesson meets India's government as exactly such a team.

## Sub lesson 1 — The political executive

Above the daily administration stands the political executive: the leaders the people's vote has put in charge.

They hold office because of the election, and they answer for the government's work.

## Sub lesson 2 — The Prime Minister and the Council

The Prime Minister leads the Council of Ministers, the team that takes the government's big decisions together.

Ministers head the different departments, and the Council works as one government, not as separate rulers.

## Sub lesson 3 — Secretaries and the civil services

The ministers are assisted by the secretaries and the civil services — the permanent officials who carry the work on from day to day.

Governments change; the services remain, keeping the administration running under each new team.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the political executive is a team — the Prime Minister and the Council of Ministers — assisted by the secretaries and the civil services. Give one real example from your own life or from the India you live in, and name the lesson's key words: Prime Minister, Council of Ministers, civil services. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
