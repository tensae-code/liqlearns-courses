# The Prime Minister and the Council of Ministers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Who leads the Council of Ministers?
a) the Prime Minister  b) the President  c) the Chief Justice  d) the Election Commissioner
**Answer: a) the Prime Minister** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Who assists the ministers in their daily work?
a) the secretaries and the civil services  b) the opposition parties  c) foreign officials  d) the voters directly
**Answer: a) the secretaries and the civil services** — That is what this lesson teaches.
