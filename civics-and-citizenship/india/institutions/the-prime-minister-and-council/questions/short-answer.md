# The Prime Minister and the Council of Ministers — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the political executive?
**Answer:** The elected leaders — the Prime Minister and the Council of Ministers — who hold office because of the people's vote and answer for the government's work.

**2.** Why does it matter that the civil services are permanent?
**Answer:** Because administration must keep running steadily while governments come and go; the services assist each new team of ministers.
