# Hands-on — The Prime Minister and the Council of Ministers

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Think of a team you know — a sports team or a work crew. Write: who leads it, who does each job, and who does the steady daily work that keeps it running? This lesson meets India's government as exactly such a team.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the political executive is a team — the Prime Minister and the Council of Ministers — assisted by the secretaries and the civil services. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
