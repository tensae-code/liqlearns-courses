# Lesson plan — Electoral Politics

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — India · **Title:** Elections · **Lesson:** Electoral Politics
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — Democracy and the Constitution Founded (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** elections are how the people choose their rulers and hold them to account — and an election is democratic only when the choice is real and free.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Why elections are needed** — In a large democracy the people cannot all govern directly, so they choose representatives through elections.
2. **What makes an election democratic** — An election is democratic when everyone can vote, there is a real choice of candidates, and elections are held regularly and fairly.
3. **Political competition** — Parties and candidates compete for the people's vote, offering their records and their promises.
