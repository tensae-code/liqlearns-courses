# Electoral Politics — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Why do large democracies hold elections?
a) so the people can choose representatives and judge their rulers  b) so rulers can stay in power for life  c) so citizens never need to think about politics  d) to copy other countries
**Answer: a) so the people can choose representatives and judge their rulers** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these makes an election democratic?
a) a real choice of candidates, open to all voters  b) only one candidate allowed  c) voting held once in fifty years  d) votes counted in secret by the ruler
**Answer: a) a real choice of candidates, open to all voters** — That is what this lesson teaches.
