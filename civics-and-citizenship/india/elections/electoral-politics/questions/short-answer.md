# Electoral Politics — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Give two tests of a democratic election.
**Answer:** Everyone can vote; there is a real choice of candidates; elections are regular; elections are fair (any two).

**2.** How do elections hold rulers to account?
**Answer:** A government that fails the people can be voted out at the next election, so rulers must answer for their record.
