# Electoral Politics

## Do it first

Think of the last time a group you are in chose a leader — a class monitor, a team captain. Write: was there a real choice? Could the choosers change their minds later? This lesson asks the same questions about a whole country.

## Sub lesson 1 — Why elections are needed

In a large democracy the people cannot all govern directly, so they choose representatives through elections.

Elections are also how the people judge their rulers: a government that fails can be voted out.

## Sub lesson 2 — What makes an election democratic

An election is democratic when everyone can vote, there is a real choice of candidates, and elections are held regularly and fairly.

Without a real choice, voting is a ceremony, not democracy at work.

## Sub lesson 3 — Political competition

Parties and candidates compete for the people's vote, offering their records and their promises.

Competition can turn rough, but it is also what gives voters a genuine choice and keeps rulers attentive.
