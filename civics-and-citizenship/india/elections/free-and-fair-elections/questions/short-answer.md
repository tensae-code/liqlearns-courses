# Free and Fair Elections — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why must the Election Commission be independent?
**Answer:** Because it referees the contest between parties; if the government of the day controlled it, the field would not be level.

**2.** Name the three challenges to free and fair elections this lesson names.
**Answer:** Money, muscle, and whether the field is level for all contestants.
