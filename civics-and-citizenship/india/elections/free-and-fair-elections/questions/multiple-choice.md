# Free and Fair Elections — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Who conducts India's elections?
a) the independent Election Commission  b) the ruling party  c) the Prime Minister's office  d) the army
**Answer: a) the independent Election Commission** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which pair names challenges to free and fair elections in this lesson?
a) money and muscle  b) rain and heat  c) holidays and festivals  d) distance and language
**Answer: a) money and muscle** — That is what this lesson teaches.
