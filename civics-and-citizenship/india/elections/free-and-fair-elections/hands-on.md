# Hands-on — Free and Fair Elections

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Imagine a school election where one candidate's family pays for everyone's lunch and another candidate's friends threaten voters. Write down what is unfair, and who could stop it. This lesson meets the body India built for exactly that job.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: India's elections are run by an independent Election Commission, and their fairness is tested against named challenges — money, muscle and an unlevel field. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
