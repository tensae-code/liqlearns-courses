# Power-Sharing — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What did Belgium do with its communities?
a) accommodated them by sharing power  b) expelled the smaller community  c) banned all languages but one  d) ended elections
**Answer: a) accommodated them by sharing power** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Among which organs is power shared horizontally?
a) legislature, executive and judiciary  b) army, police and prisons  c) centre, states and districts only  d) parties, press and courts
**Answer: a) legislature, executive and judiciary** — That is what this lesson teaches.
