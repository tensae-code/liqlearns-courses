# Power-Sharing — matching

Match each word with its meaning.

**1.** power-sharing — a)

**2.** majoritarianism — b)

**3.** accommodation — c)


Meanings:
a) dividing power among organs of government and among communities
b) the majority community imposing its will without sharing power
c) adjusting arrangements so all communities can live together

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b; 3. c
