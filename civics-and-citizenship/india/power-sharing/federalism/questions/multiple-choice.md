# Federalism — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What kind of division is federalism?
a) a vertical division of power among levels of government  b) a division among political parties  c) a division of one office among three people  d) a division of the year into sessions
**Answer: a) a vertical division of power among levels of government** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What protects the division of power in a federation?
a) the constitution  b) the ruling party's goodwill  c) custom alone  d) the army
**Answer: a) the constitution** — That is what this lesson teaches.
