# Federalism — matching

Match each word with its meaning.

**1.** federalism — a)

**2.** jurisdiction — b)

**3.** federation — c)


Meanings:
a) the vertical division of power among levels of government
b) the sphere in which a level of government may govern
c) a system of more than one constitutionally protected level of government

**Answer key:** 1. a; 2. b; 3. c
