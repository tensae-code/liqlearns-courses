# Federalism — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name India's two levels of government in its federal working.
**Answer:** The union (central) government and the state governments.

**2.** What three marks make a system federal, as the lesson describes it in general?
**Answer:** More than one level of government; each level with its own jurisdiction; and the division guaranteed by the constitution.
