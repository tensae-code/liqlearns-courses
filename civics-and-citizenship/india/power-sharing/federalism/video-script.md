# Video script — Federalism

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Federalism divides power vertically among levels of government — and india works as a federation of a centre and states, each governing in its own sphere.

Federalism is the vertical division of power: power is divided among levels of government, not held at one level alone.

Each level governs in its own sphere, and the constitution protects the division.

The lesson first describes federalism in general — what makes a system federal anywhere: more than one level, each with its own jurisdiction, guaranteed by the constitution.

Only then does it turn to any one country, so the idea is clear before the example.

India's own working is then studied: a union government and state governments dividing the country's governing between them.

The Indian case shows federalism alive — two levels of elected government answering to the same citizens.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
