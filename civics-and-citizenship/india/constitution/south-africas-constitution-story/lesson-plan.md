# Lesson plan — South Africa's Constitution Story

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — India · **Title:** The Constitution · **Lesson:** South Africa's Constitution Story
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — Democracy and the Constitution Founded (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** South Africa shows how a divided country designs a constitution together — apartheid ended, and black and white South Africans wrote their new rules as equals.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Apartheid and its end** — South Africa was ruled under apartheid, a system that divided people by race and denied black South Africans equal rights.
2. **Mandela's words — no domination** — Mandela's stand, in the book's own telling, was against domination of any kind: he had fought white domination, and he fought black domination too.
3. **Designing a constitution together** — South Africans of all races then designed their constitution together, as citizens of one country.

- Go back to the one thing: South Africa shows how a divided country designs a constitution together — apartheid ended, and black and white South Africans wrote their new rules as equals. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
