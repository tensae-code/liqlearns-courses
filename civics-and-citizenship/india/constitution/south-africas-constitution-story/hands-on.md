# Hands-on — South Africa's Constitution Story

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Imagine two groups in one country who have been enemies for years must now write the rules for living together. Write down: what must each side give up, and what must each side be promised? This lesson tells how South Africa actually did it.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: South Africa shows how a divided country designs a constitution together — apartheid ended, and black and white South Africans wrote their new rules as equals. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
