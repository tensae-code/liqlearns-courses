# South Africa's Constitution Story — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What was apartheid?
a) a system dividing people by race and denying equal rights  b) a festival of South African cultures  c) a trade agreement between countries  d) a system of free and equal voting
**Answer: a) a system dividing people by race and denying equal rights** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What did Mandela oppose, in the book's telling?
a) domination of any kind, white or black  b) only foreign rule  c) all forms of government  d) elections of every kind
**Answer: a) domination of any kind, white or black** — That is what this lesson teaches.
