# South Africa's Constitution Story — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why does the course learn constitution-making from South Africa's story?
**Answer:** Because South Africa had to build a constitution that former enemies could all accept, so the story shows clearly what a constitution is and why a country needs one.

**2.** What promise stands at the centre of the story?
**Answer:** That no group will dominate another: the fight was against domination itself, not for a new domination.
