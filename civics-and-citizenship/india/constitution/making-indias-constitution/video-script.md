This lesson is about one thing.
India's constitution was made by the people's representatives after independence, and it carries the values that frame all of india's democratic politics.

After independence, India's Constitution was framed by an assembly of the people's representatives, debating and deciding the country's basic rules.

It was not handed down by a ruler; it was made, deliberately, as the frame for a democratic India.

The Constitution carries foundational values — among them justice, liberty, equality and fraternity — that state what the country stands for.

These values are the standard against which later laws and governments are judged.

The Constitution provides the framework for democratic politics: it sets up the institutions, distributes power and guarantees rights.

Every later lesson in this course — elections, Parliament, rights — works inside this frame.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
