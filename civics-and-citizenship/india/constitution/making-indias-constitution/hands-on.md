# Hands-on — Making India's Constitution

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write down three rules you would put at the very top if you were founding a country — rules no future government could break. Keep your list beside you: this lesson shows what India's founders did with the same task.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: India's Constitution was made by the people's representatives after independence, and it carries the values that frame all of India's democratic politics. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
