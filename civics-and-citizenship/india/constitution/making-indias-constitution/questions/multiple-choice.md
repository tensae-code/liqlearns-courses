# Making India's Constitution — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Who made India's Constitution?
a) an assembly of the people's representatives  b) a single king  c) a foreign government  d) the army
**Answer: a) an assembly of the people's representatives** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does the Constitution provide for democratic politics?
a) the framework within which it works  b) a list of election results  c) the names of future ministers  d) a timetable of festivals
**Answer: a) the framework within which it works** — That is what this lesson teaches.
