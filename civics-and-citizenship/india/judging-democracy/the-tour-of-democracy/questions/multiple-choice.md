# The Tour of Democracy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What do the two books together form, by their own naming?
a) a tour of democracy  b) two unrelated subjects  c) a history of elections only  d) a guide to Parliament alone
**Answer: a) a tour of democracy** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What carries the argument forward from chapter to chapter?
a) each chapter's opening overview  b) the exercise pages  c) the cartoons alone  d) the covers
**Answer: a) each chapter's opening overview** — That is what this lesson teaches.
