# Video script — The Tour of Democracy

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The two books are one course — a tour of democracy from what democracy is to what challenges it — and each chapter's overview carries the argument one step forward.

The books name their own design: Classes 9 and 10 together form a single tour of democracy.

The first book founds the frame; the second tests and deepens it — neither half is the whole course alone.

The tour runs in one direction: from what democracy is and why it is worth having, through constitution, elections, institutions and rights, to power-sharing, diversity, parties, outcomes and challenges.

Each stop answers the question the stop before it raised.

Each chapter opens with an overview that carries the argument forward from the chapters before it.

Reading the overviews in a row is reading the tour's map — the design this whole course has followed.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
