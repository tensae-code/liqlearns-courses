# Challenges to Democracy — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the three kinds of challenge the chapter sets out.
**Answer:** The foundational challenge of transition, the challenge of expansion, and the challenge of deepening democracy.

**2.** How does the chapter end — in complaint or in reform?
**Answer:** In reform: it asks what can be done to reform democratic politics, with the formed citizen as the instrument.
