# Challenges to Democracy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does the final chapter ask after naming democracy's challenges?
a) what can be done to reform democratic politics  b) whether to abandon democracy  c) which country is perfect  d) when elections should stop
**Answer: a) what can be done to reform democratic politics** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which is one of the challenge kinds the chapter names?
a) the challenge of deepening democracy  b) the challenge of forgetting the constitution  c) the challenge of ending all parties  d) the challenge of banning movements
**Answer: a) the challenge of deepening democracy** — That is what this lesson teaches.
