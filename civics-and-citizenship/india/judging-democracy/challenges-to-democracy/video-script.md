# Video script — Challenges to Democracy

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Democracy faces challenges in india and elsewhere — and the course ends by asking what can be done to reform democratic politics.

The concluding chapter gathers both years of the tour: everything studied — constitution, elections, institutions, rights, parties, outcomes — is now brought to one question.

That question is: what challenges does this democracy face?

The challenges are named for India and for democracies elsewhere: the founding challenge of making the transition, the challenge of expansion, and the challenge of deepening democracy.

Each challenge belongs to a stage — and India's own stage can be judged from the list.

The chapter does not end in complaint: it asks what can be done to reform democratic politics.

The citizen it has been forming is the answer's instrument — challenges are met by citizens who understand the system they must repair.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
