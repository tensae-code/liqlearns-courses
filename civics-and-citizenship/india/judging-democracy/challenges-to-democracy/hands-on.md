# Hands-on — Challenges to Democracy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Write down the biggest weakness you see in how your town or country is governed, and one reform that could fix it. Hold both in mind: the whole course has been walking toward this exact exercise.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: democracy faces challenges in India and elsewhere — and the course ends by asking what can be done to reform democratic politics. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street, or in the public life of your town. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
