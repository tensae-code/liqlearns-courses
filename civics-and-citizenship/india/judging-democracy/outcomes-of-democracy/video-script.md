# Video script — Outcomes of Democracy

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Democracy is judged by its outcomes — what it actually does for accountable government, dignity and people's lives — not by its name alone.

The chapter steps back and asks the general questions: what does democracy do, and what outcomes can we expect from it?

After two years of studying democracy's parts, the course now weighs the whole.

A democracy is judged by its outcomes — whether it produces accountable and legitimate government and answers people's expectations.

The test is practical: not what democracy promises in speeches, but what it delivers in citizens' lives.

The weighing is honest: democracy is compared with what other systems actually deliver, not with a perfect imaginary system.

That fair comparison — outcome against outcome — is the chapter's method and its conclusion's strength.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
