# Outcomes of Democracy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** By what is democracy judged in this chapter?

**2.** What is the fair comparison the chapter makes?
a) democracy's outcomes against other systems' actual outcomes  b) democracy against a perfect imaginary system  c) one party against another  d) one election against another
