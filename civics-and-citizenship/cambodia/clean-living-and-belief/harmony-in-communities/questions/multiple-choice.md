# Harmony in Communities — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Living in agreement inside one's communities.  b) it has nothing to do with how Cambodia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Living in agreement inside one's communities.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Living in agreement inside one's communities.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Cambodia
**Answer: a) Living in agreement inside one's communities.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
