# Children's Rights — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The rights of the child and their protection.  b) it has nothing to do with how Cambodia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The rights of the child and their protection.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) The rights of the child and their protection.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Cambodia
**Answer: a) The rights of the child and their protection.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
