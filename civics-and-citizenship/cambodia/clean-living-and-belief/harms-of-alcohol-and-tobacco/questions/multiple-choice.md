# Harms of Alcohol and Cigarettes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The damage alcohol and tobacco do and how to refuse them.  b) it has nothing to do with how Cambodia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The damage alcohol and tobacco do and how to refuse them.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) The damage alcohol and tobacco do and how to refuse them.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Cambodia
**Answer: a) The damage alcohol and tobacco do and how to refuse them.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
