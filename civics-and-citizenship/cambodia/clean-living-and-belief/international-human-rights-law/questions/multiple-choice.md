# International Law on Human Rights — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The international law that guards human rights.  b) it has nothing to do with how Cambodia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The international law that guards human rights.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) The international law that guards human rights.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Cambodia
**Answer: a) The international law that guards human rights.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
