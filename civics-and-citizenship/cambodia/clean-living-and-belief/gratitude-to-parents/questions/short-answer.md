# Gratitude to Parents — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In your own words, what does this lesson teach?
**Answer:** The debt of gratitude to mother and father — the mother's care in pregnancy and birth, the parents as first teachers, and repaying their goodness.

**2.** In your own words, state this lesson's one thing.
**Answer:** The debt of gratitude to mother and father — the mother's care in pregnancy and birth, the parents as first teachers, and repaying their goodness.
