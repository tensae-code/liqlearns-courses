# Decision-Making — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) How decisions are made and owned.  b) it has nothing to do with how Cambodia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) How decisions are made and owned.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) How decisions are made and owned.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Cambodia
**Answer: a) How decisions are made and owned.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
