# Responsibility and Discipline — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Owning one's acts and the discipline that holds them.  b) it has nothing to do with how Cambodia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Owning one's acts and the discipline that holds them.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Owning one's acts and the discipline that holds them.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Cambodia
**Answer: a) Owning one's acts and the discipline that holds them.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
