# Rights and Law — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Rights as the law protects them.  b) it has nothing to do with how Cambodia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Rights as the law protects them.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Rights as the law protects them.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Cambodia
**Answer: a) Rights as the law protects them.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
