# World Environment — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The shared environment of the world and its care.  b) it has nothing to do with how Cambodia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The shared environment of the world and its care.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) The shared environment of the world and its care.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Cambodia
**Answer: a) The shared environment of the world and its care.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
