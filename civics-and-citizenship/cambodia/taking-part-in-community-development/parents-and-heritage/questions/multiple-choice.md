# Parents and Heritage — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Parents and the heritage handed down.  b) it has nothing to do with how Cambodia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Parents and the heritage handed down.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Parents and the heritage handed down.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Cambodia
**Answer: a) Parents and the heritage handed down.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
