# Peaceful Community — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The community that lives in peace.  b) it has nothing to do with how Cambodia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The community that lives in peace.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) The community that lives in peace.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Cambodia
**Answer: a) The community that lives in peace.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
