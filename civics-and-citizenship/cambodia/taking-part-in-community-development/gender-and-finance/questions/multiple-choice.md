# Gender and Finance — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Gender and household finance as community life.  b) it has nothing to do with how Cambodia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Gender and household finance as community life.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Gender and household finance as community life.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Cambodia
**Answer: a) Gender and household finance as community life.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
