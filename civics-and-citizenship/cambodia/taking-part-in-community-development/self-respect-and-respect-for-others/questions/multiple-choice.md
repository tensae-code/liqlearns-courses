# Self-Respect and Respect for Others — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Respecting oneself and others as the ground of good relations.  b) it has nothing to do with how Cambodia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Respecting oneself and others as the ground of good relations.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Respecting oneself and others as the ground of good relations.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Cambodia
**Answer: a) Respecting oneself and others as the ground of good relations.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
