# Labour Law — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The law that governs work, workers and employers.  b) it has nothing to do with how Cambodia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The law that governs work, workers and employers.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) The law that governs work, workers and employers.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Cambodia
**Answer: a) The law that governs work, workers and employers.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
