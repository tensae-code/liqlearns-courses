Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Equality and inequality — their causes in wealth and poverty, their effects, and how inequality is reduced.  b) it has nothing to do with how Cambodia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Equality and inequality — their causes in wealth and poverty, their effects, and how inequality is reduced.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
