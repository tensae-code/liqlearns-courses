# Equality and Inequality

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Equality and Inequality. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Cambodia's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Equality and inequality — their causes in wealth and poverty, their effects, and how inequality is reduced.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Equality and inequality — their causes in wealth and poverty, their effects, and how inequality is reduced. Give one real example from your own life or from the Cambodia you live in, and name the lesson's key words: equality, and. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
