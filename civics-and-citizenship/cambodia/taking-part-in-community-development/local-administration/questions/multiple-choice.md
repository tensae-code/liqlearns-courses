# Local Administration — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Administration at the local level, closest to the citizen.  b) it has nothing to do with how Cambodia governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Administration at the local level, closest to the citizen.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Administration at the local level, closest to the citizen.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Cambodia
**Answer: a) Administration at the local level, closest to the citizen.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
