# Community Problems and Solutions

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Community Problems and Solutions. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Cambodia's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Naming a community's problems and working out their solutions.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Naming a community's problems and working out their solutions. Give one real example from your own life or from the Cambodia you live in, and name the lesson's key words: community, problems. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
