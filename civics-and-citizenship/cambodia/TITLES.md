# Civics and Citizenship — Cambodia course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

Civics belongs to the country: this course is Cambodia's
own — built from Cambodia's សីលធម៌-ពលរដ្ឋវិជ្ជា
(Morality–Civics) books (Ministry of Education, Youth and
Sport (MoEYS), Khmer): the student books for Grades 11
and 12 and the Grades 10–12 curriculum framework, all
PARTIAL as filed. Inside the course the organisation is
by **levels, not grades**: the titles below ARE the
levels' titles, in path order — what the learner proves,
step by step. The grades the books come from live only in
each lesson's sources.

The path's own logic is Cambodia's, from the filing's
ladder sketch: one subject in a repeating three-chapter
frame — Human Value, Clean Living, Taking Part in
Community/Social Development — the same frame in Grades
11 and 12, going deeper each year: values and character
first, then belief, health and rights in daily life, then
democracy, law codes and the state. Every lesson in the
student books opens with an objectives box ("at the end
of this lesson, students can…") and teaches by
definition, typology lists, and Cambodian examples. The
Grade 10 detailed study programme (Book 3, the ministry
framework) lays out the same frame where it starts, in a
four-chapter form (human value, good relations in the
community, culture and religion, our society); its
Grade 10 lessons stock into the three titles by their
own subjects, in grade order — framework first, then the
Grade 11 book, then the Grade 12 book — inside each
level. So Level 1 is the frame's first chapter, Level 2
its second, Level 3 its third. That ladder IS the path
order (recommended, never forced — a learner can jump in
higher; the "start here instead" hints point down this
ladder).

The coined lesson names in the filing were deduped
before building, by lesson name: 104 placements stock
103 lessons. One coined name is used by two books —
Creation (Grade 10 framework, printed p. 9; Grade 12
book, printed p. 6) — and stocks into exactly one
lesson, which sits at Level 1 and carries its two steps
as sub lessons. The frame's other returns are coined
differently by the books themselves — Harms of Alcohol
and Cigarettes (Grade 11) and Harms of Drinking Alcohol
and Smoking (Grade 10 framework), Citizens' Rights
(Grade 11) and Citizens' Rights and Duties (Grade 10
framework), Judicial Institutions of the Kingdom of
Cambodia (Grade 12) and Cambodia's Judicial Organisation
(Grade 10 framework) — and stand as separate lessons at
their own levels, exactly as coined. Every placement
stocks into exactly one lesson, and each placement's
provenance row (book + printed page, exactly as filed)
is in that lesson's `lesson/sources.md`. Unit folders
are the filing's own theme names — one per level here,
as Cambodia's own chapter frame gives one chapter per
level.
