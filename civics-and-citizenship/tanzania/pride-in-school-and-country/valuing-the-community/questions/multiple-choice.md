# Valuing the Community — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Valuing the community the pupil lives in.  b) it has nothing to do with how Tanzania governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Valuing the community the pupil lives in.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Valuing the community the pupil lives in.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Tanzania
**Answer: a) Valuing the community the pupil lives in.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
