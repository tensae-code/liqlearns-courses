# Loving and Being Proud of Your Country — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Loving and being proud of one's country and promoting that pride.  b) it has nothing to do with how Tanzania governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Loving and being proud of one's country and promoting that pride.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Loving and being proud of one's country and promoting that pride.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Tanzania
**Answer: a) Loving and being proud of one's country and promoting that pride.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
