# Proud of Your School, Loving Your Country — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** What brings a school a good name, pride in the school, and valuing Tanzania's money and national symbols — the chapter works through notes and coins, the giraffe, elephant, coat of arms and the president's portrait.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that Proud of Your School, Loving Your Country — and that it has no connection to the rest of Tanzania's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Tanzania's own course, in its own order.
