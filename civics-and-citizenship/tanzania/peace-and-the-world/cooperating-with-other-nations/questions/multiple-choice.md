# Cooperating with Other Nations — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Cooperating with other nations.  b) it has nothing to do with how Tanzania governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Cooperating with other nations.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Cooperating with other nations.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Tanzania
**Answer: a) Cooperating with other nations.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
