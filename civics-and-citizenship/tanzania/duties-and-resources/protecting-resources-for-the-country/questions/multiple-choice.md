# Protecting Resources in the Interest of Our Country — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Protecting resources for the country's development.  b) it has nothing to do with how Tanzania governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Protecting resources for the country's development.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Protecting resources for the country's development.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Tanzania
**Answer: a) Protecting resources for the country's development.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
