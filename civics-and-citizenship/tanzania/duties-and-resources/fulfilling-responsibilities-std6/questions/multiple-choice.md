# Fulfilling Responsibilities — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Identifying and fulfilling one's responsibilities effectively.  b) it has nothing to do with how Tanzania governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Identifying and fulfilling one's responsibilities effectively.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Identifying and fulfilling one's responsibilities effectively.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Tanzania
**Answer: a) Identifying and fulfilling one's responsibilities effectively.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
