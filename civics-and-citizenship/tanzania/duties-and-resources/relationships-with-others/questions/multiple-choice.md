# Building Good Relationships with Others — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Building good relationships with fellow pupils and the community.  b) it has nothing to do with how Tanzania governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Building good relationships with fellow pupils and the community.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Building good relationships with fellow pupils and the community.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Tanzania
**Answer: a) Building good relationships with fellow pupils and the community.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
