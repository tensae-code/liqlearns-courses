# Civics and Citizenship — Tanzania course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

Civics belongs to the country: this course is Tanzania's own —
built from Tanzania's Civic and Moral Education / Uraia na
Maadili pupil's books for Standards 3–7 (Tanzania Institute
of Education, in English and Kiswahili). Inside the course
the organisation is by **levels, not grades**: the titles
below ARE the levels' titles, in path order — what the
learner proves, step by step. The grades the books come
from live only in each lesson's sources.

The path's own logic is Tanzania's, reconciled from the two
filings' ladder sketches: the youngest books (Standards 3
and 4) teach belonging and good habits first — love of
society, school and country, responsibility, honesty, peace —
with democracy and law appearing inside that frame at
Standard 4; the Standards 5–7 books then run their own
six-step ladder: love and care first (every book opens
there — it is Tanzania's foundation), pride in school and
country, government and democracy, duties and resources,
law and character, and finally rights, peace and the world.
That ladder IS the path order (recommended, never forced —
a learner can jump in higher; the "start here instead"
hints point down this ladder).
