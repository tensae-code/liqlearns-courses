# Democracy in Tanzania — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) How democracy is practised in Tanzania.  b) it has nothing to do with how Tanzania governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) How democracy is practised in Tanzania.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) How democracy is practised in Tanzania.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Tanzania
**Answer: a) How democracy is practised in Tanzania.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
