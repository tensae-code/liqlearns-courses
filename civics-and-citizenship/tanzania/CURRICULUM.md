# Civics and Citizenship — Tanzania course — CURRICULUM (the path)

One course for Tanzania inside the Civics and Citizenship
folder, organised by **levels, not grades**: a learner climbs
when they have proved the level, not when a school calendar
says so. The school grades the books come from appear only in
each lesson's sources (provenance), never as the course's
organisation. The path follows Tanzania's own order as its
books lay it out, reconciled from the two filings' ladder
sketches: the youngest books (Standards 3 and 4) teach
belonging and good habits first — love of society, school
and country, responsibility, honesty, peace — with democracy
and law appearing inside that frame at Standard 4; the
Standards 5–7 books then run their own six-step ladder:
love and care first (every book opens there — civics begins
with love, of self, family, school and country, and only
then meets government), pride in school and country,
government and democracy, duties and resources, law and
character, and finally rights, peace and the world. The
filings for this build (`tanzania-placements.md` — 4 records
/ 3 distinct books, all read in full; and
`tanzania-placements-b.md` — the same census records plus 87
link leads triaged, adding the Standard 3 and 4 books, which
were NOT in the census, read and placed there, and the
English Standard 5 as record-only) stock 62 placements into
60 lessons: the coined lesson names were deduped before
building — two lessons are stocked by two placements each
(the Standard 3 and Standard 4 passes of the same teaching:
loving our school, and loving our country) — and every
placement stocks into exactly one lesson.

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in
this course comes from government textbooks — this country's
own Civic and Moral Education / Uraia na Maadili books —
except the **Tip boxes**: a tip in a box is from us, the
people making LiqLearns. Tips are ours; the lessons are the
books'. See ABOUT.md for the full book list and the
acceptance reckoning. **All 60 lessons stand on student
books; no lesson in this course stands on a teacher guide
or a programme.**

## The levels (the path, in order)

1. **Belonging and Good Habits** — the youngest book in the set opens here: loving society, school and country, valuing society, then the habits of a good member of the community — responsibility, handling difficulties, honesty — and keeping peace, before any institution is named. 8 lessons, in 3 title(s): `belonging/` (4 lessons); `conduct/` (3 lessons); `peace/` (1 lesson).
2. **Widening the Circle — Democracy and Law Appear** — the circle widens: love of self and others, school and country again at greater depth, living with people of different origins — and democracy and law appear for the first time, inside the same love-and-habits frame. 9 lessons, in 4 title(s): `belonging/` (2 lessons); `democracy/` (1 lesson); `conduct/` (5 lessons); `law/` (1 lesson).
3. **Love and Care** — every book from Standard Five on opens here — loving self, family and others, and caring, including health; it is Tanzania's foundation. 6 lessons, in 1 title(s): `love-and-care/` (6 lessons).
4. **Pride in School and Country** — belonging widens from family to school to nation: school pride, national symbols and holidays, valuing the community. 5 lessons, in 1 title(s): `pride-in-school-and-country/` (5 lessons).
5. **Government and Democracy** — the state appears only after belonging, and is taught concretely: government leadership from ward level, the structure of the United Republic's government, democracy in Tanzania. 4 lessons, in 1 title(s): `government-and-democracy/` (4 lessons).
6. **Duties and Resources** — the citizen's work, placed after the state is known: responsibilities, protecting public and national resources, the environment, relationships. 9 lessons, in 1 title(s): `duties-and-resources/` (9 lessons).
7. **Law and Character** — the formed character that keeps the law willingly: obeying laws, patience, goals, self-discipline, tolerance, trustworthiness, critical learning. 13 lessons, in 2 title(s): `character/` (3 lessons); `law-and-character/` (10 lessons).
8. **Rights, Peace and the World** — the widest circle closes every book in the set: keeping peace, observing then protecting human rights, relations and friendships with other countries. 6 lessons, in 2 title(s): `peace-and-the-world/` (2 lessons); `rights-and-the-world/` (4 lessons).

**Totals: 60 lessons** (Level 1: 8 · Level 2: 9 · Level 3: 6 · Level 4: 5 · Level 5: 4 · Level 6: 9 · Level 7: 13 · Level 8: 6 — from five read student books: *Civic and Moral Education*, Standard 3 (Tanzania Institute of Education, English edition 2019, second edition 2021, ISBN 978-9987-09-340-3, with its Kiswahili parallel edition, COMPLETE), *Civic and Moral Education*, Standard 4 (Tanzania Institute of Education, English edition 2019, second edition 2021, ISBN 978-9987-09-341-0, with its Kiswahili parallel edition, COMPLETE), *Uraia na Maadili*, Darasa la Tano (Taasisi ya Elimu Tanzania, 2021, Kiswahili, COMPLETE), *Civic and Moral Education / Uraia na Maadili*, Standard 6 (Tanzania Institute of Education, 2021, English with Kiswahili parallel edition, COMPLETE), and *Civic and Moral Education*, Standard Seven (Tanzania Institute of Education, 2021, English, COMPLETE); 62 placements stocking 60 lessons).

## Where the build stands

- **Course frame — BUILT** (this CURRICULUM, TITLES.md,
  ABOUT.md, and the folder README entry).
- **Levels 1–8 — BUILT**: 60 lessons, stocked from 62
  placements in the five read student books (Standards
  3–7).
- **Not built, named plainly**: the Standard 3 book's
  assignments and its patriotic-song / national-anthem
  notation are inventory-only in the filing (named, not
  placed) and are not lessons; the English Standard 5
  edition is record-only in the filings (placed once, from
  the Kiswahili edition) and adds no lesson of its own. The
  filings contain no other Tanzania book — no teacher guide,
  no programme, and no UNREAD book. Nothing else is in the
  course, and no lesson pretends to one (see ABOUT.md).
