# Achieving Goals — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The importance of setting goals and avoiding hypocrisy in pursuing them.  b) it has nothing to do with how Tanzania governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The importance of setting goals and avoiding hypocrisy in pursuing them.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) The importance of setting goals and avoiding hypocrisy in pursuing them.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Tanzania
**Answer: a) The importance of setting goals and avoiding hypocrisy in pursuing them.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
