# Obeying Laws and Regulations — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Obeying the laws and regulations governing the country.  b) it has nothing to do with how Tanzania governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Obeying the laws and regulations governing the country.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Obeying the laws and regulations governing the country.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Tanzania
**Answer: a) Obeying the laws and regulations governing the country.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
