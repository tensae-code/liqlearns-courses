# Building Friendships with Other Countries — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Friendship with other countries and coping with the challenges of globalisation.  b) it has nothing to do with how Tanzania governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Friendship with other countries and coping with the challenges of globalisation.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Friendship with other countries and coping with the challenges of globalisation.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Tanzania
**Answer: a) Friendship with other countries and coping with the challenges of globalisation.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
