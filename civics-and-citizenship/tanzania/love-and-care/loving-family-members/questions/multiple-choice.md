# Loving Family Members — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The concept of family and the different needs of family members, and loving them.  b) it has nothing to do with how Tanzania governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The concept of family and the different needs of family members, and loving them.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) The concept of family and the different needs of family members, and loving them.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Tanzania
**Answer: a) The concept of family and the different needs of family members, and loving them.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
