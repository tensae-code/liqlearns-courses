# Loving Yourself and Others — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Acts that show love for oneself and for other people, as the first civic virtue.  b) it has nothing to do with how Tanzania governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Acts that show love for oneself and for other people, as the first civic virtue.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Acts that show love for oneself and for other people, as the first civic virtue.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Tanzania
**Answer: a) Acts that show love for oneself and for other people, as the first civic virtue.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
