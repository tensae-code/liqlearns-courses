# Caring for Yourself and Others — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Caring for oneself and caring for other people, including avoiding harm to children and speaking against abuse.  b) it has nothing to do with how Tanzania governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Caring for oneself and caring for other people, including avoiding harm to children and speaking against abuse.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**Answer: a) Caring for oneself and caring for other people, including avoiding harm to children and speaking against abuse.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
