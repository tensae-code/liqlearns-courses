# Loving Others in the Community — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Loving others in the community and educating the community, with ways of promoting that love.  b) it has nothing to do with how Tanzania governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Loving others in the community and educating the community, with ways of promoting that love.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Loving others in the community and educating the community, with ways of promoting that love.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Tanzania
**Answer: a) Loving others in the community and educating the community, with ways of promoting that love.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
