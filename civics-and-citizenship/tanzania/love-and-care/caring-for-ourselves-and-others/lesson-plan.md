# Lesson plan — Caring for Ourselves and Others

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Tanzania · **Title:** Love and Care · **Lesson:** Caring for Ourselves and Others
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Love and Care (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Caring for self and others, including reproductive health education. This lesson stands on a student book.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 3 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: Caring for self and others, including reproductive health education.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, school or town — or, for this book's lessons, from the public life of Tanzania they live in.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the learner's own life in Tanzania and the book's own teaching, as the book itself works.
