# Trustworthiness — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Being trustworthy and of good conduct.  b) it has nothing to do with how Tanzania governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Being trustworthy and of good conduct.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Being trustworthy and of good conduct.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Tanzania
**Answer: a) Being trustworthy and of good conduct.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
