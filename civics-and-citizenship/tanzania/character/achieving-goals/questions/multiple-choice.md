# Achieving Goals — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Setting and reaching goals.  b) it has nothing to do with how Tanzania governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Setting and reaching goals.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Setting and reaching goals.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Tanzania
**Answer: a) Setting and reaching goals.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
