# Loving Our School — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Loving your school: keeping a good environment for learning, and loving and promoting the status of our school.  b) it has nothing to do with how Tanzania governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Loving your school: keeping a good environment for learning, and loving and promoting the status of our school.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Loving your school: keeping a good environment for learning, and loving and promoting the status of our school.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Tanzania
**Answer: a) Loving your school: keeping a good environment for learning, and loving and promoting the status of our school.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
