# Living with People of Different Origins — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Living with people of different origins.  b) it has nothing to do with how Tanzania governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Living with people of different origins.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Living with people of different origins.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Tanzania
**Answer: a) Living with people of different origins.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
