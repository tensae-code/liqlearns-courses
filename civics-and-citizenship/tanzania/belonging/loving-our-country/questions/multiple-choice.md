# Loving Our Country — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Loving our country and the good things about it, and behaving in ways that show it.  b) it has nothing to do with how Tanzania governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Loving our country and the good things about it, and behaving in ways that show it.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Loving our country and the good things about it, and behaving in ways that show it.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Tanzania
**Answer: a) Loving our country and the good things about it, and behaving in ways that show it.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
