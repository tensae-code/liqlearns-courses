# Civics and Citizenship — Algeria course — CURRICULUM (the path)

One course for Algeria inside the Civics and Citizenship folder,
organised by **levels, not grades**: a learner climbs when they
have proved the level, not when a school calendar says so. The
school grade the book comes from appears only in each lesson's
sources (provenance), never as the course's organisation. The path
follows the book's own order: heritage first (belonging), then
dialogue and living together (conduct with others), then school
and property (the child's nearest institutions and the
mine/everyone's distinction), as Algeria's Grade 4 Civic
Education book lays it out.

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in this
course comes from government textbooks — this country's own
Civic Education book — except the **Tip boxes**: a tip in a box
is from us, the people making LiqLearns. Tips are ours; the
lessons are the books'. See ABOUT.md for the full book list, the
contents map, and the acceptance reckoning.

## The levels (the path, in order)

1. **Our Heritage → Dialogue and Living Together → School and
   Property** — our heritage is a treasure: its landmarks, the
   old Casbah, material and intangible heritage, Tabriza,
   Algeria's nature reserves, the duty to preserve it with
   pride, and UNESCO; then dialogue and why it matters, how to
   talk well, running dialogue in class, dialogue through
   difficulties, helping resolve conflicts, and racial
   discrimination named and refused; then the primary, middle
   and secondary schools, private property, public property,
   and cleaning the school as shared work. 21 lessons, in three
   titles: `our-heritage/` (9 lessons), then
   `dialogue-and-living-together/` (6 lessons), then
   `school-and-property/` (6 lessons).

**Totals: 21 lessons** (Level 1: 21 — all from one student book).
