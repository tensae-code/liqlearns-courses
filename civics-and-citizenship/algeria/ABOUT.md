# About the Civics and Citizenship — Algeria course

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in this
course comes from government textbooks — this country's own
Civic Education book for the 4th Year Primary. The one
exception is the **Tip boxes**: a tip in a box is from us, the
people making LiqLearns. Tips are ours; the lessons are the
books'.

## How this course is organised

This is ONE course for Algeria inside the Civics and Citizenship
folder (one course per country). Inside it, the organisation is by
**levels, not grades**: a learner's place is found by what they
prove, and each level stands on the ones before it as a
recommended-first path — never a forced gate. The school grade the
book comes from appears only in each lesson's
`lesson/sources.md`, as provenance.

The course as built has **one level**:

1. **Level 1 — heritage, dialogue, school and property.**
   Algeria's primary civics, as its Grade 4 book teaches it, runs
   in three fields: first Our Heritage (what the homeland holds
   and hands down — landmarks, the old Casbah, material and
   intangible heritage, a named heritage item, nature reserves,
   the duty to preserve it, and UNESCO); then Dialogue and Living
   Together (dialogue and its etiquette, dialogue in class,
   dialogue through difficulties, resolving conflicts, and racial
   discrimination named and refused); then School and Property
   (the primary, middle and secondary schools as the pupil's own
   ladder, private property, public property, and cleaning the
   school as shared work). All 21 lessons stand on a **student
   book** and count in the acceptance reckoning below.

**Course-intro video script** (written here, made later; the
book's own shape, walked in order):

- *Heritage, then dialogue, then school:* "Start with what your
  homeland holds: its heritage is a treasure — guard it and be
  proud of it. Then learn to live with others: talk, listen,
  settle conflicts, refuse discrimination. Then your own nearest
  world: your school ladder, what is yours, what is everyone's,
  and the shared work that keeps both."

## Honesty notes (course level)

- **PARTIAL by scope, carried exactly as filed:** this course is
  built from ONE book only — Algeria's Grade 4 Civic Education
  student book. No other Algeria book is in hand for this
  course, so the course holds only what that one book's three
  fields teach. The ladder stops at Level 1 because the book in
  hand stops there — not because Algeria teaches nothing more.
  Other grades of the same series are named in the group log as
  leads not read this pass; the ladder above Level 1 is sketched
  by their grade order only, and no lesson is built from them.

  "أقوم تعلماتي" (I assess my learning) pages (printed
  pp. 18, 29, 38) are the book's own assessment items — named in
  the contents map above, per the standing exclusions, and feed
  no lesson.

  and no ISBN. None is claimed anywhere in this course.
- **Image-only scan:** the book is an image-only scan, read in
  full from rendered pages, as filed. Nothing is transcribed —
  every lesson is written in our own words from the filings'
  teaching summaries.
