# Hands-on — The Old Casbah

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Picture an old neighbourhood: narrow streets, old houses close together, people living their daily life among buildings from long ago. That is the Casbah. Say one way an old quarter is different from a new street.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the old Casbah quarter is living heritage — an old neighbourhood where the homeland's past is still lived in today. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
