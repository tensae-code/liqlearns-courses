# Video script — The Old Casbah

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The old casbah quarter is living heritage — an old neighbourhood where the homeland's past is still lived in today.

The Casbah (حي القصبة العتيق) is the old quarter — its houses, streets and ways of life come from earlier generations, yet people still live there today. It is heritage you can walk through.

Because the Casbah is both a home and a piece of the homeland's heritage, caring for it means two things at once: respecting the people who live there and protecting the old quarter itself.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
