# The Old Casbah

## Do it first

Picture an old neighbourhood: narrow streets, old houses close together, people living their daily life among buildings from long ago. That is the Casbah. Say one way an old quarter is different from a new street.

## An old quarter that is still alive

The Casbah (حي القصبة العتيق) is the old quarter — its houses, streets and ways of life come from earlier generations, yet people still live there today. It is heritage you can walk through.

## Living heritage must be cared for

Because the Casbah is both a home and a piece of the homeland's heritage, caring for it means two things at once: respecting the people who live there and protecting the old quarter itself.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the old Casbah quarter is living heritage — an old neighbourhood where the homeland's past is still lived in today. Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood, and name the lesson's key words: Casbah, old quarter, living heritage. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
