# The Old Casbah — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the Casbah?
**Answer:** The old quarter — an old neighbourhood whose houses, streets and ways come from earlier generations, where people still live today.

**2.** How do we care for the Casbah?
**Answer:** By respecting the people who live there and protecting the old quarter itself.
