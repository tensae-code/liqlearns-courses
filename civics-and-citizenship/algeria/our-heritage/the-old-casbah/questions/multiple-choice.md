# The Old Casbah — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) the old Casbah quarter is living heritage — an old neighbourhood where the homeland's past is still lived in today  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What makes the Casbah living heritage?
a) people still live in the old quarter today  b) it is empty and no one may enter  c) it was built last year  d) it is a picture in a book only
**Answer: a) people still live in the old quarter today** — That is what this lesson teaches.
