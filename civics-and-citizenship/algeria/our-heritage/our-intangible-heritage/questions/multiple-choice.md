# Our Intangible Heritage — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) part of our heritage is intangible — customs, arts and knowledge handed down without being things: songs, stories, celebrations and skills carried in people  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these is intangible heritage?
a) a traditional song taught from elder to child  b) an old clay pot  c) a stone ruin  d) an old house
**Answer: a) a traditional song taught from elder to child** — That is what this lesson teaches.
