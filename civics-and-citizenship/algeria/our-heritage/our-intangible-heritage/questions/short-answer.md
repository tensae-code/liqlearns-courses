# Our Intangible Heritage — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is intangible heritage?
**Answer:** Customs, arts and knowledge handed down without being things — songs, stories, celebrations and skills carried in people.

**2.** How is intangible heritage kept alive?
**Answer:** By practising it, teaching it and joining in, so it passes to the next generation.
