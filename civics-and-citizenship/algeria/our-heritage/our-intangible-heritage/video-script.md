# Video script — Our Intangible Heritage

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Part of our heritage is intangible — customs, arts and knowledge handed down without being things: songs, stories, celebrations and skills carried in people.

Intangible heritage (تراثنا اللامادي) is heritage that is not a thing: customs, music, stories, celebrations, crafts-knowledge and ways of doing that live in people and pass from person to person.

A pot survives in a cupboard; a song survives only while people sing it. Intangible heritage is kept by practising it, teaching it and joining in — that is how it reaches the next generation.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
