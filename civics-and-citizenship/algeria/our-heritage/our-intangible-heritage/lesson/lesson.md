# Our Intangible Heritage

## Do it first

Think of a song, a celebration or a skill that older people taught younger people in your family — without any object changing hands. This lesson names that kind of heritage: our intangible heritage.

## Heritage you cannot touch, but can learn

Intangible heritage (تراثنا اللامادي) is heritage that is not a thing: customs, music, stories, celebrations, crafts-knowledge and ways of doing that live in people and pass from person to person.

## It lives only while people practise it

A pot survives in a cupboard; a song survives only while people sing it. Intangible heritage is kept by practising it, teaching it and joining in — that is how it reaches the next generation.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: part of our heritage is intangible — customs, arts and knowledge handed down without being things: songs, stories, celebrations and skills carried in people. Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood, and name the lesson's key words: intangible heritage, customs, handed down. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
