# Hands-on — Our Intangible Heritage

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Think of a song, a celebration or a skill that older people taught younger people in your family — without any object changing hands. This lesson names that kind of heritage: our intangible heritage.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: part of our heritage is intangible — customs, arts and knowledge handed down without being things: songs, stories, celebrations and skills carried in people. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
