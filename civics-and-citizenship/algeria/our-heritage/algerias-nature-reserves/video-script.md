# Video script — Algeria's Nature Reserves

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Algeria's nature reserves are protected places — parts of the homeland's nature kept safe, and counted as national heritage.

A nature reserve (محمية طبيعية) is a place set apart and protected by the country, so its plants, animals and landscapes stay safe. Algeria's reserves (المحميات الطبيعية في الجزائر) guard the homeland's living nature.

The book places the reserves inside the heritage field: the homeland's nature is part of what past generations hand to us, and protecting it is part of keeping the national heritage whole.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
