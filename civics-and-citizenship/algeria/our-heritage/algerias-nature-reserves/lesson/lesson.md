# Algeria's Nature Reserves

## Do it first

Think of a wild place — mountains, forest, desert or coast — where animals and plants live freely. Now imagine a fence of rules around it that says: this place is protected. This lesson names those places: Algeria's nature reserves.

## Nature the homeland protects on purpose

A nature reserve (محمية طبيعية) is a place set apart and protected by the country, so its plants, animals and landscapes stay safe. Algeria's reserves (المحميات الطبيعية في الجزائر) guard the homeland's living nature.

## Protected nature is heritage too

The book places the reserves inside the heritage field: the homeland's nature is part of what past generations hand to us, and protecting it is part of keeping the national heritage whole.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Algeria's nature reserves are protected places — parts of the homeland's nature kept safe, and counted as national heritage. Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood, and name the lesson's key words: nature reserves, protected places, Algeria. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
