# Algeria's Nature Reserves — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is a nature reserve?
**Answer:** A place set apart and protected by the country so its plants, animals and landscapes stay safe.

**2.** Why do the reserves belong in the heritage field?
**Answer:** Because the homeland's nature is part of what is handed down to us, and protecting it keeps the national heritage whole.
