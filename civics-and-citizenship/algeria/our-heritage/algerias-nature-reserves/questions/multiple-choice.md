# Algeria's Nature Reserves — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) Algeria's nature reserves are protected places — parts of the homeland's nature kept safe, and counted as national heritage  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Why does a country make a nature reserve?
a) to keep a place's plants, animals and landscapes safe  b) to build shops on the land  c) so no one ever learns about nature  d) to empty the place of animals
**Answer: a) to keep a place's plants, animals and landscapes safe** — That is what this lesson teaches.
