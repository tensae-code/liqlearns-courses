# Tabriza — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) Tabriza (التبرزة) is a named item of my homeland's traditional heritage — one of the named things the book teaches us to recognise as part of what our homeland hands down  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** How does the book present Tabriza?
a) as a named item of the homeland's traditional heritage  b) as something to throw away  c) as a new invention from abroad  d) as a kind of school rule
**Answer: a) as a named item of the homeland's traditional heritage** — That is what this lesson teaches.
