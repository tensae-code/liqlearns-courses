# Tabriza — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is Tabriza in this lesson?
**Answer:** A named item of the homeland's traditional heritage, taught by name in the book.

**2.** Why does the book teach heritage items by name?
**Answer:** Because we protect what we can name — knowing the name lets us recognise the item, speak of it with pride and keep it from being forgotten.
