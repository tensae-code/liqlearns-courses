# Tabriza

## Do it first

Your book names some heritage items one by one, so you learn to recognise them by name. Say the name aloud: Tabriza — التبرزة. This lesson is about knowing it as a named item of the homeland's traditional heritage.

## Heritage has names

The book names Tabriza (التبرزة, printed exactly so) among the homeland's traditional heritage. Learning heritage means learning its named items — each name holds a piece of the homeland's own life and memory.

## Knowing the name is the first keeping

We protect what we can name. A child who knows Tabriza by name can point to it, speak of it with pride, and help keep it from being forgotten.
