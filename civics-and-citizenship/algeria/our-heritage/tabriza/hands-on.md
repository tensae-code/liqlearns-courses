# Hands-on — Tabriza

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Your book names some heritage items one by one, so you learn to recognise them by name. Say the name aloud: Tabriza — التبرزة. This lesson is about knowing it as a named item of the homeland's traditional heritage.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: Tabriza (التبرزة) is a named item of my homeland's traditional heritage — one of the named things the book teaches us to recognise as part of what our homeland hands down. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
