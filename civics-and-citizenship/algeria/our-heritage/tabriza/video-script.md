# Video script — Tabriza

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Tabriza (التبرزة) is a named item of my homeland's traditional heritage — one of the named things the book teaches us to recognise as part of what our homeland hands down.

The book names Tabriza (التبرزة, printed exactly so) among the homeland's traditional heritage. Learning heritage means learning its named items — each name holds a piece of the homeland's own life and memory.

We protect what we can name. A child who knows Tabriza by name can point to it, speak of it with pride, and help keep it from being forgotten.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
