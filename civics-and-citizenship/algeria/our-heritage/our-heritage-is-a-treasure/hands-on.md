# Hands-on — Our Heritage Is a Treasure

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Look around your home and street. Name three old things — a building, an object, a custom — that were there before you were born and that people still value. This lesson names all of them together: our heritage.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: our heritage is a precious treasure — the valuable things our homeland's past generations made, kept and handed down to us. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
