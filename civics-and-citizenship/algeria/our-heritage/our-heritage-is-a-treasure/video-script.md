# Video script — Our Heritage Is a Treasure

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Our heritage is a precious treasure — the valuable things our homeland's past generations made, kept and handed down to us.

Heritage — تراثنا — is everything valuable that earlier generations made, built, practised and kept, and then handed down to us: old buildings and objects, crafts, customs, arts and knowledge.

The book calls heritage a precious treasure (كنز ثمين) because we did not create it ourselves — we received it. A treasure is not spent carelessly; it is guarded, valued and passed on again.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
