# Our Heritage Is a Treasure — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) our heritage is a precious treasure — the valuable things our homeland's past generations made, kept and handed down to us  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these is part of our heritage?
a) an old craft handed down in the family  b) a brand-new toy bought yesterday  c) a game invented this morning  d) nothing old can be heritage
**Answer: a) an old craft handed down in the family** — That is what this lesson teaches.
