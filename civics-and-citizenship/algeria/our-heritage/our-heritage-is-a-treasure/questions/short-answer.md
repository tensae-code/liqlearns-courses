# Our Heritage Is a Treasure — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is heritage, in your own words?
**Answer:** Heritage is the valuable things — buildings, objects, customs, arts and knowledge — that past generations made and handed down to us.

**2.** Why does the book call heritage a treasure?
**Answer:** Because it is precious and we received it from those before us, so we must value it, guard it and pass it on.
