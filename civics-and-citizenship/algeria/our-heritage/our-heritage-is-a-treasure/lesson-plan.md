# Lesson plan — Our Heritage Is a Treasure

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Algeria · **Title:** Our Heritage · **Lesson:** Our Heritage Is a Treasure
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — Our Heritage (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** our heritage is a precious treasure — the valuable things our homeland's past generations made, kept and handed down to us.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — it stands at the foot of Algeria's ladder, on nothing before it in this course except the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. ## Sub lessons

## Before this lesson
- This is the first lesson of the course. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: our heritage is a precious treasure — the valuable things our homeland's past generations made, kept and handed down to us.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson's scenes are the learner's own home, school and neighbourhood.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks heritage means only money or gold → the book's treasure is wider: buildings, objects, customs and knowledge handed down are the treasure.
- Go back to the one thing: our heritage is a precious treasure — the valuable things our homeland's past generations made, kept and handed down to us. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
