# Our Material Heritage — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) part of our heritage is material — the things past generations made with their hands and left behind: buildings, tools, clothes, crafts and objects  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which of these is material heritage?
a) a clay pot made by an earlier generation  b) a song everyone sings  c) a custom at weddings  d) an idea in a story
**Answer: a) a clay pot made by an earlier generation** — That is what this lesson teaches.
