# Our Material Heritage

## Do it first

Hold or picture one old made thing — a woven carpet, a clay pot, an old tool. Say who might have made things like it long ago. This lesson names all such things: our material heritage.

## Heritage you can touch

Material heritage (تراثنا المادي) is the heritage made of things: the buildings, tools, clothing, pottery, jewellery and crafts that past generations produced and left for us.

## Made things carry the makers with them

Every made thing carries the skill and life of its makers. Keeping material heritage safe keeps their work — and what it teaches us about how they lived — alive.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: part of our heritage is material — the things past generations made with their hands and left behind: buildings, tools, clothes, crafts and objects. Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood, and name the lesson's key words: material heritage, crafts, objects. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
