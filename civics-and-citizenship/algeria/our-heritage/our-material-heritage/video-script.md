# Video script — Our Material Heritage

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Part of our heritage is material — the things past generations made with their hands and left behind: buildings, tools, clothes, crafts and objects.

Material heritage (تراثنا المادي) is the heritage made of things: the buildings, tools, clothing, pottery, jewellery and crafts that past generations produced and left for us.

Every made thing carries the skill and life of its makers. Keeping material heritage safe keeps their work — and what it teaches us about how they lived — alive.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
