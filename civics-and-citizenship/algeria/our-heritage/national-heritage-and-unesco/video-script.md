# Video script — National Heritage and UNESCO

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The homeland's national heritage is recognised by the world — unesco is the world organisation that names and helps protect the heritage of all peoples.

UNESCO (منظمة اليونيسكو) is the world organisation for education, science and culture. It recognises heritage of outstanding value from countries around the world and helps peoples protect it.

World recognition does not replace the homeland's own duty: a country's heritage reaches UNESCO's care because the country itself first values and protects it. Pride at home and recognition abroad belong together.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
