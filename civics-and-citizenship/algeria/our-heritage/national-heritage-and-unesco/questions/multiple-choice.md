# National Heritage and UNESCO — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) the homeland's national heritage is recognised by the world — UNESCO is the world organisation that names and helps protect the heritage of all peoples  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does UNESCO do for heritage?
a) it recognises heritage of outstanding value and helps peoples protect it  b) it buys countries' old buildings  c) it forbids countries to keep customs  d) it replaces the homeland's own care
**Answer: a) it recognises heritage of outstanding value and helps peoples protect it** — That is what this lesson teaches.
