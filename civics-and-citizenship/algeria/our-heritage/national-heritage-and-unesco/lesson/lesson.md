# National Heritage and UNESCO

## Do it first

Your homeland's heritage matters first at home. Now widen the view: there is a world organisation whose work is heritage itself. This lesson joins the two: national heritage and UNESCO.

## Heritage recognised beyond the homeland

UNESCO (منظمة اليونيسكو) is the world organisation for education, science and culture. It recognises heritage of outstanding value from countries around the world and helps peoples protect it.

## The world's list starts with the homeland's care

World recognition does not replace the homeland's own duty: a country's heritage reaches UNESCO's care because the country itself first values and protects it. Pride at home and recognition abroad belong together.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the homeland's national heritage is recognised by the world — UNESCO is the world organisation that names and helps protect the heritage of all peoples. Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood, and name the lesson's key words: UNESCO, national heritage, world recognition.
