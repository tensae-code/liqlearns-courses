# Hands-on — National Heritage and UNESCO

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Your homeland's heritage matters first at home. Now widen the view: there is a world organisation whose work is heritage itself. This lesson joins the two: national heritage and UNESCO.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the homeland's national heritage is recognised by the world — UNESCO is the world organisation that names and helps protect the heritage of all peoples. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
