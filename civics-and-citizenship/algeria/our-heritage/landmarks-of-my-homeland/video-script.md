# Video script — Landmarks of My Homeland

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
My homeland has archaeological landmarks — old places and remains that show its long history, and they belong to its heritage.

Archaeological landmarks (المعالم الأثرية) are the old sites and remains in our homeland — places built long ago that still stand and show how people lived in earlier times.

These landmarks are part of the national heritage: they prove the homeland's long history, and every citizen can point to them with pride — and with a duty to protect them.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
