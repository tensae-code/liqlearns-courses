# Landmarks of My Homeland — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) my homeland has archaeological landmarks — old places and remains that show its long history, and they belong to its heritage  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does an archaeological landmark show?
a) how people in the homeland lived long ago  b) only what was built this year  c) nothing about the past  d) only the weather
**Answer: a) how people in the homeland lived long ago** — That is what this lesson teaches.
