# Landmarks of My Homeland — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is an archaeological landmark?
**Answer:** An old site or remains in the homeland that still stands and shows how people lived in earlier times.

**2.** Why do landmarks matter to the homeland?
**Answer:** They are part of its heritage and prove its long history, so they are a source of pride and must be protected.
