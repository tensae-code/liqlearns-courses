# Lesson plan — I Protect My Heritage

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Algeria · **Title:** Our Heritage · **Lesson:** I Protect My Heritage
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — Our Heritage (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** I preserve the national heritage and take pride in it — knowing our heritage ends in a duty: protect it, and be proud of it.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — it stands at the foot of Algeria's ladder, on nothing before it in this course except the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: I preserve the national heritage and take pride in it — knowing our heritage ends in a duty: protect it, and be proud of it.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson's scenes are the learner's own home, school and neighbourhood.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks protecting heritage is only the government's job → the book puts it in the first person — I preserve it: the duty belongs to every citizen, children included.
- Go back to the one thing: I preserve the national heritage and take pride in it — knowing our heritage ends in a duty: protect it, and be proud of it. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
