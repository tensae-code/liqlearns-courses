# I Protect My Heritage

## Do it first

Go back over the heritage you have met in this field — landmarks, the Casbah, made things, songs and customs, reserves. Now ask: what is MY job toward all of it? This lesson gives the answer: I preserve the national heritage and take pride in it.

## Pride and protection go together

The book's closing word for the heritage field is a duty in the first person: أحافظ على التراث الوطني وأعتز به — I preserve the national heritage and I take pride in it. Pride without protection is empty; protection grows out of pride.

## What protecting looks like for a child

A child protects heritage by not damaging old places and things, by learning the names and stories, by joining in customs, and by speaking of the homeland's heritage with respect — small acts that keep the treasure whole.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: I preserve the national heritage and take pride in it — knowing our heritage ends in a duty: protect it, and be proud of it. Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood, and name the lesson's key words: preserve, national heritage, pride. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
