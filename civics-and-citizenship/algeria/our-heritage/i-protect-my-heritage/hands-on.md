# Hands-on — I Protect My Heritage

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Go back over the heritage you have met in this field — landmarks, the Casbah, made things, songs and customs, reserves. Now ask: what is MY job toward all of it? This lesson gives the answer: I preserve the national heritage and take pride in it.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: I preserve the national heritage and take pride in it — knowing our heritage ends in a duty: protect it, and be proud of it. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
