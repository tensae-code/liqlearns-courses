# Video script — I Protect My Heritage

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
I preserve the national heritage and take pride in it — knowing our heritage ends in a duty: protect it, and be proud of it.

The book's closing word for the heritage field is a duty in the first person: أحافظ على التراث الوطني وأعتز به — I preserve the national heritage and I take pride in it. Pride without protection is empty; protection grows out of pride.

A child protects heritage by not damaging old places and things, by learning the names and stories, by joining in customs, and by speaking of the homeland's heritage with respect — small acts that keep the treasure whole.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
