# I Protect My Heritage — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) I preserve the national heritage and take pride in it — knowing our heritage ends in a duty: protect it, and be proud of it  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What two things does the book join together in this lesson?
a) preserving the national heritage and taking pride in it  b) forgetting the past and ignoring it  c) buying heritage and selling it  d) hiding heritage from everyone
**Answer: a) preserving the national heritage and taking pride in it** — That is what this lesson teaches.
