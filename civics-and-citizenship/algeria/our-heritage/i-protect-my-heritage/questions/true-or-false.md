# I Protect My Heritage — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** I preserve the national heritage and take pride in it — knowing our heritage ends in a duty: protect it, and be proud of it.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson's teaching does not apply to a child's own home, school or street.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite: it is learned from scenes in the child's own world and lived there.
