# Hands-on — How to Talk Well

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
In your next conversation today, watch for three things: do people listen, do they wait their turn, do they interrupt? This lesson teaches the etiquette that makes dialogue succeed: how to talk well.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: dialogue has etiquette — listen to the other, take your turn, speak with respect and do not interrupt: good manners are what make talk work. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
