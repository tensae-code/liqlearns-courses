# Video script — How to Talk Well

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Dialogue has etiquette — listen to the other, take your turn, speak with respect and do not interrupt: good manners are what make talk work.

The etiquette of dialogue (آداب الحوار) is its good manners: listen fully to the other person, wait for your turn to speak, speak calmly and respectfully, and do not interrupt or mock.

Without manners, dialogue collapses into noise — everyone shouting, nobody heard. With manners, even a hard disagreement can be talked through, because each person is heard in turn.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
