# How to Talk Well — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) dialogue has etiquette — listen to the other, take your turn, speak with respect and do not interrupt: good manners are what make talk work  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Which behaviour belongs to the etiquette of dialogue?
a) listening fully and waiting for your turn  b) interrupting to win  c) mocking the other speaker  d) shouting over everyone
**Answer: a) listening fully and waiting for your turn** — That is what this lesson teaches.
