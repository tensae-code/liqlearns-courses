# How to Talk Well — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name two rules of the etiquette of dialogue.
**Answer:** Listen fully to the other person; wait for your turn; speak calmly and respectfully; do not interrupt or mock (any two).

**2.** Why does dialogue need manners?
**Answer:** Without them talk collapses into noise where nobody is heard; with them, even hard disagreements can be talked through.
