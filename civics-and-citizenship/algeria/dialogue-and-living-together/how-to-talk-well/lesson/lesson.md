# How to Talk Well

## Do it first

In your next conversation today, watch for three things: do people listen, do they wait their turn, do they interrupt? This lesson teaches the etiquette that makes dialogue succeed: how to talk well.

## The manners of dialogue

The etiquette of dialogue (آداب الحوار) is its good manners: listen fully to the other person, wait for your turn to speak, speak calmly and respectfully, and do not interrupt or mock.

## Manners protect the talk itself

Without manners, dialogue collapses into noise — everyone shouting, nobody heard. With manners, even a hard disagreement can be talked through, because each person is heard in turn.
