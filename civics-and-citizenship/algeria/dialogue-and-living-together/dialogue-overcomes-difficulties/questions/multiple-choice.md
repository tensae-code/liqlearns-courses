# Dialogue Overcomes Difficulties — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) dialogue smooths away difficulties — talking together is the way through hard problems between people, the alternative to quarrel and deadlock  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does dialogue do to difficulties, in the book's words?
a) it smooths them away  b) it makes them harder  c) it hides them forever  d) it turns them into fights
**Answer: a) it smooths them away** — That is what this lesson teaches.
