# Dialogue Overcomes Difficulties — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What does 'dialogue smooths away difficulties' mean?
**Answer:** When people in difficulty speak and listen together, misunderstanding, suspicion and anger soften and the problem can be worked through.

**2.** What are the two roads a difficulty can take?
**Answer:** Quarrel, which hardens it, or dialogue, which works through it step by step.
