# Lesson plan — Dialogue Overcomes Difficulties

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Algeria · **Title:** Dialogue and Living Together · **Lesson:** Dialogue Overcomes Difficulties
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — Dialogue and Living Together (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** dialogue smooths away difficulties — talking together is the way through hard problems between people, the alternative to quarrel and deadlock.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — it stands at the foot of Algeria's ladder, on nothing before it in this course except the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: dialogue smooths away difficulties — talking together is the way through hard problems between people, the alternative to quarrel and deadlock.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson's scenes are the learner's own home, school and neighbourhood.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks a difficulty means the talking has failed → the book teaches the reverse: difficulty is exactly when dialogue is needed, because dialogue is what smooths it.
- Go back to the one thing: dialogue smooths away difficulties — talking together is the way through hard problems between people, the alternative to quarrel and deadlock. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
