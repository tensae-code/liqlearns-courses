# Hands-on — Dialogue Overcomes Difficulties

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Think of a hard problem between two people that stayed stuck — nobody talking, both sides angry. What finally moves such a problem? This lesson answers: dialogue smooths away difficulties.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: dialogue smooths away difficulties — talking together is the way through hard problems between people, the alternative to quarrel and deadlock. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
