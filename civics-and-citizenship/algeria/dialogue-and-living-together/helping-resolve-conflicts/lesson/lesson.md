# Helping Resolve Conflicts

## Do it first

Two of your friends are in a dispute and both are angry. You are not part of it — but could you be part of ending it? This lesson says yes: I contribute to resolving conflicts.

## The child as peacemaker

Resolving a conflict (حل النزاعات) means helping the sides settle their dispute without fighting. The book puts it in the first person — أساهم, I contribute: even a child can help, by calming, listening to both sides and pointing to a fair settlement.

## How a peacemaker works

A peacemaker does not take one side against the other. They listen to each side, cool the anger, name what is fair, and help the sides agree — the dialogue skills of this whole field, put to work for others.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: I contribute to resolving conflicts — a child is not only a speaker in dialogue but a peacemaker who helps others settle their disputes fairly. Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood, and name the lesson's key words: conflict, peacemaker, settling fairly. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
