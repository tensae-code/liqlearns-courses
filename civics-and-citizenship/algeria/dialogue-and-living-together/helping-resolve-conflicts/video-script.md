# Video script — Helping Resolve Conflicts

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
I contribute to resolving conflicts — a child is not only a speaker in dialogue but a peacemaker who helps others settle their disputes fairly.

Resolving a conflict (حل النزاعات) means helping the sides settle their dispute without fighting. The book puts it in the first person — أساهم, I contribute: even a child can help, by calming, listening to both sides and pointing to a fair settlement.

A peacemaker does not take one side against the other. They listen to each side, cool the anger, name what is fair, and help the sides agree — the dialogue skills of this whole field, put to work for others.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
