# Helping Resolve Conflicts — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What does 'I contribute to resolving conflicts' mean?
**Answer:** I help people in a dispute settle it fairly without fighting — by calming, listening to both sides and pointing to a fair settlement.

**2.** How does a peacemaker work?
**Answer:** They listen to each side, cool the anger, name what is fair, and help the sides agree.
