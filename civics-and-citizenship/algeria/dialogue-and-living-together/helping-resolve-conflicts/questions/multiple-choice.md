# Helping Resolve Conflicts — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) I contribute to resolving conflicts — a child is not only a speaker in dialogue but a peacemaker who helps others settle their disputes fairly  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does a peacemaker refuse to do?
a) take one side against the other  b) listen to both sides  c) calm the anger  d) help the sides agree
**Answer: a) take one side against the other** — That is what this lesson teaches.
