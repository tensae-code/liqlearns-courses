# Hands-on — Helping Resolve Conflicts

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Two of your friends are in a dispute and both are angry. You are not part of it — but could you be part of ending it? This lesson says yes: I contribute to resolving conflicts.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: I contribute to resolving conflicts — a child is not only a speaker in dialogue but a peacemaker who helps others settle their disputes fairly. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
