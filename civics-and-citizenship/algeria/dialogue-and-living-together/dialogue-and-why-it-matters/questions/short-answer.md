# Dialogue and Why It Matters — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is dialogue?
**Answer:** People speaking, listening and answering each other about a shared matter, to understand and reach what is right together.

**2.** Why is dialogue important?
**Answer:** Because it solves problems by words, keeps respect between people, and lets everyone be heard without quarrelling or fighting.
