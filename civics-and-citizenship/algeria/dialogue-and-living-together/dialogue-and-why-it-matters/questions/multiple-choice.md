# Dialogue and Why It Matters — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) dialogue is people talking and listening together to understand each other and solve things — and it matters because it is how people settle matters without quarrelling  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does dialogue turn a disagreement into?
a) understanding, solved by words  b) a louder quarrel  c) silence forever  d) a fight
**Answer: a) understanding, solved by words** — That is what this lesson teaches.
