# Video script — Racial Discrimination

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Racial discrimination is treating people unfairly because of their race — the book names it so it can be refused: people are equal in dignity whatever their origin.

Racial discrimination (التمييز العنصري) is treating a person worse — excluding, insulting or denying them their share — because of their race or origin. The book names it plainly, in a field about living together, because living together fails wherever it stands.

To refuse discrimination is to treat every person by their worth, not their origin: include them, speak fairly of them, and stand against the unfair act when you see it. Equality in dignity is the ground dialogue stands on.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
