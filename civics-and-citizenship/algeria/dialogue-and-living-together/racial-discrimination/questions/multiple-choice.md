# Racial Discrimination — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) racial discrimination is treating people unfairly because of their race — the book names it so it can be refused: people are equal in dignity whatever their origin  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is racial discrimination?
a) treating people unfairly because of their race or origin  b) treating everyone by their worth  c) inviting everyone in  d) listening to both sides
**Answer: a) treating people unfairly because of their race or origin** — That is what this lesson teaches.
