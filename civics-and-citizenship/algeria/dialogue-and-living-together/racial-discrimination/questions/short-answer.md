# Racial Discrimination — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is racial discrimination?
**Answer:** Treating a person worse — excluding, insulting or denying them their share — because of their race or origin.

**2.** How do we refuse discrimination?
**Answer:** By treating every person by their worth, not their origin — including them, speaking fairly of them, and standing against the unfair act.
