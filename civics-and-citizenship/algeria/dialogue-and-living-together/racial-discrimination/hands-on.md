# Hands-on — Racial Discrimination

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Imagine someone refused a place, a game or a kind word only because of where their family comes from or how they look. There is a name for that unfairness, and this lesson teaches it — so it can be refused.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: racial discrimination is treating people unfairly because of their race — the book names it so it can be refused: people are equal in dignity whatever their origin. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
