# Video script — Running Dialogue in Class

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Dialogue in the classroom is managed — the class practises ordered discussion, with turns, a shared subject and respect, so everyone can speak and be heard.

Managing dialogue in the classroom (إدارة الحوار في القسم) means the class talks in an ordered way: one subject at a time, speakers in turn, others listening, and the talk guided so it stays useful and fair.

The class is a small society: practising ordered discussion there trains the habits — turns, listening, respect — that dialogue everywhere else needs. What is learned in the section is carried into the home and the street.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
