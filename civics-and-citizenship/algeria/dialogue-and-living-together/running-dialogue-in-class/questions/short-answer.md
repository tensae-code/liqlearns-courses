# Running Dialogue in Class — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What does managing dialogue in the classroom mean?
**Answer:** The class discusses in an ordered way — one subject at a time, speakers in turn, others listening, the talk guided to stay useful and fair.

**2.** Why practise dialogue in class?
**Answer:** Because the class trains the habits — turns, listening, respect — that dialogue at home and in society needs.
