# Hands-on — Running Dialogue in Class

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Imagine the whole class discussing one question at once, with no order. What happens? Now imagine it managed — turns, listening, one subject. This lesson is that practice: managing dialogue in the classroom.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: dialogue in the classroom is managed — the class practises ordered discussion, with turns, a shared subject and respect, so everyone can speak and be heard. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
