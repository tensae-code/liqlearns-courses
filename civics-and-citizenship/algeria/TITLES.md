# Civics and Citizenship — Algeria course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

Civics belongs to the country: this course is Algeria's own —
built from Algeria's Civic Education book for the 4th Year
Primary (student book, Arabic). Inside the course the
organisation is by **levels, not grades**: the titles below sit
inside Level 1, in path order — what the learner proves, step by
step. The grade the book comes from lives only in each lesson's
sources.

The path's own logic is the book's: civics taught as learning
situations opening from the child's own world — belonging first
(our heritage: what the homeland holds and hands down), then
conduct with others (dialogue, conflict, discrimination), then
the child's nearest institutions and the mine/everyone's
distinction (the school ladder, private and public property,
shared work). That ladder IS the path order (recommended, never
forced — a learner can jump in higher; the "start here instead"
hints point down this ladder).

## The titles

**Level 1 — heritage, dialogue, school and property** (Civic
Education, Grade 4 student book):

- `our-heritage/` — Level 1, first title. Our Heritage Is a
  Treasure (heritage itself, a precious treasure handed down) ·
  Landmarks of My Homeland (the archaeological landmarks) · The
  Old Casbah (the old quarter as living heritage) · Our Material
  Heritage (the things past generations made) · Our Intangible
  Heritage (customs, arts and knowledge in people) · Tabriza (a
  named item of traditional heritage) · Algeria's Nature
  Reserves (protected places as national heritage) · I Protect
  My Heritage (preserve the national heritage, with pride) ·
  National Heritage and UNESCO (the homeland's heritage
  recognised by the world).
- `dialogue-and-living-together/` — Level 1, second title.
  Dialogue and Why It Matters (talking as the way people solve
  things together) · How to Talk Well (the etiquette of dialogue)
  · Running Dialogue in Class (ordered discussion practised) ·
  Dialogue Overcomes Difficulties (talk as the alternative to
  quarrel) · Helping Resolve Conflicts (the child as peacemaker)
  · Racial Discrimination (named and refused).
- `school-and-property/` — Level 1, third title. My Primary
  School (the pupil's own first institution) · The Middle School
  (the next institution on the path) · The Secondary School (the
  school ladder completed) · Private Property (what is a
  person's own) · Public Property (what belongs to everyone) ·
  Cleaning the School (shared work for the common place).
