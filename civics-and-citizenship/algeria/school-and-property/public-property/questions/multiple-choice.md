# Public Property — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) public property is what belongs to everyone together — schools, parks, roads and shared things, which every citizen must care for and none may damage  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Who must care for public property?
a) everyone — it belongs to all together  b) no one at all  c) only one person  d) only visitors
**Answer: a) everyone — it belongs to all together** — That is what this lesson teaches.
