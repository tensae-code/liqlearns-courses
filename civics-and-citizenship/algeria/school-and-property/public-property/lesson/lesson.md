# Public Property

## Do it first

Now name three things that are not one person's but everyone's — your school, a park, the road. Who must look after them? This lesson names them: public property.

## What belongs to everyone

Public property (الملكية العامة) is what belongs to all together: the school and its furniture, parks, roads, public buildings and shared equipment. Because it serves everyone, everyone shares in it.

## Everyone's means everyone's care

What belongs to everyone is damaged by anyone's carelessness and kept by everyone's care. Not breaking, not dirtying, not wasting shared things — and reporting damage — is how a citizen guards public property.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: public property is what belongs to everyone together — schools, parks, roads and shared things, which every citizen must care for and none may damage. Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood, and name the lesson's key words: public property, everyone, shared care. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
