# Hands-on — Public Property

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Now name three things that are not one person's but everyone's — your school, a park, the road. Who must look after them? This lesson names them: public property.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: public property is what belongs to everyone together — schools, parks, roads and shared things, which every citizen must care for and none may damage. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
