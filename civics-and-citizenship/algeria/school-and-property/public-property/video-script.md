# Video script — Public Property

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Public property is what belongs to everyone together — schools, parks, roads and shared things, which every citizen must care for and none may damage.

Public property (الملكية العامة) is what belongs to all together: the school and its furniture, parks, roads, public buildings and shared equipment. Because it serves everyone, everyone shares in it.

What belongs to everyone is damaged by anyone's carelessness and kept by everyone's care. Not breaking, not dirtying, not wasting shared things — and reporting damage — is how a citizen guards public property.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
