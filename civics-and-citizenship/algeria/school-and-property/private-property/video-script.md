# Video script — Private Property

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Private property is what belongs to a person themselves — their own things, which others must respect and may not take or damage.

Private property (الملكية الخاصة) is what belongs to a person: their clothes, tools, books, home and goods. What is privately owned is the owner's to use, and no one may take it, use it or damage it without the owner's leave.

The rule runs both ways: my property is respected by others, and I respect theirs. Asking before using, returning what is borrowed, and never taking what is not mine — that is private property lived out.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
