# Private Property

## Do it first

Name three things that are yours — your bag, your book, your clothes. Now ask: may anyone take them without asking? This lesson names what protects them: private property.

## What is a person's own

Private property (الملكية الخاصة) is what belongs to a person: their clothes, tools, books, home and goods. What is privately owned is the owner's to use, and no one may take it, use it or damage it without the owner's leave.

## Respecting what is another's

The rule runs both ways: my property is respected by others, and I respect theirs. Asking before using, returning what is borrowed, and never taking what is not mine — that is private property lived out.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: private property is what belongs to a person themselves — their own things, which others must respect and may not take or damage. Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood, and name the lesson's key words: private property, owner, respect. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
