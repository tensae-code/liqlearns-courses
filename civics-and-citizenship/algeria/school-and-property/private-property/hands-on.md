# Hands-on — Private Property

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name three things that are yours — your bag, your book, your clothes. Now ask: may anyone take them without asking? This lesson names what protects them: private property.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: private property is what belongs to a person themselves — their own things, which others must respect and may not take or damage. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
