# Private Property — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) private property is what belongs to a person themselves — their own things, which others must respect and may not take or damage  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What may you do with another person's property?
a) respect it, and use it only with the owner's leave  b) take it whenever you want  c) damage it if you are angry  d) hide it as a joke
**Answer: a) respect it, and use it only with the owner's leave** — That is what this lesson teaches.
