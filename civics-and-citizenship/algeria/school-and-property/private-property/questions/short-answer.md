# Private Property — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is private property?
**Answer:** What belongs to a person themselves — their clothes, tools, books, home and goods — which others may not take, use or damage without the owner's leave.

**2.** How do we live out respect for private property?
**Answer:** By asking before using, returning what is borrowed, and never taking what is not ours.
