# The Middle School — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) the middle school is the next institution on the pupil's path — the school that follows primary school and carries learning further  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Where does the middle school stand on the pupil's path?
a) after primary school, before secondary school  b) before primary school  c) instead of all other schools  d) only for teachers
**Answer: a) after primary school, before secondary school** — That is what this lesson teaches.
