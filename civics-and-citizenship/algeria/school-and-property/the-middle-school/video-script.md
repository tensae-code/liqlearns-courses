# Video script — The Middle School

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The middle school is the next institution on the pupil's path — the school that follows primary school and carries learning further.

The middle school (المتوسطة) is the stage that follows the primary school: the pupil moves up to it, learning deepens, and new subjects and teachers take the pupil further.

The book teaches the schools as a ladder the pupil climbs: primary, then middle, then secondary. Knowing the next step early lets a pupil aim at it and respect the whole path, not only the step they stand on.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
