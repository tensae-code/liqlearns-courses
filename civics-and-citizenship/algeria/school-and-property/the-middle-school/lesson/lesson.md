# The Middle School

## Do it first

Your path does not end at primary school. Picture the next gate you will walk through when you are older. This lesson names it: the middle school.

## The step after primary

The middle school (المتوسطة) is the stage that follows the primary school: the pupil moves up to it, learning deepens, and new subjects and teachers take the pupil further.

## One path, rising steps

The book teaches the schools as a ladder the pupil climbs: primary, then middle, then secondary. Knowing the next step early lets a pupil aim at it and respect the whole path, not only the step they stand on.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the middle school is the next institution on the pupil's path — the school that follows primary school and carries learning further. Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood, and name the lesson's key words: middle school, next step, school path. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
