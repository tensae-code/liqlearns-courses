# Hands-on — The Middle School

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Your path does not end at primary school. Picture the next gate you will walk through when you are older. This lesson names it: the middle school.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the middle school is the next institution on the pupil's path — the school that follows primary school and carries learning further. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
