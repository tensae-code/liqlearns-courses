# Video script — Cleaning the School

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Cleaning the school is shared work that keeps the common place clean — pupils together care for the school that belongs to them all.

Cleaning the school (تنظيف المدرسة) is the practical close of this field: pupils take part in keeping their school clean — picking up litter, tidying shared places, leaving rooms ready for the next day.

The school belongs to everyone in it, so its cleanliness is everyone's work. Working together to clean it teaches the field's whole lesson in one act: what is everyone's is cared for by everyone.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
