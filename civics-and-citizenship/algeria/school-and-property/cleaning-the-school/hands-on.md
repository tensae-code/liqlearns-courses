# Hands-on — Cleaning the School

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Your school is public property — everyone's. At the end of the day its yard and rooms carry the day's litter. Whose hands put that right? This lesson answers: ours, together — cleaning the school.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: cleaning the school is shared work that keeps the common place clean — pupils together care for the school that belongs to them all. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
