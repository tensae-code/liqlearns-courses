# Cleaning the School — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is cleaning the school, in this lesson?
**Answer:** Shared work in which pupils help keep their school clean — picking up litter, tidying shared places, leaving rooms ready.

**2.** What does cleaning the school teach?
**Answer:** The field's whole lesson in one act: what belongs to everyone is cared for by everyone.
