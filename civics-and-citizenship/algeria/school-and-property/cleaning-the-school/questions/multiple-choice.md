# Cleaning the School — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) cleaning the school is shared work that keeps the common place clean — pupils together care for the school that belongs to them all  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** Why do pupils take part in cleaning the school?
a) because the school is everyone's, so its care is everyone's work  b) because pupils have nothing to learn  c) because the school belongs to one person  d) only as a punishment
**Answer: a) because the school is everyone's, so its care is everyone's work** — That is what this lesson teaches.
