# Cleaning the School

## Do it first

Your school is public property — everyone's. At the end of the day its yard and rooms carry the day's litter. Whose hands put that right? This lesson answers: ours, together — cleaning the school.

## Care you can do with your hands

Cleaning the school (تنظيف المدرسة) is the practical close of this field: pupils take part in keeping their school clean — picking up litter, tidying shared places, leaving rooms ready for the next day.

## Shared work for a shared place

The school belongs to everyone in it, so its cleanliness is everyone's work. Working together to clean it teaches the field's whole lesson in one act: what is everyone's is cared for by everyone.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: cleaning the school is shared work that keeps the common place clean — pupils together care for the school that belongs to them all. Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood, and name the lesson's key words: cleaning, school, shared work. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
