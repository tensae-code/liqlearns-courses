# Lesson plan — Cleaning the School

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Algeria · **Title:** School and Property · **Lesson:** Cleaning the School
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — School and Property (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** cleaning the school is shared work that keeps the common place clean — pupils together care for the school that belongs to them all.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — it stands at the foot of Algeria's ladder, on nothing before it in this course except the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: cleaning the school is shared work that keeps the common place clean — pupils together care for the school that belongs to them all.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, street or school.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson's scenes are the learner's own home, school and neighbourhood.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks cleaning the school is only the cleaners' job and never the pupils' → the book makes it shared work: pupils take part in caring for their own common place.
- Go back to the one thing: cleaning the school is shared work that keeps the common place clean — pupils together care for the school that belongs to them all. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
