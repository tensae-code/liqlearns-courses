# The Secondary School — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) the secondary school completes the school ladder — the stage after middle school, where the pupil's schooling rises to its highest school level  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does the secondary school complete?
a) the school ladder — primary, middle, secondary  b) only the first step  c) the pupil's first year  d) nothing; it stands alone
**Answer: a) the school ladder — primary, middle, secondary** — That is what this lesson teaches.
