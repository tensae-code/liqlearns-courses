# Hands-on — The Secondary School

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Climb the ladder in your mind: primary school, then middle school — one step remains. This lesson names the top of the school ladder: the secondary school.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the secondary school completes the school ladder — the stage after middle school, where the pupil's schooling rises to its highest school level. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
