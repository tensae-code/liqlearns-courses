# The Secondary School

## Do it first

Climb the ladder in your mind: primary school, then middle school — one step remains. This lesson names the top of the school ladder: the secondary school.

## The top of the school ladder

The secondary school (الثانوية) is the stage after the middle school — the highest stage of school itself, where pupils deepen their learning and prepare for what comes after school.

## The ladder whole

With this lesson the ladder stands whole: primary → middle → secondary. A pupil who sees the whole ladder knows where they are, where they are heading, and why each step is respected and cared for.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the secondary school completes the school ladder — the stage after middle school, where the pupil's schooling rises to its highest school level. Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood, and name the lesson's key words: secondary school, ladder, highest stage. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
