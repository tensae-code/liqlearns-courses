# Video script — The Secondary School

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The secondary school completes the school ladder — the stage after middle school, where the pupil's schooling rises to its highest school level.

The secondary school (الثانوية) is the stage after the middle school — the highest stage of school itself, where pupils deepen their learning and prepare for what comes after school.

With this lesson the ladder stands whole: primary → middle → secondary. A pupil who sees the whole ladder knows where they are, where they are heading, and why each step is respected and cared for.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
