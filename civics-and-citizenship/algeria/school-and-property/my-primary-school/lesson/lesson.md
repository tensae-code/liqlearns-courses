# My Primary School

## Do it first

Walk into your school in your mind: the gate, the yard, your classroom, your teacher. Who is there, and what is each place for? This lesson names the whole: the primary school.

## The school the child belongs to

The primary school (المدرسة الابتدائية) is the first school of a child's path — where learning begins. It has its people (pupils, teachers, those who run and keep it) and its places (classrooms, yard, library), all working together for the pupils' learning.

## Knowing your school is the start of caring for it

This field ends with property and shared care; it begins here because a pupil who knows their school — its people and its purpose — is ready to respect it and look after it.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the primary school is the pupil's own first institution — the school where the child's learning begins, with its people, its places and its shared life. Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood, and name the lesson's key words: primary school, pupil, institution. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
