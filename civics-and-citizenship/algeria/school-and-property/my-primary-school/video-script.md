# Video script — My Primary School

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The primary school is the pupil's own first institution — the school where the child's learning begins, with its people, its places and its shared life.

The primary school (المدرسة الابتدائية) is the first school of a child's path — where learning begins. It has its people (pupils, teachers, those who run and keep it) and its places (classrooms, yard, library), all working together for the pupils' learning.

This field ends with property and shared care; it begins here because a pupil who knows their school — its people and its purpose — is ready to respect it and look after it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
