# My Primary School — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the primary school?
**Answer:** The first school of a child's path, where learning begins — an institution with its people and places all working for the pupils' learning.

**2.** Why does this field begin with the primary school?
**Answer:** Because a pupil who knows their school — its people and its purpose — is ready to respect it and care for it.
