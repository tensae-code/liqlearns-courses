# My Primary School — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) the primary school is the pupil's own first institution — the school where the child's learning begins, with its people, its places and its shared life  b) it teaches nothing in particular  c) the opposite of the book's teaching  d) only adults need it
**Answer: a)** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is the primary school in a child's path?
a) the first school, where learning begins  b) the last school  c) a place with no teachers  d) only a playground
**Answer: a) the first school, where learning begins** — That is what this lesson teaches.
