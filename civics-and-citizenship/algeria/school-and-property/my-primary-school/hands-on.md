# Hands-on — My Primary School

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Walk into your school in your mind: the gate, the yard, your classroom, your teacher. Who is there, and what is each place for? This lesson names the whole: the primary school.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the primary school is the pupil's own first institution — the school where the child's learning begins, with its people, its places and its shared life. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
