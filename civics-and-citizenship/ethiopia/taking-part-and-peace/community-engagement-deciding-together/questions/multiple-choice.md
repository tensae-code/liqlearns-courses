# Community engagement — deciding together — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) **"Communis" means —
a) common, shared  b) distant, foreign  c) ruled, ordered
**Answer: a) common, shared**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) **A school's pupils, teachers and staff form a community by —
a) living in one street  b) needs, interests and roles  c) sharing one family
**Answer: b) needs, interests and roles**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) **Engagement shifts the focus from —
a) the collective to officials only  b) meetings to letters  c) the individual to the collective
**Answer: c) the individual to the collective**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) **Engagement is NOT —
a) one-way information handed down  b) a long-term partnership  c) parents' meetings
**Answer: a) one-way information handed down**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) **Name one school example of engagement.
a) silence from families  b) home visits  c) locked school gates
**Answer: b) home visits**
