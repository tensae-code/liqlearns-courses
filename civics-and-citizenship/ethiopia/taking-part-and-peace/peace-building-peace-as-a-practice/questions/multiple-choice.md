# Peace building — peace as a practice — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) **Peace building runs —
a) before, during and after violent outbreaks  b) only after wars end  c) only during elections
**Answer: a) before, during and after violent outbreaks**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) **It is the work of —
a) soldiers alone  b) everyone: citizens, religious leaders, civil societies, parties, unions  c) foreign missions
**Answer: b) everyone: citizens, religious leaders, civil societies, parties, unions**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) **Its soil is —
a) suspicion  b) silence  c) tolerance
**Answer: c) tolerance**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) **Name one thing peace building prevents.
a) escalation of conflict  b) all disagreement  c) elections
**Answer: a) escalation of conflict**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) **It saves —
a) time spent celebrating  b) the cost of restoring what conflict destroys  c) the need for institutions
**Answer: b) the cost of restoring what conflict destroys**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) **Its named illustration is —
a) a military parade  b) a court session  c) Nations and Nationalities Day
**Answer: c) Nations and Nationalities Day**
