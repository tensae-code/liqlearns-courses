# Video script — Peace building — peace as a practice

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. A short script — this lesson's design names no full video-script need, so the script stays short.

---

**Peace building** never clocks off:
before, during and after conflict.
Everyone's work — citizens, faith leaders,
parties, unions. Its soil is tolerance.
Repair relations. Rebuild trust. Address causes.
The cheapest bridge is the one never burned.
