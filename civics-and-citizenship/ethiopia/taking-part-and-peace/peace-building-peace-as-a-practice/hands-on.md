# Hands-on — Peace building — peace as a practice

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening again, for real
A broken bridge, being rebuilt plank by plank — by both villages together, meeting in the middle. Before reading: is the bridge only wood? Name everything else being rebuilt as the planks go down.

## Sort it on the table
On paper, write the lesson's key pairs from its matching questions — one term per slip. Shuffle the slips, then sort and match them again from memory, saying each meaning aloud as you place it. Any pair you cannot explain goes back into the lesson for one more read.

## Take it home
Find this lesson where you live: one real example of its one thing — peace building is the continuous work — before, during and after conflict — of repairing relations, rebuilding trust and addressing causes — in your family, your school or your street. Write two sentences about it: what happened, and what the lesson says about it. Bring the sentences back and read them to your companion.
