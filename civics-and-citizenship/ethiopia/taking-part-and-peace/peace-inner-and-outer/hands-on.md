# Hands-on — Peace — Inner and Outer

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The same calm, two scales
Draw one calm face large. Around it, draw the same person's street, and mark every place the street's peace enters the face — and every place the face's unrest enters the street. Present the two-way arrows.

## The elder interview
In pairs, prepare three questions for an elder about peace at home and peace in the village. Conduct the interview for real outside class and report back one sentence you did not expect.
