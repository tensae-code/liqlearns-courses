# Video script — Peace — Inner and Outer

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing: peace is of two kinds that hold each other up — inner peace, the calm of a mind free of worry and ill-will, and outer peace, a society with harmony, justice and respect for rights.
Peace has two faces.\nInner peace — a mind free of worry, greed and hatred.\nThe true peace. The foundation.\nOuter peace — no war, no injustice; harmony and rights present.\nThe worried person is always ready to fight.
