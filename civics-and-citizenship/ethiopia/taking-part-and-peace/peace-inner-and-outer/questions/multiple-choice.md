# Peace — Inner and Outer — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** From daily life, peace is —
a) a state of mutual harmony between people or groups, and an agreement to end hostilities  b) only the silence after a war  c) a soldier's rest  d) a locked door
**Answer: a) a state of mutual harmony between people or groups, and an agreement to end hostilities**

**2.** Internal peace is freedom from —
a) worry, anxiety, greed, hatred, ill-will and delusion  b) all work  c) all neighbours  d) all law
**Answer: a) worry, anxiety, greed, hatred, ill-will and delusion**

**3.** Why does the book call inner peace the foundation?
a) The worried person is always ready to fight  b) It is easier to grade  c) It needs no society  d) It is the oldest word
**Answer: a) The worried person is always ready to fight**
