# Conflict and peace — what they really are — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) **Conflict arises from —
a) opposing viewpoints, ideas, interests or goals  b) bad weather  c) poor eyesight
**Answer: a) opposing viewpoints, ideas, interests or goals**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) **Conflict is unavoidable and —
a) always destructive  b) not always negative  c) always avoidable with care
**Answer: b) not always negative**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) **Nemera, though wronged first, —
a) bears no blame at all  b) is blamed for everything  c) shares the blame for escalating
**Answer: c) shares the blame for escalating**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) **True peace, in the quoted line, is —
a) the presence of justice  b) merely the absence of tension  c) the absence of all disagreement
**Answer: a) the presence of justice**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) **Negative peace means —
a) justice fully present  b) no fighting, but justice not yet present  c) war resumed
**Answer: b) no fighting, but justice not yet present**
