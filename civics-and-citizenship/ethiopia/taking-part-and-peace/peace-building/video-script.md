# Video script — Peace Building

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing: stopping the fighting does not end the violence — peace building is the long work after: parties talking, relationships repaired, institutions rebuilt to address root causes.
The guns stop. The work starts.\nParties talking. Views understood. Relationships repaired.\nInstitutions rebuilt against root causes.\nA rope re-tied, knot by knot —\nthat is peace building.
