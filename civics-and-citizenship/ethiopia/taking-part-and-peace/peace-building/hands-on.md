# Hands-on — Peace Building

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Raise the first wall
In two 'opposing' groups, jointly raise the first wall of a shared building from boxes or books: no group may place a block alone — every block needs one hand from each side. Time it; then discuss what the rule changed.

## Tie the rope
Two learners hold a rope with a gap in the middle, ends frayed. The class directs the re-tying, one knot per work of peace building named aloud. The rope is tested with a gentle pull only when all knots are in.
