# Lesson plan — Causes of conflict — home and school

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Taking Part, Conflict and Peace · **Lesson:** Causes of conflict — home and school
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 20 — Taking Part, Conflict and Peace (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** conflict grows at home over limits and rivalry, and at school over incidents, misunderstanding, power and put-downs — and anger is often displaced from its real root.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the levels before it in this course's path order (recommended, never forced); inside its own title it stands on the lessons before it.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
1. **At home** — With **parents**, conflict grows over **limits, responsibilities and expectations** — rules felt unjust, uneven chores — with the book's reminder held alongside: limits **guard children's safety and health**; a limit is not automatically an injustice.
2. **At school** — At school, conflict grows from **incidents, misunderstanding, power-seeking and put-downs**.
3. **Story thread: displaced anger** — The story, in summary.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

1. Sort causes by place: home, siblings, school.
