# Causes of conflict — home and school — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) **Name one cause of conflict with parents.
a) limits felt unjust  b) different surnames  c) school holidays
**Answer: a) limits felt unjust**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) **The book's reminder about limits is that they —
a) are always unjust  b) guard children's safety and health  c) exist to annoy
**Answer: b) guard children's safety and health**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) **Between siblings, conflict feeds on —
a) homework alone  b) meal times alone  c) property, space, attention and rivalry
**Answer: c) property, space, attention and rivalry**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) **Name one school cause.
a) power-seeking  b) punctuality  c) neat handwriting
**Answer: a) power-seeking**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) **The labelled pupil's route out of bullying is —
a) silence forever  b) trusted adults  c) fighting back alone
**Answer: b) trusted adults**

**6. (Sub lesson 3) **In the story, the slap's real root was —
a) the spilled water itself  b) the son's disobedience that morning  c) the father's humiliation at work, displaced onto his son
**Answer: c) the father's humiliation at work, displaced onto his son**
