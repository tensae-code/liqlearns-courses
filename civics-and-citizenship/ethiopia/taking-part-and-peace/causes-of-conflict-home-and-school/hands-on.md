# Hands-on — Causes of conflict — home and school

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening again, for real
A father comes home insulted and fined by his manager. His son accidentally spills water. The father slaps him. Before reading: what is the slap really about? If you can answer that, you already understand this lesson's deepest point.

## Sort it on the table
On paper, write the lesson's key pairs from its matching questions — one term per slip. Shuffle the slips, then sort and match them again from memory, saying each meaning aloud as you place it. Any pair you cannot explain goes back into the lesson for one more read.

## Take it home
Find this lesson where you live: one real example of its one thing — conflict grows at home over limits and rivalry, and at school over incidents, misunderstanding, power and put-downs — and anger is often displaced from its real root — in your family, your school or your street. Write two sentences about it: what happened, and what the lesson says about it. Bring the sentences back and read them to your companion.
