# Video script — Causes of conflict — home and school

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. This is the lesson's full read-aloud script, where the design names a video-script need.

---

A father is insulted and fined at work.
He says nothing. He comes home.
His son spills water — an accident.
The slap lands.

Was the slap about water?
No. The cause sat in the father's day,
and landed on the nearest small person.
Spot **displaced anger** — and refuse to pass it on.
