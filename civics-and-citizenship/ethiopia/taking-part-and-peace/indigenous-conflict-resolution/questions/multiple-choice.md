# Indigenous conflict resolution — elders, forgiveness, compensation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) **The shared pattern aims at —
a) reconciling families rather than punishing offenders  b) imprisoning offenders longer  c) fining whole villages
**Answer: a) reconciling families rather than punishing offenders**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) **Jaarsumma's council is chosen from —
a) the winning clan  b) a peacemaking clan not party to the conflict  c) government officials
**Answer: b) a peacemaking clan not party to the conflict**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) **Shimgilina works through —
a) professional judges only  b) the police  c) the Yehager Shimagille — elders, religious fathers, prominent figures
**Answer: c) the Yehager Shimagille — elders, religious fathers, prominent figures**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) **Xeer is —
a) unwritten and orally transmitted  b) a printed code from 1995  c) a regional constitution
**Answer: a) unwritten and orally transmitted**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) **Xeer Guud covers —
a) one clan only  b) criminal and civil matters for all clans  c) trade only
**Answer: b) criminal and civil matters for all clans**

**6. (Sub lesson 3) **The fieldwork sends pupils to —
a) read a national textbook chapter  b) visit a prison  c) interview parents and elders about their own community's institution
**Answer: c) interview parents and elders about their own community's institution**
