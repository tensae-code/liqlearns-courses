# Video script — Indigenous conflict resolution — elders, forgiveness, compensation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. A short script — this lesson's design names no full video-script need, so the script stays short.

---

No prison. No punishment as the point.
Elders under the odda tree. **Forgiveness.**
**Compensation** that restores relations.
Jaarsumma. Shimgilina. Xeer. Yajoka. Mada'a.
Ethiopia's own way of ending a conflict —
by reconciling the people in it.
