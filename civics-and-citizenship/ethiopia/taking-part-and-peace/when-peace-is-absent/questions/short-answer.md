# When Peace Is Absent — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the first three impacts in order.
**Answer:** Security impacts; continuous social and political disharmony; failure to accommodate cultural diversity.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name three mechanisms by which development is undermined.
**Answer:** Any three of: resources diverted to military spending; weapons accumulation feeding violent crime; trauma, stress, eroded trust, prejudice and injustice; trade, farming, fishing and investment declining amid fear and blocked movement; displacement and migration; tax bases destroyed while military spending rises.
