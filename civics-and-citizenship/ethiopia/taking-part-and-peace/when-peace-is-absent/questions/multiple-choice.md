# When Peace Is Absent — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Oppression through police or judiciary denying participation is —
a) structural violence inside the security impact  b) a form of mediation  c) positive peace  d) an economic policy
**Answer: a) structural violence inside the security impact**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Accusation and counter-accusation, escalating to bloody feud and civil war, belong to —
a) continuous social and political disharmony  b) the diversity impact only  c) the rights impact  d) development
**Answer: a) continuous social and political disharmony**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Resources diverted from health, education and investment go, in the fourth impact, to —
a) military spending  b) savings  c) foreign aid  d) festivals
**Answer: a) military spending**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In the fifth impact, those at high risk of sexual and gender-based violence, abuse and displacement are —
a) women and children  b) officials  c) soldiers only  d) traders
**Answer: a) women and children**
