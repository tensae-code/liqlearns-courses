# Video script — When Peace Is Absent

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing: where peace is absent, the damage runs in five directions — security, harmony, diversity, development and rights — each one feeding the others.
When peace is absent, five cracks run.\nSecurity broken — the chain of payback.\nHarmony eroded. Diversity turned to rivalry.\nDevelopment diverted to weapons.\nRights gravely violated. One village, five cracks.
