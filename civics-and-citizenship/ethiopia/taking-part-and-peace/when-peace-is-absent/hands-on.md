# Hands-on — When Peace Is Absent

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The chain of payback
Stand in a circle. One learner mimes a blow; the next must answer bigger, by the rule of the chain. Freeze at the fifth exchange. The class names the exact point where the chain could have been broken, and by whom.

## Read the cracked village
Draw the five-crack village large. Learners walk it in pairs as inspectors, reading each crack aloud with its impact's mechanism, and pinning one repair beside each crack — repairs are the next lesson's work.
