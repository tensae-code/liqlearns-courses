# Video script — Why engage the community — knowledge, trust, power, solutions

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. A short script — this lesson's design names no full video-script need, so the script stays short.

---

Why engage the community?
For its **knowledge** — terraces, crafts, medicine, settlement.
For **trust** — information shared, not rumoured.
For **power** — citizens owning their problems.
For **solutions** — skill built by deciding together.
