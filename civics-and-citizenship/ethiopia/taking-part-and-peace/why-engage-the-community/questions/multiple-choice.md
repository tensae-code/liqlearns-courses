# Why engage the community — knowledge, trust, power, solutions — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. **Name the four uses.
a) local knowledge, trust, empowerment, problem-solving skill  b) speed, size, secrecy, silence  c) funding only
**Answer: a) local knowledge, trust, empowerment, problem-solving skill**

**2. **Name one item of Ethiopia's local knowledge stock.
a) imported machinery only  b) terracing and bunding  c) foreign law codes
**Answer: b) terracing and bunding**

**3. **An uninformed community breeds —
a) trust  b) patience  c) misinformation
**Answer: c) misinformation**

**4. **Empowerment means citizens —
a) own their neighbourhood's problems  b) wait for rescue  c) defer every decision to officials
**Answer: a) own their neighbourhood's problems**
