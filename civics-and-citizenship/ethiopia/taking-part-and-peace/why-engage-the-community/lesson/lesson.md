# Why engage the community — knowledge, trust, power, solutions

## Do it first

Four panels: a terraced hillside; a handshake between an official and an elder; residents raising hands; a problem being mapped on paper. Before reading, match each panel to a gain: knowledge, trust, power, or skill.

## The four uses

**One — local knowledge.** Engagement draws on what the community already knows. Ethiopia's stock is listed with pride: terracing and bunding, moving barns to fertilise fields, horn-work, stone, mud and ash building, furniture and metal work, traditional conflict settlement, indigenous medicine. Solutions rooted locally prove practical and effective.

**Two — trust.** Keeping everyone informed builds trust; an uninformed school community breeds misinformation instead.

**Three — power.** Engagement empowers citizens to own their neighbourhood's problems rather than wait for rescue.

**Four — solutions.** It builds problem-solving skill through real communication and real decision practice — the community gets better at deciding by deciding.
