# Hands-on — Indigenous Conflict Resolution — Features

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The elders' circle
Form the circle: two disputants, elders between them, the community around. Act a small dispute — a borrowed ox returned lame. The elders must end with both disputants rising and shaking hands, and compensation agreed. The class names each feature as it appears.

## Feature hunt
Watch or recall any customary proceeding (or the acted one). Each learner holds one feature card and raises it the moment their feature is enacted. A feature never raised must be argued for or honestly marked absent.
