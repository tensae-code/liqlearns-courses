# Indigenous Conflict Resolution — Features — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Indigenous mechanisms are best defined as —
a) grassroots, community-based ways of solving conflicts by customary practices  b) village courts run by the state  c) family arguments  d) religious ceremonies only
**Answer: a) grassroots, community-based ways of solving conflicts by customary practices**

**2.** 'No winner and loser, both benefiting' belongs to which feature?
a) Negotiation and compromise  b) Dynamism  c) Context-specificity  d) Forgiveness and compensation
**Answer: a) Negotiation and compromise**

**3.** Enforcement in these mechanisms works by —
a) social pressure and exclusion  b) prison sentences  c) fines to the state  d) police patrols
**Answer: a) social pressure and exclusion**
