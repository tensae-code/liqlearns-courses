# Indigenous Conflict Resolution — Features — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** List the student book's seven features.
**Answer:** Context-specific; voluntary and consensual; accepted and flexible norms; negotiation and compromise; dynamism; restoration of peaceful coexistence; forgiveness and compensation.

**2.** How does the guide's count differ, and how does this course handle it?
**Answer:** The guide keys ten features, adding inclusiveness, local circumscription and group-based responsibility as distinct items. The course teaches the student book's seven as the lesson and keeps the guide's ten as a labelled evidence layer — the two lists are not merged.
