# Hands-on — Community engagement — the social, political and economic sides

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening again, for real
Three scenes: a wedding association collecting for a member; a public meeting arguing about a road; a market cooperative setting prices together. Same community, three kinds of taking part. Sort them as you read — the guide's exercise does exactly this.

## Sort it on the table
On paper, write the lesson's key pairs from its matching questions — one term per slip. Shuffle the slips, then sort and match them again from memory, saying each meaning aloud as you place it. Any pair you cannot explain goes back into the lesson for one more read.

## Take it home
Find this lesson where you live: one real example of its one thing — engagement has three aspects: social (meeting social needs together), political (shaping who rules and what policy does), and economic (communities inside development projects) — in your family, your school or your street. Write two sentences about it: what happened, and what the lesson says about it. Bring the sentences back and read them to your companion.
