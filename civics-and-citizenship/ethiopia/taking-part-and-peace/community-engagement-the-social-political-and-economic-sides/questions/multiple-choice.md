# Community engagement — the social, political and economic sides — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) **Social engagement is above all in —
a) old age care and education  b) tax collection  c) road building contracts
**Answer: a) old age care and education**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) **Its rule for pupils, teachers and parents is —
a) parents receive complaints quietly  b) no passivity from any of them  c) teachers execute without question
**Answer: b) no passivity from any of them**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) **The most active form of political engagement is —
a) voting once  b) reading news  c) party membership
**Answer: c) party membership**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) **Name one political activity.
a) contacting legislators  b) attending a wedding  c) joining a savings group
**Answer: a) contacting legislators**

**5. (Sub lesson 3) **Development engagement includes communities —
a) only at the opening ceremony  b) continuously, in identifying and carrying out projects  c) only when projects fail
**Answer: b) continuously, in identifying and carrying out projects**

**6. (Sub lesson 3) **Name one listed gain.
a) projects need no maintenance  b) benefits need not be shared  c) projects cost less and cover more people
**Answer: c) projects cost less and cover more people**
