# Conflict resolution — negotiation and the win-win — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. **Ignoring conflict —
a) aggravates it  b) settles it quietly  c) is the book's advice
**Answer: a) aggravates it**

**2. **Resolution aims at —
a) a winner and a loser  b) a win-win satisfactory to both  c) the stronger party's terms
**Answer: b) a win-win satisfactory to both**

**3. **In negotiation, the parties —
a) never speak to each other  b) hire lawyers first  c) face each other directly, with no third party
**Answer: c) face each other directly, with no third party**

**4. **Compromise means —
a) each side gives something up  b) both gain fully with nothing given  c) one side yields everything
**Answer: a) each side gives something up**

**5. **Collaboration means —
a) splitting every loss evenly  b) working together to an outcome serving both fully  c) postponing the decision
**Answer: b) working together to an outcome serving both fully**
