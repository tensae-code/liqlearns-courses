# Conflict resolution — negotiation and the win-win — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word or words.

**1. **Resolution is not a winner and a ____.
**Answer: loser**

**2. **The parties state feelings, wants and ____.
**Answer: refusals**

**3. **Negotiation proceeds civilly and ____, with no third party.
**Answer: fairly**
