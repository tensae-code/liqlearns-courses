# Conflict resolution — negotiation and the win-win — matching

Match each item on the left with its meaning on the right.

**1. **Negotiation — b
**2. **Win-win — a
**3. **Compromise — d
**4. **Collaboration — c

**Answer key:**
1. Negotiation — direct talk between the parties
2. Win-win — an outcome satisfactory to both
3. Compromise — each side gives something up
4. Collaboration — building an outcome that serves both fully
