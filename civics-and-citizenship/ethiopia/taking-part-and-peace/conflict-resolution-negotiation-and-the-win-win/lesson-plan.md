## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Causes of conflict — home and school. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State why ignoring conflict fails.
2. Tell compromise from collaboration.
3. Describe negotiation's steps in conversation.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — Two pupils across one table. Each arrives with a full speech bubble. Watch the bubbles as they talk: parts empty out, and a new shared bubble grows between them. Before reading, decide what each pupil must give up — and what neither should have to.
2. **The explanation** — the lesson's parts in order (lesson/lesson.md).
