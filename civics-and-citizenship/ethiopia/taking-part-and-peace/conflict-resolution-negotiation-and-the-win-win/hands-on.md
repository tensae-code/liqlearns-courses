# Hands-on — Conflict resolution — negotiation and the win-win

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening again, for real
Two pupils across one table. Each arrives with a full speech bubble. Watch the bubbles as they talk: parts empty out, and a new shared bubble grows between them. Before reading, decide what each pupil must give up — and what neither should have to.

## Sort it on the table
On paper, write the lesson's key pairs from its matching questions — one term per slip. Shuffle the slips, then sort and match them again from memory, saying each meaning aloud as you place it. Any pair you cannot explain goes back into the lesson for one more read.

## Take it home
Find this lesson where you live: one real example of its one thing — negotiation resolves conflict face to face: the parties state feelings and wants, and build compromise and collaboration toward a win-win — in your family, your school or your street. Write two sentences about it: what happened, and what the lesson says about it. Bring the sentences back and read them to your companion.
