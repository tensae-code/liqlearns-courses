# Video script — Peace — Positive and Negative

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing: peace without justice is only negative peace — a false calm over boiling tension; positive peace is lasting peace built on justice, its root causes removed.
Two kinds of quiet.\nNegative peace — violence absent, justice absent too.\nTension boiling under the lid.\nPositive peace — justice present, causes removed,\nbonds strengthened. Presence, not just absence.
