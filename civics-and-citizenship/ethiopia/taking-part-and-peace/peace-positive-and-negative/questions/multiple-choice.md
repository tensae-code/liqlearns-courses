# Peace — Positive and Negative — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Positive peace is built on —
a) justice for all  b) stronger armies  c) silence  d) distance between groups
**Answer: a) justice for all**

**2.** Negative peace is —
a) the mere absence of direct violence, with tension boiling beneath  b) lasting peace with justice  c) peace after reconciliation  d) peace with root causes removed
**Answer: a) the mere absence of direct violence, with tension boiling beneath**

**3.** Crisis management, deterrence and arms control belong to —
a) negative peace's efforts  b) positive peace's efforts  c) neither  d) both equally
**Answer: a) negative peace's efforts**
