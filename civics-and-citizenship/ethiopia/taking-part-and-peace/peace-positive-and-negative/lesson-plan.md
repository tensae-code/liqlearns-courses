# Lesson plan — Peace — Positive and Negative

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Taking Part, Conflict and Peace · **Lesson:** Peace — Positive and Negative
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 20 — Taking Part, Conflict and Peace (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** peace without justice is only negative peace — a false calm over boiling tension; positive peace is lasting peace built on justice, its root causes removed.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the home-knowledge level, where settling conflicts was first met at home — this level is its Grade 10 depth: what peace is, what its absence costs, and how Ethiopia's own institutions build it.

## If the learner is stuck
- Calls any quiet peace positive → positive peace is defined by presence and justice, not by quiet; a silent but sullen village is negative peace.
- Treats deterrence and arms control as positive peace → the book files them under negative peace: they manage symptoms, not causes.
