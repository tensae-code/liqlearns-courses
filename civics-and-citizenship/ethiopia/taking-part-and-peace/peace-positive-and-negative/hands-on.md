# Hands-on — Peace — Positive and Negative

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two villages
Two groups build the two villages from paper and boxes: one silent, doors barred; one noisy, with a market and a mediation under a tree. Each group must defend its village as 'peaceful' in one minute — then the class judges both by the table.

## Boiling under the lid
Hold a lid down on a pan of 'tension' — grievances called out from under it, one by one. Each grievance named and answered lets one learner lift a finger off the lid. Feel what negative peace costs to maintain.
