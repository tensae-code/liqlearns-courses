# Video script — Volunteering — giving time for nothing back

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. This is the lesson's full read-aloud script, where the design names a video-script need.

---

She fled a forced marriage at eleven.
She built a life. Then, on a pilgrimage in 1972,
she met two children dying in drought.
Within a year: twenty-one children in her home.
Then an association serving thousands.
Honours came. Money for herself never did.
She died in 2021, unrewarded in money to the end.

That is **volunteering**.
Whose story is it? This book leaves that for you to discover.
