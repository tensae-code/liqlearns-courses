# Lesson plan — Volunteering — giving time for nothing back

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Taking Part, Conflict and Peace · **Lesson:** Volunteering — giving time for nothing back
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 20 — Taking Part, Conflict and Peace (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** volunteering is the unpaid gift of time, effort and talent — and it returns connection, confidence, purpose and career ground to the giver.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the levels before it in this course's path order (recommended, never forced); inside its own title it stands on the lessons before it.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
1. **Story: the Selfless Mother** — The story, in summary — and in this lesson she stays **unnamed**, exactly as this book leaves her, asking its readers to discover whose story it is.
2. **What the giver gains** — And yet the giver gains.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Why engage the community. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Define volunteering against required or contracted work.
2. Retell the Selfless Mother story in summary.
3. List what the giver gains.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — A woman opens her door to two thin, drought-stricken children. Behind her, the house is already full of children she has taken in. She is not paid. Nobody ordered her. Before reading, ask: what is she doing called — and what, if anything, does she get?
2. **The explanation** — the lesson's parts in order (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 28722]: a picture of a woman opening her door to two drought-thin children, a house already full behind her — the student says what she expects in return, and what she will actually gain.
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 28723]: a picture of a pupil volunteer in four small scenes: new friends, speaking confidently, planting a tree with purpose, helping organise an event — the student matches each scene to a gain.
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 28724]: a picture of a woman welcoming children at the door of a modest home, arms open — the student retells the story's first act from the picture.
   - ADD AUDIO: none named in the placements for this lesson — no audio slot is built.
