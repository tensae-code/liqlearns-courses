# Volunteering — giving time for nothing back — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) **Volunteering is distinct from —
a) required or contracted work  b) all paid employment anywhere  c) family duty of every kind
**Answer: a) required or contracted work**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) **She took in how many children within a year?
a) 2  b) 21  c) 200
**Answer: b) 21**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) **Her association grew to serve —
a) her own street only  b) twelve children  c) thousands
**Answer: c) thousands**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) **Name one gain for the giver.
a) connection  b) a salary  c) a title
**Answer: a) connection**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) **Youth who volunteer, the book says, tend to —
a) leave their communities  b) do better at school and avoid risky behaviour  c) avoid all teamwork
**Answer: b) do better at school and avoid risky behaviour**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) **Employers read volunteering as —
a) lost time  b) a hobby only  c) self-motivation
**Answer: c) self-motivation**
