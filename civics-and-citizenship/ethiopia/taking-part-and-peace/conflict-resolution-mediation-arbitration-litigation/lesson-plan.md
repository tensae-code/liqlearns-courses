**The one thing this lesson teaches:** when talk alone fails: a mediator helps the parties agree, an arbitrator decides bindingly, and a court litigates — in that order of cost and formality.

## Sub lessons
1. **Mediation** — In **mediation**, a **neutral third party** helps the parties reach **their own** agreement.
2. **Arbitration** — In **arbitration**, a chosen neutral hears both sides and renders a **binding decision** — arranged precisely to avoid court formality and delay.
3. **Litigation** — **Litigation** is the formal court route: a judge weighs the evidence and decides publicly.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
