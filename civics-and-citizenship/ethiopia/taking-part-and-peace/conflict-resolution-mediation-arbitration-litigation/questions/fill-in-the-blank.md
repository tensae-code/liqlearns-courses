# Conflict resolution — mediation, arbitration, litigation — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word or words.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) **Courts are the ____ door, not the first.

**3. (Sub lesson 3) **The comparison runs on time, money and ____.
