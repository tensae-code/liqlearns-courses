# Conflict resolution — mediation, arbitration, litigation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) **A mediator may —
a) help the parties reach their own agreement  b) impose a binding decision  c) fine both parties
**Answer: a) help the parties reach their own agreement**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) **Mediation needs neutrality, listening, impartiality and —
a) speed above all  b) root-cause investigation  c) publicity
**Answer: b) root-cause investigation**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) **Arbitration ends in —
a) an optional suggestion  b) a new negotiation  c) a binding decision by the chosen neutral
**Answer: c) a binding decision by the chosen neutral**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) **Arbitration is arranged to avoid —
a) court formality and delay  b) hearing both sides  c) any decision at all
**Answer: a) court formality and delay**

**5. (Sub lesson 3) **Litigation is the costliest in —
a) paperwork only  b) money, time and energy  c) travel only
**Answer: b) money, time and energy**

**6. (Sub lesson 3) **The book's rule of order says begin with —
a) litigation, always  b) the largest court available  c) the cheaper, less formal mechanisms
**Answer: c) the cheaper, less formal mechanisms**
