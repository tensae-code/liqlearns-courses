# Indigenous Conflict Resolution in Ethiopia — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The Xeer system belongs to —
a) the Somali  b) the Afar  c) the Gamo  d) the Gurage
**Answer: a) the Somali**

**2.** Shimagille is the system of —
a) the Amhara  b) Tigray  c) the Oromo  d) Sidama
**Answer: a) the Amhara**

**3.** These systems answer conflicts ranging from —
a) petty offences and civil disputes to murder and blood feud  b) only land cases  c) only school discipline  d) only trade debts
**Answer: a) petty offences and civil disputes to murder and blood feud**
