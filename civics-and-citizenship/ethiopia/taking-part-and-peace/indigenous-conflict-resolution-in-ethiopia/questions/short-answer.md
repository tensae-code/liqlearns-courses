# Indigenous Conflict Resolution in Ethiopia — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name four systems with their peoples.
**Answer:** Any four of: Makabanna — Afar; Shimagille — Amhara; Bayto — Tigray; Jarsumma — Oromo; Sera — Sidama, Halaba, Hadiya and Kembata; Xeer — Somali; Korefinie — Gamo; Ye Joka — Gurage.

**2.** Who leads the process, and on what standing?
**Answer:** Elders and community leaders — influential, knowing both parties, of higher social status by age, cultural knowledge, lineage or influence — as facilitators and negotiators, alongside religious leaders, family and community members.
