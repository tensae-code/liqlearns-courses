# Hands-on — Indigenous Conflict Resolution in Ethiopia

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Letter the map
On a large outline map, teams place the eight system cards over their regions and present one system each: its people, its range, its leaders. Misplaced cards are corrected by the class, from the lesson.

## The elders' bench
Seat three 'elders' — chosen for knowing both parties in a mock dispute. They must open the hearing by stating where their authority comes from. The class judges the statement against the lesson.
