# Hands-on — Indigenous Institutions and Peace

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Eat together after
Stage the settlement's next day: two 'families' share one meal at one table, served by the class. Each family must name one cohesion practice they will keep. The class records them as the village's own rules.

## The open door
Build the institution house from boxes with its door open to a path. Both sides enter together, rehearsing what each role does inside — healer, includer, neighbour-elder — one role per entrant, spoken aloud.
