# Indigenous Institutions and Peace — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Consensus letting accuser and accused argue without interruption belongs to —
a) building community cohesion  b) accessibility  c) healing  d) restoration
**Answer: a) building community cohesion**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Restoration is aimed at —
a) the future, not punishment for the past  b) heavier penalties  c) forgetting the wrong entirely  d) exiling the wrongdoer
**Answer: a) the future, not punishment for the past**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Purification and healing by customary healers, priests and sheiks belong to —
a) the psycho-social and spiritual role  b) accessibility  c) cohesion  d) none — they are forbidden
**Answer: a) the psycho-social and spiritual role**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Win-win outcomes covering honour, prestige and face belong to —
a) inclusion and participation  b) restoration  c) healing  d) accessibility
**Answer: a) inclusion and participation**
