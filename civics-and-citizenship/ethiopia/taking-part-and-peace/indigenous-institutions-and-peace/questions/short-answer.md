# Indigenous Institutions and Peace — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the first two roles and one mark of each.
**Answer:** Cohesion — e.g. community values kept alive, wide participation, consensus without interruption, sanctions guaranteeing future conduct. Restoration — order and relationships re-established, aimed at the future, deviant members reintegrated.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the last three roles.
**Answer:** Addressing psycho-social and spiritual dimensions; providing inclusion and participation; ensuring accessibility.
