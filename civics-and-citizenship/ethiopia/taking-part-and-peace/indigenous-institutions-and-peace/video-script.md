# Video script — Indigenous Institutions and Peace

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing: indigenous institutions keep peace in five roles — building cohesion, restoring relationships, healing the inner wounds, including everyone, and standing near enough to reach.
Five roles guard the peace.\nCohesion — one people, common ground.\nRestoration — the future, not punishment.\nHealing — wounds inside and out.\nInclusion — everyone answers, everyone shares.\nAccess — justice standing near.
