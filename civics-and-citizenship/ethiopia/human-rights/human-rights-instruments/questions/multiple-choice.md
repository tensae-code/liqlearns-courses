# Human rights instruments — world, Africa, nation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) **The UDHR was adopted in —
a) 1948  b) 1981  c) 1966
**Answer: a) 1948**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) **The UDHR has —
a) 68 articles  b) 30 articles  c) 44 articles
**Answer: b) 30 articles**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) **The African Charter dates to —
a) 1948  b) 2006  c) 1981
**Answer: c) 1981**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) **Ethiopia's rights catalogue stands in —
a) Chapter 3, Articles 14–44, of the FDRE Constitution  b) Chapter 1 of a regional law  c) the 1931 constitution
**Answer: a) Chapter 3, Articles 14–44, of the FDRE Constitution**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) **The CRC, protecting children, dates to —
a) 1965  b) 1989  c) 1979
**Answer: b) 1989**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) **Instruments are —
a) speeches by leaders  b) school textbooks  c) treaties, declarations, conventions and protocols serving as legal sources
**Answer: c) treaties, declarations, conventions and protocols serving as legal sources**
