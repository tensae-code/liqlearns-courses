# Lesson plan — Human rights instruments — world, Africa, nation

**Place in the curriculum:** Level 19 — Human Rights (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** human rights are written down in instruments at three levels: international, regional and national.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the levels before it in this course's path order (recommended, never forced); inside its own title it stands on the lessons before it.

## Sub lessons
1. **International and regional** — **Instruments** are the treaties, declarations, conventions and protocols that serve as legal sources for rights.
2. **The dated ladder and the national floor** — The full dated ladder, as evidence depth: **ICERD 1965; ICCPR and ICESCR 1966; CEDAW 1979; CAT 1984; CRC 1989; ICMW 1990; CPED and CRPD 2006** — and Africa's own **Charter on the Rights and Welfare of the Child, 1990**.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — Three documents stacked as steps. The top one is for the whole world, written in 1948. The middle one is Africa's, from 1981. The bottom one is Ethiopia's own constitution. Before reading, decide: which step protects you most directly — and can any step stand without the others?
