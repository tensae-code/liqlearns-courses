# Lesson plan — Human Rights — The Concept

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Human Rights · **Lesson:** Human Rights — The Concept
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 19 — Human Rights (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** human rights are the fundamental entitlements belonging to every member of the human race — claimable simply for being human, without discrimination of any kind.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the constitution level — the learner knows the frame that carries rights; this level teaches the rights themselves: what they are, their marks, their generations, their obligations and their protection.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Define human rights in the book's terms.
2. Say from what they derive — fundamental human dignity and worth.
3. Say what they set: the minimum standards for a life worth living.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else.

## If the learner is stuck
- Defines rights as gifts of government → they are entitlements held by being human, derived from dignity; governments recognise and protect, they do not create the claim.
- Adds conditions — good behaviour, citizenship, age → the book's only criterion is humanness, without distinction.
