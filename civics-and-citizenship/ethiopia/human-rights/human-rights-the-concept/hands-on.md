# Hands-on — Human Rights — The Concept

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The newborn's card
Each learner writes one card: 'rights — because human'. Hold the cards up together — no names, no ages, no marks. The class says what is identical about every card, and why.

## Standards walk
Walk the classroom as inspectors of the minimum standards: control of one's life, free choice, basic needs guaranteed. At each 'station' (a labelled corner), report one local fact for or against.
