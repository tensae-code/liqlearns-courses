# Human Rights — The Concept — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Human rights belong to —
a) every member of the human race  b) citizens only  c) adults only  d) those who earn them
**Answer: a) every member of the human race**

**2.** Scholars agree human rights derive from —
a) fundamental human dignity and worth  b) government generosity  c) ancient custom alone  d) wealth
**Answer: a) fundamental human dignity and worth**

**3.** UDHR Article 2 stipulates enjoyment of rights —
a) without distinction — the only criterion being humanness  b) by nationality  c) by merit  d) by age
**Answer: a) without distinction — the only criterion being humanness**
