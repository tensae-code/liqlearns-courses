# Video script — Human Rights — The Concept

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

This lesson is about one thing: human rights are the fundamental entitlements belonging to every member of the human race — claimable simply for being human, without discrimination of any kind.
Human rights: what is yours because you are human.\nNot earned. Not granted. Not for citizens only.\nFrom dignity itself — for every member of the human race.\nThe minimum standards of a life worth living.
