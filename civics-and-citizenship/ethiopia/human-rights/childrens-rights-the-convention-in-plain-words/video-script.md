# Video script — Children's rights — the Convention in plain words

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. This is the lesson's full read-aloud script, where the design names a video-script need.

---

One magical night.
A child who was neglected and hurt falls asleep —
and the rights come to visit, one by one.
Protection. Health. School. Play. A name. A voice.
Every right was already hers.
The **Convention on the Rights of the Child** says so,
article by article.
The night only showed her what the day had failed to give.
