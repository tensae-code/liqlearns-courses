# Lesson plan — Children's rights — the Convention in plain words

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Human Rights · **Lesson:** Children's rights — the Convention in plain words
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 19 — Human Rights (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the Convention on the Rights of the Child sets out, article by article, the rights that protect every child.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the levels before it in this course's path order (recommended, never forced); inside its own title it stands on the lessons before it.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. ## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — A child falls asleep in a house where she has been neglected and hurt. In the night, something magical happens: one by one, the rights that should have protected her come to show themselves. Before reading, list what a child needs protection for — then compare your list with the Convention's.
2. **The explanation** — the lesson's parts in order (lesson/lesson.md).
