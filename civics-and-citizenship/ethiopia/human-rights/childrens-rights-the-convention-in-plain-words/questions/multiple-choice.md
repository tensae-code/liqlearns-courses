# Children's rights — the Convention in plain words — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) **The Magical Night shows the child —
a) the rights that should have protected her  b) how to run away forever  c) a new family abroad
**Answer: a) the rights that should have protected her**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) **The document retold in the story is —
a) the school rule book  b) the Convention on the Rights of the Child  c) a regional constitution
**Answer: b) the Convention on the Rights of the Child**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) **Name one article-range right the story covers.
a) the right to drive  b) the right to vote at ten  c) protection from violence (Article 19)
**Answer: c) protection from violence (Article 19)**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) **The two lists are —
a) civil and political rights; economic, social and cultural rights  b) boys' rights; girls' rights  c) town rights; village rights
**Answer: a) civil and political rights; economic, social and cultural rights**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) **Education sits in which list?
a) civil and political rights  b) economic, social and cultural rights  c) neither list
**Answer: b) economic, social and cultural rights**
