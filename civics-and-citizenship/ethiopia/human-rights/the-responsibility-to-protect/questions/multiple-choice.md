# The responsibility to protect — state first, world behind it — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. **Name the four crimes.
a) genocide, war crimes, ethnic cleansing, crimes against humanity  b) theft, fraud, smuggling, truancy  c) war, famine, flood, fire
**Answer: a) genocide, war crimes, ethnic cleansing, crimes against humanity**

**2. **The doctrine was approved on —
a) 10 December 1948  b) 16 September 2005  c) 1 January 2000
**Answer: b) 16 September 2005**

**3. **It was approved —
a) by one region only  b) by a small committee  c) unanimously, by UN heads of state and government
**Answer: c) unanimously, by UN heads of state and government**

**4. **When a state fails its people, action runs through —
a) the Security Council  b) any single neighbour  c) private armies
**Answer: a) the Security Council**
