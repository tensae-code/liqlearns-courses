Facts taught as both books state them: approved unanimously by UN heads of state and government on 16 September 2005; the four crimes are genocide, war crimes, ethnic cleansing and crimes against humanity; collective action runs through the Security Council when a state fails its own people. The guide's lesson title in its book is "Moral responsibilities in protecting human rights" — its content is the Responsibility to Protect, taught as such.
