# Hands-on — Features of human rights — for all, inborn, untakeable, joined

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening again, for real
Four rings, linked in one chain. Pull any one ring out and the chain breaks. Before reading, guess what the four rings are: what must be true of a right for it to protect a stranger, a prisoner, and you, equally?

## Sort it on the table
On paper, write the lesson's key pairs from its matching questions — one term per slip. Shuffle the slips, then sort and match them again from memory, saying each meaning aloud as you place it. Any pair you cannot explain goes back into the lesson for one more read.

## Take it home
Find this lesson where you live: one real example of its one thing — human rights are universal, inherent, inalienable, and indivisible and interdependent — in your family, your school or your street. Write two sentences about it: what happened, and what the lesson says about it. Bring the sentences back and read them to your companion.
