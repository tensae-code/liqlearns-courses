# Features of human rights — for all, inborn, untakeable, joined — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. **Universal means rights are held —
a) by all, everywhere, without discrimination  b) only by adults  c) only in one's own country
**Answer: a) by all, everywhere, without discrimination**

**2. **Inherent means rights are —
a) given by governments  b) not granted, bought, earned or inherited  c) earned by good behaviour
**Answer: b) not granted, bought, earned or inherited**

**3. **Inalienable means —
a) they can be sold  b) they end at age 60  c) they cannot be taken away or surrendered
**Answer: c) they cannot be taken away or surrendered**

**4. **The exception to inalienability is —
a) liberty limited through due process after a court finds guilt  b) any official's order  c) a neighbour's accusation
**Answer: a) liberty limited through due process after a court finds guilt**

**5. **Indivisible means —
a) rights can be ranked and dropped  b) each right leans on the others  c) only some rights matter
**Answer: b) each right leans on the others**
