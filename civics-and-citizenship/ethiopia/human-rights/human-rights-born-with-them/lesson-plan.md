# Lesson plan — Human rights — born with them

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Human Rights · **Lesson:** Human rights — born with them
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 19 — Human Rights (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** human rights are the fundamental rights every person holds from birth to death, simply for being human.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the levels before it in this course's path order (recommended, never forced); inside its own title it stands on the lessons before it.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
1. **Yours because you are human** — **Human rights** are the fundamental rights and freedoms belonging to every person **from birth to death**, simply for being human — regardless of origin, belief or way of life.
2. **Story: the two boys and the crying child** — The story, in summary.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the Law and the Constitution level. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Define human rights and list the seven named rights.
2. Explain rights and duties as two sides of one coin.
3. Retell the rescue story in summary.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else.
