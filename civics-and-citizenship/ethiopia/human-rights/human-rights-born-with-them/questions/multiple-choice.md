# Human rights — born with them — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) **Human rights belong to every person —
a) from birth to death, simply for being human  b) only after the age of 18  c) only to citizens who pay tax
**Answer: a) from birth to death, simply for being human**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) **Name one of the seven listed rights.
a) driving  b) life  c) inheritance of office
**Answer: b) life**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) **Rights and duties are —
a) enemies of each other  b) the same thing exactly  c) two sides of one coin
**Answer: c) two sides of one coin**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) **The two boys respond to the crying by —
a) investigating for days and bringing her rescue  b) ignoring it as a private matter  c) moving house
**Answer: a) investigating for days and bringing her rescue**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) **The right underneath the story is —
a) a right to detective work  b) a child's right to protection  c) a right to privacy for the abuser
**Answer: b) a child's right to protection**
