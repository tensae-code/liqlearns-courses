# Video script — Human rights — born with them

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. This is the lesson's full read-aloud script, where the design names a video-script need.

---

Two boys love detective stories.
Then, at night, they hear a small girl crying.
Day after day, they investigate — quietly, carefully.
And they bring her rescue from brutal beating.

Her right was **protection**.
Their part was refusing to ignore it.
Rights are born with us. Protecting them is learned.
