# Human rights — born with them

## Do it first

A newborn's hand, held inside an adult's hand. Before anyone asks who this child is, what family, what language — name what is already hers, before she can speak a word. That list is this lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Yours because you are human

**Human rights** are the fundamental rights and freedoms belonging to every person **from birth to death**, simply for being human — regardless of origin, belief or way of life. They are values reflecting respect for life and dignity.

[Picture 28695 — a newborn's hand inside an adult's, seven rights labelled around]

The rights named here: **life, liberty, equality, movement, privacy, religion, opinion**.

And one balance to hold from the first day: **rights and duties are two sides of one coin**. Your right to privacy carries your duty not to violate others' privacy. A right you claim is a duty you owe, facing the other way.

## Sub lesson 2 — Story: the two boys and the crying child

The story, in summary. Two boys who love detective stories keep hearing a small girl crying at night in their building. Instead of ignoring it, they investigate for days — watching, listening, piecing clues together — and bring about her rescue from brutal beating.

The right underneath the story is a child's **right to protection**. Notice what the boys did with it: a right they did not themselves need rescued became their business. That is what it means to live among rights-holders.
