# Hands-on — Human rights — born with them

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening again, for real
A newborn's hand, held inside an adult's hand. Before anyone asks who this child is, what family, what language — name what is already hers, before she can speak a word. That list is this lesson.

## Sort it on the table
On paper, write the lesson's key pairs from its matching questions — one term per slip. Shuffle the slips, then sort and match them again from memory, saying each meaning aloud as you place it. Any pair you cannot explain goes back into the lesson for one more read.

## Take it home
Find this lesson where you live: one real example of its one thing — human rights are the fundamental rights every person holds from birth to death, simply for being human — in your family, your school or your street. Write two sentences about it: what happened, and what the lesson says about it. Bring the sentences back and read them to your companion.
