# The right to equality — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) **Article 25 guarantees —
a) equality before the law without discrimination  b) equality of income  c) the same job for all
**Answer: a) equality before the law without discrimination**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) **Name one forbidden ground.
a) height  b) race  c) favourite colour
**Answer: b) race**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) **Equal opportunity means the chance to make the most of —
a) other people's property  b) an official's favour  c) one's life and talents
**Answer: c) one's life and talents**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) **Special consideration for women stands in —
a) Article 35/2  b) Article 25  c) Article 89/4
**Answer: a) Article 35/2**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) **Special assistance to the least-favoured stands in —
a) Article 35/2  b) Article 89/4  c) Article 9/1
**Answer: b) Article 89/4**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) **Why do the affirmative duties exist?
a) to replace Article 25  b) to end all queues  c) because sameness alone can freeze an unfair starting line
**Answer: c) because sameness alone can freeze an unfair starting line**
