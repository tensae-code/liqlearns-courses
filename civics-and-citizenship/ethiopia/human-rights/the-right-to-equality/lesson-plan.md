# Lesson plan — The right to equality

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Human Rights · **Lesson:** The right to equality
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 19 — Human Rights (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** equality means the same treatment and the same opportunity — tempered by special consideration for women and special assistance to the least-favoured.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the levels before it in this course's path order (recommended, never forced); inside its own title it stands on the lessons before it.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
1. **The same service, in the same order** — **Equality** is equal treatment and service from government and institutions, and equal opportunity to make the most of one's life and talents, regardless of birth, belief or disability.
2. **The two affirmative duties** — But sameness alone can freeze an unfair starting line in place.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Human rights classified. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State Article 25's guarantee.
2. List forbidden grounds of discrimination.
3. Explain the two affirmative duties.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — A principal's office. People are served strictly in the order they arrived — no pushing in for the important, no waiting longer for the unknown. Before reading: is strict order the whole of equality? This lesson says it is the start, not the whole.
2. **The explanation** — the lesson's parts in order (lesson/lesson.md).

   - ADD AUDIO: none named in the placements for this lesson — no audio slot is built.
