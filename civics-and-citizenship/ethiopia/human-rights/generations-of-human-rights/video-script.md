# Video script — Generations of Human Rights

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing: rights grew in three generations — first, civil and political; second, economic, social and cultural; third, solidarity — three trees of increasing breadth, planted in 1789, 1917 and 1970 soil.
Rights grew in three generations.\nFirst — civil and political: vote, speak, fair trial.\nSecond — economic, social, cultural: work, schooling, security.\nThird — solidarity: peoples' self-determination, development.\nThree trees. One human family.
