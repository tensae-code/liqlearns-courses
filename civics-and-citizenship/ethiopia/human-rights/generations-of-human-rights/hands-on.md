# Hands-on — Generations of Human Rights

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Plant the trees
Three groups plant three paper trees on a timeline wall — 1789, 1917, 1970. Each group hangs its generation's rights as fruits and presents the roots: what history planted this tree?

## The family portrait
Pose the guide's portrait with three learners — grandparent, parent, child — the eldest holding a 'constitution'. Each speaks one right of their generation and one debt to the others.
