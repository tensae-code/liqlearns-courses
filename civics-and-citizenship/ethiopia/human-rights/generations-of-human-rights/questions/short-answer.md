# Generations of Human Rights — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the three generations in historical order with one root for each.
**Answer:** First — civil and political, from 17th–18th-century revolutionary theories (English, American, French). Second — economic, social and cultural, from the socialist traditions of Marx and Lenin. Third — solidarity, from the nation-state's rise and decline in the late 20th century.
