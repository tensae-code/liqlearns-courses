# Generations of Human Rights — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The first generation of rights is —
a) civil and political rights  b) economic, social and cultural rights  c) solidarity rights  d) environmental rights
**Answer: a) civil and political rights**

**2.** The second generation conceives rights positively and encourages —
a) state intervention  b) limited government  c) no government  d) hereditary rule
**Answer: a) state intervention**

**3.** UDHR Articles 22–27 carry which generation?
a) The second — economic, social and cultural  b) The first  c) The third  d) None
**Answer: a) The second — economic, social and cultural**

**4.** Self-determination and participation in the common heritage of mankind belong to —
a) the third generation  b) the first generation  c) the second generation  d) no generation
**Answer: a) the third generation**
