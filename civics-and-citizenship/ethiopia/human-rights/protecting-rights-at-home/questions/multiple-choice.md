# Protecting rights at home — institutions and the state's three duties — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) **How much of the 1995 Constitution is devoted to rights and freedoms?
a) a third  b) a tenth  c) one article
**Answer: a) a third**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) **Name the two institutions.
a) the police and the army  b) the Human Rights Commission and the Institution of the Ombudsman  c) the courts and parliament only
**Answer: b) the Human Rights Commission and the Institution of the Ombudsman**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) **"Respect" means the state must —
a) build schools first  b) arrest violators itself in every case  c) take no action against treaty rights
**Answer: c) take no action against treaty rights**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) **"Protect" is shown when —
a) a prisoner is kept from being beaten  b) speech is stopped  c) worship is directed
**Answer: a) a prisoner is kept from being beaten**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) **"Fulfil" includes —
a) only stepping back  b) rights education and free or affordable schooling  c) closing complaint offices
**Answer: b) rights education and free or affordable schooling**
