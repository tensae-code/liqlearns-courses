# Video script — Protecting rights at home — institutions and the state's three duties

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. A short script — this lesson's design names no full video-script need, so the script stays short.

---

Two doors: the **Commission**, the **Ombudsman**.
And one state with three duties.
**Respect** — step back from people's rights.
**Protect** — stand guard over them.
**Fulfil** — build the schools and clinics that make them real.
