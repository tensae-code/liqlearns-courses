# Lesson plan — Protecting rights at home — institutions and the state's three duties

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Human Rights · **Lesson:** Protecting rights at home — institutions and the state's three duties
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 19 — Human Rights (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** at home, rights are guarded by the Human Rights Commission and the Ombudsman, and by the state's three duties: respect, protect, fulfil.

## Sub lessons
1. **Institutions at home** — A **third of Ethiopia's 1995 Constitution** is devoted to rights and freedoms, and it is to be read consistently with the international instruments Ethiopia has adopted.
2. **The state's three duties: respect, protect, fulfil** — The state itself owes three duties.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Human rights instruments. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the two institutions.
2. Define respect, protect, fulfil with the book's examples.
3. Say how much of the constitution is devoted to rights.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — Two office doors in one corridor: 'Human Rights Commission' and 'Ombudsman'. A citizen with a complaint walks toward them. Before reading: what are those offices for — and what must the state itself do, beyond keeping offices open?
