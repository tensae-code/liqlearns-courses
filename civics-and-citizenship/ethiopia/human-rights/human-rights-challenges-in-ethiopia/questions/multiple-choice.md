# Human rights challenges in Ethiopia — traditions, poverty, flight — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) **Name one harmful practice against women.
a) early marriage  b) girls' schooling  c) clinic vaccination
**Answer: a) early marriage**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) **The abduction role play ends with the book insisting —
a) elders may legitimise it  b) a court decision must finally govern  c) the family must pay and forget
**Answer: b) a court decision must finally govern**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) **"Tattate" is —
a) a food taboo  b) a marriage custom  c) cauterisation (burning) used for infections and fractures
**Answer: c) cauterisation (burning) used for infections and fractures**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) **Hunger, in rights language, is —
a) itself a rights violation  b) a private misfortune only  c) not a rights matter
**Answer: a) itself a rights violation**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) **Child labour is set against Article —
a) 25  b) 36  c) 9/1
**Answer: b) 36**

**6. (Sub lesson 3) **Rights need investigators such as —
a) party officials  b) market inspectors  c) the Commission, the Ombudsman and public defenders
**Answer: c) the Commission, the Ombudsman and public defenders**

**7. (Sub lesson 3) **Rights and democracy are —
a) mutually constitutive — each needs the other  b) unrelated  c) opposites
**Answer: a) mutually constitutive — each needs the other**
