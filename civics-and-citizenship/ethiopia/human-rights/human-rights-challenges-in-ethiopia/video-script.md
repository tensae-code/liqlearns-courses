# Video script — Human rights challenges in Ethiopia — traditions, poverty, flight

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. A short script — this lesson's design names no full video-script need, so the script stays short.

---

Rights can be written and still fail.
Traditions that harm, practised for generations.
Poverty — hunger itself a violation.
Children at looms instead of desks.
Families fleeing. Institutions too weak to answer.
The catalogue is done. The work is not.
