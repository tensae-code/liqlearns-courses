# Human rights classified — liberty, equality, solidarity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) **First-generation rights are —
a) civil and political (liberty) rights  b) economic and social rights  c) solidarity rights
**Answer: a) civil and political (liberty) rights**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) **Freedom of expression sits in —
a) the second generation  b) the first generation  c) the third generation
**Answer: b) the first generation**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) **Second-generation rights stand in UDHR Articles —
a) 2–21  b) 28 only  c) 22–27
**Answer: c) 22–27**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) **Consumer rights are added to —
a) the second generation  b) the first generation  c) no generation
**Answer: a) the second generation**

**5. (Sub lesson 3) **Third-generation rights are also called —
a) liberty rights  b) solidarity (fraternity) rights  c) equality rights
**Answer: b) solidarity (fraternity) rights**

**6. (Sub lesson 3) **The right singled out as the foundation of all others is —
a) the right to property  b) the right to association  c) the right to life
**Answer: c) the right to life**
