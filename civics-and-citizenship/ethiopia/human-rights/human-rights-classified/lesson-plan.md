# Lesson plan — Human rights classified — liberty, equality, solidarity

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Human Rights · **Lesson:** Human rights classified — liberty, equality, solidarity
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 19 — Human Rights (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** human rights fall into three generations: civil and political, economic social and cultural, and solidarity rights.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the levels before it in this course's path order (recommended, never forced); inside its own title it stands on the lessons before it.

## Sub lessons
1. **First generation: civil and political rights** — The **first generation** holds **civil and political rights** — the liberty rights: life, security, freedom from slavery, privacy, due and fair process, thought and expression, religion, association, property.
2. **Second generation: economic, social and cultural rights** — The **second generation** holds **economic, social and cultural rights** — the equality rights: work, social security, protection from unemployment, a good living, education, health.
3. **Third generation: solidarity rights** — The **third generation** holds **solidarity rights** — the fraternity rights: a just distribution of wealth, economic and social development, participation in development, peace, and a clean and healthy environment owed to future generations too.
