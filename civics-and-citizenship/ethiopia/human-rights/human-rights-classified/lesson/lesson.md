# Human rights classified — liberty, equality, solidarity

## Do it first

Three steps ascending. On the first, a speaker speaking freely. On the second, a pupil and a patient. On the third, a clean river valley everyone shares. Before reading: why would rights come in generations at all — like a family growing?

## Sub lesson 1 — First generation: civil and political rights

The **first generation** holds **civil and political rights** — the liberty rights: life, security, freedom from slavery, privacy, due and fair process, thought and expression, religion, association, property. They stand in **UDHR Articles 2–21** and **FDRE Articles 14–40**.

[Picture 28707 — three steps ascending: speaker, pupil and patient, clean valley]

These are the rights that say to power: **thus far, no further**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Second generation: economic, social and cultural rights

The **second generation** holds **economic, social and cultural rights** — the equality rights: work, social security, protection from unemployment, a good living, education, health. They stand in **UDHR Articles 22–27** and **FDRE Articles 41–42**.

[Picture 28708 — a three-storey house of rights]

**Consumer rights** are added to this generation: safety, information, choice, redress, respect. These rights say to society: **no one left without the means of a decent life**.

## Sub lesson 3 — Third generation: solidarity rights

The **third generation** holds **solidarity rights** — the fraternity rights: a just distribution of wealth, economic and social development, participation in development, peace, and a clean and healthy environment owed to future generations too. They stand in **UDHR Article 28** and **FDRE Articles 43–44**.

One right is singled out above the whole classification: the **right to life**, the foundation of all the others.
