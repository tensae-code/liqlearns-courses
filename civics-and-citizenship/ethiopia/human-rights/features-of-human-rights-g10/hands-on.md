# Hands-on — Features of Human Rights

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Link the rings
Cut six paper rings, letter them with the features, and link them into a real chain — each ring must be threaded through another before closing. Hang the chain; a visitor must read all six from it.

## The qualification debate
Two learners argue a curfew case: one for the emergency derogation, one for inviolability even in crises — the book gives both their material. The class judges which book sentences each side used correctly.
