# Features of Human Rights — matching

Match each item on the left with the one on the right that belongs with it.

**Match these:**
1. Inalienability — c
2. Inviolability — b
3. Irrevocability — d
4. Eternality — a

The right-hand side, in order:
a) enjoyed until natural death; in principle eternal
b) not to be violated or even restricted by anyone
c) cannot be lost — linked to human existence itself
d) cannot be taken away by any power or authority
