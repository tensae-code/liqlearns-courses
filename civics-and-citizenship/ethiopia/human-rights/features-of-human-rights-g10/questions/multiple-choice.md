# Features of Human Rights — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Universalism expressly does NOT mean —
a) uniformity  b) equal application  c) no time limit  d) no distinction
**Answer: a) uniformity**

**2.** Rights enjoyed until natural death, in principle eternal, show —
a) eternality  b) inviolability  c) irrevocability  d) indivisibility
**Answer: a) eternality**

**3.** A curfew in a publicly declared emergency is the book's example of —
a) derogation inside inalienability's qualification  b) the end of human rights  c) a coup  d) uniformity
**Answer: a) derogation inside inalienability's qualification**
