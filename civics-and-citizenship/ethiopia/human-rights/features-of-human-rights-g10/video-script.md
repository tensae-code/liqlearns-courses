# Video script — Features of Human Rights

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing: human rights are known by six marks — universalism, eternality, inalienability, indivisibility and interdependence, inviolability, irrevocability — six rings locked together, none removable alone.
Six marks make rights rights.\nUniversal — for all, everywhere. Eternal. Inalienable.\nIndivisible — progress in one set lifts the other.\nInviolable. Irrevocable.\nSix rings, one chain — remove none.
