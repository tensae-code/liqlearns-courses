# Protecting Human Rights — World, Regional and Ethiopian Instruments — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The UDHR was adopted on —
a) 10 December 1948  b) 1 January 1950  c) 25 December 1945  d) 10 December 1966
**Answer: a) 10 December 1948**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The UDHR's legal character, precisely, is —
a) not legally binding, but defining the Charter's human-rights terms member states must observe  b) a treaty with penalties  c) a world constitution  d) binding only on Europe
**Answer: a) not legally binding, but defining the Charter's human-rights terms member states must observe**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The ICCPR and ICESCR are both dated —
a) 1966  b) 1948  c) 1951  d) 1926
**Answer: a) 1966**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The regional systems stand for —
a) Africa, the Americas and Europe  b) Asia, Africa and Oceania  c) Europe only  d) the UN's five regions
**Answer: a) Africa, the Americas and Europe**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** By Articles 9(4) and 13, as the book gives them, ratified international agreements are —
a) integral to the law of the land, and constitutional rights are interpreted in conformity with them  b) advisory only  c) below regional custom  d) valid only in Addis Ababa
**Answer: a) integral to the law of the land, and constitutional rights are interpreted in conformity with them**
