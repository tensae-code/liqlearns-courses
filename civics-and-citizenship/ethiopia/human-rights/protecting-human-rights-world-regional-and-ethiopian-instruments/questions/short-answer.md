# Protecting Human Rights — World, Regional and Ethiopian Instruments — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name five treaties from the book's family.
**Answer:** Any five of: Genocide Convention (1948); Refugee Convention (1951); Slavery Convention (1926, amended 1953); ICCPR (1966); ICESCR (1966); CAT; ICERD; CEDAW; CRC; Migrant Workers Convention.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the institutions the constitution requires for rights, beyond the judiciary.
**Answer:** A national human rights commission, the attorney general's office, and the ombudsman — with an independent, resourced judiciary standing behind them.
