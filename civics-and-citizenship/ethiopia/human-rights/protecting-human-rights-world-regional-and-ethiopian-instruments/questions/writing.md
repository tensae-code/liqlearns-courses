# Protecting Human Rights — World, Regional and Ethiopian Instruments — writing

Write what each prompt asks, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Write the three-rings brief for a violated right of your choosing: the right, the inner ring's remedy attempted, the regional door, and the world standard invoked — each ring doing only what it can actually do.
**A good answer does this:** Instruments and dates are exact; each ring's power is stated realistically.
