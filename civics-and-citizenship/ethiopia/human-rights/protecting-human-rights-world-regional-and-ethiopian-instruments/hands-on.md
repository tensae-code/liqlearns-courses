# Hands-on — Protecting Human Rights — World, Regional and Ethiopian Instruments

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Radiate the treaties
Place a scroll card — UDHR, 10 December 1948 — at the centre of the table. Learners lay treaty booklets outward from it by year, each reader announcing the treaty and its year as they lay it. The circle must close without gaps in the story.

## Three shields up
Three learners raise shield cards — Africa, Americas, Europe — above a fourth who sits as a courthouse with a commission sign. The class names what each shield adds that the world's layer alone does not give.
