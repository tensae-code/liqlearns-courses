# Video script — Protecting Human Rights — World, Regional and Ethiopian Instruments

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing: rights are protected by instruments in three layers — the world's: the UDHR of 1948 and its treaty family; the regions': Africa, the Americas, Europe; and Ethiopia's own: the FDRE Constitution and the institutions it requires.
Protection has layers.\n1948: a declaration defines the words.\nTreaties follow — genocide, refugees, covenants, children.\nThree regional shields: Africa, the Americas, Europe.\nAt home: a constitution, a commission, an ombudsman, independent courts.
