# Human rights under Ethiopia's regional constitutions — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) **Regional constitutions are empowered by —
a) FDRE Article 52/2b  b) Article 35/2  c) Article 74
**Answer: a) FDRE Article 52/2b**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) **Most regional constitutions were adopted in —
a) 1998  b) 1995  c) 2020
**Answer: b) 1995**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) **Sidama's was adopted in —
a) 1995  b) 1996  c) 2020
**Answer: c) 2020**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) **No region may —
a) fall below the federal rights floor  b) copy the federal catalogue  c) protect rights further
**Answer: a) fall below the federal rights floor**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) **Ato Tona was —
a) a regional judge  b) a 78-year-old Wolaita elder  c) a young trader
**Answer: b) a 78-year-old Wolaita elder**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) **Name one right violated in his case.
a) the right to vote twice  b) the right to office  c) freedom from inhuman treatment
**Answer: c) freedom from inhuman treatment**
