# Hands-on — Human rights under Ethiopia's regional constitutions

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening again, for real
Nine regional constitutions stand under one federal constitution. In each, one chapter glows the same colour as the federal rights chapter. Before reading: may a region write fewer rights than the federal floor? May it write more?

## Sort it on the table
On paper, write the lesson's key pairs from its matching questions — one term per slip. Shuffle the slips, then sort and match them again from memory, saying each meaning aloud as you place it. Any pair you cannot explain goes back into the lesson for one more read.

## Take it home
Find this lesson where you live: one real example of its one thing — every regional constitution copies the federal rights catalogue in substance — no region may fall below the federal floor — in your family, your school or your street. Write two sentences about it: what happened, and what the lesson says about it. Bring the sentences back and read them to your companion.
