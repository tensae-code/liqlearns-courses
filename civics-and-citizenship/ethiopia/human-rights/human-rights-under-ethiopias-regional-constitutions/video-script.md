# Video script — Human rights under Ethiopia's regional constitutions

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. This is the lesson's full read-aloud script, where the design names a video-script need.

---

Ato Tona is seventy-eight, a respected elder.
Attacked on his way home — then detained without court,
beaten unseen, denied family and counsel,
tried in private, while his attackers walk free.

Now audit it, article by article.
Liberty. Freedom from inhuman treatment.
Access to court. A public trial.
The regional constitutions promise all of it.
The floor is written. The case asks whether it held.
