This lesson is about one thing: the FDRE Constitution gives human rights a whole chapter — Chapter Three, Articles 13–44, a third of the whole — with Article 10 declaring rights emanating from the nature of mankind, inviolable and inalienable.
Ethiopia's constitution opens its heart: Chapter Three.\nArticles 13 to 44 — a third of the whole book.\nHuman Rights, 14 to 28. Democratic Rights, 29 to 44.\nArticle 10: rights from the nature of mankind — inviolable, inalienable.
