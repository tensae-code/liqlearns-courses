# Lesson plan — Human Rights in Ethiopia's Constitution

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Human Rights · **Lesson:** Human Rights in Ethiopia's Constitution
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 19 — Human Rights (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the FDRE Constitution gives human rights a whole chapter — Chapter Three, Articles 13–44, a third of the whole — with Article 10 declaring rights emanating from the nature of mankind, inviolable and inalienable.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the constitution level — the learner knows the frame that carries rights; this level teaches the rights themselves: what they are, their marks, their generations, their obligations and their protection.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the chapter, the article range, and the proportion — as the book gives them.
2. Separate the two halves: 'Human Rights' Arts. 14–28, 'Democratic Rights' Arts. 29–44.
3. Sample the human-rights side of the book's table from memory.

## If the learner is stuck
- Swaps the halves' ranges → Human Rights are Arts. 14–28; Democratic Rights are Arts. 29–44; the whole chapter runs 13–44.
- Treats the proportion as trivia → one-third of the constitution is the book's own emphasis claim: the distinguishing feature of this constitution is its weight on rights.
