# Human Rights in Ethiopia's Constitution — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Human rights and democratic rights stand in which chapter of the FDRE Constitution?
a) Chapter Three  b) Chapter One  c) Chapter Five  d) The preamble only
**Answer: a) Chapter Three**

**2.** Article 10 declares rights emanating from the nature of mankind to be —
a) inviolable and inalienable  b) granted by parliament  c) temporary  d) for citizens only
**Answer: a) inviolable and inalienable**

**3.** The 'Human Rights' half of Chapter Three runs —
a) Articles 14–28  b) Articles 29–44  c) Articles 1–13  d) Articles 45–60
**Answer: a) Articles 14–28**
