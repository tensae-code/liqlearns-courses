# Hands-on — Human Rights in Ethiopia's Constitution

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Open at Chapter Three
With a real or mock constitution, open at Chapter Three and read Article 10 aloud in chorus. Then each learner reads one sampled right and mimes the event it forbids — the class names the right.

## The one-third wall
Cover one third of a wall with paper headed Chapter Three. Class members pin sampled rights inside it until the wall's sample matches the book's table — then step back and read the proportion with their eyes.
