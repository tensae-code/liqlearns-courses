**The duty to respect is taught generally** — both books illustrate it with disability-convention examples (student §6.2.2; the guide's states-parties list is disability-specific text inside a general lesson). Those examples stay as examples, named as such here, and are not taught as the duty's whole content. The guide's Lesson 2 activity is misnumbered 6.1 — activity numbers are not cited as unique identifiers.
