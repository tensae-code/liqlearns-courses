# Video script — Obligations for Human Rights — Citizens and the State

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing: rights arrive with duties on both sides — citizens owe respect: for religious and gender equality, for others' rights, for the flag; the state owes three obligations: respect, protect, fulfil.
Rights arrive with duties.\nCitizens: respect — religion, gender, others' rights, the flag.\nThe state, prime responsibility, three obligations:\nRespect — hands off. Protect — shield. Fulfil — build.\nThe constitution that entitles also obliges.
