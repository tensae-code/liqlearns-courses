# Hands-on — Obligations for Human Rights — Citizens and the State

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Load the seesaw
Two learners sit the seesaw of rights and duties with stone weights lettered for each item of the lesson. The class loads the state's three weights on a second seesaw and judges both balances — what tips, what falls.

## Three doors
Three doors (chairs) lettered Respect, Protect, Fulfil. A case is read — a school interfered with, a clinic unbuilt, a right blocked by an official. The 'state' learner must choose the right door, walk through it, and act the obligation.
