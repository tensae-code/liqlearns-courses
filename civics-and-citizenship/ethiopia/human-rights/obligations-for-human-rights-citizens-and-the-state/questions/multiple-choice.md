# Obligations for Human Rights — Citizens and the State — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The citizen's named obligations include respect for —
a) religious equality, gender equality, others' rights, and the national flag  b) officials' privacy in all things  c) party discipline  d) market prices
**Answer: a) religious equality, gender equality, others' rights, and the national flag**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The obligation arises from —
a) the right enjoyed — the constitution that entitles also obliges  b) a tax payment  c) an official's request  d) custom alone
**Answer: a) the right enjoyed — the constitution that entitles also obliges**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The state's obligation to refrain from interfering with enjoyment of rights is —
a) respect  b) protect  c) fulfil  d) enforce
**Answer: a) respect**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Shielding individuals against abuses by non-state actors is —
a) protect  b) respect  c) fulfil  d) delegate
**Answer: a) protect**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Free compulsory primary schooling is the book's example of —
a) fulfil  b) respect  c) protect  d) none of these
**Answer: a) fulfil**
