# Obligations for Human Rights — Citizens and the State — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Define the three state obligations in one line each.
**Answer:** Respect — refraining from interfering with enjoyment of rights. Protect — shielding individuals against abuses by non-state actors, foreign agents or state agents outside their capacity, preventively and remedially. Fulfil — positive action creating the legal, institutional and procedural conditions for full enjoyment.
