# Why human rights must be respected — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. **Respect for rights secures —
a) democracy, justice and growth  b) silence and obedience  c) one-party rule
**Answer: a) democracy, justice and growth**

**2. **It is a prerequisite of —
a) higher taxes  b) lasting peace and development  c) bigger armies
**Answer: b) lasting peace and development**

**3. **Name a specially protected group.
a) officials  b) soldiers  c) women and children
**Answer: c) women and children**

**4. **It gives governments a —
a) universal standard by which they are held accountable  b) excuse for any act  c) holiday from criticism
**Answer: a) universal standard by which they are held accountable**

**5. **Without equality, the book says, lasting peace is —
a) automatic  b) unthinkable  c) unnecessary
**Answer: b) unthinkable**
