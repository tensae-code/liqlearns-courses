# Video script — Indigenous Knowledge and Responsible Citizens — Local Technologies

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing: indigenous technologies — pottery, smithing, jewellery, weaving, carving, leather work — are local, cheaper and at hand; and the craftspeople who kept them alive against contempt teach what a responsible citizen looks like.
The pot on your shelf was made near here.
The cloth on your back was woven near here.
Cheaper. At hand. Ours.
Do not love the product and despise the producer.
The hands that kept these crafts alive
are a citizen's hands.
