# Indigenous Knowledge and Responsible Citizens — Local Technologies — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which is NOT one of the named long-practised crafts?
a) Pottery  b) Jewellery  c) Leather work  d) Aircraft building
**Answer: d) Aircraft building**

**2.** Local technology's advantages are —
a) higher cost and scarcity  b) reasonable cost, better accessibility, less dependence on imports  c) fashion  d) secrecy
**Answer: b) reasonable cost, better accessibility, less dependence on imports**
