# Indigenous Knowledge and Responsible Citizens — Local Technologies — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the six crafts.
**Answer:** Pottery, iron-smithing, jewellery, weaving, wood carving and leather work.

**2.** What marks of the responsible citizen do the craftspeople show?
**Answer:** Determination, and valuing their own wisdom — keeping knowledge alive against contempt.
