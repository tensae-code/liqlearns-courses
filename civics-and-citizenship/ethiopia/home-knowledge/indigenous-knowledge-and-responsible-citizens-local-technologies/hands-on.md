# Hands-on — Indigenous Knowledge and Responsible Citizens — Local Technologies

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The loom, by hand
With sticks and yarn or string, the group rigs a simple frame loom and passes a shuttle across and back, row by row, until a small cloth exists. Slow down: feel what the weaver's speed costs in years.

## Products and producers
Two columns on the board: products we love / how we treat their makers. The class fills both honestly, then drafts one class rule that closes the gap.
