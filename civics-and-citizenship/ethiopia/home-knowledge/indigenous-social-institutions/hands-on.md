# Hands-on — Indigenous Social Institutions

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The Iqqub circle
The group sits in a circle with a pot (a bowl) and play-money contributions. The lottery is drawn; the pot travels. Then discuss: what makes members keep paying after their own turn has passed?

## Debo against the rain
A timed team task — moving a pile of stones or books before a 'rain' countdown ends — done first by one learner alone, then by the whole group as Debo. Compare loads, times and spirits.
