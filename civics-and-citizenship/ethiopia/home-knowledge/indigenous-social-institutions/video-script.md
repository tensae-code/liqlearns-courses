# Video script — Indigenous Social Institutions

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing: Ethiopia's indigenous social institutions organise care itself: Siinqee guards women's rights, Idir buries and Iqub saves, Mahber feasts and Debo harvests — voluntary, trust-based, and built by neighbours.
A stick that stops a fight.
Neighbours who bury together.
A pot that travels hand to hand.
A table that feeds the poor.
A field harvested by many hands.
Care, organised — centuries ago, still working.
