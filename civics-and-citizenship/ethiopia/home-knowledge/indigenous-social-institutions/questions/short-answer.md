# Indigenous Social Institutions — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** What kind of associations are these, in the lesson's own words?
**Answer:** Voluntary, trust-based associations that locals form to support each other.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What does Siinqee represent in the Gadaa system?
**Answer:** Married women's participation across political, economic, social, cultural and religious life — a socially endorsed set of rights exercised by women.
