# Indigenous Social Institutions — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Siinqee's stick marks —
a) a wealthy woman  b) a legally married woman, owed respect and power  c) a judge  d) a bride on one day only
**Answer: b) a legally married woman, owed respect and power**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** When fighting breaks out, Siinqee women —
a) leave at once  b) raise their sticks and order the fighters to stop, then mediate  c) call the police first  d) join the stronger side
**Answer: b) raise their sticks and order the fighters to stop, then mediate**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Idir exists primarily for —
a) trade  b) mutual aid in burial and mourning  c) lending at interest  d) harvest labour
**Answer: b) mutual aid in burial and mourning**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The Iqub pot reaches its member —
a) by wealth order  b) by lottery, one at a time  c) by age  d) all at once
**Answer: b) by lottery, one at a time**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Debo/Jigie is —
a) a feast association  b) labour-sharing — ploughing, weeding, harvesting, building together  c) a savings pot  d) a women's institution
**Answer: b) labour-sharing — ploughing, weeding, harvesting, building together**
