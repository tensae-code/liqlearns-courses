# Indigenous Social Institutions — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Idir also answers sudden risks like fire, theft and illness.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Mahber/Senbete never concerns itself with members in serious trouble.
**Answer: False** — helping a member in serious trouble is one of its named works.
