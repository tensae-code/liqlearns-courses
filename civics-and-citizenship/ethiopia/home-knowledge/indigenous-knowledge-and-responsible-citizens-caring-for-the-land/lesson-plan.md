# Lesson plan — Indigenous Knowledge and Responsible Citizens — Caring for the Land

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Home Knowledge and Its Institutions · **Lesson:** Indigenous Knowledge and Responsible Citizens — Caring for the Land
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 23 — Home Knowledge and Its Institutions (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** environmental conservation is the wise use of natural resources — and indigenous knowledge carries it in Ethiopia, in the Konso terraces and the Gedeo agroforestry, bound to the land by culture itself.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the citizenship levels before it — the learner knows the state's written frame; this band teaches the older knowledge that was governing home ground long before it.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Define environmental conservation.
2. Tell the two flagship examples: Konso and Gedeo.
3. Explain the cultural mechanism that protects the land.
