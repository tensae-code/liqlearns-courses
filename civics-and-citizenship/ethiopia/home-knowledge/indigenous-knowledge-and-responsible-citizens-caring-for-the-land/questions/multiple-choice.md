# Indigenous Knowledge and Responsible Citizens — Caring for the Land — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Environmental conservation is —
a) locking nature away from people  b) the wise use of natural resources  c) planting one tree a year  d) a city programme
**Answer: b) the wise use of natural resources**

**2.** The Konso are famed for —
a) agroforestry layers  b) water- and soil-conservation terracing  c) sea fishing  d) pottery
**Answer: b) water- and soil-conservation terracing**

**3.** The Gedeo system layers —
a) stones only  b) trees with crops in a living architecture  c) roads  d) fences
**Answer: b) trees with crops in a living architecture**
