# Video script — Indigenous Knowledge and Responsible Citizens — Caring for the Land

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing: environmental conservation is the wise use of natural resources — and indigenous knowledge carries it in Ethiopia, in the Konso terraces and the Gedeo agroforestry, bound to the land by culture itself.
The rain tests every hillside.
Konso answered with stone steps.
Gedeo answered with layered trees.
No inspector guards them.
Culture itself guards them —
responsibility, and prohibition, carried in the people.
