# Hands-on — Indigenous Knowledge and Responsible Citizens — Caring for the Land

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Terrace in miniature
On a slope of soil in a tray, the group builds tiny stone terraces on one half and leaves the other bare. Pour water over both. Measure what each half kept.

## Layer the forest
With drawings or real leaves and sticks, the group layers a Gedeo-style plot: tall tree, mid crop, ground crop — and names what each layer gives the others.
