# Indigenous Knowledge and Responsible Citizens — Settling Conflicts — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** A responsible citizen is a change agent for —
a) only their own wealth  b) sustainable peace and socio-economic transformation  c) winning court cases  d) avoiding community life
**Answer: b) sustainable peace and socio-economic transformation**

**2.** Jaarsummaa belongs to the —
a) Afar  b) Somali  c) Oromo  d) Gurage
**Answer: c) Oromo**

**3.** Elder-settlement is rated above the modern court for —
a) punishing harder  b) ensuring sustainable peace  c) raising fees  d) speed alone
**Answer: b) ensuring sustainable peace**
