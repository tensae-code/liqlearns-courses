# Indigenous Knowledge and Responsible Citizens — Settling Conflicts — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name four named systems with their peoples.
**Answer:** Any four of: Jaarsummaa (Oromo); Shimglina and Erq (Amhara); Yejoka (Gurage); Mada'a and Mablo (Afar); Gedana (Sidama); Guurt (Somali).

**2.** Why does elder-settlement safeguard more than the case it settles?
**Answer:** Because it protects the community's political, social, cultural, economic and religious life together — restoring the neighbourhood, not merely ending a file.
