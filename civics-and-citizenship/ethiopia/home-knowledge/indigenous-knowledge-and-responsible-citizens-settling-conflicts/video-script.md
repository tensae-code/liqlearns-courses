# Video script — Indigenous Knowledge and Responsible Citizens — Settling Conflicts

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The scene: a Saturday, a great tree, a boundary dispute.

Two neighbours arrive angry, each with his story sharpened.
The elders sit them down — in the shade, in the open, before the community.

First elder: 'Speak. All of it. We will listen to the end.'
The first neighbour speaks. Nobody interrupts.
The second neighbour speaks. Nobody interrupts.

Questions come, slowly. The old markers are remembered. Witnesses are called.
A settlement is shaped that both men can live inside —
not a winner and a loser, but two neighbours again.

It ends with coffee, shared. The boundary is walked together.
That is the mechanism. That is what you are apprenticed to.
