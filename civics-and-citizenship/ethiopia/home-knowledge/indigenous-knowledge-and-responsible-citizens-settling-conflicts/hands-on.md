# Hands-on — Indigenous Knowledge and Responsible Citizens — Settling Conflicts

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The elder mediation, role-played
Two learners dispute a boundary; three play elders; the rest observe with a checklist: Was it low-cost? Peaceful? Did both sides keep dignity? Did the settlement plan for tomorrow, not just today?

## Systems map
On a wall map, the group pins the named systems to their peoples' regions, in the book's spellings, and each learner learns to say two names not their own people's.
