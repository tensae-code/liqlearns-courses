# Indigenous Knowledge — What It Is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Indigenous knowledge is developed through —
a) one inventor's lifetime  b) long histories of social life and interaction with the environment  c) imported textbooks  d) government decree
**Answer: b) long histories of social life and interaction with the environment**

**2.** Which is NOT named among Ethiopia's rich fields of it?
a) Pottery  b) Medicine  c) Conflict resolution  d) Rocket science
**Answer: c) Conflict resolution**
