# Indigenous Knowledge — What It Is — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Give the full definition in your own words.
**Answer:** Knowledge a local people develops through long histories of living together and with its environment, based on its culture, traditions, rituals, values and beliefs.

**2.** Why is the elders-first pattern called a *knowledge* system, not just a habit?
**Answer:** Because it carries tested, organised know-how — how to settle conflict to a win-win — handed down and applied deliberately across most of Ethiopia.
