# Video script — Indigenous Knowledge — What It Is

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing: indigenous knowledge is the knowledge a local people builds through long histories of living with its environment — carried in its culture, rituals, values and beliefs, and different from society to society.
Who taught the village to settle a feud?
No book. No school.
Long life on this land taught it.
Knowledge carried in elders, in proverbs, in hands —
that is indigenous knowledge.
Your community holds a library with no shelves.
