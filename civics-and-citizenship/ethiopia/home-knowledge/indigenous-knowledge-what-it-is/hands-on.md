# Hands-on — Indigenous Knowledge — What It Is

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The knowledge walk
The group walks the neighbourhood with a guide-elder or craftsperson if one will come, noting knowledge in use: a terrace, a remedy, a settlement seat under a tree. Everything noted is reported back and sorted by field.

## Proverb carriers
Each learner brings one proverb from home and teaches it: its words, and the knowledge compressed inside it. Elders' proverbs are knowledge in travelling form.
