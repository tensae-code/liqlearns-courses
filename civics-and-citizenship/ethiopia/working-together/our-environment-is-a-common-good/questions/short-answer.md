# Our Environment Is a Common Good — Plant, Terrace, Waste Properly, Reuse — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Whose responsibility is protecting the environment?
**Answer:** Everyone's — individuals, groups, communities, government and non-governmental organisations worldwide.

**2.** Name three ways of protecting it.
**Answer:** Any three of: plant seedlings; build terraces; dispose of waste properly; use alternative energy; cut misuse of water, fuel and electricity; reuse; repair; recycle.

**3.** In your own words, what is environment?
**Answer:** Where we live and everything around us.
