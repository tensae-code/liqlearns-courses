# Our Environment Is a Common Good — Plant, Terrace, Waste Properly, Reuse — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The environment is where we live and everything around us, and protecting it is the responsibility of everyone .
**Answer: True**

**2.** Plant seedlings and build terraces.
**Answer: True**

**3.** Protecting the environment is the job of governments only; individuals have no part.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite.
