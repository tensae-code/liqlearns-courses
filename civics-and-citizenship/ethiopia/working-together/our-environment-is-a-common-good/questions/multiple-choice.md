# Our Environment Is a Common Good — Plant, Terrace, Waste Properly, Reuse — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) the political, economic and social benefits of heritage  b) what endangers heritage  c) protecting the environment — everyone's responsibility  d) the nine core community values
**Answer: c) protecting the environment — everyone's responsibility** — That is the one thing this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does “environment” mean?
a) to process used plastic, paper and glass so they can be used again  b) where we live and everything around us  c) keeping a thing able to last and serve, now and for the future  d) a stepped bank built on a slope to hold soil and water
**Answer: b) where we live and everything around us** — “environment” means: where we live and everything around us.

**3.** What does “sustainability” mean?
a) keeping a thing able to last and serve, now and for the future  b) where we live and everything around us  c) a stepped bank built on a slope to hold soil and water  d) to process used plastic, paper and glass so they can be used again
**Answer: a) keeping a thing able to last and serve, now and for the future** — “sustainability” means: keeping a thing able to last and serve, now and for the future.

**4.** What does “terrace” mean?
a) where we live and everything around us  b) keeping a thing able to last and serve, now and for the future  c) to process used plastic, paper and glass so they can be used again  d) a stepped bank built on a slope to hold soil and water
**Answer: d) a stepped bank built on a slope to hold soil and water** — “terrace” means: a stepped bank built on a slope to hold soil and water.

**5.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  b) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  c) Dispose of dry and liquid waste properly; oppose dumping waste in ditches, rivers, ponds and green places  d) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.
**Answer: c) Dispose of dry and liquid waste properly; oppose dumping waste in ditches, rivers, ponds and green places** — It is one of this lesson's own teachings.
