# Lesson plan — Our Environment Is a Common Good — Plant, Terrace, Waste Properly, Reuse

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Working Together and the Common Good · **Lesson:** Our Environment Is a Common Good — Plant, Terrace, Waste Properly, Reuse
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 5 — Working Together and the Common Good (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the environment is a global common good and protecting it is everyone's responsibility: plant seedlings, build terraces, dispose of waste properly, save water and energy, reuse and recycle.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the lessons before it in this title — the learner already knows the earlier circles of this course (myself, then family, neighbours, school, community and culture) as far as this title. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. The environment is where we live and everything around us, and protecting it is the responsibility of everyone — individuals, groups, communities, government and non-governmental organisations worldwide.
2. The environment is called a global common good.
3. Protecting it means guarding it from danger for sustainability and human wellbeing.
4. Plant seedlings and build terraces.
5. Dispose of dry and liquid waste properly; oppose dumping waste in ditches, rivers, ponds and green places.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson's scenes are the learner's own home, school and neighbourhood.
