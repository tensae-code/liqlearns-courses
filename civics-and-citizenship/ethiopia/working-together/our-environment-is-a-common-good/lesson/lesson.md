# Our Environment Is a Common Good — Plant, Terrace, Waste Properly, Reuse

## Do it first

Plant or tend one living thing today, or rescue one thrown thing and reuse it. Small act, large lesson: the environment is a common good, and you are one of its keepers.

## Everyone's responsibility

The environment is where we live and everything around us, and protecting it is the responsibility of everyone — individuals, groups, communities, government and non-governmental organisations worldwide.

The environment is called a global common good.

Protecting it means guarding it from danger for sustainability and human wellbeing.

## The ways

Plant seedlings and build terraces.

Dispose of dry and liquid waste properly; oppose dumping waste in ditches, rivers, ponds and green places.

Use alternative energy — hydro, solar, wind — and cut misuse of water, fuel and electricity.

Reuse materials, repair broken things for long use, and recycle plastic, paper and glass.

A protected environment gives clean air and water, lasting resources, healthy life and economic growth; an unprotected one brings pollution, wildfire, disease, drought, displacement and resource conflict.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the environment is a global common good and protecting it is everyone's responsibility: plant seedlings, build terraces, dispose of waste properly, save water and energy, reuse and recycle. Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood, and name the lesson's key words: environment, sustainability, terrace. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: protecting the environment — everyone's responsibility.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
