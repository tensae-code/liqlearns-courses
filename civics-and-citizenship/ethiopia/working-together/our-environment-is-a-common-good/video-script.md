# Video script — Our Environment Is a Common Good — Plant, Terrace, Waste Properly, Reuse

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The environment is a global common good and protecting it is everyone's responsibility: plant seedlings, build terraces, dispose of waste properly, save water and energy, reuse and recycle.

The environment is where we live and everything around us, and protecting it is the responsibility of everyone — individuals, groups, communities, government and non-governmental organisations worldwide.

The environment is called a global common good.

Protecting it means guarding it from danger for sustainability and human wellbeing.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
