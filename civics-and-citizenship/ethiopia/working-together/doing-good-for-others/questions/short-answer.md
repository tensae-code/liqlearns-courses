# Doing Good for Others — at School and at Home — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the chapter's working definition of doing good for others?
**Answer:** Putting others' benefit and good before one's own.

**2.** Where is the behaviour shown and practised?
**Answer:** At school and in the community; role-play at home is set as practice.

**3.** In your own words, what is virtue?
**Answer:** A good quality of character, practised until it is yours.
