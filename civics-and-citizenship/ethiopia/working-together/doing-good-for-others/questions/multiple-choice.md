# Doing Good for Others — at School and at Home — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) cooperation: together for a common benefit  b) doing good for others, at school and at home  c) joining in: clubs and co-curricular activities  d) neighbourliness: friendly, cooperative neighbours
**Answer: b) doing good for others, at school and at home** — That is the one thing this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does “virtue” mean?
a) a good quality of character, practised until it is yours  b) honest comment that builds the person up and improves the act  c) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  d) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.
**Answer: a) a good quality of character, practised until it is yours** — “virtue” means: a good quality of character, practised until it is yours.

**3.** What does “constructive feedback” mean?
a) a good quality of character, practised until it is yours  b) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  c) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  d) honest comment that builds the person up and improves the act
**Answer: d) honest comment that builds the person up and improves the act** — “constructive feedback” means: honest comment that builds the person up and improves the act.

**4.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  b) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  c) Pupils must show the behaviour of doing good for others at school and in the community  d) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.
**Answer: c) Pupils must show the behaviour of doing good for others at school and in the community** — It is one of this lesson's own teachings.

**5.** Which of these is true in this lesson?
a) Promises, truth and fairness are not needed between people.  b) Doing good for others is a named content line of the chapter  c) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  d) The strongest person may take whatever they want.
**Answer: b) Doing good for others is a named content line of the chapter** — It is stated in the lesson.
