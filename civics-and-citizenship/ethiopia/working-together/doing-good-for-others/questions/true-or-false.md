# Doing Good for Others — at School and at Home — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Doing good for others is a named content line of the chapter: a matter of one's own belief and action.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Pupils must show the behaviour of doing good for others at school and in the community.
**Answer: True**

**3.** Doing good for others means always putting your own benefit first.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite.
