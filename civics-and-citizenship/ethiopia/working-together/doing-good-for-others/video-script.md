# Video script — Doing Good for Others — at School and at Home

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Doing good for others means putting others' benefit and good before your own — shown in behaviour at school and in the community, and practised at home.

Doing good for others is a named content line of the chapter: a matter of one's own belief and action.

Putting others' benefit and good before one's own is the chapter's working definition of the virtue.

Pupils must show the behaviour of doing good for others at school and in the community.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
