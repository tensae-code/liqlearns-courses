# Doing Good for Others — at School and at Home

## Do it first

Do one unasked good for someone today — carry, fetch, help, share — before you read a word. Then come and read what you just did, named as a virtue.

## The virtue named

Doing good for others is a named content line of the chapter: a matter of one's own belief and action.

Putting others' benefit and good before one's own is the chapter's working definition of the virtue.

[Picture 28053 — helping an elder carry a load]

## Shown and practised

Pupils must show the behaviour of doing good for others at school and in the community.

Role-play at home is set as practice, with constructive feedback — the home is the training ground.

[GIF 28054 — a helping hand taking half a heavy load]

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: doing good for others means putting others' benefit and good before your own — shown in behaviour at school and in the community, and practised at home. Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood, and name the lesson's key words: virtue, constructive feedback. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
