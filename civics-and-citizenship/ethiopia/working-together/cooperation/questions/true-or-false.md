# Cooperation — Together for a Common Benefit — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Cooperation is a group of people working together in unity for a common benefit, formed of two or more people.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Working together brings sharing new ideas, and hard work made easy.
**Answer: True**

**3.** Cooperation needs only one person working alone.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite.
