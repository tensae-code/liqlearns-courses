# Cooperation — Together for a Common Benefit — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is cooperation?
**Answer:** A group of people working together in unity for a common benefit — two or more people.

**2.** Name two gains of working together, and one good use of working alone.
**Answer:** Together: sharing new ideas, hard work made easy. Alone: studying for an exam or doing simple tasks.

**3.** In your own words, what is cooperation?
**Answer:** A group of people working together in unity for a common benefit.
