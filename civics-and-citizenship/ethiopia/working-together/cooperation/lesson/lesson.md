# Cooperation — Together for a Common Benefit

## Do it first

Find one heavy or hard thing near you that you cannot do alone. Who could lift it with you? That moment — two or more, one aim — is cooperation. Read its definition with your hands still remembering the weight.

## What cooperation is

Cooperation is a group of people working together in unity for a common benefit, formed of two or more people.

[Picture 28060 — lifting together what none lifts alone]

## Together and alone

Working together brings sharing new ideas, and hard work made easy.

Working alone keeps its own uses — studying for an exam, doing simple tasks.

The wise person knows which work asks for hands together and which asks for quiet alone.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: cooperation is a group of people working together in unity for a common benefit — sharing new ideas and making hard work easy. Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood, and name the lesson's key words: cooperation, common benefit, unity. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
