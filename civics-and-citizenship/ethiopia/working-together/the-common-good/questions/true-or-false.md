# The Common Good — What Belongs to All of Us — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The common good is the materials, benefits, institutions, services and culture that are a benefit or wealth for all human beings .
**Answer: True**

**2.** The common good serves only people of one religion, one age or one culture.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite.
