# The Common Good — What Belongs to All of Us — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is the common good?
**Answer:** The materials, benefits, institutions, services and culture that benefit all human beings.

**2.** Whom does it serve?
**Answer:** Everyone, without difference of sex, age, religion, culture or race.

**3.** In your own words, what is common good?
**Answer:** Materials, benefits, institutions, services and culture that benefit all.
