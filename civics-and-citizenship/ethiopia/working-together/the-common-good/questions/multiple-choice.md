# The Common Good — What Belongs to All of Us — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) learning truthfulness from Kebede and from Yonatan's story  b) the dignity we are born with, and the dignity we earn  c) tangible and intangible heritage; movable and immovable  d) the common good: what benefits all of us
**Answer: d) the common good: what benefits all of us** — That is the one thing this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does “common good” mean?
a) a good a thing brings to people  b) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  c) materials, benefits, institutions, services and culture that benefit all  d) a standing body or service that serves everyone — a school, a clinic
**Answer: c) materials, benefits, institutions, services and culture that benefit all** — “common good” means: materials, benefits, institutions, services and culture that benefit all.

**3.** What does “institution” mean?
a) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  b) a standing body or service that serves everyone — a school, a clinic  c) materials, benefits, institutions, services and culture that benefit all  d) a good a thing brings to people
**Answer: b) a standing body or service that serves everyone — a school, a clinic** — “institution” means: a standing body or service that serves everyone — a school, a clinic.

**4.** What does “benefit” mean?
a) a good a thing brings to people  b) materials, benefits, institutions, services and culture that benefit all  c) a standing body or service that serves everyone — a school, a clinic  d) The strongest person may take whatever they want.
**Answer: a) a good a thing brings to people** — “benefit” means: a good a thing brings to people.

**5.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  b) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  c) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  d) People may hold different identities in their social, economic, political and cultural lives 
**Answer: d) People may hold different identities in their social, economic, political and cultural lives ** — It is one of this lesson's own teachings.
