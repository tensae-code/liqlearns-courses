# The Common Good — What Belongs to All of Us

## Do it first

Name five things in your village or town that no single family owns, but everyone uses. That list is this lesson's subject — the common good.

## What the common good is

The common good is the materials, benefits, institutions, services and culture that are a benefit or wealth for all human beings — individuals, groups, the community, the country's people and the world.

It serves everyone without difference of sex, age, religion, culture or race.

People may hold different identities in their social, economic, political and cultural lives — yet all are human beings, so the value of humankind as a whole is the common good of all its members, and the growth of the members is the growth of all.

In short: common goods are the things we all use together and share equally.
