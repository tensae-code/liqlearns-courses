# Lesson plan — The Common Good — What Belongs to All of Us

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Working Together and the Common Good · **Lesson:** The Common Good — What Belongs to All of Us
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 5 — Working Together and the Common Good (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the common good is the materials, benefits, institutions, services and culture that benefit all — serving everyone without difference of sex, age, religion, culture or race.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the lessons before it in this title — the learner already knows the earlier circles of this course (myself, then family, neighbours, school, community and culture) as far as this title. It needs nothing else.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. The common good is the materials, benefits, institutions, services and culture that are a benefit or wealth for all human beings — individuals, groups, the community, the country's people and the world.
2. It serves everyone without difference of sex, age, religion, culture or race.
3. People may hold different identities in their social, economic, political and cultural lives — yet all are human beings, so the value of humankind as a whole is the common good of all its members, and the growth of the members is the growth of all.
4. In short.

- Gives the words but no example → ask for the lesson happening once in their own home, school or street today; the example is the proof.
