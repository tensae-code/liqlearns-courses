# Video script — How Ethiopia Works Together — Debo, Idir, Equb

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Ethiopia's own culture of cooperation: debo, the communal work party; idir, the mutual-aid association; equb, the rotating savings association — and group work at school.

Debo (the placement prints the Amharic gloss ጂጊ beside it) is the communal work party: neighbours gather to do one household's heavy work together.

Idir is the mutual-aid association: members support each other, above all in times of loss and burial.

Equb is the rotating savings association: members pay in together and the gathered sum goes to each member in turn.

At school, the same culture lives as group work.

Pupils identify the cooperation and teamwork culture in their own community and school.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
