# How Ethiopia Works Together — Debo, Idir, Equb — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) why self-control matters, and that it grows by practice  b) building positive relationships at school  c) protecting your own safety, at home and at school  d) debo, idir and equb — Ethiopia works together
**Answer: d) debo, idir and equb — Ethiopia works together** — That is the one thing this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does “debo (ጂጊ)” mean?
a) the rotating savings association — the gathered sum goes to each member in turn  b) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  c) the communal work party — neighbours doing one household's heavy work together  d) the mutual-aid association, supporting members in times of loss
**Answer: c) the communal work party — neighbours doing one household's heavy work together** — “debo (ጂጊ)” means: the communal work party — neighbours doing one household's heavy work together.

**3.** What does “idir” mean?
a) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  b) the mutual-aid association, supporting members in times of loss  c) the communal work party — neighbours doing one household's heavy work together  d) the rotating savings association — the gathered sum goes to each member in turn
**Answer: b) the mutual-aid association, supporting members in times of loss** — “idir” means: the mutual-aid association, supporting members in times of loss.

**4.** What does “equb” mean?
a) the rotating savings association — the gathered sum goes to each member in turn  b) the communal work party — neighbours doing one household's heavy work together  c) the mutual-aid association, supporting members in times of loss  d) The strongest person may take whatever they want.
**Answer: a) the rotating savings association — the gathered sum goes to each member in turn** — “equb” means: the rotating savings association — the gathered sum goes to each member in turn.

**5.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  b) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  c) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  d) Equb is the rotating savings association
**Answer: d) Equb is the rotating savings association** — It is one of this lesson's own teachings.
