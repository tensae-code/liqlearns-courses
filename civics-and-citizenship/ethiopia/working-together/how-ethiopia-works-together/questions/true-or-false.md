# How Ethiopia Works Together — Debo, Idir, Equb — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Debo (the placement prints the Amharic gloss ጂጊ beside it) is the communal work party: neighbours gather to do one household's ….
**Answer: True**

**2.** Idir is the mutual-aid association: members support each other, above all in times of loss and burial.
**Answer: True**

**3.** Equb is a work party that harvests one family's field.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite.
