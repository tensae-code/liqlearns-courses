# How Ethiopia Works Together — Debo, Idir, Equb — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is idir?
**Answer:** The mutual-aid association — members support each other, above all in times of loss and burial.

**2.** How does equb work?
**Answer:** Members pay in together and the gathered sum goes to each member in turn.

**3.** In your own words, what is debo (ጂጊ)?
**Answer:** The communal work party — neighbours doing one household's heavy work together.
