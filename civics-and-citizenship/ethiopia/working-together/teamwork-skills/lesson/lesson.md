# Teamwork Skills — Listen, Discuss, Debate, Use Time, Solve Problems

## Do it first

Take one real small problem you have. Walk it through seven steps as you read: identify, analyse, prepare criteria, consider solutions, decide, carry out, follow up. Your problem is this lesson's practice ground.

## The skills, one by one

Goodwill — frankness of heart (ቅን-አሳቢነት): wanting the team's good honestly.

Communication and organisation: saying clearly, and arranging the work.

Thinking deeply, and adaptability — fitting yourself or things to changing conditions.

Debate: a formal discussion on a special matter at a meeting, where debaters try to persuade opponents or audience. The sample topic is “which is better, money or knowledge” — and debate topics are kept free of religious or other sensitive content.

Listening to others' ideas is named among the skills.

## Using time well

Set achievable goals.

Do tasks by urgency and importance.

Set a time for each task; plan; and work by the plan.

## Problem-solving in seven steps

Step 1: identify the problem. Step 2: analyse it. Step 3: prepare criteria.

Step 4: consider possible solutions. Step 5: decide on the solution.

Step 6: carry the decision out. Step 7: follow up the decision.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: teamwork runs on named skills — goodwill, communication, organisation, deep thinking, adaptability, debate, using time well, listening, and problem-solving in seven fixed steps. Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood, and name the lesson's key words: goodwill (ቅን-አሳቢነት), adaptability, debate. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
