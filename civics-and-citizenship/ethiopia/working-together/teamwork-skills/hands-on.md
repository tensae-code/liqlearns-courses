# Hands-on — Teamwork Skills — Listen, Discuss, Debate, Use Time, Solve Problems

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Take one real small problem you have. Walk it through seven steps as you read: identify, analyse, prepare criteria, consider solutions, decide, carry out, follow up. Your problem is this lesson's practice ground.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the skills of teamwork, and the seven problem-solving steps. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
