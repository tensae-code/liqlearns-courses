# Teamwork Skills — Listen, Discuss, Debate, Use Time, Solve Problems — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) selfishness against the common good  b) the traits that grow good friendships  c) the skills of teamwork, and the seven problem-solving steps  d) ways of respecting parents, and what harms family peace
**Answer: c) the skills of teamwork, and the seven problem-solving steps** — That is the one thing this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does “goodwill (ቅን-አሳቢነት)” mean?
a) the measures you judge possible solutions by  b) frankness of heart — honestly wanting the team's good  c) fitting yourself or things to changing conditions  d) a formal discussion where debaters try to persuade opponents or audience
**Answer: b) frankness of heart — honestly wanting the team's good** — “goodwill (ቅን-አሳቢነት)” means: frankness of heart — honestly wanting the team's good.

**3.** What does “adaptability” mean?
a) fitting yourself or things to changing conditions  b) frankness of heart — honestly wanting the team's good  c) a formal discussion where debaters try to persuade opponents or audience  d) the measures you judge possible solutions by
**Answer: a) fitting yourself or things to changing conditions** — “adaptability” means: fitting yourself or things to changing conditions.

**4.** What does “debate” mean?
a) frankness of heart — honestly wanting the team's good  b) fitting yourself or things to changing conditions  c) the measures you judge possible solutions by  d) a formal discussion where debaters try to persuade opponents or audience
**Answer: d) a formal discussion where debaters try to persuade opponents or audience** — “debate” means: a formal discussion where debaters try to persuade opponents or audience.

**5.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  b) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  c) Set achievable goals  d) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.
**Answer: c) Set achievable goals** — It is one of this lesson's own teachings.
