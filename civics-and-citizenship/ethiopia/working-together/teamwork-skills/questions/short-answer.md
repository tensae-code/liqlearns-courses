# Teamwork Skills — Listen, Discuss, Debate, Use Time, Solve Problems — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** List the seven problem-solving steps in order.
**Answer:** Identify the problem; analyse it; prepare criteria; consider possible solutions; decide on the solution; carry the decision out; follow up the decision.

**2.** What are the time-use steps?
**Answer:** Set achievable goals; do tasks by urgency and importance; set a time for each task; plan; work by the plan.

**3.** In your own words, what is goodwill (ቅን-አሳቢነት)?
**Answer:** Frankness of heart — honestly wanting the team's good.
