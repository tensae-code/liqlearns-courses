# Why Cooperation and Teamwork Matter

## Do it first

Think of a hard concept you only understood after discussing it with others. That is one of this lesson's four reasons, lived. Read the other three.

## The importance list

It helps share experience, knowledge, skill and strength among the group's members.

It makes people from different backgrounds and experiences work together and support each other.

It introduces and grows peer relationships and interactions.

It helps pupils discuss and understand difficult concepts.

Each member can contribute knowledge, talent and skill to the group, and suggest new ideas for solving problems and reaching the common goal.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: cooperation and teamwork matter because they share experience, knowledge, skill and strength, join different backgrounds into one work, grow friendships, and unlock difficult learning. Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood, and name the lesson's key words: peer, contribute. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: why cooperation and teamwork matter.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
