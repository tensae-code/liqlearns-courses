# Lesson plan — Why Cooperation and Teamwork Matter

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Working Together and the Common Good · **Lesson:** Why Cooperation and Teamwork Matter
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 5 — Working Together and the Common Good (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** cooperation and teamwork matter because they share experience, knowledge, skill and strength, join different backgrounds into one work, grow friendships, and unlock difficult learning.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the lessons before it in this title — the learner already knows the earlier circles of this course (myself, then family, neighbours, school, community and culture) as far as this title. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Teamwork Skills — Listen, Discuss, Debate, Use Time, Solve Problems (the previous lesson in this title). Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. It helps share experience, knowledge, skill and strength among the group's members.
2. It makes people from different backgrounds and experiences work together and support each other.
3. It introduces and grows peer relationships and interactions.
4. It helps pupils discuss and understand difficult concepts.
5. Each member can contribute knowledge, talent and skill to the group, and suggest new ideas for solving problems and reaching the common goal.
