# Video script — Why Cooperation and Teamwork Matter

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Cooperation and teamwork matter because they share experience, knowledge, skill and strength, join different backgrounds into one work, grow friendships, and unlock difficult learning.

It helps share experience, knowledge, skill and strength among the group's members.

It makes people from different backgrounds and experiences work together and support each other.

It introduces and grows peer relationships and interactions.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
