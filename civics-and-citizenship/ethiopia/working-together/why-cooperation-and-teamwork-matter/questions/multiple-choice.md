# Why Cooperation and Teamwork Matter — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) the five ways of protecting heritage  b) why cooperation and teamwork matter  c) true words, kept words, clean hands  d) the ten elements of local culture
**Answer: b) why cooperation and teamwork matter** — That is the one thing this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does “peer” mean?
a) a person of your own level — a fellow pupil or age-mate  b) to give your share into a shared work  c) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  d) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.
**Answer: a) a person of your own level — a fellow pupil or age-mate** — “peer” means: a person of your own level — a fellow pupil or age-mate.

**3.** What does “contribute” mean?
a) a person of your own level — a fellow pupil or age-mate  b) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  c) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  d) to give your share into a shared work
**Answer: d) to give your share into a shared work** — “contribute” means: to give your share into a shared work.

**4.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  b) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  c) It introduces and grows peer relationships and interactions  d) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.
**Answer: c) It introduces and grows peer relationships and interactions** — It is one of this lesson's own teachings.

**5.** Which of these is true in this lesson?
a) Promises, truth and fairness are not needed between people.  b) It helps share experience, knowledge, skill and strength among the group's members  c) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  d) The strongest person may take whatever they want.
**Answer: b) It helps share experience, knowledge, skill and strength among the group's members** — It is stated in the lesson.
