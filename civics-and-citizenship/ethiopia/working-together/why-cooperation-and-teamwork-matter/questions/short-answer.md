# Why Cooperation and Teamwork Matter — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name three reasons cooperation matters.
**Answer:** Any three of: sharing experience, knowledge, skill and strength; different backgrounds working together; growing peer relationships; understanding difficult concepts together.

**2.** What can each member contribute?
**Answer:** Knowledge, talent and skill — and new ideas for solving problems and reaching the common goal.

**3.** In your own words, what is peer?
**Answer:** A person of your own level — a fellow pupil or age-mate.
