# Public Property Is Ours — Use It Properly, Keep It for Tomorrow — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) the obstacles to the common good  b) what a community is, and its four kinds  c) positive social interaction: good relationships, on purpose  d) using public property properly, keeping it for tomorrow
**Answer: d) using public property properly, keeping it for tomorrow** — That is the one thing this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does “public property” mean?
a) using more of a shared thing than is fair or lasting  b) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  c) wealth held for everyone's use — natural and man-made materials and services  d) a people's habit of using resources economically and keeping for tomorrow
**Answer: c) wealth held for everyone's use — natural and man-made materials and services** — “public property” means: wealth held for everyone's use — natural and man-made materials and services.

**3.** What does “saving culture” mean?
a) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  b) a people's habit of using resources economically and keeping for tomorrow  c) wealth held for everyone's use — natural and man-made materials and services  d) using more of a shared thing than is fair or lasting
**Answer: b) a people's habit of using resources economically and keeping for tomorrow** — “saving culture” means: a people's habit of using resources economically and keeping for tomorrow.

**4.** What does “over-use” mean?
a) using more of a shared thing than is fair or lasting  b) wealth held for everyone's use — natural and man-made materials and services  c) a people's habit of using resources economically and keeping for tomorrow  d) The strongest person may take whatever they want.
**Answer: a) using more of a shared thing than is fair or lasting** — “over-use” means: using more of a shared thing than is fair or lasting.

**5.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  b) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  c) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  d) Use resources responsibly and economically, and build a saving culture
**Answer: d) Use resources responsibly and economically, and build a saving culture** — It is one of this lesson's own teachings.
