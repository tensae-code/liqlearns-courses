# Public Property Is Ours — Use It Properly, Keep It for Tomorrow — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What does using public property properly mean?
**Answer:** Handling and guarding it carefully, responsibly, lawfully, scientifically and morally.

**2.** Name two things expected of the public.
**Answer:** Any two of: use resources responsibly and economically; know correct use; prevent destruction; share, maintain and protect; fight selfishness, waste, pollution and over-use.

**3.** In your own words, what is public property?
**Answer:** Wealth held for everyone's use — natural and man-made materials and services.
