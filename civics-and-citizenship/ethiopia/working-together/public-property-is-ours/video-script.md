# Video script — Public Property Is Ours — Use It Properly, Keep It for Tomorrow

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Public property is the people's wealth: use it carefully, responsibly, lawfully and morally — so it is saved from destruction and kept for the coming generation.

Public wealth is the natural and man-made materials, services and other things that contribute to human benefit.

Using it properly means handling and guarding it carefully, responsibly, lawfully, scientifically and morally.

Proper use saves it from destruction, theft, shortage and unfair distribution, creates an equal chance for the community to use it, and keeps it for the coming generation.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
