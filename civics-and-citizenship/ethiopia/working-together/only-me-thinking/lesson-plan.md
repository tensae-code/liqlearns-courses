# Lesson plan — Only-Me Thinking — Selfishness Against the Common Good

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Working Together and the Common Good · **Lesson:** Only-Me Thinking — Selfishness Against the Common Good
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 5 — Working Together and the Common Good (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** selfishness (ግለኝነት) is thinking only of yourself and protecting only yourself — set directly against the common good.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the lessons before it in this title — the learner already knows the earlier circles of this course (myself, then family, neighbours, school, community and culture) as far as this title. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video.
