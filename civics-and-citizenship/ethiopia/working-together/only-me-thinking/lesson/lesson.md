# Only-Me Thinking — Selfishness Against the Common Good

## Do it first

Watch for one only-me moment today — yours or another's: a queue jumped, a shared thing grabbed. Name what it cost the others. This lesson puts that thinking face to face with the common good.

## Two opposite ways

Selfishness (ግለኝነት) is thinking only of oneself, protecting only oneself.

The contents set selfishness directly against the common good.

Pupils explain the difference between the common good and selfish individualism as a chapter outcome.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: selfishness (ግለኝነት) is thinking only of yourself and protecting only yourself — set directly against the common good. Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood, and name the lesson's key words: selfishness (ግለኝነት), individualism (selfish). Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: selfishness against the common good.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
