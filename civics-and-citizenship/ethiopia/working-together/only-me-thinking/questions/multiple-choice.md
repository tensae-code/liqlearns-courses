# Only-Me Thinking — Selfishness Against the Common Good — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) selfishness against the common good  b) the benefits of a healthy social life  c) the four marks of a healthy social life  d) what self-respect is, why it matters, and how it is shown and built
**Answer: a) selfishness against the common good** — That is the one thing this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does “selfishness (ግለኝነት)” mean?
a) living as if only your own good counts  b) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  c) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  d) thinking only of oneself, protecting only oneself
**Answer: d) thinking only of oneself, protecting only oneself** — “selfishness (ግለኝነት)” means: thinking only of oneself, protecting only oneself.

**3.** What does “individualism (selfish)” mean?
a) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  b) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  c) living as if only your own good counts  d) thinking only of oneself, protecting only oneself
**Answer: c) living as if only your own good counts** — “individualism (selfish)” means: living as if only your own good counts.

**4.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  b) The contents set selfishness directly against the common good  c) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  d) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.
**Answer: b) The contents set selfishness directly against the common good** — It is one of this lesson's own teachings.

**5.** Which of these is true in this lesson?
a) Selfishness (ግለኝነት) is thinking only of oneself, protecting only oneself  b) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  c) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  d) Promises, truth and fairness are not needed between people.
**Answer: a) Selfishness (ግለኝነት) is thinking only of oneself, protecting only oneself** — It is stated in the lesson.
