# Video script — Why the Common Good Matters — Safety, Law and Each Other

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
No good life is lived alone: shared values make unity, standing for the common good needs participation and responsibility — and peace, law and rights stand or fall together.

No one can live a good life separated alone — our private wants are often the same as others'.

Shared values create unity, a common feeling, satisfaction and happiness.

Standing for the common good needs participation, cooperation, mutual support and citizens who take responsibility — striving for it is called a patriotic virtue of citizens.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
