# Hands-on — Why the Common Good Matters — Safety, Law and Each Other

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Try to name one good thing in your life that needs no other person at all. It is harder than it sounds. This lesson shows why the common good stands at the centre.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: why the common good matters: each other, safety and law. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
