# Why the Common Good Matters — Safety, Law and Each Other — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What does standing for the common good need?
**Answer:** Participation, cooperation, mutual support and citizens who take responsibility.

**2.** What could there be none of, without police, courts and a defence force?
**Answer:** No peace, no rule of law and no respect for human rights.

**3.** In your own words, what is patriotic virtue?
**Answer:** A good quality of a citizen who loves and serves their country.
