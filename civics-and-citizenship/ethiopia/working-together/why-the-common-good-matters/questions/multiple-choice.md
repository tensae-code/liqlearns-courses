# Why the Common Good Matters — Safety, Law and Each Other — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) respecting teachers, family, neighbours and classmates  b) learning truthfulness from Kebede and from Yonatan's story  c) why the common good matters: each other, safety and law  d) neighbourliness: friendly, cooperative neighbours
**Answer: c) why the common good matters: each other, safety and law** — That is the one thing this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does “patriotic virtue” mean?
a) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  b) a good quality of a citizen who loves and serves their country  c) everyone, rulers included, standing under the law  d) people holding each other up, both ways
**Answer: b) a good quality of a citizen who loves and serves their country** — “patriotic virtue” means: a good quality of a citizen who loves and serves their country.

**3.** What does “rule of law” mean?
a) everyone, rulers included, standing under the law  b) a good quality of a citizen who loves and serves their country  c) people holding each other up, both ways  d) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.
**Answer: a) everyone, rulers included, standing under the law** — “rule of law” means: everyone, rulers included, standing under the law.

**4.** What does “mutual support” mean?
a) a good quality of a citizen who loves and serves their country  b) everyone, rulers included, standing under the law  c) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  d) people holding each other up, both ways
**Answer: d) people holding each other up, both ways** — “mutual support” means: people holding each other up, both ways.

**5.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  b) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  c) Standing for the common good needs participation, cooperation, mutual support and citizens who take respons…  d) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.
**Answer: c) Standing for the common good needs participation, cooperation, mutual support and citizens who take respons…** — It is one of this lesson's own teachings.
