# Why the Common Good Matters — Safety, Law and Each Other — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** No one can live a good life separated alone .
**Answer: True**

**2.** Without police, courts and a defence force there could be no peace, no rule of law and no respect for human rights .
**Answer: True**

**3.** A good life can be lived completely separated and alone, needing no one.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite.
