# Lesson plan — What Blocks the Common Good

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Working Together and the Common Good · **Lesson:** What Blocks the Common Good
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 5 — Working Together and the Common Good (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the common good is blocked by selfishness, waste, destruction, theft, unfair distribution and pollution — naming the blockers is the first defence.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the lessons before it in this title — the learner already knows the earlier circles of this course (myself, then family, neighbours, school, community and culture) as far as this title. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.
