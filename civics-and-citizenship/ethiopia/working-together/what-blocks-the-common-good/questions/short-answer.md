# What Blocks the Common Good — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the blockers from the chapter's contrasts.
**Answer:** Selfishness, waste, destruction, theft, unfair distribution and pollution.

**2.** What do pupils do with the obstacles, as an outcome?
**Answer:** Identify the factors that influence common goods.

**3.** In your own words, what is obstacle?
**Answer:** Whatever blocks or hinders achieving a thing.
