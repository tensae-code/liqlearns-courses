# What Blocks the Common Good — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) the benefits of honesty, and the harms of its absence  b) the obstacles to the common good  c) cultural heritage: what one generation hands to the next  d) protecting your own safety, at home and at school
**Answer: b) the obstacles to the common good** — That is the one thing this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does “obstacle” mean?
a) whatever blocks or hinders achieving a thing  b) sharing out shared goods unequally for no just reason  c) dirtying air, water or land so they harm life  d) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.
**Answer: a) whatever blocks or hinders achieving a thing** — “obstacle” means: whatever blocks or hinders achieving a thing.

**3.** What does “unfair distribution” mean?
a) whatever blocks or hinders achieving a thing  b) dirtying air, water or land so they harm life  c) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  d) sharing out shared goods unequally for no just reason
**Answer: d) sharing out shared goods unequally for no just reason** — “unfair distribution” means: sharing out shared goods unequally for no just reason.

**4.** What does “pollution” mean?
a) sharing out shared goods unequally for no just reason  b) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  c) dirtying air, water or land so they harm life  d) whatever blocks or hinders achieving a thing
**Answer: c) dirtying air, water or land so they harm life** — “pollution” means: dirtying air, water or land so they harm life.

**5.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  b) Pupils identify the factors that influence common goods as a chapter outcome  c) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  d) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.
**Answer: b) Pupils identify the factors that influence common goods as a chapter outcome** — It is one of this lesson's own teachings.
