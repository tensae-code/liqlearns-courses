# Video script — What Blocks the Common Good

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The common good is blocked by selfishness, waste, destruction, theft, unfair distribution and pollution — naming the blockers is the first defence.

The chapter closes with the obstacles that keep common goods from being worked for and used properly.

Pupils identify the factors that influence common goods as a chapter outcome.

From the chapter's own contrasts, the blockers are: selfishness, waste, destruction, theft, unfair distribution and pollution.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
