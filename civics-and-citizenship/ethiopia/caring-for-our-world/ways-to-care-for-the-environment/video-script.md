**The practices.** The ways to care are habits of repair and restraint: closing taps so water is not wasted; fixing leaks instead of walking past them; and the chapter's other named habits of the same kind — repairing what drips, leaks and wastes, and restraining your own use to what is needed.

**Repair and restraint.** Sort the ways into two families and you will remember them: repair — fix the leak, mend the thing, stop the waste at its source; and restraint — close the tap, take what is needed, leave the rest for others and for tomorrow.

Now you. Tell your companion the ways to care for the environment from this lesson, sorted into repair and restraint.
