# Ways to Care for the Environment — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Closing taps so water is not wasted is —
a) a named way to care  b) a waste of time  c) only for plumbers
**Answer: a) a named way to care**

**2.** A drip you walk past every day should be —
a) ignored — drips are natural  b) covered with a bucket forever  c) fixed, or reported so it is fixed
**Answer: c) fixed, or reported so it is fixed**

**3.** Restraint means —
a) never using water  b) taking what is needed and leaving the rest  c) taking everything first
**Answer: b) taking what is needed and leaving the rest**

**4.** The two families of care in this lesson are —
a) repair and restraint  b) speech and song  c) buying and selling
**Answer: a) repair and restraint**
