# Ways to Care for the Environment — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1.** Name the practices this lesson teaches.
**Answer:** Closing taps so water is not wasted; fixing leaks; and the chapter's other named habits of repair and restraint.

**2.** Give one example of repair and one of restraint.
**Answer:** Repair: fixing a dripping tap. Restraint: closing a tap / taking only the water needed.
