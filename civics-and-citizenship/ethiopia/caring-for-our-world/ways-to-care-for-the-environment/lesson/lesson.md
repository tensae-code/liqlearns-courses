# Ways to Care for the Environment

## Do it first

Listen — really listen — for a drip anywhere near you: a tap, a pipe, a roof edge after rain. Each drip is water leaving. This lesson is a doing-lesson: its practices are things your hands can do today.

## The practices

The ways to care are habits of repair and restraint: closing taps so water is not wasted; fixing leaks instead of walking past them; and the chapter's other named habits of the same kind — repairing what drips, leaks and wastes, and restraining your own use to what is needed. Watch the tap in the moving picture: drip, drip — slower — stopped. That is the shape of this lesson's care.

## Repair and restraint

Sort the ways into two families and you will remember them: repair — fix the leak, mend the thing, stop the waste at its source; and restraint — close the tap, take what is needed, leave the rest for others and for tomorrow. A caring person practises both families every day.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the ways to care for the environment from this lesson, sorted into repair and restraint. Then describe the tap picture: drip, slower, stopped.

## Do It Yourself

1. Close one tap fully today that you find left dripping or running.
2. Report or help fix one leak at home or school this week.
3. For one day, take only the water you need — restraint in practice.
