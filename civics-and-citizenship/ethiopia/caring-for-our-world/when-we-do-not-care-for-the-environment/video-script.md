# Video script — When We Do Not Care for the Environment

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Not caring has consequences, and they come as a chain: water shortage; pollution; food shortage; loss of biodiversity — the variety of living things; health problems; flooding and storms; and acid rain.

**The chain of consequences.** Not caring has consequences, and they come as a chain: water shortage; pollution; food shortage; loss of biodiversity — the variety of living things; health problems; flooding and storms; and acid rain.

**Why the chain is taught.** The chain is not taught to frighten you into doing nothing.

Now you. Tell your companion the chain of consequences, link by link, in order.

And this week: reading the chain backwards — every link names where care would have held.
