# When We Do Not Care for the Environment — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which is a consequence of not caring for the environment?
a) Fuller rivers  b) Cleaner air  c) Water shortage
**Answer: c) Water shortage**

**2.** Loss of biodiversity means loss of —
a) market days  b) the variety of living things  c) school subjects
**Answer: b) the variety of living things**

**3.** The consequences come as —
a) a chain, each link pulling the next  b) unconnected accidents  c) punishments from people
**Answer: a) a chain, each link pulling the next**

**4.** Acid rain belongs to —
a) the four gifts  b) the ways to care  c) the chain of consequences
**Answer: c) the chain of consequences**
