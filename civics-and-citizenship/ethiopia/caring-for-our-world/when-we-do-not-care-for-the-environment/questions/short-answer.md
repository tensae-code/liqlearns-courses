# When We Do Not Care for the Environment — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1.** List the consequences of not caring, as the lesson gives them.
**Answer:** Water shortage; pollution; food shortage; loss of biodiversity; health problems; flooding and storms; acid rain.

**2.** Why is the chain taught?
**Answer:** So you know exactly what your care prevents — each link names a place where care would have held.
