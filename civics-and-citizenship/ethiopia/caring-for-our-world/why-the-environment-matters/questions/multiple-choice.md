# Why the Environment Matters — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The environment gives —
a) exams and holidays  b) money and fame  c) food, water, air and shelter
**Answer: c) food, water, air and shelter**

**2.** Care for the environment is important because —
a) it is fashionable  b) life itself is drawn from it  c) teachers check it daily
**Answer: b) life itself is drawn from it**

**3.** No breath is possible without —
a) air  b) shelter  c) grain
**Answer: a) air**

**4.** Caring for the environment is caring for —
a) scenery only  b) other people's problem only  c) life itself — yours and everyone's
**Answer: c) life itself — yours and everyone's**
