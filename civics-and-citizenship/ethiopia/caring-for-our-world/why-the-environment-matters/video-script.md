# Video script — Why the Environment Matters

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The environment gives food, water, air and shelter.

**Four gifts.** The environment gives food, water, air and shelter.

**Care follows the gifts.** That is the whole argument of the lesson, and it needs nothing added: life is drawn from the environment, therefore caring for it is caring for life itself — your own, your family's, and everyone's.

Now you. Tell your companion the four gifts of the environment, and complete the sentence: 'Care is important because ___.

And this week: matching what you live from to the gift it came from.
