# Why the Environment Matters

## Do it first

Before you read: list everything you have eaten, drunk, breathed or sheltered under today. Now ask where each one came from. Keep the list open beside you — the lesson will name its source.

## Four gifts

The environment gives food, water, air and shelter. Four gifts, and life itself is drawn from them: no food without soil and rain, no drink without water, no breath without air, no home without materials the earth provides. Care is important because of this simple fact — what we live from, we must not spoil.

## Care follows the gifts

That is the whole argument of the lesson, and it needs nothing added: life is drawn from the environment, therefore caring for it is caring for life itself — your own, your family's, and everyone's. The small acts of the last lesson are how this large truth gets done.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the four gifts of the environment, and complete the sentence: 'Care is important because ___.'

## Do It Yourself

1. Match today's meals and drinks to the four gifts — name which gift each came from.
2. Do one small act of care today, knowing which gift it protects.
3. Thank — in words or in care — one part of your environment: a tree, a water point, a garden.
