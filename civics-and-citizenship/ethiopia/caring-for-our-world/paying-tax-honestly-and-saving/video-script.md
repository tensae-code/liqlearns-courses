Trade is buying, selling and exchanging goods and services.

**Trade and market.** Trade is buying, selling and exchanging goods and services.

**Paying tax honestly.** Tax is the payment individuals and organisations make to government from their income.

**Saving.** Saving is putting aside money or time for the future instead of consuming it all.

Now you. Tell your companion: what trade is, what a market is, what tax is and why it must be paid, and what saving is.

And this week: the three piles — what is spent, what is saved, what is honestly owed.
