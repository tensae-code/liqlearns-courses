# Paying Tax Honestly and Saving — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Define tax and say why everyone has a duty to pay it.
**Answer:** The payment individuals and organisations make to government from their income; without it the common services — education, health, defence — cannot be had.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the modern saving institutions the lesson gives.
**Answer:** Banks and insurance companies.
