# Paying Tax Honestly and Saving — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Trade is —
a) only selling  b) only farming  c) buying, selling and exchanging goods and services
**Answer: c) buying, selling and exchanging goods and services**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Tax is —
a) a fine for wrongdoing  b) the payment individuals and organisations make to government from their income  c) a gift traders may give or skip
**Answer: b) the payment individuals and organisations make to government from their income**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Without tax, the common services that cannot be had include —
a) education, health and defence  b) markets and roads home  c) rain and sunlight
**Answer: a) education, health and defence**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Saving means —
a) spending slowly  b) hiding money from everyone  c) putting aside money or time for the future instead of consuming it all
**Answer: c) putting aside money or time for the future instead of consuming it all**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Modern saving institutions named are —
a) neighbours and friends  b) banks and insurance companies  c) markets and schools
**Answer: b) banks and insurance companies**
