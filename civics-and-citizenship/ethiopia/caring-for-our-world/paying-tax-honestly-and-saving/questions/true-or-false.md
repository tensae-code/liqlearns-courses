# Paying Tax Honestly and Saving — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Paying tax is a legal duty, not a favour.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A market is where goods are bought and sold.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Saving can be of time as well as money.
**Answer: True**
