# Lesson plan — Paying Tax Honestly and Saving

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Caring for Our Shared World · **Lesson:** Paying Tax Honestly and Saving
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 10 — Caring for Our Shared World (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Trade is buying, selling and exchanging goods and services.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the lessons before it in Level 10 — Caring for Our Shared World — and on the learner's own daily life at home, at school and in the community. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Trade and market** — Trade is buying, selling and exchanging goods and services.
2. **Paying tax honestly** — Tax is the payment individuals and organisations make to government from their income.
3. **Saving** — Saving is putting aside money or time for the future instead of consuming it all.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

1. Say and show what “Trade and market” teaches, in their own words, and do the practice named for it.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — every activity uses the learner's own home, school and street.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — Imagine a market stall at closing time. The seller counts the day: some coins paid over as tax with a receipt, some coins dropped in a savings box, the rest for tomorrow's goods. Three piles, three duties. Which pile is most often forgotten? Read on.
2. **The explanation** — the lesson's parts, in order (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 28246]: a picture of a market scene with a tax receipt being given, and a savings box beside the stall — the student studies the picture and names what it shows about the lesson before reading on.
   - ADD AUDIO: a voice reading the lesson's key sentences aloud — the student listens once, then reads them aloud in the same voice.
3. **Say it to your companion** — as written in lesson/lesson.md.
   - ADD VIDEO: the script in video-script.md, filmed — the student watches and then does the Say it to your companion with a real companion.
4. **Questions** — from questions/: multiple-choice, true-or-false, short-answer, fill-in-the-blank — served at the learner's level and teaching-mix (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** — the numbered doing block at the end of lesson/lesson.md.

## If the learner is stuck
- Cannot say what the lesson teaches → go back to the Do it first and the picture; name what the picture shows, in one sentence, then read the first part again slowly.
- Mixes this lesson up with a neighbour lesson → hold the one thing: Trade is buying, selling and exchanging goods and services.
- Finds the practice hard → shrink it to the smallest real version and do that today; small and done beats grand and promised.
