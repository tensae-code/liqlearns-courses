# Serving the Community the Right Way — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which is one of the guide's examples of service?
a) Planting trees  b) Planting rumours  c) Cleaning only your own desk and no more, ever
**Answer: a) Planting trees**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Confidentiality in service means —
a) never speaking to the person again  b) keeping your own acts secret  c) not repeating what someone told you in trust
**Answer: c) not repeating what someone told you in trust**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Consent means —
a) asking the whole town first  b) not acting for people without their agreement  c) agreeing with everything you are told
**Answer: b) not acting for people without their agreement**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Help that tramples people's rights —
a) stops being service, however busy it looks  b) is still perfect service  c) matters only for adults
**Answer: a) stops being service, however busy it looks**
