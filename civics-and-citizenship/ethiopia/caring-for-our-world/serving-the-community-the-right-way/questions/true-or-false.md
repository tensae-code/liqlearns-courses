# Serving the Community the Right Way — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Cleaning the classroom after lessons is counted as service.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A helper may repeat private things learned while helping, if it is interesting.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Basic rights limit how you may help people.
**Answer: True**
