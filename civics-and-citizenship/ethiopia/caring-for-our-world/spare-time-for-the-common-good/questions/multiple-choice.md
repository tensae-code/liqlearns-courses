# Spare Time for the Common Good — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Spare time is —
a) time when duties are ignored  b) only holiday months  c) time outside regular work and duties
**Answer: c) time outside regular work and duties**

**2.** The common good is —
a) the government's property only  b) what serves all members of the community  c) what serves the strongest member
**Answer: b) what serves all members of the community**

**3.** This lesson says spare time —
a) can be shared, in part, for the common good  b) must all be given away  c) should never be used for play
**Answer: a) can be shared, in part, for the common good**

**4.** Help given regularly in holidays becomes —
a) a burden nobody wants  b) invisible and useless  c) a pattern the community can rely on
**Answer: c) a pattern the community can rely on**
