# Spare Time for the Common Good — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1.** Define spare time and the common good.
**Answer:** Spare time: time outside regular work and duties. Common good: what serves all members of the community.

**2.** Give one example of spare time used for the common good.
**Answer:** Any community-serving use of break time, e.g. helping an elder carry water, cleaning a shared place, planting with neighbours.
