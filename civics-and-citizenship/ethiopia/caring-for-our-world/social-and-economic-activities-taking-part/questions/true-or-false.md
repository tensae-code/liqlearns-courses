# Social and Economic Activities — Taking Part — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Saving money is economic participation.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Repairing a communal well together is social participation.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Economic participation is shameful and should be avoided.
**Answer: False**
