# Social and Economic Activities — Taking Part — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Define both kinds of participation.
**Answer:** Social: members acting together to solve the community's problems. Economic: any act done to gain income, money or wealth — earning, consuming, saving, investing.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give the four economic acts named in the lesson.
**Answer:** Earning, consuming, saving, investing.
