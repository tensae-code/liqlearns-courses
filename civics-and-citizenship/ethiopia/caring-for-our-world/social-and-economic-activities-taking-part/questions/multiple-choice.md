# Social and Economic Activities — Taking Part — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Social participation is —
a) any act done to gain income  b) watching others work  c) members acting together to solve the community's problems
**Answer: c) members acting together to solve the community's problems**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Economic participation includes —
a) settling a village dispute  b) earning, consuming, saving, investing  c) repairing the communal well together
**Answer: b) earning, consuming, saving, investing**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The market stall in the picture stands for —
a) economic participation  b) social participation  c) neither kind
**Answer: a) economic participation**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** A person's life is shaped by —
a) social participation only  b) neither kind  c) both kinds of participation together
**Answer: c) both kinds of participation together**
