# Social and Economic Activities — Taking Part

## Do it first

Sort your own last week into two piles: acts where you acted with others to solve a shared problem, and acts done to earn, use, save or invest money or goods. Which pile is bigger? Which is empty? Read on with your piles in mind.

## Sub lesson 1 — Social participation

Social participation is members acting together to solve the community's problems. The well in the picture is being repaired by many hands — that is social participation: the community's problems met by the community's members, together, on purpose.

[Picture 28243 — the well and the market stall]

## Sub lesson 2 — Economic participation

Economic participation is any act done to gain income, money or wealth: earning, consuming, saving, investing. The market stall in the picture is its scene. Do not despise the second kind or idolise the first — a person's life and a community's growth are shaped by both kinds of participation together.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the difference between social and economic participation, with the picture's two scenes. Then give one example of each from your own life.

## Do It Yourself

1. Take part in one social act this week — a shared problem worked on together.
2. Do one economic act deliberately: save a small amount instead of consuming it.
3. Name one way the two kinds of participation help each other in your community.
