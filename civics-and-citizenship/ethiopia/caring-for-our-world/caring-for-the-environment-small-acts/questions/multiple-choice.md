# Caring for the Environment — Small Acts — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Care for the environment, in this lesson, consists of —
a) feelings about nature only  b) small acts like reusing a bottle and switching off lights  c) large speeches
**Answer: b) small acts like reusing a bottle and switching off lights**

**2.** Reusing a plastic bottle is better here than —
a) discarding it after one use  b) refilling it  c) washing it
**Answer: a) discarding it after one use**

**3.** Lights that are not needed should be —
a) left on all day  b) covered with cloth  c) switched off
**Answer: c) switched off**

**4.** Small acts matter because —
a) they are easy to announce  b) they are done a thousand times a day by everyone  c) they are photographed often
**Answer: b) they are done a thousand times a day by everyone**
