# Caring for the Environment — Small Acts — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1.** Name the two small acts the lesson gives.
**Answer:** Reusing a plastic bottle instead of discarding it; switching off lights that are not needed.

**2.** Why are small acts the real thing?
**Answer:** Because they are repeated daily by everyone everywhere — that is where a community's care actually lives.
