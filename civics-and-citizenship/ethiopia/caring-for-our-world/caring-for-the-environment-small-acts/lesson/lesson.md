# Caring for the Environment — Small Acts

## Do it first

Two small acts, right now if you can: refill a bottle instead of reaching for a new one, and switch off one light nobody needs. Care, in this lesson, is exactly that size — do the small acts first, then read why they carry the whole idea.

## Care made concrete

What does 'care for the environment' actually consist of? The lesson answers with small acts: reusing a plastic bottle instead of discarding it, and switching off lights that are not needed. Care is not a feeling about nature. Care is a bottle refilled and a switch clicked — repeated daily, by everyone, everywhere.

## Why small acts are the real thing

Big speeches about the environment change nothing by themselves. The small acts are where a community's care truly lives, because they are done a thousand times a day. A child who refills and switches off is not doing a symbol of care; they are doing care itself, in its commonest form.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the two small acts this lesson names, and explain why small acts are care itself, not symbols of it.

## Do It Yourself

1. Refill one bottle today instead of discarding it.
2. Switch off every unneeded light you pass today.
3. Count your small acts at day's end and tell someone the number.
