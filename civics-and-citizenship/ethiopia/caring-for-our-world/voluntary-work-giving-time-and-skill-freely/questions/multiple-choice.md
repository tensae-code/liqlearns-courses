# Voluntary Work — Giving Time and Skill Freely — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Voluntary work is giving —
a) advice from a distance  b) time, energy and knowledge freely, without seeking profit  c) only money
**Answer: b) time, energy and knowledge freely, without seeking profit**

**2.** Which is a named form of voluntary work?
a) Planting seedlings  b) Planting demands  c) Selling seedlings at double price
**Answer: a) Planting seedlings**

**3.** Volunteering can be done —
a) only in large groups  b) only by adults  c) alone or together
**Answer: c) alone or together**

**4.** The mark of volunteering is that it is —
a) compulsory  b) free — benefit flows to the community, not payment to the giver  c) secret
**Answer: b) free — benefit flows to the community, not payment to the giver**
