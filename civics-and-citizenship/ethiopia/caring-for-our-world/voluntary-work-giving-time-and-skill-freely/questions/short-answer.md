# Voluntary Work — Giving Time and Skill Freely — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1.** Define voluntary work in your own words.
**Answer:** Giving your time, energy and knowledge freely for the community's benefit, without seeking profit.

**2.** Name the four forms of voluntary work the answers give.
**Answer:** Helping people who need help; planting seedlings; cleaning the surroundings; farm work.
