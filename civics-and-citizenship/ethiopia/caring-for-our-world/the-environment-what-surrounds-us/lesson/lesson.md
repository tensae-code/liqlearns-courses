# The Environment — What Surrounds Us

## Do it first

Stand and turn slowly in a full circle. Everything your eyes pass over — air you breathe, ground under you, plants, water, animals, buildings, weather — is the subject of this lesson. Name five things in your circle before you read on.

## What the environment is

The environment is everything around a living thing that it lives in and from. Not only far forests and rivers — the ring around you where you stand: air, water, plants, animals, soil. A living thing does not sit beside its environment; it lives inside it, drawing every breath and meal from it.

## The floor of this band

This definition is the floor the rest of the band stands on. Caring, importance, ways and consequences in the next lessons all lean on it: you cannot care well for a thing you have not first seen clearly. So see it: you are at the centre of a ring you live from.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what the environment is, naming the ring around a living thing. Then name the parts of your own ring, standing where you are.

## Do It Yourself

1. Draw your own ring: yourself at the centre, and air, water, plants, animals and soil around you.
2. Name one thing you drew from your environment today — a breath, a drink, a meal.
3. Teach the definition to someone at home with their own circle-turn.
