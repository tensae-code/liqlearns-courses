# The Environment — What Surrounds Us — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The environment is —
a) only forests far away  b) only the weather  c) everything around a living thing that it lives in and from
**Answer: c) everything around a living thing that it lives in and from**

**2.** Which belongs in the ring around a child?
a) Only buildings  b) Air, water, plants, animals, soil  c) Only school books
**Answer: b) Air, water, plants, animals, soil**

**3.** A living thing lives —
a) inside its environment, drawing breath and food from it  b) beside its environment, untouched by it  c) above its environment
**Answer: a) inside its environment, drawing breath and food from it**

**4.** This definition is —
a) a side note of no importance  b) the last lesson of the band  c) the floor the rest of the band stands on
**Answer: c) the floor the rest of the band stands on**
