# Critical thinking skills — teamwork — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. **Teamwork means —
a) collaborating toward a common goal  b) each working alone fastest  c) following one star player
**Answer: a) collaborating toward a common goal**

**2. **Name one teamwork habit.
a) refusing unpopular jobs  b) accepting an unchosen task for the group's sake  c) silent agreement with everything
**Answer: b) accepting an unchosen task for the group's sake**

**3. **When consensus fails, the group —
a) abandons the task  b) votes again all day  c) follows the facilitator
**Answer: c) follows the facilitator**

**4. **The Walias image teaches that —
a) planning and cooperation put the ball at the striker's feet  b) the striker wins alone  c) defence does not matter
**Answer: a) planning and cooperation put the ball at the striker's feet**
