# Critical thinking skills — teamwork

## Do it first

A football move: four passes, defence to striker, goal. The striker scores — but whose goal is it really? Beside the pitch, four pupils pass work around a table the same way. Before reading, name what the passes have in common.

## The goal is shared

**Teamwork** is collaborating toward a common goal: **planning together, dividing tasks, checking progress, delivering on time**, debating approaches respectfully, **accepting an unchosen task** for the group's sake, and following the facilitator when consensus fails.

Its habits are listed: cooperation, responsibility, contributing ideas and effort, two-way communication, respect for different opinions and customs.

The Ethiopian national football team, the **Walias**, carries the image: the striker scores, but planning and cooperation put the ball at his feet. A goal in teamwork is never one person's, even when one name is on it.

## Say it to your companion

Use the lesson's own case or example, not the book's sentences. Your companion asks one hard question; answer it from the lesson, or say honestly what you would check.
