# Hands-on — Critical thinking skills — teamwork

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening again, for real
A football move: four passes, defence to striker, goal. The striker scores — but whose goal is it really? Beside the pitch, four pupils pass work around a table the same way. Before reading, name what the passes have in common.

## Sort it on the table
On paper, write the lesson's key pairs from its matching questions — one term per slip. Shuffle the slips, then sort and match them again from memory, saying each meaning aloud as you place it. Any pair you cannot explain goes back into the lesson for one more read.

## Take it home
Find this lesson where you live: one real example of its one thing — teamwork is collaborating toward a common goal — planning, dividing, checking, and accepting an unchosen task for the group's sake — in your family, your school or your street. Write two sentences about it: what happened, and what the lesson says about it. Bring the sentences back and read them to your companion.
