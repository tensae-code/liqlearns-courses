# Video script — Problem solving — what it is

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. A short script — this lesson's design names no full video-script need, so the script stays short.

---

A problem is a barrier between you and a goal.
**Problem solving** clears it:
listen fully, stay objective, propose what both can accept.
And at village scale — two farmers, a drought,
trees planted, grass restored, recovery within a year.
Old knowledge. New situation. Goal reached.
