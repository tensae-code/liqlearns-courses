# Problem solving — what it is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) **Problem solving is —
a) overcoming the barriers between you and success  b) waiting for problems to pass  c) blaming the barrier loudly
**Answer: a) overcoming the barriers between you and success**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) **In the friends case, the first step is to —
a) take the nearer friend's side  b) listen to both fully  c) ban both from the circle
**Answer: b) listen to both fully**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) **You must stay —
a) silent  b) neutral about your own results only  c) objective, as their mutual friend
**Answer: c) objective, as their mutual friend**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) **The deeper definition: reaching a goal when —
a) the solution is not obvious  b) help is guaranteed  c) the problem is small
**Answer: a) the solution is not obvious**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) **The two chosen remedies were —
a) resting the land and reducing cattle  b) community tree-planting and drought-resistant grass  c) moving the whole village
**Answer: b) community tree-planting and drought-resistant grass**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) **Recovery came within —
a) a month exactly  b) ten years  c) a year
**Answer: c) a year**
