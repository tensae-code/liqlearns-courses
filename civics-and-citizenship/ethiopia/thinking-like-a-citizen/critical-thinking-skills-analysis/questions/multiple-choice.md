# Critical thinking skills — analysis — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. **Analysis means —
a) examining something closely to judge its worth  b) memorising its parts  c) agreeing with it quickly
**Answer: a) examining something closely to judge its worth**

**2. **Which advice survives examination?
a) respect only your family  b) respect everyone  c) respect whoever is strongest
**Answer: b) respect everyone**

**3. **The failing advice fails because —
a) it is too short  b) it is old  c) it excludes
**Answer: c) it excludes**

**4. **In the fighting case, analysis weighs —
a) the consequences: injury, disrupted learning  b) who is more popular  c) who started it, only
**Answer: a) the consequences: injury, disrupted learning**

**5. **Analysis is finished when the analyst can —
a) keep the judgement private  b) explain why to the class  c) write it in a diary
**Answer: b) explain why to the class**
