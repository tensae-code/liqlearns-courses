# Hands-on — Critical thinking skills — analysis

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening again, for real
Two advice slips under a magnifier. One says: respect everyone. The other says: respect only your family. Before reading, examine both: which survives examination, and can you say exactly why?

## Sort it on the table
On paper, write the lesson's key pairs from its matching questions — one term per slip. Shuffle the slips, then sort and match them again from memory, saying each meaning aloud as you place it. Any pair you cannot explain goes back into the lesson for one more read.

## Take it home
Find this lesson where you live: one real example of its one thing — analysis is examining something closely from every side to judge its worth — and being able to explain the judgement — in your family, your school or your street. Write two sentences about it: what happened, and what the lesson says about it. Bring the sentences back and read them to your companion.
