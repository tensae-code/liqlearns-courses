# Lesson plan — Getting better at critical thinking — strategies that work

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Thinking Like a Citizen · **Lesson:** Getting better at critical thinking — strategies that work
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 21 — Thinking Like a Citizen (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** ten strategies improve critical thinking — and the guide's three core ones go deeper: estimate first, work backward, break the problem down.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the levels before it in this course's path order (recommended, never forced); inside its own title it stands on the lessons before it.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
1. **The ten strategies** — The book's warning opens the set: **children are not born critical thinkers, and most people never learn**.
2. **The guide's three core strategies in depth** — The guide's evidence goes deep on three.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Habits of a critical thinker. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. List the ten strategies.
2. Apply the guide's three core strategies.
3. Name the twin dangers: overload and paralysis.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — A maze. One thinker measures before entering. One traces the path backward from the exit. One stands frozen at the entrance, thinking and thinking, never stepping in. Before reading: all three are 'thinking hard'. Only two are strategies.
2. **The explanation** — the lesson's parts in order (lesson/lesson.md).
