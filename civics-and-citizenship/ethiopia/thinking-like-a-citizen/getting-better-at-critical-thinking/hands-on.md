# Hands-on — Getting better at critical thinking — strategies that work

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening again, for real
A maze. One thinker measures before entering. One traces the path backward from the exit. One stands frozen at the entrance, thinking and thinking, never stepping in. Before reading: all three are 'thinking hard'. Only two are strategies.

## Sort it on the table
On paper, write the lesson's key pairs from its matching questions — one term per slip. Shuffle the slips, then sort and match them again from memory, saying each meaning aloud as you place it. Any pair you cannot explain goes back into the lesson for one more read.

## Take it home
Find this lesson where you live: one real example of its one thing — ten strategies improve critical thinking — and the guide's three core ones go deeper: estimate first, work backward, break the problem down — in your family, your school or your street. Write two sentences about it: what happened, and what the lesson says about it. Bring the sentences back and read them to your companion.
