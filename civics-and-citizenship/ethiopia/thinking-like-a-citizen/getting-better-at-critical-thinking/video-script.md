# Video script — Getting better at critical thinking — strategies that work

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. A short script — this lesson's design names no full video-script need, so the script stays short.

---

Ten strategies. Three go deepest.
**Guesstimate** — guess the size before you calculate.
**Work backward** — from the goal to where you stand.
**Break it down** — pieces small enough to solve.
And beware the frozen thinker:
**analysis paralysis** is thinking that never decides.
