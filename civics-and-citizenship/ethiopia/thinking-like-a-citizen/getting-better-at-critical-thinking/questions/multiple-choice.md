# Getting better at critical thinking — strategies that work — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) **Name one of the ten strategies.
a) seeking patterns  b) rushing to the first answer  c) waiting to be born a thinker
**Answer: a) seeking patterns**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) **Manipulatives means —
a) handling people  b) moving objects to organise a problem  c) buying special equipment
**Answer: b) moving objects to organise a problem**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) **The book's opening warning is that most people —
a) are born critical thinkers  b) learn it without effort  c) never learn to think critically
**Answer: c) never learn to think critically**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) **A guesstimate is —
a) estimating before calculating  b) guessing the final answer and stopping  c) copying an estimate
**Answer: a) estimating before calculating**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) **Working backward starts from —
a) the first step  b) the goal  c) the hardest piece
**Answer: b) the goal**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) **Analysis paralysis is —
a) thinking in a group  b) checking an estimate  c) thinking so long that nothing is decided
**Answer: c) thinking so long that nothing is decided**
