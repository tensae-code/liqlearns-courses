# Video script — Problem-Solving and the Good Citizen

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing: solving problems with available information, using soft social skills, is what makes a competent, responsible citizen — and two attributes carry it: conflict-resolution skill and critical-thinking skill.
A citizen is not only good. A citizen can work.
Define. Gather. Decide. Do.
Handle conflict so bonds grow stronger.
Think with an open, logical mind.
Competence, practised, becomes character.
