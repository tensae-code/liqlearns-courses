# Hands-on — Problem-Solving and the Good Citizen

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The handshake checklist
Two learners enact a resolved dispute's handshake while a third reads the thought-bubble checklist aloud — what matters recognised; forgiveness ready; compromise sought; both served. The actors must be able to say yes to each line truthfully, in character.

## Wide perspectives
A local issue is placed on the table. Each learner must argue a perspective not their own, fairly enough that its real holder would accept the portrait. The class judges fairness, not agreement.
