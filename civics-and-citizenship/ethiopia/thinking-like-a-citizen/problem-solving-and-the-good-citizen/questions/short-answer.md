# Problem-Solving and the Good Citizen — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** State the lesson's binding claim.
**Answer:** Solving problems with available information, using soft social skills, is what makes a competent, responsible citizen.

**2.** Give the critical thinker's four marks.
**Answer:** Open-minded; logical; willing to reassess information; considers a wide range of perspectives.
