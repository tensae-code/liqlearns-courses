# Problem-Solving and the Good Citizen — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Solving problems well makes a citizen —
a) wealthy  b) competent and responsible  c) famous  d) exempt from duties
**Answer: b) competent and responsible**

**2.** Which is NOT a mark of healthy conflict handling?
a) readiness to forgive and forget  b) seeking compromise and avoiding punishment  c) believing resolution can serve both parties  d) making the loser pay fully
**Answer: c) believing resolution can serve both parties**

**3.** The critical thinker is willing to —
a) never change  b) reassess information  c) avoid perspectives  d) conclude first
**Answer: b) reassess information**
