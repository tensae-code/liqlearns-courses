# Video script — Improving Problem-Solving — Thinking Critically and Creatively

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing: creative problem-solving runs on two engines: creative thinking generates the ideas, critical thinking analyses and evaluates them — and the creative solver needs both, meshed like gears.
Two gears make the machine.
One generates — wild, plentiful, new.
One evaluates — clear, fair, exact.
Mesh them, and problems move.
Grind with one gear alone,
and nothing turns.
