# Hands-on — Improving Problem-Solving — Thinking Critically and Creatively

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Gears meshed
Two learners stand as gears, arms as teeth, and must turn together without breaking mesh — one may only propose, the other may only test. A real problem is fed through the machine until a solution both gears accept emerges.

## Outside the box, literally
A problem is written inside a chalk box on the floor. Ideas spoken from inside the box are 'ordinary'; to speak an inventive one, the learner must physically step outside the lines — then a critic inside evaluates it fairly.
