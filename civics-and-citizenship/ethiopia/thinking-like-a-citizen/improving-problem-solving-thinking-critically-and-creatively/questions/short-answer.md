# Improving Problem-Solving — Thinking Critically and Creatively — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Contrast the two engines in one sentence each.
**Answer:** Creative thinking generates new methods and solutions — thinking outside the box. Critical thinking is reflective, rational thought that understands connections, weighs facts and evaluates information scientifically.

**2.** On the staircase, where does each gear turn?
**Answer:** Brainstorming is the creative gear spinning freely; deciding is the critical gear engaging to judge among the options.
