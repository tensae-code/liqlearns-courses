# Improving Problem-Solving — Thinking Critically and Creatively — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Creative thinking's work is —
a) evaluating information scientifically  b) generating new methods and solutions  c) keeping records  d) judging fairly
**Answer: b) generating new methods and solutions**

**2.** Critical thinking understands —
a) only feelings  b) logical connections, weighing facts and evaluating information  c) how to draw  d) other languages
**Answer: b) logical connections, weighing facts and evaluating information**

**3.** Creative problem-solving is framed as —
a) escaping your surroundings  b) taking control of one's surroundings  c) waiting for experts  d) avoiding strategy
**Answer: b) taking control of one's surroundings**
