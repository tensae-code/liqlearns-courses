# Hands-on — What Problem-Solving Is

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two cliffs, one bridge
The group draws two cliffs — 'what is' and 'what should be' — and builds a paper bridge in four labelled spans: define, cause, options, act. A real class problem is walked across it.

## Define it three ways
One scenario card (the lost bag) is read. Three groups define the problem three different ways and defend which definition deserves solving first.
