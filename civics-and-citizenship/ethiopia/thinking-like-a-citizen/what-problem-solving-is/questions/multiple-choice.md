# What Problem-Solving Is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** A problem is —
a) any hard question  b) an unpleasant situation preventing people achieving what they want  c) an exam  d) a disagreement only
**Answer: b) an unpleasant situation preventing people achieving what they want**

**2.** Problem-solving contains, inside it —
a) luck  b) decision-making  c) punishment  d) guessing
**Answer: b) decision-making**

**3.** The critical start of solving is —
a) implementing fast  b) defining the problem properly  c) asking a teacher  d) buying tools
**Answer: b) defining the problem properly**
