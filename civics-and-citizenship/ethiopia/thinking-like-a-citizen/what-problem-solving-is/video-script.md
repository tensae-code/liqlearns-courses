# Video script — What Problem-Solving Is

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing: a problem is an unpleasant situation blocking what people want; problem-solving is defining it, finding its cause, choosing among alternatives, and implementing the solution — and it contains decision-making inside it.
A problem blocks what you want.
Solving it is a process, in steps.
Define it. Find its cause.
Weigh the options. Act.
Define it wrong — and you solve the wrong problem.
Define it well — and the bridge starts building.
