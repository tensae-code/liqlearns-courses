# Video script — Critical thinking — analyse, triangulate, decide

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. This is the lesson's full read-aloud script, where the design names a video-script need.

---

Obang believes his phone.
Every headline. Every post. No checking.
Fake accounts, fake news — all of it true, to him.

Now watch a critical thinker take the same claim.
A book. A teacher. A newspaper.
Three sources before one tick.
That check — **triangulation** — is the whole difference.
