# Critical thinking — analyse, triangulate, decide — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) **Critical thinking is —
a) analysing information objectively and reaching a reasoned decision  b) collecting the most facts  c) memorising quickly
**Answer: a) analysing information objectively and reaching a reasoned decision**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) **Triangulation means —
a) asking one friend three times  b) checking a claim across several sources  c) believing the loudest source
**Answer: b) checking a claim across several sources**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) **In the debate, the winning side won on —
a) volume  b) the teacher's favour  c) evidence
**Answer: c) evidence**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) **Obang's failure is that he —
a) never checks anything elsewhere  b) has no phone  c) reads too many books
**Answer: a) never checks anything elsewhere**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) **Critical thinking is NOT —
a) drawing consequences  b) collecting facts or having a good memory  c) finding relevant sources
**Answer: b) collecting facts or having a good memory**
