# Video script — Why Problem-Solving Matters

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

This lesson is about one thing: problem-solving skill buys six gains — good decisions, brainstorming, self-awareness and creativity, an analytical mind, emotional intelligence, and power over complex challenges — and it lives in everyone, trained or not.
Solving buys you six tools.
Decisions that hold. Ideas in plenty.
Knowing yourself. Thinking straight.
Reading hearts. Facing the complex.
And the tools are already in your hands —
elders' ways and analysts' ways, on one table.
