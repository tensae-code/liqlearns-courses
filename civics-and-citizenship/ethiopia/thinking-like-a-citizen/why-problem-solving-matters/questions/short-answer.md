# Why Problem-Solving Matters — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the six gains.
**Answer:** Making good decisions; brainstorming ability; self-awareness and creativity; becoming analytical; developing emotional intelligence; overcoming complex challenges.

**2.** Name the society's own stock of problem-solving skills.
**Answer:** Negotiation, cooperation/collaboration, and diversity-tolerance skills.
