# Why Problem-Solving Matters — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** How many gains does the lesson list?
a) Four  b) Five  c) Six  d) Eight
**Answer: c) Six**

**2.** The skilled person's cycle ends with —
a) applying the solution  b) receiving and responding to the response  c) defining the problem  d) celebrating
**Answer: b) receiving and responding to the response**

**3.** Problem-solving is framed as —
a) a hard skill for specialists  b) a soft skill everyone holds in some degree without formal training  c) an inherited gift  d) a school subject only
**Answer: b) a soft skill everyone holds in some degree without formal training**
