# Why Problem-Solving Matters

## Do it first

Rain is falling, you are already late, and the road to school is flooded at the low crossing. In the next sixty seconds your mind will run a whole process without being taught it. Afterwards, this lesson will show you the gears you just used — and what owning them buys.

## Six gains

Problem-solving skill and process are a **critical part of daily life**, for individuals and organisations alike. Watch the skilled person's cycle: they **identify and define** the problem, **create alternatives**, **evaluate and select** the best, **apply** it — and **receive and respond to the response**. Solving does not end at acting; it ends at listening to what the acting did.

Six gains are listed, and they read like tools laid out on a school desk: **making good decisions; brainstorming ability; self-awareness and creativity; becoming analytical; developing emotional intelligence; and overcoming complex challenges.**

Now the framing that makes this a citizen's lesson and not a talent show. The skill is a **soft skill** and a personal strength — everyone holds it **in some degree without formal training**, drawing on experience and indigenous knowledge. The society's own stock is named: negotiation, cooperation and collaboration, and diversity-tolerance skills.

And the direction of travel is integration: **indigenous skills joined with scientific ones**, each strengthening the other — the elder's negotiation and the analyst's table on the same desk. @@M2@@ Your flooded road, by the way, was the book's own activity: run the process on it now, in writing, all five beats of the cycle.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the six gains as six tools — then which tool your flooded-road run used most.

## Do It Yourself

1. Run the rain activity fully in writing: define, alternatives (at least three), evaluate, apply, and the response you would watch for.
2. Audit yourself against the six gains: strongest tool, weakest tool, one honest line each.
3. Ask an elder for a negotiation they ran. Map it against the cycle. Where did their indigenous skill beat the textbook order?
