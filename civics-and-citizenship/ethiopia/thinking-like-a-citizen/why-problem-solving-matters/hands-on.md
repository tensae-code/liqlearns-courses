# Hands-on — Why Problem-Solving Matters

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two toolkits, one table
Two toolkits are laid out in imagination and drawn: an elder's staff (negotiation, cooperation, tolerance) and a scientist's instruments (analysis, evidence, testing). The group assigns one shared problem to be solved with tools from both.

## The flooded road, staged
A crossing is chalked on the floor with 'water' across it. Learners in pairs run the cycle aloud — define, alternatives, choose, act, listen — while the class ticks off each beat as it is spoken.
