# Habits of a critical thinker — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) **Name one habit.
a) suspending judgement without sufficient evidence  b) judging by preference  c) hiding ignorance
**Answer: a) suspending judgement without sufficient evidence**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) **A critical thinker questions —
a) only other people's views  b) their own views and assumptions  c) nothing at all
**Answer: b) their own views and assumptions**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) **Bias in oneself must be —
a) denied  b) projected onto others  c) recognised, as fallibility
**Answer: c) recognised, as fallibility**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) **Marshet and Mohammed changed their classroom by —
a) escalating questions until committees and fundraising rebuilt it  b) accepting it as normal  c) leaving the school
**Answer: a) escalating questions until committees and fundraising rebuilt it**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) **Obong's failures include —
a) asking too many questions  b) judging by first impressions and ruling by emotion  c) suspending judgement too long
**Answer: b) judging by first impressions and ruling by emotion**
