# Video script — Habits of a critical thinker

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. A short script — this lesson's design names no full video-script need, so the script stays short.

---

A skill used once is an exercise.
A skill used daily is a **habit**.
Evidence impartially. Judgement suspended.
Ignorance admitted. Curiosity alive.
Your own views — questioned first.
Two pupils lived it, and rebuilt a classroom.
One teacher skipped it, and believed three lies before breakfast.
