# Why critical thinking matters — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. **Decisions built on false information —
a) go wrong  b) are usually lucky  c) do not matter
**Answer: a) go wrong**

**2. **The nurse who qualified by cheating —
a) harms no one  b) endangers every patient  c) only cheated herself
**Answer: b) endangers every patient**

**3. **An inherited practice the book names for questioning is —
a) terracing  b) coffee ceremony  c) female genital mutilation
**Answer: c) female genital mutilation**

**4. **Critical thinking underwrites democracy because citizens judge policy —
a) on evidence  b) on rumours  c) on whoever shouts loudest
**Answer: a) on evidence**
