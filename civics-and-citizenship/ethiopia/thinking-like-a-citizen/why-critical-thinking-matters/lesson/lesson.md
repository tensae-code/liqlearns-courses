# Why critical thinking matters

## Do it first

Two paths fork. Down one, a cheat sheet; at its end, a clinic where a nurse who qualified by cheating treats patients. Down the other, an evidence file; at its end, a ballot box. Before reading: which path did each traveller's thinking choose for them?

## True information, hard questions, democracy

Decisions built on false information go wrong. The book's cases are small and sharp: a friend says exam cheating is harmless, a teacher says it ruins — the pupil who **checks** chooses honestly. The **nurse who qualified by cheating** endangers every patient she treats: one unchecked decision, harming strangers for years.

Critical thinking also raises a community's fundamental questions and tests even inherited practice — **female genital mutilation** is named as a practice to question and abolish.

And it underwrites democracy: citizens who vote and judge policy on evidence resist the poor thinking behind poverty, environmental destruction, ethnic conflict, moral decay, corruption, rights violations and displacement.
