# The Good Problem Solver — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name all six characteristics.
**Answer:** Define the problem clearly; reasonably open-minded yet logically sceptical; look for the opportunity inside the problem; see problems as challenges and learn from them; prevention more than intervention; reasonable expectations.

**2.** What two powers do good solvers combine?
**Answer:** Intuition and logic.
