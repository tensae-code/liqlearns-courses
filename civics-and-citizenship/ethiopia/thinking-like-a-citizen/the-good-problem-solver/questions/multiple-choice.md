# The Good Problem Solver — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** How many characteristics does the student book list?
a) Five  b) Six  c) Seven  d) Four
**Answer: b) Six**

**2.** 'Prevention more than intervention' means —
a) never acting  b) spending strength upstream so crises do not arrive  c) waiting for others  d) avoiding problems
**Answer: b) spending strength upstream so crises do not arrive**

**3.** Good solvers generate —
a) one perfect answer instantly  b) several alternatives before choosing  c) excuses  d) meetings
**Answer: b) several alternatives before choosing**
