# Video script — The Good Problem Solver

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing: good problem solvers share six characteristics: they define clearly, stay open-minded yet sceptical, hunt the opportunity inside the problem, treat problems as challenges to learn from, prevent more than they intervene, and keep reasonable expectations.
The good solver defines first.
Open mind, tested claims.
Inside every problem, an opportunity hides.
Problems are tuition.
Prevention beats rescue.
And the expectations stay reasonable.
Six marks. Measure yourself.
