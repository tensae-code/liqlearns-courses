# Hands-on — The Good Problem Solver

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Three routes around
A path is chalked and blocked with chairs. One learner must cross: the group forbids the first solution, then the second — forcing alternatives until three real routes exist. The six marks are called out as they appear.

## The five-tool figure
The group draws the evidence portrait: one figure carrying five labelled tools — lightbulb, team hands, balance, heart, signpost. Each tool is matched to its quality, and the class debates which of the six marks it nearest resembles.
