# Lesson plan — Deciding by critical thinking — seven steps

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Thinking Like a Citizen · **Lesson:** Deciding by critical thinking — seven steps
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 21 — Thinking Like a Citizen (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** decisions are made critically in seven steps: identify the decision, gather information, identify alternatives, weigh the evidence, choose, act, and review.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the levels before it in this course's path order (recommended, never forced); inside its own title it stands on the lessons before it.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. ## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — A principal's desk. Report files. Warning letters. A scale for weighing them. An unsigned decision stamp. Before reading: two pupils face dismissal. What must happen on that desk before the stamp may fall — and what would make the decision a critical one rather than an angry one?
2. **The explanation** — the lesson's parts in order (lesson/lesson.md).
