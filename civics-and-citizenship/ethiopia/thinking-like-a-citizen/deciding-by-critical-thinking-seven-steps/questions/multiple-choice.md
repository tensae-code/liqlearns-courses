# Deciding by critical thinking — seven steps — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. **The seven steps begin with —
a) identify the decision  b) take action  c) review the consequences
**Answer: a) identify the decision**

**2. **Information's validity depends on —
a) its length  b) its type, source and method  c) who shouts it
**Answer: b) its type, source and method**

**3. **Step 4 weighs —
a) the people involved  b) the oldest alternative  c) the evidence, ranking alternatives by their chance of meeting the need
**Answer: c) the evidence, ranking alternatives by their chance of meeting the need**

**4. **Step 7 reviews —
a) the decision and its consequences  b) the budget only  c) nothing — the method ends at action
**Answer: a) the decision and its consequences**

**5. **The guide judges the dismissal in its case —
a) the only possible one  b) not the best solution  c) unnecessary to examine
**Answer: b) not the best solution**
