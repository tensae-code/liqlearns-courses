# Critical thinking skills — creativity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. **Creativity is —
a) bringing a genuinely new idea  b) collecting money fastest  c) copying a known answer neatly
**Answer: a) bringing a genuinely new idea**

**2. **The class raised —
a) 40 birr exactly  b) 35 birr against a 40-birr price  c) 50 birr
**Answer: b) 35 birr against a 40-birr price**

**3. **The creative turn was thinking of —
a) another collection day  b) a cheaper second-hand stall  c) a local organisation supplying guidebooks to poor pupils
**Answer: c) a local organisation supplying guidebooks to poor pupils**

**4. **Creativity in the story appears —
a) at the wall, after the obvious route failed  b) before any effort  c) instead of all effort
**Answer: a) at the wall, after the obvious route failed**
