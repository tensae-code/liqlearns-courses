# Hands-on — Critical thinking skills — creativity

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening again, for real
A price tag: 40 birr. A purse, opened and counted twice: 35 birr. A classmate is falling behind for want of one guidebook. Before reading: the obvious routes have all been tried. What would a genuinely new idea look like here?

## Sort it on the table
On paper, write the lesson's key pairs from its matching questions — one term per slip. Shuffle the slips, then sort and match them again from memory, saying each meaning aloud as you place it. Any pair you cannot explain goes back into the lesson for one more read.

## Take it home
Find this lesson where you live: one real example of its one thing — creativity is bringing a genuinely new idea — the answer nobody had yet — when the obvious routes fall short — in your family, your school or your street. Write two sentences about it: what happened, and what the lesson says about it. Bring the sentences back and read them to your companion.
