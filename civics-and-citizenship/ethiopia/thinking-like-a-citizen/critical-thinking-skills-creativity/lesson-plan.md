# Lesson plan — Critical thinking skills — creativity

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Thinking Like a Citizen · **Lesson:** Critical thinking skills — creativity
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 21 — Thinking Like a Citizen (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** creativity is bringing a genuinely new idea — the answer nobody had yet — when the obvious routes fall short.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the levels before it in this course's path order (recommended, never forced); inside its own title it stands on the lessons before it.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons

## What the learner can do when it ends

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — A price tag: 40 birr. A purse, opened and counted twice: 35 birr. A classmate is falling behind for want of one guidebook. Before reading: the obvious routes have all been tried. What would a genuinely new idea look like here?
2. **The explanation** — the lesson's parts in order (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 28749]: a picture of a price tag of 40 birr, a purse of 35 birr, and a light-bulb path to an aid office door — the student names the moment the light bulb appears.
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 28750]: a picture of a blocked path and three drawn ways around it: over, under, through — the student invents a fourth way and defends it.
   - ADD AUDIO: none named in the placements for this lesson — no audio slot is built.
3. **Say it to your companion** — explain the one thing in your own words, with the lesson's own case (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD VIDEO: the script in video-script.md, filmed — in the super-simple voice (made later, when videos are made).
4. **Questions** — from questions/: multiple-choice, matching, fill-in-the-blank — served at the learner's level and teaching-mix (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
