# Critical thinking skills — creativity

## Do it first

A price tag: 40 birr. A purse, opened and counted twice: 35 birr. A classmate is falling behind for want of one guidebook. Before reading: the obvious routes have all been tried. What would a genuinely new idea look like here?

## The answer nobody had yet

**Creativity** is bringing a genuinely new idea. The story, in summary: a classmate from a poor family has no guidebook and is falling behind. The class collects family contributions — and raises **35 birr against a 40-birr price**. The obvious route has failed, five birr short.

Instead of giving up, one pupil thinks of a **local organisation that supplies guidebooks to poor pupils**, presents the case — and the organisation provides the book.

Watch where creativity sits: not at the start, but at the wall. The collection was effort; the new door was creativity.
