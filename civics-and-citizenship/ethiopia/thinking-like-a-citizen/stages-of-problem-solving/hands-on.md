# Hands-on — Stages of Problem-Solving

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The staircase, walked
Four large stage-cards are laid on the floor. A learner with a real problem walks them, stopping on each to speak that stage's work aloud. The class may send them back down a stage — politely, with reasons.

## The worked example, performed
The tap story is performed as a slow procession: questioning voices at stage one, a storm of facts at two, weighing hands at three, and spanners at four — then the warning is read aloud by all.
