# Video script — Stages of Problem-Solving

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

One school problem, climbed in full. The tap drips; the yard floods.

Stage one — DEFINE. We ask, we look, we view it from every side.
The washer has perished. Nobody owns the repair. The puddle blocks the path.
That is the problem, defined to its roots.

Stage two — BRAINSTORM. Facts, gathered from everyone connected.
A washer costs little. The caretaker has tools. The path can move for a week.

Stage three — DECIDE. The pieces are broken small and joined.
Replace the washer. Name the repairer. Name the day. Move the path.
We commit — we do not admire the options.

Stage four — IMPLEMENT. The plan is shared. The spanner turns.
The tap stops. The yard dries. The path returns.
Remember the plate on this stage: the best solution fails
through ineffective implementation. Follow through.
