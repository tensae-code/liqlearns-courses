# Stages of Problem-Solving — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Defining includes finding root causes by questioning and viewing from different angles.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Creative thinking's gift to the staircase is better judgement among options.
**Answer: False** — creative thinking gives *more* options; critical thinking gives better judgement.
