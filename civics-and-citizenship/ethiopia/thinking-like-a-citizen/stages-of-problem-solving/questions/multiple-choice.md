# Stages of Problem-Solving — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The correct order of the stages is —
a) brainstorm, define, decide, implement  b) define, brainstorm, decide, implement  c) define, decide, brainstorm, implement  d) implement, decide, define, brainstorm
**Answer: b) define, brainstorm, decide, implement**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Brainstorming balances —
a) speed with silence  b) creativity with logic  c) cost with pride  d) elders with youth
**Answer: b) creativity with logic**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Deciding on the solution ends by —
a) listing options forever  b) committing to the selected strategy  c) asking everyone again  d) postponing
**Answer: b) committing to the selected strategy**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** The warning on stage four is —
a) plans are useless  b) even the best solution fails through ineffective implementation  c) implementation is optional  d) follow-through is luck
**Answer: b) even the best solution fails through ineffective implementation**
