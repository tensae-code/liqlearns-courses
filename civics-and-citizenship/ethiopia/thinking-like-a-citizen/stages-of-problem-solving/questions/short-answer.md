# Stages of Problem-Solving — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the four stages in order.
**Answer:** Define the problem; brainstorm; decide on the solution; implement.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** What three moves happen inside stage three?
**Answer:** Organise and analyse the gathered information; break the big problem into smaller pieces and build links between options; select the best strategy and commit to it.
