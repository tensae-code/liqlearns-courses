# Critical thinking skills — understand, connect, test, conclude, explain, self-check — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. **Name the six skills — the first is —
a) comprehending and expressing meaning  b) speed reading  c) perfect memory
**Answer: a) comprehending and expressing meaning**

**2. **Identifying relationships means seeing —
a) who is friends with whom  b) the inferential links between statements, reasons and opinions  c) the oldest source
**Answer: b) the inferential links between statements, reasons and opinions**

**3. **Assessing credibility judges —
a) the speaker's clothes  b) the length of the account  c) the truth of accounts and the strength of inferences
**Answer: c) the truth of accounts and the strength of inferences**

**4. **Self-regulation means —
a) monitoring and correcting one's own thinking  b) controlling other thinkers  c) never changing a judgement
**Answer: a) monitoring and correcting one's own thinking**

**5. **Explanation must rest on —
a) confidence alone  b) evidence, concepts, method and context  c) repetition
**Answer: b) evidence, concepts, method and context**
