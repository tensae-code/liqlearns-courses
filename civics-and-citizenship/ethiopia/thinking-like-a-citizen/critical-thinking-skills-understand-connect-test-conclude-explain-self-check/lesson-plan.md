# Lesson plan — Critical thinking skills — understand, connect, test, conclude, explain, self-check

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Thinking Like a Citizen · **Lesson:** Critical thinking skills — understand, connect, test, conclude, explain, self-check
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 21 — Thinking Like a Citizen (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** six trainable skills make critical thinking: comprehending meaning, identifying relationships, assessing credibility, drawing conclusions, explanation, and self-regulation.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the levels before it in this course's path order (recommended, never forced); inside its own title it stands on the lessons before it.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short, single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09).

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Deciding by critical thinking — seven steps. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name and define the six skills.
2. Apply the set to a claim of your own.
3. Explain self-regulation.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — Six tools on a workbench, each with a label. A thinker picks them up one at a time to work on a single claim. Before reading, guess the tools: what must a mind be able to DO with an idea before it may trust it?
2. **The explanation** — the lesson's parts in order (lesson/lesson.md).

   - ADD AUDIO: none named in the placements for this lesson — no audio slot is built.
