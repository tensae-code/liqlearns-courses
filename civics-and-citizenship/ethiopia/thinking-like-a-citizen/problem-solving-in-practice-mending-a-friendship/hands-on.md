# Hands-on — Problem-Solving in Practice — Mending a Friendship

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The mediation, live
Three learners play the trio: two friends, one mediator. The book's whereabouts stays unknown to all players. The class observes the mediator against the four parts and reports where the solving strengthened or broke.

## Find the book
A book is hidden in the room before the session. The 'friends' accuse; the mediator must run a search *together* instead of a trial. Debrief: what did searching together do that accusing could not?
