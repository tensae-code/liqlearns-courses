# Problem-Solving in Practice — Mending a Friendship — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** State the case in two sentences, exactly.
**Answer:** Two long-time close friends fell out because one denies holding a reference book borrowed from the other; the trio stopped studying and enjoying together, and you — friend to both — must act.

**2.** What two definitions of the problem compete in this case?
**Answer:** A missing book (a thing to find) versus broken trust (a relationship to mend) — and the solving differs by which you choose.
