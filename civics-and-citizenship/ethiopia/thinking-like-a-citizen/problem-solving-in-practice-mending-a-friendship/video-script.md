# Video script — Problem-Solving in Practice — Mending a Friendship

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing: a case with no model answer: two close friends fall out over a borrowed reference book one denies holding — and you, friend to both, must act to restore the friendship.
A borrowed book. A denied loan.
Two friends, suddenly strangers.
Nobody hands you the answer.
Define it. Hunt the cause.
Weigh the ways back. Then act.
The friendship is the thing being solved for.
