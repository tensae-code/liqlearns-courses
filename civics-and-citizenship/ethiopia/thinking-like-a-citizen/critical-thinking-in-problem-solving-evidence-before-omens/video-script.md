# Video script — Critical thinking in problem solving — evidence before omens

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. This is the lesson's full read-aloud script, where the design names a video-script need.

---

Zerfe sets out for water.
A bird sings. She turns back.
She stumbles. She turns back.
A stranger in a black coat. She turns back.
The water never gets fetched.

Her daughter's teacher says it plainly:
birds sing naturally. Omens bear nothing.
**Evidence before omens** —
that is what critical thinking is for.
