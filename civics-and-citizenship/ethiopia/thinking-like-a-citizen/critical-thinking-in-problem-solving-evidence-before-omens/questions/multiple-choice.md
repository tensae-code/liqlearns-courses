# Critical thinking in problem solving — evidence before omens — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) **Critical thinking makes problem solving —
a) more than luck  b) unnecessary  c) a game of chance
**Answer: a) more than luck**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) **Name one gain from the book's list.
a) memorising omens  b) detecting fake or illogical reasoning  c) avoiding all decisions
**Answer: b) detecting fake or illogical reasoning**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) **Zerfe turned back for —
a) a flooded river and a storm  b) news of real danger ahead  c) a singing bird, a stumble, a passer-by in a black coat
**Answer: c) a singing bird, a stumble, a passer-by in a black coat**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) **The teacher shows the omens have —
a) no bearing on danger  b) some bearing on weather  c) meaning only for elders
**Answer: a) no bearing on danger**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) **Uncritical thinking, the lesson says, wastes —
a) nothing at all  b) resources and strains relationships  c) only time
**Answer: b) resources and strains relationships**
