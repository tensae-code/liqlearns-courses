# Problem solving in six steps — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. **Recite the six steps — the first is —
a) define the problem  b) select the solution  c) implement the fix
**Answer: a) define the problem**

**2. **Step 2 determines —
a) the budget  b) the root cause, by public discussion  c) the leader
**Answer: b) the root cause, by public discussion**

**3. **Alternatives are weighed by —
a) speed of guessing  b) who proposed them  c) benefits and drawbacks
**Answer: c) benefits and drawbacks**

**4. **Step 5, implement, means —
a) action, not sitting and thinking  b) more discussion  c) writing a report only
**Answer: a) action, not sitting and thinking**

**5. **Step 6 evaluates —
a) the workers  b) whether the fix worked, and improves it  c) the weather
**Answer: b) whether the fix worked, and improves it**

**6. **Steps must not be —
a) written down  b) discussed publicly  c) skipped or jumped
**Answer: c) skipped or jumped**
