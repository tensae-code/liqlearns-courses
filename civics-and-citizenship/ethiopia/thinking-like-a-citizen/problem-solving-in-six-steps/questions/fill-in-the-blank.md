# Problem solving in six steps — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word or words.

**1. **Do not chase symptoms; find the ____ cause.
**Answer: root**

**2. **The sequence removes ____, steadies decisions and justifies the result.
**Answer: bias**

**3. **The farmers' work was finished within a ____; recovery within a year.
**Answer: month**
