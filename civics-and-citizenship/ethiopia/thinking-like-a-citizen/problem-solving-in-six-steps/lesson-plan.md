# Lesson plan — Problem solving in six steps

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Thinking Like a Citizen · **Lesson:** Problem solving in six steps
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 21 — Thinking Like a Citizen (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the six steps: define the problem, find its root cause, develop alternatives, select the best fit, implement it, evaluate the outcome — in order, none skipped.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the levels before it in this course's path order (recommended, never forced); inside its own title it stands on the lessons before it.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. ## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Problem solving — what it is. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Recite the six steps in order.
2. Retrace the farmers' case through all six.
3. Say why steps must not be skipped.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — Six footprints cross a cracked field to a water trough. Each footprint has a word on it. Before reading, arrange your own guess of the six words: what must come first — choosing a solution, or knowing what the problem actually is?
2. **The explanation** — the lesson's parts in order (lesson/lesson.md).

   - ADD GIF [GIF 28757]: a GIF of a figure stepping stone to stone across the stream — the student counts the stones and says why none may be skipped.
   - ADD AUDIO: none named in the placements for this lesson — no audio slot is built.
