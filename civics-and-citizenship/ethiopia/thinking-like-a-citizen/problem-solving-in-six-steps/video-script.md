# Video script — Problem solving in six steps

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. This is the lesson's full read-aloud script, where the design names a video-script need.

---

Walk the farmers' case through the six steps.
**Define** it: grazing gone, water scarce.
**Root cause** — found by discussion, not guesswork.
**Alternatives** — four remedies, weighed.
**Select** — trees and grass: most benefit, least drawback.
**Implement** — finished within a month.
**Evaluate** — recovery within a year.
Six steps. None skipped. That order is the method.
