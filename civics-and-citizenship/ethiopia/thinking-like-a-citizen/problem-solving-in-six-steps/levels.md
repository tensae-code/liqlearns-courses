# Problem solving in six steps — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state and use the whole lesson's one thing — the six steps: define the problem, find its root cause, develop alternatives, select the best fit, implement it, evaluate the outcome — in order, none skipped — in their own words, on a fresh example. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners of the same one thing: the lesson's cases judged from the other side, its lists recalled in a different order, its rule applied to the learner's own street and school.
