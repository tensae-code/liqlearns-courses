# Why Work Culture Matters — At School and at Work — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A school's work culture includes how time is kept and how tasks are finished.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Drifting at school has no connection to drifting at work.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Work valued and finished properly is needed in both fields.
**Answer: True**
