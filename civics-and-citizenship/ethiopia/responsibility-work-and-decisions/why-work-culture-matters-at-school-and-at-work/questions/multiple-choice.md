# Why Work Culture Matters — At School and at Work — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Work culture matters at school because —
a) it decides who is popular  b) learning depends on it, and it trains habits for work  c) teachers say so and that is all
**Answer: b) learning depends on it, and it trains habits for work**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Habits formed at school —
a) carry into the world of work  b) vanish at the school gate  c) matter only for exams
**Answer: a) carry into the world of work**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The pupil who finishes tasks properly becomes —
a) the worker who avoids jobs  b) no different from anyone  c) the worker who finishes jobs properly
**Answer: c) the worker who finishes jobs properly**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The two fields of this lesson are —
a) play and rest  b) school and the world of work  c) home and market
**Answer: b) school and the world of work**
