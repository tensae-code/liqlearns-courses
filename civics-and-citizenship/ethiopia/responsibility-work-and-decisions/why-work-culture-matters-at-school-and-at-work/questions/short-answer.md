# Why Work Culture Matters — At School and at Work — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does work culture matter in both fields at once?
**Answer:** Because habits formed at school carry into work — the two fields are one road walked at different ages.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two things a school's work culture can be seen in.
**Answer:** Any two of: how lessons are met, how tasks are finished, how time is kept, how shared work is treated.
