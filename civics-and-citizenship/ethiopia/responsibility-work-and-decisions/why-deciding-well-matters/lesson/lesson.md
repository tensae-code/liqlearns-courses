# Why Deciding Well Matters

## Do it first

Drop an imaginary pebble in still water. Watch the rings spread — ring by ring, further than the pebble could ever reach. Your choices do the same. Name one choice of yours whose rings touched someone else before you read on.

## What good decisions protect

Deciding morally matters because decisions do not stay inside the decider. A good decision protects the decider — from harm, from regret, from the slow damage of a conscience ignored — and it protects everyone the decision touches: family, class, neighbours. The ripple picture is literal: the rings reach people the decider may never see.

## The decider and the touched

Hold both sides together: what good decisions protect for the decider, and what they protect for everyone else. A careless decision also ripples — which is why the five steps are worth their minutes. The cost of deciding well is small; the cost of deciding badly is paid by many people, over a long time.

Tell your companion why deciding well matters — one protection for the decider, and one for everyone the decision touches.

1. Trace one past choice of yours: draw its ripples — who did it reach?
2. Take the five-step time on one decision this week instead of rushing it.
3. Protect one person this week by deciding carefully about something that touches them.
