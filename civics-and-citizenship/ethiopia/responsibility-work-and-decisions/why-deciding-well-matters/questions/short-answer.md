# Why Deciding Well Matters — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1.** Name two things good decisions protect.
**Answer:** For the decider: safety from harm/regret/conscience-damage; for others: the family, class and neighbours the decision touches.

**2.** Why compare a decision to a pebble in water?
**Answer:** Because its effects spread ring by ring to people far beyond the decider, like ripples.
