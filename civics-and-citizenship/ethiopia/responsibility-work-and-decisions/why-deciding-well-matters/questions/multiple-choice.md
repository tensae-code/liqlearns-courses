# Why Deciding Well Matters — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Good decisions protect —
a) nobody — decisions stay private  b) the decider and everyone the decision touches  c) only the decider
**Answer: b) the decider and everyone the decision touches**

**2.** The ripple picture teaches that —
a) a choice reaches people the decider may never see  b) choices have no effects beyond the moment  c) only big choices matter
**Answer: a) a choice reaches people the decider may never see**

**3.** A careless decision —
a) costs nothing if unnoticed  b) can always be fully undone  c) also ripples, and its cost is paid by many
**Answer: c) also ripples, and its cost is paid by many**

**4.** The five steps are worth their minutes because —
a) they make decisions slower for its own sake  b) deciding badly is expensive for many people  c) teachers require them
**Answer: b) deciding badly is expensive for many people**
