# Responsibility — Owning What Is Yours to Do

## Do it first

Pick up something breakable near you — a cup, a jar, a phone — and carry it across the room with both hands, slowly. That careful carrying is responsibility in your hands. Put it down gently, then read.

## What responsibility is

Responsibility is owning what is yours to do. Accountability stands with it: answering for how you did it. The two together are the concept this whole level stands on. A responsible person does not say 'someone should do it' about their own task, and does not say 'it just happened' about their own work — they own the doing, and they answer for the result.

## The concept before the lists

Notice the order: the concept comes before any duty is listed. First know what responsibility is — owning and answering — and only then do the kinds and lists of duties in the next lessons make sense. A duty carried without ownership is just obedience; responsibility is ownership.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what responsibility is, and what accountability adds to it. Then name one thing that is yours to do today.

## Do It Yourself

1. Carry one duty today with both hands — carefully, to the end.
2. When something of yours goes wrong today, answer for it plainly instead of saying 'it just happened'.
3. Name tonight one duty you owned well.
