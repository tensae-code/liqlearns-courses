# Responsibility — Owning What Is Yours to Do — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1.** Define responsibility and accountability in your own words.
**Answer:** Responsibility: owning what is yours to do. Accountability: answering for how you did it.

**2.** Why does the concept come before the duty lists?
**Answer:** So duties are carried with ownership, not mere obedience — the lists only make sense once owning and answering are understood.
