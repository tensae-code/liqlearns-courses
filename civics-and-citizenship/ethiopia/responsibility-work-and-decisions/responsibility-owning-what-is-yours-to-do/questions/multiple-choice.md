# Responsibility — Owning What Is Yours to Do — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Responsibility is —
a) owning what is yours to do  b) doing only what you enjoy  c) waiting to be reminded three times
**Answer: a) owning what is yours to do**

**2.** Accountability means —
a) keeping accounts of money only  b) blaming carefully  c) answering for how you did your duty
**Answer: c) answering for how you did your duty**

**3.** A responsible person, about their own task, does not say —
a) 'I will answer for the result'  b) 'someone should do it'  c) 'it is mine; I will do it'
**Answer: b) 'someone should do it'**

**4.** In this level, the concept of responsibility comes —
a) before the lists of duties  b) after every list is memorised  c) nowhere — only lists matter
**Answer: a) before the lists of duties**
