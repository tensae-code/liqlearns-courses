# Responsibility — Owning What Is Yours to Do — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1.** Responsibility includes answering for the result of your work.
**Answer: True**

**2.** A duty carried without ownership is the same as responsibility.
**Answer: False**

**3.** Owning what is yours to do is the floor the next lessons stand on.
**Answer: True**
