# Video script — What Makes Deciding Hard — Pressure and Pulls

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Five named pressures push against a good decision: low self-esteem — not trusting your own judgement; other commitments — duties pulling at the same hour; peer pressure — friends pushing toward their choice; parents' wishes; and the community's wishes.
