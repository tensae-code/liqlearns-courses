# What Makes Deciding Hard — Pressure and Pulls — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1.** List the five pressures that make deciding hard.
**Answer:** Low self-esteem; other commitments; peer pressure; parents' wishes; the community's wishes.

**2.** How do the five steps help under pressure?
**Answer:** They give a frame: clear problem, options beyond the pushed one, outcomes weighed for everyone — so pressure is weighed, not obeyed blindly.
