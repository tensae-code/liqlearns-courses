# What Makes Deciding Hard — Pressure and Pulls — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which is one of the five named pressures?
a) Quiet weather  b) Peer pressure  c) Good eyesight
**Answer: b) Peer pressure**

**2.** Parents' wishes and the community's wishes are —
a) pressures to weigh, not orders to obey or defy blindly  b) always wrong  c) always the whole answer
**Answer: a) pressures to weigh, not orders to obey or defy blindly**

**3.** Naming a pressure —
a) makes it stronger  b) is forbidden  c) already weakens it
**Answer: c) already weakens it**

**4.** Low self-esteem pushes against deciding by —
a) removing all options  b) making you distrust your own judgement  c) giving you too much time
**Answer: b) making you distrust your own judgement**
