# Video script — Why Doing Your Duty Matters

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A read-aloud of the two cases — told only as the guide's answers give them, in summary.

There are two boys, and one broken window.

The first boy is Ali. Ali broke a neighbour's window — and Ali fled.
He ran from the damage. He left the broken window unowned,
and the neighbour wronged, and the secret sitting on his own chest.

The second boy is Faris. Faris apologised.
Faris went to the neighbour. Faris said what he had done.
The apology owned the act — and owning the act is where repair begins.

Now the class judges both boys. And so do you.

Which boy did his duty?
Which boy can stand straight, and sleep well?

Doing what you are responsible for matters.
And when you fail — owning the failure is duty, continued.
