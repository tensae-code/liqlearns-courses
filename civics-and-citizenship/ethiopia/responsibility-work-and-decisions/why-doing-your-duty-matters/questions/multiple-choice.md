# Why Doing Your Duty Matters — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** In the cases, Ali —
a) apologised at the door  b) repaired the window at once  c) broke a neighbour's window and fled
**Answer: c) broke a neighbour's window and fled**

**2.** Faris —
a) blamed another boy  b) apologised  c) hid until the matter was forgotten
**Answer: b) apologised**

**3.** Owning a failure is —
a) part of responsibility, not its opposite  b) an admission that duty does not matter  c) only for adults
**Answer: a) part of responsibility, not its opposite**

**4.** Duties matter because —
a) teachers enjoy giving them  b) they fill the timetable  c) other people hang their lives on them
**Answer: c) other people hang their lives on them**
