# Why Doing Your Duty Matters — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1.** Judge Ali and Faris, with reasons, using only the summary given.
**Answer:** Ali fled: the damage stayed unowned and the neighbour wronged. Faris apologised: he owned the act and began the repair — the responsible path.

**2.** Why does fulfilling responsibility matter to other people?
**Answer:** Because duties are threads others hang their lives on — the neighbour, the family, the class rely on them being done.
