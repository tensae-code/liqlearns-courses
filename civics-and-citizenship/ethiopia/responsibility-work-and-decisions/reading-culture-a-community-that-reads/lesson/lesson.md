# Reading Culture — A Community That Reads

## Do it first

Picture the tree from the picture slot: a grandmother, a father, two children, a neighbour — all reading, each in their own book. Before reading on, ask: what would a whole community look like if that scene were ordinary?

## Reading as work culture

A community's reading habit is part of its work culture. Reading is work you do for your own growth: it feeds the mind the way field work feeds the household, and like field work, it pays over seasons, not minutes. A community that reads is a community investing in its own people.

[Picture 28230 — reading under one tree]

## Built person by person

A reading culture is built person by person. No decree makes it; it grows each time one person picks up a book, reads where others can see, lends the book on, and talks about what was read. Your reading is not only yours — it is a brick in your community's culture.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion why reading belongs to work culture, and how a reading culture is built. Then name the book or text you will read this week.

## Do It Yourself

1. Read for a set time today where others can see you reading.
2. Lend or recommend one good text to a friend this week.
3. Talk about one thing you read with a family member.
