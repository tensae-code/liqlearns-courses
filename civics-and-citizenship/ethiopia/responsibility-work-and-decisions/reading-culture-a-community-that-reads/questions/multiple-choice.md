# Reading Culture — A Community That Reads — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Reading is part of work culture because —
a) it is work you do for your own growth  b) it is a rest from all work forever  c) only scholars do it
**Answer: a) it is work you do for your own growth**

**2.** A reading culture is built —
a) by one decree  b) by buying books nobody opens  c) person by person
**Answer: c) person by person**

**3.** Reading pays —
a) only in exams  b) over seasons, not minutes  c) never
**Answer: b) over seasons, not minutes**

**4.** Your own reading is also —
a) a brick in your community's culture  b) a private matter with no effect on anyone  c) a way to avoid people
**Answer: a) a brick in your community's culture**
