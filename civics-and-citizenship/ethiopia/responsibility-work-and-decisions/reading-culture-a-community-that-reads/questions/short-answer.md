# Reading Culture — A Community That Reads — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1.** Why is reading counted as work?
**Answer:** It is work done for your own growth — it feeds the mind and pays over seasons, like field work for a household.

**2.** Name two ways one person helps build a reading culture.
**Answer:** Any two of: reading visibly, lending a book on, talking about what was read.
