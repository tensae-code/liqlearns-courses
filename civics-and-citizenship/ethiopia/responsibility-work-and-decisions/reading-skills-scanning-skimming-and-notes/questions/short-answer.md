# Reading Skills — Scanning, Skimming and Notes — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1.** Name five reading strategies from the lesson.
**Answer:** Any five of: reading with a purpose, scanning, skimming, noting key words, reading phrase by phrase, using marks, taking notes, shared reading.

**2.** What line do this lesson's answers draw about children's work?
**Answer:** The line between a child's fair share of work and child labour exploitation — work that crushes schooling, health or childhood.
