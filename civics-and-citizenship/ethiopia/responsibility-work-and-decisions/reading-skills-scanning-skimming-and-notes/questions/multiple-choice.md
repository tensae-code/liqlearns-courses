# Reading Skills — Scanning, Skimming and Notes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Skimming means —
a) reading every word slowly  b) skipping the text entirely  c) flying over a text to get the gist
**Answer: c) flying over a text to get the gist**

**2.** Scanning means —
a) memorising a page  b) hunting fast for one particular thing  c) reading for pleasure slowly
**Answer: b) hunting fast for one particular thing**

**3.** Reading with a purpose means —
a) knowing why you are reading before you start  b) reading whatever your eye lands on  c) never choosing texts
**Answer: a) knowing why you are reading before you start**

**4.** Child work and child labour exploitation —
a) are the same thing  b) are both always good  c) must never be confused — exploitation crushes schooling and health
**Answer: c) must never be confused — exploitation crushes schooling and health**
