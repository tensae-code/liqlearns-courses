# Video script — Reading Skills — Scanning, Skimming and Notes

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A short demonstration: three ways to meet one page.

First — SKIM. Watch the eyes fly over the page.
Not reading. Flying. Getting the gist — what is this page about, in one breath?

Second — SCAN. Now the eyes hunt.
One thing only: a name, a date, a word. Fast, fast — found it. Stop.

Third — READ CLOSELY. Now slow right down.
Phrase by phrase. This line matters. Mark it. Note the key word.

Three tools, one reader.

Read with a purpose — know why you are reading before you start.
Note key words. Use your marks. Take notes.
And sometimes — read together. Shared reading shows you what another reader saw.

Skim for the gist. Scan for the one thing. Read closely for the meaning.
Choose the tool the task needs.
