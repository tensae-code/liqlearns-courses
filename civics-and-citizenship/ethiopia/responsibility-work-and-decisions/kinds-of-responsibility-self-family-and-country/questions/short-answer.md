# Kinds of Responsibility — Self, Family and Country — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three kinds of responsibility in order.
**Answer:** To yourself; to your family and society; to your country.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give one example duty for the middle circle.
**Answer:** Any true family/society duty, e.g. the work of the home, care for family members, a share in community life.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words: what is the heart of “Responsibility to your family and society”?
**Answer:** The middle circle: responsibility to your family and society.
