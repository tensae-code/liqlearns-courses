# Kinds of Responsibility — Self, Family and Country — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A person practises responsibility first in the duties they owe themselves.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Duty to country replaces duty to family.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Duty owed to the nation as a member of it is the country kind.
**Answer: True**
