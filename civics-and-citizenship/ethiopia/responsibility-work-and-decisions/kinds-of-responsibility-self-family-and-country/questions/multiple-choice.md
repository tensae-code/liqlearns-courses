# Kinds of Responsibility — Self, Family and Country — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The innermost kind of responsibility is to —
a) yourself  b) your country  c) your school only
**Answer: a) yourself**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Responsibility to family and society is —
a) the smallest circle  b) outside all the circles  c) the middle circle
**Answer: c) the middle circle**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The civics part of this lesson is —
a) responsibility at play  b) responsibility to your country  c) responsibility to yourself
**Answer: b) responsibility to your country**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The circles are nested because —
a) wider duties rest on nearer ones  b) country duties cancel family duties  c) self duties do not matter
**Answer: a) wider duties rest on nearer ones**
