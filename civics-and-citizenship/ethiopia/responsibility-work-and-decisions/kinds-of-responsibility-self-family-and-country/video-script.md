# Video script — Kinds of Responsibility — Self, Family and Country

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

The innermost circle: responsibility to yourself.

**Responsibility to yourself.** The innermost circle: responsibility to yourself.

**Responsibility to your family and society.** The middle circle: responsibility to your family and society.

**Responsibility to your country.** The widest circle: responsibility to your country — the duty owed to the nation as a member of it.

Now you. Tell your companion the three kinds of responsibility, with one duty in each circle.

And this week: keeping one duty in each circle — self, family and society, country.
