# Lesson plan — Kinds of Responsibility — Self, Family and Country

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Responsibility, Work and Decisions · **Lesson:** Kinds of Responsibility — Self, Family and Country
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 9 — Responsibility, Work and Decisions (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The innermost circle: responsibility to yourself.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the lessons before it in Level 9 — Responsibility, Work and Decisions — and on the learner's own daily life at home, at school and in the community. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **“a picture of …” in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Responsibility to yourself** — The innermost circle: responsibility to yourself.
2. **Responsibility to your family and society** — The middle circle: responsibility to your family and society.
3. **Responsibility to your country** — The widest circle: responsibility to your country — the duty owed to the nation as a member of it.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of Level 9 — Responsibility, Work and Decisions. Recommended, never forced.
- The learner's own experience of the lesson's subject in daily life is the real doorway; the Do it first opens there.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say and show what “Responsibility to yourself” teaches, in their own words, and do the practice named for it.
2. Say and show what “Responsibility to your family and society” teaches, in their own words, and do the practice named for it.
3. Say and show what “Responsibility to your country” teaches, in their own words, and do the practice named for it.
4. Carry the lesson's practice into the week: keeping one duty in each circle — self, family and society, country.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — every activity uses the learner's own home, school and street.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — Draw three circles in the air with your finger — small, medium, large, one inside the other. In each circle, point and name one duty that lives there. Keep the picture: three circles, one you standing in the middle.
2. **The explanation** — the lesson's parts, in order (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 28225]: a picture of three nested circles labelled self, family and society, country, each with one duty drawn inside — the student studies the picture and names what it shows about the lesson before reading on.
   - ADD AUDIO: a voice reading the lesson's key sentences aloud — the student listens once, then reads them aloud in the same voice.
3. **Say it to your companion** — as written in lesson/lesson.md.
   - ADD VIDEO: the script in video-script.md, filmed — the student watches and then does the Say it to your companion with a real companion.
4. **Questions** — from questions/: multiple-choice, true-or-false, short-answer, fill-in-the-blank — served at the learner's level and teaching-mix (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.

## If the learner is stuck
- Cannot say what the lesson teaches → go back to the Do it first and the picture; name what the picture shows, in one sentence, then read the first part again slowly.
- Mixes this lesson up with a neighbour lesson → hold the one thing: The innermost circle: responsibility to yourself.
- Finds the practice hard → shrink it to the smallest real version and do that today; small and done beats grand and promised.
