# What Makes a Decision Moral — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1.** Name the three marks of a moral decision.
**Answer:** Thoughtfulness, reasonableness, balanced judgement.

**2.** What does the first-aid case teach about moral decisions?
**Answer:** That the moral choice must also be the sensible one — goodness and sense found together by a mind that thinks, reasons and weighs.
