# What Makes a Decision Moral — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** A moral decision is marked by —
a) speed above all  b) doing what the crowd does  c) thoughtfulness, reasonableness and balanced judgement
**Answer: c) thoughtfulness, reasonableness and balanced judgement**

**2.** Reasonableness means the decision —
a) needs no reasons  b) can be explained with reasons that hold up  c) pleases everyone
**Answer: b) can be explained with reasons that hold up**

**3.** Balanced judgement means —
a) the sides are weighed fairly  b) your side always wins  c) feelings decide alone
**Answer: a) the sides are weighed fairly**

**4.** In the first-aid case, the moral choice must also be —
a) the slowest one  b) the one that avoids helping  c) the sensible one
**Answer: c) the sensible one**
