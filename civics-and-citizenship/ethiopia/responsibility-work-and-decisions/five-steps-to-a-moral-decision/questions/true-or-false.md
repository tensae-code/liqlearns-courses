# Five Steps to a Moral Decision — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A decision aimed at the wrong problem solves nothing.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 4)** Following through is part of step four, not an extra.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 5)** Once you have decided, looking back is a waste of time.
**Answer: False**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Step two: think of the options.
**Answer: True**
