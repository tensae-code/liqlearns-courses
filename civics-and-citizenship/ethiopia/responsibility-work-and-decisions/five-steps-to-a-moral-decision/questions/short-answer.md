# Five Steps to a Moral Decision — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 3)** List the five steps in order.
**Answer:** Identify the problem; think of the options; identify each option's likely positive and negative outcomes; decide and follow through; review and evaluate what happened.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Why must outcomes be weighed for everyone the decision touches, not only you?
**Answer:** Because a decision's harms and goods fall on others too — the third step names outcomes for you and for everyone affected.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words: what is the heart of “Identify the problem”?
**Answer:** Step one: identify the problem.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words: what is the heart of “Think of the options”?
**Answer:** Step two: think of the options.

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** In your own words: what is the heart of “Decide and follow through”?
**Answer:** Step four: decide — and follow through.
