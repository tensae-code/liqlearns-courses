# Five Steps to a Moral Decision — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Step one is —
a) decide at once  b) review what happened  c) identify the problem
**Answer: c) identify the problem**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Before judging options, you should —
a) ask the loudest person  b) list them — an option never thought of can never be chosen  c) pick the first one seen
**Answer: b) list them — an option never thought of can never be chosen**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** On the outcomes stone, you look for —
a) each option's likely positive and negative outcomes  b) only the positives of your favourite  c) what happened last year
**Answer: a) each option's likely positive and negative outcomes**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Step four is complete only when you —
a) decide and wait for someone else to act  b) announce the decision loudly  c) decide and follow through
**Answer: c) decide and follow through**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Step five, review, is where —
a) blame is assigned  b) a decider becomes a better decider  c) the decision is forgotten
**Answer: b) a decider becomes a better decider**
