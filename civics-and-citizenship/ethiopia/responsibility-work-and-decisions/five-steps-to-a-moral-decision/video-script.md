# Video script — Five Steps to a Moral Decision

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A worked example, walking one decision through all five steps.
(This example is an illustration of the guide's five steps — it is not a case from the book.)

The decision: you find a classmate's exercise book left behind,
with tomorrow's homework completed inside it. Your own homework is not done.

STEP ONE — Identify the problem.
The problem is not the book. The problem is: my homework is undone,
and a wrong shortcut is lying in front of me.

STEP TWO — Think of the options.
Copy the work. Do my own work quickly and honestly. Ask the teacher for help in the morning.
Leave it undone and say nothing.

STEP THREE — Identify each option's outcomes.
Copying: the page looks done — but I learn nothing, and if it is found out, trust breaks.
My own work: less perfect, perhaps — but learned, and honest.
Asking for help: costs pride, gains understanding.

STEP FOUR — Decide and follow through.
I do my own work, honestly, and I return the book untouched.
And I follow through — I actually sit down and do it.

STEP FIVE — Review and evaluate.
The next day: my work was mine. I can answer questions on it.
The decision held. The steps worked.

Problem. Options. Outcomes. Decide and follow through. Review.
Five stones — cross them all.
