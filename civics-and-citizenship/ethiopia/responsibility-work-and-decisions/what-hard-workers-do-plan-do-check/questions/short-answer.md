# What Hard Workers Do — Plan, Do, Check — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1.** List the four marks of hard workers in order.
**Answer:** Use time well; plan before you act; carry the plan out; evaluate what you did.

**2.** Why is the rhythm a loop and not a line?
**Answer:** Because the evaluation of the last task becomes the wisdom of the next plan.
