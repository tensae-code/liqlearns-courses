# What Hard Workers Do — Plan, Do, Check — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The rhythm of hard workers is —
a) plan endlessly and never start  b) use time well, plan, carry out, evaluate  c) rush in, hope, forget
**Answer: b) use time well, plan, carry out, evaluate**

**2.** A plan never carried out is —
a) only a wish  b) a finished task  c) better than action
**Answer: a) only a wish**

**3.** Evaluating means —
a) praising yourself whatever happened  b) blaming the tools  c) looking back honestly at what you did
**Answer: c) looking back honestly at what you did**

**4.** The evaluation of one task feeds —
a) other people's plans only  b) the wisdom of the next plan  c) nothing — the loop is open
**Answer: b) the wisdom of the next plan**
