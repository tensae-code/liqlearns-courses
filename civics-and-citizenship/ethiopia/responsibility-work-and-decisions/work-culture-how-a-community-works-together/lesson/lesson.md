# Work Culture — How a Community Works Together

## Do it first

Imagine a field that must be harvested before the rain. One family alone cannot finish. Now picture the whole village arriving with tools at dawn. That arrival — expected, organised, willing — is this lesson's subject.

## What work culture is

Culture and hard work, defined together: a work culture is a community's settled way of valuing and doing work. It is 'settled' — not one good day, but the way work is normally regarded and normally done: whether work is honoured, whether people show up for each other, whether a job is finished properly.

## Ethiopia's own forms: debo and wonfel

Ethiopia's communal work forms are named in the answers as living examples: debo and wonfel — the traditions by which neighbours gather to do one household's heavy work together, field by field, house by house. They are work culture you can point at: a community that has decided, for generations, that heavy work is shared work.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what a work culture is. Then name Ethiopia's two communal work forms and explain what happens in them.
