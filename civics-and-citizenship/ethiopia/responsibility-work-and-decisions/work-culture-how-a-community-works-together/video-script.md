**What work culture is.** Culture and hard work, defined together: a work culture is a community's settled way of valuing and doing work.

**Ethiopia's own forms: debo and wonfel.** Ethiopia's communal work forms are named in the answers as living examples: debo and wonfel — the traditions by which neighbours gather to do one household's heavy work together, field by field, house by house.

Now you. Tell your companion what a work culture is.

And this week: showing up for shared work the debo and wonfel way — willing and on time.
