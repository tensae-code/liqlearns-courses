# Work Culture — How a Community Works Together — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** A work culture is —
a) a celebration after work  b) a community's settled way of valuing and doing work  c) one person's favourite job
**Answer: b) a community's settled way of valuing and doing work**

**2.** Debo and wonfel are —
a) Ethiopia's communal work forms  b) names of tools  c) harvest festivals only
**Answer: a) Ethiopia's communal work forms**

**3.** In debo and wonfel, neighbours —
a) watch one household struggle  b) compete to finish last  c) gather to do one household's heavy work together
**Answer: c) gather to do one household's heavy work together**

**4.** 'Settled' in the definition means —
a) work finished early  b) the way work is normally regarded and done, not one good day  c) work done sitting down
**Answer: b) the way work is normally regarded and done, not one good day**
