# Work Culture — How a Community Works Together — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1.** Work culture can be seen in whether people show up for each other.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Communal work forms are a modern invention with no history.
**Answer: False**

**3.** How a job is finished — properly or carelessly — is part of work culture.
**Answer: True**
