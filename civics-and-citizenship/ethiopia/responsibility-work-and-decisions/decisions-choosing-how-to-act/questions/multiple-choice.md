# Decisions — Choosing How to Act — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** A decision is —
a) choosing between ways to act  b) doing the first thing that comes to mind  c) letting others choose always
**Answer: a) choosing between ways to act**

**2.** Deciding well is —
a) a talent only some are born with  b) luck  c) a skill that can be learned
**Answer: c) a skill that can be learned**

**3.** A choice made by drifting —
a) is always the best choice  b) is still a choice, but rarely a good one  c) is not a choice at all
**Answer: b) is still a choice, but rarely a good one**

**4.** This chapter treats decision-making as —
a) a whole learnable craft  b) a mystery nobody can teach  c) a matter of age only
**Answer: a) a whole learnable craft**
