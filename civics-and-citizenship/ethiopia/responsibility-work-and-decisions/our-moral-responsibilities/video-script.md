# Video script — Our Moral Responsibilities

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Our moral responsibilities, taught as one list: keep promises; help others; use resources well; take an active part in the community; pay tax; respect rules — including road safety; and care for the environment.

**The seven responsibilities.** Our moral responsibilities, taught as one list: keep promises; help others; use resources well; take an active part in the community; pay tax; respect rules — including road safety; and care for the environment.

**The civics part inside the list.** Two items on the list are the civics part: paying tax, and respecting rules including road safety.

Now you. Tell your companion all seven moral responsibilities from memory.

And this week: judging acts against the seven duties — red, amber, green.
