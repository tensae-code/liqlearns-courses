# Our Moral Responsibilities — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which is one of the seven moral responsibilities?
a) Keep promises  b) Keep every advantage  c) Keep secrets from your parents
**Answer: a) Keep promises**

**2.** The civics part of the list is —
a) help others, and keep promises  b) care for the environment only  c) pay tax, and respect rules including road safety
**Answer: c) pay tax, and respect rules including road safety**

**3.** Using resources well means —
a) letting others save instead  b) wasting nothing of what is entrusted to you  c) using as much as you can
**Answer: b) wasting nothing of what is entrusted to you**

**4.** The list is practised with —
a) a traffic-light card activity  b) a spelling test  c) a race
**Answer: a) a traffic-light card activity**
