# Our Moral Responsibilities — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1.** List all seven moral responsibilities.
**Answer:** Keep promises; help others; use resources well; take an active part in the community; pay tax; respect rules including road safety; care for the environment.

**2.** Name the two responsibilities that are the civics part.
**Answer:** Paying tax; respecting rules, including road safety.
