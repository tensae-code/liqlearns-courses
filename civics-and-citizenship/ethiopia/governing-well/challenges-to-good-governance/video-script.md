# Video script — Challenges to Good Governance

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing: good governance is challenged above all by poverty and corruption — people fighting for their next meal cannot press their claims, and corruption eats the institutions from inside.
Two cracks threaten the bridge.\nPoverty — hunger silences the citizen's voice.\nCorruption — economies distorted, trust collapsed, power a prize to seize.\nAnd a third stone on the path: services that fail.
