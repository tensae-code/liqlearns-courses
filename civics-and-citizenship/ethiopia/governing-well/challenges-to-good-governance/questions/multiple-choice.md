# Challenges to Good Governance — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** People worried about their next meal do not —
a) press participation claims  b) eat  c) work  d) sleep
**Answer: a) press participation claims**

**2.** Extreme poverty blocks full enjoyment of —
a) fundamental rights  b) holidays  c) ceremonies  d) markets
**Answer: a) fundamental rights**

**3.** Corruption's damage to development is —
a) triple  b) single  c) imaginary  d) temporary only
**Answer: a) triple**

**4.** The third challenge — inefficient service delivery — comes from —
a) the teacher guide's evidence layer  b) the student book's list of two  c) this course's invention  d) a newspaper
**Answer: a) the teacher guide's evidence layer**
