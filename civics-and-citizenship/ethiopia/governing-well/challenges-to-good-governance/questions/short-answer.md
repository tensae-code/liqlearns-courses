# Challenges to Good Governance — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Give corruption's triple damage in order.
**Answer:** Distorted economies (persistent deficits and inflation eroding purchasing power); undermined public confidence (reinforcing the power relationships that cause the problem, sharpening injustice amid poverty and unemployment); entrenched clientelism (state control a zero-sum struggle, weakened rule of law, mobilisation for violence, electoral fraud, collapsed trust).
