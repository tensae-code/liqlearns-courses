# Hands-on — Challenges to Good Governance

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The bridge inspection
Draw the bridge of good governance on two piers. The class calls out loads — a new law, an election, a drought year. Each time a challenge is named, crack a pier a little more on the drawing, until the class judges the bridge unsafe and says exactly why.

## The silenced meeting
Hold a mock community meeting where half the learners must spend the whole meeting worrying aloud about tonight's meal and may not speak on the agenda. Afterwards, the silent half reports what poverty did to their participation.
