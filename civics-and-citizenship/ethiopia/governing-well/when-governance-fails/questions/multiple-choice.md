# When Governance Fails — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Decisions driven by the interests of those in power, without public participation, produce —
a) political instability  b) consensus  c) empowerment  d) transparency
**Answer: a) political instability**

**2.** Rights to life, liberty, property and pursuit of happiness unsecured is the impact called —
a) inability of rule of law  b) lack of transparency  c) administrative irregularities  d) lack of empowerment
**Answer: a) inability of rule of law**

**3.** Government performance unknowable until disgrace surfaces is —
a) lack of transparency  b) inability to secure justice  c) inefficient service delivery  d) political instability
**Answer: a) lack of transparency**

**4.** Clinic medicines and equipment sold off privately belongs to —
a) inefficient service delivery  b) lack of empowerment  c) inability of rule of law  d) administrative irregularities
**Answer: a) inefficient service delivery**
