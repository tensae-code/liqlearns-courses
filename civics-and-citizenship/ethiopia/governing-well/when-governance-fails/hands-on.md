# Hands-on — When Governance Fails

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Read the street
Draw the guide's street: clinic, court, office. The class walks it in role — a mother at the clinic, a farmer at the court, a trader at the office — each reporting what the closed door costs their household.

## Seven doors
Seven learners stand as seven doors, each lettered with an impact. A citizen walks the row asking each door one question; the door answers with its mechanism. The citizen reports which door frightened them most, and why.
