# Fighting Corruption — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How is the civil service strengthened, in the book's terms?
**Answer:** By depoliticisation and professionalisation — attractive career paths, qualified officials — and by increasing its accountability and transparency through accessible, simplified information, processes and regulations.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give the media's four ways in the book's order.
**Answer:** Raising public awareness of causes, consequences and remedies and fostering a culture of integrity; informing and educating people on corruption's effects; detecting, investigating, exposing and reporting incidents; enhancing accountability by disseminating information on governmental, administrative and business activity.
