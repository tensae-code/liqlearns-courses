# Fighting Corruption — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Public awareness work teaches the public —
a) what is and is not allowed in officials' duties, legal standards, and citizens' own rights  b) how to avoid all offices  c) party slogans  d) officials' private lives
**Answer: a) what is and is not allowed in officials' duties, legal standards, and citizens' own rights**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The worked example of citizen participation is —
a) the participatory budget hearing  b) a military parade  c) a closed tender  d) a secret ballot on taxes
**Answer: a) the participatory budget hearing**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Simplifying administrative procedures fights corruption by cutting unjustified —
a) discretion  b) salaries  c) office hours  d) paperwork for citizens
**Answer: a) discretion**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The media's third way of fighting corruption is —
a) detecting, investigating, exposing and reporting incidents  b) printing party notices  c) entertaining the public  d) censoring rumours
**Answer: a) detecting, investigating, exposing and reporting incidents**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The judiciary closes the chain because reliable, independent courts —
a) make laws actually enforced  b) write the laws  c) elect officials  d) collect taxes
**Answer: a) make laws actually enforced**
