# Hands-on — Fighting Corruption

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The budget hearing
Hold a participatory budget hearing for a class fund: the spending drawn as a pie on the board, every birr argued in public. Afterwards, name what the hearing made impossible that a closed office allows.

## Five hands, one stone
Five learners — citizen, teacher, journalist, clerk, judge — mime lifting one heavy stone together, each calling out their mechanism as they lift. Then one hand lets go; the class feels and names what is lost.
