# Video script — Fighting Corruption

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing: corruption is fought on two fronts — people and the civil service first: awareness, participation, a professional service, simpler procedures — then the media's four ways and the independent judiciary that closes the chain.
Fight corruption on two fronts.\nPeople awake. Citizens participating.\nA professional service. Simpler procedures — less discretion.\nMedia exposing, four ways. Courts independent, closing the chain.\nFive hands lift the stone.
