# Video script — What Good Governance Does

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing: good governance is called the most important factor in development — it builds economies and living standards, and it holds a society together in cohesion, peace, justice, performance and rights.
What does good governance do?\nIt grows economies — fairly, by law.\nIt lengthens lives and teaches children.\nIt holds people together: cohesion, peace, justice.\nPerformance you can trust. Rights that stand.
