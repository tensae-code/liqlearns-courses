# What Good Governance Does — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** List social development's items as the book gives them.
**Answer:** Better living conditions; ability to feed, clothe and shelter oneself from a livelihood; longer life through health facilities and disease prevention by sanitation; literacy and understanding through education; meaningful participation in public affairs.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the five further roles of good governance.
**Answer:** Strengthening social cohesion and unity; promoting peace; ensuring social justice; enhancing government performance; ensuring respect for human rights.
