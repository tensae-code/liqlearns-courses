# What Good Governance Does — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The book calls good governance — in economic development.
a) the most important factor  b) a minor help  c) an obstacle  d) irrelevant
**Answer: a) the most important factor**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In decentralised development under good governance, citizens are —
a) subjects taking responsibility for their lives  b) objects of development  c) spectators  d) tax sources only
**Answer: a) subjects taking responsibility for their lives**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Exclusion under non-participatory governance is named as a driver of —
a) instability  b) prosperity  c) unity  d) efficiency
**Answer: a) instability**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Government performance is hallmarked by —
a) accountability, transparency and responsiveness  b) secrecy and speed  c) size and ceremony  d) silence and obedience
**Answer: a) accountability, transparency and responsiveness**
