# Hands-on — What Good Governance Does

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Five hands, one rope
Five learners hold one rope level, each named for a role — cohesion, peace, justice, performance, rights. A sixth learner, Governance, holds the middle. When Governance lets go, feel the rope sag; say what sagged first.

## Subjects, not objects
In pairs, plan the same small project twice: once with residents as objects (told what will happen), once as subjects (deciding and responsible). Present both plans; the class judges which the book blesses.
