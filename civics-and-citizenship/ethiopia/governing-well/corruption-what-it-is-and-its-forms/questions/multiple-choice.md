# Corruption — What It Is and Its Forms — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Corruption, in the book's definition, is —
a) the misuse of public office or power for private gain  b) any lie told anywhere  c) losing an election  d) a private quarrel
**Answer: a) the misuse of public office or power for private gain**

**2.** The book's example of fraud is —
a) teaching on a licence obtained on false grounds  b) a queue moving slowly  c) a late tax payment  d) a lost file
**Answer: a) teaching on a licence obtained on false grounds**

**3.** Appointment favouritism toward one's own relatives is —
a) nepotism  b) bribery  c) extortion  d) embezzlement
**Answer: a) nepotism**

**4.** Extracting money by coercion, violence or threats is —
a) extortion  b) fraud  c) nepotism  d) bribery
**Answer: a) extortion**

**5.** Theft or misappropriation of public resources by officials is —
a) embezzlement  b) extortion  c) fraud  d) nepotism
**Answer: a) embezzlement**
