# Video script — Corruption — What It Is and Its Forms

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

This lesson is about one thing: corruption is the misuse of public office or power for private gain — and it walks through five doors: bribery, embezzlement, fraud, nepotism and extortion.
Corruption: public office misused for private gain.\nIt grows where enforcement is weak and accounts are dark.\nFive doors off one corridor:\nBribery. Embezzlement. Fraud. Nepotism. Extortion.\nLearn the doors — the next lesson closes them.
