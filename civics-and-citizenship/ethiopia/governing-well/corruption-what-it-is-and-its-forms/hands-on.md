# Hands-on — Corruption — What It Is and Its Forms

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Five doors, one corridor
Five learners stand as doors lettered Bribery, Embezzlement, Fraud, Nepotism, Extortion. A case is read aloud; the citizen must knock on the right door and say why. Wrong door — the corridor explains.

## Name the soil
Lay five condition cards on the floor. For each condition, the class mimes the office where it lives — the unpredictable queue, the absent accounts book — until the condition is guessed from the mime alone.
