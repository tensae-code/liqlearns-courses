# Lesson plan — Corruption — What It Is and Its Forms

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Governing Well and Fighting Corruption · **Lesson:** Corruption — What It Is and Its Forms
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 26 — Governing Well and Fighting Corruption (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** corruption is the misuse of public office or power for private gain — and it walks through five doors: bribery, embezzlement, fraud, nepotism and extortion.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the democracy level before it — the learner knows who builds democracy and its institutions; this level asks how governing is done well, and what it costs when it is done badly.

## If the learner is stuck
- Calls every dishonesty corruption → the definition needs public office or power misused for private gain, or integrity impaired inside a power structure; a private lie is wrong, but it is not this lesson's word.
- Mixes bribery and extortion → bribery offers an undue advantage to obtain a favour; extortion extracts by coercion, violence or threats. Offer versus force.
