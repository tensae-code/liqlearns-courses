The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

This lesson is about one thing: good governance has eight elements — accountability, participation, rule of law, transparency, equity and inclusiveness, responsiveness, consensus orientation, and effectiveness and efficiency — and they hold together or fall together.
Eight elements hold good governance.\nAccountable. Participatory. Law-bound. Transparent.\nFair to all. Responsive. Seeking consensus. Effective.\nMr. Alemu does them all — openly, on time, for everyone.\nOne spoke breaks, the wheel warps.
