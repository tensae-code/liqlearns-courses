# Hands-on — Elements of Good Governance

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The eight-spoked wheel
Draw a wheel with eight spokes and letter each spoke with an element, the office at the hub. Cut one spoke away on a second drawing and roll both wheels across the desk — say why the book insists they are interrelated.

## Be Mr. Alemu for a day
Run a class office hour: one learner sits as the official, budget and vacancies posted on the wall, a queue of citizens with concerns. The class scores the official against the eight elements afterwards.
