# Elements of Good Governance — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Which elements does Mr. Alemu realise by publishing his office budget and vacancies?
**Answer:** Chiefly transparency (information freely available) and accountability (an official answerable, deciding openly) — with participation served by the same openness.

**2.** What does rule of law require of the judiciary and the police?
**Answer:** An independent judiciary and an impartial, incorruptible police, enforcing fair frameworks impartially.
