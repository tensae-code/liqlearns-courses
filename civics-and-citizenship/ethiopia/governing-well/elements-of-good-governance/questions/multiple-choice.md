# Elements of Good Governance — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** An informed public is the precondition of which element?
a) Accountability  b) Responsiveness  c) Consensus orientation  d) Effectiveness
**Answer: a) Accountability**

**2.** Information freely available and directly accessible defines —
a) transparency  b) participation  c) rule of law  d) equity
**Answer: a) transparency**

**3.** Institutions serving all stakeholders within a reasonable time is —
a) responsiveness  b) transparency  c) accountability  d) consensus orientation
**Answer: a) responsiveness**

**4.** Mediating differing interests toward broad consensus on society's best interest is —
a) consensus orientation  b) participation  c) equity and inclusiveness  d) rule of law
**Answer: a) consensus orientation**
