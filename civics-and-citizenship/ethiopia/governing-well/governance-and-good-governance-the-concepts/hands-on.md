# Hands-on — Governance and Good Governance — The Concepts

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The two-way counter
Build the scene with two chairs and a desk: a citizen states a concern; the official must record it word for word and read it back. Then swap. Governance happens at the counter, in both directions.

## Circle and building
Draw a big circle on the ground with chalk or string. Place cards inside it: government offices in the middle, and around them associations, firms, courts, citizens. Stand outside and say why the circle is the bigger word.
