# Governance and Good Governance — The Concepts — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Governance is wider than government because it includes —
a) all the mechanisms and processes by which common concerns are decided, not only the state's  b) a bigger building  c) more soldiers  d) higher taxes
**Answer: a) all the mechanisms and processes by which common concerns are decided, not only the state's**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In the second agreed definition, good governance is a measure of how public institutions —
a) conduct public affairs and manage public resources  b) win elections  c) build monuments  d) collect taxes
**Answer: a) conduct public affairs and manage public resources**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The fourth definition says good governance empowers people to —
a) take increasing control of their own development  b) avoid all obligations  c) rule without law  d) leave development to others
**Answer: a) take increasing control of their own development**
