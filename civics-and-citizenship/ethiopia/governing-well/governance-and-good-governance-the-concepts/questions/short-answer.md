# Governance and Good Governance — The Concepts — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** State the guide's distinction in one sentence.
**Answer:** Government is one actor — the building; governance is the whole field of mechanisms, processes and institutions through which a country's affairs are managed — the circle around it.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give two of the four definitions in your own words.
**Answer:** Any two of: all processes of governing by which common concerns are decided and regulated; the measure of how public institutions conduct affairs and manage resources (efficient service, reliable judiciary, accountable administration); effective mechanisms through which citizens articulate interests, exercise rights, meet obligations and mediate differences; norms and practices empowering people to control their own development without impinging on others' rights.
