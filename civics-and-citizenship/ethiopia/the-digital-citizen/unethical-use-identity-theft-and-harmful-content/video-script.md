# Video script — Unethical Use — Identity Theft and Harmful Content

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing: behind a hidden identity people do what they would never do face to face — identity theft steals a person's very name, and harmful content passed hand to hand breaks friendships, learning and families.
A mask makes the coward bold.
Behind a stolen name, a thief borrows —
and you carry the debt.
And what your hands forward, your hands answer for.
Do not hide to do wrong.
Do not pass harm along.
