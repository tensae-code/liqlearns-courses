# Unethical Use — Identity Theft and Harmful Content — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1.** The victim of identity theft can be left carrying debts they never made.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Forwarding harmful content is harmless if you did not create it.
**Answer: False** — sharing and forwarding are the harm's vehicle; the lesson holds the forwarder responsible.
