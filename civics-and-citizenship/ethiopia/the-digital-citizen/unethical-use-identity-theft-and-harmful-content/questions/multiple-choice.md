# Unethical Use — Identity Theft and Harmful Content — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Identity theft is —
a) forgetting a password  b) the fraudulent use of another's personality to obtain credit or loans  c) changing your profile photo  d) using a nickname among friends
**Answer: b) the fraudulent use of another's personality to obtain credit or loans**

**2.** People behave more antisocially online when they —
a) are tired  b) expect no consequences for hidden acts  c) use new phones  d) write slowly
**Answer: b) expect no consequences for hidden acts**
