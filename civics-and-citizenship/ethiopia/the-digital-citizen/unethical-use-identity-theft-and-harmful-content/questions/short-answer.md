# Unethical Use — Identity Theft and Harmful Content — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why does hiding identity change behaviour?
**Answer:** Because shame and consequence — the guards of open behaviour — sleep when the face is hidden; people do in the dark what they would not dare face to face.

**2.** Name two harms the lesson ties to irresponsibly shared harmful videos among the young.
**Answer:** Any two of: broken friendships; addiction; early sexual experience; consequent harms to education and family stability.
