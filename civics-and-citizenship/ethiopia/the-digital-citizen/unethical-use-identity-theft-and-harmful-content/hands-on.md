# Hands-on — Unethical Use — Identity Theft and Harmful Content

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The mask test
One learner gives the group an instruction twice: once face to face, once from behind a chair with a disguised voice — a mildly unfair instruction. The group discusses how the hiding changed everyone's behaviour.

## Stop the chain
A paper 'forward' travels hand to hand around the room. Any learner may stop it by standing and saying why they will not pass harm on. See how few hands it takes to break a chain.
