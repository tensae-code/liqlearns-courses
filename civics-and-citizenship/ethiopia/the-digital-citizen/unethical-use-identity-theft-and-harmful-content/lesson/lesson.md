# Unethical Use — Identity Theft and Harmful Content

## Do it first

Imagine meeting a stranger who is wearing your name, your photo and your story — and using them to borrow money from your friends. Everything false about them is true about you. That theft is this lesson's first half.

## The stolen name, and content that harms

Digital technologies give wide scope to **hide identity** — and people behave more antisocially when they expect no consequences. Out of that hiding comes **identity theft**: the fraudulent use of another person's personality — their name, their identity — to obtain credit or loans, leaving the real person to carry debts and a ruined name they never made. 

[Picture 28826 — A mask held in front of a phone's camera]



The mask is the whole mechanism. Face to face, shame and consequence guard behaviour. Behind a stolen or hidden face, both guards sleep — and what a person would never dare openly gets done in the dark.

The lesson's second harm concerns the irresponsible sharing, creation and forwarding of suggestive and aggressive sexual videos among the young. Taught plainly, as it stands: imitation of what is seen leads to broken friendships and addiction, to early sexual experience, and onward to harms against education and family stability.

The thread joining both halves is responsibility for what flows through your hands. A stolen identity flows through a thief's hands. A harmful video flows through a forwarder's. The citizen this course is building does not pass harm along — and does not hide to do it.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what identity theft steals, and why a hidden face makes people bolder in wrongdoing.

## Do It Yourself

1. List what a thief would need to pretend to be you — then guard one item on that list today (a password, a document, a detail you overshare).
2. Make your rule now, before the moment comes: what will you do when harmful content reaches your phone? Write the rule; the moment is too fast for deciding then.
3. Delete yourself from one place where your personal details sit exposed and unused.
