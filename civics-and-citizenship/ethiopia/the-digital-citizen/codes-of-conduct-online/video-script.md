# Video script — Codes of Conduct Online

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing: online life has a code: the Grade 9 rule list first, then the six ethical codes — etiquette, responsibility, safety, security, confidentiality, accuracy.
Online, nobody stands at the gate.
So carry your own rules.
Use media for good. Guard privacy.
Fight lies with facts. Correct your mistakes.
Etiquette. Responsibility. Safety.
Security. Confidentiality. Accuracy.
Six codes — one good citizen.
