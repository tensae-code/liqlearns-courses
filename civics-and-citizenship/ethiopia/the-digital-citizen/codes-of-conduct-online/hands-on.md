# Hands-on — Codes of Conduct Online

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The class code wall
On a big sheet, the group writes the six codes as six shields. Under each shield, one real violation the class has actually seen — no names named.

## Spot the break
One learner acts out a short online scene (a forwarded rumour, a shared password, a copied project). The others call out which code breaks, and when.
