# Codes of Conduct Online — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the six ethical codes in order.
**Answer:** Etiquette, responsibility, safety, security, confidentiality, accuracy/trustworthiness.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Give three rules from the Grade 9 code list.
**Answer:** Any three of: use media for good; turn learning into skill; ignore violence-provoking content; treat others as you wish to be treated; respect privacy; fight fake news; no defamation; correct mistakes promptly; do not waste time; protect family, study and work; free yourself from addiction.
