# Codes of Conduct Online — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Pupils texting non-class messages in class breaks the code of —
a) security  b) etiquette  c) accuracy  d) safety
**Answer: b) etiquette**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Telling others your Facebook password breaks —
a) confidentiality  b) etiquette  c) safety  d) responsibility
**Answer: a) confidentiality**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Working long hours in a poorly lit room breaks the code of —
a) security  b) accuracy  c) safety  d) etiquette
**Answer: c) safety**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The Grade 9 list tells you to fight fake news by —
a) sharing it faster  b) exposing it with facts  c) ignoring all news  d) deleting your account
**Answer: b) exposing it with facts**
