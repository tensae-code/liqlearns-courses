# Codes of Conduct Online — writing

Write what each prompt asks, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A friend forwards you a shocking story you suspect is false. Write what you do, step by step, using at least three of the codes by name.
**A good answer does this:** Checks before sharing (accuracy), refuses to spread it, corrects with facts, and treats the forwarder with etiquette — codes named and used.
