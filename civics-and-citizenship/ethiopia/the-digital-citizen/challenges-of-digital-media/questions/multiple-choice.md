# Challenges of Digital Media — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** How many challenges does this lesson develop?
a) One  b) Two  c) Three  d) Five
**Answer: c) Three**

**2.** Fake news is fabricated to —
a) spread misinformation and harvest clicks for advertising income  b) teach history  c) save printing costs  d) protect privacy
**Answer: a) spread misinformation and harvest clicks for advertising income**

**3.** A 'private' post becomes public because —
a) phones are magic  b) it can be reshared to anyone  c) the platform prints it  d) friends always delete it
**Answer: b) it can be reshared to anyone**
