# Challenges of Digital Media — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the three challenges of digital media.
**Answer:** Disregard for ethical values; fabrication of fake news; privacy and security threats.

**2.** Why is uncontrolled transmission a standing worry?
**Answer:** Because nobody checks information at the gate, so its accuracy and its impact on society cannot be trusted without the user's own ethical care.
