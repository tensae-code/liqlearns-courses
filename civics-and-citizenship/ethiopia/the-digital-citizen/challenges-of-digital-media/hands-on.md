# Hands-on — Challenges of Digital Media

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Race the correction
One learner whispers a false 'headline' down a line of classmates — fast. A second learner carries the correction the same way, starting ten seconds later. Time both. Discuss the gap.

## Public or private?
The group sorts picture-cards of posts into 'safe to reshare' and 'never reshare', defending each sort with the privacy challenge.
