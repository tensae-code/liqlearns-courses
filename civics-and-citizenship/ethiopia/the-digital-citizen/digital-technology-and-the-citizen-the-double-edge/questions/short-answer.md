# Digital Technology and the Citizen — The Double Edge — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** State the governing rule in your own words.
**Answer:** Good and moderate use of digital technology leads to positive effects; extremely excessive and addictive use leads to negative effects.

**2.** What can the technology threaten, besides feelings?
**Answer:** Privacy, ethical and cultural values, security — and it can fuel inequality.
