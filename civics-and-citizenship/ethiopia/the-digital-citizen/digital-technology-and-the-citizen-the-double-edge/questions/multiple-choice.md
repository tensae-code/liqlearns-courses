# Digital Technology and the Citizen — The Double Edge — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The governing rule says the effect depends on —
a) the price of the phone  b) the dose — moderate use helps, excessive addictive use harms  c) the user's age  d) the network
**Answer: b) the dose — moderate use helps, excessive addictive use harms**

**2.** The effects are worked through three fields:
a) emotions, social life, ethical values  b) price, size, colour  c) school, church, market  d) past, present, future
**Answer: a) emotions, social life, ethical values**
