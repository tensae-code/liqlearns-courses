# Hands-on — Digital Technology and the Citizen — The Double Edge

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The two pans
The group builds a real balance from a stick and two plates. Habits written on cards are weighed: moderate cards on one side, excessive on the other, each defended aloud.

## Kobalo and Ojulu, as stories
Two learners retell — as short spoken stories — the cases the evidence names: Kobalo, whose gaming addiction cost him his friends, and Mr. Ojulu, whose public telephone centre emptied as customers moved to smartphones. The group names which side of the balance each life fell on.
