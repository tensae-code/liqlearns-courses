# Lesson plan — Digital Technology and the Citizen — The Double Edge

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** The Digital Citizen · **Lesson:** Digital Technology and the Citizen — The Double Edge
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 22 — The Digital Citizen (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** digital technology cuts both ways, and the dose makes the effect: good and moderate use leads to good effects; excessive, addictive use leads to harm — across emotions, social life and ethical values.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the citizenship levels before it — the learner already knows what a citizen owes and is owed; this level teaches the same citizen how to live in the digital world.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short, single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09).

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the Grade 9 half of this level — what the technology is, its purposes, its codes, its challenges. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the governing rule of dose and effect.
2. Name the three fields the effects run through.
3. Sort a real habit into the moderate or the excessive side.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else.

1. **Do it first** — the learner weighs one of their own habits on the lesson's scale before the rule is stated (lesson/lesson.md).
2. **The explanation** — the double edge stated; the governing rule of dose; the three fields named, which the next three lessons work one by one; the evidence cases previewed (lesson/lesson.md).

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks the lesson calls the technology bad → the rule is about dose: moderate use leads to positive effects.
- Thinks the effects are only emotional → they run through three fields: emotions, social life, ethical values.
