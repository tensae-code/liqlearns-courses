# Video script — Digital Technology and the Citizen — The Double Edge

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Two stories, told plainly, as summaries.

Kobalo loved his online games.
He played more. And more.
Slowly, his friendships were pushed aside —
the game took the hours his friends used to have.

Mr. Ojulu ran a public telephone centre.
People came to his phones to call and to talk.
Then the customers bought smartphones of their own —
and the centre emptied.

Same technology in both stories.
In one, the dose took a boy's friends.
In the other, progress took a man's living.
The rule stands: moderate use helps; excess harms.
