# Digital Technology and Social Life — Gains and Harms — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the seven social gains.
**Answer:** Increased social connectivity; speedy communication; advancing education; facilitating transportation; advancing banking and finance; improving health care; promoting economic growth.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the six social harms.
**Answer:** Social alienation; eroding privacy; job insecurity; data insecurity; aggravated crime and terrorism; physical-health effects.
