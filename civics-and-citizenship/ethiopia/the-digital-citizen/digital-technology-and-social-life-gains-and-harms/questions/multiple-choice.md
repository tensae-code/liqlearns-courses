# Digital Technology and Social Life — Gains and Harms — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which is NOT one of the seven social gains?
a) Advancing banking and finance  b) Improving health care  c) Eroding privacy  d) Facilitating transportation
**Answer: c) Eroding privacy**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Helen's case teaches the harm of —
a) eyestrain  b) eroding privacy — her photos taken and used by strangers  c) job insecurity  d) echo chambers
**Answer: b) eroding privacy — her photos taken and used by strangers**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Driving and delivery are named as jobs next threatened by —
a) automation  b) broadband  c) virtual laboratories  d) reminders
**Answer: a) automation**
