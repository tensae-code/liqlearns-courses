# Hands-on — Digital Technology and Social Life — Gains and Harms

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Helen's photos, acted carefully
Using paper 'photos' (drawings), act Helen's case: a drawing is copied, carried off, and used to knock on her friends' doors. The group stops the play at each point where a code or a setting could have blocked it.

## The town on one street
Draw one street of your town. Place the seven gains along it as labelled buildings and stalls. Then mark, with a cross, where each of the six harms could creep in.
