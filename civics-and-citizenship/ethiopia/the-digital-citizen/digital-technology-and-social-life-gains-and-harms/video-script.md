# Video script — Digital Technology and Social Life — Gains and Harms

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Helen put her photos on Facebook — pictures of herself, for her friends.

Then she discovered them living a second life.
Strangers had taken her photos as *their* profile pictures.
Wearing her face, they sent friend requests to *her* friends.

Helen deleted the photos.
The copies did not die with the originals.
They were already out — being her, somewhere she could not reach.

That is the privacy harm, in one true-shaped story:
what you post can be taken, copied, and used —
and deleting your copy does not call the others home.
