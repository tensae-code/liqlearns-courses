# Video script — Culture and Digital Technology

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing: culture and digital technology shape each other — so a people's digital life must stay in line with its own moral, social and cultural values.
Your culture lives in your language.
In your dress. Your food. Your holidays.
A phone can carry all of it to the world.
Carry it with respect.
And never let the screen empty your own table.
The guest does not rule the house.
