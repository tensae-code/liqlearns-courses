# Hands-on — Culture and Digital Technology

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Carry it well
With paper and pencils, design the front of a video or page that presents one piece of your local culture — title, picture, first line. Show it to an elder and ask: is this us?

## The silent table
Role-play the meal scene twice: once with two people lost in their phones, once with the phones face-down. Afterwards, each player says what the second version gave back.
