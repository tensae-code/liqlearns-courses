# Culture and Digital Technology — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Culture and digital technology are —
a) enemies that must never meet  b) two forces that shape each other  c) the same thing  d) unrelated
**Answer: b) two forces that shape each other**

**2.** Which can digital media carry to the world?
a) Only foreign cultures  b) Language, dress, food, arts, historic sites and holidays  c) Nothing of value  d) Only advertisements
**Answer: b) Language, dress, food, arts, historic sites and holidays**
