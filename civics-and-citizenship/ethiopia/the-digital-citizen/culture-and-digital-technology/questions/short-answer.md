# Culture and Digital Technology — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What does culture live by, and why does that make digital media powerful for it?
**Answer:** Culture lives by communication — it is learned, transmitted and preserved through it — and digital media is communication at great speed, so it can carry a culture worldwide.

**2.** Describe the warning picture at the family table.
**Answer:** A couple at a meal, both absorbed in social media, ignoring their child — the screens full, the family empty.
