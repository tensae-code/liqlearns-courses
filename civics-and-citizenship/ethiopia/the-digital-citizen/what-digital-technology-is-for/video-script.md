# Video script — What Digital Technology Is For

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing: digital technology has four purposes: social connection, communication speed, online learning, and digital marketing.
What is the phone for?
One: to connect people far apart.
Two: to send things fast.
Three: to learn without travelling.
Four: to buy and sell, even at night.
Use it for these — and it serves you.
