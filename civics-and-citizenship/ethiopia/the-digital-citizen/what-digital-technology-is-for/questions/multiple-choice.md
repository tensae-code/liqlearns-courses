# What Digital Technology Is For — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** How many purposes does this lesson teach?
a) Two  b) Three  c) Four  d) Seven
**Answer: c) Four**

**2.** A course reached in seconds, with no travel to a centre, is the purpose called —
a) digital marketing  b) online learning  c) social connection  d) communication speed
**Answer: b) online learning**

**3.** Customers buying while the shop is closed is —
a) social connection  b) communication speed  c) digital marketing  d) none of the purposes
**Answer: c) digital marketing**
