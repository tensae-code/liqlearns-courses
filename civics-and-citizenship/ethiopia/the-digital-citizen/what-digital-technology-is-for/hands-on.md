# Hands-on — What Digital Technology Is For

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Four doors
Draw four doors on one big sheet, lettered Connection, Speed, Learning, Market. Under each, the group writes two real examples from your own town.

## The closed shop
Role-play it: a shop is closed for the night, but a customer buys through the shop's page. One plays the customer, one the sleeping shopkeeper who wakes to a sale.
