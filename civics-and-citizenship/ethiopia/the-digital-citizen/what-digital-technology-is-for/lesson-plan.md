# Lesson plan — What Digital Technology Is For

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** The Digital Citizen · **Lesson:** What Digital Technology Is For
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 22 — The Digital Citizen (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** digital technology has four purposes: social connection, communication speed, online learning, and digital marketing.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the citizenship levels before it — the learner already knows what a citizen owes and is owed; this level teaches the same citizen how to live in the digital world.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short, single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09).

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Digital Technology — What It Is. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the four purposes, in order.
2. Give a real example of each purpose.
3. Say why using the technology for the right purpose is the lesson's frame.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the learner sorts yesterday's digital doings into piles and discovers the four purposes before they are named (lesson/lesson.md).
2. **The explanation** — the four purposes one by one — social connection, communication speed, online learning, digital marketing — each with its worked example (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 28805]: a picture in four panels: a video call, a message flying fast, an online lesson, and an online shop — the student names the purpose each panel shows.
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 28806]: a picture of one phone opening onto four doors — a library, a friend, a classroom, a market stall — the student walks a finger through each door and says what is found behind it.
3. **Say it to your companion** — explain the lesson's one thing in your own words (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD VIDEO: the script in video-script.md, filmed — the lesson retold in the super-simple voice (made later, when videos are made).
4. **Questions** — from questions/: fill-in-the-blank, matching, multiple-choice, short-answer, true-or-false, writing — served at the learner's level and teaching-mix (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** — the doing block at the end of lesson/lesson.md.

## If the learner is stuck
- Mixes up speed and connection → connection is *who* you reach; speed is *how fast* anything reaches them.
- Thinks marketing means only big companies → the smallest stall with a page is doing digital marketing.
