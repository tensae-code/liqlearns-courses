# Hands-on — Digital Technology and Emotions — Gains and Harms

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two columns, one life
On a big sheet, two columns: Gains I live / Harms I risk. Each learner writes one honest entry in each, and the group discusses the dose that moves entries across.

## Firsthand hour
The group spends one session on a firsthand pleasure — a real game with a real ball, a song sung together — then names what no screen delivered in it.
