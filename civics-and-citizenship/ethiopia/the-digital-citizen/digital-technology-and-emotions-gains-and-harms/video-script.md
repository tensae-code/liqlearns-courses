# Video script — Digital Technology and Emotions — Gains and Harms

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing: on emotions, digital technology gives seven gains — from self-control to security — and five harms, from isolation to addiction; the dose decides which list you live in.
A screen can steady you.
It can cool a quarrel. It can carry a grandmother's smile.
It can also isolate you —
and sell you a secondhand life.
Seven gains. Five harms.
The dose decides.
