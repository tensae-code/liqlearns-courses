# Digital Technology and Emotions — Gains and Harms — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the five emotional harms.
**Answer:** Isolation; secondhand living; depression and anxiety; anonymity and fake personality; addiction.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Name any four of the seven gains.
**Answer:** Any four of: self-control; fostering socialisation; maintaining emotional balance; supporting happiness and wellbeing; regulating emotions; a sense of security; increased emotional satisfaction.
