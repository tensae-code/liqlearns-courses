# Digital Technology and Emotions — Gains and Harms — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Heavier social-media users can feel *more* isolated, though the tools were built to connect.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Anonymity online tends to make people kinder.
**Answer: False** — hidden identity licenses bullying, trolling, stalking and fraud.
