# Lesson plan — Digital Technology — What It Is

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** The Digital Citizen · **Lesson:** Digital Technology — What It Is
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 22 — The Digital Citizen (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** digital technology is the use of computers, phones and the internet to move information quickly between people — and it already runs through Ethiopian daily life.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the citizenship levels before it — the learner already knows what a citizen owes and is owed; this level teaches the same citizen how to live in the digital world.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short, single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09).

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the citizenship levels before this one — what a citizen is and owes. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what digital technology is, in your own words.
2. Name today's common forms of it — computer, smartwatch, ATM, camera, drone, GPS and more.
3. Give three Ethiopian daily-life uses: family calls, online services, mobile banking.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks digital technology means only phones → point at the ATM, the camera, the GPS in a car: the family is bigger than one device.
- Cannot say what it *does* → return to the core power: it moves information quickly between people.
