# Digital Technology — What It Is — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name three forms of digital technology in daily use today.
**Answer:** Any three of: computer, smartwatch, ATM, digital camera, automated vehicles, drone, Bitcoin, GPS — and the phones, tablets and social media reached through them.

**2.** Give two ways digital technology already serves Ethiopian daily life.
**Answer:** Family calls across the country, online services that save a trip, and mobile-banking money transfer that saves time and travel.
