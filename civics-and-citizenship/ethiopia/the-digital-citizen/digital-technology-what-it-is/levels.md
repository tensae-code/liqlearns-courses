# Digital Technology — What It Is — depth

Whole-lesson pass mark: the learner can say what digital technology is, name at least five of its common forms, and give an Ethiopian daily-life use of their own.
Practice deepens inside the one thing: first the plain cases from the lesson, then the same thing in the learner's own street, school and home, then the hard corners the lesson names — judging real cases against what was taught. The questions live in the type files; this file is the depth view.
