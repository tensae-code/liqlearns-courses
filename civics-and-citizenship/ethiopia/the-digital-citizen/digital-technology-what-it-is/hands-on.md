# Hands-on — Digital Technology — What It Is

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The device census
Walk through your home or classroom and count the digital devices. For each one, say out loud what information it moves and to whom.

## Then and now
With an elder or parent, act out sending the same urgent message twice: once the old way (a messenger, a journey), once the digital way. Feel the difference in time.
