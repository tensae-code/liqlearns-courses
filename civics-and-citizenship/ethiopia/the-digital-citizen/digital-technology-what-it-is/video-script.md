# Video script — Digital Technology — What It Is

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing: digital technology is the use of computers, phones and the internet to move information quickly between people — and it already runs through Ethiopian daily life.
Digital technology.
It is the computer. The phone. The tablet.
It moves words, pictures and money — fast.
A call crosses the country in a second.
Money moves, and nobody travels.
That power is already in your hand.
