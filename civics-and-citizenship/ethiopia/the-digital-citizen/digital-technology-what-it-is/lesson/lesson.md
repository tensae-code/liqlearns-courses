# Digital Technology — What It Is

## Do it first

Look at the nearest phone, watch or machine screen around you. In the last day, what did it do for you? Who did it connect you to, and how fast? Keep your list — this lesson names what you were already using.

## What it is

Digital technology is the use of computers, tablets, mobile phones — and the digital and social media reached through the internet on those devices — together with newer communication technologies that can reshape political, economic, social and cultural life. 

[Picture 28800 — Phone, ATM, drone, GPS and smartwatch around one pupil]



Its core power is simple to say: it moves information and data quickly between people, and across the whole world, through apps, social platforms and digital marketing sites. A message that once travelled for days on foot now travels in a second.

The technology is young — it dates from the middle of the twentieth century — but its forms are already everywhere: the computer, the smartwatch, the ATM, the digital camera, automated vehicles, the drone, Bitcoin, and GPS, the satellite guide that knows where you stand. 

[Picture 28801 — A student at the centre of a web of connections (teacher guide — evidence)]



And it is not foreign to Ethiopian life. It is the family call that crosses the country without a journey. It is the online service that saves a trip to an office. It is mobile-banking money transfer that saves time and travel — money moving from hand to hand without either hand moving at all.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion: what is digital technology? Name four of its forms, and one way it saved your family time this month.

## Do It Yourself

1. List every digital device in your home. Beside each one, write what it moves: words, pictures, money, or directions.
2. Ask an elder: how did your family send an urgent message before phones? Write the two ways side by side — then and now.
3. Find one service near you that can be reached online or by phone instead of by travel. Note what the journey would have cost.
