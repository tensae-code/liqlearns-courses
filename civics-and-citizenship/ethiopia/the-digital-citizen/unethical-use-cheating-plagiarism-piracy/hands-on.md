# Hands-on — Unethical Use — Cheating, Plagiarism, Piracy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The Zebenay court
The class holds a hearing: one learner prosecutes the copiers, one defends them, one speaks for the workers, and Mrs. Zebenay's decision is judged with reasons, trait by trait.

## Credit where due
Each learner brings one fact they learned from somewhere else and writes it twice: once plagiarised (no credit), once honest (source named). The group feels the difference in effort — and in worth.
