# Unethical Use — Cheating, Plagiarism, Piracy — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** How did Mrs. Zebenay treat the copiers and the workers, and why was it just?
**Answer:** She punished the copiers and rewarded those who worked properly from books — because the copiers were stealing the workers' four days of honest work.

**2.** Name three forms of cheating from the definition.
**Answer:** Any three of: copying another's work; letting your work be copied; submitting another's work as your own; fabricating data; unauthorised consultation or aids in examinations.
