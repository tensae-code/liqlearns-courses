# Unethical Use — Cheating, Plagiarism, Piracy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Presenting others' ideas and words as your own is —
a) cheating only  b) plagiarism  c) research  d) piracy
**Answer: b) plagiarism**

**2.** Letting your own work be copied counts as —
a) generosity  b) cheating  c) teamwork  d) nothing
**Answer: b) cheating**

**3.** Piracy violates —
a) traffic rules  b) creators' property rights  c) school uniform rules  d) nothing, copies are free
**Answer: b) creators' property rights**
