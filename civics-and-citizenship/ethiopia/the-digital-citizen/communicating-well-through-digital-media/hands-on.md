# Hands-on — Communicating Well Through Digital Media

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The price messenger
Role-play Megarsa: one learner phones in a price, others are farmers deciding whether to travel to market. Then swap. Debrief: what made the message trustworthy?

## Two windows
Write the same request twice on paper — once confused and heated, once clear and calm. The group reads both aloud and names what changed.
