# Communicating Well Through Digital Media — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Communication decides —
a) only exam results  b) success and failure for persons and organisations  c) nothing important  d) only prices
**Answer: b) success and failure for persons and organisations**

**2.** Which improvement enabled e-commerce and transactions anywhere?
a) Computers  b) Satellite links  c) Mobile phones  d) The internet
**Answer: c) Mobile phones**

**3.** Megarsa Tullu used his phone to —
a) play games  b) follow market prices and pass them to other farmers  c) avoid the market  d) sell phones
**Answer: b) follow market prices and pass them to other farmers**
