# Communicating Well Through Digital Media — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the four improvements that built digital communication.
**Answer:** Cellular and satellite links; the internet; computers; mobile phones.

**2.** What did Megarsa's communication give the farmers around him?
**Answer:** Knowledge of market prices across the country — information that let them judge fairly instead of travelling and selling blind.
