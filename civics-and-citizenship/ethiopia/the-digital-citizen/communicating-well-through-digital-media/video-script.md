# Video script — Communicating Well Through Digital Media

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

This lesson is about one thing: good digital communication is clear, ethical and effective — and four inventions made it possible: cellular links, the internet, computers, and mobile phones.
A message can fail — or it can feed a village.
Megarsa the farmer follows prices on his phone.
Then he passes the prices on.
Clear words. The right time. A fair price.
That is communication doing its work.
