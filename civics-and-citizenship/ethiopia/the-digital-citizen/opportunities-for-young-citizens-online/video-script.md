# Video script — Opportunities for Young Citizens Online

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing: for young citizens, digital technology opens five opportunities: information, connectivity, learning, work, and innovation.
Five doors stand open.
Information. People. Learning. Work.
And the fifth door: Invention.
Walk through the first four,
and the fifth opens by itself.
Stop only watching the world. Add to it.
