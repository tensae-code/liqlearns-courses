# Hands-on — Opportunities for Young Citizens Online

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Five doors on the wall
The group draws five doors on a big sheet and fills each with real, reachable examples from your own area — named sites, named courses, named people.

## The ladder interview
Interview a young person who earns or learns online. The group maps their story onto the five doors: which ones did they climb, in what order?
