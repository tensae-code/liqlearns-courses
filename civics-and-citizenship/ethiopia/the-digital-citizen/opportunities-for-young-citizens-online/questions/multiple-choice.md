# Opportunities for Young Citizens Online — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** How many opportunities does the lesson name?
a) Three  b) Four  c) Five  d) Seven
**Answer: c) Five**

**2.** Innovation grows out of —
a) luck alone  b) information, connectivity and digital learning as its fertile ground  c) expensive equipment  d) leaving your country
**Answer: b) information, connectivity and digital learning as its fertile ground**

**3.** Working from hundreds of miles away is the opportunity of —
a) information  b) versatile work  c) connectivity only  d) none
**Answer: b) versatile work**
