# Hands-on — Digital Technology and Ethical Values — Gains and Harms

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## One source, many sources
The group hears a short claim from one 'source' (a card), accepts or rejects it, then receives two more cards that complicate it. Discuss: when did real thinking start?

## Ali, counselled
In pairs: one is Ali, confused by clashing philosophies; the other counsels him — not by closing his mind, but by teaching him to examine. Swap and repeat.
