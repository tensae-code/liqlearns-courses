# Digital Technology and Ethical Values — Gains and Harms — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A person with only one source, the lesson says, —
a) thinks better  b) has no choice but to accept it as ultimate truth  c) is protected from confusion  d) becomes a philosopher
**Answer: b) has no choice but to accept it as ultimate truth**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Ali's case ends with him —
a) famous  b) confused and depressed  c) teaching philosophy  d) offline forever
**Answer: b) confused and depressed**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Chasing likes, subscribes and shares belongs to the harm of —
a) untruthfulness  b) uncertainty  c) security  d) self-learning
**Answer: a) untruthfulness**
