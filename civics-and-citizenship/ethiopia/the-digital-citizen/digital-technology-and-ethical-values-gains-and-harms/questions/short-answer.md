# Digital Technology and Ethical Values — Gains and Harms — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three ethical gains.
**Answer:** Developing critical thinking; developing universal moral values; self-learning of good habits.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the six ethical harms.
**Answer:** Ethical dilemma; increasing uncertainty; creating unhappiness; untruthfulness; eroding norms of freedom; induction of negative emotions.
