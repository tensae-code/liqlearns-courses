# Why Cultures Link and What Makes It Hard — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Linkage matters because exchange —
a) only benefits traders  b) enriches both cultures and grows understanding  c) erases all cultures
**Answer: b) enriches both cultures and grows understanding**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Ashenda and Irreecha are named as —
a) festivals where linkage is lived  b) obstacles to linkage  c) names of tools
**Answer: a) festivals where linkage is lived**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What makes linkage hard includes —
a) too much dancing  b) having festivals  c) suspicion instead of curiosity, and unequal contact
**Answer: c) suspicion instead of curiosity, and unequal contact**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The difficulties of linkage are —
a) imaginary  b) terrain to cross, not a reason to abandon it  c) proof linkage should stop
**Answer: b) terrain to cross, not a reason to abandon it**
