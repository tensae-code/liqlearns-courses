**Why linkage matters.** Cultural linkage matters because exchange enriches: each culture gains what the other carries — knowledge, craft, food, music, friendship — and understanding grows where contact is real.

**What makes linkage hard.** But linkage has difficulties inside it, and the lesson names the second half honestly: difference can be met with suspicion instead of curiosity, contact can be unequal, and old habits of standing apart pull against new bridges.
