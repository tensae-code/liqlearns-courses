# Unity in Diversity — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1.** Explain unity in diversity with the cloth picture.
**Answer:** Different threads, each keeping its colour, woven into one cloth — many cultures holding together as one community, stronger for the variety.

**2.** Give two daily acts that practise accommodation.
**Answer:** Any two ordinary acts: making a seat, including difference in a game, giving a listening turn, welcoming another's food or song.
