# Unity in Diversity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Unity in diversity means —
a) everyone becoming the same  b) cultures kept apart forever  c) many cultures, one community, stronger for its manyness
**Answer: c) many cultures, one community, stronger for its manyness**

**2.** Forcing sameness makes a community's cloth —
a) more colourful  b) thinner, not stronger  c) stronger in every way
**Answer: b) thinner, not stronger**

**3.** Accommodation is —
a) practised daily, not only praised  b) only for holiday speeches  c) finished once announced
**Answer: a) practised daily, not only praised**

**4.** In the weaving picture, the threads —
a) must all change to one colour  b) never touch each other  c) stay themselves while becoming one cloth
**Answer: c) stay themselves while becoming one cloth**
