# Unity in Diversity

## Do it first

Watch the threads in the moving picture: red, gold, green, dark, light — none of them changes colour, yet together they become one cloth. Hold the two truths at once: the threads stay themselves; the cloth is one.

## Unity does not require sameness

Unity in diversity means a community of many cultures is one community — and stronger for its manyness. Unity does not require sameness: the cloth is not woven from one thread repeated, but from different threads holding together. A community that forces sameness gets a thinner cloth, not a stronger one.

## Accommodation, practised daily

And the closing word of the unit: accommodation is something practised daily, not only praised. Speeches praise diversity on holidays; practice makes room on ordinary Tuesdays — in seating, in games, in who is listened to, in whose food and song are welcomed. Unity is a daily weaving, or it is a poster.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what unity in diversity means, using the cloth. Then name two daily acts — not holiday speeches — that practise accommodation.

## Do It Yourself

1. Practise one daily accommodation today: a seat, a game, a listening turn given to difference.
2. Learn one word or song-line from a culture not your own this week.
3. When you see difference mocked, be the thread that holds: include, don't join the mocking.
