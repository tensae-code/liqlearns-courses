**Accommodating cultural diversity.** To accommodate cultural diversity is to make room for other cultures: receiving other people's languages, customs and celebrations as part of the community's wealth rather than as intrusions.

**Room, practised.** Making room shows in ordinary welcomes: greeting a neighbour in their language, honouring a celebration that is not your own, learning a custom instead of mocking it.
