# Making Room for Other Cultures — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1.** Define accommodating cultural diversity in your own words.
**Answer:** Making room for other cultures — receiving their languages, customs and celebrations as part of the community's wealth, not as intrusions.

**2.** Name two ordinary acts that make room.
**Answer:** Any two of: greeting in another's language, honouring a celebration not your own, learning a custom instead of mocking it.
