# Making Room for Other Cultures — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Accommodating diversity means receiving other cultures as —
a) intrusions to endure  b) problems to hide  c) part of the community's wealth
**Answer: c) part of the community's wealth**

**2.** Making room is pictured as —
a) closing a gate politely  b) moving along a bench to make a real seat  c) building a fence
**Answer: b) moving along a bench to make a real seat**

**3.** Which act makes room?
a) Learning a custom instead of mocking it  b) Laughing at another dress  c) Refusing to greet in another language ever
**Answer: a) Learning a custom instead of mocking it**

**4.** A guest culture in an accommodating community is —
a) tolerated at the edge only  b) asked to disappear  c) genuinely given space, not squeezed into a corner
**Answer: c) genuinely given space, not squeezed into a corner**
