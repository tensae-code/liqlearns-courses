# Why Tolerance Matters and What Destroys It — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Exclusion — shutting people out — is an enemy of tolerance.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Where tolerance stands, difference can become wealth.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Division into camps strengthens a tolerant culture.
**Answer: False**
