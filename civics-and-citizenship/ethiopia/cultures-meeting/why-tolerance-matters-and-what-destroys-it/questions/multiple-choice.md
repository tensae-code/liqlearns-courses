# Why Tolerance Matters and What Destroys It — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tolerance matters because it is —
a) a decoration for festivals  b) a rule for guests only  c) the condition for one shared life among different people
**Answer: c) the condition for one shared life among different people**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Discrimination means —
a) studying differences carefully  b) treating people unequally for their difference  c) choosing your own friends
**Answer: b) treating people unequally for their difference**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is an enemy of tolerance?
a) Feelings of superiority  b) Feelings of gratitude  c) Learning another language
**Answer: a) Feelings of superiority**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Blind hatred destroys tolerance because it —
a) sees the person too clearly  b) is too patient  c) refuses to see the person
**Answer: c) refuses to see the person**
