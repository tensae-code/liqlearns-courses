# Why Tolerance Matters and What Destroys It — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the five enemies of tolerance.
**Answer:** Discrimination; exclusion; blind hatred; feelings of superiority; division.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Why is tolerance called load-bearing for a community?
**Answer:** Because the shared life — learning, working, settling troubles together — rests on it; when it falls, difference becomes fuel for conflict.
