# Video script — Why Tolerance Matters and What Destroys It

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A tolerant culture matters because it is the condition in which different people can actually live one shared life: learning together, working together, settling troubles with words.

**Why tolerance matters.** A tolerant culture matters because it is the condition in which different people can actually live one shared life: learning together, working together, settling troubles with words.

**What destroys tolerance.** Its enemies are named: discrimination — treating people unequally for their difference; exclusion — shutting people out; blind hatred — hatred that refuses to see the person; feelings of superiority — one group counting itself above others; and division — the splitting of the community into camps.

Now you. Tell your companion why tolerance matters, then name its five enemies one by one and say what each one does.

And this week: keeping every place at the table — refusing exclusion in your own conduct.
