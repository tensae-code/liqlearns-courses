# Cultural Linkage — Cultures in Conversation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Cultural linkage is —
a) the connection and exchange between cultures  b) cultures living side by side without speaking  c) one culture absorbing all others
**Answer: a) the connection and exchange between cultures**

**2.** Side-by-side living is geography; linkage is —
a) silence  b) competition  c) conversation
**Answer: c) conversation**

**3.** In linkage, what crosses between cultures?
a) Only goods for sale  b) Food, music, words and ideas  c) Nothing — borders are sealed
**Answer: b) Food, music, words and ideas**

**4.** Linkage grows out of accommodation by becoming —
a) active — talking, trading, celebrating together  b) more distant  c) a rule on paper
**Answer: a) active — talking, trading, celebrating together**
