# Cultural Linkage — Cultures in Conversation — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1.** Define cultural linkage in your own words.
**Answer:** The connection and exchange between cultures — people of different cultures in real communication, not merely side by side.

**2.** What is the difference between accommodation and linkage?
**Answer:** Accommodation makes room — lets another culture sit beside you; linkage is active — talking, trading and celebrating with it.
