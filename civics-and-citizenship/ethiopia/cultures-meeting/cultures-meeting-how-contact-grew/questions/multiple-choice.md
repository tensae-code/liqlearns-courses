# Cultures Meeting — How Contact Grew — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The tools the lesson names, in order, include —
a) stone, stick, drum only  b) only the internet, nothing before it  c) letter, phone, fax, email, internet
**Answer: c) letter, phone, fax, email, internet**

**2.** Contact between cultures today is —
a) forbidden  b) constant  c) rarer than ever
**Answer: b) constant**

**3.** The chapter opens cultural linkage by —
a) noticing how contact has grown  b) judging other cultures  c) memorising definitions only
**Answer: a) noticing how contact has grown**

**4.** Cultures now meet —
a) almost never  b) only at official summits  c) daily — in pockets, screens, markets, classrooms
**Answer: c) daily — in pockets, screens, markets, classrooms**
