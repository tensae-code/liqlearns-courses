# Signs That Cultures Are Truly Linked — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** A sign of true linkage is —
a) cultures never speaking  b) one culture's speeches about itself  c) effective communication across cultures
**Answer: c) effective communication across cultures**

**2.** 'Intercultural linkage in practice' means —
a) festivals once a decade  b) exchange happening in real life, not only in speeches  c) linkage written in a constitution only
**Answer: b) exchange happening in real life, not only in speeches**

**3.** Active communication behaviour means people —
a) take part, initiate, respond, join in  b) wait silently to be addressed  c) avoid all contact
**Answer: a) take part, initiate, respond, join in**

**4.** The three signs together answer the question —
a) 'Which culture is best?'  b) 'Should cultures meet at all?'  c) 'Is linkage really happening here?'
**Answer: c) 'Is linkage really happening here?'**
