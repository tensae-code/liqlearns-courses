# Signs That Cultures Are Truly Linked — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1.** List the three signs that cultures are truly linked.
**Answer:** Effective communication; intercultural linkage in practice; active communication behaviour.

**2.** Apply one sign: what would 'linkage in practice' look like in a market?
**Answer:** Real exchange in daily life — traders and buyers across cultures dealing, talking, and returning — not linkage only in speeches.
