# Tolerance — What It Is and What It Is Made Of — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Language and law are among the fields where equality is practised.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Tolerance is practised by a whole community, not only announced.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A shared meal can be a scene of tolerance.
