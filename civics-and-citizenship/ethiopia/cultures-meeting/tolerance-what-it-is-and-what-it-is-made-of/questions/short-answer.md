# Tolerance — What It Is and What It Is Made Of — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the five fields where equality is practised.
**Answer:** Language, politics, law, religion, social life.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why is tolerance more than patience?
**Answer:** Because its substance is equal treatment across every field — not enduring others while feeling superior inside.
