# Tolerance — What It Is and What It Is Made Of — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A culture of tolerance is —
a) ignoring all differences by force  b) a community's settled way of letting different people live fully alongside each other  c) one person's good mood
**Answer: b) a community's settled way of letting different people live fully alongside each other**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Tolerance is made of —
a) equality practised across named fields  b) patience while feeling superior  c) silence about injustice
**Answer: a) equality practised across named fields**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which field is named for practising equality?
a) Weather  b) Sport only  c) Religion
**Answer: c) Religion**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Tolerance shown as equal treatment, not mere patience — because —
a) treatment does not matter  b) feeling superior inside while tolerating outside is not tolerance  c) patience is a bad thing
**Answer: b) feeling superior inside while tolerating outside is not tolerance**
