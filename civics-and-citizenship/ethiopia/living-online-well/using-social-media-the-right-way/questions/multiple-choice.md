# Using Social Media the Right Way — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The rules of right use are —
a) stay safe, be honest with parents, talk before lasting decisions  b) hide everything, decide alone, never ask  c) use every app as much as possible
**Answer: a) stay safe, be honest with parents, talk before lasting decisions**

**2.** An online life that can be shown is —
a) a shameful thing  b) impossible for children  c) an online life that can be guided
**Answer: c) an online life that can be guided**

**3.** A lasting online decision should be —
a) decided by online friends  b) talked through with parents first  c) made in secret at once
**Answer: b) talked through with parents first**

**4.** The video script for this lesson is —
a) a model of the parent talk built from the three rules  b) a script printed in the book  c) a story about a stranger
**Answer: a) a model of the parent talk built from the three rules**
