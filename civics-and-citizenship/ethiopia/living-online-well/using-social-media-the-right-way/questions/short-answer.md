# Using Social Media the Right Way — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1.** List the three rules of using social media the right way.
**Answer:** Stay safe; be honest with your parents about your online life; talk with your parents before lasting decisions.

**2.** Describe the shape of the protective talk.
**Answer:** Say what happened or what you want to do, show it honestly, listen, and decide together where the decision lasts.
