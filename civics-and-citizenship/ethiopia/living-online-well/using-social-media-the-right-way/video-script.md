# Video script — Using Social Media the Right Way

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A short script modelling the talk with a parent about a social-media decision —
built from this lesson's three rules: stay safe, be honest with your parents, talk before lasting decisions.

CHILD: Mama, I want to show you something on my phone before I decide.
PARENT: Show me. I am listening.

CHILD: A page I follow is asking people my age to join a private group.
The people in it are strangers to me. I have not joined. I wanted to talk first.

PARENT: Thank you for showing me openly. What do you know about who runs the group?
CHILD: Nothing I can check. That is what worries me.

PARENT: Then we decide together: you do not join a group run by strangers we cannot check.
If a real friend invites you somewhere we can look at together, we will look again.

CHILD: Agreed. I will leave it, and I will tell my friend why.

See the shape of the talk: honest showing, listening, and a lasting decision made together.
That is using social media the right way.
