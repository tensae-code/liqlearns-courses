# Using Social Media the Right Way

## Do it first

Picture the kitchen-table scene: a child turns the phone toward a parent and shows the screen, openly, before being asked. Everything in this lesson is in that gesture. Read the three rules with the picture in mind.

## The three rules of right use

Using social media the right way rests on three rules: stay safe; be honest with your parents about your online life; and talk with your parents before lasting decisions. This is the chapter's ethical core — right use is not mainly about the app. It is about openness at home: an online life that can be shown is an online life that can be guided.

## The talk that protects

The video script for this lesson models the talk with a parent about a social-media decision — a builder's model built from these three rules, not a script from the book. The shape of the talk: say what happened or what you want to do, show it honestly, listen, and decide together where the decision lasts. A lasting decision made alone and in secret is the exact shape of online danger; the same decision talked through is the shape of safety.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the three rules of right use. Then role-play the talk: one of you is the child with a decision, the other the parent — honest showing, listening, deciding together.

## Do It Yourself

1. Show one parent or guardian your online activity openly this week, unasked.
2. Before one lasting online decision, talk it through at home first.
3. Write the three rules where you charge or keep a phone.
