# What Social Media Is Good For

## Do it first

Picture a grandparent in a far village and a child in town, faces on one small screen, laughing. The distance between them has not moved — but it has been crossed. Before reading, name two more goods a tool like that can do.

## The goods, named

What social media is good for: connection — people far apart staying close; learning — knowledge and teaching travelling to where the learner is; sharing — news, work, art and help passed hand to hand; and keeping in touch — the ordinary thread of family and friendship, maintained daily instead of lost to distance.

## A tool, not a threat — first

The goods are taught before the dangers, on purpose: pupils should meet social media as a tool, not a threat. A tool is judged by its use. The next lessons give the right use and the safety rules; this lesson fixes the starting point — the thing has real goods, worth using well.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the four goods of social media, with one example of each. Then say why the goods are taught before the dangers.

## Do It Yourself

1. Use social media once this week purely for learning — find one true, useful thing.
2. Keep in touch with one far person this week with a kind message or call.
3. Share one genuinely helpful thing online — help, news, or encouragement.
