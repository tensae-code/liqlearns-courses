# Video script — What Social Media Is Good For

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

What social media is good for: connection — people far apart staying close; learning — knowledge and teaching travelling to where the learner is; sharing — news, work, art and help passed hand to hand; and keeping in touch — the ordinary thread of family and friendship, maintained daily instead of lost to distance.

**The goods, named.** What social media is good for: connection — people far apart staying close; learning — knowledge and teaching travelling to where the learner is; sharing — news, work, art and help passed hand to hand; and keeping in touch — the ordinary thread of family and friendship, maintained daily instead of lost to distance.

**A tool, not a threat — first.** The goods are taught before the dangers, on purpose: pupils should meet social media as a tool, not a threat.

Now you. Tell your companion the four goods of social media, with one example of each.

And this week: using the tool for its goods — connect, learn, share, keep in touch.
