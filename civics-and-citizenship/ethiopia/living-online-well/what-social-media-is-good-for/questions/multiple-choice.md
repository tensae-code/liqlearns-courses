# What Social Media Is Good For — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which is a named good of social media?
a) Keeping in touch across distance  b) Forgetting your family faster  c) Avoiding all learning
**Answer: a) Keeping in touch across distance**

**2.** Learning through social media means —
a) copying homework unseen  b) skipping school  c) knowledge and teaching travelling to the learner
**Answer: c) knowledge and teaching travelling to the learner**

**3.** The goods are taught before the dangers so pupils meet social media as —
a) a game  b) a tool, not a threat  c) a perfect thing with no dangers
**Answer: b) a tool, not a threat**

**4.** A tool is judged by —
a) its use  b) its price  c) its colour
**Answer: a) its use**
