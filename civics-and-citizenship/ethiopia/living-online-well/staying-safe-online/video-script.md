# Video script — Staying Safe Online

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A read-aloud of Sara's story — told only in the summary the guide gives.

Sara had a friend on Facebook.
Not a friend her parents knew. A friend Sara kept secret from her parents.

That is the whole story we are given — and it is enough,
because the class's work is to ask: what does the secrecy itself endanger?

A secret friend means no parent can check who this person is.
A secret friend means no parent can help if the talk turns strange, or frightening, or wrong.
A secret friend means Sara faces everything alone —
the questions, the pressure, the danger — with the door closed against her own protectors.

The danger is not only the stranger.
The danger is the secrecy itself.

So the lesson ends with a check — yes or no, honestly:
Is anything in my online life secret from my parents?
If the answer is yes — that is the danger. End the secrecy.
