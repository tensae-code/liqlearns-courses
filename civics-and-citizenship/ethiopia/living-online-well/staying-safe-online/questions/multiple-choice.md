# Staying Safe Online — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which is a named danger of careless online use?
a) Slow rain  b) Violated privacy  c) Too much homework
**Answer: b) Violated privacy**

**2.** Strangers online use ___ against the young.
a) anonymity  b) their real names and addresses  c) school rules
**Answer: a) anonymity**

**3.** In Sara's story, the danger the class works out is —
a) the phone's battery  b) a broken screen  c) the secrecy itself
**Answer: c) the secrecy itself**

**4.** Adults who befriend children online to exploit them are —
a) a made-up worry  b) a named danger to refuse and report  c) online friends to trust
**Answer: b) a named danger to refuse and report**
