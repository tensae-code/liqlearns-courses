# Staying Safe Online — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1.** Name four dangers of unethical or careless online use.
**Answer:** Any four of: violated privacy; physical insecurity; information insecurity; strangers using anonymity; bullying; adults befriending children online to exploit them.

**2.** Tell Sara's story as the summary gives it, and state its point.
**Answer:** A girl keeps a 'Facebook friend' secret from her parents; the class works out what the secrecy itself endangers. Point: the secrecy itself is the danger — it cuts the child off from protection.
