# Social Media — What It Is

## Do it first

Name every app on a phone you know that lets people talk, post and reply. Count them on your fingers. This lesson gives the name for the whole family of them — and its exact list, as the guide gives it.

## What social media is

Social media is the places online where people connect, publish and respond. The guide names the platforms children actually meet: Facebook, Twitter, Instagram, Telegram, YouTube and LinkedIn. Learn the list as it is given: connecting, publishing and responding — those three verbs are what make a platform social media, whatever its name.

## A tool met clearly first

This level meets social media as a tool first: what it is, then what it is good for, then the right way to use it, then how to stay safe. Meeting it clearly — neither worshipped nor feared — is the ground of using it well.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what social media is, using the three verbs. Then name the six platforms the guide lists.

## Do It Yourself

1. Sort six apps you know: which are social media by the three verbs — connect, publish, respond?
2. Name one thing you connected, one you published, one you responded to online.
3. Teach the platform list to someone at home, exactly as given.
