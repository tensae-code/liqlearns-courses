# Social Media — What It Is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Social media is where people —
a) connect, publish and respond  b) only play games alone  c) only read the news silently
**Answer: a) connect, publish and respond**

**2.** Which platform is on the guide's list?
a) A platform invented for this quiz  b) None of them  c) Telegram
**Answer: c) Telegram**

**3.** The guide's list has how many platforms?
a) Ten  b) Six  c) Three
**Answer: b) Six**

**4.** This level meets social media first as —
a) a tool to understand clearly  b) a threat only  c) a toy only
**Answer: a) a tool to understand clearly**
