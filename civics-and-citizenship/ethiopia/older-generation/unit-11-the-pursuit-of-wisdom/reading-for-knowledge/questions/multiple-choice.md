# Reading for knowledge — literacy, women and Galileo's banned book — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** 95% of the world's adult illiterates live in —
a) developing countries  b) Europe  c) cities only
**Answer: a) developing countries**

**2.** Ethiopia's male literacy in 2000 was —
a) 31%  b) 95%  c) 47%
**Answer: c) 47%**

**3.** Female literacy in 2000 was —
a) 47%  b) 20%  c) 31%
**Answer: c) 31%**

**4.** In 1990 the figures were —
a) equal  b) 37% men, 20% women  c) 47% and 31%
**Answer: b) 37% men, 20% women**

**5.** Ethiopia's literacy stood below —
a) Kenya's and Sudan's  b) every country's  c) no country's
**Answer: a) Kenya's and Sudan's**

**6.** Educating a woman educates —
a) an entire family  b) only herself  c) only her sons
**Answer: a) an entire family**

**7.** Galileo's telescope year was —
a) 1616  b) 1689  c) 1609
**Answer: c) 1609**

**8.** His book was placed on the Index of Forbidden Books in —
a) 1609  b) 1700  c) 1616
**Answer: c) 1616**

**9.** The telescope demoted the earth to —
a) the centre of all  b) a star  c) a planet among planets
**Answer: c) a planet among planets**

**10.** The discovery survived the ban through —
a) smuggled telescopes  b) a papal pardon at once  c) the printing press
**Answer: c) the printing press**
