# Reading for knowledge — literacy, women and Galileo's banned book — short answer

Answer in one to three sentences, from the lesson.

**1.** Give Ethiopia's 2000 literacy figures and their 1990 comparison.
**Answer:** 47% of men and 31% of women in 2000, up from 37% and 20% in 1990 — but below Kenya's and Sudan's.

**2.** What is Mayor's chain about educating women?
**Answer:** Educating a woman educates an entire family — children's education becomes a living reality from the day they are born.

**3.** How did Galileo's discovery survive its ban?
**Answer:** The printing press carried it past the Index of Forbidden Books; conceptions of the universe changed anyway.
