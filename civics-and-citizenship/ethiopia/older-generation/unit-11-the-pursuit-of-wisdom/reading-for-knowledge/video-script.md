One lesson from the older course.

Reading is the citizen's self-education. UNESCO's figures, as the guide keys them: 95% of the world's adult illiterates live in developing countries; Ethiopia's literacy in 2000 stood at 47% of men and 31% of women — up from 37% and 20% in 1990, but below Kenya's and Sudan's. Mayor's chain explains why the women's figure matters most: educating a woman educates an entire family, making children's education 'a living reality from the day they are born'.
