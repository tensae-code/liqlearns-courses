# Knowledge — why it rules, and the eclipse that proves it can be tested — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Mayor wants education to promote the successes of —
a) only top students  b) officials' children  c) everybody, not a selected elite
**Answer: c) everybody, not a selected elite**

**2.** Elites were marked for destruction in —
a) Botswana  b) India  c) Burundi 1972, Cambodia, China's Red Guard years
**Answer: c) Burundi 1972, Cambodia, China's Red Guard years**

**3.** Ethiopia's 70% rule favours —
a) law  b) languages only  c) science and technology in higher education
**Answer: c) science and technology in higher education**

**4.** The sun is how many times the moon's width?
a) 400  b) 40  c) 4,000
**Answer: a) 400**

**5.** And how many times further away?
a) 400  b) 40  c) 4,000
**Answer: a) 400**

**6.** The moon recedes each year by —
a) 3.8 metres  b) 38 cm  c) 3.8 cm
**Answer: c) 3.8 cm**

**7.** Total eclipses will one day end because —
a) the earth speeds up  b) the moon is moving away  c) the sun is shrinking
**Answer: b) the moon is moving away**

**8.** The eclipse passage is adapted from —
a) a myth book  b) Pankhurst  c) New Scientist, 31.1.2009
**Answer: c) New Scientist, 31.1.2009**

**9.** Knowledge can be trusted when it —
a) can be tested  b) is very old  c) is printed
**Answer: a) can be tested**

**10.** Knowledge is called democracy's —
a) precondition  b) enemy  c) decoration
**Answer: a) precondition**
