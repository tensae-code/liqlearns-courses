# Knowledge — why it rules, and the eclipse that proves it can be tested — short answer

Answer in one to three sentences, from the lesson.

**1.** What does Mayor demand of education?
**Answer:** That it promote 'the successes and accomplishments of everybody', not select an elite.

**2.** What is Ethiopia's 70% rule?
**Answer:** 70% of higher-education provision for science and technology.

**3.** Why do the sun and moon fit exactly at an eclipse?
**Answer:** The sun is 400 times the moon's width and 400 times further away, so the two discs match in our sky.
