# Truth versus myth — Sheba, Alexander and how to tell — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** A myth is —
a) believed but unprovable  b) always a lie by enemies  c) proven history
**Answer: a) believed but unprovable**

**2.** One purpose myths are created for is —
a) navigation only  b) politics  c) cooking
**Answer: b) politics**

**3.** El Dorado was —
a) a golden city that never was  b) a real Ethiopian town  c) a Greek port
**Answer: a) a golden city that never was**

**4.** The Sheba and Solomon story is taken by historians as —
a) myth, though embroiled in Ethiopia's history  b) proven fact in all parts  c) a foreign invention of 2009
**Answer: a) myth, though embroiled in Ethiopia's history**

**5.** Menelik, in the story, is —
a) Alexander's son  b) a Greek hero  c) the child of Sheba and Solomon
**Answer: c) the child of Sheba and Solomon**

**6.** Archaeology keeps proving —
a) all myths false  b) no myths  c) parts of Greek myths
**Answer: c) parts of Greek myths**

**7.** Bucephalus was —
a) his teacher  b) his ship  c) Alexander's horse
**Answer: c) Alexander's horse**

**8.** Alexander tamed him by —
a) starving him  b) turning him toward the sun, away from his shadow  c) whipping him
**Answer: b) turning him toward the sun, away from his shadow**

**9.** Alexander's teacher was —
a) Philip  b) Gombrich  c) Aristotle
**Answer: c) Aristotle**

**10.** Alexander died at thirty-two in —
a) 1977  b) 323 BC  c) 1616
**Answer: b) 323 BC**
