# Truth versus myth — Sheba, Alexander and how to tell — short answer

Answer in one to three sentences, from the lesson.

**1.** How is myth defined in the lesson?
**Answer:** Believed but unprovable, created for purposes — among which politics is one.

**2.** How did Alexander tame Bucephalus?
**Answer:** He turned the horse from his own shadow — facing him toward the sun, so the shadow he feared vanished.

**3.** Why are myths not pure invention?
**Answer:** They carry real people and places — archaeology keeps proving parts of them.
