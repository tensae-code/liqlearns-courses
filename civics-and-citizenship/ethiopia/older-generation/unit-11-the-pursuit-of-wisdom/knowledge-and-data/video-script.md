One lesson from the older course.

Data is knowledge's raw material. Scientific knowledge's works surround us — drugs, vaccinations, infrastructure in health, technology, communication, power, farming and manufacturing — and every one began as observations honestly gathered. Mayor's warning refines the point: knowledge 'is always positive'; only its application can be perverse. His dilemmas make it concrete: fertilisers that feed and poison; cures that require animal testing. The data does not decide the values; the data makes honest deciding possible.

Remember it with its case. Now — say it back, in your own words.
