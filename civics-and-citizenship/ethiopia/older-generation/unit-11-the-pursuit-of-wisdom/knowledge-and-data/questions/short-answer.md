# Knowledge and data — evidence before conclusion — short answer

Answer in one to three sentences, from the lesson.

**1.** What is Mayor's warning about knowledge?
**Answer:** Knowledge 'is always positive'; only its application can be perverse.

**2.** Name Mayor's two dilemmas.
**Answer:** Fertilisers that feed and poison; cures that require animal testing.

**3.** What raw data would the school study collect?
**Answer:** Enrolments and examination results, by sex and grade.
