# Knowledge and data — evidence before conclusion — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Data is —
a) knowledge's raw material  b) the enemy of knowledge  c) decoration for reports
**Answer: a) knowledge's raw material**

**2.** Scientific knowledge's works include —
a) drugs and vaccinations  b) only weapons  c) only myths
**Answer: a) drugs and vaccinations**

**3.** Mayor says knowledge is always —
a) dangerous  b) useless  c) positive; its application can be perverse
**Answer: c) positive; its application can be perverse**

**4.** Fertilisers in Mayor's dilemma —
a) feed and poison  b) only feed  c) only poison
**Answer: a) feed and poison**

**5.** Animal testing appears as —
a) a settled crime  b) a school activity  c) a dilemma in developing cures
**Answer: c) a dilemma in developing cures**

**6.** The case study examines —
a) female students' good performance  b) boys' football  c) teachers' pay
**Answer: a) female students' good performance**

**7.** Raw data to collect: enrolments and —
a) teachers' opinions  b) examination results, by sex and grade  c) rumours
**Answer: b) examination results, by sex and grade**

**8.** Data makes possible —
a) no decisions  b) honest deciding  c) automatic virtue
**Answer: b) honest deciding**

**9.** Evidence before conclusion means —
a) conclude, then find support  b) ask the loudest person  c) gather data first, judge after
**Answer: c) gather data first, judge after**

**10.** Infrastructure named spans —
a) schools only  b) health, power, farming, manufacturing and more  c) roads only
**Answer: b) health, power, farming, manufacturing and more**
