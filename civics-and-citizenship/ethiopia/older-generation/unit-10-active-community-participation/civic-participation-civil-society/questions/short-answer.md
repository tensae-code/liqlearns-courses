# Civic participation — civil society, EWLA and the stand against violence — short answer

Answer in one to three sentences, from the lesson.

**1.** Give Dessalegn's definition of civil society.
**Answer:** The space intermediary between the state on the one hand and the lowest unit of social life, the family, on the other.

**2.** Name EWLA's three public-education tools.
**Answer:** Workshops for law-enforcement officials and judges; radio broadcasts; leaflets.

**3.** What did the 2008 study document?
**Answer:** Violence against girls in primary schools — in school and on the way to it.
