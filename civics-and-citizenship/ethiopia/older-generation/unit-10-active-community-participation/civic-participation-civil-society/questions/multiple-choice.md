# Civic participation — civil society, EWLA and the stand against violence — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Civil society sits between —
a) two families  b) the army and police  c) the state and the family
**Answer: c) the state and the family**

**2.** Dessalegn calls that space —
a) empty  b) forbidden  c) intermediary
**Answer: c) intermediary**

**3.** EWLA is —
a) the Ethiopian Women Lawyers' Association  b) a court  c) a ministry
**Answer: a) the Ethiopian Women Lawyers' Association**

**4.** EWLA's workshops are for —
a) farmers only  b) foreign lawyers  c) law-enforcement officials and judges
**Answer: c) law-enforcement officials and judges**

**5.** EWLA also uses —
a) posters in banks  b) radio broadcasts and leaflets  c) television adverts only
**Answer: b) radio broadcasts and leaflets**

**6.** Its target injustices include —
a) marital injustice and gender discrimination  b) tax evasion  c) littering
**Answer: a) marital injustice and gender discrimination**

**7.** The 2008 study concerned violence against —
a) drivers  b) girls in primary schools  c) shopkeepers
**Answer: b) girls in primary schools**

**8.** Campaign rallies in the lesson stand against —
a) school fees  b) elections  c) violence against women
**Answer: c) violence against women**

**9.** Learners audit —
a) a bank  b) their school's development activities and civic societies  c) the ministry
**Answer: b) their school's development activities and civic societies**

**10.** Civic participation means —
a) only obeying  b) organising and acting in the space between family and state  c) only voting
**Answer: b) organising and acting in the space between family and state**
