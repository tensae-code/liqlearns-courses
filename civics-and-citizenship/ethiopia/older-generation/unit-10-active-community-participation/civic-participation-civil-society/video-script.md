# Video script — Civic participation — civil society, EWLA and the stand against violence

One lesson from the older course.

Civil society, in Dessalegn's words, occupies 'the space intermediary between the state on the one hand and the lowest unit of social life, the family, on the other'. Two cases embody it. EWLA — the Ethiopian Women Lawyers' Association — runs a public-education programme of workshops for law-enforcement officials and judges, radio broadcasts and leaflets, bringing marital injustice and gender discrimination into public view. And campaign rallies stand against violence against women, backed by the 2008 study of violence against girls in primary schools, in school and on the way to it.

Remember it with its case. Now — say it back, in your own words.
