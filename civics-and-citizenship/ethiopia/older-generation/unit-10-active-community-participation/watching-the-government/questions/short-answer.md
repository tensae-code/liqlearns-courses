# Watching the government — participation every day, and the 2008 campaign case — short answer

Answer in one to three sentences, from the lesson.

**1.** Quote Jefferson's standard for participation.
**Answer:** The citizen as participator 'not merely at an election one day in the year but everyday' (1816).

**2.** Name participation's three enemies.
**Answer:** Apathy; fear in one-party states; ignorance of how to begin.

**3.** Why does the lesson keep the 2008 campaign in the book's present tense?
**Answer:** Because the book was written in 2008/09 — the era tense is itself older-generation evidence, kept honestly rather than silently updated.
