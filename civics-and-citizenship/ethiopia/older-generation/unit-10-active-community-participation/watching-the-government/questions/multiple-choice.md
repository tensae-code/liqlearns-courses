# Watching the government — participation every day, and the 2008 campaign case — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Jefferson's words date to —
a) 1963  b) 1816  c) 2008
**Answer: b) 1816**

**2.** Participation is 'not merely at an election' but —
a) monthly  b) when officials invite it  c) everyday
**Answer: c) everyday**

**3.** Apathy grows where —
a) everything seems fine  b) elections are banned  c) schools close
**Answer: a) everything seems fine**

**4.** Fear blocks participation especially in —
a) families  b) one-party states  c) villages
**Answer: b) one-party states**

**5.** Ethiopia's own risk as a new democracy is named as —
a) too many parties  b) excess wealth  c) ignorance of how to begin
**Answer: c) ignorance of how to begin**

**6.** A framed public agenda means —
a) a party manifesto only  b) citizens setting out common interests for officials to answer  c) a poster in a frame
**Answer: b) citizens setting out common interests for officials to answer**

**7.** Demonstrations are cross-referred to —
a) the syllabus only  b) Unit 1, Lesson 3 of this same course  c) a foreign textbook
**Answer: b) Unit 1, Lesson 3 of this same course**

**8.** The campaign case is —
a) a 2024 campaign  b) a local election  c) Obama's 2008 presidential primary campaign
**Answer: c) Obama's 2008 presidential primary campaign**

**9.** After the speeches end, citizens should —
a) applaud regardless  b) expect and check  c) forget politics
**Answer: b) expect and check**

**10.** Watching the government is —
a) participation, not disloyalty  b) a crime  c) officials' work only
**Answer: a) participation, not disloyalty**
