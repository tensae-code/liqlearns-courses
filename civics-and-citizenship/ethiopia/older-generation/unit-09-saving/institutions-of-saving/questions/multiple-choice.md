# Institutions of saving — equb, Iddir, banks and insurers, from salt bars to policies — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Gondar's money-lending was banned in —
a) 1905  b) 1974  c) 1678
**Answer: c) 1678**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Sahla Sellase's treasury was —
a) a bank in Addis  b) a ship  c) a cave north of Ankobar with near 300 jars
**Answer: c) a cave north of Ankobar with near 300 jars**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Interest was reckoned at one bar of salt per —
a) house  b) Maria Theresa dollar  c) cow
**Answer: b) Maria Theresa dollar**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** An equb works by —
a) lottery  b) bank loans  c) members contributing in turn; the pot rotates
**Answer: c) members contributing in turn; the pot rotates**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** An Iddir traditionally organises neighbours for —
a) trade fairs  b) elections  c) funerals and support
**Answer: c) funerals and support**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Metebaber Meredaja is —
a) an insurer  b) a modern Iddir debated in the lesson  c) a bank
**Answer: b) a modern Iddir debated in the lesson**

**7. (Sub lesson 2)** The debate asks whether an Iddir should also cover —
a) only funerals forever  b) foreign travel  c) medication and members' economic life
**Answer: c) medication and members' economic life**

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** Ethiopian banking begins with an agreement between Menelik II and —
a) a Gondar council  b) the National Bank of Egypt  c) the World Bank
**Answer: b) the National Bank of Egypt**

**9. (Sub lesson 3)** Insurance in Ethiopia dates from —
a) 1678  b) 1991  c) 1905
**Answer: c) 1905**

**10. (Sub lesson 3)** Insurance was nationalised in —
a) 2000  b) 1974  c) 1963
**Answer: b) 1974**

**11. (Sub lesson 3)** By 2000 there were —
a) two companies  b) ninety companies  c) nine insurance companies and eleven brokerages
**Answer: c) nine insurance companies and eleven brokerages**

**12. (Sub lesson 3)** Salt bars functioned as —
a) money — interest and value reckoned in salt  b) building blocks  c) medicine
**Answer: a) money — interest and value reckoned in salt**
