# Institutions of saving — equb, Iddir, banks and insurers, from salt bars to policies — short answer

Answer in one to three sentences, from the lesson.

**1.** What did the church council of 1678 ban, and where?
**Answer:** Money-lending, at Gondar.

**2.** What is debated about Metebaber Meredaja?
**Answer:** Whether an Iddir should serve only funerals, or also medication and members' economic life.

**3.** Trace the insurance line in three steps.
**Answer:** Insurance from 1905; nationalised into the Ethiopian Insurance Corporation in 1974; private insurers again after 1991 (nine companies and eleven brokerages by 2000).
