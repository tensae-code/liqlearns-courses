One lesson from the older course.

Saving is reframed as national economics: buy local and avoid wastage, so the country stops importing what it can make. The guide honestly supplies the cynical counter — de Rivero: transnational corporations invest only where technical capacity, infrastructure, market size, legal security and stability already promise profit. New thinking must build those conditions, not just chant 'buy local'.
