# New thinking in saving — buy local, waste nothing, the leather proof — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** New thinking in saving means —
a) buy local and avoid wastage, nationally  b) never spending  c) hoarding cash
**Answer: a) buy local and avoid wastage, nationally**

**2.** Importing what a country can make —
a) is always cheaper  b) builds capacity  c) sends its money out needlessly
**Answer: c) sends its money out needlessly**

**3.** De Rivero says corporations invest only where —
a) capacity, infrastructure, market, legal security and stability promise profit  b) labour is cheapest  c) governments plead
**Answer: a) capacity, infrastructure, market, legal security and stability promise profit**

**4.** The leather case is offered as —
a) a warning  b) a fairy tale  c) proof the new thinking can work
**Answer: c) proof the new thinking can work**

**5.** Leather exports rose in 2008 by —
a) 13%  b) 1.3%  c) 130%
**Answer: a) 13%**

**6.** The programme behind the rise was —
a) a tax holiday  b) a lottery  c) the engineering capacity building programme
**Answer: c) the engineering capacity building programme**

**7.** A European glove manufacturer —
a) set up to buy from local tanneries  b) banned Ethiopian leather  c) moved to Asia
**Answer: a) set up to buy from local tanneries**

**8.** Export-standard shoes means —
a) shoes for exporters only  b) paper shoes  c) quality good enough for foreign markets
**Answer: c) quality good enough for foreign markets**

**9.** Avoiding wastage is part of saving because —
a) wasted resources are savings thrown away  b) waste is taxed  c) the guide says so without reason
**Answer: a) wasted resources are savings thrown away**

**10.** New thinking must build —
a) the conditions investors and makers need  b) only slogans  c) import bans alone
**Answer: a) the conditions investors and makers need**
