# New thinking in saving — buy local, waste nothing, the leather proof — short answer

Answer in one to three sentences, from the lesson.

**1.** State de Rivero's counter-argument honestly.
**Answer:** Transnational corporations invest only where technical capacity, infrastructure, market size, legal security and stability already promise profit.

**2.** How much did leather exports grow in 2008?
**Answer:** 13%, under the engineering capacity building programme.

**3.** What two foreign moves showed confidence in Ethiopian leather?
**Answer:** A European glove manufacturer set up to buy from local tanneries; a German company planted itself in Ethiopia.
