One lesson from the older course.

Economists argue the direction both ways: saving as the engine of growth, against growth as the cause of saving. The machinery, either way, is the banks — Ethiopian banks' savings, investment and loans — and, internationally, borrowing through the World Bank and IMF under strict tender procedures against corruption: the Bank's HIV/AIDS support of over $1.6 billion to more than 30 sub-Saharan African countries since 2000, and its lighting-by-2030, broadband and trade-corridor aims, show the scale.
