# Saving becomes investment — banks, the World Bank and Ethiopia's banking story — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The economists' debate is —
a) saving drives growth versus growth drives saving  b) banks versus cash  c) tax versus saving
**Answer: a) saving drives growth versus growth drives saving**

**2.** World Bank/IMF borrowing runs under —
a) no procedures  b) verbal promises  c) strict tender procedures against corruption
**Answer: c) strict tender procedures against corruption**

**3.** World Bank HIV/AIDS support since 2000 exceeded —
a) $16 million  b) $160,000  c) $1.6 billion
**Answer: c) $1.6 billion**

**4.** The Bank of Abyssinia opened in —
a) 1906  b) 1943  c) 1963
**Answer: a) 1906**

**5.** It was liquidated in —
a) 1994  b) 1931  c) 1974
**Answer: b) 1931**

**6.** The State Bank dates to —
a) 1964  b) 1943  c) 1906
**Answer: b) 1943**

**7.** The National Bank and Commercial Bank date to —
a) 1991 and 1992  b) 1963 and 1964  c) 1943 and 1944
**Answer: b) 1963 and 1964**

**8.** The Commercial Bank began with —
a) 2 million Birr  b) 200 million Birr  c) 20 million Birr
**Answer: c) 20 million Birr**

**9.** Banking was nationalised in —
a) 1931  b) 2000  c) 1974
**Answer: c) 1974**

**10.** In 1994 the National Bank was re-established as —
a) a private company  b) a judicial entity separate from government  c) a ministry department
**Answer: b) a judicial entity separate from government**
