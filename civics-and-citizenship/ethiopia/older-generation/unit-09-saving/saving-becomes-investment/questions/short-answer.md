# Saving becomes investment — banks, the World Bank and Ethiopia's banking story — short answer

Answer in one to three sentences, from the lesson.

**1.** State the economists' argument in both directions.
**Answer:** Saving as the engine of growth, against growth as the cause of saving.

**2.** How much HIV/AIDS support had the World Bank given since 2000, and to how many countries?
**Answer:** Over $1.6 billion, to more than 30 sub-Saharan African countries.

**3.** Give the banking biography's spine in five dates.
**Answer:** 1906 Bank of Abyssinia (liquidated 1931); 1943 State Bank; 1963 National Bank and 1964 Commercial Bank; 1974 nationalisation; 1994 National Bank re-established separate from government.
