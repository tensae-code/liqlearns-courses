One lesson from the older course.

Household saving takes real forms: gold and silver ornaments; life insurance; provident funds and pension funds, where employee and employer contribute an agreed percentage; deposits; government securities — treasury bills and bonds of the National Bank of Ethiopia; and physical assets such as houses and cars. Learners cost their own futures in the lesson's exercise — single, married, family of four, retired — with inflation set against bank interest: saving that loses value is a leaking bucket.
