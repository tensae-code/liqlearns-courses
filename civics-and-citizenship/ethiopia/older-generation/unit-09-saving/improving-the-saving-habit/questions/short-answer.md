# Improving the saving habit — seven ways to save, and the advertising trap — short answer

Answer in one to three sentences, from the lesson.

**1.** List five real forms of household saving from the lesson.
**Answer:** Any five of: gold and silver ornaments; life insurance; provident/pension funds; deposits; government securities (treasury bills and bonds); physical assets such as houses and cars.

**2.** Name three advertising tricks the lesson dissects.
**Answer:** Product placement; window displays without prices; haggling that never dips below the seller's margin.

**3.** Why set inflation against bank interest when costing a future?
**Answer:** Because saving that grows slower than prices loses value — a leaking bucket.
