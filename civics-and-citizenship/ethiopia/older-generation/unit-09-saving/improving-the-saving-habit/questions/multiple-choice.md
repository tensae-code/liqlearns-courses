# Improving the saving habit — seven ways to save, and the advertising trap — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** A provident fund is built by —
a) lottery wins  b) employee and employer contributing an agreed percentage  c) the state alone
**Answer: b) employee and employer contributing an agreed percentage**

**2.** Treasury bills and bonds are issued through —
a) private shops  b) the Iddir  c) the National Bank of Ethiopia
**Answer: c) the National Bank of Ethiopia**

**3.** Gold and silver ornaments count as —
a) a tax  b) a real form of household saving  c) mere decoration
**Answer: b) a real form of household saving**

**4.** Physical assets named include —
a) shoes and hats  b) songs  c) houses and cars
**Answer: c) houses and cars**

**5.** Product placement hides advertisements —
a) on tax forms  b) in courtrooms  c) inside dramas and stories
**Answer: c) inside dramas and stories**

**6.** Window displays without prices aim to —
a) draw you in before cost can cool you  b) save paper  c) follow the law
**Answer: a) draw you in before cost can cool you**

**7.** Haggling, in the lesson's analysis, never dips below —
a) the seller's margin  b) half price  c) one birr
**Answer: a) the seller's margin**

**8.** Ato Molla is the lesson's —
a) warning — extravagance ruining a saver  b) model saver  c) banker
**Answer: a) warning — extravagance ruining a saver**

**9.** Inflation set against interest shows whether saving —
a) really grows or quietly leaks  b) is fashionable  c) is taxed
**Answer: a) really grows or quietly leaks**

**10.** Learners cost their futures as —
a) students only  b) officials  c) single, married, family of four, retired
**Answer: c) single, married, family of four, retired**
