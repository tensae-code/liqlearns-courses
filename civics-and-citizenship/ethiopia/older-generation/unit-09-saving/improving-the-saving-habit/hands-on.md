# Hands-on — Improving the saving habit — seven ways to save, and the advertising trap

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Work it with a case
Take the lesson's central case and re-stage it with people around you: assign the parts, speak the positions, and judge the outcome using only what the lesson taught.

## Map it home
Find this lesson's issue within walking distance of your school — a place, an office, a meeting, a market. Write three lines: what you found, and what the lesson says about it.

## The two-source check
Take one claim from this lesson and check it against a second source — another book, a knowledgeable elder, an official notice. Write what agreed and what differed.
