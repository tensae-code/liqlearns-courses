# Equality of nations, nationalities and peoples — language and the land — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Nigeria's civil war ran —
a) 1991–95  b) 1967–70  c) 1974–91
**Answer: b) 1967–70**

**2.** Suberu says federalism in Nigeria —
a) ended schooling  b) defused ethnic conflict and redistributed resources  c) caused the war
**Answer: b) defused ethnic conflict and redistributed resources**

**3.** Haile Selassie's language aim was —
a) fifteen languages  b) English only  c) Amharic only
**Answer: c) Amharic only**

**4.** The Derg's literacy campaign used —
a) fifteen languages  b) one language  c) no languages
**Answer: a) fifteen languages**

**5.** The Derg's campaign still failed to —
a) develop the local languages  b) print posters  c) find teachers
**Answer: a) develop the local languages**

**6.** Cohen's role in the lesson is arguing —
a) against all schools  b) both sides of developing local languages  c) for Amharic only
**Answer: b) both sides of developing local languages**

**7.** Language equality decides whether a child —
a) meets school in her own tongue or a stranger's  b) attends school at all  c) pays taxes
**Answer: a) meets school in her own tongue or a stranger's**

**8.** The Bale and Gedeo case detail is deferred by the guide to —
a) the student book of Grade 7  b) the Grade 12 Teacher Guide  c) a newspaper
**Answer: b) the Grade 12 Teacher Guide**

**9.** Equality of peoples is presented as —
a) a foreign import  b) nation-building  c) mere politeness
**Answer: b) nation-building**

**10.** Redistributing resources among peoples is part of —
a) ending federalism  b) the language debate only  c) making equality real
**Answer: c) making equality real**
