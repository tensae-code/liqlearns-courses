# Equality of nations, nationalities and peoples — language and the land — short answer

Answer in one to three sentences, from the lesson.

**1.** What did federalism do for Nigeria after its civil war, in Suberu's account?
**Answer:** It defused ethnic conflict and redistributed resources.

**2.** Contrast Haile Selassie's and the Derg's language policies.
**Answer:** Haile Selassie aimed at Amharic only; the Derg campaigned for literacy in fifteen languages but still failed to develop them.

**3.** Why does this lesson teach the Bale and Gedeo case only as a summary?
**Answer:** Because the guide itself only summarises it and defers the detail to the Grade 12 Teacher Guide.
