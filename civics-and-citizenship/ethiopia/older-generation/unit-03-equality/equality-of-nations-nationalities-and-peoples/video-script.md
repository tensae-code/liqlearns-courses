One lesson from the older course.

Equality of peoples is nation-building, not politeness. Nigeria's answer to its 1967–70 civil war, in Suberu's account, was federalism: it defused ethnic conflict and redistributed resources among the peoples. Ethiopia's hardest equality question is language. Cohen argued both sides of developing local languages for education. Haile Selassie aimed at Amharic only. The Derg ran a literacy campaign in fifteen languages — yet still failed to develop them. Each settlement decides whether a child meets school in her own tongue or in a stranger's.

Remember it with its case. Now — say it back, in your own words.
