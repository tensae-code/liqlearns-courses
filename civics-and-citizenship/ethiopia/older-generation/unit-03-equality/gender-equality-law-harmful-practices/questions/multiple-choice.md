# Gender equality — law, harmful practices and women's achievement — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** CEDAW in this lesson is —
a) a political party  b) the convention whose implementation report Ethiopia filed in 2009  c) a tax
**Answer: b) the convention whose implementation report Ethiopia filed in 2009**

**2.** The Constitution declares women's equality —
a) only in cities  b) as a suggestion  c) unequivocally, and institutions must enforce it
**Answer: c) unequivocally, and institutions must enforce it**

**3.** Female circumcision and infibulation are crimes under —
a) the Civil Code of 1967  b) no law  c) Criminal Code Articles 565–566
**Answer: c) Criminal Code Articles 565–566**

**4.** Women's seats in the third parliament reached —
a) 117 — 25%  b) 12 — 2%  c) 300 — 60%
**Answer: a) 117 — 25%**

**5.** Maternal mortality fell from 871 to —
a) 673 per 100,000  b) 100 per 100,000  c) 900 per 100,000
**Answer: a) 673 per 100,000**

**6.** The mortality figures compare the years —
a) 2000 and 2005  b) 1990 and 2020  c) 2005 and 2009
**Answer: a) 2000 and 2005**

**7.** Tirunesh Dibaba is celebrated as —
a) a champion runner  b) a minister  c) a judge
**Answer: a) a champion runner**

**8.** Aster Awoke is named as —
a) a general  b) a banker  c) a performer — a singer
**Answer: c) a performer — a singer**

**9.** The National Committee works to eradicate —
a) women's sports  b) harmful traditional practices  c) girls' schooling
**Answer: b) harmful traditional practices**

**10.** The fall in maternal mortality is described as —
a) real, and still high  b) complete victory  c) a printing error
**Answer: a) real, and still high**
