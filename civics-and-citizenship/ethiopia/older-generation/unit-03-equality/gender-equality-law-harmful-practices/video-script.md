The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. Short script.

One lesson from the older course.

The lesson's spine is Ethiopia's own CEDAW implementation report of 2009. The Constitution declares women's equality unequivocally, and every institution has a duty to enforce it. A National Committee works on eradicating harmful traditional practices, and the Criminal Code (Articles 565–566, 2005) makes female circumcision and infibulation crimes. Progress is counted, not just promised: women's parliamentary seats tripled and reached 117 — 25% — in the third parliament; maternal mortality fell from 871 to 673 per 100,000 between 2000 and 2005 — a real fall, and still high.

Remember it with its case. Now — say it back, in your own words.
