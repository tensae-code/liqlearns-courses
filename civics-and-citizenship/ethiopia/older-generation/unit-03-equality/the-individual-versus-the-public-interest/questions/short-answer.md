# The individual versus the public interest — dams and resettlement — short answer

Answer in one to three sentences, from the lesson.

**1.** What share of Ethiopia's population had power in the March 2009 report?
**Answer:** Only 25%.

**2.** Name the opposition's three arguments against the Omo scheme.
**Answer:** Displacement of people along the Omo and Lake Turkana; damage to the environment; food security.

**3.** Which four parties debate resettlement at the lesson's close?
**Answer:** Residents, environmentalists, electricity users, and government.
