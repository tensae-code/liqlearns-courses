# The individual versus the public interest — dams and resettlement — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The Tekeze case concerns —
a) a railway  b) a hospital  c) a hydro-electric plant
**Answer: c) a hydro-electric plant**

**2.** In the 2009 report, power reached —
a) 25% of the population  b) 90% of the population  c) 5% of the population
**Answer: a) 25% of the population**

**3.** How many large schemes were under construction in the report?
a) One  b) Twenty  c) Five
**Answer: c) Five**

**4.** The opposition's case included —
a) higher taxes  b) school uniforms  c) displacement, environment and food security
**Answer: c) displacement, environment and food security**

**5.** Lake Turkana appears in the case because —
a) it is in Europe  b) a dam was built on it in 1923  c) the Omo flows toward it and its people are affected
**Answer: c) the Omo flows toward it and its people are affected**

**6.** A resettlement debate party is —
a) foreign tourists  b) the army only  c) the residents themselves
**Answer: c) the residents themselves**

**7.** The public interest must —
a) justify itself to the individual  b) never be questioned  c) always win silently
**Answer: a) justify itself to the individual**

**8.** Export revenue in the report comes from —
a) selling power to neighbours  b) selling the dam  c) tourism
**Answer: a) selling power to neighbours**

**9.** The lesson's question is when —
a) taxes are due  b) school opens  c) one person's rights must yield to everyone's benefit
**Answer: c) one person's rights must yield to everyone's benefit**

**10.** Environmentalists in the debate speak for —
a) the city only  b) the environment and those who depend on it  c) the construction company
**Answer: b) the environment and those who depend on it**
