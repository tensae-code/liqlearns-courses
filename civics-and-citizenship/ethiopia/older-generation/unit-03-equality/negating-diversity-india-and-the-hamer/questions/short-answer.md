# Negating diversity — India's lesson and the Hamer — short answer

Answer in one to three sentences, from the lesson.

**1.** Why was federalism in India 'less of choice than of necessity'?
**Answer:** Eight major religious systems and fifteen major language groups made a single uniform system impossible.

**2.** What is Bhargava's conclusion about asymmetrical treatment?
**Answer:** That treating units differently can be the only way to realise equality — Ethiopia too must find its legitimate asymmetries.

**3.** Describe the Hamer bull-jumping rite.
**Answer:** A rite of passage into adulthood: a young man runs and leaps over a line of bulls.
