# Negating diversity — India's lesson and the Hamer — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Denying diversity is called —
a) good housekeeping  b) a shortcut to unity  c) playing with fire
**Answer: c) playing with fire**

**2.** India accepted language-based federal units by —
a) the late 1940s  b) 2009  c) 1923
**Answer: a) the late 1940s**

**3.** India has how many major language groups in the lesson?
a) Fifteen  b) Three  c) Eighty
**Answer: a) Fifteen**

**4.** Partition in India happened along —
a) river lines  b) trade routes  c) religious lines
**Answer: c) religious lines**

**5.** Bhargava's asymmetrical treatment means —
a) punishing minorities  b) abolishing states  c) not treating every unit identically, to realise equality
**Answer: c) not treating every unit identically, to realise equality**

**6.** The Hamer number about —
a) fifteen thousand  b) fifteen million  c) fifty thousand
**Answer: a) fifteen thousand**

**7.** The Hamer are —
a) city bankers  b) fishermen only  c) semi-nomadic cattle pastoralists
**Answer: c) semi-nomadic cattle pastoralists**

**8.** Bull-jumping is —
a) a court procedure  b) a rite of passage into adulthood  c) a tax ceremony
**Answer: b) a rite of passage into adulthood**

**9.** Hamer crafts named include —
a) glass-blowing  b) pottery, beekeeping and smithing  c) printing and coding
**Answer: b) pottery, beekeeping and smithing**

**10.** The Hamer live with others with —
a) no contact at all  b) respect, dignity and harmony  c) suspicion and walls
**Answer: b) respect, dignity and harmony**
