# Video script — Responsible behaviour against HIV/AIDS — orphans, women and the Dawn of Hope

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. Short script.

---

One lesson from the older course.

The epidemic's human ledger (UNESCO 2004, as the guide keys it): over 18 million AIDS orphans projected in sub-Saharan Africa by 2010; widows disinherited and made homeless; affected children leaving school — in Addis Ababa, over three-quarters of interviewed child domestic labourers were orphaned, 80% unable to leave their jobs, many working over 11 hours a day, 7 days a week. Women are first tested and first blamed, burdened as caregivers and driven to the street. The 2004 strategy for orphans and vulnerable children is the policy answer summarised in the lesson.

Remember it with its case. Now — say it back, in your own words.
