# Responsible behaviour against HIV/AIDS — orphans, women and the Dawn of Hope — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** AIDS orphans projected in sub-Saharan Africa by 2010: —
a) 1.8 million  b) 180,000  c) over 18 million
**Answer: c) over 18 million**

**2.** Widows in the ledger are —
a) untouched by the epidemic  b) disinherited and made homeless  c) given double property
**Answer: b) disinherited and made homeless**

**3.** Interviewed child domestic labourers orphaned: —
a) none  b) over three-quarters  c) one in ten
**Answer: b) over three-quarters**

**4.** 80% of those child labourers —
a) could not leave their jobs  b) owned their homes  c) attended university
**Answer: a) could not leave their jobs**

**5.** Many worked over —
a) 11 hours a day, 7 days a week  b) 4 hours a day  c) 2 days a week
**Answer: a) 11 hours a day, 7 days a week**

**6.** Women in the epidemic are —
a) first tested and first blamed  b) never affected  c) always protected
**Answer: a) first tested and first blamed**

**7.** The 2004 strategy concerned —
a) tax reform  b) orphans and vulnerable children  c) road building
**Answer: b) orphans and vulnerable children**

**8.** The Dawn of Hope was founded by —
a) eleven HIV-positive people  b) a ministry  c) foreign doctors
**Answer: a) eleven HIV-positive people**

**9.** Its membership grew to over —
a) 10,000  b) 100  c) 1,000
**Answer: a) 10,000**

**10.** The association demands —
a) separate cities  b) respect for the rights of people living with HIV  c) silence about HIV
**Answer: b) respect for the rights of people living with HIV**
