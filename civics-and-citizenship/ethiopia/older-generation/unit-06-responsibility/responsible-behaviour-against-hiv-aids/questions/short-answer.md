# Responsible behaviour against HIV/AIDS — orphans, women and the Dawn of Hope — short answer

Answer in one to three sentences, from the lesson.

**1.** How many AIDS orphans were projected in sub-Saharan Africa by 2010?
**Answer:** Over 18 million (UNESCO 2004, as keyed).

**2.** What did the Addis Ababa interviews with child domestic labourers find?
**Answer:** Over three-quarters were orphaned; 80% could not leave their jobs; many worked over 11 hours a day, 7 days a week.

**3.** Who founded the Dawn of Hope, and how large did it grow?
**Answer:** Eleven HIV-positive people, including Berehane Kelkay; it grew to over 10,000 members with branches across Ethiopia including Awassa.
