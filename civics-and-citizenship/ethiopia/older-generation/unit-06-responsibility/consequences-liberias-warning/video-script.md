# Video script — Consequences — Liberia's warning about promises and power

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. Full script — the design names a video-script need for this lesson.

---

This lesson is told as a story.

Sub lesson 1 — Tolbert: the fortune, the conference hall and the rice. President Tolbert of Liberia amassed a corrupt fortune and spent half the national budget on a conference hall, while his cousin's rice imports rose 50% and ordinary people queued. Troops fired on protesters; in 1980 Tolbert was murdered. Sub lesson 2 — Doe: the coup's promise, the rigged re-count and civil war. Samuel Doe's coup promised liberation and delivered a dictatorship as brutal as what it replaced — 'same taxi, different driver'. Doe amassed a $300 million fortune, and his 1985 re-election rested on a rigged re-count giving him 50.9%. Liberia descended into civil war.

Sub lesson 3 — The citizens' lesson: non-participation has consequences too. The case study's moral is addressed to citizens: promises unexamined and power unwatched are also choices, and their consequences also belong, in part, to those who chose not to participate and not to stand by principles. Responsibility for consequences is this unit's theme — and it does not stop at presidents.

That is the lesson. Tell it back — in your own words, with the names and dates in place.
