# Consequences — Liberia's warning about promises and power — short answer

Answer in one to three sentences, from the lesson.

**1.** What did Tolbert spend half the national budget on, and what rose 50%?
**Answer:** A conference hall; his cousin's rice imports.

**2.** What did Doe's 1985 re-count give him, and what did Liberians call his rule?
**Answer:** 50.9%, by a rigged re-count; 'same taxi, different driver'.

**3.** What is the citizens' lesson of the Liberia story?
**Answer:** Failing to participate and stand by principles has consequences too — responsibility does not stop at presidents.
