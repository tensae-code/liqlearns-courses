# Consequences — Liberia's warning about promises and power — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tolbert amassed —
a) a library  b) public trust  c) a corrupt fortune
**Answer: c) a corrupt fortune**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Half the national budget went to —
a) a conference hall  b) schools  c) rice subsidies
**Answer: a) a conference hall**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Whose rice imports rose 50%?
a) Tolbert's cousin's  b) The farmers'  c) Nobody's
**Answer: a) Tolbert's cousin's**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Tolbert was murdered in —
a) 1980  b) 1974  c) 1991
**Answer: a) 1980**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Doe came to power by —
a) a coup promising liberation  b) an honest election  c) inheritance
**Answer: a) a coup promising liberation**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Doe's fortune reached —
a) nothing  b) $300 million  c) $3 million
**Answer: b) $300 million**

**7. (Sub lesson 2)** Doe's 1985 re-count gave him —
a) 90%  b) 30%  c) 50.9%
**Answer: c) 50.9%**

**8. (Sub lesson 2)** Liberians called Doe's rule —
a) the people's dawn  b) same taxi, different driver  c) a golden age
**Answer: b) same taxi, different driver**

**9. (Sub lesson 2)** Liberia descended into —
a) civil war  b) prosperity  c) a federation
**Answer: a) civil war**

**10. (Sub lesson 3)** The citizens' lesson is that —
a) non-participation has consequences too  b) citizens bear no responsibility ever  c) promises should never be questioned
**Answer: a) non-participation has consequences too**

**11. (Sub lesson 3)** Troops firing on protesters under Tolbert shows —
a) power answering complaint with force  b) a parade  c) an accident of weather
**Answer: a) power answering complaint with force**

**12. (Sub lesson 3)** Responsibility for consequences in this unit applies to —
a) presidents only  b) foreigners only  c) leaders and citizens alike
**Answer: c) leaders and citizens alike**
