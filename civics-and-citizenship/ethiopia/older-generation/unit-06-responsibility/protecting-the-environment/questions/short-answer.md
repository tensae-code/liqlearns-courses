# Protecting the environment — Maathai, the Ethiopian wolf and Susenios's castles — short answer

Answer in one to three sentences, from the lesson.

**1.** How many Ethiopian wolves survive, and what do they eat?
**Answer:** About 500, in the highlands; they eat the giant mole rat, itself endemic.

**2.** What estimate of rainforest loss is quoted with Maathai's case?
**Answer:** Dumont's estimate of 74,000 acres lost daily.

**3.** Name Susenios's two palaces and their builders' origins.
**Answer:** Gorgora, built under the Spanish Jesuit Pero Paes's direction; Danqaz, by an Indian builder.
