# Protecting the environment — Maathai, the Ethiopian wolf and Susenios's castles — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** De Rivero warns against viewing nature as —
a) merely another raw material to be consumed  b) a home  c) a trust
**Answer: a) merely another raw material to be consumed**

**2.** Wangari Maathai was —
a) an environmental activist  b) a general  c) a banker
**Answer: a) an environmental activist**

**3.** Rainforest lost daily, by Dumont's estimate: —
a) 74,000 acres  b) 740 acres  c) 7 acres
**Answer: a) 74,000 acres**

**4.** The Ethiopian wolf is also called —
a) the desert lion  b) the highland tiger  c) the Simien Fox or Red Fox
**Answer: c) the Simien Fox or Red Fox**

**5.** About how many Ethiopian wolves survive?
a) 500  b) 50,000  c) 5 million
**Answer: a) 500**

**6.** The wolf eats —
a) cattle mainly  b) fish  c) the giant mole rat
**Answer: c) the giant mole rat**

**7.** Gorgora palace was built under the direction of —
a) a Portuguese king  b) Pero Paes, a Spanish Jesuit  c) an Indian builder
**Answer: b) Pero Paes, a Spanish Jesuit**

**8.** Danqaz palace was built by —
a) Pero Paes  b) Susenios alone  c) an Indian builder
**Answer: c) an Indian builder**

**9.** Cultural heritage counts in this lesson as —
a) separate from all duty  b) a tourist matter only  c) part of the environment to be saved
**Answer: c) part of the environment to be saved**

**10.** Learners close the lesson by —
a) mapping their own locality's heritage and habitats  b) writing to Maathai  c) counting wolves
**Answer: a) mapping their own locality's heritage and habitats**
