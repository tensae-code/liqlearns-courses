One lesson from the older course.

Environmental duty is argued from de Rivero: when nature is viewed 'merely as another raw material to be consumed', farmland is urbanised, fish stocks depleted, greenhouse gases rise, toxic waste is buried, and goods are wrapped in deforestation. Against that habit stands Wangari Maathai, the environmental activist — with Dumont's estimate of 74,000 acres of rainforest lost daily as the scale of what she fought.

Remember it with its case. Now — say it back, in your own words.
