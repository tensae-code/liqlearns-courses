# Moral and legal obligations — the Shango, and caring for the elderly — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Legal obligations are enforced by —
a) conscience only  b) neighbours' gossip  c) the state, through Constitution and laws
**Answer: c) the state, through Constitution and laws**

**2.** Moral obligations are —
a) enforced by prison  b) what is felt to be right  c) written in tax law
**Answer: b) what is felt to be right**

**3.** The Shango is —
a) a tax office  b) a school club  c) a Gurage customary court
**Answer: c) a Gurage customary court**

**4.** The Shango carries which kind of sanction?
a) Financial only  b) Moral and ritual  c) Military
**Answer: b) Moral and ritual**

**5.** Care for aged parents is among —
a) the Shango's objectives  b) forbidden customs  c) tax duties
**Answer: a) the Shango's objectives**

**6.** The Shango works to suppress —
a) all customs  b) arbitration  c) harmful customs
**Answer: c) harmful customs**

**7.** MacKinnon argues ethics —
a) needs state enforcement  b) is impossible  c) needs no religious grounding
**Answer: c) needs no religious grounding**

**8.** Reason and experience can —
a) replace all courts  b) measure rainfall  c) judge right and wrong
**Answer: c) judge right and wrong**

**9.** Abebe is —
a) a tax collector  b) an artist who cares for the elderly  c) a judge
**Answer: b) an artist who cares for the elderly**

**10.** NGOs in Abebe's case —
a) run the Shango  b) collect tribute  c) house, feed and employ the elderly
**Answer: c) house, feed and employ the elderly**
