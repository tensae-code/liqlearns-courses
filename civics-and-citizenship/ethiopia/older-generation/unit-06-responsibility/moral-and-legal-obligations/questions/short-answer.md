# Moral and legal obligations — the Shango, and caring for the elderly — short answer

Answer in one to three sentences, from the lesson.

**1.** Name the Shango's objectives.
**Answer:** To preserve customary law, suppress harmful customs, honour arbitration and the equb, and care for aged parents.

**2.** What is MacKinnon's point?
**Answer:** Ethics needs no religious grounding — reason and experience can judge right and wrong.

**3.** How does Abebe the artist embody moral obligation?
**Answer:** He cares for the elderly — his own, and through NGOs that house, feed and employ them — though no law forced him.
