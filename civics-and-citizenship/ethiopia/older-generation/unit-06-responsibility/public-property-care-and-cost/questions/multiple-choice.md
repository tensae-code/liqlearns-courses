# Public property — care, cost and the school itself — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Desks and books are bought with —
a) foreign gifts only  b) parents' taxes  c) students' fines
**Answer: b) parents' taxes**

**2.** Graffiti and dirty fingers —
a) are required  b) shorten the life of public property  c) decorate it lawfully
**Answer: b) shorten the life of public property**

**3.** A torn page may be an accident, but —
a) accidents can be avoided  b) accidents are crimes  c) pages do not matter
**Answer: a) accidents can be avoided**

**4.** Cared-for resources should —
a) be locked away unused  b) last for many years  c) be replaced yearly
**Answer: b) last for many years**

**5.** The school is the case because its users —
a) built it themselves  b) own nothing directly yet use everything  c) pay per desk
**Answer: b) own nothing directly yet use everything**

**6.** What belongs to everyone is looked after by no one — unless —
a) the police watch it  b) it is sold  c) citizens choose to care
**Answer: c) citizens choose to care**

**7.** The lesson's product is —
a) a new desk design  b) a plan to protect school property  c) an essay on taxes
**Answer: b) a plan to protect school property**

**8.** Learners must also —
a) report to the ministry  b) persuade other students to respect property  c) fine other students
**Answer: b) persuade other students to respect property**

**9.** Carelessness is framed as —
a) a civic failing  b) a talent  c) a private matter only
**Answer: a) a civic failing**

**10.** Public property means property —
a) held for everyone's use, funded by all  b) owned by the principal  c) found on the street
**Answer: a) held for everyone's use, funded by all**
