# Public property — care, cost and the school itself — short answer

Answer in one to three sentences, from the lesson.

**1.** Why do desks and books wear out early, in the lesson's account?
**Answer:** Graffiti and dirty fingers — carelessness — shorten their life, though they were bought with parents' taxes.

**2.** What must learners produce in this lesson?
**Answer:** A plan to protect school property, and a way to persuade other students to respect it.

**3.** Why is the school the perfect case of public property?
**Answer:** Its users own nothing directly and pay for nothing at the desk — so care must be deliberate.
