# Obligations in society — participation, negotiation, compromise — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first stage of settling a dispute is to —
a) describe the dispute clearly  b) punish one side  c) call the police at once
**Answer: a) describe the dispute clearly**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Whose land-dispute case gives the stages?
a) A Senegalese case, from Meyer-Bisch  b) A Russian case  c) A US case
**Answer: a) A Senegalese case, from Meyer-Bisch**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Calming and defusing belongs to —
a) the signing stage  b) no stage  c) the second stage, with listening
**Answer: c) the second stage, with listening**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** 'Each party listens and understands' means —
a) writing a speech  b) hearing the other's account whole  c) interrupting politely
**Answer: b) hearing the other's account whole**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The basic cause of a dispute is found in —
a) after the settlement  b) the third stage  c) the first stage
**Answer: b) the third stage**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Mediators are brought in when —
a) both sides agree at once  b) direct talks fail  c) the dispute begins
**Answer: b) direct talks fail**

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** Ethiopian elders have settled disputes for —
a) since 2009 only  b) centuries  c) one decade
**Answer: b) centuries**

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** A solution serving both sides is needed because —
a) elders demand gifts  b) a hated settlement is a pause, not a peace  c) paper requires it
**Answer: b) a hated settlement is a pause, not a peace**

**9. (Sub lesson 4)** The negotiator separates —
a) the problem from the personalities  b) the parties forever  c) truth from evidence
**Answer: a) the problem from the personalities**

**10. (Sub lesson 4)** Judging by objective criteria means —
a) who speaks loudest  b) standards both sides can check, not moods  c) the elder's feelings
**Answer: b) standards both sides can check, not moods**

**11. (Sub lesson 4)** Sidama elders are held to —
a) silence  b) probity, integrity, honesty and truthfulness  c) wealth tests
**Answer: b) probity, integrity, honesty and truthfulness**

**12. (Sub lesson 4)** Compromise in this lesson is —
a) only for traders  b) a core citizen obligation  c) a weakness
**Answer: b) a core citizen obligation**

**13. (Sub lesson 1)** Before feelings rename a dispute, the parties must first —
a) say what it actually is — land, boundary, inheritance  b) choose a winner  c) stop speaking
**Answer: a) say what it actually is — land, boundary, inheritance**

**14. (Sub lesson 2)** Listening in the second stage means hearing the other side's account —
a) whole, before answering  b) only if it is short  c) through a messenger only
**Answer: a) whole, before answering**
