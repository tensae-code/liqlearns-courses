# Obligations in society — participation, negotiation, compromise — short answer

Answer in one to three sentences, from the lesson.

**1.** List the negotiator's four craft rules.
**Answer:** Separate the problem from the personalities; avoid premature judgement; judge by objective criteria; communicate.

**2.** To what standards are Sidama elders held?
**Answer:** Probity, integrity, honesty and truthfulness.

**3.** When should mediators be brought in?
**Answer:** When direct talks fail — at the stage of finding the basic cause.
