One lesson from the older course.

Political participation, negotiation and compromise are core obligations of a citizen, not hobbies. The negotiator's craft is spelled out plainly: separate the problem from the personalities; avoid premature judgement; judge by objective criteria; communicate. Ethiopia has practised this for centuries — elders settling disputes, with Sidama elders held to standards of probity, integrity, honesty and truthfulness. The craft is old; the lesson makes it deliberate.

Remember it with its case. Now — say it back, in your own words.
