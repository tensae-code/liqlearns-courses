# Citizens' obligations — and the demonstration as right and duty — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Obligations are described as —
a) more important than the constitution  b) only for soldiers  c) the other face of rights
**Answer: c) the other face of rights**

**2.** Reardon's list includes —
a) forcing others to work unpaid  b) silencing other opinions  c) defending the rights of others
**Answer: c) defending the rights of others**

**3.** Forced unpaid work is —
a) forbidden — no one may be forced into it  b) required of all citizens  c) allowed for students only
**Answer: a) forbidden — no one may be forced into it**

**4.** The persecuted may claim —
a) a minister's job  b) tax exemption forever  c) asylum
**Answer: c) asylum**

**5.** US anti-Vietnam-war protests —
a) banned all elections  b) helped change government policy  c) were never held
**Answer: b) helped change government policy**

**6.** Ethiopia's 1974 student protests —
a) helped bring down the monarchy  b) built a railway  c) changed the calendar
**Answer: a) helped bring down the monarchy**

**7.** Tiananmen Square 1989 shows —
a) protest crushed where it is not protected  b) a model election  c) a trade fair
**Answer: a) protest crushed where it is not protected**

**8.** Voting is presented as —
a) a punishment  b) a citizen's responsibility  c) only an official's job
**Answer: b) a citizen's responsibility**

**9.** A lawful demonstration needs —
a) respect for the law, like the permit in the leader's hand  b) violence to be heard  c) foreign permission
**Answer: a) respect for the law, like the permit in the leader's hand**

**10.** Respecting opinions you disagree with is —
a) a sign of weakness  b) forbidden  c) part of a citizen's obligations
**Answer: c) part of a citizen's obligations**
