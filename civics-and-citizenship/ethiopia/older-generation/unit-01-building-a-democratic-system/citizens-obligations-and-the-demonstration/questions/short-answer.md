# Citizens' obligations — and the demonstration as right and duty — short answer

Answer in one to three sentences, from the lesson.

**1.** Give three obligations from Reardon's list as matched to the Ethiopian Constitution.
**Answer:** Any three of: treat others with respect and defend their rights; no forced unpaid work; asylum for the persecuted; respect opinions and religious freedom; vote; be educated.

**2.** How did protest change things in the US and Ethiopia examples?
**Answer:** Anti-Vietnam-war protests helped change US policy; Ethiopia's 1974 student protests helped bring down the monarchy.

**3.** What do Tiananmen Square 1989 and the Myanmar monks' protest show?
**Answer:** That where demonstration is not protected as a right, protest is repressed.
