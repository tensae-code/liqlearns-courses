# Features of democracy — transparency and accountability — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Transparency means —
a) only ministers may see reports  b) decisions and money are visible to the public  c) officials hide their budgets
**Answer: b) decisions and money are visible to the public**

**2.** Accountability means —
a) no questions after elections  b) officials must explain and answer for what they do  c) citizens answer to officials
**Answer: b) officials must explain and answer for what they do**

**3.** The 'transparent officials' case used —
a) posters and telephone hotlines  b) secret police  c) bribery
**Answer: a) posters and telephone hotlines**

**4.** Botswana's traditional public meetings carry which risk?
a) No risk at all  b) A gender risk — who gets heard  c) Too many elections
**Answer: b) A gender risk — who gets heard**

**5.** Kenya's rigged election named in the readings was in —
a) 1988  b) 1948  c) 2010
**Answer: a) 1988**

**6.** Uganda's Movement system —
a) set multi-party competition aside  b) was a football league  c) abolished schools
**Answer: a) set multi-party competition aside**

**7.** The lesson's school activity asks learners to —
a) elect a president  b) audit how transparent their own school is  c) paint the notice board
**Answer: b) audit how transparent their own school is**

**8.** An organisation earns public respect when —
a) it arrests critics  b) its delivery satisfies its users and it works openly  c) it hides its accounts
**Answer: b) its delivery satisfies its users and it works openly**

**9.** Corruption and assassinations in the Kenya reading show —
a) what happens when citizens cannot see or question power  b) a healthy democracy  c) a weather problem
**Answer: a) what happens when citizens cannot see or question power**

**10.** Between elections, democracy stays real through —
a) posters alone  b) transparency and accountability  c) silence and obedience
**Answer: b) transparency and accountability**
