# Features of democracy — transparency and accountability — short answer

Answer in one to three sentences, from the lesson.

**1.** Define transparency and accountability in one line each.
**Answer:** Transparency: decisions and money are visible to the public. Accountability: officials must explain and answer for their actions.

**2.** How did the 'transparent officials' organisation expose corruption?
**Answer:** Through posters and telephone hotlines — and it earned respect by delivering what satisfied its users.

**3.** What went wrong in Kenya under Kenyatta and Moi, as the readings tell it?
**Answer:** Corruption, assassinations and the rigged 1988 election.
