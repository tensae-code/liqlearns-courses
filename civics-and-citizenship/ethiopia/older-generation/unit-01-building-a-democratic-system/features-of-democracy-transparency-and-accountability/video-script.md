# Video script — Features of democracy — transparency and accountability

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. Short script.

---

One lesson from the older course.

Two working features make a democracy real between elections: **transparency** — decisions and money are visible to the public — and **accountability** — officials must explain and answer for what they did. The 'transparent officials' case shows an organisation that exposed corrupt officials through posters and telephone hotlines, and earned respect because its delivery satisfied its users. The lesson's activity turns the lens home: audit your own school — what is posted, what is hidden, who answers questions?
