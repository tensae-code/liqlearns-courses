One lesson from the older course.

Rights have deep roots. Religions and philosophies taught the worth of the human person long before modern states existed. In 1948, after the Second World War, fourteen nations drafted the Universal Declaration of Human Rights as 'a common standard of achievement for all peoples and all nations'. Ethiopia was among the first African members of the United Nations to sign — rights are not a foreign idea Ethiopia borrowed late; Ethiopians helped set the standard early.

Remember it with its case. Now — say it back, in your own words.
