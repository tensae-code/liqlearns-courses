# Human and democratic rights — from the UDHR to Ethiopia's constitution — short answer

Answer in one to three sentences, from the lesson.

**1.** When was the Universal Declaration of Human Rights drafted, and by how many nations?
**Answer:** In 1948, by fourteen nations, as a common standard for all peoples and nations.

**2.** Name two concrete protections for prisoners named in this lesson.
**Answer:** The right to see a lawyer, and to speak with a religious counsellor unheard by warders; police and warders are trained in these rights.

**3.** What unlisted right does the lesson's closing debate argue about?
**Answer:** Whether there is a right to food.
