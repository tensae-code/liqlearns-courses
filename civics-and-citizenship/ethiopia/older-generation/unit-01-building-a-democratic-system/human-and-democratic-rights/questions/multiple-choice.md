# Human and democratic rights — from the UDHR to Ethiopia's constitution — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The Universal Declaration of Human Rights was drafted in —
a) 1991  b) 1948  c) 1900
**Answer: b) 1948**

**2.** How many nations drafted the Declaration?
a) Fourteen  b) Three  c) Fifty
**Answer: a) Fourteen**

**3.** Ethiopia's place among early signatories: it was —
a) the last country to sign  b) never a signatory  c) among the first African UN members to sign
**Answer: c) among the first African UN members to sign**

**4.** Ethiopia acceded to the Convention against Torture in —
a) 1948  b) 2009  c) 1994
**Answer: c) 1994**

**5.** A prisoner in Ethiopia has the right to —
a) choose the warden  b) see a lawyer and speak with a religious counsellor unheard by warders  c) leave prison on request
**Answer: b) see a lawyer and speak with a religious counsellor unheard by warders**

**6.** Which body gathers evidence on human rights in Ethiopia?
a) The Football Federation  b) The Chamber of Commerce  c) The Ethiopian Human Rights Commission
**Answer: c) The Ethiopian Human Rights Commission**

**7.** Religions and philosophies matter in this lesson because —
a) they wrote the Declaration alone  b) they replaced the constitution  c) rights were rooted in them long before 1948
**Answer: c) rights were rooted in them long before 1948**

**8.** The Declaration calls itself —
a) a tax treaty  b) a trade agreement  c) a common standard of achievement for all peoples and all nations
**Answer: c) a common standard of achievement for all peoples and all nations**

**9.** Police and prison-warden training is part of —
a) prison building  b) making the torture ban real in practice  c) military parades
**Answer: b) making the torture ban real in practice**

**10.** The lesson's closing debate asks whether there is a right to —
a) drive a car  b) own a television  c) food
**Answer: c) food**
