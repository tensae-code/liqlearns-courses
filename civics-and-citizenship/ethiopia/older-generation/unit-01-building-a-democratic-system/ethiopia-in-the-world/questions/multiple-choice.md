# Ethiopia in the world — League of Nations, OAU to AU, and its foreign policy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Ethiopia joined the League of Nations in —
a) 1923  b) 1963  c) 2000
**Answer: a) 1923**

**2.** The League failed Ethiopia when —
a) Italy invaded and the League did not act  b) the AU was founded  c) the ECA opened
**Answer: a) Italy invaded and the League did not act**

**3.** The OAU charter was engineered in —
a) 1963  b) 1923  c) 1996
**Answer: a) 1963**

**4.** The African Union replaced the OAU in —
a) 2000  b) 1958  c) 1974
**Answer: a) 2000**

**5.** AU Article 4 allows intervention for —
a) football violence  b) war crimes, genocide and crimes against humanity  c) trade disputes
**Answer: b) war crimes, genocide and crimes against humanity**

**6.** The ECA has been in Addis Ababa since —
a) 1958  b) 1996  c) 2009
**Answer: a) 1958**

**7.** IGAD was founded in —
a) 1923  b) 1963  c) 1996
**Answer: c) 1996**

**8.** Ethiopia's foreign policy embarking points include —
a) rejecting globalisation entirely  b) national honour  c) isolation from the world
**Answer: b) national honour**

**9.** Addis Ababa won the seat of —
a) the United Nations  b) the World Bank  c) the OAU, then the AU
**Answer: c) the OAU, then the AU**

**10.** The AU's intervention right answers the lesson of —
a) the Omo flood  b) the League's failure in 1936  c) the 1988 election
**Answer: b) the League's failure in 1936**
