# Ethiopia in the world — League of Nations, OAU to AU, and its foreign policy — short answer

Answer in one to three sentences, from the lesson.

**1.** When did Ethiopia join the League of Nations, and what did the League fail to do?
**Answer:** In 1923; it failed to act when Italy invaded, despite its own covenant.

**2.** What new right did the AU's Article 4 create in 2000?
**Answer:** A right to intervene in a member state for war crimes, genocide and crimes against humanity.

**3.** State Ethiopia's three embarking points of foreign policy.
**Answer:** Development and building a democratic system; national honour; globalisation accepted as the century's reality.
