# Principles of the FDRE Constitution — sovereignty, supremacy, rights, secular state, accountability — short answer

Answer in one to three sentences, from the lesson.

**1.** Name the five principles of the FDRE Constitution.
**Answer:** Sovereignty of the people; supremacy of the Constitution; respect for human and democratic rights; separation of state and religion; accountability of government.

**2.** What does 'supremacy of the Constitution' mean in practice?
**Answer:** Every other law and official practice must agree with the Constitution; anything against it is null and void.

**3.** How did the imperial constitutions treat the Church, and how does the FDRE Constitution differ?
**Answer:** The imperial constitutions made the Church part of government; the FDRE Constitution separates state and religion — the state is secular.
