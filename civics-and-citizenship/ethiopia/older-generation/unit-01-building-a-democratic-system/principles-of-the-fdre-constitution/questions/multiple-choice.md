# Principles of the FDRE Constitution — sovereignty, supremacy, rights, secular state, accountability — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Where does power come from under the FDRE Constitution?
a) The army  b) The richest families  c) The people — sovereignty belongs to the citizens
**Answer: c) The people — sovereignty belongs to the citizens**

**2.** What happens to a law that contradicts the Constitution?
a) It applies only in cities  b) It is null and void  c) It waits ten years
**Answer: b) It is null and void**

**3.** Which principle says the state has no official religion?
a) Accountability of government  b) Separation of state and religion  c) Supremacy of the Constitution
**Answer: b) Separation of state and religion**

**4.** Human and democratic rights are described as —
a) an instrument of peace and a key to nation building  b) a gift from foreign countries  c) rules only for officials
**Answer: a) an instrument of peace and a key to nation building**

**5.** Accountability of government means —
a) ministers can never be questioned  b) those who govern must answer for what they do  c) citizens must answer to officials
**Answer: b) those who govern must answer for what they do**

**6.** Susenyos's story is told in this lesson because —
a) he built the first school  b) he wrote the Constitution  c) his attempt to change the country's religion shows why state and religion are separated
**Answer: c) his attempt to change the country's religion shows why state and religion are separated**

**7.** Under the imperial constitutions, the Church was —
a) separated from the state  b) part of government  c) banned completely
**Answer: b) part of government**

**8.** Sovereignty of the people means governments hold power —
a) by inheritance forever  b) by force of arms  c) only because the citizens give it
**Answer: c) only because the citizens give it**

**9.** The Constitution is supreme — it stands above —
a) only regional laws  b) only tax laws  c) every other law
**Answer: c) every other law**

**10.** Which pair of buildings stands apart but equal in the lesson's picture?
a) A bank and a shop  b) A school and a prison  c) A church and a state building
**Answer: c) A church and a state building**
