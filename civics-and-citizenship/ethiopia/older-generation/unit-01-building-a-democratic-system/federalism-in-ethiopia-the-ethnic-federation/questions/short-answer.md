# Federalism in Ethiopia — the ethnic federation and how it was argued for — short answer

Answer in one to three sentences, from the lesson.

**1.** How did Turton judge Ethiopia's post-1991 transformation?
**Answer:** As 'radical and pioneering' — self-determination for federated units, ethnicity as the organising principle taken further than anywhere else in Africa.

**2.** What does the Omo Basin flood case show?
**Answer:** Federal and regional governments cooperating in a crisis — federalism working in practice.

**3.** How many House of Federation seats do the Hamer, Bana and Kwego each hold?
**Answer:** One seat each, though they number only thousands.
