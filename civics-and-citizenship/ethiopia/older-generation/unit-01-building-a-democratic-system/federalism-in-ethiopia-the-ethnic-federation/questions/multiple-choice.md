# Federalism in Ethiopia — the ethnic federation and how it was argued for — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Ethiopia's post-1991 system is —
a) a unitary empire  b) a military council  c) ethnic federalism
**Answer: c) ethnic federalism**

**2.** Turton called the transformation —
a) a foreign copy  b) radical and pioneering  c) a small reform
**Answer: b) radical and pioneering**

**3.** The organising principle of the federation is —
a) wealth  b) age  c) ethnicity — nations, nationalities and peoples
**Answer: c) ethnicity — nations, nationalities and peoples**

**4.** Merera Gudina's history of the national question runs from —
a) Tewodros II through the 1960s struggle and the Derg to 1991  b) Menelik to 2020 only  c) the Axumite kings only
**Answer: a) Tewodros II through the 1960s struggle and the Derg to 1991**

**5.** The Omo Basin flood case shows —
a) a dam debate  b) an election dispute  c) federal–regional cooperation in crisis
**Answer: c) federal–regional cooperation in crisis**

**6.** Articles 51 and 52 of the Constitution concern —
a) federal and regional powers  b) school holidays  c) trade taxes only
**Answer: a) federal and regional powers**

**7.** The Hamer people number about —
a) 10–20 thousand  b) 10 million  c) 200 million
**Answer: a) 10–20 thousand**

**8.** Each tiny people in the activity holds how many House of Federation seats?
a) Ten  b) None  c) One
**Answer: c) One**

**9.** Before 1991 Ethiopia was —
a) already a federation  b) ruled by regions  c) a centralised state
**Answer: c) a centralised state**

**10.** Self-determination in this lesson means —
a) every village becoming a country  b) no central government at all  c) federated units governing their own affairs within the federation
**Answer: c) federated units governing their own affairs within the federation**
