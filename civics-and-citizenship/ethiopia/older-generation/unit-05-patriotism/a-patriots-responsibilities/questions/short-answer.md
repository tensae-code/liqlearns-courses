# A patriot's responsibilities — the nurse, the whistle-blower and the drunk driver — short answer

Answer in one to three sentences, from the lesson.

**1.** Why must Chaltu refuse the money?
**Answer:** Queue-jumping for payment is unethical; if every nurse did it, the hospital would collapse into bribery and the sickest would wait behind the richest.

**2.** What is the guide's answer for Aster?
**Answer:** The aggrieved should stand together; whistle-blowing protects everyone.

**3.** Why must the officer arrest his friend?
**Answer:** A car in drunk hands is a lethal weapon; feelings must not displace duty.
