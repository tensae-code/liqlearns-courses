# A patriot's responsibilities — the nurse, the whistle-blower and the drunk driver — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Chaltu is offered money to —
a) buy medicine  b) jump a patient queue  c) work overtime
**Answer: b) jump a patient queue**

**2.** If every nurse took queue money, the hospital would —
a) run faster  b) hire more nurses  c) collapse into bribery
**Answer: c) collapse into bribery**

**3.** Aster's dilemma is —
a) exposing her boss versus her own exposure  b) a pay rise  c) a transfer request
**Answer: a) exposing her boss versus her own exposure**

**4.** The guide's answer for Aster: the aggrieved should —
a) resign quietly  b) stand together  c) stay silent
**Answer: b) stand together**

**5.** Whistle-blowing protects —
a) only the boss  b) nobody  c) everyone, including future victims
**Answer: c) everyone, including future victims**

**6.** The drunk driver turns out to be the officer's —
a) own friend  b) brother-in-law only  c) chief
**Answer: a) own friend**

**7.** The officer's duty is to —
a) drive him home  b) arrest him  c) let him go
**Answer: b) arrest him**

**8.** A car in drunk hands is called —
a) a lethal weapon  b) a private matter  c) a small mistake
**Answer: a) a lethal weapon**

**9.** Patriotism in this lesson is —
a) only border guarding  b) only flag ceremonies  c) public good, constitutional respect, ethical anti-corruption in ordinary jobs
**Answer: c) public good, constitutional respect, ethical anti-corruption in ordinary jobs**

**10.** The three cases share this: the patriotic act —
a) is done by officials only  b) costs something  c) is always rewarded at once
**Answer: b) costs something**
