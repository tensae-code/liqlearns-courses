# Voluntarism — from Iddir roots to the 2009 Charities Proclamation — short answer

Answer in one to three sentences, from the lesson.

**1.** Name Proclamation 621/2009's four classes of organisation.
**Answer:** Ethiopian Charities/Societies; Ethiopian Residents Charities/Societies (over 10% foreign funds); Foreign Charities; Mass-Based Societies.

**2.** Name two diagnosed weaknesses of Ethiopian voluntarism.
**Answer:** Any two of: minimal giving by citizens, firms and public bodies; the brain drain since the late 1970s; civil society less developed and fewer than in other African countries.

**3.** When was Ethiopia's first Cardiac Center inaugurated, and by whom?
**Answer:** 12 February 2009, by Dr. Belay.
