# Voluntarism — from Iddir roots to the 2009 Charities Proclamation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Voluntary action's Ethiopian roots are —
a) started by foreign NGOs  b) extended family, the Church, indigenous organisations  c) imported in 2009
**Answer: b) extended family, the Church, indigenous organisations**

**2.** Most NGOs arrived after —
a) 1970  b) 1923  c) 2009
**Answer: a) 1970**

**3.** Food aid followed the famines of —
a) 1999 and 2000  b) 1973/4 and 1984/5  c) 1960 and 1961
**Answer: b) 1973/4 and 1984/5**

**4.** A brain drain since the late 1970s —
a) ended famines  b) depleted the voluntary sector  c) built the sector
**Answer: b) depleted the voluntary sector**

**5.** Proclamation No. 621/2009 has —
a) two classes  b) no classes  c) four classes of charities and societies
**Answer: c) four classes of charities and societies**

**6.** Ethiopian Residents Charities are those with —
a) over 10% foreign funds  b) no funds  c) only government funds
**Answer: a) over 10% foreign funds**

**7.** Tolosa's initiative included help from —
a) the Diaspora  b) the League of Nations  c) a foreign army
**Answer: a) the Diaspora**

**8.** Ethiopia's first Cardiac Center opened on —
a) 12 February 2009  b) 12 February 1999  c) 12 February 2019
**Answer: a) 12 February 2009**

**9.** Dr. Belay recalls being a Grade 9 student —
a) already a surgeon  b) without shoes  c) in London
**Answer: b) without shoes**

**10.** Civil society in Ethiopia is described as —
a) the strongest in Africa  b) fully state-run  c) less developed, less active and much fewer than in other African countries
**Answer: c) less developed, less active and much fewer than in other African countries**
