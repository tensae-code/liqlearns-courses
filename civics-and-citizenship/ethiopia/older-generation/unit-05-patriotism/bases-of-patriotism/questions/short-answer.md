# Bases of patriotism — true history, the flag, tolerance — short answer

Answer in one to three sentences, from the lesson.

**1.** What is the discipline of objective history, as the lesson teaches it?
**Answer:** Check references and use at least two sources — because sources lie in both directions.

**2.** What happened in Sarajevo on 18 April 1994, in Huntington's scene?
**Answer:** Two thousand people waved Saudi and Turkish flags to tell the world who their real friends were.

**3.** What caveat does Luper add to Locke's tolerance?
**Answer:** Locke's own tolerance excluded atheists and Catholics.
