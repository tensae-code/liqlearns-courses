# Bases of patriotism — true history, the flag, tolerance — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The three bases of patriotism are —
a) army, taxes, anthems  b) pride, silence, obedience  c) objective history, the flag, tolerance
**Answer: c) objective history, the flag, tolerance**

**2.** A Haile Selassie-era schoolbook called him —
a) the best of kings — reverent myth-making  b) a minor official  c) a foreigner
**Answer: a) the best of kings — reverent myth-making**

**3.** Objective history requires —
a) at least two sources, references checked  b) one official book  c) no questions
**Answer: a) at least two sources, references checked**

**4.** The Cuban account praising Mengistu was written during —
a) the 2009 election  b) the Red Terror  c) the Axumite age
**Answer: b) the Red Terror**

**5.** Sarajevo's crowd waved —
a) Ethiopian flags  b) UN flags only  c) Saudi and Turkish flags
**Answer: c) Saudi and Turkish flags**

**6.** Flags are waved in pride and —
a) burned in anger — they carry identity  b) never touched  c) only at funerals
**Answer: a) burned in anger — they carry identity**

**7.** Locke's Letter Concerning Toleration is dated —
a) 1989  b) 1689  c) 1789
**Answer: b) 1689**

**8.** Locke argued belief —
a) is the state's property  b) cannot be coerced  c) must be forced
**Answer: b) cannot be coerced**

**9.** For Locke, instability is bred by —
a) free speech  b) persecution, not diversity  c) tolerance
**Answer: b) persecution, not diversity**

**10.** Luper's caveat: Locke's tolerance excluded —
a) farmers  b) atheists and Catholics  c) soldiers
**Answer: b) atheists and Catholics**
