# Development — measuring it, and Ethiopia's potential — short answer

Answer in one to three sentences, from the lesson.

**1.** How fast was Ethiopia's under-five mortality falling in 2004?
**Answer:** About 1% a year — while child mortality globally was 'seriously off-track'.

**2.** How many children were out of primary school, and where were most?
**Answer:** 75 million, 70% of them in sub-Saharan Africa.

**3.** Name Ethiopia's three great assets and three drags named in the lesson.
**Answer:** Assets: agricultural potential, livestock, the rivers ('water tower of Africa'). Drags: population, an undereducated workforce, governance.
