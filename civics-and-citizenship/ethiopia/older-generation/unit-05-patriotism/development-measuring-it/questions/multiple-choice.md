# Development — measuring it, and Ethiopia's potential — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Ethiopia's under-five mortality in 2004 was falling about —
a) not at all  b) 1% a year  c) 10% a year
**Answer: b) 1% a year**

**2.** Gender parity in education was set to be missed by how many countries in 2015?
a) 150  b) 54  c) 5
**Answer: b) 54**

**3.** Children out of primary school numbered —
a) 750 million  b) 75 million  c) 7.5 million
**Answer: b) 75 million**

**4.** 70% of out-of-school children were in —
a) sub-Saharan Africa  b) Europe  c) South America
**Answer: a) sub-Saharan Africa**

**5.** Under-fives dying daily of largely preventable causes: —
a) 2,900  b) 290  c) 29,000
**Answer: c) 29,000**

**6.** Ethiopia is called —
a) the desert of Africa  b) the island of Africa  c) the water tower of Africa
**Answer: c) the water tower of Africa**

**7.** The Abbay flows toward —
a) the Red Sea directly  b) Lake Turkana only  c) the Nile
**Answer: c) the Nile**

**8.** The Nile Basin framework was hoped for signature in 2009 by all but —
a) all riparian states  b) Egypt and Sudan  c) Kenya and Uganda
**Answer: b) Egypt and Sudan**

**9.** A reason poverty persists is —
a) an undereducated workforce  b) too many rivers  c) too few neighbours
**Answer: a) an undereducated workforce**

**10.** Development in this lesson is —
a) measured with indicators, then audited against potential  b) a feeling  c) only about roads
**Answer: a) measured with indicators, then audited against potential**
