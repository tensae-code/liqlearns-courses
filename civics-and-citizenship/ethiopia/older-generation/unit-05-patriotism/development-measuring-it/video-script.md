One lesson from the older course.

Development is measured, not felt. UNICEF's Millennium Development Goal evidence, as the guide keys it, is sobering: child mortality 'seriously off-track', though Ethiopia's under-five rate was falling about 1% a year in 2004; gender parity in education set to be missed by 54 countries in 2015; 75 million children out of primary school, 70% of them in sub-Saharan Africa; 29,000 under-fives dying daily of largely preventable causes.

Remember it with its case. Now — say it back, in your own words.
