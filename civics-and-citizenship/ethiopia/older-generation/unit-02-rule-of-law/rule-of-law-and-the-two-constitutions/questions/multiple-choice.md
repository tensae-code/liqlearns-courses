# Rule of law and the two constitutions — federal and regional — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** In a federation, a citizen lives under —
a) both a federal and a regional constitution  b) only the regional constitution  c) no constitution
**Answer: a) both a federal and a regional constitution**

**2.** SNNPR is used in the readings because it has —
a) no schools  b) the greatest ethno-cultural diversity  c) the smallest population
**Answer: b) the greatest ethno-cultural diversity**

**3.** Vaughan found peasants —
a) welcomed mother-tongue schooling  b) rejected all schooling  c) demanded Latin
**Answer: a) welcomed mother-tongue schooling**

**4.** The educated feared mother-tongue schooling would —
a) disadvantage school leavers  b) cost too little  c) please the peasants
**Answer: a) disadvantage school leavers**

**5.** English was selected as the language of learning for civics from —
a) Grade 5  b) Grade 1  c) university only
**Answer: a) Grade 5**

**6.** Rule of law means first of all —
a) a rulebook that stands above everyone, rulers included  b) the ruler's word is law  c) custom only
**Answer: a) a rulebook that stands above everyone, rulers included**

**7.** The lesson's method is —
a) memorising one book  b) a debate about flags  c) comparison of the two constitutions by two halves of the class
**Answer: c) comparison of the two constitutions by two halves of the class**

**8.** A region's constitution must agree with —
a) the federal constitution  b) the mayor's wishes  c) foreign law
**Answer: a) the federal constitution**

**9.** The language-of-education problem shows —
a) that English is banned  b) federalism's principles meeting daily life  c) that languages do not matter
**Answer: b) federalism's principles meeting daily life**

**10.** Mother-tongue schooling's supporters in Vaughan's finding were —
a) peasants  b) foreign experts  c) prison warders
**Answer: a) peasants**
