# Rule of law and the two constitutions — federal and regional — short answer

Answer in one to three sentences, from the lesson.

**1.** Why does the lesson split the class to read two constitutions?
**Answer:** Because a citizen in a federation lives under both the federal and the regional constitution; each side teaches the other what its book says.

**2.** What did Vaughan find about mother-tongue schooling?
**Answer:** Peasants welcomed it; the educated feared it would disadvantage school leavers.

**3.** From which grade was English selected as the language of learning for civics?
**Answer:** From Grade 5.
