# Limited versus unlimited government — Soweto and Biko — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** A limited government —
a) accepts a constitution above itself  b) may do anything  c) has no courts
**Answer: a) accepts a constitution above itself**

**2.** Ethiopia since 1991 is given as building —
a) separation of powers under a supreme constitution  b) a military regime  c) one-party rule forever
**Answer: a) separation of powers under a supreme constitution**

**3.** An unlimited government example is —
a) Britain  b) the United States  c) Franco's Spain
**Answer: c) Franco's Spain**

**4.** Franco's military rule in Spain lasted —
a) 35 years  b) 2 years  c) 100 years
**Answer: a) 35 years**

**5.** The Soweto uprising was on —
a) 16 June 1976  b) 16 June 1966  c) 16 June 1986
**Answer: a) 16 June 1976**

**6.** Soweto's students protested against —
a) examinations  b) being forced to learn in Afrikaans  c) school uniforms
**Answer: b) being forced to learn in Afrikaans**

**7.** Hector Peterson was —
a) a minister  b) a soldier  c) thirteen years old when shot dead
**Answer: c) thirteen years old when shot dead**

**8.** Steve Biko died —
a) as president  b) in police custody in September 1977  c) in exile in 1994
**Answer: b) in police custody in September 1977**

**9.** Biko led —
a) the apartheid government  b) a football team  c) the Black Consciousness movement
**Answer: c) the Black Consciousness movement**

**10.** Ethiopia's military regime ruled —
a) 1960–74  b) 1974–91  c) 1991–2005
**Answer: b) 1974–91**
