# Limited versus unlimited government — Soweto and Biko — short answer

Answer in one to three sentences, from the lesson.

**1.** What makes a government 'limited'?
**Answer:** It accepts that a constitution stands above it — separation of powers under a supreme constitution.

**2.** What happened in Soweto on 16 June 1976?
**Answer:** School students rose against forced Afrikaans-medium learning; thirteen-year-old Hector Peterson was shot dead.

**3.** Who was Steve Biko and how did he die?
**Answer:** The Black Consciousness leader — banned, harassed, tortured, he died in police custody in September 1977.
