# Fighting corruption — Watergate and the Ethics and Anti-Corruption Commission — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Corruption is described in the seminar papers as —
a) invented in Africa  b) a new internet problem  c) an old phenomenon, not an African invention
**Answer: c) an old phenomenon, not an African invention**

**2.** Stolen sums hidden abroad can exceed —
a) a school budget  b) some countries' whole public debt  c) a mayor's salary
**Answer: b) some countries' whole public debt**

**3.** Corruption's most devastating form is —
a) late tax forms  b) electoral fraud  c) littering
**Answer: b) electoral fraud**

**4.** In Watergate, 'Deep Throat' was —
a) the reporters' secret source  b) the president  c) a judge
**Answer: a) the reporters' secret source**

**5.** Nixon was re-elected in a landslide in —
a) November 1960  b) November 1980  c) November 1972
**Answer: c) November 1972**

**6.** Nixon resigned in —
a) 1974  b) 1972  c) 1980
**Answer: a) 1974**

**7.** What forced Nixon's resignation?
a) A drought  b) A football result  c) His own tapes surfacing
**Answer: c) His own tapes surfacing**

**8.** Ethiopia's anti-corruption body is —
a) IGAD  b) the Ethics and Anti-Corruption Commission  c) the ECA
**Answer: b) the Ethics and Anti-Corruption Commission**

**9.** Corruption is development's —
a) small detail  b) major obstacle  c) engine
**Answer: b) major obstacle**

**10.** Watergate proves that in a transparent system —
a) journalists should be jailed  b) accountability reaches the top  c) presidents are untouchable
**Answer: b) accountability reaches the top**
