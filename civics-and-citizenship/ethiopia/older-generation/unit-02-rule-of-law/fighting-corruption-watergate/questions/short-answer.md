# Fighting corruption — Watergate and the Ethics and Anti-Corruption Commission — short answer

Answer in one to three sentences, from the lesson.

**1.** Why is electoral fraud called corruption's most devastating form?
**Answer:** Because it steals the people's voice itself — it corrupts democracy, not only money.

**2.** How did Watergate end, and what forced it?
**Answer:** President Nixon, re-elected in a landslide in November 1972, resigned in 1974 when the tapes surfaced, after two reporters pursued the story with a secret source.

**3.** What is Ethiopia's named anti-corruption body?
**Answer:** The Ethics and Anti-Corruption Commission.
