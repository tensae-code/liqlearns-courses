# Why rule of law is necessary — when it breaks down — short answer

Answer in one to three sentences, from the lesson.

**1.** What happened to the journalist Gitobu Imanyara, and why does it matter?
**Answer:** He was arrested after exposing corruption in Moi's Kenya — a sign that power stood above the law.

**2.** What showed breakdown in Nkrumah's Ghana by 1960?
**Answer:** Rule by decree, and an anti-corruption committee whose findings were never published.

**3.** How does the South Omo case answer the two breakdown stories?
**Answer:** Youths of different cultures building peace together — knowing each other's cultures builds the tolerance rule of law needs.
