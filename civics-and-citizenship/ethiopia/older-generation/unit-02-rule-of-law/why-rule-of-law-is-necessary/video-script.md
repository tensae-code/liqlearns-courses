# Video script — Why rule of law is necessary — when it breaks down

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. Short script.

---

One lesson from the older course.

The necessity of rule of law is shown by its absence. In Kenya under Moi, corruption became institutionalised; the president was quoted in the press — 'I am said to be above the law, but I do not break the law' — while the journalist Gitobu Imanyara, who exposed corruption, was arrested. In Nkrumah's Ghana, the slide came by 1960: rule by decree, and an anti-corruption committee whose findings were never published. A committee that never reports, a journalist in prison — that is what breakdown looks like from inside.

Remember it with its case. Now — say it back, in your own words.
