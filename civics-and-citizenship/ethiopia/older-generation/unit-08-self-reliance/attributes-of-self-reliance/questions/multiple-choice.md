# Attributes of self-reliance — esteem, awareness, confidence, assertiveness — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Self-reliance as a national strategy means —
a) refusing all trade  b) banning aid workers  c) depending primarily on one's own resources and course
**Answer: c) depending primarily on one's own resources and course**

**2.** Many developing countries pursued it in —
a) the 2010s only  b) the 1970s and 80s  c) the 1890s
**Answer: b) the 1970s and 80s**

**3.** Self-esteem is —
a) judgement of one's own worth  b) pride in others  c) a bank balance
**Answer: a) judgement of one's own worth**

**4.** Absence of self-esteem breeds —
a) dependency  b) confidence  c) assertiveness
**Answer: a) dependency**

**5.** Self-awareness is knowing —
a) others' secrets  b) the future  c) one's capabilities and limits
**Answer: c) one's capabilities and limits**

**6.** Self-confidence includes —
a) never listening  b) never failing visibly  c) accepting criticism and learning from mistakes
**Answer: c) accepting criticism and learning from mistakes**

**7.** Tolla secured what was rightfully his by —
a) bribery  b) threats  c) polite but assertive action
**Answer: c) polite but assertive action**

**8.** Atitegeb farmed successfully among —
a) male farmers and development workers  b) no community  c) foreign companies
**Answer: a) male farmers and development workers**

**9.** Assertiveness is —
a) shouting loudest  b) silent obedience  c) esteem made audible — standing up, politely, for one's right
**Answer: c) esteem made audible — standing up, politely, for one's right**

**10.** Aid is acceptable in self-reliance when —
a) it is secret  b) it replaces all local work  c) it does not undermine independence
**Answer: c) it does not undermine independence**
