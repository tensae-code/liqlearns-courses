# Attributes of self-reliance — esteem, awareness, confidence, assertiveness — short answer

Answer in one to three sentences, from the lesson.

**1.** Define self-esteem, self-awareness and self-confidence as the lesson gives them.
**Answer:** Self-esteem: judgement of one's own worth. Self-awareness: knowing one's capabilities and limits. Self-confidence: courage to defend one's beliefs, learn from mistakes, accept criticism, stay visionary and ambitious.

**2.** Does national self-reliance forbid all aid and exchange?
**Answer:** No — it does not preclude exchange, or aid that does not undermine independence.

**3.** How did Atitegeb win her independence?
**Answer:** After her husband's death she broke the traditional impositions on her, farmed successfully among male farmers, and won independence by hard work.
