One lesson from the older course.

Self-reliance scales from person to nation. As a development strategy it means depending primarily on one's own human and material resources and determining one's own course — the strategy many developing countries pursued in the 1970s and 80s as a guarantee of emancipation — without precluding exchange, or aid that does not undermine independence. Its personal attributes are defined one by one: **self-esteem**, the judgement of one's own worth (its absence breeds dependency); **self-awareness**, knowing one's capabilities and limits; **self-confidence**, the courage to defend one's beliefs, learn from mistakes, accept criticism, and stay visionary and ambitious — with assertiveness, esteem made audible, beside them.

Remember it with its case. Now — say it back, in your own words.
