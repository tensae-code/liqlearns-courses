# Decision-making — Lambebo's briefcase and the SWOT habit — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The good decision-maker works from —
a) the loudest advice  b) accurate information  c) wishes and rumours
**Answer: b) accurate information**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Shouldering responsibility is —
a) one of the decision-maker's attributes  b) a weakness  c) only for elders
**Answer: a) one of the decision-maker's attributes**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** SWOT's four boxes are —
a) Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats  b) Sales, Wages, Orders, Taxes  c) Stop, Wait, Observe, Think
**Answer: a) Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Shmebo's case applies SWOT to —
a) a briefcase  b) a farm sale  c) his ambition
**Answer: c) his ambition**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A SWOT analysis turns a hope into —
a) a plan  b) a guarantee  c) a complaint
**Answer: a) a plan**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Lambebo finds —
a) a wallet of his own  b) a lost child  c) a briefcase
**Answer: c) a briefcase**

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** Lambebo's dishonest option is to —
a) report it  b) return it whole  c) return it light and lie
**Answer: c) return it light and lie**

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** Marrying too young is classed as —
a) easily amended  b) not a decision  c) a decision not easily amended
**Answer: c) a decision not easily amended**

**9. (Sub lesson 3)** Where to live is classed as —
a) unamendable  b) forbidden  c) amendable
**Answer: c) amendable**

**10. (Sub lesson 3)** The method's closing virtue is —
a) secrecy  b) flexibility  c) speed
**Answer: b) flexibility**

**11. (Sub lesson 3)** A job taken only for money is —
a) always right  b) not discussed  c) hard to amend once taken
**Answer: c) hard to amend once taken**

**12. (Sub lesson 3)** Self-reliance in action, in this lesson, is —
a) decision-making  b) saving  c) voting
**Answer: a) decision-making**

**13. (Sub lesson 1)** A decision built on wishes instead of accurate information —
a) fails the decision-maker's first duty  b) is the SWOT habit  c) is always amendable
**Answer: a) fails the decision-maker's first duty**
