# Decision-making — Lambebo's briefcase and the SWOT habit — short answer

Answer in one to three sentences, from the lesson.

**1.** Name four attributes of the good decision-maker.
**Answer:** Any four of: courage, social-mindedness, innovativeness, confidence, awareness, self-respect, shouldering responsibility.

**2.** What does SWOT stand for?
**Answer:** Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats.

**3.** Give one amendable and one unamendable decision from the lesson.
**Answer:** Amendable: where to live, which course. Unamendable (not easily): marrying too young, a job taken only for money.
