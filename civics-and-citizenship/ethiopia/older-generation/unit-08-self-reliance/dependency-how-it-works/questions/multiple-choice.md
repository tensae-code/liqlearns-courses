# Dependency — how it works, Nyerere's experiment, and Menelik's imports — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Dependency is —
a) no trade at all  b) equal partnership  c) asymmetric interdependence
**Answer: c) asymmetric interdependence**

**2.** Primary products are over what share of developing countries' exports?
a) 17%  b) 30%  c) 70%
**Answer: c) 70%**

**3.** Manufacturing's share in developed countries' exports is —
a) 50%  b) 81%  c) 18%
**Answer: b) 81%**

**4.** Prices in dependency are set by —
a) the sellers  b) village elders  c) the rich buyers
**Answer: c) the rich buyers**

**5.** Ujamaa was —
a) a Tanzanian port  b) a tax  c) Nyerere's village socialism experiment
**Answer: c) Nyerere's village socialism experiment**

**6.** Nyerere's 1981 admission was —
a) 'We succeeded fully'  b) 'Debt is freedom'  c) 'We are poorer now than we were in 1972'
**Answer: c) 'We are poorer now than we were in 1972'**

**7.** Black African external debt grew from $6 billion in 1970 to —
a) $600 billion by 1975  b) $86 billion by 1983  c) $9 billion by 1983
**Answer: b) $86 billion by 1983**

**8.** The 1973 shock was —
a) an election  b) the oil shock  c) a flood
**Answer: b) the oil shock**

**9.** Menelik exported coffee and imported —
a) rice  b) steel mills  c) abujedid cotton
**Answer: c) abujedid cotton**

**10.** Marcus says Menelik imported Europe's goods but not —
a) its languages  b) its religions  c) its mode of production
**Answer: c) its mode of production**
