# Dependency — how it works, Nyerere's experiment, and Menelik's imports — short answer

Answer in one to three sentences, from the lesson.

**1.** Give the trade figures that document dependency.
**Answer:** Primary products: over 70% of developing countries' exports; manufacturing: 81% share in developed countries' exports.

**2.** What did Nyerere admit in 1981?
**Answer:** 'We are poorer now than we were in 1972' — his ujamaa experiment's honest verdict.

**3.** What is Marcus's judgement on Menelik?
**Answer:** He imported Europe's goods but not its mode of production.
