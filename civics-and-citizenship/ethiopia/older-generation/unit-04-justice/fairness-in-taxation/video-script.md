# Video script — Fairness in taxation — from cattle tribute to VAT, and the 4,000-Birr debate

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. Full script — the design names a video-script need for this lesson.

---

This lesson is told as a story.

Taxation has a long Ethiopian history. From Pankhurst: cattle tribute since Emperor Amda Seyon's reign (1313–44) and his 'scribes of cattle'; Alvares's report of Hadeya's great cattle payments and Bugna's 150 plough oxen; Gurage's thousand live cows a year. Today's cash taxes fund what tribute never did — free primary education for all, against the past's elite-only schooling. The VAT case study explains taxing consumption, with exemptions where a tax would fall unfairly on those who cannot pay.

Then the debate: Mr. X and Mr. Y both earn 4,000 Birr. X supports a family of seven and pays for expensive treatment for a serious illness; Y supports himself. Both pay the same tax. Horizontal equity says equals should be treated equally — but are X and Y really equals? The lesson chairs the argument and lets the class judge.

That is the lesson. Tell it back — in your own words, with the names and dates in place.
