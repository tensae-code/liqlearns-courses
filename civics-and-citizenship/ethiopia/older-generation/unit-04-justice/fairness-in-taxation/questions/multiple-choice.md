# Fairness in taxation — from cattle tribute to VAT, and the 4,000-Birr debate — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Cattle tribute is recorded since the reign of —
a) Amda Seyon (1313–44)  b) Menelik II  c) Haile Selassie
**Answer: a) Amda Seyon (1313–44)**

**2.** Amda Seyon's officials were called —
a) tax farmers  b) customs men  c) scribes of cattle
**Answer: c) scribes of cattle**

**3.** Bugna's payment was —
a) a thousand cows  b) 150 plough oxen  c) 15 coins
**Answer: b) 150 plough oxen**

**4.** Gurage paid a thousand —
a) salt bars  b) coffee sacks  c) live cows a year
**Answer: c) live cows a year**

**5.** Today's cash taxes fund —
a) palaces  b) free primary education for all  c) elite-only schooling
**Answer: b) free primary education for all**

**6.** VAT taxes —
a) land only  b) imports only  c) consumption
**Answer: c) consumption**

**7.** VAT exemptions exist so tax does not —
a) fall unfairly on those who cannot pay  b) help the rich  c) confuse traders
**Answer: a) fall unfairly on those who cannot pay**

**8.** Mr. X and Mr. Y both earn —
a) 40,000 Birr  b) 4,000 Birr  c) 400 Birr
**Answer: b) 4,000 Birr**

**9.** X supports —
a) a family of seven, plus expensive treatment  b) only himself  c) a company
**Answer: a) a family of seven, plus expensive treatment**

**10.** Horizontal equity asks whether —
a) salaries should double  b) equals are really being treated equally — and whether X and Y are equals  c) taxes should be abolished
**Answer: b) equals are really being treated equally — and whether X and Y are equals**
