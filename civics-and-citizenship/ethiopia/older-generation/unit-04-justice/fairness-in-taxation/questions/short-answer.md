# Fairness in taxation — from cattle tribute to VAT, and the 4,000-Birr debate — short answer

Answer in one to three sentences, from the lesson.

**1.** Name two old forms of cattle tribute Pankhurst records.
**Answer:** Any two of: tribute since Amda Seyon's reign with his 'scribes of cattle'; Hadeya's great cattle payments; Bugna's 150 plough oxen; Gurage's thousand live cows a year.

**2.** Why does VAT carry exemptions?
**Answer:** So that the tax does not fall unfairly on those who cannot pay.

**3.** State the 4,000-Birr debate in one sentence.
**Answer:** Mr. X and Mr. Y earn the same 4,000 Birr, but X feeds seven and pays for expensive treatment — is the same tax on both fair?
