# How courts work — impartiality, nepotism and stereotyping — short answer

Answer in one to three sentences, from the lesson.

**1.** Name three structural guards of a judge's impartiality.
**Answer:** The judge may not consider who is in power; may not compete for political office while serving; removal is only conditional, with retirement fixed by law.

**2.** What did Ato Worku's case show?
**Answer:** Corruption through nepotism — a boss engineering a job for his family.

**3.** What were the chief targets of the 1991 looting?
**Answer:** Resented service cooperatives, hit by arson and destruction after the Derg fell.
