One lesson from the older course.

The justice system has two doors, both under one constitution. The formal judiciary: independent judges directed by the FDRE Constitution and the criminal, family and civil laws. And the traditional instruments: community justice by customary norms, with the litigants' consent — recognised by the Constitution itself (Article 34 on religious and customary marriage; Article 78(5), letting the House of Peoples' Representatives and State Councils recognise religious and customary courts). Crucially, any party may refuse the customary door and claim the conventional courts instead. Consent is what makes it justice.
