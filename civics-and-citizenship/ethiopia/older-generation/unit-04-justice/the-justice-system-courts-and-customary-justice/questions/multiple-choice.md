# The justice system — courts, customary justice, and the Red Terror's warning — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Formal judges are directed by —
a) the mayor  b) custom alone  c) the FDRE Constitution and the criminal, family and civil laws
**Answer: c) the FDRE Constitution and the criminal, family and civil laws**

**2.** Customary justice requires —
a) the litigants' consent  b) the army's order  c) a foreign judge
**Answer: a) the litigants' consent**

**3.** Article 78(5) concerns —
a) recognising religious and customary courts  b) tax rates  c) school terms
**Answer: a) recognising religious and customary courts**

**4.** Article 34 concerns —
a) elections  b) religious and customary marriage  c) land ownership
**Answer: b) religious and customary marriage**

**5.** A party who refuses customary courts may —
a) be jailed  b) pay a fine  c) claim the conventional courts instead
**Answer: c) claim the conventional courts instead**

**6.** The Red Terror case is the system's —
a) negative pole — justice's total absence  b) model example  c) founding charter
**Answer: a) negative pole — justice's total absence**

**7.** Mengistu smashed bottles of —
a) red ink, to legitimise bloodshed  b) water  c) milk
**Answer: a) red ink, to legitimise bloodshed**

**8.** The *natsa ermejja* means —
a) free pardon  b) unrestricted license to kill  c) fair trial
**Answer: b) unrestricted license to kill**

**9.** Kebele defence squads received the license by —
a) an election  b) the May Day 1977 climax  c) a court ruling
**Answer: b) the May Day 1977 climax**

**10.** Consent makes customary justice —
a) optional law for officials  b) a foreign custom  c) justice rather than mere power
**Answer: c) justice rather than mere power**
