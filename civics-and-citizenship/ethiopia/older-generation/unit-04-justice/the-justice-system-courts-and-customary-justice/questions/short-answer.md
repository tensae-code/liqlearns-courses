# The justice system — courts, customary justice, and the Red Terror's warning — short answer

Answer in one to three sentences, from the lesson.

**1.** Which constitutional articles recognise customary and religious courts and marriage?
**Answer:** Article 34 (religious and customary marriage) and Article 78(5) (recognition of religious and customary courts).

**2.** What protects a person who does not want customary justice?
**Answer:** Consent: any party may refuse it and claim the conventional courts instead.

**3.** What was the *natsa ermejja*?
**Answer:** The 'unrestricted license to kill' devolved to city councils and kebele defence squads during the Red Terror.
