One lesson from the older course.

Fairness is equity of benefits and burdens. Citizens receive: education free to the end of the first secondary cycle, and partly subsidised health care. Citizens carry: taxation above all. Other countries balance the pans differently — the United Kingdom's health care is fully free (its 2000/01 budget spent about £109 billion on social security, £72 billion on health, £50 billion on education, at £1 = 15 Birr as the guide converts it), while the United States leaves health largely to private insurance. Neither balance is automatic; each is a choice about fairness.

Remember it with its case. Now — say it back, in your own words.
