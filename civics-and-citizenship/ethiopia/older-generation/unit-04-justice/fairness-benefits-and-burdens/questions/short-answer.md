# Fairness — benefits and burdens, and who pays — short answer

Answer in one to three sentences, from the lesson.

**1.** What do Ethiopian citizens receive and carry, in the lesson's terms?
**Answer:** They receive education free to the end of the first secondary cycle and partly subsidised health care; they carry taxation above all.

**2.** How does the UK balance health care, and the US?
**Answer:** The UK's health care is fully free, funded from the budget; the US leaves health largely to private insurance.

**3.** What is the benefits principle at the fuel pump?
**Answer:** Those who benefit from the roads help pay for them — fuel duty decisions move every transport price.
