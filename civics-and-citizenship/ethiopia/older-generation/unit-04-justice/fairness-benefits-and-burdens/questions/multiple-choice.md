# Fairness — benefits and burdens, and who pays — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Fairness is equity of —
a) benefits and burdens  b) winners and losers  c) cities and villages
**Answer: a) benefits and burdens**

**2.** Education in Ethiopia is free to —
a) nowhere  b) the end of the first secondary cycle  c) university doctorate
**Answer: b) the end of the first secondary cycle**

**3.** Health care in Ethiopia is —
a) fully free  b) fully private  c) partly subsidised
**Answer: c) partly subsidised**

**4.** The UK's health care is —
a) fully free  b) fully private  c) closed to citizens
**Answer: a) fully free**

**5.** The guide converts £1 as —
a) 1 Birr  b) 100 Birr  c) 15 Birr
**Answer: c) 15 Birr**

**6.** The US model leaves health largely to —
a) village elders  b) the army  c) private insurance
**Answer: c) private insurance**

**7.** The main burden citizens carry is —
a) building palaces  b) taxation  c) military parades
**Answer: b) taxation**

**8.** Teshome's case is about —
a) a football match  b) a worker treated unfairly by Ato Gadissa  c) a tax form
**Answer: b) a worker treated unfairly by Ato Gadissa**

**9.** Fuel duty decisions move —
a) nothing  b) every transport price  c) only taxi fares in Addis
**Answer: b) every transport price**

**10.** The benefits principle says those who benefit from roads —
a) help pay for them  b) never pay  c) pay double
**Answer: a) help pay for them**
