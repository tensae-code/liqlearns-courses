# Equitability analysed — tenants, landlords and Bloody Sunday — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Ethiopian tenants owed what share of their produce?
a) 75 percent  b) 25 percent  c) 10 percent
**Answer: a) 75 percent**

**2.** On top of the produce, tenants owed —
a) a yearly holiday  b) free labour and services  c) nothing
**Answer: b) free labour and services**

**3.** Tenants lived in perpetual fear of —
a) promotion  b) rain  c) eviction
**Answer: c) eviction**

**4.** Bloody Sunday happened in —
a) 1905  b) 1861  c) 1974
**Answer: a) 1905**

**5.** About how many petitioners were killed on Bloody Sunday?
a) 950  b) 95  c) 9
**Answer: b) 95**

**6.** Russian serfs were abolished in —
a) 1905  b) 1967  c) 1861
**Answer: c) 1861**

**7.** The 1967 code in the Ethiopian case is —
a) the Civil Code  b) the Criminal Code  c) a tax circular
**Answer: a) the Civil Code**

**8.** Three-quarters of Russia's population were —
a) landlords  b) soldiers  c) poor peasants
**Answer: c) poor peasants**

**9.** The role-play includes —
a) a shoe-shine street child and a rural teacher  b) only ministers  c) only landlords
**Answer: a) a shoe-shine street child and a rural teacher**

**10.** Equitability asks whether distribution is —
a) decided by landlords  b) fair in what each receives and carries  c) equal in names only
**Answer: b) fair in what each receives and carries**
