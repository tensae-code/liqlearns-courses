# Equitability analysed — tenants, landlords and Bloody Sunday — short answer

Answer in one to three sentences, from the lesson.

**1.** What did an Ethiopian tenant owe under the 1967 Civil Code settlement?
**Answer:** 75 percent of his produce, plus free labour, transport, firewood, domestic service and granary building.

**2.** What was Bloody Sunday 1905?
**Answer:** Hymn-singing petitioners in Russia were fired on; about 95 were killed.

**3.** Why does the lesson use Russia as a mirror?
**Answer:** Its inequitable distribution — poor peasants and serfs — mirrors Ethiopia's landlord–tenant order.
