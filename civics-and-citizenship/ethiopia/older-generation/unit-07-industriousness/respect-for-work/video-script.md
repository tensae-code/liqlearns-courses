The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. Short script.

One lesson from the older course.

All work is honoured. The guide reaches back to Grade 10's body-parts case — every part needed for the body to work — and to Pankhurst's medieval Ethiopia, where blacksmiths were 'regarded with distrust, fear and at times even hatred', and weavers — many of them Falasha or Muslim — were kept a class apart, as potters and tailors still are. Contempt for makers is an old injustice, not a law of nature.

Remember it with its case. Now — say it back, in your own words.
