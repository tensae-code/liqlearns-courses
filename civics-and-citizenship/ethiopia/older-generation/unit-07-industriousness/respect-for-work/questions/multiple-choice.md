# Respect for work — blacksmiths, cleaners and principals — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Grade 10's body-parts case teaches —
a) hands matter most  b) only the head matters  c) every part is needed for the body to work
**Answer: c) every part is needed for the body to work**

**2.** Medieval Ethiopian blacksmiths were regarded with —
a) indifference  b) distrust, fear and at times hatred  c) the highest honour
**Answer: b) distrust, fear and at times hatred**

**3.** Weavers were often —
a) nobles  b) foreigners  c) Falasha or Muslim, kept a class apart
**Answer: c) Falasha or Muslim, kept a class apart**

**4.** Potters and tailors —
a) rule the villages  b) never existed  c) are still kept apart in places
**Answer: c) are still kept apart in places**

**5.** Remove the cleaners from a school and —
a) the activity ceases, as with any level  b) nothing changes  c) lessons improve
**Answer: a) the activity ceases, as with any level**

**6.** Pay rises honestly with —
a) qualifications and experience  b) family name  c) flattery
**Answer: a) qualifications and experience**

**7.** Tirualem's case shows —
a) luck alone  b) inheritance  c) hard work and study changing a future
**Answer: c) hard work and study changing a future**

**8.** Respect for work means —
a) all pay must be identical  b) no one may supervise  c) every job's value to individual and country stands
**Answer: c) every job's value to individual and country stands**

**9.** The school hierarchy runs from principal through —
a) teachers, secretaries, cleaners, caretakers and guards  b) students only  c) the ministry only
**Answer: a) teachers, secretaries, cleaners, caretakers and guards**

**10.** Contempt for makers is described as —
a) an old injustice, not a law of nature  b) a religious duty  c) a modern invention
**Answer: a) an old injustice, not a law of nature**
