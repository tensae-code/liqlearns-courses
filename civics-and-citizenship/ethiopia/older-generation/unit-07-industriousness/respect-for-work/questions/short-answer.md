# Respect for work — blacksmiths, cleaners and principals — short answer

Answer in one to three sentences, from the lesson.

**1.** How were blacksmiths regarded in Pankhurst's medieval Ethiopia?
**Answer:** With distrust, fear and at times even hatred; weavers were kept a class apart, as potters and tailors still are.

**2.** How does the school hierarchy prove the lesson?
**Answer:** Remove any level — principal to guards and cleaners — and the school's activity ceases.

**3.** Why is higher pay for qualifications not against the lesson?
**Answer:** Pay honestly follows qualifications and experience; respect for every job's value stands alongside that.
