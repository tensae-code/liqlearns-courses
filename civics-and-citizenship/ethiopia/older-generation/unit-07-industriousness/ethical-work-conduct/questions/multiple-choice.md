# Ethical work conduct — codes, competence and the bank dress code — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Ethical work conduct is —
a) dress only  b) speed only  c) competence plus conduct
**Answer: c) competence plus conduct**

**2.** A firefighter must —
a) never train  b) be on time and work his equipment daily  c) own the station
**Answer: b) be on time and work his equipment daily**

**3.** A guard at a burglary-risk post must —
a) know his weapon  b) sleep on duty  c) lend his weapon
**Answer: a) know his weapon**

**4.** Keeping knowledge current is the duty of —
a) the employee alone  b) the ministry alone  c) employer and employee together
**Answer: c) employer and employee together**

**5.** Bank dress codes are a case of —
a) dress as a professional signal  b) fashion competition  c) hiding identity
**Answer: a) dress as a professional signal**

**6.** Uniforms appear in —
a) only schools  b) hospitals, pharmacies, police, armed forces, judiciary  c) only banks
**Answer: b) hospitals, pharmacies, police, armed forces, judiciary**

**7.** Breaking a work code can —
a) earn promotion  b) endanger others  c) never matter
**Answer: b) endanger others**

**8.** Learners list the professional skills of —
a) their own teacher  b) a foreign celebrity  c) no one
**Answer: a) their own teacher**

**9.** A worker whose skills rot is —
a) retired  b) a risk someone else pays for  c) a model employee
**Answer: b) a risk someone else pays for**

**10.** A uniform tells the public —
a) this person is rich  b) this person is off duty  c) this person is on duty and accountable
**Answer: c) this person is on duty and accountable**
