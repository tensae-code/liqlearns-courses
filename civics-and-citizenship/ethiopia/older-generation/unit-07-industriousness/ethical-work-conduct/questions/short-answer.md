# Ethical work conduct — codes, competence and the bank dress code — short answer

Answer in one to three sentences, from the lesson.

**1.** Why must a firefighter's competence be daily, not occasional?
**Answer:** Because breaking the code endangers others — he must be on time and able to work his equipment every day.

**2.** Whose duty is it to keep knowledge current?
**Answer:** Employer and employee share the duty.

**3.** What does a uniform or dress code signal?
**Answer:** That the person is on duty and accountable — a professional signal to the public.
