# Ethical work conduct — codes, competence and the bank dress code

## Do it first

A firefighter arrives late, and his ladder does not work. Is that only his private failure? Ethical work conduct is where competence becomes morality — because other people's safety sits in your hands.

## The teaching

Workplace ethics is competence plus conduct. Codes of conduct exist because breaking them endangers others: the firefighter must be on time and able to work his equipment daily; the guard at a burglary-risk establishment must know his weapon. Keeping knowledge current is a shared duty of employer and employee alike — a worker whose skills rot is a risk someone else will pay for.

[Picture 29030 — a firefighter checking equipment, a bank clerk's tie]

The case is the dress code in banks: dress as a professional signal, as uniforms are in hospitals, pharmacies, police, armed forces and the judiciary — clothing that tells the public 'this person is on duty and accountable'. Learners close by listing their own teacher's professional skills, and how two of them could improve: ethics examined close to home.
