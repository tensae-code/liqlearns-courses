One lesson from the older course.

Workplace ethics is competence plus conduct. Codes of conduct exist because breaking them endangers others: the firefighter must be on time and able to work his equipment daily; the guard at a burglary-risk establishment must know his weapon. Keeping knowledge current is a shared duty of employer and employee alike — a worker whose skills rot is a risk someone else will pay for.

Remember it with its case. Now — say it back, in your own words.
