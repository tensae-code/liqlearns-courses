# Ethiopia's development strategies — ADLI, industry, capacity, and globalisation weighed — short answer

Answer in one to three sentences, from the lesson.

**1.** Name the four strategy documents.
**Answer:** ADLI; the Industrial Development Strategy; Issues of Building a Democratic System; the FDRE Foreign Relations and Security Policy.

**2.** What share of Ethiopia's population does agriculture carry?
**Answer:** Over 80%.

**3.** Give the UNDP's 1999 definition of globalisation.
**Answer:** 'Shrinking space, shrinking time and disappearing borders.'
