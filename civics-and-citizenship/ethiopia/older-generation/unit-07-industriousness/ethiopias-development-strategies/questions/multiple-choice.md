# Ethiopia's development strategies — ADLI, industry, capacity, and globalisation weighed — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** ADLI stands for —
a) Agricultural Development-Led Industrialisation  b) African Development Loan Initiative  c) Army Development Land Institute
**Answer: a) Agricultural Development-Led Industrialisation**

**2.** Agriculture carries over —
a) half the exports only  b) 80% of the population  c) 8% of the population
**Answer: b) 80% of the population**

**3.** The strategy documents number —
a) seven  b) four  c) three
**Answer: b) four**

**4.** The fourth document is —
a) the school syllabus  b) the FDRE Foreign Relations and Security Policy  c) a tax circular
**Answer: b) the FDRE Foreign Relations and Security Policy**

**5.** Issues of Building a Democratic System is the —
a) foreign document  b) capacity-building document  c) industrial document
**Answer: b) capacity-building document**

**6.** The UNDP definition of globalisation dates to —
a) 1989  b) 2009  c) 1999
**Answer: c) 1999**

**7.** Globalisation's credit column includes —
a) wealth creation, technology spread, women's rights  b) exploitation and pollution  c) suppressed unions
**Answer: a) wealth creation, technology spread, women's rights**

**8.** Its debit column includes —
a) exploitation, pollution, overwhelmed cultures, suppressed unions  b) technology spread  c) wealth creation
**Answer: a) exploitation, pollution, overwhelmed cultures, suppressed unions**

**9.** The guide itself corrects the student text's —
a) 'three documents' — they are actually four  b) page numbers  c) map of Ethiopia
**Answer: a) 'three documents' — they are actually four**

**10.** ADLI-led growth is meant to free the country from —
a) markets  b) dependence  c) farming
**Answer: b) dependence**
