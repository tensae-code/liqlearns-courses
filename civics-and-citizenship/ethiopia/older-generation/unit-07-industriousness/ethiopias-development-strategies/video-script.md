One lesson from the older course.

Ethiopia's chosen instruments are four strategy documents: **ADLI** — Agricultural Development-Led Industrialisation, the rural development strategy centred on agriculture, which carries over 80% of the population: agriculture-led growth assures accelerated growth, lets the people benefit, frees the country from dependence and builds a market economy. Then the Industrial Development Strategy; Issues of Building a Democratic System (the capacity-building document); and the FDRE Foreign Relations and Security Policy as the fourth.
