# Hard work and development — can Ethiopia copy the take-off? — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** De Rivero argues new industrialised countries are —
a) already 50 in number  b) unlikely to emerge  c) guaranteed
**Answer: b) unlikely to emerge**

**2.** The last through, in de Rivero's list, include —
a) Brazil and India  b) South Korea and Singapore  c) Ethiopia and Kenya
**Answer: b) South Korea and Singapore**

**3.** Liberalising alone will never suffice without —
a) bigger armies  b) technological modernisation and solid democratic institutions  c) more imports
**Answer: b) technological modernisation and solid democratic institutions**

**4.** The IMF and World Bank's faith is expressed as —
a) permanent poverty  b) no growth anywhere  c) take-offs and economic miracles
**Answer: c) take-offs and economic miracles**

**5.** A take-off means —
a) growth becoming self-sustaining, like a plane leaving the ground  b) a tax holiday  c) an airport opening
**Answer: a) growth becoming self-sustaining, like a plane leaving the ground**

**6.** Ethiopia must cut —
a) its schools  b) population growth, poor education, poor health care  c) its rivers
**Answer: b) population growth, poor education, poor health care**

**7.** Chinese development appears in the lesson as —
a) a myth  b) a warning only  c) a case visible across Ethiopia
**Answer: c) a case visible across Ethiopia**

**8.** Both camps agree Ethiopia must —
a) wait for aid only  b) work hard  c) do nothing
**Answer: b) work hard**

**9.** Poverty statistics are used as —
a) a secret  b) evidence brought home  c) decoration
**Answer: b) evidence brought home**

**10.** Hard work in this lesson is joined to —
a) fixing education and health, not effort alone  b) effort alone  c) speeches
**Answer: a) fixing education and health, not effort alone**
