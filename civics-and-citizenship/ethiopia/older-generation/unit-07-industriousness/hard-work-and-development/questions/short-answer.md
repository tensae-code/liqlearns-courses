# Hard work and development — can Ethiopia copy the take-off? — short answer

Answer in one to three sentences, from the lesson.

**1.** What is de Rivero's pessimist case?
**Answer:** No new industrialised countries are likely to emerge; liberalising alone will never suffice without technological modernisation and solid democratic institutions.

**2.** Name the last industrialised group de Rivero lists.
**Answer:** South Korea, Taiwan, Singapore, Hong Kong.

**3.** What three drags must Ethiopia cut, on the lesson's shared list?
**Answer:** Population growth, poor education, lack of health care.
