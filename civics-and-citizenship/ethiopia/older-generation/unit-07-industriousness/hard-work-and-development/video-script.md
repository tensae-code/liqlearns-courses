Development's pessimists and optimists argue. De Rivero's case: no new industrialised countries are likely to emerge — the last ones (South Korea, Taiwan, Singapore, Hong Kong) are already through, and liberalising alone 'will never suffice' without technological modernisation and solid democratic institutions. Against him, the IMF and World Bank keep faith that growth can be copied — their language of 'take-offs' and 'economic miracles'.
