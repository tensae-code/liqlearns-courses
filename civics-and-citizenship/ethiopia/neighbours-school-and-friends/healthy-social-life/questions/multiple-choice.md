# Healthy Social Life — Relationships That Keep Us Well — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) what endangers heritage  b) the benefits of honesty, and the harms of its absence  c) how honesty is learned and grown  d) what a healthy social life is
**Answer: d) what a healthy social life is** — That is the one thing this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does “healthy social life” mean?
a) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  b) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  c) relationships that keep us well and fit us comfortably into social life  d) the health of the mind — how we think, feel and bear life
**Answer: c) relationships that keep us well and fit us comfortably into social life** — “healthy social life” means: relationships that keep us well and fit us comfortably into social life.

**3.** What does “mental health” mean?
a) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  b) the health of the mind — how we think, feel and bear life  c) relationships that keep us well and fit us comfortably into social life  d) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.
**Answer: b) the health of the mind — how we think, feel and bear life** — “mental health” means: the health of the mind — how we think, feel and bear life.

**4.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) They affect our mental health, our physical health and our whole lives  b) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  c) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  d) The strongest person may take whatever they want.
**Answer: a) They affect our mental health, our physical health and our whole lives** — It is one of this lesson's own teachings.

**5.** Which of these is true in this lesson?
a) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  b) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  c) Promises, truth and fairness are not needed between people.  d) Social relationships connect with how comfortably we fit into social situations
**Answer: d) Social relationships connect with how comfortably we fit into social situations** — It is stated in the lesson.
