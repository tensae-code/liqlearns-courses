# Healthy Social Life — Relationships That Keep Us Well

## Do it first

Name the people you feel comfortable and safe with — at home, at school, next door. That web of people is your social life. This lesson asks what makes it healthy.

## The concept

Social relationships connect with how comfortably we fit into social situations.

They affect our mental health, our physical health and our whole lives.

Pupils start from the earlier equality lesson, give real examples from their own surroundings, and define a healthy social relationship.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: a healthy social life is made of relationships that fit us comfortably into social life — and they touch our mental health, our physical health and our whole lives. Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood, and name the lesson's key words: healthy social life, mental health. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: what a healthy social life is.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
