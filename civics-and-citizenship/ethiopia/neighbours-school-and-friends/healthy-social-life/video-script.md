# Video script — Healthy Social Life — Relationships That Keep Us Well

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A healthy social life is made of relationships that fit us comfortably into social life — and they touch our mental health, our physical health and our whole lives.

Social relationships connect with how comfortably we fit into social situations.

They affect our mental health, our physical health and our whole lives.

Pupils start from the earlier equality lesson, give real examples from their own surroundings, and define a healthy social relationship.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
