# Why Healthy Relationships Keep Us Well — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name three benefits for the person.
**Answer:** Any three of: belonging and purpose; worry falls when people stand beside you; refreshed mind; stronger resistance to disease; longer life; support in hard times.

**2.** What does it do for society in general?
**Answer:** Improves a society's social, economic and political affairs.

**3.** In your own words, what is belonging?
**Answer:** Feeling that you are part of a people and a place.
