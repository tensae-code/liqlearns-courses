# Why Healthy Relationships Keep Us Well — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** People with healthy social relationships and friends feel belonging and purpose.
**Answer: True**

**2.** It helps people live in unity.
**Answer: True**

**3.** A healthy social life raises worry and weakens the body against disease.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite.
