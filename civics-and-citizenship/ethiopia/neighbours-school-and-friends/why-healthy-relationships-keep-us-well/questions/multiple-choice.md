# Why Healthy Relationships Keep Us Well — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) the results that follow when self-control is missing  b) the benefits of a healthy social life  c) what self-respect is, why it matters, and how it is shown and built  d) protecting the environment — everyone's responsibility
**Answer: b) the benefits of a healthy social life** — That is the one thing this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does “belonging” mean?
a) feeling that you are part of a people and a place  b) a reason that gives life direction  c) the body's power to fight off sickness  d) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.
**Answer: a) feeling that you are part of a people and a place** — “belonging” means: feeling that you are part of a people and a place.

**3.** What does “purpose” mean?
a) feeling that you are part of a people and a place  b) the body's power to fight off sickness  c) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  d) a reason that gives life direction
**Answer: d) a reason that gives life direction** — “purpose” means: a reason that gives life direction.

**4.** What does “resist disease” mean?
a) a reason that gives life direction  b) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  c) the body's power to fight off sickness  d) feeling that you are part of a people and a place
**Answer: c) the body's power to fight off sickness** — “resist disease” means: the body's power to fight off sickness.

**5.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  b) Friends support each other in hard times, and the value one person gives another grows  c) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  d) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.
**Answer: b) Friends support each other in hard times, and the value one person gives another grows** — It is one of this lesson's own teachings.
