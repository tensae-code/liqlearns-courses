# Why Healthy Relationships Keep Us Well

## Do it first

Remember a hard day when someone stood beside you. What changed inside you? This lesson lists what standing-beside does — for the mind, the body and the whole society.

## For the person

People with healthy social relationships and friends feel belonging and purpose.

When people stand beside you in joy and in sorrow, your level of worry falls.

A healthy social life refreshes the mind, raises the body's power to resist disease, and even lengthens life.

Friends support each other in hard times, and the value one person gives another grows.

[Picture 28048 — standing beside — sad, then smiling]

## For everyone

It helps people live in unity.

It helps an individual's physical, social and other behaviours develop.

In general, it improves a society's social, economic and political affairs.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: healthy relationships give belonging and purpose, lower worry, refresh the mind, strengthen the body against disease — and hold a whole society together. Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood, and name the lesson's key words: belonging, purpose, resist disease. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: the benefits of a healthy social life.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
