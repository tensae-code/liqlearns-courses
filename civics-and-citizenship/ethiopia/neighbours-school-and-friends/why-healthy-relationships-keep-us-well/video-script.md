# Video script — Why Healthy Relationships Keep Us Well

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Healthy relationships give belonging and purpose, lower worry, refresh the mind, strengthen the body against disease — and hold a whole society together.

People with healthy social relationships and friends feel belonging and purpose.

When people stand beside you in joy and in sorrow, your level of worry falls.

A healthy social life refreshes the mind, raises the body's power to resist disease, and even lengthens life.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
