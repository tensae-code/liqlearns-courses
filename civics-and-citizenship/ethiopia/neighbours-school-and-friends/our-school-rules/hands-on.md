# Hands-on — Our School Rules — Time, Grounds, Lessons, Dress

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Check yourself against four questions: Am I on time? Do I leave grounds and latrines clean? Do I attend fully? Is my dress proper? Score yourself honestly — this lesson is those four questions.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the four rule areas: time, grounds, lessons, dress. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
