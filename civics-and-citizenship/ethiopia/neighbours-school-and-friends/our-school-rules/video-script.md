# Video script — Our School Rules — Time, Grounds, Lessons, Dress

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The school's rules cover four things: coming on time, using the grounds and latrines properly, attending lessons and assemblies, and proper dress.

Rule one: come to school on time.

Rule two: use the school grounds and the latrines properly.

Rule three: attend lessons and assemblies regularly.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
