# Our School Rules — Time, Grounds, Lessons, Dress

## Do it first

Check yourself against four questions: Am I on time? Do I leave grounds and latrines clean? Do I attend fully? Is my dress proper? Score yourself honestly — this lesson is those four questions.

## Time and grounds

Rule one: come to school on time.

Rule two: use the school grounds and the latrines properly.

[Picture 28038 — four rule panels]

## Lessons and dress

Rule three: attend lessons and assemblies regularly.

Rule four: come in the proper dress or uniform.

When pupils judge a rule statement true or false, they must be able to say why they chose it — the reasoning is part of the rule-learning.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the school's rules cover four things: coming on time, using the grounds and latrines properly, attending lessons and assemblies, and proper dress. Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood, and name the lesson's key words: grounds, assembly, uniform. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: the four rule areas: time, grounds, lessons, dress.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
