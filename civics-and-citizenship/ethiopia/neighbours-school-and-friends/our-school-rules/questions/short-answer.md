# Our School Rules — Time, Grounds, Lessons, Dress — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the four things the school's rules cover.
**Answer:** Coming on time; using the grounds and latrines properly; attending lessons and assemblies regularly; proper dress or uniform.

**2.** What must a pupil be able to do after judging a statement true or false?
**Answer:** Say why they chose true or false.

**3.** In your own words, what is grounds?
**Answer:** The school's land and yard.
