# Our School Rules — Time, Grounds, Lessons, Dress — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) who is in a family, and why each person matters  b) cultural values: the principles a community stands on  c) what self-control is, and how a self-controlled child acts  d) the four rule areas: time, grounds, lessons, dress
**Answer: d) the four rule areas: time, grounds, lessons, dress** — That is the one thing this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does “grounds” mean?
a) the proper dress the school sets for its pupils  b) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  c) the school's land and yard  d) the whole school gathered together
**Answer: c) the school's land and yard** — “grounds” means: the school's land and yard.

**3.** What does “assembly” mean?
a) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  b) the whole school gathered together  c) the school's land and yard  d) the proper dress the school sets for its pupils
**Answer: b) the whole school gathered together** — “assembly” means: the whole school gathered together.

**4.** What does “uniform” mean?
a) the proper dress the school sets for its pupils  b) the school's land and yard  c) the whole school gathered together  d) The strongest person may take whatever they want.
**Answer: a) the proper dress the school sets for its pupils** — “uniform” means: the proper dress the school sets for its pupils.

**5.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  b) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  c) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  d) Rule three
**Answer: d) Rule three** — It is one of this lesson's own teachings.
