# Our School Rules — Time, Grounds, Lessons, Dress — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Rule one: come to school on time.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Rule three: attend lessons and assemblies regularly.
**Answer: True**

**3.** The school rules say nothing about latrines or dress; they only cover lessons.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite.
