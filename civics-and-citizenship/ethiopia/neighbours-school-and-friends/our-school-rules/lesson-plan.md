# Lesson plan — Our School Rules — Time, Grounds, Lessons, Dress

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Neighbours, School and Friends · **Lesson:** Our School Rules — Time, Grounds, Lessons, Dress
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Neighbours, School and Friends (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the school's rules cover four things: coming on time, using the grounds and latrines properly, attending lessons and assemblies, and proper dress.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the lessons before it in this title — the learner already knows the earlier circles of this course (myself, then family, neighbours, school, community and culture) as far as this title. It needs nothing else.
