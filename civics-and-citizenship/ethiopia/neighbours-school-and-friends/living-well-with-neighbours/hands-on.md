# Hands-on — Living Well with Neighbours — Building It and Not Spoiling It

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name one thing that builds friendship between neighbouring homes — and one thing that has spoiled it somewhere you know. Keep both in mind; this lesson works on both lists.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: building good neighbour relations, and removing what harms them. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
