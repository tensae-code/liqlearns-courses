# Lesson plan — Living Well with Neighbours — Building It and Not Spoiling It

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Neighbours, School and Friends · **Lesson:** Living Well with Neighbours — Building It and Not Spoiling It
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Neighbours, School and Friends (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** good relations with neighbours are built on purpose — and protected by removing the conditions that damage them.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the lessons before it in this title — the learner already knows the earlier circles of this course (myself, then family, neighbours, school, community and culture) as far as this title. It needs nothing else.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: The Good Neighbour — Traits, and Help in Good Times and Hard (the previous lesson in this title). Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Various ways of bringing positive relations between neighbours are listed in the chapter.
2. Greeting, helping, sharing and cooperating are the building acts the chapter's work practises.
3. The chapter also gives guidance for removing the conditions that damage good neighbourliness.
4. Pupils discuss in groups what can harm it — quarrels over fences, noise, and unkindness are the kind of conditions to remove.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson's scenes are the learner's own home, school and neighbourhood.
