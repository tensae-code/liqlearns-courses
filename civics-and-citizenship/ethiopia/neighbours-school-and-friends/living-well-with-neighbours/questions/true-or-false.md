# Living Well with Neighbours — Building It and Not Spoiling It — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Various ways of bringing positive relations between neighbours are listed in the chapter.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The chapter also gives guidance for removing the conditions that damage good neighbourliness.
**Answer: True**

**3.** Once neighbours quarrel, nothing can be done — damaging conditions cannot be removed.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite.
