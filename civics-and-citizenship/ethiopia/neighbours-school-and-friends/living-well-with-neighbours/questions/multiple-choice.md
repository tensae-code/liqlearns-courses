# Living Well with Neighbours — Building It and Not Spoiling It — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) honesty: no lying, no cheating, no stealing  b) building good neighbour relations, and removing what harms them  c) the political, economic and social benefits of heritage  d) the traits that grow good friendships
**Answer: b) building good neighbour relations, and removing what harms them** — That is the one thing this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does “positive relations” mean?
a) good, friendly connections between people  b) a state of things that brings harm about  c) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  d) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.
**Answer: a) good, friendly connections between people** — “positive relations” means: good, friendly connections between people.

**3.** What does “condition (that damages)” mean?
a) good, friendly connections between people  b) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  c) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  d) a state of things that brings harm about
**Answer: d) a state of things that brings harm about** — “condition (that damages)” means: a state of things that brings harm about.

**4.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  b) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  c) The chapter also gives guidance for removing the conditions that damage good neighbourliness  d) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.
**Answer: c) The chapter also gives guidance for removing the conditions that damage good neighbourliness** — It is one of this lesson's own teachings.

**5.** Which of these is true in this lesson?
a) Promises, truth and fairness are not needed between people.  b) Various ways of bringing positive relations between neighbours are listed in the chapter  c) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  d) The strongest person may take whatever they want.
**Answer: b) Various ways of bringing positive relations between neighbours are listed in the chapter** — It is stated in the lesson.
