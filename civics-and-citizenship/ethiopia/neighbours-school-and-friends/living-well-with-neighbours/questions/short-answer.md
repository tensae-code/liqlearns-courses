# Living Well with Neighbours — Building It and Not Spoiling It — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What do pupils discuss in groups in this part of the chapter?
**Answer:** What can harm good neighbourliness.

**2.** What guidance does the chapter give about damage?
**Answer:** Guidance for removing the conditions that damage good neighbourliness.

**3.** In your own words, what is positive relations?
