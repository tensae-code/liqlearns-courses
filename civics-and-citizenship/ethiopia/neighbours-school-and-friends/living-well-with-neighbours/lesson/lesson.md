# Living Well with Neighbours — Building It and Not Spoiling It

## Do it first

Name one thing that builds friendship between neighbouring homes — and one thing that has spoiled it somewhere you know. Keep both in mind; this lesson works on both lists.

## Building good relations

Various ways of bringing positive relations between neighbours are listed in the chapter.

Greeting, helping, sharing and cooperating are the building acts the chapter's work practises.

[Picture 28036 — cooperating — and no fence dispute]

## Not spoiling it

The chapter also gives guidance for removing the conditions that damage good neighbourliness.

Pupils discuss in groups what can harm it — quarrels over fences, noise, and unkindness are the kind of conditions to remove.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: good relations with neighbours are built on purpose — and protected by removing the conditions that damage them. Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood, and name the lesson's key words: positive relations, condition (that damages). Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: building good neighbour relations, and removing what harms them.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
