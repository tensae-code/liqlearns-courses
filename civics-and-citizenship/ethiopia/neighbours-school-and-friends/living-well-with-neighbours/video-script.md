# Video script — Living Well with Neighbours — Building It and Not Spoiling It

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Good relations with neighbours are built on purpose — and protected by removing the conditions that damage them.

Various ways of bringing positive relations between neighbours are listed in the chapter.

Greeting, helping, sharing and cooperating are the building acts the chapter's work practises.

The chapter also gives guidance for removing the conditions that damage good neighbourliness.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
