# Lesson plan — The Good Neighbour — Traits, and Help in Good Times and Hard

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Neighbours, School and Friends · **Lesson:** The Good Neighbour — Traits, and Help in Good Times and Hard
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Neighbours, School and Friends (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a good neighbour is known by their traits — and matters most across both seasons of life: help in hard times, shared joy in good times.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the lessons before it in this title — the learner already knows the earlier circles of this course (myself, then family, neighbours, school, community and culture) as far as this title. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. ## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Neighbourliness — Friends Who Live Next Door (the previous lesson in this title). Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. The traits of good neighbours are shown in role-play, with stories and pictures used to mark what a good neighbour looks like.
2. Friendliness, readiness to help, and respect for the people next door are the shape the activities draw.
3. The importance of a good neighbour is explained fully for both good times and hard times.
4. In hard times the good neighbour helps carry the load; in good times the good neighbour shares the celebration.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson's scenes are the learner's own home, school and neighbourhood.
