# Video script — The Good Neighbour — Traits, and Help in Good Times and Hard

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A good neighbour is known by their traits — and matters most across both seasons of life: help in hard times, shared joy in good times.

The traits of good neighbours are shown in role-play, with stories and pictures used to mark what a good neighbour looks like.

Friendliness, readiness to help, and respect for the people next door are the shape the activities draw.

The importance of a good neighbour is explained fully for both good times and hard times.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
