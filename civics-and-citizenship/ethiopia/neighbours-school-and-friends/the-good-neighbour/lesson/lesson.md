# The Good Neighbour — Traits, and Help in Good Times and Hard

## Do it first

Think of the best neighbour you know. Name what they did in a hard week — and what they did in a happy week. Those two lists are this lesson.

## Traits of a good neighbour

The traits of good neighbours are shown in role-play, with stories and pictures used to mark what a good neighbour looks like.

Friendliness, readiness to help, and respect for the people next door are the shape the activities draw.

[Picture 28035 — help in a hard week, joy in a good week]

## Good times and hard times

The importance of a good neighbour is explained fully for both good times and hard times.

In hard times the good neighbour helps carry the load; in good times the good neighbour shares the celebration.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: a good neighbour is known by their traits — and matters most across both seasons of life: help in hard times, shared joy in good times. Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood, and name the lesson's key words: trait, good times / hard times. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: the good neighbour's traits, in good times and hard.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
