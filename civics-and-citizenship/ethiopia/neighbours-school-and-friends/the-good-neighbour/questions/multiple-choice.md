# The Good Neighbour — Traits, and Help in Good Times and Hard — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) justice: each person given what is due  b) the three goods of local culture  c) the good neighbour's traits, in good times and hard  d) the ten elements of local culture
**Answer: c) the good neighbour's traits, in good times and hard** — That is the one thing this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does “trait” mean?
a) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  b) a quality a person is known by  c) seasons of plenty and joy, and seasons of trouble and need  d) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.
**Answer: b) a quality a person is known by** — “trait” means: a quality a person is known by.

**3.** What does “good times / hard times” mean?
a) seasons of plenty and joy, and seasons of trouble and need  b) a quality a person is known by  c) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  d) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.
**Answer: a) seasons of plenty and joy, and seasons of trouble and need** — “good times / hard times” means: seasons of plenty and joy, and seasons of trouble and need.

**4.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  b) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  c) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  d) The importance of a good neighbour is explained fully for both good times and hard times
**Answer: d) The importance of a good neighbour is explained fully for both good times and hard times** — It is one of this lesson's own teachings.

**5.** Which of these is true in this lesson?
a) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  b) Promises, truth and fairness are not needed between people.  c) The traits of good neighbours are shown in role-play, with stories and pictures used to mark what a good neigh  d) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.
**Answer: c) The traits of good neighbours are shown in role-play, with stories and pictures used to mark what a good neigh** — It is stated in the lesson.
