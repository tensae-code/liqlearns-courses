# The Good Neighbour — Traits, and Help in Good Times and Hard — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The traits of good neighbours are shown in role-play, with stories and pictures used to mark what a good neighbour looks like.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The importance of a good neighbour is explained fully for both good times and hard times.
**Answer: True**

**3.** A good neighbour is only needed in good times; in hard times neighbours should be left alone.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite.
