# The Good Neighbour — Traits, and Help in Good Times and Hard — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** How are the good neighbour's traits learned in class?
**Answer:** Shown in role-play, with stories and pictures marking what a good neighbour looks like.

**2.** For which two seasons is the good neighbour's importance explained?
**Answer:** Good times and hard times.

**3.** In your own words, what is trait?
**Answer:** A quality a person is known by.
