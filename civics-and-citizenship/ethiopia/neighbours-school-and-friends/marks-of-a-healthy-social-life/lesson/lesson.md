# Marks of a Healthy Social Life

## Do it first

Test your own social life against four marks as you read — strong bonds? free speech both ways? working together? respect? Mark each one honestly: strong, growing, or missing.

## The four marks

Building strong relationships with family and friends.

Expressing your own outlook and accepting others' ideas.

Cooperating and working together.

Treating each other with respect.

Pupils read the characteristics, reflect on them, and discuss each with examples in groups.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the marks of a healthy social life are four: strong relationships, expressing your outlook and accepting others' ideas, cooperating, and treating each other with respect. Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood, and name the lesson's key words: outlook, characteristic. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
