# Video script — Marks of a Healthy Social Life

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The marks of a healthy social life are four: strong relationships, expressing your outlook and accepting others' ideas, cooperating, and treating each other with respect.

Building strong relationships with family and friends.

Expressing your own outlook and accepting others' ideas.

Cooperating and working together.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
