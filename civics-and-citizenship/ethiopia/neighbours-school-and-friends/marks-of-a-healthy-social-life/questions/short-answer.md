# Marks of a Healthy Social Life — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the four marks.
**Answer:** Strong relationships with family and friends; expressing your outlook and accepting others' ideas; cooperating and working together; treating each other with respect.

**2.** How do pupils study the marks?
**Answer:** Read them, reflect on them, and discuss each with examples in groups.

**3.** In your own words, what is outlook?
**Answer:** Your way of seeing and judging things.
