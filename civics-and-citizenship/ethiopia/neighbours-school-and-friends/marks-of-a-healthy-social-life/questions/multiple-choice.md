# Marks of a Healthy Social Life — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) joining in: clubs and co-curricular activities  b) the obstacles to the common good  c) the four marks of a healthy social life  d) the ways of accommodating multiculturalism
**Answer: c) the four marks of a healthy social life** — That is the one thing this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does “outlook” mean?
a) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  b) your way of seeing and judging things  c) a mark by which a thing is known  d) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.
**Answer: b) your way of seeing and judging things** — “outlook” means: your way of seeing and judging things.

**3.** What does “characteristic” mean?
a) a mark by which a thing is known  b) your way of seeing and judging things  c) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  d) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.
**Answer: a) a mark by which a thing is known** — “characteristic” means: a mark by which a thing is known.

**4.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  b) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  c) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  d) Cooperating and working together
**Answer: d) Cooperating and working together** — It is one of this lesson's own teachings.

**5.** Which of these is true in this lesson?
a) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  b) Promises, truth and fairness are not needed between people.  c) Building strong relationships with family and friends  d) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.
**Answer: c) Building strong relationships with family and friends** — It is stated in the lesson.
