# Video script — Positive Social Interaction — Good Relationships on Purpose

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Human beings are social by nature: a positive bond is a good relationship where people share interests, thoughts, feelings, experiences and materials — ready to give and to receive.

The chapter opens with the saying that man is a social animal: people by nature seek social bonds with many others.

A good relationship matters from the home to the workplace and through the whole of life.

A positive social bond is a good relationship — one of good character, whose participants share common interests and ideas, are ready to give and receive, and share thoughts, feelings, experiences and materials.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
