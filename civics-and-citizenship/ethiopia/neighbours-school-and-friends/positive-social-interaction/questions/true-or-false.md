# Positive Social Interaction — Good Relationships on Purpose — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The chapter opens with the saying that man is a social animal: people by nature seek social bonds with many others.
**Answer: True**

**2.** A positive social bond is a good relationship .
**Answer: True**

**3.** A positive bond means taking from others and never giving anything back.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite.
