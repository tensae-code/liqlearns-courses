# Positive Social Interaction — Good Relationships on Purpose — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What do participants in a positive bond share?
**Answer:** Common interests and ideas; thoughts, feelings, experiences and materials — ready to give and receive.

**2.** What is an obstacle?
**Answer:** Whatever blocks or hinders achieving a thing.

**3.** In your own words, what is positive social bond?
**Answer:** A good relationship of good character, sharing interests and ideas.
