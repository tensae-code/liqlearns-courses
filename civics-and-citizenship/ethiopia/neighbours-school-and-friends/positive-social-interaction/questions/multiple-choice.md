# Positive Social Interaction — Good Relationships on Purpose — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) how positive interaction helps learning  b) the factors that damage a healthy social life  c) protecting the environment — everyone's responsibility  d) positive social interaction: good relationships, on purpose
**Answer: d) positive social interaction: good relationships, on purpose** — That is the one thing this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does “positive social bond” mean?
a) improper behaviour that damages a good relationship  b) whatever blocks or hinders achieving a thing  c) a good relationship of good character, sharing interests and ideas  d) sharing both ways — thoughts, feelings, experiences and materials
**Answer: c) a good relationship of good character, sharing interests and ideas** — “positive social bond” means: a good relationship of good character, sharing interests and ideas.

**3.** What does “give and receive” mean?
a) whatever blocks or hinders achieving a thing  b) sharing both ways — thoughts, feelings, experiences and materials  c) a good relationship of good character, sharing interests and ideas  d) improper behaviour that damages a good relationship
**Answer: b) sharing both ways — thoughts, feelings, experiences and materials** — “give and receive” means: sharing both ways — thoughts, feelings, experiences and materials.

**4.** What does “negative interaction” mean?
a) improper behaviour that damages a good relationship  b) a good relationship of good character, sharing interests and ideas  c) sharing both ways — thoughts, feelings, experiences and materials  d) whatever blocks or hinders achieving a thing
**Answer: a) improper behaviour that damages a good relationship** — “negative interaction” means: improper behaviour that damages a good relationship.

**5.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  b) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  c) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  d) A positive social bond is a good relationship 
**Answer: d) A positive social bond is a good relationship ** — It is one of this lesson's own teachings.
