# Positive Social Interaction — Good Relationships on Purpose

## Do it first

Start one good interaction today: share an idea, a feeling, or a thing with someone, and receive theirs openly. You have just practised this lesson's definition.

## Social by nature

The chapter opens with the saying that man is a social animal: people by nature seek social bonds with many others.

A good relationship matters from the home to the workplace and through the whole of life.

[Picture 28042 — sharing an idea, a feeling, a thing]

## Positive, negative, obstacle

A positive social bond is a good relationship — one of good character, whose participants share common interests and ideas, are ready to give and receive, and share thoughts, feelings, experiences and materials.

Its opposite is also defined: negative interaction is improper behaviour or skill that damages a good relationship or friendship.

An obstacle is whatever blocks or hinders achieving a thing.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: human beings are social by nature: a positive bond is a good relationship where people share interests, thoughts, feelings, experiences and materials — ready to give and to receive. Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood, and name the lesson's key words: positive social bond, give and receive, negative interaction. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
