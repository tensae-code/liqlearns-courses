# Hands-on — Positive Social Interaction — Good Relationships on Purpose

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Start one good interaction today: share an idea, a feeling, or a thing with someone, and receive theirs openly. You have just practised this lesson's definition.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: positive social interaction: good relationships, on purpose. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
