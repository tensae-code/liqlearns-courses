# Lesson plan — Positive Social Interaction — Good Relationships on Purpose

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Neighbours, School and Friends · **Lesson:** Positive Social Interaction — Good Relationships on Purpose
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Neighbours, School and Friends (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** human beings are social by nature: a positive bond is a good relationship where people share interests, thoughts, feelings, experiences and materials — ready to give and to receive.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the lessons before it in this title — the learner already knows the earlier circles of this course (myself, then family, neighbours, school, community and culture) as far as this title. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. ## What the learner can do when it ends
1. The chapter opens with the saying that man is a social animal.
2. A good relationship matters from the home to the workplace and through the whole of life.
3. A positive social bond is a good relationship — one of good character, whose participants share common interests and ideas, are ready to give and receive, and share thoughts, feelings, experiences and materials.
4. Its opposite is also defined.
5. An obstacle is whatever blocks or hinders achieving a thing.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson's scenes are the learner's own home, school and neighbourhood.

## If the learner is stuck
- Believes that “a positive bond means taking from others and never giving anything back” → go back to the lesson's own words: human beings are social by nature: a positive bond is a good relationship where people share interests, thoughts, feelings, experiences and materials — ready to give and to receive. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
- Gives the words but no example → ask for the lesson happening once in their own home, school or street today; the example is the proof.
