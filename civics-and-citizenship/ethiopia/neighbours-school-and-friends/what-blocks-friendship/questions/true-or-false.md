# What Blocks Friendship — and the Greedy Dog — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The chapter closes with the obstacles to positive social interaction, identified in pair and group work from a table of example….
**Answer: True**

**2.** The chapter's story anchor is the tale of the greedy (selfish) dog, which pupils read and draw the lesson from.
**Answer: True**

**3.** Misunderstanding and mistrust make friendships stronger and longer-lasting.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite.
