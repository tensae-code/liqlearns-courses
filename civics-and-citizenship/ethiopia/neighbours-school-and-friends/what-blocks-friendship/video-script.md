# Video script — What Blocks Friendship — and the Greedy Dog

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Friendship is blocked by misunderstanding, mistrust and the failure to build lasting friendship — the greedy dog is the book's warning picture.

The chapter closes with the obstacles to positive social interaction, identified in pair and group work from a table of examples, notes and demonstrations.

The activity answers point to three: misunderstanding, mistrust, and the failure to build lasting friendship.

The chapter's story anchor is the tale of the greedy (selfish) dog, which pupils read and draw the lesson from.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
