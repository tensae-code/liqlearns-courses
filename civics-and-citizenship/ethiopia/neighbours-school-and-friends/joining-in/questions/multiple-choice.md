# Joining In — Clubs and Co-curricular Activities at School — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) joining in: clubs and co-curricular activities  b) building good neighbour relations, and removing what harms them  c) the good neighbour's traits, in good times and hard  d) true words, kept words, clean hands
**Answer: a) joining in: clubs and co-curricular activities** — That is the one thing this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does “co-curricular activities” mean?
a) taking an active part, not only watching  b) trust in your own ability, grown by doing  c) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  d) the school's accompanying activities beyond lessons — clubs, sport, arts
**Answer: d) the school's accompanying activities beyond lessons — clubs, sport, arts** — “co-curricular activities” means: the school's accompanying activities beyond lessons — clubs, sport, arts.

**3.** What does “participation” mean?
a) trust in your own ability, grown by doing  b) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  c) taking an active part, not only watching  d) the school's accompanying activities beyond lessons — clubs, sport, arts
**Answer: c) taking an active part, not only watching** — “participation” means: taking an active part, not only watching.

**4.** What does “self-confidence” mean?
a) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  b) trust in your own ability, grown by doing  c) the school's accompanying activities beyond lessons — clubs, sport, arts  d) taking an active part, not only watching
**Answer: b) trust in your own ability, grown by doing** — “self-confidence” means: trust in your own ability, grown by doing.

**5.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
