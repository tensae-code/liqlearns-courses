# Joining In — Clubs and Co-curricular Activities at School — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name three fruits of active participation.
**Answer:** Any three of: physical and mental strength; responsibility; self-confidence; special skills; meeting new tasks.

**2.** What kind of participation does the chapter value?
**Answer:** Active participation — taking part, not watching.

**3.** In your own words, what is co-curricular activities?
**Answer:** The school's accompanying activities beyond lessons — clubs, sport, arts.
