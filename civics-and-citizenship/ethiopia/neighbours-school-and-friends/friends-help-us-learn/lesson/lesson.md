# Friends Help Us Learn — Working With Others

## Do it first

Try one hard task alone for five minutes. Then try it with a friend, talking as you work. Feel the difference? This lesson is about that difference.

## Interaction and learning

A whole content line is given to the importance of positive social interaction for pupils' learning.

It helps pupils build readiness to work with others.

The chapter treats behaviour and attitude change as its proper fruit — learning together changes how pupils act and think, not only what they know.
