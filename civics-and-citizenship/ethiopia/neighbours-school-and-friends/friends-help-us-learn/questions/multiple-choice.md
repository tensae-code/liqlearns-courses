# Friends Help Us Learn — Working With Others — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) building positive relationships at school  b) how positive interaction helps learning  c) debo, idir and equb — Ethiopia works together  d) good customs we keep, harmful practices we stop
**Answer: b) how positive interaction helps learning** — That is the one thing this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does “readiness” mean?
a) being prepared and willing when the moment comes  b) the way a person thinks and feels about a thing, which guides their acts  c) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  d) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.
**Answer: a) being prepared and willing when the moment comes** — “readiness” means: being prepared and willing when the moment comes.

**3.** What does “attitude” mean?
a) being prepared and willing when the moment comes  b) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  c) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  d) the way a person thinks and feels about a thing, which guides their acts
**Answer: d) the way a person thinks and feels about a thing, which guides their acts** — “attitude” means: the way a person thinks and feels about a thing, which guides their acts.

**4.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  b) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  c) It helps pupils build readiness to work with others  d) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.
**Answer: c) It helps pupils build readiness to work with others** — It is one of this lesson's own teachings.

**5.** Which of these is true in this lesson?
a) Promises, truth and fairness are not needed between people.  b) A whole content line is given to the importance of positive social interaction for pupils' learning  c) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  d) The strongest person may take whatever they want.
**Answer: b) A whole content line is given to the importance of positive social interaction for pupils' learning** — It is stated in the lesson.
