# My Responsibilities at School — and the Rights That Go With Them — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the three responsibilities.
**Answer:** Using school property properly; being considerate to others; showing good conduct.

**2.** Name the two rights.
**Answer:** The right to learn; the right to get correct information from teachers.

**3.** In your own words, what is responsibility?
**Answer:** What is yours to do, and to answer for.
