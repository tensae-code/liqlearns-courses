# My Responsibilities at School — and the Rights That Go With Them — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Use school property properly.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Pupils have the right to learn.
**Answer: True**

**3.** At school a pupil has responsibilities only; there are no rights that go with them.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite.
