# My Responsibilities at School — and the Rights That Go With Them — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) the eleven marks of being human  b) building positive relationships at school  c) responsibilities at school, and the rights that go with them  d) the traits of a respected person: humility and honesty
**Answer: c) responsibilities at school, and the rights that go with them** — That is the one thing this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does “responsibility” mean?
a) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  b) what is yours to do, and to answer for  c) what is due to you, which others must respect  d) thinking of others' feelings and needs in your acts
**Answer: b) what is yours to do, and to answer for** — “responsibility” means: what is yours to do, and to answer for.

**3.** What does “right” mean?
a) what is due to you, which others must respect  b) what is yours to do, and to answer for  c) thinking of others' feelings and needs in your acts  d) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.
**Answer: a) what is due to you, which others must respect** — “right” means: what is due to you, which others must respect.

**4.** What does “considerate” mean?
a) what is yours to do, and to answer for  b) what is due to you, which others must respect  c) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  d) thinking of others' feelings and needs in your acts
**Answer: d) thinking of others' feelings and needs in your acts** — “considerate” means: thinking of others' feelings and needs in your acts.

**5.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  b) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  c) Pupils have the right to learn  d) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.
**Answer: c) Pupils have the right to learn** — It is one of this lesson's own teachings.
