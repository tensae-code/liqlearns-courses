# Video script — My Responsibilities at School — and the Rights That Go With Them

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
At school, responsibilities and rights stand together: use property properly, be considerate, show good conduct — and you have the right to learn and to correct information.

Use school property properly.

Be considerate to others.

Show good conduct.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
