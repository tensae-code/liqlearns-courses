# Lesson plan — My Responsibilities at School — and the Rights That Go With Them

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Neighbours, School and Friends · **Lesson:** My Responsibilities at School — and the Rights That Go With Them
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Neighbours, School and Friends (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** at school, responsibilities and rights stand together: use property properly, be considerate, show good conduct — and you have the right to learn and to correct information.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the lessons before it in this title — the learner already knows the earlier circles of this course (myself, then family, neighbours, school, community and culture) as far as this title. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. ## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Use school property properly.
2. Be considerate to others.
3. Show good conduct.
4. Pupils have the right to learn.
5. Pupils have the right to get correct information from their teachers.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson's scenes are the learner's own home, school and neighbourhood.

## If the learner is stuck
- Believes that “at school a pupil has responsibilities only; there are no rights that go with them” → go back to the lesson's own words: at school, responsibilities and rights stand together: use property properly, be considerate, show good conduct — and you have the right to learn and to correct information. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
- Gives the words but no example → ask for the lesson happening once in their own home, school or street today; the example is the proof.
