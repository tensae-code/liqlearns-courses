# My Responsibilities at School — and the Rights That Go With Them

## Do it first

Name one thing you may rightly expect at school — and one thing your school may rightly expect from you. Hold both. This lesson keeps them in balance, as the book does.

## My responsibilities

Use school property properly.

Be considerate to others.

Show good conduct.

[Picture 28039 — two balanced scales — rights and responsibilities]

## The rights that go with them

Pupils have the right to learn.

Pupils have the right to get correct information from their teachers.

Rights and responsibilities are paired in one content line and one picture-discussion: each side keeps the other standing.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: at school, responsibilities and rights stand together: use property properly, be considerate, show good conduct — and you have the right to learn and to correct information. Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood, and name the lesson's key words: responsibility, right, considerate. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: responsibilities at school, and the rights that go with them.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
