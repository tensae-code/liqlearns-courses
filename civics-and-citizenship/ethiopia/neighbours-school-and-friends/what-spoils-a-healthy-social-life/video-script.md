# Video script — What Spoils a Healthy Social Life — Hatred, Exclusion, Silence

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A healthy social life is spoiled by hatred and exclusion, by silence where openness is needed, by separation, unemployment, carelessness and crime.

Hatred and exclusion.

The quality of the parent-child relationship, and parenting types and practices.

The absence of a culture of openness: without openness and open communication there can be no healthy social life, since it requires both.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
