# Lesson plan — What Spoils a Healthy Social Life — Hatred, Exclusion, Silence

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Neighbours, School and Friends · **Lesson:** What Spoils a Healthy Social Life — Hatred, Exclusion, Silence
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Neighbours, School and Friends (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a healthy social life is spoiled by hatred and exclusion, by silence where openness is needed, by separation, unemployment, carelessness and crime.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the lessons before it in this title — the learner already knows the earlier circles of this course (myself, then family, neighbours, school, community and culture) as far as this title. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short, single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09).

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Why Healthy Relationships Keep Us Well (the previous lesson in this title). Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Hatred and exclusion.
2. The quality of the parent-child relationship, and parenting types and practices.
3. The absence of a culture of openness.
4. Family separation by divorce or other causes, which increases single parenthood.
5. Unemployment.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson's scenes are the learner's own home, school and neighbourhood.

Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
