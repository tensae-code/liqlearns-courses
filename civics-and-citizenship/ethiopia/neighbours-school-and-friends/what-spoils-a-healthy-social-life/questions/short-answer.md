# What Spoils a Healthy Social Life — Hatred, Exclusion, Silence — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name three factors that damage a healthy social life.
**Answer:** Any three of: hatred and exclusion; poor parent-child relationship; parenting practices; absence of openness; family separation; unemployment; carelessness/harming others; spread of crime.

**2.** Why does openness matter so much?
**Answer:** A healthy social life requires openness and open communication — without them it cannot be had.

**3.** In your own words, what is exclusion?
**Answer:** Shutting a person or group out.
