# What Spoils a Healthy Social Life — Hatred, Exclusion, Silence — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) the factors that damage a healthy social life  b) how we respect elders and religious fathers  c) the three goods of local culture  d) joining in: clubs and co-curricular activities
**Answer: a) the factors that damage a healthy social life** — That is the one thing this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does “exclusion” mean?
a) speaking and listening honestly — a culture of open communication  b) one parent raising children alone, often after separation  c) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  d) shutting a person or group out
**Answer: d) shutting a person or group out** — “exclusion” means: shutting a person or group out.

**3.** What does “openness” mean?
a) one parent raising children alone, often after separation  b) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  c) speaking and listening honestly — a culture of open communication  d) shutting a person or group out
**Answer: c) speaking and listening honestly — a culture of open communication** — “openness” means: speaking and listening honestly — a culture of open communication.

**4.** What does “single parenthood” mean?
a) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  b) one parent raising children alone, often after separation  c) shutting a person or group out  d) speaking and listening honestly — a culture of open communication
**Answer: b) one parent raising children alone, often after separation** — “single parenthood” means: one parent raising children alone, often after separation.

**5.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Unemployment  b) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  c) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  d) The strongest person may take whatever they want.
