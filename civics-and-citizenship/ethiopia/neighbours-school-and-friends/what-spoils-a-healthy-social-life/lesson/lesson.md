# What Spoils a Healthy Social Life — Hatred, Exclusion, Silence

## Do it first

Picture a circle of friends with one child standing outside it, uninvited. Hold that picture. This lesson names everything that keeps that child outside — and what opens the circle.

## Inside the family

Hatred and exclusion.

The quality of the parent-child relationship, and parenting types and practices.

The absence of a culture of openness: without openness and open communication there can be no healthy social life, since it requires both.

Family separation by divorce or other causes, which increases single parenthood.

[Picture 28049 — outside the circle — the circle opening]

## Around the family

Unemployment.

Carelessness and harming others.

The spread of crime.

Hatred and exclusion between individuals and groups make it hard for them to live together in a peaceful environment.

[GIF 28050 — the circle opening to let the child in]

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: a healthy social life is spoiled by hatred and exclusion, by silence where openness is needed, by separation, unemployment, carelessness and crime. Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood, and name the lesson's key words: exclusion, openness, single parenthood. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: the factors that damage a healthy social life.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
