# Neighbourliness — Friends Who Live Next Door

## Do it first

Name the people who live nearest your home. When did you last greet them, help them, or share something with them? That relationship — this lesson gives it its name.

## What neighbourliness is

Neighbourliness is the friendly, cooperative relationship between neighbours.

Pupils begin by talking in pairs about what they know of their own neighbours — the lesson starts from real doors and real names.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: neighbourliness is the friendly, cooperative relationship between neighbours. Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood, and name the lesson's key words: neighbourliness, neighbour, cooperative. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: neighbourliness: friendly, cooperative neighbours.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
