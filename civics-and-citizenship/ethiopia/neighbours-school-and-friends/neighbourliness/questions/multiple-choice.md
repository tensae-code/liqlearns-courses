# Neighbourliness — Friends Who Live Next Door — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) why cooperation and teamwork matter  b) the moral values and principles a family lives by  c) responsibilities at school, and the rights that go with them  d) neighbourliness: friendly, cooperative neighbours
**Answer: d) neighbourliness: friendly, cooperative neighbours** — That is the one thing this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does “neighbourliness” mean?
a) working together willingly  b) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  c) the friendly, cooperative relationship between neighbours  d) a person who lives next door or nearby
**Answer: c) the friendly, cooperative relationship between neighbours** — “neighbourliness” means: the friendly, cooperative relationship between neighbours.

**3.** What does “neighbour” mean?
a) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  b) a person who lives next door or nearby  c) the friendly, cooperative relationship between neighbours  d) working together willingly
**Answer: b) a person who lives next door or nearby** — “neighbour” means: a person who lives next door or nearby.

**4.** What does “cooperative” mean?
a) working together willingly  b) the friendly, cooperative relationship between neighbours  c) a person who lives next door or nearby  d) The strongest person may take whatever they want.
**Answer: a) working together willingly** — “cooperative” means: working together willingly.

**5.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  b) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  c) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  d) Pupils begin by talking in pairs about what they know of their own neighbours 
**Answer: d) Pupils begin by talking in pairs about what they know of their own neighbours ** — It is one of this lesson's own teachings.
