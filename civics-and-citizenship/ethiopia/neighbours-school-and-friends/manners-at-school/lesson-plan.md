# Lesson plan — Manners at School — What School Discipline Means

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Neighbours, School and Friends · **Lesson:** Manners at School — What School Discipline Means
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Neighbours, School and Friends (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** school discipline is guidance that brings a distinct standard of conduct — given so behaviour problems are prevented, not just punished.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the lessons before it in this title — the learner already knows the earlier circles of this course (myself, then family, neighbours, school, community and culture) as far as this title. It needs nothing else.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. School discipline is strategic guidance given to the pupil to bring a distinct standard of conduct.
2. It passes on knowledge and skill so as to prevent behaviour problems in children at school.
3. Pupils discuss the school's code of conduct in groups — the rules are studied, not just feared.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson's scenes are the learner's own home, school and neighbourhood.
