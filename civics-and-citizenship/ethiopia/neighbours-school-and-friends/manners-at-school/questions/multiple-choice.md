# Manners at School — What School Discipline Means — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) what school discipline means: guidance to a standard of conduct  b) the dignity we are born with, and the dignity we earn  c) the obstacles to the common good  d) using public property properly, keeping it for tomorrow
**Answer: a) what school discipline means: guidance to a standard of conduct** — That is the one thing this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does “discipline (at school)” mean?
a) a school's written rules of behaviour  b) the level of behaviour a community agrees to keep  c) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  d) guidance that brings a distinct standard of conduct
**Answer: d) guidance that brings a distinct standard of conduct** — “discipline (at school)” means: guidance that brings a distinct standard of conduct.

**3.** What does “code of conduct” mean?
a) the level of behaviour a community agrees to keep  b) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  c) a school's written rules of behaviour  d) guidance that brings a distinct standard of conduct
**Answer: c) a school's written rules of behaviour** — “code of conduct” means: a school's written rules of behaviour.

**4.** What does “standard of conduct” mean?
a) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  b) the level of behaviour a community agrees to keep  c) guidance that brings a distinct standard of conduct  d) a school's written rules of behaviour
**Answer: b) the level of behaviour a community agrees to keep** — “standard of conduct” means: the level of behaviour a community agrees to keep.

**5.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) It passes on knowledge and skill so as to prevent behaviour problems in children at school  b) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  c) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  d) The strongest person may take whatever they want.
**Answer: a) It passes on knowledge and skill so as to prevent behaviour problems in children at school** — It is one of this lesson's own teachings.
