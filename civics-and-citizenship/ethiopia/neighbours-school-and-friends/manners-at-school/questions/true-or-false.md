# Manners at School — What School Discipline Means — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** School discipline is strategic guidance given to the pupil to bring a distinct standard of conduct.
**Answer: True**

**2.** School discipline exists only to punish after problems happen; it has nothing to do with guidance or prevention.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite.
