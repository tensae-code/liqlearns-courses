# Manners at School — What School Discipline Means — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is school discipline?
**Answer:** Strategic guidance given to the pupil to bring a distinct standard of conduct.

**2.** What do pupils discuss in groups?
**Answer:** The school's code of conduct.

**3.** In your own words, what is discipline (at school)?
**Answer:** Guidance that brings a distinct standard of conduct.
