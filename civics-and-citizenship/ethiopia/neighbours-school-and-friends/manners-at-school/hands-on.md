# Hands-on — Manners at School — What School Discipline Means

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Read your school's code of conduct if you can find it — or name three rules of conduct your school lives by. This lesson explains what those rules are for.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: what school discipline means: guidance to a standard of conduct. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
