# Video script — Manners at School — What School Discipline Means

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
School discipline is guidance that brings a distinct standard of conduct — given so behaviour problems are prevented, not just punished.

School discipline is strategic guidance given to the pupil to bring a distinct standard of conduct.

It passes on knowledge and skill so as to prevent behaviour problems in children at school.

Pupils discuss the school's code of conduct in groups — the rules are studied, not just feared.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
