# Video script — Good Relationships at School — With Classmates and Teachers

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Positive relationships at school — with classmates and teachers — are built on purpose, and their importance can be explained.

Pupils look at a picture and discuss good relationships at school in pairs or groups.

Then they judge statements about building positive relationships — deciding each one true or false, with reasons.

The chapter outcomes ask pupils to explain the importance of positive relationships at school.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
