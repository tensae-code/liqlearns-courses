# Good Relationships at School — With Classmates and Teachers

## Do it first

Do one relationship-building act today before you read: share a material, invite someone in, or settle a small disagreement by talking. Then read how the book teaches it.

## Building positive relationships

Pupils look at a picture and discuss good relationships at school in pairs or groups.

Then they judge statements about building positive relationships — deciding each one true or false, with reasons.

[Picture 28040 — sharing materials, settling by talking]

## Why they matter

The chapter outcomes ask pupils to explain the importance of positive relationships at school.

Good relationships make learning possible together: help is near, disagreements can be talked through, and no one is shut out.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: positive relationships at school — with classmates and teachers — are built on purpose, and their importance can be explained. Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood, and name the lesson's key words: relationship, importance. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
