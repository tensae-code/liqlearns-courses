# Good Relationships at School — With Classmates and Teachers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) cultural values: the principles a community stands on  b) building positive relationships at school  c) justice: each person given what is due  d) the traits of a respected person: humility and honesty
**Answer: b) building positive relationships at school** — That is the one thing this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does “relationship” mean?
a) the connection between people who know and deal with each other  b) why a thing matters — what it makes possible  c) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  d) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.
**Answer: a) the connection between people who know and deal with each other** — “relationship” means: the connection between people who know and deal with each other.

**3.** What does “importance” mean?
a) the connection between people who know and deal with each other  b) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  c) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  d) why a thing matters — what it makes possible
**Answer: d) why a thing matters — what it makes possible** — “importance” means: why a thing matters — what it makes possible.

**4.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  b) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  c) The chapter outcomes ask pupils to explain the importance of positive relationships at school  d) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.
**Answer: c) The chapter outcomes ask pupils to explain the importance of positive relationships at school** — It is one of this lesson's own teachings.

**5.** Which of these is true in this lesson?
a) Promises, truth and fairness are not needed between people.  b) Pupils look at a picture and discuss good relationships at school in pairs or groups  c) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  d) The strongest person may take whatever they want.
**Answer: b) Pupils look at a picture and discuss good relationships at school in pairs or groups** — It is stated in the lesson.
