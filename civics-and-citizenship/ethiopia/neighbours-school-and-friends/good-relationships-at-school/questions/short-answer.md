# Good Relationships at School — With Classmates and Teachers — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** How does the class study good relationships at school?
**Answer:** By looking at a picture and discussing in pairs or groups, then judging statements about building positive relationships.

**2.** What must pupils be able to explain as an outcome?
**Answer:** The importance of positive relationships at school.

**3.** In your own words, what is relationship?
**Answer:** The connection between people who know and deal with each other.
