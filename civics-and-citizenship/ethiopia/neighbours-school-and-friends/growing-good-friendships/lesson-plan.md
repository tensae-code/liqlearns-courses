# Lesson plan — Growing Good Friendships — Sharing, Trust, Keeping Confidences

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Neighbours, School and Friends · **Lesson:** Growing Good Friendships — Sharing, Trust, Keeping Confidences
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 4 — Neighbours, School and Friends (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** good friendships grow by named traits: sociability, openness, respect, trust, caring, keeping confidences and team spirit — ready to give and to receive.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the lessons before it in this title — the learner already knows the earlier circles of this course (myself, then family, neighbours, school, community and culture) as far as this title. It needs nothing else.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. A shared interest or idea in particular.
2. Readiness to give and to receive — sharing thoughts, feelings, experiences and materials.
3. From the activity answers, the acceptable traits.
4. The chapter teaches ways of increasing positive social interaction at school and among the public — friendship is grown on purpose, in pairs and groups.
5. One activity turns on what friends like and dislike, and on speaking about hidden wants inside a friendship — openness practised, not just named.

## What you need
