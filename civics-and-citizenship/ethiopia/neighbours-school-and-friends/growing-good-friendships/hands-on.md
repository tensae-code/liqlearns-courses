# Hands-on — Growing Good Friendships — Sharing, Trust, Keeping Confidences

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Keep one confidence today: something a friend told you in trust stays with you. Notice how it feels to be a safe person. This lesson lists the traits that make friends safe for each other.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the traits that grow good friendships. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
