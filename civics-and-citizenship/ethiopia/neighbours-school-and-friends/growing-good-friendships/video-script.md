# Video script — Growing Good Friendships — Sharing, Trust, Keeping Confidences

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

This lesson is about one thing.
Good friendships grow by named traits: sociability, openness, respect, trust, caring, keeping confidences and team spirit — ready to give and to receive.

A shared interest or idea in particular.

Readiness to give and to receive — sharing thoughts, feelings, experiences and materials.

From the activity answers, the acceptable traits: sociability, openness, keeping respect, trust, caring for others, keeping confidences and team spirit.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
