# Growing Good Friendships — Sharing, Trust, Keeping Confidences — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) community values: the beliefs we share  b) why self-control matters, and that it grows by practice  c) the traits that grow good friendships  d) why the common good matters: each other, safety and law
**Answer: c) the traits that grow good friendships** — That is the one thing this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does “confidence (kept)” mean?
a) standing with your group and working for its shared aim  b) a secret told in trust, which a good friend does not pass on  c) being easy and glad in company  d) speaking honestly about what you think and want
**Answer: b) a secret told in trust, which a good friend does not pass on** — “confidence (kept)” means: a secret told in trust, which a good friend does not pass on.

**3.** What does “sociability” mean?
a) being easy and glad in company  b) a secret told in trust, which a good friend does not pass on  c) speaking honestly about what you think and want  d) standing with your group and working for its shared aim
**Answer: a) being easy and glad in company** — “sociability” means: being easy and glad in company.

**4.** What does “openness” mean?
a) a secret told in trust, which a good friend does not pass on  b) being easy and glad in company  c) standing with your group and working for its shared aim  d) speaking honestly about what you think and want
**Answer: d) speaking honestly about what you think and want** — “openness” means: speaking honestly about what you think and want.

**5.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  b) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  c) From the activity answers, the acceptable traits  d) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.
**Answer: c) From the activity answers, the acceptable traits** — It is one of this lesson's own teachings.
