# Growing Good Friendships — Sharing, Trust, Keeping Confidences — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name four traits of a good friendship.
**Answer:** Any four of: sociability, openness, keeping respect, trust, caring for others, keeping confidences, team spirit.

**2.** What does readiness to give and receive cover?
**Answer:** Sharing thoughts, feelings, experiences and materials.

**3.** In your own words, what is confidence (kept)?
**Answer:** A secret told in trust, which a good friend does not pass on.
