# Moral Integrity — Inside and Outside in Agreement — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1.** Integrity is a pattern of life, not a single good day.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Integrity means your thoughts and actions may disagree as long as nobody sees.
**Answer: False**

**3.** Honesty and self-confidence are named among integrity's components.
**Answer: True**
