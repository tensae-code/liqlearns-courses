# Moral Integrity — Inside and Outside in Agreement — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Moral integrity is —
a) lasting agreement between inner thought and outer action  b) saying fine words whatever you do  c) pleasing everyone around you
**Answer: a) lasting agreement between inner thought and outer action**

**2.** Which is a component of integrity?
a) Changing your mind with every wind  b) Hiding what you believe  c) Steadfastness in thought
**Answer: c) Steadfastness in thought**

**3.** A person of integrity is —
a) good only when watched  b) the same person inside and outside  c) one person in public, another in private
**Answer: b) the same person inside and outside**

**4.** Living by your ___ is part of integrity.
a) basic values  b) moods of the moment  c) friends' demands
**Answer: a) basic values**
