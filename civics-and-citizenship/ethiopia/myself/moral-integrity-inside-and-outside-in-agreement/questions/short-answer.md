# Moral Integrity — Inside and Outside in Agreement — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1.** Name three components of integrity.
**Answer:** Any three of: steadfastness in thought, basic values, honesty, self-confidence, faith.

**2.** Explain 'inside and outside in agreement' in your own words.
**Answer:** What you believe and value inside is what your words and actions do outside, as a lasting pattern.
