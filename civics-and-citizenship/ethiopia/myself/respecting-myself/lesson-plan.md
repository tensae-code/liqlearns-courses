# Lesson plan — Respecting Myself — What It Is, Why It Matters, How It Grows

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Myself · **Lesson:** Respecting Myself — What It Is, Why It Matters, How It Grows
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — Myself (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** respecting yourself — knowing your own worth, showing it in body, word, time, goals and boundaries, and growing it day by day.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the lessons before it in this title — the learner already knows the earlier circles of this course (myself, then family, neighbours, school, community and culture) as far as this title. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The concept, its importance, ways of showing it, how it is built** — Self-respect is valuing yourself
2. **Ways I respect myself** — Respect your beliefs and values — hold to what you know is right; do not change it just to please anyone.
3. **Showing self-respect** — Self-respect is proved in pictures and acts, not only in words

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: When Self-Control Is Missing — What Follows (the previous lesson in this title). Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Self-respect is valuing yourself.
2. It matters because respecting others begins from respecting yourself — self-respect is the basis of a person's moral character and growth.
3. It is shown in daily acts.
4. And it is built.
5. Respect your beliefs and values — hold to what you know is right; do not change it just to please anyone.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson's scenes are the learner's own home, school and neighbourhood.

## If the learner is stuck
- Believes that “respecting yourself means changing your beliefs whenever it pleases other people” → go back to the lesson's own words: respecting yourself — knowing your own worth, showing it in body, word, time, goals and boundaries, and growing it day by day. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
- Gives the words but no example → ask for the lesson happening once in their own home, school or street today; the example is the proof.
- This lesson's next door: the next lesson in Myself (a different thing, its own lesson).
