# Respecting Myself — What It Is, Why It Matters, How It Grows

## Do it first

Stand tall for a moment. Say silently three things that are good and true about you. Now sit down and read: this lesson is about treating your own self with that same respect, every day, in what you do.

## Sub lesson 1 — The concept, its importance, ways of showing it, how it is built

Self-respect is valuing yourself: knowing that your life, your body, your word and your time are worth care.

It matters because respecting others begins from respecting yourself — self-respect is the basis of a person's moral character and growth.

It is shown in daily acts: keeping clean, dressing neatly, keeping your word, finishing your tasks.

And it is built: by practice, by honest effort, and by learning from failures instead of being crushed by them.

## Sub lesson 2 — Ways I respect myself

Respect your beliefs and values — hold to what you know is right; do not change it just to please anyone.

Respect your body — care for it, live healthily, and make exercise a way of life.

Respect your surroundings — keep them clean and beautiful.

Respect your wishes and feelings — share your real feelings openly and honestly.

Respect your word — be firm in honesty; what you promise, do.

Respect your personal boundaries — know them, and be able to say no to what harms you.

Respect your fears and your failures — face fears rather than fleeing; treat failures as feedback and learn from them.

Respect your time — use it effectively; respect your goals and dreams — list them in order and start from the first.

Respect the advice of your elders, and respect your weaknesses — take them as chances to grow.

Respect your opportunities — say yes to new experiences; and respect your thoughts — let them range, then train them toward the positive.

## Sub lesson 3 — Showing self-respect

Self-respect is proved in pictures and acts, not only in words: a child washing, dressing neatly, refusing a harmful dare, finishing a task.

The chapter's picture work asks you to connect each way of showing self-respect to what a picture shows.

The safe child is part of the showing: a child who respects himself also guards his own safety — the next lesson takes this up in full.

## Say it to your companion
