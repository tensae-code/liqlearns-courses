# Hands-on — Respecting Myself — What It Is, Why It Matters, How It Grows

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Stand tall for a moment. Say silently three things that are good and true about you. Now sit down and read: this lesson is about treating your own self with that same respect, every day, in what you do.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: what self-respect is, why it matters, and how it is shown and built. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
