# Respecting Myself — What It Is, Why It Matters, How It Grows — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Self-respect is valuing yourself: knowing that your life, your body, your word and your time are worth care.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Respect your beliefs and values .
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Respecting yourself means changing your beliefs whenever it pleases other people.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite.
