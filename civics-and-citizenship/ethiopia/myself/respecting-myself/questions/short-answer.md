# Respecting Myself — What It Is, Why It Matters, How It Grows — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does self-respect matter beyond yourself?
**Answer:** Respecting others begins from respecting oneself; it is the basis of moral character and growth.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Name three ways of respecting yourself.
**Answer:** Any three of the ways in Sub lesson 2 — e.g. respect your body, respect your word, respect your time.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words, what is self-respect?
**Answer:** Valuing yourself — your body, word, time and goals are worth care.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Name one thing this part of the lesson teaches.
**Answer:** Self-respect is proved in pictures and acts, not only in words.
