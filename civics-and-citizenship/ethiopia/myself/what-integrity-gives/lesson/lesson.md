# What Integrity Gives

## Do it first

Remember a promise someone kept to you. Remember how it changed what you felt about that person. Now remember a promise that was broken. This lesson is about what the kept kind gives — to you and to everyone around you.

## The benefits, for person and society

Integrity pays — not in coins, but in the things a life runs on. It strengthens social bonds: people draw closer to someone whose word holds. It speeds a community's development, because work can be built on trusted people. It grows people's problem-solving capacity, since problems surface honestly instead of hiding. And it wins trust and respect.

## The benefits in class

In the classroom the same gifts appear in small size: teachers trust the pupil of integrity, and friendship among pupils grows better and stronger. Trust is not given once and kept forever — it is the harvest of integrity practised again and again.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion two benefits of integrity for a society and one benefit in a classroom. Then name one person you trust, and what they did to earn it.

## Do It Yourself

1. Keep one promise today, exactly as you gave it.
2. Tell the truth promptly in one place where hiding would be easier.
3. Thank one person whose integrity you trust, and tell them why.
