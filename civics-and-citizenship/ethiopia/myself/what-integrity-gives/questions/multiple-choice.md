# What Integrity Gives — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Integrity strengthens —
a) social bonds  b) suspicion between neighbours  c) excuses
**Answer: a) social bonds**

**2.** A community develops faster when —
a) nobody believes anyone  b) promises mean nothing  c) its people can be trusted
**Answer: c) its people can be trusted**

**3.** In class, integrity wins —
a) the right to cheat quietly  b) teachers' trust and better friendship  c) permission to skip tasks
**Answer: b) teachers' trust and better friendship**

**4.** Problems are solved better where integrity lives because —
a) problems surface honestly instead of hiding  b) problems are blamed on strangers  c) nobody mentions problems
**Answer: a) problems surface honestly instead of hiding**
