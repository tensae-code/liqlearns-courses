# Self-Confidence and Self-Control — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Self-confidence is —
a) trusting you can do a thing and attempting it  b) knowing you will never fail  c) waiting for others to do it
**Answer: a) trusting you can do a thing and attempting it**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Self-control is —
a) never feeling angry or eager  b) controlling other people  c) holding back impulses and governing your own behaviour
**Answer: c) holding back impulses and governing your own behaviour**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which shows self-control?
a) Shouting so you get your way  b) Pausing and breathing instead of snatching  c) Grabbing first and thinking later
**Answer: b) Pausing and breathing instead of snatching**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** A confident child faced with a question —
a) raises a hand and tries an answer  b) hides so the teacher cannot see them  c) copies a neighbour silently
**Answer: a) raises a hand and tries an answer**
