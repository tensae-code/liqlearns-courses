# Self-Confidence and Self-Control — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Confidence means being sure you will succeed every single time.
**Answer: False**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Self-control is built in small everyday moments.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Trying, even when the answer may be wrong, grows ability.
**Answer: True**
