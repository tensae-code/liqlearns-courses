# Self-Confidence and Self-Control — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Define self-control in your own words.
**Answer:** Holding back your impulses and governing your own behaviour — pausing and choosing instead of rushing.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What does a self-confident child do when a question is asked?
**Answer:** Raises a hand and attempts an answer.
