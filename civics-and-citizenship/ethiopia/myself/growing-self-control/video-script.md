# Video script — Growing Self-Control — Why It Matters and How to Build It

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Self-control matters for a child's whole life, and it can be grown — by practice, discussion and daily small acts.

Self-control is the foundation a child's other good habits stand on: a child who can guide himself can learn, play fairly, and be trusted.

The chapter teaches the importance of self-control as its own topic — it matters enough to be named, discussed and practised, not left to chance.

Self-control can be improved — it is built, not simply born.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
