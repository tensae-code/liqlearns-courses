# Growing Self-Control — Why It Matters and How to Build It

## Do it first

Think of something you could not do well at first — tying shoes, reading a hard word — that practice made easier. Self-control grows the same way. Name one small act of self-control you could practise this week.

## Why self-control matters

Self-control is the foundation a child's other good habits stand on: a child who can guide himself can learn, play fairly, and be trusted.

The chapter teaches the importance of self-control as its own topic — it matters enough to be named, discussed and practised, not left to chance.

## How self-control is built

Self-control can be improved — it is built, not simply born.

In class, pupils discuss in short bursts how a child's self-control can be improved, and why it matters, in between the other work of the chapter.

The way of growing it is practice: small right acts, repeated daily, until guiding yourself becomes a habit.
