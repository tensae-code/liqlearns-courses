# Lesson plan — Growing Self-Control — Why It Matters and How to Build It

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Myself · **Lesson:** Growing Self-Control — Why It Matters and How to Build It
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — Myself (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** self-control matters for a child's whole life, and it can be grown — by practice, discussion and daily small acts.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the lessons before it in this title — the learner already knows the earlier circles of this course (myself, then family, neighbours, school, community and culture) as far as this title. It needs nothing else.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Self-Control — What It Is and How a Self-Controlled Child Acts (the previous lesson in this title). Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Self-control is the foundation a child's other good habits stand on.
2. The chapter teaches the importance of self-control as its own topic — it matters enough to be named, discussed and practised, not left to chance.
3. Self-control can be improved — it is built, not simply born.
4. In class, pupils discuss in short bursts how a child's self-control can be improved, and why it matters, in between the other work of the chapter.
5. The way of growing it is practice.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson's scenes are the learner's own home, school and neighbourhood.
