# Growing Self-Control — Why It Matters and How to Build It — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) the importance of accommodating diversity  b) how we respect elders and religious fathers  c) why self-control matters, and that it grows by practice  d) the traits that grow good friendships
**Answer: c) why self-control matters, and that it grows by practice** — That is the one thing this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does “habit” mean?
a) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  b) a thing you do so often, by practice, that it becomes your usual way  c) doing a thing again and again to grow strong in it  d) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.
**Answer: b) a thing you do so often, by practice, that it becomes your usual way** — “habit” means: a thing you do so often, by practice, that it becomes your usual way.

**3.** What does “practice” mean?
a) doing a thing again and again to grow strong in it  b) a thing you do so often, by practice, that it becomes your usual way  c) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  d) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.
**Answer: a) doing a thing again and again to grow strong in it** — “practice” means: doing a thing again and again to grow strong in it.

**4.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  b) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  c) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  d) Self-control can be improved 
**Answer: d) Self-control can be improved ** — It is one of this lesson's own teachings.

**5.** Which of these is true in this lesson?
a) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  b) Promises, truth and fairness are not needed between people.  c) Self-control is the foundation a child's other good habits stand on  d) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.
**Answer: c) Self-control is the foundation a child's other good habits stand on** — It is stated in the lesson.
