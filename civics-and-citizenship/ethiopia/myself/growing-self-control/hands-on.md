# Hands-on — Growing Self-Control — Why It Matters and How to Build It

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Think of something you could not do well at first — tying shoes, reading a hard word — that practice made easier. Self-control grows the same way. Name one small act of self-control you could practise this week.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: why self-control matters, and that it grows by practice. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
