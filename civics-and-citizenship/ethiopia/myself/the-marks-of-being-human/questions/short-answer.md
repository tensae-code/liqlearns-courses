# The Marks of Being Human — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the four marks in the first part of this lesson.
**Answer:** Love, compassion, empathy, charity.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give one real example of volunteering.
**Answer:** Any one of: Green Legacy planting, cleaning the surroundings, giving blood — or another unpaid service of time and skill.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words, what is compassion (ርኅራሄ)?
**Answer:** Deeply understanding another's harm and wishing it eased.
