# The Marks of Being Human — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Love is the strongest feeling for a person or a thing, holding attachment, commitment, closeness and desire. We can tell apart ….
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Volunteering is serving other people's benefit with your time and skill, expecting no payment. Its examples are the Green Legac….
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Empathy means feeling sorry for yourself when your own plans fail.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite.
