# The Marks of Being Human — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach?
a) the eleven marks of being human  b) justice: each person given what is due  c) doing good for others, at school and at home  d) neighbourliness: friendly, cooperative neighbours
**Answer: a) the eleven marks of being human** — That is the one thing this lesson teaches.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What does “compassion (ርኅራሄ)” mean?
a) sharing others' feelings and seeking the solution with them  b) good-doing — the highest expression of love  c) serving others with your time and skill, expecting no payment  d) deeply understanding another's harm and wishing it eased
**Answer: d) deeply understanding another's harm and wishing it eased** — “compassion (ርኅራሄ)” means: deeply understanding another's harm and wishing it eased.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** What does “empathy (ሀዘኔታ)” mean?
a) good-doing — the highest expression of love  b) serving others with your time and skill, expecting no payment  c) sharing others' feelings and seeking the solution with them  d) deeply understanding another's harm and wishing it eased
**Answer: c) sharing others' feelings and seeking the solution with them** — “empathy (ሀዘኔታ)” means: sharing others' feelings and seeking the solution with them.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** What does “charity (በጎ አድራጊነት)” mean?
a) serving others with your time and skill, expecting no payment  b) good-doing — the highest expression of love  c) deeply understanding another's harm and wishing it eased  d) sharing others' feelings and seeking the solution with them
**Answer: b) good-doing — the highest expression of love** — “charity (በጎ አድራጊነት)” means: good-doing — the highest expression of love.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Politeness, or gentleness (ጨዋነት), is showing respect to others   b) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  c) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  d) The strongest person may take whatever they want.
**Answer: a) Politeness, or gentleness (ጨዋነት), is showing respect to others ** — It is one of this lesson's own teachings.
