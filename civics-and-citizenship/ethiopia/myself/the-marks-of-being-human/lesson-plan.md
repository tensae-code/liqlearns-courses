# Lesson plan — The Marks of Being Human

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Myself · **Lesson:** The Marks of Being Human
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — Myself (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the marks of a truly human life: love, compassion, empathy and charity — and volunteering, politeness, brotherhood, patience, peace, respect and fairness.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the lessons before it in this title — the learner already knows the earlier circles of this course (myself, then family, neighbours, school, community and culture) as far as this title. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Love, compassion, empathy, charity** — Love is the strongest feeling for a person or a thing, holding attachment, commitment, closeness and desire. We can tell apart love of self, family love, a friend's love, love of work and love of country.
2. **Volunteering, politeness, brotherhood, patience, peace, respect, fairness** — Volunteering is serving other people's benefit with your time and skill, expecting no payment. Its examples are the Green Legacy planting, cleaning the surroundings, and giving blood.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Human Dignity — the Dignity We Are Born With and the Dignity We Earn (the previous lesson in this title). Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Love is the strongest feeling for a person or a thing, holding attachment, commitment, closeness and desire. We can tell apart love of self, family love, a friend's love, love of work and love of country.
2. Compassion (ርኅራሄ) is deeply understanding another person's harm and wishing it eased — wanting to help even when helping is not easy or practical.
3. Empathy (ሀዘኔታ) is sharing other people's feelings.
4. Charity, or good-doing (በጎ አድራጊነት), is called the highest expression of love. It is also the name of the organisation that gathers and gives money to help the disabled, the poor and the sick.
5. Volunteering is serving other people's benefit with your time and skill, expecting no payment. Its examples are the Green Legacy planting, cleaning the surroundings, and giving blood.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson's scenes are the learner's own home, school and neighbourhood.

Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
- Gives the words but no example → ask for the lesson happening once in their own home, school or street today; the example is the proof.
