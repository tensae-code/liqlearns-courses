# Hands-on — The Marks of Being Human

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Think of the kindest person you know. Name three things that person does that show their kindness. As you read, check how many of the marks in this lesson match your three things.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the eleven marks of being human. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
