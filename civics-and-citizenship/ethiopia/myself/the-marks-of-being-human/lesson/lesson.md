# The Marks of Being Human

## Do it first

Think of the kindest person you know. Name three things that person does that show their kindness. As you read, check how many of the marks in this lesson match your three things.

## Sub lesson 1 — Love, compassion, empathy, charity

Love is the strongest feeling for a person or a thing, holding attachment, commitment, closeness and desire. We can tell apart love of self, family love, a friend's love, love of work and love of country.

Compassion (ርኅራሄ) is deeply understanding another person's harm and wishing it eased — wanting to help even when helping is not easy or practical.

Empathy (ሀዘኔታ) is sharing other people's feelings: entering into their problem and looking for its solution with them.

Charity, or good-doing (በጎ አድራጊነት), is called the highest expression of love. It is also the name of the organisation that gathers and gives money to help the disabled, the poor and the sick.

## Sub lesson 2 — Volunteering, politeness, brotherhood, patience, peace, respect, fairness

Volunteering is serving other people's benefit with your time and skill, expecting no payment. Its examples are the Green Legacy planting, cleaning the surroundings, and giving blood.

Politeness, or gentleness (ጨዋነት), is showing respect to others — caring for a person's identity, wishes and belief without degrading them. Humility and good behaviour are its marks.

Brotherhood and sisterhood are groups of people formed on shared goals and interests — including people who work or learn in the same place.

Patience is bearing things calmly, without complaint.

Peace is social friendship and unity, the absence of suffering and of rights violations — commonly, the absence of conflict or war. Inner peace is peace of mind; outer peace is peace between people, groups and nations.

Respect is the ability to honour and value another person, in word or in deed.

Fairness is reasonable and just treatment of people, applying accepted laws and principles — under it, all people must be seen equally.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the marks of a truly human life: love, compassion, empathy and charity — and volunteering, politeness, brotherhood, patience, peace, respect and fairness. Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood, and name the lesson's key words: compassion (ርኅራሄ), empathy (ሀዘኔታ), charity (በጎ አድራጊነት). Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
