# Video script — The Marks of Being Human

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The marks of a truly human life: love, compassion, empathy and charity — and volunteering, politeness, brotherhood, patience, peace, respect and fairness.

Love is the strongest feeling for a person or a thing, holding attachment, commitment, closeness and desire. We can tell apart love of self, family love, a friend's love, love of work and love of country.

Compassion (ርኅራሄ) is deeply understanding another person's harm and wishing it eased — wanting to help even when helping is not easy or practical.

Empathy (ሀዘኔታ) is sharing other people's feelings: entering into their problem and looking for its solution with them.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
