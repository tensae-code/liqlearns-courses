# Signs of Self-Worth and Standing on Your Own

## Do it first

Try it now: do one small thing entirely by yourself that you often ask others to do for you — tie, carry, tidy, or finish something. Notice how it feels to stand on your own.

## Sub lesson 1 — Signs of self-worth

A person who values themselves shows it. The signs are visible: they hold themselves with quiet confidence, they speak about themselves truthfully — neither boasting nor shrinking — they try things, and they do not collapse when something is hard. Watch for these marks in yourself and in others: self-worth is not hidden; it walks around in daily behaviour.

## Sub lesson 2 — Standing on your own

Self-reliance is self-worth in action: doing for yourself what you can do — tying your own shoes, carrying your own bag, finishing your own task — and saying what you think, honestly and respectfully, instead of only echoing others. Standing on your own does not mean refusing help when you truly need it. It means not handing over to others the things that are yours to do.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion two signs of self-worth you have seen in a real person. Then say one thing you will do for yourself this week instead of asking someone else.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do one task today that is yours to do, without asking for help you do not need.
2. Say what you truly think in one conversation — politely, but honestly.
3. At the end of the day, name one sign of self-worth you showed.
