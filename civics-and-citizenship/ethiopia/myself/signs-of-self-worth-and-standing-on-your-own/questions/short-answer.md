# Signs of Self-Worth and Standing on Your Own — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two visible signs of a person who values themselves.
**Answer:** Any two of: quiet confidence, truthful speech about themselves, trying hard things, not collapsing when something is hard.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does a self-reliant child do?
**Answer:** Does for themselves what they can do, and says what they think honestly and respectfully.
