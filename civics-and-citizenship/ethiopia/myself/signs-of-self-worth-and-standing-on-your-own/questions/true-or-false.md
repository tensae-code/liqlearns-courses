# Signs of Self-Worth and Standing on Your Own — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Tying your own shoes and carrying your own bag can show self-reliance.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Standing on your own means you must refuse help even when you truly need it.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Self-worth shows in daily behaviour, not only in words.
**Answer: True**
