# Signs of Self-Worth and Standing on Your Own — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which is a sign of self-worth?
a) Trying a hard thing instead of giving up at once  b) Boasting about yourself all day  c) Shrinking so nobody notices you
**Answer: a) Trying a hard thing instead of giving up at once**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Self-reliance means —
a) never accepting any help ever  b) making others do your work  c) doing for yourself what you can do
**Answer: c) doing for yourself what you can do**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Standing on your own includes —
a) never listening to anyone  b) saying what you think, honestly and respectfully  c) shouting over everyone else
**Answer: b) saying what you think, honestly and respectfully**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** A person who values themselves speaks about themselves —
a) truthfully, neither boasting nor shrinking  b) only with big boasts  c) as if they were worth nothing
**Answer: a) truthfully, neither boasting nor shrinking**
