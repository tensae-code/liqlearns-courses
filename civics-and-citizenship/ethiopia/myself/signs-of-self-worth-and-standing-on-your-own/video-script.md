**Signs of self-worth.** A person who values themselves shows it.

**Standing on your own.** Self-reliance is self-worth in action: doing for yourself what you can do — tying your own shoes, carrying your own bag, finishing your own task — and saying what you think, honestly and respectfully, instead of only echoing others.

Now you. Tell your companion two signs of self-worth you have seen in a real person.

And this week: doing one task fully by yourself, then saying what you think, honestly and politely.
