# The Person of Integrity

## Do it first

Imagine a crowd hurrying past a notice board. One pupil stops, reads a promise they wrote there last week, and stays to do what it says — even though nobody would have checked. Hold that picture. This lesson paints the whole portrait of that person.

## The portrait

A person of integrity lives by their principles — their settled standards of right — and does what they said they would do. They hold their principles under outside pressure, when the crowd pushes the other way. They never claim to know what they do not know; 'I don't know' is an honest sentence in their mouth. And they approach people for being people, not for their rank — the small and the great receive the same honest treatment.

## Say it to your companion

Describe the person of integrity to your companion without looking: five marks. Then say which mark you want to grow first.

## Do It Yourself

1. Say 'I don't know' honestly once today instead of inventing an answer.
2. Treat one person of low rank and one of high rank with the same respect today.
3. Do one thing you said you would do, even if the pressure is to drop it.
