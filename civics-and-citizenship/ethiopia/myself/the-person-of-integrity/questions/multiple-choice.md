# The Person of Integrity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** A person of integrity, under outside pressure —
a) holds their principles  b) drops their principles to please the crowd  c) hides until the pressure passes
**Answer: a) holds their principles**

**2.** When they do not know something, they —
a) invent an answer  b) change the subject with a joke every time  c) say so honestly
**Answer: c) say so honestly**

**3.** They approach people —
a) for what the people can give them  b) for being people, not for their rank  c) only if the people are important
**Answer: b) for being people, not for their rank**

**4.** Living by their principles means —
a) their settled standards of right guide their actions  b) following every passing fashion  c) obeying the loudest voice
**Answer: a) their settled standards of right guide their actions**
