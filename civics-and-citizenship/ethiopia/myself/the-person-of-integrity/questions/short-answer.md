# The Person of Integrity — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1.** Name three marks of the person of integrity.
**Answer:** Any three of: lives by their principles, does what they said they would do, holds principles under pressure, never claims to know what they do not know, treats people as people not by rank.

**2.** What honest sentence does the person of integrity use freely?
**Answer:** 'I don't know' — when they truly do not know.
