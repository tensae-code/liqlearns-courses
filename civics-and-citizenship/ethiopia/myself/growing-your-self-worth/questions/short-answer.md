# Growing Your Self-Worth — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two ways to grow your self-worth.
**Answer:** Any two of: govern your impulses, finish what you start, speak truthfully about yourself, attempt things instead of hiding, keep small promises to yourself.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one benefit of holding yourself in good regard.
**Answer:** Standing steadier under problems, trying and learning more, or treating others better.
