# Growing Your Self-Worth — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Saying the benefit of self-worth aloud helps you notice it.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Self-worth is finished growing once you feel good for one day.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A person who values themselves treats others better.
**Answer: True**
