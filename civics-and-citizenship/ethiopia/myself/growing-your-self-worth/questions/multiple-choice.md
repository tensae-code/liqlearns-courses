# Growing Your Self-Worth — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Self-worth grows like —
a) a plant, by steady small care  b) a stone, without any care  c) a prize, given once and finished
**Answer: a) a plant, by steady small care**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which builds self-worth?
a) Hiding from every hard thing  b) Boasting without doing  c) Keeping small promises to yourself
**Answer: c) Keeping small promises to yourself**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What does self-worth give?
a) Permission to look down on others  b) Steadiness under problems and courage to try  c) A life with no problems at all
**Answer: b) Steadiness under problems and courage to try**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The ways to grow self-worth build on —
a) self-control  b) luck  c) other people's praise only
**Answer: a) self-control**
