# Growing Your Self-Worth

## Do it first

Name one way you will water your self-worth this week — one small kept promise. Say it out loud now, then say one benefit you expect it to give you.

## Sub lesson 1 — Ways to grow self-worth

Self-worth grows the way a plant grows: by steady small care. The ways build on the self-control you have just learned — governing your impulses, finishing what you start, speaking truthfully about yourself, and attempting things instead of hiding from them. Each kept small promise to yourself waters the plant.

## Sub lesson 2 — What self-worth gives

Holding yourself in good regard gives real fruit: you stand steadier under problems, you try and learn more, and you treat others better because you are not fighting your own worth all day. Say the benefit aloud in your own words — naming what self-worth gives helps you notice it when it comes.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion one way to grow self-worth and the benefit it brings. Your companion repeats the benefit back in their own words.

## Do It Yourself

1. Keep one small promise to yourself every day this week.
2. Each evening, say aloud one benefit you felt from holding yourself in good regard.
3. When you fail at something, answer yourself truthfully — no boasting, no shrinking — and try again.
