# When Self-Control Is Missing — What Follows — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The chapter closes by having pupils identify the results of not controlling oneself.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Seeing the results clearly is itself a protection: a child who knows where the uncontrolled road leads can stop at its start.
**Answer: True**

**3.** When a child does not control himself, nothing bad follows for anyone.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite.
