# When Self-Control Is Missing — What Follows — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) the factors that damage a healthy social life  b) the results that follow when self-control is missing  c) the common good: what benefits all of us  d) every person is free and has dignity — so we respect them
**Answer: b) the results that follow when self-control is missing** — That is the one thing this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does “lack of self-control” mean?
a) not guiding your own acts; doing whatever the moment pushes you to do  b) the result that follows from an act  c) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  d) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.
**Answer: a) not guiding your own acts; doing whatever the moment pushes you to do** — “lack of self-control” means: not guiding your own acts; doing whatever the moment pushes you to do.

**3.** What does “outcome” mean?
a) not guiding your own acts; doing whatever the moment pushes you to do  b) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  c) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  d) the result that follows from an act
**Answer: d) the result that follows from an act** — “outcome” means: the result that follows from an act.

**4.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  b) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  c) The chain is plain  d) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.
**Answer: c) The chain is plain** — It is one of this lesson's own teachings.

**5.** Which of these is true in this lesson?
a) Promises, truth and fairness are not needed between people.  b) The chapter closes by having pupils identify the results of not controlling oneself  c) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  d) The strongest person may take whatever they want.
**Answer: b) The chapter closes by having pupils identify the results of not controlling oneself** — It is stated in the lesson.
