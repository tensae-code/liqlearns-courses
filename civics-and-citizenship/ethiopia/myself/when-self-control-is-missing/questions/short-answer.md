# When Self-Control Is Missing — What Follows — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** How does the book teach the results of missing self-control?
**Answer:** Pupils identify the results and act them out in a short drama (role-play).

**2.** Why does seeing the results help a child?
**Answer:** A child who knows where the uncontrolled road leads can stop at its start.

**3.** In your own words, what is lack of self-control?
**Answer:** Not guiding your own acts; doing whatever the moment pushes you to do.
