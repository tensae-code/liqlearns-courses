# Video script — When Self-Control Is Missing — What Follows

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
When a child does not control himself, unhappy results follow — for the child and for the people around him.

The chapter closes by having pupils identify the results of not controlling oneself.

The book's own way of learning them is to act them out: pupils show the negative outcomes of a lack of self-control in a short drama.

The chain is plain: an act done without control — snatching, shouting, striking, refusing every duty — brings quarrels, hurt feelings, broken trust and trouble at home and at school.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
