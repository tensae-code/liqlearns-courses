# Hands-on — Keeping Myself Safe

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Think of your way to school. Name the safest road you use. Now name what you would do if that road were blocked one morning. This lesson makes that plan strong — and adds what to do when anything feels unsafe.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: protecting your own safety, at home and at school. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
