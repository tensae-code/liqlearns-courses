# Keeping Myself Safe — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What are the three steps that protect you from a harmful act?
**Answer:** Stop, move away, tell an adult.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two things that keep you safe on the way to school.
**Answer:** Knowing your safe route (and an alternative route), and following the school's safety rules — any two child-facing points from the lesson.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words, what is trusted adult?
**Answer:** A grown-up you know will listen and help — a teacher, a parent, a trusted neighbour.
