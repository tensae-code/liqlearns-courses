# Keeping Myself Safe — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this lesson teach?
a) why community values matter  b) the obstacles to friendship — and the greedy dog  c) the good neighbour's traits, in good times and hard  d) protecting your own safety, at home and at school
**Answer: d) protecting your own safety, at home and at school** — That is the one thing this lesson teaches.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What does “trusted adult” mean?
a) the school's rules that protect everyone, to be known and followed  b) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  c) a grown-up you know will listen and help — a teacher, a parent, a trusted neighbour  d) a second safe way to travel, ready if your usual road is blocked
**Answer: c) a grown-up you know will listen and help — a teacher, a parent, a trusted neighbour** — “trusted adult” means: a grown-up you know will listen and help — a teacher, a parent, a trusted neighbour.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What does “alternative route” mean?
a) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  b) a second safe way to travel, ready if your usual road is blocked  c) a grown-up you know will listen and help — a teacher, a parent, a trusted neighbour  d) the school's rules that protect everyone, to be known and followed
**Answer: b) a second safe way to travel, ready if your usual road is blocked** — “alternative route” means: a second safe way to travel, ready if your usual road is blocked.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What does “safety rules” mean?
a) the school's rules that protect everyone, to be known and followed  b) a grown-up you know will listen and help — a teacher, a parent, a trusted neighbour  c) a second safe way to travel, ready if your usual road is blocked  d) The strongest person may take whatever they want.
**Answer: a) the school's rules that protect everyone, to be known and followed** — “safety rules” means: the school's rules that protect everyone, to be known and followed.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  b) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  c) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  d) Know the routes you travel to and from school 
**Answer: d) Know the routes you travel to and from school ** — It is one of this lesson's own teachings.
