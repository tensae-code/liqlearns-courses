# Video script — Keeping Myself Safe

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Keeping yourself safe — from harmful acts to body and feelings, and at school: safe routes, safety rules, and telling a trusted adult.

Keeping yourself safe is a skill to be shown, not just named: useful practices for protecting your own safety from physically and emotionally harmful acts.

The practice has three steps: stop, move away, tell an adult.

Harm to feelings counts too: acts that frighten, shame or hurt you inside are harmful acts, and the same three steps protect you.

Know the routes you travel to and from school — and an alternative route if a road is blocked.

Know and follow the school's safety rules, the way the adults around you follow them: they are there to protect everyone.

If you feel unsafe: find a teacher, reach a trusted neighbour, and know how to reach your own family. The school-safety reading prints the police number 995.

Tell school staff what they need to protect you — a health condition, a food allergy, or bullying — so they can help.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
