# Facing Problems and What Low Self-Worth Costs — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Asking for help, where help is truly needed, is part of facing a problem.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A low view of yourself does no damage if you keep it quiet.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Facing problems is one way the damage of low self-worth is turned back.
**Answer: True**
