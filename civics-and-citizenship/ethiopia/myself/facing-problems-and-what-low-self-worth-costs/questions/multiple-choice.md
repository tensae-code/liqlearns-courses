# Facing Problems and What Low Self-Worth Costs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Facing a problem means —
a) pretending it is not there  b) waiting for it to vanish by itself  c) meeting it rather than fleeing it
**Answer: c) meeting it rather than fleeing it**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Running away from a problem usually —
a) makes you stronger  b) lets it grow while you lose practice at solving  c) solves it quietly
**Answer: b) lets it grow while you lose practice at solving**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What does low self-worth cost?
a) Attempts, learning and chances pass by  b) Nothing — it does no harm  c) Only other people, never yourself
**Answer: a) Attempts, learning and chances pass by**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The first move in facing a problem is —
a) blame someone quickly  b) hide the problem from everyone  c) look honestly at what the problem really is
**Answer: c) look honestly at what the problem really is**
