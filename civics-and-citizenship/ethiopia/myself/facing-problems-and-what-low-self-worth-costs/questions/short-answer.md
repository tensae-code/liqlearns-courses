# Facing Problems and What Low Self-Worth Costs — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two costs of low self-worth.
**Answer:** Any two of: attempting little, fleeing problems, letting chances pass, less learning, weaker friendships.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the stepping-stone way to face a big problem?
**Answer:** Take the next small step through it instead of trying to leap the whole journey at once.
