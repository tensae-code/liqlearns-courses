# When Integrity Is Missing

## Do it first

Picture a glass jar labelled 'trust'. Now picture it cracked, with good things spilling out onto the floor. Before you read on, guess: what spills out when integrity is missing?

## The costs

When integrity is missing, three losses follow. First, loss of self-confidence: a person whose inside and outside disagree cannot stand steadily in themselves. Second, loss of people's trust: others learn that this person's word does not predict their deed, and they stop building on it. Third, loss of true friendship, because friendship is built on trust and cannot survive its absence.

## The costs at school

At school the missing integrity shows in plain acts: cheating in exams, inattention in lessons, and tasks left improperly done. Each act looks small. Together they crack the jar — and trust, once spilled, is slow to gather again. That is why the lessons before this one built integrity piece by piece.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the three losses that follow when integrity is missing, and the three school acts that show it. Then say which loss would hurt you most, and why.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do one task today properly and fully, even the boring part.
2. Pay full attention in one whole lesson — no drifting.
3. If you have broken trust with someone, take one honest step toward repairing it.
