# When Integrity Is Missing — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1.** Name the three losses when integrity is missing.
**Answer:** Loss of self-confidence, loss of people's trust, loss of true friendship.

**2.** Name two school acts that show integrity is missing.
**Answer:** Any two of: cheating in exams, inattention in lessons, tasks left improperly done.
