# When Integrity Is Missing — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** When integrity is missing, a person loses —
a) only free time  b) self-confidence, people's trust and true friendship  c) nothing at all
**Answer: b) self-confidence, people's trust and true friendship**

**2.** Why does true friendship fail without integrity?
a) Friendship is built on trust  b) Friends prefer dishonest people  c) Friendship needs no honesty
**Answer: a) Friendship is built on trust**

**3.** Which school act shows missing integrity?
a) Asking a question in class  b) Helping a classmate  c) Cheating in an exam
**Answer: c) Cheating in an exam**

**4.** People stop trusting someone when —
a) they admit a mistake and repair it  b) their word does not predict their deed  c) they work quietly
**Answer: b) their word does not predict their deed**
