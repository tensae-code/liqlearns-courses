# Human Dignity — the Dignity We Are Born With and the Dignity We Earn

## Do it first

Think of a person in your neighbourhood whom everyone respects. Say one thing that person does. Now think: was that person born respected, or did their acts earn it? Keep both answers in mind as you read.

## The dignity we are born with

Every human being has dignity simply by being a member of the human race.

This born dignity needs no test and no qualification beyond being human.

Because it belongs to everyone, no person's born dignity may be taken away or treated as worthless.

## The dignity we earn

There is also an earned kind of dignity: the dignity a person gains or loses by their conduct among people.

Good behaviour wins dignity; bad behaviour loses it.

The chief ways people lose earned dignity are selfishness, copying and cheating, fraud, and lying.

Abebech Gobena is the example of earned dignity: an Ethiopian woman who won high human dignity for her great contribution to her society.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: every human being is born with dignity, and a person also earns more dignity — or loses it — by the way they live. Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood, and name the lesson's key words: inherited dignity, earned dignity, fraud. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
