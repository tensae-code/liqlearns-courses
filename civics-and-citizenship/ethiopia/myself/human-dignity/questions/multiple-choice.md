# Human Dignity — the Dignity We Are Born With and the Dignity We Earn — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) the forms under which diversity appears  b) the dignity we are born with, and the dignity we earn  c) the traits of a respected person: humility and honesty  d) responsibilities at school, and the rights that go with them
**Answer: b) the dignity we are born with, and the dignity we earn** — That is the one thing this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does “inherited dignity” mean?
a) the dignity every human being has simply by being human  b) the dignity a person gains or loses by their conduct  c) cheating people by deceiving them  d) thinking only of yourself, which loses a person dignity
**Answer: a) the dignity every human being has simply by being human** — “inherited dignity” means: the dignity every human being has simply by being human.

**3.** What does “earned dignity” mean?
a) the dignity every human being has simply by being human  b) cheating people by deceiving them  c) thinking only of yourself, which loses a person dignity  d) the dignity a person gains or loses by their conduct
**Answer: d) the dignity a person gains or loses by their conduct** — “earned dignity” means: the dignity a person gains or loses by their conduct.

**4.** What does “fraud” mean?
a) the dignity a person gains or loses by their conduct  b) thinking only of yourself, which loses a person dignity  c) cheating people by deceiving them  d) the dignity every human being has simply by being human
**Answer: c) cheating people by deceiving them** — “fraud” means: cheating people by deceiving them.

**5.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  b) There is also an earned kind of dignity  c) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  d) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.
**Answer: b) There is also an earned kind of dignity** — It is one of this lesson's own teachings.
