# Human Dignity — the Dignity We Are Born With and the Dignity We Earn — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Every human being has dignity simply by being a member of the human race.
**Answer: True**

**2.** There is also an earned kind of dignity: the dignity a person gains or loses by their conduct among people.
**Answer: True**

**3.** A person is born with no dignity at all, and only rich or famous people can have any.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite.
