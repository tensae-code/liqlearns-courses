# Human Dignity — the Dignity We Are Born With and the Dignity We Earn — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What wins a person earned dignity?
**Answer:** Good behaviour — living and acting well among people.

**2.** Name two things that make a person lose earned dignity.
**Answer:** Any two of: selfishness, copying/cheating, fraud, lying.

**3.** In your own words, what is inherited dignity?
**Answer:** The dignity every human being has simply by being human.
