# Video script — Human Dignity — the Dignity We Are Born With and the Dignity We Earn

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Every human being is born with dignity, and a person also earns more dignity — or loses it — by the way they live.

Every human being has dignity simply by being a member of the human race.

This born dignity needs no test and no qualification beyond being human.

Because it belongs to everyone, no person's born dignity may be taken away or treated as worthless.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
