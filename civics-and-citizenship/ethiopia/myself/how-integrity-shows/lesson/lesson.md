# How Integrity Shows

## Do it first

Picture your last school day from morning to evening. Walk through it slowly and mark every place where integrity could show: a question you answered honestly, a task you finished, property you handled. This lesson names the marks to look for.

## The marks of integrity in action

Integrity shows. Its manifestations are: honesty — telling the truth, not stealing, not cheating; strength to pass through difficulties without fear; seeing other people's problems as your own; humility; accountability — answering for what you did; and consistency — being the same person in private and in public. None of these is a feeling. Every one of them can be seen.

## Integrity at school

In the classroom integrity has plain shapes: respecting teachers, doing set tasks on time, and caring for school property. A pupil who cheats in an exercise but is polite to the teacher is showing only half the picture — integrity is the whole pattern, in every part of the day.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the manifestations of integrity, one by one. Then choose the one you find hardest, and say why.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do today's set tasks on time, without being chased.
2. Handle one piece of school property with extra care today.
3. When something goes wrong that is yours, say so plainly — that is accountability.
