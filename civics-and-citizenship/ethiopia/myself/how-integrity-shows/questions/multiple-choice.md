# How Integrity Shows — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which is a manifestation of integrity?
a) Hiding every mistake  b) Consistency — the same in private and in public  c) Being good only when the teacher watches
**Answer: b) Consistency — the same in private and in public**

**2.** Honesty in this lesson includes —
a) telling the truth, not stealing, not cheating  b) telling people what they want to hear  c) keeping every secret you are told
**Answer: a) telling the truth, not stealing, not cheating**

**3.** Seeing others' problems as — is a mark of integrity.
a) nothing to do with you  b) chances to look better  c) your own
**Answer: c) your own**

**4.** Integrity at school shows in —
a) finishing first at any cost  b) respecting teachers, tasks on time, caring for school property  c) loud promises
**Answer: b) respecting teachers, tasks on time, caring for school property**
