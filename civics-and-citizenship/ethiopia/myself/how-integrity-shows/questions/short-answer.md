# How Integrity Shows — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1.** Name four manifestations of integrity.
**Answer:** Any four of: honesty, strength through difficulties without fear, seeing others' problems as your own, humility, accountability, consistency.

**2.** Give two classroom examples of integrity.
**Answer:** Any two of: respecting teachers, doing set tasks on time, caring for school property (also: not cheating, telling the truth).
