# Self-Control — What It Is and How a Self-Controlled Child Acts — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Self-control is the right moral behaviour a child develops and keeps.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Such children respect their parents.
**Answer: True**

**3.** A self-controlled child waits to be told before doing any good thing.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite.
