# Self-Control — What It Is and How a Self-Controlled Child Acts — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) building good neighbour relations, and removing what harms them  b) culture: a people's way of life, handed on  c) the nine core community values  d) what self-control is, and how a self-controlled child acts
**Answer: d) what self-control is, and how a self-controlled child acts** — That is the one thing this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does “self-control” mean?
a) behaving in the right, kind and honest way  b) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  c) the right moral behaviour a child develops and keeps  d) a moment when you must choose between right and wrong
**Answer: c) the right moral behaviour a child develops and keeps** — “self-control” means: the right moral behaviour a child develops and keeps.

**3.** What does “moral problem” mean?
a) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  b) a moment when you must choose between right and wrong  c) the right moral behaviour a child develops and keeps  d) behaving in the right, kind and honest way
**Answer: b) a moment when you must choose between right and wrong** — “moral problem” means: a moment when you must choose between right and wrong.

**4.** What does “good conduct” mean?
a) behaving in the right, kind and honest way  b) the right moral behaviour a child develops and keeps  c) a moment when you must choose between right and wrong  d) The strongest person may take whatever they want.
**Answer: a) behaving in the right, kind and honest way** — “good conduct” means: behaving in the right, kind and honest way.

**5.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  b) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  c) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  d) They keep themselves clean, and they eat properly
**Answer: d) They keep themselves clean, and they eat properly** — It is one of this lesson's own teachings.
