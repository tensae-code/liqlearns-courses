# Self-Control — What It Is and How a Self-Controlled Child Acts — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name three acts of a self-controlled child.
**Answer:** Any three of: respecting parents, keeping clean, eating properly, helping parents and others, playing safely and honestly, working hard peacefully.

**2.** What does a self-controlled child do when facing a moral problem?
**Answer:** Has the courage to ask about it.

**3.** In your own words, what is self-control?
**Answer:** The right moral behaviour a child develops and keeps.
