# Hands-on — Self-Control — What It Is and How a Self-Controlled Child Acts

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Think of one moment today when you wanted to do something you knew was wrong — to shout, to snatch, to skip a duty. What did you do? Hold that moment in mind; this lesson is about the strength that guides such moments.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: what self-control is, and how a self-controlled child acts. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
