# Video script — Self-Control — What It Is and How a Self-Controlled Child Acts

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Self-control is the right moral behaviour a child grows and keeps — shown in clean, kind, honest, hardworking daily acts.

Self-control is the right moral behaviour a child develops and keeps.

It is learned: children try hard to learn and understand the rules of good conduct, and practise them every day.

A self-controlled child has the courage to ask when facing a moral problem, instead of hiding it.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
