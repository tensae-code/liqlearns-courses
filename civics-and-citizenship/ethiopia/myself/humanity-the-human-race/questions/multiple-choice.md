# Humanity — the Human Race, and the Mark of Being Good — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) how honesty is learned and grown  b) the benefits of a healthy social life  c) the human race, and being good to others  d) who is in a family, and why each person matters
**Answer: c) the human race, and being good to others** — That is the one thing this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does “humanity (the human race)” mean?
a) helping those who need help, without leaving anyone out  b) all human beings together  c) cannot be swapped for another, person for person  d) picking favourites; helping some and leaving others out for no fair reason
**Answer: b) all human beings together** — “humanity (the human race)” means: all human beings together.

**3.** What does “irreplaceable” mean?
a) cannot be swapped for another, person for person  b) all human beings together  c) picking favourites; helping some and leaving others out for no fair reason  d) helping those who need help, without leaving anyone out
**Answer: a) cannot be swapped for another, person for person** — “irreplaceable” means: cannot be swapped for another, person for person.

**4.** What does “partiality” mean?
a) all human beings together  b) cannot be swapped for another, person for person  c) helping those who need help, without leaving anyone out  d) picking favourites; helping some and leaving others out for no fair reason
**Answer: d) picking favourites; helping some and leaving others out for no fair reason** — “partiality” means: picking favourites; helping some and leaving others out for no fair reason.

**5.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  b) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  c) In its second meaning, humanity is the mark of goodness in a person   d) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.
**Answer: c) In its second meaning, humanity is the mark of goodness in a person ** — It is one of this lesson's own teachings.
