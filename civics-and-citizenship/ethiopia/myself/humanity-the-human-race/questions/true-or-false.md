# Humanity — the Human Race, and the Mark of Being Good — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Humanity, in its first meaning, is the human race itself .
**Answer: True**

**2.** In its second meaning, humanity is the mark of goodness in a person .
**Answer: True**

**3.** A human being can be replaced by another person, just like a broken cup can be replaced by a new cup.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite.
