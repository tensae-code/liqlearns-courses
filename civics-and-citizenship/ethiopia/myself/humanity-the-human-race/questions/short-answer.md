# Humanity — the Human Race, and the Mark of Being Good — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** In the first meaning, what is humanity?
**Answer:** The human race — all human beings together.

**2.** What does a person with the mark of goodness do?
**Answer:** Helps those who need help, without partiality or exclusion.

**3.** In your own words, what is humanity (the human race)?
**Answer:** All human beings together.
