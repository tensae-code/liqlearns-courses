# Video script — Humanity — the Human Race, and the Mark of Being Good

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Humanity means two things: the human race we all belong to, and the mark of being good — helping others without leaving anyone out.

Humanity, in its first meaning, is the human race itself — all human beings together, different from the other animals.

A human being is precious and irreplaceable: a thing can be replaced thing-for-thing, but a human being cannot be replaced by another.

Human beings differ in race, height, sex and language — yet in humanity all human beings are equal.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
