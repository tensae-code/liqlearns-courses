# Humanity — the Human Race, and the Mark of Being Good

## Do it first

Look around your class or your home. Point to three things that could be replaced if they broke — a cup, a chair, a pencil. Now point to a person. Could that person be replaced, person for person, like a cup? Talk about what you find.

## Humanity — the human race

Humanity, in its first meaning, is the human race itself — all human beings together, different from the other animals.

A human being is precious and irreplaceable: a thing can be replaced thing-for-thing, but a human being cannot be replaced by another.

Human beings differ in race, height, sex and language — yet in humanity all human beings are equal.

No one is to be left out or pushed aside for being different.

[Picture 28000 — children in one circle — irreplaceable]

## Humanity — the mark of being good

In its second meaning, humanity is the mark of goodness in a person — being human as a quality.

A person with this mark helps those who need help, without leaving anyone out and without picking favourites.

Helping without partiality teaches people to understand each other and be friends.

It builds working together, and a peaceful, healthy way of life — at school and in the whole society.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: humanity means two things: the human race we all belong to, and the mark of being good — helping others without leaving anyone out. Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood, and name the lesson's key words: humanity (the human race), irreplaceable, partiality. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: the human race, and being good to others.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
