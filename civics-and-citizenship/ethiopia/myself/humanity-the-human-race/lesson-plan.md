# Lesson plan — Humanity — the Human Race, and the Mark of Being Good

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Myself · **Lesson:** Humanity — the Human Race, and the Mark of Being Good
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — Myself (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** humanity means two things: the human race we all belong to, and the mark of being good — helping others without leaving anyone out.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Humanity, in its first meaning, is the human race itself — all human beings together, different from the other animals.
2. A human being is precious and irreplaceable.
3. Human beings differ in race, height, sex and language — yet in humanity all human beings are equal.
4. No one is to be left out or pushed aside for being different.
5. In its second meaning, humanity is the mark of goodness in a person — being human as a quality.
