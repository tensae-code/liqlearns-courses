# Knowing and Valuing Yourself

## Do it first

Stand up straight. Say your name out loud, then say three true things about yourself: one thing you can do, one thing you are learning, and one thing you like about how you treat people. Say them slowly, like you mean them.

## What self-worth is

Self-worth is the value you give yourself. It is the quiet answer you carry inside to the question: what am I worth? A person with self-worth knows themselves — what they think, what they feel, what they can do — and treats that knowledge with respect. Knowing yourself comes first: you cannot value what you have never looked at honestly.

## Saying what you think and know about yourself

This lesson begins with your own voice. When you say what you think and know about yourself — your strengths, your weak places, the things you are still learning — you are building self-worth on truth. Self-worth built on truth stands firm. Self-worth built on pretending falls at the first hard day.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion: what self-worth is, in your own words. Then each say one true thing you know about yourself and one thing you value about yourself.

## Do It Yourself

1. Write or draw three true things you know about yourself.
2. Say one of them out loud in front of a mirror, standing tall.
3. Name one thing about yourself you want to grow, and one small step toward it.
