# Knowing and Valuing Yourself — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What is self-worth?
a) The marks a person gets in one test  b) The value a person gives themselves  c) The money a person owns
**Answer: b) The value a person gives themselves**

**2.** What comes first in building self-worth?
a) Knowing yourself honestly  b) Being praised by everyone  c) Winning every game
**Answer: a) Knowing yourself honestly**

**3.** Self-worth is built best on —
a) pretending to be perfect  b) other people's opinions only  c) truth about yourself
**Answer: c) truth about yourself**

**4.** A person with self-worth —
a) never needs anyone's help  b) knows what they think and can do, and respects it  c) never makes a mistake
**Answer: b) knows what they think and can do, and respects it**
