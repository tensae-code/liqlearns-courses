# Hands-on — Knowing and Valuing Yourself

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The core practice
Do the lesson's own practice in real life, today: the mirror practice: stand tall, say your name and one true, good thing about yourself. Use real things and real people — your home, your classroom, your street. Name what you are doing as you do it, in the lesson's own words.

## Teach it with your hands
Show a younger child or a friend the picture idea of this lesson — a child looking in a mirror, standing tall — by acting it, drawing it, or finding it happening in real life. Teaching it with your hands fixes it in your own memory.

## The week's tally
On paper, keep a tally for one week: one line per day, one mark each time you used this lesson's teaching. At the week's end, read the tally aloud to your companion and say which day was hardest, and why.
