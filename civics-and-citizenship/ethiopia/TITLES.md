# Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia course — titles, FROM THE BOTTOM

Civics belongs to the country: this course is Ethiopia's own —
built from Ethiopia's Moral Education books (Grades 1–6),
Citizenship Education books (Grades 7–10), and the older Civics
and Ethical Education course (Grade 11, 2002 E.C.), kept whole
and labelled at the end. Inside the course the organisation is by
**levels, not grades**: the titles below ARE the levels, in path
order — what the learner proves, step by step. The grades the
books come from live only in each lesson's sources.

The path's own logic is the books': character first, in widening
circles — myself, family, neighbours, school, community, culture —
then the citizen: rights, character, belonging, the state,
democracy, law, and the skills to judge all of it — then the
world. That ladder IS the path order (recommended, never forced —
a learner can jump in higher; the "start here instead" hints point
down this ladder).

## The titles

**The opening levels — morals and manners** (Moral Education;
teacher-guide evidence only throughout):

- `myself/` — Level 1. What a human being is, dignity,
  self-control and self-respect, keeping myself safe; deepened by
  self-worth and moral integrity.
- `my-family-and-respect/` — Level 2. The family, its values and
  manners; respecting parents, elders, teachers, neighbours,
  classmates — every person.
- `honesty-justice-and-fairness/` — Level 3. Honesty (true words,
  kept words, clean hands), justice, fairness, and standing
  against what is unfair.
- `neighbours-school-and-friends/` — Level 4. Neighbourliness,
  manners and rules at school, rights and responsibilities,
  friendships, a healthy social life.
- `working-together/` — Level 5. The common good, public
  property, the environment as a common good, cooperation —
  debo, idir, equb — and teamwork skills.
- `community-and-culture/` — Level 6. Community and its nine
  values, culture and local culture, heritage in danger and
  keeping it safe, customs, many cultures one society.
- `peace-and-cooperation/` — Level 7. Peace and its meanings,
  settling conflict without fighting, the settled community,
  cooperation.
- `right-conduct/` — Level 8. Ethics, permitted and forbidden,
  staying safe on the road, good behaviour and the marks of good
  character.
- `responsibility-work-and-decisions/` — Level 9. Responsibility
  and its kinds, our moral responsibilities, work culture,
  reading culture, decisions and the five steps to a moral
  decision.
- `caring-for-our-world/` — Level 10. Serving the community the
  right way, the environment and caring for it, taking part,
  voluntary work, spare time for the common good, paying tax
  honestly and saving.
- `living-online-well/` — Level 11. Social media — what it is,
  what it is good for, using it the right way, staying safe
  online.
- `cultures-meeting/` — Level 12. Tolerance, making room for
  other cultures, unity in diversity, cultures meeting, linking,
  and the signs they are truly linked.

**The citizenship levels:**

- `rights-and-law-when-young/` — Level 13. The Moral guides'
  civics strand, at a young level: dignity first, equality and
  the four equalities, children's rights, human rights,
  law-abidingness, patriotism. *(Teacher-guide evidence only.)*
- `character-of-a-citizen/` — Level 14. Good citizenship, virtue
  ethics, the virtues one by one (honesty, courage, compassion,
  generosity, fairness, self-discipline, prudence), civic
  virtues, ethical decisions; deepened by ethics, integrity,
  loyalty, concern, respect, law-abidingness, excellence and
  applied ethics.
- `belonging-and-citizenship/` — Level 15. Citizen and
  citizenship, becoming a citizen (birth, descent,
  naturalisation, adoption), losing citizenship, Ethiopian
  citizenship law.
- `the-state-and-government/` — Level 16. The state, its elements
  and what government does; forms of government; state
  structures (unitary, federal, confederation); parliamentary
  and presidential systems; the governments Ethiopia has lived
  under, and judging them.
- `democracy/` — Level 17. Democracy's meaning, Athens, liberal
  and social forms, direct and representative, principles and
  values, indigenous democracy in Ethiopia; deepened by
  democratization, who builds democracy, democratic institutions,
  transformation, aspects.
- `law-and-the-constitution/` — Level 18. The rule of law, its
  sources, signs and enemies; constitutions — purposes, kinds,
  principles; constitutionalism; Ethiopia's constitutions from
  Kibre Negest to 1995. (Law-and-Government material lives here
  and in `the-state-and-government/` and
  `federalism-and-patriotism/`.)
- `human-rights/` — Level 19. Human rights and their features,
  children's rights (evidence), why rights must be respected,
  instruments (world, Africa, nation), institutions, rights
  classified, equality, regional constitutions, challenges in
  Ethiopia; deepened by the concept, the generations of rights,
  rights in Ethiopia's constitution, obligations, protection.
- `taking-part-and-peace/` — Level 20. Community engagement,
  volunteering, conflict and its causes, negotiation, mediation,
  arbitration, litigation, peace building, indigenous conflict
  resolution; deepened by peace inner and outer, positive and
  negative peace, indigenous institutions and peace.
- `thinking-like-a-citizen/` — Level 21. Critical thinking —
  analyse, triangulate, decide — its skills and habits;
  problem solving, its six steps, deciding in seven steps;
  deepened by problem-solving in practice and the good problem
  solver.
- `the-digital-citizen/` — Level 22. Digital technology and
  culture, codes of conduct online, communicating well,
  challenges, the double edge, emotions, social life, ethical
  values, opportunities, unethical use.
- `home-knowledge/` — Level 23. Indigenous knowledge — settling
  conflicts, local technologies, caring for the land — and
  indigenous social institutions.
- `living-together-in-diversity/` — Level 24. Culture,
  multiculturalism and its pillars, forms and elements of
  diversity, unity in diversity, accommodating diversity,
  managing diversity in Ethiopia.
- `ethiopia-and-the-world/` — Level 25. Foreign relations,
  national interest, foreign policy and its instruments,
  Ethiopia and its region; globalization — meaning, dimensions,
  gains, harms, fighting the harms; global issues — climate,
  poverty and population, work, disease, terror.
- `governing-well/` — Level 26. Governance and good governance,
  its elements, what it does, its challenges and failures;
  corruption — what it is, its forms, fighting it.
- `federalism-and-patriotism/` — Level 27. Federalism — meaning,
  dual and cooperative types, features, advantages and
  disadvantages, federalism in Ethiopia and its challenges;
  patriotism — meaning, six types, bases, why it matters, traits
  of patriots, duties of patriots.
