# Unity in Diversity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Unity in diversity is oneness among peoples who —
a) become identical  b) acknowledge and respect their rich diversity and live as one undivided nation  c) stop meeting  d) share one language only
**Answer: b) acknowledge and respect their rich diversity and live as one undivided nation**

**2.** The first formula is: not uniformity, but —
a) silence  b) harmony between dissimilar people  c) competition  d) separation
**Answer: b) harmony between dissimilar people**
