# Unity in Diversity — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Give both formulas from memory.
**Answer:** Not uniformity, but harmony between dissimilar people; commonality without uniformity, and diversity without fragmentation.

**2.** Why is neither column of the table the enemy of the other?
**Answer:** Because unity is built *over* common destinies among members whose diversity is real — the columns name the two truths the nation holds at once.
