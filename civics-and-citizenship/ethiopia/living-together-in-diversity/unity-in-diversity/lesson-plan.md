# Lesson plan — Unity in Diversity

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Living Together in Diversity · **Lesson:** Unity in Diversity
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 24 — Living Together in Diversity (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** unity in diversity is oneness without sameness: peoples of many cultures, religions and languages who respect their rich diversity and agree to live together as one undivided nation.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the citizenship levels before it — the learner knows one constitution over one country of many peoples; this band teaches how those peoples live together inside it.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. ## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks unity requires uniformity → the formula refuses it by name: harmony between *dissimilar* people.
- Thinks diversity means fragmentation → the second formula refuses that too: diversity *without fragmentation*.
