# Hands-on — Unity in Diversity

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The choir, actually sung
The group learns one short song in parts — high, middle, low — however roughly. Sing it unified (one note), then fragmented (everyone their own song), then in parts. The third singing is the definition, heard.

## Rivers into one lake
Coloured ribbons or chalk streams of many colours are drawn flowing into one lake on the board. Each stream keeps its colour inside the lake. Learners label the streams with Ethiopia's real diversities.
