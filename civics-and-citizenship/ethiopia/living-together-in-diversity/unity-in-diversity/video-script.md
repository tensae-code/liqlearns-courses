# Video script — Unity in Diversity

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing: unity in diversity is oneness without sameness: peoples of many cultures, religions and languages who respect their rich diversity and agree to live together as one undivided nation.
Many voices. One song.
Not uniformity — harmony.
Commonality without uniformity.
Diversity without fragmentation.
Many notes, each one necessary.
That is unity in diversity.
