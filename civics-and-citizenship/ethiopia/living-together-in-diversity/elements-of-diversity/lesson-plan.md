# Lesson plan — Elements of Diversity

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Living Together in Diversity · **Lesson:** Elements of Diversity
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 24 — Living Together in Diversity (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Ethiopia's diversity has elements: cultural, religious and linguistic — many cultures held equal, many faiths respected, many languages carrying literary wealth.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the citizenship levels before it — the learner knows one constitution over one country of many peoples; this band teaches how those peoples live together inside it.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short, single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09).

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Unity in Diversity. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the three elements.
2. State the equality rule inside cultural diversity.
3. Say what linguistic diversity gives a country.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the learner counts the languages and faiths within reach of one market day (lesson/lesson.md).
2. **The explanation** — the three elements worked in turn — cultural, religious, linguistic — over the map of Ethiopia (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 28863]: a picture of three overlapping circles lettered Culture, Religion, Language, laid over a map of Ethiopia — the student names the three elements and one thing in the overlap of all three.

   - ADD VIDEO: the script in video-script.md, filmed — the lesson retold in the super-simple voice (made later, when videos are made).
4. **Questions** — from questions/: fill-in-the-blank, matching, multiple-choice, short-answer, true-or-false, writing — served at the learner's level and teaching-mix (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks elements means a ranking → the cultural element's spine is equality: no culture superior or inferior.
- Thinks languages are interchangeable labels → each carries a cultural entity and literary wealth that translation cannot fully move.
