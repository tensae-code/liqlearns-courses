# Video script — Elements of Diversity

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing: Ethiopia's diversity has elements: cultural, religious and linguistic — many cultures held equal, many faiths respected, many languages carrying literary wealth.
One country. Three kinds of wealth.
Cultures — none above another.
Faiths — churches and mosques standing close.
Languages — each one a room a culture lives in.
Count them, and you have counted treasure.
