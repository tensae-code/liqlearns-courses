# Hands-on — Elements of Diversity

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The patchwork map
On a big outline map, the group fills regions with patches — languages, scripts, highlands, lowlands — until the map itself argues the lesson: one country, many pieces, no patch ranked.

## Three circles
Three hoops or chalk circles overlap on the floor: Culture, Religion, Language. Learners place fact-cards about their town in the right circle — and defend the ones that belong in the overlap.
