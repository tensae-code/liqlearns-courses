# Elements of Diversity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** How many elements does this lesson work?
a) Two  b) Three  c) Five  d) Eight
**Answer: b) Three**

**2.** Inside cultural diversity, cultures are —
a) ranked best to worst  b) all seen equally — none superior or inferior  c) merged into one  d) counted
**Answer: b) all seen equally — none superior or inferior**

**3.** Linguistic diversity gives a country —
a) one official voice  b) literary wealth and many carried cultures  c) confusion only  d) cheaper printing
**Answer: b) literary wealth and many carried cultures**
