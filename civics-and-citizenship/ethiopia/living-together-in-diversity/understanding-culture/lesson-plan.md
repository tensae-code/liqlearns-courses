# Lesson plan — Understanding Culture

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Living Together in Diversity · **Lesson:** Understanding Culture
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 24 — Living Together in Diversity (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** culture is the shared language, beliefs, values, norms, behaviours and material things a people passes from generation to generation — and it works as both a mirror and a lens.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the citizenship levels before it — the learner knows one constitution over one country of many peoples; this band teaches how those peoples live together inside it.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short, single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09).

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the home-knowledge band — culture as the ground indigenous knowledge grows in. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Define culture with its full list.
2. Explain mirror and lens.
3. Say why the lens misjudges other cultures, and the cure.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — the learner describes a proper meal, dress and greeting and catches their own judgements at work (lesson/lesson.md).
2. **The explanation** — culture defined in full; mirror and lens; stereotyping as the lens's disease; diversity received — accepted, respected, tolerated, appreciated (lesson/lesson.md).

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks culture is only festivals and dress → the definition runs deeper: beliefs, values, norms, law, morals — the judgements themselves are culture.
- Thinks the lens can be discarded → it cannot; it can be *known*, and its misjudgements corrected by learning without stereotype.
