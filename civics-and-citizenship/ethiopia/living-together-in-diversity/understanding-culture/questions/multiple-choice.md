# Understanding Culture — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Culture is passed —
a) by law only  b) from generation to generation  c) by traders  d) in one lifetime
**Answer: b) from generation to generation**

**2.** The lens of culture is what members —
a) eat with  b) judge everything through — proper or improper  c) wear  d) sing
**Answer: b) judge everything through — proper or improper**
