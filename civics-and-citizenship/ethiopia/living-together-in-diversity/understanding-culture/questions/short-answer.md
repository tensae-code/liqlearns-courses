# Understanding Culture — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Give the definition of culture with its list.
**Answer:** The shared language, beliefs, values, norms, behaviours and material objects passed from generation to generation — a whole of knowledge, belief, art, morals, law, custom and habit acquired as a member of society.

**2.** What is the cure for the lens's misjudgement?
**Answer:** Learning culture itself — recognition of difference without stereotype.
