# Hands-on — Understanding Culture

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Mirror, lens
Each learner holds a card: one side a mirror ('this is us…'), one side a lens ('we judge…'). Learners read both sides aloud for their own community, then discuss where the lens has wronged a neighbour.

## Three ways
The coffee ceremony is drawn three regional ways by three groups from their own knowledge, then the drawings are compared: same ceremony, three cultures — and no copy is 'wrong'.
