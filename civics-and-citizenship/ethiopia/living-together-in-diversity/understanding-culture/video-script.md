# Video script — Understanding Culture

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing: culture is the shared language, beliefs, values, norms, behaviours and material things a people passes from generation to generation — and it works as both a mirror and a lens.
Culture is the language you dream in.
The dress, the food, the way you greet.
It is a mirror: this is us.
It is a lens: through it, you judge the world.
Know your lens — or it will misjudge your neighbour.
