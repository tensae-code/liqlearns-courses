# Hands-on — Forms of Diversity

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The classroom door audit
The group walks the school with the disability definition in hand: door, steps, desk, board. What limits a major life activity here? The audit is reported with fixes priced in effort, not only money.

## Four callouts at the market
At a real or drawn market, learners collect the evidence sort's four callouts: languages heard, dresses worn, foods sold, houses of worship passed. The harvest is presented as a diversity census.
