# Forms of Diversity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ethnicity is identification on —
a) income  b) cultural grounds — language, origins, descent, shared culture or history  c) height  d) examination results
**Answer: b) cultural grounds — language, origins, descent, shared culture or history**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Ethnicity hardened into ______ threatens multiculturalism.
a) celebration  b) ethnocentrism  c) friendship  d) federalism
**Answer: b) ethnocentrism**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Gender, in this lesson's definition, is —
a) biology itself  b) a social construct of ascribed roles, rights and relative power  c) a census category  d) a clothing rule
**Answer: b) a social construct of ascribed roles, rights and relative power**
