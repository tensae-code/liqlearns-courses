# Forms of Diversity — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which example carries religious diversity in this lesson?
**Answer:** Ethiopia's Christians and Muslims, who have lived together for a long period in mutual respect and tolerance.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What aim does the gender-and-disability sub lesson set beyond tolerance?
**Answer:** Embracing and celebrating the dimensions of diversity inside each individual — with self-awareness and knowledge of the world among its gains.
