# Video script — Forms of Diversity

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing: diversity takes named forms: ethnicity and religion, and gender and disability — each a real dimension of human difference, and each asking more than tolerance.
Difference has forms, and names.
Ethnicity — a people's own ground.
Religion — belief lived side by side.
Gender — roles a culture assigns.
Disability — diversity itself, not its exception.
More than tolerate: embrace.
