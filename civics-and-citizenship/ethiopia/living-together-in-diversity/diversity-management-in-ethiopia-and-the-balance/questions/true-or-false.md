# Diversity Management in Ethiopia — And the Balance — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Making a united Ethiopia and promoting its diverse differences are declared equally important.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Abdella's relatives were right about the welcome he would receive.
**Answer: False** — he was warmly welcomed, by a community proud of its diversity.
