# Diversity Management in Ethiopia — And the Balance — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ethiopia holds over —
a) eight ethnic groups  b) eighty ethnic groups  c) eight hundred ethnic groups  d) eighteen ethnic groups
**Answer: b) eighty ethnic groups**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Abdella was posted to a community of —
a) his own faith  b) a Christian majority, as a Muslim teacher  c) no faith  d) his home town
**Answer: b) a Christian majority, as a Muslim teacher**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** National integration is created by —
a) erasing differences  b) recognising and respecting differences  c) ignoring history  d) one language for all
**Answer: b) recognising and respecting differences**
