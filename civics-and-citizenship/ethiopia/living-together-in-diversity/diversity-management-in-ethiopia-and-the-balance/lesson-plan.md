# Lesson plan — Diversity Management in Ethiopia — And the Balance

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Living Together in Diversity · **Lesson:** Diversity Management in Ethiopia — And the Balance
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 24 — Living Together in Diversity (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** in a country of over 80 ethnic groups, diversity management is the adopted work: accommodating diversity while holding unity — because unity is built by respecting difference, not by erasing it.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the citizenship levels before it — the learner knows one constitution over one country of many peoples; this band teaches how those peoples live together inside it.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Why diversity management matters in Ethiopia** — over 80 groups; the multicultural-federal solution; Abdella welcomed — the case as summary
2. **The balance — unity built by respecting difference** — respecting difference as the means of integration; both pans filled, neither sacrificed

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

- Thinks respect for difference weakens unity → the lesson makes it the *means* of integration: unity is built by respecting difference.
