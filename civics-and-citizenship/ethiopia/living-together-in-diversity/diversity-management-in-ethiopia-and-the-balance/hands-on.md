# Hands-on — Diversity Management in Ethiopia — And the Balance

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The gardener's field
The group lists the 'crops' of one field — the peoples and practices of your area — and for each, what watering *it* needs: a language kept, a holiday honoured, a history told truly. One field, many waterings.

## The tipping scale
A real or drawn balance: remove the 'differences respected' weights and watch it tip; remove the 'one nation' weights and watch it tip the other way. Restore both. The class narrates what each tipping would mean in a real town.
