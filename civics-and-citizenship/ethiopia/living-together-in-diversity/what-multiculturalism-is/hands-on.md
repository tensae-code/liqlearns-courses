# Hands-on — What Multiculturalism Is

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The market roof
The group builds a paper roof over figures of many dress styles. The roof is labelled with the shared values the group agrees on; each figure keeps its own dress. Remove the roof; remove a figure; discuss what each removal loses.

## Threads in one cloth
Strips of differently coloured paper are woven into one mat, no colour bleached or cut away. The weavers name what the mat can do that no strip alone can.
