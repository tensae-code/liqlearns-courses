# Video script — What Multiculturalism Is

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing: multiculturalism is the body of thought — and the system of belief and behaviour — for responding properly to cultural and religious diversity: every group recognised and respected, inside shared fundamental values.
Many peoples. One roof.
Not sameness — that is erasure.
Not fragments — that is a fallen roof.
Recognise every group. Respect every group.
Share the values at the centre.
That is multiculturalism.
