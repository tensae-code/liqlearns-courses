# What Multiculturalism Is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Multiculturalism is a system for —
a) ending diversity  b) responding properly to cultural and religious diversity  c) choosing one culture  d) tourism
**Answer: b) responding properly to cultural and religious diversity**

**2.** Difference inside multiculturalism is —
a) endured at the edges  b) acknowledged and valued, inside shared fundamental values  c) privatised and hidden  d) ranked best to worst
**Answer: b) acknowledged and valued, inside shared fundamental values**
