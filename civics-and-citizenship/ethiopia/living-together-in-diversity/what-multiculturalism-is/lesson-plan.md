# Lesson plan — What Multiculturalism Is

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Living Together in Diversity · **Lesson:** What Multiculturalism Is
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 24 — Living Together in Diversity (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** multiculturalism is the body of thought — and the system of belief and behaviour — for responding properly to cultural and religious diversity: every group recognised and respected, inside shared fundamental values.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the citizenship levels before it — the learner knows one constitution over one country of many peoples; this band teaches how those peoples live together inside it.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Define multiculturalism in full.
2. Name what it asks of groups and of the shared frame.
3. Say what it is Ethiopia's adopted means of doing.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks multiculturalism means many cultures merely existing side by side → it is a *response system*: recognition, respect, valuing — inside shared fundamental values.
- Thinks shared values erase difference → the frame is shared precisely so difference can be valued safely inside it.
