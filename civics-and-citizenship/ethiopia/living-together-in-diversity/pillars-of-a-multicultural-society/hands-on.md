# Hands-on — Pillars of a Multicultural Society

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Four pillars standing
Four learners stand as pillars holding a roof-board labelled 'a multicultural society'. Each, in character, states their pillar's definition. The appreciate-pillar must also state the appropriation caution.

## The festival next door
The four neighbours scene is acted with the festival audible through the wall. At each attitude, freeze: the class names the pillar and what it protects.
