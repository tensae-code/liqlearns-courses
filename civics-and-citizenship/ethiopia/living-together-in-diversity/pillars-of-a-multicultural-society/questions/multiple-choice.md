# Pillars of a Multicultural Society — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** How many pillars does the student book teach?
a) Five  b) Four  c) Three  d) Seven
**Answer: b) Four**

**2.** Tolerance is the willingness to accept differences —
a) that you enjoy  b) that you dislike or disagree with  c) in other countries only  d) in theory
**Answer: b) that you dislike or disagree with**

**3.** Appreciation is not —
a) learning  b) connecting  c) appropriation — taking a piece of another's culture for your own interest  d) understanding
**Answer: c) appropriation — taking a piece of another's culture for your own interest**
