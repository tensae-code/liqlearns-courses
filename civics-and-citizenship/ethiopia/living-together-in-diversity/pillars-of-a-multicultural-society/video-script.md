# Video script — Pillars of a Multicultural Society

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing: a multicultural society stands on four pillars: accept differences, respect them, tolerate them, and appreciate them — each one a step beyond the last.
Accept: it exists, and all are treated with respect.
Respect: what differences bring is a gift.
Tolerate: even what you dislike may exist.
Appreciate: go and learn from it —
but never help yourself to it.
Four pillars. One society stands.
