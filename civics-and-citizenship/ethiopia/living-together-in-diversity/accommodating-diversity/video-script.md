# Video script — Accommodating Diversity

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing: accommodation is the strategy that recognises more than one community in the state and secures their coexistence — set against integration's demand to blend, and assimilation, which eliminates difference altogether.
Three streets answer the stranger.
Disappear. Blend in. Or: there is room here.
Accommodation makes room — in the open.
Difference walks in public,
and nobody asks its permission.
That is a society accommodating.
