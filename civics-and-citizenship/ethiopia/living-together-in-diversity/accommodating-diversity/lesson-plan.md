# Lesson plan — Accommodating Diversity

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Living Together in Diversity · **Lesson:** Accommodating Diversity
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 24 — Living Together in Diversity (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** accommodation is the strategy that recognises more than one community in the state and secures their coexistence — set against integration's demand to blend, and assimilation, which eliminates difference altogether.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the citizenship levels before it — the learner knows one constitution over one country of many peoples; this band teaches how those peoples live together inside it.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. ## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Elements of Diversity. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Define accommodation and diversity management.
2. Contrast accommodation, integration and assimilation.
3. Say when a society counts as accommodating.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else.

## If the learner is stuck
- Confuses integration with accommodation → integration privatises difference; accommodation recognises communities in the state itself.
- Thinks accommodation is declared by policy papers → it is achieved when components mutually accept differences *and* everyone can participate fully.
