# Accommodating Diversity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Accommodation recognises —
a) one community only  b) more than one community in the state, and secures their coexistence  c) no communities  d) communities abroad
**Answer: b) more than one community in the state, and secures their coexistence**

**2.** Assimilation is the strategy that —
a) values difference  b) ignores, eliminates and excludes diversity  c) makes room  d) shares power
**Answer: b) ignores, eliminates and excludes diversity**

**3.** Diversity management creates an environment where similarities and differences are —
a) hidden  b) valued  c) taxed  d) ranked
**Answer: b) valued**
