# Hands-on — Accommodating Diversity

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## A house of many rooms
The group draws two buildings: a many-roomed house (each room a community, one roof) and a single melting pot. Learners place their own practices in the right building and defend the placements.

## The three streets
Three corners act the three streets' answers to the arriving family. The family walks each street and reports where they could live as themselves.
