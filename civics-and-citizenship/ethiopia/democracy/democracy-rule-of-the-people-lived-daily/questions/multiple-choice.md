# Democracy — rule of the people, lived daily — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) **"Demos" and "kratos" mean —
a) people and rule  b) city and army  c) law and king
**Answer: a) people and rule**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) **"By the people" in Lincoln's formula means —
a) rulers act for the people  b) the people participate and govern themselves  c) power is legitimate
**Answer: b) the people participate and govern themselves**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) **In the class case, 35 pupils elect —
a) one teacher's favourite  b) two class monitors for life  c) a male and a female representative
**Answer: c) a male and a female representative**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) **The core of democracy as a way of life is —
a) freedom  b) silence  c) obedience without question
**Answer: a) freedom**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) **Is democracy lawlessness?
a) yes — anyone may do anything  b) no — it rests on the rule of law  c) yes — in schools only
**Answer: b) no — it rests on the rule of law**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) **Democracy is built —
a) in a single election day  b) by one strong leader  c) over time, not overnight
**Answer: c) over time, not overnight**
