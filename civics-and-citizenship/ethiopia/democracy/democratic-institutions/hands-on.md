# Hands-on — Democratic Institutions

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Run the school election
Set up a class election with real jobs: a commission of three runs registration, fixes the date, secures the ballot box and educates voters. Afterwards, check your commission against the nine functions — which did you miss?

## The watchdog's folder
One learner is the watchdog outside a ministry door, holding a folder of public advice. The ministry must answer three questions from the folder, kindly and on the record. Swap roles and repeat.
