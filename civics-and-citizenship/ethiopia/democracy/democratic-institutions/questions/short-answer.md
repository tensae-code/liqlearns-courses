# Democratic Institutions — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** List any five of the electoral commission's nine functions.
**Answer:** From: conducting elections; referendums and plebiscites; registering voters; delimiting constituencies; registering and monitoring parties; providing electoral officers and materials; fixing election dates; polling-station security; educating the electorate.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How does the parties' picture in this lesson differ from the last lesson?
**Answer:** In the last lesson parties are actors — vectors carrying democracy toward power. Here they are standing institutions with nine listed functions, doing the work whoever wins.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** List any four of the pressure groups' eight functions.
**Answer:** From: useful advice and suggestions; economic stability; general welfare services; educating members and public on national issues and human rights; link between people and government; watchdog over public policies; supporting candidates and parties; manpower bank of experts.
