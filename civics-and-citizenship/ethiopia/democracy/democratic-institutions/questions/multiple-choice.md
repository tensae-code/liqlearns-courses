# Democratic Institutions — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The test that disqualifies autocratic and oligarchic governments is —
a) equality under the law for the whole population  b) having a large army  c) owning the media  d) holding elections often
**Answer: a) equality under the law for the whole population**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which branch interprets the law?
a) The judiciary  b) The executive  c) The legislature  d) The electoral commission
**Answer: a) The judiciary**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** As institutions, parties provide a forum for —
a) national unity across ethnic and religious groups  b) one family's rule  c) silencing criticism  d) private business only
**Answer: a) national unity across ethnic and religious groups**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** A pressure group's watchdog function means it —
a) watches over public policies  b) guards buildings at night  c) trains police dogs  d) counts votes
**Answer: a) watches over public policies**
