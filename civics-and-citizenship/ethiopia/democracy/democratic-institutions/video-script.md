# Video script — Democratic Institutions

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing: democracy lasts when it is institutionalised — an electoral commission, parties and pressure groups, each with named functions, doing the work whoever is in power.
Institutions make democracy last.\nA commission runs the elections — nine jobs.\nParties stand as institutions — nine jobs.\nPressure groups watch and advise — eight jobs.\nPeople change. The work remains.
