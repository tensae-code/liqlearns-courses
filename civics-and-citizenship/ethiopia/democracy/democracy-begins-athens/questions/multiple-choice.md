# Democracy begins — Athens — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) **Democracy began in —
a) 5th-century BC Athens  b) Rome  c) Axum
**Answer: a) 5th-century BC Athens**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) **Name one of Athens' gifts.
a) hereditary kingship  b) direct participation by citizens  c) secret voting by officials
**Answer: b) direct participation by citizens**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) **The Council of 500 worked in committees of —
a) 5  b) 500  c) 50
**Answer: c) 50**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) **Athens' citizens were only —
a) men born of two Athenian parents  b) all residents of the city  c) all free adults
**Answer: a) men born of two Athenian parents**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) **Name one limit of the Athenian machinery.
a) it met too often to decide  b) it was slow and costly  c) it had no assembly
**Answer: b) it was slow and costly**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) **Ethiopia's contrasting promise is —
a) citizenship for men only  b) slavery kept legal  c) every citizen participating without discrimination
**Answer: c) every citizen participating without discrimination**
