# Lesson plan — Democracy begins — Athens

**Place in the curriculum:** Level 17 — Democracy (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** democracy began in Athens, which gave direct participation and equality among citizens — and excluded most of the people.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the levels before it in this course's path order (recommended, never forced); inside its own title it stands on the lessons before it.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — Draw a circle. Inside it, men raising hands to vote on everything themselves. Outside it stand women, slaves and foreigners who live in the same city. The world's first democracy is both of those pictures at once. Judge it whole.
2. **The explanation** — the lesson's parts in order (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 28646]: a picture of the Athenian Assembly on its hill, with the excluded (women, slaves, foreigners) drawn outside the circle — the student points inside and outside and names both.
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 28647]: a picture of the Athenian Assembly on its hill, citizens raising hands — the student counts what the hands are doing that subjects' hands never did.
   - ADD AUDIO: none named in the placements for this lesson — no audio slot is built.
3. **Say it to your companion** — explain the one thing in your own words, with the lesson's own case (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD VIDEO: the script in video-script.md, filmed — in the super-simple voice (made later, when videos are made).
4. **Questions** — from questions/: multiple-choice, matching, fill-in-the-blank — served at the learner's level and teaching-mix (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** — the closing doing block (lesson/lesson.md).
