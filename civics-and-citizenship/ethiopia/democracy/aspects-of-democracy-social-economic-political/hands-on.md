# Hands-on — Aspects of Democracy — Social, Economic, Political

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The three-legged stool
Build or draw a stool with three legs lettered Social, Economic, Political. Set a cup of water level on it. Saw one leg shorter (or fold one under) — watch the water. Say what spills in a real country.

## Face by face
In threes, each learner presents one aspect to the others as a street scene — a queue, a wage packet, a ballot — acted without words. The others name the aspect and its demand.
