# Lesson plan — Aspects of Democracy — Social, Economic, Political

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Democracy · **Lesson:** Aspects of Democracy — Social, Economic, Political
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 17 — Democracy (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** democracy can be looked at from three sides — social, economic and political — and a country is only democratic when all three faces show it.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the constitution level before it — the learner already knows the frame of the state; this level teaches democracy itself: what it is, who builds it, and the values it runs on.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short, single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09).

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Democratic Values — Liberty, Justice, Equality, just before this lesson in this level. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the social aspect: what discrimination must be removed.
2. State the economic aspect: distribution, work, leisure, fair wages.
3. State the political aspect: the rights and the freedoms underwriting them.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else.

1. **Do it first** — the learner names which aspect of democracy their country shows and which it hides, before the three aspects are taught (lesson/lesson.md).
2. **The explanation** — the social aspect — removal of discrimination; the economic aspect — equitable distribution and the rights of work; the political aspect — political rights underwritten by freedoms; democracy as a social balance of interests (lesson/lesson.md).

## If the learner is stuck
- Confuses aspects with the values of the previous lesson → values are what democracy runs on (liberty, justice, equality); aspects are the three sides from which democracy is viewed and measured.
- Recites political rights and stops → the aspect is underwritten by freedoms of speech, association and publication; rights without their freedoms are paper.
