# Aspects of Democracy — Social, Economic, Political — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The social aspect of democracy is —
a) removal of discrimination on grounds of class, caste, creed, colour, sex, ethnicity or race  b) a national dress code  c) equal army sizes  d) one official language
**Answer: a) removal of discrimination on grounds of class, caste, creed, colour, sex, ethnicity or race**

**2.** The economic aspect recognises rights to —
a) work, leisure and fair wages  b) inherit office  c) tax-free wealth for officials  d) own two votes
**Answer: a) work, leisure and fair wages**

**3.** Political rights in the political aspect include —
a) vote, contest elections, hold public office  b) only voting  c) only speech  d) only movement
**Answer: a) vote, contest elections, hold public office**
