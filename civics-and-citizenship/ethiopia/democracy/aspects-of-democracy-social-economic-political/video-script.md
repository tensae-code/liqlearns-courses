# Video script — Aspects of Democracy — Social, Economic, Political

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing: democracy can be looked at from three sides — social, economic and political — and a country is only democratic when all three faces show it.
One democracy. Three faces.\nSocial — no discrimination; all equals.\nEconomic — fair shares, work, leisure, fair wages.\nPolitical — rights to vote, stand, speak, move.\nA balance of interests, held together.
