# Video script — Principles of democracy — sovereignty, constitution, participation, checks, openness, choice, law

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. A short script — this lesson's design names no full video-script need, so the script stays short.

---

Nine stones hold democracy's arch.
The people sovereign. The constitution supreme.
Participation, checks, openness, real choice —
and the **rule of law** as the keystone stone.
Remove the keystone, and the arch falls.
