# Lesson plan — Principles of democracy — sovereignty, constitution, participation, checks, openness, choice, law

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Democracy · **Lesson:** Principles of democracy — sovereignty, constitution, participation, checks, openness, choice, law
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 17 — Democracy (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** democracy stands on nine principles, from popular sovereignty to the rule of law.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the levels before it in this course's path order (recommended, never forced); inside its own title it stands on the lessons before it.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio.
