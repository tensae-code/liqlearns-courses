# Principles of democracy — sovereignty, constitution, participation, checks, openness, choice, law — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) **Popular sovereignty means authority comes only from —
a) the consent of the governed  b) the army's strength  c) hereditary right
**Answer: a) the consent of the governed**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) **Supremacy of the constitution stands in —
a) Article 11  b) FDRE Article 9/1  c) Article 74
**Answer: b) FDRE Article 9/1**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) **Citizen participation is —
a) only a right, never a duty  b) only for officials  c) a duty as well as a right
**Answer: c) a duty as well as a right**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) **Secularism stands in —
a) FDRE Article 11  b) Article 9/1  c) Article 25
**Answer: a) FDRE Article 11**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) **One party, the lesson warns, means —
a) strong democracy  b) dictatorship  c) efficient government
**Answer: b) dictatorship**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) **The keystone principle, above king and president alike, is —
a) secrecy  b) hereditary succession  c) the rule of law
**Answer: c) the rule of law**
