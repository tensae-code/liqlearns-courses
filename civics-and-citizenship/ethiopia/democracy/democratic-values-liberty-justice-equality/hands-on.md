# Hands-on — Democratic Values — Liberty, Justice, Equality

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Build the three columns
Stand three columns of books or boxes lettered Liberty, Justice, Equality. On each, stack three cards with its parts. Remove one card at a time and say what weakens.

## Judge the case
In pairs, judge three small cases read aloud — a search without reason, an unfair share-out, a vote refused. Name the value and the part each case touches, and what the value demands.
