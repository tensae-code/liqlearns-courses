# Video script — Democratic Values — Liberty, Justice, Equality

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing: democracy runs on three values — liberty, justice and equality — and each of the three is itself threefold.
Three values hold democracy up.\nLiberty — personal, political, economic.\nJustice — distributive, corrective, procedural.\nEquality — political, social, economic.\nOne adult. One vote. One value.
