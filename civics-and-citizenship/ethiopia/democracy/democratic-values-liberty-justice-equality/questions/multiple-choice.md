# Democratic Values — Liberty, Justice, Equality — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Personal liberty includes freedom from —
a) arbitrary arrest and detention  b) all taxation  c) school rules  d) family duties
**Answer: a) arbitrary arrest and detention**

**2.** Vote-buying and intimidation attack which part of liberty?
a) Political liberty  b) Personal liberty  c) Economic liberty  d) None — they are normal
**Answer: a) Political liberty**

**3.** Sharing benefits and burdens by agreed standards of fairness is —
a) distributive justice  b) corrective justice  c) procedural justice  d) personal liberty
**Answer: a) distributive justice**

**4.** A proportional response that corrects a wrong already done is —
a) corrective justice  b) distributive justice  c) economic equality  d) political liberty
**Answer: a) corrective justice**
