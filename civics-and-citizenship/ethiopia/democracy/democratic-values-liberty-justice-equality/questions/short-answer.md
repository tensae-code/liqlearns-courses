# Democratic Values — Liberty, Justice, Equality — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name equality's three parts.
**Answer:** Political equality (one adult, one vote, one value), social equality (no hierarchy or discrimination), and economic equality (equal and fair access to national resources and services).

**2.** Name liberty's three parts, with one example inside any one of them.
**Answer:** Personal (e.g. freedom from arbitrary arrest; homes secure from unreasonable search), political (election participation without harassment or vote-buying), economic (owning and transferring property; seeking and changing employment; joining lawful unions).
