# Video script — Who Builds Democracy — Parties, Civil Society, Interest Groups, Media

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing: democracy is built by four actors — political parties, civil society, interest groups and the media — each carrying a different part of the work.
Who builds democracy?\nParties — carrying programmes to elections.\nCivil society — defending rights, seeking no throne.\nInterest groups — belts carrying your wants upward.\nMedia — free, and judged by truth, not noise.\nFour builders. One house.
