# Who Builds Democracy — Parties, Civil Society, Interest Groups, Media — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The book calls political parties — of democracy.
a) vectors  b) enemies  c) owners  d) spectators
**Answer: a) vectors**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Parties attain power through —
a) free, fair and transparent elections  b) inheritance  c) lottery  d) appointment by elders
**Answer: a) free, fair and transparent elections**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Civil society differs from parties because it —
a) does not seek to govern  b) never organises  c) is part of government  d) avoids all public questions
**Answer: a) does not seek to govern**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Interest groups are called transmission belts because they —
a) carry citizens' wants on policy to leaders who are otherwise remote  b) build roads  c) print newspapers  d) count votes
**Answer: a) carry citizens' wants on policy to leaders who are otherwise remote**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A country's media importance is measured by —
a) the quality of information given to the public  b) the number of outlets  c) the size of its transmitters  d) the age of its newspapers
**Answer: a) the quality of information given to the public**
