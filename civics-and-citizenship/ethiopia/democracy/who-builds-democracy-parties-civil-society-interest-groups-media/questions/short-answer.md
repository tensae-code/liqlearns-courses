# Who Builds Democracy — Parties, Civil Society, Interest Groups, Media — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What work do parties and civil society share?
**Answer:** Raising consciousness, defending legitimate interests, and protecting individual and collective rights and freedoms — parties do this while also seeking power; civil society does it without seeking to govern.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What must the media be, to do its democratic work?
**Answer:** Free and independent, with sufficient material and human resources — its work is the political training of citizens and the building of democratic culture.
