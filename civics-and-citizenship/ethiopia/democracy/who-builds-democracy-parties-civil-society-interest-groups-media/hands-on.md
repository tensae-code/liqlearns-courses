# Hands-on — Who Builds Democracy — Parties, Civil Society, Interest Groups, Media

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sort the voices
Write eight local voices on cards — a party branch, a women's association, a farmers' group, a radio station, a union, a youth group, a newspaper, a religious council. Sort them into the four builders and defend each placing aloud.

## Carry the message
In threes: one is a citizen with a want, one is the transmission belt, one is the distant official. Pass the want along the belt; the official may only answer what actually arrives. Feel why the belt matters.
