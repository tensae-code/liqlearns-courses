# Video script — Democracy and Social Transformation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing: democracy transforms society — through social change and civic engagement first, then through dialogue, social capital and community development.
Democracy changes society.\nA person rises beyond the status they inherited.\nA whole people rebuilds its way of life.\nDialogue with judgement suspended.\nTrust like capital — bonding, bridging.\nCommunities developing themselves.
