# Hands-on — Democracy and Social Transformation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The dialogue circle
In a circle, discuss a real school question with the dialogue rules posted: conflicting views welcome, empathy first, assumptions spoken aloud, judgement suspended. Afterwards, name which rule was hardest to keep.

## Ropes between houses
Each learner holds a string to one other learner — bonding pairs first (similar partners), then bridging pairs (different partners). Pull gently: feel how the whole net holds when one person is tugged.
