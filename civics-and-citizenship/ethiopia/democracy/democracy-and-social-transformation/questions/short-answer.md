# Democracy and Social Transformation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Through what steps does social change run, in the book's channel?
**Answer:** From awareness and attitude change, through civic participation and public will, to policy change correcting injustice — starting with the individual.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does community development weigh?
**Answer:** A community's economic, social and physical dimensions — housing, job training, commercial development, small-business start-up, and wider youth, health, recreation and cultural goals.
