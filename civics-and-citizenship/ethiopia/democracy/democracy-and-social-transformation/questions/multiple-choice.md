# Democracy and Social Transformation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first sense of social transformation is —
a) an individual changing an inherited status into an achieved one  b) a new tax law  c) a bigger army  d) a change of flag
**Answer: a) an individual changing an inherited status into an achieved one**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Civic engagement includes all of these EXCEPT —
a) inheritance of office  b) volunteering  c) voting  d) community organising
**Answer: a) inheritance of office**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In civic dialogue, judgement is —
a) suspended while viewpoints work toward understanding  b) handed to the loudest voice  c) avoided by silence  d) decided by vote at once
**Answer: a) suspended while viewpoints work toward understanding**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Bridging social capital connects —
a) diverse people, sustaining generalised reciprocity  b) only close family  c) banks to borrowers  d) villages to armies
**Answer: a) diverse people, sustaining generalised reciprocity**
