# Hands-on — Democratic Values in Indigenous Institutions

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The values on the branches
Draw a tree. On nine branches write the nine values. Under the tree, sit the elders of your locality's institution as you imagine them — then mark each branch: does your institution's shade really cover it?

## Sit in the circle
Hold a class discussion seated in a circle, elders' style: one speaker at a time, no interruption, decision by agreement. Afterwards, name which of the nine values the circle itself practised.
