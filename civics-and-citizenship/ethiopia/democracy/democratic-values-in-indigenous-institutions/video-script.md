# Video script — Democratic Values in Indigenous Institutions

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

This lesson is about one thing: Ethiopia's indigenous institutions already carry democratic values — trust, justice, inclusiveness, cooperation, dialogue, respect, reciprocity, tolerance and common humanity — in their own way of working.
Democracy's values live at home too.\nTrust. Justice. Inclusiveness.\nCooperation. Dialogue. Respect.\nReciprocity. Tolerance. Common humanity.\nThe elders' tree was carrying them all along.
