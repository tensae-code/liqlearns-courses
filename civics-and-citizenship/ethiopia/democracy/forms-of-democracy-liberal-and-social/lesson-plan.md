# Lesson plan — Forms of democracy — liberal and social

**Place in the curriculum:** Level 17 — Democracy (CURRICULUM.md).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Tell the two forms apart by the state's economic role.
2. Say what each form secures and what it does not.
3. Give the services social democracy names.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — Two town squares. In one, the state runs only the courts and the roads, and the market runs itself. In the other, the state also runs the clinic, the school and the housing. Both squares vote. Both are democracies. What differs?
