# Forms of democracy — liberal and social — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. **The two forms are sorted by —
a) how far government enters economic life  b) how old the country is  c) how large the army is
**Answer: a) how far government enters economic life**

**2. **Liberal democracy secures —
a) full economic equality  b) political and legal equality, not economic equality  c) equality of income for all
**Answer: b) political and legal equality, not economic equality**

**3. **Liberal democracy is also called —
a) social democracy  b) direct democracy  c) capitalist democracy
**Answer: c) capitalist democracy**

**4. **Social democracy pursues equality through —
a) reform within capitalism  b) abolishing all markets by decree  c) ending elections
**Answer: a) reform within capitalism**

**5. **Name one service social democracy names.
a) private armies  b) free education  c) hereditary office
**Answer: b) free education**
