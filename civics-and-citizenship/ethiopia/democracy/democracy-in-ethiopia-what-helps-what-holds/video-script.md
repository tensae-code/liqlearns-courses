# Video script — Democracy in Ethiopia — what helps it, what holds it

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. A short script — this lesson's design names no full video-script need, so the script stays short.

---

What helps Ethiopia's democracy?
Reformed institutions. Schools spreading. Roads joining.
What holds it back?
Weak institutions. Silence. Parties without alternatives. Hunger.
See both pans. Then work on the one you can lift.
