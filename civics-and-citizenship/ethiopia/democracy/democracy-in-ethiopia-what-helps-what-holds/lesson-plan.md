# Lesson plan — Democracy in Ethiopia — what helps it, what holds it

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Democracy · **Lesson:** Democracy in Ethiopia — what helps it, what holds it
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 17 — Democracy (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Ethiopia's democracy is helped by reform, education and connection — and held back by weak institutions, silence and poverty.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the levels before it in this course's path order (recommended, never forced); inside its own title it stands on the lessons before it.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. List the opportunities honestly.

3. Weigh both sides for one real institution.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — A balance. On one pan: new schools, roads being built, institutions being reformed. On the other: empty institutions, media the public cannot reach, hunger. Which pan is heavier today — and what would you add to the lighter one?
2. **The explanation** — the lesson's parts in order (lesson/lesson.md).

   - ADD AUDIO: none named in the placements for this lesson — no audio slot is built.
3. **Say it to your companion** — explain the one thing in your own words, with the lesson's own case (lesson/lesson.md).
