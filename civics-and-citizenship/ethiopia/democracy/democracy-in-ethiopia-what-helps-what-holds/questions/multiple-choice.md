# Democracy in Ethiopia — what helps it, what holds it — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. **Name one opportunity for democracy in Ethiopia.
a) fast education expansion raising consciousness  b) media the public cannot access  c) an uninformed society
**Answer: a) fast education expansion raising consciousness**

**2. **Which institutions are named as being reformed?
a) the army and the prisons  b) the Human Rights Commission and the National Electoral Board  c) foreign election boards
**Answer: b) the Human Rights Commission and the National Electoral Board**

**3. **Name one challenge.
a) too much infrastructure  b) too many alternative parties  c) weak institutions, so administration follows leaders' preferences
**Answer: c) weak institutions, so administration follows leaders' preferences**

**4. **Why is poverty itself a challenge to democracy?
a) hunger puts basic rights in question  b) poor people cannot vote legally  c) poverty ends elections
**Answer: a) hunger puts basic rights in question**
