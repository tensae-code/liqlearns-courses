# Video script — Indigenous democracy in Ethiopia — councils, consensus, elders

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. This is the lesson's full read-aloud script, where the design names a video-script need.

---

Eight names. One pattern.
**Jarsuma. Shimigilina. Yejoka. Deira-Cimma.**
**Enashma. Baito. Mada'a. Xeer.**

Elders in a circle. Open debate.
Agreement by consensus.
And in the Gada system, power itself
handed over in peace, on schedule, like a staff.
Ethiopia did not wait for the word democracy
to practise the thing.
