# Indigenous democracy in Ethiopia — councils, consensus, elders — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) **Name the Oromo tradition in the Grade 7 set.
a) Jarsuma  b) Xeer  c) Baito
**Answer: a) Jarsuma**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) **Xeer belongs to which people?
a) the Gurage  b) the Somali  c) the Afar
**Answer: b) the Somali**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) **The shared pattern of all eight is —
a) written constitutions and parties  b) hereditary kingship  c) open debate, consensus, elders carrying the agreement
**Answer: c) open debate, consensus, elders carrying the agreement**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) **Sidama Luwa is noted for —
a) peaceful transfer of power  b) written law codes  c) tax collection
**Answer: a) peaceful transfer of power**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) **Gada governs —
a) only religious ceremonies  b) political, economic, social and religious life  c) only cattle disputes
**Answer: b) political, economic, social and religious life**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) **These institutions still work —
a) instead of all courts, everywhere  b) only in museums  c) alongside the courts, especially in rural areas
**Answer: c) alongside the courts, especially in rural areas**
