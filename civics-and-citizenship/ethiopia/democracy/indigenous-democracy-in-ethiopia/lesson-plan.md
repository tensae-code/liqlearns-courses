# Lesson plan — Indigenous democracy in Ethiopia — councils, consensus, elders

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Democracy · **Lesson:** Indigenous democracy in Ethiopia — councils, consensus, elders
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 17 — Democracy (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Ethiopia practised democracy in its own institutions — councils, consensus and elders — long before the word arrived.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the levels before it in this course's path order (recommended, never forced); inside its own title it stands on the lessons before it.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. ## Sub lessons
1. **Ethiopia's own traditions — the Grade 7 set** — Long before modern states and multi-party systems, Ethiopian and African societies governed themselves with **open debate, consensus and participation**.
2. **Working democracies — the Grade 8 depth** — The deeper telling shows these as **working democracies** with offices, terms and accountability.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the Grade 7 set of eight traditions.
2. Describe how the Grade 8 institutions work.
3. Explain Gada's peaceful transfer of power.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — Under a great tree, elders sit in a circle. No ballot box, no parliament building — yet decisions are debated openly, agreed by consensus, and disputes are settled. Is this democracy? This lesson says: look at what it does, not what it is called.
2. **The explanation** — the lesson's parts in order (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 28663]: a picture of elders seated in a circle under a tree with the eight practice names placed around Ethiopia's map — the student matches three names to their peoples.
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 28664]: a picture of six named institutions placed on Ethiopia's map with an assembly tree at each — the student picks one institution and says what it does.
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 28665]: a picture of elders seated in a circle under a tree, the eight named traditions lettered around the ring — the student reads the ring aloud in order.
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 28666]: a picture of an assembly under a sycamore tree, offices passing a staff to the next generation — the student says why the passing is peaceful by design.
   - ADD GIF [GIF 28667]: a GIF of the staff of office being handed over from one generation's hands to the next — the student watches and says what Gada transfers this way.
   - ADD AUDIO: none named in the placements for this lesson — no audio slot is built.
