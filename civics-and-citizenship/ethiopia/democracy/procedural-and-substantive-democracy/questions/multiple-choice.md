# Procedural and substantive democracy — the vote, and what follows it — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. **Procedural democracy judges —
a) how decisions are made  b) what government delivers  c) how rich the country is
**Answer: a) how decisions are made**

**2. **Substantive democracy judges —
a) only the election process  b) what government actually delivers  c) the size of the parliament
**Answer: b) what government actually delivers**

**3. **Name one procedural measure.
a) roads built after the election  b) clinics opened  c) free elections properly run
**Answer: c) free elections properly run**

**4. **Under the substantive measure, the people must have —
a) a real, continuing say, not merely a vote  b) a vote once in a lifetime  c) no say between elections
**Answer: a) a real, continuing say, not merely a vote**
