## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Define both kinds with their measures.
2. Apply both measures to the same election.
3. Say why a vote alone is not enough.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — An election is held. The voting is free and fair. Five years later: no new school, no clean water, no change. Was that democracy? One measure says yes. Another says not yet. Learn both measures in this lesson.
