# Hands-on — Procedural and substantive democracy — the vote, and what follows it

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening again, for real
An election is held. The voting is free and fair. Five years later: no new school, no clean water, no change. Was that democracy? One measure says yes. Another says not yet. Learn both measures in this lesson.

## Sort it on the table
On paper, write the lesson's key pairs from its matching questions — one term per slip. Shuffle the slips, then sort and match them again from memory, saying each meaning aloud as you place it. Any pair you cannot explain goes back into the lesson for one more read.

## Take it home
Find this lesson where you live: one real example of its one thing — procedural democracy judges the process of deciding; substantive democracy judges what government actually delivers — in your family, your school or your street. Write two sentences about it: what happened, and what the lesson says about it. Bring the sentences back and read them to your companion.
