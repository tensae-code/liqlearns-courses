# Exercising democracy — directly and through representatives — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) **Ethiopians elect parliamentarians every ____ years.
**Answer: five**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) **A direct vote on a set question is a ____.
**Answer: referendum**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) **Members must stay in ____ with their voters.
**Answer: contact**
