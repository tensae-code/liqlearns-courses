# Exercising democracy — directly and through representatives — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) **In direct democracy, decisions are made by —
a) all adult citizens themselves  b) elected officials only  c) judges
**Answer: a) all adult citizens themselves**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) **A referendum is —
a) a speech by a minister  b) a direct vote of all citizens on a set question  c) a court ruling
**Answer: b) a direct vote of all citizens on a set question**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) **Direct democracy still works well in —
a) running a whole modern state alone  b) replacing all elections  c) local committees, schools and associations
**Answer: c) local committees, schools and associations**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) **Ethiopians elect parliamentarians every —
a) five years  b) two years  c) seven years
**Answer: a) five years**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) **A member of parliament represents —
a) only their own party  b) all citizens, not only their voters  c) only their home town
**Answer: b) all citizens, not only their voters**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) **The people's continuing control is —
a) daily referendums on every law  b) appointing ministers directly  c) removing failed members at the next election
**Answer: c) removing failed members at the next election**
