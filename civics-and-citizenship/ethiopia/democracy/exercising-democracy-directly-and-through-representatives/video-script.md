# Video script — Exercising democracy — directly and through representatives

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. A short script — this lesson's design names no full video-script need, so the script stays short.

---

Two ways to rule yourselves.
**Directly** — everyone votes on the question: a referendum.
**Through representatives** — you elect them for five years,
they decide, and you judge them at the next election.
The vote is a leash, not a gift.
