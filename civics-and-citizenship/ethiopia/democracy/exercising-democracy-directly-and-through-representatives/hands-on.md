# Hands-on — Exercising democracy — directly and through representatives

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening again, for real
Your zone could become a region. Who should decide — every adult voting on that one question, or representatives you elected earlier deciding for you? Both are democracy at work, but they are not the same work. This lesson separates them.

## Sort it on the table
On paper, write the lesson's key pairs from its matching questions — one term per slip. Shuffle the slips, then sort and match them again from memory, saying each meaning aloud as you place it. Any pair you cannot explain goes back into the lesson for one more read.

## Take it home
Find this lesson where you live: one real example of its one thing — citizens exercise democracy directly — deciding themselves, as in a referendum — or indirectly, through elected representatives — in your family, your school or your street. Write two sentences about it: what happened, and what the lesson says about it. Bring the sentences back and read them to your companion.
