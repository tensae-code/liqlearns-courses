# Democracy and Democratization — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What fills out the bare definition 'rule by the people'?
**Answer:** Equality, individual freedom, and real opportunity for common people to be actual wielders of power — with compromise and bargaining inside decision-making.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why can democratization not be an overnight programme?
**Answer:** Because it is a process, not an event: it needs time, stages, and actors — people and organisations who do the walking.
