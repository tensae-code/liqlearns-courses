# Democracy and Democratization — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The word democracy is built from demos and kratos, meaning —
a) people and rule  b) king and law  c) army and state  d) wealth and power
**Answer: a) people and rule**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does the book refuse one single fixed definition of democracy?
a) The word is elastic — it stretches with time, place and circumstance  b) Nobody has ever tried to define it  c) It is a secret word  d) Definitions are forbidden
**Answer: a) The word is elastic — it stretches with time, place and circumstance**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Democratization is best described as —
a) a long-term, open-ended process toward democracy  b) a single election  c) a one-day ceremony  d) a military parade
**Answer: a) a long-term, open-ended process toward democracy**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is a waypoint on the democratization road?
a) Semi-democracy  b) Monarchy forever  c) One-party rule  d) Rule by decree
**Answer: a) Semi-democracy**
