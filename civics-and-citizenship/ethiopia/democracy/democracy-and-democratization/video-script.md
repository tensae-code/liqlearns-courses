# Video script — Democracy and Democratization

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

This lesson is about one thing: democracy is rule by the people — and democratization is the long road a country walks to reach it, not an overnight programme.
Democracy.\nDemos — the people. Kratos — rule.\nRule by the people.\nNo single stiff definition — the word lives.\nAnd democratization?\nThe long road toward it. Walked slowly. Walked together.
