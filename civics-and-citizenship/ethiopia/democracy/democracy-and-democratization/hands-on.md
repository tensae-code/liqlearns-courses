# Hands-on — Democracy and Democratization

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two stones, one arch
Write DEMOS on one stone or card and KRATOS on another. Lean them together like an arch and write on the keystone card what holds them up: rule by the people. If either stone is removed, say what falls.

## Walk the road
Lay three cards on the ground — authoritarian, semi-democracy, democracy. Stand on the first and walk slowly to the last, naming aloud one thing that must change at each step. Slow is the point.
