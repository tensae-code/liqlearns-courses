# Video script — Values of democracy — tolerance, liberty, justice, equality, civility, diversity

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. A short script — this lesson's design names no full video-script need, so the script stays short.

---

Principles are the skeleton. **Values** are the life.
Tolerance, so difference can live together.
Liberty, justice, equality.
Civility — even with no officer watching.
And diversity, valued: **unity in diversity**.
