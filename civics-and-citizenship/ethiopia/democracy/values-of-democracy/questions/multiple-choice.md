# Values of democracy — tolerance, liberty, justice, equality, civility, diversity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) **Tolerance is needed because —
a) natural differences make coexistence impossible without it  b) everyone is the same  c) laws command feelings
**Answer: a) natural differences make coexistence impossible without it**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) **Gandhi's quoted words call intolerance —
a) a private virtue  b) a form of violence  c) a minor fault
**Answer: b) a form of violence**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) **The mobile schools case teaches justice because —
a) herders refuse all schools  b) schools are free everywhere already  c) a fixed school would exclude herder children
**Answer: c) a fixed school would exclude herder children**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) **Civility is pictured by —
a) drivers yielding to pedestrians with no officer watching  b) queues at a ballot box  c) a judge at a bench
**Answer: a) drivers yielding to pedestrians with no officer watching**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) **"Unity in diversity" belongs to —
a) separation of powers  b) valuing diversity  c) secularism
**Answer: b) valuing diversity**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) **Distributive justice may give a disadvantaged region —
a) exactly the same as rich regions, always  b) less, to save money  c) more than an equal share, to stand truly equal
**Answer: c) more than an equal share, to stand truly equal**
