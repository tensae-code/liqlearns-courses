# Lesson plan — Values of democracy — tolerance, liberty, justice, equality, civility, diversity

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Democracy · **Lesson:** Values of democracy — tolerance, liberty, justice, equality, civility, diversity
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 17 — Democracy (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** democracy lives as culture through six values: tolerance, liberty, justice, equality, civility, and valuing diversity.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the levels before it in this course's path order (recommended, never forced); inside its own title it stands on the lessons before it.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. ## Sub lessons
1. **Tolerance, liberty, justice** — Principles are democracy's skeleton; **values** are its lived culture.
2. **Equality, civility, valuing diversity** — **Equality:** equal access to political, social and economic opportunity — wealth, ethnicity and religion are external to equal humanity.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Principles of democracy. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Define each of the six values in action.
2. Work the mobile-schools case for justice.
3. Explain distributive justice with the Afar example.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — Herders move with their cattle for pasture and water. Their children cannot attend a fixed school. If the school will not move, are those children treated justly? Hold that case — it carries this lesson's idea of justice.
2. **The explanation** — the lesson's parts in order (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 28660]: a picture of a mobile classroom tent beside herded cattle, children arriving with books — the student says which value the moving school serves.
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 28661]: a picture of pupils of many backgrounds in one meeting circle, each with an equal-sized place — the student names the values the circle shows.
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 28662]: a picture of a water pipe reaching a dry distant village while a nearer one already flows, labelled distributive justice — the student explains why equal shares were not enough here.
   - ADD AUDIO: none named in the placements for this lesson — no audio slot is built.
