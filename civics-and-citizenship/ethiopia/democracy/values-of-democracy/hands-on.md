# Hands-on — Values of democracy — tolerance, liberty, justice, equality, civility, diversity

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening again, for real
Herders move with their cattle for pasture and water. Their children cannot attend a fixed school. If the school will not move, are those children treated justly? Hold that case — it carries this lesson's idea of justice.

## Sort it on the table
On paper, write the lesson's key pairs from its matching questions — one term per slip. Shuffle the slips, then sort and match them again from memory, saying each meaning aloud as you place it. Any pair you cannot explain goes back into the lesson for one more read.

## Take it home
Find this lesson where you live: one real example of its one thing — democracy lives as culture through six values: tolerance, liberty, justice, equality, civility, and valuing diversity — in your family, your school or your street. Write two sentences about it: what happened, and what the lesson says about it. Bring the sentences back and read them to your companion.
