# Lesson plan — Elements of the state — people, land, government, sovereignty

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** The State and Government · **Lesson:** Elements of the state — people, land, government, sovereignty
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 16 — The State and Government (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a state stands on four elements: population, territory, government and sovereignty.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the levels before it in this course's path order (recommended, never forced); inside its own title it stands on the lessons before it.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. ## Sub lessons
1. **Population and territory** — **Population:** no fixed size is required.
2. **Government and sovereignty** — **Government** is the state's working arm: it makes, enforces and interprets laws and carries out the state's authority.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: The state — an organised political community. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the four elements of a state.
2. Explain why neither people nor land has a fixed size.
3. Tell sovereignty's two faces apart.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — Take the definition from the last lesson apart. A state was called a people, on land, under a government, free from outside control. Four pieces. Before reading, guess: must the people be huge? Must the land be vast? The answers may surprise you.
2. **The explanation** — the lesson's parts in order (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 28612]: a picture of Ethiopia's map with its six neighbours labelled and a disputed boundary line between two village plots — the student names a neighbour by its direction.
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 28613]: a picture of a flag and anthem scroll above two panels: a council ruling inside the border; ambassadors meeting outside it as equals — the student matches each panel to internal or external sovereignty.
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 28614]: a picture of a four-part diagram: people, land outline, parliament building, flag of sovereignty — the student labels all four elements from memory.
   - ADD AUDIO: none named in the placements for this lesson — no audio slot is built.
3. **Say it to your companion** — explain the one thing in your own words, with the lesson's own case (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD VIDEO: the script in video-script.md, filmed — in the super-simple voice (made later, when videos are made).
4. **Questions** — from questions/: multiple-choice, matching, fill-in-the-blank — served at the learner's level and teaching-mix (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** — the closing doing block (lesson/lesson.md).
