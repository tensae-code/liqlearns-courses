# Lesson plan — The state — an organised political community

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** The State and Government · **Lesson:** The state — an organised political community
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 16 — The State and Government (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a state is a formally established, organised political community under one government.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the levels before it in this course's path order (recommended, never forced); inside its own title it stands on the lessons before it.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. ## Sub lessons

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the Belonging level. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Define a state in your own words.
2. Say why states exist.
3. Name Ethiopia as one of the oldest states.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — Look at a map of the world. It is divided into coloured shapes, each with a name, a flag and a government. Why do those shapes exist at all? What would happen to shared life inside one shape if there were no laws to settle disputes? Start there.
2. **The explanation** — the lesson's parts in order (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 28610]: a picture of a map outline filled with people, a government building and a border line — the student names the pieces of the definition they can see.
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 28611]: a picture of a map of Ethiopia ringed by its uniqueness list: river, calendar page, coffee branch, script letters — the student picks one item and says what makes it Ethiopia's own.
   - ADD AUDIO: none named in the placements for this lesson — no audio slot is built.
3. **Say it to your companion** — explain the one thing in your own words, with the lesson's own case (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD VIDEO: the script in video-script.md, filmed — in the super-simple voice (made later, when videos are made).
