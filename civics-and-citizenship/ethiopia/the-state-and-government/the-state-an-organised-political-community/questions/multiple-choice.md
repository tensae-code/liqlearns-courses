# The state — an organised political community — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. **A state is —
a) an organised political community under one government  b) any large crowd  c) a single ruling family
**Answer: a) an organised political community under one government**

**2. **States exist because shared life needs —
a) more borders on maps  b) laws to settle disputes  c) a flag for every village
**Answer: b) laws to settle disputes**

**3. **Roughly how many states exist today?
a) about 50  b) over 2,000  c) over 200
**Answer: c) over 200**

**4. **Ethiopia is presented as —
a) among the oldest states  b) the youngest state  c) a state founded last year
**Answer: a) among the oldest states**
