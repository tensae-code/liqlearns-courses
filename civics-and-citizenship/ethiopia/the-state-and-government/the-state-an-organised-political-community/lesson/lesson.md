# The state — an organised political community

## Do it first

Look at a map of the world. It is divided into coloured shapes, each with a name, a flag and a government. Why do those shapes exist at all? What would happen to shared life inside one shape if there were no laws to settle disputes? Start there.

## What a state is

A **state** is a formally established, organised political community under one government: a more-or-less numerous people, living permanently on a set territory, with their own government, to which most of them submit, free from outside control.

States exist because shared life needs laws to settle disputes and someone to stand behind them. Over 200 states exist today. Ethiopia is presented in this course as among the oldest of them — a political community with a very long history.

Hold the shape of the definition: people, territory, government, freedom from outside control. The next lesson takes those pieces one by one.
