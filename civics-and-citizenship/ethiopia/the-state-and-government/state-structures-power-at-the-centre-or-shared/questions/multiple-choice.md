# State structures — power at the centre or shared — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. **State structure means —
a) how power is distributed among a state's parts  b) how rich a state is  c) how a state elects its president
**Answer: a) how power is distributed among a state's parts**

**2. **Name the three structures.
a) monarchy, aristocracy, democracy  b) unitary, federal, confederal  c) local, regional, national
**Answer: b) unitary, federal, confederal**

**3. **Is a federal state automatically democratic?
a) yes — always  b) yes — by definition  c) no — no structure is democratic or undemocratic by itself
**Answer: c) no — no structure is democratic or undemocratic by itself**

**4. **In household Z, decisions are made by —
a) the father alone  b) the whole family by written rules  c) the children
**Answer: a) the father alone**
