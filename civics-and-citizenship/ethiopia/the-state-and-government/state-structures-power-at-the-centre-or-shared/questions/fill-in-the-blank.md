# State structures — power at the centre or shared — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word or words.

**1. **State structure is the ____ of power among a state's parts.
**Answer: distribution**

**2. **No structure is ____ or undemocratic by itself.
**Answer: democratic**

**3. **Ethiopia shares power constitutionally with its ____.
**Answer: regions**
