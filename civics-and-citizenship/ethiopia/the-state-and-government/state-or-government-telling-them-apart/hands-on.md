# Hands-on — State or government? — telling them apart

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening again, for real
Ethiopia has existed for a very long time. Its governments, in just the last fifty years, have been an empire, a military regime and a republic. So which changed — the state, or its governments? Keep that question open through this lesson.

## Sort it on the table
On paper, write the lesson's key pairs from its matching questions — one term per slip. Shuffle the slips, then sort and match them again from memory, saying each meaning aloud as you place it. Any pair you cannot explain goes back into the lesson for one more read.

## Take it home
Find this lesson where you live: one real example of its one thing — the state endures and holds everyone; a government is its temporary working part — in your family, your school or your street. Write two sentences about it: what happened, and what the lesson says about it. Bring the sentences back and read them to your companion.
