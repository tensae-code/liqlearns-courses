# Video script — State or government? — telling them apart

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. A short script — this lesson's design names no full video-script need, so the script stays short.

---

The **state** endures. **Governments** change.
The state is everyone, everywhere in the land, for good.
A government is a few people, in offices, for a time.
Ethiopia stands. Its governments have come and gone.
That difference explains half of politics.
