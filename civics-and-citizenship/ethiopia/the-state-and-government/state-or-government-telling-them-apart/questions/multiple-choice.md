# State or government? — telling them apart — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) **Can a state exist without a government?
a) no — government is one of the state's elements  b) yes — easily  c) yes — most do
**Answer: a) no — government is one of the state's elements**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) **Government is called the state's —
a) flag  b) brain  c) border
**Answer: b) brain**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) **The state gives the government its —
a) salary  b) uniform  c) legitimacy
**Answer: c) legitimacy**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) **The state is abstract; the government is —
a) concrete — named people in offices  b) also invisible  c) a foreign idea
**Answer: a) concrete — named people in offices**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) **State membership is —
a) won by election  b) automatic and compulsory  c) bought by application
**Answer: b) automatic and compulsory**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) **Which endures?
a) the government  b) neither  c) the state — governments come and go
**Answer: c) the state — governments come and go**

**7. (Sub lesson 2) **Government is intrinsic because —
a) even stateless societies govern themselves  b) every state has a flag  c) laws are written down
**Answer: a) even stateless societies govern themselves**
