# State or government? — telling them apart — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) **There is no state without a ____.
**Answer: government**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) **The state ____ while governments come and go.
**Answer: endures**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) **The state holds ____ power; government derives its power from it.
**Answer: original**
