# State structures — the federal state — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) **A federal state's powers are divided by —
a) the constitution itself  b) the centre's daily decisions  c) foreign governments
**Answer: a) the constitution itself**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) **Name one federal (centre) power.
a) state police  b) defence  c)  regional public order
**Answer: b) defence**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) **Federalism specially suits —
a) tiny uniform islands only  b) countries with one language only  c) large, diverse countries
**Answer: c) large, diverse countries**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) **Article 51 lists —
a) the federal powers  b) the state powers  c) citizens' duties
**Answer: a) the federal powers**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) **Powers not listed anywhere belong to —
a) the federal centre  b) the states  c) no one
**Answer: b) the states**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) **A centre that deposes regional cabinets at will —
a) follows Article 52  b) strengthens federalism  c) breaks federal principle
**Answer: c) breaks federal principle**
