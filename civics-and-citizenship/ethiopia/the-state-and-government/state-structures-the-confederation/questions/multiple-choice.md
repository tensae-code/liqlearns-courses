# State structures — the confederation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. **In a confederation, members keep —
a) full sovereignty  b) no sovereignty  c) only cultural rights
**Answer: a) full sovereignty**

**2. **The confederal centre is —
a) legally supreme  b) weak — members may ignore its lead  c) a world government
**Answer: b) weak — members may ignore its lead**

**3. **Name one common goal members delegate for.
a) setting every local law  b) choosing each other's leaders  c) defence
**Answer: c) defence**

**4. **The modern-form example given is —
a) the European Union  b) the United Nations army  c) a unitary kingdom
**Answer: a) the European Union**

**5. **The guide's classic historical case of a confederation is —
a) the Swiss cantons  b) the Roman Empire  c) a unitary kingdom
**Answer: a) the Swiss cantons** — sovereign cantons in a league with a weak centre (teacher guide — evidence).

**6. **The Confederate States of America (1861–65) show a confederation's centre as —
a) all-powerful over its members  b) unable to command its member states  c) headed by a king
**Answer: b) unable to command its member states** — the arrangement lived only as long as the members' agreement held (teacher guide — evidence).
