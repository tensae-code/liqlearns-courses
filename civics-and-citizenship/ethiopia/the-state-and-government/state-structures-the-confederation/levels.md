# State structures — the confederation — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state and use the whole lesson's one thing — a confederation is an alliance of sovereign states delegating a little power to a weak shared centre — in their own words, on a fresh example. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners of the same one thing: the lesson's cases judged from the other side, its lists recalled in a different order, its rule applied to the learner's own street and school.
