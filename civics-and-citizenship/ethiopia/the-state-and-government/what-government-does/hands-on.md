# Hands-on — What government does — order, services, security, the economy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening again, for real
Walk, in your mind, through one ordinary day. A road you travel on, a health post, safety on the street, the price of bread. Behind each of these stands work a government does. This lesson sorts that work into four functions.

## Sort it on the table
On paper, write the lesson's key pairs from its matching questions — one term per slip. Shuffle the slips, then sort and match them again from memory, saying each meaning aloud as you place it. Any pair you cannot explain goes back into the lesson for one more read.

## Take it home
Find this lesson where you live: one real example of its one thing — governments do four common things: keep law and order, provide public services, guard national security, and steer the economy — in your family, your school or your street. Write two sentences about it: what happened, and what the lesson says about it. Bring the sentences back and read them to your companion.
