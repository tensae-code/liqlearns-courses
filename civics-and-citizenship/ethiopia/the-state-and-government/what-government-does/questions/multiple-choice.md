# What government does — order, services, security, the economy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) **Name the four common functions of government.
a) law and order, public services, national security, economic decisions  b) farming, trade, sport, music  c) policing only
**Answer: a) law and order, public services, national security, economic decisions**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) **Law and order protects —
a) only government buildings  b) life, rights and property  c) only soldiers
**Answer: b) life, rights and property**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) **Public services are tied to —
a) private profit  b) party membership  c) basic human rights
**Answer: c) basic human rights**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) **National security is guarded through —
a) defence forces and peacekeeping  b) school examinations  c) market days
**Answer: a) defence forces and peacekeeping**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) **Why does government steer the economy?
a) to own every shop  b) because no country can meet every want  c) to set every family's budget
**Answer: b) because no country can meet every want**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) **The dam pictured as an economic decision is —
a) a village well  b) a private mill  c) the Grand Ethiopian Renaissance Dam
**Answer: c) the Grand Ethiopian Renaissance Dam**
