# Lesson plan — What government does — order, services, security, the economy

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** The State and Government · **Lesson:** What government does — order, services, security, the economy
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 16 — The State and Government (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** governments do four common things: keep law and order, provide public services, guard national security, and steer the economy.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the levels before it in this course's path order (recommended, never forced); inside its own title it stands on the lessons before it.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
1. **Law and order, and public services** — **Law and order:** government makes laws governing everyone's activity, and keeps courts and mechanisms that settle conflicts in time and protect life, rights and property.
2. **National security and economic decisions** — **National security:** government guards the country against external and internal threats through defence forces and peacekeeping, and keeps good foreign relations so danger is met with friends, not alone.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Elements of the state. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. List the four common functions of government.
2. Give a local example of each function.
3. Say why a government steers the economy at all.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — Walk, in your mind, through one ordinary day. A road you travel on, a health post, safety on the street, the price of bread. Behind each of these stands work a government does. This lesson sorts that work into four functions.
