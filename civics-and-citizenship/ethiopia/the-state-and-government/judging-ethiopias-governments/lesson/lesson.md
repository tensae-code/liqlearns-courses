# Judging Ethiopia's governments — what each built, what each broke

## Do it first

Three columns on a page. In each column, a building on one side and a crack on the other. No era in this lesson is all building or all crack. Your job is to weigh, not to cheer or condemn.

## Three eras, one measure

**The monarchy built:** the state and nation itself, a ministerial executive, unbroken sovereignty and diplomacy — Adwa standing as a world symbol, Addis Ababa made Africa's capital, and the 1931 written constitution as a foundation. **It broke:** it ignored the peoples' diversity, kept hereditary power and a state religion, and allowed no political parties.

**The Derg built:** sovereignty and unity held — "Ethiopia First" — land redistributed to the tillers, literacy campaigns, equality of cultures decreed, and secularism. **It broke:** gross rights violations, including the Red Terror, and interstate and internal wars, all under one party.

**The post-1991 order built:** self-determination rights — language, cultural and representation rights — territorial autonomy inside unity-in-diversity, infrastructure growth and secularism. **It broke:** federalism implemented unevenly, feeding extremism, conflict and displacement.

Same measures, three eras. That is what judging history means.
