# Video script — Judging Ethiopia's governments — what each built, what each broke

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. A short script — this lesson's design names no full video-script need, so the script stays short.

---

Judge every era by the same measure.
The monarchy: built a nation — ignored its diversity.
The Derg: land to the tillers — and the Red Terror.
The republic: rights and autonomy — unevenly delivered.
A building and a crack, in every column.
