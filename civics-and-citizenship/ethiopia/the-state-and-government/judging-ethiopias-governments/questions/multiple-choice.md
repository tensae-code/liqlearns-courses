# Judging Ethiopia's governments — what each built, what each broke — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. **One strength of the monarchy was —
a) state and nation building, with Adwa as its symbol  b) land to the tillers  c) abolishing hereditary power
**Answer: a) state and nation building, with Adwa as its symbol**

**2. **One limitation of the monarchy was —
a) it allowed many parties  b) it ignored the peoples' diversity  c) it separated state and religion
**Answer: b) it ignored the peoples' diversity**

**3. **One strength of the Derg was —
a) hereditary succession protected  b) a state religion kept  c) land redistributed to the tillers
**Answer: c) land redistributed to the tillers**

**4. **One limitation of the Derg was —
a) gross rights violations, including the Red Terror  b) too much press freedom  c) weak central unity
**Answer: a) gross rights violations, including the Red Terror**

**5. **One strength of the post-1991 order is —
a) a state religion  b) self-determination rights in unity-in-diversity  c) one-party rule as a goal
**Answer: b) self-determination rights in unity-in-diversity**

**6. **One limitation of the post-1991 order is —
a) no infrastructure growth  b) no secularism  c) federalism implemented unevenly
**Answer: c) federalism implemented unevenly**
