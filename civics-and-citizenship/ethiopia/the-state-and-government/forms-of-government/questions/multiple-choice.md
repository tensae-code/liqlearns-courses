# Forms of government — one, few, force, the people — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. **Governments here are classified by —
a) the number wielding sovereign power  b) the size of the country  c) the age of the ruler
**Answer: a) the number wielding sovereign power**

**2. **In a monarchy, power is held by —
a) elected representatives  b) one hereditary ruler  c) a small council of judges
**Answer: b) one hereditary ruler**

**3. **Aristocracy means rule by —
a) the whole people  b) one soldier  c) a few held to be best qualified
**Answer: c) a few held to be best qualified**

**4. **Dictatorship is power —
a) held by force, without limit  b) shared by election  c) limited by a constitution
**Answer: a) held by force, without limit**

**5. **In a democracy, rulers are —
a) born to the throne  b) chosen by the citizens  c) appointed by an army
**Answer: b) chosen by the citizens**
