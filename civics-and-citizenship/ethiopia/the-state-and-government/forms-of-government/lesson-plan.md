# Lesson plan — Forms of government — one, few, force, the people

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** The State and Government · **Lesson:** Forms of government — one, few, force, the people
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 16 — The State and Government (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** governments are sorted by who holds sovereign power: one, a few, one by force, or the people.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the levels before it in this course's path order (recommended, never forced); inside its own title it stands on the lessons before it.

## Before this lesson

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the four forms and who rules in each.
2. Tell dictatorship from monarchy.
3. Say where democracy is developed in this course.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — Four rooms. In the first, one crowned person decides. In the second, a small circle decides. In the third, an armed person decides and nobody may object. In the fourth, everyone decides together. Walk through all four before reading on.
