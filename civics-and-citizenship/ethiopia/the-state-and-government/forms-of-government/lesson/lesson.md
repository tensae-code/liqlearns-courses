# Forms of government — one, few, force, the people

## Do it first

Four rooms. In the first, one crowned person decides. In the second, a small circle decides. In the third, an armed person decides and nobody may object. In the fourth, everyone decides together. Walk through all four before reading on.

## The four forms

Governments are classified by the number wielding sovereign power.

[Picture 28618 — four panels: crown, few chairs, fist, wide circle]

[Picture 28619 — four thrones-to-ballot panels]

**Monarchy:** one hereditary ruler — a king or queen; the throne passes usually to the eldest son or nearest male descendant.

**Aristocracy:** a few, held to be the best qualified, ruling supposedly for the many.

**Dictatorship:** one person or a small group with unlimited power, held by force — authoritarian or totalitarian.

**Democracy:** rulers chosen by the citizens; equality fostered; free elections; every eligible citizen with a voice.

Democracy is only named here — it is developed fully in the Democracy level of this course, exactly as this book itself defers it.
