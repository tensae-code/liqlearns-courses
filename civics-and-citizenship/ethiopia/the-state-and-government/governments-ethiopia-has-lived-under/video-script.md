# Video script — Governments Ethiopia has lived under

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. A short script — this lesson's design names no full video-script need, so the script stays short.

---

Three Ethiopias, one after another.
A **crown**, until 1974 — sovereignty in the Emperor.
A **star**, 1974 to 1991 — the Derg, soldiers ruling as a committee.
A **ballot box**, since 1991 — representatives, by election.
Same country. Three answers to: who rules?
