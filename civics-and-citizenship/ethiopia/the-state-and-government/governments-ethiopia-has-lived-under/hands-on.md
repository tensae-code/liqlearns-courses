# Hands-on — Governments Ethiopia has lived under

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening again, for real
A timeline with three seals: a crown, a star, a ballot box. Two dates cut it: 1974 and 1991. Before reading, place what you already know of Ethiopia's history along that line.

## Sort it on the table
On paper, write the lesson's key pairs from its matching questions — one term per slip. Shuffle the slips, then sort and match them again from memory, saying each meaning aloud as you place it. Any pair you cannot explain goes back into the lesson for one more read.

## Take it home
Find this lesson where you live: one real example of its one thing — Ethiopia has lived under three successive forms: imperial monarchy, the Derg military regime, and the post-1991 republic — in your family, your school or your street. Write two sentences about it: what happened, and what the lesson says about it. Bring the sentences back and read them to your companion.
