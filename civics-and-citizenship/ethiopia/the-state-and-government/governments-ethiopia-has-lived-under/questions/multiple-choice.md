# Governments Ethiopia has lived under — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) **The imperial monarchy ended in —
a) 1974  b) 1991  c) 1931
**Answer: a) 1974**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) **Under the 1931 Constitution, sovereignty sat with —
a) the Senate  b) the Emperor  c) the people by election
**Answer: b) the Emperor**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) **The Derg ruled from —
a) 1931 to 1974  b) 1991 to today  c) 1974 to 1991
**Answer: c) 1974 to 1991**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) **"Derg" is a word from —
a) Amharic, meaning committee/council  b) Arabic  c) Ge'ez law Latin
**Answer: a) Amharic, meaning committee/council**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) **The Derg claimed sovereignty for —
a) the Emperor's family  b) the working class  c) foreign powers
**Answer: b) the working class**

**6. (Sub lesson 3) **Since 1991 Ethiopia has been —
a) an absolute monarchy  b) a military regime  c) a parliamentary republic
**Answer: c) a parliamentary republic**

**7. (Sub lesson 3) **One complaint the book records against the post-1991 order is —
a) one-party dominance  b) too many elections  c) hereditary succession
**Answer: a) one-party dominance**
