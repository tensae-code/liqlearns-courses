## Sub lessons
1. **The imperial monarchy (until 1974)** — Until 1974 Ethiopia was an **imperial monarchy**: absolutist and centralised, with sovereignty in the Emperor under the 1931 Constitution — the throne hereditary, under its Article 4.
2. **The Derg military regime (1974–91)** — The **Derg** replaced the monarchy in 1974: a dictatorial, unitary military regime that replaced feudalism and monarchy with socialism and a presidential system, and ended hereditary succession.
3. **The post-1991 republic** — Since 1991 Ethiopia has been a **parliamentary republic**: rule by citizens' representatives through free, fair, periodic elections, in a federal order.
