# State structures — the unitary state — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. **In a unitary state, power concentrates in —
a) the central government  b) the regions  c) independent provinces
**Answer: a) the central government**

**2. **A sub-unit's power in a unitary state is —
a) protected by the constitution  b) delegated by the centre, not guaranteed by law  c) greater than the centre's
**Answer: b) delegated by the centre, not guaranteed by law**

**3. **Name one advantage of the unitary form.
a) no central government  b) many competing laws  c) uniformity of administration
**Answer: c) uniformity of administration**

**4. **Name one cost of the unitary form.
a) concentration and misuse of power  b) too much local diversity recognised  c) slow elections
**Answer: a) concentration and misuse of power**

**5. **Which is a unitary example in this lesson?
a) Ethiopia  b) France  c) the United States
**Answer: b) France**
