# Government systems — parliamentary — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) **The question behind both government systems is —
a) how executives are formed and held  b) how flags are designed  c) how capitals are chosen
**Answer: a) how executives are formed and held**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) **In the parliamentary system, the government is formed by —
a) the army  b) the majority party or coalition in parliament  c) the president alone
**Answer: b) the majority party or coalition in parliament**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) **A parliamentary government falls when —
a) the term reaches exactly ten years  b) the president cuts a ribbon  c) it loses parliament's confidence
**Answer: c) it loses parliament's confidence**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) **In Ethiopia, the nominal head of state is —
a) the President, under Article 71  b) the Prime Minister  c) the Speaker alone
**Answer: a) the President, under Article 71**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) **Real executive power in Ethiopia sits with —
a) the President  b) the Prime Minister, under Article 74  c) foreign ambassadors
**Answer: b) the Prime Minister, under Article 74**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) **Collective responsibility means —
a) each minister answers only for themselves  b) parliament never votes  c) the cabinet stands or falls together
**Answer: c) the cabinet stands or falls together**
