# Hands-on — Government systems — parliamentary

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening again, for real
A council of seven runs a city block. It elects its own chair, and the chair must keep the council's trust or fall. Now scale that block up to a whole country. That scaling is this lesson.

## Sort it on the table
On paper, write the lesson's key pairs from its matching questions — one term per slip. Shuffle the slips, then sort and match them again from memory, saying each meaning aloud as you place it. Any pair you cannot explain goes back into the lesson for one more read.

## Take it home
Find this lesson where you live: one real example of its one thing — in the parliamentary system the majority in parliament forms the government, and the prime minister answers to parliament — in your family, your school or your street. Write two sentences about it: what happened, and what the lesson says about it. Bring the sentences back and read them to your companion.
