# Government systems — presidential — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word or words.

**1. **The president is head of state and head of ____ in one person.
**Answer: government**

**2. **A president cannot be dismissed by a simple vote of no ____.
**Answer: confidence**

**3. **____ is the legislature's removal weapon in this system.
**Answer: Impeachment**
