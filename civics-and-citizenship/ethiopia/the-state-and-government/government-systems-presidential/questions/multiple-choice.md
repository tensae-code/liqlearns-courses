# Government systems — presidential — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. **In the presidential system, the president is —
a) both head of state and head of government  b) only ceremonial  c) appointed by parliament
**Answer: a) both head of state and head of government**

**2. **A US president's term is —
a) two years, unlimited  b) four years, at most twice  c) six years, once only
**Answer: b) four years, at most twice**

**3. **US presidents are elected through —
a) parliament's confidence vote  b) the Supreme Court  c) the Electoral College
**Answer: c) the Electoral College**

**4. **The legislature's removal weapon is —
a) impeachment, for oath-breaking  b) a vote of no confidence  c) a petition
**Answer: a) impeachment, for oath-breaking**

**5. **Block 14 models the system by —
a) a council electing its own chair  b) households electing council and chair separately  c) a king appointing both
**Answer: b) households electing council and chair separately**
