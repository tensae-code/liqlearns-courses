# Hands-on — Government systems — presidential

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening again, for real
One polling day, two ballot boxes. Into one, voters choose a president. Into the other, a congress. Neither can simply dismiss the other. Before reading, decide: is that stability, or deadlock waiting to happen?

## Sort it on the table
On paper, write the lesson's key pairs from its matching questions — one term per slip. Shuffle the slips, then sort and match them again from memory, saying each meaning aloud as you place it. Any pair you cannot explain goes back into the lesson for one more read.

## Take it home
Find this lesson where you live: one real example of its one thing — in the presidential system a separately elected president is both head of state and head of government for a fixed term — in your family, your school or your street. Write two sentences about it: what happened, and what the lesson says about it. Bring the sentences back and read them to your companion.
