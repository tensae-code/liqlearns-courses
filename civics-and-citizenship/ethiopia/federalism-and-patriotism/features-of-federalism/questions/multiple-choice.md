# Features of Federalism — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Political power in a federation is divided —
a) constitutionally, between federal government and states  b) by the centre's annual decision  c) by lot  d) only in wartime
**Answer: a) constitutionally, between federal government and states**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The dual desire behind the division is —
a) to be united and to be autonomous  b) to be rich and feared  c) to be silent and safe  d) to be central and supreme
**Answer: a) to be united and to be autonomous**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Local governments in a decentralised unitary state are —
a) creations of the centre by statute, revocable unilaterally  b) creatures of the constitution  c) sovereign states  d) elected by the federation
**Answer: a) creations of the centre by statute, revocable unilaterally**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Written constitutions are necessary in federations because they are —
a) the records of the bargain's terms  b) cheaper than memory  c) required by foreign powers  d) easier to print
**Answer: a) the records of the bargain's terms**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Supremacy of the constitution is a logical necessity because each level's authority must derive from —
a) something above both levels  b) the larger level  c) the older level  d) the army
**Answer: a) something above both levels**
