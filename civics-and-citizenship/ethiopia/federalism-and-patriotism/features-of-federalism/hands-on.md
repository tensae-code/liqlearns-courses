# Hands-on — Features of Federalism

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two keys, one strongroom
Two keepers, two keys, one box that needs both. Inside: a card reading 'the compact'. Each keeper tries alone; then together. The class states the feature the mime teaches.

## The bargain table
Two delegations draft a three-line bargain for a shared schoolyard, sign it, and pin it above both their desks. A week of disputes is then judged only from the pinned paper — feel why the record must be written, and above both.
