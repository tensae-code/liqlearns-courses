# Hands-on — Duties of Patriots

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Many hands, one pot
The class carries one large vessel of water together across the yard — every hand needed. One learner, briefed secretly, tries to tip it toward themselves. The carriers must correct the tip without spilling: the common good, physically defended.

## Open the crate
Act Zeleke's ending: a crate of books opened before pupils and elders. Each elder speaks one sentence on what the books oblige the school to become; each pupil, one on what they oblige the pupils to do.
