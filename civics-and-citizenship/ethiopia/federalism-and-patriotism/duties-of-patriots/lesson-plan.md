# Lesson plan — Duties of Patriots

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Federalism and Patriotism · **Lesson:** Duties of Patriots
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 27 — Federalism and Patriotism (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a patriot's duties are three — knowing the true history, promoting the common good, and reducing poverty through voluntarism — and Zeleke, who raised over 200,000 birr for his old school's library, is their portrait.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the federalism lessons just before it in this level — the learner knows the house the federation built; this part teaches the heart that keeps it: patriotism, its types, bases, worth, traits and duties.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Knowing the true history** — The first duty: a past based on fact, against distortion — because patriotism itself is shaped by the history learned.
2. **Promoting the common good** — The second duty: the common good promoted, secrets guarded, and its two destroyers named.
3. **Poverty reduction and voluntarism** — The third duty: poverty fought as a patriotic task, and voluntarism as its personal form — Zeleke the portrait.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Traits of Patriots — And the Nurse Offered 500 Birr, just before this lesson in this level. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Explain the first duty: knowing the true history, and how history is distorted.
2. Explain the second: the common good, state secrets, and what damages it.
3. Explain the third: poverty reduction and voluntarism, with Zeleke's case as summary.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else.

## If the learner is stuck
- Treats duties as the state's job list → these are the patriot-citizen's duties: history known truly, the common good promoted, poverty fought by personal initiative.
- Reads Zeleke as fundraising technique → the case portraits voluntarism: initiative not for price or enrichment, elders' agreement won, a committee organised, a community's own problem solved by its own people.
