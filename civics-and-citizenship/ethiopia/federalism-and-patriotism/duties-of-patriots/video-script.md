# Video script — Duties of Patriots

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing: a patriot's duties are three — knowing the true history, promoting the common good, and reducing poverty through voluntarism — and Zeleke, who raised over 200,000 birr for his old school's library, is their portrait.
A patriot's duties are three.\nKnow the true history — refuse the fabricated kind.\nPromote the common good — guard what must be guarded.\nFight poverty by volunteering —\nlike Zeleke, whose old school now has books.
