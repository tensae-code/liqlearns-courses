# Duties of Patriots — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** History can be distorted by historians who —
a) serve political ambitions, use wrong sources, select facts or create evidence  b) write too slowly  c) use footnotes  d) teach children
**Answer: a) serve political ambitions, use wrong sources, select facts or create evidence**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** True history, learned correctly, develops in citizens —
a) tolerance and mutual understanding to live in harmony  b) pride in one group only  c) forgetfulness  d) obedience without thought
**Answer: a) tolerance and mutual understanding to live in harmony**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Guarding state secrets belongs to which duty?
a) Promoting the common good  b) Knowing the true history  c) Poverty reduction  d) None — secrets are always wrong
**Answer: a) Promoting the common good**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The factors that damage the common good are —
a) selfish personal interest and corruption  b) volunteering and saving  c) history and debate  d) taxes and law
**Answer: a) selfish personal interest and corruption**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Poverty, for poorer countries, is defined as —
a) inability to meet basic needs  b) having few luxuries  c) living rurally  d) low exports
**Answer: a) inability to meet basic needs**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Zeleke collected, for his old school's library and laboratory —
a) over 200,000 birr plus equipment and books  b) 500 birr  c) a government grant only  d) nothing — he only advised
**Answer: a) over 200,000 birr plus equipment and books**
