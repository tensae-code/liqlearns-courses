# Duties of Patriots — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give the book's three reasons knowing true history is a duty.
**Answer:** Understanding the past helps solve current problems; learned history shapes values, beliefs and patriotism itself; citizens aware of correct history develop the tolerance and mutual understanding to live in harmony, appreciating all peoples' roles in war, peace and development.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Define the common-good duty in the book's terms.
**Answer:** Subordinating personal interest to the community's good — civic virtue — because individuals' interests are interdependent and joint effort against common problems perpetuates the community's and nation's wellbeing.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Tell Zeleke's case as a summary.
**Answer:** Zeleke, an elementary teacher in the Southern Nations, Nationalities and Peoples Region and a former pupil of his school, saw it lacked laboratory equipment and library reference books; he won the elders' agreement, wrote to former pupils across and outside the country, organised a committee of community members and teachers, and collected over 200,000 birr plus equipment and books.
