# Duties of Patriots — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The history one learns shapes values and beliefs, and so the sense of patriotism itself.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Information whose exposure endangers the nation's existence must not be communicated to third parties.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Voluntarism is personal initiative serving the community for price and enrichment.
**Answer: False** — not for price or enrichment — that is its definition.
