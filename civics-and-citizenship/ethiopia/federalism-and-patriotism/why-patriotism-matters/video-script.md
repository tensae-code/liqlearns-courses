# Video script — Why Patriotism Matters

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

This lesson is about one thing: patriotism matters for three headline reasons — it brings people together, it builds a stronger nation, and patriotic spirit is itself an admirable quality in a person.
Why does patriotism matter?\nIt brings people together — one flag, one direction.\nIt builds a stronger nation — everyone working, honestly.\nAnd it is an admirable quality in a person —\nvalues known, virtues kept, loyalty shown.
