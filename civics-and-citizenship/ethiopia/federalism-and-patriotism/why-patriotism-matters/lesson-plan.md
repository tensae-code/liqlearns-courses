# Lesson plan — Why Patriotism Matters

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Federalism and Patriotism · **Lesson:** Why Patriotism Matters
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 27 — Federalism and Patriotism (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** patriotism matters for three headline reasons — it brings people together, it builds a stronger nation, and patriotic spirit is itself an admirable quality in a person.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the federalism lessons just before it in this level — the learner knows the house the federation built; this part teaches the heart that keeps it: patriotism, its types, bases, worth, traits and duties.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. ## If the learner is stuck
- Recites importances as feelings → each has a mechanism: motivation toward betterment, a nation working together with honesty, and citizens made stronger by holding their values openly.
- Skips the family root → the book argues from the family outward: sentiment raised at home becomes the citizen's sense of self and then the nation's strength.
