# Hands-on — Why Patriotism Matters

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## One crowd, one flag
The class stands as a stadium crowd; as a flag card rises, each learner whispers to a neighbour one work they will do for the country this term. The volume of small promises is the second importance, heard.

## Headline sort
Deal twelve statement cards — the lesson's claims and six distractors. Teams sort them under the three importances, or into 'not the book's claim'. Every placing must be defended from the lesson.
