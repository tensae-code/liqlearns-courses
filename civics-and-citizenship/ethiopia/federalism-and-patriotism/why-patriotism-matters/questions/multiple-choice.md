# Why Patriotism Matters — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The book argues patriotism's value starting from —
a) the family outward  b) the army inward  c) the market  d) abroad
**Answer: a) the family outward**

**2.** The first headline importance is —
a) bringing people together  b) raising taxes  c) winning wars  d) building stadiums
**Answer: a) bringing people together**

**3.** A stronger nation is built, in the book, by —
a) each individual working for the country's betterment, hardworking and honest, supporting each other  b) one heroic leader alone  c) foreign investment only  d) silence about problems
**Answer: a) each individual working for the country's betterment, hardworking and honest, supporting each other**
