# Video script — Bases of Patriotism

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing: patriotism stands on named bases — equality, tolerance and loyalty first; then concern, sacrifice, the constitution, duty and defence — with Adwa, 1 March 1896, as the worked example of the second set.
What does patriotism stand on?\nEquality. Tolerance — an obligation, not a gift. Loyalty.\nConcern. Sacrifice. The constitution defended. Duty done without enforcement.\nAnd Adwa — 1 March 1896 —\nall peoples, one hill, agreement against a common enemy.
