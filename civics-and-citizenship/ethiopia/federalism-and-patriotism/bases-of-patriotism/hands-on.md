# Hands-on — Bases of Patriotism

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Lay the foundation
Eight stones (or books) lettered with the bases are laid in two courses under a house card. The class shakes the table gently: which stone's absence would crack the house first? Argue from the definitions.

## Converge on the hill
Learners start at the room's corners as the nations and nationalities, each carrying one base-card. On the drum or clap, all converge on one 'hill' card lettered Adwa 1896 — the hill is only held when every corner has arrived.
