# Bases of Patriotism — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Define the first three bases in one line each.
**Answer:** Equality — respect for constitutional democracy and rights; every citizen protecting others' rights. Tolerance — accepting differences as normal and natural, an obligation giving every group common ground and pride. Loyalty — to constitution, people, elected government and community, with care for others' property and one's duties performed.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the second set of bases.
**Answer:** Concern for the well-being of one's country; sacrificing personal desires and interests for the common good; protecting the constitution; carrying out personal and civic duties; defending the country in external attack.
