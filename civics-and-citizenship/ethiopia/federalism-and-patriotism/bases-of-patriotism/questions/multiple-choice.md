# Bases of Patriotism — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ethiopia's present basis of patriotism, per the book, is —
a) citizens' rights and responsibilities  b) praise of geography and minerals  c) one language for all  d) military glory alone
**Answer: a) citizens' rights and responsibilities**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Tolerance in the democratic constitutional system is —
a) a constitutional and civil obligation, not a gift  b) a favour the majority grants  c) optional politeness  d) silence about differences
**Answer: a) a constitutional and civil obligation, not a gift**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Constitutional loyalty is owed to —
a) the constitution, the people, the elected government and the community  b) one leader personally  c) one's own group only  d) the army
**Answer: a) the constitution, the people, the elected government and the community**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Adwa stands in this lesson as the worked example of —
a) concern for the country's well-being and defence of the country  b) capital patriotism  c) symbolic patriotism  d) tolerance
**Answer: a) concern for the country's well-being and defence of the country**
