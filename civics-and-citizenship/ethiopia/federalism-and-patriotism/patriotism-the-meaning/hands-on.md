# Hands-on — Patriotism — The Meaning

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The gesture, examined
Stand and make the hand-over-heart gesture. A partner interviews the gesture: what do you love — place, people, principles? The wearer must answer in the book's terms, three answers deep.

## Old measure, new measure
Two learners stand under one flag card: one mimes the old measure (the soldier's sacrifice), one the modern (the engineer-teacher building). The class names what is identical between them: devotion that costs something.
