# Patriotism — The Meaning — writing

Write what each prompt asks, in your own words.

**1.** Distinguish patriotism from nationalism using the summary's framing, in two short paragraphs that never borrow each other's words.
**A good answer does this:** Keeps patriotism as devotion to homeland and its founding values; nationalism as the movement holding the nation-state paramount, bound by common descent, language and religion.
