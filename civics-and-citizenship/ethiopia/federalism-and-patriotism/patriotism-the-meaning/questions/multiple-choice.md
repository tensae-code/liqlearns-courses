# Patriotism — The Meaning — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Patriotism derives from the Latin patria, meaning —
a) fatherland  b) victory  c) army  d) law
**Answer: a) fatherland**

**2.** In a democracy, patriotism means devoting time, ability, knowledge and even life to —
a) the nation's founding principles  b) one party  c) one family  d) one region only
**Answer: a) the nation's founding principles**

**3.** The book insists patriotism is also —
a) practical and action-oriented  b) a private feeling only  c) for soldiers only  d) a holiday ceremony
**Answer: a) practical and action-oriented**
