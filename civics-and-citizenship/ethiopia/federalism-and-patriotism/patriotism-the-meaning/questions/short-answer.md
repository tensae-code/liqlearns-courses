# Patriotism — The Meaning — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Define patriotism in the book's terms.
**Answer:** A sentiment and psychological attachment to one's nation — deep-rooted devotion to one's country and the fundamental values its security and welfare depend on, with pride in its history, culture and tradition; practical and action-oriented, resembling family affection.

**2.** Give the guide's two measures of a model patriot.
**Answer:** The old measure: sacrificing one's life. The modern measure: believing in oneself and contributing something great that benefits generations, not only oneself — keyed in the guide, held here as evidence.
