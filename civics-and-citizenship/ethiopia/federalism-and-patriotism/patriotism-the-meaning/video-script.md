This lesson is about one thing: patriotism — from patria, fatherland — is love, devotion and attachment to one's homeland: a sentiment that is also practical and action-oriented, proved in work, ethics and responsibility, not only on the war front.
Patriotism — from patria, the fatherland.\nLove and devotion to one's homeland —\nand practical: research, ethics, competence, responsibility.\nThe old measure: a life given.\nThe modern: something great, built for generations.
