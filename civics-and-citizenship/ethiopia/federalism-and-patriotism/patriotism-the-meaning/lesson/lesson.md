# Patriotism — The Meaning

## Do it first

Put your hand over your heart, the way crowds do at an anthem. What exactly is the hand claiming? Love of a place? Of a people? Of an idea? Write two sentences before reading. This lesson takes that gesture apart and gives it the book's full meaning.

## What patriotism is

**Patriotism**, from the Latin *patria* — fatherland — is a sentiment and psychological attachment to one's nation: belonging to a definite historical and cultural setting and a specific country, with its language and customs. It is deep-rooted sentiment for a father- or motherland: living for, and devotion to, one's country and to the fundamental values on which its security and welfare depend — and pride in its history, culture and tradition. 

[Picture 28966 — A pupil's hand over the heart before a flag, with a workshop, a library and a field drawn small behind]



But the book insists patriotism is also **practical and action-oriented**, resembling family affection: it is expressed on the war front, yes — and equally in research, in good ethics, competence at work, leadership, and carrying responsibility, continuously. Its content follows the political system: in a **democracy** it means devoting time, ability, knowledge and even life to the nation's founding principles.

As national pride, it is love, devotion and attachment to homeland allied with fellow citizens in a feeling of oneness — compounded of ethnic, cultural, political and historical feelings. The unit summary carries the contrast the design keeps: **nationalism** as the movement holding the nation-state paramount for a people's social, economic and cultural aspirations, bound by common descent, language and religion — a different word, kept apart.

The guide's keyed model-patriot answer is evidence worth holding: the **old measure** — sacrificing one's life; the **modern** — contributing something great that benefits generations. Its picture sets the two side by side under one flag. 

[Picture 28967 — Two figures under one flag — a soldier of the old wars and a young engineer-teacher of today (teacher guide — evidence)]



## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion: the derivation, the definition, and the practical insistence. End with the guide's two measures — and say which one your generation is asked for.

## Do It Yourself

1. Write the definition from memory. Underline every word that demands action, not only feeling.
2. List five places patriotism is expressed, per the book — war front, research, ethics, competence, leadership, responsibility. Beside each, one person you know who expresses it there.
3. Write the patriotism/nationalism contrast in two columns, from the unit summary's framing. Keep the columns apart.
