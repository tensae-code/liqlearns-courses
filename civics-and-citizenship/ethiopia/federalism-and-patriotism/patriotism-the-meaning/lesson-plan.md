# Lesson plan — Patriotism — The Meaning

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Federalism and Patriotism · **Lesson:** Patriotism — The Meaning
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 27 — Federalism and Patriotism (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** patriotism — from patria, fatherland — is love, devotion and attachment to one's homeland: a sentiment that is also practical and action-oriented, proved in work, ethics and responsibility, not only on the war front.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the federalism lessons just before it in this level — the learner knows the house the federation built; this part teaches the heart that keeps it: patriotism, its types, bases, worth, traits and duties.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. ## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Derive patriotism from patria and define it in the book's terms.
2. Say how its content follows the political system — what it means in a democracy.
3. State the guide's two measures of a patriot — the old and the modern — as evidence.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else.

## If the learner is stuck
- Reduces patriotism to a feeling → the book insists it is practical and action-oriented: research, ethics, competence, leadership, responsibility — continuously.
- Merges patriotism with nationalism → the summary's contrast is kept: nationalism is the movement holding the nation-state paramount, bound by descent, language and religion. Different word, different lesson weight.
