# Lesson plan — Federalism — The Meaning

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Federalism and Patriotism · **Lesson:** Federalism — The Meaning
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 27 — Federalism and Patriotism (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** federalism is a system where power is divided between federal and regional governments — neither supreme, each deriving its authority from the constitution, dealing directly with citizens.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the constitution level and the diversity level — the learner knows the state's frame and the peoples it holds; this level teaches the federal answer: power divided, unity kept.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Define federalism in the book's working words.
2. Give the scholarly definitions the book selects, in your own words.
3. Tell the Lower Omo flood case as a summary, and what it proved.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else.

## If the learner is stuck
- Defines federalism as the centre lending power it can take back → in a federation neither level is supreme and both derive authority from the constitution, not from each other.
