# Federalism — The Meaning — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell the Lower Omo flood case as a summary, and what it proved.
**Answer:** The Omo River flooded the Lower Omo Basin — villages and farms under water, livestock dead, thousands homeless — beyond the regional state's capacity; the federal government stepped in and Ethiopians of all backgrounds, religious institutions and other regions mobilised help. It proved the units interdependent — coordinated action and national unity of care.

**2.** What world frame does the guide add, as evidence?
**Answer:** 25 countries, holding over 40% of the world's people, are federal.
