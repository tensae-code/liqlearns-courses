# Federalism — The Meaning — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** In federalism, power is divided so that —
a) neither level is supreme  b) the centre commands the regions  c) the regions command the centre  d) one level is abolished
**Answer: a) neither level is supreme**

**2.** Devolution means —
a) power transferred downward to the regions  b) power gathered to the centre  c) elections postponed  d) laws unwritten
**Answer: a) power transferred downward to the regions**

**3.** In the second scholarly definition, each level derives its sovereign powers from —
a) the constitution  b) the other level  c) the army  d) custom alone
**Answer: a) the constitution**
