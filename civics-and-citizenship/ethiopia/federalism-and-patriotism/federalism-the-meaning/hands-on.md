# Hands-on — Federalism — The Meaning

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two storeys, one roof
Build the house from boxes: two storeys lettered Federal and Regional, one roof card lettered The Constitution. Try removing the roof; try letting one storey 'evict' the other. Say, from the lesson, why neither can be done.

## The flood table
Around a table 'flood map', regional players hold too few relief cards; federal and other-region players add theirs. Count what the stricken region lacked and what the federation supplied — interdependence, counted.
