This lesson is about one thing: federalism is a system where power is divided between federal and regional governments — neither supreme, each deriving its authority from the constitution, dealing directly with citizens.
Federalism: power divided, neither level supreme.\nFederal and regional — each final somewhere.\nShared-rule and self-rule, under one constitution.\nAnd when the river rose,\nthe whole federation carried the boats.
