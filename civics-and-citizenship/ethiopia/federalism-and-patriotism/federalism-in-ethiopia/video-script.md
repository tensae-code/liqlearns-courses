# Video script — Federalism in Ethiopia

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing: Ethiopia's federation was built to reconcile ethnic diversity with political unity — powers listed for both levels, the rest left to the states, and two houses: one of the people's representatives, one of the nations themselves.
Why is Ethiopia federal?\nTo reconcile diversity with unity — after a century of one centre.\nPowers listed: federal, state, shared — the rest to the states.\nTwo houses: the people's, and the nations'.\nOne federation, many peoples, one roof.
