**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (Ethiopia).**

**The book's article numbers for the two houses (its §5.5.1 set: "article 45, 53"; Arts. 53(3) and 55) do not line up cleanly with the constitution's own numbering and are NOT taught as fact** — institutions and functions are taught as the book gives them; the shaky numbers are named here only. The guide's answer-key list of the federation's units (including Sidama and the South West Ethiopia Peoples' Region, and the Addis Ababa and Dire Dawa city administrations) is evidence.
