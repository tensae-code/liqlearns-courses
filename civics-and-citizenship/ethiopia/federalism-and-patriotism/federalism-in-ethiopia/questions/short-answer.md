# Federalism in Ethiopia — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What did the centralised century sideline, and under what slogans?
**Answer:** Diversity — under slogans of one nation, one government, one language and one religion; the centralised, unified state proved ineffective at uniting and containing Ethiopia's peoples.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does this lesson not teach the book's article numbers for the two houses?
**Answer:** Because the book's article numbers for the houses (its §5.5.1 set) do not line up cleanly with the constitution's own numbering; institutions and functions are taught as the book gives them, and the shaky numbers are named in sources only.
