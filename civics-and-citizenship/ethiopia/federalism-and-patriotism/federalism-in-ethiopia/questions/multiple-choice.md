# Federalism in Ethiopia — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ethiopian federalism's rationale is —
a) reconciling ethnic diversity with political unity  b) copying a foreign fashion  c) weakening all government  d) ending all regional identity
**Answer: a) reconciling ethnic diversity with political unity**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Residual power — whatever is not listed — belongs to —
a) the states  b) the federal government  c) the House of Federation  d) the courts
**Answer: a) the states**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Mutual non-intervention, as the book paraphrases Article 50(8), means —
a) each level respects the other's constitutionally limited jurisdiction  b) the levels never cooperate  c) states may veto all federal law  d) the centre may enter any state matter
**Answer: a) each level respects the other's constitutionally limited jurisdiction**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The highest authority of the federal government rests in —
a) the House of Peoples' Representatives  b) the House of Federation  c) the federal courts  d) the state councils
**Answer: a) the House of Peoples' Representatives**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Each nationality in the House of Federation has at least one member, plus —
a) one more per additional million of population  b) one per town  c) ten regardless of size  d) one per party
**Answer: a) one more per additional million of population**
