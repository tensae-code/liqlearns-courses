# Hands-on — Federalism in Ethiopia

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Three lists and a remainder
Deal power cards — army, schools, foreign trade, local markets, police, land, health, taxes — into three baskets lettered Federal, State, Shared. Unlisted cards go to a fourth basket: Residual — to the States. Defend each dealing from the constitution exercise.

## Two houses, one roof
Two groups sit as the two houses. A bill about a river crossing two states is read: the Peoples' house debates the law; the Nations' house checks whose rights it touches. Both must pass it, each in its own voice.
