# Lesson plan — Federalism in Ethiopia — Challenges

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Federalism and Patriotism · **Lesson:** Federalism in Ethiopia — Challenges
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 27 — Federalism and Patriotism (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Ethiopia's federation lives inside four named tensions — unity against diversity, self-determination joined to federalism, states multiplying along nationality lines, and individual against collective rights — and the book argues toward asymmetric federalism, at least for now.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the constitution level and the diversity level — the learner knows the state's frame and the peoples it holds; this level teaches the federal answer: power divided, unity kept.
