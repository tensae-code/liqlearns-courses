# Hands-on — Federalism in Ethiopia — Challenges

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Steady the beam
Two learners hold a beam (a ruler on a book) with pans of stones lettered Self-rule and One Union. The class adds tension-stones one by one, calling each tension by name; a third learner's hand steadies the beam — the class debates whose hand that is in real life.

## Argue it in its framing
One side presents the book's asymmetric argument exactly as the book frames it; the other side must first repeat it faithfully before answering it. The class judges fidelity before judging merit.
