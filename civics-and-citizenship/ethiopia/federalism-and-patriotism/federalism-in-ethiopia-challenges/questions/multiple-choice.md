# Federalism in Ethiopia — Challenges — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Ethiopia has implemented federalism since —
a) 1995  b) 1991  c) 2005  d) 1974
**Answer: a) 1995**

**2.** Which of these is NOT one of the four named tensions?
a) The price of coffee  b) Unity against diversity  c) States multiplying along nationality lines  d) Individual against collective rights
**Answer: a) The price of coffee**

**3.** The constitution grants self-determination straightforwardly to —
a) all nationalities  b) only historically mobilised ones  c) only the largest groups  d) no groups
**Answer: a) all nationalities**
