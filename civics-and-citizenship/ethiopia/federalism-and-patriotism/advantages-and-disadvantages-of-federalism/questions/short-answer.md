# Advantages and Disadvantages of Federalism — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** List the first five advantages — the ones restating the features.
**Answer:** Division of powers (administered two ways); separate governments at centre and units, each with legislatures and executives; a written constitution as the partnership's agreement; a rigid constitution guarding the agreement; a special judiciary as the constitution's custodian and guardian.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** List the five disadvantages.
**Answer:** Conflict of authority; regionalism over patriotism; lack of accountability; inequalities between states; blockage of national policies by states.
