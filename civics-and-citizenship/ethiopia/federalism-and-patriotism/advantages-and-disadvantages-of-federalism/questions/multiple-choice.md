# Advantages and Disadvantages of Federalism — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How many advantages does the student book list?
a) Nine  b) Five  c) Seven  d) Twelve
**Answer: a) Nine**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** 'Cookie-cutter' policy is what federalism avoids by —
a) states crafting solutions fitting themselves  b) cheaper printing  c) fewer elections  d) one national language
**Answer: a) states crafting solutions fitting themselves**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Protection against tyranny comes, in the list, from —
a) divided and checked power  b) a rigid army  c) a single strong ruler  d) secret courts
**Answer: a) divided and checked power**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Each level releasing blame to the other when things go wrong is —
a) lack of accountability  b) regionalism  c) blockage  d) conflict of authority
**Answer: a) lack of accountability**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** States obstructing enforcement of national laws is —
a) blockage of national policies  b) inequalities between states  c) regionalism over patriotism  d) conflict of authority
**Answer: a) blockage of national policies**
