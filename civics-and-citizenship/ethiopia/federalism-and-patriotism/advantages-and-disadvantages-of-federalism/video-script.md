# Video script — Advantages and Disadvantages of Federalism

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing: federalism's case is argued in the open — nine advantages in the student book, from divided power to grassroots participation, against five disadvantages, from conflict of authority to blocked national policies.
The case for federalism: nine advantages.\nPower divided. Tyranny checked. Citizens nearer power.\nSolutions fitted, not cookie-cut.\nThe case against: five.\nConflict, regionalism, blame passed, inequality, blockage.\nWeigh them honestly — the book does.
