# Hands-on — Advantages and Disadvantages of Federalism

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Nine tiles, one shield
Write the nine advantages on nine tiles and build one shield over a region-divided map. Remove tiles one at a time as a partner argues each is overstated; the shield-holder must defend or surrender each tile from the lesson.

## The unanswered letter
Act the picture: a citizen's letter on the table, two clerks pointing at each other. The citizen may ask one question per minute. The clerks may only answer by pointing — until the class names the disadvantage being performed.
