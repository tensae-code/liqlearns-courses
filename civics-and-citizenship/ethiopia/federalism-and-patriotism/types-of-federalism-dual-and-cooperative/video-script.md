This lesson is about one thing: federations come in two types — dual, the layer cake, where each level keeps to its own layer; and cooperative, the marble cake, where the colours swirl through both.
Two cakes, two federations.\nLayer cake — dual: each level in its own layer.\nMarble cake — cooperative: colours swirled through both.\nFederal money, state hands, one highway.\nWhich cake for Ethiopia? The book asks you.
