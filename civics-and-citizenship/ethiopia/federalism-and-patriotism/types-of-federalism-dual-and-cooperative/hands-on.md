# Hands-on — Types of Federalism — Dual and Cooperative

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Bake the argument
Two teams receive the same policy problem. Team Layer must solve it with each level sealed in its layer; Team Marble must solve it swirled. Each team presents; the class names where each design strained.

## Gears and boxes
Mime the guide's diagrams: two learners as sealed boxes, arms crossed, refusing a shared load; then two as gears, turning one imaginary road-machine together. The class says which mime tired first, and why.
