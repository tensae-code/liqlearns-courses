# Types of Federalism — Dual and Cooperative — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Federations are first classified by —
a) how the centre executes its laws across the territory  b) their size  c) their age  d) their language
**Answer: a) how the centre executes its laws across the territory**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In a confederation, the centre —
a) has no direct authority over citizens  b) rules citizens directly  c) appoints regional governors  d) holds all taxation
**Answer: a) has no direct authority over citizens**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Layer cake federalism means —
a) authority clearly divided, each level in its own sphere  b) all power shared on everything  c) states abolished  d) the centre executes all laws
**Answer: a) authority clearly divided, each level in its own sphere**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The marble cake's worked example is —
a) federal tax revenue funding interstate highways the states build and maintain  b) states printing federal money  c) the centre staffing all local offices  d) regions signing foreign treaties
**Answer: a) federal tax revenue funding interstate highways the states build and maintain**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Cooperative federalism's one named disadvantage is —
a) decreased state power  b) too much unity  c) excessive local law  d) slow elections
**Answer: a) decreased state power**
