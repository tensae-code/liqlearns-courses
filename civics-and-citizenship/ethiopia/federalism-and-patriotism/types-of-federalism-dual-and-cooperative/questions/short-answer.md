# Types of Federalism — Dual and Cooperative — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give dual federalism's two advantages with the book's examples.
**Answer:** Protection of local jurisdictions from federal overreach; and local laws fitted to local requirements — divorce, marriage and speed limits varying by state population and congestion.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two advantages of cooperative federalism.
**Answer:** Any two of: efficient joint service provision; practicality as problems outgrow single levels; both levels keeping their powers, giving participation and preventing excess power; states incentivised to cooperate; states influencing programme implementation.
