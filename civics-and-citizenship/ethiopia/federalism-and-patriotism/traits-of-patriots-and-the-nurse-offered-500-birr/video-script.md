# Video script — Traits of Patriots — And the Nurse Offered 500 Birr

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

[Read aloud, slowly. The case as a summary — no invented voices.]
Roza is a nurse at a referral hospital.
Her waiting lists are long. Patients with chronic illness — diabetes, cancer — wait for days.
One day, a patient at the very bottom of the list offers her 500 birr — to be treated first.
Consider what the offer asks. Not money for medicine. Money to pass every person waiting above him.
Consider what refusal protects. The queue is not a queue only — it is the country's fairness, standing in a corridor.
The fourth trait of a patriot: not taking undue advantage. Roza's desk is where that trait either lives or dies.
And consider the last question the book asks. If many nurses accept — what happens to the hospital? To the poor at the bottom of the list? To trust itself?
Judge the offer. The traits are the judges.
