# Hands-on — Traits of Patriots — And the Nurse Offered 500 Birr

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The desk and the envelope
Act the guide's picture: a desk, an envelope, a queue of patients as learners. The nurse must refuse in words that name the trait, not merely 'no'. The queue reacts; the class judges whether the refusal was patriotic or only polite.

## Trait court
Four learners hold the four trait cards as judges. Cases are read — a clerk's shortcut, a student's copied exam 'for the school', an official's free fuel. Each judge rules only from their trait; the verdict needs at least three traits concurring.
