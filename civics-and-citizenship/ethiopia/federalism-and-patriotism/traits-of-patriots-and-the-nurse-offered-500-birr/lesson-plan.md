# Lesson plan — Traits of Patriots — And the Nurse Offered 500 Birr

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Federalism and Patriotism · **Lesson:** Traits of Patriots — And the Nurse Offered 500 Birr
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 27 — Federalism and Patriotism (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a patriot is known by four traits — awareness of rights and duties, fighting for a better nation, eagerness to make the country better, and not taking undue advantage of privileges — and Roza the nurse, offered 500 birr to jump a queue, is where the traits are tested.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the federalism lessons just before it in this level — the learner knows the house the federation built; this part teaches the heart that keeps it: patriotism, its types, bases, worth, traits and duties.

## If the learner is stuck
- Calls Roza merely honest → honesty is inside the fourth trait, but the lesson's frame is patriotism: the queue she protects is the country in miniature, and her refusal is a citizen's trait, contagious by example.
- Quotes the guide's fourth trait without its negative → taught corrected: NOT taking undue advantage of privileges; the dropped negative is named in sources only.
