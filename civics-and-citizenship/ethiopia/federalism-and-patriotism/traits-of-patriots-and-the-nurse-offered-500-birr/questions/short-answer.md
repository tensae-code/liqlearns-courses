# Traits of Patriots — And the Nurse Offered 500 Birr — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell Roza's case as a summary.
**Answer:** Roza, a nurse at a referral hospital with long waiting lists of chronic patients (diabetes, cancer) waiting days, is offered 500 birr by a patient at the bottom of the list to be treated first.

**2.** Which trait decides Roza's refusal, and why?
**Answer:** Not taking undue advantage of privileges (with awareness of rights and duties behind it): accepting would sell a position's power to pass others who wait — others suffer for the advantage taken.
