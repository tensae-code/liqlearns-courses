# Traits of Patriots — And the Nurse Offered 500 Birr — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The first trait — awareness of rights and duties — includes —
a) taking no undue advantage of rights and fulfilling duties  b) knowing anthem words  c) owning a flag  d) voting twice
**Answer: a) taking no undue advantage of rights and fulfilling duties**

**2.** 'Fighting for a better nation' means —
a) naming the country's drawbacks as citizens' responsibility and voicing opinions on wrongdoing  b) arguing with citizens  c) silence about injustice  d) praising everything
**Answer: a) naming the country's drawbacks as citizens' responsibility and voicing opinions on wrongdoing**

**3.** The fourth trait, taught corrected, is —
a) not taking undue advantage of privileges  b) taking undue advantage of privileges  c) taking no duties  d) taking every advantage offered
**Answer: a) not taking undue advantage of privileges**
