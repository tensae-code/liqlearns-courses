# Video script — Types of Patriotism — Six Types

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing: patriotism has six types — constitutional, traditional, icon, symbolic, capital and environmental — a set the book pairs off two by two, and counts as six whatever its summary claims.
Six types of patriotism.\nConstitutional — bound to principles. Traditional — bound to belonging.\nIcon — proved in heroic deeds. Symbolic — carried by flag and anthem.\nCapital — built in the economy. Environmental — guarding the land.\nSix. Count them: six.
