# Types of Patriotism — Six Types — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Constitutional patriotism binds love of country to —
a) democracy, human rights and fundamental principles  b) descent and blood alone  c) one party's programme  d) military strength
**Answer: a) democracy, human rights and fundamental principles**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Traditional patriotism is marked by —
a) readiness to give one's life for the motherland  b) guarding citizens' rights  c) economic development  d) environmental protection
**Answer: a) readiness to give one's life for the motherland**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Icon patriotism shows love of country through —
a) actions of positive impact, even risking life for the homeland  b) respect for symbols only  c) trading goods  d) planting trees
**Answer: a) actions of positive impact, even risking life for the homeland**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Symbolic patriotism lives at the level of —
a) flag, national anthem and other national symbols  b) tax policy  c) court verdicts  d) school exams
**Answer: a) flag, national anthem and other national symbols**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Capital patriotism is expressed through —
a) developing the national economy  b) defending borders  c) singing the anthem  d) guarding forests
**Answer: a) developing the national economy**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Environmental patriotism holds the environment to be —
a) the source and foundation of life, every human's responsibility to protect  b) a resource to exhaust  c) separate from the country  d) the state's property only
**Answer: a) the source and foundation of life, every human's responsibility to protect**
