# Lesson plan — Types of Patriotism — Six Types

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Federalism and Patriotism · **Lesson:** Types of Patriotism — Six Types
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 27 — Federalism and Patriotism (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** patriotism has six types — constitutional, traditional, icon, symbolic, capital and environmental — a set the book pairs off two by two, and counts as six whatever its summary claims.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the federalism lessons just before it in this level — the learner knows the house the federation built; this part teaches the heart that keeps it: patriotism, its types, bases, worth, traits and duties.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Constitutional and traditional** — The first pairing: love bound to democratic principles, against love bound to belonging and sacrifice.
2. **Icon and symbolic** — The second pairing: love proved in heroic action, and love carried by the nation's symbols.
3. **Capital and environmental** — The third pairing: love at work in the economy, and love guarding the land itself.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Patriotism — The Meaning, just before this lesson in this level. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the six types and define each.
2. Hold the book's pairings: constitutional/traditional, icon/symbolic, capital/environmental.
3. State the count — six — against the summary's claim, which lives in sources only.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else.

## If the learner is stuck
- Counts seven types from the summary → the summary's 'seven' is the book's error (it names only four); six types are taught, and the error is named in sources only.
- Merges icon and symbolic → icon is love proved in heroic, positive-impact action; symbolic is love at the level of flag and anthem. Deed versus emblem.
