# Hands-on — Types of Patriotism — Six Types

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Raise the flag at dawn
At the GIF, the class stands as watchers. Each learner silently chooses one type and, when the flag tops the pole, speaks that type's definition aloud to a partner — six definitions rising with one flag.

## Trader and planter
Two learners shake hands over a seedling in a sack of grain — capital and environmental in one grip. Each must state the other's type's claim before stating their own.
