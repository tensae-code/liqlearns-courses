## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the four sources in order.
2. Tell the fundamental source from the primary source.
3. Say how custom became law.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else.

1. **Do it first** — Four springs feed one river. One spring is largest. Before reading, guess the four: where can a rule come from before anyone writes it in a law book? A people's long habit? A holy teaching? A parliament? A founding document?
