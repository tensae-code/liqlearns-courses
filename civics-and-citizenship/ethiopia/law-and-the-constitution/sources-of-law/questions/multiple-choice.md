# Sources of law — constitution, custom, belief, legislation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. **Name the four sources of law.
a) constitution, custom, religion and morality, legislation  b) courts, police, prisons, fines  c) kings, armies, taxes, flags
**Answer: a) constitution, custom, religion and morality, legislation**

**2. **The fundamental source is —
a) legislation  b) the constitution  c) custom
**Answer: b) the constitution**

**3. **The primary source today is —
a) custom  b) religion  c) legislation
**Answer: c) legislation**

**4. **Custom became state law when —
a) customs were written into authoritative rules  b) kings abolished it  c) schools stopped teaching it
**Answer: a) customs were written into authoritative rules**

**5. **A school code of conduct illustrates —
a) the constitution  b) legislation  c) custom
**Answer: b) legislation**
