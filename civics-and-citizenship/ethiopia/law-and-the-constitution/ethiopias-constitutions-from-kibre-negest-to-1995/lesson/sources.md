The student book prints the front that overthrew the Derg as “EPDRF” in §3.3; it is taught corrected as EPRDF, and the misprint is named here only. The book's section title spells “Constitutional experiances”; the lesson title uses the correct spelling. The video script stages the teacher guide's kebele distribution role play (a chairman distributing 5 kg of oil and sugar, tried for transparency and accountability) as a summary scene; no words are put in the book's mouth beyond its own scenario.
