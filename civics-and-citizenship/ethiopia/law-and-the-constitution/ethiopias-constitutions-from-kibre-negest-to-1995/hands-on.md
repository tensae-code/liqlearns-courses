# Hands-on — Ethiopia's Constitutions — From Kibre Negest to 1995

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The kebele trial (role play)
A chairman distributes 5 kg of oil and sugar to a kebele. Was it transparent? Was he accountable? The class stages the distribution, then tries the chairman — chair, accusers, defenders, judges — judging only by the two principles named.

## Six doors
Six learners hold six door-cards in line. Each opens their door and states, in character, whom their constitution secured. The class walks the line and discusses the turn at 1995.
