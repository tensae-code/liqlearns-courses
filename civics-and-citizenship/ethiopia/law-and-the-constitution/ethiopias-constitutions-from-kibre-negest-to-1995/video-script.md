# Video script — Ethiopia's Constitutions — From Kibre Negest to 1995

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The scene: a kebele hall. Sacks of oil and sugar stand at the front — five kilos for each household, the chairman says.

The chairman calls names from his list. Some households receive. Some, whose names are 'lost', do not.
A woman asks: 'Where is the full list? Who decided the order?'
The chairman shrugs: 'I am the chairman.'

Now the trial. The class sits as judges.
The charge is named in two words: transparency — was everything open?
Accountability — must he answer for the missing names?

Speak for the chairman if you can. Speak for the woman if you can.
The constitution's twelfth article is watching this small hall.
