# Ethiopia's Constitutions — From Kibre Negest to 1995 — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** The 1955 Revised Constitution guaranteed citizens' rights.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** The 1987 constitution introduced secularism for the first time.
