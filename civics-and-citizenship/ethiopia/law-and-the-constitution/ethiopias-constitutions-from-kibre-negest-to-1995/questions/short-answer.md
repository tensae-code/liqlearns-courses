# Ethiopia's Constitutions — From Kibre Negest to 1995 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three traditional constitutional texts.
**Answer:** The Kibre Negest, the Fetha Negest and the Sirate Mengist.

**2. (Sub lesson 4)** Name the five principles of the 1995 Constitution with their articles.
**Answer:** Popular sovereignty (Art. 8); supremacy of the constitution (Art. 9); respect for human and democratic rights (Art. 10); secularism (Art. 11); transparency and accountability (Art. 12).

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** State the unit's closing judgement.
**Answer:** Ethiopia has a long history of constitutions, but not a good history of constitutionalism.
