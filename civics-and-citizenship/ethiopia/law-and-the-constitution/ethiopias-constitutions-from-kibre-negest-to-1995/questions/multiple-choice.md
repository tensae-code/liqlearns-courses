# Ethiopia's Constitutions — From Kibre Negest to 1995 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Fetha Negest made challenge to the king —
a) a civic duty  b) rebellion against the Creator  c) a court case  d) a festival
**Answer: b) rebellion against the Creator**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The 1931 constitution's aim was —
a) founding citizens' rights  b) entrenching the king and appearing modern abroad  c) federalism  d) secularism
**Answer: b) entrenching the king and appearing modern abroad**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Between 1974 and 1987 Ethiopia lived under —
a) the 1955 constitution  b) a constitutional vacuum of decrees  c) the Transitional Charter  d) two houses
**Answer: b) a constitutional vacuum of decrees**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The 1991 Transitional Charter had only —
a) 106 articles  b) 20 articles  c) 55 articles  d) 131 articles
**Answer: b) 20 articles**

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** Article 11 of the 1995 Constitution carries the principle of —
a) supremacy  b) secularism — separation of state and religion  c) popular sovereignty  d) accountability
**Answer: b) secularism — separation of state and religion**
