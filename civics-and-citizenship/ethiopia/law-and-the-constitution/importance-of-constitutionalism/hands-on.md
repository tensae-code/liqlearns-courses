# Hands-on — Importance of constitutionalism — limited power, real rights

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening again, for real
An official must hand an account book to watching citizens outside parliament: here is what I did with your power and your money. That handing-over is this whole lesson in one picture. Read for the six reasons it matters.

## Sort it on the table
On paper, write the lesson's key pairs from its matching questions — one term per slip. Shuffle the slips, then sort and match them again from memory, saying each meaning aloud as you place it. Any pair you cannot explain goes back into the lesson for one more read.

## Take it home
Find this lesson where you live: one real example of its one thing — constitutionalism matters six ways, from enhancing democracy to promoting the common good — in your family, your school or your street. Write two sentences about it: what happened, and what the lesson says about it. Bring the sentences back and read them to your companion.
