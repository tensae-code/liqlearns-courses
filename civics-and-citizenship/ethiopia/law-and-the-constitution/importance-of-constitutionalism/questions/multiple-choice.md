# Importance of constitutionalism — limited power, real rights — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. **Name one of the six importances.
a) it enhances democracy  b) it enriches officials  c) it ends all elections
**Answer: a) it enhances democracy**

**2. **Officials under constitutionalism are —
a) owners of the state  b) trustees, replaceable at elections when they fail  c) above accounting
**Answer: b) trustees, replaceable at elections when they fail**

**3. **Equality before the law means no twisting law for —
a) citizens of one region  b) the educated  c) rich or poor, king or servant
**Answer: c) rich or poor, king or servant**

**4. **Popular sovereignty includes —
a) recall of failed representatives  b) hereditary succession  c) rule by decree
**Answer: a) recall of failed representatives**

**5. **The common good puts —
a) personal interest first  b) social interest above personal interest  c) party interest above all
**Answer: b) social interest above personal interest**
