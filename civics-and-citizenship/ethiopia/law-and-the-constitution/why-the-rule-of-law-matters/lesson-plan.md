# Lesson plan — Why the rule of law matters

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Law and the Constitution · **Lesson:** Why the rule of law matters
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 18 — Law and the Constitution (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the rule of law matters four ways: it grounds democracy, frees judges, protects freedom, and blocks dictatorship.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the levels before it in this course's path order (recommended, never forced); inside its own title it stands on the lessons before it.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. ## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — A father is driving his son, late for school. Empty road. Red light. No officer anywhere. The father wants to go through. His wife says wait. You already know the Zeberega version of this story — now judge the question underneath it: do we obey only when watched?
