# Why the rule of law matters — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. **Name the four importances — first: the rule of law is —
a) a precondition of democratic government  b) a favour governments grant  c) a foreign invention
**Answer: a) a precondition of democratic government**

**2. **It frees the judiciary to —
a) follow popular pressure  b) decide by law alone  c) obey ministers
**Answer: b) decide by law alone**

**3. **Individual freedom under it includes —
a) freedom from all law  b) freedom to intimidate  c) opposing views and a free media
**Answer: c) opposing views and a free media**

**4. **Dictatorship's tools, which it prevents, are —
a) force, fear and intimidation  b) debate and elections  c) courts and constitutions
**Answer: a) force, fear and intimidation**

**5. **The red-light story asks —
a) whether traffic laws matter in towns  b) whether we obey only when watched  c) who drives better
**Answer: b) whether we obey only when watched**
