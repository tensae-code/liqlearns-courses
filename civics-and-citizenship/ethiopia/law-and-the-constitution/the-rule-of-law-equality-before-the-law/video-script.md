# Video script — The rule of law — equality before the law

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. This is the lesson's full read-aloud script, where the design names a video-script need.

---

A guard punishes the wrong boy —
because the other boy is the principal's son.

That is what the **rule of law** forbids.
No arbitrary power. No one above the law.
Penalty follows conduct, never connection.

And beneath the rule stands **justice**:
a law that discriminates is unjust,
however evenly it is applied.
