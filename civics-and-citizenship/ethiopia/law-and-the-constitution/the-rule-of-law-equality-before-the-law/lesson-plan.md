# Lesson plan — The rule of law — equality before the law

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Law and the Constitution · **Lesson:** The rule of law — equality before the law
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 18 — Law and the Constitution (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the rule of law means no arbitrary power and equality before the law — and justice standing beneath it.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the levels before it in this course's path order (recommended, never forced); inside its own title it stands on the lessons before it.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
1. **No one above the law** — Laws are binding rules protecting safety and rights against everyone — **including government itself**.
2. **Justice beneath it** — Beneath the rule stands **justice**: moral rightness and fairness.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the Democracy level. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Define the rule of law by its two marks.
2. Judge the school-guard case.
3. Explain why a discriminating law is unjust.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — A school guard catches two boys fighting over a rope. He knows the principal's son started it. He punishes the other boy — the innocent one. Before reading: which rule has been broken, even if no written school rule names it?
2. **The explanation** — the lesson's parts in order (lesson/lesson.md).

   - ADD AUDIO: none named in the placements for this lesson — no audio slot is built.
