# Hands-on — The rule of law — equality before the law

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening again, for real
A school guard catches two boys fighting over a rope. He knows the principal's son started it. He punishes the other boy — the innocent one. Before reading: which rule has been broken, even if no written school rule names it?

## Sort it on the table
On paper, write the lesson's key pairs from its matching questions — one term per slip. Shuffle the slips, then sort and match them again from memory, saying each meaning aloud as you place it. Any pair you cannot explain goes back into the lesson for one more read.

## Take it home
Find this lesson where you live: one real example of its one thing — the rule of law means no arbitrary power and equality before the law — and justice standing beneath it — in your family, your school or your street. Write two sentences about it: what happened, and what the lesson says about it. Bring the sentences back and read them to your companion.
