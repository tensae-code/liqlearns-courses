# The rule of law — equality before the law — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) **No ____ person holds benefits unavailable to all.
**Answer: legal**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) **A discriminating law is ____, even if applied evenly.
**Answer: unjust**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) **Justice must be ____ — favouring neither side.
**Answer: unbiased**
