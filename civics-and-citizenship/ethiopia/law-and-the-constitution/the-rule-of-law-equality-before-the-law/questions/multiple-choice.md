# The rule of law — equality before the law — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) **The rule of law binds —
a) everyone, including government itself  b) only ordinary citizens  c) only pupils and workers
**Answer: a) everyone, including government itself**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) **Its first mark is —
a) fast courts  b) absence of arbitrary government power  c) many laws
**Answer: b) absence of arbitrary government power**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) **In the guard case, penalty followed —
a) the evidence  b) the school rules  c) connection, not conduct
**Answer: c) connection, not conduct**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) **Justice is defined as —
a) moral rightness and fairness  b) speed of punishment  c) loyalty to officials
**Answer: a) moral rightness and fairness**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) **A law discriminating by race, even applied evenly, is —
a) just, because it is even  b) unjust  c) not a law
**Answer: b) unjust**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) **Zeberega and Tinkuk's story teaches law is obeyed —
a) only near police  b) only in cities  c) always, not only when watched
**Answer: c) always, not only when watched**
