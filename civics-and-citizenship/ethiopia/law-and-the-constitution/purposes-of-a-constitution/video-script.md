# Video script — Purposes of a constitution

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. A short script — this lesson's design names no full video-script need, so the script stays short.

---

Why does a country write a constitution?
To **limit power** and **secure rights** — its core.
Then: structure, supremacy, duties and welfare,
the form of government, legitimacy,
and the nation's values in its flag and anthem.
One frame. A whole country inside it.
