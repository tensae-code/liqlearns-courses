# Purposes of a constitution

## Do it first

Draw a house frame: beams, rooms, a flag on the roof. A constitution is drawn the same way in this lesson — a frame within which a whole country lives. Guess its jobs before reading: what must a frame do?

## Core purpose, and the six

Above everything stands the **core purpose**: limiting government power while securing citizens' rights. Then the six purposes.

[Picture 28685 — a constitution as a house frame with a flag on the roof]

[Picture 28686 — a constitution book used as a plumb line for smaller laws]

**One:** it defines the political structure and distributes power — federal or unitary; who does what. **Two:** it stands as supreme law and national symbol, under which everything operates. **Three:** it sets citizens' duties and rights and welfare guarantees — life, liberty, thought, speech, association, assembly, freedom from arbitrary arrest at minimum. **Four:** it determines the government system's form — presidential, parliamentary-republican, monarchical. **Five:** it legitimises government, above all in newly formed states. **Six:** it reflects the nation's values and fixes its flag, anthem and symbols for nation building.

A plumb line tests every later law against this frame: measure the law by the constitution, never the other way round.
