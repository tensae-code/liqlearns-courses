# Lesson plan — Purposes of a constitution

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Law and the Constitution · **Lesson:** Purposes of a constitution
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 18 — Law and the Constitution (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a constitution's core purpose is limiting government power while securing citizens' rights — and it does six more things besides.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the levels before it in this course's path order (recommended, never forced); inside its own title it stands on the lessons before it.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. ## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the core purpose.
2. List the six purposes.
3. Use the house-frame picture to recall them.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — Draw a house frame: beams, rooms, a flag on the roof. A constitution is drawn the same way in this lesson — a frame within which a whole country lives. Guess its jobs before reading: what must a frame do?
2. **The explanation** — the lesson's parts in order (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 28685]: a picture of a constitution as a house frame: structure beams, rights rooms, flag on the roof — the student assigns each purpose to beams, rooms or roof.
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 28686]: a picture of a constitution book used as a plumb line against which smaller law books are measured — the student says which book measures, and which is measured.
   - ADD AUDIO: none named in the placements for this lesson — no audio slot is built.
3. **Say it to your companion** — explain the one thing in your own words, with the lesson's own case (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD VIDEO: the script in video-script.md, filmed — in the super-simple voice (made later, when videos are made).
4. **Questions** — from questions/: multiple-choice, matching, fill-in-the-blank — served at the learner's level and teaching-mix (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
