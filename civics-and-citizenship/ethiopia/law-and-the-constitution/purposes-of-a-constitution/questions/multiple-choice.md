# Purposes of a constitution — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. **The core purpose is —
a) limiting government power while securing citizens' rights  b) raising taxes efficiently  c) choosing a capital city
**Answer: a) limiting government power while securing citizens' rights**

**2. **Name one of the six purposes.
a) it appoints all officials personally  b) it defines the political structure and distributes power  c) it sets market prices
**Answer: b) it defines the political structure and distributes power**

**3. **Citizens' guarantees named include —
a) only property rights  b) only voting rights  c) life, liberty, thought, speech, association, assembly
**Answer: c) life, liberty, thought, speech, association, assembly**

**4. **A constitution legitimises government especially in —
a) newly formed states  b) the oldest empires  c) city councils
**Answer: a) newly formed states**

**5. **It fixes the nation's —
a) holidays only  b) flag, anthem and symbols  c) army uniforms only
**Answer: b) flag, anthem and symbols**
