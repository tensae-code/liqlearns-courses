# Kinds of constitution — written, unwritten, rigid, flexible — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) **A written constitution is —
a) gathered in one document  b) kept only in memory  c) illegal to print
**Answer: a) gathered in one document**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) **The unwritten example is —
a) Ethiopia  b) the United Kingdom  c) Nigeria
**Answer: b) the United Kingdom**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) **An unwritten constitution lives in —
a) a single sealed book  b) foreign archives  c) scattered documents, customs, conventions, acts and decisions
**Answer: c) scattered documents, customs, conventions, acts and decisions**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) **A rigid constitution changes —
a) only through special procedures  b) by any ordinary law  c) by a minister's order
**Answer: a) only through special procedures**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) **A flexible constitution can be amended by —
a) no process at all  b) ordinary legislation  c) foreign consent
**Answer: b) ordinary legislation**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) **Amendment means —
a) abolishing the constitution  b) electing a president  c) inserting or altering provisions for changed circumstances
**Answer: c) inserting or altering provisions for changed circumstances**
