# Kinds of constitution — written, unwritten, rigid, flexible — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) **Ethiopia's constitution is ____ and rigid.
**Answer: written**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) **The United Kingdom's constitution is ____.
**Answer: unwritten**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) **A rigid constitution's amendment needs special ____.
**Answer: procedures**
