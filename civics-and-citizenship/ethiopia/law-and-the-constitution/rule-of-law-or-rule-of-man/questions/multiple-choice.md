# Rule of law or rule of man? — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. **Under rule of man, law is —
a) the ruler's tool  b) above the ruler  c) made by the people
**Answer: a) the ruler's tool**

**2. **Rule of man historically meant —
a) fair trials for all  b) imprisonment and seizure, often with no formal charge  c) laws binding the ruler
**Answer: b) imprisonment and seizure, often with no formal charge**

**3. **Under rule of law, even the ruler must —
a) obey only his own conscience  b) change laws by a gesture  c) rule through legal means
**Answer: c) rule through legal means**

**4. **Law under rule of law is made by —
a) the land's supreme lawmaking authority  b) the ruler's household  c) foreign advisors
**Answer: a) the land's supreme lawmaking authority**
