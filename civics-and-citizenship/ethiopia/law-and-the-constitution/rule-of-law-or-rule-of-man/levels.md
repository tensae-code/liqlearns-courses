# Rule of law or rule of man? — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can state and use the whole lesson's one thing — under rule of man the ruler's will is the law; under rule of law the law stands above every individual will, the ruler's included — in their own words, on a fresh example. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners of the same one thing: the lesson's cases judged from the other side, its lists recalled in a different order, its rule applied to the learner's own street and school.
