# Kinds of constitution — effective or nominal, federal or unitary — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) **An effective constitution is one whose provisions are —
a) actually respected and exercised  b) beautifully printed  c) very old
**Answer: a) actually respected and exercised**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) **A nominal constitution —
a) has no text at all  b) promises in text what practice denies  c) is always unwritten
**Answer: b) promises in text what practice denies**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) **The book's nominal example is —
a) the United Kingdom  b) Ethiopia  c) the former Soviet Union
**Answer: c) the former Soviet Union**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) **A unitary constitution can —
a) create or abolish lower governments at will  b) never change any region  c) guarantee every region forever
**Answer: a) create or abolish lower governments at will**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) **A federal constitution —
a) concentrates all power centrally  b) divides power between levels, each guaranteed  c) is always nominal
**Answer: b) divides power between levels, each guaranteed**
