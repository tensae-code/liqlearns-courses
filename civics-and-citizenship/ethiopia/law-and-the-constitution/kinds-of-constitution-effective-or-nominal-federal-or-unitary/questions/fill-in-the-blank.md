# Kinds of constitution — effective or nominal, federal or unitary — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) **Paper freedoms that cannot be used mark a ____ constitution.
**Answer: nominal**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) **The test of effectiveness is whether a citizen can ____ on the promise.
**Answer: act**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) **A unitary constitution gives lower governments no constitutional ____ of survival.
**Answer: guarantee**
