# Lesson plan — Kinds of constitution — effective or nominal, federal or unitary

**The one thing this lesson teaches:** constitutions are also classified by performance — effective or nominal — and by structure — federal or unitary.

## Sub lessons
1. **Lived or paper-only** — By performance: an **effective constitution** has its provisions actually respected and exercised — constitutionalism in action.
2. **Shared or central power** — By structure: a **federal constitution** divides power between federal and regional levels, each guaranteed its sphere.
