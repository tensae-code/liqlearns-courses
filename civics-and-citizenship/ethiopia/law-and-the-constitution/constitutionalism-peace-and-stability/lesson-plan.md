# Lesson plan — Constitutionalism, peace and stability

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Law and the Constitution · **Lesson:** Constitutionalism, peace and stability
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 18 — Law and the Constitution (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** where constitutionalism holds, rights are safe and life is predictable; where it fails come lawlessness, arbitrary rule and fear.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the levels before it in this course's path order (recommended, never forced); inside its own title it stands on the lessons before it.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
1. **Where constitutionalism holds** — Where constitutionalism holds, **individual and group rights are safe** from interference.
2. **What its absence brings** — Its absence brings three fruits.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Kinds of constitution — effective or nominal, federal or unitary. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Describe life where constitutionalism holds.
2. List the three fruits of its absence.
3. Judge the closing exercise against the provisions.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — Two versions of the same street. In one, an officer helps a child cross; people plan their week without fear. In the other, the street is empty — everyone indoors, unsure what tomorrow's rule will be. The difference between the streets is this lesson.
