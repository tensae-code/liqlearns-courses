# Constitutionalism, peace and stability — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) **Where constitutionalism holds, life is —
a) predictable and secure  b) ruled by daily decree  c) planned by officials alone
**Answer: a) predictable and secure**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) **Police under constitutionalism —
a) answer to no law  b) prevent crime rather than prey on people  c) collect fines personally
**Answer: b) prevent crime rather than prey on people**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) **Name one fruit of its absence.
a) predictability  b) protected rights  c) lawlessness
**Answer: c) lawlessness**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) **Arbitrary rule means —
a) action by personal will, nothing predictable  b) strict court procedure  c) rule by constitution
**Answer: a) action by personal will, nothing predictable**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) **The closing exercise imagines an officer who may beat and torture with —
a) a court's permission  b) no court to appeal to  c) a public trial first
**Answer: b) no court to appeal to**
