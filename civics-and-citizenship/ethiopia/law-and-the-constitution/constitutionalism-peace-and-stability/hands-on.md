# Hands-on — Constitutionalism, peace and stability

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening again, for real
Two versions of the same street. In one, an officer helps a child cross; people plan their week without fear. In the other, the street is empty — everyone indoors, unsure what tomorrow's rule will be. The difference between the streets is this lesson.

## Sort it on the table
On paper, write the lesson's key pairs from its matching questions — one term per slip. Shuffle the slips, then sort and match them again from memory, saying each meaning aloud as you place it. Any pair you cannot explain goes back into the lesson for one more read.

## Take it home
Find this lesson where you live: one real example of its one thing — where constitutionalism holds, rights are safe and life is predictable; where it fails come lawlessness, arbitrary rule and fear — in your family, your school or your street. Write two sentences about it: what happened, and what the lesson says about it. Bring the sentences back and read them to your companion.
