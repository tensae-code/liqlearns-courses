# Constitution and constitutionalism — the rulebook, and obeying it — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) **A constitution holds three things — name one.
a) the nation's basic principles  b) the year's tax rates  c) party membership lists
**Answer: a) the nation's basic principles**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) **A law contradicting the constitution is —
a) automatically supreme  b) void  c) postponed one year
**Answer: b) void**

**3. (Sub lesson 2) **Constitutionalism means —
a)  owning a printed constitution  b) writing long preambles  c) government actually conducted within the constitution's bounds
**Answer: c) government actually conducted within the constitution's bounds**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) **Can a country have a constitution without constitutionalism?
a) yes — it can still be ruled arbitrarily  b) no — never  c) only in monarchies
**Answer: a) yes — it can still be ruled arbitrarily**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) **Constitutionalism is described as —
a) a type of court  b) a political culture more than a text  c) a tax system
**Answer: b) a political culture more than a text**
