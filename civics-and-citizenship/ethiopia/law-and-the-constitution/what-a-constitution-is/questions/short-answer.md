# What a Constitution Is — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is gathered inside the word 'aggregate' in the definition?
**Answer:** Laws, customs, conventions, principles, rules and regulations — the whole frame by which a government carries on its work.

**2.** Why can no public body stand outside the constitution's reach?
**Answer:** Because it is the frame inside which everything else is built — parliament, courts and officials all work downstream of it.
