# What a Constitution Is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** A constitution is —
a) one law among many  b) the fundamental, supreme law of a state  c) a speech by a ruler  d) a party's programme
**Answer: b) the fundamental, supreme law of a state**

**2.** A constitution defines —
a) only taxes  b) the nature of government, its functions, and citizens' rights and duties  c) only the army  d) only school rules
**Answer: b) the nature of government, its functions, and citizens' rights and duties**
