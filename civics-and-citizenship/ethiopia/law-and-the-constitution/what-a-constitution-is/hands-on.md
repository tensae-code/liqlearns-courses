# Hands-on — What a Constitution Is

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The plinth
The group builds the picture with boxes and books: one book raised on a pile, with paper 'parliament', 'courts' and 'citizens' placed below it. Each learner explains the arrangement to a partner.

## Above everyone
Role-play the school with no rulebook for two minutes of chaos-by-consent, then with the rulebook restored. Discuss what the one book changed.
