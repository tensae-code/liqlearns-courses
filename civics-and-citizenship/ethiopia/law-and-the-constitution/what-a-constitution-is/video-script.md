# Video script — What a Constitution Is

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing: a constitution is the fundamental, supreme law of a state — the whole frame of laws, principles and rules by which a government works and citizens hold their rights and duties.
One book stands above the whole state.
It tells the government what it is.
It tells citizens what they hold.
Laws are made beneath it.
Courts judge beneath it.
That book is the constitution.
