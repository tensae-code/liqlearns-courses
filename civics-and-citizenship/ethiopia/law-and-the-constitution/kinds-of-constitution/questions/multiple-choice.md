# Kinds of Constitution — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Most states' constitutions are —
a) unwritten  b) written in a single codified document  c) memorised  d) secret
**Answer: b) written in a single codified document**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The written constitution's great advantage is —
a) it can never change  b) access — citizens can find and read their own rights  c) it is shorter  d) it needs no courts
**Answer: b) access — citizens can find and read their own rights**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A rigid constitution —
a) changes by ordinary law  b) demands a special, difficult amendment procedure  c) is always unwritten  d) has no amendments at all
**Answer: b) demands a special, difficult amendment procedure**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The 1995 FDRE Constitution is the lesson's example of a ______ constitution.
a) unitary  b) unwritten  c) federal  d) flexible-only
**Answer: c) federal**
