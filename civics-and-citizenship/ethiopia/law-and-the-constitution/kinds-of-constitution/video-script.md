# Video script — Kinds of Constitution

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing: constitutions are sorted two ways: written or unwritten, and rigid or flexible — and also by where power sits, federal or unitary.
Some constitutions live in one book.
You can open it. You can read your rights.
Others live in custom — harder to reach.
Some are rigid: change is difficult.
Some are flexible: change is ordinary.
Ethiopia's is written, rigid, and federal.
