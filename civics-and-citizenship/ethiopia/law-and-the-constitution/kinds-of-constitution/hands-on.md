# Hands-on — Kinds of Constitution

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## One book or five elders
Two corners of the room: in one, a rule is checked in a written book; in the other, by asking elders what has always been done. Learners run the same query both ways and compare certainty and speed.

## The grid on the floor
Chalk a two-by-two grid — written/unwritten, rigid/flexible. Country name-cards are placed by the group, each placement defended from the lesson.
