# Principles of a Constitution — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** How many fundamental principles does this lesson teach?
a) Four  b) Five  c) Six  d) Eight
**Answer: c) Six**

**2.** 'The people are the ultimate source of power' is —
a) limited government  b) popular sovereignty  c) separation of powers  d) transparency
**Answer: b) popular sovereignty**

**3.** Officials answerable for their failures belongs to —
a) rule of law  b) transparency and accountability  c) popular sovereignty  d) limited government
**Answer: b) transparency and accountability**
