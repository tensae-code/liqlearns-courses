# Principles of a Constitution — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name all six principles.
**Answer:** Popular sovereignty; limited government; separation of powers; rule of law; respect for human rights; transparency and accountability.

**2.** How do separation of powers and check-and-balance join?
**Answer:** Legislative, executive and judiciary are kept apart so that each can check the others and balance power.
