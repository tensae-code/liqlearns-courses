# Video script — Principles of a Constitution

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing: six fundamental principles live inside a constitution: popular sovereignty, limited government, separation of powers, rule of law, respect for human rights, and transparency and accountability.
Six pillars hold the roof.
The people are the source of power.
Power is limited. Powers are separated.
The law binds the rulers too.
Rights are respected. Officials answer.
Remove one pillar — and feel the roof move.
