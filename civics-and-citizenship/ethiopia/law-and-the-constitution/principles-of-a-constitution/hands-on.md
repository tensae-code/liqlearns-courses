# Hands-on — Principles of a Constitution

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Six pillars standing
Six learners stand as pillars, each holding their principle's name. A 'roof' card — the constitution — rests across raised hands. Remove one pillar: feel what the roof does.

## Judge the mayor
The mayor scene is acted, then frozen at three moments. At each freeze, the class calls out which principles are cracking.
