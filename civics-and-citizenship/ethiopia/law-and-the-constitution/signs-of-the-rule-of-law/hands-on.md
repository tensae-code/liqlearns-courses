# Hands-on — Signs of the rule of law — independent courts, equal law, peace

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening again, for real
How would you know, walking through a town, whether the rule of law lives there? Not by its law books. This lesson gives you three signposts to look for — check any real place against them.

## Sort it on the table
On paper, write the lesson's key pairs from its matching questions — one term per slip. Shuffle the slips, then sort and match them again from memory, saying each meaning aloud as you place it. Any pair you cannot explain goes back into the lesson for one more read.

## Take it home
Find this lesson where you live: one real example of its one thing — you can see the rule of law by three signs: independent courts, equal application of law, and prevailing peace and order — in your family, your school or your street. Write two sentences about it: what happened, and what the lesson says about it. Bring the sentences back and read them to your companion.
