# Signs of the rule of law — independent courts, equal law, peace — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. **Name the three signs.
a) independent judiciary, equal application of law, prevailing peace and order  b) many laws, large prisons, strict police  c) elections, parties, media
**Answer: a) independent judiciary, equal application of law, prevailing peace and order**

**2. **An independent judge answers to —
a) the loudest crowd  b) the law alone  c) the minister who appointed them
**Answer: b) the law alone**

**3. **Presumed innocent means —
a) guilty until proven rich  b) free only after punishment  c) innocent until a court finds guilt
**Answer: c) innocent until a court finds guilt**

**4. **Punishment must be set by a court and be —
a) proportionate  b) as harsh as possible  c) decided by the arresting officer
**Answer: a) proportionate**

**5. **When security fails, people —
a) plan better by the law  b) take the law into their own hands  c) trust government more
**Answer: b) take the law into their own hands**
