# Signs of the rule of law — independent courts, equal law, peace — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word or words.

**1. **Everyone is presumed ____ until a court finds guilt.
**Answer: innocent**

**2. **There must be no ____ detention, imprisonment or seizure.
**Answer: arbitrary**

**3. **Punishment is set by a court and must be ____.
**Answer: proportionate**
