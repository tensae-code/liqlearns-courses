# Signs of the rule of law — independent courts, equal law, peace — matching

Match each item on the left with its meaning on the right.

**1. **Independent judiciary — a
**2. **Equal application — c
**3. **Presumption of innocence — d
**4. **Peace and order — b

**Answer key:**
1. Independent judiciary — judges free of party, interest and pressure
2. Equal application — like cases treated alike
3. Presumption of innocence — innocent until a court finds guilt
4. Peace and order — the third sign

Meanings, as lettered above:
a) judges free of party, interest and pressure
b) the third sign
c) like cases treated alike
d) innocent until a court finds guilt
