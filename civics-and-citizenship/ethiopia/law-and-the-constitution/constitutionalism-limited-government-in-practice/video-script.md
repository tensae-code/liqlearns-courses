# Video script — Constitutionalism — Limited Government in Practice

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing: constitutionalism is a constitution actually obeyed — limited government in real practice, where rights are realised, power is checked, and the rule of law stands against arbitrary judgement.
A price list on the wall is not a price.
A constitution on a shelf is not a limit.
Constitutionalism is the till obeying the wall —
rights lived, power checked, law ruling.
Unenforced, the mightiest text is a paper tiger.
