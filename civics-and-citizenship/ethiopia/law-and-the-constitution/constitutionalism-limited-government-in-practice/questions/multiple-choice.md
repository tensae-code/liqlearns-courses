# Constitutionalism — Limited Government in Practice — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Constitutionalism is —
a) owning a constitution  b) government acting according to the supreme law, with rights actually realised  c) writing a constitution  d) a kind of election
**Answer: b) government acting according to the supreme law, with rights actually realised**

**2.** Which is NOT one of the three importances?
a) It realises human rights  b) It controls abuse and misuse of power  c) It founds the rule of law against arbitrary judgement  d) It guarantees the ruler's comfort
**Answer: c) It founds the rule of law against arbitrary judgement**
