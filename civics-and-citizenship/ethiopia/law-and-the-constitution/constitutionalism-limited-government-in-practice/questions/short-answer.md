# Constitutionalism — Limited Government in Practice — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What does the paper tiger warn against?
**Answer:** A constitution that exists on paper but is unenforced — it has the stripes and frightens no one; only enforced law makes it real.

**2.** How does constitutionalism prevent tyranny?
**Answer:** By realising human rights in practice, checking officials' acts for legitimacy, and giving institutional safeguards — power meets limits it cannot step over.
