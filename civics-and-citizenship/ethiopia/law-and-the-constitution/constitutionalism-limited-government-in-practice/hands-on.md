# Hands-on — Constitutionalism — Limited Government in Practice

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The wall and the till
A learner plays a shopkeeper with a fine price list who charges double at the till; customers may appeal only to the list. The class discusses why the appeal failed — and what would make it succeed.

## Paper tiger, real fence
The group draws the paper tiger behind a fence labelled 'enforced law'. Each learner adds one bar to the fence: a real enforcement that turns paper into practice.
