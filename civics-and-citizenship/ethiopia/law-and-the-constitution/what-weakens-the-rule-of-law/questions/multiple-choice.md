# What weakens the rule of law — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) **Name the four factors.
a) corruption, illiteracy, poor leadership, lack of patriotism  b) taxes, elections, media, courts  c) poverty, weather, distance, age
**Answer: a) corruption, illiteracy, poor leadership, lack of patriotism**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) **The chief impediment is —
a) illiteracy  b) corruption  c) poor leadership
**Answer: b) corruption**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) **Illiteracy weakens the rule of law because —
a) readers break more laws  b) books are expensive  c) unaware citizens stay silent when government acts above the law
**Answer: c) unaware citizens stay silent when government acts above the law**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) **The bakery worker discovered —
a) injera mixed with harmful substances  b) a new recipe  c) an unlocked door
**Answer: a) injera mixed with harmful substances**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) **After her report, the court —
a) warned her to stay silent  b) imprisoned and fined the offenders  c) closed the bakery only
**Answer: b) imprisoned and fined the offenders**
