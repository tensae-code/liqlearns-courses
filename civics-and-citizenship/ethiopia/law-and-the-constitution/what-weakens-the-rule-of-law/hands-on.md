# Hands-on — What weakens the rule of law

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening again, for real
A courthouse wall with four cracks. Each crack has a name. Before reading, guess the four: what eats a legal system from inside? Then meet one bakery worker who patched the wall instead of cracking it.

## Sort it on the table
On paper, write the lesson's key pairs from its matching questions — one term per slip. Shuffle the slips, then sort and match them again from memory, saying each meaning aloud as you place it. Any pair you cannot explain goes back into the lesson for one more read.

## Take it home
Find this lesson where you live: one real example of its one thing — four factors weaken the rule of law — corruption, illiteracy, poor leadership, lack of patriotism — and one citizen's report shows the answer — in your family, your school or your street. Write two sentences about it: what happened, and what the lesson says about it. Bring the sentences back and read them to your companion.
