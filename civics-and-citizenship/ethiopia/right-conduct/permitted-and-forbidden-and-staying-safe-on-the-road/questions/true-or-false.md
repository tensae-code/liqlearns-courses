# Permitted and Forbidden and Staying Safe on the Road — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Crossing on the marked crossing is part of staying safe on the road.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Asking when you are unsure is itself good conduct.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Road rules only protect other people, never you.
**Answer: False**
