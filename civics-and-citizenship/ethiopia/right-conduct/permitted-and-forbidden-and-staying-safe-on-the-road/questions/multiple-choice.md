# Permitted and Forbidden and Staying Safe on the Road — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Things permitted and forbidden exist so that —
a) adults can punish freely  b) shared life stays safe and fair  c) children live in fear
**Answer: b) shared life stays safe and fair**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** When unsure whether a thing is permitted, you should —
a) ask before you act  b) do it secretly  c) do it quickly before anyone sees
**Answer: a) ask before you act**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The safe crossing sequence is —
a) run across between cars  b) cross while looking at a phone  c) stop, look both ways, cross on the marked crossing
**Answer: c) stop, look both ways, cross on the marked crossing**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Road rules are taught in this lesson as —
a) rules for drivers only  b) ethics you can be hurt by breaking  c) suggestions you may ignore
**Answer: b) ethics you can be hurt by breaking**
