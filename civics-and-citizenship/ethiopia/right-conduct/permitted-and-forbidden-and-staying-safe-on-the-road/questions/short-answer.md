# Permitted and Forbidden and Staying Safe on the Road — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does a community mark some things forbidden?
**Answer:** To keep its shared life safe and fair — the forbidden things are the ones that bring harm.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Walk through the safe way to cross a road.
**Answer:** Stop at the edge, look both ways (left, right, left again), cross on the marked crossing, walking in a straight line.
