## Sub lesson 1 — Marks of a person of good morals
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: state and apply this part's teaching — The marks of a person of good morals are: integrity, trustworthiness, accountability, giving others value, respect and admiration, self-control, being worthy of trust, and keeping secrets.

## Sub lesson 2 — Respect
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: state and apply this part's teaching — Respecting means honouring people for their personhood, their ability and their good qualities — and it includes respecting others' property: what is theirs is not yours to take or spoil.
