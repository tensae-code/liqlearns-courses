# Marks of Good Character and Respect — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name five marks of a person of good morals.
**Answer:** Any five of: integrity, trustworthiness, accountability, giving others value, respect and admiration, self-control, being worthy of trust, keeping secrets.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give three reasons respect is needed.
**Answer:** Any three of: conscience's satisfaction, solving problems, showing honesty, building better relationships.
