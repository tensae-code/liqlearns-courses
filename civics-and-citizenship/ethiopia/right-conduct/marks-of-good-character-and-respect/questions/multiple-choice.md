# Marks of Good Character and Respect — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which is a mark of a person of good morals?
a) Keeping secrets you were trusted with  b) Winning every argument  c) Being feared by classmates
**Answer: a) Keeping secrets you were trusted with**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Respecting a person means honouring them for —
a) their wealth only  b) their power to punish  c) their personhood, ability and good qualities
**Answer: c) their personhood, ability and good qualities**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** An honest person is —
a) clever at hiding theft  b) always truthful and never steals  c) truthful only when watched
**Answer: b) always truthful and never steals**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Respect is needed because it —
a) builds better relationships  b) makes you richer  c) excuses you from work
**Answer: a) builds better relationships**
