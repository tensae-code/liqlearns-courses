# Marks of Good Character and Respect — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Respect includes respecting others' property.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Respect begins from self-respect and extends outward.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Being worthy of trust and trustworthiness are the same idea twice and count as no mark at all.
**Answer: False**
