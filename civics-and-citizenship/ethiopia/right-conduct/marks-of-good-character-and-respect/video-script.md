**Marks of a person of good morals.** The marks of a person of good morals are: integrity, trustworthiness, accountability, giving others value, respect and admiration, self-control, being worthy of trust, and keeping secrets.

**Respect.** Respecting means honouring people for their personhood, their ability and their good qualities — and it includes respecting others' property: what is theirs is not yours to take or spoil.

Now you. Tell your companion the marks of a person of good morals.
