# Marks of Good Character and Respect

## Do it first

Hold out your hand as if someone just dropped a purse into it by accident. Decide now, before it ever happens: untouched, returned, every coin inside. That decision, made early, is what this lesson is for.

## Sub lesson 1 — Marks of a person of good morals

The marks of a person of good morals are: integrity, trustworthiness, accountability, giving others value, respect and admiration, self-control, being worthy of trust, and keeping secrets. Read the list slowly — several marks sound alike because they are one cloth: a person whose inside and outside agree, who can be trusted, who answers for their acts, and who guards what others entrust to them.

## Sub lesson 2 — Respect

Respecting means honouring people for their personhood, their ability and their good qualities — and it includes respecting others' property: what is theirs is not yours to take or spoil. An honest person is always truthful and never steals. Why is respect needed? For conscience's satisfaction — you can rest inside your own judgement; for solving problems; for showing honesty; and for building better relationships. Respect begins from self-respect and extends outward — even beyond people, to the world around you.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the marks of a person of good morals. Then explain what respecting means, who an honest person is, and two reasons respect is needed.

## Do It Yourself

1. Return or hand back one thing this week that is not yours, exactly as you found it.
2. Guard one secret you were trusted with — do not pass it on.
3. Greet one person today with deliberate respect for who they are, not for what they can give you.
