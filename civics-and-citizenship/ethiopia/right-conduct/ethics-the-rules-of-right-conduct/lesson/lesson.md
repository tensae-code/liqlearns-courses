# Ethics — The Rules of Right Conduct

## Do it first

Pause at the next doorway you pass today — a real one. In the pause, ask: when I go through, how will I treat the people on the other side? That small question, asked all day long, is where this lesson lives.

## What ethics means

Ethics — ሥነ-ምግባር (sine-migbar) — is the rules of right conduct: the settled rules a person carries into everything they do with others. Ethics is not one rule for school and another for home. It is one set of rules about how people may be treated, carried through every door you pass.

## Rules you carry

A rule you carry is different from a rule written on a wall. The wall rule stays at school; the carried rule goes with you to the market, the road, the neighbour's house. That is why ethics is learned as conduct — what you do — and not only as words you can repeat.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what ethics means, and say the Amharic word: ሥነ-ምግባር. Then explain why ethics is a rule you carry, not a rule on a wall.

## Do It Yourself

1. Name three rules of right conduct you carried through today.
2. Pause at one doorway today and choose your conduct before you enter.
3. Teach the word ሥነ-ምግባር to someone at home and tell them what it means.
