Ethics — ሥነ-ምግባር (sine-migbar) — is the rules of right conduct: the settled rules a person carries into everything they do with others.

**What ethics means.** Ethics — ሥነ-ምግባር (sine-migbar) — is the rules of right conduct: the settled rules a person carries into everything they do with others.

**Rules you carry.** A rule you carry is different from a rule written on a wall.

Now you. Tell your companion what ethics means, and say the Amharic word: ሥነ-ምግባር.

And this week: pausing at doorways to choose your conduct before you enter.
