# Ethics — The Rules of Right Conduct — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1.** Define ethics in your own words.
**Answer:** The rules of right conduct — the settled rules a person carries into everything they do with others.

**2.** Why must ethics be carried rather than left on a wall?
**Answer:** Because you deal with others everywhere — home, road, market — not only where the rule is written.
