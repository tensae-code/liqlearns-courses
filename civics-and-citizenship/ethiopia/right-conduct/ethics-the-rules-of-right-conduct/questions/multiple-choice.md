# Ethics — The Rules of Right Conduct — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Ethics means —
a) the rules of one game  b) manners for guests only  c) the rules of right conduct
**Answer: c) the rules of right conduct**

**2.** The Amharic word for ethics given here is —
a) ፍትሕ  b) ሥነ-ምግባር  c) ሰላም
**Answer: b) ሥነ-ምግባር**

**3.** A carried rule differs from a wall rule because —
a) it goes with you everywhere you deal with others  b) it is written in bigger letters  c) it only matters at school
**Answer: a) it goes with you everywhere you deal with others**

**4.** Ethics covers —
a) only classroom behaviour  b) only speech, never actions  c) everything you do with others
**Answer: c) everything you do with others**
