# Why Good Morals Are Worth Having

## Do it first

Picture one tree with good shade and fruit, standing between two houses. Both families sit in the shade; both eat. Nobody's share shrinks the other's. Hold that picture — it explains the motto of this lesson: live and let live.

## The value of wearing good behaviour

Good morals are worth having because life is lived together, and people need each other. The strategies named alongside the benefit are part of how the benefit is kept: developing self-control, accepting and strengthening moral values and principles, and being loyal to the law. Good behaviour is not a costume for special days — it is worn daily, and its value shows daily.

[Picture 28221 — sharing one tree's shade]

## Live and let live

'Live and let live' means wanting for others the life you want for yourself. You want shade; let them have shade too. You want your life to go forward; let theirs go forward too. Because people need each other, a good life fenced against everyone else is not a good life at all — it is a small, lonely one.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what 'live and let live' means, and name the strategies that keep good morals strong.

## Do It Yourself

1. Share one thing today — shade, time, food, help — without shrinking your own good.
2. Practise self-control once today as a way of strengthening your morals.
3. Wish one person well out loud, and mean it: their life going forward, like yours.
