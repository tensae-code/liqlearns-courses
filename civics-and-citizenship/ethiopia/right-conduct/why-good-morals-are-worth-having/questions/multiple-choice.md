# Why Good Morals Are Worth Having — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Good morals are worth having because —
a) people need each other and life is lived together  b) they make you better than everyone  c) they excuse hard work
**Answer: a) people need each other and life is lived together**

**2.** 'Live and let live' means —
a) ignoring everyone completely  b) living only for yourself  c) wanting for others the life you want for yourself
**Answer: c) wanting for others the life you want for yourself**

**3.** Which strategy keeps the benefit of good morals?
a) Bending the law when useful  b) Developing self-control  c) Hiding your values
**Answer: b) Developing self-control**

**4.** Being loyal to — is named with the benefit of good morals.
a) the law  b) the strongest group  c) your own moods
**Answer: a) the law**
