# Why Good Morals Are Worth Having — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1.** Name the strategies named alongside the benefit of good morals.
**Answer:** Developing self-control; accepting and strengthening moral values and principles; being loyal to the law.

**2.** Explain 'live and let live' with an example.
**Answer:** Wanting for others the life you want for yourself — e.g., sharing the tree's shade and fruit so both families sit and eat.
