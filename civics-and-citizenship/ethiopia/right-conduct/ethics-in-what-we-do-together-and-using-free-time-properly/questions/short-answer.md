# Ethics in What We Do Together and Using Free Time Properly — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does a community's shared activity need ethics inside it?
**Answer:** Because shared life runs on the conduct of the people sharing it — fairness, honesty and respect keep it from breaking.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name three good uses of free time.
**Answer:** Any three of: resting truly, reading, helping at home, playing fairly.
