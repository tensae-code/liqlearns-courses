# Ethics in What We Do Together and Using Free Time Properly — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ethics inside social activities means —
a) the loudest person decides what is fair  b) right conduct travels into the group's shared life  c) rules stop at the classroom door
**Answer: b) right conduct travels into the group's shared life**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Free time is described as —
a) a trust  b) worthless scraps of the day  c) time when conduct does not count
**Answer: a) a trust**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is free time used well?
a) Idle mischief that damages things  b) Spoiling other people's rest  c) Reading, resting truly, helping at home
**Answer: c) Reading, resting truly, helping at home**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Shared life runs on —
a) having no rules at all  b) the conduct of the people sharing it  c)  luck alone
**Answer: b) the conduct of the people sharing it**
