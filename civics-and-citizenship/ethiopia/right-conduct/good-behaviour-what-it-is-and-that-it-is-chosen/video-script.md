# Video script — Good Behaviour — What It Is and That It Is Chosen

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Behaviour is the indicator of how we treat family, friends, teachers and the community — it shows, outwardly, what a person is like inwardly.

**What good behaviour is.** Behaviour is the indicator of how we treat family, friends, teachers and the community — it shows, outwardly, what a person is like inwardly.

**Behaviour is chosen — and learned from what you watch.** Behaviour is a result of choices.

Now you. Tell your companion what behaviour indicates, name the marks of good behaviour, and then say which example — honest or cheating — a watching child learns from, and why the choice of what to copy matters.

And this week: choosing which examples you copy, on purpose.
