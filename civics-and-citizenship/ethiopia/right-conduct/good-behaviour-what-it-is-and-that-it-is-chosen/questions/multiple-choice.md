# Good Behaviour — What It Is and That It Is Chosen — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Behaviour is the indicator of —
a) how we treat family, friends, teachers and community  b) how tall a person is  c) how much a person owns
**Answer: a) how we treat family, friends, teachers and community**

**2.** Which is a mark of good behaviour?
a) Coldness toward everyone  b) Pride that looks down on others  c) Compassion — sharing others' feelings
**Answer: c) Compassion — sharing others' feelings**

**3.** A child who watches cheating —
a) learns nothing at all  b) learns to think cheating  c) is protected from ever cheating
**Answer: b) learns to think cheating**

**4.** Behaviour is —
a) a result of choices  b) fixed at birth and unchangeable  c) decided by neighbours
**Answer: a) a result of choices**
