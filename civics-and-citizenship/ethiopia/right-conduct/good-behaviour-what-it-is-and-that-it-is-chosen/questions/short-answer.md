# Good Behaviour — What It Is and That It Is Chosen — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1.** Name four marks of good behaviour.
**Answer:** Any four of: humanity, generosity, compassion, sharing others' feelings, thoughtfulness, friendliness, humility.

**2.** Why does it matter what a child watches?
**Answer:** Behaviour is shaped by watched examples — a child who watches cheating learns to think cheating.
