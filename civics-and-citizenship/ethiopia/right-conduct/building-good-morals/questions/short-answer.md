# Building Good Morals — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1.** List the five ways of building good morals.
**Answer:** Self-control; respecting the law; accepting and following the community's values and principles; respecting the school's rules; respecting the community's principles.

**2.** Define self-control and say why it matters.
**Answer:** Restraining yourself from needless thoughts and acts; it saves from harm and makes a balanced life.
