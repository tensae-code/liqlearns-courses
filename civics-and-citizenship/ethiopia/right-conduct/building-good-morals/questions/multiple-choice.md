# Building Good Morals — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which is one of the five ways of building good morals?
a) Respecting the school's rules  b) Avoiding all rules  c) Copying the loudest pupil
**Answer: a) Respecting the school's rules**

**2.** Self-control is —
a) controlling other people's thoughts  b) never thinking at all  c) restraining yourself from needless thoughts and acts
**Answer: c) restraining yourself from needless thoughts and acts**

**3.** Self-control is justified because it —
a) excuses you from duties  b) saves from harm and makes a balanced life  c) makes you popular
**Answer: b) saves from harm and makes a balanced life**

**4.** The advice in the Thomas case is —
a) count what your acts do to yourself and others  b) act first and never think  c) blame the teacher
**Answer: a) count what your acts do to yourself and others**
