# Video script — Building Good Morals

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Good morals are built, and the ways are named — at least five of them: self-control; respecting the law; accepting and following the community's values and principles; respecting the school's rules; and respecting the community's principles.

**The five ways.** Good morals are built, and the ways are named — at least five of them: self-control; respecting the law; accepting and following the community's values and principles; respecting the school's rules; and respecting the community's principles.

**Self-control, defined and justified.** Self-control is restraining yourself from needless thoughts and acts.

Now you. Tell your companion the five ways of building good morals, in order.

And this week: counting what your acts do to yourself and others before you act.
