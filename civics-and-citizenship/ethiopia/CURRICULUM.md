# Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia course — CURRICULUM (the path)

One course for Ethiopia inside the Civics and Citizenship folder,
organised by **levels, not grades**: a learner climbs when they
have proved the level, not when a school calendar says so. The
school grades the books come from appear only in each lesson's
sources (provenance), never as the course's organisation. The path
follows the books' own arcs: character in widening circles first
(Ethiopia's Moral Education, Grades 1–6), then the citizen's
rights and the machinery of the state (Citizenship Education,
Grades 7–10), and at the very end the older generation's course,
kept whole and labelled (Civics and Ethical Education, Grade 11,
2002 E.C.).

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in this
course comes from government textbooks — this part from Ethiopia's
Moral Education and Citizenship Education books — except the
**Tip boxes**: a tip in a box is from us, the people making
LiqLearns. Tips are ours; the lessons are the books'. See
ABOUT.md for the full book list, the guides-only footing of the
Moral Education levels, the older-generation section's label, and
the acceptance reckoning.

## The levels (the path, in order)

**The opening levels — morals and manners** *(Moral Education,
teacher-guide evidence only — every lesson's sources say so)*:

1. **Myself** — what a human being is, dignity, self-control,
   self-respect, keeping myself safe; deepened by self-worth and
   integrity. 18 lessons.
2. **My Family and Respect** — the family and its values, manners
   at home, respecting parents, elders, teachers and every person.
   9 lessons.
3. **Honesty, Justice and Fairness** — honesty in words, promises
   and hands; justice and fairness where we live; standing against
   what is unfair. 10 lessons.
4. **Neighbours, School and Friends** — neighbourliness, manners
   and rules at school, friendships and a healthy social life.
   16 lessons.
5. **Working Together and the Common Good** — the common good,
   public property, the environment as a common good, cooperation
   — debo, idir, equb — and teamwork. 11 lessons.
6. **Community, Culture and Many Cultures** — community and its
   nine values, culture and local culture, heritage and keeping it
   safe, customs, and many cultures in one society. 19 lessons.
7. **Peace and Cooperation** — peace and what it is, making peace
   without fighting, the settled community, cooperation. 6 lessons.
8. **Right Conduct With Others** — ethics, permitted and
   forbidden, road safety, good behaviour and good character.
   7 lessons.
9. **Responsibility, Work and Decisions** — responsibility and its
   kinds, work culture, reading culture, and moral decisions in
   five steps. 14 lessons.
10. **Caring for Our Shared World** — serving the community,
    caring for the environment, taking part, voluntary work,
    saving. 10 lessons.
11. **Living Online Well** — social media: what it is, what it is
    good for, using it rightly, staying safe. 4 lessons.
12. **Tolerance and Cultures Meeting** — tolerance, unity in
    diversity, cultures meeting and linking. 8 lessons.

**The citizenship levels**:

13. **Rights and Law When Young** — the Moral guides' civics
    strand: dignity first, equality, children's rights, human
    rights, the law and obeying it, patriotism. *(Teacher-guide
    evidence only.)* 20 lessons.
14. **Character of a Citizen** — good citizenship, virtue ethics
    and the virtues one by one, ethical decisions; deepened by the
    Grade 9 ethics unit. 23 lessons.
15. **Belonging and Citizenship** — citizen and citizenship,
    becoming a citizen, Ethiopian citizenship law. 5 lessons.
16. **The State and Government** — the state and its elements,
    what government does, forms of government, state structures,
    government systems, Ethiopia's governments judged. 13 lessons.
17. **Democracy** — rule by the people, Athens, forms and
    principles and values of democracy, indigenous democracy;
    deepened by democratization, its builders, institutions and
    aspects. 16 lessons.
18. **Law and the Constitution** — the rule of law and its
    sources, constitutions and constitutionalism; deepened by
    Ethiopia's constitutions from Kibre Negest to 1995. 17 lessons.
19. **Human Rights** — rights we are born with, their features,
    instruments and institutions, rights in Ethiopia; deepened by
    the generations of rights and our obligations. 17 lessons.
20. **Taking Part, Conflict and Peace** — community engagement,
    volunteering, conflict and its resolution, peace building;
    deepened by indigenous conflict resolution. 17 lessons.
21. **Thinking Like a Citizen** — critical thinking and its
    skills, problem solving in steps, deciding well. 19 lessons.
22. **The Digital Citizen** — digital technology, culture and
    conduct online, its double edge, unethical use. 13 lessons.
23. **Home Knowledge and Its Institutions** — indigenous
    knowledge and responsible citizens, indigenous social
    institutions. 5 lessons.
24. **Living Together in Diversity** — culture, multiculturalism
    and its pillars, forms and elements of diversity, unity in
    diversity, managing diversity in Ethiopia. 8 lessons.
25. **Ethiopia and the World** — foreign relations, national
    interest, foreign policy and its instruments, Ethiopia and its
    region; deepened by globalization and global issues.
    14 lessons.
26. **Governing Well and Fighting Corruption** — governance and
    good governance, its elements and failures, corruption and
    fighting it. 7 lessons.
27. **Federalism and Patriotism** — federalism and its types,
    features and challenges in Ethiopia; patriotism, its types,
    bases, traits and duties. 12 lessons.

**The older-generation section** *(its own band, labelled on every
lesson: "Older generation — Civics and Ethical Education, Grade 11
(2002 E.C.) — teacher-guide evidence"; it keeps the older course's
own value-per-unit shape and is not merged into the levels above)*:

28. **The Older Course (2002 E.C.)** — eleven units: Building a
    Democratic System (6 lessons) · Rule of Law (4) · Equality
    (4) · Justice (5) · Patriotism (4) · Responsibility (6) ·
    Industriousness (4) · Self-Reliance (3) · Saving (4) · Active
    Community Participation (2) · The Pursuit of Wisdom (4).
    46 lessons.

**Totals: 382 lessons** (Moral opening 132 · Moral civics strand
20 · Citizenship Education 184 · older generation 46).
