# Hands-on — The Patriotic Citizen

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do it with a partner
Act out the lesson's opening scene together: one of you takes the unfair or wrong part, the other takes the lesson's right answer. Then swap. Say out loud what changed.

## Make it on paper
Make a poster card of this lesson's one thing: the patriotic citizen loves the country and is proud of its culture and history, raises children to know and love it, treats neighbours' children with love, plants and waters greenery, and respects others' beliefs and cultures — and loving the country is not the same as loving the government. Draw the lesson's picture in your own hand under the words.

## Take it home
Teach this lesson's one thing to someone at home tonight, in two sentences, with one example from your own street or school. Tomorrow, write down what they said back.
