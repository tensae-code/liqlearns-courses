# Video script — The Patriotic Citizen

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The patriotic citizen: proud of the country's culture and history.
Raising children to know it and love it.
Loving neighbours' children too. Planting and watering greenery.
Respecting others' beliefs and cultures.
And remember: loving the country is not the same as loving the government.
