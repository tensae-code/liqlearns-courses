# The Patriotic Citizen

## Do it first

Describe a patriotic citizen without using the word 'flag'. What do they do at home, with children, with trees, with neighbours? This lesson gives the portrait.

## The portrait — and one distinction kept clear

Patriotic citizens love their country and are proud of its culture and history. They raise their children to know and love it, and treat neighbours' children with love too. They plant and water greenery. They respect others' beliefs and cultures. And keep this distinction clear, as the teaching itself insists: loving the country is not the same thing as loving the government.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion four marks of the patriotic citizen, and the distinction about country and government.

## Do It Yourself

1. Write the portrait as a checklist; tick the marks you already practise.
2. Plant or water one green thing this week, as the portrait teaches.
3. Write the country/government distinction in your own words.
