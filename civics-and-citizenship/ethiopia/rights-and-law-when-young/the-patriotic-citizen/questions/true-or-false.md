# The Patriotic Citizen — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** Patriotic citizens respect others' beliefs and cultures.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Treating neighbours' children with love is part of the portrait.
**Answer: True**

**3.** Loving the country and loving the government are the same thing.
**Answer: False**

**4.** Patriotic citizens are proud of the country's culture and history.
**Answer: True**
