# The Patriotic Citizen — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Patriotic citizens are proud of the country's —
a) culture and history  b) wealth only  c) army only  d) cities only
**Answer: a) culture and history**

**2.** They raise their children to —
a) know and love the country  b) leave the country  c) ignore history  d) fear neighbours
**Answer: a) know and love the country**

**3.** Planting and watering greenery is —
a) part of the portrait  b) against patriotism  c) only farmers' work  d) a punishment
**Answer: a) part of the portrait**

**4.** Loving the country is —
a) not the same as loving the government  b) exactly loving the government  c) hating the government  d) unrelated to citizens
**Answer: a) not the same as loving the government**
