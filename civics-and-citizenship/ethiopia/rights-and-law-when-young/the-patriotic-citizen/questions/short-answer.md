# The Patriotic Citizen — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1.** Name four marks of the patriotic citizen.
**Answer:** Any four of: loves the country, proud of its culture and history, raises children to know and love it, treats neighbours' children with love, plants and waters greenery, respects others' beliefs and cultures.

**2.** State the distinction this lesson keeps.
**Answer:** Loving the country is not the same thing as loving the government.

**3.** How do patriotic citizens treat neighbours' children?
**Answer:** With love.

**4.** What do they do for greenery?
**Answer:** Plant it and water it.
