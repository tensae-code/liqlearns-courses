# Children's Rights at School — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which is a right at school?
a) To ask questions and receive answers  b) To break school property  c) To exclude a classmate  d) To skip all homework
**Answer: a) To ask questions and receive answers**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Pupils have the right to get — from teachers
a) correct information  b) wrong information  c) no information  d) secret information
**Answer: a) correct information**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Which is a responsibility at school?
a) Protect school property  b) Damage school property  c) Ignore the rules  d) Refuse every task
**Answer: a) Protect school property**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Protection from discrimination at school is —
a) a right  b) a favour  c) not allowed  d) only for teachers
**Answer: a) a right**
