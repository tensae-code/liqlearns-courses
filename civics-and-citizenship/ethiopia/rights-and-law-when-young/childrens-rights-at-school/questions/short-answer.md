# Children's Rights at School — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name three rights at school.
**Answer:** Any three of: learn, get correct information, ask questions, receive answers, take part in clubs, participate in matters that concern you, use school property properly, protection from discrimination.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name three responsibilities at school.
**Answer:** Any three of: respect the school's rules, be an example, do homework and extra tasks on time, respect teachers, protect school property.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** What does 'not to be excluded' protect?
**Answer:** Protection from discrimination at school.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Why do rights and responsibilities travel together?
**Answer:** A right used well — with its responsibility carried — stays strong for everyone.
