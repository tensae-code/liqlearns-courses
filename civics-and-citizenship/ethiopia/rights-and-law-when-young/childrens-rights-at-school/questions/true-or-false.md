# Children's Rights at School — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Children may participate in matters that concern them at school.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Having rights means having no responsibilities.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Doing homework on time is one of the responsibilities listed.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A pupil may be excluded from clubs for no fair reason.
**Answer: False**
