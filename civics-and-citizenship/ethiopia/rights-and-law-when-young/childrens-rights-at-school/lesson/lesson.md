# Children's Rights at School

## Do it first

Raise your hand in your mind and ask a question. Should anyone be allowed to stop you just for asking? This lesson lists the rights you hold at school — including the right to learn and to get correct information from your teachers — and the responsibilities that travel with them.

## Sub lesson 1 — My rights at school

At school you have the right to learn and to get correct information from your teachers. You have the right to ask questions, and the right to receive answers. You have the right to take part in clubs, and to participate in matters that concern you. You have the right to handle and use school property properly, and the right to protection from discrimination — not to be excluded.

## Sub lesson 2 — My responsibilities with them

Rights travel with responsibilities: respect the school's rules; be an example to other pupils; do your homework and extra tasks on time; respect your teachers; and protect school property from damage and theft. A right used well is a right kept strong.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion three rights you hold at school and three responsibilities that go with them.

## Do It Yourself

1. Write your school rights as a list; pin it where you study.
2. Match each right with the responsibility that protects it.
3. This week, use one right well: ask a real question, or join a club.
