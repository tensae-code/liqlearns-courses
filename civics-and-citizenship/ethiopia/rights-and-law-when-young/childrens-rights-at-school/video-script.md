# Video script — Children's Rights at School

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

At school, you hold rights.
Ask questions. Receive answers. Join clubs. Be included.
And carry your responsibilities: respect the rules, do your homework, protect school property.
Rights and responsibilities travel together.
