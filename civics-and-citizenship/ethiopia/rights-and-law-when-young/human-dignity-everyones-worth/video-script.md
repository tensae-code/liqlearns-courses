# Video script — Human Dignity — Everyone's Worth

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Every person carries worth. That worth is dignity.
Rights protect it. That is why rights may not be taken away.
Elder, child, worker — the same respect.
Treat every person as someone of worth.
