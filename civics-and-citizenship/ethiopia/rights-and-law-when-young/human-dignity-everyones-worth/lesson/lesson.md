# Human Dignity — Everyone's Worth

## Do it first

Greet three people in your mind: an elder, a small child, a worker. Do they deserve the same respectful greeting, or different worth? This lesson answers.

## Sub lesson 1 — Dignity and rights

Dignity is the worth every person carries. Rights exist to protect that worth — dignity is why rights may not be taken away. Take away a person's dignity in your treatment of them, and you attack the ground their rights stand on.

[Picture 28409 — same respect for every person]

## Sub lesson 2 — Dignity in everyday treatment

So treat every person as someone of worth: an elder, a child and a worker greeted with the same respect. Worth is not measured by age, wealth or work. Every person is someone.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what dignity is, why it protects rights, and one everyday act that honours it.

## Do It Yourself

1. Write one sentence: what dignity is.
2. List three people of different ages and work; write the same respectful greeting for each.
3. Notice one moment today when someone was treated as worth less — write what respectful treatment would have looked like.
