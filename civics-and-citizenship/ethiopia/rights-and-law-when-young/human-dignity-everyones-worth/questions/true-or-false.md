# Human Dignity — Everyone's Worth — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Rights exist to protect human dignity.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A worker has less worth than an elder.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Everyday respectful treatment is part of dignity.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Dignity can be taken away by unkind treatment of a person's rights.
**Answer: False**
