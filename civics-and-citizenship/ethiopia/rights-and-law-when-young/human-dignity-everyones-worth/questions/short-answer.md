# Human Dignity — Everyone's Worth — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is human dignity?
**Answer:** The worth every person carries simply as a person.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why may rights not be taken away?
**Answer:** Because they protect dignity — the worth every person carries.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Give one everyday act that honours dignity.
**Answer:** Greeting and treating every person — elder, child, worker — with the same respect (any sound example).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two things worth is NOT measured by.
**Answer:** Any two of age, wealth, work, rank.
