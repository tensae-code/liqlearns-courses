# Human Dignity — Everyone's Worth — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Dignity is —
a) the worth every person carries  b) a person's wealth  c) a person's age  d) a prize
**Answer: a) the worth every person carries**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Dignity is why rights —
a) may not be taken away  b) are sold  c) expire  d) belong to few
**Answer: a) may not be taken away**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Who deserves respectful treatment?
a) Every person  b) Only elders  c) Only workers  d) Only children
**Answer: a) Every person**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Worth is measured by —
a) being a person  b) wealth  c) work  d) age
**Answer: a) being a person**
