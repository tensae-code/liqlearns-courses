# Hands-on — The Law-Abiding Citizen and What Lawlessness Brings

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do it with a partner
Act out the lesson's opening scene together: one of you takes the unfair or wrong part, the other takes the lesson's right answer. Then swap. Say out loud what changed.

## Make it on paper
Make a poster card of this lesson's one thing: the law-abiding citizen respects law and order, reports what threatens peaceful life, and avoids violating others' rights — while lawlessness brings loss of peace, rights violations, corruption, addiction, child trafficking and tax evasion. Draw the lesson's picture in your own hand under the words.

## Take it home
Teach this lesson's one thing to someone at home tonight, in two sentences, with one example from your own street or school. Tomorrow, write down what they said back.
