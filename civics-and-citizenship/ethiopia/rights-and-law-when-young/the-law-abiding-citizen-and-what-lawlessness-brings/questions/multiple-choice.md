# The Law-Abiding Citizen and What Lawlessness Brings — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Laws are established by —
a) people's will  b) accident  c) one person's whim  d) foreigners only
**Answer: a) people's will**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The law-abiding person reports —
a) acts that threaten the community's peaceful life  b) every private conversation  c) nothing ever  d) only friends' faults
**Answer: a) acts that threaten the community's peaceful life**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Which is a harm of lawlessness?
a) Child trafficking  b) Peace  c) Trust  d) Honesty
**Answer: a) Child trafficking**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Theft and lying are acts the law-abiding person —
a) avoids  b) praises  c) teaches  d) hides
**Answer: a) avoids**
