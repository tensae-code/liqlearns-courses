# The Law-Abiding Citizen and What Lawlessness Brings — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name three marks of the law-abiding person.
**Answer:** Any three of: respects law and order at all times, reports acts threatening peaceful life, avoids violating others' rights, avoids theft and lying.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name three harms of lawlessness.
**Answer:** Any three of: absence of peace, human-rights violations, corruption, addiction, child trafficking, tax evasion.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** What are laws, in this lesson's teaching?
**Answer:** Established by people's will, and instruments of social change.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Why is lawlessness not freedom?
**Answer:** Because it destroys the peace and rights that law protects.
