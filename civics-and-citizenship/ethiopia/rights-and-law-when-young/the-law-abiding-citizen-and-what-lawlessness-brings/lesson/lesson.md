# The Law-Abiding Citizen and What Lawlessness Brings

## Do it first

Two streets: on one, a citizen reports a wrong to an official. On the other, disorder grows and no one acts. Which street would you live on? This lesson paints both.

## Sub lesson 1 — The law-abiding person

The law-abiding person understands that laws are established by people's will and are instruments of social change. They respect law and order at all times, report acts that threaten the community's peaceful life, and avoid violating others' rights — and avoid acts like theft and lying that disturb peaceful coexistence.

## Sub lesson 2 — What lawlessness brings

Where law is not kept, heavy harms follow: absence of peace, human-rights violations, corruption, addiction, child trafficking and tax evasion. Lawlessness is not freedom; it is the loss of the peace law protects.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion three marks of the law-abiding person and three harms of lawlessness.

## Do It Yourself

1. Write the law-abiding portrait in your own words.
2. List the harms of lawlessness; mark the one you find heaviest and say why.
3. Write one act you can do that protects your community's peaceful life.
