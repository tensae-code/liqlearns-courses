# Video script — The Law-Abiding Citizen and What Lawlessness Brings

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The law-abiding citizen respects law and order — always.
They report what threatens peaceful life.
They never violate another's rights.
Lawlessness brings the opposite: no peace, corruption, harm.
Choose the first street.
