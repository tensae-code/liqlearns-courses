# Four Equalities — Gender, Language, Religion, Culture — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** People may learn and write creatively in their mother tongue.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** One Ethiopian language is worth more than the others.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Christianity, Islam and indigenous faiths such as Waaqeffanna live with tolerance and mutual respect.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Gender equality applies only at school, not in farm work or trade.
**Answer: False**
