# Four Equalities — Gender, Language, Religion, Culture — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In past times, women lacked —
a) ability  b) the chance to participate  c) intelligence  d) strength
**Answer: b) the chance to participate**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which languages are named as equal in Ethiopia?
a) Only Amharic  b) Afar, Amharic, Somali, Oromo and Tigrinya, among others  c) Only English  d) None
**Answer: b) Afar, Amharic, Somali, Oromo and Tigrinya, among others**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Religion equality gives every citizen the right to —
a) follow the faith he or she chooses  b) force others to change faith  c) ban other faiths  d) hide their faith
**Answer: a) follow the faith he or she chooses**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Culture equality says —
a) one culture is best  b) no culture is greater or lesser than another  c) cultures must never change  d) only old cultures count
**Answer: b) no culture is greater or lesser than another**
