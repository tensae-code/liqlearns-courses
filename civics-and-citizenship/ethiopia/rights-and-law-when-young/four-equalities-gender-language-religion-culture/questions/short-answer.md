# Four Equalities — Gender, Language, Religion, Culture — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the four equalities.
**Answer:** Gender, language, religion and culture.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give two fields where gender equality applies.
**Answer:** Any two of housework, education, farm work, family responsibility, trade.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What right does language equality protect?
**Answer:** Using your language for learning, creative writing and literature, and expressing yourself easily in your mother tongue.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Can a culture change?
**Answer:** Yes — culture can change with social and economic development, and still deserves equal respect.
