# Lesson plan — Four Equalities — Gender, Language, Religion, Culture

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Rights and Law When Young · **Lesson:** Four Equalities — Gender, Language, Religion, Culture
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 13 — Rights and Law When Young (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** equality has four faces at school and at home: gender, language, religion and culture — none greater, none lesser.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the learner's own life at home and at school, and on the earlier lessons of this title where they are named below. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.
