# Dignity First — Where Human Rights Come From — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Where do human rights come from?
a) Human dignity  b) Wealth  c) Exams  d) Rank
**Answer: a) Human dignity**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Inherited dignity belongs to —
a) only adults  b) every human being  c) only citizens with papers  d) only the strong
**Answer: b) every human being**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Which act respects dignity?
a) Helping a friend with food and water  b) Laughing at a friend in need  c) Hiding a friend's need  d) Taking a friend's food
**Answer: a) Helping a friend with food and water**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which practice injures bodies and rights and must stop?
a) Idir  b) Equb  c) Under-age marriage  d) Hospitality
**Answer: c) Under-age marriage**
