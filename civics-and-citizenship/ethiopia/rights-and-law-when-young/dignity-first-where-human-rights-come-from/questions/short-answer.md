# Dignity First — Where Human Rights Come From — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is inherited dignity?
**Answer:** The dignity every human being has simply by being human, needing no qualification beyond humanity.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give two reasons for respecting dignity.
**Answer:** Any two of: it respects human rights; it moves us to give humanitarian aid; it is the ground on which human laws are framed.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two helping acts that respect dignity.
**Answer:** Helping with money, clothing, food or water (any two).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two harmful practices named in this lesson.
**Answer:** Any two of: female circumcision, under-age marriage, cutting the face in mourning, cutting the body with blades, wastefulness, abduction.
