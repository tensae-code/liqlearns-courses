# Dignity First — Where Human Rights Come From — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Every person is born free and equal in dignity and in rights.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A person must earn inherited dignity by passing an exam.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Respecting another's dignity is a way your own dignity is respected in return.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Human rights can be understood apart from human dignity.
**Answer: False**
