# Video script — Dignity First — Where Human Rights Come From

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Every person has dignity.
You are born with it. Nobody gives it. Nobody can take it away.
Because every person has dignity, every person has rights.
Rights stand on dignity, the way a house stands on its foundation.
So respect dignity. Help a friend who needs food, water, clothing or money.
And stop every custom that cuts or harms a person's body.
Dignity first. Rights follow.
