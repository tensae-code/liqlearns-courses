# Keeping Peace and Law — Police, Courts and Defence

## Do it first

Imagine a town with no police, no courts and no one to defend it. What happens to peace in that town? What happens to people's rights? This lesson follows that question to its answer.

## Why peace and law need keepers

Without police, courts and a defence force there could be no peace, no rule of law and no respect for human rights. Instead, disorder, social turmoil and destruction would spread. Peace is not kept by wishes; it is kept by people and institutions doing their work, and by citizens who take responsibility, participate, cooperate and support one another.

## A world scope

The common good reaches beyond one town. The peoples of the world must cooperate for world peace, a clean environment, equality and justice. Keeping peace and law at home is one part of that wider work.
