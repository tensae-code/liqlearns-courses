# Hands-on — Keeping Peace and Law — Police, Courts and Defence

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do it with a partner
Act out the lesson's opening scene together: one of you takes the unfair or wrong part, the other takes the lesson's right answer. Then swap. Say out loud what changed.

## Make it on paper
Make a poster card of this lesson's one thing: without police, courts and a defence force there could be no peace, no rule of law and no respect for human rights — and the peoples of the world must cooperate for peace, equality and justice. Draw the lesson's picture in your own hand under the words.

## Take it home
Teach this lesson's one thing to someone at home tonight, in two sentences, with one example from your own street or school. Tomorrow, write down what they said back.
