# Keeping Peace and Law — Police, Courts and Defence — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** Police, courts and a defence force protect respect for human rights.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Peace is kept by wishes alone.
**Answer: False**

**3.** Striving for the common good is called a patriotic virtue of citizens in this teaching.
**Answer: True**

**4.** Keeping peace is only one town's work, with no world scope.
**Answer: False**
