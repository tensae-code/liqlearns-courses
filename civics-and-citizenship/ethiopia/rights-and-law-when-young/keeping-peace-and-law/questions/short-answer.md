# Keeping Peace and Law — Police, Courts and Defence — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1.** Name the three keepers of peace and law.
**Answer:** Police, courts and a defence force.

**2.** What three harms spread without them?
**Answer:** Disorder, social turmoil and destruction.

**3.** Name two citizen acts that keep peace.
**Answer:** Any two of participation, cooperation, mutual support, taking responsibility.

**4.** What must the world's peoples cooperate for?
**Answer:** World peace, a clean environment, equality and justice.
