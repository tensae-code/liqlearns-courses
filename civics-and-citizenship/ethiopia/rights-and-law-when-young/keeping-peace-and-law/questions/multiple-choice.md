# Keeping Peace and Law — Police, Courts and Defence — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Without police, courts and a defence force, there could be no —
a) peace, rule of law and respect for human rights  b) disorder  c) turmoil  d) destruction
**Answer: a) peace, rule of law and respect for human rights**

**2.** What spreads without keepers of law?
a) Disorder, social turmoil and destruction  b) Trust  c) Honour  d) Friendship
**Answer: a) Disorder, social turmoil and destruction**

**3.** Citizens keep peace by —
a) participation, cooperation and mutual support  b) silence only  c) avoiding everyone  d) breaking rules
**Answer: a) participation, cooperation and mutual support**

**4.** The peoples of the world must cooperate for —
a) world peace, a clean environment, equality and justice  b) nothing  c) competition only  d) separation
**Answer: a) world peace, a clean environment, equality and justice**
