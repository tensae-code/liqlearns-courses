# Rights of Persons With Disabilities

## Do it first

Picture a group game. Where does a child in a wheelchair belong — at the edge watching, or in the middle playing? This lesson answers.

## No rights removed; respect and inclusion owed

A person with a disability holds every right everyone else holds. Disability removes no rights. The class's duty is respect and inclusion: a child in a wheelchair plays in the middle of the group game, not at its edge — welcomed, included, and treated as a full member.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion: what rights does a person with a disability hold, and what does inclusion look like in a game?

## Do It Yourself

1. Write the lesson's one thing in your own words.
2. List two ways to include a classmate with a disability in a game or task.
3. Check your classroom in your mind: what would make joining in easier for everyone? Write one idea.
