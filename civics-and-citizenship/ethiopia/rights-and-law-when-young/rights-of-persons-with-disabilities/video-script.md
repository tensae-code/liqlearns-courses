# Video script — Rights of Persons With Disabilities

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Disability removes no rights.
Every right stands. Every person belongs.
Not at the edge — in the middle of the game.
Respect. Inclusion. That is the duty.
