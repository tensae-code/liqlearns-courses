# Rights of Persons With Disabilities — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Disability removes —
a) no rights  b) some rights  c) all rights  d) school rights only
**Answer: a) no rights**

**2.** The class's duty is —
a) respect and inclusion  b) pity only  c) exclusion  d) silence
**Answer: a) respect and inclusion**

**3.** In the picture, the child in a wheelchair is —
a) in the middle of the game  b) at the edge watching  c) outside the room  d) alone
**Answer: a) in the middle of the game**

**4.** A person with a disability holds —
a) every right everyone else holds  b) half the rights  c) rights only at home  d) no new rights
**Answer: a) every right everyone else holds**
