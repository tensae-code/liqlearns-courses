# Rights of Persons With Disabilities — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1.** What does disability do to a person's rights?
**Answer:** Nothing — disability removes no rights.

**2.** What two duties does the class owe?
**Answer:** Respect and inclusion.

**3.** Where does the child in this lesson's picture play?
**Answer:** In the middle of the group game, not at its edge.

**4.** Give one act of inclusion.
**Answer:** Inviting and making room for a classmate with a disability to play a real part in a game or task (any sound example).
