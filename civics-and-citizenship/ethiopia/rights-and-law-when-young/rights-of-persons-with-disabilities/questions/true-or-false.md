# Rights of Persons With Disabilities — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** Persons with disabilities hold the same rights as everyone else.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Inclusion means watching from the edge.
**Answer: False**

**3.** Respect is part of the duty owed.
**Answer: True**

**4.** A disability makes a person less a member of the group.
**Answer: False**
