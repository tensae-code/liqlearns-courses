# What Patriotism Gives — To Citizen and Country

## Do it first

A road and a school built by one generation are handed to children. Who built them — and why? This lesson lists what patriotism gives, to citizens and to the country.

## What loving the country gives

Patriotism gives on both sides. Citizens grow their capacity — they become more able people. The next generation inherits a better country than the one handed to their parents. And the country gains defence against outside attack, because its people stand for it.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the three gifts of patriotism: one for citizens, one for the next generation, one for the country.

## Do It Yourself

1. Write the three gifts from memory.
2. Name one capacity you want to grow for your country's sake.
3. Write one thing your generation could hand on better than you received it.
