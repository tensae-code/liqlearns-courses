# What Patriotism Gives — To Citizen and Country — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Patriotism helps citizens —
a) grow their capacity  b) avoid all work  c) stop learning  d) leave the country
**Answer: a) grow their capacity**

**2.** The next generation inherits —
a) a better country  b) nothing  c) only debts of honour  d) a worse country
**Answer: a) a better country**

**3.** The country gains — against outside attack
a) defence  b) weakness  c) silence  d) division
**Answer: a) defence**

**4.** The picture hands children —
a) a road and a school  b) a flag only  c) a statue  d) a rule-book
**Answer: a) a road and a school**
