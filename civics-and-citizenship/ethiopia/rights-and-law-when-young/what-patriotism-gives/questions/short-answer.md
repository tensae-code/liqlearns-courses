# What Patriotism Gives — To Citizen and Country — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1.** Name the three gifts of patriotism.
**Answer:** Citizens grow their capacity; the next generation inherits a better country; the country gains defence against outside attack.

**2.** Who builds the road and school in the picture?
**Answer:** One generation, handing them to the children of the next.

**3.** Give one example of growing capacity.
**Answer:** Learning a skill, trade or knowledge that makes you more able to serve (any sound example).

**4.** Why does a loved country gain defence?
**Answer:** Because its people stand for it.
