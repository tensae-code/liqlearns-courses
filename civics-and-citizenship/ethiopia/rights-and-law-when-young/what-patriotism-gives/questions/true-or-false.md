# What Patriotism Gives — To Citizen and Country — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** Patriotism's gifts reach both citizen and country.
**Answer: True**

**2.** A better country for the next generation is one of patriotism's gifts.
**Answer: True**

**3.** Loving the country makes citizens less able.
**Answer: False**

**4.** Defence against outside attack is named in this lesson.
**Answer: True**
