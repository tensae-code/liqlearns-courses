# Women's Rights — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1.** What rights do women and girls hold?
**Answer:** The same rights as everyone else.

**2.** Duty or favour — which is respecting women's rights?
**Answer:** A duty.

**3.** Give two scenes from this lesson where women's rights appear.
**Answer:** A girl raising her hand in class; a woman speaking at a community meeting.

**4.** Why is this taught as its own lesson?
**Answer:** Because respecting the rights of women and girls is a named, separate teaching and duty.
