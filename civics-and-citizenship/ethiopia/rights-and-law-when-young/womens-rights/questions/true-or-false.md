# Women's Rights — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** Women hold the same rights as men.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Respecting women's rights is a favour people may choose to give.
**Answer: False**

**3.** Girls hold rights at school.
**Answer: True**

**4.** A woman's rights shrink when she speaks in public.
**Answer: False**
