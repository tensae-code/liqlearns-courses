# Women's Rights — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Women and girls hold —
a) the same rights  b) fewer rights  c) rights only at home  d) no rights at meetings
**Answer: a) the same rights**

**2.** Respecting women's rights is —
a) a duty  b) a favour  c) optional  d) only for women to do
**Answer: a) a duty**

**3.** A girl raising her hand in class is —
a) using her rights  b) breaking a rule  c) asking a favour  d) disturbing class
**Answer: a) using her rights**

**4.** A woman speaking at a community meeting stands on —
a) the same rights as everyone else  b) borrowed rights  c) no rights  d) smaller rights
**Answer: a) the same rights as everyone else**
