# Women's Rights

## Do it first

A girl raises her hand in class. A woman speaks at a community meeting. Are their rights smaller than a man's or a boy's? This lesson answers plainly: no.

## The same rights, respected as a duty

Women and girls hold the same rights as men and boys. Respecting the rights of women and girls is a named, separate duty — not a favour done when it is convenient. A girl in class and a woman speaking to her community stand on the same rights as everyone else.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion: what rights do women and girls hold, and is respecting them a duty or a favour?

## Do It Yourself

1. Write the lesson's one thing in your own words.
2. Give two places where women's rights must be respected: in class, at a meeting — add one from home.
3. Write what you would say if a girl's right to speak was brushed aside.
