# Why Obey the Law — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Obeying the law helps citizens —
a) enjoy freedom fully  b) lose freedom  c) avoid all rules  d) ignore others
**Answer: a) enjoy freedom fully**

**2.** Your freedom has limits where —
a) others' rights begin  b) you feel tired  c) school ends  d) no one watches
**Answer: a) others' rights begin**

**3.** Cooperating with authorities includes —
a) reporting suspicious movements to the responsible bodies  b) hiding wrongdoing  c) breaking road rules  d) refusing tax
**Answer: a) reporting suspicious movements to the responsible bodies**

**4.** Fair distribution of wealth is —
a) a purpose of obedience  b) against the law  c) impossible  d) unrelated to law
**Answer: a) a purpose of obedience**
