# Why Obey the Law — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1.** Name three purposes of obedience.
**Answer:** Any three of: enjoying freedom fully, keeping peace, fair distribution of wealth, securing sovereignty, protecting citizens' private interests.

**2.** Where does your freedom end?
**Answer:** Where others' rights begin.

**3.** Name two parts of the citizen's part.
**Answer:** Any two of: know and obey the laws, pay tax, respect rules including road safety, cooperate with the authorities/report suspicious movements.

**4.** Why does the junction in the picture run smoothly?
**Answer:** Because everyone keeps the rules — obedience protects everyone's freedom to travel.
