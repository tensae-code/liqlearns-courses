# Why Obey the Law — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** Keeping peace is a purpose of obeying the law.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Paying tax and respecting road-safety rules are part of the citizen's part.
**Answer: True**

**3.** Freedom means doing anything, even where others' rights begin.
**Answer: False**

**4.** Securing sovereignty is named among obedience's purposes.
**Answer: True**
