# Why Obey the Law

## Do it first

A road junction: every driver keeps the rules, and traffic flows. One driver ignores them — what happens to everyone's freedom to travel? This lesson lists what obedience protects.

## What obedience protects — and the citizen's part

Obeying the law has named purposes: enjoying freedom fully, keeping peace, fair distribution of wealth, securing sovereignty, and protecting citizens' private interests. Your freedom has limits where others' rights begin. The citizen's part is plain: know and obey the laws, pay tax and respect the rules — including road safety — and cooperate with the authorities, including reporting suspicious movements to the responsible government bodies.
