# Loving Your Country — Pride, Peace and Progress — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Loving your country means wanting its —
a) peace and progress  b) wealth only  c) fame only  d) silence
**Answer: a) peace and progress**

**2.** Love of country binds —
a) people together  b) nothing  c) only families  d) only officials
**Answer: a) people together**

**3.** It builds a —
a) strong country  b) wall between peoples  c) market only  d) school only
**Answer: a) strong country**

**4.** Pride in this lesson is pride in the country's —
a) history and culture  b) army only  c) weather  d) size only
**Answer: a) history and culture**
