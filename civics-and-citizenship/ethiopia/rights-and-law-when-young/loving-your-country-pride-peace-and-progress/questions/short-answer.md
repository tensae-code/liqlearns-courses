# Loving Your Country — Pride, Peace and Progress — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1.** What does loving your country mean?
**Answer:** Being proud of its history and culture and wanting its peace and progress.

**2.** Name the two things love of country does.
**Answer:** It binds people together and builds a strong country.

**3.** What does the picture show?
**Answer:** Many hands of different ages holding one flag rope together.

**4.** Give one act serving peace and one serving progress.
**Answer:** Learner's own sound examples, one for peace and one for progress.
