# Loving Your Country — Pride, Peace and Progress

## Do it first

Many hands of different ages hold one flag rope together. Why together? This closing lesson gathers the whole patriotism teaching into pride, peace and progress.

## Pride, peace and progress — and what they build

Loving your country means being proud of its history and culture, and wanting its peace and progress. Its use is great: it binds people together, and it builds a strong country. Many hands of different ages holding one rope — that is the picture of it.

[Picture 28419 — many hands, one flag rope]

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what loving your country means, and the two things it does.

## Do It Yourself

1. Write the closing definition from memory.
2. Name one thing you are proud of in your country's history or culture.
3. Write one act that serves your country's peace, and one that serves its progress.
