# Hands-on — Loving Your Country — Pride, Peace and Progress

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do it with a partner
Act out the lesson's opening scene together: one of you takes the unfair or wrong part, the other takes the lesson's right answer. Then swap. Say out loud what changed.

## Make it on paper
Make a poster card of this lesson's one thing: loving your country means being proud of its history and culture and wanting its peace and progress — and its use is that it binds people together and builds a strong country. Draw the lesson's picture in your own hand under the words.

## Take it home
Teach this lesson's one thing to someone at home tonight, in two sentences, with one example from your own street or school. Tomorrow, write down what they said back.
