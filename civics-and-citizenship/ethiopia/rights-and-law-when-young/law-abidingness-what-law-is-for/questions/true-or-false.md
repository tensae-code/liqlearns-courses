# Law-Abidingness — What Law Is For — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Without tax, common services like education, health and defence cannot be had.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Law exists only to punish.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A law-abiding citizen settles problems peacefully.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** School rules and the country's laws are unrelated habits.
**Answer: False**
