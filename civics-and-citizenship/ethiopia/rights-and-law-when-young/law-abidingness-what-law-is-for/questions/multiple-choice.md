# Law-Abidingness — What Law Is For — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Law-abidingness is —
a) readiness and commitment to obey your country's laws  b) fear of police only  c) knowing one rule  d) obeying only when watched
**Answer: a) readiness and commitment to obey your country's laws**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is a purpose of law?
a) To secure citizens' equality  b) To enrich officials  c) To confuse citizens  d) To replace all customs
**Answer: a) To secure citizens' equality**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Tax is —
a) a legal duty paid from income to government  b) an optional gift  c) a fine for wrongdoing  d) only for traders
**Answer: a) a legal duty paid from income to government**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** At school, the law-abiding shape includes —
a) knowing the school's rules and respecting peers  b) ignoring tasks  c) breaking rules quietly  d) settling problems by fighting
**Answer: a) knowing the school's rules and respecting peers**
