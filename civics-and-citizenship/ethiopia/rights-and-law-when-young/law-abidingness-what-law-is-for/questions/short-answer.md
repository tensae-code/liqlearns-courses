# Law-Abidingness — What Law Is For — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name law's three purposes.
**Answer:** To make citizens know their rights and duties; to direct behaviour; to secure citizens' equality.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name three duties of a law-abiding citizen.
**Answer:** Be ready to obey; know the country's laws; settle problems peacefully (at school: know the rules, complete tasks, respect peers).

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** What is tax?
**Answer:** The payment individuals and organisations make to government from their income.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Why must tax be paid?
**Answer:** It is a legal duty, and without it the common services — education, health, defence — cannot be had.
