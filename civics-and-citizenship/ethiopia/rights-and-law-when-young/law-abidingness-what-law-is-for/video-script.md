# Video script — Law-Abidingness — What Law Is For

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Why does a country have laws?
So citizens know their rights and duties.
So behaviour is directed. So equality is secured.
Be ready to obey. Know the laws. Settle problems peacefully.
At school the same: know the rules, do your tasks, respect your peers.
