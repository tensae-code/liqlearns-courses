# Human Rights — What They Are — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Human rights are owed to a person because —
a) they are human  b) they are rich  c) they won a prize  d) they are adults
**Answer: a) they are human**

**2.** Who holds human rights?
a) Everyone  b) Only officials  c) Only adults  d) Only some peoples
**Answer: a) Everyone**

**3.** Can you buy human rights?
a) No  b) Yes, cheaply  c) Yes, at school  d) Only sometimes
**Answer: a) No**

**4.** A right you are born with is most like —
a) something owed to you as a person  b) a prize for winning  c) a wage for work  d) a gift you must return
**Answer: a) something owed to you as a person**
