# Human Rights — What They Are — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** Every human being holds rights simply by being human.
**Answer: True**

**2.** You must pass an exam to hold human rights.
**Answer: False**

**3.** Children hold rights too.
**Answer: True**

**4.** Rights belong only to people of one kind.
**Answer: False**
