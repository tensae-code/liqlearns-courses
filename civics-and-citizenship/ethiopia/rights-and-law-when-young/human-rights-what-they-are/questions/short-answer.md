# Human Rights — What They Are — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1.** What are human rights?
**Answer:** What every human being is owed simply by being human.

**2.** Who holds them?
**Answer:** Every human being.

**3.** Name two things rights are NOT.
**Answer:** Any two of: bought, earned by exam, won as a prize, given as a favour.

**4.** Why does each child in the picture hold the same sign?
**Answer:** Because rights belong equally to everyone.
