# Human Rights — What They Are

## Do it first

Hold an empty sign in your mind. Its word is 'rights'. Who may hold that sign — only some people, or every person? This lesson answers.

## Rights belong to everyone

Human rights are what every human being is owed simply by being human. You do not buy them, earn them or pass an exam for them. Children of many kinds stand in one line, and each one holds the same sign: rights.

[Picture 28407 — every child holding rights]

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion, in one sentence, what human rights are and who holds them.

## Do It Yourself

1. Write the definition of human rights in your own words.
2. Give three examples of people who hold rights — and notice that the list is everyone.
3. Say why a right you are born with is different from a prize you win.
