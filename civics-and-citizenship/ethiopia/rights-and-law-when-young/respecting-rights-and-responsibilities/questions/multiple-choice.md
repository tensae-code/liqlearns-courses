# Respecting Rights and the Responsibilities That Come With Them — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Every right carries a matching —
a) responsibility  b) price  c) punishment  d) secret
**Answer: a) responsibility**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Respecting rights means honouring —
a) everyone else's rights  b) only your own  c) only friends' rights  d) no one's
**Answer: a) everyone else's rights**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** When helping, you may NOT —
a) repeat what a person told you in trust  b) ask their agreement  c) respect their rights  d) listen to them
**Answer: a) repeat what a person told you in trust**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Acting for a person without their agreement breaks —
a) consent  b) the timetable  c) a promise to yourself  d) nothing
**Answer: a) consent**
