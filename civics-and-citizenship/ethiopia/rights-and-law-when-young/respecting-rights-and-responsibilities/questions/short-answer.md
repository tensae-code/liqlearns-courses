# Respecting Rights and the Responsibilities That Come With Them — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does respecting rights matter?
**Answer:** Rights belong to everyone; honouring others' rights keeps everyone's rights — including your own — safe.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What three limits govern helping others?
**Answer:** Basic rights, confidentiality and consent.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** What keeps the balance level?
**Answer:** Responsibilities matching rights.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Give one example of a right and its matching responsibility.
**Answer:** Example: the right to be heard matches the responsibility to listen to others (any sound pairing).
