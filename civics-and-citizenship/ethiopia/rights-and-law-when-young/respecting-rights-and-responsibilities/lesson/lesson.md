# Respecting Rights and the Responsibilities That Come With Them

## Do it first

Hold a balance in your mind: 'my rights' on one side. What must sit on the other side to keep it level? This lesson answers — responsibilities.

## Sub lesson 1 — Why respecting rights matters

Respecting other people's rights matters because rights belong to everyone, not only to you. When each person honours the rights of others, everyone's rights stand safe — including your own.

## Sub lesson 2 — Rights go with responsibilities — and help has limits

Every right carries a matching responsibility. Claiming your own rights means honouring everyone else's. The same rule governs helping: when you serve people, basic rights, confidentiality and consent limit how you may help. You may not help in ways that trample a person's rights, you may not repeat what they told you in trust, and you may not act for them without their agreement.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion why respecting rights matters, and the three limits on helping: rights, confidentiality, consent.
