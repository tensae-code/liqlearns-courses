# How Patriotism Shows — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1.** Name three ways patriotism shows.
**Answer:** Any three of: pride in the country, feeling the country is one's own, readiness to sacrifice, fighting corruption, honouring heroes.

**2.** Name three acts of patriotism.
**Answer:** Any three of: respecting the flag, guarding sovereignty, putting the country's interest before private interest, pride in every national achievement.

**3.** What do schoolchildren in the picture do?
**Answer:** Garland a hero's statue — honouring the country's heroes.

**4.** Why must love of country show?
**Answer:** Because patriotism is proved in feelings carried into acts, not in words alone.
