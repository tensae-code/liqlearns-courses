# How Patriotism Shows — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which shows patriotism?
a) Fighting corruption  b) Cheating the country  c) Hiding national achievements  d) Disrespecting the flag
**Answer: a) Fighting corruption**

**2.** Respecting the flag is —
a) an act of patriotism  b) against patriotism  c) unrelated  d) only for soldiers
**Answer: a) an act of patriotism**

**3.** Putting the country's interest first means before —
a) private interest  b) all duties  c) family meals  d) school
**Answer: a) private interest**

**4.** Honouring the country's heroes is —
a) a way patriotism shows  b) forgetting history  c) only for elders  d) a punishment
**Answer: a) a way patriotism shows**
