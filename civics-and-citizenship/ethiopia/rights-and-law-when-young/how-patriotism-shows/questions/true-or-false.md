# How Patriotism Shows — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** Readiness to sacrifice for the country shows patriotism.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Pride in every national achievement is named in this lesson.
**Answer: True**

**3.** Patriotism shows only in big ceremonies, never in ordinary acts.
**Answer: False**

**4.** Guarding sovereignty is an act of patriotism.
**Answer: True**
