# How Patriotism Shows

## Do it first

Love that never shows is hard to believe. List, before reading, two ways love of country could show in an ordinary week. Then compare with this lesson's list.

## How love of country shows — feelings and acts

Patriotism shows in pride in the country, in a feeling that the country is one's own, in readiness to sacrifice for it, in fighting corruption, and in honouring the country's heroes. And it shows in acts: respecting the flag, guarding sovereignty, putting the country's interest before private interest, and pride in every national achievement.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion three ways patriotism shows and three acts it takes.

## Do It Yourself

1. Write five ways patriotism shows from memory.
2. Give one ordinary-week act for two of them.
3. Write what 'putting the country's interest before private interest' could mean at school.
