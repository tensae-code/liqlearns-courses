# Why Equal Treatment Matters — Less Favouritism, More Trust — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1.** List three things equal treatment does.
**Answer:** Any three of: reduces partiality/favouritism, gains trust and honour, strengthens social bonds, supports development, grows problem-solving ability.

**2.** Name the four places equality is practised.
**Answer:** Home, school, community and workplace.

**3.** What is favouritism?
**Answer:** Partiality — treating one person better than another for no fair reason.

**4.** How does equal treatment help problem-solving?
**Answer:** It grows individuals' problem-solving ability, as people trust and work with each other.
