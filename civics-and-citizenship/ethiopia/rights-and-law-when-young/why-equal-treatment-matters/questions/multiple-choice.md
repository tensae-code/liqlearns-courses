# Why Equal Treatment Matters — Less Favouritism, More Trust — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Equal treatment reduces —
a) favouritism  b) trust  c) friendship  d) learning
**Answer: a) favouritism**

**2.** Equal treatment helps people gain —
a) trust and honour  b) fear  c) enemies  d) debts
**Answer: a) trust and honour**

**3.** Equal treatment strengthens —
a) social bonds  b) walls between people  c) quarrels  d) silence
**Answer: a) social bonds**

**4.** Equality is practised —
a) only at school  b) at home, at school, in the community and at the workplace  c) only at work  d) nowhere in particular
**Answer: b) at home, at school, in the community and at the workplace**
