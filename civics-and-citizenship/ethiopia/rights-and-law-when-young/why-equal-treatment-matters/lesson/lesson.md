# Why Equal Treatment Matters — Less Favouritism, More Trust

## Do it first

Think of a teacher who serves every child in the row the same, in turn. Do the children trust that teacher more, or less? This lesson lists what equal treatment does.

## What equal treatment does

Treating people equally does five things. It reduces partiality and favouritism. It enables people to gain trust and honour from others. It strengthens social bonds. It supports the community's development. And it grows individuals' problem-solving ability.

## Where equality is practised

Equality is practised at home, at school, in the community and at the workplace — everywhere people live and work together.
