# Patriotism — Loving Your Country — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1.** Give both definitions of patriotism.
**Answer:** A person's deep love for their country; and love for its citizens too.

**2.** What duty travels with the love?
**Answer:** The duty owed to the nation as a member of it — the country kind of responsibility.

**3.** Answer the opening question briefly.
**Answer:** Learner's own answer to: what does being Ethiopian mean? (Any honest two-sentence answer.)

**4.** Feeling or duty — which is patriotism?
**Answer:** Both: the deep love, and the duty it carries.
