# Patriotism — Loving Your Country — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Patriotism is —
a) a deep love for your country  b) pride in yourself only  c) love of government only  d) a school subject
**Answer: a) a deep love for your country**

**2.** The second definition adds love for —
a) its citizens  b) its flag only  c) its army only  d) its cities only
**Answer: a) its citizens**

**3.** The opening question of this teaching is —
a) What does being Ethiopian mean?  b) What is for lunch?  c) Who is tallest?  d) Where is the market?
**Answer: a) What does being Ethiopian mean?**

**4.** Duty to your country is owed because you are —
a) a member of it  b) a visitor  c) an official only  d) an adult only
**Answer: a) a member of it**
