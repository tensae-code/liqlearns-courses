# Patriotism — Loving Your Country — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** Patriotism includes love for the country's citizens.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Patriotism is only a feeling, with no duty in it.
**Answer: False**

**3.** Responsibility to your country is one kind of responsibility.
**Answer: True**

**4.** Only officials owe duty to the country.
**Answer: False**
