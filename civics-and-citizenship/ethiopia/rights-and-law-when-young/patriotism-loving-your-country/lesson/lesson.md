# Patriotism — Loving Your Country

## Do it first

Ask yourself the opening question: what does being Ethiopian mean? Sit with it a moment. This lesson gives patriotism its definition.

## Deep love for country and citizens — and the duty it carries

Patriotism is a person's deep love for their country — and, in the second definition, love for its citizens too. Love of country is not only a feeling: as a member of your country you owe it a duty — the country kind of responsibility, owed to the nation as one of its members. Feeling and duty travel together.

## Do It Yourself

1. Answer the opening question in two sentences: what does being Ethiopian mean to you?
2. Write both definitions of patriotism from memory.
3. Name one duty you owe your country as a member of it.
