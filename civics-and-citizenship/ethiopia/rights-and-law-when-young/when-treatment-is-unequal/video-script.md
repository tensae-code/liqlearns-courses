# Video script — When Treatment Is Unequal — Discrimination and Its Damage

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

When treatment is unequal, rights are violated.
Conflict grows. Trust breaks.
Reduce discrimination: treat people equally.
Live in peace and agreement. Give every person equal rights.
And speak — fairly and calmly — against what is unjust.
