# When Treatment Is Unequal — Discrimination and Its Damage

## Do it first

Picture a queue where one child is pushed aside while others pass. What breaks in that moment — only the queue, or something bigger? This lesson names the damage, and how it is reduced.

## How discrimination is reduced

Discrimination is reduced by applying the principles of equal treatment, by living together in peace and agreement, and by giving every individual and group equal rights. Partiality is mostly practised at home, in play places, in the school compound and in the workplace — where there is no equality, partiality is practised everywhere. And when you see what is unjust — at school or in society — the fair person speaks against it, fairly and calmly.

## What inequality causes

The common results of inequality are heavy: violation of human rights, conflict, injustice, instability, unrest and misunderstandings between individuals and groups. Gender inequality grows from society's attitude and from discriminatory laws. Gender equality does the opposite good: it narrows the gaps in education, political participation and work, and supports women and girls to use their rights.
