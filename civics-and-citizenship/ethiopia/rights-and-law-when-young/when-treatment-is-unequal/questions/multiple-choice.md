# When Treatment Is Unequal — Discrimination and Its Damage — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Discrimination is reduced by —
a) applying equal treatment  b) ignoring it  c) more partiality  d) silence
**Answer: a) applying equal treatment**

**2.** Partiality is mostly practised at —
a) home, play places, the school compound and the workplace  b) only in books  c) only in other countries  d) nowhere
**Answer: a) home, play places, the school compound and the workplace**

**3.** Which is a result of inequality?
a) Conflict and instability  b) Trust  c) Honour  d) Development
**Answer: a) Conflict and instability**

**4.** Gender equality narrows gaps in —
a) education, political participation and work  b) height  c) age  d) weather
**Answer: a) education, political participation and work**
