# When Treatment Is Unequal — Discrimination and Its Damage — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** Inequality can bring violation of human rights.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Where there is no equality, partiality is practised everywhere.
**Answer: True**

**3.** Discriminatory laws are one source of gender inequality.
**Answer: True**

**4.** The fair person stays silent when a classmate is wronged.
**Answer: False**
