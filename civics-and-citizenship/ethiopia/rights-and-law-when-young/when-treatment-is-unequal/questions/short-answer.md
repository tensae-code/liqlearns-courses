# When Treatment Is Unequal — Discrimination and Its Damage — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1.** Name three results of inequality.
**Answer:** Any three of: violation of human rights, conflict, injustice, instability, unrest, misunderstandings.

**2.** Name two sources of gender inequality.
**Answer:** Society's attitude, and the existence of discriminatory laws.

**3.** How is discrimination reduced?
**Answer:** By applying equal treatment, living together in peace and agreement, and giving every individual and group equal rights.

**4.** What does the fair person do about injustice?
**Answer:** Speaks against what is unjust in the school and in society, fairly and calmly.
