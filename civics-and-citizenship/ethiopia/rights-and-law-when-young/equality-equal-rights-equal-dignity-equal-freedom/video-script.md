# Video script — Equality — Equal Rights, Equal Dignity, Equal Freedom

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Equality. What is it?
Equal rights. Equal dignity. Equal freedom.
We use rights and benefits equally.
We carry responsibilities equally.
Race, religion or language never decide how a person is treated.
And equality is shown in acts — not only in words.
