# Equality — Equal Rights, Equal Dignity, Equal Freedom

## Do it first

Imagine two children do the same work and one is given more, for no reason. Is that equality? This lesson gives equality its full meaning, part by part.

## What equality is

Equality is having equal rights, dignity and freedom with others. It means using rights and benefits equally, and carrying responsibilities equally too. No one is treated differently because of race, religion or language. Individuals and groups alike are given their honour.

## Equality in acts, not words

Equality is not finished when we say it. Pupils must show equality in acts, not words — sharing fairly, including everyone, and treating each person the same in the classroom and outside it.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the five parts of equality from this lesson, then give one act that shows equality today.

## Do It Yourself

1. Write the five parts of equality in your own words.
2. Watch one day at school: note one moment of equal treatment and one moment that was not equal.
3. Change the unequal moment: write what should have happened.
