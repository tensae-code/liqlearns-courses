# Equality — Equal Rights, Equal Dignity, Equal Freedom — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1.** Give the three equal things in this lesson's title.
**Answer:** Equal rights, equal dignity and equal freedom.

**2.** Name two things that must never decide how a person is treated.
**Answer:** Any two of race, religion, language.

**3.** How do pupils show equality?
**Answer:** In acts, not words — by treating everyone the same in practice.

**4.** Who is given honour under equality?
**Answer:** Individuals and groups alike.
