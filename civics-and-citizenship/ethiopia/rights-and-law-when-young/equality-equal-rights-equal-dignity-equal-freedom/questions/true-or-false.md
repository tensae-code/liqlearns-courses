# Equality — Equal Rights, Equal Dignity, Equal Freedom — true or false

Say true or false, and say why.

**1.** Equality includes using rights and benefits equally.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Under equality, one group may be given less honour than others.
**Answer: False**

**3.** Responsibilities are shared equally under equality.
**Answer: True**

**4.** Saying the word equality is enough; acts do not matter.
**Answer: False**
