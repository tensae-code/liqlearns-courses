# Lesson plan — Respect for Others — Dignity for Being Human

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Character of a Citizen ·
**Lesson:** Respect for Others — Dignity for Being Human
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 14 — Character of a Citizen (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** respect for others — positive regard and
treatment with dignity, owed to every person for the mere fact of being human.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands inside Level 14 — Character of a Citizen; the
learner needs the world of home, school and community, which they already have, and
nothing else is forced before it.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Define respect: positive regard and treatment with dignity regardless of ethnicity,
religion, language, colour, sex, economic background, disability or age.
2. Name where respect is learned first: in the family, toward parents, elders, teachers,
friends and neighbours.
3. List respectful acts: kind words when upset, please and thank you, waiting one's
turn, disagreeing kindly, care for places and things, a quiet voice in public, taking
only what one needs of shared resources.
4. Name the price of disrespect: exclusion, no respect or love returned, no support in
hardship.

## If the learner is stuck
- If respect is heard as something elders alone are owed, use the regardless list: it is
owed to every person, in every direction, for being human. If it sounds abstract, drop
to the acts — each is small enough to do before lunch.
