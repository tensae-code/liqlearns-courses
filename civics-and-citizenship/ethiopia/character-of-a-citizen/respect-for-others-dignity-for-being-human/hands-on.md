# Hands-on — Respect for Others — Dignity for Being Human

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The door held
Draw a pupil holding a door and greeting an elder. Around them, add small panels of the
other acts: waiting a turn, a quiet voice, thank you spoken.

## The price tag
Draw two price tags. On one write what respect costs: four seconds, small acts. On the
other write what disrespect costs: exclusion, nothing returned, no support in hardship.
