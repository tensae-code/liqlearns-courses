# Respect for Others — Dignity for Being Human — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1.** Respect must be earned by achievement before it is owed.
**Answer: False** — It is owed for being human.

**2.** Taking only what you need of shared resources is a respectful act.
**Answer: True** — Named in the lesson.

**3.** A person who shows disrespect can still expect full support in hardship.
**Answer: False** — No support in hardship is one of its prices.
