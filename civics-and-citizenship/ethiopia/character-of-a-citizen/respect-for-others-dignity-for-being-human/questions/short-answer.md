# Respect for Others — Dignity for Being Human — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name four respectful acts from the lesson.
**Answer:** Any four of: kind words when upset; please and thank you; waiting one's
turn; accepting difference or disagreeing kindly; care for places and things; a quiet
voice in public; taking only what one needs of shared resources.

**2.** Name the three prices of disrespect.
**Answer:** Exclusion; receiving no respect or love back; no support in hardship.

**3.** Where is respect learned first, and toward whom?
**Answer:** In the family — toward parents, elders, teachers, friends and neighbours.
