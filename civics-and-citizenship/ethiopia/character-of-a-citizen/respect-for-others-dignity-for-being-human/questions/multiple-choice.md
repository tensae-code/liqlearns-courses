# Respect for Others — Dignity for Being Human — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Respect is owed regardless of —
a) wealth only  b) ethnicity, religion, language, colour, sex, economic background,
disability or age  c) school marks  d) family fame
**Answer: b) ethnicity, religion, language, colour, sex, economic background, disability
or age** — The regardless list.

**2.** The ground of respect is —
a) high status  b) dignity for the mere fact of being human  c) being feared  d) being
older
**Answer: b) dignity for the mere fact of being human** — Nothing must be earned first.

**3.** Respect is learned first —
a) at university  b) in the family  c) in government offices  d) abroad
**Answer: b) in the family** — Toward parents, elders, teachers, friends, neighbours.

**4.** Which is a respectful act?
a) shouting in public  b) kind words when upset; please and thank you; waiting one's
turn  c) taking extra of shared resources  d) interrupting elders
**Answer: b) kind words when upset; please and thank you; waiting one's turn** — The
small repeatable acts.

**5.** The price of disrespect includes —
a) exclusion, no respect or love returned, no support in hardship  b) a fine only  c)
nothing at all  d) more invitations
**Answer: a) exclusion, no respect or love returned, no support in hardship** — Charged
to the one who pays it out.

**6.** Disagreeing, respectfully, is done —
a) loudly  b) kindly — accepting difference  c) by exclusion  d) by silence forever
**Answer: b) kindly — accepting difference** — Disagree kindly.
