# Hands-on — Concern for Others — Having a Place for People

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Rebuilding together
Draw neighbours carrying materials together to rebuild a damaged house. Label the
carriers with the roles concern takes: listener, decider-with, supporter.

## The house of rooms
Draw a house with a room for each named group — people with disabilities, children,
elders, women, people living with HIV/AIDS — and write inside each room one support that
should exist there.
