# Concern for Others — Having a Place for People — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the five groups the book names.
**Answer:** People with disabilities, children, elders, women, and people living with
HIV/AIDS.

**2.** What three things does concern do with people's needs?
**Answer:** Holds space for them; listens to and weighs their needs and feelings; lets
affected people share in decisions (and supports people in hardship).

**3.** What is Debo/Jigi?
**Answer:** Cooperative work parties, where neighbours work together on what one
household cannot do alone.
