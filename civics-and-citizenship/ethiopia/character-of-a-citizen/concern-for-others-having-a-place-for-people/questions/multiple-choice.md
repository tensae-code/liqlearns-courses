# Concern for Others — Having a Place for People — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Concern for others means —
a) worrying quietly  b) holding space for people: listening to and weighing their needs
and feelings, letting them share in decisions, supporting them in hardship  c) agreeing
with everyone  d) giving speeches
**Answer: b) holding space for people: listening to and weighing their needs and
feelings, letting them share in decisions, supporting them in hardship** — A place kept,
on purpose.

**2.** Which groups does the book name?
a) only elders  b) people with disabilities, children, elders, women, and people living
with HIV/AIDS  c) only officials  d) only students
**Answer: b) people with disabilities, children, elders, women, and people living with
HIV/AIDS** — The five named groups.

**3.** Debo/Jigi are —
a) taxes  b) cooperative work parties  c) examinations  d) festivals only
**Answer: b) cooperative work parties** — Neighbours working together.

**4.** Concern is grounded in home practice at —
a) weddings, births, mourning, disaster and sickness  b) only funerals  c) only markets
d) nowhere
**Answer: a) weddings, births, mourning, disaster and sickness** — The five occasions
named.

**5.** The lesson's survey asks what —
a) prices in the market  b) support actually exists in your community, proposing
remedies where it does not  c) the weather history  d)  examination results
**Answer: b) support actually exists in your community, proposing remedies where it does
not** — Findings, then a proposal.

**6.** Affected people, under concern for others, should —
a) be informed afterwards  b) share in the decisions that touch them  c) be kept out  d)
be thanked and ignored
**Answer: b) share in the decisions that touch them** — They share in decisions.
