# Hands-on — Virtues — compassion

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## At the spring
Role-play the spring: an old woman asks for water; play the younger sister's answer and
the elder's, and let the class name which was compassion and why.

## Words or a rope?
Draw a well with a fox at the bottom. On one side, draw speech bubbles; on the other, a
rope. Label which one the fox asked for.
