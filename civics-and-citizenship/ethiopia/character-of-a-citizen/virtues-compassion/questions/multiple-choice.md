# Virtues — compassion — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Compassion is —
a) feeling sorry and walking on  b) empathic understanding joined to an unselfish desire
to act  c) agreeing with everyone  d) crying easily
**Answer: b) empathic understanding joined to an unselfish desire to act** — Feeling
with, then acting for.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** At the spring, compassion was shown by —
a) the elder sister, who refused the princess  b) the younger sister, who gave the old
woman a drink  c) the princess  d) no one
**Answer: b) the younger sister, who gave the old woman a drink** — The drink given is
the act.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A society with more compassion would know less —
a) hunger, conflict, homelessness and unhappiness  b) music  c) rain  d) trade
**Answer: a) hunger, conflict, homelessness and unhappiness** — The lesson's own list.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The wolf at the well —
a) pulled the fox out  b) offered long pitying talk and no practical help  c) fetched
other animals  d) jumped in himself
**Answer: b) offered long pitying talk and no practical help** — Pity that does nothing.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The fox begged the wolf to —
a) talk longer  b) pull him out instead of talking about it  c) bring food tomorrow  d)
tell the village
**Answer: b) pull him out instead of talking about it** — Help, not speeches.

**6. (Sub lesson 1)** The root meaning teachers give for compassion is —
a) suffering together  b) winning together  c) eating together  d) working alone
**Answer: a) suffering together** — Feeling another's trouble as one's own.
