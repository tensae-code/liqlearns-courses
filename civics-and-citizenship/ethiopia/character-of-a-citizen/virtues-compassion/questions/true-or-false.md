# Virtues — compassion — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Listening and encouragement are practices of compassion, even
across disagreement.
**Answer: True** — Named in the lesson.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The wolf showed compassion because his words were kind.
**Answer: False** — Compassion must act; his pity did nothing.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Compassion can include appreciation of a person you disagree with.
**Answer: True** — Appreciation even across disagreement is named.
