# Virtues — compassion — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Define compassion in one sentence.
**Answer:** Empathic understanding of another's feelings joined to an unselfish desire
to act.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why is the wolf's pity not compassion?
**Answer:** Because it never became action — he talked at length and gave no practical
help.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Which sister showed compassion at the spring, and how?
**Answer:** The younger sister — she kindly gave a drink to the poor old woman who
asked.
