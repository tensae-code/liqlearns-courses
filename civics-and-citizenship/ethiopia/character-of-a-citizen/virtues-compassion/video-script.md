# Video script — Virtues — compassion

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md,
add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form
until then.

This lesson's script is a read-aloud of its story or case, in plot summary only.

---

**Virtues — compassion.**

Compassion — feeling with another person, then acting for them; pity that does nothing
is not compassion.

Compassion is empathic understanding of another's feelings, joined to an unselfish
desire to act.

Its practices are ordinary and warm: encouragement, listening, understanding, and
appreciation — even across disagreement.

Teachers name its root: suffering together — feeling another's trouble as one's own, and
then moving.

A society with more of it would know less hunger, less conflict, less homelessness and
less unhappiness.

Compassion is not decoration on a society; it is load-bearing.

The case is small and clear.

Two sisters draw water from a spring.

The younger kindly gives a drink to the poor old woman who asks.

The elder refuses the thirsty princess and tells her to fetch her own.

Which sister showed compassion?

Now you. Tell your companion both stories — the sister who gave the drink, and the wolf
who only talked. Then give the definition: feeling with, then acting for.
