# Making an ethical decision — five steps — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** List the five steps in order.
**Answer:** Identify the problem; identify stakeholders and interests; develop
solutions; evaluate strengths and weaknesses; select and justify.

**2.** Why develop more than one solution?
**Answer:** A decision with only one option in view is usually not yet thought through;
comparing options lets you evaluate and choose the best.

**3.** What question gives the method its virtue-ethics colouring?
**Answer:** 'Do I want to be a generous, honest person?'
