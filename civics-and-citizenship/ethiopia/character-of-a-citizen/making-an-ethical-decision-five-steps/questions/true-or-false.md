# Making an ethical decision — five steps — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1.** The five steps can be used in any order, skipping whichever you like.
**Answer: False** — They are inseparable and run in order.

**2.** Stakeholders include people affected besides the decider.
**Answer: True** — Family, school, strangers, the community can all be stakeholders.

**3.** A decision you cannot justify aloud is finished.
**Answer: False** — Justifying is the fifth step — without it the decision is not
finished.
