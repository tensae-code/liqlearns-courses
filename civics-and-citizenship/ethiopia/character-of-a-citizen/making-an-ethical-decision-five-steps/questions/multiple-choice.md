# Making an ethical decision — five steps — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Step one of ethical decision-making is —
a) select the solution  b) identify the central ethical problem, through investigation
c) ask your friends  d) ignore the problem
**Answer: b) identify the central ethical problem, through investigation** — Look before
you judge.

**2.** Step two asks you to identify —
a) the cheapest option  b) the stakeholders and their interests  c) the loudest person
d) yesterday's news
**Answer: b) the stakeholders and their interests** — Who is affected, and what do they
stand to gain or lose?

**3.** In step three you —
a) develop possible solutions — more than one  b) announce the answer  c) stop thinking
d) blame someone
**Answer: a) develop possible solutions — more than one** — More than one option must be
in view.

**4.** Step four is evaluating each solution's —
a) price only  b) strengths and weaknesses  c) popularity  d) age
**Answer: b) strengths and weaknesses** — Test each solution honestly.

**5.** The final step is —
a) select and justify the best solution  b) forget the problem  c) ask the teacher to
choose  d) wait a year
**Answer: a) select and justify the best solution** — Choose, and say why.

**6.** The virtue-ethics question inside the steps is —
a) what is my duty only?  b) do I want to be a generous, honest person?  c) what will I
gain?  d) who is watching?
**Answer: b) do I want to be a generous, honest person?** — The person you are becoming
matters.
