# Hands-on — Making an ethical decision — five steps

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The staircase
Draw five ascending steps on a large sheet. Label each with its step. Set a charity box
and a speech bubble marked 'the truth' at the top — the two examples this lesson
carries.

## A live climb
In a small group, take one real problem and climb together: one person per step, each
saying only what their step adds. Swap problems and climb again.
