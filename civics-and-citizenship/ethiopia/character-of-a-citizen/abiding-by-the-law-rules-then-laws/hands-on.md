# Hands-on — Abiding by the Law — Rules, Then Laws

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The rule-board
Draw a school gate with its rule-board: uniform, punctuality, respect for teachers, no
exam cheating. Draw pupils arriving on time in uniform beneath it.

## Two columns
Make a two-column chart — Rules | Laws — and sort six examples from home, school and
country into the right columns together.
