# Abiding by the Law — Rules, Then Laws — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Tell a rule from a law in one sentence each.
**Answer:** A rule is a prescribed standard guiding conduct in a society or
organisation, breach bringing punishment, condemnation or exclusion; a law is a written
standard endorsed by authority and binding on all.

**2.** Name the school's four 'laws'.
**Answer:** Uniform, punctuality, respect for teachers, no exam cheating.

**3.** What three words open the definition of abiding by the law?
**Answer:** Willingness, commitment and responsibility.
