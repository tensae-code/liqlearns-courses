# Abiding by the Law — Rules, Then Laws — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Abiding by the law is —
a) fear of police  b) the willingness, commitment and responsibility to obey rules and
regulations  c) obeying only written laws  d) agreeing with every rule's wisdom
**Answer: b) the willingness, commitment and responsibility to obey rules and
regulations** — Willingness first.

**2.** Rules are —
a) written by parliament only  b) prescribed standards guiding conduct in a society or
organisation  c) suggestions with no consequences  d) the same as laws
**Answer: b) prescribed standards guiding conduct in a society or organisation** —
Conduct guides inside a group.

**3.** Breaking a rule can bring —
a) punishment, condemnation or exclusion  b) a prize  c) nothing  d) promotion
**Answer: a) punishment, condemnation or exclusion** — The three consequences named.

**4.** Laws are —
a) customs  b) written standards endorsed by authority, binding on all  c) family habits
d) rumours
**Answer: b) written standards endorsed by authority, binding on all** — Written,
endorsed, binding on all.

**5.** The school's 'laws' in the worked example are —
a) uniform, punctuality, respect for teachers, no exam cheating  b) silence, speed,
strength, height  c) fees, books, pens, bags  d) songs and games
**Answer: a) uniform, punctuality, respect for teachers, no exam cheating** — The four
at the gate.

**6.** The duties named under rules include not to —
a) study  b) steal, cheat or corrupt  c) ask questions  d) arrive early
**Answer: b) steal, cheat or corrupt** — Steal, cheat, corrupt.
