# Abiding by the Law — Rules, Then Laws — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1.** Laws bind only members of one organisation.
**Answer: False** — Laws bind all.

**2.** Willingness — not fear — is the definition's first word.
**Answer: True** — Obedience owned, not extracted.

**3.** Exam cheating breaks one of the school's stated 'laws'.
**Answer: True** — No exam cheating is on the board.
