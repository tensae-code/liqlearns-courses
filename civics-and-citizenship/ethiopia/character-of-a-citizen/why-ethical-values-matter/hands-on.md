# Hands-on — Why Ethical Values Matter

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## One street, twice
Draw one street twice: orderly and trusting; then fearful and divided. Give both
drawings the same caption question: what changed, if the laws did not?

## Lit streets, dark lanes
Draw a simple map: lit streets labelled 'law reaches here', dark lanes labelled 'values
walk here'. Draw one honest figure walking the dark lane.
