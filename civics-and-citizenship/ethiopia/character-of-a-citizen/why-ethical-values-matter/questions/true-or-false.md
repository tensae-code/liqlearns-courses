# Why Ethical Values Matter — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1.** Every item in the pictured country can be delivered by a ministry alone.
**Answer: False** — Each is a sum of citizens' conduct.

**2.** Mutual trust strengthens in a society of ethical citizens.
**Answer: True** — Named among the goods.

**3.** Values matter only where laws already reach.
**Answer: False** — Their special work is where law cannot reach.
