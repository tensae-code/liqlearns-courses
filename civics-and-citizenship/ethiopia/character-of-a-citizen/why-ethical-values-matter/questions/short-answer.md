# Why Ethical Values Matter — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** List the pictured country's eight features.
**Answer:** Sustainable peace, social harmony, rapid socio-economic transformation, no
corruption, rule of law, justice, equality, national unity.

**2.** Why does the lesson use ageing parents as its example?
**Answer:** Because care of ageing parents lies where law cannot reach — no law can
schedule a child's kindness — so character alone decides there.

**3.** What does ethics build in a person?
**Answer:** Character — and it determines a person's real value.
