# Why Ethical Values Matter — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Ethics is called the single factor that determines —
a) a person's wealth  b) a person's real value  c) a person's height  d) a person's luck
**Answer: b) a person's real value** — And a builder of character.

**2.** A society of ethical citizens becomes —
a) fearful and divided  b) peaceful and harmonious, with stronger mutual trust and
reduced conflict  c) silent  d) dependent
**Answer: b) peaceful and harmonious, with stronger mutual trust and reduced conflict**
— The social goods named.

**3.** The example of where law cannot reach is —
a) tax collection  b) care of ageing parents  c) traffic control  d) examinations
**Answer: b) care of ageing parents** — No law can schedule a child's kindness.

**4.** The pictured country of ethical citizens has —
a) corruption managed  b) no corruption, rule of law, justice, equality and national
unity  c) more conflict  d) weaker trust
**Answer: b) no corruption, rule of law, justice, equality and national unity** — The
full picture as taught.

**5.** The problem the values answer, as the lesson opens, is —
a) a shortage of laws  b) the present erosion of moral integrity in the country  c)  bad
weather  d) low examination marks
**Answer: b) the present erosion of moral integrity in the country** — Named at the
lesson's opening.

**6.** On the teachers' map, the single honest figure walks —
a) only the lit streets  b) the dark lanes where law does not reach  c) outside the map
d) at noon only
**Answer: b) the dark lanes where law does not reach** — Values walk everywhere.
