# Video script — Loyalty — Faithfulness at Every Level

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md,
add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form
until then.

---

**Loyalty — Faithfulness at Every Level.**

Loyalty — truthfulness and faithfulness to a person, a society, an institution or the
country, worked by conscience at every level.

Loyalty is truthfulness and faithfulness to a person, a society, an institution or the
country.

The book gives its seat: loyalty is primarily a matter of conscience — it holds when no
contract is being checked and no watcher is near, because the watcher is inside.

It is needed between friends, who must be faithful when faithfulness costs; between
spouses; and between lender and debtor, where trust is quite literally the currency.

At work it widens: leaders must serve everyone equally and use public property fairly —
loyalty to the public, not to favourites.

And professionals — teachers, doctors, accountants, soldiers, engineers are named — must
serve their institution and the public loyally, each at their own post: the teacher at
the desk, the doctor at the clinic, the soldier at the post, under one shared caption:
faithful service.

The lesson leaves the classroom.

Its field activity sends you to a nearby government office to ask employees how loyal
they are, and what loyalty gains.

Ask respectfully, listen fully, and bring the answers back: loyalty sounds different
described by the people who practise it daily than it does in a definition.

Now you. Tell your companion what loyalty is, why it is a matter of conscience, and
three places it is needed — one personal, one between lender and debtor, one
professional.
