# Loyalty — Faithfulness at Every Level — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can teach
the lesson's one thing back in their own words — definition, case and point — and answer
its questions from the lesson alone. Practice deepens by more items and harder corners:
the same teaching applied to new cases from the learner's own school, street and home,
judged by the lesson's own criteria.
