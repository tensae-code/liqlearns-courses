# Loyalty — Faithfulness at Every Level — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Why is loyalty a matter of conscience?
**Answer:** Because it must hold when no contract is checked and no watcher is near —
the watcher is inside.

**2.** Name the five professionals the book names.
**Answer:** Teachers, doctors, accountants, soldiers, engineers.

**3.** What two things must a loyal leader do?
**Answer:** Serve everyone equally, and use public property fairly.
