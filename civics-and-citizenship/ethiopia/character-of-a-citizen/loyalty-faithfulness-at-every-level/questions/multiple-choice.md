# Loyalty — Faithfulness at Every Level — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Loyalty is —
a) obeying the loudest voice  b) truthfulness and faithfulness to a person, a society,
an institution or the country  c) liking someone  d) a military rank
**Answer: b) truthfulness and faithfulness to a person, a society, an institution or the
country** — The lesson's definition.

**2.** The book calls loyalty primarily a matter of —
a) contract  b) conscience  c) salary  d) custom
**Answer: b) conscience** — The watcher is inside.

**3.** Loyalty is needed between —
a) lender and debtor  b) rivals only  c) strangers only  d) no one in business
**Answer: a) lender and debtor** — Trust is the currency there.

**4.** A loyal leader —
a) serves favourites first  b) serves everyone equally and uses public property fairly
c) uses public property as their own  d) serves only voters
**Answer: b) serves everyone equally and uses public property fairly** — Loyalty to the
public.

**5.** The professionals named include —
a) teachers, doctors, accountants, soldiers, engineers  b) only soldiers  c) only
teachers  d) actors and singers
**Answer: a) teachers, doctors, accountants, soldiers, engineers** — Five are named.

**6.** The field activity sends pupils to —
a) a market, to bargain  b) a nearby government office, to ask employees about loyalty
and its gains  c) a stadium  d) another school to compete
**Answer: b) a nearby government office, to ask employees about loyalty and its gains**
— Ask, listen, bring answers back.
