# Loyalty — Faithfulness at Every Level — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1.** Loyalty can require honest words, not only silent obedience.
**Answer: True** — It is truthfulness as well as faithfulness.

**2.** Loyalty is needed between friends and spouses.
**Answer: True** — Both are named.

**3.** A loyal professional serves their own interest before the public.
**Answer: False** — Professionals serve their institution and the public loyally.
