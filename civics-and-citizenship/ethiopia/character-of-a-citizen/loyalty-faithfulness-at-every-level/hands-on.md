# Hands-on — Loyalty — Faithfulness at Every Level

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Three at their posts
Draw three panels: a teacher at a desk, a doctor at a clinic, a soldier at a post. Give
all three one shared caption: faithful service.

## The thread
Draw a thread running from two friends, through a lender and debtor shaking hands, to a
public office. Label what breaks the thread, and what keeps it whole.
