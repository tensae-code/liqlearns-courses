# Hands-on — Commitment to Excellence — Responsibility Through Hardship

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Shoes and books
Draw the picture the lesson opens with: a boy polishing shoes, schoolbooks beside him,
an exam result sheet on top. Label what he was looking at.

## The road in beats
Draw Omod's road as six stepping stones — poverty, work, first rank, straight As,
scholarship and medal, mega-projects — and walk a finger along it, saying each beat
aloud.
