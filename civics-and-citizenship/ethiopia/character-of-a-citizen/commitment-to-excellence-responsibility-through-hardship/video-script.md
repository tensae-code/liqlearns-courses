# Video script — Commitment to Excellence — Responsibility Through Hardship

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md,
add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form
until then.

This lesson's script is a read-aloud of its story or case, in plot summary only.

---

**Commitment to Excellence — Responsibility Through Hardship.**

Commitment to excellence — dedication that keeps building inner strength through
hardship — and omod, who shone shoes and ranked first.

Commitment is dedication.

Excellence is continuously building inner strength to face challenges.

Together — commitment to excellence — they mean taking full responsibility for your
behaviour, your decisions and your actions, in every situation.

The book is honest about the path: it is frankly challenging and painful.

No shortcut is promised, and none is needed.

The case, in summary, is Omod.

From a very poor family, he shone shoes and worked as a daily labourer outside school —
and never gave up.

He ranked first throughout, scored straight As in the Grade 12 national examination, won
a Japanese government engineering scholarship against East African competition,
graduated with distinction and a gold medal, took his MSc and PhD in Germany, and now
works on Ethiopia's mega-projects.

Read the story the lesson's way: not as luck, and not as talent falling from the sky.

Every line of it is responsibility taken in a hard situation — work and study carried
together, year after year.

Now you. Tell your companion both definitions, and Omod's whole road in six beats:
poverty, shoe-shining, first rank, straight As, scholarship, gold medal — and where he
works now.
