# Commitment to Excellence — Responsibility Through Hardship — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Commitment means —
a) dedication  b) delay  c) doubt  d) decoration
**Answer: a) dedication** — Dedication, plainly.

**2.** Excellence is —
a) a medal only  b) continuously building inner strength to face challenges  c) being
born gifted  d) winning once
**Answer: b) continuously building inner strength to face challenges** — Continuously
building.

**3.** Together they mean taking full responsibility for —
a) others' behaviour  b) your behaviour, decisions and actions in every situation  c)
nothing in hardship  d) results only
**Answer: b) your behaviour, decisions and actions in every situation** — In every
situation.

**4.** The book describes the path as —
a) easy and short  b) challenging and painful — honestly  c) painless for the talented
d) a lottery
**Answer: b) challenging and painful — honestly** — No shortcut is promised.

**5.** Omod outside school —
a) rested  b) shone shoes and worked as a daily labourer  c) played football
professionally  d) traded in a market
**Answer: b) shone shoes and worked as a daily labourer** — Work and study carried
together.

**6.** Omod now works on —
a) a farm  b) Ethiopia's mega-projects  c) a foreign navy  d) his own shop
**Answer: b) Ethiopia's mega-projects** — After MSc and PhD in Germany.
