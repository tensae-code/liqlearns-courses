# Commitment to Excellence — Responsibility Through Hardship — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1.** Omod ranked first throughout his schooling.
**Answer: True** — As the case is told.

**2.** Hardship, carried responsibly, can be excellence's training ground.

**3.** Commitment to excellence removes the need for responsibility in easy situations.
**Answer: False** — Responsibility is for every situation.
