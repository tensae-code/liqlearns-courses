# Good citizenship — obey the law and be responsible — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Good citizenship is —
a) born in a person, like eye colour  b) learned — taught by family, community, friends
and teachers  c) only for adults  d) the same as holding a passport
**Answer: b) learned — taught by family, community, friends and teachers** — It is
learned, not born.

**2.** Which of these is obeying the law, as this lesson teaches it?
a) paying taxes honestly and protecting the environment  b) following rules only when
watched  c) obeying every order without thinking  d) never reporting anything
**Answer: a) paying taxes honestly and protecting the environment** — Paying taxes and
protecting the environment are named in the lesson.

**3.** Responsibility for a pupil includes —
a) only scoring high marks  b) learning, homework, respect, and reporting wrongdoing  c)
doing whatever friends ask  d) avoiding all rules
**Answer: b) learning, homework, respect, and reporting wrongdoing** — Those four are
the lesson's own list.

**4.** The Parents' Day pupil was honoured for —
a) winning a race  b) following rules, building a club, and living in harmony across
ages, religions, languages and ethnic groups  c) being the oldest pupil  d) owning a
shop
**Answer: b) following rules, building a club, and living in harmony across ages,
religions, languages and ethnic groups** — That is the case as taught.

**5.** The second case in this lesson is about a pupil who —
a) founded an anti-corruption club at school  b) left school early  c) won a scholarship
abroad  d) became a teacher
**Answer: a) founded an anti-corruption club at school** — The club founder is the
guide's keyed case.

**6.** A responsible citizen who learns of tax fraud or smuggling —
a) keeps quiet to stay popular  b) joins in  c) reports the wrongdoing  d) moves away
**Answer: c) reports the wrongdoing** — Reporting wrongdoing is part of responsibility.
