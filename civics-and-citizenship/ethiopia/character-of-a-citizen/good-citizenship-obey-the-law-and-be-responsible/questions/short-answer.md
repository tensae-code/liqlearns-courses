# Good citizenship — obey the law and be responsible — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the four teachers of good citizenship.
**Answer:** Family, community, friends and teachers.

**2.** Give two examples of obeying the law from the lesson.
**Answer:** Paying taxes honestly; protecting the environment (also: following just laws
and rules).

**3.** What did the pupil in the guide's case found at school?
**Answer:** An anti-corruption club.
