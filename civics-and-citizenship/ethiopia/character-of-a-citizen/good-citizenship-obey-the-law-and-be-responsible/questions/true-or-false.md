# Good citizenship — obey the law and be responsible — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1.** A person is born a good citizen and cannot learn to be one.
**Answer: False** — Good citizenship is learned — family, community, friends and
teachers teach it.

**2.** Protecting the environment is part of obeying the law in this lesson.
**Answer: True** — It is named alongside paying taxes.

**3.** Responsibility is only about doing homework.
**Answer: False** — It also covers learning, respect and reporting wrongdoing.
