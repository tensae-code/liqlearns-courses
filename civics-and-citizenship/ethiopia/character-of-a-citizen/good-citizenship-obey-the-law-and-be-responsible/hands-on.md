# Hands-on — Good citizenship — obey the law and be responsible

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The feature hunt
Walk through your school or neighbourhood in your mind and list five places where
obeying the law protects people: a road crossing, a water point, a market, a classroom,
a tax office. For each, write the rule and what it protects.

## Two cases, one list
On paper, draw two columns. Head one 'the Parents' Day pupil' and the other 'the club
founder'. Under each, write what that pupil did. Then draw the line underneath both: the
features they share.
