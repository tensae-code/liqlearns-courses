# Video script — Good citizenship — obey the law and be responsible

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md,
add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form
until then.

---

**Good citizenship — obey the law and be responsible.**

Two features of a good citizen — obeying the law, and being responsible — and that good
citizenship is learned, not born.

Good citizenship is learned, not born.

Nobody arrives in the world knowing how to be a good citizen.

It is taught — by family, by the community, by friends and by teachers — and it is
practised until it becomes the way a person lives.

The first feature is obeying the law.

A good citizen follows just laws and rules.

That means paying taxes honestly.

It means protecting the environment instead of spoiling it.

It means treating the rules of the road, the school and the country as real guides for
living, not as traps to escape when nobody is watching.

The second feature is responsibility.

For a pupil, responsibility looks ordinary: learning seriously, doing homework, and
treating other people with respect.

Now you. Tell your companion the two features of good citizenship in this lesson. For
each one, give an example from your own school or street — one thing a person does, not
a slogan.
