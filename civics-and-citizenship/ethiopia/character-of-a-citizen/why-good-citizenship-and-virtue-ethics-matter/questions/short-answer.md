# Why good citizenship and virtue ethics matter — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name four goods that arrive with good citizenship.
**Answer:** Any four of: strong moral code; safe and supportive society; constructive
debate; mutual respect; common-ground working; wider perspectives; attention to
injustice and oppression; resistance to extremism; less violence.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** List the five importances of virtue ethics briefly.
**Answer:** It teaches right from wrong amid deteriorating values; forms responsible
members and cohesion; reveals life's true purpose; builds positive approach and self-
esteem; is society's hope of eliminating social evils.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the torn-papers classroom show?
**Answer:** Citizenship absent — everyone stepping over a shared harm; the cared-for
room shows the same pupils with citizenship present.
