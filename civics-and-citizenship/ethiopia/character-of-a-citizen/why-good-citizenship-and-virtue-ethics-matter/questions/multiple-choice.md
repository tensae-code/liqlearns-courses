# Why good citizenship and virtue ethics matter — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** With good citizenship comes —
a) a strong moral code and a safe, supportive society  b) more exclusion  c) silence in
debate  d) extremism
**Answer: a) a strong moral code and a safe, supportive society** — The lesson's list
begins there.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The everyday form of challenging injustice is —
a) reporting a theft or a drug dealer  b) stepping over torn papers  c) arguing loudly
d) moving away
**Answer: a) reporting a theft or a drug dealer** — Small, near, and named.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Good citizenship brings less violence because —
a) police are everywhere  b) no one is excluded  c) everyone agrees on everything  d)
laws are harsher
**Answer: b) no one is excluded** — Inclusion removes a root of violence.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Virtue ethics teaches right from wrong in a time of —
a) perfect values  b) deteriorating values  c) no values at all  d) imported values only
**Answer: b) deteriorating values** — The first importance, as taught.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Virtues make laws workable and officials —
a) richer  b) trustworthy  c) untouchable  d) unnecessary
**Answer: b) trustworthy** — The teachers' mechanism: character carries law.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Roosevelt's warning, as the book gives it, is that educating mind
without morals educates —
a) a genius  b) a menace to society  c) a teacher  d) a citizen
**Answer: b) a menace to society** — The book's own opener.
