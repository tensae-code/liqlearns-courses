# Why good citizenship and virtue ethics matter — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Constructive debate across opposing views is one of good
citizenship's goods.
**Answer: True** — Arguments meet instead of people fighting.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Virtue ethics aims only at better individuals, not society.
**Answer: False** — It forms contributing members and social cohesion.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Virtue ethics is society's hope against discrimination, oppression
and violence.
**Answer: True** — The fifth importance.
