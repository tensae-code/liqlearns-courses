# Hands-on — Why good citizenship and virtue ethics matter

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two rooms
Draw the same classroom twice: torn papers ignored; the room cared for together. Under
each, write what its citizens did.

## The street from above
Draw a market street running smoothly — queue at the water point, swept road, open shops
— and label three citizen acts that keep it so.
