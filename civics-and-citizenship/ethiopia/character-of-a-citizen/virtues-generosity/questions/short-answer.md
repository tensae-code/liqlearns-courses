# Virtues — generosity — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the three things generosity expends.
**Answer:** Time, effort and resources.

**2.** Name two false kinds of giving this lesson rejects.
**Answer:** Showing off (giving to be seen), and giving what costs the giver nothing.

**3.** What does generosity build between people?
**Answer:** Connectedness — it pulls people closer and makes community.
