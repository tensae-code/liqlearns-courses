# Virtues — generosity — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1.** Giving away something you were about to throw away, with ceremony, is generosity
at its best.
**Answer: False** — Giving what costs nothing is not the virtue.

**2.** Time and effort count as generosity, not only money.
**Answer: True** — Time, effort and resources are all named.

**3.** A generous person can feel good about giving.
**Answer: True** — Good feeling and confidence are among its rewards.
