# Virtues — generosity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Generosity is the disposition to expend — for others
a) only money  b) time, effort and resources  c) excuses  d) advice only
**Answer: b) time, effort and resources** — All three are named.

**2.** Generosity gives the giver —
a) debts  b) good feeling, confidence, freedom from self-hatred, and closeness with
people  c) a public prize  d) nothing at all
**Answer: b) good feeling, confidence, freedom from self-hatred, and closeness with
people** — The giver is rewarded too.

**3.** True generosity is —
a) giving to be seen  b) giving beyond what duty requires, with good intention  c)
giving away rubbish with ceremony  d) giving only to family
**Answer: b) giving beyond what duty requires, with good intention** — Beyond duty, with
good intention.

**4.** The farmer sharing seed grain shows generosity because —
a) seed is worthless  b) the seed is next year's life — the gift costs him something
real  c) he had too much  d) he wanted praise
**Answer: b) the seed is next year's life — the gift costs him something real** — The
cost is left visible.

**5.** Showing off differs from generosity because —
a) it is bigger  b) its intention is to be seen, not to give  c) it uses money  d) it
happens outdoors
**Answer: b) its intention is to be seen, not to give** — Intention separates them.

**6.** Generosity builds in a community —
a) competition  b) connectedness — people pulled closer  c) suspicion  d) debt
**Answer: b) connectedness — people pulled closer** — It makes community a community.
