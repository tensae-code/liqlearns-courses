# Video script — Virtues — generosity

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md,
add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form
until then.

---

**Virtues — generosity.**

Generosity — giving time, effort and resources for others, with good intention, and
giving that costs the giver something.

Generosity is the disposition to expend time, effort and resources for others — kind,
understanding, selfless giving with good intention.

Notice the breadth: not money only.

Time given, effort spent and resources shared all count.

Generosity rewards the giver too.

It brings good feeling and confidence, freedom from self-hatred, and it pulls people
closer — building the sense of connectedness that makes a community a community.

The generous person is not poorer in the ways that matter.

Teachers add the honest edge, and it matters.

Generosity is giving beyond what duty requires — of goods, of time, of attention.

It is not showing off: giving done to be seen is a performance, not a virtue.

And it is not giving what costs the giver nothing — the thing you would have thrown
away, handed over with ceremony.

Now you. Tell your companion what generosity is, what it gives back to the giver, and
the two false kinds: showing off, and giving what costs nothing.
