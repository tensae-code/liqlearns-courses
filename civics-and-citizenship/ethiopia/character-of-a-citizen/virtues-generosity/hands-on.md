# Hands-on — Virtues — generosity

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Across the table
Set books and food on a schoolyard table. In pairs, practise sharing across it — each
pair must give something the giver will genuinely miss, and say nothing about it
afterwards.

## The farmer's store
Draw two grain stores: one full, one empty. Draw the sack carried from the full store to
the empty one, labelled: seed for next year, shared.
