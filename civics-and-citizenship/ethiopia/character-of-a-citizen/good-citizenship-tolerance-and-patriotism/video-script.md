# Video script — Good citizenship — tolerance and patriotism

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md,
add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form
until then.

---

**Good citizenship — tolerance and patriotism.**

Two more features of a good citizen — tolerance, living peacefully with difference, and
patriotism, love of country shown in work as well as in battle.

Tolerance is the willingness to live peacefully with opinions, religions, cultures and
languages different from your own.

It does not ask you to agree with everyone.

It asks you to let difference exist beside you without turning it into a fight.

A tolerant citizen shares ideas across those lines and lets others share theirs.

Patriotism is genuine love of country, shown by a willingness to sacrifice for it.

Ethiopia's heroes showed it in battle, and that sacrifice is honoured.

But the lesson is wider than the battlefield: a patriot also serves through diplomacy,
through education, through medicine, through farming, through trade and through sport.

That is why athletes who raise the Ethiopian flag abroad are named as patriots: Haile
Gebrselassie and Derartu Tulu carried their country with them every time they ran, and
the whole country felt carried too.

Tolerance and patriotism belong together.

Love of country that cannot tolerate the country's own differences is not the love this
lesson teaches — the country is its people, in all their languages, faiths and opinions.

Now you. Tell your companion what tolerance means, and then name three ways — not
counting battle — that a person can show patriotism. Finish with the two athletes this
lesson names.
