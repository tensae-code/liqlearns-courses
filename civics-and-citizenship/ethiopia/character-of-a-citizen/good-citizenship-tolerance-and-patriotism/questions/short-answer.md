# Good citizenship — tolerance and patriotism — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name four peaceful ways of showing patriotism.
**Answer:** Any four of: diplomacy, education, medicine, farming, trade, sport.

**2.** What is tolerance, in one sentence?
**Answer:** The willingness to live peacefully with opinions, religions, cultures and
languages different from your own.

**3.** Name the two athletes this lesson calls patriots.
**Answer:** Haile Gebrselassie and Derartu Tulu.
