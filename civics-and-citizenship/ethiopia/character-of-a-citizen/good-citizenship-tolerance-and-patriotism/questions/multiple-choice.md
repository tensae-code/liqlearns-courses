# Good citizenship — tolerance and patriotism — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Tolerance means —
a) agreeing with everyone  b) the willingness to live peacefully with opinions,
religions, cultures and languages different from your own  c) ignoring other people  d)
having no opinions of your own
**Answer: b) the willingness to live peacefully with opinions, religions, cultures and
languages different from your own** — That is the lesson's definition.

**2.** Patriotism is —
a) only fighting in wars  b) genuine love of country shown by willingness to sacrifice
for it  c) supporting one football team  d) living abroad
**Answer: b) genuine love of country shown by willingness to sacrifice for it** — Love
of country, shown by sacrifice.

**3.** Which of these is patriotism as this lesson teaches it?
a) serving through medicine, farming or sport  b) refusing to work  c) hating other
countries' people  d) avoiding taxes
**Answer: a) serving through medicine, farming or sport** — The lesson names those
services.

**4.** Haile Gebrselassie and Derartu Tulu are named in this lesson as —
a) soldiers  b) patriots — athletes who raised the flag abroad  c) teachers  d)
diplomats
**Answer: b) patriots — athletes who raised the flag abroad** — Athletes who raise the
flag are patriots too.

**5.** A tolerant citizen, meeting an opinion they dislike, —
a) starts a fight  b) lives peacefully beside the difference  c) bans the speaker from
the street  d) pretends to agree
**Answer: b) lives peacefully beside the difference** — Tolerance is peaceful living
with difference.

**6.** Tolerance and patriotism fit together because —
a) the country is its people, in all their languages and faiths  b) they are the same
word  c) both are about sport  d) neither needs the other
**Answer: a) the country is its people, in all their languages and faiths** — Love of
country includes its different people.
