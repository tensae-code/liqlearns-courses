# Good citizenship — tolerance and patriotism — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1.** Patriotism can only be shown in battle.
**Answer: False** — It is also shown through diplomacy, education, medicine, farming,
trade and sport.

**2.** Tolerance asks you to give up your own opinions.
**Answer: False** — It asks peaceful living with difference, not agreement.

**3.** Athletes who raise their country's flag abroad can be patriots.
**Answer: True** — The lesson names Haile Gebrselassie and Derartu Tulu.
