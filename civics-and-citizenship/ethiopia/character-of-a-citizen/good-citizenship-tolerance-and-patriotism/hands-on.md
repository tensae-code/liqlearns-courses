# Hands-on — Good citizenship — tolerance and patriotism

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Difference at one table
With paper figures or drawings, set pupils of different languages and faiths around one
table sharing ideas. Label what each person is free to keep: their language, their
faith, their opinion.

## The flag carried far
Draw a runner raising the Ethiopian flag. Around the runner, write the other carriers of
the flag: the teacher, the doctor, the farmer, the trader, the diplomat.
