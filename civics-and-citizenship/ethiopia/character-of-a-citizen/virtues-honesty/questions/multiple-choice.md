# Virtues — honesty — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Honesty includes —
a) truthfulness, trustworthiness, loyalty, fairness and sincerity  b) only never lying
c) being clever  d) winning arguments
**Answer: a) truthfulness, trustworthiness, loyalty, fairness and sincerity** — The
lesson's five words.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Honesty is owed —
a) only at school  b) at home, among friends, in community and at work  c) only to
elders  d) nowhere in particular
**Answer: b) at home, among friends, in community and at work** — Everywhere a person
lives.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The honest person never —
a) makes a mistake  b) tells, accepts, steals or cheats  c) asks a question  d) changes
their mind
**Answer: b) tells, accepts, steals or cheats** — Never telling, accepting, stealing or
cheating.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In the fable, Falsehood tricked —
a) Truth into giving the cattle away  b) Water into quenching Fire, and Truth into
hiding the cattle uphill  c) Wind into blowing  d) Fire into raining
**Answer: b) Water into quenching Fire, and Truth into hiding the cattle uphill** — Two
tricks, as the story is told.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Wind, called to judge the cattle's ownership, —
a) chose Truth at once  b) could not settle the ownership  c) gave them to Falsehood  d)
sold them
**Answer: b) could not settle the ownership** — Dishonesty leaves nothing decidable.

**6. (Sub lesson 1)** Dishonesty in office — abuse of public property, corruption — is
named in this lesson as —
a) clever business  b) a disaster for development that every responsible citizen must
fight  c) a private matter  d) unavoidable and fine
**Answer: b) a disaster for development that every responsible citizen must fight** —
Named plainly, as the book names it.
