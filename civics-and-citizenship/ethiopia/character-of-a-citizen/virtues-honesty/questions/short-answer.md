# Virtues — honesty — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the five words inside honesty.
**Answer:** Truthfulness, trustworthiness, loyalty, fairness, sincerity.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What could Wind not do in the fable, and why?
**Answer:** Wind could not settle who owned the cattle — Falsehood's tricks had made the
truth undecidable.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Where is honesty owed? Name the four places.
**Answer:** At home, among friends, in the community, at work.
