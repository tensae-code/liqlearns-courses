# Hands-on — Virtues — honesty

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The returned wallet
Role-play at a school gate: one learner finds a wallet, one owns it, one watches
silently. Play it twice — the honest return, and the kept wallet — and let the watchers
say what changed in the room.

## Four cattle, four figures
Draw Truth, Falsehood, Water and Fire as four figures around four cattle. Underneath,
write the ending: Wind could not settle it.
