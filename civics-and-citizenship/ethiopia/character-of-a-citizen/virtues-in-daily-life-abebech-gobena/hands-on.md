# Hands-on — Virtues in daily life — Abebech Gobena

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The growing circle
Draw a woman at the centre of a growing circle of children. Around the ring, letter the
virtues her life displays: compassion, generosity, self-discipline.

## Dresses remade
Draw the table inside her door: dresses being cut and resewn to fit small children.
Label what the giver gave up in the same picture.
