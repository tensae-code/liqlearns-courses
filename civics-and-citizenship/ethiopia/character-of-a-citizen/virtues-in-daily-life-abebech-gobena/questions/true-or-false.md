# Virtues in daily life — Abebech Gobena — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1.** She lived with the children around the clock.
**Answer: True** — As the case is told.

**2.** Her own words were: 'Commitment is everything to me.'
**Answer: True** — The lesson carries her sentence.

**3.** The second baby she took in was found beside his living father.
**Answer: False** — Beside his dead father, as printed.
