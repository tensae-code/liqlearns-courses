# Virtues in daily life — Abebech Gobena — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Abebech Gobena's care began when she —
a) won a grant  b) found an abandoned baby on a journey to Wollo and brought her home
c) opened a school  d) was appointed by a ministry
**Answer: b) found an abandoned baby on a journey to Wollo and brought her home** — One
baby, found and taken home.

**2.** Within a year she had taken in —
a) 2 children  b) 7 children  c) 21 children  d) 200 children
**Answer: c) 21 children** — 21 within a year; 200 came later.

**3.** Her organisation was licensed in —
a) 1979  b) 1988  c) 2003  d) 1995
**Answer: b) 1988** — Licensed in 1988, as printed.

**4.** At its growth, the organisation had six branches serving over —
a) 200 children  b) 1,000 children  c) 12,000 children  d) 120 children
**Answer: c) 12,000 children** — Over 12,000 children, as printed.

**5.** She funded the care at first by —
a) government salary  b) her own money — selling her gold ornaments and remaking her own
dresses for the children  c) foreign lottery  d) charging the children
**Answer: b) her own money — selling her gold ornaments and remaking her own dresses for
the children** — Her own money, gold and dresses.

**6.** The virtues her life displays, as this lesson extracts them, are —
a) compassion, generosity, self-discipline  b) courage only  c) prudence only  d) fame
and wealth
**Answer: a) compassion, generosity, self-discipline** — The three named virtues.
