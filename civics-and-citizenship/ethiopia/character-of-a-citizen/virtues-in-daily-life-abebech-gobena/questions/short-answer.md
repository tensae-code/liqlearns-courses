# Virtues in daily life — Abebech Gobena — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the three virtues extracted from her life.
**Answer:** Compassion, generosity, self-discipline.

**2.** Give the story's number line.
**Answer:** 21 children within a year; later 200; licensed 1988; six branches serving
over 12,000 children.

**3.** What did she cut up and remake, and why?
