# Video script — Virtues in daily life — Abebech Gobena

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md,
add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form
until then.

This lesson's script is a read-aloud of its story or case, in plot summary only.

---

**Virtues in daily life — Abebech Gobena.**

The virtues in one real life — abebech gobena, who took in abandoned children until her
care served thousands — and the virtues her life displays.

On a journey to Wollo, a woman found an abandoned baby and brought her home.

Then she found a second baby, lying beside his dead father, and brought him home too.

Within a year she had taken in 21 children, raising them on her own money — until the
children numbered 200.

Licensed in 1988, her Children's Care and Development organisation grew to six branches
serving over 12,000 children.

The numbers matter, but the cost matters more, because the cost is where the virtues
live.

She sold her gold ornaments.

She cut up and remade her own dresses to fit the children.

She lived with them around the clock.

Her own words carry the whole lesson: 'Commitment is everything to me.' Now do the
lesson's work: extract the virtues on display.

Compassion — she felt with abandoned children and acted.

Now you. Tell your companion the story with its numbers — 21 within a year, later 200,
licensed 1988, six branches, over 12,000 children — and name the three virtues her life
displays.
