# Virtues — fairness — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** When is a reward unfair, in this lesson's words?
**Answer:** When it turns on the giver's opinion of a person rather than equal ability
and time.

**2.** What does the jury role play audit at the end?
**Answer:** Its own fairness — whether the verdict followed evidence and criteria, not
liking and disliking.

**3.** Complete: fairness treats like situations ______.
**Answer:** Alike, and consistently.
