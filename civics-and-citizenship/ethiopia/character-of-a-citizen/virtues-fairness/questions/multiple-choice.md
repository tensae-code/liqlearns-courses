# Virtues — fairness — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Fairness means decisions —
a) follow the decider's feelings  b) are free of bias, giving everyone a real chance to
benefit  c) always give identical things to all  d) favour the decider's friends
**Answer: b) are free of bias, giving everyone a real chance to benefit** — Free of
bias, a real chance for all.

**2.** A reward is unfair when it turns on —
a) equal ability and time  b) the giver's opinion of a person  c) the task done  d)
proper criteria
**Answer: b) the giver's opinion of a person** — Opinion of the person, not the work.

**3.** Fairness is not sameness because —
a) people are treated equitably, on merit and ability; like situations alike  b) rules
do not matter  c) the teacher decides by mood  d) sameness is too hard
**Answer: a) people are treated equitably, on merit and ability; like situations alike**
— The book's own qualification.

**4.** The two things that must never bend a decision are —
a) evidence and criteria  b) favouritism and prejudice  c) time and effort  d) questions
and answers
**Answer: b) favouritism and prejudice** — Favouritism and prejudice are banned.

**5.** In the jury role play, after sentencing, the class —
a) goes home  b) audits its own fairness  c) destroys the slips  d) elects a judge for
life
**Answer: b) audits its own fairness** — The audit is part of the lesson.

**6.** Like situations must be handled —
a) alike, and consistently  b) however the day feels  c) by lottery  d) in secret
**Answer: a) alike, and consistently** — Alike and consistently.
