# Hands-on — Virtues — fairness

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The classroom courtroom
Arrange the room as a courtroom. Draw a paper crime from a hat, hear the case briefly,
let a jury decide — then audit the verdict together against the criteria.

## The scales
Draw scales. On each pan put an identical task card; above both, identical reward cards.
Underneath, write the two banned weights: favouritism, prejudice.
