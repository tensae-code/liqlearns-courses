# Civic virtues — tax, information, participation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Define a progressive tax correctly.
**Answer:** A tax that takes a greater share from higher incomes, according to ability
to pay.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the three channels for staying informed.
**Answer:** Newspaper, radio and television.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name three ways of participating in public government.
**Answer:** Any three of: community meetings, debate, associations and clubs, campaigns
and civic societies, voting, reading and writing on issues, service in the defence force
and other institutions.
