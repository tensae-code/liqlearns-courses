# Civic virtues — tax, information, participation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A civic virtue is —
a) a tax form  b) the reasoned commitment every citizen owes the state, real when the
moral virtues are internalised  c) a government job  d) a voting card
**Answer: b) the reasoned commitment every citizen owes the state, real when the moral
virtues are internalised** — Commitment, built on the inner virtues.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A progressive tax —
a) takes a greater share from lower incomes  b) takes a greater share from higher
incomes, according to ability to pay  c) takes nothing from anyone  d) is a flat fee for
all
**Answer: b) takes a greater share from higher incomes, according to ability to pay** —
Corrected meaning: higher incomes carry the greater share.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Fair taxes fund —
a) one official's house  b) every government function — roads, schools, clinics, courts
c) nothing visible  d) only ceremonies
**Answer: b) every government function — roads, schools, clinics, courts** — They fund
the functions everyone uses.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Staying informed uses the channels —
a) rumour only  b) newspaper, radio and television  c) silence  d) dreams
**Answer: b) newspaper, radio and television** — The lesson's everyday channels.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Participating in public government includes —
a) community meetings, debate, associations, voting, writing on issues, service  b)
avoiding all public life  c) only holding office  d) paying a fee
**Answer: a) community meetings, debate, associations, voting, writing on issues,
service** — Many shapes, at all levels.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Participation happens at —
a) local level only  b) local, regional and national levels  c) national level only  d)
nowhere yet
**Answer: b) local, regional and national levels** — All three levels are named.
