# Civic virtues — tax, information, participation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tax evasion and smuggling stand against the fair tax and harm
honest citizens.
**Answer: True** — They steal from every honest citizen at once.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** An uninformed citizen is neutral in public life.
**Answer: False** — The lesson calls them absent, not neutral.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Civic virtue can be real without the moral virtues inside the
person.
**Answer: False** — It is real only when the moral virtues are internalised.
