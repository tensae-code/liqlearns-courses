# Hands-on — Civic virtues — tax, information, participation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Three panels
Draw the three panels: a cash register issuing a receipt; a reader with a newspaper; a
queue at a polling station. Label each with its form of civic virtue.

## The scale of incomes
Draw a tax-office scale: a small income on one side, a large income on the other, the
larger income carrying the larger share of tax. Write under it: according to ability to
pay.
