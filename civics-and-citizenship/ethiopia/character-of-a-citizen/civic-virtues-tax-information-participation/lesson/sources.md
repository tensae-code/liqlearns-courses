BOOK ERROR taught corrected: the Grade 8 guide (G8T #10, Unit 1 Lesson 9) defines a
fair/progressive tax reversed — as imposing 'a greater burden on those with lower incomes
than on those with higher incomes'. That is exactly backwards. The lesson teaches the
corrected meaning — a progressive tax takes a greater share from higher incomes, according
to ability to pay — and the student book's own wording (taxes proportional to income) is the
pupil-taught base. The guide's error is named here only, never in lesson prose.
