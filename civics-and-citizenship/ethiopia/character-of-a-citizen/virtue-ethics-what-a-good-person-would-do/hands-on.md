# Hands-on — Virtue ethics — what a good person would do

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The sorting table
Make real trait cards from paper — honesty, dishonesty, courage, cowardice, generosity,
selfishness, fairness, cheating — and sort them into two columns on a table: virtues,
vices. Read each card aloud as it lands.

## The staked tree
Draw a young tree tied to a straight stake. Around it, write the virtue names of this
unit. The stake is practice and imitation; the tree is you.
