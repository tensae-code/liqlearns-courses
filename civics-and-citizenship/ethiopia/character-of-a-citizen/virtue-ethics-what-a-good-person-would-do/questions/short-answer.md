# Virtue ethics — what a good person would do — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name a virtue and its opposite vice.
**Answer:** For example: honesty and dishonesty; courage and cowardice.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How does a learner acquire a virtue?
**Answer:** By practice — doing the act again and again — and by imitating exceptionally
virtuous people, until it becomes habit.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** What does virtue ethics put first when judging an action?
**Answer:** Character — what a virtuous person would do.
