# Virtue ethics — what a good person would do — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Dishonesty is the opposite — the vice — of honesty.
**Answer: True** — Honesty/dishonesty is the book's example pair.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Ethics is the study of good and bad ends and of right and wrong
conduct.
**Answer: True** — That is the lesson's definition.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Once a virtue becomes habit, practice is no longer part of it.
**Answer: False** — Habit is practice completed — the pattern is now your own.
