# Virtue ethics — what a good person would do — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A virtue is —
a) one lucky action  b) a disposition — a habit of character — serving the community's
healthy functioning and common good  c) a prize given at school  d) a rule written by
the government
**Answer: b) a disposition — a habit of character — serving the community's healthy
functioning and common good** — Virtues are habits of character.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Virtue is acquired by —
a) being born with it  b) practice, and imitating exceptionally virtuous people  c)
reading one book once  d) winning it
**Answer: b) practice, and imitating exceptionally virtuous people** — Practice and
imitation, until habit.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The virtue-ethics question is —
a) what is my duty?  b) what will happen if I do it?  c) what would a virtuous person
do?  d) what do my friends want?
**Answer: c) what would a virtuous person do?** — Character first — that is the
question.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Virtue ethics is traced to —
a) Aristotle and the ancient Greeks  b) modern advertising  c) the tax office  d) no one
**Answer: a) Aristotle and the ancient Greeks** — As the book states.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Practised long enough, a virtue becomes —
a) boring  b) a habit — simply what you do  c) impossible  d) a secret
**Answer: b) a habit — simply what you do** — Effort becomes habit.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Imitating a virtuous person works like —
a) a young tree staked straight, growing straight  b) a stone thrown in a river  c) a
closed door  d) a market price
**Answer: a) a young tree staked straight, growing straight** — The guide's own picture
of growth.
