# Video script — What Ethics Is

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md,
add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form
until then.

This lesson's script is a read-aloud of its story or case, in plot summary only.

---

**What Ethics Is.**

Ethics itself — the reflective study and practice of right and wrong conduct — and the
friend who would not help cheat.

Ethics — from the Greek ethos, custom — is the discipline concerned with right and
wrong, good and bad, acceptable and unacceptable conduct.

Morality names the standards themselves; ethics is the reflective study and practice of
them.

One is the map; the other is learning to read it, and then walking by it.

Ethics obliges.

It obliges a person to refrain from acts such as stealing, fraud, assault and slander.

And it binds a person in virtues such as honesty, compassion and loyalty, and in rights
such as life, privacy and freedom from injury.

Teachers add the distinctions that keep the idea clean: ethics is not the same as law,
which can be obeyed from fear; not the same as custom, which can simply be old; and not
the same as habit, which can be thoughtless.

Ethics is conduct reflected on — and then chosen.

The opening case puts it in an exam room, in summary.

Three classmates: X, the top student; Y; and Z.

Now you. Tell your companion the difference between morality and ethics, and the whole
X, Y and Z case in four sentences. Finish by saying whose act you appreciate, and why.
