# Lesson plan — What Ethics Is

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Character of a Citizen ·
**Lesson:** What Ethics Is
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 14 — Character of a Citizen (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** ethics itself — the reflective study and practice
of right and wrong conduct — and the friend who would not help cheat.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands inside Level 14 — Character of a Citizen; the
learner needs the world of home, school and community, which they already have, and
nothing else is forced before it.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked
below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every
slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the
student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while
the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot
and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule.
GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the Grade 7–8 lessons of this title — the virtues and the good
citizen. This lesson stands on them and names the study they all belong to.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Define ethics: the discipline concerned with right and wrong, good and bad,
acceptable and unacceptable conduct.
2. Tell morality (the standards themselves) from ethics (the reflective study and
practice of them).
3. Name what ethics obliges: refraining from stealing, fraud, assault and slander;
binding in honesty, compassion, loyalty and rights.
4. Tell the X, Y and Z cheating case in summary and judge it.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else.

## If the learner is stuck
- If ethics and morality are used as one word, use the map: morality is the standards;
ethics is studying them and practising them. If the case is judged only by the rule
broken, widen it — Y also protected trust, friendship and the meaning of results.
