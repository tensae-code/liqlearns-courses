# What Ethics Is — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Define ethics in one sentence.
**Answer:** The discipline concerned with right and wrong, good and bad, acceptable and
unacceptable conduct.

**2.** Name two rights ethics binds a person in.
**Answer:** Any two of: life, privacy, freedom from injury.

**3.** What did Y call cheating?
**Answer:** Serious academic dishonesty.
