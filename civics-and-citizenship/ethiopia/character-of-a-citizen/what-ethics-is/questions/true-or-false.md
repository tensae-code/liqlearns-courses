# What Ethics Is — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1.** Ethics is exactly the same thing as law.
**Answer: False** — The guide distinguishes ethics from law, custom and habit.

**2.** Ethics binds a person in virtues such as honesty, compassion and loyalty.
**Answer: True** — Named in the lesson.

**3.** Y helped Z cheat because they were best friends.
**Answer: False** — Y refused, angrily.
