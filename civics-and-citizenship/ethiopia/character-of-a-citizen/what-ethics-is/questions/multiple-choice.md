# What Ethics Is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Ethics is —
a) a list of school rules  b) the discipline concerned with right and wrong, good and
bad, acceptable and unacceptable conduct  c) the study of plants  d) a synonym for law
**Answer: b) the discipline concerned with right and wrong, good and bad, acceptable and
unacceptable conduct** — The lesson's definition.

**2.** Morality and ethics differ as —
a) nothing at all  b) morality names the standards; ethics is the reflective study and
practice of them  c) ethics is older  d) morality is for adults only
**Answer: b) morality names the standards; ethics is the reflective study and practice
of them** — Map, and walking by it.

**3.** The word ethics comes from the Greek ethos, meaning —
a) custom  b) speed  c) wealth  d) examination
**Answer: a) custom** — Ethos — custom.

**4.** Ethics obliges a person to refrain from —
a) stealing, fraud, assault and slander  b) asking questions  c) helping friends  d)
studying
**Answer: a) stealing, fraud, assault and slander** — The lesson's four.

**5.** In the case, Y refuses because —
a) he dislikes Z  b) cheating is serious academic dishonesty — and he warns the
friendship will end  c) his phone is broken  d) X asked him to refuse
**Answer: b) cheating is serious academic dishonesty — and he warns the friendship will
end** — Y's refusal, as told.

**6.** The case ends with Z —
a) cheating successfully  b) repenting, apologising and promising to work hard  c)
leaving school  d) reporting Y
**Answer: b) repenting, apologising and promising to work hard** — Z repents and
promises to work hard.
