# Hands-on — What Ethics Is

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Three pupils, one bench
Draw the exam bench: three pupils, one phone being offered, one pupil turning away.
Label each figure X, Y, Z and write Y's refusal under the drawing.

## Map and walking
On one sheet draw a map (morality — the standards) and a pair of footprints walking by
it (ethics — the study and practice). Label both.
