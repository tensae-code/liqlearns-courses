# Hands-on — Virtues — courage

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The spider's wall
On a wall chart, draw a cave wall and a thread. Mark each failed climb with a small
cross and the successful one with a star. Count them aloud together.

## Outward courage
Role-play the two scenes: a friend mocked by a group; a stranger dropping litter at a
shared place. Practise words that are brave and kind at once.
