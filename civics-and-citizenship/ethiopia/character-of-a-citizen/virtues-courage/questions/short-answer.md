# Virtues — courage — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What did the spider do that taught the king?
**Answer:** It failed again and again to climb the wall on its thread, kept climbing,
and succeeded — so the king took heart and tried again.

**2.** Define courage in one sentence.
**Answer:** Facing what is difficult or painful instead of avoiding it — persistence
without a shortcut, never losing heart after failure.

**3.** Name the inventor in this lesson and his year.
**Answer:** Edison — the light bulb of 1879, after many failed attempts.
