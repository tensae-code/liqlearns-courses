# Virtues — courage — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1.** Courage means a person never fails.
**Answer: False** — Bruce and the spider both failed repeatedly — courage is the next
attempt.

**2.** The spider's success gave the king back the will to try again.
**Answer: True** — That is the story's point.

**3.** Courage is only for soldiers and kings.
**Answer: False** — It is practised at school size: defending a friend, challenging
littering.
