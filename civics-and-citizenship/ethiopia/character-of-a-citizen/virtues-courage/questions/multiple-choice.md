# Virtues — courage — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Courage is —
a) never feeling fear  b) facing what is difficult or painful instead of avoiding it  c)
being the strongest person  d) winning every fight
**Answer: b) facing what is difficult or painful instead of avoiding it** — Facing
difficulty, not avoiding it.

**2.** The mark of courage in this lesson is —
a) a shortcut to success  b) persistence without a shortcut, never losing heart after
failure  c) giving up quietly  d) blaming others
**Answer: b) persistence without a shortcut, never losing heart after failure** —
Persistence without a shortcut.

**3.** Edison's story teaches that —
a) he succeeded on his first try  b) many failed attempts came before the 1879 light
bulb  c) failure ends everything  d) invention is luck
**Answer: b) many failed attempts came before the 1879 light bulb** — Failures were the
road to the bulb.

**4.** King Robert Bruce watched a spider —
a) sleep all day  b) fail again and again to climb, then succeed  c) build a web for
food  d) frighten the English
**Answer: b) fail again and again to climb, then succeed** — Fail, climb, fail, climb —
then succeed.

**5.** Bruce succeeded on his —
a) first try  b) third attempt  c) seventh attempt  d) never
**Answer: c) seventh attempt** — The seventh attempt, as the story is told.

**6.** An outward act of courage at school is —
a) defending a mocked friend  b) mocking someone weaker  c) dropping litter  d) hiding
from work
**Answer: a) defending a mocked friend** — The lesson's role plays name it.
