# Applied Ethics — Professional and Environmental — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Recite the eight shared professional traits.
**Answer:** Honesty, integrity, transparency, confidentiality, impartiality,
punctuality, respectfulness, obedience to the law.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why is environmental care a matter of survival?
**Answer:** Because the environment is the foundation of all survival: a safe
environment means a healthy life, a damaged one damages us — and resources are finite.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Define a profession in the lesson's terms.
**Answer:** Work needing advanced expertise, self-regulation and service to the public
good, carrying a social contract of competence, integrity, altruism and accountability.
