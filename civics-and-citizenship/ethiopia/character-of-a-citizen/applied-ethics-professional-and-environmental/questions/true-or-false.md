# Applied Ethics — Professional and Environmental — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Professional ethics includes confidentiality and impartiality.
**Answer: True** — Both are among the eight traits.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Earth's resources are infinite, so care can wait.
**Answer: False** — Resources are finite; degradation is worsening.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The clean-up task is: photograph, clean, photograph again,
compare.
**Answer: True** — The doing lesson's own sequence.
