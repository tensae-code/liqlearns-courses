# Applied Ethics — Professional and Environmental — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Applied ethics reflects on moral dilemmas —
a) in the abstract only  b) inside particular social practices  c) in examinations  d)
in fiction
**Answer: b) inside particular social practices** — Particular practices: medical,
business, environmental…

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A profession carries a social contract of —
a) competence, integrity, altruism and accountability  b) secrecy and profit  c) rank
and privilege  d) speed and silence
**Answer: a) competence, integrity, altruism and accountability** — The four contract
words.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Which set is the eight shared traits?
a) honesty, integrity, transparency, confidentiality, impartiality, punctuality,
respectfulness, obedience to the law  b) wealth, fame, power, luck, height, age, accent,
dress  c) silence, distance, pride, speed, caution, humour, strength, memory  d) none of
these
**Answer: a) honesty, integrity, transparency, confidentiality, impartiality,
punctuality, respectfulness, obedience to the law** — The eight, as listed.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The lesson's field task is to interview —
a) three teachers about their professional ethics  b) three traders  c) one official
only  d) no one
**Answer: a) three teachers about their professional ethics** — Three teachers.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Environmental ethics is the moral obligation to —
a) use resources first  b) protect and maintain the environment's well-being  c) study
plants only  d) build more roads
**Answer: b) protect and maintain the environment's well-being** — Protection as
obligation.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Care for the environment, in the book's emphasis, is —
a) a matter of taste  b) not a matter of choice, rather a matter of survival  c) only
for farmers  d) postponable forever
**Answer: b) not a matter of choice, rather a matter of survival** — Survival, not
choice.
