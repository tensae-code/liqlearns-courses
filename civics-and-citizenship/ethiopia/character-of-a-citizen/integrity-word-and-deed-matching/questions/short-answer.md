# Integrity — Word and Deed Matching — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Name any four of the eight marks.
**Answer:** Any four of: respecting humanity regardless of race, ethnicity, language,
religion or birthplace; walking one's talk; honesty; taking responsibility; kindness
without harmful jealousy or exploiting others; knowing one's strengths and weaknesses;
hard work; freedom from addiction.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** How is integrity built? Name the three ways.
**Answer:** Making and keeping commitments to oneself; standing for known values;
staying open in mind, heart and ears.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Across which six fields does integrity spread?
**Answer:** Humanity, self, family, society, religion and country.
