# Integrity — Word and Deed Matching — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Taking responsibility for one's actions is framed as a ladder to
success, not a loss.
**Answer: True** — Mark four, as taught.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** A promise made to oneself — like quitting smoking — does not
really bind.
**Answer: False** — It binds an audience of one, and the binding is real.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A person of integrity behaves one way in private and another in
public.
**Answer: False** — Word and deed align in both.
