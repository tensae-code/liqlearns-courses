# Integrity — Word and Deed Matching — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Integrity is —
a) saying fine words  b) devotion to moral principles — what a person says and does
align, in private and in public  c) being popular  d) never making promises
**Answer: b) devotion to moral principles — what a person says and does align, in
private and in public** — Word and deed matching.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The book says integrity in today's Ethiopia is —
a) optional  b) a responsibility, not a choice  c) only for officials  d) a private
taste
**Answer: b) a responsibility, not a choice** — Responsibility, not choice.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Ms. X donated her building as a Covid-19 treatment centre, saying
—
a) rent is everything  b) lives outweigh rent; humanity is measured by kindness  c) the
city owed her  d) nothing at all
**Answer: b) lives outweigh rent; humanity is measured by kindness** — Her words matched
her deed.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is one of the eight marks?
a) knowing one's strengths and weaknesses  b) avoiding all work  c) keeping every
promise vague  d) jealousy used well
**Answer: a) knowing one's strengths and weaknesses** — Mark six, as listed.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Bulcha, tempted by contraband profit, —
a) took it quietly  b) refused, paid his taxes honestly and kept public trust  c) blamed
Alemu  d) left the country
**Answer: b) refused, paid his taxes honestly and kept public trust** — The honest
importer.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Never promise —
a) at all  b) beyond your power to deliver  c) to family  d) before noon
**Answer: b) beyond your power to deliver** — The guard rule of promise-keeping.
