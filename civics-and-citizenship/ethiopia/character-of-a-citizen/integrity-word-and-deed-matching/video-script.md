# Video script — Integrity — Word and Deed Matching

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md,
add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form
until then.

This lesson's script is a read-aloud of its story or case, in plot summary only.

---

**Integrity — Word and Deed Matching.**

Integrity — what you say and what you do, matching, in private and in public — its eight
marks, and how it is grown.

Integrity is devotion to moral principles in decisions and actions: what a person says
and what a person does align — in private and in public.

Such people are known as trustworthy, honest and kind.

The book insists on its weight: integrity in today's Ethiopia is a responsibility, not a
choice.

It spreads across six fields — humanity, self, family, society, religion and country —
and judgement inside it is guided by values rather than personal interest.

Its case is told in summary.

Ms.

X of Addis Ababa gave up the high rent on her building and donated it as a Covid-19
treatment centre, saying lives outweigh rent, and humanity is measured by kindness, not
self-indulgence.

Her words about humanity and her deed with the building are the same sentence — that
matching is integrity.

A person of integrity can be recognised.

The book lists eight marks: (1) respecting humanity regardless of race, ethnicity,
language, religion or birthplace; (2) walking one's talk; (3) honesty — with honest tax-
paying as the worked example; (4) taking responsibility for one's actions, framed as a
ladder to success, not a loss; (5) kindness without harmful jealousy or exploiting
others; (6) knowing one's strengths and weaknesses; (7) hard work as the only relief
from poverty; and (8) freedom from addiction, substance — alcohol, cigarettes, khat,
drugs — and non-substance, gambling, alike.

Now you. Tell your companion the definition of integrity, any four of the eight marks,
and the Lemlem story. Finish with the promise rule: never promise beyond your power to
deliver.
