# Hands-on — Integrity — Word and Deed Matching

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Eight badges
Draw one figure and eight labelled portrait badges around it, one per mark. Under the
figure write: word and deed matching.

## Keys handed over
Draw a building doorway becoming a treatment centre, keys passing hand to hand. Label it
with Ms. X's reason: lives outweigh rent.
