# Lesson plan — Integrity — Word and Deed Matching

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Character of a Citizen ·
**Lesson:** Integrity — Word and Deed Matching
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 14 — Character of a Citizen (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** integrity — what you say and what you do,
matching, in private and in public — its eight marks, and how it is grown.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands inside Level 14 — Character of a Citizen; the
learner needs the world of home, school and community, which they already have, and
nothing else is forced before it.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked
below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every
slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the
student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while
the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot
and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule.
GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
1. **What integrity is** — part 1 of this lesson, in the order the books teach it.
2. **The eight marks of integrity** — part 2 of this lesson, in the order the books
teach it.
3. **Growing integrity and keeping promises** — part 3 of this lesson, in the order the
books teach it.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in
questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: What Ethics Is, the lesson before this one. Integrity is the first
and highest of the values that lesson's study names.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Define integrity: devotion to moral principles, word and deed aligned in private and
in public.
2. Recite the eight marks of a person of integrity.
3. Tell the Ms. X, Alemu/Bulcha and Lemlem/Abdi cases in summary and judge them.
4. Say how integrity is grown: making and keeping commitments, standing for known
values, staying open in mind, heart and ears.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else.

## If the learner is stuck
- If the eight marks blur into a list to memorise, anchor each to the cases: tax honesty
to Bulcha, responsibility to the contrast with Alemu, promise-keeping to Lemlem. Marks
are recognised in stories before they are recalled in lists.
