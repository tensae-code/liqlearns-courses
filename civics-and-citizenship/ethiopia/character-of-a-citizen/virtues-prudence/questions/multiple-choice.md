# Virtues — prudence — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Prudence is —
a) luck  b) practical wisdom: care, caution, good judgement and foresight  c) acting
fastest  d) never deciding
**Answer: b) practical wisdom: care, caution, good judgement and foresight** — The
lesson's definition.

**2.** Before acting, the prudent person —
a) closes their eyes  b) pauses to examine consequences  c) copies the crowd  d) asks
for a shortcut
**Answer: b) pauses to examine consequences** — Examine consequences first.

**3.** The prudent person asks —
a) what is easiest?  b) what is the right and kind thing?  c) who will praise me?  d)
what happened yesterday on the road?
**Answer: b) what is the right and kind thing?** — Right and kind, not only fast.

**4.** Past mistakes, for the prudent person, are —
a) hidden forever  b) signposts — lessons learned from  c) someone else's fault  d)
forgotten by rule
**Answer: b) signposts — lessons learned from** — Learned from, not repeated blindly.

**5.** Prudence plans for —
a) today only  b) short and long goals alike  c) other people only  d) nothing
**Answer: b) short and long goals alike** — Both horizons are named.

**6.** The traveller on the rope bridge shows prudence by —
a) running across  b) testing each plank before crossing  c) refusing all bridges  d)
jumping the river
**Answer: b) testing each plank before crossing** — Testing is how the crossing gets
made.
