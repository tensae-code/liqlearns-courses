# Virtues — prudence — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the three habits inside the prudent pause.
**Answer:** Think before deciding; ask what the right and kind thing is; learn from past
mistakes.

**2.** What two regrets does prudence help you avoid?
**Answer:** Needless risk, and the later regret of unexamined action.

**3.** At the fork, which path does prudence examine first — and how?
**Answer:** Both — it weighs each path's consequences (the bridgeless river crossing
against the bridge) before choosing.
