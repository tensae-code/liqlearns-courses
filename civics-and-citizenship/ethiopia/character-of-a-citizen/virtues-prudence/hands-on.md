# Hands-on — Virtues — prudence

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The fork
Draw the fork: river without a bridge one way, bridge the other. In a thought bubble
above the standing figure, write both paths' consequences.

## Plank by plank
Role-play the traveller: one learner crosses an imaginary rope bridge, testing each
plank aloud — naming a risk and a check at each step — while the class calls the
consequences.
