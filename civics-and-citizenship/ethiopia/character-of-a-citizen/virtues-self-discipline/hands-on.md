# Hands-on — Virtues — self-discipline

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## One desk, in order
Arrange a real desk: alarm clock, closed game box, open book — in that order. Photograph
it in your mind; recreate it each study day this week.

## The two lists
On one sheet, two columns: 'when I governed myself' and 'what it cost when I did not'.
Fill both from this week's real events, at home and in class.
