# Virtues — self-discipline — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Define self-discipline in one sentence.
**Answer:** Following acceptable rules and standards freely, on one's own initiative,
with no external control.

**2.** Name two things self-discipline keeps.
**Answer:** Focus on goals; mastery of one's reactions.

**3.** Where does the lesson ask you to audit self-discipline?
**Answer:** At home, in class and in the community.
