# Virtues — self-discipline — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Self-discipline is following rules and standards —
a) only when watched  b) freely, on one's own initiative, with no external control  c)
out of fear of punishment  d) when friends insist
**Answer: b) freely, on one's own initiative, with no external control** — Freely, from
inside.

**2.** Its portrait is —
a) denying yourself an extravagant pleasure for a harder charitable deed  b) taking
every pleasure available  c) obeying the loudest voice  d) never resting
**Answer: a) denying yourself an extravagant pleasure for a harder charitable deed** —
The swap the lesson paints.

**3.** Self-discipline keeps a person —
a) asleep  b) focused on goals and master of their reactions  c) free of all rules  d)
angry more slowly at others only
**Answer: b) focused on goals and master of their reactions** — Focus and mastery of
reactions.

**4.** On the lesson's desk, the right order is —
a) game, sweets, book  b) alarm clock, closed game box, open book  c) book, clock, game
d) sweets first
**Answer: b) alarm clock, closed game box, open book** — Clock, game closed, book open.

**5.** Self-discipline makes which things possible, teachers say?
a) study, saving and promise-keeping  b) excuses  c) forgetting  d) boasting
**Answer: a) study, saving and promise-keeping** — Named in the guide's telling.

**6.** The lesson's audit lists self-discipline's presence and cost —
a) only at school  b) at home, in class and in the community  c) only in sport  d)
nowhere
**Answer: b) at home, in class and in the community** — Three places are named.
