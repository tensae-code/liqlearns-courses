# Peace and Cooperation — Each Needs the Other

## Do it first

Watch the woven ring: strand laid over strand until the circle closes. Which strand could be removed without breaking the ring? Think about that while you read — it is the whole lesson in one picture.

## Each needs the other

Peace is kept by people working together. Where people cooperate for peace, peace holds; war and conflict are what come from failing to work together for peace. And the other way round: cooperation itself needs peace, because people at war cannot work together. Each one is the other's keeper — like strands of one ring, neither holds alone.

## The values gathered

The values of peaceful behaviour are: respect, understanding, patience, sharing, discussion, and settling conflict peacefully. The values of cooperation are: brotherhood, sincerity, openness, and thinking of others. Learn both lists — they are the toolkit the whole level has been building toward.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion why peace and cooperation each need the other. Then list the values of peaceful behaviour and the values of cooperation from memory, as many as you can.

## Do It Yourself

1. Practise one peaceful-behaviour value today: patience, sharing, or a peaceful settlement.
2. Practise one cooperation value today: openness, or thinking of others first.
3. Write both value lists from memory tomorrow and check them.
