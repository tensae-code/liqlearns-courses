# Peace and Cooperation — Each Needs the Other — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Peace is kept by —
a) everyone acting alone  b) ignoring all conflict  c) people working together
**Answer: c) people working together**

**2.** War and conflict come from —
a) sharing too openly  b) failing to work together for peace  c) too much discussion
**Answer: b) failing to work together for peace**

**3.** Which belongs to peaceful behaviour?
a) Patience  b) Suspicion  c) Revenge
**Answer: a) Patience**

**4.** Which belongs to cooperation?
a) Keeping every idea secret  b) Brotherhood's opposite — division  c) Thinking of others
**Answer: c) Thinking of others**
