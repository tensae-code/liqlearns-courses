Peace is kept by people working together.

**Each needs the other.** Peace is kept by people working together.

**The values gathered.** The values of peaceful behaviour are: respect, understanding, patience, sharing, discussion, and settling conflict peacefully.

Now you. Tell your companion why peace and cooperation each need the other.

And this week: holding both lists of values — peaceful behaviour and cooperation — in daily use.
