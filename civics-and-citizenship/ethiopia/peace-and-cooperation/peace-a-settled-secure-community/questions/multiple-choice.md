# Peace — A Settled, Secure Community — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which is one of the three definitions of peace?
a) The presence of security and order in a society  b) The victory of the stronger side  c) Everyone staying indoors
**Answer: a) The presence of security and order in a society**

**2.** A community that is quiet only because it is afraid —
a) is the perfect picture of peace  b) needs nothing more  c) does not hold the whole of peace
**Answer: c) does not hold the whole of peace**

**3.** Pupils share in solving community problems such as —
a) nothing — problems belong to adults alone  b) early marriage and violence  c) only their homework problems
**Answer: b) early marriage and violence**

**4.** The cost of peace's absence includes —
a) conflict, war and loss of freedom  b) too much free time  c) quieter markets
**Answer: a) conflict, war and loss of freedom**
