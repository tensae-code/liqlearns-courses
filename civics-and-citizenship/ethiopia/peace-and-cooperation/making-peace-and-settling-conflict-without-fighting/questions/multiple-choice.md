# Making Peace and Settling Conflict Without Fighting — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Peacemaking is —
a) waiting for conflicts to end themselves  b) taking the stronger side  c) work done on purpose, not bystanding
**Answer: c) work done on purpose, not bystanding**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The peaceful way through conflict is —
a) never mentioning the problem again  b) talking, listening, and finding a settlement both sides accept  c) fighting until one side gives up
**Answer: b) talking, listening, and finding a settlement both sides accept**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A settlement both sides accept —
a) lasts, because neither side is waiting to fight again  b) never works in real life  c) is a sign of weakness
**Answer: a) lasts, because neither side is waiting to fight again**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Which helps build peace before conflict starts?
a) Keeping a list of everyone's faults  b) Stirring others' troubles  c) Treating people fairly so grievances do not pile up
**Answer: c) Treating people fairly so grievances do not pile up**
