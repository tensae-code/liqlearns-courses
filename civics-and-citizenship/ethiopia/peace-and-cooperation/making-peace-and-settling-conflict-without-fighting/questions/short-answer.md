# Making Peace and Settling Conflict Without Fighting — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the steps of settling a conflict peacefully.
**Answer:** Each side says what happened and what they need; each side listens; together they find a settlement both sides can accept.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two ways peace is built and kept before conflict comes.
**Answer:** Any two of: speak before anger grows, treat people fairly, apologise when wrong, help others settle troubles.
