# Making Peace and Settling Conflict Without Fighting — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Listening is part of settling conflict, not only talking.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A forced victory settles a conflict permanently.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Apologising when you are wrong is peacemaking work.
**Answer: True**
