# Video script — Making Peace and Settling Conflict Without Fighting

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Two children, one ball, one quarrel. Watch how it is settled without fighting.

CHILD 1: You took my turn!
CHILD 2: No — the turn was mine!

They do not push. They do not shout louder. A third child steps in — a peacemaker.

PEACEMAKER: Stop. Let us talk. One at a time. You first — what happened?
CHILD 1: I was waiting. He ran in front of me.
PEACEMAKER: Now you. What happened?
CHILD 2: I thought the turn was finished. I did not see her waiting.

See it: each side has spoken. Now each side listens.

PEACEMAKER: What do you need?
CHILD 1: My turn, fairly.
CHILD 2: A turn too — and to know when it comes.

PEACEMAKER: Then here is a settlement. This turn is hers — she was waiting. The next turn is yours. And after that, we take turns in order. Can you both accept it?

CHILD 1: Yes.
CHILD 2: Yes.

Talking. Listening. A settlement both sides can accept.
That is how conflict is settled — without fighting.
