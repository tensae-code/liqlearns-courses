# What Happens When There Is No Peace — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1.** Name three effects of the absence of peace.
**Answer:** Any three of: fear in people's daily lives, schools emptying / learning failing, trading and work stopping, neighbours no longer trusting or helping each other, shared life breaking down.

**2.** Why should a person guard peace before it is lost?
**Answer:** Because its absence costs so much — and peace is built and kept by small acts done in time.
