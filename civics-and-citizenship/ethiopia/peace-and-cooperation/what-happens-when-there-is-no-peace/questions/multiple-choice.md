# What Happens When There Is No Peace — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** When peace is absent, learning suffers because —
a) frightened pupils cannot study and schools empty  b) pupils suddenly learn faster  c) teachers no longer matter
**Answer: a) frightened pupils cannot study and schools empty**

**2.** On a community's life, no peace means —
a) more shared work gets done  b) celebrations grow  c) trading stops and neighbours stop trusting each other
**Answer: c) trading stops and neighbours stop trusting each other**

**3.** In a person, fear takes the place of —
a) appetite only  b) the settled mind  c) sleep only
**Answer: b) the settled mind**

**4.** This lesson stands in the course to show —
a) why peace is worth building and guarding  b) that conflict is entertaining  c) that peace does not matter much
**Answer: a) why peace is worth building and guarding**
