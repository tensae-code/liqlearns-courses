# Peace — What It Is and Why It Matters

## Do it first

Sit quietly for one minute. Notice what a settled, unthreatened minute feels like in your own body. Then look around and name three ordinary good things happening around you that peace makes possible.

## Sub lesson 1 — Peace — what it is

Peace is the settled, unthreatened state of a person and of a community. Inside a person, peace is a mind at rest, not braced for trouble. In a community, peace is people living their ordinary lives — trading, working, playing, learning — without fear walking beside them. Peace is not just silence after noise; it is the settled condition itself.

## Sub lesson 2 — Why peace matters

Peace matters everywhere life happens. At home, peace lets a family eat, rest and talk without fear. At school, peace lets pupils learn — a frightened mind cannot study. Between neighbours, peace lets people help each other, share work and settle small troubles with words. Take peace away, and every one of these goods becomes hard or impossible.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what peace is, then one reason it matters at home, one at school, and one between neighbours.

## Do It Yourself

1. Name three ordinary things in your home that peace makes possible.
2. Do one small thing today that keeps your classroom settled — a calm voice, a fair turn, a quick apology.
3. Write one sentence: 'Without peace, ___ would be hard because ___.'
