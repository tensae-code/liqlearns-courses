# Peace — What It Is and Why It Matters — short answer

Answer in your own words, in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Define peace in your own words.
**Answer:** The settled, unthreatened state of a person and of a community.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give two places where peace matters and say why.
**Answer:** Any two of: home (rest, talk, safety), school (learning needs an unafraid mind), between neighbours (help and settling troubles with words).
