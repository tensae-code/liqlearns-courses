# Peace — What It Is and Why It Matters — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Peace is —
a) the settled, unthreatened state of a person and a community  b) winning every argument  c) silence because everyone is afraid to speak
**Answer: a) the settled, unthreatened state of a person and a community**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does peace matter at school?
a) Peace means no lessons are taught  b) Peace lets pupils skip work  c) A frightened mind cannot study — peace lets pupils learn
**Answer: c) A frightened mind cannot study — peace lets pupils learn**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Between neighbours, peace makes it possible to —
a) take each other's property  b) help each other and settle troubles with words  c) never speak to each other again
**Answer: b) help each other and settle troubles with words**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Peace inside a person is —
a) a mind at rest, not braced for trouble  b) never caring about anything  c) always getting your own way
**Answer: a) a mind at rest, not braced for trouble**
