# Peace — What It Is and Why It Matters — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Peace is more than silence after noise — it is the settled condition itself.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Learning goes just as well when pupils are frightened.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Peace matters at home, at school and between neighbours.
**Answer: True**
