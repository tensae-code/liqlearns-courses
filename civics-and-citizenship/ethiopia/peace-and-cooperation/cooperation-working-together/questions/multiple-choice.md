# Cooperation — Working Together — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Cooperation makes tasks —
a) impossible to finish  b) easier and more enjoyable  c) slower in every case
**Answer: b) easier and more enjoyable**

**2.** Working together does what to people's bonds?
a) Strengthens them  b) Weakens them  c) Has no effect on them
**Answer: a) Strengthens them**

**3.** Which is a classroom form of cooperation?
a) Hiding your notes  b) Refusing group work  c) Sharing ideas
**Answer: c) Sharing ideas**

**4.** Pupils should take part in —
a) only games  b) decisions that affect them  c) nothing at all
**Answer: b) decisions that affect them**
