# Video script — Ethiopia and Its Region

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing: in its region, Ethiopia's foreign policy works three fields: cooperation over shared rivers and through IGAD, peace and security against terror and civil war, and the trade, oil and power flows that bind the Horn's economies together.
Rivers cross borders without passports.
A dam rises on the Abay — negotiate.
Blue helmets stand guard so markets reopen.
Oil flows in. Power flows out.
One region, bound together — that is the policy at work.
