# Hands-on — Ethiopia and Its Region

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Rivers across borders
On a large floor map, blue ribbons run the named rivers from Ethiopia outward — Nile system, Shebelle system, Omo — with the dam marked on the Abay. Learners walk a river and narrate every border it crosses and what fair use must mean there.

## Peacekeepers' market
A market scene is set: shuttered stalls. Ethiopian peacekeepers (blue-helmet cards) take their posts at the edges; stall by stall, the market reopens as security holds. The class names what reopened first, and why it was the market.
