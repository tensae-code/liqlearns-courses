# Ethiopia and Its Region — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How many states does the books' Horn list name?
a) Six  b) Seven  c) Eight  d) Ten
**Answer: c) Eight**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** IGAD is the key regional institution for —
a) sport  b) peace, security and economic integration  c) a single currency only  d) tourism
**Answer: b) peace, security and economic integration**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Ethiopia saves millions yearly importing oil from —
a) Kenya  b) Sudan  c) Somalia  d) Eritrea
**Answer: b) Sudan**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Ethiopia exports electric power to —
a) Egypt only  b) Djibouti, Sudan and Kenya  c) Uganda only  d) no one
**Answer: b) Djibouti, Sudan and Kenya**
