# Lesson plan — Disadvantages of Globalization

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Ethiopia and the World · **Lesson:** Disadvantages of Globalization
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 25 — Ethiopia and the World (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** globalization's harms run through culture and politics, economy and security — languages fading, economies marginalised, and four insecurities: personal, job and income, health, environmental — all governed by one frame: outcomes follow how governments manage the web.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the foreign-relations lessons earlier in this level — the learner knows how Ethiopia deals with the world; this part teaches the world Ethiopia deals in: globalization, its dimensions, gains, harms and answers, and the global issues that test every nation.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Culture and politics** — The first harm set: identity thinning under imported culture; pressure and fragmentation in politics; the management frame over all.
2. **Economy and security** — The second harm set: marginalised economies, drained skills, suppressed home technology — and insecurity in its four forms.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Advantages of Globalization, just before this lesson in this level. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Give the cultural and political harms.
2. Give the economic harms and the four insecurities.
3. State the governing frame: management decides.

## If the learner is stuck
- Teaches harms as globalization's verdict → the governing frame stands over both lessons: outcomes follow how governments manage the web. Harms are the unmanaged case, not the only case.
- Merges the four insecurities → personal (crime networks), job and income (precarious work), health (travelling disease), environmental (emissions, depletion, pollution). Four forms, four mechanisms.
