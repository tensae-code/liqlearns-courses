# Disadvantages of Globalization — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Homogenisation means —
a) local languages and practices disappearing as imported cultures replace them  b) cultures trading fairly  c) one language chosen freely  d) festivals multiplying
**Answer: a) local languages and practices disappearing as imported cultures replace them**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The book's Ethiopian cultural evidence includes —
a) young people preferring Western fast food and clothes, mixing English into local languages, foreign names for children  b) rural depopulation  c) falling coffee prices  d) new highways
**Answer: a) young people preferring Western fast food and clothes, mixing English into local languages, foreign names for children**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Fragmentation weakens —
a) national consciousness and cohesion, facilitating authoritarian rule  b) trade volumes  c) language diversity  d) nothing
**Answer: a) national consciousness and cohesion, facilitating authoritarian rule**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Developing economies are marginalised by dependence on —
a) a few primary goods whose demand and prices are set externally  b) too many exports  c) their own industries  d) regional trade
**Answer: a) a few primary goods whose demand and prices are set externally**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Imported capital-intensive production tends to —
a) suppress indigenous technology, worsening unemployment and poverty  b) create local skills  c) lower all prices fairly  d) protect crafts
**Answer: a) suppress indigenous technology, worsening unemployment and poverty**
