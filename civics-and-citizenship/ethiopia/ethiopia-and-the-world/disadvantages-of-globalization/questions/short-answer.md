# Disadvantages of Globalization — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** State the governing frame over both ledgers.
**Answer:** Whether globalization brings advantages or disadvantages follows how different governments manage it.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the four insecurities with one mechanism each.
**Answer:** Personal — crime networks harvesting deregulated capital, ICT and cheap transport (drugs, women, weapons; laundered money). Job and income — restructuring and dismantled protection leaving precarious work. Health — travel and migration spreading disease. Environmental — emissions, depletion, deforestation, pollution and accelerated climate change.
