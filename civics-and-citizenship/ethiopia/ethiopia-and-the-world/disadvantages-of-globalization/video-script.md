# Video script — Disadvantages of Globalization

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing: globalization's harms run through culture and politics, economy and security — languages fading, economies marginalised, and four insecurities: personal, job and income, health, environmental — all governed by one frame: outcomes follow how governments manage the web.
The loss side of the balance.\nLanguages fading. Economies marginalised.\nFour insecurities: crime, precarious work, travelling disease, a warming world.\nAnd over it all: outcomes follow how governments manage the web.
