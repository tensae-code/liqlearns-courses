# Hands-on — Disadvantages of Globalization

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Four warning signs
Four sign-boards are raised — factory gate, wallet, clinic, smokestack. Learners bring case cards and must pin each under its insecurity, defending the pin from the lesson's mechanisms. A card fitting two signs is pinned between them, honestly.

## The street, debated
Two stalls face off: the clothes stall and the fast-food sign. Each side argues from the book — gains admitted, losses named. A third team holds the management frame and rules what the street's future depends on.
