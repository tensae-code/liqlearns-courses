# Video script — Global Issues — Work, Disease, Terror

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing: the last tour stops at three signposts — unemployment that wastes people and destabilises societies, disease that travels with the travellers, and terrorism, which the book defines by its targets: violence against non-combatants to force a political cause on the world's attention.
Three signposts on one road.\nA chained factory gate — work denied, dignity denied.\nA climbing clinic chart — disease travelling with the travellers.\nA cordoned street — violence against the uninvolved,\nto force a cause upon the world's attention.
