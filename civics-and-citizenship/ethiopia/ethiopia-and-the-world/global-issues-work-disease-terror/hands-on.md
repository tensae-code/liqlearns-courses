# Hands-on — Global Issues — Work, Disease, Terror

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Three signposts
Three signposts stand along a taped road. Learners walk it in pairs; at each signpost they must speak that issue's book-definition before passing. The road is only finished when all three definitions have been spoken correctly by every pair.

## Define it exactly
The definition is dealt as word-cards, shuffled, with four intruder cards. Teams reassemble the book's definition exactly — targets and three purposes in place — and must reject the intruders aloud, saying why each does not belong.
