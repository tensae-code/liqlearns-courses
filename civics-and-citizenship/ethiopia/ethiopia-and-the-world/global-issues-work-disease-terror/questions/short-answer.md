# Global Issues — Work, Disease, Terror — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Give the book's definition of terrorism.
**Answer:** Violence against non-combatants, civilians or other normally illegitimate targets, to attract attention to a political cause, force the aloof to join, or intimidate opponents into concessions.

**2.** Why is terrorism hard to define, per the book?
**Answer:** It is inherently political: one side's terrorism is the actor's independence, liberation, struggle, jihad, self-defence or revolution — and the word is rarely applied to one's own governments.
