# Global Issues — Work, Disease, Terror — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Unemployment is —
a) looking for work and not finding it  b) choosing rest  c) working informally  d) studying full-time
**Answer: a) looking for work and not finding it**

**2.** High youth unemployment stands behind —
a) conflicts, uprisings and unrest  b) higher savings  c) stable families  d) falling crime
**Answer: a) conflicts, uprisings and unrest**

**3.** Meaningful employment gives —
a) dignity, a place in the community, character and developed human potential  b) only wages  c) only status  d) nothing beyond survival
**Answer: a) dignity, a place in the community, character and developed human potential**
