# Lesson plan — Global Issues — Work, Disease, Terror

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Ethiopia and the World · **Lesson:** Global Issues — Work, Disease, Terror
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 25 — Ethiopia and the World (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the last tour stops at three signposts — unemployment that wastes people and destabilises societies, disease that travels with the travellers, and terrorism, which the book defines by its targets: violence against non-combatants to force a political cause on the world's attention.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the foreign-relations lessons earlier in this level — the learner knows how Ethiopia deals with the world; this part teaches the world Ethiopia deals in: globalization, its dimensions, gains, harms and answers, and the global issues that test every nation.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. ## If the learner is stuck
- Defines terrorism by the actor's cause → the book defines it by targets and purposes: violence against non-combatants to attract attention, force the aloof to join, or intimidate into concessions — whatever the cause claims.
- Treats unemployment as a personal failing → the book's accounting is societal: wasted skills, concentrated wealth, destabilised societies, exclusion's long shadow — and dignity as employment's positive gift.
