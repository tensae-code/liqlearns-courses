# Video script — Foreign Relations — What They Are

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing: no state lives alone: a state's external relations with the world's countries, undertaken to fulfil and ensure its national interests, are its foreign relations — and Ethiopia's are anchored in Article 86 of its constitution.
No person lives alone. No state lives alone.
States reach outward — for their interests.
Peace. Development. Influence.
Ethiopia's reaching is anchored in its constitution.
That reaching has a name: foreign relations.
