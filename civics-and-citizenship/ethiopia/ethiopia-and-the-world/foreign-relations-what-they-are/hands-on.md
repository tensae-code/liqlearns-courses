# Hands-on — Foreign Relations — What They Are

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The web of handshakes
Learners stand as Ethiopia and its named neighbours — Eritrea, Kenya, Sudan, South Sudan, Somalia, Djibouti. Strings join hands for each real relation the class can name: rivers, trade, power, peacekeeping. The web is photographed in words: who holds the most strings, and why?

## Three chairs
Three chairs are placed at a table lettered Peace, Development, Influence. Learners bring one real Ethiopian interest to the right chair and defend the seating.
