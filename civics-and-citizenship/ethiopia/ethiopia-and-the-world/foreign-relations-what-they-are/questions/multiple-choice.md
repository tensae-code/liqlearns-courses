# Foreign Relations — What They Are — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The argument begins from Aristotle's saying that man is —
a) a political economist  b) a social animal  c) a born ruler  d) self-sufficient
**Answer: b) a social animal**

**2.** Foreign relations are a state's external relations undertaken to —
a) impress tourists  b) fulfil and ensure its national interests  c) avoid neighbours  d) sell flags
**Answer: b) fulfil and ensure its national interests**

**3.** Ethiopia's foreign relations are anchored in —
a) Article 86 of the 1995 FDRE Constitution  b) a party manifesto  c) custom alone  d) the market
**Answer: a) Article 86 of the 1995 FDRE Constitution**
