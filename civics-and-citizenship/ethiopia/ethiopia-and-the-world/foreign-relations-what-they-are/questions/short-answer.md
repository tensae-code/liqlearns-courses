# Foreign Relations — What They Are — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name Ethiopia's three interests as the evidence itemises them.
**Answer:** Peace and security; socio-economic development; global influence.

**2.** Give two more of Article 86's anchors besides non-interference.
**Answer:** Any two of: protection of national interests and sovereignty; mutual benefit and equality of states; observance of international agreements; emphasis on mutual economic development, peaceful coexistence and strong public relations with neighbours and other African states.
