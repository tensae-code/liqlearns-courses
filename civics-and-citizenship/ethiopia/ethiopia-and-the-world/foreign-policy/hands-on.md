# Hands-on — Foreign Policy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Chart the voyage
The class, as one state's council, charts a voyage: destination (an interest), chart (facts), compass (values), and three hazards (other states' decisions). The chart is drawn and defended.

## Drift and steer
Two paper boats are raced across a basin of water — one pushed at random, one guided by a straw's breath along a marked course. The class names what the breath-and-course stand for.
