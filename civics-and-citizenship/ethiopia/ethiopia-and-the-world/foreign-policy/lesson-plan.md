# Lesson plan — Foreign Policy

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Ethiopia and the World · **Lesson:** Foreign Policy
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 25 — Ethiopia and the World (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** foreign policy is the ship's steering: the agreements, strategies, methods and guidelines that convert a state's broad goals and interests into concrete reality — without it, a state drifts.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on all the citizenship levels before it — the learner knows the state from inside; this band turns the state outward, to its relations with neighbours and the world.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks foreign policy is only speeches → it is agreements, strategies, methods and guidelines — the whole steering, judged by conversion into reality.
- Thinks a strong state needs no steering → every state is subject to others' decisions; strength without direction is drift with bigger sails.
