# Video script — Foreign Policy

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing: foreign policy is the ship's steering: the agreements, strategies, methods and guidelines that convert a state's broad goals and interests into concrete reality — without it, a state drifts.
Interest names the destination.
Policy does the steering.
Agreements. Strategies. Methods. Guidelines.
A state without a foreign policy
is a ship in the deep sea
with no knowledge of direction.
