# Foreign Policy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Foreign policy converts —
a) ships into planes  b) broadly conceived goals and interests into concrete reality  c) neighbours into enemies  d) speeches into laws at home
**Answer: b) broadly conceived goals and interests into concrete reality**

**2.** Its main aim is —
a) accomplishing the national interest and maintaining national ideology  b) avoiding all agreements  c) impressing foreigners  d) ending trade
**Answer: a) accomplishing the national interest and maintaining national ideology**
