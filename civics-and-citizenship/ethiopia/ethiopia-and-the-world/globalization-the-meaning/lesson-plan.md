# Lesson plan — Globalization — The Meaning

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Ethiopia and the World · **Lesson:** Globalization — The Meaning
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 25 — Ethiopia and the World (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** globalization is the multiplicity of linkages and interconnections between the states and societies of the modern world system — so that events in one part can carry significant consequences for people far away.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the foreign-relations lessons earlier in this level — the learner knows how Ethiopia deals with the world; this part teaches the world Ethiopia deals in: globalization, its dimensions, gains, harms and answers, and the global issues that test every nation.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. ## If the learner is stuck
- Defines globalization as trade only → trade is the face the word often names; the definition is the whole web of linkages between states and societies, with distant consequences.
- Thinks distance protects → the definition's core is that it does not: events in one part come to have significant consequences in quite distant parts.
