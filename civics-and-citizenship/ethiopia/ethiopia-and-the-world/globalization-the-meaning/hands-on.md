# Hands-on — Globalization — The Meaning

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## String the stall
A table of pictured goods; yarn runs from each to a wall map. Learners follow a string hand over hand and narrate the journey aloud — every hand that touches the yarn adds one link: maker, shipper, seller, buyer.

## Pull one thread
One learner changes one distant 'event' card — a port closes, a price doubles, a cable is cut. The web's holders must each say what changed at their end. Consequence, felt in the hands.
