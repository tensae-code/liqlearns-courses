# Globalization — The Meaning — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Globalization is —
a) the multiplicity of linkages and interconnections between states and societies of the modern world system  b) one world government  c) a trade agreement  d) the internet alone
**Answer: a) the multiplicity of linkages and interconnections between states and societies of the modern world system**

**2.** Its defining consequence is that —
a) events in one part of the world can significantly affect distant individuals and communities  b) distance no longer exists  c) all economies are equal  d) borders are abolished
**Answer: a) events in one part of the world can significantly affect distant individuals and communities**

**3.** The word is often used for its economic face — the integration of national economies through —
a) trade, investment, capital flows, migration and technology spread  b) armies and alliances  c) sport and festivals  d) law courts
**Answer: a) trade, investment, capital flows, migration and technology spread**
