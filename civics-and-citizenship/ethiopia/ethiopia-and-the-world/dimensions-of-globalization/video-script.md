# Video script — Dimensions of Globalization

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing: globalization has four dimensions — political, economic, social and cultural — one web seen through four windows.
Four dimensions of one web.\nPolitical — policies and institutions spread.\nEconomic — markets integrate.\nSocial — ideas and information reshape lives.\nCultural — meanings and values circulate.\nOne house, four windows.
