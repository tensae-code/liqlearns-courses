# Hands-on — Dimensions of Globalization

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Four windows
Four window-frames are held up around the room. Event cards are read; the class carries each to its window — or splits the card, tearing it into the pieces each window claims. Torn cards are the lesson's honesty.

## Cargo check
Four 'ships' (boxes) lettered with the dimensions. Learners load cargo cards — treaty, cargo of coffee, a viral song, a pension rule — and must state the cargo rule that admits each item.
