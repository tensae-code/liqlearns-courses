# Dimensions of Globalization — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Define the four dimensions in one line each.
**Answer:** Political — intensification and expansion of political interrelations, diffusing policies, ideologies and institutions. Economic — interdependence through trade, capital flow and technology diffusion, integrating markets. Social — sharing of ideas and information affecting lives, work, families and societies. Cultural — circulation of ideas, meanings and values, connecting people themselves.
