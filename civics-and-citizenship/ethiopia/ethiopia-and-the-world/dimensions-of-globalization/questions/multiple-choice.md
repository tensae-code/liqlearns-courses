# Dimensions of Globalization — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The four dimensions are —
a) political, economic, social, cultural  b) local, national, regional, global  c) trade, aid, war, peace  d) land, labour, capital, law
**Answer: a) political, economic, social, cultural**

**2.** Political globalization is the worldwide diffusion of —
a) policies, ideologies, political culture and institutions  b) goods and capital  c) songs and stories  d) diseases
**Answer: a) policies, ideologies, political culture and institutions**

**3.** Economic globalization intensifies interdependence through —
a) cross-border trade, international capital flow and rapid technology diffusion  b) alliances and treaties  c) migration of songs  d) family video calls
**Answer: a) cross-border trade, international capital flow and rapid technology diffusion**

**4.** Cultural globalization is the circulation of —
a) ideas, meanings and values — people connected, not only money and commodities  b) containers and cargo  c) laws and constitutions  d) wages and prices
**Answer: a) ideas, meanings and values — people connected, not only money and commodities**
