# Lesson plan — Dimensions of Globalization

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Ethiopia and the World · **Lesson:** Dimensions of Globalization
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 25 — Ethiopia and the World (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** globalization has four dimensions — political, economic, social and cultural — one web seen through four windows.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the foreign-relations lessons earlier in this level — the learner knows how Ethiopia deals with the world; this part teaches the world Ethiopia deals in: globalization, its dimensions, gains, harms and answers, and the global issues that test every nation.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short, single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09).

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Globalization — The Meaning, just before this lesson in this level. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the four dimensions.
2. Define each by what intensifies and spreads.
3. Sort real examples into the four windows without forcing.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else.

## If the learner is stuck
- Merges social and cultural → social carries information and life-patterns (work, family, protection); cultural carries meanings and values — people connected, not only commodities.
- Counts three or five dimensions → the book's set is four: political, economic, social, cultural.
