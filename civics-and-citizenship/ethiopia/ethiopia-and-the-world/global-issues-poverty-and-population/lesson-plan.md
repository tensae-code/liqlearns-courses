# Lesson plan — Global Issues — Poverty and Population

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Ethiopia and the World · **Lesson:** Global Issues — Poverty and Population
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 25 — Ethiopia and the World (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** poverty and population explosion are taught as the book pairs them: deprivation that weakens states and breeds the conditions for terror, inequality perpetuating poverty three ways — and growth pressing on land, air, soil, water and politics itself.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the foreign-relations lessons earlier in this level — the learner knows how Ethiopia deals with the world; this part teaches the world Ethiopia deals in: globalization, its dimensions, gains, harms and answers, and the global issues that test every nation.

2. Give the three ways inequality perpetuates poverty.
3. Give population growth's environmental and political pressures.

## If the learner is stuck
- Reads poverty as a private misfortune → the book's frame is global: deprivation weakens states against terror, crime, conflict and disease, with regional and global consequences.
- Counts inequality's turns loosely → three: political participation discouraged (and services with it); impartial institutions and rule of law prevented; the wealthy refusing compromise.
