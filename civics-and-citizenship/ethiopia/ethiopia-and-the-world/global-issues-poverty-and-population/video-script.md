# Video script — Global Issues — Poverty and Population

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing: poverty and population explosion are taught as the book pairs them: deprivation that weakens states and breeds the conditions for terror, inequality perpetuating poverty three ways — and growth pressing on land, air, soil, water and politics itself.
Poverty: deep, widespread — hungry, unschooled, unlit.\nInequality keeps it turning: voice denied, law bent, compromise refused.\nPopulation: more people pressing land, air, soil, water —\nand unmet needs pressing politics itself.
