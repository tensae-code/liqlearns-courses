# Hands-on — Global Issues — Poverty and Population

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The widening gap
Two house cards are moved apart step by step as the lesson's gap-facts are read. At each step, a learner states one consequence that grows with the distance — schooling, light, health, state weakness.

## Tree by tree
A hillside of paper trees. Each population-pressure card read aloud removes one tree. When the hillside is bare, the class must name what else fell with the trees — soil, water, habitat, patience, peace.
