# Global Issues — Poverty and Population — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Despite progress, the poorest remain —
a) hungry, less schooled, often without light at night, in poorer health  b) a small minority everywhere  c) only in cities  d) unaffected by growth
**Answer: a) hungry, less schooled, often without light at night, in poorer health**

**2.** Population growth raises —
a) the absolute number of the extremely poor, even as their share falls  b) everyone's income  c) school enrolment automatically  d) nothing measurable
**Answer: a) the absolute number of the extremely poor, even as their share falls**

**3.** Inequality perpetuates poverty by discouraging —
a) the poor's political participation and access to services  b) the wealthy from investing  c) migration  d) trade
**Answer: a) the poor's political participation and access to services**
