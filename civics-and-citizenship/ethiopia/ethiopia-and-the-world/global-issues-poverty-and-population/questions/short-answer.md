# Global Issues — Poverty and Population — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Give the three ways inequality perpetuates poverty.
**Answer:** It discourages the poor's political participation, and with it access to education, health and growth services; it prevents impartial institutions and the rule of law; and the wealthy refuse political and economic compromise.

**2.** Give population growth's environmental pressures.
**Answer:** Forest-clearing cultivation, rising fuel consumption, fertiliser and pesticide acidifying rain and polluting, overproduction depressing resources and biodiversity, waste and habitat destruction — pressure on land, air, soil, water, security and health.
