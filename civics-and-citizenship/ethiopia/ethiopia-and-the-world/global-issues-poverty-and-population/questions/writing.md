# Global Issues — Poverty and Population — writing

Write what each prompt asks, in your own words.

**1.** Trace the book's political chain from unmet needs to exported insecurity, link by link, marking which links you judge strongest and weakest.
**A good answer does this:** The full chain — unmet needs, anger, violence, migration; migrants targeted and excluded; some driven toward terrorist groups — with the learner's marked judgement on the links.
