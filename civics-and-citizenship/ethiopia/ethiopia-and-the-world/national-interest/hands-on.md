# Hands-on — National Interest

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The shield over the port
The group draws the shield and the port together and labels the paradox: sovereignty guarded, dependence admitted. Each learner adds one rope between the two states that makes the dependence a relation, not a weakness.

## Interlock web
State cards are joined by strings for every interlock the class can evidence: crisis, port, river, power line. Cut one string: the group narrates what both ends lose.
