# National Interest — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** In its core sense, national interest is —
a) trade growth  b) survival — sovereignty protected from intervention or aggression  c) prestige  d) population growth
**Answer: b) survival — sovereignty protected from intervention or aggression**

**2.** Which heads every state's list?
a) Fashion, sport, tourism  b) Security, political independence, territorial integrity  c) Exports, imports, aid  d) Language, dress, food
**Answer: b) Security, political independence, territorial integrity**

**3.** About what share of Ethiopia's imports arrive through Djibouti's port?
a) Half  b) 90 per cent  c) 10 per cent  d) All of them
**Answer: b) 90 per cent**
