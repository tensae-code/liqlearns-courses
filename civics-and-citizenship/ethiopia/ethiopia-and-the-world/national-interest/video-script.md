# Video script — National Interest

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing: national interest is what every state is protecting: its goals, claims and demands among nations — and at its core, survival: sovereignty, security, political independence and territorial integrity.
Every state guards something first.
Its survival. Its sovereignty.
Security. Independence. Its land, whole.
But no list is private:
a neighbour's crisis crosses the border,
and ninety per cent of imports ride one neighbour's port.
