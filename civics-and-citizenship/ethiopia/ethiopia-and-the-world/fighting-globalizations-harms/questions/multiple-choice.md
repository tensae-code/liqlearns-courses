# Fighting Globalization's Harms — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The book's stance toward globalization's harms is —
a) better management at national and international levels  b) retreat from the world  c) ignoring the costs  d) blaming other countries
**Answer: a) better management at national and international levels**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Indigenous knowledge is to be disseminated through —
a) the education system  b) foreign agencies only  c) markets alone  d) prohibition of Western books
**Answer: a) the education system**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The worked example of community development is —
a) collectively maintained irrigation channels sharing water fairly  b) a foreign-built dam  c) a private water company  d) a new highway
**Answer: a) collectively maintained irrigation channels sharing water fairly**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** School feeding is which kind of protection?
a) Protective  b) Preventive  c) Promotive  d) None of them
**Answer: a) Protective**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Universal child allowances are —
a) preventive protection  b) protective protection  c) promotive protection  d) a trade policy
**Answer: a) preventive protection**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The innovation fund rewards researchers for —
a) innovations' value to developing countries — big rewards for cures for widespread costly diseases  b) publishing papers  c) patenting fastest  d) importing technology
**Answer: a) innovations' value to developing countries — big rewards for cures for widespread costly diseases**
