# Video script — Fighting Globalization's Harms

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing: the answer to globalization's harms is management, not retreat — promoting indigenous knowledge, valuing community development, building indigenous protection in three kinds, cooperating regionally, and funding innovation where the need is.
Not retreat — management.\nTeach the home knowledge. Trust the community's channel.\nProtect, prevent, promote.\nCooperate across the region. Reward invention that serves need.
