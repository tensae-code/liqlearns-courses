# Hands-on — Fighting Globalization's Harms

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Clear the channel
The class clears a real or mimed channel together while two learners record the elders' knowledge aloud — where the water turns, why the bend is there. The record is read back: indigenous knowledge, disseminated, exactly as the lesson prescribes.

## Three shields drill
Three shield cards — Protect, Prevent, Promote. Case cards are thrown: a starving elder, a worker one illness from destitution, a youth with skill and no tools. The right shield must rise for each case, with its instrument named.
