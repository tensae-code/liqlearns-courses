# Lesson plan — Fighting Globalization's Harms

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Ethiopia and the World · **Lesson:** Fighting Globalization's Harms
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 25 — Ethiopia and the World (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the answer to globalization's harms is management, not retreat — promoting indigenous knowledge, valuing community development, building indigenous protection in three kinds, cooperating regionally, and funding innovation where the need is.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the foreign-relations lessons earlier in this level — the learner knows how Ethiopia deals with the world; this part teaches the world Ethiopia deals in: globalization, its dimensions, gains, harms and answers, and the global issues that test every nation.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Home knowledge and community** — The first counter-set: home knowledge promoted through the schools, and the community valued as the pillar that sustains.
2. **Protection and cooperation** — The second counter-set: protection in three kinds, cooperation across the region, and prizes that point invention at need.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Disadvantages of Globalization, just before this lesson in this level. Recommended, never forced.

1. State the book's stance: management, not retreat.

3. Give protection's three kinds, regional cooperation, and the innovation fund.

## If the learner is stuck
- Reads the lesson as anti-globalization → the stance is management, not retreat: costs faced honestly, the web governed better at national and international levels.
- Flattens protection into charity → three kinds with different jobs: protective stops starvation, preventive stops falls, promotive raises incomes and capabilities.
