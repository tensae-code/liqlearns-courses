# Video script — Instruments of Foreign Policy

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing: states work their foreign policy through instruments in a fixed order: diplomacy first, economic means second, and military force last — the resort when peaceful fulfilment fails.
First, the table: reason, conciliation, exchange.
Second, the ledger: trade that binds.
Last — only when peace cannot fulfil the interest —
the sword, still sheathed, and heavy with cost.
The order itself is the lesson.
