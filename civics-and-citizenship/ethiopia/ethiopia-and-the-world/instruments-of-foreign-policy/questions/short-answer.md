# Instruments of Foreign Policy — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Define diplomacy.
**Answer:** The ways states manage sovereign relations with other mutually recognised states — the art and practice of conducting negotiations between nations, handling affairs without arousing hostility.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What does diplomacy seek, by its maxim's paraphrase?
**Answer:** By reason, conciliation and exchange of interests, to prevent major conflicts between sovereign states.
