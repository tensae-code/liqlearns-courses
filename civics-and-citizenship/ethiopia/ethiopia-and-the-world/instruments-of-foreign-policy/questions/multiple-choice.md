# Instruments of Foreign Policy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The instruments stand in the order —
a) force, trade, diplomacy  b) diplomacy, economic means, military force  c) trade, diplomacy, force  d) diplomacy only
**Answer: b) diplomacy, economic means, military force**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Diplomacy is, by its maxim —
a) an end in itself  b) not an end but a means; not a purpose but a method  c) a ceremony  d) a threat
**Answer: b) not an end but a means; not a purpose but a method**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Economic methods arise from —
a) equal development  b) unequal development and the interdependence it creates  c) isolation  d) uniform poverty
**Answer: b) unequal development and the interdependence it creates**
