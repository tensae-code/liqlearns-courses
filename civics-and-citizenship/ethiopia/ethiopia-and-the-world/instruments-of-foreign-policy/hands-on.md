# Hands-on — Instruments of Foreign Policy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The negotiators' table
Two flags, one signed paper: pairs negotiate a shared-river water split in ten minutes, using only reason, conciliation and exchange of interests. Observers log every move that fits the maxim.

## The scale of means
A balance is loaded: trade goods placed one by one on one pan while a sheathed sword waits beside it, untouched. The class discusses what would have to happen before the sword may even be lifted — and who pays when it is.
