# Video script — Advantages of Globalization

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing: globalization's advantages run in five ledgers — economic, political, social, cultural, financial — from a common market and rising revenues to coffee carrying Ethiopia's name around the world, and remittances arriving by MoneyGram.
The gains, in five ledgers.\nTrade and growth. Standards and treaties.\nIdeas and education spreading. Coffee carrying a name worldwide.\nMoney arriving home — simpler, faster.\nOne web, five columns of gain.
