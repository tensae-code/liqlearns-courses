# Advantages of Globalization — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A global common market rests on —
a) free exchange of goods and capital  b) one currency imposed  c) closed borders  d) state ownership everywhere
**Answer: a) free exchange of goods and capital**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Environmental comparative advantage includes —
a) forests, fisheries, tourist attractions and carbon sinks  b) armies and ports  c) oil alone  d) nothing — nature is not an advantage
**Answer: a) forests, fisheries, tourist attractions and carbon sinks**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Treaty participation, in the political ledger, protects —
a) human rights, multiplying global standards like copyright, patent and world trade  b) governments from criticism  c) prices from falling  d) borders from maps
**Answer: a) human rights, multiplying global standards like copyright, patent and world trade**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The book's worked example of cultural advantage is —
a) coffee, said to originate in Ethiopia, drunk worldwide  b) the national anthem  c) a trade treaty  d) a new airport
**Answer: a) coffee, said to originate in Ethiopia, drunk worldwide**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Remittances via MoneyGram illustrate which ledger?
a) Financial advantages  b) Political advantages  c) Social advantages  d) Cultural advantages
**Answer: a) Financial advantages**
