# Advantages of Globalization — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give the economic advantages in the book's terms.
**Answer:** Sharply increased trade and exchange, strong global growth, rapid industrial development, expanding technologies and commodities; a global common market on free exchange; larger diverse markets raising revenues and domestic growth; transport and ICT advances leaping productivity; environmental comparative advantages.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the five ledgers of advantage.
**Answer:** Economic, political, social, cultural, financial.
