# Hands-on — Advantages of Globalization

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Load the crane
Teams load a 'ship' with advantage cards — each card must name its ledger before it boards. A card naming no ledger is refused by the crane operator, kindly and finally.

## Two pans, held
A balance is loaded with gain-cards only, this lesson. The class photographs the tilt in memory — next lesson loads the other pan, and the honest picture is the two together.
