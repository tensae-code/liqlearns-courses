# Lesson plan — Advantages of Globalization

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Ethiopia and the World · **Lesson:** Advantages of Globalization
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 25 — Ethiopia and the World (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** globalization's advantages run in five ledgers — economic, political, social, cultural, financial — from a common market and rising revenues to coffee carrying Ethiopia's name around the world, and remittances arriving by MoneyGram.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the foreign-relations lessons earlier in this level — the learner knows how Ethiopia deals with the world; this part teaches the world Ethiopia deals in: globalization, its dimensions, gains, harms and answers, and the global issues that test every nation.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Economy and politics** — The first ledger pair: markets, growth and natural wealth; then standards, treaties and a common political culture.
2. **Society, culture, money** — The second ledger set: social spread, cultural exchange with coffee as example, and money made simpler — remittances the example.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Dimensions of Globalization, just before this lesson in this level. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Give the economic and political advantages.
2. Give the social, cultural and financial advantages.
3. Use the book's worked examples: coffee and remittances.

## If the learner is stuck
- Lists gains without ledgers → the book's arrangement is the memory: economic, political, social, cultural, financial — five columns, each with its own kind of gain.
- Skips the failure clause → the political ledger itself admits many countries fail to practise the circulating principles; the advantage is the standard's spread, not universal practice.
