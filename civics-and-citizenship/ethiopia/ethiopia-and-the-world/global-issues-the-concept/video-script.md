# Video script — Global Issues — The Concept

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

[Tour script — criteria first, then the eight issues. Evidence layer throughout: the guide's keyed tour.]
What makes an issue global? Watch the three gauges.
Extent — does it reach large parts of the globe and its peoples?
Urgency — how serious is the threat, and how much attention does the whole world give it?
Centrality — where does it sit, and what vital interests does it touch?
Now the tour. First cloud: climate change — the sky itself changing over every border.
Second: poverty and inequality — hunger and lightlessness in a connected world.
Third: population explosion — more people pressing on land, air, soil and water.
Fourth: government corruption — trust leaking out of the states meant to act.
Fifth: unemployment — skills wasted, dignity denied, societies destabilised.
Sixth: disease — travelling with the travellers, as the last years taught everyone.
Seventh: violence — and eighth: terrorism — fear used as a political instrument.
Eight clouds, one map. The coming lessons open three of them.
