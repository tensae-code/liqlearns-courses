# Global Issues — The Concept — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** A global issue arises when —
a) two or more states cannot agree how to solve a problem  b) one village has a dispute  c) a price changes  d) a law is passed
**Answer: a) two or more states cannot agree how to solve a problem**

**2.** The three identification criteria are —
a) extent, urgency, centrality  b) size, cost, speed  c) cause, effect, cure  d) local, national, regional
**Answer: a) extent, urgency, centrality**

**3.** Centrality includes —
a) links to other vital interests, not only geography  b) population size alone  c) wealth alone  d) being on the equator
**Answer: a) links to other vital interests, not only geography**
