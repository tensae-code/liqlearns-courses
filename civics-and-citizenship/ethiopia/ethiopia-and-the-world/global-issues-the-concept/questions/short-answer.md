# Global Issues — The Concept — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Define a global issue in the book's full terms.
**Answer:** An issue arising when two or more states cannot agree how to solve a problem; any major trend, shock or development with potential to seriously impact the globe, adversely affecting the global community and environment — from minor issues affecting everyone to catastrophic risks.

**2.** Name the eight issues the guide tours.
**Answer:** Climate change; poverty and inequality; population explosion; government corruption; unemployment; disease; violence; terrorism.
