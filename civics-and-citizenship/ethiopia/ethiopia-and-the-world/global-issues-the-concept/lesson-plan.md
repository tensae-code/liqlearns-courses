# Lesson plan — Global Issues — The Concept

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Ethiopia and the World · **Lesson:** Global Issues — The Concept
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 25 — Ethiopia and the World (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a global issue is a problem two or more states cannot agree how to solve — any trend, shock or development able to seriously impact the globe — and it is recognised by three criteria: extent, urgency and centrality.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the foreign-relations lessons earlier in this level — the learner knows how Ethiopia deals with the world; this part teaches the world Ethiopia deals in: globalization, its dimensions, gains, harms and answers, and the global issues that test every nation.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. ## If the learner is stuck
- Defines global as 'very big' → the book's entry test is disagreement between states plus serious global impact; the three gauges then do the identifying.
- Treats centrality as pure geography → it is partly sensitivity: links to other vital interests pull attention as much as location does.
