# Hands-on — Global Issues — The Concept

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Three gauges
Three large gauge cards are held over a problem card. The class reads each gauge aloud — extent? urgency? centrality? — and the holders raise or lower their gauge with reasons. A problem is certified global only when the class defends all three readings.

## Eight clouds
Eight learners hold the eight issue clouds over a floor map. Each cloud must pass one gauge spoken by another learner before it may rain questions on the map — the tour the guide keys, done standing.
