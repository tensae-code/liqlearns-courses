# Hands-on — Global Issues — Climate Change

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Marks on the wall
At the GIF, learners call each rising mark. For each mark, one learner names a warming effect from the lesson that arrives with that water — sea, storm, disease, failed field. The wall fills with consequences.

## Three causes, three corners
The room's corners hold the three causes. Case cards — a new factory's smoke, a hillside cleared for farms, a city's garbage mountain — are carried to their corners; contested cards are argued from the mechanisms.
