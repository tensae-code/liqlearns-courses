# Video script — Global Issues — Climate Change

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing: climate change stands among humanity's greatest challenges: growth that degraded the resources it fed on, a family of problems from acid rain to ozone loss — and global warming, driven by industrialisation, population growth and urbanisation, as the biggest.
Among humanity's greatest challenges: climate change.\nAcid rain. Forests falling. Seas fouled. Ozone thinning.\nAnd the biggest — a warming world, rising seas, failing fields.\nThree causes: industry, population, the swelling city.
