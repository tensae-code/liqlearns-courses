# Global Issues — Climate Change — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The book's problem family includes —
a) acid rain, forest destruction, falling biodiversity, desertification, contaminated oceans, ozone destruction  b) only hotter summers  c) only floods  d) earthquakes and volcanoes
**Answer: a) acid rain, forest destruction, falling biodiversity, desertification, contaminated oceans, ozone destruction**

**2.** The biggest member of the family is —
a) global warming  b) acid rain  c) overfishing  d) soil erosion
**Answer: a) global warming**

**3.** Global warming's effects include —
a) rising seas submerging island and coastal towns, more drought, declining agriculture and fishing  b) colder winters everywhere  c) more forests  d) cheaper food
**Answer: a) rising seas submerging island and coastal towns, more drought, declining agriculture and fishing**
