# Global Issues — Climate Change — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the three human causes with one mechanism each.
**Answer:** Massive industrialisation — energy and resource demand emitting harmful gases and particles. Rapid population growth — competition for resources: overgrazing, over-cultivation, deforestation, food pressure. Rapid urbanisation — pollution, congestion, waste, wood energy and construction driving deforestation, automobile air pollution.
