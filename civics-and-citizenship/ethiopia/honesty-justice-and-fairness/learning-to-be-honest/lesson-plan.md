# Lesson plan — Learning to Be Honest — Praise Truth, Keep Your Word, Choose Honest Friends

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Honesty, Justice and Fairness · **Lesson:** Learning to Be Honest — Praise Truth, Keep Your Word, Choose Honest Friends
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Honesty, Justice and Fairness (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** honesty is learned: praise truth when you see it, treat your word as sacred, return what you borrow, and choose honest friends.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the lessons before it in this title — the learner already knows the earlier circles of this course (myself, then family, neighbours, school, community and culture) as far as this title. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short, single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09).

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Three Kinds of Honesty — True Words, Kept Words, Clean Hands (the previous lesson in this title). Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Speak truth — or keep silent rather than speak empty or false words.
2. Treat your word as a sacred word.
3. Care for others' property.
4. Choose honest friends — “show me your friend and I will tell you who you are.”.
5. Truth is paid for with strong praise rather than a rush to punish — praise builds trust, and a child who is not trapped by trick questions learns that honesty is safe.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson's scenes are the learner's own home, school and neighbourhood.
