# Learning to Be Honest — Praise Truth, Keep Your Word, Choose Honest Friends — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Speak truth .
**Answer: True**

**2.** Truth is paid for with strong praise rather than a rush to punish .
**Answer: True**

**3.** The best way to teach honesty is to set trap questions that catch children into lying.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite.
