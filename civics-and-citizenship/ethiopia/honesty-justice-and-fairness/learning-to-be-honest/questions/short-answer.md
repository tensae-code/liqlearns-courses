# Learning to Be Honest — Praise Truth, Keep Your Word, Choose Honest Friends — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What should you do rather than speak false words?
**Answer:** Keep silent.

**2.** What must always happen to borrowed things?
**Answer:** They must always be returned.

**3.** In your own words, what is sacred word?
**Answer:** Your promise treated as holy — to be kept without fail.
