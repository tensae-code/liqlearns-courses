# Learning to Be Honest — Praise Truth, Keep Your Word, Choose Honest Friends — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) multiculturalism: many cultures together  b) cooperation: together for a common benefit  c) the traits of a respected person: humility and honesty  d) how honesty is learned and grown
