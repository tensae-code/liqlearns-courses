# Video script — Learning to Be Honest — Praise Truth, Keep Your Word, Choose Honest Friends

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Honesty is learned: praise truth when you see it, treat your word as sacred, return what you borrow, and choose honest friends.

Speak truth — or keep silent rather than speak empty or false words.

Treat your word as a sacred word: keep it.

Care for others' property: borrowing is good, but what is borrowed must always be returned.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
