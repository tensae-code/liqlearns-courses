# Learning to Be Honest — Praise Truth, Keep Your Word, Choose Honest Friends

## Do it first

Confess one small mistake today, out loud, to the person it concerns — before anyone asks. Feel how hard and how freeing it is. This lesson is about building a life where that gets easier.

## The child's core of the ten ways

Speak truth — or keep silent rather than speak empty or false words.

Treat your word as a sacred word: keep it.

Care for others' property: borrowing is good, but what is borrowed must always be returned.

Choose honest friends — “show me your friend and I will tell you who you are.”

## How the grown-ups help

Truth is paid for with strong praise rather than a rush to punish — praise builds trust, and a child who is not trapped by trick questions learns that honesty is safe.

When lying is caught, it has a consequence, and the mistake is corrected: the child learns how lying destroys trust, and how hard trust is to win back.

The example leads: a small habit of lying leads to a big habit of lying — children who hear even a harmless white lie conclude that lying is acceptable.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: honesty is learned: praise truth when you see it, treat your word as sacred, return what you borrow, and choose honest friends. Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood, and name the lesson's key words: sacred word, trap question, consequence. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: how honesty is learned and grown.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
