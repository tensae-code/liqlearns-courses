# Three Kinds of Honesty — True Words, Kept Words, Clean Hands — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) true words, kept words, clean hands  b) cultural values: the principles a community stands on  c) how positive interaction helps learning  d) local culture: what we share where we live
**Answer: a) true words, kept words, clean hands** — That is the one thing this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does “truthfulness” mean?
a) doing what you promised to do  b) to grab and take what is not yours  c) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  d) speaking what is true
**Answer: d) speaking what is true** — “truthfulness” means: speaking what is true.

**3.** What does “keeping your word” mean?
a) to grab and take what is not yours  b) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  c) doing what you promised to do  d) speaking what is true
**Answer: c) doing what you promised to do** — “keeping your word” means: doing what you promised to do.

**4.** What does “snatch” mean?
a) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  b) to grab and take what is not yours  c) speaking what is true  d) doing what you promised to do
**Answer: b) to grab and take what is not yours** — “snatch” means: to grab and take what is not yours.

**5.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Pupils identify the types of truthfulness and honesty as a chapter outcome  b) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  c) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  d) The strongest person may take whatever they want.
**Answer: a) Pupils identify the types of truthfulness and honesty as a chapter outcome** — It is one of this lesson's own teachings.
