# Three Kinds of Honesty — True Words, Kept Words, Clean Hands — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The first kind of honesty is speaking the truth.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The third kind is in the book's own words: do not steal, do not snatch.
**Answer: True**

**3.** Honesty has only one kind: speaking the truth. Promises and other people's property have nothing to do with it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite.
