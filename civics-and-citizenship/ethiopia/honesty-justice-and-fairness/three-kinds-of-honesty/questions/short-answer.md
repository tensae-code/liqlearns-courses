# Three Kinds of Honesty — True Words, Kept Words, Clean Hands — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the three kinds of honesty.
**Answer:** Speaking the truth; keeping your word; do not steal, do not snatch.

**2.** What does 'clean hands' mean here?
**Answer:** Not stealing or snatching — what is not yours stays untouched, and borrowed things go back.

**3.** In your own words, what is truthfulness?
**Answer:** Speaking what is true.
