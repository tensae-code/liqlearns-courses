# Video script — Three Kinds of Honesty — True Words, Kept Words, Clean Hands

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Honesty is practised in three kinds: speaking the truth, keeping your word, and not stealing or snatching.

The first kind of honesty is speaking the truth.

The second kind is keeping your word — doing what you promised.

Pupils identify the types of truthfulness and honesty as a chapter outcome.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
