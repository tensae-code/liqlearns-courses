# Lesson plan — Three Kinds of Honesty — True Words, Kept Words, Clean Hands

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Honesty, Justice and Fairness · **Lesson:** Three Kinds of Honesty — True Words, Kept Words, Clean Hands
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Honesty, Justice and Fairness (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** honesty is practised in three kinds: speaking the truth, keeping your word, and not stealing or snatching.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the lessons before it in this title — the learner already knows the earlier circles of this course (myself, then family, neighbours, school, community and culture) as far as this title. It needs nothing else.
