# Three Kinds of Honesty — True Words, Kept Words, Clean Hands

## Do it first

Make one small promise today — and keep it exactly. Then tell one hard truth kindly. Then return one thing that is not yours. You have done all three kinds before you finish reading.

## True words and kept words

The first kind of honesty is speaking the truth.

The second kind is keeping your word — doing what you promised.

Pupils identify the types of truthfulness and honesty as a chapter outcome.

[Picture 28024 — three kinds of honesty]

## Clean hands

The third kind is in the book's own words: do not steal, do not snatch.

Clean hands means: what is not yours stays untouched, and what you borrowed goes back.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: honesty is practised in three kinds: speaking the truth, keeping your word, and not stealing or snatching. Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood, and name the lesson's key words: truthfulness, keeping your word, snatch. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: true words, kept words, clean hands.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
