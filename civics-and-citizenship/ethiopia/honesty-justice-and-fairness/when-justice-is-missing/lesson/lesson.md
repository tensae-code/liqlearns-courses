# When Justice Is Missing — What Breaks

## Do it first

Imagine your class with no fairness for one whole week: turns stolen, blame falling anywhere, no rule protecting anyone. List what breaks first. Then read what the book lists.

## The damages of absence

The chapter's contents close with the damages of the absence of justice, and pupils list them as an outcome.

In the chapter's own summary logic, a society without fairness rules falls into disagreement.

Then quarrelling, and refusal to cooperate.

The end of the chain: places nobody wants to live in.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: when justice is absent, a society falls into disagreement, quarrelling and refusal to cooperate — places nobody wants to live in. Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood, and name the lesson's key words: absence, cooperate. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
