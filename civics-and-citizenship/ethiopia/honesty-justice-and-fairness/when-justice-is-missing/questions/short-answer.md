# When Justice Is Missing — What Breaks — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name two damages of the absence of justice.
**Answer:** Any two of: disagreement, quarrelling, refusal to cooperate, places nobody wants to live in.

**2.** What is the end of the chain when fairness rules are missing?
**Answer:** Places nobody wants to live in.

**3.** In your own words, what is absence?
**Answer:** Being missing; not there.
