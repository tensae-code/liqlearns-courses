# When Justice Is Missing — What Breaks — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) the damages that follow when justice is absent  b) selfishness against the common good  c) multiculturalism: many cultures together  d) the eleven marks of being human
**Answer: a) the damages that follow when justice is absent** — That is the one thing this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does “absence” mean?
a) to work together with others  b) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  c) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  d) being missing; not there
**Answer: d) being missing; not there** — “absence” means: being missing; not there.

**3.** What does “cooperate” mean?
a) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  b) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  c) to work together with others  d) being missing; not there
**Answer: c) to work together with others** — “cooperate” means: to work together with others.

**4.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  b) Then quarrelling, and refusal to cooperate  c) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  d) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.
**Answer: b) Then quarrelling, and refusal to cooperate** — It is one of this lesson's own teachings.

**5.** Which of these is true in this lesson?
a) The chapter's contents close with the damages of the absence of justice, and pupils list them as an outcome  b) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  c) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  d) Promises, truth and fairness are not needed between people.
**Answer: a) The chapter's contents close with the damages of the absence of justice, and pupils list them as an outcome** — It is stated in the lesson.
