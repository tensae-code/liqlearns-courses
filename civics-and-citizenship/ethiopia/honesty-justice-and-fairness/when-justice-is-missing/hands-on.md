# Hands-on — When Justice Is Missing — What Breaks

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Imagine your class with no fairness for one whole week: turns stolen, blame falling anywhere, no rule protecting anyone. List what breaks first. Then read what the book lists.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the damages that follow when justice is absent. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
