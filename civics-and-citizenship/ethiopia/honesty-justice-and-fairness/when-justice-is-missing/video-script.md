# Video script — When Justice Is Missing — What Breaks

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
When justice is absent, a society falls into disagreement, quarrelling and refusal to cooperate — places nobody wants to live in.

The chapter's contents close with the damages of the absence of justice, and pupils list them as an outcome.

In the chapter's own summary logic, a society without fairness rules falls into disagreement.

Then quarrelling, and refusal to cooperate.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
