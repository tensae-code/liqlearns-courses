# Standing Against What Is Unfair — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Where must the fair person's character be shown?
**Answer:** In the pupil's own behaviour.

**2.** Against what must pupils explain the need to struggle?
**Answer:** What is unjust in the school and in society.

**3.** In your own words, what is unjust?
**Answer:** Not just — against what each person is due.
