# Standing Against What Is Unfair — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The fair person's character must be shown in the pupil's own behaviour .
**Answer: True**

**2.** Pupils explain the need to struggle against what is unjust in the school and in society.
**Answer: True**

**3.** Fairness is only something to talk about; it does not need to show in your behaviour.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite.
