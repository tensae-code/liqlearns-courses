# Standing Against What Is Unfair — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) selfishness against the common good  b) showing fairness, and standing against injustice  c) what a healthy social life is  d) the nine core community values
**Answer: b) showing fairness, and standing against injustice** — That is the one thing this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does “unjust” mean?
a) not just — against what each person is due  b) a state or act where people are not given their due  c) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  d) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.
**Answer: a) not just — against what each person is due** — “unjust” means: not just — against what each person is due.

**3.** What does “injustice” mean?
a) not just — against what each person is due  b) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  c) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  d) a state or act where people are not given their due
**Answer: d) a state or act where people are not given their due** — “injustice” means: a state or act where people are not given their due.

**4.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  b) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  c) Pupils explain the need to struggle against what is unjust in the school and in society  d) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.
**Answer: c) Pupils explain the need to struggle against what is unjust in the school and in society** — It is one of this lesson's own teachings.

**5.** Which of these is true in this lesson?
a) Promises, truth and fairness are not needed between people.  b) The fair person's character must be shown in the pupil's own behaviour — fairness is proved in acts, not claim  c) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  d) The strongest person may take whatever they want.
**Answer: b) The fair person's character must be shown in the pupil's own behaviour — fairness is proved in acts, not claim** — It is stated in the lesson.
