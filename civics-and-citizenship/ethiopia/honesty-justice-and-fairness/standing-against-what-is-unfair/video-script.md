# Video script — Standing Against What Is Unfair

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A fair person shows fairness in their own behaviour — and speaks up, calmly and fairly, against what is unjust at school and in society.

The fair person's character must be shown in the pupil's own behaviour — fairness is proved in acts, not claimed in words.

One activity studies pictures of individuals who stood for justice for their people and their government.

Pupils explain the need to struggle against what is unjust in the school and in society.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
