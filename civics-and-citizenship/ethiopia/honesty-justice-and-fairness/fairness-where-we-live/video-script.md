# Video script — Fairness Where We Live — Rules, Respect, Nobody Shut Out

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Fairness where we live: accepting everyone equally, excluding no one, keeping the rules, and never asking of others what you would not do yourself.

Fairness is accepting everyone equally and excluding no one.

Fair people follow the rules in sports, games, activities and their community, and are honest and trusted.

In a fair community, people keep the law, respect neighbours, guard their animals and their rubbish, and help one another.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
