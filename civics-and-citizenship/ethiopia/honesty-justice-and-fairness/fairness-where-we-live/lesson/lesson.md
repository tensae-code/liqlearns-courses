# Fairness Where We Live — Rules, Respect, Nobody Shut Out

## Do it first

Join a game today and watch its rules: does everyone get a turn? Is anyone left outside for no reason? That watching is fairness at work — this lesson names it.

## Fairness in the community

Fairness is accepting everyone equally and excluding no one.

Fair people follow the rules in sports, games, activities and their community, and are honest and trusted.

In a fair community, people keep the law, respect neighbours, guard their animals and their rubbish, and help one another.

## The golden check

The book's own form of the famous line: it is not fair to ask of others what you would not do yourself.

Without fairness, the chapter summary says, no one agrees, people quarrel, no one works together, and no one wants to live there.
