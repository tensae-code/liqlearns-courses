# Hands-on — Fairness Where We Live — Rules, Respect, Nobody Shut Out

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Join a game today and watch its rules: does everyone get a turn? Is anyone left outside for no reason? That watching is fairness at work — this lesson names it.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: fairness in daily life: rules kept, nobody excluded. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
