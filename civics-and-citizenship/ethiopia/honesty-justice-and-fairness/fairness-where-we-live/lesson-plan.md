# Lesson plan — Fairness Where We Live — Rules, Respect, Nobody Shut Out

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Honesty, Justice and Fairness · **Lesson:** Fairness Where We Live — Rules, Respect, Nobody Shut Out
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 3 — Honesty, Justice and Fairness (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** fairness where we live: accepting everyone equally, excluding no one, keeping the rules, and never asking of others what you would not do yourself.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the lessons before it in this title — the learner already knows the earlier circles of this course (myself, then family, neighbours, school, community and culture) as far as this title. It needs nothing else.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Fairness is accepting everyone equally and excluding no one.
2. Fair people follow the rules in sports, games, activities and their community, and are honest and trusted.
3. In a fair community, people keep the law, respect neighbours, guard their animals and their rubbish, and help one another.
4. The book's own form of the famous line.
5. Without fairness, the chapter summary says, no one agrees, people quarrel, no one works together, and no one wants to live there.
