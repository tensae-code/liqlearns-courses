# Fairness Where We Live — Rules, Respect, Nobody Shut Out — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is fairness, in the book's words?
**Answer:** Accepting everyone equally and excluding no one.

**2.** Complete the book's line: it is not fair to ask of others…
**Answer:** …what you would not do yourself.

**3.** In your own words, what is exclude?
**Answer:** To shut someone out, leaving them outside what others share.
