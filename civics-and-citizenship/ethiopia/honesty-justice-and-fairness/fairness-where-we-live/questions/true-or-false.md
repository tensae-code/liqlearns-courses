# Fairness Where We Live — Rules, Respect, Nobody Shut Out — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Fairness is accepting everyone equally and excluding no one.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The book's own form of the famous line: it is not fair to ask of others what you would not do yourself.
**Answer: True**

**3.** Fairness means the rules apply to everyone except you and your friends.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite.
