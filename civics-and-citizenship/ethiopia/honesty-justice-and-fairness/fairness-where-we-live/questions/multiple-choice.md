# Fairness Where We Live — Rules, Respect, Nobody Shut Out — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) the common good: what benefits all of us  b) what disrespect is, and what it breaks  c) fairness in daily life: rules kept, nobody excluded  d) responsibilities at school, and the rights that go with them
**Answer: c) fairness in daily life: rules kept, nobody excluded** — That is the one thing this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does “exclude” mean?
a) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  b) to shut someone out, leaving them outside what others share  c) the people living together in one place  d) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.
**Answer: b) to shut someone out, leaving them outside what others share** — “exclude” means: to shut someone out, leaving them outside what others share.

**3.** What does “community” mean?
a) the people living together in one place  b) to shut someone out, leaving them outside what others share  c) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  d) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.
**Answer: a) the people living together in one place** — “community” means: the people living together in one place.

**4.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  b) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  c) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  d) In a fair community, people keep the law, respect neighbours, guard their animals and their rubbish, and he…
**Answer: d) In a fair community, people keep the law, respect neighbours, guard their animals and their rubbish, and he…** — It is one of this lesson's own teachings.

**5.** Which of these is true in this lesson?
a) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  b) Promises, truth and fairness are not needed between people.  c) Fairness is accepting everyone equally and excluding no one  d) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.
**Answer: c) Fairness is accepting everyone equally and excluding no one** — It is stated in the lesson.
