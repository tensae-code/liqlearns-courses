# What Honesty Wins, What Dishonesty Costs — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The chapter closes with the importance of truthfulness and honesty, and the benefits of being a truthful and honest person.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Against the benefits stand the damages of honesty's absence .
**Answer: True**

**3.** Dishonesty costs nothing; only being caught costs something.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite.
