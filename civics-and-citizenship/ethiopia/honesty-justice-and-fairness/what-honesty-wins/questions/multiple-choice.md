# What Honesty Wins, What Dishonesty Costs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) the dignity we are born with, and the dignity we earn  b) the benefits of honesty, and the harms of its absence  c) good manners in the family, shown in greeting, listening and helping  d) what self-control is, and how a self-controlled child acts
**Answer: b) the benefits of honesty, and the harms of its absence** — That is the one thing this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does “benefit” mean?
a) a good that something brings  b) a harm that something causes  c) a visible sign in conduct that a person is truthful  d) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.
**Answer: a) a good that something brings** — “benefit” means: a good that something brings.

**3.** What does “damage” mean?
a) a good that something brings  b) a visible sign in conduct that a person is truthful  c) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  d) a harm that something causes
**Answer: d) a harm that something causes** — “damage” means: a harm that something causes.

**4.** What does “mark (of a truthful person)” mean?
a) a harm that something causes  b) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  c) a visible sign in conduct that a person is truthful  d) a good that something brings
**Answer: c) a visible sign in conduct that a person is truthful** — “mark (of a truthful person)” means: a visible sign in conduct that a person is truthful.

**5.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  b) Against the benefits stand the damages of honesty's absence   c) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  d) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.
**Answer: b) Against the benefits stand the damages of honesty's absence ** — It is one of this lesson's own teachings.
