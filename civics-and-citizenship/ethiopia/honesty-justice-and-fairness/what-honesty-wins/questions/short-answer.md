# What Honesty Wins, What Dishonesty Costs — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name one benefit of being truthful and honest.
**Answer:** Trust — honest people are believed, relied on and given responsibility (the chapter's closing content).

**2.** How must pupils prove this lesson?
**Answer:** By showing the marks of a truthful person in their own conduct, and identifying the harms of dishonesty.

**3.** In your own words, what is benefit?
**Answer:** A good that something brings.
