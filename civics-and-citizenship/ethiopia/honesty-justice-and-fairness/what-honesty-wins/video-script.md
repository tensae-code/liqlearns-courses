# Video script — What Honesty Wins, What Dishonesty Costs

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Truthfulness and honesty win trust and honour — and their absence brings real harms; a truthful person shows the marks in daily conduct.

The chapter closes with the importance of truthfulness and honesty, and the benefits of being a truthful and honest person.

The first benefit is trust: honest people are believed, relied on, and given responsibility.

Against the benefits stand the damages of honesty's absence — pupils must identify the harms of dishonesty as a chapter outcome.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
