# Hands-on — What Honesty Wins, What Dishonesty Costs

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Think of someone everyone trusts with important things. Now think of someone no one trusts with anything. What did each one build, act by act? This lesson names the two roads.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the benefits of honesty, and the harms of its absence. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
