# The Rule of Justice — Same Work, Same Treatment — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** State the main principle of justice.
**Answer:** Equals are treated equally and unequals unequally — people are treated equally unless they differ in a relevant way.

**2.** In the worked example, why must Jack and Jill be paid equally?
**Answer:** Because they do the same work equally; colour and sex are not relevant to the work.

**3.** In your own words, what is relevant?
**Answer:** Actually connected to the matter at hand — here, to the work done.
