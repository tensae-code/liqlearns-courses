# The Rule of Justice — Same Work, Same Treatment — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The main principle of justice: equals are treated equally, and unequals unequally.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Jack and Jill do the same work equally, so under justice they must be paid equally.
**Answer: True**

**3.** It is just to pay a worker more because of his colour, even when the work is the same.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite.
