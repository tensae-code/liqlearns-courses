# The Rule of Justice — Same Work, Same Treatment — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) showing fairness, and standing against injustice  b) what school discipline means: guidance to a standard of conduct  c) the ten elements of local culture  d) same work, same treatment — differences must be relevant
**Answer: d) same work, same treatment — differences must be relevant** — That is the one thing this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does “relevant” mean?
a) the same treatment for people who are equal in the relevant way  b) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  c) actually connected to the matter at hand — here, to the work done  d) a standing rule that decides many cases
**Answer: c) actually connected to the matter at hand — here, to the work done** — “relevant” means: actually connected to the matter at hand — here, to the work done.

**3.** What does “principle” mean?
a) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  b) a standing rule that decides many cases  c) actually connected to the matter at hand — here, to the work done  d) the same treatment for people who are equal in the relevant way
**Answer: b) a standing rule that decides many cases** — “principle” means: a standing rule that decides many cases.

**4.** What does “equal treatment” mean?
a) the same treatment for people who are equal in the relevant way  b) actually connected to the matter at hand — here, to the work done  c) a standing rule that decides many cases  d) The strongest person may take whatever they want.
**Answer: a) the same treatment for people who are equal in the relevant way** — “equal treatment” means: the same treatment for people who are equal in the relevant way.

**5.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  b) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  c) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  d) Jack and Jill do the same work equally, so under justice they must be paid equally
**Answer: d) Jack and Jill do the same work equally, so under justice they must be paid equally** — It is one of this lesson's own teachings.
