# Video script — The Rule of Justice — Same Work, Same Treatment

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The rule of justice: equals are treated equally, and a difference in treatment is just only when it rests on a difference that is relevant.

The main principle of justice: equals are treated equally, and unequals unequally.

In today's form: people are treated equally unless they differ in a way that is relevant to the treatment.

Jack and Jill do the same work equally, so under justice they must be paid equally.

Paying Jack more because he is white, or because of sex, is an unjust difference — colour and sex are not relevant to the work.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
