# Video script — Justice — Giving Each Person What Is Due

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Justice is giving every person what is due to them — the measure of rightness at the centre of moral life.

Justice is giving every person what is due to him or her.

It is presented as the central pillar of morality — the idea at the heart of right and wrong between people.

Justice and fairness (ሚዛናዊነት) are close, but not the same word.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
