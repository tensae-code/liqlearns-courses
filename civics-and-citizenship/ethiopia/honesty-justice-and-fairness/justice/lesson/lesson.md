# Justice — Giving Each Person What Is Due

## Do it first

Share something fairly today — food, turns, time — giving each person their due share, not the same favour to your favourite. That act is this lesson's whole subject.

## What justice is

Justice is giving every person what is due to him or her.

It is presented as the central pillar of morality — the idea at the heart of right and wrong between people.

[Picture 28029 — a balance — equal shares]

## Justice and fairness

Justice and fairness (ሚዛናዊነት) are close, but not the same word.

Justice is chiefly the standard or measure of rightness.

Fairness is the capacity to judge without putting your own feeling or interest into the judgement.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: justice is giving every person what is due to them — the measure of rightness at the centre of moral life. Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood, and name the lesson's key words: justice, due, fairness (ሚዛናዊነት). Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
