# Justice — Giving Each Person What Is Due — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is justice?
**Answer:** Giving every person what is due to him or her.

**2.** How is fairness different from justice?
**Answer:** Justice is the measure of rightness; fairness is judging without your own feeling or interest in it.

**3.** In your own words, what is justice?
**Answer:** Giving every person what is due to them.
