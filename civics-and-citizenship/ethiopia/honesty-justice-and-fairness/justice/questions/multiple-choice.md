# Justice — Giving Each Person What Is Due — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) justice: each person given what is due  b) honesty: no lying, no cheating, no stealing  c) what disrespect is, and what it breaks  d) the four rule areas: time, grounds, lessons, dress
**Answer: a) justice: each person given what is due** — That is the one thing this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does “justice” mean?
a) what rightly belongs to a person in a case  b) judging without putting your own feeling or interest in  c) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  d) giving every person what is due to them
**Answer: d) giving every person what is due to them** — “justice” means: giving every person what is due to them.

**3.** What does “due” mean?
a) judging without putting your own feeling or interest in  b) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  c) what rightly belongs to a person in a case  d) giving every person what is due to them
**Answer: c) what rightly belongs to a person in a case** — “due” means: what rightly belongs to a person in a case.

**4.** What does “fairness (ሚዛናዊነት)” mean?
a) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  b) judging without putting your own feeling or interest in  c) giving every person what is due to them  d) what rightly belongs to a person in a case
**Answer: b) judging without putting your own feeling or interest in** — “fairness (ሚዛናዊነት)” means: judging without putting your own feeling or interest in.

**5.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Justice and fairness (ሚዛናዊነት) are close, but not the same word  b) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  c) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  d) The strongest person may take whatever they want.
**Answer: a) Justice and fairness (ሚዛናዊነት) are close, but not the same word** — It is one of this lesson's own teachings.
