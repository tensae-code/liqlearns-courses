# Justice — Giving Each Person What Is Due — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Justice is giving every person what is due to him or her.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Justice and fairness (ሚዛናዊነት) are close, but not the same word.
**Answer: True**

**3.** Justice means giving the biggest share to the person you like most.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite.
