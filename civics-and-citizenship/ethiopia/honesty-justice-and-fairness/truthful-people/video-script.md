# Video script — Truthful People — Kebede and Yonatan

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Truthful people are examples to learn from: teacher kebede, a truthful man — and the story of yonatan, who tells the truth.

Teacher Kebede is named as a truthful man, and pupils are to learn the trait from him.

The second example is the story “Yonatan tells the truth”: pupils first write their own view, then discuss in groups the good traits of a truthful and honest person.

A third activity works on the harms of cheating and stealing — from parents, from friends and from neighbours.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
