# Hands-on — Truthful People — Kebede and Yonatan

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name one person you know who is famous among your people for telling the truth. What do others say about that person? This lesson learns from two such examples.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: learning truthfulness from Kebede and from Yonatan's story. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
