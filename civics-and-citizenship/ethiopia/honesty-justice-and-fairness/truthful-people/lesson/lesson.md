# Truthful People — Kebede and Yonatan

## Do it first

Name one person you know who is famous among your people for telling the truth. What do others say about that person? This lesson learns from two such examples.

## Two truthful examples

Teacher Kebede is named as a truthful man, and pupils are to learn the trait from him.

The second example is the story “Yonatan tells the truth”: pupils first write their own view, then discuss in groups the good traits of a truthful and honest person.

[Picture 28027 — telling the truth while classmates listen]

## The harms on the other side

A third activity works on the harms of cheating and stealing — from parents, from friends and from neighbours.

Cheating and stealing break the trust that families, friendships and neighbourhoods stand on.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: truthful people are examples to learn from: Teacher Kebede, a truthful man — and the story of Yonatan, who tells the truth. Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood, and name the lesson's key words: trait, example. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: learning truthfulness from Kebede and from Yonatan's story.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
