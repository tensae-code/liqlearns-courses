# Truthful People — Kebede and Yonatan — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Teacher Kebede is named as a truthful man, and pupils are to learn the trait from him.
**Answer: True**

**2.** A third activity works on the harms of cheating and stealing .
**Answer: True**

**3.** Cheating your parents or stealing from a friend harms no one, as long as you are not caught.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite.
