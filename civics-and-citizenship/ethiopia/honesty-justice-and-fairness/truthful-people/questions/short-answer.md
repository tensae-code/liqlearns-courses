# Truthful People — Kebede and Yonatan — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Who is Kebede, in this chapter?
**Answer:** A teacher named as a truthful man, whose trait pupils learn from.

**2.** What do pupils do with the Yonatan story, in the book's order?
**Answer:** First write their own view, then discuss in groups the good traits of a truthful and honest person.

**3.** In your own words, what is trait?
**Answer:** A quality of character a person is known by.
