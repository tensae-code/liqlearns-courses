# Truthful People — Kebede and Yonatan — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) cultural heritage: what one generation hands to the next  b) doing good for others, at school and at home  c) learning truthfulness from Kebede and from Yonatan's story  d) what self-control is, and how a self-controlled child acts
**Answer: c) learning truthfulness from Kebede and from Yonatan's story** — That is the one thing this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does “trait” mean?
a) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  b) a quality of character a person is known by  c) a person or case others learn from  d) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.
**Answer: b) a quality of character a person is known by** — “trait” means: a quality of character a person is known by.

**3.** What does “example” mean?
a) a person or case others learn from  b) a quality of character a person is known by  c) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  d) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.
**Answer: a) a person or case others learn from** — “example” means: a person or case others learn from.

**4.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  b) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  c) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  d) A third activity works on the harms of cheating and stealing 
**Answer: d) A third activity works on the harms of cheating and stealing ** — It is one of this lesson's own teachings.

**5.** Which of these is true in this lesson?
a) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  b) Promises, truth and fairness are not needed between people.  c) Teacher Kebede is named as a truthful man, and pupils are to learn the trait from him  d) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.
**Answer: c) Teacher Kebede is named as a truthful man, and pupils are to learn the trait from him** — It is stated in the lesson.
