# Honesty — No Lying, No Cheating, No Stealing — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Honesty is defined as not even thinking of lying, cheating or stealing.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Lies differ in weight .
**Answer: True**

**3.** A lie told for a good end is no longer a lie.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite.
