# Honesty — No Lying, No Cheating, No Stealing — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) what school discipline means: guidance to a standard of conduct  b) honesty: no lying, no cheating, no stealing  c) community values: the beliefs we share  d) fairness in daily life: rules kept, nobody excluded
**Answer: b) honesty: no lying, no cheating, no stealing** — That is the one thing this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does “honesty” mean?
a) not even thinking of lying, cheating or stealing  b) knowingly saying what is false  c) breaking the rules to gain — kin to lying  d) taking what is not yours
**Answer: a) not even thinking of lying, cheating or stealing** — “honesty” means: not even thinking of lying, cheating or stealing.

**3.** What does “lying” mean?
a) not even thinking of lying, cheating or stealing  b) breaking the rules to gain — kin to lying  c) taking what is not yours  d) knowingly saying what is false
**Answer: d) knowingly saying what is false** — “lying” means: knowingly saying what is false.

**4.** What does “cheating” mean?
a) knowingly saying what is false  b) taking what is not yours  c) breaking the rules to gain — kin to lying  d) not even thinking of lying, cheating or stealing
**Answer: c) breaking the rules to gain — kin to lying** — “cheating” means: breaking the rules to gain — kin to lying.

**5.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  b) Lies differ in weight   c) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  d) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.
**Answer: b) Lies differ in weight ** — It is one of this lesson's own teachings.
