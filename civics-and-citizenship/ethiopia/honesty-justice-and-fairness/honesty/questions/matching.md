# Honesty — No Lying, No Cheating, No Stealing — matching

Match each word with its meaning.

**1.** honesty — d)
**2.** lying — c)
**3.** cheating — b)
**4.** stealing — a)

Meanings:
a) taking what is not yours
b) breaking the rules to gain — kin to lying
c) knowingly saying what is false
d) not even thinking of lying, cheating or stealing

**Answer key:** 1. d; 2. c; 3. b; 4. a
