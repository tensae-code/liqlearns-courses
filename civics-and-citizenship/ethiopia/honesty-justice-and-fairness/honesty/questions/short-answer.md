# Honesty — No Lying, No Cheating, No Stealing — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is lying?
**Answer:** Knowingly saying what is false.

**2.** Why do people lie, as the reading gives it?
**Answer:** To escape difficulty, or to gain from the lie.

**3.** In your own words, what is honesty?
**Answer:** Not even thinking of lying, cheating or stealing.
