# Honesty — No Lying, No Cheating, No Stealing

## Do it first

Think of a time you were tempted to hide a mistake with a small lie. What stopped you — or what happened after? Read this lesson with that moment in mind.

## Three things honesty refuses

Honesty is defined as not even thinking of lying, cheating or stealing.

Lying is knowingly saying what is false — the opposite of honesty.

Stealing is taking what is not yours; cheating breaks the law and is kin to lying.

## No good excuse makes a lie good

Lies differ in weight — from the small “white lie” to lies that wreck people's dealings with each other.

But even a lie told for a good end remains a lie.

People lie to escape difficulty, or to gain from the lie — knowing the reasons helps a child refuse them.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: honesty means not even thinking of lying, cheating or stealing — and a lie told for a good end is still a lie. Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood, and name the lesson's key words: honesty, lying, cheating. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
