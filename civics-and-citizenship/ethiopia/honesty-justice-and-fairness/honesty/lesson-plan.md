## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Honesty is defined as not even thinking of lying, cheating or stealing.
2. Lying is knowingly saying what is false — the opposite of honesty.
3. Stealing is taking what is not yours; cheating breaks the law and is kin to lying.
4. Lies differ in weight — from the small “white lie” to lies that wreck people's dealings with each other.
5. But even a lie told for a good end remains a lie.
