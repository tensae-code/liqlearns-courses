# Video script — Honesty — No Lying, No Cheating, No Stealing

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Honesty means not even thinking of lying, cheating or stealing — and a lie told for a good end is still a lie.

Honesty is defined as not even thinking of lying, cheating or stealing.

Lying is knowingly saying what is false — the opposite of honesty.

Stealing is taking what is not yours; cheating breaks the law and is kin to lying.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
