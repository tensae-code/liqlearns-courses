# Culture — a People's Way of Life — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) culture: a people's way of life, handed on  b) why cooperation and teamwork matter  c) the skills of teamwork, and the seven problem-solving steps  d) the political, economic and social benefits of heritage
**Answer: a) culture: a people's way of life, handed on** — That is the one thing this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does “culture” mean?
a) a story handed on by telling, not by writing  b) all the people born and living at one stage — grandparents, parents, children  c) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  d) a group's way of life, expressed in music, belief, dress, foods and other practices
**Answer: d) a group's way of life, expressed in music, belief, dress, foods and other practices** — “culture” means: a group's way of life, expressed in music, belief, dress, foods and other practices.

**3.** What does “oral narrative” mean?
a) all the people born and living at one stage — grandparents, parents, children  b) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  c) a story handed on by telling, not by writing  d) a group's way of life, expressed in music, belief, dress, foods and other practices
**Answer: c) a story handed on by telling, not by writing** — “oral narrative” means: a story handed on by telling, not by writing.

**4.** What does “generation” mean?
a) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  b) all the people born and living at one stage — grandparents, parents, children  c) a group's way of life, expressed in music, belief, dress, foods and other practices  d) a story handed on by telling, not by writing
**Answer: b) all the people born and living at one stage — grandparents, parents, children** — “generation” means: all the people born and living at one stage — grandparents, parents, children.

**5.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) It is passed from generation to generation in writing and in oral narratives  b) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  c) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  d) The strongest person may take whatever they want.
**Answer: a) It is passed from generation to generation in writing and in oral narratives** — It is one of this lesson's own teachings.
