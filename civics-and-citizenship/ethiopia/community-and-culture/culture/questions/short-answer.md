# Culture — a People's Way of Life — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is culture?
**Answer:** A group's way of life.

**2.** In what two ways is culture passed on?
**Answer:** In writing, and in oral narratives.

**3.** In your own words, what is culture?
**Answer:** A group's way of life, expressed in music, belief, dress, foods and other practices.
