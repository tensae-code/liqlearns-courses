# Lesson plan — Culture — a People's Way of Life

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Community, Culture and Many Cultures · **Lesson:** Culture — a People's Way of Life
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 6 — Community, Culture and Many Cultures (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** culture is a group's way of life — expressed in music, belief, dress, foods and other practices, and passed from generation to generation in writing and in oral narrative.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the lessons before it in this title — the learner already knows the earlier circles of this course (myself, then family, neighbours, school, community and culture) as far as this title. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short, single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09).

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Why Shared Values Help a Community (the previous lesson in this title). Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Culture is a group's way of life.
2. It can be expressed in many ways — music, belief, dress, foods and other practices.
3. It is passed from generation to generation in writing and in oral narratives.
4. One activity shows an elder telling a story to a group of children as one way culture is handed on.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson's scenes are the learner's own home, school and neighbourhood.

## If the learner is stuck
- Believes that “culture is passed on only in writing; stories told aloud carry nothing” → go back to the lesson's own words: culture is a group's way of life — expressed in music, belief, dress, foods and other practices, and passed from generation to generation in writing and in oral narrative. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
- Gives the words but no example → ask for the lesson happening once in their own home, school or street today; the example is the proof.
