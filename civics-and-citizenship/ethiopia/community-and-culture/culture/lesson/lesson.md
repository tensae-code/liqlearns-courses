# Culture — a People's Way of Life

## Do it first

Ask an elder to tell you one short story from their childhood — or remember one you have heard. That telling is culture on the move. This lesson names what moved.

## What culture is

Culture is a group's way of life.

It can be expressed in many ways — music, belief, dress, foods and other practices.

It is passed from generation to generation in writing and in oral narratives.

One activity shows an elder telling a story to a group of children as one way culture is handed on.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: culture is a group's way of life — expressed in music, belief, dress, foods and other practices, and passed from generation to generation in writing and in oral narrative. Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood, and name the lesson's key words: culture, oral narrative, generation. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: culture: a people's way of life, handed on.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
