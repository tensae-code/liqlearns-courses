# Hands-on — Culture — a People's Way of Life

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Ask an elder to tell you one short story from their childhood — or remember one you have heard. That telling is culture on the move. This lesson names what moved.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: culture: a people's way of life, handed on. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
