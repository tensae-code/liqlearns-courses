# Video script — Culture — a People's Way of Life

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Culture is a group's way of life — expressed in music, belief, dress, foods and other practices, and passed from generation to generation in writing and in oral narrative.

Culture is a group's way of life.

It can be expressed in many ways — music, belief, dress, foods and other practices.

It is passed from generation to generation in writing and in oral narratives.

One activity shows an elder telling a story to a group of children as one way culture is handed on.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
