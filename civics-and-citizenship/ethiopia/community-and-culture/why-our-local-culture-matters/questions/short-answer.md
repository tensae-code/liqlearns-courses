# Why Our Local Culture Matters — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the three goods of local culture.
**Answer:** It grows tolerance; improves quality of life; helps build relationships and the community's overall wellbeing.

**2.** What does local culture grow between different people?
**Answer:** Tolerance.

**3.** In your own words, what is quality of life?
**Answer:** How good life is, day by day — health, peace, sufficiency.
