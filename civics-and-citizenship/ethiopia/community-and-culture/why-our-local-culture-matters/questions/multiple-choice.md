# Why Our Local Culture Matters — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) the moral values and principles a family lives by  b) the three goods of local culture  c) the eleven marks of being human  d) how honesty is learned and grown
**Answer: b) the three goods of local culture** — That is the one thing this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does “quality of life” mean?
a) how good life is, day by day — health, peace, sufficiency  b) the state of being well in body, mind and community  c) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  d) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.
**Answer: a) how good life is, day by day — health, peace, sufficiency** — “quality of life” means: how good life is, day by day — health, peace, sufficiency.

**3.** What does “wellbeing” mean?
a) how good life is, day by day — health, peace, sufficiency  b) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  c) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  d) the state of being well in body, mind and community
**Answer: d) the state of being well in body, mind and community** — “wellbeing” means: the state of being well in body, mind and community.

**4.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  b) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  c) It helps to improve the quality of life  d) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.
**Answer: c) It helps to improve the quality of life** — It is one of this lesson's own teachings.

**5.** Which of these is true in this lesson?
a) Promises, truth and fairness are not needed between people.  b) Local culture helps to grow tolerance  c) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  d) The strongest person may take whatever they want.
**Answer: b) Local culture helps to grow tolerance** — It is stated in the lesson.
