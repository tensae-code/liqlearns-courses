# Why Our Local Culture Matters

## Do it first

Name one celebration or practice of your locality that brings everyone together. Feel what it does to the people in it. This lesson names its three goods.

## The three goods

Local culture helps to grow tolerance.

It helps to improve the quality of life.

It is also a help for building relationships and achieving the community's overall wellbeing.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: local culture matters because it grows tolerance, improves the quality of life, and helps build relationships and the community's overall wellbeing. Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood, and name the lesson's key words: quality of life, wellbeing. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: the three goods of local culture.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
