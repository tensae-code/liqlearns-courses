# Our Customs — the Good We Keep, the Harmful We Stop — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) the obstacles to friendship — and the greedy dog  b) multiculturalism: many cultures together  c) good customs we keep, harmful practices we stop  d) debo, idir and equb — Ethiopia works together
**Answer: c) good customs we keep, harmful practices we stop** — That is the one thing this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does “custom” mean?
a) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  b) a practice a people habitually keeps  c) carrying a person off by force — named among the harmful practices  d) a custom that injures people's bodies or rights
**Answer: b) a practice a people habitually keeps** — “custom” means: a practice a people habitually keeps.

**3.** What does “abduction (ጠለፋ)” mean?
a) carrying a person off by force — named among the harmful practices  b) a practice a people habitually keeps  c) a custom that injures people's bodies or rights  d) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.
**Answer: a) carrying a person off by force — named among the harmful practices** — “abduction (ጠለፋ)” means: carrying a person off by force — named among the harmful practices.

**4.** What does “harmful practice” mean?
a) a practice a people habitually keeps  b) carrying a person off by force — named among the harmful practices  c) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  d) a custom that injures people's bodies or rights
**Answer: d) a custom that injures people's bodies or rights** — “harmful practice” means: a custom that injures people's bodies or rights.

**5.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  b) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  c) The harmful practices are named plainly, in words only  d) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.
**Answer: c) The harmful practices are named plainly, in words only** — It is one of this lesson's own teachings.
