# Our Customs — the Good We Keep, the Harmful We Stop — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Good practices are a basis for the country's development and change.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The harmful practices are named plainly, in words only: female circumcision, under-age marriage, cutting the face in mourning, ….
**Answer: True**

**3.** All customs must be kept forever, even the ones that injure bodies.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite.
