# Our Customs — the Good We Keep, the Harmful We Stop — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name three good customs to keep.
**Answer:** Any three of: respecting elders; idir; equb; helping the needy; working together; hospitality.

**2.** Why are the harmful practices stopped?
**Answer:** Because they injure people's bodies and rights.

**3.** In your own words, what is custom?
**Answer:** A practice a people habitually keeps.
