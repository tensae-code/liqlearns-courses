# Video script — Our Customs — the Good We Keep, the Harmful We Stop

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A people's customs are sorted by equality: keep the good — respecting elders, idir, equb, helping the needy, working together, hospitality — and stop the harmful practices that injure bodies and rights.

Good practices are a basis for the country's development and change.

They are: respecting elders; idir and equb — the community's mutual-aid and rotating-savings associations; helping the needy; working together; and hospitality.

The harmful practices are named plainly, in words only: female circumcision, under-age marriage, cutting the face in mourning, cutting the body with blades, wastefulness, and abduction (ጠለፋ).

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
