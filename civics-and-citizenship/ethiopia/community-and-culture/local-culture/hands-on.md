# Hands-on — Local Culture — What We Share Where We Live

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name three things people in your own locality share that outsiders would notice — a dress, a food, a greeting, a hairstyle. Those are your local culture. Now meet the book's example.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: local culture: what we share where we live. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
