# Video script — Local Culture — What We Share Where We Live

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Local culture is what people share in their daily lives in the place where they live — the hamer community's dress, hairstyle and ornaments are the book's worked example.

Local culture is anything people share in their daily lives in the place where they live — their shared experiences and customs.

The chapter's worked example is the Hamer community.

Two pictures show Hamer local culture in dress, hairstyle and ornaments, and pupils discuss what the people in the pictures share.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
