# Local Culture — What We Share Where We Live — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Local culture is anything people share in their daily lives in the place where they live .
**Answer: True**

**2.** The chapter's worked example is the Hamer community.
**Answer: True**

**3.** Local culture is what people keep private and never share with anyone in their place.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite.
