# Local Culture — What We Share Where We Live — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is local culture?
**Answer:** Anything people share in their daily lives in the place where they live — shared experiences and customs.

**2.** What do the Hamer pictures show?
**Answer:** Hamer local culture in dress, hairstyle and ornaments.

**3.** In your own words, what is local culture?
**Answer:** What people share in daily life in the place where they live.
