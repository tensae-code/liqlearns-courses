# Local Culture — What We Share Where We Live — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) the three goods of local culture  b) the five ways of protecting heritage  c) same work, same treatment — differences must be relevant  d) local culture: what we share where we live
**Answer: d) local culture: what we share where we live** — That is the one thing this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does “local culture” mean?
a) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  b) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  c) what people share in daily life in the place where they live  d) the practices a people habitually share
**Answer: c) what people share in daily life in the place where they live** — “local culture” means: what people share in daily life in the place where they live.

**3.** What does “customs” mean?
