# Local Culture — What We Share Where We Live

## Do it first

Name three things people in your own locality share that outsiders would notice — a dress, a food, a greeting, a hairstyle. Those are your local culture. Now meet the book's example.

## What local culture is

Local culture is anything people share in their daily lives in the place where they live — their shared experiences and customs.

## The Hamer example

The chapter's worked example is the Hamer community.

Two pictures show Hamer local culture in dress, hairstyle and ornaments, and pupils discuss what the people in the pictures share.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: local culture is what people share in their daily lives in the place where they live — the Hamer community's dress, hairstyle and ornaments are the book's worked example. Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood, and name the lesson's key words: local culture, customs. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
