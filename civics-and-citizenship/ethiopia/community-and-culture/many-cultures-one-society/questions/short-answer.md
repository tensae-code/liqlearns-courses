# Many Cultures, One Society — What Multiculturalism Means — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is multiculturalism?
**Answer:** The presence of many cultures together.

**2.** What does diversity give individuals?
**Answer:** The chance to have meaningful choices — since every culture has its own social life and organisation.

**3.** In your own words, what is multiculturalism?
**Answer:** The presence of many cultures together in one society.
