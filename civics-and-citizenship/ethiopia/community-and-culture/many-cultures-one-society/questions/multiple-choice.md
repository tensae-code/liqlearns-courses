# Many Cultures, One Society — What Multiculturalism Means — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) showing fairness, and standing against injustice  b) multiculturalism: many cultures together  c) culture: a people's way of life, handed on  d) debo, idir and equb — Ethiopia works together
**Answer: b) multiculturalism: many cultures together** — That is the one thing this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does “multiculturalism” mean?
a) the presence of many cultures together in one society  b) many different kinds living side by side  c) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  d) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.
**Answer: a) the presence of many cultures together in one society** — “multiculturalism” means: the presence of many cultures together in one society.

**3.** What does “diversity” mean?
a) the presence of many cultures together in one society  b) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  c) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  d) many different kinds living side by side
**Answer: d) many different kinds living side by side** — “diversity” means: many different kinds living side by side.

**4.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  b) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  c) Every culture has its own social life and organisation   d) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.
**Answer: c) Every culture has its own social life and organisation ** — It is one of this lesson's own teachings.

**5.** Which of these is true in this lesson?
a) Promises, truth and fairness are not needed between people.  b) Multiculturalism is the presence of many cultures together  c) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  d) The strongest person may take whatever they want.
**Answer: b) Multiculturalism is the presence of many cultures together** — It is stated in the lesson.
