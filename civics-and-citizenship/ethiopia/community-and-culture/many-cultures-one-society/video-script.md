# Video script — Many Cultures, One Society — What Multiculturalism Means

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Multiculturalism is the presence of many cultures together in one society — and because every culture has its own social life, diversity gives individuals meaningful choices.

Multiculturalism is the presence of many cultures together.

Every culture has its own social life and organisation — so cultural diversity gives individuals the chance to have meaningful choices.

Pupils discuss the concept in pairs and bring living examples from their own society.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
