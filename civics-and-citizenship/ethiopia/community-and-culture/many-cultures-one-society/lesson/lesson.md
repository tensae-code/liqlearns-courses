# Many Cultures, One Society — What Multiculturalism Means

## Do it first

Walk one market street in your mind: how many peoples' dress, foods and languages meet there? That meeting is this lesson's word — multiculturalism.

## The meaning

Multiculturalism is the presence of many cultures together.

Every culture has its own social life and organisation — so cultural diversity gives individuals the chance to have meaningful choices.

Pupils discuss the concept in pairs and bring living examples from their own society.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: multiculturalism is the presence of many cultures together in one society — and because every culture has its own social life, diversity gives individuals meaningful choices. Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood, and name the lesson's key words: multiculturalism, diversity. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
