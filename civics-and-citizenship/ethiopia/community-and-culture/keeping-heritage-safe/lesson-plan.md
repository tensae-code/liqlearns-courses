# Lesson plan — Keeping Heritage Safe — Teach, Record, Watch, Protect by Law, Belong

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Community, Culture and Many Cultures · **Lesson:** Keeping Heritage Safe — Teach, Record, Watch, Protect by Law, Belong
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 6 — Community, Culture and Many Cultures (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** heritage is protected by keeping it as it is — through five ways in the book's order: awareness, records, monitoring, legal frameworks, and local people visiting so they feel ownership.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the lessons before it in this title — the learner already knows the earlier circles of this course (myself, then family, neighbours, school, community and culture) as far as this title. It needs nothing else.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Heritage in Danger — Looting, Neglect and Disaster (the previous lesson in this title). Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Protection means keeping a thing as it is — guarding its safety, original identity, size, shape, colour, character and meaning.
2. First, awareness.
3. Second, records.
4. Third, continuing and periodic monitoring of the heritage and its site's history and condition.
5. Fourth, legal frameworks that protect it.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson's scenes are the learner's own home, school and neighbourhood.
