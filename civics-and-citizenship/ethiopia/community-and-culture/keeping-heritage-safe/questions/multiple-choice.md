# Keeping Heritage Safe — Teach, Record, Watch, Protect by Law, Belong — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) the five ways of protecting heritage  b) how positive interaction helps learning  c) every person is free and has dignity — so we respect them  d) good manners in the family, shown in greeting, listening and helping
**Answer: a) the five ways of protecting heritage** — That is the one thing this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does “protection (of heritage)” mean?
a) continuing and periodic checking of a thing's condition  b) a kept collection of records, digital and paper  c) the sense that a thing is ours, to care for  d) keeping a thing as it is — its safety, identity, size, shape, colour, character and meaning
**Answer: d) keeping a thing as it is — its safety, identity, size, shape, colour, character and meaning** — “protection (of heritage)” means: keeping a thing as it is — its safety, identity, size, shape, colour, character and meaning.

**3.** What does “monitoring” mean?
a) a kept collection of records, digital and paper  b) the sense that a thing is ours, to care for  c) continuing and periodic checking of a thing's condition  d) keeping a thing as it is — its safety, identity, size, shape, colour, character and meaning
**Answer: c) continuing and periodic checking of a thing's condition** — “monitoring” means: continuing and periodic checking of a thing's condition.

**4.** What does “archive” mean?
a) the sense that a thing is ours, to care for  b) a kept collection of records, digital and paper  c) keeping a thing as it is — its safety, identity, size, shape, colour, character and meaning  d) continuing and periodic checking of a thing's condition
**Answer: b) a kept collection of records, digital and paper** — “archive” means: a kept collection of records, digital and paper.

**5.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Third, continuing and periodic monitoring of the heritage and its site's history and condition  b) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  c) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  d) The strongest person may take whatever they want.
**Answer: a) Third, continuing and periodic monitoring of the heritage and its site's history and condition** — It is one of this lesson's own teachings.
