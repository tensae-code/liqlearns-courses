# Keeping Heritage Safe — Teach, Record, Watch, Protect by Law, Belong — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Protection means keeping a thing as it is .
**Answer: True**

**2.** First, awareness: teach the public what heritage is worth, what can harm it and how its safety is kept .
**Answer: True**

**3.** Protection means changing a heritage item freely so it looks new.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite.
