# Keeping Heritage Safe — Teach, Record, Watch, Protect by Law, Belong — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** List the five ways in the book's order.
**Answer:** Awareness; records; continuing and periodic monitoring; legal frameworks; local people visiting for a small fee so they feel ownership.

**2.** What does protection keep unchanged?
**Answer:** A thing's safety, original identity, size, shape, colour, character and meaning.

**3.** In your own words, what is protection (of heritage)?
**Answer:** Keeping a thing as it is — its safety, identity, size, shape, colour, character and meaning.
