# Lesson plan — Cultural Values — the Principles a Community Stands On

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Community, Culture and Many Cultures · **Lesson:** Cultural Values — the Principles a Community Stands On
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 6 — Community, Culture and Many Cultures (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** cultural values are the basic principles and ideas a whole community is founded on — beliefs — distinct from customs, and from culture itself, the community's guide.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the lessons before it in this title — the learner already knows the earlier circles of this course (myself, then family, neighbours, school, community and culture) as far as this title. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Sub lessons

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Why Our Local Culture Matters (the previous lesson in this title). Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Cultural values are the basic principles and ideas on which a whole community is founded — and they are beliefs.
2. Customs are traditions and ceremonies.
3. Culture itself is the community's guide.
4. Pupils are to value their own cultural values and show them.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson's scenes are the learner's own home, school and neighbourhood.

## If the learner is stuck
- Believes that “cultural values and customs are exactly the same thing” → go back to the lesson's own words: cultural values are the basic principles and ideas a whole community is founded on — beliefs — distinct from customs, and from culture itself, the community's guide. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
- Gives the words but no example → ask for the lesson happening once in their own home, school or street today; the example is the proof.
