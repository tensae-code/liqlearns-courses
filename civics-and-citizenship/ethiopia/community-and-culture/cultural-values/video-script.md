# Video script — Cultural Values — the Principles a Community Stands On

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

This lesson is about one thing.
Cultural values are the basic principles and ideas a whole community is founded on — beliefs — distinct from customs, and from culture itself, the community's guide.

Cultural values are the basic principles and ideas on which a whole community is founded — and they are beliefs.

Customs are traditions and ceremonies.

Culture itself is the community's guide.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
