# Cultural Values — the Principles a Community Stands On — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) cultural values: the principles a community stands on  b) the moral values and principles a family lives by  c) responsibilities at school, and the rights that go with them  d) the obstacles to friendship — and the greedy dog
**Answer: a) cultural values: the principles a community stands on** — That is the one thing this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does “cultural values” mean?
a) traditions and ceremonies  b) the community's guide — its whole way of life  c) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  d) the basic principles and ideas a community is founded on — beliefs
**Answer: d) the basic principles and ideas a community is founded on — beliefs** — “cultural values” means: the basic principles and ideas a community is founded on — beliefs.

**3.** What does “customs (second sense)” mean?
a) the community's guide — its whole way of life  b) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  c) traditions and ceremonies  d) the basic principles and ideas a community is founded on — beliefs
**Answer: c) traditions and ceremonies** — “customs (second sense)” means: traditions and ceremonies.

**4.** What does “culture (the guide)” mean?
a) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  b) the community's guide — its whole way of life  c) the basic principles and ideas a community is founded on — beliefs  d) traditions and ceremonies
**Answer: b) the community's guide — its whole way of life** — “culture (the guide)” means: the community's guide — its whole way of life.

**5.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Culture itself is the community's guide  b) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  c) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  d) The strongest person may take whatever they want.
**Answer: a) Culture itself is the community's guide** — It is one of this lesson's own teachings.
