# Cultural Values — the Principles a Community Stands On

## Do it first

Name one principle your community would never give up — the stone it stands on. Then sort three words as you read: culture, customs, cultural values. They are not the same word.

## Three words, kept distinct

Cultural values are the basic principles and ideas on which a whole community is founded — and they are beliefs.

Customs are traditions and ceremonies.

Culture itself is the community's guide.

Pupils are to value their own cultural values and show them.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: cultural values are the basic principles and ideas a whole community is founded on — beliefs — distinct from customs, and from culture itself, the community's guide. Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood, and name the lesson's key words: cultural values, customs (second sense), culture (the guide). Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
