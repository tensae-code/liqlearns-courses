# Video script — Kinds of Heritage — Things We Can Touch and Things We Carry in Song

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Heritage is tangible or intangible: tangible divides into movable (coins, writings, clothes, transport) and immovable (monuments, churches, bridges); intangible lives in songs, oral literature and religious festivals.

Tangible heritage is what can be seen and touched.

Movable heritage can be carried from place to place: coins, writings, clothes and means of transport.

Immovable heritage's base is tied to the land: monuments, castles, graves, mosques, churches, palaces and bridges.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
