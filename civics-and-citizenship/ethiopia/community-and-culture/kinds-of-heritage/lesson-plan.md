# Lesson plan — Kinds of Heritage — Things We Can Touch and Things We Carry in Song

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Community, Culture and Many Cultures · **Lesson:** Kinds of Heritage — Things We Can Touch and Things We Carry in Song
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 6 — Community, Culture and Many Cultures (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** heritage is tangible or intangible: tangible divides into movable (coins, writings, clothes, transport) and immovable (monuments, churches, bridges); intangible lives in songs, oral literature and religious festivals.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the lessons before it in this title — the learner already knows the earlier circles of this course (myself, then family, neighbours, school, community and culture) as far as this title. It needs nothing else.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Tangible heritage is what can be seen and touched.
2. Movable heritage can be carried from place to place.
3. Immovable heritage's base is tied to the land.
4. Intangible heritage is glossed in the key words as songs, oral literature and religious festivals.
5. One activity has pupils research two intangible heritages from foods, drinks, dress styles and festival-day practices.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson's scenes are the learner's own home, school and neighbourhood.
