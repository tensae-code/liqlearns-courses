# Kinds of Heritage — Things We Can Touch and Things We Carry in Song — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Tangible heritage is what can be seen and touched.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Intangible heritage is glossed in the key words as songs, oral literature and religious festivals.
**Answer: True**

**3.** A song is tangible heritage because you can touch it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite.
