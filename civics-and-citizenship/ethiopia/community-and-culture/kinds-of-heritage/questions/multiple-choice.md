# Kinds of Heritage — Things We Can Touch and Things We Carry in Song — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) culture: a people's way of life, handed on  b) fairness in daily life: rules kept, nobody excluded  c) tangible and intangible heritage; movable and immovable  d) same work, same treatment — differences must be relevant
**Answer: c) tangible and intangible heritage; movable and immovable** — That is the one thing this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does “tangible” mean?
a) heritage carried in songs, oral literature and religious festivals  b) can be seen and touched  c) tangible heritage that can be carried from place to place  d) tangible heritage whose base is tied to the land
**Answer: b) can be seen and touched** — “tangible” means: can be seen and touched.

**3.** What does “movable heritage” mean?
a) tangible heritage that can be carried from place to place  b) can be seen and touched  c) tangible heritage whose base is tied to the land  d) heritage carried in songs, oral literature and religious festivals
**Answer: a) tangible heritage that can be carried from place to place** — “movable heritage” means: tangible heritage that can be carried from place to place.

**4.** What does “immovable heritage” mean?
a) can be seen and touched  b) tangible heritage that can be carried from place to place  c) heritage carried in songs, oral literature and religious festivals  d) tangible heritage whose base is tied to the land
**Answer: d) tangible heritage whose base is tied to the land** — “immovable heritage” means: tangible heritage whose base is tied to the land.

**5.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  b) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  c) Immovable heritage's base is tied to the land  d) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.
**Answer: c) Immovable heritage's base is tied to the land** — It is one of this lesson's own teachings.
