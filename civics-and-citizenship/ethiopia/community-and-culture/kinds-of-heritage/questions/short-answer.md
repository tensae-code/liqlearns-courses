# Kinds of Heritage — Things We Can Touch and Things We Carry in Song — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Give two examples of movable heritage.
**Answer:** Any two of: coins, writings, clothes, means of transport.

**2.** Give two examples of immovable heritage.
**Answer:** Any two of: monuments, castles, graves, mosques, churches, palaces, bridges.

**3.** In your own words, what is tangible?
**Answer:** Can be seen and touched.
