# Why Shared Values Help a Community

## Do it first

Watch your community for one day with this question: where do shared values quietly steer what people do? Note two moments. Then read the book's four reasons.

## The guide's reasons

Community values make us steer our behaviour properly.

They make us appreciate diversity.

They make us cooperate with each other.

They make us respect the other members of the community.

Pupils write the importance, and groups identify the uses of the values.
