# Why Shared Values Help a Community — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) how positive interaction helps learning  b) why community values matter  c) selfishness against the common good  d) what a healthy social life is
**Answer: b) why community values matter** — That is the one thing this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does “steer” mean?
a) to guide in a direction, as a rudder guides a boat  b) to value a thing for what it truly is  c) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  d) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.
**Answer: a) to guide in a direction, as a rudder guides a boat** — “steer” means: to guide in a direction, as a rudder guides a boat.

**3.** What does “appreciate” mean?
a) to guide in a direction, as a rudder guides a boat  b) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  c) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  d) to value a thing for what it truly is
**Answer: d) to value a thing for what it truly is** — “appreciate” means: to value a thing for what it truly is.

**4.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  b) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  c) They make us cooperate with each other  d) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.
**Answer: c) They make us cooperate with each other** — It is one of this lesson's own teachings.

**5.** Which of these is true in this lesson?
a) Promises, truth and fairness are not needed between people.  b) Community values make us steer our behaviour properly  c) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  d) The strongest person may take whatever they want.
**Answer: b) Community values make us steer our behaviour properly** — It is stated in the lesson.
