# Why Shared Values Help a Community — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the four uses of community values.
**Answer:** Steering behaviour properly; appreciating diversity; cooperating; respecting other members.

**2.** How do pupils study the importance?
**Answer:** They write it, and groups identify the uses of the values.

**3.** In your own words, what is steer?
**Answer:** To guide in a direction, as a rudder guides a boat.
