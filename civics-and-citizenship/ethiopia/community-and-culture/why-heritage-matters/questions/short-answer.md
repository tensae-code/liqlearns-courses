# Why Heritage Matters — Who We Are, and Work for Our People — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name one economic benefit of heritage.
**Answer:** Any one of: visitors and income; lifting hotels and transport; jobs for guides, drivers and museum workers; drawing foreign investors.

**2.** What classroom activity does the book set?
**Answer:** Comparing the heritages pictured on the new Ethiopian 100-birr note.

**3.** In your own words, what is civilisation?
**Answer:** A people's developed way of life across history.
