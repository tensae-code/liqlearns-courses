# Why Heritage Matters — Who We Are, and Work for Our People — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) why the common good matters: each other, safety and law  b) showing fairness, and standing against injustice  c) what a healthy social life is  d) the political, economic and social benefits of heritage
**Answer: d) the political, economic and social benefits of heritage** — That is the one thing this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does “civilisation” mean?
a) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  b) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  c) a people's developed way of life across history  d) a person or body that puts money into a country's work, expecting it to grow
**Answer: c) a people's developed way of life across history** — “civilisation” means: a people's developed way of life across history.

**3.** What does “investor” mean?
a) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  b) a person or body that puts money into a country's work, expecting it to grow  c) a people's developed way of life across history  d) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.
**Answer: b) a person or body that puts money into a country's work, expecting it to grow** — “investor” means: a person or body that puts money into a country's work, expecting it to grow.

**4.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Economically  b) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  c) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  d) The strongest person may take whatever they want.
**Answer: a) Economically** — It is one of this lesson's own teachings.
