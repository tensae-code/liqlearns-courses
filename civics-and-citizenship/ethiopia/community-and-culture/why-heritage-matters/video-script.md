# Video script — Why Heritage Matters — Who We Are, and Work for Our People

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Heritage matters politically, economically and socially: it tells our history and builds the country's image, brings visitors, income and jobs, and gives the people ownership and pride.

Heritage gives factual information about our history, civilisation and development, and builds Ethiopia's image worldwide.

Through it, the book teaches, the country is known as the origin of mankind, a civilisation more than 5,000 years old, owner of an ancient civilisation with its own alphabet and calendar, and the one African country never colonised.

Knowing our past teaches our shared values, unity and differences, successes and failures.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
