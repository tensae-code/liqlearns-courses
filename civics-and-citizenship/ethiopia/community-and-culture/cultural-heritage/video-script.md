# Video script — Cultural Heritage — What Our Grandparents Hand Us

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Heritage is the part of culture one generation receives and hands to the future as wealth — and culture itself is symbolic, shared, learned and passed on, and adaptable.

Culture is the complex whole holding knowledge, belief, art, morality, custom and the other things a society wants.

It is not one person's: it is a people's shared way of living together and their way of relating to the natural environment.

Culture has four characteristics: it is symbolic (expressed in language, art and image); it is shared by nature; it is learned and passed from generation to generation; and it can be adapted as people find fitting.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
