# Cultural Heritage — What Our Grandparents Hand Us — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is heritage?
**Answer:** The part of culture one generation receives and hands to the future as wealth.

**2.** Name culture's four characteristics.
**Answer:** Symbolic; shared; learned and passed from generation to generation; adaptable.
