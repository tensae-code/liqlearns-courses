# Cultural Heritage — What Our Grandparents Hand Us — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Culture is the complex whole holding knowledge, belief, art, morality, custom and the other things a society wants.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Heritage is the part of culture one generation receives and hands to the future as wealth.
**Answer: True**

**3.** Heritage is a private possession that each person keeps from the next generation.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite.
