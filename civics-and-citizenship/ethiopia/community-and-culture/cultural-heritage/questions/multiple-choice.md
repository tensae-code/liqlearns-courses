# Cultural Heritage — What Our Grandparents Hand Us — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) what disrespect is, and what it breaks  b) doing good for others, at school and at home  c) good customs we keep, harmful practices we stop  d) cultural heritage: what one generation hands to the next
**Answer: d) cultural heritage: what one generation hands to the next** — That is the one thing this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does “heritage” mean?
a) able to be fitted to changing conditions  b) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  c) the part of culture one generation receives and hands to the future as wealth  d) expressed in language, art and image — signs that carry meaning
**Answer: c) the part of culture one generation receives and hands to the future as wealth** — “heritage” means: the part of culture one generation receives and hands to the future as wealth.

**3.** What does “symbolic” mean?
a) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  b) expressed in language, art and image — signs that carry meaning  c) the part of culture one generation receives and hands to the future as wealth  d) able to be fitted to changing conditions
**Answer: b) expressed in language, art and image — signs that carry meaning** — “symbolic” means: expressed in language, art and image — signs that carry meaning.

**4.** What does “adaptable” mean?
a) able to be fitted to changing conditions  b) the part of culture one generation receives and hands to the future as wealth  c) expressed in language, art and image — signs that carry meaning  d) The strongest person may take whatever they want.
**Answer: a) able to be fitted to changing conditions** — “adaptable” means: able to be fitted to changing conditions.

**5.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  b) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  c) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  d) Culture has four characteristics
**Answer: d) Culture has four characteristics** — It is one of this lesson's own teachings.
