# Nine Values Our Community Lives By

## Do it first

Count the values in this lesson on your fingers as you meet them — nine in all. For each one, think of one act you have seen that shows it. Nine values, nine memories.

## The nine values

Cooperation — working together. Tolerance — bearing with difference patiently. Inclusiveness — shutting no one out.

Meaningful participation — taking a real part, not a token part. Mutual respect — respect going both ways. Hospitality — welcoming the guest.

Integrity — wholeness of character, the same inside and out. Accepting others' ideas — letting another's thought be heard and weighed. Hope — looking forward in trust.

One activity puts pupils in nine groups, each taking one core value and matching it to a picture; another works from a sign carrying four of the values.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the community's nine core values: cooperation, tolerance, inclusiveness, meaningful participation, mutual respect, hospitality, integrity, accepting others' ideas, and hope. Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood, and name the lesson's key words: tolerance, inclusiveness, hospitality. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: the nine core community values.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
