# Lesson plan — Nine Values Our Community Lives By

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Community, Culture and Many Cultures · **Lesson:** Nine Values Our Community Lives By
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 6 — Community, Culture and Many Cultures (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the community's nine core values: cooperation, tolerance, inclusiveness, meaningful participation, mutual respect, hospitality, integrity, accepting others' ideas, and hope.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the lessons before it in this title — the learner already knows the earlier circles of this course (myself, then family, neighbours, school, community and culture) as far as this title. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Cooperation — working together. Tolerance — bearing with difference patiently. Inclusiveness — shutting no one out.
2. Meaningful participation — taking a real part, not a token part. Mutual respect — respect going both ways. Hospitality — welcoming the guest.
3. Integrity — wholeness of character, the same inside and out. Accepting others' ideas — letting another's thought be heard and weighed. Hope — looking forward in trust.
4. One activity puts pupils in nine groups, each taking one core value and matching it to a picture; another works from a sign carrying four of the values.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson's scenes are the learner's own home, school and neighbourhood.

## If the learner is stuck
- Believes that “the nine core values include shutting people out and refusing others' ideas” → go back to the lesson's own words: the community's nine core values: cooperation, tolerance, inclusiveness, meaningful participation, mutual respect, hospitality, integrity, accepting others' ideas, and hope. Work the Do it first act again, slowly, and name each step aloud.
- Gives the words but no example → ask for the lesson happening once in their own home, school or street today; the example is the proof.
