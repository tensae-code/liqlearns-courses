# Hands-on — Nine Values Our Community Lives By

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Count the values in this lesson on your fingers as you meet them — nine in all. For each one, think of one act you have seen that shows it. Nine values, nine memories.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the nine core community values. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
