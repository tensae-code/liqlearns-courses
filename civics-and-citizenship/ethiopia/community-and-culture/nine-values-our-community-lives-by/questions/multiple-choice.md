# Nine Values Our Community Lives By — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) the damages that follow when justice is absent  b) who is in a family, and why each person matters  c) the nine core community values  d) what a healthy social life is
**Answer: c) the nine core community values** — That is the one thing this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does “tolerance” mean?
a) wholeness of character — the same inside and out  b) bearing with difference patiently  c) shutting no one out  d) welcoming the guest
**Answer: b) bearing with difference patiently** — “tolerance” means: bearing with difference patiently.

**3.** What does “inclusiveness” mean?
a) shutting no one out  b) bearing with difference patiently  c) welcoming the guest  d) wholeness of character — the same inside and out
**Answer: a) shutting no one out** — “inclusiveness” means: shutting no one out.

**4.** What does “hospitality” mean?
a) bearing with difference patiently  b) shutting no one out  c) wholeness of character — the same inside and out  d) welcoming the guest
**Answer: d) welcoming the guest** — “hospitality” means: welcoming the guest.

**5.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  b) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  c) Integrity   d) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.
**Answer: c) Integrity ** — It is one of this lesson's own teachings.
