# Nine Values Our Community Lives By — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the nine core values.
**Answer:** Cooperation, tolerance, inclusiveness, meaningful participation, mutual respect, hospitality, integrity, accepting others' ideas, hope.

**2.** What does one activity do with the nine values?
**Answer:** Pupils in nine groups each take one core value and match it to a picture.

**3.** In your own words, what is tolerance?
**Answer:** Bearing with difference patiently.
