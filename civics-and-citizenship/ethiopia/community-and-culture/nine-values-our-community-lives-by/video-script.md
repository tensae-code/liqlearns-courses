# Video script — Nine Values Our Community Lives By

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The community's nine core values: cooperation, tolerance, inclusiveness, meaningful participation, mutual respect, hospitality, integrity, accepting others' ideas, and hope.

Cooperation — working together. Tolerance — bearing with difference patiently. Inclusiveness — shutting no one out.

Meaningful participation — taking a real part, not a token part. Mutual respect — respect going both ways. Hospitality — welcoming the guest.

Integrity — wholeness of character, the same inside and out. Accepting others' ideas — letting another's thought be heard and weighed. Hope — looking forward in trust.

One activity puts pupils in nine groups, each taking one core value and matching it to a picture; another works from a sign carrying four of the values.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
