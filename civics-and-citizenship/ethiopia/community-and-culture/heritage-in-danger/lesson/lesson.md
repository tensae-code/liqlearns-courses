# Heritage in Danger — Looting, Neglect and Disaster

## Do it first

Name one old and precious thing in your region. Now list everything that could destroy it in one generation. Compare your dangers with the book's list.

## The threats

Foreign invasion and internal religious, ethnic and political conflicts have plundered and destroyed heritage.

Theft and illegal trafficking take heritage away in secret.

Lack of awareness leaves heritage uncared for.

Illegal settlement, hunting and mining around heritage sites damage them.

Natural disasters — flood, earthquake and heavy rain — destroy them suddenly.

## The book's own example

Ethiopian treasures including crowns, books and gold crosses were looted by British forces — the book's own example, taught as the book presents it.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: heritage is endangered by invasion and conflict, theft and illegal trafficking, lack of awareness, illegal settlement, hunting and mining around sites — and natural disasters. Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood, and name the lesson's key words: looting, trafficking, plunder. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: what endangers heritage.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
