# Video script — Heritage in Danger — Looting, Neglect and Disaster

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Heritage is endangered by invasion and conflict, theft and illegal trafficking, lack of awareness, illegal settlement, hunting and mining around sites — and natural disasters.

Foreign invasion and internal religious, ethnic and political conflicts have plundered and destroyed heritage.

Theft and illegal trafficking take heritage away in secret.

Lack of awareness leaves heritage uncared for.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
