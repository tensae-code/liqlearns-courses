# Heritage in Danger — Looting, Neglect and Disaster — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) why cooperation and teamwork matter  b) what endangers heritage  c) what school discipline means: guidance to a standard of conduct  d) the ways of accommodating multiculturalism
**Answer: b) what endangers heritage** — That is the one thing this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does “looting” mean?
a) stealing treasures in war or disorder  b) illegal trading — here, in stolen heritage  c) to rob by force, taking a people's wealth  d) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.
**Answer: a) stealing treasures in war or disorder** — “looting” means: stealing treasures in war or disorder.

**3.** What does “trafficking” mean?
a) stealing treasures in war or disorder  b) to rob by force, taking a people's wealth  c) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  d) illegal trading — here, in stolen heritage
**Answer: d) illegal trading — here, in stolen heritage** — “trafficking” means: illegal trading — here, in stolen heritage.

**4.** What does “plunder” mean?
a) illegal trading — here, in stolen heritage  b) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  c) to rob by force, taking a people's wealth  d) stealing treasures in war or disorder
**Answer: c) to rob by force, taking a people's wealth** — “plunder” means: to rob by force, taking a people's wealth.

**5.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  b) Illegal settlement, hunting and mining around heritage sites damage them  c) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  d) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.
**Answer: b) Illegal settlement, hunting and mining around heritage sites damage them** — It is one of this lesson's own teachings.
