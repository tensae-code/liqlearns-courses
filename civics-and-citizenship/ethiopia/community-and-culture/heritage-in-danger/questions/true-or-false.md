# Heritage in Danger — Looting, Neglect and Disaster — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Foreign invasion and internal religious, ethnic and political conflicts have plundered and destroyed heritage.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Ethiopian treasures including crowns, books and gold crosses were looted by British forces .
**Answer: True**

**3.** Heritage is safe from all danger once it is old and famous.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite.
