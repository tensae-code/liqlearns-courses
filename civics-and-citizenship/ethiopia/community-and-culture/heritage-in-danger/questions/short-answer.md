# Heritage in Danger — Looting, Neglect and Disaster — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name three things that endanger heritage.
**Answer:** Any three of: invasion and conflict; theft and illegal trafficking; lack of awareness; illegal settlement, hunting and mining around sites; natural disasters.

**2.** What treasures does the book name as looted?
**Answer:** Ethiopian treasures including crowns, books and gold crosses.

**3.** In your own words, what is looting?
**Answer:** Stealing treasures in war or disorder.
