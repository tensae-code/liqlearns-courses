# The Parts of a Local Culture — Language, Dress, Food, Music and More

## Do it first

Walk your locality in your mind and collect one example for each element as you read — a language, a symbol, a food, a song. Ten elements, ten finds.

## The ten elements

The chapter's closing list of the main local cultures runs: language, signs/symbols, customs, values, dress, music, arts, beliefs, food and religion.

The notes name language, values and material things among the main elements; the activity answers widen the list — the closing list of ten governs.

Pupils identify the main elements in their own locality in pairs or groups.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: the main elements of local culture are ten: language, signs and symbols, customs, values, dress, music, arts, beliefs, food and religion. Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood, and name the lesson's key words: element, signs/symbols. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: the ten elements of local culture.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
