# Video script — The Parts of a Local Culture — Language, Dress, Food, Music and More

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
The main elements of local culture are ten: language, signs and symbols, customs, values, dress, music, arts, beliefs, food and religion.

The chapter's closing list of the main local cultures runs: language, signs/symbols, customs, values, dress, music, arts, beliefs, food and religion.

The notes name language, values and material things among the main elements; the activity answers widen the list — the closing list of ten governs.

Pupils identify the main elements in their own locality in pairs or groups.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
