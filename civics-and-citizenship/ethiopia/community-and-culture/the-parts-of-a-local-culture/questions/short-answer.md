# The Parts of a Local Culture — Language, Dress, Food, Music and More — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** List the ten elements.
**Answer:** Language, signs/symbols, customs, values, dress, music, arts, beliefs, food, religion.

**2.** Where do pupils identify the elements?
**Answer:** In their own locality, in pairs or groups.

**3.** In your own words, what is element?
**Answer:** One part of a larger whole.
