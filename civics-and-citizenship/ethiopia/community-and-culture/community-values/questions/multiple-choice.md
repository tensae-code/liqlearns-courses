# Community Values — the Beliefs We Share — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) responsibilities at school, and the rights that go with them  b) how we respect elders and religious fathers  c) debo, idir and equb — Ethiopia works together  d) community values: the beliefs we share
**Answer: d) community values: the beliefs we share** — That is the one thing this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does “community values” mean?
a) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  b) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  c) the beliefs the members of a community share  d) a thing held to be true by a person or a people
**Answer: c) the beliefs the members of a community share** — “community values” means: the beliefs the members of a community share.

**3.** What does “belief” mean?
a) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  b) a thing held to be true by a person or a people  c) the beliefs the members of a community share  d) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.
**Answer: b) a thing held to be true by a person or a people** — “belief” means: a thing held to be true by a person or a people.

**4.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Pupils discuss their nature in groups  b) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  c) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  d) The strongest person may take whatever they want.
**Answer: a) Pupils discuss their nature in groups** — It is one of this lesson's own teachings.

**5.** Which of these is true in this lesson?
a) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  b) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  c) Promises, truth and fairness are not needed between people.  d) Community values are the beliefs the members of a community share
**Answer: d) Community values are the beliefs the members of a community share** — It is stated in the lesson.
