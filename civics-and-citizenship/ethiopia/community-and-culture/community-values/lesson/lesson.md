# Community Values — the Beliefs We Share

## Do it first

Name one belief almost everyone in your community holds — about helping, about honesty, about guests. Hold it up: that is a community value, and this lesson defines the kind.

## What community values are

Community values are the beliefs the members of a community share.

Pupils discuss their nature in groups.

One activity has five groups each take one of five pictured values and gather what it means — the next lesson names the community's nine core values in full.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: community values are the beliefs the members of a community share. Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood, and name the lesson's key words: community values, belief. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: community values: the beliefs we share.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
