# Hands-on — Living With Diversity — Respect, Not Just Tolerance

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Think of one custom of another culture near you. Move it through three stations in your mind: tolerate it, value it, respect it. Feel the difference between the stations. This lesson walks all the way to the last one.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the ways of accommodating multiculturalism. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
