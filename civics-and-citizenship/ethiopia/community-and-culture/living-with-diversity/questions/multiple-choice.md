# Living With Diversity — Respect, Not Just Tolerance — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) true words, kept words, clean hands  b) the four marks of a healthy social life  c) the ways of accommodating multiculturalism  d) protecting your own safety, at home and at school
**Answer: c) the ways of accommodating multiculturalism** — That is the one thing this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does “unity in diversity” mean?
a) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  b) many cultures, one shared life — differences respected, not erased  c) favouring one side unfairly  d) what a person can become and do, when freed to grow
**Answer: b) many cultures, one shared life — differences respected, not erased** — “unity in diversity” means: many cultures, one shared life — differences respected, not erased.

**3.** What does “partiality” mean?
a) favouring one side unfairly  b) many cultures, one shared life — differences respected, not erased  c) what a person can become and do, when freed to grow  d) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.
**Answer: a) favouring one side unfairly** — “partiality” means: favouring one side unfairly.

**4.** What does “potential” mean?
a) many cultures, one shared life — differences respected, not erased  b) favouring one side unfairly  c) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  d) what a person can become and do, when freed to grow
**Answer: d) what a person can become and do, when freed to grow** — “potential” means: what a person can become and do, when freed to grow.

**5.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  b) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  c) Be ready to admire other cultures, and encourage different groups' contributions  d) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.
**Answer: c) Be ready to admire other cultures, and encourage different groups' contributions** — It is one of this lesson's own teachings.
