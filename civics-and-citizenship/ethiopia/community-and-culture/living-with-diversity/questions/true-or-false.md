# Living With Diversity — Respect, Not Just Tolerance — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Recognise multiculturalism.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Pupils' own communities' ways of accommodating diversity are the discussion matter.
**Answer: True**

**3.** The climax of the ways is to merely tolerate differences.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite.
