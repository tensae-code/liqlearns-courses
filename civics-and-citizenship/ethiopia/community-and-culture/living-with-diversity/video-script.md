# Video script — Living With Diversity — Respect, Not Just Tolerance

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Living with diversity is done in named ways — recognising it, refusing to rank cultures, respecting difference — and its climax is unity in diversity: respect differences rather than merely tolerating them.

Recognise multiculturalism.

Remove the tendency to count your own culture superior or inferior.

Respect difference, and remove counting an individual's or group's way of life as backward.

Recognise the correctness and contributions of different cultural expressions, and value what other cultures give.

Be ready to admire other cultures, and encourage different groups' contributions.

Encourage people, freed from partiality, to develop their potential for themselves and others.

And the list's own climax: to bring unity in diversity, respect differences rather than merely tolerating them.

Pupils' own communities' ways of accommodating diversity are the discussion matter.

The pictures of the lesson are people celebrating, listening, respecting and getting to know each other together.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
