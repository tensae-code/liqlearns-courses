# Lesson plan — Living With Diversity — Respect, Not Just Tolerance

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Community, Culture and Many Cultures · **Lesson:** Living With Diversity — Respect, Not Just Tolerance
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 6 — Community, Culture and Many Cultures (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** living with diversity is done in named ways — recognising it, refusing to rank cultures, respecting difference — and its climax is unity in diversity: respect differences rather than merely tolerating them.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the lessons before it in this title — the learner already knows the earlier circles of this course (myself, then family, neighbours, school, community and culture) as far as this title. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 2 seconds.

## Before this lesson

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Recognise multiculturalism.
2. Remove the tendency to count your own culture superior or inferior.
3. Respect difference, and remove counting an individual's or group's way of life as backward.
4. Recognise the correctness and contributions of different cultural expressions, and value what other cultures give.
5. Be ready to admire other cultures, and encourage different groups' contributions.

## What you need
