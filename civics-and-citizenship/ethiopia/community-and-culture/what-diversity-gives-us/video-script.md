# Video script — What Diversity Gives Us — Peace, New Ideas, New Knowledge

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Diversity gives us peace and cooperation, new creative works, and the gifts of other cultures: language skills, new thoughts, new knowledge and varied experiences.

To live in peace and cooperation.

Different cultural groups give us new creative works and creativity.

People of different cultures contribute language skills, new thoughts, new knowledge and varied experiences.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
