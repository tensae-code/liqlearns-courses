# What Diversity Gives Us — Peace, New Ideas, New Knowledge — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name three gifts of diversity.
**Answer:** Any three of: peace and cooperation; new creative works and creativity; language skills; new thoughts; new knowledge; varied experiences.

**2.** What is the chapter's key instruction?
**Answer:** Respect differences rather than merely tolerating them.

**3.** In your own words, what is creativity?
**Answer:** Making new things — works, ideas, solutions.
