# What Diversity Gives Us — Peace, New Ideas, New Knowledge — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) the human race, and being good to others  b) the results that follow when self-control is missing  c) ways of respecting parents, and what harms family peace  d) the importance of accommodating diversity
**Answer: d) the importance of accommodating diversity** — That is the one thing this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does “creativity” mean?
a) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  b) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  c) making new things — works, ideas, solutions  d) to make room for, and live well with
**Answer: c) making new things — works, ideas, solutions** — “creativity” means: making new things — works, ideas, solutions.

**3.** What does “accommodate” mean?
a) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  b) to make room for, and live well with  c) making new things — works, ideas, solutions  d) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.
**Answer: b) to make room for, and live well with** — “accommodate” means: to make room for, and live well with.

**4.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) People of different cultures contribute language skills, new thoughts, new knowledge and varied experiences  b) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  c) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  d) The strongest person may take whatever they want.
**Answer: a) People of different cultures contribute language skills, new thoughts, new knowledge and varied experiences** — It is one of this lesson's own teachings.

**5.** Which of these is true in this lesson?
a) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  b) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  c) Promises, truth and fairness are not needed between people.  d) To live in peace and cooperation
**Answer: d) To live in peace and cooperation** — It is stated in the lesson.
