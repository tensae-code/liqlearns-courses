# What Diversity Gives Us — Peace, New Ideas, New Knowledge

## Do it first

Name one thing you know, sing, cook or say that came to you from another culture. That gift is this lesson's whole argument, in one memory.

## The importance list

To live in peace and cooperation.

Different cultural groups give us new creative works and creativity.

People of different cultures contribute language skills, new thoughts, new knowledge and varied experiences.

And the chapter's key instruction stands behind the whole list: respect differences rather than merely tolerating them.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: diversity gives us peace and cooperation, new creative works, and the gifts of other cultures: language skills, new thoughts, new knowledge and varied experiences. Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood, and name the lesson's key words: creativity, accommodate. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: the importance of accommodating diversity.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
