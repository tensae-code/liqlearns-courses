# My Community — People Living Together, Four Kinds

## Do it first

Look out from your door: who lives around you, and how do they live? Name the kind of community you see. This lesson names all four kinds — yours is one of them.

## What a community is

A community is a group of people living together in one place.

[Picture 28065 — four kinds of community]

## The four kinds

The rural community lives in small villages and is mostly farmers.

The pastoralist community makes its life from herding livestock.

The urban community lives in towns and cities.

People are also organised by their religion — the religious community.

Pupils discuss the concept in pairs and identify the kinds in groups.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: a community is a group of people living together in one place — and there are four kinds: rural, pastoralist, urban and religious. Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood, and name the lesson's key words: community, rural, pastoralist. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
