# My Community — People Living Together, Four Kinds — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A community is a group of people living together in one place.
**Answer: True**

**2.** The rural community lives in small villages and is mostly farmers.
**Answer: True**

**3.** There is only one kind of community: the urban kind.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite.
