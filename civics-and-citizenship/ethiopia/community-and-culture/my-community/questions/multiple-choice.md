# My Community — People Living Together, Four Kinds — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) what a community is, and its four kinds  b) showing fairness, and standing against injustice  c) the human race, and being good to others  d) justice: each person given what is due
**Answer: a) what a community is, and its four kinds** — That is the one thing this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does “community” mean?
a) of the countryside — villages and farming  b) making life from herding livestock  c) of towns and cities  d) a group of people living together in one place
**Answer: d) a group of people living together in one place** — “community” means: a group of people living together in one place.

**3.** What does “rural” mean?
a) making life from herding livestock  b) of towns and cities  c) of the countryside — villages and farming  d) a group of people living together in one place
**Answer: c) of the countryside — villages and farming** — “rural” means: of the countryside — villages and farming.

**4.** What does “pastoralist” mean?
a) of towns and cities  b) making life from herding livestock  c) a group of people living together in one place  d) of the countryside — villages and farming
**Answer: b) making life from herding livestock** — “pastoralist” means: making life from herding livestock.

**5.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) The urban community lives in towns and cities  b) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  c) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  d) The strongest person may take whatever they want.
**Answer: a) The urban community lives in towns and cities** — It is one of this lesson's own teachings.
