# Lesson plan — My Community — People Living Together, Four Kinds

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Community, Culture and Many Cultures · **Lesson:** My Community — People Living Together, Four Kinds
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 6 — Community, Culture and Many Cultures (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a community is a group of people living together in one place — and there are four kinds: rural, pastoralist, urban and religious.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the lessons before it in this title — the learner already knows the earlier circles of this course (myself, then family, neighbours, school, community and culture) as far as this title. It needs nothing else.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. A community is a group of people living together in one place.
2. The rural community lives in small villages and is mostly farmers.
3. The pastoralist community makes its life from herding livestock.
4. The urban community lives in towns and cities.
5. People are also organised by their religion — the religious community.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson's scenes are the learner's own home, school and neighbourhood.
