# Hands-on — Seeing Diversity — Peoples, Languages, Dress, Music, Food, Weddings

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
At the next festival or market you attend, count the forms of diversity you can spot: a language, a dress, a food, a song. This lesson gives you the full list to spot with.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the forms under which diversity appears. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
