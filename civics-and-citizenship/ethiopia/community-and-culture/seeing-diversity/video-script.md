# Video script — Seeing Diversity — Peoples, Languages, Dress, Music, Food, Weddings

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Diversity shows itself in forms you can see and hear: ethnic group, language, religion, dress, traditional music, traditional food and the wedding ceremony.

Ethnic group (ጎሳ) — the peoples themselves.

Language and religion.

The state of dress and traditional clothing.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
