# Seeing Diversity — Peoples, Languages, Dress, Music, Food, Weddings

## Do it first

At the next festival or market you attend, count the forms of diversity you can spot: a language, a dress, a food, a song. This lesson gives you the full list to spot with.

## The forms

Ethnic group (ጎሳ) — the peoples themselves.

Language and religion.

The state of dress and traditional clothing.

Traditional music and traditional food.

The wedding ceremony.

Pupils in groups of five identify the kinds in their own community.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: diversity shows itself in forms you can see and hear: ethnic group, language, religion, dress, traditional music, traditional food and the wedding ceremony. Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood, and name the lesson's key words: ethnic group (ጎሳ), traditional. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: the forms under which diversity appears.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
