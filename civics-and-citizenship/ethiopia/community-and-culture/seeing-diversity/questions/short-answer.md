# Seeing Diversity — Peoples, Languages, Dress, Music, Food, Weddings — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name five forms of diversity.
**Answer:** Any five of: ethnic group, language, religion, state of dress, traditional clothing, traditional music, traditional food, wedding ceremony.

**2.** In what group size do pupils identify the kinds?
**Answer:** In groups of five.

**3.** In your own words, what is ethnic group (ጎሳ)?
**Answer:** A people — a community sharing descent, language and culture.
