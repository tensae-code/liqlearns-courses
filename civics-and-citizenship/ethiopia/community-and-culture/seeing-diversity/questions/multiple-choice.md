# Seeing Diversity — Peoples, Languages, Dress, Music, Food, Weddings — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) the forms under which diversity appears  b) what endangers heritage  c) tangible and intangible heritage; movable and immovable  d) what a community is, and its four kinds
**Answer: a) the forms under which diversity appears** — That is the one thing this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does “ethnic group (ጎሳ)” mean?
a) handed down in a people's own way across generations  b) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  c) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  d) a people — a community sharing descent, language and culture
**Answer: d) a people — a community sharing descent, language and culture** — “ethnic group (ጎሳ)” means: a people — a community sharing descent, language and culture.

**3.** What does “traditional” mean?
a) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  b) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  c) handed down in a people's own way across generations  d) a people — a community sharing descent, language and culture
**Answer: c) handed down in a people's own way across generations** — “traditional” means: handed down in a people's own way across generations.

**4.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  b) Traditional music and traditional food  c) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  d) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.
**Answer: b) Traditional music and traditional food** — It is one of this lesson's own teachings.

**5.** Which of these is true in this lesson?
a) Ethnic group (ጎሳ) — the peoples themselves  b) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  c) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  d) Promises, truth and fairness are not needed between people.
**Answer: a) Ethnic group (ጎሳ) — the peoples themselves** — It is stated in the lesson.
