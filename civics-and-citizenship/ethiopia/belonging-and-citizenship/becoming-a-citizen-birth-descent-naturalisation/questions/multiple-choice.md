# Becoming a citizen — birth, descent, naturalisation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) **The right of soil means citizenship by —
a) place of birth  b) parents' citizenship  c) marriage
**Answer: a) place of birth**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) **The right of blood means citizenship by —
a) the town you live in now  b) descent from citizen parents  c) the language you speak best
**Answer: b) descent from citizen parents**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) **Who sets a state's citizenship rules?
a) the United Nations for every state  b) neighbouring countries  c) the state itself
**Answer: c) the state itself**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) **Naturalisation is —
a) a legal process by which a foreigner applies and is accepted  b) automatic after one year abroad  c) a prize given at school
**Answer: a) a legal process by which a foreigner applies and is accepted**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) **In this lesson, naturalisation is met through —
a) winning an election  b) marriage to a citizen  c) buying property
**Answer: b) marriage to a citizen**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) **Does marriage skip the state's process?
a) yes — it is automatic  b) yes — no application is needed  c) no — the state still decides the application
**Answer: c) no — the state still decides the application**
