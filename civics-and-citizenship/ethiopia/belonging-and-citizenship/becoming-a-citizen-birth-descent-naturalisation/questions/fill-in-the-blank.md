# Becoming a citizen — birth, descent, naturalisation — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) **Citizenship by place of birth is called the right of ____.
**Answer: soil**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) **Citizenship that follows the parents is the right of ____.
**Answer: blood**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) **____ is the legal process by which a foreigner becomes a citizen.
**Answer: Naturalisation**
