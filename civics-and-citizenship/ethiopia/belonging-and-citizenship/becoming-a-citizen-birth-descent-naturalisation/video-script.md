# Video script — Becoming a citizen — birth, descent, naturalisation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. A short script — this lesson's design names no full video-script need, so the script stays short.

---

Three doors into one country.

Door one: **soil** — you were born on its land.
Door two: **blood** — your parents are its citizens.
Door three: **application** — you ask, by law, and the state accepts.

Every state keeps its own doors. Learn which door is yours.
