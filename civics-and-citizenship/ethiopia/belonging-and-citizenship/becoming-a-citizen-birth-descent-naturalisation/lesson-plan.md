# Lesson plan — Becoming a citizen — birth, descent, naturalisation

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Belonging and Citizenship · **Lesson:** Becoming a citizen — birth, descent, naturalisation
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 15 — Belonging and Citizenship (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** people become citizens by place of birth, by descent from citizen parents, or by naturalisation.

## Sub lessons
1. **By place of birth (right of soil) and by descent (right of blood)** — Each state sets its own rules for who its citizens are.
2. **By naturalisation (legal process; taught here through marriage)** — The third door is **naturalisation**: the legal process by which a foreigner applies to become a citizen and the state accepts.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
