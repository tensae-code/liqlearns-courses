# Citizen and citizenship — member of the state — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) **What is a citizen?
a) a full member of a state, holding rights under its law  b) a visitor passing through a country  c) a person who owns land anywhere
**Answer: a) a full member of a state, holding rights under its law**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) **What does the state give the citizen?
a) only orders and punishments  b) rights and protection  c) nothing at all
**Answer: b) rights and protection**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) **Name one duty a citizen owes in return.
a) ignoring tax rules  b) breaking shared property  c) obeying the law
**Answer: c) obeying the law**

**4. (Sub lesson 1) **Citizenship is a legal status and also —
a) a sense of identity and belonging  b) a kind of passport photo  c) a school uniform
**Answer: a) a sense of identity and belonging**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) **In the story, why do the parents refuse school?
a) the school is too far to walk  b) they say the children's help at home is worth more  c) the teachers never visited
**Answer: b) they say the children's help at home is worth more**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) **The lesson asks you to judge whether schooling is —
a) a prize for the richest family  b) a thing only towns may have  c) the child's own right, not only a parents' gift
**Answer: c) the child's own right, not only a parents' gift**
