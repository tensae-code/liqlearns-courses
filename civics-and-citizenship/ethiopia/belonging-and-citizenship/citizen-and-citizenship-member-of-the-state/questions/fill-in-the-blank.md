# Citizen and citizenship — member of the state — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) **A citizen holds defined ____ and responsibilities under the law.
**Answer: rights**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) **Citizenship is full and responsible ____ of the state.
**Answer: membership**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) **The state grants rights and ____ to the citizen.
**Answer: protection**
