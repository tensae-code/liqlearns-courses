# Hands-on — Citizen and citizenship — member of the state

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening again, for real
Think of one thing your country gives you — a school place, safety, a road — and one thing you give back, like obeying a rule or keeping a shared place clean. Hold both in your mind. That two-way tie is this lesson.

## Sort it on the table
On paper, write the lesson's key pairs from its matching questions — one term per slip. Shuffle the slips, then sort and match them again from memory, saying each meaning aloud as you place it. Any pair you cannot explain goes back into the lesson for one more read.

## Take it home
Find this lesson where you live: one real example of its one thing — a citizen is a full member of a state, holding rights from it and owing duties to it — in your family, your school or your street. Write two sentences about it: what happened, and what the lesson says about it. Bring the sentences back and read them to your companion.
