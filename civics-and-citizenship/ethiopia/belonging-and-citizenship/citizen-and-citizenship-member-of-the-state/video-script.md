# Video script — Citizen and citizenship — member of the state

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. This is the lesson's full read-aloud script, where the design names a video-script need.

---

Look at the child in this story.

She helps at home. Her work matters.
Her teachers come to the house.
"Send the children to school next year."

The parents say no.
"Their help at home is worth more."

Now you judge.
Is schooling a gift parents can give or keep back?
Or is it the child's own right?
A citizen holds rights. A citizen owes duties.
Schooling sits right where those two meet.
