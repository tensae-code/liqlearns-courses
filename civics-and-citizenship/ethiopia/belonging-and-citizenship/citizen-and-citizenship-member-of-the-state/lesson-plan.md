# Lesson plan — Citizen and citizenship — member of the state

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Belonging and Citizenship · **Lesson:** Citizen and citizenship — member of the state
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 15 — Belonging and Citizenship (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a citizen is a full member of a state, holding rights from it and owing duties to it.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the levels before it in this course's path order (recommended, never forced); inside its own title it stands on the lessons before it.

## Sub lessons
1. **Citizen and citizenship: rights held, duties owed** — A **citizen** is a person who holds defined rights and responsibilities under the law of a state.
2. **Story: the village children kept from school** — Here is the case to judge, in summary.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the Character level — who a good citizen is. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what a citizen is and what citizenship means.
2. Name rights a citizen holds and duties a citizen owes.
3. Judge the village-school case: whose right is schooling?.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — Think of one thing your country gives you — a school place, safety, a road — and one thing you give back, like obeying a rule or keeping a shared place clean. Hold both in your mind. That two-way tie is this lesson.
2. **The explanation** — the lesson's parts in order (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 28600]: a picture of two arrows between a citizen and the state, one labelled rights/protection, the other duties — the student traces both arrows and says what travels along each.
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 28601]: a picture of teachers talking with parents outside a village house while two children listen — the student says what each person in the picture wants.
   - ADD IMAGE [Picture 28602]: a picture of a child with a school bag in one hand and a market basket in the other, both claims visible — the student names the two claims the child is caught between.
   - ADD AUDIO: none named in the placements for this lesson — no audio slot is built.
3. **Say it to your companion** — explain the one thing in your own words, with the lesson's own case (lesson/lesson.md).
   - ADD VIDEO: the script in video-script.md, filmed — in the super-simple voice (made later, when videos are made).
4. **Questions** — from questions/: multiple-choice, matching, fill-in-the-blank — served at the learner's level and teaching-mix (levels.md).
5. **Hands-on** — hands-on.md.
6. **Do It Yourself** — the closing doing block (lesson/lesson.md).
