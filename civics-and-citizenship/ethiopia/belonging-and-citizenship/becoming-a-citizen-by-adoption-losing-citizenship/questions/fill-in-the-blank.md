# Becoming a citizen by adoption; losing citizenship — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) **A child under ____ adopted by citizen parents can gain citizenship.
**Answer: 18**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) **Freely giving up citizenship is called ____ loss.
**Answer: voluntary**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) **When the state revokes citizenship, the loss is ____.
**Answer: involuntary**
