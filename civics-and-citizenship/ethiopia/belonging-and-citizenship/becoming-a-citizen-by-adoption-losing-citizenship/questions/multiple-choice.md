# Becoming a citizen by adoption; losing citizenship — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) **Through adoption, a child gains citizenship through —
a) citizen parents who adopt them  b) the school they attend  c) the town council alone
**Answer: a) citizen parents who adopt them**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) **How old must the adopted child be in this lesson's rule?
a) over 21  b) under 18  c) any age at all
**Answer: b) under 18**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) **Adoption for citizenship follows —
a) no steps at all  b) only a family promise  c) set steps set by the state
**Answer: c) set steps set by the state**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) **Voluntary loss of citizenship means —
a) the person freely renounces it  b) the state seizes it overnight  c) a court fines the person
**Answer: a) the person freely renounces it**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) **Involuntary loss means —
a) the person chooses to leave  b) the state revokes it through a process  c) the passport is lost in travel
**Answer: b) the state revokes it through a process**

**6. (Sub lesson 2) **Why does involuntary loss need a process?
a) because paper is expensive  b) because all doors need paint  c) because the person does not choose it
**Answer: c) because the person does not choose it**
