Book errors taught corrected, named here only: the student book labels two different subsections "B)" — Citizenship by Adoption (printed p.23) and Ways of Losing Citizenship (printed p.24) — a numbering error; the lesson follows the content in order. The guide's involuntary-loss answer key prints the garbled phrase "declared by a competent authority, a disaster in the armed forces in the time of war" — evidently "deserter"; the corrected reading is taught. Ethiopia's protected position (involuntary loss not permitted) is the final Belonging lesson; this lesson teaches the general ways.
