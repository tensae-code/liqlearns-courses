# Video script — Becoming a citizen by adoption; losing citizenship

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. A short script — this lesson's design names no full video-script need, so the script stays short.

---

A child joins a new family — and a new country.
That is citizenship by **adoption**, through set steps.

And citizenship can end two ways.
**Freely** — the person gives it up.
**Taken** — the state revokes it, through a process.
Same ending. Very different doors.
