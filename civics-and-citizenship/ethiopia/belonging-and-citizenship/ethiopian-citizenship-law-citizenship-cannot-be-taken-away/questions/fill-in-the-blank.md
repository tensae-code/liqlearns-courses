# Ethiopian citizenship law — citizenship cannot be taken away — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word or words.

**1. **No Ethiopian may be ____ of citizenship by government decision.
**Answer: stripped**

**2. **Involuntary loss is not ____ under Ethiopian law.
**Answer: permitted**

**3. **Citizenship ends by the citizen's own ____ act.
**Answer: voluntary**
