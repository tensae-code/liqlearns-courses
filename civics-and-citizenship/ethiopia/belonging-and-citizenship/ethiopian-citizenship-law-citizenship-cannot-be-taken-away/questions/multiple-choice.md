# Ethiopian citizenship law — citizenship cannot be taken away — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. **Under Ethiopian law, can the government strip a citizen of citizenship?
a) no — involuntary loss is not permitted  b) yes — at any time  c) yes — for unpaid taxes
**Answer: a) no — involuntary loss is not permitted**

**2. **Which article protects the citizen this way?
a) Article 6  b) Article 33(1)  c) Article 52
**Answer: b) Article 33(1)**

**3. **Name one way Ethiopian citizenship can end.
a) a minister's announcement  b) a neighbour's complaint  c) voluntary renunciation by the citizen
**Answer: c) voluntary renunciation by the citizen**

**4. **The other way it can end is —
a) voluntarily taking another nationality  b) reaching old age  c) moving to another region
**Answer: a) voluntarily taking another nationality**
