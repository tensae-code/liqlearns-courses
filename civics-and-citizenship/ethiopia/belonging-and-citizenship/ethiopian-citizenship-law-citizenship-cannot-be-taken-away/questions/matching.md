# Ethiopian citizenship law — citizenship cannot be taken away — matching

Match each item on the left with its meaning on the right.

**1. **Article 33(1) — b
**2. **Renunciation — a
**3. **Involuntary loss — c

**Answer key:**
1. Article 33(1) — the rule that citizenship cannot be taken away
2. Renunciation — the citizen's own free giving-up
3. Involuntary loss — loss imposed by the state — not permitted in Ethiopia

Meanings, as lettered above:
a) the citizen's own free giving-up
b) the rule that citizenship cannot be taken away
c) loss imposed by the state — not permitted in Ethiopia
