# Hands-on — Ethiopian citizenship law — citizenship cannot be taken away

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening again, for real
Could a government you angered simply announce: 'You are no longer a citizen'? In some times and places, yes. Read this short lesson and find out what Ethiopian law answers.

## Sort it on the table
On paper, write the lesson's key pairs from its matching questions — one term per slip. Shuffle the slips, then sort and match them again from memory, saying each meaning aloud as you place it. Any pair you cannot explain goes back into the lesson for one more read.

## Take it home
Find this lesson where you live: one real example of its one thing — under Ethiopian law no one can be stripped of citizenship — it ends only by the citizen's own voluntary act — in your family, your school or your street. Write two sentences about it: what happened, and what the lesson says about it. Bring the sentences back and read them to your companion.
