# Ethiopian citizenship law — citizenship cannot be taken away

## Do it first

Could a government you angered simply announce: 'You are no longer a citizen'? In some times and places, yes. Read this short lesson and find out what Ethiopian law answers.

## The protected rule

Under **Article 33(1) of the Constitution** and the same Nationality Proclamation, no Ethiopian may be stripped of citizenship by a government decision. Involuntary loss is **not permitted** under Ethiopian law.

[Picture 28609 — a shield over a citizen's certificate]

Ethiopian citizenship ends in only two ways, and both are the citizen's own act: the citizen's **voluntary renunciation** — freely giving it up — or the citizen's **voluntary taking of another nationality**.

That is the whole lesson, and it is a shield: your membership of your country cannot be used as a punishment against you.
