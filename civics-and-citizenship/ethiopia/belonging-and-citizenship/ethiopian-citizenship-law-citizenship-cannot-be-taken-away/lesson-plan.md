# Lesson plan — Ethiopian citizenship law — citizenship cannot be taken away

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** Belonging and Citizenship · **Lesson:** Ethiopian citizenship law — citizenship cannot be taken away
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 15 — Belonging and Citizenship (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** under Ethiopian law no one can be stripped of citizenship — it ends only by the citizen's own voluntary act.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the protected rule and where it stands.
