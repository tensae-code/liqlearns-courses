## Sub lessons
1. **The Ethiopian rule: descent** — Ethiopia's first written citizenship law came in 1930; before it, custom governed who belonged.
2. **How a foreigner becomes Ethiopian** — A foreigner who wants to become Ethiopian must meet the Proclamation's requirements — all of them: be an **adult**; have **lived in Ethiopia at least four years**; **speak a language** of the country's nations and nationalities; have **lawful, sufficient income**; be of **good character, with no criminal record**; show **release, or coming release, from the previous nationality — or be stateless**; and take the **oath of allegiance**.
3. **Worked problem: the child of an Ethiopian mother and American father born in France** — Now work the opening case with the rule.
