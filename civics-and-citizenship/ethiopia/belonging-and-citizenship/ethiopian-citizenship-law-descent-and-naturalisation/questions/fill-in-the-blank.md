# Ethiopian citizenship law — descent and naturalisation — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own word or words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) **Anyone with one or both Ethiopian parents is an Ethiopian ____.
**Answer: citizen**

**2. (Sub lesson 2) **A foreigner must have lived in Ethiopia at least ____ years to apply.
**Answer: four**

**3. (Sub lesson 3) **Before 1930, ____ governed who belonged in Ethiopia.
**Answer: custom**
