# Ethiopian citizenship law — descent and naturalisation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) **Ethiopia's citizenship rule is —
a) descent from an Ethiopian parent  b) birthplace alone  c) long residence alone
**Answer: a) descent from an Ethiopian parent**

**2. (Sub lesson 1) **Which article of the 1995 Constitution carries the rule?
a) Article 25  b) Article 6  c) Article 9
**Answer: b) Article 6**

**3. (Sub lesson 1) **Ethiopia's first written citizenship law came in —
a) 1995  b) 2003  c) 1930
**Answer: c) 1930**

**4. (Sub lesson 2) **How long must a foreigner have lived in Ethiopia to apply?
a) at least four years  b) at least one year  c) at least ten years
**Answer: a) at least four years**

**5. (Sub lesson 2) **Name one requirement besides residence.
a) owning a house in Addis Ababa  b) speaking a language of the country's nations and nationalities  c) having Ethiopian grandparents
**Answer: b) speaking a language of the country's nations and nationalities**

**6. (Sub lesson 3) **The France-born baby of an Ethiopian mother is —
a) not Ethiopian, because she was born abroad  b) only French, because of her birthplace  c) Ethiopian by descent
**Answer: c) Ethiopian by descent**

**7. (Sub lesson 3) **Until what age does the family hold the citizenship choice?
a) 18  b) 12  c) 21
**Answer: a) 18**
