# Video script — Ethiopian citizenship law — descent and naturalisation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. This is the lesson's full read-aloud script, where the design names a video-script need.

---

A baby is born in France.
Her mother is Ethiopian. Her father is American.
Whose citizen is she?

Ethiopia's rule is **descent**.
One Ethiopian parent is enough. She is Ethiopian.

Her father's country may claim her too.
France, where she was born, may claim her too.
But Ethiopia allows no dual citizenship.
The family holds the choice until she is eighteen.

Rule first. Then the case. Every time.
