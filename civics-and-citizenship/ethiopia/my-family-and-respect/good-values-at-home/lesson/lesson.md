# Good Values at Home — the Principles a Family Lives By

## Do it first

Name three rules your home lives by — not rules about objects, but about how people treat each other. Say what each rule protects. Those are values at work.

## The values a family lives by

Every family has its own moral values and principles — the good rules of life it holds and lives by.

They are named as their own topic of the chapter: definitions, explanations, stories and examples are given for each one in the teaching.

[Picture 28014 — values at home — sharing, truth, helping]

## Showing the values

A child shows good moral values to the family members — and practises them.

The practising happens everywhere, including in play by yourself: how you play alone still shows the family's values.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: a family lives by moral values and principles — and a child shows them to the family in daily acts, even in play. Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood, and name the lesson's key words: value, principle, moral. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: the moral values and principles a family lives by.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
