# Video script — Good Values at Home — the Principles a Family Lives By

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A family lives by moral values and principles — and a child shows them to the family in daily acts, even in play.

Every family has its own moral values and principles — the good rules of life it holds and lives by.

They are named as their own topic of the chapter: definitions, explanations, stories and examples are given for each one in the teaching.

A child shows good moral values to the family members — and practises them.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
