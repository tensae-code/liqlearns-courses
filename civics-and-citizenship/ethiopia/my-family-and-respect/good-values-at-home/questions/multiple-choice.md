# Good Values at Home — the Principles a Family Lives By — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) respecting teachers, family, neighbours and classmates  b) the moral values and principles a family lives by  c) the importance of accommodating diversity  d) tangible and intangible heritage; movable and immovable
**Answer: b) the moral values and principles a family lives by** — That is the one thing this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does “value” mean?
a) a good rule of life a person or family holds and lives by  b) a guiding rule that stands behind many single acts  c) about right and wrong in how we treat others  d) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.
**Answer: a) a good rule of life a person or family holds and lives by** — “value” means: a good rule of life a person or family holds and lives by.

**3.** What does “principle” mean?
a) a good rule of life a person or family holds and lives by  b) about right and wrong in how we treat others  c) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  d) a guiding rule that stands behind many single acts
**Answer: d) a guiding rule that stands behind many single acts** — “principle” means: a guiding rule that stands behind many single acts.

**4.** What does “moral” mean?
a) a guiding rule that stands behind many single acts  b) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  c) about right and wrong in how we treat others  d) a good rule of life a person or family holds and lives by
**Answer: c) about right and wrong in how we treat others** — “moral” means: about right and wrong in how we treat others.

**5.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  b) A child shows good moral values to the family members   c) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  d) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.
**Answer: b) A child shows good moral values to the family members ** — It is one of this lesson's own teachings.
