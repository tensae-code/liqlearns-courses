# Good Values at Home — the Principles a Family Lives By — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What are a family's moral values?
**Answer:** The good rules of life the family holds and lives by.

**2.** Where can a child practise the family's values?
**Answer:** Everywhere — with the family, and even in solo play.

**3.** In your own words, what is value?
**Answer:** A good rule of life a person or family holds and lives by.
