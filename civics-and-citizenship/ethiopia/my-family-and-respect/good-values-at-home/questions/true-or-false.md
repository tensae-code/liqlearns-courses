# Good Values at Home — the Principles a Family Lives By — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Every family has its own moral values and principles .
**Answer: True**

**2.** A child shows good moral values to the family members .
**Answer: True**

**3.** A family's values only matter when guests are watching; in play alone they do not count.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite.
