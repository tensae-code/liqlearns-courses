# Video script — How a Respected Person Behaves

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A respected person is known by their character — humility and honesty are its marks, against rudeness and fraud.

Character is a person's distinguishing mark — so a respected person's traits are that person's identifiers.

Pupils discuss the traits in groups and show a respected person's character in role-play.

The sample good traits are humility and honesty.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
