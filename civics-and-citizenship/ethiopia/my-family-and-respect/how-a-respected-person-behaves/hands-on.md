# Hands-on — How a Respected Person Behaves

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name one person in your community whom people respect. Now name the traits that earn it — not their money or clothes, but how they behave. Compare your list with this lesson's.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: the traits of a respected person: humility and honesty. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
