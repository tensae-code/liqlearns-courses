# How a Respected Person Behaves — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Character is a person's distinguishing mark .
**Answer: True**

**2.** The sample good traits are humility and honesty.
**Answer: True**

**3.** A respected person is known by rudeness and fraud.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite.
