# How a Respected Person Behaves — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What are the two sample good traits of a respected person?
**Answer:** Humility and honesty.

**2.** Which bad traits stand against them?
**Answer:** Rudeness/coarseness and fraud.

**3.** In your own words, what is character?
**Answer:** A person's distinguishing mark — the way they habitually behave.
