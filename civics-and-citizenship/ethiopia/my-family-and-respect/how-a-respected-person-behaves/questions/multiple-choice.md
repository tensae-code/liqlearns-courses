# How a Respected Person Behaves — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) the benefits of a healthy social life  b) the forms under which diversity appears  c) why the common good matters: each other, safety and law  d) the traits of a respected person: humility and honesty
**Answer: d) the traits of a respected person: humility and honesty** — That is the one thing this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does “character” mean?
a) deceiving people to cheat them  b) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  c) a person's distinguishing mark — the way they habitually behave  d) not putting yourself above others; gentleness about your own importance
**Answer: c) a person's distinguishing mark — the way they habitually behave** — “character” means: a person's distinguishing mark — the way they habitually behave.

**3.** What does “humility” mean?
a) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  b) not putting yourself above others; gentleness about your own importance  c) a person's distinguishing mark — the way they habitually behave  d) deceiving people to cheat them
**Answer: b) not putting yourself above others; gentleness about your own importance** — “humility” means: not putting yourself above others; gentleness about your own importance.

**4.** What does “fraud” mean?
a) deceiving people to cheat them  b) a person's distinguishing mark — the way they habitually behave  c) not putting yourself above others; gentleness about your own importance  d) The strongest person may take whatever they want.
**Answer: a) deceiving people to cheat them** — “fraud” means: deceiving people to cheat them.

**5.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  b) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  c) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  d) The sample good traits are humility and honesty
**Answer: d) The sample good traits are humility and honesty** — It is one of this lesson's own teachings.
