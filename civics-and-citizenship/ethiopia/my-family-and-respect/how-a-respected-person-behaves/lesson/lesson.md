# How a Respected Person Behaves

## Do it first

Name one person in your community whom people respect. Now name the traits that earn it — not their money or clothes, but how they behave. Compare your list with this lesson's.

## Character is the mark

Character is a person's distinguishing mark — so a respected person's traits are that person's identifiers.

Pupils discuss the traits in groups and show a respected person's character in role-play.

[Picture 28021 — a respected person — humility and honesty]

## Good traits, bad traits

The sample good traits are humility and honesty.

Against them stand the bad ones: rudeness and coarseness, and fraud.

A respected member of the community may be invited as a living example — the guide names no one, so this lesson names no one either.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: a respected person is known by their character — humility and honesty are its marks, against rudeness and fraud. Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood, and name the lesson's key words: character, humility, fraud. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: the traits of a respected person: humility and honesty.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
