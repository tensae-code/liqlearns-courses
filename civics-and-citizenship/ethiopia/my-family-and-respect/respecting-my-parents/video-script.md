# Video script — Respecting My Parents — and What Spoils Peace at Home

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Respecting your parents in named, daily ways — and knowing the things that damage family relationships, so you can avoid them.

Respecting parents is taught as its own topic, with named ways of showing it.

Listening without interrupting, speaking gently, obeying what is right, and helping willingly are the daily shape of that respect.

The chapter's last outcome turns to the other side: pupils identify the factors that harm the positive relationships between family members.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
