# Respecting My Parents — and What Spoils Peace at Home — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name two ways of respecting parents.
**Answer:** Any two named ways, e.g. listening without interrupting and speaking gently / obeying what is right / helping willingly.

**2.** What does the chapter ask pupils to identify at its end?
**Answer:** The factors that harm positive relationships between family members.

**3.** In your own words, what is respect?
**Answer:** Honouring a person's worth in word and deed.
