# Respecting My Parents — and What Spoils Peace at Home — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) how we respect elders and religious fathers  b) true words, kept words, clean hands  c) the ways of accommodating multiculturalism  d) ways of respecting parents, and what harms family peace
**Answer: d) ways of respecting parents, and what harms family peace** — That is the one thing this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does “respect” mean?
a) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  b) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  c) honouring a person's worth in word and deed  d) one thing among several that brings a result about
**Answer: c) honouring a person's worth in word and deed** — “respect” means: honouring a person's worth in word and deed.

**3.** What does “factor” mean?
a) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  b) one thing among several that brings a result about  c) honouring a person's worth in word and deed  d) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.
**Answer: b) one thing among several that brings a result about** — “factor” means: one thing among several that brings a result about.

**4.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) The chapter's last outcome turns to the other side  b) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  c) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  d) The strongest person may take whatever they want.
**Answer: a) The chapter's last outcome turns to the other side** — It is one of this lesson's own teachings.

**5.** Which of these is true in this lesson?
a) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  b) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  c) Promises, truth and fairness are not needed between people.  d) Respecting parents is taught as its own topic, with named ways of showing it
