# Respecting My Parents — and What Spoils Peace at Home

## Do it first

Listen to a parent or elder speak today without interrupting — all the way to the end. That one act is the heart of this lesson. Now read the other ways, and the things that spoil them.

## Ways of respecting parents

Respecting parents is taught as its own topic, with named ways of showing it.

Listening without interrupting, speaking gently, obeying what is right, and helping willingly are the daily shape of that respect.

[Picture 28017 — listening without interrupting]

## What spoils peace at home

The chapter's last outcome turns to the other side: pupils identify the factors that harm the positive relationships between family members.

Arguing, refusing to listen, and unkind words are the kind of factors that damage the family's peace — naming them is the first step to avoiding them.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: respecting your parents in named, daily ways — and knowing the things that damage family relationships, so you can avoid them. Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood, and name the lesson's key words: respect, factor. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
