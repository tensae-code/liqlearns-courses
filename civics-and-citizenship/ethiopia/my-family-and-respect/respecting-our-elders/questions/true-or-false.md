# Respecting Our Elders and Religious Fathers — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Be humble and gentle when you meet your elders.
**Answer: True**

**2.** Religious fathers are respected too, with their own things to consider.
**Answer: True**

**3.** When an elder is counselling you, interrupting quickly shows you are clever and respectful.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite.
