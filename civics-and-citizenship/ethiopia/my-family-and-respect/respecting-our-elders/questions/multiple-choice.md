# Respecting Our Elders and Religious Fathers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) culture: a people's way of life, handed on  b) how we respect elders and religious fathers  c) neighbourliness: friendly, cooperative neighbours  d) why the common good matters: each other, safety and law
**Answer: b) how we respect elders and religious fathers** — That is the one thing this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does “elder” mean?
a) an older person, owed respect for their years and counsel  b) wise advice, given to guide  c) peace-making — bringing people who quarrelled back together  d) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.
**Answer: a) an older person, owed respect for their years and counsel** — “elder” means: an older person, owed respect for their years and counsel.

**3.** What does “counsel” mean?
a) an older person, owed respect for their years and counsel  b) peace-making — bringing people who quarrelled back together  c) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  d) wise advice, given to guide
**Answer: d) wise advice, given to guide** — “counsel” means: wise advice, given to guide.

**4.** What does “reconciliation (እርቅ)” mean?
a) wise advice, given to guide  b) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  c) peace-making — bringing people who quarrelled back together  d) an older person, owed respect for their years and counsel
**Answer: c) peace-making — bringing people who quarrelled back together** — “reconciliation (እርቅ)” means: peace-making — bringing people who quarrelled back together.

**5.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  b) Each community also has its own customary moral principles of elder-respect   c) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  d) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.
**Answer: b) Each community also has its own customary moral principles of elder-respect ** — It is one of this lesson's own teachings.
