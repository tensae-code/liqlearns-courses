# Respecting Our Elders and Religious Fathers — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Name the three ways of respecting elders from the chapter's answers.
**Answer:** Be humble and gentle when you meet them; listen carefully when they counsel; do not interrupt while they speak.

**2.** What is እርቅ (reconciliation)?
**Answer:** Peace-making — bringing people who quarrelled back together.

**3.** In your own words, what is elder?
**Answer:** An older person, owed respect for their years and counsel.
