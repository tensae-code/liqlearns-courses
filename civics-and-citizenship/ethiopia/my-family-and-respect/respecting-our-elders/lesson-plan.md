# Lesson plan — Respecting Our Elders and Religious Fathers

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Ethiopia · **Title:** My Family and Respect · **Lesson:** Respecting Our Elders and Religious Fathers
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 2 — My Family and Respect (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** respecting elders — humbly, gently, listening without interrupting — and respecting religious fathers, with peace-making at the heart.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the lessons before it in this title — the learner already knows the earlier circles of this course (myself, then family, neighbours, school, community and culture) as far as this title. It needs nothing else.

## Before this lesson

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Be humble and gentle when you meet your elders.
2. Listen carefully when they counsel you.
3. Do not interrupt while they speak.
4. Each community also has its own customary moral principles of elder-respect — pupils name the ones found in their own society.
5. Religious fathers are respected too, with their own things to consider.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson's scenes are the learner's own home, school and neighbourhood.
