# Disrespect — What It Is and What It Breaks — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) good manners in the family, shown in greeting, listening and helping  b) the human race, and being good to others  c) what disrespect is, and what it breaks  d) protecting the environment — everyone's responsibility
**Answer: c) what disrespect is, and what it breaks** — That is the one thing this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does “disrespect” mean?
a) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  b) giving no regard to another's feelings, wishes, freedom or dignity  c) rude behaviour meant to wound or belittle  d) a person's place and honour among the people around them
**Answer: b) giving no regard to another's feelings, wishes, freedom or dignity** — “disrespect” means: giving no regard to another's feelings, wishes, freedom or dignity.

**3.** What does “insulting” mean?
a) rude behaviour meant to wound or belittle  b) giving no regard to another's feelings, wishes, freedom or dignity  c) a person's place and honour among the people around them  d) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.
**Answer: a) rude behaviour meant to wound or belittle** — “insulting” means: rude behaviour meant to wound or belittle.

**4.** What does “social standing” mean?
a) giving no regard to another's feelings, wishes, freedom or dignity  b) rude behaviour meant to wound or belittle  c) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  d) a person's place and honour among the people around them
**Answer: d) a person's place and honour among the people around them** — “social standing” means: a person's place and honour among the people around them.

**5.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  b) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  c) Disrespect weakens friendship, cooperation, mutual respect and humility  d) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.
**Answer: c) Disrespect weakens friendship, cooperation, mutual respect and humility** — It is one of this lesson's own teachings.
