# Disrespect — What It Is and What It Breaks — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What does disrespect give no regard to?
**Answer:** Another's feelings, wishes, freedom, dignity, humanity or social standing (any of these).

**2.** Name two things disrespect weakens.
**Answer:** Any two of: friendship, cooperation, mutual respect, humility.

**3.** In your own words, what is disrespect?
**Answer:** Giving no regard to another's feelings, wishes, freedom or dignity.
