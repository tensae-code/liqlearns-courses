# Disrespect — What It Is and What It Breaks — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Disrespect is the opposite of respect: giving no regard to another's feelings, wishes, freedom, dignity, humanity or social sta….
**Answer: True**

**2.** Disrespect weakens friendship, cooperation, mutual respect and humility.
**Answer: True**

**3.** Disrespect strengthens friendship and cooperation.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite.
