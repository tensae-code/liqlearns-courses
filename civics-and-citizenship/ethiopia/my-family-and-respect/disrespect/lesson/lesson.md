# Disrespect — What It Is and What It Breaks

## Do it first

Remember a time someone's rude words landed on you. What happened to your wish to work or play with that person afterwards? This lesson names that breaking — and what causes it.

## What disrespect is

Disrespect is the opposite of respect: giving no regard to another's feelings, wishes, freedom, dignity, humanity or social standing.

It shows no appreciation and no humility.

It can show itself in insulting behaviour — rude, unmannerly acts.

[Picture 28022 — an insult lands — a circle cracks]

## What disrespect breaks

Disrespect weakens friendship, cooperation, mutual respect and humility.

Instead, it supports suffering and unhealthy relationships in society.

Against it stands what respect gives: unity and peaceful relations, happiness and safety, and being respected in return.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: disrespect gives no regard to another's feelings, wishes, freedom or dignity — and it weakens friendship, cooperation and humility. Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood, and name the lesson's key words: disrespect, insulting, social standing. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
