# Video script — Disrespect — What It Is and What It Breaks

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Disrespect gives no regard to another's feelings, wishes, freedom or dignity — and it weakens friendship, cooperation and humility.

Disrespect is the opposite of respect: giving no regard to another's feelings, wishes, freedom, dignity, humanity or social standing.

It shows no appreciation and no humility.

It can show itself in insulting behaviour — rude, unmannerly acts.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
