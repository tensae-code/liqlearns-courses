# Hands-on — Getting On Well at Home — Good Manners in the Family

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Greet someone in your home properly — face them, use kind words, listen to the answer. Notice how the greeting changes the next minute. This lesson is about doing that on purpose, every day.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: good manners in the family, shown in greeting, listening and helping. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
