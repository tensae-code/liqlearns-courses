# Getting On Well at Home — Good Manners in the Family

## Do it first

Greet someone in your home properly — face them, use kind words, listen to the answer. Notice how the greeting changes the next minute. This lesson is about doing that on purpose, every day.

## Positive family relationships

Good manners inside the home are their own named topic: greeting kindly, listening, helping, speaking gently.

Positive family relationships are shown, not only spoken: pupils show them in role-play — acting the greeting, the listening, the helping.

## Watching for it at home

The book's homework watches for the classroom facts in the real family situation at home: which manners did you actually see and use today?

What you practise at home becomes the family's daily peace.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: good manners in the family, shown in greeting, listening and helping.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
