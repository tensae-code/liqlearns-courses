# Video script — Getting On Well at Home — Good Manners in the Family

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Good manners at home — greeting, listening and helping — are how positive family relationships are built and shown.

Good manners inside the home are their own named topic: greeting kindly, listening, helping, speaking gently.

Positive family relationships are shown, not only spoken: pupils show them in role-play — acting the greeting, the listening, the helping.

The book's homework watches for the classroom facts in the real family situation at home.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
