# Getting On Well at Home — Good Manners in the Family — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) good manners in the family, shown in greeting, listening and helping  b) the results that follow when self-control is missing  c) why self-control matters, and that it grows by practice  d) the common good: what benefits all of us
**Answer: a) good manners in the family, shown in greeting, listening and helping** — That is the one thing this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does “manners” mean?
a) acting a situation out, to practise and to show it  b) a connection between people that is kind, helping and good for both  c) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  d) the kind, respectful way of behaving with other people
**Answer: d) the kind, respectful way of behaving with other people** — “manners” means: the kind, respectful way of behaving with other people.

**3.** What does “role-play” mean?
a) a connection between people that is kind, helping and good for both  b) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  c) acting a situation out, to practise and to show it  d) the kind, respectful way of behaving with other people
**Answer: c) acting a situation out, to practise and to show it** — “role-play” means: acting a situation out, to practise and to show it.

**4.** What does “positive relationship” mean?
a) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  b) a connection between people that is kind, helping and good for both  c) the kind, respectful way of behaving with other people  d) acting a situation out, to practise and to show it
**Answer: b) a connection between people that is kind, helping and good for both** — “positive relationship” means: a connection between people that is kind, helping and good for both.

**5.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
