# Hands-on — Respecting Others — Every Person Is Free and Has Dignity

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Today, greet three different people properly — a friend, an elder, someone who works at your school. Notice: each one is a whole person, free and standing on their own. This lesson says why that matters.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: every person is free and has dignity — so we respect them. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
