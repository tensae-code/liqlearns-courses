# Video script — Respecting Others — Every Person Is Free and Has Dignity

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Respecting others means recognising that every person is self-standing, special and free — and giving value to their human dignity.

“Others” means anyone besides the speaker — friends, brothers and sisters, mother and father, neighbours, and so on.

Respecting others begins from recognising that the other person is self-standing, special and free.

Respecting a person confirms the person's human dignity — it is giving value to people's human dignity.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
