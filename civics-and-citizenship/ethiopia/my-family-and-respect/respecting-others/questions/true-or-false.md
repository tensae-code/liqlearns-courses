# Respecting Others — Every Person Is Free and Has Dignity — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** “Others” means anyone besides the speaker .
**Answer: True**

**2.** Respecting a person confirms the person's human dignity .
**Answer: True**

**3.** Respect is only for elders; younger people and friends do not need it.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite.
