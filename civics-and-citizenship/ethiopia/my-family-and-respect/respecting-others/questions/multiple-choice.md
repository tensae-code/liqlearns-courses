# Respecting Others — Every Person Is Free and Has Dignity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) the four rule areas: time, grounds, lessons, dress  b) building good neighbour relations, and removing what harms them  c) every person is free and has dignity — so we respect them  d) learning truthfulness from Kebede and from Yonatan's story
**Answer: c) every person is free and has dignity — so we respect them** — That is the one thing this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does “others” mean?
a) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  b) anyone besides the speaker — friends, family, neighbours and so on  c) a whole person in your own right, free, not anyone's property  d) the worth every human being has by being human
**Answer: b) anyone besides the speaker — friends, family, neighbours and so on** — “others” means: anyone besides the speaker — friends, family, neighbours and so on.

**3.** What does “self-standing” mean?
a) a whole person in your own right, free, not anyone's property  b) anyone besides the speaker — friends, family, neighbours and so on  c) the worth every human being has by being human  d) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.
**Answer: a) a whole person in your own right, free, not anyone's property** — “self-standing” means: a whole person in your own right, free, not anyone's property.

**4.** What does “human dignity” mean?
a) anyone besides the speaker — friends, family, neighbours and so on  b) a whole person in your own right, free, not anyone's property  c) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  d) the worth every human being has by being human
**Answer: d) the worth every human being has by being human** — “human dignity” means: the worth every human being has by being human.

**5.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  b) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  c) Respecting a person confirms the person's human dignity   d) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.
**Answer: c) Respecting a person confirms the person's human dignity ** — It is one of this lesson's own teachings.
