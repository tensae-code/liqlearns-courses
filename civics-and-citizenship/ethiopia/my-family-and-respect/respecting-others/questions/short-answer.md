# Respecting Others — Every Person Is Free and Has Dignity — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** Who are 'others'?
**Answer:** Anyone besides the speaker — friends, brothers and sisters, mother and father, neighbours and so on.

**2.** What does respecting a person confirm?
**Answer:** The person's human dignity — it gives value to it.

**3.** In your own words, what is others?
**Answer:** Anyone besides the speaker — friends, family, neighbours and so on.
