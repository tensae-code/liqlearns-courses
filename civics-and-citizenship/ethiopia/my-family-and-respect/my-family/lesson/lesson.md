# My Family — Who Is in It and Why Each Person Matters

## Do it first

Name everyone who lives in your home, one by one. For each person, name one thing the home would miss without them. That list is this lesson.

## What a family is

A family is the group of people bound together in one home — parents, children, and the relatives who live and share life together.

Pupils begin from what they already know: first talk in pairs about your own family, then write, discuss and share.

## Why each person matters

Each member of a family has importance: the home needs what each one gives — care, work, teaching, help, company.

No member is too small to matter: even the youngest child's presence, help and love are part of what makes the family whole.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: a family is the people bound together at home — and every member matters, each in their own way. Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood, and name the lesson's key words: family, member. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: who is in a family, and why each person matters.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
