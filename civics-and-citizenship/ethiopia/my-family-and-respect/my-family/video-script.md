# Video script — My Family — Who Is in It and Why Each Person Matters

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
A family is the people bound together at home — and every member matters, each in their own way.

A family is the group of people bound together in one home — parents, children, and the relatives who live and share life together.

Pupils begin from what they already know: first talk in pairs about your own family, then write, discuss and share.

Each member of a family has importance: the home needs what each one gives — care, work, teaching, help, company.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
