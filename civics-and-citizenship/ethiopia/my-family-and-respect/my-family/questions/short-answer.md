# My Family — Who Is in It and Why Each Person Matters — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** What is a family?
**Answer:** The group of people bound together in one home, sharing life together.

**2.** Why does each member matter?
**Answer:** The home needs what each one gives — care, work, teaching, help, company.

**3.** In your own words, what is family?
**Answer:** The people bound together in one home, sharing life together.
