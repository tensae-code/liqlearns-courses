# My Family — Who Is in It and Why Each Person Matters — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) debo, idir and equb — Ethiopia works together  b) why community values matter  c) who is in a family, and why each person matters  d) tangible and intangible heritage; movable and immovable
**Answer: c) who is in a family, and why each person matters** — That is the one thing this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does “family” mean?
a) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  b) the people bound together in one home, sharing life together  c) one person belonging to a group — here, one person of the family  d) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.
**Answer: b) the people bound together in one home, sharing life together** — “family” means: the people bound together in one home, sharing life together.

**3.** What does “member” mean?
a) one person belonging to a group — here, one person of the family  b) the people bound together in one home, sharing life together  c) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  d) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.
**Answer: a) one person belonging to a group — here, one person of the family** — “member” means: one person belonging to a group — here, one person of the family.

**4.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  b) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  c) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  d) Each member of a family has importance

**5.** Which of these is true in this lesson?
