# My Family — Who Is in It and Why Each Person Matters — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** A family is the group of people bound together in one home .
**Answer: True**

**2.** Each member of a family has importance: the home needs what each one gives .
**Answer: True**

**3.** In a family, only the grown-ups matter; small children do not matter at all.
**Answer: False** — The lesson teaches the opposite.
