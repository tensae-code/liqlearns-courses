# Hands-on — My Family — Who Is in It and Why Each Person Matters

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Name everyone who lives in your home, one by one. For each person, name one thing the home would miss without them. That list is this lesson.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: who is in a family, and why each person matters. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
