# Video script — Respecting Teachers, Family, Neighbours and Classmates

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This lesson is about one thing.
Respect practised person by person — to teachers, to family, to neighbours and to classmates — with mutual respect shrinking disagreements.

Respecting teachers: pupils express appreciation for teachers they see as good examples.

Respecting family: shown at home with exchange of experiences — the importance of family respect is stated plainly.

Respecting neighbours: for example, showing respect in greeting — and building peaceful mutual interaction with them.

Now you. Say it back, in your own words. Then go and do it — today, at home, at school, in your street.
