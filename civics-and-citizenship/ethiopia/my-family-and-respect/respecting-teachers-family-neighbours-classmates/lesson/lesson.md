# Respecting Teachers, Family, Neighbours and Classmates

## Do it first

Before you read, do one round: thank a teacher, greet a neighbour properly, and help one person at home without being asked. Four respects, four doors — this lesson walks through each one.

## Respecting teachers and family

Respecting teachers: pupils express appreciation for teachers they see as good examples.

Respecting family: shown at home with exchange of experiences — the importance of family respect is stated plainly.

[Picture 28020 — four greetings — classroom, home, gate, playground]

## Respecting neighbours and classmates

Respecting neighbours: for example, showing respect in greeting — and building peaceful mutual interaction with them.

Respecting classmates and friends: show it in practice, inside and outside the classroom.

Mutual respect shrinks disagreements between classmates.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: respect practised person by person — to teachers, to family, to neighbours and to classmates — with mutual respect shrinking disagreements. Give one real example from your own home, school or neighbourhood, and name the lesson's key words: appreciation, mutual, interaction. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: respecting teachers, family, neighbours and classmates.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
