# Respecting Teachers, Family, Neighbours and Classmates — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, in your own words.

**1.** How do pupils show respect for teachers in this chapter?
**Answer:** By expressing appreciation for teachers they see as good examples.

**2.** What does mutual respect do between classmates?
**Answer:** It shrinks disagreements between them.

**3.** In your own words, what is appreciation?
**Answer:** Saying and showing thanks for what a person is and does.
