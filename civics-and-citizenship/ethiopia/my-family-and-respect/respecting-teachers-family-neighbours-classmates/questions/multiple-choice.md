# Respecting Teachers, Family, Neighbours and Classmates — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** What does this lesson teach?
a) respecting teachers, family, neighbours and classmates  b) joining in: clubs and co-curricular activities  c) why community values matter  d) the traits of a respected person: humility and honesty
**Answer: a) respecting teachers, family, neighbours and classmates** — That is the one thing this lesson teaches.

**2.** What does “appreciation” mean?
a) going both ways — respect given and returned  b) how people act with and respond to each other  c) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  d) saying and showing thanks for what a person is and does
**Answer: d) saying and showing thanks for what a person is and does** — “appreciation” means: saying and showing thanks for what a person is and does.

**3.** What does “mutual” mean?
a) how people act with and respond to each other  b) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  c) going both ways — respect given and returned  d) saying and showing thanks for what a person is and does
**Answer: c) going both ways — respect given and returned** — “mutual” means: going both ways — respect given and returned.

**4.** What does “interaction” mean?
a) The strongest person may take whatever they want.  b) how people act with and respond to each other  c) saying and showing thanks for what a person is and does  d) going both ways — respect given and returned
**Answer: b) how people act with and respond to each other** — “interaction” means: how people act with and respond to each other.

**5.** Which of these belongs to this lesson?
a) Respecting neighbours  b) It is best to shut other people out and think only of yourself.  c) Rules and values do not matter if nobody is watching.  d) The strongest person may take whatever they want.
**Answer: a) Respecting neighbours** — It is one of this lesson's own teachings.
