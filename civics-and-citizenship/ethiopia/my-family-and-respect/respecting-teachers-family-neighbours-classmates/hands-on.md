# Hands-on — Respecting Teachers, Family, Neighbours and Classmates

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the opening act again
Before you read, do one round: thank a teacher, greet a neighbour properly, and help one person at home without being asked. Four respects, four doors — this lesson walks through each one.

## Show it with real things
Take paper and a pencil — or real objects from your home — and show the lesson's main teaching: respecting teachers, family, neighbours and classmates. Arrange the objects, draw the scene, or act it with someone at home. Say each part aloud as you place it.

## Watch for it for one day
For one whole day, watch for this lesson happening in real life — at home, at school, in the street. Each time you see it (or see its opposite), note it: what happened, who was there, what the lesson would say about it. Bring your notes back to the lesson.
