# Forms and Principles of Democracy

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: what democracy means, its principles, and its forms (direct and representative). Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Rwanda's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## Sub lesson 1 — What democracy means

Democracy is rule by the people: the citizens themselves hold the power to choose how they are governed and who governs them.

## Sub lesson 2 — The principles of democracy

Democracy rests on principles the book names — the people taking part, free choice, and respect for the rights of every citizen.

## Sub lesson 3 — The forms of democracy

Democracy takes two forms: direct democracy, where citizens decide matters themselves, and representative democracy, where citizens choose representatives to decide for them.
