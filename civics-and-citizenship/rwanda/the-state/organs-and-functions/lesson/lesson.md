# Organs and Functions

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: the organs, role and functions of the state and of government. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Rwanda's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## Sub lesson 1 — The organs of the state and of government

The state and the government work through their organs — the distinct parts, each with its own place in the whole.

## Sub lesson 2 — The role and functions

Each organ has a role and functions: what it exists to do for the state and for the citizens it serves.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The organs, role and functions of the state and of government. Give one real example from your own life or from the Rwanda you live in, and name the lesson's key words: organs, functions. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: The organs, role and functions of the state and of government.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
