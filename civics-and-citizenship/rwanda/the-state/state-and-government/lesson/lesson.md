# State and Government

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: the definitions of state and government and the difference between them. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Rwanda's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## Sub lesson 1 — State and government defined

The state is the whole political community organised under its own authority; the government is the group of people and institutions that run the state at a given time.

## Sub lesson 2 — How state and government differ

The state endures while governments change: the government is the state's working arm, not the state itself.
