# Conflict Transformation and Law

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: conflict transformation defined, and the sources of Rwandan codes and laws. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Rwanda's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## Sub lesson 1 — Conflict transformation defined

Conflict transformation means changing the conditions and relationships that produced a conflict, not only stopping its violence.

## Sub lesson 2 — The sources of Rwandan codes and laws

Rwanda's codes and laws have their sources; knowing where the law comes from is the first step to using it in transforming conflict.
