# Features and Stages of Genocide

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: the features and stages of genocide. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Rwanda's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## Sub lesson 1 — The features of genocide

Genocide has recognisable features — the signs by which it is known and named for what it is.

## Sub lesson 2 — The stages of genocide

Genocide does not arrive all at once: it develops through stages, and knowing the stages is part of how a society stops it early.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The features and stages of genocide. Give one real example from your own life or from the Rwanda you live in, and name the lesson's key words: features, stages. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
