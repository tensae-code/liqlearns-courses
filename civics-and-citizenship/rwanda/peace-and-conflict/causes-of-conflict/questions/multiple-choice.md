# Causes of Conflict — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The causes of conflict and violence.  b) it has nothing to do with how Rwanda governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The causes of conflict and violence.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) The causes of conflict and violence.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Rwanda
**Answer: a) The causes of conflict and violence.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
