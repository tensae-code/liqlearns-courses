# Values and Their Sources — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) What values are and where they come from.  b) it has nothing to do with how Rwanda governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) What values are and where they come from.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) What values are and where they come from.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Rwanda
**Answer: a) What values are and where they come from.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
