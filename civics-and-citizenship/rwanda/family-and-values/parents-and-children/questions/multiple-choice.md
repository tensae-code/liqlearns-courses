# Parents and Children — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Conflict and misunderstanding between parents and children, and dialogue that develops respect for each other's rights to hold different values.  b) it has nothing to do with how Rwanda governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Conflict and misunderstanding between parents and children, and dialogue that develops respect for each other's rights to hold different values.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Conflict and misunderstanding between parents and children, and dialogue that develops respect for each other's rights to hold different values.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Rwanda
**Answer: a) Conflict and misunderstanding between parents and children, and dialogue that develops respect for each other's rights to hold different values.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
