# Parents and Children

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: conflict and misunderstanding between parents and children, and dialogue that develops respect for each other's rights to hold different values. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Rwanda's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## Sub lesson 1 — Conflict and misunderstanding between parents and children

Parents and children can clash and misunderstand each other, especially where their values differ; the guide names this honestly.

## Sub lesson 2 — Dialogue that develops respect

Dialogue — talking and listening across the difference — develops respect for each other's rights to hold different values.

Give one real example from your own life or from the Rwanda you live in, and name the lesson's key words: parents, children. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
