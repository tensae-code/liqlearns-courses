# Concept of Human Rights — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The concept of human rights and their characteristics.  b) it has nothing to do with how Rwanda governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The concept of human rights and their characteristics.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) The concept of human rights and their characteristics.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Rwanda
**Answer: a) The concept of human rights and their characteristics.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
