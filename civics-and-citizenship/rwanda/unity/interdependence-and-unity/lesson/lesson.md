# Interdependence and Unity

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: interdependence and unity. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Rwanda's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## Sub lesson 1 — Interdependence

People, groups and nations depend on one another: no one produces everything they need alone, and that mutual dependence binds a society together.

## Sub lesson 2 — Unity

Unity is what interdependence grows into when people choose it — one people standing together across their differences.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Interdependence and unity. Give one real example from your own life or from the Rwanda you live in, and name the lesson's key words: interdependence, unity. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
