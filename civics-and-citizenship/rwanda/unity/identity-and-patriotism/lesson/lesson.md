# Identity and Patriotism

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: personal identity, national identity (being Umunyarwanda) and patriotism. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Rwanda's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## Sub lesson 1 — Personal identity

Personal identity is who you are — the name, roots and qualities that make you yourself.

## Sub lesson 2 — National identity — being Umunyarwanda

National identity is belonging to Rwanda: being Umunyarwanda, one Rwandan people, whatever one's personal story.

## Sub lesson 3 — Patriotism

Patriotism is love of one's country shown in action — serving it, defending its unity and working for its good.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Personal identity, national identity (being Umunyarwanda) and patriotism. Give one real example from your own life or from the Rwanda you live in, and name the lesson's key words: identity, patriotism. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
