# Identity and Patriotism — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Personal identity, national identity (being Umunyarwanda) and patriotism.  b) it has nothing to do with how Rwanda governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Personal identity, national identity (being Umunyarwanda) and patriotism.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Personal identity, national identity (being Umunyarwanda) and patriotism.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Rwanda
**Answer: a) Personal identity, national identity (being Umunyarwanda) and patriotism.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
