# Social Cohesion

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: social cohesion, the factors that influence it and the challenges to it. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Rwanda's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## Sub lesson 1 — What social cohesion is

Social cohesion is the glue of a society: the trust, shared values and willingness to live and work together that hold people in one community.

## Sub lesson 2 — The factors that influence it

Cohesion grows or weakens with what people share — common purpose, fairness, inclusion and everyday cooperation.

## Sub lesson 3 — The challenges to it

Cohesion is challenged by division, exclusion and distrust; the book names the challenges so citizens can answer them.
