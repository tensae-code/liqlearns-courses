# Concept and Types of Disability

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: the concept and types of disability. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Rwanda's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## Sub lesson 1 — The concept of disability

Disability means a long-term condition of body or mind that, together with the barriers around a person, can limit full participation in everyday life.

## Sub lesson 2 — The types of disability

The book sorts disability into its types, so learners can recognise the different forms it takes and respond to each with understanding.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: The concept and types of disability. Give one real example from your own life or from the Rwanda you live in, and name the lesson's key words: concept, types. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
