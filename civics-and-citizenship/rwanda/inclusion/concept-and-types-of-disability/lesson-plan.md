# Lesson plan — Concept and Types of Disability

**Course:** Civics and Citizenship — Rwanda · **Title:** Inclusion · **Lesson:** Concept and Types of Disability
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 1 — Democracy, Rights, Peace and Rwandan Values (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The concept and types of disability. This lesson stands on a student book.
**This lesson's own level:** Level 1 — it stands on the lessons before it in path order (recommended, never forced).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The concept of disability** — Disability means a long-term condition of body or mind that, together with the barriers around a person, can limit full participation in everyday life.
2. **The types of disability** — The book sorts disability into its types, so learners can recognise the different forms it takes and respond to each with understanding.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the lesson's one thing in their own words: The concept and types of disability.
2. Give one real example of it from their own home, school or town — or, for this book's lessons, from the public life of Rwanda they live in.
3. Answer the lesson's questions from the lesson, without looking back.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else — the lesson is worked from the learner's own life in Rwanda and the book's own teaching, as the book itself works.
