# Helping and Inclusive Education

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: ways to help learners with special needs and the impact of inclusive education. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Rwanda's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

## Sub lesson 1 — Ways to help learners with special needs

Learners with special needs are helped in concrete ways — support fitted to the need, given inside the ordinary classroom community.

## Sub lesson 2 — The impact of inclusive education

Inclusive education changes everyone: the learner with special needs belongs, and the whole class learns inclusion by living it.
