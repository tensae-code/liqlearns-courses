# Rights and Freedoms — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The catalogue of constitutional rights and freedoms and the duties that accompany them, summarised by topic.  b) it has nothing to do with how Kazakhstan governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The catalogue of constitutional rights and freedoms and the duties that accompany them, summarised by topic.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

a) The catalogue of constitutional rights and freedoms and the duties that accompany them, summarised by topic.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Kazakhstan
**Answer: a) The catalogue of constitutional rights and freedoms and the duties that accompany them, summarised by topic.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
