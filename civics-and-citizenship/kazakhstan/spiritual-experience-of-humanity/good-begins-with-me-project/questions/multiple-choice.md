# Good Begins with Me Project — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) A year-long good-deeds project kept in the workbook project diary and presented at year's end.  b) it has nothing to do with how Kazakhstan governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) A year-long good-deeds project kept in the workbook project diary and presented at year's end.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) A year-long good-deeds project kept in the workbook project diary and presented at year's end.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Kazakhstan
**Answer: a) A year-long good-deeds project kept in the workbook project diary and presented at year's end.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
