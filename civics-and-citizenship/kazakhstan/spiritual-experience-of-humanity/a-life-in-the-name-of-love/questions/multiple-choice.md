# A Life in the Name of Love — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Lives devoted to love of people as exemplars.  b) it has nothing to do with how Kazakhstan governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Lives devoted to love of people as exemplars.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Lives devoted to love of people as exemplars.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Kazakhstan
**Answer: a) Lives devoted to love of people as exemplars.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
