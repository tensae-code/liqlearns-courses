# At the Call of the Heart — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Acting from inner moral calling.  b) it has nothing to do with how Kazakhstan governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Acting from inner moral calling.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Acting from inner moral calling.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Kazakhstan
**Answer: a) Acting from inner moral calling.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
