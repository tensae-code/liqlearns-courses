# The Voice of Conscience — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Hearing and obeying conscience.  b) it has nothing to do with how Kazakhstan governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Hearing and obeying conscience.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Hearing and obeying conscience.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Kazakhstan
**Answer: a) Hearing and obeying conscience.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
