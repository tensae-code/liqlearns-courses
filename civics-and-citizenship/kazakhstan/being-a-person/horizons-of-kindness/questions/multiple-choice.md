# Horizons of Kindness — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Widening the reach of one's kindness, volunteering among it.  b) it has nothing to do with how Kazakhstan governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Widening the reach of one's kindness, volunteering among it.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Widening the reach of one's kindness, volunteering among it.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Kazakhstan
**Answer: a) Widening the reach of one's kindness, volunteering among it.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
