# To Be Not to Seem — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Authenticity against showing off.  b) it has nothing to do with how Kazakhstan governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Authenticity against showing off.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Authenticity against showing off.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Kazakhstan
**Answer: a) Authenticity against showing off.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
