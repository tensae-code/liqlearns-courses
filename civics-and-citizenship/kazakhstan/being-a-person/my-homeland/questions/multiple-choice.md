# My Homeland — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Kazakhstan as the child's homeland, its land and people.  b) it has nothing to do with how Kazakhstan governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Kazakhstan as the child's homeland, its land and people.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Kazakhstan as the child's homeland, its land and people.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Kazakhstan
**Answer: a) Kazakhstan as the child's homeland, its land and people.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
