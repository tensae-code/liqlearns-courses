# Generosity and Magnanimity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Giving freely and forgiving greatly.  b) it has nothing to do with how Kazakhstan governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Giving freely and forgiving greatly.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Giving freely and forgiving greatly.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Kazakhstan
**Answer: a) Giving freely and forgiving greatly.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
