# The Joy of Work — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Labour as joy and dignity.  b) it has nothing to do with how Kazakhstan governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Labour as joy and dignity.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Labour as joy and dignity.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Kazakhstan
**Answer: a) Labour as joy and dignity.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
