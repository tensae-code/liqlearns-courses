# Goodwill to Others — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Wishing others well and acting on it.  b) it has nothing to do with how Kazakhstan governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Wishing others well and acting on it.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Wishing others well and acting on it.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Kazakhstan
**Answer: a) Wishing others well and acting on it.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
