# Independence and Responsibility — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Acting on your own and answering for it.  b) it has nothing to do with how Kazakhstan governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Acting on your own and answering for it.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Acting on your own and answering for it.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Kazakhstan
**Answer: a) Acting on your own and answering for it.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
