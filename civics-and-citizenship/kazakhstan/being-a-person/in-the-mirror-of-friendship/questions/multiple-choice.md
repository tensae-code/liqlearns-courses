# In the Mirror of Friendship — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Friendship showing you yourself.  b) it has nothing to do with how Kazakhstan governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Friendship showing you yourself.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Friendship showing you yourself.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Kazakhstan
**Answer: a) Friendship showing you yourself.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
