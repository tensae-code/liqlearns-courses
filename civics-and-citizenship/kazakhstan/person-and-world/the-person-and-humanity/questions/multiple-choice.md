# The Person and Humanity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) One person's place in humankind.  b) it has nothing to do with how Kazakhstan governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) One person's place in humankind.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) One person's place in humankind.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Kazakhstan
**Answer: a) One person's place in humankind.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
