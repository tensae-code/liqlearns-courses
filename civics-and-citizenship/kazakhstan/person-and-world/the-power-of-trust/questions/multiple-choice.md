# The Power of Trust — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Trust as the ground of relationships.  b) it has nothing to do with how Kazakhstan governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Trust as the ground of relationships.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Trust as the ground of relationships.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Kazakhstan
**Answer: a) Trust as the ground of relationships.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
