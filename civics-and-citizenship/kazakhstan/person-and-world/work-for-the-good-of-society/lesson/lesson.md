# Work for the Good of Society

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Work for the Good of Society. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Kazakhstan's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Labour directed beyond oneself to society's benefit.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Labour directed beyond oneself to society's benefit. Give one real example from your own life or from the Kazakhstan you live in, and name the lesson's key words: work for. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Do the opening act of this lesson again today, on purpose: repeat the Do it first act and notice what changes.
2. Write or draw the lesson's main teaching in your own words: Labour directed beyond oneself to society's benefit.
3. Teach one part of this lesson to someone at home, and watch for one place this week where the lesson happens in real life. Note it.
