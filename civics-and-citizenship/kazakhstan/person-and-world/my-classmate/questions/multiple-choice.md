# My Classmate — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Knowing and valuing the people beside you.  b) it has nothing to do with how Kazakhstan governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Knowing and valuing the people beside you.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Knowing and valuing the people beside you.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Kazakhstan
**Answer: a) Knowing and valuing the people beside you.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
