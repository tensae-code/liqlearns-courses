# The Warmth of the Family Hearth — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The home as warmth the pupil helps keep.  b) it has nothing to do with how Kazakhstan governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The home as warmth the pupil helps keep.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) The home as warmth the pupil helps keep.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Kazakhstan
**Answer: a) The home as warmth the pupil helps keep.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
