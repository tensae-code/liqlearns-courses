# Happiness in Unity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Unity with people and world as happiness.  b) it has nothing to do with how Kazakhstan governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Unity with people and world as happiness.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Unity with people and world as happiness.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Kazakhstan
**Answer: a) Unity with people and world as happiness.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
