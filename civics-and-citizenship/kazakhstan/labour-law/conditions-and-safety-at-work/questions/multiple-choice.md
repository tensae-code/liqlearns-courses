# Conditions and Safety at Work — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Working time, rest and safety at work.  b) it has nothing to do with how Kazakhstan governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Working time, rest and safety at work.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Working time, rest and safety at work.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Kazakhstan
**Answer: a) Working time, rest and safety at work.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
