# Caring for Native Nature — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Practical care for local nature.  b) it has nothing to do with how Kazakhstan governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Practical care for local nature.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Practical care for local nature.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Kazakhstan
**Answer: a) Practical care for local nature.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
