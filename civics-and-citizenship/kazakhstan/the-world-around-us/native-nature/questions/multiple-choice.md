# Native Nature — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Loving and noticing the nature of the home land.  b) it has nothing to do with how Kazakhstan governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Loving and noticing the nature of the home land.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Loving and noticing the nature of the home land.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Kazakhstan
**Answer: a) Loving and noticing the nature of the home land.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
