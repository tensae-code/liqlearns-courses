# In the World of Beauty — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Beauty in nature, art and conduct.  b) it has nothing to do with how Kazakhstan governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Beauty in nature, art and conduct.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Beauty in nature, art and conduct.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Kazakhstan
**Answer: a) Beauty in nature, art and conduct.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
