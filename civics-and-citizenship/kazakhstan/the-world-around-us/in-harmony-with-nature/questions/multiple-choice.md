# In Harmony with Nature — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Living in balance with the natural world.  b) it has nothing to do with how Kazakhstan governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Living in balance with the natural world.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Living in balance with the natural world.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Kazakhstan
**Answer: a) Living in balance with the natural world.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
