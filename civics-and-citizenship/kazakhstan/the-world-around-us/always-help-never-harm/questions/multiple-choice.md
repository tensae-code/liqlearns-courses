# Always Help Never Harm — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Care for living things and doing no harm.  b) it has nothing to do with how Kazakhstan governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Care for living things and doing no harm.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Care for living things and doing no harm.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Kazakhstan
**Answer: a) Care for living things and doing no harm.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
