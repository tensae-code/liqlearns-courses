# Earth — Our Common Home — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** The planet as a shared home to protect; Shared responsibility for the planet; The planet shared by all peoples.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that Earth — Our Common Home — and that it has no connection to the rest of Kazakhstan's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Kazakhstan's own course, in its own order.
