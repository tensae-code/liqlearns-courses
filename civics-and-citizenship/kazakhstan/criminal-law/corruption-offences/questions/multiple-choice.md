# Corruption Offences — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Corruption as a crime and the state's fight against it.  b) it has nothing to do with how Kazakhstan governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Corruption as a crime and the state's fight against it.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Corruption as a crime and the state's fight against it.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Kazakhstan
**Answer: a) Corruption as a crime and the state's fight against it.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
