# Beginning Life with Honesty — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Honesty as the first value, through stories and wise sayings for the youngest pupils.  b) it has nothing to do with how Kazakhstan governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Honesty as the first value, through stories and wise sayings for the youngest pupils.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Honesty as the first value, through stories and wise sayings for the youngest pupils.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Kazakhstan
**Answer: a) Honesty as the first value, through stories and wise sayings for the youngest pupils.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
