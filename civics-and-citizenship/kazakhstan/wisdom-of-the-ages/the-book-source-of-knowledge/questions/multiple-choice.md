# The Book Source of Knowledge — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Reading as the road to knowledge.  b) it has nothing to do with how Kazakhstan governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Reading as the road to knowledge.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Reading as the road to knowledge.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Kazakhstan
**Answer: a) Reading as the road to knowledge.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
