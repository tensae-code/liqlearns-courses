# Study is my Work — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false, and be ready to say why.

**1.** Learning as the schoolchild's own labour and duty.
**Answer: True**

**2.** This lesson teaches that Study is my Work — and that it has no connection to the rest of Kazakhstan's story.
**Answer: False** — The lesson places it inside Kazakhstan's own course, in its own order.
