# Love Asks No Reward — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Unselfish love for people close to you.  b) it has nothing to do with how Kazakhstan governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Unselfish love for people close to you.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Unselfish love for people close to you.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Kazakhstan
**Answer: a) Unselfish love for people close to you.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
