# Andersen's Wise Tales — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Moral judgement through fairy tales.  b) it has nothing to do with how Kazakhstan governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Moral judgement through fairy tales.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Moral judgement through fairy tales.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Kazakhstan
**Answer: a) Moral judgement through fairy tales.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
