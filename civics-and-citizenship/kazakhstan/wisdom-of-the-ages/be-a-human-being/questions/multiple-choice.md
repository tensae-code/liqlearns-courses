# Be a Human Being — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) What it means to be truly human.  b) it has nothing to do with how Kazakhstan governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) What it means to be truly human.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) What it means to be truly human.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Kazakhstan
**Answer: a) What it means to be truly human.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
