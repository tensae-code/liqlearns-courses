# Discipline and Duty

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Discipline and Duty. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Kazakhstan's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

What discipline and a sense of duty mean for a schoolchild.

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: What discipline and a sense of duty mean for a schoolchild. Give one real example from your own life or from the Kazakhstan you live in, and name the lesson's key words: discipline and. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
