# School Our Common Home — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) The school community, its rules and shared care.  b) it has nothing to do with how Kazakhstan governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) The school community, its rules and shared care.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) The school community, its rules and shared care.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Kazakhstan
**Answer: a) The school community, its rules and shared care.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
