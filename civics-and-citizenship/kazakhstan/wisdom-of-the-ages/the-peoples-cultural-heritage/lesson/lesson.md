# The Peoples Cultural Heritage

## Do it first

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: The Peoples Cultural Heritage. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Kazakhstan's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Kazakh cultural heritage — tales, customs and wise figures — as the child's inheritance.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what this lesson teaches: Kazakh cultural heritage — tales, customs and wise figures — as the child's inheritance. Give one real example from your own life or from the Kazakhstan you live in, and name the lesson's key words: peoples cultural. Your companion asks one question back — answer it from the lesson.
