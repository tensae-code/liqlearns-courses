# My Home — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Home and family as the child's first community.  b) it has nothing to do with how Kazakhstan governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Home and family as the child's first community.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Home and family as the child's first community.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Kazakhstan
**Answer: a) Home and family as the child's first community.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
