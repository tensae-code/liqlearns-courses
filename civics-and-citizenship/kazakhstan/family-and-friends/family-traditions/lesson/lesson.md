# Family Traditions

Before anyone explains anything, say in your own words what you already think this is about: Family Traditions. Write it or say it aloud — this lesson starts from what Kazakhstan's own book teaches about it, and you will compare your first thought with the book's teaching as you go.

Traditions that hold a family together, Kazakh family customs among them.
