# Everything Begins with Love — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Love as the start of family and friendship.  b) it has nothing to do with how Kazakhstan governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Love as the start of family and friendship.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Love as the start of family and friendship.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Kazakhstan
**Answer: a) Love as the start of family and friendship.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
