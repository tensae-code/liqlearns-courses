# Civil Society — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Civil society and its relation to the state.  b) it has nothing to do with how Kazakhstan governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Civil society and its relation to the state.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Civil society and its relation to the state.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Kazakhstan
**Answer: a) Civil society and its relation to the state.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
