# What Law is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Law as a system of norms among social norms, its features and place.  b) it has nothing to do with how Kazakhstan governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Law as a system of norms among social norms, its features and place.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Law as a system of norms among social norms, its features and place.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Kazakhstan
**Answer: a) Law as a system of norms among social norms, its features and place.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
