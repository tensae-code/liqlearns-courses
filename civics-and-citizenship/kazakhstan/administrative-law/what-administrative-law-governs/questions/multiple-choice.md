# What Administrative Law Governs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which statement best gives this lesson's teaching?
a) Administrative law and public administration.  b) it has nothing to do with how Kazakhstan governs itself  c) it was invented by foreign governments  d) it matters only in examinations
**Answer: a) Administrative law and public administration.** — That is what this lesson teaches.

**2.** What is this lesson's one thing?
a) Administrative law and public administration.  b) citizens should never ask where a rule comes from  c) only officials need to know this  d) it applies in other countries but not in Kazakhstan
**Answer: a) Administrative law and public administration.** — That is what this lesson teaches.
