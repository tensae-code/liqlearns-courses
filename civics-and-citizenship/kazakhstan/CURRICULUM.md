# Civics and Citizenship — Kazakhstan course — CURRICULUM (the path)

One course for Kazakhstan inside the Civics and Citizenship folder,
organised by **levels, not grades**: a learner climbs when they
have proved the level, not when a school calendar says so. The
school grades the books come from appear only in each lesson's
sources (provenance), never as the course's organisation. The path
follows the books' own arcs, as sketched at the end of the filing:
Kazakhstan teaches citizenship through **Samopoznanie
(Self-Knowledge)** — a ten-year spiral of universal human values,
in paired story lessons, opening with honesty for the youngest
child and widening circle by circle — and, at Grade 9, through its
one school law course, **Fundamentals of Law**, a full tour of
Kazakhstan's own legal system, constitution first.

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in this
course comes from government textbooks — this country's own
Samopoznanie (Self-Knowledge) and Fundamentals of Law books —
except the **Tip boxes**: a tip in a box is from us, the people
making LiqLearns. Tips are ours; the lessons are the books'. See
ABOUT.md for the full book list, the parallel-edition footing of
every book, and the acceptance reckoning.

**Parallel editions, as filed:** every book in this course was
issued in parallel Russian and Kazakh editions (the Grade 9 law
book also in Uyghur). Per the filing's parallel-edition rule, one
edition of each book was read in full and the parallel
spot-checked; placements are filed once per distinct book, and
this course is built once per distinct book.

## The levels (the path, in order)

1. **Honesty, Home and the First Homeland** — Grade 1 Samopoznanie — wisdom-of-the-ages / family-and-friends / being-a-person / the-world-around-us: the youngest child's values — honesty, discipline and duty, home, friends, helping, gratitude, the homeland and the Earth as a common home, taught in paired story lessons. 17 lessons (17 placements filed at the level).

2. **Wisdom, School and Learning to Forgive** — Grade 2 — the same four units: the spiral repeats with Abai's wisdom, Andersen's tales, school as a common home, forgiveness and the country where we live. 16 lessons (17 placements filed at the level).

3. **Universal Values and the People's Heritage** — Grade 3 — the same four units: values are named universal, the people's cultural heritage enters, and patriotism of one's country becomes an explicit lesson. 15 lessons (17 placements filed at the level).

4. **Conscience and "I Am a Citizen!"** — Grade 4 — the same four units: primary school culminates in conscience and in "I am a citizen!" — the first civic self-naming, on Abai and Shakarim's teaching about life's purpose. 15 lessons (17 placements filed at the level).

5. **Knowing Yourself — the Middle-School Course Begins** — Grade 5 — joy-of-knowing / being-a-person / person-and-world / spiritual-experience-of-humanity: the sections are renamed for middle school; self-knowledge, character, trust and the people's spiritual heritage, with the "Good begins with me" project. 18 lessons (18 placements filed at the level).

6. **Empathy, Honour and Work for the Good of Society** — Grade 6 — the same four units: empathy, conscience and honour, work for the good of society, and the ecological year project "Nature is a priceless gift". 17 lessons (17 placements filed at the level).

7. **Dignity, Duty and Service to Society** — Grade 7 — the same four units: dignity, responsibility, discipline maturing into duty, service to society, and the "Friendship in the collective" project. 17 lessons (17 placements filed at the level).

8. **Calling and the Road to the Motherland** — Grade 8 — the same four units: vocation and the road to the motherland — calling, self-mastery, creating the future; the Kazakh edition also stages a "Service to society" project. 16 lessons (16 placements filed at the level).

9. **Freedom, Service and the Law of Kazakhstan** — Grade 9 — Samopoznanie's four units plus the law strand (law-and-the-state / constitutional-law / civil-law / labour-law / family-law / administrative-law / criminal-law): the ladder's civic peak — Samopoznanie turns to path-choice, freedom and service while Fundamentals of Law gives the pupil the whole of Kazakhstan's legal system, constitution first. 44 lessons (44 placements filed at the level).

10. **Worldview, Leadership and Moral Choice** — Grade 10 — on-the-path-of-knowing / formation-of-the-personality / life-in-society / the-world-of-humanity: the capstone — worldview, self-education, leadership, the joy of service, life online and moral choice, gathered in the "My calling" project. 17 lessons (17 placements filed at the level).

- **Footing — student books throughout**: every lesson in this
  course stands on a student book (the Samopoznanie textbooks
  and the Grade 9 Fundamentals of Law textbook). No lesson
  stands on a teacher guide or a programme — the filing names
  none — so no lesson in this course stands at acceptance 0
  (see ABOUT.md).
- **Companion workbooks, carried as filed**: the six pupil's
  workbooks (Grades 5, 6, 7, 8, 9 and 10 Samopoznanie) are filed
  COMPLETE with 0 placements each — companion workbooks whose
  tasks are not placed separately under the standing exclusion
  for pure activity items; the year projects they serve are
  placed with their textbooks.
- **Not built, named plainly**: the filing names no Samopoznanie
  book above Grade 10 — Book 16 is filed as "the final
  Samopoznanie volume in the census" — so the ladder ends at
  Level 10 exactly as filed, and no level pretends to a Grade 11
  book. The books' closing consolidation tests, "Check yourself"
  tasks, glossaries, name indexes, family-reading appendix and
  recommended-literature lists are assessment or inventory
  apparatus in the filing, named not placed, and no lesson
  pretends to them.
