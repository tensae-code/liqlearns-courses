# Video script — Congratulating and wishing

Super-simple voice. Read slow. The learner hears this
before or beside the written lesson.

---

Good news!
Your friend passed the exam.
Say: Congratulations!
Congratulations on your exam!
Well done! Good job!

It is a birthday.
Happy birthday!
Best wishes! Good luck!

Someone helped you.
Write a thank-you card.
Thank you. Thank you very much.

Now bad news.
Your friend is sad. Her father is in hospital.
Do not say Congratulations.
Say: I'm so sorry.
What a shame.
You poor thing.

She says: Thank you for your kind words.

Good news — big smile, big words.
Bad news — soft voice, kind words.
Now you try.
One good news. One bad news.
Say the right words for both.
