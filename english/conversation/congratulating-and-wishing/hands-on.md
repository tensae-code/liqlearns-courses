# Hands-on — Congratulating and wishing

You do it, we guide you. Everything here is done with a
companion or the Explain Companion, and six paper cards
you make yourself.

1. **The six cards.** Make Mongolia's six cards: Birthday,
   Valentine's, Christmas, Thank-you, Appreciation,
   Graduation. Your companion names a moment — "my sister
   finished school", "Tom baked me a cake" — you hold up
   the right card and write its first line.
2. **The news game.** Draw a news card: passed an exam,
   won a race, a birthday, a new baby — or bad ones: a
   stolen car, a friend in hospital, a fight with a
   friend. React in two seconds, the Libya way: the right
   words, the right voice. A congratulation on bad news
   loses the round.
3. **The real message.** Nepal's writing task, for real:
   write a true congratulations message to someone you
   know — four lines: the news, "Congratulations on …",
   one wish, your name. Send it or keep it in the card.
4. **The phone congratulation.** Nepal's guide runs it as
   a telephone tapescript — Jubeda congratulates Krishna
   on an award. Do it live: call your companion and
   congratulate them, voice only, with one wish added.
