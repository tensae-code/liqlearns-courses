# Congratulating and wishing — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Congratulations, the three shapes · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The wishes bank · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 4 — Sympathy, the bad-news half.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Your friend passed her exam. You say —
a) Congratulations!  b) What a shame!  c) You poor thing!
**Answer: a) Congratulations!** — Mauritania's first
shape, for Omar's pass (Lesson 17).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The full shape with the win named is —
a) Congratulations to win  b) Congratulations on your
exam!  c) Congratulate the exam is you
**Answer: b)** — "Congratulations on…" is Mauritania's
second shape (Lesson 17).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Your friend has an exam TOMORROW. You
say —
a) Well done!  b) Good luck!  c) Happy birthday!
**Answer: b) Good luck!** — good luck looks forward, to
a thing not yet done (Mauritania's wishes bank,
Lesson 18).

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Your friend says: "My car was stolen."
You say —
a) Congratulations!  b) Well done!  c) I'm so sorry.
**Answer: c) I'm so sorry** — Libya sorts the stolen car
to the negative vibes, with "I'm so sorry" (Grade 11,
Unit 7, Lesson 6).
