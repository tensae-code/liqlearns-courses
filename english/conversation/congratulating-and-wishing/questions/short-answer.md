# Congratulating and wishing — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Congratulations, the three shapes · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 3 — Cards and messages (item 2 writes a card's first line) · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 4 — Sympathy, the bad-news half.

Answer in whole sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Congratulate your teacher on winning an
award, naming the win.
**Answer: Congratulations on your award!** (Mauritania's
"Congratulations on…" shape, Lesson 17; Nepal's message
to Mahabir Pun is the written model.)

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Write the first line of a birthday card.
**Answer: Happy birthday! Best wishes!** (Any line from
Mauritania's wishes bank earns it; the card kind is
Mongolia's Birthday card.)

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** Your friend's father is in hospital.
She thanks you for your kind words — first, say the
kind words she is thanking you for.
**Answer: You poor thing! / You must be really
worried.** (Libya's Farah line to Reema, Grade 11,
Unit 7, Lesson 6.)
