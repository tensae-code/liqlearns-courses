# Congratulating and wishing — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Congratulations, the three shapes · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 3 — Cards and messages · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 4 — Sympathy, the bad-news half.

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** "I congratulate you on…" is the full,
formal shape of congratulating.
**True** — Mauritania prints all three shapes together
(Lesson 17).

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** A Graduation card is one of the greeting
card kinds.
**True** — it stands in Mongolia's six kinds, with
Birthday, Valentine's, Christmas, Thank-you and
Appreciation (English VIII, Unit 1).

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** "Congratulations!" is a kind answer to
bad news.
**False** — bad news takes the sympathy set: "I'm so
sorry", "What a shame!", "You poor thing!" (Libya,
Grade 11, Unit 7, Lesson 6; Syria pairs the two halves
the same way).
