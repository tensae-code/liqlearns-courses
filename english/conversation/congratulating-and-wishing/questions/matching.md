# Congratulating and wishing — matching

Match each piece of news to the right answer.

**Set 1 (Level 1–3)**
1. I passed my driving test! — **Congratulations!**
2. I got full marks in maths! — **Well done!**
3. My car was stolen — **I'm so sorry**
4. I had a fight with my best friend — **What a shame!**

(Set 1 is Libya's own sorted set — Grade 11, Unit 7,
Lesson 6, "Positive and Negative Vibes.")
