# Congratulating and wishing — fill in the blank

Fill each gap with the right words.

**1. (Level 1)** "______ on your exam! You passed!"
**Answer: Congratulations** — Mauritania's shape with the
win named (Lesson 17).

**2. (Level 1)** "You won the race! ______ done!"
**Answer: Well** — Well done looks back at a finished
win (Mauritania's bank, Lesson 18).

**3. (Level 2)** "______ birthday! ______ wishes for your
new year!"
**Answer: Happy / Best** — two bank lines for the
birthday card (Mauritania, Lesson 18; Mongolia's card
kinds).

**4. (Level 3)** "I fought with my best friend." —
"What a ______!"
**Answer: shame** — Libya sorts the fight to "What a
shame!" (Grade 11, Unit 7, Lesson 6).
