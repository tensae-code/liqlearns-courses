**Acceptance count: taught in 5 countries (Mauritania,
Nepal, Mongolia, Libya, Syria).** Mauritania teaches the
function whole and names its final unit for it —
congratulating and wishing — with the three congratulation
shapes at Lesson 17 and the wishes bank at Lesson 18.
Nepal writes it down: a congratulation letter and
congratulation messages in its Grade 9 book, and a whole
"Expressing congratulation" unit in its Grade 9 Teachers'
Guide (guide evidence for an older textbook generation,
as filed; the guide's "Roman" misprint for Ramon
Magsaysay is taught corrected here). Mongolia stocks the
cards — six kinds matched, a card and a reply written.
Libya stocks the answering of news whole, good and bad,
in its "Positive and Negative Vibes" lesson — the
sympathy dialogue and the sorted news exchanges. Syria
pairs congratulating with expressing sympathy in one
Everyday English slot, in both its Student's Book and its
Workbook. Other closed countries join as their books are
read (see READING-QUEUE.md).
