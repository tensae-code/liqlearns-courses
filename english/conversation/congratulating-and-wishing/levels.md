# Congratulating and wishing — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB
LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub
Lessons"). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number, as
before. The questions live in the type files; this file is the
by-sub-lesson view. The sub lessons are the depths inside this one thing — answering news — the congratulation shapes first, then the wishes bank and cards, then the bad-news half and its answer.

Mapping from the old depth layers: old Level 1 = Sub lesson 1 (its "Well done!" sits in Sub lesson 2's bank, and its answering-with-thanks in Sub lesson 5); old Level 2 splits into Sub lesson 2 (the wishes bank) and Sub lesson 3 (cards and messages); old Level 3 (sympathy) = Sub lesson 4, with its kind-words answer carried in Sub lesson 5.
