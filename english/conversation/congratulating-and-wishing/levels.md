# Congratulating and wishing — questions by level

Practice by level, tied to the skill number. The questions
live in the type files; this file is the by-level view. The
levels are the depths inside this one thing — answering
news — set by the pattern across the countries' books: the
congratulation shapes first (Mauritania teaches them as a
unit of their own), then the wishes bank and cards, then
the bad-news half.

## Level 1 — congratulations
Level 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: give
"Congratulations!", "Congratulations on …" and "Well
done!" to the right wins, and answer with thanks, without
a miss.

## Level 2 — wishes and cards
Level 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: choose
from the wishes bank for a birthday, an exam tomorrow and
a thank-you — and name the right card kind for each
moment (Mongolia's six).

## Level 3 — sympathy
Level 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: meet
bad news with the sympathy set — "I'm so sorry", "What a
shame!", "You poor thing!" — and never with a
congratulation; and answer kindness with "Thank you for
your kind words."
