# Video script — Everyday Replies and Social Responses

Super-simple voice. The presenter is the LiqLearns mascot,
front-facing, on screen the whole video; the pictures named here
appear beside him.

[A girl walks up smiling; the mascot's face worries.] "Oh no. I
forgot her name! … I'm sorry — tell me your name again? …
Sara! Of course! How are you, Sara?"

[An escalator; the mascot stands on the right while a boy walks
up on the left.] "On the escalator: stand on the RIGHT. Walk on
the LEFT. Excuse me!"

[A small bump in a corridor; both smile.] "Sorry! … After you!"
