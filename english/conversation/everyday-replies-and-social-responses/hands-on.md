# Hands-on — Everyday Replies and Social Responses

These words are learned in the moment, with real people. Do
these with a partner, out loud.

## The forgotten name

Walk around the room greeting your classmates — but in the game,
nobody may use a name until it has been given inside the game.
When a name is "forgotten," use the lesson's repair: say sorry,
ask kindly, and use the name at once when it comes back.

## Escalator drill

On the stairs (or a real escalator): practise standing in a
line on the right while one person passes on the left, saying
"Excuse me" as they pass. The person passed answers with a
smile and steps in if they are standing wrong: "Sorry!"
