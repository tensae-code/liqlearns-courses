## Multiple choice

1. You meet a girl you know, but you have forgotten her name. What is the best thing to do? (Sub lesson 1)
   a) Pretend you remember and say nothing
   b) Run away before she speaks
   c) Say sorry and ask kindly: "Tell me your name again?"
   **Answer: c)** — say sorry, ask kindly; pretending and running away make the moment worse.

2. She says: "I'm Sara." What do you do next? (Sub lesson 1)
   a) Use her name at once: "Sara! Of course. How are you, Sara?"
   b) Say nothing
   c) Ask her name again
   **Answer: a)** — using the name at once fixes it in your memory and shows you are glad to know it.

3. On an escalator, where do people stand? (Sub lesson 2)
   a) On the left  b) On the right  c) In the middle, holding both rails
   **Answer: b) the right** — stand on the right; the left is for people walking up.

4. You bump into someone in a corridor. The first word is… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) "Sorry!"  b) "Move!"  c) nothing — walk on
   **Answer: a) "Sorry!"** — the small word, said at once with a smile.

5. Someone has forgotten YOUR name and looks worried. The kind thing is… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) Wait and watch them struggle
   b) Give your name with a smile: "Hi, I'm Tomas — we met at school."
   c) Ask why they forgot
   **Answer: b)** — giving your name costs nothing and saves their moment.

6. You are passing people on the escalator, on the left. As you pass, you say… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) "Excuse me."  b) "Faster!"  c) "After you."
   **Answer: a) "Excuse me"** — the small word for passing.
