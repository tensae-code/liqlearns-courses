## True or false

1. If you forget a name, the best plan is to pretend you remember it. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: False** — say sorry and ask kindly; pretending makes the moment worse.

2. Using a person's name straight after they tell it to you helps you keep it in memory. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: True** — use the name at once, in your next words.

3. On an escalator you should stand on the left and walk on the right. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: False** — stand on the right; walk on the left.

4. A quick "sorry," said at once with a smile, can turn an awkward moment into nothing. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: True** — that is the rule under all the small repairs.
