## Matching

Match the moment to the words that fix it. (Sub lessons 1–2)

1. You have forgotten a name — b
2. You bump into someone — a
3. You pass on the escalator — c
4. You let someone go through the door first — d
5. Someone tells you their name again — e

a) "Sorry!"
b) "I'm sorry — I've forgotten your name."
c) "Excuse me."
d) "After you."
e) Use their name at once: "Sara! Of course."

**Answer key with reasons:** 1–b (the honest sorry opens the repair), 2–a (the bump's word), 3–c (the passing word), 4–d (the door word), 5–e (using the name fixes it in memory).
