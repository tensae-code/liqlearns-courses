## Fill in the blank

1. "I'm sorry — I've ___ your name." (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: forgotten** — the honest word that opens the repair.

2. "Sorry, tell me your ___ again?" (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: name** — the kind question that brings the name back.

3. On an escalator, stand on the ___ and walk on the ___. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: right; left** — the escalator rule.

4. You are blocking a doorway. As you move, you say: "___ me." (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: Excuse** — "Excuse me" is the word for the block.

5. Holding the door and letting the other person go first: "___ you." (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: After** — "After you," said with a smile.
