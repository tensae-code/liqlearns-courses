## Short answer

1. A boy you know says hello, and his name is gone. Write what you say. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: "I'm sorry — I've forgotten your name," or "Sorry, tell me your name again?"** — sorry first, then the kind question.

2. You are standing on the left side of an escalator and someone comes up behind you. What do you say and do? (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: Say "Sorry!" and step to the right** — the left side is for walking.

3. You drop your books in front of a stranger in the corridor. What is the first thing you say? (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: "Sorry!"** — said at once, with a smile, as you pick them up.
