# Video script — Agreeing and disagreeing

Super-simple voice. Read slow. The learner hears this
before or beside the written lesson.

---

[Picture: two friends, one bowl of soup]

I like soup.
So do I!
Your friend says a yes-thing. You are the same.
So do I. Short and glad.

I don't like tea.
Neither do I.
A no-thing — and you are the same.
Neither do I. Nor do I.

I don't like tea.
Oh — I do.
Not the same? Say your own truth.
I don't. Or: I do.

[Picture: a speech bubble with steps]

Now a big view. Give it a frame.
I think… In my opinion…
First… Second… Finally…
A view with steps can climb.

[Picture: two debaters shaking hands]

I agree. You're right.
I agree — but…
I don't agree. I disagree.
Disagree with the idea. Stay friends with the person.

Now you try.
Tell me one thing you like.
I will echo. Tell me one view.
I will agree — or disagree kindly.
Then we change places.
