# Agreeing and disagreeing — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** "I like swimming." You like it too:
a) So do I.  b) Neither do I.  c) I don't.
**Answer: a) So do I.** — the yes-world echo (Mongolia, English VI, p.80; Cambodia, Grade 10, Unit 8).

**2. (Level 1)** "I don't like getting up early." You don't like it either:
a) So do I.  b) Neither do I.  c) Yes, I do.
**Answer: b) Neither do I.** — the no-world echo; Mongolia's dress for it is "Nor do I" (English VI, Unit 7).

**3. (Level 2)** Which sentence states a view in its frame?
a) In my opinion, the town needs a library.  b) The library is over there.  c) A library is a building.
**Answer: a) In my opinion, the town needs a library.** — Cambodia's opinion frame (Grade 10, Unit 29).

**4. (Level 2)** You agree with most of a view but not all. The door-open answer is:
a) I agree, but…  b) Nonsense!  c) You're wrong.
**Answer: a) I agree, but…** — Cambodia's debate set (Grade 10, Unit 29, pp.157–162).

**5. (Level 3)** Mauritania's argument frame runs: claim → reasons → —
a) silence  b) conclusion  c) a new topic
**Answer: b) conclusion** — claim ("In my view / I think…"), reasons ("First… Second… Finally…"), conclusion (Study English Grade 12, Lesson 7, pp.23–24).
