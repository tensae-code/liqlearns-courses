# Agreeing and disagreeing — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — So do I, the yes-world echo · Sub lesson 2 — Neither do I, the no-world echo · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 3 — Stating a view, I think and In my opinion · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 (item 4 asks for the frame's last reason step, boxed there).

Fill each gap with the right word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** "I love fishing." — "______ do I!"
**Answer: So** — "So do I," the glad echo (Cambodia, Grade 10, Unit 8).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** "I don't mind the rain." — "______ do I." (you don't mind it either)
**Answer: Neither** — the no-world echo (Mongolia writes it "Nor do I," English VI, Unit 7).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** "______ my opinion, school should start later."
**Answer: In** — "In my opinion…" (Cambodia, Grade 10, Unit 29; Mauritania, Lesson 7).

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** "First… Second… ______…" (the last reason step in Mauritania's frame)
**Answer: Finally** — the argument frame's reason steps (Study English Grade 12, p.24).
