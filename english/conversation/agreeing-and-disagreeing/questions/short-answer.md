# Agreeing and disagreeing — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — So do I, the yes-world echo · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 3 — Stating a view, I think and In my opinion · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 4 — Agreeing and disagreeing with a view.

Answer in whole sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Your friend says, "I get up at six." You also get up at six. Echo it.
**Answer: So do I.** (Mongolia's chain game, English VI, Unit 7, p.80.)

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** State your view: students should / should not bring phones to school. Use the frame.
**Answer: In my view, students should not bring phones to school.** (Any true framed view with "In my view / I think…" earns it — Mauritania, Lesson 7.)

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** Your companion argues tourism is only good. Disagree kindly, with one reason.
**Answer: I agree, but tourism can also hurt a town, because visitors leave rubbish.** (Agree-but + a reason — Cambodia, Grade 10, Unit 29's debate shape; any true reason earns it.)
