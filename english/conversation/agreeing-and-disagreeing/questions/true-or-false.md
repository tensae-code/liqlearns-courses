# Agreeing and disagreeing — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> by the world the item turns on: item 1 is the no-world echo, Sub lesson 2 — Neither do I, the no-world echo · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 4 — Agreeing and disagreeing with a view · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 4.

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** "So do I" is the right echo after "I don't like tea."
**False** — no-worlds echo with "Neither do I / Nor do I"; So belongs to yes-worlds (Mongolia, English VI, p.80).

**2. (Sub lesson 4)** "I agree, but…" agrees and disagrees at the same time, honestly.
**True** — it holds the agreement and opens the door to the difference (Cambodia, Grade 10, Unit 29).

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** In Uzbekistan's lessons, agreeing and disagreeing can only be spoken, never written.
**False** — its Grade 9 book teaches the move through letters for and against a school system (English Matters 9, Unit 3, Lessons 5–6, p.30).
