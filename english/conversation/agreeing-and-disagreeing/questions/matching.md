# Agreeing and disagreeing — matching

Match each sentence to its true echo or answer.

**Set 1 (Level 1–2)**
1. I like reading. — **So do I.**
2. I don't like cold mornings. — **Neither do I.**
3. I love football! — **I don't. I like swimming.**
4. I think our town needs a park. — **I agree. You're right.**

(The echoes are Mongolia's Grade 6 box set, printed p.80,
and Cambodia's Grade 10 leisure set, Unit 8; the differing
answer and the full agreement are Cambodia's Unit 29
debate answers in small form.)
