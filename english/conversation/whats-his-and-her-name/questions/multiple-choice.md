# What's his and her name? — multiple choice

Choice questions only. Answers at the bottom.

## Level 1
1. You point at a girl across the room. You ask:
   a) What's your name?  b) What's her name?  c) What's his name?  d) Who's this?
2. You point at a boy across the room. You ask:
   a) What's her name?  b) My name is?  c) What's his name?  d) How is she?
3. "What's her name?" — a right answer is:
   a) My name is Muna  b) Muna  c) I'm Muna's friend  d) Her is Muna

## Answers
1-b, 2-c, 3-b
