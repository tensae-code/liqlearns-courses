# Giving directions — questions by level

Practice by level, tied to the skill number. The questions
live in the type files; this file is the by-level view. The
levels are the depths inside this one thing — a route
another person can walk — set by the pattern across the
countries' books: asking and the straight/turn core first
(Saudi Arabia and Cambodia open there), then placing the
end, then whole routes with distance.

## Level 1 — ask, straight, turn
Level 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: ask
"Excuse me, where's the …?" and give "Go straight" and
"Turn left / Turn right" correctly, every time.

## Level 2 — placing the end
Level 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: finish
a route with its place words — on your left/right,
opposite, next to, in front of, behind, in between — and
use go along, go past and cross in the right seats.

## Level 3 — whole routes
Level 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: give a
whole three-step route in order, with a how-far or
how-long answer ("just around the corner", "about five
minutes"), so a partner can follow it on a map without
asking again.
