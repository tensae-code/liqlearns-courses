# Hands-on — Giving directions

You do it, we guide you. Everything here is done in the
learner's real home and street, with a companion or the
Explain Companion.

1. **The voice-only treasure.** Hide one thing in another
   room. Direct your companion to it with your voice
   only — go straight, turn left, go past the door, it's
   on your right. No pointing: the words must do the
   walking, the way Rwanda's written directions from the
   airstrip must.
2. **The map route.** Draw a five-building map (hotel,
   bank, post office, station, park) like Libya's street
   map. Your companion asks one of Libya's four questions
   — "Excuse me, where's the train station?" — you answer
   with a route; they trace it with a finger and must
   land on the right building.
3. **The place-word lap.** Walk the home naming places
   the Rwanda way: the chair is next to the table, the
   door is opposite the window, the bag is behind the
   bed. Ten true place sentences, said while touching
   each thing.
4. **The real corner.** Outside, at a real corner: ask
   the way to a place you both know, and give the route
   back — with how far: "It's just around the corner" or
   "It's far from here — about twenty minutes." Saudi
   Arabia's two answers, used on a real street.
