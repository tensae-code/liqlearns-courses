**Acceptance count: taught in 10 countries (Saudi Arabia,
Mongolia, Cambodia, Rwanda, Malaysia, Libya, Kuwait, Bolivia, China, Indonesia).****
Saudi Arabia teaches the exchange whole in one primary
unit — the ask, both distance answers, how long, and the
left/right landing. Mongolia works direction dialogues
over a town map inside its transport unit. Cambodia
teaches it twice: a Grade 6 route home ("go straight …
then turn right") and a Grade 7 town unit whose third
lesson is named "Turn left, turn right". Rwanda teaches
it over a real town map — Butare — with its place-word
set, imperatives and compass points, and written
directions as the unit's writing. Malaysia models a full
guided route with sequence connectors in its independence
chapter. Libya gives directions a lesson of its own at
the hotel desk, four questions and four full routes over
a street map. Kuwait names the grammar of the route —
imperatives for directions — inside its culture unit.
China carries the route from the classroom out: position
words in Grade 4, turn-right route talk and a full
Recycle directions set in Grade 6, and polite way-asking
as a whole Grade 9 unit (stocked 2026-11-08 by the
re-verification sweep). Indonesia asks and gives
directions inside the school in Grade 7 (stocked
2026-11-09 by the re-verification sweep merge). Other closed countries join as their books are read (see
READING-QUEUE.md).
