# Video script — Giving directions

Super-simple voice. Read slow. The learner hears this
before or beside the written lesson.

---

Excuse me. Where is the post office?
Listen. I will tell you the way.

Go straight.
Go along this street.
Turn left at the corner.
Go past the bank.
The post office is on your right.
It is opposite the park.
It is next to the school.

Is it far?
No. It is just around the corner.
It is about five minutes away.

Turn right. Turn left.
In front of you. Behind you.
Next to. Opposite.

Now you try.
Show me the way to the station.
Straight. Turn. Past.
I can walk it now.
