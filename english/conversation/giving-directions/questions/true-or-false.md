# Giving directions — true or false

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Level 1)** Directions are given with imperatives —
"Turn left", "Go straight" — with no "you" in them.
**True** — Kuwait names imperatives as the grammar for
commands, instructions and directions (Skyline 5B,
Unit 7).

**2. (Level 2)** "Opposite" means the same as "next to".
**False** — opposite is facing across; next to is
beside. Rwanda teaches both in one set, as different
words (P.4, Unit 3).

**3. (Level 3)** A good route gives every step at once
with no order words.
**False** — Malaysia's model route holds its steps in
order with sequence connectors and turn language; order
is what lets a stranger carry the route (Form 1,
Chapter 13).
