# Giving directions — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** You are lost. The polite opening is —
a) Excuse me, where's the station?  b) Station give me
c) You, station now
**Answer: a)** — Saudi Arabia's unit opens with "Excuse
me. Where's the (post office)?" (Unit 11).

**2. (Level 1)** "Go ______ and turn left at the corner."
a) straight  b) opposite  c) cornering
**Answer: a) straight** — the route core (Cambodia,
Grade 7, Unit 24; Kuwait, Unit 7).

**3. (Level 2)** The bank stands facing the park, across
the street. The bank is ______ the park.
a) opposite  b) between the park  c) ago the park
**Answer: a) opposite** — Rwanda's place-word set
(P.4, Unit 3).

**4. (Level 3)** A place two minutes away is —
a) far from here  b) just around the corner  c) on your
left yesterday
**Answer: b) just around the corner** — Saudi Arabia's
near answer, against "far from here" (Unit 11).
