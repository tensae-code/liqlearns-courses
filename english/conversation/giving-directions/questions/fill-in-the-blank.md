# Giving directions — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 2 — The route verbs, go straight and turn · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 (item 2's first gaps, go past and cross, sit there; its "next to" is Sub lesson 3's) · Sub lesson 3 — Placing the end, opposite and next to · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 4 — How far, how long.

Fill each gap with the right words.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** "Turn ______ at the corner, then go
______."
**Answer: left (or right) / straight** — Cambodia's
lesson names: "Turn left, turn right" (Grade 7, Unit 24).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** "Go ______ the bank and cross the road.
The shop is ______ to the school."
**Answer: past / next** — past and cross are Mongolia's
direction-dialogue core (English VIII, Unit 6); next to
is Rwanda's place set.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** "It's ______ front of you, not behind
you."
**Answer: in** — in front of stands in both Saudi
Arabia's and Rwanda's sets (We Can! 3, Unit 11; P.4,
Unit 3).

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** "How ______ does it take?" — "About five
______."
**Answer: long / minutes** — Saudi Arabia's question
(Unit 11) with Libya's answer shape (Grade 10, Unit 3,
Lesson 7).
