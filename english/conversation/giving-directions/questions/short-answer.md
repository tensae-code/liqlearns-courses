# Giving directions — short answer

Answer in whole sentences.

**1. (Level 1)** Ask a stranger the way to the post
office, politely.
**Answer: Excuse me. Where's the post office?** (Saudi
Arabia, Unit 11; Libya's porter answers the same shape,
Grade 10, Unit 3, Lesson 7.)

**2. (Level 2)** The station is past the car park, on
Main Street, on the right. Give that route.
**Answer: Go along Main Street. It's on the right, just
past the car park.** (Libya's own answer, Grade 10,
Unit 3, Lesson 7.)

**3. (Level 3)** Give a whole route to the river: Bank
Street, left, straight on, the river, on the left — and
say how long it takes.
**Answer: It's about five minutes away. Turn left on
Bank Street and go straight on until you reach the
river. It's on the left.** (Libya's fullest short route,
Grade 10, Unit 3, Lesson 7.)
