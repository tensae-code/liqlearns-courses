# Video script — Making suggestions

Super-simple voice. Read slow. The learner hears this
before or beside the written lesson.

---

[Picture: two friends, a rainy window]

It is raining. We have nothing to do.
Do not wait. Make a suggestion.
A suggestion is an idea you give.

Let's go fishing.
Let's — and the plain verb.
Let's swim. Let's cook. Let's read.

[Picture: a compass and a map]

Why don't we walk here?
Why don't we — it looks like a question.
It is not a question. It is a suggestion.
We could go to the waterfalls.

[Picture: two friends talking]

How about swimming?
How about — and the verb wears -ing.
How about fishing? How about a game?

[Picture: the two answer faces]

Now answer.
Yes? That's a good idea!
It sounds great! I'd love to!

No? Say it kindly.
I don't feel like it.
I don't think so. I'd rather stay home.

Now you try.
Suggest one thing to me.
I will answer. Then you answer mine.
Let's start.
