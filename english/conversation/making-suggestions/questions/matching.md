# Making suggestions — matching

Match each suggestion to its best answer.

**Set 1 (Level 1–3)**
1. Let's go fishing near the river. — **That's a good idea!**
2. Why don't we walk to the waterfalls? — **I'd love to.**
3. How about swimming? — **I don't feel like it.**
4. We could take the bus. — **I don't think so. I'd rather walk.**

(The suggestions are Cambodia's fishing line, Grade 10,
printed p.93; Syria's walk suggestions, Starters 5, Unit 3;
and Mongolia's form list, English VII, p.26 — the answers
are the glad and kind replies those same books teach.)
