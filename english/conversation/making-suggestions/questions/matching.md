# Making suggestions — matching

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1–3 set) -> split row by row by the suggestion's shape: Sub lesson 1 — Let's, the shortest suggestion · Sub lesson 2 — Why don't we, the question-shaped suggestion · Sub lesson 3 — How about, the -ing suggestion.

Match each suggestion to its best answer.

**Set 1 (Sub lessons 1, 2 and 3)**
**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Let's go fishing near the river. — **That's a good idea!**
**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why don't we walk to the waterfalls? — **I'd love to.**
**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How about swimming? — **I don't feel like it.**
**4. (Sub lesson 2)** We could take the bus. — **I don't think so. I'd rather walk.**

printed p.93; Syria's walk suggestions, Starters 5, Unit 3;
and Mongolia's form list, English VII, p.26 — the answers
