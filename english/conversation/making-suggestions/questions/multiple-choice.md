# Making suggestions — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Let's, the shortest suggestion, except item 2 (the glad answer), which is Sub lesson 4 — Answering a suggestion, glad or kind · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Why don't we, the question-shaped suggestion · Sub lesson 3 — How about, the -ing suggestion, split item by item by shape · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 4.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The afternoon is free. You suggest a walk:
a) Let's go for a walk.  b) Let's going for a walk.  c) Let's to go for a walk.
**Answer: a) Let's go for a walk.** — after Let's, the verb stays plain (Cambodia, Grade 10, Unit 17; Mongolia, English VII, p.26).

**2. (Sub lesson 4)** Your friend says, "Let's go fishing." You like the idea:
a) I don't feel like it.  b) That's a good idea!  c) I don't think so.
**Answer: b) That's a good idea!** — Cambodia's glad yes (Grade 10, Unit 17, pp.93–98).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which suggestion is right?
a) How about swim?  b) How about swimming?  c) How about to swim?
**Answer: b) How about swimming?** — after How about, the verb wears -ing (Mongolia, English VII, p.26).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** "Why don't we…?" is really —
a) a question asking for a reason  b) a suggestion  c) an order
**Answer: b) a suggestion** — it wears a question's coat but suggests (Syria, Starters 5, Unit 3, pp.11–13).

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** Your friend suggests swimming, but you are tired. The kind no is:
a) No!  b) I don't feel like it. How about a walk?  c) Swimming is bad.
**Answer: b) I don't feel like it. How about a walk?** — refuse the idea kindly, then suggest your own (Cambodia, Grade 10, Unit 17; Mongolia, English VII, p.26).
