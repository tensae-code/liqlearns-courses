# Making suggestions — true or false

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Level 1)** After "Let's", the verb wears -ing.
**False** — after Let's the verb stays plain ("Let's go"); -ing rides after How about (Mongolia, English VII, p.26).

**2. (Level 2)** "Why don't we…?" asks the other person for a reason.
**False** — it is a suggestion in a question's coat (Syria, Starters 5, Unit 3; Cambodia, Grade 10, Unit 17).

**3. (Level 3)** "I don't feel like it" refuses the person, not the idea.
**False** — it refuses the IDEA kindly; the books teach it as the polite refusal (Cambodia, Grade 10, Unit 17, pp.93–98).
