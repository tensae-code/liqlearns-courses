# Making suggestions — fill in the blank

Fill each gap with the right word or shape.

**1. (Level 1)** "______ go to the river." (the shortest suggestion)
**Answer: Let's** — Let's + the plain verb.

**2. (Level 2)** "Why ______ we visit the waterfalls?" (Syria's walk suggestion)
**Answer: don't** — "Why don't we…?" (Syria, Starters 5, Unit 3, pp.11–13).

**3. (Level 2)** "How about ______ (play) a game?"
**Answer: playing** — How about carries the -ing form (Mongolia, English VII, p.26).

**4. (Level 3)** "We ______ go by bus." (Syria's third suggestion shape)
**Answer: could** — "We/You could…" stands in Syria's Study Box beside Let's and Why don't we (Starters 5, Unit 3).
