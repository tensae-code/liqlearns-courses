# Making suggestions — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. Fill each gap with the right word or shape.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** "______ go to the river." (the shortest suggestion)
**Answer: Let's** — Let's + the plain verb.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** "Why ______ we visit the waterfalls?" (Syria's walk suggestion)

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** "How about ______ (play) a game?"
**Answer: playing** — How about carries the -ing form (Mongolia, English VII, p.26).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** "We ______ go by bus." (Syria's third suggestion shape)
