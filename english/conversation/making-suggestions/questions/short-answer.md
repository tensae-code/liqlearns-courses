# Making suggestions — short answer

Answer in whole sentences.

**1. (Level 1)** Suggest going fishing, the way Cambodia's book does.
**Answer: Let's go fishing.** (Cambodia, Grade 10, Unit 17A — "Let's go fishing near the river," printed p.93.)

**2. (Level 2)** Suggest a visit to the waterfalls as a question-shaped suggestion.
**Answer: Why don't we visit the waterfalls?** (Syria, Starters 5, Unit 3 — the walk-planning dialogue.)

**3. (Level 3)** Your companion suggests a long run. You would rather rest. Refuse kindly and suggest something else.
**Answer: I'd rather rest. How about reading together?** (Any kind no with "I'd rather…" plus a new How about suggestion earns it — Mongolia, English VII, p.26.)
