# Making suggestions — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Let's, the shortest suggestion · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Why don't we, the question-shaped suggestion · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 4 — Answering a suggestion, glad or kind.

Answer in whole sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Suggest going fishing, the way Cambodia's book does.
**Answer: Let's go fishing.** (Cambodia, Grade 10, Unit 17A — "Let's go fishing near the river," printed p.93.)

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Suggest a visit to the waterfalls as a question-shaped suggestion.
**Answer: Why don't we visit the waterfalls?** (Syria, Starters 5, Unit 3 — the walk-planning dialogue.)

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** Your companion suggests a long run. You would rather rest. Refuse kindly and suggest something else.
**Answer: I'd rather rest. How about reading together?** (Any kind no with "I'd rather…" plus a new How about suggestion earns it — Mongolia, English VII, p.26.)
