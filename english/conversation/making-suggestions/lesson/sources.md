**Acceptance count: taught in 3 countries (Cambodia,
Mongolia, Syria).** Syria teaches the three shapes first in
its York Press Grade 5 course, inside a walk planned from a
forecast and a compass, and carries the exponents again in
Grade 6; Cambodia's Grade 10 book names a whole unit for
"Why don't we…?" and teaches the glad yes and the kind no
beside it; Mongolia's Grade 7 book lists all three forms on
one page with their approving and rejecting answers. The
plan also named Mongolia Grade 8 Unit 8 for this lesson;

teaches quantifiers, some/any and might/may/could with
