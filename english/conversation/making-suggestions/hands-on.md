# Hands-on — Making suggestions

You do it, we guide you. Everything here is done in the learner's
real day, with a companion or the Explain Companion.

1. **Plan a real walk, the Syria way.** Syria's Starters 5 class
   plans a walk with a weather forecast and a compass. Do the
   same: look at today's weather, pick a direction together, and
   make every plan a suggestion — "Let's go north." "Why don't
   we take the river path?" "How about stopping at the bridge?"
   Then walk it.
2. **The suggestion chain.** Sit in a circle (two is enough).
   Person one suggests: "Let's…" Person two answers glad or
   kind, then adds a new suggestion with a DIFFERENT shape:
   "Why don't we…?" Person three answers and adds "How about…?"
   A shape may not repeat until all three have been used.
3. **The kind-no practice.** Your companion makes five
   suggestions you truly do not want. Refuse every one kindly —
   "I don't feel like it," "I don't think so," "I'd rather…" —
   and after each refusal, offer your own suggestion instead.
   A kind no always leaves a door open.
4. **Weekend poster.** Write the weekend as three suggestions
   on one page — one Let's, one Why don't we, one How about —
   and put it where your family or friends will see it. Count
   the glad answers it collects.
