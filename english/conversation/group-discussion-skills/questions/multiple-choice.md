# Multiple choice — Group Discussion Skills

1. Which of these is one of the ground rules of a good group? (Sub lesson 1)
   - A. One person does all the work
   - B. Every member shares the work
   - C. The loudest voice decides
   - D. Ideas are kept secret from the group
   Answer: B

2. What does keeping confidences mean in a group? (Sub lesson 1)
   - A. Never speaking in the group
   - B. What is said inside the group stays inside the group
   - C. Only the leader may speak
   - D. Writing everything down
   Answer: B

3. What is the FIRST phase of solving a problem in a group? (Sub lesson 2)
   - A. Decide straight away
   - B. Examine the pros and cons
   - C. Identify the possibilities
   - D. Ask the teacher
   Answer: C

4. What can humour do in a group? (Sub lesson 2)
   - A. End the discussion
   - B. Tell a manager something needs fixing without direct criticism
   - C. Replace all decisions
   - D. Make one member the leader
   Answer: B
