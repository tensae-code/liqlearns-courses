# Matching — Group Discussion Skills

Match each phase or rule to what it means.

1. Identify the possibilities (Sub lesson 2) — list every option before judging any of them
2. Examine the pros and cons (Sub lesson 2) — weigh the good and bad points of each option
3. Decide (Sub lesson 2) — choose, once the weighing is done
4. Encourage (Sub lesson 1) — help other members speak and try their ideas
5. Keep confidences (Sub lesson 1) — what is said in the group stays in the group
