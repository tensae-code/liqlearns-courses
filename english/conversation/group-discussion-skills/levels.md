# Levels

Built by the Samoa expansion merge, 2026-11-11. Depth by sub lesson, from Sāmoa's books only at build time.

## Sub lesson 1 — How a Good Group Works
- Depth: the ground rules a group agrees before it starts — every member shares the work, listens to others, respects their ideas, encourages them and keeps the group's confidences (Samoa, *English Year 9 Book 1*, printed pp.8–9); saying your own ideas clearly and building on what others say (printed pp.10–11).

## Sub lesson 2 — Solving Problems and Speaking Up in a Group
- Depth: the three phases of group problem-solving — identify the possibilities, examine the pros and cons of each, then decide — worked through a venue-choice example (Samoa, *English Year 13*, printed pp.59–61); humour as a group tool: telling a manager something needs fixing without direct criticism, and softening criticism between equals (printed pp.62–63); and how to step in, politely and firmly, when a discussion goes wrong (Year 9 Book 1, printed pp.10–11).
