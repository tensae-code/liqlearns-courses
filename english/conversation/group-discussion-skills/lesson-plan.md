# Group Discussion Skills — lesson plan

**Unit:** Unit 5, Conversation

Slots are written as described picture/video slots: the words say what the picture shows, then what the student does with it.

- **Sub lesson 1:** A picture of four learners sitting in a circle around a table, each holding a different part of one job — one reads the task aloud, one writes, one gathers materials, one watches the clock. *The learners look at the picture, then say the four jobs aloud and point to who does each one.*
- **Sub lesson 1:** A picture of two panels: in the first, one learner talks while the others look away; in the second, the same learners face each other and one holds up a hand to ask to speak. *The learners pick the panel that shows good group work and say one reason why.*
- **Sub lesson 2:** A picture of three signposts in a row on one road: the first says THINK OF CHOICES, the second GOOD POINTS AND BAD POINTS, the third DECIDE. *The learners read the signs in order and say which step a group is on in a short spoken example.*
- **Sub lesson 2:** A picture of a learner smiling and pointing at a broken tap with a speech bubble that says, in a joking way, that the tap has been on holiday all week. *The learners say why the joke makes the problem easier to raise than a direct complaint.*
