# Hands-on — Group Discussion Skills

- **Ground-rules card.** Before any group task this week, the group writes its five ground rules on one card and keeps it on the table: share the work, listen, respect ideas, encourage, keep confidences.
- **Three-phase problem.** Pick a real choice the group faces. List every possibility first (no judging), then the pros and cons of each, then decide — and note which phase was hardest to keep in order.
- **Kind words audit.** For one discussion, each member tallies how many times they built on someone else's idea. Compare tallies at the end.
