# Video script — Group Discussion Skills

Built by the Samoa expansion merge, 2026-11-11. Short clips, one per sub lesson; the presenter works with a small group on screen.

## Sub lesson 1 — How a Good Group Works
- Beat 1: Four learners around a table; one talks, the others look away. Freeze. The presenter asks: is this group working?
- Beat 2: The same group, re-run properly: jobs shared, eyes up, ideas welcomed. The presenter names each ground rule as it appears: share the work, listen, respect ideas, encourage, keep confidences.
- Beat 3: One learner disagrees — with the idea, not the person. The presenter points out the difference.

## Sub lesson 2 — Solving Problems and Speaking Up in a Group
- Beat 1: The group must choose a venue. Three signposts appear one by one: THINK OF CHOICES — GOOD POINTS AND BAD POINTS — DECIDE.
- Beat 2: A broken tap. A learner raises it with a joke instead of a complaint; the manager laughs and writes it down. The presenter names what the humour did.
