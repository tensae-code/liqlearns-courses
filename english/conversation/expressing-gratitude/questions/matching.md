## Matching

Match each thank-you to its strength. (Sub lessons 1–4)

1. "Thanks." — a
2. "Thank you for the book." — b
3. "I'm grateful to you for housing me." — c
4. "That's really kind of you." — d
5. A written letter naming the gift — e

a) the plain, small thanks
b) thanks with the gift named
c) the strongest thanks, for a big kindness
d) praise for the kindness itself
e) thanks in writing

**Answer key with reasons:** 1–a (plain), 2–b (for + gift), 3–c (grateful, the weighted form), 4–d (kind praises the giver), 5–e (the letter).
