## True or false

1. "Thanks for the tablet" names the gift. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: True** — for + the gift is the naming step.

2. "Grateful" is a smaller word than "thanks". (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: False** — grateful is the bigger, weighted word.

3. A thank-you letter can be short. (Sub lesson 4)
   **Answer: True** — named and warm matters, not length.

4. "Thank you for helping" uses -ing because helping is a doing. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: True** — for + -ing for doings.

5. Bare "thank you" is the last step of this lesson. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: False** — it is only the first step; naming and grateful follow.
