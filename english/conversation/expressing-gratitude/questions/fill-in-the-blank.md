## Fill in the blank

1. "___ you very much." (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: Thank** — thank you — the base form.

2. "Thanks ___ helping me." (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: for** — thanks for + -ing.

3. "Thank you for ___ book." (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: the** — the gift named: the book.

4. "I'm ___ to you for the tablet." (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: grateful** — I'm grateful to you for…

5. "That's really ___ of you." (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: kind** — kind praises the giver.

6. A thank-you letter opens: "___ Aunty…" (Sub lesson 4)
   **Answer: Dear** — letters open with Dear.
