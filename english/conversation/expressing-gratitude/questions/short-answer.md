## Short answer

1. Thank me plainly for this lesson. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: "Thank you."** — the plain form.

2. Thank a friend for a book, naming the gift. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: "Thank you for the book." / "Thanks for the book."** — for + the gift named.

3. Thank your teacher for explaining, naming the doing. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: "Thank you for explaining."** — for + -ing for a doing.

4. Say the strongest thanks to a family that housed you. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: "I am really grateful to you."** — grateful = the bigger thanks.

5. Name the four parts of a thank-you letter. (Sub lesson 4)
   **Answer: "The person's name; the thanks with the gift named; what the gift did for you; a warm closing."** — the letter's shape.
