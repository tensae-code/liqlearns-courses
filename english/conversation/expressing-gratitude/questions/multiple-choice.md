## Multiple choice

1. A friend lends you a pen. The plain thanks is: (Sub lesson 1)
   a) "I'm grateful for the lesson."
   b) "Thank you."
   c) "That's really kind."
   **Answer: b) "Thank you."** — the plain form fits a small kindness.

2. "Thanks ___ the tablet!" (Sub lesson 2)
   a) to
   b) for
   c) from
   **Answer: b) for** — thanks for + the gift.

3. "Thank you for ___ me." (Sub lesson 2)
   a) help
   b) helping
   c) helps
   **Answer: b) helping** — for + -ing when the gift is a doing.

4. The stronger, bigger thanks is: (Sub lesson 3)
   a) "Thanks."
   b) "I'm grateful to you for this."
   c) "Thank you."
   **Answer: b) "I'm grateful to you for this."** — grateful is the weighted form.

5. Someone gives you a big gift. A warm answer to the kindness is: (Sub lesson 3)
   a) "That's really kind of you."
   b) "Thanks for nothing."
   c) "I beg your pardon."
   **Answer: a) "That's really kind of you."** — it praises the kindness itself.

6. A thank-you letter must: (Sub lesson 4)
   a) be long
   b) name the person and the gift
   c) use no names
   **Answer: b) name the person and the gift** — named and warm is the letter's art.
