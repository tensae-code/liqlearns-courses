# Video script — Expressing Gratitude

Super-simple voice. The presenter is the LiqLearns mascot, front-facing,
on screen the whole video; the pictures named here appear beside him.

[The mascot receives a small gift box, beaming.] "THANK YOU!"

[The mascot holds a tablet.] "Thank you FOR the tablet!"

[The mascot puts both paws on his heart.] "I'm GRATEFUL to you! That's really KIND of you!"

[The mascot writes a letter and holds it up.] "And we can WRITE our thanks too!"
