# Hands-on — Expressing Gratitude

## The kindness wall

For one week the class pins small paper notes on a wall: each note records a kindness done in class. At the week's end, every learner picks one note — not their own — and delivers the full thanks aloud: plain, named, and (if the kindness was big) grateful.

## Letter writing

Real paper, real letters: each learner writes the thank-you letter from sub lesson 4 to a person in the school — a teacher, a cook, a guard — and delivers it the same day.
