# Video script — Giving advice

Super-simple voice. Read slow. The learner hears this
before or beside the written lesson.

---

Your friend has a problem.
His tooth hurts.
What can you say?

Soft words first.
How about seeing a dentist?
Why don't you rest today?

Stronger words.
You should go to a dentist.
You should drink water. You shouldn't stay up late.

Now stand in his shoes.
If I were you, I would go today.
If I were in your shoes, I would call the doctor.

One shape has a warning inside.
You'd better wash it first. It might be dirty.
You'd better not eat it. It can make you ill.

Soft. Strong. Strongest.
Pick the right one for the problem.

Now you try.
Give me advice.
My tooth hurts. I am tired. I have no money.
Soft, strong, strongest — one of each.
