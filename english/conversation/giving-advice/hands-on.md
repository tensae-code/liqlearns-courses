# Hands-on — Giving advice

You do it, we guide you. Everything here is done with a
companion or the Explain Companion and a set of problem
cards you write yourselves.

1. **The problem cards.** Write six small problems: a
   toothache, no money, a dirty spinach leaf, a test
   tomorrow, a lost bag, a friend who is sad. Draw one —
   your companion must advise you before you count to
   ten. Swap.
2. **One problem, three strengths.** Take the toothache.
   Advise it three ways in a row: gentle (How about…?),
   middle (You should…), warning (You'd better…). Say
   which one you would really use for a friend, and why.
3. **The shoes walk.** Malaysia's task, live: four problem
   situations on paper. For each one, answer first with
   "You should…", then again standing in their shoes:
   "If I were in your shoes, I would…" Hear how the
   second one feels kinder.
4. **The advice leaflet.** Libya's Grade 12 writing task:
   make a one-page leaflet of advice for one real thing —
   saving water, safe roads, or not smoking. Five lines,
   at least two rungs of the strength ladder, one of them
   "It's best to…".
