# Giving advice — fill in the blank

Fill each gap with the right words.

**1. (Level 1)** "______ don't you rest today?"
**Answer: Why** — Why don't you…? is Nepal's gentle
shape (Grade 7, Unit 5).

**2. (Level 1)** "______ about seeing a doctor?"
**Answer: How** — How about…? (Malaysia, Form 1,
Chapter 12; Nepal, Grade 7, Unit 5).

**3. (Level 2)** "It's a good ______ to drink water."
**Answer: idea** — from Libya's strength box: "It's a
good idea to …" (Grade 12, Unit 3, Lesson 6).

**4. (Level 3)** "You'd ______ add a tin of tomatoes —
that'll make it less salty."
**Answer: better** — Libya's cooking dialogue (Grade 11,
Unit 5, Lesson 4).
