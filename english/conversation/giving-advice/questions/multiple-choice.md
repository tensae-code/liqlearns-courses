# Giving advice — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** Your friend is tired. The gentle advice
is —
a) How about resting today?  b) You'd better not sleep
c) Don't be tired
**Answer: a)** — How about…? is the gentle question shape
(Nepal, Grade 7, Unit 5; Malaysia, Form 1, Chapter 12).

**2. (Level 1)** "What ______ I do?" — you are asking for
advice.
a) should  b) did yesterday  c) is a dentist
**Answer: a) should** — the door into advice (Albania,
Chapter 35).

**3. (Level 2)** "If I were you, I ______ see a doctor."
a) will yesterday  b) would  c) am
**Answer: b) would** — the shoes shape finishes with
would (Nepal, Grade 7, Unit 5; Libya, Grade 11, Unit 5).

**4. (Level 3)** The spinach may be dirty. The warning
advice is —
a) You'd better wash it first  b) How about the spinach
is blue  c) It is a good idea to dirty it
**Answer: a)** — Libya's own line: "You'd better wash it
first. It might be dirty." (Grade 11, Unit 5, Lesson 4).
