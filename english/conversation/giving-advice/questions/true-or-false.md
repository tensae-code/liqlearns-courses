# Giving advice — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 2 — The gentle shapes, How about and Why don't you · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 5 — Libya's strength scale · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 6 — had better, advice with a warning inside.

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** "How about…?" gives advice as a gentle
question.
**True** — it is one of Nepal's gathered suggestion
forms (Grade 7, Unit 5).

**2. (Sub lesson 5)** In Libya's strength box, a bare "Don't…"
is the gentlest advice of all.
**False** — Don't… is a command, the top of the ladder;
the gentle offer is "It's a good idea to…" (Libya,
Grade 12, Unit 3, Lesson 6).

**3. (Sub lesson 6)** had better only ever gives kind advice,
never an order or a threat.
**False** — India's Beehive sorts had better into three
jobs: advice, order and threat; the voice tells you
which (Class 9, Unit 7).
