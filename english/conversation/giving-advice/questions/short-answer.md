# Giving advice — short answer

Answer in whole sentences.

**1. (Level 1)** Your friend's tooth hurts. Give the
middle-strength advice, Albania's way.
**Answer: You should go to a dentist.** (Albania,
Chapter 34: "You should go to a dentist if your tooth
hurts.")

**2. (Level 2)** Give advice about saving money, standing
in your friend's shoes, Malaysia's way.
**Answer: If I were in your shoes, I would …** (Any true
saving action in that shape earns it; Form 1, Chapter 12.)

**3. (Level 3)** Your brother will add yoghurt to the
stew and make it white. Warn him not to, with had better.
**Answer: You'd better not add the yoghurt.** (Libya's
not-form, Grade 11, Unit 5, Lesson 4.)
