# Giving advice — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 3 — should, the middle rung (item 1 asks for the middle-strength advice) · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 4 — If I were you, the shoes shape · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 6 — had better, advice with a warning inside.

Answer in whole sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 3)** Your friend's tooth hurts. Give the
middle-strength advice, Albania's way.
**Answer: You should go to a dentist.** (Albania,
Chapter 34: "You should go to a dentist if your tooth
hurts.")

**2. (Sub lesson 4)** Give advice about saving money, standing
in your friend's shoes, Malaysia's way.
**Answer: If I were in your shoes, I would …** (Any true
saving action in that shape earns it; Form 1, Chapter 12.)

**3. (Sub lesson 6)** Your brother will add yoghurt to the
stew and make it white. Warn him not to, with had better.
**Answer: You'd better not add the yoghurt.** (Libya's
not-form, Grade 11, Unit 5, Lesson 4.)
