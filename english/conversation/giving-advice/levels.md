# Giving advice — questions by level

Practice by level, tied to the skill number. The questions
live in the type files; this file is the by-level view. The
levels are the depths inside this one thing — advice at
the right strength — set by the pattern across the
countries' books: the gentle shapes first (Nepal and
Malaysia open with them), then should and the shoes
shape, then the strength scale and had better.

## Level 1 — the gentle shapes
Level 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: ask
"What should I do?" and answer with should and
How about…? / Why don't you…? without a miss.

## Level 2 — the shoes and the scale
Level 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: give
"If I were you, I would…" advice whole, and place advice
lines on Libya's strength scale in the right order —
gentle idea at the bottom, Don't… at the top.

## Level 3 — had better
Level 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: choose
had better exactly where a warning lives inside the
advice ("You'd better wash it first — it might be
dirty"), say its not-form ("You'd better not"), and tell
advice from order and threat the way India's book sorts
them.
