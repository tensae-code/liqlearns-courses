# Video script — Telephoning

Super-simple voice. Read slow. The learner hears this
before or beside the written lesson.

---

The phone is ringing.
Pick it up.
Say: Hello. This is Ana speaking.

Someone wants to talk.
Can I speak to Mr Manou, please?
Yes. Hold on, please.
I will put you through.

Sometimes the person is not there.
Can I take a message?
Please tell him: Ana called.
My number is five, five, five — two, three, four.
Please call me back.

Sometimes the line is busy.
The line is engaged.
I will call back later.

You can also make an appointment by phone.
Could I make an appointment, please?
I'd like to come on Tuesday at ten.
Is it possible to come earlier?

Now the call ends.
Thank you for calling.
Goodbye.

Now you try.
Ring. Answer. Ask. Take a message.
You can do a whole call.
