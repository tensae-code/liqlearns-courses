# Telephoning — true or false

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Level 1)** "I'll put you through" means "I will
connect you to the person."
**True** — put through is in Greece's Unit 2 telephone
strand.

**2. (Level 2)** A good message needs only the caller's
name.
**False** — it needs the name, the number and what to do
(call back); a name alone cannot be answered (Malaysia,
Chapter 5; Greece, Unit 2).

**3. (Level 3)** If the line is engaged, you shout louder
until the person hears you.
**False** — an engaged line means the person is on
another call; you hang up and call back (Greece, Unit 2).
