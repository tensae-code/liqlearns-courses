# Telephoning — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 3 — Waiting, hold on and put through (item 1 turns on "I'll put you through") · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 4 — Taking and leaving messages · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 6 — Closing the call.

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 3)** "I'll put you through" means "I will
connect you to the person."
**True** — put through is in Greece's Unit 2 telephone
strand.

**2. (Sub lesson 4)** A good message needs only the caller's
name.
**False** — it needs the name, the number and what to do
(call back); a name alone cannot be answered (Malaysia,
Chapter 5; Greece, Unit 2).

**3. (Sub lesson 6)** If the line is engaged, you shout louder
until the person hears you.
**False** — an engaged line means the person is on
another call; you hang up and call back (Greece, Unit 2).
