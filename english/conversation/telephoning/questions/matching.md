# Telephoning — matching

Match each phone line to the job it does.

**Set 1 (Level 1–2)**
1. Hello, Ana speaking — **answering the phone**
2. Can I speak to Mr Manou, please? — **asking for a
   person**
3. Hold on, please — **asking the caller to wait**
4. Can I take a message? — **offering to take a message**

(The phrasal verbs put through, ring up, cut off and
hang up are Greece's Unit 2 telephone set, practised in
the lesson and the hands-on.)
