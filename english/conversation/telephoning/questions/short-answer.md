# Telephoning — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Answering the call · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 4 — Taking and leaving messages · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 5 — Making an appointment by phone.

Answer in whole sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Answer a call to your house and say who
is speaking.
**Answer: Hello, … speaking.** (Your own name in the
seat; Greece's answering shape, Unit 2.)

**2. (Sub lesson 4)** Your friend calls for your sister. She is
out. Take the message: what three things must it hold?
**Answer: Who called, the caller's number, and what to
do — please call back.** (Malaysia's message-taking,
Chapter 5; Greece, Unit 2.)

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** Leave a message for a teacher who is out:
say who you are and ask for a call back.
**Answer: Please tell him/her … called. Please call me
back.** (Any true message in that shape earns it.)

**4. (Sub lesson 5)** The clinic says Tuesday is full and
offers Wednesday at ten. Reply politely and agree.
**Answer: Yes, that's fine. Wednesday at ten. Thank
you.** (Albania, Chapter 34 — responding to appointment
changes politely.)
