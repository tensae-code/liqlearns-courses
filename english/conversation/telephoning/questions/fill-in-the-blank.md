# Telephoning — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> split item by item: the waiting line is Sub lesson 3 — Waiting, hold on and put through, and the ask is Sub lesson 2 — Asking for a person · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 4 — Taking and leaving messages · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 5 — Making an appointment by phone.

Fill each gap with the right words.

**1. (Sub lesson 3)** "Hold ____, please. I'll put you
through."
**Answer: on** — Greece's waiting line, Unit 2.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** "Can I ____ to Sara, please?"
**Answer: speak** — the ask for a person.

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** "Please ____ me back when you are free."
**Answer: call** — call back is one of Greece's Unit 2
telephone phrasal verbs.

**4. (Sub lesson 5)** "____ I make an appointment, please?
____ like to come on Tuesday. Is it ____ to come
earlier?"
**Answer: Could / I'd / possible** — Albania's polite
appointment ladder, Chapter 34.
