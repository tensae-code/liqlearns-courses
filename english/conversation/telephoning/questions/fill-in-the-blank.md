# Telephoning — fill in the blank

Fill each gap with the right words.

**1. (Level 1)** "Hold ____, please. I'll put you
through."
**Answer: on** — Greece's waiting line, Unit 2.

**2. (Level 1)** "Can I ____ to Sara, please?"
**Answer: speak** — the ask for a person.

**3. (Level 2)** "Please ____ me back when you are free."
**Answer: call** — call back is one of Greece's Unit 2
telephone phrasal verbs.

**4. (Level 3)** "____ I make an appointment, please?
____ like to come on Tuesday. Is it ____ to come
earlier?"
**Answer: Could / I'd / possible** — Albania's polite
appointment ladder, Chapter 34.
