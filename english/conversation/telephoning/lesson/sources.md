**Acceptance count: taught in 5 countries (Greece,
Uzbekistan, Albania, Barbados, Malaysia).** Greece teaches
telephoning whole, as a trade skill: its Hotel and Tourist
Trade book gives the call a full unit — the reservation
call itself, the telephone strand (answering, asking for a
person, put through, engaged, messages, call back) and the
telephone nouns and phrasal verbs. Uzbekistan teaches the
telephone inside its communication unit — the phone's
parts, telephone expressions, and a review that
unscrambles whole phone conversations. Albania teaches
the appointment half of the call — by phone, in person or
by text — with the polite ladder Could I…? / I'd like
to… / Is it possible to…?, from its teacher's guide (the
student side is a digital platform, as filed). Barbados
stocks the telephone at syllabus level only — using the
telephone as an early speaking activity and telephone
directories as early functional reading — and is counted
as syllabus evidence, stated plainly. Malaysia practises
the social call: a telephone invitation with
message-taking, and a telephone conversation mined for
polite requests. Other closed countries join as their
books are read (see READING-QUEUE.md).
