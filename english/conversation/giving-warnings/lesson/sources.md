**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (Eritrea).**

This lesson exists because of the 2026-11-10 expansion read of Eritrea's English books: the read found giving warnings taught as a speaking function in the Grade 7 book's unit on people and manners, with no lesson in the course for the function; its diff marks the topic "NEW LESSON NEEDED: giving warnings". The lesson's founding evidence is that one unit's warnings strand, and it is stated as such: the rest of the unit (apology letters, the adverbs) is stocked in apologising and adverbs. Warning language also appears inside safety-rules (from the Grade 3 and 4 books), where it serves the safety topic; this lesson teaches the function itself. Other countries join as their books are read.
