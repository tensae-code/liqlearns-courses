# Video script — Giving Warnings

Super-simple voice. The mascot is front-facing on screen the whole video, talking to the learner. Bracketed lines are visual cues for the maker, not spoken lines.

[A cartoon friend steps backwards towards a step, not looking.]

"Watch out! Be careful! … Did he hear in time? A warning must be FAST."

[The mascot shakes its head at a drawing of a child running with scissors.] "Don't run with scissors! Never!"

"And if there is time, say why: Don't run — or you will fall and cut yourself. A warning keeps a friend safe."
