## Short answer

1. Give two shapes of a quick warning. (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: any two of: "Be careful!", "Watch out!", "Don't …!", "Never …!".**

2. Turn this into a warning with its reason: "The knife is sharp." (Sub lesson 2) **Answer: any true warning carrying the consequence, e.g. "Be careful with that knife, or you will cut yourself."**

3. Why does the fuller warning say what will happen? (Sub lesson 2) **Answer: so the hearer learns the why, judges for himself, and next time warns himself.**
