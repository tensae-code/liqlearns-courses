## Matching

Match each danger to the warning that fits it. (Sub lesson 1 and 2)

1. a friend stepping backwards off a step
2. a child reaching for a hot pot
3. an open gate and goats inside
4. a slippery river bank

a) Don't touch it — it will burn you!
b) Watch out — there is a step behind you!
c) Don't play there — the bank is slippery and you will fall in.
d) Shut the gate, or the goats will get out.

**Answers: 1-b, 2-a, 3-d, 4-c.**
