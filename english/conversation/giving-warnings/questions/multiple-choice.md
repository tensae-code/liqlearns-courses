## Multiple choice

1. A warning's job is to… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) tell a long story  b) reach someone in time, before the danger does  c) ask a question
   **Answer: b).**

2. Which is a quick warning? (Sub lesson 1)
   a) "Watch out!"  b) "Yesterday I saw a pot."  c) "What time is it?"
   **Answer: a).**

3. The stronger warning carries… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) a song  b) its reason — what will happen  c) a list of names
   **Answer: b).**

4. "Don't leave the gate open, ______ the goats will get out." (Sub lesson 2)
   a) or  b) yesterday  c) please yesterday
   **Answer: a).**
