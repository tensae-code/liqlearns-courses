## True or false

1. A quick warning names the safe action, not the danger's whole story. (Sub lesson 1) **True.**

2. A warning is said for the other person, to protect them. (Sub lesson 1) **True.**

3. A warning with its reason can only use the word "because". (Sub lesson 2) **False — the consequence can be carried by or, by if, or by a plain second sentence.**

4. Eritrea's Grade 7 book teaches giving warnings inside its unit on people and manners. (Sub lesson 2) **True.**
