# Hands-on — Giving Warnings

1. **Mime and warn.** One learner mimes walking into a danger (a hot pot, a deep hole, a busy road). The other must warn them before the mime reaches it — quick warning first, then the warning with its reason.

2. **Warning signs.** Draw three warning signs for real places in your school or home, each with its warning written under it in English — quick form on the sign, reason in small letters below.
