# Asking politely — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The casual rung, Can you and Will you · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The formal rung, Could you and Would you mind · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 4 — Choosing the rung.

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** "Can you…?" is rude to say to a friend.
**False** — it is the casual rung, warm and right for friends and family (Cambodia, Grade 8, Unit 8).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** After "Would you mind", the verb stays plain: "mind pass."
**False** — the verb wears -ing: "Would you mind passing the vegetables, please?" (Cambodia, Grade 8, printed p.58).

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** The formal rung is always the right rung, for every hearer.
**False** — the rung must fit the hearer: formal to your little brother sounds like a joke; the skill is choosing (Cambodia's sorting task, Grade 8, Unit 8 and its teacher's guide).
