# Asking politely — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The casual rung, Can you and Will you · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The formal rung, Could you and Would you mind (item 3 asks for the highest-rung form itself).

Answer in whole sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ask your friend to open the window, casually.
**Answer: Can you open the window, please?** (Any casual-rung request with please earns it.)

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Ask your teacher to repeat the question, formally.
**Answer: Could you repeat the question, please?** (The formal rung: Could you…? + please.)

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A stranger at a wedding is holding the water jug. Ask for water at the highest rung.
**Answer: Would you mind passing the water, please?** ("Would you mind…?" + -ing + please — Cambodia, Grade 8, Unit 8.)
