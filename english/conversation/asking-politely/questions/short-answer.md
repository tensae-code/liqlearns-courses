# Asking politely — short answer

Answer in whole sentences.

**1. (Level 1)** Ask your friend to open the window, casually.
**Answer: Can you open the window, please?** (Any casual-rung request with please earns it.)

**2. (Level 2)** Ask your teacher to repeat the question, formally.
**Answer: Could you repeat the question, please?** (The formal rung: Could you…? + please.)

**3. (Level 3)** A stranger at a wedding is holding the water jug. Ask for water at the highest rung.
**Answer: Would you mind passing the water, please?** ("Would you mind…?" + -ing + please — Cambodia, Grade 8, Unit 8.)
