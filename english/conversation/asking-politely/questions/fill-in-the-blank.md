# Asking politely — fill in the blank

Fill each gap with the right word or shape.

**1. (Level 1)** "______ you help me with my homework?" (to a friend)
**Answer: Can** — "Can you…?" is the casual rung (Cambodia, Grade 8, Unit 8).

**2. (Level 2)** "______ you pass me that knife, please?" (the softer rung)
**Answer: Could** — "Could you pass me that knife, please?" (Cambodia, Grade 10, Unit 16).

**3. (Level 2)** "Would you mind ______ (close) the window?"
**Answer: closing** — mind carries the -ing form.

**4. (Level 3)** "Will you…?" stands on the ______ rung, beside "Can you…?".
**Answer: casual** — Cambodia's own notes name can/will casual and could/would/would-you-mind formal (Grade 8, Unit 8).
