# Asking politely — matching

Match each request to its rung.

**Set 1 (Level 1–3)**
1. Can you help me carry this? — **casual rung (a friend)**
2. Will you close the door? — **casual rung (family)**
3. Could you pass me that knife, please? — **formal rung (the table)**
4. Would you mind passing the vegetables, please? — **formal rung (the wedding)**

(The rungs are Cambodia's own distinction — Grade 8,
Unit 8: can/will casual, could/would/would-you-mind
formal; the knife line is Grade 10, Unit 16, and the
vegetables line is the Grade 8 wedding story, printed
p.58.)
