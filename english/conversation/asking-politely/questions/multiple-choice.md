# Asking politely — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** You ask your best friend for help with a bag:
a) Can you help me?  b) Would you mind helping me?  c) Help me at once.
**Answer: a) Can you help me?** — friends stand on the casual rung (Cambodia, Grade 8, Unit 8, pp.56–61).

**2. (Level 1)** The warm request always carries —
a) a loud voice  b) please  c) a reason first
**Answer: b) please** — please in requests, thank you in accepting (Cambodia, Grade 10, Teacher's Guide Book, Unit 16).

**3. (Level 2)** At a wedding, you ask an elder for the vegetables:
a) Pass the vegetables.  b) Can you pass the vegetables?  c) Would you mind passing the vegetables, please?
**Answer: c) Would you mind passing the vegetables, please?** — Cambodia's own formal model, wedding story, printed p.58.

**4. (Level 2)** Which formal request is right?
a) Would you mind open the door?  b) Would you mind opening the door?  c) Would you mind to open the door?
**Answer: b) Would you mind opening the door?** — after mind, the verb wears -ing.

**5. (Level 3)** "Could you pass me that knife, please?" is —
a) an offer  b) a polite request  c) an apology
**Answer: b) a polite request** — Cambodia's Grade 10 unit teaches telling requests from offers on these very lines (Unit 16, pp.87–92).
