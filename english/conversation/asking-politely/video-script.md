# Video script — Asking politely

Super-simple voice. Read slow. The learner hears this
before or beside the written lesson.

---

[Picture: a ladder with two rungs]

Asking is climbing.
Low rung — for friends.
High rung — for teachers and strangers.
Same ask. Different words.

Can you help me?
That is the low rung. For a friend. It is fine.

[Picture: a teacher's desk]

Now the teacher. Climb.
Could you help me, please?
Would you help me, please?
Would you mind helping me?

Mind — and the verb wears -ing.
Would you mind passing the vegetables, please?

[Picture: the word PLEASE landing]

And please. Do not forget please.
Please sits at the end. Or in the middle.
Could you pass me that knife, please?

Bare ask — rough.
Ask with please — soft.
Ask one rung higher — softer still.

Now you try.
Ask me like a friend: Can you…?
Now ask me like a stranger: Could you…, please?
Feel the ladder under your feet.
