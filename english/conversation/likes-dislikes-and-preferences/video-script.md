# Video script — Likes, Dislikes and Preferences

Super-simple voice. The presenter is the LiqLearns mascot, front-facing,
on screen the whole video; the pictures named here appear beside him.

[The mascot bounces a football.] "I LIKE football! My FAVOURITE!"

[The mascot shakes his head at a rain cloud.] "I DON'T LIKE rain."

[The mascot reaches for a cup.] "I WOULD LIKE tea, please! I FEEL LIKE walking!"

[The mascot stands between a book and a ball, then hugs the book.] "I prefer reading TO watching. I'd RATHER read THAN run!"
