# Likes, Dislikes and Preferences

## Do it first

Two doors: tea or coffee? Football or books? You choose things every day. Try saying one choice now: "I prefer tea." Go on — in this lesson you will learn to say what you like, what you want, and what you would rather do.

## Sub lesson 1 — Likes and dislikes — like, dislike, favourite

Saying tastes starts with two verbs:

- "**I like** football." "I like **reading**." (like + thing, or like + -ing)
- "**I dislike** rain." / stronger and commoner: "**I don't like** rain."
- The top one is your **favourite**: "I like football, but my **favourite** sport is boxing."

Say three now: one like, one dislike, one favourite.

## Sub lesson 2 — Wanting — want, would like, feel like

Wanting has three forms, from plain to polite to feeling:

- "**I want** a rest." "I want **to travel** abroad." (want + thing, or want + to)
- "**I would like** a coke." "I'd like **to go** for a walk." (**would like** — the polite want)
- "**I feel like going** for a walk." (**feel like** + -ing — the wanting that is a mood)

Say one want each way: a thing, a polite ask, a mood.

## Sub lesson 3 — Preferences — prefer X to Y

Choosing between things takes **prefer**:

- "**I prefer** reading books **to** watching videos." (prefer X **to** Y)
- "I prefer **reading** to **watching**." (prefer takes -ing on both sides)
- Ask: "**Do you prefer** tea **or** coffee?"

Choose aloud now: your two school subjects, prefer one to the other.

## Sub lesson 4 — I'd rather — choosing between two

The other choosing form is **'d rather** (would rather):

- "**I'd rather study** at home." ('d rather + base verb — no to)
- "**I'd rather** study **than** rest." ('d rather X **than** Y)
- Ask: "**Would you rather** walk **or** ride?"

Answer me now: would you rather read or run? Use the full form.

## Say it to your companion

Interview your companion: one like, one dislike, one favourite, one want (would like), one prefer X to Y, one 'd rather X than Y. Six sentences. Then swap — and report their six tastes to me in one breath each.

## Do It Yourself

Write eight sentences about yourself: two likes (one + -ing), one dislike, your favourite, one want with would like, one feel like, one prefer X to Y, one 'd rather X than Y. Read them aloud.
