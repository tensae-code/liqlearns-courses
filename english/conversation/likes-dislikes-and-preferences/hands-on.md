# Hands-on — Likes, Dislikes and Preferences

## The choosing game

Two activity cards are held up — swimming or drawing, tea or juice, city or village. Every learner must choose aloud with prefer or 'd rather and give the full two-sided sentence. No pointing allowed: the words do the choosing.

## Class taste chart

A wall chart with three columns — Like / Dislike / Favourite — for foods, sports and subjects. Learners add their names under each with a full sentence beneath; the chart is read aloud at the week's end.
