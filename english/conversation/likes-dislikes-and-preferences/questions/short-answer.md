## Short answer

1. Say one like with -ing and one dislike. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: "I like swimming. I don't like rain."** — like/dislike with the forms.

2. Ask politely for a juice. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: "I would like a juice, please."** — would like = the polite want.

3. Say you feel like resting, with feel like. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: "I feel like resting."** — feel like + -ing.

4. Prefer books to films, in a full sentence. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: "I prefer books to films."** — prefer X to Y.

5. Say you would rather walk than ride. (Sub lesson 4)
   **Answer: "I'd rather walk than ride."** — 'd rather X than Y.
