## True or false

1. "I dislike" and "I don't like" say the same thing. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: True** — dislike = don't like.

2. "I feel like to swim" is correct. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: False** — feel like takes -ing: I feel like swimming.

3. "Prefer X than Y" is correct. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: False** — prefer X to Y; than belongs to 'd rather.

4. "I'd rather to stay" is correct. (Sub lesson 4)
   **Answer: False** — 'd rather + base verb: I'd rather stay.

5. "Would like" is politer than "want". (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: True** — would like is the polite want.

6. "Favourite" names the one you like most. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: True** — the top one.
