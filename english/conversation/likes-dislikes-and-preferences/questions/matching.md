## Matching

Match each sentence to its job. (Sub lessons 1–4)

1. "I like swimming." — a
2. "I don't like rain." — b
3. "I would like a coke." — c
4. "I feel like resting." — d
5. "I prefer tea to coffee." — e
6. "I'd rather walk than ride." — f

a) a dislike
b) a polite want
c) a like
d) a preference with prefer
e) a choice with 'd rather
f) a wanting that is a mood

**Answer key with reasons:** 1–c (like), 2–a (dislike), 3–b (would like), 4–f (feel like — the mood want), 5–d (prefer X to Y), 6–e ('d rather X than Y).
