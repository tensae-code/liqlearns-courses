## Multiple choice

1. "I like ___ books." (Sub lesson 1)
   a) read
   b) reading
   c) to reading
   **Answer: b) reading** — like + -ing names the doing.

2. "I ___ like a coke, please." (the polite want) (Sub lesson 2)
   a) want
   b) would
   c) feel
   **Answer: b) would** — would like is the polite want.

3. "I feel like ___ for a walk." (Sub lesson 2)
   a) go
   b) going
   c) to go
   **Answer: b) going** — feel like takes -ing.

4. "I prefer reading ___ watching videos." (Sub lesson 3)
   a) than
   b) to
   c) or
   **Answer: b) to** — prefer X to Y.

5. "I'd rather ___ at home." (Sub lesson 4)
   a) study
   b) to study
   c) studying
   **Answer: a) study** — 'd rather + base verb, no to.

6. "I'd rather walk ___ ride." (Sub lesson 4)
   a) to
   b) than
   c) or
   **Answer: b) than** — 'd rather X than Y.

7. "My ___ sport is boxing." (the top one) (Sub lesson 1)
   a) dislike
   b) favourite
   c) rather
   **Answer: b) favourite** — favourite names the top one.

8. Asking a preference: "___ you prefer tea or coffee?" (Sub lesson 3)
   a) Would
   b) Do
   c) Rather
   **Answer: b) Do** — do you prefer…or…? asks the choice.
