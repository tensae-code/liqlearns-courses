## Fill in the blank

1. "I ___ football but my favourite sport is boxing." (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: like** — the book's own frame: like + favourite.

2. "They ___ to travel abroad." (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: want** — want + to-infinitive.

3. "I would ___ a fanta, please." (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: like** — would like — the polite want.

4. "I prefer reading books ___ watching videos." (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: to** — prefer X to Y.

5. "I'd ___ study at home." (Sub lesson 4)
   **Answer: rather** — 'd rather + base verb.

6. "Would you ___ walk or ride?" (Sub lesson 4)
   **Answer: rather** — would you rather…or…?
