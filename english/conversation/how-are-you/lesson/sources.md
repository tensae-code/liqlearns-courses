### Egypt — Primary 3 Term 1 (read 2026-10-25)
Egypt now JOINS this lesson. Unit 1, Lesson 1 (pp.6–9) teaches the
question inside its greeting set — "How are you? — I am fine, thank
you." — and the "Meeting a New Friend" dialog (p.7) runs it both
ways: "How are you?" — "I am fine, thank you. How are you?" — "I'm
fine too, thank you." The choose-the-right-response listening (p.9)
opens with it: "Hello, Sami. How are you?" This changes the count:
**taught in 2 countries (Egypt, Libya).**
