# How are you? — true or false

True-or-false questions only. Answers at the bottom.

## Level 1
1. "I'm not very well" is the answer for a good day. (True / False)
2. "And how are you?" asks the question back to the other person. (True / False)
3. There is only one correct answer to "How are you?" (True / False)

## Answers
1-False (it is the hard-day answer), 2-True, 3-False (the books give three)
