# How are you? — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Asking and answering how are you, except item 3 (the return question), which is Sub lesson 2 — Asking how are you back.

Choice questions only. Grouped by level. Answers at the bottom.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Someone asks you "How are you?" on a normal, all-right day. You say:
   a) I'm fine  b) Goodbye  c) My name is Omar  d) Thank you, too
**2. (Sub lesson 1)** "How are you?" — the answer for a very good day is:
   a) I'm not very well  b) I'm very well, thanks  c) I'm 8  d) Hello
**3. (Sub lesson 2)** After you answer, the polite return question is:
   a) What's your name?  b) How old are you?  c) And how are you?  d) Goodbye?

## Answers
1-a, 2-b, 3-c
