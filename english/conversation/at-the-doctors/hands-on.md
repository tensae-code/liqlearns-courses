# Hands-On — At the Doctor's

1. **Doctor and patient.** Role-play in pairs with a symptom card (fever since yesterday; stomachache since Monday) — run the whole visit.
2. **At reception.** Practise registering as a first-time patient: name, problem, appointment time.
3. **Prescription reading.** Read a mock prescription aloud: medicine, dose, times a day.
4. **Symptom match.** Match symptom cards to probable illnesses, the way the books practise it.
