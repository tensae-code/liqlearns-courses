# Lesson Plan — At the Doctor's

**Unit 5 — Conversation.**

**One thing:** the words and the talk of a medical visit — making an appointment, naming symptoms, understanding the diagnosis, the prescription and the dose.

## Sub lessons

1. **Words for the visit.** *Can-do: The visit words defined: appointment, diagnosis, prescription, dose, medical test — and the symptoms you name first (cold, allergy, food poisoning, cough, runny nose, stomachache, chills, sneeze, diarrhea, fever, headache, dizziness).*
2. **In the consultation.** *Can-do: Run the visit as a dialogue: say your problem and how long ("a bad cough and a fever since yesterday"), answer the doctor's questions (medicines taken, allergies), and take the advice — rest and take the medication.*

(The gate pool for each sub lesson is every question marked with that sub lesson's number in this lesson's `questions/` files — the whole set, because no hardness ranking is written in questions/.)

## Teaching points

- Medical-visit words defined and used in a doctor–patient dialogue (item 243): diagnosis, prescription, dose, appointment, medical test.
- A full consultation modelled (item 334): Mr Smith tells Dr Taylor about a bad cough and a fever since yesterday, an aspirin taken last night, a slight fever and no medication allergies; the advice is rest and medication.
- Symptoms matched to a probable illness (item 242, stocked in health-words) feed this lesson's first sub lesson.
