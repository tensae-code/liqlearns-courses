# Levels — At the Doctor's

| Level | What changes |
|---|---|
| Sub lesson 1 — Words for the visit | First step into the lesson. Can-do: The visit words defined: appointment, diagnosis, prescription, dose, medical test — and the symptoms you name first (cold, allergy, food poisoning, cough, runny nose, stomachache, chills, sneeze, diarrhea, fever, headache, dizziness). |
| Sub lesson 2 — In the consultation | Builds on the sub lessons before it; nothing new is locked behind the earlier work. Can-do: Run the visit as a dialogue: say your problem and how long ("a bad cough and a fever since yesterday"), answer the doctor's questions (medicines taken, allergies), and take the advice — rest and take the medication. |
