## Multiple choice

1. Your friend shows you a drawing and it is very good. What do you say? (Sub lesson 1)
   a) I don't know.  b) Good!  c) Me?
   **Answer: b)** — "Good!", with a thumbs-up.

2. Your friend says, "Let's play outside." The plan sounds good. What do you say? (Sub lesson 1)
   a) Good idea!  b) Come here.  c) I don't know.
   **Answer: a)** — "Good idea!" answers a plan before it is done.

3. Someone asks you a question and you do not have the answer. What do you say? (Sub lesson 1)
   a) Give me five!  b) Good!  c) I don't know.
   **Answer: c)** — "I don't know.", with shoulders up and hands open.

4. The teacher points your way and you ask if she means you. What do you say? (Sub lesson 1)
   a) Me?  b) Come here.  c) Good idea!
   **Answer: a)** — "Me?", pointing at your own chest.

5. You want your friend to come to where you are. What do you say? (Sub lesson 2)
   a) Me?  b) Come here.  c) I don't know.
   **Answer: b)** — "Come here.", with a beckoning hand.

6. You and your friend did something well and want to celebrate with a palm slap. What do you say? (Sub lesson 2)
   a) Good!  b) Come here.  c) Give me five!
   **Answer: c)** — "Give me five!", open palm held up high.
