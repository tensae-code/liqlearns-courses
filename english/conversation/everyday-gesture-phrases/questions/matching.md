## Matching

Match the phrase to its gesture. (Sub lessons 1–2)

1. Good! — d
2. Good idea! — f
3. I don't know. — a
4. Me? — b
5. Come here. — e
6. Give me five! — c

a) Shoulders up, hands open
b) A point at your own chest
c) An open palm held up high
d) Thumbs up
e) A beckoning hand, fingers waving toward you
f) A nod at a plan you like

**Answers:** 1–d (thumbs up), 2–f (a nod at the plan), 3–a (the shrug), 4–b (point at your own chest), 5–e (the beckoning hand), 6–c (the raised open palm).
