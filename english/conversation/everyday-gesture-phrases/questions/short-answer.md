## Short answer

1. Name the six gesture phrases of this lesson. (Sub lessons 1–2)
   **Answer:** Good! · Good idea! · I don't know. · Me? · Come here. · Give me five!

2. What gesture goes with "I don't know.", and why? (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer:** Shoulders up, hands open — the shrug. The gesture is part of the phrase: people read the shoulders before they hear the words.

3. What is the difference between "Good!" and "Good idea!"? (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer:** "Good!" answers a thing that is already done well; "Good idea!" answers a plan before it is done.

4. How do you ask for the celebration palm slap? (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer:** Hold your open palm up high and say "Give me five!"
