## True or false

1. "Good idea!" is what you say when a plan sounds good. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: True.**

2. "I don't know." is said with a thumbs-up. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: False** — it is said with shoulders up and hands open (a shrug); the thumbs-up goes with "Good!"

3. "Me?" is said while pointing at your own chest. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: True.**

4. "Come here." means "go away." (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: False** — it calls someone TO you, with a beckoning hand.

5. "Give me five!" asks for an open-palm slap, five fingers against five fingers. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: True.**
