# Hands-on — Everyday Gesture Phrases

## Gesture charades

In pairs: one partner mimes one of the six gestures without words — a thumbs-up, a shrug, a point at the chest, a beckoning hand, a raised palm. The other says the phrase that goes with it. Then swap. Every phrase must be found from its gesture alone.

## Answer me with a phrase

Your partner shows you things and makes plans: a drawing they finished, an idea for a game, a question you cannot answer, your name called from across the room. Answer each one with the right phrase AND its gesture: "Good!" / "Good idea!" / "I don't know." / "Me?" Then your partner calls you over — "Come here." — and you finish with "Give me five!"
