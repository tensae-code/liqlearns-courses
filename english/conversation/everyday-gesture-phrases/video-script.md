# Video script — Everyday Gesture Phrases

Super-simple voice. The presenter is the LiqLearns mascot, front-facing, on screen the whole video; he does every gesture himself as he says the phrase.

[Thumbs up, big smile.] "GOOD! Say it when something is good. GOOD!"

[One finger raised, nodding.] "GOOD IDEA! Say it when a plan is good. GOOD IDEA!"

[Shoulders up, hands open.] "I DON'T KNOW. Shoulders up! I DON'T KNOW."

[Hand pointing at his own chest, surprised face.] "ME? Point at YOU. ME?"

[Beckoning hand.] "COME HERE. Wave your hand — come to me. COME HERE!"

[Open palm raised high.] "GIVE ME FIVE! Up high — slap! GIVE ME FIVE!"

[Back to the mascot, all six gestures in quick replay.] "Words AND hands together — that is how these little phrases work!"
