# Lesson Plan — Everyday Gesture Phrases

**One thing:** the little phrases you say together with a gesture — the words and the action come as one piece: "Good!" with a thumbs-up, "Come here." with a beckoning hand, "Give me five!" with a raised palm.

## Sub lessons

1. Answering with words and actions — Good! · Good idea! · I don't know. · Me?
2. Calling and celebrating — Come here. · Give me five.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Teaching points

- Each phrase is taught as a pair: the words AND the gesture that goes with them — a shrug for "I don't know.", a beckoning hand for "Come here.", a raised palm for "Give me five."
- "Good!" and "Good idea!" praise: one answers a thing that is done well; the other answers a plan before it is done.
- "Me?" asks "are you talking about me?" — said with a point at your own chest.
- "Give me five!" asks for the celebration slap of open palms — five fingers against five fingers.
