# Levels — Speaking Voice — Volume and Tone

One lesson, two sub lessons. A sub lesson opens by proving the previous one (at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, drawn from the whole question set of the sub lesson just before — no hardness ranking is written in questions/).

| Sub lesson | What the learner can do at the end |
|---|---|
| 1. Volume and tone that fit the moment | Choose a volume and tone that fit where they are speaking — a conversation, a class discussion, a story being dramatised — and hold that volume when speaking to a pair or to the whole class. |
| 2. Speaking with your face and hands | Speak with the appropriate non-verbal gestures: occasional eye contact with the listener, and nodding that fits the talk. |

A learner who finishes both sub lessons can be heard clearly, at the right strength, with a voice and face that match their words.
