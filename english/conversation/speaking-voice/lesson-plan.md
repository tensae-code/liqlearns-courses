# Lesson Plan — Speaking Voice — Volume and Tone

**One thing:** the voice you speak with — using a volume and tone that fit the moment (conversation, class discussion, dramatising a story), and the non-verbal speaking that goes with it: eye contact and gestures like nodding.

## Sub lessons

1. Volume and tone that fit the moment
2. Speaking with your face and hands

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Teaching points

- Speaking at an appropriate volume and tone in conversation and class discussion is a named knowledge/skill (KSD 2.4); the volume and tone are varied on purpose when dramatising stories (NEL Guide, p.32).
- The same volume skill is practised directly: speaking in pairs and to the class at an appropriate volume (worked example "Speaking to My Friends") (p.36).
- Speaking includes non-verbal gestures: occasional eye contact and nodding (KSD 2.5) (p.32).
