# True or False — Speaking Voice — Volume and Tone

(Every item is marked with the sub lesson it belongs to: (Sub lesson N). The gate pool for a sub lesson is all of that sub lesson's questions — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the whole set is the pool.)

1. The right volume for a conversation and the right volume for a class discussion are exactly the same. (Sub lesson 1) — False
2. When dramatising a story, varying your volume and tone is part of the skill. (Sub lesson 1) — True
3. Shouting is the right way to make the class hear you. (Sub lesson 1) — False
4. Nodding is a non-verbal gesture that belongs to speaking. (Sub lesson 2) — True
5. A good speaker never lets their eyes meet the listener's eyes. (Sub lesson 2) — False
6. Your face should match your words when you speak. (Sub lesson 2) — True
