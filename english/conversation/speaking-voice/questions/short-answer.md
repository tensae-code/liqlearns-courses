# Short Answer — Speaking Voice — Volume and Tone

(Every item is marked with the sub lesson it belongs to: (Sub lesson N). The gate pool for a sub lesson is all of that sub lesson's questions — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the whole set is the pool.)

1. Name the two speaking settings KSD 2.4 names for appropriate volume and tone. (Sub lesson 1)
   — Conversation, and class discussion.

2. How should your voice change when you dramatise a story? (Sub lesson 1)
   — Vary the volume and tone on purpose for the characters and moments — e.g. low and big for a giant, small and quick for a mouse.

3. A learner whispers every answer so only the teacher hears. Which part of the skill is missing? (Sub lesson 1)
   — Appropriate volume: in class discussion the voice must reach the whole class (practised in "Speaking to My Friends" — speaking in pairs and to the class at an appropriate volume).

4. Name two non-verbal gestures that belong to speaking. (Sub lesson 2)
   — Occasional eye contact, and nodding.

5. What does "non-verbal" mean in this lesson? (Sub lesson 2)
   — Saying things without words — with the face, the eyes and the hands.
