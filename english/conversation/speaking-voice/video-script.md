# Video Script — Speaking Voice — Volume and Tone

Super simple English. Slow. Smile. The voice in the video changes volume and tone exactly as the words say.

[Picture: two children talking close together, speaking gently]

This is my talking voice. Not loud. Not soft. Just right for my friend, here with me.

[Picture: a child speaking to a whole class, voice strong]

Now I speak to the class! My voice is stronger. Everyone can hear me. But I am not shouting.

[Picture: a storyteller as a giant — low, big voice — then as a mouse — small, quick voice]

In a story, my voice can change! The giant: low and big. The mouse: small and quick. My voice tells the story too.

[Picture: the speaker looking at the listener, nodding, smiling]

And look — my eyes meet yours. I nod. My face matches my words. My whole body speaks!
