# Hands-On — Speaking Voice — Volume and Tone

Things you do with your real voice. You need a partner and a little space.

1. **Three distances.** Say one sentence to your partner standing close. Then step five paces back and say it again. Then ten paces. Adjust your volume each time so they hear you clearly without a shout.
2. **Story voices.** Take one line from a story. Say it as a giant, as a mouse, as a sad friend, as happy news. Vary volume and tone on purpose each time.
3. **Class voice.** Stand at one end of the room and speak to the class end: strong enough to reach the back, not a shout. Your partner at the back gives a thumbs up when the volume is right.
4. **Face and hands.** Tell your partner one piece of news twice — first with eyes down and a still face, then with occasional eye contact, nodding, and a face that matches the news. Your partner says which telling was better to listen to, and why.
