**Acceptance count: taught in 10 countries (Albania, Bolivia,
Malaysia, Nepal, Syria, Mauritania, Nicaragua, Pakistan, Sierra Leone, Venezuela).** Albania — teacher-guide
evidence — stocks the written party invitation, with its
time, date and place, inside its birthday chapter. Syria's York Press Grade 5
course teaches the whole spoken exchange — plan,
invitation, glad acceptance, kind three-part refusal with
reasons on role-cards. Bolivia meets the invitation
dialogue in Year 2, beside the offer dialogue in the same
lesson-part (filed from the ministry compendium that
reproduces the student books). Malaysia's Form 1 book
teaches inviting and requesting formulae in Chapter 2 and
the full telephone invitation — accepting and declining
with set expressions — in Chapter 5. Nepal gives
invitations a whole Grade 8 unit, from the heard party
invitation to the written message, the formal card and
the birthday-card project.
Mauritania invites — and accepts and declines — in its Grade 8
book (stocked 2026-11-09 by the Mauritania re-verification
sweep merge).
