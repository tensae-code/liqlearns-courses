# Inviting — fill in the blank

Fill each gap with the right word.

**1. (Level 1)** "I'm going to the cinema. Would you ______ to come?"
**Answer: like** — "Would you like to come?" (Syria, Starters 5, Unit 21).

**2. (Level 1)** "Yes, please. I'd ______ to." (the glad answer)
**Answer: love** — "I'd love to" says the wish, not a flat okay.

**3. (Level 2)** "I'd like to, but I'm ______ I can't." (the kind refusal)
**Answer: afraid** — "I'm afraid I can't" carries the can't kindly (Syria, Starters 5, Unit 21).

**4. (Level 3)** A formal invitation card and an informal email carry the same four facts: who, what, ______ and where.
**Answer: when** — the facts stay; the dress changes (Nepal, Grade 8, Unit 10).
