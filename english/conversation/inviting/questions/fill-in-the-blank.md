# Inviting — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The invitation, plan first · Sub lesson 2 — Accepting gladly, I'd love to · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 3 — Declining kindly, sorry, wish, can't · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 4 — The invitation on paper, one item per sub lesson in order.

Fill each gap with the right word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** "I'm going to the cinema. Would you ______ to come?"
**Answer: like** — "Would you like to come?" (Syria, Starters 5, Unit 21).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** "Yes, please. I'd ______ to." (the glad answer)
**Answer: love** — "I'd love to" says the wish, not a flat okay.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** "I'd like to, but I'm ______ I can't." (the kind refusal)
**Answer: afraid** — "I'm afraid I can't" carries the can't kindly (Syria, Starters 5, Unit 21).

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** A formal invitation card and an informal email carry the same four facts: who, what, ______ and where.
**Answer: when** — the facts stay; the dress changes (Nepal, Grade 8, Unit 10).
