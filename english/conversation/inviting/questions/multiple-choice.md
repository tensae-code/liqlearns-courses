# Inviting — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** You are going to the cinema. Invite your friend:
a) I'm going to the cinema. Would you like to come?  b) Go to the cinema!  c) The cinema is nice.
**Answer: a) I'm going to the cinema. Would you like to come?** — plan first, then the question (Syria, Starters 5, Unit 21, pp.61–63).

**2. (Level 1)** A friend invites you to a football match, and you are free. The glad answer is:
a) Yes, please. I'd love to.  b) Okay, if I must.  c) Maybe one day.
**Answer: a) Yes, please. I'd love to.** — Syria's Study Box acceptance.

**3. (Level 2)** You cannot come. The kind refusal is:
a) No. I can't.  b) I'm sorry. I'd like to, but I'm afraid I can't.  c) Ask someone else.
**Answer: b) I'm sorry. I'd like to, but I'm afraid I can't.** — sorry + wish + can't (Syria, Starters 5, Unit 21; Malaysia, Form 1, Chapter 5).

**4. (Level 2)** In Malaysia's telephone invitation, Wani invites Min Hui to —
a) a football match  b) a ceremony  c) the cinema
**Answer: b) a ceremony** — the Form 1 Chapter 5 listening (printed pp.51–62).

**5. (Level 3)** Which invitation card is complete?
a) "Party! Come!"  b) "Sara's birthday party — Saturday, 4 pm, at Sara's house. Please come!"  c) "A party, one day, somewhere."
**Answer: b)** — an invitation is its facts: who, what, when, where (Nepal, Grade 8, Unit 10, pp.116–127).
