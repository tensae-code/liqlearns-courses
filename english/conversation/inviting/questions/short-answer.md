# Inviting — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The invitation, plan first · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 3 — Declining kindly, sorry, wish, can't · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 4 — The invitation on paper.

Answer in whole sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Invite your companion to a football match.
**Answer: I'm going to a football match. Would you like to come?** (Syria, Starters 5, Unit 21 — one of its three Study Box plans.)

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** You are invited shopping on the day you must help at home. Decline kindly, with your reason.
**Answer: I'm sorry. I'd like to, but I'm afraid I can't. I must help at home.** (Sorry + wish + can't + reason — Syria's role-card shape.)

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** Write the invitation message for your own birthday party.
**Answer: Please come to my birthday party on Saturday at 4 pm at my house.** (Any message carrying who, what, when and where earns it — Nepal, Grade 8, Unit 10, Writing II.)
