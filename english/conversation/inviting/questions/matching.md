# Inviting — matching

Match each invitation to its best answer.

**Set 1 (Level 1–2)**
1. I'm going to the cinema. Would you like to come? — **Yes, please. I'd love to.**
2. I'm going shopping tomorrow. Would you like to come? — **I'm sorry. I'd like to, but I'm afraid I can't.**
3. Would you like to play volleyball with us? — **Yes, please. I'd love to.**
4. Please come to my birthday party on Saturday. — **Thank you! I'd love to.**

(The plans are Syria's Study Box three — shopping, the
cinema, a football match (Starters 5, Unit 21) — the
volleyball invitation is Bolivia's Year 2 dialogue
(Dagmer invites Maydee), and the birthday line is Nepal's
Grade 8 project in spoken form.)
