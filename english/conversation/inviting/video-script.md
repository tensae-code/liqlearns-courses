# Video script — Inviting

Super-simple voice. Read slow. The learner hears this
before or beside the written lesson.

---

[Picture: a girl with two cinema tickets]

I have a plan. And I want you with me.
That is an invitation.

I'm going to the cinema.
Would you like to come?
Plan first. Then the question.

[Picture: a happy friend]

Yes? Say it gladly.
Yes, please. I'd love to!
Glad and full. Not just "okay."

[Picture: a busy calendar]

No? Say it kindly. Three parts.
I'm sorry.
I'd like to, but I'm afraid I can't.
I must help my mother.
Sorry — wish — reason. All three.

[Picture: an invitation card and a phone]

An invitation can travel on paper too.
A card. A message. An email.
Who? What? When? Where?
The facts stay. The dress changes.

Now you try.
Invite me. To the cinema. To a match. To your party.
I will answer. Then invite me again —
and this time, I cannot come. Answer me kindly.
