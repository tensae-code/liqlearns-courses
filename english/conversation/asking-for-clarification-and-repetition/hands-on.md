# Hands-on — Asking for Clarification and Repetition

## The bad line

Pairs sit back to back with paper phones. One reads a hotel booking slowly but with three planted faults — a whisper, a fast name, an unknown word. The other must use all three repairs to complete the booking form: name spelled, room kind understood, price heard.

## Repair cards

Cards carry a fault each (too quiet / too fast / hard word / new name). The learner draws a card, the teacher commits the fault, and the learner repairs it with the right sentence before the talk may continue.
