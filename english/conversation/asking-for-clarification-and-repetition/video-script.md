# Video script — Asking for Clarification and Repetition

Super-simple voice. The presenter is the LiqLearns mascot, front-facing,
on screen the whole video; the pictures named here appear beside him.

[The mascot holds a phone to his ear, puzzled.] "I CAN'T HEAR YOU! Can you SPEAK UP, please?"

[The mascot writes as letters appear.] "How do you SPELL it? D… I… E… N… G!"

[The mascot points at a word, head tilted.] "I beg your pardon — what does it MEAN?"
