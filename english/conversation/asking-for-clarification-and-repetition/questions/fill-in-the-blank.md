## Fill in the blank

1. "I can't ___ you. Can you speak up, please?" (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: hear** — I can't hear you — the volume fault.

2. "Can you ___ it again, please?" (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: say** — say it again — repetition.

3. "How do you ___ it?" — "D-i-e-n-g." (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: spell** — spelling gives the letters.

4. "What does it ___?" (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: mean** — what does it mean — the meaning repair.

5. "I beg your ___, what does 'single room' mean?" (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: pardon** — I beg your pardon — the softener.
