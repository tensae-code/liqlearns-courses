## Multiple choice

1. The voice on the phone is too quiet. You say: (Sub lesson 1)
   a) "What does it mean?"
   b) "I can't hear you. Can you speak up, please?"
   c) "How do you spell it?"
   **Answer: b) "I can't hear you. Can you speak up, please?"** — a quiet voice needs a volume repair.

2. You did not catch the name. The spelling repair is: (Sub lesson 2)
   a) "How do you spell it?"
   b) "Speak up, please."
   c) "I beg your pardon."
   **Answer: a) "How do you spell it?"** — spelling gives the name letter by letter.

3. One word blocks you: "double". You ask: (Sub lesson 3)
   a) "Say it again."
   b) "What does it mean?"
   c) "Can you speak up?"
   **Answer: b) "What does it mean?"** — an unknown word needs a meaning repair.

4. The polite softener before a repair is: (Sub lesson 3)
   a) "Hurry up."
   b) "Listen to me."
   c) "I beg your pardon."
   **Answer: c) "I beg your pardon."** — I beg your pardon softens the ask.

5. "Can you say it again, please?" asks for: (Sub lesson 2)
   a) a louder voice
   b) the words one more time
   c) a new word
   **Answer: b) the words one more time** — say it again = repetition.

6. You hear every word but cannot write the surname. You ask: (Sub lesson 2)
   a) "How do you spell it?"
   b) "What does it mean?"
   c) "Speak up."
   **Answer: a) "How do you spell it?"** — writing a name needs its letters.
