## Matching

Match each fault to its repair. (Sub lessons 1–3)

1. The voice is too quiet — a
2. You missed the words — b
3. You cannot write the name — c
4. One word is unknown — d
5. You want to soften the ask — e

a) "Can you say it again, please?"
b) "How do you spell it?"
c) "What does it mean?"
d) "I beg your pardon…"
e) "I can't hear you. Can you speak up, please?"
