## True or false

1. "I can't hear you" is a polite repair sentence. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: True** — it names the fault politely.

2. "How do you spell it?" asks about a word's meaning. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: False** — it asks for the letters; meaning is "What does it mean?"

3. "I beg your pardon" softens a repair ask. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: True** — the softener opens the ask politely.

4. If you do not understand, the repair set says: stay silent. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: False** — the whole lesson is asking — never stay silent.

5. "Say it again" asks the person to speak louder. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: False** — it asks for the words one more time; louder is speak up.
