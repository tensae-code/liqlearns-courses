## Short answer

1. Ask a quiet speaker to speak louder, politely. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: "Can you speak up, please?"** — the volume repair.

2. Ask me to say the last sentence one more time. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: "Can you say it again, please?"** — the repetition repair.

3. Ask how to spell the name Dieng. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: "How do you spell it?"** — spell it = letter by letter.

4. Ask what the word " pardon" means, with the softener. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: "I beg your pardon — what does it mean?"** — softener + meaning question.

5. Say the sentence that tells the other person the voice is too small. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: "I can't hear you."** — naming the fault is the first repair.
