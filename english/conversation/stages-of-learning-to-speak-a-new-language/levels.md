# Levels — Stages of Learning to Speak a New Language

One lesson, two sub lessons. A sub lesson opens by proving the previous one (at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, drawn from the whole question set of the sub lesson just before — no hardness ranking is written in questions/).

| Sub lesson | What the learner can do at the end |
|---|---|
| 1. Watching and joining in | Name and describe the first two phases — Observation (watching silently, taking the language in) and Participation (joining in with the group's speaking). |
| 2. Practising and performing | Name and describe the last two phases — Practice (using the language with support, getting it stronger) and Performance (using it independently to express needs, wants and ideas) — and place any learner, including themselves, on the four-phase road. |

A learner who finishes both sub lessons knows where they stand in learning to speak a new language — and that every phase, including the silent first one, is part of the road, not a stop on it.
