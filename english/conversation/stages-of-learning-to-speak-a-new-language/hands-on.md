# Hands-On — Stages of Learning to Speak a New Language

Things you do with a real language you are learning. You need paper and a pencil.

1. **Walk the road in miniature.** Choose a language you barely know. Spend two minutes in Observation — listen to it (a song, a video, a speaker) and say nothing. Then join one repeated phrase: Participation. Then repeat three short sentences after a speaker: Practice. Then say one need, one want and one idea in it, alone: Performance.
2. **Place yourself.** On paper, draw the four stones: Observation, Participation, Practice, Performance. Mark where you stand in each language you are learning, and write the evidence — what you actually do in that language.
3. **Place a friend.** Think of someone learning your language. Which phase are they in? What should they be asked to do — and what should they NOT be forced to do yet?
4. **Next step.** Write one sentence: "My next step on the road is…" and do that step today.
