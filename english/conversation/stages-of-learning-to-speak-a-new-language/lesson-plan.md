# Lesson Plan — Stages of Learning to Speak a New Language

**One thing:** the road every learner of a new language walks, in four phases — Observation, Participation, Practice, Performance — from watching silently at the start to using the language independently, to express needs, wants and ideas, at the end.

## Sub lessons

1. Watching and joining in
2. Practising and performing

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Teaching points

- Learning to use a language climbs four phases: Observation → Participation → Practice → Performance (NEL Guide, Ch.1, pp.13–14).
- The road runs "from watching silently to using language independently to express needs, wants and ideas" (pp.13–14).
- The phases are a map for the learner and for whoever teaches them: silence at the start is a phase, not a failure.
