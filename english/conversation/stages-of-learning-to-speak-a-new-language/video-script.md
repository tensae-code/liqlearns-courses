# Video Script — Stages of Learning to Speak a New Language

Super simple English. Slow. Smile. One learner walks a road of four stones.

[Picture: a child standing at the first of four stones, watching other children talk, saying nothing]

First: Observation. The learner watches. The learner listens. No talking yet. The ears are working!

[Picture: the child joining a circle of children, saying a greeting with them]

Second: Participation. Now the learner joins in! Greetings. Songs. Little phrases — together with everyone.

[Picture: the child practising sentences with a partner, trying, smiling, trying again]

Third: Practice. The learner tries. And tries again. Sentences can wobble. That is fine. This is practice.

[Picture: the child standing alone, speaking freely, hands open]

Fourth: Performance! The learner speaks alone. Needs, wants, ideas — in the new language. The road is walked: from silence… to speaking for yourself.
