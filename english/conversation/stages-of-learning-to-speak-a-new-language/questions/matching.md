# Matching — Stages of Learning to Speak a New Language

Match each learner to their phase.

1. A learner who only watches and listens, and says nothing (Sub lesson 1)
2. A learner who joins the class greeting and sings the group songs (Sub lesson 1)
3. A learner who practises short exchanges with a partner, with wobbles allowed (Sub lesson 2)
4. A learner who asks for what they need and says their own ideas, alone (Sub lesson 2)

   a) Participation
   b) Performance
   c) Observation
   d) Practice

Answers: 1–c, 2–a, 3–d, 4–b ✓
