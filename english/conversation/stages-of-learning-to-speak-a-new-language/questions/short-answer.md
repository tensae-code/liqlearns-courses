# Short Answer — Stages of Learning to Speak a New Language

(Every item is marked with the sub lesson it belongs to: (Sub lesson N). The gate pool for a sub lesson is all of that sub lesson's questions — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the whole set is the pool.)

1. Name the four phases in order. (Sub lesson 1)
   — Observation, Participation, Practice, Performance.

2. What is the learner doing in Observation? (Sub lesson 1)
   — Watching silently: listening to the new language and taking it in, before using it.

3. What changes when a learner moves from Observation to Participation? (Sub lesson 1)
   — The learner begins to join in with the group's speaking instead of only watching.

4. Why are wobbling sentences normal in the Practice phase? (Sub lesson 2)
   — Practice is the phase of supported tries; using the language again and again, imperfectly, is exactly the work of the phase.

5. What three kinds of things does the learner express at Performance? (Sub lesson 2)
   — Needs, wants and ideas.

6. The road of the four phases runs from what, to what? (Sub lesson 2)
   — From watching silently, to using language independently to express needs, wants and ideas.
