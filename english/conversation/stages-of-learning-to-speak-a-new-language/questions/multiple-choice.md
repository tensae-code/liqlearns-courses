# Multiple Choice — Stages of Learning to Speak a New Language

(Every item is marked with the sub lesson it belongs to: (Sub lesson N). The gate pool for a sub lesson is all of that sub lesson's questions — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the whole set is the pool.)

1. What are the four phases of learning to use a language, in order? (Sub lesson 1)
   a) Performance, Practice, Participation, Observation
   b) Observation, Participation, Practice, Performance ✓
   c) Observation, Performance, Practice, Participation
   d) Participation, Observation, Performance, Practice

2. In the Observation phase, the learner: (Sub lesson 1)
   a) Speaks independently to express ideas
   b) Watches silently and takes the language in ✓
   c) Performs for the class
   d) Writes sentences alone

3. A learner who joins the group's greetings and songs but does not yet speak alone is in: (Sub lesson 1)
   a) Observation
   b) Performance
   c) Participation ✓
   d) No phase — something is wrong

4. In the Practice phase, the learner: (Sub lesson 2)
   a) Only watches
   b) Uses the language again and again, with support; imperfect tries are allowed ✓
   c) Must speak perfectly or stay silent
   d) Stops learning

5. Performance means: (Sub lesson 2)
   a) Singing on a stage
   b) Using the language independently to express needs, wants and ideas ✓
   c) Repeating exactly what the teacher says
   d) The first day of learning

6. A learner is silent for weeks in their new class, only watching and listening. The map says: (Sub lesson 2)
   a) They are failing and must be forced to perform
   b) They are in Observation — the first phase; silence is part of the road ✓
   c) They should skip to Performance
   d) The phases do not apply to them
