# Short Answer — Making Appointments

(Every item is marked with the sub lesson it belongs to: (Sub lesson N). The gate pool for a sub lesson is all of that sub lesson's questions — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the whole set is the pool.)

1. What is the polite opening sentence for making an appointment? (Sub lesson 1)
   — "I'd like to make an appointment."

2. Why is a time proposed as a question and not announced as a statement? (Sub lesson 1)
   — Because the time must work for both sides; a question lets the other person say yes or no.

3. The time you proposed is taken. What do you do, and with what words? (Sub lesson 2)
   — Offer another time: "How about 3:30?"

4. How does the appointment talk end once a time is agreed? (Sub lesson 2)
   — By confirming the agreed time out loud: "I'll see you then."

5. Name the four moves of making an appointment, in order. (Sub lesson 2)
   — Ask politely, propose a time, offer another time if needed, confirm.
