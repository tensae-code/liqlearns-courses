# Video Script — Making Appointments

Super simple English. Slow. Smile. Act the little talk out with a partner at a desk.

[Picture: a learner standing at a desk, the other person looking up]

You need an appointment. What do you say? First — be polite. Say what you want.

[Picture: the learner speaking, a speech bubble: "I'd like to make an appointment"]

"I'd like to make an appointment." Good. Now — a time. Ask it like a question.

[Picture: a clock showing three o'clock, a speech bubble: "Is 3:00 o'clock O.K.?"]

"Is 3:00 o'clock O.K.?" … No? Three is not good. Try another time!

[Picture: the clock moving to half past three, a speech bubble: "How about 3:30?"]

"How about 3:30?" Yes! That time is good. Now close the talk. Say the time is fixed.

[Picture: both people nodding and smiling, a speech bubble: "I'll see you then"]

"I'll see you then." Ask. Ask a time. Try again. Confirm. You made an appointment!
