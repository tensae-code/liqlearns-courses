# Hands-On — Making Appointments

Things you do with a partner. You need two chairs and a pretend desk.

1. **The four moves.** One partner sits at the desk. The other asks for an appointment, proposes a time, and confirms. The sitting partner calls out the move numbers — one, two, three, four — as each move happens. If a move is missing, start again.
2. **The taken time.** Play it again, but the desk partner's card says "3:00 is taken". The asker must offer another time with "How about…?" before the talk can end. Try it with two taken times in a row.
3. **Statement or question?** The asker proposes times in two ways: as a statement ("We meet at four") and as a question ("Is four o'clock O.K.?"). The desk partner says which one lets them answer yes or no — and why the question is the polite tool for fixing a time together.
4. **The lock.** After agreeing a time, both partners write down the day and time they think was agreed — separately, without looking. Compare. If the two papers differ, the closing confirmation did not do its job: play the talk again and close with "I'll see you then" plus the time said out loud.
