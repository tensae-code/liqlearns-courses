# Levels — Making Appointments

One lesson, two sub lessons. A sub lesson opens by proving the previous one (at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, drawn from the whole question set of the sub lesson just before — no hardness ranking is written in questions/).

| Sub lesson | What the learner can do at the end |
|---|---|
| 1. Asking for an appointment | Open the talk politely: say "I'd like to make an appointment", and propose a time by asking "Is 3:00 o'clock O.K.?" |
| 2. Fixing the time | Handle a time that does not work: offer another time with "How about 3:30?", agree on one time together, and close the talk confirmed with "I'll see you then". |

A learner who finishes both sub lessons can make an appointment from start to finish: ask politely, propose a time, adjust it if needed, and leave with both sides knowing exactly when they will meet.
