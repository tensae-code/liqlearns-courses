# Lesson Plan — Making Appointments

**One thing:** asking for an appointment politely, and fixing the time together — proposing a time, accepting it, or offering another one — until both sides agree and the talk closes confirmed.

## Sub lessons

1. Asking for an appointment
2. Fixing the time

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Teaching points

- The polite opening: "I'd like to make an appointment" — say what you want, politely, first (Tenth and Eleventh Grades syllabus, printed pp.15–16).
- Propose a time as a question: "Is 3:00 o'clock O.K.?" (pp.15–16).
- If that time does not work, offer another one: "How about 3:30?" (pp.15–16).
- Close by confirming the agreed time: "I'll see you then" (pp.15–16).
- The whole exchange is four moves in order: ask → propose → adjust → confirm.
