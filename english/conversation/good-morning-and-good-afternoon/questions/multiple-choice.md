# Good morning and good afternoon — multiple choice

Choice questions only. Grouped by level. Answers at the bottom.

## Level 1
1. It is 9 o'clock in the morning. You meet your teacher. You say:
   a) Good afternoon  b) Good morning  c) Goodbye  d) Good night
2. It is 3 o'clock in the afternoon. You meet a friend. You say:
   a) Good morning  b) Hello, yesterday  c) Good afternoon  d) Goodbye
3. What chooses between "Good morning" and "Good afternoon"?
   a) Your name  b) The time of day  c) The weather  d) Your book

## Answers
1-b, 2-c, 3-b
