# Good morning and good afternoon — matching

Matching questions only. Answers at the bottom.

## Level 1
Join each time to its greeting.

1. 8 in the morning    a) Good afternoon
2. 4 in the afternoon  b) Good morning
3. 11 in the morning   c) Good afternoon

## Answers
1-b, 2-a, 3-b (11 is still morning — midday has not come yet,
so c is the trap)
