# Good morning and good afternoon — video script (visual way)

Written as simple as possible, like explaining to a grade-one child.
Saved now; the video gets made later, when there is budget.

---

[Picture: the sun coming up]

The sun is coming up.
It is morning.
We say: Good morning!

[Picture: the sun high, children playing after lunch]

Now it is afternoon.
The sun is still up, but the day is older.
We say: Good afternoon!

[Picture: a clock, morning on one side, afternoon on the other]

Morning — Good morning.
Afternoon — Good afternoon.
The time tells us which one.
Now you try it. What time is it now?
