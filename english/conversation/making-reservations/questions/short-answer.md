# Short Answer — Making Reservations

(Every item is marked with the sub lesson it belongs to: (Sub lesson N). The gate pool for a sub lesson is all of that sub lesson's questions — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the whole set is the pool.)

1. What is a reservation, and when is it made? (Sub lesson 1)
   — A booking for a room or a trip, made before you travel, so the room or seat is held in your name.

2. Say the polite sentence for asking for a reservation. (Sub lesson 1)
   — "I'd like to make a reservation, please."

3. What is the difference between a round trip flight and a nonstop flight? (Sub lesson 1)
   — A round trip goes out and back; a nonstop flight flies without stopping on the way. A flight can be both.

4. Which sentence tells you the reservation is really made, and why does it matter? (Sub lesson 1)
   — "Your room/flight has been booked" — until you hear the confirmation, nothing is held for you.

5. Name the hotel words for: the place you check in, the entrance room, the thing that carries you up, and the safe place for important things. (Sub lesson 2)
   — The front desk, the lobby, the elevator, and the security box.

6. How is a hostel different from a three-star hotel? (Sub lesson 2)
   — A hostel is a simple, cheaper place to stay; a three-star hotel is a hotel of the middle kind.
