# Hands-On — Making Reservations

Things you do with a partner. You need two chairs as a pretend front desk.

1. **The whole talk.** One partner is the guest, one is the front desk. The guest asks for a reservation, names a room or a flight, and does not stop until the desk says the confirmation: "Your room has been booked" or "Your flight has been booked." Swap roles and book the other thing.
2. **Round trip or nonstop?** The desk offers two flights on paper: one that stops twice, one round trip nonstop. The guest must pick and say the right sentence for it — "I'd like a round trip flight" or "I'd like a nonstop flight" — and say why the words fit the paper.
3. **Hotel word hunt.** Draw a quick hotel: entrance room, desk, lift, pool, and a safe box. Label each drawing with its word — lobby, front desk, elevator, swimming pool, security box. Your partner points at a drawing; you say the word and one thing it is for.
4. **Hostel or three-star?** Your partner names a place to stay — a hostel, a three-star hotel. Say one sentence about what changes in your reservation talk, and one thing that stays the same: the ask, and the confirmation you must hear.
