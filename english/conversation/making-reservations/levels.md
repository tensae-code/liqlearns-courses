# Levels — Making Reservations

One lesson, two sub lessons. A sub lesson opens by proving the previous one (at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, drawn from the whole question set of the sub lesson just before — no hardness ranking is written in questions/).

| Sub lesson | What the learner can do at the end |
|---|---|
| 1. Asking for a reservation | Ask for a booking politely — "I'd like to make a reservation, please" — name the trip ("I'd like a round trip/nonstop flight"), and recognise the confirmation "Your room/flight has been booked". |
| 2. The hotel words and the reservation talk | Use the lodging set (hostel, three-star hotel, front desk, security box, elevator, lobby, swimming pool) inside a whole reservation conversation, from the ask to the confirmation. |

A learner who finishes both sub lessons can book a room or a flight ahead in English: ask, say exactly what they want, and leave knowing the booking is held for them.
