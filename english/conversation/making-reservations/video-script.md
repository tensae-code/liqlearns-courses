# Video Script — Making Reservations

Super simple English. Slow. Smile. Point to each place in the hotel as its name is said.

[Picture: a traveller with a bag, standing at a hotel front desk]

You travel far. You need a room. Book it before you go! Say it politely.

[Picture: the traveller speaking, a speech bubble: "I'd like to make a reservation, please"]

"I'd like to make a reservation, please." Good. For a plane, say your trip.

[Picture: a plane flying out and back, then a plane flying straight, a speech bubble: "I'd like a round trip flight"]

"I'd like a round trip flight." Out — and back! Or straight, no stops: a nonstop flight.

[Picture: the hotel — the lobby, the elevator going up, a swimming pool]

This is the hotel. The lobby. The elevator — up it goes! The swimming pool. And the front desk keeps your things safe in the security box.

[Picture: the person at the desk nodding, a speech bubble: "Your room has been booked"]

"Your room has been booked." Yes! The room waits for YOU. Ask. Say your trip. Hear "booked". That is a reservation!
