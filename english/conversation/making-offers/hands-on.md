# Hands-on — Making offers

You do it, we guide you. Everything here is done in the learner's
real day, with a companion or the Explain Companion.

1. **The tray game.** Put three real things on a tray — a drink,
   a fruit, a book. Carry it to your companion and offer each one:
   "Would you like…?" The companion must answer one "Yes, please,"
   one "Yes, I would," and one "No, thank you." Then change places.
2. **The heavy-bag offer.** Load two bags (books are fine). One
   person carries both, slowly, past the other. The other must
   offer help two ways: "Shall I carry that?" and "Would you like
   me to open the door?" The carrier accepts one offer and refuses
   the other — politely, with thanks both times.
3. **The offer collection (Nepal's project).** Nepal's Grade 8
   offers unit sends learners out to collect offer expressions.
   Do the same for one day: write down every offer you hear or
   read — at home, in a shop, in a film. Bring back at least five,
   and say which ones give a thing and which ones give help.
4. **Shopkeeper.** One person is the shop worker, one the
   customer. The worker must open with "Can I help you?" The
   customer answers the offer before asking for anything. Play it
   three times: a food shop, a clothes shop, a book shop.
