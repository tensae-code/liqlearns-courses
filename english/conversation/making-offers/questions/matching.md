# Making offers — matching

Match each offer to its best answer.

**Set 1 (Level 1–2)**
1. Would you like some milk? — **Yes, I would.**
2. Shall I carry that bag? — **Yes, please. Thank you.**
3. Can I help you? — **Yes — I'm looking for a book.**
4. Would you like to be an explorer? — **No, I wouldn't.**

(The offers are Syria's explorer question, Starters 5, Unit 2;
Mongolia's table offer, English VI, p.90; Cambodia's shop
offer, Grade 10, Unit 16; and the Shall I help offer of
Malaysia, Academy Stars Year 6, p.56.)
