# Making offers — fill in the blank

Fill each gap with the right word.

**1. (Level 1)** "______ you like some juice?" (the offer word)
**Answer: Would** — "Would you like…?" is the offer shape.

**2. (Level 1)** "Would you like to be an explorer?" — "No, I ______."
**Answer: wouldn't** — Syria's explorer exchange (Starters 5, Unit 2, pp.8–10).

**3. (Level 2)** "______ I open the door for you?" (offering your help)
**Answer: Shall** — "Shall I…?" is the help offer.

**4. (Level 2)** "Would you like ______ to carry your bag?" (the longer help offer)
**Answer: me** — "Would you like me to…?" (Malaysia, Academy Stars Year 6, p.56).
