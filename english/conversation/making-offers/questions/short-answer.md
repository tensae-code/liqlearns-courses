# Making offers — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Offering a thing with Would you like · (Level 2) -> by what the item asks: the polite refusal is Sub lesson 4 — Answering an offer, with thanks · (Level 3) -> the longer help offer is Sub lesson 2 — Offering help with Shall I.

Answer in whole sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Offer your companion a cup of tea.
**Answer: Would you like a cup of tea?** (Any true "Would you like…?" offer earns it.)

**2. (Sub lesson 4)** Someone offers you a drink, and you do not want it. Refuse politely.
**Answer: No, thank you.** — the refusal keeps its thanks.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Your teacher's arms are full of books at a closed door. Make the longer help offer.
**Answer: Would you like me to open the door?** (Malaysia, Academy Stars Year 6, p.56 — "Would you like me to…?" + the job.)
