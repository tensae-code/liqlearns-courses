# Making offers — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Offering a thing with Would you like · (Level 2) -> by what the item turns on: the polite refusal is Sub lesson 4 — Answering an offer, with thanks · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — The shop offer, Can I help you.

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An offer gives something before the other person asks.
**True** — that is what an offer is: a gift in words.

**2. (Sub lesson 4)** "No" alone is a polite answer to an offer.
**False** — the polite refusal carries its thanks: "No, thank you" (Cambodia, Grade 10, Unit 16 — accepting and rejecting politely).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** "Can I help you?" is a request for help.
**False** — it is the shop worker's OFFER of help; the thing (help) moves from the speaker (Cambodia, Grade 10, Unit 16).
