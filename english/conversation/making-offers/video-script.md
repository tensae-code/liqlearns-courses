# Video script — Making offers

Super-simple voice. Read slow. The learner hears this
before or beside the written lesson.

---

[Picture: a guest comes to the door]

A friend comes to your home.
You want to give. You do not wait to be asked.
You offer.

Would you like some juice?
Yes, please.
Thank you.

Would you like some milk?
No, I wouldn't.
No, thank you.
Say no — but say it with thanks.

[Picture: a shop, a worker and a customer]

Now a shop.
The worker speaks first.
Can I help you?
That is an offer. The worker offers help.

[Picture: a friend with two heavy bags]

Your friend has two heavy bags.
You can help.
Shall I carry that?
Would you like me to open the door?

Shall I — I offer my hands.
Would you like — I offer a thing.

[Picture: the two answer cards]

An offer is a small gift in words.
Answer it with thanks.
Yes, please.
No, thank you.

Now you try.
Offer me a drink.
Offer me your help.
I will answer. Then we change places.
