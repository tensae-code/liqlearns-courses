**Acceptance count: taught in 7 countries (Cambodia,
Malaysia, Mongolia, Nepal, Syria, China, Mauritania).** Syria opens the lesson
with the explorer offer in its older York Press Grade 5
course; Mongolia teaches the table offer in Grade 6 and
names shall offers in Grade 7; Cambodia gives offers two
Grade 10 units — dinner offers, then requests told from
offers with polite accepting and rejecting; Nepal gives
offers a whole Grade 8 unit, speaking work and a collecting
project; Malaysia's Year 6 pupil's book, read directly,
teaches the Shall I / Would you like me to help-offer pair.
The plan's shorthand "Nepal G10 U17" for this lesson was
checked against the filing: Nepal's offers unit is Grade 8
Unit 17 (Grade 10 Unit 17 is Agreeing and disagreeing, and
is cited in that lesson instead). China carries the
help-offer at its youngest: offering to clean the
classroom in Grade 4, and the table's "Help yourself"
beside it (stocked 2026-11-08 by the re-verification
sweep).
Mauritania offers help and things, with accepting and
declining, in its Grade 8 book (stocked 2026-11-09 by the
Mauritania re-verification sweep merge).
