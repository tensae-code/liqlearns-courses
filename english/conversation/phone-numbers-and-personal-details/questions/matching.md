## Matching

Match the question to its answer. (Sub lesson 1 and 2)

1. "What's your phone number?"
2. "How do you say 0 in this number?"
3. "How old are you?"
4. "What's your name?"

a) "I'm twelve."
b) "You can say zero, or oh."
c) "It's five five two, oh one four."
d) "My name is Amina."

**Answers: 1-c, 2-b, 3-a, 4-d.**
