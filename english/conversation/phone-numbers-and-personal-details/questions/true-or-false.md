## True or false

1. A phone number is said as one big number, like a price. (Sub lesson 1) **False — it is said digit by digit.**

2. In phone numbers, 0 can be said as "oh". (Sub lesson 1) **True.**

3. Your age is one of the personal details on a school card. (Sub lesson 2) **True.**

4. You should say your phone number as fast as you can. (Sub lesson 2) **False — say it slowly, in small groups, so it can be written down.**
