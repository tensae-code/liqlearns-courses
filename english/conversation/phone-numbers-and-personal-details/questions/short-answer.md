## Short answer

1. Say the phone number 71 045 209 out loud, digit by digit. (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: seven one, zero (oh) four five, two zero (oh) nine.**

2. Why do we say a phone number digit by digit, and not as one big number? (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: so the listener can write each digit correctly, one at a time.**

3. Which three personal details go on a school card? (Sub lesson 2) **Answer: your name, your age and your phone number.**
