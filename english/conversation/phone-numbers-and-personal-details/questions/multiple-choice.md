## Multiple choice

1. How do you say the number 552014 on the phone? (Sub lesson 1)
   a) fifty-five thousand, two hundred and fourteen  b) five five two zero one four, digit by digit  c) fifty-two fourteen
   **Answer: b).**

2. In a phone number, the digit 0 can be said as… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) only "zero"  b) "zero" or "oh"  c) "nothing"
   **Answer: b).**

3. The school secretary asks, "What's your phone number?" You answer with… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) your age  b) your number, digit by digit  c) your address
   **Answer: b).**

4. A personal details card holds… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) name, age and phone number  b) only your name  c) your favourite sport
   **Answer: a).**
