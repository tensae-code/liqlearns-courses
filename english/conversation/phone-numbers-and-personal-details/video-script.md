# Video script — Phone Numbers and Personal Details

Super-simple voice. The mascot is front-facing on screen the whole video, talking to the learner. Bracketed lines are visual cues for the maker, not spoken lines.

[A phone appears behind the mascot.]

"Someone asks: what is your phone number? We do not say it as one big number."

[The digits light up one by one.] "We say the digits, one by one: five — five — two … And zero has two names: zero, or oh."

"Now the card: your name, your age, your phone number. Three things. Say them slowly, and spell your name if they ask."
