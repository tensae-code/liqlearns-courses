# Hands-on — Phone Numbers and Personal Details

1. **Digit dictation.** One learner says a phone number slowly, digit by digit (0 as "oh" sometimes, sometimes "zero"). The other writes the digits down. Swap, and check each other's numbers digit by digit.

2. **Fill the card.** Each learner makes a personal details card: name, age, phone number. Then pairs ask and answer: "What's your name? How old are you? What's your phone number?" — and check the answers against the card.
