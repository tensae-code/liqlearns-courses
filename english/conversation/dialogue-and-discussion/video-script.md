# Video script — Dialogue and Discussion

Super-simple voice. The presenter is the LiqLearns mascot,
front-facing, on screen the whole video; the pictures named here
appear beside him.

[Two chairs face each other.] "A TALK is built by TWO people!"

[One chair's figure speaks; the other listens, eyes on the
speaker.] "One speaks. One LISTENS."

[The chairs swap roles.] "Then you CHANGE!"

[Speech bubbles alternate, each answering the last.] "And you
answer WHAT WAS SAID."

[A hall, two rows facing each other.] "A DEBATE has SIDES.
Reasons with BECAUSE. The side that ANSWERS better wins."

[Back to the mascot, two chairs behind him.] "Talk. Listen.
Answer. You can hold a whole conversation!"
