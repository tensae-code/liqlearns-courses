# Levels — Dialogue and Discussion

This file is the sub-lesson depth record for the lesson
(restructure 2026-11-08): what each sub lesson holds, in teaching
order. The old layer wording is not used; the sub lessons below
are the lesson's own parts, exactly as listed in lesson-plan.md.

| Sub lesson | What it holds |
|---|---|
| Sub lesson 1 — Talking in pairs | Turn-taking with a partner; answering what the partner said. Sierra Leone Primary 1–2 oral discussion and Primary 6 responding-to-a-partner plans. |
| Sub lesson 2 — The good listener and the good speaker | The named qualities of each, and the say-it-back check. Primary 5 Term 3 and Primary 6 Term 2 plans. |
| Sub lesson 3 — Conversations and role play | Whole conversations (opening, answering middle, closing); role play; dialogue read aloud in parts, including the JSS abridged Shakespeare lessons. Primary 5–6 plans and JSS 1–3 / SSS 1 handbook lessons. |
| Sub lesson 4 — Discussion and debate | Class discussion with reasons (or / because), up to formal two-side debate. Primary 2 and 6 plans; SSS 1–3 debate lessons. |

Pass mark: the sources state no pass mark and none is invented. In
this course a sub lesson opens when the learner proves the previous
one with the prove-it check (at most 5 questions, the sub lesson's
whole question set as the pool).
