# Hands-on — Dialogue and Discussion

All of this lesson is done with other people. Do these out
loud.

## Say it back

One partner speaks for half a minute about their home. The
other says back what they heard — "You said …" The speaker
says "right" or fixes what is wrong. Change places.

## The conversation chain

In a circle: the first person opens a conversation with one
line. The next answers **that line** and adds one line. The
next answers again. If anyone answers a line that was not
said, the chain stops and starts again.

## Two sides

The group splits in two. One side must argue "Cats are
better than dogs", the other "Dogs are better than cats" —
whichever you truly think. Every reason needs a **because**.
After five minutes, each side names the other side's best
reason.
