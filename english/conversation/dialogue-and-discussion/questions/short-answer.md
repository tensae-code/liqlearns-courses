## Short answer

1. What is the pair rule of talking? (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer:** One speaks, the other listens; then they change — and each answers what the other said.

2. Name two things a good speaker does. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer:** Any two of: speaks clearly and not too fast; says one idea at a time; looks at the listener; stops and lets the listener answer.

3. How does a conversation close? (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer:** With a last line that ends the talk kindly.

4. What is the difference between a discussion and a debate? (Sub lesson 4)
   **Answer:** A discussion talks a topic through together; a debate has two sides arguing for and against a question.

5. In a debate, what must each side do in its second turn? (Sub lesson 4)
   **Answer:** Answer what the other side said, not only repeat its own reasons.
