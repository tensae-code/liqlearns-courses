## Fill in the blank

1. "In pair talk, one speaks and the other ___." (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: listens** — then they change places.

2. "A good listener can say ___ what the speaker said." (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: back** — the saying-back proves the listening.

3. "A conversation opens, has a middle, and then ___." (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: closes** — the closing ends the talk kindly.

4. "In role play, each person takes a ___." (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: part** — a customer, a shopkeeper, a visitor, a guide.

5. "In a discussion, give your view with a reason: 'I think … ___.'" (Sub lesson 4)
   **Answer: because** — a view without a reason is only half a view.

6. "A debate is a discussion with ___." (Sub lesson 4)
   **Answer: sides** — one side for the question, one against it.
