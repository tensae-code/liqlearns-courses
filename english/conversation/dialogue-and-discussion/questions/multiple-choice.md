## Multiple choice

1. In pair talk, what does the listener do when the speaker finishes? (Sub lesson 1)
   a) Starts a new topic of their own  b) Answers what the speaker said  c) Stays silent
   **Answer: b) Answers what the speaker said** — a talk is built by answering, turn by turn.

2. Which of these is a good listener? (Sub lesson 2)
   a) One who speaks while the other is speaking  b) One who looks at the speaker and can say back what was said  c) One who looks away and waits
   **Answer: b)** — listening shows in the saying-back.

3. What does a good speaker do? (Sub lesson 2)
   a) Says many ideas at once, very fast  b) Never stops to let the listener answer  c) Says one idea at a time, clearly
   **Answer: c) Says one idea at a time, clearly.**

4. What are the three parts of a whole conversation? (Sub lesson 3)
   a) Opening, middle, closing  b) Beginning, shouting, end  c) Question, question, question
   **Answer: a) Opening, middle, closing.**

5. In role play, what does each person do? (Sub lesson 3)
   a) Reads a list alone  b) Takes a part and builds the talk with the other parts  c) Watches
   **Answer: b) Takes a part and builds the talk with the other parts.**

6. In a debate, what makes one side stronger? (Sub lesson 4)
   a) Shouting louder  b) Giving reasons and answering the other side's reasons  c) Speaking first
   **Answer: b) Giving reasons and answering the other side's reasons.**
