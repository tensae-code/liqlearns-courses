## True or false

1. In a talk, both people should speak at the same time. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: False** — a talk is built in turns: one speaks, one listens.

2. A good listener looks at the speaker. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: True.**

3. A good speaker never lets the listener answer. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: False** — a good speaker stops and lets the listener answer.

4. Reading a play's dialogue aloud in parts is a kind of role play. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: True** — the words are given, and the talking still has to work.

5. In a debate, the louder side always wins. (Sub lesson 4)
   **Answer: False** — the side that answers better wins.

6. A discussion view should carry a reason with "because". (Sub lesson 4)
   **Answer: True.**
