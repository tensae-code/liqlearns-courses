## Matching

Match the word to its meaning. (Sub lessons 1–4)

1. Pair talk — c
2. Good listener — a
3. Role play — e
4. Discussion — b
5. Debate — d

a) One who listens for the idea and can say back what was said
b) A whole group talking one topic through, views with reasons
c) Two people talking in turns, each answering the other
d) A discussion with sides, for and against a question
e) A conversation acted out, each person in a part

**Answers:** 1–c (pair talk is two people in turns), 2–a (the good listener
says it back), 3–e (role play is acted conversation), 4–b (a discussion
talks a topic through), 5–d (a debate has sides).
