# Interview Role-Play

## Do it first

A man in your town says he saw lights in the sky last night. You are the reporter — what do you ask him? This lesson plays the interview, from Costa Rica's Grade 11 book.

## Sub lesson 1 — Asking like a reporter

Grade 11 sets a role-play: a witness — **Joe Cole** — saw **UFO sightings** one night, and **reporters interview** him about it.

The reporter's craft is the question: what did you see? when? where? what happened next? Questions in order, ears open — the answers are the story's bones.

*Difficulty: Questions that pull a story out of a witness, in order.*

## Sub lesson 2 — Playing the interview and writing it up

Then the play: one learner is the witness, the others are reporters. After the talk, the work turns to writing — **write the story**: the night's sightings, told from the interview, in order.

Talk first, then text — the interview is the bridge between them.

*Difficulty: Holding a role, then turning talk into a story that stands on its own.*

## Say it to your companion

Your companion is Joe Cole, the witness. Interview them with three reporter questions. Then swap roles.

## Do It Yourself

1. Write five reporter questions for a witness of a strange night event.
2. Play the interview with a partner and note the answers.
3. Write the story in five sentences, in the order things happened.
