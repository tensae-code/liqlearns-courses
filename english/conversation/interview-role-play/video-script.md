# Video script — Interview Role-Play

Super-simple voice. The mascot is front-facing on screen the whole video, talking to the learner. Bracketed lines are visual cues for the maker, not spoken lines.

[A microphone rises; a nervous witness sits.]

“Mr Cole — what did you see that night?”

[Notes fill a page.] “Questions first. Ears open.”

[The page becomes a headline.] “Then — write the story.”
