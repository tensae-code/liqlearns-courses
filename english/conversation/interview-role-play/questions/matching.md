## Matching

Match the reporter question to its job. (Sub lesson 1)

1. What did you see?
2. When was this?
3. Where were you?
4. What happened next?

a) the order of events
b) the sighting itself
c) the time
d) the place

**Answers: 1-b, 2-c, 3-d, 4-a.**
