# Hands-On — Travel Dialogues

Things you do with a partner, a table and your voice. No special materials needed.

1. **Check-in role play.** Set up a desk. One of you is the check-in worker, one is the traveller: passport, window or aisle, luggage, boarding pass, gate. Then change roles.
2. **Make a boarding pass.** Draw a boarding pass with a name, a flight, a seat and a gate number. Use it in the role play.
3. **Taxi!** One of you drives (hands on an imaginary wheel), one rides. Agree the place and the price — £30 — before the ride starts.
4. **Hotel desk.** Act the reception: single room, £50, pay by card, fill in a form you draw, take the key — room 101, second floor. Knock on the "door" and go in.
5. **The whole journey.** Play the full first day in order with a partner: check in, fly (arms out, gently), taxi, hotel, door, bed. Every stop needs its English.
