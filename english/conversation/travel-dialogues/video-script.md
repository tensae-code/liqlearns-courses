# Video Script — Travel Dialogues

Super simple English. Slow. Smile. The airport and the hotel desk appear as they are named.

[Picture: an airport check-in desk]

Hello! Your passport, please. — Here it is. A window seat, please!

[Picture: bags on a belt, a boarding pass held up]

Your luggage goes here. This is your boarding pass. Your gate is number five. Good flight!

[Picture: a taxi outside the airport]

Taxi! To the hotel, please. How much is it? Thirty pounds. Okay, let's go!

[Picture: a hotel desk, a key held up]

Hello! A single room, please. Fifty pounds. I pay by card. … Thank you! Room 101 — second floor. Sleep well!
