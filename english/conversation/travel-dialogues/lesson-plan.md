# Lesson Plan — Travel Dialogues

**One thing:** the set conversations of a journey — checking in at the airport, taking a taxi from the airport, and arriving at the hotel — whole dialogues you can step into.

## Sub lessons

1. At the airport — check-in: show your passport, choose a window seat or an aisle seat, hand over your luggage, take your boarding pass and find your gate; then the airport taxi: asking where it goes and what it costs (£30)
2. At the hotel — reception: asking for a single room (£50), paying by card, filling in the form, taking the key — room 101, on the second floor

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Teaching points

- **Check-in** runs in a fixed order: the desk asks for your **passport**; you choose **a window seat or an aisle seat**; your **luggage** is taken; you receive your **boarding pass**; the pass tells you your **gate**. Learn the order once and every airport in the world works the same way.
- **The airport taxi:** say where you are going and ask the price before you ride — the book's taxi costs **£30**.
- **Hotel reception:** ask for **a single room**; the book's room costs **£50**; you can **pay by card**; you **fill in the form** with your name and details; you take **the key** — **room 101, on the second floor**.
- The dialogues are half-dialogues: one side is printed, the other side is yours to complete — that gap is where the learning happens.
