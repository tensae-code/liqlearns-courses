# Levels — Travel Dialogues

One lesson, two sub lessons. A sub lesson opens by proving the previous one (at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, drawn from the whole question set of the sub lesson just before — no hardness ranking is written in questions/).

| Sub lesson | What the learner can do at the end |
|---|---|
| 1. At the airport | Run a check-in conversation in order — passport, window or aisle seat, luggage, boarding pass, gate — and take the airport taxi, asking the price (£30) first. |
| 2. At the hotel | Run a hotel-reception conversation — ask for a single room (£50), pay by card, fill in the form, and take the key to room 101 on the second floor. |

A learner who finishes both sub lessons can travel the whole first day of a trip in English: off the plane, into the taxi, and into the hotel room.
