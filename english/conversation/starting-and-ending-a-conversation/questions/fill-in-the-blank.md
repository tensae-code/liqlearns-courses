## Fill in the blank

1. "Hello! Are you ___ here?" — a friendly opener. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: new** — asking about the person opens the talk.

2. "I like your bag — ___ did you get it?" (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: where** — a question invites the other person in.

3. "I'm from Surabaya. What ___ you?" (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: about** — "What about you?" throws the ball back.

4. "Yes, I love football — do you ___?" (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: play** — answer + ask back.

5. "I ___ to go now — my class starts." (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: have** — the reason part of a kind ending.

6. "It was ___ talking to you! See you tomorrow!" (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: nice** — the warm line before the goodbye.
