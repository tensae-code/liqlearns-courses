## Matching

Match the line to its job. (Sub lessons 1–3)

1. Hello! Are you new here? — a
2. Nice day, isn't it? — b
3. Yes, I love it — do you play? — c
4. I'm from Surabaya. What about you? — d
5. I have to go now — my class starts. — e
6. It was nice talking to you! Bye! — f

a) an opener — asking about the person
b) an opener — something you share
c) keeping it going — answer + ask back
d) keeping it going — throwing the ball back
e) closing — the reason
f) closing — the warm line and goodbye

**Answer key with reasons:** 1–a (opener: a friendly question), 2–b (opener: the shared weather), 3–c (answer + ask back), 4–d (What about you? returns the turn), 5–e (the reason to leave), 6–f (warm line + goodbye).
