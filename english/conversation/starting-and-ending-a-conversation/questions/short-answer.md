## Short answer

1. Open a conversation with a new classmate. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: "Hello! Are you new here? I'm Ana."** (any friendly opener) — greet, notice, ask.

2. Answer with ask-back: "Do you like reading?" (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: "Yes, I read every night. What do you like reading?"** — answer + ask back.

3. Your friend answers only "Yes." What went wrong? (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: The answer dropped the ball — no new words, no question back.** — talks live on answer + ask back.

4. Close a talk politely: you must catch a bus. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: "I have to go now — my bus is coming. It was nice talking to you! Bye!"** — reason, warm line, goodbye.

5. Why not just walk away when a talk ends? (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: It is rude — the other person doesn't know why. A closer tells them kindly.** — endings are part of the talk.
