## Multiple choice

1. A good opener is… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) "Hello! Are you new here?"  b) Silence  c) "Go away."
   **Answer: a)** — greet, notice, ask: a friendly first line.

2. "Nice day, isn't it?" is… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) a closer  b) an opener about something you share  c) a question for the teacher only
   **Answer: b)** — shared things (weather, place) open talks easily.

3. She asks: "Do you like music?" The BEST answer is… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) "Yes."  b) "Yes, I love it — do you play anything?"  c) Nothing
   **Answer: b)** — answer + ask back keeps the ball in the air.

4. One-word answers usually… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) keep talks alive  b) end talks  c) are polite closers
   **Answer: b)** — they drop the ball.

5. A polite closing is… (Sub lesson 3)
   a) "I have to go now — it was nice talking to you! Bye!"  b) Walking away mid-sentence  c) "This talk is boring."
   **Answer: a)** — reason, warm line, goodbye.

6. The three parts of a kind ending are… (Sub lesson 3)
   a) a shout, a wave, a run  b) a reason, a warm line, a goodbye  c) a question, an answer, a test
   **Answer: b)** — reason + warm line + goodbye.
