## True or false

1. The first line of a conversation can be learned and practised. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: True** — openers are a teachable set.

2. A good opener insults the other person's clothes. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: False** — openers are friendly: greet, notice, ask.

3. Answering "Yes" and stopping keeps a conversation going. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: False** — answer + ask back keeps it going.

4. Asking a question back shows you are listening. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: True** — it throws the ball back with interest.

5. A kind ending gives a reason before the goodbye. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: True** — reason, warm line, goodbye.

6. Walking away in the middle of a talk is a polite closing. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: False** — close with words, kindly.
