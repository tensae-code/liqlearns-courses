# Hands-on — Starting and Ending a Conversation

Conversations are practised live, with real people.

## The two-minute talk

Pairs talk for exactly two minutes about anything. Rules: open with a real opener (no bare "hi" alone), every answer must ask something back, and when the bell rings, close with a reason, a warm line and a goodbye.

## Opener hunt

In one day, hear three real conversations start — at home, at school, outside. Write down each first line. Which opener worked best? Why?
