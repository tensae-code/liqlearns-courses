# Video script — Starting and Ending a Conversation

Super-simple voice. The presenter is the LiqLearns mascot, front-facing,
on screen the whole video; the pictures named here appear beside him.

[The mascot meets a friend and waves.] "Starting is easy! Hello! Are you new here? A friendly first line OPENS the talk!"

[A ball bounces between two friends.] "Answer — then ask back! The ball stays in the air!"

[The mascot points at a clock, smiling.] "Time to end kindly: I have to go now. It was nice talking to you! Bye!"

[The mascot waves.] "Open well. Keep it going. Close kindly. That is a conversation!"
