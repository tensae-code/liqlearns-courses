# How old are you? — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB
LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub
Lessons"). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number, as
before. The questions live in the type files; this file is the
by-sub-lesson view. The sub lessons are the depths inside this one thing — asking and answering age — the short answer first, then the full form the lesson text itself calls its next layer, then birthdays, then the question turned to other people.

Mapping from the old depth layers: old Level 1 (the basics) = Sub lesson 1; old Level 2 (a step deeper — this year's and next year's sentences) is a deeper pass over Sub lesson 1's own content, so its pass mark is carried in Sub lesson 1's section; Sub lesson 2 is the full-form layer, which had no old question group of its own. Sub lessons 3 and 4 were added 2026-11-08 under the whole-arc rule, from the lesson's own stocked sources (the birthday exchange and the third-person age question were stocked but untaught); Sub lesson 2 also gained question items in every type file, since it is no longer the last sub lesson.

Pass marks: the books state no separate pass mark for the
birthday and third-person bands, so Sub lessons 3 and 4 keep
the same observable form — the learner can run the exchange
whole — rather than an invented mark.

## Sub lesson 1 — The short answer, I'm plus the number
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: ask "How old are you?" and answer about yourself in the whole short sentence, "I'm ___" — and say next year's sentence too, telling what changed (the number). (Old Level 1's pass mark, with old Level 2's deeper pass on the same content.)

## Sub lesson 2 — The full answer, I am 8 years old
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: answer in the full form: "I am 8 years old." (The lesson text's own next layer; no old depth layer of its own.)

## Sub lesson 3 — Birthdays: When is your birthday?
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: ask
"When is your birthday?" and answer with your own date in
the books' shape: "My birthday is on …"

## Sub lesson 4 — Asking about other people: How old is he?
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: ask
"How old is he/she?" and "How old is your brother/sister?"
and answer with "He's/She's ___ (years old)."
