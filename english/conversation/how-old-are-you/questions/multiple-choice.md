# How old are you? — multiple choice

Choice questions only. Answers at the bottom.

## Level 1
1. You want to know a child's age. You ask:
   a) What's your name?  b) How are you?  c) How old are you?  d) Who's this?
2. "How old are you?" — a right-shaped answer is:
   a) I'm Omar  b) I'm 8  c) My name is 8  d) I'm fine
3. Next year, what changes in your answer?
   a) The question  b) The word "I'm"  c) The number  d) Nothing

## Answers
1-c, 2-b, 3-c
