# Introducing a friend — true or false

True-or-false questions only. Answers at the bottom.

## Level 1
1. "This is my friend" introduces a new person. (True / False)
2. After the introduction, the new people cannot talk to each
   other. (True / False)
3. "Who's this?" asks about a person. (True / False)

## Answers
1-True, 2-False (they greet each other directly), 3-True
