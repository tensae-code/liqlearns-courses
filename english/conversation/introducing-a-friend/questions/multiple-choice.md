# Introducing a friend — multiple choice

Choice questions only. Answers at the bottom.

## Level 1
1. A new person stands with your friend. You want to know who they
   are. You ask:
   a) How old are you?  b) Who's this?  c) Goodbye?  d) What's this?
2. "Who's this?" — the bridge answer is:
   a) I'm fine  b) This is my friend, Sarah  c) Good morning  d) I'm 6
3. After the introduction, Sarah and Zeinah:
   a) Leave  b) Greet each other directly  c) Ask the time  d) Sit down

## Answers
1-b, 2-b, 3-b
