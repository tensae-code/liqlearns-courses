### Egypt — Primary 3 Term 1 (read 2026-10-25)
Unit 2 "My Family and I" (pp.27–42) grows the introducing-family
layer: the whole family lineup named in one picture (father,
mother, sister, brother, grandmother, grandfather, uncle, aunt,
cousin — "This is my family", p.28), the possessives my / his / her
taught on family sentences (p.30: "My mother is a teacher. / His
brother is a student. / Her baby sister is cute."), family members
introduced by their jobs (farmer, doctor, teacher, baker, pilot,
pp.39–42: "My aunt works as a doctor. / My father is a teacher. /
My uncle bakes delicious bread. He is a baker. / My mother is a
pilot."), and the family celebrations named (Eid Al-Fitr, Sham
El-Nessim, Mother's Day, 6th of October, pp.32–34). Same country,
deeper layer — the count does not change.
