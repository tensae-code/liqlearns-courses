mother / sister …" with the "Who's this?" exchange, and a year
deeper the whole family lineup with my / his / her on family
sentences; China adds the third-person introduction as a taught exchange
with its "Nice to meet you" answer, and the from-where frame;
Mongolia adds the same meeting work carried by pictures alone.
Iran and Indonesia joined 2026-11-03: Iran teaches the
"This is my friend …" frame with its nice-to-meet-you answer in
its second lesson, and the introduce-a-friend role play a year
later; Indonesia names introducing others as a chapter
objective and works it through comic strips. Jordan joined
2026-11-03: it teaches "Who's this? — This is (my) …" in its
Grade 1 first unit and names "Introducing family and friends"
as a taught function in Grade 2. Greece joined 2026-11-03 in
a thinner form than the countries above: presenting a partner
is named work in its Grade 7 Beginners opening unit,
introductions are a named functions strand in its Grade 8
Advanced workbook, and its Grade 10 EPAL book teaches
greetings and introductions fitted to who is being met — but
no "This is my friend …" frame is printed in its filed read.
Kazakhstan joined 2026-11-03 in the family form, like
Egypt and Jordan: its Grade 1 Lilly presents her mummy
with "This is my …", its Grade 2 Activity Book matches
the presentation sentences to the family portraits, and
its Top Stars Grade 3 asks "Who's that? — It's …" over
family and friends; a year-band up, introductions are
named Everyday English in Excel Grade 5 and a named
"Meeting people" speaking slot in English Plus Grade 6.

presents the family with "This is my (dad) …" and its
