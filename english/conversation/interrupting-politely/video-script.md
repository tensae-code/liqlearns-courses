# Video script — Interrupting Politely

Super-simple voice. The mascot is front-facing on screen the whole video, talking to the learner. Bracketed lines are visual cues for the maker, not spoken lines.

[Two people talk; the mascot raises a hand, gently.]

“Sorry to interrupt…”

[The talk pauses; the mascot speaks one line, then steps back.]

“A polite key opens the door. Then — give the floor back.”
