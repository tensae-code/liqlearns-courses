## Short answer

1. Write the three interrupting phrases. (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: Sorry to interrupt…; Could I jump in?; May I interject?**

2. You are in a meeting and a wrong date is announced. Break in politely. (Sub lesson 2) **Answer: any of the three phrases, with the correction (learner's own wording).**

3. How do you give the floor back? (Sub lesson 2) **Answer: “…but please, go on.”**
