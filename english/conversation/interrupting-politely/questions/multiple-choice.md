## Multiple choice

1. Which is a polite interrupting phrase? (Sub lesson 1)
   a) “Be quiet!”  b) “Sorry to interrupt…”  c) “Listen to me!”
   **Answer: b).**

2. “May I interject?” means… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) may I leave  b) may I break in with a point  c) may I sit down
   **Answer: b).**

3. The phrases are set by the book for… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) letters  b) conversations and meetings  c) songs
   **Answer: b).**

4. After your point, the polite close is… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) “…but please, go on.”  b) silence  c) talking louder
   **Answer: a).**
