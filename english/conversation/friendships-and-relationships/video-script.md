# Video script — Friendships and Relationships

Super-simple voice. The mascot is front-facing on screen the whole video, talking to the learner. Bracketed lines are visual cues for the maker, not spoken lines.

[Two friends wave at each other behind the mascot.]

"Friends! How do we talk about friends in English?"

"We get on well. We keep company. Sometimes friends fight — then they make up."

[The mascot points at the learner.] "And you? Do you get on well with your friends? Strike up a friendship — say hello, and talk."
