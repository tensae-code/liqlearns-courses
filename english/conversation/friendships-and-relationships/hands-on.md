# Hands-on — Friendships and Relationships

1. **Word sort.** Put the lesson's words into two boxes: "good times" (get on well, keep company, strike up a friendship) and "hard times" (fight). Talk about what each one looks like in real life.

2. **My friendship.** Tell your partner about one friend: "I get on well with … We keep company at … We never fight about …" Your partner asks one question back.
