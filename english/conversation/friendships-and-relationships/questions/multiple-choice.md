## Multiple choice

1. "Get on well with someone" means… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) to run fast with them  b) to be friendly and happy together  c) to meet them once
   **Answer: b).**

2. To START a new friendship, you… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) strike up a friendship  b) keep company  c) fight
   **Answer: a).**

3. Which sentence talks about your own friendship correctly? (Sub lesson 2)
   a) "I get on well with my friend."  b) "I get on well my friend."  c) "I am get on well with my friend yesterday."
   **Answer: a).**

4. Friends who argue are having a… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) friendship strike  b) fight  c) company
   **Answer: b).**
