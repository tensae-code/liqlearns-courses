## Short answer

1. Say one sentence about a friend you get on well with. (Sub lesson 2) **Answer: any true sentence with "get on well with", e.g. "I get on well with my friend Sami."**

2. What is the difference between "keep company" and "strike up a friendship"? (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: you strike up a friendship when it starts; you keep company when you spend time together inside it.**

3. Name the lesson's word for arguing with a friend. (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: fight.**
