## Multiple choice

1. Which sentence states an opinion? (Sub lesson 1)
   a) "Water is wet."  b) "I think reading is fun."  c) "The book has 100 pages."
   **Answer: b)** — I think… states what you think, not a checkable fact.

2. "___ my opinion, school starts too early." (Sub lesson 1)
   a) In  b) On  c) At yesterday
   **Answer: a) In** — the frame is "In my opinion,…"

3. "I think graffiti is art ___ it makes grey walls beautiful." (Sub lesson 2)
   a) but  b) because  c) or not
   **Answer: b) because** — because gives the reason.

4. An opinion with no reason is… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) stronger  b) lighter — it needs its because  c) a fact
   **Answer: b)** — reasons give an opinion weight.

5. The pros of a question are… (Sub lesson 3)
   a) the points for it  b) the points against it  c) the questions
   **Answer: a)** — pros = for; cons = against.

6. "Some say graffiti is art. Others say it is vandalism." This is… (Sub lesson 3)
   a) one side only  b) both sides of the question  c) a fact
   **Answer: b)** — strong opinions hear both sides.

7. The polite way to answer an opinion you don't share: (Sub lesson 4)
   a) "You are wrong and silly."  b) "I see your point, but I think…"  c) Say nothing and leave angrily
   **Answer: b)** — answer the idea, not the person.

8. "I don't agree, ___ I think phones help us learn." (Sub lesson 4)
   a) because  b) yesterday  c) very
   **Answer: a) because** — disagreement also carries its reason.
