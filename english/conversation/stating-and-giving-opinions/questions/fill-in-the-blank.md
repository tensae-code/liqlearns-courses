## Fill in the blank

1. I ___ graffiti is beautiful. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: think** — I think… states an opinion.

2. In my ___, homework is useful. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: opinion** — In my opinion,…

3. I like this school ___ the teachers are kind. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: because** — because gives the reason.

4. The points against something are called the ___. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: cons** — pros for, cons against.

5. ___ say it is art; others say it is vandalism. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: Some** — Some say… Others say… names both sides.

6. I see your ___, but I think differently. (Sub lesson 4)
   **Answer: point** — "I see your point" hears the other side first.
