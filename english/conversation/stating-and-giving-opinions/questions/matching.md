## Matching

Match the sentence to its job. (Sub lessons 1–4)

1. I think reading is fun. — a
2. In my opinion, the wall is beautiful. — b
3. …because it helps us practise. — c
4. Some say yes; others say no. — d
5. The pros are… the cons are… — e
6. I see your point, but I think… — f

a) stating an opinion with I think
b) stating an opinion with In my opinion
c) giving a reason
d) naming both sides
e) weighing pros and cons
f) answering politely

**Answer key with reasons:** 1–a (I think states), 2–b (In my opinion states), 3–c (because gives the reason), 4–d (Some/Others names both sides), 5–e (pros and cons weigh), 6–f (the polite answer frame).
