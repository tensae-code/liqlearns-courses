## Short answer

1. State your opinion about football. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: "I think football is exciting."** (any true opinion) — I think… + your view.

2. Give a reason for that opinion. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: "…because the whole town watches together."** — because + a reason.

3. Say one point FOR and one point AGAINST phones in class. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: "For: we can look up words. Against: we play games instead of listening."** — both sides named.

4. Answer this politely: "I think homework is useless." (Sub lesson 4)
   **Answer: "I see your point, but I think homework helps us practise."** — polite frame + reason.

5. Land on your own side of the graffiti question. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: "I still think graffiti is art, because it tells the city's stories."** — landing + reason after both sides.
