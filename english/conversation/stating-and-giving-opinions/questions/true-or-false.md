## True or false

1. An opinion is a fact everyone can check. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: False** — an opinion is what you think; facts can be checked.

2. "In my opinion" is a polite frame for stating what you think. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: True** — one of the stating frames.

3. A reason makes an opinion weaker. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: False** — reasons give an opinion weight.

4. Hearing the other side makes your own opinion stronger. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: True** — you answer their best point, not a straw one.

5. Pros are the points against something. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: False** — pros are for; cons are against.

6. "You are silly" is a good way to answer an opinion. (Sub lesson 4)
   **Answer: False** — answer the idea, not the person.
