# Hands-on — Stating and Giving Opinions

Opinions are practised live, on real questions.

## The graffiti wall

Show one picture of street art. Half the room argues FOR (art), half AGAINST (vandalism). Each speaker must say "I think… because…" — no reason, no turn.

## Swap sides

After five minutes, swap: argue the side you were against. Finish by saying your own true landing: "I still think… because…"
