# Video script — Stating and Giving Opinions

Super-simple voice. The presenter is the LiqLearns mascot, front-facing,
on screen the whole video; the pictures named here appear beside him.

[A painted wall appears.] "Is it art — or a mess? I THINK it is art! I think — that is an opinion!"

[A scale appears and tips.] "Why? BECAUSE it makes the wall beautiful. An opinion needs a reason!"

[Two arrows point opposite ways.] "Some say yes. Some say no. Hear BOTH sides — then choose."

[The mascot listens, then smiles.] "I see your point — BUT I think… Answer the idea, politely!"
