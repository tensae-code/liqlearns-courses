**SPLIT NOTE (2026-10-25, his one-thing rule):** Libya's books run
several other things inside the same greeting talk. They used to be

- "Good morning / Good afternoon" (Libya G1, Unit 1 Lesson 2) →
  conversation/good-morning-and-good-afternoon

  conversation/how-are-you
- Introducing a friend — "Who's this? This is my friend, Sarah"
  (Libya G1 Teacher's Book, Track 108) →
  conversation/introducing-a-friend

  conversation/whats-his-and-her-name
- "How old are you? I'm ___" (Libya G1–G3) → age/how-old-are-you —
  age is not a greeting at all; it has its own title.

**Acceptance count: taught in 7 countries (Egypt, Libya, Nicaragua, Laos, China, North Macedonia, Mongolia).**
China adds name exchanges from its first primary page and the
"Where are you from? — I'm from …" origin frame (Grades 3), plus
five printed ways to say goodbye (Grade 6 Volume 2); North

family in one frame; Mongolia adds the same name work carried
by pictures alone, its Grade 5 book printing no English at all.
This lesson is the first layer. Courses are built from every CLOSED
country's books (his ruling, 2026-11-03 — superseding the 2026-10-25
finished-only rule), and updated as the library updates: when a
country closes, its books merge in as the files are in hand, its name
joins this list, and the count grows — that count is the acceptance
points.

### Egypt — Primary 3 Term 1 (read 2026-10-25)
Unit 1, Lesson 1 "Greetings and Introductions" (pp.6–9): the whole
greeting set as one list — Hello! · Good morning! · How are you? ·
I am fine, thank you. · Nice to meet you. · See you later! ·
Goodbye! — plus Introducing Yourself (My name is… / I am… / I am
from Egypt. / I am in Primary 3). The "Meeting a New Friend" dialog
(p.7, Sara and Lina) runs the entire conversation in one flow:
hello → good morning → how are you (both ways) → names (both ways)
→ how old are you → nice to meet you (both ways) → see you later →
goodbye. Fill-in dialog and choose-the-right-response listening
follow (pp.8–9). Same country, deeper layer — the count does not
change. (Full map: EGYPT-BOOK-MAP.md.)

**Laos Grade 3 added 2026-10-27** — *English for Primary Schools,

is Laos's own.
