# Greetings — hands-on (learn by doing)

## Project 1: the real greeting
You need: one real person.
1. Greet them with the whole shape: Hello → What's your name? →
   My name is ___ → Nice to meet you → shake hands → Goodbye.
2. Then write down the five sentences you said, one on each line.
3. Count: did every sentence end properly? That count is your score.

## Project 2: teach your companion
You need: your explain companion (the pillow or character you picked).
1. Read the Maya and Hana talk out loud to your companion, both parts.
2. Explain to your companion, in your own words: what are the three
   parts of a greeting? (Open — names — close.)
3. Do the greeting one more time, with your own name, without reading.
