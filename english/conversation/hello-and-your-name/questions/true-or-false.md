# Greetings — true or false

True-or-false questions only. Answers at the bottom.

## Level 1
1. "Goodbye" is the word you say when you arrive. (True / False)
2. "My name is Maya" is a complete sentence. (True / False)
3. You shake hands when you say "Nice to meet you." (True / False)
4. "I'm" is the short way of saying "I am." (True / False)

## Answers
1-False (it is the word for leaving), 2-True, 3-True, 4-True
