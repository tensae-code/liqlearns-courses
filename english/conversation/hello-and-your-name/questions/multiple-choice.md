# Greetings — multiple choice

Choice questions only. Grouped by level. Answers at the bottom.
All questions are built from Egypt, English Primary 1 Term 1, Unit 1 Lesson 1.

## Level 1
1. Someone says "Hello!" to you. What do you say back?
   a) Goodbye  b) Hello!  c) Thank you  d) Play
2. "What's your name?" — the right answer is:
   a) I'm fine  b) My name is Omar  c) Nice to meet you  d) Shake hands
3. When you leave a friend, you say:
   a) Hello  b) What's your name?  c) Goodbye  d) I'm Hana
4. "Nice to meet you" — the answer with the same words back is:
   a) Nice to meet you, too  b) Goodbye  c) My name is Maya  d) Play

## Answers
1-b, 2-b, 3-c, 4-a
