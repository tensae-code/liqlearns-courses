# Levels — Expressing Sympathy

This file is the sub-lesson depth record for the lesson (restructure
2026-11-08): what each sub lesson holds, in teaching order. The old
layer wording is not used; the sub lessons below are the lesson's own
parts, exactly as listed in lesson-plan.md.

| Sub lesson | What it holds |
|---|---|
| Sub lesson 1 — I'm sorry to hear that — answering bad news | The first answer to bad news: I'm sorry to hear that, I'm sad to hear that, oh dear, poor you. |
| Sub lesson 2 — I'm here for you — standing with the person | Presence words: I'm here for you — sympathy as staying, not only saying. |
| Sub lesson 3 — For a death — condolences | The death set: I'm sorry for your loss, my condolences, my deepest sympathies. |
| Sub lesson 4 — Thinking of you — sympathy that lasts | The written/lasting form over the Grade 10 disaster text: thinking of you at this difficult time. |

Pass mark: the sources state no pass mark and none is invented. In this course a sub lesson opens when the learner proves the previous one with the prove-it check (at most 5 questions, the sub lesson's whole question set as the pool).
