# Hands-on — Expressing Sympathy

## Fit the words to the weight

News cards are dealt face down: a lost umbrella, a broken leg, a burned house, a death in the family. Each learner reads their card aloud as news; the class answers together with sympathy of the right weight — and says why condolences do or do not fit.

## The class sympathy card

One big card, written by the whole class to an imagined family after an earthquake: the condolence forms, the thinking-of-you line, and every learner's name. It stays in the classroom as the model for real cards.
