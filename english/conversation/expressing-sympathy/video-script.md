# Video script — Expressing Sympathy

Super-simple voice. The presenter is the LiqLearns mascot, front-facing,
on screen the whole video; the pictures named here appear beside him.

[The mascot hears news, face falling.] "I'm SORRY to hear that."

[The mascot sits beside a sad friend, paw on his shoulder.] "I'm HERE for you."

[The mascot bows his head, quietly.] "I'm sorry for your loss. My deepest CONDOLENCES."

[The mascot writes a card.] "Thinking of you at this difficult time."
