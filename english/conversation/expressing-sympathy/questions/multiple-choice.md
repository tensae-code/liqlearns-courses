## Multiple choice

1. A friend says: "I failed my exam." You answer: (Sub lesson 1)
   a) "My deepest condolences."
   b) "I'm sorry to hear that."
   c) "That's really kind of you."
   **Answer: b) "I'm sorry to hear that."** — sorrow fits bad news; condolences are for a death.

2. The presence sentence is: (Sub lesson 2)
   a) "I'm here for you."
   b) "Poor you."
   c) "Oh dear."
   **Answer: a) "I'm here for you."** — it promises staying with the person.

3. For a death, the set form is: (Sub lesson 3)
   a) "Oh dear."
   b) "My condolences."
   c) "I'm sad to hear that."
   **Answer: b) "My condolences."** — condolences are the death set.

4. "I'm sorry for your ___" completes the condolence. (Sub lesson 3)
   a) news
   b) loss
   c) time
   **Answer: b) loss** — I'm sorry for your loss.

5. The written sympathy line is: (Sub lesson 4)
   a) "Thinking of you at this difficult time."
   b) "Thanks for your loss."
   c) "How do you spell it?"
   **Answer: a) "Thinking of you at this difficult time."** — the lasting, written form.

6. A lost pen should get: (Sub lesson 1)
   a) my deepest condolences
   b) oh dear — I'm sorry to hear that
   c) my deepest sympathies
   **Answer: b) oh dear — I'm sorry to hear that** — fit the words to the weight.
