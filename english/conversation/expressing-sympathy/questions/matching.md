## Matching

Match each news item to its sympathy. (Sub lessons 1–4)

1. "I failed my test." — a
2. "I will sit with you; you are not alone." — b
3. "Her grandfather died yesterday." — c
4. A card to a family after the earthquake — d
5. "I lost my umbrella." — e

a) "Thinking of you at this difficult time."
b) "I'm sorry to hear that."
c) "Oh dear. Poor you."
d) "I'm sorry for your loss. My condolences."
e) "I'm here for you."

**Answer key with reasons:** 1–b (sorrow for bad news), 2–e (presence), 3–d (condolences for a death), 4–a (the written line), 5–c (small warm words for small news).
