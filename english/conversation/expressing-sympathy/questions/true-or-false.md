## True or false

1. "My deepest condolences" fits a lost pen. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: False** — condolences are for a death only.

2. "I'm here for you" promises presence, not things. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: True** — it is a staying word.

3. Written sympathy can be re-read on later days. (Sub lesson 4)
   **Answer: True** — that is why the written form lasts.

4. "I'm sorry to hear that" answers good news. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: False** — it answers bad news; good news takes congratulations.

5. "Oh dear" is a small, warm sympathy answer. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: True** — small news, small words.
