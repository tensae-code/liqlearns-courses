## Short answer

1. Answer this news: "My bag was stolen." (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: "I'm sorry to hear that." / "Oh dear."** — sorrow for bad news.

2. Say the sentence that promises your presence. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: "I'm here for you."** — the staying word.

3. Say a condolence for a death. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: "My condolences." / "I'm sorry for your loss."** — the death set.

4. Write the thinking-of-you line. (Sub lesson 4)
   **Answer: "Thinking of you at this difficult time."** — the written form.

5. Say a small warm answer to "I lost my pen." (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: "Oh dear." / "Poor you."** — small news, small warm words.
