# Multiple Choice — Keeping a Conversation Going

(Every item is marked with the sub lesson it belongs to: (Sub lesson N). The gate pool for a sub lesson is all of that sub lesson's questions — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the whole set is the pool.)

1. The listener's small answers are called listening answers because they ___. (Sub lesson 1)
   a) end the conversation
   b) tell the speaker you are listening ✓
   c) are whispered
   d) correct the speaker's grammar

2. Which of these is in the book's set of listening answers? (Sub lesson 1)
   a) Whatever.
   b) I see. ✓
   c) Be quiet.
   d) Stop talking.

3. "Really?" tells the speaker ___. (Sub lesson 1)
   a) I do not believe you at all
   b) stop now
   c) that surprises me — tell me more ✓
   d) I am leaving

4. "Sure." as a listening answer means ___. (Sub lesson 1)
   a) yes, willingly ✓
   b) I am not sure
   c) maybe tomorrow
   d) no, thank you

5. In a mini-dialogue, a listening answer comes ___. (Sub lesson 2)
   a) only at the very end
   b) before the speaker starts
   c) after the speaker's lines, again and again ✓
   d) instead of the speaker's lines

6. Answering before the speaker finishes is ___. (Sub lesson 2)
   a) good listening
   b) interrupting, not listening ✓
   c) the fastest way to help
   d) called back-channelling

7. Using only "Yeah. Yeah. Yeah." for everything tells the speaker ___. (Sub lesson 2)
   a) you agree with everything
   b) you are very interested
   c) you stopped listening long ago ✓
   d) the talk is over

8. A listening answer should be ___. (Sub lesson 2)
   a) long and detailed
   b) short, warm and at the right moment ✓
   c) a question about something else
   d) silent
