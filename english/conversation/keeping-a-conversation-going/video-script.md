# Video Script — Keeping a Conversation Going

Super simple English. Slow. Smile. The two speakers appear on screen; each small answer pops up in a bubble as it is said.

[Picture: two friends talking; one speaks, the other is silent and looks away]

Look — she is talking. He says nothing. The talk stops. Silence kills a conversation!

[Picture: the seven answers in bubbles — I see. Great! Really? Right. Yeah. Sure. Awesome.]

Small answers save the talk! I see. Great! Really? Right. Yeah. Sure. Awesome!

[Picture: the friends again — she speaks, he answers "Really?", she smiles and goes on]

She talks. He answers: "Really?" She smiles — and talks more! The answer tells her: I am listening!

[Picture: the two friends laughing, the talk flowing back and forth]

Answer… answer… answer. Short. Warm. On time. Now the conversation goes and goes!
