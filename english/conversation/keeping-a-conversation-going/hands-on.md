# Hands-On — Keeping a Conversation Going

Things you do with a real partner and real talk. No special materials needed.

1. **Three true things.** Your partner tells you three true things about their week. After each one, answer with a listening answer — a different one each time. Then swap roles.
2. **Silence test.** Tell your partner a short story twice: first while they stay completely silent, then while they answer with the set. Feel the difference — then say which talk was easier, and why.
3. **Answer hunt.** Write the seven answers on seven small cards. Your partner reads speaker lines from any dialogue; you hold up the card that fits each line. Fast round: one second per answer.
4. **Mini-dialogue performance.** Perform the mini-dialogue from the lesson with a partner, then perform your own: eight lines, four answers, none repeated.
