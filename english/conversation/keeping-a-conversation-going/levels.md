# Levels — Keeping a Conversation Going

One lesson, two sub lessons. A sub lesson opens by proving the previous one (at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, drawn from the whole question set of the sub lesson just before — no hardness ranking is written in questions/).

| Sub lesson | What the learner can do at the end |
|---|---|
| 1. The listening answers | Name the seven listening answers and say what each one tells the speaker. |
| 2. Keeping a mini-dialogue going | Hold a short dialogue alive as the listener, placing the right answer after each of the speaker's lines. |

A learner who finishes both sub lessons can keep any short conversation going: listening, answering, and never letting the talk fall silent.
