## Matching

Match the sentence to its job. (Sub lessons 1–4)

1. I like football. — a
2. I don't like rain. — b
3. My favourite activity is swimming. — c
4. My hobby is reading. — d
5. Do you like music? — e
6. What is your hobby? — f

a) saying a like
b) saying a dislike
c) naming the favourite
d) naming a hobby
e) asking about a like
f) asking about a hobby

**Answer key with reasons:** 1–a (I like states a like), 2–b (don't like states a dislike), 3–c (favourite = the top one), 4–d (a hobby is a free-time activity), 5–e (Do you like…? asks a like), 6–f (the hobby question).
