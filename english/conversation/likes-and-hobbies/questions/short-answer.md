## Short answer

1. Say one thing you like. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: "I like music."** (any true like sentence) — I like + the thing.

2. Say one thing you don't like. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: "I don't like rain."** (any true dislike) — don't like for the opposite.

3. Say your favourite activity in a full sentence. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: "My favourite activity is swimming."** — my favourite activity is…

4. Name a hobby and say when you do it. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: "My hobby is reading. I do it every evening."** — hobby + a time.

5. Ask someone about tea. (Sub lesson 4)
   **Answer: "Do you like tea?"** — Do you like…? with short answers "Yes, I do / No, I don't."
