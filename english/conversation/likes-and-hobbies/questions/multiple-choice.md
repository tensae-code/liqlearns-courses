## Multiple choice

1. "I ___ football." (saying what you like) (Sub lesson 1)
   a) likes  b) like  c) liking yesterday
   **Answer: b) like** — I like + the thing.

2. "She ___ music." (Sub lesson 1)
   a) likes  b) like  c) don't like always
   **Answer: a) likes** — he/she takes likes.

3. "I ___ rain. It makes me wet and cold." (Sub lesson 1)
   a) like  b) favourite  c) don't like
   **Answer: c) don't like** — don't like says the opposite.

4. "My ___ activity is swimming." (Sub lesson 2)
   a) hobby  b) favourite  c) every
   **Answer: b) favourite** — favourite names the top one.

5. A hobby is… (Sub lesson 3)
   a) something you do in your free time because you like it  b) a school subject  c) a kind of food
   **Answer: a)** — a hobby is a like you do.

6. "What is your ___?" — "Reading. I do it every week." (Sub lesson 3)
   a) name  b) hobby  c) age
   **Answer: b) hobby** — the hobby question asks about free-time activities.

7. "___ you like music?" — "Yes, I do." (Sub lesson 4)
   a) Do  b) Does she  c) Is
   **Answer: a) Do** — Do you like…? asks about likes.

8. "Do you like tea?" — the short NO answer is… (Sub lesson 4)
   a) "No, I don't."  b) "No, I do."  c) "No, I amn't."
   **Answer: a) "No, I don't."** — Do questions answer with do/don't.
