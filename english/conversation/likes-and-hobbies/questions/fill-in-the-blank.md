## Fill in the blank

1. I ___ (like) drawing very much. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: like** — I like + the activity.

2. He ___ (not like) cold weather. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: doesn't like** — he/she takes doesn't like.

3. My favourite ___ is fishing. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: activity** — my favourite activity is…

4. Her favourite ___ is noodles. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: food** — favourite works for food too.

5. My ___ is playing chess; I do it every Sunday. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: hobby** — a hobby is done often, in free time.

6. ___ you like bananas? — Yes, I do. (Sub lesson 4)
   **Answer: Do** — Do you like…?

7. What is your favourite ___? — Swimming. (Sub lesson 4)
   **Answer: activity** — the favourite question.
