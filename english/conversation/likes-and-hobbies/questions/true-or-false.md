## True or false

1. "I likes football" is correct. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: False** — I like; likes is for he/she.

2. "I don't like rain" says rain makes me happy. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: False** — don't like is the opposite of like.

3. Your favourite is the one you like most of all. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: True** — favourite names the top one.

4. A hobby is something you do in your free time because you like it. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: True** — a hobby is a like you do, often.

5. Liking cake makes cake your hobby. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: False** — a hobby is an activity you do, not a food you like.

6. "Do you like music?" can be answered "Yes, I do." (Sub lesson 4)
   **Answer: True** — Do questions take do/don't short answers.
