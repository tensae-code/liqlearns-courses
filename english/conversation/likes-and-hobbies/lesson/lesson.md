# Likes and Hobbies

## Do it first

What do you like doing? Swimming? Singing? Playing with friends? Say one thing now, starting "I like…". Go on — try before we start. In this lesson you will learn to say what you like, name your favourite activity, talk about hobbies, and ask others what they like.

## Sub lesson 1 — Saying what you like — I like…

**Like** says something makes you happy: "I like football." "I like bananas." "She likes music."

Not happy with it? "I **don't like** rain." "He doesn't like cold water."

Indonesia's Grade 6 book works likes and dislikes side by side — because real talk needs both: what you choose, and what you leave.

Say two about yourself now: one "I like…", one "I don't like…".

## Sub lesson 2 — Your favourite — the one you like most

Of all the things you like, one is the top one — your **favourite**.

"My favourite activity is swimming." "Her favourite food is noodles."

Indonesia's Grade 3 book builds a whole chapter on this: children name their favourite activity and say it proudly. Favourite takes "the" thinking away — just say "my favourite".

What is YOUR favourite activity? Say it in one full sentence.

## Sub lesson 3 — Hobbies — what you do in your free time

A **hobby** is something you do often in your free time because you like it: reading, fishing, dancing, collecting stamps, playing chess.

Indonesia's Grade 7 book gives hobbies their own work — naming them, and saying how often you do them: "My hobby is photography. I do it every week."

A hobby is more than a like: you can like cake without it being a hobby. A hobby is a like you *do*.

Name one hobby of yours — or one you want to start.

## Sub lesson 4 — Asking about likes — Do you like…?

Turn it into talk. Ask with **Do you like…?**: "Do you like music?" — "Yes, I do." / "No, I don't."

Ask the top one: "What is your favourite activity?" Ask about hobbies: "What is your hobby?"

Indonesia's books ask these in class surveys — everyone asks, everyone answers, and the class learns who likes what.

Ask me one now: "Do you like…?" Then answer mine: what is your hobby?

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion: two things you like, one thing you don't like, your favourite activity, and your hobby. Then ask them the same four things, and listen to their answers.

## Do It Yourself

Write five sentences: two "I like…", one "I don't like…", one "My favourite activity is…", and one about your hobby. Read them aloud to someone at home and ask them "Do you like…?" about one thing.
