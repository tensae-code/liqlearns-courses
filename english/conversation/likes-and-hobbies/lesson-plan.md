# Lesson plan — Likes and Hobbies

**Course:** English (Zero to Story)
**Title:** Likes and Hobbies
**Lesson:** likes-and-hobbies — saying what you like, naming your favourite activity, and talking about hobbies
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 5 — Conversation
**The one thing:** Likes and hobbies talk — I like…, my favourite activity is…, and asking others what they like.
**This lesson's own level:** Conversation lesson (likes): like + noun or -ing; favourite for the top one; hobby words for free-time activities; Do you like…? questions.

## Why this lesson exists

Indonesia teaches likes and hobbies in three books and none had a lesson: the Grade 3 book's favourite-activity work, the Grade 6 book's likes-and-dislikes work, and the Grade 7 book's hobbies work. The course's conversation lessons cover greetings, shopping and polite asking, but not the likes talk every learner needs first. This lesson is built from the placements under the everything-goes-in ruling (2026-11-06).

## Sub lessons

1. Saying what you like — I like…
2. Your favourite — the one you like most
3. Hobbies — what you do in your free time
4. Asking about likes — Do you like…?

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
Nothing is needed before this lesson. Hello and Your Name makes introductions; this lesson gives the learner something to say next, and each stands alone.

## What the learner can do when it ends
- Say what you like and don't like: "I like football. I don't like rain."
- Name your favourite activity: "My favourite activity is swimming."
- Talk about hobbies — the things you do in your free time.
- Ask others: "Do you like music?" "What is your hobby?"

## What you need
Nothing special — your own likes are the material.

## The run of the lesson
1. ADD IMAGE: children doing four things — swimming, singing, drawing, playing football. The learner points and says one "I like…" before any rule appears.
2. Teach sub lesson 1 with the learner's own life: "I like rice. I like music." Then the top one: "My favourite is…"
3. ADD GIF (1–2 seconds): a child with a ball, a book and a guitar choosing one — hobbies are what we pick in free time.
4. Sub lesson 4 turns it into talk: ask three people "Do you like…?" and report one answer each.

## Add-ons
- Like-chain: each learner says "I like…" and adds one; the next repeats all before and adds their own.
- Hobby show: bring or draw one hobby thing and talk about it for three sentences.

## If the learner is stuck
Keep to "I like + thing" until it is automatic. Favourites and hobby words are one step each after that.

## Teacher note
Indonesia's Grade 3 book works the favourite activity inside its "My Favorite Activity" chapter; Grade 6 works likes and dislikes together; Grade 7 names hobbies as their own topic. The lesson keeps that order: likes, the favourite, hobbies, asking.
