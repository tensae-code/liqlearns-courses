# Video script — Likes and Hobbies

Super-simple voice. The presenter is the LiqLearns mascot, front-facing,
on screen the whole video; the pictures named here appear beside him.

[The mascot swims, then sings.] "I LIKE swimming! I LIKE music! What do YOU like?"

[The mascot holds up one finger, smiling big.] "My FAVOURITE activity is swimming — my number one!"

[A guitar, a book and a ball appear.] "A HOBBY is what you do in your free time. My hobby is reading!"

[The mascot turns to a friend.] "Do YOU like football? What is YOUR hobby? Ask — and answer!"
