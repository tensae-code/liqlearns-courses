# Recommending

## Sub lesson 1 — Recommending words

The core recommending phrases:

- **I recommend…** — *I recommend the fish — it is fresh today.*
- **You should try…** — *You should try the honey cake.*
- **It's worth…** — *It's worth visiting the old town.*
- **You mustn't miss…** — for the strongest praise.
- Always give the reason: *…because…*, or a short taste of it: *It's sweet, soft and warm.*

Answering a recommendation: *That sounds good. I'll try it.* / *Thank you — maybe next time.*

Do it: recommend three foods to a visitor to your town, one phrase each, with a reason.

## Sub lesson 2 — Recommending for a person and a purpose

A good recommendation fits the person in front of you.

- Ask or notice what they like: a child, a guest who eats no meat, a friend with little money — the best choice is different for each.
- Match the strength: a small *you could try…* for a guess; *you must try…* only when you are sure.
- In a restaurant — the source book's setting — recommend a dish, say what is in it, and why THIS person will like it.

Do it: your friend is hungry, eats no meat, and has little money. Recommend one dish and say why it fits all three.

## Say it to your companion

Read the key sentences aloud and explain them to your companion in your own words — what the thing is, and one example of your own.

## Do It Yourself

Close the lesson and use the thing once in real life today — say it, write it, or notice it — then say how it went in one sentence.
