# Multiple choice — Recommending

(Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson(s) named — no hardness ranking is written here, so a sub lesson's whole question set is its gate pool.)

1. Which phrase recommends something?
   A) I recommend the fish.
   B) I ate the fish yesterday.
   C) Is this fish?
   D) The fish is on the table.
   **Answer: A** (Sub lesson 1)

2. A recommendation should always give:
   A) the price only
   B) a reason
   C) a command
   D) a story
   **Answer: B** (Sub lesson 1)

3. "It's worth visiting the old town" means:
   A) the town costs money
   B) visiting it is a good idea
   C) the town is old
   D) you must pay
   **Answer: B** (Sub lesson 1)

4. Your guest eats no meat. The best recommendation is:
   A) the beef steak, it is big
   B) the vegetable soup — fresh and cheap
   C) any meat dish
   D) nothing
   **Answer: B** (Sub lesson 2)

5. "You mustn't miss the honey cake" is:
   A) a weak guess
   B) the strongest praise
   C) a question
   D) an order to leave
   **Answer: B** (Sub lesson 2)

6. A good recommendation fits:
   A) only the speaker
   B) the person in front of you
   C) nobody
   D) the menu's first page
   **Answer: B** (Sub lesson 2)
