# Matching — Recommending

(Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson(s) named — no hardness ranking is written here, so a sub lesson's whole question set is its gate pool.)

1. Match the phrase to its job.
   1. I recommend…
   2. It's worth…
   3. That sounds good.
   a. answering a recommendation
   b. saying a thing is a good idea
   c. naming your choice
   **Answer: 1-c, 2-b, 3-a** (Sub lesson 1)

2. Match the guest to the fitting recommendation.
   1. a child
   2. a guest with little money
   3. a guest who eats no meat
   a. the cheap daily soup
   b. a sweet, mild dish
   c. the vegetable plov
   **Answer: 1-b, 2-a, 3-c** (Sub lesson 2)
