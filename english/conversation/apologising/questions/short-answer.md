# Apologising — short answer

Answer in whole sentences.

**1. (Level 1)** You stepped on your friend's toe. Apologise.
**Answer: I'm sorry.** (Nepal's Grade 9 speaking clues include stepping on a toe — Grade 9, Unit 5.)

**2. (Level 2)** You left your friend's book at home. Apologise in full.
**Answer: I'm really sorry. I'm afraid I left your book at home.** (Sorry + "I'm afraid I left…" — Syria, Starters 5, Unit 16.)

**3. (Level 3)** Write the first line of an apology email to your teacher for the homework you forgot.
**Answer: I'm terribly sorry. I'm afraid I forgot my homework.** (Any full apology — strength + "I'm afraid I forgot…" — earns it; the letter shape is Nepal, Grade 9, Unit 5, Writing II.)
