# Apologising — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** You forgot a tiny thing — a pencil. The right strength is:
a) I'm terribly sorry.  b) I'm sorry.  c) I am not sorry.
**Answer: b) I'm sorry.** — match the strength to the accident (Syria, Starters 5, Unit 16, pp.47–49).

**2. (Level 1)** You broke your friend's computer game. The full apology begins:
a) I'm afraid I broke it. I'm terribly sorry.  b) It broke itself.  c) Games break.
**Answer: a) I'm afraid I broke it. I'm terribly sorry.** — sorry at full strength + what happened (Syria, Starters 5, Unit 16 Study Box).

**3. (Level 2)** "I'm afraid I ______ your pen." (you cannot find it)
a) lost  b) lose yesterday it  c) am lost it
**Answer: a) lost** — Syria's Study Box set: broke / left / forgot / lost.

**4. (Level 2)** Your friend says, "I'm really sorry. I spilled your drink." You reply:
a) Don't worry.  b) You are bad.  c) Give me two drinks.
**Answer: a) Don't worry.** — one of Syria's three replies: "It doesn't matter. / It's all right. / Don't worry."

**5. (Level 3)** A company writing to its customers after a bad week opens its apology:
a) We don't care.  b) We are sorry and embarrassed.  c) It was your fault.
**Answer: b) We are sorry and embarrassed.** — the printed apology letter in Nepal's Grade 9 book (Unit 5, printed p.60).
