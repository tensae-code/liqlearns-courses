# Apologising — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> by what the item turns on: a bare sorry's completeness is taught in Sub lesson 2 — Saying what happened, I'm afraid I · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 4 — The apology at full length, in writing.

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** "Sorry" alone, with nothing after it, is a complete apology.
**False** — the books complete it with what happened: "I'm afraid I broke / lost / forgot…" (Syria, Starters 5, Unit 16).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** "I'm afraid I…" begins bad news inside an apology; it does not mean you are frightened.
**True** — it is the polite functional opener (Malaysia, Academy Stars Year 6, p.56; Scheme of Work Year 6, Unit 4, mapped evidence).

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** An apology can only be spoken, never written.
**False** — Syria's unit matches apology letters to their replies, and Nepal's Grade 9 writing work is an apology letter/email (Nepal, Grade 9, Unit 5, pp.52–64).
