# Apologising — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The sorry at three strengths · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Saying what happened, I'm afraid I, except item 3 (a forgiving reply), which is Sub lesson 3 — The replies that forgive · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 (item 4 asks for a reply from Syria's three).

Fill each gap with the right word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** "I'm ______ sorry." (the middle strength)
**Answer: really** — sorry / really sorry / terribly sorry (Syria, Starters 5, Unit 16).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** "I'm ______ I forgot my book." (the opener that completes the apology)
**Answer: afraid** — "I'm afraid I…" (Syria, Starters 5, Unit 16; Malaysia, Academy Stars Year 6, p.56).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** "It doesn't ______." (a reply that forgives)
**Answer: matter** — "It doesn't matter" (Syria, Starters 5, Unit 16 Study Box).

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** "______ worry." (the shortest forgiving reply)
**Answer: Don't** — "Don't worry," beside "It's all right" (Syria, Starters 5, Unit 16).
