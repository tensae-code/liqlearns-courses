# Apologising — matching

Match each apology to its best reply.

**Set 1 (Level 1–2)**
1. I'm terribly sorry. I broke your game. — **It doesn't matter.**
2. I'm sorry. I ate your chocolates. — **Don't worry.**
3. I'm really sorry. I spilled your drink. — **It's all right.**
4. I'm afraid I lost your pen. — **Don't worry. I have another one.**

(The accidents are Syria's role-cards and Nepal's speaking
clues — a broken game, a lost pen, eaten chocolates, a
spilled drink; the replies are Syria's Study Box three:
"It doesn't matter. / It's all right. / Don't worry.")
