# Hands-on — Apologising

You do it, we guide you. Everything here is done in the learner's
real day, with a companion or the Explain Companion.

1. **Syria's role-cards.** Syria's Starters 5 book practises
   apologies from pair role-cards. Play them: one card each —
   a broken computer game, a lost pen, a book left at home,
   forgotten fruit. The owner of the card mimes the accident;
   the partner apologises in full — sorry, at the right
   strength, plus what happened. The owner must answer with
   one of the three replies.
2. **The strength ladder.** Your companion names ten accidents,
   small to big: a late hello, a borrowed pencil kept, a
   spilled drink, a lost book, a broken phone. For each one,
   hold up one, two or three fingers — sorry, really sorry,
   terribly sorry — and say the apology at that strength.
3. **Nepal's practice situations.** Nepal's Grade 9 speaking
   work drills apologising on three clues: eating someone's
   chocolates, spilling a drink, stepping on a toe. Act all
   three, both ways — apologiser and forgiver — until both
   parts come without thinking.
4. **The letter.** Write one real apology letter or email,
   three lines at least: the sorry, what happened, a kind
   ending. Nepal's books carry the apology in letter form —
   Syria's six short letters and Nepal's apology letter/email
   writing both do the same work. Send it if it is owed.
