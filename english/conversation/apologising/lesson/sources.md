**Acceptance count: taught in 5 countries (Malaysia,
Nepal, Syria, South Sudan, Venezuela).** Syria gives apologies a full Grade 5 unit
— strengths of sorry, the "I'm afraid I…" completion, the
three reassuring replies, role-cards and letters — and
recycles "I'm really sorry" in Grade 6. Malaysia teaches
the "I'm afraid I…" apology in its Year 6 pupil's book,
read directly, with the same polite language mapped in
the Year 6 Scheme of Work (mapped evidence, worded as
such). Nepal teaches apologising and responding as paired
speaking work in Grade 9, opens the unit with the
cross-cultural "I'm Sorry" reading and a company apology
letter, sets the apology letter as writing, and its
(DRAFT) teacher's guide carries a seven-lesson apologising
unit of its own.
