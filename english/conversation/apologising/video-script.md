# Video script — Apologising

Super-simple voice. Read slow. The learner hears this
before or beside the written lesson.

---

[Picture: a boy with a broken game]

Oh no. I broke your game.
I must say sorry. Say it well.

I'm sorry.
I'm really sorry.
I'm terribly sorry.
Small thing — sorry. Big thing — terribly sorry.

[Picture: a lost pen, a forgotten book]

Do not stop at sorry. Say what happened.
I'm afraid I lost your pen.
I'm afraid I forgot my book.
I'm afraid I left it at home.

Sorry — and what happened.
Now the other person believes you.

[Picture: a spilled drink, wiped up]

And the answer? It is kind.
It doesn't matter.
It's all right.
Don't worry.

An apology is two people.
One says sorry. One forgives.
Both parts matter.

Now you try.
Break nothing! Just pretend.
Say sorry to me. Say what happened.
I will forgive you. Then we change places.
