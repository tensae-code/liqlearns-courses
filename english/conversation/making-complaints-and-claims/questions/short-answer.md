# Short Answer — Making Complaints and Claims

(Every item is marked with the sub lesson it belongs to: (Sub lesson N). The gate pool for a sub lesson is all of that sub lesson's questions — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the whole set is the pool.)

1. Name three complaint nouns and give an example of one. (Sub lesson 1)
   — Any three of: service problems, delivery issues, poor product quality, high prices, hidden fees, long wait times. Example: hidden fees — the bill had a charge nobody mentioned.
2. What complaint fits a bus that comes an hour late? (Sub lesson 1)
   — A complaint about late public transportation (and a long wait time).
3. Turn it polite: "Your stupid app crashed again!" (Sub lesson 2)
   — Example: "The app crashed three times during my lesson. I would like it fixed, please."
4. What does PRO CONSUMIDOR do? (Sub lesson 2)
   — It protects consumers in the Dominican Republic when a shop or company will not fix a problem.
