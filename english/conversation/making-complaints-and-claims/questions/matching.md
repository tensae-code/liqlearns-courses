Match the word to what it means.

1. long wait times (Sub lesson 1)
2. service problems (Sub lesson 1)
3. delivery issues (Sub lesson 1)
4. poor product quality (Sub lesson 1)
5. high prices (Sub lesson 1)
6. hidden fees (Sub lesson 2)

a) the thing itself is badly made
b) you waited and waited — 45 minutes!
c) extra charges nobody showed you
d) the service does not work as it should
e) it costs too much
f) the order came late, wrong, or never

Answers: 1-b, 2-d, 3-f, 4-a, 5-e, 6-c
