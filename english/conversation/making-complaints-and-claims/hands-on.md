# Hands-On — Making Complaints and Claims

1. **Complaint desk.** In pairs: customer and shopkeeper at a returns desk; the customer must name the problem and the fix they want.
2. **Ranking the complaints.** In groups, rank the everyday complaints (traffic, prices, internet, transport) — most serious first — and defend the order.
3. **Listening check.** Listen to a partner's complaint story and write down: the problem, the wait, the fix asked for.
4. **Claim letter starter.** Draft the first three lines of a claim to consumer protection about a hidden fee.
