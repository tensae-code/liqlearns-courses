# Levels — Making Complaints and Claims

| Level | What changes |
|---|---|
| Sub lesson 1 — Naming the problem | First step into the lesson. Can-do: The complaints vocabulary: long wait times, service problems, delivery issues, poor product quality, high prices, hidden fees — and the everyday scenes that carry them (traffic congestion, late public transportation, slow internet, phone batteries dying, apps crashing). |
| Sub lesson 2 — Complaining politely, claiming fairly | Builds on the sub lessons before it; nothing new is locked behind the earlier work. Can-do: Run the complaint as a conversation — state the problem, keep it polite and respectful, and ask for the fix; and know the claim behind it: consumer protection (PRO CONSUMIDOR defends Dominican consumers). |
