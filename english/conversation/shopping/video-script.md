# Video script — Shopping

Super-simple voice. Read slow. The learner hears this
before or beside the written lesson.

---

Come into the shop.
The assistant smiles.
Can I help you?

Yes.
I want to buy bread, please.
Anything else?
No, thanks.

How many eggs do you want?
Six eggs, please.
How much is the bread?
It is two euros.

Now a clothes shop.
I want this T-shirt, please.
What size?
Medium, please.
Can I try it on?
Yes. The fitting room is there.

It is good. I will take it.
How will you pay?
By card, please.
Here is your receipt.
Thank you.

Now you try.
Be the assistant. Be the customer.
Buy one thing. Buy it well.
