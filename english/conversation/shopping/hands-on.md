# Hands-on — Shopping

You do it, we guide you. Everything here is done with
real things from the home and a companion or the Explain
Companion.

1. **The home shop.** Put five foods on a table — Cambodia
   style: fish, garlic, honey, ice cream, jam (or your own
   five). One person is the assistant: "Can I help you?"
   The customer buys two things, answers "Anything else?"
   and pays with pretend money. Swap.
2. **The price hunt.** Write a price on each thing. The
   customer may only ask questions: "How much is the
   honey?" "How much are the eggs?" Five prices found by
   asking, none by pointing.
3. **The fitting room game.** Three shirts (or three
   scarves) in small, medium and large. The customer asks
   for a size, tries it on — Mongolia's try on — and says
   "Too small", "Too big" or "Just right" before paying
   by card or cash and taking a paper receipt.
4. **The party budget.** Greece's task at home: a list
   for a small party with prices. Add them up, say the
   total aloud, and answer one question truly: is the
   money in the purse enough?
