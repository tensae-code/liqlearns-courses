**Acceptance count: taught in 6 countries (Cambodia,
Mongolia, Greece, Saudi Arabia, Bolivia, China).** Cambodia gives the
conversation its frame and its prices: a whole opening
chapter of shops and shopping ("Can I help you? / I want
to buy …, please / Anything else? / No, thanks"), the
"How many … do you want to buy?" question, and Riel
prices with "How much does … cost?" later in the same
book. Mongolia gives the clothes-shop half: sizes, trying
on, the fitting room, paying by card or cash and the
receipt, inside its clothing unit. Greece gives the
supermarket version — how many against how much over real
food, with a budgeting task in euros. Saudi Arabia stocks
the conversation as a workbook shopping dialogue (Goal
80) in its We Can! 3 set, cited for the goal as filed —
the held copy is the Term 3 extract, and the dialogue's
own text is not reproduced in the filing, so nothing
beyond the goal is claimed from it. China runs the
clothes-shop talk whole in Grade 4 — help, trying on,
sizes, and the too small / too big / just right
judgements — with a dinner-ordering dialogue beside it
and buying-for-plans at Grade 6 (stocked 2026-11-08 by
the re-verification sweep). Other closed
countries join as their books are read (see
READING-QUEUE.md).
