# Shopping — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The assistant opens, Can I help you · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — How many, for counted things · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 5 — Paying and the receipt.

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** "Anything else?" asks if you want one
more thing.
**True** — it is the assistant's check before paying
(Cambodia, Chapter 1).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** "How much eggs do you want?" is good
English.
**False** — eggs are counted, so the question is "How
many eggs…?"; how much is for prices and for things you
weigh or pour (Greece, Unit 9; Cambodia, Chapter 1,
Unit 4).

**3. (Sub lesson 5)** After paying, the shop gives you a
receipt.
**True** — the receipt is in Mongolia's shopping
vocabulary set, with cash, the debit card and the
checkout (English X, Unit 5).
