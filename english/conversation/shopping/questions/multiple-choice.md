# Shopping — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** You walk into a shop. The assistant
says —
a) Can I help you?  b) Anything is a shop  c) Buy is me
**Answer: a) Can I help you?** — the line that opens
Cambodia's whole shopping chapter (Grade 5, Chapter 1).

**2. (Level 1)** You want some bread. You say —
a) I want to buy bread, please  b) Bread is wanting me
c) Give breading
**Answer: a)** — Cambodia's customer frame, Chapter 1,
Unit 1.

**3. (Level 2)** For eggs, the right question is —
a) How much eggs?  b) How many eggs do you want?
c) How cost eggs?
**Answer: b)** — counted things take how many (Cambodia,
Chapter 1, Unit 4; Greece, Unit 9).

**4. (Level 3)** The shirt is too small. You say —
a) It doesn't fit. Can I try the medium?  b) The size is
sleeping  c) I pay the fitting room
**Answer: a)** — sizes and trying on are Mongolia's
clothes-shop dialogue work (English X, Unit 5).
