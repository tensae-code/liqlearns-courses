# Shopping — matching

Match each line to the person who says it.

**Set 1 (Level 1–3)**
1. Can I help you? — **the assistant**
2. I want to buy bread, please — **the customer**
3. How much is it? — **the customer**
4. Anything else? — **the assistant**

(The full frame — offer, want, how many, how much, size,
paying, receipt — is Cambodia's Chapter 1, Mongolia's
Unit 5 and Greece's Unit 9 working together.)
