# Shopping — fill in the blank

Fill each gap with the right words.

**1. (Level 1)** "Can I ______ you?" — "I want to ______
jam, please."
**Answer: help / buy** — Cambodia's opening pair,
Chapter 1, Unit 1.

**2. (Level 2)** "______ many eggs do you want?" —
"______ much is the honey?"
**Answer: How / How** — how many counts, how much
prices and weighs (Cambodia; Greece, Unit 9).

**3. (Level 2)** "An orange ______ 900 Riels."
**Answer: costs** — Cambodia's price sentence, Chapter 4,
Unit 6.

**4. (Level 3)** "Can I ______ it on?" — "Yes, the
______ room is there."
**Answer: try / fitting** — Mongolia's clothes-shop
words, English X, Unit 5.
