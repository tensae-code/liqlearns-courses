# Shopping — short answer

Answer in whole sentences.

**1. (Level 1)** The assistant asks "Anything else?"
You want nothing more. Answer politely.
**Answer: No, thanks.** (Cambodia's closing line,
Chapter 1.)

**2. (Level 2)** Ask the price of one orange, the
Cambodia way.
**Answer: How much does an orange cost?** (Chapter 4,
Unit 6 — its answer in the book is "An orange costs
900 Riels.")

**3. (Level 3)** You have tried on the T-shirt and it
fits. Tell the assistant you will buy it and that you
will pay by card.
**Answer: I'll take it. By card, please.** (Mongolia's
shop dialogue runs on sizes and paying by card or cash,
English X, Unit 5.)
