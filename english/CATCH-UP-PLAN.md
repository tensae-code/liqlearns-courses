# English CATCH-UP PLAN — everything the read books teach goes in

Built 2026-11-06/07 under his ruling (README, commit 17e6715):
"blend everything into one course… I do not care if they teach it
or not… So do not leave out anything. Any book." The old practice
of holding "unkeyed" stock aside is REVOKED. Every teaching item
in every read book lands in the course; a topic with no lesson
gets a NEW lesson; a country's own way of teaching a thing is
content too (kept as examples in that lesson).

## How this plan was made

Six inventory sweeps mined all 42 country filings in
english/books/ (+ EGYPT-BOOK-MAP.md) for topics the current 51
lessons do NOT cover: (1) Egypt map + Iraq/China/Kazakhstan,
(2) Armenia/Afghanistan/Ethiopia/Ghana/South Sudan/Syria,
(3) India/Ecuador/Jordan/Sierra Leone, (4) Indonesia/Bangladesh/
Greece, (5) Libya/Laos, (6) the remaining 23 countries (Albania,
Barbados, Bolivia, Cambodia, Kuwait, Malaysia, Mauritania,
Mexico, Mongolia, Nepal, Nicaragua, North Macedonia, Pakistan,
Palestine, Rwanda, Saudi Arabia, South Africa, Sudan, Tanzania,
Uzbekistan, Venezuela, Vietnam, Yemen). Total uncovered topic
blocks found: **~250** across grammar, writing, vocabulary,
conversation and other. Merged and de-duplicated below into
**110 new lessons**.

## Placement decisions (coordinator's call, per the brief)

- **Vocabulary sets → new lessons inside Level 7** (Words for
  the World), folder `word/`. A set is one thing; Level 7 is
  where sets already live (the-home, the-town…).
- **Conversation functions → new lessons inside Level 5**
  (Conversation), folder `conversation/`.
- **Pronunciation extensions → inside Level 3** (Sounds
  Together), folder `phonics/`. **Joined-up writing → inside
  Level 2**, folder `alphabet/`. **Ordering words → inside
  Level 8** (Using the Dictionary), folder `word/`.
- **Everything else → new levels 19–44.** A level here is a
  small family of lessons on one rung (the Level 18 pattern:
  4 lessons); every lesson is still one thing, stands alone
  (DIRECT-ENTRY rule — prerequisites are soft hints only), and
  carries its own questions and sources.
- **Sub-points that only deepen an existing lesson's own
  topic** get no new lesson; they are listed at the end as
  EXTENSION STOCK mapped to the lesson they deepen, with their
  filing evidence, so nothing is lost and nothing is
  double-taught.
- Specialist sector vocabulary (Greece ESP: hotels, offices,
  tourism, maritime) becomes ONE Level 7 lesson,
  `working-words`, flagged specialist — the ruling is
  everything goes in.
- Standing flags honoured in every build: Libya filing text
  carries the known scrub note and its lessons cite Libya
  under that banner; NOTHING is cited as Libya Grade 4 (those
  records are Grade 3 books re-covered); Ghana evidence is
  NaCCA curriculum only (no pupil textbook exists); Malaysia
  primary evidence marked "mapped" (Scheme of Work) is worded
  as mapped, never as a pupil's-book read; Venezuela evidence
  is teacher's-guide only and worded so; South Sudan S3 is
  TG-only; book errors and wrong guide keys (Rwanda P.5 TG
  keys, Ethiopia G9 TG antonym key, South Africa HL G6 future
  perfect rule, Afghanistan G11 "used to" rule) are taught
  corrected or cited nowhere.
