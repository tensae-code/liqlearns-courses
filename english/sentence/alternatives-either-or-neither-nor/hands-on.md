# Hands-on — Alternatives — Either…Or, Neither…Nor

## The stall

A classroom stall holds pairs — two fruits, two drinks, two books. Buyers must speak only in the lesson's frames: either…or to choose, neither…nor to refuse a pair, and one whether sentence to the class about a choice they have not made yet.

## Two doors

Two doors of the classroom are labelled with the day's two options (inside games or outside games, story or songs). Each learner passes through a door only after saying their either…or sentence aloud; those who refuse both must say their neither…nor sentence and stand between the doors.
