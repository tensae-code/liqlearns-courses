# Lesson plan — Alternatives — Either…Or, Neither…Nor

**Course:** English (Zero to Story)
**Title:** Alternatives — Either…Or, Neither…Nor
**Lesson:** alternatives-either-or-neither-nor — naming the choices: either…or, neither…nor, whether or if
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 31 — Linking and Fixing Sentences
**The one thing:** The alternative frames: either…or (one of the two), neither…nor (not one and not the other), and whether/if for naming a choice.
**This lesson's own level:** Sentence lesson (alternatives): either…or joining two choices; neither…nor refusing both; whether/if after verbs like ask/know/decide; alternative and possibility as the naming words.

## Why this lesson exists

Mauritania's Grade 13 book teaches the alternatives as their own lesson — either…or, neither…nor in its reading text, whether/if — and the course's linking unit held connectors, clefts and object clauses but no alternatives lesson. This lesson is built from the placements under the everything-goes-in ruling (2026-11-06).

## Sub lessons

1. Either…or — one of the two
2. Neither…nor — not one, not the other
3. Whether or if — naming the choice

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
Nothing is needed before this lesson. Connectors and Clauses joins with and/but/so; this lesson joins choices, and each stands alone.

## What the learner can do when it ends
- Offer a choice: "I will have either a coke or a fanta."
- Refuse both: "He neither lives at a residence nor has easy circumstances."
- Name a choice: "I don't know whether to walk or ride."

## What you need
Nothing special — two drinks, two roads, two plans.

## The run of the lesson
1. ADD IMAGE: a learner at a stall with two bottles, coke and fanta, hand hovering. The learner tries the choice sentence before any rule appears.
2. Teach sub lesson 1 with real classroom choices: this pen or that pen, now or later.
3. ADD GIF (1–2 seconds): two doors both closing — neither…nor shuts both.
4. Sub lesson 3 gathers the class's open choices (whether to…) into one list on the board.

## Add-ons
- Choice market: sellers may only offer with either…or; buyers may refuse only with neither…nor.
- Open questions wall: every learner pins one whether-question they truly hold; the class reads them aloud.

## If the learner is stuck
Keep to either…or with two nouns until the choice frame is automatic. Neither…nor is the mirror step; whether comes last.

## Teacher note
The Grade 13 book teaches the alternatives inside a reading text about choices and possibilities — the frames are drawn out of real sentences, and the lesson keeps that: frames first on the book's own examples, then on the learner's choices.
