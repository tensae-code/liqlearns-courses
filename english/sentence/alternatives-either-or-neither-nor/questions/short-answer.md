## Short answer

1. Offer me tea or coffee in one sentence. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: "I will have either tea or coffee."** — either…or.

2. Refuse both the bus and the train in one sentence. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: "Neither the bus nor the train came."** — neither…nor, no second negative.

3. Say you do not know whether to stay or go. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: "I don't know whether to stay or go."** — whether + or names the choice.

4. Ask the teacher if she is coming, with if. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: "Ask her if she is coming." / "Is she coming? — I asked if she is coming."** — if after ask.

5. Say: not the teacher, not the students — nobody was there. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: "Neither the teacher nor the students were there."** — the verb follows the nearer side (students → were).
