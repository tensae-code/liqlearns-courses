## Fill in the blank

1. "___ tea or juice — choose." (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: Either** — either opens the choice.

2. "I will have either a coke ___ a fanta." (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: or** — either…or.

3. "___ the rain nor the wind stopped us." (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: Neither** — neither refuses both.

4. "Neither rain ___ wind can stop us." (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: nor** — neither…nor.

5. "Tell me ___ you are coming or not." (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: whether** — whether…or not.

6. "This idea is another ___." (a choice-thing) (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: alternative** — the naming word.
