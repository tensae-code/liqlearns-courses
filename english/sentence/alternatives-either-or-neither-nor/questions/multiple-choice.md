## Multiple choice

1. "I will have ___ a coke or a fanta." (Sub lesson 1)
   a) either
   b) neither
   c) whether
   **Answer: a) either** — either…or leaves the choice open.

2. "He ___ lives at a residence nor has easy circumstances." (Sub lesson 2)
   a) either
   b) neither
   c) whether
   **Answer: b) neither** — neither…nor refuses both.

3. "Neither the bus ___ the train came." (Sub lesson 2)
   a) or
   b) nor
   c) and
   **Answer: b) nor** — neither pairs with nor.

4. "I don't know ___ to walk or ride." (Sub lesson 3)
   a) either
   b) whether
   c) nor
   **Answer: b) whether** — whether names the choice and carries its or.

5. "Ask him ___ he is coming." (Sub lesson 3)
   a) if
   b) nor
   c) either
   **Answer: a) if** — if/whether name a yes-no choice after ask.

6. A choice itself, as a thing, is called: (Sub lesson 3)
   a) a connector
   b) an alternative
   c) a refusal
   **Answer: b) an alternative** — the book's naming word.

7. "Either ___ or fanta — choose one." (Sub lesson 1)
   a) coke
   b) nor coke
   c) whether coke
   **Answer: a) coke** — either + first choice, or + second.
