## Matching

Match each frame to its work. (Sub lessons 1–3)

1. either…or — a
2. neither…nor — b
3. whether…or — c
4. if (after ask) — d
5. an alternative — e

a) refusing both choices
b) naming a choice as a thing
c) one of two will happen
d) naming an open choice
e) naming a yes-no choice after a verb

**Answer key with reasons:** 1–c (one of the two), 2–a (both refused), 3–d (whether names the open choice), 4–e (ask if…), 5–b (alternative = the choice itself).
