# Video script — Alternatives — Either…Or, Neither…Nor

Super-simple voice. The presenter is the LiqLearns mascot, front-facing,
on screen the whole video; the pictures named here appear beside him.

[The mascot holds a coke in one paw and a fanta in the other.] "EITHER a coke OR a fanta!"

[The mascot pushes both bottles away gently.] "NEITHER this NOR that!"

[The mascot scratches his head at two roads.] "I don't know WHETHER to walk or ride!"
