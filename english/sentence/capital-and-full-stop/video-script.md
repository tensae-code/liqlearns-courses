# Video script — Capital letter and full stop

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Look — a bare sentence. No hat, no shoes!
"the cow eats grass"

Give it a hat! **T** — the first letter stands TALL.
"The cow eats grass"
Give it shoes! A **full stop** at the end.
"The cow eats grass**.**"

Every sentence wears them:
a CAPITAL to open… a FULL STOP to close.

And names? Names are tall EVERYWHERE.
**Mita** is tall. **Dhaka** is tall.
**Friday** is tall. **January** is tall!
But "girl"… "city"… small. They are kinds, not names.

A question? It wears a hook: **?**
"What's the time**?**"

Now you. Dress your sentences!
Tall at the start. A stop at the end.
That is writing — in English.
