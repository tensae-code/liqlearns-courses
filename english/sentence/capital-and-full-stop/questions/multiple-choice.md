# Capital letter and full stop — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** Which line is dressed right?
a) the cow eats grass.  b) The cow eats grass  c) The cow
eats grass.
**Answer: c)** — capital opens, full stop closes (Ghana's
strand; Bangladesh's box).

**2. (Level 1)** A statement closes with a ______.
a) question mark  b) full stop  c) comma
**Answer: b) full stop** (Ghana, B2.5.2.1).

**3. (Level 2)** Which word must stand tall in "my friend
lives in dhaka"?
a) friend  b) lives  c) dhaka
**Answer: c) dhaka** — a place NAME (Bangladesh's grammar
focus).

**4. (Level 2)** Which word stays small?
a) Mita  b) girl  c) Friday
**Answer: b) girl** — a kind, not a name; Mita and Friday
are names and wear capitals.

**5. (Level 2)** Ghana's strand gives capitals to days of
the week. Rightly dressed is —
a) on friday we play  b) on Friday we play  c) On friday We
play
**Answer: b)** — Friday tall as a name; we small mid-sentence.

**6. (Level 3)** "what's the time" closes with —
a) .  b) !  c) ?
**Answer: c) ?** — question marks close questions (Ghana's
pair).
