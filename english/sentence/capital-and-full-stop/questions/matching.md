# Capital letter and full stop — matching

Match each item to its dressing rule.

**Set 1 (Level 1)** — the marks.
1. end of a statement — **full stop ( . )**
2. end of a question — **question mark ( ? )**
3. start of a sentence — **capital letter**
4. inside a list — **comma ( , ) — shown in Bangladesh's
   first exercise; its own lesson comes later**

**Set 2 (Level 2)** — tall or small?
1. Sylhet (a place) — **tall**
2. city — **small (a kind)**
3. Pavel (a person) — **tall**
4. monday → Monday (a day) — **tall**
5. month (the kind word) — **small**
6. October (a month's name) — **tall**

**Set 3 (Level 3)** — which rule?
1. "The" opening a page — **first word of a sentence**
2. "Nini" mid-sentence — **a name (Bangladesh's cat)**
3. "." after "grass" — **end of a sentence**
4. "Friday" mid-sentence — **a name (day of the week —
   Ghana's strand)**
