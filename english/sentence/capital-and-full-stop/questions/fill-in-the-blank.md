# Capital letter and full stop — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the mark or word the dressing needs.

**Box:** capital · full stop · question mark · tall ·
small · names

**1. (Level 1)** The first word of a sentence begins with a
______ letter.
**Answer: capital.**

**2. (Level 1)** A statement ends with a ______.
**Answer: full stop.**

**3. (Level 1)** A question ends with a ______.
**Answer: question mark.**

**4. (Level 2)** Persons, places, days, and months are
______ — they wear capitals anywhere.
**Answer: names.**

**5. (Level 2)** In "Mita is a girl," Mita stands ______ and
girl stays ______.
**Answer: tall — small** (name vs kind, Bangladesh's box).

**6. (Level 3)** The dressing has two edges and one hunt:
open tall, close with a stop, and grow the ______.
**Answer: names.**
