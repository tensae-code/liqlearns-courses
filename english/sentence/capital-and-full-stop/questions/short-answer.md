# Capital letter and full stop — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Two Edges · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The Names · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Whole Texts.
Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** State the two edges of a written sentence.
**Answer:** a capital letter opens it; a full stop closes
it (a question mark closes a question).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Dress it: "the tiger eats meat"
**Answer:** "The tiger eats meat."

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the four kinds of names this lesson
dresses with capitals.
**Answer:** persons, places, days of the week, months
(Bangladesh's focus box; Ghana's strand; the months rule).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Dress it: "orpa has a cat called nini"
**Answer:** "Orpa has a cat called Nini." — Orpa and Nini
are names; cat is a kind (Bangladesh's own cat, Class 2).

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Dress the whole run: "january is cold in
my town school starts on monday"
**Answer:** "January is cold in my town. School starts on
Monday." — two sentences found and dressed: January,
School, Monday tall; two stops.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** One of Bangladesh's texts helps the learner
see the rule — how?
**Answer:** it prints the first letter of every sentence in
red (Class 3, the cleanliness text).
