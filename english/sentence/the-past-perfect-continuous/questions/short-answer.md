# The past perfect continuous — short answer

Answer in a word or a whole sentence, as asked.

**1. (Level 1)** Why was the ground wet when you woke? Answer with this tense. **Answer: It had been raining (all night)** — any true stretch that explains the past mark earns it.

**2. (Level 2)** Say what you had been doing for an hour before your last meal. **Answer: (Learner's own true sentence in had been + -ing + for)** — the stretch and its measure must both stand.

**3. (Level 3)** Ask your companion how long they had been waiting when the lesson began yesterday. **Answer: How long had you been waiting when the lesson began?** — had first for the question; the past moment anchors the stretch.
