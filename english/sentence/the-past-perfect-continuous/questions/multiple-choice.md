# The past perfect continuous — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Past Perfect Continuous Shape · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Past Perfect Continuous Stretch and Measures · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Past Perfect Continuous Marks and Twin Border.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** "She ______ for an hour before the bus came." a) had been waiting  b) has wait  c) had waited three buses **Answer: a) had been waiting** — a stretch moving up to a past moment wears had been + -ing.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In "had been running," the piece that never changes in any sentence is — a) had only  b) been  c) the verb **Answer: b) been** — had carries the tense; been is fixed in every perfect continuous.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** "He was tired because he ______ all morning." a) had been working  b) works yesterday  c) has been work **Answer: a) had been working** — Egypt's own seat for the tense: a past mark explained by the stretch before it.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Which sentence counts a FINISHED step, not a stretch? a) She had been writing for an hour.  b) She had written three letters.  c) She had been writing since nine. **Answer: b)** — three letters is a done count — the past perfect simple; the others measure a moving stretch.
