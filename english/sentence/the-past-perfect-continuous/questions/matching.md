# The past perfect continuous — matching

Match each item to its partner.

**1. (Level 1)** tired eyes at night, books everywhere **Answer: had been studying** — a past mark is explained by the stretch that had been going on.

**2. (Level 2)** "for an hour" beside "three letters finished" **Answer: "for an hour" — this tense; "three letters" — the past perfect simple** — length belongs to the continuous; a finished count belongs to its simple twin.

**3. (Level 3)** Venezuela's place in this lesson's sources **Answer: teacher's-guide evidence (My Victory, Grade 11 guide, Lesson 14)** — Venezuela is held as a guide only for this grade and is worded so, never as a pupil's-book read.
