# The past perfect continuous — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Past Perfect Continuous Shape · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Past Perfect Continuous Stretch and Measures · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Past Perfect Continuous Marks and Twin Border.

Fill each gap with the right shape.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** When I arrived, they ______ (play) for two hours. **Answer: had been playing** — had been + -ing; the playing stretched up to my arrival.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** He ______ (not sleep) well for weeks before the trip. (negative) **Answer: hadn't been sleeping** — the not folds after had, before been.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** It ______ (rain) since morning, so the ground was wet when we left. **Answer: had been raining** — since names the start of the stretch, aimed at the past moment of leaving.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** How long ______ you ______ (wait) when the door finally opened? **Answer: had … been waiting** — had steps forward to ask; the question asks the stretch's length.
