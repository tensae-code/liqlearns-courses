# The past perfect continuous — true or false

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Level 1)** "Had been" changes to "hads been" for he and she. **Answer: False** — had and been are both fixed; only the main verb wears -ing.

**2. (Level 2)** "She had been running for an hour" can explain why she was exhausted at that past time. **Answer: True** — Egypt's box gives exactly this seat — duration up to a past point, with its mark showing.

**3. (Level 3)** The past perfect continuous is the right coat for ONE single completed earlier step, like locking a door. **Answer: False** — a single completed step wears the past perfect simple (had locked); this tense is for a moving stretch.
