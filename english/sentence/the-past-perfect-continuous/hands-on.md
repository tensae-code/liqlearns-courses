# Hands-on — The past perfect continuous

You do it, we guide you. Everything here is done in the
learner's real day, with a companion or the Explain
Companion. The TARGET forms are always said in English;
the talk around them may be in the learner's own language.

1. **The before-the-door report.** Pick three real past moments (the bus came, dinner was served, the match ended). For each, report what had been going on before it, with for or since. The companion asks "how long?" — every answer must carry its measure.
2. **Read the marks, past edition.** Hunt three marks from earlier today or yesterday — a tired face, a wet yard, flour on hands — and explain each in the past: "His hands were white because he had been painting."
3. **The twin drill.** Take one verb (write, cook, study). Say it both ways about yesterday: the count (had written two pages) and the stretch (had been writing for an hour). Four verbs; the companion names which question each sentence answers — how many, or how long.
