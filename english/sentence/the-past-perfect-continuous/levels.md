# The past perfect continuous — questions by level

Practice by level, tied to the skill number. The questions live in
the type files; this file is the by-level view. The levels are the
depths (RANKS) inside this one thing — a stretch still moving up to a past point — set by the
pattern across the countries' books: the shape first, then its job,
then its borders with its neighbours.

## Level 1 — the shape
Level 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: build had been + verb-ing for every who, statements, not-forms and questions — had and been fixed, the -ing spellings right.

## Level 2 — the stretch and its measures
Level 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: answer "How long had you been …?" about a real past moment, placing for (length) and since (starting point) aimed at that past point without a swap.

## Level 3 — the marks and the twin border
Level 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: explain a past mark with a just-stopped stretch (She was exhausted because she had been running), and choose live between this tense and the past perfect — stretch versus finished count.
