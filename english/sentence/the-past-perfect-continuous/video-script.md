# Video script — The past perfect continuous

Super-simple voice. Read slow. The learner hears this
before or beside the written lesson.

---

Yesterday, at six, I was tired.

Why? I had been working all day.

Not finished and counted. Moving. For hours.

That is today's shape.

Had. Been. And the verb with -ing.

I had been waiting.

She had been cooking.

They had been playing.

Had never changes. Been never changes.

How long?

For two hours — the length.

Since morning — the start.

But the end of the stretch is in the PAST.

When the guests arrived, we had been cooking since morning.

She was exhausted because she had been running for an hour.

The action stopped. Its mark stayed.

A finished step? That is had + done: had left.

A moving stretch? Had been + -ing.

Now you try.

One past moment. What had been going on before it?
