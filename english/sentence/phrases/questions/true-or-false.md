## True or false

1. A phrase can stand alone as a complete sentence. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: False** — it has no subject and verb of its own.

2. A phrase can be two words or six words long. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: True** — the job, not the count, makes it one piece.

3. Every phrase in a sentence does the same job. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: False** — each kind does its own job: naming, describing, acting, or telling where/when/how.

4. "Has kicked" is a verb phrase. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: True.**

5. A noun phrase names the person or thing. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: True.**
