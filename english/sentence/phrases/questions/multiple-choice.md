## Multiple choice

1. What is a phrase? (Sub lesson 1)
   a) One word  b) A group of words that works as one piece inside a sentence  c) A whole book
   **Answer: b)** — it does one job together.

2. Why can a phrase not stand alone as a sentence? (Sub lesson 1)
   a) It is too short  b) It has no subject and verb of its own  c) It has no full stop
   **Answer: b)** — a phrase lives inside a sentence.

3. "The tall boy" in "The tall boy kicked the ball" is a… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) Verb phrase  b) Noun phrase  c) Adverbial phrase
   **Answer: b) Noun phrase** — it does the noun's job: it names who.

4. "Into the net" in "He kicked it into the net" is a… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) Adjectival phrase  b) Noun phrase  c) Adverbial phrase
   **Answer: c) Adverbial phrase** — it tells where.

5. "Has been working" is a… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) Verb phrase  b) Adjectival phrase  c) Noun phrase
   **Answer: a) Verb phrase** — the action words together.
