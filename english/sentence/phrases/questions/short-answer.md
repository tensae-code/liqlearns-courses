## Short answer

1. What test tells you a word-group is a phrase? (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer:** It works as one piece doing one job, and it has no subject and verb of its own.

2. Name the four kinds of phrases. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer:** Noun phrases, adjectival phrases, verb phrases and adverbial phrases.

3. In "My little sister with the broken cup ran to the door", pick out two phrases and name their kinds. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer:** "My little sister" — noun phrase; "with the broken cup" — adjectival phrase; "to the door" — adverbial phrase (any two).

4. Grow "The dog slept" with one adverbial phrase. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer:** Any where/when/how phrase added, e.g. "The dog slept under the mango tree."
