## Fill in the blank

1. "A phrase is a group of words that works as one ___." (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: piece.**

2. "A phrase has no ___ and verb of its own." (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: subject.**

3. "A phrase that does a noun's job is a ___ phrase." (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: noun.**

4. "'In the red shirt' describes the boy, so it is an ___ phrase." (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: adjectival.**

5. "A phrase that tells where, when or how is an ___ phrase." (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: adverbial.**
