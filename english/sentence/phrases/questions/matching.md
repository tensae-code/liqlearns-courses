## Matching

Match the phrase to its kind. (Sub lesson 2)

1. the tall boy — b
2. in the red shirt — d
3. has been working — a
4. into the net — c
5. all day long — c

a) Verb phrase
b) Noun phrase
c) Adverbial phrase
d) Adjectival phrase

**Answers:** 1–b (it names who), 2–d (it describes), 3–a (the action
words together), 4–c (it tells where), 5–c (it tells how long — when).
