# Hands-on — Phrases

## Bracket it

Write one long sentence on the board. In pairs, bracket its
phrases and label each with its kind. Pairs compare: a bracket
both pairs found is almost surely a phrase.

## Grow the sentence

Start: "The girl sang." Each partner in turn adds ONE phrase
— any kind — and says its kind aloud. The sentence must still
stand when read whole. Six phrases is a strong sentence.
