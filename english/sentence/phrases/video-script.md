# Video script — Phrases

Super-simple voice. The presenter is the LiqLearns mascot,
front-facing, on screen the whole video; the pictures named here
appear beside him.

[A sentence appears, its word-groups lighting up one by one.]
"Some words travel in GROUPS!"

[One group glows.] "A group that works as ONE piece is a…
PHRASE!"

[Four groups glow in four colours.] "Noun phrases.
Adjectival phrases. Verb phrases. Adverbial phrases."

[Back to the mascot.] "Find the phrases. Name their jobs.
You can take a sentence apart!"
