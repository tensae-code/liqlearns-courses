# Hands-on — Reported questions

You do it, we guide you. Everything here is done with a
companion or the Explain Companion.

1. **The interview report.** The companion plays a
   visitor being interviewed (the Mauritania Lesson 14
   shape: a job interview; Sudan's Chapter Two shape:
   Rachel's interview retold to her family). Five
   questions — two yes/no, three wh-. The learner then
   reports the whole interview to a third person or the
   wall: He asked if… / She asked where… Every question
   mark the learner writes costs a point.
2. **The two doors.** Cards with eight yes/no questions.
   The learner reports four through the if door and four
   through the whether door, then picks two where whether
   feels better (a choice, an or not) and says why.
3. **Straighten the train.** The companion reads mixed-up
   reports: "He asked where do I live", "She asked if
   did I see it." The learner must stop the train, take
   out the helper, and lay the cars straight — where I
   live; if I had seen it.
4. **The cushion round.** Blunt questions on cards
   (Where is the office? What time do you close? Why is
   this so expensive?) are handed to three "strangers":
   a teacher, an elder, an official. The learner wraps
   each one — I was wondering…, Could I ask you…?, Do you
   have any idea…? — the Cambodia Grade 12 frames, with
   the straight order kept inside.
