**Acceptance count: taught in 9 countries (Cambodia,
China, Kuwait, Mauritania, Nepal, Sudan, Vietnam, Indonesia, India).** India joins with reporting questions, as a teacher-note point in Class 8 (stocked 2026-11-09 by the India re-verification sweep merge).
Mauritania splits the lesson exactly as this lesson
does: wh- questions in Lesson 12, yes/no questions with
if in Lesson 14, each with its own correspondence chart.
Kuwait states the whole rule set (if/whether, wh-,
statement order, no question mark) in one grammar pass —
and teaches no other reported speech. Cambodia supplies
the polite frames at Grade 12 and the conversion work at
Grade 11 (teacher's-book spreads; the student's book is
not held). Nepal opens its reported-speech ladder with
yes/no questions. Sudan's SPINE 6 and Vietnam's Grade 9
are honestly narrow witnesses, and their limits are
stated where they are cited: SPINE 6 reports questions
only, never statements; Vietnam's Grade 9 book reports
yes/no questions only. China is a cousin witness, worded
as filed: it teaches the same straight order as object
clauses inside polite requests and never teaches
reported speech. Indonesia teaches reported questions in
its Grade 9 time-travel chapter (stocked 2026-11-09 by
the re-verification sweep merge).
