# Reported questions — matching

Match each direct question to its report.

**1. (Level 1)** "Do you speak English?" **Answer: He
asked if I spoke English.** — a yes/no question hangs
on if, with the present stepped back to the past.

**2. (Level 2)** "What are you doing?" **Answer: She
asked what I was doing.** — the wh- word in front, then
straight words, are doing stepped back to was doing.

**3. (Level 3)** "Would you like tea or coffee?" (the
host asks you — report it to a friend) **Answer: The
host asked whether I wanted tea or coffee.** — where a
choice stands in the question, whether is the door to
pick.
