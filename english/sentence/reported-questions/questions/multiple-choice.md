# Reported questions — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Reported Questions Yes/No Reports · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Reported Questions Wh- Reports · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Reported Questions Polite Frames.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Direct: "Do you like tea?" Reported:
a) He asked if I like tea.  b) He asked if I liked tea.
c) He asked did I like tea?
**Answer: b)** — if carries the yes/no question, the
words run straight, and the tense steps back
(Kuwait, Pearls 9B, p.45).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Direct: "Are you ready?" Reported:
a) She asked whether I was ready.  b) She asked whether
was I ready.  c) She asked whether I am ready?
**Answer: a)** — whether is the second door for a yes/no
report; statement order after it, backshift, no question
mark.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Direct: "Where do you live?" Reported:
a) He asked where do I live.  b) He asked where I live
did.  c) He asked where I lived.
**Answer: c)** — the wh- word stays in front and the
rest runs straight, one step back (Mauritania, Lesson
12, pp.38–39).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Direct: "What did you buy?" Reported:
a) She asked what I had bought.  b) She asked what did I
buy.  c) She asked what I buy yesterday.
**Answer: a)** — did drops away on the bridge and the
past perfect carries the past: bought → had bought.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Ask a stranger politely where the market
is.
a) Where is the market?  b) I was wondering where the
market is.  c) I was wondering where is the market.
**Answer: b)** — the polite frame wraps the question,
and inside the cushion the order stays straight
(Cambodia, Grade 12, Unit 3).
