# Reported questions — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Reported Questions Yes/No Reports · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Reported Questions Wh- Reports · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Reported Questions Polite Frames.

Fill each gap with the right shape.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** "Can you swim?" → He asked ______
I could swim.
**Answer: if (whether is also right)** — a yes/no
question hangs on if or whether; both doors open to the
same room.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** "Did you see the match?" → She asked if
I ______ seen the match.
**Answer: had** — did drops away and the past steps back
to the past perfect, carried by had.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** "When will you come?" → They asked
______ I would come.
**Answer: when** — the wh- word itself is the hook of a
wh- report; will steps back to would behind it.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** "Could I ______ you where the office
is?" — the polite frame for asking a stranger.
**Answer: ask** — Could I ask you…? is one of Cambodia
Grade 12's three polite frames (Unit 3), with statement
order inside.
