# Video script — Wish and if only

Super-simple voice. Read slow. The learner hears this
before or beside the written lesson.

---

Some things are not true. But you want them.

It is raining. You want sun.
Say it with WISH.

I wish it weren't raining.
The rain is real. The wish is not real.

Wish uses the past coat.
I wish I knew the answer. I do not know it now.
I wish I ate more vegetables. I do not eat them now.
If only I had more time. If only is a strong wish.

Now a wish about yesterday.
Yesterday is finished. It cannot change.
I wish I had left earlier. But I left late.
If only I hadn't done it.
Wish, and HAD, and the third shape. That is the past wish.

Now a wish for a change.
You want somebody to stop. Or start.
I wish you would listen to me.
I wish the lesson would end.
I wish you wouldn't borrow my clothes.
Wish and WOULD ask the world to change.

And a wish for a power you do not have.
I wish I could swim.
I wish I could fly.
Wish and COULD name the missing power.

Three wishes. Three coats.
Now — past coat.
Then — had coat.
Change — would. Power — could.

Now you try.
Say one wish about now.
Say one wish about yesterday.
Say one wish with would.
