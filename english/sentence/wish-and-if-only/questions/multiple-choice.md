# Wish and if only — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Wishes About Now · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Wishes About a Finished Day · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Wish + Would — a Wanted Change, except items about a missing ability, which are Sub lesson 4 — Wish + Could — a Missing Ability · and the item asking whether if only is a different shape, which is Sub lesson 5 — If Only — the Same Wishes Said Stronger.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** "I wish I ______ more vegetables, but I don't."
a) eat  b) ate  c) will eat
**Answer: b) ate** — a present wish wears the past simple coat (Bolivia, Year 6).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** "If only it ______ Friday today!"
a) is  b) was / were  c) will be
**Answer: b) was / were** — wishes take the past coat, with were in careful English, for a wish about now.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** "I wish I ______ that letter yesterday."
a) didn't send  b) hadn't sent  c) don't send
**Answer: b) hadn't sent** — a regret about a finished day takes had + participle (Bolivia).

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** "I wish you ______ my clothes without asking!"
a) wouldn't borrow  b) don't borrow  c) hadn't borrow
**Answer: a) wouldn't borrow** — wish + would asks somebody to stop an annoying thing (Bolivia's own example).

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** "I wish I ______ swim like my brother."
a) would  b) could  c) will
**Answer: b) could** — wish + could names your own missing ability (Malaysia, Form 4).
