# Wish and if only — true or false

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Level 1)** In "I wish I knew", the word knew means the knowing happened in the past.
**Answer: False** — the past coat marks NOT REAL, not past time; the wish is about now.

**2. (Level 1)** If only is a different grammar shape from wish.
**Answer: False** — if only is the same three shapes said stronger; Malaysia teaches wish and if only as one family (Form 4).

**3. (Level 2)** "I wish I hadn't done it" is a wish about a finished day.
**Answer: True** — had + participle is the past wish (Bolivia's own example).

**4. (Level 3)** "I wish I would swim" is the right way to wish for a swimming ability you do not have.
**Answer: False** — your own missing ability takes could: "I wish I could swim." Would points out at the world and other people.
