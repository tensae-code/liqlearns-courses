# Wish and if only — short answer

Answer in whole sentences.

**1. (Level 1)** It is raining now and you want sun. Say the wish.
**Answer: I wish it weren't raining. / If only it weren't raining.** — past coat, present meaning.

**2. (Level 2)** Yesterday you forgot your umbrella and got wet. Say the past wish.
**Answer: I wish I had taken my umbrella. / If only I hadn't forgotten it.** — had + participle for the finished day.

**3. (Level 3)** Your brother keeps taking your pen. Say the would wish, kindly.
**Answer: I wish you wouldn't take my pen. / I wish you would ask first.** — wish + would for a change (Bolivia's pattern).

**4. (Level 3)** You cannot ride a bicycle and you want to. Say the wish.
**Answer: I wish I could ride a bicycle.** — wish + could names the missing ability.
