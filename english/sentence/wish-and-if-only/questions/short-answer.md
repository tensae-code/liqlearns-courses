# Wish and if only — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Wishes About Now · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Wishes About a Finished Day · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Wish + Would — a Wanted Change, except items about a missing ability, which are Sub lesson 4 — Wish + Could — a Missing Ability · and the item asking whether if only is a different shape, which is Sub lesson 5 — If Only — the Same Wishes Said Stronger.

Answer in whole sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** It is raining now and you want sun. Say the wish.
**Answer: I wish it weren't raining. / If only it weren't raining.** — past coat, present meaning.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Yesterday you forgot your umbrella and got wet. Say the past wish.
**Answer: I wish I had taken my umbrella. / If only I hadn't forgotten it.** — had + participle for the finished day.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Your brother keeps taking your pen. Say the would wish, kindly.
**Answer: I wish you wouldn't take my pen. / I wish you would ask first.** — wish + would for a change (Bolivia's pattern).

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** You cannot ride a bicycle and you want to. Say the wish.
**Answer: I wish I could ride a bicycle.** — wish + could names the missing ability.
