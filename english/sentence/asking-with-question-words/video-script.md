# Video script — Asking with question words

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Yes and no are small answers.
Want the THING? Send a hunting word!

**What** hunts a thing: "What do you eat?"
**Where** hunts a place: "Where do you go?"
**When** hunts a time: "When do you get up?"
**Who** hunts a person: "Who is that?"
**How many** counts: "How many days?"

The word lands FIRST.
Then the engine you know: do… does…
"What does the tiger eat?"

And the answer? WHOLE!
"I sleep in the bedroom."
Not "bedroom." The whole sentence!

The voice falls down-down-down ↘:
"Where do you go? ↘"

Now you. Pick a word. Hunt the thing.
That is asking — in English.
