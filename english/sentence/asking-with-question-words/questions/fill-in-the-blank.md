# Asking with question words — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Words and Their Prey · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The Build · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Whole Answers, Falling Voice.
Fill each blank with the question word that hunts the
answer given.

**Box:** What · Where · When · Who · Why · How many

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** "______ do you eat for breakfast?" — "Bread
and eggs."
**Answer: What.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** "______ is that boy?" — "He is my brother."
**Answer: Who.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** "______ days are there in a week?" — "Seven."
**Answer: How many** (Egypt's question, p.6).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** "______ does the tiger eat meat?" — "Because
it is hungry."
**Answer: Why.**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** "______ do you go when you're sick?" — "To
the hospital."
**Answer: Where.**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** "______ does school begin?" — "At 7 a.m."
**Answer: When** — and asked with the falling voice
(Bangladesh's rule).
