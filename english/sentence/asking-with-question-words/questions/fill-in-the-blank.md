# Asking with question words — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the question word that hunts the
answer given.

**Box:** What · Where · When · Who · Why · How many

**1. (Level 1)** "______ do you eat for breakfast?" — "Bread
and eggs."
**Answer: What.**

**2. (Level 1)** "______ is that boy?" — "He is my brother."
**Answer: Who.**

**3. (Level 1)** "______ days are there in a week?" — "Seven."
**Answer: How many** (Egypt's question, p.6).

**4. (Level 2)** "______ does the tiger eat meat?" — "Because
it is hungry."
**Answer: Why.**

**5. (Level 2)** "______ do you go when you're sick?" — "To
the hospital."
**Answer: Where.**

**6. (Level 3)** "______ does school begin?" — "At 7 a.m."
**Answer: When** — and asked with the falling voice
(Bangladesh's rule).
