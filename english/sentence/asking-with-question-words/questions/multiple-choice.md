# Asking with question words — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** You want to know a PLACE. Your word is —
a) when  b) where  c) what
**Answer: b) where** — each word hunts one prey (Mongolia's
table).

**2. (Level 1)** "______ do you get up?" (answer: "At six.")
a) Where  b) When  c) Who
**Answer: b) When** — the prey is a time.

**3. (Level 2)** Build it: "______ does the tiger eat?"
a) What  b) Who  c) How many
**Answer: a) What** — and the verb stands bare after does:
eat, not eats.

**4. (Level 2)** Which question is built right?
a) Where you go?  b) Where do you go?  c) Where does you go?
**Answer: b)** — word + engine (do for you) + rest.

**5. (Level 2)** "Who lives in a nest?" has no do/does
because —
a) it is short  b) who IS the subject, standing in its place
c) nests are special
**Answer: b)** — the honest exception, with its reason.

**6. (Level 3)** "What do you do in the kitchen?" — the
course's whole answer is —
a) "Cook."  b) "In the kitchen."  c) "I cook in the
kitchen."
**Answer: c)** — the prey inside a whole sentence (Egypt's
drilled shape).
