# Asking with question words — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Words and Their Prey · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The Build · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Whole Answers, Falling Voice.
Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the prey: what / where / when / who /
how many.
**Answer:** a thing / a place / a time / a person / a count.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which word? The answer is "Because I was
hungry."
**Answer:** why — reasons answer why; the answer opens with
because.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Build the question: statement "Mita visits
her grandmother at three." Hunt the time.
**Answer:** "When does Mita visit her grandmother?" — does
carries the -s; visit stands bare.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Build it: hunt the place in "The crow lives
in a nest."
**Answer:** "Where does the crow live?"

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Answer whole: "How do you go to school?"
(answer for yourself, truly)
**Answer:** "I go to school by ___ / on foot." — whole
sentence, the way inside it.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Mongolia's table pairs each word with an
answer shape. Give the shapes for when and where.
**Answer:** when → "At 7:00" (a time); where → "In Boston"
(a place) — as the table prints them.
