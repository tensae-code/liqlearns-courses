# Asking with question words — true or false

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Level 1)** A what-question can be answered "Yes."
**False** — thing-questions hunt things; yes belongs to
yes/no questions. The answer hands over the thing.

**2. (Level 2)** "What does the tiger eats?" is built right.
**False** — "What does the tiger eat?" Does carries the -s;
the verb stands bare.

**3. (Level 2)** "Who does live in a nest?" is the standard
shape for asking about the doer.
**False** — "Who lives in a nest?" Who as subject asks with
no engine; who IS the subject.

**4. (Level 3)** The voice rises at the end of a Wh-
question.
**False** — it falls, like a statement's (Bangladesh, Class
4 U22). The rise belongs to yes/no questions.

**5. (Level 3)** "In the bedroom" is this course's finished
answer to "What do you do in the bedroom?"
**False** — the prey must ride inside a whole sentence: "I
sleep in the bedroom" (Egypt's drilled answer).

**6. (Level 1)** How many hunts a count.
**True** — "How many days are there in a week?" — "Seven."
