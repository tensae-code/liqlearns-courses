# Asking with question words — matching

Match each word to its prey and its model pair, the way the
books give them.

**Set 1 (Level 1)** — words and prey.
1. what — **a thing**
2. where — **a place**
3. when — **a time**
4. who — **a person**
5. why — **a reason**
6. how — **a way**
7. how many — **a count**

**Set 2 (Level 3)** — questions and whole answers.
1. What do you do in the bathroom? — **I brush my teeth in
   the bathroom.** (Egypt)
2. Where do you go when you're sick? — **I go to the
   hospital.** (Egypt)
3. What time do you wake up? — **I wake up at 7 a.m.**
   (Egypt)
4. How many days are there in a week? — **There are 7 days
   in a week.** (Egypt)
5. Who lives in a nest? — **The crow lives in a nest.**
   (Bangladesh's sentence, asked backward)

**Set 3 (Level 2)** — which machinery runs behind the word?
1. What is she doing? — **the swap (be walks front)**
2. What does she do? — **the engine (does)**
3. Who eats meat? — **no machinery — who is the subject**
