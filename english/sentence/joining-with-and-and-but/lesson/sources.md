**Acceptance count: taught in 18 countries (Bolivia, Ghana, Laos, China, Indonesia, Iran, Sierra Leone, Jordan, Greece, Kazakhstan, Iraq, Ethiopia, South Africa, Syria, South Sudan, Nepal, Armenia, Vietnam).**
Ghana opens the compound sentence with AND, seats BUT
beside it, lists the four coordinators, and widens the
family in its own order; Bolivia posts the but/however
border; China adds a whole lesson that sorts the linking
family by the job each word does; Indonesia sorts
conjunctions as the writing tool of an exposition; Iran
builds the compound sentence joiner by joiner — and, but,
or, so, each with its job — and drills the choice in its
workbook; Sierra Leone joins with and, but, because, or
at Primary 5 and states sentence combining at senior
level; Jordan joins likes with and/too, but and or in
Grade 2, joins habits with and/but in Grade 3, and
links a profile with and/but/so/because in Grade 8.
Greece joins with so / because at Grade 6, carries a postcard on because, as, although, but and so at Grade 9 (the teacher's book adds and to the set), and presents because, as, although, but as cause-and-effect connectors at Grade 10 EPAL. Kazakhstan works and / but / or as the writing point of a Grade 5 description lesson, names and, but, because, so as a whole stories module's language focus, and lists the five linkers — and, but, or, so, because — as a Grade 6 lesson point. Iraq states the pair as a box in its Grade 6 Activity Book — *and* to add information, *but* to show a contrast — gives linking words their own Grade 7 lesson (*and, but, or, because, as well as*), and names joining sentences with *and, but, or* in its Grade 7 Activity Book Round up. Ethiopia teaches simple and compound sentences in Grade 6, joins sentences with because/but/or in Grade 7's Writing strand, and joins ideas in a paragraph with cohesive devices — also / Besides / Moreover / Generally — in Grade 9. South Africa stocks this lesson in full: Grade 3 HL joins sentences with and, so, because and but, Grade 4 HL joins with because, but, and, before, then, Grade 3 FAL joins with because and so, and Grade 6 FAL works the wider connective set — however, nevertheless, furthermore, therefore, consequently, although, whereas. Syria previews and/but in its Grade 6 natural-resources unit, teaches and/but/so/or in the rights unit beside it, and practises and/but sentence joining in the Grade 6 Workbook. South Sudan joined 2026-11-03: its Primary 5 Pupil's Book teaches and/but/or/because/so joining clauses, defined as clause joiners in its Teacher's Guide, its Primary 6 Pupil's Book joins with because and links with however and therefore, and its Secondary 4 Student's Book lists the conjunctions, paired and single, with because-clauses set against in-order-to phrases.
