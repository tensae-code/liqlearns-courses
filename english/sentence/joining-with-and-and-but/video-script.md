# Video script — Joining with and and but

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Two sentences. One road!

"The boy woke up." "The boy prayed."
Join them — **AND**!
"The boy woke up **and** prayed."
AND goes STRAIGHT. Same road!

But feel the BEND:
"The mango is sweet… **but** small."
"I wanted to play, **but** it rained."
BUT turns the road!

Straight? AND.
A turn? BUT.
The meaning chooses — every time!

Now you. Join two sentences. Feel the road.
That is joining — in English.
