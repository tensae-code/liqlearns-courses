# Joining with and and but — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — And, the Adder · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — But, the Turner · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Choosing Cold · except item 3, which asks or, the choice-brother named in Sub lesson 3, and is Sub lesson 3.
Fill each blank with the true joiner: and, but, or or.

**Box:** and · but · or

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The crow lives in a tree ______ eats
insects.
**Answer: and** — same bird, same direction.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The day was hot, ______ we stayed inside.
**Answer: but** — hot calls you out; staying in turns
against it. (So would tell the result — its own later home;
but is this lesson's true joiner.)

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Do you want mango ______ banana?
**Answer: or** — a choice.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** She is small ______ very strong.
**Answer: but** — small one way, strong bending back.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** I woke up, washed, ______ went to school.
**Answer: and** — a chain of doings, one direction.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** He ran fast, ______ he missed the bus.
**Answer: but** — fast runs toward catching; missing turns
the road.
