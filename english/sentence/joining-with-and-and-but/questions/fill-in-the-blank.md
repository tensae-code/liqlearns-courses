# Joining with and and but — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the true joiner: and, but, or or.

**Box:** and · but · or

**1. (Level 1)** The crow lives in a tree ______ eats
insects.
**Answer: and** — same bird, same direction.

**2. (Level 2)** The day was hot, ______ we stayed inside.
**Answer: but** — hot calls you out; staying in turns
against it. (So would tell the result — its own later home;
but is this lesson's true joiner.)

**3. (Level 1)** Do you want mango ______ banana?
**Answer: or** — a choice.

**4. (Level 2)** She is small ______ very strong.
**Answer: but** — small one way, strong bending back.

**5. (Level 3)** I woke up, washed, ______ went to school.
**Answer: and** — a chain of doings, one direction.

**6. (Level 3)** He ran fast, ______ he missed the bus.
**Answer: but** — fast runs toward catching; missing turns
the road.
