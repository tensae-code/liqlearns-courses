**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ghana's finished join of "The boy woke up"
+ "The boy prayed" is —
a) The boy woke up and the boy prayed.  b) The boy woke up
and prayed.  c) The boy woke up, prayed boy.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** "I eat rice ______ fish."
a) but  b) and  c) or, always
**Answer: b) and** — same direction, one list.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** "The mango is sweet ______ very small."
a) and, best  b) but  c) so it is not a mango
**Answer: b) but** — sweet runs one way, small bends it.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which join is TRUE in meaning? "I wanted to
play ______ it rained all day."

**Answer: b) but** — wanting and raining fight; the road
turns.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** "Tea ______ coffee?" — the joiner of CHOICE

choice-brother.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Bolivia warns: HOWEVER is not a conjunction
