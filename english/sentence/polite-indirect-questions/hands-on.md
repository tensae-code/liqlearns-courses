# Hands-on — Polite Indirect Questions

Hands teach these words best. Do these with real things, not only
pictures.

## The map game

Draw a quick map of your town together: the shop, the bank, the
market, the station, the clinic. One learner closes their eyes
while the other hides a coin "in" one place. The first learner
may only ask polite indirect questions: "Can you tell me where
the coin is?" The other answers with a street or a neighbour
place until the coin is found.

## Ask a real person

Go with a partner to a real place you do not know well — a new
shop, an office, a market stall. Each of you must ask one real
question with the polite frame: "Can you tell me where the… is?"
Say what answer you got.
