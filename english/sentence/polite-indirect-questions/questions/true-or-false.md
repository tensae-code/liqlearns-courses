## True or false

1. "Can you tell me where is the shop?" is right. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: False** — inside the frame the order stays: "where the shop is".

2. "Do you know where the bank is?" is a polite indirect question. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: True** — the question rides inside the Do you know…? frame.

3. The inside of "Can you tell me what time it is?" keeps the order of a plain sentence. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: True** — "it is", as in "It is five o clock" — statement order.
