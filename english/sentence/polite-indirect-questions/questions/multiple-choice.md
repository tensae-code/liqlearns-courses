## Multiple choice

1. The polite way to ask about the shop: (Sub lesson 1)
   a) Where is the shop, quick!  b) Can you tell me where the shop is?  c) Tell shop where.
   **Answer: b)** — the question rides inside the polite frame: "Can you tell me…?"

2. Which inside part is right? "Can you tell me where ___?" (Sub lesson 2)
   a) is the bank  b) the bank is  c) bank the is
   **Answer: b) the bank is** — inside the frame, the order is statement order.

3. Which sentence is right? (Sub lesson 2)
   a) Do you know what time is it?  b) Do you know what time it is?  c) Do you know it is what time?
   **Answer: b)** — "what time it is": no flip inside the frame.

4. "___ where the station is?" (Sub lesson 3)
   a) Do you know  b) Know you do  c) Is know
   **Answer: a) Do you know** — the Do you know…? frame asks what the person knows.

5. Wrap it politely: "When does the bus leave?" (Sub lesson 3)
   a) Do you know when does the bus leave?  b) Do you know when the bus leaves?  c) Do you know the bus when leaves?
   **Answer: b)** — statement order inside: "when the bus leaves".
