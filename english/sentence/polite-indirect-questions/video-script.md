# Video script — Polite Indirect Questions

Super-simple voice. The presenter is the LiqLearns mascot,
front-facing, on screen the whole video; the pictures named here
appear beside him.

[The mascot stands in a new town, looking around, lost.]
"Where is the shop? Hmm. Let me ask — politely!"

[He turns to a kind passer-by.]
"Can you tell me where the shop is?"

[The passer-by points down the street; the mascot smiles.]
"Do you know where the bank is? Thank you!"
