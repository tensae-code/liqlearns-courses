**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (Mexico).** This
lesson exists because of Mexico's Grade 9 book: its
classified-ads and fake-news adventures teach reported
offers, reported requests and reported suggestions as
named grammar, and no lesson in the course taught the
three kinds together. The placements routed the offers
item to reported-commands and the suggestions item to
reported-statements, and both lessons cite Mexico for
them; this lesson is the fuller treatment, built from
that one book alone (stated plainly: built 2026-11-09 by
the Mexico re-verification sweep merge). Other countries
join as their books are read.
