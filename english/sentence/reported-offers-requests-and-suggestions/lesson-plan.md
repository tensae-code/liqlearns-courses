# Lesson Plan — Reported Offers, Requests and Suggestions

Unit 22 — Telling What People Said. Planned shape: four sub
lessons — reported offers first (offered + to-infinitive), then
reported requests (asked + to-infinitive, with the polite
request shapes), then reported suggestions (suggested +
that… / -ing / to-infinitive), and last choosing the right one
— telling an offer, a request and a suggestion apart and
reporting each with its own verb. Built only from Mexico's
Grade 9 book: offers (printed p. 124), requests (printed
p. 132) and suggestions (printed p. 142). The plain
reported-statements and reported-commands lessons sit beside
this one in the same unit.

## Sub lessons

1. **Reported offers** — "I will carry your bag" becomes "He
   offered to carry my bag": offered + to-infinitive.
2. **Reported requests** — "Can you open the window?" becomes
   "She asked me to open the window": asked/requested +
   to-infinitive, and the polite shapes can you / could you /
   would you.
3. **Reported suggestions** — "Let's visit the museum"
   becomes "They suggested visiting the museum": suggested /
   proposed / recommended + that…, -ing or to-infinitive.
4. **Choosing the right one** — offer, request or suggestion:
   name the kind first, then report it with its own verb.

Gate pool for each sub lesson's prove-it check: the items of the
earlier sub lessons, hardest first.
