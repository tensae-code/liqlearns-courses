# Short Answer — Reported Offers, Requests and Suggestions

Answer in a few words.

1. Report this offer: "I will drive you home." (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer:** He (or She) offered to drive me home.

2. What carries the kindness of "I will help you" into a report? (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer:** The reporting verb "offered" — offered to help.

3. Report this request: "Can you lend me your pen?" (Sub lesson 4)
   **Answer:** She asked me to lend her my pen.

4. Report this suggestion: "Let's practise every day." (Sub lesson 4)
   **Answer:** They suggested practising every day.

5. A friend says: "I will cook tonight." Offer, request or suggestion — and how do you report it? (Sub lesson 4)
   **Answer:** An offer — He offered to cook that night.
