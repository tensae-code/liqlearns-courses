# Multiple Choice — Reported Offers, Requests and Suggestions

Choose the one right answer.

1. "I will carry your bag." To report this offer you say: (Sub lesson 1)
   - A) He said me to carry the bag
   - B) He offered to carry my bag
   - C) He asked to me carry the bag
   - D) He suggested carry the bag
   **Answer: B**

2. Which verb reports an offer? (Sub lesson 1)
   - A) offered
   - B) told
   - C) explained
   - D) answered
   **Answer: A**

3. After "offered" comes… (Sub lesson 1)
   - A) that
   - B) -ing
   - C) to + verb
   - D) a person + that
   **Answer: C**

4. "Can you open the window?" reported as a request: (Sub lesson 2)
   - A) She offered to open the window
   - B) She asked me to open the window
   - C) She suggested opening the window
   - D) She said that open the window
   **Answer: B**

5. A polite request often begins… (Sub lesson 2)
   - A) "Do it now."
   - B) "Could you…?" or "Would you…?"
   - C) "I demand…"
   - D) "Why don't we…?"
   **Answer: B**

6. Which verb reports a request? (Sub lesson 2)
   - A) offered
   - B) promised
   - C) asked
   - D) hoped
   **Answer: C**

7. "Let's visit the museum." reported: (Sub lesson 3)
   - A) They asked to visit the museum
   - B) They offered visiting the museum
   - C) They told to visit the museum
   - D) They suggested visiting the museum
   **Answer: D**

8. After "suggested" you can use… (Sub lesson 3)
   - A) -ing or that…
   - B) a person + to, and only that
   - C) only a question
   - D) nothing — the sentence ends
   **Answer: A**

9. Which verb reports a suggestion? (Sub lesson 3)
   - A) ordered
   - B) recommended
   - C) shouted
   - D) carried
   **Answer: B**

10. "Shall I help you?" — this is an offer. Reported: (Sub lesson 4)
    - A) He asked me to help him
    - B) He suggested helping me
    - C) He offered to help me
    - D) He said that help me
    **Answer: C**

11. "Please sit down." — this is a request. Reported: (Sub lesson 4)
    - A) She offered to sit down
    - B) She asked us to sit down
    - C) She suggested sitting down
    - D) She promised to sit down
    **Answer: B**

12. "Why don't we rest?" — this is a suggestion. Reported: (Sub lesson 4)
    - A) He suggested resting
    - B) He asked to rest us
    - C) He offered to rest me
    - D) He told rest
    **Answer: A**
