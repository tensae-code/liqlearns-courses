# True or False — Reported Offers, Requests and Suggestions

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

1. Offers, requests and suggestions are all reported with "said that". (Sub lesson 4)
   **Answer: False** — each kind has its own verb: offered, asked, suggested.

2. "Offered to" and "asked to" keep the kind of speech in the report. (Sub lesson 4)
   **Answer: True**
