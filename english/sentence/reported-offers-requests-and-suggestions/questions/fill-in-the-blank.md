# Fill in the Blank — Reported Offers, Requests and Suggestions

Fill each blank with the right word.

1. "I will lend you my book." → She offered ____ lend me her book. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: to**

2. He offered ____ carry the boxes. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: to**

3. "Could you wait here?" → He asked me ____ wait there. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: to**

4. A polite request: "____ you help me, please?" (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: Could** (Would is also right)

5. They suggested ____ (go) by bus. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: going**

6. She suggested that we ____ early. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: leave**
