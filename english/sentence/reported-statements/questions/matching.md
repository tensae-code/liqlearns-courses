# Reported statements — matching

Match each direct sentence to its report.

**1. (Level 1)** "I am busy now." **Answer: He said he
was busy then.** — present continuous steps to past,
and now turns to then.

**2. (Level 2)** "I can finish it tomorrow." **Answer:
She said she could finish it the next day.** — can →
could, and tomorrow turns to the next day (Nepal Grade
8's turns, p.253).

**3. (Level 3)** "The earth goes round the sun." (a
teacher, stating a fact) **Answer: The teacher said
that the earth goes round the sun.** — a universal
truth: no step back, the present stays (Nepal Grade 7,
Unit 19).
