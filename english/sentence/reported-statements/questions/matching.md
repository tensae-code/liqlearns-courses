# Reported statements — matching

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Reported Statements Frame and Staircase · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Reported Statements Pronouns and Pointing Words · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Reported Statements Truths and Verb Bank.

Match each direct sentence to its report.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** "I am busy now." **Answer: He said he
was busy then.** — present continuous steps to past,
and now turns to then.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** "I can finish it tomorrow." **Answer:
She said she could finish it the next day.** — can →
could, and tomorrow turns to the next day (Nepal Grade
8's turns, p.253).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** "The earth goes round the sun." (a
teacher, stating a fact) **Answer: The teacher said
that the earth goes round the sun.** — a universal
truth: no step back, the present stays (Nepal Grade 7,
Unit 19).
