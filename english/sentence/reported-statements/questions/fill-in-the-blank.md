# Reported statements — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Reported Statements Frame and Staircase · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Reported Statements Pronouns and Pointing Words · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Reported Statements Truths and Verb Bank.

Fill each gap with the right shape.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** "I will help you." → He said that he
______ help me.
**Answer: would** — will steps one step back to would
(Cambodia Grade 9's one-step-back rule, Unit 31).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** "I can swim." → She ______ me that she
could swim.
**Answer: told** — the frame carries a person (me), so
tell, not say, opens it.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** "We are living here." → They said they
were living ______.
**Answer: there** — here turns to there when the words
are carried to a new place (Nepal Grade 8, p.253).

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** "I am sorry." → He ______ sorry.
**Answer: said** — the collocation is say sorry, never
tell sorry (Cambodia Grade 12, Unit 16's say/tell list).
