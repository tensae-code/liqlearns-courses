# Reported statements — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** Direct: "I am tired." Reported:
a) She said that she is tired.  b) She said that she was
tired.  c) She told that she was tired.
**Answer: b)** — said takes the words alone, and the
tense steps back one: am → was (the one-step-back rule).

**2. (Level 1)** Which frame is right?
a) He told that he was busy.  b) He told me that he was
busy.  c) He said me that he was busy.
**Answer: b)** — tell must carry its person (told me);
say takes the words alone.

**3. (Level 2)** Direct: "I have lost my key." Reported:
a) He said he has lost his key.  b) He said he had lost
his key.  c) He said he loses his key.
**Answer: b)** — present perfect steps down to past
perfect, and my turns to his (Mauritania's chart, p.35).

**4. (Level 2)** Direct: "I must finish today." Reported:
a) She said she must finish that day.  b) She said she
had to finish that day.  c) She said she will finish
today.
**Answer: b)** — must steps down to had to, and today
turns to that day (Nepal Grade 8's list, p.253).

**5. (Level 3)** Direct, the teacher: "The sun rises in
the east." Reported:
a) The teacher said that the sun rose in the east.
b) The teacher said that the sun rises in the east.
c) The teacher said that the sun rise in the east.
**Answer: b)** — a universal truth keeps the present
tense inside a report (Nepal Grade 7, Unit 19).
