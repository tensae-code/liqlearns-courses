# Reported statements — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Reported Statements Frame and Staircase · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Reported Statements Pronouns and Pointing Words · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Reported Statements Truths and Verb Bank.

Answer in whole sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Report it: Anna says, "I like tea."
**Answer: Anna said (that) she liked tea.** — present
simple steps back to past simple, and I turns to she.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Report it: "I bought this yesterday,"
said Karim (to you).
**Answer: Karim said (that) he had bought that the day
before.** (Karim told me… is equally right) — past steps
to past perfect; this → that, yesterday → the day
before (Mauritania's chart; Nepal's turns).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Report it with the person: "I will wait
for you," she said to Ben.
**Answer: She told Ben that she would wait for him.**
— tell carries its person (Ben), will steps to would,
you turns to him.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Report it without naming any speaker:
"People say the old bridge is dangerous."
**Answer: It is said that the old bridge is dangerous.**
— Jordan's impersonal frame (Grade 12, Semester 2, Unit
6): no speaker is named, and a standing fact keeps its
present.
