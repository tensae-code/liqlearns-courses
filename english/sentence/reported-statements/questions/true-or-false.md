# Reported statements — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Reported Statements Frame and Staircase · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Reported Statements Pronouns and Pointing Words · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Reported Statements Truths and Verb Bank.

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In reported speech, "will" becomes
"would".
**Answer: True** — one step back, on every country's
chart (Mauritania, p.35; Bolivia, p.215).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Would, could, should and might step
back again in a report — would becomes "would have".
**Answer: False** — those five (with ought to) already
stand at the bottom of the stairs and do not change
(Mauritania's chart, p.35).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** "He told that he was tired" is a good
reported sentence.
**Answer: False** — tell must carry its person: "He
told me that he was tired", or drop the person and use
said.
