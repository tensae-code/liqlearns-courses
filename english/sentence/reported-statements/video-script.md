# Video script — Reported statements

Super-simple voice. Read slow. The learner hears this
before or beside the written lesson.

---

Sara says: "I am tired."
Later, you tell your friend.
You do not use her words.
You say: Sara said that she was tired.

Look at the changes.
I becomes she.
Am becomes was.
The words walked one step back.

That is reported speech.
One step back. Always one step.

I like tea. → He said he liked tea.
I bought a book. → She said she had bought a book.
I will come. → He said he would come.
I can swim. → She said she could swim.
I must go. → He said he had to go.

Some words do not move.
Would stays would.
Could stays could.
Should stays should.
Might stays might.

The small words move too.
Today becomes that day.
Here becomes there.
This becomes that.
My becomes his, or her.

Say and tell. Two doors.
Say takes the words only.
Sara said that she was tired.
Tell takes a person.
Sara told ME that she was tired.
Never: Sara told that she was tired. No person, no tell.

Say sorry. Say yes.
But tell the truth.
Tell a lie. Tell a joke. Tell a secret.

One family never moves.
"The sun rises in the east."
The teacher said that the sun rises in the east.
Rises. Not rose.
A truth is true today. It stays in the present.

It is the same now. It will be the same tomorrow.
Truths do not walk down the stairs.

Now you try.
Your friend says three things.
Tell me later, in your words.
One step back. Pronouns turned.
And one truth — kept standing.
