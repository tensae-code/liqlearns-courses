# Reported statements — questions by level

Practice by level, tied to the skill number. The questions live in
the type files; this file is the by-level view. The levels are the
depths inside this one thing — retelling what somebody said — set by
the pattern across the countries' books: the frame and the first
backshift steps first (Cambodia's Grade 9 one-step-back rule), then
the full chart with pronouns and pointing words (Mauritania's and
Nepal's full passes), then the truth exception and the reporting
verbs that colour a report (Nepal Grade 7's truths, Bolivia's and
Jordan's verb banks).

## Level 1 — the frame and the first steps
Level 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: frame
a report with say (that)… and tell + person (that)…,
and step the common tenses down one — present simple to
past simple, will to would, can to could.

## Level 2 — the full staircase
Level 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: run
the whole backshift chart (past and perfect stepping to
past perfect, continuous to past continuous, must to
had to; would/could/should/might staying put) and turn
the pronouns and the pointing words (today → that day,
here → there, this → that) with the report.

## Level 3 — truths stay, verbs colour
Level 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: hold
a universal truth in the present tense inside a report;
choose say vs tell inside their collocations (say sorry,
tell a joke); and report with the wider verb bank and
the impersonal frame (It is said that…) without
breaking the staircase.
