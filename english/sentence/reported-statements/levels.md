# Reported statements — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB
LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub
Lessons"). Practice is tied to the skill number, as before. The
questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson
view. The sub lessons are the depths inside this one thing — retelling
what somebody said — set by the pattern across the countries'
books: the frame and the first backshift steps first (Cambodia's
Grade 9 one-step-back rule), then the full chart with pronouns and
pointing words (Mauritania's and Nepal's full passes), then the
truth exception and the reporting verbs that colour a report
(Nepal Grade 7's truths, Bolivia's and Jordan's verb banks).

Mapping from the old depth layers: old Level 1 = Sub lesson 1;
old Level 2 = Sub lesson 2; old Level 3 = Sub lesson 3.

## Sub lesson 1 — Reported Statements Frame and Staircase
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: frame
a report with say (that)… and tell + person (that)…,
and step the common tenses down one — present simple to
past simple, will to would, can to could.

## Sub lesson 2 — Reported Statements Pronouns and Pointing Words
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: run
the whole backshift chart (past and perfect stepping to
past perfect, continuous to past continuous, must to
had to; would/could/should/might staying put) and turn
the pronouns and the pointing words (today → that day,
here → there, this → that) with the report.

## Sub lesson 3 — Reported Statements Truths and Verb Bank
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: hold
a universal truth in the present tense inside a report;
choose say vs tell inside their collocations (say sorry,
tell a joke); and report with the wider verb bank and
the impersonal frame (It is said that…) without
breaking the staircase.
