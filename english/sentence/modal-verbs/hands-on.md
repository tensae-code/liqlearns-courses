# Hands-on — Modal Verbs

Strength you can hear. Practise saying the same duty in three voices.

## Rule wall

Write classroom rules on cards: two with must/mustn't (strong voice), two with should (kind voice). Pin them up and read the wall aloud, changing your voice with the word.

## Strength sort

Take six mixed duty sentences. Sort them strongest to softest and defend your order: “This one is a law — must. This one is advice — should.”
