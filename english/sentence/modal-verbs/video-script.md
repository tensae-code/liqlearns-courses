# Video script — Modal Verbs

Super-simple voice. The presenter is the LiqLearns mascot, front-facing,
on screen the whole video; the pictures named here appear beside him.

[A locked gate, a strong voice.] “You MUST stop! You MUSTN'T run! Must is IRON — the strongest duty word.”

[A friendly signpost, a kind voice.] “You SHOULD rest. You SHOULD study more. Should is advice — must with a kind face.”

[An official sign; then a boy speaking.] “Written rules say MUST TO. But in speech we say: I HAVE TO go. Three voices of duty — rule, speech, advice!”
