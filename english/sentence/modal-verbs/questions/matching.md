## Matching

Match the sentence to its strength or job. (Sub lessons 1–3)

1. You must wear a uniform. — a
2. You mustn't run in the hall. — b
3. You should rest. — c
4. I should call my grandmother. — d
5. Students must to… (official sign) — e
6. I have to go now. — f

a) strong obligation
b) prohibition — not allowed
c) advice
d) personal, weaker obligation
e) the official written rule-form
f) the spoken way of saying a duty

**Answer key with reasons:** 1–a (must = strongest), 2–b (mustn't = forbidden), 3–c (should = advice), 4–d (should = personal obligation), 5–e (must to, in the compendium's comparison), 6–f (have to in speech).
