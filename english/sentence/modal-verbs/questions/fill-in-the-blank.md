## Fill in the blank

1. You ___ (must) wear your uniform. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: must** — strong obligation, never changing.

2. You ___ (must not) cheat in the exam. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: mustn't** — prohibition takes mustn't.

3. She ___ (should) study more for the test. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: should** — advice and weaker obligation.

4. We ___ (should not) waste water. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: shouldn't** — advice against takes shouldn't.

5. Written rules, in the compendium, use ___ ___ for strong moral obligation. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: must to** — the official/written form, in the book's comparison.

6. In speech: “I ___ ___ go now.” (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: have to** — have to replaces must in speech.

7. Of the three, the personal and weaker obligation is ___. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: should** — should is personal and weaker, between rule and speech.
