**Acceptance count: taught in 9 countries (Egypt,
Mauritania, Cambodia, Kuwait, Nepal, Mongolia,
Venezuela, Bolivia, Iran).** Iran joins with tag questions in talk in its Grade 12 book (stocked 2026-11-09 by the re-verification sweep merge). Egypt states the whole system in one
eight-rule box in its final school year. Mauritania
teaches tags across two full lessons — the polarity rule
and special cases first, then main-verb tags with the
indefinite-subject and negative-word rules. Cambodia's
evidence is its Grade 11 Teacher's Book (the student's
book is not held): Unit 28 teaches tags for checking
information. Kuwait works tags across four tense/modal
families and is the set's intonation witness. Nepal
teaches tags as confirmation at Grade 7. Mongolia meets
tags by discovery at Grade VIII and again at Grade X,
with the reference entry naming both jobs — checking
truth and asking agreement. Venezuela's evidence is
teacher's-guide only: its guide works perfect-tense tags
with a HAVE chart for a student book not held in full.
