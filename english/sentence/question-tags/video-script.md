# Video script — Question tags

Super-simple voice. Read slow. The learner hears this
before or beside the written lesson.

---

You say something.
Then you add a tiny question at the end.

You are coming, aren't you?
It is hot today, isn't it?

That tiny question is a TAG.

The rule is a mirror.
A yes statement takes a no tag.
You are coming — aren't you?
A no statement takes a yes tag.
You are not coming — are you?

The tag copies your helper word.
She can swim — can't she?
They will come — won't they?
He has finished — hasn't he?

No helper word? Use do, does, did.
You live here — don't you?
She came yesterday — didn't she?

Some tags are special. Listen.

I am late — aren't I?
Not "amn't I". Aren't I.
Let's go — shall we?
Close the door — will you?

Somebody, everybody, nobody.
The tag says: they.
Everybody is here — aren't they?
Nobody called — did they?

Careful with quiet no-words.
Never. Seldom. Rarely. Few.
They make the sentence a no sentence.
She seldom comes late — does she?
He never writes — does he?

Now the voice.
Voice goes DOWN: you are checking.
You are almost sure. You want a yes.
The bus comes at six, doesn't it? (down)

Voice goes UP: you really ask.
You do not know. You want the truth.
The bus comes at six, doesn't it? (up)

Same words. Two voices. Two meanings.

Now you try.
Say three things about your friend.
Add the tag.
Say one going down. Say one going up.
