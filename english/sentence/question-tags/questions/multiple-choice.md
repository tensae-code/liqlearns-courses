# Question tags — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** "You live in this town, ______?"
a) don't you  b) do you  c) aren't you
**Answer: a) don't you** — a positive statement takes a
negative tag, and with no helper verb the tag brings
do-support (Egypt's eight rules).

**2. (Level 1)** "She can't swim, ______?"
a) can't she  b) can she  c) does she
**Answer: b) can she** — a negative statement takes a
positive tag, copying the statement's own auxiliary can
(Mauritania's polarity rule).

**3. (Level 2)** "I am late, ______?"
a) amn't I  b) aren't I  c) isn't I
**Answer: b) aren't I** — the special case: I am always
takes aren't I (Egypt's box; Mauritania, pp.46–47).

**4. (Level 2)** "Let's go home, ______?"
a) will we  b) do we  c) shall we
**Answer: c) shall we** — Let's always takes shall we
(Egypt's eight-rule box).

**5. (Level 3)** "He seldom writes letters, ______?"
a) doesn't he  b) does he  c) isn't he
**Answer: b) does he** — seldom is a hidden negative, so
the statement counts as negative and the tag goes
positive, with do-support (Mauritania, p.50).
