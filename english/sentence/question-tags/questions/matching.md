# Question tags — matching

Match each statement to its tag.

**1. (Level 1)** "She has left, ______?" **Answer:
hasn't she** — the tag copies the perfect auxiliary has
and flips it negative.

**2. (Level 2)** "Open the window, ______?" **Answer:
will you (or won't you)** — commands take will you?,
or the softer inviting won't you?

**3. (Level 3)** "Few people know this road, ______?"
**Answer: do they** — few is a hidden negative, so the
tag is positive; people-words answer to they, and a
plain present statement brings do-support.
