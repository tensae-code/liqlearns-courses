# Question tags — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Question Tags Mirror Rule · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Question Tags Special Cases · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Question Tags Negative Words and Two Voices.

Fill each gap with the right tag.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** "It is hot today, ______?"
**Answer: isn't it** — positive statement, negative tag,
copying the auxiliary is.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** "They didn't come yesterday, ______?"
**Answer: did they** — negative statement, positive tag;
the past auxiliary did steps back in.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** "Everyone is here, ______?"
**Answer: aren't they** — everyone takes a singular verb
in the statement but the plural pronoun they in the tag
(Egypt's own example runs "Everyone knows the answer,
don't they?").

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** "Close the door, ______?"
**Answer: will you (won't you is also right)**
— a command takes will you?, or the softer inviting
won't you? (Egypt's box; Mauritania, pp.46–47).
