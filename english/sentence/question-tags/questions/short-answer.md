# Question tags — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Question Tags Mirror Rule · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Question Tags Special Cases · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Question Tags Negative Words and Two Voices.

Answer in whole sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Add the tag: "You have finished your
work."
**Answer: You have finished your work, haven't you?**
— the tag copies the auxiliary have and flips it
negative.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Add the tag: "Nobody called this
morning."
**Answer: Nobody called this morning, did they?**
— nobody is a hidden negative, so the tag is positive;
the indefinite crowd answers to they, and the past
brings did (Mauritania, p.50).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** You are almost sure the bus leaves at
six, and you want your friend to confirm it. Say the
check, with the tag.
**Answer: The bus leaves at six, doesn't it?** (said
with a falling voice) — a plain present statement takes
do-support, and the falling voice marks a check, not a
real question (Kuwait's intonation guidance, p.57).

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Fix this sentence: "You are coming
tomorrow, are you?"
**Answer: You are coming tomorrow, aren't you?**
— a positive statement may not take a positive tag; the
mirror must flip (Mauritania's polarity rule).
