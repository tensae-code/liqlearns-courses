**Acceptance count: taught in 13 countries (China, Egypt, Malaysia, Nepal, Vietnam, Mauritania, Palestine, Sudan, Yemen, Iraq, Armenia, Jordan, Libya).** Libya joins with the causative — get/have something done (Grade 10) (stocked 2026-11-11 by the re-verification sweep merge). Yemen joins with causative make (Grade 10) and causative have/get (Grade 12) (stocked 2026-11-11 by the re-verification sweep merge). Egypt states the
whole family as one system in a single box — active
have/get with persons, passive have/get with things,
make for force, let/allow for permission. Malaysia
teaches have/get something done at Form 3 and Form 4,
and adds have someone do something at Form 5. Nepal
lists the complete family in one Grade 8 grammar line.
Vietnam names active and passive causatives together
at Grade 12. China is counted for its make + person
pattern only, which this lesson uses as an example
inside make — it stocks nothing of the have/get
system, and no part of the lesson claims it does.
Mauritania teaches the causative whole in its Grade 13 book —
have/get someone to do, have/get something done, and the
experienced causative (stocked 2026-11-09 by the Mauritania
re-verification sweep merge). Iraq joins with its Grade 10 causative have and get (stocked 2026-11-11 by the re-verification sweep merge).
