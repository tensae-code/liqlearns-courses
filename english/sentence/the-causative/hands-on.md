# Hands-on — The causative

You do it, we guide you. Everything here is done with a
companion or the Explain Companion.

1. **The errand round.** The companion names jobs around
   the house (a leaking tap, a torn shirt, a dirty
   car). The learner may not do any of them "personally"
   — each must be reported arranged: "I'll have the tap
   fixed." "She got her shirt mended." A sentence with
   the learner as the doer must be rebuilt. (Egypt's
   Secondary 12 family, played as errands.)
2. **The three doors.** The companion acts three scenes:
   a strict teacher, a kind sister, a busy manager. The
   learner reports each with the right door — made us…,
   let us…, had us… / got us to… — bare verb after make,
   let and have; to after get. A wrong verb shape loses
   the scene.
3. **The service diary.** List the last five services
   other people did for you or your family — haircuts,
   repairs, deliveries, photos. Report each in the
   causative with its time coat: "Last month I had…"
   "Next week we will have…" (Malaysia Form 5's
   have-someone-do and have-something-done, p.139, on
   your own diary.)
4. **Arranger or nobody?** The companion reads mixed
   sentences — some plain passives, some causatives.
   The learner shouts ARRANGER or NOBODY and points at
   the piece that proves it: the have/get + person
   standing in front, or its absence.
