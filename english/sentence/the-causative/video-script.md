# Video script — The causative

Super-simple voice. Read slow. The learner hears this
before or beside the written lesson.

---

You do not cut your own hair.
The barber cuts it.
But the sentence is about YOU.
I had my hair cut.

Had. The thing. The third shape.
I had my hair cut.
She is getting her eyes checked.
We will have the car repaired.

You arrange it. Somebody else does it.
The doer's name? Not needed. Not the point.

Have and get do the same job.
Get is the everyday word. Have is the calm one.
I got my phone fixed. I had my phone fixed.

Now with a person.
I had the mechanic check the car.
Have — then the person — then the bare verb. No to.
I got the mechanic to check the car.
Get — then the person — then TO. Remember the pair.

Two more members of the family.
Make means forced.
The teacher made us wait.
Sad movies make me cry.
Let means allowed.
She let me go early.
Make pushes. Let opens the gate.

And the plain passive? It has no arranger.
My bike was repaired. By whom? Who arranged it?
The sentence does not care.
I had my bike repaired. Now the arranger stands there:
me.

Now you try.
One job for your hair. One for your phone. One for your
shoes.
Say who arranged them: you.
I had… I got… I will have…

Done by other hands. Arranged by you.
