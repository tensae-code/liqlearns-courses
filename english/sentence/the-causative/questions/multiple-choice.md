# The causative — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** "I ______ my hair ______ yesterday."
a) had / cut  b) have / cutting now  c) did / cuts
**Answer: a) had / cut** — have + object + past
participle, in the past coat: the barber did the
cutting, the speaker arranged it (Egypt, Secondary 12,
pp.74–76).

**2. (Level 1)** "She is getting her eyes ______."
a) check  b) checked  c) to check
**Answer: b) checked** — Egypt's own box example: "She
is getting her eyes checked"; the participle, never
the bare verb, follows the object.

**3. (Level 2)** "I ______ the mechanic ______ the car
tomorrow." (get, active)
a) will get / to check  b) will get / checked my
c) get yesterday / check
**Answer: a) will get / to check** — get + person
takes the to-verb (Egypt's box), and will moves the
arranging into the future.

**4. (Level 3)** The teacher forced the class to stay:
"The teacher ______ us ______."
a) let / stay  b) made / stay  c) had got / stayed us
**Answer: b) made / stay** — make = forced, and make +
person takes the bare verb (Nepal, Grade 8, Unit 6;
China's make pattern is the same shape).

**5. (Level 3)** Which sentence has an ARRANGER in it?
a) The window was fixed on Monday.
b) I had the window fixed on Monday.
c) The window is broken.
**Answer: b)** — the plain passive reports the thing;
the causative keeps the arranger (I) standing in the
sentence.
