# The causative — matching

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Causative Have/Get Something Done · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Causative Tenses and Active Family · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Causative Force, Permission and Arrangement.

Match each beginning to the ending the family rule
demands.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** "I had my phone…"
**Answer: "…repaired."** — have + object + past
participle: the phone received the job, a technician
did it (Malaysia, Form 3, Unit 11).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** "I got my brother…"
**Answer: "…to carry the bags."** — a person after get
takes the to-verb (Vietnam, Grade 12, Unit 6's active
causative).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** "Sad movies…"
**Answer: "…make me cry."** — make + person + the bare
verb, China's Grade 9 example sentence itself (Unit 11,
Grammar Focus p.84).
