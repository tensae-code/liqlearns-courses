# The causative — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Causative Have/Get Something Done · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Causative Tenses and Active Family · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Causative Force, Permission and Arrangement.

Fill each gap with the right shape.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** We ______ (have) the roof ______
(repair) last month.
**Answer: had / repaired** — past coat on have; the
object (the roof) and the participle keep their seats.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** I had the plumber ______ (fix) the tap
this morning.
**Answer: fix** — have + person takes the bare verb,
no to (Egypt, Secondary 12, pp.74–76).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** I got the plumber ______ (fix) the tap
this morning.
**Answer: to fix** — get + person takes the to-verb:
the pair differs by exactly this word (Egypt).

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Mother ______ (let) me ______ (go) to
the match.
**Answer: let / go** — let = permission, and let +
person takes the bare verb (Nepal, Grade 8, Unit 6).
