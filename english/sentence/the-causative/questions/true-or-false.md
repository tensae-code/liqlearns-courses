# The causative — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Causative Have/Get Something Done · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Causative Tenses and Active Family · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Causative Force, Permission and Arrangement.

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In "I had my hair cut," the speaker
cut the hair personally.
**Answer: False** — the causative means the speaker
ARRANGED it; other hands did the cutting (Egypt,
Secondary 12, pp.74–76).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** "I will have my car repaired" and "I
had my car repaired" differ only in the coat have
wears.
**Answer: True** — object and participle never move;
have/get alone carries the tense.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** "She let me to leave early" is correct.
**Answer: False** — let takes the bare verb: "She let
me leave early" (Nepal, Grade 8, Unit 6's family line).
