# The causative — true or false

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Level 1)** In "I had my hair cut," the speaker
cut the hair personally.
**Answer: False** — the causative means the speaker
ARRANGED it; other hands did the cutting (Egypt,
Secondary 12, pp.74–76).

**2. (Level 2)** "I will have my car repaired" and "I
had my car repaired" differ only in the coat have
wears.
**Answer: True** — object and participle never move;
have/get alone carries the tense.

**3. (Level 3)** "She let me to leave early" is correct.
**Answer: False** — let takes the bare verb: "She let
me leave early" (Nepal, Grade 8, Unit 6's family line).
