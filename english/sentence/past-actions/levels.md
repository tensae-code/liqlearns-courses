# Past actions — questions by level

Practice by level, tied to the skill number. The questions live in
the type files; this file is the by-level view. The levels are the
depths inside this one thing — the past — set by the pattern
across the countries' books: the regular coat first, then the
movers and be, then Did/didn't and whole retellings.

## Level 1 — the -ed coat
Level 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: put any
regular verb into the past (spellings included: lived,
stopped), for every who, without a miss.

## Level 2 — the movers and be
Level 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: the great
movers by heart as pairs (go–went, eat–ate, see–saw, come–
came, take–took, give–gave, drink–drank, run–ran), and was/
were placed by the who every time.

## Level 3 — Did, didn't, and the retelling
Level 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: ask and
deny with the verb stepped back to base after Did/didn't —
never two past marks — and retell a short finished story in
whole past sentences.
