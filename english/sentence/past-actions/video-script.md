# Video script — Past actions

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Yesterday! Let's wear the PAST.

Most verbs take the coat: ED!
play — play**ed**. walk — walk**ed**.

But the famous verbs CHANGE!
go — **went**! eat — **ate**! see — **saw**!
Learn them as pairs!

Be? "I **was**. They **were**."

Ask about yesterday: "**Did** you walk?"
Did holds the past — walk stays PLAIN!
"No, I **didn't** walk."

One past mark. Did is holding it!

Now you. Tell me yesterday!
That is the past — in English.
