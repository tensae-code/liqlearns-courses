**Acceptance count: taught in 5 countries (Bolivia, Ghana,
Mongolia, Laos, China).** Mongolia boxes the -ed frame and drills the
movers by name; Bolivia states the whole paradigm including
the Did/didn't law; Ghana seats the simple past where
stories live; China adds the past staged regular first,
irregular second, each with its own formation table, plus
was/were past states. Other finished countries join as their books
are read (see READING-QUEUE.md).

**Laos Grade 6 added 2026-10-27** — *English for Secondary
Schools, Book One* (MoES/RIES, 2010; full read in
english/books/laos.md). The past arrives as a system: **past
simple with a regular/irregular table** (pp.133, 136), Did
you…? questions (pp.132–143) — and the pronunciation of the
ending itself, **-ed said three ways: /t/ /d/ /id/** (p.138),
the mirror of the -s system in Present Actions.

**Laos Grade 8 added 2026-10-27** — *English for Secondary
Schools, Book Three* (MoES/RIES, 2012; full read in
english/books/laos.md). The past grows two more shapes:
**used to** as a full system (pp.34–37) and the **past
continuous with while** — what was happening when the past
simple thing struck (pp.61–73) — plus the **-ed spelling
rules** tabled (p.28).
