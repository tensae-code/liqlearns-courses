# Past actions — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The -Ed Coat · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The Movers and Be · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Did, Didn't, and the Retelling.
Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** "Yesterday we ______ football."
a) play  b) played  c) playing
**Answer: b) played** — the regular coat.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The past of "stop" is —
a) stoped  b) stopped  c) stopd
**Answer: b) stopped** — short verbs double the last letter.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** "She ______ to the market yesterday."
a) goed  b) went  c) goes
**Answer: b) went** — go is a mover: go–went (Mongolia's
list).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** "They ______ happy last night."
a) was  b) were  c) be
**Answer: b) were** — they takes were.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Which question is built right?
a) Did you walked home?  b) Did you walk home?  c) You did
walked home?
**Answer: b)** — Did holds the past; the verb steps back to
base (Bolivia's paradigm).

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** "I didn't ______ breakfast."
a) ate  b) eaten  c) eat
**Answer: c) eat** — after didn't, the verb stands at base.
