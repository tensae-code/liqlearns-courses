# Past actions — ordering

Stand the words in sentence order. (This type exists here
because the Did-frame is an order law: Did first, verb at
base.)

**1. (Level 3)** you / did / yesterday / walk / ?
**Answer:** Did you walk yesterday?

**2. (Level 3)** didn't / she / breakfast / eat
**Answer:** She didn't eat breakfast — she before didn't;
eat at base.

**3. (Level 1)** played / we / in the field / football
**Answer:** We played football in the field.

**4. (Level 2)** went / to the river / the crow
**Answer:** The crow went to the river — went, the mover.

**5. (Level 2)** were / happy / they / very
**Answer:** They were very happy.

**6. (Level 3)** Unbuild it — turn the question back into a
statement: "Did Mita visit her grandmother?"
**Answer:** Mita visited her grandmother. — did leaves;
the coat returns to the verb.
