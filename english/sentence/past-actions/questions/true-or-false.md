# Past actions — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The -Ed Coat · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The Movers and Be · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Did, Didn't, and the Retelling · except item 5, which asks the clue word "ago" — Sub lesson 3's retelling words — and is Sub lesson 3.
Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The -ed coat is the same for every who.
**True** — I played, she played, they played. (The -s guard
belongs to the present.)

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** "Read" changes its spelling in the past.
**False** — same spelling, new sound: read → read ("red").
The mover that hides.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** "Did you went?" is wrong because two past
marks stand in one sentence.
**True** — did holds the past; the verb steps back: "Did
you go?"

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** "They was at the market" is right.
**False** — they takes were: "They were at the market."

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** "Ago" comes AFTER the time it measures:
"two days ago."
**True** — the past's own clue word order.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** A finished story is told in the present
simple.
**False** — stories of finished things wear the past
(Ghana's narrative strand). The present tells habits and
truths.
