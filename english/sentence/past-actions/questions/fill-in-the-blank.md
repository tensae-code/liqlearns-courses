# Past actions — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the verb in its past coat.

**Box (present faces):** play · go · eat · be (I) · be (they)
· come

**1. (Level 1)** We ______ football last Friday. (play)
**Answer: played.**

**2. (Level 2)** He ______ home late. (come)
**Answer: came.**

**3. (Level 2)** I ______ rice and fish. (eat)
**Answer: ate.**

**4. (Level 2)** She ______ to school by bus. (go)
**Answer: went.**

**5. (Level 2)** I ______ at home all day. (be)
**Answer: was.**

**6. (Level 2)** They ______ very happy. (be)
**Answer: were.**
