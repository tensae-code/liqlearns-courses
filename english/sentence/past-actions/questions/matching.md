# Past actions — matching

Match each present face to its past face, the way Mongolia's
list drills them.

1. go — **went**
2. eat — **ate**
3. see — **saw**
4. come — **came**
5. take — **took**
6. give — **gave**
7. drink — **drank**

1. play — **played**
2. cook — **cooked**
3. live — **lived**
4. stop — **stopped**

1. Did you walk? — **Yes, I did.**
2. Did she eat? — **No, she didn't.**
