# Past actions — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Level 1)** Coat them: visit, cook, live, wash.
**Answer:** visited, cooked, lived (e + d), washed.

**2. (Level 2)** Pair them (present — past): see, drink,
take, write.
**Answer:** see–saw, drink–drank, take–took, write–wrote.

**3. (Level 2)** Was or were: I ___ tired. We ___ at school.
It ___ hot.
**Answer:** was — were — was.

**4. (Level 3)** Ask it in the past: "You saw a tiger."
**Answer:** "Did you see a tiger?" — see back at base after
Did.

**5. (Level 3)** Deny it, whole: you did not eat fish
yesterday.
**Answer:** "I didn't eat fish yesterday" — eat at base
after didn't.

**6. (Level 1)** Give three clue words that call the past.
**Answer:** yesterday; last (night/week/year); …ago.
