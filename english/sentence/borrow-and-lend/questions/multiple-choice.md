## Multiple choice

1. "Can I ___ your eraser?" — you are the taker. (Sub lesson 1)
   a) lend  b) borrow  c) keep
   **Answer: b) borrow.**

2. "I can ___ you my ruler." — you are the giver. (Sub lesson 2)
   a) borrow  b) lend  c) take
   **Answer: b) lend.**

3. You borrow a book ___ the library. (Sub lesson 1)
   a) to  b) from  c) for giving
   **Answer: b) from.**

4. You lend a book ___ your friend. (Sub lesson 2)
   a) from  b) to  c) by
   **Answer: b) to.**
