## Short answer

1. Who is the borrower — the giver or the taker? (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer:** The taker.

2. Who is the lender? (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer:** The giver.

3. What do borrow and lend both promise? (Sub lessons 1–2)
   **Answer:** The thing comes back — the loan is for a while, not forever.
