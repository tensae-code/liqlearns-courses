## Matching

Match the sentence to its direction. (Sub lessons 1–2)

1. "Can I borrow your pen?"
2. "I lent her my umbrella."
3. "He borrowed a ladder from us."
4. "We can lend you a chair."

a) giving (lend)
b) taking (borrow)
c) taking (borrow)
d) giving (lend)

**Answers:** 1–b, 2–a, 3–c, 4–d.
