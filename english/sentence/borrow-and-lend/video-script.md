# Video script — Borrow and Lend

Super-simple voice. The mascot is front-facing on screen the whole video, talking to the learner. Bracketed lines are visual cues for the maker, not spoken lines.

[A pen travels from one hand to another, then travels back.]

"Can I borrow your pen? — the pen comes to me."

"I can lend you my pen. — the pen goes from me."

"Borrow takes. Lend gives."

"And both promise: the thing comes home."
