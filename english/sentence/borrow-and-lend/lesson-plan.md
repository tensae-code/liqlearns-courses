# Lesson plan — Borrow and Lend

**Course:** English (Zero to Story)
**Title:** Borrow and Lend
**Lesson:** borrow-and-lend — the two directions of a loan: borrow — you take a thing for a while; lend — you give a thing for a while — and the sentences each one builds
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 10 — Building a Sentence

**The one thing:** the learner can keep borrow and lend straight: who takes, who gives, and the shape of each sentence.

**This lesson's own level:** sentence level — it stands in Unit 10 (Building a Sentence), beside have-and-has, the course's other verb-pair lesson.

## Why this lesson exists
Venezuela's Grade 9 book teaches borrow against lend as its own named point inside the politeness lesson — the classic pair learners mix up, given its own treatment. No lesson in the course housed the pair, so under everything-goes-in it is built here.

## Sub lessons
1. **Borrow — taking for a while**
2. **Lend — giving for a while**
Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
Nothing is needed before this lesson. If the learner knows asking-politely first, the request sentences will feel easier — they might try it first.

## What the learner can do when it ends
- after sub lesson "Borrow — taking for a while": use borrow for the taker: "Can I borrow your eraser?" — the thing moves TO you, and it comes back
- after sub lesson "Lend — giving for a while": use lend for the giver: "I can lend you my eraser." — the thing moves FROM you, and it comes back

## What you need
A pen or a book to pass back and forth.

## If the learner is stuck
Act it. Every time the thing moves, say who borrows and who lends, out loud, before any writing.
