# Levels — Borrow and Lend

How deep each sub lesson goes. The course-wide level ladder (Zero to Story) places this lesson at sentence level — it stands in Unit 10 (Building a Sentence), beside have-and-has, the course's other verb-pair lesson.

| Sub lesson | What it holds |
|---|---|
| Borrow — taking for a while | use borrow for the taker: "Can I borrow your eraser?" — the thing moves TO you, and it comes back |
| Lend — giving for a while | use lend for the giver: "I can lend you my eraser." — the thing moves FROM you, and it comes back |

Pass mark: the sources state no pass mark and none is invented. In this course a sub lesson opens when the learner proves the previous one with the prove-it check (at most 5 questions, the sub lesson's whole question set as the pool).
