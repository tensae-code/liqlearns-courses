# Video script — Cleft sentences

Super-simple voice. Read slow. The learner hears this
before or beside the written lesson.

---

A plain sentence spreads its light everywhere.
"Sara scored the goal in the final minute."
Who? When? The sentence does not say what matters most.

English has a shape that picks one part up.
And puts a light on it.

"It was Sara who scored the goal."
Now the light is on Sara.
"It was in the final minute that Sara scored."
Now the light is on the time.
"It was the goal that changed the match."
Now the light is on the thing.

Look at the shape.
It was… or It is…
Then the part you choose.
Then WHO for a person. THAT for a thing.
Then the rest of the sentence, just as it was.

Past event? It was.
Present truth? It is.
"It is hard work that makes a master."

Its neighbour flips the sentence.
"Never before had I seen such heat."
Never at the front. The helper jumps first.
Same family: emphasis. Different dress.

Now you try.
Take one true event.
Light the person. Light the time. Light the place.
Three sentences. One event. Three lights.
