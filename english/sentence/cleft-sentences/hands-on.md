# Hands-on — Cleft sentences

You do it, we guide you. Everything here is done with a
companion or the Explain Companion.

1. **Three lights, one event.** The learner tells one
   real event in a plain sentence. The companion asks
   Who? When? Where? in turn, and the learner must
   answer each with a cleft that lights exactly the part
   asked about.
2. **The correction game.** The companion makes a false
   claim about the learner's day ("You came by bus!").
   The learner corrects it with a cleft: "It was on foot
   that I came." Five rounds, swapping roles.
3. **Block lifting.** A sentence is written as word
   blocks on paper strips. The learner physically lifts
   one block behind a card reading IT WAS, adds WHO or
   THAT, and reads the cleft aloud — then lifts a
   different block and hears the meaning move.
4. **Never before.** Each partner says one true
   "Never before had I…" sentence about a real first
   time (eating something, seeing somewhere), in the
   flipped order, with the stress on Never.
