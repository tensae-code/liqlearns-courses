**Acceptance count: taught in 2 countries (Jordan,
Vietnam).** The narrow base is stated plainly, as the
build brief requires: these are the ONLY two sources in
the whole read set that teach cleft sentences. Jordan
teaches them at Grade 12 beside negative inversion, in
one emphasis unit, with workbook practice and cleft
intonation. Vietnam teaches them at Grade 11 as the
single grammar point of its independence unit, under
the box heading "Cleft sentences with It is/was …
that/who …". No other closed country's book names or
drills the cleft, and this lesson claims no other
witness.
