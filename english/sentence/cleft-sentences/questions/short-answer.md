# Cleft sentences — short answer

Answer in whole sentences.

**1. (Level 1)** Turn it into a cleft lighting the place: "We met in the library."
**Answer: It was in the library that we met.** — place focus, that, was for the finished event.

**2. (Level 2)** Answer with a cleft: "WHO taught you to swim?" (your grandfather)
**Answer: It was my grandfather who taught me to swim.** — the question asks for the person — the cleft answers with the light on him.

**3. (Level 3)** Correct the claim with a cleft: "You won the prize in 2022!" (it was 2023)
**Answer: No — it was in 2023 that I won the prize.** — the correction cleft lights exactly the part the claim got wrong.
