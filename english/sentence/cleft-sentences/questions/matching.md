# Cleft sentences — matching

Match each plain sentence to the cleft that lights the named part.

**1. (Level 1)** "Lina bought the red bicycle on Saturday." — light LINA
**Answer: It was Lina who bought the red bicycle on Saturday.** — the person lifted behind It was, with who.

**2. (Level 2)** "Lina bought the red bicycle on Saturday." — light SATURDAY
**Answer: It was on Saturday that Lina bought the red bicycle.** — a time takes that, and the rest follows unchanged.

**3. (Level 3)** "I had never tasted tej before that day." — in Jordan's flipped dress
**Answer: Never before had I tasted tej (before that day).** — negative inversion: Never before + helper + subject (Jordan, Grade 12, pp.18–19).
