# Video script — Participles as Verb Forms

Super-simple voice. The presenter is the LiqLearns mascot,
front-facing, on screen the whole video; the pictures named here
appear beside him.

[The word EAT appears and grows a tail.] "EAT… EATING!"

[BE steps in beside it.] "BE + the -ing form: the action GOING
ON."

[EAT changes shape.] "EAT… EATEN! The third form."

[HAVE steps in.] "HAVE + the third form: the action COMPLETE."

[Back to the mascot.] "Two shapes build every tense. Learn
the shapes!"
