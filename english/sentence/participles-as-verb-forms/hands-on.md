# Hands-on — Participles as Verb Forms

## Three-form races

A verb is called out. Partners race to say the triple — base,
-ing form, past participle: "eat – eating – eaten!" First
correct triple scores. A wrong spelling rule (runing?) loses
the point and gets fixed aloud.

## Build the pairings

Cards: be, have, and participle cards. One partner lays a
helping verb; the other must match it with the right
participle form and say the tense it makes — continuous or
perfect.
