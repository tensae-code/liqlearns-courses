## Matching

Match the verb to its past participle. (Sub lesson 2)

1. eat — c
2. go — a
3. see — e
4. write — b
5. walk — d

a) gone
b) written
c) eaten
d) walked
e) seen

**Answers:** 1–c (eat – ate – eaten), 2–a (go – went – gone), 3–e
(see – saw – seen), 4–b (write – wrote – written), 5–d (walk – walked –
walked, the regular -ed).
