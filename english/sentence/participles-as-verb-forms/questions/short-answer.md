## Short answer

1. Give the present participle of "take" and say the spelling rule you used. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer:** Taking — a verb ending in silent e drops the e before -ing.

2. What two pairings build the tense system? (Sub lessons 1–2)
   **Answer:** Be + present participle (the action going on — continuous) and have + past participle (the action complete — perfect).

3. Write the triple for "speak". (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer:** Speak – spoke – spoken.

4. In "The letter was written yesterday", which words make the passive? (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer:** "Was" (be) + "written" (the past participle of write).
