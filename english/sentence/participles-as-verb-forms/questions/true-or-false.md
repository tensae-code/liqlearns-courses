## True or false

1. The present participle of "write" is "writing". (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: True** — the silent e drops before -ing.

2. "Swiming" is the right spelling. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: False** — swim doubles its last letter: swimming.

3. Regular verbs make their past participle with -ed. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: True** — walk → walked, watch → watched.

4. The past participle of "see" is "saw". (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: False** — "saw" is the past simple; the past participle is "seen".

5. The passive uses be + past participle. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: True** — "It was written by hand."
