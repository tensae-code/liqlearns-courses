## Fill in the blank

1. "The present participle is the verb's ___ form." (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: -ing.**

2. "'Sit' becomes '___' — the last letter doubles." (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: sitting.**

3. "Be + present participle makes the ___ tenses." (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: continuous.**

4. "The past participle is the verb's ___ form." (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: third.**

5. "Go – went – ___." (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: gone.**
