## Multiple choice

1. What is the present participle of "walk"? (Sub lesson 1)
   a) Walked  b) Walking  c) Walks
   **Answer: b) Walking** — the verb + -ing form.

2. What happens to "make" when it takes -ing? (Sub lesson 1)
   a) Nothing: makeing  b) The silent e drops: making  c) The k doubles: makking
   **Answer: b) The silent e drops: making.**

3. "Run" becomes… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) Runing  b) Runned  c) Running
   **Answer: c) Running** — short verb, one vowel: double the last letter.

4. What is the past participle of "eat"? (Sub lesson 2)
   a) Ate  b) Eaten  c) Eating
   **Answer: b) Eaten** — the third form of the triple eat – ate – eaten.

5. "Have + past participle" builds… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) The continuous tenses  b) The perfect tenses  c) Questions
   **Answer: b) The perfect tenses** — "I have walked", "she had eaten".
