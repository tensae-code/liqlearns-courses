**Acceptance count: taught in 5 countries (Bolivia,
Egypt, Malaysia, Nepal, Venezuela).** Egypt prints the
past ladder inside the same numbered box as the
present one, and adds blame and regret to it. Nepal's
criticism forms rest on teacher's-guide evidence and
are worded so; Venezuela's set likewise rests on its
Grade 11 guide's plan, with the student poster not
reproduced. Bolivia works might have for past
conjecture, and Malaysia stocks the set at Form 4.
Needn't have is taught here as the system's own
member — the books stock the set it completes
(Egypt's box carries the necessity modals beside the
past forms); no filing was found presenting needn't
have under its own heading, and that limit is stated
rather than papered over.
