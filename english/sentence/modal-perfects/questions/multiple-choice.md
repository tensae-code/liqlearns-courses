# Modal perfects — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** The plate is empty and there are crumbs everywhere. "Somebody ______ here."
a) must have eaten  b) must has eaten  c) must have eat
**Answer: a) must have eaten** — modal + have + participle; have never changes (Egypt's past ladder).

**2. (Level 1)** The door is still locked and the key never moved. "He ______ out."
a) must have gone  b) can't have gone  c) should have gone
**Answer: b) can't have gone** — 95–100% sure it did NOT happen (Egypt).

**3. (Level 2)** You are wet through, and your umbrella is hanging at home. "You ______ your umbrella."
a) must have taken  b) can't have taken  c) should have taken
**Answer: c) should have taken** — criticism: the right thing was not done (Egypt's blame and regret; Nepal TG G9 U14).

**4. (Level 2)** "You ______ the receipt — now the shop won't take the radio back."
a) should have kept  b) needn't have kept  c) might have kept
**Answer: a) should have kept** — Nepal's own anchor sentence from its Criticising unit (TG, p.110).

**5. (Level 3)** "You ______ those heavy bags up the hill — the empty bus was right behind you!"
a) should have carried  b) needn't have carried  c) must have carried
**Answer: b) needn't have carried** — the thing WAS done, but the need was not there.
