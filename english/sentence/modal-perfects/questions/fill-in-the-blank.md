# Modal perfects — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Modal Perfects — Deduction About the Past · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Modal Perfects — Criticism and Regret · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Could Have — the Open Door, except items about needn't have, which are Sub lesson 4 — Needn't Have — Done, But Not Needed.

Fill each gap with the right shape.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** She is not answering and her bag is gone. She ______ (leave) already.
**Answer: must have left** — a proved deduction about the past: must have + participle.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** I ______ (not eat) the whole cake — now I feel sick.
**Answer: shouldn't have eaten** — the wrong thing WAS done: shouldn't have + participle.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** We ______ (win) the game — we missed by one goal!
**Answer: could have won** — the open door: a real past possibility nobody used (Venezuela TG G11 L17's set).

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** You ______ (cook) so much food — half the guests had already eaten.
**Answer: needn't have cooked** — it was done, and it was not needed.
