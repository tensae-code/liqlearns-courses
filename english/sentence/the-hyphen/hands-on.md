# Hands-on — The Hyphen

## Meaning pairs

In pairs: one partner writes a word on a card WITHOUT its
hyphen — reformed, resigned, recover (a cover made again /
to get better). The other reads it aloud and says what it
means now. Then the hyphen goes in, and the meaning is said
again. Change places after five cards.

## Hyphen hunt

Take a page of your own writing. Hunt for three things:
numbers written in words (do they carry their hyphens?),
phrases standing before nouns (are they tied together?),
and any re- word (does it need its hyphen to say the right
thing?). Fix what you find, and read the fixed lines aloud.
