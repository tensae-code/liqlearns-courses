## Multiple choice

1. What is a hyphen? (Sub lesson 1)
   a) A long dash  b) A short line that joins words  c) Two dots, one over the other
   **Answer: b) A short line that joins words.**

2. Which number is spelled the right way? (Sub lesson 1)
   a) twenty one  b) twenty-one  c) twenty -one
   **Answer: b) twenty-one** — the hyphen joins the parts of the number.

3. "She re-formed the clay" means… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) she changed it for the better  b) she formed it again  c) she broke it
   **Answer: b) she formed it again** — "reformed" without the hyphen means changed for the better.

4. Why does "re-enter" carry a hyphen? (Sub lesson 2)
   a) To make the word look longer  b) To stop the two e's crashing into each other  c) Because all long words need one
   **Answer: b)** — the hyphen avoids the letter collision.

5. In "two- and three-year-olds", the hyphen after "two" is… (Sub lesson 3)
   a) A mistake  b) Suspended — it waits for the shared ending  c) A dash
   **Answer: b)** — the suspended hyphen holds its word open until "-year-olds" arrives.
