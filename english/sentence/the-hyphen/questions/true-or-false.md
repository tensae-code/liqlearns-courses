## True or false

1. A hyphen is the same mark as a full stop. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: False** — a hyphen is a short line that joins words; a full stop ends a sentence.

2. We write twenty-one with a hyphen. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: True.**

3. "Reformed" and "re-formed" mean exactly the same thing. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: False** — re-formed means formed again; reformed means changed for the better.

4. A hyphen can split a long word at the end of a written line. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: True.**

5. In written dialogue, hyphens can show a speaker hesitating. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: True** — they write down the breaks in the voice: "I-I don't know."
