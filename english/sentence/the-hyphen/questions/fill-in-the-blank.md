## Fill in the blank

1. "The short line that joins words into one idea is called a ___." (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: hyphen.**

2. "In words, 21 is written twenty-___." (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: one.**

3. "To say he signed again and stayed, write ___-signed with a hyphen." (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: re.**

4. "In 'two- and three-year-olds', the hyphen after 'two' is called a ___ hyphen." (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: suspended.**

5. "At the end of a written line, a long word can be split with a ___." (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: hyphen.**
