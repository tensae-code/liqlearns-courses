## Matching

Match the part to its job. (Sub lessons 1–3)

1. The hyphen — c
2. twenty-one — a
3. re-enter — e
4. a ten-year-old boy — b
5. two- and three-year-olds — d

a) A number spelled out with a hyphen
b) A whole phrase used as an adjective before a noun
c) The short line that joins words
d) A suspended hyphen waiting for the shared ending
e) A hyphen stopping two vowels crashing into each other

**Answers:** 1–c (the mark itself), 2–a (numbers spelled out), 3–e (letter collision avoided), 4–b (phrase as adjective), 5–d (the suspended hyphen).
