# Video script — The Hyphen

Super-simple voice. The presenter is the LiqLearns mascot,
front-facing, on screen the whole video; the pictures named here
appear beside him.

[A short line appears, big.] "This is a HYPHEN. A short
little line. It JOINS words."

[The words "twenty" and "one" slide together with the hyphen
between them.] "Twenty-ONE! Numbers hold hands with the
hyphen."

[Two cards appear: RE-FORMED and REFORMED.] "With the
hyphen: form it AGAIN. Without it: change for the BETTER.
One little line — a different meaning!"

[The words "two-" and "three-year-olds" appear, the first
hyphen hanging in the air.] "And sometimes the hyphen WAITS
— two- and three-year-olds!"

[Back to the mascot.] "Join words. Keep meanings apart.
That is the HYPHEN!"
