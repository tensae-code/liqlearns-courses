# Lesson plan — Future continuous

**Course:** English · **Title:** Sentence · **Lesson:** Future continuous
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 20 — The Future in Detail (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the future continuous — **will be + verb-ing** — an action that will be IN PROGRESS at a future moment: not the plan, not the finished thing, but the moving scene of tomorrow, caught mid-action.
**What the learner can do at the end:** build will be + verb-ing for every who; picture an action in progress at a named future time; ask and deny in the tense; and keep it apart from will's plain decisions and going to's plans.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees

it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons

1. **Future Continuous Shape** — build will be + verb-ing for every who — will and be fixed, the -ing spellings right. (Was depth Level 1.)
2. **Future Continuous Future Moment** — actions in progress at named future points (this time tomorrow, at eight tonight, all day), and the polite scene-question (Will you be using…?); the asking-and-denying block sits in this sub lesson's text. (Was depth Level 2.)
3. **Future Continuous Neighbours** — sort futures live: decided now (will), planned before (going to), in progress at a moment (this tense). (Was depth Level 3.)

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Going to: later (plans) and The past continuous (the moving scene — this lesson aims the same scene forward). Recommended, never forced — this lesson stands alone for a learner who lands on it directly (DIRECT-ENTRY rule).
- Speaker convention: the explanation is read in the learner's main language; the TARGET forms are never translated and are read in the English voice.

## Media slots
1. **Slot 1 — a picture of a train window view at sunrise tomorrow, fields moving past, with a clock showing eight o'clock.** What the student does: the student says what the traveller will be doing at that clock time — one will be + -ing sentence — before reading the shape.
2. **Slot 2 — a picture of a house drawn at night with every room lit and busy — cooking, reading, mending — under the words THIS TIME TOMORROW.** What the student does: the student gives one moving action for each room, all in this tense.
3. **Slot 3 — a picture of a calendar page for next Monday with a small aeroplane drawn across the whole day.** What the student does: the student says the all-day sentence: "Next Monday, I will be travelling all day."

## The run of the lesson, in order
1. **Do it first**: tomorrow at one exact moment, as a moving scene, in the learner's own language.
2. **The shape**: will be + verb-ing; will and be fixed (Egypt Sec 11 T2 future work; Bolivia's FUTURE CONTINUOUS heading — affirmative, negative, interrogative).
3. **The job**: in progress at a future point — this time tomorrow, at eight tonight, all day, the next Monday (Bolivia's Santa Cruz model).
4. **The pair**: Malaysia F5 U5 teaches it beside the future perfect (p.63); Uzbekistan EM9 U2 carries it in the grammar peak.
5. **Asking and denying**: Will you be using your car tonight? He won't be coming.
6. **The neighbours**: plain will = decided now; going to = planned before; this tense = the moving film.
7. **Do it yourself**: the three closing tasks.

## If the learner is stuck
- Stuck on the pieces: will never changes and be never changes — only the -ing verb moves. Three fixed pieces, one coat.
- Stuck choosing against going to: ask "am I reporting a plan, or painting the action moving at a moment?" The moment with its clock (this time tomorrow, at eight) calls this tense.
- Stuck on the spelling of -ing: it is the same coat as every continuous — travelling, making, running — carried over whole.
- This lesson's next door: The future perfect (same level) — tomorrow's finished-by-then result, this lesson's pair.
