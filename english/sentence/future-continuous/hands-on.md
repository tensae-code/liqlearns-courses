# Hands-on — Future continuous

You do it, we guide you. Everything here is done in the
learner's real day, with a companion or the Explain
Companion. The TARGET forms are always said in English;
the talk around them may be in the learner's own language.

1. **The future camera.** Take four photographs in words of tomorrow — morning, noon, evening, night. Under each, one will be sentence about a real person. Pin them in order; at tomorrow's end, check which scenes truly happened.
2. **The polite-ask round.** Ask three people one Will you be …-ing question about tonight (the car, the kitchen, the road). Write each answer whole. Notice: nobody felt pushed — that is this tense's manners.
3. **Scene swap.** One person names a future moment; both write the scene they picture, then read them aloud and mark every will be + -ing. Any plain will or going to that sneaks in must be re-shot as a moving scene.
