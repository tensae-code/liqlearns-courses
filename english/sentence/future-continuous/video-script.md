# Video script — Future continuous

Super-simple voice. Read slow. The learner hears this
before or beside the written lesson.

---

Tomorrow at eight, picture yourself.

You are not finished. You are not starting.

You are IN THE MIDDLE.

I will be travelling.

She will be sleeping.

They will be working.

Will. Be. And the verb with -ing.

Will never changes. Be never changes.

This time tomorrow, I will be travelling.

At eight tonight, she will be cooking.

All day tomorrow, we will be working.

A plan? That is going to.

A decision now? That is plain will.

This one is the film of tomorrow, moving.

Will you be using your car tonight?

Ask about the scene. It is the polite way.

Now you try.

Tomorrow at eight — what will you be doing?
