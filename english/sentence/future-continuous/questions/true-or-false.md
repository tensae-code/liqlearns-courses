# Future continuous — true or false

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Level 1)** "Will be" changes to "wills be" for he and she. **Answer: False** — will and be never change; only the main verb wears -ing.

**2. (Level 2)** The future continuous needs a future moment to stand on, like "at eight tonight" or "this time tomorrow." **Answer: True** — the tense paints an action in progress AT a future point; its time words name that point.

**3. (Level 3)** "We are going to visit on Sunday" and "On Sunday at ten we will be visiting" do exactly the same job. **Answer: False** — going to reports the plan decided before; the future continuous paints the visit moving at its moment.
