# Future continuous — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Future Continuous Shape · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Future Continuous Future Moment · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Future Continuous Neighbours.

Answer in a word or a whole sentence, as asked.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What will you be doing at nine tonight? Answer whole and truly. **Answer: (Learner's own true sentence in will be + -ing)** — the action must be moving at that named future time.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Ask politely whether your companion needs the car tonight, in this tense. **Answer: Will you be using the car tonight?** — asking about the scene, not the decision, is the polite form.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Will you be at home at six tomorrow? Give the short answer that fits your real plan. **Answer: Yes, I will. / No, I won't** — short answers ride will, the tense's first piece.
