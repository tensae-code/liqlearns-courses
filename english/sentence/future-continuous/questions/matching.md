# Future continuous — matching

Match each item to its partner.

**1. (Level 1)** "this time tomorrow" beside "yesterday at this time" **Answer: this time tomorrow — the future continuous; yesterday at this time — the past continuous** — the same moving scene, aimed forward or back; the time words choose the tense.

**2. (Level 2)** "I'll get the door!" beside "At nine I'll be studying" **Answer: the door — plain will (decided now); studying — future continuous (scene at a moment)** — the neighbours kept apart: decision of the moment versus the moving film.

**3. (Level 3)** Bolivia's model sentence for this tense **Answer: "I will be travelling to Santa Cruz the next Monday"** — the compendium's own writing-task model, as filed.
