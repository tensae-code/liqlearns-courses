# Future continuous — fill in the blank

Fill each gap with the right shape.

**1. (Level 1)** Tomorrow at this time, we ______ (work) in the garden. **Answer: will be working** — will be + -ing, aimed at the future moment.

**2. (Level 1)** He ______ (not come) to school this week. (negative) **Answer: won't be coming** — the not folds into will: won't be + -ing.

**3. (Level 2)** ______ you ______ (use) your bicycle tonight? **Answer: Will … be using** — will steps to the front — the polite scene-question.

**4. (Level 3)** All day next Monday, she ______ (travel). **Answer: will be travelling** — an all-day stretch at a future day is still this tense; the point is the whole day.
