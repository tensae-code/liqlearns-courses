# Future continuous — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Future Continuous Shape · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Future Continuous Future Moment · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Future Continuous Neighbours.

Fill each gap with the right shape.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tomorrow at this time, we ______ (work) in the garden. **Answer: will be working** — will be + -ing, aimed at the future moment.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** He ______ (not come) to school this week. (negative) **Answer: won't be coming** — the not folds into will: won't be + -ing.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** ______ you ______ (use) your bicycle tonight? **Answer: Will … be using** — will steps to the front — the polite scene-question.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** All day next Monday, she ______ (travel). **Answer: will be travelling** — an all-day stretch at a future day is still this tense; the point is the whole day.
