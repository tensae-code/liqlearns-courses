# Future continuous — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** "This time tomorrow, I ______ to town." a) will be travelling  b) will travels  c) am travelled yesterday **Answer: a) will be travelling** — a scene moving at a future moment wears will be + -ing.

**2. (Level 1)** In "will be working," the pieces that never change are — a) will and be  b) the verb  c) the time word **Answer: a) will and be** — will and be are fixed for every who; only the -ing verb carries the action.

**3. (Level 2)** "At eight tonight, she ______ dinner." a) will be cooking  b) cooked at eight yesterday  c) has cooking **Answer: a) will be cooking** — the named future point (at eight tonight) with the action moving around it is this tense's home.

**4. (Level 3)** Which sentence reports a PLAN decided before, not a moving scene? a) At nine, I will be studying.  b) We are going to visit Ana on Sunday.  c) Will you be using the car? **Answer: b)** — going to reports the decided plan; the others paint or ask about the moving scene.
