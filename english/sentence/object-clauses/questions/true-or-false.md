# Object clauses — true or false

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Level 1)** In "I think that the shop is open", the words after that form a whole clause with its own subject and verb.
**Answer: True** — "the shop is open" stands alone as a sentence — that is what makes it a clause, not a phrase.

**2. (Level 2)** "Tell me where does he live" is the correct carried question.
**Answer: False** — statement order inside the clause: Tell me where he lives — the do-flip belongs to the free question only.

**3. (Level 3)** A that-clause after a verb is always reported speech.
**Answer: False** — Albania's filing says it in so many words: that-clauses of thinking and certainty are NOT reported speech — no reporting pattern, no backshift.
