# Hands-on — Object clauses

You do it, we guide you. Everything here is done with a
companion or the Explain Companion.

1. **Finish my thought.** The companion starts frames —
   I think…, I know…, I hope…, I'm sure… — and the
   learner must finish each with a whole that-clause
   about real things. A finish that is not a whole
   clause does not count.
2. **The question carrier.** The companion asks direct
   questions ("Is the market open today?", "When does
   the bus leave?"). The learner must carry each one
   inside "Tell me…" or "I want to know…", in statement
   order, choosing if/whether or the wh- word.
3. **The certainty ladder.** One fact, four strengths:
   the learner says it as I think that…, I believe
   that…, I'm sure that…, There's no doubt that… — and
   says which rung they would defend in an argument.
4. **Two doors.** Cards with that-sentences are sorted
   onto two doors, SAID and KNOW: reported speech one
   way, object clauses the other. The learner must say
   the reason for every card: whose words, and when?
