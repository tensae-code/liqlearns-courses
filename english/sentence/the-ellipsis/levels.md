# Levels — The Ellipsis

This file is the sub-lesson depth record for the lesson
(restructure 2026-11-08): what each sub lesson holds, in teaching
order. The old layer wording is not used; the sub lessons below
are the lesson's own parts, exactly as listed in lesson-plan.md.

| Sub lesson | What it holds |
|---|---|
| Sub lesson 1 — Leaving words out | The ellipsis itself — three dots in a row; the mark standing where words are dropped: repeated words left out in dialogue, and long quotations shortened with the meaning kept whole. Jamaica, NSC Grades 7–9 guide, printed pp.148, 222. |
| Sub lesson 2 — Pauses and speech that trails off | The ellipsis in speech: pauses and hesitation, and speech trailing off unfinished in dialogue and drama; the mark told apart from the full stop and set among the other punctuation marks (brackets, colons, dashes). Jamaica, NSC Grades 7–9 guide, printed p.222; Grade 6 Exploratory Core guide, Scope and Sequence, printed p.9. |

Pass mark: the sources state no pass mark and none is invented. In

one with the prove-it check (at most 5 questions, the sub lesson's
whole question set as the pool).
