# Hands-on — The Ellipsis

## Trim the talk

In pairs: write six lines of dialogue together, letting the
same place or thing be named again and again. Then go back
through it and drop every repeated naming you can, leaving an
ellipsis where the words fell away. Read both versions aloud —
the trimmed one must still make full sense.

## Hear the dots

One partner reads lines of speech from a story, pausing or
fading the voice wherever they choose. The other writes the
lines down and puts an ellipsis at every pause and trail-off
they heard. Swap the papers and check: does each ellipsis
stand at a real silence?
