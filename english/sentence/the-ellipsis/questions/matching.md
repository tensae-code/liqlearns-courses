## Matching

Match the line to what its ellipsis is doing. (Sub lessons 1–2)

1. "Are you ready?" — "I am …" — b
2. The mayor said: "We will repair the bridge … and the school roof." — a
3. "The noise came from over … listen!" — d
4. "If I had known the road was closed …" — c
5. "She opened the letter and read … and read." — b

a) Words cut from the middle of a quotation
b) Repeated words left out
c) Speech trailing off unfinished
d) A pause while the speaker listens/thinks
e) The end of a finished sentence

**Answers:** 1–b (the repeated word "ready" is dropped), 2–a (part of the quotation is cut, meaning kept), 3–d (a pause), 4–c (the sentence never finishes), 5–b ("read" repeated, dropped behind the dots). (e) is the full stop's job, not the ellipsis's.
