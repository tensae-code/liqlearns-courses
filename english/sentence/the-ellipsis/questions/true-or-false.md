## True or false

1. An ellipsis is three dots in a row. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: True.**

2. An ellipsis can stand where repeated words are left out of dialogue. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: True.**

3. When you shorten a quotation with an ellipsis, you may change what the speaker meant. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: False** — the meaning must stay whole; only words may fall away.

4. An ellipsis can show a speaker pausing to think. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: True.**

5. Speech that trails off unfinished ends with a full stop, never an ellipsis. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: False** — the trailing off is exactly what the ellipsis marks.
