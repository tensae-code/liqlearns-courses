## Fill in the blank

1. An ellipsis is ______ dots in a row. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: three.**

2. "Do you want tea?" — "I want …" The ellipsis stands for the words ______ ______. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: left out** (the repeated word "tea").

3. When a report shortens a quotation with an ellipsis, the ______ of the words that stay must not change. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: meaning.**

4. "Wait … I hear something." The ellipsis marks a ______. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: pause.**

5. "I wanted to tell her, but …" The speaker's words ______ ______ unfinished. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: trail off** (fade away).
