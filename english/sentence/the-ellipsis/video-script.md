# Video script — The Ellipsis

Super-simple voice. The presenter is the LiqLearns mascot,
front-facing, on screen the whole video; the pictures named here
appear beside him.

[Three dots appear in a row, big.] "This is an ELLIPSIS.
One, two, three dots — in a row."

[A sentence appears: "I am coming to the river." The words
"to the river" fade out and the three dots take their place.]
"Words left out? The dots stand in their place!"

[A face thinking, a speech bubble that stops half-way.] "A
pause … a voice trailing away … the dots show the silence."

[Back to the mascot.] "Words missing, a pause, a voice
fading — three dots. That is the ELLIPSIS!"
