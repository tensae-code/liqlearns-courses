# Used to and would — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Used to and would — the finished past · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Used to and would — would for repeated actions · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Used to and would — the accustomed family.

Fill each gap correctly.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** She ______ (negative) like spinach, but now she eats it every day.
**Answer: didn't use to** — the standard negative — did carries the past, use to stays bare.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Every evening after supper, Grandfather ______ tell us a story. (repeated action, past scene set)
**Answer: would** — would + base verb for the repeated action; a state could not stand here.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** After a month in the city, he is used to ______ (sleep) through the noise.
**Answer: sleeping** — be used to + -ing: accustomed now, not a finished past habit.
