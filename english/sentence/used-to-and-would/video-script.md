# Video script — Used to and would

Super-simple voice. Read slow. The learner hears this
before or beside the written lesson.

---

Look at an old photo of you.
Small. Different hair. Different street.
What was true then, and is not true now?

English says it with USED TO.
"I used to live in a village."
"I used to walk to school."
Habits. And states. Both finished. Both past.

No more now? "I didn't use to like coffee."
Asking? "Did you use to play outside?"
Listen: DID takes the past. So USE TO has no D.
Did you USE to. I didn't USE to.

Now sit by the fire. Tell the old days.
Every summer, we would swim in the river.
Every evening, Grandfather would tell a story.
WOULD is for actions that happened again and again.
But not for states. Never "would live", "would be".
States belong to USED TO only.
And he won't do it? "He wouldn't listen." WOULDN'T
refuses.

One more. Different animal.
"I am used to the noise now."
Not past. Not finished. Accustomed. Used to it.
"I am getting used to my new shoes."
Day by day, it gets easier.
BE USED TO takes an -ing word or a thing.
Never mix the two families.

Now you try.
Three things from your old photo.
Used to. Didn't use to. Did you use to.
