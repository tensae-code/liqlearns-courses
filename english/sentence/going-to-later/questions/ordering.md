# Going to: later — ordering

Stand the words in sentence order. (This type exists here
because the frame IS an order: be, going to, base verb.)

**1. (Level 1)** going to / she / is / study
**Answer:** She is going to study.

**2. (Level 1)** are / going to / they / play
**Answer:** They are going to play.

**3. (Level 2)** you / going to / are / visit / who / ?
**Answer:** Who are you going to visit? — Unit 2's question
word walking in front of the frame.

**4. (Level 2)** isn't / come / he / going to
**Answer:** He isn't going to come.

**5. (Level 3)** am / I / tomorrow / going to / work
**Answer:** I am going to work tomorrow — the clue word
rests at the end, as later-words do.

**6. (Level 3)** Unbuild it — push the plan back into a
present truth: "She is going to study at UMSA."
**Answer:** She studies at UMSA. — the frame leaves; the
every-day face remains.
