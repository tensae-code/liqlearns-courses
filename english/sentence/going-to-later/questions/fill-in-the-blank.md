# Going to: later — fill in the blank

Fill each blank so the future stands right.

**1. (Level 1)** I ______ going to visit my grandmother.
**Answer: am.**

**2. (Level 1)** They are going to ______ shopping on
Sunday. (go)
**Answer: go** — base verb (Bolivia's exercise sentence).

**3. (Level 2)** We ______ going to play on Saturday.
**Answer: are.**

**4. (Level 2)** ______ you going to study tonight?
**Answer: Are** — be walks to the front to ask.

**5. (Level 3)** He ______ going to come; he told me so.
(negative)
**Answer: isn't** — the denial with be + not.

**6. (Level 3)** Watch the clouds — it is going to ______
.
**Answer: rain** — base verb; the prediction's verb stands
plain.
