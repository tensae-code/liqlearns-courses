# Going to: later — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Level 1)** Give the frame for "they": "They ______
going to play."
**Answer:** "They are going to play" — are for they.

**2. (Level 1)** Deny it: "He is going to come."
**Answer:** "He isn't going to come" — not rides after be.

**3. (Level 2)** Say your real plan for tonight, whole.
**Answer:** "I am going to ___ tonight." — true plan, be +
going to + base verb.

**4. (Level 2)** Ask about her plan: "She is going to study
at UMSA" — ask the yes/no question.
**Answer:** "Is she going to study at UMSA?" (Bolivia's own
plan, asked.)

**5. (Level 3)** One sign, one prediction: the pot is
boiling over.
**Answer:** "It is going to spill!" — any seen-coming
prediction in the frame.

**6. (Level 3)** Name going to's two jobs, from Bolivia's
note.
**Answer:** intentions/plans (future ideas decided) and
predictions (things seen coming).
