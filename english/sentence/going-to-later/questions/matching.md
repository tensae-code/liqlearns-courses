# Going to: later — matching

Match each item to its future home or its frame half.

**Set 1 (Level 1)** — be by the who.
1. I — **am going to**
2. she — **is going to**
3. they — **are going to**
4. we — **are going to**
5. it — **is going to**

**Set 2 (Level 3)** — three futures, three homes.
1. "We are going to visit Oruro in February." (decided last
   month) — **going to — the plan**
2. "I'll get the door!" (the knock came just now) —
   **will — decided while speaking**
3. "The bus leaves early tomorrow." — **plain present —
   the timetable**

**Set 3 (Level 2)** — signs and predictions.
1. black clouds — **It is going to rain.**
2. the baby's mouth trembling — **She is going to cry.**
3. the pot boiling over — **It is going to spill.**
4. the queue moving to the gate — **The bus is going to
   leave.**
