# Going to: later — questions by level

Practice by level, tied to the skill number. The questions live in
the type files; this file is the by-level view. The levels are the
depths inside this one thing — the decided future — set by the
pattern across the countries' books: the frame first, then its
two jobs, then the borders with its neighbours.

## Level 1 — the frame
Level 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: build be +
going to + base-verb sentences for every who (am/is/are
right every time), and deny them with not after be.

## Level 2 — the two jobs
Level 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: say true
plans and intentions, read visible signs into predictions,
and ask the plans interview's questions with the be-swap and
whole answers.

## Level 3 — the borders
Level 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: sort futures
by their making — decided before (going to), decided while
speaking (will), printed on a timetable (plain present) — and
keep the base verb standing after going to under every
pressure.
