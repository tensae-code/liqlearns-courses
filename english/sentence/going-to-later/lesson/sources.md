**Acceptance count: taught in 15 countries (Bolivia, Ghana,
Mongolia, Laos, China, Iran, Sierra Leone, Jordan, Greece, Kazakhstan, Iraq, Ethiopia, South Africa, Syria, South Sudan).** Bolivia tables going to whole and names its
jobs; Mongolia boxes the will neighbour; Ghana keeps the
scheduled present as the third future; China adds be going
to as a full system with its time words, will worked for
plans and promises, and the two futures set side by side in
one table; Iran tables will whole and sets be going to
beside it as its own table, then speaks the two as one
plans strategy; Sierra Leone teaches will/shall and
going to in one JSS pair, then gives going to its own
senior lesson for plans and immediate-future conclusions;
Jordan plans with will/won't in Grade 5, meets going
to beside will in a Grade 6 travel booster, and in
Grade 7 predicts with will and runs the present
continuous and the timetabled present as futures.
Greece teaches the future as one three-form system at Grade 5 — going to, arrangements continuous and will — contrasts will with going to by job at Grade 6, and runs all three futures through one Grade 7 unit. Kazakhstan teaches *be going to* in full at Grade 5 — affirmative, negative and questions — and again at Grade 6 beside *will/won't* for predictions, with Full Blast! adding the arrangements-continuous note. No primary Kazakhstan book teaches any future form — the form arrives at Grade 5, in the secondary-style series. Indonesia was checked and adds nothing here: no future
form is taught in any of its read books — will/going to
occur only incidentally inside other texts (see
english/books/indonesia.md, honest negatives). Iraq runs *going to* for plans through its Grade 4 school-trip unit, boxes the full paradigm in Grade 5, divides *going to* plans from *will* predictions in Grade 6, and states the pair as one box — plans against certainties — in its Grade 9 Student's Book. Ethiopia sets will / is going to / shall / are to in substitution tables in Grade 3, teaches the simple future with will in Grade 5, groups simple future, going to and the present continuous for intention and plan in Grade 6, and contrasts going to vs will in Grade 7. South Africa stocks this lesson in full: Grade 4 FAL teaches the future with will, going to and shall together, Grade 3 FAL teaches the future tense explicitly with will, and HL writes the future with Tomorrow/will frames in Grade 3 and teaches it with will in Grade 5. Syria meets going to for plans in Grade 5, sets be going to against will by job in Grade 6 — predictions with present evidence, plans against instant decisions — and tables the will/going to contrast in Grade 9. South Sudan joined 2026-11-03: its Primary 4 Pupil's Book teaches going to with adverbs of time, its Primary 3 Pupil's Book tables present, past and future and quotes shall/will futures, its Primary 2 Pupil's Book carries will for future intentions in stories and models only, and its Secondary 4 Student's Book practises will/shall futures.
