# Video script — Going to: later

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Tomorrow! Let's say it TODAY.

The frame: BE… GOING TO… verb!
"I **am going to** visit Grandma."
"She **is going to** study."
"They **are going to** play."

Plans! Already decided.
Say your plan: "I'm going to…"

Look — DARK clouds!
"It **is going to** rain!"
You can SEE it coming. Say it!

Ask me: "**Are** you going to come?"
"Yes, I am!"

Now you. Tell me tomorrow — today!
That is later — in English.
