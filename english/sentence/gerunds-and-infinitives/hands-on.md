# Hands-on — Gerunds and infinitives

You do it, we guide you. Everything here is done with a
companion or the Explain Companion.

1. **The two-track sort.** The companion deals verb
   cards (enjoy, want, finish, promise, keep, decide,
   avoid, refuse, need, love, start, remember, stop).
   The learner lays each card on the -ING track, the TO
   track, or the BOTH bridge, and must defend each
   both-card with two sentences. (Egypt's four classes,
   Secondary 10 Term 1, Unit 4, played as a game.)
2. **The remember drill, live.** The companion gives
   orders across the day: "Remember to bring the book"
   (before — the learner must repeat it as a promise),
   then later tests memory: "Do you remember bringing
   it?" (after — the learner answers with the -ing
   shape). Swap the shapes on purpose once and catch
   the meaning move.
3. **Make and let in the room.** Act it out: the
   companion makes the learner do small things (The
   teacher made me stand up) and lets them do others
   (She let me sit down). Every sentence reported aloud
   with the bare verb — a stray to costs the point.
   (Malaysia Form 5, Unit 9's pairing, played live.)
4. **Firsts and lasts.** Walk the day back: the first
   person to wake, the last to eat, the next to speak.
   Five noun + to-infinitive labels, spoken, then
   written — Vietnam Grade 11 Unit 6's pattern on your
   own house.
