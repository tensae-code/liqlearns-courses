# Gerunds and infinitives — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Gerunds and Infinitives Two Tracks · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Gerunds and Infinitives Both Shapes and Bare Infinitive · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Gerunds and Infinitives Meaning Changes and Perfect Gerund.

Answer in whole sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say one thing you enjoy doing, in a
full sentence.
**Answer: I enjoy reading.** (Any enjoy + -ing sentence
earns it; enjoy never takes to.)

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Report it with let: your mother
allowed you to stay out late.
**Answer: My mother let me stay out late.** — let +
person + the bare verb (Malaysia, Form 5, Unit 9).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Warn your brother not to forget his
passport, using remember.
**Answer: Remember to take your passport.** — remember
+ to, the forward-looking shape: the action is still
to come (Cambodia, Grade 10, Unit 25).

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Confess, in the perfect gerund: you
read your sister's letter.
**Answer: I admit having read my sister's letter.**
(Any admit/deny + having + past participle earns it;
Vietnam, Grade 11, Unit 7.)
