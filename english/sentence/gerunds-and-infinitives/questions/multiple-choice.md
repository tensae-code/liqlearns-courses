# Gerunds and infinitives — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Gerunds and Infinitives Two Tracks · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Gerunds and Infinitives Both Shapes and Bare Infinitive · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Gerunds and Infinitives Meaning Changes and Perfect Gerund.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** "I enjoy ______ in the sea."
a) to swim  b) swimming  c) swim yesterday
**Answer: b) swimming** — enjoy is an -ing-only verb
(Egypt, Preparatory 9, Tip p.42).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** "She promised ______ early."
a) coming  b) come  c) to come
**Answer: c) to come** — promise stands on the to-list
with want, need, refuse and decide (Egypt, Tip p.42).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** "The teacher ______ us wait outside."
a) made  b) wanted  c) enjoyed
**Answer: a) made** — made us wait, with the bare verb:
make strips the to off (Malaysia, Form 5, Unit 9).

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** "I remember ______ the door, so stop
worrying."
a) to lock  b) locking  c) lock tomorrow
**Answer: b) locking** — the -ing shape looks BACK at
an action already done; the speaker can still see it
(Egypt, Secondary 10, Unit 4).

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** "He denied ______ the money."
a) to take  b) having taken  c) take
**Answer: b) having taken** — the perfect gerund puts
the earlier, finished action after the verb (Vietnam,
Grade 11, Unit 7).
