# Gerunds and infinitives — fill in the blank

Fill each gap with the right shape of the verb in
brackets.

**1. (Level 1)** ______ (Swim) in the morning is good
for you.
**Answer: Swimming** — a gerund can stand as the
sentence's subject (Mongolia, English X, Unit 5).

**2. (Level 2)** She let me ______ (use) her phone.
**Answer: use** — after let, the bare infinitive: no
to (Malaysia, Form 5, Unit 9, p.113).

**3. (Level 2)** He was the first person ______ (reach)
the top.
**Answer: to reach** — first/last/next + noun takes a
to-infinitive (Vietnam, Grade 11, Unit 6).

**4. (Level 3)** Please remember ______ (post) the
letter on your way home.
**Answer: to post** — remember + to looks forward: the
posting is not done yet, and must not be forgotten
(Cambodia, Grade 10, Unit 25).
