# Gerunds and infinitives — matching

Match each verb to the shape (or shapes) that follow it.

**1. (Level 1)** enjoy, finish, keep, avoid
**Answer: -ing only** — Egypt's -ing list (Preparatory
9, Tip p.42), drilled again in Sudan's Grammar Corners.

**2. (Level 1)** want, promise, need, refuse, decide
**Answer: to only** — Egypt's to-list (Preparatory 9,
Tip p.42).

**3. (Level 3)** remember, forget, stop, try
**Answer: both — with a change of meaning** — the
fourth of Egypt's four classes (Secondary 10, Unit 4,
p.63): the clock test (before/after) tells them apart.
