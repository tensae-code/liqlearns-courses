# Gerunds and infinitives — true or false

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Level 1)** "I finished to read the book" is
correct English.
**Answer: False** — finish is -ing only: "I finished
reading the book" (Egypt, Preparatory 9, Tip p.42).

**2. (Level 2)** "You had better to go now" is correct.
**Answer: False** — had better takes the bare
infinitive: "You had better go now" (Malaysia, Form 3,
Unit 11).

**3. (Level 3)** "He stopped to smoke" and "He stopped
smoking" mean the same.
**Answer: False** — stopped to smoke = he paused in
order to smoke; stopped smoking = he gave the habit up
(Egypt, Secondary 12, Unit 9's meaning-change set).
