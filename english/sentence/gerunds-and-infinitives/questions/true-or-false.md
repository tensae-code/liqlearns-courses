# Gerunds and infinitives — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Gerunds and Infinitives Two Tracks · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Gerunds and Infinitives Both Shapes and Bare Infinitive · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Gerunds and Infinitives Meaning Changes and Perfect Gerund.

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** "I finished to read the book" is
correct English.
**Answer: False** — finish is -ing only: "I finished
reading the book" (Egypt, Preparatory 9, Tip p.42).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** "You had better to go now" is correct.
**Answer: False** — had better takes the bare
infinitive: "You had better go now" (Malaysia, Form 3,
Unit 11).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** "He stopped to smoke" and "He stopped
smoking" mean the same.
**Answer: False** — stopped to smoke = he paused in
order to smoke; stopped smoking = he gave the habit up
(Egypt, Secondary 12, Unit 9's meaning-change set).
