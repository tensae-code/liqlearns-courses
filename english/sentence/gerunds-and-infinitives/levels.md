# Gerunds and infinitives — questions by level

Practice by level, tied to the skill number. The questions live in
the type files; this file is the by-level view. The levels are the
depths inside this one thing — the two shapes after a verb — set
by the pattern across the countries' books: the two lists first
(Egypt's Preparatory 9 Tip and Sudan's Grammar Corners agree),
then the both-classes and the bare infinitive, then the meaning
changes, the perfect gerund and the noun's to-infinitive.

## Level 1 — the two tracks
Level 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: send
the common verbs down the right track — to only (want,
promise, need, refuse, decide) and -ing only (enjoy,
finish, keep, avoid) — and use a gerund as a sentence's
subject (Swimming is…).

## Level 2 — both, and the bare infinitive
Level 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: use
the both-with-no-change verbs (like, love, start,
begin) in either shape; drop to after make/let and the
modals; and hang a to-infinitive on a noun (the first
person to…).

## Level 3 — meaning changes and the perfect gerund
Level 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: keep
remember/forget/stop straight in both shapes by the
before/after test, and put a finished-first action into
having done after verbs like admit, deny and regret
(Vietnam, Grade 11, Unit 7).
