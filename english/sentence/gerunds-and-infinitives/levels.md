# Gerunds and infinitives — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB
LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub
Lessons"). Practice is tied to the skill number, as before. The
questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson
view. The sub lessons are the depths inside this one thing — the two
shapes after a verb — set by the pattern across the countries'
books: the two lists first (Egypt's Preparatory 9 Tip and Sudan's
Grammar Corners agree), then the both-classes and the bare
infinitive, then the meaning changes, the perfect gerund and the
noun's to-infinitive.

Mapping from the old depth layers: old Level 1 = Sub lesson 1;
old Level 2 = Sub lesson 2; old Level 3 = Sub lesson 3.

## Sub lesson 1 — Gerunds and Infinitives Two Tracks
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: send
the common verbs down the right track — to only (want,
promise, need, refuse, decide) and -ing only (enjoy,
finish, keep, avoid) — and use a gerund as a sentence's
subject (Swimming is…).

## Sub lesson 2 — Gerunds and Infinitives Both Shapes and Bare Infinitive
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: use
the both-with-no-change verbs (like, love, start,
begin) in either shape; drop to after make/let and the
modals; and hang a to-infinitive on a noun (the first
person to…).

## Sub lesson 3 — Gerunds and Infinitives Meaning Changes and Perfect Gerund
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: keep
remember/forget/stop straight in both shapes by the
before/after test, and put a finished-first action into
having done after verbs like admit, deny and regret
(Vietnam, Grade 11, Unit 7).
