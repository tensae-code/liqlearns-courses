## Short answer

1. Build a dummy-it sentence with "important" and a noun. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer:** e.g. "It is an important sport." — It + be + adjective + noun.

2. Build a dummy-it sentence with "difficult" and an action. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer:** e.g. "It is difficult to learn." — It + be + adjective + to-infinitive.

3. Why is the subject called a dummy? (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer:** It points at nothing real; it only holds the subject's place.
