## Multiple choice

1. In "It is important to practise", the word it… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) points at a thing  b) holds the subject's place  c) is a mistake
   **Answer: b)** — the dummy subject holds the seat; the weight comes after.

2. Which sentence has the dummy subject? (Sub lesson 1)
   a) "The book is heavy."  b) "It is difficult."  c) "She is kind."
   **Answer: b).**

3. The full shape of the second sub lesson is… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) It + be + adjective + to-infinitive  b) It + verb + noun  c) Adjective + it + be
   **Answer: a).**

4. "It is easy ___ a word and easy to forget it." (Sub lesson 2)
   a) learn  b) to learn  c) learning it is
   **Answer: b) to learn.**
