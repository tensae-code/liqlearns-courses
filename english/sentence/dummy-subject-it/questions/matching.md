## Matching

Match the sentence to its shape. (Sub lessons 1–2)

1. "It is difficult."
2. "It is an important match."
3. "It is important to rest."
4. "It was exciting."

a) It + be + adjective + noun
b) It + be + adjective + to-infinitive
c) It + be + adjective
d) It + be + adjective (past)

**Answers:** 1–c, 2–a, 3–b, 4–d.
