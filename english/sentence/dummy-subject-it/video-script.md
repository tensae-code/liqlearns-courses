# Video script — The Dummy Subject It

Super-simple voice. The mascot is front-facing on screen the whole video, talking to the learner. Bracketed lines are visual cues for the maker, not spoken lines.

[The word IT sits in an empty chair at the head of a sentence.]

"Some sentences start with it. It points at nothing."

"It is important. It is difficult. It was exciting."

"And then the action: It is important to practise."

"An empty word, doing a big job."
