# Lesson plan — The Dummy Subject It

**Course:** English (Zero to Story)
**Title:** The Dummy Subject It
**Lesson:** dummy-subject-it — sentences that begin with an it that points at nothing: It + be + adjective — "It is important", "It is difficult" — and the shape that follows it, a noun or a to-infinitive
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 18 — Deeper Sentences

**The one thing:** the learner can build sentences on the dummy subject it: It + be + adjective, with a noun or a to-infinitive after it.

**This lesson's own level:** sentence level — it stands in Unit 18 (Deeper Sentences), with the machinery that reaches beyond the plain sentence.

## Why this lesson exists
Venezuela's Grade 10 book names this pattern outright, twice: in its sports lesson and again in its weather lesson, where the shapes are presented as tables — It + be + adjective + noun, and It + be + adjective + to-infinitive. No lesson in the course housed the dummy-subject pattern, so under everything-goes-in it is built here.

## Sub lessons
1. **It + be + adjective**
2. **It + be + adjective + to-infinitive**
Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
Nothing is needed before this lesson. If the learner knows gerunds-and-infinitives first, the to-infinitive step will feel easier — they might try it first.

## What the learner can do when it ends
- after sub lesson "It + be + adjective": build "It is important", "It is difficult", "It was exciting" — the it that holds the sentence's place
- after sub lesson "It + be + adjective + to-infinitive": build "It is important to practise", "It is difficult to learn" — the pattern completed by an action

## What you need
Nothing special.

## The run of the lesson
1. **Do it first** — the learner meets the words in a doing task before any explaining.
2. **ADD IMAGE** — a picture for the lesson's world, when the picture pass reaches this lesson.
3. **Sub lesson 1 — It + be + adjective.**
4. **Sub lesson 2 — It + be + adjective + to-infinitive.**

## Add-ons
- **Say it to your companion:** the spoken round at the end of lesson/lesson.md.
- **Do It Yourself:** the closing task in lesson/lesson.md — the learner uses the lesson's words about their own life.

## If the learner is stuck
Keep the pair side by side: "Learning is difficult" (heavy head) against "It is difficult to learn" (light dummy). The learner feels the shape before naming it.
