## Multiple choice

1. "___ book in my hand is new." (Sub lesson 1)
   a) This
   b) That
   c) Those
   **Answer: a) This** — the book is close — one thing near takes this.

2. "___ chair across the room is broken." (Sub lesson 1)
   a) These
   b) That
   c) This
   **Answer: b) That** — one thing far away takes that.

3. "___ pens in my hand are blue." (Sub lesson 2)
   a) That
   b) This
   c) These
   **Answer: c) These** — many things near take these.

4. "___ bags over there are heavy." (Sub lesson 2)
   a) Those
   b) This
   c) These
   **Answer: a) Those** — many things far away take those.

5. You are holding a jacket in the shop. You ask: (Sub lesson 3)
   a) "How much are those?"
   b) "How much is this?"
   c) "Which one is far?"
   **Answer: b) "How much is this?"** — the thing in your hand is this, one thing.

6. "Which one do you want?" — "___ one." (pointing across the shop) (Sub lesson 3)
   a) This
   b) These
   c) That
   **Answer: c) That** — one thing far away is that one.
