## Fill in the blank

1. ___ is my pen. (in your hand) (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: This** — one thing near.

2. ___ is her house. (across the street) (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: That** — one thing far.

3. ___ are my shoes. (on your feet) (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: These** — many things near.

4. ___ are their goats. (in the far field) (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: Those** — many things far.

5. "Can I have ___ jacket, please?" (pointing at it on the far rail) (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: that** — the jacket is far — that.

6. "How much is ___?" (holding a cup) (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: this** — the cup is in your hand — this.
