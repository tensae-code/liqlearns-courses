## Short answer

1. Say the pointing word for one thing close to you. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: "This."** — one thing near = this.

2. Say the pointing word for many things far from you. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: "Those."** — many things far = those.

3. Ask the price of the book in your hand. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: "How much is this?"** — this for the thing you hold.

4. Your friend points at a far bag and asks "Which one?" Answer for her. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: "That one."** — one far thing = that one.

5. Name the four pointing words. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: "This, that, these, those."** — this/that for one, these/those for many.
