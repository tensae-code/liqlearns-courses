## True or false

1. "This" is for a thing far from you. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: False** — this is for a thing close to you; that is far.

2. "These" is for many things close to you. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: True** — these = many + near.

3. "Those pens" can name one pen. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: False** — those is for many things.

4. In a shop you ask "How much is this?" about the thing in your hand. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: True** — the held thing is this.

5. "That one" answers "Which one?" for a thing across the room. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: True** — one far thing = that one.
