# Video script — This, That, These and Those

Super-simple voice. The presenter is the LiqLearns mascot, front-facing,
on screen the whole video; the pictures named here appear beside him.

[The mascot holds a book.] "THIS book — close to me!"

[The mascot points across the room.] "THAT chair — far away!"

[The mascot holds three pens, then points at three bags far off.] "THESE pens — close! THOSE bags — far!"

[At a shop table, pointing at a jacket.] "How much is THIS? THAT one, please!"
