# This, That, These and Those

## Do it first

Look around you. Point at one thing close to you and try to name it with "this". Now point at one thing far away and try "that". Go on — in this lesson you will learn the four pointing words and when each one fits.

## Sub lesson 1 — This and that — one thing, near or far

For ONE thing, English points with two words:

- **this** — the thing is close to you: "**This** book is mine." (in your hand)
- **that** — the thing is far from you: "**That** book is yours." (across the room)

"Can I have **that** jacket, please?" — the jacket is over there, not in your hands.

Point now: one thing near you — "this…"; one thing far — "that…".

## Sub lesson 2 — These and those — many things

For MANY things, the words grow an -se:

- **these** — many things close to you: "**These** pens are mine." (in your hand)
- **those** — many things far from you: "**Those** pens are yours." (across the room)

One thing: this/that. Many things: these/those. Near: this/these. Far: that/those.

Say four: this book, that chair, these pens, those bags — with the real things in your room.

## Sub lesson 3 — Pointing in the shop — which one?

In a shop, the pointing words name the thing you want:

- "How much is **this**?" (holding it)
- "How much are **those**?" (pointing across the shop)
- "**Which one** do you want?" — "**This one**." / "**That one**."

The seller asks which one; the pointing word answers. Practise the exchange: point, ask the price, choose one.

## Say it to your companion

Take four things — put two near your companion and two far away. Point and say all four sentences: "This … is near. That … is far. These … are near. Those … are far." Then swap: your companion points, you say.

## Do It Yourself

Write six sentences: one with this, one with that, two with these and those about things in your room, one shop question ("How much is this?"), and one answer ("This one, please"). Read them aloud and point as you read.
