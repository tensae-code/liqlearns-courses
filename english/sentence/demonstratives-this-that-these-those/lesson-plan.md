# Lesson plan — This, That, These and Those

**Course:** English (Zero to Story)
**Title:** This, That, These and Those
**Lesson:** demonstratives-this-that-these-those — pointing at things: this one here, that one over there
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 27 — The Small Words That Point
**The one thing:** The four pointing words — this/that for one thing, these/those for many — chosen by how far the thing is from you.
**This lesson's own level:** Sentence lesson (pointing words): this/these for things near you, that/those for things far from you; singular against plural.

## Why this lesson exists

Mauritania's Grade 7 book teaches the pointing words as their own point inside its shopping lesson — this/these for close things, that/those for far things — and the course's pointing-words unit held articles, quantifiers and the some/any grid but no demonstratives lesson. This lesson is built from the placements under the everything-goes-in ruling (2026-11-06).

## Sub lessons

1. This and that — one thing, near or far
2. These and those — many things
3. Pointing in the shop — which one?

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
Nothing is needed before this lesson. Quantifiers counts how much; this lesson points at which one, and each stands alone.

## What the learner can do when it ends
- Point at one thing near you and say "this"; at one far thing and say "that".
- Point at many things near and far: "these" and "those".
- Ask "How much is this?" and "Which one — this one or that one?" in a shop.

## What you need
Nothing special — things near you and things across the room.

## The run of the lesson
1. ADD IMAGE: a jacket in the learner's hands and another jacket across the room. The learner points and tries "this" and "that" before any rule appears.
2. Teach sub lesson 1 with real things: a book in the hand — "this book"; a chair across the room — "that chair".
3. ADD GIF (1–2 seconds): three pens near, three pens far — the words grow: these, those.
4. Sub lesson 3 runs the shop talk: "Can I have that jacket, please? — Which one? — This one. How much is it?"

## Add-ons
- Point and name: the teacher points near/far, one/many; the learner says the full pointing phrase.
- Shop role-play: one is the seller, one the buyer, with things placed near and far on purpose.

## If the learner is stuck
Keep to this/that with one thing in the hand against one thing across the room until near/far is automatic. The plural pair is one step after.

## Teacher note
The Grade 7 book teaches the four words inside shopping talk — identifying the thing you want is their first job — so the lesson keeps the shop as the third sub lesson's ground.
