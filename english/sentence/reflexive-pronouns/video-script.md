# Video script — Reflexive pronouns

Super-simple voice. Read slow. The learner hears this
before or beside the written lesson.

---

Some words point back. Back to the doer.

I wash myself.
You wash yourself.
He washes himself. She washes herself.
It washes itself.
We wash ourselves.
You wash yourselves.
They wash themselves.

Myself. Yourself. Himself. Herself. Itself.
Ourselves. Yourselves. Themselves.
Self for one. Selves for many.
Careful: MYself — not meself. OURselves. THEMselves.

The action comes back.
She cut herself.
He taught himself English.
Enjoy yourselves!

Alone takes BY.
He lives by himself. Alone.
She did it by herself. No help.
I made this by myself.

And strong words.
I did it MYSELF. Nobody helped me.
The teacher herself came.

Now two people. The arrows cross.
They help EACH OTHER.
We write to ONE ANOTHER.
Not back — across.
Himself points back. Each other points across.

Now you try.
Say three things about your morning.
I washed myself. I dressed myself.
Say one alone thing. I did it by myself.
Say one crossing thing. We help each other.
