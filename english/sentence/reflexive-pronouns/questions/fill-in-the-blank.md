# Reflexive pronouns — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Reflexive Pronouns — the Forms and the Returning Action · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Reflexive Pronouns — Emphasis and By + Reflexive · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Reflexive or Each Other?.

Fill each gap with the right reflexive — or each other.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Be careful with that knife! You'll cut ______.
**Answer: yourself** — Yemen's own safety warning: the action returns to the doer.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The children dressed ______ without any help.
**Answer: themselves** — plural doers, -selves: themselves.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** My grandfather lives ______ — alone, in a small house by the river.
**Answer: by himself** — by + reflexive says alone (the by-phrase is the whole answer).

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Ana and Maria wrote letters to ______ all winter.
**Answer: each other (one another also earns it)** — the letters crossed between two people; no arrow bent back.
