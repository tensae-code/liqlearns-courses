# Reflexive pronouns — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Reflexive Pronouns — the Forms and the Returning Action · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Reflexive Pronouns — Emphasis and By + Reflexive · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Reflexive or Each Other?.

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** "Meself" is the correct first-person reflexive.
**Answer: False** — it is built on MY, not me: myself. (The two rebels: myself, and -selves in the plurals.)

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In "She cut herself", the word herself points at a different woman.
**Answer: False** — herself points BACK at she, the doer; "her" alone would point at a different woman.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** "He did it by himself" means somebody helped him quietly.
**Answer: False** — by himself means alone, unaided — the opposite of helped.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** "They love themselves" and "They love each other" mean the same thing.
**Answer: False** — themselves bends the love back to each own self; each other crosses it between them (Malaysia F5's paired set).
