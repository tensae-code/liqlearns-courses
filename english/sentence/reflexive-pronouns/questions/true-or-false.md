# Reflexive pronouns — true or false

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Level 1)** "Meself" is the correct first-person reflexive.
**Answer: False** — it is built on MY, not me: myself. (The two rebels: myself, and -selves in the plurals.)

**2. (Level 1)** In "She cut herself", the word herself points at a different woman.
**Answer: False** — herself points BACK at she, the doer; "her" alone would point at a different woman.

**3. (Level 2)** "He did it by himself" means somebody helped him quietly.
**Answer: False** — by himself means alone, unaided — the opposite of helped.

**4. (Level 3)** "They love themselves" and "They love each other" mean the same thing.
**Answer: False** — themselves bends the love back to each own self; each other crosses it between them (Malaysia F5's paired set).
