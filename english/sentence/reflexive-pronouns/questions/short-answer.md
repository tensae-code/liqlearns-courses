# Reflexive pronouns — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Reflexive Pronouns — the Forms and the Returning Action · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Reflexive Pronouns — Emphasis and By + Reflexive · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Reflexive or Each Other?.

Answer in whole sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say one true sentence about something you do to yourself every morning.
**Answer: I wash myself. / I dress myself before breakfast. (Any true returning-action sentence.)** — the reflexive names the same person twice, once.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Your friend carried all the bags alone. Say it with by.
**Answer: She / He carried all the bags by herself / himself.** — by + reflexive = alone, unaided.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Two sisters each combed their own hair. Now say it so it means each combed the OTHER's hair — change one word-pair only.
**Answer: They combed each other's hair.** — themselves (own hair) becomes each other's (crossing arrows).

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Correct this sentence: "We looked at ourselves in the photo and laughed at ourselves for helping ourselves" — one of the three is wrong: the friends helped ONE ANOTHER carry the tent.
**Answer: …laughed at ourselves for helping each other carry the tent.** — helping crossed between people, so each other; looking and laughing bent back, so ourselves stands.
