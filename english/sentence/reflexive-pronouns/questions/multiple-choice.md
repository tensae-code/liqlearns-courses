# Reflexive pronouns — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Reflexive Pronouns — the Forms and the Returning Action · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Reflexive Pronouns — Emphasis and By + Reflexive · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Reflexive or Each Other?.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** "He taught ______ English from a book."
a) himself  b) him  c) his self
**Answer: a) himself** — doer and done-to are the same person: the reflexive takes the seat (Bolivia Y6 T7).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** "We enjoyed ______ at the party."
a) us  b) ourselves  c) ourself
**Answer: b) ourselves** — the plural reflexive, with -selves: ourselves.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** "She travelled alone — all ______."
a) by herself  b) with herself  c) herself by
**Answer: a) by herself** — by + reflexive = alone, unaided (Yemen's review set).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** "I made this cake ______ — no one helped me."
a) me  b) myself  c) each other
**Answer: b) myself** — the reflexive as emphasis, at the sentence's end.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** "The two brothers help ______ with their homework."
a) themselves  b) each other  c) himself
**Answer: b) each other** — the action crosses between two people; a reflexive would bend it back (Malaysia F5's paired choice).
