## Fill in the blank

1. We ___ (have to) help at home. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: have to** — we takes have to.

2. He ___ (have to) catch the early bus. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: has to** — he takes has to.

3. Does she ___ (have to) come? (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: have to** — after does, always have to.

4. I ___ (have to) visit the doctor yesterday. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: had to** — yesterday → had to.

5. Did you ___ (have to) wait long? (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: have to** — after did, have to returns.

6. You ___ (not have to) bring a pen; I have two. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: don't have to** — not needed = don't have to.

7. She ___ (not have to) cook tonight; we are eating out. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: doesn't have to** — third person negative: doesn't have to.

8. We ___ (not have to) pay last time; it was free. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: didn't have to** — past + not needed.
