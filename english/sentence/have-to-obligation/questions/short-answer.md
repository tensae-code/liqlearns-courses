## Short answer

1. Say one thing you have to do today. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: “I have to do my homework.”** (any true sentence) — I + have to.

2. Say one thing your mother or father has to do. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: “She/He has to work.”** (any true sentence) — third person takes has to.

3. Ask a friend if they must go home now. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: “Do you have to go home now?”** — Do + have to.

4. Say one thing you had to do yesterday. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: “I had to get up early.”** (any true past sentence) — had to for the past.

5. Say one thing she had to do last week. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: “She had to stay at home.”** (any true sentence) — had to for every person.

6. Say one thing you don't have to do on Sundays. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: “I don't have to go to school.”** (any true sentence) — don't have to = not needed.

7. Say one thing that is forbidden at school, with mustn't. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: “We mustn't run in the hall.”** (any forbidden thing) — mustn't = not allowed, different from don't have to.

8. Say one thing you didn't have to do yesterday. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: “I didn't have to pay.”** (any true sentence) — past not needed.
