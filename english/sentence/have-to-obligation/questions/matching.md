## Matching

Match the sentence to its meaning. (Sub lessons 1–3)

1. I have to study. — a
2. She has to work. — b
3. Do you have to go? — c
4. I had to wait. — d
5. Did you have to pay? — e
6. I don't have to cook. — f
7. You mustn't run here. — g
8. We didn't have to pay. — h

a) a duty I have now
b) a duty she has now
c) asking about a duty now
d) a duty I had in the past
e) asking about a duty in the past
f) cooking is not needed for me
g) running is not allowed here
h) paying was not needed for us

**Answer key with reasons:** 1–a (have to now), 2–b (has to, third person), 3–c (do-question now), 4–d (had to, past), 5–e (did-question, past), 6–f (don't have to = not needed), 7–g (mustn't = forbidden), 8–h (didn't have to = past, not needed).
