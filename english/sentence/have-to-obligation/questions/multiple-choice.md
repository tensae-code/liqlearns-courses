## Multiple choice

1. I ___ study tonight. (Sub lesson 1)
   a) has to  b) have to  c) haves to
   **Answer: b) have to** — I takes have to.

2. She ___ work today. (Sub lesson 1)
   a) have to  b) has to  c) do have to
   **Answer: b) has to** — he/she/it takes has to.

3. “Do you ___ go now?” (Sub lesson 1)
   a) has to  b) have to  c) had to
   **Answer: b) have to** — after do/does it is always have to.

4. Yesterday I ___ get up at five. (Sub lesson 2)
   a) have to  b) has to  c) had to
   **Answer: c) had to** — past obligation is had to for everyone.

5. They ___ wait a long time yesterday. (Sub lesson 2)
   a) had to  b) have to  c) has to
   **Answer: a) had to** — they + past = had to.

6. It's Sunday — I ___ get up early! (not needed) (Sub lesson 3)
   a) mustn't  b) don't have to  c) hasn't to
   **Answer: b) don't have to** — don't have to = not needed.

7. “You ___ run in the hall — it is forbidden.” (Sub lesson 3)
   a) don't have to  b) mustn't  c) didn't have to
   **Answer: b) mustn't** — forbidden = mustn't; don't have to would mean “no need to run”.

8. We ___ pay yesterday; it was free. (Sub lesson 3)
   a) didn't have to  b) mustn't  c) don't has to
   **Answer: a) didn't have to** — past + not needed = didn't have to.
