# Hands-on — Have To for Obligation

Duties are real — practise on real ones.

## Duty lists

Write today's duty list for your family: who has to do what. Read it aloud in full sentences: “I have to feed the dog. My brother has to wash the dishes.”

## Freeing game

Take Sunday's list. Take turns crossing duties out with the sentence: “You don't have to…!” The last duty standing is the winner — or the loser!
