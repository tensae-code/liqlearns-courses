# Video script — Have To for Obligation

Super-simple voice. The presenter is the LiqLearns mascot, front-facing,
on screen the whole video; the pictures named here appear beside him.

[A morning list.] “I HAVE TO get up. I HAVE TO go to school. She HAS TO work. Have to says: it must be done!”

[The list fades to yesterday.] “Yesterday? I HAD TO get up at five. Had to — one form for everybody!”

[A Sunday list, lines crossing out.] “But Sunday… I DON'T HAVE TO get up early! Don't have to means: not needed. Not the same as mustn't — mustn't means not allowed!”
