# Video script — The past perfect

Super-simple voice. Read slow. The learner hears this
before or beside the written lesson.

---

Two things happened yesterday.

Both are past.

But one happened FIRST.

Listen.

When I arrived, the train had left.

Arrived — past.

Had left — earlier. The past inside the past.

The shape is small.

Had. And the third shape of the verb.

I had finished.

She had finished.

They had finished.

Had never changes.

By the time I got home, my brother had already eaten.

Already sits inside. Had — already — eaten.

One thing alone in the past? No had.

I locked the door yesterday. Just the past.

Two things? The earlier one wears had.

Now you try.

Tell me yesterday — with one step back.

When the film started, I had already found my seat.
