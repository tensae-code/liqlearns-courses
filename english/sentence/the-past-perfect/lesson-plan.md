# Lesson plan — The past perfect

**Course:** English · **Title:** Sentence · **Lesson:** The past perfect
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 19, "Earlier Than the Past" (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the past perfect — **had + past participle** — the earlier of two past actions: when a story is already in the past and one thing happened BEFORE another past thing, the earlier one wears had.
**What the learner can do at the end:** build had + participle for every who (it never changes); order two past actions with before, after, when and by the time; and keep the later action in the plain past simple.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Past actions (the participle is the past's third shape) and The present perfect (had is have's past). Recommended, never forced — this lesson stands alone for a learner who lands on it directly (DIRECT-ENTRY rule).
- Speaker convention: the explanation is read in the learner's main language; the TARGET forms are never translated and are read in the English voice.

## Media slots
1. **Slot 1 — a picture of two footprints on a path, the first one older and fainter, with the words HAD LEFT under the first and ARRIVED under the second.** What the student does: the student points at the older footprint and says which action happened first — had always stands on the earlier one.
2. **Slot 2 — a picture of a train pulling away from an empty platform as a family runs in at the gate.** What the student does: before reading on, the student says the two actions in time order — the train left FIRST, the family arrived SECOND — then hears which one wears had.
3. **Slot 3 — a picture of a clock face with two hands stopped at two different past times, the earlier time circled.** What the student does: the student names the earlier time aloud and builds one had sentence about it.

## The run of the lesson, in order
1. **Do it first**: a two-action story in the learner's own language; the earlier action is marked.
2. **The shape**: had + past participle; had never changes (Egypt Sec 12 T2 box; China G9 p.92; Kuwait Pearls 9B p.21).
3. **The job**: the earlier of two past actions — Sudan SPINE 5 Ch.6's "Too Late" story as the model.
4. **The travelling words**: before / after / when / by the time; already and just inside (China's frames).
5. **Asking and denying**: Had you finished? Yes, I had. / He hadn't seen it.
6. **The border**: one lone past action never wears had.
7. **Do it yourself**: the three closing tasks in the lesson.

## If the learner is stuck
- Stuck on the participle: it is the same third shape as in The present perfect — drill the triples (go–went–gone) and re-enter.
- Stuck on when to use it: ask "is there a LATER past action beside it?" If no second action stands in the past, the past simple is the coat.
- Stuck on had changing: it never does — had for I, she, they alike.
- This lesson's next door: The past perfect continuous (same level) — the earlier action shown still moving.
