# The past perfect — questions by level

Practice by level, tied to the skill number. The questions live in
the type files; this file is the by-level view. The levels are the
depths (RANKS) inside this one thing — the earlier of two past actions — set by the
pattern across the countries' books: the shape first, then its job,
then its borders with its neighbours.

## Level 1 — the shape
Level 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: build had + participle for every who, statements, not-forms and questions — had never changes, and the participle is the true third shape (gone, seen, eaten, left).

## Level 2 — the earlier step
Level 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: order two past actions live: the earlier wears had, the later wears the past simple, joined by before, after, when or by the time, with already/just in their seats.

## Level 3 — the border
Level 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: hold the line against the neighbours: no had for a lone past action, no past perfect where the present perfect's tie to NOW is meant, and read a short story picking out every had sentence and naming what it is earlier than.
