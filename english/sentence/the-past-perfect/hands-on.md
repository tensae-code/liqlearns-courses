# Hands-on — The past perfect

You do it, we guide you. Everything here is done in the
learner's real day, with a companion or the Explain
Companion. The TARGET forms are always said in English;
the talk around them may be in the learner's own language.

1. **The too-late game (Sudan's own story title).** One person names a late arrival — the bus stop, the cinema, the classroom door. The other must say what had already happened before it: "When you arrived, the bus had left." Five rounds; swap.
2. **Yesterday, backwards.** Tell your day backwards in four steps. Each step back must wear had: "I went to bed. Before that, I had eaten. Before that, I had come home…" The companion checks that every step back truly is earlier.
3. **The story fix.** Take any short story you know. Find its first event and say what had happened just before the story opens — one true had sentence that the story leaves out.
