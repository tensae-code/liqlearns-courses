# The past perfect — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Past Perfect Shape · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Past Perfect Earlier Step · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Past Perfect Border.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** "When we arrived, the train ______ left." a) has  b) had  c) have **Answer: b) had** — two past actions; the leaving is the earlier one, so had + participle.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The past participle of "eat" is — a) ate  b) eaten  c) eating **Answer: b) eaten** — the triple is eat–ate–eaten; ate is the past simple's shape.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** "By the time I got home, my brother ______ dinner." a) had cooked  b) cooks  c) has cooking **Answer: a) had cooked** — by the time names the later moment; the cooking stands earlier, in had.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Which sentence is RIGHT? a) I had locked the door yesterday, alone at noon.  b) When I got back, I found I had locked the door.  c) I have locked the door yesterday. **Answer: b)** — had needs a later past action beside it; (a) is a lone action and (c) ties an exact time to the present perfect.
