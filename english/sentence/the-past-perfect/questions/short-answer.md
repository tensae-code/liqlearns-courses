# The past perfect — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Past Perfect Shape · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Past Perfect Earlier Step · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Past Perfect Border.

Answer in a word or a whole sentence, as asked.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say it earlier: "The film started. I arrived late." Join them with when. **Answer: When I arrived, the film had already started** — the starting is earlier, so it wears had; arriving stays past simple.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Had you eaten before school today? Answer in a short answer. **Answer: Yes, I had. / No, I hadn't** — short answers ride had, the tense's only moving piece.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Tell one true thing that had happened before you woke up today. **Answer: (Learner's own true sentence in had + participle)** — any true earlier action earns it — the waking is the later past point.
