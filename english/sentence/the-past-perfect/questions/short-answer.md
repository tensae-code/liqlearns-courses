# The past perfect — short answer

Answer in a word or a whole sentence, as asked.

**1. (Level 1)** Say it earlier: "The film started. I arrived late." Join them with when. **Answer: When I arrived, the film had already started** — the starting is earlier, so it wears had; arriving stays past simple.

**2. (Level 2)** Had you eaten before school today? Answer in a short answer. **Answer: Yes, I had. / No, I hadn't** — short answers ride had, the tense's only moving piece.

**3. (Level 3)** Tell one true thing that had happened before you woke up today. **Answer: (Learner's own true sentence in had + participle)** — any true earlier action earns it — the waking is the later past point.
