# The past perfect — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Past Perfect Shape · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Past Perfect Earlier Step · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Past Perfect Border.

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Had changes with the who: "she haves, they hads." **Answer: False** — had is the same for every who; nothing is added.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** "I had read the book in 2020" is the best coat for one lone past action with its year named. **Answer: False** — a lone, dated past action wears the past simple: "I read the book in 2020." Had needs a later past neighbour.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In "After she had locked the door, she went to bed," the locking happened first. **Answer: True** — after + past perfect marks the earlier step; the past simple carries the main line.
