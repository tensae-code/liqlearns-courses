# The past perfect — true or false

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Level 1)** Had changes with the who: "she haves, they hads." **Answer: False** — had is the same for every who; nothing is added.

**2. (Level 2)** "I had read the book in 2020" is the best coat for one lone past action with its year named. **Answer: False** — a lone, dated past action wears the past simple: "I read the book in 2020." Had needs a later past neighbour.

**3. (Level 3)** In "After she had locked the door, she went to bed," the locking happened first. **Answer: True** — after + past perfect marks the earlier step; the past simple carries the main line.
