# The past perfect — matching

Match each item to its partner.

**1. (Level 1)** "the train left at six" beside "we arrived at seven" **Answer: the train had left** — the earlier of the two past actions wears had.

**2. (Level 2)** "for two hours" beside "since Monday" in a past-perfect sentence **Answer: neither — those measures belong to the continuous lessons** — the simple past perfect counts the done step, not a stretch; stretches are the next lesson's ground.

**3. (Level 3)** "When I arrived, the lesson ______" **Answer: had already started** — already sits between had and the participle, before the later arrival.
