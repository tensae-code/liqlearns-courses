# The past perfect — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Past Perfect Shape · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Past Perfect Earlier Step · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Past Perfect Border.

Fill each gap with the right shape.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** She ______ (finish) her work before the bell rang. **Answer: had finished** — had + participle; finishing is earlier than the bell.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** They ______ (never / see) snow before that winter. (negative) **Answer: had never seen** — never sits between had and the participle.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** ______ you ______ (meet) him before the party? **Answer: Had … met** — had steps to the front to ask.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** After he ______ (leave), we found his keys on the table. **Answer: had left** — after marks his leaving as the earlier step.
