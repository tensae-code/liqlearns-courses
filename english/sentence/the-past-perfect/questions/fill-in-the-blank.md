# The past perfect — fill in the blank

Fill each gap with the right shape.

**1. (Level 1)** She ______ (finish) her work before the bell rang. **Answer: had finished** — had + participle; finishing is earlier than the bell.

**2. (Level 1)** They ______ (never / see) snow before that winter. (negative) **Answer: had never seen** — never sits between had and the participle.

**3. (Level 2)** ______ you ______ (meet) him before the party? **Answer: Had … met** — had steps to the front to ask.

**4. (Level 3)** After he ______ (leave), we found his keys on the table. **Answer: had left** — after marks his leaving as the earlier step.
