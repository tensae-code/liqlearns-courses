# Video script — Introductory There

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Point far away. "There!" That there points.

Now look at the table. One book.
**There is** a book on the table.
This there does not point. It only starts the sentence.

Three books now!
**There are** three books on the table.

One thing — **there is**.
Many things — **there are**.
The word after tells you which one.
Your turn — open the sentence!
