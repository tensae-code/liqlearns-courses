# Multiple choice — Introductory There

Sub-lesson markers: every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to — Sub lesson 1 — There that points nowhere · Sub lesson 2 — There is, one thing · Sub lesson 3 — There are, more than one. This lesson was built new with its sub lessons (Bangladesh re-verification sweep merge, 2026-11-08), so its markers are original — there are no older depth layers to map from.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In "There is a book on the table," the word "there" —
a) points at the table  b) points at the book  c) points nowhere — it only opens the sentence  d) means "here"
**Answer: c)** — this there is the opener: it introduces the sentence and points at nothing.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In "There are three books on the table," the real subject is —
a) there  b) are  c) three books  d) the table
**Answer: c) three books** — with introductory there, the real subject comes after the verb.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** "___ a school in our village."
a) There are  b) There is  c) There pointing  d) There be
**Answer: b) There is** — the real subject, a school, is one thing, so the verb is is.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which sentence opens the right way for ONE thing?
a) There are a cat at the door.  b) There is a cat at the door.  c) There are one cat at the door.  d) Is there are a cat at the door.
**Answer: b)** — one cat takes There is.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** "___ many students in the class."
a) There is  b) There pointing  c) There are  d) There was one
**Answer: c) There are** — many students is more than one, so the verb is are.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** "There ___ two chairs in the room." The word is —
a) is, because "there" is one word  b) is, because a room is one  c) are, because the real subject — two chairs — is more than one  d) is, because chairs cannot be counted
**Answer: c) are** — the verb fits the real subject after it, never the there that opens.
