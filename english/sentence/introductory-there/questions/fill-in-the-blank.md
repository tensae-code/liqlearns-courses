# Fill in the blank — Introductory There

Sub-lesson markers: every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to — Sub lesson 1 — There that points nowhere · Sub lesson 2 — There is, one thing · Sub lesson 3 — There are, more than one. This lesson was built new with its sub lessons (Bangladesh re-verification sweep merge, 2026-11-08), so its markers are original — there are no older depth layers to map from.

Fill each gap with the word that fits.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In "There is a book on the table," the real subject — a book — comes ______ the verb.
**Answer: after** — introductory there holds the door; the real subject walks in after the verb.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The "there" that starts "There is a book on the table" points at ______.
**Answer: nothing (nowhere)** — it only opens the sentence; the pointing there is a different word at work, as in "over there!"

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** There ______ a cup on the table.
**Answer: is** — one cup, one thing, so is.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** ______ is a teacher in the class.
**Answer: There** — the opener that introduces the sentence.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** There ______ thirty students in the class.
**Answer: are** — thirty students is more than one, so are.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** To choose between is and are, cover the there and read the ______ subject that follows.
**Answer: real** — the real subject after the verb always decides; the there never decides.
