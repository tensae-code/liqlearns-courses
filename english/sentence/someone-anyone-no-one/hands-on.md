# Hands-on — Someone, anyone, no one

You do it, we guide you. Everything here is done with a
companion or the Explain Companion, out loud.

1. **Knock, knock (Bolivia's box, live).** One person
   hides behind a door or a chair. The other must ask
   three questions — "Is anyone there?" "Does someone
   want…?" — and the hidden one answers with grid words
   only: "Someone is here." "No one is here." Five
   knocks; the some/any choice is scored each time.
2. **The empty-chair report.** Set a place at the table
   for a guest who does not come. Report the scene in
   six grid sentences: "Someone set a plate. No one sat
   down. Nothing was eaten…" The verb after a no- word
   must stay positive — a doubled no restarts the report.
3. **The lost-thing hunt.** Hide a small thing in the
   room. The hunter may only ask grid questions ("Is it
   somewhere high?" "Did anyone hide it?") and the
   guide may only answer with grid words ("It is
   somewhere warm." "No one touched it."). Found in
   under ten exchanges is a pass.
4. **Grid at speed.** Your companion calls a crossing —
   "NO + place!" "EVERY + thing!" "ANY + person!" — and
   you must shout the word and use it in a sentence
   before they count to three. Sixteen crossings is the
   whole grid.
