# Someone, anyone, no one — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** "______ ate the cake. The plate is empty!"
a) Someone  b) Anyone  c) No one
**Answer: a) Someone** — a positive statement takes the some- row (Bolivia's box).

**2. (Level 1)** "I didn't see ______ at the station."
a) someone  b) anyone  c) everyone
**Answer: b) anyone** — a negative sentence takes the any- row (Bolivia).

**3. (Level 2)** "Who helped you?" — "______. I did it all myself."
a) Anyone  b) Someone  c) No one
**Answer: c) No one** — the no- row answers alone, with no second negative needed.

**4. (Level 2)** Which sentence is RIGHT?
a) Nobody didn't come.  b) Nobody came.  c) I didn't see nobody.
**Answer: b) Nobody came** — one no per sentence: the no- word carries the negative, the verb stays positive.

**5. (Level 3)** "______ is ready for the trip."
a) Everyone  b) Everyones  c) Someone places
**Answer: a) Everyone** — the every- row takes a singular verb and no plural -s (South Africa's full list).
