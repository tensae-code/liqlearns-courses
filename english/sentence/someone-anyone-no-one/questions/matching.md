# Someone, anyone, no one — matching

Match each word to its crossing (row × door).

**1. (Level 1)** "anywhere" **Answer: ANY × place** — the any- row through the place door (questions and negatives).

**2. (Level 2)** "nobody" **Answer: NO × person** — nobody = no one; it carries its own no, so its verb stays positive.

**3. (Level 3)** "everything" **Answer: EVERY × thing** — the full set for things, with a singular verb (Mongolia X U9's discovery set).
