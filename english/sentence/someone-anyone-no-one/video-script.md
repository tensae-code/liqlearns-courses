# Video script — Someone, anyone, no one

Super-simple voice. Read slow. The learner hears this
before or beside the written lesson.

---

Knock, knock. Who is there?
SOMEONE is there. I do not know who.
Someone ate the cake.
Someone is at the door.

Questions take ANYONE.
Is anyone home?
Does anyone want pizza? — hmm, listen.
A question that hopes for yes can take someone.
Does someone want pizza? The books show both.
But a plain question takes anyone.

No answers take anyone too.
I don't know anyone here.
I didn't see anything.
We didn't go anywhere.

And the NO family stands alone.
NO ONE came. Nobody came.
NOTHING happened.
We went NOWHERE.
One no only. Nobody came — not "nobody didn't come".

EVERY is the full row.
EVERYONE is here. Everyone — one word, one verb: IS.
EVERYTHING is ready.
EVERYWHERE — all places.

Four rows. Three doors.
People: someone, anyone, no one, everyone.
Things: something, anything, nothing, everything.
Places: somewhere, anywhere, nowhere, everywhere.

Now you try.
Answer me.
Who did it? — Someone.
What did you see? — Nothing.
Where did you go? — Nowhere.
Good. The grid is yours.
