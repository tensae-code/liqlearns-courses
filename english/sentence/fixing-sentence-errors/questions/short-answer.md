# Fixing sentence errors — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Fixing sentence errors — the fragment · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Fixing sentence errors — run-ons and comma splices · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Fixing sentence errors — dangling modifiers and parallelism.

Answer in whole sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Fix the fragment: "The book on the table near the window."
**Answer: The book on the table near the window is mine. (any true verb completes it)** — the piece had a subject but no verb; the verb is the repair.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Fix the comma splice two ways: "The bus was late, we walked."
**Answer: The bus was late, so we walked. / The bus was late; we walked. (full stop or Because… also earns it)** — coordinator and semicolon — two of the four honest joints.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Fix the dangling modifier: "Covered in mud, the farmer washed the dog."
**Answer: Covered in mud, the dog was washed by the farmer. (or: The farmer washed the dog, which was covered in mud.)** — as written, the farmer is covered in mud; the description must sit on its true owner.
