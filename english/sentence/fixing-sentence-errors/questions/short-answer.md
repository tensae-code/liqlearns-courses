# Fixing sentence errors — short answer

Answer in whole sentences.

**1. (Level 1)** Fix the fragment: "The book on the table near the window."
**Answer: The book on the table near the window is mine. (any true verb completes it)** — the piece had a subject but no verb; the verb is the repair.

**2. (Level 2)** Fix the comma splice two ways: "The bus was late, we walked."
**Answer: The bus was late, so we walked. / The bus was late; we walked. (full stop or Because… also earns it)** — coordinator and semicolon — two of the four honest joints.

**3. (Level 3)** Fix the dangling modifier: "Covered in mud, the farmer washed the dog."
**Answer: Covered in mud, the dog was washed by the farmer. (or: The farmer washed the dog, which was covered in mud.)** — as written, the farmer is covered in mud; the description must sit on its true owner.
