# Fixing sentence errors — fill in the blank

Complete each repair.

**1. (Level 1)** Complete the fragment: "After the lesson ended" → "After the lesson ended, ______ ." (any true completion)
**Answer: we went home (any true main clause)** — a dependent clause needs its main clause to stand (Sierra Leone, SSS2, Lesson 40).

**2. (Level 2)** Join honestly with a coordinator: "It rained hard ______ we stayed inside."
**Answer: so** — rain is the cause, staying inside the result — so is the joint that says it.

**3. (Level 3)** Straighten the list: "She enjoys singing, to dance, and ______ ." (one shape for all)
**Answer: dancing (or: to sing, to dance, and to play — one shape throughout)** — parallelism: every item in the same form (Ethiopia, Grade 12, Unit 6, p.151).
