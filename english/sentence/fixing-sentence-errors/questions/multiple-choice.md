# Fixing sentence errors — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Fixing sentence errors — the fragment · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Fixing sentence errors — run-ons and comma splices · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Fixing sentence errors — dangling modifiers and parallelism.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which one is a fragment? a) The rain came. b) Because the rain came suddenly. c) The rain came suddenly, and we ran.
**Answer: b)** — a dependent clause standing alone — it never says what happened because of the rain (Ethiopia, Grade 11, Unit 1).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** "The shop was shut, we went home." This is: a) a fragment b) a comma splice c) faulty parallelism
**Answer: b)** — two full sentences joined by a comma alone; a comma is a pause, not a joint.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Best repair for "The shop was shut, we went home": a) The shop was shut; we went home. b) The shop was shut, we, went home. c) The shop was shut we went home.
**Answer: a)** — the semicolon is one of the four honest joints; the others keep or worsen the crash.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** "Walking home, the stars came out." The error is: a) a dangling modifier b) a run-on c) a comma splice
**Answer: a)** — the stars were not walking home — the description's true doer is missing (Ethiopia, Grade 12, Unit 4).
