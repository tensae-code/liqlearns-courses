# Fixing sentence errors — matching

Match each broken sentence to its error's name.

**1. (Level 1)** "Under the big tree near the river."
**Answer: fragment** — no subject and no verb — a place phrase wearing a full stop.

**2. (Level 2)** "I called him he did not answer I called again."
**Answer: run-on** — three sentences crashed with no signal between them (Ethiopia, Grade 12, Unit 2).

**3. (Level 3)** "Full of dust, the boy sneezed all morning." — is this dangling? Name the verdict.
**Answer: not dangling — the boy is full of dust and he is named next** — a modifier dangles only when its doer is missing or wrong; here the doer stands beside it.
