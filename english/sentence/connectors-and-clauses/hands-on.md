# Hands-on — Connectors and clauses

You do it, we guide you. Everything here is done with a
companion or the Explain Companion.

1. **The five joins.** The companion says five plain
   pairs about the day (I woke up late. I ran to school.)
   and names a job — reason! purpose! contrast! The
   learner must join the pair with a connector that does
   that job, aloud, in one breath.
2. **The two doors.** Cards are laid out: some carry
   clauses ("it was raining", "she was ill"), some carry
   nouns ("the rain", "her illness"). The learner sends
   each card through the BECAUSE door or the BECAUSE OF
   door, then again through ALTHOUGH or IN SPITE OF.
3. **Move it to the front.** The learner takes three
   joined sentences and moves the clause to the front,
   saying the comma out loud as a short pause: "Although
   it rained — pause — we played outside."
4. **The however rewrite.** One paragraph of the
   learner's own writing with three buts: each but-join
   is rewritten once with however as a new sentence,
   full stop and comma placed and read aloud.
