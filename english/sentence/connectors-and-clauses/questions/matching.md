# Connectors and clauses — matching

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Connectors and clauses — the five jobs · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Connectors and clauses — time, reason and purpose clauses · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Connectors and clauses — contrast and concession.

Match each pair of sentences to the connector that joins them truly.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** "He studied hard. He passed the exam." (result of the first in the second, stated forward) — Therefore / Although / Until
**Answer: Therefore** — the passing is the result; Mauritania's cause-effect set runs in both directions.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** "The shop was shut. We needed bread." joined with a purpose turned into the past: "We went to the shop ______ buy bread."
**Answer: in order to** — in order to + base verb carries purpose with the same subject (Nepal Grade 10, Unit 8).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** "Sara likes tea. Omar prefers coffee." as one balanced contrast sentence — on the other hand / because of / so that
**Answer: on the other hand** — the balanced second side is on the other hand's job (Kuwait, Pearls 9B, p.33).
