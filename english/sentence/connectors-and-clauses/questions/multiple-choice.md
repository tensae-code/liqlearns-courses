# Connectors and clauses — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** Join by REASON: "I went to bed early. I was tired." a) I went to bed early although I was tired. b) I went to bed early because I was tired. c) I went to bed early so that I was tired.
**Answer: b)** — because carries the reason clause; although would claim a contrast that is not there.

**2. (Level 1)** Join by PURPOSE: "She saved money. She wanted to buy a bicycle." a) She saved money so that she could buy a bicycle. b) She saved money because she could buy a bicycle. c) She saved money however she could buy a bicycle.
**Answer: a)** — so that + clause states the purpose, with could for the aimed-at future.

**3. (Level 2)** Which sentence is right? a) We stayed home because of it rained. b) We stayed home because of the rain. c) We stayed home because the rain.
**Answer: b)** — because of takes a noun — the rain; a clause after it breaks the door rule (Nepal Grade 10, Unit 8).

**4. (Level 3)** Which punctuation is right? a) It was late, however we kept working. b) It was late. However, we kept working. c) It was late however, we kept working.
**Answer: b)** — however is a sentence connector: full stop before it, comma after it (Kuwait, Pearls 9B, p.33).
