# Connectors and clauses — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Connectors and clauses — the five jobs · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Connectors and clauses — time, reason and purpose clauses · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Connectors and clauses — contrast and concession.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Join by REASON: "I went to bed early. I was tired." a) I went to bed early although I was tired. b) I went to bed early because I was tired. c) I went to bed early so that I was tired.
**Answer: b)** — because carries the reason clause; although would claim a contrast that is not there.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Join by PURPOSE: "She saved money. She wanted to buy a bicycle." a) She saved money so that she could buy a bicycle. b) She saved money because she could buy a bicycle. c) She saved money however she could buy a bicycle.
**Answer: a)** — so that + clause states the purpose, with could for the aimed-at future.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which sentence is right? a) We stayed home because of it rained. b) We stayed home because of the rain. c) We stayed home because the rain.
**Answer: b)** — because of takes a noun — the rain; a clause after it breaks the door rule (Nepal Grade 10, Unit 8).

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Which punctuation is right? a) It was late, however we kept working. b) It was late. However, we kept working. c) It was late however, we kept working.
**Answer: b)** — however is a sentence connector: full stop before it, comma after it (Kuwait, Pearls 9B, p.33).
