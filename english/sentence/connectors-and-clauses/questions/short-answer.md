# Connectors and clauses — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Connectors and clauses — the five jobs · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 3 — Connectors and clauses — contrast and concession · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Connectors and clauses — contrast and concession.

Answer in whole sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Join with a time clause: "The bell rang. The pupils stood up." (use when)
**Answer: When the bell rang, the pupils stood up.** — when + clause at the front, comma after the clause.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Rewrite with although: "It was raining. We played football."
**Answer: Although it was raining, we played football.** — although carries the clause; in spite of would need the noun the rain.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Rewrite the but-join as a however-join: "The test was hard, but I finished it."
**Answer: The test was hard. However, I finished it.** — however leaves the clause and starts a new sentence — full stop before, comma after.
