# Connectors and clauses — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 2 — Connectors and clauses — time, reason and purpose clauses · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 3 — Connectors and clauses — contrast and concession · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 4 — Connectors and clauses — result connectors.

Fill each gap with the right connector.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** ______ I finished my work, I went to the park. (time, front position)
**Answer: After** — a fronted time clause, with its comma after the clause (Bolivia, Year 5, Topic 4).

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** In spite ______ the cold, the children played outside.
**Answer: of** — in spite of is the whole connector, and it takes a noun — the cold (Nepal Grade 10, Unit 9).

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** The road was closed. ______, we had to walk.
**Answer: Therefore** — a result connector opening its own sentence, comma after it (Mauritania, Lesson 6 set).
