# Connectors and clauses — fill in the blank

Fill each gap with the right connector.

**1. (Level 1)** ______ I finished my work, I went to the park. (time, front position)
**Answer: After** — a fronted time clause, with its comma after the clause (Bolivia, Year 5, Topic 4).

**2. (Level 2)** In spite ______ the cold, the children played outside.
**Answer: of** — in spite of is the whole connector, and it takes a noun — the cold (Nepal Grade 10, Unit 9).

**3. (Level 3)** The road was closed. ______, we had to walk.
**Answer: Therefore** — a result connector opening its own sentence, comma after it (Mauritania, Lesson 6 set).
