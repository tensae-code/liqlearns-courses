# Connectors and clauses — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Connectors and clauses — the five jobs · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Connectors and clauses — time, reason and purpose clauses · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 4 — Connectors and clauses — result connectors.

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** "Because of" can stand before a whole clause, like "because of it rained".
**Answer: False** — because of takes a noun only; a clause takes because (Nepal Grade 10, Unit 8).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A time clause can go at the beginning of a sentence to emphasise the time.
**Answer: True** — Bolivia's form lesson states it in those terms (Year 5, Topic 4).

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** "It rained, therefore we stayed home" is a correct joined sentence.
**Answer: False** — a comma alone cannot join two sentences under therefore — that is a comma splice; therefore needs a full stop (or semicolon) before it.
