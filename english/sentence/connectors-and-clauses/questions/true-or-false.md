# Connectors and clauses — true or false

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Level 1)** "Because of" can stand before a whole clause, like "because of it rained".
**Answer: False** — because of takes a noun only; a clause takes because (Nepal Grade 10, Unit 8).

**2. (Level 2)** A time clause can go at the beginning of a sentence to emphasise the time.
**Answer: True** — Bolivia's form lesson states it in those terms (Year 5, Topic 4).

**3. (Level 3)** "It rained, therefore we stayed home" is a correct joined sentence.
**Answer: False** — a comma alone cannot join two sentences under therefore — that is a comma splice; therefore needs a full stop (or semicolon) before it.
