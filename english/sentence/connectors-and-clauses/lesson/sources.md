**Acceptance count: taught in 6 countries (Bolivia,
Mauritania, Kuwait, Vietnam, Nepal, Libya).** Bolivia
gives the time-clause form lesson and the
but/however warning. Mauritania builds its whole
cause-and-effect unit on the connector sets. Kuwait
devotes one grammar lesson to the four contrast linkers
with their punctuation. Vietnam tables the linking
words by function at Grade 11 and completes the
adverbial-clause map at Grade 12. Nepal teaches the
connectives whole at Grade 8 and splits reason/purpose
from concession at Grade 10. Libya is cited under its standing banner: the libya.md read is agent-assisted and the 10-citation spot-check is still owed; every Libya line above is worded within that state. Two filing
checks are recorded plainly: the catch-up plan's Nepal

10 Units 8–9 and in Grade 8 Unit 15, and those are what
is cited. And no Libya book in the filing teaches a
