# Connectors and clauses — questions by level

Practice by level, tied to the skill number. The questions live in
the type files; this file is the by-level view. The levels are the
depths inside this one thing — joining sentences by meaning, not
by glue — set by the pattern across the countries' books: the
jobs first (Vietnam's and Nepal's tables by function), then the
clause shapes, then position and punctuation (Kuwait's lesson).

## Level 1 — the five jobs
Level 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: pick
the connector family by the job — time, reason, purpose,
contrast, result — and join a pair with when, because,
so that, although or therefore without changing the
meaning.

## Level 2 — clause or noun?
Level 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: keep
the pairs apart live — because + clause against because
of + noun, although + clause against in spite of /
despite + noun — and build time clauses with before,
after, until and while in both positions.

## Level 3 — position and punctuation
Level 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: front
a clause with its comma; place however, therefore,
consequently and on the other hand as sentence
connectors with a full stop (or semicolon) before and a
comma after; and rewrite a but-join as a however-join
without breaking the sentence.
