# Connectors and clauses — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB
LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub
Lessons"). Practice is tied to the skill number, as before. The
questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson
view. The sub lessons are the depths inside this one thing —
joining sentences by meaning, not by glue — cut at the lesson's
own jumps: the jobs first (Vietnam's and Nepal's tables by
function), then the clause shapes, then position and punctuation
(Kuwait's lesson).

Mapping from the old depth layers: old Level 1 = Sub lesson 1;
old Level 2 splits at its natural jump — its time clauses and
because pair are Sub lesson 2, and its in spite of pair is Sub
lesson 3; old Level 3 splits the same way — fronting a clause is
Sub lesson 2 (time) and Sub lesson 3 (contrast), however is Sub
lesson 3, and the result connectors are Sub lesson 4. The old
pass marks split with them below.

## Sub lesson 1 — Connectors and clauses — the five jobs
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: pick
the connector family by the job — time, reason, purpose,
contrast, result — and join a pair with when, because,
so that, although or therefore without changing the
meaning.

## Sub lesson 2 — Connectors and clauses — time, reason and purpose clauses
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: build
time clauses with before, after, until and while in both
positions, and front a clause with its comma; and keep the
reason pair apart live — because + clause against because
of + noun — with so that and in order to carrying purpose.

## Sub lesson 3 — Connectors and clauses — contrast and concession
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: keep
although + clause apart from in spite of / despite + noun;
place however and on the other hand as sentence
connectors with a full stop (or semicolon) before and a
comma after; and rewrite a but-join as a however-join
without breaking the sentence.

## Sub lesson 4 — Connectors and clauses — result connectors
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: place
therefore, consequently and as a result as sentence
connectors with a full stop (or semicolon) before and a
comma after — never a comma alone between two sentences.
