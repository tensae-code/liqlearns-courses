# Lesson plan — Time Clauses

**Course:** English (from the world's government textbooks)
**Title:** Time Clauses
**Lesson:** Time Clauses
**Place in the course:** Sentence course, Unit 13 (Joining and Describing) — beside joining-with-and-and-but and relative-clauses.

**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Joining two actions with a time word — *when, while, as, as soon as, before, after, until* — and getting the tenses right on both sides.

Level: set by the material's own depth, not by a school grade — the learner's level inside the lesson is their sub lesson.

## Why this lesson exists

Kazakhstan's Full Blast Grade 8 teaches time clauses twice (Module 3 for the present–future pattern, Module 5 adding *when/while/as/as soon as* with the past perfect) and no course lesson carried the strand. Under the everything-goes-in rule it gets its own lesson.

## Sub lessons

1. When, while, as, as soon as — joining two times
2. Time clauses with the future — no will after when

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson

Nothing is forced. A learner can open this lesson directly; the course shows easier lessons only as a soft hint.

## What the learner can do when they finish

Use what this lesson teaches on their own, in speech and in writing, without the lesson open in front of them.

## What you need

Nothing special — the learner's book or device and their explain companion.

## The run of the lesson

1. **See the sub lessons listed.** The learner sees both parts from the start; the second is locked.
2. **Sub lesson 1.** Learn it in lesson/lesson.md, with the media slots in hands-on.md where they fit.
3. **Prove it.** At most 5 questions drawn from sub lesson 1's whole question set, mixed with earlier parts where the course has them. Fail: review the questions and answers just done, then a new set — never the same set, and nothing ever re-locks.
4. **Sub lesson 2.** Opens when sub lesson 1 is proved.
5. **Do It Yourself.** The closing block in lesson/lesson.md.
