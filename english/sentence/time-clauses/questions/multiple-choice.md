# Multiple choice — Time Clauses

(Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson(s) named — no hardness ranking is written here, so a sub lesson's whole question set is its gate pool.)

1. Which word means "immediately after"?
   A) while
   B) as soon as
   C) before
   D) until
   **Answer: B** (Sub lesson 1)

2. "While I was cooking, the phone rang." The cooking was:
   A) after the phone rang
   B) happening when the phone rang
   C) finished long before
   D) in the future
   **Answer: B** (Sub lesson 1)

3. Where does the comma go? "When the lesson ended we went home."
   A) after "we"
   B) after "ended"
   C) no comma is needed
   D) after "the"
   **Answer: B** (Sub lesson 1)

4. Choose the correct sentence.
   A) When I will get home, I will phone you.
   B) When I get home, I will phone you.
   C) When I got home yesterday, I will phone you.
   D) When I getting home, I will phone.
   **Answer: B** (Sub lesson 2)

5. "As soon as the rain stops, we will go out" — inside the time clause we use:
   A) will stop
   B) stops
   C) stopped yesterday
   D) stopping
   **Answer: B** (Sub lesson 2)

6. In "When I had finished, I went to bed", the finishing happened:
   A) after going to bed
   B) before going to bed
   C) at the same moment
   D) never
   **Answer: B** (Sub lesson 2)
