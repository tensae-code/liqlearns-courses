# Fill in the blank — Time Clauses

(Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson(s) named — no hardness ranking is written here, so a sub lesson's whole question set is its gate pool.)

1. _____ the bell rang, the children left the classroom at once. (As soon as / While cooking)
   **Answer: As soon as** (Sub lesson 1)

2. Wash your hands _____ you eat.
   **Answer: before** (Sub lesson 1)

3. I will wait here until you _____ ready. (are / will are)
   **Answer: are** (Sub lesson 2)

4. When she _____ tomorrow, we will start. (arrives / will arrives)
   **Answer: arrives** (Sub lesson 2)
