# Matching — Time Clauses

(Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson(s) named — no hardness ranking is written here, so a sub lesson's whole question set is its gate pool.)

1. Match the time word to its meaning.
   1. while
   2. as soon as
   3. until
   a. immediately after
   b. during the time that
   c. up to the time that
   **Answer: 1-b, 2-a, 3-c** (Sub lesson 1)

2. Match the half to its correct partner.
   1. When I get home,
   2. As soon as the rain stops,
   3. I will wait until
   a. we will go out.
   b. I will phone you.
   c. you are ready.
   **Answer: 1-b, 2-a, 3-c** (Sub lesson 2)
