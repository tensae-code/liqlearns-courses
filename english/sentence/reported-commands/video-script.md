# Video script — Reported commands

Super-simple voice. Read slow. The learner hears this
before or beside the written lesson.

---

Mother says: "Sweep the house."
Later, you tell your friend.
"Mother told Gatete to sweep the house."

Look at the shape.
Told. Then the person. Then TO.
Then the verb, just as it was.
Told — Gatete — to sweep.

A command has no tense.
Nothing steps back.
The verb freezes behind TO.

"Wait here." → He told me to wait here.
"Help me, please." → She asked me to help her.
Please makes it a request. So: asked.

"Don't touch it!" → He warned me not to touch it.
Don't go there. → She told us not to go there.

Look at the NOT.
Not stands before TO.
Not to touch. Not to go.
Always in that order.

The verb tells the force.
A friend hopes: ASKED me to wait.
A teacher speaks: TOLD me to wait.
A guard shouts: ORDERED me to wait.
Good counsel: ADVISED me to rest.
Danger ahead: WARNED me not to run.

Many verbs can carry the parcel.
Ask. Tell. Advise. Warn.
Order. Command. Instruct.
Invite. Encourage. Remind. Persuade.

Same shape for all of them.
Verb. Person. To. Action.

Now you try.
Three orders from home.
Tell me who said them.
Pack each one: told me to…, asked me to…,
warned me not to…
