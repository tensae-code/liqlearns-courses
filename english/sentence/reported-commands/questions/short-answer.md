# Reported commands — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Reported Commands Shape · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Reported Commands Negative · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Reported Commands Verb Force.

Answer in whole sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Report it: "Bring me the book," the
teacher said to Ana.
**Answer: The teacher told Ana to bring him the book.**
(the person turns with the report) — verb + person +
to-infinitive.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Report it: "Don't leave your bag here,"
said Mother to Gatete.
**Answer: Mother told Gatete not to leave his bag
there.** — not before to; your turns to his, here to
there; Rwanda's own scene, Unit 9.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Report the request: "Please wait for
me," she said.
**Answer: She asked me to wait for her.** — please marks
a request, so asked carries it.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** A sign says: KEEP OFF THE GRASS. Report
it as a warning an adult gives children.
**Answer: She warned the children to keep off the
grass.** (…warned them not to walk on the grass also
earns it) — warn carries rules and danger; the shape
does not change.
