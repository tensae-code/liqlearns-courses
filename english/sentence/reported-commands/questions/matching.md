# Reported commands — matching

Match each direct command to its report.

**1. (Level 1)** "Prepare the tea." (a mother, at home)
**Answer: Mother told Gatete to prepare the tea.** —
the plain carrier for a home order, Rwanda's scene
(Pupil's Book 6, pp.114–115).

**2. (Level 2)** "Don't be late." **Answer: He reminded
us not to be late.** (told us not to… also matches) —
a negative carried by not + to-infinitive, with remind
from Vietnam's bank for a memory-point command.

**3. (Level 3)** "You must see the head teacher at
once." (the secretary, formally) **Answer: The
secretary instructed me to see the head teacher at
once.** — instruct sits at the formal, power end of
Vietnam's eleven-verb bank.
