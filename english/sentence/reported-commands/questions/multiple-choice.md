# Reported commands — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** Direct: "Sweep the house." Reported:
a) Mother told Gatete sweep the house.  b) Mother told
Gatete to sweep the house.  c) Mother told Gatete that
sweep the house.
**Answer: b)** — verb + person + to-infinitive, Rwanda's
own model sentence (Pupil's Book 6, Unit 9, p.115).

**2. (Level 1)** Direct (a polite request): "Please help
me." Reported:
a) She asked me to help her.  b) She ordered me help
her.  c) She told me helping her.
**Answer: a)** — a request rides on asked, with the same
shape: person + to-infinitive (Nepal Grade 8, Unit 5).

**3. (Level 2)** Direct: "Don't eat with your fingers."
Reported:
a) He told me to not eat with my fingers.  b) He told
me not to eat with my fingers.  c) He told me don't eat
with my fingers.
**Answer: b)** — the not stands guard before the to:
not to eat (Mauritania's own practice item, p.41).

**4. (Level 2)** Direct: "Don't swim here — it is
dangerous." Reported:
a) He asked us to swim there.  b) He warned us not to
swim there.  c) He invited us not swim there.
**Answer: b)** — danger takes warn, with not +
to-infinitive and here turned to there (Nepal's
four-verb family).

**5. (Level 3)** A guard at the gate shouts "Stop!"
Report it with the verb that keeps his force.
a) The guard asked me to stop.  b) The guard encouraged
me to stop.  c) The guard ordered me to stop.
**Answer: c)** — power speaking takes order/command;
ask and encourage would soften the shout away (Vietnam
Grade 12's verb bank, Unit 10).
