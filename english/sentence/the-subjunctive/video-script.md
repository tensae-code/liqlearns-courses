# Video script — The Subjunctive

Super-simple voice. The mascot is front-facing on screen the whole video, talking to the learner. Bracketed lines are visual cues for the maker, not spoken lines.

[Two sentences appear behind the mascot.]

"Look at this strange sentence: 'It is important that he come on time.' Come — not comes!"

"After words like important and advisable, the verb stays plain. That is the subjunctive."

[The mascot points at the second sentence.] "It is advisable that a scientist be patient. Be — for everybody. Try one yourself."
