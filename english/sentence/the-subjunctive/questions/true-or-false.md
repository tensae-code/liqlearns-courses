## True or false

1. In the subjunctive, "he come" is correct after "It is important that". (Sub lesson 1) **True.**

2. The subjunctive verb changes for he/she with -s. (Sub lesson 1) **False — it stays plain for every person.**

3. "Be" is the subjunctive form used for all persons. (Sub lesson 2) **True.**

4. Every sentence about facts uses the subjunctive. (Sub lesson 2) **False — only importance/advice expressions call for it.**
