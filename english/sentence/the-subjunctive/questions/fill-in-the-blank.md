## Fill in the blank

Use the plain form of the verb in brackets.

1. It is important that he ______ (come) on time. (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: come.**

2. It is advisable that a scientist ______ (be) patient. (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: be.**

3. It is important that every pupil ______ (bring) the book. (Sub lesson 2) **Answer: bring.**

4. It is advisable that they ______ (be) ready. (Sub lesson 2) **Answer: be.**
