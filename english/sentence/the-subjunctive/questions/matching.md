## Matching

Match the beginning to its correct subjunctive ending. (Sub lesson 1 and 2)

1. It is important that he…
2. It is advisable that she…
3. It is important that they…
4. It is advisable that a scientist…

a) be patient.
b) come on time.
c) be ready.
d) bring their books.

**Answers: 1-b, 2-c, 3-d, 4-a.**
