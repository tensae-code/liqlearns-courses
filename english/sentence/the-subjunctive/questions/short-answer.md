## Short answer

1. What is special about the verb after "It is important that…"? (Sub lesson 1) **Answer: it takes its plain form — no -s, no past ending (he come, not he comes).**

2. Write one sentence with "It is advisable that…" and the subjunctive of *be*. (Sub lesson 2) **Answer: any correct sentence, e.g. "It is advisable that you be careful."**

3. Why does "He comes on time every day" not use the subjunctive? (Sub lesson 2) **Answer: it reports an ordinary fact; the subjunctive appears after importance/advice expressions.**
