## Multiple choice

1. Choose the subjunctive sentence. (Sub lesson 1)
   a) It is important that he comes on time.  b) It is important that he come on time.  c) It is important that he came on time yesterday.
   **Answer: b).**

2. After "It is advisable that…", the verb… (Sub lesson 1)
   a) takes -s for he/she  b) stays in its plain form  c) goes into the past
   **Answer: b).**

3. "It is advisable that a scientist ______ patient." (Sub lesson 1)
   a) is  b) be  c) are
   **Answer: b).**

4. Which sentence is an ordinary fact and keeps the ordinary form? (Sub lesson 2)
   a) He comes on time every day.  b) It is important that he come.  c) It is advisable that she be ready.
   **Answer: a).**
