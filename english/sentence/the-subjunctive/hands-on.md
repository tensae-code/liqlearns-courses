# Hands-on — The Subjunctive

1. **Fix the verb.** Cards carry sentences with the ordinary form ("It is important that he comes early"). Learners rewrite each with the subjunctive and read both aloud, naming what changed.

2. **Advice round.** In pairs, take turns giving formal advice with the pattern: "It is advisable that you …" / "It is important that every pupil …" — the partner repeats the sentence and checks the verb form.
