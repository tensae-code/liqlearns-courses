# Video script — Will for the Future

Super-simple voice. The presenter is the LiqLearns mascot, front-facing,
on screen the whole video; the pictures named here appear beside him.

[A crystal ball over a village; rain inside it.] “It WILL rain tomorrow! Will makes predictions — and will never changes!”

[A phone rings; the mascot jumps up.] “I'LL get it! A decision made NOW — that's will too!”

[A calendar: next week, next year circled.] “Will you travel next year? Yes, I will! Tomorrow, soon, next week — will loves the future!”
