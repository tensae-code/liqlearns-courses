## Matching

Match the sentence to its job. (Sub lessons 1–3)

1. It will rain tomorrow. — a
2. She will win the race. — a
3. I'll get it! — b
4. I'll help you. — b
5. I won't forget. — c
6. Will you travel next year? — d
7. No, I won't. — e
8. We will leave soon. — f

a) a prediction
b) a decision made now
c) a negative promise
d) a will question
e) a short negative answer
f) will with a future time word

**Answer key with reasons:** 1–a, 2–a (predictions), 3–b, 4–b (instant decisions), 5–c (won't promise), 6–d (Will you…?), 7–e (short answer), 8–f (soon).
