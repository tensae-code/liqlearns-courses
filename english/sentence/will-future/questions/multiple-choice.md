## Multiple choice

1. It ___ rain tomorrow. (Sub lesson 1)
   a) will  b) wills  c) is will
   **Answer: a) will** — will never changes.

2. She ___ win the race, I think. (Sub lesson 1)
   a) will  b) will to  c) does will
   **Answer: a) will** — after will, the plain verb: will win.

3. The phone is ringing! “___ get it!” (Sub lesson 2)
   a) I'll  b) I will yesterday  c) I am get
   **Answer: a) I'll** — a decision made now: I'll = I will.

4. “I ___ forget your birthday, I promise.” (Sub lesson 2)
   a) won't  b) willn't  c) am not will
   **Answer: a) won't** — won't = will not.

5. “___ you come next week?” — “Yes, I will.” (Sub lesson 3)
   a) Will  b) Do will  c) Are
   **Answer: a) Will** — Will you…? asks about the future.

6. Which is a future time expression? (Sub lesson 3)
   a) yesterday  b) next year  c) last week
   **Answer: b) next year** — tomorrow, soon, next week/month/year travel with will.

7. They will visit us ___. (Sub lesson 3)
   a) tomorrow  b) yesterday  c) ago
   **Answer: a) tomorrow** — will sentences point forward in time.

8. “Will it be cold tonight?” — “No, it ___” (Sub lesson 3)
   a) willn't  b) won't  c) doesn't
   **Answer: b) won't** — the short negative answer to a will question.
