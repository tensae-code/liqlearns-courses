## True or false

1. We say “He wills come.” (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: False** — will never changes: “He will come.”

2. After will, the verb stays plain: “will rain”, “will win”. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: True** — no -s, no -ing after will.

3. “I'll get it!” is a decision made at the moment of speaking. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: True** — that is will's second job.

4. “Won't” means will not. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: True** — won't is will not, short.

5. A plan you made yesterday is the same as an instant will decision. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: False** — will-for-decisions is born in the same second as the words; old plans are another job (see Going To for Later).

6. “Will you…?” questions take the short answers “Yes, I will / No, I won't.” (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: True** — will comes back in the answer.

7. “Yesterday” is a future time expression. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: False** — yesterday is past; tomorrow, soon and next… are future.
