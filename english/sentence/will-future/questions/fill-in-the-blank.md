## Fill in the blank

1. We ___ (will) visit Uyuni next year. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: will** — will + plain verb visit.

2. He ___ (will) be happy tomorrow. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: will** — will is the same for he.

3. ___ (I will) carry that for you! (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: I'll** — I'll is the short form of I will, for instant decisions.

4. She ___ (will not) forget. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: won't** — won't is the short form of will not.

5. ___ you help me tomorrow? (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: Will** — Will you…? starts the question.

6. It will snow ___ (next / last) week. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: next** — future times take next, not last.

7. They will arrive ___. (a future word meaning “in a short time”) (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: soon** — soon is a future time expression.
