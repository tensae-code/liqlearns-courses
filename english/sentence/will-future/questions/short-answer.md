## Short answer

1. Predict tomorrow's weather. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: “It will rain / It will be sunny tomorrow.”** (any prediction) — will + plain verb.

2. Predict one thing about your next birthday. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: “I will be (age).”** (any prediction) — will never changes.

3. A friend is carrying heavy bags. Decide now. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: “I'll help you.”** — an instant decision takes I'll.

4. Promise not to be late. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: “I won't be late.”** — won't = will not.

5. Ask a friend about travelling next year. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: “Will you travel next year?”** — Will you…? + a future time word.

6. Name two future time expressions. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: tomorrow; next week** (also tonight, soon, next month, next year, in the future).

7. Answer negatively: “Will you stay at home tonight?” (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: “No, I won't.”** — the short negative answer.
