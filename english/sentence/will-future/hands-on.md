# Hands-on — Will for the Future

Predictions and instant decisions are games before they are grammar.

## Prediction ball

Hold an imaginary crystal ball. Each learner looks in and says one prediction: “You will pass your exam. It will rain on Saturday.” The group answers “Yes, it will!” or “No, it won't!”

## Instant decisions

The teacher makes things happen — a ringing phone, a falling book, a knock at the door. The learner must shout the decision in the same second: “I'll answer it!” “I'll pick it up!” “I'll open it!”
