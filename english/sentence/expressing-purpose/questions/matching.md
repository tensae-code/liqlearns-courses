## Matching

Match each purpose form to its job. (Sub lessons 1–3)

1. used for + -ing — a
2. in order to — b
3. so as to — c
4. so that — d
5. "What is it used for?" — e

a) the formal twin of in order to
b) purpose with a whole clause
c) a tool's job
d) asking a tool's job
e) the actor's aim, folded short

**Answer key with reasons:** 1–c (a tool's job), 2–e (aim folded short + base verb), 3–a (so as to = the formal twin), 4–b (so that + clause), 5–d (the question form).
