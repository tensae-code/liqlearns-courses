## Short answer

1. Say what a toothbrush is used for. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: "It is used for brushing teeth."** — used for + -ing.

2. Ask what a needle is for, in the lesson's question form. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: "What is a needle used for?"** — the used for question.

3. Give your aim — to succeed — with in order to. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: "I study in order to succeed."** — in order to + base verb.

4. Give the same aim with so that. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: "I study so that I can succeed."** — so that + subject + can.

5. Say why he saved money, with so that. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: "He saved money so that his children could learn."** — the clause form carries its own subject.
