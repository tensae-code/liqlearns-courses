## Fill in the blank

1. "A pump is used ___ lifting water." (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: for** — used for + -ing.

2. "What is this knife used ___?" (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: for** — the question keeps for at the end.

3. "In ___ to succeed, work hard." (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: order** — in order to.

4. "He came early ___ as to help." (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: So** — so as to — capital at the start.

5. "I read so ___ I can learn." (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: that** — so that + clause.

6. "Speak slowly so that everyone ___ understand." (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: can** — so that clauses take can/will.
