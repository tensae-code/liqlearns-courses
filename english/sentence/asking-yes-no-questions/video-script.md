# Video script — Asking yes/no questions

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A statement says. A question ASKS.
Watch the swap!

"She **is** happy." Say it as a question:
"**Is** she happy?"
The IS jumped to the front!

"You **like** mango."
No IS? Send the helper!
"**Do** you like mango?"

"He **plays** football."
"**Does** he play?"
Hear it? The S moved — play, not plays!

And the voice goes UP-up-up ↗:
"Do you like guava?"
The answer comes DOWN ↘:
"Yes, I do."

Now you. Make a statement. Swap it. Ask it.
That is a question — in English.
