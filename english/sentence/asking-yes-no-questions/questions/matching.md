# Asking yes/no questions — matching

Match each statement to its question, and each question to
its answer.

**Set 1 (Level 1)** — the swap.
1. She is a teacher. — **Is she a teacher?**
2. They are at home. — **Are they at home?**
3. It is hot today. — **Is it hot today?**
4. You are ready. — **Are you ready?**

**Set 2 (Level 2)** — the engine.
1. You like figs. — **Do you like figs?**
2. He walks to school. — **Does he walk to school?**
3. The cow eats grass. — **Does the cow eat grass?**
4. We play on Friday. — **Do we play on Friday?**

**Set 3 (Level 3)** — answers with tails.
1. Is the mango sweet? — **Yes, it is.**
2. Do they live in a town? — **No, they don't.**
3. Does she read at night? — **Yes, she does.**
4. Are you a student? — **Yes, I am.**
