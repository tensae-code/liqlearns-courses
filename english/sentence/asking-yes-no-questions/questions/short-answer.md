# Asking yes/no questions — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Be-Swap · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The Do/Does Engine · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Voice and Whole Answers.
Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Swap it: "The door is open."
**Answer:** "Is the door open?"

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Answer with tails: "Is the cow big?" (yes)
"Are the ducks small?" (no)
**Answer:** "Yes, it is." — "No, they aren't."

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Engine it: "They eat rice." / "She reads
books."
**Answer:** "Do they eat rice?" / "Does she read books?" —
does takes the -s; read stands bare.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Why not "Does he plays?"
**Answer:** one -s per sentence — does is carrying it, so
the verb stands bare: "Does he play?"

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Turn this short answer whole: Q "Does Mita
walk to school?" A "Yes, she does."
**Answer:** "Yes, she walks to school." (whole sentence, the
-s back on the verb in the answer's statement shape.)

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Which costume is the swap wearing here:
"You are reading" → "Are you reading?"
**Answer:** the be-swap on a doing-now sentence — be already
stands in the sentence, so it walks to the front (Mongolia's
questions box).
