# Asking yes/no questions — ordering

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Be-Swap · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The Do/Does Engine · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Voice and Whole Answers.
Stand the words in question order. (This type exists for
this lesson because a question is a statement whose front
moved — ordering IS the move.)

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** she / is / a student / ?
**Answer:** Is she a student?

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** open / the shop / is / ?
**Answer:** Is the shop open?

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** like / you / do / guava / ?
**Answer:** Do you like guava?

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** football / does / play / he / ?
**Answer:** Does he play football? — play bare; does carries
the -s.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** reading / are / now / you / ?
**Answer:** Are you reading now? — the be-swap on a doing-now
sentence (Mongolia's box).

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Unbuild it — turn the question back into a
statement: "Does the tiger eat meat?"
**Answer:** The tiger eats meat. — does leaves, and the -s
goes home to the verb.
