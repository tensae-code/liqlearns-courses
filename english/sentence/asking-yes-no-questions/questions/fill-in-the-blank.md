# Asking yes/no questions — fill in the blank

Fill each blank so the question stands right.

**1. (Level 1)** ______ she a student? (statement: She is a
student.)
**Answer: Is.**

**2. (Level 1)** ______ they your friends?
**Answer: Are.**

**3. (Level 2)** ______ you like mango?
**Answer: Do.**

**4. (Level 2)** ______ it rain here?
**Answer: Does** — it takes does.

**5. (Level 2)** Does he ______ football? (play / plays)
**Answer: play** — bare, because does carries the -s.

**6. (Level 3)** "Is the shop open?" — "No, it ______."
**Answer: isn't** — the short answer's tail matches the
question's be word.
