# Levels — Commas, Colons and Semicolons

One lesson, 2 sub lessons. A sub lesson opens by proving the previous one (at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, drawn from the whole question set of the sub lesson just before — no hardness ranking is written in questions/).

| Sub lesson | What the learner can do at the end |
|---|---|
| 1. Commas | Use commas appropriately — to separate the parts of a sentence, mark pauses, and keep meanings clear. |
| 2. Colons and semicolons | Use colons and semicolons appropriately — the colon to point forward to a list or explanation, the semicolon to join two closely linked parts of a sentence and to divide heavy list items. |

A learner who finishes all 2 sub lessons has the whole of this lesson: the three middle-of-the-sentence marks used appropriately, as one named curriculum requirement: the comma that separates and pauses, the colon that points forward to what follows, and the semicolon that joins and divides weightier parts.
