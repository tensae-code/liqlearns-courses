# Video Script — Commas, Colons and Semicolons

Super simple English. Slow. Smile. Each point is shown as it is named.

[Picture: a sentence running, a comma appearing as a small stepping stone]

The comma is a small pause. The sentence runs on after it — it does not stop!

[Picture: a colon shining like an arrow before a list of three things]

The colon points forward: here comes the list! Bring these: a pen, a book, a ruler.

[Picture: two short sentences holding hands with a semicolon between them]

The semicolon joins two parts that belong together. The rain stopped; the match began.
