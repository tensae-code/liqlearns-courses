# Matching — Commas, Colons and Semicolons

(Every item is marked with the sub lesson it belongs to: (Sub lesson N). The gate pool for a sub lesson is all of that sub lesson's questions — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the whole set is the pool.)

Match each item to what it means.

1. comma (Sub lesson 1)
2. a list inside a sentence (Sub lesson 1)
3. colon (Sub lesson 2)
4. semicolon (Sub lesson 2)
5. a heavy list (Sub lesson 2)
6. full stop (Sub lesson 1)

a) the mark that points forward to a list or explanation
b) items separated by commas, or by semicolons when the items are heavy
c) the mark that ends a sentence
d) a small pause and separator inside a sentence
e) the stronger pause that joins two linked parts
f) things named one after another inside a sentence

Answers: 1-d, 2-f, 3-a, 4-e, 5-b, 6-c
