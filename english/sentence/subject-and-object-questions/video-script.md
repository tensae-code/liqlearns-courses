# Video script — Subject and object questions

Super-simple voice. Read slow. The learner hears this
before or beside the written lesson.

---

A cup is broken.
Two questions can come.

Who broke it?
What did the boy break?

Look. They are not the same.

Who broke it?
We ask about the DOER.
The doer is missing.
So the question word stands in the doer's place.
No did. No helper.
Who broke it?
Who lives here?
Who wants tea?

The verb keeps its normal shape.
Who lives here? Not: who does live here?
Just: who lives.

Now the other one.
What did the boy break?
We ask about the THING.
The thing is missing. The doer is here: the boy.
So the normal machine runs.
Did comes. Then the doer. Then the verb.
What did the boy break?
Who did you see?
What did she buy?

A test for you.
Answer the question in your head.
"I saw Maria."
Who did you see? — Maria is at the END. The thing seen.
"Maria saw me."
Who saw you? — Maria is at the START. The doer.

Start or end. That is the whole secret.

One more shape.
The surprised check.
Didn't you see it?
Aren't you coming?
The speaker thought YES. The world said NO.
That is surprise, in a question.

Now you try.
One accident. Two questions.
Who did it?
And: what did he do?
