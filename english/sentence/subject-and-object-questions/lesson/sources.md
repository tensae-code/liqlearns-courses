**Acceptance count: taught in 2 countries (Cambodia,
Malaysia).** Cambodia carries the fullest treatment: its
Grade 11 course — evidenced by the Teacher's Book, the
student's book not being held — devotes a whole unit to
the pair, object questions first and subject questions
second, with distinguishing the two as the unit's second
half; its Grade 12 book then sets the pair together
inside the space unit and recycles them in consolidation.
Malaysia's Form 3 book teaches the pair as one grammar
item in Unit 7 and lists negative questions beside them —
this lesson's source for the surprised-check shape. No
other closed country's filing teaches the subject/object
distinction as a named point; the lesson does not claim
them.
