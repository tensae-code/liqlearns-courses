# Subject and object questions — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Object Questions · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Subject Questions · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Both Shapes and Negative Questions.

Fill each gap with the right shape.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** "What ______ you see at the zoo?"
**Answer: did** — an object question in the past runs
the ordinary machine: did + doer + plain verb.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** "Who ______ he give the book to?"
**Answer: did** — who can be the done-to as well as the
doer; here the giver (he) is present, so the missing who
is the object and did steps in.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** "Who ______ (want) a cup of tea?"
**Answer: wants** — a subject question: no helper, and
the verb dresses for one unseen person, -s included.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** "______ she finished her work?" — the
speaker is surprised the desk is still full.
**Answer: Hasn't** — a negative question checks in
surprise: the shrunken not rides on the helper.
