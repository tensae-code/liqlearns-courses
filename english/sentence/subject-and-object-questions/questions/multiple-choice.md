# Subject and object questions — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** You want to know the person your friend
met. Ask it.
a) Who met you?  b) Who did you meet?  c) Who you met?
**Answer: b) Who did you meet?** — the missing piece is
the done-to, so the ordinary machine runs: helper did,
then the doer you, then the plain verb.

**2. (Level 1)** "______ did she buy at the market?"
a) Who broke  b) What  c) Who lives
**Answer: b) What** — the missing piece is a thing, the
object of buy; an object question about a thing asks
with what.

**3. (Level 2)** You find a wet bicycle and want the
rider. Ask it.
a) Who did ride it?  b) Who rode it?  c) Who did it ride?
**Answer: b) Who rode it?** — the missing piece is the
doer, so the question word stands in the doer's seat
and no did enters (Cambodia, Grade 11, Unit 31B).

**4. (Level 2)** "______ lives in that house?"
a) Who does  b) Who  c) Whom does
**Answer: b) Who** — a subject question keeps the
statement shape: Who lives…, the verb wearing its -s for
the unseen doer.

**5. (Level 3)** "______ you see the letter I left?"
— said in surprise, because the letter is still on the
table.
a) Didn't  b) Who  c) What did
**Answer: a) Didn't** — the surprised check is a negative
question: the speaker expected yes (Malaysia, Form 3,
Unit 7's grammar list).
