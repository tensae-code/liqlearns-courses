# Subject and object questions — short answer

Answer in whole sentences.

**1. (Level 1)** The answer is "The cat drank the milk."
Ask about the milk.
**Answer: What did the cat drink?** — the wanted piece
(the milk) stands at the end of the answer, so this is
an object question and did runs the machine.

**2. (Level 2)** The answer is "The cat drank the milk."
Ask about the cat.
**Answer: Who drank the milk?** — the wanted piece (the
cat) stands at the start, the doer's seat: a subject
question, no helper (the answer test, Cambodia Grade 11,
Unit 31B).

**3. (Level 2)** A window in your school is broken. Ask
who did it.
**Answer: Who broke the window?** — doer missing,
question word in the doer's seat, verb in its plain past
shape.

**4. (Level 3)** Your friend promised to phone and did
not. Check it in surprise.
**Answer: Didn't you phone me?** (any true negative
question in the same shape earns it) — the negative
question is the surprised check, not an ordinary
question.
