# Subject and object questions — matching

Match each answer to the question that hunts it.

**1. (Level 1)** Answer: "She bought bread." — the
question hunts the bread. **Answer: What did she buy?**
— the wanted piece stands at the end of the answer, so
an object question with did.

**2. (Level 2)** Answer: "She bought bread." — the
question hunts she. **Answer: Who bought bread?** — the
wanted piece stands at the start, the doer's seat, so a
subject question with no helper.

**3. (Level 3)** Answer: "The rain stopped the match."
— the question hunts the rain. **Answer: What stopped
the match?** — a thing can be a doer too: subject
question, statement shape, no did.
