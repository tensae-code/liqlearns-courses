# Building a sentence — matching

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every set's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Whole or Waiting · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The Two Parts · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Order and Growth.
Match each piece to its job, the way the books name them.

**Set 1 (Sub lesson 2)** — the parts (Ghana's names; Bolivia's
formula).
1. the WHO or WHAT — **the subject**
2. what the sentence says about it — **the predicate**
3. the doing/being word — **the verb**
4. the rest that completes it — **the complement**

**Set 2 (Sub lesson 2)** — split the sentence "The tiger eats
meat at night."
1. The tiger — **subject**
2. eats — **verb**
3. meat at night — **complement**

**Set 3 (Sub lesson 1)** — whole or waiting?
1. The children are happy. — **whole**
2. In the morning at six — **waiting**
3. Mita reads. — **whole**
4. The big brown dog of my uncle — **waiting**
5. It rains. — **whole**
