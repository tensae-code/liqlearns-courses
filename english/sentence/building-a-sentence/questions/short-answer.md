# Building a sentence — short answer

Answer in a word or a short line.

**1. (Level 1)** Finish this waiting-piece two ways: "The
old man…"
**Answer:** any verb-completed finishes — "The old man
walks slowly." / "The old man is happy."

**2. (Level 2)** Split "The crow lives in a nest": subject /
verb / complement.
**Answer:** The crow / lives / in a nest (Bangladesh's
sentence, pp.86–87).

**3. (Level 2)** Name Ghana's two parts of a sentence.
**Answer:** the subject and the predicate (NaCCA B4–B6,
Strand 5).

**4. (Level 2)** Build three sentences from this table: WHO
— the deer, the parrot, the dog; DOES — eats; REST — leaves,
fruits, meat.
**Answer:** The deer eats leaves. The parrot eats fruits.
The dog eats meat (Bangladesh's strips).

**5. (Level 3)** What two legs can a sentence never lose?
**Answer:** the subject and the verb — take either away and
the thought waits.

**6. (Level 3)** Grow "Birds fly" with a complement of place
and time.
**Answer:** any true growth — "Birds fly over the river in
the morning."
