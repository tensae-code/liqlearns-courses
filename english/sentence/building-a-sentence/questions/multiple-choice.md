# Building a sentence — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Whole or Waiting · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The Two Parts · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Order and Growth.
Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which line is a sentence?
a) the big black cat  b) The big black cat sleeps.  c) on
the roof of the house
**Answer: b)** — it makes a whole thought; a) and c) leave
you waiting.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** "The tiger eats" — what is it?
a) a whole sentence, always  b) a waiting-piece until the
thought is finished in context — the books complete it with
its complement ("The tiger eats meat")  c) a question
**Answer: b)** — subject + verb alone can stand when the
verb completes itself (Birds fly); "eats" calls for its
rest, and Bangladesh's table always gives it.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In "Mita gets up at six o'clock," the
subject is —
a) gets up  b) at six o'clock  c) Mita
**Answer: c) Mita** — the WHO; Ghana's subject, Bolivia's S
in S + V + C.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Bolivia's formula for the plain sentence
is —
a) Verb + Subject + Nothing  b) Subject + Verb + Complement
c) Complement + Verb + Verb
**Answer: b)** — the compendium's board paradigm.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** From the table (dog / cow / monkey × eats ×
meat / grass / fruits), a TRUE row is —
a) The cow eats meat.  b) The monkey eats grass.  c) The cow
eats grass.
**Answer: c)** (Bangladesh's strips, pp.88–89).

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Which sentence says the cow does the
eating?
a) Grass eats the cow.  b) The cow eats grass.  c) Eats the
cow grass.
**Answer: b)** — order is meaning; only b) stands the
subject first.
