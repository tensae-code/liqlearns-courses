# Building a sentence — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Whole or Waiting · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The Two Parts · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Order and Growth.
Fill each blank so the line becomes a true, whole sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The cow ______ grass.
**Answer: eats** — subject + verb + complement whole.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** ______ sleeps in the bedroom. (Hint: a who.)
**Answer:** any true who — I / The cat / My brother.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Mita ______ up at six o'clock.
**Answer: gets** (Bangladesh's Mita).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** I ______ a student.
**Answer: am** — the verb can be a BE word; the leg still
stands.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The monkey lives ______ a tree.
**Answer: in** — the complement's little joining word rides
with it (Bangladesh, pp.86–87).

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** A sentence is a group of words in ______
that makes a whole thought.
**Answer: order.**
