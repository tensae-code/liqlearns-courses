# Video script — Building a sentence

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Words alone are waiting.
"The big cat…" — and? And?
"The big cat **sleeps**." — WHOLE!

A sentence is a whole thought.
It stands on two legs:
WHO — and DOES WHAT.

The **cow**… eats… **grass**.
**Mita**… gets up… **at six**.
**I**… am… **a student**.

Build them like blocks:
WHO + DOES + the rest.
Seven animals, seven foods —
seven sentences, all true!

Careful with the order!
"The cow eats grass." Good.
"Grass eats the cow"? NO!

Now you. Take a who. Add a does.
Say it WHOLE. That is a sentence!
