# Video Script — Have and Has for Parts

Super simple English. Slow. Smile. Each part lights up as it is named.

[Picture: a bicycle]

Look — a bike! The bike has two wheels. The bike has a chain. The bike has two brakes. Has, has, has!

[Picture: a car]

And a car! The car has four wheels. The car has a big engine. It has four doors!

[Picture: the bike again, with a question mark over the water]

But wait. Does the bike have a paddle? No! The bike doesn't have a paddle. Bikes do not paddle!

[Picture: a strange invented vehicle with a sail and six wheels]

Now invent! My vehicle has six wheels. It has a big sail. It doesn't have an engine. What does YOUR vehicle have?
