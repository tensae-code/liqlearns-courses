# Levels — Have and Has for Parts

One lesson, two sub lessons. A sub lesson opens by proving the previous one (at most 5 questions, mixed with earlier parts, drawn from the whole question set of the sub lesson just before — no hardness ranking is written in questions/).

| Sub lesson | What the learner can do at the end |
|---|---|
| 1. Saying what a thing has | Use "The [thing] has …" to name a thing by its parts — "The bike has 2 brakes", "It has four wheels" — for bikes and other vehicles and things. |
| 2. What it doesn't have, and inventing a vehicle | Say what a thing doesn't have ("The bike doesn't have a paddle"), and invent a vehicle and present it part by part. |

A learner who finishes both sub lessons can describe any machine or thing by the parts it has and the parts it lacks.
