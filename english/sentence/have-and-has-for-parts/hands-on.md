# Hands-On — Have and Has for Parts

Things you do with a real bike, real things and your imagination. No special materials needed.

1. **Walk around a bike.** Touch each part and say it: "The bike has two wheels. The bike has a chain. The bike has a saddle." Count the parts out loud.
2. **Has or doesn't have?** Take turns with things in the room: "The chair has four legs." "The table doesn't have wheels." Right or wrong — your partner checks.
3. **Draw and label.** Draw a bike or a car and label every part you can name, each with its has-sentence underneath.
4. **Invent a vehicle.** Draw the strangest vehicle you can imagine — wings, sails, ten wheels. Present it: "It has … It has … It doesn't have …"
5. **Guess the thing.** Describe a thing by its parts only — "It has four legs and a flat top. It doesn't have wheels." Your partner guesses the thing.
