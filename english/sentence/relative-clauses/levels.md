# Relative clauses — questions by level

Practice by level, tied to the skill number. The questions live in
the type files; this file is the by-level view. The levels are the
depths inside this one thing — joining two sentences with
who/which/that/where/when/whose — set by the pattern across the
countries' books: the word choice and the join first (Cambodia
Grade 9 and Egypt both open there), then the subject/object seats
and the drop, then the full family at work.

## Level 1 — the join and the word
Level 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: join
two sentences with the right word — who for people,
which for things, that for either — and say which noun
the clause is stuck to.

## Level 2 — subject, object, and the drop
Level 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: tell
a subject clause from an object clause, and drop the
joining word exactly where it is the object — never
where it is the subject (Vietnam Grade 9, Unit 9's rule).

## Level 3 — the whole family
Level 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: run
whose (possession), where (place) and when (time)
alongside who/which/that, building clauses from two
sentences of your own without a wrong word or a wrong
drop.
