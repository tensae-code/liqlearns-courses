# Relative clauses — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Relative Clauses Join and Word · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Relative Clauses Subject, Object and the Drop · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Relative Clauses Whole Family.

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A relative clause gives more
information about a noun.
**Answer: True** — Egypt's Preparatory 9 Tip states it
in those words (Unit 8, Tip p.29).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In "The man who lives here," the word
who can be dropped, like in "the book I bought."
**Answer: False** — who is the subject there, the doer
of lives; only an object word may drop (Vietnam,
Grade 9, Unit 9).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** That can point back to people or to
things, but whose only ever names possession.
**Answer: True** — that covers both families (Cambodia,
Grade 9, Unit 19), and whose is the possession word in
every book's list.
