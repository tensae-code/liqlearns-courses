# Relative clauses — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Relative Clauses Join and Word · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Relative Clauses Subject, Object and the Drop · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Relative Clauses Whole Family.

Fill each gap with the right joining word — or with
nothing, where the drop is allowed.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The house ______ I was born is small.
**Answer: where** — the word points back to a place;
where is the place word (Egypt, Preparatory 9, Tip p.29).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The woman ______ I saw yesterday wears
a red coat.
**Answer: that / who — or nothing** — the word is the
object (I did the seeing), so it may be dropped
(Vietnam, Grade 9, Unit 9).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The man ______ lives next door is a
teacher. (No drop is possible here.)
**Answer: who** — who is the clause's subject, the doer
of lives, so the word must stand.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The shop ______ sells bread opens at
six, and the man ______ shop it is opens it himself.
**Answer: that/which; whose** — a thing takes that or
which; the second clause names the owner, so whose.
