# Relative clauses — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Relative Clauses Join and Word · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Relative Clauses Subject, Object and the Drop · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Relative Clauses Whole Family.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** "The man ______ lives here is my uncle."
a) which  b) who  c) whose
**Answer: b) who** — the word points back to a person,
and who is the people word (Egypt, Preparatory 9, Tip p.29).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Join them: "The book is new. I bought
the book yesterday."
a) The book who I bought yesterday is new.
b) The book that I bought yesterday is new.
c) The book where I bought yesterday is new.
**Answer: b) The book that I bought yesterday is new.**
— that serves things, and the clause sits behind its noun.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which sentence may LOSE its joining word?
a) The man who lives here.
b) The book that I bought.
c) The girl who called me.
**Answer: b) The book that I bought.** — that is the
object there (I did the buying); a subject word may
never drop (Vietnam, Grade 9, Unit 9).

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** "The girl ______ bag is red is my sister."
a) who  b) which  c) whose
**Answer: c) whose** — the clause says whose thing the
bag is: possession takes whose (Vietnam, Grade 9, Unit 8).

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** "That was the day ______ we first met."
a) where  b) when  c) whose
**Answer: b) when** — the word points back to a time,
and when is the time word (Kuwait, Pearls 6A, p.64).
