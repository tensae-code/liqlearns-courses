# Relative clauses — matching

Match each noun to the joining word that points back
to it.

**1. (Level 1)** a person — your uncle, the teacher,
the woman in red. **Answer: who (or that)** — who is
the people word; that serves people too (Cambodia,
Grade 9, Unit 19 rule box).

**2. (Level 1)** a thing — a book, a bird, a coat.
**Answer: which (or that)** — which is the things word,
and Pearls 6A's mini-story gives a bird which… (Kuwait,
Pearls 6A, p.64).

**3. (Level 3)** a place, a time, an owner — the park,
the day, the girl and her bag. **Answer: where, when,
whose** — place takes where, time takes when, and
possession takes whose (Egypt, Preparatory 9, Tip p.29).
