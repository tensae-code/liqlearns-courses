# Relative clauses — short answer

Answer in whole sentences.

**1. (Level 1)** Join: "I know a man. The man speaks
four languages."
**Answer: I know a man who speaks four languages.** —
who points back to a person and stands as the clause's
subject.

**2. (Level 2)** Shorten, if the rule allows: "The
film that we watched was long."
**Answer: The film we watched was long.** — that is
the object (we did the watching), so it may drop
(Vietnam, Grade 9, Unit 9).

**3. (Level 3)** Describe your home town so one street
is picked out: use where in your sentence.
**Answer: The street where I live is quiet.** (Any true
sentence with a place noun + where + clause earns it.)

**4. (Level 3)** Say whose phone this is, in a full
clause: the phone belongs to your brother, and it is
ringing.
**Answer: The boy whose phone is ringing is my
brother.** — whose carries the possession into the
clause; the defining shape (no commas) is this
lesson's.
