# The passive — questions by level

Practice by level, tied to the skill number. The questions live in
the type files; this file is the by-level view. The levels are the
depths inside this one thing — the sentence turned around — set
by the pattern across the countries' books: Mongolia and Egypt
both start with the present and past coats and by; the future
and modal coats come next; the perfect coat and the choosing
come last, where Libya's five-tense table puts the whole row in
one view.

## Level 1 — the turn-around
Level 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: turn an
active sentence around and back again in the present and
past coats (is/are, was/were + participle), and place by
+ the doer when the name is given.

## Level 2 — the coats
Level 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: dress
the same participle in the future coat (will be), the
modal coat (can be) and the perfect coat (has/have been),
and drop the doer where it is unknown or not the point.

## Level 3 — the choosing
Level 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: choose
active or passive on purpose for a real message — doer
is the story, receiver is the story, doer unknown or
obvious — and defend the choice in one breath, across
any coat including Libya's continuous row (is being
built).
