# The passive — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Turn-Around · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 3 — The Coats (item 3), except item 4, the unknown doer dropped -> Sub lesson 2 — Dropping the Doer · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 (item 5, the perfect and continuous coats in use). Items are marked by what each item actually asks.

Answer in whole sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Who painted Red Sunset, and when?
Answer the book's way.
**Answer: Red Sunset was painted in 1686 by Smithers.**
(Mongolia, English X, p.83 — the model answer as filed.)

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Turn it around: "Starley invented the
bicycle in 1885."
**Answer: The bicycle was invented in 1885 by
Starley.** (Mongolia, English X, p.84 — the quiz's own
sentence.)

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Say it in the future passive: "We will
finish this project next Friday."
**Answer: This project will be finished next Friday.**
(Egypt, Prep 9 T1, Tip p.45 — the book's own pair.)

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Your phone is gone and nobody knows who
took it. Report it.
**Answer: My phone has been stolen. / My phone was
stolen.** (Either coat earns it; the doer must be
dropped — he is unknown.)

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** A hospital has just been built in your
town and it is still being fitted out. Say both facts.
**Answer: A new hospital has been built in our town.
It is being fitted out now.** (The perfect coat for the
done part; Libya's five-tense table, p.82, holds the
continuous coat — is being + participle — for the part
still moving.)
