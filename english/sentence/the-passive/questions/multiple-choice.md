# The passive — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** "The light bulb ______ by Edison in
1879."
a) was invented  b) invented  c) is invent
**Answer: a) was invented** — be in its past coat +
participle (Mongolia, English X, p.86 — the book's own
pair).

**2. (Level 1)** "Football boots ______ of leather or
nylon."
a) are made  b) makes  c) making
**Answer: a) are made** — present coat, doer dropped:
factories make them, everyone knows (Mongolia, English
X, Unit 4 Self-Check).

**3. (Level 2)** "This project ______ next Friday."
a) will finish  b) will be finished  c) is finish
**Answer: b) will be finished** — Egypt's own active/
passive pair (Prep 9 T1, Tip p.45).

**4. (Level 2)** "The homework ______."
a) has been done  b) has being done  c) have did
**Answer: a) has been done** — the perfect coat: have/
has + been + participle (Egypt, Prep 9 T1, Tips p.17).

**5. (Level 2)** "Plastic bottles ______ into a
recycling bin."
a) can turned  b) can be turned  c) can been turn
**Answer: b) can be turned** — the modal coat (Mongolia,
English X, p.131 — the book's own discovery sentence).

**6. (Level 3)** Which sentence keeps the camera on the
inventor?
a) The telephone was invented in 1876.
b) Bell invented the telephone in 1876.
c) The telephone was invented in 1876 by Bell.
**Answer: b)** — the active keeps the doer at the front,
as the subject; in (c) Bell is named but the telephone
still holds the front chair.
