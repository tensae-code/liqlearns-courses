# The passive — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Turn-Around · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 3 — The Coats · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 (item 5, the continuous coat from Libya's table).

Fill each gap with the right shape.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first photograph ______ ______
(take) by Joseph Niepce.
**Answer: was taken** — past coat + participle
(Mongolia, English X, p.84).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The collections ______ ______ (update)
frequently.
**Answer: are updated** — present coat (Mongolia,
English X, p.57, as filed).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The Pyramids ______ ______ ______ (visit)
by thousands of people every year.
**Answer: have been visited** — Egypt's perfect-passive
example turned to every year (Prep 9 T1, Tips p.17: "The
Pyramids have been visited").

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** This letter ______ ______ ______ (write)
tomorrow.
**Answer: will be written** — the future coat: will be
+ participle.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The windows ______ ______ ______ (clean)
right now — I can see the man doing it.
**Answer: are being cleaned** — the continuous coat from
Libya's five-tense table (Grade 9, p.82).
