# The passive — matching

**Set 1 (Level 1)** — match each active sentence to its
passive twin.
1. Edison invented the light bulb in 1879. — **The
   light bulb was invented by Edison in 1879.**
2. Smithers painted Red Sunset in 1686. — **Red Sunset
   was painted in 1686 by Smithers.**
3. Factories make football boots of leather. —
   **Football boots are made of leather.**
4. We will finish this project next Friday. — **This
   project will be finished next Friday.**
5. Somebody has done the homework. — **The homework
   has been done.**

**Set 2 (Level 2)** — match each passive to its coat.
1. is locked — **present**
2. was sold — **past**
3. will be finished — **future**
4. has been visited — **perfect**
5. can be turned — **modal**
6. is being built — **continuous**

(The pairs are the books' own sentences: Mongolia,
English X, pp.83–86 and p.131; Egypt, Prep 9 T1, Tips
p.17 and Tip p.45; the continuous coat is Libya's
five-tense table row, Grade 9, p.82.)
