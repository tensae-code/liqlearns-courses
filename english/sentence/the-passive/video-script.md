# Video script — The passive

Super-simple voice. Read slow. The learner hears this
before or beside the written lesson.

---

Edison invented the light bulb in 1879.

Now turn it around.

The light bulb was invented by Edison in 1879.

Same fact. New chair at the front.
Now the BULB is the story.

That is the passive.

Be, and the third shape of the verb.
The boots are made of leather.
The door was locked.
The work will be finished on Friday.

The third shape never changes.
Only be changes its coat.

Is. Are. Now.
Was. Were. Before.
Will be. Later.
Has been. Done by now.
Can be. Possible.

And the doer?
Sometimes we keep the name.
The bicycle was invented in 1885 by Starley.
The name is famous. The name is the news.

Most times, we drop the doer.
My phone was stolen. Who did it? Nobody knows.
It was sold for a lot of money. The price is the news.
The thief was arrested. By the police — who else?

Unknown. Obvious. Not the point.
Then by walks off.

When do you turn the sentence?
The doer is the story — keep it active.
The thing is the story — turn it around.

Now you try.
Say five facts with no hands.
The bridge was built long ago.
Tea is grown far away.
My school was cleaned this morning.

No hands. All facts.
