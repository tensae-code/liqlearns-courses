# Exclamations and orders — fill in the blank

Fill each gap correctly.

**1. (Level 1)** Negative order: "______ touch the wet paint."
**Answer: Don't** — don't + base verb — Bolivia's box and Egypt's Primary 3 agree (p.8).

**2. (Level 2)** Rule for the library: "You ______ shout in the library."

**3. (Level 3)** "How ______ she sings!" (one adverb)
