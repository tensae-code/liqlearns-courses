# Exclamations and orders — matching

Match each utterance to its job.

**1. (Level 1)** "Don't climb the tree, please!"
**Answer: a polite negative order** — don't + verb, softened by please — Nicaragua's own example (p.13).

**2. (Level 2)** "Turn left, then go straight."
**Answer: directions in imperatives** — the map-speaking shapes of Kuwait's Unit 7, Lesson 2.

**3. (Level 3)** "What a performance!"
**Answer: an exclamation of feeling** — What a + noun phrase, ending in the mark Egypt assigns to strong feelings (p.19).
