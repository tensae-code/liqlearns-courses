# Exclamations and orders — short answer

Answer in whole sentences.

**1. (Level 1)** Turn "You must not run" into a sharp moment-order for a child near a road.
**Answer: Don't run!** — the imperative drops the subject and speaks to the moment; the strong instruction earns its ! (Egypt, p.8).

**2. (Level 2)** Give directions: from the gate, straight on, then right at the shop.
**Answer: Go straight. Turn right at the shop. (bare imperatives throughout)** — directions ride as a chain of imperatives (Kuwait, Skyline 5B, Unit 7).

**3. (Level 3)** Cry about a tall building both ways.
**Answer: What a tall building! / How tall it is!** — thing + adjective rides What a…!; the quality alone rides How…!
