# Exclamations and orders — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Exclamations and orders — the order shape · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Exclamations and orders — orders at work · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Exclamations and orders — the cry.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which one is a true imperative? a) You stand up. b) Stand up. c) Standing up.
**Answer: b)** — the bare verb with no subject pronoun (Nicaragua, Activity Book 3, p.13; Bolivia, Year 1, p.173).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The polite order is: a) Sit down! b) You sit down now. c) Sit down, please.
**Answer: c)** — please is the softener Nicaragua's Grammar Tip names.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which direction chain is all imperatives? a) You turn left and you go straight. b) Turn left. Go straight. Walk forward. c) Turning left, going straight.
**Answer: b)** — Kuwait's direction set in its own shapes (Skyline 5B, Unit 7, Lesson 2).

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** "___ a beautiful voice!" a) How b) What c) Which
**Answer: b)** — a noun phrase (a beautiful voice) takes What a…!; How takes an adjective or adverb.
