# Exclamations and orders — questions by level

Practice by level, tied to the skill number. The questions live in
the type files; this file is the by-level view. The levels are the
depths inside this one thing — sentences that act instead of
state — set by the pattern across the countries' books: the
imperative shape first (Bolivia's box, Nicaragua's tip), then its
work in directions and rules (Kuwait, Rwanda, Egypt's must), then
the cry of feeling and its mark (Egypt's punctuation teaching).

## Level 1 — the order shape
Level 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: make
affirmative and negative imperatives with the bare
verb and no subject, and soften one with please.

## Level 2 — orders at work
Level 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark:
give a short direction chain in imperatives, phrase
safety rules with must/mustn't beside Don't, and tell
an order from an invitation or advice by its shape.

## Level 3 — the cry
Level 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark:
build What a…! and How…! exclamations on the right
shapes, place the exclamation mark where Egypt's rule
puts it (strong instructions, strong feelings), and
keep statements out of the cry shapes.
