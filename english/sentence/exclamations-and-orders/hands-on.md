# Hands-on — Exclamations and orders

You do it, we guide you. Everything here is done with a
companion or the Explain Companion.

1. **Simon-says drill.** The leader gives imperatives —
   Stand up, Turn around, Don't move, Touch your nose,
   please — and the learner obeys only the polite or
   correctly-shaped ones. Wrong shapes (with a subject
   pronoun) are traps to catch, not obey.
2. **The map walk.** With a simple map (a drawn street
   grid is fine), one partner directs the other from
   start to star using only imperatives: Go straight,
   Turn right, Stop — Kuwait's map-speaking task, live.
3. **The rule board.** Together, write five safety
   rules for a real place (kitchen, road, classroom):
   two with Don't, two with mustn't, one with must —
   and read them aloud with the warning voice.
4. **Two cries.** Show or describe a real moment — a
   goal, a storm, a surprise gift — and each partner
   must answer with both shapes: What a…! and How…!,
   with the mark in the voice.
