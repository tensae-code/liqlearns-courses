# Video script — Mine and yours

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Whose hat is this? THREE ways to say it!

One: the name! "**Carlo's** hat!"
Two: the partner! "It's **his** hat."
Three: alone! "The hat is **his**!"

Partners hold a thing: my book, your book, her book.
Alone words stand by themselves:
**mine! yours! hers! ours! theirs!**

Never "mine book." Mine ATE the book!
Never "the book is my." My can't stand alone!

Ask it: "**Whose** is it?"
Answer it: "It's **mine**!"

Now you. Claim your things!
That is owning — in English.
