# Mine and yours — questions by level

Practice by level, tied to the skill number. The questions live in
the type files; this file is the by-level view. The levels are the
depths inside this one thing — the standing-alone owners — set by
the pattern across the countries' books: the name-tag first,
then the partner forms, then the alone forms and the law.

## Level 1 — the name-tag and the partner forms
Level 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: tag any named
owner with 's (Carlo's hat), and hold every noun with its right
partner form (my/your/his/her/its/our/their) by the owner.

## Level 2 — the alone forms
Level 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: answer whose
questions and claims with the alone family (mine, yours, his,
hers, ours, theirs), and climb Mongolia's three-rung ladder in
both directions without a slip.

## Level 3 — the law under pressure
Level 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: catch and fix
the mirror errors (mine + noun; partner form standing alone)
on sight, handle the two honest exceptions (his unchanged; its
with no alone twin), and run a whole lost-property exchange in
the right forms.
