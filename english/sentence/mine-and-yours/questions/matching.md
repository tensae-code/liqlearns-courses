# Mine and yours — matching

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every set's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Name-Tag and the Partner Forms · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The Alone Forms · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — The Law Under Pressure · (Level 1–2) -> Sub lessons 1–2 — the families (Set 1).
Match each owner to their two forms, and each claim to its
rung.

**Set 1 (Sub lessons 1–2)** — the families.
1. I — **my / mine**
2. you — **your / yours**
3. he — **his / his**
4. she — **her / hers**
5. we — **our / ours**
6. they — **their / theirs**
7. it — **its / — (no alone form)**

**Set 2 (Sub lesson 2)** — rungs of the ladder (Nina's hat,
Mongolia's box).
1. "Nina's hat" — **the name-tag rung**
2. "her hat" — **the partner rung**
3. "The hat is hers" — **the alone rung**

**Set 3 (Sub lesson 3)** — claims and their fixes.
1. "Mine pen is red" — **"My pen is red" (partner holds the
   noun)**
2. "The pen is my" — **"The pen is mine" (alone word at the
   end)**
3. "Hers bag" — **"Her bag"**
4. "It is Pavel" (claiming Pavel's book, badly) — **"It is
   Pavel's" (the name-tag can stand where the thing is
   known)**
