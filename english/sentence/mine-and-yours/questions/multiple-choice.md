# Mine and yours — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Name-Tag and the Partner Forms · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The Alone Forms · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — The Law Under Pressure.
Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The name-tag for Naran's ball, written
right, is —
a) Narans ball  b) Naran's ball  c) ball of Naran's the
**Answer: b) Naran's ball** — owner + 's + thing (Mongolia's
's box, p.42).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** "This is Nina's hat. It's ______ hat."
a) hers  b) her  c) she
**Answer: b) her** — the partner form holds the noun
(Mongolia's three-rung box).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** "The hat is ______." (Nina's)
a) her  b) hers  c) she's
**Answer: b) hers** — the alone form ends the claim.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** "Whose pen is this?" — "It's ______."
a) my  b) mine  c) me
**Answer: b) mine** — standing alone, the alone word.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Which sentence is built WRONG?
a) Mine book is red.  b) The book is mine.  c) My book is
red.
**Answer: a)** — mine already carries the book; no noun
follows an alone form.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** The one word that is the SAME in both
families is —
a) my  b) his  c) her
**Answer: b) his** — "his hat" and "the hat is his," one
word, both shelves.
