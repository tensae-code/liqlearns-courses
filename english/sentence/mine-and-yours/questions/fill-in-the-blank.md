# Mine and yours — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Name-Tag and the Partner Forms · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The Alone Forms · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — The Law Under Pressure.
Fill each blank with the owning word in the right form.

**Box:** my · mine · your · yours · her · hers · their ·
theirs

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This is ______ book. (speaking of my own)
**Answer: my** — partner form, holding the noun.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The book is ______.
**Answer: mine** — the claim ends; the alone form stands.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Is that ______ hat, Naraa? (asking about
Naraa's own)
**Answer: your.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** No, it isn't ______. It's Nina's.
**Answer: yours.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** (to Naraa, about Nina's hat) This hat is
not ______. It's ______.
**Answer: yours — hers** — alone forms both times: the
claims end the sentences, no noun follows them.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** The children put ______ bags down; the big
bag is ______.
**Answer: their — theirs.**
