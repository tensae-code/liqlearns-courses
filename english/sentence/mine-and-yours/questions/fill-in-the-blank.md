# Mine and yours — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the owning word in the right form.

**Box:** my · mine · your · yours · her · hers · their ·
theirs

**1. (Level 1)** This is ______ book. (speaking of my own)
**Answer: my** — partner form, holding the noun.

**2. (Level 2)** The book is ______.
**Answer: mine** — the claim ends; the alone form stands.

**3. (Level 1)** Is that ______ hat, Naraa? (asking about
Naraa's own)
**Answer: your.**

**4. (Level 2)** No, it isn't ______. It's Nina's.
**Answer: yours.**

**5. (Level 3)** (to Naraa, about Nina's hat) This hat is
not ______. It's ______.
**Answer: yours — hers** — alone forms both times: the
claims end the sentences, no noun follows them.

**6. (Level 3)** The children put ______ bags down; the big
bag is ______.
**Answer: their — theirs.**
