**Acceptance count: taught in 8 countries (Egypt, Mongolia, Laos, China, Indonesia, Iran, Sierra Leone, Jordan).**
Mongolia's "That's Mine" unit carries the whole lesson —
the 's box, the whose matching, the three-rung box, the
game; Egypt's youngest books hold the partner forms from
the first terms; China adds the complete system twice over
— the 's answers and standalone mine/yours in one unit,
then the full pronoun set, its included, in another;
Indonesia works the 's and adjective rungs under a Whose
question and pairs personal with possessive forms at
Grade 7; Iran sets 's against of and tables the full
adjective set; Sierra Leone tables the standalone
pronouns whole in JSS 1 Term 2 and sets pronoun against
adjective as one contrast in Term 3; Jordan works the
partner adjectives our/your/their in Grade 3, answers
Whose…? with the standalone mine and his/hers in the
same grade, and explains the 's shop names as of in
Grade 5. Other closed countries join as their
books are read (see READING-QUEUE.md).
