# Video script — Can for Ability

Super-simple voice. The presenter is the LiqLearns mascot, front-facing,
on screen the whole video; the pictures named here appear beside him.

[The mascot swims, then rides a bike.] “I CAN swim! I CAN ride a bike! Can never changes — he can, she can, we can!”

[The mascot tries to fly, flaps, lands.] “I CAN'T fly! Can you fly? No, I can't.”

[A smaller mascot on a small bike.] “When I was small, I COULD ride a bike. Could is can in the past!”

[Clouds gather; the mascot points.] “It COULD rain. We COULD go home. Could you open the door, please? Could is polite!”
