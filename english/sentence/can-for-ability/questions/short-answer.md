## Short answer

1. Say one thing you can do. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: “I can swim.”** (any true can sentence) — can + plain verb.

2. Say one thing a bird can do and you can't. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: “A bird can fly. I can't fly.”** — can for able, can't for not able.

3. Ask a friend about swimming. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: “Can you swim?”** — can first, short answer “Yes, I can / No, I can't.”

4. Say one thing you could do when you were seven. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: “I could ride a bike.”** (any true past sentence) — could = past can.

5. Say one thing you couldn't do last year. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: “I couldn't read English.”** (any true sentence) — couldn't = could not.

6. Make a suggestion with could. (Sub lesson 4)
   **Answer: “We could go to the park.”** (any suggestion) — could gives ideas politely.

7. Ask the teacher politely for help. (Sub lesson 4)
   **Answer: “Could you help me, please?”** — Could you…? is the polite request.

8. Say a possibility about tomorrow's weather. (Sub lesson 4)
   **Answer: “It could rain tomorrow.”** (any possibility) — could = maybe.
