## Multiple choice

1. She ___ play the piano. (Sub lesson 1)
   a) cans  b) can  c) does can
   **Answer: b) can** — can never changes; no -s for she.

2. They ___ read English. (Sub lesson 1)
   a) can  b) cans  c) are can
   **Answer: a) can** — one form for every person.

3. I ___ fly. It is impossible for me. (Sub lesson 2)
   a) can  b) can't  c) could yesterday
   **Answer: b) can't** — can't says not able.

4. “___ you swim?” — “Yes, I can.” (Sub lesson 2)
   a) Does  b) Do  c) Can
   **Answer: c) Can** — can comes first in its own questions; no do/does.

5. When I was five, I ___ ride a bike. (Sub lesson 3)
   a) can  b) could  c) cans
   **Answer: b) could** — could is can in the past.

6. “Could you walk at one?” — “No, I ___” (Sub lesson 3)
   a) couldn't  b) can't  c) doesn't
   **Answer: a) couldn't** — past question, past answer.

7. “It ___ rain today.” (Maybe — possibility.) (Sub lesson 4)
   a) could  b) cans  c) is can
   **Answer: a) could** — could also says possibility.

8. The polite way to ask: (Sub lesson 4)
   a) “Could you open the window, please?”  b) “Open window!”  c) “You coulds open it.”
   **Answer: a)** — Could you…? is the polite request.
