## Matching

Match the sentence to its job. (Sub lessons 1–4)

1. I can swim. — a
2. He can't drive. — b
3. Can you sing? — c
4. I could read at four. — d
5. We couldn't come last year. — e
6. It could rain. — f
7. We could go to the park. — g
8. Could you help me, please? — h

a) ability now
b) not able now
c) asking about ability
d) ability in the past
e) not able in the past
f) possibility
g) suggestion
h) polite request

**Answer key with reasons:** 1–a (can now), 2–b (can't now), 3–c (Can you…? asks), 4–d (could = past ability), 5–e (couldn't = past not able), 6–f (could = maybe), 7–g (could gives an idea), 8–h (Could you…? asks politely).
