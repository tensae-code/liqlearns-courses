## True or false

1. We say “She cans sing.” (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: False** — can never changes: “She can sing.”

2. We say “I can swim” and “They can swim” — the same can. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: True** — one form for every person.

3. Questions with can need “do” or “does”. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: False** — “Can you swim?” needs no do/does.

4. Could is can in the past. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: True** — “I could ride a bike at five.”

5. “Couldn't” means could not. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: True** — couldn't is the short form of could not.

6. Could can only talk about the past. (Sub lesson 4)
   **Answer: False** — could also gives possibilities, suggestions and polite requests.

7. “Could you help me?” is politer than “Help me!” (Sub lesson 4)
   **Answer: True** — Could you…? is the polite request form.
