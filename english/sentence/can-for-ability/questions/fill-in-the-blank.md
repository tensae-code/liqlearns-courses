## Fill in the blank

1. He ___ (can) play football very well. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: can** — can never changes, even for he.

2. We ___ (can) speak two languages. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: can** — the same form for we.

3. She ___ (not can) drive a car. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: can't** — can't = can not, for not able.

4. ___ you help me? — Yes, I can. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: Can** — can starts its own question.

5. At four, I ___ (could) count to 100. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: could** — past ability takes could.

6. They ___ (not could) swim last year, but now they can. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: couldn't** — couldn't = could not.

7. We ___ go to the market tomorrow. (a suggestion) (Sub lesson 4)
   **Answer: could** — could makes suggestions.

8. ___ you close the door, please? (a polite request) (Sub lesson 4)
   **Answer: Could** — Could you…? is polite.
