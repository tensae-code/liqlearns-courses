# Hands-on — Can for Ability

Your own body and life are the practice material here.

## Can parade

Mime an ability — swimming, piano, football. Your partner must guess with the question: “Can you swim?” Answer truly: “Yes, I can” or “No, I can't.”

## Then and now

Draw two boxes: THEN (age seven) and NOW. Write two could sentences in THEN and two can sentences in NOW. Read them to a partner: “I could climb trees. Now I can read English.”
