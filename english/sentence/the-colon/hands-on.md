# Hands-on — The Colon

## Promise and list

In pairs: one partner says a promise — "For the trip you need
the following things" — and stops. The other writes the
promise with its colon, then writes the list the speaker
dictates: three things, commas between them, a full stop at
the end. Change places after three lists.

## Colon hunt

Take a page of your own writing. Every time you wrote "as
follows" or "the following", check: does the colon stand
there? Fix the ones that are missing it, and read the fixed
sentences aloud with a pause at the colon.
