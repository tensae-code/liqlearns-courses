## Short answer

1. What does the colon tell the reader? (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer:** That a list is coming — it stands after the promise and before the first item.

2. Name the two phrases the colon follows in this lesson. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer:** "As follows" and "the following".

3. Fix this sentence and say what you fixed: "You need the following a book, a pencil, a bag." (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer:** "You need the following: a book, a pencil, a bag." — the colon was missing after "the following".

4. How do you check with your ears whether a colon is in the right place? (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer:** Read the sentence aloud: at the colon your voice stops and waits a moment before the list begins.
