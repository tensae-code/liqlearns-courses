## True or false

1. A colon is two dots, one over the other. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: True.**

2. The colon stands at the end of a list. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: False** — it stands before the list, after the promise.

3. When you read a colon aloud, your voice stops and waits a moment. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: True.**

4. "The rules are as follows we listen, we share." is punctuated correctly. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: False** — "as follows" must be closed by a colon before the list begins.

5. A full stop ends the sentence after the list. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: True.**
