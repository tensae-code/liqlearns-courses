## Multiple choice

1. What does a colon look like? (Sub lesson 1)
   a) One dot  b) Two dots, one over the other  c) A small line
   **Answer: b) Two dots, one over the other.**

2. What job does the colon do in this lesson? (Sub lesson 1)
   a) It ends every sentence  b) It stands before a list and says the list is coming  c) It joins two names
   **Answer: b)** — it opens the door to the list.

3. Where does the colon stand? (Sub lesson 1)
   a) After the promise, before the first item of the list  b) In the middle of the list  c) At the very end of the sentence
   **Answer: a)** — after the words that promise the list, before the list itself.

4. Which sentence has its colon in the right place? (Sub lesson 2)
   a) "Bring the following: a cup, a spoon, a plate."  b) "Bring: the following a cup, a spoon, a plate."  c) "Bring the following a cup, a spoon: a plate."
   **Answer: a)** — the colon closes the promise phrase, then the list follows.

5. What goes between the items of a list after a colon? (Sub lesson 2)
   a) Full stops  b) Colons  c) Commas
   **Answer: c) Commas** — and a full stop ends the list.
