## Fill in the blank

1. "A colon is made of two ___, one over the other." (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: dots.**

2. "The colon stands ___ the list it opens." (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: before.**

3. "The rules are as ___: we listen, we share, we clean up." (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: follows.**

4. "Bring the ___: a cup, a spoon, a plate." (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: following.**

5. "Between the items of a list we put ___." (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: commas.**
