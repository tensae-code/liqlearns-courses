## Matching

Match the part to its job. (Sub lessons 1–2)

1. The colon — b
2. "as follows" — d
3. "the following" — e
4. Commas in a list — a
5. The full stop — c

a) Stand between the items of the list
b) Two dots that open the door to a list
c) Ends the sentence after the list
d) A promise phrase the colon follows
e) The other promise phrase the colon follows

**Answers:** 1–b (the mark itself), 2–d (first signal phrase), 3–e (second signal phrase), 4–a (items are separated by commas), 5–c (the list sentence still ends with a full stop).
