# Video script — The Colon

Super-simple voice. The presenter is the LiqLearns mascot,
front-facing, on screen the whole video; the pictures named here
appear beside him.

[Two dots appear, one over the other, big.] "This is a
COLON. Two dots — one on top, one below."

[The words "the following" appear, then the colon, then three
things pop in one by one: a book, a pencil, a bag.] "It stands
BEFORE a list. It says: HERE THEY COME!"

[The sentence "You need the following:" appears, the colon
glowing.] "After THE FOLLOWING — the colon!"

[Back to the mascot, holding the list.] "Promise the list.
Colon. Then the list. That is the COLON!"
