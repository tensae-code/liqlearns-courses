# Participle clauses — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Participle Clauses Reduced Relatives · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Participle Clauses Reason, Time and Perfect Participle · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Participle Clauses Hidden-Subject Law.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Shrink it: "the man who is talking to
my father" →
a) the man talked to my father
b) the man talking to my father
c) the man talk to my father
**Answer: b) the man talking to my father** — an active
clause shrinks to the present participle (Egypt,
Secondary 12 Term 2, Unit 10).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Grow it back: "the car stolen last
night" =
a) the car which stole last night
b) the car which was stolen last night
c) the car which is stealing last night
**Answer: b)** — the past participle hides a passive:
the car did not steal; it was stolen (Egypt).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** "______ the site was old, the team dug
carefully." (reason)
a) Knowing  b) Known  c) To know yesterday
**Answer: a) Knowing** — a present participle clause
can carry the reason: because they knew (Armenia,
Grade 11, §5.2).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** "______ the work, we went home." (the
work finished FIRST)
a) Finishing now  b) Having finished  c) To finish
tomorrow
**Answer: b) Having finished** — the perfect participle
marks the action that finished before the main one
(Armenia, §5.2; Vietnam, Grade 11, Unit 7).

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Which sentence breaks the
hidden-subject law?
a) Walking home, I got wet.
b) Walking home, the rain started.
c) Having eaten, we rested.
**Answer: b)** — the main subject is the rain, so the
clause claims the rain was walking: a misplaced
participle (Ethiopia, Grade 12, Unit 3).
