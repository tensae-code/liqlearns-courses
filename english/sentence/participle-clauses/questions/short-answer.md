# Participle clauses — short answer

Answer in whole sentences.

**1. (Level 1)** Shrink: "the song that was written in
1900."
**Answer: the song written in 1900.** — passive inside
(was written), so the past participle carries it.

**2. (Level 2)** Open a sentence with a reason clause:
you knew the way, so you walked fast.
**Answer: Knowing the way, I walked fast.** (Any
present-participle opener whose doer is the main
subject earns it; Armenia, §5.2.)

**3. (Level 2)** Put the earlier action first: you
locked the shop, then you went home.
**Answer: Having locked the shop, I went home.** — the
finished-first action wears having + the past
participle.

**4. (Level 3)** Repair: "Driving through the desert,
the heat was terrible."
**Answer: Driving through the desert, we found the heat
terrible.** (Any repair that makes the driver the main
subject earns it — the heat cannot drive.)
