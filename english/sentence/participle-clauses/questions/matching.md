# Participle clauses — matching

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Participle Clauses Reduced Relatives · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Participle Clauses Reason, Time and Perfect Participle · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Participle Clauses Hidden-Subject Law.

Match each short clause to the full clause it shrinks.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** "the house built in 1900"
**Answer: the house which was built in 1900** — past
participle, passive inside (Egypt, Secondary 12
Term 2).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** "the girl carrying the basket"
**Answer: the girl who is carrying the basket** —
present participle, active inside (Egypt).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** "Having read the letter, …"
**Answer: after he/she had read the letter** — the
perfect participle unfolds into a finished earlier
action (Vietnam, Grade 11, Unit 7).
