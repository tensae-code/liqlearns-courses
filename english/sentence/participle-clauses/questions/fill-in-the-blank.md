# Participle clauses — fill in the blank

Fill each gap with the right participle shape.

**1. (Level 1)** The letter ______ (write) in French
was hard to read.
**Answer: written** — the letter was written: a passive
meaning takes the past participle.

**2. (Level 1)** The students ______ (wait) outside
were cold.
**Answer: waiting** — the students were waiting: an
active meaning takes the present participle.

**3. (Level 2)** ______ (lock) the door, she slept
well.
**Answer: Having locked** — locking finished before
sleeping; the earlier action wears having + the past
participle.

**4. (Level 3)** Repair it in the gap: "Opening the
box, ______ fell out." → "Opening the box, I saw the
keys fall out" — the opener of the box must be ______.
**Answer: the main subject (I)** — the participle's
hidden doer is whoever stands as the main sentence's
subject (Ethiopia, Grade 12, Unit 3).
