# Participle clauses — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Participle Clauses Reduced Relatives · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Participle Clauses Reason, Time and Perfect Participle · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Participle Clauses Hidden-Subject Law.

Fill each gap with the right participle shape.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The letter ______ (write) in French
was hard to read.
**Answer: written** — the letter was written: a passive
meaning takes the past participle.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The students ______ (wait) outside
were cold.
**Answer: waiting** — the students were waiting: an
active meaning takes the present participle.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** ______ (lock) the door, she slept
well.
**Answer: Having locked** — locking finished before
sleeping; the earlier action wears having + the past
participle.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Repair it in the gap: "Opening the
box, ______ fell out." → "Opening the box, I saw the
keys fall out" — the opener of the box must be ______.
**Answer: the main subject (I)** — the participle's
hidden doer is whoever stands as the main sentence's
subject (Ethiopia, Grade 12, Unit 3).
