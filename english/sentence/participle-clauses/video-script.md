# Video script — Participle clauses

Super-simple voice. Read slow. The learner hears this
before or beside the written lesson.

---

A long way to say it:
the man who is talking to my father.

A short way:
the man talking to my father.

Same man. Same talking.
The small words — who is — are gone.
Only the participle stays. Talking.

That is a participle clause.

Active? Use the -ing shape.
The girl carrying the basket.
The students waiting outside.

Passive? Use the third shape.
The car stolen last night.
The house built in 1900.
Stolen. Built. The thing did not do it.
Somebody did it TO the thing.

Now the clause can walk to the front.
Knowing the road, we drove fast.
That means: because we knew the road.
Reason, in three words.

Two actions? One finished first?
Having finished lunch, we left.
Having locked the door, she slept well.
Having done — the first action, finished.
Then the main action.

One law. Never forget it.
The hidden doer is the main subject.
Having finished lunch, WE left. We finished. We left.
Same person.

Break the law and the sentence falls down.
Walking home, the rain started.
Who is walking? The rain? No!
Walking home, I got wet in the rain. Now it stands.

Now you try.
Shrink one. Grow one.
The letter written in French.
The letter which was written in French.

Short outside. Full inside. You choose.
