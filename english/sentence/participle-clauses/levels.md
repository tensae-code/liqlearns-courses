# Participle clauses — questions by level

Practice by level, tied to the skill number. The questions live in
the type files; this file is the by-level view. The levels are the
depths inside this one thing — the clause shrunk to its
participle — set by the pattern across the countries' books: the
reduced relatives first (Egypt's pair is the cleanest statement),
then free clauses for reason and time with the perfect participle
(Armenia, Vietnam), then the hidden-subject law and its repair
(Ethiopia's misplaced participles).

## Level 1 — reduced relatives
Level 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: shrink
who/which + be clauses both ways — present participle
for active, past participle for passive — and grow them
back without changing the meaning (Egypt, Secondary 12
Term 2, Unit 10).

## Level 2 — reason, time, and the perfect participle
Level 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: open
a sentence with a participle clause for reason or time
(Having finished…, Knowing…, Built…) with the main
subject doing the participle's work — the perfect
participle for the action that finished first (Armenia,
Grade 11, §5.2; Vietnam, Grade 11, Units 5 and 7).

## Level 3 — the hidden-subject law
Level 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: catch
and repair a misplaced participle — name the wrong
subject the clause points at and rebuild the sentence
so the participle's doer and the main subject are one
(Ethiopia, Grade 12, Unit 3).
