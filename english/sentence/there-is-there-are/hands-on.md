# Hands-on — There Is and There Are

The room you are sitting in is the worksheet.

## Room tour

Walk slowly around the room. Point and say six sentences: three “There is a…”, three “There are …” with numbers. Your partner counts your sentences, not the things.

## Kitchen questions

In the kitchen, ask five questions: “Is there any milk?” “Are there any eggs?” “How many cups are there?” Answer each in a full sentence, opening doors to check.
