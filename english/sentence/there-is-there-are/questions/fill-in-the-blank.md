## Fill in the blank

1. There ___ a book on the table. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: is** — one book: there is.

2. There ___ water in the bottle. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: is** — water is uncountable: there is.

3. There ___ three trees in the park. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: are** — three trees: there are.

4. There ___ many students at school today. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: are** — many takes are.

5. ___ there a cinema in your town? (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: Is** — Is there…? asks about one thing.

6. How many legs ___ there on a dog? — Four. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: are** — How many… are there?

7. There ___ (not) any sugar. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: isn't** — sugar takes is; negative: isn't any.

8. There ___ (not) any bananas. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: aren't** — bananas are plural; negative: aren't any.
