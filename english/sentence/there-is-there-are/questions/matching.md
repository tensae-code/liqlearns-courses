## Matching

Match the beginning to the correct ending. (Sub lessons 1–3)

1. There is — c
2. There are — a
3. There is — d
4. Is there — e
5. Are there — f
6. How many books — b
7. There isn't — g
8. There aren't — h

a) five fish in the river.
b) are there? — There are ten.
c) a moon tonight.
d) water in the glass.
e) a shop near your house?
f) any oranges?
g) any rice.
h) any pencils.

**Answer key with reasons:** 1–c (one thing), 2–a (fish plural, unchanged word), 3–d (uncountable water), 4–e (one-thing question), 5–f (plural question with any), 6–b (number question and answer), 7–g (uncountable negative with any), 8–h (plural negative with any).
