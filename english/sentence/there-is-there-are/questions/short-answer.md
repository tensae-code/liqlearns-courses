## Short answer

1. Say what is in your bag with “there is”. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: “There is a book in my bag.”** (any one true thing) — one thing takes there is.

2. Say something uncountable that is in your kitchen. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: “There is water in the bottle.”** (any uncountable) — uncountables take there is.

3. Count the chairs in your room in a full sentence. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: “There are (number) chairs.”** — many takes there are + number.

4. Say a sentence with “there are” and the word sheep. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: “There are five sheep.”** — sheep does not change in the plural.

5. Ask if a library is in your town. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: “Is there a library in your town?”** — Is there…? for one thing.

6. Ask how many students are in your class. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: “How many students are there in your class?”** — How many… are there?

7. Say there is no bread, negatively. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: “There isn't any bread.”** — negative + any.

8. Say there are no apples, negatively. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: “There aren't any apples.”** — plural negative + any.
