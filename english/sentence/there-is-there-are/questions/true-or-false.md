## True or false

1. “There is two parks” is correct. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: False** — two parks takes are: “There are two parks.”

2. “There is milk” is correct. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: True** — uncountable milk takes there is.

3. The plural of sheep is “sheeps”. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: False** — sheep never changes: one sheep, five sheep.

4. “Is there…?” asks about one thing. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: True** — Is there a clock? Are there any apples?

5. We say “there isn't some milk”. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: False** — negatives take any: “There isn't any milk.” (One Bolivia book prints “some” here — its own some/any page corrects it.)

6. “How many chairs are there?” asks for a number. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: True** — answer: “There are four.”

7. “There's” is the short form of “there is”. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: True** — “There's a park.”
