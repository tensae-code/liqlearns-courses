# Verbs with two objects — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** In "I sent Mary some flowers," the
PERSON object is —
a) some flowers  b) Mary  c) sent
**Answer: b) Mary** — Mary receives; the flowers are
the thing that travels (Mongolia, English IX, Unit 7).

**2. (Level 1)** "Show ______ the photo, please."
a) me  b) my photo is  c) to mine
**Answer: a) me** — shape A: verb + person + thing,
with no word between verb and person.

**3. (Level 2)** Rebuild in the other shape: "He gave
his sister a pen."
a) He gave a pen to his sister.
b) He gave a pen his sister to.
c) He gave to a pen his sister.
**Answer: a) He gave a pen to his sister.** — thing
first, then to, then the person: Mongolia's matched
pair, swapped.

**4. (Level 2)** "I cooked dinner ______ us."
a) to  b) for  c) at from
**Answer: b) for** — cook is a doing-for verb: the work
is done for the person's benefit, and nothing is handed
across.

**5. (Level 3)** The safe everyday sentence is —
a) Give her it.  b) Give it to her.  c) Give to it her.
**Answer: b) Give it to her.** — with two pronouns,
everyday English takes the to-shape and keeps it in
front.
