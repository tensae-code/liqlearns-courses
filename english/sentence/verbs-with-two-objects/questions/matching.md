# Verbs with two objects — matching

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Verbs with Two Objects Person and Thing · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Verbs with Two Objects Swap · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Verbs with Two Objects Pronouns and Live Choice.

Match each verb to its signpost in the thing-first
shape.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** give, send, show, lend, pass
**Answer: to** — the handing-over family: "Give the
book to Mary" (Mongolia, English IX, Unit 7's pair).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** buy, cook, make, get, find
**Answer: for** — the doing-for family: "I bought a
book for Mary"; the benefit, not a parcel, travels.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** tell (a story), offer (a seat)
**Answer: to in the thing-first shape — "He told a
story to us," "She offered a seat to me"** — tell and
offer ride with the handing-over family when their
loads swap seats.
