# Verbs with two objects — true or false

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Level 1)** In "I sent Mary some flowers," Mary
is the indirect object and the flowers are the direct
object.
**Answer: True** — the person receives (indirect); the
thing travels (direct) — Cambodia Grade 11's Unit 15A
distinction, on Mongolia's own pair.

**2. (Level 2)** "I bought a book to Mary" is a correct
swap of "I bought Mary a book."
**Answer: False** — buy takes for: "I bought a book for
Mary." To belongs to the handing-over family only.

**3. (Level 3)** "Give Mary the book" and "Give the
book to Mary" are both correct, and say the same.
**Answer: True** — with full nouns the two shapes stand
free and the delivery does not change (Mongolia,
English IX, Grammar Reference).
