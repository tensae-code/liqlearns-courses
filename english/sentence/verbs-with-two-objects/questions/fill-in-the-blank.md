# Verbs with two objects — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Verbs with Two Objects Person and Thing · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Verbs with Two Objects Swap · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Verbs with Two Objects Pronouns and Live Choice.

Fill each gap with the right word — sometimes the word
is the missing signpost.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** She told ______ (we) a long story.
**Answer: us** — shape A takes the person straight
after the verb: told us a story.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** I sent some flowers ______ Mary.
**Answer: to** — the thing-first shape carries the
parcel across with to (Mongolia, English IX, Grammar
Reference pair).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** He bought a scarf ______ his mother.
**Answer: for** — buy belongs to the doing-for family;
its signpost is for, never to.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Send ______ (the keys) ______ (she),
please — the pronoun-safe shape.
**Answer: them / to her** — "Send them to her": both
loads are pronouns, so the to-shape carries them.
