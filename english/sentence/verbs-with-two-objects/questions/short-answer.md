# Verbs with two objects — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Verbs with Two Objects Person and Thing · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Verbs with Two Objects Swap · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Verbs with Two Objects Pronouns and Live Choice.

Answer in whole sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give your friend the pen — say it in
shape A.
**Answer: I gave my friend the pen.** (Any verb +
person + thing earns it; Cambodia, Grade 11, Unit 15A.)

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Say the same delivery the other way:
"I showed the teacher my work."
**Answer: I showed my work to the teacher.** — thing
first, to, person: the seesaw swap.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Your keys, to Ana — both pronouns
where you can. Say it safely.
**Answer: Give them to her.** (Ana = her, keys = them;
the two-pronoun sentence rides in the to-shape.)

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Cook a meal for your family tonight —
report the plan, then name the signpost and its family.
**Answer: I will cook a meal for my family — for, the
doing-for family.** (Any true for-sentence with the
reason named earns it.)
