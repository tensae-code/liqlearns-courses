## Short answer

1. Say what you are doing right now. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: "I am learning English."** (any true now sentence) — I am + verb-ing.

2. Say what your teacher is doing. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: "She is teaching."** (any true sentence) — she takes is + -ing.

3. Ask a friend what they are doing. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: "What are you doing?"** — be first, then you, then -ing.

4. Answer: "Is the cat sleeping?" (look — it is eating). (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: "No, it isn't. It is eating."** — short answer + the true action.

5. Say one now sentence and one every-day sentence about reading. (Sub lesson 4)
   **Answer: "I am reading now. I read every day."** — -ing for now, simple for habits.
