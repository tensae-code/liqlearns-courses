## Multiple choice

1. "I ___ reading now." (Sub lesson 1)
   a) am  b) is  c) are
   **Answer: a) am** — I takes am.

2. "She ___ cooking." (Sub lesson 2)
   a) am  b) is  c) are
   **Answer: b) is** — he/she/it takes is.

3. "They ___ playing football." (Sub lesson 2)
   a) is  b) am  c) are
   **Answer: c) are** — you/we/they take are.

4. "What ___ you doing?" (Sub lesson 3)
   a) is  b) are  c) does
   **Answer: b) are** — be comes first in the question.

5. "Is he sleeping?" — "No, he ___. He is studying." (Sub lesson 3)
   a) isn't  b) not  c) doesn't
   **Answer: a) isn't** — short negative answer with be.

6. RIGHT NOW points to… (Sub lesson 4)
   a) the habit form — "I read"  b) the -ing form — "I am reading"  c) the past form
   **Answer: b)** — now-actions take am/is/are + -ing.

7. "She ___ every day." (a habit) (Sub lesson 4)
   a) is cooking  b) cooks  c) am cooking
   **Answer: b) cooks** — every-day habits take the simple present.

8. "We ___ listening to the teacher at this moment." (Sub lesson 2)
   a) are  b) is  c) be yesterday
   **Answer: a) are** — we takes are.
