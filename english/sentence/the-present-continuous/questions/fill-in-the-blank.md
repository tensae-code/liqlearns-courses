## Fill in the blank

1. I ___ writing now. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: am** — I am + verb-ing.

2. Mother ___ making tea. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: is** — she takes is.

3. The children ___ playing in the yard. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: are** — they takes are.

4. What ___ she doing? (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: is** — be first in questions.

5. ___ you listening to me? — Yes, I am. (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: Are** — Are you…? asks about you.

6. Right now I ___ (read), but I ___ (read) a story every night too. (Sub lesson 4)
   **Answer: am reading; read** — now takes -ing; the habit takes the simple form.
