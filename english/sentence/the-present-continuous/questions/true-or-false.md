## True or false

1. "I is reading" is correct. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: False** — I takes am: "I am reading."

2. The -ing form is for actions happening now. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: True** — am/is/are + -ing = happening now.

3. "They is playing" is correct. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: False** — they takes are: "They are playing."

4. Questions start with the be word: "What are you doing?" (Sub lesson 3)
   **Answer: True** — be comes first.

5. "I am read every day" is the right habit sentence. (Sub lesson 4)
   **Answer: False** — habits take the simple form: "I read every day."

6. "Now" and "right now" point to the -ing form. (Sub lesson 4)
   **Answer: True** — they name this moment.
