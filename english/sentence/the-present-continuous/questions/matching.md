## Matching

Match the person to their be word, then finish each sentence. (Sub lessons 1–4)

1. I — a
2. She — b
3. They — c
4. We — d
5. It (the cat) — e
6. You — f

a) am reading
b) is cooking
c) are playing
d) are listening
e) is sleeping
f) are writing

**Answer key with reasons:** 1–a (I am), 2–b (she is), 3–c (they are), 4–d (we are), 5–e (it is), 6–f (you are) — the -ing never changes; only am/is/are moves.
