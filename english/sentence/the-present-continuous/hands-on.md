# Hands-on — The Present Continuous

The room you are in is the practice material here.

## Mime and guess

One learner mimes an action — cooking, swimming, sleeping. The others ask: "Are you cooking?" The mimer answers truly: "Yes, I am!" or "No, I'm not. I'm swimming!"

## One-minute reporter

Watch through a window or door for one minute. Report five things happening: "A man is walking. Two girls are laughing. A dog is running…"
