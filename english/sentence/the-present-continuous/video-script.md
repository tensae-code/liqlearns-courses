# Video script — The Present Continuous

Super-simple voice. The presenter is the LiqLearns mascot, front-facing,
on screen the whole video; the pictures named here appear beside him.

[The mascot reads a big book.] "I AM reading! Am — plus ING! This is happening NOW!"

[The mascot points at a sleeping cat.] "The cat IS sleeping. HE IS, SHE IS, IT IS!"

[Two friends run past.] "They ARE running! What ARE YOU doing?"

[A calendar and a clock appear.] "NOW — I am reading! EVERY DAY — I read. Two forms, two meanings!"
