# Non-defining relative clauses — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** Which sentence carries EXTRA
information between commas?
a) The man who lives here is my uncle.
b) My uncle, who is a doctor, lives here.
c) The book that I bought is new.
**Answer: b)** — the name already identifies the man;
the clause is a gift, and the commas mark it (Egypt,
Secondary 11, Grammar Box p.77).

**2. (Level 1)** Lift the clause out of "Hanoi, which
is very old, is the capital." What remains?
a) Nothing complete.  b) Hanoi is the capital.
c) Which is very old.
**Answer: b) Hanoi is the capital.** — the sentence
still makes sense without the clause: Vietnam's own
test for the kind (Grade 9, Unit 10).

**3. (Level 2)** "My teacher, ______ retired last
year, taught my father too."
a) that  b) who  c) — (nothing)
**Answer: b) who** — that is never used in a
non-defining clause, and the pronoun can never be
dropped (Egypt, Grammar Box p.77).

**4. (Level 2)** "Mrs Adel, ______ son is in my class,
teaches us."
a) whose  b) who  c) that
**Answer: a) whose** — possession between commas;
Egypt's box includes whose in this family.

**5. (Level 3)** "He passed the exam, ______ surprised
everyone."
a) that  b) which  c) what
**Answer: b) which** — the word comments on the whole
sentence before it, not on one noun (Vietnam, Grade 12,
Unit 3).
