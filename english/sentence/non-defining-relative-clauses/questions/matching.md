# Non-defining relative clauses — matching

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Non-Defining Relative Clauses Extra Information and Commas · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Non-Defining Relative Clauses Three Hard Rules · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Non-Defining Relative Clauses Whole-Sentence Which.

Match each sentence to its kind.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** "The girl who won the prize is my
cousin." **Answer: defining** — no commas; without the
clause you cannot tell which girl, so the clause is
necessary information.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** "My cousin, who won the prize, is
visiting us." **Answer: non-defining** — commas, and
"my cousin" is already fully named; the clause is
extra (Malaysia, Form 3, Unit 4 teaches the pair side
by side).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** "It snowed in April, which closed the
roads." **Answer: non-defining, whole-sentence** —
which comments on the whole fact of the snow, not on
April (Vietnam, Grade 12, Unit 3).
