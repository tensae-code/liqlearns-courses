# Non-defining relative clauses — true or false

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Level 1)** A non-defining clause can be lifted
out and the sentence still makes sense.
**Answer: True** — that is Vietnam Grade 9's own
statement of the kind (Unit 10).

**2. (Level 2)** "My car, that is ten years old, still
runs well" is a correct non-defining sentence.
**Answer: False** — that is never used in a
non-defining clause; say "My car, which is ten years
old, …" (Egypt, Grammar Box p.77).

**3. (Level 2)** In "My uncle, who I visited in June,
lives in Cairo," the who may be dropped because it is
the object.
**Answer: False** — the drop belongs to defining
clauses only; a non-defining pronoun cannot be omitted
(Vietnam, Grade 9, Unit 10).
