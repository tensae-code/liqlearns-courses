# Non-defining relative clauses — fill in the blank

Fill each gap — a word, or the commas the clause needs.

**1. (Level 1)** Add the commas: "My town which is
very old has a castle."
**Answer: My town, which is very old, has a castle.**
— extra information is set off by commas (Egypt,
Secondary 11, Grammar Box p.77).

**2. (Level 2)** "Our neighbour, ______ we visited in
June, has moved away." (The word may NOT be dropped
here, even though it is the object.)
**Answer: who/whom** — in a non-defining clause the
pronoun cannot be omitted (Vietnam, Grade 9, Unit 10).

**3. (Level 3)** "The letter arrived a week late,
______ annoyed me greatly."
**Answer: which** — a whole-sentence comment takes
which, with a comma before it (Vietnam, Grade 12,
Unit 3).

**4. (Level 3)** "Mr Omar, ______ I spoke yesterday,
agreed to help." (fronted preposition)
**Answer: to whom** — the preposition may stand fronted
in a non-defining clause, commas kept (Yemen, Pupil's
Book 6, Language review 4, p.15).
