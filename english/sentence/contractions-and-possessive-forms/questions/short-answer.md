## Short answer

1. Write "she is" and "they are" in short form. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer:** she's; they're.
2. What are the apostrophe's two jobs in this lesson? (Sub lessons 1–2)
   **Answer:** 1) It stands where letters fell out in a short form. 2) With s after a name, it shows that a thing belongs to someone.
3. Write one sentence with a possessive form about a thing in your room. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer:** any true sentence, e.g. "My brother's bag is on the chair."
4. Write two sentences about yourself with short forms. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer:** any two true sentences, e.g. "I'm a student. I don't live far from school."
