## Matching

Match the full form to its short form. (Sub lessons 1–2)

1. I am — c
2. you are — e
3. do not — a
4. cannot — f
5. it is — b
6. they are — d

a) don't
b) it's
c) I'm
d) they're
e) you're
f) can't

**Answer key with reasons:** 1–c, 2–e, 3–a, 4–f, 5–b, 6–d — in each short form the apostrophe stands where letters fell out. (The possessive job is the other half of the lesson: it adds 's to a name and leaves nothing out.)
