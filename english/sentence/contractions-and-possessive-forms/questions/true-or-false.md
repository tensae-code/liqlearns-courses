## True or false

1. In "don't", the apostrophe stands where a letter fell out. (Sub lesson 1)
   **True** — the o of "not" fell out.
2. "Cant" is the right short form of "cannot". (Sub lesson 1)
   **False** — it is "can't", with the apostrophe.
3. "Sara's book" shows that the book belongs to Sara. (Sub lesson 2)
   **True.**
4. "It's tail" is the right way to write about a dog's tail. (Sub lesson 2)
   **False** — the belonging word is "its", with no apostrophe; "it's" is the short form of "it is".
5. A contraction makes two words into one short form. (Sub lesson 1)
   **True.**
