## Multiple choice

1. The short form of "I am" is ___. (Sub lesson 1)
   a) I'm  b) I's  c) Im
   **Answer: a) I'm** — the apostrophe stands where the a fell out.

2. The short form of "do not" is ___. (Sub lesson 1)
   a) donot  b) don't  c) do'nt
   **Answer: b) don't** — the o of not falls out.

3. "___ a student," she said — the short form of "She is". (Sub lesson 1)
   a) Shes  b) She'is  c) She's
   **Answer: c) She's.**

4. Which sentence shows that the book belongs to Sara? (Sub lesson 2)
   a) Sara book is red.  b) Sara's book is red.  c) Saras book is red.
   **Answer: b) Sara's book is red** — the possessive form takes 's.

5. "The dog wagged ___ tail." The belonging word for it — no apostrophe. (Sub lesson 2)
   a) it's  b) its'  c) its
   **Answer: c) its** — it's with an apostrophe is the short form of "it is".

6. The short form of "cannot" is ___. (Sub lesson 1)
   a) can't  b) can'not  c) cant
   **Answer: a) can't.**
