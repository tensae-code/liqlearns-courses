# Video script — Contractions and Possessive Forms

Super-simple voice. The presenter is the LiqLearns mascot,
front-facing, on screen the whole video; the pictures named here
appear beside him.

[The words I AM stand side by side; the A falls out and an
apostrophe takes its place.] "I AM… I'M! Short and fast!"

[The words DO NOT shrink the same way.] "DO NOT… DON'T!"

[The mascot holds a book; SARA grows on the cover: SARA'S
BOOK.] "And this book? It is SARA'S — it belongs to Sara!"
