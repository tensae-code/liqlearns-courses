# Hands-on — Contractions and Possessive Forms

These forms are learned by using them about real people and
real things. Do these out loud, with a partner.

## Whose is it?

Pick up things in the room, one at a time. Your partner must
say whose it is with a possessive form: "It's Layla's pen."
Then write it.

## Say it short

Your partner says a full sentence: "I am happy." You say it
back in its short form: "I'm happy." Take turns — five
sentences each, then write them both ways.
