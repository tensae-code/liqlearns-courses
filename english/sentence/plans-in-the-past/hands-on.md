# Hands-on — Plans in the past

You do it, we guide you. Everything here is done in the
learner's real day, with a companion or the Explain
Companion. The TARGET forms are always said in English;
the talk around them may be in the learner's own language.

1. **The plan that met a but.** Each person tells one real plan from the last month that met a but, in was going to… but… Three each; the companion draws the plan and the but as two pictures while listening, then retells it in the same coats.
2. **Catch the edge.** One person mimes an action and freezes ONE second before it (pouring, leaving, biting). The other must say the edge sentence before the action may move: "You were about to pour the water!" Five freezes; swap.
3. **The expectation board.** Write three real expectations from your week (I was supposed to call…, We were supposed to finish…). Mark each KEPT or BROKEN, and tell each one whole to your companion, with what happened instead where it broke.
