# Plans in the past — matching

Match each item to its partner.

**1. (Level 1)** "a plan held in the past" beside its coat **Answer: was/were going to** — the decided plan of a past moment, very often meeting a but.

**2. (Level 2)** "one second before it starts" beside its coat **Answer: was/were about to** — the edge of the moment — China's Grade 9 frame, worn in the past.

**3. (Level 3)** "it was arranged, in a newspaper's stiff voice" beside its coat **Answer: was/were + to-infinitive** — the formal arranged future: "The meeting was to take place in May."
