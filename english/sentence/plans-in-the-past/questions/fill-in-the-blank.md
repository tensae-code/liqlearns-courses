Fill each gap with the right shape.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** He ______ (just / go) to leave when he felt hungry. (Libya's burglar) **Answer: was just going to** — Libya's own model: was just going to + base verb, caught by when.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** My grandfather ______ (go) to return it, but he died. (Libya's Mr Dodd) **Answer: was going to** — the book's model sentence for the past plan and its but.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** She ______ (about) to sing when the lights failed. **Answer: was about to** — about to stands the action on the edge, a second before it starts.
