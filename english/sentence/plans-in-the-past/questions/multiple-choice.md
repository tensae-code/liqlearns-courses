# Plans in the past — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Plans in the Past Plan and Edge · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Plans in the Past Expectation · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Plans in the Past Formal Coat and Story.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** "We ______ travel on Friday, but the car broke." a) were going to  b) are going tomorrow  c) has going to **Answer: a) were going to** — the past plan wears be's past: were going to + base verb.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** "I was about to ______ when the phone rang." a) leave  b) left  c) leaving **Answer: a) leave** — after about to, the verb stands at its base.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** "The pupils ______ wear uniforms, but that day was a holiday dress day." a) were supposed to  b) about to yesterday  c) going **Answer: a) were supposed to** — supposed to reports the standing expectation or rule.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Which coat is the FORMAL newspaper one? a) The meeting was to take place in May.  b) The meeting was gonna happen in May.  c) The meeting was about to cookies. **Answer: a)** — was/were + bare to-infinitive is the formal arranged future met in print.
