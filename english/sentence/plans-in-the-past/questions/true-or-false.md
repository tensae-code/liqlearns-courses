# Plans in the past — true or false

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Level 1)** "Was going to" always means the plan failed. **Answer: False** — the form only holds the past plan; the story — often a but… — tells whether it happened.

**2. (Level 2)** Libya's Grade 12 book names this whole family "the future in the past." **Answer: True** — Units 3 and 4, Lesson 5 (pp.34 and 46) teach it under that name.

**3. (Level 3)** "I was supposed to meet him" can be true even when the meeting never happened. **Answer: True** — supposed to reports the expectation; whether it was kept is the story's next sentence.
