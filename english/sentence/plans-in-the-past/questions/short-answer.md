# Plans in the past — short answer

Answer in a word or a whole sentence, as asked.

**1. (Level 1)** Tell one true plan from last week in this lesson's main coat, with its ending. **Answer: (Learner's own sentence in was/were going to, with happened or but)** — the plan and its fate must both stand.

**2. (Level 2)** Catch the moment: your friend lifts the cup to drink, and the table shakes. Say the edge sentence. **Answer: He was about to drink when the table shook** — any true edge caught by when earns it.

**3. (Level 3)** Was he supposed to come at six? Give the short answer for NO. **Answer: No, he wasn't** — short answers ride was/were, the forms' moving piece.
