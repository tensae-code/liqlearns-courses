# Video script — Plans in the past

Super-simple voice. Read slow. The learner hears this
before or beside the written lesson.

---

Yesterday, I had a plan.

Listen to its coat.

I was going to travel.

Was going to. The plan of a past day.

And often — a but comes.

We were going to travel, but the car broke.

On the very edge?

I was about to leave when the phone rang.

About to. One second before.

What was expected?

We were supposed to meet at six.

Supposed to. The arrangement. Kept — or not.

And in books, the stiff, formal coat:

The meeting was to take place in May.

Was — to — take. No going. No about.

Four coats for the future, inside the past.

Now you try.

Tell me a plan from last week. With its but.
