# Video script — Auxiliary Verbs

Super-simple voice. The presenter is the LiqLearns mascot,
front-facing, on screen the whole video; the pictures named here
appear beside him.

[Three small helpers stand beside one big worker.]
"Some words HELP. They do not do the big job. They HELP the
big word!"

[The helper BE holds a running word.] "BE helps: I AM READING."

[The helper HAVE holds a finished word.] "HAVE helps: she
HAS EATEN."

[The helper DO holds a question mark.] "DO helps us ASK:
DO you know?"

[The whole family lines up.] "BE, DO, HAVE — and their modal
cousins. The helping verbs!"
