## Fill in the blank

1. A verb that helps the main verb is called an ______ verb. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: auxiliary**

2. "I ______ reading" — the auxiliary is a form of be. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: am**

3. "She ______ finished her work" — the auxiliary is a form of have. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: has**

4. "______ you know the way?" — the auxiliary that makes the question comes from do. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: Do**

5. "What on earth ______ we do?" — the book's own gap takes shall. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: shall**

6. "I promise I ______ tell lies again" — the book's own gap takes the will family, in its negative. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: will not (won't)**
