## Multiple choice

1. What is an auxiliary verb? (Sub lesson 1)
   a) The verb that carries the action  b) A verb that helps the main verb  c) A verb that ends a sentence
   **Answer: b) A verb that helps the main verb** — the main verb carries the action.

2. Which three are the most common auxiliary verbs? (Sub lesson 1)
   a) Go, make, take  b) Be, do, have  c) Can, must, will
   **Answer: b) Be, do, have** — the chief three; can, must and will are modals.

3. In "She has eaten", which word is the auxiliary? (Sub lesson 1)
   a) She  b) Eaten  c) Has
   **Answer: c) Has** — have helps; eaten, the past participle, carries the action.

4. Which helper makes the question "Do you play?" (Sub lesson 1)
   a) Do  b) Have  c) Be
   **Answer: a) Do** — do builds questions and negatives.

5. Which of these is a modal auxiliary? (Sub lesson 2)
   a) Walk  b) Must  c) Eaten
   **Answer: b) Must** — must belongs to the modal family.

6. After a modal, the main verb stands… (Sub lesson 2)
   a) In the -ing form  b) In the past participle  c) In its base form
   **Answer: c) In its base form** — "She can swim", never "can swims".
