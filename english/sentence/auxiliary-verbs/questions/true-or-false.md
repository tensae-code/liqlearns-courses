## True or false

1. The auxiliary verb carries the sentence's main action. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: False** — the main verb carries the action; the auxiliary helps.

2. Be + the -ing form builds the continuous tenses. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: True**

3. A statement like "You play" needs do before it can become a question. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: True** — "Do you play?"

4. The modal auxiliaries are a second family of helpers beside be, do and have. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: True**

5. After a modal, the main verb takes an -s: "She cans swim." (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: False** — the main verb stands in its base form: "She can swim."
