## Short answer

1. In "They are playing", name the auxiliary and the main verb. (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: auxiliary — are (be); main verb — playing.**

2. Which auxiliary builds the continuous tenses, and with what form of the main verb? (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: be, with the -ing form** — "I am reading", "They were playing".

3. Which auxiliary builds the perfect tenses, and with what form of the main verb? (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: have, with the past participle** — "She has eaten".

4. What three jobs does do do in sentences? (Sub lesson 1)
   **Answer: it makes questions, it makes negatives, and it adds stress (emphasis).**

5. Name four modal auxiliaries. (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: any four of can, could, may, might, must, shall, will, would, should, ought to.**

6. What does the modal must add to "You stop"? (Sub lesson 2)
   **Answer: obligation** — "You must stop" says stopping is required, not just happening.
