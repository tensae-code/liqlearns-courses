# Hands-on — Auxiliary Verbs

## Helper hunt

Take any page you are reading. Find five sentences that have a
helping verb. For each one, say the helper and the main verb:
"am — reading", "has — eaten", "do — know". A sentence with no
helper scores nothing; find another.

## Build and break

Build a sentence with a helper: "She is singing." Now take the
helper away and try to ask the question and make the negative
without it. Put do to work: "Does she sing? She does not
sing." Feel what each helper carries.
