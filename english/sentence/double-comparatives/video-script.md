# Video script — Double comparatives

Super-simple voice. Read slow. The learner hears this
before or beside the written lesson.

---

Read more. Learn faster.

The more you read, the faster you learn.

Two changes. Locked. Like two climbers on one rope.

The higher we climb, the colder it gets.

The more developed the city is, the more crowded it becomes.

Now one thing. Changing. Step by step.

The snowball gets bigger and bigger.

The days get longer and longer.

Life gets more and more difficult.

One change, said twice. That is the second shape.

Careful. Never say "very better."

Very is for plain words. Very big. Not very bigger.

Now you try. Lock two changes. Then grow one thing, step by step.
