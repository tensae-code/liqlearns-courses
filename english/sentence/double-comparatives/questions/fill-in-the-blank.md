# Double comparatives — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to, by what the item actually asks. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Double Comparatives — the Locked Pair, except gradual-change items, which are Sub lesson 2 — Double Comparatives — Gradual Change · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2, except locked-pair items, which are Sub lesson 1 · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 4 — Double Comparatives — the Error to Correct, except the item about China's use-evidence, which is Sub lesson 3 — Double Comparatives — the Shape in Use, and locked-pair items, which are Sub lesson 1.

Fill each gap with the right shape.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** ______ higher we climb, ______ colder it gets. **Answer: The / the** — both sides of the lock carry the + comparative (Greece, Hotel, Unit 4).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The traffic is getting worse and ______. **Answer: worse** — gradual change repeats the same comparative: worse and worse.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Life in the city gets ______ and ______ expensive every year. (more-form) **Answer: more / more** — long adjectives repeat the whole more-form: more and more expensive (Vietnam, Grade 12, Unit 4).

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Correct the gap: "Your English is very ______ today." (good → comparative, with a correct grader) ______ **Answer: much better** — very better is the error; the corrected grader is much (or far / a lot) better.
