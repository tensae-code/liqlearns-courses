# Double comparatives — fill in the blank

Fill each gap with the right shape.

**1. (Level 1)** ______ higher we climb, ______ colder it gets. **Answer: The / the** — both sides of the lock carry the + comparative (Greece, Hotel, Unit 4).

**2. (Level 2)** The traffic is getting worse and ______. **Answer: worse** — gradual change repeats the same comparative: worse and worse.

**3. (Level 2)** Life in the city gets ______ and ______ expensive every year. (more-form) **Answer: more / more** — long adjectives repeat the whole more-form: more and more expensive (Vietnam, Grade 12, Unit 4).

**4. (Level 3)** Correct the gap: "Your English is very ______ today." (good → comparative, with a correct grader) ______ **Answer: much better** — very better is the error; the corrected grader is much (or far / a lot) better.
