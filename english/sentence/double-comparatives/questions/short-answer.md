# Double comparatives — short answer

Answer in a word or a whole sentence, as asked.

**1. (Level 1)** Lock two true changes from your own life into one the more…the more sentence. **Answer: (Learner's own true locked sentence, the + comparative on both sides)** — the lever must stand first and the follower second.

**2. (Level 2)** Report one real gradual change near you in comparative-and-comparative. **Answer: (Learner's own sentence, e.g. "The queue is getting longer and longer.")** — one thing, one comparative, said twice with and.

**3. (Level 3)** Your friend says: "My new phone is very better." Correct the whole sentence and name the rule in one line. **Answer: "My new phone is much better." — very never grades a comparative.** — the corrected grader is much / far / a lot; very grades plain adjectives only.
