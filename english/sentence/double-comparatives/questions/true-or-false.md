# Double comparatives — true or false

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Level 1)** "The more, the merrier" follows the same locked shape as "the more you read, the faster you learn." **Answer: True** — both are the + comparative …, the + comparative …, with the second comparative understood.

**2. (Level 2)** In "bigger and bigger", two different things are being compared. **Answer: False** — comparative-and-comparative follows ONE thing changing step by step (Vietnam, Grade 12, Unit 4).

**3. (Level 3)** China's Grade 9 book teaches the rule of the more…the more in a grammar box. **Answer: False** — China carries the shape in dialogues only — use-evidence, never taught there, as its filing states.
