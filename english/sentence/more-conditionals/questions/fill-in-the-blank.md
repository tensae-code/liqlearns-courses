# More conditionals — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Mixed Conditionals · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Inverted Conditionals · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Unless and Provided That, except items about subjunctive were, which are Sub lesson 4 — Subjunctive Were.

Fill each gap with the right shape.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** If I had taken the job in 2020, I ______ (live) in another city now.
**Answer: would live** — the condition is past (had taken), the result is now, so would + verb.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** ______ you need anything, call me. (the if-less form, with should)
**Answer: Should** — Should you…? is the inverted first conditional: if drops, should steps first.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** ______ you study, you will fail the exam. (= if you do not study)
**Answer: Unless** — unless means if…not (Nepal, G10 U7).

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** If I ______ (be) you, I would apologise. (the unreal form for every who)
**Answer: were** — subjunctive were serves every who in unreal conditions.
