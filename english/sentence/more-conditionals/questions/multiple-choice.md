# More conditionals — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Mixed Conditionals · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Inverted Conditionals · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Unless and Provided That, except items about subjunctive were, which are Sub lesson 4 — Subjunctive Were.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** "If I had saved money then, I ______ rich now."
a) would have been  b) would be  c) will be
**Answer: b) would be** — past condition, present result: the result half wears the present coat (Armenia, §6.2).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** "If she weren't so shy, she ______ at the party last night."
a) would speak  b) speaks  c) would have spoken
**Answer: c) would have spoken** — present condition (she is shy now), past result: the reverse mix.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** "______ I known about the traffic, I would have left earlier."
a) If  b) Had  c) Have
**Answer: b) Had** — the inverted third conditional: if drops, had steps first (Nepal, G10 U7; Syria, G12 U12).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** "Were I you, I ______ rest."
a) will  b) would  c) shall
**Answer: b) would** — Were I you is the inverted second conditional; its result takes would.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** "You may go out ______ you are back by nine."
a) unless  b) provided that  c) had
**Answer: b) provided that** — provided that states a condition of permission, like a rule (Nepal, G10 U7).
