# More conditionals — short answer

Answer in whole sentences.

**1. (Level 1)** Say this as a mixed conditional: "I did not learn to swim as a child. Now I am afraid of deep water."
**Answer: If I had learned to swim as a child, I would not be afraid of deep water now.** — past condition, present result.

**2. (Level 2)** Say "If I had known" without the word if.
**Answer: Had I known…** — if drops, had steps to the front (Nepal, G10 U7).

**3. (Level 2)** Give advice to a tired friend, starting "Were I you…".
**Answer: Were I you, I would go to bed early.** — the inverted second conditional, with would in the result.

**4. (Level 3)** Warn a friend who is leaving late, using unless.
**Answer: Unless you leave now, you will miss the bus.** — unless = if you do not leave now.
