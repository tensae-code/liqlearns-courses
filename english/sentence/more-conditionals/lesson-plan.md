# Lesson plan — More conditionals

**Course:** English · **Title:** Sentence · **Lesson:** More conditionals
**Place in the curriculum:** Level 25, "Wishing and Imagining" —
the second lesson of the level (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the conditional
machine's advanced gears — **mixed conditionals** (a past
condition with a present result, and the reverse),
**inverted conditionals** (Had I known…, Were I you…,
Should you need…), the condition words **unless** and
**provided that**, and the **subjunctive were**.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Conditionals
(the four rungs) and Wish and if only (the same unreal
coats). It is the last of the imagining machinery.
**What the learner can do at the end:** mix the rungs on
purpose and say which time each half talks about; read
and build the if-less inverted forms; swap if…not for
unless and if for provided that; and use were for every
who in unreal conditions.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Conditionals (rungs 0–3) and Wish
  and if only. Recommended, never forced — this lesson
  stands alone for a learner who lands on it directly
  (DIRECT-ENTRY rule); the rungs are restated here.
- Speaker convention: the explanation is read in the
  learner's main language; the TARGET forms (Had I
  known…, unless, provided that, Were I you…) are never
  translated and are read in the English voice.

## Media slots
1. **Slot 1 — a picture of two clocks: a yesterday clock
   chained to a today clock, one arrow running from the
   past clock into a present-tense sentence.** Written
   slot: "a picture of a mixed conditional — the two
   halves keep different times." What the student does:
   the student says which half is past and which is
   present before reading the rule.
2. **Slot 2 — a picture of a door with the word IF taken
   off its hinges and standing beside it, the door still
   open.** Written slot: "a picture of an inverted
   conditional — if is gone, the helper steps first."
   What the student does: the student rebuilds the
   sentence with if, then takes if away again.
3. **Slot 3 — a picture of a gate with a small sign:
   ONLY IF YOU HURRY.** Written slot: "a picture of
   unless and provided that as gate-words." What the
   student does: the student says the warning both ways:
   "Unless you hurry…" and "If you don't hurry…".

## The run of the lesson, in order
1. **Do it first**: one regret whose result is still
   here today — said first in the learner's own language.
2. **The rungs, restated**: zero, first, second, third —
   one time in both halves.
3. **Mixed conditionals**: past condition, present
   result (Armenia G11 §6.2, pp.164–168, through
   space-history counterfactuals; Malaysia Form 5 mixed);
   present condition, past result. Ethiopia's Grade 12
   lessons circle mixed patterns in "tense balance" and
   "common variations" work without naming the category —
   worded so here.
4. **Inverted conditionals**: Had I known… (Nepal G10
   Unit 7, pp.71–82; Syria G12 Unit 12, inversion in
   full, Workbook pp.90–97); Were I you…; Should you
   need…
5. **Unless / provided that**: Nepal G10 Unit 7 teaches
   second and third conditionals with unless, provided
   that and inverted Had in one unit.
6. **Subjunctive were**: if I were you — South Africa's
   Grade 5 HL Workbook 2 carries sentence mood including
   the subjunctive, the set's peak book.
7. **The third rung, revisited**: Kuwait Pearls 9B
   teaches the third conditional (if + past perfect →
   would have + participle; regret/blame) — the base
   the mixed forms mix from.
8. **Do it yourself**: the three closing tasks.

## If the learner is stuck
- Stuck on a mixed form: label each half's time first —
  the condition's coat tells its time, the result's coat
  tells its own. The halves are free; only the meaning
  must be true.
- Stuck on inversion: put if back. "Had I known" =
  "If I had known" with if dropped and had stepped
  first. If the rebuild works, the inversion is right.
- This lesson's next door: Level 26 — How Sure Are
  You?, opening with deduction about now.
