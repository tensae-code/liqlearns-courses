# Hands-on — How sure you are

You do it, we guide you. Everything here is done with a
companion or the Explain Companion, out loud.

1. **The mystery object (Yemen's game).** Your companion
   hides a small object and shows you only a corner of
   it, or one close-up photo of part of it — the way
   Yemen's Book 6 opens the topic (Unit 5). Deduce out
   loud, rung by rung: "It can't be a spoon. It might be
   a key. It may be…" Each new corner shown, re-say the
   ladder. Score: your last must be must be right.
2. **The wet-umbrella walk.** Walk your home and find
   five signs (a wet mat, a sleeping cat, music behind
   a door). For each sign, one deduction sentence, with
   its number said after it: "…must be… — ninety-five."
3. **Mysteries (Cambodia's unit).** Take one small
   mystery — a missing pen, a sound in the night, a
   light left on. Each person offers three deductions:
   one must be, one might be, one can't be, each with
   its sign. The best-signed ladder wins.
4. **The trap round.** Your companion tries to catch
   you: they say a mustn't deduction ("He mustn't be
   home"). You must repair it on the spot: "He can't be
   home." Five traps, five repairs.
