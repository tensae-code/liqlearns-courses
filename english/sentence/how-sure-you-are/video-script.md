# Video script — How sure you are

Super-simple voice. Read slow. The learner hears this
before or beside the written lesson.

---

Look at the signs. Then say how sure you are.

The umbrella is wet. Water is on the floor.
It MUST be raining.
Must be — I am sure. Ninety-five to one hundred percent sure.

The sun is hot. The boy has a thick coat. He is sweating.
He CAN'T be cold.
Can't be — I am sure it is NOT true.
Ninety-five to one hundred percent sure it is not.

Now the middle. I am not sure.
The sky is grey. It MAY be raining soon.
It COULD be raining soon.
May. Could. Fifty percent. Half sure, half not.

And smaller still.
A small sound outside. It MIGHT be a cat.
Might. Thirty percent. Possible, but not half sure.

Four words. Four numbers.
Must — almost one hundred, yes.
Can't — almost one hundred, no.
May and could — fifty.
Might — thirty.

Careful. One trap.
Mustn't is for rules. Do not run — you mustn't run.
Mustn't never guesses. A guess that says no is CAN'T be.

Now you try.
Look around your room.
Say one must be. Say one can't be.
Say one may be. Say one might be.
Point at the sign for each one.
