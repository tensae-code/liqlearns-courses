# How sure you are — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — How Sure You Are — the Sure Ends · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — How Sure You Are — the Possible Middle · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — How Sure You Are — the Whole Ladder Live, except items that ask only about can't be or about repairing mustn't, which are Sub lesson 1 by what they ask.

Fill each gap with the right certainty word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Her voice is coming from the kitchen and something is frying. She ______ (be) cooking.
**Answer: must be** — the signs prove it: 95–100% sure (Egypt).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** His bicycle is here, but his coat and bag are gone and the house is dark. He ______ (be) home.
**Answer: can't be** — the signs kill it: 95–100% sure it is NOT true.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** I am not sure where Sara is. She ______ (be) at the library — she often goes on Fridays.
**Answer: might be (may be / could be also earn it, said less sure)** — a possible guess under half-to-half sure; might prices it at 30% (Egypt).

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Egypt's ladder: must = 95–100% true, may/could = ______, might = 30%.
**Answer: 50%** — may and could share the 50% rung (Egypt, Sec 12 T1, pp.40–43).
