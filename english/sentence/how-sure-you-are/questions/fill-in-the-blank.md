# How sure you are — fill in the blank

Fill each gap with the right certainty word.

**1. (Level 1)** Her voice is coming from the kitchen and something is frying. She ______ (be) cooking.
**Answer: must be** — the signs prove it: 95–100% sure (Egypt).

**2. (Level 1)** His bicycle is here, but his coat and bag are gone and the house is dark. He ______ (be) home.
**Answer: can't be** — the signs kill it: 95–100% sure it is NOT true.

**3. (Level 2)** I am not sure where Sara is. She ______ (be) at the library — she often goes on Fridays.
**Answer: might be (may be / could be also earn it, said less sure)** — a possible guess under half-to-half sure; might prices it at 30% (Egypt).

**4. (Level 3)** Egypt's ladder: must = 95–100% true, may/could = ______, might = 30%.
**Answer: 50%** — may and could share the 50% rung (Egypt, Sec 12 T1, pp.40–43).
