# How sure you are — matching

Match each deduction to its rung on Egypt's ladder.

**1. (Level 1)** "It must be raining." **Answer: 95–100% sure it is true** — must prices a proved guess at the top rung (Egypt).

**2. (Level 2)** "It might be a cat." **Answer: 30% possible** — might is the low possible rung, under may/could (Egypt).

**3. (Level 3)** "He can't be cold." **Answer: 95–100% sure it is NOT true** — can't mirrors must at the bottom: certainty, pointing the other way (Egypt).
