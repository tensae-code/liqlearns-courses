# How sure you are — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — How Sure You Are — the Sure Ends · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — How Sure You Are — the Possible Middle · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — How Sure You Are — the Whole Ladder Live, except items that ask only about can't be or about repairing mustn't, which are Sub lesson 1 by what they ask.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The floor is wet and her umbrella is dripping. "It ______ raining."
a) must be  b) might be  c) can't be
**Answer: a) must be** — the sign proves it: 95–100% sure it is true (Egypt's numbers).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The sun is blazing and he is sweating in a thick coat. "He ______ cold."
a) must be  b) can't be  c) may be
**Answer: b) can't be** — the sign kills it: 95–100% sure it is NOT true (Egypt).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The sky is grey, and grey skies rain about half the time here. "It ______ rain by noon."
a) must be  b) may be  c) can't be
**Answer: b) may be** — may/could stand at 50%, the coin-flip rung (Egypt).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A tiny sound outside, probably the wind. "It ______ a cat."
a) must be  b) can't be  c) might be
**Answer: c) might be** — might stands at 30%: possible, but less than half sure (Egypt).

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Which sentence is a correct deduction?
a) He mustn't be home — his light is off.  b) He can't be home — his light is off and his car is gone.  c) He mayn't be home.
**Answer: b)** — a deduction that says NO uses can't be; mustn't is a rule word and never guesses.
