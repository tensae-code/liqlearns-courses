# How sure you are — true or false

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Level 1)** "Must be" means the speaker is 95–100% sure it is true.
**Answer: True** — Egypt's printed certainty numbers (Sec 12 T1, pp.40–43).

**2. (Level 1)** "Mustn't be" is the deduction form that means "sure it is not true".
**Answer: False** — mustn't is a rule word (do not); the deduction is can't be, 95–100% sure it is NOT true.

**3. (Level 2)** On Egypt's ladder, might (30%) shows MORE sureness than may (50%).
**Answer: False** — might is the lower rung: 30% against may/could's 50%.

**4. (Level 3)** A deduction needs a sign — something the speaker can see, hear or prove now.
**Answer: True** — deduction is guessing from signs; without a sign there is nothing to price (Yemen's photo-fragment game, Book 6 U5, plays exactly this).
