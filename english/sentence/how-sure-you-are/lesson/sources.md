Egypt, Malaysia, Nepal, Yemen).** Egypt prices the
whole ladder in print — must 95–100% true, may/could
50%, might 30%, can't 95–100% NOT true — and no other
book in the set prints numbers. Yemen plays the ladder
as a guessing game from photo fragments; Cambodia
gives it a mysteries unit, on teacher's-book evidence;
Malaysia groups certainty/deduction inside its modal
units at Forms 3 and 5; Nepal works deduction inside
its Grade 7 rules unit. The build plan also named a
Nepal Grade 7 Unit 15 anchor for this lesson; the
filing's Unit 15 deduction evidence was not confirmed
(the held Grade 7 deduction unit is Unit 10), so only
Unit 10 is cited.
