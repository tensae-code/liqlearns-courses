# Stronger comparisons — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB
LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub
Lessons"). Practice is tied to the skill number, as before. The
questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson
view. The sub lessons are the depths inside this one thing —
grading a comparison — saying how big the gap is, or that there
is no gap at all — set by the pattern across the countries'
books: the graders first, big then small, then equality, then
adverbs and absolutes.

Mapping from the old depth layers: old Level 1 covered two
natural jumps, so it splits at the jump — its big-graders part
is Sub lesson 1 and its small-graders part is Sub lesson 2, and
its pass mark splits the same way below; old Level 2 = Sub
lesson 3; old Level 3 also splits — its adverbs part is Sub
lesson 4 and its absolutes part is Sub lesson 5, pass mark split
the same way. Some question items were filed under an old level
whose heading covers both halves of a split; those items are
marked by what they actually ask, item by item.
