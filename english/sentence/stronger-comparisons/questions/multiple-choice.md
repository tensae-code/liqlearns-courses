# Stronger comparisons — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** "This city is ______ bigger than ours — the gap is huge." a) far  b) a bit  c) slightly **Answer: a) far** — far grades a big gap; a bit and slightly grade small ones (Egypt, Secondary 12, Unit 11).

**2. (Level 1)** "The tea is ______ sweeter than I like — just a touch." a) far  b) a bit  c) much **Answer: b) a bit** — a small difference takes a little / a bit / slightly (Egypt, Comparative Phrases box, p.26).

**3. (Level 2)** Which sentence is RIGHT? a) She is as taller as me.  b) She is as tall as me.  c) She is as tallest as me. **Answer: b)** — inside as…as the adjective keeps its plain shape; the frame does the comparing.

**4. (Level 3)** "He drives ______ than his brother." (well) a) more well  b) weller  c) better **Answer: c) better** — well is a mover: well → better, like badly → worse (Nepal, Grade 7, Unit 9).
