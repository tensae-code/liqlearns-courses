# Stronger comparisons — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to, by what the item actually asks. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Stronger Comparisons — Big Gaps, Big Graders, except small-gap items, which are Sub lesson 2 — Stronger Comparisons — Small Gaps, Small Graders · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 3 — Stronger Comparisons — No Gap: the As…As Family, except small-gap items, which are Sub lesson 2 · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 4 — Stronger Comparisons — Adverbs Compare Too, except items about absolute adjectives, which are Sub lesson 5 — Stronger Comparisons — Absolute Adjectives.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** "This city is ______ bigger than ours — the gap is huge." a) far  b) a bit  c) slightly **Answer: a) far** — far grades a big gap; a bit and slightly grade small ones (Egypt, Secondary 12, Unit 11).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** "The tea is ______ sweeter than I like — just a touch." a) far  b) a bit  c) much **Answer: b) a bit** — a small difference takes a little / a bit / slightly (Egypt, Comparative Phrases box, p.26).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which sentence is RIGHT? a) She is as taller as me.  b) She is as tall as me.  c) She is as tallest as me. **Answer: b)** — inside as…as the adjective keeps its plain shape; the frame does the comparing.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** "He drives ______ than his brother." (well) a) more well  b) weller  c) better **Answer: c) better** — well is a mover: well → better, like badly → worse (Nepal, Grade 7, Unit 9).
