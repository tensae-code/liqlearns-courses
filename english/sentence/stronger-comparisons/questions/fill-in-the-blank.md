# Stronger comparisons — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to, by what the item actually asks. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Stronger Comparisons — Big Gaps, Big Graders, except small-gap items, which are Sub lesson 2 — Stronger Comparisons — Small Gaps, Small Graders · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 3 — Stronger Comparisons — No Gap: the As…As Family, except small-gap items, which are Sub lesson 2 · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 4 — Stronger Comparisons — Adverbs Compare Too, except items about absolute adjectives, which are Sub lesson 5 — Stronger Comparisons — Absolute Adjectives.

Fill each gap with the right shape.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The new bridge is ______ shorter than the old one. (a big-gap grader: f__) **Answer: far** — far … than names a big difference (Egypt, p.26).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** My bag is ______ heavier than yours — only a little. (a small-gap grader) **Answer: a bit / a little / slightly** — small gaps take a little / a bit / slightly.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** He is not ______ as tall as his father — he is far short of him. (n_____) **Answer: nearly** — not nearly as…as = far short; Cambodia Grade 12, Unit 28 grades the family this way.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** She speaks ______ carefully than before. (more / most) **Answer: more** — -ly adverbs compare with more: more carefully (Nepal, Grade 7, Unit 9, Grammar II).
