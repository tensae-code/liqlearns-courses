# Stronger comparisons — fill in the blank

Fill each gap with the right shape.

**1. (Level 1)** The new bridge is ______ shorter than the old one. (a big-gap grader: f__) **Answer: far** — far … than names a big difference (Egypt, p.26).

**2. (Level 2)** My bag is ______ heavier than yours — only a little. (a small-gap grader) **Answer: a bit / a little / slightly** — small gaps take a little / a bit / slightly.

**3. (Level 2)** He is not ______ as tall as his father — he is far short of him. (n_____) **Answer: nearly** — not nearly as…as = far short; Cambodia Grade 12, Unit 28 grades the family this way.

**4. (Level 3)** She speaks ______ carefully than before. (more / most) **Answer: more** — -ly adverbs compare with more: more carefully (Nepal, Grade 7, Unit 9, Grammar II).
