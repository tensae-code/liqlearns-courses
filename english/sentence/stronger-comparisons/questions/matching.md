# Stronger comparisons — matching

Match each item to its partner.

**1. (Level 1)** far / a lot / much before a comparative **Answer: a BIG gap** — Egypt's Secondary 12 restatement names much / a lot / far as the big-gap intensifiers.

**2. (Level 2)** almost as…as (Egypt's box) **Answer: a SMALL difference — near-equality** — the box sorts almost as…as / slightly…than as the small-difference forms, p.26.

**3. (Level 3)** perfect, unique, dead, empty **Answer: absolute adjectives — no more, no most** — they name a whole state; Egypt Secondary 12, Unit 11 states the refusal.
