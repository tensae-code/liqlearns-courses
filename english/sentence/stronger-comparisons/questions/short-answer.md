# Stronger comparisons — short answer

Answer in a word or a whole sentence, as asked.

**1. (Level 1)** Compare two pencils on your desk with a small-gap grader, in one whole sentence. **Answer: (Learner's own true sentence, e.g. "This pencil is a bit longer than that one.")** — the grader must match the small real gap.

**2. (Level 2)** Say that two runners finished level, then that one was a hair behind. Two sentences. **Answer: "They ran as fast as each other. One was not quite as fast as the other."** — as…as for the tie; not quite as…as for a hair short (Cambodia, Grade 12, Unit 28).

**3. (Level 3)** Why is "more empty" refused? Say it in one line, then say the sentence right. **Answer: Empty is absolute — a thing is empty or it is not, so it takes no grader: "The bottle is empty."** — absolute adjectives take no more/most (Egypt, Secondary 12, Unit 11).
