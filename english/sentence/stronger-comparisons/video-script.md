# Video script — Stronger comparisons

Super-simple voice. Read slow. The learner hears this
before or beside the written lesson.

---

Two boxes. One is big. One is small.

Plain words say: this box is bigger.

Strong words say HOW MUCH bigger.

Far bigger. A lot bigger. Much bigger.

The gap is big. The words are big.

Now two cups. Almost the same.

A little smaller. A bit cheaper. Slightly slower.

The gap is small. The words are small.

Two runners. The same speed.

As fast as. Exactly the same.

One step back? Not quite as fast.

Far back? Not nearly as fast.

And some words take no more at all.

Perfect is perfect. Empty is empty. No "more perfect."

Now you try. Take two things. Size the gap. Say it.
