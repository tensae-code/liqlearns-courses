# Hands-on — Stronger comparisons

You do it, we guide you. Everything here is done in the
learner's real day, with a companion or the Explain
Companion. The TARGET forms are always said in English;
the talk around them may be in the learner's own language.

1. **The gap-size walk.** Walk the room with a companion. At each stop compare two things twice: once with a big-gap grader (far / a lot / much), once with a small-gap grader (a little / a bit / slightly). The companion judges each time: does the grader match the real gap? Three stops each.
2. **The tie game.** Line up pairs of things that are truly equal (two same-size cups, two same-length pencils). Say the tie whole — "as long as", "exactly the same as" — then break the tie by one small move and say "not quite as…as"; by a big move, "not nearly as…as."
3. **The absolute hunt.** Hunt the room and the street for things that are absolute — empty/full, dead/alive (a battery), unique. Name each one plain, with no grader: the grader must be refused aloud ("not 'more empty' — just empty").
