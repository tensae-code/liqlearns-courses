# Quantifiers — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Quantifiers — Some, Any, Much and Many · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Quantifiers — Small Amounts, Plenty, Enough, Less and Fewer · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Quantifiers — Partitives and Containers.

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** "Any" is the quantity word of questions and negative sentences.
**Answer: True** — "Do you have any…? / I don't have any." (Cambodia G9 U7.)

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** "Few friends came" and "A few friends came" mean exactly the same.
**Answer: False** — a few = some (content); bare few = almost none (a complaint) (Nepal G10 U13).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** We say "fewer time" and "less mistakes".
**Answer: False** — the pair is the other way: less + uncountable (less time), fewer + countable (fewer mistakes).

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** "A bread" is the normal way to buy bread in a shop.
**Answer: False** — bread is uncountable; buy it by its handle: a loaf of bread (Libya's and Mongolia's sets).
