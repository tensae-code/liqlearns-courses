# Quantifiers — fill in the blank

Fill each gap with the right quantity word.

**1. (Level 1)** I don't have ______ money with me. (negative sentence)
**Answer: any** — negatives take any (some lives in positive sentences).

**2. (Level 1)** How ______ apples are in the bag?
**Answer: many** — apples are countable: how many (Bolivia Y2's contrast table).

**3. (Level 2)** We have ______ of time — two whole hours. (more than enough)
**Answer: plenty** — plenty of = more than enough, happily (Cambodia G9 U20).

**4. (Level 3)** I bought a ______ of eggs and a ______ of beans at the shop. (Libya's set)
**Answer: box / tin** — "I bought a box of eggs" is Libya's own bought sentence; a tin of beans is in the same set (book pp.104–105).
