# Quantifiers — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Quantifiers — Some, Any, Much and Many · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Quantifiers — Small Amounts, Plenty, Enough, Less and Fewer · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Quantifiers — Partitives and Containers.

Fill each gap with the right quantity word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** I don't have ______ money with me. (negative sentence)
**Answer: any** — negatives take any (some lives in positive sentences).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** How ______ apples are in the bag?
**Answer: many** — apples are countable: how many (Bolivia Y2's contrast table).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** We have ______ of time — two whole hours. (more than enough)
**Answer: plenty** — plenty of = more than enough, happily (Cambodia G9 U20).

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** I bought a ______ of eggs and a ______ of beans at the shop. (Libya's set)
**Answer: box / tin** — "I bought a box of eggs" is Libya's own bought sentence; a tin of beans is in the same set (book pp.104–105).
