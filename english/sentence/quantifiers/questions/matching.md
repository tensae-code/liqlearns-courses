# Quantifiers — matching

Match each phrase to what it does.

**1. (Level 1)** "some tea" in "Would you like some tea?" **Answer: an offer's some** — offers and requests take some, not any (Cambodia G9 U7).

**2. (Level 2)** "little money" (no a) **Answer: almost none — a complaint** — bare little/few mean almost none; the a carries the smile (Nepal G10 U13).

**3. (Level 3)** "a jar of jam" **Answer: a partitive** — jar + of makes uncountable jam countable for the shop (Mongolia VI U8, p.88).
