# Quantifiers — matching

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Quantifiers — Some, Any, Much and Many · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Quantifiers — Small Amounts, Plenty, Enough, Less and Fewer · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Quantifiers — Partitives and Containers.

Match each phrase to what it does.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** "some tea" in "Would you like some tea?" **Answer: an offer's some** — offers and requests take some, not any (Cambodia G9 U7).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** "little money" (no a) **Answer: almost none — a complaint** — bare little/few mean almost none; the a carries the smile (Nepal G10 U13).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** "a jar of jam" **Answer: a partitive** — jar + of makes uncountable jam countable for the shop (Mongolia VI U8, p.88).
