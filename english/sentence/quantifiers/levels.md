# Quantifiers — questions by level

Practice by level, tied to the skill number. The questions live in
the type files; this file is the by-level view. The levels are the
depths inside this one thing — saying how much without a
number — set by the pattern across the countries' books:
some/any and much/many first (Cambodia and Malaysia open
there), then the judging words, then the partitives that
let you shop.

## Level 1 — some/any and much/many
Level 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: some in
positive sentences and offers, any in questions and
negatives; much with uncountable, many with countable —
sorted by the pour-or-count test.

## Level 2 — a few/a little, plenty, too much, enough, less/fewer
Level 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: a few/
a little against bare few/little (the smile against the
complaint); plenty of for more than enough; too much/
too many for the spill; enough in both its seats; fewer
for countable, less for uncountable.

## Level 3 — partitives and containers
Level 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: shop
for uncountable things by their handles — a loaf of, a
bottle of, a packet of, a kilo of (Mongolia's Quantity
Nouns; Libya's shop set) — in full requests and bought
sentences.
