# The present perfect continuous — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Present Perfect Continuous's Shape · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The Stretch and Its Measures · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — The Marks — Just Stopped (item 4) and Sub lesson 4 — Recent Habits and the Neighbours (item 5).

Answer in whole sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What has Huda been doing? (taking photos)
**Answer: She's been taking photos.** (Libya, Grade 10,
p.13 — Track 10's table.)

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How long have you been learning English?
Answer with for.
**Answer: I have been learning English for … years.**
(The learner's own true length; for + a length.)

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** You arrived at the bus stop at ten. It
is now eleven and you are still there. Say it with since,
then with for.
**Answer: I have been waiting since ten o'clock. I
have been waiting for one hour.** (Libya, Grade 10 Work
Book sentences.)

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Your friend's eyes are red. Explain
why, in this tense.
**Answer: She has been crying.** (Any true just-stopped
action whose mark shows; Libya, Grade 10 Work Book has
"She's been crying for ten minutes.")

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** Ask a swimmer about their recent days,
the way Libya's Grade 9 book asks it.
**Answer: Have you been swimming a lot?** (Grade 9 Round
up, p.41.)
