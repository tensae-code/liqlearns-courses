# The present perfect continuous — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Level 1)** "The pilot ______ flying for two hours."
a) has been  b) have being  c) is been
**Answer: a) has been** — have/has + been + verb-ing
(Libya, Grade 8, p.38 — the book's own example).

**2. (Level 1)** "I ______ for the bus since four o'clock."
a) have been waiting  b) has been waiting  c) been waiting
**Answer: a) have been waiting** — have with I, and the
verb wears -ing (Libya, Grade 8, p.38).

**3. (Level 2)** "We've been waiting for a bus ______ ten
o'clock."
a) for  b) since  c) from that
**Answer: b) since** — ten o'clock is a starting point
(Libya, Grade 10 Work Book).

**4. (Level 2)** "It's been raining ______ two hours."
a) since  b) for  c) ago
**Answer: b) for** — two hours is a length (Libya, Grade
10 Work Book).

**5. (Level 3)** "I'm tired because I ______ all night."
a) have been studying  b) study yesterday  c) am study
**Answer: a) have been studying** — the action stopped;
its mark (tiredness) shows now (Egypt, Secondary 12,
pp.17–19 — the book's own example).

**6. (Level 3)** Which question asks about the moving
action, not the finished count?
a) How many books have you read?
b) How long have you been reading?
c) Did you read yesterday?
**Answer: b)** — the continuous watches the action and
its length; the simple perfect counts the done.
