# The present perfect continuous — matching

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every set's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The Stretch and Its Measures (Set 1, for and since) · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — The Marks — Just Stopped (Set 2, marks and the actions that left them).

**Set 1 (Sub lesson 2)** — match each measure to its word,
the way Libya's Grade 10 Work Book drills them.
1. two hours — **for**
2. ten o'clock — **since**
3. ten minutes — **for**
4. Tuesday — **since**
5. two days — **for**
6. four o'clock — **since**

**Set 2 (Sub lesson 3)** — match each mark to the action that
left it.
1. tired eyes after a long night at the desk — **has
   been studying**
2. wet streets in the morning — **has been raining**
3. red eyes and a wet handkerchief — **has been crying**
4. paint on both hands — **has been painting**
5. flour on the apron, cakes on the tray — **has been
   making Eid cakes**

(Set 2's actions are the books' own: Egypt's studying
sentence; Libya Grade 10's raining and crying sentences;
the Eid cakes from its Work Book, pp.11–12.)
