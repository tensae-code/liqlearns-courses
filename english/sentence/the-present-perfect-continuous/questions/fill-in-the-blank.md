# The present perfect continuous — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — The Present Perfect Continuous's Shape · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — The Stretch and Its Measures · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 1 (item 5, the shape in a real sentence), except item 6, recent habits -> Sub lesson 4 — Recent Habits and the Neighbours. Items are marked by what each item actually asks.

Fill each gap with the right shape.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** She ______ ______ ______ (cook) since
noon.
**Answer: has been cooking** — the three fixed pieces:
has + been + verb-ing.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** He ______ (not sleep) well lately.
**Answer: hasn't been sleeping** — Libya, Grade 10 Work
Book key.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** I've been writing a poem ______ Tuesday.
**Answer: since** — Tuesday is a starting point (Libya,
Grade 10 Work Book).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** You've been watching TV ______ an hour.
**Answer: for** — an hour is a length (Libya, Grade 10
Work Book).

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** I have been ______ (learn) how to ride
a quad bike.
**Answer: learning** — Libya, Grade 9 Round up's own
sentence, p.41.

**6. (Sub lesson 4)** ______ you ______ ______ (go) to the
beach lately?
**Answer: Have … been going** — Libya, Grade 9's
recent-habits box, p.40.
