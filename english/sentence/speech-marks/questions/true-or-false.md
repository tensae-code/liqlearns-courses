# Speech marks — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Speech Marks Cage and Capital · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Speech Marks Frame Positions · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Speech Marks Split Speech and Conversion.

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The full stop that ends a speech stands
outside the closing speech marks.
**Answer: False** — the speech's own punctuation is
part of the speech and lives inside the cage: "I am
ready."

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** "I am ready," Musa said — the comma
inside the marks is a mistake for a full stop.
**Answer: False** — when the frame follows, the full
stop softens into a comma inside the marks because the
whole sentence has not finished.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** South Africa's Grade 6 Workbook 1
worksheet 6, headed "Reported speech", teaches reported
speech.
**Answer: False** — the worksheet teaches direct-speech
punctuation (inverted commas, including the two-set
split speech); the heading is the book's own mislabel,
filed as a slip. This lesson teaches the punctuation,
not the label.
