# Speech marks — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Speech Marks Cage and Capital · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Speech Marks Frame Positions · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Speech Marks Split Speech and Conversion.

Fill each gap with the right mark or word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Musa said ___ "I am ready." — the mark
that stands on the doorstep between the frame and the
speech.
**Answer: , (a comma)** — when the reporting clause
leads, a comma stands before the opening speech marks.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Amina asked, "___ you coming?" — the
first letter inside the marks.
**Answer: A (a capital A)** — a speech always opens
with a capital letter, because it is a sentence in its
own right.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** "Watch out___" shouted the driver! —
the mark that ends the speech, inside the cage.
**Answer: ! (an exclamation mark, inside the closing
marks)** — the speech's own ending punctuation lives
inside; nothing of the speech stands outside the cage.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Kabanda said that he was hungry. —
Rwanda's conversion (Pupil's Book 6, p.84) turns this
report into direct speech: Kabanda said, "___ ___ ___."
**Answer: I am hungry** — converting a said-that report
into direct speech restores the speaker's own person
and tense inside the marks.
