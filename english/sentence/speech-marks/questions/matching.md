# Speech marks — matching

Match each broken line to its repair.

**1. (Level 1)** Broken: Musa said "I am ready".
**Answer: Musa said, "I am ready."** — the comma was
missing from the doorstep between the frame and the
speech.

**2. (Level 2)** Broken: "I am ready." Musa said, all on
one line after Amina's speech. **Answer: the line drops
to a new line of its own: "I am ready," Musa said.** —
a new speaker starts a new line, and the full stop
softens to a comma before its following frame.

**3. (Level 3)** Broken report: Munezero said that the
pumpkin was ready. — convert it (Rwanda, p.84).
**Answer: Munezero said, "The pumpkin is ready."** —
the conversion restores the exact words: person and
tense come back inside the marks.
