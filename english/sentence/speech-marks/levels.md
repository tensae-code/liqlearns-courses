# Speech marks — questions by level

Practice by level, tied to the skill number. The questions live in
the type files; this file is the by-level view. The levels are the
depths inside this one thing — setting a speaker's exact words on
the page — set by the pattern across the countries' books: the
cage and the capital first (South Africa's Grade 5 rule page),
then the frame in its positions, then the split speech and the
conversion Rwanda practises.

## Level 1 — the cage and the capital
Level 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: put
inverted commas around a speaker's exact words, open
the speech with a capital letter, and set the comma
before the quote when the reporting clause leads.

## Level 2 — the frame in its positions
Level 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: end
the speech with its punctuation INSIDE the marks; turn
the full stop into a comma when the reporting clause
follows; and begin a new line for every new speaker in
a dialogue.

## Level 3 — the split speech and the conversion
Level 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: cage
a split speech in two sets of marks with its reporting
clause in the middle — and convert a said-that report
into direct speech and back, keeping the exact words
exact.
