# Speech marks — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB
LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub
Lessons"). Practice is tied to the skill number, as before. The
questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson
view. The sub lessons are the depths inside this one thing — setting a
speaker's exact words on the page — set by the pattern across the
countries' books: the cage and the capital first (South Africa's
Grade 5 rule page), then the frame in its positions, then the
split speech and the conversion Rwanda practises.

Mapping from the old depth layers: old Level 1 = Sub lesson 1;
old Level 2 = Sub lesson 2; old Level 3 = Sub lesson 3.

## Sub lesson 1 — Speech Marks Cage and Capital
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: put
inverted commas around a speaker's exact words, open
the speech with a capital letter, and set the comma
before the quote when the reporting clause leads.

## Sub lesson 2 — Speech Marks Frame Positions
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: end
the speech with its punctuation INSIDE the marks; turn
the full stop into a comma when the reporting clause
follows; and begin a new line for every new speaker in
a dialogue.

## Sub lesson 3 — Speech Marks Split Speech and Conversion
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: cage
a split speech in two sets of marks with its reporting
clause in the middle — and convert a said-that report
into direct speech and back, keeping the exact words
exact.
