# The past continuous — questions by level

Practice by level, tied to the skill number. The questions live in
the type files; this file is the by-level view. The levels are the
depths (RANKS) inside this one thing — the moving past scene — interrupted and side by side — set by the
pattern across the countries' books: the shape first, then its job,
then its borders with its neighbours.

## Level 1 — the shape and the scene
Level 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: build was/were + verb-ing for every who, set a scene at a named past time, and ask and deny in the tense.

## Level 2 — the interrupted scene
Level 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: join a moving scene to a sharp past-simple cut with when, in both orders (scene first, cut first), and hold Vietnam's rule: while only before the past continuous.

## Level 3 — two scenes and the story
Level 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: run two simultaneous scenes with while, and open a mini-story with a scene-setting sentence the past-simple events can stand on.
