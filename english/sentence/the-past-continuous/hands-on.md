# Hands-on — The past continuous

You do it, we guide you. Everything here is done in the
learner's real day, with a companion or the Explain
Companion. The TARGET forms are always said in English;
the talk around them may be in the learner's own language.

1. **Freeze and cut.** One person sets a moving scene aloud ("At seven, we were eating…") and keeps it going with details. The companion shouts a sharp event — phone! knock! rain! — and the first person must catch it in one sentence with when. Five rounds.
2. **The two-room watch.** Describe one past hour in two rooms of your home at once, in while sentences: "While Mum was cooking, I was doing my homework." Three pairs; every side must be moving, none finished.
3. **Story opener swap.** Each person writes ONE scene-setting sentence about a real place (the market, the bus, the field). Swap papers: each writes the three past-simple events that happened inside the other's scene.
