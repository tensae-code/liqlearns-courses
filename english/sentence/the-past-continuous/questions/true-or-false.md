# The past continuous — true or false

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Past Continuous Shape and Scene · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Past Continuous Interrupted Scene · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Past Continuous Two Scenes and Story.

Say true or false. If it is false, say why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The past continuous reteaches the past simple from the start in this lesson. **Answer: False** — the past simple is finished work (Past actions); here it appears only as the sharp cut and the story's main line.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** "While I was cooking, the phone rang once" breaks Vietnam's joining rule. **Answer: False** — while holds the moving cooking side, which the rule allows; the ring is a past-simple cut standing outside the while side.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Two actions can both wear the past continuous in one sentence. **Answer: True** — side-by-side scenes both move: "While I was cooking, he was washing the dishes."
