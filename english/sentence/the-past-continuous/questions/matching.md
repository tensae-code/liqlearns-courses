# The past continuous — matching

Match each item to its partner.

**1. (Level 1)** "the phone rang" beside "I was having a shower" **Answer: rang — the sharp cut (past simple); shower — the moving scene (past continuous)** — length decides the coat: sharp and short against long and moving.

**2. (Level 2)** "while" beside a sentence side **Answer: while may hold only a past-continuous side** — Vietnam's Grade 9 rule, as printed: when before both tenses, while only before the past continuous.

**3. (Level 3)** "At midnight, the whole street ______" **Answer: was sleeping** — a scene at a named past time, one moving picture, wears this tense.
