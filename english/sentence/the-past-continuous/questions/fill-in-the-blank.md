# The past continuous — fill in the blank

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Past Continuous Shape and Scene · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Past Continuous Interrupted Scene · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Past Continuous Two Scenes and Story.

Fill each gap with the right shape.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** We ______ (eat) when the guests arrived. **Answer: were eating** — the eating is the moving scene; the arrival is the cut.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** While he ______ (drive), she was sleeping. **Answer: was driving** — two scenes side by side — both sides wear the past continuous.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** ______ it ______ (rain) at eight last night? **Answer: Was … raining** — was steps to the front to ask about a scene.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** She ______ (not listen) when the teacher explained it. (negative) **Answer: wasn't listening** — the not folds after was; the scene is denied, not the event.
