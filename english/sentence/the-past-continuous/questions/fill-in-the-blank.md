# The past continuous — fill in the blank

Fill each gap with the right shape.

**1. (Level 1)** We ______ (eat) when the guests arrived. **Answer: were eating** — the eating is the moving scene; the arrival is the cut.

**2. (Level 2)** While he ______ (drive), she was sleeping. **Answer: was driving** — two scenes side by side — both sides wear the past continuous.

**3. (Level 2)** ______ it ______ (rain) at eight last night? **Answer: Was … raining** — was steps to the front to ask about a scene.

**4. (Level 3)** She ______ (not listen) when the teacher explained it. (negative) **Answer: wasn't listening** — the not folds after was; the scene is denied, not the event.
