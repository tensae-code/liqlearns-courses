# The past continuous — short answer

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Past Continuous Shape and Scene · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Past Continuous Interrupted Scene · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Past Continuous Two Scenes and Story.

Answer in a word or a whole sentence, as asked.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What were you doing at eight last night? Answer whole and truly. **Answer: (Learner's own true sentence in was/were + -ing)** — the scene must be moving at that past time.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Set yesterday's rain scene: the children were playing outside, and then the rain began. Say it with when. **Answer: The children were playing outside when the rain began** — scene first in the continuous; the cut in the past simple after when.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Were you sleeping at eleven? Give the short answer that fits your real night. **Answer: Yes, I was. / No, I wasn't** — short answers ride was/were, the tense's moving piece.
