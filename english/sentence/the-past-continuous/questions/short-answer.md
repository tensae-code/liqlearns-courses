# The past continuous — short answer

Answer in a word or a whole sentence, as asked.

**1. (Level 1)** What were you doing at eight last night? Answer whole and truly. **Answer: (Learner's own true sentence in was/were + -ing)** — the scene must be moving at that past time.

**2. (Level 2)** Set yesterday's rain scene: the children were playing outside, and then the rain began. Say it with when. **Answer: The children were playing outside when the rain began** — scene first in the continuous; the cut in the past simple after when.

**3. (Level 3)** Were you sleeping at eleven? Give the short answer that fits your real night. **Answer: Yes, I was. / No, I wasn't** — short answers ride was/were, the tense's moving piece.
