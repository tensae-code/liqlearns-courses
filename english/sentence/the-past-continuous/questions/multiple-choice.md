# The past continuous — multiple choice

Sub-lesson markers (2026-11-08 restructure): every item's marker names the sub lesson it belongs to. The files' old depth groups map onto the sub lessons as: (Level 1) -> Sub lesson 1 — Past Continuous Shape and Scene · (Level 2) -> Sub lesson 2 — Past Continuous Interrupted Scene · (Level 3) -> Sub lesson 3 — Past Continuous Two Scenes and Story.

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** "At nine last night, she ______ TV." a) was watching  b) watches  c) had watch **Answer: a) was watching** — a scene moving at a named past time wears was + -ing.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** "They ______ football at six yesterday." a) was playing  b) were playing  c) is playing **Answer: b) were playing** — were goes with they; was is for I/he/she/it.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** "I was reading ______ the bell rang." a) while  b) when  c) since **Answer: b) when** — the bell's ring is a sharp cut; when joins a cut, while holds only a moving side.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Which sentence breaks the joining rule? a) I was reading when the bell rang.  b) While I was reading, he was writing.  c) While the bell rang once, I was reading. **Answer: c)** — while may hold only a past-continuous side; the bell's single ring is a sharp past-simple cut and must be joined by when.
