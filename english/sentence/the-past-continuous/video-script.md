# Video script — The past continuous

Super-simple voice. Read slow. The learner hears this
before or beside the written lesson.

---

Last night at eight, the house was moving.

Mum was cooking.

I was reading.

Dad was mending a chair.

All moving. All at the same past time.

The shape: was, were, and the verb with -ing.

I was cooking.

She was reading.

They were playing.

Then — a sharp thing cuts in.

I was walking home when it started to rain.

Walking — long, moving. This tense.

Started — short, sharp. The past you already know.

When can join them from either side.

Two scenes together? Both move.

While I was cooking, he was washing the dishes.

While holds only the moving side. Remember that.

Now you try.

Set me a scene from last night. Then cut it.
